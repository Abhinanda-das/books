# Advaitāṣṭakam 1

### Text 1

***hu-humkāra-garjanādi-ahorātra-sad-guṇaṁ***  
***hā kṛṣṇa-rādhikānātha-prāthanādi-bhāvanam***  
***dhūpa-dīpa-kasturī ca candanādi lepanaṁ***  
***sītānāth-ādvaita-caraṇāravinda bhāvanam (1)***

**Перевод:**

**“Roaring like a lion and thundering like a monsoon cloud, He prays day and night, “O Krsna, O Lord of Radhika!” he smears the Salagrama-sila with sandalwood paste and musk and offers arati with incense and lamp. I meditate upon the lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya, the lord of Sita-devi.”**

### Text 2

***gaṅgāvāri manohārī tulasyādi sañcarī***  
***kṛṣṇa-jñāna sadā-dhyāna prema-vāri jharjharī***  
***kṛpābdhi karuṇā-nātha bhaviṣyati prāthanaṁ***  
***sītānāth-ādvaita-caraṇāravinda bhāvanam (2)***

**Перевод:**

**“He worships the Salagrama with Ganga-water mixed with tulasi-manjaris and leaves. Tears of love incessantly flow from His eyes as he meditates upon Krsna and sings His glory. He prays to the Supreme Lord, who is an ocean of mercy, to descend to this world. I meditate upon the lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya, the lord of Sita-devi.”**

### Text 3

***muhur-muhuḥ kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa ucchaiḥ svare gāyataṁ***  
***ahe nātha jagatrātaḥ mama dṛṣṭi-gocaram***  
***dvibhuja karuṇa-nātha dīyatāṁ sudarśanam***  
***sītānāth-ādvaita-caraṇāravinda bhāvanam (3)***

**Перевод:**

**“Again and again He loudly sings the name of Krsna, praying to Him, “O My Lord! The deliverer of the universe, please manifest Your merciful two-armed form in My vision. Please award me the proper vision. I meditate upon the lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya, the lord of Sita-devi.”**

### Text 4

***śrī advaita-prāthanārtha jagannātha-ālayaṁ***  
***śacīmātu-garbhajāta caitanya-karuṇā-mayam***  
***śrī advaita-saṅga-raṅga-kīrtana-vilāsanaṁ***  
***sītānāth-ādvaita-caraṇāravinda bhāvanam (4)***

**Перевод:**

**“Due to the prayers of Sri Advaita Acarya, Sri Caitanyadeva appeared in the house of Jagannatha Misra in the womb of Mother Saci. In order to enjoy the association of Sri Advaita Acarya, Sri Caitanya sang the Holy Name of the Lord congregationally and danced with the devotees. I meditate upon the lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya, the lord of Sita-devi.”**

### Text 5

***advaita-caraṇāravinda-jñāna-dhyāna-bhāvanaṁ***  
***sadādvaita pāda-padma reṇurāśi-dhāraṇam***  
***dehi bhaktiṁ jagannātha rakṣa māmabhājanaṁ***  
***sītānāth-ādvaita-caraṇāravinda bhāvanam (5)***

**Перевод:**

**“The lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya are the goal of my knowledge and the object of my meditation. I always keep the dust of His lotus feet on my head. O my Lord, please bestow devotional service upon this unworthy soul and protect him. I meditate upon the lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya, the lord of Sita-devi.”**

### Text 6

***sarva-dātaḥ sītā-nātha-prāṇeśvara sad-guṇaṁ***  
***ye japanti sītā-nātha-pāda-padma kevalam***  
***dīyatāṁ karuṇā-nātha bhakti-yogaḥ tatkṣaṇaṁ***  
***sītānāth-ādvaita-caraṇāravinda bhāvanam (6)***

**Перевод:**

**“Sri Advaita Acarya, the Lord of Sita-devi, is the bestower of all benedictions and perfections, and He is the Lord of my life. That most merciful Lord immediately bestows bhakti upon those who meditate exclusively upon His lotus feet. I meditate upon the lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya, the lord of Sita-devi.”**

### Text 7

***śrī caitanya jayādvaita-nityānanda karuṇā-mayaṁ***  
***eka aṅga tridhāmūrti kaiśorādhi sadā varam***  
***jīvatrāṇa-bhakti-jñāna-huṁkarādi-garjanaṁ***  
***sītānāth-ādvaita-caraṇāravinda bhāvanam (7)***

**Перевод:**

**“All glories to Advaita Acarya along with Sri Caitanya and the most merciful Nityananda Prabhu. They are all One, but in three different forms, depicting the different phases of life, such as adolescence etc. Sri Advaita roars and thunders in order to deliver the jives by giving them transcendental knowledge and pure devotional service. I meditate upon the lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya, the lord of Sita-devi.”**

### Text 8

***dīna-hīna-nindakādi prema-bhakti-dāyakaṁ***  
***sarvadātaḥ sītānātha śāntipura-nāyakam***  
***raga-raṅga saṅga-doṣa karma-yoga-mokṣaṇaṁ***  
***sītānāth-ādvaita-caraṇāravinda bhāvanam (8)***

**Перевод:**

**“He bestows loving devotion upon the lowly, mean, blasphemous and other fallen people. The bestower of everything, Sri Advaita, the Lord of Sita-devi, is the leader of Santipura. He frees one from the bondage of material attachments, frivolous involvements, bad association, fruitive activities and mystic yoga. I meditate upon the lotus feet of Sri Advaita Acarya, the lord of Sita-devi.”**
