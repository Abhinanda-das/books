# Aitareya Upanishad \[BS\]

## Invocation

*oṁ vāṅ me manasi pratiṣṭitā*  
*mano me vāci pratiṣṭhitam āvir āvīr sma edhi*  
*vedasya ma āṇīṣṭhaḥ*  
*śrutaṁ me mā prahāsīḥ*  
*anenādhītenāhorātrān sandadhāmy*  
*ṛtam vadiṣyāmi satyaṁ vadiṣyāmi*  
*tan mād avatu tad vaktāram avatu avatu mām avatu vaktāram avatu vaktāram*  
*oṁ śāntīḥ śāṇtiḥ śāntiḥ hariḥ oṁ*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! May my speech be fixed in my mind. May my mind be fixed in the voice. Reveal yourself to me. May I understand the Vedas. May what I have heard never leave me. May I spend my days and nights in study. I will speak the truth. I will speak the truth. May that protect me. May that protect the teacher.**

## Chapter One

## Part One

### 1

*ātmā vā idam eka evāgra āsīt*  
*nānta kiñcana miṣat*  
*sa īkṣata lokān nu ṣrjā iti*

**Перевод:**

**Before creation only the Lord existed. Nothing else existed. He decided, “May I create the worlds.”**

**Комментарий:**

This section begins in order to show that the svarūpa of Paramātmā, source of the jīva, is different from the material body and its objects of enjoyment and to show the inferiority of the objects of the material senses.

Before the creation of the universe (idam agre), the Lord alone (ātmā) existed. Vai is for emphasis.

*tamaḥ pare deva ekībhavati*

**Matter was one with the supreme Lord. Subala Upaniṣad**

Thus matter was in a state which was undifferentiated from the Lord. How was he alone if Brahmā and others also existed? Nothing except the Lord existed.

*eko ha vai nārāyaṇa āsīt na brahma neśānaḥ*

**Only Viṣṇu existed, not Brahmā or Śiva. Mahā-nārāyaṇa Upaniṣad**

The Lord decided (īkṣata), “May I create (sṛjai) the worlds.”

### 2

*sa imāl lokān aṣrjata*  
*ambho marīcīr maram āpaḥ*  
*ado ambhaḥ pareṇa divaṁ dyauḥ pratiṣṭhā ‘ntarīkṣaṁ marīcayaḥ*  
*pṛthivī maro yā adhastāt tā āpaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord created the worlds: spiritual planets, the upper planets, the earth and the lower planets. Above is the spiritual planet and below is the place of devatās. The spiritual planet takes supportr of the heavens. The upper planets are in the antarīkṣa. Earth is the place of death. Below it are the hellish planets.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord created the universe. This is then described. Ambhaḥ means a planet beyond the heavens (pareṇa divam). It is the basis (pratiṣṭhā) of the heavens (dyauḥ pratiṣṭhā) (being above heaven). Antarīkṣa is the planet of rays (marīcayaḥ). They are called planets of light because they spread rays like the sun. Maraḥ refers to earth, where living beings die. Below earth are the hellish regions (āpaḥ).

### 3

*sa iksāteme nu lokā lokapālān su sṛjā iti*  
*so ‘mbhya eva puruṣaṁ samuddṛtyāmūrcchayat*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord decided, “I have created the planets. I should create protectors of the planets.” He extracted the puruṣa from the elements and made it into a form.**

**Комментарий:**

After creating the places of enjoyment, he decided to create protectors of those places. From the five elements like water (ambhyaḥ) he extracted a puruṣa endowed with head, feet etc. from the elements and gave it a form (amūrcchayat).

### 4

*tam abhyatapat tasyābhitaptasya mukhaṁ nirabhidyata yathā ‘ṇḍam*  
*mukhād vāk, vāco ‘gniḥ nāsike nirabhidhyetām*  
*nāsikābhyaṁ prāṇaḥ prāṇād vāyuḥ*  
*akṣiṇī nirabhidhetām akṣibhyām cakṣuś cakṣuṣa ādityaḥ*  
*karṇau nirabhidyetām karṇābhyāṁ śrotram*  
*śrotrād dikṣaḥ tvaṅnirabhidyata tvaco lomāni*  
*lomābhya oṣhadhi-vanaṣpatayaḥ*  
*hrdayam nirabhidyata hrdayān mano manasaś candramāḥ*  
*nābhir nirabhidyata nābhyā apāṇaḥ apānān mṛtyuḥ*  
*śiśnām nirabhidyata śiṣnād retaḥ retasa āpaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord meditated. From that form a mouth separated, like a chick appearing from an egg. From the mouth arose the organ of speech and Agni. The nostrils separated and from the nostrils arose the nose and Vāyu. The eye become distinct and from the eyes arose the sense organ of vision and Aditya (sun). The ears became distinct and from the ears arose the senses organ of hearing and the directions. Skin became distinct and from skin arose the sense organ of touch and from that arose the plants (presiding deity). The heart became distinct and from the heart arose mind. From the mind arose the moon deity. The navel became distinct and from the navel arose the organ of excretion. From that arose its deity, death. The genital became distinct, and from that arose the sense organ of procreation. From that arose its deity, water.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord meditated (abhyatapat) on that form as a puruṣa. A mouth became distinct in that meditated form, like a chick emerging from an egg. From the mouth (location) arose the sense organ of speech (vāk) and its presiding deity Agni. In this way the locations, sense organs separated out along with their presiding deities (location of nose, organ of smell and Vāyu, location of eyes, eyes and the sun, location of ears, ears and directions etc.) From the navel arose apāna or the anus. From the organ of excretion arose death, its presiding deity. From the place of genital (śiśnāt) arose the sense organ for procreation (retaḥ). From this arose water, its presiding deity.

## Part Two

### 1

*tā etā devatāḥ sṛṣṭā asmin mahaty arṇave prāpataṁs tam aśanāyā-pipāsābhām anvavārjat*  
*tā enam abruvann āyatanaṁ naḥ prajānīhi yasmin pratiṣṭhitā annam adāmeti*

**Перевод:**

**The devatās, having being created, fell in the great ocean of suffering. The Lord endowed the form with hunger and thirst. The devatās approached the Lord and said, “Give us a shelter and situation where we can eat food.”**

**Комментарий:**

After the Lord created the devatās for protecting the planets, the devatās fell in the great ocean of saṁsāra, afflicted by attachment and repulsion. The Lord linked that form (tam anvavārjat) with hunger and thirst (aśanāyā-pipāsabhyām). The form as a cause was afflicted by hunger and thirst and thus the devatās, the result, were also afflicted by hunger and thirst. The devatās, afflicted, approached their grandfather and said to him “Arrange a place for us. Being situated there, we will eat and thus we will be free of hunger.”

### 2

*tābho gām ānayat tā abruvan*  
*na vai no ‘yam alam iti*  
*tābho ‘śvam ānayat tā abruvan*  
*na vai no ‘yam alam iti*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord brought them a cow. They said, “That is not sufficient.” He brought them a horse. They said “That is not sufficient.”**

**Комментарий:**

He brought lump in the form of a cow to them, as a place of support. They said “That is not sufficient for us.” The Lord brought a horse. They said, “This is not enough.” Animals do not have enough capacity for the senses and senses objects, which was necessary for the devatās to function.

### 3

*tābhyaḥ puruṣam ānayat*  
*tā abruvan sukṛtaṁ bateti*  
*puruṣo vāva sukṛtam*  
*tā abravīt yathāyatanaṁ praviśateti*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord brought them a human. They said, “This is a suitable form. The human form has arisen by many puṇyas.” The Lord said, “Enter that shelter.”**

**Комментарий:**

Seeing the form of a human, they said this is very suitable (sukṛtam). It is proper because the puruṣa arose from all puṇya karmas (puruṣaḥ vāva sukṛtam). The Lord said, “Enter this abode.”

### 4

*agnir vāg bhūtvā mukhaṁ prāviśat*  
*vāyuḥ prāṇo bhūtvā nāsike prāviśa*  
*ādityāc cakṣur bhūtvā ‘kṣinṇi prāviśad*  
*diśaḥ śṛotraṁ bhūtvā karṇau prāviśan*  
*oṣadhi-vanaspatayo lomāni bhūtvā tvacam prāviśan*  
*candamā mano bhūtvā hṛdayaṁ prāviśat*  
*mṛtyur apāno bhūtvā nābhiṁ prāviśat*  
*āpo reto bhūtvā śiśnaṁ prāviśan*

**Перевод:**

**Agni became speech and entered the mouth. Vāyu became prāṇa (organ smell) and entered the nostrils. Āditya became the eye (vision) and entered the eyes. The directions became organ hearing and entered the ears. The planets became hairs (touch) and entered the skin. The moon became the mind and entered the heart. Death became the anus and entered the navel. Water became organ procreation and entered the genital.**

**Комментарий:**

The devatā Agni became the form of speech and entered its womb, the mouth.

### 5

*tam aśanāyā-pipase abrutām āvābhyām abhi prajānīhīti*  
*sa te abravīd etāsv eva vāṁ devatāsv ābhajāmy etāsu*  
*bhaginyau karomīti*  
*tasmād yasyai kasyai ca devatāyai*  
*havir gṛhyate bhāginyāv evāsyām aśanāyā-pipāse bhavataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Then hunger and thirst said, “Give us a place.” The Lord said, “I make you co-sharers with all the devatās. You share whatever offerings are made to the devatās.”**

**Комментарий:**

When the devatās had obtained places, hunger and thirst said to the Lord, “Give us a place also.” He said to them, I make you co-sharers with the devatās. You are entitled to a share of the sacrifices offered to the devatās, who are afflicted by hunger and thirst.

## Part Three

### 1

*sa īkṣateme nu lokāś caloka-pālāś ca annam ebhyaḥ sṛjā iti*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord considered, “These are the planets and the protectors of the planets. Let me create food for them.”**

**Комментарий:**

Having created the planets and their devatās, he decided, “Let me create food for them, since they are hungry.”

### 2

*so ‘po ‘bhyatapat tābhyo ‘bhitaptābhyo mūrtir ajāyata*  
*yā vai ṣā mūrtir ajāyatānnaṁ vai tat*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord meditated on water and from the water, the object of his meditation, arose a form which was food.**

**Комментарий:**

He meditated (abhyatapat) on water. From the water upon which he meditated a substantial form of food arose.

### 3

*tad enad abhi sṛsṭaṁ parāg atyājighāṁsat*  
*tad vācā ‘jighṛkṣat tan nāśaknot vācā grahītum*  
*sa yad dhainad vācā ‘grahaiṣyad abhivyāhṛtya haivānnam atrapsyat*

**Перевод:**

**The created food wanted to flee. The form tried to catch the food by speech, but he could not catch it by speech. If he had caught the food by speech, saying the word “food” everyone would have been satisfied.**

**Комментарий:**

That created food for the planets and protectors who were thirsty and hungry, which was to be eaten, wanted to run away (ājighāṁsat). It began to run away, like a mouse on facing the mouth of a cat. The form which first arose, that of the planets and their protectors, wanted to catch the food by means of speech, but could not catch the food by speech. If he had caught the food by speech, by saying the word “food,” everyone would have been satisfied.

### 4

*tat prāṇenājighṛkṣat tan nāśaknot prāṇena grahītum*  
*sa yad dhainat prāṇenāgrahaiṣyad abhiprāṇya haivānnam atrapsyat*

**Перевод:**

**The form tried to catch the food with prāṇa, but could not catch it with prāṇa. If he had caught it with prāṇa, smelling it, everyone would have been satisfied.**

**Комментарий:**

Meaning is similar to previous verse.

### 5

*tac cakṣuṣā ajighṛkṣat tan nāśaknoc cakṣuṣā grahītum*  
*sa yad dhainac cakṣuṣā ‘grahaiṣyad dṛstvā haivānnam atrapsyat*

**Перевод:**

**He tried to grasp it with the eye, but could not grasp it with the eye.**

**If he had grasped it with the eye, seeing food, everyone would have been satisfied.**

### 6

*tac chrotreṇājighṛkṣat tan nāśaknoc chrotreṇa grahītum*  
*sa yad dhainac chrotreṇā grahaiṣyac chrutvā haivānnam atrapsyat*

**Перевод:**

**He tried to catch it with the ear, but could not catch it with the ear.**

**If he had caught the food with the ear, hearing about food, everyone would have been satisfied.**

### 7

*tat tvacā ‘jighṛkṣat tan nāśaknot tvacā grahītum*  
*sa yad dhainat tvacā ‘grahaisyat spṛṣṭvā haivannam atrapsyat*

**Перевод:**

**He tried to catch the food with the skin but could not catch it with the skin. If he had caught the food with skin, by touching food, everyone would have been satisfied.**

### 8

*tan manasā ‘jighrkṣat tan nāśaknon manasā grahītum*  
*sa yad dhainan manasā grahaiṣyad dhyātvā haivānnam atrapsyat*

**Перевод:**

**He tried to catch food with the mind, but could not catch it with the mind.**

**If he had caught it with the mind, by meditating upon food, everyone would have been satisfied.**

### 9

*tac chiśnenājighrkṣat tan naśaknoc chiśnena grahītum*  
*sa yad dhainac chiśnenāgrahaiṣyad visṛjya haivannam atrapasyat*

**Перевод:**

**He tried to catch it with the genital, but could not catch it using the genital. If he had caught it with the genital, by discharging food, everyone would have been satisfied.**

### 10

*tad apānenājighṛkṣat tad āvayat*  
*saiṣo ‘nnasya graho ‘*  
*yad vāyur annāyur vā eṣa tad vāyuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The form grasped food using apāna air and ate it. This air is the grasper of food. Air has food as its very life.**

**Комментарий:**

He grasped food using apāna, and ate it (āvayat). Vāyur (air) was the grasper of food. Food is the life of air.

### 11

*sa īkṣata kathaṁ nv idaṁ mad ṛte syād iti*  
*sa īkṣata katareṇa prapadyā iti*  
*sa īksāta yadi vācābhivyāhṛtam yadi prāṇenābhiprānitaṁ*  
*yadi cakṣuṣā dṛṣṭaṁ yadi śrotreṇa śrutam*  
*yadi tvacā spṛstām, yadi manasā dhyātam*  
*yady apānenābhyapānitam yadi śiśnena visṛṣtam*  
*atha ko ‘ham iti*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord considered, “How will the body function without me? Through which organ will I enter this body? If voice speaks without me, if breath breathes without me, if the eye sees without me, if the ear hears without me, if the skin touches without me, if the mind meditates without me, if apāna leaves the body without me, if the genital discharges without me, who am I?”**

**Комментарий:**

Having created the planets and their protectors, having them situated in a body, and making them dependent on food, the Lord thought, “How can the body where the devatās reside function without me, the master of the body? A city cannot function without a king. If speech etc. were to function without me, then who will I be the master of? Who do I become?”

### 12

*sa etam eva sīmānaṁ vidāryaitayā dvārā prāpadyata*  
*saiṣā viḍrtir nāma dvās tad etan ānandanam*  
*tasya traya āvasathās trayaḥ svapnāḥ*  
*ayam āvasatho ‘yam āvasatho ‘yam āvasatha iti*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord pierced the top of the skull and entered by that door. That door is called vidṛti, which is a cause of joy. In the body of the jīva the Lord has three abodes, astonishing like a dream: the eye during waking, the antaḥkaraṇa during dreaming and the ākāśa in the heart during deep sleep.**

**Комментарий:**

He thought that it would be improper to enter by means of the mouth or the breath etc. which were all his servants. Thus piercing the top of the head, he entered by that door (dvārā), following the statement anena jīvenātmane: the Lord enter the creation with the jīva. (Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad) Thus the opening is called vidṛti (hole in the skull). It is a door which is a cause of rejoicing, unlike other doors of the body. For the Lord in the body of the jīva (tasya) there are three abodes (āvasathāḥ). These are astonishing like dreams. What are these? They are the eye during the waking state, the antaḥ karaṇa during the dream state and the ākāśa in the heart during the deep sleep state.

### 13

*sa jāto bhūtāny abhivyaikṣat kim ihānyaṁ vāvadisad iti*  
*sa etam eva puruṣaṁ brahma-tatamam apaśyad idam adarśam itī*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord manifested names and forms for the material elements and gave the jīvas separate identities. The jīva knew the Lord, spread as Brahman. The jīva realized him directly.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord manifested with name and form (abhivyaikṣat) the five elements into which he had entered. He arranged that the jīva appear separate from himself. The jīva saw that person, the Lord within, excellent Brahman. The puruṣa is Vāsudeva, situated in the jīva’s body and spread out (tatamam) as Brahman, the huge shelter. He knew Nārāyaṇa. How did he know him? He saw him directly (adarśam) like seeing an amalakī in one’s hand. Tatamam should actually be tatatamam, but is shortened for metrical reasons. Idam indicates that the Lord is different from the jīva.

### 14

*tasmād idandro nāma idandro ha vai nāma*  
*tam idandraṁ santam indra ity ācakṣate parokṣeṇa*  
*parokṣa-priya iva hi devāḥ prokṣa-priyā iva hi devāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore the Lord is called idandra (he who was seen by the jīva). He is called Indra, by indirect speech, since the devatās are fond of indirect speech.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva saw (dra) the Lord (idam). Thus the Lord is called idandra (he who was seen by the jīva). He is called Indra, since the devatās are fond of speaking indirectly (parokṣeṇa).

## Chapter Two - Jiva’s birth and death for producing renunciation

### 1

*puruṣe ha vā ayam ādito garbho bhavati yad etad retaḥ*  
*tad etat sarvebho ‘ṅgebhyas tejaḥ sambhūtam ātmany evātmānam bibharti*  
*tad yadā striyāṁ siñcaty atheitaj janayati*  
*tad asya prathamaṁ janma*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva first becomes an embryo as semen. Semen, which is the potency of all the limbs, carries the jīva. When deposited in a woman it produces the embryo. This is the first birth.**

**Комментарий:**

The puruṣa first becomes and embryo in the form of semen. This semen is called the potency (tejas) which has arisen from all the limbs. It carries the jīva (ātmānam) within itself (ātmani). The jīva is combined with the semen. This explanation is given to induce renunciation of material life. When placed in the woman, it produces the embryo. That is the first birth.

### 2

*tat striyā ātmabhūyaṁ gacchati yathā svam aṅga yathā*  
*tasmād enāṁ na hinasti*  
*sāsyaitam ātmanām atra gataṁ bhāvayati*

**Перевод:**

**The semen becomes one with the woman, just like her own limb. Therefore one should not harm her. She increases herself as the embryo.**

**Комментарий:**

The semen in the women becomes one with the women. It remains as a limb of the woman. She increases this embryo which has entered in the form of semen, this embryo which has become herself, who is acting as a nourisher. Therefore one does not injure her.

### 3

*sā bhāvayitṛī bhāvayitavyā bhavati taṁ strī garbhaṁ bibharti*  
*so ‘gra eva kumāraṁ janmano ‘gre dhibhāvayati*  
*sa yat kumāraṁ janmano ‘gre dhibhāvayaty ātmānam eva*  
*tad bhāvayaty eṣāṁ lokānāṁ santatyā*  
*evaṁ santatā hīme lokās tad asya dvitīyaṁ janma*

**Перевод:**

**The women, the nourisher, should be nourished by her husband since she nourishes the embryo. The father nourishes the son before birth and nourishes the child after birth. He nourishes himself (in the form of a son), for continuing the population. By production of sons the population continues. This is the second birth.**

**Комментарий:**

The woman (bhāvayitrī) who increases herself as the embryo which has entered her as semen should be nourished by food and other items by her husband and others. One should repay someone who has given help. She nourishes the embryo which entered as semen. The father nourishes the young son before birth. He nourishes the child after birth, first (agre) with jāta-karma etc. By this he nourishes himself since he entered the embryo as semen. Patir jāyāṁ praviśati: the husband enters the wife. Why does he produce himself as a son? He does this for continuing the population. If people did not produce sons, the population would dwindle. Thus the population continues or is increased by production of sons. This is the second birth. This indicates upanayana and other saṁskāras.

### 4

*so ‘syāyam ātmā puṇyebhyaḥ karmabhyaḥ pratidhīyate*  
*athāsyāyam itara ātmā kṛta-kṛtyaḥ vayogataḥ praiti*  
*sa itaḥ prayann eva punar jāyate tad asya tṛtīyaṁ janma*

**Перевод:**

**The son represents the father by performing puṇya karmas for him. The father becomes successful and departs in old age. He departs from this world and is born attain. This is the third birth.**

**Комментарий:**

This self, the father in the form of the son, acts as surety by performing puṇya karmas that should be done (for the father). The son does karmas for the departed father. Whatever karmas the father did not do which should have been done are performed by the son. That is mentioned in Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad, in sampatti-vidyā. The soul of the father (asya ātmā) becomes successful, because of the karmas performed by his son. Having given responsibility to the son, growing old (vayogataḥ) the father dies. After death (itaḥ prayan) he is again born.

The first birth is entering the woman as semen. The second birth is the child coming out of the womb. The third birth is the father being born again. Though the first two births are those of the son and the third is that of the father, since the father and son are considered one entity, this is stated.

### 5

*tad uktam ṛṣiṇā*  
*garbhe nu sann anv eṣām avedam ahaṁ devānām janimāni viśvā*  
*śataṁ mā pura āyasīr arakṣann adhaḥ śyeno javasā niradīyam iti*  
*garbha eva etac chayāno vamadeva evam uvāca*

**Перевод:**

**This was said by a sage:**

**While in the womb I experienced all the births of devatās. I was kept in hundreds of bodies which were like iron prisons. Later I escaped quickly, like a hawk. Vāmadeva spoke in this way while situated in the womb.**

**Комментарий:**

This was spoke by a sage in a mantra. While in the womb, I experienced all (viśvā) the births (janimāni) of the devatās. Nu indicates questioning. I was kept in hundreds of bodies (pura) tough as iron (āyasīḥ), which were like prisons to prevent me from leaving the body. Then I escaped quickly like a hawk, by the knowledge of a method taught by a guru. Vāmadeva spoke in this way while resting (śayānaḥ) in the womb.

### 6

*sa evaṁ vidvān asmāc charīra-bhedād ūrddham utkramaty āmuṣmin*  
*svarge loke sarvān kāmān āptvā ‘mrṭaḥ samabhavat samabhavat*

**Перевод:**

**This wise man, giving up the body, arises above this world and having attained all desires in the spiritual world, became liberated.**

**Комментарий:**

Vāmadeva, previously situated in saṁsāra, attained knowledge of Brahman and from this body ascended by the path of the sun, to the world of the Lord (svarga-loke). Having attained all desires, experiencing all auspicious equalities, such no sin etc., he became liberated. One may say “He opens his mouth and sleeps.” But this does not indicate the sequence of events. In this way the two statements do not indicate that first he experienced all desires and then he became liberated. He was in knowledge (vidvān). All others, thinking of their sons and daughters as themselves, immersed in thinking they are the body while rotating in the wheel of saṁsāra are most lamentable.

## Chapter Three

### 1

*ko ’yam ātmeti vayam upāsmahe? kataraḥ sa ātmā*  
*yena vā rūpa paśyati, yena vā śabda śṛṇoti*  
*yena vā gandhān ājighrati, yena vā vācaṁ vyākaroti*  
*yena vā svādu cāsvādu ca vijnānāti*

**Перевод:**

**Who is the ātmā that we worship? What are the qualities of this ātmā, by which one sees form, hears sound, smells fragrance, speaks words, and experiences taste?**

**Комментарий:**

Who is this ātmā that we worship? What is the nature of this ātmā? Previously all the wise sages gathered and deliberated, “Who is this ātmā which we worship? What type of qualities does he have?” The word ātmā is used to signify the body, senses, mind prāṇa, intellect jīva and supreme Lord. Among these, who is the ātmā worthy of worship? This was their deliberation. Deliberating, they also specified this question. Who is this by whom one sees form, by whom one becomes aware of taste? Who is the person from whom the eye, ear, nose, voice—all the external senses—arise?

### 2

*yad etad dhṛdayaṁ manaś caitat saṁjñānam ājñānaṁ vijñānaṁ*  
*prajñānaṁ medhā dṛṣṭir dhṛtir matir manīṣā jūtiḥ smṛtiḥ sañkalpaḥ*  
*kratur asuḥ kāmo vaśa iti*  
*sarvāṇy evaitāni prajñānasya nāmadheyāhi bhavanti*

**Перевод:**

**The heart, the mind, inclusive awareness, slight knowledge, particular knowledge, excellent knowledge, concentrated intelligence, direct perception, confirmatory knowledge, contemplation special, independent intelligence, affection, worship, prāṇa, longing and desire are all names of Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Heart and mind are internal senses. The words starting with saṁjñānam indicate particular forms of dharma-bhūta-jñāna. Saṁjñānam is inclusive awareness. Ājñānam is slight knowledge. Vijñāna is knowledge of particulars. Prajñānam is excellent knowledge. Medhā is concentrated intelligence. Dṛsti is direct perception. Dhṛti is confirmatory knowledge. Mati is contemplation. Manīṣā is special intelligence which is independent. Jūti is affection. Kratuḥ is worship. Asuḥ is prāṇaḥ. kāma is longing for objects not attained. Vaśa is desire.

All of these are names of Brahman, the svarūpa of jñāna (prajñānasya). They are his body. They are different forms of Brahman. Just as all these names resolve into knowledge, everything resolves into the Lord. Showing that the Lord is different from senses, mind prāṇa and intelligence, he is also different from Brahmā, Indra and others.

### 3

*eṣa brahmaiṣa indra eṣa prajāpatir ete sarve devā*  
*imāni ca pañca mahābhūtāni*  
*pṛthivī vāyur ākāśa āpo jyotīṁṣīty etānīmāni ca kṣudra-miśrāṇīva bījāni*  
*itarāṇi cetarāṇi ca aṇḍajāni ca jārujāni ca*  
*sveda-jāni codbhijjāni aśvā gāvaḥ puruṣā hastino*  
*yat kiñcedaṁ prāṇi jaṅgamaṁ ca patatri ca*  
*yac sthāvaraṁ sarvaṁ tat prajñānetraṁ prajñāne pratiṣṭhitaṁ prajñānetro*  
*lokaḥ prajñā pratiṣṭhā prajñānaṁ brahma*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is Brahmā, Indra, Prajāpati and all these devatās. He is all the five great elements—earth, air, ether, water and fire. He is all these seeds, mixed with the small living beings. He is other types of beings: those born from eggs and those born as embryos, those born from perspiration and those born from seeds. Whatever beings such as horses, cows, humans or elephnats exist, as well as all plants, all are guided by the Lord. They all take shelter of the Lord. The world is under the shelter of the Lord. Everything is established within the Lord. Prajñānam is the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahman is all the devatās. This means that Brahmā and all devatās with their names are his body. The ātmā who should be worshipped is different from the jīvas. That he is different from matter and material bodies is then explained. He is the five elements—earth, air, ether, water and fire. He is the seeds mixed with the small entities ( such as insects). The word iva is without meaning. Two types are shown: those not born from wombs and those born from wombs: itarāni and cetarāṇi. Those born of eggs are birds etc. Those born of embryos are humans etc. (These are born from wombs.) Those born of perspiration are lice etc. Those born of seeds are trees etc. (The last two are not born from wombs).

Animals like cows, horses, humans, elephants, birds and non-moving plants – who are all guided by the antaryāmī (prajñā-netram) all these are established in prajñāna or Brahman at the time of pralaya. They take shelter of the Lord or prajñāna. The whole world (lokaḥ) is under the shelter of the Lord (prajñā-netraḥ). He is the shelter of the whole world (prajñā pratiṣṭhā). Prajñānam thus is Brahman alone. Since the Lord is the antaryāmī of everything, he, being the greatest by svarūpa and qualities (brahman), exists in prajñānam.

### 4

*sa etena prajñenātmanā ‘smāl lokād utkramya amusmin svarge loke sarvān kāmān āptvā amṛtaḥ samabhavat samabhavat oṁ*

**Перевод:**

**He who knows this surpassef this world and attained all spiritual qualities along with the Lord in the spiritual world. He became immortal.**

**Комментарий:**

He, knowing this, going from this planet to the planet of the Lord (svarge loke) by the path of the sun, manifested all auspicious qualities along with the Lord (prajñena ātmanā sarva-kāmān āptva).

*so ‘śnute sarvān kāmān saha brāhmaṇa vipaścitā*

**He who knows that Brahman enjoys all pleasures along with the all-knowing Brahman. Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**2.1.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.1.2)
