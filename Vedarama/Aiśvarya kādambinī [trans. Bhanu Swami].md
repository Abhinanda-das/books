# Aiśvarya kādambinī \[trans. Bhanu Swami\]

## Chapter One - Description of the Lord’s tripad vibhūti

### Verse 1

*kṛṣṇābhidhāyai kanakāmbarāyai śyāmābja-tanvai sarasīruhāyai \|*  
*nitya-śriyai nitya-guṇa-vrajāyai namo’stu tasyai para-devatāyai \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the supreme Lord named Kṛṣṇa, dressed in golden cloth, with bodily complexion of a blue lotus, who is the embodiment of a lotus, decorated with eternal qualities and eternally engaged with Rādhā.**

### Verse 2

*sanātanaṁ rūpam ihopadarśayann ānanda-sindhuṁ paritaḥ pravardhayan \|*  
*antas-tamaḥ-stoma-haraḥ sa rājatāṁ caitanya-rūpo vidhur adbhutodayaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May Kṛṣna in the form of Caitanya, the moon arising in the most wonderful away, who increases the ocean of bliss everywhere, who revealed Sanātana and Rūpa to the world, who destroys all internal darkness, remain victorious!**

### Verse 3

*bahu-bhūma-saudha-sadṛśo vijñāna-ghano bahiḥ-stoma-stomāt \|*  
*parama-vyomābhikhyo vibhāti viṣṇor mahādbhuto lokaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The most astonishing planet of Viṣṇu called the supreme sky, like a combination of many palaces, filled with pure consciousness, exists outside the many layers of the universe.**

### Verse 4

*āste kṛṣṇo yatra nārāyaṇātmā vyūhair juṣṭo vāsudevādi saṁjñaiḥ \|*  
*kurvan krīḍāṁ pārṣada-grāma-siddhāṁ dīvyad-bhūti-nārasiṁhādi-rūpī \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There resides Kṛṣna, in the form of Nārāyaṇa, served by Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Aniruddha and Pradyumna. Manifesting like Narasiṁha full of spiritual powers, he plays with his many associates.**

### Verse 5

*nityaṁ lakṣmīr yam upāste sva-nāthaṁ nānā-rūpā bahu-rūpaṁ pareśam \|*  
*cit-saukhyātmā sva-samābhiḥ sakhībhiḥ sarveśānā bahu-sambhāra-pūrṇā \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lakṣmī, having many forms, composed of knowledge and bliss, the ruler of all beings, full of all wealth, constantly worships her master, the supreme Lord having many forms.**

### Verse 6

*dīvyati tad-upari lokaḥ kuśa-sthalī madhupurī vrajābhikhyaḥ \|*  
*yasmin vilasati kṛṣṇo janaiḥ svakīyaiḥ sa devakī-sūnuḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The planet above this is composed of Dvārakā, Mathurā, and Vraja. There Kṛṣna, the son of Devakī, plays with his associates.**

### Verse 7

*dvāravatyāṁ madhu-puryāṁ ca kṛṣṇaṁ śaineyādyair uddhavādyaiś ca pūjyam \|*  
*nānā-sampan-nibhrtāyāṁ pareśaṁ rukmiṇy-ādyāḥ sambhajante śriyas tam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Dvārakā and Mathurā, endowed with various types of wealth, Rukmiṇī and other consorts serve the supreme Lord Kṛṣṇa who is worshipped by Satyāki, Uddhava and others.**

### Verse 8

*śrī-gokule harir asau vraja-nātha-sūnuḥ śrī-carcite bahu-sakho’sti sa-bhṛtya-vargaḥ \|*  
*śrī-rādhikā-priya-sakhībhir adhīśvarīyaṁ saṁsevate sva-sadṛśībhir ananya-vṛttiḥ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Gokula, a place desired by Lakṣmī, the son of Nanda resides with many friends and servants. There Rādhā, the supreme ruler, completely devoted to Kṛṣṇa, is served with by priya-sakhīs similar in nature to her.**

### Verse 9

*evaṁ rūpo harir udbhāti nityaṁ yad gopālopaniṣat taṁ tathāha \|*  
*prādurbhāvaṁ sa kadācit prapañce’py añcet svāmī sakalāṁśair viśiṣṭaḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This form eternally manifests there. Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad describes him in this way. Sometimes he appears in the material world, accompanied by all his expansions.**

### Verse 10

*madhuraiśvarya-caritra-rūpavattvān madhurād veṇu-ravāc ca nanda-sūnuḥ \|*  
*priyatamatā-pūrṇa-tamāj jana-vrajāc ca sphuṭam uktaḥ kavibhir vibhur varīyān \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The poets clearly describe the son of Nanda as the best and the most powerful because he has a form and pastimes of sweetness and power, because he plays the flute sweetly and because he is surrounded by devotees possessing the highest love.**

## Chapter Two - The Lord’s appearance in the material world

### Verse 1

*saṅkarṣaṇo harir atha pralayāvasāne jīvān udīkṣya karuṇaḥ kṣubhitān samantān \|*  
*praikṣiṣṭa sva-prakṛtim aṇḍa-ghaṭās tatas tu prādur babhūvur uru-bhoga-cayān dadhānaḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At the end of pralaya, the lord as Saṅkarṣaṇa, seeing all the agitated jīvas, glanced at his prakṛti. Then all the universes, possessing great varieties of enjoyment, appeared.**

### Verse 2

*teṣāṁ sva-garbheṣu haris tadābhūt pradyumna-saṁjño janako viriñceḥ \|*  
*bhavanti yasmād bahavo’vatārā mīnādayo’nanta-guṇā vibhumnaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord called Pradyumna then appeared in each universe. He gives birth to Brahmā and from that powerful Lord arose many avatāras having unlimited qualities such as Matsya.**

### Verse 3

*antaryāmī vyaṣṭi-jīva-vrajānāṁ jātas teṣu kṣīradhi-stho’niruddhaḥ \|*  
*sārdhaḥ devaiḥ krīḍati prājya-tejās teṣāṁ śatrūn nāśayan yaḥ samantāt \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Aniruddha, situated in the Milk Ocean, appeared in each universe. He is the antaryāmī of all the individual jīvas in the universes. Endowed with great powers, he plays with the devatās and thoroughly kills the enemies of the devatās.**

### Verse 4

*yadā yadā rākṣasa-sainya-jālair dharma-kṣatiḥ syāt praśamāya tasyāḥ \|*  
*tadā tadā śrī-mahilaḥ sarāmaḥ sa vāsudevaś ca bhavet kadācit \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Whenever dharma is injured by the host of demons, in order to alleviate the suffering, Vāsudeva appears along with his consort and Balarāma.**

### Verse 5

*prahlādaṁ yaḥ khidyamānaṁ sva-mṛtyuṁ vīkṣya stambhād āvīrāsīn nṛsiṁhaḥ \|*  
*ugro’dārīt tad-ripuṁ sānukampaḥ śrī-govindo nanda-sūnuḥ sajīyāt \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Prahlāda suffering and about to die, ferocious Nṛsiṁha appeared from a pillar and tore apart his enemy. May merciful Govinda, the son of Nanda, be victorious!**

### Verse 6

*svayaṁ hariḥ sa kadācit sadhāmā sa-pārṣado yadi gacchen nṛlokam \|*  
*bhuvo bharaḥ sa tadeyāt praṇāśaṁ bhaved bahuḥ svajanānāṁ pramodaḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If the Lord personally comes with his associates and abode into the human world the burden of the earth is destroyed and the devotees greatly rejoice.**

### Verse 7

*āvirbhavet prathamaṁ dhāma-viṣṇoḥ pitrādayaḥ kramatas tatra mukhyaḥ \|*  
*paścād asau ramayā tat samābhiḥ sārdhaṁ prabhuḥ paramarddhiḥ priyābhiḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**First his abode and then chief associates like his parents gradually appear on earth. Later the Lord endowed with all earth appears with his consort and other women of equal qualities.**

### Verse 8

*vidyās tatra svayam eva prabhātāś cāturyāpy akhilāḥ pārṣadeṣu \|*  
*sva-svāpekṣyā hari-bhaktiḥ pratītā vibhrājeran nikhilāḥ sampadaś ca \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In that place, knowledge and all skills spontaneously manifest in all the associates. Devotion to the Lord according to the individual’s inclinations also manifests. All types of wealth manifest for them.**

## Chapter Three - Description of Vasudeva, Nanda and others who arose in the Vṛṣṇi dynasty

### Verse 1

*vṛṣṇir vaṁśe devamīḍhaḥ sa o’bhūt bhārye tasya kṣatriyārye prasiddhe śūrābhikhyaḥ kṣatriyāyāṁ kumāraḥ parjanyākhyaḥ sambabhūvārya-kāyām \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Devamīḍha of the Vṛṣṇi dynasty had two famous wives, one of kṣatriya lineage and the other of vaiśya lineage. Śūra was born to the kṣatriya wife and Parjanya was born to the vaiśya wife.**

### Verse 2

*śūrād āsīd vasudevo mahātmā patnī yasya praguṇā devakī sā \|*  
*parjanyāt tu vraja-bhūpāt sanando patnī yasyottama-kāntir yaśodā \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The great devotee Vasudeva appeared from Śūra. Vasudeva’s wife was Devakī, endowed with great qualities. Nanda appeared from the king of Vraja, Parjanya. Nanda’s wife was Yaśodā, of the highest beauty.**

### Verse 3

*yasmin jāte trideveśair akāri prīty-utphullair vara-vāditra-ghoṣaḥ \|*  
*sthānaṁ viṣṇor vasudevaṁ sa śaurir mānyo dātā dvija-sevī babhūva \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At Vasudeva’s birth, the devatās with ecstatic joy loudly glorified him with the best music. The son of Śūra was respected as the place of Viṣṇu and was thus called Vāsudeva. He was generous and served the brāhmaṇas.**

### Verse 4

*vaiyāsakir yāṁ kila sarva-devatāṁ jagāda vidvān api deva-rūpiṇīm \|*  
*sā devakī viśvadharaṁ maheśvaraṁ dadhāra kukṣau kim u citram uccakaiḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Devakī, whom learned Śukadeva described as the sum of all devatās, or deva-rūpinī, another form of the Lord, held in her womb the supreme Lord, who holds within himself the universe. How amazing this is!**

### Verse 5

*nandaḥ śrī-kānta-bhakto vraja-dharaṇī-patiḥ śāstravid dharma-niṣṭhaḥ sāmantaiḥ snigdha-cittair api saciva-varaiḥ śāsanasthair variṣṭhaḥ \|*  
*prākārī vara-saudho’parimita-dhavalaś citra-vāditra-nādair juṣṭo yānair athādyair bahu-vidha-vibhavaḥ sarva-mānyaḥ sa āsīt \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda was a devotee of Viṣṇu. He was the lord of Vraja, learned in scriptures and fixed in dharma. He was served by affectionate, obedient ministers and neighboring leaders. Living in an excellent palace, having an unlimitedly handsome face, welcomed by various musical instruments, using excellent chariots, endowed with many types of wealth, he was respected by all.**

### Verse 6

*viṣṇur viśvaṁ coṣatuḥ kukṣi-koṇe yasyāḥ stanyenāpa tṛptiṁ sa bhūmā \|*  
*lakṣmīḥ pādau sādarātmā vavande sā kalyāṇī kena varṇyo yaśodā \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Who can describe auspicious Yaśodā in whose womb Viṣṇu and the universe dwelled, and by whose breast Kṛṣṇa was satisfied, and at whose feet Lakṣmī offered obeisance with reverence?**

### Verse 7

*bandhavo vrajapater bahuvidyāḥ sāgnayo hari-guru-dvija-bhaktāḥ \|*  
*sampado’ti-vipulāḥ kila yeṣāṁ dhenavo bahu-hayāś ca virejuḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The friends of Nanda were very learned, devoted the Lord, elders and the brāhmaṇas, and kept the sacred fire in their houses. They were very wealthy, having many cows and horses.**

### Verse 8

*āsīt sakhā vṛṣabhānur mahīpo nandasya yo guṇa-vṛndair varīyān \|*  
*kanyā yataḥ praguṇā rādhikā sā vedaḥ śriyām adhipāṁ yām avocat \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda’s great friend was the king Vṛṣabhānu, who possessed a host of most excellent qualities. He produced a daughter, Rādhikā, having the best qualities, whom the Vedas praised as the queen of all Lakṣmīs.**

### Verse 9

*prītiṁ yasmin suṣṭhu taurya-trika-jñāḥ prāpuḥ sūtā māgadhā vandinaś ca \|*  
*sarvābhijñā-darśita-sva-sva-vidyā yasmāt kāmān lebhire te’bhimṛgyān \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sūtās, māgadhas and vandis, knowing song, dance and instruments, had affection for Nanda and displayed all their arts to him. From Nanda they obtained whatever they requested.**

### Verse 10

*dānāmbhasāṁ yasya nadībhir uccair nīvṛn nadī-mātṛkāṁ dadhāra \|*  
*kalpa-drumāḥ kāma-dughāś ca śaśvat kāmān samastān vavṛṣur manojñān \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His kingdom was the mother of rivers, flowing with waters of charity. The desire trees and kāma-dhenus continually showered all attractive objects desired.**

### Verse 11

*govardhano yasya saratnaḥ śailaḥ sunirjharaḥ kandara-mandirāḍhyaḥ \|*  
*puṣpaiḥ phalaiḥ sad-yavasaiś ca ramyo yathārtha-nāmā vitatāna sevām \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Govardhana Mountain, filled with jewels and beautiful waterfalls, caves and pavilions, fulfilling its name (“increaser of the cows”) served him by offering flowers, fruits, and fresh grass.**

## Chapter Four - Description of Nanda’s City

### Verse 1

*bṛhad-vane yasya bṛhat-kapāṭaṁ puraṁ bṛhat-saudha-varaṁ babhāse \|*  
*ajanmano janma harasya yasmin babhūva janma praguṇasya viṣṇoḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda’s city endowed with a huge palace and huge gates exists within Mahāvana. In that place was born Viṣṇu of great qualities, who though unborn, destroys birth and death.**

### Verse 2

*bhānu-bhūpa-bhavanaṁ yad-antike kānti-kandala-supuṣkalaṁ babhau \|*  
*preyasī vraja-vidhor maheśvarī sambabhūva kila yatra rādhikā \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nearby is the palace of Vṛṣabhānu, replete with all types of beauty. The dear queen of Kṛṣṇa, Rādhikā, manifested at that place.**

### Verse 3

*nandīśvarādrer maṇi-citra-sānor ūpetyakāyāṁ bahu-nirjharasya \|*  
*puṣpaiḥ phalaiś cātimanoharasya puraṁ vrajeśasya mahat tadāsīt \|\|3\|*

**Перевод:**

**The city of Nanda is on the jeweled plateau of Nandīśvara, which has many waterfalls and is most attractive with flowers and fruits.**

### Verse 4

*yasmin vicitrair maṇibhiḥ praṇītā bhānti sma harmyāṭṭaka-niṣkuṭādayaḥ \|*  
*samāna-sūtrair vihitā vipaṇyaḥ kūpāḥ sarasyaś ca tathā-vidhās tāḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The palaces, towers, and groves shine with colorful gems. The shops are in a straight line as are the wells and lakes.**

### Verse 5

*yad aharan mano ratna-gopurair urubhir aṣṭabhiś cāru-gopuraiḥ \|*  
*rurucire bhṛśaṁ yeṣu rakṣiṇaḥ kanaka-bhūṣaṇā bhūpa-pakṣiṇaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The city is attractive with eight huge, beautiful cow sheds with jeweled arches at the gates. In those sheds are guards ornamented with gold, supporting the king.**

### Verse 6

*yan-madhyamaṁ vraja-pateḥ kila sapta-bhūmaṁ saudha-rāja-vimalaṁ vilasat-patākam \|*  
*vaidūrya-vidruma-masāra-maṇi-praṇīta-stambhāli-jāla-valabhī-kula-sat-valīkam \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the center of the city is the pure palace of Nanda, seven stories high, with fluttering flags. The pillars, lattice work, pinnacles, and roof projections are made of vaidūrya, coral, and sapphires.**

### Verse 7

*nirasta-māyāpi vicitra-māyā vāso ramāyā nikhilārcitasya \|*  
*sabhāḥ sabhānanda-nṛpasya yasmin samājitā śilpi-varair adīpi \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though the palace is devoid of māyā, it has various types of māyā (mercy). It is the residence of Lakṣmī, who is worshipped by all people. The palace of Nanda, who gives joy to all, shines unsurpassed by the best of craftsmen.**

### Verse 8

*indra-garva-hara-parva-bhūṣitair yasya rāja-puruṣair adhiṣṭhitāḥ \|*  
*toraṇāś ca kanakādi-nirmitāḥ projjihāna-maṇi-toraṇāvabhūḥ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Its entrance arches, made of gold and other precious metals, guarded by royal servants whose many ornaments destroy the pride of Indra, defeat arches made of gems.**

### Verse 9

*nalikāvali-vartmabhir jalaughaiḥ kaṭaka-sthāt sarasaḥ samutpatadbhiḥ \|*  
*sadaneṣu saniṣkuṭeṣu yasmin jala-yantrāṇy udagur vicitra-bhāni \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In that city, water dispensers of various shapes extend to all houses and cottages by using pipes, to bring flowing water from lakes in the hills.**

### Verse 10

*vaidūrya-vajrādi-vinirmitāni sphurat-patākāny aniśotsavāni \|*  
*sadmāni padma-mahilasya viṣṇor babhuḥ prabhūta-dyutimantir yasmin \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At Nandīśvara the sparkling temples of Viṣṇu, wife of Lakṣmī, has fluttering flags made of vaidūrya and diamonds, indicating a continuous festival.**

### Verse 11

*sthiracayo bṛhad-valayocchritaḥ kapi-śiraś cayair ati-mañjulaḥ \|*  
*giri-sarāmbubhṛt parikhāñcito yad amito lasad-varaṇo varaḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**A high firm wall whose upper portions are attractive with many mounds of earth surround the city. It is surrounded by a moat containing water from the Malaya Mountains and has numerous shining, attractive bridges.**

### Verse 12

*bandhana-kraśima-kardama-śabdāḥ keśa-madhya-mṛganābhiṣu yasmin \|*  
*cāmarādiṣu ca daṇḍa-ninādaḥ sormitā-rata-sarita-sarit-sarasīṣu \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In that city the word “tying” means tying the hair, “thin” means slender-waisted, “Mud” means musk ointment. “Stick” (for punishment) refers only to the handle of a cāmara.**

**“Crying out” refers only to the sound of waves in the moving river or lake.**

### Verse 13

*tīkṣṇatā-kaṭhinate yuvatīnāṁ varṇita-kila-kaṭākṣa-kuceṣu \|*  
*chidritā-kuṭilate kramatas te mauktikeṣu ca kaceṣu yatra \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sharpness refers only to the glances of young women and hardness refers to young women’s breasts. “Having holes” refers to pearls and crookedness refers to curls in the hair.**

### Verse 14

*puraṁ bṛhat sānu-girer upānte hareḥ priyaṁ tādṛśam udbabhāse \|*  
*sarasvatī-juṣṭham adhi pravīraṁ yad adhyatiṣṭhad vṛṣabhānu-bhūpaḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Near to the huge plateau is a similar large city dear to the Lord, served by Sarasvatī and having many heroes. King Vṛṣabhānu lived there.**

## Chapter Five - Description of the birth of the Lord and his associates

### Verse 1

*prādurbhūto nandam evaṁ sa kṛṣṇaḥ śrīmān śaurīṁ cāviveśāmbujākṣaḥ \|*  
*tābhyāṁ nyastaṁ vaidha-dīkṣānvitābhyāṁ tat-patnyau samprāpya taṁ dadhratus te \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Manifesting himself in this world, lotus-eyed Kṛṣṇa entered Nanda and Vasudeva. Their two wives received him by their husbands placing him there through authorized dīkṣa.**

### Verse 2

*sakhyos tayor deva-garbhatva-yogād vidyun-nibhā kāya-kāntir babhāse \|*  
*saṅgha-satāṁ modayantī samantād vṛndaṁ dviṣāṁ tāpayantī samāsīt \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because the Lord had appeared in the two friends, their bodies shone like lightning. They gave joy to the devotees everywhere and gave suffering to the demons.**

### Verse 3

*prādurbhāvaṁ bhajamāne mukunde vāditrāṇi svayam eva praṇeduḥ \|*  
*samphullābhūd vanarājī-samantāt sārdhaṁ cittair dvija-bhakta-vrajānām \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the Lord appeared, instruments played by themselves. The forests blossomed with flowers everywhere while the devotees and brāhmaṇas hearts became filled with joy.**

### Verse 4

*namasya-māsi pādmabhe’sitāṣṭamī-niśārdhake vrajeśvarī sa-durgakaṁ hariṁ sukhād ajījanat \|*  
*asūta devakī ca taṁ tadaiva kevalaṁ mudā babhūva moda-sañcayaḥ satāṁ viśuddha-cetasām \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the eighth tithi of the waning moon, during Bhādra month, Rohinī constellation, at midnight, Yaśoḍā gave birth to the Lord and Durgā. Devakī gave birth to Kṛṣṇa alone in bliss. The pure hearts of the devotees rejoiced.**

### Verse 5

*dṛṣṭvā putraṁ vasudevaḥ pareśaṁ hṛṣṭaḥ prādād ayutaṅgāḥ hṛdaiva \|*  
*kaṁsād bhīto vraja-rājasya gehaṁ ninye bhrātus tvaritaṁ taṁ pravīram \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing his son as the supreme Lord, joyful Vasudeva gave unlimited cows mentally in charity. In fear of Kaṁsa he immediately brought his heroic son to the house of his brother Nanda.**

### Verse 6

*hitvā tasminn ātma-putraṁ yaśodā kanyāṁ nītvā so’bhyadāt kaṁsarāje \|*  
*aikyaṁ bimbīr arbhayor vā tadābhūd ekānaṁśācintya-śaktir yato’sau \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Leaving his son at Nanda’s house he took Yaśodā’s daughter and gave her to Kaṁsa. The two forms of Kṛṣṇa became one through the inconceivable power of Ekānaṁśā.**

### Verse 7

*sūtaṁ vadan parijana-vaktrato hariṁ pariplutaḥ parihita-veśa-bhūṣaṇaḥ \|*  
*acīkaran nija-tanayasya jātakaṁ dvijottamaiḥ śruta-vidhinā vrajādhipaḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing from his associates that his son was the Lord, Nanda dressed himself with fine ornaments and had his son’s birth chart made by the best brāhmaṇas according to scriptural rules.**

### Verse 8

*putrotsave sampradadau sanando harṣārdito bhūpatir atyudāraḥ \|*  
*svalaṅkṛtā vatsa-yutāś ca dhenūḥ śraddhānvito dve niyute dvijebhyaḥ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The most generous, joyful king, overcome with happiness on the occasion of his son’s birth, faithfully gave two million decorated cows with their calves to the brāhmaṇas.**

### Verse 9

*sapta-prāsād brāhmaṇebhyas tilādrīn raukmaiś cailai ratna-vṛndaiś ca juṣṭān \|*  
*jātaḥ sarvais tatra citro vraje’sau gāvaḥ sarvā maṇḍitāṅgā babhūvuḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He gave seven mountains of sesame along with gold cloth and gems to the brāhmaṇas. Everyone in Vraja was amazed by this. The cows were all decorated.**

### Verse 10

*somāṅgalyaṁ bhūsurās tatra peṭhuḥ sūtās tadvan māgadhā vandinaś ca \|*  
*vāditrāṇi sphotam āśu praṇedur gītiṁ nṛtyaṁ cāticitraṁ didīpe \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The brāhmaṇas recited praises on this occasion. Sūtas, māgadhas and vandis offered praise. Instruments suddenly burst into music. The songs and dancing were remarkable.**

### Verse 11

*sutam amita-guṇaṁ niśamya gopā vraja-nṛpater muditāḥ suramya-veśāḥ \|*  
*dhṛta-maṇimaya-bhūṣaṇāḥ suyatnāḥ sadanam atha bali-pāṇayaḥ samīyuḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing of the unlimited qualities of Nanda’s son, the cowherds of Nanda, joyful, dressed attractively and carefully ornamented with jewels, went to his house with offerings.**

### Verse 12

*vraja-pura-vanitā vicitra-veśā vara-maṇi-kuṇḍala-nūpuroruhārāḥ \|*  
*tam upāyayur upāyanāgra-hastā nṛpa-nilayaṁ harim īkṣituṁ praharṣāt \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The women of Vraja, in colorful dress, wearing the best gems, earrings, ankle bells and necklaces, joyfully approached Nanda’s house with gifts in their hands to see the Lord.**

### Verse 13

*ghṛta-dadhi-rajanī-rasāt kiranto vraja-nilayā jaya-ghoṣa-bhūṣitās yāḥ \|*  
*vidhi-śiva-sanakādayaś ca tasmin parinanṛtur nṛpa-catvare’timattāḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The houses of Vraja were sprinkled with ghee, yogurt and turmeric, and resounded with shouts of “Jaya”! Brahmā, Śiva, the Kumāras and others in great joy danced in Nanda’s yard.**

### Verse 14

*vraja-patir atha bhūṣaṇair anarghyair vasana-cayair vara-saurabhaiś ca bandhūn \|*  
*parijana-sahitān api prapūrṇān mudita-manāḥ sakalān api samārcīt \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda, with joyful mind, worshiped all his friends along with all their associates by giving priceless ornaments, heaps of cloth and excellent perfumes.**

### Verse 15

*tanaya-janma-mahe nṛpatir babhau racita-kośa-kapāṭa-vimocanaḥ \|*  
*pratijagur nija-vāñchita-pūraṇaṁ pramada-sampluti-yācaka-sañcayaḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The king, on the occasion of his son’s birth, opened the doors to his treasury. Receiving all they requested, the people offered praise, saying that all their desires had been fulfilled.**

### Verse 16

*parimitam iva yad babhūva saukhyaṁ vraja-nagare vraja-bhūpa-tat-prajānām \|*  
*tad-aparimitatām avāpa sadyo yad avadhi tat paramo jagāma kṛṣṇaḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The happiness of Nanda and the citizens which seemed limited now became unlimited when the supreme Kṛṣna came there.**

### Verse 17

*śrī-rāma śrī-dāma-mukhyā babhur ye pūrva-paścād ujjvalādyāś ca ḍimbhāḥ \|*  
*jyotiṣmadbhir bhrājamāno vrajas tai ratna-vyūhai ratna-sānur yathābhūt \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Balarāma and Śrīdhāmā and others appeared previous to Kṛṣṇa and Ujjvala and younger boys appeared after him. Just as Meru shines with heaps of gems, Vraja shone with the shining boys.**

### Verse 18

*nandādīnāṁ tiṣṭhatāṁ goṣṭha-bhūmyāṁ govindādyair ātmajair lakṣmavadbhiḥ \|*  
*nānā-sampat-sevitānāṁ sameṣāṁ gehe gehe saukhya-puñjo jajṛmbhe \|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Nanda and the cowherds lived in Vraja with Kṛṣna and their sons, who were signs of good luck, and were served equally by various types of wealth, the happiness increased in every house.**

### Verse 19

*yāṁ nanda-sūnur manute pumarthaḥ pumartha-bhūto’pi paraḥ pareśaḥ \|*  
*rādhādi-rūpādi-guṇair agādhā babhūva sā dhāmani kīrtidāyāḥ \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā, most profound for the highest beauty and highest qualities, whom Kṛṣna, the supreme Lord, the goal of all beings, regarded as his supreme being, appeared in the house of Kīrtidā.**

### Verse 20

*janmotsavenaiva jagat sutṛptaṁ yasyāḥ sureśair api saṁstutena \|*  
*pādābja-lakṣmāṇi nirīkṣya nāryo rameva kanyeyam iti pratīyuḥ \|\|20\|*

**Перевод:**

**The universe celebrated her birth with a festival and the devatās celebrated with praises. The women, on seeing the signs on her feet, thought that this girl was Lakṣmī herself.**

### Verse 21

*yāṁ varṇayantaḥ kavayo’pi bibhyuś candrāravindādi ninindur uccaiḥ \|*  
*dhānyena yasyā natibhiś ca śaśvat pramodam uccair hṛdayeṣu bhejuḥ \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Poets, in describing her, became frightened, and loudly criticized the moon and the lotus. By meditating on her and offering respects to her, they experienced the greatest joy in their hearts.**

### Verse 22

*kaṭākṣa-pātād abhajanta yasyā vibhūtayaḥ sarva-vidyāḥ prakāśam \|*  
*guṇa-vrajān vaktum adhīśvaro’pi śaśāka no nanda-sutaḥ samastān \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**From her glance, all knowledge and all powers manifested. Even Kṛṣna, the supreme Lord, could not describe her wealth of qualities.**

### Verse 23

*sakhyas tu tasyāḥ sama-rūpa-śīla- guṇāḥ sva-sevāti-paṭutva-bhājaḥ \|*  
*prādur babhūvur vraja-rāja-dhānyāṁ tadaiva gopa-pravarālayeṣu \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her friends, who had the same beauty, conduct and qualities, and were skilful in serving her, appeared at that time in the city of Nanda, in the houses of the best cowherds.**

## Chapter Six - Description of the Pastimes in sequence

### Verse 1

*ambhoja-cakra-dara-jambu-yavārdha-candra- mīnāṅkuśa-dhvaja-pavi-pramukhān vrajeśau \|*  
*aṅkān sutasya karayoḥ padayoś ca vīkṣya so’yaṁ mahān iti parāṁ mudam āpatus tau \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The two parents, seeing the marks of lotus, wheel, conch, jambu, barley corn, crescent moon, fish, goad, flag and thunderbolt on their son’s hands and feet, thought, “He is a great personality” and were overjoyed.**

### Verse 2

*dhṛtvā kūṭaṁ kāla-kūṭaṁ ca papa yāsau dhātrī pūtanā hantum āgāt \|*  
*tasyai tuṣṭo veśa mātrāt sa dimbhaḥ prādād dhātrī-sthānakaṁ śuddhi-pūrvam \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sinful Pūtanā, having immense poison on her breasts, came to kill the child, but the child, satisfied because she was dressed like a mother, purified her and gave her the position of a nurse in the spiritual world.**

### Verse 3

*kapaṭāvṛtaṁ śakaṭāsuraṁ harir añjasā tam akhaṇḍam \|*  
*marutaṁ ca taṁ balinaṁ vibhur vana-vāsināṁ sukhadaḥ śiśuḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord easily defeated the cart demon who had deviously disguised himself. The baby who was the Lord killed the strong wind demon and gave happiness to the inhabitants of the forest.**

### Verse 4

*yadā yadā mātur aṅke niviṣṭaḥ sacāpalaṁ divya-ḍimbho vyatānīt \|*  
*tadā tadā mātṛ-vargā nyamāṅkṣur vrajaukasaś cākhila-saukhya-sindhau \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the small child, sitting on his mother’s lap, displayed fickleness, all the women and people of Vraja drowned in an ocean of complete happiness.**

### Verse 5

*gargācāryād ātma-nāmāni bheje gūḍhaṁ bhāvaṁ vyañjayan pūtanāriḥ \|*  
*tene’nvarthaṁ corikā-narma-devo gopālibhir varṇyamānaṁ mukundaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**From Garga, the enemy of Pūtanā received his names, which revealed his hidden nature. Mukunda, who stole and joked with the elder gopīs, made the meanings described in his names successful.**

### Verse 6

*yadā śiśur dhūli-kelau rato’bhūt mahā-manā sa tadā kāmukebhyaḥ \|*  
*dadau mahān dhūli-muṣṭi-cchalena prabhur varān amṛtān tān munibhyaḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the child was absorbed in playing in the dust, the great Lord generously gave the sages who desired to see him the greatest nectar on the pretext of giving them a fistful of dust.**

### Verse 7

*janakam upāgataḥ sadasi nanda-nṛpaṁ capalo dhṛta-vara-bhūṣaṇo madhura-bhāṣaṇo moda-karaḥ \|*  
*alika-lasan-masī-kalita-candra-kalaḥ kutukī harir akhilān vyadhād aticiraṁ viramat-karaṇān \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The fickle child, wearing charming ornaments and speaking sweet words, came to his father Nanda in the assembly, giving everyone great joy. The inquisitive Lord, with gleaming black tilaka on his forehead, made everyone forget their duties for a long time.**

### Verse 8

*kiṅkiṇī-valaya-nūpura-dhārī niṣka-kuṇḍala-varāṅgada-hārī \|*  
*pīta-cīna-vasanaḥ sa-ḍimbhaḥ śiñjitair api manāṁsi jahāra \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Wearing bells, belt, anklets, medallion, earrings, excellent bracelets and necklace, and fine yellow cloth, he attracted the minds of everyone by the jingling of his bells.**

### Verse 9

*ratha-śibikāñcito hari-rabhasād uṭajeṣu yadā paricarituṁ munīn sva-niratān jananī-sahitaḥ \|*  
*dhṛta-dadhi-modakādi-balikaḥ sa-balaś ca vibhuḥ pramudam agus tadā subahu te vibudhāś ca parām \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the Lord as a child went with his mother and Balarāma by chariot or palanquin to the cottages in order to serve the sages who were his devotees, holding in his beautiful hands yogurt and sweets, the sages and the devatās became overjoyed.**

### Verse 10

*bala-kṛṣṇayoḥ sa jagdhau mudād amīyāṁ samādaduḥ phelām \|*  
*velāṁ pratītya devāś citraṁ śakuntāḥ sureśvarāḥ nityam \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**How astonishing! Understanding when Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma would be eating, the devatās, disguised as birds, would always joyfully take their unlimited remnants at that eating place.**

### Verse 11

*muṣṇan gavyaṁ gopikānāṁ sa-mitraḥ puṣṇan kīśān muktavat yaś ca kṛṣṇaḥ nopālabdho’py uktayāpi sa dhātryā prītiṁ nītā sābhyanandot sutena \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With his friends he stole the gopīs’ milk products and fed the monkeys liberally. When they told Yaśodā, she did not scold him. Developing more love for him, she was pleased with her son.**

### Verse 12

*mṛtsāprāśī jñāpitaḥ svāgrajena krodhān mātrā bhartsitaḥ pūtanāriḥ \|*  
*bhītaḥ svāsye viśvam etat pradarśya krodhaṁ tasyāḥ śraṁsayann abhyanandīt \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Balarāma informed Yaśodā that Kṛṣṇa had eaten dirt, she scolded Kṛṣṇa in anger. Afraid, he showed her the universe in his mouth. Her anger subsided and she became happy.**

### Verse 13

*vilokyāparādhaṁ jananyā nibaddho vibhutvaṁ svakīyaṁ mudā darśayat tām \|*  
*vibhajyārjunau tau ca muktau cakāra svayaṁ baddha-mūrtir batāsau mukundaḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing his offenses, Yaśoḍā bound him and he joyfully showed her his powers. He broke the two arjuna trees and liberated them, while he, Mukunda, giver of liberation, remained bound to the mortar.**

### Verse 14

*vṛndāṭavīm adhivasan harir ambujākṣaḥ sañcārayan sakhi-kulaiḥ saha tarṇakaughān \|*  
*vatsāsuraṁ bakam aghaṁ ca jaghāna sadyaḥ śuddhaṁ vyadhāt kamalajaṁ ca sa jagdhi-mugdhaḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The lotus-eyed Lord, staying in Vṛndāvana, herded the calves with his friends. He quickly killed Vatsāsura, Baka and Agha. Absorbed in eating his lunch he purified Lord Brahmā.**

### Verse 15

*kāliyaṁ bata vimardya sanāgaḥ sūrajām racitavān pariśuddhām \|*  
*nirvivāra khalu gokula-bhājāṁ bhāvam adbhutam udāram udīkṣya \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The most excellent Kṛṣṇa subdued Kāilya and purified the Yamunā. Seeing the people of Gokula becoming astonished, he prevented Kāliya from staying there.**

### Verse 16

*dīvyan dvandvī-bhāvato’han pralambaṁ devārātiṁ dhenuka-dveṣiṇā yaḥ \|*  
*muñjāṭavyāṁ dāva-vahniṁ nipīya vyaktīcakre sādhu-sauhārdam īśaḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**While playing, he had Balarāma, enemy of Dhenuka, kill Pralamba, enemy of the devatās, by playing in pairs. Drinking the forest fire in the forest of sedge grass, the Lord revealed his friendship with the devotees.**

### Verse 17

*gopa-kumārī-vasana-nikāyaṁ skandhe nidadhau sa khalu vimātham \|*  
*vīkṣita-sakala-kalevara-śobhaḥ sūcita-śuddha-janā-mitra-lobhaḥ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Gazing upon the beauty of their bodies, revealing his desire for friendship with those pure women, snatching their cloth, he placed all the gopīs’ clothing on his shoulder.**

### Verse 18

*stotrayatsu na ca yasya kaṭākṣaḥ sanateṣv api bhaved vibudheṣu \|*  
*saṁstuvan vraja-bhuvas taru-saṅghān sasvaje’timuditaḥ sa bhujābhyām \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though he did not even glance at the devatās who praised him with reverence, he blissfully embraced the trees of Vraja with his arms, while praising them.**

### Verse 19

*bhuktvānnāni brāhmaṇīnāṁ mukundaḥ prādāt tābhyaḥ svāṅghri-lābhaṁ varaṁ saḥ \|*  
*saṁskārādyān helayan nātma-bhakteḥ śraddhām eva khyāpayām āsa hetum \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After eating the food offered by the brāhmaṇas’ wives, he blessed them to attain his lotus feet. Rejecting the purification rites of sacrifice, he showed that the cause of devotion to him was faith alone.**

### Verse 20

*kaiśore vayasi harir dharaṁ sādadhre garviṣṭaṁ tridaśa-patiṁ jigāya śakram \|*  
*uddrāvaṁ vraja-vanitā-manāṁsi yasmāt samprāpur madana-kulān ivāgni-puñjāt \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In his kaiśora age, the Lord lifted Govardhana and defeated proud Indra, lord of Svarga.**

**Because of him, the minds of the gopīs became increasingly passionate, like a host of flames.**

### Verse 21

*gāndharvī vidhir abhavad vrajāṅganānāṁ dāmpatyai vraja-vidhunā sakhākhilānām \|*  
*gīr-vāṇyaḥ kusuma-kiro jagur vicitraṁ nṛtyantyo dhvanita-mṛdaṅgikāḥ praharṣāt \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, the moon of Vraja, married all the gopīs of Vraja according to gāndharva rites.**

### Verse 22

*vidhiṁ stāvakaṁ bhāvakaṁ candra-cūḍaṁ tato nirjarān kiṅkarān indra-mukhyān \|*  
*harer nanda-sūnor amayanta gopās tṛṇebhyo’surān kaṁsa-pakṣāśritāṁs te \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Brahmā’ praises, Śiva’s devotion, and the devatās headed by Indra serving Kṛṣṇa, the cowherds understood that the allies of Kaṁsa were as insignificant as grass.**

### Verse 23

*śrī-kāntaṁ praṇataika-bandhum atasī-puṣpa-prabhaṁ cid-ghanaṁ candrāsyaṁ kamalekṣaṇaṁ malayajāliptaṁ lasad-bhūṣaṇam \|*  
*citroṣṇīṣam udāra-gaura-vasanaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ surendrārcitaṁ vīkṣya svānuga-mud yayuḥ paramikāṁ prītiṁ vrajasthā bhṛśam \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Kṛṣṇa, the beloved of Lakṣmī, the friend of all beings, blue as a flax flower, filled with knowledge, with moon-like face and lotus eyes, anointed with sandalwood and glittering with ornaments, with colorful turban and bright yellow cloth, worshipped by Indra, the people of Vraja felt great affection and joy for their companion.**

### Verse 24

*atha vrajapatir udīkṣya sad-guṇair varaṁ hariṁ vinayinam ātmajaṁ mudā \|*  
*śubha-kṣaṇe śubha-vidhinā vrajāvane rajīgamat kila yuva-rājatām asau \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing his humble but excellent son with good qualities, with auspicious ceremonies Nanda joyfully made him the prince of Vraja at an auspicious moment.**

### Verse 25

*balabhadraṁ ca cakāra bhaumikaṁ vraja-bhūmair hari-mantriṇaṁ ca tam \|*  
*sadanaṁ tasya sucāru nirmame sukha-sindhau nikhilān nimamajjayat \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He made Balarāma Kṛṣṇa’s minister and owner of the land of Vraja. He made him a beautiful house and made all the people drown in an ocean of happiness.**

### Verse 26

*ādideśa nija-śilpi-kumāraṁ buddhi-sāgaram apāra-balaṁ saḥ \|*  
*saudham adbhutatamaṁ racaya tvaṁ yena rajyati haris tava mitram \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He ordered a young craftsman, an ocean of intelligence with unlimited strength,**  
**“Build the most remarkable palace where your friend the Lord can live in splendor.”**

### Verse 27

*puru-kānti-valīka-jāla-ramyaṁ vara-vedī-gṛha-sandhi-lāñchitaṁ saḥ \|*  
*valitāśrayam ambu-yantra-rāji vraja-candrasya cakāra sadma sadyaḥ \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He immediately built Kṛṣṇa’s palace, with thresholds, altars, rooms, lattices, gleaming roofs and water clocks.**

### Verse 28

*maṇi-baddha-taṭaiḥ sphuṭat sarojaiḥ śuśubhe yad vimalāmbubhiḥ sarobhiḥ \|*  
*ali-guñjita-mañjubhiś caturbhiḥ sphuṭa-prakaraiḥ suniṣkuṭaiś ca \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The palace had four clear lakes filled with blossoming lotuses and jeweled banks, with pleasant gardens possessing blossoming flowers and humming bees.**

### Verse 29

*sa ca racayāñcakāra girisānuṣu bhūri-vidhān maṇi-nilayāṁs tathaiva sura-śilpi-mano-haraṇān \|*  
*sapadi sarvais tutoṣa rasikaḥ khalu tatra mudā saha manasā dadau sa-maṇi-bhūṣaṇa-cela-sañcayān \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the plateaus of the mountain, he built many types of jeweled palaces which attracted the mind of Viśvakarmā. Kṛṣṇa was immediately pleased with all this work and with joyful mind gave him piles of fine cloth and ornaments with gems.**

### Verse 30

*smita-vīkṣaṇa-viddha-cetasā vara-saundarya-sudhā-su-dhāmanī*  
*svajanaiḥ saha rādhikācyutaiḥ sphuratas teṣu sadaiva medurau \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā and Kṛṣna, the supreme abodes of the highest, sweetest beauty, soft because their hearts were pierced by smiling glances, always shine in these palaces along with their associates.**

### Verse 31

*vraja-nṛpatir jagāma sa yadā saha dāra-kumāra-pārṣador atha śibikā-hayaiḥ surucirair vṛṣabhānu-puraṁ nimantritaḥ \|*  
*sumaṇidharaḥ sa-turya-ninado vara-cāmara-sevito dyutim atulāṁ vilokya diviṣan nikaro’pi tadā visismiye \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Nanda, wearing fine jewels, along with his wife, son and attendants, on being invited, went to Vṛṣabhānu’s city using attractive palanquins and horses, accompanied by drums, and served by best cāmaras, the devatās, seeing the procession’s incomparable luster, became astonished.**

### Verse 32

*adhigamya bhānu-nṛpatir vrajeśvaraṁ bhavanaṁ nināya racitārcana-kramaḥ \|*  
*paribhojyataṁ bahu-vidhān rasān prabhuḥ saha-pārṣadaḥ pramudito babhūva saḥ \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When he arrived, Vṛṣabhānu, worshipping him, brought Nanda to the palace and fed him food of various tastes. Vṛṣabhānu and his followers became overjoyed.**

### Verse 33

*sakhi-vṛndair nikhilā samujjihāna- madhurāsecanakaṁ vilokya kṛṣṇam \|*  
*janatā tatra sukhāmbudhau nyamajjat puru-bhāvās tu viśeṣatas taruṇyaḥ \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Kṛṣṇa endowed with increasingly sweet beauty along with his friends, everyone drowned in an ocean of happiness. The young girls particularly developed feelings of great love.**

### Verse 34

*pibator api susmitāmṛtāni rati-tṛṣṇākulayor adhisnu-yunoḥ \|*  
*samudaid asitāmbuja-chadābhā taḍid-abhra-prabhayoḥ kaṭākṣa-vṛṣṭiḥ \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The couple, like lightning and a dark cloud, became anxious with intense desire for pleasure and drank each other’s sweet smiles. A shower of glances, beautiful as blue lotuses, arose.**

### Verse 35

*atho bhānu-bhūpo varair maṇḍanādyaiḥ samarcya vrajādhāra-varaṁ sānugaṁ saḥ \|*  
*anuvrajya taṁ sānugas tad-visṛṣṭaḥ svakaṁ kṛcchrato mañju bheje nikuñjam \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛṣabhānu worshiped Nanda and his followers with the best ornaments and then, with his attendants, following Nanda, bid him farewell with difficulty, and entered his attractive bower.**

### Verse 36

*tadā sāravindā jananyā sa-vṛndā samārādhi sā rādhikā bhūṣaṇādyaiḥ \|*  
*hareḥ prema-pātrī yadā rāja-putrī vraja-kṣema-dhātrī prayātu sahaicchat \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Rādhā, object of Kṛṣṇa’s love, giver of auspiciousness to Vraja, desired to go to Kṛṣṇa, holding a lotus in her hand, she, with her friends, was decorated by her mother with ornaments.**

### Verse 37

*śivikāś ca rathāś ca rukma-celaiḥ pihitā jālibhir abhrakācakaiś ca \|*  
*tad upāyayur ujjvalair lalāmair bahu-bhāso nṛpa-catvaraṁ tadānīm \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The palanquins and chariots, shining brightly with flashing ornaments, covered with gold cloth, endowed glass and mica windows, quickly arrived in the courtyard.**

### Verse 38

*balair uddhatānām kiśorī-vṛtānāṁ lasad-yauvanānāṁ raṇad-bhūṣaṇānām \|*  
*tadā gujjarīṇāṁ tatir vāgminīnāṁ mudāyāna saṁvāhanārthādhyatiṣṭhat \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**A joyful group of chattering Gujarati women, proud of their strength, gleaming with youth, decorated with jingling ornaments, arrived to mount the palanquins.**

### Verse 39

*samārūḍhayānā balad-bhūrigānāḥ śanair vījyamānā varaiś cāmarādyaiḥ \|*  
*priyā nanda-sūnoḥ pareśasya-vadhvas tato niryayuḥ subhruvo rādhikādyāḥ \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**While loudly singing many songs and being fanned gently by the best cāmaras, beautiful Rādhā and her friends, dear beloveds of Kṛṣṇa, mounted the palanquins and departed.**

### Verse 40

*babhau kāmbavo bhairikaṁ sauṣirāpi dhvanir maṅgalo rāja-putryāḥ prayāṇe \|*  
*lasat-svarṇa-vetrāsi cāpeṣu hastā dadhāvuḥ puraḥ pārśvato’pi pravīrāḥ \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Auspicious sounds of conches, kettle drums and horns manifested at the departure of Rādhā. Soldiers holding shining golden maces, arrows and bows ran at the sides and in front.**

### Verse 41

*vavau manda-mandaṁ tadā gandhavāho dadhārātapatraṁ mahad-vārido’pi \|*  
*vitenur varaṁ nṛtya-gītaṁ ca devyo mṛdaṅgādi-nādaṁ nutiṁ cāticitram \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**A fragrant breeze gently blew. A huge cloud served as an umbrella. The heavenly women sang and danced expertly, played drums and offered astonishing praise.**

### Verse 42

*phaṇi-phakkikām iva vīkṣya tāṁ sa-kuṇḍala-nāma-purīm \|*  
*dyu-latām ivākhiladāṁ nutāṁ pramadā hareḥ pramudaṁ daduḥ \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Nanda’s city surrounded by moats, the Lord’s women, like fresh creepers from heaven which bestow all desires, became happy.**

### Verse 43

*avatīryatā maṇi-yānataḥ paritoṣya sārthika-sañcayān \|*  
*praṇipatya gokula-bhūmipāṁ jagṛhus tato vara-vīṭikāḥ \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Descending from the jeweled palanquins, they satisfied all the persons accompanying them. They offered respects to Yaśodā and accepted from her the best betel nuts.**

### Verse 44

*atha śiñjitāmṛta-vandita- priya-mānasāḥ sva-gṛhān gatāḥ \|*  
*kṛta-majjanāḥ kamalekṣaṇāḥ priya-karma tat pratipedire \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The lotus-eyed women, whose jingling ornaments were praised by their husbands, returned their homes, bathed and performed services for their husbands.**

### Verse 45

*sampālayan naicikīnāṁ kadambaṁ tam pākimaṁ bhāvam eṇī-dṛśāṁ saḥ \|*  
*kampākulaḥ sandadhe dīpta-kīrtir lampaṭa-hṛt sundaro nanda-sūnuḥ \|\|45\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**While herding the cows, beautiful Kṛṣṇa, with passionate heart and shining fame, trembling with longing, developed intense love for doe-eyed Rādhā.**

### Verse 46

*tātam ambu-patitāpanītaṁ vandito viracītārcana īśaḥ \|*  
*ānināya bhavanaṁ puru-tejā modayan vraja-bhuvaṁ babhāṣe \|\|46\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Worshipped and praised by Varuṇa, powerful Kṛṣṇa, bringing back Nanda who had fallen in the water and had been taken by Varuṇa, gave bliss to the people of Vraja.**

### Verse 47

*vṛndāraṇya-candikā-vṛnda-ramyaṁ paśyan vaṁśī vadāyām āsa kṛṣṇaḥ \|*  
*āyātābhis tatra gopāṅganābhir dīvyantībhir maṇḍito’sau babhūva \|\|47\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing the beauty of the moon beams in the Vṛndāvana forest, Kṛṣṇa played his flute. He became decorated with the brilliant gopīs who had come there.**

### Verse 48

*mādhavyas tā mañju-taurya-trikādyair mañju-rūpyaiś ca kṛṣṇām \|*  
*premnānarcuḥ sārthikā saucakāśe anantānandākhyāyinī vāk tadaiva \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They worshipped Kṛṣna with their sweet forms, sweet music, singing and dancing. This confirmed the statement that the Lord is infinite bliss.**

### Verse 49

*vīṇā-veṇu-mṛdaṅga-nūpura-lasat-kāñcyādi-nādair abhūt tātāthai tatathaiś ca tāla-militair nṛtyaiś ca gītaiś ca yat \|*  
*citraiḥ pāṇi-vidhūnanais tanu-maṇi-dyotanaiś ca rāsāṅgane tad vaktuṁ pramavate kathaṁ sukham aho vāg-devatāpi svayam \|\|49\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**How can Sarasvatī herself attempt to describe with ease what happened in the arena of the rāsa dance, with the sounds of flutes, vīṇās, drums, angle bells, and quivering belts, with the dancing and singing, mixed with the rhythms and calls of “tātāthai tatathai”, hand movements and shining ornaments?**

### Verse 50

*kuṇḍalitvam anayat sudarśanaṁ kuṇḍalitvam apahāyan vibhuḥ \|*  
*śaṅkhacūḍam api taṁ svam antakaṁ prāpayann udaharat syamantakam \|\|50\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord rescued Sudarśana from a snake’s body and restored him to his normal self with earrings. He killed Śaṅkhacūḍa and took his Syamantaka jewel.**

### Verse 51

*vraja-vanitā vanānta-nirataṁ harim ambuda-sodaraṁ yadā viraha-dhutāḥ purāṇa-puruṣaṁ jagur ambuja-locanāś ciram \|*  
*bhuvana-talaṁ tadedam akhilaṁ sarid-uṣṇa-sukhāmbu-saṅkulā duradhigamā samādhi-nilayair api haṁsa-kulaiḥ sabhādade \|\|51\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the lotus-eyed girls of Vraja, agitated by separation, praised the eternal Lord who was occupied in the forest and who has brother to the clouds, profuse, unfathomable rivers of hot and cold tears covered the whole earth--and even the detached sages rapt in meditation.**

### Verse 52

*vraja-vipine vicitra-vihago hari-veṇu-ravo yadā babhau vidhi-śiva-śakra-tumburu-mukhā vibudho’pi dadhur vicitratām \|*  
*prakṛti-viparyayaṁ tu sarito girayaś ca yayur mithas tadā vraja-mahilās tu bhejur akhilāś calatā sarasīṣu majjanam \|\|52\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the sound of Kṛṣṇa’s flute arose like a colorful bird in the forest of Vraja, Brahmā, Śiva, Indra, Tumburu, and all devatās became astonished. Rivers and mountains exchanged their natures. All the women of Vraja in agitation submerged themselves in the lakes.**

### Verse 53

*jāto’riṣṭaḥ kaṣṭakāsāra-vāsī yasmāt keśī mṛtyu-veśī babhūva \|*  
*vyomaḥ prāpa vyomatām eva sadyaḥ so’yaṁ kṛṣṇo deva-vṛndair vavande \|\|53\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa was praised by all the devatās because Ariṣṭa (who could not be harmed) was put into a shower of difficulty, Keśī (with a long mane) was made to wear death as his clothing, and Vyoma (air demon) was immediately merged with the air.**

### Verse 54

*harir atha mathurāṁ gataḥ sa kaṁsaṁ praṇihatavān vṛjinaṁ jahāra pitroḥ \|*  
*yadu-nṛpam akṛtāhukiṁ pareśaḥ sapadi kuśasthalikām adhiṣṭhito’bhūt \|\|54\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Then the Lord went to Mathurā, killed Kaṁsa and relieved his parents of their difficulties. He immediately made Ugrasena the king of the Yadus and established his home at Dvārakā.**

### Verse 55

*kuru-pati-tanayān nihatya duṣṭān vyadhita patiṁ nikhilasya dharma-putram \|*  
*kṣata-khala-nicayo viveśa goṣṭhaṁ saphalam idaṁ kṛtavān asau tu mābhyām \|\|55\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Killing wicked Dhṛtarastra, he made Yudhiṣṭhira the king of all people. After killing many miscreants he entered Vraja and satisfied everyone for two months.**

## Chapter Seven - Description of the Lord’s Later Pastimes

### Verse 1

*śīghra-gaiḥ prati nivedite harau dundubhiḥ kila jagarja susvanam \|*  
*maṅgala-dhvanir abhūd gṛhe gṛhe kānanāni dadhire madhu-srutim \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When it was announced by quick messengers that the Lord was returning, drums thundered loudly, auspicious sounds arose in all houses and the forests flowed with honey.**

### Verse 2

*udite vidhau pramude dadhe \| vraja-bhūr asau jaladhir yathā \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the ocean rejoices when the moon rises, the land of Vraja rejoiced when Kṛṣna entered.**

### Verse 3

*samupāgate bata mādhave \| aṭavīva sāgamad etatām \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the forest rejoices when spring comes, Vraja rejoiced when Kṛṣṇa arrived.**

### Verse 4

*pariṣasvajire hariṁ mudā nija-bhāva-nikhilā vrajaukasāḥ \|*  
*sravad-asra-parīta-vakṣaso vara-nīpa-stavaka-prabhojjvalāḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All the people of Vraja embraced the Lord according to their particular mood of love, while tears flowed over their chests and they shone with the effulgence of excellent kadambas (with hairs standing on end).**

### Verse 5

*tatrāgatās te munayo vanasthā draṣṭuṁ hariṁ saṁyamino vanasthāḥ \|*  
*sampūjitās tena dhṛtātma-bhāvās taṁ tuṣṭhuvuḥ saṁsphurad ātma-bhāvāḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sages who lived in the forest and householders with controlled minds came to see the Lord. He worshipped these persons who meditated on him. They praised him with full realization.**

### Verse 6

*sarveśvaras tvaṁ para-muktidas tvaṁ svātma-pradas tvaṁ svajanānurāgī \|*  
*tvam eva vijñāna-sukhātma-mūrtiḥ śrī-vatsa-lakṣmī-nilayas tvam eva \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“You are the lord of all beings. You are the giver of the highest liberation. You give yourself. You are attracted to your devotees. You are knowledge and bliss personified. You are the abode of Śrīvatsa and Lakṣmī.**

### Verse 7

*vibhrājitaḥ kaustubha-kānti-vṛndair jagaj-jani-sthema-layaika-hetuḥ \|*  
*acintya-śaktiḥ puruṣādi-rūpo vidhyādayo deva tavaiva bhṛtyāḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“You are illuminated by many rays from the Kaustubha gem. You are the cause of creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe. You are endowed with inconceivable energy. You are the puruṣāvataras. Brahmā and the devatās are your servants.**

### Verse 8

*govinda nandātmaja-kaṁsa-vaṁśa- nisūdanaḥ śrīdharaḥ naḥ puṇīhi \|*  
*śrī-gokulādhīśa jaya tvam uccair iha svakaiḥ sārdham udāra-kīrtiḥ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“O Govinda, son of Nanda, destroyer of Kaṁsa’s dynasty! O abode of Lakṣmī! Please protect us. O lord of Gokula! May you who have great fame be victorious with your associates!**

### Verse 9

*tava bhaktir acyuta karoti parāṁ mudira-dyute mudam udāra-maṇe \|*  
*pratidehi tāṁ nava-vidhāṁ tad imāṁ vṛṇumo vayaṁ varam atona na param \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“O Acyuta with the complexion of a cloud! O jewel of generosity! Bhakti to you produces the highest bliss. Please bestow the nine types of bhakti. We do not ask for any other benediction. There is nothing superior to this.”**

### Verse 10

*śivikā-ratha-vāji-rājitair vipineṣu svajanair athāvṛtaḥ \|*  
*viharan rasabhojanair atho mumude’sau parayā śriyārcitaḥ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Surrounded by his associates, he played in the forests using many palanquins, chariots and horses. Worshipped by the supreme Lakṣmī, he was overjoyed by tasting rasa.**

### Verse 11

*sakhibhiḥ saha dhenu-sañcayān sva-samānair guṇa-rūpa-sampadā \|*  
*girirāja-vaneṣu pālayan vividhāḥ keli-kalās tatāna saḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With his friends who were similar in form, qualities and wealth, he herded the multitude of cows in the groves of Govardhana. He performed various pastimes there.**

### Verse 12

*vanitāḥ sa nitānta-sundarīr niśi vṛndā-vipine viśan darīḥ \|*  
*sukha-sīma-vilāsa-lālasaḥ prabhur ānanda-mayo’py arīramat \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Eager for pastimes that gave the highest bliss, the Lord, though full of bliss, entered caves in the Vṛndāvana forest at night and enjoyed the exquisitely beautiful women.**

### Verse 13

*etā viṣṇor nanda-putrasya nityā līlā-nityānanda-mūrteḥ pradiṣṭāḥ \|*  
*śraddhāvadbhiḥ kīrtyamānāḥ samantāt saṁsārāgni-prauḍham unmūlayati \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**These pastimes of Kṛṣna or Viṣṇu, who is the form of eternal bliss, which were taught and glorified by the faithful devotees, completely uproot the fire of saṁsāra.**

### Verse 14

*vidyā-bhūṣaṇa-praṇītaṁ hari-caritaṁ cit-sukhātmakaṁ hy etat \|*  
*parigītaṁ śuka-muninā sadasi sevyaṁ svarūpam iva \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The pastimes of the Lord presented by Vidyābhūṣaṇa, which are pure knowledge and bliss, first sung by Śukadeva in Parīkṣit’s assembly, should be served like the Lord himself.**

### Verse 15

*aiśvaryāparikīrtanād vraja-vidhoḥ kṛṣṇasya ye sādhavas tāpāgni-pratilīḍha-hṛt-sarasijā mlāyanti śuṣyat-tviṣaḥ \|*  
*teṣāṁ tāpa-vimardanāya viśadā śrī-sārvabhauma-prabhoḥ kāruṇyād uditeyam āśu bhavatād aiśvarya-kādambinī \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Thus, may the spotless Aiśvarya-kādambinī (Cloud bank of the Lord’s powers) quickly arise by the mercy of Sārvabhauma Prabhu.**

### Verse 16

*aiśvarye pūrveyam pūrva-parvā kādambinī nanda-sutāvalambā \|*  
*syād bhū-viyat-sindhu-śaśāṅka-śāke satāṁ priyā tac-caraṇāśritānām \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Aiśvarya-kādambinī, concerning unprecedented topics of Kṛṣṇa, appeared in 1701 śakābda. May it be dear to the devotees who have taken shelter of the Lord’s lotus feet.**
