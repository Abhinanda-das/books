# Anarpita-carīṁ-cirād Explanation \[BS\]

## Sanskrit

anarpita-cariṁ cirāt karuṇayāvatirṇaḥ kalau  
samparyitum unnatojjvala-rasām sva-bhakti-śriyam \|  
hariḥ puraṭa-sundara-dyuti-kadamba-sandīpitaḥ  
sadā hṛdaya-kandare sphuratu vaḥ śaci-nandanaḥ

oṁ namo gauracandrāya \| śrī-nityānandādvaita-candrāya namaḥ \|

atyantāgati-gati-dānāya, nija-prema-ratna-rakṣaṇa-yatnodgatatva-kārpaṇya-doṣa-dūrīkaraṇāya, tad-dāne tad-āsvādanam iti tattva-prakāśanāya, preyasī-mahābhāva-svarūpa-śrīrādhikā-tattva-jñāpanāya, tat-prema-mahima-tattva-tat-tad-āsvādya sva-madhurima-tat-tacceto-mātrānubhūta-sva-mādhurī-saṅkalana-saukhyāsvādāya ca aviśeṣa-viśeṣa-bhakta-varga-vāsanā-viṣaya-premāmbudhi-pīyūṣa-karaṇaka-tad-abhiṣecanāya, tad-dvārā jagad abhivyāpanāya śrī-śrī-rādhā-dāsya-mātra-labhyānanya-labhya-vraja-sumadhura-premaprakāśanāya, tasyāḥ sarva-varīyastva-prakāśanāya ca, prema-sāgarāvalambana-navadhābhajana-krama-rūpe śrīla-navadvīpe śrīman-nanda-nandanaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇa-candro rasika-śekharaḥ sva-preyasī-vara-bhāva-kānti-saṁvalitaḥ parama-karuṇā-mayo’vatīrṇa iti \|

candra-grahaṇa-samaye hare-kṛṣṇeti nāma saṅgrāhayann eva candra-grahaṇa-prakāraḥ \| yato harati svabhāva-kānti-vilāsaiḥ śrī-kṛṣṇasya cittam iha punar varṇa-dehādikaṁ hṛtvā sva-svabhāvaṁ taṁ karoti yā sā rādhā ity arthaḥ \| tathā karṣati guṇais tadīyānāṁ cittam iha punaḥ sarvāgragā-śrī-rādhāyā varṇa-bhāvādikam iti kṛṣṇa ity arthaḥ \| nāma-vyutpatti-sīmā tatraiva darśitaḥ \|

tatra dātu-svabhāvo’yaṁ miṣṭa-dravya-bhakṣaṇa-samaye anyebhyo na dattva sva-bandhubhyo na vibhajya svayam eva nāśnāti \| ataḥ āpāmarebhyo’pi nirupama-prema dattvā sarva-dhāma-sarvāvatāraka-parikarān ekatrīkṛtya prema-mahotsavaṁ karoti iti tatratyān etya viśeṣābhijñaḥ śrīmān gosvāmi prabhur nāṭakādau nāndīrūpeṇa jagad-āśīr-vyañjanayā nirūpayati anarpitam iti \|

śrī-śacīnandano harir vā yuṣmākaṁ hṛdaya-kandare sphuratu \| yathā siṁha-pratāpena karimado’pagacchati tathā asyodaye yuṣmākaṁ viṣayādi-mada-matta-kari-rūpaṁ manaḥ śāntaṁ bhāvīti dhvaniḥ \| sa yathā saṁghātako nāyaṁ tathā kintu śodhaka eva \| kiṁ kartum avatīrṇa ity ata āha—pūrvānarpitāṁ nija-bhakti-sampattiṁ tathā unnatojjvala-rasāṁ kalau sarva-sādhana-hīnebhyo’pi dīnebhyo dātuṁ tathā tad-arthaṁ puraṭa-sundara-dyutir iti śrī-rādhā-sāmyam eva \|

tatrāt’nanyatva-jñāpanāya ca tadīya-rūpa-bhāvāviṣkaraṇaṁ nigūḍha-premṇo bahiṣ-karaṇenāntar-nigūḍha-kanaka-rucer api bahiḥ prakāśaḥ svayam evāgataḥ \| prema-sāra-rūpatvāt śrī-rādhāyā iti pīta-rucittvam avagamyate, tan-nimagnatvāt śrī-nityānanda-prabhu-prabhṛtīnām api gauratvam eva \| tatra pramāṇāni tāval lakṣyate—

rasajñāḥ śrī-vṛndāvanam iti yam āhur bahu-vido yam etaṁ golokaṁ katipaya-janāḥ prāhur apare \| śvetadvīpaṁ prāhuḥ param api para-vyoma jagadur navadvīpaḥ so’yaṁ jayati paramāścarya-mahimā \|\|

kasyacit viśuddhādvaitaika-praṇaya-rasa-pīyūṣa-jaladhau ghanībhūte dvīpe samudayati vṛndāvanam aho \| mithaḥ premodghūrṇad-rasika-mithunākrīḍam aniśaṁ tad evādhyāsīnaḥ praviśati pade kvāpi madhure \|\|

ity atra dvīpākhyā-prakāśanāya śrī-navadvīpa iti nāma \| etena śrī-vṛndāvana-rūpa-prakāśa-prema-ghūrṇana-prakārādikam api adhunaiva prakāśitam iti jñeyam \|

yad vā, atyantāgatika-gati-dānāya nija-prema-ratna-rakṣaṇa-yatnodgata-sva-kārpaṇya-doṣāpavādāpavādāt tad-dāne tad-āsvādanam iti tattva-prakāśanāya tat-parīpākāv adhi-vraja-vadhū-śiromaṇi-śrī-vṛṣabhānunandinī-sva-mādhurī-dhurīṇa-mātrānantarāntargata-rasāsvādāya ca aśeṣa-viśeṣa-bhaktavarga-vāsanā-viṣaya-premāmṛtenābhiṣecanāya tad-dvārā jagad-abhivyāpanāya ca premasāgarāvalambana-navadhā-bhakti-rūpe śrī-navadvīpe śrī-vraja-rāja-kumāraḥ sva-preyasī-vara-svabhāva-kānti-sandīpitaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇa-candro’vatatāra \| yadā tāṁ candra-mukhīm ākalayyaiva candra iti tad-grahaṇa-samaye viśva-loka-mukhena śrī-kṛṣṇeti nāmāvalīṁ grāhayann eva vicakṣaṇa-śiromaṇir eṣā vaidagdhīti \| yato hare ity asya harati svabhāva-kāntibhyāṁ śrīkṛṣṇasya cittam iti punar atra varṇa-bhāvādikam iti vyutpattyā śrī-rādhe ity arthatvāt \| atas tad-anugata-rūpaḥ śrī-rūpo mat-prabhur janmādi-līlāṁ sa-prayojanikām ekena padyenaivāha—anarpita-carīm iti \| śrī-śacīnandano harir vo yuṣmākaṁ hṛdaya-kandare karuṇayā sphuratu ity anvayaḥ \|

nanu śrī-śacīnandanāvatāras tu daśāvatārādau na śrūyate \| kadā avatīrṇaḥ pūrṇo’ṁśo vā kathaṁ vā kena vā kiṁ varṇo vā līlā vā kety ata āha—kalau avatīrṇa iti \| kalau vaivasvatamanvantarīyāṣṭa-viṁśati-caturyugīya-catuḥ-sahasra-sapta-śata-varṣa-mita-kali-yugāṁśe caturdaśa-śata-sapta-saṅkhye śāke phālguṇī-paurṇamāsyāṁ pūrva-phalgunī-nakṣatre iti sarvathā paripūrṇa-rūpeṇa avatīrṇa iti svayam eva prāya-sva-prayojanāyāvatīrṇaḥ prakaṭo na tu brahmaṇā prārthito na vā daitya-vināśāya iti \| kṛṣṇa-varṇaṁ tviṣā-kṛṣṇam iti śrībhāgavatāt \|

paurṇamāsyāṁ phālgunasya phalgunī ṛca-yogataḥ \| bhaviṣye gaura-rūpeṇa śacī-garbhe purandarāt \|\| iti vāyavyāt \|

suvarṇa-varṇa-hemāṅgo varāṅga iti mahābhāratāt \|

niḥsvādhyāya-vaṣaṭ-kāre svadhā-svāhā-vivarjite \|

tataḥ prāvirasau vipraḥ kvacil loke bhaviṣyati \|\| iti viṣṇu-purāṇāt \|

hiraṇya-varṇaṁ brahma-yonim iti śruteś ca \|

pūrṇo’vatārīti nāvatāra-gaṇanāyāṁ gaṇitaḥ \| yadā śrī-kṛṣṇo’vatarati tadaivāyam avataratīti niyamāt \| sa evāyam iti na pṛthag upādānam \|

nanu, yadi śrī-nandanandano’yaṁ tasya tu śrī-vṛndāvana-līlāyāḥ paraṁ saukhyaṁ nāstīti tad eva māthura-vipralambhādikam udbhāvya gāḍhāveśenādhunāpi samṛddhimat-sambhogena mādana-bhāvenātmakāntayā tayā kāntimatyā śrī-vṛndāvane nija-rūpa-vikārair lalitādi-sakhīvargais tām eva līlāṁ karotīti kiṁ kartum atrāgatas tadātrāha—sva-bhakti-śriyaṁ samarpayituṁ sva-bhakti-sampadas tāvat trividhāḥ rūpa-svarṇādi-mudrātmikā indranīlādimaṇy-ādi-rūpāś cintāmaṇi-rūpāś ca \| sādhana-bhāva-prema-bhedeneti carama-kakṣā-prāptā yā eva svayam ātmārāmeṇa vinā tad-dānābhāvāt \|

tathā ca, durlabhālokayor yūnoḥ pāratantryād viyuktayoḥ \| upabhogātireko yaḥ kīrtyate sa samṛddhimān \|\| \[UN 15.206\]

iti nitānta-pāratantrya-gamanaṁ ca aikyaṁ vinā na sambhavatīti tasyā api anapekṣyatvāt nitāntātmārāmatvaṁ ca aikyaṁ vinā neti -- sarva-bhāvodgamollāsī mādano’yaṁ parāt paraḥ \[UN 14.219\], yoga eva bhaved eṣa vicitraḥ ko’pi mādanaḥ \[UN 14.225\] iti nitānta-yogo’pi naikyaṁ vineti \| atraivātmani tayā samṛddhimat-sambhogena mādanena ca ramate ata eva pātrāpātra-vicāraṇaṁ na kurute mahānanda-sīmā-sthaḥ premonmatto jagad api mādayatīti \| na cedam anucitam eva yat pātrāpātra-vicārābhāva iti vācyam \| viśva-jayānantaraṁ mahārājādhirāja-gamanāgamane tat parikarair ānandena sva-sva-hasta-dvayenāpi dhanāni pārśva-dvaye vikīryaiva gamyate iti vyavahārān na glānikaram idaṁ tasyāpi tu mahaiśvaryaprakāśakam iti asyāpi mahā-kṛpālutva-prakāśakam eva \| yan nityānandena tan mahā-dhanaprema-vitaraṇaṁ na punar haraṇam iti \|

nanv aho kim ucyate bhavatā prahlādāmbarīṣa-hanumad-vibhīṣaṇa-pāṇḍavādibhyaḥ premadānād asya tad-dānaṁ nādhunikaṁ yuge yuga eva dṛśyate ity ata āha anarpita-carīm iti \| pūrvam anarpitā tatra tatra tat-tad-bhakta-kṛpayā tad anu teṣāṁ niṣṭhayā tair balenaiva nītatvāt kiṁ tasya dātṛtvaṁ rājā balena mahā-kṛpaṇasya dhanāni netuṁ śaknoti tadā kiṁ tasya dātṛtvaṁ yadi śatru-mitrodāsīnebhyaḥ hitāhitānusandhānaṁ vinā jagāi-mādhāiprabhṛtivat sarvatraiva dātuṁ śakyate tadā dānaṁ śasyate, anyathā kiṁ teneti bhāvaḥ \|

nanu pūtanā loka-bāla-ghnīty ādau tathā darśanam api ata eva āha—unnatojjvala-rasam iti unnataḥ gopī-bhāvena paramotkarṣa-kakṣāṁ prāptaḥ \| ujjvalo madhuro raso yasyām iti sa tu gopanena sampuṭe sthāpito’sti—muktiṁ dadāti karhicit sma na bhakti-yogam iti nyāyenāsya rasasya tu tatrātigopyatvam iti bhāvaḥ \|

nanv aho kim ānandaḥ kim ānandaḥ tadāyaṁ kathaṁ pūrvaṁ nāvatarat ity ata āha—cirāt karuṇayā iti ayaṁ navadvīpa-nāthaḥ sadaivedam apekṣya sthitaḥ svacchā mahā-sādhakā bahavo bhavanti kasyāpi yad itthaṁ bhāgyaṁ jāyate tadā tad avalambyaiva jagatīdaṁ vitariṣyāmīti \| tatra kasyāpi īdṛśaṁ bhāgyaṁ na jātaṁ tadā karuṇā-prerita eva kalau ananya-gatika-dīna-hīna-janālocanena prādurbabhūveti cira-kālānantaram iti bhāvaḥ \|

nanv advaita-huṅkāraḥ kāraṇatvam asya kutaḥ ? karuṇayā \| tathā ca—advaita-prakaṭīkṛtaḥ ity ādi-pramāṇena gaṅgāyāṁ tulasī-patraṁ dattvā pāṣaṇḍa-jana-vṛddhi-vaiṣṇava-jana-santāpena bhagavat-pratyāsattim apaśyatā huṅkāraṁ kurvatādvaitācārya-gosvāminā parama-sadā-śivena bhuvi ānīto’yam iti \| tathāpi bhajanena paśyati, jñānena jānāti, tapasānubhavati premṇā vaśīkaroti \| kiṁ punar yaśodayā udūkhale baddha ity ādi sarvatraiva tat karuṇā mukhyaprayojikā gaṇyate \| yathā, kṛpayāsīt sva-bandhane ity ādi pramāṇena yathā ca bhagavataḥ sarvāgamyatvam uktvā paścāt, yeṣāṁ sa eva bhagavān dayayed anantaḥ ity ādinā dayā-mātraprāpyatvaṁ tasya nirdhāritam \| tathā ca, akāṇḍe prakāṇḍa-huṅkāreṇa pralaya-kartur mahārudrasyādvaitācāryasya lokā nikṣyantīti jhaṭity utthayā karuṇayaiva bhagavataḥ prādurbhūtatvam iti bhāvaḥ \|

nanu, bhoḥ ! śrī-kṛṣṇenāpi daṇḍakāraṇya-vāsi-muni-śruti-kanyā-gāyatrī-prabhṛtibhyo vraje gopa-gopa-kanyā-rūpiṇībhyo gokula-candreṇāpi ujjvala-rasa-pradānaṁ kṛtam iti śrūyate— kṛṣṇād anyaḥ ko vā latāsv api premado bhavati ity ādi premāṇenāyogyebhyo’pi tasya premadātṛtvaṁ ca \| satyaṁ bhoḥ sa eva vraja-rāja-kumāro’yaṁ tenāne bheda-vivakṣāyāṁ kiṁ prayojanaṁ kintu antaḥpure mahārājā kalpa-taru-rūpeṇa sarvasvam api dadātīty ukte’pi na dātṛtvaṁ yadi dīna-hīna-patitādibhyo’deyam api dīyate mahā-dātṛtvaṁ tadaiva api ca kṛpaṇa-pakṣe hy uttamaḥ-śloka-śabdaḥ \[BhP 10.47.15\] iti smaraṇāt \|

nanu, īdṛśa-prema-ratnānāṁ sthāpanāgārāni bhuvi kati prakāśitāni yebhyo nītvānyadāpi sukṛtinaḥ prema-dhanino bhavanti ? satyam \| aṣṭādaśa-sahasra-saṅkhyaka-ślokāḥ śrīmadbhāgavatīyāḥ santi, yac-chravaṇa-mātreṇāpi premā jāyate yasyāṁ vai śrūyamāṇāyāṁ \[BhP 1.7.7\] ity ādi vacanāt \| svayam eva tad-rūpatayā tayā dadātīti na citram \|

nanu, ko vādhyakṣaḥ ko vā koṣa-dvāra-rakṣako dvāri kapāṭādikaṁ kiṁ nāsti ? asti yo’dhyakṣaḥ śrī-nityānandaḥ sa tu hasta-dvayena dadāti tasmād api vicāra-rahitaḥ premakādambinīmattatvāt \| yas tu dvāra-rakṣakaḥ śrī-sanātanaḥ sa tu pūrva-sthitam api kavāṭakīlakādikaṁ vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇy-ādi rūpeṇonmucyāhvayati—

āgaccha bhrātar etasmin premāmbudhi-nimajjane \| yat saukhyam anubhūyeha tuccha-brahma-padādikam \|\|

iti jñātvaiva prabhunāpi tasmai tasmai tat tad-adhikāro dattaḥ \| nāpremado’pi premada ity etad-dharmasya projjhita-kaitavatvāt \| ata eva tri-kapaṭī varjitaḥ \| yathā dhana-kapaṭī balakapaṭī prema-kapaṭī ceti \| yas tu śruta-śrī-bhāgavata-dharmaḥ guru-kṛṣṇa-vaiṣṇava-sevane śrībhāgavata-prītyai tad-vyaya-parāṅmukhaḥ pratyuta-tad-viparīta-vyavahāra-vartmani sahasrādhikādi-vyaya-nirato’dhika-tal-lābhāya pūrvatra śatādi-dāne’pi bhītaḥ sa ādyaḥ \| yas tu satyām api śaktau kṛṣṇa-vaiṣṇava-karmaṇi jaḍaḥ śrī-caitanya-saṅkīrtana-tāṇḍavādy-alasaḥ sa dvitīyaḥ \| yas tu premṇi ajāte’pi utkaṇṭhā-rahitaḥ ahaṁ bhakta iti sābhimānaḥ sa tṛtīyaḥ \| eteṣāṁ kāpaṭya-tyāgena muhur muhur mahat-kṛpā-sāpekṣyaṁ prema \|

nanu, ayaṁ nandanandanas tadā sarva-śruti-purāṇa-prasiddha-śyāma-rūpam evāsya syād ata āha—puraṭa-dyuti-kadamba-sandīpitaḥ \| puraṭāj jāmbunadādi-suvarṇāt sundarī yā dyutis tatsamūhena kānti-puñjena sandīpitaḥ ācchādita-cikkaṇa-marakata-maṇi-kāntika iti bhāvaḥ \| ata eva hariḥ traijagan-mohanaḥ anarpita-carīṁ cirāt \| kṛṣṇa-rūpeṇa punaḥ kṛṣṇa-mohinī-rūpasaṁvalitatvāt \| tato’sya darśane cintane eva premodayaḥ na punar dānādānāpekṣā \| harati sarvaṁ premṇā iti bhāvaḥ \|

nanu, asya līlāṁ bhāvayāmi kṛpayā brūhīty ata āha—sphuratu vaḥ \| bho tvam adyāpi pradhāna-girir eva, yataḥ śrī-gaurāṅga-candra-līlāpi te na gocarā \| ataḥ saty api jñāne tava hṛdayaṁ śaila-kandaram eva, tatra kṛpayā prabhur me dīna-dayā-mayaḥ sphuratu sarvadā tadā tal-līlāpi prema-mayī sarvadā sphuriṣyatīti pratyakṣīkariṣyaty eva kiṁ kathanena \| kathane’pi te bila-tulya-karṇe kiṁ jñātuṁ śaknuta iti bhāvaḥ \|

nanu, mahāparādhī ahaṁ hā śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-līlāyām api na me ratir jātā kathaṁ sa mām anugrahīṣyati ity ata āha—śrī-śacī-nandanaḥ \| jagan-mātā śacī tasyā bālakeṣu sneho vartata eva kuputro jāyate kvacid api kumātā na bhavati iti nyāyena mātṛ-bhakto gaurāṅgas tvām anugrahīṣyaty eveti bhāvaḥ \|

yad vā, evaṁ mahānubhāva-mata-prasiddhir vartate saṁnyāsānantaram advaita-gṛhe śacīgaurāṅga-saṁvādaḥ \| yathā śacī āha—re viśvambhara ! viśva-rūpa-śokādi-jñātam \| madīyajanmāntarīya-pāpa-puñjaḥ sthita eva kālena phalitaḥ \| yadi bhavat-pitṛvan man-mṛtir adyāpi sahasā sambhavati tad aihika-śoka-santāpa-loka-dhik-kāra-lajjādibhyo muktā syām amutra yad bhavatu \| yadijīvanaṁ naśyet tadā mayā kiṁ viṣādi pātavyaṁ kiṁ suradhunī-nīramadhyam adhyāsanīyam anyathā viśvambhara-jananīti sarvaiḥ ślāghyā sthitāham atīva tiraskṛtā syām \| na ko’pi mayā saṁvadiṣyati na vā man-nāma grahīṣyati aho asyāḥ nāmagrahaṇena kara-gateṣṭam api naṅkṣyati atīva durbhāginīm enām asmāt purān niḥsāraya iti paraspara-pravada-śīlo navadvīpa-loko bhaviṣyati \| kim ataḥ kāryaṁ mameti brūhi viśvambharaḥ \| tataḥ śrī-gaurāṅgenoktam –

mātar mā vada mā vadedṛśam aho kaṣṭātikaṣṭaṁ vacas tathyaṁ te nigadāmi yāmi bhavanāt tiṣṭhāmi yady anyataḥ \| tvaṁ mātām mama sarva-deśa-vasatau devādi-janmasv api tvat-putro’ham ahaṁ sadā trijagatām arcyas tvam arcyā mama \|\| iti \|

sarva-sādhana-hīno’pi mahā-pāpa-sahasra-kṛt śacī-sūta iti proktaḥ sa-gaṇaṁ māṁ vaśaṁ nayet \|\| sa jñānī sarva-śāstra-jñaḥ sa premī mati-priyo’dhikaḥ \| śacī-nandana ity eva yena harṣeṇa gīyate \|\| māṁ krīṇāti jano dhanyaḥ parikrīṇāti mad-gaṇam \| śacī-nandana ity evaṁ yo’bhidhatte kathañcana \|\| tasya vidyā tasya vittaṁ sa tīrtha-sadṛśo bhavet \| śacī-nandana ity evaṁ yena harṣeṇa gītyate \|\| śacīṁ vā māṁ śacī-putraṁ navadvīpaṁ śacī-sthalam \| yo vadet so’vadac chāstraṁ veda-vedānta-saṅgraham \|\| janma janmani tat-prīto jāto’haṁ yo vadet kvacit \| śrī-śacīti śrī-śacīti śrī-śacīti sa me guruḥ \|\|

ity ataḥ śrī-śacī-nāma-grahaṇna mahāparādhino’pi śrī-gaurāṅgo’tipriyo bhavatīty uktaṁ śrī-śacī-nandana iti \| tathā ca śrī-śacī-nandana ity evaṁ kīrtaya tenaiva kīrtenāparādha-yukto’pi tasmān mukto bhaviṣyasīti \|

iti śrī-śrīpāda-jīva-gosvāmi-viracitā anarpita-carīm iti ślokasya vyākhyā

## Translation

*anarpita-cariṁ cirāt karuṇayāvatirṇaḥ kalau*  
*samparyitum unnatojjvala-rasām sva-bhakti-śriyam \|*  
*hariḥ puraṭa-sundara-dyuti-kadamba-sandīpitaḥ*  
*sadā hṛdaya-kandare sphuratu vaḥ śaci-nandanaḥ*

I offer respects to Gauracandra and to Nityānanda and Advaita.

The son of Nanda, Kṛṣṇa, the highest taster of rasa, endowed with the complexion and bhāva of his beloved, filled with the highest mercy, appeared in Navadvīpa--the form of the nine types of bhakti, which supports the ocean of prema--in order to award the most unattainable goal, in order to remove the fault of miserliness arising from carefully protecting his jewel of prema, in order to manifest the truth—the taste of prema after giving it, in order make known the nature of dear Rādhā, the very form of mahā-bhāva, in order to taste the greatness of her prema, in order to taste the happiness and complete sweetness on experiencing his sweetness in her heart, in order to sprinkle his unlimited devotees with the nectar of the ocean of prema, which would fulfill the desires of unlimited devotee, in order to spread this ocean of prema to the world, in order to reveal the sweet prema of Vraja, attained only by becoming the servant of Rādhā, and in order to reveal Rādhā’s supreme position.

(As hari, the moon) he made people chant Hare Kṛṣṇa at the time of the eclipse of the moon. Rādhā steals Kṛṣṇa’s heart by her gestures, complexion and nature. After taking away his complexion and body, she makes him adopt her own nature. That is Rādhā. Kṛṣṇa is he who attracts the hearts of the devotees by his qualities and attracts the bhāva and complexion of Rādhā. This is the etymology of the names.

The nature of the giver is that at the time of eating sweets, he does not eat, without giving to others, without dividing the sweets among his friends. After giving unlimited prema to the lowest rascals, he has a festival of prema, bringing together all the associates who descended with him, from all the spiritual abodes.

Approaching the audience, knowing the details, Rūpa Gosvāmī describes the Lord with indications of blessing to the world, as an invocation at the beginning of the drama.

May the son of Śacī, or the Lord, appear in the cavity of your hearts. As a mad elephant flees with the strong lion approaches, your minds, mad with intoxication of material enjoyment, will become peaceful. That is hinted. He does not destroy (like the lion) but purifies only.

Why did he appear? He appeared to give to the fallen souls, devoid of all sādhanas in Kali-yuga, the wealth of bhakti to Kṛṣṇa (*nija-bhakti-śriyam*), with madhurya-rasa (*unnatojjvala-rasām*), previously not given (*anarpita*).

To do that, he took on the golden complexion similar to Rādhā’s (*purata-sundara-dyutiḥ*).

In order to make known her exclusive position, he manifested her form and bhāva. The Lord personally came, manifesting externally her golden complexion which was deep within-- externalizing the deep prema. The golden complexion of Rādhā indicates that she is the essence of prema. Absorbed in that prema, Nityānanda and others also became golden.

The proofs are as follows.

*rasajñāḥ śrī-vṛndāvanam iti yam āhur bahu-vido*  
*yam etaṁ golokaṁ katipaya-janāḥ prāhur apare \|*  
*śvetadvīpaṁ prāhuḥ param api para-vyoma jagadur*  
*navadvīpaḥ so’yaṁ jayati paramāścarya-mahimā \|\|*

**What persons in knowledge call Vṛndāvana, and others in knowledge call Goloka, others call Śvetadvīpa or the supreme para-vyoma Navadvīpa, remains victorious with the most astonishing greatness. Gaura-gaṇodeśa-dīpikā**

*kasyacit viśuddhādvaitaika-praṇaya-rasa-pīyūṣa-jaladhau*  
*ghanībhūte dvīpe samudayati vṛndāvanam aho \|*  
*mithaḥ premodghūrṇad-rasika-mithunākrīḍam aniśaṁ*  
*tad evādhyāsīnaḥ praviśati pade kvāpi madhure \|\|*

**Vṛndāvana, endowed continually with the pastimes of the couple who are unsteady with prema, manifests on the spiritual island in an ocean of nectar, of pure affection. Situated in Navadvīpa, one enters into their sweetness.**

The verse is quoted to show the island named Navadvīpa. By this, it is understood that the prema manifested in Vṛndāvana is now manifested again.

Or the moon of Kṛṣṇa, glowing with the complexion and nature of Rādhā, the young son of Nanda, appeared in Navadvīpa, the form of nine types of bhakti which support the ocean of prema, in order to give the highest goal, in order to reveal the truth, tasting prema, by giving that goal, so that the fault of miserliness of hoarding the gem of his own prema would be removed, in order to taste the rasa of sweetness as experienced by Rādhā in its highest state, in order to sprinkle his unlimited devotees with the nectar of prema that they desired, and to spread this in the world.

His face is like the moon (hariḥ). At the time of the eclipse of the moon, the pinnacle of cleverness arranged that everyone chant Kṛṣṇa’s name, since he is hari: he takes on the form and nature of Rādhā, who steals Kṛṣṇa’s heart by hers nature and bhāva. My master Śrī Rūpa, who follows her, in one verse describes the pastime of Mahāprabhu’s birth along with his goal. May the son of Śacī, Hari, manifest by mercy in the core of your hearts.

“The avatāra of Mahāprabhu is not mentioned among the ten avatāras. When did he appear? Is he a full expansion? Why did he appear? What is his color and activity?” That is explained. He appeared in Kali-yuga (*kalau*) after four thousand and seven hundred years in the twenty-eighth cycle of Vaivasvata Manvantara, or 714 śaka, on the Phalguna full moon, during Pūrva-phalguṇi nakṣatra. He appeared in the most complete form. He appeared with his own goal, not because of Brahmā’s prayers or to destroy demons. Bhāgavatam describes him as Kṛṣṇa, but with golden complexion.

*paurṇamāsyāṁ phālgunasya phalgunī ṛkṣa-yogataḥ \|*  
*bhaviṣye gaura-rūpeṇa śacī-garbhe purandarāt*

**On the full moon of Phalguna month, with pūrva-phalgunī constellation, the Lord will be born in the womb of Śacī, with golden form, from the great brāhmaṇa (Jagannātha Miśra). Vāyu Purāṇa**

*suvarṇa-varṇa-hemāṅgo varāṅga*

**The Lord will have a golden body. Mahābhārata**

*niḥsvādhyāya-vaṣaṭ-kāre svadhā-svāhā-vivarjite \|*  
*tataḥ praviralo vipraḥ kvacil loke bhaviṣyati \|\|*

**Not studying the Vedas or offering sacrifice, the Lord will be sometimes born as a brāhmaṇa in this world. Viṣṇu Purāṇa**

*hiraṇya-varṇaṁ brahma-yonim*

**The lord will be born in a brāhmaṇa family, with a golden complexion. Śruti**

Since he is a complete avatārī, source of the avatāras, he is not counted among the avatāras. It is a rule that when Kṛṣṇa appears in this world, then Mahāprabhu appears. They are accepted as non-different.

“If Kṛṣṇa does not gain the highest happiness from his pastimes in Vṛndāvana, after having experienced separation when he goes to Mathurā, he then performs pastimes of union (*samṛddhimān*), manifesting mādana, with transformations of his own form as Lalitā and others in Vṛndāvana, and with Rādhā. Why would he then come as Mahāprabhu?” He comes to offer a wealth of bhakti. This wealth has three forms: silver and gold coins, sapphire gems and cintāmaṇi; or sādhana, bhāva and prema. Without being completely self-satisfied, he would not give that which is the highest attainment.

*durlabhālokayor yūnoḥ pāratantryād viyuktayoḥ \|*  
*upabhogātireko yaḥ kīrtyate sa samṛddhimān \|\|*

**The great enjoyment of lovers uniting, who have been separated and who find it most difficult to meet because of others’ control is called** ***samṛddhimān.*** **Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 15.206**

The state of samṛddhimān will not take place without meeting and escaping from other’s control. It will not occur without meeting and having completely enjoying, not depending on others.

*sarva-bhāvodgamollāsī mādano ’yaṁ parāt paraḥ \|*  
*rājate hlādinī-sāro rādhāyām eva yaḥ sadā \|\|219\|\|*

***Mādana,*** **the essence of** ***hlādinī-śakti,*** **superior to** ***mohana*****, making all** ***bhāvas*** **shine, is continually present in Rādhā. Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.219**

*yoga eva bhaved eṣa vicitraḥ ko ’pi mādanaḥ \|*  
*yad-vilāsā virājante nitya-līlāḥ sahasradhā \|\|225\|\|*

**During union,** ***mādana*** **takes on unprecedented astonishing qualities, whose effects manifest as thousands of continuous pastimes. Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.225**

*Yoga* here means it does not take place unless they meet. He enjoys with her by samṛddhi and mādana.

There is no consideration of qualified or unqualified. Situated in the highest limit of great bliss, mad with prema, he makes the whole world full of mādana. It is not improper at all to say there is no consideration of who is qualified or unqualified to receive prema. It is the custom that after conquering the world, the greatest king, along with his assistants, in bliss, distributes wealth with his two hands on both sides as he comes or goes. This is not tiring, but displays his great wealth. It also displays his great mercy. Thus with Nityānanda he distributed the great wealth of prema and did not take it back.

“Are you saying that prema was not given to Prahlāda, Ambarīṣa, Hanumān, Vibhīṣaṇa, the Pāṇḍavas and others and that one does not see it given in every yuga?” It was previously not offered but by the mercy of the Lord’s devotees, by their insistence, it was taken by force and given to these devotees. What is the giving on the part of the Lord? The king can take the wealth of a great miser by force. But then does the miser really give? Without considering benefit or no benefit, the mercy has now been given everywhere to enemies, friends, and neutral people, as it was given to Jagaī and Mādhāi. Then, it is called giving. Otherwise, it cannot.

“But the Lord gave mercy to Pūtanā, who was a killer of children and to others as well.” Mahāprabhu gives the highest madhurya-rasa (*unnatojjvala-rasam*), endowed with the bhāva of the gopīs. That is hidden in a casket. *muktiṁ dadāti karhicit sma na bhakti-yogam*: the Lord gives liberation but rarely prema. That rasa is most confidential.

“Is the Lord blissful? Did the Lord not previously appear?” He appears after a long time (*cirāt*) by mercy. The Lord of Navadvīpa is always situated in bliss, with dependence on this. “There are many great, pure sādhakas. When such good fortunate manifests in one of them, I will distribute prema to the world, taking support of it.” But the good fortune does not manifest in anyone. Inspired by mercy, in kali-yuga he has manifested for the unfortunate people without refuge. Thus after a long time he appears and gives this prema.

“Was not the calling of Advaita the cause of Mahāprabhu appearing?” He appeared by mercy. He manifested through Advaita. Advaita worshipped the Lord by offering tulasī leaves on the bank of the Gaṅgā, lamenting for the elder devotees and atheists. Not seeing any worship of the Lord, the highest Sadāśiva cried out and brought the Lord. One sees the Lord by worship, knows him by knowledge, realizes him by austerity, and controls him by prema. He was bound by Yaśodā to the mortar. In all cases, his mercy is the main cause. By mercy he allowed himself to be bound up.

Though the Lord cannot be understood by anyone, Bhāgavatam then says:

*yeṣāṁ sa eṣa bhagavān dayayed anantaḥ*  
*sarvātmanāśrita-pado yadi nirvyalīkam \|*  
*te dustarām atitaranti ca deva-māyāṁ*  
*naiṣāṁ mamāham iti dhīḥ śva-śṛgāla-bhakṣye \|\|*

**Those to whom the infinite Lord shows mercy -- when they attain devotees who are surrendered completely to the lotus feet of the Lord and who have no material desires -- cross the insurmountable ocean of** ***māyā*** **and understand the Lord. They are devoid of the intelligence which thinks in terms of “my body” and the “bodies of my family members” which are worthy food for dogs and jackals. SB 2.7.42**

Thus by mercy alone is he attained. Thus when Advaita, Mahā-rudra, the destroyer of the universe cried out loudly, the Lord thought, “The worlds are being destroyed” and quickly rising up, appeared by his mercy.

“It is also said that Kṛṣṇa gave madhurya-rasa to the sages of Daṇḍakāraṇya and to the śrutis and gāyatrī when they took forms of gopīs in Vraja. Who except Kṛṣṇa gives prema even to the creepers? This is proof that he gave prema to unqualified entities.”

That is true. Kṛṣṇa does not make distinctions. If the king gives everything in the form of a desire tree in the palace, he is not considered a giver but if he gives to most fallen people then he is called a great giver. It is *said api ca kṛpaṇa-pakṣe hy uttamaḥ-śloka-śabdaḥ*: those who are wretched can chant his name, Uttamaḥśloka. (SB 10.47.15)

“Do other fortunate people possess this wealth of prema? Were the jewels of prema kept in a storeroom and revealed to others on earth?” Yes. There are eighteen thousand verses in Bhāgavatam. By hearing Bhāgavatam, prema appears.

*yasyāṁ vai śrūyamāṇāyāṁ kṛṣṇe parama-pūruṣe \|*  
*bhaktir utpadyate puṁsaḥ śoka-moha-bhayāpahā \|\|*

**By hearing Bhāgavatam, bhaktifor the Supreme Lord Kṛṣṇa appears. Hearing it also destroys lamentation, illusion and fear in the human being. SB 1.7.7**

“Is there no manager, no protector of the treasury, and no bolts on the door?” Nityānanda is the manager. He gives using his two hands, without discrimination, since he is drunk with cloudbanks of prema. The protector of the door is Sanātana. He frees the bolts on the door situated in front and calls out:

*āgaccha bhrātar etasmin premāmbudhi-nimajjane \|*  
*yat saukhyam anubhūyeha tuccha-brahma-padādikam \|\|*

**O brothers! Come and submerge yourselves in the ocean of prema. Experiencing its happiness you will consider impersonal brahman to be insignificant.”**

Knowing this about him, the Lord gives him the post. Though the Lord usually does not give prema, he still gives it. But he rejects cheating dharma. Three types of cheating are rejected: cheating for wealth, cheating for power and cheating for prema. The cheater of wealth is opposed to spending for serving guru and the devotees to please the Lord. Rather he spends plentifully on opposite affairs. And is afraid to spend on them, in order to gain more. The person who, though able, is indifferent to performing bhakti, who is lazy in dancing and singing, is the second type. The person who, not having attained prema, is without longing for it, but thinks he is a devotee is the third type of cheater. By giving up these types of falsity, persons can attain prema, depending on great mercy.

“The son of Nanda should have a black form, which famous in all the Purāṇas and śrutis.” He shines with the great beauty (*dyuti*) of gold (*purata*), covering up his sapphire black color. He attracts the three worlds (*hariḥ*). He gave what was not offered previously, since he now has a form which enchants Kṛṣṇa, but is Kṛṣṇa’s form. On seeing that form and meditating on that form, one attains prema. The prema does not depend on giving or not giving*. Hariḥ* means he attracts all by prema.

“I will meditate on his pastimes. Please describe them.” He will manifest to you (*sphuratu vaḥ*). “You are now like a great mountain since the pastimes of Gaurāṅga cannot be seen by you. Though knowing about him, you heart is hard like stone.” May the Lord, who is merciful to the miserable, always manifest to you by his mercy. His pastimes will also appear to you at all times. What is the use of speaking? Even when the pastimes are described, can persons understanding with ears which are merely holes?

“I am a great offender. I have not developed attraction for the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. Will he be merciful to me?” He is the son of Śacī. Śacī is the mother of the universe. She has affection for her children. Though she may give birth to a bad son, she does not consider him bad. Devoted to his mother, Gaurāṅga will be merciful to you.

According to the devotees there is a conversation between Śacī and Gaurāṅga at Advaita’s house after he took sannyāsa. She said, “O Viśvambara! You know my grief for Viśvarūpa. I have piles of sins from previous lives. They will in time manifest. If my death were to suddenly occur, like that of your father, I would be liberated from daily suffering and lamentation, criticism by people and shame. If I were to die, by poison or being placed in the Gaṅgā, everyone would praise me as the mother of Viśvambara. Remaining, I will be criticized. No one will speak with me or say my name. The people of Navadvīpa will say, ‘By saying her name, one will not attain one’s desires. Take away the unfortunate woman for our sight.’ What should I do? Please tell me, O Viśvambara.”

Gaurāṅga said, “O mother! Do not say this. Do not say this. These words are most cruel. I will explain to you what is proper. I will go from the house but I will remain even if I am elsewhere. You are my mother. I am your son in many births among devatās etc. in all places. I am worshipped in the three worlds. You are worshipped by me.

“The person devoid of all sadhāna who has committed thousands of sins, if he says “son of Śacī” brings me and my followers under his control. He who glorifies the son of Śacī with joy is full of knowledge, is the knower of all scriptures, is endowed with prema and is dear to me. The fortunate person who says “son of Śacī” purchases me and my followers. He who glorifies the son of Śacī in joy is like a tīṛtha, has all knowledge and all wealth. He who speaks of Śacī, and of me, the son of Śacī, and of Navadvīpa the place of Śacī, has known all scriptures, and Vedas. He who says the name “Śacī” is my guru, and I have affection for him birth after birth.”

Thus even the greatest offender, if he utters the name of Śacī, becomes dear to Gaurāṅga. That is the significance of the words *śacī-nandana*. Thus one should chant the name *śrī-śacī-nandana*. By that kīrtana, even if one has committed offenses, one will become free from them.
