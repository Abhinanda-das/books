# Aścarya-rāsa-prabandhaḥ \[BS\]

### Text 1

*jayati jayati rādhāpāṅga-saṅgī-bhujaṅgī-*  
*kavalita uru-bādhā-mūrcchito’nanya-sādhyaḥ*  
*tad-adhara-sudhayoccair jīvitaḥ śyāma-dhāmā*  
*tad-ativiṣa-viṣaṅgenaiva kaścit kiśoraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The indescribable youth, endowed with dark body, devoured by Radha’s snake-like glance, and thus agitated with pain, and who could not be cured by any other means, who was brought to life by the nectar of her lips, which destroyed the snake’s intense poison, remains ever victorious.**

### Text 2

*jayati jayati vṛndāraṇya-candro’ticitron*  
*mada-rasa-maya-rāsollāsa-sambhrānta-mūrtiḥ*  
*pramada-madana-līlā-mohanaḥ śyāma-dhāmā*  
*nirupama-sukha-sīmābhīra-rāmābhirāmaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The moon of Vrindāvana, his dark form excited by the joy of the rāsa dance composed of astonishing, intoxicating mellows, bewildered in his pastimes of joy and love, giving delight to the gopīs who experience unparalleled bliss, remains ever victorious.**

### Text 3

*asti mahādbhuta-vṛndāraṇyaṁ*  
*santata-vāhi-mahā-rasa-vanyam*  
*parama-manohara-parama-supuṇyaṁ*  
*rasa-maya-sakala-dhāma-mūrdhanyam*

**Перевод:**

**Most astonishing Vrindāvana is a flood of constant great rasa, most pure and most attractive, the chief of all abodes made of rasa.**

### Text 4

*sakala-guṇānāṁ sphurad-atibhūmi*  
*projjvala-cintāmaṇi-maya-bhūmi*  
*śruti-durgama-tṛṇa-mātra-vibhūti*  
*sphīta-mahā-sukha-sindhv-anubhūti*

**Перевод:**

**The expansive land shines will all good qualities. It is composed of glittering cintāmaṇi. The power of even the grass cannot be described by the Vedas. It gives the experience of an increasing ocean of great joy.**

### Text 5

*āścarya-rāsa-prabandhaḥ*  
*prakṛti-pare paripūrṇānande*  
*mahasi mahādbhuta-hari-rasa-kande*  
*bhrājamānam akhilojjvala-ramyaṁ*  
*madhura-viśada-hari-bhāva-sugamyam*

**Перевод:**

**It is most attractive, shining with the most astonishing rasa of Krsna, which is filled with the most complete, great bliss, beyond the material world, through which one may easily access the bhāva of Krsna, which manifests sweetness.**

### Text 6

*mukhya-rasātmaka-paramākāraṁ*  
*vimala-manoja-bīja-ruci-sāram*  
*māyā-vidyā-pāram apāraṁ*  
*rādhā-mādhava-nitya-vihāram*

**Перевод:**

**That abode is the supreme form, composed of the chief rasa. It is the essence of taste, which is the seed of pure love. It is without limit, beyond knowledge of this material world. That abode is the place of the eternal pastimes of Rādhā and Mādhava.**

### Text 7

*rādhā-madhupati-cāru-padāṅkair*  
*aṅkita-matula-sudhā-rasa-paṅkaiḥ*  
*svaccha-suśītala-mṛdula-suvāsaṁ*  
*bibhrad-avani-talam adbhuta-bhāsam*

**Перевод:**

**It is mild, cool, clean, and fragrant, sustaining ground which shines in an astonishing way whose earth is made of sweet rasas and marked with the attractive footprints of Rādhā and Krsna.**

### Text 8

*kvacana parāga-puñja-kamanīyaṁ*  
*kvaca makaranda-pūra-ramaṇīyam*  
*kvacana galita-kusumaiḥ kṛta-śobhaṁ*  
*kvaca maṇi-karpūra-raja-rucirābham*

**Перевод:**

**It is somewhere pleasant with clouds of pollen, in some places pleasant with honey, in some places beautiful with flowers oozing with nectar, and in some places attractive with dust made of gems and camphor.**

### Text 9

*santata-phala-kusumādi-vicitraiḥ*  
*koṭi-mahāsura-pādapa-jaitraiḥ*  
*gulma-latā-tarubhiḥ supavitrair*  
*maṇḍitam īśa-juṣām api citraiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The abode is decorated with various flowers and fruit at all times, with pure trees, bushes and creepers which are more attractive than millions of huge, heavenly trees. These trees are remarkable even for persons serving the Lord.**

### Text 10

*kusumita-pallavita-druma-valli*  
*sphuṭita-kadambaka-kiṁśuka-malli*  
*smera-kumuda-karavīra-virāji*  
*prahasita-ketaka-campaka-rāji*

**Перевод:**

**It has creepers and trees with buds and flower. It has blossoming kadamba, kiṁśuka and malli trees. It has smiling lotuses and karavīra flowers as well has groups of laughing ketaka and campaka flowers.**

### Text 11

*vikasita-kuṭaja-kunda-mandāraṁ*  
*suphalita-panasa-pūga-sahakāram*  
*hari-caraṇa-priya-tulasī-vipinaiḥ*  
*śobhamānam uru-parimala-masṛṇaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The place has blossoming kuṭaja, kunda and mandāra trees and fruiting panasa (jack fruit), pūga (betal-nut) and mango trees. The place is beautiful with groves of tulasī, dear to the Lord’s lotus feet, which have mild, but abundant fragrance.**

### Text 12

*vilasaj-jātī-yūthikam atulaṁ*  
*vikaca-sthala-paṅkaja-baka-vañjulam*  
*santata-santānaka-santānaṁ*  
*vara-hari-candana-candana-vipinam*

**Перевод:**

**The abode has groves of excellent haricandana and sandalwood, abundant of desire trees, shining land lotuses, baka and vañjula trees, and incomparably shining jasmine and yuthika.**

### Text 13

*pārijāta-vana-paramāmodaṁ*  
*rādhā-kṛṣṇa-janita-bahu-modam*  
*kuruvaka-maruvaka-mādhavikābhir*  
*damanaka-dāḍima-mālatikābhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The abode is filled with the fragrance of parijāta forests which gives great pleasure to Rādhā and Krsna, with kuruvaka, maruvaka and mādhavi plants, as well as damanaka trees, pomegranate trees and mālati creepers.**

### Text 14

*śephalikayā nava-mālikayā*  
*śobhitam api bahuvidha-jhiṇṭikayā*  
*lalita-lavaṅga-vanair atimadhuraṁ*  
*nava-punnāga-nāga-ruci-ruciram*

**Перевод:**

**The place is splendid with jhiṇṭi, nava-mallika and śephalika. It is sweet with forests of graceful clove bushes. It is attractive with the beauty of fresh punnāga and nāga trees.**

### Text 15

*stavakita-navakāśoka-vanāli*  
*smera-śirīṣa-parisphuṭa-pāṭali*  
*bandhuram abhinava-bandhuka-vipinaiḥ*  
*śobhitam abhitas tilaka-mlānaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**There are forests of fresh aśoka trees laden with buds, smiling śiraṣa trees and blossoming pāṭali. The place is charming with forests of fresh bandhuka, with dark tilaka trees everywhere.**

### Text 16

*nija-nija-vibhavaiḥ pratipadam*  
*adhikaṁ vilasad-ananta-jāti-taru-latikam*  
*niravadhi-vardhi-madhura-guṇa-sindhu*  
*suvicira-nindita-koṭi-ravīndu*

**Перевод:**

**The place has an infinite variety of trees and creepers which shine more brilliantly at every moment with their greatness. It is an ocean of sweet qualities that derides a million suns and moons eternally.**

### Text 17

*vāpī-kūpa-taḍāgair lalitaṁ*  
*maṇimaya-keli-mahīdhara-mahitam*  
*rāsocita-maṇi-kuṭṭima-rājaṁ*  
*rañjayad eka-vimala-rasa-rājam*

**Перевод:**

**It is graced by lakes, wells and tanks. It is graced with jeweled mountains used for pastimes. It shines with jeweled courtyards suitable for the rāsa dancewhich attract mādhurya-rasa.**

### Text 18

*rakta-kanaka-karpūra-parāgaṁ*  
*bibhrad-ravijā-pulina-subhāgam*  
*rādhā-mādhava-keli-nikuñjaṁ*  
*dadhad-atimañjura-guñjad-ali-puñjam*

**Перевод:**

**It has auspicious banks with glowing lotuses and is filled with red, gold and white pollen. It has bowers for the pastimes of Rādhā and Mādhava which have most attractive humming bees.**

### Text 19

*mada-kala-kokila-pañcama-rāgaṁ*  
*sthira-cara-nikara-mūrcchad-anurāgam*  
*mada-śikhaṇḍi-kṛta-tāṇḍava-raṅgaṁ*  
*cakita-cakita-parilola-kuraṅgam*

**Перевод:**

**It is filled with intoxicated cuckoos singing the fifth note, which causes all moving and nonmoving beings to faint. It is filled with the dancing of intoxicated peacocks and fearful, trembling antelope.**

### Text 20

*parama-vicitratarākṛtir-rāvaiḥ*  
*khaga-paśubhir bahubhir bahu-bhāvaiḥ*  
*śobhitam api śuka-sārī-nicayair*  
*vara-dampatyoḥ sva-pada-vineyaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The place is beautiful with many types of birds, whose cries and forms are most amazing and endowed with many bhāvas, and with parrots and sārī birds attracted to the lotus feet of Rādhā and Krsna.**

### Text 21

*atyadbhutatama-ṛtu-ṣaṭka-śri*  
*śraṁsitanaiḥ śreyasi vipina-śri*  
*manda-sugandha-suśītala-marutā*  
*juṣṭam amṛta-yamunāmbhasi viśatā*

**Перевод:**

**The place has the beauty of the most astonishing six seasons. It is endowed with the beauty of auspicious forests. The place is endowed with cool, fragrant, soft breezes endowed with the sweet waters of the Yamunā.**

### Text 22

*ādya-viśuddha-mahā-rasa-rūpaṁ*  
*khelad-eka-vara-manmatha-bhūpam*  
*sāndrānanda-parama-rasa-kāṣṭhaṁ*  
*rādhā-nāgara-bhāva-gariṣṭham*

**Перевод:**

**The place is the original form of the pure, great rasa, where the king of the most excellent Cupids plays. At that place resides the pinnacle of the highest rasa of condensed bliss, thick with the mood of Rādhā and Krsna.**

### Text 23

*adhi-lalitādika-sulalita-bhāvaṁ*  
*prakaṭita-sahaja-rasavad-anubhāvam*  
*nikhila-nigama-gaṇa-durgama-mahima*  
*premānanda-camatkṛti-sīma*

**Перевод:**

**The place possesses gentle affection of Lalitā and others and manifests explicit, charming indications of natural love for the Lord. It possesses the height of astonishing bliss of prema, whose greatness is difficult for all the Vedas to describe.**

### Text 24

*śārada-candra-kara-khacitaṁ*  
*sphīta-rasāmbudhi-vīcī-nicitam*  
*adhi-rajanī-mukham ujjvala-veśaḥ*  
*ko’pi kiśoras tatra praviveśa*

**Перевод:**

**Into this place which was purified by the rays of the autumn moon and covered with swelling waves from the ocean of rasa. The young boy with shining dress entered in the evening.**

### Text 25

*mahā-camatkāra-nidhāna-rūpa*  
*vilāsa-bhūṣādibhir atyapūrvaḥ*  
*rāsotsavāya praviśan pradoṣe*  
*vṛndāvanaṁ nandati kṛṣṇa-candraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Most astonishing with decorations, pastimes and form, which are the very form of great amazement, Krsna, entering Vrindāvana for the rāsa dance in the evening, was pleased.**

### Text 26

*rasa-maya-līlaḥ kuvalaya-nīlaḥ*  
*sakala-yuvati-mohana-guṇa-śīlaḥ*  
*kuñcita-keśa-sakala-kaleśaḥ*  
*pīta-paṭāñcita-pṛthu-kaṭi-deśaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Krsna, with the complexion of a blue lotus, has pastimes full of rasa. His actions and qualities attract all young women. With his curled locks, he is the master of all arts. Around his broad hips he wears yellow silk cloth.**

### Text 27

*makarākṛti-maṇi-kuṇḍala-dolaḥ*  
*sphurad-atiruci-kallola-kapolaḥ*  
*muktā-ratna-vicitra-nicolaḥ*  
*smara-rasa-madhura-vilocana-khelaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Jewelled earrings in the shape of makaras sway from his ears. His cheeks shine like attractive waves. He wears a treasury of various pearls and gems. His playful, sweet glance is laden with love.**

### Text 28

*ratna-tilaka-ruci-rañjita-bhālaḥ*  
*snigdha-capala-kuṭilālaka-jālaḥ*  
*kalita-lalitatara-bahu-vidha-mālaḥ*  
*keli-kalā-rabhasātirasālaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**His forehead isdecorated with the beauty of jewels and tilaka. His curly locks of hair are shiny and fickle. He wears a variety of charming garlands. He is most sweet with his passion for amorous pastimes.**

### Text 29

*pramudita-vadana-manohara-hāsaḥ*  
*kambu-kaṇṭha-taṭa-padaka-vilāsaḥ*  
*viracita-yuvati-vimohana-cūḍaś*  
*citra-mālya-kṛta-barhāpīḍaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He has an attractive smile on his joyful face. A golden ornament swings from his conch-like neck. His topknot enchants the young girls. His peacock feather ornament is adorned with a colorful garland.**

### Text 30

*pīnorasi lasad-uru-maṇi-hāraḥ*  
*sphuṭad-aṅgada-kaṅkaṇa-dhāraḥ*  
*subhaga-nitamba-raṇan-maṇi-rasanaḥ*  
*parihita-rāsocita-vara-vasanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**A large jeweled necklace hangs over his thick chest. He wears glittering bracelets and armlets. His jeweled belt jingles on his attractive waist. He wears attractive cloth suitable for the rāsa dance.**

### Text 31

*maṇi-mañjīra-mañju-ruta-caraṇaḥ*  
*prasṛmara-pādāṅgada-maṇi-kiraṇaḥ*  
*śravaṇa-virājita-ratna-vataṁsaḥ*  
*kara-dhṛta-maṇi-maya-mohana-vaṁśaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**His jeweled anklets jingle sweetly. The jewels on his anklets spread out rays of light. Jeweled ornaments hang on his ears. He holds an attractive jeweled flute in his hand.**

### Text 32

*rādhānusmṛti-muhur-utpulakaḥ*  
*sakala-rasika-vara-nāgara-tilakaḥ*  
*pratyaṅgādbhuta-suṣamā-sindhuḥ*  
*pratipada-vardhi-madana-rasa-sindhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**His hairs constantly stand on end from remembrance of Rādhā. He is the leader of all rasikas. He is an astonishing ocean of beauty in all his limbs. He is an ever-increasing ocean of mādhurya-rasa.**

### Text 33

*prodvelādbhuta-madhurima-sindhuḥ*  
*prakaṭa-mahā-rasa-maya-guṇa-sindhuḥ*  
*matta-mataṅgaja-laṅgima-gamanaḥ*  
*parama-rasaika-nimajjita-bhuvanaḥ*  
*kāśmīrāguru-candana-liptaḥ*  
*śyāma-tanur maṇi-bhūṣaṇa-dīptaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is an overflowing ocean of astonishing sweetness. He is an ocean of conspicuous qualities filled with great rasa. He walks with the gait of a mad elephant. He inundates the world in the highest rasa. Anointed with sandalwood, aguru and saffron, his black body shines with jeweled ornaments.**

### Text 34

*tribhaṅgī-vinyāsa-sthita-tanu-kadamba-druma-tale*  
*yadā rādhā-nāmāṅkita-madhura-saṅketa-muralīm*  
*nidhāya śrī-bimbādhara-vara-puṭe nāgara-gurur*  
*jagau gopyo’dhāvann abhikam abhi tarhy eva vivaśāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When the guru of lovers at the base of a kadamba tree in three fold bending form takes up his flute, which sweetly indicates Rādhā’s name, in his excellent red lips and sings, the gopīs, out of control, run to meet their lover.**

### Text 35

*atha nīpa-kalpa-taru-mūla-gataḥ*  
*kalita-tribhaṅga-lalitāṅga-yutaḥ*  
*aruṇādhare nihita-veṇu-varaḥ*  
*kalam ujjagau sa rasika-pravaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**His delicate limbs bent in three places, the best of rasikas, going to the base of a kadamba desire tree and placing the flute on his red lips, played a soft melody.**

### Text 36

*śrutvā mādhava-muralī-ninādaṁ*  
*tat-kṣaṇam ujjhita-guru-jana-vādam*  
*dhvany-abhimukham anudhāvitavatyaḥ*  
*pratidiśam abhinava-gopa-yuvatyaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing the sound of Mādhava’s flute, which makes them reject the reproaches of their elders, the young gopīs immediately ran from all directions to the sound.**

### Text 37

*kāścid vyatyastāmbara-bharaṇāḥ*  
*kāścana nūpuraika-yuta-caraṇāḥ*  
*aparā añjitaika-vara-nayanāḥ*  
*kā api parihṛta-nija-pati-śayanāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Some of the gopīs have their ornaments placed in reverse. Some are wearing on one anklet. Some have only one eye painted with mascara. Some have given up their husbands’ beds.**

### Text 38

*snānam athodvartanam anulepaṁ*  
*nīvi-nibandhana-mārjana-lepam*  
*kurvatyo ‘tijavād yayur aparāḥ*  
*kāścid athārdha-prasādhita-cikurāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Some, while taking bath, rubbing oil on their bodies, applying cosmetics, tying their dresses, sweeping the floor or smearing cow dung on the floor, quickly prepare to depart. Some left with their hair half-tied.**

### Text 39

*kāścid gurvādiṣu bhuñjāneṣv*  
*api pariviveṣaṁ hitvā yāne*  
*cakrur matim atikhaṇḍita-lajjāḥ*  
*kevala-vāṁśika-saṅgama-sajjāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Some, giving up serving food to the elders, having given up shame, preparing to meet the flute player, made up their minds to go.**

### Text 40

*kāścana hāra-grathane saktāḥ*  
*sūtra-karā yayur atyanuraktāḥ*  
*mugdhā dugdhāvartana-niratā*  
*yayur aparā api hari-rasa-bharitāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Some absorbed in stringing necklaces, becoming greatly attached to him, left while holding the threads in their hands. Other innocent girls involved in stirring milk, departed, filled with rasafor the Lord.**

### Text 41

*loka-veda-vidhi-kṛta-samupekṣā*  
*dūra-dalita-gṛha-dehāpekṣāḥ*  
*prema-mahāgraha-gāḍha-gṛhītā*  
*harim abhisasrur vraja-pura-vanitāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Ignoring the rules prescribed by custom and the Vedas, fully rejecting their houses and bodies, fully possessed by the powerful planet (Rāhu) of prema, the women of Vraja went to meet Krsna.**

### Text 42

*gaṇḍa-lola-maṇi-kuṇḍala-suṣamāḥ*  
*mukta-kavara-bhara-vigalita-kusumāḥ*  
*vipula-nitamba-stana-bhara-vikalās*  
*tanu-ruci-prakaṭīkṛta-bahu-capalāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**They appeared beautiful as their jeweled earrings swung on their cheeks. The flowers fell from their loosened hair. Their progress was impeded by their wide hips and heavy breasts. The beauty of their bodies was like many flashes of lightning.**

### Text 43

*upari vinirmita-śata-śata-candramā*  
*madhya-racita-cala-hema-girīndrāḥ*  
*bhuvi vihita-sthala-paṅkaja-valanā*  
*rejur diśi diśi tā vraja-lalanāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Radiating hundreds of moon rays from their faces (sky), appearing like moving golden mountains illuminating their hips (middle region), appearing as moving land lotuses with their feet (earth), the women of Vraja shone in all directions.**

### Text 44

*nūpura-kāñcī-valaya-ghaṭānāṁ*  
*jhaṅkṛta-mukharita-sakala-diśānām*  
*jaṅgama-kala-kalatāyita-vapuṣāṁ*  
*reje rājiḥ sā vraja-sudṛśām*

**Перевод:**

**That group of Vraja women, whose bodies, an assembly of anklets, belts and bracelets which resounded in all directions, which resembled moving bodies of jingling music, shone brightly.**

### Text 45

*yuvatiṣu yā nija-pati-sambhuktā*  
*daivād antar-gṛha-yātās tāḥ*  
*gopair dṛḍhatara-pihite dvāre*  
*pratihata-gatayaḥ petur agāre*

**Перевод:**

**Those gopīs who by chance had gone inside the house and were enjoyed by their husbands, were obstructed by the doors, which were firmly locked by the cowherds, and were thus kept inside their houses.**

### Text 46

*aśubhaṁ puruṣāntara-saṅga-kṛtaṁ*  
*kṛtvā hari-virahārtyā nihatam*  
*parama-mahā-maṅgala-sunidānaṁ*  
*cakrur madhupati-madhura-dhyānam*

**Перевод:**

**Having destroyed, out of pain in separation from Krsna, the inauspiciousness of association with any other male than their husbands, they performed meditation on the sweetness of the master of all sweetness, which would cause the highest auspiciousness.**

### Text 47

*śuddha-mahā-rasa-cid-ghana-dehā*  
*hari-para-bahir-antara-sakalehāḥ*  
*sapadi prāptāḥ preṣṭha-padāntaṁ*  
*tāś ca tadā rucirās tu nitāntam*

**Пословный перевод:**

Manifesting bodies full of pure knowledge and great rasa, with all their activities internally and externally dedicated to Krsna, becoming most beautiful, they immediately attained the highest position near their lover.

### Text 48

*evaṁ vraja-vara-yuvatī-vṛndaiḥ*  
*śyāma-kiśoraḥ prema-madāndhaiḥ*  
*hari-gatir indirayāpi na dṛṣṭā*

**Перевод:**

## About Srila Prabodhananda Sarasvati

Srila Prabodhananda Sarasvati was a great Vaishnava devotee of Lord Caitanya Mahaprabhu. While traveling in southern India, Caitanya Mahaprabhu met Prabodhananda Sarasvati, who had two brothers, Venkata Bhatta and Tirumalaya Bhatta, who were Vaishnavas of the Ramanuja-sampradaya. Gopala Bhatta Gosvami was the nephew of Prabodhananda Sarasvati. Empowered by Lord Caitanya Mahaprabhu, he taught Srimad Bhagavatam and other devotional works to Gopala Bhatta Gosvami. His eloquent speech would engladden everyone.

From historical records it is found that Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu traveled in South India in the year 1433 Sakabda (A.D. 1511) during the Caturmasya period, and it was at that time that He met Prabodhananda.

Prabodhananda Sarasvati serves Sri Radha in Goloka Vrndavana as Tungavidya-gopi, one of Her asta-sakhis.

His completed works are:

1. Caitanya-candramrta,
2. Radha-rasa-sudha-nidhi,
3. Sangita-madhava,
4. Vrndavana-mahimamrta,
5. Navadvipa-astaka,
6. Aúcarya-rasa-prabandha
7. Sruti-stuti-vakya
8. Viveka-œataka
9. Kama-gayatri-vyakhya
10. Commentary on Gita Govinda

## About Bhānu Swami

His Holiness Bhānu Swami Maharaja was born in Canada on the 26th December 1948 to the most fortunate Japanese parents. He is one of the senior disciples of His Divine Grace A.C Bhaktivedanta Swami Śrīla Prabhupāda, founder ācārya of ISKCON, the International Society for Krishna Consciousness.

He holds a BA Degree in Oriental fine arts history from the University of British Colombia. He joined the Hare Krishna movement in India in 1970. Initiated in 1971 by Śrīla Prabhupāda, he took sannyāsa vows in 1984. By 1972, His Holiness Bhānu Swami Maharaja had already earned credit from Śrīla Prabhupāda for his exact Sanskrit pronunciation, expertise in cooking and excellence in deity worship. He also began to translate Śrīla Prabhupāda’s books into Japanese.

A scholar in Sanskrit and Bengali, Bhānu Swami has translated more than fifty significant works of the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava canon. Many of these works are commentaries from primary members of the Gauḍīya sampradāya.

Bhānu Swami is a quiet achiever and has studied a variety of disciplines including martial arts, Reiki healing, Carnatic music (specifically veena).

He continues with this translation service to this day, giving us the nectar from the Bengali and Sanskrit works of the previous Vaiṣṇava ācāryas to enhance our understanding of the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava philosophy. He is also a member of the Governing Body Commission (GBC) of ISKCON.
