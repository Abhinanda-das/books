# Aṣjaneya Trikāla Vandanaṁ

## Añjaneya Trikāla Vandanaṁ

*prātaḥ smarāmi hanumān ānanta-vīryaṁ*  
*śrī rāmacandra caraṇāmbhuja cancarīkaṁ \|*  
*laṅkāpurī-dahana nandita-deva-vṛndaṁ*  
*sarvārtha-siddhisadanaṁ prathita-prabhāvaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“I remember that Hanumān during the early hours as one whose valor is immeasurable. I remember that bee who stays always at Lord Rāmacandra's feet. I remember he who burnt Laṅka and made the demigods happy. I remember him who is the store-house of all** ***siddhis*** **and who is capable of anything.”**

*mādhyam namāmi vṛjinārṇava tāraṇaikā-*  
*-dhāraṁ śaraṇya muditānupama prabhāvaṁ \|*  
*sītādhi sindhu pariśoṣaṇa karma dakṣaṁ*  
*vandāru kalpatarum avyayam āñjaneyaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“I bow to Lord Añjaneya during the mid day as the one capable person to crossing the ocean, who blesses the person with enormous happiness when he takes refuge in him. He is entrusted with the responsibility of annihilating Sītā's sorrows. He is like a wish-fulfilling tree for one who bows to him.”**

*śāyam bhajāmi śaraṇopa smṛtākhilārti*  
*puṅja praṇāśana vidhau prathita pratāpaṁ \|*  
*akśāntakaṁ sakala rākśasa vaṁśa dhūma-*  
*-ketuṁ pramodita videha sutaṁ dayāluṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“I worship that Añjaneya during the evening as the one who saves everyone who takes his name. He is most valorous, who killed Akṣā and was the** ***dhūmaketu*** **for all the demons. He also made Sīta Devī (daughter of the Videha country) happy.”**
