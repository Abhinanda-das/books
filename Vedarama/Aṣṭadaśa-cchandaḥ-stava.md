# Aṣṭadaśa-cchandaḥ-stava

### Text 1

*jīyān nāma murārer prema-marandasya navyam aravindam*  
*bhavati yad-ābhāso ’pi svāti-jalaṁ mukti-muktāyāḥ*

**Пословный перевод:**

*jīyāt*—all glories; *nāma*—to the holy name; *murāreḥ*—of Lord Kṛṣṇa; *prema*—of pure love; *marandasya*—of the nectar; *navyam*—new; *aravindam*—lotus flower; *bhavati*—is; *yat*—of which; *ābhāsaḥ*—the dim reflection; *api*—even; *svāti-jalam*—a tiny drop of water; *mukti*—of liberation; *muktāyāḥ*—of the pearl.

**Перевод:**

**All glories to the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa, which is a new lotus flower filled with the nectar of pure love. The dim reflection of His name is the drop of water that creates the pearl of liberation.**

### Text 2

*nandotsavādayas tāḥ kaṁsa-vadhāntā harer mahā-līlāḥ*  
*chandobhir lalitāṅgair aṣṭādaśabhir nirūpyante*

**Пословный перевод:**

*nanda*—of Nanda Mahārāja; *utsava*—festival; *ādayaḥ*—beginning with; *tāḥ*—them; *kaṁsa*—of Kaṁsa; *vadha*—the killing; *antāḥ*—ending; *hareḥ*—of Lord Kṛṣṇa; *mahā*—the great; *līlāḥ*—pastimes; *chandobhiḥ*—with chandaḥ verses; *lalita-aṅgaiḥ*—beautiful; *aṣṭādaśabhiḥ*—with 18; *nirūpyante*—are described.

**Перевод:**

**The magnificent pastimes of Lord Kṛṣṇa, beginning with the festival of Mahārāja Nanda, and ending with the killing of Kaṁsa, are described in the following 18 beautiful chandaḥ verses.**

### Text 3

*danuja-tṛṇa-kadamboddāma-dāvāgni-rāśe*  
*kusumita-ravikanyā-tīra-vanyā-karīndra*  
*mada-kala-paśupālī-locanendīvarendo*  
*bhavatu tava caritraṁ mad-girāṁ maṇḍanāya*

**Пословный перевод:**

*danuja*—of the demons; *tṛṇa*—the grass; *kadamba*—multitude; *uddāma*—powerful; *dāvāgni-rāśe*—forest-fire; *kusumita*—with blossoming flowers; *ravikanyā*—of the Yamunā River; *tīra*—on the shore; *vanyā*—in the forest; *kari-indra*—O regal elephant; *mada-kala*—intoxicated with love; *paśupālī*—the *gopīs*; *locana*—of the eyes; *indīvara*—for the lotus flowers; *indo*—O moon; *bhavatu*—may be; *tava*—of You; *caritram*—the transcendental pastimes; *mat*—of me; *girām*—of the words; *maṇḍanāya*—for the decoration.

**Перевод:**

**O great forest-fire that burns the grass of the demons, O regal elephant that plays in the flower-garden forest by the shore of the Yamunā, O moon that opens the lotus flowers of the eyes of the gopīs intoxicated with love, I pray that Your pastimes may decorate my words.**

### Text 4

*mūlotkhāta-vidhāyinī bhava-taroḥ kṛṣṇānya-tṛṣnā-kṣayāt*  
*kheladbhir muni-cakravāka-nicayair ācamyamānā muhuḥ*  
*karṇāndi-kala-svanā vahatu me jihvā-taṭi-prāṅgaṇe*  
*ghūrṇat-tuṅga-rasāvalis tava kathā-pīyūṣa-kallolinī*

**Пословный перевод:**

*mūla*—the root; *utkhāta*—digging; *vidhāyinī*—performing the action; *bhava*—of repeated birth and death; *taroḥ*—of the tree; *kṛṣṇa*—than Kṛṣṇa; *anya*—another; *tṛṣna*—thirst; *kṣayāt*—because of the destruction; *kheladbhiḥ*—playing; *muni*—of the sages; *cakravāka*—of *cakravāka* birds; *nicayaiḥ*—by the multitudes; *ācamyamānā*—sipped; *muhuḥ*—repeatedly; *karṇa*—the ears; *ānandi*—blissful; *kala*—sweet; *svanā*—sound; *vahatu*—may flow; *me*—of me; *jihvā*—of the tongue; *taṭi*—of the surface; *prāṅgaṇe*—in the courtyard; *ghūrṇat*—rolling; *tuṅga-rasa*—sweet nectar; *avaliḥ*—abundance; *tava*—of You; *kathā*—of the topics; *pīyūṣa*—of nectar; *kallolinī*—the river filled with waves.

**Перевод:**

**O Lord, I pray that the nectar river of words about You, which by destroying the thirst for anything other than Kṛṣṇa uproots the tree of repeated birth and death, which is continually sipped by the cakravāka birds of the great sages, which makes a sweet sound delighting the ears, and which is filled with swiftly moving currents of nectar exchanges of transcendental love, may flow into the courtyard of my tongue.**

**and who is the fresh sandal paste that anoints all the** ***gopīs’*** **hearts.**

**O Nanda-nandana, all glories to the very sweet devotional service honey that flows from the lotus flower of Your feet. When the smallest drop of that sweetness is present, the happiness of impersonal liberation becomes like an oyster.**

### Text 6
