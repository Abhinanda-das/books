The Glories of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam from the Skanda Purāṇa, Vaiṣṇava-khaṇḍa

# Bhāgavata-māhātmyam (from Skanda Purāṇa)

## \<strong\>The Glories of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam from the Skanda Purāṇa, Vaiṣṇava-khaṇḍa\</strong\>

## Chapter One

### Verse 1

*vyāsa uvāca*  
*śrī-sac-cid-ānanda-ghana-svarūpiṇe*  
*kṛṣṇāya cānanta-sukhābhivarṣiṇe*  
*viśvodbhava-sthāna-nirodha-hetave*  
*numo vayaṁ bhakti-rasāptaye ’niśam*

**Перевод:**

**Vyāsa said: We offer our respects again and again to Lord Kṛṣṇa so that we may achieve the transcendental taste of His devotional service. He is the splendid, full embodiment of eternity, knowledge and bliss, raining down upon us unlimited joy. He is the cause of the universe’s creation, maintenance and withdrawal.**

### Verse 2

*naimiṣe sūtam āsīnam*  
*abhivādya mahā-matim*  
*kathāmṛta-rasāsvāda-*  
*kuśalā ṛṣayo ’bruvan*

**Перевод:**

**At Naimiṣa the sages, expert in tasting the nectarean juice of divine topics, greeted wise Sūta, who was seated before them, and spoke to him.**

### Verse 3

*ṛṣaya ucuḥ*  
*vajraṁ śrī-māthure deśe*  
*sva-pautraṁ hastināpure*  
*abhiṣicya gate rājñi*  
*tau kathaṁ kiṁ ca cakratuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The sages said: After the king installed Vajra on the throne in Śrī Mathurā district and his own grandson in Hastināpura, how did these two fare, and what did they do?**

### Verse 4

*sūta uvāca*  
*nārāyaṇaṁ namaskṛtya*  
*naraṁ caiva narottamam*  
*devīṁ sarasvatīṁ vyāsaṁ*  
*tato jayam udīrayet*

**Перевод:**

**Sūta said: Before reciting this narration, which is the very means of conquest, we should offer respectful obeisances unto the Personality of Godhead, Nārāyaṇa, unto Nara-nārāyaṇa Ṛṣi, the supermost human being, unto mother Sarasvatī, the goddess of learning, and unto Śrīla Vyāsadeva.**

### Verse 5

*mahā-pathaṁ gate rājñi*  
*parīkṣit pṛthivī-patiḥ*  
*jagāma mathurāṁ viprā*  
*vajranābha-didṛkṣayā*

**Перевод:**

**When the king had left for the final journey, O** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **the lord of the earth Parīkṣit went to Mathurā to visit Vajranābha.**

### Verse 6

*pitṛvyam āgataṁ jñātvā*  
*vajraḥ prema-pariplutaḥ*  
*abhigamyābhivādyātha*  
*nināya nija-mandiram*

**Перевод:**

**Learning that his uncle had come, Vajra was overcome with loving affection. He came forward, greeted him, and brought him to his residence.**

### Verse 7

*pariṣvajya sa taṁ vīraḥ*  
*kṛṣṇaika-gata-mānasaḥ*  
*rohiṇy-ādyā hareḥ patnīr*  
*vavandāyatanāgataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Arriving at the palace, the hero, who always thought only of Kṛṣṇa, embraced Vajra and offered respects to Rohiṇī and the other wives of Hari.**

### Verse 8

*tābhiḥ sammānito ’tyarthaṁ*  
*parīkṣit pṛthivī-patiḥ*  
*viśrāntaḥ sukham āsīno*  
*vajranābham uvāca ha*

**Перевод:**

**They showed all honor to King Parīkṣit. When he had taken rest as was comfortably seated, he spoke to Vajranābha.**

### Verse 9

*śrī-parīkṣid uvāca*  
*tāta tvat-pitṛbhir nūnam*  
*asmat pitṛ-pitāmahāḥ*  
*uddhṛtā bhūri-duḥkhaughād*  
*ahaṁ ca parirakṣitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Parīkṣit said, “Dear boy, your forefathers certainly delivered my father and grandfathers from many terrible miseries, and also saved me.**

### Verse 10

*na pārayāmy ahaṁ tāta*  
*sādhu kṛtvopakārataḥ*  
*tvām ataḥ prārthayāmy aṅga*  
*sukhaṁ rājye ’nuyujyatām*

**Перевод:**

**“There is nothing I can do to properly repay you, dear boy. So, my child, I request you to make yourself at ease in your kingdom.**

### Verse 11

*kośa-sainyādi-jā cintā*  
*tathāri-damanādi-jā*  
*manāg api na kāryā te*  
*su-sevyāḥ kintu mātaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“You shouldn’t worry at all about your treasury, army and so on, or about subduing enemies and other problems. Just take good care of your mothers.**

### Verse 12

*nivedya mayi kartavyaṁ*  
*sarvādhi-parivarjanam*  
*śrutvaitat parama-prīto*  
*vajras taṁ pratyuvāca ha*

**Перевод:**

**“Leave all your responsibilities to me and forget all your anxieties.” Hearing this, Vajra was extremely gratified and replied.**

### Verse 13

*śrī-vajranābha uvāca*  
*rājann ucitam etat te*  
*yad asmāsu prabhāṣate*  
*tvat-pitropakṛtaś cāhaṁ*  
*dhanur-vidyā-pradānataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Vajranābha said: “O king, it is very appropriate for you to speak to me like this. I was also shown kindness by your father when he gave me knowledge of the science of weapons.**

### Verse 14

*tasmān nālpāpi me cintā*  
*kṣātraṁ dṛḍham upeyuṣaḥ*  
*kintv ekā paramā cintā*  
*tatra kiñcid vicāryatām*

**Перевод:**

**For that reason I have not the slightest anxiety, and I have acquired a solid grasp on the skills of a** ***kṣatirya.*** **But I have one great worry about which you should please give your opinion.**

### Verse 15

*māthure tv abhiṣikto ’pi*  
*sthito ’haṁ nirjane vane*  
*kva gatā vai prajātratyā*  
*yatra rājyaṁ prarocate*

**Перевод:**

**Although I have been installed as king of Mathurā district, I am now sitting in a vacant forest. Where have all the citizens of this district gone? Only when citizens are present is ruling a pleasant task.**

### Verse 16

*ity ukto viṣṇurātas tu*  
*nandādīnāṁ purohitam*  
*śāṇḍilyam ājuhāvāśu*  
*vajra-sandeha-nuttaye*

**Перевод:**

**Told this, to dispel Vajra’s doubt Viṣṇurāta at once called for Śāṇḍilya, the priest of Nanda and the other cowherds.**

### Verse 17

*athoṭajaṁ vihāyāśu*  
*śāṇḍilyaḥ samupāgataḥ*  
*pūjito vajranābhena*  
*niṣasādāsanottame*

**Перевод:**

**Śāṇḍilya quickly left his hut and came. Vajranābha worshiped him and accomodated him on an excellent seat.**

### Verse 18

*upodghātaṁ viṣṇurātaś*  
*cakārāśu tatas tv asau*  
*uvāca parama-prītas*  
*tāv ubhau parisāntvayan*

**Перевод:**

**Viṣṇurāta quickly summarized the question and the sage, fully satisfied, addressed the two kings to settle their doubt.**

### Verse 19

*śrī-śāṇḍilya uvāca*  
*śṛṇutaṁ datta-cittau me*  
*rahasyaṁ vraja-bhūmi-jam*  
*vrajanaṁ vyāptir ity uktyā*  
*vyāpanād vraja ucyate*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Śāṇḍilya said: Both of you listen with fixed attention and hear from me a secret about Vraja-bhūmi. Because the word** ***vrajana*** **means “expanding,” Vraja gets its name from the idea of being all-expansive.**

### Verse 20

*guṇātītaṁ paraṁ brahma*  
*vyāpakaṁ vraja ucyate*  
*sad-ānandaṁ paraṁ jyotir*  
*muktānāṁ padam avyayam*

**Перевод:**

**The all-pervasive Absolute Truth, beyond the material modes, is called Vraja. It is purely blissful, the supreme light, the inexhaustible abode of liberated souls.**

### Verse 21

*tasmin nandātmajaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ*  
*sad-ānandāṅga-vigrahaḥ*  
*ātmārāmaś cāpta-kāmaḥ*  
*premāktair anubhūyate*

**Перевод:**

**There the son of Nanda, Kṛṣṇa, His body and all its limbs purely blissful, the self-satisfied Lord whose desires are always fulfilled, is perceived by those who are endowed with** ***prema.***

### Verse 22

*ātmā tu rādhikā tasya*  
*tayaiva ramaṇād asau*  
*ātmārāmatayā prājñaiḥ*  
*procyate gūḍha-vedibhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhikā is His own self. Wise sages who know the confidential essence of the** ***Vedas*** **call Him “self-satisfied” because He takes His pleasure in Her company.**

### Verse 23

*kāmās tu vāñcitās tasya*  
*gāvo gopāś ca gopikāḥ*  
*nityāḥ sarve vihārādyā*  
*āpta-kāmas tatas tv ayam*

**Перевод:**

**Because His desired enjoyments—His cows, His cowherd men and women, and His pastimes—are eternally present, thus it is said that His desires are always fulfilled.**

### Verse 24

*rahasyaṁ tv idam etasya*  
*prakṛteḥ param ucyate*  
*prakṛtyā khelatas tasya*  
*līlānyair anubhūyate*

**Перевод:**

**This confidential truth about Him is described as beyond the realm of matter. Others perceive the other kind of pastimes He has when He plays with material nature.**

### Verse 25

*sarga-sthity-apyayā yatra*  
*rajaḥ-sattva-tamo-guṇaiḥ*  
*līlaivaṁ dvi-vidhā tasya*  
*vāstavī vyāvahārikī*

**Перевод:**

**In these other pastimes the universe is created, maintained and destroyed by the modes of passion, goodness and ignorance. Thus He has two kinds of pastimes, the real and the mundane.**

### Verse 26

*vāstavī tat-sva-saṁvedyā*  
*jīvānāṁ vyāvahārikī*  
*ādyāṁ vinā dvitīyā na*  
*dvitīyā nādya-gā kvacit*

**Перевод:**

**The real pastimes are known properly only by Himself and His intimate devotees, while the common** ***jīvas*** **can know His mundane pastimes. The second kind cannot occur separate from the first, nor does it ever enter the realm of the first.**

### Verse 27

*āvayor gocareyaṁ tu*  
*tal-līlā vyāvahārikī*  
*yatra bhūr-ādayo lokā*  
*bhuvi māthura-maṇḍalam*

**Перевод:**

**But both of you can see His mundane pastimes, in which the earth and other worlds have appeared, and on the earth the district of Mathurā.**

### Verse 28

*atraiva vraja-bhūmiḥ sā*  
*yatra tattvaṁ su-gopitam*  
*bhāsate prema-pūrṇānāṁ*  
*kadācid api sarvataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In this district is the land of Vraja, where the factual truth is well hidden. It is visible to those who are full of pure love, and occasionally it is visible to everyone.**

### Verse 29

*kadācid dvāparasyānte*  
*raho-līlādhikāriṇaḥ*  
*samavetā yadātra syur*  
*yathedānīṁ tadā hariḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes, at the end of the Dvāpara age, the devotees fit to participate in His confidential pastimes gather together here, as they have just recently, and then Lord Hari Himself appears.**

### Verse 30

*svaiḥ sahāvataret sveṣu*  
*samāveśārtham īpsitāḥ*  
*tadā devādayo ’py anye*  
*’vataranti samantataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**To be with His dear devotees, Kṛṣṇa descends among them. Demigods and others whose help is wanted also descend in various places.**

### Verse 31

*sarveṣāṁ vāñchitaṁ kṛtvā*  
*harir antarhito ’bhavat*  
*tenātra tri-vidhā lokāḥ*  
*sthitāḥ pūrvaṁ na saṁśayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Fulfilling the desires of all of them, Lord Hari has now disapppeared. It can be concluded without a doubt, therefore, that three different kinds of people have been placed here in the past.**

### Verse 32

*nityās tal-lipsavaś caiva*  
*devādyāś ceti bhedataḥ*  
*devādyās teṣu kṛṣṇena*  
*dvārikāṁ prāpitāḥ purā*

**Перевод:**

**These are categorized as the eternal devotees, those who want the same status, and other persons like the demigods. Among them, the demigods and other ordinary persons Kṛṣṇa previously dispatched to Dvārakā.**

### Verse 33-34

*punar mauśala-mārgeṇa*  
*svādhikāreṣu cāpitāḥ*  
*tal-lipsūṁś ca sadā krṣṇaḥ*  
*premānandaika-rūpiṇaḥ*  
*vidhāya svīya-nityeṣu*  
*samāveśitavāṁs tadā*  
*nityāḥ sarve ’py ayogyeṣu*  
*darśanābhāvatāṁ gatāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Later, He sent them back to their assigned responsibilities by the device of the pastime of the fight with clubs. Kṛṣṇa then blessed those who desired to also have spiritual identities, bestowing on them forms of pure, blissful** ***prema*** **and arranging for them to join the company of His eternal devotees. At that time all His eternal devotees became invisible to the unqualified.**

### Verse 35

*vyāvahārika-līlā-sthās*  
*tatra yan nādhikāriṇaḥ*  
*paśyanty atrāgatās tasmān*  
*nirjanatvaṁ samantataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Because there thus remain no participants for earthly pastimes, people who have since come here see this whole place devoid of population.**

### Verse 36

*tasmāc cintā na te kāryā*  
*vajranābha mad-ājñayā*  
*vāsayātra bahūn grāmān*  
*saṁsiddhis te bhaviṣyati*

**Перевод:**

**So you have no cause for anxiety, Vajranābha. Take my order to establish many villages here, and your complete success will be assured.**

### Verse 37

*kṛṣṇa-līlānusāreṇa*  
*kṛtvā nāmāni sarvataḥ*  
*tvayā vāsaya tā grāmān*  
*saṁsevyā bhūr iyaṁ parā*

**Перевод:**

**Name these villages after Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, and bring people to settle them. This land is most worthy to be served.**

### Verse 38

*govardhane dīrgha-pure*  
*mathurāyāṁ mahā-vane*  
*nandi-grāme bṛhat-sānau*  
*kāryā rājya-sthitis tvayā*

**Перевод:**

**You should establish your government at Govardhana, Dīrghapura, Mathurā, Mahāvana and on the great peak of Nandagrāma.**

### Verse 39

*nady-adri-droṇi-kuṇḍādi-*  
*kuñjān saṁsevatas tava*  
*rājye prajāḥ su-sampannās*  
*tvaṁ ca prīto bhaviṣyasi*

**Перевод:**

**If you serve carefully the rivers, hills, villages, lakes, ponds and groves, the citizens will prosper under your rule. You will also feel satisfaction.**

### Verse 40

*sac-cid-ānanda-bhūr eṣā*  
*tvayā sevyā prayatnataḥ*  
*tava kṛṣṇa-sthalāny atra*  
*sphurantu mad-anugrahāt*

**Перевод:**

**You should give all attention to serving this land of eternity, knowledge and bliss. The places of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes will manifest to you by my grace.**

### Verse 41

*vajra saṁsevanād asyā*  
*uddhavas tvāṁ miliṣyati*  
*tato rahasyam etasmāt*  
*prāpsyasi tvaṁ sa-mātṛkaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**And by perfectly serving this land, dear Vajra, you will be able to meet Uddhava. From him you and your mothers will receive the most confidential knowledge.**

### Verse 42

*evam uktvā tu śāṇḍilyo*  
*gataḥ kṛṣṇam anusmaran*  
*viṣṇurāto ’tha vajraś ca*  
*parāṁ prītim avāpatuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Having said this, Śāṇḍilya left, remembering Kṛṣṇa. Viṣṇurāta and Vajra both felt the highest satisfaction.**

## Chapter Two

### Verse 1

*śrī-ṛṣayaḥ ūcuḥ*  
*śāṇḍilye tau samādiśya*  
*parāvṛtte svam āśramam*  
*kiṁ kathaṁ cakratus tau tu*  
*rājānau sūta tad vada*

**Перевод:**

**The sages said: After Śāṇḍilya gave His instructions to the two kings and returned to his** ***āśrama,*** **what did the kings do, and how did they do it? Sūta, please tell us.**

### Verse 2

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*tatas tu viṣṇurātena*  
*śreṇī-mukhyāḥ sahasraśaḥ*  
*indraprasthāt samānāyya*  
*mathurā-sthānam āpitāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sūta said: Viṣṇurāta then gathered thousands of well-bred citizens of Indraprastha and brought them to Mathurā district.**

### Verse 3

*māthurān brāhmaṇāṁs tatra*  
*vānarāṁś ca purātanān*  
*vijñāya mānanīyatvaṁ*  
*teṣu sthāpitavān svarāṭ*

**Перевод:**

**Recognizing the native** ***brāhmaṇas*** **and monkeys of Mathurā as worthy of respect, emperor Parīkṣit settled them among these newcomers.**

### Verse 4-6

*vajras tu tat-sahāyena*  
*śāṇḍilyasyāpy anugrahāt*  
*govinda-gopa-gopīnāṁ*  
*līlā-sthānāny anukramāt*  
*vijñāyābhidhayāsthāpya*  
*grāmān āvāsayad bahūn*  
*kuṇḍa-kūpādi-pūrtena*  
*śivādi-sthāpanena ca*  
*govinda-haridevādi-*  
*svarūpāropaṇena ca*  
*kṛṣṇaika-bhaktiṁ sve rājye*  
*tatāna ca mumoda ha*

**Перевод:**

**With Parīkṣit’s help and the mercy of Śāṇḍilya, Vajra discovered one after another the pastime places of Govinda and His** ***gopas*** **and** ***gopīs.*** **He established many villages, constructed ponds, wells and other public works, and installed deities of Lord Śiva and others. He also installed the Kṛṣṇa Deities of Govinda, Harideva and so on. Thus he spread pure devotion for Kṛṣṇa throughout his kingdom, which gave him great pleasure.**

### Verse 7

*prajās tu muditās tasya*  
*kṛṣṇa-kīrtana-tatparāḥ*  
*paramānanda-sampannā*  
*rājyaṁ tasyaiva tuṣṭuvuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**His citizens were very happy with him. Devoted to** ***kṛṣṇa-kīrtana,*** **they enjoyed the highest bliss and always praised his rule.**

### Verse 8

*ekadā kṛṣṇa-patnyas tu*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-virahāturāḥ*  
*kālindīṁ muditāṁ vīkṣya*  
*papracchur gata-matsarāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Once Kṛṣṇa’s queens, who were tormented by the pain of separation from Śrī Kṛṣṇa, noticed that Kālindī was happy. Without succumbing to jealousy, they inquired from her.**

### Verse 9

*śrī-kṛṣṇa-patnya ūcuḥ*  
*yathā vayaṁ kṛṣṇa-patnyas*  
*tathā tvam api śobhate*  
*vayaṁ viraha-duḥkhārtās*  
*tvaṁ na kālindi tad vada*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s queens said: You are a wife of Kṛṣṇa just like the rest of us, or so you appear in all your grace. We, however, are distressed by the misery of separation while you are not. Please explain this.**

### Verse 10

*tac chrutvā smayamānā sā*  
*kālindī vākyam abravīt*  
*sāpatnyaṁ vīkṣya tat tāsāṁ*  
*karuṇā-para-mānasā*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing this, Kālindī smiled and spoke in response. Since being co-wives might make them feel rivalry, she was only thinking of how to treat them kindly.**

### Verse 11

*śrī-kālindī uvāca*  
*ātmārāmasya kṛṣṇasya*  
*dhruvam ātmāsti rādhikā*  
*tasyā dāsya-prabhāveṇa*  
*viraho ’smān na saṁspṛśet*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Kālindī said: For sure Śrī Rādhikā is the very self of self-satisfied Kṛṣṇa. It is on the strength of being situated in Her service that the pain of separation does not affect us.**

### Verse 12

*tasyā evāṁśa-vistārāḥ*  
*sarvāḥ śrī-kṛṣṇa-nāyikāḥ*  
*nitya-sambhoga evāsti*  
*tasyāḥ sāmmukhya-yogataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**All ṭhe consorts of Śrī Kṛṣṇa are Her partial expansions. His eternal pleasures go on by virtue of Her being present.**

### Verse 13

*sa eva sā sa saivāsti*  
*vaṁśī tat-prema-rūpikā*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-nakha-candrāli-*  
*saṅgāc candrāvalī smṛtā*

**Перевод:**

**He is She, and She is He. His flute is the very form of Their love. The scriptures say that Candrāvalī appears from the contact of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s moonlike fingernails.**

### Verse 14

*rūpāntaraṁ ca gṛhṇānā*  
*tayoḥ sevāti-lālasā*  
*rukmiṇy-ādi-samāveśo*  
*mayātraiva vilokitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Overeager to continue serving the two of Them, I have assumed a different form and have joined the company of Rukmiṇī and the other queens. Thus you see me here.**

### Verse 15

*yuṣmākam api kṛṣṇena*  
*viraho naiva sarvataḥ*  
*kintu evaṁ na jānītha*  
*tasmād vyākulatām itāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Actually, you are never completely separated from Kṛṣṇa. But you are not aware of this and so are experiencing distress.**

### Verse 16

*evam evātra gopīnām*  
*akrūrāvasare purā*  
*virahābhāsa evāsīd*  
*uddhavena samāhitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Similarly, in this same place the** ***gopīs*** **also earlier experienced a mere semblance of pain in separation when Akrūra came here. This pain was remedied by Uddhava.**

### Verse 17

*tenaiva bhavatīnāṁ ced*  
*bhaved atra samāgamaḥ*  
*tarhi nityaṁ sva-kāntena*  
*vihāram api lapsyatha*

**Перевод:**

**If you good women were to meet the same Uddhava here, then you could surely achieve eternal enjoyment with your beloved.**

### Verse 18

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*evam uktās tu tāḥ patnyaḥ*  
*prasannāṁ punar abruvan*  
*uddhavālokanenātma-*  
*preṣṭha-saṅgama-lālasāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sūta said: Thus addressed, Kṛṣṇa’s wives further inquired from the placid Kālindī. There were very eager to gain the association of their dearmost by meeting Uddhava.**

### Verse 19

*śrī-kṛṣṇa-patnya ūcuḥ*  
*dhanyāsi sakhi kāntena*  
*yasyā naivāsti vicyutiḥ*  
*yatas te svārtha-saṁsiddhis*  
*tasyā dāsyo babhūvima*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s wives said: O friend, you are most fortunate to not be feeling deprived of your beloved. Because your life is so successful we want to be your maidservants.**

### Verse 20

*parantūddhava-lābhe syād*  
*asmat-sarvārtha-sādhanam*  
*tathā vadasva kālindi*  
*tal-lābho ’pi yathā bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**But if we could only find Uddhava everything we desire would be achieved. So please tell us, dear Kālindī, how we can meet him.**

### Verse 21

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*evam uktā tu kālindī*  
*pratyuvācātha tās tathā*  
*smarantī kṛṣṇa-candrasya*  
*kalā-ṣoḍaśa-rūpiṇī*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sūta said: Told this, Kālindī, the embodiment of Kṛṣṇa’s sixteenth part, answered them.**

### Verse 22

*sādhana-bhūmir badarī*  
*vrajatā kṛṣṇena mantriṇe proktā*  
*tatrāste sa tu sākṣāt tad-*  
*vayunaṁ grāhayal lokān*

**Перевод:**

**\[Kālindī said:\] Badarī was assigned by Kṛṣṇa as He was departing to be the place for his minister’s spiritual practice. There Uddhava is present in his manifest form, teaching spiritual wisdom to the world.**

### Verse 23

*phala-bhūmir vraja-bhūmir*  
*dattā tasmai puraiva sa-rahasyam*  
*phalam iha tirohitam sat*  
*tad ihedānīṁ sa uddhavo ’lakṣyaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**But earlier Kṛṣṇa granted him Vraja-bhūmi with all its secrets as the land of his perfection. As this perfection remains hidden here, so even now Uddhava is present here unseen.**

### Verse 24

*govardhana-giri-nikaṭe*  
*sakhī-sthale tad-rajaḥ-kāmaḥ*  
*tatratyāṅkura-vallī-rūpeṇ-*  
*āste sa uddhavo nūnam*

**Перевод:**

**At the** ***gopīs’*** **place near Govardhana Hill, Uddhava is indeed present in the form of sprouting plants and creepers, eager to obtain dust touched by the** ***gopīs*****.**

### Verse 25

*ātmotsava-rūpatvaṁ hariṇā*  
*tasmai samarpitaṁ niyatam*  
*tasmāt tatra sthitvā kusuma-*  
*saraḥ-parīsare sa-vajrābhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Lord Hari has blessed him that he will always appear as the festival of His own glories. Therefore you should go for some time to the neighborhood of the Kusuma lake, and take Vajra along with you.**

### Verse 26

*vīṇā-veṇu-mṛdaṅgaiḥ kīrtana-*  
*kāvyādi-sarasa-saṅgītaiḥ*  
*utsava ārabdhavyo hari-*  
*rata-lokān samānāyya*

**Перевод:**

**Bring people who are devoted to Hari and begin a festival of music on** ***vīṇās,*** **flutes and** ***mṛdaṅgas,*** **of** ***kīrtana,*** **poetry and devotional songs.**

### Verse 27

*tatroddhavāvaloko bhavitā*  
*niyataṁ mahotsave vitate*  
*yauṣmākīṇām abhimata-siddhiṁ*  
*savitā sa eva savitānām*

**Перевод:**

**There, as you perform the festival, you will surely catch sight of Uddhava. He will certainly award the success you desire in your endeavors.**

### Verse 28

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*iti śrutvā prasannās tāḥ*  
*kālindīm abhivandya tat*  
*kathayām āsur āgatya*  
*vajraṁ prati parīkṣitam*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Śuka said: Hearing this, the queens were very satisfied. They offered their respects to Kālindī and went to Vajra and Parīkṣit, whom they informed about this.**

### Verse 29

*viṣṇurātas tu tac chrutvā*  
*prasannas tad-yutas tadā*  
*tatraivāgatya tat sarvaṁ*  
*kārayām āsa satvaram*

**Перевод:**

**Whea Viṣṇurāta heard this he was glad. He took the queens with him and went to that place, where without delay he carried out everything.**

### Verse 30

*govardhanād adūreṇa*  
*vṛndāraṇye sakhī-sthale*  
*pravṛttaḥ kusumāmbhodhau*  
*kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanotsavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In the Vṛndāvana forest, at the place of the** ***gopīs*** **not far from Govardhana, by the Kusuma lake, a festival of** ***kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana*** **was begun.**

### Verse 31

*vṛṣabhānu-sutā-kānta-*  
*vihāre kīrtana-śriyā*  
*sākṣād iva samāvṛtte*  
*sarve ’nanya-dṛśo ’bhavan*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***kīrtana*** **was so splendid that the pastimes of the beloved of Vṛṣabhānu’s daughter seemed to appear directly. Everyone in the** ***kīrtana*** **appeared like direct partipants in those pastimes.**

### Verse 32

*tataḥ paśyatsu sarveṣu*  
*tṛṇa-gulma-latā-cayāt*  
*ājagāmoddhavaḥ sragvī*  
*śyāmaḥ pītāmbarāvṛtaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Then, as every watched, Uddhava came out of a grove of grass, bushes and creepers. He wore a flower garland, had a dark-blue complexion, and wore a yellow garment.**

### Verse 33

*guñjā-mālā-dharo gāyan*  
*ballavī-vallabhaṁ muhuḥ*  
*tad-āgamanato reje*  
*bhṛśaṁ saṅkīrtanotsavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Wearing also a necklace of** ***guñjā*** **berries, he was constantly singing about the darling of the cowherd girls. With his arrival the** ***saṅkīrtana*** **festival reached a peak of splendor.**

### Verse 34

*candrikāgamato yadvat*  
*sphāṭikāṭṭāla-bhū-maṇiḥ*  
*atha sarve sukhāmbhodhau*  
*magnāḥ sarvaṁ visasmaruḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He glowed like a jewel embeded in a crystal tower caught in the rays of the moon. They all then became submerged in an ocean of joy and forgot everything else.**

### Verse 35

*kṣaṇenāgata-vijñānā*  
*dṛṣṭvā śrī-kṛṣṇa-rūpiṇam*  
*uddhavaṁ pūjayāṁ cakruḥ*  
*pratilabdha-manorathāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**After a moment they regained their external awareness. Seeing that Uddhava’s appearance was exactly like Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s, they worshiped him. They thought that now their cherished desire was fulfilled.**

## Chapter Three

### Verse 1

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*athoddhavas tu tān dṛṣṭvā*  
*kṛṣṇa-kīrtana-tatparān*  
*sat-kṛtyātha pariṣvajya*  
*parikṣitam uvāca ha*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sūta said: Then, seeing all of them absorbed in** ***kṛṣṇa-kīrtana,*** **Uddhava offered them his respects. He embraced Parīkṣit and addressed him.**

### Verse 2

*uddhava uvāca*  
*dhanyo ’si rājan kṛṣṇaika-*  
*bhaktyā pūrṇo ’si nityadā*  
*yas tvaṁ nimagna-citto ’si*  
*kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanotsave*

**Перевод:**

**Uddhava said: O king, you are most fortunate. You are always full in exclusive devotion to Kṛṣṇa. Indeed, you mind is merged completely in this festival of** ***kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana.***

### Verse 3

*kṛṣṇa-patnīṣu vajre ca*  
*diṣṭyā prītiḥ pravartitā*  
*tavocitam idaṁ tāta*  
*kṛṣṇa-dattāṅga-vaibhava*

**Перевод:**

**By the favor of fortune you have inspired pure love in Kṛṣṇa’s wives and in Vajra. This befits you, dear boy. The beauty and strength of your body was granted by Kṛṣṇa Himself.**

### Verse 4

*dvārakā-stheṣu sarveṣu*  
*dhanyā ete na saṁśayaḥ*  
*yeṣāṁ vraja-nivāsāya*  
*pārtham ādiṣṭavān prabhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Among all the inhabitants of Dvārakā, these queens are without doubt the most fortunate. The Lord ordered Arjuna to settle them in Vraja.**

### Verse 5

*śrī-kṛṣṇasya manaś-candro*  
*rādhāsya-prabhayānvitaḥ*  
*tad-vihāra-vanaṁ gobhir*  
*maṇḍayan rocate sadā*

**Перевод:**

**The moon of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s mind, enriched by the radiance of Śrī Rādhā’s face, shines forever, adorning with its moonbeams this forest of His pastimes.**

### Verse 6

*kṛṣṇa-candraḥ sadā pūrṇas*  
*tasya ṣoḍaśa yāḥ kalāḥ*  
*cit-sahasra-prabhā bhinnā*  
*atrāste tat-svarūpatā*

**Перевод:**

**The moon of Kṛṣṇa is always full. It has sixteen phases, whose brilliant light, divided into thousands of rays, is now present in the form of Her nondifferent expansions.**

### Verse 7

*evaṁ vajras tu rājendra*  
*prapanna-bhaya-bhañjakaḥ*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-dakṣiṇe pāde*  
*sthānam etasya vartate*

**Перевод:**

**O king of kings, in the same way that shatterer of his followers’ fear, lightning-like Vajra, is present in his place by the right side of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s feet.**

### Verse 8

*avatāre ’tra kṛṣṇena*  
*yoga-māyāti-bhāvitā*  
*tad-balenātma-vismṛtyā*  
*sīdanty ete na saṁśayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In this descent Kṛṣṇa has empowered His Yogamāyā to show her full power. Doubtlessly it is by her influence that these devotees have forgotten themselves and are suffering in anxiety.**

### Verse 9

*ṛte kṛṣṇa-prakāśaṁ tu*  
*svātma-bodho na kasyacit*  
*tat-prakāśas tu jīvānāṁ*  
*māyayāpihitaḥ sadā*

**Перевод:**

**Unless Kṛṣṇa becomes visible to them none of them will regain their proper consciousness. But Māyā has now covered the vision of the** ***jīvas*** **from ever seeing Kṛṣṇa again.**

### Verse 10

*aṣṭāviṁśe dvāparānte*  
*svayam eva yadā hariḥ*  
*utsārayen nijāṁ māyāṁ*  
*tat-prakāśo bhavet tadā*

**Перевод:**

**At the end of the twenty-eighth Dvārapa-yuga, when Kṛṣṇa Himself sends forth His bewildering potency, He then becomes visible.**

### Verse 11

*sa tu kālo vyatikrāntas*  
*tenedam aparaṁ śṛṇu*  
*anyadā tat-prakāśas tu*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavatād bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**That time, however, is now finished, and we have entered a different age. But listen and I will tell you another way He can become visible. That is from** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 12

*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ śāstraṁ*  
*yatra bhāgavatair yadā*  
*kīrtyate śrūyate cāpi*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇas tatra niścitam*

**Перевод:**

**Wherever and whenever devotees of Bhagavān recite and listen to the scripture** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam,*** **there for certain Śrī Kṛṣṇa is present.**

### Verse 13

*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ yatra*  
*ślokaṁ ślokārdham eva ca*  
*tatrāpi bhagavān kṛṣṇo*  
*ballavībhir virājate*

**Перевод:**

**Wherever a single verse or even half a verse of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **is heard, the Personality of Godhead Kṛṣṇa shines forth along with the** ***gopīs.***

### Verse 14

*bhārate mānavaṁ janma*  
*prāpya bhāgavataṁ na yaiḥ*  
*śrutaṁ pāpa-parādhīnair*  
*ātma-ghātas tu taiḥ kṛtaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Those who have taken a human birth in the land of Bhārata but have not heard the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **are slaves of sin and commit spiritual suicide.**

### Verse 15

*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ śāstraṁ*  
*nityaṁ yaiḥ parisevitam*  
*pitur mātuś ca bhāryāyāḥ*  
*kula-paṅktiḥ su-tāritā*

**Перевод:**

**The fathers, mothers, wives and whole family lines of those who regularly serve the scripture** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **are all easily delivered from material existence.**

### Verse 16

*vidyā-prakāśo viprāṇāṁ*  
*rājñāṁ śatru-jayo viśām*  
*dhanaṁ svāsthyaṁ ca śūdrāṇāṁ*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavatād bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**On the strength of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, brāhmaṇas*** **develop wisdom, kings conquer their enemies,** ***vaiśyas*** **gain wealth and** ***śūdras*** **become healthy.**

### Verse 17

*yoṣitām apareṣāṁ ca*  
*sarva-vāñchita-pūraṇam*  
*ato bhāgavataṁ nityaṁ*  
*ko na seveta bhāgyavān*

**Перевод:**

**Women and others obtain everything they desire. Thus what fortunate person would fail to regularly serve the** ***Bhāgavatam*****?**

### Verse 18

*aneka-janma-saṁsiddheḥ*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ labhet*  
*prakāśo bhagavad-bhakter*  
*udbhavas tatra jāyate*

**Перевод:**

**As the perfection of many lifetimes, one may obtain** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.*** **In it the revelation of devotion to the Supreme Lord is born.**

### Verse 19

*saṅkhyāyana-prasādāptaṁ*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ purā*  
*bṛhaspatir dattavān me*  
*tenāhaṁ kṛṣṇa-vallabhaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In the past Bṛhaspati received** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **by the grace of Saṅkhyāyana, and Bṛhaspati gave it to me. Because of this I have become dear to Kṛṣṇa.**

### Verse 20

*ākhyāyikāṁ ca tenoktāṁ*  
*viṣṇurāta nibodha tām*  
*jñāyate sampradāyo ’pi*  
*yatra bhāgavata-śruteḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Now hear from me, Viṣṇurāta, a narration Bṛhaspati told me. From this account you can understand the disciplic succession of this Vedic text, the** ***Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 21

*śrī-bṛhaspatir uvāca*  
*īkṣāṁ cakre yadā kṛṣṇo*  
*māyā-puruṣa-rūpa-dhṛk*  
*brahmā viṣṇuḥ śivaś cāpi*  
*rajaḥ-sattva-tamo-guṇaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Bṛhaspati said: When Kṛṣṇa, assuming the form of the Lord of Māyā, the Puruṣa, glanced at her, Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva appeared along with the modes of passion, goodness and ignorance.**

### Verse 22

*puruṣās traya uttasthur*  
*adhikārāṁs tadādiśat*  
*utpattau pālane caiva*  
*saṁhāre prakrameṇa tān*

**Перевод:**

**These three persons rose up and the Lord assigned them their responsibilities to work for generation, protection and destruction.**

### Verse 23

*brahmā tu nābhi-kamalād*  
*utpannas taṁ vyajijñapat*  
*śrī brahmā uvāca*  
*nārāyaṇādi-puruṣa*  
*paramātman namo ’stu te*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā then took another birth from the lotus growing from the Lord’s navel, and inquired from the Lord.**  
**Śrī Brahmā said: O Nārāyaṇa, original person, Supreme Soul, obeisances to You.**

### Verse 24

*tvayā sarge niyukto ’smi*  
*pāpīyān māṁ rajo-guṇaḥ*  
*tvat-smṛtau naiva bādheta*  
*tathaiva kṛpayā prabho*

**Перевод:**

**You have engaged me in creating. By rememberence of You and by Your mercy, O master, may the sinful mode of passion not obstruct me.**

### Verse 25

*śrī-bṛhaspatir uvāca*  
*yadā tu bhagavāṁs tasmai*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ purā*  
*upadiśyābravīd brahman*  
*sevasvainat sva-siddhaye*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Bṛhaspati said: When at that time, long ago, the Personality of Godhead taught him** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*****, he told him, “Dear Brahmā, serve this scripture to achieve your perfection.”**

### Verse 26

*brahmā tu parama-prītas*  
*tena kṛṣṇāptaye ’niśam*  
*saptāvaraṇa-bhaṅgāya*  
*saptāhaṁ samavartayat*

**Перевод:**

**Most pleased, Brahmā took the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **and instituted the constant performance of seven-day recital for achieving Kṛṣṇa and destroying the seven coverings of material existence.**

### Verse 27

*śrīmad-bhāgavata-saptāha-*  
*sevanāpta-manorathaḥ*  
*sṛṣṭiṁ vitanute nityaṁ*  
*sa saptāhaḥ punaḥ punaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Attaining all his desires by observing the seven-day recital of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam,*** **Brahmā expands the creation regularly, week after week, again and again.**

### Verse 28

*viṣṇur apy arthayām āsa*  
*pumāṁsaṁ svārtha-siddhaye*  
*prajānāṁ pālane puṁsā*  
*yad anenāpi kalpitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Viṣṇu also prayed to the Supreme Person for achieving His personal goal, since the Lord had appointed Him to maintain those who are born into the universe.**

### Verse 29

*śrī-viṣṇur uvāca*  
*prajānāṁ pālanaṁ deva*  
*kariṣyāmi athocitam*  
*pravṛttyā ca nivṛttyā ca*  
*karma-jñāna-prayojanāt*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Viṣṇu said: O Lord, as is fit I am going to protect the universe’s progeny, by the paths of fruitive work and renunciation motivated by the goals of good** ***karma*** **and knowledge.**

### Verse 30

*yadā yadaiva kālena*  
*dharma-glānir bhaviṣyati*  
*dharmaṁ saṁsthāpayiṣyāmi*  
*hy avatārais tadā tadā*

**Перевод:**

**Whenever, in the course of time, the principles of religion will decline, I will re-establish** ***dharma*** **by various incarnations.**

### Verse 31

*bhogārthibhyas tu yajñādi-*  
*phalaṁ dāsyāmi niścitam*  
*mokṣārthibhyo viraktebhyo*  
*muktiṁ pañca-vidhaṁ tathā*

**Перевод:**

**I will bestow the sure fruits of sacrifices and other works to those who hanker for sense gratification, and the five kinds of liberation to the renunciants who desire them.**

### Verse 32

*ye ’pi mokṣaṁ na vāñcanti*  
*tān kathaṁ pālayāmy aham*  
*ātmānāṁ ca śriyaṁ cāpi*  
*pālayāmi kathaṁ vada*

**Перевод:**

**But how can I maintain those who do not want even liberation? How will I maintain myself and goddess Śrī? Please tell me.**

### Verse 33

*tasmā api pumān ādyaḥ*  
*śrī-bhāgavatam ādiśat*  
*uvāca ca paṭhasvainat*  
*tava sarvārtha-siddhaye*

**Перевод:**

**To Viṣṇu also the original person taught** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.*** **He told Him, “Study this scripture to achieve all Your goals.”**

### Verse 34

*tato viṣṇuḥ prasannātmā*  
*paramārthaka-pālane*  
*samartho ’bhūc chriyā māsi*  
*māsi bhāgavataṁ smaran*

**Перевод:**

**Then Viṣṇu, satisfied in His heart, became competent to maintain the living beings for their ultimate success. In the company of goddess Śrī He remembered the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **month after month.**

### Verse 35

*yadā viṣṇuḥ svayaṁ vaktā*  
*lakṣmīś ca śravaṇe ratā*  
*tadā bhāgavata-śrāvo*  
*māsenaiva punaḥ punaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When Viṣṇu Himself is the speaker and Lakṣmī is intent on listening, the hearing of the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **goes on repeatedly, completed each time in one month.**

### Verse 36

*yadā lakṣmīḥ svayaṁ vaktrī*  
*viṣṇuś ca śravaṇe rataḥ*  
*māsa-dvayaṁ rasāsvādas*  
*tadātīva suśobhate*

**Перевод:**

**When Lakṣmī herself is the sepaker and Viṣṇu is eager to hear, the relishing of the book takes two months. Then it is especially wonderful.**

### Verse 37

*adhikāre sthito viṣṇur*  
*lakṣmīr niścinta-mānasā*  
*tena bhāgavatāsvādas*  
*tasyā bhūri prakāśate*

**Перевод:**

**When Viṣṇu can properly assume His responsibilities, Lakṣṁī’s mind becomes free from worry. Thus she is seen constantly relishing the** ***Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 38

*atha rudro ’pi taṁ devaṁ*  
*saṁhārādhikṛtaḥ purā*  
*pumāṁsaṁ prārthayām āsa*  
*sva-sāmarthya-vivṛddhaye*

**Перевод:**

**Then Rundra, appointed to the task of destruction, also prayed to the Supreme Lord to increase his ability to do his duty.**

### Verse 39

*śrī-rudra uvāca*  
*nitye naimittike caiva*  
*saṁhāre prākṛte tathā*  
*śaktayo mama vidyante*  
*deva-deva mama prabho*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Rudra said: O Lord of lords, my master, I possess the powers for constant and occasional destruction.**

### Verse 40

*ātyantike tu sāmhāre*  
*mama śaktir na vidyate*  
*mahad duḥkhaṁ mamaitat tu*  
*tena tvāṁ prārthayāmy aham*

**Перевод:**

**I lack the power, however, for ultimate destruction. This causes me great distress, and so I am praying to You.**

### Verse 41

*śrī-bṛhaspatir uvāca*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ tasmā*  
*api nārāyaṇo dadau*  
*sa tu saṁsevanād asya*  
*jigye cāpi tamo-guṇam*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Bṛhaspati said: Lord Nārāyaṇa also gave Rudra** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.*** **By serving it, he conquered the mode of ignorance.**

### Verse 42

*kathā bhāgavatī tena*  
*sevitā varṣa-mātrataḥ*  
*laye tv ātyantike tenā-*  
*vāpa śaktiṁ sadā-śivaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Lord Sadāśiva observed the recitation of the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **in the course of one year, and then He obtained the power for ultimate destruction.**

### Verse 43

*uddhava uvāca*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavata-māhātmya*  
*imām ākhyāyikāṁ guroḥ*  
*śrutvā bhāgavataṁ labdhvā*  
*mumude ’haṁ praṇamya tam*

**Перевод:**

**Uddhava said: Hearing this narration from my** ***guru*** **in glorification of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam,*** **and obtaining the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **from him, I became joyful and bowed down to him.**

### Verse 44

*tatas tu vaiṣṇavīṁ rītiṁ*  
*gṛhītvā māsa-mātrataḥ*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavatāsvādo*  
*mayā samyaṅ niṣevitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter I accepted the Vaiṣṇava custom of relishing** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **in one month, and observed the custom correctly.**

### Verse 45

*tāvataiva babhūvāhaṁ*  
*kṛṣṇasya dayitaḥ sakhā*  
*kṛṣṇenātha niyukto ’haṁ*  
*vraje sva-preyasī-gaṇe*

**Перевод:**

**In that course of one month I became a dear friend of Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa engaged Me in visiting His beloved girlfriends in Vraja.**

### Verse 46

*virahārtāsu gopīṣu*  
*svayaṁ nitya-vihāriṇā*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-sandeśo*  
*man-mukhena prayojitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He who is always enjoying with the** ***gopīs*** **personally sent them the message of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **through my mouth.**

### Verse 47

*taṁ yathā-mati labdhvā tā*  
*āsan viraha-varjitāḥ*  
*nājñāsiṣaṁ rahasyaṁ tac*  
*camatkāras tu lokitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Receiving this message according to their own understanding, the** ***gopīs*** **became freed from the pain of separation. I could not understand the mystery of this, but I saw the surprise they exhibited.**

### Verse 48

*svar-vāsaṁ prārthya kṛṣṇaṁ ca*  
*brahmādyeṣu gateṣu me*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavate kṛṣṇas*  
*tad-rahasyaṁ svayaṁ dadau*

**Перевод:**

**After Brahmā and the other demigods prayed to Kṛṣṇa that He return to His residence in the spiritual sky, and after they departed, Kṛṣṇa personally bestowed this mystery on me through the medium of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 49

*purato ’śvattha-mūlasya*  
*cakāra mayi tad dṛḍham*  
*tenātra vraja-vallīṣu*  
*vasāmi badarīṁ gataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He perfectly imparted this knowledge this to me while sitting at the base of a banyan tree. Thus I now dwell among the creepers of Vraja even after having gone to Badarī.**

### Verse 50

*tasmān nārada-kuṇḍe ’tra*  
*tiṣṭhāmi svecchayā sadā*  
*kṛṣṇa-prakāśo bhaktānāṁ*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavatād bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore I live here always as I desire at Nārada-kuṇḍa. Kṛṣṇa reveals Himself to His devotees through** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 51

*tad eṣām api kāryārthaṁ*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ tv ahaṁ*  
*pravakṣyāmi sahāyo ’tra*  
*tvayaivānuṣṭhito bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**So to achieve the purposes of these devotees I am going to speak** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.*** **You should help me in this.**

### Verse 52

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*viṣṇurātas tu śrutvā tad*  
*uddhavaṁ praṇato ’bravīt*  
*śrī-parīkṣid uvāca*  
*haridāsa tvayā kāryaṁ*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-kīrtanam*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sūta said: Hearing this, Viṣṇurāta bowed down to Uddhava and spoke.**  
**Śrī Parīkṣit said: O Haridāsa, please recite** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 53

*ājñāpyo ’haṁ yathā-kāryaṁ*  
*sahāyo ’tra mayā tathā*  
*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*śrutvaitad uddhavo vākyam*  
*uvāca prīta-mānasaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I am ready to carry out your order in whatever you need me for. I am ready to help you do this.**  
**Śrī Sūta said: Hearing this, Uddhava felt pleased and spoke the following words.**

### Verse 54

*uddhava uvāca*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇena parityakte*  
*bhū-tale balavān kaliḥ*  
*kariṣyati paraṁ vighnaṁ*  
*saṅkārye samupasthite*

**Перевод:**

**Uddhava said: Now that Śrī Kṛṣṇa has abandoned the earth, Kali will become a strong force on its surface. He will create great disturbances as unwanted population increases.**

### Verse 55

*tasmād dig-vijayaṁ yāhi*  
*kali-nigraham ācara*  
*ahaṁ tu māsa-mātreṇa*  
*vaiṣṇavīṁ rītim āsthitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore please go conquer all the directions and subdue Kali. Meanwhile I will observe the one-month Vaiṣṇava custom.**

### Verse 56

*śrīmad-bhāgavatāsvādaṁ*  
*pracārya tvat-sahāyataḥ*  
*etān samprāpayiṣyāmi*  
*nitya-dhāmni madhu-dviṣaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I will thus disseminate the relishing of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **with your help. I will help these devotees attain the eternal abode of the enemy of Madhu.**

### Verse 57

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*śrutvaivaṁ tad-vaco rājā*  
*muditaś cintayāturaḥ*  
*tadā vijñāpayām āsa*  
*svābhiprāyaṁ tam uddhavam*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sūta said: Hearings these words, the king became happy and also anxious. He revealed his mind to Uddhava.**

### Verse 58

*śrī-parīkṣid uvāca*  
*kaliṁ tu nigrahīṣyāmi*  
*tāta te vacasi sthitaḥ*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-samprāptiḥ*  
*kathaṁ mama bhaviṣyati*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Parīkṣit said: Obeying your command, I will certainly subdue Kali. But how will I obtain** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*****?**

### Verse 59

*ahaṁ tu samanugrāhyas*  
*tava pāda-tale śritaḥ*  
*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*śrutvaitad vacanaṁ bhūyo*  
*’py uddhavas tam uvāca ha*

**Перевод:**

**You should be more merciful to me because I have taken shelter at your feet.**  
**Śrī Sūta said: Hearing this statement, Uddhava responded.**

### Verse 60

*uddhava uvāca*  
*rājaṁś cintā tu te kāpi*  
*naiva kāryā kathañcana*  
*tavaiva bhagavac-chāstre*  
*yato mukhyādhikāritā*

**Перевод:**

**Uddhava said: O king, you should not worry at all for any reason. You are the most qualified person to hear the scripture** ***Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 61

*etāvat-kāla-paryantaṁ*  
*prāyo bhāgavata-śruteḥ*  
*vārtām api na jānanti*  
*manuṣyāḥ karma-tatparāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Time has so advanced that now most humans, addicted to material work, have not even heard of the** ***Bhāgavatam śruti*****.**

### Verse 62

*tvat-prasādena bahavo*  
*manuṣyā bhāratājire*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavata-prāptau*  
*sukhaṁ prāpsyanti śāśvatam*

**Перевод:**

**By your grace many humans in this land of Bhārata will obtain** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **and thus achieve perpetual happiness.**

### Verse 63

*nanda-nandana-rūpas tu*  
*śrī-śuko bhagavān ṛṣiḥ*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ tubhyaṁ*  
*śrāvayiṣyaty asaṁśayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The great, powerful sage Śrī Śuka is the very image of the son of Nanda. There is no doubt that he will recite** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **for you.**

### Verse 64

*tena prāpsyasi rājaṁs tvaṁ*  
*nityaṁ dhāma vrajeśituḥ*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-sañcāras*  
*tato bhuvi bhaviṣyati*

**Перевод:**

**By this, O king, you will attain the eternal abode of the Lord of Vraja. And thenceforth** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **will be transmitted throughout the world.**

### Verse 65

*tasmāt tvaṁ gaccha rājendra*  
*kali-nigraham ācara*  
*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*ity uktas taṁ parikramya*  
*gato rājā diśāṁ jaye*

**Перевод:**

**So please go, ruler of kings, and subdue Kali.**  
**Śrī Sūta said: Told this, King Parīkṣit circumambulated Uddhava and went off to conquer the directions.**

### Verse 66

*vajras tu nija-rājyeśaṁ*  
*pratibāhuṁ vidhāya ca*  
*tatraiva mātṛbhiḥ sākaṁ*  
*tasthau bhāgavatāśayā*

**Перевод:**

**Vajra, meanwhile, designated Pratibāhu to rule his kingdom. He remained in that place with his mothers, desiring to hear the** ***Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 67

*atha vṛndāvane māsaṁ*  
*govardhana-samīpataḥ*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavatāsvādas*  
*tūddhavena pravartitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Thus Uddhava conducted the relishing of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **for one month in Vṛndāvana forest near Govardhana.**

### Verse 68

*tasminn āsvādyamāne tu*  
*sac-cid-ānanda-rūpiṇī*  
*pracakāśe harer līlā*  
*sarvataḥ kṛṣṇa eva ca*

**Перевод:**

**As this relishing went on, the pastimes of Hari revealed themselves all around in their transcendental form of eternity, knowledge and bliss. Kṛṣṇa also revealed Himself.**

### Verse 69

*ātmānaṁ ca tad-antaḥ-sthaṁ*  
*sarve ’pi dadṛśus tadā*  
*vajras tu dakṣiṇe dṛṣṭvā*  
*kṛṣṇa-pāda-saroruhe*

**Перевод:**

**Everyone then saw themselves situated within Him, and Vajra saw himself at the right side of Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet.**

### Verse 70

*svātmānaṁ kṛṣṇa-vaidhuryān*  
*muktas tad bhuvy aśobhata*  
*tāś ca tan-mātaraḥ kṛṣṇe*  
*rāsa-rātri-prakāśini*

**Перевод:**

**As Vajra became free from the bereavement of separation from Kṛṣṇa, he and his mothers appeared splendid in that holy land, which exhibits for Kṛṣṇa the nighttime of His** ***rāsa-līla.***

### Verse 71

*candre kalā-prabhā-rūpam*  
*ātmānaṁ vīkṣya vismitāḥ*  
*sva-preṣṭha-viraha-vyādhi-*  
*vimuktāḥ sva-padaṁ yayuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**They saw themseves as rays of the moon’s effulgence and were astonished. Cured of the disease of separation from their beloved, they returned to their eternal abode.**

### Verse 72

*ye ’nye ca tatra te sarve*  
*nitya-līlāntaraṁ gatāḥ*  
*vyāvahārika-lokebhyaḥ*  
*sadyo ’darśanam āgatāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**They and all the others there who belonged to Kṛṣṇa’s eternal pastimes suddenly disappeared from the sight of ordinary people.**

### Verse 73

*govardhana-nikuñjeṣu*  
*goṣu vṛndāvanādiṣu*  
*nityaṁ kṛṣṇena modante*  
*dṛśyante prema-tatparaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In the groves around Govardhana, among the cows in Vṛndāvana and other forests, they enjoy eternally with Kṛṣṇa. Those who are fully dedicated to loving Kṛṣṇa can see them.**

### Verse 74

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*ya etāṁ bhagavat-prāptiṁ*  
*śṛṇuyāc cāpi kīrtayet*  
*tasya vai bhagavat-prāptir*  
*duḥkha-hāniś ca jāyate*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sūta said: Anyone who hears or chants about how these devotees attained the Personality of Godhead will also attain Him and be relieved of all distress.**

## Chapter Four

### Verse 1

*śrī-ṛṣayaḥ ūcuḥ*  
*sādhu sūta ciraṁ jīva*  
*ciram evaṁ praśādhi naḥ*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-māhātmyam*  
*apūrvaṁ tvan-mukhāc chrutam*

**Перевод:**

**The sages said: Well spoken, Sūta! May you live long, and may you long instruct us. These glories of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **we have heard from your mouth are unique.**

### Verse 2

*tat-svarūpa-pramāṇaṁ ca*  
*vidhiṁ ca śravaṇe vada*  
*tad-vaktur lakṣaṇaṁ sūta*  
*śrotuś cāpi vadādhunā*

**Перевод:**

**Please tell us just what the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **is, its extent and the method of studying it. Also tell us now, Sūta, the qualifications of its speaker and hearer.**

### Verse 3

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavatasyātha*  
*śrīmad-bhagavataḥ sadā*  
*svarūpam ekam evāsti*  
*sac-cid-ānanda-lakṣaṇam*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sūta said: The identities of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **and Śrī Bhagavān are always one and the same. They can be described as eternal knowledge and bliss.**

### Verse 4

*śrī-kṛṣṇāsakta-bhaktānāṁ*  
*tan-mādhurya-prakāśakam*  
*samujjṛmbhati yad vākyaṁ*  
*viddhi bhāgavataṁ hi tat*

**Перевод:**

**Know the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **to be the text which is full of revealations of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness for the benefit of the devotees who have attachment to Him.**

### Verse 5

*jñāna-vijñāna-bhakty-aṅga-*  
*catuṣṭaya-paraṁ vacaḥ*  
*māyā-mardana-dakṣaṁ ca*  
*viddhi bhāgavataṁ ca tat*

**Перевод:**

**Its words aim at describing the four topics of knowledge, realization, devotion and the limbs of devotional practice. It very expertly subdues illusion. Know this to be the** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 6

*pramāṇaṁ tasya ko veda*  
*hy anantasyākṣarātmanaḥ*  
*brahmaṇe hariṇā tad-dik*  
*catuḥ-ślokyā pradarśitā*

**Перевод:**

**Who knows its extent? Indeed, its text goes on without limit. Lord Hari showed a small sample of it to Brahmā in four verses.**

### Verse 7

*tad-ānantyāvagāhena*  
*svepsitāvahana-kṣamāḥ*  
*ta eva santi bho viprā*  
*brahma-viṣṇu-śivādayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**By diving into its infinity, dear** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva become competent to fulfill their ambitions.**

### Verse 8

*mita-buddhy-ādi-vṛttīnāṁ*  
*manuṣyāṇāṁ hitāya ca*  
*parīkṣic-chuka-saṁvādo*  
*yo ’sau vyāsena kīrtitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**For human beings whose intelligence and other faculties are limited Vyāsa has recited the famous conversation between Parīkṣit and Śukadeva.**

### Verse 9

*grantho ’ṣṭādaśa-sāhasro*  
*yo ’sau bhāgavatābhidhaḥ*  
*kali-grāha-gṛhītānāṁ*  
*sa eva paramāśrayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**This is the book of eighteen thousand verses known as the** ***Bhāgavatam.*** **It is the ultimate shelter for those who are in the grasp of the sea-monster Kali.**

### Verse 10

*śrotāro ’tha nirūpyante*  
*śrīmad-viṣṇu-kathāśrayāḥ*  
*pravarā avarāś ceti*  
*śrotāro dvi-vidhā matāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Now I will describe its hearers. Dedicated to the narrations of Śrī Viṣṇu’s glories, these hearers are considerd to be of two kinds, superior and inferior.**

### Verse 11

*pravarāś cātako haṁsaḥ*  
*śuko mīnādayas tathā*  
*avarā vṛka-bhūruṇḍa-*  
*vṛṣoṣṭrādyāḥ prakīrtitāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The superior hearers are the sparrow, swan, parrot, fish and others. The inferior hearers are called the wolf, jackal, bull, camel and others.**

### Verse 12

*akhilopekṣayā yas tu*  
*kṛṣṇa-śāstra-śrutau vratī*  
*sa cātako yathāmbhoda-*  
*mukte pāthasi cātakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One who ignores everything else and is only intent on listening to Kṛṣṇa’s scripture is a sparrow, like the sparrow intent on the water released from a cloud.**

### Verse 13

*haṁsaḥ syāt sāram ādatte*  
*yaḥ śrotā vividhāc chrutāt*  
*dugdhenaikyaṁ gatāt toyād*  
*yathā haṁso ’malaṁ payaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The swan is he who takes the essence from variuos scriptures, as a swan extracts pure milk from water mixed with milk.**

### Verse 14

*śukaḥ suṣṭhu mitaṁ vakti*  
*vyāsaṁ śrotṝṁś ca harṣayan*  
*su-pāṭhinaḥ śuko yadvac*  
*chikṣakaṁ pārśva-gān api*

**Перевод:**

**By speaking little, only what is appropriate, the parrot pleases Vyāsadeva, the hearers, the instructor and the audience who are well engaged in reciting the scripture, just like an ordinary parrot.**

### Verse 15

*śabdaṁ nānimiṣo jātu*  
*karoty āsvādayan rasam*  
*śrotā snigdho bhaven mīno*  
*mīnaḥ kṣīra-nidhau yathā*

**Перевод:**

**The eager hearer who while relishing the nectar does not speak for even a moment is like a fish in the ocean of milk.**

### Verse 16

*yas tudan rasikāñ cchrotṝn*  
*virauty ajño vṛko hi saḥ*  
*veṇu-svana-rasāsaktān*  
*vṛko ’raṇye mṛgān yathā*

**Перевод:**

**A fool who makes rude comments and gives trouble to the expert hearers is a wolf, just like the wolf in the forest who disturbs the deer engrossed in the beauty of a flute’s song.**

### Verse 17

*bhūruṇḍaḥ śikṣayed anyāñ*  
*chrutvā na svayam ācaret*  
*yathā himavataḥ śṛṅge*  
*bhūruṇḍākhyo vihaṅgamaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**A** ***bhūruṇda*** **teaches other without himself having actually heard and practiced, like the** ***bhūruṇḍa*** **bird on the peaks of the Himālayas.**

### Verse 18

*sarvaṁ śrutam upādatte*  
*sārāsārāndha-dhīr vṛṣaḥ*  
*svādu-drākṣāṁ khaliṁ cāpi*  
*nirviśeṣaṁ yathā vṛṣaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One who accepts everything he hears, blind to what is important or not important, is a bull, like a bull who sees no difference between tasty grapes and dry oil cakes.**

### Verse 19

*sa uṣṭro madhuraṁ muñcan*  
*viparīte rameta yaḥ*  
*yathā nimbaṁ caraty uṣṭro*  
*hitvāmram api tad-yutam*

**Перевод:**

**He is a camel who rejects what is sweet and instead takes pleasure in the opposite, like a camel who leaves a side sweet mangos and feeds himself with lemons.**

### Verse 20

*anye ’pi bahavo bhedā*  
*dvayor bhṛṅga-kharādayaḥ*  
*vijñeyās tat-tad-ācārais*  
*tat-tat-prakṛti-sambhavaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**There are many other varieties of the two kinds of hearers, like the bee and donkey. They can be understood by their different behaviors, born out of their different characters.**

### Verse 21

*yaḥ sthitvābhimukhaṁ praṇamya*  
*vidhivat tyaktānya-vādo harer līlāḥ*  
*śrotum abhīpsate ’ti-nipuṇo*  
*namro ’tha kḷptāñjaliḥ*  
*śiṣyo viśvasito ’nucintana-paraḥ*  
*praśne ’nuraktaḥ śucir nityaṁ*  
*kṛṣṇa-jana-priyo nigaditaḥ*  
*śrotā sa vai vaktṛbhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One presents himself before the speaker with folded palms as a trusting disciple, who bows down as enjoined, avoids other talks, and is humble, very expert and eager to hear, who is serious to give careful thought to what he hears, who likes to ask questions, and is pure and always affectionate to Kṛṣṇa’s devotees is called by the speakers a proper hearer.**

### Verse 22

*bhagavan-matir anapekṣaḥ*  
*suhṛdo dīneṣu sānukampo yaḥ*  
*bahudhā-bodhana-caturo*  
*vaktā sammānito munibhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One who thinks about the Supreme Lord, who doesn’t have material concerns, who is a compassionate friend to the wretched and is expert in explain things in various ways is honored by sages as a suitable speaker.**

### Verse 23

*atha bhārata-bhū-sthāne*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-sevane*  
*vidhiṁ śṛṇuta bho viprā*  
*yena syāt sukha-santatiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Now hear, dear** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **of the rules for serving** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **in the the land of Bhārata. By following these rules one will enjoy continuous happiness.**

### Verse 24

*rājasaṁ sāttvikaṁ cāpi*  
*tāmasaṁ nirguṇaṁ tathā*  
*catur-vidhaṁ tu vijñeyaṁ*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-sevanam*

**Перевод:**

**There are four ways to serve** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.*** **These should be understood as service in the modes of passion, goodness and ignorance, and transcendental service.**

### Verse 25

*saptāhaṁ yajña-vad yat tu*  
*sa-śramaṁ satvaraṁ mudā*  
*sevitaṁ rājasaṁ tat tu*  
*bahu-pūjādi-śobhanam*

**Перевод:**

**When a busy endeavor like a Vedic sacrifice is made with fanfare for seven days, with pomp and opulent worship, then the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **is being served in the mode of passion.**

### Verse 26

*māsena ṛtunā vāpi*  
*śravaṇaṁ svāda-saṁyutam*  
*sāttvikaṁ yad anāyāsaṁ*  
*samastānanda-vardhanam*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing for a month or a whole season with tasteful relishing and not too much endeavor is in the mode of goodness. It promotes all varieties of ecstasy.**

### Verse 27

*tāmasaṁ yat tu varṣeṇa*  
*sālasaṁ śraddhayāyutam*  
*vismṛti-smṛti-saṁyuktaṁ*  
*sevanaṁ tac ca saukhya-dam*

**Перевод:**

**Service is done in the mode of ignorance when hearing is done over the course of a year, lazily and without much faith, sometimes remembering what is heard and sometimes forgetting. But even this creates happiness.**

### Verse 28

*varṣa-māsa-dinānāṁ tu*  
*vimucya niyamāgraham*  
*sarvadā prema-bhaktyaiva*  
*sevanaṁ nirguṇaṁ matam*

**Перевод:**

**When the regulations are put aside of hearing for a year, a month or some days, and when the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **is served constantly with pure loving devotion, that is considered transcendental.**

### Verse 29

*pārīkṣite ’pi saṁvāde*  
*nirguṇaṁ tat-prakīrtitam*  
*tatra sapta-dinākhyānāṁ*  
*tad-āyur-dina-saṅkhyayā*

**Перевод:**

**It is said that in the discussion between Parīkṣit and Śukadeva the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **was served transcendentally. It is referred to as a seven-day recital only because that was how many days remained in Parīkṣit’s life.**

### Verse 30

*anyatra tri-guṇaṁ cāpi*  
*nirguṇaṁ ca yathecchayā*  
*yathā-kathañcit kartavyaṁ*  
*sevanaṁ bhagavac-chruteḥ*

**Перевод:**

**But whatever the situation, one should by all means, however one can and however one likes, serve the** ***Bhāgavatam śruti*** **either in one of the three modes or in the transcendental mode.**

### Verse 31

*ye śrī-kṛṣṇa-vihāraika-*  
*bhajanāsvāda-lolupāḥ*  
*muktāv api nirākāṅkṣās*  
*teṣāṁ bhāgavataṁ dhanam*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***Bhāgavatam*** **is the wealth of those who hanker only to taste the pleasure of worshiping Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, and who have no aspiration even for liberation.**

### Verse 32

*ye ’pi saṁsāra-santāpa-*  
*nirviṇṇā mokṣa-kāṅkṣiṇaḥ*  
*teṣāṁ bhavauṣadhaṁ caitat*  
*kalau sevyaṁ prayatnataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**But also for those who are tired of being tormented in material life and desire liberation this scripture is the medicine for the disease of birth and death. In this Kali age it should be served with all care.**

### Verse 33

*ye cāpi viṣayārāmāḥ*  
*sāṁsārika-sukha-spṛhāḥ*  
*teṣāṁ tu karma-mārgeṇa*  
*yā siddhiḥ sādhunā kalau*

**Перевод:**

**Even for those who like sense gratification and have hopes to enjoy material life this is the best means in Kali-yuga to achieve success in the path of fruitive work.**

### Verse 34

*sāmarthya-dhana-vijñānā-*  
*bhāvād atyanta-durlabhā*  
*tasmāt tair api saṁsevyā*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavatī kathā*

**Перевод:**

**This fruitive success is very hard to achieve because of lack of capability, wealth and practical knowledge. Therefore such people should also honor the narration of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 35

*dhanaṁ putrāṁs tathā dārān*  
*vāhanādi yaśo gṛhān*  
*asāpatnyaṁ ca rājyaṁ ca*  
*dadyād bhāgavatī kathā*

**Перевод:**

**Narration of the** ***Bhāgavatam*** **bestows wealth, a wife, sons, vehicles and other property, fame, residences and unrivaled sovereignty.**

### Verse 36

*iha loke varān bhuktvā*  
*bhogān vai manasepsitām*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-saṅgena*  
*yānty ante śrī-hareḥ padam*

**Перевод:**

**By the association of** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **these pesons can enjoy benefits in this world, whatever their hearts desire, and in the end go to the abode of Śrī Hari.**

### Verse 37

*yatra bhāgavatī vārtā*  
*ye ca tac-chravaṇe ratāḥ*  
*teṣāṁ saṁsevanaṁ kuryād*  
*dehena ca dhanena ca*

**Перевод:**

**Wherever anyone is serious about hearing the message of the** ***Bhāgavatam,*** **those hearers should be served nicely with one’s bodily faculties and one’s wealth.**

### Verse 38

*tad-anugrahato ’syāpi*  
*śrī-bhāgavata-sevanam*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-vyatiriktaṁ yat*  
*tat sarvaṁ dhana-saṁjñitam*

**Перевод:**

**By the favor of those hearers the person who serves them will also be able to begin serving** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.*** **What we call “wealth” is anything separate from Śrī Kṛṣṇa.**

### Verse 39

*kṛṣṇārthīti dhanārthīti*  
*śrotā vaktā dvidhā mataḥ*  
*yathā vaktā tathā śrotā*  
*tatra saukhyaṁ vivardhate*

**Перевод:**

**There are two kinds of speakers and hearers—those who want Kṛṣṇa and those who want wealth. When the hearer is compatible with the hearer, then their happiness increases.**

### Verse 40

*ubhayor vaiparītye tu*  
*rasābhāse phala-cyutiḥ*  
*kintu kṛṣṇārthināṁ siddhir*  
*vilambenāpi jāyate*

**Перевод:**

**If the two are of opposite natures, however, there will be an inappropriate mixture of tastes and the endeavor will prove fruitless. But even in such a case one who actually wants to attain Kṛṣṇa will succeed eventually.**

### Verse 41

*dhanārthinas tu saṁsiddhir*  
*viddhi sampūrṇatā-vaśāt*  
*kṛṣṇārthino ’guṇasyāpi*  
*premaiva vidhir uttamaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**If this process of hearing** ***Bhāgavatam*** **is completed, he who desires wealth will gain all success. For the transcendentally situated person who desires Kṛṣṇa the only absolute injunction is pure love.**

### Verse 42

*ā-samāpti sa-kāmena*  
*kartavyo hi vidhiḥ svayam*  
*snāto nitya-kriyāṁ kṛtvā*  
*prāśya pādodakaṁ hareḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One who has material desires is enjoined to execute the process to its completion. He should take bath, perform his daily rituals and offer water at the feet of Lord Hari.**

### Verse 43

*pustakaṁ ca guruṁ caiva*  
*pūjayitvopacārataḥ*  
*brūyād vā śṛṇuyād vāpi*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ mudā*

**Перевод:**

**He should worship the book and his spiritual master with regular offerings. Then with pleasure he should speak or hear** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.***

### Verse 44

*payasā vā haviṣyeṇa*  
*maunaṁ bhojanam ācaret*  
*brahmacaryam adhaḥ-suptiṁ*  
*krodha-lobhādi-varjanam*

**Перевод:**

**He should observe silence and take milk or plain** ***kicharī*** **for his meals. He should maintain celibacy, sleep on the floor and avoid anger, greed and so on.**

### Verse 45

*kathānte kīrtanaṁ nityaṁ*  
*samāptau jāgaraṁ caret*  
*brāhmaṇān bhojayitvā tu*  
*dakṣiṇābhiḥ pratoṣayet*

**Перевод:**

**Every day the recital should finish with** ***kīrtana,*** **and at the end one should remain awake through the night. One should feed the** ***brāhmaṇas*** **and satisfy them with gifts of honor.**

### Verse 46

*gurave vastra-bhūṣādi*  
*dattvā gāṁ ca samarpayet*  
*evaṁ kṛte vidhāne tu*  
*labhate vāñchitaṁ phalam*

**Перевод:**

**The spiritual master should be offered cloth, ornaments, and other items, including a cow. Having executed this prescribed procedure, one will achive the desired result.**

### Verse 47

*dārāgāra-sutān rājyaṁ*  
*dhanādi ca yad īpsitam*  
*parantu śobhate nātra*  
*sa-kāmatvaṁ viḍambanam*

**Перевод:**

**He will gain a wife, a kingdom, money, or whatever he wants. But this hearing with material desires is not preferable. It is actually a pretensious show.**

### Verse 48

*kṛṣṇa-prāpti-karaṁ śaśvat-*  
*premānanda-phala-pradam*  
*śrīmad-bhāgavataṁ śāstraṁ*  
*kalau kīreṇa bhāṣitam*

**Перевод:**

**In Kali-yuga, the scripture** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **spoken by the parrot Śukadeva is the means of obtaining Kṛṣṇa. Its bestows the fruit of eternal bliss in pure love.**
