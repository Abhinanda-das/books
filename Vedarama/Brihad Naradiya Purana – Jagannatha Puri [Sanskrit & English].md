# Brihad Naradiya Purana – Jagannatha Puri \[Sanskrit & English\]

## Brihad Naradiya Purana – Jagannatha Puri

***uttara-khaṇḍa***  
***ṣaṣṭitamo ‘dhyāyaḥ***

*vasur uvāca*  
*tato gacched vidhisute / tīrthaṁ yajñāṅga-sambhavam*  
*indradyumna-saro nāma / yatrāste pāvanaṁ śubham 1*

**Перевод:**

***Therefore O daughter of Brahmā, one should go to that bathing place born of the body of Lord Viṣṇu, named Indradyumna-saraḥ. It is very sanctified and causes auspiciousness*****.**

*tatra gatvā śucir vidvān / ācamya manasā harim*  
*dhyātvopasthāya ca vibhuṁ / mantram enam udīrayet 2*

**Перевод:**

***Then after bathing and sipping water, meditating within his mind upon Lord Hari and worshipping that all-pervading Lord one should utter this mantra:***

*aśvamedhāṅga-saṁbhūta / tīrtha sarvāgha-nāśana*  
*snānaṁ tvayi karomy adya / pāpaṁ hara / namo ‘stu te 3*

**Перевод:**

***O holy place, born of the aśvamedha-yajña, who destroy all sin. Now I bathe in your waters Please remove my sins. I bow unto you.***

*evam uccārya vidhivat / snatvā devān ṛṣīn pitṝn*  
*tilodakena vānyāṁś ca / santarpyācampya vāgyataḥ 4*

**Перевод:**

***After thus uttering clearly this mantra in accord with precept, and having satisfied with a tiny portion of that water the devas, sages, forefathers and any others.***

*dattvā pitṛṇāṁ piṇḍāṁś ca / saṁpūjya puruṣottamam*  
*daśāśvamedhikaṁ samyak / phalaṁ prāpnoti mānavaḥ 5*

**Перевод:**

***After offering piṇḍa to the forefathers, and worshipping the Supreme Personality of Godhead, a man achieves the full benefit of performing ten aśvamedha sacrifices.***

*saptāvarān sapta-parān / vaṁśānuddhṛtya devavat*  
*kāmagena vimānena / viṣṇu-lokaṁ sa gacchati 6*

**Перевод:**

***One liberates seven generations before and after oneself and shining like a god one goes to Viṣṇu loka on a airplane which can travel according to one’s desire.***

*bhuktvā tatra varān bhogān / yāvac candrārka-tārakān*  
*cyutas tasmād ihāyāto / mokṣaṁ ca labhate dhruvam 7*

**Перевод:**

***Having enjoyed there blessings and pleasures for as many years as there are moons, suns and stars, he falls down from that place. Then in this world he makes further efforts and surely attains liberation.***

*evaṁ kṛtvā pañca-tīrthīm / ekādaśyām upoṣitaḥ*  
*jyeṣṭha-śukle pañcadaśyāṁ / yah paśyet puruṣottamam 8*

**Перевод:**

***Having thus bathed at the five tīrthas while fasting on the jyeṣṭha-śukla ekādaśī, one can see Puruṣottama (the Supreme Personality of Godhead) on the pañcadaśī (full moon day).***

*sa pūrvokta-phalaṁ prāpya / krīḍitvā cācyutālaye*  
*prāyāti paramaṁ sthānaṁ / yasmān nāvartate punaḥ 9*

**Перевод:**

***Having obtained the benefits which I have formerly mentioned,after sporting in the abode of Acyuta, one now goes to the supreme situation from whence one never again returns.***

*pṛthivyāṁ yāni tīrthāni / saritaś ca sarāṁsi ca*  
*puṣkariṇyas taḍāgāni / vāpyaḥ kūpas tathā hradāḥ 10*  
*nānā nadyaś ca samudrāś ca / saptāhaṁ puruṣottame*  
*jyeṣṭha-śukla-daśamy-ādi / pratyakṣaṁ yānti sarvadā 11*

**Перевод:**

***On the earth as many holy bathing places as there are, including rivers, lakes, ponds of lotus flowers, tanks, wells, and lagoons as well as many streams and the seven oceans all of them become manifest in Jagannātha Purī on the seven days beginning with daśamī on the bright fortnight in the month of Jyeṣṭha.***

*snāna-dānādikaṁ yasmād / devatā-prekṣane sati*  
*nṛbhir yat kriyate tatra / tat sarvaṁ cākṣayaṁ bhavet 12*

**Перевод:**

***Because of this when humans during that time, bathe at these holy places, give charity and take the sacred sight of the Supreme Lord’s Deity form, all of that becomes an imperishable trancendental asset.***

*jyeṣṭha-māse tu daśamī / śukla-pakṣasya mohini*  
*harate daśa pāpāni / tasmād daśa-harā smṛtā 13*

**Перевод:**

***O mohini, the tenth day of bright fortnight in the month of jyeṣṭha, takes away the ten kinds of sins. For this reason it is known as daśa-harā (remover of the ten).***

*yas tasyāṁ halinaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ / paśyed bhadraṁ ca suvrataḥ*  
*sarva-pāpa-vinirmukto / viṣṇu-lokaṁ vrajen naraḥ 14*

**Перевод:**

***That dedicated man who in the*** **daśa-harā** ***looks upon Balarāma, the wielder of the plough, Lord Krṣṇa and Bhadrā their sister, he liberated from all sin can enter the abode of Lord Viṣṇu.***

*naro dolā gataṁ dṛṣṭvā / govindaṁ puruṣottamam*  
*phalgunyāṁ saṁyato bhūtvā / govindasya param vrajet 15*

**Перевод:**

***The man who with controlled senses looks upon Śrī Govinda, the transcendental Personality of Godhead, swung upon a swing in the month of Phalguna, can enter the supreme abode of Govinda.***

*viṣuve divase prāpte / pañca-tīrtha-vidhānataḥ*  
*dṛṣṭvā saṅkarṣaṇaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ / subhadrāṁ ca sulocane 16*  
*naraḥ samasta-yajñānāṁ phalaṁ prāpnoti durlabham*  
*vimuktaḥ sarva-pāpebhyo / viṣṇu-lokaṁ sa gacchati 17*

**Перевод:**

***O girl with beautiful eyes, on the day of the equinox, that man who follows the practice of pañca-tirtha, and sees Ṣaṅkarṣaṇa, Kṛṣṇa and Subhadrā obtains the rarely attained fruit of performing all sacrifices, and freed from all sins he enters the planet of Lord Viṣṇu.***

*yaḥ paśyati tṛtīyāyāṁ / kṛṣṇaṁ candana-rūpinam*  
*vaiśākhasya site pakṣe / sa yāty acyuta-mandiram 18*

**Перевод:**

***He who sees Śrī Kṛṣṇa in the dress of sandalwood on the third day of the dark fortnight in the month of Vaiśākha enters Lord Acyuta’s house.***

*yadā bhaven mahā-jyaiṣṭhī / rāśī-nakṣatra yogataḥ*  
*prayatnena tadā martyair / gantavyaṁ puruṣottamam 19*  
*kṛṣṇaṁ dṛṣṭvā mahā-jyaiṣṭhyāṁ / rāmaṁ bhadraṁ ca mohini*  
*naro dvādaśa-yātrāṇāṁ / phalaṁ prāpnoti cādhikam 20*

**Перевод:**

***When by the conjunction of the full moon and the stars the mahā-jyaiṣṭhī (in the month of Jyeṣṭha), is formed, mortal men should endeavor to go to Puruṣottama Kṣetra (Purī). O Mohini, by seeing Kṛṣṇa, Rāma and Bhadrā on the Mahā-jyaiṣṭhī, a man obtains more benefit than from attending twelve*** **rathayātrās*****.***
