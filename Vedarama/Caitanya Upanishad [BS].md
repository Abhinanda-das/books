# Caitanya Upanishad \[BS\]

### 1

*Atha pippalādah samit-pāṇir bhagavantaṁ brahmāṇam upasanno bhagavan me śubhaṁ kim atra cakṣasveti \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Pippalāda, with fuel in his hands, approached Lord Brahmā, and asked, “O master! Please explain what is auspicious for me.”**

**Комментарий:**

I write this commentary on Caitanya Upaniṣad named Śrī-caitanya-caraṇāmṛta to purify myself, after offering respects to Caitanya, the form of rasa, along with Pañca-tattva.

Caitanya Upaniṣad, which gives bliss to all is included in the beginningless Atharvaveda. It has not been revealed till now, since it wasn invisible to the eyes of materialistic persons. When Caitanya appeared in this world, it has become known by the efforts of the pure devotees. Since, according to Bhāgavatam, the Lord is covered in Kali-yuga (SB 7.9.38), scriptures do not clearly describe Caitanya, who is to be worshipped in Kali-yuga. Thus it is not surprising, unfortunately, that followers of karma and jñāna have no faith in him.

Let us return to the subject. After hearing many scriptures and many types of worship, the sage Pippalāda, desiring to know what was most auspicious for himself, holding firewood in his hand, went to his guru, Brahmā and inquired. “By what will I attain the highest auspiciousness?”

### 2

*Sahovāca bhuya eva tapasā brahmacaryeṇa śaśvat ramasva mano vaśeti \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Engaging in austerity and brahmacārya, control the mind.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahmā said “Creating purity of body and mind by practice of yoga (tapasā) and by pure conduct of renunciation (brahmacaryeṇa) for a whole year, come back.” This was his instruction.

### 3

*Sa tathā bhūtvā bhuya enam upasadyāhā—bhagavan kalau pāpācchannāḥ prajāḥ kathaṁ mucyeran iti \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After purifying himself, Pippalāda again approached Brahmā. “O master! How will the people of Kali-yuga, covered with sin, become liberated?”**

**Комментарий:**

Pippalāda (*saḥ*), becoming pure in body and mind by austerity and brahmacarya, again approached his guru and asked, “In Kali, how will the people, covered with sin by their nature, become liberated?”

### 4

*Ko vā devatā ko vā mantro bruhīti \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“Please explain which devatā to worship or what mantra to chant.”**

**Комментарий:**

O master! Who is the lord for jīvas whose hearts are contaminated by Kali? Please tell me the mantra for worship of the Lord.

### 5

*sahovāca \| rahasyaṁ te vadiṣyāmi—jāhnavī-tīre navadvīpe golokākhye dhāmni govindo dvi-bhujo gauraḥ sarvātmā mahā-puruṣo mahātma mahā-yogī tri-guṇātītaḥ sattva-rūpo bhaktiṁ loke kāśyatīti \| tad ete ślokā bhavanti \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā spoke. I will tell you a secret. On the bank of the Gaṅgā, in the abode Goloka called Navadvīpa, Govinda with two arms, the mahāpuruṣa, soul of all beings, the great soul, the great yogī, beyond the three guṇas, possessing a spiritual form, assuming a golden complexion, will manifest bhakti to the population. There are some verses concerning this.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahmā, who knew all secrets, spoke to him. I will speak to you this secret. In the abode of Navadvīpa, called Goloka, most attractive, which appears on the bank of the Gaṅgā, the soul of all beings (*sarvatmā*), the great puruṣa, the great soul (*mahātmā*), the possessor of great śaktis (*mahā-yogī*), who is beyond the three guṇas, Bhagavān possessing a pure spiritual form (*sattva-rūpaḥ*), two armed Kṛṣṇa, becoming Gaurāṅga, will reveal bhakti in the form of prema to the world (*loke*). This form of Caitanya-deva, who is the mahāpuruṣa, the saviour of Kali-yuga, is explained in the Eleventh Canto of Bhāgavatam:

*Dhyeyaṁ sadā paribhava-ghnam abhīṣṭa-dohaṁ*  
*Tīrthāspadaṁ śiva-viriñci-nutaṁ śaraṇyam*  
*bhṛtyārti-haṁ praṇata-pāla bhavābdhi-potaṁ*  
*vande mahā-puruṣa te caraṇāravindam*

**O great** ***puruṣa*****! O protector of those who bows down! I offer respects to your lotus feet which are the object of constant meditation, which destroy all injury caused by the senses, which award all desires, which fulfill the results of visiting all holy places, which are worshipped by Śiva and Brahmā, which are worthy of surrender, which relieve the devotee of distress, and which act as boat to cross the ocean of material life.**

*Tyaktvā su-dustyaja-surepsita-rājya-lakṣmīṁ*  
*Dharmiṣṭha ārya-vacasā yad agād araṇyam*  
*māyā-mṛgaṁ dayitayepsitam anvadhāvad*  
*vande mahā-puruṣa te caraṇāravindam*

**O great** ***puruṣa*****! I offer respects to the lotus of feet of you who gave up Sītā who was worshipped by the** ***devatās*** **and hard to give up. I offer respects to you who, fixed in** ***dharma,*** **went to the forest, obeying the words of your father, and who pursued a false deer desired by Śitā. SB** [**11.5.33-34**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/5.33)

### 6

*Eko devaḥ sarva-rūpī mahātmā*  
*Gauro rakta-śyāmala-śveta-rūpaḥ*  
*Caitanyātmā sa vai caitanya-śaktir*  
*bhaktāvatāro bhakti-do bhakti-vedyaḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Gaurāṅga, the one Lord, the form of all beings, the great soul, takes forms of red, black and white complexion in other yugas. Taking the form of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, endowed with spiritual śakti, he appears on earth as a devotee. He bestows bhakti and is known through bhakti.**

**Комментарий:**

The golden form (*saḥ*) the mahā-puruṣa, sometimes, in three yugas (satya, treta and dvāparā), becomes white, red, and blackish. This is described by Garga.

*Āsan varṇās trayo hy asya gṛhṇato ’nuyugaṁ tanūḥ*  
*Śuklo raktas tathā pīta idānīṁ kṛṣṇatāṁ gataḥ*

**Your son Kṛṣṇa appears as an incarnation in every millennium. In the past, he assumed three different colors—white, red and yellow—and now he has appeared in a blackish color. SB** [**10.8.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/8.13)

In Kali-yuga he takes the form of a devotee (*bhaktāvataraḥ*). To quickly give auspiciousness to the jīvas of Kali-yuga, inspiring devotees like Śaṭhakopa, Rāmānuja, Viṣṇusvāmī, Madhvācārya, and Nimbārka etc., desiring to give the highest secret, prema with madhura-rasa, the Lord personally takes the form of a devotee Kṛṣṇa Caitanya (*caitanyātmā*). He descended along with his abode Goloka in Bengal, which became the greatest tīrtha. He is understood by bhakti not by jñāna (*bhakti-vedyaḥ*).

### 7

*Namo vedānta-vedyāya kṛṣṇāya paramātmane*  
*sarva-caitanya-rūpāya caitanyāya namo namaḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I repeatedly offer respects to Kṛṣṇa, who is known by Vedānta, who is Paramātmā, the form of all consciousness, and who is Caitanya-deva.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahmā offers respects to Caitanya, on considering his great mercy to the Madhva-sampradāya.

### 8

*vedānta-vedyāṁ puruṣa purāṇaṁ*  
*caitanyātmānam viśva-yoniṁ mahāntam*  
*tam eva viditva‘ti-mṛtyum eti*  
*nānyaḥ panthā vidyate ‘yanāya \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing Caitanya, the primeval person, the source of the universe, the greatest, a person surpasses death. There is no other path for delivering the jīvas.**

**Комментарий:**

Considering all the words of the Vedānta, Brahma speaks to Pippalāda. Caitanya, though taking the form of a devotee, is directly Kṛṣṇa, known by Vedānta. There is no other path for delivering the jīvas (*anayāya*) than taking shelter of Caitanya’s lotus feet in order to enter the spiritual world, after giving up the material world. They cannot be delivered at all by the path of dāsya-rasa revealed by Rāmānujācārya and others. In their works, one sees that by serving in dāsya-rasa, jīvas attain Vaikuṇṭha. Since Caitanya is the source of the universe and can alone teach the subject of Vraja-rasa, one must take shelter of him alone.

### 9

*sva-nāma-mūla-mantreṇa sarvaṁ hlādayati vibhuḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord gives bliss to everyone by the mūla-mantra of his name.**

**Комментарий:**

*Sva-nāma* means Kṛṣṇa’s name, not Caitanya’s name since uttering Caitanya’s name would contradict verse 11 which instructs one to chant the names of Hari and Kṛṣṇa. By the mūla-mantra of these names, the Lord who possesses all powers (*vibhuḥ*) gives bliss to all moving and non-moving beings.

### 10

*Dveśaktī parame tasya hlādinī samvid eva ca \| iti \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord has two supreme śaktis: hlādinī and saṁvit.**

**Комментарий:**

The two śaktis of the Lord are described: hlādinī and saṁvit. By the hlādinī-śakti, spreading bhakti and prema, the Lord gives bliss to all beings. By the saṁvit-śakti, spreading knowledge of the Lord as approved by pure Vedānta, the Lord destroys the root of ignorance in the hearts of the jīvas, which takes various forms of dharma and adharma.

### 11

*Sa eva mūla-mantraṁ japati haririti kṛṣṇaiti rāmaiti \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Caitanya utters the mūla-mantra consisting of the names Hari, Kṛṣṇa and Rāma.**

**Комментарий:**

The supreme Lord in the form of Caitanya (*saḥ*), teaching the jīvas, utters the powerful mūla mantra Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare.

### 12

*Harati hṛdaya-granthiṁ vāsanā-rūpamiti hariḥ*  
*Kṛṣiḥ smaraṇe tac ca ṇas tad-ubhaya-melanam iti kṛṣṇaḥ*  
*Ramayati sarvamiti rāmānanda-rūpaḥ*  
*Atra sloko bhavati \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hari is he who removes the knot in the heart, the impressions of material desire. Kṛṣṇa is he who takes away (*****ṇa*****) all sufferingjust by remembering him (*****kṛṣ*****). Rāma is he who gives pleasure to all beings. He is the form of bliss.There is a verse concerning this.**

**Комментарий:**

The three names are explained. The knot in the heart consists of vāsanās—thirst for material objects which arises from the jīvas forgetting that his nature is to be the servant of Kṛṣṇa. *Bhidyate hṛdaya-granthiś chidyante sarva-saṁśayā*: the knot in the heart is cut and all doubts are destroyed. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad and Bhāgavatam). Hari is he who destroys that knot. The lord of Vraja, endowed with attractive pastimes, is Kṛṣṇa. By conventional usage the word Kṛṣṇa means *yaśodāstanandhaye tamāla-śyāmala-tviṣi*:

**He who has the complexion of a dark** ***tamāla*** **tree and who drank milk from the breast of Yaśodā. Nāma-kaumudī**

From this meaning quoted in the Sandarbhas, other meanings are insignificant. Rāma means he who gives pleasure to all beings.

By this mantra, the jīva gives up his bondage and becomes a spiritual assistant in Vṛndāvana pastimes. Then the jīva attains the highest pleasure of the rāsa dance etc.

### 13

*mantro guhyaḥ paramo bhakti-vedyaḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The mantra is most secret and known by bhakti.**

**Комментарий:**

This secret mantra is the highest dharma of mankind since it is realized only by persons having pure bhakti, and not by persons having bhakti mixed with karma or jñāna as explained in Bhāgavatam.

*Sa vai puṁsāṁ parodharmo yatobhaktir adhokṣaje \|*  
*Ahaituky apratihatā yayātmā suprasīdati \|\|*

**The supreme** ***dharma*** **for all human beings (*****sādhana bhakti*****) is that by which** ***prema-bhakti*** **to the Lord arises, which is not caused by anything other than itself, cannot be obstructed, and which satisfies the mind completely. SB** [**1.2.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/2.6)

### 14

*Nāmāny aṣṭāvaṣṭa ca śobhanāni tāni nityaṁ ye japanti dhīras te vai māyām atitaranti nānyaḥ \| paramaṁ mantra parama-rahasyaṁ nityam āvartayanti \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who chant these sixteen glorious names completely surpass māyā. Others do not. Even the nitya-siddhas chant this supremely secret mantra at all times.**

**Комментарий:**

It was shown in verse 12 that the mantra gives results even for those performing sādhana. By constantly chanting this mantra of sixteen names, the sādhakas cross over māyā excellently (*atitaranti*). Even nitya-siddhās constantly chant this mantra as their very nature.

### 15

*Caitanya eva saṇkarṣaṇo vāsudevaḥ parameṣṭhī rudraḥ śakro bṛhaspatiḥ sarve devāḥ sarvāṇi bhūtāni sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca yat kiñcit sad-asat kāraṇaṁ sarvam \| tad atra ślokāḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Caitanya is Saṅkarṣaṇa, Vāsudeva, Brahmā, Śiva, Indra, Bṛhaspati, all devatās, all beings, moving and non-moving. He is the cause of everything--whatever exists gross or subtle. There are verses concerning this.**

**Комментарий:**

The devotees do not care for worshipping devatās.

### 16

*Yat kiñcid asad bhuṅkte kṣaraṁ tat kāryam ucyute \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Whatever is experienced as impermanent is called a temporary effect.**

**Комментарий:**

This universe is temporary, since it is an effect.

### 17

*sat-kāraṇaṁ paraṁ jīvas tad akṣaram itīritam \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva is eternal and the cause. It is thus called akṣara or permanent.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva is eternal and is the cause of the temporary. Because of forgetting his nature, the jīva by māyā thinks himself a doer. He is actually eternal or akṣara.

### 18

*Kṣarākṣarābhyāṁ paramaḥ sa eva puruṣottamaḥ*  
*Caitanyākhyaṁ paraṁ tattvaṁ sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The supreme Lord is superior to temporary matter and the jīva. Caitanya is the supreme truth, the cause of all causes.**

**Комментарий:**

The universe is temporary *(kṣara*) and the jīva is eternal (*akṣara*). Superior to these is the cause of all causes called Caitanya, the supreme person (*puruṣottamaḥ*). By this statement, the three existing objects explained in Vedānta are confirmed.

### 19

*Ya enaṁ rasayati bhajati dhyāyati sa pāpmānāṁ tarati sa pūto bhavati, sa tattvaṁ jānāti, sa tarati śokam \| gatiṣ tasyāste nānyasyeti \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who has affection for Caitanya, who worships him and meditates upon him becomes free of all sins. He becomes purified. He knows the truth. He becomes free of lamentation. He attains the goal whereas others do not.**

**Комментарий:**

The devotee who thinks of Caitanya as the give of madhurya-rasa in Vraja, who serves Caitanya while accepting a suitable svarūpa, and who meditates on Caitanya, surpasses all sins and all ignorance. Surpassing ignorance, he attains purity with a spiritual form. He knows the truth – acintya-bhedābheda of the śakti and śaktimān. He surpasses lamentation.

The lowly Bhaktivinoda has composed this commentary on the Upaniṣad in the four hundredth year of Caitanya on the bank of the Gaṅgā. The worshippers of Caitanya should mercifully place the dust of their feet, which give all auspiciousness, on his head.

This completes the commentary on Caitanya Upaniṣad called Śrī-caitanya-caraṇāmṛta.
