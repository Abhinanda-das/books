# Camatkāra-candrikā \[BS\]

## Chapter 1

*yat kāruṇyaṁ śuci-rasa-camatkāra-vārāṁ-nidhīṁs tān*  
*nṛbyho rādhā-girivara-bhṛtoḥ sparśayet tarṣayen naḥ \|*  
*tasyaivaikaṁ pṛṣatam acirāl labdhum ākāṅkṣi-dānaiḥ*  
*so’vyān mṛtyor daśana-vitateḥ kṛṣṇa-caitanya-rūpaḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**May the Lord in the form of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, whose mercy allows us humans to touch the great astonishing ocean of mādhurya-rasa of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa and makes us thirsty for it protect us from the set of teeth of death by continual bestowal of hope to quickly attain a drop of that ocean.**

*mātaḥ prātaḥ kim iha kuruṣe nayate peṭikeyaṁ*  
*yatnād asyāṁ kim iha nihitaṁ kiṁ tavānena sūno \|*  
*jñātavyena praṇaya-sakhibhiḥ khela gehād bahis tvaṁ*  
*jijñāsā me bhavati mahatī brūhi no cen na yāmi \|\|2\|\|*

**Yaśodā brought a small box. “O Mother! What are you doing with this in the morning? What have you put in the box?” “O son! It does not concern you.” Telling him this she then said, “Go out of the room and play with our friends.” “I am very curious. Please tell me. Otherwise I will not leave.”**

*asyāṁ candana-candra-paṅkaja-rajaḥ kastūrikā kuṅkumāny*  
*aṅgānām anulepanārtham atha tan nepathya-hetos tathā \|*  
*kāñcī kuṇḍala-kaṅkaṇādy-anupamaṁ vaidūrya-muktā-harid-*  
*ratnāny ambara-jātam apy atimahārghyaṁ kramād vartate \|\|3\|\|*

**“In the box there are sandalwood, camphor, lotus pollen, musk and kuṅkuma for smearing on the body. For decorating the body there are the best belts, earrings, bangles. Vaidurya gems, pearls and emeralds. There is very valuable cloth as well.”**

*ambedaṁ nidadhāsi kiṁ mama kṛte rāmasya vā nandana*  
*brūmas tām avadhehi yātu bhavato hetoḥ kṛtā peṭikā \|*  
*sānyāto’pi bṛhaty anarghya-maṇi-bhāg evaṁ balasyāparā*  
*tat kasmiṁś ca na te janany urur iyān sneho yato yāsyati \|\|4\|\|*

**“Are you putting this in the box for me or for Balarāma” “O son! I will tell you. Please listen. I have made a box bigger than any other for you, filled with priceless jewels. I have made another box for Balarāma.” “O mother! It seems you have great affection for someone.”**

*asmat-puṇya-tapaḥ-phalena vidhinā datto’si mahyaṁ yathā*  
*mat-prāṇāvana-hetave vraja-purālaṅkāra-sūno tathā \|*  
*kanyā kācid ihāsti man-nayanayoḥ karpūra-vartiḥ parā*  
*tasyā ambara-maṇḍanādi-dhṛtaye seyaṁ kṛtā peṭikā \|\|5\|*

**“Brahmā has given to me as a result of my pious austerities a person like the son who ornaments the town of Vraja in order to give joy to my life. One maiden is like soothing camphor lamp for my eyes. I made this box for holding her cloth and ornaments.”**

*kāsau kasya kutastarāṁ janani vā yasyām atisnihyasi*  
*kvāste tad vada sarvam eva śṛṇu bho yā me sakhī kīrtidā \|*  
*tasyāḥ kukṣi-khaner anarghyam atulaṁ māṇikyam etat prabhā*  
*vīcībhir vṛṣabhānum ujjvalayate mūrtaṁ tadīyaṁ tapaḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**“O mother! Who is she? Who is her father? What are the qualities of this girl for whom you have such great affection? Where does she live? Please tell me everything about her.” “Please listen. She is the priceless, unequalled gem from the womb of my friend Kīṛtidā. The personification of his austerity, she lights up Vṛṣabhānu (the sun in Taurus sign), personification of heat, with the waves of her effulgence.**

*saundaryāṇi suśīlatā gurukule bhaktis trapā-śālitā*  
*sāralyaṁ vinayitvam ity adhi dharaṁ ye brahma-sṛṣṭā guṇāḥ \|*  
*te tatraiva mahattvamā purathame snehas tu naisargikaḥ*  
*sārādhety atha gātram utpulakitaṁ kṛṣṇo’ṁśukenāpy adhāt \|\|7\|\|*

**“All the qualities which Brahmā created on earth, such as beauty, good manners, devotion to elders, shyness, sincerity and humility become the greatest and most complete in her alone. I have spontaneous affection for her. She is Rādhā.” Kṛṣṇa’s hairs stood on end. But he hid that with his cloth.**

*sā patyuḥ sadane’sti samprati patiś cāsyā ihaivāgato*  
*goṣṭhendreṇa samaṁ svagaihika-kṛte vyāsaṅa-hetor bahiḥ \|*  
*āste saṁsadi yarhi vīkṣitum ayaṁ mām eṣyati prītito*  
*vakṣyāmy enam imāṁ vahan nija-gṛhaṁ tāṁ prāpayan yāsyati \|\|8\|\|*

**“She lives in the house of her husband. Her husband has come here now. He is outside with Nanda, busy with some household affairs. When he is in the assembly hall he will come to see me out of affection. I will tell him to take this box home and give it to his wife.”**

*atrāntare nikaṭam āgatayā lavaṅga-*  
*vatyā drutaṁ nijagāda śṛṇu goṣṭharājñi \|*  
*āhūta-pūrvam iha yat tad idaṁ suvarṇa-*  
*kāra-dvayaṁ kalaya raṅgana-ṭaṅkanākhyam \|\|9\|\|*

**Lavaṅgavatī then entered the room and approached, saying, “O Queen! Please listen. You must meet two goldsmiths called Raṅgana and Taṅkana whom you had previously called here.”**

*śrutvaitad ākṛtam uvāca tato vrajeśā*  
*kṛṣṇasya kuṇḍala-kirīṭa-padāṅgadādi \|*  
*nirmāpayantya cirato bahir emi yāvat*  
*tvaṁ peṭikāṁ naya gṛhāntarato dhaniṣṭhe \|\|10\|\|*

**Hearing of this event, Yaśodā then spoke. “O Dhaniṣṭhā! While I go outside for some time to have the goldsmiths make earrings, crown and armlets, you bring the box from inside the house.”**

*ity uktvāsyāṁ gatāyāṁ subala-mukha-suhṛt-svāgateṣv ātma-modais*  
*taiḥ sākaṁ mantrayitvā kim api rahasitāṁ peṭikām udghāṭayya \|*  
*niṣkaṣātaḥ samastaṁ maṇi-vasana-kulādy arpayitvā dhaniṣṭhā*  
*pānau tasyāṁ praviśya svayam atha sakhibhir mudrayāmāsa tāṁ saḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**After she said this and went away, Kṛṣṇa’s friends headed by Subala arrived. Kṛṣṇa discussed with them and then opened the box stealthily. Taking out all the jewels and cloth he gave it all to Dhaniṣṭhā’s hands and got inside the box. With his friends he closed the box.**

*dvi-tri-kṣaṇo pari tataḥ praṇamantam etya*  
*tatrābhimanyum abhivīkṣya puro yaśodā \|*  
*pṛṣṭvāśam āha śṛṇuto bhavato gṛhiṇyā*  
*hetoḥ kṛtādya-maṇi-maṇḍana-peṭikeyam \|\|12\|\|*

**Returning after a few moments, Yaśodā saw Abhimanyu offering respects in front of her. Asking about his health, she then said, “Please listen! Here is a box of jewels and decorations for your wife that I prepared today.**

*asyām anarghya maṇikāñcana-dāma-vāsaḥ*  
*kastūrikādy-atimanoharam asti vastu \|*  
*nānyatra viśvaśimi tena vahatvam eva*  
*gatvā gṛhaṁ nibhṛtam arpaya rādhikāyai \|\|13\|\|*

**“In the box there are priceless belts and cords made of gems, cloth, musk and other attractive items. I do not trust anyone. Therefore only you should carry it. Go to your house and give it to Rādhikā privately.**

*sandeṣṭavyam idaṁ mad-akṣi sukhade śrī-kīrtidā-kīrtide*  
*rādhe preṣita-peṭikāntara-gatenātyujjvala-jyotiṣā \|*  
*tvad-gātrocita-maṇḍanena nitarāṁ tvad-vallabhena sphuṭaṁ*  
*tvaṁ śṛṅgāravatī sadā bhava ciraṁ jīvātisaubhāgyataḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**“Give her this message: O Rādhā who gives joy to my eyes! O fame of Kīrtidā! May you always be decorated nicely with ornaments contained in the box I sent! The dear ornaments are suited to your body and shine brightly. May you live long with great auspiciousness!”**

*śrutvaitat tvaritaṁ vrajeśvarī yathaivājñā taveti bruvan*  
*dhṛtvā mūrdhani peṭikāṁ sva-bhavanaṁ prītyābhimanyur yadā \|*  
*gantuṁ prakramate sma tarhy abhisaran kṛṣṇas tam āruhya tad-*  
*bhāryāṁ hanta nija-priyāṁ smitam adhāt svaṁ kautukābdhau kiran \|\|15\|\|*

**Hearing this, Abhhimanyu said “O queen of Vraja! I will do as you have ordered.” When he placed the box on his head with affection and started off for home, Kṛṣṇa who was on his head got the chance to meet his wife, dear to Kṛṣṇa. He smiled, exuding an ocean of pleasure.**

*gopaḥ so’pi mudā hṛd-āha tad ahaṁ dhanyaḥ kṛtārtho’smi yan*  
*mañjūṣāntar ihāsti kāñcana-maṇi-rāśī mahā-durlabhaḥ \|*  
*bhārād eva mayānumīyata itaḥ krīṇāmi koṭir gavāṁ*  
*yad govardhana-mallavan mama gṛhe lakṣmīr bhavitrī parā \|\|16\|\|*

**Abhimanyu said with a joyful heart, “I am most fortunate. My desire is fulfilled. In the box there are heaps of gold and jewels, very hard to attain. I can guess that from that weight that I can buy millions of cows. Just like Govardhana Malla (husband of Candrāvalī) I will have the supreme Lakṣmī in my house.”**

*goṣṭhādhīśa purād vrajan sva-nīlayābhyāsāvadhi-sthānam*  
*apy ārohat pulakollasat tanur ati prīti-plutākṣi-dvayaḥ \|*  
*tādṛg-bhāra-śirā api kṣaṇam api glāniṁ sa naivānvabhūt*  
*pūrṇānanda-ghanaṁ vahan katham aho jānāti vartma-śramam \|\|17\|\|*

**Going from Nanda’s palace, he came to his accustomed house. His hairs were standing on end and his eyes were wide with joy. Though he was carrying such a heavy load on his head, he did not experience fatigue in the slightest. In carrying Kṛṣna, the very form of full bliss, how could he experience fatigue on the path?**

*gatvā paraṁ sva-jananīṁ jaṭilām uvāca*  
*mātaḥ śubha-kṣaṇatayaiva gṛhād agacchan \|*  
*paśyādya kāṁcana-maṇi-vasanādi-pūrṇā*  
*labdhātibhāgya-bharataḥ kila peṭikeyam \|\|18\|\|*

**After returning home he said to his mother Jaṭilā, “O mother! I left the house at an auspicious moment today. Look! I have obtained a box full of gold, jewels and cloth by great good fortune.**

*dattvā svayaṁ vraja-vadhūś ca tava snuṣāyai*  
*śṛṅgāra-hetava ihāpratima-prasādam \|*  
*kurvāṇa yā sapadi tāṁ pratipādyam ekaṁ*  
*proce sma tat kalaya sāpi śṛṇoty adūre \|\|19\|\|*

**“Yaśodā has given this out of affection for you. By incomparable mercy, in the box there are many items for decorating Rādhā.” Composing a verse at that instant, she said, “Read it and she will listen at a short distance.”**

*hṛdāha tuṣṭā jaṭilātibhadram*  
*abhūd idaṁ sāmpratam eva dṛṣṭvā \|*  
*vadhūr bhaviṣyaty atisuprasannā*  
*putreṇa me labdha-nijopacārā \|\|20\|\|*

**Jaṭilā was satisfied and said in great joy, “This has been very fortunate. Seeing this, your wife will become very happy at having obtained this gift from my son.”**

*smitātha sāspaṣṭam uvāca sūno*  
*snuṣā tathāhaṁ bhavataḥ svasā vā \|*  
*na pārayiṣyaty atibhāram etad*  
*itaḥ samutthāpayituṁ kadāpi \|\|21\|\|*

**Slightly smiling she said, “O son! I will never be able to lift this heavy box, and nor can your wife or sister.**

*mañjūṣikāṁ tat tvam ito gṛhītvā*  
*śayyā-gṛhāntar vṛṣabhānu-putryāḥ \|*  
*vedyāṁ nidhāyaihi yathodghaṭaya*  
*semāṁ priyaṁ maṇḍanam āśu paśyet \|\|22\|\|*

**“You must take this box, place it on the table of Rādhā in the bedroom, and come back here so that she can open it and immediately behold the precious ornaments.”**

*atrāntare sahacarīṣv atiharṣiṇīṣu*  
*rādhā rahasy amala-dhīr lalitām uvāca \|*  
*adyāli-vāma-kuca-dor-nayanoru-cāru*  
*kiṁ spandate mama vadety atha sā jagāda \|\|23\|\|*

**Within the room while her friends became overjoyed, Rādhā, who had pure intelligence, spoke to Lalitā on the side. “O friend! Please tell me why my left breast, arm, eye and thigh are quivering today?” Lalitā then spoke.**

*manye manoharam ihāsti maṇīndra-bhūṣā*  
*jātaṁ svayaṁ vraja-patnyāpy ata eva dattam \|*  
*tat-prāpti-rūpa-śubha-sūcaka eva rādhe*  
*spandātisaubhaga-bharāvadhi-hetur eṣaḥ \|\|24\|\|*

**“I think that something very attractive, having ornaments made of the king of jewels is in the box, since it is given by the wife of Nanda herself. O Rādhā! The quivering indicates the auspicious event of attaining that object. It is the cause of the greatest fortune.”**

*dṛṣṭvaiva man-manasi kiñcana bhāvam eṣā*  
*mañjūṣikaiva lalite vitanoti bāḍham \|*  
*udghāṭayāmi tad imām adhunaiva vīkṣya*  
*saubhāgyadaṁ kim api bhūṣaṇa-ratnam asti \|\|25\|\|*

**“O Lalitā! Just on seeing it, the box produces some indescribable emotion in my mind. I will immediately open the box and see if there is some auspicious jewel ornament inside.”**

*itthaṁ sakhīṣu sakalāsu tad-utsukāsu*  
*tāṁ peṭikām abhita eva samāsitāsu \|*  
*draṣṭuṁ gatāsu niviḍatvam atha svayaṁ sā*  
*dāmāny udasya rabhasād udghāṭayat tām \|\|26\|\|*

**While the friends gathered around the box closely, eager to see, Rādhā untied the ropes and quickly opened the box.**

*yāvat kim etad iti tā ahaheti nocur*  
*yāvad bhṛśaṁ jahasur eva sva-hasta-tālam \|*  
*yāvat trapā sahacarī pratibodham āpa*  
*yāvat pramoda-laharī śatam ullalāsa \|\|27\|\|*

**“Ah! What is this?” they said and burst into laughter, while clapping their hands. Rādhā’s companion called shyness arose while many waves of delight manifested.**

*yāvan nivāraṇam aṅgam anaṅga-nakro*  
*jagrāsa yāvad atisambhramam āpa puṣṭim \|*  
*tat-pūrvam eva sahasā tata utthitaḥ sa*  
*sarvāḥ kalā-nidhir aho yugapac cucumba \|\|28\|\|*

**The crocodile of Cupid (desire) seized her opposing limbs, while her confusion and embarrassment increased. Previous to this, Kṛṣṇa, the ocean of arts, had boldly stood up and kissed all the gopīs simultaneously.**

*dhanyaṁ bhūṣaṇam astu te gṛha-patir dhanyo yad ānītavān*  
*dhanyā goṣṭha-maheśvarī sakhi yayā snehād idaṁ preṣitam \|*  
*svaṁ śṛṅgāravatī bhaveti ca punar dhanyaiva sandeśa-vāk*  
*dhanyaṁ geham idaṁ yad ety nibhṛtaṁ mañjūṣikā khelati \|\|29\|\|*

**“May the ornament be glorious! May your husband who brought the ornament be glorious! O friend! May the queen of Vraja who sent the ornament be glorious! May the message she sent “Ornament yourself!”be glorious! May the house where this box plays secretly be glorious!**

*goṣṭheśā nidideśate bahutara-snehātatas te patiḥ*  
*śvaśrūr āli tad anvatīva rabhasād datvaiva mañjūṣikām \|*  
*tvaṁ śṛṅgāravatī bhavet ayi guru-trayyā vacaḥ-pālanaṁ*  
*gāndharve kuru sarvatheti lalitā vāṇy ātha sā tatrape \|\|30\|\|*

**“Yaśodā instructed you to wear the ornament out of great affection. O friend! Your husband and mother-in-law, having given the box, also agree. O Rādhā! You should obey the words of your three elders exactly.” When Lalitā spoke in this way, Rādhā became shy.**

*mañjūṣikāntar iha me bahu-ratna-bhūṣā*  
*āsan svayaṁ vraja-payā sakhi yā vitīrṇā \|*  
*saṁrakṣya tāḥ kvacana dhūrta iha praviṣṭaś*  
*cauro ‘yam asti tad idaṁ vada bho mad-āryām \|\|31\|\|*

**“My previous jewel ornaments were in the box but were taken out and hidden somewhere by the cowherd boy. The rascal has got into the box. He is a thief. Please tell this to my mother-in-law.”**

*rādhābhisārin abhimanyu-vāhana!*  
*kṣitiṁ satī-śūnyatamāṁ cikīrṣuḥ \|*  
*prayaccha ratnābharaṇāni śīghraṁ*  
*no ced ihāryām aham ānayāmi \|\|32\|\|*

**Lalitā said, “O Kṛṣna, the person desiring to meet Rādhā! You used Abhimanyu as your vehicle! You desire to rid the earth of chaste women! Immediately bring back the jewel ornaments. If you do not do so, I will bring Jaṭilā here.”**

*dhūrtā sakhī te lalite sva-kṛtye*  
*dakṣāvahitthām adhunā lalambe \|*  
*mām ānayat preṣya patiṁ balād yā*  
*mañjūṣikāntaḥ kutukād vasantam \|\|33\|\|*

**Kṛṣṇa said, “O Lalitā, your friend is devious! She is skilful in her actions. She is now hiding the truth. She sent her husband and brought me here by force when I got into the box out of curiosity.**

*mañjūṣāyāḥ saurabhaṁ vīkṣa tasyā*  
*vastudasya prāpayaṁs tāṁ dhaniṣṭhām \|*  
*tatra prītyā prāviśaṁ svaṁ sugandhī*  
*kartuṁ daivād ānayan māṁ patis te \|\|34\|\|*

**“Seeing the fragrance of the box, I removed the contents of the box and gave it all to Dhaniṣṭhā. Attracted to the fragrance, I got into the box to make myself fragrant. By chance your husband brought me here.**

*nyāyaṁ sakhyo nau kurudhvaṁ yad asyā*  
*doṣaḥ syāc ced astu daṇḍyā mameyam \|*  
*no ced yuṣmad-dor-bhujāṅgogra-pāśair*  
*baddha sthāsyāmy atra gamyaṁ tri-rātram \|\|35\|\|*

**“O friends! Please judge whether it is her fault. If so, I should punish her. If it is my fault, I will remain here for the next three nights tied up by the terrifying ropes of your snake-like arms.”**

*yasyaivaṁ vibhavena tan-nava-yuva-dvandvaṁ sphurad yauvanaṁ*  
*saukhyāny akṣi-cakorikāḥ śara-tatiṁ kāmo rasāḥ svādanam \|*  
*dhyānaṁ bhakta-tatiḥ sadā kavi-kulaṁ svīyā vicitrā giraḥ*  
*kīrtiṁ kṣmā-bhuvaneṣu sādhu-saphalī-cakre numas tat param \|\|36\|\|*

**I offer my respects to the couple endowed with new youth, by whose influence shining youth has become successful, by whose influence the sakhīs with cakora eyes attained great happiness, by whose influence Cupid put his arrows to best use, by whose influence rasas manifested their highest taste, by whose influence the devotees became satisfied with their meditation, and by whose influence the poets have made their colorful words meaningful and the fame of the couple has reached its limits on earth and in all worlds.**

## Chapter 2

*prātaḥ pataṅga-tanayām anayā padavyā*  
*snānāya yāti kim iyaṁ vṛṣabhānu-putrī \|*  
*ity ākulaiva kuṭilā vraja-rāja-veśma-*  
*kṛṣṇaṁ vilokitum agān miṣato ‘timandā \|\|1\|\|*

**Rādhā went to bathe early in the morning in the Yamunā, going along a path. Kuṭilā, who was very foolish, became anxious and, with an excuse, went to see Kṛṣṇa, who should have been in Nanda’s house.**

*snātuṁ sa cāpi nija-mātur anujñayaiva*  
*tad yāmunaṁ taṭam agād iti saṁvidānā \|*  
*gantuṁ tadīya-pada-lakṣma-diśecchad eṣā*  
*tatra vai yatra sa tayā suvilalāsāti \|\|2\|\|*

**Leaning that Kṛṣṇa, with permission from his mother, had gone to the bank of the Yamunā, Kuṭilā desired to go there, where Kṛṣṇa would be enjoying with her, by following his footprints.**

*atrāntare sahacarī tulasī praviśya*  
*kuñjaṁ vilokya lalitādi-sakhī-sametām \|*  
*rādhāṁ priyeṇa saha-hāsa-vilāsa-līlā*  
*lāvaṇya-majjita-hṛdaṁ mumude’vadac ca \|\|3\|\|*

**Tulasī, an assistant, entered a bower and saw Rādhā surrounded by Lalitā and other sakhī. Rādhā’s heart was filled with beauty in performing playful, humorous pastimes with Kṛṣṇa. Overjoyed, Tulasī spoke.**

*bho bhoḥ prasūna-dhanuṣo januṣo’tibhāgya-*  
*vikhyāpanāya yad imaṁ maham ātanudhve \|*  
*tat sāmprataṁ śṛṇuta sāmpratam enam eva*  
*draṣṭuṁ vrajāl laghutaraṁ kuṭilā sameti \|\|4\|\|*

**“Oh! You have suitably celebrated this festival to announce auspiciously the birth of Cupid. Please listen. At this moment Kuṭilā is swiftly coming from Vraja to see this festival.”**

*sā kva kva hanta katham eti sa-śaṅka-netraṁ*  
*pratyāśam ālibhir iyaṁ nijagāda pṛṣṭā \|*  
*saṭṭīkarāṭavim asau samayā vyaloki*  
*tarhy eva samprati tavāntikam apy upāgāt \|\|5\|\|*

**“Where is she? Ah! Why is she coming?” When the friends inquired from Tulasī, while their worried eyes went in all directions, Tulasī replied. “I saw her near Saṭtīkara forest. Now she must be approaching close.”**

*proce hariḥ kṣaṇam udarkam ihaiva kuñje*  
*sthitvālayaḥ kalayatāham ito jihānaḥ \|*  
*tāṁ vāñcayan pratibhayā racitābhimanyu-*  
*veśaḥ kutūhalam ito’py adhikaṁ vidhāsye \|\|6\|\|*

**Kṛṣna spoke. “O sakhīs! Very quickly this will end. Remain in this bower. I will drive her away with my skill. Wearing Abhimanyu’s clothing with fitness, I will fool her and produce some additional fun.”**

*ity uktvā rahasi praviśya vipinādhīśātta-tat-tat-pṛthaṅ-*  
*nepathyāḥ pihita-sva-lakṣma-nicayaḥ kaṇṭha-svaraṁ taṁ śrayana \|*  
*niṣkramyānusasāra tāṁ sṛtim ayaṁ sā ‘yāti dūrād yayā*  
*nārthe hanta vicakṣaṇaḥ kva nu bhaven nānā-kalā-kovidaḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Saying this Kṛṣṇa entered a private bower and, putting on different clothing supplied by Vṛndā, thereby hiding his own qualities, he imitated Abhimanyu’s voice. He then went out and walked on the path by which Kuṭilā was coming at a distance. Cannot a clever person who knows various arts fulfill his purpose?**

*kasmāt tvaṁ kuṭile vrajād bhramasi kiṁ vadhvā ihānveṣaṇā*  
*yā yātā kva nu sārkajāpasu makara-snānaṁ miṣaṁ kurvatī \|*  
*atraivāsti gatā kvacit kva ramaṇī-cauraḥ sa cāpy āgataḥ*  
*snātuṁ bhrātar ato ‘nvayāsmi gamitā kurve kim ājñāpaya \|\|8\|\|*

**“O Kuṭilā! Why have you wandered away from Vraja”? “I am searching for your wife here.” “Why would she come here?” “She came on the pretext of bathing in the Yamunā’s waters in Māgha month. She has certainly come here and is hiding somewhere. Where is the thief who steals women? He also has come here to bathe. O brother! I have come with this purpose. Please tell me what I should do.”**

*yadyapy adya paricyuto mama vṛṣo navyo hale yojanād*  
*anveṣṭuṁ tam ihāgato ‘smi tad api svalpaiva sā hṛd-vyathā \|*  
*mad-dāreṣv api lampaṭatvam iti yat soḍhuṁ kim etat kṣame*  
*gatvā kaṁsam itaḥ phalaṁ tad ucitaṁ dāsyāmi tasmai svasaḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**“Though I have come here to search for the new bull who escaped today when I tried to yoke him to the plough, the anxiety in my heart is very little concerning that. How can I bear to tolerate it if that playboy has stolen my wife? O sister! I will go to Kaṁsa and through Kaṁsa will give him appropriate punishment.**

*yuktiṁ kām api me śṛṇu prathamato nihnutya tiṣṭhāmy ahaṁ*  
*kuñje’smin paritas tvayā tu rabhasād anviṣyatāṁ rādhikā \|*  
*sā kṛṣṇena vināsti ced iha miṣeṇānīyatāṁ so’pi ced*  
*āste ‘lakṣitam eva tatra naya māṁ vīkṣyaiva taṁ dūrataḥ \|\|10\|\|*

**“Hear my plan. First I will stay here in this bower and hide. You should thoroughly search for Rādhikā all around. If she is without Kṛṣṇa, bring her here on some pretext. If he is with her, unseen by them, bring me there, and I will observe them from a distance.”**

*bhrāmaṁ bhrāmaṁ phaṇi-hrada-taṭād vīkṣya vīkṣyaiva kuñjān*  
*antaḥ prodyat-kuṭilāmadhurā keśi-tīrthopakaṇṭhe \|*  
*puṣpodyāne ‘mala-parimalāṁ kīrtidā kīrti-vallīṁ*  
*prāpālīnāṁ tatibhir abhitaḥ sevyamānāṁ śanaiḥ sā \|\|11\|\|*

**She looked within all the bowers as she wandered about starting from Kaliya-hrada, with her pronounced crooked heart and unpleasant manners. She finally met Rādhā, Kīrtidā’s creeper of fame, with her pure fragrance, at a flower garden near Keśī-tīrtha. She was being served gently by her surrounding friends.**

*kiṁ snātum eṣi kuṭile na hi tat kim-arthaṁ*  
*yuṣmac-caritram avagantum ihānvagaccham \|*  
*jñātaṁ tad āśu lalite vada tad bravīmi*  
*kiṁ vātra vakti nikhilaṁ hari-gandha eva \|\|12\|\|*

**“O Kuṭilā! Have you come to bathe?” “I did not come here for that purpose.” “Why did you come?” “I have come to understand your character. O Lalitā! I have understood everything immediately.” “Please tell me.” “I now speak. What else should I say? Everything is understood from the fragrance of Hari.”**

*siṁhasya gandham api vetsi sa ced ihāsti*  
*nihnutya kutracana tad bibhimo ‘ti-mugdhāḥ \|*  
*tūrṇaṁ palāyya tad ito gṛham eva yāmaḥ*  
*snehaṁ vyadhās tvam amalaṁ yad ihaivam āgāḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**“If you smell a lion (hari) he must be hiding somewhere here. Being very innocent girls, we are afraid. We will flee from here and go home. In coming here, you have shown us real affection.”**

*yāsyanti gehamayi-dharma-ratā bhavatyaḥ*  
*kīrtiṁ vaneṣu viracayya kula-dvayasya \|*  
*kintv agrato ya iha rājati nīpa-kuñjas*  
*tad-dvāram udghaṭayatāsmi didṛkṣur etam \|\|14\|\|*

**Kuṭilā said, “Absorbed in your household duties you wander in the forests to make your families on your side and your husband’s side famous. Here before us is a bower of kadamba trees. Open the door. I want to see him.”**

*etat kayā ‘pi vana-devatayā sva-veśma*  
*ruddhā gataṁ śara-śalākayā kavāṭikābhyām*  
*kā nāma sāhasavatī parakīya-geha-*  
*dvāraṁ vinudya bata doṣam aśeṣam ṛcchet \|\|15\|\|*

**Lalitā said, “Some forest devatā has closed his house with a door make of reeds and bamboo. If I boldly open the door of someone else’s house I will commit unlimited sin.”**

*satyaṁ bravīṣi lalite kulajā ‘si mugdhā*  
*naivāviśaḥ para-gṛhaṁ januṣo’pi madhye \|*  
*kintu praveṣayasi bhoḥ sva-gṛhaṁ paraṁ yat*  
*tac-chāstra-pāṭhana-kṛte tvam ihāvatīrṇā \|\|16\|\|*

**“O Lalitā! You speak the truth. You are simple girl of good family and have not entered another’s house from birth. However you let strange men enter your house. You have appeared in this world to teach this scripture.”**

*ity uktvāruṇitekṣaṇā drutam iyaṁ gatvā kuṭhīrāntikaṁ*  
*bhittvā puṣpa-kavāṭikām atijavād antaḥ praviṣṭā sphuṭam \|*  
*dṛṣṭvā kausuma-talpam atra ca harer mālyaṁ tathā rādhikā*  
*hāraṁ ca truṭitaṁ pragṛhya rabhasād āgād agārād bahiḥ \|\|17\|\|*

**Saying this, with red eyes, going to the bower, she impetuously entered the bower, breaking the flower door. She clearly saw a flower bed. On it was Kṛṣṇa’s garland and Rādhā’s broken necklace. Taking the necklace and garland she quickly went outside the bower.**

*māgha-snānam idaṁ yathā-vidhi-kṛtaṁ puṇyaṁ tathopārjitaṁ*  
*pūtaṁ yena kula-dvayaṁ ravi-sutā-tīre raviś cārcitaḥ \|*  
*tad yūyaṁ lalite yiyāsyatha gṛhaṁ kiṁ vātra rātrin divaṁ*  
*dharmaṁ kartum abhīpsatheti vada me śrotuṁ samutkaṇṭhate \|\|18\|\|*

**“By bathing during Māgha month according to the scriptural rules, you have gained great merit. This will purify the families of you and your husband. You have also worshipped the sun god on the bank of the Yamunā. O Lalitā! Do you want to return home or do you want to enact dharma day and night at this place: Please tell me. I am eager to hear.”**

*kiṁ kupyasīha kuṭile na mamaiṣa hāro*  
*bhrātus tavaiva śapathaṁ karavai prasīda \|*  
*ity uktavaty amala-candra-mukhī sakampa-*  
*śīraṣaṁ sa-huṅkṛti kaṭu-bhrutayā tatarje \|\|19\|\|*

**With a face like the spotless moon, Rādhā spoke. Shaking her head and with a sharp frown, she scolded Kuṭilā in a load voice. “O Kuṭilā! Why are you so angry? That is not my necklace. I swear that it belongs to your brother. Please become calm.”**

*netaḥ prayāsyatha gṛhaṁ yadi na prayāta*  
*rājyaṁ kurudhvam iha tāvad ahaṁ tu yāmi \|*  
*tāṁ mātaraṁ bhagavatīm api hāra-mālye*  
*sandarśya yuṣmad-uciteṣṭa-vidhau yatiṣye \|\|20\|\|*

**“If you will not go home, then don’t go. Make this your kingdom. But I am going. I will show the necklace and garland to my mother and Paurṇamāsī. I will see that destiny will give you what you deserve.”**

*kāmaṁ prayāhi kuṭile kaṭu kiṁ bravīṣi*  
*hāraṁ pradarśaya gṛhaṁ gṛham eva sarvāḥ \|*  
*nāsmākam eṣa yad ato na bibhemi kiñcit*  
*mithyā-pravādam api no na kadā dadāsi \|\|21\|\|*

**“Leave if you like, Kuṭilā! Why do you speak so harshly? If you go and show the necklace to everyone in every house, I am not afraid since it is not mine. You can never make false rumors about me.”**

*sā kruddhā drutam eva goṣṭha-gamanaṁ svasya pradarśyaiva tā*  
*yatrāste harir ājagāma śanakais tatraiva nihnutya sā \|*  
*bhrātar mālyam aghadviṣaḥ kalaya bho vadhvāś ca hāraṁ mayā*  
*prāptaṁ saurata-talpa-gaṁ rahasi tā dṛṣṭāḥ sa nālokitaḥ \|\|22\|\|*

**Angrily, Kuṭilā showed that she was quickly going home, but secretly she hurried to the place where Kṛṣṇa (disguised as Abhimanyu) had come. O brother! Look at this garland belonging to Kṛṣṇa and your wife’s necklace that I obtained on the bed of pleasure. I saw them but I did not see Kṛṣṇa.”**

*bhadraṁ bhadram idaṁ babhūva mathurāṁ gacchāmi tūrṇaṁ bhaginy*  
*etāvad dvayam eva me balam abhūd vijñāpane rājani \|*  
*kintu svīya-gṛhasya vaktum ucito na syāt kalaṅko mahāṁs*  
*tasmin vṛṣṇi-sadasy ataś cāturim āmnātavya eko mayā \|\|23\|*

**“O sister! This has been good. I will quickly go to Mathurā. These two pieces of evidence make my case strong in explaining to the king. But it is necessary to speak so that our family will not be greatly stained in the assembly of Vṛṣnis. I will speak cleverly at that time.**

*govardhanaṁ priya-sakhaṁ prati vācyam etat*  
*candrāvalīm api bhavad-gṛhiṇīṁ nikuñje \|*  
*ānīya dūṣayati nanda-sutas tad etad*  
*vastu-dvayaṁ kalaya tan-mithunasya labdham \|\|24\|\|*

**“I will say to my dear friend Govardhana. ‘Kṛṣna took your wife Candāvalī into the bower and spoiled her. Look at these two objects of the couple that I obtained.’**

*itthaṁ lampaṭatāṁ vraje pratigṛhaṁ dṛṣṭvaiva tasyādhikāṁ*  
*tvām ājñāpayam adya tattvam adhunā vijñāpya rājñi drutam \|*  
*pattīnāṁ śatam aśva-vāra-daśakaṁ preṣyaiva nandīśvarān*  
*nandaṁ sātmajam ānayan madhu-purīṁ taṁ tat-phalaṁ prāpaya \|\|25\|\|*

**‘Having seen him perform such lusty acts in every house in Vraja, I have informed you now of the truth. You must quickly inform the king at once to send a hundred foot soldiers and ten horsemen and bring Nanda’s son from Nandīśvara to Mathurā. There he should be punished for his deeds.’**

*ity uktvaiva mayā punaḥ sva-bhavanaṁ pūrvāhna evaiṣyate*  
*madhyāhne khalu rājakīya-puruṣā yāsyanti te tu vrajam \|*  
*tvaṁ gatvā gṛha eva mātṛ-sahitā tiṣṭher iti procivān*  
*kṛṣṇo dakṣiṇa diṅ-mukho ‘vrajad atho sā tāś ca veśmā yayuḥ \|\|26\|\|*

**“Saying this, I will return home before noon. The king’s soldiers wll arrive in Vraja at noon. You should go home and remain with mother.” Saying this, Kṛṣṇa went south. Kuṭilā and the gopīs returned to their homes.**

*kṛṣṇo’vilambya ghaṭikā-trayato’tha tādṛg-*  
*veśaḥ svayaṁ sa jaṭilā-gṛham āsasāda \|*  
*bho kvāsi mātar ayi bhoḥ kuṭile sametya*  
*jānīhi vṛttam akhilaṁ tv iti kiñcid ūce \|\|27\|\|*

**After three ghaṭikās, Kṛṣṇa in the same dress went to Jaṭilā’s house. Then he said, “O mother! Where are you? O Kuṭilā! Come and hear all the news.**

*vijñāpitaḥ sa nṛpatiḥ prajighāya yad yad*  
*drāg-aśva-vāra-daśakaṁ tad ihaiti dūre \|*  
*kintv atra lampaṭa-varo dhṛta-mat-svarūpo*  
*mad-geham eti tad-anīkṣitam āgato’smi \|\|28\|\|*

**“The king has been informed and ten horsemen are coming here in all haste to beat him. But the lecher, dressing himself like me is coming to my house. I have come without him seeing me.**

*bahir dvāraṁ ruddhvā bhagini saha mātrā drutm itaḥ*  
*samāruhyaivāṭṭaṁ kalaya taruṇī-lampaṭa-patham \|*  
*tam eṣyāntaṁ tarjanty atikaṭu-girā tiṣṭha suciraṁ*  
*vadhūṁ rundan varte tala-sadana evāham adhunā \|\|29\|\|*

**“O sister! Lock the door and then go with mother to the upper terrace and see when the stealer of young girls is approaching. Remain there and scold him with harsh words for a long time when he comes. I will remain on the ground floor after restraining my wife.”**

*athāyāntaṁ dṛṣṭvā tvaritam abhimanyuṁ kaṭu raṭanty*  
*are dharma-dhvaṁsin vraja-kula-bhuvāṁ kiṁ nu yatase \|*  
*praveṣṭuṁ mad-bhrātur bhavanam ayi loṣṭrālibhir itaḥ*  
*śiro’bhinandī te bata capala dāsye pratiphalam \|\|30\|\|*

**Seeing Abhimanyu hastily returning, they all shouted at him rudely. “O destroyer of dharma of the women of Vraja! Are you trying to enter the house of my brother? I will break your head with stones. O fickle one! I will give you what you deserve.**

*tavānyāyaṁ śrutvā kupita-manasaḥ kaṁsa-nṛpater*  
*bhaṭā āyānty addhā sa-pitṛkam api tvāṁ sukhayitum \|*  
*yadā kārāgāre nṛpati-nagare sthāsyasi ciraṁ*  
*niruddhas tarhy etac capalataratā yāsyati śamam \|\|31\|\|*

**“Hearing of your wicked conduct, King Kaṁsa has become angry. His soldiers are now coming to give happiness to you and your father. When you have been locked in the prison in Mathurā for a long time your fickle nature will be extinguished.”**

*iti śrutvā jalpaṁ vikalam abhimanyuḥ katham aho*  
*svasāraṁ me preto ‘lagad ahaha kaścit kaṭutaraḥ \|*  
*tad ānetuṁ yāmi tvaritaṁ iha tan-māntrika-janān*  
*iti grāmopāntaṁ vitata-bahu-cintaḥ sa gatavān \|\|32\|\|*

**Hearing these confused utterances, Abhimanyu thought, “Some fierce ghost has possessed my sister. I will go quickly to bring some exorcists.” Greatly worried, he went to the edge of the village.**

*evaṁ hariḥ sa jaṭilā gṛha eva tasyā*  
*vadhvā sahāramata citra-caritra-ratnaḥ \|*  
*yatnaḥ ka eva phalatvam agān na tasya*  
*kiṁ vā phalaṁ para-vadhū-ramaṇād ṛte’sya \|\|33\|\|*

**In this way Kṛṣṇa, the jewel of colorful exploits, enjoyed with Jaṭilā’s daughter-in-law in her own house. What effort of his will not bear fruit? There is no other fruit than enjoying others’ wives.**

## Chapter 3

*athaikadā sā jaṭilā vivikte*  
*cintāturā kiñcid uvāca putrīm \|*  
*na rakṣituṁ hā prabhavāni kṛṣṇād*  
*vadhūṁ tataḥ kiṁ karavāṇy upāyam \|\|1\|\|*

**Jaṭilā, understanding Rādhā’s love for Kṛṣṇa, became anxious and spoke to her daughter Kuṭilā. “I cannot protect her from Kṛṣna. What should I do?**

*tvaṁ putri tasmād gṛha eva rundhi*  
*vadhūṁ bahir yāti kadāpi neyam \|*  
*yathā tathā hā bhava sāvadhānā*  
*sa lampaṭo naiva yathā hy upaiti \|\|2\|\|*

**“O daughter! You must keep Rādhā in the house. She can never go outside. Similarly be very careful that the lecher Kṛṣna can never enter the house.”**

*mātar bhavatyā na vadhūr niroddhuṁ*  
*śakyā yataḥ pratyaham eva yatnāt \|*  
*vrajeśvarī bhojayituṁ sva-putraṁ*  
*pākārtham enāṁ nayati sva-gehe \|\|3\|\|*

**Kuṭilā said, “O mother! It is not possible to restrict your daughter-in-law, because everyday Yaśodā carefully brings her to her house to cook, in order to feed her son.”**

*putri tvam adya vraje tāṁ vadaitan*  
*nātaḥ paraṁ kvāpi vadhūḥ sva-gehāt \|*  
*prayāty atas tvaṁ suta-bhojanārthaṁ*  
*pāke niyuktāṁ kuru rohiṇīṁ tām \|\|4\|\|*

**“O daughter! Go today and tell Yaśodā that, from now on, Rādhā cannot leave the house. She should engage Rohiṇī in cooking in order to feed her son.”**

*mātas tayā vakṣyata eva tasyai*  
*durvāsasā ko’pi varo vitīrṇaḥ \|*  
*tvad dhasta-pakkaudana-bhoktur āyur*  
*nirvighnam astvity adhikā prasiddhiḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**“O mother! Yaśodā will say that Durvāsā gave her a benediction that whoever eats the food cooked by Rādhā will have long life. This is well known.**

*ekaḥ suto me bahu-duṣṭa-dānavādy-*  
*ariṣṭa-vatve ‘pi kuśaly abhūd yataḥ \|*  
*tatas tayā sādhita-modanādikaṁ*  
*nityaṁ sutaṁ bhojayituṁ prayatsyate \|\|6\|\|*

**“She will say, ‘My only son has remained safe, uninjured by many wicked demons because I have endeavored that my son always eats delicious food cooked by Rādhā.”**

*putri tvayā vācyam idaṁ paraśvaḥ*  
*śvo vā āgatya muniḥ pradadyāt \|*  
*rādhā spṛśed yaṁ sa cirāyur astv*  
*ity evaṁ varaṁ ced ayi tarhi kiṁ syāt \|\|7\|\|*

**“O daughter, say this. ‘What would happen if a sage came tomorrow or the next day and gave a blessing that whoever Rādhā touched would have long life?**

*kiṁ sparśayantī nija-putram etām*  
*ākārayiṣyasy ayi nīti-vijñe \|*  
*kulāṅganā yat para-veśma gatvā*  
*nityaṁ paced ity api kiṁ nu nītiḥ \|\|8\|\|*

**‘O Yaśodā, learned in morality! Would you call Rādhā and have her touch your son? Is it proper that a respectable girl should go to someone else’s house and cook daily?’**

*vadhvāḥ kalaṅkaḥ pratideśam eṣa*  
*bhūyān abhūd yat kim u sahyam etat \|*  
*sneho yathā te nija-putra evaṁ*  
*sneho mamāpy asti nija-snuṣāyām \|\|9\|\|*

**“The ill fame of my daughter-in-law has spread everywhere. How can I tolerate it? Just as you have affection for your son, I have affection for my daughter-in-law.**

*tathāpi te prauḍhir iyaṁ bhavec ced*  
*dhaniṣṭhayā preṣitayaiva nityam \|*  
*vadhū-kṛtaṁ modaka-laḍḍukādi*  
*trisandhyam evānaya putra-hetoḥ \|\|10\|\|*

**“If your pride still remains, then bring sweets made by Rādhā daily three times for your son by sending Dhaniṣṭhā to my house to fetch them.**

*ity evam uktvāpi yadi vrajeśā*  
*kupyet tadā tan-nagarīṁ vihāya \|*  
*gatvaiva deśāntara eva vāsaṁ*  
*vadhūm aviṣyāmi tadīya-putrāt \|\|11\|\|*

**“If, after saying this. Yaśodā becomes angry, we should leave this town and go to live in another place. I will protect my daughter-in-law from her son.”**

*evaṁ nirodhe sati tau vilāsinau*  
*parasparādarśana-dāva-tāpitau \|*  
*babhūvatur hanta yathā tathā svayaṁ*  
*sarasvatī varṇayituṁ kṣameta kim \|\|12\|\|*

**When Rādhā was confined in this manner, Rādhā and Kṛṣna burned in the forest fire of not seeing each other. Could Sarasvatī in person describe their condition?**

*saroja-patrair vidhu-gandhasāra-*  
*paṅka-praliptai racitāpi śayyā \|*  
*rādhāṅga-saṁsparśanataḥ kṣaṇena*  
*hā hanta hā murmuratāṁ prapede \|\|13\|\|*

**The bed made of lotus petals smeared with camphor and sandalwood pulp began to burn at the very touch of Rādhā’s body.**

*ninded vidhiṁ pakṣma-kṛtaṁ bhṛśaṁ yā*  
*vāñched apakṣmottama-mīna-janma \|*  
*nandātmajālokam ṛte kathaṁ sā*  
*yāmāṣṭakaṁ yāpayituṁ kṣameta \|\|14\|\|*

**How will Rādhā who harshly criticized the creator for making eyelids and desired to be born as a fish without eyelids pass a day and night without seeing the son of Nanda?**

*nāvekṣate nāpi śṛṇoti kiñcid*  
*acetanā sīdati puṣpa-talpe \|*  
*dhaniṣṭhayāthaitya tathāvidhā sā*  
*vrajeśvarī preṣitayā vyaloki \|\|15\|\|*

**Rādhā could not see anything. She could not hear anything. She collapsed unconscious on the flower bed. When Dhaniṣṭhā, sent by Yaśodā, arrived, she saw Rādhā in that condition.**

*adya prabhāte lalite papāca*  
*śrī-rohiṇī kṛṣṇa-kṛte yad annam \|*  
*tat prāśya so’gād vipinaṁ vrajeśā*  
*māṁ prāhiṇod atra viṣaṇṇa-cetāḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**“O Lalitā! This morning Rohiṇī cooked food for Kṛṣṇa. After eating it Kṛṣṇa went to the forest. Yaśodā, with dejected heart sent me here.**

*sāyaṁ rajanyām api yat tathā śvaḥ*  
*sa bhokṣyate tasya kṛte ‘ham āgām \|*  
*iyaṁ tu saṁjñā-rahitaiva paktuṁ*  
*kathaṁ kṣametādya karomi hā kim \|\|17\|\|*

**“I have come for the food Rādhā has made for him which can be eaten this evening and tomorrow. But how can Rādhā cook today when she is unconscious? What will I do?”**

*kṛṣṇaḥ puras te kalayeti tad vāk*  
*tāṁ bhagna-mūrcchām akarod yadaiva \|*  
*tadā dhaniṣṭhā sahasā vrajeśā*  
*sandis tam āha sma saroruhākṣīm \|\|18\|\|*

**“Kṛṣna is in front of you. Please look.” These words made Rādhā regain consciousness. Then Dhaniṣṭhā immediately delivered Yaśodā’s message to lotus-eyed Rādhā.**

*kaṭāham atrānaya rūpa-mañjari*  
*pralipya cūllīm iha vahnim arpaya \|*  
*yathā vrajeśādiśed evam etat*  
*kṛṣṇasya bhakṣyaṁ kila sādhayāmy aham \|\|19\|\|*

**Rādhā said, “O Rūpa-mañjarī! Bring the pot, smear the stove with clay and light the fire. I will prepare Kṛṣṇa’s food just as Yaśodā has instructed.**

*karomi yāvat sakhi nityam etac*  
*caturguṇaṁ kurva iti bruvāṇā \|*  
*cullī-taṭe divya-catuṣkikāyāṁ*  
*rādhopaveśaṁ sahasā cakāra \|\|20\|\|*

**“I will prepare four times the amount that I usually make.” She immediately sat down near the stove in the four pillared room.**

*yat-sparśanāt paṅkaja-patra-śayyā*  
*yayau kṣaṇān murmuratāṁ tadaiva \|*  
*pakvānna-karmaṇy analārciṣaiva*  
*rādhā-vapuḥ śītalatāṁ prapede \|\|21\|\|*

**Though by her touch the lotus petal bed instantly burned up, Rādhā’s body became cool when she cooked food by the flames of the fire.**

*premottamotto’tarkya vicitra-dhāmā*  
*yato janaṁ tāpayate śaśāṅkaḥ \|*  
*vahniḥ punaḥ śītalayaty atas taṁ*  
*tad āśrayaṁ vā kim u ko’pi vetti \|\|22\|\|*

**The astonishing condition arising from the highest prema is inconceivable because in that state the moon heats a person and fire cools the person. Who can understand the person with this prema?**

*jagāda kiñcil lalitā dhaniṣṭhe*  
*vidyud-ghanāvagraha eṣa bhūyān \|*  
*śamaṁ kim eṣyaty adhunā sakhīnām*  
*ānanda-saśyāni vināśam īyuḥ \|\|23\|\|*

**Lalitā said a few words. “O Dhaniṣṭhā! Will the obstacles to the lightning and cloud be removed? The grains of the sakhīs’ bliss will soon perish.”**

*bravīṣi satyaṁ lalite vayasyaiḥ*  
*saha svayaṁ sīdati so’pi kṛṣṇaḥ \|*  
*vṛndāvana-sthāḥ śuka-keki-bhṛṅga-*  
*mṛgādayo’py ākulatām avāpuḥ \|\|24\|\|*

**“O Lalitā! You speak the truth. And Kṛṣna also along with his friends suffers. The parrots, peacocks, bees and deer also have become afflicted.”**

*tataś ca rādhā lalitādi-karṇe*  
*kāñcit kathāṁ procya gṛhaṁ yayau sā \|*  
*svayaṁ viśākhā jaṭilām upetyā-*  
*līkaṁ rurodādhi dharaṁ luṭhantī \|\|25\|\|*

**Then Rādhā whispered something in Lalitā’s ear and went to her room. Viśākhā met Jaṭilā and pretended to cry while rolling on the ground.**

*hā kiṁ viśākhe nanu rodiṣi tvaṁ*  
*rādhāṁ dadaṁśāhiralakṣya-rūpaḥ \|*  
*kathaṁ kva vā koli-tale tadīya-*  
*ratne gṛhīte nija-ratna-buddhyā \|\|26\|\|*

**Jaṭilā said, “O Viśākhā! Why are you crying?” Viśākha said, “A snake which she could not see bit Rādhā.” “How and where did it happen?” “Thinking it was her own jewel, she grabbed the jewel on the snake’s head, under the badari tree.”**

*hā mūrdhni ko’yaṁ mama vajra-pāta*  
*iti bruvāṇā tvarayā yayau sā \|*  
*vilokya rādhāṁ bhuvi vepamānāṁ*  
*tatāḍa soccaiḥ svam uraḥ karābhyām \|\|27\|\|*

**Saying “This is a thunderbolt to my head” Jaṭilā quickly went and saw Rādhā shaking on the ground. Jaṭilā began beating her chest violently with her hands.**

*gavāṁ gṛhād ānayaputri tāvat*  
*sva-bhrātaraṁ śīghram itaḥ prayātu \|*  
*samāntrikān ānayatu prakṛṣṭāṁs*  
*te me vadhūṁ nirviṣayantu mantraiḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**“O daughter! Bring your brother from the cowshed and send him off immediately. He should bring some expert doctors. They should remove the poison in my daughter-in-law using mantras.”**

*ity evam uktvā jaṭilā jagāda*  
*snuṣe tanuḥ samprati kīdṛśī te \|*  
*sandahyamānāṁ viṣa-vahninemām*  
*avaimi vaktuṁ prabhavāmi nārye \|\|29\|\|*

**Having said this, Jaṭilā went to Rādhā and asked “How is your body now?” “I understand that my body is burning up with the fire of poison. O noble lady! I cannot speak.**

*mantraiḥ karābhyāṁ mama māntrikaś ced*  
*ekāṁ padasyāṅgulikām apīha \|*  
*spṛśet tadāsūn sahasā tyajāmi*  
*kulaṅganāyā niyamo mamaiṣaḥ \|\|30\|\|*

**“If the doctor touches even one of my toes with his hands while chanting mantras I will immediately give up my life. This is my principle as a girl of good family.”**

*snuṣe kim evaṁ vadasīha bhakṣayed*  
*abhakṣyam aspṛśyam api spṛśen naraḥ \|*  
*mantrauṣadhādau nahi dūṣaṇaṁ bhaved*  
*āpad-gatasyeti vidāṁ śruti-smṛtī \|\|31\|\|*

**“O daughter-in-law! I will explain. For those who know, the śrutis and smṛtis say that in times of danger there is no fault if a person eats what is forbidden to eat or touches what should not be touched when using mantras or taking medicine.”**

*ājñāṁ tavemāṁ na hi pālayāmi*  
*prāṇān puras te kalaya tyajāmi \|*  
*śrutveti vadhvā vacanaṁ sa-cintāṁ*  
*jagāda kācit prativāsinī tām \|\|32\|\|*

**“I cannot follow your order. I will give up my life in front of you. Look!” Hearing her daughter-in-law say this, Jaṭilā became worried. A neighbor then spoke to her.**

*yaḥ kāliyādyādi bhujaṅga-mardī*  
*dṛṣṭvaiva tāḥ pīta-viṣodakā gāḥ \|*  
*ajīvayat taṁ harim ānayārye*  
*sa te vadhūṁ nirviṣayed vilokya \|\|33\|\|*

**The neighbor said, “Kṛṣṇa who subdued Kāliya, Agha and other snakes, brought the cows which had drunk poison back to life. O lady! Bring Kṛṣna. Just by glancing at Rādhā. he will free her of the poison.”**

*rādhābravīd yat parivāda-pīḍāṁ*  
*viṣānalād apy adhikām avaimi \|*  
*tam eva yā darśayituṁ yatante*  
*tā vairiṇīr eva cireṇa vedmi \|\|34\|\|*

**Rādhā said, “I think the pain caused by accusation is more painful that the fire of poison. I take as my enemies forever persons who try to make me see Kṛṣṇa.”**

*tarhi snuṣe ‘haṁ sa-sūtā prāyāmi*  
*tāṁ paurṇamāsīṁ drutam ānayāmi \|*  
*sutantra-mantrāgama-śāstra-vijñā*  
*sāsusthayiṣyaty alam adya yuktyā \|\|35\|\|*

**“O daughter-in-law! With Kuṭilā I will quickly go to Paurṇamāsī and bring her. Since she is knowledgeable of mantra, tantra and āgama scriptures. she will cure you. Enough of other means!”**

*proce viśākhā tad alaṁ vilambair*  
*viṣaṁ mayāruddham avaihi sūtraiḥ*  
*yamārddha-paryantam ataḥ parantu*  
*śiro ‘dhirūḍhaṁ tad asādhyam eva (36)*

**Viśākhā then said, “Do not delay. Understand that I have stopped the poison from spreading by these threads. This will last for an hour and a half. Then. when the poison reaches her head, there is no remedy.”**

*sā paurṇamāsyāḥ sthalam abhyupetya*  
*natvākhilaṁ vṛttam avedayat tām \|*  
*papraccha gārgīm atha paurṇamāsī*  
*tvaṁ sarva-mantrān pitur adhyagāṣṭhāḥ \|\|37\|\|*

**Jaṭilā went to Paurṇamāsī’s place, offered respects and informed her of what had happened. Paurṇamāsī asked Gārgī, “O daughter! Have you studied all mantras from your father?”**

*kiṁ putri sākhyan nahi vedmi kiñcit*  
*karṇīyasī me bhaginī tu vetti \|*  
*kvā sā kim ākhyā kila kiṁ-nivāsā*  
*kāśī-purāt sā śvaśurasya gehāt \|\|37\|\|*

**Gārgī said, “O friends! I do not know but my younger sister knows.” “Where is she? What is her name? Where does she live?” “She has come from the house of her husband in Kāśī to my father’s house in Mathurā.**

*pitur gṛhaṁ vṛṣṇi-pure gatābhūt*  
*tato’pi mām atra didṛkṣamāṇā \|*  
*pūrvedyur āgatya yad asti nāmnā*  
*vidyāvalir mad-gṛha-madhya eva \|\|38\|\|*

**“From there, desiring to see me, she came here yesterday and is staying in my house. Her name is Vidyāvali.”**

*jaraty athoce bahu-viklavāśru*  
*siktānanā gārgi natāsmy ahaṁ te \|*  
*tām ānayāsmad bhavanaṁ sa-putrāṁ*  
*krīṇīhi māṁ svīya-kṛpāmṛtena \|\|39\|\|*

**Jaṭilā with distressed face filled with tears said “O Gārgī! I offer respects to you. Bring her to my house. Purchase me and my son with the sweetness of your mercy.”**

*gārgi tvam ādau sva-gṛhaṁ prayāhi*  
*tataḥ sa-kanyā jaṭilā prayātu \|*  
*prasādyatām ānayatāṁ tataḥ sā*  
*rādhāṁ dhruvaṁ nirviṣayiṣyati drāk \|\|40\|\|*

**Paurṇamāsī said, “O Gārgī! First, you go to your house. Jaṭilā and her daughter should also go there. Please Vidyāvali and bring her to Rādhā. Certainly she will quickly cure Rādhā of the poison.”**

*pūrvaṁ dhaniṣṭhā-vacasaiva gārgī*  
*strī-veśinaṁ kṛṣṇam agāra-madhye \|*  
*asthāpayat tarhi tu sā jaratyā*  
*sahaiva tat-pārśva-gatā jagāda \|\|41\|\|*

**Previously Dhaniṣṭhā told Gārgī to leave Kṛṣna dressed as a girl in her house. Gārgī now went with Jaṭilā by her side to the house.**

*vidyā-bale bho bhagini vraje’smin*  
*yā nitya-rājad-guṇa-rūpa-kīrtiḥ \|*  
*tvayā śrutā śrī-vṛṣabhānu-putrī*  
*tasyā vipattim arhati batādya \|\|42\|\|*

**“O my sister Vidyāvali! The daughter of Vṛṣabhānu, famous for eternally shining qualities, who you have heard of, has met with misfortune today.**

*kenāpi daṣṭā maṇi-dhāriṇā sā*  
*sarpeṇa hālāhala-pūritābhūt \|*  
*śvaśrur amuṣyāḥ sa-sutā prapannā*  
*tvāṁ tat tvam etad-bhavanaṁ jihīthāḥ \|\|43\|\|*

**“She has been bitten by a jeweled snake and is full of poison. Her mother-in-law and daughter surrender to you. You should hurry to their house.”**

*vidyāvaliḥ prāha bhaginy ayi tvaṁ*  
*vijñāpya vijñeva giraṁ tanoṣi \|*  
*kulāṅganā vipra-vadhūr ahaṁ kiṁ*  
*bhavan-mate jāṅgulikī bhavāmi \|\|44\|\|*

**Vidyāvali then spoke. “O sister! Tell me this, you speak as if learned. I am the wife of a brāhmaṇa of good family. How can I be a snake charmer as you claim?**

*pituḥ kulaṁ vṛṣṇi-pure’sti patyuḥ*  
*kulaṁ tu kāsyāṁ pṛthitaṁ nṛloke \|*  
*kalaṅka-paṅke vinimarjayantī*  
*māṁ tvaṁ kathaṁ snihyasi tan na vedmi \|\|45\|\|*

**“My father’s family lives in Mathurā. My husband’s family is famous in Kāśī. You drown me in the mud of infamy. Do you have affection for me? I do not understand.”**

*jaraty avocat tava pāda-padme*  
*natāsmi saṁjīvya vadhūṁ madīyām \|*  
*māṁ tvaṁ saputrāṁ nija-pāda-dhūli-*  
*krītāṁ vidhehīty atha kiṁ bravīmi \|\|46\|\|*

**Jaṭilā spoke. “I offer respects to your lotus feet. Please bring my daughter-in-law to life. Please purchase me and my son with the dust from your feet. What more can I say?”**

*vidyāvaliḥ prākhyad ayi vrajasthe*  
*jānāsi na brahma-kulasya rītim \|*  
*gṛhaṁ gṛhaṁ gopya iva bhrameṣur*  
*na vipra-vadhvaḥ su-mahābhijātyāt \|\|47\|\|*

**Vidyāvali then spoke. “O women of Vraja! You do not know the custom of brāhmaṇa families. The wives of brāhmaṇas do not wander from house to house like cowherds since they have very high birth.”**

*provāca gārgī śṛṇu bhoḥ śruti-smṛti-*  
*proktaṁ niṣiddhaṁ vihitaṁ tu yad bhavet \|*  
*jñātvāpi tat sarvam idaṁ bravīṣi cen*  
*na te’sti dṛṣṭiḥ kila pāramārthikī \|\|48\|\|*

**Gārgī then spoke. “Please listen. You know what is prescribed and forbidden in śruti and smṛti scriptures. But if you simply state all that, you do not have spiritual vision.**

*vraje sthitāḥ kīrti-dayānvitāyā*  
*gopyas tathā ye vṛṣabhānu-tulyāḥ \|*  
*gopā na teṣāṁ tvam avaiyi tattvaṁ*  
*nāpy ābhijātyaṁ na ca viṣṇu-bhaktiḥ \|\|49\|\|*

**“The gopīs of Vraja are endowed with good qualities and compassion. The cowherds are equal to Vṛṣabhānu. You do not know the truth about them, their birth or their devotion to Viṣṇu.**

*kāśyāṁ sthitā viṣṇu-bahirmukhā ye*  
*viprā bhavatyāḥ śvaśurādayas tān \|*  
*jānāmi no vācaya māṁ taveyaṁ*  
*kāśyāṁ sthiter buddhir abhūt kaṭhorā \|\|50\|\|*

**“I know the brāhmaṇas of Kāśī on your husband’s side. They are all opposed to Viṣṇu. Do not speak to me. Your intelligence has become hard because of living in Kāśī.”**

*mā kupya śāntiṁ bhaja tāvad ārye*  
*bhaginy ahaṁ hanta tavāśritā ‘smi \|*  
*yathā bravīṣy evam ahaṁ karomi*  
*kintv atra śaṅkā mama kācid asti \|\|51\|\|*

**“O respectable woman, my sister! Do not be angry. Become peaceful. I am surrendered to you. I will do what you say but I have an apprehension.**

*pure śrutā kācana kiṁ vadantī*  
*nandasya putro’jani ko’pi vīraḥ \|*  
*sa svaira-caryo bata lampaṭatvān*  
*na brahma-jāter api bhītim eti \|\|52\|\|*

**“Rumor have appeared and are heard in Mathurā that the son of Nanda is extraordinarily courageous, independent, and has no fear of the brāhmaṇas because of his lusty nature.**

*anyatra nārīṣv iva mayy api drāk*  
*sa-lobha-dṛṣṭir yadi vartmani syāt \|*  
*sadyas tadāsūn visṛjāmi naiva*  
*kula-dvayaṁ hanta kalaṅkayiṣye \|\|53\|\|*

**“If he looks at me with his greedy glance on the path as he does with other women, I will give up my life immediately. I will not bring infamy to the families of me and my husband.”**

*na tatra śaṅkā tava kāpi yasmad*  
*ahaṁ svayaṁ tvat-sahitā prayāmi \|*  
*ity eva gārgī vacanāc calantī*  
*vidyāvalir vartmani kiñcid ūce \|\|54\|\|*

**“You should have no worry since I will personally be with you.” Because of Gārgī’s words she went. Vidyāvali then spoke while walking on the path.**

*mantrauṣadhābhyāṁ garalasya nāśas*  
*tatrāsti mantro mama kaṇṭha eva \|*  
*yac cauṣadhaṁ tat tv ahi-valli-parṇaṁ*  
*mantraṁ japitvā rada-piṣṭam eva \|\|55\|\|*

**“The poison will be destroyed by mantras and medicine. The mantra is in my throat. The medicine is betel leaf which I will pulverize with my teeth while chanting the mantra.**

*tat te vadhūḥ sā mama abhakṣayet kiṁ*  
*na veti pṛṣṭā jaṭilā jagāda \|*  
*sā me snuṣā brāhmaṇa-jāti-bhaktā*  
*tad bhakṣayed eva kim atra citram \|\|56\|\|*

**“Will your daughter-in-law eat what I chewed or not?” Jaṭilā then replied. “My daughter-in-law is devoted to those of brāhmaṇa stock. She should eat it. What doubt is there?”**

*provāca gārgī na kilauṣadhādāv*  
*abhakṣya-bhakṣyasya bhaved vicāraḥ \|*  
*tatrāpi bhūdeva-kulasya śeṣaṁ*  
*rājāpi bhuṅkte kim utānya-jātiḥ \|\|57\|\|*

**Gārgī said, “There is no consideration of edible or inedible concerning medicines. As well. even the king eats the remnants of the brāhmaṇas. What to speak of other castes.”**

*praviṣṭavatyāḥ svagṛhaṁ tataḥ sā*  
*vidyāvaleḥ pāda-yugaṁ sa-putrā \|*  
*ādhāvayat taṁ salilaṁ sva-vadhvāś*  
*cikṣepa mūrdhākṣi-mukhorasi drāk \|\|58\|\|*

**When Vidyāvali entered Jaṭilā’s house, Jaṭilā and her son washed her feet with water and immediately sprinkled that water on Rādhā’s head, eyes, face and chest.**

*proce snuṣe kāpi mahānubhāvā*  
*gargasya putryāgamad atra bhāgyāt \|*  
*sā susthayiṣyaty acireṇa vijñā*  
*mantrais tv aṅgāni muhuḥ spṛśantī \|\|59\|\|*

**Jaṭilā said, “O daughter-in-law! What great fortune! By good luck, the daughter of Garga has come. Being learned, she will quickly cure you. She will touch your limbs constantly while uttering mantras.**

*kiṁ cāhivalli-dala-vīṭikāṁ ca*  
*saṁcarvya dantaiḥ paṭhitaiḥ sva-mantraiḥ \|*  
*nidhāsyate tvan-mukha-madhya eva*  
*ghṛṇā na kāryā śapatho mamātra \|\|60\|\|*

**“Chewing betel nut and its leaf with her teeth while chanting mantras she will place that in your mouth. Promise me that you will not have contempt for this.”**

*vidyāvalis tan-nilayaṁ praviṣṭā*  
*vilokya rādhāṁ vasanāvṛtāṅgīm \|*  
*vadhvāḥ padān mastakataś ca vastram*  
*udañcayādau jaratīm avocat \|\|61\|\|*

**Entering the room. Vidyāvali saw Rādhā covered with cloth. She told Jaṭilā, “First remove the cloth of your daughter-in-law covering her from foot to head.**

*bhujaṅga-mantrair abhimantrya pāṇiṁ*  
*sañcālayāmy aṅghrita ūrdhva-gātre \|*  
*yad yāvad aṅgaṁ viṣam āruroha*  
*tathaiva tan nirviṣayāmi mantraiḥ \|\|62\|\|*

**“Chanting the snake mantra, I will move my hand from her feet to her upper limbs. To whatever limb the poison has ascended, I will neutralize the poison with the mantras.”**

*tataś calan pāṇir agād amuṣyā*  
*vakṣaḥ-sthalaṁ nordhvam ataḥ paraṁ yat \|*  
*udghāṭayām āsa muhuḥ karābhyām*  
*asyā uro gāruḍa-mantra-pāṭhaiḥ \|\|63\|\|*

**Her moving hand came to Rādhā’s breasts and then did not go further. She rubbed her chest with her two hands while chanting Garuḍa mantras.**

*vidyāvaliḥ prākhyad aho kim etat*  
*viṣaṁ na śāmyet karavāṇi kiṁ vā \|*  
*vṛddhā ‘bravīt svāsyata auṣadhaṁ tad*  
*āsye snuṣāyāḥ kṣipa bhojayāmum \|\|64\|\|*

**Vidyāvali then said, “Ah! What is this? This does not neutralize the poison. What shall I do?” Jaṭilā said, “Put the medicine from your mouth into my daughter-in-law’s mouth and make her eat it.”**

*muhur muhuḥ prākṣipam auṣadhaṁ tad*  
*āsye amuṣyāḥ kṛta-mantra-pāṭhā \|*  
*tathāpi vaivarṇavatī vadhūs te*  
*prakampate niśvasiti pragāḍham \|\|65\|\|*

**“I have put the medicine in her mouth many times and recited the mantra. But your daughter-in-law has changed color, is shaking violently and is breathing heavily.**

*sarve bahir yāta gṛhaṁ kavāṭe-*  
*nāvṛtya sarpasya japāmi mantram \|*  
*muhūrta-mātreṇa tam eva sarpam*  
*āhūya tenāpi sahālapāmi \|\|66\|\|*

**“Everyone should go outside the room and close the door. I will utter the snake mantra, and, calling the snake in moment, will speak with it.**

*cintā na kāryā tila-mātry api drāk*  
*saṁjīvayiṣyāmi vadhūṁ tvadīyām \|*  
*ekāgra-cittā ghaṭikā-trayānte*  
*mantraṁ prajapyākhilam īkṣayāmi \|\|67\|\|*

**“Do not worry at all. I will revive your daughter-in-law very quickly. Chanting the mantra with full concentration, after an hour I will observe everything.”**

*gārgī-girā tā yayur anya-gehaṁ*  
*muhūrtataś cāyayur apy athātra \|*  
*vidyāvaler vācam aheś ca gopyo*  
*gṛhāntare bho śṛṇutety athocuḥ \|\|68\|\|*

**On Gārgī’s order everyone went to another room. After some time they returned. The gopīs then said, “You should all listen to the words of Vidyāvali and the snake within the room.”**

*svara-dvayenaiva jagāda kṛṣṇo*  
*yat tat tu sakhyaḥ sahasāvajagmuḥ \|*  
*yāḥ kautukānanda-samudrayor drāg*  
*āvarta-magnāḥ subhṛśaṁ virejuḥ \|\|69\|\|*

**Kṛṣṇa spoke with two different voices. The sakhīs knew this immediately and they became forcefully submerged in whirlpools in the ocean of festive bliss.**

*bhoḥ sarpa-rājātra kutas tvam āgāḥ*  
*kailāsataḥ kasya nideśa-kṛt tvam \|*  
*candrārdha-mauleḥ sa ca kīdṛśo’bhūd*  
*bhuṅkṣvābhimanyuṁ jaṭilā-sutaṁ drāk \|\|70\|\|*

**“O king of the snakes! From where have you come?” “I have come from Kaiḷāsa.” “Whose orders to you follow?” “I take orders from Śiva.” “What are his instructions?” “He ordered me to immediately eat Abhimanyu, son of Jaṭilā.”**

*āgaḥ kim etasya na kiñca kintu*  
*tan-mātur evāsty aparādha-yugmam \|*  
*sā kiṁ na daṣṭā garalānalād apy*  
*apatya-śokāgnir atīva-tīvraḥ \|\|71\|\|*

**“What sin has Abhimanyu committed?” “Nothing, but his mother has committed two offenses.” “Why do you not bite his mother then?” “The fire of grief for a son is much more intense than the fire of poison.**

*tayānubhūto bhavatu pragāḍham*  
*ity etad-arthaṁ nahi daśyate sā \|*  
*tyaktvābhimanyuṁ katham asya jāyā*  
*daṣṭātra sādhavya-vara-pradānāt \|\|72\|\|*

**“Her experience will be more intense with death of her son. For this reason I will not bite her.” “Why did you bite his wife instead?” “Because of Durvāsā’s benediction that her husband should live as long as she lives.**

*durvāsasāsau prathamaṁ na tasmād*  
*daṣṭaḥ sa daṣṭavya iha prabhāte \|*  
*putrasya vadhvāś ca yathātiśokair*  
*jājvalyate sā nikhilaṁ svam āyuḥ \|\|73\|\|*

**“Therefore I will not bite him first. He will be bitten at dawn. Jaṭilḷa will then suffer her whole life with extreme lamentation for her son and daughter-in-law.”**

*kiṁ hanta tasyā aparādha-yugmaṁ*  
*durvāsasi śrīla-hara-svarūpe \|*  
*kaṭākṣa eko’sty aparan tu śambhor*  
*ya iṣṭa devo harir asya cāṁśe \|\|74\|\|*

**“What were Jaṭilā’s two offenses?” She leered at Durvāsā, who is another form of Śiva, whose deity is Viṣṇu.**

*nandātmaje’līka-mahāpavādas*  
*tad-bhojanev bādha-karaḥ-sva-vadhvāḥ \|*  
*nirodhatas tan-nija-kanyayā sā*  
*sārdhaṁ vraje roditu sarva-kālam \|\|75\|\|*

**“She spread false rumors about Kṛṣṇa, who is the source of Viṣṇu. She then prevented Kṛṣna from eating by stopping Rādhā cooking for Kṛṣṇa. May she weep in Vraja eternally along with her daughter.”**

*hā putra hā prāṇa-same snuṣe kiṁ*  
*śṛṇomi hā hanta cirāyuṣau staḥ \|*  
*vidyāvale tvac-caraṇau prapannā*  
*prasādayāmuṁ bhujagādhirājam \|\|76\|\|*

**“O my son! O daughter-in-law, my very life! Will I ever hear the blessing ‘May you two life a long life’? O Vidyāvali! I surrender to your feet. Please pacify the king of snakes.**

*vadhūṁ na rotsyāmi kadāpi seyaṁ*  
*prayātu nandasya gṛhaṁ yatheṣṭham \|*  
*sambhojayanty eva hariṁ prakāmaṁ*  
*paktvā punar mad-gṛham etu nityam \|\|77\|\|*

**“I will not stop my daughter-in-law. She can go to Nanda’s house much as she likes. She can feed Kṛṣṇa to his satisfaction. After cooking she can come home daily.**

*durvāsasaṁ taṁ śataśo namāmi*  
*mune’parādhaṁ mama hā kṣamasva \|*  
*jvarāturāyā atimanda-buddher*  
*ājanma-vālutayatā sthitāyāḥ \|\|78\|\|*

**I offer respects to Durvāsā a hundred times. O sage! Forgive my offense for I am old, very foolish, and crazy from birth.**

*kanyā mameyaṁ tu sadā kubuddhir*  
*vadhūm suśīlāṁ prasabhaṁ dunoti \|*  
*śrutveti mātur vacanaṁ dharaṇyāṁ*  
*nipatya soce kuṭilāpi natvā \|\|79\|\|*

**“My daughter has crooked intelligence at all times. She harasses my gentle daughter-in-law at great lengths.” Hearing her mother’s words, Kuṭilā fell to the ground lamenting.**

*kṣamasva sarpendra kṛpāṁ kuruṣva*  
*mad-bhrātaraṁ mā daśanaiva rotsye \|*  
*vadhūṁ na cāpi pravadāmi yātu*  
*tatrālibhir yatra bhavet tad-icchā \|\|80\|\|*

**Offering respects to the snake she said, “Please forgive me, O king of the snakes! Be merciful. Do not bite my brother. I will not obstruct Rādhā and will not spread rumors about her. She can go out with her friends wherever she wants.”**

*sarpo’vadad bhoḥ śṛṇutāśu gopyaḥ*  
*sādhvy eva rādhā śapatho’tra śambhoḥ \|*  
*tvaṁ cāpi kṛtvā śapathaṁ sva-sūnor*  
*mūrdhno vadātrāstu mama pratītiḥ \|\|81\|\|*

**The snake then said, “O gopīs! Please listen. Rādhā is chaste. This is the oath of Śiva himself. Swearing on the head of your son, please say this. Let me see this.”**

*tvad-ukta itthaṁ śapathaḥ kṛto ’yaṁ*  
*vadhūṁ na rotsyāmi kadāpy ahīndra!*  
*snuṣā ca putraś ca cirāya jīvatv*  
*imaṁ varaṁ me kṛpayā prayaccha (83)*

**Jaṭilā said, “I have made this oath as you said. I will not restrict my daughter-in-law again. O king of the serpent! May my son and daughter-in-law live long lives! Please grant this benediction to me.”**

*bāḍhaṁ prasanno’smi jaraty ayi tvaṁ*  
*durvāsasaṁ pūjaya bhojayasva \|*  
*rādhāṅgataḥ svaṁ garalaṁ gṛhītvā*  
*vrajāmi kailāsam ito’dhunaiva \|\|83\|\|*

**“O Jaṭilā! I am satisfied with this. You should worship and feed Durvāsā. Taking my poison from the limbs of Rādhā, I will depart from here to Kailāsa right now.**

*kṛṣṇa-pravādaṁ ca yadi snuṣāyai*  
*dadāsi dehy atra na me’sti kopaḥ \|*  
*ruṇatsi tāṁ ced aham āgatas te*  
*vadhūṁ ca putraṁ ca ruṣā daśāmi \|\|84\|\|*

**“If you accuse your daughter-in-law of relations with Kṛṣṇa, give it up and I will not be angry. If you restrict her, I will come and bite your son and daughter-in-law in anger.”**

*provāca vidyāvalir ātta-modā*  
*bho gopikā dhatta mudaṁ mahiṣṭhām \|*  
*viṣaṁ gṛhītvānta radhād ahīndro*  
*nirāmayābhūd vṛṣabhānu-putrī \|\|85\|\|*

**Vidyāvali then spoke (from inside the room). “O gopīs! Rejoice to the highest degree! The king of snakes has taken the poison from Rādhā. She has recovered.”**

*udghāṭayāmāsa yadā kavāṭaṁ*  
*tadaiva sarvāviviśur gṛhāntaḥ \|*  
*papracchur etām ayi kīdṛśītvaṁ*  
*susthāsmi tāpo mama nāsti ko’pi \|\|86\|\|*

**When Vidyāvali opened the door, everyone entered the room. They asked Rādhā about her condition. She replied “I am fine and without any pain at all.”**

*vidyāvaler aṅghri-yugaṁ praṇemur*  
*dhanyaiva vidyā tava dhanya-kīrte \|*  
*sañjīvya rādhām ayi puṇya-vithīṁ*  
*dhanyām avindas tava dhanyam āyuḥ \|\|87\|\|*

**They offered respects at Vidyāvali’s feet. “O woman, you will famous for your virtue. Your knowledge brings good fortune. Having brought Rādhā to life, you have gained great pious credits. Your life is full of good fortune.”**

*lalāga karṇe kuṭilā jaratyaḥ*  
*sā prāha kanye kim idaṁ bravīṣi \|*  
*ekena hāreṇa kim adya sarvā-*  
*laṅkāram asyā adhunaiva dāsye \|\|88\|\|*

**Kuṭilā whispered in her mother’s ear. Jaṭilā said, “O daughter! Why do you say this? Why only one necklace? I will give Vidyāvali all of Rādhā’s ornaments today.**

*snuṣe prasīda sva-kareṇa sarvā-*  
*laṅkāram etāṁ paridhāpaya tvam \|*  
*vrajeśvarī tvaj-jananī ca śīghraṁ*  
*dāsyaty anekābharaṇāni tubhyam \|\|89\|\|*

**“O Rādhā! Please decorate Vidyāvali with all your ornaments using your own hands. Yaśodā and your mother will soon give you many more ornaments.**

*vidyāvale mac-chapatho na neti*  
*mā brūhy ato maunavatī bhava tvam \|*  
*tatas tu rādhā paridhāpayantī*  
*bhūṣāmbarādi svagataṁ jagāda \|\|90\|\|*

**“O Vidyāvali! Do not refuse and curse me. Remain silent.” Rādhā placed all the ornaments and cloth on Vidyāvali, but uttered to herself.**

*yo māṁ sakhīnāṁ purato’pi naiva*  
*śaśāka sambhoktum ayaṁ priyo me \|*  
*śvaśrvā nanānduś ca samakṣam eva*  
*māṁ nirvivādaṁ samabhuṅkta bāḍham \|\|91\|\|*

**“My beloved cannot enjoy with me in front of my friends. But he has satisfied me, without my protests, in front of my mother-in-law. He has enjoyed to the fullest extent.**

*vāmyaṁ ca kartuṁ mama nāvakāśo*  
*‘bhūvaṁ paraṁ kevala-dakṣiṇaiva \|*  
*kintv adya vāñchā januṣo’py apūri*  
*tac carvitaṁ bhuktam aho muhur yat \|\|92\|\|*

**“I had no opportunity to display an uncooperative mood, but rather was completely compliant. Today, my desire held from birth is fulfilled, for I have enjoyed his chewed betel nut many times.**

*pade nipatyaiva madīya-kāntam*  
*ānīya sākṣāt samabhojayan mām \|*  
*vadhūṁ tad asyāś caraṇe nanānduḥ*  
*śvaśrvāś ca me bhaktir avicyutāstu \|\|93\|\|*

**“My mother-in-law has brought my lover and fallen at his feet. She has arranged so that I can enjoy with him. He satisfied Kuṭilā. May I have devotion to the feet of my mother-in-law and sister-in-law.**

*sambhoga-paścād api tan-nideśe*  
*śṛṅgārayāmi priyam agrato’pi \|*  
*asyā aye dhanya vidhe namas tvāṁ*  
*vṛttaṁ tavaitat kva nu varṇayāmi \|\|94\|\|*

**“After enjoying with him, on Jaṭilā’s order, I was made to decorate my beloved in front of her. O blessed creator! I offer you respects. How can I describe your actions?”**

*vidyāvaliḥ prāha bhaginy ataḥ kim*  
*ārye tvad-ājñāṁ karavai vadaitat \|*  
*yāvo gṛhaṁ śīghram ataḥ paran tu*  
*rātrir niśīthād api hy adhikābhūt \|\|95\|\|*

**Vidyāvali said, “O respected Jaṭilā! What should we do now? I will follow your order. Please tell me. Then we two will quickly go home. It is now past midnight. ”**

*jaraty avādīd ayi gārgi vidyā-*  
*valis tathā tvaṁ ca haṭhād iyatyām \|*  
*rātrau kathaṁ yāsyatha āḥ sukhena*  
*mamaiva gehe svapitaṁ kathaṁ na \|\|96\|\|*

**Jaṭilā then spoke. “O Gārgī! How can Vidyāvali and you go so late at night? Ah! Will you not comfortably sleep in my house?”**

*jagāda gārgī jaṭile tvad-uktam*  
*avaśyam etat karavāva gāḍham \|*  
*na yāti cittād viṣa-śeṣa-gandhas*  
*tad-bhāvanā me khala-sarpa-jāteḥ \|\|97\|\|*

**Gārgī then spoke. “O Jaṭilā! We will do what you have said. A remainder of the poison does not leave the heart completely. That is my thought about this type of wicked snake.”**

*provāca bāḍhaṁ jaṭilā sa-kanyā*  
*tad adya vadhvā saha puṣpa-talpe \|*  
*ekatra vidyāvalir iddha-mantrā*  
*sukhaṁ valabhyāṁ svapitu prakāmam \|\|98\|\|*

**Jaṭilā and Kuṭilā said, “That is fine. Vidyāvali, potent with mantras, should sleep comfortably next to Rādhā on the flower bed today in the topmost room for as long as she wants.”**

*itthaṁ vilāsa-rasikau rata-sindhu-cāru-*  
*hillola-khelana-kalāḥ kila tena tuṣṭau \|*  
*premābdhi-kautuka-mahiṣṭha-taraṅga-raṅge*  
*sakhyaḥ sukhena nanṛtur na virāmam āpuh \|\|99\|\|*

**In this way Rādhā and Kṛṣna, expert at tasting rasa of pastimes, are satisfied with the small portion of playing in the beautiful waves of the ocean of enjoyment. The sakhīs danced joyfully in the arena of great, festive waves in the ocean of prema and did not stop.**

## Chapter 4

*rādhā kadācid atimānavatī babhūva*  
*tāṁ na prasādayitum aiṣṭa hariḥ prasahya \|*  
*sāmādibhir bahuvidhair vitatair upāyaiḥ*  
*kaundyā sahātha kim api pratatāna mantram \|\|1\|\|*

**Once, Rādhā became very angry. Kṛṣna desired to pacify her, attempting to subdue her anger using many ways such as gentle words. He then consulted with Kundalatā.**

*bhūṣāmbarādi paridhāya vidhāya nārī-*  
*veśaṁ vikasvara-pika-svara-mañju-kaṇṭhaḥ \|*  
*sārdhaṁ tayā mṛdu raṇan-maṇi-nūpurābhyāṁ*  
*padbhyāṁ jagāma jaṭilā-nilayaṁ nilīya \|\|2\|\|*

**Using a woman’s clothing, he dressed up with cloth and ornaments. His voice sounded like the clear notes of the cuckoo. Secretly he went with Kundalatā to Jaṭilā’s house while his two feet softly jingled with jeweled ankle bells.**

*ārād vilokya sahasā sahasā sahāliḥ*  
*saundarya-vismiti-manā avadan mṛgākṣī \|*  
*ehy ehi kundalatike vada vṛttam āśu*  
*kiṁ-hetukam gamanam etad abhūd akasmāt \|\|3\|\|*

**Suddenly seeing Kṛṣṇa (dressed as a woman) at a distance, doe-eyed Rādhā who was with her friends, became astonished by that woman’s beauty. She said, “O Kundalatā! Come here. Quickly tell me the news. Why have you come so suddenly?”**

*keyaṁ kutaḥ kim abhidhānavatīti pṛṣṭā*  
*śrī-rādhayāvadad imāṁ prati kundavallī \|*  
*nāmnā kalāvalir iyaṁ mathurā-pradeśād*  
*atrāgatā śruta-bhavad-guṇa-nāma-kīrtiḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**She asked, “Who is this? Where has she come from? What is her name?” Kundalatā replied, “She is named Kalāvali. She comes for Mathurā district. Hearing of your name and qualities she has come here.”**

*gānair girāṁ gurum api prabhaved vijetuṁ*  
*kiṁ vācyam etad avagacchata gāpayitvā \|*  
*kasmād aśikṣad iyatīm ayi gāna-vidyāṁ*  
*sākṣāt purandara-guroḥ kva nu tat-prasaṅgaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**“She defeats the guru of singing with her songs. What more can be said? Have her sing and you will understand.” “From whom did she learn such expertise in singing?” “She learned from Bṛhaspati in person.” “How did she meet him?”**

*satraṁ yad āṅgirasam atra varāṅgi vṛṣṇi-*  
*puryāṁ vyatanyata nu māthura-vipra-varyaiḥ \|*  
*tarhy eva so ’mara-purāt sahasaitya māsam*  
*vāsaṁ vidhāya paramādṛta ānananda \|\|6\|\|*

**“When the best of Mathurā’s brāhmaṇas performed an āṅgirasa sacrifice in Mathurā, Bṛhaspati suddenly came from Svarga and stayed for a month. He was honored and greatly pleased.**

*madhye sabhāṁ sa hi kadācid agāyad ekaṁ*  
*gītaṁ yadaitad adadhād iyam āli sadyaḥ \|*  
*medhāvatī tad aparedyur aho jagau tat*  
*tena svareṇa bata tair api tāna-tālaiḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**“One day in the assembly Bṛhaspati sang a song. O friend! The next day the intelligent girl promptly sang the very same song with the same notes and same rhythms.**

*śrutvā bṛhaspatir aho mama gītam ārāt*  
*kā gāyatīti bahu-vismayavān avādīt \|*  
*martyāpy aśikṣad api tat sakṛd-uktito yad*  
*durgaṁ dyu-gānam api vipra tad ānayaitām \|\|8\|\|*

**“Hearing the song, astonished Bṛhaspati said, ‘Who is singing my song? Though she is human, she learned this difficult song from heaven though I sang it only once. O brāhmaṇa! Bring her here.’**

*viprādeśam avāpya gīṣpati-puro yātām imāṁ so’bravīt*  
*tvām adyāpayitāsmi dhīmatiparāṁ gāndharva-vidyām aham \|*  
*medhā te’nupamā pikāli-vijayī kaṇṭho yadā dṛśyate*  
*naitādṛṅ manujeṣu labdha-januṣāṁ no kinnarīṇām api \|\|9\|\|*

**“Following his order, she was brought before Bṛhaspati. He said to her, ‘O intelligent girl! I will teach you music. Your intelligence is beyond compare. Your voice surpasses the cuckoo’s song. Such talent cannot be see among humans or even Kinnaras.’**

*adhyāpya māsam iha varṣam api svayaṁ svar-*  
*nītām apāṭhayad imām iyam āśvinānte \|*  
*prāptāvanīṁ madhupurīm agamad vraje hyaḥ*  
*sāyaṁ tathādya tu tavāgrataḥ āgatābhūt \|\|10\|\|*

**“He taught her for a month in Mathurā and taught her for a year in Svarga. At the end of Āśvina month she returned to earth and came to Mathurā yesterday. Today in the evening she has come before you in Vraja.”**

*tad gīyatāṁ kim api bhāvini kaṁ nu rāgaṁ*  
*gāyāmi mālavam iha prathama-pradoṣe*  
*kiṁ vā svaraṁ sumukhi saḍjam atha śrutiṁ vā*  
*kiṁ vacmi tatra catasṛṣv iti cādiśa tvam \|\|11\|\|*

**“Beautiful Kalāvali! Will you sing?” “Which rāga shall I sing?” “Since it is the beginning of evening sing mālava rāga.” “Which notes shall I use, beautiful faced Rādhā?” “Use the first note.” “Which of the four microtones should I use? Please tell me.”**

*kaṇṭhe śrutir na tava vāta-kaphādi-doṣāt*  
*śuddhā bhaviṣyati kadāpi vinaiva vīṇām \|*  
*tad rāga-tāna-gamaka-svara-jāti-tāla-*  
*grāma-śriyā madhuram ātanu gītam ekam \|\|12\|\|*

**“Without the accompaniment of a vīṇā the śruti will never be pure in the throat, because of faults in vāta and kapha. Sing one sweet song with beauty of rāga, tāna, gamaka, svara, jāti, tāla and grāma.”**

*rādhe vinaiva bhavatīm iha gāna-vidyāṁ*  
*jānanti kāḥ kalayatāmilitāḥ śrutīs tāḥ \|*  
*procyettham ātanuta keky-ali-vṛnda-nindi*  
*tānānanā-tanana rīti-surīti-gānam \|\|13\|\|*

**“O Rādhā! Who here knows the art of singing other than you? Everyone listen to this song, without śrutis.” Saying this she sang the syllables tā-nā-na-nā ta-na-na in a beautiful manner which defeated the singing of bees and peacocks.**

*ādau priyāli-vitater nayanāśru-nadyaḥ*  
*saśrus tata sthagitatāṁ yayur eva madhye \|*  
*antya-kṣaṇe tu karakopalatām avāpya*  
*petuṣ ṭhanat-ṭhanad iti-kṣiti-pṛṣṭha eva \|\|14\|\|*

**In the beginning of the song, rivers of tears flowed from the eyes of all the sakhīs. Then tears stagnated in the middle of the song. At the end of the song, the tears became hail stones and fell to the earth making a pattering sound.**

*tasyāḥ kaṭhoratara-māna-juṣaṁ tu cittaṁ*  
*hīropalaṁ dravam avāpa yadaiva sadyaḥ \|*  
*sāścaryam ākhyad ayi devi kalāvale tvad-*  
*gānaṁ sudhāṁ sura-purasya tiraskaroti \|\|15\|\|*

**When Rādhā’s heart which was as hard as a diamond because of her intense anger began to suddenly melt, she exclaimed in astonishment “O goddess Kalāvali! Your song defeats the nectar of Svarga.”**

*tvādṛg-jano yadi mamāntika eva tiṣṭhed*  
*bhāgyāj janus tad akhilaṁ saphalīkaromi \|*  
*nandātmajo yadi punaḥ śṛṇuyād guṇaṁ te*  
*kaṇṭhād bahir na hi karoti tadā kadāpi \|\|16\|\|*

**She said, “If a person like you were to stay with me, my birth would be completely successful because of good fortune. If Kṛṣṇa heard of your qualities he could not take you from his neck.”**

*abrūta kundalatikā na vadaitad etāṁ*  
*sādhvīṁ tvam eva nija-kaṇṭha-taṭīṁ nayainām \|*  
*naivānyathā kuru tatas tu parārdhya-niṣkaṁ*  
*ditsuḥ sukhena parirabdham iyeṣa rādhā \|\|17\|\|*

**Kundalatā then spoke. “Do not say this to this chaste girl. Bring her to your own neck and embrace her. Do not do anything else.” Rādhā went to embrace her with joy, desiring to give her a valuable necklace.**

*karṇe lalāga lalitātha vimṛṣya subhrūr*  
*ūce bravīṣi vara-varṇini satyam etat \|*  
*sammānanaṁ samucitaṁ na hi niṣka-dānāt*  
*syāt tena sarva-vasanā-bharaṇāni dāsye \|\|18\|\|*

**Considering the situation, beautiful Lalitā then whispered in her ear. Considering this, Rādhā then said, “O Lalitā! You speak the truth. I cannot give proper respect to her by giving her the necklace. Therefore I will give her all my cloth and ornaments.”**

*tad rūpa-mañjari mad-agrata eva yūyaṁ*  
*citrāmbarāṇi paridhāpayata prayatnaiḥ \|*  
*udghāṭya samprati purātana-kañcukaṁ drāk*  
*navyaṁ samarpayata tuṅga-kuca-dvaye’syāḥ \|\|19\|\|*

**“O Rūpa Mañjarī! In front of me, you all should dress her carefully in colorful clothing. Take off her old bodice and quickly cover her raised breasts with a new one.”**

*kaundy abravīt sumukhi nodghaṭayāṅgam asyāḥ*  
*saṅkocam āpsati paraṁ bhavad-agra eṣā \|*  
*tad dehi yad yad ayi ditsasi sarvam etad*  
*gatvā sva-dhāma paridhāsyati na tv ihaiva \|\|20\|\|*

**Kundalatā then said, “O Rādhā! Do not take her clothes off. She will become very embarrassed in front of you. Just give her everything you want to give her. Going to her house she can put them on there, not here.”**

*na strī-sadasy ayi bhayaṁ kurute hriyaṁ ca*  
*strīti prasiddhir adhikā sakhi sarva-deśe \|*  
*ānanda- vartmani kathaṁ na yiyāsasi tvaṁ*  
*saṅkoca-kañcukam ihārpayasi svayaṁ kim \|\|21\|\|*

**“O friend! It is well known everywhere that a woman has no fear or shyness among other women. Why do you not want to tread on the road of bliss? Why do you not offer up your bodice of reserve among us?”**

*rādhe na mālya-vasanābharaṇādi kiñcid*  
*aṅgīkaromi kim u gāyaka-kanyakāham \|*  
*tvaṁ cet prasīdasi sakṛt parirambham ekaṁ*  
*dehy ehi māṁ na dhana-gṛdhnum avehi mugdhe \|\|22\|\|*

**“O Rādhā! I will not accept at all garlands, clothing or ornaments. Am I the daughter of a professional singer? If you are pleased with me, come and give me one embrace. O innocent girl! Do not consider me greedy for wealth.”**

*vāmyaṁ kim atra kuruṣe paridhehi sādhu*  
*no ced balād api vayaṁ paridhāpayāmaḥ \|*  
*ekā tvam eva śataśo vayam ity atas te*  
*svātantryam astu katham ity avadhehi mugdhe \|\|23\|\|*

**“Virtuous girl! Why are you so stubborn at this place? Please put on the clothing. If you do not, we will dress you by force. You are one and we are many. Why are you acting independently? Innocent girl, please note this.”**

*dve skandhayor dadhatur añcalam agrato shāḥ*  
*pṛṣṭhe vyamocayata kañcuka-bandham ekā \|*  
*vakṣaḥ-sthalād apatatāṁ subṛhat-kadamba-*  
*puṣpe tadā sapadi kartita kiñcid aṁśe \|\|24\|\|*

**Two sakhīs in front took the cloth from her shoulders while one sakhī in the back unfastened her bodice. From her chest two large kadamba flowers, cut on one side, fell out.**

*kiṁ hanta kiṁ patitam etad ayīti pṛṣṭā*  
*dāsyo’khilā jahasur eva sa-hasta-tālaṁ \|*  
*labdhāvaguṇṭhana-paṭā rabhasān mṛgākṣī*  
*pṛṣṭhīcakāra tam atho vṛṣabhānu-putrī \|\|25\|\|*

**Rādhā asked, “Oh! What has fallen out?" All the servants laughed while clapping their hands.**  
**Quickly covering her face with her veil, Rādhā turned her back to Kṛṣṇa.**

*ālī-kulasya sa durācara eva vaktre*  
*vastrāvṛto’py ajani sa-svana eva hāsaḥ \|*  
*rādhāpy adhān nibhṛtam asvanam eva hāsyaṁ*  
*kṛṣṇaś ca kundalatikā ca jahāsa paścāt \|\|26\|\|*

**Though the sakhīs covered their faces with their cloth because it would be impolite, they burst into loud laughter. Rādhā also laughed, but very quietly. Kṛṣṇa and Kundalatā then laughed.**

*mūrto hāsya-raso muhūrtam abhavat sadyas tataḥ procire*  
*sakhyo hanta bṛhat-kadamba-kusume dhanye yuvāṁ bhū-tale \|*  
*dhūrte prāpita-kaitave api punar niṣkaitave antato*  
*bhūtvā hāsa-rasāmṛtābdhim anu ye sarvā nidhattaḥ sma naḥ \|\|27\|\|*

**Hāsya-rasa incarnate appeared for some time. Then the sakhīs proclaimed, “Ah! O two kadamba flowers! You are fortunate on earth. Though you are cunning and became cheaters, finally you have become honest since you have placed us all in the sweet ocean of hāsya-rasa.”**

*bho bhoḥ kundalate kva te sahacarī lajjā na sā dṛśyate*  
*pātālasya tale mamajja salile sā kundavallyā saha \|*  
*tu cchāyaiva bhavāmi hanta vigata cchāyātra vaḥ kiṁ bruve*  
*tad yuṣmad-vadaneṣu nṛtyati girāṁ devī yatheṣṭhaṁ muhuḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**“O Kundalatā! Where is your assistant bashfulness? She is nowhere to be seen.” “She has drowned in the ocean at the bottom of Pātāla along with Kundavalī. I am her shadow.” “But the shadow would disappear with the person.” “What shall I say to you? The goddess of speech dances on your faces constantly. ”**

*premnā gīṣpati-śiṣyayā saha sadā satsaṅga ājanmato*  
*mithyā vāṅ na ca jihvayā paricitā sādhvīḥ sva-dharmaṁ muhuḥ \|*  
*adhyāpyātanu-karma kārayasi te khyātir vraje bhūyasī*  
*nādyābhūt tava vāñchitaṁ yad iyatī kāpi vyathā sahyatām \|\|29\|\|*

**Lalitā said, “From birth you have always associated with the disciple of Bṛhaspati out of love. You do not speak lies. Instructing chaste girls about their duties constantly, you work on behalf of Cupid. Your fame is great in Vraja. Since your desire was not fulfilled today, you have to tolerate so much pain.**

*ānītā vividha-prayatna-racitā vidyātidūrād guror*  
*vikretuṁ sudhiyā tvayādya rabhasād gopī-sadasyāpaṇe \|*  
*vikrītā nahi sābhavat punar aho hāsyāspadībhūtatāṁ*  
*prāptā drāg aśubha-kṣaṇaḥ sa hi yad āyātaṁ bhavadbhyām iha \|\|30\|\|*

**“With great intelligence today you eagerly brought your guru’s knowledge produced with various efforts from far away to sell at the marketplace of the gathering of gopīs. It was not sold but immediately became the object of laughter. You two came at an inauspicious time.**

*atrāpaṇe drutam imāṁ lalite’dya vidyāṁ*  
*vikrīya vāñchitam ahaṁ yadi sādhayiṣye \|*  
*tvat-kañcukaṁ vitarasīha na ced dadāmi*  
*tubhyaṁ sva-kañcukam ahaṁ kriyatāṁ paṇo’yam \|\|31\|\|*

**Kṛṣṇa said, “O Lalitā! If I easily sell this knowledge in the market and fulfill my desire, you must give me your bodice. If you do not, I will sell you my bodice. This is the wager.”**

*śuṣkaṁ prasūnam ayi korakatāṁ na gacchet*  
*prāṇe gate na khalu ceṣṭata eva dehaḥ \|*  
*dambhī kathaṁ vidita-tattva upaiti pūjāṁ*  
*svāmin mṛṣā-pratibhayālam alaṁ prayāhi \|\|32\|\|*

**Lalitā said, “O deceptive Kṛṣṇa! A dried up flower does not become a bud. When the life airs are gone, the body does not move. How can a person whose deceit is revealed be worshipped? O master! Enough of your deceptive intelligence! Enough! Please go away!”**

*kṛṣṇaḥ sva-vakṣasi punaḥ kusuma-dvayaṁ tan*  
*nītvā jagāma jaṭilā-gṛham eva sadyaḥ \|*  
*soccaiḥ svayaṁ bhūvi nipatya tathā ruroda*  
*yenākuleva jaṭilā muhur āpa khedam \|\|33\|\|*

**Kṛṣṇa then took the two kadamba flowers to his chest and quickly went to Jaṭilā’s room. He fell on the ground and loudly cried out so that bewildered Jaṭilā began lamenting.**

*kā tvaṁ rodiṣi kiṁ kuto’si kim abhūt te vipriyaṁ putri tat*  
*sarvaṁ brūhi vimṛjya locana-jala-klinnaṁ mukhāmbhoruham \|*  
*kiṁ hanta prabhavāmi bhāgya-rahitā dhiṅ me janur dhik tanuṁ*  
*dhiṅ māṁ dhig dhig iti prabaddha-davathuḥ proce’rdham ardhaṁ vacaḥ \|\|34\|\|*

**“Who are you? Why are you crying? Where did you come from? Has some misfortune struck? O daughter! Tell me everything while wiping the tears from your lotus face.” Speaking with great pain and weak voice Kalāvalit said, “Oh! I am so misfortunate. What use is my birth? What use is my body? What use am I?**

*vāso me vṛṣabhānu-bhūpa-nagare śrī-kīrtidāyāḥ svasuḥ*  
*kanyāhaṁ saha rādhayā mama sadā suprītir ābālyataḥ \|*  
*āyātāsmi cirād ahaṁ nija-gṛhāt tāṁ draṣṭum utkaṇṭhayā*  
*sā māṁ naiva vilokate na vadati premnā na cāliṅgati \|\|35\|\|*

**“I live in the town of King Vṛṣabhānu. I am the daughter of Kīrtidā’s sister. I have had affection for Rādhā since childhood. In great longing, I came after a long time from my house to see her. She did not even look at me. She does not speak to me. She did not embrace me with affection.**

*māṁ dṛṣṭvādriyitena naiva kuśala-praśnaṁ karoty ādarāt*  
*tat prāṇair mama kiṁ prayojanam imāṁs tyakṣyāmy ahaṁ tvat-puraḥ \|*  
*ārye tvaṁ vimṛṣāvadhāraya kadā ko me’parādho’bhavat*  
*tāṁ tvaṁ pṛccha muhuḥ pradāya śapathaṁ sā me kathaṁ kupyati \|\|36\|\|*

**“Not looking at me with respect, she did not ask about my health out of concern. What is the use of my life? I will give up my life in front of you. O respectable woman! Reflecting, try to understand if I have committed any offense. Please ask her, making an oath, why she is anger with me.”**

*vatse samāsvasihi ko’pi na te’parādho*  
*gacchāmi sarvam adhunaiva samādadhāmi \|*  
*taṁ snehayāmi bhavatīṁ parirambhayāmi*  
*saṁlāpayāmi rajanīṁ saha śāyayāmi \|\|37\|\|*

**Jaṭilā saidk, “O child! Please be calm. You have not committed any offense. I will go right now and solve the problem. I will make Rādhā show affection for you, embrace you and speak to you. I will have you sleep with her.”**

*ity uktvā sahasā snuṣālayam agād dṛṣṭvālipālīḥ puraḥ*  
*prāvocal lalite kim īdṛg abhavad vadhvāḥ svabhāvo’dhunā \|*  
*tasyās tāta-purād iyaṁ sva-bhaginīṁ tāṁ draṣṭum utkaṇṭhayai-*  
*āgāt sā katham atra sa-praṇayam āśv enāṁ na sambhāṣate \|\|38\|\|*

**Saying this, Jaṭilā went quickly to Rādhā’s room. Seeing all the sakhīs in front, she said, “O Lalitā! Why is my daughter-in-law’s nature like this now? This girl came from her father’s village with great eagerness to see her sister. Why does Rādhā not talk to her with affection immediately at this place?**

*paśyaiṣā nayanāśru-sikta-sicayā khinnā asmad antar mahā-*  
*kāraṇyaṁ janayaty ataḥ sucarite sādguṇya-pūrṇe snuṣe \|*  
*enāṁ sādhu pariṣvaja sva-kuśalaṁ pṛccha priyaṁ kiñcana*  
*brūhy asyā hṛdaya-vyathāpasaratu prīṇihi māṁ prīṇaya \|\|39\|\|*

**“Just look at this lamenting girl whose clothes are soaked with tears. My heart feels great compassion for her. Since you are of good conduct, full of all good qualities, O my daughter-in-law, please embrace her nicely and ask affectionately about her health. Say something to her. Remove the pain in her heart. Satisfy her and satisfy me.”**

*ārye yāhi gṛhaṁ yathā diśasi tat kurve sukhenādhunā*  
*tvaṁ tv etāvati bālikā-jana-vṛthā-vāde svayaṁ māpata \|*  
*ālyas tā sadṛśo’lpa-buddhi-vayaso’bhīkṣṇa-prasāda-krudhas*  
*tāsu tvādṛg apāra-buddhir atulā prāmāṇikī kiṁ patet \|\|40\|\|*

**Rādhā said “Respected lady! Go to your room! As you have instructed, I will happily do at once. Do not personally become involved in such false talks of young girls. The sakhīs are like this. They have meager intelligence and age and are always pleased or angry. Why should your superior intelligence, incomparable and authoritative, degrade itself?”**

*uttiṣṭha mā vada paraṁ mama mūrdhna eva*  
*datto mayā śapatha āśu gale gṛhāṇa \|*  
*ātma-svasāram anayā saha bhuṅkṣva miṣṭaṁ*  
*mā bhindhi me guru-janasya nideśam etam \|\|41\|\|*

**Jaṭila said, “Stand up. Do not speak further. I make an oath upon my head. Please embrace her immediately. Eat sweets with your sister. Do not break this order of your elder.”**

*ārye sa-prauḍhi mām ādiśasi yadi tato vacmi satyaṁ yad eṣā*  
*prāvocat kundavallīṁ kaṭutaram adhikaṁ duḥsahaṁ tena kopāt \|*  
*nāsyā vaktraṁ viloke yadi punar adhunā seyam asyāṁ prasīdet*  
*tarhy evāhaṁ prasannā diśasi yad akhilaṁ tat karomy eva bāḍham \|\|42\|\|*

**Rādhā said, “O respected lady! Since you have spoken to me strongly, I must speak the truth. This girl spoke intolerably harsh words to Kundalatā. Because of this, I am angry and do not want to see her face. If again she pleases Kundalatā then I will be pleased and will do as you have instructed.”**

*ārye vakti mṛṣā snuṣā tava na mām eṣā kaṭu vyāharat*  
*nāpy asyai kupitāsmi tāṁ prati tataḥ provāca rādhā sphuṭam \|*  
*kiṁ mithyā vadasīha kupyasi na ced asyai prasīdasy alaṁ*  
*kaṇṭha-grāham iyaṁ tvayādya rabhasād āliṅgyatām agrataḥ \|\|43\|\|*

**Kundalatā said, “O lady! Your daughter-in-law is lying. Kalāvali did not deal with me harshly and I am not angry with her.” Rādhā then addressed Kundalatā in a clear voice. “How can you lie in front of Jaṭilā? If you are not anger with her, and are pleased with her then firmly embrace her right now in front of everyone.”**

*tūṣṇīṁ sthitāṁ sapadi kundalatāṁ vilokya*  
*prāha sma sapratibham eva tadā mṛgākṣī \|*  
*ārye parāmṛśa ciraṁ katarābravīn no*  
*mithyeti tāṁ paribhavasya vidhehi pātrīm \|\|44\|\|*

**Seeing Kundalatā suddenly standing there silently, Rādhā spoke cleverly. “O respected Jaṭilā! Decide finally which of us lying and then deal with the disgraced one.**

*etāṁ yad atra na pariṣvajate saharṣaṁ*  
*tat-kopa-liṅgam iha kaḥ khalu saṁśayaḥ syāt \|*  
*vṛddhāvadan mama vadhūr iha vakti satyam*  
*antaḥ prasīdati na kundalatā tad asyām \|\|45\|\|*

**“If Kundalatā does not embrace Kalāvali with joy, there must be some doubt. She must be angry with her.” Jaṭilā said, “My daughter-in-law speaks the truth about this. Kundalatā is not pleased with her in her heart.”**

*yena prasīdasi tad eva karomi kaundi*  
*mānyā smi te’dya racitāñjalir asmi tubhyam \|*  
*vīkṣyaiva man-mukham imāṁ parirabdhum ehi*  
*nātaḥ paraṁ vada hahā śapatho mamātra \|\|46\|\|*

**Jaṭilā continued to speak. “O Kundalatā! I will do whatever it takes so that you are happy. Though I am to be honored today I come to you with folded hands. Before my eyes go and embrace her. Do not speak further. This is my oath.**

*āryā dadāti śapathaṁ na bibheṣy ato’pi*  
*kā dhīr iyaṁ tava tad ehi pariṣvajasva \|*  
*ity ālayaś ca jaṭilā-kuṭile ca dhṛtvai-*  
*vāliṅgayan bata mitho hari-kundavallyau \|\|47\|\|*

**“Do you not fear the oath that Jaṭilā made? What are you thinking? Go and embrace Kalāvali.” Then the sakhīs, Jaṭilā and Kuṭilā grabbed Kundalatā and made her embrace Kṛṣṇa (disguised as Kalāvali).**

*vṛddhā tadā kila na ced abhaviṣyad ārād*  
*ālī-tater hasa-raso na vikāśam aiṣyat \|*  
*tāś cela-ruddha-vadanās tad api prahāsaṁ*  
*niḥśabdam eva vidadhuś ca dadhuś ca modam \|\|48\|\|*

**If Jaṭilā had not been present, the sakhīs would not have been able to restrain their laughter. Covering their faces with their veils, they laughed silently and became joyful.**

*vṛddhā vadhūm atha jagāda nija-svasāraṁ*  
*brūhi priyaṁ parirabhasva ca nirvivādam \|*  
*ity ātmapāṇi-vidhṛtau drutam eva rādhā*  
*kṛṣṇau mitho’tiparirambham avāpayat tau \|\|49\|\|*

**Jaṭilā then spoke to Rādhā. “Please speak to your sister and affectionately embrace her without argument. Grasping them in her hands, she made Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa tightly embrace.**

*harṣāśru-bindu-nikaraṁ nudataṁ pratisvaṁ*  
*celena bhoḥ sukhayataṁ ca mitho bhaginyau \|*  
*sambhujya kiñcana sukhena kṛtaika-talpa-*  
*svāpe dṛḍha-praṇayato nayataṁ triyāmām \|\|50\|\|*

**“Wipe each others’ tears of joy with your cloth. You two sisters should make each other happy. Eat something happily and spend the night sleeping together on one bed out of strong affection.”**

*vṛddhā jagāma śayituṁ nija-geham ārāt*  
*kṛṣṇaḥ pragalbhataratāṁ dadhad ākhyad ālīḥ \|*  
*vidyāṁ vigītata-matāṁ gamitām api drāg*  
*vikrīya vāñchitam avindam atojitā stha \|\|51\|\|*

**Jaṭilā went to sleep in her room at some distance. Kṛṣṇa became bold and spoke to the sakhīs. “Quickly selling the knowledge that you had criticized, I have attained my desire. You are defeated.”**

*bhrātur vadhūr yad iha bhoḥ samabhoji tasmād*  
*adyaiva vāñchitam alambhi jayaś ca bhūyān \|*  
*setur yadi truṭata eva tad-ardha bhuktvā*  
*naivāstv iyaṁ bhavatu pūrṇa-manorathaiva \|\|52\|\|*

**Lalitā said, “Since you have enjoyed your sister-in-law, Kundalatā, you have attained your desired goal and great victory. If the rules of society have been broken, why should she be left half-enjoyed? Fulfill her desire completely!”**

*bhrātrāpi śuddha-manasā bhaginī-sutāpi*  
*pitrā ca kiṁ na parirabhyata eva loke \|*  
*yuṣmākam ānakha-śikhaṁ smara-bhāva eva*  
*vyaktas tad-ātma-samam eva jagac ca vettha \|\|53\|\|*

**Kundalatā said, “O Lalitā! Cannot a brother embrace his sister or a father his daughter with pure heart in this world? Your whole body is manifesting lust from head to foot and thus you see the whole world in terms of your lust.”**

*ity uktavaty atiruṣeva nivedya kunda-*  
*vallī bahir-bhavanam eva yadādhyatiṣṭhat \|*  
*tasyāḥ prasādana-kṛte niraguś ca sakhyas*  
*tatraika eva kusumeṣur apād yuvānau \|\|54\|\|*

**Saying this, Kundalatā, making a show of anger, went to the outer room. The sakhīs went to pacify her. Only Cupid remained to protect the couple.**

*sa-bhrū-vibhaṅga kuṭilāsya-saroja-sīdhu*  
*mādyan madhu-vrata-vilāsa-susaurabhāṇi \|*  
*samprāpya jala-vivareṣu jughūrṇur eva*  
*preṣṭhālayaḥ prati-padaṁ pramadormi-puñje \|\|55\|\|*

**Attaining the sweet varieties of fragrance from the pastimes of the bee intoxicated with the nectar of Rādhā’s lotus face disfigured by her frowning brow, the dearest sakhīs began to reel about in the waves of delight at every moment on looking through the lattice windows.**
