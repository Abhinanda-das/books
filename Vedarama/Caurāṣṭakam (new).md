# Caurāṣṭakam (new)

## Eight prayers glorifying the Divine Thief

(Upajāti meter)

### Text 1

*ādau bakī-prāṇa-malaugha-cauraṁ*  
*bālye prasiddhaṁ navanīta-cauram*  
*vraje carantaṁ ca mṛdo hi cauraṁ*  
*caurādhipaṁ kṛṣṇam ahaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*ādau*—in the beginning (of His earthly pastimes); *bakī*—of the sister of the Baka (crane) demon, i.e. Pūtanā; *prāṇa*—of life-air; *mala*—and impurity (sinful reactions); *ogha*—many; *cauram*—the thief*; bālye*—in His childhood; *prasiddham*—celebrated; *navanīta*—of butter; *cauram*—the thief*; vraje*—in Vraja; *carantam*—to one who is walking; *ca*—and; *mṛdaḥ*—of clay; *hi*—indeed; *cauram*—the thief*; caura*—of thieves; *adhipam*—to the king; *kṛṣṇam*—to Lord Kṛṣṇa; *aham*—I; *namāmi*—offer obeisances.

**Перевод:**

**I offer obeisances to the king of thieves, Lord Kṛṣṇa. In the beginning of His pastimes He stole the life-air of the sister of demon Baka—Pūtanā—along with all her sins. In His childhood He was celebrated as the thief of butter. And roaming in Vraja, He once stealthily ate some clay.**

**Комментарий:**

First Pūtanā’s life and wicked sins, He’s stolen.

His childhood happiness-fresh butter, He’s stolen.

He steals the mud in Vraja, while wandering ‘round.

My respects to Kṛṣṇa, divine thievery’s crown’d.

### Text 2

*vidheḥ surendrasya ca garva-cauraṁ*  
*go-gopa-gopī-jana-citta-cauram*  
*śrī-rādhikāyā hṛdayasya cauraṁ*  
*caurādhipaṁ kṛṣṇam ahaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*vidheḥ*—of Lord Brahmā; *sura-indrasya*—of the king of demigods (Indra); *ca*—and; *garva*—of pride; *cauram*—the thief*; go-gopa-gopī-jana*—of the cows, cowherd men and women; *citta*—of the minds; *cauram*—the thief*; śrī-rādhikāyāḥ*—of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; *hṛdayasya*—of the heart; *cauram*—the thief*.*

**Перевод:**

**He stole the pride of Lord Brahmā and King Indra. He stole the minds of the cows and the villagers of Vraja. He stole the heart of Śrīmatī Rādhikā. I offer obeisances to the king of thieves, Lord Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

By whom, Brahmā’s and Indra’s vast pride, are stolen.

Cows, boys, girls-Vraja’s hosts-thoughts, He’s stolen.

Śrī Rādhikā’s heart, stolen (along with Her frown).

My respects to Kṛṣṇa, divine thievery’s crown’d.

### Text 3

*nāgādhirājasya viṣasya cauraṁ*  
*śrī-sūrya-kanyākhila-kaṣṭa-cauram*  
*gopī-janājñāna-dukūla-cauraṁ*  
*caurādhipaṁ kṛṣṇam ahaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*nāga*—of serpents; *adhirājasya*—of the king (Kāliya); *viṣasya*—of the poison; *cauram*—the thief*; śrī-sūrya-kanyā*—of the daughter of sun-god Sūrya, i.e. Yamunā; *akhila*—of all; *kaṣṭa*—misery; *cauram*—the thief*; gopī-jana*—of the cowherd girls; *ajñāna*—of the innocence; *dukūla*—and the garments; *cauram*—the thief*.*

**Перевод:**

**He stole the poison of the king of serpents, Kāliya. He stole all miseries of the daughter of the sun-god Sūrya—the Yamunā river. He stole the innocence and the garments of the cowherd girls of Vraja. I offer obeisances to the king of thieves, Lord Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

By Whom, all snake-king’s-Kāliya’s poison is stolen.

Sūrya’s daughter, Yamuṇā’s all obstacles, stolen.

Innocent Gopī’s clothes, He has stolen-renowned.

My respects to Kṛṣṇa, divine thievery’s crown’d.

### Text 4

*vatsāsurāder bala-māna-cauraṁ*  
*pitros tathā bandhana-duḥkha-cauram*  
*kubjārcana-vyāja-manoja-cauraṁ*  
*caurādhipaṁ kṛṣṇam ahaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*vatsa-asura-ādeḥ*—of the calf-demon Vatsāsura and other demons; *bala*—of the strength; *māna*—and pride; *cauram*—the thief*; pitroḥ*—of His parents, Vasudeva and Devakī; *tathā*—also; *bandhana*—of the imprisonment; *duḥkha*—of the pain; *cauram*—the thief*; kubjā*—of the hunch-backed girl Kubjā; *arcana*—of being worshiped; *vyāja*—on the pretext; *manaḥ-ja*—of the lust; *cauram*—the thief*.*

**Перевод:**

**He stole the strenght and the pride of the demons like Vatsāsura. He stole the pain of imprisonment of His parents, Vasudeva and Devakī. On the pretext of being worshiped by the hunch-backed girl Kubjā, He stole her lust. I offer obeisances to the king of thieves, Lord Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Calf demons (with others!) strength – reputation’s, He’s stolen.

He’s stolen His imprisoned fathers distressful sounds.

Passionate worship by Kubjā- He stole- (Her of back-bowed).

My respects to Kṛṣṇa, divine thievery’s crown’d.

### Text 5

*niśācarāṇām atha jīva-cauraṁ*  
*jīvātmanaḥ kalmaṣa-saṅgha-cauram*  
*upāsakānāṁ ca vipatti-cauraṁ*  
*caurādhipaṁ kṛṣṇam ahaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*niśā-carāṇām*—of demons (*rākṣasas*); *atha*—; *jīva-cauram*—the thief of lives, i.e. the killer*; jīva-ātmanaḥ*—of the living entities; *kalmaṣa*—of sins; *saṅgha*—of the heaps; *cauram*—the thief*; upāsakānām*—of the worshipers; *ca*—and; *vipatti-cauram*—the thief of misfortune*.*

**Перевод:**

**He steals the lives of demons. He steals the heaps of sins of the conditioned living entities. He steals the misfortune of those who worship Him. I offer obeisances to the king of thieves, Lord Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Night’s demons blight-exampled life, He’s stolen.

Living entities culmination dirt’s vision, he’s-stolen.

Misfortunes of worshippers and theirs- He has bound (stolen).

My respects to Kṛṣṇa, divine thievery’s crown’d.

### Text 6

*suhṛt-sudāmno hy adhanatva-cauraṁ*  
*śokasya gatvā vidurasya cauram*  
*kṛṣṇā-paṭākarṣaka-garva-cauraṁ*  
*caurādhipaṁ kṛṣṇam ahaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*suhṛt*—of the friend; *sudāmnaḥ*—of Sudāmā; *hi*—indeed; *adhanatva-cauram*—the thief of poverty*; śokasya*—of grief; *gatvā*—having gone (to his house); *vidurasya*—of Vidura; *cauram*—the thief*; kṛṣṇā*—of Draupadī; *paṭa-ākarṣaka*—of one who dragged the garments (Duḥśāsana); *garva*—of pride; *cauram*—the thief*.*

**Перевод:**

**He stole the poverty of His friend Sudāmā. He stole the grief of Vidura by visiting his house. He stole the pride of Duḥśāsana who tried to disrobe Draupadī. I offer obeisances to the king of thieves, Lord Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Close friend’s Sudāma’s poverty-He’s certainly stolen.

Lamentation has gone, (of Vidura’s,) He’s stolen.

He stole Duḥśāsana’s arrogance (who’d clutched Draupadī’s chastity gown).

My respects to Kṛṣṇa, divine thievery’s crown’d.

### Text 7

*yuddhe hi pārthasya vimoha-cauraṁ*  
*puraḥ-sthitānāṁ ca balasya cauram*  
*dine ca māyā-bala-sūrya-cauraṁ*  
*caurādhipaṁ kṛṣṇam ahaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yuddhe*—in the battle; *hi*—indeed; *pārthasya*—of Arjuna; *vimoha-cauram*—the thief of bevilderment*; puraḥ-sthitānām*—of those standing in front of Him; *ca*—and; *balasya*—of the strength; *cauram*—the thief*; dine*—in the daytime; *ca*—and; *māyā*—by His illusory energy; *bala-sūrya*—of the sunset; *cauram*—the thief*.*

**Перевод:**

**He stole Arjuna’s bewilderment during battle. He steals the strength of those who oppose Him. With His illusory energy He stole the sun during daytime (thus letting Arjuna to kill Jayadratha). I offer obeisances to the king of thieves, Lord Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Arjuna’s bewilderment in Mahābhārata battle- indeed-He stole.

All assembled army soldiers (and relatives!) strength-He stole.

He stole- the strength of sunshine (Jayadratha confound’d).

My respects to Kṛṣṇa, divine thievery’s crown’d.

### Text 8

*cittasya śīlasya janasya cauraṁ*  
*aneka-janmārjita-pāpa-cauram*  
*dāsyaṁ gatānāṁ ca samasta-cauraṁ*  
*caurādhipaṁ kṛṣṇam ahaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*cittasya*—of the heart; *śīlasya*—and the good behavior; *janasya*—of people in general; *cauram*—the thief*; aneka*—within many; *janma*—lifetimes; *arjita*—acquired; *pāpa*—sins; *cauram*—the thief*; dāsyam*—His service; *gatānām*—of those who have entered; *ca*—and; *samasta*—of everything; *cauram*—the thief*.*

**Перевод:**

**He steals the hearts and good behavior of people. He steals their sins accumulated within many lifetimes. He steals everything from those who take to His service. I offer obeisances to the king of thieves, Lord Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

The conscious good natured behavior of all people-He steals.

Thus, sins they have accrued over many births-He steals.

Whose servants gone with theirs, (relatives) - He has stolen-all around.

My respects to Kṛṣṇa, divine thievery’s crown’d.
