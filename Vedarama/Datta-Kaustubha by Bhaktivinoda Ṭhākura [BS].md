# Datta-Kaustubha by Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura \[BS\]

### Text 1

*śrī kṛṣṇacandrāya namaḥ*  
*kubuddhīnāṁ kutarkoktyā bhrāmyamāṇasya me manaḥ \|*  
*vaśīkṛtaṁ purā yena vande taṁ prabhu saṁjñakam \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to he who is called the master (prabhu), by whom previously my mind was brought under control, though I was overwhelmed with illusion because of the worst logic among unintelligent people.**

**Комментарий:**

I offer obeisances to the Supreme Lord who is called prabhu, by whom my heart – which was wandering due to deliberating on books having various doctrines – was made steady in the tattvaof the supreme goal (āviśkṛtaṁ purā).

### Text 2

*kaler malam apākartuṁ caitanyo jīva-sad-guruḥ \|*  
*granthasyāsya vidhāne tu mac-citte sa pravarttakaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Caitanya, the true guru of the jīvas, inspired my heart to write this work in order to destroy the impurities of Kali-yuga.**

**Комментарий:**

The goal of the book is explained. Bhāgavatam says:

*kṛtādiṣu prajā rājan kalāv icchanti sambhavam*

**The people of the other yugas desire to take birth in Kaliyuga. (SB** [**11.5.38**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/5.38)**)**

According to this, there is no fault in Kali-yuga. The contamination of the preached scriptural instructions, the fault of Kali-yuga, are described by Parīkṣit in Bhāgavata-māhātmya of Padma Purāṇa. Evaṁ pralayatāṁ prāpto vastu-sāraḥ sthale sthale: the essential teachings have been destroyed in various places. The word “Caitanya” means the son of Śacī who taught the essence (sāra-grāhī). Caitanya also means the function of intelligence. It is recognized that the Lord appears at all times for the jīva, as is stated in Gītā. yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānir bhavati bhārata:whenever there is decline in dharma, I appear. (BG [4.7](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/4.7))

### Text 3

*sampradāye tathānyatra varttate hi sanātanam \|*  
*heyabhāva-vinirmuktaṁ sāragrāhi-mataṁ śubham \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The eternal, auspicious philosophy of the sāra-grāhīs, which is devoid of all impurities, exists in the traditional sects (sampradāyas) and outside as well.**

**Комментарий:**

There are karmīs, jñānīs and bhaktas, of various sampradāyas at all times in all places. There are worshippers of Durgā, Sūrya, Ganeśa, Śiva and Viṣṇu in sampradāyas. Or, there are sampradāyasof the darśanas like Sāṅkhya, etc., or four Vaiṣṇava sampradāyas.In other places there are different groups outside of sampradāyas(anyatra). Or in India and other countries there are sampradāyasdedicated to the Lord. Elsewhere, in Bhaviṣya Purāṇa, śabara bhaktas are described. In Bhāgavatam, there are devotees like Bharata. They are without a sampradāya.

*aṇubhyaś ca mahadbhyaś ca śāstrebhyaḥ kuśalo naraḥ*  
*sarvataḥ sāram ādadyāt puṣpebhya iva ṣaṭpadaḥ*

**Just as the honeybee takes nectar from all flowers, big and small, an intelligent human being should take the essence from all religious scriptures. (SB** [**11.8.10**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/8.10)**)**

As Bhāgavatam says, these devotees (sāra-grāhīs) take the essence from scriptures describing tattvas applicable in all places and times. This is eternal philosophy (sanātana). Taking the essence, prīti, from objects offered by the devotees, the Lord who is the essence of the essence, accepts this essence. He is also sāra-grāhī. Seeing the Lord through samādhi, Brahmā, the first jīva in the universe, received the essence. Though he accepts all anarthas,Śiva accepts the essence, bhakti to the Lord. The Purāṇas say that Nārada, Vyāsa, Parāśara, Parīkṣit and others of the sampradāyaaccepted the essence only. The Vedānta-sūtras are the essence of the Vedas. Bhāgavatam has taken that essence. Sāraṁ sāram samuddhṛtam: Vyāsa extracted the cream of all Vedic literatures and histories of the universe. (SB [1.3.41](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/3.41))

What is the necessity of giving more examples? Seeing the tradition of those who took the essence, the perfection of all meanings is established in the concept of taking the essence (sāra-grāhī). The appearance of all followers of sampradāya which arose from the sāra-grāhīs’ ideas was approved by scriptures and logic. But since the followers of the sampradāyas become possessive of the external rituals such as markings of the unique group, anarthaswhich gave up the essence became the negative aspect of the group. Hatred of other groups, on mistaking the external signs for the internal purificatory ritual taught by the guru of their own group, became the group’s negative aspect. As well, qualification for elevation disappeared in the absence of enthusiasm to progress to a higher qualification, because of dependence of rules of the group, and, with no provisions for rules for those outside the group, this became a negative aspect of such groups. The philosophy of the sāra-grāhīs is devoid of all this.

### Text 4

*sarva-śāstrāt svayaṁ vidvān gṛhṇīyāt sāram uttamam \|*  
*sambandhikaṁ svarūpaṁ ca pātra-bheda-vicārataḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The learned should personally take the very essence from all scriptures, in its highest form, as well as its relative form, considering the differently qualified receivers.**

**Комментарий:**

The essence is of two types: relative and svarūpa. That essence which transcends all time and place, situated in the pure jīva,depending on his svarūpa, is very rare. The essence, which is subject to various qualifications of the jīvas, taking support of methods for unlimited advancement from a despicable low position, is called the relative essence. It becomes clear by judging qualification..

### Text 5

*sva-deśa-niṣṭhatā-buddhiḥ svabhāvād dhi pravartate \|*  
*tathāpi para-deśīye nāśraddhā sāra-bhāginaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person has faith in his own country naturally. The sāragrāhīs do not lack faith in other localities.**

**Комментарий:**

By childhood influence, jīvas are very attached to their own land. They naturally respect the country’s conduct, knowledge, clothing and customs. But the sāra-grāhīs, worried about hatred of foreign qualities and fearing attachment to the faults of one’s own country, are not attached to any of this.

### Text 6

*yaj-jñāne sarva-vijñānaṁ manuṣyāṇāṁ prayojanam \|*  
*labhyate cetasā sākṣāt tat-tattvaṁ viṣayo mama \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**My subject is that truth by knowing which all knowledge, the prayojana of mankind, is attained.**

**Комментарий:**

The tattva which is the only goal for humanity is the subject of this work.

### Text 7

*akhaṇḍaṁ tad-bṛhat-tattvam advaya-jñānam ucyate \|*  
*sambandhāviṣkṛtaṁ śaśvac cābhidheyena sādhitam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That vast unified tattva called advaya-jñānam, non-dual knowledge is revealed by sambandha-jñāna and achieved by the abhidheya.**

**Комментарий:**

Now the tattva is described. The non-duality of tattva is explained in the Bhāgavatam in the verses jīvasya tattva-jijñāsā, vadanti tat tattva-vidas tattvam. (SB [1.2.10-11](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/2.10)) It is called advaya because all upādhis end in prīti, because everything in existence ends in the existence of Parabrahman with form, and because it is one form of consciousness for all beings, devoid of faults of māyā. This tattva is revealed by sambandha-jñāna. While taking shelter of sambandha-jñāna, one must achieve the tattva by the abhidheyaof bhakti.

### Text 8

*vicāre kartṛ-niṣṭhā yā samyag ālocane kṣamā \|*  
*karma-niṣṭha-vicāreṇa sarvam ālocitaṁ na hi \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One who is fixed in deliberation can deliberate well. Nothing can be concluded by deliberating while being attached to karmas.**

**Комментарий:**

These verses discuss the method of accessing this work. The jīva is the deliberator in all acts of deliberation. If one is to deliberate on sambandha-jñāna thoroughly, the person must be fixed in deliberation. If one is fixed in a topic it is not possible to deliberate on everything else, since there are unlimited topics. Scholars who write books on topics like tithi and malamāsa are known as specialists in that topic. I write this book while taking support of this system, in which a goal (prayojana), defined by a deliberation on a relationship (sambandha), is achieved by a certain method (abhidheya).

### Text 9

*na kāryaṁ kṣudra-jīvena vibhuti-gaṇaṁ prabhoḥ \|*  
*yat tasyāvaśyakaṁ nityaṁ tadeva syāt prayojanam \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The insignificant jīva should not measure the powers of the Lord. He should aim for the eternal necessity, the prayojana.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva, small by nature, should not estimate the powers of the Lord who is infinite and all pervading. What is eternally necessary for the jīva in relating to the Lord is his prayojana (goal). It is meaningless for the jīva to measure the unlimited nature of the Lord, since his intelligence is very small.

### Text 10

*svaṁ paraṁ dvi-vidhaṁ proktaṁ pratyakṣañ cendriyātmanoḥ \|*  
*anumānaṁ dvidhā tadvat pramāṇaṁ dvi-vidhaṁ matam \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are two types of perception related to self and others: perception by senses and perception by ātmā. There are similarly two types of inference.**

**Комментарий:**

In this second section, in defining tattvas, pramāṇa is defined in three verses (10-12). There are two pramāṇas: perception and inference. These are of two types of perception: by the ātmā and by the material senses. Perception by ātmā and perception by senses have two types: perception of self and perception of others. Inference also is those two types: self and other. According to Vaiśeṣikas there is no perception by ātmā since all their pramāṇasend in material objects and there is no perception through samādhi. The perception in samādhi is not from material sense perception. Since there is direct seeing in realization, the quality of perception cannot be avoided. Upamānā (one of their pramāṇas) is included in inference. It is not different from inference. It is not a separate pramāṇa. The two pramāṇas have categories of perception and inference of self and others according to the seer’s choice.

### Text 11

*asādhya-sādhya-bhedena pramādo dvi-vidhaḥ smṛtaḥ \|*  
*pramāda-rahitaṁ yat tat prameyaṁ satya-saṁjñakam \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are two type of error: because of one’s specific bad impressions or due to natural human limitations. That which is proved to be without error is called truth.**

**Комментарий:**

The goal in pramāṇa is to define the truth. Logic cannot find the truth. The tendency of arguers to gain victory in an assembly is to be criticized, since its aim is money. It arises from illusion and produces illusion. Pramāṇa is suitably used to indicate the prameya (what is to be proved). The prameya devoid of illusion is truth. Illusion has two varieties: acquired and inherent. Acquired illusion is error arising from bad conditioning. Inherent illusion is natural illusion concerning immeasurable tattva because of the jīva’s limited svarūpa. One should not try to remove it by the power of one’s own knowledge since it arises because of the Lord’s power and can be removed only by the Lord’s mercy. One can remove illusion which is acquired by practice of pure knowledge.

### Text 12

*pratyakṣam-anumānañ ca śāstraṁ para-kṛtaṁ yadi \|*  
*tasmāc-chāstraṁ pramāṇaṁ syan-mitravat kāryasādhane \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If a scripture is made by great sages and based on sense perception and inference, it acts as proof, like a friend, in determining the meaning.**

**Комментарий:**

Can the sāra-grāhīs give up śabda-pramāṇa? This verse answers. Scripture as pramāṇa is accomplished by someone’s inference and perception. This refers to persons from Brahmā down to Vyāsa, etc. By their inference and perception, scriptures are regarded as pramāṇa. Scripture acts as a friend for the sāra-grāhī as stated in Gītā, tasmāc chāstraṁ pramāṇam te kāryākārya-vyavastitau: Therefore, scripture is the authority in determining what to do and what not to do. (BG [16.24](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/16.24)) Scripture naturally acts as a master for the bhāra-vāhīs since they operate by others’ intelligence and because they lack deliberation on what is beneficial and unbeneficial.

### Text 13

*karma-jñānādi-śastreṣu jīvānāṁ śiva-hetave \|*  
*yad-yat prakāśitaṁ vijñais tat-tātparya-vidāṁ satām \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who take the essence of what is revealed by sages in scriptures of karma and jñāna for the benefit of the jīvas,**

### Text 14

*jātyādi-guṇa-doṣeṣu niṣedha-vidhiṣu kvacit \|*  
*na sajjate mano yeṣāṁ prayojana-vidāṁ sadā \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**whose minds are not attached at all to good or bad qualities of birth, etc., or to prohibitions and rules, and who know the goal**

### Text 15

*ūrdhvordhva-gamane kintu pravṛttir varttate yadi \|*  
*adhikāra evaiteṣāṁ bhaktānāṁ sama-darśināṁ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**are qualified for this book if with equal vision they have a desire to elevate themselves.**

**Комментарий:**

Who is qualified for this book? To answer this, three verses (13-15) establish the sāra-grāhīs as qualified. There are many types of scriptures for karma and jñāna. The sāra-grāhī is expert at extracting the meaning of all the rules and prohibitions for producing auspiciousness in this and next life as described in these books. Whether there is birth, knowledge, qualities, beauty, power, or none of these, the people devoid of attachment or hatred to these subjects have equal vision. If they follow the path of bhakti and gradually raise themselves from lower to higher qualification, make endeavors and always aim for the goal of spiritual prema, they are qualified as sāra-grāhīs for what is expressed in this book. Persons who wear the marks of a group, and hate other groups, who are devoid of bhakti, falsely displaying dharma, who are deceitful, cheated and simply carry the orders of a devotee without understanding the conclusion, who have false pride, are not qualified for this book. Though they are bhāra-vāhīs, if they follow the mood of the sāra-grāhīs while giving up their faults, they can attain qualification as sāra-grāhīslike Khaṭvāṅga, Vālmīki and others.

### Text 16

*īṣat-sāmmukhyād ārabhya prīti-sampannatāvadhi \|*  
*adhikārā hy asaṅkhyeyā guṇāḥ pañcavidhā matāḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This is because there are countless qualifications beginning from slight inclination for the Lord until achievement of prīti. There are five types of guṇas.**

**Комментарий:**

There are five types of guṇas: tamas, rajas-tamas, rajas, rajas-sattvaand sattva. In tamo-guṇa, worshippers of śakti search for the Lord in material objects. Worshippers of the sun are a mixture of rajasand tamas, since their intelligence is specialized in heat within the material world, since heat is the cause of movement. From rajo-guṇa arises worship of Gaṇeśa, worship of man and animal. From rajas mixed with sattva one worships Śiva, representing the pure jīva. In sattva one worships Viṣṇu, the form of the Lord different from prakṛti. These five are secondary forms of worship.

### Text 17

*sva-svādhikāra-niṣṭhāyām uttarottaragāminī \|*  
*pravṛttir varttate śaśvat sārabhājāṁ kramānvayāt \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The taste of the sāra-grāhīs manifests as a progression to higher and higher levels by being fixed in specific qualifications.**

### Text 18

*keṣāñcit prabalā bhūtvā sarva-kṣudrādhikārataḥ \|*  
*sarvonnataṁ padaṁ datte na cirād iha janmani \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some persons, from a very low qualification reach a strong taste quickly and attain the highest qualification in this life.**

**Комментарий:**

Examples are Khaṭvāṅga and others.

### Text 19

*janmāntaram apekṣante karmaṇāṁ bhāra-vāhinaḥ \|*  
*tathāpi karma-cāturye spṛhā teṣāṁ na jāyate \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those attached to karmas take many lifetimes (to change qualification) but they never develop desire for performing karmas correctly.**

**Комментарий:**

One does not see teachings to attain another qualification in this life among those carrying the burden of karmas—the smartas and others.

### Text 20

*koṭi-janmāntare’pi syān na sāra-grāhaṇe matiḥ \|*  
*yāvan na ghaṭate teṣāṁ sādhu-saṅgaḥ mad-ātmakaḥ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**As long as they do not have association with devotees who are absorbed in Kṛṣṇa, they do not develop into sāra-grāhīs even after millions of births.**

**Комментарий:**

The medicine for them is association with devotees absorbed in Kṛṣṇa from their birth. In the story of King Prācīnabarhi one sees how a smarta bhāra-vāhī can become a sāra-grāhī by the strength of devotee association.

### Text 21

*viśiṣṭaḥ śakti-sampannaḥ krīḍāvān bhakta-vatsalaḥ \|*  
*bhakti-vyaṅgyo’py ameyātmā prītimān sundaro vibhuḥ \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The beautiful, affectionate, Supreme Lord, unattainable by jñāna, endowed with śakti, endowed with qualities, who performs pastimes and is affection to the devotee, is attained by bhakti.**

**Комментарий:**

Starting with this verse, Bhagavat-tattva is described. Since the Lord is immeasurable (ameya), he is not understood or attainable by jñāna. The Lord by his mercy manifests through bhakti, and because of affection for his devotees, though he is the all-pervading Lord, he reveals his beauty to them. He performs pastimes affectionately with the jīvas in the spiritual realm, as master with his servants, as a friend with his friends, as a father with his children (Dvārakā), as a child with his father and mother, and as a lover with the gopīs. All these relationships are confidential, endowed with spiritual emotions, not material emotions. “How can the Parabrahman perform pastimes like an ordinary person?” The Lord is endowed with all śaktis. This is his astonishing quality, proved by statements of Purāṇas and Vedas. Parāsya śaktir vividhaiva śruyate: it is said that the Lord has various śaktis. (Śvetāśvara Upaniṣad [6.8](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.8))

### Text 22

*guṇebhyaś ca guṇī bhinnaḥ prākṛte parilakṣyate \|*  
*na tathā prākṛtātīte nirguṇe nitya-dehini \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The possessor of qualities (the jīva in his material body) in the material world is different from his qualities. There is no difference between the eternal possessor of his body and his qualities, who is devoid of material guṇas and is beyond prakṛti.**

**Комментарий:**

The difference between the quality and possessor of the quality, limb and possessor of the limb, and body and possessor of the body arises because of a relationship of different items: consciousness and matter. Difference from the cit-tattva of Vaikuṇṭha arises because of the faulty nature of its reflection, māyā. This dual nature does not arise in the cit-tattva. When the material condition is removed, nondifference in the pure conscious jīva’snature is accomplished because there is no difference between the body and possessor of the body. What then to speak of the Lord, eternally endowed with spiritual svarūpa, beyond the material guṇas (nirguṇa), having non-difference of his qualities and his self. Because of the fault of duality of quality and possessor of quality in our gross bodies, we must endeavor for non-duality. Because the Lord lacks this duality, without effort non-duality manifests. His senses are spiritual, devoid of any material taint.

Just as in the material body all the senses situated in their appropriate places give beauty to the body, in the spiritual body all the senses situated in their places spread astonishing, unprecedented beauty. Seeing that beauty, all the jīvas become attracted to the Lord. If one does not accept the spiritual body of the Lord, beauty of the Lord is absent, and one cannot become attracted to the Lord. The principle here is that the Lord has one cit-śakti. Māyā-śakti arises as its reflection. The material world is created by māyā influenced by the Lord’s glance. The gross and subtle bodies of the jīva thus arise. But all this is just reflection of the cit or spiritual. It is not a new type of existence (tattva). The gross and subtle bodies are reflections of the jīva’s spiritual body. The spiritual senses and limbs in the cit-tattva are all reflected in the gross body. Actually, if the state of difference between quality and possessor of the quality were removed, the body would be situated in its svarūpa.

The whole world devoid of inferior elements takes on the nature of Vaikuṇṭha. Therefore Lord is fixed in his natural, eternal form. In that form the Lord mentioned in the Upaniṣads, though always situated fully, by the power of his acintya-śakti, spreads everywhere. The tattva mentioned in the Upaniṣad is confirmed as proof by the ātmā’s perception. At the beginning of Bhāgavatamit is said:

*apaśyat purusaṁ purṇāṁ māyāṁ ca tad apāśrayām*

**He saw the complete puruṣa and māyā which took shelter of that puruṣa. (SB** [**1.7.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/7.4)**)**

This statement shows that Vyāsa in samādhi realized this fact.

### Text 23

*bindu-bindutayā jīve ye ye śakti-guṇādayaḥ \|*  
*te sarve kila varttante nityaṁ pūrṇatayā harau \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The śakti, qualities, etc., which are present in very small quantity in the jīva, are all present in the Lord eternally, in full manifestation.**

### Text 24

*anye ca bahavaḥ santi guṇāḥ kṛṣṇe svabhāvataḥ \|*  
*nopalabdhir bhavet teṣāṁ nṛṇāṁ śakter abhāvataḥ \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Many other qualities are present in the Lord naturally. Humans cannot realize these qualities because they do not have the power to do so.**

**Комментарий:**

Śaktis and qualities such as deliberation, mercy, etc., are present to a very small degree in the jīva. All these are present in the Lord eternally, in full. Additionally, the Lord has many extra qualities by his nature. It is not possible for the jīva to realize all these, since he lacks the necessary śakti to do so.

### Text 25

*prapañca-vijayas tasya līlayā nija-śaktitaḥ \|*  
*tathāpi parameśasya nirguṇatvaṁ na hīyate \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord remains triumphant in the material world by his own śakti with pastimes. He remains without material qualities at this time.**

**Комментарий:**

It is possible for the Lord to appear within the material world by the power of his svarūpa-śakti. But there is no destruction of his quality of being nirguṇa whenever he appears, unlike the jīva. Since māyā is his servant, she is restricted when he comes. But on seeing māyā, the Lord of māyā, the Lord of Vaikuṇṭha, is not restricted. But this is what Śaṅkara and the Māyāvādīs say.

### Text 26

*hy avatārā harer bhāvā manasy ūrdhvordhva-gāmini \|*  
*sarvordhva-bhāva sampannaṁ vraja-tattvaṁ mahīyate \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The avatāras manifest as forms in the jīvas’ minds which have achieved higher and higher qualifications. The tattva of Vraja endowed with the highest bhāva of all is worshipped as the highest.**

**Комментарий:**

*avirbhāva-tirobhāvā sva-pade tiṣṭhati \|*  
*tāmasī rājasī sāttvikī mānuṣī \|*  
*vijñāna-ghana ānanda-ghana*  
*sac-cid-ānandaika-rase bhakti-yoge tiṣṭhati \|\|*

**The form which appears in the material world and then disappears remains in the spiritual abode. The forms for humans are tāmasī, rājasī and sāttvikī. The form of Kṛṣṇa, filled with knowledge and bliss, resides in bhakti-yoga, which is also full of eternity, knowledge and bliss.**

By the statement of Gopāla-tāpanī, Uttara-khaṇḍa, it is proved that all the avatāras of the Lord manifest to express the heart’s functions (bhāva-mūrti) according to the jīvas’ gradual unfurling of knowledge and intelligence and according to his qualification of concentration.

1. In the first stage, when the jīva’s body lacks a spine (has soft spine), the Lord has a fish form.
2. When the jīva’s body has a hard shell, the Lord manifests the condition of a turtle.
3. When the jīva has a spine, the Lord manifests as Varāha.
4. When the jīva is a combination of animal and human he manifests as Narasiṁha.
5. When the jīva is a small human form, the Lord takes the form of Vāmana.
6. When the jīva takes the form of an uncivilized human, the Lord becomes Paraśurāma.
7. When the jīva becomes civilized human the Lord becomes Rāma.
8. When the jīva becomes the receptacle of the highest rasa, the Lord becomes Kṛṣṇa.
9. When the jīva tries to measure the Lord by knowledge derived from the senses, the Lord becomes Buddha.
10. When atheism becomes strong, the Lord becomes Kalki.

These are the states of the ten avatāras. Whatever faults appeared in these states are faults in the beholder not in the object perceived. Though there are many opinions, one can see the various states through scientific deliberation. These states of the Lord are all eternal, existing within the variety of Vaikuṇṭha. The sāra-grāhīsunderstand all these forms to be without fault.

### Text 27

*cid-ātmā prīti-dharmāyaṁ bhagavac-chakti-bhāvitaḥ \|*  
*prapañce dviguṇo jīvaḥ svarūpī-nitya-dhāmani \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The conscious jīva, whose nature is prema, manifests by the Lord’s śakti. This jīva in the material world is bound by two ropes (gross and subtle bodies). The jīva in the spiritual world is situated in his svarūpa.**

**Комментарий:**

Four verses (27-30) now describe the nature of the jīva, spiritual in nature. The jīva is situated in his svarūpa in the eternal abode of Vaikuṇṭha. The jīva is a conscious particle with a sense of self (cid-ātmā) that is his svarūpa. Being endowed with prīti is his quality, to be understood by his actions. The jīva is created or nourished (bhāvita) by the Lord’s śakti. When the jīva is situated in the material world in a bound state, though the jīva is a spiritual ātmā, it becomes endowed with two bodies, the gross and the subtle. The gross body is perceived by the action senses and the subtle body is perceived by the knowledge senses.

### Text 28

*saṅkoce vikace śaśvat ṣaḍ-vikāra-vivarjjitaḥ \|*  
*bhoktṛtva-bhrama-jālāt sa svadharmād dhi bahir-mukhaḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Whether in the material world or in the spiritual world, the jīva is devoid of the six changes. The jīva deviates from his sva-dharma because of the error of thinking himself an enjoyer.**

**Комментарий:**

The six changes are birth, existence, growth, deterioration, reproduction and death. Situated in Vaikuṇṭha (vikace) in his svarūpa, the jīva is devoid of the six changes. In the material world (saṅkoce), the pure jīva is still devoid of the six changes, but the gross and subtle bodies of the jīva undergo these changes. The jīva experiences suffering in this world because of identification with his body. In his svarūpa the jīva is enjoyed or subordinate, and the Lord is the enjoyer or master. The Lord gives the jīva independence to desire his independent prīti but the jīva, because of foolishness, treats this gift in an opposite way, desiring his own enjoyment. Bound by the net of illusion by identifying as the enjoyer, the jīva becomes averse to his svadharma. Because of this, whenthe jīva attains the material world, the abode of enjoyment, bound up in the net of illusion in the form of identifying with the body, the jīva experiences great suffering in relation to these transformations.

### Text 29

*sva-dharmaḥ kṛṣṇa-dāsyaṁ hi tasmiṁs tiṣṭhan sukhī sadā \|*  
*tad abhāvāt tridhā kleśā māyāsaktasya duḥkha-dāḥ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sva-dharma of the jīva is to be the servant of Kṛṣṇa. In that state the jīva is always happy. Deviating from his sva-dharma the jīva, absorbed in māyā, experiences the three miseries which give him suffering.**

**Комментарий:**

*naisargikaṁ tu jīvānāṁ dāsyaṁ viṣṇoḥ sanātanatam*  
*tad vinā vartate mohād ātma-cauraḥ sa kathyate*

**It is nature for the jīva to be the eternal servant of Viṣṇu. Without that, out of illusion, the jīva is called a stealer of the ātmā. (Vasiṣṭha-smṛti)**

This proof from Vasiṣṭha-smṛti is accepted by all scriptures. The sva-dharma of all jīvas is to be the servant of Kṛṣṇa. Though the jīva is bound by a net of illusion as the enjoyer, the bliss from cultivating sva-dharma is eternal. The attachment to happiness in the material world is temporary and superficial. Attachment to māyā, caused by absence of sva-dharma, produces the three types of misery: ādhyātmika, ādhidaivika and ādhibhautika. In Rūpa Gosvāmī’s Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, the three sufferings are listed as ignorance, seed of sin and sin.

### Text 30

*sat-saṅgāj jāyate śraddhā tasmāj jñānaṁ sunirmalam \|*  
*jñānād dhyānaṁ tato bhaktiḥ kleśa-ghnī kṛṣna-toṣaṇī \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Комментарий:**

Faith arises from association with devotees. Those who experience the Lord are devotees. They are not just persons who are marked with signs of an āśrama such as detached sannyāsīs. Beyond wearing these signs, the devotees have experience of the Lord. Beggars who say they are devotees are not devotees. Respecting a person because he wears signs of one’s own sampradāyaarises from attachment to one’s sampradāya. Those not in the sampradāya hate a person wearing the sampradāya marks, out of illusion, thinking that those who wear such marks are imposters. The sāra-grāhī is devoid of both attachment or hatred.

From association with the best sāra-grāhīs endowed with experience of the Lord, by the power of following their conduct, persons endowed with faith attain very pure sambandha-jñāna concerning the Lord. Attaining that, they then meditate on the Lord. Bhakti which manifests in that meditation destroys all suffering and pleases Kṛṣṇa.

### Text 32

*māyāsūtaṁ jagat sarvaṁ sthūla-liṅga-svarūpakam \|*

### Text 33

*yad yad bhāti hy asad-bimbe tat-tat sarvaṁ viśeṣataḥ \|*  
*vartate bhagavad-dhāmni śiva-rūpam anāmayam \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Everything reflected as temporary in the material world exists excellently with auspicious, faultless form in the spiritual world.**

**Комментарий:**

Māyā-śakti is now discussed. The one śakti of the Lord is called by various names — cit-śakti, svarūpa-śakti, etc. It is the form of the highest bliss for the jīvas who are servants of the Lord.

Māyā-śakti, which covers averse jīvas with māyā and which causes error in the jīvas, is a temporary (asat) reflection of the qualities tripād-vibhūti of Vaikuṇṭha. It manifests the universe with subtle and gross bodies which take shelter of material qualities. There is great similarity of the material universe manifested by māyā-śaktiwith Vaikuṇṭha manifested by the svarūpa-śakti. But everything which is despicable in the material world is auspicious in the spiritual world.

The suffering is the faulty part of the material world. There is no difference in earth, water, etc., form, fragrance, etc., action etc. But all the inferior things which give suffering in the material world, a transformation of everything in the spiritual world, do not exist in the spiritual world. Everything in the spiritual world is auspicious. That everything in Vaikuṇṭha is auspicious is beyond the conceptual process of the mind, which is in a distorted condition caused by contact with inferior place, time and object in the material world.

The concept of absence of qualities in the spiritual realm, something contrary to material qualities, and the concept of formlessness are all filled with fault, are contrary to the knowledge gained by samādhi, and are an obstacle to the prayojana. They increase illusion and are contrary to the pure jīva’s discernment of spiritual svarūpa within the variety of the spiritual world.

The dry jñānīs using false logic, find fault with our philosophy of the Lord with qualities. According to them, the reflected state of the spiritual world which manifest in one’s vision by material sentiments, is a material condition only. This conclusion is false. The sāra-grāhīs confirm that the state of the spiritual world when reflected becomes the material world. If one rejects this conclusion, one will speculate that thoughts of the existence of the Lord arise from material conditions. If one believes this, one becomes atheistic.

It is necessary to determine what is despicable. Spatial inferiority such as distance exists in the material world. The auspicious aspect of distance exists in Vaikuṇṭha. Inferiority of time consists of the conception of past, future and present. They are all auspicious in the spiritual world. Objects of water, earth, the body, etc., are inferior in this world. Fatigue in attaining goals, costliness of purchasing items, contrary natures, etc., exist in this world. In this condition we cannot perfectly conceive of what is despicable and auspicious since we lack the auspicious condition. But by the strength of self-accomplished knowledge (svataḥ siddha jñāna) one can realize the existence of what is despicable and auspicious.

### Text 34

*dhyānādau bhakti-mat-kāryye prākṛte’pi svarūpataḥ \|*  
*sārāṁśā nīta-vaikuṇṭhāḥ kṛṣṇoddeśe hṛdi sthite \|\|34\|\|*

**Комментарий:**

Thus, the effect of meditation undertaken by the mind produces a material state. Using opposite actions, one attains opposite results. By this principle by material meditation, how can one achieve spiritual Vaikuṇṭha?”

If one says this, it is not proper. By saying dhyānādau in the verse all mental and bodily actions are implied. If one aims at the Lord in one’s actions of worship of the Lord, all those actions are never fruitless, since the Lord is omniscient and compassionate. Thus, even in “material” sādhana, the essence-- in the form of rasa for the deity-- is brought to the Lord by the cit-śakti. The Lord’s servant māyā, supplying the ingredients for worship, offers the acts of service of the bound jīva to the lotus feet of the Lord. Thus, the sāra-grāhīs should pay no attention to the hatred that the dry jñānīs show for the deity form in ārcana.

### Text 46

*jīvānāṁ baddha-bhūtānāṁ karttavyam abhidheyakam \|*

**Комментарий:**

The discussion of sambandha-jñāna has been completed. Now, the abhidheya or sādhana-tattva begins with this verse. Prīti for the Lord is the sva-dharma of the liberated jīva. But because of accepting māyā, sva-dharma of the bound jīva is hard to discern. Various sages produced various opinions.

*muninaikena yat proktaṁ tad anyo na niṣedhati*  
*pratyutodāharet tasmāt sarvoktiḥ sarva-sammatā*

**One sage will not deny what is said by another sage. One should gather all the statements that agree. (Laghu-parāṣara)**

According to this, one should not find fault with the sages. Rather all the sages are sāra-grāhīs. The sages have indicated that the main method is that by which the prayojana, prīti for the Lord, is achieved. They arranged various methods for persons of various qualifications. The bhāra-vāhīs never understand the conclusion but on seeing various scriptures, they give much respect to statements which give importance to methods such as karma. Thus, becoming attached to karma, jñāna or bhakti, and they criticize others. But the sāra-grāhīs take the essence of each method such as jñāna, etc., and reject the unessential parts.

*na bhuddi-bhedaṁ janayed ajñānāṁ karma-saṅginām*

**One should not disturb the minds of ignorant persons attached to karma. (BG** [**3.26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/3.26)**)**

Karma is a method of directing persons without jñāna towards jñāna. According to the words of the Lord, while judging the qualification of a person, one should make arrangements for what should be done and what should not be done by that person. Judging qualification of materialistic persons, for destroying opportunities for anarthas and purifying their hearts, they arrange for material karmas. For those who are not grossly material but are ignorant of pure spiritual topics, they prescribe jñāna-kāṇḍawith mahā-vākyas like tattvam asi, etc. Those who have surpassed karma and jñāna search for sva-dharma and their sva-bhāva. They see karma and jñāna as directed towards prīti for the Lord, the chief prayojana. According to different qualifications, karma, jñāna, etc., are accepted as the abhidheya. These methods will be briefly described separately in this book.

### Text 47

### Text 48

*yan nākarma-vikarma syāt tad eva karma-śabdyate \|*  
*puruṣārtha-vihīnañ cet karma cākarmavad bhavet \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**What is neither akarma nor vikarma is called karma. If the karma is devoid of the puruṣārtha it is equivalent to akarma.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī says in his commentary that karma is what is prescribed by scriptures. Akarma is the opposite (not doing), and that is forbidden. Vikarma is performing reprehensible acts and not doing the prescribed karma. What is opposite — forbidden acts and sinful acts — should be given up according to one’s qualification.

All the sinful acts are listed in the Eleventh Canto:

*steyaṁ hiṁsānṛtaṁ dambhaḥ kāmaḥ krodhaḥ smayo madaḥ*  
*bhedo vairam aviśvāsaḥ saṁspardhā vyasanāni ca*  
*ete pañcadaśānarthā hy artha-mūlā matā nṛṇām*  
*tasmād anartham arthākhyaṁ śreyo’rthī dūratas tyajet*

**Theft, violence, speaking lies, duplicity, lust, anger, perplexity, pride, quarreling, enmity, faithlessness, envy and the dangers caused by women, gambling and intoxication are the fifteen undesirable qualities that contaminate men because of greed for wealth. These are the fifteen anarthas. One desiring to achieve the real benefit of life should therefore remain aloof from these anarthas which men believe are valuable. (SB** [**11.23.18-19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/23.18)**)**

Vyasanāni means women, gambling and intoxications. Association with women outside of Vedic rules is a famous anartha. Any intoxication is dangerous. Gambling refers to useless actions which produce laziness. What is not vikarma and is not akarmaaccording to qualification is called karma according to the Vedas. But if it is devoid of the puruṣārthas it is equivalent to akarma.

### Text 49

He is a sāra-grāhī, skillful at karma. All others carry a useless intermediate result and karma like an ox carrying a load of gravel.

### Text 50

*kvacit sākṣāt kvacid gauṇaṁ karma bhakti prayojakam \|*  
*ādyaṁ tac-chravaṇādau tu cāntyaṁ varṇāśramādiṣu \|\|50\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Actions directly or indirectly aim at bhakti. Direct actions are hearing about the Lord, etc. Indirect actions are varnāśrama, etc.**

Actions which stimulate bhakti are of two types: primary and secondary. Those acts whose result is only bhakti, directly stimulate bhakti. Hearing, chanting, etc., are such acts. Thus, various aṅgas of bhaktiinvolving action (karma) are different from karma and have the name bhakti.

It should be concluded in discussing bhakti that the nitya and naimittika karmas, charity, austerities, recitation of the Vedas, iṣṭāpūrta and vratas in the varṇāśrama society are karmas that produce bhakti secondarily. Hospitals and schools for puṇyaare included in īṣṭāpūrta. These actions give many results and sometimes aim at sense pleasure and sometimes aim at the Lord. When they are inclined to sense pleasure and pleasure from material assets, they are averse to the Lord, material, and contrary to the sva-dharma of the jīva. Persons dedicated to karmas say the opposite and their conclusion is contrary to the śrutis, smṛtis and proper conduct. Yājñavalkya says:

*ijyācāra-damāhiṁsā-dāna-svādhyāya-karmaṇām*  
*ayaṁ tu paramo dharmo yad yogenātma-darśanam*

**Seeing the Lord through yoga is better than karmas like worship, conduct, controlling the senses, nonviolence, charity, and study of the Vedas.**

**Devotional service unto Lord Kṛṣṇa is attained by charity, strict vows, austerities and fire sacrifices, by japa, study of Vedic texts, observance of regulative principles and, indeed, by the performance of many other auspicious practices. (SB** [**10.47.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/47.24)**)**

Criticism of material karma is stated negatively in scriptures:

*dharmaḥ svanuṣṭhitaḥ puṁsāṁ viṣvaksena-kathāsu yaḥ*

**Varṇāśrama-dharma of the human being, which does not produce attraction for topics of the Lord, is only wasted effort. (SB** [**1.2.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/2.8)**)**

### Text 51

*indriyārthe parijñāte tattva-jñānaṁ bhaven na hi \|*

**When knowledge is for sense pleasure, it does not produce knowledge of tattva. When there is knowledge of sambandha there is pure knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

Now jñāna is described. Knowledge of form, taste, smell, touch, sound, hardness, liquidity, etc., is not tattva-jñāna. This is material knowledge – realization of the inferior nature of objects.

Knowing by which nothing remains to be known is tattva-jñāna. Knowledge of the relationship of cit, acit and the Lord is tattva-jñāna.

*yasmin vijñāte sarvaṁ vijñātam bhavati*

**Knowing whom, everything is known. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad** [**1.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mundaka/1.1.3)**)**

Partial knowledge is never suitable for giving happiness of svarūpa.

### Text 53

*jīvasya laya-sāyujyaṁ yaj jñānaṁ tad asammatam \|*

**The knowledge that the jīva ultimately merges in Brahman is a false claim. Eternal service of the jīva to the Lord is praised in scriptures.**

**Комментарий:**

What is that jñāna? To answer this the author speaks verse 52. Some say that the jīva merging with Brahman in liberation is jñāna. That is not correct because in the scriptures being the servant of the Lord is stated to be the goal. In merging, bhaktiis not possible. Knowledge of the relationships between cit, acitand the Lord produces advaya-jñāna. According to Sāṅkhya the twenty-four elements constitute prakṛti. Among them there are five elements, five tan-mātras and ten senses. These are the gross elements. Mind, intelligence, citta and ahaṅkāra are the subtle elements. The jīva is the twenty-fifth element and the Paramātmā is the twenty-sixth element. By knowing these elements completely, without doubt sambandha-jñāna is accomplished. Śrīdhara Svāmī says:

*ṣaḍ-viṁśo dasame vyaktaḥ ṣaḍ-viṁśo daṣamo hariḥ*

**In the Tenth Canto twenty-six tattvas are revealed. The twenty-sixth element and the tenth topic of Bhāgavatam is the Lord. May I, the twenty-fifth element, be separate from the twenty-four elements.**

Statements in Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad and Viṣṇu Purāṇa in the story of Prahlāda that say, “I am Brahman,” are meditation techniques within sādhana for clearing selfish tendencies within the mood of service. They do not indicate merging. Even though this is not a fault for Prahlāda and others who are surrendered to dāsya, the ordinary devotees do not follow this procedure.

### Text 54

*karma-jñānāṅga-sārāṇi nava-pañca-vibhāgataḥ \|*  
*prayojanāya yuktāni sarvaṁ tad bhakti-saṁjñakam \|\|54\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nine actions and five types of knowledge, engaged for the prayojana, are called bhakti.**

**Комментарий:**

Now the section of bhakti begins. The nine aṅgas are hearing about the Lord, glorifying him, remembering him, serving his lotus feet, performing deity worship, offering prayers, dāsya,sakhya and ātma-nivedana. These are actions related to the Lord, used as sādhana of nine types employing the gross (using senses) and subtle bodies (using mind). Taking shelter of a guru’s feet, purification by sampradāya rites, wearing the sampradāyamarkings, eating the Lord’s food remnants, and following vratasconnected with worship of the Lord are delineated as subdivisions of arcana in Bhakti-sandarbha of Jīva Gosvāmī. The five bhāvas of śānta, dāsya, sakhya, vātsalya and madhura, since they employ the subtle body and ātmā are the aṅgas of knowledge in the form of rati. Those who practice these five aṅgas execute the action aṅgas connected with the gross body by previous saṁsākarasin a neutral manner. The three aṅgas – hearing, chanting, remembering – are eternal, existing in the jīva in bondage and the jīva in liberation (though in another form), since they are the chief elements in prayojana. One understands that all other aṅgaslead to the cit-tattva.

The action aṅgas as sādhana are vaidha. The knowledge aṅgasof bhakti taking the form of rati and involving the ātmā, become rāgānuga for the sādhaka and rāgātmikā for the perfected jīvas. When the rāga or desire for material objects of a person absorbed in material life becomes linked to the supreme, pure consciousness, then pure rāga arises. When that attachment is fully manifested, becoming the svarūpa of the jīva, it is called rāgātmikā. This function is possible for the perfected jīva, not for the sādhaka. Sometimes, seeing by samādhi the rāga of the Vraja inhabitants within the spiritual abode, the sādhaka develops an inclination to follow them. This is rāgānuga-bhakti.

In the state of prema, svarūpa of all the vaidha aṅgas changes. The svarūpa of knowledge aṅgas does not change but becomes pure. In the aṅgas of śānta-bhāva there is no relationship between the Lord and the jīva. Though this is a type of rasa, in the śānta aṅga, attachment to jñāna is strong. However, in dāsya, sakhya, vātsalya and madhura bhāvas, the relationship between the Lord and the jīva becomes gradually deeper.

Giving up the chief prayojana is a huge fault in all the aṅgas of bhakti. This happens when followers of the sampradāya become attached to the external markings and renounced persons start to hate the external signs. Thus, bhakti means all the aṅgas of action and knowledge linked to the prayojana. This is what has been stated.

### Text 58

*viraktir vaimukhyācchede jñānañ cānya niṣedhane \|*

Vairāgya does not mean hatred of all material life. That is called false vairāgya. This will be clear in this book when harmonizing the various yogas. The effect of jñāna is to increase prīti for the Lord by making one give up desires for bhukti and mukti, and by removing belief in many gods. Bhakti is the queen and, to remove obstacles, it engages jñāna and vairāgya as two gate keepers.

### Text 59

*prītyātmikā yadā bhaktir virakti-jñāna-karmaṇām \|*  
*bhinna-bhāve’pi tat sarvaṁ prītāv ekātmatāṁ bhajet \|\|59\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When bhakti becomes prema, detachment, jñāna and karma, though separate items, become one with prīti.**

**Комментарий:**

Is it possible for jñāna, karma and vairāgya to have separate existence in the perfection of prema? This verse answers.

*yathā nadyāḥ syandamānāḥ samudre*  
*’stam gacchanti nāma-rūpe vihāya*  
*tathā vidvān nāma-rūpād vimuktaḥ*  
*parāt-paraṁ puruṣam upaiti divyam*

**Just as rivers flowing into the ocean, giving up names and forms (colors), disappear, so the man in knowledge, liberated from names and forms attains the attractive Supreme Lord. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad** [**3.2.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mundaka/3.2.8)**)**

It is here stated that in his final state the jīva is devoid of upādhis. Just as the jīva is devoid of upādhis, one should understand that the jīva’s sva-dharma of prīti is devoid of the upādhis of jñāna, karma and vairāgya. This state however is realized through samādhi and not revealed by speaking. In this situation, naturally yukta-vairāgya, in the form of incapacity for karmas, arises. Giving up karmas with effort while wearing the dress of renunciate is cheating. That is the conclusion of the sāra-grāhīs.

### Text 61

*kurvanti yoginas tat-tat-tyajanti na kṣamaṁ yadā \|\|62\|\|*

**The yogīs may sometimes perform karma or jñāna which is favorable for gaining prīti. They will reject them if they are unfavorable.**

**Комментарий:**

Now the author describes the method of combination (samanvaya) of karma, jñāna and bhakti. This samanvaya-yogīs are of three types: ārurukṣu, ārūḍha and sampanna. Those defective sādhakasconcerning karma, jñāna and bhakti, are of two types: sāra-grāhī and bhāra-vāhī. For those whoare bhāra-grāhīs, laboring in karma is best, since by that labor there is no opportunity for committing sins. Their goal is atonement by karmas. Sāra-grāhīshave a strong desire to elevate by the syncretic process because of their inclination to progress. They quickly reach the ārūḍhastage. Gradually by the strength of sādhana and by the strength of disposition to accept the essence (sāra), the ārūḍhas very quickly become sampanna.

Repentance is the atonement for destroying sin for the ārurukṣas. Remembering the Lord is the only atonement for the ārūḍhas. One should review the lives of Parīkṣit, Khaṭvāṅga and others. All these yogīs were not simply involved only in karma, jñānaor vairāgya. Because of not knowing the syncretic method, or out of illusion, the deficient jñānīs, have attraction for destroying bondage, making vairāgya, etc., individually prominent. Sometimes they become absorbed in karma and criticize vairāgya. Sometimes they become absorbed in jñāna and become detached from practices beneficial to body, house and wife.

But the samanvaya-yogīs always make efforts for producing auspicious for everyone — including wife, body and house, which act as assistants to attaining prīti for the Lord while in the state of bondage. By the strength of their inclination for progress, while accepting the essence of all rules and prohibitions, they gradually attain the wealth of prema.

When karma and jñāna assist bhakti, these yogīs perform these processes with great effort. If at some time these do not increase prīti for the Lord, on considering place, time and candidate, then they give up this karma and jñāna, thinking them most abominable. This is the great secret. The bhāra-vāhis, having deficient intelligence, never enter this secret.

When ascending in yoga, all these impurities are gradually burned up. When akarma or vikarma occur at some time, they do not in the end bestow results of being imprisoned in saṁsāra since they have attained the result of destroying karma by their maturity. In the sampanna state, the jīva has no contamination. Nārada is a famous example. We accept gradual extinction of saṁsāra in a natural way, like Ṛṣabhadeva and Jaḍa-bharata, where the jīvahas great prīti for the Lord at the sampanna stage, with inability to even deal with material actions. The cheaters’ rejection of saṁsāra, taking support of fraud, is criticized as worthless.

### Text 66

*bhuktyo muktayaḥ kintu na nivāryyāḥ kadācana \|*

The sāra-grāhī never seeks out the intermediate results but when these results appear on their own, they serve the devotee, assisting him in attaining prīti.

In the material world all atoms follow the principle of attraction. This is the opinion of material scientists. This proves that the material world is a reflection of the spiritual world. Moreover, this attractive force is very strong in large material objects in the form of gravity. Thus, planets revolve around the sun and all larger entities revolve around Dhruva constellation. The universe created as a reflection of Vaikuṇṭha is very beautiful. ### Text 70

Śānta-rasa has only pure rati. Dāsya has rati and prema. Sakhya has rati, premaand praṇaya. Vātsalya has rati, prema, praṇaya and sneha (and rāga). ### Text 72

Having entered the ocean of scriptures, though difficult to cross, I, Kedarnātha Datta, obtained the pure essence, the Kaustubha jewel through the methods of pramāṇa—perception and inference. I have given it to the sāra-grāhīs, the most intelligent people. What is that essence (sāraḥ)? It was almost lost by the quarrels arising from attachment or hatred of sampradāya by the influence of intoxicated Kali. It is for pleasing Rādhā’s lover, who is the abode of the highest sweetness. Rādhā is the hlādinī-śakti, the svarūpa of bliss of the Lord for all bhāvas up to mahābhāva for the jīvas, who are qualified for madhura-rasa.

*kaustubheśa pradatto me dattasya kaustubho mudā \|*  
*vaiṣṇavānāṁ śirodhāryyaḥ sārabhājāṁ viśeṣataḥ \|\|*  
*aṣṭādaśaśate śāke pañcābdārahite mayā \|*  
*nirmitaṁ kaustubhaṁ kṣudraṁ kṣetre śrī-puruṣottame \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This kaustubha given to me, Kedaranātha Datta, by the lord of the Kaustubha, should be held joyfully on the heads of the Vaiṣṇavas and particularly the sāra-grāhīs. This Kaustubha was written by me in 1873 in Purī.**

**Комментарий:**

The Kaustubha which has been lifted from the ocean of scriptures was given to me by the lord of the Kaustubha. It should be held on the heads of the sāra-grāhīs particularly. It is not proper to disrespect scriptures dealing with the Lord because of faults in grammar or literary ornament. This scripture should be especially respected because, by the power of its words, it is suitable preparation for studying large scriptures like the Bhāgavatam.

*samāptaś cāyaṁ granthaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**The scripture here end.**
