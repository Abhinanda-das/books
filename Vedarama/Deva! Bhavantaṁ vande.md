# Deva! Bhavantaṁ vande

*deva! bhavantaṁ vande*  
*man-mānasa-madhukaram arpaya nija-pada-paṅkaja-makarande (1)*

**Перевод:**

**O Bhagavān Śrī Kṛṣṇa! I am offering a prayer to You. Please attach the bee of my mind to the nectar of Your lotus feet.**

*yadyapi samādhiṣu vidhir api paśyati, na tava nakhāgramarīcim*  
*idam icchāmi niśamya tavācyuta! tad api kṛpādbhuta-vīcim (2)*

**Перевод:**

**Although Brahmā in his samādhi cannot obtain a glimpse of even a ray of the tips of the nails of Your feet, still Oh Achyuta! Having heard of the waves of Your astonishing mercy, I yearn to see You.**

*bhaktir udañcati yadyapi mādhava! na tvayi mama tila-mātrī*  
*parameśvaratā tad api tavādhika, durghaṭa-ghaṭana-vidhātrī (3)*

**Перевод:**

**Oh Mādhava, although I don’t have even a sesame seed of devotion for You, Your supreme power can make even the impossible become possible.**

*ayam avilolatayādya sanātana, kalitādbhuta-rasa-bhāram*  
*nivasatu nityam ihāmṛta-nindini, vindan madhurima-sāram (4)*

**Перевод:**

**Hey Sanātana! Your lotus feet put to shame the sweetness of heavenly nectar. Therefore please make the bee of my mind undistracted by material desires and give me eternal residence at Your lotus feet - this is my only prayer.**
