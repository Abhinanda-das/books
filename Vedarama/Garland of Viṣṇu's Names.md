# Garland of Viṣṇu's Names

## Garland of Viṣṇu's Names

**According to the Commentary of Sri Parasara Bhatta**

### Verse 1

*1. om viśvasmai namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the plenum - fill in all respects.**

*2. om viṣṇave namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the one who pervades everything in the universe.**

*3. om vaṣaṭ-kārāya namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the controller & director of the universe.**

*4. om bhūta-bhavya bhavat-prabhave namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the master of all things past, present and future.**

*5. om bhūta-krte namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the one who creates everything without depending on anything.**

*6. om bhūta-bhrte namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the supporter of all, who bears all things.**

*7. om bhāvāya namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the existent one**

*8. om bhūtātmane namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the Over-self of all being**

*9. om bhūta-bhāvanāya namaḥ \|\|*

### Verse 2

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the nourisher of all beings**

*10. om pūtātmane namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the pure self — who in spite of being the over-self of all beings he remains**  
**unaffected by Material Nature.**

*11. om paramātmane namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the Supreme Self**

*12. om muktānām paramā-gataye namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the Supreme goal of the liberated ones (muktas)**

*13. om avyayāya namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to him who rejects none, no one who makes an effort to reach Him is sent away.**

*14. om puruṣāya namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the generous one who bestows boons in plenty.**

*15. om sakṣiṇe namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the supreme witness who sees all things.**

*16. om kṣetrajñāya namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Salutations to the knower of all fields of action and the particular place to which to send the**  
**liberated ones (muktas)**
