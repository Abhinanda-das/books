# Gaura-Govinda-arcana-smaraṇa-paddhati \[BS\]

## Gaura-Govinda-arcana-smaraṇa-paddhati

*śrī-śrī-guru-gaurāṅgau jayataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Guru and Gaurānga remain excellent!**

*atha śrī-kṛṣṇasya svarūpaṁ nirūpyate—*

**Перевод:**

**The svarūpa of Kṛṣṇa is defined:**

*śrī-kṛṣṇas tu svayaṁ-rūpo bhedāḥ sarve tato’khilāḥ \|*  
*prādurbhūtās tataḥ kṛṣṇa upāsyeṣu varaḥ smṛtaḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa is svayaṁ-rūpa. All others are distinct from him. Other forms of the Lord have manifested from him. Kṛṣṇa is the best among all worshippable objects.**

*yathā brahma-saṁhitāyām—*

**Перевод:**

**Brahma-saṁhitā (**[**5.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/5.1)**) says:**

*īśvaraḥ paramaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sac-cid-ānanda-vigrahaḥ \|*  
*anādir ādir govindaḥ sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam \|\| iti \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa is the supreme controller. He is the enjoyer of innumerable consorts, and the possessor of an eternal form of knowledge and bliss. Though, he is without origin, he is the source of all other forms of God, and is the cause of all causes. He is Govinda, the boy who cares for the cows.**

*nanda-nandana evāyaṁ sarva-śāstreṣu sammataḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This Kṛṣṇa is the son of Nanda as approved by all scriptures.**

*yathā śrī-gautamīya-tantre daśākṣara-kathane—*

**Перевод:**

**In Gautamīya-tantra (2.18-19, 22-23), while discussing the tensyllable mantra, it is said:**

*gopīti prakṛtiṁ vidyāj janas tattva-samūhakaḥ \|*  
*anayor āśrayo vyāptyā kāraṇatvena ceśvaraḥ \|\|4\|\|*  
*sāndrānandaṁ paraṁ jyotir vallabhatvena kathyate \|*  
*athavā gopī prakṛtir janas tad-aṁśa-maṇḍalam \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should know that the term gopī (in the ten-syllable mantra) means prakṛti. The word jana means the assembly of all the material elements starting with mahat-tattva. The word vallabha means the shelter of prakṛti (cause) and its elements (effect), the Lord, since he is the final cause and pervades everything and who is the supreme effulgence, condensed bliss. Or, gopī can mean the spiritual prakṛti, and jana can mean the assembly of aṁśas of the Lord.**

*anayor vallabhaḥ proktaḥ svāmī kṛṣṇākhya īśvaraḥ \|*  
*kārya-kāraṇayor īśaḥ śrutibhis tena gīyate \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Their master, addressed as Vallabha, is the Lord called Kṛṣṇa. The Lord of cause (prakṛti) and effect (elements) is thus glorified by the śrutis in this mantra.**

*aneka-janma-siddhānāṁ gopīnāṁ patir eva vā \|*  
*nanda-nandana ity uktas trailokyānanda-vardhanaḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Another meaning is that Kṛṣṇa is the master of many cowherd women who achieved perfection after many births. He is called Nanda-nandana, son of Nanda, and increases the bliss of the three worlds.**

*tathā ca daśame brahma-stutau—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Tenth Canto, in Brahmā’s prayers, it is said:**

*naumīḍya te’bhra-vapuṣe taḍid-ambarāya*  
*guñjāvataṁsa-paripiccha-lasan-mukhāya \|*  
*vanya-sraje kavalavetra-viṣāṇaveṇu-*  
*lakṣma-śriye mṛdu-pade paśu-pāṅgajāya \|\| iti \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O worshippable Lord! I offer my obeisances to you to attain you, Nanda’s son, having a body the complexion of a rain cloud, wearing cloth flashing like lightning, with face shining among peacock feathers and guñja berry decorations, with colorful forest garland, and possessing soft feet, his beauty enhanced by yogurt rice, stick, horn and flute. (SB** [**10.14.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/14.1)**)**

*śrī-kṛṣṇas tu svayaṁ-rūpaḥ prādurbhūto vraje’bhavat \|*  
*nanda-gehe śuci-rasaṁ bhaktebhyo dātum unnatam \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa is svayaṁ-rūpa who has appeared in Nanda’s house in Vraja to give to the devotees (śrutis) intense mādhurya-rasa.**

*bhaktebhyaḥ śrutyādibhya ityarthaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**“The devotees” (in masculine) refers to the śrutis, etc.**

*yathā bṛhad-vāmana-purāṇe—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Bṛhad-vāmana Purana:**

*gāyatrī-muni-devebhyo dātuṁ śuci-rasaṁ nijam \|*  
*nanda-gehe svayaṁ kṛṣṇo vraje prādurbabhūva ha \|\| iti \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa appeared in Nanda’s house in Vraja to give mādhurya-rasato Gāyatrī, sages and the devatās.**

*gāyatrī vraje gopītvaṁ prāpteti viṣṇu-purāṇe kathitam \|*

**Перевод:**

**It is said in Viṣṇu Puṛāna that Gāyatrī became a gopī in Vraja.**

*tad yathā*  
*brahma-lokāt samāgatya vraje gāyatry ajāyata \|*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇasya mahā-gūḍham upabhoktuṁ rasaṁ śucim \|\| iti \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Gāyatrī came from Brahma-loka and was born in Vraja in order to enjoy most confidential mādhurya-rasa with Kṛṣṇa. Viṣṇu Purāṇa**

*gāyatrī ca tatra jātā iti pādme sṛṣṭi-khaṇḍe*

**Перевод:**

**Gāyatrī was born in Vraja according to Padma Purāṇa, Sṛṣṭikhaṇḍa:**

*yathā brahmaṇo gopa-kanyā-rūpāyā*  
*gāyatryā udvāhe gopeṣu śrī-viṣṇu-vacanam*

**Перевод:**

**Viṣṇu spoke to the cowherds in the marriage of Brahmā with Gāyatrī, who was in the form of a cowherd girl.**

*mayā jñātvā tataḥ kanyā dattā caiṣā variñcaye \|*  
*yuṣmākaṁ ca kule cāhaṁ deva-kāryārtha-siddhaye \|*  
*avatāraṁ kariṣyāmi mat-kāntā tu bhaviṣyati \|\| iti \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Known by me, this cowherd girl, Gāyatrī, has been given to Brahmā. But I will appear as an avatāra in your family to execute activities for the devatās and at that time, she will be my lover.**

*ādau kṛṣṇas tato māyā yugmaṁ prādurabhūd vraje \|*  
*kanyām ādāya mathurāṁ vāsudeve gate sati \|*  
*prādurbhūtaṁ nanda-sutaṁ vasudeva-suto’viśat \|\| iti \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**First Kṛṣṇa and then Māyā, both appeared in Vraja. When Vasudeva went to Mathurā taking Nanda’s daughter, the son of Vasudeva entered into Nanda’s son who had just appeared.**

*yathā bṛhad-viṣṇu-purāṇe—*

**Перевод:**

**In Bṛhad-viṣṇu Puṛāna it is said:**

*śrī-kṛṣṇe māyayā sārdhaṁ yaśodā-purato gate \|*  
*prākāśyaṁ mohitāḥ sarve babhūvur vraja-vāsinaḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛṣṇa along with Māyā appeared in front of Yaśodā, the people of Vraja become evidently unconscious.**

*mathurāyāḥ sutaṁ gṛhṇann āgatyānakadundubhiḥ \|*  
*nandasya sadanaṁ gatvāpaśyat kanyāṁ na vai sutam \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vasudeva, taking his son and coming from Mathurā, went to Nanda’s house and saw a daughter, not a son.**

*svasutaṁ tatra saṁsthāpya kanyām ādāya nirgate \|*  
*vasudeve vāsudevaḥ prāviśan nanda-nandanam \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When, after placing his son there, Vasudeva took the daughter and left, Vasudeva’s son merged into Nanda’s son.**

*tadā vrajālayāḥ sarve babhūvuḥ prāpta-cetasaḥ \|*  
*diśaḥ prasedur maruto vavuḥ puṇyāḥ sukha-pradāḥ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Then everyone in Vraja regained consciousness. The directions were pleased and pure wind which gave happiness blew.**

*meghā vicchinnatāṁ yātāś candro’bhūn nirmalodayaḥ \|*  
*nirmalās tārakāḥ sarvā grahāś ca gagane’bhavan \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The clouds parted and the moon rose purely. The shining stars and all planets appeared in the sky.**

*tadānanda-paro nando brāhmaṇair veda-pāragaiḥ \|*  
*kārayāmāsa vidhinā jāta-karmātmajasya ca \|\| iti \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda became blissful and had brāhmaṇas expert in the Vedas perform the jāta-karma for his son according to the rules.**

*yathā śrī-daśame—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Tenth Canto it is said:**

*nandas tv ātmaja utpanne jātāhlādo mahāmanāḥ \|*  
*āhūya viprān veda-jñān snātaḥ śucir alaṅkṛtaḥ \|\|20\|\|*  
*vācayitvā svasty-ayanaṁ jāta-karmātmajasya ca \|*  
*kārayāmāsa vidhivat pitṛ-devārcanaṁ tathā \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛṣṇa appeared as his son, Nanda Mahārāja, fully devoted, was overwhelmed by jubilation. After bathing and purifying himself and dressing properly, he invited brāhmaṇas who knew how to recite Vedic mantras. After having these qualified brāhmaṇas recite auspicious Vedic hymns, he arranged to have the Vedic birth ceremony celebrated for his newborn child according to the rules and regulations, and he also arranged for worship of the devatās and Pitṛs. (SB** [**10.5.1-3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/5.1)**)**

*dhenūnāṁ niyute prādād viprebhyaḥ samalaṅkṛte \|*  
*tilādrīn sapta-ratnaugha-śāta-kumbhāmbarāvṛtān \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda Mahārāja gave two million cows, completely decorated with cloth and jewels, in charity to the brāhmaṇas. He also gave them seven hills of grain, covered with jewels and with cloth decorated with golden embroidery.**

*tathā ca śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**This is described in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta:**

*kecid bhāgavatāḥ prāhur evam ete purātanāḥ \|*  
*vyūho harer bhaved ādyo gṛheṣv ānakadundubheḥ \|\|23\|\|*  
*goṣṭhe tu māyayā sārddhaṁ śrī-līlā-puruṣottamaḥ \|*  
*gatvā yadu-varo goṣṭaṁ tatra sūti-gṛhaṁ viśan \|\|24\|\|*  
*kanyām eva paraṁ vīkṣya tām ādāyāvrajat puram \|*  
*prāviṣad vāsudevas tu śrī-līlā-puruṣottamam \|\|25\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some devotees of ancient times say that Vāsudeva, the first member of the catur-vyūha, appeared in the house of Vasudeva and the supreme performer of pastimes (līlā-puruṣottama) appeared in the house of Nanda along with yoga-māyā.**  
**When Vasudeva went to Gokula and entered the delivery room of Yaśodā, he saw only a daughter, and taking her, he left for Mathurā. The son of Vasudeva (the catur-vyūha Vāsudeva) then merged into the son of Nanda (līlā-puruṣottama). Laghubhāgavatāmṛta**

*etac cātirahasyatvān noktaṁ tatra katha-krame \|*  
*kintu kvacit prasaṅgena procyate’tra śukādibhiḥ \|\| iti \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because it is very confidential, it is not mentioned directly in the text. But it is indicated by Śukadeva in some incidental remarks, such as the following statements.**

*yathā daśame—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Tenth Canto:**

*nandas tv ātmaja utpanne jātāhlādo mahāmanāḥ \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛṣṇa appeared as his son Nanda Mahārāja, fully devoted, was overwhelmed byjubilation. (SB** [**10.5.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/5.1)**)**

*nandaḥ svaputram ādāya pretyāgatam udāradhīḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Mahārāja Parīkṣit, best of the Kurus, Nanda Mahārāja was very liberal and simple. He immediately took his son Kṛṣṇa on his lap as if Kṛṣṇa had returned from death. (SB** [**10.6.43**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/6.43)**)**

*nāyaṁ sukhāpo bhagavān dehināṁ gopikāsutaḥ \|\| iti \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, son of Yaśodā, is not easily available to materialists or to jñānīs with realization of ātmā. (SB** [**10.9.21**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/9.21)**)**

*yathā tatraiva śrī-brahmaṇaḥ stave—*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā says in his prayers:**

*vanya-sraje kavala-vetra-viṣāṇa-veṇu-*  
*lakṣma-śriye mṛdu-pade paśupāṅgajāya \|\| iti \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O worshippable Lord! I offer my obeisances to you to attain you, Nanda’s son, with colorful forest garland, and possessing soft feet, his beauty enhanced by yogurt rice, stick, horn and flute. (SB** [**10.14.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/14.1)**)**

*vyūho nandātmajasyādyo vasudeva-gṛhe’bhavat \|*  
*prakāśaś ceti siddhāntaḥ purāṇeṣu viniścitaḥ \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The first vyūha (expansion) of Nanda’s son appeared in Vasudeva’s home.**  
**This is definitely proved in the Purāṇas.**

*bhū-bhāra-haraṇārthāya yācñayā divaukasām \|*  
*kṣīrābdhiśāyī tatraivāviṣṭo’bhūd iti niścitam \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the devatās requested the Lord to remove the burden of the earth, it is understood that Kṣīrodakaśāyī entered Kṛṣṇa also.**

*yathā śrī-daśame nandaṁ prati śrī-garga-vākyam—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Tenth Canto, Garga speaks to Nanda:**

*prāgayaṁ vasudevasya kvacij jātas tavātmajaḥ \|\| iti \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This beautiful son of yours somewhere appeared previously as the son of Vasudeva. (SB** [**10.8.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/8.14)**)**

*tathā ca śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**In Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta it is said:**

*yad-vilāso mahāśrīśaḥ śrī-līlā-puruṣottamaḥ \|*  
*āvirbubhūṣur atrāviṣkṛtya saṅkarṣaṇaṁ puraḥ \|*  
*antaḥsthitāviṣkartavya-tad-anya-vyūha īśvaraḥ \|\|34\|\|*  
*hṛdaye prakaṭastasya bhavaty ānakadundubheḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord of pastimes of whom the Lord of Vaikuṇṭha is a vilāsa expansion, desiring to appear in Gokula and Mathurā, first made Saṅkarṣaṇa appear. Containing with himself the rest of the vyūhas, he appeared in the heart of Vasudeva.**

*bhūmi-bhāra-nivārāya devānām abhayāya ca \|\|35\|\|*  
*dvāparasyāvasāne’sminn aṣṭāviṁśa-catur-yuge \|*  
*kṣīrābdhiśāyi-yad-rūpam aniruddhatayā smṛtam \|\|36\|\|*  
*tad idaṁ hṛdaya-sthena rūpeṇānakadundubheḥ \|*  
*aikyaṁ prāpya tato gacchet prākaṭyaṁ devakī-hṛdi \|\| iti \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At the end of Dvāpara-yuga in the twenty-eighth yuga cycle of Vaivasvata Manu, in order to remove the burden of the earth and in order to make the devatās fearless, the form known as Aniruddha who rests on the milk ocean merged with that form in Vasudeva’s heart. The Lord then appeared in the heart of Devakī.**

*āvirbhāvo manasyeva kṛṣṇasyātmajatā mataḥ \|*  
*devakyā vasudevasya tathā nanda-yaśodayoḥ \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa appeared as a son like a human being, to Devakī and Vasudeva and then to Nanda and Yaśodā.**

*tathā śrī-daśame—*

**Перевод:**

**In Tenth Canto it is said:**

*āviveśāṁśa-bhāgena mana ānakadundubheḥ \|\| iti \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa entered the mind of Vasudeva in full splendor. (SB** [**10.2.16**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/2.16)**)**

*dadhāra sarvātmakam ātmabhūtaṁ*  
*kāṣṭhā yathānanda-karaṁ manastaḥ \|\| iti \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Devakī, became beautiful by carrying Kṛṣṇa, joy to all beings, who appeared on his own, just as the east becomes beautiful by carrying the rising moon. (SB** [**10.2.18**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/2.18)**)**

*brahma-saṁhitā-ṭīkāyāṁ śrī-jīva-gosvāmi-caraṇair uktam—*

**Перевод:**

**In the commentary on Brahma-saṁhitā (**[**5.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/5.1)**), Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī says:**

*ātmajatvaṁ hi tasya śrī-vasudevasya manasy āvirbhūtatvam eva matam;*  
*yathā śrī-devakyā api \| śrī-vrajeśvarayor api tathāsīd eva, phalena phala*  
*kāraṇam anumīyate iti śrī-bhagavat-prādurbhāvasya pūrvāvyavahita*  
*kāle vyāpitayā sarvatra darśanāt \| kintv ātmani tasyāvirbhāve saty apy*  
*ātmajatvāya pitṛ-bhāva-mayaṁ śuddhaṁ mahā-premaiva prayojakam \|*  
*yathā brahmaṇaḥ sakāśād varāha-devasyāvirbhāve saty api parasparaṁ*  
*tathā darśanābhāvāt; tathā nṛsiṁha-deva-stambhayor api \|*  
*na ca vaktavyam udara-praveśe sati putratvaṁ syāt; parīkṣid*  
*rakṣaṇārthaṁ tan-mātur udare praviṣṭe ca tayos tādṛśa*  
*vyavahārābhāvāt \| tasmād vātsalyābhidha-premaiva putratve kāraṇam;*  
*tādṛśaṁ śuddhaṁ prema tu śrī-vrajeśvarayor eva, śrī-vasudeva-devakyos*  
*tu paramaiśvarya-jñānaṁ pratibandhakam \| iti*

**Перевод:**

**He was the son of Vasudeva because he appeared in Vasudeva’s mind: āviveśāṁśa-bhāgena mana ānakadundubheḥ: he entered the mind of Vasudeva in partial form. (SB** [**10.2.16**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/2.16)**) Just as he appeared in Devakī. Dadhāra sarvātmakam ātma-bhūtaṁ kāṣṭhā yathānanda-karaṁ manastaḥ: Devakī became beautiful by carrying Lord Kṛṣṇa, the original consciousness for everyone, the cause of all causes, within the core of her heart, just as the east becomes beautiful by carrying the rising moon. (SB** [**10.2.18**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/2.18)**)**

**In the same way he appeared to Nanda and Yaśodā also. One can infer the cause of a result by the cause itself. One sees this everywhere, over contiguous time before the Lord’s appearance. When Kṛṣṇa appeared in their minds, the great, pure prema with parental sentiments was the cause of his being their son.**

**Though Varāha appeared from the nostril of Brahmā, there was no such sentiments seen between Brahmā and Varāha. Similarly Nṛsiṁha and the pillar (from which he appeared) too did not have such sentiments. It should not be said that one becomes a son by entering into the womb. In order to protect Parīkṣit, the Lord entered Uttara’s womb but there was no vātsalya sentiments between her and the Lord. Thus the prema called vātsalya is the cause of the Lord becoming a son. Such pure prema belongs to Nanda and Yaśodā alone. In Vasudeva and Devakī it is obstructed by their knowledge of Kṛṣṇa’s supreme powers.**

*yathā śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta:**

*asamordhvena bhagavān vātsalyena vrajeśayoḥ \|*  
*sutatvenaiva sa tayor ātmānaṁ vetti sarvathā \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord at all times identifies himself as son of Yaśodā and Nanda due to their incomparable parental affection. (Laghubhāgavatāmṛta)**

*prakaṭāprakaṭā ceti kṛṣṇa-līlā dvidhā matā \|*

**Перевод:**

**The pastimes of Kṛṣṇa are of two types: the manifest and the unmanifest.**

*iti dhāma-traye kṛṣṇo viharaty eva sarvadā \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Thus it may be said that in the three dhāmas, Kṛṣṇa eternally performs pastimes.**

*dhāma-trayaṁ vrajaṁ madhupurī dvārāvatīti ca \|*  
*kṛṣṇena prakaṭā līlā yā yā dhāma-traye kṛtāḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**The three dhāmas are Vraja, Mathurā and Dvārakā, where Kṛṣṇa performed his manifested pastimes.**

*tās tāḥ śrī-daśama-skandhe varṇitāḥ santi kṛtsnaśaḥ \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**These are described in the Tenth Canto in detail.**

*aprakaṭa-līlāyāṁ vraje kṛṣṇasya sadā vihāro \|*

**Перевод:**

**In his unmanifest pastimes, Kṛṣṇa eternally sports in Vraja.**

*yathā skānde śvetadvīpa-pater uktiḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**In Skanda Purāṇa, the Lord of Śvetadvīpa speaks:**

*asti me paramaṁ rūpaṁ yad-aṁśāt sambhavo mama \|*  
*tan-nityaṁ ramate yatra ballavī-gaṇa-veṣṭitam \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That is my supreme form. From his aṁśa, I arise. There he enjoys eternally, surrounded by the gopīs.**

*bhū-loke bhārate varṣe māthure maṇḍale śubhe \|*  
*bhūmiḥ pavitrātitarāṁ tatra vṛndāvanaṁ mahat \|\|45\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On Bhū-loka, in Bhārata-varṣa, in splendid Mathurā, there is the great, most pure land of Vṛndāvana.**

*vatsair vatsatarībhiś ca sadā krīḍati mādhavaḥ \|*  
*vṛndāvanāntaragataḥ sa-rāmo bālakair vṛtaḥ \|\|46\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Mādhava surrounded by the boys, along with Balarāma, always plays with the cows and calves within Vṛndāvana.**

*yathā ca sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām—*

**Перевод:**

**It is said in Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā:**

*dāsāḥ sakhāyaḥ pitarau preyasyaś ca harer iha \|*  
*sarve nityā muni-śreṣṭha tat-tulya-guṇaśālinaḥ \|\|47\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O best of sages! Kṛṣṇa’s servants, friends, parents, and beloveds, endowed with qualities similar to his, are all eternal.**

*yathā prakaṭa-līlāyāṁ purāṇeṣu prakīrtitāḥ \|*  
*tathā te nitya-līlāyāṁ santi vṛndāvane bhuvi \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as they are glorified in Purāṇas in the manifest pastimes, in the same way they exist in the eternal pastimes in Vṛndāvana on earth.**

*gamanāgamane nityaṁ tathaiva vana-goṣṭhayoḥ \|*  
*gocāraṇaṁ vayasyaiś ca vināsura-vighātanam \|\|49\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He herds cows with his friends, coming and going from the forest to the village. This is devoid of obstructions from demons.**

*parakīyābhimāninyas tathāsya ca priyā janāḥ \|*  
*pracchannenaiva bhāvena ramayanti nijaṁ priyam \|\| iti \|\|50\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His beloveds exist there, with the identity of parakīya-bhāva. They give Kṛṣṇa enjoyment there in secret.**

*madhupuryām aprakaṭa-līlāyāṁ śrī-kṛṣṇasya sadā vihāro*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa plays eternally in unmanifest pastimes in Mathurā.**

*yathā pādme*

**Перевод:**

**In Padma Purāṇa (Pātāla-khaṇḍa)—**

*aho madhupurī dhanyā vaikuṇṭhā garīyasī \|*  
*dinam ekaṁ nivāsena harau bhaktiḥ prajāyate \|\|*  
*vasudeva-suto yatra sadā tiṣṭhati kaṁsahā \|\| iti \|\|51\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Please hear about Vaikuṇṭha which is superior to the earth. Oh!**  
**Mathurā is superior to Vaikuṇṭha. By staying there for one day bhakti to the Lord arises. Kṛṣṇa, the son of Vasudeva, resides there eternally. dvārakāyām aprakaṭa-līlāyāṁ śrī-kṛṣṇasya vihāro-Kṛṣṇa eternally performs unmanifest pastimes in Dvārakā.**

*yathā śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta:**

*līlāṁ cāprakaṭāṁ tatra dvāravatyāṁ cikīrṣuṇā \|*  
*svayaṁ prakāśyate tena muniśāpādi-kaitavaṁ \|\|52\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring to make his pastimes in Dvārakā unmanifest, Kṛṣṇa himself brought about the pretext of the curse of the sage.**

*devādy-aṁśāvatāreṇa ye ca vṛṣṇiṣv avātaran \|*  
*kṣīrābdhiśāyi-rūpas taiḥ sārdhaṁ sva-padam āpnuyāt \|\|53\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṣīrodakaśāyī (who had previously merged into Kṛṣṇa) along with the aṁśas of devatās and others who appeared in the Vṛṣṇi dynasty, returned to their places.**

*nitya-līlā-parikarā ye syur yaduvarādayaḥ \|*  
*taiḥ sārdhaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇo dvāravatyām eva dīvyati \|\| iti \|\|54\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa performs pastimes eternally in Dvārakā with the eternal associates who are members of the Yadu clan.**

*ataḥ prakaṭa-līlāyāṁ vrajāt kṛṣṇaḥ puraṁ gataḥ \|*  
*dvārakāṁ ceti sarveṣu purāṇeṣu viniścitam iti \|\|55\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the manifest pastimes, Kṛṣṇa goes from Vraja to Mathurā and Dvārakā. This is confirmed in all Purāṇas.**

*yathā śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta:**

*atha prakaṭa-rūpeṇa kṛṣṇo madhu-purīṁ gataḥ \|*  
*vrajeśajatvam ācchādya svāṁ vyañjan vāsudevatām \|\|56\|\|*  
*yo vāsudevo dvibhujas tathā bhāti caturbhujaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**Actually Kṛṣṇa in manifest form went to Mathurā, hiding the fact that he was the son of Nanda, and revealing himself as the son of Vasudeva and in that way the son of Vasudeva (who was merged in the son of Nanda) a form with two arms, manifests a form with four arms.**

*tās tā madhupurī-līlāḥ prakaṭayya yadūdvahaḥ \|\|57\|\|*  
*dvāravatyāṁ tathā yāti tāṁ tāṁ līlāṁ prakāśayan \|\| iti \|\|58\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, protecting the Yadus, performed pastimes in Mathurā, and then went to Dvārakā to reveal the pastimes there.**

*vraje prakaṭa-līlāyāṁ kṛṣṇena viraho’bhavat \|*  
*trīn māsān vraja-lokānām iti śāstreṣu niścitam \|\|59\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the manifest pastimes on earth, the inhabitants of Vraja experienced the pain of separation for three months. This is confirmed by scriptures.**

*tad yathā tatraiva (746)*

**Перевод:**

**There it is said:**

*vraje prakaṭa-līlāyāṁ trīn māsān viraho’munā \|\| iti \|\|60\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the manifest pastimes in Vraja, they were separated from Kṛṣṇa for three months.**

*virahe’pi ca visphūrtiḥ prādurbhāvopamā hareḥ \|*  
*trimāsyāḥ paratas teṣāṁ sākṣāt kṛṣṇena saṅgatiḥ \|\|61\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**However, at that time there appeared a special sphūrti similar to his presence to give them bliss. Then, after three months, Kṛṣṇa met with them directly.**

*tad yathā tatraiva (746-768)*

**Перевод:**

**There it is said:**

*tatrāpy ajani visphūrtiḥ prādurbhāvopamā hareḥ \|*  
*trimāsyāḥ paratas teṣāṁ sākṣāt kṛṣṇena saṅgatiḥ \|\|62\|\|*  
*āvirbhāvāgatibhyāṁ sā dviprakārāsya sambhavet \|\|63\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**However, at that time there appeared a special sphūrti similar to his presence to give them bliss. Then, after three months, Kṛṣṇa met with them directly.**  
**Thus meeting with Kṛṣṇa is of two types: through unexpected appearance (after three months) and through returning (many years later).**

*tatrāvirbhāvaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Āvirbhāva (unexpected appearance):**

*vaiśleṣika-klamodreka-vivaśīkṛta-cetasām \|*  
*preṣṭhānāṁ sahasaivāgre vyagraḥ prādurbhaved asau \|\|64\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Out of great eagerness, Kṛṣṇa will unexpectedly appear in front of his beloved devotees whose hearts have been extremely exhausted due to separation.**

*uddhavāt kṛṣṇa-sandeśa ebhir yad-avadhi śrutaḥ \|*  
*prādurbhāvas tad-avadhi syād vraje vana-mālinaḥ \|\|65\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Ever since they heard the message of Kṛṣṇa delivered by Uddhava, Kṛṣṇa became present in Vraja.**

*vraje dvāravatī-sthasya prādurbhāvo muradviṣaḥ \|*  
*bṛhad-viṣṇu-purāṇādāv asakṛd bahudhocyate \|\|66\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That Kṛṣṇa while situated in Dvārakā was also present in Vraja is stated in the Bṛhad-viṣṇu Purāṇa and other scriptures many times in many ways.**

*vraje viharamāṇe’smin prādurbhūya harau tadā \|*  
*bhavet tasya pure yātrā svapnavad vraja-vāsinām \|\|67\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After making his appearance and performing pastimes in Vraja, the inhabitants of Vraja took his previous departure to Mathurā as some sort of bad dream.**

*athāgamanam—*

**Перевод:**

**His return:**

*prema sandarśayan sveṣu svavacaḥ satyatāṁ ca saḥ \|*  
*punaḥ priyaṁ harir goṣṭhaṁ āgacchati rathādinā \|\|68\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In order to give an experience of prema to his relatives, and demonstrate the truthfulness of his words (to return to Vraja), Kṛṣṇa then returns to the dear village on his chariot.**

*sva-vaco yathā śrī-daśame—*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa’s own words in the Tenth Canto:**

*tās tathā tapyatīr vīkṣya svaprasthāne yadūttamaḥ \|*  
*sāntvayāmāsa sa-premair āyāsya iti dautyakaiḥ \|\|69\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**As he departed, that best of the Yadus saw how the gopīs were lamenting, and thus he consoled them by sending messengers with this loving promise: “I will return.” (SB** [**10.39.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/39.35)**)**

*tathā tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**Also from the Tenth Canto:**

*yāta yūyaṁ vrajaṁ tāta vayaṁ ca sneha-duḥkhitān \|*  
*jñātīn vo draṣṭum icchāmo vidhāya suhṛdāṁ sukhaṁ \|\| iti \|\|70\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Now you should all return to Vraja, dear father. We shall come to see you, our dear relatives who suffer in separation from us, as soon as we have given some happiness to your well-wishing friends. (SB** [**10.45.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/45.23)**)**

*nija-priyatamasyāpi vacasā yadu-mantriṇaḥ \|*  
*etadeva vacaḥ svīyaṁ punas tenojjvalī-kṛtam \|\|71\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Uddhava spoke Kṛṣṇa’s message, the gopīs became bright.**

*yathā tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**There it is said:**

*hatvā kaṁsaṁ raṅga-madhye pratīpaṁ sarva-sātvatām \|*  
*yad āha vaḥ samāgatya kṛṣṇaḥ satyam karoti tat \|\| iti \|\|72\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having killed Kaṁsa, the enemy of all the Yadus, in the wrestling arena, Kṛṣṇa will now surely fulfill his promise to you by coming back. (SB** [**10.46.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/46.35)**)**

*tat-satyatā prakaṭitā dvārakāvāsināṁ girā \|\|73\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The truth of these words is revealed in the words of the people of Dvārakā.**

*yarhy ambujākṣāpasasāra bho bhavān*  
*kurūn madhūn vātha suhṛd-didṛkṣayā \|*  
*tatrābda-koṭi-pratimaḥ kṣaṇo bhavet*  
*raviṁ vinākṣnoriva nas tavācyuta \|\| iti \|\|74\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O lotus-eyed Lord! When you go to Hastināpura or Vraja to see your friends, one moment becomes like a trillion years for us, who belong to you, and who become like eyes without the sun.**

*atra kārike—*

**Перевод:**

**Explanatory verses:**

*bho ambujākṣa suhṛdāṁ nandādīnāṁ didṛkṣayā \|*  
*bhavān apasasārāsmān apahāya gato madhūn \|\|75\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O lotus-eyed Lord! Desiring to see Nanda and other friends you give us up and go to Mathurā.**

*mathurām iti vispaṣṭaṁ mathurā-maṇḍale vrajam \|*  
*tadānīṁ suhṛdāṁ tatra madhupuryām abhāvataḥ \|\|76\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The word Mathurā means Vraja in the area of Mathurā, since his friends at that time did not reside in Mathurā city.**

*kiñca—*

**Перевод:**

**Moreover:**

*rathena mathurāṁ gatvā dantavakraṁ nihatya ca \|*  
*sphuṭaṁ padma-purāṇe’sya kṛṣṇasyoktā vrajāgatiḥ \|\|77\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After going to Mathurā on his chariot, Kṛṣṇa, killing Dantavakra, went to Vraja. This is clearly stated in Padma Purāṇa.**

*gadya-padyādi yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**In prose and poetry verses it is said:**

*kṛṣṇo’pi taṁ hatvā yamunām uttīrya nanda-vrajaṁ gatvā sotkaṇṭhau*  
*pitarāv abhivādyāśvasya tābhyāṁ sāśrusekam āliṅgitaḥ sakala-gopa*  
*vṛddhān praṇamyāśvāsya bahu-ratna-vastrābharaṇādibhis tatrasthān*  
*sarvān santarpayāmāsa \|*

**Перевод:**

**After killing him, Kṛṣṇa, crossing the Yamunā, went to Vraja and greeting his eager parents, comforted them, and was embraced by them with tears in their throats. He offered respects to all the elder cowherds and satisfied everyone present with many jewels, clothes, ornaments, etc. (Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa, 94th Chapter)**

*kālindī-puline ramye puṇya-vṛkṣa-samācite \|*  
*gopa-nārībhir aniśaṁ krīḍayāmāsa mādhavaḥ \|\|78\|\|*  
*ramya-keli-sukhenaiva gopa-veśa-dharaḥ prabhuḥ \|*  
*baddha-prema-rasenātra māsa-dvayam uvāsa ha \|\| iti \|\|79\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the bank of the Yamunā, covered with pure trees, Kṛṣṇa sported incessantly with the gopīs. Wearing the dress of a cowherd he lived there for two months with full rasa of prema, happily performing charming pastimes.**

*yad uttīryety uttaraṇaṁ tadā plavanam ucyate \|*  
*duṣṭaṁ hatvā vraje yānaṁ snāna-pūrvam ihocitam \|\|80\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Uttīrya means he took a bath. Having killed the demon, it was proper to bathe and then go to Vraja.**

*ataḥ prakaṭa-līlāyām apy ayogo’lpa eva hi \|*  
*iti dhāma-traye kṛṣṇo viharaty eva sarvadā \|\|81\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Thus, even in the manifested pastimes, the separation was very little. It is concluded that Kṛṣṇa plays eternally in his three dhāmas.**

*vrajāgamana-kāle ca pādmokte’nyac ca vartate \|\|82\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are other facts mentioned in relation to Kṛṣṇa’s return to Vraja mentioned in the Padma Purāṇa.**

*yathā tatraiva pādmottara-khaṇḍe—*

**Перевод:**

**There in Uttara-khaṇḍa of Padma Purāṇa (94th Chapter) it is said:**

*atha tatrasthā nanda-gopādayaḥ sarve janāḥ putra-dārādi-sahitāḥ*  
*paśupakṣi-mṛgādayaś ca śrī-vāsudeva-prasādena divya-rūpa-dharā*  
*vimānārūḍhāḥ paramaṁ vaikuṇṭha-lokam avāpuḥ \|\| iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Then Nanda Mahārāja, all the cowherd men with their wives and children, all in their transcendental forms, boarded a celestial airplane and went to the supreme Vaikuṇṭha planet by the mercy of Kṛṣṇa.**

*atra kārike—*

**Перевод:**

**Explanatory verses:**

*vrajeśāder aṁśa-bhūtā ye droṇādyā avātaran \|*  
*kṛṣṇas tān eva vaikuṇṭhe prāhiṇod iti sāmpratam \|\|83\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa sent only the aṁśas of Nanda, and other, such as Droṇa, etc., (who had prayed to have Kṛṣṇa as his son in his previous birth) who had descended for pastimes on earth to Vaikuṇṭha. This was the proper action.**

*prāṇebhyo’pi priyatamair janair gokula-vāsibhiḥ \|*  
*vṛndāraṇye sadaivāsau vihāraṁ kurute hariḥ \|\| iti \|\|84\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa performs pastimes eternally in Vṛndāvana with the inhabitants of Gokula, who are dearer than the dearest persons.**

*vrajendra-nandanaḥ śrīmān tasmin vṛndāvane tataḥ \|*  
*nandādibhir vraja-janaiḥ sahitaḥ prakaṭo’bhavat \|\|85\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa became visible in Vṛndāvana along with Nanda and others of Vraja.**

*dvārakādhipatis tasmāt punar dvāravatīṁ yayau \|*  
*dvārakādhipatir dvāravatyām aprakaṭo’bhavat \|\|86\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**As lord of Dvārakā, he went again to Dvārakā. He disappeared in Dvārakā.**

*kṣīrābdhiśāyi-rūpas tu tataḥ svīyaṁ padaṁ yayau \|*  
*sakhībhiḥ preyasībhiś ca sthito nandasuto vraje \|\|87\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṣīrodakaśāyī went to his own abode. The son of Nanda stayed in Vraja with the friends and gopīs.**

*līlayā krīḍati sadā prakaṭāprakaṭākhyayā \|\|88\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He plays there always in pastimes which are called prakaṭa(manifest) and aprakaṭa (unmanifest).**

*tad yathā pādmottare gadya-padye yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**That is stated in the Uttara-khaṇḍa of Padma Purāṇa (94thChapter):**

*śrī-kṛṣṇas tu nanda-gopa-vrajaukasāṁ sarveṣāṁ paramaṁ nirāmayaṁ*  
*sva-padaṁ dattvā divi deva-gaṇaiḥ saṁstūyamāno dvāravatīṁ śrīmatīṁ*  
*viveśa \| iti \|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, having given his supreme, pure abode to the people of Gokula, entered Dvārakā, while being praised by devatās in the sky.**

*atra sārdha-kārike—*

**Перевод:**

**Explanatory verses:**

*nirāmayaṁ ca paramaṁ svīyaṁ vṛndāvanaṁ padam \|*  
*kṛṣṇas tu nanda-gopādīn vraja-sthān apy avīviśat \|\|89\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**But Kṛṣṇa made Nanda and the people of Vraja enter his Vṛndāvana which was supreme and beyond māyā.**

*dvārakādhipatiḥ śrīmān stūyamānaḥ surair divi \|*  
*purīṁ dvāravatīṁ yāta ity arthaḥ khalu sāmpratam \|\|90\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord of Dvārakā, praised by the devatās in Svarga, went to Dvārakā. This is the proper meaning.**

*kṛṣṇas tv iti tu-śabdena gadyasyārthaḥ sphuṭīkṛtaḥ \|\|91\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By the word tu in the phrase kṛṣṇas tu, the meaning of the prose passage is made clear.**

*dvārakāyāṁ śrī-kṛṣṇasyāprākaṭya-varṇanāntaraṁ yathā tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**After describing Kṛṣṇa’s disappearance in Dvārakā it is said there (Padma Purāṇa):**

*dvāravatyāṁ samāsīnaṁ rukmiṇī-sahitaṁ harim \|*  
*smaran vai śrīmad aiśvaryaṁ kṣaṇenāpnoty asaṁśayam \|\| iti \|\|92\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Remembering the Lord still situated with Rukmiṇī in Dvārakā, in a moment one without doubt attains great opulence.**

*ataḥ kārike—*

**Перевод:**

**Explanatory verses:**

*āsīnam iti śānasya vartamāne vidhānataḥ \|*  
*dvārakādhipatis tasyāṁ vihāraṁ kurute sadā \|\|93\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Since the suffix śānac is used in the present tense in the word āsīnam, it indicates that “the Lord of Dvārakā always performs pastimes there”.**

*atha kṣīrābdhināthas tu gataḥ svīyaṁ padaṁ tataḥ \|*  
*ayam evārtha āyātaḥ padasyāsya pradhānataḥ \|\|94\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṣīrodakaśāyī Viṣṇu went to his own abode. This is the predominant meaning which is obtained for this word (āsīna).**

*yathā ca tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**There also it is said:**

*yaḥ smared gopa-kanyābhiḥ krīḍantaṁ go-vraje śubhe \|*  
*sa sarvakāmān āpnoti saubhāgyaṁ caiva vindati \|\| iti \|\|95\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One who remembers the Lord playing with the cowherds and gopīsin Vraja attains all his desires and experiences auspiciousness.**

*atra kārikā—*

**Перевод:**

**An explanatory verse:**

*śatuḥ krīḍantam iti tu vartamāne vidhānataḥ \|*  
*gopa-kanyābhir aniśaṁ ramate śrī-harir vraje \|*  
*abhiprāyo ’sya padasya prādhānyenāyam āgataḥ \|\|96\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Since the suffix śatṛ in krīḍantam is used in the present tense, the predominant meaning of the word is, “Kṛṣṇa enjoys constantly with the gopīs in Vraja.”**

*tathā ca śrī-daśame—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Tenth Canto it is said:**

*jayati jana-nivāso devakī-janma-vādo*  
*yadu-vara-pariṣat svair dorbhir asyann adharmam \|*  
*sthira-cara-vṛjina-ghnaḥ su-smita-śrī-mukhena*  
*vraja-pura-vanitānāṁ vardhayan kāma-devam \|\|97\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa is the ultimate resort of all living entities, and is also known as the son of Devakī and Yaśodā. He is the guide of the Yadu dynasty, and with his mighty arms he kills everything inauspicious, as well as every man who is impious. By his presence he destroys all things inauspicious for all living entities, moving and inert. His blissful smiling face always increases the lusty desires of the gopīs of Vṛndāvana. May he be all glorious and happy! (SB** [**10.90.48**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/90.48)**)**

*atra kārikā—*

**Перевод:**

**An explanatory verse:**

*jayatīti pratyayasya vartamāne vidhānataḥ \|*  
*śrīmad vṛndāvane nityaṁ ramate nanda-nandanaḥ \|\|98\|\|*  
*ślokasyāyam abhiprāyaḥ pradhānatvena saṅgataḥ \|\|99\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The verbal affix in jayati indicates the present tense. Thus the verse’s meaning, “Kṛṣṇa enjoys eternally in Vṛndāvana.” is predominantly suitable.**

*vraje sadā prakaṭa-līlā-vihāritvaṁ yathā, yāmala-tantre—*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa eternally performs prakaṭa pastimes in Vraja, as stated in Yāmala-tantra:**

*kṛṣṇo’nyo yadu-sambhūto yaḥ pūrṇaḥ so’sty ataḥ paraḥ \|*  
*vṛndāvanaṁ parityajya sa kvacin naiva gacchati \|\|100\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Kṛṣṇa, who is born to the Yadus is different. The Kṛṣṇa who is complete is superior to him and never leaves Vṛndāvan to go elsewhere.**

*dvibhujaḥ sarvadā so’tra na kadācic caturbhujaḥ \|*  
*gopyaikayā yutas tatra parikrīḍati nityadā \|\| iti \|\|101\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He is always in the two-armed form in Vṛndāvana and never with four arms. He plays always in Vraja with one gopī.**

*śrī-sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyāṁ ca—*

**Перевод:**

**In Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā, it is said:**

*viharāmy anayā nityam asyāḥ prema-vaśīkṛtaḥ \|*  
*imāṁ tu mat-priyāṁ viddhi rādhikāṁ para-devatām \|\|102\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I play with Rādhā eternally, controlled by her prema. Know that Rādhā is the supreme deity, dear to me.**

*asyāś ca paritaḥ paśya sakhyaḥ śata-sahasraśaḥ \|*  
*nityāḥ sarvā imās tatra yathāhaṁ nitya-vigrahaḥ \|\|103\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**See all around, her hundreds of thousands of friends are all eternal, just as I have an eternal form.**

*sakhāyaḥ pitarau gopā gāvo vṛndāvanaṁ mama \|*  
*sarvam evaṁ nityam eva cid-ānanda-rasātmakam \|\|104\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**My friends, parents, cowherds, cows and Vṛndāvana are all eternal, composed of consciousness, bliss and rasa.**

*idam ānanda-kandākhyaṁ viddhi vṛndāvanaṁ mama \|*  
*yasmin praveśa-mātreṇa na punaḥ saṁsṛtiṁ viśet \|\|105\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Know my Vṛndāvana ānanda-kanda (the root of bliss). One who enters it will not return to saṁsāra.**

*vṛndāvanaṁ parityajya naiva gacchāmy ahaṁ kvacit \|*  
*nivasāmy anayā sārdham aham atraiva sarvadā \|\| iti \|\|106\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Leaving Vṛndāvana, I do not go elsewhere ever. I live here with her at all times.**

*tathā ca mahāvārahe—*

**Перевод:**

**It is said in Mahā-varāha Purāṇa:**

*vṛndāvane kadambas tu nityaṁ bhavati puṣpitaḥ \|*  
*tatra krīḍā-setu-bandhe śrī-kṛṣṇo vaḍabhīṁ kṛtaḥ \|*  
*tatraiva ramaṇārthaṁ hi nitya-kālaṁ sa gacchati \|\| iti \|\|107\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The kadamba tree eternally blossoms in Vṛndāvana. There, Kṛṣṇa made a lookout pavilion on a bridge. He goes there daily to enjoy.**

*tathā ca śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**In Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta it is said:**

*ced adyāpi didṛkṣerann utkaṇṭhārtā nija-priyāḥ \|*  
*tāṁ tāṁ līlāṁ tataḥ kṛṣṇo darśayet tān kṛpā-nidhiḥ \|\|108\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Even today, if his dear devotees who are full of pain of longing, desire to see them, then Kṛṣṇa, the ocean of mercy, reveals those pastimes to them.**

*kair api prema-vaivaśya-bhāgbhir bhāgavatottamaiḥ \|*  
*adyāpi dṛśyate kṛṣṇaḥ krīḍan vṛndāvanāntare \|\|109\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Even today, Kṛṣṇa, playing in Vṛndāvana, is seen by some great devotees overcome by ecstasy due to prema.**

*ādivārāhe ca—*

**Перевод:**

**It is said in Ādi-varāha Purāṇa:**

*vṛndāvanaṁ dvādaśamaṁ vṛndayā parirakṣitam \|*  
*hariṇādhiṣṭhitaṁ caiva brahma-rudrādi-sevitam \|\|110\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The twelfth forest called Vṛndāvana is protected by Vṛndā, inhabited by Kṛṣṇa, and served by Brahmā, Śiva and others.**

*kṛṣṇa-krīḍā-setu-bandhaṁ mahāpātaka-nāśanam \|*  
*krīḍārthaṁ tatra vaḍabhīṁ kṛtvā devo gadādharaḥ \|*  
*tatraiva ramaṇārthaṁ hi nitya-kālaṁ sa gacchati \|\| iti \|\|111\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The bridge built for Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes destroys the greatest of sins. Having constructed a lookout pavilion there for playing, the Lord, holder of the club, goes there to enjoy daily.**

*tato vṛndāvanaṁ kṛṣṇo na jahāti kadācana \|*  
*prakaṭāprakaṭāṁ līlāṁ tatraiva kurute mudā \|\|112\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Thus Kṛṣṇa never leaves Vṛndāvana. There he joyfully performs manifested and unmanifested pastimes.**

*yathā bṛhad-gautamīya-tantre śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**In Bṛhad-gautamīya-tantra, Nārada says:**

*kim idaṁ dvādaśaṁ vanaṁ vṛndāraṇyaṁ viśāmpate \|*  
*śrotum icchāmi bhagavan yadi yogyo ’smi me vada \|\|113\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O master of the people! I desire to hear about the twelfth forest – Vṛndāvana. If I am qualified please tell me.**

*śrī-kṛṣṇa uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa then spoke:**

*idaṁ vṛndāvanaṁ ramyaṁ mama dhāmaiva kevalam \|*  
*atra ye paśavaḥ pakṣi-mṛgāḥ kīṭā narāmarāḥ \|*  
*vasanti mamādhiṣṭhāne mṛtā yānti mamālayam \|\|114\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This pleasant Vṛndāvana is my only abode. There, all cows, animals, birds, insects and humans are without death. Those who live under my mercy come to my abode on dying.**

*atra yā gopa-kanyāś ca nivasanti mamālaye \|*  
*yoginyas tā matā nityaṁ mama sevā-parāyaṇāḥ \|\|115\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The daughters of the cowherd men living in my abode are considered yoginīs. They are dedicated to serving me continually.**

*pañca-yojanam evāsti vanaṁ me deha-rūpakam \|*  
*kālindīyaṁ suṣumnākhyā paramāmṛta-vāhinī \|\|116\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This place, measuring five yojanas, is non-different from my body. The Yamunā, flowing with the sweetest nectar, is the suṣumnā (central nerve) of that body.**

*atra devāś ca bhūtāni vartante sūkṣma-rūpataḥ \|\|117\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The devatās and the elements exist here in a spiritual form.**

*sarva-deva-mayaś cāhaṁ na tyajāmi vanaṁ kvacit \|*  
*āvirbhāva-tirobhāvau syātāṁ me ’tra yuge yuge \|\|118\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I, the embodiment of all devatās, do not leave that forest for a moment. I appear and disappear there in every yuga.**

*tejo-mayam idaṁ ramyam adṛśyaṁ carma-cakṣuṣā \|\| iti \|\|119\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**But this radiant forest full of pleasure cannot be seen by material eye.**

*brahma-saṁhitā ṭīkāyāṁ ca śrī-jīva-caraṇāḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**In his commentary on Brahma-saṁhitā (**[**5.43**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/5.43)**), Jīva Gosvāmī says:**

*etad-rūpam evāśritya vārāhādau nitya-kadambādayo varṇitāḥ \|\| iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Relying on such description the kadambas and other things are described as eternal in the Varāha Purāṇa (in verse 107) and other scriptures.**

*kvacid vṛndāvane kṛṣṇo darśayan doś catuṣṭayam \|*  
*na tyajet kṛṣṇa-rūpatvaṁ sarvathāpi sa eva saḥ \|\|120\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places, Kṛṣṇa, showing four arms in Vṛndāvana, does not give up the nature of being Kṛṣṇa in any way. He is Kṛṣṇa.**

*yathā śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta:**

*kvacic caturbhujatve ’pi na tyajet kṛṣṇa-rūpatām*  
*ataḥ prakāśa eva syāt tasyāsau dvibhujasya ca \|\|121\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes, though he manifests a four-armed form, Kṛṣṇa does not give up his nature as Kṛṣṇa, the son of Yaśodā. That form should be considered a prakāśa of the two-armed form.**

*prakāśa-lakṣaṇaṁ yathā tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**The characteristics of a prakāśa are described there:**

*anekatra prakaṭatā rūpasyaikasya yaikadā \|*  
*sarvathā tat-svarūpaiva sa prakāśa itīryate \|\| iti \|\|122\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When one form manifests in many places and is, in all respects, the same as the original form it is called prakāśa.**

*viyogo yat tu kṛṣṇena gopīnāṁ mathurāgamāt \|*  
*tad eva rasa-vaiśiṣṭyaṁ kṛṣṇena prakaṭī-kṛtam \|\|123\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The separation of the gopīs from Kṛṣṇa after he went to Mathurā was a special rasa arranged by Kṛṣṇa.**

*cintāmaṇi-mayaṁ vṛndāvanam ekaṁ na tu dvayam \|*  
*prāpañca-cakṣuṣādṛśyaṁ dṛśyaṁ premāḍhya-cakṣuṣā \|\|124\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndāvana made of cintāmaṇi, is one not two. It is invisible to the material eye, but visible to the eye imbued with prema.**

*śrī-nanda-nandanas tatra sthito’ntarhitatāṁ gataḥ \|*  
*vasudeva-sutas tatra praviṣṭo mathurāṁ yayau \|\|125\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Nanda situated in Vṛndāvana, became invisible and the son of Vasudeva, who had entered him, went to Mathurā.**

*yathā brahma-saṁhitā-ṭīkāyāṁ śrīla-jīva-gosvāmi-caraṇāḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**In his commentary on Brahma-saṁhitā (**[**5.43**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/5.43)**), Jīva Gosvāmī says:**

*yadā cāsmad-dṛśyamāna-prakāśe sa-parikaraḥ śrī-kṛṣṇa āvirbhavati,*  
*tadaiva tasyāvatāra ityucyate \| tadaiva ca rasa-viśeṣa-poṣāya saṁyoga*  
*virahaḥ punaḥ saṁyogādi-mayī vicitra-līlā para-dārādi-vyavahāraś*  
*ca gamyate \| yadā tu yathātra yathā vānyatra tantra-yāmala-saṁhitā*  
*pañcarātrādiṣu tathā dig-darśanena viśeṣā jñeyāḥ \| iti \|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛṣṇa appears with his associates in a visible manifestation, it is called his avatāra. At that time, for increasing the excellence of rasa, there are various pastimes of meeting, separation and again meeting, and dealing with others’ wives. What is presented here and in other places in the tantras, yāmalas, saṁhitā and pañcarātra scriptures (concerning separation and dealing with others’ wives) should be understood to be a few of the unique pastimes when Kṛṣṇa is present on earth.**

*evaṁ dhāma-traye kṛṣṇo vihāraṁ kurute sadā \|*  
*tatrāpi ca vraje tasya mādhuryaṁ sarvato’dhikam \|\|126\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa eternally performs pastimes in these three abodes. Even among them, his sweetness is supreme in Vraja.**

*yathā śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta:**

*iti dhāma-traye kṛṣṇo viharaty eva sarvadā \|*  
*tatrāpi ca gokule tasya mādhurī sarvato’dhikā \|\| iti \|\|127\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the three dhāmas, Kṛṣṇa eternally performs pastimes. However, his sweetness in Gokula is greater than that in the other places.**

*yathā ca brahmāṇḍe—*

**Перевод:**

**In Brahmānda Purāṇa it is said:**

*santi bhūrīṇi rūpāṇi mama pūrṇāni ṣaḍ-guṇaiḥ \|*  
*bhaveyus tāni tulyāni na mayā gopa-rūpiṇā \|\| iti \|\|128\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have many forms filled with six special qualities, but they are not equal to my form as a cowherd boy.**

*tathā ca pādme bhagavad-vākye—*

**Перевод:**

**In Padma Purāṇa (Pātāla-khaṇḍa)the Lord says:**

*paśya tvaṁ darśayiṣyāmi svarūpaṁ veda-gopitam \|*

**Перевод:**

**“Look! I will show you the form hidden in the Vedas.”**

*tato ’paśyam ahaṁ bhūpa bālaṁ kālāmbuda-prabham \|*  
*gopa-kanyāvṛtaṁ gopaṁ hasantaṁ gopa-bālakaiḥ \|\|129\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O King (Ambarīṣa), then I (Vyāsa) saw a young cowherd boy colored like a black cloud, wearing yellow cloth, surrounded by young gopīs, laughing with young cowherd boys.**

*tathā ca gopāla-tāpanyām—*

**Перевод:**

**In Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad it is said:**

*tad u hovāca \| brahmaṇo’sāv anavarataṁ me dhyātaḥ stutaḥ \|*  
*parārdhānte so’budhyata \| gopa-veśo me puruṣaḥ purastād āvirbabhūva \|\| iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā then explained. When I passed the first half of my day in meditating on and praising the Lord, after that, the Supreme Lord awoke to my presence. He appeared before me wearing the dress of a cowherd.**

*tathā ca brahma-saṁhitāyām—*

**Перевод:**

**In Brahma-saṁhitā it is said:**

*cintāmaṇi-prakara-sadmasu kalpa-vṛkṣa*  
*lakṣāvṛteṣu surabhīr abhipālayantam \|*  
*lakṣmī-sahasra-śata-sambhrama-sevyamānaṁ*  
*govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi \|\|130\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I worship the Supreme Lord Govinda who affectionately tends the cows in stables whose walls are made of cintāmaṇi, surrounded by desire trees. He is eagerly served by countless gopīs.**

*sarvatra varṇitā tasya vrajasyādbhutatā parā \|\|131\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Everywhere the extraordinary nature of Vraja is described.**

*yathā śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta:**

*sa tu māthura-bhū-rūpaḥ paricchanno ’py athādbhutaḥ \|*  
*sphāraḥ saṅkucitaś ca syāt kṛṣṇa-līlānusārataḥ \|\|132\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Mathurā on earth, though of limited area, is astonishing. It expands and contracts according to the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa.**

*atraivājāṇḍa-mālāpi paryāptim upagacchati \|*  
*vṛndāvana-pratīke ’pi yānubhūtaiva vedhasā \|\|133\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā realized that even a multitude of universes could fit within just one portion of Vṛndāvana.**

*ity ato rāsa-līlāyāṁ puline tatra yāmune \|*  
*pramadā-śata-koṭyo ’pi mamur yat tat kim adbhutam \|\| iti \|\|134\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**And is it astonishing that during the rasa-līlā on the bank of the Yamunā a billion gopīs fit within that area?**

*vraje kṛṣṇasya mādhuryaṁ rājate ca caturvidham \|*  
*aiśvarya-krīḍayor veṇos tathā śrī-vigrhasya ca \|\|135\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are four types of sweetness of Kṛṣṇa in Vraja: his powers, his pastimes, his flute, and his form.**

*yathā śrī-kṛṣṇāmṛte—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta:**

*caturdhā mādhurī tasya vraja eva virājate \|*  
*aiśvarya-krīḍayor veṇos tathā śrī-vigrahasya ca \|\|136\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are four types of sweetness present in Kṛṣṇa in Vraja: sweetness of his powers, sweetness of his pastimes, sweetness of his flute and sweetness of his form.**

*tatraiśvaryasya—*

**Перевод:**

**Sweetness of his powers in Vraja:**

*kutrāpy aśruta-pūrveṇa madhuraiśvarya-rāśinā \|*  
*sevyamāno haris tatra vihāraṁ kurute vraje \|\|137\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord performs pastimes in Vraja, being served by a multitude of sweet powers unheard of anywhere else.**

*yatra padmaja-rudrādyaiḥ stūyamāno ’pi sādhvasāt \|*  
*dṛg-anta-pātam apy eṣu kurute na tu keśavaḥ \|\|138\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vraja, Kṛṣṇa, though being praised by Brahmā, Rudra and others out of fear, does not even glance upon them.**

*yathā śrī-brahmāṇḍe śrī-nārada-vākyam—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Brahmāṇda Purāṇa, Nārada speaks:**

*ye daityā duḥśakā hantuṁ cakreṇāpi rathāṅginā \|*  
*te tvayā nihatāḥ kṛṣṇa navyayā bāla-līlayā \|\|139\|\|*  
*sārdhaṁ mitrair hare krīḍan bhrū-bhaṅgaṁ kuruṣe yadi \|*  
*sa-śaṅkā brahma-rudrādyāḥ kampante kha-sthitās tadā \|\| iti \|\|140\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa! The demons difficult to kill by your cakra when you showed your Dvārakā form were easily killed by you during your childhood pastimes. O Kṛṣṇa! If you move your eyebrow while playing with your friends in Vraja, Brahmā, Rudra and others situated in the sky shake with fear.**

*krīḍāyāḥ, yathā pādme—*

**Перевод:**

**Sweetness of his pastimes, as mentioned in Padma Purāṇa:**

*caritaṁ kṛṣṇa-devasya sarvam evādbhutaṁ bhavet \|*  
*gopāla-līlā tatrāpi sarvato ’timanoharā \|\|141\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All the activities of Kṛṣṇa are astonishing. But his pastimes with the cowherd boys and the gopīs are more attractive than the others.**

*bṛhad-vāmane—*

**Перевод:**

**In Bṛhad-vāmana Purāṇa it is said:**

*santi yadyapi me prājyā līlās tās tā manoharāḥ \|*  
*na hi jāne smṛte rāse mano me kīdṛśaṁ bhavet \|\| iti \|\|142\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though there are many attractive pastimes of mine, I do not know what will happen to my mind when I think of the rāsa dance.**

*veṇoḥ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The sweetness of the flute:**

*yāvatī nikhile loke nādānām asti mādhurī \|*  
*tāvatī vaṁśikā-nāda-paramānau nimajjati \|\|143\|\|*  
*cara-sthāvarayoḥ sāndra-paramānanda-magnayoḥ \|*  
*bhaved dharma-viparyāso yasmin dhvanati mohane \|\|144\|\|*  
*mohanaḥ ko ’pi mantro vā padārtho vādbhutaḥ paraḥ \|*  
*śruti-peyo ’yam ity uktvā yatrāmuhyañ chivādayaḥ \|\|145\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Whatever sweetness resides in all the sounds in the whole universe is contained in one atom of the sound of Kṛṣṇa’s flute. When the flute sounds attractively, all moving and living entities, absorbed in the most intense bliss, reverse their qualities. Śiva and others become bewildered and say, “What kind of bewitching mantra is this that we are drinking with our ears? What most astonishing object is this?”**

*yathā śrī-daśame—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Tenth Canto it is said:**

*vividha-gopa-caraṇeṣu vidagdho*  
*veṇu-vādya urudhā nija-śikṣāḥ \|*  
*tava sutaḥ sati yadādhara-bimbe*  
*datta-veṇur anayat svara-jātīḥ \|\|146\|\|*  
*savanaśas tad-upadhārya sureśāḥ*  
*śakra-śarva-parameṣṭhi-purogāḥ \|*  
*kavaya ānata-kandhara-cittāḥ*  
*kaśmalaṁ yayur aniścita-tattvāḥ \|\| iti \|\|147\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O pious mother Yaśodā, your son, who is expert in all the arts of herding cows, has invented many new styles of flute-playing. When he takes his flute to his bimba-red lips and sends forth the tones of the harmonic scale in variegated melodies, Brahmā, Śiva, Indra and other chief demigods become confused upon hearing the sound. Although they are the most learned authorities, they cannot ascertain the essence of that music, and thus they bow down with their heads and hearts. (SB** [**10.35.14-15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/35.14)**)**

*ekaviṁśe tathā pañcatriṁśe cādhyāya īritā \|*  
*mādhurī vraja-devībhir veṇor eva mahādbhutā \|\|148\|\|*

*tasya yat kathanaṁ tat tu tad-aṁśānāṁ tatra praveśāpekṣayā \| tad alam*  
*ativistareṇa śrī-kṛṣṇa-sandarbhe darśita-careṇa” iti \|*

**Перевод:**

**In Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad, Brahmā says:**

*tad u hovāca brahmaṇo ’sāv anavarataṁ me dhyātaḥ stutaḥ \|*  
*parārdhānte so ’budhyata \| gopaveśo me puruṣaḥ purastād āvirbabhūva\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I, Brahmā, meditate on and praise him continually. After half my life I realized him. He appeared in front of me as a male in the dress of a cowherd.**  
**Some people speak of Kṛṣṇa as an avatāra of Kṣirodakaśayī Viṣṇu, but that is in regard to those aṁśa forms entering into Kṛṣṇa. Enough with the topic, which has been shown previously in great detail in the Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha. (Now we return to the main subject.)**

*ayaṁ vrajeśvara-suto rasa-rūpo hi sarvadā \|*  
*gopīnāṁ vihvalaḥ premṇopabhuṅkte svarasaṁ śuciṁ\|\|156\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, son of Nanda, is the form of rasa at all times. Overwhelmed by the prema of the gopīs, he experiences his own mādhurya-rasa.**

*yathā lalita-mādhave—*

**Перевод:**

**In Lalita-mādhava it is said:**

*aparikalita-pūrvaḥ kaś camatkāra-kārī*  
*sphurati mama garīyān eṣa mādhurya-pūraḥ \|*  
*ayam aham api hanta prekṣya yaṁ lubdha-cetāḥ*  
*sarabhasam upabhoktuṁ kāmaye rādhikeva \|\|157\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have never before seen myself with such astonishing sweetness. I become greedy and impetuously desire to enjoy it like Rādhā.**

*śrī-sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyāṁ ca—*

**Перевод:**

**In Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā it is said:**

*aham āsāṁ mahā-deva gopīnāṁ prema-vihvalaḥ \|*  
*kriyāntaraṁ na jānāmi nātmānam api nārada \|\|158\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Śiva! I was overwhelmed with prema of those gopīs. O Nārada! I do not know other activities or even myself.**

*tathā ca padme—*

**Перевод:**

**In Padma Purāṇa it is said:**

*santi yadyapi me prājyā līlās tās tā manoharāḥ \|*  
*na hi jāne smṛte rāse mano me kīdṛśaṁ bhavet \|\| iti \|\|159\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though there are many attractive pastimes of mine, I do not know what will happen to my mind when I think of the rāsa dance.**

*atra kārikā—*

**Перевод:**

**An explanatory verse:**

*śrī-kṛṣṇo rasa-rūpo’pi rasam āsvādayaty alam \|*  
*candro’mṛta-śarīro’pi pibaty eva sudhā-rasam \|\|160\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, the form of rasa, tastes rasa in full, just as the moon, though having a body of nectar, drinks tasty nectar.**

*ato vrajeśvara-suto bhaktyā rāgānugākhyayā \|*  
*sādhakaiḥ prema-vaivaśya-bhāgbhir bhāvyaḥ sadaiva hi \|\|161\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Nanda is constantly experienced by the sādhakas who are controlled by prema, through rāgānuga-bhakti.**

*rāgānugā-lakṣaṇaṁ tayor bhajanaṁ ca yathā śrī-bhakti-rasāmṛtasindhau—*

**Перевод:**

**The characteristics of rāgānugā bhakti and the worship of the couple are described in Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu:**

*virājantīm abhivyaktāṁ vraja-vāsi-janādiṣu \|*  
*rāgātmikām anusṛtā yā sā rāgānugocyate \|\|162\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rāgānuga-bhakti is defined as that bhakti which follows after the rāgātmika-bhakti found distinctively in the inhabitants of Vraja.**

*rāgānugā-vivekārtham ādau rāgātmikocyate \|\|163\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In order to define rāgānuga-bhakti first rāgātmika-bhakti is discussed.**

*iṣṭe svārasikī rāgaḥ paramāviṣṭatā bhavet \|*  
*tan-mayī yā bhaved bhaktiḥ sātra rāgātmikoditā \|\|164\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rāga is defined as spontaneous, deep thirst for the object of love. Bhakti that is impelled exclusively by such a thirst is called rāgātmika-bhakti.**

*sā kāmarūpā sambandha-rūpā ceti bhaved dvidhā \|\|165\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are two types of rāgātmika-bhakti: that impelled by amorous feelings (kāma-rūpā) and that impelled by other relationships (sambandha-rūpā).**

*tathā hi saptame—*

**Перевод:**

**These two types of rāgātmika-bhakti are illustrated in the Seventh Canto:**

*kāmād dveṣād bhayāt snehād yathā bhaktyeśvare manaḥ \|*  
*āveśya tad-aghaṁ hitvā bahavas tad-gatiṁ gatāḥ \|\|166\|\|*  
*gopyaḥ kāmād bhayāt kaṁso dveṣāc caidyādayo nṛpāḥ \|*  
*sambandhād vṛṣṇayaḥ snehād yūyaṁ bhaktyā vayaṁ vibho \|\| iti \|\|167\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as by vaidhi-bhakti one can attain one’s spiritual goals, many persons have attained suitable forms, according to their type of absorption, after absorbing their minds in the Lord out of lust, hatred, fear and affection, and after giving up their sinful mentality (in the case of hatred and fear). (SB** [**7.1.30**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.30)**)**  
**My dear King Yudhiṣṭhira, the gopīs by their amorous desires, Kaṁsa by his fear, Śiśupāla and other kings by envy, the Vṛṣṇis of Vraja by their relationship with Kṛṣṇa, you Pāṇḍavas by your great affection for Kṛṣṇa, and we (Nārada), by our vaidhi-bhakti, have obtained the mercy of Kṛṣṇa. (SB** [**7.1.31**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.31)**)**

*ānukūlya-viparyāsād bhīti-dveṣau parāhatau \|*  
*snehasya sakhya-vācitvād vaidha-bhakty-anuvartitā \|\|168\|\|*  
*kiṁ vā premābhidhāyitvān nopayogo’tra sādhane \|*  
*bhaktyā vayam iti vyaktaṁ vaidhī bhaktir udīritā \|\|169\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because fear and hatred are not favorable, they are rejected as modes of bhakti. Affection (sneha) of the Pāṇḍavas, if it means friendliness (sakhya), belongs to vaidhi-bhakti (because the sakhya is predominated by veneration). If sneha means prema or a stage of prema, it still can not be admitted here, as the topic is sādhana-bhakti. In the phrase “bhaktyā vayam” (and we, the sages (Nārada), attained befitting goals by bhakti), bhakti refers to vaidhi-bhakti.**

*yad-arīṇāṁ priyāṇāṁ ca prāpyam ekam ivoditam \|*  
*tad brahma-kṛṣṇayor aikyāt kiraṇārkopamā-juṣoḥ \|\|170\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When it is said that the enemies and the dear friends of the Lord attained the same end, it means the same end only in the sense that Brahman and Kṛṣṇa are one entity, in the manner that the rays of the sun and the sun are one.**

*brahmaṇy eva layaṁ yānti prāyeṇa ripavo hareḥ \|*  
*kecit prāpyāpi sārūpyābhāsaṁ majjanti tat-sukhe \|\|171\|*

**Перевод:**

**The enemies of the Lord generally merge into the impersonal brahman. Some of them, attain semblance of a form similar to the Lord’s (sārūpyābhāsam) and immerse in that happiness.**

*tathā ca brahmāṇḍa-purāṇe—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa it is said:**

*siddha-lokas tu tamasaḥ pāre yatra vasanti hi \|*  
*siddhā brahma-sukhe magnā daityāś ca hariṇa hatāḥ \|\| iti \|\|172\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Siddha-loka (the spiritual world) is beyond prakṛti. There, demons killed by the Lord and perfected jīvas dwell, merged in the happiness of Brahman.**

*rāga-bandhena kenāpi taṁ bhajanto vrajanty amī \|*  
*aṅghri-padma-sudhāḥ prema-rūpās tasya priyā janāḥ \|\|173\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those persons most devoted to the Lord, who are the very form of prema and who worship him with intense, spontaneous absorption, attain the nectar of his lotus feet.**

*tathā hi śrī-daśame śruti-stave—*

**Перевод:**

**Thus, it says in the Tenth Canto, in the prayers of the Vedas:**

*nibhṛta-marun-mano ’kṣa-dṛḍha-yoga-yujo hṛdi yan*  
*munaya upāsate tad-arayo ’pi yayuḥ smaraṇāt \|*  
*striya uragendra-bhoga-bhuja-daṇḍa-viṣakta-dhiyo*  
*vayam api te samāḥ sama-dṛśo ’ṅghri-saroja-sudhāḥ \| iti \|\|174\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Simply by constantly thinking of him, the enemies of the Lord attained the same Brahman that sages fixed in yoga worship by controlling their breath, mind and senses. Similarly, we śrutis, adopting a mood similar to the gopīs, and finally attaining similar bodies, will achieve the same nectar from your lotus feet that your consorts are able to relish because of their loving attraction to your mighty, serpentine arms. (SB** [**10.87.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/87.23)**)**

*tatra kāmarūpā—*

**Перевод:**

**The definition of kāmarūpa-rāgātmika-bhakti:**

*sā kāmarūpā sambhoga-tṛṣṇāṁ yā nayati svatām \|*  
*yad asyāṁ kṛṣṇa-saukhyārtham eva kevalam udyamaḥ \|\|175\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kāma-rūpa-bhakti is prema-bhakti which turns the thirst for amorous relationship into prema, because when it is present, there is only eagerness for giving pleasure to Kṛṣṇa.**

*iyaṁ tu vraja-devīṣu suprasiddhā virājate \|*  
*āsāṁ prema-viśeṣo ’yaṁ prāptaḥ kām api mādhurīṁ \|*  
*tat-tat-krīḍā-nidānatvāt kāma ity ucyate budhaiḥ \|\|176\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This very famous kāma-rūpa-bhakti shines in the women of Vraja. Their special prema, which has attained some sweetness is called kāma by the wise because it is the cause of various amorous actions.**

*tathā ca tantre—*

**Перевод:**

**Thus, in a tantra it is said:**

*premaiva gopa-rāmāṇāṁ kāma ity agamat prathām \|\| iti \|\|177\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The prema of the gopīs has become famous as kāma.**

*ity uddhavādayo ’py etaṁ vāñchati bhagavat-priyāḥ \|\|178\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**And, because it is a form of exalted prema, persons such as Uddhava, dear to the Lord, desire that aspect of it.**

*kāma-prāyā ratiḥ kintu kubjāyām eva sammatā \|\|179\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**But the wise agree that the attraction to Kṛṣṇa seen in Kubjā is essentially kāma.**

*tatra sambandha-rūpā—*

**Перевод:**

**Next sambandha-rūpa-rāgātmika-bhakti will be discussed:**

*sambandha-rūpā govinde pitṛtvādy-ābhimānitā \|*  
*atropalakṣaṇatayā vṛṣṇīnāṁ ballavā matāḥ \|*  
*yad aiśya-jñāna-śūnyatvād eṣāṁ rāge pradhānatā \|\|180\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sambandha-rūpa-rāgātmika-bhakti is that bhakti inspired by great absorption arising from identifying oneself as the parent, friend or servant of Govinda. In sambandhād vṛṣṇayaḥ (SB** [**7.1.31**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.31)**) by secondary meaning, the word vṛṣṇayaḥ refers to the cowherd people, because these cowherds are the chief among those having intense affection (rāga), caused by a lack of awareness of Kṛṣṇa as God.**

*kāma-sambandha-rūpe te prema-mātra-svarūpake \|*  
*nitya-siddhāśrayatayā nātra samyag vicārite \|\|181\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Since kāma-rūpa-rāgātmika-bhakti and sambandha-rūparāgātmika-bhakti, which are caused by prema alone, take shelter of the nitya-siddhas, they have not been discussed in this section (on sādhana-bhakti) thoroughly.**

*rāgātmikāyā dvaividhyād dvidhā rāgānugā ca sā \|*  
*kāmānugā ca sambandhānugā ceti nigadyate \|\|182\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**From these two types of rāgātmika-bhakti (siddha-bhakti), two types of rāgānuga-bhakti (sādhana-bhakti), called kāmānuga and sambandhānuga-bhakti, are derived.**

*tatra adhikārī—*

**Перевод:**

**The qualification for rāgānuga-bhakti is as follows:**

*rāgātmikāika-niṣṭhā ye vraja-vāsi-janādayaḥ \|*  
*teṣāṁ bhāvāptaye lubdho bhaved atrādhikāravān \|\|183\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That person who is greedy for attaining a bhāva similar to that of the inhabitants of Vraja—who are fixed solely in rāgātmikabhakti—is qualified forrāgānuga-bhakti.**

*tat-tad-bhāvādi-mādhurye śrute dhīr yad apekṣate \|*  
*nātra śāstraṁ na yuktiṁ ca tal-lobhotpatti-lakṣaṇaṁ \|\|184\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The appearance of that greed is indicated when the intelligence does not depend on injunctive statements of scripture and logic, after realizing to some degree the sweetness of their (inhabitants of Vraja) love on hearing from the scriptures.**

*vaidha-bhakty-adhikārī tu bhāvāvirbhavanāvadhi \|*  
*atra śāstraṁ tathā tarkam anukūlam apekṣate \|\|185\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those qualified for vaidhi-bhakti are dependent on the injunctive statements of scripture and favorable use of logic until the appearance of bhāva-bhakti.**

*kṛṣṇaṁ smaran janaṁ cāsya preṣṭhaṁ nija-samīhitam \|*  
*tat-tat-kathā-rataś cāsau kuryād vāsaṁ vraje sadā \|\|186\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Remembering the Vṛndāvana form of Kṛṣṇa and his dear associates who have inclinations for service similar to one’s own, one who is attached to hearing topics related to them, should always live in Vraja.**

*sevā sādhaka-rūpeṇa siddha-rūpeṇa cātra hi \|*  
*tad-bhāva-lipsunā kāryā vraja-lokānusārataḥ \|\|187\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Following after the inhabitants of Vraja, one who desires that bhāva should perform service in one’s physical body and in one’s siddha body.**

*śravaṇotkīrtanādīni vaidha-bhakty-uditāni tu \|*  
*yāny aṅgāni ca tāny atra vijñeyāni manīṣibhiḥ \|\|188\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The discriminating practitioners should know that the aṅgaswhich were mentioned in vaidhi-bhakti such as hearing and chanting are the aṅgas of rāgānuga-bhakti.**

*tatra kāmānugā—*

**Перевод:**

**The elements of kāmānuga will be described:**

*kāmānugā bhavet tṛṣṇā kāma-rūpānugāminī \|\|189\|\|*  
*sambhogecchā-mayī tat-tad-bhāvecchātmeti sā dvidhā \|\|190\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That rāgānuga-sādhana-bhakti which is filled with longing and which follows after the kāma-rūpa-rāgātmika-bhakti of thesiddha-bhaktas, is called kāmānuga-bhakti. There are two types: sambhogecchā-mayī and tad-tad-bhāvecchātmikā.**

*keli-tātparyavaty eva sambhogecchā-mayī bhavet \|*  
*tad-bhāvecchātmikā tāsām bhāva-mādhurya-kāmitā \|\|191\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sambhogecchā-mayī-bhakti is characterized by enjoying amorously with Kṛṣṇa. Tad-bhāvecchātmikā-bhakti is characterized by desiring the sweet mood of love of the kāma-rūpa-siddha devotees.**

*śrī-mūrter mādhurīṁ prekṣya tat-tal-līlāṁ niśamya vā \|*  
*tad-bhāvākāṇkṣiṇo ye syus teṣu sādhanatānayoḥ \|*  
*purāṇe śrūyate pādme puṁsam api bhaved iyam \|\|192\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who develop longing for the bhāva of the gopīs after seeing the sweetness in the deity of Kṛṣṇa and the gopīs, or after hearing about his pastimes with the gopīs, are qualified for sādhana of either of these two types of kāmānuga-bhakti. In the Padma Purāṇa, it is said that even men can attain this bhakti.**

*yathā padmottara—*

**Перевод:**

**Examples of males becoming gopīs are as follows, from Padma Purāṇa:**

*purā maharṣayaḥ sarve daṇḍakāraṇya-vāsinaḥ \|*  
*dṛṣṭvā rāmaṁ hariṁ tatra bhoktum aicchan suvigraham \|\|193\|\|*  
*te sarve strītvam āpannāḥ samudbhūtāś ca gokule \|*  
*hariṁ samprāpya kāmena tato muktā bhavārṇavāt \|\|194\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Previously, all the sages living in Daṇḍakāraṇya forest, who after seeing Lord Rāma, desired enjoyment with his beautiful form, attained forms of women and appeared in Gokula. Attaining the Lord by that kāma, they became liberated from the ocean of the material world.**

*riraṁsāṁ suṣṭhu kurvan yo vidhi-mārgeṇa sevate \|*  
*kevalenaiva sa tadā mahiṣītvam iyāt pure \|\|195\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**A person who serves on the path of vaidhi-bhakti with a desire for an amorous relationship with the Lord and a high position, but without desire for the gopis’ type of love, after some time becomes a follower of the Queens in Dvārakā.**

*tathā ca mahā-kaurme—*

**Перевод:**

**Thus, it says in the Mahā-kurma Purāṇa:**

*agni-putrā mahātmānas tapasā strītvam āpire \|*  
*bhartāraṁ ca jagad-yoniṁ vāsudevam ajaṁ vibhum \|\| iti \|\|196\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The saintly sons of Agni attained bodies of women by the path of vaidhi-bhakti, and attained as their husband (not lover), the unborn, powerful Vāsudeva, source of the universe.**

*atha sambandhānugā—*

**Перевод:**

**Sambandhānuga-bhakti is defined as follows:**

*sā sambandhānugā bhaktiḥ procyate sadbhir ātmani \|*  
*yā pitṛtvādi-sambandha-mananāropanātmikā \|\|197\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The devotees define sambandhānuga-bhakti as that bhakti in which there is a constant contemplation of oneself as a parent, friend or servant of Kṛṣṇa, and identification with the role.**

*lubdhair vātsalya-sakhyādau bhaktiḥ kāryātra sādhakaiḥ \|*  
*vrajendra-subalādīnāṁ bhāva-ceṣṭita-mudrayā \|\|198\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Practicing devotees greedy for parental, friendly or servant relationships perform this bhakti with actions performed with the mood of Nanda (parent), Subala (friend), and others.**

*tathā hi śrūyate śāstre kaścit kurupurī-sthitaḥ \|*  
*nanda-sūnor adhiṣṭhānaṁ tatra putratayā bhajan \|*  
*nāradasyopadeśena siddho ’bhūd vṛddha-vardhakiḥ \|\|199\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**It is said in the scriptures that some old carpenter living in Hastināpura worshipped a deity form of Kṛṣṇa as his son, on the instructions of Nārada, and attained perfection.**

*ata eva nārāyaṇa-vyūha-stave—*

**Перевод:**

**Thus it is said in the Nārāyaṇa-vyūha-stava:**

*pati-putra-suhṛd-bhrātṛ-pitṛvan maitravad dharim \|*  
*ye dhyāyanti sadodyuktās tebhyo’pīha namo namaḥ \|\| iti \|\|200\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I pay my respects repeatedly to those who constantly and eagerly meditate upon the Lord as their husband, son, well-wisher, brother, father or friend.**

*kṛṣṇa-tad-bhakta-kāruṇya-mātra-lābhaika-hetukā \|*  
*puṣṭi-mārgatayā kaiścid iyaṁ rāgānugocyate \|\|201\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This bhakti, whose only cause of attainment is the mercy of Kṛṣṇa or his devotees, is called rāgānugā and is known as puṣṭi-mārgaby some.**

*atha śrī-mūrti-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Serving the deity:**

*yathā sādhaka-dehena sevyā mūrtir harer api \|*  
*tathā tat-sevanaṁ tasya procyate rāga-mārgikam \|\|202\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the deity of the Lord is served in one’s sādhaka body, in the same way one serves deity on the path of rāga.**

*yathā śrī-hari-bhakti-vilāse—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in Hari-bhakti-vilāsa:**

*dvibhujā jalada-śyāmā tribhaṅgī madhurākṛtiḥ \|*  
*sevyā dhyānānurūpaiś ca mūrtiḥ kṛṣṇasya daivataiḥ \|\| iti \|\|203\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should serve the deity of Kṛṣṇa, having two arms, colored like a cloud, sweet in form, bending in three places, with forms suitable for meditation. (HBV** [**5.213**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/hbv/5.213)**)**

*tathā śrī-ekādaśe—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Eleventh Canto it is said:**

*mahā-puruṣam abhyarcen mūrtyābhimatayātmanaḥ \|\|204\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The devotee should worship the Supreme Lord in the particular personal form of the Lord the devotee finds most attractive. (SB** [**11.3.48**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/3.48)**)**

*śrī-hari-bhakti-vilāsa-dhṛta-śrī-gautamīya-tantre ca—*

**Перевод:**

**In Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, quoting from Gautamīya-tantra, it is said:**

*kṛṣṇa-pratikṛtiṁ kuryād divyāṁ keśa-sucihnitām \|*  
*tāṁ tu saṁsthāpayen mantrī gṛhe vā goṣṭha-madhyataḥ \|\|205\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should make an attractive deity of Kṛṣṇa with fine hair. The practitioner of mantras should establish that deity in a house or a cow-shed. (HBV** [**18.97**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/hbv/18.97)**)**

*bahubhir janmabhiḥ puṇyair yadi kṛṣṇa-śilāṁ labhet \|*  
*goṣpadena tu cihnena janus tena samāpyate \|\|206\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one, due to the puṇya of many births, obtains a Kṛṣṇa-śālagrāmaśilā marked with a cow’s footprint, one does not take birth again.**

*agni-purāṇe ca—*

**Перевод:**

**In Agni Purāṇa also it is said:**

*dhātrī-phala-pramāṇā yā kareṇobhaya-sampuṭā \|*  
*pūjanīyā prayatnena śilā caitādṛśī varā \|\|207\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should worship a śilā the size of an amalaka, raised like the back of the hand and curved on the bottom like the palm of the hand.**

*iṣṭā tu yasya yā mūrtiḥ sa tāṁ yatnena pūjayet \|*  
*pūjite phalam āpnoti ceha loke paratra ca \|\|208\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should carefully worship the deity which one desires. One will attain the result of the worship in this and next life.**

*śālagrāma-śilā-kṛṣṇa-pratikṛtyoś ca sevanam \|*  
*pūjanaṁ cāpi vijñeyaṁ hari-bhakti-vilāsataḥ \|\|209\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should understand the worship and service of śālagrāma and the Kṛṣṇa deity from the Hari-bhakti-vilāsa.**

*siddhīcchoḥ sādhakasyāpi bhagavad-bhakti-mārgataḥ \|*  
*eka-mūrty-eka-mantrasya bhāvanā paramā matā \|\|210\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**For sādhakas desiring perfection on the path of bhakti, meditation on one form using one mantra is considered the best.**

*yathā śrī-bilvamaṅgale —*

**Перевод:**

**Bilvamaṅgala says:**

*vihāya cāpaṁ saśaraṁ karāgrād*  
*gṛhāṇa pāṇau maṇi-cāru-veṇum \|*  
*mayūra-picchaṁ kuru cottamāṅge*  
*sītā-pate tvāṁ praṇamāmi paścāt \|\| iti \|\|211\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O lord of Sītā! Giving up bow and arrows, take in your hands the jeweled flute and put a peacock feather on your head. Then I offer respects to you.**

*atha śrī-kṛṣṇa-candrasya vayo-veśādayo’khilāḥ \|*  
*rasa-śāstrānusāreṇa nirūpyante yathā mati \|\|212\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Next, the age, dress and other aspects of Kṛṣṇa will be described according to rasa scriptures, as is appropriate.**

*vayaḥ kaumāra-paugaṇḍa-kaiśoram iti tat tridhā \|\|213\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa has three ages: childhood, boyhood and youth. (BRS** [**2.1.308**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/2.1.308)**)**

*kaumāraṁ pañcamābdāntaṁ paugaṇḍaṁ daśamāvadhi \|*  
*ā-ṣoḍaśāc ca kaiśoraṁ yauvanaṁ syāt tataḥ param \|\|214\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Childhood (kaumāra) ends with the fifth year. Boyhood (paugaṇḍa) ends with the tenth year. Youth (kaiśora) ends with the sixteenth year. After that is manhood (yauvana). (BRS** [**2.1.309**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/2.1.309)**)**

*ādya-madhyānta-bhedena kaumārādīni ca tridhā \|*  
*aṣṭa-māsādhikaṁ varṣaṁ bhāgatvena ca kīrtitam \|\|215\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Childhood (kaumāra), etc., have three divisions: beginning, middle and end, each lasting about a year and eight months.**

*tad yathā,—ādya-kaumāram aṣṭa-māsādhikam eka-varṣam, evaṁ*  
*madhya-kaumāram, evaṁ ca śeṣa-kaumāram; evaṁ pañcama-varṣa*  
*paryantaṁ kaumāraṁ jñeyam \|*  
*ādya-paugaṇḍam aṣṭa-māsādhikam eka-varṣam; evaṁ madhya*  
*paugaṇḍam; evaṁ ca śeṣa- paugaṇḍam ; evaṁ krameṇa ṣaṣṭha-varṣam*  
*ārabhya daśama-varṣa-paryantaṁ paugaṇḍaṁ jñeyam \|*  
*ādya-kaiśoraṁ sārdha-dina-dvayottaraikādaśa-māsādhikam eka*  
*varṣam; evaṁ madhya- kaiśoram, evaṁ śeṣa-kaiśoram; evaṁ*  
*krameṇaikādaśa-varṣam ārabhya pañca-daśa-varṣa- nava-māsa-sārdha*  
*saptadina-paryantaṁ kaiśoraṁ jñeyam \|*

**Перевод:**

**For instance, the first part of kaumāra lasts till one year and eight months. The middle of kaumāra is another year and eight months, and the final part of kaumāra is another year and eight months. Thus kaumāra lasts till the fifth year.**  
**The first part of paugaṇḍa lasts a year and eight months, and similarly the middle and end periods of paugaṇḍa. This paugaṇḍalasts from the sixth to the tenth year.**  
**The first part of kaiśora lasts one year, eleven months, two and a half days. The middle and end period of kaiśora lasts the same time. Kaiśora lasts from the eleventh year to fifteen years, nine months and seven and a half days.**

*atha śrī-kṛṣṇasya vraja-līlā*

**Перевод:**

**Now Kṛṣṇa’s Vraja pastimes will be discussed.**

*tatra śrī-kṛṣṇasya vraja-līlā pañca-dinottara*  
*ṣaṇ-māsādhika-daśa-varṣīyā jñeyā*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa’s Vraja pastimes lasted for ten years, six months and five days.**

*tathā ca—*  
*ekādaśa-samās tatra gūḍhārciḥ sa-balo ’vasat iti \|\|216\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Like a covered flame, Kṛṣṇa remained there incognito with Balarāma for eleven years. (SB** [**3.2.26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/2.26)**)**

*mahārāja-kumāratayā bhogātiśayena samṛddhyā varṣa-māsa-dinānāṁ*  
*sārdhatayā sārdha-sapta-dinottara-nava-māsādhika-pañca-daśa-varṣa*  
*parimitaṁ śrī-kṛṣṇasya vayo jñeyam \|*

**Перевод:**

**Because of growth due to great enjoyment as the son of a king, Kṛṣṇa’s age of ten years, six months and five days extends by another half, so that his age becomes fifteen years, nine months and seven and a half days.**

*atraiva śeṣa-kaiśore ṣoḍaśa-hāyane sadā \|*  
*vraje vihāraṁ kurute śrīman-nandasya nandanaḥ \|\|217\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa eternally performs pastimes in Vraja in the sixteenth year, the final part of kaiśora period.**

*vaṁśī-pāṇiḥ pīta-vāsā indranīla-maṇi-dyutiḥ \|*  
*kaṇṭhe kaustubha-śobhāḍhyo mayūra-dala-bhūṣaṇaḥ \|\|218\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has a flute in his hand, wears yellow cloth, and has the complexion of a sapphire. On his throat is the glorious kaustubha. He has peacock feather ornament.**

*guñjā-hāra-lasad-vakṣā ratna-hāra-virājitaḥ \|*  
*vana-mālā-dharo niṣka-śobhollasita-kaṇṭhakaḥ \|\|219\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His chest shines with a guñja necklace and jeweled necklaces. He wears a forest garland. He also wears a glittering gold ornament around his neck.**

*vāma-bhāga-sthita-svarṇa-rekhā-rājad-uraḥ-sthalaḥ \|*  
*vaijayantī-lasad-vakṣā gaja-mauktika-nāsikaḥ \|\|220\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His chest shines with a golden line on the left side and a Vaijayantī garland. He has an elephant pearl hanging from his nose.**

*karṇayor makarākāra-kuṇḍalābhyāṁ virājitaḥ \|*  
*ratna-kaṅkana-yug ghastaḥ kauṅkumaṁ tilakaṁ dadhat \|\|221\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has makara earrings in his ears and jeweled bracelets on his hands. He has kuṅkuma tilaka on his forehead.**

*kiṅkiṇī-yukta-kaṭiko ratna-nūpura-yuk-padaḥ \|*  
*mālatī-mallike jāti-yūthī ketakī-campake \|\|222\|\|*  
*nāga-keśara ityādi puṣpa-mālā-svalaṅkṛtaḥ \|*  
*iti veśa-dharaḥ śrīmān dhyeyaḥ śrī-nandanandanaḥ \|\|223\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has a belt made of bells and jeweled anklets. He has garlands made of mālatī, mallikā, jāti, yūthī, ketakī, campaka and nāgakeśara flowers. In this way, one should meditate on Kṛṣṇa, who has worn such attire.**

*atha śrī-kṛṣṇasya go-cāraṇa-veśo yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa’s cowherd dress is described:**

*śṛṅgaṁ vāmodara-parisare tunda-bandhāntara-sthaṁ*  
*dakṣe tadvan nihita-muralīṁ ratna-citrāṁ dadhānaḥ \|*  
*vāmenāsau sarala-laguḍaṁ pāṇinā pīta-varṇaṁ*  
*līlāmbhojaṁ kamala-nayanaḥ kampayan dakṣiṇena \|\| iti \|\|224\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lotus-eyed Kṛṣṇa puts a horn on his left side, tucked in his sash. On his left side in his belt is his jeweled flute. In his left hand, he holds a straight stick. He shakes a yellow play lotus in his right hand.**

*asyaiva kṛṣṇa-candrasya mantrāḥ santi trayo ’malāḥ \|*  
*siddhāḥ kṛṣṇasya sat-prema-bhakti-siddhi-karā matāḥ \|\|225\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are three pure, powerful Kṛṣṇa mantras which produce pure prema-bhakti for him.**

*(1) tatrādau mantroddhāro yathā sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām—*

**Перевод:**

**The first mantra is explained in Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā:**

*hare-kṛṣṇau dvir āvṛttau kṛṣṇa tādṛk tathā hare \|*  
*hare rāma tathā rāma tathā tādṛg ghare manuḥ \|\|226\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The two words hare and kṛṣṇa should be repeated twice. The word kṛṣṇa should be repeated twice, and then the word hare (twice). Hare rāma should be repeated twice, and then rāma should be repeated twice followed by hare being repeated twice.**

*mantro yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**Thus the mantra is:**

*hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare \|*  
*hare rāma hare rāma rāma rāma hare hare \|\|227\|\|*  
*asya dhyānaṁ yathā tantreṣu—*

**Перевод:**

**The meditation using the mantra is as follows in Sanat-kumārasaṁhitā:**

*dhyāyed vṛndāvane ramye gopa-gobhir alaṅkṛte \|*  
*kadamba-pādapa-cchāye yamunā-jala-śītale \|\|228\|\|*  
*rādhayā sahitaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vaṁśī-vādana-tat-param \|*  
*tribhaṅga-lalitaṁ devaṁ bhaktānugraha-kārakam \|\| iti \|\|229\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate on Kṛṣṇa with Rādhā, playing his flute, in a pose with three bends in his body, who shows mercy to his devotees, in attractive Vṛndāvana, surrounded by cowherd boys and cows, in the shade of a tree near the cool water of the Yamunā.**

*viśeṣato daśārṇo ’yaṁ japa-mātreṇa siddhi-daḥ \|*  
*pañcāṅgāny asya mantrasya vijñeyāni manīṣibhiḥ \|\| iti \|\|230\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Particularly the ten-syllable mantra, just by japa, gives perfection. The wise understand that this mantra has five limbs. Gautamīyatantra**

*(2) śrī-gautamīya-tantra-vākyāt rāga-mārge daśākṣara-gopāla*  
*mantrasya prasiddhiḥ; tad-uddhāro likhyate, sa yathā gautamīya*  
*tantre—*

**Перевод:**

**According to Gautamīya-tantra, the ten syllable Gopāla-mantra is famous on the path of rāga. Its derivation is described in Gautamīya-tantra:**

*khāntākṣaraṁ samuddhṛtya trayodaśa-svarānvitam \|*  
*pārṇaṁ turya-svara-yutaṁ chāntaṁ dhāntaṁ tathā dvayam \|\|231\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the syllable ga the thirteenth vowel o is added (go). The syllable p is joined with the fourth vowel ī (pī). This is followed by jana.**

*amṛtārṇaṁ māṁsa-yugmaṁ mukha-vṛttena saṁyutam \|*  
*bhārṇaṁ tu mukha-vṛttāḍhyaṁ pavanārṇaṁ tathaiva ca \|\|232\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Va is followed by two l’s and the vowel a. This is followed by bhā and ya (vallabhāya).**

*bīja-śakti-samāyukto mantro ’yaṁ samudāhṛtaḥ \|*  
*gupta-bīja-svabhāvatvād daśārṇaḥ khalu kathyate \|\|233\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To this is added the bīja (klīm? ) and the śakti (svāhā). Because the bīja is naturally hidden, ten syllables are counted (instead of eleven.)**

*brahmārṇaṁ turya-saṁyuktaṁ māṁsārṇa-samanvitam \|*  
*nāda-bindu-samāyuktaṁ jagad-bījam udāhṛtam \|\|234\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The universal bījam is klīm? consisting of k, l, ī and m? .**

*śukrārṇam amṛtārṇena mukha-vṛttena saṁyutam \|*  
*gaganaṁ mukha-vṛttena proktā śaktiḥ parātparā \|\|235\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Supreme śakti is svāhā consisting of s, v, ā and hā.**

*mantro yathā—*  
*klīm? gopī-jana-vallabhāya svāhā*

**Перевод:**

**Thus, the mantra is klīm? gopījana-vallabhāya svāhā.**

*asya dhyānaṁ yathā tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**The meditation of this mantra is described there:**

*phullendīvara-kāntim indu-vadanaṁ barhāvataṁsa-priyaṁ*  
*śrīvatsāṅkam udāra-kaustubha-dharaṁ pītāmbaraṁ sundaram \|*  
*gopīnāṁ nayanotpalārcita-tanuṁ go-gopa-saṅghāvṛtaṁ*  
*govindaṁ kala-veṇu-vādana-paraṁ divyāṅga-bhūṣaṁ bhaje \|\|236\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I worship beautiful, Govinda, absorbed in playing the flute, decorated with attractive ornaments, with the complexion of a blossoming blue lotus, with a moon-like face, peacock feather head ornament, the mark of Śrīvatsa, the Kaustubha on his chest, wearing yellow cloth, whose body is worshipped by the lotuseyes of the gopīs, who issurrounded by cows and cowherds.**

*(3) atha kāma-gāyatrī-mantroddhāro yathā svāyambhuvāgame—*

**Перевод:**

**Kāma-gāyatrī is derived in the following manner from Svāyambhuvāgama:**

*klīm? tataḥ kāma-devāya vidmahe ca padaṁ tataḥ \|*  
*tataś ca puṣpa-bāṇāya dhīmahīti padaṁ tataḥ \|\|237\|\|*  
*tatas tan no ’naṅga iti tataś caiva pracodayāt \|*  
*eṣā vai kāma-gāyatrī caturviṁśākṣarī matā \|\|238\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Klīm? is followed by kāma-devāya vidmahe, then puṣpa-bāṇāya dhīmahi. This is followed by tan no ’naṅga and procodayāt. This is the kāma-gāyatrī of twenty-four syllables.**

*klīm? kāma-devāya vidmahe puṣpa-bāṇāya dhīmahi*  
*tan no ’naṅgaḥ pracodayāt \|*

**Перевод:**

**Thus the mantra is klīm? kāma-devāya vidmahe puṣpa-bāṇāya dhīmahi tan no ’naṅgaḥ pracodayāt.**

*asyā dhyānaṁ yathā tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**The meditation of the mantra is described there:**

*krīḍāsakto madana-vaśa-go rādhayāliṅgitāṅgaḥ*  
*sa-bhrū-bhaṅgaḥ smita-suvadano mugdha-nepathya-śobhaḥ \|*  
*vṛndāraṇye prati-nava-latā-sadmasu prema-pūrṇaḥ*  
*pūrṇānando jayati muralīṁ vādayāno mukundaḥ \|\|239\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Mukunda, playing his flute, full of bliss, attached to play, obedient to Cupid, his limbs embraced by Rādhā, moving his eyebrows and smiling, dressed in charming clothing, full of prema, remains glorious in Vṛndāvana, among the fresh creeper bowers.**

*atha śrī-rādhāyāḥ svarūpaṁ vayo-veśādayaś ca nirūpyante*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā’s svarūpa, age and dress, etc., are described.**

*yathā bṛhad-gautamīya-tantre—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Bṛhad-gautamīya-tantra it is said:**

*devī kṛṣṇa-mayi proktā rādhikā para-devatā \|*  
*sarva-lakṣmī-mayī sarva-kāntiḥ sammohinī parā iti \|\|240\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā, non-different from Kṛṣṇa, is the supreme devatā. All Lakṣmīs reside within her. She has all beauty, bewilders everyone and is supreme.**

*ṛk-pariśiṣṭe ca—*

**Перевод:**

**In Ṛk-pariśiṣṭa it is said:**

*rādhayā mādhavo devo mādhavenaiva*  
*rādhikā vibhrājante janeṣv ā \|\| iti \|\|241\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā shines with Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa shines with Rādhā among the people, everywhere.**

*mātsye ca—*

**Перевод:**

**In Matsya Purāṇa it is said:**

*vārāṇasyāṁ viśālākṣī vimalā puruṣottame \|*  
*rukmiṇī dvāravatyāṁ tu rādhā vṛndāvane vane \|\| iti \|\|242\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vārāṇasī, Viśālākṣī presides. In Purī, Vimalā presides. In Dvārakā, Rukmiṇī presides and in Vṛndāvana, Rādhā presides.**

*pādme ca—*

**Перевод:**

**In Padma Purāṇa it is said:**

*yathā rādhā priyā viṣṇos tasyāḥ kuṇḍaṁ priyaṁ tathā \|*  
*sarva-gopīṣu saivaikā viṣṇor atyanta-vallabhā \|\| iti \|\|243\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as Rādhā is dear to Kṛṣṇa, so is her kuṇḍa. Among all the gopīs she is dearest to Kṛṣṇa. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 4.5)**

*mahā-bhāva-svarūpeyaṁ guṇair ativarīyasī \|*  
*gopālottara-tāpanyāṁ yad gāndharveti viśrutā \|\|244\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is the very form of mahā-bhāva, and excels in all qualities. Rādhā, known as Gāndharvā, is said to excel all others in Gopālatāpanī Upaniṣad. (Ujjvalanīlamaṇi 4.3-4)**

*hlādinī yā mahā-śaktiḥ sarva-śakti-varīyasī \|*  
*tat-sāra-bhāva-rūpeyam iti tantre pratiṣṭhitā \|\|245\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**It is explained in Gautamīya-tantra that Rādhā is the essence of that great śakti called hlādinī, the best of all śaktis. (Ujjvalanīlamaṇi 4.6)**

*suṣṭhu-kānta-svarūpeyaṁ sarvadā vārṣabhānavī \|*  
*dhṛta-ṣoḍaśa-śṛṅgārā dvādaśābharaṇānvitā \|\|246\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā, the very form of the highest beauty, has sixteen accoutrements and twelve ornaments. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 4.7)**

*tatra suṣṭhu-kānta-svarūpā, yathā śrī-kṛṣṇa-vākyam—*

**Перевод:**

**She is the form of the highest beauty, as stated in the words of Kṛṣṇa:**

*kacās tava sukuñcitā mukham adhīra-dīrghekṣaṇaṁ*  
*kaṭhora-kuca-bhāg-uraḥ kraśima-śāli madhya-sthalam \|*  
*nate śirasi dorlate karaja-ratna-ramyau karau*  
*vidhūnayati rādhike tri-jagad eṣa rupotsavaḥ \|\|247\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Your hair is nicely curled. Your face has unsteady, long eyes. Your chest holds two firm breasts. Your waist is thin. Your shoulders are low. Your hands have pleasing, jewel-like fingernails. O Rādhā! This festival of beauty makes the three worlds tremble. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 4.8)**

*dhṛta-ṣoḍaśa-śṛṅgārā yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The sixteen accoutrements are described:**

*snātā nāsāgra-jāgran-maṇi-rasita-paṭā sūtriṇī baddha-veṇī*  
*sottaṁsā carcitāṅgī kusumita-cikura sragviṇī padma-hastā \|*  
*tāmbūlāsyoru-bindu-stavakita-cibukā kajjalākṣī sucitrā*  
*rādhālaktojjvalāṅghriḥ sphuriti tilakinī ṣoḍaśā-kalpinīyam \|\|248\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā, shines with sixteen accoutrements: freshly bathed body, a pearl shining at the tip of her nose, a colored cloth, a sash around her waist, tied up braid, ear ornaments, anointed limbs, flowers in her hair, garland around her neck, lotus in her hand, betel nut in her mouth, musk dot on her chin, mascara on her eyes, patterns on her cheeks, lac on her feet, and tilaka on her forehead. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 4.9)**

*dvādaśābharaṇāśritā yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**Her twelve ornaments:**

*divyaś cūḍāmaṇīndraḥ puraṭa-viracitāḥ kuṇḍala-dvandva-kāñcī*  
*niṣkāś cakrī-śalākā-yuga-valaya-ghaṭāḥ kaṇṭha-bhūṣormikāś ca \|*  
*hārās tārānukārā bhuja-kaṭaka-tulākoṭayo ratna-kḷptās*  
*tuṅgā pādāṅgurīya-cchavir iti ravibhir bhūṣaṇair bhāti rādhā \|\|249\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā shines like the sun with twelve ornaments: her shining crown jewel, her two gold earrings, her gold belt, her medallion, her two cakrī-śalākas, her two bracelets, her throat ornament, her rings, her necklace, her arm bands glowing like a constellation, her anklets made of jewels, and her dazzling toe rings. (Ujjvalanīlamaṇi 4.10)**

*madhye vayasi kaiśora eva tasyāḥ sarvadā sthitiḥ \| pūrvavad divasa*  
*gaṇanayā viṁśati-dinottara-pañca-māsādhika-nava-varṣa-parimitaṁ*  
*madhya-kaiśoraṁ vayaḥ; rāja-kumārītvād bhogātiśayena samṛddhyā*  
*varṣa-māsa-dinānāṁ sārdhatayā pañca-daśa-dinottara-māsa*  
*dvayādhika-caturdaśa-varṣa-parimitaṁ vayo ’syā jñeyam \|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā is in the middle of kaiśora period eternally. Like previously, by calculation of the days, her middle kaiśora age is of nine years, five months and twenty days. By enjoying as a young daughter of a king, with intense enjoyment her age becomes increased (by an extra half of that age) to become fourteen years, two months and fifteen days.**

*asyā madīyatā-bhāvo madhu-snehas tathaiva ca \|*  
*mañjiṣṭhākhyo bhaved rāgaḥ samarthā kevalā ratiḥ \|\|250\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She thinks of Kṛṣṇa as hers (madīyatā-bhāva) and thus her sneha is madhu-sneha. Her rāga is called mañjiṣṭha and her rati is samarthā alone.**

*kandarpa-kautukaṁ kuñjaṁ gṛham asyās tu yāvaṭe \|*  
*mātāsyāḥ kīrtidā proktā vṛṣabhānuḥ pitā smṛtaḥ \|\|251\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her bower is called Kandarpa-kautuka (the festival of Cupid). Her house is in Yāvaṭa. Her mother is Kīrtidā and her father is Vṛṣabhānu.**

*abhimanyuḥ patis tasyā durmukho devaraḥ smṛtaḥ \|*  
*jaṭilākhyā smṛtā śvaśrūr nanandā kuṭilā matā \|\|252\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her husband is Abhimanyu. Her brother-in-law is Durmukha. Her mother-in-law is Jaṭilā and her sister-in-law is Kuṭilā.**

*yathā syur nāyakāvasthā nikhilā eva mādhave \|*  
*tathaiva nāyikāvasthā rādhāyāṁ prāyaśo matāḥ \|\|253\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as all types of nāyakas (heros such as dhīra-lalita, etc.) exist in Kṛṣṇa, so do all types of nāyikās (heroines such as khaṇḍitā,etc.) exist in Rādhā.**

*tasyā vṛndāvaneśvaryāḥ sakhyaḥ pañca-vidhā matāḥ \|*  
*sakhyaś ca nitya-sakhyaś ca prāṇa-sakhyaś ca kāścana \|*  
*priya-sakhyaś ca parama-preṣṭha-sakhyaś ca viśrutāḥ \|\|254\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs of Rādhā are of five types: sakhī, nitya-sakhī, prāṇasakhī, priya-sakhī and paramapreṣṭha-sakhī. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 4.50)**

*sakhyaḥ kusumikā-vindhyā-dhaniṣṭhādyāḥ prakīrtitāḥ \|*  
*nitya-sakhyaś ca kastūrī-maṇi-mañjarikādayaḥ \|\|255\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs are women like Kusumikā, Vindhyā, and Dhaniṣṭhā. The nitya-sakhīs include Kastūrī and Maṇi-mañjarī. (Ujjvalanīlamaṇi 4.51)**

*prāṇa-sakhyaḥ śaśimukhī-vāsantī-lāsikādayaḥ \|*  
*gatā vṛndāvaneśvaryāḥ prāyeṇemāḥ sarūpatām \|\|256\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The prāṇa-sakhīs include Śaśimukhī, Vāsantī, and Lāsikā. All these three types generally have the same qualities as Rādhā. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 4.52)**

*priya-sakhyaḥ kuraṅgākṣī sumadhyā madanālasā \|*  
*kamalā mādhurī mañju-keśī kandarpa-sundarī \|*  
*mādhavī mālatī kāma-latā śaśikalādayaḥ \|\|257\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The priya-sakhīs include Kuraṅgākṣī, Sumadhyā, Madanālasā, Kamalā, Mādhurī, Mañjukeśī, Kandarpa-sundarī, Mādhavī, Mālatī, Kāmalatā and Śaśikalā. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 4.53)**

*parama-preṣṭha-sakhyas tu lalitā sa-viśākhikā \|*  
*sa-citrā campakalatā tuṅgavidyendulekhikā \|*  
*raṅgadevī sudevī cety aṣṭau sarva-gaṇāgrimāḥ \|\|258\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The eight parama-preṣṭha-sakhīs Lalitā, Viśākhā, Citrā, Campakalatā, Tuṅgavidyā, Indulekhā, Raṅgadevī and Sudevī are the best of all in the group. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 4.54)**

*yūthādhipātve ’py aucityaṁ dadhānā lalitādayaḥ \|*  
*sveṣṭa-rādhādi-bhāvasya lobhāt sakhya-ruciṁ dadhuḥ \|\|259\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though Lalitā, Viśākhā, Padmā and Śaibyā13 are capable of being leaders, still because of their affection for serving other leaders like Rādhā, they have a taste as friends of leaders. (Ujjvalanīlamaṇi 3.61)**

*madīyatā-bhāva-lakṣaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The characteristics of madīyatā-bhāva are as follows:**

*śṛṅgāra-rasa-sarvasvaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ priyatamo mama \|*  
*iti yaḥ prauḍha-nirbandho bhāvaḥ sa syān madīyatā \|\|260\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The constant, deep emotion where woman lover thinks, “Kṛṣṇa, the culmination of rasa, is mine” is called madīyatā.**

*udāharaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**An example:**

*śikhi-piñcha-lasan-mukhāmbujo muralīvān mama jīvaneśvaraḥ \|*  
*kva gato ’tra vihāya mām ito vada nārāyaṇa sarva-vittama \|\|261\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“O Nārāyaṇa, best of knowers! Please say where the lord of my life, holding a flute, whose lotus face shines with a peacock feather, has gone, giving me up?”**

*bhuja-catuṣṭayaṁ kvāpi narmaṇā darśayann api \|*  
*vṛndāvaneśvarī-premṇā dvi-bhujaḥ kriyate hariḥ \|\|262\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though he sometimes shows four arms for joking, Kṛṣṇa becomes two armed by the prema of Rādhā. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 5.6)**

*yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**An example:**

*rāsārambha-vidhau nilīya vasatā kuñje mṛgākṣī-gaṇair*  
*dṛṣṭaṁ gopayituṁ svam uddhura-dhiyā yā suṣṭhu saṁdarśitā \|*  
*rādhāyāḥ praṇayasya hanta mahimā yasya śriyā rakṣituṁ*  
*sā śakyā prabhaviṣṇunāpi hariṇā nāsīc catur-bāhutā \|\|263\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At the beginning of the rāsa dance Kṛṣṇa hid himself in a bower. By inspiration he decided to show his form with four arms in order to hide himself from the gopīs. The greatness of Rādhā was such that by the effulgence of her glory he was unable to maintain his form with four arms. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 5.7)**

*madhu-sneha-lakṣaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**Characteristics of madhu-sneha:**

*madīyatātiśaya-bhāk priye sneho bhaven madhu \|*  
*svayaṁ prakaṭa-mādhuryo nānā-rasa-samāhṛtiḥ \|\|264\|\|*  
*mattatoṣma-dharaḥ sneho madhu-sāmyān madhūcyate \|\|265\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sneha is called madhu when the lover thinks “You are mine.” This sneha is called madhu because it is similar to honey in that it is naturally sweet, it possesses various bhāvas, it intoxicates with bliss and it causes heating. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.93-94)**

*udāharaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**An example:**

*rādhā sneha-mayena hanta racitā mādhurya-sāreṇa sā*  
*saudhīva pratimā ghanāpy uru-guṇair bhāvoṣmaṇā vidrutā \|*  
*yan-nāmany api dhāmani śravaṇayor yāti prasaṅgena me*  
*sāndrānanda-mayī bhavaty anupamā sadyo jagad-vismṛti \|\|266\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though Rādhā is like a statue of nectar fashioned from sneha, the essence of sweetness, and is thick with many qualities, burning with longing with thoughts of Kṛṣṇa, she remains completely liquid. When her name enters my ears by accident, I become immediately filled with incomparable concentrated bliss and I forget the existence of the entire world. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.95)**

*māñjiṣṭha-rāga-lakṣaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The characteristic of māñjiṣṭha-rāga:**

*ahāryo ’nanya-sāpekṣo yaḥ kāntyā vardhate sadā \|*  
*bhaven māñjiṣṭha-rāgo ’sau rādhā-mādhavayor yathā \|\|267\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rāga which never vanishes, which does not depend on other elements, and increases in beauty constantly is called māñjiṣṭharāga. It is seen in Rādhā andMādhava. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.139)**

*udāharaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**An example:**

*dhatte drāg anupādhi janma vidhinā kenāpi nākampate*  
*sūte ’ty āhita-sañcayair api rasaṁ te cen mitho vartmane \|*  
*ṛddhiṁ sañcinute camatkṛti-karoddāma-pramodottarāṁ*  
*rādhā-mādhavayor ayaṁ nirupamaḥ premānubandhotsavaḥ \|\|268\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The incomparable festival arising from the prema of Rādhā and Mādhava appears quickly without cause, is not at all moved by any means, produces great taste if met by heaps of opposing elements, and increases with the greatest, unlimited, astonishing bliss. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.140)**

*samarthā-rater lakṣaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The characteristic of samartha-rati:**

*kañcid viśeṣam āyāntyā sambhogecchā yayābhitaḥ \|*  
*ratyā tādātmyam āpannā sā samartheti bhaṇyate \|\|269\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That rati, attaining a special nature, in which the desire for enjoyment becomes one with it completely, is called samartharati. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.52)**

*sva-svarūpāt tadīyād vā jātā yat kiñcid anvayāt \|*  
*samarthā sarva-vismāri-gandhā sāndratamā matā \|\|270\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Arising from lalanā-niṣṭha-svarūpa (fixed in identity as a gopī) or a slight connection with anything related to Kṛṣṇa, samartharati is most condensed, and evena trace of it makes one forget everything else. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.53)**

*udāharaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**An example:**

*prekṣyāśeṣe jagati madhurāṁ svāṁ vadhūṁ śaṅkayā te*  
*tasyāḥ pārśve gurubhir abhitas tvat-prasaṅgo nyavāri \|*  
*śrutvā dūre tad api bhavataḥ sā tulā-koṭi-nādaṁ*  
*hā kṛṣṇety aśruta-caram api vyāharanty unmadāsīt \|\|271\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Considering their daughter-in-law, the most beautiful in the unlimited universe, her elders prevented her from associating with you by all methods. But though she had never heard of you, hearing in the distance the sound of your ankle bells, she exclaimed “O Kṛṣṇa” and became insane with passion. (Ujjvalanīlamaṇi 14.54)**

*sarvādbhuta-vilāsormi-camatkāra-kara-śriyaḥ \|*  
*sambhogecchā-viśeṣo ’syā rater jātu na bhidyate \|*  
*ity asyāṁ kṛṣṇa-saukhyārtham eva kevalam udyamaḥ \|\|272\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In this rati which astonishes Lakṣmī by its waves of pleasure which are most astonishing, the desire for one’s own pleasure does not manifest separately. For this reason, there is only the desire for Kṛṣṇa’s happiness in samartha-rati. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi14.55)**

*iyam eva ratiḥ prauḍhā mahābhāva-daśāṁ vrajet \|*  
*yā mṛgyā syād vimuktānāṁ bhaktānāṁ ca varīyasām \|\|273\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Samartha-rati, when it becomes deep, becomes the state of mahābhāva, which is sought by liberated souls and the best of devotees. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.56)**

*yathā śrī-daśame—*

**Перевод:**

**As stated in the Tenth Canto:**

*etāḥ paraṁ tanu-bhṛto bhuvi gopa-vadhvo*  
*govinda eva nikhilātmani rūḍha-bhāvāḥ \|*  
*vāñchanti yad bhava-bhiyo munayo vayaṁ ca*  
*kiṁ brahma-janmabhir ananta-kathārasasya \|\| iti \|\|274\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Among all persons on earth, these cowherd women alone have actually perfected their embodied lives, for they have achieved the perfection of unalloyed love for Lord Govinda. Their pure love is hankered after by those who fear material existence, by great sages, and by ourselves as well. For one who has tasted the narrations of the infinite Lord, what is the use of taking birth as a high-class brāhmaṇa, or even as Lord Brahmā himself? (SB** [**10.47.58**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/47.58)**)**

*śrī-rādhā-mantroddhāro yathā gaurī-tantre—*

**Перевод:**

**The derivation of the mantra of Rādhā is described in Gaurītantra:**

*śrī-nāda-bindu-saṁyuktā tathāgnir mukha-vṛtta-yuk \|*  
*caturthī vahni-jāyāntā rādhikāṣṭākṣaro manuḥ \|\|275\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī with m? , followed by the fire bīja with long ā (rām? ), followed by rādhā in dative case followed by svāhā constitutes the eightsyllable Rādhā mantra.**

*mantro yathā—*  
*śrīm? rām? rādhikāyai svāhā \|*

**Перевод:**

**The mantra is thus śrīm? rām? rādhikāyai svāhā.**

*asyā dhyānaṁ yathā tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**Her meditation is as follows:**

*smerāṁ śrī-kuṅkumābhāṁ sphurad-aruṇa-paṭa-prānta-kḷptāvaguṇṭhāṁ*  
*ramyāṁ veśena veṇī-kṛta-cikura-śikhālambi-padmāṁ kiśorīm \|*  
*tarjjany-aṅguṣṭha-yuktyā hari-mukha-kamale yuñjatīṁ nāgavallī*  
*parṇaṁ karṇāyatākṣīṁ tri-jagati madhurāṁ rādhikām arcayāmi \|\|276\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I worship beautiful, smiling Rādhā, of kaiśora age, who has a complexion like kuṅkuma, who covers her face with the edge of her red dress, whose braid ends with a swinging lotus, whose eyes reach to her ears, who offers betel leaves to the mouth of Kṛṣṇa with her forefinger and thumb, who is the sweetest in the three worlds.**

*yathā vānyatra—*

**Перевод:**

**An alternative:**

*tapta-hema-prabhāṁ nīla-kuntala-baddha-mallikām \|*  
*śarac-candra-mukhīṁ nṛtya-cakorī-cañcalekṣaṇām \|\|277\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I take shelter of Rādhā, who has a complexion like molten gold, who has jasmine flowers tied in her black hair, whose face is like the autumn moon, whose glance is fickle like a dancing cakorabird.**

*bimbādhara-smita-jyotsnāṁ jagaj-jīvana-dāyikām \|*  
*cāru-ratna-stanālambi-muktādāma-vibhūṣaṇām \|\|278\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her bimba-red lips smile like moonlight. She is merciful to everyone in the universe. She is decorated with a string of pearls hanging over her beautifully jeweled breasts.**

*nitamba-nīla-vasanāṁ kiṅkiṇī-jāla-maṇḍitām \|*  
*nānā-ratnādi-nirmāṇa-ratna-nūpura-dhāriṇīm \|\|279\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She wears blue cloth around her waist with a network of bells. She wears anklets made of various gems.**

*sarva-lāvaṇya-mugdhāṅgīṁ sarvāvayava-sundarīm \|*  
*kṛṣṇa-pārśva-sthitāṁ nityaṁ kṛṣṇa-premaika-vigrahām \|*  
*ānanda-rasa-sammagnāṁ kiśorīm āśraye vane \|\|280\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She bewilders all beauty, and is beautiful in all her limbs. She is situated by Kṛṣṇa’s side constantly, and is the very form of Kṛṣṇa prema. She is absorbed in the rasa of bliss. I take shelter of kaiśora-aged Rādhā in the forest.**

*saurīṁ raktāmbarāṁ ramyāṁ sunetrāṁ susmitānanām \|*  
*śyāmāṁ śyāmākhilābhīṣṭāṁ rādhikām āśraye vane \|\|281\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the forest, I take shelter of the worshipper of the sun god, Rādhā, beautiful, wearing red cloth, endowed with charming eyes and smile, who has matured to kaiśora age, and is the goal of all other mature women.**

*vinā rādhā-prasādena kṛṣṇa-prāptir na jāyate \|*  
*tataḥ śrī-rādhikā-kṛṣṇau smaraṇīyau susaṁyutau \|\|282\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Without the mercy of Rādhā one cannot attain Kṛṣṇa. Thus one should meditate on Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa together.**

*yathā bhaviṣyottare—*

**Перевод:**

**Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa says:**

*prema-bhaktau yadi śraddhā mat-prasādaṁ yadīcchasi \|*  
*tadā nārada bhāvena rādhāyārādhako bhava \|\|283\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If you have faith in prema-bhakti, if you desire my mercy, then O Nārada, please become the worshipper of Rādhā with affection.**

*tathā ca nāradīye—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Nārada Purāṇa it is said:**

*satyaṁ satyaṁ punaḥ satyaṁ satyam eva punaḥ punaḥ \|*  
*vinā rādhā-prasādena mat-prasādo na vidyate \|\|284\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This is the truth, the truth, and again the truth. It is the truth again and again. Without the mercy of Rādhā, one cannot gain my mercy.**

*śrī-rādhikāyāḥ kāruṇyāt tat-sakhī-saṅgitām iyāt \|*  
*tat-sakhīnāṁ ca kṛpayā yoṣid-aṅgam avāpnuyāt \|\|285\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Due to the mercy of Rādhā, one becomes an associate of her sakhīs. By the mercy of her sakhīs, one attains the body of a gopī.**

*atha smaraṇa-kramaḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**The sequence of remembering:**

*vṛndāvane ’smad-adṛśye’tra vikrīḍā-sthale hariḥ \|*  
*vihāraṁ kurute nityam adṛśyaṁ carma-cakṣuṣā \|\|286\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord eternally plays invisible to material eyes, in Vṛndāvana in his pastime places, which we cannot see.**

*yathā’smin siddha-dehena rāga-mārgeṇa vaiṣṇavaiḥ \|*  
*śrīmad-vṛndāvanādhīśau sevanīyau tathocyate \|\|287\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The devotees say that the couple should be served on the path of rāga in a siddha-deha.**

*nānā-ratna-maye citra-maṇḍape ca gṛhācite \|*  
*śrīmad-gaura-bhakta-vṛnde svīya-svīya-gaṇānvite \|*  
*rūpa-svarūpa-pramukhe svagaṇa-sthān gurūn smaret \|\|288\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate upon the gurus situated amidst their followers, among those headed by Rūpa and Svarūpa, with their individual associates, a host of Caitanya’s devotees, in colorful pavilions, in many rooms, made of various gems.**

*tatrādau śrī-guru-smaraṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate on guru as follows:**

*śaśāṅkāyuta-saṅkāśaṁ varābhaya-lasat-karaṁ \|*  
*śuklāmbara-dharaṁ divyaṁ śukla-mālyānulepanam \|\|289\|\|*  
*prasanna-vadanaṁ śāntaṁ bhajanānanda-nirvṛtam \|*  
*divya-rūpa-dharaṁ dhyāyed varadaṁ kamalekṣaṇam \|\|290\|\|*  
*sundaraṁ dvibhujam gauraṁ kaiśora-vayasojjvalam*  
*rūpa-pūrva-guru-gaṇānugataṁ bhajanotsukam \|*  
*evaṁ rūpaṁ guruṁ dhyāyen manasā sādhakaḥ śuciḥ \|\|291\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate on guru radiant as ten thousand moons, whose hands shine with the mudras of benediction and fearlessness, who wears white cloth, is attractive with white garland and is anointed with unguents, whose face is smiling, who is peaceful, blissful with service, possessing an attractive form and lotus eyes, who gives benedictions. He is beautiful with two arms, golden in complexion, shining with kaiśora age. Using his mind, the purified devotee should meditate on the form of guru who is enthusiastic for service and obedient to previous gurus like Rūpa.**

*atraiva guru-gāyatrī-smaraṇaṁ; tatra kārikā—*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate on Guru-gāyatrī. An explanatory verse is as follows:**

*smaraṇaṁ guru-devasya gāyatrī-samudīraṇam \|*  
*tataḥ śrī-gauracandrasya gāyatry uccāraṇaṁ tathā \|\|293\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate on guru while pronouncing the Gurugāyatrī. Then one should utter the Gauracandra-gāyatrī.**

*tatrādau guru-gāyatrī yathā—*  
*śrīṁ gurudevāya vidmahe rādhā-priyāyai dhīmahi*  
*tan no guruḥ pracodayāt iti \|*  
*guru-gāyatrī-smaraṇāntaraṁ guru-vargaṁ smaret \|*

**Перевод:**

**The Guru-gāyatrī is śrīm? gurudevāya vidmahe, rādhā-priyāyai dhīmahi, tan no guruḥ pracodayāt.**  
**After meditating on the Guru-gāyatrī one should meditate on the series of gurus.**

*tataḥ śrī-gauracandra-smaraṇam, yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate on Gauracandra as follows:**

*śrī-caitanyaṁ śacī-putraṁ prema-bhakti-prakāśakam \|*  
*vande ’haṁ sarva-lokeśaṁ svabhakta-gaṇa-sevitam \|\|294\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Caitanya, son of Śacī, revealer of prema-bhakti,the Lord of all the worlds, who is served by all his devotees.**

*tathā ca—*  
*śrīman-mauktika-dāma-baddha-cikuraṁ susmera-candrānanaṁ*  
*śrīkhaṇḍāguru-cāru-citra-vasanaṁ srag-divya-bhūṣāñcitam \|*  
*nṛtyāveśa-rasānumoda-madhuraṁ kandarpa-veśojjvalaṁ*  
*caitanyaṁ kanaka-dyutiṁ nija-janaiḥ saṁsevyamānaṁ bhaje \|\|295\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I worship Caitanya, of golden complexion, worshipped by his devotees, whose hair is bound with strands of beautiful pearls, whose moon-like face is smiling, who wears attractive, colorful cloth as well as sandalwood and aguru, who wears attractive ornaments and garlands, who experiences sweet pleasure from the taste arising from absorption in dancing, and who is more brilliant than Cupid.**

*atraiva śrī-gaura-gāyatrī-smaraṇam; gāyatrī ca yathā—*  
*klīm? caitanyāya vidmahe viśvambharāya dhīmahi*  
*tan no gauraḥ pracodayāt iti \|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate on the Gaura-gāyatrī. The Gāyatrī is as follows:**

*klīm? caitanyāya vidmahe viśvambharāya dhīmahi*  
*tan no gauraḥ pracodayāt*  
*sādhako’sminn eva bhārata-varṣe prakaṭita-kṛṣṇa-vividha-vihāre vraje*  
*tasminn api śrī-vṛndāvane tatrāpi kālindī-taṭastha-tintiḍī-vṛkṣa-mūle*  
*tatrāpi nānā-vṛkṣopaśobhita-ratna-maṇḍape śrī-caitanya-deva-bhakta*  
*gaṇa-madhye tasminn eva śrī-guru-paramaguru- parātparaguru*  
*parameṣṭiguru-gosvāmibhiḥ saha sāmarthye sati nija-dehena, tad-abhāve*  
*‘hṛdāpi ca’ ityādi-vākyānusāreṇa tatra sādhaka-dehena nivāsaṁ kṛtvā*  
*sūryodayāt pūrvaṁ śayanād utthāya saṁskṛta-śarīrāṁśukaḥ san śrī*  
*guru-paramaguru-parātparaguru-parameṣṭiguru-gosvāmi-caraṇān*  
*krameṇa daṇḍavat-praṇamya tataḥ śrī-caitanya-devasya śrī-rūpa*  
*svarūpa-pramukhān bhakta-mukhyān praṇamya tataḥ śrī-caitanya*  
*devaṁ praṇamya guru-paramaguru-parātparaguru-parameṣṭhiguru*  
*gosvāmi-krameṇa tat-tad-ājñayā śrī-caitanya-devasya śayanotthānādi*  
*pūrvaka-punaḥ-svāpa-paryantaṁ nānopakaraṇair vividha-paricaryāṁ*  
*vidadhyāt \|*

**Перевод:**

**A sadhāka, in this very Bhārata-varṣa, in Vraja where Kṛṣṇa performed various pastimes, and that too in Vṛndāvana, and even there, under a tamarind tree on the bank of the Yamunā, and that too in a jeweled pavilion decorated with various trees, among the devotees of Caitanya, along with his guru, and three previous generations of gurus, if he is able, in the present body and if he is not able, then in his heart14, residing there in his sādhakabody, previous to the sunrise, should rise from bed, dress himself after cleansing the body, and offer respects to guru and three generations of guru, one after the other. Then he should offer respects to Caitanya-deva, Rūpa, Svarūpa, and other devotees. Offering respects to Caitanya-deva, by the order of his guru and the three generations of gurus, he should perform various services for Caitanya-deva, from waking him up to putting him to bed using various items.**

*atra kārikā—*

**Перевод:**

**An explanatory verse:**

*gaurasya śayanotthānāt punas tac-chayanāvadhi \|*  
*nānopakaraṇaiḥ kuryāt sevanaṁ tatra sādhakaḥ \|\|296\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka should serve using various items from the time that Gaurāṅga rises from bed till he sleeps.**

*evaṁ śrī-caitanya-devaṁ niṣevya tataḥ*  
*siddha-dehena śrī-kṛṣṇa-sevāṁ vidadhyāt \|*

**Перевод:**

**In this way serving Caitanya-deva, the sādhaka should serve Kṛṣṇa in one’s siddha-deha.**

*atra kārikā—*

**Перевод:**

**An explanatory verse:**

*tac-cintanādi-samaye kuryāt tad-anusārataḥ \|*  
*cintanaṁ tu tayos tatra vasan guru-gaṇānvitaḥ \|\|297\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At the time of meditation, in the company of his gurus, following them, living in Vṛndāvana, he should think of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.**

*punaś cākṣuṣa-līle ’smin siddha-dehena sādhakaḥ \|*  
*manasā mānasīṁ sevām aṣṭa-kālocitāṁ vrajet \|\|298\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka in his siddha-deha should perform mental service by his mind according to the eight parts of the day while in his present body.**

*sādhakaḥ siddha-dehena kuryāt kṛṣṇa-priyā-gṛhe \|*  
*guru-rūpa-priyā-pārśve lalitādi-priyā-gaṇe \|\|299\|\|*  
*nivāsaṁ yāvaṭe nityaṁ śrī-rūpā-mañjarī-yutaḥ \|*  
*śrī-yāvaṭa-pure śrīmad-vṛṣabhānu-pure ’pi ca \|\|300\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka in his siddha-deha should always live in the house of a gopī, near a gopī acting as his guru, with Lalitā and other sakhīs, in the company of Rūpa-mañjarī, in Yāvaṭa, and even in Vṛṣabhānu’s town (Varsāna).**

*nandīśvara-pure rādhā-kṛṣṇa-kuṇḍa-taṭa-dvaye \|*  
*śrīmad-vṛndāvane ramye śrīmad-vṛndāvaneśayoḥ \|\|301\|\|*  
*prātar-ādy-aṣṭa-samaye sevanaṁ tu krameṇa ca \|*  
*nānopakaraṇair divyair bhakṣya-bhojyādibhiḥ sadā \|*  
*cāmara-vyajanādyaiś ca pāda-saṁvāhanādibhiḥ \|\|302\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the banks of Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Śyāma-kuṇḍa within pleasant Vṛndāvana, during the eight parts of the day, one should constantly serve Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa with various charming ingredients, with food and other enjoyable items, with cāmaras, fans and foot massage.**

*siddhāṅga-bhāvanā-kramo yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The sequence of meditating on one’s siddha limbs:**

*kiśorī gopa-vanitā sarvālaṅkāra-bhūṣitā \|*  
*pṛthu-tuṅga-kuca-dvandvā catuḥṣaṣṭi-guṇānvitā \|\|303\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The form of the sādhaka is a gopī of kaiśora age, decorated with all ornaments, having full breasts and endowed with sixty-four kalās.**

*nigūḍha-bhāvā govinde madanānanda-mohinī \|*  
*nānā-rasa-kalālāpa-śālinī divya-rūpinī \|\|304\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She has deep love that bewilders the bliss of Cupid. She is endowed with speech which reveal arts of various rasas and she possesses an attractive form.**

*saṅgīta-rasa-sañjāta-bhāvollāsa-bharānvitā \|*  
*divā-niśaṁ mano-madhye dvayoḥ prema-bharākulā \|\|305\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She takes great joy in the moods arising from relishing music. Day and night, she is overwhelmed with great prema for Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in her mind.**

*sarva-lakṣana-sampūrṇā bhāva-hāvādi-bhūṣitā \|*  
*guru-prasāda-jananī guru-rūpā-priyānugā \|*  
*gāndharvikā-sva-yūtha-sthā lalitādi-gaṇānvitā \|\|306\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is endowed with all characteristics, ornamented with bhāvaand hāva, is born from guru’s mercy, is a follower of the sakhīacting as her guru, is situated in Rādhā’s group with Lalitā and others.**

*rūpa-mañjary-anugatā yāvaṭa-grāma-vāsinī \|*  
*cintanīyākṛtiḥ sā ca kāma-rūpānugāminī \|\|307\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is obedient to Rūpa manjarī, the group leader, lives in Yāvaṭa village, follows a kāma-rūpa-bhakta, and has a form worthy of meditation.**

*cid-ānanda-rasamayī druta-hema-sama-prabhā \|*  
*sucīna-nīla-vasanā nānālaṅkāra-bhūṣitā \|\|308\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is composed of the rasa of knowledge and bliss. Her complexion is that of molten gold. She wears fine blue cloth and is decorated with various ornaments.**

*śrī-rādhā-kṛṣṇayoḥ pārśva-vartinī nava-yauvanā \|*  
*guru-dattasya nāmno ’syā mātā vargādy amañjarī \|*  
*pitā varga-tṛtīyākhyo vargāntāhvayakaḥ patiḥ \|\|309\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She remains at the side of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. She has entered navayauvana age. Her mother’s name starts with a syllable of the same varga as the mañjarī name given by guru, though the mother is not a mañjarī. The father’s name starts with the syllable of the same varga of the third syllable of that name. The husband’s name starts with the syllable of the same varga of the last syllable of that name.**

*nivāso yāvaṭe tasyā dakṣiṇā mṛdvikā hi sā \|*  
*śrī-rādhā-vastra-bhūṣāḍhyā nānālaṅkāra-bhūṣitā \|\|310\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She lives in Yāvaṭa. She is dakṣiṇā and mṛdvī in quality. She wears the nirmālya clothing of Rādhā and is decorated with various ornaments.**

*asyaiva siddha-dehasya sādhanāni yathā-kramam \|*  
*ekādaśa-prasiddhāni lakṣyante ’timanoharam \|\|311\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The eleven famous practices for the siddha-deha are characterized attractively in sequence.**

*nāma rūpaṁ vayo veśaḥ sambandho yūtha eva ca \|*  
*ājñā sevā parākāṣṭhā pālya-dāsī nivāsakaḥ \|\|312\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They are name, form, age, clothing, relationship, group, order, service, ambition, pālya-dāsī and residence.**

*eteṣāṁ viśeṣa-lakṣaṇāny ucyante—*

**Перевод:**

**Their special characteristics are described:**

*(1) tatrādau nāma-lakṣāṇām—*

**Перевод:**

**First the characteristic of the name:**

*śrī-rūpa-mañjarītyādi-nāmākhyānānurūpataḥ \|*  
*cintanīyaṁ yathā-yogyaṁ sva-nāma vraja-subhruvām \|\|313\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate suitably upon one’s name among the Vraja gopīs, following after the name of Rūpa-mañjarī and others.**

*(2) atha rūpam—*

**Перевод:**

**The form:**

*rūpaṁ yūtheśvarī-rūpaṁ bhāvanīyaṁ prayatnataḥ \|*  
*trailokya-mohanaṁ kāmoddīpanaṁ gopikā-pateḥ \|\|314\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate carefully on one’s form, which is similar to that of the group’s leader (Rādhā). It is most attractive in the three worlds and excites the love of Kṛṣṇa.**

*(3) atha vayaḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**The age:**

*vayo nānā-vidhaṁ tatra yat tu tridaśa-vatsaram \|*  
*mādhuryādbhuta-kaiśoraṁ vikhyātaṁ vraja-subhruvām \|\|315\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are various ages, but thirteen years is famous as the sweet, astonishing part of kaiśora for the girls of Vraja.**

*(4) atha veśaḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**The clothing:**

*veśo nīla-paṭādyaiś ca vicitrālaṅkṛtais tathā \|*  
*svasya dehānurūpeṇa svabhāva-rasa-sundaraḥ \|\|316\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her clothing is beautiful with natural charm, suitable to her body, using blue cloth and colorful ornaments.**

*(5) atha sambandhaḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**The relationship:**

*sevya-sevaka-sambandhaḥ sva-manovṛtti-bhedataḥ \|*  
*prāṇātyaye ’pi sambandhaṁ na kadā parivartayet \|\|317\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The relationship between the objects of service (Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa) and the servant is individualized according to one’s mental tendencies. One should not give up that relationship even if one’s life is at stake.**

*(6) atha yūthaḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**The group:**

*yatha yūtheśvarī-yūthaḥ sadā tiṣṭhati tad-vaśe \|*  
*tathaiva sarvathā tiṣṭhed bhūtvā tad-vaśa-vartinī \|\|318\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the group always remains obedient to the leader, so the mañjarī should remain obedient to the leader.**

*(7) atha ājñā—*

**Перевод:**

**The order:**

*yūtheśvaryāḥ śirasy ājñām ādāya hari-rādhayoḥ \|*  
*yathoditāṁ tac-chuśrūṣāṁ kuryād ānanda-saṁyutā \|\|319\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Taking the order of the leader of the group on her head, she should with bliss perform services described.**

*(8) atha sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**The service:**

*cāmara-vyajanādīnām saṁyoga-pratipālanam \|*  
*iti sevā parijñeyā yathā-mati vibhāgaśaḥ \|\|320\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Service is understood as fully attending with cāmara, fan, etc., distinguished according to one’s liking.**

*(9) atha parākāṣṭhā—*

**Перевод:**

**The ultimate:**

*śrī-rādhā-kṛṣṇayor yadvad rūpa-mañjarikādayaḥ \|*  
*prāptā nitya-sakhītvaṁ ca tathā syām iti bhāvayet \|\|321\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should think that, just as Rūpa-mañjarī and others have attained identities as nitya-sakhīs of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, I also will. (parākāṣṭhā)**

*(10) atha pālya-dāsī—*

**Перевод:**

**Pālya-dāsī:**

*pālya-dāsī ca sā proktā paripālyā priyaṁvadā \|*  
*sva-mano-vṛtti-rūpeṇa yā nitya-paricārikā \|\|322\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is called a pālya-dāśī who is to be protected, speaks gently, and constantly serves according to her mental inclination.**

*(11) atha nivāsaḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**Residence:**

*nivāso vraja-madhye tu rādhā-kṛṣṇa-sthale mataḥ \|*  
*vaṁśī-vaṭaś ca śrī-nandīśvaraś cāpy atikautukaḥ \|\|323\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The place of residence is at the place of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in Vraja, Vaṁśī-vaṭa and Nandīśvara, full of joy.**

*atha mañjarī-gaṇa-niṣṭhā—*

**Перевод:**

**Description of the mañjarīs:**

*lavaṅga-mañjarī proktā vilāsa-mañjarī tathā \|*  
*aśoka-mañjarī ceti rasa-mañjarikā tathā \|\|324\|\|*  
*rasāla-mañjarī nāmnā tathā kamala-mañjarī \|*  
*karuṇā-mañjarī khyātā vikhyātā guṇa-mañjarī \|\|325\|\|*  
*evaṁ sarvāś ca vikhyātāḥ sva-sva-nāmākṣaraiḥ parāḥ \|*  
*mañjaryo bahuśaḥ rūpa-guṇa-śīla-vayo ’nvitāḥ \|\|326\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The following are excellent mañjarīs, famous by their individual names endowed with excellent form, qualities, characteristics and age: Lavaṅga-mañjarī, Vilāsa-mañjarī, Aśoka-mañjarī, Rasamañjarī, Rasāla-mañjarī,Kamala-mañjarī, Karuṇa-mañjarī, and Guṇa-mañjarī.**

*nāma-rūpādi tat sarvaṁ guru-dattaṁ ca bhāvayet \|*  
*tatra tatra sthitā nityaṁ bhajet śrī-rādhikā-harī \|\|327\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate on all of them with their names and forms as explained by guru and, situated among them, should constantly worship Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.**

*bhāvayan sādhako nityaṁ sthitvā kṛṣṇa-priyā-gṛhe \|*  
*tad-ājñā-pālako bhūtvā kāleṣv aṣṭasu sevate \|\|328\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meditating, the sādhaka, remaining always in the house of Kṛṣṇa’s beloved, following her order, should serve during the eight parts of the day.**

*sakhīnāṁ saṅginī-rūpām ātmānaṁ vasanā-mayīm \|*  
*ājñā-sevā-parākāṣṭhā-kṛpālaṅkāra-bhūṣitām \|*  
*tataś ca mañjarī-rūpān gurvādīn api saṁsmaret \|\|329\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka should meditate on the self in a mental form, associating with the sakhīs, ornamented with the order of Rādhā, her own service, her goal and mercy, and should also meditate on the succession of gurus possessing mañjarī forms.**

*tad yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**That is done as follows:**

*vakreśvara-paṇḍitaṁ ca vande śrī-tuṅgavidyakām \|*  
*śrī-caitanyaṁ śacī-putraṁ vande śrī-nanda-nandanam \|\|330\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Vakreśvara Paṇḍita, who is Tuṅgavidyā. I offer respects to Caitanya Mahāprabhu, son of Śacī, who is Kṛṣṇa.**

*atha vṛndāvanādhīśau padma-keśara-madhyagau \|*  
*koṭi-kandarpa-lāvaṇyau dhyāyet priya-sakhī-vṛtau \|\|331\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate upon the couple, the rulers of Vṛndāvana, situated in the middle of lotus stamens, beautiful as a million cupids, surrounded by dear sakhīs.**

*ukta-veśa-vayo-rūpa-saṁyutau sumanoharau \|*  
*saṁsmaret siddha-dehena sādhakaḥ sādhanair yutaḥ \|\|332\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka, employing his practices (of ekādaśa-bhāvas), using his siddha-deha, should meditate upon the attractive couple endowed with the clothing, age and form described.**

*atha yugala-mantroddhāro yathā sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām—*

**Перевод:**

**The derivation of the yugala-mantra is given in Sanat-kumārasaṁhitā:**

*gopījana-vallabheti caraṇān iti ca kramāt \|*  
*śaraṇaṁ ca prapadye ca tata etat pada-dvayam \|\|333\|\|*  
*pada-trayātmako mantraḥ ṣoḍaśārṇa udāhṛtaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**Gopījana-vallabha is combined with caraṇān and the two words śaraṇaṁ and prapadye to make a sixteen-syllable mantra of three words.**

*namo gopījanety uktvā vallabhābhyāṁ vadet tataḥ \|*  
*pada-dvayātmako mantro daśārṇaḥ khalu kathyate \|\|334\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should utter namo gopī-jana followed by vallabhābhyāṁ. This is said to be the two worded, ten-syllable mantra.**

*mantro yathā—gām?gopījana-vallabha-caraṇān śaraṇaṁ prapadye,*  
*namo gopījana-vallabhābhyām \|*

**Перевод:**

**The mantras are thus: gām? gopījana-vallabha-caraṇān śaraṇaṁ prapadye, namo gopījana-vallabhābhyām**

*asya dhyānaṁ yathā tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**The meditation on the mantra as described there is as follows:**

*atha dhyānaṁ pravakṣyāmi mantrasyāsya dvijottama \|*  
*pītāmbaraṁ ghana-śyāmaṁ dvi-bhujaṁ vana-mālinam \|\|335\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O brāhmaṇa! Now I will explain the meditation on this mantra. He is wearing yellow clothing, has a deep bluish complexion, has two arms and wears a forest garland.**

*barhi-barha-kṛtāpīḍaṁ śaśi-koṭi-nibhānanam \|*  
*ghūrṇāyamāna-nayanaṁ karṇikārāvataṁsinam \|\|336\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has a peacock feather in his crown. His face shines like a million moons. His eyes roll. He has karṇika flowers as ear decorations.**

*abhitaś candanenātha madhye kuṅkuma-bindunā \|*  
*vicitra-tilakaṁ bhāle bibhṛtaṁ maṇḍalākṛtim \|\|337\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On his forehead, he has colorful tilaka having circular form with a dot of kuṅkuma in the middle with sandalwood around.**

*taruṇāditya-saṅkāśa-kuṇḍalābhyāṁ virājitam \|*  
*gharmāmbu-kaṇikā-rājad-darpaṇābha-kapolakam \|\|338\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has earrings resembling the rising sun. His cheeks are like mirrors shining with drops of perspiration.**

*priyā-mukhe kṛtāpāṅga-līlayā connata-bhruvam \|*  
*agra-bhāga-lasan-muktā-sphurad-ucca-sunāsikam \|\|339\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He raises his brows playfully when he glances at his beloved. His fine nose shines with a radiant pearl at the tip of his nose.**

*daśana-jyotsnayā rājat-pakva-bimba-phalādharam \|*  
*keyūrāṅgada-sad-ratna-mudrikādi-lasat-karam \|\|340\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His lips are like ripe bimba fruits shining in the moonlight of his teeth. His hands shine with rings of precious gems, bracelets, and armbands.**

*bibhṛtaṁ muralīṁ vāme pāṇau padmaṁ tathottare \|*  
*kāñcī-dāma-sphuran-madhyaṁ nūpurābhyāṁ lasat-padam \|\|341\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He holds a flute in his left hand and a lotus in the other. He has a lustrous belt on his waist and glittering anklets on his feet.**

*rati-keli-rasāveśa-capalaṁ capalekṣaṇam \|*  
*hasantaṁ priyayā sārdhaṁ hāsayantaṁ ca tāṁ muhuḥ \|\|342\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His restless eyes flicker from absorption in the taste of amorous affairs. He laughs with his beloved and makes her laugh at all times.**

*itthaṁ kalpa-taror mūle ratna-siṁhāsanopari \|*  
*vṛndāraṇye smaret kṛṣṇaṁ saṁsthitaṁ priyayā saha \|\|343\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should in this way remember Kṛṣṇa with his beloved in Vṛndāvana, upon a jeweled throne at the base of a desire tree.**

*vāma-pārśva-sthitāṁ tasya rādhikāṁ ca smaret tataḥ \|*  
*sucīna-nīla-vasanāṁ druta-hema-sama-prabhām \|\|344\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should then remember Rādhikā situated at his left side. She wears fine blue cloth. Her complexion is like molten gold.**

*paṭāñcalenāvṛtāṅgāṁ sa-smitānana-paṅkajām \|*  
*kānta-vaktre nyasta-nṛtyac-cakorī-cañcalekṣaṇām \|\|345\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She has a smile on her lotus feet, covered by the edge of her cloth. Her restless eyes dance like cakora birds on her beautiful face.**

*aṅguṣṭha-tarjanībhyāṁ ca nija-priya-mukhāmbuje \|*  
*arpayantīṁ nāga-vallīṁ pūga-cūrṇa-samanvitām \|\|346\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She offers betel leaf with ground powder to the mouth of Kṛṣṇa with her thumb and forefinger.**

*muktāhāra-sphurac-cāru-pīnonnata-payodharām \|*  
*kṣīṇa-madhyāṁ pṛthu-śroṇiṁ kiṅkiṇī-jāla-maṇḍitām \|\|347\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her beautiful, heavy, raised breasts shine with a pearl necklace. She has a thin waist and broad hips decorated with a network of bells.**

*ratna-tāḍaṅka-mañjīra-ratna-pādāṅgulīyakām \|*  
*lāvaṇya-sāra-mugdhāṅgīṁ sarvāvayava-sundarīm \|\|348\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She has jeweled ear ornaments, anklets and toe rings. She is attractive, possessing the essence of beauty, in which each limb is beautiful.**

*ānanda-rasa-sammagnāṁ prasannāṁ nava-yauvanām \|*  
*sakhyaś ca tasyā viprendra tat-samāna-vayo-guṇāḥ \|*  
*tat-sevana-parā bhāvyāś cāmara-vyajanādibhiḥ \|\|349\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is absorbed in tasting bliss and is pleased. She is of navayauvana age. O brāhmaṇa! Her friends have similar age and qualities. They are engaged in service to her with cāmaras and fans.**

*atha ca—*  
*dīvyad-vṛndāraṇya-kalpa-drumādhaḥ-*  
*śrīmad-ratnāgāra-siṁhāsana-sthau \|*  
*śrīmad-rādhā-śrīla-govinda-devau*  
*preṣṭhālībhiḥ sevyamānau smarāmi \|\| iti \|\|350\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I remember Rādhā and Govinda, situated on a jeweled throne beneath a desire tree in attractive Vṛndāvana, served by the sakhīs.**

*padmaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The lotus is described:**

*prathamaṁ ṣaḍ-dalaṁ padmaṁ tad-bahir vasu-patrakam \|*  
*tad-bahir daśa-patraṁ ca daśopadala-saṁyutam \|\|351\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**First, there is a six-petalled lotus. Outside of that is an eightpetalled lotus. Outside that is a ten-petalled lotus, with ten secondary petals.**

*śrīmad-rādhā-kṛṣṇa-līlā-rasa-pūrita-vigraham \|*  
*tat-tad-icchā-vaśenaivonmīlitaṁ bhāti mudritam \|\|352\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This lotus is a form filled with the rasa of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes. By their will, it opens and closes.**

*prākāras tad-bahis tatra dikṣu dvāra-catuṣṭayam \|*  
*catuṣ-koṇāś ca ṣaḍ-dalyāṁ ṣaṭ-padyāṣṭādaśākṣarī \|\|353\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Outside this, there are walls, with four gates in the four directions, in rectangular form. On the six petals (in the center) are the syllables of the eighteen-syllable mantra, divided into six groups (for the six petals).**

*cintanīyam— prathanaṁ ṣaḍ-dala-padmam, tad-bahir aṣṭa-dala*  
*padmam, tatra daśa-dala-padmam; tatra daśopadalāni; evaṁ viṁśati*  
*dala-padmam \| tathā-bhūtasya padmasya caturdikṣu dvāra-catuṣṭayam;*  
*caturṣu dikṣu koṇa-catuṣṭayam; evam aṣṭa-daleṣu aṣṭau kuñjāni \| ṣaḍ*  
*daleṣv aṣṭādaśākṣaro gopāla-mantro vartate \|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate as follows: first there is a six-petal lotus. Outside that there is an eight-petal lotus, a ten-petal lotus, with ten secondary petals, thus a twenty petal lotus. There are four gates in the four directions for this lotus. In the four directions around the lotus is a square with four corners. On the eight petals are eight bowers. On the six petals are the syllables of the eighteen-syllable Gopāla mantra.**

*yathā brahma-saṁhitāyām—*

**Перевод:**

**The lotus is described in Brahma-saṁhitā (**[**5.2-4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/5.2)**):**

*sahasra-patraṁ kamalaṁ gokulākhyaṁ mahat padam \|*  
*tat-karṇikāraṁ tad-dhāma tad-anantāṁśa-sambhavam \|\|354\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The dhāma of Kṛṣṇa called Gokula is the supreme abode. It is the center (pericarp) of a lotus with a thousand petals and it is sustained by a portion of the spiritual energy of Baladeva called Ananta.**

*karṇikāraṁ mahad yantraṁ ṣaṭ-koṇaṁ vajra-kīlakam \|*  
*ṣaḍ-aṅgaṁ ṣaṭ-padī-sthānaṁ prakṛtyā puruṣeṇa ca \|\|355\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The pericarp of the lotus is also a great yantra with six points and a diamond centerpiece. The six parts of the mantra are placed on the six points of the yantra. The yantra is accompanied by prakṛti(deity of the yantra) and puruṣa (deity of the mantra).**

*premānanda-mahānanda-rasenāvasthitaṁ hi yat \|*  
*jyotī-rūpeṇa manunā kāma-bījena saṅgatam \|\|356\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This yantra produces a variety of rasas of great bliss arising from prema. The yantra is also endowed with the kāma-bīja, a selfrevealing, non-material mantra.**

*tat-kiñjalkaṁ tad-aṁśānām tat-patrāṇi śriyām api \|\|357\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Around the pericarp is a wall with spires like stamens. In the place are also various divisions and bowers for the gopīs, which are like petals.**

*śyāmair gauraiś ca raktaiś ca śuklaiś ca pārṣada-rṣabhaiḥ*  
*śobhitaṁ śaktibhis tābhir adbhutābhiḥ samantataḥ \|\| iti \|\|358\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**It is resplendent with astonishing śaktis and followers with black, golden, red and white complexions.**

*tatra—*  
*śyāmaś caturbhujo viṣṇuḥ pūrva-dig-dvāra-pālakaḥ \|*  
*gauraś caturbhujo viṣṇur dakṣiṇa-dvāra-pālakaḥ \|\|359\|\|*  
*raktaś catubhujo viṣṇuḥ paścima-dvāra-pālakaḥ \|*  
*śuklaś catubhujo viṣṇur uttara-dvāra-pālakaḥ \|\|360\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The eastern door keeper is four-armed Viṣṇu with black complexion.**  
**The southern door keeper is four-armed Viṣṇu with gold complexion.**  
**The western door keeper is four-armed Viṣṇu with red complexion.**  
**The northern door keeper is four-armed Viṣṇu with white complexion.**

*atha (1) śrī-lalitā-sakhyāḥ paricayaḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**Description of Lalitā:**

*anaṅga-sukhadākhyo ’sti kuñjas tasyottare dale \|*

**In the north, there is the king of bowers called Lalitānanda-da. Lalitā, bright yellow in complexion, lives there at all times.17**

*khaṇḍitā-bhāvam āpannā rati-yuktā harau sadā \|\|363\|\|*

**She is absorbed in serving camphor and betel nut. She is decorated with attractive ornaments. Her age is fourteen years, eight months and twenty-seven days.**

*śāradā ca patir yasyā bhairavākhyo mato budhaiḥ \|\|365\|\|*

**When the lover breaks an appointment, and enjoys with another woman, and then comes with the marks of enjoyment the next morning, the woman becomes devastated (khaṇḍitā). She shows anger, breathes heavily, and remains silent. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi5.85)**

*yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**An example:**

*yāvair dhūmalitaṁ śiro bhuja-taṭīṁ tāṭaṅka-mudrāṅkitāṁ*

**Seeing Kṛṣṇa in the morning with reddish lac on his head, with the marks of earrings on his shoulders, kuṅkuma from someone’s breast smeared on his chest, and a crushed garland on his chest, with eyes rolling, Śyāmalā became angry in her heart, showed a smile on her face, and remained silent. **Перевод:**

**The vāmā:**

*māna-grahe sadodyuktā tac chaithilye ca kopanā \|*  
*abhedyā nāyake prāyaḥ krūrā vāmeti kīrtyate \|\|373\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The vāma is always ready to manifest māna. When māna slackens it becomes anger, which cannot be pacified by the hero. She is generally harsh towards him. (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 8.32)**

*yūthe ’tra vāma-prakharā lalitādyāḥ prakīrtitāḥ \|\|374\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Rādhā’s group Lalitā is famous as a vāma-prakharā. (Ujjvalanīlamaṇi 8.37)**

*vāma-prakharodāharaṇaṁ, yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**An example of vāma-prakharā:**

*amūr vraja-mṛgekṣaṇāś catur-aśīti-lakṣādhikāḥ*  
*pratisvam iti kīrtitaṁ sa-vayasā tavaivāmunā \|*

*kathaṁ tad api sāhasī śaṭha! jighṛkṣur enām asi \|\|375\|\|*

**There are eight million four hundred thousand gopīs. Even your friend has praised each of them. But it is well known in the world that my dear friend Rādhā is the most valuable among them all. Cheater! How can you desire to grab her so boldly? (Ujjvalanīlamaṇi 8.36)**

*asyā yūtho, yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**Her group:**

*lakṣmī līlā ca lalitā ṅe tato vahni-nāyikā \|*

**The mādhyandinīya śākha of the Yajur Veda says:**

*tāṁ vāṁ vāstūny uśmasi gamadhyai*  
*yatra gāvo bhūri-śṛṅgā ayāsaḥ iti*

**Перевод:**

**We desire to attain the places where Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma perform pastimes. In that place the cows, endowed with happiness in serving, have large horns.**

*asyārthaḥ——‘tāṁ’ tāni śaso’m-ādeśa ārṣaḥ; ‘vāṁ’ yuvayoḥ śrī-rāma*  
*kṛṣṇayoḥ; ‘vāstūni’ līlā-sthānāni śrī-vṛndavana-madhya-gata-pradeśānīty*  
*ārthaḥ; ‘gamadhyai’ gantuṁ gamer ārṣas tum artho dhyai-pratyayaḥ;*  
*‘uśmasi’ kāmayāmahe, vaṣṭer liṭy uttama-puruṣa-bahu-vacanam;*  
*tad-uttara i-kāra ārṣaḥ; yatra yeṣu ‘bhūri-śṛṅgāḥ’ mahā-śṛṅgyaḥ*  
*‘gāvo’ vartante \| gāvaḥ kathambhūtāḥ?—‘ayāsaḥ’ śubhāvahāḥ; “ayaḥ*  
*śubhāvaho vidhiḥ” ityamaraḥ; atra śubhaṁ śrī-kṛṣṇasya prema-sevā*  
*sukham iti jñeyam \|*

**Перевод:**

**Tām stands for tāni. The substitution of am for the affix śas is poetic license. Vām refers to Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma. Vāstūnimeans the places in Vṛndāvana where they perform pastimes. Gamadhyai means ‘to go’, an infinite expressed by the affix dhyai. Uśmasi means “we desire,” first person plural. The root is vaś. The ending “i” is poetic license. Bhūri-śṛṅgāḥ means cows having large horns. How are they? They confer auspiciousness. Ayaḥmeans conferring auspiciousness according to Amara-kośa. Here auspiciousness means the happiness of serving Kṛṣṇa with prema.**

**Перевод:**

**The wise explain that there are four types of auspiciousness (śubha): affection for all living entities, being attractive to all living entities, possession of good qualities, and happiness, as well as other items. (BRS** [**1.1.27**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/1.1.27)**)**
