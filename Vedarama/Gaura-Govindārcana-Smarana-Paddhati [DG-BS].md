I worship Navadvipa, incomparable in the three worlds, non-different from Vrndavana, filled with constant sankirtana of Krsna’s names, manifesting great, expanding affection in every house, situated on the attractive bank of the Ganga, an abode similar to the huge back of Kurma, surrounded by charming gardens, and filled with a multitude of huge houses made of gold and precious gems.

Transliterated Sanskrit verses with English verse translations by Bhanu Swami.

# Gaura-Govindārcana-Smarana-Paddhati \[DG-BS\]

### Text 1

*sādhako brāhma-muhūrte cotthāya nijeñṭa-nāmāni smaret kīrtayed vā –*

**Перевод:**

**The sadhaka should rise during the brahma-muhūrta period, remember or chant the names of worshipable lord.**

*sa jayati viśuddha-vikramaḥ*  
*kanakābhaḥ kamalāyatekñaṇaḥ \|*  
*vara-jānu-lambi-sad-bhujo*  
*bahudhā bhakti-rasābhinartakaḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Displaying pure actions, having a golden complexion and lotus eyes, having attractive arms that extend to his knees, Caitanya, filled abundantly with the rasas of bhakti, remains ever glorious.**

### Text 2

*srī-rāmeti janārdaneti jagatāṁ nātheti nārāyaṇe-*  
*tyānandeti dayāpareti kamalākānteti kṛñṇeti ca \|*  
*śrīman nāma-mahāmṛtābdhi-laharī-kallola-magnaṁ muhur*  
*muhyantaṁ galad-aśru-netram avaśaṁ māṁ nātha nityaṁ kuru \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord, may I always and spontaneously have tears flowing in my eyes. Make me constantly bewildered and absorbed in the billowing waves of the great, sweet ocean of the Lord’s names, as I chant names like Rāma, Janārdana, Jagannātha, Nārāyaṇa, Ānanda, Dayāpara, Kamalā-kānta, and Krsna.**

### Text 3

*srī-kanta krsna karuṇāmaya kañjanābha*  
*kaivalya-vallabha mukunda murāntaketi \|*  
*namavalim vimala-mauktika-hara-lakñmī*  
*lavanya-vancana-karim karavani kanthe \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May I have in my throat the names Śrī-kānta, Krsna, Karunāmaya, Kanjanabha, Kaivalya-vallabha, Mukunda, Murāntaka, which surpass the beauty of a wealth of pure pearl necklaces.**

### Text 4

*krsna rama mukunda vāmana vāsudeva jagadguro*  
*matsya kacchapa narasimha varāha rāghava pāhi mām \|*  
*deva-dānava-naradadi munīndra-vandya dayānidhe*  
*devakī-suta dehi me tava pada-bhaktim acancalam \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Krsna! Rāma! Mukunda! Vāmana! Vāsudeva! Jagadguru! Matysa! Kacchapa! Narasiṁha! Varāha! Rāghava! Please protect me! O Lord worshipped by devatās, demons, humans and sages! O ocean of mercy! O son of Devakī! Give me fixed devotion at your lotus feet.**

### Text 5

*he gopalaka he kraā-jala-nidhe he sindhu-kanya-pate*  
*he kaṁsāntaka he gajendra-karuna-kārina he madhava \|*  
*he rāmanuja he jagattraya-guro he pundarikaksa mam*  
*he gopī-jana-natha palaya param janāmi na tvam vina \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O protector of the cows! O ocean of mercy! O husband of Laksmī! O killer of Kaṁsa! O giver of mercy to Gajendra! O Mādhava! O younger brother of Balarāma! O guru of the three worlds! O lotus-eyed Lord! O master of the gopīs! Please nourish me! I do not know anyone except you.**

### Text 6

*srī-narayana pundarika-nayana srī-rāma sīta-pate*  
*govindācyuta nanda-nandana mukundānanda damodara \|*  
*visno raghava vasudeva nrhare devendra-cudāmane*  
*samsararnava-karna-dharaka hare srī-krsna tubhyam namah \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Narayana! O lotus-eyed Lord! O Rāma! O husband of Sītā! O Govinda! O Acyuta! O son of Nanda! O Mukunda! O Ānanda! O Dāmodara! O Viñṇu! O Rāghava! O Vāsudeva! O Nṛhari! O crest jewel of the king of devatās! O helmsman for crossing the ocean of saṁsāra! O Hari! O Kṛñṇa! I offer respects to you.**

### Text 7

*bhandriresa sikhanda-mandana vara srikhanda-liptanga he*  
*vrndaranya-purandara sphurad-amandendivara syamala \|*  
*kalindi-priya nanda-nandana paranandaravindeksana*  
*srī-govinda mukunda sundara-tano mam dinam anandaya \|\|7\|\|*  
*(Padyāvalī 33-38)*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord of Bhāndira forest! O Lord ornamented with a peacock feather! O excellent Lord, whose limbs are anointed with sandalwood! O Indra of Vṛndāvana! O Lord, dark as a bright, blossoming, blue lotus! O dearest of Kālindī! O son of Nanda! O supreme bliss! O lotus-eyed Lord! O Govinda! O Mukunda! O lord with beautiful body! Give bliss to this suffering soul.**

### Text 8

*samudra-mekhale devi parvata-stana-mandale \|*  
*visnu-patni namas tubhyam pada-sparsam kñamasva me \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O goddess earth, who is surrounded by the ocean and whose breasts are the mountains! O wife of Viñṇu! I offer respects to you. Forgive me for touching you with my feet.**

### Text 9

*tato bahir gatva maitra-krtyadi-vidhim kuryat, danta-dhāvanādim*  
*acaret, suddhāsane purvabhimukhi upavisya niscala-manah -*

**Перевод:**

**Going outside, one should relieve himself, brush the teeth, sit on a pure āsana facing east, and concentrate the mind.**

*smaret srimad gaura-candram svardhunya darsine tate \|*  
*cintamani-citta-dhamni srī-navadvipa-namake \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He should meditate on Gauracandra, on the southern bank of the Gaṅgā, in an abode named Navadvīpa, whose heart is cintamani.**

### Text 10

*svardhunyām caru-tire sphuritam atibrhat-kurma-prsthabha-gatram*  
*ramyaramavrtam sanmani-kanaka-maha-sadma-sandaih paritam \|*  
*nityaṁ pratyalayodyat-pranaya-bhara-lasat-krsna-samkirtanatyṁm*  
*srī-vrndatavy abhinnam tri-jagad anupamaṁ srī-navadvipam ide \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I wordship Navadvīpa, incomparable in the three worlds, nondifferent from Vṛndāvana, filled with constant saṅkīrtana of Kṛñṇa’s names, manifesting great, expanding affection in every house, situated on the attractive bank of the Gaṅgā, an abode similar to the huge back of Kūrma, surrounded by charming gardens, and filled with a multitude of huge houses made of gold and precious gems.**

### Text 11

*phullac-chrimad druma-valli-tallaja-lasat-tira tarangavali-*  
*ramya manda-marum-marala-jalaja-srenisu bhrngaspadam \|*  
*sad-ratnācita-divya-tirtha-nivahā sri-gaura-pādambuja-*  
*dhuli-dhusaritanga-bhava-nicitā gaṅgāsti sampāvani \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The purifying Gaṅgā, attractive with its groups of waves, whose bank carrying attractive bathing places studded with precious gems, is radiant with excellent varieties of attractive blossoming creepers and trees, the abode of bees amidst clusters of lotuses which sway in the gentle breeze and are inhabited by swans, has ecstatic symptoms on her limbs from touching the dust of Gaurāṅga’s lotus feet.**

### Text 12

*tasyās tira-suramya-hema-surasā-madhye lasac chri-nava-*  
*dvipo bhāti sumaṅgalo madhu-ripor ānanda-vanyo mahān \|*  
*nana-puspa-phalāḍhya-vṛkña-latikāramyo mahat sevito*  
*nana-varna-vihaṅgamali-ninadair hrt-karna-hāri hi yah \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Auspicious, glorious, gigantic Navadvīpa, a flood of bliss for Kṛñṇa, served by the devotees, pleasant with creepers and trees laden with various fruits and flowers, which steals the ear and heart with the cries of variously colored flocks of birds, shines amidst the beauty of the charming golden bank of the Gaṅgā.**

### Text 13

*kāṇḍaṁ mārakataṁ prabhūta-viṭapī-śākhā suvarṇātmikā*  
*patrāliḥ kuruvinda-komala-mayī prāvālikāḥ korakāḥ \|*  
*puñpāṇāṁ nikaraḥ suhīraka-mayo vaidūryakīyā phala-*  
*śreṇī yasya sa ko ’pi śākhi-nikaro yatrātimātrojjvalaḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Navadvīpa there are most brilliant groves of trees whose trunks are made of emeralds, whose branches are made of gold, whose leaves are made of pleasing rubies, whose buds are made of coral, whose flowers are made of diamonds and whose fruits are made of vaidūrya gems.**

### Text 14

*tan madhye dvija-bhavya-loka-nikarāgārāli-ramyāṅganam*  
*ārāmopavanāli-vilasad vedī-vihārāspadam \|*  
*sad-bhakti-prabhayā virājita-mahā-bhaktāli-nityotsavaṁ*  
*praty āgāram aghāri-mūrti-sumahad-bhātīha yat pattanam \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Navadvīpa, the city has attractive yards surrounding houses of gentle brāhmaṇas, made attractive with many groves and gardens, where there are platforms for resting. There are continual festivals of exalted devotees, which shine because of the power of pure bhakti. In every house, there is intense radiance coming from the deities of Kṛñṇa.**

### Text 15

*evaṁbhūte śrī-navadvīpa-madhye manasi nivāsaṁ kṛtvā tatra śrī-*  
*gurudevasya śayyotthāna-mukha-prakñālana-danta-dhāvanādi-krameṇa*  
*yathā-yogyaṁ sevāṁ kuryāt sevānantaraṁ dhyāyet yathā yāmale-*

**Перевод:**

**Making a residence in the mind within Navadvīpa just described, one should serve one’s guru in a suitable way, meditating on waking him up from bed, washing his mouth, brushing his teeth, etc. After that service one should meditate as follows:**

*tatra śrī-guru-dhyānam-*  
*kṛpā-marandānvita-pāda-paṅkajaṁ*  
*śvetāmbaraṁ gaura-ruciṁ sanātanam \|*  
*śandaṁsumālyābharaṇaṁ guṇālayaṁ*  
*smarāmi sad-bhaktam ahaṁ guruṁ harim \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meditation on guru at that place:**  
**I meditate on my guru, non-different from the Lord, a true devotee, eternal, golden in complexion, dressed in white cloth, who is an abode of good qualities, decorated with a beautiful garland, whose lotus feet flow with the nectar of mercy.**

### Text 16

*srī-guru-parama-guru-parātpara-guru-parameñṭhi-gurūṇām*  
*anugāmitvena śrīman mahāprabhor mandiraṁ gacchet \| tatra tad-ājñayā*  
*śrī-navadvīpa-candrasya śayyotthānaṁ suvāsita-jalena śrī-mukha-*  
*prakñālanādi-krameṇa sevāṁ kuryāt \|*

**Перевод:**

**Following one’s guru, his guru, parātpara guru and parameñṭhi guru, one should go the house of Mahāprabhu. There, by their order, one should serve by waking Gaurāṅga, and washing his mouth with pure water, etc.**

*tatra srīman mahāprabhor dhyānaṁ yathā ūrddhvāmnāye (3.15)-*  
*dvi-bhujaṁ svarṇa-ruciraṁ varābhaya-karaṁ tathā \|*  
*premāliṅgana-sambaddhaṁ gṛṇantaṁ hari-nāmakam \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The meditation on Mahāprabhu is as follows from Uddhvāmnāya:**  
**He has two arms, has a golden complexion, and with his hands offers blessings and fearlessness. Tightly embraced by prema, he chants the name of the Lord.**

### Text 17-18

*anantaraṁ śrī-vṛndāvanaṁ dhyāyet-*  
*vṛndāvanaṁ divya-latā-parītaṁ*  
*latāś ca puñpa-sphuritāgra-bhājaḥ \|*  
*puñpāṇy api sphīta-madhu-vratāni*  
*madhu-vratāś śruti-hāri-gītāḥ \|\|17\|\|*

*madhye vṛndāvane ramye pañcāśat-kuñja-maṇḍite \|*  
*kalpa-vṛkña-nikuñje tu divya-ratna-maye gṛhe \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should then meditate on Vṛndāvana:**  
**Vṛndāvana is filled with attractive creepers. The creepers have flowers blossoming at their tips. The flowers are bursting with bees. The bees’ humming surpasses the sound of the Vedas. Attractive Vṛndāvana is decorated with fifty bowers, with groves of desire trees, and a house made of attractive gems.**

### Text 19

*tatra siddha-dehena śrī-rādhā-kṛñṇayor niśānta-līlāṁ smared yathā-*

**Перевод:**

**In one’s siddha-deha, one should meditate on Rādhā and Kṛñṇa’s pastimes at the end of night.**

*niśāvasāne śrī-rādhā-kṛñṇau śrī-vṛndā-niyukta-rasamaya-parama*  
*vidagdha-śuka-śāri-vṛnda-padya-paṭhana-janita-prabodhāv*  
*api gāḍhopagūhana-sukha-bhaṅgād asahiñṇutayā kñaṇam*  
*avakāśyamāna-jāgarau tat-tat-padya-prapaṭhita-niśāvasāna-sātaṅkau*  
*puñpamayānanda-talpotthitau sva-sva-kuñjāt tat-kālāgata-śrīmal-lalitā*  
*viśākhādi-priya-sakhī-vṛnda-sanarma-vāg-vilāsena sāntarānandau*  
*kakkhaṭy-udita-jaṭilā-śravaṇāt sa-śaṅkau saṅga-tyāga-bhayam*  
*asahamānau tau bhītyotkaṇṭhākulau sva-sva-gṛhaṁ gacchataḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**At the end of night, Rādhā and Kṛñṇa are awakened by most expert, attractive parrots and śārīs reciting poetry on the order of Vṛndā-devī. For a moment not being able to tolerate breaking their joyous, deep embrace, they then visible wake up, frightened that the end of night has come as described in the parrots’ recitation. They rise from the bed of bliss made of flowers. They become internally blissful from the clever joking words of the many dear sakhīs like Lalitā and Viśakhā who have come at that time from their own bowers. Hearing the monkey Kakaṭi utter the name of Jaṭilā, unable to tolerate fear of separation, they go to their houses out of fear, though overwhelmed with desire**

*evaṁ krameṇa śrī-gauracandrasya śrī-rādhā-kṛñṇayor līlāṁ smaret \|*  
*niśānta-līlā-smaraṇānantaraṁ gurv-ādīn daṇḍavat praṇamet yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**Thus one should meditate on pastimes of Gaurāṅga and Rādhā and Kṛñṇa. After remembering the pastimes at the end of night, one should offer respects to guru, etc. as follows:**

*ajñāna-timirāndhasya jñānāñjana-śalākayā \|*  
*cakñur unmīlitaṁ yena tasmai śrī-gurave namaḥ \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to guru who opened the eyes of a person blinded by dark ignorance with the ointment of knowledge.**

### Text 20

*iti mantraṁ paṭhitvā śrī-guruṁ daṇḍavat praṇamya evaṁ parama-guru-parātpara-guru-parameñṭhi-guru-gosvāmi-caraṇān krameṇa daṇḍavat praṇamet \| tataḥ śrī-gauracandraṁ praṇamet-*

**Перевод:**

**After reciting this mantra and offering respects to guru, one should offer respects to parama-guru, parātpara-guru and parameñṭhi-guru and the gosvāmīs. Then one should offer respects to Gauracandra.**

*viśvambharāya gaurāya caitanyāya mahātmane \|*  
*śacī-putrāya mitrāya lakñmīśāya namo namaḥ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer repeated respects to Viśvambhara-gaura, the great soul Caitanya, companion to all beings, the son of Śacī, and lord of Lakñmī.**

### Text 21

*nityānandam ahaṁ vande karṇe lambita-mauktikam \|*  
*caitanyāgraja-rūpeṇa pavitrī-kṛta-bhūtalam \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the elder brother of Caitanya, Nityānanda, having a pearl hanging from his ear, who purifies the earth.**

### Text 22

*nistāritāśeña-janaṁ dayāluṁ*  
*premāmṛtābdhau parimagna-cittam \|*  
*caitanya-candrādṛtam arcitaṁ tam*  
*advaita-candraṁ śirasā namāmi \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With my head, I offer respects to merciful Advaita-candra, the deliverer of unlimited people, whose heart is absorbed in an ocean of sweet prema, who is respected and worshipped by Caitanya.**

### Text 23

*gadādhara namas tubhyaṁ yasya gaurāṅgo jīvanam \|*  
*namas te śrī-śrīnivāsa-paṇḍita prema-vigraha \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Gadādhara! I offer respects to you, the life of Gaurāṅga. O Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita, form of prema, I offer respects to you.**

### Text 24

*evaṁ krameṇa gaura-bhakta-gaṇān daṇḍavat praṇamet \|*

**Перевод:**

**In this way, one should offer respects to the associates of Gaurāṅga.**

*śrī-navadvīpa-dhāmne namaḥ \|*  
*śrī-gaṅgāyai namaḥ \|*  
*śrī-saṅkīrtanāya namaḥ \|*  
*śrī-gauḍa-maṇḍalāya namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Navadvīpa dhāma, to the Gaṅgā, to saṅkīrtana, and to Gaura-maṇḍala.**

*kandarpa-koṭi-ramyāya sphurad-indīvara-tviñe \|*  
*jagan-mohana-līlāya namo gopendra-sūnave \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the son of Nanda, more beautiful than a million Cupids, endowed with the complexion of a blossoming blue lotus, whose pastimes enchant the universe.**

### Text 25

*tapta-kāñcana-gaurāṅgi rādhe vṛndāvaneśvari \|*  
*vṛñabhānu-sute devi praṇamāmi hari-priye \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Rādhā, possessing limbs of molten gold! O queen of Vṛndāvana!O son of Vṛñabhānu! O goddess, dear to Kṛñṇa! I offer respects to you.**

### Text 26

*śrī-rādhikā-prāṇa-samāṁ kanīyasīṁ*  
*viśākhikā-śikñita-saukhya-sauñṭhavām \|*  
*līlāmṛtenocchalitāṅga-mādhurīm*  
*anaṅga-purvāṁ praṇamāmi mañjarīm \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Anaṅga-mañjarī, Rādhikā’s very life and Rādhā’s younger sister, who is skillful at creating joy because of Viśākhā’s teachings, and whose limbs increase in sweetness by the sweetness of couple’s pastimes.**

### Text 27

*lalitādi-parama-preñṭha-sakhī-vṛndebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to all the parama-preñṭha-sakhis like Lalitā.**

*kusumikādi-sakhī-vṛndebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to all the sakhīs like Kusumikā.**

*kastūryādi-nitya-sakhī-vṛndebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to all the nitya-sakhīs like Kasturī.**

*śaśimukhyādi-prāṇa-sakhī-vṛndebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to all the prāṇa-sakhīs like Śaśimukhī.**

*kuraṅgākñyādi-priya-sakhī-vṛndebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to all the priya-sakhīs like Kuraṅgākñī.**

*śrī-rūpādi-mañjarībhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Śrī-rūpa-mañjarī and others.**

*śrīdāmādi-sakhi-vṛndebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to all the sakhas like Śrīdāma.**

*sarva-gopa-gopībhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to all cowherds and cowherd women.**

*vraja-vāsibhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the inhabitants of Vraja.**

*śrī-vṛndā-vipinebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the forests of Vṛndāvana.**

*śrī-rāsa-maṇḍalāya namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the circle of the rāsa dance.**

*śrī-yamunāyai namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Yamunā River.**

*śrī-rādhā-kuṇḍa-śyāma-kuṇḍābhyāṁ namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Śyāma-kuṇḍa.**

*śrī-govardhanāya namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Govardhana.**

*śrī-dvādaśa-vipinebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the twelve forests.**

*śrī-vraja-maṇḍalāya namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the area of Vraja.**

*śrī-mathurā-maṇḍalāya namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the area of Mathurā.**

*sarvāvatārebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to all avatāras.**

*ananta-koṭi-vaiñṇavebhyo namaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to millions of devotees.**

*vāñchā-kalpa-tarubhyaś ca kṛpā-sindhubhya eva ca \|*  
*patitānāṁ pāvanebhyo vaiñṇavebhyo namo namaḥ \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer repeated respects to the devotees, who are desire trees and oceans of mercy, deliverers of the fallen souls.**

### Text 28

*atha snānam ācaret yathā-nady ādau pravāhābhimukhe*
