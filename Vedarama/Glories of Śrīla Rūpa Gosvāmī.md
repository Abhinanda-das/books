# Glories of Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī

### 1

*jaya jaya rūpa mahā-rasa-sāgara*  
*daraśana paraśana, bacana rasāyana*  
*ānandahuke gāgara (1)*

**Перевод:**

**All glories, all glories to Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī, the ocean of the most exalted mahā-rasa! By seeing him, coming in touch with him, or hearing of his nectarean words, one is so overwhelmed in ecstatic bliss that the voice instantly chokes up.**

### 2

*ati gambhīra, dhīra karuṇāmoy*  
*prema-bhaktite āgara*  
*ujjvala-prema, mahā-maṇi prakaṭita*  
*deśa gauḍa vairāgara (2)*

**Перевод:**

**He is very grave, calm and merciful by nature. He is like a storehouse of prema-bhakti. He revealed the great jewel of divine conjugal love after he left Gauḍa-deśa and went to stay in Vṛndāvana.**

### 3

*sad-guṇa-maṇḍita, paṇḍita-rañjana*  
*vṛndāvana nija nāgara*  
*kīrita bimala jaśa, śuno taṅhi mādho*  
*satata raholo hiye jāgara (3)*

**Перевод:**

**Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī is decorated by all good qualities, and is pleasingly colored by poetic genius. His own residence is Śrī Vṛndāvana. One who simply hears about his spotlessly pure glories find that Lord Mādhava’s vision is continuously awakened within his heart.**
