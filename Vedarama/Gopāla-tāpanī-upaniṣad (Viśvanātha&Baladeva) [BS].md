# Gopāla-tāpanī-upaniṣad (Viśvanātha&Baladeva) \[BS\]

## Foreword

Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad, part of Atharva Veda, is often quoted by Gauḍīya ācāryas since it is a śruti text which clearly praises Kṛṣṇa and the path of bhakti as supreme. Its main subject is Kṛṣṇa. It also explains the Kṛṣṇa mantra in detail. Jīva, Prabhodānanda, Viśvanātha, and Baladeva have written commentaries on this Upaniṣad. This work includes the commentaries of both Viśvanātha and Baladeva.

## Section One – Pūrva Vibhāga

### 1.1

*saccidānanda rūpāya kṛṣṇāyākliṣṭakāriṇe \|*  
*namo vedānta-vedyāya gurave buddhi-sākṣiṇe \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Kṛṣṇa, the form of eternity, knowledge, and bliss, who performs actions without fatigue, who is to be known by the conclusion of the Vedas, who is the guru of all knowledge, and who reveals himself by guiding the intelligence of the jīva.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

*mūrta brahma svarūpārtha varṣiṇīṁ bhakta-harṣiṇīm \|*  
*gopāla tāpanīṁ naumi śrīmad gopāla tāpanīm \|\|*

**I offer respect to Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad, which gives joy to Gopāla, gives joy to the devotees, and showers the meaning of the svarūpa of Brahman with form.**

The śruti called Gopāla-tapanī, recited in the Pippalāda-śākhā of the Atharva Veda, obtained in the sampradāya of Brahmā, of the Parāśara gotra famous in Gujarat, which explained Kṛṣṇa’s svarūpa, pastimes and mercy, offers respect to Kṛsṇa.

Respect is offered to Kṛṣṇa, most famous in the Sāma upaniṣad (Chāndogya) with the words kṛṣṇāya devakī-nandanāya and in Bhāgavatam with kṛṣṇastu bhagavān svayam. The word Kṛṣṇa is explained. His svarūpa is described. That svarūpa is sac-cidānanda, Brahman.

*kṛṣṇa śabdaś ca sat tārtho ṇaś cānanda-sva-rūpakaḥ \|*  
*tayoraikyaṁ paraṁ brahma kṛṣṇa ity abhidhīyatīti smṛteḥ*

**Kṛi means “attractive” and ṇa means “having a form of bliss.” Combined together, the two roots indicate that Kṛṣṇa is the supreme form of God.**

Or his form or svarūpa exists in all places and times (sat), attracting all, and is knowledge and bliss (cid-ānanda).

His mercy is described. His nature is to make all people devoid of the five types of suffering starting with avidyā and asmita (akliṣṭakāriṇe) since it is heard that he gave liberation even to enemies like Keśi and Kaṁsa. That form is the purport of scriptures. He is to be known by the Vedānta. He should be known since he gives liberation whereas no one else does. His special mercy is described. He personally teaches about his own worship. In what form does he teach? He has the form of antaryāmī (buddhisākṣiṇe). He teaches his worship as antaryāmī by saying “Worship me alone to attain me, after making one of my devotees into a guru.”

*ācārya-caittya-vapuṣā sva-gatiṁ vyanakti*

**Appearing externally as guru and internally as Paramātmā, you reveal the goal of prema to the devotees. (SB** [**11.29.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/29.6)**)**

*dadāmi buddhi yogaṁ taṁ yena mām upayānti te*

**I give them the intelligence by which they can attain me. (BG** [**10.10**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/10.10)**)**

I offer respects to Kṛsṇa who teaches about the method to attain him.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

*satyānantācintya-śakty eka pakṣe sarvādhyakṣe bhakta-rakṣātidakṣe \|*  
*śrīgovinde viśva-sargādi kande pūrṇānande nityam āstāṁ matir naḥ \|\|*

**May my mind be constantly fixed upon Govinda, endowed with Section One – Pūrva Vibhāga 5 acintya-śakti, eternal and unlimited, the ruler of all, most skillful at protecting his devotees, the creator of the universe.**

*sanātanaṁ rūpam ihopadarśayann*  
*ānanda-sindhuṁ paritaḥ pravardhayan \|*  
*antas tama-stoma-haraḥ sa rājatāṁ*  
*caitanya-rūpo vidhur adbhutodayaḥ \|\|*

**May the moon in the form of Caitanya, arising in an astonishing way, displaying on earth Sanātana and Rūpa, increasing everywhere the ocean of bliss, destroying dense darkness within, shine brilliantly.**

*gopāla-tāpanīṁ naumi yā kṛṣṇaṁ svayam īśvaraṁ \|*  
*karastha-ratna-saṅkāśaṁ sandarśayati sad-dhiyaḥ \|\|*

**I offer respects to Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad which shows svayam bhagavān Kṛṣṇa like a jewel in the hand to the person of pure intelligence.**

The Gopāla tāpanī Upaniṣad, of the Pippalāda-śākhā, studied by the followers of the Atharva Veda in Orissa, offers respects to the Lord who will be explained in this work.

I offer respects to Kṛṣṇa. This means the Lord who is dark like an atasī flower.

In Nārāyaṇīya, Kṛṣṇa says kṛṣṇo varṇaś ca me yasmāt tasmāt kṛṣṇo’ham arjuna: O Arjuna! I am called Kṛṣṇa because I am dark complexioned. Kṛṣ (one who attracts) is not the meaning here since that is expressed when the verse also says sac-cid-ānanda.

He is described by his svarūpa. He is pervasive of all time and place by sandhinī-śakti (sat). He is self-manifesting (cit) and full of bliss. He has a form of sac-cid-ānanda. This will be explained in verse 7.

Endowment of acintya and ananta śakti is described. He performs all actions just by his decision (akliṣṭa-kāriṇe). He shows to Brahmā immediately all the universes with their elements and devatās and gives liberation rare for yogīs to the demons he kills.

To show proof of such a nature he is further described. He is to be known by Brahmā through the Upaniṣads. He is the teacher about his own nature to Brahmā and others (gurave). But he is the cause of realizing the meaning of what is taught (buddhisakṣiṇe). He causes one to be fixed in realization since he is the superintendent of intelligence.

I offer respects to the Lord of all, most merciful, the cause of creation of the universe, endowed with many qualities like satyasaṅkalpa, omniscient, dark in complexion, full of knowledge and bliss and all-pervading. Please accept me.

The offering of respects indicates that one is qualified (adhikārī), being niṣkāma. Kṛsṇa is the subject of this work (viṣāya). The statements are all related to this subject (sambandha). The goal (prayojana) is attaining Kṛṣṇa. Thus the four elements have been suitably expressed.

### 1.2

*oṁ munayo ha vai brāhmaṇam ūcuḥ \|*  
*kaḥ paramo devaḥ kuto mṛtyur bibheti kasya jñānenākhilaṁ*  
*vijñātaṁ bhavati kenedaṁ viśvaṁ saṁsarati iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sages spoke to Brahmā: Who is the supreme object of worship? Who does death fear? By knowing which person does all knowledge become manifest? By whose power does this universe move?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The sages ask a question so that the worship in the form of Section One – Pūrva Vibhāga 7 the eighteen-syllable mantra attained by guru’s mercy will be explained. Ha means clearly.

Brahmā (brāhmaṇam) means one who knows the Vedas. They ask Brahmā. By what knowledge, is everything - material and spiritual (akhilam) - known?

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

By inquiry, by general and specific questions and answers, one can understand the excellent meaning. This śruti thus begins in order to gain that understanding.

Oṁ indicates auspiciousness since it is the essence of all the Vedas. The sages are Brahma’s sons like the four Kumāras. Ha vai indicate a traditional teaching. They spoke to Brahmā (brāhmaṇam), since later he is also called Hairaṇya, meaning Hiraṇyagarbha. They asked (ūcuḥ). Parama (supreme) means a person having eternal good fortune to whom no one is equal or greater. Deva means one who is to be worshipped by all. Who is that? They ask about his qualities. Who destroys death? By knowing which person is everything about bhagavat-tattva understood? By whom does the whole universe operate with its activities? Who is the originator of the universe?

### 1.3

*tad uhovāca brāhmaṇaḥ \|*  
*kṛṣṇo vai paramaṁ daivatam \|*  
*govindān mṛtyur bibheti \|*  
*gopījanavallabha-jñānena taj-jñātaṁ bhavati \|*  
*svāhayedaṁ saṁsaratīti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā, knower of the Vedas, then spoke with clarity. Kṛṣṇa is the supreme object of worship. Death fears Govinda. By knowing the lover of the gopīs, all knowledge is revealed. The universe moves because of svāhā.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Kṛṣṇa is the viṣaya, since he is the object of worship (paramadaivatam).

Death fears Kṛṣṇa. As Govinda, he is a cowherd and protects the cows from tigers. He protects the jīvas from death.

Knowing him as the lover of the gopīs, all things material and spiritual are known even without investigating. There is nothing else to know. Bhakti, with Kṛṣṇa as the lover of the gopīs, is the highest type of bhakti. Bhaktyā mām abhijānāti: by bhakti one knows me. (BG 18.55) When one reaches the highest bhakti, one attains the highest knoweldge. He binds up the universe by the form of svāhā.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Though having been generally questioned, Brahmā answers with particulars since the meaning is excellent.

He spoke to them (tat). U (from uhocuḥ = u + ha + ūcuḥ) indicates emphasis. Brāhmaṇa means Brahmā, who realized supreme Brahman. He gives answers starting with kṛṣna vai… indicating the meaning of the mantra that he will teach.

### 1.4

*tad uhocuḥ \|*  
*kaḥ kṛṣṇo govindaśca ko’sāviti*  
*gopījanavallabhaḥ kaḥ kā svāheti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Kumaras spoke. Who is Kṛṣṇa? Who is Govinda? Who is the lover of the gopīs? Who is svāhā?**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

They ask Brahmā again in order to understand the special qualities of the meaning of the words Kṛṣṇa etc. of the śrutis.

The sages spoke clearly (ha) to ascertain (u) to Brahmā. The meanings should be understood without doubt, using derivation of the words. You should reveal the meaning with derivations.

### 1.5

*tān uvāca brāhmaṇaḥ \|*  
*pāpa-karṣaṇo go-bhūmi-veda-vidito gopījanāvidyā*  
*kalā-prerakas tanmāyā ceti sakalaṁ paraṁ brahmaivetat \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā spoke to them. Kṛṣṇa means he who destroys offenses. Govinda means he who is known among the cows, on earth and in the Vedas. Gopī-jana-vallabha means he who inspires the gopīs with prema. Svāhā indicates his spiritual energy. Kṛṣṇa, defined as above, is the supreme Brahman.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

He destroys (kārṣaṇa for Kṛṣṇa) accumulated prārabdha and aprārabhda sins. He attracts the sins and even the heart to his pastimes. He kills the demons like Pūtanā, Agha and Keśī in order to give them liberation. Kṛṣṇa alone is the supreme object of worship since he is most merciful.

He is known among the cows of Vraja (go) since he wanders there. He is known on earth (go) since he relieves her burden. He is known in the Vedas (go), since they describe him. He engages in acts to protect the cows, earth and Vedas. Thus he is known as Govinda. He protects the jīvas from death.

*gopāyati sakalam idaṁ gopāyati vā paraṁ pumāṁsam iti gopī \|*  
*prakṛtiḥ tasyāṁ jāta jana iti mahadādikaṁ pṛthivyantam \|\|*

**Gopī means prakṛti which protects the world or which hides the supreme Brahman from men. Jana means everything that is born in prakṛti from mahat to earth - (Krama-dīpikā)**

Kṛṣṇa inspires avidyā (kalā-prerakaḥ) in the jīvas by the material tattvas (gopī-jana).

Or gopī refers to the gopīs of Vraja, forms of the hlādinī-śakti. Avidyā in this case cannot refer to material ignorance. Thus avidyā means bewilderment in prema. He inspires distribution (kalā) of prema (avidyā) in the gopīs.

Or he inspires digits of the moon of ignorance (avidyā-kalā) in the gopīs. This means he bewilders them with his prema, stealing their knowledge by his beauty and sweetness.

He bewilders his beloveds by his qualities (vallabha). Thus the ignorance or bewilderment that is caused by his sweetness is superior to all knowledge since this is never seen even in jivanmuktas and ātmārāmas.

Svāhā is indicated by tan-māyā. Everything is supreme Brahman since māyā is his śakti.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He then gave an answer. Another meaning of the word Kṛsṇa is given after analyzing the syllables according to the meaning of kṛṣ and ṇa. He destroys sin. I say that the word Kṛsṇa means he who destroys all sins, ending avidyā. According to the rule kṛṣer varṇe (Unādi sūtra – 3.004) na is added to kṛṣ, to mean the name of a person as well as the color black.

*kṛṣṇeti maṅgalaṁ nāma yasya vāci pravartate \|*  
*bhasmībhavanti tasyāśu mahāpātaka koṭayaḥ \|*

**One who chants the auspicious name of Kṛṣṇa burns to ashes millions of mahapātakas immediately.**

*kṛṣṇaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ kṛṣṇa ityantakāle jalpan*  
*jantur jīvitaṁ yo jahāti \| ādyaḥ śabdaḥ kalpate*  
*tasya muktyai vrīḍā namrau tiṣṭhato dvārṇa-sthāu*

**One who at the end of life chants “Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa” and gives up his life attains liberation by the first word and the remaining two words stand there shyly bowing their heads.**

He is famous among the special animals, on planets, and in the Vedas. He is the master of the surabhī cows. He is the antaryāmī of all planets. He is known by all the Vedas. Thus I call him by the name Govinda. The word go has many meanings.

*gauḥ svarge ca balīvarderaśmau ca kuliśe pumān \|*  
*strī saurabheyī dṛg bāṇadig vāg bhūṣv apsu bhūmni ceti*

**Go means svarga, a bull, a ray, thunderbolt, surabhi cow, the eye, arrow, direction, word, earth, and water. (Medinī’s dictionary)**

Being of this nature, he destroys fear of saṁsāra for those who worship him. It was previously said that death fears Govinda. He stimulates the portions of āvidyā in the gopīs headed by Rādha. Āvidya means complete vidyā, special prema bhakti. Through his pastimes he stimulates astonishing forms (kalā) of that prema bhakti. This is the meaning of gopī-jana, shown by the word vallabha (lover). Later it will be said sa vo svāmī bhavati: he will become your master.

It will also be said later that bhakti, the essence of hlādini and saṁvit, composed of sac-cid-ānanda, is situated in the Lord. The word kalā means Rādhā and others who are actually forms of the Lord.

*ānanda-cinmaya-rasa-pratibhāvitābhis*  
*tābhir ya eva nija-rūpatayā kalābhiḥ*

**I worship the Supreme Lord Govinda who, though regarding all the inhabitants as his very self, resides in Goloka exclusively with the young gopīs, who are embodiments of madhura-rasa. (Brahma-saṁhitā** [**5.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/37)**)**

Ballavyo me madātmikāḥ: the gopīs are absorbed in me. (SB [10.46.6](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/46.6))

The supreme, unbroken tattva is endowed with the gopīs. It was already said that by knowing the lover of the gopīs (gopī-janavallabha-jñānena), everything is known. Being with him, they are his special manifestations.

*tatrātiśuśubhe tābhir bhagavān devakī-sutaḥ*

**Although the son of Devakī, is the reservoir of all kinds of beauty, when He is among the gopīs he nonetheless becomes more beautiful. (SB** [**10.33.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/33.6)**)**

Svaha revers to his māyā, his parokṣā-śakti (invisible) by which everyone acts. The question was asked “by what, does the universe move?”. Māyā here means the svarūpa-śakti.

*svarūpa bhūtayā nityaśaktyā māyākhyayā yutaḥ \|*  
*ato māyāmayaṁ viṣṇuṁ pravadanti sanātanamiti śruteḥ \|*

**The Lord is endowed with his eternal śakti called māyā arising from his svarūpa. Thus, the wise call eternal Viṣṇu māyā-mayam, made of māyā. (Caturveda-śikhā)**

*triguṇātmikā’tha jñānaṁ ca tathā cicchaktir eva ca \|*  
*māyāśabdena bhaṇyante śabdatattvārtha vedibhir*

**Those who know the meaning of words say that māyā means prakṛti made of the three guṇas, knowledge, and it also means the śakti of Viṣṇu. (Śabda-mahodadhi)**

Avidyā-kalā indicates that he produces bewilderment in the gopīs since it is impossible that they can be in material ignorance, being intimate parts of himself.

*māyā paraity abhimukhe ca vilajjamānā*

**Māyā, being ashamed, cannot stand before the Lord. (SB** [**2.7.47**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/7.47)**)**

Those who say that tan-māyā means the three material guṇas are foolish since material māyā cannot cause everything. Nor do the eternal associates (kalā) move by the three guṇas. Thus māyā refers to the svarūpa-śakti, the superior energy. It is superior to the three guṇas. Thus the meaning is suitable.

According to the questions of the sages, he defines the four meanings in a seemingly different way, but the conclusion is the same.

All the previous four meanings with their limbs are Kṛṣṇa (sakalam), who is para-brahman with human form, since Govinda and Gopījana-vallabha are modifiers of Kṛṣṇa and since he is not separate from his svarūpa-śakti (tan-māyā).

His nature as supreme brahman is explained later, after the words katha vāsyāvatārasya brahmatā bhavati: How can this avatāra be Svayam Bhagavān? (Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad [2.34](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.34))

### 1.6

*yo \[etad\] dhyāyati rasati bhajati so’mṛto bhavati so’mṛto bhavati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who meditates on Kṛṣṇa, chants his name, and serves him without self-interest becomes an associate of the Lord.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Rasati means he who utters. The word bhajati indicates serving. Amṛtaḥ means not having the quality of death. He becomes an associate of the Lord.

“If svāhā means māyā, what does ‘gopījana-vallabhāya māyā’ (māyā unto Gopījana-vallabhā) mean, instead of ‘gopījana-vallabhāya svāhā’ (offerings to Gopījana-vallabha)?” The meaning is ‘bestow māyā unto Gopījana-vallabha.’ Considering the dative case of gopījana-vallabhāya, the verb “bestow” is added. Māyā here means spiritual possessiveness (mamatā) and spiritual identity (ahantā).

Thus the meaning will be “Offer with spiritual possessiveness and spiritual identity (māyā) to Gopījana-vallabha.” Or “Offer to Gopījana-vallabha with intelligence and senses (māyā).” It is like the phase praviśa piṇḍam which acutally means “enter with the pinda offering.”

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Contemplating this, showing the supreme puruṣāṛtha, he establishes the tattva in this verse. He who meditates on this mantra, utters the mantra (rasati), and makes it favorable to the deity of the mantra with devotion, attains liberation. Repetition of the last phrase (so’mṛto bhavati) indicates an ending. Another version has etad yo dhyāyati.

### 1.7

*te hocuḥ \| kiṁ tadrūpaṁ kiṁ rasanaṁ kathaṁ vā*  
*ho tadbhajanaṁ tat sarvaṁ vividiṣatāmākhyāhīti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sages spoke. What form is the object of meditation, what is the utterance and how should one serve the Lord? Please explain this to those who desire to know.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Hearing the words meditation etc., the sages inquire. What is the method of meditation etc.? Kim indicates conjecture, as a indeclinable, or a question, disrespect or criticism according to Śrīdhara.

What form is the object of meditation? What name is to be chanted (rasanam)? How does one worship him? They speak to Brahmā.

### 1.8

*taduhovāca hairaṇyo gopa-veśam*  
*abhrābhaṁ taruṇaṁ kalpadrumāśritaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā spoke to them. The form of Kṛṣṇa wears the dress of a cowherd. He has the complexion of a cloud. He is youthful and sits under a desire tree.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Hairaṇyaḥ means Brahmā.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The form is described. Gopa-veśam means he has a flute and horn. This will be explained later.

### 1.9-10

*tadiha ślokā bhavanti \|\|*  
*sat-puṇḍarīkanayanaṁ meghābhaṁ vaidyutāmbaraṁ \|*  
*dvibhujaṁ jñāna-mudrāḍhyaṁ \[mauna-mudrāḍhyaṁ\]*  
*vanamālinamīśvaram \|\|*  
*gopa gopīgavāvītaṁ suradrumatalāśrayaṁ \|*  
*divyālaṅkaraṇopetaṁ ratnapaṅkaja madhyagam \|\|*  
*kālindījala kallola saṅgimāruta sevitaṁ \|*  
*cintayaṁścetasā kṛṣṇaṁ mukto bhavati saṁsṛteḥ \|\| iti*

**Перевод:**

**Meditating with concentration on two-armed lotus-eyed, silent Kṛṣṇa, who has the complexion of a cloud, who wears cloth flashing like lightning and a garland, who is surrounded by cowherds, gopīs and cows, who is bedecked with shining ornaments, while sitting under a desire tree on a jeweled lotus, who is served by a breeze mixed with water drops from the Yamunā, one becomes liberated from saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Having mentioned meditation, the form used for medition in the mantra is described. He is silent because he has a flute in his mouth.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The form is described. The verses describing the form become mantras.

He has eyes opened wide (sat) like a lotus. Puṇḍarīka is a synonym for lotus according to Dhananjaya. Because the eye has a red portion, it becomes very beautiful.

The word aravinda-netra could also be used. He wears cloth which is like lightning. It shines intensely. Jñāna-mudrāḍhyam means he does not speak with his mouth, since he is thinking of his beloved and is absorbed in playing the flute. Another version has mauna-mudrāḍhyam. Kṛṣṇa’s svarūpa is indicated by the adjectives like lotus-eyed. Thus the Lord, writer of the sūtras says:

Arūpavad eva hi tatpradhānatvāt: the Lord does not “possess” a form since his form is his very self. (Brahma-sūtra [3.2.14](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/3.2.14))

His form is himself. Dvibhujam indicates the form worshipped in Gokula. The cowherds are Śrīdāma, Subala and other boys. The gopīs are Rādhā, Candrāvalī and others. The cows are desire cows such as Piśaṅgā. He is surrounded by them. These represent other associates as well. The word iti indicates that the meditation is complete.

### 1.11-12

*rasyaṁ punārasanaṁ jalabhūmīndusampātakām ādi kṛṣṇāyety ekaṁ*  
*padaṁ govindāyeti dvitīyaṁ gopījaneti tṛtīyaṁ vallabhāyeti turīyam \|*  
*svāheti pañcamam iti \|\|*  
*pañcapadīṁ \[pañcapadaṁ\] japan pañcāṅgaṁ dyāv ābhūmī*  
*sūryācandramasau sāgnī tad-rūpatayā brahma saṁpadyate brahma*  
*sampadyata iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should chant the mantra repeatedly. The first part of the mantra is klīṁ kṛṣṇāya. The second part is govindāya. The third part is gopījana. The fourth part is vallabhāya. The fifth part is svāhā. Uttering these five parts while thinking of their presiding deities—sky, earth, sun, moon, and fire—one attains Brahman in the form of Kṛṣṇa by this mantra.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The question was asked, “What is uttering?” This is the answer. What is to be chanted as japa (rasyam) should be done repetitively (punar rasanam).

Water (jala) refers to the syllable k since ka means water according to the dictionary. Earth (bhūmi) refers to the syllable l since the bīja mantra of earth is laṁ. Fire (ī) refers to ī and the moon (indu) is the anusvara (since the moon and anusvara have a similar form of a crescent.) Together they form klīṁ, the kāmabīja. This is placed at the beginning of the mantra. The meaning of the bīja is Kṛṣṇa, the moon who gives joy, who is glorious with the devotees who have melted with prema.

The five limbs of the mantra are placed on the limbs of the body starting with the heart. Chanting the mantra of five parts, one directly attains Kṛsṇa (brahma). Meditating on sky, earth, sun, moon and fire as forms of the Lord in the different parts of the mantra, one attains Kṛṣṇa. One should meditate on these as vibhūtis of the Lord.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The question kiṁ rasanam (what is to be chanted) is answered with the word rasyam. After meditating, how does one utter the mantra? Rasyam means softly uttering. Rasya is a participle form of the verb ras: to utter.

The svarūpa of the mantra is shown esoterically. Water indicates ka, since ka means water. Earth (bhūmi) represents la since laṁ is the earth bīja. After those two, there is long ī. This is compared to fire since it disappears just as ī disappeared here by sandhi. Moon (indu) means anusvāra since anusvāra is round like the moon. This is placed in front of the mantra (sāmpāta). By that, the kāma-bīja becomes perfect. Kṛṣnāya is one pada (part). Gopījana is the next pada, mentioned without its declension ending. One should contemplate on the five devatās as vibhūtis of the Lord in the five parts of the mantra as one chants them, placing them on five limbs of the body.

One attains Gopāla, with his associates (brahma), who are equated with Gopāla, who is explained in this mantra with this form. It is also said that from the later words one should understand the previous word (brahma here should be taken to mean Kṛṣṇa since Kṛṣṇa was mentioned as the goal previously). Sky and earth are upper and lower limits, and sun and moon with fire are located within that. One will attain Brahman of five limbs with the manifestation of that form.

There is a manifestation of Kṛṣṇa as the shelter of all, by manifestations of sky and earth, the first two padas. By the last three padas, there is manifestation of Kṛṣṇa as the manifestor of last three items sun, moon and fire.

The first pada linked to the sky is the highest, since Kṛṣṇa’s name is most glorious. The second pada is linked to the earth since Kṛṣṇa manifests similar powers on earth. The third pada is linked to the sun since its nature is to shine more than anything else. The fourth pada is linked to the moon, since Kṛṣṇa gives joy to all by his beauty. The fifth pada is linked to fire since fire is eternally related with offering to the Lord in sacrifice.

Taking both meanings, one attains Gopāla, the supreme Brahman. This will be illustrated in the story of Brahmā and Candra-dhvaja. Repetition of the last phrase indicates that the rasana (chanting) explanation is complete.

### 1.13

*tadeṣa ślokaḥ \|*  
*oṁ klīm ity etad ādāv ādāya kṛṣṇāyeti govindāyeti ca gopī-jana-*  
*vallabhāya bṛhad-bhānavyā sakṛd uccared \|*  
*yā gatis tasyāsti maṁkṣu nānyā gatiḥ syād iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There is this verse: one who utters this mantra klīṁ kṛṣṇāya govindāya gopījana-vallabhāya svāhā will quickly attain Kṛṣṇa. He will not attain any other goal.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The method of chanting is shown. After chanting klīṁ, the words with dative endings should be chanted. Along with that, the word svāhā indicating the consort of the great manifestation (bṛhad-bhānu) – the Lord, or the fire – should be chanted. By this, one will quickly attain the goal, Kṛṣṇa, previously described, and nothing else.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The previous statement is supported. Bṛhad-bhānavyā means with the wife of fire. One who once chants this quickly (maṁkṣu) achieves the result, Kṛṣṇa, previously described. Maṁkṣu means quick according to Amara-koṣa. There is no other goal. Therefore, one should accept the chanting with firm faith, from guru.

### 1.14

*bhaktir asya bhajanaṁ tad ihāmutropādhi nairāsyenāmuṣmin*  
*manaḥ-kalpanam etad eva naiṣkarmyam iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Bhajanam or service means devotional service to Kṛṣṇa. It means concentration of the mind on Kṛṣṇa without desires for enjoyment in this life or the next. It destroys all karma.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

How does one worship the Lord? The answer is given. The word bhajana is explained. One should concentrate the mind and all senses on Kṛṣṇa (amuṣmin), while devoid of kāma (upādhinairāsyena) in this life and the next. Bhajana is remembering and hearing about Kṛṣṇa devoid of desire for material results.

“Should one not even desire liberation?” This is liberation (naiṣkarmyam). According to Padma Purāṇa, harāv aikāntikī bhaktir muktir ity abhidhīyate: unalloyed devotion to the Lord is called liberation.

Yathā-varṇa-vidhānam apavargaś cāpi bhavati: all these destinations are prescribed for the self, according to the quality of their actions, as indicated in the Vedas; liberation is then achieved. (SB [5.19.19](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/19.19))

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

This answers the question of how to worship. The characteristic of bhakti, well known in the Purāṇas to mean hearing and chanting, is worship of Kṛṣṇa (asya bhajana). One should offer the mind (kalpanam) to Kṛṣṇa (amuṣmin). This is the definition. It is also the bhāva caused by hearing and other means.

*smarantaḥ smārayantaś ca mitho ’ghaugha-haraṁ harim*  
*bhaktyā sañjātayā bhaktyā bibhraty utpulakāṁ tanum*

**The devotee, who remembers, and induces others to remember, the Lord, who takes away heaps of sin, develops symptoms like hairs standing on end by bhakti, which has sprung from bhakti. (SB** [**11.3.31**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/3.31)**)**

For achieving the goal, it should be done in this and next life. Giving up desire for happiness in this and the next world, one should take birth only with a desire for that goal. It should not be obstructed by nitya or naimittika karmas (naiṣkarmyam). The ending yam (in naiṣkarmyam) has the same meaning as without. Some say that liberation is an accompanying result.

Bhajana is worship favorable to Kṛṣṇa, unobstructed by nitya and naimittika karmas, devoid of desire for results other than Kṛṣṇa. This is the essence of attaining the states of hearing etc. Thus it is said in Nārada-pañcarātra:

*sarvopādhi-vinirmuktaṁ tat-paratvena nirmalam \|*  
*hṛṣīkeṇa hṛṣīkeśa sevanaṁ bhaktir ucyate*

**Bhakti is defined as service to the Lord using the senses. It should be done with the intention of pleasing the Lord, free of other desires, and unobstructed by other processes.**

This verse explains that it should be devoid of desires for other results (sarvopādhi-vinirmuktam). It should manifest with favorableness (tat-paratvena).

It should not be obstructed by karmas (nirmalam). It is service to Viṣṇu accomplished by the senses such as the ear. This characteristic or bhāva with favorableness is called bhakti. Anukūlya means “having a pleasing mentality when directing one’s senses to Kṛṣṇa.”

### 1.15

*kṛṣṇaṁ taṁ viprā \[brāhmaṇā\] bahudhā yajanti*  
*govindaṁ santaṁ bahudhārādhayanti*  
*gopījanavallabho bhuvanāni dadhre*  
*svāhāśrito jagad-ejayat su-retāḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Many persons following scripture worship Kṛṣṇa. Many serve him as the cowherd boy Govinda with devotion. As Gopī-janavallabha he shows mercy to the universe. Svāhā, taking shelter of the Lord, who becomes filled with great power, makes the world tremble in the currents of saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The methods of worship, results of worship, and opposite results, by chanting the padas of the mantra are shown. Many such as Nārada worship Kṛsṇa by the path of scriptures. Many worship as Govinda, when he manifests kaiśora age, by the path of rāga, such as the gopīs. As Gopī-jana-vallabha he nourishes by giving the results of bhajana (dadhre).

Māyā takes shelter of him (svāhāśritā). U in svāhāśrito (svāhāśritā + u) is an indeclinable, a vocative or a syllable to complete the meter. She makes the world tremble with the currents of saṁsāra when they do not worship him. She has great power (su-retāḥ) or possesses the prowess of the Lord.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Having taught worship using the mantra, the special qualities of the mantra are shown.

Many Vedic brāhmaṇas worship Kṛṣṇa, who is the form of knowledge and happiness, and who doesn’t manifest any particular relationships with them. Though he is in a human form, realizing that he is the supreme Lord, they worship him with many elaborate items.

Many \[other\] brāhmaṇas who have attained rāga like the inhabitants of Gokula serve Govinda, who performs pastimes as a cowherd, who manifests special relationships, with items suitable to their relationship with him.

Govinda, becoming Gopījana-vallabha, shows mercy to the worlds (dadhre). This shows his special mercy to those worshippers spread throughout the worlds in parampara.

Kṛsṇa, to whom worshippers have performed ātma-nivedana according to their bhāva, makes the universe tremble. The devotees in the world lose control, because of prema for him. He has great power (su-retāḥ) to cause them to lose control. The bīja (klīṁ) is not mentioned here since it is included in the mantra itself.

### 1.16

*vāyur yathaiko bhuvanaṁ praviṣṭo janye janye pañcarūpo babhūva \|*  
*kṛṣṇas tathaiko jagad dhitārtha śabdenāsau pañca-pado vibhātīti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as air alone, entering the earth, takes on five forms of prāṇa in various bodies, Kṛṣṇa, though one, manifests as five by his various names in order to give benefit to the universe.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The special meaning according to the mantras padas is clarified with an example. Janye means in a body. Pañcarūpaḥ means five forms of prāṇa. Pañcapadaḥ refers to the five parts of the mantra.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The special manifestation with five padas (parts) of the mantra with one meaning is shown through an example. The prāṇa enters various bodies and is designated by five names like prāṇa (prāṇa, apāna, samāna, udāna, and vyāna). It takes on five forms known by special names.

### 1.17

*te hocuḥ \| upāsanam etasya paramātmano*  
*govindasyākhilādhāriṇo brūhīti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Kumāras spoke. Tell us about the worship of Govinda, who gives life to all beings and is the shelter of all beings.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

They ask about the method of worshipping Gopāla. Akhilaādhāriṇaḥ means the shelter of all tattvas. Upāsana comes from the verb upās, to worship with a kṛt affix.

### 1.18-19

*tānuvāca \|*  
*yat tasya pīṭhaṁ hairaṇyāṣṭapalāśam ambujaṁ*  
*tadantarālikānalāsrayugaṁ*  
*tadantarādyarṇākhilabījaṁ kṛṣṇāya nama iti bījārḍhyaṁ sa*  
*brahmāṇamādhāya*  
*\[tadantarādyarṇaṁ khilavitaṁ kṛṣṇāya nama iti*  
*brahmāgām ādhāya\]*  
*anaṅga-gāyatrīṁ yathāvad vyālikhya bhūmaṇḍalaṁ śūlaveṣṭitaṁ*  
*kṛtvāṅga vāsudevādi rukmiṇyādi svaśaktīndrādi \[svaśaktīndravajrādi\]*  
*vasudevādi pārthādi nidhyādi vītaṁ yajet \[yajeta\] \|\|*  
*sandhyāsu pratipattibhir upacārais tenāsyākhilaṁ bhavaty akhilaṁ bhavatīti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā spoke to the Kumāras about Kṛṣṇa’s pīṭha (throne). One should establish the throne with a golden lotus of eight petals in whose center are two interlocking triangles. One should place the kāma-bīja, the seed of one’s actions, within that. One should write the Kṛṣṇa mantra around that. One should write the syllables of kāma-gāyatrī on the stamens and, drawing a square, surround it with eight thunderbolts. One should then worship various deities in the seven āvaraṇas. One should worship the Lord with the sixteen items, with devotional meditation, at the three junctures of the day. The worshipper will attain all four puruṣārthas by this worship.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The pītha in the form of a yantra is described. In the center are two triangles one facing up and the other down. Within it, one should write the kāma-bīja (ādhy-ākṣāram) surrounded by other syllables of the mantra (khila), along with kṛṣṇāya namaḥ and the kāma-bīja. This is six syllables. Brahma means praṇava (oṁ).

Brahmāgam ādhāya: aga means Himālaya mountain. Pārvatī is agā, having Himālaya as her father. Her bīja is hrīṁ. In pañcarātra, aṁ is Viṣṇu. Lakṣmī goes to him and is thus called agā. Her bīja is śrīṁ. Offering that bīja (oṁ and hrīm, śrīm), one should surround it with kāma-gāyatri along with kāma-bīja (anaṅga-manum). One should surround bhū-maṇḍala with spears. In the directions, one should worship the limbs (aṅga): heart, head, śikhā and weapons.

One should worship Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha, as well as Śānti, Śrī, Sarasvatī, and Rati and the nine śaktis starting with Vimalā or the eight queens starting with Rukmiṇī. One should worship Indra, etc., and the thunderbolt etc., in two āvaraṇas.

One should then worship Vasudeva, Devakī, Nanda, Yaśodā, Balarāma, and Subhadra, gopas and gopīs.

One should worship Arjuna, Nārada, Parvata, Śatha, Uddhava, Dāruka, Viṣvaksena, and Sātyaki. In front one should worship Garuḍa. One should worship the treasures indra-nīla, kumuda, nandanaka, kacchapa, makara, śaṅkha, and padma who surround the area. One should worship the Lord with the easily available leaves and flowers at the three junctures of the day.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Being asked, Brahma explains the pīṭha in the form of a yantra. He explained to them about the pīṭha of Kṛṣṇa. The following should be understood. One should establish a washed pītḥa (throne) in one’s house and place in it a golden lotus with eight petals. Or one should draw a lotus using sandalwood paste made fragrant with gandha. Within the lotus’s center one should draw two interlocking triangles (analāsra-yugam). In its center one should write the kāma-bīja, the seed of one’s actions (ādyārnākhilabījam) and the accomplisher of the result.

It is said in the Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā:

*karṇikāyāṁ likhed vahni-puṭitaṁ maṇḍala-dvayam \|*  
*tasya madhye likhed bījaṁ sādhyākhyaṁ karma-saṁyutam \|\|*

**One should draw in the center two interlocking triangles and in that draw the bīja which allows one to accomplish the result.**

One should write the six syllables kliṁ kṛṣṇāya namaḥ on the tips of the six points. This location is described in Krama-dīpikā (7.27). One should then place the eighteen-syllable mantra (sabrahmāṇam) with the kāma-bīja previously written, in the center.

*tataḥ śiṣṭair manorvarṇaistaṁ kāmaṁ veṣṭayet sudhī*

**The intelligent person should surround the kāma-bīja with the remaining syllables of the mantra.**

The mantra is called Brahmā because the mantra and its sage or devatā are non-different. Some say that one should meditate on the oneness of the devatā and the mantra.

One should write the “śrīṁ” bīja in the eastern, south-western, and north-western points of the six-pointed figure. One should write the hrīṁ bīja on the south-eastern, western and northeastern points.

*śriyaṁ ṣaṭkoṇakoṇeṣv aindraṁ nairṛtyavāyuṣu \|*  
*ālikhya vilikhenmāyāṁ vahnivāruṇa śūliṣviti \|*

**One should write the śrīṁ bīja in the eastern, southwestern, and northwestern points of the six-pointed figure. One should write the hrīṁ bīja on the south-eastern, western and north-eastern points. (Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā)**

One should write three syllables of the kāma-gāyatrī on each of the eight stamens (to make the complete twenty-four syllable mantra).

One should then write the following māla-mantra of forty-eight syllables, placing six syllables on each of the eight petals:

*namaḥ kāmadevāya sarvajana priyāya*  
*sarvajana sammohanāya jvala jvala prajvala*  
*sarvajanasya hṛdayaṁ mama vaśīkuru vaśīkuru svāhā*

**Obeisances to Kāmadeva, who is dear to everyone, and who bewilders everyone. \[O Lord, may you\] shine, shine, and brightly shine in everyone’s heart, and make me yours!**

Making a circle around the eight petals, one should surround it with the letters of the alphabet. This is explained in the Sanatkumāra-saṁhitā also:

*akṣaraiḥ kāma-gāyatryā veṣṭayet keśare sudhīḥ \|*  
*kāma-mālā-manor varṇair daleṣv aṣṭasu mantra-vit \|\|*  
*likhed guhānanair bhaktair māntrikāṁs tad-bahir likhet \|\|*

**The intelligent person should surround this with the syllables of the kāma-gāyatrī on the stamens. The knower of mantras should write the syllables of the māla-mantra on the eight petals. Outside of this he should write the syllables of the alphabet divided into six parts.**

Guhānanair bhaktair means divided into six parts.

One should then draw a square bhū-maṇḍala and surround it with eight thunderbolts (bhū-maṇḍalaṁ śūla-veṣṭitaṁ kṛtvā). This is explained in the saṁhitā as bhū-gṛhaṁ caturasraṁ syād aṣṭavajra-yutaṁ mune: O sage, there should be a square enclosure of earth, endowed with eight thunderbolts.

Since this is a yantra for wearing, one should also write the goal in the beginning. The method of wearing the yantra and its result are mentioned in the Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā:

*hutvā sahasram ājyena yantre sampāta-pūrvakam \|*  
*mārjayitvāyutaṁ hutvā dhārayed yantram uttamam \|*  
*trailokyaiśvaryam āpnoti devair api supūjitaḥ \|\|*

**After offering a thousand oblations of ghee, purifying the yantra with the ghee remnants each time, and then uttering the mantra ten thousand times to purify it, one should wear the best yantra. Worshipped by the devatās, one will attain the wealth of the three worlds.**

*yat tu pūjārthaṁ yantraṁ taccoktaṁ tasyām eva—*  
*maṇḍakādipṛthivyantaṁ pūjayet karṇikopari \|*  
*agnyādipīṭhapādeṣu dharmādīṁścaturo yajet \|\|*  
*tāravarṇa prabhinnāni maṇḍalāni kramāttataḥ \|*  
*sattvaṁ rajastama iti yajedātmacatuṣṭayaṁ \|\|*  
*ātmāntarātmā paramātmā jñānātmeti te kramāt \|*  
*vimalotkarṣiṇī jñānā kriyā yogeti pañcamī \|\|*  
*prahvī satyā tatheśānānugrahā navamī tu smṛtā \|*  
*prāgādyaṣṭasu patreṣu karṇikāyāṁ yajenmuniḥ \|\|*

**When making the yantra for deity worship however one should do as follows: One should worship devatās from Maṇḍūka to the earth in the center of the lotus.**

**One should worship adharma, ajñāna and avairāgya and anaiśvarya in the south-eastern and other directions and dharma, jñaṇa, vairāgya and aiśvarya in the eastern and other directions. One should worship the three parts of oṁ and then worship sattva, rajas and tamas and the four ātmās: jīva, all pervading ātmā, Paramātmā beyond everything, and jñānātma (intellectual function of ātmā as witness). The sage should worship Vimalā, Utkarṣinī, Jñaṇa, Kriyā, Yogā, Prahvī, Satyā, Iśānā, and Anugrahā on the eight petals and center (9 total).**

One should then place the words oṁ namo bhagavate viṣṇave sarva-bhūtātmane vāsudevāya sarvātma-saṁyoga-yaugapadyapīṭhātmane namaḥ on the lotus as instructed in the Saṁhitā.

*tataḥ pīṭhaṁ samabhyarcya devam āvāhya nārada*  
*arghyādi dhūpadīpadīsu upacārān prakalpayet*

**Worshipping the pīṭha one should call the Lord and worship him with items such as arghya, incense and lamps.**

Then one should worship the āvaraṇas or outer circles of associates. The first circle (worship of the aṅgas or bodily limbs) is described with the word aṅga.

On four of the six points worship the limbs (aṅga): heart, head, śikhā and arms on the south-east, south-west, north-west and north-east. In front, worship the eyes, and in east, south, west and north directions worship the weapon with astrāya phaṭ.

The second circle is indicated with the word vāsudeva. One should worship Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha, on the east, west, south and north petals respectively. In the southeast, south-west, north-west and north-east worship Śānti, Śrī, Sarasvatī and Rati.

The third circle is indicated with the word rukmiṇī. This refers to Kṛṣṇa’s śaktis (sva-śakti). They are worshipped on the petals. The auspicious Rukmiṇī, Satyabhāmā, Jāmbavatī, Nāgnajitī, Mitravindā, Kālindī, Lakṣmaṇā and Bhadrā should be worshipped on the petals.

Worship Vasudeva, yellow in color in the east. Worship Devakī, blackish in the south-east. Worship Nanda, white and yellow in the south. Worship Yaśodā, gold and red in the south-west. Worship Balarāma, white like a conch, a jasmine flower or the moon, in the west. Worship in the north-west Subhadrā, dark like a peacock feather. Worship cowherds in the north and gopīs in the northeast.

Some accept the colors of Devakī and Yaśodā in reverse as sequence given in Krama-dīpika. But Gautamīya-tantra states as above.

The fifth circle is indicated by pārthādi. These are Arjuna, Niśaṭha (son of Balarāma), Uddhava, Dāruka, Viśvakṣena, Sātyaki, Nārada and Parvata.

The sixth circle has the treasures. In the east is Indra-nidhi, in the south-east is Nīla-nidhi. In the south is Kunda. In the south-west is Makara. In the west is Anaṅga. In the northwest is Kacchapa. In the north is Śaṅkha. In the north-east is Padma-nidhi.

The seventh circle is indicated by indrādi. Indra, yellow in color, is on the eastern petal. Agni, red in color, is on the south-eastern petal. Yama, blue like a lotus, is in the south. Nairṛti, black in color, is in the south-west. Vāyu, grey, is in the west. Varuṇa, white, is in the north-west. Kuvera, blue in color, is in the north. Īśāna, white in color, is in the north-east. Between the east and north-east is Brahmā, red like a palāśa flower (representing upwards). Between south-west and west is Śeṣa, white in color (representing downwards).

The eighth circle has Vajra, yellow, on the eastern petal. Respectively there are Śakti, white in color, Daṇḍa, blue in color, Śaṅkha, white in color, Pāśa (noose), lightning color, Dhvāja, red in color, Gadā, blue in color, and Triśūla, white in color.

According to different branches the versions of the text are different. One should consult the relevant text and explanations.

The result of worshipping Kṛṣṇa is described. One should worship the Lord with five or sixteen items (pratipattibhiḥ), at the three junctures of the day. Another version has atipattibhiḥ: one should worship with what is easily available such as tulasī and flowers. By this worship, all desires will be fulflled. Repetition indicates that the worship is completed.

### 1.20

*tad iha ślokā bhavanti—*  
*eko vaśī sarvagaḥ kṛṣṇa īḍya eko’pi san bahudhā yo vibhāti \|*  
*taṁ pīṭhasthaṁ ye nu bhajanti dhīrās teṣāṁ sukhaṁ śāśvataṁ netareṣām \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa is supreme, all-pervading, and most worthy of praise. Though he is one, he is also many. The wise who worship him in the pīṭha obtain constant happiness not available to others.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Approval of this mantra in the above worship is described. The form of Svayam Bhagavān, dependent on no other form, is described. He is svayam bhagavān (eko mukhyaḥ) as stated in kṛṣṇas tu bhagavān svayam (SB [1.3.28](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/3.28)).

He is the controllor of prakṛti, time, and all others (vaśī). He is all pervading as vibhu. He is praised because of that (iḍyaḥ). Though one, and not leaving that oneness, he manifests eternally as many such as Nārāyana.

Manifesting all qualities, Nārāyaṇa, his vilāsa form is generally equal to him, but having slightly less qualities, such forms are his vyūha, svāṁśa and kalās. Those particularly inquistive should see bhāṣya-pīṭhaka. Those who worship the Lord situated on the pīṭha previously described attain eternal happiness.

### 1.21

*nityo nityānāṁ cetanaś cetanānām eko bahūnāṁ yo vidadhāti kāmān \|*  
*taṁ pīṭhagaṁ ye’nubhajanti dhīrās teṣāṁ siddhiḥ śāśvatī netareṣām \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The wise, who worship Kṛṣṇa in his pīṭha—Kṛṣṇa who is the principal being among many, who is the chief eternal among eternals and the chief knower among knowers, who satisfies their prema—attain eternal bliss not available to others.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Because he is the Lord, he is the chief eternal. Because he has the chief spiritual form, he is the chief of the eternal jīvas.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Since he gives auspicious results, he gives liberation. Those Vedic brāhmaṇas at the present time who worship Kṛsṇa established in the pīṭha, who, worshipped by many jīvas who are eternal and conscious beings like Brahmā, bestows what is desired, attain liberation (siddhiḥ). This indicates that Kṛṣṇa with human-form is eternally liberated.

### 1.22

*etad viṣṇoḥ paramaṁ padaṁ ye nitya-yuktāḥ saṁyajante na kāmān*  
*\[kāmāt\] \|*  
*teṣām asau goparūpaḥ prayatnāt prakāśayed ātmapadaṁ tadaiva \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**For those who constantly worship Kṛṣṇa, the supreme svarūpa in the pīṭha, and who have no other desires, Kṛṣṇa in his cowherd form reveals his feet.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Padam means svarūpa. Ātma-padam means his feet.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He gives himself to those who are completely dedicated to Kṛṣṇa. To those who worship with affecion the previously mentioned pīṭha and do not desire other things (kāmān), Kṛṣṇa shows his feet and his Gokula (ātma-padam).

Tadaiva indicates that without regard for the path of arcirādi, he leads the devotee personally. This is stated by the writer of the sūtras. viśeṣaṁ ca darśayat In exceptional cases, the Lord takes the devotee. This is shown in scripture. (Brahma-sūtra [4.3.16](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/4.3.16))

### 1.23

*yo brahmāṇaṁ vidadhāti pūrvaṁ yo vai*  
*vidyās tasmai gopāyati sma kṛṣṇaḥ \|*  
*taṁ ha devam ātma-buddhi-prakāśaṁ*  
*mumukṣur vai śaraṇam anuvrajeta \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa created Brahmā and preserved knowledge of the eighteensyllable mantra for him. Those who desire to give up everything should surrender to Kṛṣṇa, the revealer of all knowledge, who is endowed with all pastimes.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Vidadhāti means he created. Ātma-buddhi-prakāśam means he reveals the functions of one’s intelligence.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Having shown the beginningless nature of Kṛṣṇa previously described, it is now explained that by his mercy, the worshipper’s desires are fulfilled.

Vidadhāti means he produces. He protected the four Vedas, including the eighteen-syllable mantra (vidyāḥ). He gives knowledge to jīvas like Brahmā (ātma-buddhi-prakāśam).

Tvatto jñānaṁ hi jīvānāṁ pramoṣas te ’tra śaktitaḥ: from you alone through your energy, the jīva’s knowledge arises and disappears. (SB [11.22.28](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/22.28))

This destroys the doubt of the unintelligent who say that Kṛṣna appeared from Garbhodakaśāyī in Dvāpara-yuga in the twentheighth yuga cycle of Vaivasvata manvantara in the Śveta-varāha kalpa.

### 1.24

*oṁkāreṇāntaritaṁ ye japanti govindasya pañca-padaṁ manuṁ taṁ \|*  
*teṣām asau darśayed ātmarūpaṁ tasmān mumukṣur abhyasen nityaṁ śāntyai \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To those who utter the five-part mantra of Govinda enclosed at both ends by oṁ, the Lord shows his form. Therefore, those who desire to give up all other desires repeatedly chant this mantra for attaining the eternal abode.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

One attains the results immediately by chanting the mantra Section One – Pūrva Vibhāga 35 enclosed by oṁ. Antaritaṁ means enclosed in. Those who desire liberation, who desire to give up everything contrary to service, repeat this mantra, for attaining Kṛsṇa’s eternal abode devoid of dualities.

### 1.25

*etasmād anye pañcapadād abhūvan govindasya manavo mānavānāṁ*  
*daśārṇādyās te’pi saṁkrandanādyair abhyasyante bhūtikāmair yathāvat \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Other mantras of Govinda appeared from this five-part mantra for men with various desires. The ten-syllable mantra and other mantras were repeated by Indra and others to fulfill their respective desires.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Manavaḥ means mantra. The mantras were repeated by Sanaka and others desiring liberation and Nārada and others desiring bhakti (saṅkrandanadyaiḥ).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He speaks of the manifestation of other mantras from the king of mantras. The mantras were manifested for jīvas having various desires. Saṁkrandanādyair means Indra and others.

### 1.26

*yad etasya svarūpārthaṁ vācāvedayeti te papracchuḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Kumāras asked “Please reveal results arising from the svarūpa of the mantra by words.”**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

They asked “Please reveal (āvedaya) things which arise from the svarūpa of the mantra.”

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The sages ask another question. Reveal by words the purpose of śakti which manifests the whole unvierse situated in the svarūpa of the king of mantras.

### 1.27

*tad uhovāca brāhmaṇaḥ*  
*asāvanavarataṁ \[brahmasavanaṁ carato\] me dhyātaḥ stutaḥ*  
*parārdhānte so’budhyata \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā then explained. When I passed the first half of my life in meditating on and praising the Lord, after that, the Supreme Lord awoke to my presence.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

What did he say? I meditated and praised Gopāla. When? At the end of first half of his life at the end of padma-kalpa.

*tasyaiva cānte kalpo ‘bhūd yaṁ pādmam abhicakṣate*

**The kalpa at the end of the first half of Brahmā’s life is called the Pādma-kalpa. (SB** [**3.11.36**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/11.36)**)**

When I meditated on and praised him, he carefully manifested himself (abudhyata).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Brahmā spoke to the sages for this purpose. O sages! Gopāla, Kṛsṇa (asau), was meditated on constantly by me and was praised by me. After half my life, by his mercy, he was known to me (abudhyata).

### 1.28

*gopaveśo me purastād āvirbabhūva*  
*tataḥ praṇato mayānukūlena hṛdā*  
*mahyam aṣṭādaśārṇaṁ svarūpaṁ sṛṣṭaye dattvāntarhitaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He appeared before me wearing the dress of a cowherd. I offered sincere respects. By his mercy, he gave me the eighteen-syllable mantra for creating the universe and then disappeared.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

I offered respects.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Then dressed as a cowherd, holding a flute and horn, that puruṣa appeared before me. He appeared to my eyes. With a merciful heart (anukūlena hṛdā), for creation, he gave the mantra which was his svarūpa, himself, filled with power to create the whole universe. According to my bhāva, he gave me another great result.

### 1.29

*punaḥ sisṛkṣato \[sisṛkṣā\] me prādurabhūt teṣv*  
*akṣareṣu bhaviṣya jagad-rūpaṁ prakāśayan \[prakāśayat\] \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When I desired to create, he again appeared and revealed the future universe within the syllables of the mantra.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Then he revealed in the syllables of the mantra the universe in a subtle form. Kṛṣṇa personally revealed this.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Later when I desired to create and meditated, he again appeared. He manifested directly. He revealed the form of the future world in a subtle form in the syllables of the mantra. He made it visible to me.

### 1.30

*tad iha kād āpo lāt pṛthivī īto’gnir bindor indus tat-saṁpātātad arka*  
*\[tasya nādād arkaḥ\] iti oṁ klīṁ kārād asṛjaṁ \|*  
*kṛṣṇāyād ākāśaṁ khād vāyur ity*  
*uttarāt surabhiṁ vidyāḥ prādurakārṣaṁ \|*  
*tad uttarāt strī-pumādi cedaṁ sakalam iti sakalam iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**From k arose water. From l arose earth. From ī arose fire. From m arose the moon and from the nāda, arose the sun. From kṛṣṇāya I created ether and from ether came air. From govindāya I created cows. From gopījana-vallabhāya I created the fourteen types of knowledge. From svāhā I created men, women and moving and non-moving beings.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

From ka within the kāma-bīja, by the power of chanting k, water appeared. From l earth appeared. From ī fire appeared. From anusvāra, the moon appeared. From the nāda of that sound, the sun appeared. From ether, air arose (verb is understood). From govindāya, cows appeared. From gopījana appeared fourteen types of knowledge. From vallabhāya arose men and women. The Lord is the creator but Brahmā says that he creates. This shows his nondifference from the Lord, since the servant is also called the puruṣa, just as the king’s servant is sometimes addressed as the king.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He created the universe just as it was revealed to him. When the universe was revealed in the mantra (tad iha) I created the five elements from klīṁ which gives rise to all elements together and individually: water from k, earth from l, fire from ī, the moon from m and the sun from the combination klīm.

From kṛsṇa (of kṛṣṇāya), I created ether. From the suffix ya I created air. Thus from kṛṣnāya came two elements. From the word govindāya I created the desire cow. From that I manifested the fourteen types of knowledge. From gopījana I created men and women, the whole universe. Repetition indicates the completion of the explanation.

“It is said everywhere that the Lord is the creator of all tattvas. Then why is it said that Brahmā is the creator?” That is true. In the universe, Brahmā creates the tattvas like water on their attaining transformation in secondary creation.

### 1.31

*etasyaiva yajanena candradhvajo gatamoham ātmānaṁ vedayitvā oṁkārāntarālikaṁ manum āvartayan saṅga-rahito’bhyāpa tat \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By worship using the mantra, Candradhvaja, free of illusion, realized ātmā. Chanting the mantra enclosed with oṁ using concentration, he saw the Lord.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Realizing his own ātmā while being free of illusion, chanting the mantra enclosed by oṁ, he directly attained the Lord’s abode (tat).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

It is shown that the main result of the mantra appeared elsewhere also. The sage and king called Candradhvaja, destorying ignorance, realizing himself as ātmā, as a servant of the Lord, becoming detached, chanted the mantra enclosed in oṁ and attained Gopāla, known through the mantra. Viśveśvara Bhaṭṭa explains that Candradhvaja is Śiva.

### 1.32-33

*tad viṣṇoḥ paramaṁ padaṁ sadā paśyanti*  
*sūrayaḥ divīva cakṣur ātatam \|\|*  
*tasmād etan nityam abhyasen nityam abhyased \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The devotees always see the supreme abode of Kṛṣṇa, which spreads in space like the sun. Therefore, one should constantly chant the mantra.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The details of the abode attained by Candradhvaja is explained. Devotees see Goloka, the abode superior to even Vaikuṇtḥa (paramaṁ padam), with no interruption in time (sadā). Where? The place spreads out like the sun (cakṣuḥ) in the sky. The word cakṣuḥ indicates the sun as in the following, namaḥ savitre jagadeka-cakṣuṣe: I offer respects to the sun, the sole eye of the universe (found in sandhyā-vandanam prayers).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He explains the place of Gopāla which Candradhvaja attained. The devotees see the supremely beautiful place Gokula of Gopāla who pervades everywhere (viṣṇoḥ). Nānārtha-varga explains that padam means a place. Sūrayaḥ refers to the nitya-muktas, who have eternally eradicated ignorance since they see this place always. It also refers to those who became liberated by sādhana. Since there are many devotees there endowed with powers and qualities like the Lord, there is no need of moonlight to see within the forest.

What is the nature of that place? It is like the sun in the sky. It is radiant like the sun, full of intense knowledge. The word cakṣuḥ indicates the sun as in the following, namaḥ savitre jagadekacakṣuṣe: I offer respects to the sun, the sole eye of the universe. Ātatam means it spreads out unlimitedly. He attained Kṛsṇa with his associates (dhāma). Therefore one should chant this mantra (etam).

### 1.34

*tad āhur eke \|*  
*yasya prathama-padād bhūmir dvitīya-padāj jalaṁ tṛtīya-padāt tejaś*  
*caturtha-padād vāyuś carama-padād vyometi vaiṣṇavaṁ pañca-*  
*vyāhṛtimayaṁ mantraṁ kṛṣṇāvabhāsakaṁ kaivalya-sṛtyai satatam*  
*āvartayed iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some give this explanation of the mantra. From the first part of the mantra, earth arises. From the second part, water arises. From the third part, fire arises. From the fourth part, air arises and from the last part, ether arises. One should continually chant this Vaiṣṇava mantra of five parts which brings about the appearance of Kṛṣṇa for attaining pure bhakti.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Another opinion is given. Vaiṣṇavaṁ pañca-vyāhṛtimayam means the five parts of the mantra explained. Vyāhāra means what is uttered. This mantra, made of those five parts, reveals Kṛsṇa. One should chant this mantra for attaining the path of pure bhakti (srtyai), unmixed with jñāna and karma (kaivalyena).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

It was explained that from k came water etc. Sometimes creation takes place in the following manner. Some knowers of the mantra (eke) say this. The meaning is clear. The mantra has power to produce everything everywhere. Vaiṣṇavyaḥ pañca vyāhṛtayaḥ means the mantra with five parts previously explained which reveals Kṛṣṇa in order to attain the path of liberation (kaivalya).

### 1.35-36

*tad atra gāthāḥ—*  
*yasya pūrvapadād bhūmir dvitīyāt salilodbhavaḥ \|*  
*tṛtīyāt teja udbhūtaṁ caturthād gandhavāhanaḥ \|\|*  
*pañcamād ambarotpattis tam evaikaṁ samabhyaset \|\|*  
*candradhvajo’gamad viṣṇoḥ paramaṁ padam avyayaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Concerning this there are verses: From the first part of the mantra came earth. From the second part came water. From the third part arose fire. From the fourth part arose air. From the fifth part arose ether. One should repeat this mantra alone. Candradhvaja attained the supreme abode of Kṛṣṇa by this means.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

This meaning is explained through verses. He attains the abode by chanting the mantra.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

There are verses concerning this mantra (tatra). Gāthā means a verse according to Medinī-kara. Since Candradhvaja was a very great devotee, his story is again mentioned.

### 1.37

*tato viśuddhaṁ vimalaṁ viśokam aśeṣa-lobhādi nirasta-saṅgaṁ \|*  
*yat tat-padaṁ pañcapadaṁ tad eva sa vāsudevo na yato’nyad asti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The abode is pure sattva, without contamination, without lamentation, without unlimited greed. The abode is the mantra of five parts and is Vāsudeva, since nothing exists separately from him.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Viśuddham means special purity, where there are spiritual planets. Vimalam means devoid of contamination by māyā. Viśokam means without lamentation because it is not temporary.

It is devoid of any contact with greed etc. That abode called Goloka is the eighteen-syllable mantra of five parts. That mantra is Vāsudeva, since no object other than Vāsudeva exists.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The place is described. Because of the great glories of the place, it is the object of meditation. What is its nature? It is a spiritual, pleasurable place (viśuddham). It is devoid of material contamination (vimalam), and thus without lamentation. It is devoid of association with greed and other defects. It is again described. It is none other than the mantra of five parts. It is Vāsudeva, Kṛṣna, known by words. Śiva says vasanād eva vāde ca vāsudeveti śabdita: the word vāsudeva means “he dwells in words.” (Hari-vaṁśa) These three (abode, mantra and person) are worshipable as one. These words are spoken for those desiring the truth since nothing exists except Vāsudeva. Everything is Vāsudeva since everything depends on him, he pervades everywhere, and everything is his śakti. This is explained in detail in the bhāṣya-pīthika.

### 1.38

*tam ekaṁ govindaṁ saccidānanda vigrahaṁ*  
*pañcapadaṁ vṛndāvana surabhūruha talāsīnaṁ*  
*satataṁ sa-marud-gaṇo’haṁ paramayā stutyā toṣayāmi \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Along with the devatāṣ, I eternally satisfy Govinda, a form of eternity, knowledge and bliss, non-different from his mantra, seated under a desire tree in Vṛndāvana, by offering praise.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Sa-marud-gaṇaḥ means “with the devatās.” Marut means devatā according to Amara-koṣa.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

At the end, Brahmā teaches the sages about his object of meditation. Along with the devatās I satisfy Govinda, nondifferent from the five part mantra. Śrīdhara says that marut means devatā.

This praise incorporates two manifestations as one. Kṛṣṇa, in his abode of Gokula above the three portions of Vaikuṇtha, shines eternally, but sometimes manifests with his abode and associates in this material world.

Within the material world he plays in Gokula in a manifested and unmanifested form. When the material world disappears, that place joins with the place situated above Vaikuntha.

### 1.39

*oṁ namo viśvarūpāya viśvasthityantahetave \|*  
*viśveśvarāya viśvāya govindāya namo namaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Govinda the form of the universe, the cause of maintenance and destruction of the universe, the Lord of the universe, nondifferent from the universe.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

I offer respects to Govinda who has his beautiful form within the universe (viśvāya).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Twelve verses are composed because of the great beauty of the combination of power and sweetness. Viśvarūpam means he produces the form of the universe. He is nondifferent from the universe (viśvāya) by his śaktis of jīva and prakṛti.

### 1.40

*namo vijñānarūpāya paramānanda rūpiṇe \|*  
*kṛṣṇāya gopīnāthāya govindāya namo namaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Govinda, Kṛṣṇa, master of the gopīs, the form of realization, the form of prema.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Vijñāna means the bliss of brahman. Paramānanda means prema.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Govinda is praised as the shelter of all, including the jīvas.

### 1.41

*namaḥ kamala-netrāya namaḥ kamala-māline \|*  
*namaḥ kamala-nābhāya kamalā-pataye namaḥ \|\| (38)*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Kṛṣṇa with lotus eyes, with lotus garland, with lotus navel, who is the master of the gopīs.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Kamalā-pataye namaḥ means I offer respects to the master of the gopīs, for it is said lakṣmī-sahasra-śatasambhrama-sevyamānam: Kṛṣṇa is served by hundreds of thousands of gopīs. (Brahmā-saṁhitā [5.29](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/29)). Also it is said śriyaḥ kāntāḥ kāntaḥ paramapuruṣa: Kṛṣṇa is the supreme lover and the gopīs are his beloveds. (Brahma-saṁhitā [5.56](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/56)).

### 1.42

*barhāpīḍābhirāmāya rāmāyākuṇṭha-medhase \|*  
*ramā-mānasa-haṁsāya govindāya namo namaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Govinda, with a peacock feather ornament, most attractive in form, who is also the form of Balarāma, with unlimited intelligence, who is like a swan in the mind of the gopīs.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Rāmāya means the form of Baladeva.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He is attractive with a peacock feather on his head. He has the form of Balarāma (rāmāya).

He has full knoweldge, being omniscient (akuṇṭha medhase). He is a swan in the mānasa-sarovara lake of the mind of the gopī (ramā) Rādhā. This is the ornament of rūpaka (metaphor). Rādhā is Mahā-lakṣmī (ramā).

*dve pārśve candrāvalī rādhikā ca yasyā*  
*aṁśo lakṣmī durgādikā śakti*

**On Kṛṣṇa’s two sides are Candrāvalī and Rādhikā. Lakṣmī, Durgā and others exist. They are all his expansions. (Puruṣa-bodhinī śruti)**

*devī kṛṣṇamayī proktā rādhikā paradevatā \|*  
*sarvalakṣmīmayī sarvakāntiḥ saṁmohinī pareti*

**Rādhā, non-different from Kṛṣṇa, is the supreme devatā. All Lakṣmīs reside within her. She has all beauty, bewilders everyone and is supreme. (Gautamīya-tantra)**

### 1.43

*kaṁsavaṁśa vināśāya keśi cāṇūra ghātine \|*  
*vṛṣabha-dhvaja-vandyāya pārtha sārathaye namaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the destroyer of Kaṁsa’s dynasty, to the killer of Keśī and Cāṇūra, to the driver of Arjuna, to Govinda, worshipped by Śiva.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He is worshipped by Śiva because he pulled Śiva’s chariot during Section One – Pūrva Vibhāga 47 the battle with Tripura and because he defeated Śiva in battle. Situated on Arjuna’s chariot, by his glance of time, he destroyed unlimited enemies or gave liberation to the enemies he killed.

### 1.44-45

*veṇuvādanaśīlāya gopālāyāhita mardine \[gopālāyāhimardine\] \|*  
*kālindīkūlalolāya lola-kuṇḍala-valgave \|\|*  
*vallavīvadanāmbhoja māline nṛtya-śāline \|*  
*namaḥ praṇata pālāya śrī-kṛṣṇāya namo namaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Kṛṣṇa, who plays a flute, who herds cows, who defeats Kāliya, who is eager for the banks of the Yamunā, who possesses swinging earrings, who wears a lotus garland of the eyes of the gopīs, who dances continually, and who protects those who are surrendered.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Lola-kuṇḍala-valgave means he is very attractive with swinging earrings. He has a garland of lotuses of the gopīs’ eyes since their eyes fall upon him like a garland.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He is attractive with priceless, beautiful pearl earrings. Though the son of a king and lord of all lords, he is fond of dancing and is fond of playing the flute. Singing and dancing are not inferior since they are seen in Śiva, Bharata and Arjuna. Singing and dancing are also not faults in the gopīs since this is also seen in Pārvatī and Uttarā. Singing and dancing for a living is not similar to this; but singing and dancing for taste is similar, especially when offered to the object of love it attains excellence.

Cow-herding also is not inferior since it is following sva-dharma for the person designated as yajña-puruṣa. It is accomplished using cows. He has a garland of the gopīs’ lotus-like glances since the glances reflect on his form which shines like a mirror as they gaze at him every day.

### 1.46

*namaḥ pāpa praṇāśāya govardhana dharāya ca \|*  
*pūtanā jīvitāntāya tṛṇāvārtāsuhāriṇe \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the destroyer of sin, the lifter of Govardhana, the killer of Pūtanā and Tṛnāvarta.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He destroys sin or ignorance when he is glorified or remembered.

### 1.47

*niṣkalāya vimohāya śuddhāyāśuddha-vairiṇe \|*  
*advitīyāya mahate śrī-kṛṣṇāya namo namaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Kṛṣṇa, who wears a niṣka ornament, who is devoid of illusion, who is pure, who is the destroyer of the devotee’s impurities, who is without equal, and who is therefore great.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

He is without māyā (niṣkalāya). Or he receives (la) a chest ornament called niṣka. He cannot be bewildered by Brahmā, Śiva and others (vimohāya). He is pure, though accepting others’ wives. He destroys the impurities in persons absorbed in hearing his pastimes (aśuddha-vairiṇe). He is without a second, since no one else is the supreme controller.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Niṣkalāya means he wears a padaka ornament (niṣka). This meaning is given by Medinīkara. He destroys ignorance (vimohāya), gives knowledge. He destroys the bhāva of demons. This means he gives liberation to those he kills. Because there is no one equal to him, he is great (mahate).

### 1.48

*prasīda paramānanda prasīda parameśvara \|*  
*ādhivyādhi bhujaṅgena daṣṭaṁ mām uddhara prabho \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O supreme bliss, be pleased with me. O supreme Lord! Be pleased with me. O master! Deliver me, since I have been bitten by the snake of internal and external pain.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

I am bitten by the snake of mental pain, because of not attaining my desires, and physical pain because of getting sick. Please bring me to your lotus feet (uddhara).

### 1.49

*śrī-kṛṣṇa rukmiṇī-kānta gopījana-manohara \|*  
*saṁsāra sāgare magnaṁ mām uddhara jagad-guro \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, dear to Rukmiṇī! O Kṛṣṇa, attractive to the gopīs! O guru of the universe! Deliver me, who have fallen in the ocean of saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

You are attractive to the gopis. You attract their minds by your colorful dress and ornaments and by your favorable actions.

### 1.50

*keśava kleśa-haraṇa nārāyaṇa janārdana \|*  
*govinda paramānanda māṁ samuddhara mādhava \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Keśava, destroyer of suffering! O Nārāyaṇa! O Janārdana! O Govinda, full of bliss! O Mādhava! Please deliver me.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Keśava means the creator of Brahmā and Śiva. Śiva says to the Lord in Hari-vaṁśa:

*ka iti brahmaṇo nāma īśo ’haṁ sarva-dehinām*  
*āvāṁ tavāṅga-sambhūtau tasmāt keśava-nāma-bhāk*

**Ka means Brahmā, and Īśa refers to me, the lord of all embodied beings. Both of us arise from Your body. Therefore, you are called “Keśava.”**

Janārdana means he who is entreated by people desiring the highest benefit.

### 1.51

*atha haivaṁ stutibhir ārādhayāmi yathā yūyaṁ tathā pañcapādaṁ*  
*japantaḥ kṛṣṇaṁ dhyāyantaḥ saṁsṛtiṁ*  
*tariṣyatheti hovāca hairaṇyaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā thus said: Just as I have worshipped with prayers, you should chant this mantra of five parts, meditate on Kṛṣṇa, and you will cross saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Brahmā taught bhakti to his sons. Just as I worship by such praises, you, Sanaka and others should also do. The rest is clear.

### 1.52-53

*amuṁ pañcapadaṁ mantram āvartayed yaḥ*  
*sa yāty anāyāsataḥ kevalaṁ tat padaṁ \|\|*  
*anejad ekamanaso javīyo naitad devā āpnuvan pūrvam arśad iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who repeats this mantra of five parts easily attains the pure abode of the Lord. The Lord does not move. He is beyond the powers of the mind. The devatās cannot attain him. He pervades everything in time and space.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Thus he speaks. He does not tremble but he is swifter than the mind. This is a contradiction. But though he does not move, he is faster than the mind. He is beyond the powers of Brahmā. The devatās like Brahmā and Śiva do not know him (etat) since he arose before mahat-tattva and ahaṅkara and before Brahmā and Śiva (pūrvam arśat). One should consider the statement bahu syām: may I shall become many. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad)

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

A modern person who surrenders to guru, who gives him life easily without effort, attains the pure (kevalam) abode of the Lord. The abode is described. It does not move. It is beyond the impure mind. Devatās cannot understand it. It existed before all other things. The recent cannot know the ancient.

### 1.54

*tasmāt kṛṣṇa eva paro devas taṁ dhyāyet taṁ*  
*raset taṁ bhajet taṁ yajed ity oṁ tat sad iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, Kṛṣṇa is the supreme being. One should meditate on him, utter his name, worship him, and serve him.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Kṛṣṇa is superior to even Brahman (tasmāt). Gītā says brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāham: I am the basis of Brahman. (BG [14.27](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/14.27)) Bhāgavatam says:

*madīyaṁ mahimānaṁ ca paraṁ brahmeti śabditam*

**You will realize my power known as the impersonal Brahman. (SB** [**8.24.38**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/24.38)**)**

Raset means one should glorify or chant. Rasayet is another version. One should taste his sweetness. Bhajet means one should serve by fanning etc. Yajet means one should worship by offering pādya etc.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

He summarizes. Because of his great glory, how can he be described? Oṁ indicates that the phrases of the mantra are identical with vedic words such as oṁ.

Meaning of oṁ tat sat: oṁ ityetad brahmaṇo nediṣṭaṁ nāma - Oṁ is another name of Brahman (Ṛg Veda); tat tvam asi - You are that Brahman (Chāndogya Upaniṣad); sad eva saumya - O gentle one! Brahman alone existed (Chāndogya Upaniṣad). iti means the teaching is completed.

## Section Two – Uttara Vibhāga

### 2.1-2

*ekadā hi vrajastriyaḥ sakāmāḥ śarvarīm uṣitvā*  
*sarveśvaraṁ gopālaṁ kṛṣṇam hi tā ūcire \|\|*  
*uvāca tāḥ kṛṣṇaḥ \[kṛṣṇam anu\] \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Once the women of Vraja, desiring his constant association, inquired from Gopāla, the Supreme Lord, after spending the night with him. Kṛṣṇa replied to them.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

In the first section, the mantra and worship of Gopāla were explained. In the second section, the sweet and majestic abodes of the gopīs and Kṛṣṇa who are the subject of the mantra are described. The gopīs desired (sakāmāḥ) to achieve unimpeded pastimes of the rāsa dance. Or they had prema (sakāmaḥ).

*premaiva gopa rāmāṇāṁ kāma ity agamat prathām*

**The kāma-rūpa-bhakti of the gopīs has become famous simply as prema.**

They had spent the night with Kṛṣṇa in the rāsa dance. Sarveśvaram means he was the enjoyer of all the gopīs. Gopālam means he gave happiness to all the senses (go). After that Kṛṣṇa spoke to them. Ucire and uvāca indicate the speakers, the gopīs and Kṛṣṇa.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

*nityaṁ nivasatu hṛdaye caitanyātmā murārir naḥ \|*  
*niravadyo nirvṛtimān gajapatir anukampayā yasya \|\|*

**May Murārī, whose essence is Caitanya, by whose mercy the king became faultless and blissful, live in my heart eternally.**

It was determined that Kṛṣṇa personally resides in Kṛsṇa-loka, which shines above Vaikuṇtha, appearing differently because of different followers. In Dvārakā and Mathurā there are pastimes of a king and in Gokula there are pastimes of a cowherd. Brahmā described him, established firmly with mantra and associates, to the sages in the first section of the Upaniṣad. Sometimes Kṛṣṇa, thinking of performing pastimes in the material world, manifests there his places and associates. To teach in another way what was previously described, in the place called Gokula in Mathurā, this second section of the Upaniṣad is presented.

A very attractive pastime of Kṛṣṇa with the gopīs is described. The Vraja gopīs headed by Rādhā, desiring only Kṛṣṇa, having passed the night, having performed pastimes with him, giving bliss to him with pastimes of the rāsa dance, asked the Lord, the hero of all of them, in the early morning.

Kṛṣṇā said to them, “What do you ask?”

### 2.3

*anu kasmai brāhmaṇāya bhakṣyaṁ \[bhaikṣyaṁ\] dātavyaṁ bhavati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which brāhmaṇa should we give food?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

What did they say? What did he say? They first spoke to Kṛṣṇa (anu).

Bhaikṣyaṁ means four types of food for giving by one’s will. Out of shyness they did not say the purpose of offering the food: it was to accomplish the pastimes of the rāsa dance with Kṛṣṇa.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

They speak. To whom should food be given (dātavyam) by us who are together (anu). Anu means “with” according to Viśva. To which brāhmaṇā should we give food so that our desires will be fulfilled?

### 2.4

*durvāsaseti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Give food to Durvāsā.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Kṛṣṇa said give food to Durvasā. Durvāsasā should actually be durvāsase.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Kṛṣṇa spoke to them. The sandhi is poetic license, for meter. It should be durvāsase. The meaning of giving food to Durvāsā is this. “Durvāsā knows about me in truth through my Upaniṣad taught by Brahma, Sanaka and Nārada. He is my devotee and lives at the edge of Vraja. Since he is without desires and very austere, they should acknowledge him.” He will explain what you desire.

### 2.5

*kathaṁ yāsyāmo’tīrtvā jalaṁ yamunāyā yataḥ śreyo bhaviṣyati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**How can we cross the water of the Yamunā so that we can attain benefit from Durvāsā?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The gopīs speak.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Seeing that the Yamunā was high, they spoke. Since they had to cross the river, it is understood that they were on the opposite side.

### 2.6

*kṛṣṇeti brahmacārītyuktvā mārgaṁ vo dāsyati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After saying the words Kṛṣṇa and brahmacārī, the Yamunā will give you a path.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Kṛṣṇa spoke. Standing there, say, “Kṛṣna is a brahmacārī.” Kṛṣṇeti is for meter. Yamunā will give you a path.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Kṛṣṇā spoke to them. Saying to Yamunā, “Kṛṣṇa is a brahmacārī,” she will give you a path who are standing there.

### 2.7

*yaṁ māṁ smṛtvāgādhā gādhā bhavati*  
*yaṁ māṁ smṛtvāpūtaḥ pūto bhavati*  
*yaṁ māṁ smṛtvāvratī vratī bhavati*  
*yaṁ māṁ smṛtvā sakāmo niṣkāmo bhavati*  
*yaṁ māṁ smṛtvāśrotrīyaḥ śrotrīyo \[śrotrī śrotrī\] bhavati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Remembering me, a deep river becomes shallow. Remembering me, the impure becomes pure. Remembering me, the person without vratas obtains the results of all vratas. Remembering me, a person with desire loses all desires. Remembering me, a person with no ears develops ears.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Remembering me, deep Yamunā becomes shallow up to the knee for you, standing here. That is not the only power of remembering me. The impure becomes pure. The Vedas without desire, become afflicted with desire.1 A person without ears or any senses, becomes endowed with senses, just as Śuka became liberated. Speechless he became able to speak.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

How is this possible? Remembering me, the deep Yamunā becomes shallow. Agāḍham means bottomless according to Amara-koṣa. This is not the only result. There are other great results.

Impure demons become pure and liberated. A person devoid of vrata attains the result of vratas. A person desiring sons, animals, gold etc, becomes devoid of such desires. Remembering me destroys all other desires. A person who has not studied the Vedas becomes knowledgeable of the Vedas. By the power of remembering me, the Vedas manifest in him as it happened for Lord Brahmā (before the beginning of secondary creation).

Another version has niṣkāmaḥ sakāmo bhavaty aśrotrīṁ śrotrī bhavati. The ātmārāma develops desire for me, like the sages of Daṇḍakāraṇya. A person devoid of ears becomes endowed with ears since his hairs stand on end by hearing the flute.

### 2.8

*śrutvā tad vācaṁ hi vai smṛtvā raudraṁ tadvākyena tīrtvā tat sauryāṁ*  
*hi vai gatvāśramaṁ puṇyatamaṁ hi*  
*natvā muniṁ śreṣṭhatamaṁ hi vai raudrañceti*  
*dattvāsmai brāhmaṇāya kṣīramayaṁ ghṛtamayaṁ*  
*miṣṭatamaṁ ha vai \[vi pustake nāsti\] \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Remembering the words and remembering Durvāsā, they crossed the Yamunā using those words. They went to his pure hermitage and offered respects to the best of sages. They gave the brāhmaṇa sweet rice cooked with milk and ghee.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

They said, “O Yamunā! If Kṛṣṇā is a brahmacārī, then give us a path.” Immediately on saying this, they crossed the Yamunā and offered respects to Durvāsā, an aṁśa of Śiva (raudram) and stood there.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Hearing the Lord’s words, with smiles and astonishment, they obeyed his instructions and acted. Hearing Kṛṣṇa’s words which indicated his greatness, becoming enthusiastic on seeing him, remembering Durvāsā was to be seen, they said “If Kṛsṇa is a brahmacārī, give us a path.” They crossed Yamunā, daughter of the sun god, who gave them a path by making the water shallow to the knees. They went to the pure āśrama and offered respects to the excellent sage Durvāsā. Giving the rice mixed with ghee and milk to the brāhmana, they stood there in silence.

### 2.9

*tuṣṭaḥ satvābhuktvā hi tvāśiṣaṁ prayujyānvājñāṁ tv adāt \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Satisfied, he ate it completely. Rejecting some small remnants and giving them blessings, he told them to return home.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Durvāsā was endowed with intelligence (satva). These gopīs, dear to Kṛṣṇa, have given me various types of rice cooked by their own hands, giving me great good fortune. I will eat it. This was his thought. Satisfied, after eating, not leaving anything, he gave blessings. Hi and tu are two words or are added to complete the sentence. I have eaten food cooked by your hands. May you have long life, free of sickness. May your hand produce nectar. Giving this blessing, he then gave them permission to go home.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Durvāsā ate the food completely (ābhuktivā) out of great affection. Then leaving some remnants on the plate, he performed ācamana, washing his hands and feet; he was very satisfied. He gave them blessings of being able to cook sweet food and that they would be the object of Kṛsṇa’s mercy. He then gave them permission to go. He did not show any crookedness as with Rukmiṇī and others2 . This indicates their great position.

### 2.10

*kathaṁ yāsyāmo’tīrtvā sauryāṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**How will we go home, crossing the river?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

How will we go, crossing the Yamunā? The river had again become deep.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Seeing the Yamunā was with deep water they spoke to the sage.

### 2.11

*sa hovāca—muniṁ*  
*durvāśinaṁ māṁ smṛtvā mārgaṁ vo dāsyatīti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sage spoke clearly. Remembering me, who eats only durva grass, the river will give you a path.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Durvāsā spoke clearly. Remembering me who eats only durva grass (durvāsīnam), the river will give a path. Durva instead of dūrva, irregular like the word pṛṣodara.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Durvāsā spoke. Remembering me, the sage who is detached from eating, eating only the juice of dūrva grass, she will give a path to you, standing here. The short vowel in durvāśinam is for metrical reasons.

### 2.12

*tāsāṁ madhye śreṣṭhā gāndharvīty uvāca \|*  
*taṁ ha vai tābhir evaṁ vicārya \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Among them, the best woman named Gāndharvī spoke to the sage after consulting with her friends.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

She discussed with the gopīs. We saw that he enjoyed the sweet rice with great joy. And Kṛṣṇa enjoys with us. How does Dūrvāsā not eat anything other than durva grass and how is Kṛṣṇa a brahmacārī? We should ask him the truth about this.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

When the sage spoke, among the gopīs, Rādhā, since she was the king’s daughter and the aṁśī of all Lakṣmīs, consulted with her friends and then spoke to the sage. She is called Gāndharvī since she sometimes, as an aṁśā, is born in a family of gandharvas. Though she knew the truth, she asked in order to relish hearing it.

### 2.13-14

*kathaṁ kṛṣṇo brahmacārī kathaṁ dūrvāśano muniḥ \|\|*  
*tāṁ hi mukhyāṁ vidhāya pūrvam anu kṛtvā tūṣṇīm āsuḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**How can it be said that Kṛṣṇa is a brahmacārī? How can it be said that the sage has eaten only durva grass? Taking her as the chief person and coming in front of him, they remained silent.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

After consulting she spoke to the sage. Kṛṣṇa is very attached to us. How can he be a brahmacārī? How can the sage who alone had eaten food that is supposed to be eaten by a thousand brāhmaṇas, can be said to eat only durva grass?

What did the other gopīs do? Taking Gāndharvī as their chief, and going before Durvāsā, they remained silent.

### 2.15-19

*śabdavān ākāśaḥ śabdākāśābhyāṁ bhinnas tasminn ākāśe tiṣṭhati sa hy*  
*ākāśas taṁ na veda sa hy ātmāhaṁ kathaṁ bhoktā bhavāmi \|\|*  
*sparśavān vāyuḥ sparśavāyubhyāṁ bhinnastasmin vāyau tiṣṭhati*  
*vāyurna veda taṁ sa hy ātmāhaṁ kathaṁ bhoktābhavāmi \|\|*  
*rūpavad idaṁ hi tejo rūpāgnibhyāṁ bhinnastasminnagnau tiṣṭhatyagnir*  
*na veda taṁ sa hy ātmāhaṁ kathaṁ bhoktā bhavāmi \|\|*  
*rasavatya āpaḥ rasādbhyo bhinnastāsv apsu tiṣṭhati taṁ hy āpo na viduḥ*  
*sa hy ātmāha kathaṁ bhoktā bhavāmi \|\|*  
*gandhavatīyaṁ bhūmi gandhabhūmibhyāṁ bhinnastasyāṁ bhūmau*  
*tiṣṭhāti bhūmirna veda taṁ sa hy ātmāhaṁ kathaṁ bhoktā bhavāmi \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\[Durvāsā spoke:\] Ether is endowed with sound. The ātmā is different from both. It is situated in the ether. The ether does not know the ātmā. How can I, the ātmā be the enjoyer?**

**Air is endowed with touch. The ātmā is different from both. It is situated in the air. The air does not know the ātmā. How can I – the ātmā – be the enjoyer?**

**Fire is endowed with form. The ātmā is different from both. It is situated in the fire. The fire does not know the ātmā. How can I – the ātmā – be the enjoyer?**

**Water is endowed with taste. The ātmā is different from both. It is situated in the water. The water does not know the ātmā. How can I – the ātmā – be the enjoyer?**

**Earth is endowed with fragrance. The ātmā is different from both. It is situated in the earth. The earth does not know the ātmā. How can I – the ātmā – be the enjoyer?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Durvāsā spoke. How can I, the ātmā, different from sound and ether, be the enjoyer? Not being absorbed in these things, enjoyment with the ear is then forbidden. The same is understood for touch etc.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

“He spoke” is understood from the context. He thought, “Rādhā, having doubt whether Kṛsṇa is a brahmacārī and that I, a jīva and his amśa, do not eat, asks. I should answer both questions by clarifying the nature of one’s svarūpa.” Sound, multiplying itself, becomes ether. The ātmā, different from unconscious sound and ether, remains situated in the body, starting from the state of saṁsāra, in the ether endowed with sound.

The ether, which is a support, does not know the ātmā, supported by it. How can I, the ātmā different from it, be the enjoyer? Because the pure ātmā is not absorbed in it, it is forbidden to enjoy sound with the ear. The jīva situated in saṁsāra, absorbed in material body, senses and prāṇa, enjoys material sound etc. but this is not so for the pure jīva. For that reason, such enjoyment is forbidden. Enjoyment characterized by experiencing happiness of oneself and the Lord is the quality of the pure ātmā. In this way the verses should be explained.

### 2.20

*idaṁ hi manas teṣv eva hi manute tān idaṁ gṛhṇāti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The mind, situated in the senses, thinks according to the particular sense. The mind accepts ether and other elements as well as their senses.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

This mind experiences (manute) being situated in the ear etc. The experience is explained. The mind accepts the sense objects like sound, along with lamentation, illusions etc. from those sense objects.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

How is there a perception that “I enjoy this rāga, I am hearing this rāga?” The mind has knowledge as its quality and is situated as the ruler of the senses like the ear. It accepts sound etc., through the ear for the jīva, its ruler. The jīva, the ruler of the mind, thinks, “I am the enjoyer of the rāga.” This explains being how the ātmā which has received a material body becomes the enjoyer of material rāga.

### 2.21

*yatra sarvam ātmaivābhūt tatra vā kutra manute kva vā gacchatīti*  
*sa hy ātmāhaṁ kathaṁ bhoktā bhavāmi \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the ātmā becomes manifest to the realizer of ātmā, what does he experience, and what does ātmā attain? At that time how can a person think that he is the enjoyer or that the ātmā goes anywhere?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Sound etc. experienced in the mind becomes ātmā (at time of realizing ātmā). Though situated in the mind, how does ātmā experience itself at that time? What state does it attain (gacchati)? Mind does not know it. How can I, the ātmā, enjoy? What does ātmā experience during deep sleep? What does it attain in samādhi?

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

In the liberated state, the enjoyment, the bliss of the ātmā and the Lord follows one’s decision. The jīva takes on the body of an associate by parā-śakti instituted by the decision of the Lord and attains knowledge arising from ātmā. That is expressed in this verse.

In the liberated state (yatra) how can one think that ātmā and paramātmā are merely the body and senses (sarvam)? In that state how does the ātmā experience enjoyment in objects like sound? How does he act as an enjoyer of that object? He does not experience truth in that object since it lacks truth. He does not interact with it at all.

The Upaniṣad says that there is only enjoyment of the ātmā \[available to it at liberated state, and it can’t enjoy anything else\]. However, the liberated ātmā becomes an enjoyer by the mercy of the Lord, and attains a form \[to enjoy\].

*yam evaiṣa vṛṇute tena labhyas tasyaiva*  
*ātmā vivṛṇute tanuṁ svām*

**The Lord is attained by the person whom the Lord choses. He reveals his (the devotee’s) own form to that person. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad)**

The tāṇḍins3 say that spiritual bodies in the form of Lord’s associates, endowed with grandeur in the spiritual sky was shining previously. The liberated ātmā merges into one of these forms.4

*aśva iva romāṇi vidhūya pāpaṁ śarīramakṛta kṛtātmā \|*  
*brahmalokam abhisambhavānīti \|*

**He is like a horse, shaking off all sin. The realized person attains the spiritual world with a spiritual body. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad)**

Thus it’s said in the Bhāgavatam,

*vasanti yatra puruṣāḥ sarve vaikuṇṭha-mūrtayaḥ \|*  
*ye’nimitta nimittena dharmeṇārādhayan harimiti*

**In that place all men reside with forms like the Lord, and they worship the Lord with unmotivated service. (SB** [**3.15.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/15.14)**)**

This being the case, devoid of absorption in the material body, maintaining himself like a snake in his skin, identifying self as ātmā, how can I be an enjoyer of material food? I cannot. The food you gave however is not material but full of spiritual rasa. Eating that, I have become an associate in the spiritual sky. However I eat dūrva grass to imitate an austere person.

### 2.22

*ayaṁ hi kṛṣṇo yo vo hi preṣṭhaḥ śarīra*  
*dvaya-kāraṇaṁ bhavati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This Kṛṣṇa, who is dear to you, is the cause of the two bodies (individual and total).**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

I, a liberated jīva, an ātmārāma, enjoying because of prārabdhakarma, am not an enjoyer because of having no bodily identification. I actually do not eat durva grass or sweet rice. He then answers how Kṛṣṇa can be a brahmacārī. He is the cause of the gross and subtle bodies. He is the creator through his svāṁśa (expansion).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Since he as a jīvan-mukta is not absorbed in the body, being unavoidably controlled, he imitates a man of austerity, not overcome by the qualities of the body, by hunger and thirst. This was stated.

Kṛṣṇa, is a brahmacārī, since he is detached from thirst for material objects, because of bliss, because he is full of bliss as the supreme Lord. In order to discard other meanings of the word Kṛṣṇa, the statement ‘he is dear to you’ is made. Since he is the cause of the gross and subtle bodies, he is not related to the qualities of the bodies.

### 2.23

*dvā suparṇau bhavato brahmaṇo ’ṁśa-bhūtas tathetaro*  
*bhoktā bhavati anyo hi sākṣī bhavati*  
*bhoktā’bhoktārau vṛkṣa-dharme tiṣṭhataḥ*  
*pūrvo hi bhoktā bhavati tathetaro ’bhoktā bhavati \[kṛṣṇo bhavati\] \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Two conscious entities exist. The jīva, an aṁśa of the antaryāmī, thinks he is the enjoyer. The other, the antaryāmī, is the witness. The two reside in the tree of dharma. One is the enjoyer, and one is not the enjoyer. The jīva is the enjoyer. The other, Kṛṣṇa, is not the enjoyer.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

There are two birds jīva and paramātmā. Of the two the jīva is an aṁśa of Brahman. Gītā says mamaivāṁśo jīvaloke jīvabhūtaṁ: the jīva is my aṁśa in the world of jīvas.

The inferior one, the jīva is the enjoyer. The other, excellent Paramātmā, is the witness of all and not the enjoyer. The enjoyer and the non-enjoyer, jīva and Paramātmā, remain in the tree of the body.

The irregular word formation of bhoktābhoktārau is peculiar to Vedas.

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

“It is said that the puruṣāvatara Garbhodakaśayī is the cause of the body not Kṛṣṇa.” There are two birds. Who are they? One is the aṁśa of Kṛṣnā, dear to you, svayam bhagavān, Brahman, having great qualites. He is an enjoyer, greedy for material enjoyment. Different from him is a svāṁśa form Garbhodakaśayī, a puruṣāvatāra, full of bliss who is the witness. He is not greedy for enjoyment like the jīva.

*svāṁśaś cātha vibhinnāṁśa iti dvedhāṁśa iṣyate \|*  
*aṁśino yattu sāmarthyaṁ yatsvarūpaṁ yathāsthiti \|*  
*tadeva nāṇumātro’pi bhedaḥ svāṁśāṁśinoḥ kvacit \|*  
*vibhinnāṁśo’lpaśaktiḥ syāt kiñcit sāmarthyamātrayugiti*

**There are two types of aṁśas: svāṁśa and vibhinnāṁśa. The svāṁśa does not differ at all from the aṁśī in powers, nature and position. The vibhinnāṁśa has little power and only some ability. (Varāha Purāṇa)**

Bhoktā instead of bhoktṛ in bhoktā’bhoktārau is Vedic usage. The body has the quality of a tree: it is liable to be cut. The jīva is the cause of knoweldge in the body. This statement is repeated for emphasis.

### 2.24

*yatra vidyāvidye na vidāmo vidyāvidyābhyāṁ bhinno*  
*vidyāmayo hi yaḥ sa kathaṁ viṣayī bhavati iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**We do not know vidyā and avidyā in Kṛṣṇa since he is beyond māyā. Since he is filled with cit-śakti, how can he be a material enjoyer?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Kṛṣṇa is superior to even Paramātmā, since he is the aṁśī. We do not know knowledge and ignorance, functions of māyā in Kṛṣṇa (yatra) since he is different from knowledge and ignorance: he is beyond māyā, since (hi) he possesses a svarūpa of knowledge. He possesses cit-śakti.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Saṅkarṣaṇa etc. the three avatāras, as Kāraṇodakaśayī etc., deal with matter as the regulators of the effects of prakṛti.

But Vāsudeva, the fourth member of the vyūha, does not touch māyā. This is clear in Nārāyaṇīya and other scriptures. Since even Garbhodakaśayī and others do not enjoy material rasa since they are filled with bliss as Kṛṣṇa’s aṁśas, without doubt Kṛṣna, their aṁśī, is a brahmacārī, not being an enjoyer at all. That is explained here.

We do not know the functions of māyā, knowledge and ignorance in svayam bhagavān Kṛṣṇa, dear to you. He is their controller. He is different from knowledge and ignorance. He is not touched by them. Kṛṣnā is filled with real knowledge. How can he be a material enjoyer. He cannot be greedy for the taste of material sound etc.

### 2.25-26

*yo ha vai kāmena kāmān kāmayate sa kāmī bhavati \|\|*  
*yo ha vai tvakāmena kāmān kāmayate so’kāmī bhavati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who desires objects with desire to enjoy them is an enjoyer.**

**He who desires objects without a desire to enjoy them is not an enjoyer.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Brahmā and others are filled with desire (kāmī) by minds filled with desire. But Kṛṣṇa is not a kāmī because his mind is filled with prema (akāmena). Thus, though he enjoys with you, he is a brahmacārī.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

“We, his lovers, experience him as an object of desire. How can the sage deny this?” The devatā or human, any living being, who desires to enjoy objects such as sound and touch of a young woman which pierce his nerve centers, by Cupid, servant of Indra, is called an enjoyer (kāmī).

The word tu indicates a different subject. Tu means difference according to Amara-koṣa. But (tu) Kṛṣṇa is different from all others. Kṛṣṇa, who desires sound and touch (kāmena) but by something resembling kāma (akāmena), anointed with prema, is called akāmī, a brahmacārī.

The following should be said. It is understood from statements like ānanda-cinmaya-rasa (Brahma-saṁhitā [5.37](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bs/37)), ballavyo me madātmikāḥ (gopīs devoted to me – SB [10.46.6](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/46.6)) and verse [2.71](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.71) starting with vraja-strī spoken by me, you – arising from his svarūpa-śakti – offer your limbs to Kṛṣṇa, your soul, with a desire for happiness. Attracted to you who arise from him, he enjoys those limbs. Both of you are ātmāramas. This I know. Thus Kṛṣṇa is a brahmacārī. Without doubt, you desire only the happiness of Kṛṣṇa, for you have said:

*yatte sujāta caraṇāmburuhaṁ staneṣu*  
*bhītāḥ śanaiḥ priya dadhīmahi karkaśeṣu \|*  
*tenāṭavīm atṣasti tad vyathate na kiṁsvit*  
*kūrpādibhir bhramati dhīr bhravad āyuṣāṁ naḥ*

**O dearly beloved! Your lotus feet are so soft that we place them gently on our breasts, fearing that your feet will be hurt. Our life rests only in you. Our minds, therefore, are filled with anxiety that your tender feet might be wounded by pebbles as you roam about on the forest path. (SB** [**10.31.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/31.19)**)**

Kṛṣṇa says to Arjuna:

*nijāṅgam api yā gopyo mameti samupāsate*

**The gopīs worship me while thinking of their bodies as mine. (Ādī Purāṇa)**

His quality is to engage in action with gopīs who arise from Brahman as his svarūpa-śakti. Their quality is to act for Kṛṣṇa, Brahman who is the svāṁśī. Thus what appears to be pastimes of material lust are qualities of being ātmārāma. The pastimes are pure.

Though there is no difference in the Lord as understood from neha nānāsti kiñcana (in the Lord there is no variety – Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad [4.4.19](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/4.4.19)), there is an appearance of difference in the love of the gopīs and the Lord who gives and accepts their limbs, because of viśeṣa as in “existence exists, and time exists at all times.”

### 2.27

*janmajarābhyāṁ bhinnaḥ sthāṇur ayam acchedyo’yaṁ yo’sau saurye*  
*tiṣṭhati yo’sau goṣu tiṣṭhati yo’sau gopān pālayati yo’sau gopeṣu tiṣṭhati*  
*yo’sau sarveṣu vaideṣu tiṣṭhati yo’sau sarvair vedair gīyate yo’sau sarveṣu*  
*bhūteṣv āviśya bhūtāni vidadhāti sa vo hi svāmī bhavati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who is beyond birth and old age, who is steady in form, qualities, pastimes and abode, who is indivisible, who resides on the bank of the Yamuna, who lives among the cows, who protects the cows, who lives among the cowherds, who resides in all the Vedas, who is praised in all the Vedas, and who, entering all beings, makes them special, is none other than the master of you gopīs.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

“If he is beyond māyā, why is it seen that he undergoes states of māyā like birth, infancy etc.?”

He does not have a birth related to dhātus like a jīva but makes an appearance only. And he never grows old. His infancy, paugaṇḍa and kaiśora periods are spiritual. He remains fixed for all time, devoid of death in old age (sthānuḥ). The reason is that he is uncut by time etc. He stays in the water of Yamunā to play and stays among the cows to herd them. He stays among the cowherds to play with them. He stays among the Vedas to prove what they say. He enters among all beings, to inspire them to work and to give results. That Kṛṣṇa is your master (svāmī): he is qualified to enjoy with you. Pāṇinī says svāminn-aiśvarye (svāmī means lordship), just as one says this brāhmaṇa is the svāmī (ruler) of this land. It does not mean husband. One should not explain the sentence as “He is your husband.”

In Gokula, Kṛṣna had not received upanayana and thus was unqualified for marriage. One should not say that after killing Dantavakra, Kṛṣṇa came to Vraja and married them. Thus the meaning is “He is your master.” What was said agrees with what was explained.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Explaining the meaning of the mantra of Gopāla previously given, he explains Kṛṣṇa’s supreme position. The first phrases indicate that he is beyond the six transformations. He is eternal. He is fixed in his svarūpa (sthānuḥ), with special qualities. He dwells in the sun globe (saurye) as antaryāmī, the golden puruṣa, who has risen above all sins. He destroys all sins of the worshippers. In this way the word Kṛṣṇa is explained.

Then the word govinda is explained. He lives among the cows and protects and maintains them in a special way. In this way the word govinda is explained similar to the previous explanation gobhūmi-veda-vidita.

The meaning of the third word (gopī-jana-vallabha) is explained. Hi is for emphasis. He is the eternal lover of you gopīs.

Some say that saurye means on the Yamunā. He stays at a place not far from the Yamunā, at Vṛndāvana, which is on both banks. He is near the Yamunā. Thus Bhiṣma has given the Lord the name Su-yāmuna (viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma).

### 2.28

*sā hovāca gāndharvī—kathaṁ vā’smāsu jāto’sau gopālaḥ kathaṁ vā*  
*jñāto’sau tvayā mune kṛṣṇaḥ ko vā’sya mantraḥ kiṁ vā’sya sthānaṁ*  
*kathaṁ vā devakyāṁ jātaḥ ko vā’sya jyāyān rāmo bhavati kīdṛśī*  
*pūjā’sya gopālasya bhavati sākṣāt prakṛtiparo’yam ātmā gopālaḥ kathaṁ*  
*tv avatīrṇo bhūmyāṁ hi vai \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Gāndharvī then spoke. Why was this cowherd born among us? How is Kṛṣṇa known to you? What is his mantra? Where is his residence? How was he born to Devakī? Who is his elder brother Balarāma? How does one worship Gopāla? Why has this Gopāla, who is beyond prakṛti, made his appearance on earth?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Sā gāndharvī is Śrī Rādhā.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Pleased on hearing about her desired goal, Rādhā asked details. According to Viśva (dictionary) the word katham can mean deliberation or reflection as well as a question.

One should reflect on his birth among us of human type. According to the qualities described, he is understood from which guru? (i.e. from which guru did you learn about him?) What is his excellence by his birth from Devakī? The word devakī indicates the wife of Nanda as well as the wife of Vasudeva. There is a well known statement from Ādi Purāṇa, dve nāmnī nandabhāryāyā yaśodā devakī: Yaśodā and Devakī are two names of Nanda’s wife. Śukadeva also says Kṛṣṇa was born from both.

*niśīthe tama udbhūte jāyamāne janārdane \|*  
*devakyāṁ devarūpiṇyāṁ viṣṇur sarva-guhāśayaḥ \|*  
*āvirāsīd yathā prācyāṁ diśīndur iva puṣkalaḥ \|\|*

**Then the Lord, complete with all his expansions, who is situated in the core of everyone’s heart, responding to the request of his devotees, appeared from Devakī, nondifferent from the Lord, in the dense darkness of night, like the full moon rising on the eastern horizon. (SB** [**10.3.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/3.8)**)**

In this verse the word Devakī indicates both, like a lamp on a threshold5.

*yaśodā nanda patnī ca jātaṁ param abudhyata \|*  
*na tal liṅgaṁ pariśrāntā nidrayāpagata smṛtiḥ*

**Exhausted by the labor of childbirth, Yaśodā was overwhelmed with sleep and unable to understand what kind of child had been born to her. (SB** [**10.3.53**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/3.53)**)**

Another version of SB [10.3.53](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/3.53) has na tad veda: she did not know. Yaśodā, wife of Nanda, did not know that the surpeme Lord was born from her womb as he was born from the wife of Vasudeva. She was unaware of the signs of Vasudeva coming there \[to place Kṛṣṇa near her\]. She did not know that he had come since he had already left \[Vraja to Kaṁsa’s prison\]. In Ādi Purāṇa, Nārada says nanda-gopa gṛhe putro yaśodā-garbha-sambhavaḥ: the son appeared in the womb of Yaśodā in Nanda’s house. Thus statements speaking of the son of Nanda and Yaśodā are very correct. The objection that he was called the son of Yaśodā so that Vasudeva could hide his son is refuted. Such statements were made to fool Kaṁsa. Because the son of Devakī and the son of Yaśodā became one, after going to Mathurā as one son, he later returned to Vraja as one son. There is no possible doubt since the meaning is clear. The description given by Śukadeva is suitable following the secretive nature of the Lord.

“He may be the son of Yaśodā by thinking like that, but why be related to her womb?” This was the reality there. Otherwise, one could also say the same about his being the son of Devakī. This gives rise to another doubt. Why did the Lord, complete in himself, appear among humans (bhūmyām), beyond being born to Devakī?

Ko vā means “Is he Saṅkarṣaṇa?”

### 2.33-36

*sa hovācābjayoniḥ—*  
*avatārāṇāṁ madhye śreṣṭho’vatāraḥ ko bhavati*  
*yena lokās tuṣṭā devās tuṣṭā bhavanti*

*tāsāṁ madhye sākṣād brahma gopāla-purīti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā spoke. Who is the best among the avatāras, by whom all people and devatās become satisfied, by whose remembrance people become liberated from saṁsāra? How can this avatāra be Svayam Bhagavān?**

**Nārāyaṇa spoke to Brahmā. Just as there are seven delightful cities on top of Meru, there are seven cities which bestow spiritual opulence and liberation on earth. Among them is the city of Gopāla, which is directly Brahman.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Brahmatā means being the greatest position, Svayam Bhagavān.

He is eternally situated with you, and so the first question “why was he born among us?” In text [2.28](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.28) is not answered. Similarly, it is impossible for everyone to know him. Thus the second question, “how is he known to me?” is not answered. The third question “what is his place (kiṁ vā’sya sthānam)?” is answered by the statement of Nārāyaṇa. He spoke to Brahmā (tam). Sākṣāt brahma (directly Brahman) means it is devoid of change. Sakāmyā means the places have people who have desires.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Brahmā asked his question.

O Lord! Many avatāras appear in the material world. Among them, which avatāra will be the best, manifesting all his śaktis? He indicates the qualities which will indicate his superiority. By him all brahmarṣis, rājarṣis, and devatās like Indra will be satisfied. Remembering him, all beings, having devotee association, will be delivered from saṁsāra.

“How can the best avatāra described by you to me be Svayam Bhagavān, endowed with great qualities (brahmatā)? His qualities will be the same since his form depends on yours.” Svayam bhagavān has a form which is independent.

“How is he born among us? How was he born to Devakī? What is his mantra?” In answer to these questions (asked in verse 28), it was said he is beyond birth and old age (verse 27). I have thus answered you already. Devoid of transformations like birth, He plays with the cowherds and gopīs in Gokula. At the end of Dvāpara yuga, he sometimes appears in Devakī in his complete form, like the moon rising in the east. By his attractive pastimes he gives bliss to the sādhaka devotees and increases his own bliss.

By your words you understand about his birth, but you ask in order to gain a special taste by hearing from me. The eighteen syllable mantra was revealed by him. I will give the answers to the remaining questions such as the places, through understanding the conversations of Brahmā and Nārāyaṇa.

The answer is now given about the places.

There are seven cities on top of Meru, which is called the spiritual sky because it destroys sin and is very high. According to Śrīdhara (dictionary), Meru means a mountain, a peak, excellence and power. These places manifest on earth for pastimes by the Lord’s will.

*yā yathā bhuvi vartante pūryo bhagavataḥ priyāḥ \|*  
*tās tathā santi vaikuṇṭhe tat-tal-līlārtham ādṛtāḥ \|\|*

**Just as there are cities on earth dear to the Lord, there are cities in Vaikuṇṭha for his pastimes. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

The places are full of enjoyable items for those desiring the rasa of his pastimes. They are devoid of enjoyable items for those who desire only liberation. Then the desired item is explained. The city of Gopāla is directly Brahman since it belongs to the Lord and manifests great qualities. One should not take Meru as a golden mountain in this verse. The cities of Brahmā and the eight dikpālas are not described.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The place is free of transformations that take place on earth. Because of this, the city of Gopāla, who is Parabrahman with human form, is the best, the highest in the spiritual sky. The city manifests all śaktis and is thus the complete Brahman.

Outside the city there are forests. It is called Bṛhad-vana because it is huge and because it was the place of Kṛṣṇa’s first pastimes. At Madhu-vana, the Madhu demon was killed. Tāla-vana has many tāla trees. Kāmya-vana is so called because there the devotees desire Kṛṣṇa. Bahulā was a wife of the Lord according to Skanda Purāṇa. Since she resides there the place is called Bahulā-vana. arśa ādibhyo’c: the ending ‘a’ indicates the possessor of that object. Many lotuses bloom in Kumuda-vana. There are many khadira trees in Khadira-vana. Balarāma (balabhadra) plays in Bhadra-vana. There is a banyan tree called bhaṇḍīra in Bhāṇḍiravana. Śrī-vana has a forest of śrī fruit (āmalakī). Lauha-vana was protected by Lauha-jaṅgha. The last two are not defined by the scripture. Vṛndāvana is presided by the deity Vṛndā.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

After describing the city, the forests of the city are described. It is called Bṛhad-vana because it is vast. The demon Madhu previously stayed in the forest. Thus it is called Madhu-vana. Tāla in the singular indicates a species. There are many tāla trees in Tāla-vana. Kāmya means very attractive. Kṛṣṇa played there. It is called Bahulā-vana because it has a huge area and Bahulā stayed there. The long ā in Bahulā-vana is similar to the name Miśrakā-vana (Indra’s pleasure garden).

Kumuda-vana is so called because lotuses always blossomed there. There are many khadira trees in Khadira-vana. Balabhadra plays in Bhadra-vana. At Bhāṇḍira-vana there is a banyan tree called bhāṇḍira with jewels shining on its sides. Śrī-vana is the forest of Lakṣmī. She did penance there, desiring Kṛṣṇa. The demon Loha-jaṅgha stayed in Lohavana previously. Vṛndā is the presiding deity of Vṛndāvana. It is placed last because it is the place of many very sweet pastimes. It is the best of all. Because the city is surrounded with these twelve forests, it is very great and most attractive.

Each deity form was established individually in the forests grouped in threes (thus the four deities were established in all the twelve forests). Mathurāsu (in Mathurā) modifies vaneṣu (in forests). The twelve forms exist in all the forests since the forests constitute Mathurā.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

In order to understand Kṛṣṇa as Svayam Bhagavān, his identity as the catur-vyūya is explained. There is a deity of Balarāma called Rāma. This is not his secondary form as Śeṣa. This is an answer to the question “Who is his elder brother?” (asked in Verse [2.28](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.28)). Vāsudeva is not mentioned separately since he is included in Kṛṣṇa.

They are in the forests within Mathurā area. They are located there, but not all of them everywhere. Four are located in Mathurā. In Vṛndāvana and other forests, forms of Balarāma and Kṛṣṇa are present.

*ekāṁ hi rudrā yajanti dvitīyāṁ brahmā yajati tṛtīyāṁ brahmajā*

**Перевод:**

**The Rudras worship the first form. Brahmā worships the Section Two – Uttara Vibhāga 83 second form. Brahmā’s sons worship the third form. The Maruts worship the fourth form. The Vināyakas worship the fifth form. The Vasus worship the sixth form. The sages worship the seventh form. The Gandharvas worship the eighth form. The Apsarās worship the ninth form. The tenth form is invisible. The eleventh form is in the spiritual sky (his own abode). The twelfth form is on the earth.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The first form, that of Balarāma is situated in Bṛhad-vana. It is worshipped on Śiva’s planet. The second form, Pradyumna is situated in Madhu-vana and is worshipped on Brahma-loka. The third deity, Aniruddha is situated in Tāla-vana and the knowers of Brahmā worship him on Satya-loka.

The Maruts on Deva-loka worship the fourth form, a deity of Kṛṣṇa situated in Kāmya-vana. The fifth form again is Balarāma, situated in Bahulā-vana. The sixth form is again Pradymuna, situated in Kumuda-vana. The seventh form is again Aniruddha, situated in Khadira-vana. The eighth form is again Kṛṣṇa situated in Bhadra-vana. The ninth form is again Balarāma situated in Bhāṇḍira-vana. The tenth form in Śrī-vana is Pradyumna but has disappeared. That form never manifests and thus has no worshippers. The eleventh form Aniruddha is situated in Lauhavana but has gone to Dvārakā or Śvetadvīpa. The twelfth form is a deity of Kṛṣṇa, manifesting on earth. They worship the seventh form. The tenth form is invisible (antardhāne). This deity is the presiding deity of Vṛndāvana and remains on earth.

Ekādaśam means the eleventh form. This form also is invisible. The twelfth form is famous as Govinda. Out of his great mercy, producing great auspiciousness, he is visible to the people of earth.

The enumeration is for making a total of twelve, and does not indicate a sequence of deity and worshipper, as this and the previous list of worshippers (verse 41) differ. The third list (Uttara tāpanī, verse 95) gives the names of the deities with the worshippers.

### 2.49

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Just as an insect by meditating on a wasp becomes a wasp, by absorption in the Lord one attains sārūpya. One should worship the Lord with intense absorption in his svarūpa and qualities. The Lord teaches meditaton on himself with that intention. One who becomes absorbed in the object of meditation attains sārūpya. There is oneness in the mind such as saying “Bhadrasena is my very self.”

One becomes a pure particle of consciousness beyond prakṛti. One considers “I am he.” The sādhaka understands that he is ātmā. Becoming absorbed in Gopāla, his master, the aṁśī, he meditates “I am Gopāla.” Later it is said manasaikyam kuryāt: one should become one in the mind. A person thinking in this way attains liberation. He attains eight qualities of the Lord such as freedom from sin (brahmatvam). This was taught to Brahmā. Then one attains the svarūpa of Gopāla, Brahman.

### 2.50-53

*gopān jīvān vai ātmatvenāsṛṣṭiparyantam ālāti*

**He is called Gopāla because he accepts the jīvas as himself from the beginning to the end of creation. I identify with Gopāla who is oṁ. Kṛṣṇa is the supreme Brahman, full of eternal bliss. I identify with him, the supreme Brahman. Gopāla is the highest truth. One should think of the self and identify the self with Gopāla in the mind. One should think “I am Gopāla.” But Gopāla does not show himself to all. He is unlimited and eternal.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The word gopāla is explained according to those desiring liberation. One who protects the senses (go) or one who drinks or tastes sense objects like sound is called gopī, the jīva. The Lord accepts (āla) these jīvas as himself from creation till the end. That Gopāla is oṁ, tat and sat. These words indicate paraṁ brahma. He is the form of Kṛṣṇa (kṛṣṇātmakaḥ). “Aren’t forms material?” The form of Bhagavān is eternal bliss. It cannot be material. na tu bhūta-mayasya: are you not the cause of all entities? (SB [10.14.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/14.2))

Since he has a form of eternal bliss, gopāla is unrefuted as the supreme (param abādhitam). He is the real object (satyam). He then teaches the method of jñāna with so’ ham. Taking the jīva by the mind, one should produce oneness of the jīva with so ‘ham. One should think “I am Gopāla.” He is invisible, devoid of birth and form. He is without destruction (ananta). One should attain sāyujya with him. He attains that status of oneness easily.

*anejadekaṁ manaso javīyo*  
*naitad devā āpnuvan pūrvam ārśad*

**The Lord is without fear, supreme, faster than the mind.**

The devatās cannot understand him completely, but he has understood everything since eternity. (Īśopaniṣad 4)

In the previous section it was said:

*tasmāt kṛṣṇa eva paro devas taṁ dhyāyet*  
*taṁ rasayet taṁ yajet taṁ bhajet*

**Therefore Kṛṣna is the supreme Lord. One should meditate upon him, chant his mantra, worship and serve him (verse** [**1.54**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/1.54)**).**

Gītā says brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāham: I am the basis of Brahman. Those who know that Kṛṣṇa is superior to Brahman do not desire sāyujya with Brahman. The great devotees endowed with dāsyabhāva, tasting sweetness in prema also meditate on “I am Gopāla” in worship. But in deriving the etymological meaning of Gopāla, according to the Lord’s intentions in saying ṛṣayah parokṣam ca mama priya: sages and I also are fond of indirect expression, the devotees find difficulty in giving the meaning of jīva to the word go in gopāla (the Lord accepts the jīva as himself) in śrutis which hide the meaning of certain words.

*evaṁ janmāni karmāṇi hy akartur ajanasya ca*  
*varṇayanti sma kavayo veda-guhyāni hṛt-pateḥ*

**Thus learned men describe the births and activities of the unborn and inactive, which is undiscoverable even in the Vedic literatures. He is the Lord of the heart. (SB** [**1.3.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/3.35)**)**

Thus the word gopāla means the one who protects the cows, the cowherd. Those who are in pure dāsya meditate with steadiness on “I am Gopāla” (meaning “I’m a cowherd”) among the cowherds who are servants of Kṛṣṇa. Those who are in sakhya, among Śrīdāma, Subala and other cowherd boys think, “I am Gopāla.” Those who are in vātsalya, Kṛṣṇa’s parents, among the elders like Nanda and Upananda, meditate on themselves as “I am Gopāla.” Those in madhura-rasa, among the mañjaris like Rūpa and the gopīs like Lalitā and Viśākhā, remember themselves in transformed forms as “I am Gopāla.”

This thus does not contradict the saying nādevo devam arcayet: one who does not have the mood of the Lord should not worship the Lord. The worshippers of the associates of Kṛṣṇa do not deviate from understanding Kṛṣna as the object of worship. But these pure devotees do not respect the concept of worshiping the self as the Lord. Everything is thus adjusted.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Derivation of the word gopāla is given in order to express the idea that one should not deviate from attaining Gopāla.

*gā bhagavad-aṅga-kānti pānīyāni udagrasamābhiḥ pibanti*

**Go means the effulgence of the Lord and drinking a huge amount of water – Nirukti.**

Thus the word gopāla means one who accepts (ālāti) the jīvas (gopa) who are nithya-siddhas and sādhana-siddhas as the object of prema, as oneself, out of absorption in them, from creation to destruction. That Gopāla is nondifferent from Kṛṣṇa, personification of eternal bliss. Hi means certainly. He is also the protector of the cows, land and Vedas. He is parambrahma expressed by oṁ tat sat, glorified by all the Vedas. One should meditate on the phrase “I am that Gopāla”, as expressed in the words om tat sat. There is no return for one who has attained Brahman. The followers of Chāndogya say na sa punar āvartate – He doesn’t return.

Liberation is not described as worship of the svarūpa of the jīva as in the following:

*ātmānaṁ ced vijānīyād ayam asmīti pūruṣaḥ \|*  
*kim icchan kasya kāmāya śarīram anu saṁjvared*

**If a person understands that he is ātmā, “I am this,” will he make the body suffer for developing desire for material objects?7 (Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**4.4.12**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/4.4.12)**)**

“Then what is the use of absorption in worship where one identifies oneself completely as Gopāla?” Though the jīva is real, his knowledge by transformation (ignorance), is restricted, and so he cannot be the surpeme truth. And Gopāla is confirmed as the surpeme entity (param satyam) since he lacks that restriction of knowledge. He is free of restriction caused by relationship with a material body. Even the realization of the jīva’s svarūpa is also attained by worship of Gopāla.

One should produce oneness with Gopāla using absorption of mind, not by merging one’s svarūpa. One sees in this world absorption of a pure person out of great affection. In that sense there is identity of “I am you.” Thus the writer of the sūtras says:

*pūrva-vikalpaḥ prakaraṇāt syāt kriyā mānasavat*

**So ham means a type of bhakti because of the context. It is just like deity worship and meditation. (Brahma-sūtras –** [**3.3.45**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/3.3.45)**)**

The meaning of that sūtra is this. The meaning of “I am Gopāla” is that it is a type of bhakti (pūrva-vikalpaḥ), because of the subject matter previously discussed in this work: bhaktir asya bhajanam (verse [1.14](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/1.14)) etc. An example is given. Deity worship (kriyā) and meditation (mānasa) are such types of bhakti. One should not have faith in the philosophy of kevalādvaita since it has been refuted in the bhāṣyā-pīṭhakā. The phrase manasaikyaṁ kuryāt (become one using the mind) in the verse does not support their claim. Gopāla is a form to be realized internally (avyaktaḥ). He is fixed (nitya). Thus it is recommended that one should practice meditation using the method of identity wi th him.

### 2.54

*mathurāyāṁ sthitir brahman sarvadā me bhaviṣyati \|*  
*śaṅkha-cakra-gadā-padma-vanamālāvṛtas tu vai \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Brahmā! Endowed with conch, cakra, club, lotus, and forest garland, I reside at all times in Mathurā.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The supreme excellence of Śrī-gopāla has been stated. Nārāyaṇa now shows that he himself is obedient to Gopāla. He remains as an āvaraṇa-devatā (guardian) always. When Gopāla manifests, Nārāyaṇa will manifest in this way. Nārāyaṇa is covered by a forest garland.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

That city is Nāryāṇa’s eternal place of residence. O Brahmā, my incomparable associate! In Mathurā I reside always.

*mathurā bhagavān yatra nityaṁ sannihito harir*

**Kṛṣṇa lives in Mathurā eternally. (SB** [**10.1.28**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/1.28)**)**

I will be there at the end of Dvāparā yuga visible to all. He describes his form. He holds the conch etc.

### 2.55

*viśva-rūpaṁ \[cit-svarūpaṁ\] paraṁ jyotiḥsvarūpaṁ rūpa-varjitaṁ \|*  
*hṛdā māṁ saṁsmaran brahman mat-padaṁ yāti niścitaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who in his heart remembers me, the shelter of all existence, devoid of material elements, endowed with extraordinary form and supremely effulgent, certainly attains my abode.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Rūpa-varjitam means devoid of material form. Worship of material vairājya (universal form) is rejected (as Śrī Viśvanātha takes the verse to start with cit-svarūpam rather than viśva-rūpam). One should remember my spiritual form, not a material form. Matpadam means my abode.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

One who meditates on me attains liberation. Viśva-rūpam means I who have all (viśva) forms such as Matsya. My form is spiritual (jyotihsvarūpam) not material. Padam means feet.

### 2.56

*mathurāmaṇḍale yastu jambūdvīpe sthito’pi vā \|*  
*yo’rcayet pratimāṁ māñca sa me priyataro bhuvi \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who worships me and my deity form in Mathurā or anywhere in Jambudvīpa is dearest to me on earth.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

If one cannot live in Mathurā, one can live anywhere in Jambudvīpa. One should worship a deity (pratimām).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Since it is difficult to meditate on my spiritual form, one should worship the deity. One should worship the deity situated somewhere in Jambudvīpa, what to speak of Mathura-maṇḍala. The deity is myself (mām). This rejects the idea that the deity is mere stone.

### 2.57-59

*tasyām adhiṣṭhitaḥ kṛṣṇarūpī pūjyas tvayā sadā \|\|*  
*caturdhā cāsyādhikāra bhedatvena yajanti mām \|\|*  
*yugānuvartino lokā yajantīha sumedhasaḥ \|*  
*gopālaṁ sānujaṁ rāma-rukmiṇyā saha tatparaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You should always worship the form of Kṛṣṇa in Mathurā. People of four qualifications worship me in different ways. Following the process for the yuga, the intelligent people worship Gopāla with Balarāma and Rukmiṇī, along with Pradyumna and Aniruddha, with great devotion in Mathurā.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

One should worship the deity of Kṛṣṇa, since he is the most excellent.

According to qualification of a person, he worships members of the catur-vyūha (caturdhā). Some worship with Rukmiṇī. Tatparam means that person and others. Or they worship Kṛṣṇa, dedicated to Rukmiṇī.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Situated in the deity as Kṛṣṇa, Śyāmasundara, I should be worshipped by you, Brahmā. Because of having four different qualifications, the ārta, jijñāsu, arthārthī and jñānī (caturdhā) worhsip me, according to their bhāva.

Intelligent persons fixed in meditation etc. performed in Satya and other yugas, worship me, Gopāla at Mathurā, with sons Pradyumna and Aniruddha (anujam) and with Balarāma (rāma) and Rukmiṇī. She represents Satyabhāṁa, Revatī and others. Tatparam means with devotion.

### 2.60

*gopālo’ham ajo nityaḥ pradyumno’haṁ sanātanaḥ \|*  
*rāmo’ham aniruddho’ham ātmānam arcayed budhaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I am Gopāla. The wise man should worship eternal, unborn Paramātmā, understanding that he is not different from Kṛṣṇa, Pradyumna, Balarāma and Aniruddha, with whom one should identify.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Worship of the self is shown (ahaṁgrahopasana).

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

One should worship me with complete absorption. The knower of bhāva (budhaḥ) should worship the Lord (ātmānam), full of knowledge and bliss.

### 2.61

*mayoktena svadharmeṇa niṣkāmeṇa vibhāgaśaḥ \|*  
*tair ahaṁ pūjanīyo vai bhadra-kṛṣṇa-nivāsibhiḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa is worshipped in various ways by the residents of Bhadravana and Kṛṣṇa-vana according to the pure methods explained by me in scriptures.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

I am to be worshipped in different forms (vibhāgaśaḥ) as the four forms such as Gopāla, according to scriptures.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

I am to be worshipped by the residents of the two types of forests previously described, Bhadra and Kṛṣṇa, without other desires (niṣkāmena) while performing svadharma with twenty qualities, starting with lack of pride.

### 2.62

*taddharmagati-hīnā ye tasyāṁ mayi parāyaṇāḥ \|*  
*kalinā grasitā ye vai teṣāṁ tasyām avasthitiḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who are devoid of devotion to me but are surrendered to me in Mathurā, and though they are addicted to sin because of Kali yuga, are all qualified to reside in Mathurā.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Moreover there is no fault if one lacks knowledge of worship of those forms. Those who are devoid of the goal of bhakti, but take shelter of me, though attached to sin are qualified to live in Mathurā-maṇḍala. It is said that Mathurā is the best of all tīrthas because it is most merciful

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

“It is not auspicious for those who are devoid of the mentioned qualities to live in Mathurā.” Those who live in Mathurā, but are devoid of qualities, and are my devotees, devoid of pretense, though having bad conduct such as violence against others, attain results of living there. Unlike other tīrthas, there is no requirement of performance of sat-karmas. Thus Ādi-varāha Purāṇa says

*yeṣāṁ kvāpi gatir nāsti teṣāṁ mathurāpurī gatir*

**Those without any shelter have the shelter of Mathurā. It deliveres even the sinful, just by living there. It is most powerful and very merciful.**

### 2.63

*yathā tvaṁ sahaputraiś ca yathā rudro gaṇaiḥ saha \|*  
*yathā śriyābhiyukto’haṁ tathā bhakto mama priyaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as you are affectionate to your sons, just as Śiva is affectionate to his associates, and just as I am affectionate to Lakṣmī, so I am affectionate to my devotee.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

“Why do you permit sinners to live in Mathurā, the form of Brahman?” The devotee, whatever quality he may have, is dear to me.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

What is the purpose of performing the dharma you explained for the people living there? You have affection for sons like Sanaka. Śiva has affection for the pramathas (associates). I have affection for Rukmiṇī and others. Like that, I am affectionate to the devotees. Those who follow dharma attain great results. Others who just live there attain liberation without doubt.

### 2.64

*sa hovācābjayoniḥ—*  
*caturbhir devaiḥ katham eko devaḥ syād ekam akṣaraṁ*  
*yad viśrutam anekākṣaraṁ katham bhūtam \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā spoke. Why does Kṛṣṇa become four forms? How does the one syllable oṁ known in the scriptures become many syllables?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

How does the Lord called Kṛṣṇa become one with the four starting with Balarāma? The syllable oṁ is famous as one, not four. But oṁ is made of four components. How does the one syllable known as oṁ become many?

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

It was said that Kṛṣṇa situated in Mathurā and the one powerful oṁ had four forms (verse [2.48](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.48)). To clarify the meaning an explanation is given. How does one Lord become four starting with Balarāma? Four and one are contradictions together. Oṁ or brahman is one syllable. This is stated in scripture. How did it become many, such as a, u, m and dot (on top of ‘m’)?

### 2.65

*sa hovāca taṁ ha vai*  
*pūrvaṁ hy ekam evādvitīyaṁ brahmāsīt*  
*tasmād avyaktaṁ vyaktam evākṣaraṁ*  
*tasmād akṣarān mahan mahato va*  
*ahaṁkāra tasmād evāhaṁkārāt pañcatanmātrāṇi*  
*tebhyo bhūtāni tair āvṛtam akṣaraṁ bhavati\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nārāyaṇa spoke clearly to Brahmā. Before creation there was only one Brahman. From that Brahman arose indestructible prakṛti. From that arose mahat-tattva. From mahat-tattva arose ahaṅkāra. From ahaṅkāra arose the five tanmātras and from them arose the gross elements. Prakṛti became covered by them.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Nārāyaṇa spoke. There was only one absolute entity (ekam). By the acintya-śakti of his svarūpa, he becomes many forms such as Balarāma. Bahu-mūrty-eka-mūrtikam: your one form has many forms (SB [10.40.7](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/40.7)) This is not cause and effect. According to nyāya, the cause has effects inherent in it. This is the Lord’s natural state. This is shown. Prakṛti (avyakta) becomes vyakta, since the material elements become visible. Akṣaram means it is indestructible. From that came the destructible elements such as mahat-tattva. Prakṛti (akṣaram) became covered by its effects.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Being asked, Nārāyaṇa, Kṛṣṇa, speaks to Brahmā. Ha vai indicates it is traditional knowledge.

By my parākhya śakti (superior potency) though I am one, I have many svarūpas such as Balarāma. Oṁ has many svarūpas such as a, u, m etc. An example is given. By the material śakti, matter takes on many forms.

Before the material creation there was Brahman with human form, endowed with material śakti. Akṣara, which is invisible, became visible as jīva-tattva with matter as his body. From the jīva tattva visible with material body arose three types of mahattattva. From mahat-tattva arose three types of ahaṅkāra.

From tāmasic ahaṅkāra arose the five tanmāntras starting with sound. From those five arose the five gross elements starting with ether. From rājasic ahaṅkāra arose the senses, and from sāttvika ahaṅkāra arose the devatās and the mind. The akṣara jīva-tattva became covered by those elements as the gross body. That jīva with a body made of those elements enjoys material objects.

### 2.66

*ajo’ham asmy akṣaro’ham oṁkāro’ham asmi \|\|*  
*\[akṣaro’ham oṅkāro’jaro’maro’bhayo’mṛto brahmābhayaṁ hi sa \|*  
*mukto’ham evasmi akṣaro’ham asmi\]*

**Перевод:**

**I am Brahman and I am oṁ. Oṁ is without old age, without death, without fear, and full of bliss. Since Brahman is without fear, so is oṁ. I am liberated. I am unchanging Brahman (akṣara).**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Since mahat-tattva etc. are made of prakṛti, since prakṛti is my śakti, and since śakti and the possessor of śakti are considered one, only I alone exist. I am the one syllable oṁ (akṣara), which expresses me. I am Kṛṣṇa, famous as the meaning of oṁ. The adjectives ajara etc. describe him.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The Lord’s state of being as a verbal expression is described. I am oṁ devoid of destruction. Smṛti says, praṇava pratipādyāya namaḥ - I offer respects to the Lord in the form of oṁ. Aham is repeated in order to indicate the Lord’s own real nature. Oṁ is nondifferent from him. One should see the bhāṣya-pīṭhaka.

### 2.67-68

*sattā-mātraṁ viśvarūpaṁ prakāśaṁ vyāpakaṁ tathā \|\|*  
*ekam evādvayaṁ brahma māyayā tu catuṣṭayaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That one Brahman is sac-cid-ānanda, has variegated forms and qualities, has the power to reveal qualities in himself and others and is spread everywhere. That one Brahman manifests as four forms by his śakti.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

One Brahman becomes four by his māyā, which is part of his svarūpa.

*svarūpa-bhūtayā nitya-śaktyā māyākhyayā yutaḥ \|*  
*ato māyāmayaṁ viṣṇuṁ pravadanti sanātanam \|\|*

**The Lord is endowed with his eternal śakti called māyā arising from his svarūpa. Thus, the wise call eternal Viṣṇu māyā-mayam, made of māyā. (Caturveda-śikhā)**

This is quoted by Madhva in his commentary.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Just as the sound oṁ is eternal, the Lord does not have qualities of being created or destroyed. Sattā-mātram means that which cannot be deprived of its existence. Akṣara is always endowed with sattā.

“But it is endowed with prakṛti etc.” It has a svarūpa of knowledge.

“But still it would have something insentient (prakṛti) within itself. Though it manifests knowledge as its part the logicians say that it has matter in itself.” It manifests to itself (prakāśam).

“It is endowed with jīva.” It is all-pervading (vyāpakam). Brahman is self manifesting and all-pervading, and that is oṁ; it is not a material sound related to ether.

The one tattva becomes four by his śakti. By the acintya-śakti arising from his svarūpa, the four forms starting with Balarāma are manifest. It was previously explained that the word māyā refers to the svarūpa-śakti.

### 2.69

*rohiṇītanayo rāma akārākṣara sambhavaḥ \|*  
*taijasātmakaḥ pradyumna ukārākṣarasambhavaḥ \|*  
*prājñātmako’niruddho vai makārākṣara-sambhavaḥ \|*  
*ardhamātrātmakaḥ kṛṣṇo yasmin viśvaṁ pratiṣṭhitaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Balarāma, the son of Rohiṇī, arose from the syllable a. Pradyumna, representing the dream state, arose from the syllable u. Aniruddha, representing deep sleep, arose from the syllable m. Kṛṣṇa is the nāda, and the complete sound oṁ, in whom the universe is established.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Just as one Brahman manifests as four by its svarūpa, the one syllable oṁ, becomes four syllables by its svarūpa. Balarāma manifests from the syllable a. Pradyumna is the presiding deity of the dream state (taijasātmakaḥ). This means that Balarāma is the presiding deity of waking state.

Kṛṣṇa is the manifestor (ātmā) of the nāda (ardha-mātrā) manifesting within oṁ as the dot. The whole universe is established upon him since even Brahman is resting on Kṛṣṇa, the fourth state. Gītā says brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāham: I am the basis of brahman. (BG [14.27](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/14.27))

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The four forms of the Lord and oṁ manifest the object of sound and the sound itself through māyā. By mentioning the name as son of Rohiṇī no mistake can be made about the identity of the vyūha. He manifests as the syllable a as a limb of oṁ. It is understood that he is related to the waking state. Pradyumna and Aniruddha are related to the dream and deep sleep states. Thus Balarāma with waking state indicates more manifestation of powers than the other two. The nāda or ardha-mātra is different from those three states of the jīva. This is Kṛsṇa. All the limbs of praṇava also indicate Kṛṣṇa by the process of ajahal-lakṣaṇa (oṁ ultimately indicates Kṛsṇa)8 . The last sentence indicates that Kṛṣṇa is complete, being the combination of all the four just as oṁ is complete with all the syllables starting with a.

### 2.70-71

*kṛṣṇātmikā jagat-kartrīr mūla-prakṛtī rukmiṇī \|\|*  
*vraja-strī-jana-sambhūta-śrutibhyo brahma-saṅgataḥ \|*  
*praṇavatvena prakṛtiṁ vadanti brahma-vādinaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rukmiṇī, whose aṁśa is prakṛti, is the cit-śakti, the wife of the producer of all things and she is non-different from Kṛṣṇa, since Kṛṣṇa attained association with the Vedas when the śṛutis became women of Vraja. The knowers of Brahman praise the śrutis as praṇava.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Mūla-prakṛti is an aṁśa of Rukminī since Rukminī arises from Kṛṣṇa’s svarūpa (kṛṣṇātmikā) as the hlādinī śakti, not the bahiraṅgā māyā. The word aṁśa is missing as in the expression śāka-pārthiva, a spinach-eating king. Rukminī is the wife of Kṛṣṇa (jagat-kartrīḥ) who is the creator of the universe, since she herself lacks such a svarūpa.

The gopīs attained Kṛṣṇa’s intimate association by anurāga, not by being his wife. Kṛṣṇa gained the association of the śrutis when they became women of Vraja by austerities, as mentioned in Bṛhad-vāmana Purāṇa. The ablative case (saṅgataḥ) can express a participle.

“It was said that oṁ manifested as Kṛṣṇa having four forms. But then Kṛṣṇa’s self manifesting nature is refuted.” The knowers of Brahman say that he is the cause of all the Vedas (prakṛtim) by being the svarupa of oṁ.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The Lord speaks to indicate that like himself, oṁ indicates Rukmiṇī and others. The parā śakti, or mūla prakṛtī, is Rukmiṇī. Thus the śakti is nondifferent from him.

*parāsya śaktir vividhaiva śrūyate svābhāvikī jñānabalakriyā*

**The parā śakti is said to have various types: jñāna, bala and kriyā. (Śvetāśvatara upaniṣad** [**6.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.8)**)**

She is the root, the cause of the three guṇas like an image and its reflection. Rukminī represents Satyabhāmā and others. Since they are parā, forms of Lakṣmī they are like Kṛṣṇa.

*reme ramābhir nija-kāma-sampluto*

**There, fully saturated with a special prema, he enjoyed with his pleasing wives. (SB** [**10.59.43**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/59.43)**)**

*paraiva lakṣmīr iti procyate parameśo ya*

**Lakṣmī is supreme. Viṣṇu is the lord of Lakṣmī. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa)**

She is described as the creator of the universe. But the real cause of the universe is Paramātmā, the possesor of the parā śakti.

Then it’s explained (atha) that the women like Rādhā are also indicated in oṁ, like Kṛṣṇa himself. \[How? The explanation is as follows.\] Because of association of women of Vraja headed by Rādhā with Kṛṣṇa (brahma-saṅgataḥ), he said to them “Give food to Durvāsa” (verse [2.4](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.4)). Inspired by his words, they went to Durvāsa and asked him about the Lord. The words (śrutibhyaḥ) starting with ajo’ ham (verse [2.66](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.66)) and ending with yasmin viśvam partiṣṭhitam (verse [2.69](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.69)) were spoken by Durvāsa (saṁbhūta) when they asked. By understanding these words, persons like Gautama (brahma-vādinaḥ) speak of prakṛti in the form of Rādhā, belonging to Kṛṣna who is the svarūpa of oṁ. \[Since Rādhā also belongs to Kṛṣṇa who in-turn is the svarūpa of oṁ, she is also thus indicated in the oṁ.\] Kṛṣṇa with his human form is most astonishing. This is the cause of Rādhā being most astonishing. Rādhā is unique as the consort of Kṛsṇa with human form. This is a cause of astonishment and shows her to be eternally perfect.

*sattvaṁ tattvaṁ paratvaṁ ca tattva-trayam ahaṁ kila \|*  
*tri-tattva-rūpiṇī sāpi rādhikā mama vallabhā \|*  
*prakṛteḥ para evāhaṁ sāpi mac-chakti-rūpiṇīti \|*

**I am the three principles: sattva (effect), tattva (case) and paratva (spiritual energy). The form of these three is Rādhikā, my consort. I am beyond prakṛti, and she is my śakti. (Gautamīya-tantra)**

Brahma-saṁhitā says ānanda-cinmaya-rasa: the gopīs are forms of spiritually blissful rasa. Thus it is shown that Rādhā who is nondifferent from Kṛṣṇa is expressed in oṁ like the Lord himself.

### 2.72

*tasmād oṁkāra-sambhūto gopālo viśva-saṁsthitaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore Gopāla, arising with oṁkāra, is the controller of the universe.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Though his svarūpa is oṁ, being its manifester he is not destroyed. Viśva-saṁsthitaḥ means he is the controller of the whole universe. In him the whole universe is situated.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Because of the previously mentioned reasons, Gopāla, the aṁśī of everything, manifests in oṁ and is situated in the limbs (viśva) of oṁ. He is situated as Balarāma etc. in the syllables starting with a which are sounds expressing the Lord. Kṛṣṇa is endowed with Rukmiṇī and others as sound in the ardha-mātrā.

### 2.73

*klīm oṁkārayor ekatvaṁ paṭhyate brahma-vādibhiḥ \|\|*

**Those who know the Vedas say that klīṁ and oṁkāra are one.**

### 2.74-78

*mathurāyāṁ viśeṣeṇa māṁ dhyāyan mokṣam aśnute \|\|*  
*aṣṭa-patraṁ vikasitaṁ hṛt-padmaṁ tatra saṁsthitaṁ \|*  
*śaṅkha-dhvajātapatraiś-ca cihnitaṁ caraṇa-dvayam \|\|*  
*śrīvatsa-lāñchanaṁ hṛt-sthaṁ kaustubhaṁ prabhayā yutam \|*  
*catur-bhujaṁ śaṅkha-cakra-śārṅga-padma-gadānvitam \|\|*  
*sukeyurānvitaṁ bāhuṁ kaṇṭha-mālā-suśobhitam \|*  
*dyumat-kirīṭa-valayaṁ sphuran-makara kuṇḍalaṁ \|\|*  
*hiraṇmayaṁ saumya-tanuṁ svabhaktāyābhaya-pradaṁ \|*  
*dhyāyen \[dhyāyen manasi mā\] mama priyo veṇu-śṛṅga-dharaṁ tu vā \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meditating on me in Mathurā with attention, one quickly becomes free of obstacles to attaining me. One should constantly meditate on me, situated in Mathurā, situated on an eightpetaled lotus in the heart, endowed with two feet marked with attractive flag and umbrella, wearing the shining Kaustubha on my chest marked with Śrīvatsa, endowed with four arms holding conch, cakra, bow, lotus and club and decorated with arm bands, wearing a garland on my neck. One should meditate on me, offering fearlessness to my devotee, holding a flute and horn, with an attractive, shining form decorated with effulgent crown and bracelet and sparkling makara earrings.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The meditation is described. The lotus and bow are held in one hand since he has four hands. Holding flute and horn indicate his two armed form in Vṛndāvana.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Previously it was said to worship the deity in Mathurā or Jambudvīpa. Details are now given. One attains liberation quickly. That is special (viśeṣeṇa). Or viśeṣeṇa means special liberation, in the spiritual world. The meditation is made clear. He has sixteen marks including a half moon on his feet, not just those marks mentioned. Extraordinary marks are mentioned in the line starting with śrīvatsa. The form is described in Harivaṁśa:

*sa cāsyorasi vistīrṇe romāñcodgamarājimān \|*  
*śrīvatso rājate śrīmān stana-dvaya-mukhāñcita \|\|*

**Beautiful Śrīvatsa with hairs standing on end, marking the surface of his breasts, shines on his broad chest.**

These are his special marks not found on others as stated in Bhāgavatam: vinā śrīvatsa kaustubhau – \[The associates of the Lord who were looking like him but\] without the mark of Śrīvatsa and the Kaustubha jewel. (SB [6.9.29](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/9.29))

The bow is placed in front of him since he cannot hold more in his four arms. He has bracelets on all his arms but the singular is used to indicate a type. He has shining crown and bracelets.

Hiraṇmayam (golden) means his body is attractive like gold or effulgent. Saubhya-tanuṁ means his body is made attractive. He gives fearlessness to his devotee. The word devotee is in singular to express a class. Having spoke of the four-armed form for meditation in Mathurā, he describes the two armed from of Gokula, the place which contains that tattva.

### 2.79

*mathyate tu jagat sarvaṁ brahma-jñānena yena vā \|*  
*tat sārabhūtaṁ yad yasyāṁ mathurā sā nigadyate \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The place is called Mathurā because in that place, Bhagavān, who is most excellent, the essence of the whole universe, is made manifest (mathyate) by knowledge and by bhakti-yoga.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Stating the derivation of the word Mathurā, it is glorified. The milk ocean of the whole world is churned by the Mandara Mountain of brahma-jñāna. The word vā indicates bhakti-yoga. By churning with bhakti-yoga, the nectar which is the essence and which is also merging in Brahman is attained in Mathurā. The object, which is the goal attained in Mathura, is extracted.

Math + u + rā becomes Mathurā. Math is the root of the verb mathyate, and rā means giving.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Stating the derivation of the word Mathurā, it is glorified. Like yogurt is churned, the whole world is churned by knowledge, by discernment of the controller of the world for attaining the essence or fixed form of that knowledge, Brahman in human form, which is established as the highest form just as butter is extracted from yogurt. That essence is called Mathurā. Sāra means the essence or fixed portion according to Viśva.

### 2.80

*aṣṭa-dikpālibhir bhūmi-padmaṁ vikasitaṁ jagat \|*  
*saṁsārārṇava sañjātaṁ sevitaṁ mama mānase \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should worship the blossoming lotus – the earth or universe – which has arisen from the ocean of samsāra and is endowed with eight protectors of the directions which is produced by my mind.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The vibhūtis of the Lord should be the object of meditation. The lotus in the form of the earth with eight dikpālas, blossoming, and filled with the ocean of saṁsāra produced by my knowledge should be the object of meditation of the devotees. The vibhūti of my mind is the earth.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Worship of the universal form – a gross meditation – is described. The lotus in the form of the earth, with petals in the form of the dikpālas, manifesting, arising as the form of saṁsāra, this whole universe, is produced by my decision (mānase). This is the first to be worshipped as my form.

### 2.81

*candra-sūryārciṣo divyā dhvajā merur hiraṇmayaḥ \|*  
*ātapatraṁ brahma-lokam adhordhvaṁ caraṇaṁ smṛtam \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Worship my astonishing flag whose effulgence is the sun and moon, as golden Meru. Worship Brahma-loka as my umbrella and worship the lower planets as my feet.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

My flag whose effulgence is the moon and sun is Meru. Meru is my vibhūti, representing my flag. The lower planets above Pātāla are my feet.

Candra-sūrya with no declension is vedic usage. The moon and sun are my attractive effulgence. Golden Meru is my flag. Brahmaloka should be worshipped as my umbrella. The seven lower planets should be worshipped as my feet. The sandhi in adhordhvaṁ is vedic usage. The singular indicates a class.

### 2.82

*śrīvatsañca svarūpañca vartate lāñchanaiḥ saha \|*  
*śrīvatsa-lāñchanaṁ tasmāt kathyate brahma-vādibhiḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Śrīvatsa, my svarūpa, exists with marks of hair. Therefore, the knowers of Brahman call the Lord Śrīvatsa-lāñchana.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Śrīvatsa is my own svarūpa. Ca means eva (indeed). It exists with marks of hair curling clockwise (lāñchanaiḥ). For this reason (being my svarūpa) this vibhūti of Śrīvatsa does not exist on earth. Though not stated, these words are understood.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The Śrīvatsa exists in the spiritual form of the Lord. It has marks indicating its quality. The svarūpa in the form of the totality of jīvas should be worshipped along with the limbs of that form in the universal form.

### 2.83

*yena sūryāgnivākcandra tejasā sva-svarūpiṇā \|*  
*vartate kaustubhākhyaṁ maṇiṁ vadantīśamāninaḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The worshippers of the universal form say that my jewel is called Kaustubha because the sun, fire, speech, and moon shine by its light, which is the shelter of all jīvas.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Those who know the powers of the Lord say that the jewel is called Kaustubha because, by its light, becoming one with the Lord’s svarūpa by his wearing it, the sun, moon, fire, and speech exist. Thus the sun, fire and moon are vibhūtis of the Kaustubha’s light of color. Speech is a vibhūti of the light of knowledge.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The total of the sun, fire, speech (vāk) and moon exist by Kaustubha’s extraordinary (sva-svarūpiṇā) light. Those who think of the universe as the Lord speak of the jewel called Kaustubha. One should meditate on the light of the sun as the Kaustubha.

### 2.84

Sattva, rajas, tamas and ahaṅkāra are vibhūtis of his four arms. The five elements are the vibhūtis of the conch. The conch is in his hand made of rajas. This is also his vibhūti.

The hands of the universal form are sattva, rajas, tamas and ahaṅkāra. One should think of them as the Lord’s four arms. One should meditate on the five elements as pāñcajanya, caused by rajas.

### 2.85

### 2.88

*kaṇṭhaṁ tu nirguṇaṁ proktaṁ mālyate ādyayājayā \[yayā\] \|*

### 2.91-92

*dhyāyen mama priyo nityaṁ sa mokṣam adhigacchati \|*  
*sa mukto bhavati tasmai svātmānaṁ ca dadāmi vai \|\|*  
*etat sarvaṁ bhaviṣyad vai mayā proktaṁ vidhe tava \|*  
*svarūpaṁ dvividhaṁ caiva saguṇaṁ nirguṇātmakaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meditating in this way, he who is dear to me attains eternal liberation. He gives up material bondage. More than that, I give myself to him. O Brahmā! What I have said to you concerning the two forms – the material form and the non-material form – should be realized and taught in the future.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

The saguṇa form is the universal form, called a vibhūti. The nirguṇa form is my auspicious form beyond the guṇas of prakṛti. Some material vibhūtis are sometimes described as nirguṇa.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Having taught the worship of the universal form for persons with gross intelligence, he explains that liberation is attained by meditation on the direct form of the Lord by those with fine intelligence. Such a person becomes freed from māyā that covers the Lord’s svarūpa. He becomes liberated from what covers the Lord’s qualities. He becomes successful, realizing the vibhūtis and qualities of the svarūpa of Brahman. Śruti says:

Bhūyaś cānte viśvamāyā nivṛttir - At the time of death, one extinguishes the influence of prakṛti. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad [1.10](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/1.10))

Vimuktaś ca vimucyate: free of prakṛti, he becomes liberated. (Kaṭha Upaniṣad [2.2.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/katha-bs/2.2.1))

Everything is summarized. You will realize and teach everything in the future: the saguṇa svarūpa described starting with the description of eight dikpālas (verses 80-90) and the nirguṇa svarūpas with four and two arms (verses 75-79).

### 2.93

*sa hovācābjayoniḥ \|*  
*vyaktānāṁ mūrtīnāṁ proktānāṁ katham ābharaṇāni bhavanti \|\|*  
*kathaṁ vā devā yajanti rudrā yajanti brahmā yajati brahmajā*  
*\[brahmajñā\] yajanti vināyakā yajanti dvādaśādityā yajanti vasavo*  
*yajanti apsaraso yajanti gandharvā yajanti \|\| svapadaṁ gatā*  
*\[svapadantvagāta\] antardhāne tiṣṭhati kāṁ manuṣyā yajanti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā spoke. How does one decorate the visible deity forms and those described by you? What form do the devatās, Rudras, Brahmā, Brahmā’s sons, the Vināyakas, the twelve Ādityas, the Vasus, Apsarās and Gandharvas worship? What is the form in the sky? What is the invisible form? What form do men worship?**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Vyaktānāṁ means separately manifesting. Please explain how to decorate the forms of Balarāma etc. Please explain for what purpose the devatās and others worship those forms.

It was said that the eleventh form went to his abode and the tenth form disappeard (verse [2.46](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/2.46)); what are those forms? And it was said that the twelfth form remains on earth; what is this form that humans worship?

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Desiring to destroy other doubts in what was previously spoken, Brahmā inquired. He explains the questions. Of the forms described such as Balarāma, which appear among the worshippers (vyaktānām), how does one decorate them? How do the Maruts worship and which form do the Maruts worship? How to worship the form which went to its own abode, the eleventh form? How to worship the tenth form which disappeared? How to worship the form which humans worship on earth.

There are three questions:

1. How to decorate the deities?
2. How do the devatās and others worship?
3. Which form do the devatās and others worship.

### 2.94

*sa hovāca taṁ hi vai nārāyaṇo devaḥ*  
*ādyā avyaktā dvādaśa-mūrtayaḥ sarveṣu lokeṣu*  
*sarveṣu deveṣu sarveṣu manuṣyeṣu tiṣṭhanti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nārāyaṇa spoke to Brahmā. The eternal, invisible twelve forms manifest in all planets of devatās and men.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Without answering the first question he answers the second question. The twelve deities are eternal and not visible to the material eye.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Asked, Nārāyaṇa does not answer the first question but answers the other two. Ādyā means they are without beginning. Avyaktā means they are invisible to the materialist. They exist in all upper planets, among devatās and in all middle planets among men. They manifest to the worshippers in those places.

“Previously it was said that the deities existed in the twelve forests, part of Mathurā. The devatās also remain there. But here you say that the deities are situated in the various planets.” They are worshipped by the devatās etc. situated in their planets by meditation on the respective forests.

### 2.95

*rudreṣu raudrī brahmaṇyeṣu brāhmī deveṣu daivī manuṣyeṣu mānuvī*  
*vināyakeṣu vighnanāśinī ādityeṣu jyotirgandharveṣu gāndharvy*  
*apsaraḥsv evaṁ gauḥ vasuṣv evaṁ kāmyāntardhāne ‘prakāśinī \|*  
*āvirbhāva tirobhāvā svapade tiṣṭhati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Raudrī manifests to the Rudras. Brāhmī manifests to Brahmā and his sons. Daivī manifests to the devatās. A human form manifests among men. A form which destroys obstacles manifests to the Vināyakas. A form of light manifests to the Ādityas. Gāndharvī manifests to the Gandharvas. Apsarī manifests to the Āpsarās through song. Vāsavī manifests to the Vasus and fulfills all desires. Aprakāśinī manifests invisibly. The form which is in his abode Vaikuṇtḥa appears and disappears.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

Raudrī is worshipped by the Rudras on Rudra-loka. Though later situated in Vraja, they serve on that planet. The Raudrī form gives power to destroy. Brahmī gives the meaning of the Vedas. Daivi gives pleasure to the devatās. The Mānusī form gives dharma to men. Vighna-nāśinī gives power to destroy obstacles. Jyoti gives light. Gāndharvi give songs to the Gandharvas and is praised among the Apsaras. Gau means giving song. The form which is invisible, Aparakāśinī, does not manifest since it has no worshippers. The form which is in his abode Vaikuṇtḥa appears and disappears.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The question of who worships what form is answered. According to their individual bhāvas, Rudras and others perform worship and offer ornaments. Brahmaṇyeṣu means among the persons situated on Brahmaloka who follow Brahmā, qualified like Brahmā. Mānuṣeṣu means among Sanaka and others who have brahma-jñāna. Though they are devatās, they often interact with humans and thus are considered human. The Lord is manifested as song (gauḥ) among the Apsaras. The word formation of gauḥ is irregular, similar to pṛṣodara (spotted belly). Vāsavī (evam) is worshipped among the Vasus. That form bestows wealth which is desired (kāmyā). That form which remains invisible is aprakāśinī, not seen and only available through hearing. That form which appears and then disappears remains visible in Gokula (svapade) in a special manifestation. This form was shown to the people of Vraja, when they were raised from Brahma-hrada.

The eleven forms starting with Raudri are avatāras and this deity is the avatārī. Appearing and disappearing, he remains in his abode. This is stated in Gautamīya-tantra by the Lord.

*idaṁ vṛndāvanaṁ ramyaṁ mama dhāmaiva kevalaṁ \|*  
*pañca-yojanam evāsti vanaṁ me deha-rūpakam \|*  
*kālindīyaṁ suṣumnākhyā paramāmṛta-vāhinī \|\|*  
*atra devāś ca bhūtāni vartante sūkṣma-rūpataḥ \|*  
*sarva-deva-mayaś cāhaṁ na tyajāmi vanaṁ kvacit \|\|*  
*āvirbhāvas tirobhāvo bhaven me ’tra yuge yuge \|*  
*tejo-mayam idaṁ ramyam adṛśyaṁ carma-cakṣuṣā \|\|95\|\|*

**This pleasant Vṛndāvana is my only abode. This place, measuring five yojanas, is non-different from my body. The Yamunā, flowing with the sweetest nectar, is the suṣumnā (central nerve) of that body. All the devas and the elements exist there in a spiritual form. I, the embodiment of all devas, do not leave that forest for a moment, though I appear and disappear in this world age after age. But this radiant forest full of pleasure is invisible to the material eye.**

### 2.96-97

*tāmasī rājasī sāttvikī mānuṣī \|\|*  
*vijñāna-ghanānanda-ghana—sac-cid-ānandaika-rase bhakti-yoge tiṣṭhati \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The forms for humans are tāmasī, rājasī and sāttvikī.**

**The form of Kṛṣṇa, filled with knowledge and bliss, resides in bhakti-yoga, which is also full of eternity, knowledge, and bliss.**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

What form do humans worship? The deity is human in form with two arms. Since he is worshipped by bhakti mixed with the guṇas, he has tāmasī, rājasī and sāttvikī forms.

The human form worshipped by nirguṇa bhakti, is full of knowledge for the śānta bhaktas and full of bliss for the devotees in prema with dāsya, sakhya etc. Thus there are two types. That deity resides in bhakti-yoga which is nirguṇa, filled with saccidananda-rasa.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The twelfth form is described. According to three types of men, there are three manifestations as in mallānām aśanir: Kṛṣṇa appeared like a thunderbolt to the wrestlers. (SB [10.43.17](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/43.17))

This form resides among humans. The tāmasī, rājasī and sāttvikī forms should be taken with secondary meaning (since the Lord is beyond the guṇas.) The deity manifests a pure svarupa for devotees having pure bhakti. Since his form is condensed knowledge, he is said to be substantial or personified knowledge (vijñāna-ghana) and substantial personified bliss. Paṇini says ghana means mūrta, hardness or solidity. By indicating qualities actually the form possessing the qualities is meant.

To understand the svarūpa of bhakti-yoga, a statement is made. Sac-cid-ānandaika-rase means bhakti is the essence of hlādinī combined with the essence of saṁvit. This takes the form of actions of hearing and chanting etc. This is like the nails and hair of the Lord which have a form of bliss and knowledge. This is explained in detail in bhāṣya-pīṭhaka.

The dative case ending for the word bhūr-bhuvaḥ-svas is omitted according to Pāṇini 7.1.39. The meaning is “I offer respects to the universal form, of three forms, bhūr, bhuvar and svar, manifested by the Lord’s śakti called pradhāna.”

**Перевод:**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Having defined his object of worship by mantras, Nārāyaṇa teaches the devotees about the equal qualities in himself and the amśī, Kṛsṇa, without pointing out the distinguishing qualities. Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad Ekaḥ means chief according to Nānārtha-varga. I am the chief of all tattvas. I am shining, and to be worshipped by all (devaḥ). I am hidden within all beings. I stay there. To refute the idea that he splits into parts to do that, he is described as all-pervasive (sarva-vyāpī). To refute the idea that he is indifferent, he is called the innner ātmā of all beings. He gives happiness as the controller of actions (karmādhyakṣa). Worshipped by actions prescribed in the Vedas, he gives results to the worshippers. The writer of the sutras says phalam ata upapatteḥ: The Lord alone gives the results because that is reasonable – Brahma-sūtras [3.2.38](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/3.2.38). He is the shelter of all (sarva-bhūtādhivāsa). He is the seer of everything auspicious and inauspicious. He is omniscient (cetā) or he gives knowledge. He bestows consciousness. He wakes the jīva from the ignorance of sleep. He gives knowledge of himself by association with sattva. He gives liberation. He has a svarūpa attained by liberation (kevala). It means pure. What is his nature? He is untouched by the guṇas of māyā.

*nāradāc ca śrutam yathā \|*  
*tathā proktaṁ tu gāndharvi*  
*gaccha tvaṁ svālayāntikaṁ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I taught this knowledge to Brahmā. Brahmā’s sons heard from Brahmā. From them Nārada heard. I heard from him and have spoken it to you. O Gāndharvī! Go to a place near your house!**

**Комментарий. Автор Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura:**

How did the sage know this?. This verse answers. Durvāsā spoke. The sons of Brahmā – Sanaka and others – heard from Brahmā. Nārada heard from them. I heard from Nārada.

*śrīmad gopāla tāpanyā vivṛtiḥ pūrṇatāṁ gatā \|*  
*gāndharvā-sarasas tīre viśvanāthākhya-lekhakāt \|\|*

**The commentary on Gopāla-Tapani has been completed. Viśvanātha has written this on the bank of the Radha-kuṇḍa.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

The sage explains the sampradāya to Rādhā as proof of his teachings. Brahmā heard from Gopāla. From Brahmā, who is a rajo-guṇa vibhūti of Gopāla’s svarūpa, his sons such as Sanaka heard. Nāṛada heard from them. I heard from Nārada. I have spoken the secret of your master to you with no change in the syllables. Having heard this secret, go to your house. He was playing at that time in a forest at the edge of Vraja. Understanding his qualities as explained by me, go to your house. O Rādhā! You appeared as your aṁśa among the Gandharvas to preach bhakti to Kṛṣṇa. O devī! O queen of the devotees! Be merciful to me, living at the edge of Vraja. Oṁ.

*vidyābhūṣaṇa bhaṇitaṁ śrīmad gopālatāpanī bhāṣyaṁ \|*  
*toṣayatu vallavīnāṁ mitraṁ gopālakaṁ paraṁ brahma \|\|*

**May param brahma in the form of a cowherd, friend of the gopīs, be pleased with this commentary on Gopāla-tapanī Upaniṣad written by Vidyābhūṣaṇa.**

*ity ātharvaṇopaniṣatsu śrīgopālatāpanyām uttara vibhāgaḥ samāptaḥ \|\|*

**Thus ends the uttara vibhāga of Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad located in Atharva Veda.**
