# Govinda Dasera Kadaca

## Govinda Dasera Kadaca

My birth place is Kancana Nagara in Barddhaman. My father’s name is Syama dasa. My name is Govinda. Caste is blacksmith (kamara), maker of weapons, utensils, chains and vehicles. My mother’s name is Madhavl.

My wife’s name is Sasi-mukhl. One day she quarreled with me and spoke roughly. Calling me a fool with no good qualities, she rebuked me.

In the morning I left the house because of that offense. In 1430 saka, I left. I did not want to go back as I was filled with anger and pride.

Eventually I came to Katwa. Arriving there I heard the name of Srl Caitanya.

Everyone praised him. Hearing that praise, I hurried to see him.

Travelling all day, passing many fields, in the morning, crossing the Ganga, I came to a ghata on the river. Below Nadlya, the Ganga is called Misra-ghata. Seeing the capital of Nadlya, I was filled with bliss. On the right the Sarasvatl river sang, giving bliss to all, in sweet voices.

Above the ghata was Srivasa angana. There was a broad canal nearby. The dilapidated house of King Ballalal was also close by. I thought of eating, staying at the banyan tree at the ghata. At that moment Srl Caitanya came to that place.

He had a cloth tied around his waist and had an astonishing form. With him, there was Nityananda avadhuta with a smiling face. Dancing with three or four devotees Caitanya descended into the Ganga. The brave avadhuta jumped into the water from the bank, and, swimming about, played in the water.

Later, Srlvasa, Damodara, Siddha Haridasa and, to his left, Gadadhara, and finally Advaitacarya came. I have never seen anyone as effulgent as Advaita.

He had full head of hair and a large attractive beard, which hung down, just above his chest. Nityananda became absorbed in shouting the name of the Lord while swimming to the other shores. Everyone jumped into the Ganga one by one and swam, playing about.

I began to examine the astonishing form of Caitanya. I was entranced by the glow of his form. After bathing, he climbed on the bank. His curly hair hung down his back. His limbs were the color of pure gold. His eyes were long like blue lotus petals. He had beautiful cheeks and a fine forehead. He walked easily, like an actor in a play.

His thighs defeated the beauty of banana trunks. His eyes supported two attractive eyebrows. His feet were as if stained with lac, giving joy to the eyes of a sage. His body was filled with prema. He uttered the name of the Lord. He mercifully embraced whoever he saw. Saying the Lord’s name tears fell from his eyes. Tears shot from his eyes like water from a syringe.

Gauranga began to walk. Nityananda followed behind. One person introducing himself as the head of the district, introduced others.

I drowned in an ocean of bliss on seeing the water pastimes. Speaking to others, the Lord came to Sacl’s house. Her heart was longing to see him.

On the pretext of giving up my house, by good fortune, I quickly came to Navadvlpa dhama. At the ghata, I saw such pastimes. I do not know what emotions rose in my mind.

My hairs stood on end, so that my limbs were like kadamba flowers. Perspiration soaked my cloth. My tears of desire covered my feet.

The Lord came with smiling face, hair falling down his back. Seeing me by good fortune, he began speaking to me again and again. I came slowly. Turning limp, I fell on the ground, holding his feet. At his feet, my voice choked up. Merciful Nimai held my hand and made me sit.

Sitting with folded hands in front of him, I wept. Saying a few words and laughing, sweet streams flowed constantly from him. My heart was enchanted with the fragrance of his limbs. His thin body was mad with chanting the Lord’s name. Several devotees with choked voices were at his feet.

How can I describe my emotions? Millions of devatas came and rolled at his feet. Even though the Lord was standing in a dark room, the effulgence of his body destroyed the darkness. It seems the moon in a solitary place had somehow been kneaded into a paste. Anyone who sees this will become detached completely from material life.

Laughing, he spoke to me. He carefully asked my name. He asked, “What is your caste? What is your name? What is your work? Where do you live?”

Hearing his words, I folded my hands and began to speak to him.

“Why do you show such mercy to me? I am most fallen. My name is Govinda Dasa. I was a householder and did various jobs in the house. But I have become a beggar on the road. Giving up material enjoyment, I have come to see you. O Lord! Give me a place at your red lotus feet. I live in Katwa, Barddhaman. My father’s name is Syamadasa, a blacksmith.”

Hearing these words, the Lord said to me, “O Govinda! Stay at my house. You will be protected at my house. Every day you can do nama-sankirtana in joy. Every day you will take Krsna’s prasada in joy. Your mind will be completely satisfied.

You will be constantly engaged in actions of service, bringing and offering Ganga water and tulasi leaves. You will fill your stomach with prasada daily, with sauces, spinach, sukuta and banana flower.

Saying this, the Lord wanted to take me with him. I went to the Lord’s house. There was a most attractive house over the Ganga, with five rooms, beautiful to behold.

The Lord’s house was at the southern edge of the town. The Lord was always intoxicated in chanting the holy name. Nearby was a huge pond. Some called it Ballala-sagara.

I will tell all the names of the devotees who were always with the Lord. They were Advaita Acarya, Svarupa, Srivasa, as well as Advaita’s two sons Acyuta and Krsna-dasa as well as Mukunda, Murari Gupta, Gadadhara, Narahari, Vidyanidhi, Candrasekharacarya and Sridhara.

The Lord would do private bhajana with several intimate devotees. Nityananda, like a madman, would constantly weep with choked voice. Saci, the personification of peacefulness, with short stature, would constantly call out, “Nimai, Nimai!”

Visnu-priya was the Lord’s wife. She was busy day and night serving the Lord. She was shy, polite and spoke softly. I went to serve her feet. Staying in Saci’s house in this way, I cannot describe all the work I did there.

Skillful at eating, I ate in joy. Finishing work, I would walk around with him. Every day there was offering in Visnu’s temple consisting of fruit, roots, cheese, butter, condensed milk, spinach, soup, yogurt, sukta, modaka, sweet rice, big laddus, and mistaka, delicious to eat.

Everyday, Sacimata cooked and everyone one ate prasada in bliss. I am the servant of the king of gluttons. For twelve months, I ate from the merciful Lord’s plate. What can I say? There is no comparison with this prasada of which one morsel is equal to nectar.

In this way I stayed in the Lord’s house. Everyone mercifully called me “the servant of Caitanya.” The master of masters is my Caitanya. I would go everywhere with him.

His heart was always overwhelmed with attachment to Krsna. On hearing Krsna’s name, he would cry. If someone said “Radhe!” in a loud voice, immediately he would weep profusely. If someone said, “My life is Krsna” he would come running and embrace him.

One day in the evening, at Srivasa angana the Lord sat with the devotees. At that time Nityananda began shouting repeatedly, “Yamuna! This is the place of the rasa dance. This is the ghata on the Yamuna. Where are the hundreds of gopis and where is Krsna?”

Hearing Nityananda’s words, the Lord quickly came running and jumped into Ballala-sagara. Overflowing with love, the Lord began swimming while the devotees stood and watched.

In this way, daily the Lord’s anuraga increased. From the intense prema his body grew thin. Merciful Caitanya was not satisfied. He said, “Taking sannyasa I will teach the jivas. With grass in my teeth, I will travel to all villages. Giving the name of the Lord, I will deliver all jivas. Giving up family life, I will go to Katwa. Kesava Bharati guru will deliver me. I will not take sannyasa in a noisy house. Otherwise, how will I deliver all jivas?”

The Lord desired to take sannyasa. My heart was most disturbed on hearing this. The Lord called Nityananda and spoke. “I will take sannyasa. Do not prevent me. In pious Magha month during uttarayana I will take sannyasa. Keep this secret. Say this to Mukunda and Gadadhara. Do not broadcast this news everywhere. Indicate this to Saci and do not spread it among the devotees.”

Hearing this, all the servants’ hearts seemed to split into two. “Let me die. I cannot endure the suffering. My master Gauranga will take sannyasa. He will take sannyasa. My heart will break into hundred pieces. How can I work? I am lighter than a blade of grass. A hundred thunderbolts should fall on my head. How can I tolerate separation from the Lord? How will I pass time without Caitanya?”

The Lord then rose and went to Mukunda. I went along, weeping. Because of the tears, I could not see the road. He called Mukunda and spoke. “I will accept the danda and water pot. Giving up sikha and thread, I will accept sannyasa. If I do not do this, how will I deliver the jivas?”

Hearing this, Mukunda began crying. Tears flooded his broad chest. He fell on the ground. The Lord took his hand, raised him, and made him sit.

“O merciful Lord! My life departs. What did you make me hear? Hearing your words, my head breaks from misfortune. Spreading the holy name for a few days, I also will leave the house and take sannyasa.”

Hearing this, the Lord went to Gadadhara and spoke the truth. Hearing his words, Gadadhara shouted. The sky split and fell on his head. He fell on the ground limply. His body became red with streams of blood. Recovering and sitting up, Gadadhara spoke.

“Bring poison. I will drink it immediately. How will I bear separation from the Lord? The pain of being bitten by a million scorpions is insignificant compared to this. If Nimai, my life, renounces, I will go with him because of my attachment. When Murari and other devotees hear this, their minds will become vacant.”

“What is the use of the body if Caitanya leaves? At that moment a thunderbolt will strike our heads.”

Then Gadadhara held his feet and began to speak while showering tears. “When your mother hears this, how will she maintain the life air in her body?”

When the other devotees heard this from each other, they began to speak with agitation. Someone said, “A million scorpions bite my body.” Someone said, “My life burns in fire.” Someone fell from a standing position like a cut down tree. Someone fell backwards with clenched teeth.

Hearing all this, my Visvambara began to inform everyone in detail. The Lord went to the devotees and spoke sweet words to them all. Then, on hearing the news, Sacl fell down unconscious, as if insane. She cried loudly, shedding tears upon her chest, covering it completely. “O child! O Nimai! Where will you go?”

Hearing her lamentation, the birds and animals began weeping. Then Nityananda entered the Lord’s yard and heard the news with Sacl. The words like a thunderbolt split her heart.

She fell on the ground with force. Beating her chest, crying at the top of her voice, she turned the ground into mud with her tears. Hearing this news whispered by others, Visnu-priya sat down, putting her hands on her head. Tears flowed over her chest. It seemed that an ocean of lamentation arose. Gauranga did not glance at her, but went to Srlvasa angana. There at Srlvasa’s house, he began a huge sankirtana and became unconscious. Overwhelmed by the kirtana, he began dancing and his clothing fell off.

His whole body became like a kadamba flower. My dharma-vlra danced in an unconscious state. Blood oozed from his hair pores. His limbs became injured as he fell down.

Telling them to dance, wearing a garland and clapping his hands, he danced with the devotees. On the evening of the Pausa sankranti, the Lord returned to his own house. Many people came and went and Sacl’s lamentation increased. Saying sweet words to his mother, he came to the kitchen and met her. At two praharas in the night the Lord ate and slept. I went to my place to rest, but at the order of the Lord woke up.

At the end of night, the merciful Lord suddenly went outside and called me. “Wait here and be ready,” he said. “Bidding farewell to my mother’s feet, I will come.” Saying this he went inside, again came out and called me.

Disturbed, he said to me, “Come with me. I am going to Katwa to cut my bondage. Do not say this here and there. Taking sannyasa I will deliver the jivas. The jivas of Kali-yuga are selfish, sinful, drink intoxicants and eat meat. Tell me, what is the method for them? Dedicated to their genital and belly, devoid of steadiness, wanting money, they constantly lie. Like worms in the womb, they devour the saliva from young women’s mouths. They reject the streams of nectar of bhakti. They follow prostitutes and like their food. They are absorbed in lustful acts, desiring money and women. For this reason, I will give up my sikha and thread and wander from door to door, giving out the name of the Lord. I will preach the name of the Lord, the maha-mantra which does not require diksa, at the ghata and on the road. Outcastes, youths, householders, children, elders and women, becoming intoxicated with the name, will stand in lines. Young girls and young boys will chant the name. Pasandas, on the path to hell, will become intoxicated with the name. The flag of the name will fly, piercing the sky. Kings and citizens will become choked up together. If I do not accept sannyasa and the kaupina, how will I deliver the sinful, suffering and miserable people? Seeing the contaminated condition of the jiva’s of Kali-yuga, my heart trembles and cannot tolerate it. Accepting the water pot and kaupina, I will take sannyasa. Chanting the names of Radha and Krsna I will deliver everyone. My heart breaks always for the sinful, suffering people.”

As he spoke with me in this way, Sacl devl appeared at that time. In a confused hurry, she came outside and stood facing him, her hands spread out. The Lord held his mother’s feet, bid farewell, and left. Standing at the door, his mother cried.

I followed behind him carrying his wooden shoes. Sacl stood like wooden doll, shedding tears constantly. Leaving by the door, the Lord crossed the Ganga and left. Crossing, he went to Katwa. I followed to perform services.

Whatever astonishing pastimes I saw I recorded as a chronicle and keep according to my ability. We arrived at Katwa in the evening. Cymbals, conches, and bells resounded in every house. In the evening, he saw all the devotees—Mukunda, Candrasekhara, Nityananda, Brahmananda, Gadadhara, Ganga-dasa, his teacher, and the singer Siva. At night filled with prema, Gauranga began dancing while chanting. Thousands of people came to see. Seeing the bhakti for Krsna, everyone was astonished.

Some gave me fruits and others gave garlands. From the Lord’s form, light emanated in all directions. Million Cupids could not be compared to his beauty. The movements of his waist conquered the middle of a damaru drum.

Wherever his eyes glanced there were showers of blue lotuses. He was tall, with arms extending to his knees. With grass in my teeth, I desire dasya-bhakti to Gauranga. In this way, the night passed in dancing and singing. Seeing many people, Gauranga spoke.

“O brothers! Everyone, pay attention to my words. There is no difference between Krsna and Krsna’s name. Worship Krsna, think of Krsna and say Krsna’s name. O brothers! By the power of the name, you will go to the eternal dhama. Everything you see is illusory. In Vedas this is called the shadow of prakrti. Only a shadow of your desires will remain. When Yama comes, he will squeeze your throat. There is no difference between sleeping on a bed or on the ground. Those who see difference will lament. The person who becomes proud on obtaining material objects becomes an insect in hell according to the scriptures.

“There is no difference between a king and a pauper. O brother!

Seeing difference brings about ignorance. A fistful of food fills the king’s belly. A pauper is satisfied with that also. Lying on the ground, one goes to sleep, breathing happily. The king cannot fall asleep on a priceless bed. The king cannot eat gold, diamonds, emeralds or pearls. But intoxicated with wealth, he does not think of this. Just as the pauper will die at his time, the king will go to the house of Yama.

“Maya binds up the king’s eyes. He is blind, moving like an ox used for grinding. The ox walks around in one place and dies. He does not change his course in a million years. When the atmarama becomes liberated, he leaves his material body, like breaking out of a limp cage. When the revered body rots, jackals and dogs eat it and fill their bellies.

“The intoxicated Jiva, sinking in dark samsara, suffers and dies, attached to material objects. In ignorance a person accepts a daughter or son, a palace, a pond, a garden, beautiful women, and gold. Sometimes he is someone’s daughter and sometimes someone’s son and sometimes a husband. Rejecting all material conditions, think of Krsna. Sons, daughters, wealth, grains are all illusory. The only true entity is spiritual consciousness. Speaking about rotten householders, absorbed in sons and daughters, one becomes exhausted. When one increases material possessions with consultations, one simply obtains great pain like an insect in a root. The form of Krsna shines everywhere. A person with pure eyes can see that form.

“The fool sees with material eyes, being attached to material objects. The devotee, eternally liberated, sees with eyes of spiritual knowledge. How can the person whose eyes are blinded by material dust see subtle tattva? Filled with prema, a person sees prema in everyone. “Who knows what is the nature of prema? Is it service to one’s wife? When one sees man and woman as nondifferent, then certainly the tattva of prema will manifest. If prema means longing for children then that is not prema at all.

If one longs for the Lord, then certainly the great souls call that prema.

“With attention hear about the tattva of pure prema. A small wave of prema will cool the burning heart. The devotee does not experience the pain that a young woman feels on seeing a young man. For this reason the devotees worship Krsna. They fix their minds on him in the mood of being his wife. For those who feel longing for the atmarama, how can lust remain in his mind? Just as darkness does not remain where there is light, lust becomes ash near Krsna. Only the longing of prema remains. Saying this, I give healing prema. Yearning for prema, act to attain it. You will be successful and the pain of samsara will go. Life becomes hell by quarrels, argument, hatred and lying, with a duplicitous mind.

“Desiring wealth a householder lies and sometimes cheats and kills other human beings. He speaks rotten household topics and becomes exhausted taking care of sons and daughters. He does not think, “Who do you belong to? Who belongs to you?”

“He becomes busy earning for the material body, a dull lump of matter. He does not see that death is certain. He only thinks that he will live a long time. Or he studies many scriptures, negating matter. All such people become utter fools. The more he studies and becomes intelligent, the more he becomes selfish. He says that other’s wives are like mothers but when alone, he becomes full of lust. For others, lust and anger are enemies but for himself they are friends.

“One can only imagine the unlimited suffering in hell. As long as there is no bhakti to the Lord, such persons stay in hell. One can give up ordinary wealth for oneself, but one can deceive oneself if there are a million coins. The only essence for the jivas of Kali-yuga is the name of the Lord. Chanting that name, one goes to the eternal dhama. Always chant the name of the Lord with hairs standing on end. Why cause difficulty in the market of Kali? What is the difference between a palace and a hut? O brother!

Ask and see from a learned person. Just as a king sleeps on a bed in a palace, a pauper sleeps in his hut. The king eats and drinks from gold vessels and plates. A man in a hut drinks water from an earthen pot. Both are relieved of thirst.

“The gold plate of the king is just an illusion. He has a crown for soup and jewels for vegetables. Can the king eat diamonds cooked nicely? The king intoxicated with pride cannot see this. Again and again, he comes and goes.”

Caitanya taught in this way. There was a crowd of people. Sitting beneath a bilva tree in Katwa, he gave various instructions in a loud voice. The instructions from his mouth were the essence of the Vedas, hearing which, the Jivas are liberated from samsara.

In this way the day and night passed. The next day at dawn, the Lord took his bath. The women wept, wiping their eyes with the edge of their cloth. They shed torrents of tears.

Someone said “His form takes away all suffering. How can his mother maintain her life? He defeats the pride of a million Cupids. His hair is beauty for the eyes. His locks of hair are beautiful and pure. His lotus eyes conquer the blue lotus. I have never seen such an amazing form. Why should Nimai wear a kaupina and carry a danda? Kesava Bharatl is made of stone. Shaving off his hair is most cruel. The garland on his neck is splendid. The beauty of his face illuminates all directions.” In this way the women spoke.

At that time, the Lord called me skilfully. He said, “Quickly bring the ingredients. I will shave off my hair to accept sannyasa. I will no longer remain in the house in a state of bondage. There is hellish pain in the house.”

Hearing this talk, Gadadhara, who was suddha-sattva, Nityananda and Candrasekhara, brought the various materials suitable for sannyasa and filled the sannyasi’s store room. They called and brought a barber named Deva. The barber came and sat under the bilva tree.

Then Caitanya said to the barber, “O Deva! Shave my head. I will go to Vraja.”

Receiving the order from Bharatl, the barber sat and began shaving off his hair. When the barber finally began cutting off all his hair, the women wept loudly. The women said, “O barber! Do not do this job. Do not cut off his hair.” Saying this, they began weeping. Then the barber began shaving him.

Thousands upon thousands of people came to Katwa to see the sannyasa ceremony. At the end of the day, with head shaved, Nimai put on a sannyasi’s dress. With danda and water pot in his hands, he wore a kaupina and red cloth. Standing in front of Bharatl, Nimai excellently illuminated the directions with his beauty. Nityananda, Gadadhara, and Ganga-dasa stood beside him. Seeing the Lord’s astonishing form, in his mind Bharatl offered respects at his lotus feet. In his mind he said, “O Gosai, you are the Lord. The universe of moving and nonmoving beings is dependent on you. Wanting to teach the people, thinking of the suffering people, you put on a kaupina to show the path of bhakti.”

He became a sannyasi in the afternoon. The women began to ululate. The creepers, leaves, branches and trees floated in prema. Birds, animals and insects began dancing. Thousands of people showered flowers. Katwa became the Nandana garden of Svarga.

Bharatl gave him the name Srl Krsna Caitanya. Hundreds of thousands of people gathered. The pious women filled their hands with laja (popped rice) and showered it on his head. The sound of ululation pierced the sky. Everyone became choked up with the taste of bhakti. The name pierced the ether and wandered in the sky. All the devatas heard this and became mad with bliss.

The Lord woke me up in the night. He spent the night chanting japa madly. In the morning, he spoke to Candrasekhara. “You all go to my house in Nadlya. Go with Brahmananda to my mother.

Tell her that I took sannyasa. If she cries, immediately pacify her with words.”

Then Nityananda went with Gadadhara, taking Bharatl as well, while playing in various ways. I followed behind carrying his wooden shoes. Caitanya was intoxicated with the name. Hundreds of thousands of people followed the Lord. Many scholars came to see the Lord.

Rudradeva,Ramaratna,Jagai Pandita, Ganga-dasa, Sambhucandra, famous on earth, Isana, Sankara, Balarama, Gadadhara, Candacandesvara, the best of scholars, Kaslsvara Nyayaratna and Siddhesvara and Pancanana Vedantika as well as Ratnakara, all great scholars, went along with the Lord.

Mad with prema, Krsna Caitanya went joyfully. Dancing constantly, the Lord ran in front. Sometimes he ran and sometimes jumped and fell on the earth. Tears flowed from his eyes. Bharatl cried on tasting prema. Fully absorbed the Lord went eastwards. He ran to Acarya’s house, intoxicated with prema. He stayed some time in Acarya’s house and at that time, Sacl came and was allowed to see him.

Offering respects at his mother’s feet, Lord Krsna Caitanya spoke a few words to satisfy her. Saying a few words, escaping maya, he journeyed south, leaving everyone. Isana, Pratapa, Ganga-dasa, Gadadhara, and Vanesvara went with the Lord.

When we came to Barddhaman, he thought what would be best for me. Glancing at me he spoke. His words fell like streams of nectar. Slapping me on the back he spoke.

“O Govinda! Go to your house.” I was astonished. Laughing he went away in an affected way. When the Lord took sannyasa, I held his kaupina. Giving up pride, I became humble. I no longer had a desire to engage in samsara. I went with the Lord while dancing. Going on the road, I folded my hands and spoke a few words in response.

“O Lord! I will not go to Kancana nagara again. I gave up low family life, considering it to be equal to stool.”

As I said these words, my wife, somehow hearing, had quickly come there. Tears flowing from her eyes, she fell at my feet. With tearful face, she began speaking these words. “Return to the house. I will go with you. You gave the family life because of some ordinary talk. What did you do, making me a maidservant? Where will I go to beg? Who will mercifully give me alms? By what fate will I go to some door and beg, wandering around hungry?”

Hearing her words, I bowed my head. I began to speak eventually in my mind, “Hari Hari.” By remembrance of the Lord, all bondage is cut. I began to think of the Lord’s feet.

Merciful Caitanya then saw me and began speaking sweet words. Hearing the Lord’s words, the suffering woman became soaked with tears and became intelligent. While she cried, glancing in all directions, the Lord spoke words of truth to her. Suddenly hearing the Lord’s words, she wiped her eyes with the cloth as she wept. The Lord’s mercy arose because of her crying. He then turned to me and spoke. He said, “Govinda! Remain in your house. I will go to Puri with another servant.”

Hearing those words, my eyes filled with tears. I held his feet in pain. My tears covered his feet. The Lord turned and left. My neighbors gathered and began speaking, making me confused. I began speaking about the poison of family life. I explained the secret of women. “O brother! Hear the woman’s message. The wife becomes the life of the husband. If you look at a portion of the atma, you will see the wife and husband together. Those who think that this is sweeter than nectar close their eyes and drink the wife’s saliva. The rascal who thinks this happiness is eternal bliss passes his life uselessly. Gauranga is the complete eternal Brahman. Giving up his association I will accept family life.”

While saying this, Damodara took me to Kaslmitra’s house. Kaslmitra was a most pious person. The Lord went and stayed in his house. Arranging the meal, Mitra offered fine rice. Seeing the rice, the Lord praised it. The Lord said, “This rice is very fine. What do people call this rice?”

Mitra said, “It is called Jagannatha-bhoga. If one prepares a meal from it, all desires are fulfilled.” Hearing the word “Jagannatha-bhoga” the Lord was astonished. Immediately waves of prema began to flow. Weeping he said, “O Jagannatha! Quickly bring me to you and accept me.” Cooking spinach, soup and various items, putting them together, the Lord was intoxicated with bliss. The directions became fragrant with the aroma of beto-saka. My mind became unsteady, with the food being offered.

The Lord said, “Quickly bring tulasi. Make the offering, I will energetically distribute prasada. Your child here is very hungry. He glances around many times.”

I felt most ashamed at the Lord’s words. I lowered my head. After the offering, he distributed prasada. The life air was satisfied with sukta and cooked vegetables. I ate eight pieces of fried bitter melon happily. I took big mouthfuls of food. Cukra amla became like nectar with raw sugar. Eating plenty, my face became filled with bliss.

Gauranga left Mitra’s house in the afternoon. He ran to the south, into a trap of prema. Eventually I came to the village of Hajipura. Chanting the name, the Lord made the village insane. Outside the village there was a huge banyan tree. I sat there gently and in the evening the Lord began sankirtana. The name pierced the ether and rose in the sky. The Lord began dancing, intoxicating the place. Where was his kaupina and cord? His clothing became disheveled. Sometimes he fell with force on the ground. Saliva flowed from his mouth here and there and his voice choked up. Hundreds of people joined and became insane with nama-sankirtana. All the men and women became mad with the name.

By good fortune, that was the excellence of the name’s power. Small boys and girls, elders, as well as youths and young women clapped their hands and danced with devotion.

Half the night passed in this way. The Lord then cooked the begged rice. One devotee of the village brought ghee. The Lord fried my bitter melon with ghee. My king of Nadiya offered nma sukta with ghee and fried bitter melon. The Lord ate only a fistful of prasada. Then I sat down with a plate. The Lord filled my plate with prasada. Fidgeting, I took the prasada. When I filled up my stomach and stood up, I went to the Lord’s feet and surrendered.

The Lord touched my stomach and immediately my stomach became normal. I then shouted “Hari Hari.” The devotees were most astonished. The next day at dawn, beautiful Gauranga got up, called the devotees and spoke in detail. He took permission from the devotees to leave. With his associates, he then left, running. When he reached Medinipura, hearing the news, people came running.

Among them one wealthy person approached. He was speechless on seeing the form of the Lord. Named Kesava Samanta, he was very rich. On various pretexts he spoke on various topics.

Laughing, he said, “O best of sannyasis! Take some money, that your heart desires. Give up the kaupina and wear clothing. Why does a young man take sannyasa? O yogi! Be happy serving your senses. Why do you die, serving renunciation?”

Hearing the wealthy man’s words, the Lord slightly smiled and with humility gave instructions. He spoke. “Scriptures say that money, gold, and emeralds are transformations of earth. The transformations of earth will always become earth again. Why are you puffed up with pride? Do not step into the Lord’s trap of maya. If you do so, you will repeatedly have to die. The person who wants to die repeatedly never gives up the bondage of maya. Giving up everything, a person should worship with devotion.

Then the highest bliss will arise in his mind.

“Wandering about thinking, ‘This is mine,’ one does not know one is entering the mouth of death. When death chews you up in his teeth, how many beautiful women will stay with you? How much gold, silver and wealth do you have at that time? How many milk sweets remain at that time? Your respected body will be burned to ashes. If the body does not rot, jackals will eat it. Your head will be mixed with a shoemaker’s stool.

“Worship Krsna. Chant his name. Useless time will go away. Why are you proud of all these temporary things? The eternal entity is Krsna. He can bring peace. O wealthy man! Hear my words. Can you eat diamonds, pearls and emeralds? One fistful of food allays hunger. Then why are you so proud all the time?” In this way, the Lord instructed the wealthy man. Saying a few words, glancing at me. “Let us go to Narayana-gada. Going there, we will be happy.” Saying only this, he got up quickly and I put the wooden shoes on my shoulders.

Gauranga was absorbed in bliss on the road. In the evening, we came to that place. At Narayana-gada, there was a Siva-dhalesvara. He ran to see the deity. This was the village deity at Narayana-gada.

The Lord began to weep. He said in a loud voice, “Hara, Hara!” and fell on the ground. He became choked up with prema. His clothing and water pot fell away. Great sattvika-bhavas manifested. His hairs stood on end in prema and blood oozed out. His outer garment and kaupina fell off. Some sannyasis as well as vanaprasthas and brahmacaris came there to see the bhava of the Lord, the Lord of Nadiya.

They were all astonished on seeing the prema and bhakti. They fell on the ground saying that he was a devata. Chanting the Lord’s name, the Lord began to dance. Seeing his great prema, everyone’s hairs stood on end. Everyone danced under a tree.

I, most sinful and low, seeing some laddus, wanted them. I developed greed to eat them and fill my stomach. The Lord, antaryami, understanding my intentions, being merciful, gave me laddus to eat. Eating four or five laddus, my stomach became full.

One brahmana brought and offered cool water. Gradually all the people of the village, hearing the news, gathered at that place. Offering bhoga, the Lord divided the prasada. Assembling, everyone took prasada. Seeing the devotion when they took prasada, I felt strong emotion. I was greedy, and first filled my stomach. Then I became repentant in my mind. Wanting to destroy my sins, I held the Lord’s feet. Saying various words, he placed his foot on my head. The confusion in my mind immediately dissipated.

Then the Lord danced with absorption. Everyone danced, intoxicated with the rasa of the Lord. Some danced and some rolled on the ground. Someone ran to a tree, saying, “Krsna!” Gradually many people gathered at that place.

Men and women, young and old, everyone listened to the words of the new sannyasi. One by one they came and sat at that place. Viresvara Sena and Bhavani Sankara came with many people to see the Lord. Four groups of horses and elephants and many vehicles gathered before the Lord. Bhavani Sankara was a very wealthy man. He came with hundreds of people on the back of an elephant with drums and colorful flags. He made the four directions beautiful.

They were all intoxicated with pride, insects of material enjoyment. Seeing this, the Lord was merciful within. Seeing their condition, merciful Caitanya made them fortunate, giving them bhakti. Giving them teachings on bhakti, the Lord made them intoxicated. Hundreds of thousands of people became like dolls. With grass in his teeth, folding his hands, the Lord spoke.

“Everyone! Hear my ordinary words. Listen, all wealthy people! The world is like a gypsy’s magic. Overwhelmed by sleep, you climb on a throne and think you are a king. You consider hundreds of people to be your friends. Hundreds of thousands of citizens come and offer gifts. You never think what is all this? this is a shadow of a reflection, ignorance in the material world. The material world is a reflection of krsna-tattva. The Vedas say it is a reflected dream. Understand there are two dreams. The only difference is that one is while sleeping and the other is while in a waking state. Being a king is a dream in the waking state. The Vedas say that this is really an illusion.

“Gold, silver, gems, and pearls are transformations of earth. The entity to be respected is Krsna. This is the real meaning. The Vedas say that Bhagavan is the eternal entity. Everything else you see is false. How can the person who is sinking in water see the entity on the bank? If you come out of the water to the bank, then you can see the object up close. How can the person absorbed in material objects see Radha and Krsna? The person whose eyes are covered by the blinkers of maya is always blind, like a bull at the grinding wheel.

“Who knows what is in the mountain cave? Who knows from outside what is the truth? In this way, who can understand in a practical way the subtle nature of the material world? The form of the Lord is the unlimited universe. The person who understands this gives up karma-kanda. When you give up material thinking and become spiritual, you can see the form of Krsna.

“The independent will of Krsna has given sakti to the material world. The person who has bhakti can see this. Matter and the Jiva are tied together. One who crosses the shore is freed from rajas and tamas. Who can cross the condition of the material world? One remains rotating like an ox at the oil mill. The ox is on a small narrow path going in a circle and falls into difficulty. Like the ox that walks on one path with blinkers on his eyes, going around and around, the jiva rotates in terrifying samsara, wearing blinkers of maya. For this reason, the jiva cannot see the subtle truth. As long as the jiva seeks other’s material objects and other’s wives, how can he achieve Krsna? The Vedas say that Krsna is the unchanging tattva. One cannot understand him with a changeable mind.”

In this way, the Lord made many places fortunate. Merciful Caitanya ran to Jalesvara. At Jalesvara there was a deity of Siva called Bilvesvara. Seeing that deity bhakti arose within the Lord. There was one sannyasi in the Siva temple. The Lord approached him slowly. Facing that sannyasi, he offered respects. Seeing the Lord, the sannyasi was surprised.

He said, “Who are you? You are not an ordinary man. Why do you offer respects to me? By what piety do I see you today? Seeing you, my bondage has been severed. You are the result of austerity. O merciful one! Seeing you, all sin is destroyed.”

Gazing at the Lord, the sannyasi began weeping and his voice choked up in prema. At that moment my Lord hid his form and saying, “Hari,” raised his arms and began dancing. Saying, “Krsna,” he jumped, stood and sometimes fell on the ground with choked voice.

There were many people in nama-sankirtana. Caitanya stayed awake and passed the night. The next day he went to the Suvarna-rekha River. He saw Raghunatha dasa whose hairs were standing on end. We then went to Harihara-pura. There Nimai became mad with the name of the Lord. Dancing and dancing, he was without awareness and fell on the ground with force. In this way the day passed. Intoxicated with bliss, the Lord began crying.

The next day we went to Balesvara. Seeing Gopala there, the Lord was filled with bliss. The next day we went to Nilagada. Going there, Nimai became mad with the name. Dancing, he became unconscious. Uncountable people came. Singing and singing the name, the bliss increased. Becoming unconscious, the Lord fell on the ground.

In this way, the devotees together became intoxicated with the experience of the highest bliss. The next day the Lord went to the bank of Vaitarani River. Weeping he said, “O Krsna! please cross.” Choked with prema, his body extended. Saying, “Hari,” he went, not seeing the surroundings.

The next day, we crossed Mahanadi. On the road we saw the deity of Gopinatha. Everyone received Gopinatha’s maha-prasada. Taking the prasada, bliss rose in the mind. Wanting to see Saksi Gopala everyone began walking with great attraction. Saying, “Hari,” raising their hands, they ran. The ground became mud because of their tears. Seeing Saksi Gopala from a distance, the Lord choked with prema and fell on the ground. Seeing the deity, what did he think? Seeing the face of the deity, he began crying.

Repeatedly he called out, “Gopala! Gopala!” Who can describe the force of his prema? Then going to Nimraja’s temple, the Lord began crying. How can it be described? Leaving Nimraja we went to Ataranala. Seeing the flag, the Lord held on to me. I have not seen such a flood of tears. The Lord made the earth into mud by the river of his tears.

Saying, “O Prabhu Jagannatha!” he flooded the earth with torrents of tears. Falling on the ground, he cried at the top of his voice. He embraced whomever he saw in front of him.

“Look! My Krsna dances as a cowherd. What beauty is in his hair!” Seeing the Lord’s temple, he cried loudly. Sometimes he fell on the ground in lamentation. Running quickly, his body became covered with dust at the gate. Because of the flowing tears he could not see the deity. He fell on the ground, shouted and rolled about.

Hundreds of thousands of people came to see his bhava. With great difficulty, the Lord stopped his flow of prema. Seeing the Lord, Mahavisnu, rose up, weeping. The devotees came running at the astonishing sound of weeping. Gadadhara placed him on his lap. Coming to the Garuda stambha, holding on to it, he cut his forehead. Blood flowed. Seeing this, Dhyana Puri gave his upper cloth and wiped away the Lord’s blood. Having seen Jagannatha, in a group they went to Misra’s house.

Staying some days in Puri in this way, the Lord experienced newer and newer happiness constantly. Nityananda, Krsna-dasa, and Hari-dasa experienced the greatest bliss with the Lord. Puri became filled with the sound of the name all day and night. All were on the ground with choked voices. Some wore garlands on their limbs and some smeared sandalwood. Some arranged for offering food. Gradually everyone came and assembled. Puri surpassed glorious Vaikuntha.

The brahmana Krsna-dasa along with pot-bellied Syama-dasa protected the Lord on two sides. Sometimes he fell down, intoxicated with prema. Sometimes he jumped into the ocean. Prema-dasa and Gopidasa Mohanta brahmana showered nectar in reciting Bhagavatam. Raghunatha dasa, Candrasekhara, Damodara, Narahari and Gadadhara always went together, surrounding Caitanya, to the temple. Without interruption, there were sweet mrdangas playing and sometimes karatalas, as he became intoxicated with the name.

In this way my Lord always experienced bliss in Misra’s house with the devotees. Kasi Misra always brought plenty of prasada and fed Caitanya. It attracted the heart by its mere fragrance. He filled his stomach with fried and roasted items and various amounts of prasada. There was excellent fried chickpeas, crushed mung dhal, and fine sesame, linseed, wheat and barley. There were hundreds of fruits and roots, and scrapped coconut. The Lord constantly took these in his hands and offered them. There was cinacura, khuramara, laddu, and gaja, amdhasa, pistaka, puli, rasapura-gaja, ghee soaked rice, bhuta-ghanta and beto-saka.

He accepted all this prasada and left nothing. In silence he always ate, filling his stomach fully. He ate till his stomach swelled. In this way, days passed. Gradually all the devotees began coming. Sankara Bharatl, Parananda Purl, Damodara Svaml, Pradyumna Brahmacarl, Cidananda Giri, and Premananda Sarasvatl would always accompany the Lord.

Many devotees met together and worshipped at Purl with great joy. At this time Sarvabhauma made his appearance. Many devotees came and met with him. Seeing Mahavisnu, the Lord had great attraction. Repeatedly, the Lord offered respects with devotion. The Lord fainted on seeing Govinda. He remained there as if dead. Sarvabhauma Bhattacarya was at that place. Taking him on his lap, he went to his house. He performed many services for Caitanya. His bhakti-rasa grew. Finally the Lord gave him bhakti.

The Lord desired to travel to the south. For three months, Caitanya stayed in Purl with Nityananda. On the seventh tithi of Vaisakha, he travelled south, floating in prema-rasa. When he was about to travel, worried Nityananda said with humility and devotion, “Do not go alone. Go together with us.”

Gadadhara, Premananda Sarasvatl, Bharatl and Sankara were ready to go with him. Hearing this, my Lord smiled and said, “I will go alone, not taking anyone.”

Hearing those words, Nityananda said, while shedding tears, “When you go travelling south far away, go with Krsna-dasa Brahmana. Being pure, the brahmana will do whatever you tell him. How can I remain giving you up?” Then everyone said, “We will all go with you.”

Hearing this, Mahaprabhu smiled slightly and prevented them. He gave instructions to them all. After hearing him, they all said, “Govinda dasa will go with you.” Hearing this, the Lord smiled at me slightly. “I like Govinda’s association. Govinda will go where others will not. Govinda will do work for me.”

Saying this the Lord bid farewell and travelled south. All the devotees pursued him. Eventually we all came softly to the temple of Alala-natha. Seeing the deity, the Lord’s emotions increased. He moistened the earth with his tears. Dancing and dancing, the Lord became unconscious and fell to the ground with force. At dawn the next day he bid farewell to all. Three men went on the southern tour.

Sarvabhauma at this time said softly, “Meet Raya on the bank of the Godavari. Ramananda is the best devotee who knows rasa. Because of Krsna’s name his eyes are always filled with tears. Ramananda experiences pure bliss. He experiences bliss within because of the Lord’s name.”

Hearing this, the Lord rushed to the bank of the Godavari. At that place Ramananda came and met him. Seeing the new sannyasi, his bhakti arose. Grasping his feet, Ramananda began weeping. He said, “You are the Lord. I am most fortunate to see you.”

The Lord said, “O Raya! Please speak about Krsna. The pain in my heart will disappear by your conclusions.”

Ramananda said, “O Lord! I know nothing. If you do not make me speak, I cannot speak. Residing in the heart, you do everything. A mute person by your mercy becomes eloquent.”

The Lord said, “By what tattva does the mind become pure?”

Raya said, “That tattva is association with devotees.”

“I want to know the fine tattva in this.”

Raya said, “Other than renunciation there is no tattva.”

The Lord said, “The fine tattva is attraction.”

Raya said, “Higher than that is prema-bhakti.”

The Lord said, “Please say more of the essence. O intelligent one!’

Raya said, “The essence of everything is having rasa.”

“O Ramananda! I want you to speak more about the essence.”

At that time the Lord lowered his head. He said, “Ghee exists hidden in milk. The person who churns attains the taste. O Raya! I do not know anything. Speak about Krsna. I will hear from your mouth. You are a detached Vaisnava, Ramananda. Speak krsna-tattva and soothe my heart.”

Hearing the Lord’s words, Ramananda, with humility, folded his two hands and spoke.

“O Lord! Why do you deceive the world? Be merciful and make me your servant. You have spread bhakti-rasa in many places. Mercifully, you have purified this earth. O Jagannatha! Be merciful to this fallen person. Give detachment in my heart. Accept me to be with you.”

Hearing this the Lord embraced Ramananda. Kneeling, Ramananda wept. Tears flooded his chest. Seeing this, with choked voice the Lord spoke.

“You are the best of devotees O Ramananda!”

Lowering his head Ramananda said, “Rama, Rama!”

The Lord said, “You are most fortunate. O Ramananda! Your auspicious talks on bhakti cannot be described.”

Ramananda said, “I am a most fallen rascal. You have been contaminated by touching me. Mercifully forgive this offense. Dwell in my heart and give a taste for bhakti.”

Ramananda and the Lord passed the night speaking in this manner. The next day Ramananda held the Lord’s feet. Bidding farewell, he departed to do his work.

The Lord said, “I will go. You go to Puri and stay with me. You, I and Sarvabhauma alone will discuss krsna-tattva, giving relief to our lives.”

Saying this, the Lord bid farewell to Raya and, offering respects, Ramananda went to his house. I have not included in this book a hundredth of what Raya discussed with the Lord. In this way Ramananda for ten days came and was blissful, seeing the sannyasi of Nadlya. The Lord, seeing Ramananda, was very pleased. Seeing the Lord, Ramananda became unconscious.

Bidding farewell to Raya, the Lord entered Trimanda. Many Buddhists lived in Trimanda town. They all came and met Gaurasundara. The Lord discussed with the Buddhists. The king of Trimanda came and acted as a referee. The Buddhists accepted defeat in the deliberation. All the spectators began to laugh. They all said, “This sannyasi is not human. A human cannot speak what he discussed. The head of the Buddhists, Ramagiri Raya, offered respects and said, “Show the path to me. You are not a human being. O young sannyasi! I would like to remain with you. I was a great learned man in the world. Mercifully show the path of bhakti to me.”

Laughing, Caitanya mercifully spoke. “You are most intelligent, Ramagiri Raya. The person who chants the name of the Lord with hairs standing on end is most intelligent. That is the method.”

Hearing the Lord’s words, Ramagiri Raya immediately fell on the ground. Falling at the Lord’s feet, Ramagiri said, “You are merciful to speak to a low person. Dwelling in all jivas, you see all jivas. Mercifully give your red feet to me.”

Bhakti manifested in Ramagiri, the pasanda. Seeing this, my Lord became filled with bliss. All the greatest Buddhist scholars went on the path of Ramagiri. The young sannyasi defeated all their arguments. Seeing this, Ramagiri looked in the four directions.

Finally, a resident of Tungabhadra, Dhundhi-rama-tlrtha, desiring debate, came. Thinking himself learned, intoxicated with pride, this jnani could not understand the path of bhakti by his dry logic. Dhundhirama was a learned man. He discussed but became full of fear. Dhundhirama Svami, coming to deliberate, rolled on the ground repeatedly with tears in his eyes.

The Lord said, “Listen, Dhundhirama Svami. I will secretly write a letter saying that I was defeated in debating with you. Caitanya was defeated in your place. By the mercy of Sarasvati, you are a great scholar. Who can defeat you in the scriptures of logic? You are qualified with logic, Sankhya, Patanjala yoga and Vedanta philosophies, with all scriptures. Being a foolish sannyasi, I know nothing. Repeatedly I am defeated in your presence. Formerly you were a great scholar in Dhundhi. Your learning was famous in the world.”

Saying this, he bid farewell to Dhundhirama. Looking in the four directions, Dhundhi did not want him to go. Looking here and there, he rolled at the Lord’s feet, having been purified in mind. The Lord gave bhakti to the pasanda Dhundhi. Remembering the Lord, Gauranga went to Panthaguha. Dhundhirama became famous as a servant of the Lord. The learned spoke many stories about him.

The Lord called me while laughing. I was carrying two wooden shoes on my shoulders. Whatever goods were there, the shoes and begging bowl, whatever Ramananda had given, I gathered together. There was a banyan tree very far away called Aksaya. In the evening the Lord arrived there. Siva, called Vatesvara resided there. The Lord ran there with devotion.

With devotion the Lord offered respects to Vatesvara. He passed the night there without eating. In the morning he went to take bath. I went from door to door to beg food. Begging, I returned at noon. I then cooked and served Gauranga. I accepted prasada, equal to nectar. At that time, a wealthy man of the tirtha came there.

He came with two prostitutes to see him, to test the seriousness of his sannyasa. The two prostitutes named Satyabai and Laksmibai came to the Lord and spoke. On the instructions of the wealthy man, the two made many arrangements to understand the Lord. Tirtharama (the wealthy man) spoke various subjects. The effulgence of the sannyasi took away their pretense. Laksmi and Satyabai while laughing made various gestures. Smiling Satyabai sat next to the Lord. She opened her bodice to show her breasts. The Lord addressed her as mother. At the Lord’s words, Satya began to tremble. Seeing this, Laksmi became afraid. The Lord’s mind was not at all agitated. She fled and Satyabai fell at his feet.

“O mother! You make me an offender.” Saying only this, the Lord fell on the ground. His hair became loose and he became covered with dust. His body quivered violently with prema.

He became all disheveled. Where did Laksmi and Satya go? They were not seen again.

The Lord began dancing saying “Hari, Hari!” His hairs stood on end and he shed profuse tears. His kaupina and outer cloth fell off. Naked he danced, breathing deeply. He fell on the ground, not aware of the thorns. The cluster of garlands fell from his throat. Having not eaten, he was flesh and bone. Blood oozed from his thin limbs.

Intoxicated with the name, Gauranga danced. Astonishing effulgence emanated from his limbs. Seeing this, the wealthy person became amazed and fell at his feet in surrender. Though he pressed the Lord’s feet, the Lord was unaware. Saying, “Hari,” with raised hands he danced and walked forward.

Holding Satya in his arms, he said, “Chant the name of the Lord. Say Hari! O Pranesvara, Mukunda, Murari! Where is the Lord? Where is Mukunda? Where is Murari?”

Seeing the Lord’s ecstasy, everyone became unconscious. Intoxicated with the name, the Lord had noexternal consciousness. Bending his shoulders, he fell down, his pranas being afflicted. Saliva flowed from his mouth. His limbs were covered with dust.

He was naked. His hairs stood on end and his eyes were closed.

Seeing his bhava, many Buddhists said “Hari, Hari.” Hearing this, Gauranga’s eyes flowed with tears. The tears flowed out as if from a syringe. Seeing this Tirtharama began crying. He said, “I am a big pasanda. O Lord! Mercifully give me hari-nama.”

Embracing Tirtharama, the pasanda, the Lord said, “O Tirtharama! You are a gentleman. I have become pure by your presence. You are the chief devotee.” He said this many times. The wealthy Tirtharama fell at his feet. Overwhelmed, he cried and cried. When bhakti arose as he cried, suddenly the Lord took his hand and embraced him.

The Lord said, “Consider wealth to be like grass. Then you will receive the priceless jewel of bhakti, the greatest wealth. Throw far way clothing and ornaments. Reject temporary wealth and worship the eternal wealth for you will repeatedly receive pain. I advise you to become desireless. The body which you find attractive is just covered by skin. In a few days it will rot. When the prana flies from the body like a bird, it becomes insects, ashes or stool. There is no greatness in wealth in the three worlds. The only greatness is in worshipping the Lord. Knowing the fickle nature of wealth, reject it completely and keep it far away.

“Having faith in the Lord, bring the Lord close. There is no need to speak further proof. Unlimited universes are the proof. The learned person does not need to see the proof. He does not see the goal in many arguments. Bring Krsna with the faith of a sadhaka. What is use in discussing many scriptures? Having faith, worship Krsna.

“The person proud of wealth brings suffering to himself day by day. The person who desires respect and glorifies it cannot prevent suffering. He thinks in terms of ‘This is me and this is mine.’ Closing his eyes, he does not see a second person or another’s possessions. He sees others falsely as his relatives. He grieves for a dead body which is like a broken doll.

“Everyone knows that the son is born from a father. The Vedas do not say that the two hearts are one. If the son dies, this does not accomplish the father’s death. The son is born from the mother’s body, but know for certain that the bodies of the two are not one. No one possesses anything. This is the essential ascertainment. That need not be proved by any proof. The Lord is the object of proof and he is the proof. The proof is present in the heart of a human. A foolish person knows the Lord from very far off. From knowing the Lord close up, a person is considered a JnanL I have spoken the essential tattva as explained in the Veda. One does not seek it in the world of fools. The person who knows all the true tattvas does not die again and again.”

Hearing all this from the Lord’s mouth, Tirtharama gave up all attachment to matter and chanted the name of the Lord. The Lord became intoxicated with sankirtana. Eventually his associates gathered.

Wealthy Tirtharama became distressed. The pasandas spoke to him. Tirtharama gave up material items considering them to be like grass and spreading his arms danced, saying the name of the Lord. Putting on tilaka, donning a kaupina, performing bhakti, he delivered the fallen and deprived. Hearing this, his wife, crying, rushed there immediately.

Holding her husband’s feet, she began weeping. Tirtharama did not hear her words. Her name was Kamala Kumari. She was very beautiful and spread effulgence in all directions. Holding her hands, he said, “O Kamala! I have given all my wealth to you. I have been delivered from hell. You may enjoy all this material wealth.”

Hearing this, Kamala Kumari fell upon the ground. Seeing the net of Kamala’s maya, Tirtharama smiled slightly and said, “Chant the Lord’s name.”

Weeping, Kamala Kumari left and Tirtha became a beggar on the road. Having delivered Tirtharama, Gauranga left Siddha Vatesvara.

Many people brought many clothes but the Lord did not accept one. He shouted “Govinda” and finally hit me on the back. “Having spent seven days in Vatesvara, let us go to see Nandisvara.” Hearing this, putting the shoes on my shoulders, I went with the Lord, leaving Vatesvara.

Going on the path I saw a huge jungle and my mind became disturbed. The jungle was ten krosa’s in breadth. “How will I cross this forest?” I thought. The Lord as my antaryaml slightly laughed. The Lord went in front. I was behind him. In this way I went on the path. By his desire, I had no fear. In the forest there were some animals living. He did not look at any of them in front or behind. Crossing the jungle, he sat at the base of a tree near Munna town, desiring to take rest.

Two householders living in Munna approached. They brought and gave some flour with great care. The Lord did not comment whether this was good or bad. The two men sat near the Lord eventually. The two looked at the young sannyasi but he did not glance once at them.

In the evening there was big confusion. People of the town came in droves to see. Effulgence flowed from the Lord’s limbs like fire. Seeing this, everyone was astonished. The people of Munna eventually approached and each came and offered respects. They spoke with devotion. “Come into the town and leave the base of this tree.”

Not hearing their words, my Lord was mad with prema. Internally he was saying the Lord’s name and continuously weeping. As the emotion swelled within, he suddenly stood up and began dancing. He fell on the ground, saying, “Hari!” With him, the people of the town became joyful. Clapping their hands, they all began dancing. Seeing that, the Lord began crying. Any pasanda who saw him developed bhakti in his mind.

In this way, half the night passed. Everyone danced saying, “Hari Hari.” Finally, from their families, the women came to see Gauranga at that place. Seeing the beauty of Gauranga, the women could not leave. “He has such a bright face.” Seeing the sannyasi their prana became unsteady.

“O sister! I have never seen such a handsome young man.” Caitanya spoke to them. “This body of bones and skin will die without eating. Why do you tie up your hair at this age?”

Weeping, overwhelmed, tears flowed from his eyes. In this way, the night passed. He did not sleep. In the morning the Lord rose and went south. The crowd of people of Munna told him to stay. My Lord would not hear of any obstruction. One woman of the place, suffering greatly, wept at the base of that tree. When the Lord was about to go, the old woman begged food and clothing.

She wore torn cloth and had no food in her stomach. Caitanya stood there looking at her. Seeing her condition, the Lord understood in his mind. He begged, while smiling slightly. He said, “O brothers, Munna inhabitants! Give alms to me. When I receive the food and clothing, I will depart.”

The residents of Munna, men and women, floated in bliss. They brought and gave heaps of food and clothing. Looking at him, they said it was supplies for the journey. For this reason, they brought heaps of goods. Overwhelmed, they gave the clothing to the Lord. Seeing the bustle, the Lord began to laugh. They said, “There is nothing as valuable as this cloth. O Caitanya! First, take my cloth.” No one could understand the mind of the Lord.

Thus, they busily brought food and cloth. The Lord spoke. “Listen, people of Munna. I accept your offering. There is a grieving woman living at the base of the tree. Give all the food and clothing to her.”

Seeing his merciful nature, they were all astonished. Someone said, “I have brought alms for this old woman.”

The Lord put on his cloth and left while chanting the Lord’s name. Giving me an indication, he glanced at me. Taking the bowl and the wooden shoes, I followed behind. Many people started walking together.

The Lord did not look back. All the people returned home one by one.

Seeing Narasimha, an ocean of prema arose. Sometimes the Lord fell on the ground and sometimes talked incoherently like a madman. Foam sprayed from his mouth. Sometimes his upper cloth became damp with perspiration. Trembling he fell on the earth. Some said, “Some madman has come.” Some fell at his feet.

There was a brahmana sadhaka called Yudhisthira, the best of devotees, of good conduct. At this place, the brahmana performed worship and prayers. He would daily recite the Gita, while weeping. Everyone laughed at the ignorant brahmana for reading Gita. He did not understand it but he liked the Gita. In studying it, he ignored what others said. He read it alone with absorbed heart. When he read it, he cried. Seeing the tears, the Lord’s mind melted.

The Lord said, “O respected brahmana! Why do you cry?”

The brahmana said, “When I read the Gita I experience great bliss. I see Krsna on Arjuna’s chariot. O sannyasi! With that greed, I read the Gita.”

The Lord said, “You have seen Krsna. Give me mercy and embrace me. I have never seen a devotee equal to you. If I worship you, I will be able to see Krsna.”

The brahmana glanced once at the Lord. He rolled at the Lord’s feet.

The Lord said, “O respected brahmana! Listen. Do not say this everywhere. You are most fortunate. You are the best of devotees. Krsna lets you see him always.”

The brahmana said, “You are Krsna. You made this happen.” Saying this, he held the Lord’s feet strongly. Backing away, the Lord spoke to the brahmana. Leaving everything, the brahmana went with the Lord. Bidding farewell to the brahmana, Sacinandana then went to Rsabha Parvata.

Parananda Puri lived at Rsabha Parvata. The Lord proceeded to see him. He discussed about Krsna with him a lot. Then he went to Ramanatha town. At that place were Rama’s footprints. He gazed at those prints with showers of tears. His hairs stood on end and he trembled. He fell unconscious on the ground. He touched those feet and his hairs stood on end. He breathed heavily. After seeing those lotus feet, he went to see many tirthas. He went to Ramesvara and took bath there. Gauranga took darsana of Siva. Siva has an astonishing form called Ramesvara. Seeing Siva, Mahaprabhu offered prayers.

Many holy men stayed there constantly. They all came at that time one by one. Seeing the Lord, one detached scholar desired to have a big debate. The Lord said, “I do not want to debate. I have been defeated by you.”

Seeing the astonishing humility in his eyes, the sannyasi began to think, being ignorant.

The Lord said, “O great sannyasi! What are you thinking? Do not speak all topics. By strong bhakti all this is removed. O brother! Sing about the qualities of Krsna in your pure heart. Worship Krsna, speak of Krsna, and chant Krsna’s name. Chanting Krsna’s name, go to the eternal abode. There is no shelter other than Krsna. This is my advice.

“You will attain suffering over and over, here and there. What is the use of pride, O devotee! What is the goal in being learned and deliberating? The sinful scholar is imprisoned in hell. All this is written in the scriptures. What will the person do, who, knowing many scriptures, after deliberating, acts out of lust? The pasanda who, wanting money, cheats, does not understand the words of scripture. If one’s mind hankers for women and gold, one’s study of the Vedas is fake. If envy always plays in someone’s heart, Yamaraja will punish him with his own hand. He whose heart melts with the name of the Lord is a very learned man. I have concluded this. The person in whose eyes tears of bliss flow from chanting the name is called a very wise man. O brother! The person who, reading and hearing, does not have taste for Krsna, is a fool and most impure.”

Hearing these words from the Lord’s mouth, the yogi was silent for some time. All the detached sannyasis gathered around the Lord and began to listen to his message, becoming unconscious. Finally, the Lord raised his two hands, and, mad with the name, became intoxicated. The Lord fell down and cut his chin on the stone. Streams of blood flowed out. The great scholar wiped away the blood carefully.

For three days the Lord performed kirtana at Setubandha. He went south to see a madhavi forest. At the madhavi forest lived one silent austere man. My begging master went to see him. There was an astonishing effulgence from the sannyasi’s form. A white beard hung over his chest. His nails had grown very long. He was sitting naked and silent. He had no cloth, no danda and no water pot. Caitanya looked at him steadily. His closed eyes were very peaceful. The base of a tree was his home and the sky was his clothing. He had no material desire. He was absorbed in austerities.

My Lord stood with folded hands in front of him. Caitanya uttered many polite praises. But the best of sannyasi did not want to return to consciousness. After three days some detached members brought and prepared fruit and roots. After three days, the great yogi ate some food to maintain his life. Breaking his meditation, he looked around. At that time, the Lord began to speak.

The yogi did not understand the Lord’s words. They then spoke in samskrt. The great yogi listened to those words with seriousness. He spoke a few words with the Lord.

“Cambani sinadi,” he said and laughed. Saying “cambani sinadi” with pure mind, he laughed and offered respects at the Lord’s feet. Gauranga offered respects in return. Floating in bliss, he sang the qualities of Krsna. Seeing the yogi offer respects, all the sannyasis held the Lord’s feet.

At that place the Lord held discussions. After seven days he departed from there. At that place there was a tirtha named Tattva-kundi. The Lord went to take bath there. Then he saw Tamraparni River. He went there to take bath. On the day of Magha full moon, many guests came to bathe at the bank of the Tamraparni. Waiting a fortnight there, they came and bathed on the Magha full moon day.

Crossing the Tamraparni, the Lord went to see Kanyakumari on the shore of the ocean. There were no forests or mountains nearby. One could only hear the sound of the ocean. Big waves came in there, consciously singing about the qualities of the Lord. Seeing that condition, the Lord became blissful. By increased prema, his hairs stood on end.

The sand was like a mountain in the shape of a stupa, broadcasting the glories of the Lord. The ocean called out hum hum constantly. What more can I say? It was most beautiful. I had never seen anything so beautiful. The mind became pure on seeing the beauty there.

The Lord called, “O Govinda!” Slightly smiling, he called me to bathe. The waves like mountains, came in with great speed. With great devotion the Lord bathed there. After bathing, the Lord wept saying, “Hari!” Prema rose in his heart. His hairs stood on end and his forehead perspired. Hairs stood on end upon his withered body.

After taking his bath, he began thinking. Calling me he said, “Which direction shall we go in?”

I said, “Whatever direction you go, I will go in that direction to serve you.”

A large group of sannyasis, after bathing, went to Sarntala Mountain. Nimai went with them. I followed behind like a shadow. After fifty krosas, we came to Sarhtala. The group of sannyasis stayed there. Caitanya went to the base of a tree. I could not think where to go to beg some food. Understanding my thoughts, the Lord smiled slightly and said, “Why think like this? I will spend the night drinking the nectar of the name. Rising in the morning I will go where I please.”

Saying this, Gauranga, closing his eyes, stayed fixed, leaning against a tree. Playing instruments, the sannyasis began singing, which was very sweet to hear. At that time one merchant came there and gave food to everyone. He divided up unbroken fruit, roots, milk and sugar and gave to all.

Receiving the food, my hairs stood on end. Taking milk and sugar the Lord prepared an offering. All the sannyasis rose in the morning and departed for Tribanku province, going through the mountains. The king of Tribanku was very pious. He protected the citizens like his sons. The townspeople were all skillful at welcoming guests. Receiving guests, they made an uproar with arrangements. They competed in receiving guests. They served guests with great respect. The king’s name was Rudrapati. He was the father and mother of the destitute, the shelter for persons with no shelter. The citizens were very happy in this king’s province. Their hearts were overwhelmed with attraction to the king. There were horses and elephants at the king’s door. There was no lack of grain in his storerooms. In the town, there were food distribution centers in three places. Travelers would come and stay in these places. The fortunate king said that everyone could stay in those places as long as they liked.

We came to Tribanku in the evening. The Lord with joyful heart sat at the base of a tree. A villager brought some ground flour. The Lord spent the night beneath the tree. The next day the news spread and the towns people slowly gathered. Seeing the Lords astonishing bhava they folded their hands and stood at that place.

With eye closed the Lord chanted the name. The people all stood chanting prayers with pure minds. The Lord was sitting and his limbs did not move. Tears streamed from the tips of his eyes. His hairs stood on end and internally as well. Seeing his bhava the village people praised him.

Someone said, “O Sannyasi! Come to my house.” Someone said, “I like to see you.” Some brought and offered fruit and roots. Opening his eyes, my Lord did not see them.

Someone said, “This sannyasi is not human. Seeing him, why does bhakti manifest so much? Seeing him I desire to give up material life. My mind no longer focusses on material life.”

Someone said, “Today I have happily ended the night. Seeing the sannyasi my heart has become purified.”

One old lady came and said with devotion, “Where is the sannyasi? Show him to me.”

Seeing her insistence, my Gauranga quickly rose and went to her. The old lady offered respects to him. She brought and offered fruits, roots and mixed flour. The rumor spread around and hundreds of jnanls came to see him.

One brahmavadl came and spoke Advaitavada. Caitanya laughed and on hearing about his ignorance informed him that the Vedas and Vedanta are scriptural proof.

The Lord said, “Listen, respected jnanl. The perfection of all sadhana is prema in the mood of Radha. Radha’s fine prema is like a mountain. Without bhakti, no one can search it out. Giving up happiness of atma, the person mad for Krsna and beautiful Radha, rejects everything else. The person who understands Radha’s deep prema does not die again. The person absorbed constantly in Mayavada will not manifest bhakti-tattva. The essence of prema is mahabhava. Radha is for certain filled with that mahabhava. O respected elder! The person who understands this tattva no longer experiences birth and death.”

After that, the Lord’s greatness spread in that place. Various people began to gather slowly. The king of the place, very eager, sent people to bring the Lord.

The Lord said, “It is not necessary for me to go there. I will not go to a materialist.”

The king’s messenger said, “Listen, respected sannyasi! Why not go? You will receive great wealth, all the cloth and ornaments that you want. Easily you will attain such wealth.”

Understanding his intention through the messenger, he began to speak to make him understand. The Lord said, slightly smiling, “O messenger of the king! Please listen. Wealth is not necessary. I do not go to associate with insects of materialism. What use is wealth? The insect of materialism desires wealth. Wealth is the root of anarthas. Please believe this. Those intoxicated with pride in wealth forgets talks about tattva. They stay completely in the hell of material life. The wealthy man does not know that the body is temporary. He thinks success in life is wealth.”

Hearing this the messenger became angry. He went to the king’s door to take revenge. On hearing the news from the messenger’s mouth, Rudrapati had no anger towards the sannyasi. Hearing the complaining words through the messenger’s mouth, the king desired to see the sannyasi. King Rudrapati went to see the sannyasi. Quickly, with devotion he came outside. Giving up elephants and horses at a great distance, wearing miserable clothing, he approached the sannyasi. With a few ministers the king came to the Lord and spoke with devotion.

Folding his hands, Rudrapati said repeatedly, “Mercifully pardon my offense. Not knowing, I called you. Forgive this offense this time. O deliverer of the lowest! Give me teachings on knowledge to deliver this fallen soul. What is the cause of Jiva’s lamentation and suffering?” The king was very learned in various scriptures. All people said he knew Bhagavatam thoroughly. The king brought a few scholars with him to learn.

The Lord said, “O King! You are most fortunate. You know Bhagavatam. What more can I say? You are most knowledgeable of many scriptures. I do not know anything except Radha and Krsna.”

Chanting Krsna’s name, prema arose. Tears began to fall. Mad with krsna-prema, the Lord stood up suddenly and began dancing with arms spread.

Gauranga said, “Haribol!” Becoming unconscious, he danced and danced and fell on the ground. The king picked him up. With him, the king went mad. Saying, “Hari!” the king began dancing. His tears soaked his chest. His hairs stood on end. His limbs became covered with dust.

Seeing the king’s bhakti, Nimai embraced him and said, “Brother, come! The person whose eyes flood with tears because of the name is the pupil of my eye. O respected King! Seeing your bhakti know for certain that my prana is soothed.”

Saying this he bid farewell to the king and went to take bath. The king sent many fruits and roots. The Lord offered this daily. The king kept some people to serve the Lord. Pleased within, the king returned to his city.

Some brought fruits and roots. Others brought flour. Others brought cuna with betel leaves. Attracted to Visvambara, people bustled about. Many people came to see him. They brought and offered whatever they desired. Gauranga did not say what was good or bad.

It was beautiful to see the place surrounded by mountains. Attractive spring water flowed. There were huge neem trees in the four directions. One cannot describe the astonishing beauty.

At that place there was a mountain called Rama-giri. Everyone described its astonishing glory. Everyone said that Ramacandra had been on its summit. For three days he lived with Slta there.

After conquering Lanka, Rama, the abode of qualities, climbed and rested on its peak. Rama, with Slta and Laksmana rested there.

Hearing this, the Lord became very greedy. He climbed the mountain to see the place. At this place Rama and Slta had rested. At this place, my Gauranga offered respects. Several hundred people climbed with the Lord to see Rama-giri with devotion. The place was huge in length and breath. He went there for a fortnight. Then he entered Payosni town. Seeing Siva and Narayana, the Lord became happy. Sankara’s students stayed in Singeri matha. The Lord went there and met them. All the followers of Sankara gathered and sitting, began deliberating on tattva. All the students were defeated in deliberation on interpretation. From the matha the Lord went to see Matsya-tlrtha. Journeying to Matsya-tlrtha, the Lord came to Kacada. There he saw Durga. At that place there was a river named Bhadra, a tributary of the Krsna River. Bathing there, he went to Naga-panca-padl. All the people there were devotees of Rama. He told me to beg for food there. I went to three houses to beg. They gave me lots of flour.

The Lord stayed there three days. Going through the mountains everyone went to Citola. Leaving Citola, he went slowly to the bank of the Tungabhadra to bathe again. Bathing there, Gauranga sang about Krsna with pleasure. Going to many places we arrive at Kotigirl, the origin of the Kaveri River. Bathing at the birthplace of Kaveri, the Lord went to Candapura village. On the left side a mountain named Satya stood gloriously. I cannot describe its beauty. From far off one could see blue lines. My eyes opened wide on seeing its beauty. Seeing Satyagiri, the Lord offered it respects. Keeping Satyagiri to the left he travelled to the right.

Coming close to Candapura, the Lord sat at the base of a tree. One renounced person stayed at Candapura. Hearing about him from others Nimai went to him. That learned man knew many scriptures well. A golden earring dangled from one of his ears.

Eventually he began to speak from scripture with great pride in his appearance.

The sannyasis name was Isvara Bharatl. People said he had all good qualities. Understanding that the sannyasi was proud, the Lord turned his head and smiled slightly. Visvambara did not say anything good or bad. Best of sannyasis then stopped speaking.

He said to the Lord, “You do not speak. I do not think you are very learned. Everyone says you are very learned. I see that you have no knowledge. You purified the place saying the name of the Lord. I have not seen even a portion of your qualities. I have heard you know scriptures, but you do not speak. You wander about and beg for food here and there. You have no knowledge for debating. Why do you mislead foolish people? Skilfully deceiving people, you show off giving all people some small truth. Why do you come to this place, telling the foolish people to chant the Lord’s name? Being clever, I understand. If you have power, then deliberate. Then I will understand your pure intelligence.” Saying this, Bharatl left in haste.

Again, Bharatl came with his associates and sat. Four people sat around the Lord. Seeing the fun, the Lord began to laugh. Bharatl said, “Running about, you laugh. Please consider what I say. Please tell me who is the worshippable Lord.”

The Lord said, “There is no one else in this world except Krsna.” Bharatl said, “Hear the proof from scripture. The Vedas say that there is one Brahman which is the Supreme Lord. Wherever you look, you will see Brahman. Defeat this proposal. Just try.”

The Lord said, “I do not know how to deliberate. I accept that you know all tattvas. You are most learned at deliberation. Nimai has been defeated by you. If you want a victory letter, I will write it. By your deliberation today I accept defeat.”

Hearing this, the yogi spoke unclearly with dissatisfaction.

The Lord said, “Perform bhakti and reject logic. Taking to bhakti, deliberate on Krsna. You destroy the conclusions of the Vedas and Vedanta. Studying many scriptures, tell what is the result. Except Krsna there is no place for anything to stand.”

Saying this, my Lord closed his eyes. With hairs standing on end, his bhakti increased. Tears began to flow on to his chest. The knot of his kaupina came undone. His heart trembled and his body perspired. He shouted “Chant Krsna’s name” and began swaying. “O Krsna! O merciful Lord! Where are you? Distribute bhakti and purify my heart!”

Saying this, the Lord began to cry. The agitation in his mind doubled. Not hearing what was good or bad, Visvambara cried intensely, constantly. Seeing a tamala tree in front, he ran to the tree and embraced it, saying “Krsna!” Seeing the Lord’s bhava with his own eyes, the yogi embraced the Lord’s feet.

The yogi said, “I beg not to deliberate. My longing for Krsna grows. O young sannyasi! Seeing your bhava I do not want to deliberate. Give to my mind the astonishing jewel of bhakti. I beg this at your feet.”

The Lord could not hear the yogi’s words. The Lord moistened the earth with his tears. Overwhelmed by mahabhava, his limbs became stunned. His golden body fell in the dust. Saying “Krsna” he rolled on the ground. His dusty body became covered with thorns. The yogi, sitting in front of him, began weeping. At that moment mercy was given to him.

Seeing Bharatl’s bhakti, the Lord put his hand on his back. Putting his hand on his back, he spoke a few words. By the touch of the Lord, the yogi developed bhakti. His mind became absorbed in bhakti-rasa. How can one describe the mercy of the Lord? The yogi became mad with prema and rolled in the dust.

The yogi said, “You alone will be my Krsna. O Lord! When will you come? Let me see you again.”

The Lord said, “Do not tell anyone these words. Krsna is the master of Vrndavana. This is the conclusion. Without bhakti, the tattva of Krsna will not arise. Know for certain that Krsna is bound by the thread of bhakti.”

The yogi said, “I know your pretence. Why, being clever, do you put dust in my eyes? I will tie myself to you with the thread of bhakti today. I will take two wooden shoes today.”

Bharatl, saying this, forcibly pulled on his wooden shoes.

The Lord said, “You should have faith in Krsna. From today your name is Krsna-dasa.”

Saying this, the Lord left Candapura. The best of yogis walked a long way with him. Laughing, the Lord bid him farewell. Offering respects, the yogi left.

Travelling two days and nights, crossing the mountain, we did not find any village or town. We could not travel on the very difficult path. We saw only rows of kadamba trees. Seeing the trees, the Lord said to me, “My Krsna plays under this tree.” Saying this, my Lord became overwhelmed and cried. He walked while swaying, confused by krsna-prema. Walking and walking we saw a small lake. I became afraid on seeing a tiger there. The Lord made a signal to the tiger. I did not hear the Lord say anything good or bad. The tiger began drinking water there. It carefully crawled to the Lord’s side. The Lord went to the right and tiger went to the left. His limbs went out of control because of absorption, mad with chanting the name. The tiger did not look back at us. With the tiger behind, we walked quickly. Seeing my state, smiling slightly, the Lord said, “Why are you afraid? Chanting the name, there is no fear of death. Chanting, “Krsna! Krsna!” have no doubt.”

Hearing this, I attained power. The strength of my body doubled. Walking and walking, we arrived at a small hamlet, desiring shelter. It was a very small village with suffering inhabitants.

Nimai the sannyasi stayed there. It was beautiful to see the village surrounded by mountains. I went within the village to collect alms. There was one very poor brahmana. He maintained his life by begging. I went to his house to beg.

The brahmana said, “Brother! Wait a moment. Sit a moment and do not go. If a guest leaves, I will go to hell. Going begging, I will give you what I receive. Wait for some time here, O brother!”

Saying this, the brahmana went begging. He brought two coconuts and gave them to me. I brought the alms to the Lord and gave them at the base of the tree. The Lord offered the fruits in joy.

Hearing the brahmana’s story, Gauranga went in the evening to see the brahmana. The brahmana and his wife lived there. He went begging for serving Gopala. Seeing the Lord at his house, he folded his hands and came and stood in front of the Lord.

The brahmana said, “With what will I worship the guest? How can I ask him to go away? I have something for Gopala’s service. Begging, we two do service. I do not have seat. What will I give for him to sit upon?”

His wife said, “O brahmana! Offer your head to his feet. This guest’s body shines like lightning. Bring tulasi and offer to the guest’s feet.”

Quickly the brahmana brought tulasi and rushed to offer them to the Lord’s feet. Holding the brahmana’s hand, Caitanya said, “Offer tulasi to Gopala’s feet.”

Hearing this, the brahmana began crying. At that moment, the Lord embraced him. The Lord said, “O brahmana! You are most fortunate. The Lord is present in your house. How can I speak of your fortune? Your Gopala plays in your house. Your wife is directly Laksmi. Consider this in your mind.”

The brahmana said, “I consider myself fortunate by your mercy. You are not an ordinary human, merciful one! Why does lightning play upon your body? Why do I infer the fragrance of lotuses on your body? Put your feet on my head.”

Saying this, the brahmana put the Lord’s feet on his head. Biting his tongue with his teeth at these words, the Lord stepped back. Confused, the brahmana with his wife rushed forward and lowered his head to the Lord’s feet.Stretching his arms, the Lord raised the brahmana. Then he began singing with devotion. The brahmana’s house became like Vrndavana. The village people came to hear the Lord’s name.

*Hare Krsna Hare Krsna Krsna Krsna Hare Hare*

*Hare Rama Hare Rama Rama Rama Hare Hare*

Merciful Caitanya began singing this. All the listeners became insane along with him.

Hearing the name from the Lord’s mouth, the villagers became choked up at the Lord’s feet. Singing and singing the song, the night passed. In the morning my Lord bid farewell. Bidding farewell, the Lord went out of the village. The people remained staring like dolls.

Signalling me, saying “Govinda,” I put the wooden shoes on my shoulders. Near Kandara province Nila-giri shone. In the afternoon we went there slowly. He said, “What beauty could be experienced at Nila-giri! By intense meditation, the Lord becomes present.” Hundreds of caves beautified its base. Its astonishing state was beautiful to the summit. Huge trees grew to its summit. They swayed in the wind like camara fans. Waterfalls gurgled. Seeing that, the mind became joyful. Wandering around the mountain, I could see fresher and fresher beauty.

Hundreds of creepers surrounded the trees, showing bondage in marriage with respect. Peacocks lived there and in groups cried out. Various types of birds sang with sweet voices. Many types of flowers blossomed brightly, like garlands hanging on the neck ofnature. In the night many creepers shone with flashes of light. In the trees, fireflies illuminated the branches. A small river flowed with gurgling sound. On its bank the Lord stayed and performed evening worship. In the night he sat under one tree. The Lord said to me, “We will spend the night here.”

Saying only that, Gauranga closed his eyes. He spent the night chanting the Lord’s name. By the Lord’s mercy, I did not experience hunger or thirst. I remained around if he wanted anything. When he said to go begging I would go to a householder’s door. In the morning we rose and went to Gurjari town. Many people lived there happily. At that place there were many large, beautiful houses. Gauranga went and stayed at the edge of the town.

There was a pond called Agastya-kunda at this place. Bathing in the pond, we experienced bliss. Gauranga bathed in Agastya-kunda and stayed on its bank, singing the glories of the Lord. Eventually several people appeared. One brahmana brought and gave milk and sugar. Someone said, “O guest! Come to my house.” Someone else said, “You chant Krsna’s name. The name of the Lord is very sweet in your mouth. Hearing the name all suffering and lamentation are destroyed. The name of Krsna in your mouth is like nectar. Speak about Krsna and sooth my prana." Word spread and many jnanis came to see the Lord.

Closing his eyes, Gauranga began to sway. Tears burst from his eyes. My Gauranga did not see the people. Saying, “O Krsna!” he dampened the earth with his crying. He began weeping with emptiness. His hair loosened and fell down his back. Hairs standing on end, he wept in distress. With unkempt clothing, the Lord spoke many confused words.

Sometimes the Lord in madness became choked in voice. Sometimes he fell on the ground. Saying “My dear friend Mukunda, Murari!” Caitanya ran about. Sometimes he said, “Come, Narahari, my life! Hearing Krsna’s name, I embrace you.”

In this way, Gauranga spoke various words and ran about. Many great people, hearing of his astonishing powers, came near the Lord.

There was one great scholar named Arjuna. He spoke to the Lord about scriptural proof. He said, “I do not accept jiva-tattva. I consider atma-tattva (i.e. Paramatma) and jiva-tattva to be one.”

The Lord said, “You are a great scholar. Hearing scriptural proof, draw the conclusion. If you know the sruti conclusion of Dvaparna (two birds) why do you consider the two to be one?”

Taking the subtle topics of Vedanta, Gauranga made Arjuna understand everything by refuting false concepts. “There is jiva and Paramatma. The Lord is a huge tree and the jiva is its leaf. O great scholar! When you read, discard all unnecessary topics. Maya is the shadow of the Lord. The Lord is not contaminated by maya. By his desire, the jivas become influenced by maya. By the power of the name, if a person can drive out maya, he is a great sage in this world. A person has a screen of maya between himself and the Lord. Taking away the screen, see the Lord.”

Saying this and saying, “O Krsna!” he shouted. Suddenly the place became silent. I heard the name from the mouth of the Lord. The hairs of my body stood on end.

The Lord began calling out, “Rama! Rama!” That place became like Vaikuntha. Pleasant wind began to blow. Groups of villagers came to see. Hundreds of people stood all around. Speechless, they were listening to the name of the Lord. It seemed that the devatas came to hear the name. Above our heads, they were listening. The scent of lotuses spread, causing bewilderment. Chanting the name, the Lord became unconscious.

Everyone looked at the face of the Lord. Tears flowed constantly. Many Marathi people came in groups. They gathered and began listening to the name. I glanced behind and hundreds of family women were standing there. Everyone was listening to the Lord’s name with devotion. Women wiped away the tears with the edge of their cloth. Unlimited sannyasis of Visnu and Siva gathered to the name, closing their eyes.

The Lord intoxicated the state with his teachings. I have never seen such power. Sometimes he spoke by mistake in Tamil and sometimes spoke Samskrt, intoxicating the listeners. In this way chanting the name, becoming unconscious, the Lord began to dance. His hair came undone along with his kaupina. He appeared most miserable with dust on his limbs. Dancing, he became unconscious and fell on the ground. He remained there inert. Seeing this, people approached him.

Someone brought water and splashed it on his face. Someone raised him up carefully. Two dandas later the Lord sat up. Everyone in astonishment chanted the Lord’s name.

In the afternoon, one brahmana brought food. My Lord offered the food under a tree. Leaving Gurjari town, my Gauranga wanted to go to Purna town. For seven days the Lord did not hold discussions. He climbed up a mountain at Bijapura. The only provision on the road was the name of the Lord. Climbing the mountain, the Lord took rest. Climbing to the summit of the mountain and looking around, the Lord felt bliss. Caitanya climbed down the mountain first, with me following behind.

He went to see completely Sahya-kulacala. Seeing Kulacala, he was thrilled with bliss. I saw Mahendra and Malaya mountains with my eyes. Sahyagiri’s beauty cannot be described. From a distance one could see a blue line. My Lord ran to see that place. The mountain stands with a grave mood. Seeing the mountain, the heart starts dancing. The Lord said, “This mountain is the abode of bliss. He offered respects to it as the abode of bliss. The Lord was so eager to see Sahyacala that his hairs stood on end. Leaving Sahyagiri and going north, the Lord chanted the name with an unprecedentedly blissful face. Without desire, with most miserable clothing, the Lord floated through various provinces with bhakti-rasa. Wearing a kaupina, he had dust all over his body. Seeing him, one would think he was a madman.

Eventually Gauranga came to Purna town. Many scholars came to see. At that place many people studied scriptures. Slowly many people saw him. There was a lake called Accha-sara. Nimai went and stayed near it. On that lake a huge bakula tree grew. My Lord sat at its base and stayed there. Hundreds of scholars resided at that place. Day and night the scholars explained various scriptures. Hundreds of students came there and studied various scriptures under a guru. At that place, many people were skillful in knowledge. Hundreds of schools shone resplendently there.

Everyone considered the person who studied Bhagavatam to be a scholar. No one considered a person who did not know Gita and Bhagavatam to be learned. One scholar explained Bhagavatam. On hearing him, the Lord wept torrents of tears.

One brahmavadi scholar came. The Lord defeated all his logical arguments. Many devotees gathered and, seeing the Lord’s bhakti, woke up. Closing his eyes, the Lord said, “Krsna! Krsna!” Tears flowed from his eyes and fell on his chest. The Lord said, “My life is Mukunda, Murari. Come and appear in my heart. Radha and Krsna, filled with all saktis, are the support of the universe. Without Krsna, this universe is destroyed. Millions of universes come from his hair holes. How can I see Krsna, my life? Eating dirt, he opened his mouth on his mother’s lap. Suddenly, his mother saw in his mouth the universe. Wanting that Krsna, my heart is disturbed. Without Krsna my life is painful.”

One scholar came near and spoke. “Your Krsna is in this lake.”

Hearing these words, the Lord suddenly got up. His hairs stood on end. I never saw such force in his tears. In separation from Krsna, distraught Nimai cried. Saying, “Krsna! Krsna!” he began to weep intensely. He said, “Without Krsna my life is useless.”

He dampened the earth with his tears. Crying and crying he said with his mouth, “Haribol!”

He said to me one time, “There is one disappointment. Without Krsna, I cannot bear the pain in my life.”

Again, that austere sannyasi said, “O sannyasi! Your Krsna is hiding in the water.”

This time, Mahaprabhu heard his words. Overwhelmed with prema he suddenly jumped in the water. Many people also entered the lake. Various people, lamenting, pulled the Lord to the shore. All the scholars said that the person who said that Krsna was in the water was speaking foolish words.

The Lord said, “Why do you uselessly scold the great man? Krsna is always present in the water, on land and in the sky. Krsna is everywhere, Krsna is the universe. The devotee can see this. Bhakti is the highest tattva in this world. The person endowed with bhakti kicks liberation on the head. The person who cannot understand the wheel of maya is most unfortunate in this world. Welfare is false. Land is false. House is false. Wanting food, the fool is disturbed internally. Who is self and who is another? Who is father and who is mother? Whose throat does the hand hold? Say O brother! Men and women are not different. The difference is in the skin. The Vedas say this repeatedly. The jiva is trapped in the darkness of illusion. He never once says the name of the Lord. O brother! Never be falsely proud of wealth. Pass your time always chanting Hare Krsna. Sin covers the universe with darkness. There is nothing true in this world except the Lord.

When the two birds leave the tree of the body, the material body drops away. O brother! Why look at a dream after waking up? No one will save you. Everyone will die eventually. O brother! Come, chant the name together. Hearing the name, death trembles in fear. Kings and emperors have great power. One day, tall kingdoms must be destroyed.

“O King! With pride the king makes a kingdom. Why does his heart burn with the three miseries? A valuable pearl will not go with you. Understand by experience that wealth is temporary and worthless. O brother! Chant Hare Krsna with devotion. Everyone will remain always in happiness. All are in illusion, bewildered by maya. Why do you become proud, desiring everything? The seven mountain ranges will be destroyed with time. In the material world, nothing remains. Bhava with bhakti is true and eternal.

“Everyone! Hold the Lord’s feet tightly. The Lord, holder of the club, will destroy all suffering. The universe will become equal to Vaikuntha. The person intoxicated with material wealth cannot cut the bondage of material life. The person with material desires becomes bound up. Everyone calls him a dishonest fool. Cut the bondage of maya with the weapon of the Lord’s name. Easily you will go to the eternal abode. You will not experience again and again birth, death and old age. Saying, ‘Krsna! Krsna!’ you will destroy the darkness. Suffering people will destroy their prarabdha-karma. Then lamentation, pain and suffering will go far away.”

People flocked to see the Lord. Many scholars came to deliberate. Someone said, “This sannyasi is not human.” Someone said, “This person is a great devotee.” The Lord never spoke to answer these statements. Streams of prema flowed from his eyes at the name of the Lord. Closing his eyes, the Lord chanted the name. He turned over and over on the ground.

The Lord said, “I will go to some tirtha. Point out the path. Where is Bholesvara?”

“Nearby Patasa village is Gora-ghata. At that place is a great holy place called Bholesvara. At Bholesvara Siva resides.” An excellent brahmana named Tunnu Maharaja gave these instructions. He was very learned. The Lord knew about him. Tunnu said, “Bholesvara is at that place.”

Hearing this, the Lord went where Siva was present. Yearly there was a festival at Bholesvara. Hearing that, the Lord’s heart danced. Looking at me, the Lord slightly smiled and said, “Go to Bholesvara. I will follow behind.”

Travelling on many paths over the mountains, we came and saw the arrangement of the tirtha. There was a huge temple on a mountain. In the temple I saw Lord Bholesvara. At that place was siddha-kupa. Taking that water, the Lord bathed. Seeing Bholesvara, the Lord’s prema arose. Folding his hands, he offered many prayers. Becoming unconscious, he fell on the earth. He turned over and over with choked voice.

Seeing Bholesvara, Gauranga ran to see Devalesvara nearby. Seeing Devalesvara, the Lord, jewel of good qualities, offered respects, rolling on the ground, choked with prema, he recited many praises. Seeing the Lord, people came with devotion. Devalesvara was situated on top of a mountain. In the far distance shone Jijuri town. In Jijuri there was a deity named Khandava.

I went with the Lord to see Khandava. They say that the women who cannot get married will get married to him by the grace of Khandava. The father and mother bring their daughter in front of Khandava and offer her to the deity with great devotion. Daughters of poor fathers stay there and serve Khandava with respect. Hundreds of girls taking Khandava as their husband were on the path begging. Being cheated, at Khandava many of these women took to prostitution. As wives of Khandava, they committed sin. They would later end up with an unfortunate destination. Many people came to visit the tirtha. With cleverness, they fell to hell.

The poor girls who came to the place said that they married Khandava and were his wives. Hearing this, the Lord went to see the girls in secret. The people called them “Murari.” Seeing them, the Lord was merciful. Hearing of their suffering, he felt mercy in his peaceful mind. How a father could be peaceful, I cannot say. How could he make his daughter into a Murari?

Hearing these words, the Lord went to deliver the women at the yard. I said, “You do not need to go there.” Caitanya did not hear my words. The Lord went to the Murari’s village. He purified everyone with the name. Not able to tolerate the women’s suffering, he wanted to deliver all the women. Hearing with their own ears about the Lord’s bhava, gradually many women came to that place.

The Lord said to the women, “Chant the name of the Lord. By the power of the name, you will attain the eternal abode. The Lord is most merciful, the shelter for persons with no shelter. Think of him as your husband. Many gopis performed Katyayani vrata with pure minds to attain Krsna as their husband. When Krsna is the husband, there is no fear of the material world. Know for certain that Krsna is everyone’s husband. Always chant Krsna’s name with devotion. Always say, “Hare Krsna Hare.”

Saying this my Lord began to chant the name. Immediately his hairs stood on end. Seeing his bhava all the women came to worship his feet. By the strength of the Lord, I begged at householders’ doors. Since I was most untouchable, they did not touch me.

Chanting the name with devotion suffering will be destroyed. By the power of the name, all sins were turned to ashes. The person absorbed in sin, not knowing this, destroys all sin by the power of the name.

Hearing this teaching, many women of Khandava stood in crowds near the Lord. Indira, a dancing girl came with folded hands and said, “Be merciful to me, O great sannyasi! Doing sinful acts, I have become old. Deliver me by the dust of your feet.”

Saying this Indira rolled in the dust. By the name the Lord delivered Indira. Indira accepted the name of the Lord. Leaving the house, she renounced everything. From that day, many women of Khandava became mad with the name and tears flowed from their eyes. I have never seen such a merciful Lord. I cannot write how many sinners he delivered.

Seeing the bhakti of those women, the Lord desired to go at dawn to Coranandl forest. The villagers said, “What is the purpose in going there? It is a mine of sin. In Coranandl forest live many thieves. Why do you want to go there?”

The Lord said, “I will go to Coranandl forest. Seeing that forest my goal will be achieved.”

The villagers said, “O sannyasi! Do not go there. It is not good for devotees to go there. Many thieves and dacoits live in that place. Your life is in doubt by going there.”

The Lord said, “What will the thieves get from me? I will go here and there.”

Rama Svaml said, “Lord! There is no tirtha at Coranandl. Why go there? If the thieves there harm you, the people will give up their lives in separation from you.”

The Lord said, “O Rama Svaml! do not fear. I will make the thieves mad with the name of the Lord.”

Saying that the Lord went to Coranandl. Going there he sat under a tree. Many wicked men gathered at that place. They maintained their lives by stealing. One person approached, speaking indistinctly.

What he said I could not understand. The Lord could understand what he said. He made him understand, speaking indistinctly. That person looked around for some time. Glancing here and there he entered the forest. One very strong person named Narojl approached with weapons. A few men gathered and watched. Seeing the sannyasi, they all offered respects.

Narojl said, “Go to my place. Stay there tonight.”

Hearing Narojl’s words, he said to him, “Tonight I will stay under the tree.”

Hearing the Lord’s words, Narojl told several men to bring food. Hearing his words, everyone left. Nimai sat in a yoga posture chanting the name. Some men brought wood and sugar. Others brought rice. Others brought milk and ghee. Others brought fruits and roots. They brought piles of food. Seeing all the food my greed increased. I wandered in many places with the Lord. I had never seen such food in any other place. Various foods were spread all around. Narojl’s followers stood around in groups.

My Lord was chanting the name. At that time he became overwhelmed with prema. What happened to the milk, sugar and rice? By the touch of his feet, all the fruits and roots were scattered.

Several men said, “What a sannyasi! Intentionally he has destroyed the pile of food.”

Narojl said, “Never have I seen such a person. Seeing the sannyasi, why does my prana weep? I cannot say how many sins I have committed. Why do I want to wear a kaupina today? Why does my prana weep today? Have I put my foot in the sannyasi’s trap? All the goods have been destroyed. Do not fear. Again, I will supply all the goods.”

Standing to one side Narojl glanced at Gauranga, the jewel of good qualities. His eyes flooded with tears. Everyone’s hairs stood on end. Hearing about this, gradually groups of thieves left the forest and came to see. In the afternoon, my Gauranga, jewel of good qualities, fainted in prema and fell on the ground. Choked with prema, he covered his body with dust. Tears fell upon his chest.

Crying Narojl said, “O sannyasi! Listen. Reveal the mantra you are chanting. Seeing you, your bhava appears in my mind. I will no longer commit sin, staying in this forest. I am sixty years old. Giving up material life I will not engage in sin. I am a wicked son of a brahmana. Do not hesitate to give the dust of your feet to me. Having had children, I no longer have a family. Why should

I do sinful acts any longer? Begging, I can fill my stomach. Why should I remain among the thieves? I have developed hatred for sinful acts. I will no longer remain as the head of the thieves.”

Saying this, Narojl glanced at the group. He threw away his weapons.

The Lord said, “O Narojl! Hear my words. I will speak to you repeatedly some words. Wearing a kaupina, give up shame. Begging, fill your stomach. Distribute your wealth to whoever wants it. No one is a father, mother, brother, or friend. If you can maintain your life with a fistful of food, why engage in sin to gather wealth? Drink from a waterfall using your hands as a cup. What is the result of collecting many utensils? Men with wealth like Kuvera will one day go to Yama’s abode. The person who goes on the path of poverty, on giving up the body, will attain a kingdom by that path. Why uselessly die, thinking in terms of possessions? O brother! Chant the name with prema-bhakti.”

Hearing these instructions, the brahmana Narojl wanted to go with us.

Narojl said, “I will show all the tirthas. I will go behind you from tirtha to tirtha. For many days, my blind eyes were bewildered by smoke. I have now thrown my weapons on the ground. I have killed many men with this hand. I have spoken harshly to many men with this mouth. I will no longer remain as the head of the thieves. O shelter of the shelterless! You have shown me the path. I stay in the jungle, hiding at all times. I did not see my body ageing with sin.”

Saying this, the chief of the bandits renounced everything, put on a kaupina and went with the Lord. The thieves went off somewhere. Narojl went with us.

Journeying from Coranandl forest, the Lord went to see Khandala. The Mula River flowed swiftly there. Going there, the Lord spoke to me.

“This river is a pious tirtha. If you bathe here all sins are destroyed.”

At the Lord’s order, I bathed and went to the town to beg. Narojl followed behind me. I returned from begging after a long time. Later, a few inhabitants of Khandala came to see the Lord. Pure-minded, they lived on the river. One said, “O Lord! Come to my house. It can serve many guests in Khandala.”

They all competed for the Lord. They ended up quarrelling. They were ready to kill each other.

One said, “I saw him first.” Another said, “I have brought food.” In this way they quarrelled among themselves. Seeing their mood, the Lord laughed in his heart.

One wealthy man said, “Take food in my garden today and stay there. Do not be disgraced by clothing. I will give cloth for you to wear. If you need money for the road, I will give whatever you want.”

The Lord laughed and said, “O King! Please listen. I have no business in pleasure and wealth. I consider torn cloth to be best to wear. I know that there is no use in wealth. By associating with wealth, pride increases. By that pride, the weight of impurity increases. See this universe with your eyes. Think where you will go. Look! All wealth and pleasure will be destroyed. Only the lord of the universe will remain. My two associates bring food. What is the use of additional food? It is not that the body will not be maintained. Sometimes they bring no food. What happens if you bring lots of food? The lack for this body will become more for the poor man who is suffering.

“Chant with prema and live under a tree. Then you will cut all bondage. There is no happiness in maya’s bondage. O brother! Chant the name of the Lord with prema. Prema for the Lord produces rasa. The devotee knows that life is a mixture of poison and nectar. Svarga is in the thread of time. The person who worships Krsna attains Vaikuntha. The person who rejects Krsna goes to hell.”

Saying this, my Lord did not speak further. Closing his eyes, he began to chant the name. His hairs stood on end and his locks came undone. When he began dancing his clothing fell off. Overwhelmed with prema, his limbs got covered with dust. How can I describe that prema?

He spent the night chanting the name. Narojl stayed beside him, wiping away his perspiration. Rising in the early morning, the Lord went to Nasika to purify his heart at that tirtha. Laksmana cut off Surpanakha’s nose at this place. In the northern area, near Trimuka, there was a place with Rama’s cottage.

Mahaprabhu went there. Chanting prayers, finally he did kirtana. The footprint of Rama is at that place. Hearing this, the Lord ran to that forest. Amidst a thick forest, by a waterfall shone the footprints on stone. Touching the footprints, the Lord became overwhelmed with deep prema. With hairs standing on end and locks of hair undone he danced. His thin body seemed to swell. The Lord said, “Where is Rama, the lord of my life? Show yourself and soothe my heart, putting a hook around my throat. Where is my Rama?”

He began crying. The scent of lotuses emanated from the Lord’s body. The wind began to blow gently. I dread describing that prema. I have never seen such astonishing bhava. Shouting, “O Krsna!” he looked around like a madman. Looking at the sky he called to someone. Sometimes he stood up as if seeing someone. He fasted for a few days. Not eating any food, his body became thin.

Then he entered Panca-vatl and saw Ganesa established by Laksmana. One day, in a cave in Panca-vatl forest, I came from begging and secretly looked. That forest was peaceful, without noise and without people. From time to time a few people lived there. Crickets were chirping in the forest. With closed eyes, Gauranga was meditating. From his limbs radiated great effulgence. My young sannyasi was meditating. Seeing his bhava my eyes were dazzled. Slowly I came near to see.

Narojl came from somewhere bringing fruit. I remained standing on one side. Hearing footsteps, the Lord suddenly withdrew his bhava and looked around. From somewhere, Narojl had brought fruits and roots. He stood in front of Caitanya with folded hands. Offering the food, Gauranga ate a little. He sat there the whole night.

Gauranga left Panca-vatl in the morning and went to Damana town. Not staying a day there, leaving the place, the Lord went north. Then, wandering for a fortnight, he went on the path in joy. Eventually he went to the kingdom of Suratha. Seeing an eight-armed form of Durga, the Lord rolled on the ground. Seeing her form, the Lord stayed there three days. Seeing the eightarmed form established by King of Suratha, the Lord experienced unlimited bliss. At Durga’s temple there was one sannyasi. He desired to inquire from the Lord.

The sannyasi said, “Come here, respected sannyasi. I have never seen a devotee like you. Seeing you, bhakti arises in my mind. Please tell me how I will cross the ocean of samsara. How to worship the Supreme Lord? Saying this, my troubles will be destroyed.”

The Lord said, “I do not know the essential truth. Durga will destroy all darkness of the mind. The ragatmika devotee sees Krsna just as a handsome hero sees a heroine. In that manner call out repeatedly to Krsna. You will become free from the darkness of the mind.”

As he spoke, one brahmana brought a goat offering into the Durga temple. The Lord said, “You give her an offering to eat, but you do not know what your destination is later. Durga is pure according to scriptures. How can she eat something inedible? The king of Suratha has given a hundred thousand offerings. In hell, a hundred thousand swords will fall on him. You do not study the words of scripture. Killing animals, you enact dharma. She has made an arrangement for meat-eating Raksasas to eat it when animals are killed. All scriptures say that nonviolence is the highest dharma. Be merciful to the jivas and bliss will manifest. Maya ties you up tightly. Without a weapon how will you cut the bondage?

“With attraction for tamasic food you become attracted to Durga in order to cut up sheep and goats. Hoping for deliverance in killing animals, you come to offer her animals. You make the atma leave the body. How can the atma be complete within a dead body? If someone with devotion to Durga cuts off your head as human sacrifice to her, tell me, if you would grieve, O brother! The animals give up their bodies. I see it with my eyes. ‘Eight-armed Durga eats meat and liquor.’ The devotee, hearing such statements will laugh. Concentrate on sanatana-dharma. According to scriptures, give up low conduct. Durga, the greatest devotee, does not eat meat. Why do you mistakenly offer her meat? If committing violence to jivas were dharma, why are dacoits not called devotees? Some fish live on eating other fish. Why do you not call them followers of dharma? Killing humans and killing animals are great sins. Committing these sins, one increases the three sufferings.”

Coming to see eight armed Durga, he gave these teachings, according to scriptures. The person who came to worship Durga heard the Lord’s words with devotion. Hearing his words, he became detached. The brahmana gave up sacrificing the goat. The best of brahmanas worshipped with flowers and bilva leaves and returned home in bliss.

The Lord sat close to the deity of Durga and offered her prayers with folded hands. Gauranga praised Durga-devl. He then ran towards the Tapatl River, a great tirtha. He bathed at the three sandhyas in the Tapatl water. He went to see the deity of Vamana. Vamana deity was established near the river on edge of a piece of land. King Bali had established the deity. For that reason, Tapatl became a tirtha. Vamana had bathed in the water of Tapatl. For this reason, they call Tapatl a great tirtha.

The Lord offered respects to Vamana’s feet and went to see Yajna-kunda. Leaving Tapatl, the Lord went to the bank of the Narmada to see Yajna-kunda at Bhamroca town. At Bhamroca, Bali made a yajna-kunda. The Lord went there with joyful heart to see it. Seeing the wide trench with his eyes, Caitanya experienced unlimited bliss.

Bathing in the great tirtha Narmada, Gauranga went joyfully to Baroda. In the eastern part of Baroda was Dankorajl deity. The Lord desired to see that deity. The courtyard of the temple was wide and clean. Sacl’s son stood below that. Seeing the deity, the Lord offered respects and prayers. He then returned to Baroda.

The king of Baroda was most pious. He was engaged in serving Govinda. He unbolted the Govinda temple with his own hands. He was glorified like Ambarlsa. He was always busy for Govinda. He was attached to serving Govinda. With his hands he picked tulasi manjaris and offered them to Govinda’s lotus feet. Gauranga went to the Govinda temple in the evening. Seeing Govinda, he rolled on the earth.

The Lord was always like a madman, absorbed in Krsna, wearing a torn outer cloth of a madman. His limbs were covered with dust and his eyes were closed. Seeing Govinda, he showered tears. After three days, a disaster occurred there. Narojl died of fever. As he died, Gauranga sat facing him. He rubbed his lotus hands over Narojl’s body. He closed Narojl’s eyes and uttered Krsna’s name in his ear. Narojl was most fortunate. The Lord gave Krsna’s name to his ear. At the time of dying Narojl, folded his hands and glancing at the Lord, said, “Hari, Hari.”

Visvambara put Narojl on his lap and buried him at the base of a tamala tree. Begging, he made Narojl a samadhi. Walking around the samadhi, the Lord did kirtana.

Hearing from others this story, the king visited the sannyasi. Seeing the Lord, the king offered respects. The Lord said nothing good or bad. With respect, the king spoke in order to give a donation. The Lord said, “I accept alms at the householders’ doors. It is not necessary to beg for show. Thus, I do not beg at your door.”

Folding his hands, the king begged him to receive alms. He said to me that I must accept it. At an indication of the Lord, I, like an ordinary person, took a fistful of alms from the king. Giving alms, the king left. Gauranga finished his daily rituals and in the morning rose and went west. At some distance we came to Mahi river. The huge, swift flowing river was a beautiful sight. In the river huge stones were quickly moving. Gauranga crossed the river in joy and arrived at Amedabada. It was an astonishing city of grandeur with many gardens and attractive houses. It was glorious with huge palaces. The people of the place were absorbed in serving guests. The city folk considered guest to be like devatas. Serving guests was the obligation of their life.

People were enchanted with the beauty of the Lord. They came and stood all around with devotion. Someone said, “Listen, O young sannyasi! Come to my house. I will give alms and serve.”

The Lord said, “I will not go to a householders’ houses. Today I will spend the night on the edge of the Nandanl garden.” There was a beautiful garden called Nandanl. Visvambara stayed at its edge. Seeing this, the townspeople brought alms. In the night Gauranga made an offering. Many people came and surrounded the Lord. Seeing the sannyasi, they spoke with devotion.

One learned man came to see him and began reciting Bhagavatam verses. The Lord said, “Please sing about the qualities of Krsna. I desire to hear many verses. Daily recite Bhagavatam. Seeing you, the pains of samsara are destroyed. Daily sing the qualities of Krsna. You are a most fortunate brahmana.'"

The brahmana conversed with the Lord. He called all the people. “Everyone, do good service to this sannyasi! He is not an ordinary sannyasi. He must be a devata. Seeing him, renunciation arises. Know for certain that he is not an ordinary human. He cannot understand all your language. Write whatever you can, indicating what you want to say by letters.'

Travelling for a long time in this country, Caitanya understood all languages. Asking a few words to the Lord, I thought of keeping a record. I secretly made notes of the pastimes that I personally saw as the Lord, always mad with krsna-prema, wandered in various tirthas and provinces.

In Amedabada many people gathered to see the Lord. Seeing many people around, Gauranga gave them all the name and made them mad with the name.

The Lord said, “Everyone chant the name with devotion. All suffering will go far away. No sorrow will remain. Do not hate anyone, because of being filled with pride. Without pride, chant, “Hare Krsna Hare.” A person whose mind is not pure with bhakti-rasa is not learned, being proud of his learning. The person who considers a million obstacles to be like grass becomes mad with prema. I consider him a devotee. The srutis say that the essence of tattva is prema-bhakti.

“The devotee of the Lord, mad with prema, does not want liberation. The person who has prema-bhakti as his neck ornament is an eternal associate of Krsna. I will not argue but serve the person who tastes the sweetness of krsna-prema. The person who cuts the bondage of this body, being mad with krsna-prema, is a great devotee.”

Caitanya removed the dust of maya from the eye of knowledge, cutting the material knot. “For this reason, foolish people in this world are bewildered and think that they are this material body. The person who gives up identity with the material body is the head of the temple according to the Vedas. The highest devotee is absorbed in krsna-prema. He does not cheat, causing disturbances. The person who does not know the chief meaning of Vedanta considers the jiva one with Brahman.”

Saying this, the next day Gauranga joyfully went west, leaving the city. Going a little distance, he saw the Subhramatl River which sang sweetly with gurgling notes. A few men who were travelling to Dvaraka on pilgrimage were crossing the river. I saw among them two Bengalis, the great devotee Ramananda and Govinda carana. After a long time, I saw someone from Bengal. My mind danced in bliss. I asked Ramananda where his house was. He told me he lived in Kulina Nagara.

The Lord bathed in the Subhramatl River. At that time Ramananda spoke with me.

Ramananda said, “Where are you going?”

I said, “I am going with the Lord to Dvaraka.”

On hearing Caitanya’s name, Ramananda’s face immediately became joyful. Ramananda ran and offered respects to the Lord. Caitanya asked him a few questions. Ramananda dasa was the greatest devotee. The Lord pacified Ramananda dasa.

The Lord said, “Seeing you O Ramananda, emotions for Bengal have awakened in my mind. For how many days have you left your house? For how many days have you come to this pious place? Let us go together to Dvaraka.”

Hearing this, from the Lord’s mouth, Ramananda dasa was happy to remain in the Lord’s association. Taking a bath, the Lord slowly went. He arrived at a village called Ghoga in Ganda-grama. At that place lived a prostitute called Varamukhi. One cannot describe her wealth. Through prostitution she attained great wealth. She wore valuable cloth and ornaments. She lived in a big house. The wealth of a wealthy person eventually goes to the Lord. Hundreds of lusty people wearing baggy pants came to her house. She had many male and female servants. Everyone knew about her ostentatious display.

There was a huge garden called Piyara kanana. The Lord went to the edge of that garden. At that place there was a huge nma tree. Thinking something, the Lord went and stayed there. On his order, I went to some householders’ doors. I gathered some fruit and roots by begging. After begging I returned at two praharas of the day. With a happy heart, the Lord prepared an offering. The three of us, myself, Ramananda and Govinda Carana, then took prasada.

Laughing, I called Govinda my friend. The Lord said, “O why do you make this deception about Ramananda? If Govinda is your friend, Ramananda is my friend.”

Laughing and laughing, saying, “Ramananda is my friend,” the Lord began chanting while clapping his hands. Hearing this from the Lord’s mouth, Ramananda stood at one side clapping his hands. Many people gathered to hear the name. Hundreds of streams of tears flowed from the Lord’s eyes. They flowed like water from a syringe. Seeing this, the people of Ghoga were astonished. Several people became mad with prema on seeing the sankirtana of the Lord.

The towns people’s eyes flowed with tears. Many people came and stood in rows. One cannot describe the emotions of their bhakti. With unblinking eyes, they gazed at the Lord’s face.

Sometimes the Lord laughed and sometimes cried. Sometimes he raised his hands and danced and sang. Sometimes perspiration flowed as he trembled violently. Sometimes he remained silent, overwhelmed with prema. Sometimes he staggered about with hairs standing on end. Sometimes he shouted loudly, “O Krsna, my life!”

The people of Ghoga spread the news about the young sannyasi mad with prema for the Lord. Saying “Hari, Hari,” with tears of bliss, he stood around with hairs standing on end. With halfclosed eyes, with hair undone, the Lord’s limbs became smeared with dust and earth. He shouted, “Where is my life Krsna?” Sometimes he raised his hands and looked up. “O Govinda! Where is Krsna? bring him. Where is my life, Krsna? show him to me.”

Saying this one time, the Lord ran away and, spreading his arms, embraced the nima tree. Nimai was mad with prema for the Lord. I have never seen such madness. For many days, I travelled with him in various places. I had never seen on any day such possession. Ramananda and Govinda Carana on the two sides clapped their hands, shouting “Hari!” again and again. There was a deep hole on the side of the road. He fell into it being overwhelmed.

One wicked fellow came in a busy mood. He said to the Lord, “Why do you cheat. You bewilder people and take their money. Then you wander about shouting “Hari”. I have come to test the sannyasi. I have seen hundreds of false sannyasis.”

When the pasanda said this, the villagers wanted to beat him.

The Lord said, “O brothers! Who do you all beat? Make him drink the name of the Lord. His throat is dry from thirst. Give his mouth one dose of nectar. His heart has dried up without bhakti. By the name, destroy his fear of death. The pasanda’s mind is like a desert. Offer him the power of removing it. O devotee, come to me. I will give you the name of the Lord. I will remove the weight of your sins. By the power of this mantra, all suffering will disappear. By reciting the mantra, the name of the Lord, immediately the result will appear. The sinful person who chants this maha-mantra will never go to hell. When such an easily available mantra exists in the world, why does the sinful person uselessly tread the wicked path?”

Saying this, Mahaprabhu went near him and, leaning forward, gave to his ear the nectar of the holy name. To deliver the jivas, merciful Caitanya wandered about everywhere without change in his attitude.

Seeing all this from a window, Varamukhi became to consider. Seeing with her eyes the amazing mercy of the Lord, sitting alone she criticized herself. Varamukhi said, “Ah! Wanting money, I sold my body a hundred times a day. I associated with other men, the form of sin. Ah! Constantly I engaged in lustful play. Seeing this sannyasi equal to the Lord, I give up everything in his presence. A sannyasi does not touch money. Why do I attain such happiness by seeing him? Why do I live in the house of hellish enjoyment? Will the Lord not give mercy to me? What mantra did the sannyasi recite in the ear of the wicked Balaji, delivering him? Going near him, I will destroy my sin. Going near, I will embrace his feet. Varamukhi said this from the window. Hearing this everyone was happy. The people spread this story in all directions and laughing, caused an uproar. A little later the prostitute came down and her maidservant named Mira followed behind. She said politely to Mira, “From today, I give all my wealth to you. There is much money but I consider it insignificant. From today I have become a beggar on the road. Mira untied Varamukhi’s hair, like a cloud next to stationary lightning. Her hair extended to her hips.

Saci’s son remained with closed eyes. Everyone saw the effulgence of his extraordinary form. They stood gazing at his face. Varamukhi with folded hands spoke again and again.

“O sannyasi! Cut my bondage. I am a great sinner, an insect from hell. If you do not give mercy, I will go away quietly. How will I deliver this body known as a prostitute? How will I avoid fear of Yama after death? What is the need of this sinful body?”

Saying this, she cut off her long hair and donned ordinary cloth without shame. Folding her hands she stood in front of the Lord.

The Lord said, “O Varamukhi! Speaking a few words to you, follow all the instructions. “Make a tulasi garden here and staying within it, worship Krsna.”

Varamukhi said, “You are Krsna. You are the Lord.” Saying only this, she fell at the Lord’s feet. When she fell at his feet the Lord stepped back three or four feet. Many people standing near seeing the prostitute blessed her. Mirabai cried profusely. Varamukhi, smiling said to her, “O Mira! Listen carefully to my words. I have given you all my wealth. If guests come serve them well. Concentrate on the Lord’s name, living in a solitary place. Swearing to me, do not commit sinful acts. Worship Radha and Krsna with attachment and prema. Express prema well but avoid duplicity. O Mira! Have affection for Krsna, offer to Krsna your body, mind and life. Then you will attain the eternal wealth, Krsna. Hear my words with attention. Do not associate with anyone except Krsna. Krsna will definitely give you mercy. Even at the cost of your life, do not give up Krsna, your wealth. By the Lord’s mercy I have cut my bondage. From today my residence is the tulasi garden.”

Saying this, Varamukhi took up japa beads. She made the tulasi garden, forgetting all pain.

The Lord, giving bhakti to the renunciate Varamukhi, went running to see Somanatha. He went towards Japharabada. After three difficult days he arrived there. The people of Japharabada were suffering greatly but respected guests nicely. Many of the villagers gave alms. Making chapatis the Lord made an offering. Going into a private garden we spent the night with blissful minds. In the morning we went to Somanatha. We arrived six days later. The temple did not have its previous glory. Seeing its dilapidated state, the eyes wept. There were signs of a broken mound. Seeing that took away one’s life. The grandeur of the temple was lost. Seeing it, my Lord was overwhelmed with crying. Crying he began to speak.

“What have the wicked yavanas done, coming here? In fear of the yavanas, they hid the deity somewhere. If they show the deity once, my heart will be pacified.” One cannot describe the suffering there. Gauranga cried for Somanatha.

The Lord said, “Who has stolen its glory? Wanting money, the wicked have brought it to this state. O Lord Somanatha! Wanting to see you, my prana cannot tolerate, being most disturbed. I cannot tolerate separation from you. My heart bursts on account of you. What sin did the devotees commit? By what sin did the Lord not see you? In separation from you hundreds of priests are submerged in an ocean of sorrow. Did you out of fear of the yavanas go away to Kailasa? Forgetting them, the dear devotees remain. Seeing all this, my heart breaks. Tears flow on my chest. Only broken remnants remain. How can the eye tolerate this sin? O lord Somanatha! Come within my heart. In my heart I will see your form. Where did they hide you? I cannot see you. I cannot describe to anyone how my life air is reacting. O holder of the Ganga, people no longer come from other places to see you. Coming to see, pilgrims gave respect. But now that respect has disappeared. Filled with hatred, the yavanas transgressed and stole the jewels and pearls. Where is Siva staying? You have not mercifully shown yourself to the devotees.”

In this way my Lord lamented. At this time a storm arose in the sky. Dust flew everywhere and made darkness. The priests locked the doors of the hut. We all stayed outside the door. The Lord came and sat amidst us saying “Hari.” At that time an avadhuta sannyasi came and repeatedly glanced at Gauranga. His body was smeared with ashes and he wore no clothing. His hair was bound up in an astonishing form. His eyes were red. He uttered “Hara Hara”. His heart was pure. His eyes were passionate to see the beauty of Siva. He came and gave blessings with raised hand.

Lord, looking at him, stood up. He disappeared, saying something. The dust rose in the four directions making everything dark. I could not see where the avadhuta went. My Lord laughed slightly and circumambulated Somanatha three times. With Ramananda and Govinda Carana, I did sankirtana with the Lord. Wanting the affection of Somanatha, the Lord did kirtana, melting with prema. Several priests came and met us. They begged from us a little. Laughing, the Lord, a sannyasi, said, “I cannot give money, food or clothing.” Hearing these words. Govinda Carana gave two coins to hands of the priests. The priests took him to the edge of a pure pond. My Lord took some water and placed it on his head.

Leaving Somanatha, we went to Junagada. It was a big village but with no tirtha. In the four directions were splendid palaces. Gauranga stayed in Junagada for two days. There was a place where Ranachora was served. We went there to see the deity in the evening. An excellent brahmana named Mirajl served the deity. We arrived at his house. He gave great respect. The Lord spent the night at his house. The brahmana brought milk, sugar and flour. The Lord spent the night in bliss.

Nearby was attractive Grnara mountain. Visvambhara went there. Mirajl came with devotion and told the Lord to stay repeatedly. Politely the Lord said to the brahmana, “We will all go to Grnara Hill. I will see Guru-datta’s feet at that place. I want to give up my body. That is my request to you.”

Hearing the Lord’s words, the respected brahmana did not say anything, either agreeing or disagreeing. Caitanya departed and we, like shadows, followed behind. A group of sannyasis lived there. They all had worried faces. Their leader, called Bhargadeva, lived there in pain. Bhargadeva was sitting restlessly under a tree.

The Lord quickly arrived there.

Gauranga, seeing Bhargadeva in pain, ordered us to serve him. The three of us went to serve the sick man. The Lord said, “Have him drink nma juice.” We made nma juice and fed him. He recovered from the illness and offered unlimited respects at the Lord’s feet. He got up and praised the Lord. Rubbing his hands with devotion, Bharga said, “Be merciful to me. Lord, you are merciful. My heart is eager for you. You freed this wretch from disease. Mercifully cut my bondage of maya. O young sannyasi! To whom are you deceitful? I desire to attain your feet. If you do not give mercy to the insignificant people, how will they call you merciful? I am old and my eyes are mistaken. I see in your heart a golden doll. Everyone says your complexion is golden. But my eyes see blackness. My eyes are making some mistake. Being merciful, deliver this sinner.”

The Lord was merciful to Bhargadeva and spread his sakti. Suddenly, in his heart bhakti appeared. Saying something to Bhargadeva, the Lord glanced at him. At that moment, his eyes filled with tears. The sannyasi’s followers did not understand the subtle truth. Bharga went with the Lord to Grnara. The hill was very high. The two feet of Guru-datta were there, at the top of the hill.

The group of sannyasis went to see the feet. We went to see the feet at dawn. We approached the feet in the afternoon. The two feet shone on top of a rock. Seeing them, the Lord offered respects. On the feet were signs of the flag, thunderbolt and goad. Seeing the lotus feet, the Lord said, “Hari Hari.”

One priest stayed there at all times. Visvambara asked him about the feet. The priest explained.

When the Yadus were to disappear, Baladeva came here. He came to perform penance. He assumed a yogasana and began austerities. Baladeva remained in austerities in his yogasana. At Prabhasa the Yadus began fighting, in a drunken state. They fought with each other, drawing blood. Satyaki and many other warriors went to Yama’s abode. By Krsna’s will, all the Yadus died. Finally, he saw Krsna on the top of the mountain. Seeing Baladeva, leader of the Yadus, the Lord spoke to him about his own destination. “I will go to Goloka. Having finished my work, I will not stay. The earth has become full of the Yadu’s sins. For this reason, the Yadus have been uprooted. If the Pandavas mourn for me, you should free them from grief. Draupadl is dearer than my life. I request that you first pacify her.”

Hearing this, Baladeva began crying. With politeness he said, “There are many devotees like Vidura and Uddhava. When you go, what will I say to them? What sign will you leave for them? Seeing that sign, they will be attracted to you. Knowing that you are their very life, you go to Goloka, but what will you do for them? They do not know anything except Krsna. How can you give them up and go to your place? Draupadl will lament. What will I say to make them understand? Please tell me.”

Hearing his, Krsna pressed his feet here. In that way his footprints remained here.

The priest, saying this, informed them of the incident. At that moment, prema arose in the Lord’s heart. Gauranga, the abode of bliss, the abode of prema, beheld the footprints with steady gaze. As he looked, suddenly in his heart torrents of prema arose.

With choked voice, the Lord said softly, “O priest! O brother! What a jewel you have shown. You will see attainment of happiness at all times. I have not seen anyone with equal piety. If the footprints fall into the heart of a stone-hearted person, he has surpassed the happiness of Brahman.”

Gauranga joyfully placed the footprints on his head. He rolled over them many times. When prema rose he ignored his body, thin as a stick. Touching those feet, the Lord closed his eyes. Tears

flowed over his chest. Clapping his hands, he circumambulated the feet. His hair and clothing became loose. Seeing his bhava, Ramananda became unconscious. Govind Carana rolled on the ground.

Gauranga descended from the mountain and spent the night on the bank of the Bhadra River. Rising in the morning, we all crossed the river. Eventually we saw Dhanvidhara jungle. Dhanvidhara jungle was very extensive. I became afraid on seeing Jhari-khanda.

At that place there were various animals such as lions and tigers. Understanding this, I was most afraid. Understanding my desire by indications, the Lord laughed, and said, “Why are you uselessly afraid? If chanting the Lord’s name destroys fear of death, why are you afraid in Jhari-khanda?”

We took a group of sixteen people. Sacmandana then entered the forest. The beauty of the jungle was most attractive. How much can I describe of its beauty? Jungle flowers gave off fragrance. Hundreds of trees and creepers swayed in the breeze. Various birds were perched on the branches. Hearing the singing, my prana was overwhelmed. In the middle was one path and on both sides was jungle. In the middle was the party of sannyasis. Above our heads one could see the sun. I looked around because of hunger. To whose door would I go for begging? There were no villagers here. Where would I receive alms? On two sides, fruit hung from various trees. Seeing the fruit, I became very joyful. I cannot describe the astonishing fruit.

Many fruits were ripe, beautiful in rows. The fruits were similar to kamaranga fruit (star fruit). I had never tasted such fruit. The fruit fell in heaps here and there. All the sannyasis ate the fruit with both their hands. I could not eat the fruit without getting permission. But seeing the fruit, I developed greed. For the Lord, I got guti fruit. In the afternoon the Lord offered the fruit. Tasting several of those fruits, Gauranga said to eat it with pleasure. I filled my stomach as much as I could, eating them. As many as I received, I cut open.

Govinda Carana ate only a little of the fruit. Ramananda slowly savored it. Everyone saw the amazing qualities of the fruit. By this forest fruit, hunger and thirst were both relieved. The tree was as wonderful as a cooked sweet. I ate with greed that amazing fruit. Whatever fruit I saw I ate. Cutting them open, I ate till my stomach was full.

There was no trace of humans in the thick jungle. Everyone chanted the name there. Before evening it was dark on the path. The Lord sat under one tree. In the middle of the jungle the king had made a place. With the Lord we went there at the side of a hill. The room was enclosed with forest wood and had no doors. All the sannyasis sat down there. Clapping his hands, the Lord began singing the name. Hearing the name, the sannyasis become intoxicated.

I went to collect wood and made a fire. Bhargadeva chanted, clapping his hands. I had no fear in the jungle. Chanting the name, everyone passed the night. The next morning, chanting the name, Gauranga went out, remembering the Lord. The jungle was dense on whatever road we walked. Seeing Jhari-khanda, my body trembled. Going a great distance, we came to a small canal. There Caitanya took his bath. After bathing we travelled quickly. Going some distance, we sat down.

The Lord told Ramananda to go and bring some fruit. Ramananda brought fruit and kept it at that place. Gathering the various types of fruit, Gauranga performed worship and offered the fruit in joy. I have never seen such sweet fruit. Everyone gathered and filled themselves by eating the fruit. There was food for thousands on the road. How can I describe the mercy of the Lord? Finishing our noon duties, we departed. In the afternoon we found a house.

At the edge of the forest was an enclosure with pillars. Gauranga joyfully entered. The sannyasis brought wood and started a fire.

Clapping his hands, the Lord began singing. He sang, “Hare Krsna Hare Krsna Hare Krsna Hare.” As he sang, I saw that he was becoming unsteady. His hairs stood on end and his body trembled. Gauranga began to cry loudly. Seeing his state, Bharga lamented. The next day at dawn we got up and left. A group of travelers were coming on the road. When they saw there were two parties on the road, everyone joyfully chanted the Lord’s names.

In seven days, we traversed Dhanvidhara jungle. Everyone experienced bliss. We all went happily to Gopitala near Amarapuri. Everyone called this Prabhasa tirtha. I was pleased in mind to see Prabhasa. The Yadus gave up their bodies there. The Lord went there, weeping profusely. The Yadus, becoming intoxicated with liquor fought with each other and gave up their bodies. By Krsna’s desire all the Yadu warriors died, not recognizing themselves and others. Carudesna, Suratni, Satyaki, Yuyudhana, Samba and Gada, all intelligent, fought with each other and died at this place.

The Lord with miserable face cried at this place. When he cried, no one could be joyful. Crying, the blissful Lord fell on the ground. To take away the lamentation and misery of the world, the name has been spread. The Lord at all times gave instruction to the people of this world: chant the name and with prema, worship the Lord. Day and night going from door to door, he drowned the world in spreading the Lord’s name.

“Who will receive the name of the Lord? Come forward. Without a price, I give this jewel to everyone. Carefully take this jewel which is priceless. Easily you will cross the ocean of samsara. If one time you utter the name, you will cut your bondage and go to the eternal abode. Other than the Lord’s name, what can cut the fearful, huge, tough knot of maya?'" Saying this, the Lord went from door to door. He scattered the rasa of prema in the world of samsara.

The people of Amarapuri gathered and, on seeing the Lord, became blissful. Like a madman, he ran about. Like a madman, he wandered about. Sometimes he exhaled loudly and became unconscious. The pupils of his eyes turned upwards. His hair fell down his back. Tears fell constantly on his chest. Like a madman, his clothing fell off. His limbs were all covered with dust. Gauranga said, “Where is the yajna kunda?”

The priests, going with him, showed him Prabhasa. We went south of Prabhasa and there saw the kunda. The Lord offered wood in this sacrifice. At this place, the Yadus fought and died. At this place Satyabhama made a pleasant forest and came with Krsna here. She used to live here in the highest bliss. Seeing this place, intelligent Gauranga began crying. He shouted and constantly wept, calling out loudly. After a while, ten priests came to see the place. Discussing here for three days, the Lord then went to Dvaraka.

He did not see any other tirthas at Prabhasa. We arrived at Dvaraka in early Asvina month. Constantly chanting Krsna’s name, the Lord travelled. We went on the shore to Dvaraka. After four days we arrived at the estuary of the ocean. We crossed on a rope. In this way we arrived at Dvaraka. We could see Raivataka mountain. Gauranga looked at the mountain in a daze. He laughed with a smirk. What was he thinking, with happy face? Mahaprabhu, laughing, glanced at me. Looking at me, he said, “Going to Dvaraka, offering respects, everyone should become successful. Rub the dust on all your limbs with devotion. You have seen Dvaraka as a result of punya. On the strength of previous births of sukrti, you all see Dvaraka today.”

Hearing this, they all together offered respects. Gauranga’s well of bliss rose. Saying, “Haribol,” the Lord rolled in the dust. The ocean of bhava increased, crossing the boundaries. Who could control the Lord? He turned over and over on the ground. Eventually he entered within the city.

With hairs standing on end, his body began trembling. His eyes opened and tears flowed. He said, “Where are you, Dvarakadhisa?” He flooded Dvaraka with his tears. His hair came loose. In a loud voice, Gauranga cried. One cannot describe this bhava. The Lord shouted repeatedly, “Krsna!” When he entered the temple of Dvarakadhisa, he became mad with bliss with double feeling. His hairs stood on end like a kadamba. Rolling on the ground, he became covered with dust.

Mad with bhava, the Lord glazed with intoxication. He rolled on the ground in front of Dvarakadhisa. As bhakti increased, he fell in the four directions and cried loudly in joy. Closing his eyes, he looked within. What did he see inside? Sometimes he glanced upwards. Tears flowed from his eyes. On seeing Krsna, his hairs stood on end. He remained staring at him.

He circumambulated the temple three times and humbly offered respects each time. In joy Gauranga offered respects with eight limbs. Seeing this, Bhargadeva rolled on the ground. Gradually, it became known in Dvaraka. Everyone talked about the Lord.

Someone said, “Brother! Go and see this sannyasi. No one has seen such a sannyasi. No one can completely describe what they said. I do not think that there is another such sannyasi on earth. I have never seen such extraordinary bhava. That sannyasi is excellent in form and qualities. If you see him, bhakti to him arises in the mind. One sees tears in his eyes. I desire to go and associate with that sannyasi. If you see him, brother, you cut material bondage. If you see him, you will no longer have a taste for samsara. Seeing his form, the sinful person becomes immediately pure. I have never seen such a merciful person. He shows mercy, distributing the holy name to everyone. His locks spread over his outer cloth. On seeing his form, desires are fulfilled.

“When he sees an avatara of the Lord, it seems his heart is always full of bhakti-rasa. The young sannyasi always intoxicated with bhava, made the Dvaraka inhabitants most joyful. He has no kama, no anger, and no material desire. He has flawless faith in Dvarakadhisa. Calling the sinful, he gives them the name. Seeing him, the heart becomes materially detached.”

Gauranga remained in Dvaraka for a fortnight. Daily he went to see the lord of Dvaraka. Daily he went to see the Lord with devotion and intoxicated the people of Dvaraka with bhakti-rasa.

The people of Dvaraka, surrendered to bhakti, all went to see Gauranga. He discussed with all of them. Doing kirtana, everyone danced in prema. The city became unsteady with the bhava of dharma. Everyone’s heart became pure. Gentle breeze began to blow softly at all times so that all direction became fragrant with the smell of flowers. Everyone was blissful with the Lord’s association. All women and men came running.

One could see signs of auspiciousness in the four directions. All directions were pleased with the Lord’s names. Priests and householders gathered and heard instructions of dharma and spread that hearing. The Lord enlightened the persons who could not understand him. The Lord spoke various words and intoxicated them. Sometimes the Lord spoke indistinctly. Speaking indistinctly he made everyone understand. Somehow, the lord, possessor of all saktis, made people understand. Hearing his instructions, they all became unconscious. Jhanis and fools all came. Seeing the Lord their hairs stood on end.

One day in the evening, the Lord arrived quietly at the Krsna’s temple. Many people followed the Lord. Not saying anything, Sacinandana went to the door of the temple and offered respects with eight limbs. Seeing this, all the people became choked up. Folding his hands, the Lord offered many praises. Immediately tears gushed from his eyes. His chest flooded with the water of prema. He saw Krsna in whatever direction he glanced.

Closing his eyes, he shouted, “Krsna!” His hairs stood on end as he became filled with prema. How will I tell the person who has not seen this bhava that he is a great fool? The river of Caitanya flowed everywhere except the desert. No sinful person remained in the whole province. The Lord delivered the sinful by bhakti.

One day the priests created joy. They made a huge festival and arranged an offering. They invited devotee guests and distributed milk, yogurt, and purls. Gauranga, jewel of good qualities, went among the lame and personally distributed prasada. In the night everyone met and went to the cottage. In great joy, we passed the night. In this way, discussions were held for a fortnight. The next day the Lord left Dvaraka. The Lord said, “This time, I will go to Nilacala. Meeting everyone there, we will pass time in bliss. With everyone I will go to Vidya-nagara. I will not go to Puri alone, giving up Ramananda Raya. He is a great bhajananandi. Thinking of his conversations, my heart is pacified. He is the crest jewel of sadhakas. Living alone, he sings about the qualities of Krsna. Bhagavatam says that detached devotee is he who sheds tears on saying, “Hare Krsna.” If a cobbler calls out to Krsna, a treasure, with devotion, I offer a million respects to his feet. Ramananda, a devotee of Krsna, is to be worshipped. He is dearer than my life. I am fond of Ramananda like my very life. He is the greatest devotee and a renounced sannyasi. He is detached from material objects. He always sees Radha and Krsna. He considers great wealth to be like grass. Day and night, he calls out “Krsna” with prema. He shouts, “I have seen Krsna.” Mad with prema, Raya stands there trembling. By the name of Krsna, he discharges tears of prema. Becoming unconscious, he falls on the ground. My prana cannot tolerate separation from Raya. I will go quickly to Raya.”

Saying this, the Lord left. Hundreds of people followed. With sweet words he bid the people farewell. He walked near Khadi. He took Bhargadeva’s group as support. They came to the bank of the Khadi in order to cross. One by one, they all crossed. My sannyasi came to Gujarat. At the end of Asvina we returned to Baroda town. The Lord chanted the name. I and Govinda Carana went to a householder’s door to beg. I received alms of fruits, roots and ground wheat flour. In a pure manner I brought the goods back and prepared it. Making a residence at the base of a tree the Lord offered the food. Taking prasada everyone was satisfied.

The next day, we journeyed from Baroda. The Lord went south. After sixteen days we came to the bank of the Narmada River. We all bathed in the Narmada water. The Lord said, “Bhargadeva where will you go? Will you go with me to Puri?”

Bharga, squeezing his hands in front of the Lord said, “I will go south. King Mohanta Aditya serves Rana-choda-ji with devotion at Bombom Nagara. Receive my respects. Mercifully put your feet on my head.”

Saying this, Bhargadeva rolled on the ground and held the Lord’s feet with his two hands. He said, “You are Krsna. You are my Lord. Give me the gift of remembering your feet when I die. You went to Dvaraka to see your own pastimes, to fool the people. Put your feet on my head. You cannot fool me anymore.”

The Lord said, “O Bharga! Why do you say this? Why say this to me? Being detached, I wander on various paths. I have no food and no clothing. I am most deprived. I go door to door begging. Do not say such words to me again. Say the name of Krsna with faith. That faith in Krsna will enable you to meet Krsna. The Supreme Lord is condensed knowledge and bliss. By bhava for his lotus feet you will cross the material ocean. Think of the Lord’s feet with prema-bhakti. He will definitely show himself to you. The Lord is very merciful to the devotee. Meditate upon him. He is the shelter of the shelterless.”

Saying this, the Lord touched Bhargadeva. At that moment Bharga’s body became pure. Standing with folded hands, Bhargadeva looked around. Having become successful, finally he left.

There were some sannyasis with Bharga. They came and stood in front of the Lord. One by one they offered respects to the Lord. The Lord departed from them after saying sweet words. Bhargadeva went south. The Lord travelled to Puri with attraction. With Ramananda and Govinda Carana, I spent the day on the bank of the Narmada. The next day we walked along the bank and arrived at Dohada town. Begging, I brought some flour. The Lord made chapattis and offered them.

We spent the night at Dohada town. Under a tree, Gauranga chanted the name. At dawn we rose and went to Kuksi town. We saw may devotees there. Wherever we went we saw tulasi groves. All the villagers were devotees of Krsna. In the evening, everyone chanted the name. Seeing this, my Lord’s hairs stood on end. One poor brahmana lived there. In his house he had deities of Laksmi and Janardana. He worshipped them with devotion. Hearing this, the Lord went to his house. The brahmana seeing the Lord welcomed him with many services. He asked many questions in serving the guest. “I am called a brahmana but I am poor. Why do you put your feet in my house? How can I serve a sannyasi? I think dharma has been destroyed in my house.”

The Lord said, “Do not worry sir! The creator will give food in plenty. Everyone is mistaken concerning why and how the Lord acts. Krsna thinks of everything always. Please listen to the bare facts. The creator says that he gives to all so that they can eat. Why does the person without friends eat under a tree? Who will give food to a miserable insect in a forest? O brahmana, why do you think falsely?”

At that time one vaisya brought milk, sugar and flour to the brahmana’s house and gave it to him.

The vaisya said, “Listen O respected brahmana! You have received great mercy. In a dream I saw your Laksmi Janardana. They wanted to eat sweet rice in my house. Narayana, in human form, stays in your house. In the dream Narayana showed this to me.

I saw this last night in a dream. Thus, I have brought milk and sugar. O brahmana! Cooking the sweet rice, offer it to Narayana.”

Hearing of this incident the brahmana began crying. He said, “From where has the milk and sugar come?”

The Lord said, “Narayana has supplied it.”

The brahmana said, “I am a sufferer. This is astonishing.”

The Lord said, “Narayana... ”.

The brahmana said, “I thought about you.”

The Lord said, “Narayana has brought this.”

The vaisya glanced at the Lord’s face, becoming unconscious on seeing something.

The brahmana said, “O vaisya! What are you seeing, O brother?” The vaisya said, “I see something confusing. Listen to what I say to you, O best of brahmanas! In the dream I saw that human form of this sannyasi.”

Hearing this, the Lord said to the vaisya, “Why do you cause trouble with this lie again and again? Who did you see in your false dream? Why do you without reasons cause disturbance? O vaisya brother! You are most fortunate. Thus, the Lord showed himself in a dream. I am an ordinary sannyasi. I have come to eat at the brahmana’s house.”

The brahmana said, “What is the necessity of this talk? I want to serve the guest, thinking of Narayana.”

Weeping, the brahmana said to the Lord, “Cooking the sweet rice. You should offer it.”

Smiling, the Lord cooked the sweet rice. The brahmana walked about nearby. In bliss everyone took prasada. With his own hand the Lord distributed the prasada. There was huge festival in the brahmana’s house. The next day in the morning the Lord rose and started his journey. While he walked, the brahmana came near. He spoke miserable words, with folded hands. The Lord bid him farewell and went out in the morning. The vaisya hid himself. He followed behind the Lord on the path. Holding the Lord’s feet, he began weeping.

Merciful Caitanya lifted him up. The Lord said, “You are devotee. What are you doing, brother?”

The vaisya said, “Be merciful to me, O Gosai. I think I cannot leave you. Give me the mercy of the dust from your feet.”

Laughing, Caitanya offered once the sweet name of the Lord in his ear. By the Lord’s mercy, his sins were destroyed. Being completely detached, the vaisya went away. By the mercy of the Lord, the vaisya giving up material life, made a tulasi grove far away and stayed there. He did not converse with people. He always meditated on Krsna with flute in his mouth. He said “O Lord! Give me mercy. Destroy all the suffering of this sinner by your mercy.”

Staying in a hut, he did not go to his house. Saying the name of the Lord, he would beg from door to door. Visvambhara gave mercy to the vaisya and left the town, going through the jungle.

We all went through the dense jungle and for two days did not see any town or village. Leaving the jungle after two days, we arrived at Amajhora town. We were disturbed with hunger pains. The unchanging Lord said to me, “Hari! Hari!”

He said, “When the Lord brings food then I will accept food.”

I begged and brought two seras of flour. The Lord made sixteen chapattis. At that time one girl came. She said, “Give me a little as I am hungry. I have no food, no clothing. I have nothing to eat. Going on the road with children I beg and eat.”

Hearing her words, the merciful Lord gave her his food. The suffering girl went away satisfied and the Lord spent the day without eating. In the evening I begged and brought some fruit. The Lord ate the fruit and passed the night. At this place there was Laksmana-kunda. Hearing of this, in the morning we went there. The attractive pond was at the edge of the town. It was small, surrounded by hills. When Slta was thirsty, Laksmana shot an arrow and made this kunda. It became famous as Laksmana’s kunda. Slta said it was a great tirtha. It was most attractive and very deep. Bathing in it, the body became cool.

Merciful Gauranga bathed in the tirtha and chanted the name. On hearing this, the heart melted. The next day we went to the top of Vindhya Mountain, where there was beautiful Mandura town. In a cave in the mountain one man lived, performing austerities. Hearing of this man, Gauranga went there to see him.

The sannyasi lived there meditating. His form was very sweet. His limbs were the color of molten gold. Rays of his effulgence spread in the four directions. He had long fingernails. His beard and moustache hung over his chest. His body was just skin and bones. He considered his body as a cage. Naked, he meditated with concentration. Seeing that form, bhakti arose. He seemed like a wooden deity. I looked at him repeatedly.

Mahaprabhu came in front of him and stood there. The austere man broke his meditation and began looking around. At that moment their four eyes met. Suddenly the tapasvi laughed. The Lord held a conversation with him. He then descended the mountain and came to Mandala town.

On the left was Vindhya mountain and on the right was the Narmada river. In three days, we went from there to Devaghara. At Devaghara there was a leper. He was called Adi-narayana. He was the best of merchants. He was wealthy but always mentally overwhelmed.

There was a banyan tree outside the village. The merciful Lord stayed near it. The four directions were illuminated by the Lord’s beauty. This became well known to the public. Several persons came to see the sannyasi. I went to the town to collect alms. Ramananda went to collect flowers. Govinda Carana went to the riverbank.

That day I received atapa rice. Ramananda brought various types of flowers. After bathing, the Lord started worship. Govinda brought dry wood. Offering food, Gauranga began chanting. As he chanted, his hairs stood on end. Choked with prema, he began dancing. Becoming unconscious, he fell on the earth. Hearing this, many people came. The leper came and stood by his side. He came and cried, folding his hands. He said while profusely weeping, “Deliver me.”

Adi-narayana was the best of devotees. The Lord made him eat prasada. Eating with devotion, he became free of leprosy. Taking prasada, his leprosy was destroyed. Hearing the news, many sick people came. Seeing the confusion, the Lord looked around. He glanced at me, giving a signal. Taking the wood shoes, I started the journey. On that pretext the Lord left the town. Adi-narayana went along with him.

The Lord said, “You have become freed by the mercy of Krsna. Why do you come with me? Go to your house and meditate on Krsna’s feet.”

Adi-narayana said, “I will not go home. I will travel with you from place to place.”

The Lord said, “Go home and enjoy your wealth.”

Adi-narayana said, “What is the use of wealth? O merciful Lord! If you do not let me accompany you, I will spend my time locked in a hut.”

The Lord said, “Make a tulasi garden. Live there and pass your time.”

Hearing the Lord’s words, Adi-narayana gave up everything and chanted, “Hari Hari.”

He became the best devotee. He passed his time in chanting Krsna’s name. Offering respects to the Lord’s feet, Adi-narayana bid farewell to the Lord.

At thirty krosa’s distance was Sivani town. Visvambara went there in two days. Malaya mountain was east of Sivani. The Lord went there with great attraction. The Lord saw Malaya mountain and then came to the town of Candipura. Seeing Candi devi at Candipura, Gauranga went to Rayapura while remembering the Lord.

At Rayapura, many people, desiring to see the Lord, came near him. Gauranga, seeing the misfortune of the jivas, spread bliss of the name at every house. The Lord then went to Vidyanagara. Seeing Ramananda, he folded his hands. Ramananda Raya came and offered respects. Holding his hand, the Lord embraced him. The greatest devotee backed away. He began weeping, with great humility.

The Lord said, “O Raya! Come with me. Together we will go and see Jagannatha. You, I and Bhattacarya will go to Puri and chant the named to full satisfaction. I will be very happy speaking tattva with you. Come with me, we will go to Puri.”

Raya said, “You go ahead. A few days later I will go to Puri.”

Hearing this, Mahaprabhu smiled slightly and, rising at dawn, went north. That day we went very far and in six days the four of us arrived to Ratnapura. Leaving Ratnapura, we came to the Mahanadi River. Going along its bank we travelled east. At some distance, Mahaprabhu went to Svarnagada. The Lord saw the beauty of the town. How can I describe the beauty of the astonishing fort? Looking in four directions it was amazing. A king named Santisvara lived at the fort. A messenger went and spoke to the king. Hearing the pleasant news, the king went humbly to see the Lord.

Seeing the Lord, the very pious king folded his hands and rolled on the ground. The king said, “O respected sannyasi! Please listen. Please purify my house today. Be merciful and today eat at my house.”

Saying this, the king recited many prayers. Hearing this, the Lord glanced at me. I wanted to take alms from the king. The king gave plenty of alms, standing with folded hands. In the afternoon the king bid farewell. Mahaprabhu spent the night under the tree. In the morning, we all went to Sambala-pura. We arrived in the evening. The city was very beautiful, surrounded by mountains. We spent the night happily in Sambala-pura. At ten krosa’s distance was Bhramara town. Mahaprabhu went there. Many devotees lived in Bhramara. The Lord stayed there four days.

An Oriya brahmana named Visnu-rudra stayed there serving Krsna. He was very devotional. The Lord went to see him. The Lord conversed with him and then went happily to Pratapana town. Giving them the name of the Lord, he then went to Dasa-pala, where a pasanda magician who greatly suffered lived. The Lord made everyone insane with the holy name. Gauranga was at all times mad with bliss. By the name he delivered many sinners.

The next day, we went to Rasala-kunda. There we saw Kurma-deva. Seeing Kurma, the Lord went mad with prema. Tears streamed rapidly from his eyes. Folding his hands, he recited many verses in praise of Kurma-deva. He fell with force on the ground. The people of Rasala-kunda had almost no devotion. Seeing that, the Lord stayed there three days. Calling all men and women, the Lord delivered them by the holy name. By the Lord’s mercy all became intoxicated. They began to chant the name with devotion.

At that place there was a Maduya Brahmana. His son met the Lord. The son said, “Be merciful to me. Take away my sorrow by the dust of your feet. I am a great rascal. I know nothing. Deliver me by bhakti. My father cannot tolerate Krsna’s name. Mercifully give bhakti to his heart. It is sad that my father hates

Krsna. O merciful Lord! Give bhakti to his mind. When he sees devotees, he scolds them. Mercifully destroy all his sins. I have heard that you are the abode of mercy. O merciful Lord! Give this benediction.”

Hearing the child, the Lord put his hand on his back. Suddenly bhakti arose in his heart. Hearing this, the brahmana became blind with anger. With stick in hand, he ran to the Lord. The brahmana said, “Listen, O wicked pretender! You have destroyed my only son. I will beat you with this stick. See who can protect you here.”

Folding his hands, the son while weeping said, “Please by your mercy forgive the offense of my father. My most ignorant father does not know you. Forgive me, as a servant of your feet.” Hearing this, several men came to beat the brahmana. Not listening to the anyone’s words, the brahmana started to beat Caitanya with his stick.

The brahmana said, “You made my son into a devotee. Deceiving him, you will take him away. Bewildering the boy, where will you go? This time I will punish you so you will understand. I have seen many sannyasis. You will receive teachings from me.”

Smiling, Caitanya said, “O brother! Listen. When you beat me, I want the name. As many times as you chant the name, that many times you can beat me. If you want to beat me, then chant the names Hare Krsna. I offer you my back. One time chant the name and beat me.”

Hearing this, the brahmana’s son folded his hands and facing the Lord spoke. He said, “O Lord! Forgive my father. Deliver him from hell. I beg at your lotus feet that peaceful Nimai does not speak like this to people. “

Slightly smiling, the Lord said to him, “Anyone born in this family will not fear hell. Millions of men will go to Vaikuntha.”

Saying this he glanced at the brahmana and said, “O brahmana!

Say the name of the Lord while beating me. Your hard heart is almost like a desert. May it become sweet by the mercy of Krsna! There is no harm if you beat me. O brother! Just say one time Hare Krsna.”

Hearing such words, the brahmana began trembling. In fear he urinated in his clothing. He cowered and could not see. Overcome with weeping, he fell on the ground. By the influence of the Lord, the brahmana, overwhelmed, held the Lord’s feet with his hands.

The brahmana said, “O merciful Lord! I request you to deliver me from hell. Having offended you, I am most fearful. By your mercy, forgive my offense. O merciful one! Not knowing, I said many things to you. O protector! Give punishment that you judge fit.” Seeing the humility of the brahmana, leaning forward, Gauranga joyfully gave the nectar of the name in his ear. The brahmana became successful and his mind became pure. He took permission to leave, holding the Lord’s feet.

Having delivered the pasanda brahmana, the Lord went to the bank of Rsi-kulya River. On both sides of the river many sages lived. All came and requested Gauranga. When the Lord came to that river, the news reached Puri. The Lord stayed there for three days. He purified Rsi-kulya with the name of the Lord. When the Lord came to Alalanatha, Gadadhara and Murari came running.

Khanjana Acarya came with deep attachment. Tracing out the Lord, he came before the others. Sarvabhauma came blowing two horns. Narahari was seen with a flag. Haridasa, Ramadasa and Krsnadasa came quickly, breathing heavily. Jagannathadasa and Devaki-nandana, Chota Haridasa, the singer Laksmana, Visnudasa, Puridasa, and Damodara, Narayana-tirtha and Giridharadasa, Sarasavati Puri and unlimited brahmanas came in lines to see the Lord. Rama Pandita played a horn. Balaramadasa came, his hairs standing on end. Hundreds of scholars were seen. My heart began dancing in bliss.

Some danced, some laughed, and some sang. With one mouth one cannot describe the bliss. Thousands of people surrounded the Lord. Mad with bliss, they all started to chant the name. Murari and Mukunda came and embraced him. Gupta fell down on his knees nearby.

Siddha Krsnadasa came and offered respects. Taking his hand, the Lord embraced him. All the devotees together brought the Lord. Many devotees were beating drums, making the Lord’s eyes move about in bliss.

Many devotees joined in kirtana. Gauranga, swinging his head danced in joy. Seeing Khanjana, the Lord, saying ‘Hari bol,’ spreading his arms, embraced him. Spreading his arms, Gauranga began dancing. Sarvabhauma rolled at his feet. Folding his hands, Sarvabhauma began to speak.

“The arrow of separation pierced my heart. I tolerated your separation as a great fool. I maintained my life for so many days. Mercifully tread upon me with your feet. Then I will know that you have affection for me.”

Saying this, Sarvabhauma’s voice choked up. Gauranga lifted him up and embraced him. While chanting the Lord’s name, everyone went to Puri with the Lord. Hundreds upon hundreds of blue and white flags flapped and many drums sounded. Some people danced, some sang, intoxicated with bliss. Many people stood staring. Leaning and swaying, Sacinandana walked. Sweet mrdangas sounded, pleasant to hear. Raising his hands, Gadadhara danced. Raghunatha dasa and Damodara also danced.

Respecting him, the Lord gave attention to Raghunatha. “I attain great bliss seeing you.”

Gauranga embraced him. Raghunatha rolled at his feet. On the third day of Magha, Gauranga arrived at Puri with all these devotees. In the afternoon Mahaprabhu arrived at Puri. Millions of people came to visit him.

Many people walked with the Lord, with bare feet. They entered the temple and saw Jagannatha. With fixed gaze they looked, and tears began to flow. Gauranga became unconscious and fell forcibly on the earth. His hair came undone and his kaupina became loose. He looked miserable with dust covered body. The devotees chanted the name in the four directions. Sarvabhauma held him in his lap. His hairs stood on end like a kadamba flower. Thousands of streams of perspiration began to flow. Reviving himself, Caitanya stood up. He fixed his eyes on the deity, Mahavisnu.

Sarvabhauma said, “O Lord! Seeing your form of Jagannatha your well of prema manifests. You teach people by looking at your form. Mad with mahabhava, you begin to cry. In front of unmoving Visnu you move about. O Lord! Why do you weep? You are pretending. You are directly the son of Nanda. Why do you blind my eyes? Do this to those who do not understand. Do not steal your form from me. You are the holder of Govardhana. You are the master of Vrndavana. You are the life of the gopis. You are the shelter of the shelterless. Those born in the Yadu dynasty did not know you. The unfortunate Yadus knew nothing. Having you in my hand, I will not give you up. Show to me your form holding the flute. On the chest of your golden form is a line by whose power the form of time cannot be seen.”

The Lord said, “O Sarvabhauma! Speak something else. Why do you not speak other subjects? Why do you speak various words, agitated with illusion? Hearing your words, I plug my ears. You say I am Krsna. Why without cause do you make me an offender? From your mouth, topics of Krsna are like nectar. O Bhatta! Speak about Krsna and soothe my prana.”

Bhatta said, “O Lord! What can I speak but what you make me speak? I am just a human animal.”

The Lord said, “There is no use in speaking further. Let us go home now together.”

After seeing the arati, the Lord went to Kasl Misra’s house with his associates. At that time Sarvabhauma obtained prasada and brought it there. Gauranga in joy distributed the prasada. All were blissful in receiving prasada.

In the large yard of Kasl Misra’s house, many people came to see the Lord. Staying in Misra’s house, merciful Gauranga in great bliss danced and sang songs about Krsna. I cannot describe how many people came and went. There was ever fresh happiness in Misra’s house. Hearing of the Lord’s arrival by mouth, many inhabitants of Bengal came to see him.

Bhatta brought prasada daily. The Lord was blissful on receiving prasada. With joy, Gauranga received the prasada. He distributed prasada to everyone’s hands. Before prasada there was nama-sankirtana. Everyone ate prasada with attraction to prema. I was fortunate to speak this now. Everyone danced in bliss, clapping their hands.

Ramananda Vasu and Govinda Carana bid farewell and went to Bengal. Wanting to see Gauranga again, hundreds of people came with attraction. Srivasa, Kesava dasa, Siddha Haridasa all came gathered near Caitanya. Santacarya, Vipra-dasa, Rupa and Sanatana came to see him. Chanting the name, they all became mad with bliss. Merciful Caitanya gave bhakti door to door.

“Who will receive the holy name? O my brothers, come!” Nimai, saying this, distributed the name of the Lord. By the Lord’s mercy no sinner and no sufferer remained. The Lord gave the name of the Lord everywhere. The great tirtha of Purl became the abode of bliss. Children, women and elders chanted the name. Hearing the name, animals and birds danced. The ocean with arms spread danced in front. Old women, young people and babies danced. Chaste women came to see him on the road. On one side the ocean began dancing. In the other direction, an ocean of prema arose. The trees and creepers became mad with prema. Snow fell on the pretext of showering tears. There was constant, fresh happiness in Puri. Those who saw know. Who should I speak to? Mrdangas, kettle drums and karatalas sounded. Among them, my Sacinandana danced.

Ramadasa very skilfully played the kettle drum. For that reason, he came constantly to the kirtana. Ramadasa was a great devotee, attracted to prema. Playing the kettle drum, he walked in front of the kirtana. In bliss Prataparudra gave up royal duties and came to Misra’s house constantly to see the dancing.

When Mahaprabhu went on nagara kirtana, the king followed behind wearing humble clothing. With hands raised, his body limp, the Lord danced and danced, mad with prema. With half closed eyes, he stared at the sky. Sometimes he fell on the ground. All men and women became mad with the name of the Lord. Mad with prema, the family women came running in lines. Thousands of people came on four sides. The Lord moved in front, dancing and dancing.

In this way the Lord passed the day. He spent the night with Ramananda alone. One day Mahaprabhu with attraction to Krsna, ran in front to catch Mahavisnu. Not considering any obstacle, he ran with attraction. In front there was a hidden obstacle and he fell on the ground.

From that day, the Lord did not go into the temple. He saw the deity from far away. Standing at the door of the bhoga-mandapa, he gazed at Mahavisnu. Putting his left hand on the Garuda-stambha, he saw the deity, mad with prema. In this way for some days, he stayed in Puri.

Wanting to see Jagannatha out of attraction, one day my Lord, at Misra’s house, sang about the qualities of Krsna with the devotees. He called “Govinda” and said that I should go to Advaita Acarya. Taking leave with a letter and an order, I departed for Santipura and offered respects. Putting his hand on my back, the Lord gave blessings. Hundreds of tears flowed from my eyes. He said, “Do not cry, Govinda, my life! Bringing this to the acarya, be blissful.”

Hearing these words, my eyes flowed with tears. I could not tolerate the arrow of separation from the Lord. How can I tolerate the intense separation from the Lord? Cruel pain arose in my mind.
