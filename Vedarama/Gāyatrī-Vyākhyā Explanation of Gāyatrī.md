Explanation of Gāyatrī Mantra from Agni Purāṇa, Chapter 216, By Jīva Gosvāmī

# Gāyatrī-Vyākhyā: Explanation of Gāyatrī

### Verse 1

*gāyaty ukthāni śāstrāṇi bhargaṁ prāṇāṁs tathaiva ca \|*  
*tataḥ smṛteyaṁ gāyatrī sāvitrī yata eva ca \|*  
*prakāśinī sā savitur vāg-rūpatvāt sarasvatī \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because it reveals (*****gayati*****) the Vedic mantras and scriptures, the Lord (*****bhargam*****) and the cause of the jīvas (*****prāṇān*****), it is called gāyatrī. Because it reveals savitā it is called Sāvitrī. Because it is the form of words it is called sarasvatī.**

**Комментарий:**

*śrī-rādhā-ramaṇo jayati \|*  
*sanātana-samo yasya jyāyān śrīmān sanātanaḥ \|*  
*śrī-vallabho’nujaḥ so’sau śrī-rūpo jīva-sad-gatiḥ \|\|*  
*athāgneya-sthā gāyatrī-vyākhyā vivriyate \| ukthāni praṇavātmaka-mantrān \| śāstrāṇi sarvān api vedān \| bhargaṁ vakṣyamāṇaṁ viṣṇu-rūpaṁ tejaḥ \| prāṇān sarva-jīva-hetūn tad-vibhūtīṁś ca \| yato yasmād gāyati prakāśayati tato gāyatrī smṛtā \| yasmād eva ca trayīmayasya savituḥ prakāśinī prādurbhāvayitrī tasmāt sṛjet savitāram iti sāvitrī ca \| vāg-rūpatvāt sarasvatī ca sā \|\|1\|\|*

**Śrī Rūpa, eternal peace incarnate, identical in mood to Sanātana, his elder brother, and whose younger brother was Śrī Vallabha, is the means of my success and the good fortune of all the** ***jīvas.***

**The explanation of gāyatrī found in Agni Purāṇa will now be explained.** ***Ukthāni*** **means mantras whose life is** ***oṁ. Śāstrāṇi*** **means all the Vedas.** ***Bhargam*** **means effulgence, the form of Viṣṇu, which will be explained.** ***Prāṇān*** **means the cause of all jīvas and the Lord’s powers or vibhūtis. Because (*****yataḥ*****) it reveals (*****gayati*****) it is called** ***gāyatrī*****. And because it reveals savitā, the sun-- because it creates savitā--it is called** ***sāvitrī.*** **Because it is the very form of words (words incarnate—*****vag-rūpatvāt*****) it is called** ***sarasvatī.***

### Verse 2

*taj-jyotiḥ paraṁ brahma bhargas tejo yataḥ smṛtam \|*  
*bhargaḥ syād bhrājata iti bahulaṁ chandasīritam \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

***Bharga*** **refers to the light, the supreme Brahman, since** ***bharga*** **means** ***tejas*** **or light.** ***Bharga*** **is derived from** ***bhrājate*** **(it shines). This is stated in the sūtras of Pāṇini.**

**Комментарий:**

*atho geyeṣu mukhyatvād bhargam eva vivṛṇoti—taj-jyotir iti \| yo’yaṁ bhargaḥ sa eva tat prasiddhaṁ paraṁ brahma \| yato bharga eva tejaḥ smṛtaḥ sva-prakāśa-jyotī-rūpatayā nirdiṣṭaḥ \| kayā niruktyā tasya bhargasya tejastvaṁ tatrāha—bhargaḥ syād bhrājata iti \| kathaṁ sidhyati ? tatrāha—bahulaṁ chandasīti \| bhagavatā pāṇininā īritaṁ sūtritam ity arthaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Being the chief among what should be described,** ***bharga*** **is explained. It is light (*****tat-jyotiḥ*****). What is** ***bharga*** **is well known as the supreme Brahman. Because it is radiant (*****bharga*****) it is called** ***tejas*** **(light, splendor)--because it is self-manifesting light. The derivation of** ***bharga*** **by which it is called** ***tejas*** **is explained.** ***Bharga*** **means “it shines” (*****bhrājate*****). How is this proved? It is stated in many verses (*****bahulam chandasi*****). This means it is declared in the sūtras of Pāṇini.**

### Verse 3

*vareṇyaṁ sarva-tejobhyaḥ śreṣṭhaṁ vai paramaṁ padam \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

***Vareṇyam*** **means the supreme shelter of all things, the source of all manifestations.**

**Комментарий:**

*atha tasya mantroktaṁ vareṇyatvaṁ sādhayati—vareṇyam ity ardhena \| sa ca bhargo vareṇyaṁ yat paramaṁ padaṁ sarvasyāthāśraya-rūpaṁ vastu \| vareṇyaṁ nāma kiṁ vastu ? tatrāha sarva-tejobhyaḥ śreṣṭhaṁ yat tad evety arthaḥ \| sarveṣāṁ tejasāṁ prakāśānāṁ prakāśakatvena sva-prakāśa-rūpam iti bhāvaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**The word** ***vareṇyam*** **is explained in one line. This light is the supreme position (*****paramaṁ padam*****), that entity which gives shelter to everything else. What is that object? It is that which is the best (*****śreṣṭham*****) among all powers (*****sarva-tejobhyaḥ*****). This means it is the self-manifesting form which causes all other manifestations.**

### Verse 4

*svargāpavarga-kāmair vā varaṇīyaṁ sadaiva hi \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Or** ***vareṇyam*** **can mean what is always sought (*****varaṇīyam*****) by persons desiring Svarga or liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

*evaṁ bhargasya vareṇya-padena rūḍhyā śreṣṭhatvaṁ darśayitvā yoga-vṛttyā sarva-prārthanīyatvaṁ darśayati svarga ity ardhena—spaṣṭam \|\|4\|\|*

**Having shown the superior nature of** ***bharga*** **by the conventional meaning of** ***vareṇyam*****, the text now shows that** ***bharga*** **is sought out by all, through etymological meaning in one line. The meaning is clear.**

### Verse 5-6

*vṛṇoter varaṇārthatvāj jāgrat-svapna-vivarjitam \|\|5-6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

***Varaṇyam*** **means** ***varaṇīyam,*** **since it is derived from** ***vṛnoti*** **(he chooses).**

**Комментарий:**

*tatra tad-artha-sampādaka-dhātv-artham api hetutvena nirdiśati vṛṇoter varaṇārthatvād iti spaṣṭam \|\|5\|\|*

**The meaning of the verbal root is given to show why** ***varaṇīyam*** **means “sought out.” The meaning is clear.**

*atha paramatva-jñāpanāya punar varam eva viśinaṣṭi jāgrat-svapna-vivarjitam iti \| turīyāvasthād api jīvāt param ity arthaḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**The word** ***vara*****, meaning choicest (also derived from** ***vṛnoti*****), is explained in order to show the supreme nature of** ***bharga*****. This means the entity is devoid of waking and sleep states. It is in the fourth state. It is superior to the jīva (who is subjected to waking and sleep states).**

### Verse 7

*nityaṁ śuddhaṁ buddham ekaṁ nityaṁ bhargam adhīśvaram \|*  
*ahaṁ brahma paraṁ jyotir dhyāyemahi vimuktaye \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

***Bharga*** **is eternally pure and conscious and eternally one entity, endowed with all śaktis. We should meditate on Brahman, the supreme light, with whom we identify, for liberation in order to meet the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

*tad eva bharga-vareṇyayoḥ padayor arthaṁ darśayitvā vākyasya prayojanam āha—nityam iti \| ahaṁ bhargaṁ dhyāyemahi, tatra bhargasya viśeṣaṇāni nitya-śuddham ity ādīni \| aham ity asya viśeṣaṇaṁ brahmeti \| tatra nityaṁ sadaiva śuddhaṁ na tu jīvavat saṁsāritvāvastham ity arthaḥ \| evaṁ buddhaṁ sadaiva bodha-yuktam ity arthaḥ \| ekaṁ na tu jīvavad anekam \| adhīśvaraṁ sarva-śakti-yuktam \| ahaṁ brahma paraṁ jyotir iti nādevo devam arcayed iti nyāyena svasya tādātmya-bhāvanā darśitā \| dhyāyemahi na kevalo'ham eva dhyāyeyaṁ kintu sarve’pi vayaṁ jīvā dhyāyemety arthaḥ \| kim-arthaṁ dhyāyasi ? tatrāha vimuktaye \| saṁsāra-mukti-pūrvaka-tat-prāptaye \| tad etan-mate bharga-śabdasyād-antatve puṁstve ca siddhe mantro’py evam eva vyākhyeyam \| supāṁ sulug ity ādinā chāndasa-sūtreṇa dvitīyayaika-vacanasyāmaḥ sutvādeśād evaṁ tatra ya ity eva vakṣyate, na tu ya ity anena savitur ākarṣaḥ kriyate \| dhyeyaḥ sadā savitṛ-maṇḍala-madhya-vartīti vidhānāt \| atas tad bhargopadeśād iti nyāyāc ca \|\|7\|\|*

**Having shown the meaning of the words** ***bhargam*** **and** ***vareṇyam,*** **the purpose of the statement is explained. We should meditate (*****dhyāyemahi*****) on** ***bhargam*****. Words are listed to describe** ***bhargam*****: it is eternal (*****nityam*****) and pure (*****śuddham*****). The word** ***aham*** **is described by the word Brahman.** ***Bharga*** **is eternal and pure unlike the jīva who is subject to saṁsāra. He is always endowed with awareness (*****buddham*****). He is one (*****ekam*****), unlike the jīvas who are many. He is endowed with all śaktis (*****adhīśvaram*****). The words** ***aham brahma param jyotiḥ*** **(I am Brahman, the supreme light) indicate identification in mentality with the supreme entity, since it is said** ***nādevo devam arcayet*****: one who is not like the Lord cannot worship the Lord. Not only should I worship this entity, but all jīvas should worship. Thus it is said “we should meditate upon this entity.” Why should one meditate? For attaining the supreme entity after liberation from saṁsāra (*****vimuktaye*****). According to the text** ***bharga*** **is in the masculine. The mantra is explained with this conclusion. Because of the sūtra** ***supāṁ suluk*****,** ***bhargas*** **(in the actual gayatri mantra) should be taken to be accusative singular masculine (rather than neuter). The gāyatrī mantra uses the word** ***yaḥ*** **(masculine singular to indicate** ***bharga—dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt*****).** ***Yaḥ*** **does not refer to** ***savituḥ*****. The usage is found in** ***dhyeyaḥ sadā savitṛ-maṇḍala-madhya-vartī:*** **one should meditate on he who resides within the sun. This is logical, since the teaching is concerning** ***bharga*****, the supreme entity (not the sun).**

### Verse 8

*taj jyotir bhagavān viṣṇur jagaj-janmādi-kāraṇam \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That light is Lord Viṣṇu, the cause of creation of the universe.**

**Комментарий:**

*tathaiva tad ity asya mantra-gata-padasya vyākhyāṁ viśiṣya darśayati—taj jyotir ity ardhena bharga-pada-vācyaṁ taj-jyotir eva tat-padena pūrvam uktam ity arthaḥ \| tac ca bhagavān viṣṇur eva, tad eva ca vedāntena darśitaṁ jagaj-janmādi-kāraṇam ity arthaḥ \| mantre ca praṇavādi tad ity antasya dhīmahīty antenānvaya eva kāryaḥ \| svayaṁ praṇavārtha-rūpaṁ kāraṇāt kāryasyānanyatvād iti bhūr-ādi-rūpaṁ ca tat tattvaṁ savitur devasya vareṇyaṁ bhargo dhīmahīti \|\|8\|\|*

**The words of the gāyatrī mantra are now explained in more detail in one line.** ***Bharga*** **means** ***taj-jyotiḥ.*** **That was previously stated. This means Bhagavān Viṣṇu only. He is the cause of creation of the universe. This is indicates that Viṣṇu is indicated in the Vedānta sūtras (*****janmādy asya yataḥ*****). The meaning should be “Let us meditate on the supreme entity Viṣṇu (*****bharga*****) who is** ***oṁ*** **and** ***tat*** **since Viṣṇu is** ***oṁ*** **Because he is the cause, the only source of all effects, he is said to be the three worlds:** ***bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ*****. Let us meditate on Viṣṇu (*****bhargo*****), oṁ, who is the best (*****vareṇyam*****), to be chosen by the sun god (*****savituh devasya*****), the cause of the three worlds.**

### Verse 9

*śivaṁ kecit paṭhanti sma śakti-rūpaṁ vadanti ca \|*  
*kecit sūryaṁ kecid agniṁ daivatāny agni-hotriṇaḥ \|*  
*agny-ādi-rūpī viṣṇur hi vedādau brahma gīyate \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some say** ***bharga*** **means Śiva. Others say it is śakti. Others say it is the sun god or Agni. Those who do fire sacrifices say it is devatās. But Viṣṇu, the very form of Agni and the others, is praised as Brahman in the Vedas.**

**Комментарий:**

*athātra vipratipadyamānān svamata-sātkaroti—śivaṁ kecid iti sārdhena sphuṭam \|\|9\|\|*

**Stating the negative, the conclusion is confirmed in three lines. The meaning is clear.**

### Verse 10

*tat padaṁ paramaṁ viṣṇor devasya savituḥ smṛtam \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Bharga, the supreme entity, refers to Viṣṇu, who is the sun god.**

**Комментарий:**

*tad evam eva viṣṇu-savitroḥ kāraṇa-kāryayos tayos tādātmyenābhedam api darśayati tat padam ity ardhena \| atra viṣṇor iti viśvātmakam ity arthaḥ \| tad iti sa bharga ity arthaḥ \|\|10\|\|*

**Though Viṣṇu is the cause and the sun god is the effect, they are considered non-different by oneness of cause and effect. That is shown in this line of the text. Viṣṇu means he who is the soul of the universe. That means** ***bharga.***

### Verse 11

*dadhāter vā dhīmahīti manasā dhārayemahi \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

***Dhīmahi*** **means to fix in the mind.**

**Комментарий:**

*dhīmahīty asya dhātv-antara-prakrāntatvena tattvena tam evārthaṁ yojayati dadhāter ity ardhena spaṣṭam \|\|11\|\|*

**The word** ***dhīmahi*** **is explained by using another verbal root (*****dhṛ*** **instead of** ***dhā*****). We meditate or hold in the mind (*****dhārayemahi*****).**

### Verse 12

*no’smākaṁ yac ca bhargas tat sarveṣāṁ prāṇināṁ dhiyaḥ \|*  
*codayāt prerayet buddhīr bhoktṝṇāṁ sarva-karmasu \|*  
*dṛṣṭādṛṣṭa-vipākeṣu viṣṇuḥ sūryāgni-rūpa-bhāk \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That which is** ***bharga*** **should inspire the intellects of us, all living beings. Viṣṇu is the form of the sun god and Agni in all actions, in all seen and unseen calamities of all experiencers.**

**Комментарий:**

*atra mantra-śabdaṁ yojayati—no’smākam iti sārdhena \| atra yac ceti tad iti ca pūrva-sūtreṇa sor luko sādhitaṁ bharga ity anenaiva tad ity asya sambandhaś ca darśitaḥ \| codayāt prerayāt ity anayoḥ pūrva-siddhāntena draḍhayati—viṣṇuḥ sūryāgni-rūpa-bhāg iti \|\|12\|\|*

**The words of the mantra are joined together and explained in three lines. We should meditate on bharga which (*****yaḥ*****) should inspire (*****pracodayāt)*** **the intelligence (*****dhiyaḥ*****) of all of us (*****naḥ*****). The words** ***codayāt*** **and** ***prerayāt*** **(should inspire) confirm the previous conclusion: Viṣṇu includes within him the forms of the sun and fire (*****sūryāgni-rūpa-bhāk*****).**

### Verse 13

*īśvara-prerito gacchet svargaṁ vā śubhram eva vā \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**A person, inspired by the Lord, will go to Svarga or the pure Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

*atra hetum āha—īśvara ity ardhena \| īśvaraḥ pūrvokta-viṣṇu-rūpaḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**The cause is given.**  ***Iśvara*** **refers to the form of Viṣṇu previously mentioned.**

### Verse 14

*īśāvāsyam idaṁ sarvaṁ mahad-ādi-jagad dhariḥ \|*  
*svargādyaiḥ krīḍate devo yo haṁsaḥ puruṣaḥ prabhuḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All this, the universe starting from mahat tattva, is under the control of Viṣṇu. He is Hari, the** ***deva*** **who plays in Svarga and other places. He is also called Haṁsaḥ, a human male form, and prabhu or master.**

**Комментарий:**

*tad eva śrutvāntareṇa pramāṇayati—īśāvāsyam iti \| tasyeśasya harir iti nāmāntareṇa viṣṇutvam eva sthāpayati harir ity ardhakena svargādyair ity ardhena haṁsaḥ paramātmā tad-rūpaḥ puruṣaḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Śruti proof is given, quoting from Īśopaniṣad.** ***Īśa*** **is called** ***hari*****, to establish that this means Viṣṇu.** ***Haṁsa*** **means Paramātmā, whose form is a man or puruṣa.**

### Verse 15

*dhyānena puruṣo’yaṁ ca draṣṭavyaḥ sūrya-maṇḍale \|*  
*satyaṁ sadā-śivaṁ brahma viṣṇor yat paramaṁ padam \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This puruṣa should be seen by meditation on the sun. That abode of Viṣṇu is eternal, devoid of the three miseries and non-different from Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

*tasya vareṇyatva-parākāṣṭhāṁ darśayitum āha—dhyāneneti \| dhyānena—dhyeyaḥ sadā savitṛ-maṇḍalam-madhya-vartīty ādy uddiṣṭena \| nanv evaṁ cet tarhi īśitavyasya sūrya-maṇḍalasya nāśe tasyaiśvarya-nāśaḥ syāt, tatrāha—satyam iti \| viṣṇor yat mahā-vaikuṇṭha-lakṣaṇaṁ paramaṁ padaṁ tat satyaṁ kāla-trayāvyabhicāri \| sadā-śivaṁ tāpa-traya-rahitaṁ ca \| brahma bṛhattvāt bṛṁhaṇatvāc ca yad brahmocyate tad-rūpam evety arthaḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**To show the height of the Lord’s excellence, the verse makes a statement. The phrase “by meditation” (*****dhyānena*****) indicates the famous statement** ***dhyeyaḥ sadā savitṛ-maṇḍalam-madhya-vartī.*** **“If that is so, when the sun is destroyed the Lord’s power of lordship would also be destroyed.” (He would have nothing to control.) The next line answers that objection. The supreme abode of Viṣṇu, Vaikuṇṭha is eternal (*****satyam*****), unaffected by the three aspects of time. It is devoid of the three miseries (*****sadā-śivam*****). It is Brahman. It is called Brahman because it is the greatest and causes increase of everything else. The Lord’s abode is a form of Brahman. (It is non-different from the Lord.)**

### Verse 16

*devasya savitur devo vareṇyaṁ hi turīyakam \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord of the shining sun god is most excellent, beyond the conditioned state of the jīvas.**

**Комментарий:**

*nanu tasmin mahā-vaikuṇṭhe savitr-antaryāmiṇo’smād vilakṣaṇa eva nārāyaṇaḥ, sa ca nitya eva \| savitr-antaryāmino’sya tu kīdṛktvam ? tatrāha—devasyety ardhena \| devasya dyotamānasya savitur yo devaḥ dhyeyaḥ sadā ity ādiṣu nirdiṣṭaḥ \| so’pi vareṇyaṁ tūrīyaṁ samaṣṭi-gataṁ jāgrat-svapnādy-atītaṁ samādhy-avasthāyām eva gamyaṁ, yat padaṁ bharga-saṁjñakaṁ sa ekadhā bhavatīty ādi śruteḥ \| sarvāśraya-rūpaṁ yad vastu tad-rūpam eva \| mahā-pralaye mahā-vaikuṇṭhe eva mahā-nārāyaṇenaikībhūya sthāyitvād iti bhāvaḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**“In Mahā-vaikuṇṭha, Nārāyaṇa is different from the being within the sun. That Nārāyaṇa is eternal. What is the nature of the being dwelling in the sun?” This line answers. The Lord of the shining (*****devasya*****) sun god (*****savituḥ*****) who is the object of meditation is the highest attainment (*****vareṇyam*****), attained in the state of samādhi, beyond waking, sleeping and deep sleep (*****turīyakam*****). This refers to bharga.** ***sa ekadhā bhavati*****: he is one. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad) The entity which is the shelter of all else is this form alone. At the time of destruction the form in the sun becomes one with Mahā-nārāyaṇa in Mahā-vaikuṇṭha. Thus that form in the sun is eternal.**

### Verse 17

*yo’sāv āditya-puruṣaḥ so’sāv aham uttamam \|*  
*janānāṁ śubha-karmādīn pravartayati yaḥ sadā \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who is the person in the sun, is I, the form in the spiritual world. He eternally inspires the auspicious actions of men.**

**Комментарий:**

*atha tat-sāmyād ity artham ahaṅgrahopāsanā-rūpaṁ tripadāyā asyāś caturthasyā ajapā nāma dhyeyasyārtham āha—so’sāv iti padena spaṣṭam \|\|17\|\|*

**Because the form in the spiritual world and the form in the sun are the same. ahaṅgrahopāsanā is described. This is for meditation called ajapā (using the mantra** ***haṁsa, soham*****). The object of meditation is described. The meaning is clear.**
