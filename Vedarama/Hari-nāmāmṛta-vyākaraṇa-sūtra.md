# Hari-nāmāmṛta-vyākaraṇa-sūtra

33\. sat-saṅgāt pūrvo vāmano ’pi guruḥ

•sānusvāraś ca dīrghaś ca

visargī ca gurur bhavet

varṇa-saṁyoga-pūrvaś ca

tathā pādāntago ’pi vā•

34\. mithaḥ-saṁlagno viṣṇujanaḥ sat-saṅga-saṁjñaḥ

35\. varṇa-svarūpe rāmaḥ

36\. tad-ādi-dvaye dvayam

37\. ādeśo viriñciḥ

38\. āgamo viṣṇuḥ

39\. lopo haraḥ

•*tatrādarśana-mātra-hetur haraḥ. ātyantika-laya-hetur mahāharaḥ*•

40\. sūtrāṇi ṣaḍ-vidhāni

•saṁjñā ca paribhāṣā ca / vidhir niyama eva ca  
atideśo ’dhikāraś ca / ṣaḍ-vidhaṁ sūtra-lakṣaṇam

1\. *nāma-karaṇaṁ saṁjñā* (“a *saṁjñā-sūtra* creates a name”).  
2\. *aniyame niyama-kāriṇī paribhāṣā* (“a *paribhāṣā-sūtra* creates a regulation where there wasn’t any”).  
3\. *kartavyenopadeśo vidhiḥ* (“a *vidhi-sūtra* is an injunction that has to be followed”).  
4\. *bahutra prāptau saṅkocanaṁ niyamaḥ* (“a *niyama-sūtra* is a restriction where a rule would otherwise apply to many things”).  
5\. *anya-tulyatva-vidhānam atideśaḥ* (“an *atideśa-sūtra* ordains similarity to another”)  
6\. *uttara-prakaraṇa-vyāpy adhikāraḥ* (“an *adhikāra-sūtra* pervades the following section”).•
