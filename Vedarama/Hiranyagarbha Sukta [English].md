From Rig Veda 10.121.

# Hiranyagarbha Sukta \[English\]

## Hiranyagarbha Sukta

*hiraṇyagarbhaḥ samavartatṛgre bhūtasya jṛtaḥ patireka ṛsīt \|*  
*sa dādhāra pṛthivīṁ dyāmutemāṁ kasmai devāya haviṣā*  
*vidhema \|\| 1 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? To He that in the beginning created the golden womb, who from the beginning was the Supreme Lord of all creation and held the earth and the heavenly planets firmly. (1)**

*ya ātmadā balandā yasya viśva upāsate praśiṇaṁ yasya*  
*devāḥ \|yasya chāyāmṛtaṁ yasya mṛtyuḥ kasmai devāya haviṣā*  
*vidhema \|\| 2 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? To He who is the giver of life, strength and vigour. Whose order all the demigods wait upon. He whose protection is immortality and whose non-protection is death. (2)**

*yaḥ prāṇato nimiṇato mahitvaika idrājā jagato babhūva \|*  
*ya īśe asya dvipadaścatuṇpadaḥ kasmai devāya haviṣā*  
*vidhema \|\| 3 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? He who by His greatness is the Sole controller in creation of breath and the flickering of the eye. He who is the Lord of all bipeds and quadrupeds. (3)**

*yasyeme himavanto mahitvā yasya samudraṁ rasayā sahāhuḥ \|*  
*yasyemāḥ pradiśo yasya bāhū kasmai devāya haviṣā vidhema \|\| 4 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? He by whose might the great Himalayas stand, and by whose might this earth floats in the ocean, and whose arms are the directions. (4)**

*yena dyaurugrā pṛthivī ca dāḍhe yena suvaḥ stabhitaṁ yena*  
*nākaḥ \|yo antarikṇe rajaso vimānaḥ kasmai devāya haviṣā vidhema \|\| 5 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? By Whom the heavens have become fierce and the earth firm. By Whom the sun and the firmament were supported and Who is the measurer of the regions in between. (5)**

*yaṁ krandasī avasā tastabhāne asyaikṣetāṁ manasā rejamāne \|*  
*yatrādhi sūra uditau vyeti kasmai devāya haviṣā vidhema \|\| 6 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? He whom challenging armies have looked up to, being supported by His favour, yet trembling in their minds and depending upon He who orders the sun to rise and shine. (6)**

*āpo ha yanmahatīrviśvamāyaṁ dakṣaṁ dadhānā*  
*janayantīragnim \| tato devānāṁ niravartatāsurekaḥ kasmai devāya haviṣā vidhema \|\| 7 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? He who is the sole breath of the demigods, coming from the mighty waters when they spread everywhere, accepting Him as their germ and generating fire. (7)**

*yaścidāpo mahinā paryapaśyad dakṣaṁ dadhānā*  
*janayantīragnim \| yo deveśvadhi deva eka kasmai devāya haviṣā vidhema \|\| 8 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? He who watched while the waters held in themselves that transcendental power and generated sacrifice for the demigods and who among them is the only Supreme Lord. (8)**

*mā no himsij janitā yaḥ pṛthivyā yo vā divaṁ satyadharmā jajāna*  
*yaś cāpas candrā bṛhatīr jajāna kasmai devāya haviṣā vidhema \|\| 9 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To which Lord should we offer oblations in sacrifice? That Lord who does not injure us and is the creator of the earth and of unfailing laws, creating the heavenly planets and the great waters. (9)**

*prajāpate na tvadetānyanyo viśvā jātāni pari tā babhūva \|*  
*yatkāmaste juhumastanno astu vayaṁ syāma patayo rayīṇām \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O master of all creatures, there is no one besides You. The Lord has encompassed everything with desires we have sacrificed unto You. May those desires be fulfilled. May we become the masters of spiritual wealth. (10)**
