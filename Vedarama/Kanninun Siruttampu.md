# Kanninun Siruttampu

## 1. Kanninun

*kaṇṇinuṇ ciṟut tāmbināl kattuṇṇap paṇṇiya perumāyan ennapanil naṇṇit tenkurukūr nambi enṟakkāl aṇṇikkum amutūṟum ennāvukkē*

**Пословный перевод:**

kaṇṇi – rough surfaced (which will hurt the body when tied around); nuṇ – sūkśma (thin – that will blend into the body); ciṟu – small (that is not sufficient in length); tāmbināl – with the string/rope; kattuṇṇap paṇṇiya – one who facilitated mother yasōdā to tie him; peru māyan – one who has wonderful abilities; en appanil – leaving behind my master who is the sarvēsvaran (supreme lord); naṇṇi – approached (āḻvār) and taking shelter of him; then kurukūr nambi enṟakkāl – while reciting the name of nammāḻvār who is the leader of kurukūr (āḻvārtirunagari) which is in the south side (of bhārata dēsam); aṇṇikkum – it is very sweet; en nāvukkē – only for my tongue; amutūṟum – it will be nectarean

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: Leaving behind kriṣṇa who is my master and the supreme lord who has wonderful abilities and who facilitated mother yasōdā to tie him down with rough surfaced, thin and small string, when I recite the name of nammāḻvār who is the leader of kurukūr (āḻvārtirunagari) which is in the south side (of bhārata dēsam), it is very sweet and will be nectarean only for my tongue.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*avatārikai) pāsuram: periyavāccān piḷḷai‘s introduction: madurakavi āḻvār glorifies kaṇṇan emperumān since emperumān is most dear to nammāḻvār who is his ācārya. Though madurakavi āḻvār is fully focussed on nammāḻvār, since nammāḻvār likes perumāḷ, madurakavi āḻvār sings about perumāḷ in this pāsuram. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār‘s introduction: madurakavi āḻvār reveals the sweet nature of nammāḻvār in this pāsuram. paratvam (bhagavān‘s supremacy which is seen in srīvaikuṇṭam) is compared to milk ocean, and vibhava avatārams are compared to amruta kalasam (the nectar pot) that is found in milk ocean (which is very sweet). Even amongst the various avatārams, kriṣṇa avatāram is the sweetest. Even amongst the various leelais in kriṣṇavatāram, his butter-theft action is the sweetest. In srī rāmāvatāram, every one likes emperumān‘s virtues. But in kriṣṇavatāram, he attracts everyone through his misciefs. Being the supremely complete bhagavān and yet desiring for a material thing like butter, stealing that butter, even though being the supremely knovledgable person yet not knowing how to hide his theft and getting caught, being supremely powerful yet being punished by his mother a simple cow-herd girl and not even knowing how to protect/rescue himself, etc., are the most enjoyable incidents which mesmerizes the āḻvārs. Knowing well that this “butter stealing” incident is very dear to nammāḻvār, madurakavi āḻvār also becomes immersed in that anubhavam. கண்ணிநுண் சிறுத் தாம்பினால் கட்டுண்ணப் பண்ணிய பெரு மாயன் என்னப்பனில் நண்ணித் தென் குருகூர் நம்பி யென்றக்கால் அண்ணிக்கும் அமுதூறு மென் நாவுக்கே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) worries for the troubles kaṇṇan [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) have gone through for being tied by a string by mother yasōdā.
- This pāsuram is [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)‘s “*ettiṟam*“. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) was bewildered thinking about [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s divine pastimes of butter-stealing, etc., and fainted crying “*ettiṟam*” (tiruvāimoḻi 1.3.1) how is this possible for the supreme lord to be like a small child who is controlled by cow-herd girls, etc?). Similarly, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is also having the same emotions/ecstasy.
- For kings, winning over enemies shows their masculinity and being bound by their queens also show their masculinity. So, [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) winning over his enemies by his valour and being humble towards his devotees – both show his supremacy/masculinity only.
- First [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) spoke about kriṣṇa since that is dear to his ācārya [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). Then he speaks about [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) who is his real interest.
- When [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “this is so sweet in my tongue” – others say “it is not so for us”. āḻvār replies only some one like me who has tasted both bhagavat viśayam and bhāgavata viśayam can appreciate the sweetness of nammāḻvār.

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) was greatly attached to kriṣṇa and that too his “butter-stealing” incidents. Such most glorious incidents are given up by [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) to pursue [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s glories. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) became bewildered contemplating kriṣṇa’s pastimes. But [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is very clear about his goal of ācārya kainkaryam and does not get bogged down by bhagavat anubhavam.
- After getting caught for stealing butter, kriṣṇa stands in front of yasōdā with great fear for her punishments. He is weeping, holding his palms together begging for her mercy, etc. Simply by her love, he who is the supreme lord of everything was tied down.  
   It is this great deed of him that makes [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) say “*perumāyan*” (great person with unimaginable abilities).
- Even though [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is ācārya niśtar, he still calls [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) as “*ennappan*” (my master). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) will not accept praises for himself but will seek out for [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s glorification. So, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) glorifies [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) for the satisfaction of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) who is his ācārya. But [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) leaves kriṣṇa behind and continues on to [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). That is because if one is fully focussed in ācārya ([carama parva niśtai](http://ponnadi.blogspot.in/p/anthimopaya-nishtai.html)) they can give up [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and fully focus on the ācārya. There is nothing wrong in it. Giving up [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and pursuing ones ācārya is more difficult than giving up material desires and focussing on [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/). Since, material pleasures are temporary and insignificant, they can be easily given up. But since bhagavat viśayam is permanent and great, it is difficult to give up that and proceed on to the ultimate goal of serving ācārya/bhāgavatas.
- Just by saying [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s name without even fully focussing in the mind, I feel great sweetness in my tongue. It is this same mind/tongue which enjoyed so many material aspects but now it enjoys [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s divine names. Others may not get the same sweet experience, since they may not be as surrendered to [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) as me ([madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)).

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- Just like a great emperor when being bound by a flower garland by his dear consort will manifest his humility and love for his consort, [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) being tied by mother yasōdā, shows his humility towards her out of love.
- One who is vorśipped by everyone stands in front of yasōdā and prays to her to be spared of punishment.  When one takes shelter of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), they need to progress up to taking shelter of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). But when one has taken shelter of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), there is nothing more to do. [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/) quotes [nanjīyar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/) saying “*ācāryargaḷai nambiyennak kaṟpittār srī madurakavigaḷiṟē*” meaning “it was [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) who started calling āḻvārs as nambi”. nambi – guṇa pūrṇa – one who is filled with all auspicious qualities.
- For bhagavat viśayam – ones mind, words and deeds should be surrendered to [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/). But for bhāgavata viśayam, just surrender by words is sufficient.

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- The mortar where kriṣṇa is tied is a broken one. This implies that it is not used by or usable for anyone and can only be used to tie him there. [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) only appreciates that which exist exclusively for his use – ananyārhatvam.
- kaṇṇi (rough), nuṇ (thin/subtle) and ciṟu (small) is compared to jñānam (knowledge), bhakti and vairājñam.
- Since kriṣṇa’s butter-stealing pastimes is the ultimate joyful aspect, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) explains that first and then rejects such anubhavam to explain about [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s greatness.
- The string (since small) cannot be expanded and the mortar cannot be shrunk, so, kriṣṇa himself shrinks his stomach with great difficulty to facilitate mother yasōdā to tie him.  
   As said in “*nāyamātmā*” sruti, even when one has bhakti (devotion), unless [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) himself allows, he cannot be pursued. He says only those who know the deep meanings of tirumantram and dvayam can tie down [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) (since they will know their total dependency/surrender towards [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)). [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s glories vanquished madurakavi āḻvār’s attachment towards material desires. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s glories vanquished [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)‘s attachment towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).
- Difference between kurungudi nambi ([emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)) and kurukūr nambi ([nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)). nambi means guṇa pūrṇa – one who is complete in auspicious qualities. [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s completeness is in jñānam (knowledge), sakti (abilities), etc. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s completeness is in jñānam (knowledge about being a servant of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)/bhāgavatas), bhakti (devotion), etc. [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s qualities are such that everything is attracted towards him. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s qualities are such that even [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) himself is attracted towards [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).

## 2. Navinal

*nāvināl naviṟṟu inbam eytinēn mēvinēn avan ponnadi meymmaiyē dēvu maṟṟaṟiyēn kurukūr nambi pāvin innisai pādit tirivanē*

**Пословный перевод:**

nāvināl – with my tongue; naviṟṟu – reciting; inbam – joy; eytinēn – attained; mēvinēn – fully surrendered; avan – his; ponnadi – golden feet; meymmaiyē – truly/eternally; dēvu – God; māṟṟaṟiyēn – do not know any other; kurukūr nambi – leader of āḻvārtirunagari (tirukkurukūr) who is filled with auspicious qualities; pāvin – pāsurams’, songs’; innisai – sweet music; pādi – singing along; tiruvanē – roam around

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: I attained great joy by reciting (nammāḻvār‘s pāsurams) with my tongue. I am truly/eternally surrendered to nammāḻvār‘s golden feet. I do not know any God other than nammāḻvār who is filled with auspicious qualities and who is the leader of āḻvārtirunagari. I will roam around (spend my time) singing the sweet music of such āḻvār’s pāsurams.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that madurakavi āḻvār says nammāḻvār‘s glories can be experienced with this body (in this life) itself. nampiḷḷai highlights that madurakavi āḻvār sustains himself by reciting nammāḻvār‘s pāsurams. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that madurakavi āḻvār revealed nammāḻvār‘s pāsurams are most reliśable for him in the first pāsuram and reveals that he sustains himself with nammāḻvār‘s pāsurams in this life. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār‘s introduction: āḻvārs assumed bhagavān as their ācārya. madurakavi āḻvār assumes his ācārya (nammāḻvār) as bhagavān (supreme lord/master). yasōdā mātā tied kaṇṇan emperumān to punish him for the misciefs and continued with her daily routines (just like the ones who would perform their sandyā vandanam without fail even during adyayana utsavam). kriṣṇa did not try to relieve himself from the binding wanting the one who tied him (his mother) to free him. Similarly, nammāḻvār was mesmerized by kriṣṇānubhavam and became totally bound to emperumān being unable to free himself. But madurakavi āḻvār relished kriṣṇānubhavam which is dear to his ācārya nammāḻvār but freed himself and focussed on nammāḻvār‘s qualities, etc. When madurakavi āḻvār was asked why he gave up relishing kriṣṇānubhavam,  madurakavi āḻvār replies “When some one gets some joy in this world or other world, they still look out for other favours since they have more time to spend. But for me, both in this world and the other world, nammāḻvār is the only master and goal. So there is no need for me to engage in bhagavat viśayam directly”. நாவினால் நவிற்றின்ப மெய்தினேன் மேவி னேனவன் பொன்னடி மெய்ம்மையே தேவு மற்றறியேன் குருகூர் நம்பி பாவினின்னிசை பாடித் திரிவனே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- It is said that “*mana: pūrvō vāguttara:*” (first thoughts and then words). Even that is not needed – just words without the initiative of mind is sufficient to glorify āḻvār.
- As said in “*pratyakśē gurava: stutyā:*” (Glorify the ācārya in public and in his presence) – the purpose of the tongue is to glorify the ācārya who has done great favours.
- Like tiruvadi (hanumān) said “*bhāvō nānyatra gaccati*” (I cannot think about any one else other than srī rāma), here [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “*maṟṟaṟiyēn*“.
- As mentioned by [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) in tiruviruttam 53 “*deivattaṇṇan tuḻāyt tārāyinum taḻaiyāyinum taṇ kombatāyinum kīḻ vērāyinum ninṟa maṇṇāyinum koṇḍu vīsuminē*” (Just scatter me [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s tuḷasi garland, its string alone, the leaves alone, its stem, its root or even the soil on which it was grown – I just need something related to [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)“). Similarly,  [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says the music of āḻvār’s pāsuram itself is sufficient for him.

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- I took shelter of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s golden feet. Here the key is to understand that [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) does not consider [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s lotus feet as just upāyam (means) to get something else – but as the ultimate goal as well.
- [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) gives up aisvaryam (material wealth/pleasures), kaivalyam (enjoying one self) and [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) too. Finally he is fully focussed on [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) alone.
- Like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) himself said “*kurukūrc catakōpan*” in tiruvāimoḻi, here [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “*kurukūr nambi*“.

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- As [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) said in tiruvāimoḻi 4.5.3 “*vīvilinbam miga ellai niganḻntanan mēvi*” (I attained utmost joy by surrendering to [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)), here  [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “*inbameytinēn*” (attained bliss) – whatever [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) experienced in bhagavat viśayam, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) experiences that in [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).
- Whatever [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) experienced by dedicating his mind, words and action (*cintaiyālum collālum ceykaiyinālum* – tiruvāimoḻi 6.5.11), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) experiences the same just by words (*nāvināl naviṟṟu*).
- As [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “*meymmaiyē mēvinēn*” (eternally surrendered), āḷavandār follows in stōtra ratnam 2 “*atra paratra cāpi*” (in this and the other world).
- “*pādit tirivan*” (spend my time singing) is like “uṇḍu tirivan” (eating and roaming around). Just like some people spend their time eating and roaming around, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) spends his time singing [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s tiruvāimoḻi and preaching the same to others.

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- Why is [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) saying “*nāvināl naviṟṟu*“? īsn’t it obvious that one can recite only with tongue? nāyanār explains that “It means simply reciting with the tongue without engagement of the mind – that itself is sufficient”.
- [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “eytinēn” (past tense). In bhagavat viśayam, the bliss is attained in the future (after reaching paramapadam). But in ācārya viśayam, bliss is attained right here in this world and right now.
- ponnadi – golden feet. This is not “*ulagamaḷanta ponnadi*” (periya tirumoḻi – that which measured the three worlds and touched everyone’s head during the process) but “*traividya vrutta jana muṟtta vibhūśaṇa*”  (parānkusa aśtakam – the lotus feet that decorate the head of most knovledgable persons). Since its golden feet, it does not become contaminated even by my touch (Gold – purity).
- dēvu maṟṟaṟiyēn – [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is fully following sāstram, as taitriya upaniśad says “*ācārya dēvo bhava*” (Let the ācārya be God). A question is raised to [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) – “But why did you say [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) as ennappan (my svamy – lord)?”. [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “that is because [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is dear to [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)“. This is like sītā pirātti helping srī rāma while he was performing tiruvārādanam to periya perumāḷ. sītā pirātti did not have any separate inclination towards periya perumāḷ – her motive was to assist srī rāma only.
- meymmaiyē – satyam (truth) only. avan ponnadi meymmaiyē (bowed in front of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) truly) – would anyone lie when srī satakōpam (srī satāri – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)) is on ones head?
- kurukūr nambi – the leader of [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) who is complete in auspicious qualities. As said in tiruvāimoḻi 3.9.11 “*ēṟkum perumpugaḻ vānavar īsan kaṇṇan tanakku ēṟkum perum pugaḻ vaṇkurukūrc catakōpan*” (kaṇṇan [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/), the leader of nityasūris and muktātmās, is the most qualified person to accept glorifications; and [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), the leader of [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) is the most qualified person to glorify [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)). [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is complete with paratvam (supremacy) and saulabhyam (simplicity). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is complete with jñānam (knowledge) and bhakti (devotion).

## 3. Tiritantagilum

*tiritantāgilum dēvapirānudaik kariyakōlat tiruvuruk kāṇban nān periya vaṇ kurukūr nagar nambikku āḷuriyanāy adiyēn peṟṟa nanmaiyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

tiritantāgilum – even if I slipped (giving up nammāḻvār); dēvapirānudai – emperumān who is the master of nityasūris; kariya – blackish (like clouds); kōlam – beautiful; tiruvuru – divine form; nān kāṇban – I will see (worship); periya vaṇ kurukūr nambikku – to nammāḻvār who is born in āḻvārtirunagari that is great and magnanimous; uriya āḷ āy – being true servitor for him alone; adiyēn peṟṟa nanmai – please see the benediction I have received

**Перевод:**

**(Even if) I slipped from my ācārya niśtai, I (will) see the blackish beautiful divine form of srīman nārāyaṇan who is the master of nityasūris (eternally free souls in paramapadam). Please see the benediction I received by being true servitor for only nammāḻvār who is born in āḻvārtirunagari that is great and magnanimous.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that madurakavi āḻvār enjoys bhagavān since he is dear to nammāḻvār. nampiḷḷai ‘s introduction: madurakavi āḻvār (who is fully surrendered to nammāḻvār) pursues emperumān (in this pāsuram) since emperumān is dear to nammāḻvār. emperumān when pursued independently like sūrpaṇakā is against the nature of jīvātmā. But the same emperumān can be pursued when he himself makes him available due to one ācārya niśtai like āṇḍāḷ said in nācciyār tirumoḻi 13.10 “tangaḷ tēvarai valla parisu varuvipparēl” (If periyāḻvār‘s emperumān comes to me, I will pursue him). A village chief named kāngēyan who was the head of guṇamalaippādi (a village) was helped by piḷḷai āttān. When kāngēyan wanted to return the favours, piḷḷai āttān said “You have already helped our ācārya who often remembers your help”. From this we can understand that, since emperumān was dear to nammāḻvār, he himself revealed his divine form to madurakavi āḻvār who is a dear siśya of nammāḻvār without any individual efforts of madurakavi āḻvār. tripurā dēviyār says that if emperumānār identifies rudran as the supreme god, she would easily accept that and follow him. That was her total dedication towards her ācārya srī rāmānuja. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights (in addition to what is highlighted by nampiḷḷai) that when emperumānār had gone to tirunārāyaṇapuram, kūrattāḻvān visited srīrangam temple. At that time, the gate-keepers highlighted the order of the king that forbids anyone who is related to emperumānār from entering the temple. But one gate-keeper said kūrattāḻvān is filled with ātma-guṇams (true auspicious qualities). āḻvān at once said “If I enter the temple citing good qualities and giving up srī rāmānuja‘s relationship, these qualities are of no use” and returned without entering the temple. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār‘s introduction: madurakavi āḻvār said “dēvu maṟṟaṟiyēn” in the previous pāsuram. bhagavān himself reveals his divine form to such madurakavi āḻvār here. Because it is dear to bhagavān one engages in glorifying ones ācārya. And because it is dear to ācārya, one engages in glorifying bhagavān. As bhagavān himself says in gītā 7.17 “priyō hi jñānina: atyarttam aham sa ca ma priya:” (Those devotees are so dear to me and I am so dear to them as well). If one likes a person, he should also like those who are liked by that person. bhagavān himself reveals his divine form to madurakavi āḻvār due to madurakavi āḻvār‘s unflinching attachment towards nammāḻvār. Being pleased with that, madurakavi āḻvār realizes this divine vision is not due to his knowledge about total dependence towards bhagavān, but due to being a servitor of nammāḻvār only, not due to surrender towards bhagavān – but only due to being totally surrendered to nammāḻvār only. This is a fine example of bhagavān himself acknowledging those who are totally surrendered to their ācārya. திரிதந்தாகிலும் தேவ பிரானுடை கரிய கோலத் திருவுருக்காண்பன் நான் பெரியவண் குருகூர் நகர் நம்பிக்காள் உரியனாய் அடியேன் பெற்ற நன்மையே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- In bhagavat viśayam, cāndōjña upaniśad declares “*na ca punar āvarttatē*”  (there is no returning after reaching paramapadam). But that is the first stage in ultimate goal, and final stage is to be totally surrendered to bhāgavatas/ācārya. If ever one slips from the final stage (of ācārya kainkaryam), they will only fall to the first stage (bhagavat kainkaryam).
- [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s divine form is so beautiful that sūrpaṇakā who was punished by lakśmaṇa started glorifying srī rāma and lakśmaṇa’s divine forms when she went to complain about her plight to kara in srī rāmāyaṇam (āraṇya 19.14) “taruṇau rūpasampannau” (they are very youthful, very beautiful).
- periya vaṇ kurukūr – the great glorious [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/). Since [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) gave us [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) who was divinely blessed by [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), it is greatly glorified. The town is filled with many greatly knovledgable persons. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) himself says in tiruvāimoḻi 8.1.11 “*periya vaṇkurukūr vaṇ satakōpan*“.

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- When he slips from his ācārya niśtai, he starts singing about [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/). Just like a yōga braśta (sage who deviated from their austerity and fell down – like visvāmitra) will start speaking about the beauty of ūrvasi, etc. (damsels of svarga lōka), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) speaks about the beauty of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s divine form.
- [kūrattāḻvān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/kurathazhwan/) incident in srīrangam (that was seen in the introduction section) is explained here.
- [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)  says “*adiyēn*” (dāsan – servitor) being humbled by [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s humility.
- Since [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is dear to [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says that [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s vision “*nanmaiyē*” (very good only) even though he himself is personally more attached to serving [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) only.

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- For *tiritantāgilum* (giving up) – the route is explained. That is, tiruvāimoḻi pāsurams ([2nd pāsuram](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/?p=1309)), the music of the same ([2nd pāsuram](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/?p=1309)) and finally āḻvār himself ([1st pāsuram](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/?p=1303)) – giving up all these,  [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) pursued [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/).
- *dēvapirān* – leader of nityasūris. Even when [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) slips from his ācārya niśtai, he only falls to see paramapadanātan only and assumes the state of nityasūris.
- [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) appeared in kaliyugam during the dark ages when [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s generosity is not visible. It is in this [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) town, the most generous [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) appeared. By saying “kurukūr nagar”, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) distinguishes [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) from srīvaikuṇṭam (paramapadam). Whatever emotions [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) had towards paramapadam while saying “*ayarvaṟum amarargaḷ adipati*” (tiruvāimoḻi 1.1.1 – unquestionable leader of nityasūris), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) feels the same emotions while saying tirunagari and [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).
- *āḷuriyanāy* – By being a suitable servant – without any personal likes and dislikes and serving solely for the desires of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) and giving up attachments to those aspects which are not liked by [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- *tiritantāgilum* – nāyanār gives a different and beautiful view point – Even while roaming around without any direct attachment towards [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) (since he already said in previous pāsuram that he is roaming around singing [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s pāsurams), [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) mercifully showed his divine form to [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/).
- While all āḻvārs had great attachment towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) had great attachment towards [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) only. As said in jitantē stōtram 1.5 “*bhaktānām tvam prakāsasē*” (you manifest your divine form, ornaments, etc., to your devotees) – here [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is such nice devotee – so [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) manifested his divine form to him.
- *kariya* (blackish form) – While nityasūris enjoyed his divine form it was pale. Even when [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) enjoyed his divine form it only became golden color as said in 2.5.1 “*ennāvi sēr ammānukku sempon tiruvudambu*” (My lord’s form is in beautiful golden color). But only when [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) had the vision of his form, [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s form became blackish. Blackish color indicates [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s audāryam (most magnanimous/generous nature) like a cloud ready to shower the rain for the benefit of everyone.
- For *tiruvuru* – nāyanāra again shows a different and beautiful aspect – the form that is accompanied by “tiru” (srī mahālakśmi).
- I saw [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) since he was blackish instead of nammāḻvār’s golden form. I saw because there was tuḷasi smell instead of vakuḷa flower smell. In any case, since the form looked different than [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s, I saw [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s form with a little bit of fear in my mind.
- *periya vaṇ kurukūr* – Great glorious [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) – When compared to [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/), paramapadam is very small (in glories). As mentioned by [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) in periya tiruvantādi 75 “*puviyum iruvisumbum ninnagatta ni en seviyin vaḻi puguntu ennuḷḷāy*” (You have the entire creation within yourself, but you have entered through my ears (through sāstram) and is inside me now” – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) holds vibhūtimān (owner of everything) and the vibhuti (aisvaryam – wealth) within himself. Such [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is inside [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) – so it is a great town.
- Even [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) was greatly longing for the vision of [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s divine form. But since I am a servitor of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) himself manifests his divine form to them. nānmugan tiruvantādi 15 “*ēttiyiruppārai vellumē maṟṟavaraic cāttiyiruppār tavam*” (the greatness of devotees of devotees of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is much higher than greatness of devotees of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)) – [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) realizes this in his own personal experience.

## 4. Nanmaiyal

*nanmaiyāl mikka nānmaṟaiyāḷargaḷ punmaiyāgak karutuvar ātalil annaiyāy attanāy ennai āṇḍidum tanmaiyān satakōpan en nambiyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

nanmaiyāl mikka – filled with virtues; nāl maṟai āḷargaḷ – vaidikas who are experts in the 4 vēdams; ennai – me (this dāsa/servitor); punmai āga karutuvar ātalil – ignoring me due to me being an embodiment of lowly qualities; annai āy – being a mother; attan āy – being a father; ennai āṇḍidum tanmaiyān – having the quality of accepting me as his servitor; satakōpan – nammāḻvār; en nambi – my master

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: Seeing that the vaidikas who are experts in the 4 vēdams and who are filled with virtues ignore me due to me being an embodiment of lowly qualities, nammāḻvār like a mother and a father who is having the quality of accepting me as his servitor, is my master.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: All the introductions for this pāsuram are seen as a continuation of previous pāsuram. nanjīyar highlights that madurakavi āḻvār observes nammāḻvār‘s favours to him for accepting him even after most saintly persons ignored him. When asked what was his action that triggered nammāḻvār to accept him, madurakavi āḻvār says, he had such lowly qualities that even most saintly persons will give up on him. nampiḷḷai  highlights that madurakavi āḻvār reveals his state before being accepted by nammāḻvār. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that madurakavi āḻvār first rejected bhagavān (in first pāsuram) but then showed attachment to bhagavān (in third pāsuram), since bhagavān is most dear to nammāḻvār. He says “If I think about nammāḻvār‘s favours to me, I should like whatever he likes” and he speaks about his lowly qualities and how nammāḻvār accepted him. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār highlights that madurakavi āḻvār reveals his heart and shows his attachment towards nammāḻvār in the previous pāsurams. Seeing that, some ask, riśis (sages) and āḻvārs have unanimously declared srīman nārāyaṇan as mother and father of all of universe. You are also part of  that same universe. So, why are you surrendering to nammāḻvār instead of bhagavān as done by these great sages and āḻvārs? madurakavi āḻvār replies “Due to my extremely lowly nature, even those saintly people have given up on me. Only nammāḻvār uplifted me seeing that lowly nature itself as a qualification. So, his completeness in giving mercy to others is to be glorified”. நன்மையால் மிக்க நான்மறையாளர்கள் புன்மை யாகக் கருது வராதலின் அன்னை யாயத்தனா யென்னை யாண்டிடும் தன்மையான் சடகோபனென் நம்பியே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- “*nanmaiyāl mikka nānmaṟaiyāḷargaḷ*” means ones who are *paradukka asahisṇu* (one who cannot bear others’ sufferings), *parasamrutti ēka prayōjanar* (others’ well-being is their only goal) and ones who have very pure mind/heart. Ones like [kūrattāḻvān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/kurathazhwan/), āṇḍāḷ ([kūrattāḻvān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/kurathazhwan/)‘s darmapatni), [srīmahālakśmi](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/), etc.
- “*annaiyāy attanāy*” – being all types of relation such as mātā, pitā, ācārya, etc. mātā (Mother) is generally focussed on priyam (that which is pleasing to the child), pitā (Father) is generally focussed on hitam (well-being – that which is ideal for the child) and ācārya is focussed fully on the hitam (well-being) of the [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/).
- “*en nambi*” (my master) – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is so complete that, not only he has the ability to purify himself, he can also purify me.

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- Except for [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), no one else will accept me – I have so much defects. I am an embodiment of all lowly qualities.
- When kākāsura who offended [sītā pirātti](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/), [srī rāma](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) launched a straw as a weapon to kill him. kākāsura tried to get help from all 3 worlds and eventually failed and finally surrendered to srī rāma who could only give him refuge forgiving his cruel act. Similarly, for the amount of blemishes I have, only [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) could accept me and reform me.
- Whatever [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) observed in [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) in tiruvāimoḻi 2.3.2 “*tāyāyt tantaiyāy aṟiyādana aṟivitta attā*” (being a mother, being a father and enlightening me with those aspects that are not known to me), madurakavi āḻvār observes the same in [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).
- “*tanmaiyān*” – just like fire is hot and water is cold naturally, it is [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s natural quality to uplift even the most fallen persons.
- Whatever done by mother, father will not do. Whatever done by father, mother will not do. Others cannot do either (mother’s task or father’s task). But [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is so complete that he fulfills all these responsibilities. Some how I tried to compare him with saintly persons – it is my mistake to try and compare him with any one since no one is as complete as [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- This vyākhyānam is mostly based on [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam.
- Experts in vēdams means ones who have understood the essence of the meanings of vēdam, i.e., becoming *ānrusamsya pradānar* (Being benevolent/compassionate towards others) – uplifting them from this miserable samsāram.
- [satakōpa](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) – one who removed my wickedness/ignorance. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is known as [satakōpa](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) since he angrily drove out the ignorance from his own mind during his birth. Now, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) uses that name of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) remembering [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s favour of driving out the ignorance from his mind as well. Up to 3rd pāsuram, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) used “*kurukūr nambi”* and in this pāsuram he uses [satakōpa](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) due to the fact that [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) dispelling his ignorance.

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- As heard from [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) who identified devotees as “*nalattāl mikkār*” (ones who are filled with good qualities) in tiruvāimoḻi 5.8.3, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “*nanmaiyāl mikka*“. They are not just filled with kindness, they are also devoid of any hatred towards any one. They are so kind hearted that even towards the ones who hurt them, they are merciful. sītā pirātti defended the rākśasis (rāvaṇa’s demoniac female assistants who troubled her so much) and advised hanuman to show mercy towards them. [kūrattāḻvān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/kurathazhwan/) requested emperumān to bless nālūrān (his own siśya who caused the loss of his eyes) with the same benediction as he himself would get. prahlāḻdāḻvān too was compassionate towards the ones who committed great offenses towards him.
- “*nanmaiyāl mikka nānmaṟaiyāḷargaḷ*” – Their most compassionate quality is learnt/acquired from vēdam. “*mātā pitā sahasrēbhyō vatsalataram sāstram*” (sāstram carries thousand times more care than mother/father will manifest). One who has not studied sāstram will not understand true compassion (based on ātmā).
- [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is more worried for such great persons giving up on him – even more than being filled with blemishes only.
- [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) accepts such [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) who is given up by every one. srīvaikuṇṭa stavam 2nd slōkam “*yadvā saraṇyam asaraṇya janasya puṇyam*” (That sacred one which is the refuge for the ones who have no other refuge).
- “*ennai āṇḍidum tanmaiyān*” – Engaged me in scribing tiruvāimoḻi while [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) dictated the same – this is the most apt/desirable kainkaryam for me.
- “*ennai āṇḍidum*” – [vadakkut tiruvīdip piḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/17/vadakku-thiruveedhi-pillai-english/) ([nāyanār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)‘s father) says “[nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) advised and engaged me in vorśipping [polindu ninṟa pirān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) ([āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) utsavar) also – Even though I skipped [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) and vorśipped [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) ensured that I also vorśipped [polindu ninṟa pirān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) as that is fitting for the [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)‘s nature”.
- “*satakōpan*” – because he drove out my “satam” (ignorance), he became to be known as “[satakōpan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)“.

## 5. Nambinen

*nambinēn piṟar nanporuḷ tannaiyum nambinēn madavāraiyum munnelām sempon mādat tirurkkurukūr nambikku anbanāy adiyēn satirttēn inṟē*

**Пословный перевод:**

adiyēn – I (who have become reformed to call myself dāsan/servitor); mun elām – previously (before being blessed by nammāḻvār); piṟar – others; nal poruḷ tannaiyum – good properties/objects; nambinēn – desired (for them); (piṟar) madavāraiyum – women (who are married to others); nambinēn – desired; inṟu – But today; sempon mādam – (having) beautiful balconies built using gold; tiru kurukūr nambikku – leader of āḻvārtirunagari; anban āy – become a devotee; sadirttēn – attained greatness

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: I previously desired for good properties/objects of others and other women. But today (after being blessed by nammāḻvār), I have become a devotee of nammāḻvār who is the leader āḻvārtirunagari which has many beautiful palaces that have balconies that were built using gold and have attained greatness (by becoming nammāḻvār‘s disciple).**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: All the introductions for this pāsuram are seen as a continuation of previous pāsuram. nanjīyar highlights that when madurakavi āḻvār was asked what lowly qualities he was talking about in previous pāsuram, he says he was desiring for others’ wives and others’ properties. But he became reformed by the unconditional grace of nammāḻvār. He is expressing his gratitude towards nammāḻvār who reformed him out of his causeless mercy. nampiḷḷai  highlights that when asked about it madurakavi āḻvār reveals the details of his previous state and how nammāḻvār reformed him (based on previous pāsuram where he said he was filled with lowly qualities). periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that the same point as nampiḷḷai . aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār highlights that madurakavi āḻvār was asked – What were your lowly qualities highlighted in the previous pāsuram? Who triggered your devotion towards nammāḻvār? madurakavi āḻvār replies “Since time immemorial I was having great ego and attachment towards wealth and lust. That too mainly towards lust. That too I wanted to enjoy many women. For that I needed lots of wealth through which I can attract women towards me. For that I went to different towns and villages. At that time, I saw the huge palaces in āḻvārtirunagari which had golden balconies. Wanting to get some wealth from this rich place, I entered the town. At that time, nammāḻvār blessed me and reformed me. I became surrendered to him and won over my tendency towards sense enjoyment. This is my current state”. நம்பினேன்பிறர் நன் பொருள் தன்னையும் நம்பினேன் மடவாரையும் முன்னெல்லாம் செம்பொன் மாடத் திருக்குருகூர் நம்பிக் கன்பனாய் அடியேஞ் சதிர்த்தேனின்றே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- *piṟar nan poruḷ* – As said in “*cōrēṇa ātmābahāṟiṇā*” (A thief who stole the [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/), i.e.,  considered [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) as ones own property while it is owned by [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)), I looked to rule over myself independently while [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) is owned by [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).
- Since “*madavār*” (women – plural) is used, it means, I am not satisfied with one woman but keep looking out for many for sense enjoyment.
- I am the one who was attached to women before. But the same me, have become attached to [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) – this is my greatness (such huge transformation).

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- *poruḷ* means wealth. Here it means [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/). āmongst dravyams (objects), [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) is highlighted primarily.  As [tirumangai āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/23/thirumangai-azhwar/) said in periya tirumoḻi 1.1.5 “*kaḷvanēnānēn*” (became a thief) and [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) says in tiruvāimoḻi 5.1.4 “*vankaḷvan*” (great thief), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) also says the same thing. Just like [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) who is a servitor of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) first and ultimately to [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) (ācārya) was stolen, only when madurakavi āḻvār becomes a servitor of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), it will become exclusively used for [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/),
- *piṟar* – [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) is represented by kaustuba jewel on [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s chest. The most cruel nature of theft is explained. The atonement/punishment for a theft is based on the object that is stolen and the owner of the object. Stolen object ātmā is like kaustubam and the owner is [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) himself – so atonement is very difficult and punishment is severe.
- If one is bold enough to steal [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s property “ātmā”, what to speak about stealing of common people’s wives – I have also been engaged in that.
- *nambi* – complete in qualities. Even after being so complete in grace/mercy, would [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) consider my lowly qualities and discard me? No (He has surely accepted me).
- Before being a disciple of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), I was very lowly/fallen. After being blessed by [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), I have become so great. ācāryas are called “nambi” and oneself being called “adiyēn” in front of them is due to [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)‘s usage of these terms in his pāsurams and action.

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- This vyākhyānam is mostly based on [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam.
- *nanporuḷ* means [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/). piṟar (other) means [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/). He is called “Other” because he is totally different from ātmās – nityasūris (eternally free souls), muktātmās (liberated souls) and baddātmās (bound souls). In gītā slōkams 15.16, 15.17, and 15.18, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is explained as “*uttama: puruśa*” (the supreme person) and he is differentiated from different types of [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)s.
- *munnelām* – forever until now. Just like [īsvara](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) is also eternal. kālam (time) is also beginning-less. [acit](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/09/thathva-thrayam-achith-what-is-matter/) sambandam (our existence in this material world) is also beginning-less (it is not that we have suddenly jumped into samsāram – material world from some where else). Thus until now, all the time, we have been engaged in such lowly activities.
- I was searching for wealth for my sense enjoyment. I reached [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) which looked very rich. But I was caught by the mānidi (great wealth) here. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)‘s vaittamānidi (emperumān at tirukkōḷūr – vaittamānidi means great wealth that is preserved).  Since [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) is as enjoyable for [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s lotus feet, he is glorifying the town here.
- As said by iḷaiya perumāḷ (lakśmaṇan) in srī rāmāyaṇam (kiśkintā kāṇḍam 4.12) “*guṇair dāsyam upāgata:*” (I am surrendered to srī rāma for his auspicious qualities), here [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “*adiyēn*” for being surrendered to him seeing [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s auspicious qualities.

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- He has so many lowly qualities and those cannot be easily listed. So, as a sample he identifies “paradravya apahāram” (stealing others’ properties) and “paradāra apahāram” (seeking out others’ wives) which are the most cruel ones. [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) apahāram means considering [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) as independent (instead of knowing [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) is totally dependent on [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)).
- *nanporuḷ* – vilakśaṇa dravyam – unique object. [acit](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/09/thathva-thrayam-achith-what-is-matter/) (Matter) is anityam (temporary), anavarata vikārāspadam (abode for change always), atayanta hēyam (very unfavourable), ajñānam (ignorance), asukam (lack of bliss), abhōjñam (lack of enjoyment). [cit (jīvātmā – soul)](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) is nityam (permanent), nirvikāram (uncangable), atyanta vilakśaṇam (very unique/different from matter), svayam prakāsamāy (self-effulgent), sukamāy (blissful), svabhāvatō bhōjñam (naturally enjoyable).
- *piṟar* – anya – one who is amongst others, yet not mingling with others. Though [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is all pervading and present along with [cit (jīvātmās)](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) and [acit](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/09/thathva-thrayam-achith-what-is-matter/) (matter), he is not touched by the defects of any of those he is present with. Even though is the antaryāmi (indvelling super-soul) of every one, he is called apahatapāpmā (being relieved of all blemishes). All of these principles are heard by [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) from [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) in his various pāsurams.
- ātmāpahāram (stealing of [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)) means svātantriyam (independence). Seeking out for sense enjoyment means dēhātmābimānam (considering body as self). It is because of this misunderstanding that [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) (soul) is dēham (body), one is seeking enjoyment for the body.
- Though I went to [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) seeking for wealth and lust, since [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) who lives in [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) is aśtākśara samsidda (one who is well situated in tirumantram) and in whose proximity there is no theft, etc., I developed an attachment towards him just like kśatrabandu became reformed in the proximity of a saint. I approached him and became very much devoted towards him.
- Like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) had attachment towards the palaces/balconies of [divya dēsams](https://koyil.org/) as mentioned in tiruvāimoḻi 6.5.1 “*tuvaḷil maṇi mādam ōngu tulaivillimangalam*“,  [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is attached to “*sempon mādam*” of [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/).
- In the previous pāsuram “*annaiyāy attanāy*” explains “*mātā pitā*” (mother and father). Here “*madavāṟ … sempon mādam*” explains “*yuvataya*:” (women) and “*vibhūti*:” (wealth). This is in reference to “*mātā pitā*” [taniyan](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/?page_id=11) for [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) that was rendered by [āḷavandār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/01/alavandhar/).
- inṟē – right now. Instead of having to experience the remaining karmā, give up the body, reach virajā and finally become pure, I have become pure right here (by [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s grace). Previously, I was accompanied by [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) (as antaryāmi) but still was in samsāram – now in the presence of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) I have become relieved from the miseries of samsāram.

adiyen saraty ramanuja dasan

## 6. Inru tottum

*inṟu tottum eḻumaiyum empirān ninṟu tan pugaḻ ētta aruḷinān kunṟamādat tirukkurukūr nambi enṟum ennai igaḻvilan kāṇminē*

**Пословный перевод:**

inṟu tottum – from today; eḻumaiyum – all of the future; ninṟu – being faithful with full conviction; tanpugaḻ – nammāḻvār‘s greatness; ētta – to glorify; em pirān aruḷinān – my svamy (master) nammāḻvār blessed; kunṟam mādam – balconies which look tall like mountains; tirukkurukūr nambi – leader of āḻvārtirunagari; enṟum – forever; ennai – in my case; igaḻvu ilan – would not ignore or give up on me; kāṇmin – you see for yourself

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: My lord and master (nammāḻvār) who is the leader of āḻvārtirunagari blessed me to be faithful with full conviction and glorify his greatness from today, eternally forever. He would not give up on me ever and you can see this for yourself.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that when madurakavi āḻvār was asked “How do you claim to be contented/accomplished?”, he replies nammāḻvār has blessed me to be so along the lines of nammāḻvār himself saying in tiruvāimoḻi 4.5.3 “vīvil kālam isai mālaigaḷ ētti mēvap peṟṟēn” (I am blessed to sing the glories of bhagavān forever from now). nampiḷḷai  highlights that when madurakavi āḻvār was asked “You were attached to worldly desires since time immemorial. Would you return to your old ways?” and he replies “Is nammāḻvār‘s grace so insignificant that it would let me return to my old ways?”. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights the same principle as nampiḷḷai. Moreover he says, nammāḻvār‘s grace is different from bhagavān‘s grace. bhagavān‘s grace is such that there is a possibility of falling down from bhakti niśtai as bhagavān himself mentions in gītā 6.41 “sucīnām srīmatām gēhē yōgabraśtō(a)bijāyatē” (One who falls from his yōgam/faith becomes born in the families of pure and virtuous families (to continue his faith from where he left)). In viṣṇu purāṇam 2.13.29 “samādi bangas tasyāsīt“, it is highlighted that the saintly bharata mahārāja (king) lost his focus on bhagavān due to attachment towards a deer even though he was completely detached from his kingdom, wealth, family, etc. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār highlights that madurakavi āḻvār explains his lowly nature, nammāḻvār accepting him due to that aspect alone, his defects being removed due to nammāḻvār‘s grace and finally him developing attachment towards nammāḻvār‘s lotus feet in the previous pāsuram. Some asked madurakavi āḻvār – even though your worldly attachments are removed, due to being present in this material world, would you not return to your old ways? madurakavi āḻvār replies “I did not get rid of my attachments by performing austerities. They were removed by nammāḻvār‘s acceptance of me. Since nammāḻvār‘s acceptance of me would never change, my material attachments would not return to me”. In the previous pāsuram, madurakavi āḻvār revealed his jñānam and bhakti towards nammāḻvār‘s lotus feet. In this pāsuram, he reveals his strong conviction towards nammāḻvār‘s lotus feet. இன்று தொட்டு மெழுமையு மெம்பிரான் நின்று தன் புகழேத்த வருளினான் குன்ற மாடத் திருக்குருகூர் நம்பி என்று மென்னையிகழ்விலன் காண்மினே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- *inṟu* – from today – from the day when I developed taste towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s devotee ([nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)) instead of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).
- *ninṟu* – with full conviction. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) says “*mānidam pāda vanta kaviyēnallēn*” (I am not a simple poet who would sing the glories of mortal humans) in tiruvāimoḻi 3.9.9. But [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says He would not sing the glories of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) but only [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s glories.
- *kunṟa mādat tirukkurukūr nambi* – just like we cannot fully understand [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s completeness, we cannot see the full height of the balconies/palaces in [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/).

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- *inṟu tottum* – from today itself. It is not something which will happen in the future. It starts from the day when [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) accepts [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) as his disciple/devotee.
- *eḻumaiyum* – seven births – upalakśaṇam (sample) – implies forever in the future
- *empirān* – pirān – upakāraka (one who gives favours). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) identifies [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) as “*pirān perunilam kīṇḍavan*” (the one who gave great favour by rescuing mother earth) in tiruvāimoḻi 1.7.6. [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) identifies [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) as the one who did great favours to him by rescuing him from samsāram.
- “*kunṟa mādat tirukkurukūr*” – [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)‘s heart is firmly placed in [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s words – in tiruvāimoḻi 4.10.1 [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) says “*kunṟam pōl maṇi māda nīdu tirukkurukūr*” (same meaning) – so [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) repeats the same here.
- “*enṟuminnai igaḻivilan kāṇminē*” – If [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) can accept me with all my defects, why would he let me return to my old ways?

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- This vyākhyānam is mostly based on [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam.
- *aruḷinān* – Like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) says in tiruvāimoḻi 1.1.1 “*mayarvaṟa madinalam aruḷinan*” ([bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) blessed me with blemiśless knowledge/devotion), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) blessed him with blemiśless knowledge/devotion.
- *kāṇiminē* – you see for yourself. This is pratyakśam (perceivable by our own senses) – can be easily perceived. Do I have to even explicitly say this?

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- *eḻumaiyum* – forever. Even when [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) goes to parampadam, [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is his master. Like srī satrugnāḻvān who followed srī bharatāḻvān (when he went to his uncle’s place), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is also nitya satrugna: (one who is always victorious against even the biggest enemies). Please read [http://ponnadi.blogspot.in/2013/12/virodi-pariharangal-1.html](http://ponnadi.blogspot.in/2013/12/virodhi-pariharangal-1.html) (point 9) to see how bhagavat kainkaryam is a hurdle for bhāgavata kainkaryam.
- *empirān* – [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is repeating the words heard from [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) in tiruvāimoḻi 1.10.3 “*empirānai entai tantai tantaikkum tampirānai*“. pirān means proprietor/owner. ēmphasising that he will be the property of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) only – not partially/sometimes for bhagavān and partially/sometimes for [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).
- *empirān –* one who has done great favours. He has done the favour of keeping me as his own servant instead of sending me away to serve [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).
- *ētta* – to glorify. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) started periya tirvuantādi saying that he starts trying to praise [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) but in the last pāsuram he still says “*moykaḻalē ētta muyal*” (try to glorify [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s lotus feet). But he blessed me so I can praise him in every pāsuram of this prabandam.
- *than pugaḻētta aruḷinān* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s aruḷ (mercy) is his upāyam (means) and singing the glories of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is his puruśārttam (ultimate goal).
- *ninṟu than pugaḻētta* – steadily glorifying. In srī rāmāyaṇam, bāla kāṇḍam 1.7, nārada riśi says “*munē! vakśyāmyaham buddvā*” (oh saint (vālmīki riśi)! Let me explain clear/steady mind about srī rāma’s auspicious qualities). nārada riśi went into a trance meditating on srī rāma’s qualities, but before speaking about the same, he first steadied himself. Similarly, here [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) blessed him so that he can speak about [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s glories steadily without going into a trance.
- *kāṇminē* – Not just pratyakśam, even by going sāstram, you can understand that [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) will never give up on me as mentioned in nīdi satakam 72 “*prāptam uttama guṇā na parityajanti*” (one who is full in qualities will never give up the task they have taken up even when faced with many hurdles) and in periya tirumoḻi 8.2.2 “*pāṇanār tiṇṇam*” (the messenger (of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)) will certainly ensure the delivery of the ones who desires for [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)).

adiyen saraty ramanuja dasan

## 7. Kandu kondu

*kaṇḍu koṇḍu ennai kārimāṟappirān paṇḍai valvinai pāṟṟi aruḷinān eṇḍisaiyum aṟiya iyambugēn oṇḍamiḻc catakōpan aruḷaiyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

pirān – being parama upakāraka (one who does great favours); kāri māṟan – auspicious son of poṟkāri; ennai – me (who do not understand his glories); kaṇḍu – blessed by his divine glance/vision; koṇḍu – engaged me in his service; paṇḍai valvinai – strong sins which were following me since time immemorial; pāṟṟi aruḷinān – mercifully drove them away; (ātalāl) oṇḍamiḻ satakōpan aruḷaiyē – (thus) mercy of nammāḻvār who is the abode (source) of beautiful tamiḻ pāsurams (poems); eṇ disaiyum – to the people of eight directions (everywhere); aṟiya – to make them know; iyambukēn – will speak

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: nammāḻvār, the auspicious son of poṟkāri, being the one who has done great favours to me having glanced me with his divine blessings and engaged me in his service. He drove away my eternal sins that have been following me since time immemorial. I will speak the glories of the mercy of nammāḻvār who is the abode (source) of beautiful tamiḻ pāsurams (poems), to the people of eight (all) directions and make them realize (his glories).**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that madurakavi āḻvār reveals his desire to spread the glories of nammāḻvār who dispelled all his hurdles (sins) out of his unconditional grace. nampiḷḷai  highlights that madurakavi āḻvār being blessed by the unconditional grace nammāḻvār , wants to speak the glories of nammāḻvār to everyone who are suffering in this material world. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that madurakavi āḻvār being blessed by the unconditional grace nammāḻvār , wonders why all the people of this world who are eligible to get that grace are not getting lifted. He realizes it is due to the lack of awareness about nammāḻvār‘s glories and reveals that he will speak about the glories so everyone becomes aware of it. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār‘s introduction In the 4th pāsuram, madurakavi āḻvār explained that though great sages rejected him, nammāḻvār blessed him with his grace as a mother/father. In the 5th pāsuram, he reveals his defects. In the previous pāsuram, he says that nammāḻvār will never give up on him going forward and has blessed that he will sing nammāḻvār‘s glories forever. In this pāsuram, madurakavi āḻvār says that nammāḻvār also mercifully removed all the hurdles (sins) in him which have been there since time immemorial. Thus, he says, he will spread nammāḻvār‘s glories everywhere. madurakavi āḻvār practically demonstrates the injunction in sāstram “gurum prakāsayēnnityam” (always spread the glories of guru/ācārya). nammāḻvār had full understanding in bhagavān‘s supremacy, dēvatāntarams not having any supremacy, bhāgavata sēśatvam (being devoted to devotees of bhagavān), having no attachment in worldly desires, etc. Yet, he looked at the sufferings of samsāris (worldly people) and out of great mercy towards them, he instructed them about the true knowledge starting from tiruvāimoḻi 1.2 “vīdu min muṟṟavum” upto tiruvāimoḻi 10.5 “kaṇṇan kaḻaliṇai” padigams. Even though bhagavān is filled with unsurpassed mercy, since he is supremely independent, he may punish the ones who approach him seeing their defects. But nammāḻvār will never do that. This aspect is discussed by madurakavi āḻvār in this pāsuram. கண்டு கொண்டென்னைக் காரி மாறப்பிரான் பண்டை வல்வினை பாற்றி யருளினான் எண்டிசையு மறிய இயம்புகேன் ஒண்டமிழ்ச் சடகோபனருளையே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- Like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) said in tiruvāimoḻi 8.7.2 “*iruntān kaṇḍu koṇḍu*” ([emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) stayed in my heart looking at me with great love), here [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) says “[nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) stayed there mercifully glancing at him”.
- *paṇḍai valvinai* – time immemorial – the origins of these strong sins not traceable. Since there is an end for the [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) in this samsāram (as there is an opportunity to reach paramapadam), it might be inferred as having a start in samsāram (but since the existence of [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) in this samsāram is said as anādi in sāstram, there is no beginning for these sins). These sins are neither atonable nor exhaustible by experiencing the fruits of the same (meaning it will take forever). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) removed all those sins. He even removed the attachment towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) since that is a hurdle for bhāgavata kainkaryam (which is the ultimate goal).
- *eṇḍisaiyum aṟiya iyambukēn* – I will speak this to both knowledgeable and ignorant people.
- *oṇḍamiḻ* – Instead of being samskrutam/vēdam as said in “*yatō vācō nivartantē*” (words/mind could not understand), this is easily understandable tamiḻ (drāvida vēdam).
- *“satakōpan aruḷaiyē*” – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s aruḷ (mercy) even captured the ones (me) who missed out [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s aruḷ (mercy).

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- *kārimāṟap pirān* – Out of gratitude, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) calls [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) as *pirān* (one who does great favours). Specifically says “*kārimāṟap pirān”* to distinguish from [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) who is normally glorified as *pirān*.
- *paṇḍai valvinai* – Many ancient hurdles such as attachment towards material desires, approaching [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) in improper ways like sūrpaṇakā, considering kainkaryam to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) as the ultimate goal, etc.
- *pāṟṟi aruḷinān* – dispelled them completely as mentioned in sātyāyana sākai “*dviśanta: pāpakrutyām*” (fruits of sins/vices are distributed to ones enemies while going to paramapadam).
- e*ndisaiyum aṟiya iyambukēn* – Preaching to all who have the desire towards this matter. Like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) said in tiruvāimoḻi 5.2.2 “*toṇḍīr ellārum vārīr”* (all devotees are welcome), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) also invites everyone following the footsteps of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- This vyākhyānam is mostly based on [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam.
- *kaṇḍu koṇḍu* – On seeing me [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) was so happy like finding a treasure as I am the most suitable recipient (filled with inauspiciousness) for his mercy (which is great).
- *ennai* – one who was so ignorant and did not even know the difference between [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) and other samsāris (materialistic people). Me who would not even comprehend [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s unconditional grace.
- *kāṟimārap pirān* – [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) himself says in gītā “*suhrudam sarva bhūtānām*” (I am the well-wisher of everyone) – but [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s favours are even greater.
- *oṇḍamiḻc catakōpan aruḷaiyē* – oḷḷiya tamiḻ – beautiful tamiḻ. His aruḷ (mercy) towards me is greater than him singing tiruvāimoḻi which was the refuge of great devotees.

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s auspicious qualities are highlighted in the beginning of this pāsuram’s commentary. jñānam (knowledge), bhakti (devotion), virakti (detachment) and krupai (mercy) are some of his main qualities. jñānam is focussed on [tatva trayam](https://vedarama.com/en/books/kanninun-chiruthambu/%20http://ponnadi.blogspot.com/p/thathva-thrayam.html) ([cit](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/), [acit](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), [īsvara](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/09/thathva-thrayam-achith-what-is-matter/)) – i.e., he is fully knovledgable in the three entities. bhakti is focussed on īsvara, i.e., being devoted to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) only. virakti is focussed on [cit](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) and [acit](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), i.e., being detached from self enjoyment and material pleasures. But beyond all of these he is filled with krupai towards others. His mercy is even greater than [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s and that is explained in this pāsuram.
- *kaṇḍu* – Like it is said in mahābhāratam sānti parvam 358.73 “*jāyamānam hi puruśam yam pascēt madusūdana: …*” (by the divine glance of madusūdanan emperumān, the ātmā is blessed in the womb), [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) blessed me with his divine glance.
- *koṇḍu* – Seeing my defects, still he accepted me.
- *ennaik kaṇḍu, ennaik koṇḍu* – He bestowed his vision on me (who should not be seen due to my defects), accepted me (who was given up by everyone). Due to his acceptance, even [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) has to accept me.
- *kārimāṟap pirān* – he is not a pirān like [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) who is svayambu (without any origin), he is the son of kāri (who comes in a very nice family which is devoted to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)).
- *pāṟṟi* – changed/dispelled my hurdles. Unlike [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) who said in gītā 18.66 “*māmēkam saraṇam vraja … mōkśayiśyāmi*” (surrender unto me, I will remove your hurdles), he already removed my hurdles without me surrendering unto him.
- *endisaiyum…* –  I will preach his glories to people who are considering themselves as independent and to those who are considering dēvatāntarams will protect them.
- *aṟiya iyambukēn* – will speak so they know – as [periyāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/20/periyazhwar/) said in tiruppallāṇḍu 4 “[*nādu nagaramum nangaṟiya*](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2015/06/thiruppallandu-4-edunilam/)” (to make people of towns and cities realize). I will speak about [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s mercy, how it materialized in my case, how my hurdles were dispelled and how I am blessed with singing the glories of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).
- srī jāmbavān glorified [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) after he completing trivikrama avatāram in “*dēvā: svastānamāyānti daityā: sarvē hatā gatā: \| na bhayam vidyatē kincit jitam bhagavatā jagat \|\|*” (dēvas are returning to their places. asuras are killed. there is no more fear. the world is won over by [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)). Here [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) glorifies [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). There bhagavān’s strength/power is glorified. Here [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s compassion is glorified. There prayōjanāntaraparars (dēvas – ones who are looking personal favours) benefited. Here ananyaprayōjanars (prapannas – ones who are looking to serve [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)/bhāgavatas) benefited. There [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s mischief is revealed. Here [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s (pure) nature is revealed.

## 8. Arul kondadum

*aruḷ koṇḍādum adiyavar inbuṟa aruḷinān avvarumaṟaiyin poruḷ aruḷ koṇḍu āyiram in tamiḻppādinān aruḷ kaṇḍīr ivvulaginil mikkatē*

**Пословный перевод:**

aruḷ koṇḍādum – glorifying bhagavān‘s mercy; adiyavar – bhaktas – devotees; inbuṟa – to relish; a aru maṟaiyin poruḷ – essence of that rare (difficult to acquire/learn) vēdam; aruḷinān – he blessed; aruḷ koṇḍu – with great mercy; in tamiḻ – in beautiful tamiḻ language; āyiram – tiruvāimoḻi 1000 pāsurams; pādinān – compiled/sung; aruḷ kaṇḍīr – it is purely his mercy only; i ulaginil – in this world; mikkatu – is great (greater than anything else)

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: nammāḻvār revealed the essence of vēdam for the pleasure of the devotees who glorify bhagavān. nammāḻvār, out of great mercy, compiled the 1000 pāsurams of tiruvāimoḻi. This mercy of nammāḻvār is very great in this world.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that madurakavi āḻvār glorifies that nammāḻvār‘s mercy of revealing tiruvāimoḻi is more valuable for this universe than bhagavān‘s mercy of revealing vēdam. nampiḷḷai  and periyavāccān piḷḷai highlight that madurakavi āḻvār says “I will speak the glories of nammāḻvār” since the glories of nammāḻvār‘s mercy is greater than all the three tatvams (entities) namely cit, acit and īsvara. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār‘s introduction Hearing madurakavi āḻvār glorifying nammāḻvār‘s mercy, some ask “While everyone is glorifying bhagavān‘s mercy, why are you glorifying āḻvār’s mercy?”, madurakavi āḻvār replies “bhagavān‘s mercy is useful to attain the ultimate goal. But that ultimate goal is attained by first having jñānam (knowledge). That knowledge is given by nammāḻvār. That is why nammāḻvār‘s mercy is greater than everything else”. prāpti (attainment of goal) is dependent on jñānam (knowledge). But jñānam is not dependent on the goal (i.e., knowledge does not occur after attaining the goal – it happens before hand). In the 4th pāsuram, madurakavi āḻvār expressed that he has many defects. In the 5th pāsuram, he explained his defects to be of two types (considering ātmā as independent, and being attached to worldly pleasures). In the 7th pāsuram, he mentioned that nammāḻvār removed all his sins/hurdles. In this pāsuram, he explains how nammāḻvār dispelled his sins/hurdles. Considering body as soul (not knowing difference between soul/body), and having attachment towards worldly pleasures (which is not fitting for the soul) are the hurdles that are removed through true knowledge about ātmā, bhagavān and their relationship (this is called adyātma jñānam/vidyā). This knowledge will stop the accumulation of karmā. The already accumulated karmā will be removed by bhagavān. ācāryan will remove the desires for more karmā. bhagavān will remove existing karmā. bhagavān removes karmā due to ācāryan giving the true knowledge. அருள் கொண்டாடு மடியவரின் புற அருளினானவ்வருமறையின் பொருள் அருள் கொண்டாயிரமின் தமிழ் பாடினான் அருள் கண்டீரிவ்வுலகினில் மிக்கதே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- Being blissful is the nature of devotees. Contemplating the grace of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), the muktas are always blissful as explained in mahābhāratam sānti parvam 358.73 “*hruśtā*:” (muktātmās are always vorśipping [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and being blissful like the ones in svēta dvīpam).
- Being gītācāryan, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) revealed the most confidential principles of vēdam to uplift the ones who have even little bit inclination. Whatever was concealed by vēdam, he mercifully revealed them in gītai. **Note: nanjīyar alone attributes first 2 lines to** [**bhagavān**](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) **revealing the principles of vēdam through gītai. In all other vyākhyānams, the pāsuram is fully attributed to** [**nammāḻvār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)**.**
- [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) explained gītai for the ones who are qualified to learn vēdam. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) explained the principles even for the unqualified ones.
- [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) revealed it for the ones who were assembled in the battle field (only few benefited). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) revealed it for the benefit of all as mentioned in tiruvaimoḻi 1.5.11 “*pālēy tamiḻar isaikārar pattar paravumum āyiram*” (tamiḻ speaking persons, musicians/poets, devotees – all take refuge in these 1000 pāsurams)  and in tiruvāimoḻi 9.4.9 “*toṇḍarkkamuduṇṇac col mālaigaḷ*” (garlands made of nectarean words for the pleasure of devotees).

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- *aruḷ koṇḍādum adiyavar* – those who glorify [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s mercy like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) in tiruvāimoḻi 8.8.3 said “*atuvum avanatu innaruḷ*” (that is also his mercy only) and in tiruvāimoḻi 10.6.1 “*aruḷ peṟuvār adiyār*” (the devotees are blessed by [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)). Other [āḻvārs](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/azhwars/) are also like him only.
- *aruḷ koṇḍu* – with mercy – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) did not sing on his own (whatever he felt like singing)- he sung based on the mercy of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) as said in tiruvāimoḻi 1.1.1 “*mayarvaṟa madinalam aruḷinan*” ([bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) blessed me blemiśless knowledge/devotion). As explained in [tiruvāimoḻi](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/thiruvaimozhi/) 7.9.4 “*ennāgiyē tapputalinṟit tanaik kavi tān solli*” (Through me, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) sung the glories of himself without any defect) – not only others, [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) himself became blissful after compiling these pāsurams.
- *intamiḻ pādinān* – even though he is giving the most complicated knowledge, he gave in such sweet tamiḻ language.
- *ivvulaginil mikkatē* – greater than [cit (sentients)](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/), [acit (insentients)](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/09/thathva-thrayam-achith-what-is-matter/) and [īsvara](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) ([bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)) tatvams (entities).

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- This vyākhyānam is mostly based on [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam.
- *aruḷ koṇḍādum adiyavar* – those who glorify [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s mercy like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) in tiruvāimoḻi 8.8.3 said “*atuvum avanatu innaruḷ*” (that is also his mercy only). [toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) says in tirumālai 46 “*ānaikkanṟu aruḷai īnta*” (one who showed his mercy to gajēndran/elephant). [bhūtattāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/10/22/mudhalazhwargal/) says in iraṇḍām tiruvantādi 56 “*aruḷ purinta sintai*” ([bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s merciful thoughts). [periyāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/20/periyazhwar/) says in periyāḻvār tirumoḻi 5.4.1 “*ninnaruḷē purintiruntēn*” (desiring for your mercy only). [pēyāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/10/22/mudhalazhwargal/) says in mūnṟām tiruvantādi 16 “*aruḷātoḻiyumē*” (always merciful). [tirumangai āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/23/thirumangai-azhwar/) says “*āḻiyānaruḷē*” (mercy of the one who holds sudarsana chakra only – [srīman nārāyaṇan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)). All these [āḻvārs](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/azhwars/) are fully situated in [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s prasādam (mercy) and have no attachment in self-effort.
- *arumaṟai* – vēdam which is very difficult to acquire/understand, i.e., the confidential/essence part of vēdam which is upaniśads. The meanings of upaniśads can only be understood by the ones who are situated in satva guṇam (pure goodness). For such persons, their wealth is that deep knowledge of upaniśads. That too acquiring the knowledge by the grace of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) (not through self-effort). Such knowledge is blissful (*inbuṟa*) as said in brahma sūtram 1.1.15 “*tattētu vyapadēsācca*” (As [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is explained as the source for [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)‘s ānandam (they are different from each other)).
- *āyiram intamiḻ pādinān* – sung 1000 pāsurams. For the well-being of an [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/), just one pāsuram from tiruvāimoḻi is sufficient. But [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) sung the most difficult principles in the manner that everyone can best relish.
- *pādinān aruḷ kaṇḍīr* – Such [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s aruḷ (mercy) is greater than [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s aruḷ since [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) uplifts the [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)s that are given up by [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) himself.

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- *aruḷ koṇḍādum adiyavar inbuṟa* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) sung [tiruvāimoḻi](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/thiruvaimozhi/) for the pleasure of sātvikas (who are fully situated in proper knowledge) and not for the pleasure of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).
- adiyavar – servants/devotees – ones who have sēśatva jñānam (knowledge of servitude) – such knowledge must lead to kainkaryam (service).
- *aruḷ koṇḍādum adiyavar* – those firmly believe that for such kainkaryam, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s mercy is the sādanam (means/process). mercy is upāyam (means) and kainkaryam is puruśārttam (goal). Ones who glorify [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s mercy more than any other of his qualities. They glorify mercy more since when there is mercy, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s other qualities will be worthy, but when there is no mercy, other qualities will become useless. Just like [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is more glorifiable than dēvatāntarams (other dēvatās), [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s mercy is more glorifiable than his other qualities.
- [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s krupai is required for saktan (one who is capable to engage in karma, jñāna, bhakti yōgams) and asaktan (one who fully surrenders revealing his incapability). For karma, jñāna, bhakti yōgam followers, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) looks at their difficult efforts, have mercy on them, becomes pleased and fulfills their desire. For prapannas, they reveal their ākincanyam (having nothing in their side) and [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) shows mercy on them to fulfill their desires.
- To show that [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) uses his mercy to uplift others, two pramāṇams are quoted – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) himself says in periya tiruvantādi 26 “*aruḷennum taṇḍu*” (stick named mercy) and tirumangai āḻvār says in periya tirumoḻi 6.2.4 “*aruḷennum oḷ vāḷ*” (sword named mercy). Since [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) has brāhmaṇyam, he uses the example of stick and since [tirumangai āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/23/thirumangai-azhwar/) has kśātram (warrior qualities) he uses the example of sword. In arumpadam, it is explained that [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is said as brāhmaṇa due to his tranquil nature – the most advanced/peaceful state of understanding brahmam clearly.
- *inbuṟa* – even though these sātvikas know that mercy is the way to get [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s benediction, they are blissful to hear the same from [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). When others recommend/speak about our favourite, we will be naturally pleased.
- *arumaṟaiyin poruḷ* – *maṟaiyin arum poruḷ* (most important meaning of vēdam) that is mercy is the means and kainkaryam is the goal.
- *poruḷai aruḷinān* – Since [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) would not recite vēdam as it is (due to his varṇam), he is revealing the meanings of the same. While the vēdam itself cannot be learned/recited by caturta varṇam (sūdra), everyone can learn/speak the *meanings* of vēdam irrespective of their varṇam.
- *āyiram intamiḻ pādinān* – Unlike praṇavam which is very short and mahābhāratam which is very long, he sung 1000 pāsurams in beautiful tamiḻ.
- *pādinān* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) sang – vēdam is apauruśēyam (not written/compiled by any individual) and is considered as the highest pramāṇam. But if compiled by individuals, will these be pramāṇam? If these were sung out of nigraham (disgust), it would not be pramāṇam. But as [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) himself says in 7.9.3 “*Am mudalvan ivanenṟu taṟṟēṟṟi en nā mutal vantu puguntu nalli kavi tūmutal pattarkkut tān tannaic conna en vāy mutal appanai*” (selecting me ([nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)) as the chosen/leading one, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) gave me full knowledge, being seated in my tongue, compiled beautiful poems on himself which are most enjoyable for mumukśus), it was [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s own words which came through him in a joyful manner – so it is a valid pramāṇam.
- *aruḷ kaṇḍīr ivvulaginil mikkatē* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s mercy is great and well-known in this world. Not the pāsuram, not the one who compiled the pāsuram ([nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)), not the mercy of the one who blessed [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) to compile the pāsuram ([bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)) – but [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s mercy is greater.
- *intamiḻ pādinān…* – Not the mercy of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) who gave the difficult gītai, but the mercy of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) who gave the sweetest tiruvāimoḻi. Not gītai, which is spoken by [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) independently, but [tiruvāimoḻi](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/thiruvaimozhi/) which is spoken by [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) by the grace of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).
- *intamiḻ pādinān aruḷ kaṇḍīr ivvulaginil mikkatē* – As said in tiruvāimoḻi 10.6.11 “*kēttārār vānavargaḷ sevikkiniya sencol*”  (the people of paramapadam wants to hear tiruvāimoḻi again and again), paramapadam is filled with [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s pāsurams. Similarly, this samsāram is filled with [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s mercy.
- *āyiram…* – No one performs anjali as soon as hearing the name of kriṣṇa who spoke gītai. But as soon as we hear [tirukkurukūr](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) (the birth place of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)), we perform an anjali (as part of tiruvāimoḻi sēvākālam, in the last pāsuram of each patikam (decad), we perform anjali hearing [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s name or his birth place [tirukkurukūr](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/)).
- *kaṇḍīr* – this is obviously visible. [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s mercy is to be understood from sāstram, but [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s mercy is obviously visible for us to see. We can see it here in this world itself – need not wait until we reach paramapadam.

adiyen saraty ramanuja dasan

## 9. Mikka vediyar

*mikka vēdiyar vēdattin utporuḷ niṟkap pādi en nenjuḷ niṟuttinān takka sīrc catakōpan ennambikku āḷ pukka kātal adimaip payan anṟē*

**Пословный перевод:**

mikka vēdiyar vēdattin – vēdam that is recited by best vaidikas; uḷ poruḷ – inner-meaning i.e., essence; niṟka – to firmly establish; pādi – sung tiruvāimoḻi; en nenjuḷ – in my heart; niṟuttinān – planted it firmly (the essence of vēdam or tiruvāimoḻi) out of his grace; takkasīr – with fitting qualities; satakōpan – nammāḻvār; en nambikku – for him (who is such complete person); āḷ pukka – to serve him; kātal – love/desire; anṟē – that same moment; adimaippayan – materialized such service as the goal

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: My love/desire to serve nammāḻvār as the ultimate goal materialized immediately, to him who firmly established in my heart the essence of vēdam, that is recited/studied by best among vaidikas.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that madurakavi āḻvār speaks about nammāḻvār firmly establishing the essence of all of vēdam which is bhāgavata sēśatvam (being surrendered to devotees of srīman nārāyaṇa) through tiruvāimoḻi 3.7 “payilum sudaroḷi” and tiruvāimoḻi 8.10 “nedumāṟkadimai” padigams (decads). nampiḷḷai  highlights that when madurakavi āḻvār was asked what was the type/state of nammāḻvār‘s mercy, madurakavi āḻvār says that nammāḻvār bestowed him the confidential essence of all of vēdam. Just like nammāḻvār revealed his gratitude towards emperumān in tiruvāimoḻi 3.3.4 “nīsanēn niṟai onṟum ilēn, nam kaṇ pāsam vaitta param sudarc cōtikkē” (I am filled with defects and have no good qualities. bhagavān became more radiant by showing his affection towards me), madurakavi āḻvār reveals his gratitude towards nammāḻvār. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that when madurakavi āḻvār was asked why is nammāḻvār‘s mercy greater than the whole world, madurakavi āḻvār says that nammāḻvār bestowed him the confidential essence of all of vēdam without looking at his defects. Just like nammāḻvār revealed his gratitude towards emperumān in tiruvāimoḻi 3.3.4 “nīsanēn niṟai onṟum ilēn, nam kaṇ pāsam vaitta param sudarc cōtikkē” (I am filled with defects and have no good qualities. bhagavān became more radiant by showing his affection towards me), madurakavi āḻvār reveals his gratitude towards nammāḻvār‘s lotus feet. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār‘s introduction Previously madurakavi āḻvār explained that nammāḻvār blessed him so that he can glorify nammāḻvār in pāsuram 6. He also explained in pāsuram 7 that whatever hurdles/sins were there, they were dispelled by by nammāḻvār and he said that he will speak about nammāḻvār‘s glories to everyone. In the previous pāsuram, he explained that nammāḻvār‘s mercy is greater than bhagavān‘s mercy. Since nammāḻvār‘s jñāna pradānam (bestowing knowledge)  was discussed in previous pāsuram, in this pāsuram, madurakavi āḻvār explains since nammāḻvār revealed the essence of vēdam, out of gratitude for that, serving such nammāḻvār is the ultimate goal. In the 4th pāsuram, ācārya visvāsam (unflinching faith towards nammāḻvār) is explained. In the 5th pāsuram and 6th pāsuram, ācārya vaibhavam (his glories) and his upakāra vaibhavam (glories of his favours/help) are explained. In this pāsuram his gratitude towards ācārya is explained. ācārya’s identity is mercy and siśya’s identity is gratitude towards the ācārya. மிக்க வேதியர் வேதத்தினுட் பொருள் நிற்கப் பாடியென் நெஞ்சுள் நிறுத்தினான் தக்க சீர்ச் சடகோபனென் நம்பிக்கு ஆட்புக்க காதலடிமைப் பயனன்றே.*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- *mikka vēdiyar* – one who has great knowledge about emperumān and great devotion towards emperumān.
- *vēdattin uḷ poruḷ* – The essence/main aspect of vēdam is [tiruvāimoḻi](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/thiruvaimozhi/). The essence of tiruvāimoḻi is tiruvāimoḻi 3.7 “*payilum sudaroḷi*” and tiruvāimoḻi 8.10 “*nedumāṟkadimai*” (both speak about bhāgavata sēśatvam).
- *niṟkappādi ennenjuḷ niṟuttinān* – firmly established it in my heart. Like drilling a rock and filling water – so it stays well.
- *āḷ pukka kātal adimaip payananṟē* – Me becoming adiyēn (dāsan – servant) means having love towards his lotus feet (Otherwise, just saying adiyēn is of no use).

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- *mikka vēdiyar* – Ones who are identified by the top-most pramāṇam (vēdam). Ones who have studied many sākās (parts) of vēdam. (Note: vēdiyar means ones who are well versed in vēdam and ones who follow it properly).
- *niṟkappādi* – Establishing the confidential essence of vēdam which is difficult to understand/accept.
- *takka sīrc catakōpan* – Fitting poet (with all auspicious qualities) to glorify [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/). Just like [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is most glorious/fitting as mentioned in tiruvāimoḻi 1.1.1 “[*ayarvaṟum amarargaḷ adipati*](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2015/03/thiruvaimozhi-1-1-1-uyarvara-uyarnalam/)” (the supreme leader of the nityasūris), [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is also most fitting/complete to glorify [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/). He himself says in tiruvāimoḻi 3.9.11 “*ēṟkum perumpugaḻ vānavar īsan kaṇṇan tanakku ēṟkum perumpugaḻ vaṇkurukūrc catakōpan*” (kaṇṇan emperumān, the leader of nityasūris, is most fitting to accept all praises. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) (me) is most fitting person to praise [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)).
- *en nambikku* – one who is most complete to accept me and reform me.
- *anṟē* – immediately. For the desire in bhagavat viśayam to be fulfilled, one has to go through arcirādi gati (the route that takes one up to virajā river) and reach paramapadam to serve him eternally/fully. But in [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s viśayam (case), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) attained the result immediately without any delay and without having to go anywhere else. For [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), the desire started in 1st pāsuram of tiruvāimoḻi and he attained the full goal after 10.10 “*muniyē nānmukanē*” padigam (which is at the end).

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- This vyākhyānam is mostly based on [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam.
- *mikka vēdiyar* – pramāṇa srēśtam (top-most of authentic scriptures). As heard from [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) in tiruvāimoḻi 1.1.7 “[*uḷan sudarmigu surutiyuḷ*](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2015/04/thiruvaimozhi-1-1-7-thidavisumbu-erivali/)” (one who is present/explained in most radiant vēdam).
- vēdattin uḷ poruḷ – vaidika sāstram determines that vēdānta rahasyam (praṇavam) establishes that bhagavat sēśatvam (servitude towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)) extends ultimately up to tadīya sēśatvam (servitude towards devotees of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)).

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- *mikka vēdiyar* – **vēdiyar** means one who ignores bhāhya sāstrams (literature outside vēdam) and are fully faithful to vēdam which is nityam (eternal – beginning-less) and nirdōśam (blemiśless). They fully understand that nāradīya purāṇam 18.33 “*vēdasāstram param nāsti*” (there is no sāstram other than vēdam or there is no sāstram higher than vēdam) and tiruccanta viruttam 72 “*vēda nūl ōtuginṟatu uṇmai allatu illai*” (vēda sāstram talks about nothing other than truth). **mikka vēdiyar** means one who is not bewildered by insignificant goals and understands that bhagavat kainkaryam is the ultimate goal. One who fully understands that to attain such goal bhakti and saraṇāgati are the two means.
- vēdattin uḷ poruḷ – *vēdapporuḷ* (meaning of vēdam) is [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) as explained by bhagavān himself in gītā 15.15 “vēdais ca sarvair ahamēva vēdya:” (vēdam fully speaks about me only). [periyāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/20/periyazhwar/) says in periyāḻvār tirumoḻi 2.9.6 “vēdap poruḷē vēnkatavā” (Oh srīnivāsa! you are the object of vēdam) and in periyāḻvār tirumoḻi 4.3.11 “*vēdānta viḻupporuḷ*” (the inner meaning of vēdāntam). *uḷ poruḷ* (inner meaning) is to understand the glories of bhāgavatas  which is explained in tiruvāimoḻi 3.7 “*payilum sudaroḷi*” and tiruvāimoḻi 8.10 “*nedumāṟkadimai*“.
- *niṟuttinān* – ([nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)) firmly established it. Even if he himself tries to shake it up, my faith will not change.
- *ennenjuḷ niṟuttinān* – Established in my heart which would not even understand the glories of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) – he made me realize the glories of bhāgavatas.
- *mikka … niṟuttinān* – He has done so much favour that he sung the essence of vēdam in tiruvāimoḻi and he made me understand the meanings of tiruvāimoḻi. Only for the ones who understand the glories of ones ācārya, one will understand the meanings of vēdam. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) established the glories of pramāṇam (vēdam), pramēyam (vēdapporuḷ – [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)), pramātru (mikka vēdiyar) and abimata viśayam ([nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) who is most dear to [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)) in the heart of madurakavi āḻvār.
- *takka sīrc catakōpan en nambi* – ones completeness is revealed by clearing limitations of others and making them complete. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) removed my ignorance and made me fully realize the important aspects. He is not tirukkuṟungudi nambi who is glorified in tiruvāimoḻi 5.5.5 as “*takka kīrttit tirukkuṟungudi nambi*” (most glorious [tirukkuṟungudi](http://thirukkurungudi.koyil.org/) nambi emperumān), but he is tirukkurukūr nambi (most glorious [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) of [āḻvār tirunagari)](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s nambi is tirukkuṟungudi nambi. [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)‘s nambi is [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).
- *adimaip payananṟē* – First, one should become a servitor to ācārya, develop love/attachment towards him, and serve him as much as possible. These 3 are desired in a siśya (towards his/her ācārya). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) preached bhagavat sēśatvam (being a servant of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)), bhagavat prēmam (love towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)) and sēśatva vrutti (kainkaryam/service towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)). But [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) desired for these towards [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) eliminated worldly desires and showed [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/). [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) eliminated [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and served the lotus feet of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/).

## 10. Payan anragilum

*payan anṟāgilum pāngallar āgilum seyal nanṟāgat tiruttip paṇi koḷvān kuyil ninṟār poḻil sūḻ kurukūr nambi muyalginṟēn un tan moykaḻaṟku anbaiyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

payan anṟu āgilum – though there is no personal gain (by reforming others); pāngu allar āgilum – though (they are) not fit to be reformed; seyal – by (his) deeds; nanṟāgat tirutti – reformed through strong instructions; paṇi koḷvān – engage (them) in service; kuyil ninṟu Al poḻil sūḻ kurukūr – (living in) āḻvārtirunagari which is surrounded by groves (gardens, etc) that are filled with the sounds of cuckoo; nambi! – Oh nammāḻvār!; un than – your; moy kaḻaṟku – unto the (your) best divine feet; anbaiyē – to develop affection only; muyalginṟēn – trying

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: Oh nammāḻvār! who is living in āḻvārtirunagari which is surrounded by groves (gardens, etc) that are filled with the sounds of cuckoo, though there is no personal gain for you (by reforming others), and though they are not fit to be reformed, you reform them and engage them in (your) service through your strong instructions and deeds. I am trying to develop love and affection only to your divine feet (which is the best).**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that madurakavi āḻvār mediates on nammāḻvār‘s great favours towards him, realizes that he could not return such great favours and says that he immediately starts developing attachment towards nammāḻvār‘s divine feet. nampiḷḷai  highlights that madurakavi āḻvār meditates on nammāḻvār‘s  great favours towards him, realizes that he has not taken any steps towards nammāḻvār and speaks the glories of nammāḻvār‘s  favours. It is explained in viṣṇu darmam 70.78 “…krutsnām vā prutivīm dadyānna tattulyam katancana:” (For the ones who give knowledge about bhagavān and the means to reach bhagavān, the whole world should be submitted. Even that will not match the favour done by such knowledge-bestover (ācārya)). Since ācārya gives bhagavān himself to the siśya, only if there is another bhagavān, the favour can be returned by the siśya. Thus, siśya will always be unsatisfied with his deeds towards his ācārya. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights the same principle as that of nampiḷḷai. But instead of simply saying ācārya gives bhagavān, he says ācārya gives the highest benefit – the divine couple (srī mahālakśmi and srīman nārāyaṇa). Only if the siśya finds another divine couple like that (which is impossible), he can return the favour to his ācārya and thus the siśya will always be unsatisfied that he cannot return the favours done by his ācārya. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār‘s introduction So far, nammāḻvār‘s unique glories and the favours done by nammāḻvār towards madurakavi āḻvār were explained by him. nammāḻvār‘s glories which are contrary to his own defects and nammāḻvār‘s  great favours which are contrary to his own limitations were explained before. In this pāsuram, he realizes that he can never return nammāḻvār‘s favours and becomes worried (that he cannot do that). In taitriya bruguvalli 10.6, it is explained that “yō mā dadāti sa i dēvamāvā:” (one who gives me the supreme lord is my rakśaka (protector)). This feeling of gratitude will occur both in mumukśu stage (when being desirous of mōkśam in this world) or mukta state (after reaching parampadam) and one cannot do anything beyond showing gratitude towards ācārya throughout one’s existence. It is the nature of ācārya to not expect anything in return and it is the nature of siśya to try to do something in return (for the great favours done by ācārya). But since nothing can be done matching the favours done by ācārya, he becomes very sad. Seeing the great favours, the siśya will desire to do something in return. But the greatness of the favours done by ācārya will make the siśya realize that such great favours cannot be returned since there is nothing as good as that (bhagavān) which was given by ācārya. So, the only way to spend one’s time is by always worrying about not being able to satisfy one’s ācārya. Since ācārya reforms the ātmā, that ātmā can be submitted to bhagavān. Even that cannot be done in the case of ācārya, since it was the ācārya who already reformed the ātmā and made oneself realize about one’s true nature. So, offering oneself is also not a fitting return for ācārya’s favours. பயனன்றாகிலும் பாங்கலராகிலும் செயல் நன்றாகத் திருத்திப் பணிகொள்வான் குயில் நின்றார்ப் பொழில் சூழ் குருகூர் நம்பி முயல்கின்றேனுன்றன் மொய் கழற்கன்பையே*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- *seyal nanrāga* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s easy approacability is such that, any one (irrespective of ones greatness/lowliness), one can approach him and accept his shelter unlike [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) who is approachable only by the ones who have adikāram (qualification).
- *kuyil ninṟār poḻil sūḻ kurukūr nambi*  – Even the cuckoos living in [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) are joyfully singing – such is [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s simplicity (easy approacability). For bhagavat viśayam, one has to go to paramapadam to engage in sāma gānam (singing of divine sāma vēdam) as mentioned in taitriya bruguvalli 10.6 “*ētat sāma gāyannāstē*” (the liberated soul is singing sāma gānam). For muktas, the destination is paramapadam. But for mumkuśus, the destination is [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/). [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is enjoyed and relished by all including [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) himself, [nityasūris](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/), [muktātmās](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/), etc.

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- *payananṟāgilum* – In general, one helps another only for personal benefit. But [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) helped me even though I would be of no real use to him.
- *pāngallarāgilum* – Even if there is no personal benefit, one would at least be able to follow the instructions. In my case, even that is not there since I am not qualified for anything good.
- *payananṟāgilum pāngallarāgilum –* Even with these limitations, [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is unable to bear the suffering of such persons in this material world – that is why he is reforming them. Just like [sītā pirātti](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) instructed rāvaṇa, looking at his pathetic state of being attached to worldly pleasure even though he was trying to harm her, [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is instructing me, to reform me. Even though a person may stay inside his home shutting all entrances (not to allow any one in), when that house is in fire, the on-lookers will naturally try to extinguish the fire. Similarly, even though one may not be qualified, [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) instructs them still and reforms them.
- *tiruttip paṇikoḷvān* – [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is glorified in tiruvāimoḻi 3.5.11 as “*tīrnta adiyavar tammai tiruttip paṇi koḷḷa valla*” (One who is capable of reforming and engaging the devotees who accept [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) as the prāpyam (goal) and prāpakam (means)) – but unlike [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) will reform even those who have not understood their true nature properly.
- *kuyil ninṟār…* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) feels sad in separation from [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) like in tiruvāimoḻi 8.5.2 “*kāṇavārāy*” (Please come and see me). He sometimes feels happy on meditating his relationship with [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) like in tiruvāimoḻi 4.5.8 “*yāvar nigar agal vānattē*” (there is no one equal to me). During all these times, the cuckoos are with him, and they learn his pāsurams from him thus and repeat the same.
- *kurukūr nambi* – the complete person from [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/). His completeness in auspicious qualities are such that, they even reform cuckoos (who don’t possess great intelligence to distinguish between [cit (soul)](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) and [acit (matter)](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/09/thathva-thrayam-achith-what-is-matter/)). He himself (in mother’s mood about her daughter parānkusa nāyaki) says in tiruvāimoḻi 6.7.2 “*ūrum nādum ulagamum tannaippōl … pitaṟṟa*” (She changed every one to be like her to be constantly uttering [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s names and glories).
- *muyalginṟēn un than moy kaḻaṟku anbaiyē* – I am trying to devote myself towards your most-praise-worthy lotus feet. [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is unable to comprehend how to show his gratitude towards [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), just like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) wondered how to show his gratitude to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) in tiruvāimoḻi 2.7.8 “*ennait tīmanam keduttāy unakken seygēn*” (You have reformed me, how am I going to repay this favour of yours?).

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- This vyākhyānam is mostly based on [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam.
- *muyalginṟēn un than moy kaḻaṟku anbaiyē* – Since he himself is not satisfied with his kainkaryam (service) towards [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), he prays that he develops more attachment towards [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s lotus feet.

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- *payananṟāgilum pāngallarāgilum –* Generally, an ācārya instructs only to a siśya who submits wealth, charity and renders service as mentioned in “*arttō darmata: susruśuradyābya:*“. [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) who is considered as *niravadika vatsalan* (one who has infinite love/affection towards his children – all) himself instructed to arjuna only after he submitted himself fully as said in gītā 2.7 “*siśyastēham sādi mām tvām prapannam*” (I am your disciple and fully surrendered to you). But [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) instructed me out of great compassion only due to my disqualification of learning anything.
- *seyal nanṟāgat tirutti* – Reformed me so that knowledge becomes fulfilled by practically following what is learned. Even kśatrabandu who was reformed by a vaiṣṇava (saintly person) attained the real goal only towards the end of his life, but I attained purity immediately. Reforming properly means – there should be sravaṇam (hear from ācārya) first, mananam (meditating on what was heard) next, mahāvisvāsam (unflinching faith) on what was meditated upon, practically following those aspects which we have faith in,  avoiding asadanuśtānam (mis-deeds) and performing only sadanuśtānam (deeds which are followed by [pūrvācāryas](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/introduction-contd/) only). He himself ensured that I go through these steps and be reformed fully (instead of letting myself try it out).
- *paṇi koḷvān* – Engaged in bhagavat, bhāgavata kainkaryam (service to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and his devotees). Once reformed, one should be engaged in kainkaryam. There should be bhagavat kincitkāram (at least little bit of service to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)), ācārya kincitkāram (at least little bit of service to ācārya) and vaiṣṇava kincitkāram (at least little bit of service to srīvaiṣṇavas). To please ācārya, one performs kainkaryam to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and bhāgavatas. To please [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and bhāgavatas, one performs kainkaryam to ācārya. All three are related and nourish each other. Among these three, bhagavat kincitkāram is nirupādikam (natural) and bhāgavata/ācārya kincitkāram are sōpādikam (acquired). bhāgavata kainkaryam is desired/acquired due to that being the ultimate goal. ācārya kainkaryam is desired/acquired due to one’s gratitude towards ācārya who shows [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and bhāgavatas.
- A true ācārya will first give proper instructions to the siśya, he will then himself practically demonstrate those instructions and will ensure that he becomes purer than himself. Only then such ācārya will be satisfied with the siśya and acknowledge him as qualified to do bhagavat/bhāgavata kincitkāram.
- *kuyil ninṟār…* – If the cuckoos in [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) themselves are reformed (due to [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s sambandam) what to speak of me? Since he has madura vāk (sweet voice/words), he glorifies [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) in connection with cuckoos who have sweet voice.
- *kurukūr nambi* – one who is so complete that he would not expect anything in return for the great favours he has done. Even [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s favours can be re-paid, but ācārya’s favours can never be re-paid.
- *moykaḻal* – Just like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) said in (the end of) periya tiruvantādi 87 “*moykaḻalē ētta muyal*” (try to glorify bhagavān’s lotus feet), here [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) also says the same thing. Previously, [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) said “*ponnadi*” in [2nd pāsuram](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/?p=1309) highlighting the pure nature of nammāḻvār’s lotus feet. In this pāsuram, the sweet/reliśable nature of nammāḻvār’s lotus feet is revealed.
- *moykaḻaṟku anbaiyē –* [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s most reliśable aspect of lotus feet induced my love towards them. That love induced me to show my gratitude towards him. But I am most bewildered since I am unable to re-pay his favours. This bewilderment will go on forever.

adiyen saraty ramanuja dasan

## 11. Anban tannai

*anban tannai adaintavarkatkellām anban tenkurukūr nagar nambikku anbanāy madurakavi sonna sol nambuvār pati vaikuntam kāṇminē*

**Пословный перевод:**

anban tannai – that emperumān who is āsrita pakśapāti (who is partial towards his devotees); adaintavarkatku ellām – to all bhāgavatas who surrendered unto him (bhagavān); anban – one who is devoted; tenkurukūr nagar nambikku – to nammāḻvār (who is the leader of beautiful āḻvārtirunagari); anban āy – being devoted to; madurakavi sonna sol – this divya prabandam which is spoken by madurakavi āḻvār; nambuvār – one who is faithful (that this is their refuge); pati – residing place; vaikuntam kāṇmin – (see it to be) paramapadam

**Перевод:**

**Simple translation: srīvaikuṇṭam (paramapadam) will be the residing place for the ones who firmly believe in this divya prabandam that is spoken with devotion by madurakavi āḻvār (me) towards nammāḻvār who is devoted to all bhāgavatas who are dear to emperumān who is partial towards his devotees.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that madurakavi āḻvār declares in the end that those who learn his prabandam will live in srīvaikuṇṭam which is fully under the orders of nammāḻvār. nampiḷḷai and periyavāccān piḷḷai highlight that madurakavi āḻvār declares in the end that those who learn this prabandam will live in srīvaikuṇṭam. aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār‘s introduction In the end, madurakavi āḻvār declares that those who learn this prabandam will live in srīvaikuṇṭam. Previously, madurakavi āḻvār explained nammāḻvār‘s most enjoyable nature (1st pāsuram) nammāḻvār being his lord and master (2nd pāsuram), he spends his time reciting nammāḻvār‘s divya prabandams (2nd pāsuram) bhagavān blessing him with his divine vision due to his relationship with nammāḻvār (3rd pāsuram) nammāḻvār accepting him inspite of his defects (4th pāsuram) his defects/vices being removed as soon as entering nammāḻvār‘s residing town of āḻvārtirunagari (5th pāsuram) subsequently nammāḻvār reformed him such that those defects/vices do not return to him, by engaging him in singing his glories (6th pāsuram) his desire of wanting to spread the glories of nammāḻvār‘s compassion everywhere (7th pāsuram) nammāḻvār‘s compassion being greater than bhagavān‘s compassion (8th pāsuram) and finally nammāḻvār doing great favours out of such compassion (9th pāsuram) him feeling agonized for him being unable to do anything in return to nammāḻvār for his great favours (10th pāsuram). In this pāsuram, madurakavi āḻvār declares that those who learn and recite this prabandam will be favoured by nityasūris in paramapadam. Though such devotees (of nammāḻvār) are only desiring to be in āḻvārtirunagari, to fulfill the desires of nityasūris, they would go to paramapadam and will enjoy nammāḻvār there too. அன்பன் தன்னை அடைந்தவர்கட்கெல்லாம் அன்பன் தென்குருகூர் நகர் நம்பிக்கு அன்பனாய் மதுரகவி சொன்ன சொல் நம்புவர் பதி வைகுந்தம் காண்மினே*

**Highlights from** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- *anban tannai…* – [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is affectionate towards everyone. As explained in srīrāmāyaṇam yudda kāṇḍam 30.56 “*ripūṇam api vatsala:*” (srī rāma shows his motherly forbearance even towards one who considers him as his enemy). This is the true nature of [īsvara/bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).
- *tannai adaintavar…* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is affectionate towards everyone who is surrendered to such [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/). This is [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s true nature. It is natural for everyone to be friendly with like minded persons.
- *tenkurukūr…* – It is the true nature of srī [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) to be affectionate towards [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). This is said by [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) after being induced by loving devotion towards him.
- *nambuvār…* – For those who learn this prabandam, srīvaikuṇṭam will be their abode. This sounds contradictory. When [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is explained as the goal, should he not declare that [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) is their abode? [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/) is under the control of polindu ninṟa pirān and [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) (Even the dēvastānam is known as ādinātar āḻvār dēvastānam). But paramapadam is explained as the abode that is fully under the control of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) as in tiruviruttam 75 as “*adiyār nilāginṟa vaikuntamō?*” (Is this srīvaikuṇṭam the land that is fully controlled by the devotees? – as per vyākhyānam).
- Another explanation. The place where those who learned this prabandam reside, that becomes srīvaikuṇṭam. nambi tiruvaḻudi vaḷaṇādu dāsar explains that after the birth of [parāsara bhattar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/11/parasara-bhattar/) as the son of [kūrattāḻvān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/kurathazhwan/) there is no barrier between samsāram and paramapadam since he destroyed the barrier and made them all a single abode.

Thus ends [nanjīyar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)‘s vyākhyānam. [nanjīyar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/) tiruvadigaḷē caraṇam.

**Highlights from** [**nampiḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)**‘s eedu vyākhyānam:**

- *anban* – (without any other special qualifications/requirements) – he is naturally vatsalan (one who has vātsalyam – motherly forbearance). His motherly forbearance is towards everyone irrespective of their attitude as explained in srīrāmāyaṇam “*ripūṇam api vatsala:*“. That is due to his eternal sambandam (relationship) with every one as explained in jitantē stōtram 2 “*dēvānam dānavānām ca sāmānyam adi daivatam*” (You are the common refuge to both dēvas who are devoted to you and asuras who are against you).
- *tannai adaintavarkatku ellām anban* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s affection is not common like [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s. His affection is specifically towards devotees as he himself says in tiruvaimoḻi 3.7.1 “*paramanaip payilum tiruvudaiyār yavarēlum avar kaṇḍīr  emmaiyāḷum paramar*” (Whoever has the wealth of devotion towards the supreme lord, they are my supreme lords).
- *tenkurukūr nagar nambikku anbanāy* – Not just stopping with [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), not just stopping with all his devotees but being affectionate to his own ācārya ([nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)). When there is personal expectation towards a goal, one will approach/serve [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/). When there is desire to fulfill [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s expectation (only), one  will approach/serve bhāgavatas. But when one desires to fulfill both [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and bhāgavatas’ expectations, one serves ācārya.  ācārya is the goal since he is the one who reveals knowledge that is not known before. Subsequently, since he engages oneself in discussing about [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) all the time and being a good companion in this world, he is the goal at that time. To attain ultimate goal of reaching [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), he is the puruśakāra bhūtan (recommendation authority) – so he is the goal at that time. Finally, in paramapadam, since he is the one who nurtures bhagavat kainkaryam, he is the goal at that last stage also.
- *nambuvārpati vaikuntam kāṇminē* – nambi tiruvaḻudi vaḷanādu dāsar says wherever such persons (who have faith in [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/)‘s words) are, that place will become srīvaikuṇṭam.
- [bhattar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/11/parasara-bhattar/)‘s explanation is explained here as repeated by [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/) as heard from peṟṟi (who is a contemporary of [nanjīyar)](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/).

Thus ends [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam. [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/) tiruvadigaḷē caraṇam.

**Highlights from** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- This vyākhyānam is mostly based on [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam.
- *anban* – Another pramāṇam for eternal relationship between [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) and [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is quoted from mahābhāratam āraṇya parvam 192.56 “*sarvēśāmēva lōkānām pitā mātā ca* mādava:” ([srīman nārāyaṇan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/), the divine consort of [srī mahālakśmi](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/), is the father and mother for every one).
- *nambikkanbanāy* – A couple of pramāṇams that show [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s affection towards his devotees are highlighted. [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) himself says in gītā 7.18 “*jñāni tu ātma Eva mē matam*” (such jñāni is only my ātmā and this is my philosophy). He also says in mahābhāratam udyōga parvam 74.27 “*mama prāṇā hi pāṇḍavā:*” (pāṇḍavas are my life breath).
- *vaikuntam* – To highlight that paramapadam is under the control of nityasūris and [muktātmās](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/), another pramāṇam is quoted from tiruvāimoḻ 3.9.9 “*vānavar nādu*” (the land of [nityasūris](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) and [muktātmās](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) – not said as the land of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)).
- *nambuvārpati vaikuntam kāṇminē* – a pramāṇam is quoted for this from tiruvāimoḻi 5.3.9 “*uraikkavallārkku vaikuntamāgum tammūrellām*” (whoever recites (tiruvāimoḻi), their residing place will become paramapadam).

Thus ends [periyavāccān piḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)‘s vyākhyānam. [periyavāccān piḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/) tiruvadigaḷē caraṇam.

**Highlights from** [**aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)**‘s vyākyanam:**

- *anban…* (full pāsuram summary) – In this pāsuram, [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/), the kaṇṇinuṇ ciṟut tāmbu divya prabandam and result of learning this prabandam somehow are explained.
- *anban* – [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is unconditionally affectionate towards everyone. stōtra ratnam 10 explains “*ēvam nisargga suhrudi – na citramidam āsrita vatsalatvam*” (Since you are doing so many favours towards everyone (irrespective of their state), there is nothing artificial/great in your motherly forbearance towards your devotees). He is explained as “*sarvalōkaika vatsala:*” (he is affectionate towards everyone in the universe), srīrāmāyaṇam sundara kāṇḍam 21.20 “*saraṇagata vatsala:*” (one who is affectionate towards the ones who surrender unto him), srīrāmāyaṇam yudda kāṇḍam 30.56 “*ripūṇam api vatsala:*” (srī rāma shows his motherly forbearance even towards one who considers him as his enemy). But more specifically, towards his devotees he even accepts their defects happily as mentioned in srīrāmāyaṇam yudda kāṇḍam 18.3 “*…dōśō yatyapi…*” (I will never give up vibhīśaṇa who approached me with friendly attitude even if he has defects. For good persons, such acceptance is not to be condemned). Even those who engage in unfavourable acts are cared with motherly forbearance by [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) as mentioned in stōtra ratnam 63 “*…pratibhavam aparāddūr mugda sāyujyadōbhū:…*” (Even sisupāla who committed many offenses was pardoned by you and was given mōkśam by you).
- Among all qualities of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), this vātsalyam (motherly forbearance) is the most glorified. It is because of this vātsalyam, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is glorified as supreme (since this quality in its truest sense cannot be seen in anyone else). And it is because of this quality, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is approachable by everyone and is being the refuge for everyone.
- [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) started with “*kaṇṇinuṇ ciṟut tāmbināl kattuṇṇappaṇṇiya perumāyan ennappan*” in the first pāsuram explaining [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s qualities such as sausīlyam (easily mixing with everyone), saulabhyam (easily approachable), svāmitvam (supremacy), etc. And in the end, he completes with vātsalyam separately since this quality is more special than any other qualities of [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/).
- *anban tannai adaintavarkatku ellām anban* – starting to explain about [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). Unlike [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) who is affectionate towards everyone due to his natural relationship with everyone, [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is affectionate towards everyone who is devoted to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/). Irrespective of such devotees’ birth, etc., [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is affectionate towards them. He himself says in tiruvāimoḻi 3.7.8 “*kumbi narakargaḷ ēttuvarēlum … em toḻu kulam tāngaḷ*” (Even if one who lives in kumbī narakam glorified [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), those are considered as my masters) and in tiruvāimoḻi 3.7.9 “*ettanai nalam tān ilāta candāḷa caṇḍāḷargaḷāgilum,  maṇivaṇṇāṟkāḷenṟu uḷ kalantāradiyār tam adiyār em adigaḷ*” (Even though one is the lowest of a caṇḍāḷa (dog-eater) with no good qualities, if he is fully surrendered to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), such caṇḍāḷa’s devotees are my masters).
- *anban* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) will even give up bhagavat anubhavam to be with bhāgavatas. He himself says in tiruvāimoḻi 8.10.7 “… *avanadiyār, nanimākkalavi inbamē nāḷum vāykka*” (instead of being with [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), I would like to have the association of his devotees only).
- *tenkurukūr…* – [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s attachment towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)/bhāgavatas is due to his birth in this divya dēsam. If the field is fertile, the crop will also be rich.
- *nambi* – One who is full in ātma guṇams (essential qualities for a [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)).
- *anbanāy* – [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) became devoted to [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) seeing such ātma guṇa pūrti (complete in qualities).
- *anbanāy madurakavi* – Just like [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) is identified as [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s most loving devotee as he himself says in tiruvāimoḻi 2.1.11 “*ārāta kātal kurukuūrc catakōpan*” (satakōpa of [āḻvārtirunagari](http://azhwarthirunagari.koyil.org/), who has unfulfillable love towards [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)), [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is identified as [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s dear devotee. [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) was focussed on praṇavam (which explains [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)‘s svarūpam as a servant of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)) and nama: (which explains [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)‘s servitude towards bhāgavatas). [madurakavi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/17/madhurakavi-azhwar/) is focussed on nama: exclusively.
- *nambikku anbanāy madurakavi* – *nambikkanbanāy* – For the heart, there is love towards [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/). *madurakavi* – For the mouth/speech, there is sweet glorification of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s qualities.
- *nambuvār* – Instead of learning the meaning and ignoring it, having firm desire/faith in the meanings of these words, always engaging in reciting this prabandam, meditating on [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s glories that are revealed in this prabandam and feeling blissful, always wanting to hear the glories of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) from others, etc.
- *nambuvār* – One who has firm faith that the glories of [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/) revealed in this prabandam are not false (exaggeration) and only truth.

Thus ends [aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/)‘s vyākhyānam. [aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/) tiruvadigaḷē caraṇam.

Thus ends the English translation of kaṇṇinuṇ ciṟut tāmbu based on the wonderful vyākhyānams written by [nanjīyar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/) , [nampiḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/16/nampillai-english/),  [periyavāccān piḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/) and [aḻagiya maṇavāḷa perumaḷ nāyanār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/12/15/azhagiya-manavala-perumal-nayanar/).
