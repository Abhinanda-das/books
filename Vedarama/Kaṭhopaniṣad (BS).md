# Kaṭhopaniṣad (BS)

## Kaṭhopaniṣad (Bhanu Swami)

*oṁ atha kaṭhopaniṣad*

## Part 1

## Chapter 1

*prathamo'dhyāyaḥ prathamā vallī*

## Invocation

*oṁ saha nāvavatu \| saha nau bhunaktu \|*  
*saha vīryaṁ karavāvahai \| tejasvi nāvadhītam astu \| mā vidviṣāvahai \|*  
*oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**May the mantra protect us. May it nourish us. May it give us power. May our studies have power. May we not develop hatred. Peace, peace, peace.**

### 1.1.1

*oṁ uśan ha vai vāja-śravasaḥ sarva-vedasaṁ dadau \|*  
*tasya ha naciketā nāma putra āsa \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring material results, Vāja-śravas gave everything in charity. He had a son named Naciketā.**

**Комментарий:**

Vāja-śravas means he who has fame by food or from giving donations. Vāja-śravasaḥ means the son of Vāja-śravas. He gave everything (sarva-vedasam) in a sacrifice as donation, desiring (uśan) results. The quality of the donation had to be excellent since he had desires in performing the sacrifice.

### 1.1.2

*taṁ ha kumāraṁ santaṁ dakṣiṇāsu \|*  
*nīyamānāsu śraddhāviveśa so’manyata \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Faith overtook the young boy as the father was leading cows to give to the priests. He began thinking.**

**Комментарий:**

Desiring benefit for his father, faith overcome the young boy, as the father was leading cows to give them to the priests.

### 1.1.3

*pītodakā jagdhatṛṇā dugdha-dohā nirindriyāḥ \|*  
*anandā nāma te lokās tān sa gacchati tā dadat \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who gives to the priests cows which have drunk water, eaten grass, have been milked and can no longer bear calves, goes to the planets famous for sorrow.**

**Комментарий:**

His faith is described in this verse. The cows have drunk water and have eaten grass (jagdha-tṛṇā). They have been milked. They can no longer give birth to calves (nirindriyāḥ). He who gives such cows to the priests (tā) as donation (dadat), goes to the planets famous for having no happiness (anandā). The boy thought in this way.

### 1.1.4

*sa hovāca pitaraṁ tata kasmai māṁ dāsyasīti \|*  
*dvitīyaṁ tṛtīyaṁ taṁ hovāca mṛtyave tvā dadāmīti \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He spoke to his father. “To whom will you give me?” He said this a second and third time. Thinking that the donation his father was giving was defective, desiring to make the sacrifice effective by offering himself, Naciketā approached his father and spoke. “To which priest will you give me as a donation?” Waiting for an answer, he repeated the question two more times. The ignorant father, becoming angry, said “I will give you to death.”**

### 1.1.5

*bahūnām emi prathamo bahūnām emi madhyamaḥ \|*  
*kiṁ svid yamasya kartavyaṁ yan mayā’dya kariṣyati \|\|5\|\|*  
*“I will go first or in the middle among many going to death. What use does death have for a small boy like me?”*

**Перевод:**

**Though his father spoke to him in this way, the son, without fear or lamentation, replied to his father. I will go first in in the middle of the many persons going to Yama’s abode. Why? What use does death, who has all his desires fulfilled, have for me, a small boy, so that offering me to him will be successful? Thus I do not lament.**

### 1.1.6

*anupaśya yathā pūrve pratipaśya tathā’pare \|*  
*sasyam iva martyaḥ pacyate sasyam ivājāyate punaḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Examine the conduct of the ancestors and examine the conduct of the well-bred. Humans die like grains and are born again like grains.**

**Комментарий:**

Hearing his son without fear or anger speak in this way, his father repented, thinking “I should not give my son to death, though I spoke out of anger.” Seeing his father, the boy again spoke. Seeing how the ancestors were without false words, and others of good conduct also were without harsh words, you should behave in the same way.

Like grain, human beings age with time, and after aging, they die. Then like grains they are born again. In this impermanent world, what is the use of false words? Maintain truth in your words and send me to death.

### 1.1.7

*vaiśvānaraḥ praviśaty atithir brāhmaṇo gṛhān \|*  
*tasyaitāṁ śāntiṁ kurvanti hara vaivasvatodakam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(The doorkeepers spoke.) The fire god in the form of a brāhmaṇa guest enters the house. The wise offer him a proper welcome. Bring foot water, O Yama!**

**Комментарий:**

Being sent to death, Naciketā waited at death’s door for three nights, since death was absent. The aged doorkeepers then spoke to death when he returned. This brāhmaṇa guest, who is directly Agni, has entered the house. The well-bred offer a welcome in the form of a seat and foot water. O Vaivasvata (Yama)! Bring water for Naciketā for washing his feet.

### 1.1.8

*āśā-pratīkṣe saṁgataṁ sūnṛtāṁ*  
*ceṣṭā-pūrte putra-paśūṁś ca sarvān \|*  
*etad vṛṅkte puruṣasyālpa-medhaso*  
*yasyānaśnan vasati brāhmaṇo gṛhe \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**For the fool who does not feed a brāhmaṇa residing in his house, that sin destroys his hopes and expectations, his chance of meeting devotees, truthful, pleasant speech, his sacrifices and charitable acts, as well as his sons and animals.**

**Комментарий:**

The result of the sin is shown. For the foolish person in whose house a brāhmaṇa resides without food, that sin (etat) destroys (vṛṅkte) his hopes and expectations (āśā-pratīkṣe) or desires for objects not yet manifested and desire for objects already manifested. It destroys association with devotees (saṁgatam) and truthful, affectionate speech (sūnṛtam). It destroys sacrifices (īṣṭā) and charitable acts (pūrte) such as digging canals.

### 1.1.9

*tisro rātrīr yad avātsīr gṛhe me-*  
*‘naśnan brahmann atithir namasyaḥ \|*  
*namaste’stu brahman svasti me’stu*  
*tasmāt prati trīn varān vṛṇīṣva \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Death spoke.) O brāhmaṇa! I offer respects to you. Since you stayed in my house as my guest, worthy of respect, for three days without eating, ask for three benedictions so that I may gain auspiciousness.**

**Комментарий:**

After the old door keepers spoke to him, death spoke. O brāhmaṇa! Because you stayed three nights as my guest, worthy of respect (namasyaḥ), without eating, ask (vṛṇīṣva) for three boons, so that I may gain auspiciousness. Since you did not eat for three days, you should ask for three gifts. Even though you have no desire for them, ask to show mercy to me.

### 1.1.10

*śānta-saṅkalpaḥ sumanā yathā syād*  
*vīta-manyur gautamo mā’bhi mṛtyo \|*  
*tvat-prasṛṣṭa mā’bhivadet pratīta*  
*etat trayāṇāṁ prathamaṁ varaṁ vṛṇe \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Naciketā spoke.) O death! My father, should have a happy mind, without worry about me. When he sees me, returning from death, he should address me in a pleasing way, giving up anger. This is the first boon of the three.**

**Комментарий:**

Naciketā spoke. O death! May my father Gautama be without anger towards me, and have a happy mind (sumanā), devoid of worrying (śānta-saṅkalpaḥ) about what happened to me on meeting death. When you send me back to him, he should speak to me in a pleasant manner (pratītaḥ). Or he should give blessings to me (abhivadet).

### 1.1.11

*yathā purastād bhavitā pratīta*  
*auddālakir āruṇir mat-prasṛṣṭaḥ \|*  
*sukhaṁ rātrīḥ śayitā vīta-manyuḥ*  
*tvāṁ dadṛśivān mṛtyu-mukhāt pramuktam \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Death spoke.) Your father, descendent of Aruṇi, will be pleased with you as previously. By my mercy, giving up anger, he will sleep peacefully at night, seeing you delivered from the mouth of death.**

**Комментарий:**

As previously your father will be pleased with you. O descendent of Uddālaka! The son of Aruṇi (your father), or who came from two dynasties, being the object of my mercy (mat-prasṛṣṭaḥ), giving up anger towards you, will sleep peacefully at night on seeing you, delivered from the mouth of death.

### 1.1.12

*svarge loke na bhayaṁ kiṁcanāsti*  
*na tatra tvaṁ na jarayā bibheti \|*  
*ubhe tīrtvā’śanāyāpipāse*  
*śokātigo modate svarga-loke \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Naciketā spoke.) In the place of liberation, there is no fear at all. You (death) have no influence. The person there has no fear of old age. Surpassing hunger and thirst, surpassing lamentation, he enjoys in that liberated state.**

**Комментарий:**

Naciketā then asked from the second boon in two verses. Svarga actually means the place of liberation, since that is explained later. O death! You have no power there (na tatra tvam). The person residing there does not have fear of old age (na jarayā bibheti). Surpassing hunger and thirst, he surpasses lamentation and enjoys in that place of liberation.

### 1.1.13

*sa tvam agniṁ svargyam adhyeṣi mṛtyo*  
*prabrūhi tvaṁ śraddadhānāya mahyam \|*  
*svarga-lokā amṛtatvaṁ bhajanta*  
*etad dvitīyena vṛṇe vareṇa \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You know the fire sacrifice that leads to liberation. Since you have faith in liberation, tell me this. Those who have attained liberation attain immortality. I choose this as the second boon.**

**Комментарий:**

You know (adhyeṣi) the fire sacrifice, or method of worship, that leads to liberation. Having faith in liberation (śraddadhānāya), tell me this. “What will you gain by that?” Those who have attained the highest abode (svarga-lokā), attain immortality. In this state the person attains a svarūpa on attaining the Lord in a special place.

*evam evaiṣa saṁprasādo’smāc charīrāt samutthāya*  
*paraṁ jyotir upasaṁpadya svena rūpeṇābhiniṣpadyate*

**The object of mercy (jīva) rises from this body, attains the supreme light, and is endowed with its own form. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad 8.12.3)**

### 1.1.14

*pra te bravīmi tad u me nibodha*  
*svargyam agniṁ naciketaḥ prajānan \|*  
*ananta-lokāptim atho pratiṣṭhāṁ*  
*viddhi tvam etaṁ nihitaṁ guhāyām \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Death spoke.) I will tell you. Understand it from my teachings. O Naciketā! Knowing this fire which leads to liberation, you will attain Vaikuṇṭha and then have no more birth. Know this knowledge, which is secret.**

**Комментарий:**

Death then spoke. I will explain what you requested. Understand from my teachings. He shows the results of this knowledge. Knowing that fire that leads to Vaikuṇṭha, one attains the planet of Viṣṇu (ananta-lokāptim), after which there is no more birth (pratiṣṭhām). How is it possible that this knowledge gives such result? Know this knowledge involving worship of Brahman, which is hidden. You can understand but not others.

### 1.1.15

*lokādim agniṁ tam uvāca tasmai*  
*yā iṣṭakā yāvatīr vā yathā vā \|*  
*sa cāpi tat pratyavadad yathoktaṁ*  
*athāsya mṛtyuḥ punar evāha tuṣṭaḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Death explained the fire which leads to liberation as well as the type, number and arrangement of the bricks. Naciketā repeated what was said. Death, pleased with him, spoke again.**

**Комментарий:**

He spoke to him about the fire that causes going to that planet (lokādim) and the type of bricks to be piled up, and their number and how they should be piled. Naciketā repeated what he had heard (prayavadat). Seeing that the disciple was capable of understanding, death was pleased with him (asya) and spoke again.

### 1.1.16

*tam abravīt prīyamāṇo mahātmā*  
*varaṁ tavehādya dadāmi bhūyaḥ \|*  
*tavaiva nāmnā bhavitā’yam agniḥ*  
*sṛṅkāṁ cemām aneka-rūpāṁ gṛhāṇa \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Pleased with the boy, death spoke to him. I will give you a fourth boon. This fire will be named after you. Accept this amazing, jingling, jeweled necklace.**

**Комментарий:**

Death, pleased with Naciketā spoke to him.

I will give you a fourth boon. The fire that I explained will be called by your name Naciketā. Accept this amazing necklace of gems which jingles (sṛṅkām).

### 1.1.17

*tri ṇāciketas tribhir etya sandhiṁ*  
*tri-karma-kṛt tarati janma-mṛtyū \|*  
*brahmajajñaṁ devam īḍyaṁ viditvā*  
*nicāyyemāṁ śāntim atyantam eti \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Performing three actions, relating with the Lord by three fires, the worshipper of the three Nāciketa fires attains freedom from birth and death. Knowing the jīva to have the Lord as his very self, realizing this, one becomes free of saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий:**

One who has studied the three anuvākas (chapters) (tri-nāciketaḥ) starting with ayaṁ vāva yaḥ pavate should perform the three actions (tri-karma-kṛt): sacrifice, study, and charity or should perform the pāvaka yājña, the havir yajña and the soma-yajña. One should attain relationship with the Lord (sandhim) by the (Naciketa) fire sacrifices undertaken three times. Having attained the Lord, one will surpass birth and death. This is also explained in Vedānta Sūtra 1.4.6.

Worship of the Lord is then explained. Brahmaja-jñam means the jīva, who is arises from Brahman and is conscious. Knowing the jīva to have the Lord as his ātmā (devam), realizing this (nicāya), one attains freedom from saṁsāra.

### 1.1.18

*tri ṇāciketas trayam etad viditvā*  
*ya evaṁ vidvāṁś cinute nāciketam \|*  
*sa mṛtyu-pāśān purataḥ praṇodya*  
*śokātigo modate svarga-loke \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing the three anuvāks and knowing the Lord, the jīva and the fire, the person who performs the nāciketa fire rites gives up material attraction before leaving his body and, free of lamentation, enjoys in Vaikuṇṭha.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowing three things (trayam etat): the svarūpa of the Lord, the svarūpa of the jīva and the svarūpa of the fire, through scriptures or guru, the person learned from knowing these three items, who makes the nāciketa fire (cinute), driving away the ropes of death--being free of material attraction--previous to leaving his body (purataḥ), surpasses lamentation and enjoys in Vaikuṇṭha.

## Extra verse (in Raṅga-rāmānuja’s version)

*yo vāpyetāṁ brahma-jajñātma-bhūtāṁ*  
*citiṁ viditvā cinute nāciketam*  
*sa eva bhutvā brahmaja-jñātma-bhutaḥ*  
*karoti tad yena punar na jāyate*

**Перевод:**

**He who, knowing the piling of wood to be the jīva with the Lord as his antaryāmī, piles the wood, in this way realizes himself with the Lord within, and performs actions of worship by which he does not take birth again.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowing the piling of wood for the fire to be the jīva with the Lord as his ātmā, he who piles that wood of the Nāciketa fire, he who is situated in worship of the Lord, which causes liberation (brahmaja-jñātma-bhutaḥ), performs actions by which he does not take birth again.

### 1.1.19

*eṣa te’gnir naciketaḥ svargyo*  
*yam avṛṇīthā dvitīyena vareṇa \|*  
*etam agniṁ tavaiva pravakṣyanti janāsaḥ*  
*tṛtīyaṁ varaṁ naciketo vṛṇīṣva \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The fire that leads to liberation, which you requested as a second boon, has been taught, O Naciketā. People will call this fire by your name. O Naciketā! Please ask for the third boon.**

**Комментарий:**

O Naciketā this fire for attaining liberation has been taught, which you requested as your second boon. People will call this fire by your name.

### 1.1.20

*yeyaṁ prete vicikitsā manuṣye-*  
*‘stīty eke nāyam astīti caike \|*  
*etad vidyām anuśiṣṭas tvayā’haṁ*  
*varāṇām eṣa varas tṛtīyaḥ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Naciketa spoke.) There is a doubt: when man dies and is qualified for liberation, does he exist or cease to exist? Learning from you, I should know about liberation and the means. That is my third boon among the three.**

**Комментарий:**

When a person who is qualified for liberation dies, there is a doubt: does he exist or does he not exist? Learning from you (anuśiṣṭaḥ tvayā), I will know this knowledge. Some say that liberation means destruction of the jīva who is mere consciousness. Others say liberation is destruction of ignorance of the jīva. Others say that liberation is kaivalya, destruction of all qualities like knowledge in the jīva, where the jīva is like a stone. Others say that liberation is becoming Paramātmā devoid of all sin, produced by destruction of the upadhis on the state of jīva which is caused by the upādhis. Others say that liberation is realization of the Lord after the jīva destroys his ignorance in the form of his beginningless karmas. I should know the nature of liberation and its method by your mercy.

### 1.1.21

*devair atrāpi vicikitsitaṁ purā*  
*na hi suvijñeyam aṇur eṣa dharmaḥ \|*  
*anyaṁ varaṁ naciketo vṛṇīṣva*  
*mā moparotsīr ati mā sṛjainam \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Death spoke.) Even the devatās had doubt about this previously. Its nature is very subtle and difficult to understand. O Nacitketā, ask for another boon. Do not trouble me. Free me from this.**

**Комментарий:**

When asked about liberation, death considered that it could not be taught since it was a deep subject to teach, with no end, with possibility of failure in teaching it. There is doubt even for the devatās concerning the nature of the jīva in liberation (atra). Ātmā-tattva is not easily to understand. Its nature is very subtle (aṇuḥ). Dharma in general is difficult to understand but the dharma of this topic is very difficult to understand. Do not trouble me. Please free me (enam sṛja) from this subject.

### 1.1.22

*devair atrāpi vicikitsitaṁ kila*  
*tvaṁ ca mṛtyo yan na sujñeyam āttha \|*  
*vaktā cāsya tvādṛg anyo na labhyo*  
*nānyo varas tulya etasya kaścit \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Naciketā spoke.) The devatās are in doubt. O death! You have said that this topic is not easy to understand. One cannot obtain another speaker of this topic like you. There is no boon equal to this.**

**Комментарий:**

Naciketā speaks. This was the doubt even of the devatās. And you have said that this tattva is not easy to understand. One cannot obtain another speaker like you.

### 1.1.23

*śatāyuṣaḥ putra-pautrān vṛṇīṣvā*  
*bahūn paśūn hasti-hiraṇyam aśvān \|*  
*bhūmer mahad-āyatanaṁ vṛṇīṣva*  
*svayaṁ ca jīva śarado yāvad icchasi \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Death spoke.) Choose a life of a hundred years, sons and grandsons, many animals, elephants, gold and horses. Choose a huge kingdom on earth. Live as long as you desire.**

**Комментарий:**

Thinking that one who has attraction to other things could not understand the topic, he tempted the boy in order to test his steadiness in desiring liberation. Accept a huge kingdom (āyatanam) on earth or choose a huge palace on earth. You can live as long as your desire.

### 1.1.24

*etat tulyaṁ yadi manyase varaṁ*  
*vṛṇīṣva vittaṁ cira-jīvikāṁ ca \|*  
*mahā-bhūmau naciketas tvam edhi*  
*kāmānāṁ tvā kāma-bhājaṁ karomi \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If you think of some equal boon, choose wealth and long live. Be a king of huge territory. I will make you the object of desire for Apsarās.**

**Комментарий:**

If you think of some boon equal to those mentioned, choose, choose wealth and long live. Be a king on expansive land. I will make you the object of desire for Apsarās and others (kāmānām).

### 1.1.25

*ye ye kāmā durlabhā martya-loke*  
*sarvān kāmāṁś chandataḥ prārthayasva \|*  
*imā rāmāḥ sarathāḥ satūryā*  
*na hīdṛśā lambhanīyā manuṣyaiḥ \|*  
*ābhir mat-prattābhiḥ paricārayasva*  
*naciketo maraṇaṁ mā’nuprākṣīḥ \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Pray as you please for all desirables which are rare on earth. The women given by me (imāḥ) along with chariots and instruments are rare for humans to attain. You should be served by these items given by me. O Naciketā! Do not ask about the condition of the liberated jīva after death.**

**Комментарий:**

Pray for all desirables as you please (chandataḥ). Women rare for humans (imā ramāḥ) will be given by me, along with chariots and instruments (satūryā). You should be served by these items given by me (mat-prattābhiḥ). Do not ask about the condition of the jīva liberated after death (maraṇam).

### 1.1.26

*śvo-bhāvā martyasya yad antakaitat*  
*sarvendriyāṇāṁ jarayanti tejaḥ \|*  
*api sarvaṁ jīvitam alpam eva*  
*tavaiva vāhās tava nṛtya-gīte \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O death! These desirables will not last till tomorrow. They dissipate the power of all the senses. Everyone’s lifespan is short. You can keep your chariots, dancing and singing.**

**Комментарий:**

Though tempted by these things, Naciketā, remaining undisturbed in his heart, spoke.

O death (antaka! All these desirables of humans offered by you, will not exist tomorrow (svobhāvāḥ). They will not last two days. All these enjoyments destroy (jarayanti) the power of all the senses. The lifespan of even Brahmā is short. So what to speak of our lives? And even with long life, we should not accept these things. Let the chariots, dancing and singing remain with you.

### 1.1.27

*na vittena tarpaṇīyo manuṣyo*  
*lapsyāmahe vittam adrākṣma cet tvā \|*  
*jīviṣyāmo yāvad īśiṣyasi tvaṁ*  
*varas tu me varaṇīyaḥ sa eva \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Humans cannot be satisfied with wealth. Seeing you, I attain wealth. As long as you rule I will live. I should ask only for the boon I requested.**

**Комментарий:**

Humans cannot be satisfied with wealth they obtain.

*na jātu kāmaḥ kāmānām upabhogena śāmyati*

**Lust is never extinguished by enjoying desirable objects. Manu-saṁhitā**

If we see you, we attain wealth. We will live as long as you remain ruling over death. No one can end my life, surpassing your order. Whether we obtain boons or not, we continue to live. Therefore I should only ask for that third boon.

### 1.1.28

*ajīryatām amṛtānām upetya*  
*jīryan-martyaḥ kva tad āsthaḥ prajānan \|*  
*abhidhyāyan varṇa-rati-pramodān*  
*atidīrghe jīvite ko rameta \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**What discriminating person, afflicted by old age and death, understanding the nature of the liberated persons devoid of old age and death, would desire enjoyable objects in this world? Understanding the complexion, love and bliss of the spiritual world, who would enjoy living a long life on earth?**

**Комментарий:**

Knowing about the svarūpa of liberated souls devoid of ageing and death (ajīryatām amṛtānām), how can the discriminating person (prajānan), afflicted by old age and death (jīryan-martyaḥ), desire enjoyable objects such as Apsarās? After understanding nicely the bliss and complexion of the body in the liberated state, who would enjoy living a long life on earth?

### 1.1.29

*yasminn idaṁ vicikitsanti mṛtyo*  
*yat sāmparāye mahati brūhi nas tat \|*  
*yo’yaṁ varo gūḍham anupraviṣṭo*  
*nānyaṁ tasmān naciketā vṛṇīte \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O death! Please tell me about the svarūpa of the jīva in liberation in the spiritual world after death, about which the devatās have doubt. Naciketā chooses nothing except the boon concerning the nature of the jīva in liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

Tell me about that svarūpa of the liberated jīva in the spiritual world (mahati), about which the devatās have doubt. Naciketā does not choose any boon except that which concerns (anupraviṣṭaḥ) the nature of ātmā (gūḍham).

## Chapter 2

*prathamādhyāye dvitīyā vallī*

### 1.2.1

*anyac chreyo’nyad utaiva preyas*  
*te ubhe nānārthe puruṣaṁ sinītaḥ \|*  
*tayoḥ śreya ādadānasya sādhu*  
*bhavati hīyate’rthād ya u preyo vṛṇīte \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The path of śreyas is one. The path of preyas is another. The two, with different goals, control the human being. Of the two, accepting the path of śreyas is best. He who accepts the path of preyas is deprived of the highest goal.**

**Комментарий:**

Testing the disciple and confirming his steadiness of desire for liberation, he praises the desire for liberation, thinking that is suitable for teaching him. The paths of śreyas and preyas, with very different goals, bind or control (sinītah) the human being.

The path of liberation is most excellent. The path of enjoyment is different. Of the two, the path of śreyas is beneficial (sādhu) for liberation. He who accepts the path of preyas is bereft of the highest human goal (arthāt).

### 1.2.2

*śreyaś ca preyaś ca manuṣyam etaḥ*  
*tau samparītya vivinakti dhīraḥ \|*  
*śreyo hi dhīro’bhi preyaso vṛṇīte*  
*preyo mando yoga-kṣemād vṛṇīte \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Śreyas and preyas approach mankind. The wise man, examining the two, makes distinctions. The wise man chooses śreyas over preyas. The foolish person chooses preyas for preserving bodily comforts.**

**Комментарий:**

Śreyas and preyas approach the human. Deliberating on these two thoroughly (samparītya), the wise man distinguishes between them (vivinakti), like a swan separating milk from water. The wise man, he who uses his intelligence (dhīraḥ) chooses śreya over preyas. The foolish person chooses preyas for protecting his worldly comforts (yoga-kṣemāt).

### 1.2.3

*sa tvaṁ priyān priya-rūpāṁś ca kāmān*  
*abhidhyāyan naciketo’tyasrākṣīḥ \|*  
*naitāṁ sṛṅkāṁ vitta-mayīm avāpto*  
*yasyāṁ majjanti bahavo manuṣyāḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Naciketā! Understanding the true nature of material objects, attractive by their very form, you have rejected them. You have not accepted the paltry objects and wealth pursued by the foolish, in which many men drown.**

**Комментарий:**

Contemplating these pleasurable objects, attractive by their very form (priya-rūpān), understanding them to have faults of intense or mixed suffering (abhidhyāyan) you have rejected them. You have not accepted the wealth enjoyed by the foolish with inferior goals (sṛṅkām).

### 1.2.4

*dūram ete viparīte viṣūcī*  
*avidyā yā ca vidyeti jñātā \|*  
*vidyābhīpsinaṁ naciketasaṁ manye*  
*na tvā kāmā bahavo’lolupanta \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The two, known as avidyā and vidyā, are very different and contradictory. I understand that you desire vidyā. Many desires do not disturb you.**

**Комментарий:**

These two which are known as avidyā, filled with kāmya-karma and vidyā, filled the jñāna and vairāgya, are very different (viśūcī) and contradictory (viparīte). I consider that Naciketā desires knowledge (vidyābhīpsinam). Many material desires do not create obstacles for you.

### 1.2.5

*avidyāyām antare vartamānāḥ*  
*svayaṁ dhīrāḥ paṇḍitaṁ-manyamānāḥ \|*  
*dandramyamāṇāḥ pariyanti mūḍhā*  
*andhenaiva nīyamānā yathāndhāḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Existing amidst ignorance but thinking themselves learned, while suffering old age and sickness, the fools wander about like blind leading the blind.**

**Комментарий:**

The path of avidyā is criticized. Existing amidst avidyā in the form of kāmya-karmas like living amidst darkness, thinking themselves learned in scriptures, suffering from old age and sickness, the people without discrimination wander about (dandramyamānāḥ).

### 1.2.6

*na sāmparāyo pratibhāti bālaṁ*  
*pramādyantaṁ vitta-mohena mūḍham \|*  
*ayaṁ loko nāsti para iti mānī*  
*punaḥ punar vaśam āpadyate me \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sādhana for the next life does not manifest in the fool who is inattentive, bewildered by the illusion of wealth. He thinks that there is no world other than this and repeatedly suffers my punishment.**

**Комментарий:**

Sādhana for the next life does not manifest (pratibhāti) in the person with no discrimination (bālam), who is inattentive (pramādyantam) and bewildered by the illusion of wealth. Thinking (mānī) that there is no world beyond this world, he falls under my control again and again. He repeatedly gets punished by me.

### 1.2.7

*śravaṇāyāpi bahubhir yo na labhyaḥ*  
*śṛṇvanto’pi bahavo yaṁ na vidyuḥ \|*  
*āścaryo vaktā kuśalo’sya labdhā*  
*āścaryo jñātā kuśalānuśiṣṭaḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Many cannot hear about the Lord. Even hearing about many cannot understand him. Rare is the skilful speaker about the Lord and rare is he who gains knowledge of the Lord through a skilful teacher.**

**Комментарий:**

The famous Lord is not attained by many even to hear about him. To hear about the Lord requires great sukṛtis. Even having heard about the Lord, people do not know him. To know about the Lord is not easy. Obtaining a skilful speaker about the Lord is rare. And to know the Lord through a skilful teacher is rare.

*manuṣyāṇāṁ sahasreṣu kaścid yatati siddhaye \|*  
*yatatām api siddhānāṁ kaścin māṁ vetti tattvataḥ \|*

**Among thousands of men, some attain success in their endeavors. Among those who attain success, hardly anyone knows me in truth. BG 7.3**

### 1.2.8

*na nareṇāvareṇa prokta eṣa*  
*suvijñeyo bahudhā cintyamānaḥ \|*  
*ananya-prokte gatir atra nāsti*  
*aṇīyān hy atarkyam aṇu-pramāṇāt \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, difficult to understand, cannot be explained by an inferior person. He is conceived variously by various philosophies. The understanding of the Lord present when explained by the realized devotee is absent in others. The Lord is smaller than the smallest and beyond logic.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is not easily understood when explained by an inferior person, who has learned Vedānta with the goal of being learned and who thinks he is the body since the Lord is conceived variously by various philosophers (bahudhā cintyamānaḥ).

The understanding (gatiḥ) of the Lord explained by a person who has experienced the Lord (ananya-prokte) is absent when the Lord is explained by an inferior person (atra). Or there will be no wandering in saṁsāra (gatiḥ atra) when the Lord is explained by a real devotee. Or there is no understanding of the Lord since that understanding is self-manifesting (depending on the Lord’s mercy alone). Another version is anya-prokte: there is no understanding of the Lord when spoken by an inferior person. The Lord is beyond logical argument since he is smaller (aṇīyān) than the smallest (aṇu-pramāṇāt).

### 1.2.9

*naiṣā tarkeṇa matir āpaneyā*  
*proktānyenaiva sujñānāya preṣṭha \|*  
*yāṁ tvam āpaḥ satya-dhṛtir batāsi*  
*tvādṛṅ no bhūyān naciketaḥ praṣṭā \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That knowledge which you have attained, is not attained by logical argument. Dear student! The knowledge, spoken by a guru, is for attaining liberation. You have unshakeable determination. May I have questioners like you!**

**Комментарий:**

Topics concerning the Lord cannot be attained logical argument. Even by skillful logic one cannot understand the Lord. O dear student (preṣṭha)! The concepts taught to one by another person, taught by a guru, produces knowledge for liberation. That knowledge you have attained. You have unshakeable determination (satya-dhṛtiḥ).

### 1.2.10

*jānāmy ahaṁ śevadhir ity anityaṁ*  
*na hy adhruvaiḥ prāpyate hi dhruvaṁ tat \|*  
*tato mayā nāciketaś cito’gniḥ*  
*anityair dravyaiḥ prāptavān asmi nityam \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I know that wealth is impermanent. The permanent cannot be attained by impermanent means. I piled up the nāciketa fire using impermanent materials. But I attained eternal knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

Satisfied, death again spoke. I know that wealth (śevādhiḥ), the result of karma, is impermanent. Ātma-tattava, the permanent (dhruvam), cannot be attained by karmas which produce impermanent results or by karmas using impermanent materials. The nāciketa fire was piled up by me, using impermanent materials, with the intention of producing knowledge for attaining the Lord. From that, I have attained knowledge which produces eternal results (nityam). The only way to attain the Lord is through knowledge.

### 1.2.11

*kāmasyāptiṁ jagataḥ pratiṣṭhāṁ*  
*krator ānantyam abhayasya pāram \|*  
*stoma-mahad-urugāyaṁ pratiṣṭhāṁ dṛṣṭvā*  
*dhṛtyā dhīro naciketo’tyasrākṣīḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Naciketā! Seeing the enjoyments achievements from karmas and seeing the infinite shore of fearlnessness, the Lord fixed in great, praiseworthy qualities, you, being wise, have rejected temporary enjoyments with determination.**

**Комментарий:**

He describes the qualification of Naciketā for hearing.

Seeing the attainment of desires of things up to enjoyment on Brahma-loka which arise as a result of karmas (kratoḥ pratiṣṭhām), and seeing the steadiness of great praiseworthy qualities in the state of liberation which is indestructible, fearless, sinless, and satya-saṅkalpa, you, being wise (dhiraḥ), have rejected material desires. Or kāmasyāptiṁ jagataḥ pratiṣṭhāṁ krator ānantyam can mean “seeing that the Lord, the form of liberation and fulfilment of all desires, who is the support for the whole world and who gives infinite results from worship.”

### 1.2.12

*taṁ durdarśaṁ gūḍham anupraviṣṭaṁ*  
*guhāhitaṁ gahvareṣṭhaṁ purāṇam \|*  
*adhyātma-yogādhigamena devaṁ*  
*matvā dhīro harṣa-śokau jahāti \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Understanding the Lord who is difficult to see, who is invisible to materialists, who has entered all beings, who resides in the heart, who is the jīva’s antaryāmī, and who is without beginning-- by realizing the jīva--the wise man gives up joy and sorrow.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses answer the third question. Just as it was said that rare to hear about the Lord, it is rare or difficult to see him (durdarśam). He is invisible because of the avidyā in the form of karma, which obscures (gūḍham). He has entered all beings (anupraviṣṭham). He resides in the heart (guhāhitam). He is the soul of the jīva (gahvareṣṭham). He is without beginning (purāṇam). One understands the Lord (devam) by realization of the jīvātmā (adhyātma-yogādhigamena). Later it will be said:

*yacched vāṅ-manasī prājñas tad yacchej jñāna ātmani \|*  
*jñānam ātmani mahati niyacchet tad yacchec chānta ātmani \|\|*

**The wise man should control the senses such as voice by the mind and control the mind by the intelligence. He should control the intelligence by the ātmā, and control the ātmā by the Lord within. Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.1.13**

*yadā pañcāvatiṣṭhante jñānāni manasā saha \|*  
*buddhiś ca na viceṣṭate tām āhuḥ paramāṁ gatim \|\|*

**When the five senses along with the mind are settled and the intelligence does not wander, they call this the highest goal. Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.3.10**

Knowledge of jīva is the cause of knowledge of the Lord. One gives up joy on attaining material objects and sorrow on losing them.

### 1.2.13

*etac chrutvā samparigṛhya martyaḥ*  
*pravṛhya dharmyam aṇum etam āpya \|*  
*sa modate modanīyaṁ hi labdhvā*  
*vivṛtaṁ sadma naciketasaṁ manye \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing about the ātmā, contemplating it, giving up the Lord produced by karma, the human being attains the Lord who is most subtle. Having obtained his svarūpa which is the object of the Lord’s affection, he becomes blissful. I think that the spiritual world is open to Naciketā.**

**Комментарий:**

Hearing about ātma-tattva and contemplating it (samparigṛhya), giving up (pravṛhya) the body produced by karma (dharmyam), he attains Paramātmā who is most subtle, invisible to the material eye (aṇum), who is the jīva’s very self, in a special place. The Lord was already described as aṇiyān anuṁ hy atarkyam.

This person in knowledge (saḥ), having attained his own svarūpa endowed with qualities like freedom from sin, a form which is the object of affection (modanīyam), becomes blissful (modate).

*eṣa saṁprasādo ‘smāc charīrāt samutthāya paraṁ jyotir upasamadya sevena rūpeṇābhiniṣpadyate*

**The liberated soul rises from the body, reaches the supreme light and becomes endowed with his own form. Chāndogya Upaniṣad**

*sa tatra paryeti jakṣan krīḍan ramamāṇaḥ*

**The liberated jīva moves, eats, and plays in the liberated state. Chāndogya Upaniṣad**

He then praises Naciketā as being qualified for liberation. I think that the abode of Brahman (sadma) is open (vivṛtam) for Naciketā.

*tasyaiṣa ātmā viśati brahma-dhāma*

**The Lord or** ***brahma-dhāma*** **appears to the wise man. Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad**

### 1.2.14

*anyatra dharmād anyatrādharmād*  
*anyatrāsmāt kṛtākṛtāt \|*  
*anyatra bhūtāc ca bhavyāc ca*  
*yat tat paśyasi tad vada \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Naciketā spoke.) Tell me what you see to be different from the method, different from the attainment, and different from the aspirant, what is done and not done, in the past and in the future.**

**Комментарий:**

Naciketā asks a question in order to clarify the nature of the Lord, the nature of the sādhana and the nature of the jīva, who attains the Lord, which were mentioned in the previous two verses.

Tell me what you see to be different from the well-known method of attainment (anyatra dharmāt). Dharma here means method. And tell me what you see is different from the result, which is different from the method (adharmāt anyatra). Tell me what is different from the sādhaka (asmāt). He is different from the performer of sacrifices, since he is detached from material results in the state of sādhana, and has a svarūpa endowed with spiritual qualities when he has attained the result.

Tell me what is different from the methods, attainments and sādhakas which are done and not done, and what is different from them in the past and future.

### 1.2.15

*sarve vedā yat padam āmananti*  
*tapāṁsi sarvāṇi ca yad vadanti \|*  
*yad icchanto brahmacaryaṁ caranti*  
*tat te padaṁ saṁgraheṇa bravīmy om ity etat \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**(Death spoke.) I will speak to you about the Lord whom all the Vedas glorify, whom all the Upaniṣads explain, and desiring whom the aspirants perform brahmacarya. The Lord is embodied in oṁ.**

**Комментарий:**

Death explains in summary the glories of the attainment of the Lord, in order to produce respect in the listener, before explaining the pure ātmā in verse 18. Padam here refers to the svarūpa of what is attained. All the Vedas explain directly or indirectly this svarūpa of the Lord. This confirms that the subject matter of this Upaniṣad is the supreme Lord, though the purified jīva is described in verse 18 and 19 and in many verses of the Gītā.

I speak to you about this padam, the Lord. This is the promise to explain the Lord who is explained in the Vedas and who is the antaryāmī of the jīva in his pure svarūpa. Tapāṁsi, later portions of the Vedas (Upaniṣads), speak of the Lord. Desiring the Lord, people observe brahmacārya—living in the gurukula and avoiding association with women. I will speak in summary (saṁgraheṇa), in the word oṁ. Praṇava or oṁ means the supreme Lord.

### 1.2.16

*etad dhy evākṣaraṁ brahma etad dhyevākṣaraṁ param \|*  
*etad dhy evākṣaraṁ jñātvā yo yad icchati tasya tat \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This syllable is Brahman. This syllable is the best for meditation. Meditating on this syllable one attains whatever one desires.**

**Комментарий:**

Praṇava or oṁ is praised in two verses.

This syllable is Brahman because it is the sādhana or object of meditation to attain Brahman. It is the best syllable among all objects of japa or meditation (akṣaram param). By meditating (jñātvā) on this syllable, whatever one desires manifests.

### 1.2.17

*etad ālambanaṁ śreṣṭham etad ālambanaṁ param \|*  
*etad ālambanaṁ jñātvā brahma-loke mahīyate \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Praṇava is the best support for meditation. Meditation using praṇava is the best. Knowing about this support, one becomes glorified in the spiritual world.**

**Комментарий:**

This syllable is the best support (ālambanam) for meditation. Meditation using the syllable is the best meditation.

### 1.2.18

*na jāyate mriyate vā vipaścin*  
*nāyaṁ kutaścin na babhūva kaścit \|*  
*ajo nityaḥ śāśvato’yaṁ purāṇo*  
*na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The conscious jīva is not born and does not die. He did not come from anywhere and was not born. He is without birth, eternal, continual and ancient by his nature. He is not killed when the body is killed.**

**Комментарий:**

This and the next verse describe the jīva. The two mantras are taken together since both describe killing. The killer (hantā) refers to the jīva only, not the Lord. Being killer or being killed cannot never be applied to the Lord. The conceptions of “I kill this person. This person desires to kill me” belong to the jīvas in material bodies only. Like the jīva, the Lord cannot be killed. However the Lord, though situated in the cavity of the heart, is never subject to transformations and is never mistaken into thinking of being a killer or being killed. Thus these two verses describe the jīva. That conscious entity (vipaścit) has no birth and death even now in this body. He does not come from anywhere: he is devoid of a source of birth. He is devoid of birth previously also as human or other forms.

The cause of the jīva not taking birth is stated. He is ajaḥ—by nature unborn. The cause of not dying is stated. He is nityaḥ—by nature eternal. The cause of not having an origin is stated. He is śāśvata—he continually exists. The cause of not having birth previously is stated. He is purāṇa—he is ancient. How is he eternal? Residing in a body, the jīva is not killed thought the body is killed.

### 1.2.19

*hantā cen manyate hantuṁ hataś cen manyate hatam \|*  
*ubhau tau na vijānīto nāyaṁ hanti na hanyate \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one thinks he is a killer or thinks that he has been killed when his body is killed, he does not know the nature of ātmā. The ātmā does not kill another ātmā and is not killed.**

**Комментарий:**

This is further explained. If a person, identifying with the body, thinks, “I will kill that person,” or if a person thinks on being killed, “I have been killed,” those two do not know the svarūpa of the ātmā. He does not kill the ātmā. The ātmā is not killed (na hanyate).

### 1.2.20

*aṇor aṇīyān mahato mahīyān*  
*ātmā’sya jantor nihito guhāyām \|*  
*tam akratuḥ paśyati vīta-śoko*  
*dhātu-prasādān mahimānam ātmanaḥ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is smaller than the small jīva and greater than the greatest. He resides within the jīva. When, by the mercy of the Lord, the jīva sees the glory of the Lord, who is devoid of kāmya-karmas, he becomes free of lamentation.**

**Комментарий:**

Having described the jīva, now the Lord who manifests in the jīva is described. The Lord is more subtle (anīyān) than the subtle jīva (aṇoḥ). Thus the Lord is capable of entering the jīva. He is greater than the ākāśa (mahataḥ), being all-pervading. This ātmā enters within the jīva (asya jantoḥ) described in the previous two mantras as its monitor. This ātmā is different from the jīva described in those mantras. When the jīva sees the greatness of Lord devoid of kāmya-karmas (akratuḥ) by the mercy of the Lord (dhātuḥ prasādāt), the jīva gives up lamentation.

*prasīdaty acyutas tasmin prasanne kleśa-saṁkṣayaḥ*

**When the Lord is pleased or merciful, all sufferings of the jīva are destroyed. Viṣṇu Purāṇa.**

### 1.2.21

*āsīno dūraṁ vrajati śayāno yāti sarvataḥ \|*  
*kas taṁ madāmadaṁ devaṁ mad-anyo jñātum arhati \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He is seated and walks far away. He is lying down and goes everywhere. Who except me can know this Lord endowed with contrary qualities like joy and anger?**

**Комментарий:**

The person devoid of the Lord’s mercy cannot understand the true nature of the Lord, since the Lord is most extraordinary. Though sitting and walking far, or lying down and everywhere appear contradictory, these qualities manifest through the jīvas, since the Lord dwells within them. Who can know the Lord (devam), having contrary qualities like joy and anger (madāmadam) other than me, who has received his mercy.

### 1.2.22

*aśarīraṁ śarīreṣv anavastheṣv avasthitam \|*  
*mahāntaṁ vibhum ātmānaṁ matvā dhīro na śocati \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing the Lord who has no material body, who is situated in all temporary bodies, who is endowed with great powers and is all-pervading, the wise man does not lament.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is without a body created by karma (aśarīram) but situated in all temporary bodies. Knowing this Lord who is endowed with great powers (mahantam) who is all-pervading (vibhum), the wise man does not lament.

### 1.2.23

*nāyam ātmā pravacanena labhyo*  
*na medhayā na bahunā śrutena \|*  
*yam evaiṣa vṛṇute tena labhyas*  
*tasyaiṣa ātmā vivṛṇute tanūṁ svām \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord cannot be attained by mere contemplation, or by meditation or by plentiful hearing. He is attained by a person whom the Lord chooses. The Lord then reveals himself to that devotee.**

**Комментарий:**

The method of attaining the Lord is described.

The Lord cannot be attained by contemplation (pravacanena). The Lord (eṣaḥ) is attained by the person whom the Lord seeks or desires (vṛṇute). This means that the Lord seeks the person who is very dear to him. The Lord holds him dearly because that devotee has affection for the Lord. The affection of the devotee for the Lord makes the Lord affectionate to the devotee. That produces attainment of the Lord. The Lord (ātmā) reveals his svarūpa (svām tanum) to that devotee. This means he gives himself to the devotee.

### 1.2.24

*nāvirato duścaritān nāśānto nāsamāhitaḥ \|*  
*nāśānta-mānaso vāpi prajñānenainam āpnuyāt \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who does not give up improper conduct, who has not controlled his anger and lust, who cannot concentrate, and who cannot control his mind, cannot attain the Lord by knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

Qualities that should be incorporate in the worship to attain the Lord are taught. One who has not desisted from acts like taking other’s wives or properties, who has not controlled lust and anger (aśāntaḥ), one whose mind is inattentive, one whose mind is uncontrolled (aśānta-mānasaḥ), cannot attain the Lord by knowledge. Not engaging in bad actions is prescribed as an aṅga of the meditation to attain the highest goal, just as not lying is prescribed as an aṅga of performing sacrifices One may desire to perform faulty worship of the Lord, disregarding the forbiddance of bad conduct, but one cannot actually produce proper worship by improperly performing the aṅga of forbidding bad conduct.

### 1.2.25

*yasyā brahma ca kṣatraṁ ca ubhe bhavata odanaḥ \|*  
*mṛtyur yasyopasecanaṁ ka itthā veda yatra saḥ \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Who knows how the Lord, who destroys all beings and has death as his relish, is situated with his variegated qualities?**

**Комментарий:**

The brāhmaṇa and kṣatriya represent the whole universe composed of moving and nonmoving entities. They become the food (odanaḥ) for the Lord. This means that everything in the universe is destroyed by the Lord. Death is the relish (upasecanam). This means that death is also destroyed, but death is also the cause of others being eaten. Who knows in which manner that destroyer of all beings is situated, endowed with various qualities (yatra ittham)? Taking the literal meaning that the Lord eats brāhmaṇas and kṣatriyas does not make sense. Odanaḥ means destroying, enjoying or eating. The Lord is not just the destroyer of brāhmaṇas and kṣatriyas, or of certain living beings. One should not claim that this is a meditation for the Lord to destroy brāhmaṇas and kṣatriyas, since the Lord would not do such a thing. Thus brāhmaṇas and kṣatriyas means all living beings.

## Chapter 3

*prathamādhyāye tṛtīyā vallī*

### 1.3.1

*ṛtaṁ pibantau sukṛtasya loke*  
*guhāṁ praviṣṭau parame parārdhe \|*  
*chāyātapau brahma-vido vadanti*  
*pañcāgnayo ye ca tri-ṇāciketāḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The knowers of Brahman who serve the five fires and perform the three Nāciketa sacrifices speak of the jīva and the Lord who involved in the results of karma, who have entered the heart, the most excellent space, on the planet for accruing sukṛti, and who are ignorance and knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

The last verse stated that the Lord is hard to understand. Thus it may be objected that we cannot meditate on the Lord. Two verses now explain that we can meditate on the Lord since the Lord is situated with the jīva in the heart.

The two have entered the heart and are present on this planet for accomplishing good deeds (sukṛtasya loke), for experiencing the inevitable results of karma (ṛtam pibantau), in the most excellent space (parārdhe parame) in the heart.

However, only the jīva experiences the results of karma. The Lord inspires the jīva to experience his karmas. Chāya refers to the jīva who is ignorant, and tāpa or light refers to the Lord who is in knowledge. One should not object that the Lord should not be situated in the body or chariot, since he is the object of attainment for the jīva.

Though the Lord is situated in the body, he remains the object of attainment since the jīva is covered by ignorance in the form of karma, arranged by the Lord, and thus does not realize the Lord. And thus they are both situated in the heart within the body, with the jīva as the attainer and the Lord as the object of attainment.

Those who are purified by service to the five fires and have performed the three nāciketa sacrifices speak of the two.

### 1.3.2

*yaḥ setur ījānānām akṣaraṁ brahma yat param \|*  
*abhayaṁ titīrṣatāṁ pāraṁ nāciketaṁ śakemahi \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May we be able to meditate upon the Lord who is the giver of results for the sacrifices, who is the unchanging supreme Brahman, who is the steady bank for those desiring to cross saṁsāra and who is attainable by the nāciketa sacrifice.**

**Комментарий:**

Let us meditate upon the Lord who is the support (setuḥ) for those who are performing sacrifice. This means he is the giver of the results of sacrifice. He is the supreme Brahma which is unchanging (akṣaram). He is the firm or fearless (abhayam) bank for those desiring to cross saṁsāra. The Lord is he who is attained by the nāciketa fire (nāciketam). One should not fear that it is too difficult to meditate upon the Lord.

### 1.3.3

*ātmānaṁ rathinaṁ viddhi śarīraṁ ratham eva tu \|*  
*buddhiṁ tu sārathiṁ viddhi manaḥ pragraham eva ca \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Know that the jīva is the passenger, and that the body is the chariot. Know that intelligence is the driver and the mind is the reins.**

**Комментарий:**

Teaching the sādhana to attain the Lord on the other bank of saṁsāra, verses 3 to 9 first teach the nature of the attainer, the jīva. Know the jīva, the chariot passenger, who rules the body. Know the body which is the chariot. Intelligence is the charioteer since it makes the body function by its decisions. Mind is the rains (pragraham).

### 1.3.4

*indriyāṇi hayāny āhur viṣayāṁs teṣu gocarān \|*  
*ātmendriya-mano-yuktaṁ bhoktety āhur manīṣiṇaḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They say the senses are the horses and the sense objects are the paths. The wise say that the jīva endowed with body, senses, mind and intelligence becomes the experiencer.**

**Комментарий:**

The senses are the horses. The senses objects are the paths (gocarān) for the horses. In the absence of the body, senses, mind and intelligence, the jīva, the passenger, cannot perform either worldly or Vedic actions. Endowed with body (ātmā) senses, mind (and intelligence), they call the jīva the enjoyer. The jīva cannot act or experience alone.

### 1.3.5

*yas tv avijñānavān bhavaty ayuktena manasā sadā \|*  
*tasyendriyāṇy avaśyāni duṣṭāśvā iva sāratheḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The ignorant person with a constantly uncontrolled mind has uncontrolled senses, like a charioteer with wild horses.**

**Комментарий:**

The purpose of the metaphor is explained in two verses.

### 1.3.6

*yas tu vijñānavān bhavati yuktena manasā sadā \|*  
*tasyendriyāṇi vaśyāni sad-aśvā iva sāratheḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who has knowledge along with a controlled mind at all times controls his senses, just like a skilful driver with obedient horses.**

**Комментарий:**

A skilful driver, holding the reins, is able to control the horses. Person with proper intelligence and mind is able to control his senses. It cannot be otherwise.

### 1.3.7

*yas tv avijñānavān bhavaty amanaskaḥ sadā’śuciḥ \|*  
*na sa tat-padam āpnoti saṁsāraṁ cādhigacchati \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who is ignorant, with uncontrolled mind and opposite thoughts, does not attain the goal and enters saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses show the result of not controlling the senses or horses. Amanaskaḥ means uncontrolled mind. Aśuciḥ means always thinking opposite. Not only does the person not attain the desire goal but attains the forest of saṁsāra.

### 1.3.8

*yas tu vijñānavān bhavati sa-manaskaḥ sadā śuciḥ \|*  
*sa tu tat-padam āpnoti yasmād bhūyo na jāyate \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who has knowledge, who controls his mind and concentrates his thoughts, attains the goal, by which he does not take birth again.**

### 1.3.9

*vijñāna-sārathir yas tu manaḥ pragrahavān naraḥ \|*  
*so’dhvanaḥ pāram āpnoti tad viṣṇoḥ paramaṁ padam \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who has good intelligence as his driver and controlled mind as his reins crosses saṁsāra and obtains the supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

What is that goal or padam? That is explained in this verse. He who has proper knowledge and controlled mind attains the Lord beyond saṁsāra (adhvanaḥ pāram).

### 1.3.10

*indriyebhyaḥ parā hy arthā arthebhyaś ca paraṁ manaḥ \|*  
*manasas tu parā buddhir buddher ātmā mahān paraḥ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sense objects are more important to control than the senses. The mind is more important, and the intelligence even more important. But more important is the great ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

The chief of factors that have to be controlled are listed in two verses. The sense objects are more important for controlling than the senses. Even if one controls the senses it is difficult to control the senses when close to sense objects. The mind is more important than the sense objects however. The mind becomes uncontrolled even in the absence of sense objects, if it is inclined to the sense objects. Intelligence is more important in this regard than the mind. But the ātmā is more important since it is the doer. Everything happens by the desire of the ātmā which is thus considered great (mahān).

### 1.3.11

*mahataḥ param avyaktam avyaktāt puruṣaḥ paraḥ \|*  
*puruṣān na paraṁ kiṁcit sā kāṣṭhā sā parā gatiḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Superior to the ātmā is the body and superior to the body is the supreme Lord. There is nothing superior to the Lord. He is the highest goal and highest attainment.**

**Комментарий:**

More important than the ātmā is the body (avyaktam), since all the jīva’s goals must take place through the body. But more important than the body is the Lord, antaryāṁī, the goal of the path, since everything including the ātmā depend on the will of the Lord. He is the inspiration for the worshipper. The ātmā as doer depends on the Lord.

*parāt tu tacchruteḥ*

**The jīva's ability as an agent arises from the Supreme Lord, since this is stated in the śruti. Vedānta sūtra 2.3.39**

The Lord is the highest object of attainment (parāgatiḥ) and the goal of the method of worship in which control of senses, etc. are employed.

### 1.3.12

*eṣa sarveṣu bhūteṣu gūḍho’’tmā na prakāśate \|*  
*dṛśyate tv agryayā buddhyā sūkṣmayā sūkṣma-darśibhiḥ \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, residing hidden within all beings, does not manifest himself. However he is seen by concentrated intelligence which has the ability to perceive the spiritual, by persons with spiritual vision.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord resides as the ātmā within all beings but is hidden. He is not revealed to those who have not conquered their internal and external senses, being hidden from them by the three guṇas of māyā. He can be seen by intelligence which is not disturbed by internal or external factors, and which has the ability to discern subtle objects, by person having subtle vision.

### 1.3.13

*yacched vāṅ-manasī prājñas tad yacchej jñāna ātmani \|*  
*jñānam ātmani mahati niyacchet tad yacchec chānta ātmani \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The wise man should control the senses such as voice by the mind and control the mind by the intelligence. He should control the intelligence by the ātmā, and control the ātmā by the Lord within.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse shows the method of becoming devoid of the actions of internal and external senses and shows the method of attaining knowledge of the svarūpa of the jīva as expressed in adhyātma-yogadhigamena. One should regulate the action and knowledge senses with the mind. The wise man should control the mind by the intelligence (jñāne) present in the ātmā (ātmani). One should control the intelligence by the great ātmā which is the doer. He should control the ātmā by the antaryāmī (ātmani) who combats the six material urges (śānte). The ātmā realizes his dependence on the Lord.

### 1.3.14

*uttiṣṭhata jāgrata prāpya varān nibodhata \|*  
*kṣurasya dhārā niśitā duratyayā*  
*durgaṁ pathas tat kavayo vadanti \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Stand up! Wake up! Approaching ācāryas, understand about the Lord. The blade of razor is sharp, and impossible to tread on. The jñānīs say that the path of understanding ātmā is also difficult to tread.**

**Комментарий:**

Having taught how to control the senses and mind, the speaker faces the qualified people. Arise! Become interested in ātma-tattva. Wake up! Destroy the sleep of ignorance. Approaching ācāryas, learn ātma-tattva. Or assuming excellent forms (for learning), understand ātma-tattva. The jñānīs describe ātma-tattva as a very difficult path to walk. The razor blade is very sharp (niśitā), impossible to tread on (duratyayā). Just as a person on sharp razor blade dies if he is inattentive, in the stage of understanding the nature of ātmā, one is destroyed by the offense of inattention, even in dreams.

### 1.3.15

*aśabdam asparśam arūpam avyayaṁ*  
*tathā’rasaṁ nityam agandhavac ca yat \|*  
*anādy anantaṁ mahataḥ paraṁ dhruvaṁ*  
*nicāyya tan mṛtyu-mukhāt pramucyate \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing the Lord, eternally devoid of material sound, touch, form, taste and smell, without change, who is without beginning or end and who is fixed and superior to the jīva, one becomes free of fearful saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse summarizes. The word nitya modifies each of the other descriptive words. Because of these qualities the Lord is unchanging (svayam) like time. Or the Lord is without decay (svayam). Mahatma refers to the jīva. Seeing (nyāya), through meditation, the Lord who is fixed, who is superior to the jīva, who is without beginning or end, one becomes free of frightful saṁsāra (mṛtyu-mukhāt).

### 1.3.16

*nāciketam upākhyānaṁ mṛtyu-proktaṁ sanātanam \|*  
*uktvā śrutvā ca medhāvī brahma-loke mahīyate \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The intelligent person, narrating or hearing the eternal story concerning Naciketā, which was related by death, is glorified in the spiritual world.**

**Комментарий:**

The story concerning Naciketā was related by death, but he was not an independent speaker. The story is thus eternal (sanātanam). Since it has no human author, it is eternal, like a river.

### 1.3.17

*ya imaṁ paramaṁ guhyaṁ śrāvayed brahma-saṁsadi \|*  
*prayataḥ śrāddha-kāle vā tad ānantyāya kalpate \|\|17\|\|*  
*tad ānantyāya kalpata iti \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The pure person who makes this highest secret heard in an assembly of brāhmaṇas or during śrāddha rites is qualified for infinite rewards.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahma-saṁsadi means “in an assembly of brāhmaṇas.” Prayataḥ means pure.

## Part 2

*dvitīyo’dhyāyaḥ prathamā vallī*

## Chapter 1

### 2.1.1

*parāñci khāni vyatṛṇat svayambhūs*  
*tasmāt parān paśyati nāntarātman \|*  
*kaścid dhīraḥ pratyag-ātmānam aikṣad*  
*āvṛtta-cakṣur amṛtatvam icchan \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord condemned the senses which move outward. Therefore one sees external objects, not the inner ātmā. Some excellent person, with senses withdrawn from external objects, desiring immortality, sees the ātmā within.**

**Комментарий:**

Seeing persons disinterested in the ātmā after telling them to wake up, the speaker laments. Parāñci khāni means the senses which move (añcati) to objects outside. The senses reveal other things, and not the ātmā. Giving the reason, he laments. The independent Lord (svayambhuḥ) broke into pieces or created (vitṛṇat) the senses.

Because of that (tasmāt) one sees external forms which are unrelated to the ātmā and does not see the ātmā within. Or facing outward, he sees sense objects only. Though people are of this nature, some excellent person is inclined to the ātmā. He sees the ātmā within. The verb has a present tense meaning. Cakṣuḥ (eye) stands for all the senses. The person who has withdrawn all his senses from their objects (āvṛta-cakṣuḥ) sees the ātmā.

### 2.1.2

*parācaḥ kāmān anuyanti bālās*  
*te mṛtyor yanti vitatasya pāśam \|*  
*atha dhīrā amṛtatvaṁ viditvā*  
*dhruvam adhruveṣv iha na prārthayante \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The foolish people pursue external objects with desire and end up in the noose of continual ignorance. However, the wise, knowing the eternal nature of the Lord, do not pray for impermanent objects in this world.**

**Комментарий:**

The unintelligent pursue external objects with desire and end up in the bondage of extensive (vitatasya) saṁsāra (mṛtyoḥ pāśam). Or they end up in the noose of uninterrupted ignorance. However (atha) the wise persons, knowing the eternal nature of the ātmā, do not pray for impermanent objects (adhruveṣu) in the material world (iha). One who knows about ātmā rejects everything else. Paramātmā is the pratyāg ātmā since he is within all jīvas and is the chief being with the sense of I.

### 2.1.3

*yena rūpaṁ rasaṁ gandhaṁ śabdān sparśāṁś ca maithunān \|*  
*etenaiva vijānāti kim atra pariśiṣyate \| etad vai tat \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**What more remains to be revealed by the Lord, by whom one knows form, taste, smell, sound, touch and sexual pleasure? Viṣṇu is that ātmā within.**

**Комментарий:**

Maitunān means the special pleasures arising from sexual encounters. By the Lord alone one knows form, taste, fragrance, sound, touch and the pleasures of sex. This is because, by the mercy of the Lord, all the senses perform their functions of revealing form etc. What remains to be revealed by the Lord (kim atra pariśiṣyate)? What was previously indicated as the attainment (etad), the supreme position of Viṣṇu is the ātmā described in this verse.

### 2.1.4

*svapnāntaṁ jāgaritāntaṁ cobhau yenānupaśyati \|*  
*mahāntaṁ vibhum ātmānaṁ matvā dhīro na śocati \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meditating on the great, all-pervading Lord, the soul of all beings, by whom people see the world of dreams and waking state, the wise man does not lament.**

**Комментарий:**

People see the world in dreams and the world in waking state by agency of the Lord. Meditating on that great Lord (mahāntam), the wise man does not lament.

### 2.1.5

*ya imaṁ madhvadaṁ veda ātmānaṁ jīvam antikāt \|*  
*īśānaṁ bhūta-bhavyasya na tato vijugupsate \| etad vai tat \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should not criticize the person who knows the jīva, who enjoys the fruits of karma and, who knows, next to him, the supreme Lord of everything in past, present future. Viṣṇu is that supreme ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

Madhvadam means eating sweetness, the enjoyer. It refers to the jīva, who eats the fruit of karma. One should also know the jīva and near him the Lord of all conscious and unconscious entities in past, present and future. One should not criticize that person even if he commits sinful acts.

### 2.1.6

*yaḥ pūrvaṁ tapaso jātam adbhyaḥ pūrvam ajāyata \|*  
*guhāṁ praviśya tiṣṭhantaṁ yo bhūtebhir vyapaśyata \| etad vai tat \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, first created Brahmā who was born from the Lord’s will, from the waters. Entering into his heart, he glanced at Brahmā endowed with the senses, elements and antaḥkaraṇa. Viṣṇu is that person.**

**Комментарий:**

*apa eva sasarjādau tāsu vīryam avāsṛjat \|\|*  
*tad aṇḍam abhavad dhaimaṁ sahasrāṁśusama-prabham \|*  
*tasmiñ jajñe svayaṁ brahmā sarvalokapitāmahaḥ \|\|*

**He first created water and impregnated that water with his semen. That became a golden sphere shining like the sun. In that egg, Brahmā, the grandfather of all planets was born. Manu-smṛti**

The Lord first created Brahmā, who was born by his will (tapasaḥ) from the waters, previous to the creation of other individuals. The Lord glanced upon Brahmā endowed with the material elements, senses and antaḥkaraṇa (bhūtebhiḥ), saying “Become the creator of the whole universe.”

### 2.1.7

*yā prāṇena sambhavaty aditir devatā-mayī \|*  
*guhāṁ praviśya tiṣṭhantī yā bhūtebhir vyajāyata \| etad vai tat \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva, who enjoys through the senses and exists with the prāṇas, who resides in the heart on entering it, and who manifests with various forms along with the elements, has the Lord as his ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

*guhāṁ praviṣṭāv ātmānau hi tad-darśanāt*

**The two beings in the space within the heart are the jīva and the Lord because that is described elsewhere. Vedānta-sūtra 1.2.11**

That enjoyer of results of karma (aditiḥ), enjoying through the senses (devatā-mayī) which exists with the prāṇas, which resides in the heart, having entered it, and who and manifests into many forms like devatās (vyajāyata) along with the material elements (bhūtebhiḥ), has the Lord as his ātmā (etad vai tat).

### 2.1.8

*araṇyor nihito jāta-vedā garbha iva subhṛto garbhiṇībhiḥ \|*  
*dive dive īḍyo jāgṛvadbhir haviṣmadbhir manuṣyebhir agniḥ \| etad vai tat \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Fire situated between two araṇis, like a fetus protected by women, is praised daily by alter priests offering oblations. That fire has the Lord as its ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

Fire (jāta-veda) is situated between two araṇis like the fetus held in pregnant women. It is praised daily by alert people (jāgṛvadbhiḥ), by priests making oblations (haviṣmadbhiḥ). That fire has the Lord as its ātmā (etad vai tat).

### 2.1.9

*yataś codeti sūryo’staṁ yatra ca gacchati \|*  
*taṁ devāḥ sarve’rpitās tad u nātyeti kaścana \| etad vai tat \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**From the Lord the sun manifests and in him it disappears. All the devatās are dependent on him. Nothing can surpass the Lord. Viṣṇu is that Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

From Brahman, the sun arises and in him (yatra) it disappears (astaṁ gacchati). All the devatās are dependent on him (arpitāḥ). No one surpasses the Lord, who is the ātmā of all beings. The jīvas are like shadows in comparison to the Lord.

### 2.1.10

*yad eveha tad amutra yad amutra tad anv iha \|*  
*mṛtyoḥ sa mṛtyum āpnoti ya iha nāneva paśyati \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, who exists in this world in various bodies, also exists in bodies in other worlds. He who sees difference in the Lord who pervades everything goes from body to body.**

**Комментарий:**

“The Lord cannot be the ātmā of all beings. The ātmā, identifying itself as I, is aware of "I exist at this place.” This excludes him being in other places. How can the Lord be the ātmā of all things, existing all places at all times?” There is no difference in the Lord as ātmā in different places.

The Paramātmā who (yat) identifies himself in this world (iha) in one place, as the ātmā within, is also the ātmā within all beings in other worlds (amutra). He who sees difference in Paramātmā goes from body to body in saṁsāra.

### 2.1.11

*manasaivedam āptavyaṁ neha nānā’sti kiṁcana \|*  
*mṛtyoḥ sa mṛtyuṁ gacchati ya iha nāneva paśyati \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By the mind alone, one can realize this. There is no difference in the Lord spread everywhere. He who sees difference goes from body to body.**

**Комментарий:**

How do we realize that the Lord is the ātmā of all beings?

This tattva can be understood by the purified mind (manasā). The previous statement is repeated to give emphasis.

### 2.1.12

*aṅguṣṭha-mātraḥ puruṣo madhya ātmani tiṣṭhati \|*  
*īśāno bhūta-bhavyasya na tato vijugupsate \| etad vai tat \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, the size of a thumb, exists within the bodies of the jīvas. Being the lord of all beings, in the past and future, he does not find fault in them. This person is the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord who is the size of a thumb, is the controller of all conscious and conscious entities in past, present and future. He resides within the body of the worshipper (madhye ātmani). He does not see faults in the jīvas within their bodies since he is their master and has affection for them (tataḥ na vijugupsate).

### 2.1.13

*aṅguṣṭha-mātraḥ puruṣo jyotir ivādhūmakaḥ \|*  
*īśāno bhūta-bhavyasya sa evādya sa u śvaḥ \| etad vai tat \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, the size of a thumb, is radiant like fire without smoke. The Lord of the past and future is the ātmā of all things today and of all things tomorrow. Viṣṇu is that Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is radiant like fire arising from dry wood, without smoke. He is the ātmā of all things produced today and produced tomorrow (sa eva ādya sa u śvaḥ).

### 2.1.14

*yathodakaṁ durge vṛṣṭaṁ parvateṣu vidhāvati \|*  
*evaṁ dharmān pṛthak paśyaṁs tān evānuvidhāvati \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who sees difference in the Lord who is everywhere falls into saṁsāra, like rain water on top of the mountain flowing in various streams.**

**Комментарий:**

Rain water on the tops of mountains (durge) falling in various ways in various currents flows on the mountains (parvateṣu). If one sees the Lord who is the antaryāmī of devatās and the antaryāmī of humans to be different entities, he falls in the hole of saṁsāra, like the water gushing from the mountains.

### 2.1.15

*yathodakaṁ śuddhe śuddham āsiktaṁ tādṛg eva bhavati \|*  
*evaṁ muner vijānata ātmā bhavati gautama \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Gautama! Just as pure water joined with pure water is similar to the pure water, the ātmā of the person who meditates on the Lord is similar to the Lord in purity.**

**Комментарий:**

The result of knowledge that the Lord is the ātmā of everything is described.

Pure water mixed with pure water is similar to it. It does not become something different in quality. Similarly the ātmā of the person who meditates (vijānataḥ), purified by knowledge of the Lord, becomes similar to the pure Lord. O Gautama! He calls to him in great joy, to indicate the greatness of the attainment.

## Chapter 2

*dvitīyādhyāye dvitīyā vallī*

### 2.2.1

*puram ekādaśa dvāram ajasyāvakra-cetasaḥ \|*  
*anuṣṭhāya na śocati vimuktaś ca vimucyate \| etad vai tat \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva, unborn and possessing honest intelligence, has a body with eleven gates.**

**Understanding the body is different from the ātmā, freed from desire and suffering, the person becomes free of prakṛti. This ātmā has the Lord as his ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

The ātmā, without birth and with straight intelligence (avakra-cetasaḥ) has a body (puram) endowed with eleven doors, the senses. Just as a house is distinct from his owner, the body is understood to be separate from the ātmā. But the body is the ātmā for a person of no discrimination. Knowing (anuṣṭhāya) that the body is different from the ātmā, free from suffering and lust related to the body (vimuktaḥ), a person is free from relationship with prakṛti (vimucyate), after destroying prarābdha-karmas and going by the path of light to the spiritual world.

*bhogena tv itare kapayitva sampadyate*

**Having given up the gross and subtle bodies, he attains a body of an associate of the Lord for enjoyment. Vedānta-sūtra 4.1.19**

The jīva in liberated state has the Lord as his ātmā (etat vai tat).

### 2.2.2

*haṁsaḥ śuci-ṣad vasur āntarikṣa-sad*  
*dhotā vedi-ṣad atithir duroṇa-sat \|*  
*nṛṣad-vara-sad-ṛta-sad vyoma-sad*  
*abjā gojā ṛtajā adrijā ṛtaṁ bṛhat \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The brilliant sun, the wind in the sky, the fire in the fire pit, the guest in the house, he who dwells in humans and devatās and in Satya-loka, who exists in the spiritual sky, and whatever is produced from water, from earth, from sacrifices, and from mountains, has the supreme Brahman, the infinite truth, as its ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord as the ātmā of everything is reiterated. The sun (haṁsa) which is brilliant (śuci-ṣat), the wind (vasuḥ) in the sky (āntarikṣa-sat), the fire or priest who exists at the fire pit, the guest in the house (duroṇa-sat), he who exists in humans, he who exists in devatās (vara-sat), he who exists in Brahma-loka (ṛta-sat), he who exists in the spiritual sky (vyoma-sat), whatever is produced from water, whatever is produced from land (gojā), whatever is produced from yajña (ṛta-jā), such as Svarga, or whatever is produced from ether, whatever is produced from mountains (adri-jā), has Brahman, the form of unlimited truth, as its ātmā (ṛtam bṛhat).

### 2.2.3

*ūrdhvaṁ prāṇam unnayaty apānaṁ pratyag asyati \|*  
*madhye vāmanam āsīnaṁ viśve devā upāsate \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord raises the prāṇa upwards and forces the apāna downwards. The pure people meditate on the worshippable Lord seated in the lotus of the heart.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord, in the hearts of all beings, raises the prāṇa upwards. He forces the apāna downwards (pratyak). Those having qualities of sattva (viśve devā) meditate on the Lord worthy of worship (vāmanam) or he who is small (vāmanam) because of adjusting to the size of the heart, seated in the lotus of the heart (madhye).

### 2.2.4

*asya visraṁsamānasya śarīra-sthasya dehinaḥ \|*  
*dehād vimucyamānasya kim atra pariśiṣyate \| etad vai tat \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**What duty remains for the worshipper situated in a body whose limbs are slack or who is dying? He has the Lord as his ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

The worshipper of the Lord has no duties remaining until he leaves the body. Tasya tāvad eva ciraṁ yāvan na vimokṣye: as long as he has karma, he will not be liberated. With the exhaustion of karmas, he achieves the result. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad 6.14.2)

For the worshipper (asya) situated in his body (śarīra-sthasya), whose limbs are slack (visraṁsamānasya) or who is about to die, what remains here, since he has attained his goal?

### 2.2.5

*na prāṇena nāpānena martyo jīvati kaścana \|*  
*itareṇa tu jīvanti yasminn etāv upāśritau \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**No mortal being lives by prāṇa and apāṇa. They all live by the Lord upon whom the prāṇa and apāna are dependent.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is glorified as the life of all beings. All beings live by something else (itareṇa), on whom prāṇa and apāna are dependent, on whom all beings are dependent for their life.

### 2.2.6

*hanta ta idaṁ pravakṣyāmi guhyaṁ brahma sanātanam \|*  
*yathā ca maraṇaṁ prāpya ātmā bhavati gautama \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I will again speak to you about secret, eternal Brahman. O Gautama! I will speak again about what happens to the jīva on attaining liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

I will again speak to you about Brahman which is eternal and secret. Hanta indicates that this is astonishing for himself. O Gautama! I will again speak about the nature of the liberated jīva (maraṇaṁ prāpya) to you, who desire liberation, and are qualified for teachings, since you are free from attachments.

### 2.2.7

*yonim anye prapadyante śarīratvāya dehinaḥ \|*  
*sthāṇum anye’nusaṁyanti yathā-karma yathā-śrutam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Others attain births as brāhmaṇas etc. in this world in order to accept bodies. Others take bodies of immobile life forms, according to their karmas and what they have heard.**

**Комментарий:**

In order to explain about the liberated jīva, first he speaks of various qualifications.

Others (anye) who have no desire to hear about the Lord, unlike you, accept birth as brāhmaṇas etc. in order to take bodies. Others achieve immobile status (as trees etc.) according to what they have heard, not going beyond worship or actions that they perform.

*tad ya iha ramaṇīya-caraṇāḥ:* the jīva with good karmas attains a good birth. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad 5.10.7)

*taṁ vidyā-karmaṇī samanvārabhete pūrva-prajñā ca*

**Vidyā** **and karma as well as previous knowledge take hold of him. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad. 4.4.2)**

### 2.2.8

*ya eṣa supteṣu jāgarti kāmaṁ kāmaṁ puruṣo nirmimāṇaḥ \|*  
*tad eva śukraṁ tad brahma tad evāmṛtam ucyate \|*  
*tasmiṁl lokāḥ śritāḥ sarve tad u nātyeti kaścana \| etad vai tat \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord who is awake while the jīvas sleep, he who creates according to his will, is brilliant Brahman, and is called the ultimate immortal. All beings take shelter of him. No one surpasses him. He is the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Stimulating his disciple, getting his attention, he introduces the main topic. The word kāmam does not means objects like sons but has a verbal meaning. While the jīvas sleep, the Lord is awake, and exists while creating (nirmimānaḥ) as he pleases (kāmam kāmam). He is the revealer or shines (śukram) and is immortal. All other immortals are dependent on him. Thus even the nityamuktas are differentiated from the Lord. His immortality is absolute.

### 2.2.9

*agnir yathaiko bhuvanaṁ praviṣṭo*  
*rūpaṁ rūpaṁ pratirūpo babhūva \|*  
*ekas tathā sarva-bhūtāntarātmā*  
*rūpaṁ rūpaṁ pratirūpo bahiś ca \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as fire element, one entity, enters the universe and becomes various forms, so the one Lord, entering all beings, becomes many.**

**Комментарий:**

Since it is difficult to understand how the Lord is the self of all beings, it is again taught. The element fire enters the universe, pervades heaven, intermediate space and earth, and takes on many different forms. Since the forms are a combination which includes fire, they are called prati-rūpa. Similarly the one Lord, his form present in all objects, spreads externally as many forms.

### 2.2.10

*vāyur yathaiko bhuvanaṁ praviṣṭo*  
*rūpaṁ rūpaṁ pratirūpo babhūva \|*  
*ekas tathā sarva-bhūtāntarātmā*  
*rūpaṁ rūpaṁ pratirūpo bahiś ca \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as one air element enters the universe and becomes many forms, so the one Lord, entering all beings becomes many forms.**

**Комментарий:**

Another example is given.

### 2.2.11

*sūryo yathā sarva-lokasya cakṣuḥ*  
*na lipyate cākṣuṣair bāhya-doṣaiḥ \|*  
*ekas tathā sarva-bhūtāntarātmā*  
*na lipyate loka-duḥkhena bāhyaḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the sun, though within the eye of all beings is not contaminated by the fluids coming out of the eye, the one Lord, though within all beings, is not contained by sufferings of all beings, since he is unrelated.**

**Комментарий:**

Though the Lord dwells in beings like the jīva, he does not have faults like the jīva. An example is given. The sun, as the presiding deity of the eye, is within the eye, but it is not contaminated by the exudations that come out of the eye. Similarly the Lord, though present in all beings as antaryāmī, is not contaminated by their faults. He is different from the jīvas, since he is beyond everything else (bāhyaḥ), being endowed with qualities like apahata-pāpmā (free from karma and sin).

### 2.2.12

*eko vaśī sarva-bhūtāntarātmā*  
*ekaṁ rūpaṁ (bījam) bahudhā yaḥ karoti \|*  
*tam ātma-sthaṁ ye’nupaśyanti dhīrāḥ*  
*teṣāṁ sukhaṁ śāśvataṁ netareṣām \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who see the Lord situated within themselves, the Lord who is the one supreme, controlling the universe, the ātmā of all beings, who expands prakṛti into many forms, become happy in liberation and not others.**

**Комментарий:**

He has no equal or superior (ekaḥ). Vaśī means he who has desire. In cases like jagad-vaśe vartate it means he who controls the universe. Or it can mean he who is controlled by bhakti.

*imau sma muni-śārdula kiṁkarau samupasthitau*

**We two (rāma and his brother) have arrived to serve you (Viśvamitra). Rāmāyaṇa**

Ekam bījam means tamas (prakṛti). The Lord made tamas, which was merged in the Lord’s body, into many forms starting with mahattattva. Those who see the antaryāmī in themselves become liberated.

### 2.2.13

*nityo’nityānāṁ cetanaś cetanānāṁ*  
*eko bahūnāṁ yo vidadhāti kāmān \|*  
*tam ātmasthaṁ ye’nupaśyanti dhīrāḥ*  
*teṣāṁ śāntiḥ śāśvatī netareṣām \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who see the chief eternal conscious being, the Lord, who gives to all other eternal conscious beings what they desire, the Lord situated within themselves, attain eternal peace, and not others.**

**Комментарий:**

The one eternal conscious being easily gives all things that the other eternal conscious beings desire.

### 2.2.14

*tad etad iti manyante’nirdeśyaṁ paramaṁ sukham \|*  
*kathaṁ nu tad vijānīyāṁ kim u bhāti vibhāti vā \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The qualified understand that Brahman is this Lord, who is undefinable, and the supreme happiness. How can I know that Brahman? Does it reveal itself clearly or does not reveal itself unclearly?**

**Комментарий:**

The disciple speaks. The amazing Lord, endowed with a svarūpa of bliss known as Brahman (tat) is this (etat). Those who have perfected yoga, persons like you, think (manyante) in this way, perceiving Brahman like holding an amalaki in their hands. Persons like you can directly realize Brahman. How can I understand Brahman, since my mind cannot grasp Brahman which is formless? Does it shine brightly (bhāti)? Or does it reveal itself dimly (uta vibhāti) because of mixing with some other energy?

### 2.2.15

*na tatra sūryo bhāti na candra-tārakaṁ*  
*nemā vidyuto bhānti kuto’yam agniḥ \|*  
*tam eva bhāntam anubhāti sarvaṁ*  
*tasya bhāsā sarvam idaṁ vibhāti \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sun does not shine in his presence, nor the moon or stars. Lightning does not shine what to speak of fire. Everything shines following the Lord who shines. By his light everything shines.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord has an auspicious, attractive form that takes shelter in the heart for the accomplished yogīs.

*avikārāya śuddhāya nityāya parmātmane \|*  
*sadaika-rūpa-rūpāya viṣṇave sarva-jiṣṇave \|\|*

**I offer respects to the Lord Viṣṇu who is without change, pure, eternal, who is always in one form and who conquers all beings. Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.2.1**

*Āditya-varṇaṁ tamasaḥ parastāt*

**He has a complexion like the sun and is beyond darkness. Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad**

With that form, the Lord shines brightly. That is explained in this verse.

The Lord eclipses all other lights (first two lines). He is the cause of all other lights (third line). He is the cause and other lights are the effect (anubhāti). He gives mercy to all other lights (fourth line).

## Chapter 3

*dvitīyādhyāye tṛtīyā vallī*

### 2.3.1

*ūrdhva-mūlo’vāk-śākha eṣo’śvatthaḥ sanātanaḥ \|*  
*tad eva śukraṁ tad brahma tad evāmṛtam ucyate \|*  
*tasmiṁl lokāḥ śritāḥ sarve tad u nātyeti kaścana \|\| etad vai tat \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This world is an eternal pippala tree with its root above and its branches below. This world is said to have brilliant, immortal Brahman as its ātmā. All the worlds take shelter of the Lord. Nothing is beyond him. This world has the Lord as its ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

Since Brahmā who is the root of the universe is situated above the seven planets, the word is described as being a tree with the root on top. It has branches, ending with the humans, animals, worms, insects and plants on earth. This world has brilliant Brahman as its ātmā.

### 2.3.2

*yad idaṁ kiṁ ca jagat sarvaṁ prāṇa ejati niḥsṛtam \|*  
*mahad bhayaṁ vajram udyataṁ ya etad vidur amṛtās te bhavanti \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The whole which is situated in the Lord and which emanates from him, trembles out of great fear of the Lord who is like an upraised thunderbolt. Those who knows this become immortal.**

**Комментарий:**

Prāṇa refers to the Lord. All beings situated (this phrase should be added) in him (prāṇe) come out of him. They tremble in great fear of him. “What will happen if we transgress his orders?” The whole world trembles in fear as if from an upraised thunderbolt. That everything comes out of the Lord (niḥsṛtam) indicates that he is the upādāna, or material cause. Ejati means tremble. Everyone performs their duties out of fear of reaction. The universe trembles out of fear of the Lord, who is like a raised thunderbolt. The phrase mahad bhayaṁ vajram udyataṁ has an ablative meaning (from). Vajram udyatam refers to the greatness of parabrahman. Some take the word bhaya to indicate a fearful nature. The Lord makes the whole universe which arises from him, who is like a greatly fearful, raised thunderbolt. Those who know this become immortal. That prāṇā indicates the Lord is confirmed by ata eva prāṇaḥ: the *prāṇa* is Brahman**. (**Vedānta-sūtra 1.1.23)

### 2.3.3

*bhayād asyāgnis tapati bhayāt tapati sūryaḥ \|*  
*bhayād indraś ca vāyuś ca mṛtyur dhāvati pañcamaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Out of fear of the Lord fire burns and the sun shines. Out of fear of the Lord Indra, Vāyu and death, the fifth, perform their functions.**

**Комментарий:**

Dhāvati means that the senses perform their functions.

### 2.3.4

*iha ced aśakad boddhuṁ prāk-śarīrasya visrasaḥ \|*  
*tataḥ sargeṣu lokeṣu śarīratvāya kalpate \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one is unable to understand the Lord before dying, then one is qualified for taking more material bodies in the created worlds.**

**Комментарий:**

If one is unable to understand Brahman in this world (iha) before the body dies (visrasaḥ), because of not having knowledge (tataḥ), he becomes qualified for taking bodies possessing birth and death in the created worlds. Therefore, before one dies, one should endeavor for ātma-jñāna.

### 2.3.5

*yathā’’darśe tathā’’tmani yathā svapne tathā pitṛ-loke \|*  
*yathā’psu parīva dadṛśe tathā gandharva-loke*  
*chāyātapayor iva brahma-loke \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Perception of the Lord is unclear, like perception of an object on a moonless night, or perception of an object in a dream state, or on Pitṛ-loka. Just as objects in water are perceived unclearly, so objects in Gandharva-loka are perceived unclearly. In Brahma-loka also it is difficult to perceive the Lord, like seeing an object in a mixture of light and shadow.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse describes how the Lord is difficult to understand. On a moonless night (darśe) or in a mirror (ādarśe) objects are not clear. The perception of the Lord (ātmani) is also unclear. In the dream state one cannot eliminate doubts decisively because it is impossible to examine things again. On Pitṛ-loke it is the same. Objects under water are not clearly visible (paridadṛṣe). So on Gandharva-loka perception is momentary. One cannot perceive an object in a mixture of shade and light as one can clearly perceive an object in pure light. Even on Brahma-loka one cannot clearly perceive the Lord. Thus the Lord is difficult to understand.

Or though one can perceive the difference between ātmā and non-ātmā one cannot easily realize the Lord there.

### 2.3.6

*indriyāṇāṁ pṛthag-bhāvam udayāsta-mayau ca yat \|*  
*pṛthag-utpadyamānānāṁ matvā dhīro na śocati \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing the creation and destruction of the senses which arise separately and knowing the different nature of the ātmā, the wise man does not lament.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowing creation and destruction (udayāsta-mayau) of how the senses (and body etc.) arising separately and knowing their separate nature, the wise man does not lament. This means, knowing that the creation and destruction of objects and their mutual difference does not occur in the ātmā, which possesses one form only, whose nature is knowledge, the wise man does not lament.

### 2.3.7

*indriyebhyaḥ paraṁ mano manasaḥ sattvam uttamam \|*  
*sattvād adhi mahān ātmā mahato’vyaktam uttamam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The mind is superior to the senses. The intelligence is superior to the mind. The individual ātmā is superior to the intelligence. The body is superior to the ātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

In this and the next verse, surrender to the Lord is indicated as the method of gaining knowledge of ātmā distinct from the body. Senses also includes sense objects. (see Katha Upaniṣad 1.3.10) . Sattva means intelligence. Manasas tu parā buddhiḥ: intelligence is superior to the mind. (BG 3.42)

### 2.3.8

*avyaktāt tu paraḥ puruṣo vyāpako’liṅga eva ca \|*  
*yaṁ jñātvā mucyate jantur amṛtatvaṁ ca gacchati \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Superior to the body is the supreme Lord, who is all-pervading and whose body cannot be understood. Knowing him, one becomes liberation and attains immortality.**

**Комментарий:**

Aliṅgaḥ means his form cannot be understood. The method of bringing the Lord under control is surrender.

### 2.3.9

*na sandṛśe tiṣṭhati rūpam asya*  
*na cakṣuṣā paśyati kaścanainam \|*  
*hṛdā manīṣā manasā’bhikḷpto*  
*ya etad vidur amṛtās te bhavanti \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His form does not stand before material vision. No one can see him by the material eye. He can be perceived by the mind, with bhakti and determination. Those who know him become immortal.**

**Комментарий:**

His form or svarūpa (rūpam asya) does not stand before one as an object of one’s perception (saṁdrśe), since it is all-pervading. Or it cannot be perceived as a blue form etc. Thus no one can see him with his eye. Hṛḍa means bhakti and manīṣā means determination.

*ca dhṛtyā ca samāhitātmā jñāna-svarūpaṁ paripaśytiha*

**The person with concentrated mind sees the Lord’s form of knowledge by bhakti and determination. Mahābhārata**

The person who concentrates with determination directly meets the Lord by bhakti. This is confirmed with bhaktyā tv ananyayā śakyaḥ: Only by *ananyā* *bhakti* is it possible to know me, see me, or become connected to me, O Arjuna. BG 11.53

### 2.3.10

*yadā pañcāvatiṣṭhante jñānāni manasā saha \|*  
*buddhiś ca na viceṣṭate tām āhuḥ paramāṁ gatim \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the five senses along with the mind are settled and the intelligence does not wander, they call this the highest goal.**

**Комментарий:**

Jñānāni refers to the senses, since by the senses things become known. When the mind makes decisions it is called intelligence. When the mind takes up functions of decision, identity and contemplation it is called intelligence, false ego and citta. The intelligence should not wander about, but should be engaged for liberation (paramāṁ gatim).

### 2.3.11

*tāṁ yogam iti manyante sthirām indriya-dhāraṇām \|*  
*apramattas tadā bhavati yogo hi prabhavāpyayau \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The wise consider that this highest goal is yoga. There should be complete attention with the senses devoid of sense objects, since at every moment there can be interruptions to what was achieved in yoga.**

**Комментарий:**

That supreme goal (paramā gatiḥ) of controlling the senses and mind they consider to be yoga. At that time, the mind is concentrated, with the senses having no sense objects (apramattaḥ). What is the purpose of controlling the mind? One must be careful since at every moment there can be interruptions to the concentration. (prabhavāpyayau). Or one can have full attention in yoga because one is aiming for the highest goal which produces the desired result and destroys everything contrary.

### 2.3.12

*naiva vācā na manasā prāptuṁ śakyo na cakṣuṣā \|*  
*astīti bruvato’nyatra kathaṁ tad upalabhyate \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord cannot be attained by words, by the mind or by the senses. How can he be realized except from scriptures which testify to his existence?**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord cannot be attained by words, by mind or by the senses. How can the Lord be known except through the scriptures which testify (bruvataḥ) to his existence? He can only be attained through the Upaniṣad.

### 2.3.13

*astīty evopalabdhavyas tattva-bhāvena cobhayoḥ \|*  
*astīty evopalabdhasya tattva-bhāvaḥ prasīdati \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord’s existence is to be known by scriptures and by the mind. For one who knows the existence of the Lord, the mind becomes free of defects.**

**Комментарий:**

Tattva-bhāvena means by using the antaḥkaraṇa (mind), that which thinks of (bhāvayati) tattva. The existence of the Lord is known (upalabdhavyaḥ) by scriptures and by contemplation and meditation using the mind, by these two (ubhayoḥ). For one who knows of the Lord’s existence, the mind becomes pleased. It becomes free of faults.

### 2.3.14

*yadā sarve pramucyante kāmā ye’sya hṛdi śritāḥ \|*  
*atha martyo’mṛto bhavaty atra brahma samaśnute \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When all the material desires situated in the heart are destroyed, the human being then attains immortality. He realized Brahman (the Lord) while meditating on Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

When the desires directed to wrong objects (kāmāḥ) which are situated in the heart are extinguished, after that (athaḥ) one becomes immortal. One becomes free of the burden of previous and future sins. At the time of meditating on Brahman (atra) one realized Brahman (brahma samaśnute).

*sam?n? c?s?ty-upakram?d am?tatvaṁ c?nupo?ya*

**The ignorant person and knower of Brahman leave the body in a similar way before entering the** ***nāḍīs.*** **The immortality of a living person refers to realization while still connected with the body. Vedānta-sūtra 4.2.11**

### 2.3.15

*yadā sarve prabhidyante hṛdayasyeha granthayaḥ \|*  
*atha martyo’mṛto bhavaty etāvad dhy anuśāsanam \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the knots in the heart are all destroyed in this body the human attains immortality. This much should be taught to the sādhaka.**

**Комментарий:**

What was said is repeated out of respect and the conclusion is reached with the last statement. Rāga and dveṣa are difficult to untie, like knots. When all these knots are cut, the human being becomes immortal. This much should be taught as necessary to perform for the worshipper (etavad anuśāsanam). What is described in the next verse is not for the sādhaka.

### 2.3.16

*śataṁ caikā ca hṛdayasya nāḍyas*  
*tāsāṁ mūrdhānam abhiniḥsṛtaikā \|*  
*tayordhvam āyann amṛtatvam eti*  
*viṣvaṅṅ anyā utkramaṇe bhavanti \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are a hundred and one nāḍīs in the heart. Among them one leads out of the head. By that nāḍī one goes upwards and attains immortality. The other nāḍīs lead to various paths in saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий:**

Vimuktaḥ and vimucyate were previously mentioned. Now that highest liberation is described. There are a hundred and one chief nāḍīs in the heart. Among them the brahma-nāḍī, called the suṣumnā, passes out through the head (abhiniḥsṛtā). Going by that nāḍī upwards, one attains the planet of the Lord, meets the Lord endowed with qualities, and manifests a spiritual body. This is the state of liberation. The other nāḍīs (viṣvaṇṇ anyā) are suitable for going on various paths of saṁsāra.

### 2.3.17

*aṅguṣṭha-mātraḥ puruṣo’ntarātmā*  
*sadā janānāṁ hṛdaye saṁniviṣṭaḥ \|*  
*taṁ svāc charīrāt pravṛhen muñjād iveṣīkāṁ dhairyeṇa \|*  
*taṁ vidyāc chukram amṛtaṁ taṁ vidyāc chukram amṛtam iti \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Paramātmā, the Lord, is the size of a thumb. He resides within the hearts of the jīvas at all times. One should distinguish the Lord from his body which is the jīvas, by skilful knowledge, like distinguishing fine iṣīkā grass from a clump of muñjā grass. One should know the brilliant, immortal Lord. One should know the brilliant, immortal Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

One should distinguish the Lord, dwelling in all jīvas (janānām), from the jīvas who are the Lord’s body (with the Lord as their ātmā). Understand that he is different, being the support of all beings, the controller of all beings and the master of all beings as in juṣṭaṁ yadā paśyaty anyam iśam: one sees the Lord who is different. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad) One should make this distinction with skilful knowledge (dhairyeṇa). The Lord is like the fine grass (iṣīkām) within the muñjā. One should know this brilliant, immortal Lord. Repetition indicates the completion of the work.

### 2.3.18

*mṛtyu-proktāṁ naciketo’tha labdhvā*  
*vidyām etāṁ yoga-vidhiṁ ca kṛtsnam \|*  
*brahma-prāpto virajo’bhūd vimṛtyur*  
*anyo’py evaṁ yo vid adhyātmam eva \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having attained the complete knowledge spoken by death and the method of yoga, Naciketā attained a spiritual body, pure and eternal. Someone else who knows this ātmā attains the same goal as Naciketā.**

**Комментарий:**

The meaning of the chapter is summarized. Obtaining knowledge of ātmā spoke by death and the method of yoga, he attained a form endowed with the eight qualities (brahma-prāptaḥ).

*param jyotir upasaṁpadya svena rupeṇābhiniṣpadyante*

**The peaceful persons, rising from this body, attains the supreme light, and are endowed with their own forms. Chāndogya Upaniṣad**

Someone else who knows ātmā-vidyā becomes like Naciketā (eva).
