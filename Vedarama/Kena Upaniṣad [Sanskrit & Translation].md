# Kena Upaniṣad \[Sanskrit & Translation\]

## Kena Upaniṣad \[Sanskrit &amp; Translation\]

## Invocation

*\|\| atha kenopaniṣat \|\|*  
*om āpyāyantu mamāṅgāni vākprāṇaścakṣuḥ*  
*śrotramatho balamindriyāṇi ca sarvāṇi \|*  
*sarvaṁ brahmaupaniṣadaṁ*  
*mā'haṁ brahma nirākuryāṁ mā mā brahma*  
*nirākārodanirākaraṇamastvanirākaraṇaṁ me'stu \|*  
*tadātmani nirate ya*  
*upaniṣatsu dharmāste mayi santu te mayi santu \|*  
*om śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ \|\|*

*oṁ*--the seed *mantra* representing the Supreme Truth;

## Chapter 1

### Text 1

*om keneṣitaṁ patati preṣitaṁ manaḥ*  
*kena prāṇaḥ prathamaḥ praiti yuktaḥ \|*  
*keneṣitāṁ vācamimāṁ vadanti*  
*cakṣuḥ śrotraṁ ka u devo yunakti \|\|1\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*oṁ*--invoking auspiciousness; *kena*--by what, or whom; *iṣitam*--toward what is desired; *patati*--flies; *preṣitam*--impelled; *manaḥ*--the mind; *kena*--by what; *prāṇaḥ*--the vital air; *prathamaḥ*--first; *praiti*--goes forth; *yuktaḥ*--engaged; *kena*--by what; *iṣitāḥ*--impelled; *vācam*--speech; *imām*--this; *vadanti*--they speak; *cakṣuḥ*--the eye; *śrotram*--and the ear; *kaḥ*--what; u--indeed; *devaḥ*--divinity; yunakti--engages.

**Перевод:**

***Oṁ.*** **Impelled by what or whom does the mind pursue its desires? Yoked by whom does the first of the vital airs begin its movement? Impelled by whom do people speak what they speak? What divinity engages the senses of the eye and ear?**

### Text 2

*śrotrasya śrotraṁ manaso mano yad*  
*vāco ha vācaṁ sa u prāṇasya prāṇaḥ \|*  
*cakṣuṣaścakṣuratimucya dhīrāḥ*  
*pretyāsmāllokādamṛtā bhavanti \|\|2\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*śrotrasya śrotram--*the ear's ear; *manasaḥ manaḥ*--the mind's mind; *yat--*which; *vācaḥ--*of speech; *ha--*indeed; *vācam--*the speech; *saḥ*--it; *u--*indeed; *prāṇasya prāṇaḥ--*the breath's breath; *cakṣuṣaḥ cakṣuḥ--*the eye's eye; *atimucya--*becoming free; *dhīrāḥ--*wise persons; *pretya--*leaving; *asmāt--*this; *lokāt*--word; *amṛtāḥ--*deathless; *bhavanti*--become.

**Перевод:**

**It is the hearing sense's power of hearing, the mind's mental capacity, the voice's speaking power, the vital breath's breathing power, and the visual sense's sight. Knowing this, the wise free themselves from entanglement and upon leaving this world become immortal.**

### Text 3-4

*na tatra cakṣurgacchati na vāggacchati no manaḥ \|*  
*na vidmo na vijānīmo yathaitadanuśiṣyāt \|\|3\|\|*

*anyadeva tadviditādatho aviditādadhi \|*  
*iti śuśruma pūrveṣāṁ ye nastadvyācacakṣire \|\|4\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*na*--not; *tatra--*there; *cakṣuḥ--*the eye; *gacchati--*goes; *na--*not; *vāk*--speech; *gacchati*--goes; *na u*--nor; *manaḥ--*the mind; *na vidmaḥ--*we do not know; *na vijānīmaḥ--*we are not aware; *yathā*--how; *etat--*it; *anuśiṣyāt*--can be explained; *anyat*--other; *eva*--certainly; *tat--*that; *viditāt*--than what is known; *atha u--*morevover; *aviditāt--*than what is unknown; *adhi--*lying beyond; *iti--*thus; *śuśruma*--we have heard; *pūrveṣām--*from our predecessors; *ye--*who; *naḥ*--to us; *tat*--that; *vyācacakṣire*--described.

**Перевод:**

**The sense of sight does not reach to this, nor does speech, nor the mind. We do not really know it, or know how to teach about it. It is other than the known and also other than the unknown, situated beyond both. So much we have heard from previous authorities who informed us.**

### Text 5

*yadvācā'nabhyuditaṁ*  
*yena vāgabhyudyate \|*  
*tadeva brahma tvaṁ viddhi*  
*nedaṁ yadidamupāsate \|\|5\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yat--*which; *vācā--*by speech; *anabhyuditam--*not uttered; *yena*--by which; *vāk*--speech; *abhyudyate--*is vibrated; *tat*--that; *eva*--alone; *brahma--*the Supreme; *tvam*--you; *viddhi*--should know; *na--*not; *idam--*this; *yat--*which; *idam--*this; *upāsate*--one worships.

**Перевод:**

**That which speech cannot express, through which speech is spoken--know that to be the Supreme, not what this world reveres.**

### Text 6

*yanmanasā na manute*  
*yenāhurmano matam \|*  
*tadeva brahma tvaṁ viddhi*  
*nedaṁ yadidamupāsate \|\|6\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yat*--which; *manasā--*by the mind; *na manute--*is not thought; *yena*--by which; *āhuḥ*--they call; *manaḥ*--the mind; *matam--*thought of; *tat*--that; *eva*--alone; *brahma--*the Supreme; *tvam*--you; *viddhi*--should know; *na--*not; *idam--*this; *yat--*which; *idam--*this; *upāsate*--one worships.

**Перевод:**

**That which the mind cannot know, through which the mind is considered known--know that to be the Supreme, not what this world reveres.**

### Text 7

*yaccakṣuṣā na paśyati*  
*yena cakṣūṁṣi paśyati \|*  
*tadeva brahma tvaṁ viddhi*  
*nedaṁ yadidamupāsate \|\|7\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yat--*what; *cakṣuṣā*--by the eye; *na paśyate*--is not seen; *yena*--which; *cakṣūṁṣi--*all eyes; *paśyati--*sees; *tat*--that; *eva*--alone; *brahma--*the Supreme; *tvam*--you; *viddhi*--should know; *na--*not; *idam--*this; *yat--*which; *idam--*this; *upāsate*--one worships.

**Перевод:**

**That which the eye cannot see, which sees every eye--know that to be the Supreme, not what this world reveres.**

### Text 8

*yacchrotreṇa na śṛṇoti*  
*yena śrotramidaṁ śrutam \|*  
*tadeva brahma tvaṁ viddhi*  
*nedaṁ yadidamupāsate \|\|8\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yat*--which; *śrotreṇa*--by the ear; *na śṛṇoti*--one does not hear; *yena*--by which; *śrotram*--the ear; *idam*--this; *śrutam*--heard; *tat*--that; *eva*--alone; *brahma--*the Supreme; *tvam*--you; *viddhi*--should know; *na--*not; *idam--*this; *yat--*which; *idam--*this; *upāsate*--one worships.

**Перевод:**

**That which the ear cannot hear, by which the ear is made to hear--know that to be the Supreme, not what this world reveres.**

### Text 9

*yatprāṇena na prāṇiti*  
*yena prāṇaḥ praṇīyate \|*  
*tadeva brahma tvaṁ viddhi*  
*nedaṁ yadidamupāsate \|\|9\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yat*--what; *prāṇena*--by the living breath; *na prāṇiti*--does not breath; *yena*--by which; *prāṇaḥ--*the breath; *praṇīyate*--is breathed; *tat*--that; *eva*--alone; *brahma--*the Supreme; *tvam*--you; *viddhi*--should know; *na--*not; *idam--*this; *yat--*which; *idam--*this; *upāsate*--one worships.

**Перевод:**

**That which the living breath does not inspire, which inspires the living breath--know that to be the Supreme, not what this world reveres.**

## Chapter 2

### Text 1

*yadi manyase suvedeti daharamevāpi*  
*nūnaṁ tvaṁ vettha brahmaṇo rūpam \|*  
*yadasya tvaṁ yadasya deveṣvatha nu*  
*mīmāṁsyemeva te manye viditam \|\|1\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yadi*--if; *manyase*--you think; *su-veda*--I fully understand; *iti*--thus; *dabhram*--meagerly; *eva--*only; *api nūnam--*certainly; *tvam--*you; *vettha--*you understand; *brahmaṇaḥ*--of the Supreme; *rūpam--*the form; *yat--*which; *asya*--of that Supreme; *tvam*--among you; *yat--*which; *asya*--of it; *ca--*and; *deveṣu*--among the gods; *atha nu--*however; *mīmāṁsyam*--to be pondered; *eva--*only; *te--*by you; *manye*--I consider; *viditam*--understood.

**Перевод:**

**If you think you know this Supreme well, you only little know what it is. But if you see the need to carefully study what this Supreme's presence is among yourselves and among the gods, then I think you understand it.**

### Text 2

*nāhaṁ manye suvedeti*  
*no na vedeti veda ca \|*  
*yo nastadveda tadveda*  
*no na vedeti veda ca \|\|2\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*na--*not; *aham*--I; *manye*--think; *su-veda*--I fully understand; *iti*--thus; *na u*--nor; *na veda*--I do not understand; *iti*--thus; *veda*--I understand; *ca*--after all; *yaḥ*--he who; *naḥ*--among us; *tat*--it; *veda*--knows; *tat*--it; *veda na u*--knows not at all; *na veda*--I do not know; *iti*--thus; *veda*--knows; *ca*--and.

**Перевод:**

**I do not say I fully understand the Supreme, nor that I understand it not at all. I do understand something about it. He among us who thinks he knows it does not at all, while he who thinks he does not know it actually does.**

### Text 3

*yasyāmataṁ tasya mataṁ*  
*mataṁ yasya na veda saḥ \|*  
*avijñātam vijānatām*  
*vijñātam avijānatām \|\|3\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yasya*--for whom; *amatam*--unknown; *tasya*--for him; *matam*--known; *matam*--known; *yasya*--for whom; *na veda*--does not know; *saḥ*--he; *avijñātam*--not understood; *vijānatām*--by those who supposedly understand; *vijñātam*--understood; *avijānatām*--by those who supposedly do not understand.

**Перевод:**

**One who thinks he does not know it in fact knows it. One who thinks he knows it in fact does not. It is not understood by those who claim to understand it, and understood by those who claim they do not.**

### Text 4

*pratibodha viditaṁ matam*  
*amṛtatvaṁ hi vindate \|*  
*ātmanā vindate vīryaṁ*  
*vidyayā vindate'mṛtam \|\|4\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*pratibodha*--by personal realization; *viditam*--understood; *matam*--known; *amṛtatvam*--immortality; *hi*--indeed; *vindate*--one attains; *ātmanā*--by diligence; *vindate*--one attains; *vīryam*--concentration of one's intention; *vidyayā*--by devotional meditation; *vindate*--one attains; *amṛtam*--the immortal Supreme.

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme is known when one has come to understand it by personal realization. Then, indeed, one can attain immortality. By diligent endeavor one focuses his intention, and by devoted meditation one ultimately attains the eternal Supreme.**

### Text 5

*iha ced avedīd atha satyam asti*  
*na ced ihāvedīn mahatī vinaṣṭiḥ \|*  
*bhūteṣu bhūteṣu vicitya dhīrāḥ*  
*pretyāsmāl lokād amṛtā bhavanti \|\|5\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*iha*--in this life; *cet*--if; *avedīt*--one has understood; *atha*--then; *satyam*--attainment of truth; *asti*--there is; *na cet*--if not; *iha*--in this life; *avedīt*--one has understood; *mahatī*--total; *vinaṣṭiḥ*--ruin; *bhūteṣu bhūteṣu*--among all created beings; *vicitya*--discerning; *dhīrāḥ*--those who are wise; *pretya--*upon departing; *āsmāt*--from this; *lokāt*--world; *amṛtāḥ*--immortal; *bhavanti*--they become.

**Перевод:**

**If during this life one learns to understand the Supreme, he will achieve true perfection. But if one fails in this life to understand the Supreme, his life will be a total failure. Because wise persons look for the Supreme among all its creations, when they depart from this world they become immortal.**

## Chapter 3

### Text 1

*brahma ha devebhyo vijigye*  
*tasya ha brahmaṇo vijaye devā amahīyanta \|*  
*ta aikṣantāsmākam evāyaṁ vijayo 'smākam*  
*evāyaṁ mahimeti \|\|1\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*brahma*--the Supreme Truth; *ha*--once; *devebhyaḥ*--for the gods; *vijigye*--was victorious; *tasya*--its; *ha*--indeed; *brahmaṇaḥ*--of the Supreme; *vijaye*--in the victory; *devāḥ*--the gods; *amahīyanta*--became glorified; *te*--they; *aikṣanta*--perceived; *asmākam eva*--our own; *ayam*--this; *vijayaḥ*--victory; *asmākam eva*--our own; *ayam*--this; *mahimā*--glory; *iti*--thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**Once the Supreme One fought on behalf of the gods and won victory for them. By this victory of the Supreme the gods became glorified. They thought, "This is our own victory and our own glory."**

### Text 2

*tad dhaiṣāṁ vijajñau*  
*tebhyo ha prādurbabhūva*  
*ta na vyajānata kim*  
*idaṁ yakṣam iti \|\|2\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tat*--the Supreme; *ha*--indeed; *eṣām*--about them; *vijajñau*--understood; *tebhyaḥ*--before them; *ha*--indeed; *prādurbabhūva*--it became visible; *te*--they; *na vyajānata*--did not recognize; *kim*--what; *idam*--this; *yakṣam*--Yakṣa; *iti*--thiṣ.

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme understood exactly their mentality, and so manifested itself before them. The gods, however, did not recognize the Supreme. They asked, "Who is this Yakṣa?"**

### Text 3

*te 'gnim abruvañ jāta-veda etad*  
*vijānīhi kim etad yakṣam iti tatheti \|\|3\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*te*--they; *agnim*--Agni, the fire-god; *abruvan*--told; *jāta-vedaḥ*--O Fire, knower of everything created; *etat*--this; *vijānīhi*--please discover; *kim*--what; *etat*--this; *yakṣam*--Yakṣa; *iti*--thus; *tathā*--yes; *iti--*thus.

**Перевод:**

**They told the fire-god Agni, "O knower of everything created, find out who this Yakṣa is." He answered, "Yes, I will do it."**

### Text 4

*tad abhyadravat tam*  
*abhyavadat ko 'sīty*  
*agnir vā aham asmīty abravīj*  
*jāta-vedā vā aham asmīti \|\|4\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tat*--it; *abhyadravat*--he approached; *tam*--him*; abhyavadati*--it asked; *kaḥ*--who; *asi*--are you; *iti*--thus; *agniḥ*--Fire; *vai*--indeed; *aham*--I; *asmi*--am; *iti*--thus; *abravīt*--he said; *jāta-vedāḥ*--the knower of everything created; *vai*--indeed;  *aham*--I; *asmi*--am; *iti*--thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**Agni came before the Yakṣa, who asked him, "Who are you?" He replied, "I am indeed the god of fire. I know everything created."**

### Text 5

*tasmiṁs tvayi kiṁ vīryam*  
*ity apīdaṁ sarvaṁ*  
*daheyaṁ yad idaṁ pṛthivyām iti \|\|5\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tasmin tvayi*--in that person, yourself; *kim*--what; *vīryam*--power; *iti*--thus; *api*--certainly; *idam*--this; *sarvam*--everything; *daheyam*--I am able to burn; *yat*--which; *idam*--this; *pṛthivyām*--on the earth; *iti--*thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**"What power do you possess?" "Indeed, I can burn anything that exists in this world."**

### Text 6

*tasmai tṛṇaṁ nidadhāv etad daheti \|*  
*tad upa preyāya sarva-javena tan na śaśāka dagdhuṁ sa tata eva nivavṛte naitad aśakaṁ vijñātuṁ yad etad yakṣam iti \|\|6\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tasmai*--in front of him; *tṛṇam*--a blade of grass; *nidadhau*--it placed; *etat*--this; *daha*--please burn; *iti*--thus; *tat*--it, the blade of grass; *upa preyāya*--he went up to; *sarva-javena*--with all his power; *tat*--it; *na śaśāka*--he was not able; *dagdhum*--to burn; *saḥ*--he; *tataḥ eva*--from there; *nivavṛte*--returned; *na*--not; *etat*--this; *aśakam*--I was able; *vijñātum*--to ascertain; *yat*--what; *etat*--this; *yakṣam*--Yakṣa-- *iti*--thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**The Yakṣa placed a blade of grass in front of Agni and said, "Burn this." Agni approached the grass and tried to burn it with all his might, but could not. Returning to the gods, he told them, "I could not discover who this Yakṣa is."**

### Text 7

*atha vāyum abruvan vāyav etad vijānīhi*  
*kim etad yakṣam iti tatheti \|\|7\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*atha*--then; *te*--they; *vāyum*--Vāyu, the wind-god; *abruvan*--told; *vāyo*--O Wind; *etat*--this; *vijānīhi*--please discover; *kim*--what; *etat*--this; *yakṣam*--Yakṣa; *iti*--thus; *tathā*--yes; *iti--*thus.

**Перевод:**

**Then they told the wind-god Vāyu, "O Wind, find out who this Yakṣa is." He answered, "Yes, I will do it."**

### Text 8

*tad abhyadravat tam abhyavadat ko 'sīti vāyur vā*  
*aham asmīty abravīn mātariśvā vā aham asmīti \|\|8\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tat*--it; *abhyadravat*--he approached; *tam*--him*; abhyavadati*--it asked; *kaḥ*--who; *asi*--are you; *iti*--thus; *vāyuḥ*--Wind; *vai*--indeed; *aham*--I; *asmi*--am; *iti*--thus; *abravīt*--he said; *mātariśvā*--the swift mover in empty space; *vai*--indeed;  *aham*--I; *asmi*--am; *iti*--thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**Vāyu came before the Yakṣa, who asked him, "Who are you?" He replied, "I am indeed the god of the wind. I am the swift traveler of outer space."**

### Text 9

*tasmiṁs tvayi kiṁ vīryam ity apīdaṁ*  
*sarvam ādadīya yad idaṁ pṛthivyām iti \|\|9\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tasmin tvayi*--in that person, yourself; *kim*--what; *vīryam*--power; *iti*--thus; *api*--certainly; *idam*--this; *sarvam*--everything; *ādadīya*--I am able to lift; *yat*--which; *idam*--this; *pṛthivyām*--on the earth; *iti--*thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**"What power do you possess?" "Indeed, I can lift up anything that exists in this world."**

### Text 10

*tasmai tṛṇaṁ nidadhāv etad ādatsveti \|*  
*tad upa preyāya sarva-javena tan na śaśākādātuṁ sa tata eva nivavṛte naitad aśakaṁ vijñātuṁ yad etad yakṣam iti \|\|10\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tasmai*--in front of him; *tṛṇam*--a blade of grass; *nidadhau*--it placed; *etat*--this; *ādatsva*--please lift up; *iti*--thus; *tat*--it, the blade of grass; *upa preyāya*--he went up to; *sarva-javena*--with all his power; *tat*--it; *na śaśāka*--he was not able; *ādātum*--to lift up; *saḥ*--he; *tataḥ eva*--from there; *nivavṛte*--returned; *na*--not; *etat*--this; *aśakam*--I was able; *vijñātum*--to ascertain; *yat*--what; *etat*--this; *yakṣam*--Yakṣa-- *iti*--thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**The Yakṣa placed a blade of grass in front of Vāyu and said, "Lift this." Agni approached the grass and tried to lift it with all his might, but could not. Returning to the gods, he told them, "I could not discover who this Yakṣa is."**

### Text 11

*athendram abruvan maghavann*  
*etad vijānīhi kim etad yakṣam iti \|*  
*tatheti tad abhyadravat tasmāt tirodadadhe \|\|11\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*atha*--then; *indram*--to Indra, king of the gods; *abruvan*--they said; *maghavan--*munificent Indra; *etat*--this; *vijānīhi*--please discover; *kim*--what; *etat*--this; *yakṣam*--Yakṣa; *iti*--thus; *tathā*--yes; *iti--*thus; *tat*--it; *abhyadravat*--he approached; *tasmāt*--from that place*; tirodadhe*--it disappeared.

**Перевод:**

**Then they told Indra, "O munificent king of the gods, find out who this Yakṣa is." He answered, "Yes, I will do it." Indra approached the Yakṣa, but as soon as he came near it the Yakṣa disappeared.**

### Text 12

*sa tasminn evākāśe striyam*  
*ājagāma bahu-śobhamānām*  
*umāṁ haimavatīṁ tāṁ hovāca*  
*kim etad yakṣam iti \|\|12\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*saḥ*--he, Indra; *tasmin eva*--in that same place; *ākāśe*--in the sky; *striyam*--a woman; *ājagāma*--went near; *bahu*--very much; *śobhamānām*--beautifuly; *umā*--named Umā; *haimavatī*--the daughter of the Himālaya mountains; *tām*--to her; *ha*--indeed; *uvāca*--he said; *kim*--what; *etat*--this; *yakṣam*--Yakṣa; *iti*--thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**Just then a resplendent women appeared in the sky above that place. She was Umā, daughter of the Himālayas. Indra addressed her, "Who was this Yakṣa?"**

## Chapter 4

### Text 1

*sā brahmeti hovāca brahmaṇo vā etad-vijaye mahīyadhvam iti tato haiva vidāṁ cakāra brahmeti \|\|1\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*sā*--she; *brahma*--the Supreme; *iti*--thus; *ha*--indeed; *uvāca*--said; *brahmaṇaḥ*--of the Supreme; *vai*--certainly; *etat*--this; *vijaye*--in the victory; *mahīyadhvam*--you have all become glorified; *iti*--thus; *tataḥ*--consequently; *ha eva*--indeed; *vidām cakāra*--he understood; *brahma*--the Supreme; *iti*--thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**She told him, "This was the Supreme One. By that Supreme One's victory only have you, the gods, become glorious." Thus Indra could understand, "This was the Supreme."**

### Text 2

*tasmād vā ete devā atitarām*  
*ivānyān devān yad agnir vāyur*  
*indras tena hy enan nediṣṭhaṁ paspṛśus te hy etat*  
*prathamo vidāṁ cakāra brahmeti \|\|2\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tasmād*--therefore; *vai*--indeed; *ete*--these; *devāḥ*--gods; *atitarām iva*--more especially; *ānyān*--than the other; *devān*--gods; *yat--*namely; *agniḥ vāyuḥ indraḥ*--Agni, Vāyu and Indra; *tena*--thus; *hy*--indeed; *enat*--this (Yakṣa); *nediṣṭham*--very close; *paspṛśuḥ*--they came in contact with; *te*--they; *hi*--indeed; *etat*--this; *prathamaḥ*--first; *vidāṁ cakāra*--understood; *brahma iti*--"This is the Supreme*."*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore these gods--Agni, Vāyu and Indra--are greater than the other gods; they came into close contact with the Supreme, and they were the first to understand, "This is the Supreme."**

### Text 3

*tasmād vā indro 'titarām*  
*ivānyān devān sa hy enan*  
*nediṣṭhaṁ pasparśa sa hy enat*  
*prathamo vidāṁ cakāra brahmeti \|\|3\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tasmād*--therefore; *vai*--indeed; *indraḥ*--Indra; *atitarām iva*--more especially; *ānyān*--than the other; *devān*--gods; *saḥ*--he; *hy*--indeed; *enat*--this; *nediṣṭham*--very close; *pasparśa*--came in contact with; *saḥ*--he; *hi*--indeed; *etat*--this; *prathamaḥ*--first; *vidāṁ cakāra*--understood; *brahma iti*--"This is the Supreme*."*

**Перевод:**

**And therefore Indra is greater than the other gods; he came into close contact with the Supreme, and he was the first to understand, "This is the Supreme."**

### Text 4

*tasyaiṣa ādeśo yad etad viyuto vyadyutad ā3 itīnnyamīmiṣad ā3 ity adhidaivatam \|\|4\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tasya*--about this Supreme; *eṣaḥ*--this following; *ādeśaḥ*--summary instruction by way of analogy; *yat*--which; *etat*--this Supreme; *viyutaḥ*--the lightning; *vyadyutat*--illumined; *ā3*--completely; *iti īt*--indeed; *nyamīmiṣat*--it closed itself from being perceived; *ā3*--completely; *iti*--thus; *adhidaivatam*--realized in terms of nature's controlling demigods*.*

**Перевод:**

**The summary instruction concerning this Supreme is as follows. First in terms of nature's controlling deities: This Supreme suddenly illuminated the lightning and other luminaries, and then just as suddenly completely concealed itself again.**

### Text 5

*athādhyātmaṁ yad etad gacchatīva ca mano 'nena caitad upasmaraty abhīkṣṇaṁ saṅkalpaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*atha*--and then; *adhyātmam*--realized in terms of one's own mental and bodily existence; *yat*--which; *etat*--this; *gacchati*--moving, acting; *iva*--as if; *ca*--and; *manaḥ*--mind; *anena*--by the agency of this Supreme; *ca*--and; *etat*--this mind; *upasmarati*--remembers; *abhīkṣṇam*--constantly manifesting; *saṅkalpaḥ*--whose intentions*.*

**Перевод:**

**And then in terms of one's own mental and bodily existence: It is by this Supreme's agency that the mind appears to be acting, engaging in recollection and exhibiting its constantly changing intentions.**

### Text 6

*tad dhi tad-vanaṁ nāma tad-vanam ity upāsitavyaṁ sa ya etad evaṁ vedābhi hainaṁ sarvāṇi bhūtāni saṁvāñchanti \|\|6\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tat*--that Supreme; *hi*--indeed; *tat-vanam nāma*--called *tad-* ("all-pervading") *vanam* ("and worshipable"); *tad-vanam iti*--by this name *tad-vanam; upāsitavyam*--it should be worshipfully meditated upon; *saḥ*--he; *ya*--who; *etat*--this; *evam*--in this way; *veda*--knows; *abhi*--everywhere; *ha*--indeed; *enam*--him; *sarvāṇi*--all; *bhūtāni*--living beings; *saṁvāñchanti*--admire*.*

**Перевод:**

**This Supreme is also called** ***tad-vanam*****, and should be worshipfully meditated upon under this name. One who understands this mystery will be admired by everyone everywhere.**

### Text 7

*upaniṣadaṁ bho brūhīty uktā ta upaniṣad brāhmīṁ vāva ta upaniṣadam abrūmīti \|\|7\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*upaniṣadam*--the confidential essence; *bho*--sir; *brūhi*--please tell; *iti*--thus; *uktā*--spoken; *te*--to you; *upaniṣat*--this confidential instruction; *brāhmīm*--regarding the Supreme; *vāva*--indeed; *te*--to you; *upaniṣadam*--*Upaniṣad; abrūmi*--I have spoken; *iti*--thus*.*

**Перевод:**

**"Sir, please tell me the confidential essence of this teaching." "This** ***Upaniṣad*** **I have spoken to you is itself a confidential instruction on the nature of the Supreme.**

### Text 8

*tasmai tapo damaḥ karmeti pratiṣṭhā vedāḥ sarvāṅgāni satyam āyatanam \|\|8\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*tasmai*--of this confidential knowledge; *tapaḥ*--austerity; *damaḥ*--sense-control; *karma*-- execution of Vedic duties; *iti*--thus; *pratiṣṭhā*--the foundation; *vedāḥ*--the Vedas; *sarva*--all; *aṅgāni*--Vedic corollaries; *satyam*--truthfulness; *āyatanam*--the framework.

**Перевод:**

**"The foundation of this confidential knowledge is austerity, sense-control and execution of Vedic duties. It supporting framework is the** ***Vedas,*** **the Vedic corrollaries and truthfulness."**

### Text 9

*yo vā etām evaṁ vedāpahatya pāpmānam anante svarge loke jyeye pratitiṣṭhati pratitiṣṭhati \|\|9\|\|*

**Пословный перевод:**

*yaḥ*--who; *vai*--indeed; *etām*--this confidential teaching; *evam*--in this way;  *veda*--understands; *apahatya*--dispelling; *pāpmānam*--reactions of transgressions; *anante*--limitless; *svarge loke*--in the spiritual world; *jyeye*--topmost; *pratitiṣṭhati*--he becomes established; *pratitiṣṭhati*--he becomes established*.*

**Перевод:**

**Whoever thus properly understands this confidential teaching relieves himself of the reactions of all his transgressions and becomes situated in the topmost realm, the spiritual world. Indeed, he becomes so situated.**
