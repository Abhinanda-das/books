# Krama-sandarbha 12 Canto

## Chapter One

### Text 1-2

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*yo 'ntyaḥ purañjayo nāma bhaviṣyo bārahadrathaḥ*  
*tasyāmātyas tu śunako hatvā svāminam ātma-jam*  
*pradyota-saṁjñaṁ rājānaṁ kartā yat-pālakaḥ sutaḥ*  
*viśākhayūpas tat-putro bhavitā rājakas tataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: The last king mentioned of the future rulers of the Māgadha dynasty was Purañjaya, a descendant of Bṛhadratha. Purañjaya's minister Śunaka will assassinate the king and install his own son, Pradyota, on the throne. The son of Pradyota will be Pālaka, his son will be Viśākhayūpa, and his son will be Rājaka.**

**Комментарий:**

*śrīrādhākṛṣṇābhyāṁ namaḥ*  
*dvādaśakramasandarbhe sandarbhāṇāṁ samāhṛtiḥ*  
*kriyate yannideśena sa me ’nanyagatergatiḥ*

**He by whose order this collection of sandarbhas is made into the Twelfth Krama Sandarbha is the goal of this person with no other goal.**

Having indicated clearly that Kṛṣṇa is the āśraya, and one’s personal deity up to the eleventh canto, the Twelfth canto commences to summarize the Purāṇa.

Having shown that when Kṛṣṇa disappeared the people would lament, Śukadeva will show that those who worship him will attain deliverance.

### Text 21-26

*kṛṣṇa-nāmātha tad-bhrātā bhavitā pṛthivī-patiḥ*  
*śrī-śāntakarṇas tat-putraḥ paurṇamāsas tu tat-sutaḥ*  
*lambodaras tu tat-putras tasmāc cibilako nṛpaḥ*  
*meghasvātiś cibilakād aṭamānas tu tasya ca*  
*aniṣṭakarmā hāleyas talakas tasya cātma-jaḥ*  
*purīṣabhīrus tat-putras tato rājā sunandanaḥ*  
*cakoro bahavo yatra śivasvātir arin-damaḥ*  
*tasyāpi gomatī putraḥ purīmān bhavitā tataḥ*  
*medaśirāḥ śivaskando yajñaśrīs tat-sutas tataḥ*  
*vijayas tat-suto bhāvyaś candravijñaḥ sa-lomadhiḥ*  
*ete triṁśan nṛpatayaś catvāry abda-śatāni ca*  
*ṣaṭ-pañcāśac ca pṛthivīṁ bhokṣyanti kuru-nandana*

**Перевод:**

**The brother of Balī, named Kṛṣṇa, will become the next ruler of the earth. His son will be Śāntakarṇa, and his son will be Paurṇamāsa. The son of Paurṇamāsa will be Lambodara, who will father Mahārāja Cibilaka. From Cibilaka will come Meghasvāti, whose son will be Aṭamāna. The son of Aṭamāna will be Aniṣṭakarmā. His son will be Hāleya, and his son will be Talaka. The son of Talaka will be Purīṣabhīru, and following him Sunandana will become king. Sunandana will be followed by Cakora and then the eight Bahus, among whom Śivasvāti will be a great subduer of enemies. The son of Śivasvāti will be Gomatī. His son will be Purīmān, whose son will be Medaśirā. His son will be Śivaskanda, and his son will be Yajñaśrī. The son of Yajñaśrī will be Vijaya, who will have two sons, Candravijña and Lomadhi. These thirty kings will enjoy sovereignty over the earth for a total of 456 years, O son of the Kurus.**

**Комментарий:**

*Aniṣṭakarmā* modifies Hāleya. (Hāleya would be his son). Eight Bahus are not mentioned by name. The ninth was called Śivasvāti. Gomatī and his son Purīmān are two kings.

### Text 27

*saptābhīrā āvabhṛtyā daśa gardabhino nṛpāḥ*  
*kaṅkāḥ ṣoḍaśa bhū-pālā bhaviṣyanty ati-lolupāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Then will follow seven kings of the Ābhīra race from the city of Avabhṛti, and then ten Gardabhīs. After them, sixteen greedy kings of the Kaṅkas will rule.**

**Комментарий:**

The total until Lomadhi is thirty kings.

## Chapter Two

### Text 6

*dūre vāry-ayanaṁ tīrthaṁ lāvaṇyaṁ keśa-dhāraṇam*  
*udaraṁ-bharatā svārthaḥ satyatve dhārṣṭyam eva hi*  
*dākṣyaṁ kuṭumba-bharaṇaṁ yaśo 'rthe dharma-sevanam*

**Перевод:**

**A sacred place will be taken to consist of no more than a reservoir of water located at a distance, and beauty will be thought to depend on one's hairstyle. Filling the belly will become the goal of life, and one who is audacious will be accepted as truthful. He who can maintain a family will be regarded as an expert man and the principles of religion will be observed only for the sake of reputation.**

**Комментарий:**

For beauty people will dress their hair.

### Text 29

*viṣṇor bhagavato bhānuḥ kṛṣṇākhyo 'sau divaṁ gataḥ*  
*tadāviśat kalir loka pāpe yad ramate janaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When the seven sages entered Maghā constellation, when Viṣṇu, the Lord of Vaikuṇṭha, the ray of Kṛṣṇa, went to Vaikuṇṭha, Kali entered this world, and people then began to take pleasure in sinful activities.**

**Комментарий:**

**Kṛṣṇa sandarbha 107:**

When Kṛṣṇa, the sun, who has the guṇāvatāra Viṣṇu as his ray, since Viṣṇu is his aṁśa, departed for Vaikuṇṭha (*divaṁ gataḥ*)--a particular manifestation of Mathurā, Dvārakā and Gokula beyond material vision--at that time Kali entered the world. Though these manifestations are situated on earth, by the antardhāna-śakti (śakti which makes invisible) they are present there without touching the earth. Thus the great kadamba tree and other items mentioned in Varāha Purāṇa are not touched by earth and other material elements. The manifestation of Mathurā or Gokula which is visible to material people, touching the earth by mercy, appears in the world. Thus the visible kadamba is touched by earth, stone etc. at that time. When the Lord appears in this visible manifestation of the abode, he contacts earth with his touch and exists there. Now however, he remains in the untouched manifestation (invisible), and thus does not touch the earth. That is the meaning of “touching the earth with his feet. “The meaning here is that the two states are different and non-different. Though the verse says that as long as Kṛṣṇa was present Kali could not enter, Kali actually entered a little before his disappearance.

### Text 30

*yāvat sa pāda-padmābhyāṁ spṛśan āste ramā-patiḥ*  
*tāvat kalir vai pṛthivīṁ parākrantuṁ na cāśakat*

**Перевод:**

**As long as Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the husband of Lakṣṁī, touched the earth with his lotus feet, Kali could not subdue this planet.**

**Комментарий:**

Kali could not subdue the planet. However previous to this, Kali had entered and spread a little.

## Chapter Three

### Text 9-13

*pṛthuḥ purūravā gādhir nahuṣo bharato 'rjunaḥ*  
*māndhātā sagaro rāmaḥ khaṭvāṅgo dhundhuhā raghuḥ*  
*tṛṇabindur yayātiś ca śaryātiḥ śantanur gayaḥ*  
*bhagīrathaḥ kuvalayāśvaḥ kakutstho naiṣadho nṛgaḥ*  
*hiraṇyakaśipur vṛtro rāvaṇo loka-rāvaṇaḥ*  
*namuciḥ śambaro bhaumo hiraṇyākṣo 'tha tārakaḥ*  
*anye ca bahavo daityā rājāno ye maheśvarāḥ*  
*sarve sarva-vidaḥ śūrāḥ sarve sarva-jito 'jitāḥ*  
*mamatāṁ mayy avartanta kṛtvoccair martya-dharmiṇaḥ*  
*kathāvaśeṣāḥ kalena hy akṛtārthāḥ kṛtā vibho*

**Перевод:**

**Such kings as Pṛthu, Purūravā, Gādhi, Nahuṣa, Bharata, Kārtavīrya Arjuna, Māndhātā, Sagara, Rāma, Khaṭvāṅga, Dhundhuhā, Raghu, Tṛṇabindu, Yayāti, Śaryāti, Śantanu, Gaya, Bhagīratha, Kuvalayāśva, Kakutstha, Naiṣadha, Nṛga, Hiraṇyakaśipu, Vṛtra, Rāvaṇa, who made the whole world lament, Namuci, Śambara, Bhauma, Hiraṇyākṣa and Tāraka, as well as many other demons and kings who were powerful controllers, were full of knowledge, heroic, all-conquering and unconquerable. Nevertheless, O almighty Lord, they lived their lives intensely trying to possess me. Those who were materialistic were unsuccessful byn passing of time and have become only historical accounts.**

**Комментарий:**

Five verses describe the kings. All these persons even the demons were possessive of me and existed in me. Or all the śūras, full of knowledge, those who were devotees or avatāras, perfect with śaktis of jñāna and kriyā, who conquered all and were unconquerable, were possessive of me. The word *sarva* is repeated to express great joy and confirmation. Those who are attached by nature to matter (*martya-dharminaḥ*) in time were unsuccessful and became only stories.

### Text 14

*kathā imās te kathitā mahīyasāṁ*  
*vitāya lokeṣu yaśaḥ pareyuṣām*  
*vijñāna-vairāgya-vivakṣayā vibho*  
*vaco-vibhūtīr na tu pāramārthyam*

**Перевод:**

**O mighty Parīkṣit! I have related to you the narrations of all these great devotees who attained the Lord, who spread their fame throughout the world, with a desire to speak about their renunciation and their realization of the Lord. I have not spoken to show the power of words, but to give you the highest spiritual knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 250:**

Though Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa are included among the kings for showing renunciation, they are included only in the manner of the crowd of umbrella bearers (some of whom are not carrying umbrellas), since their stories illustrate more than that. Though everything in the Bhāgavatam should relate to rasa, since it is the highest sweetest fruit on the desire tree of the Vedas (SB [1.1.3](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/1.3)), sometimes there is direct bhakti with various rasas starting with śānta and sometimes there is assistants for those rasas. (Thus stories of kings are there to encourage distaste for material rasa.) There are also degrees in the bhakti-rasas described in the Bhāgavatam (not just the highest rasa).

### Text 15

*yas tūttamaḥ-śloka-guṇānuvādaḥ*  
*saṅgīyate 'bhīkṣṇam amaṅgala-ghnaḥ*  
*tam eva nityaṁ śṛṇuyād abhīkṣṇaṁ*  
*kṛṣṇe 'malāṁ bhaktim abhīpsamānaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The person who desires pure** ***bhakti*** **to Kṛṣṇa should hear the narrations of the Lord’s glorious qualities, the constant chanting of which destroys everything inauspicious.**

**Комментарий:**

He should hear with great concentration those glorifications of the Lord’s avatāras. The result of everything is stated. He desires pure bhakti to Kṛṣṇa, famous as a cowherd.

### Text 20

*tretāyāṁ dharma-pādānāṁ turyāṁśo hīyate śanaiḥ*  
*adharma-pādair anṛta- hiṁṣāsantoṣa-vigrahaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In Tretā-yuga each leg of religion is gradually reduced by one quarter by the influence of the four pillars of irreligion—lying, violence, dissatisfaction and quarrel.**

**Комментарий:**

This should be understood according to the Sandarbha explanation of the First Canto.

### Text 32

*dasyūtkṛṣṭā janapadā vedāḥ pāṣaṇḍa-dūṣitāḥ*  
*rājānaś ca prajā-bhakṣāḥ śiśnodara-parā dvijāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Cities will be dominated by thieves, the Vedas will be contaminated by speculative interpretations of atheists, political leaders will consume the citizens, and the** ***brāhmaṇas*** **will be interested in eating and sex life.**

**Комментарий:**

The cities will be predominated (*utkṛṣṭā)* by thieves.

### Text 43

*kalau na rājan jagatāṁ paraṁ guruṁ*  
*tri-loka-nāthānata-pāda-paṅkajam*  
*prāyeṇa martyā bhagavantam acyutaṁ*  
*yakṣyanti pāṣaṇḍa-vibhinna-cetasaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! In the age of Kali, people whose intelligence will be diverted by atheism will almost never offer sacrifice to the Supreme Lord, supreme** ***guru*** **of the universe, at whose lotus feet the** ***devatās*** **bow.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 274:**

It has been shown that surrender to the Lord in Kali-yuga is accomplished by the power of nāma-kīrtana manifesting itself. Stating this in the opposite way, those who offend nāma-kīrtana, by becoming pākhaṇḍas, will become averse to the Lord.

### Text 44

*yan-nāmadheyaṁ mriyamāṇa āturaḥ*  
*patan skhalan vā vivaśo gṛṇan pumān*  
*vimukta-karmārgala uttamāṁ gatiṁ*  
*prāpnoti yakṣyanti na taṁ kalau janāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Terrified, about to die, collapsing, with faltering voice, if a person utters the holy name of the Supreme Lord, he can be freed from the chains of** ***karma*** **and achieve the supreme destination. But still people in the age of Kali will not worship the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

a) The pāsaṇḍis, having various other ideas, will be opposed to the worship of the Lord and his name.

b) Generally they do not chant. That is explained.

### Text 45

*puṁsāṁ kali-kṛtān doṣān dravya-deśātma-sambhavān*  
*sarvān harati citta-stho bhagavān puruṣottamaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord, situated in the heart, takes away all the faults created by Kali-yuga, by impurity of objects, places and mind.**

**Комментарий:**

The name, manifesting as the Lord, takes away the faults.

### Text 46

*śrutaḥ saṅkīrtito dhyātaḥ pūjitaś cādṛto 'pi vā*  
*nṛṇāṁ dhunoti bhagavān hṛt-stho janmāyutāśubham*

**Перевод:**

**If a person hears about, glorifies, meditates upon, worships or simply offers great respect to the Supreme Lord, who is remembered within the heart, the Lord will remove from his mind the contamination accumulated during many thousands of lifetimes.**

**Комментарий:**

This is the very nature of the Lord. Three verses explain this.

### Text 47

*yathā hemni sthito vahnir durvarṇaṁ hanti dhātu-jam*  
*evam ātma-gato viṣṇur yoginām aśubhāśayam*

**Перевод:**

**Just as fire situated in gold removes any impurities caused by traces of other metals, Viṣṇu within the heart purifies the minds of the** ***yogīs.***

**Комментарий:**

This is his very nature. This is explained in two verses.

### Text 49

*tasmāt sarvātmanā rājan hṛdi-sthaṁ kuru keśavam*  
*mriyamāṇo hy avahitas tato yāsi parāṁ gatim*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, O King, endeavor with all your might to fix the Supreme Lord Keśava within your heart. You will certainly attain the supreme destination by remembering the Lord with attention.**

**Комментарий:**

Having completed all the answers to the questions, he teaches what is necessary to do. With all effort (*sarvātmanā*), fix the Lord within your heart, since others, when dying, if they are attentive to doing just this, will attain the highest goal. What to speak of you, who are most devoted to the Lord.

### Text 50

*mriyamāṇair abhidhyeyo bhagavān parameśvaraḥ*  
*ātma-bhāvaṁ nayaty aṅga sarvātmā sarva-saṁśrayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The Supreme Lord, the ultimate controller, the soul of all beings and the supreme shelter of all beings, the object of meditation for a dying person, produces** ***prema*** **for the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Being the subject of mediation for the dying persons, the Lord produces prema thinking the person as his self (*ātma-bhāvam*).

### Text 51

*kaler doṣa-nidhe rājann asti hy eko mahān guṇaḥ*  
*kīrtanād eva kṛṣṇasya mukta-saṅgaḥ paraṁ vrajet*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Although Kali-yuga is an ocean of faults, there is still one good quality about this age. Simply by chanting about Kṛṣṇa, one can become free from material bondage and attains** ***prema.***

**Комментарий:**

He summarizes the glories of remembering and chanting the name, showing how it destroys the faults of Kali. Kali is an ocean of faults causing destruction of bhakti. But there is one great quality in Kali, respect for kīrtana. Nārada was respected by Kaṁsa. It can be practiced without obstacles and effort.

### Text 52

*kṛte yad dhyāyato viṣṇuṁ tretāyāṁ yajato makhaiḥ*  
*dvāpare paricaryāyāṁ kalau tad dhari-kīrtanāt*

**Перевод:**

**Whatever result was obtained in Satya-yuga by meditating on Viṣṇu, in Tretā-yuga by performing sacrifices, and in Dvāpara-yuga by deity worship, can be obtained in Kali-yuga simply by chanting about the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Kīrtana is an ocean of qualities in Kali. What was accomplished in other yugas by other processes (*tat*) is accomplished in Kali-yuga by kīrtana.

*dhyāyan kṛte yajan yajñais tretāyāṁ dvāpare’rcayan \|*  
*yad āpnoti tad āpnoti kalau saṅkīrtya keśavam \|\|*

**What was obtained by meditation in Sayta-yuga, by sacrifice in Tretā-yuga and by deity worship in Dvāpara-yuga is obtained in Kali-yuga by saṅkīrtana glorifying the Lord. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa)**

## Chapter Four

### Text 4

*eṣa naimittikaḥ proktaḥ pralayo yatra viśva-sṛk*  
*śete 'nantāsano viśvam ātmasāt-kṛtya cātma-bhūḥ*

**Перевод:**

**This is called the** ***naimittika,*** **or occasional, annihilation, during which the original creator, Nārāyaṇa, lies down upon the bed of Ananta Śeṣa and absorbs the universe within himself while Lord Brahmā also enters.**

**Комментарий:**

This is one type of naimittika destruction. The daily destruction after each manvantara is included in it.

### Text 23

*buddhīndriyārtha-rūpeṇa jñānaṁ bhāti tad-āśrayam*  
*dṛśyatvāvyatirekābhyām ādy-antavad avastu yat*

**Перевод:**

**Brahman manifests in the forms of intelligence, the senses and the objects of sense perception, and it is their ultimate basis. Whatever has a beginning and an end is insubstantial because of being an object perceived by limited senses and because of being non-different from its own cause.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 72:**

The intelligence and senses are considered Paramātmā’s natural śaktis, because they are take shelter of Paramātmā, are revealed by him and cannot be separated from him.

Knowledge or Paramātmā manifests as the internal and external senses. He is non-different from them since he is their shelter. Neuter gender is poetic license. Thus there is also great difference as there is difference between a king and servant. Other reasons are given. He is the revealer of the senses (*dṛśyatva)*. And they cannot exist without Paramātmā (*avyatirekha)*. Intelligence and the senses are the Lord in the sense that they are composed of the natural śakti of Paramātmā, just as fire spreads light but is non-different from the light (*eka-deśa-sthitasyāgner jyotsnā vistāriṇī yathā)*.

Illusions which have a beginning and end (such as mistaking shell for silver) lack non-difference from their source *(ādy-antavat avastu yat*). The illusion of silver can never be identical with the shell, since shell does not act as the illusory silver’s cause and shelter, does not have the power to reveal illusion of silver, and lacks non-difference from illusion of silver. The silver is not non-different from the shell. (If the world which is illusion arises from Brahman) by knowing Brahman one cannot know the illusion (since the two are not non-different). This contradicts the promise that by knowing Brahman, all things become known. This concept of illusion arising from Brahman is included in asat-kārya-vāda (the effect is not inherent in the cause).

### Text 24

*dīpaś cakṣuś ca rūpaṁ ca jyotiṣo na pṛthag bhavet*  
*evaṁ dhīḥ khāni mātrāś ca na syur anyatamād ṛtāt*

**Перевод:**

**A lamp, the eye that views by the light of that lamp, and the visible form that is viewed are non-different from the element light. In the same way, intelligence, the senses and sense perceptions have no existence separate from the supreme reality, although that Absolute Truth remains totally distinct from them.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 73:**

An example of a shelter and the object of a shelter by distinguishing the aṁśa from the substance acting as the shelter is given to make things clear.

The lamp, eye and form are non-different from the light because they are aṁśas of the element light. Similarly intelligence and the senses are non-different from Paramātmā (*ṛtāt).* Moreover, just as the element light is not contaminated by the faults of the lamp or eye, so Paramātmā is not contaminated by faults of intelligence or senses. Paramātmā is totally distinct *(anyatamāt).*

### Text 25

*buddher jāgaraṇaṁ svapnaḥ suṣuptir iti cocyate*  
*māyā-mātram idaṁ rājan nānātvaṁ pratyag-ātmani*

**Перевод:**

**The three states of intelligence are called waking consciousness, sleep and deep sleep. But, my dear King, the variegated experiences created for the pure living entity by these different states are nothing more than illusion.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 74:**

It was stated that intelligence, the senses and sense perceptions were composed of the natural śakti of Paramātmā. They were also described as having faults, because of impurity of matter. Thus Paramātmā, endowed with his antaraṅga-śakti and taṭastha-śakti, is also different from the elements which are part of the bahiraṅga-śakti. In order that one can give up the covering of the senses, sense perceptions and intelligence, how they cover the pure jīva is described.

Waking, sleeping and deep sleep are functions of intelligence. They are illusory states (*māyā-mātaram)* created by a covering made of māyā, of great variety (*nānātvam*), called *viśva, taijasa* and *prajñā* (waking, dreaming and deep sleep), imposed upon the pure jīva (*pratyag-ātmani*).

### Text 26

*yathā jala-dharā vyomni bhavanti na bhavanti ca*  
*brahmaṇīdaṁ tathā viśvam avayavy udayāpyayāt*

**Перевод:**

**Just as clouds in the sky come into being and are then destroyed, the material universe with its parts is created and destroyed within Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 75:**

Paramātmā has no contact with the material world which contains intelligence and senses. A cloud, which is vapor, and is the transformation of water, heat, and air in the form of wind, becomes visible in the sky with the appearance of its whole form and then becomes invisible. But the clouds do not touch the sky. Similarly this universe, manifest *(udaya)* by the glance of the Lord, becomes visible in Brahman and then disappears. After disappearing, it remains in a subtle form, since its cause endowed with śakti to produce the universe remains. Thus it is said *sato ‘bhivyañjakaḥ kālaḥ:* prakṛti is the material cause; the puruṣa is the foundational cause; time, the indirect cause, is the agitator of prakṛti; I am all three. (SB [11.24.19](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/24.19))

### Text 27

*satyaṁ hy avayavaḥ proktaḥ sarvāvayavinām iha*  
*vinārthena pratīyeran paṭasyevāṅga tantavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The cause of the universe is said to be factual since the cause is perceived to have a separate existence from the universe, just threads of a cloth are separate from the cloth.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 76:**

An example is given to explain that the cause continues to exist.

The cause of all gross objects (*sarvāvayavinām*) is said to be real. This is seen in the world. Threads exist without the gross form (*vinā arthena)* of the cloth.

### Text 28

*yat sāmānya-viśeṣābhyām upalabhyeta sa bhramaḥ*  
*anyonyāpāśrayāt sarvam ādy-antavad avastu yat*

**Перевод:**

**What is experienced as cause and effect is an illusion since they are mutually dependent. Everything has a beginning and end and therefore is unreal.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 77-78:**

The existence of the effect in the cause is explained by negation of the opposing view. The meaning is this. If it is said that previously the universe did not exist in a subtle state, but only pure Brahman existed everywhere (*sāmānya),* Brahman would transform into the universe, a particular effect (*viśeṣa*) by some śakti as a cause and would itself become non-existent or illusory *(bhramaḥ*)--since what is produced by the cause becoming the effect is supposed to be an illusion. This is the claim of vivarta-vāda. Pure Brahman transforms into the (illusory) universe by śakti in the form of ignorance. We do not agree with this view since sat-kārya-vāda with pariṇāma of śakti was already proved.

“Do you understand about the effect which did not previously exist, proposed by ārambha-vādaand vivarta-vāda.” The effect, which did not previously exist and has beginning and end (*ādy-antavat*) cannot be defined (*avastu),* since there must be interdependence of cause and effect: as long as the effect does not appear, earth or shell as a cause is not accomplished. If the cause is non-existent, the effect does not appear. Because of interdependence, the theory of asat-kārya-vāda cannot be accepted. The cause itself must have the śakti of the effect within it. On the other hand, actually the effect exists in a subtle condition in the cause. The effect existed previously. Clay as a cause is proved by its gross effects as a visible pot.

### Text 29

*vikāraḥ khyāyamāno 'pi pratyag-ātmānam antarā*  
*na nirūpyo 'sty aṇur api syāc cec cit-sama ātma-vat*

**Перевод:**

**This universe and even a single atom within it have no definition without reference to the Supreme Soul. If that is so, then objects related to the Lord are also spiritual and non-different from the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 79:**

The universe is the natural śakti of Paramātmā. That is summarized here.

Though manifested (*khyāyamānaḥ)*, even a slight transformation (universe) cannot be defined as independent of Paramātmā (*pratyag-ātmānam).* What is stated is oneness. If it exists without Paramātmā, the universe would be equal to his spiritual form-- self-manifesting and eternal like Paramātmā (*ātmavat).*

### Text 30

*na hi satyasya nānātvam avidvān yadi manyate*  
*nānātvaṁ chidrayor yadvaj jyotiṣor vātayor iva*

**Перевод:**

**There is no material duality in the Absolute Truth. If a person thinks there is duality he is ignorant like seeing space within two pots as different space, or two lamps as different fire or two** ***prāṇas*** **in two bodies as different** ***prāṇa,*** **when actually there is only one space, one fire and one** ***prāṇa.***

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 80:**

“If there is no change without Paramātmā, Paramātmā cannot be said to be devoid of upādhis. Does that state of absence of upādhis exist?” There is no variety in Paramātmā *(satyasya).* If you think there is variety, you are ignorant *(avidvān*), like seeing the difference (*nānātvam*) between sky in the great sky and in a pot, difference between light in the sky and in a room or air outside and inside the body.

### Text 31

*yathā hiraṇyaṁ bahudhā samīyate*  
*nṛbhiḥ kriyābhir vyavahāra-vartmasu*  
*evaṁ vacobhir bhagavān adhokṣajo*  
*vyākhyāyate laukika-vaidikair janaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just gold is perceived in different forms by various types of manufacturing in the ordinary world, the Supreme Lord, inaccessible to material senses, is described in various words, both ordinary and Vedic, by different types of men.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 81:**

Since nothing can exist without Paramātmā (SB [12.4.29](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/4.29)), all words describe Paramātmā. That is explained with an example. Gold is known by many different names, in the form of gold earrings and bracelet. Similarly the Lord is expressed by all words both secular and Vedic. It is said *sarva-nāmābhidheyaś ca sarva-vedeḍitaś ca saḥ:* the Lord is worshipped by all names in the Vedas. (Skanda Purāṇa).

### Text 32

*yathā ghano 'rka-prabhavo 'rka-darśito*  
*hy arkāṁśa-bhūtasya ca cakṣuṣas tamaḥ*  
*evaṁ tv ahaṁ brahma-guṇas tad-īkṣito*  
*brahmāṁśakasyātmana ātma-bandhanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The cloud is a product of the sun and is revealed by the sun. But the cloud covers the eye, another portion of the sun. The effects of Brahman are revealed by Brahman. Similarly the** ***ahaṅkāra*****, the effect of Brahman, helps the** ***jīva,*** **a portion of Brahman, to bind himself up.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 82:**

Having explained that the world is the natural śakti of Paramātmā and hinting that the jīva by knowledge can destroy the world (his relationship with it), the author explains the ahaṅkāra, which creates an upādhi and is made of Paramātmā’s śakti, but is an obstacle to revealing jīva’s svarūpa, in order to teach rejection of its adhyāsa (imposition)--by which one can attain liberation.

The rays of the sun become transformed into clouds and pour rain for it is said:

*agnau prāstāhutiḥ samyag ādityam upatiṣṭhate*  
*ādityāj jāyate vṛṣṭir vṛṣṭer annaṁ tataḥ prajāḥ*

**Oblations offered to Agni go directly to the sun. From the sun comes rain. From the rain comes food and from food comes progeny. (Manu-saṁhitā)**

The meaning is this. The power of the sun, revealed (*darśitaḥ)* only by the sun, becomes a dense cloud *(ghanaḥ*), a form of darkness on earth and heaven for the eyes, though arising from a portion of the sun. Similarly the material ahaṅkāra (*aham)*, arising as an effect of Paramātmā’s śakti (*brahma-guṇaḥ),* revealed by Paramātmā (*tad-īkṣitaḥ),* becomes an obstacle to revealing the svarūpa of the jīva, who is a small aṁśa of Paramātmā as taṭastha-śakti (*brahmāṁśakasya*).

### Text 33

*ghano yadārka-prabhavo vidīryate*  
*cakṣuḥ svarūpaṁ ravim īkṣate tadā*  
*yadā hy ahaṅkāra upādhir ātmano*  
*jijñāsayā naśyati tarhy anusmaret*

**Перевод:**

**When the cloud produced from the sun is destroyed, the eye can see the actual form of the sun. Similarly, when the spirit soul destroys his material covering of false ego by inquiring into the transcendental science, he realizes Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 83:**

Giving up the adhyāsa (mistaken identity) does not take place by itself but by the strength of inquiry about Paramātmā. To express this, the previous example is continued. The cloud produced from the power of the sun is not destroyed by the power of the eye but by the power of the sun. Ahaṅkāra flees or is destroyed, by inquiry about Paramātmā (*ātmanaḥ),* by his mercy. This shows that ahaṅkāra is not destroyed just by knowledge gained by a person. Vivarta-vāda does not accept this point. Ahaṅkāra is here an upādhi on the jīva, and does not refer to the ahaṅkāra arising from jīva’s svarūpa.

The eye sees the real form of the sun after attaining qualification by the strength of the sun, without the covering of great darkness caused by the cloud. The eye then knows its own svarūpa (*svarūpam īkṣate*) or manifests its power. Sometimes while seeing itself, the eye also sees the sun. Similarly one becomes qualified to meditate on Paramātmā.

### Text 34

*yadaivam etena viveka-hetinā*  
*māyā-mayāhaṅkaraṇātma-bandhanam*  
*chittvācyutātmānubhavo 'vatiṣṭhate*  
*tam āhur ātyantikam aṅga samplavam*

**Перевод:**

**O King! When the illusory false ego that binds the soul has been cut off with the sword of discriminating knowledge and one remains with firm meditation on Acyuta, the Supreme Soul, it is called the** ***ātyantika-pralaya*****.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 84:**

By the previously mentioned sword of discrimination, bondage caused by the ahaṅkāra produced by māyā, not the ahaṅkāra inherent in jīva’s svarūpa, is cut. One remains situated in one’s svarūpa *(avatiṣṭhate).* Not only that, one has realization *(anubhavaḥ*) of Paramātmā (*ātmā*) named Acyuta.

### Text 40

*saṁsāra-sindhum ati-dustaram uttitīrṣor*  
*nānyaḥ plavo bhagavataḥ puruṣottamasya*  
*līlā-kathā-rasa-niṣevaṇam antareṇa*  
*puṁso bhaved vividha-duḥkha-davārditasya*

**Перевод:**

**For a person who is suffering in the fire of countless miseries and even for persons who desires to cross the insurmountable ocean of material existence, there is no suitable boat except cultivating the nectar of the narrations of the Supreme Lord's pastimes.**

**Комментарий:**

Hearing Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes is necessary even for persons desiring liberation. No other means is possible because other actions of bhakti accompany the hearing process.

## Chapter Five

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*atrānuvarṇyate 'bhīkṣṇaṁ viśvātmā bhagavān hariḥ*  
*yasya prasāda-jo brahmā rudraḥ krodha-samudbhavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said:** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **describes the Supreme Lord, soul of the universe, from whose satisfaction Brahmā is born and from whose anger Rudra takes birth.**

**Комментарий:**

From the Lord’s mercy who thought that the sādhaka devotees previously merged at destruction should perform bhakti to me, Brahmā was the chief person who was born. From Brahmā’s anger, which produces the mentality causing materialistic people to oppose the Lord, Śiva, who punishes non-devotees, was born.

### Text 2

*tvaṁ tu rājan mariṣyeti paśu-buddhim imāṁ jahi*  
*na jātaḥ prāg abhūto 'dya deha-vat tvaṁ na naṅkṣyasi*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Give up the animal mentality, thinking “I will die.” You have never been born, you were previously not non-existent, and you will not be destroyed in the future like your body.**

**Комментарий:**

In the First Canto Parīkṣit asked what to do when he was dying. That is unsuitable for your self-interest. Such mentality is suitable for animals (*paśu-buddhim*) but not for you. You were never born because you have an internal body of an associate of the Lord which is indestructible. You will not be destroyed like the visible body.

### Text 3

*na bhaviṣyasi bhūtvā tvaṁ putra-pautrādi-rūpavān*  
*bījāṅkura-vad dehāder vyatirikto yathānalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**You will not take birth again in the form of your sons and grandsons, like a sprout taking birth from a seed and then generating a new seed. Rather, you are entirely distinct from the material body and its paraphernalia, in the same way that fire is distinct from its fuel.**

**Комментарий:**

You will not have a visible body since you are not absorbed in any material body.

### Text 4

*svapne yathā śiraś-chedaṁ pañcatvādy ātmanaḥ svayam*  
*yasmāt paśyati dehasya tata ātmā hy ajo 'maraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In a dream, one can see his own head being cut off and thus understand that his actual self is standing apart from the dream experience. Similarly, while awake, the** ***ātmā*** **sees his body, a product of the five material elements*****.*** **Therefore the** ***ātmā*** **is unborn and without death.**

**Комментарий:**

Ordinary people are absorbed in their bodies. Five verses explain this.

### Text 5

*ghaṭe bhinne ghaṭākāśa ākāśaḥ syād yathā purā*  
*evaṁ dehe mṛte jīvo brahma sampadyate punaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When a pot is broken, the portion of sky within the pot becomes sky element as before. In the same way, when the gross and subtle bodies die, the living entity becomes Brahman as before.**

**Комментарий:**

*Dehe* means gross and subtle bodies. The jīva suitably attains brahma-tattva. Association with upādhis is given up, but the jīva does not become false.

### Text 6

*manaḥ sṛjati vai dehān guṇān karmāṇi cātmanaḥ*  
*tan manaḥ sṛjate māyā tato jīvasya saṁsṛtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The material bodies, qualities and activities of the** ***ātmā*****, are created by the material mind. That mind is itself created by** ***māyā.*** **Because of the** ***upādhis,*** **the** ***ātmā*** **assumes material existence.**

**Комментарий:**

The different items are causes of one another.

### Text 7

*snehādhiṣṭhāna-varty-agni- saṁyogo yāvad īyate*  
*tāvad dīpasya dīpatvam evaṁ deha-kṛto bhavaḥ*  
*rajaḥ-sattva-tamo-vṛttyā jāyate 'tha vinaśyati*

**Перевод:**

**A flame functions as such only by the combination of its fuel, the vessel, the wick and the fire. Similarly,** ***saṁsāra*** **is composed of** ***karma*****, the mind, the body and consciousness.** ***Saṁsāra*** **related to accepting the body appears and disappears by the actions of** ***rajas, sattva*** **and** ***tamas.***

**Комментарий:**

The flame becomes a transformation (*dīpatvam*) by presiding powers of fuel, vessel, wick and fire.

### Text 8

*na tatrātmā svayaṁ-jyotir yo vyaktāvyaktayoḥ paraḥ*  
*ākāśa iva cādhāro dhruvo 'nantopamas tataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***ātmā*** **is not subject to creation and destruction. It is different from the gross and subtle bodies since it is self-luminous. Like the ether, it is the support for everything. It is unchanging, and similar to Paramātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

Because (*tataḥ*) the ātmā is self-illuminating it is similar to Paramātmā (*anantopamaḥ*).

*suparṇāv etau sadṛśau sakhāyau*  
*yadṛcchayaitau kṛta-nīḍau ca vṛkṣe*  
*ekas tayoḥ khādati pippalānnam*  
*anyo niranno ’pi balena bhūyān*

**By chance, two birds of similar nature, who are friends, have made a nest in the same tree. One of them, however, is eating the fruits of the tree, whereas the other, superior in strength, does not eat the fruits. (SB** [**11.11.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/11.6)**)**

### Text 9

*evam ātmānam ātma-stham ātmanaivāmṛśa prabho*  
*buddhyānumāna-garbhiṇyā vāsudevānucintayā*

**Перевод:**

**O King! By constantly meditating upon Vāsudeva and by using the mind and intelligence for inferring the soul’s existence, you should carefully consider your true self situated within the material coverings.**

**Комментарий:**

He teaches what was previously stated. Understand that you, situated in your body (*ātma-stham*) along with your nature as jīva (*ātmanā*) are an associate of the Lord internally (*ātmānām*), by intelligence using inference (*anumāna-garbhinyā*), previously described, that contemplates Vāsudeva.

### Text 10

*codito vipra-vākyena na tvāṁ dhakṣyati takṣakaḥ*  
*mṛtyavo nopadhakṣyanti mṛtyūnāṁ mṛtyum īśvaram*

**Перевод:**

**The snake-bird Takṣaka, sent by the curse of the** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **will not burn your true self. The agents of death will never burn you who are capable of killing the servants of death.**

**Комментарий:**

Takṣaka will not burn you, an eternal associate, since even agents of death cannot burn you.

### Text 11-12

*ahaṁ brahma paraṁ dhāma brahmāhaṁ paramaṁ padam*  
*evaṁ samīkṣya cātmānam ātmany ādhāya niṣkale*  
*daśantaṁ takṣakaṁ pāde lelihānaṁ viṣānanaiḥ*  
*na drakṣyasi śarīraṁ ca viśvaṁ ca pṛthag ātmanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“I am that Brahman, the supreme state of being. Brahman is I, the supreme position.” Considering this, place yourself in the Brahman devoid of** ***upādhi*****s. You will not see Takṣaka biting your foot with fire and poison and licking it with his tongue. You will not see your body or the universe to be separate from Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Am I the Lord? One thinks of non-difference by thinking of one’s śakti as the Lord’s śakti, by thinking of one identity. Two verses describe this*. Niṣkale* means beyond māyā. Offer the self into the Brahman (*ātmani*), beyond māyā (*niṣkale*). You will not see your body and the universe as separate from Brahman. Rather you will think of your benefit by this assistance.

### Text 13

*etat te kathitaṁ tāta yad ātmā pṛṣṭavān nṛpa*  
*harer viśvātmanaś ceṣṭāṁ kiṁ bhūyaḥ śrotum icchasi*

**Перевод:**

**Beloved King Parīkṣit, I have narrated to you the topics you originally inquired about—the pastimes of the Hari, the soul of the universe. Now, what more do you wish to hear?**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 86:**

Thus the main topic is bhakti represented by hearing the Lord’s pastimes since that is indicated in the introductory and closing remarks. Teachings of jñāna (SB [12.5.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/5.2)) in the middle are simply to show that Parīkṣit was fixed in bhakti which has already been understood. It is like the Lord’s offering of liberation to his devotees. By his being fixed in bhakti, he had given up fear of death already. After hearing the teachings of jñāna it will be shown that he remained fixed in bhakti:

*atho vihāyemam amuṁ ca lokaṁ*  
*vimarśitau heyatayā purastāt\|*  
*kṛṣṇāṅghri-sevām adhimanyamāna*  
*upāviśat prāyam amartya-nadyām \|\|*

**Having previously concluded that both were to be rejected, giving up this world and Svarga, thinking service to Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet as the highest goal, he sat down with a vow of not eating on the bank of the Gaṅgā. (SB** [**1.19.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/19.5)**)**

*iti vyavacchidya sa pāṇḍaveyaḥ*  
*prāyopaveśaṁ prati viṣṇu-padyām\|*  
*dadhau mukundāṅghrim ananya-bhāvo*  
*muni-vrato mukta-samasta-saṅgaḥ \|\|*

**Making this decision to sit without eating on the bank of the Gaṅgā, Parīkṣit, without thoughts of other processes or deities, peaceful, and free of all material attachments, concentrated on the feet of Mukunda. (SB** [**1.19.7**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/19.7)**)**

He had given up fear:

*taṁ mopayātaṁ pratiyantu viprā*  
*gaṅgā ca devī dhṛta-cittam īśe \|*  
*dvijopasṛṣṭaḥ kuhakas takṣako vā*  
*daśatv alaṁ gāyata viṣṇu-gāthāḥ \|\|*

**The brāhmaṇas and Gaṅgā-devī should know that I am surrendered, and have dedicated my heart to the Lord. Let the snake released by the brāhmaṇa*****,*** **even if it is an imposter, bite me. Please sing topics concerning the Lord. (SB** [**1.19.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/19.15)**)**

Not caring for the teachings on jñāna, he thought himself successful by bhakti through hearing:

*siddho ’smy anugṛhīto ’smi bhavatā karuṇātmanā*  
*śrāvito yac ca me sākṣād anādi-nidhano hariḥ*

**I have now achieved the purpose of my life, because a great and merciful soul like you has shown such kindness to me since you have spoken to me this narration of the Supreme Lord who has no beginning or end, whom I directly saw.**

*nāty-adbhutam ahaṁ manye mahatām acyutātmanām*  
*ajñeṣu tāpa-tapteṣu bhūteṣu yad anugrahaḥ*

**I do not find it amazing that great souls such as you, whose minds are always absorbed in the Lord, show mercy to the foolish conditioned souls suffering in this world.**

*purāṇa-saṁhitām etām aśrauṣma bhavato vayam*  
*yasyāṁ khalūttamaḥ-śloko bhagavān anuvarṇyate*

**I have heard from you this Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, which is the summary of all the Purāṇas and which describes the Supreme Lord along with other themes.**

*bhagavaṁs takṣakādibhyo mṛtyubhyo na bibhemy aham*  
*praviṣṭo brahma nirvāṇam abhayaṁ darśitaṁ tvayā*

**My Lord, I now have no fear of Takṣaka or any other living being, or even of repeated deaths, but I fear entering the impersonal Brahman which destroys fear, about which you have just taught me. (SB** [**12.6.2-5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/6.2)**)**

This one verse speaks of brahma-jñāna being the cause of destroying fear of Takṣaka, but only to show respect for previous statements made by Kṛṣṇa. However after that, he prays for permission to meditate on the Lord and glorify the Lord’s name using words and mind:

*anujānīhi māṁ brahman vācaṁ yacchāmy adhokṣaje*  
*mukta-kāmāśayaṁ cetaḥ praveśya visṛjāmy asūn*

**O brāhmaṇa, please give me permission to resign my speech to Adhokṣaja. Absorbing my mind, purified of desires and impressions, in the Lord, permit me to give up my life. (SB** [**12.6.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/6.6)**)**

Then in the next verse Parīkṣit shows that jñāna which destroys ignorance is included in the bliss of seeing the lotus feet of the Lord:

*ajñānaṁ ca nirastaṁ me jñāna-vijñāna-niṣṭhayā*  
*bhavatā darśitaṁ kṣemaṁ paraṁ bhagavataḥ padam*

**You have revealed to me the supreme form of the Lord. By being fixed in knowledge of the Lord and in realization of his sweetness and powers, my ignorance has been eradicated. (SB** [**12.6.7**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/6.7)**)**

The word *padam* indicates the Lord’s lotus feet since this was previously stated.

*sa vai mahā-bhāgavataḥ parīkṣid*  
*yenāpavargākhyam adabhra-buddhiḥ \|*  
*jñānena vaiyāsaki-śabditena*  
*bheje khagendra-dhvaja-pāda-mūlam \|\|*

**Parīkṣit, the great devotee, with pure intelligence, attained liberation, in the form of the feet of the Lord having a flag marked with Garuḍa, through knowledge spoken by Śukadeva. (SB** [**1.18.16**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/18.16)**)**

The meaning of the section is made clear by Sūta:

*brahma-kopotthitād yas tu takṣakāt prāṇa-viplavāt \|*  
*na sammumohorubhayād bhagavaty arpitāśayaḥ \|\|*

**Because he had taken shelter of the Lord, he did not become bewildered with great fear from the life threatening snake called Takṣaka called by the curse of the brāhmaṇa. (SB** [**1.18.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/18.2)**)**

*nottamaśloka-vārtānāṁ juṣatāṁ tat-kathāmṛtam \|*  
*syāt sambhramo ’nta-kāle ’pi smaratāṁ tat-padāmbujam \|\|*

**Those who remember the lotus feet of the Lord; those who relish the nectar of pastimes concerning the Lord; and those whose very lives are the Lord, do not have confusion even at the time of death. (SB** [**1.18.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/18.4)**)**

The king asks the question:

*ataḥ pṛcchāmi saṁsiddhiṁ yogināṁ paramaṁ gurum \|*  
*puruṣasyeha yat kāryaṁ mriyamāṇasya sarvathā \|\|*

**I ask the supreme guru of the yogīs** ***“*****What is complete perfection and what is to be done at all times for attaining that perfection for a man about to die?” (SB** [**1.19.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/19.37)**)**

Śukadeva instructs chanting and meditating on the Lord:

*tasmāt sarvātmanā rājan hṛdi-sthaṁ kuru keśavam*  
*mriyamāṇo hy avahitas tato yāsi parāṁ gatim*

**Therefore, O King, endeavor with all your might to fix the Supreme Lord Keśava within your heart while dying. You will certainly attain the supreme destination by remembering the Lord with attention.**

Fix your mind (*avahitaḥ*) on Keśava.

*mriyamāṇair abhidhyeyo bhagavān parameśvaraḥ*  
*ātma-bhāvaṁ nayaty aṅga sarvātmā sarva-saṁśrayaḥ*

**O King! The Supreme Lord, the ultimate controller, the soul of all beings and the supreme shelter of all beings, the object of meditation for a dying person, produces prema for the Lord. (SB** [**12.3.50**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/3.50)**)**

The Lord produces bhāva for himself (*ātma-bhāvam*). What is the use of jñāna, which takes effort to bring results?

*kaler doṣa-nidhe rājann asti hy eko mahān guṇaḥ*  
*kīrtanād eva kṛṣṇasya mukta-saṅgaḥ paraṁ vrajet*

**O King! Although Kali-yuga is an ocean of faults, there is still one good quality about this age. Simply by chanting about Kṛṣṇa, one can become free from material bondage and attain prema. (SB** [**12.3.49-51**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/3.49)**)**

There is one good quality in Kali-yuga because (*hi*) from chanting alone (*kīrtanād eva*), which does not require effort like jñāna, one attains the supreme Lord.

*na hy ato ’nyaḥ śivaḥ panthā viśataḥ saṁsṛtāv iha \|*  
*vāsudeve bhagavati bhakti-yogo yato bhavet \|\|*

**There is no other auspicious path for those suffering in the material world, because this path produces prema for Bhagavān Vāsudeva. (SB** [**2.2.33**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/2.33)**)**

This section ends by concluding in bhakti-yoga with its various aṅgas:

*evam etan nigaditaṁ pṛṣṭavān yad bhavān mama \|*  
*nṛṇāṁ yan mriyamāṇānāṁ manuṣyeṣu manīṣiṇām \|\|*

**In response to your question about the activities of a dying man, I have explained the two paths according to yoga, and for those who are intelligent among men and dying, I have explained the path of bhakti. (SB** [**2.3.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/3.1)**)**

Bhakti culminates in hearing the pastimes of the Lord:

*pibanti ye bhagavata ātmanaḥ satāṁ*  
*kathāmṛtaṁ śravaṇa-puṭeṣu sambhṛtam \|*  
*punanti te viṣaya-vidūṣitāśayaṁ*  
*vrajanti tac-caraṇa-saroruhāntikam \|\|*

**Those who drink the sweet pastimes of the Lord and his devotees held in the cups of their ears clean their hearts of all contamination and attain the lotus feet of the Lord for service. (SB** [**2.2.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/2.37)**)**

Therefore it is correctly concluded that Śukadeva’s instruction on dying starting in SB [12.5.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/5.2) which appear to be jñāna are to show Parīkṣit’s fixed bhakti only. The Lord’s instructions indicate bhakti only.

That is the meaning of the following also:

*kathayasva mahābhāga yathāham akhilātmani \|*  
*kṛṣṇe niveśya niḥsaṅgaṁ manas tyakṣye kalevaram \|\|*

**O great soul! Please speak so that I can give up the body absorbing my mind, devoid of material desires, in Kṛṣṇa who is the soul of all beings. (SB** [**2.8.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/8.3)**)**

## Chapter Six

### Text 3

*nāty-adbhutam ahaṁ manye mahatām acyutātmanām*  
*ajñeṣu tāpa-tapteṣu bhūteṣu yad anugrahaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I do not find it amazing that great souls such as you, whose minds are always absorbed in the Lord, show mercy to the foolish conditioned souls suffering in this world.**

**Комментарий:**

Even those whose very nature is Acyuta (*acyutātmanām*), who are moreover ātmārāmas (*mahatma*) show qualities like mercy, since they are established in the Lord’s qualities. This is not astonishing.

### Text 5

*bhagavaṁs takṣakādibhyo mṛtyubhyo na bibhemy aham*  
*praviṣṭo brahma nirvāṇam abhayaṁ darśitaṁ tvayā*

**Перевод:**

**My lord, I now have no fear of Takṣaka or any other living being, or even of repeated deaths, but having become absorbed in Bhagavān, about which you have just taught me.**

**Комментарий:**

What you have now taught has produced this in me: I have become completely absorbed in the great tattva of Bhagavān the form of the highest bliss (*brahma nirvāṇam*).

### Text 6

*anujānīhi māṁ brahman vācaṁ yacchāmy adhokṣaje*  
*mukta-kāmāśayaṁ cetaḥ praveśya visṛjāmy asūn*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **please give me permission to resign my speech to Adhokṣaja. Absorbing my mind, purified of desires and impressions, in the Lord, permit me to give up my life.**

**Комментарий:**

*Adhokṣaje* means “to Kṛṣṇa” since the word means he slept below the cart (*adho nena śayānena --* Hari-vaṁśa).

### Text 7

*ajñānaṁ ca nirastaṁ me jñāna-vijñāna-niṣṭhayā*  
*bhavatā darśitaṁ kṣemaṁ paraṁ bhagavataḥ padam*

**Перевод:**

**You have revealed to me the supreme lotus feet of the Lord. By being fixed in knowledge of the Lord and in realization of his sweetness and powers, my ignorance has been eradicated.**

**Комментарий:**

*Pada* means lotus feet as shown in the First Canto:

*sa vai mahā-bhāgavataḥ parīkṣid*  
*yenāpavargākhyam adabhra-buddhiḥ \|*  
*jñānena vaiyāsaki-śabditena*  
*bheje khagendra-dhvaja-pāda-mūlam \|\|*

**Parīkṣit, the great devotee, with pure intelligence, attained liberation, in the form of the feet of the Lord having a flag marked with Garuḍa, through knowledge spoken by Śukadeva. (SB** [**1.18.16**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/18.16)**)**

### Text 9-10

*parīkṣid api rājarṣir ātmany ātmānam ātmanā*  
*samādhāya paraṁ dadhyāv aspandāsur yathā taruḥ*  
*prāk-kūle barhiṣy āsīno gaṅgā-kūla udaṅ-mukhaḥ*  
*brahma-bhūto mahā-yogī niḥsaṅgaś chinna-saṁśayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The saintly King Parīkṣit then sat down on the bank of the Ganges, upon a seat made of** ***darbha g*****rass with the tips of its stalks facing east, and turned himself toward the north. Having attained the perfection of** ***yoga*****, having freed himself of all material association, having cast off all doubts, merging his life airs within, concentrating intently, situated in the spiritual realm, he placed Kṛṣṇa in his mind by his intelligence.**

**Комментарий:**

He concentrated on Kṛṣṇa (*param dadhyau*). He had already achieved the stage of Brahman.

*brahma-bhūtaḥ prasannātmā na śocati na kāṅkṣati*  
*samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu mad-bhaktiṁ labhate parām \|\|*

**Having attained realization of** ***ātmā*****, being a pure soul, he does not lament for or desire anything except me, and looks upon all other beings as equally inferior. He attains** ***bhakti*** **in the form of realizing me. (BG** [**18.54**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/18.54)**)**

Or he became absorbed in Kṛṣṇa who is param brahma.

*yūyaṁ nṛ-loke bata bhūri-bhāgā*  
*lokaṁ punānā munayo 'bhiyanti*  
*yeṣāṁ gṛhān āvasatīti sākṣād*  
*gūḍhaṁ paraṁ brahma manuṣya-liṅgam*

**You Pāṇḍavas are most fortunate in this world. Sages who purify the planets come and visit your houses because the Supreme Brahman personally resides in your houses in a human form. (SB** [**7.10.48**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/10.48)**)**

### Text 30-31

*na yatra dambhīty abhayā virājitā*  
*māyātma-vāde 'sakṛd ātma-vādibhiḥ*  
*na yad vivādo vividhas tad-āśrayo*  
*manaś ca saṅkalpa-vikalpa-vṛtti yat*  
*na yatra sṛjyaṁ sṛjatobhayoḥ paraṁ*  
*śreyaś ca jīvas tribhir anvitas tv aham*  
*tad etad utsādita-bādhya-bādhakaṁ*  
*niṣidhya cormīn virameta tan muniḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Wherever the form of Viṣṇu resides,** ***māyā,*** **though fearless with deceitful persons, does not exist. Where Viṣṇu, who gives shelter to** ***māyā,*** **exists, there can be no constant discussions among** ***ātmavādīs*** **or various quarrels. Where Viṣṇu exists, there can be no mind subjected to accepting and rejecting. Where Viṣṇu exists there can be no created objects or creators, since he is beyond created and creator. Where Viṣṇu exists, there can be no results of pious actions and no** ***jīvas*** **with** ***ahaṅkāra*** **affected by the three** ***guṇas.*** **Therefore, the form of Viṣṇu is devoid of suffering** ***jīvas*** **and causes of suffering in the form of** ***māyā.*** **The devotee who has destroyed lust and anger in his heart becomes completely detached.**

**Комментарий:**

Is Viṣṇu deceitful, having māyā act for him? Since māyā is close (*āvirajitā*) to Viṣṇu, having attained qualification, she is not afraid on seeing a deceitful person. Not seeing deceit in a person, she is fearful. She acts without restraints towards the deceitful person.

*vilajjamānayā yasya sthātum īkṣā-pathe ’muyā \|*  
*vimohitā vikatthante mamāham iti durdhiyaḥ \|\|*

**The ignorant** ***jīvas*****, bewildered by** ***māyā*** **who is ashamed to stand in sight of the Lord, boast about “I” and “mine.” (SB** [**2.5.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/5.13)**)**

Where the Lord who gives shelter to māyā exists (*yad āśrayaḥ*), there are no arguments with persons who argue about ātmā, having gone beyond ātmā. Where the Lord exists the mind does not partake of argumentation. Thus māyā is situated near the Lord, but she is not of the same mind. Since he is not attached to her, she is ashamed to stand before him.

The shelter of māyā, Viṣṇu, has no obstructions: there are no created objects. There are no jīvas having ahaṅkāra (*aham*), effected by the guṇas. As previously described, Viṣṇu is devoid of suffering *jīvas* and causes of suffering in the form of *māyā.* Thus the devotee rejects māyā (*ūrmīn*), and stops material existence (*virameta*). Naturally he become completely detached. It does not require effort.

### Text 32

*paraṁ padaṁ vaiṣṇavam āmananti tad*  
*yan neti netīty atad-utsisṛkṣavaḥ*  
*visṛjya daurātmyam ananya-sauhṛdā*  
*hṛdopaguhyāvasitaṁ samāhitaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Giving up evil tendencies of me and mine, understanding what is not favorable for** ***bhakti,*** **eager to reject all things not related to Viṣṇu because of affectionate for the unalloyed devotees, the devotees fix themselves on the supreme form of Viṣṇu who is embraced and understood even by ātmārāmas.**

**Комментарий:**

This is further explained. The devotees reject what is devoid of Viṣṇu-tattva (*daurātmyam*) and have no friendship with such things (*ananya-sauhrḍā*). They fix themselves on the Lord who is realized (*avasitam*) even by ātmārāmas who have controlled their minds (*samāhitaiḥ*)

### Text 33

*ta etad adhigacchanti viṣṇor yat paramaṁ padam*  
*ahaṁ mameti daurjanyaṁ na yeṣāṁ deha-geha-jam*

**Such devotees understand the supreme position of Viṣṇu, because they are no longer polluted by the concepts of "I" and "my," which are based on body and home.**

**Комментарий:**

The preliminary steps are described in two verses.

### Text 35

*namo bhagavate tasmai kṛṣṇāyākuṇṭha-medhase*  
*yat-pādāmburuha-dhyānāt saṁhitām adhyagām imām*

**I offer my obeisances to Vyāsa, the invincible persons by remembrance of whose lotus feet I have understood this Purāṇa.**

### Text 37

*sūta uvāca*  
*samāhitātmano brahman brahmaṇaḥ parameṣṭhinaḥ*  
*hṛdy ākāśād abhūn nādo vṛtti-rodhād vibhāvyate*

**Перевод:**

**Sūta Gosvāmī said: O** ***brāhmaṇa*****! First, the** ***nāda*** **appeared from the ether in the heart of Lord Brahmā, whose mind was perfectly fixed in spiritual realization. One can perceive this subtle vibration when one controls the mind.**

*Vṛtti-rodhāt* means “By the strength of guru’s teachings the antaḥkaraṇa becomes fixed.”

### Text 38

*yad-upāsanayā brahman yogino malam ātmanaḥ*

**By worship of this subtle form of the Vedas, O** ***brāhmaṇa*****, mystic sages cleanse their hearts of all contamination of object, activity and doer, and thus they attain freedom from repeated birth and death.**

**Комментарий:**

The results of this worship are described.

### Text 39

*tato 'bhūt tri-vṛd oṁkāro yo 'vyakta-prabhavaḥ sva-rāṭ*

**Перевод:**

**From** ***nāda*** **arose the** ***oṁkāra*** **composed of three sounds and whose origin is unclear.** ***Oṁ*** **is directly the Supreme Lord and causes realization of Bhagavān, Paramātmā and Brahman.**

*Liṅgam* means “cause of realizing” or a limb.

### Text 40-41

*śṛṇoti ya imaṁ sphoṭaṁ supta-śrotre ca śūnya-dṛk*  
*yena vāg vyajyate yasya vyaktir ākāśa ātmanaḥ*  
*sva-dhāmno brāhmaṇaḥ sākṣād vācakaḥ paramātmanaḥ*  
*sa sarva-mantropaniṣad veda-bījaṁ sanātanam*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is the person who hears** ***oṁ*** **which arises from unmanifest** ***nāda*** **when the senses do not function, since his knowledge does not depend on senses. By that Lord*****,*** **who manifests effects like ether, the senses like speech appear.** ***Oṁ*** **indicates Brahman, which is its own shelter, and as well indicates Paramātmā and Bhagavān*****. Oṁ*** **contains all** ***mantras*** **and Upaniṣads and it is the eternal cause of the Vedas.**

**Комментарий:**

“What is the proof that this sound exists? If one covers the ears one hears a humming sound only. It is not possible to understand it by calling out a thousand times in dream or waking state. It is not heard by people born deaf, without senses.”

The supreme ātmā who hears with ears during sleep and even in waking state (*ca*), has a condition independent of the senses, like the jīva. He does not have material senses (*śūnya-dṛk*). *Apāṇi-pādaḥ*: the Lord does not have material hands and feet. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad) *svābhāvikī jñāna-bala-kriyā ca*: Lord has natural knowledge, strength and action. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad). The cause is explained.

By him the power of speech and other senses manifest (*vag vyajyate*). He has (*yasya ātmanaḥ*) a manifestation as effect, such as ether which is the shelter of sound, or air which is the shelter of touch. Thus the jīva hears by his mercy, by worshipping his upādhis.

Om indicates Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān. Indirect interpretation of the Māyāvādīs is not respected. Śruti says *om ity etad brahmaṇo nediṣṭhaṁ nāma*: *oṁ* is the name nearest to Brahman. (Ṛg Veda)

### Text 43

*tato 'kṣara-samāmnāyam asṛjad bhagavān ajaḥ*  
*antasthoṣma-svara-sparśa- hrasva-dīrghādi-lakṣaṇam*

**Перевод:**

**From that** ***oṁkāra*** **Brahmā created all the sounds of the alphabet—the vowels, consonants, semivowels, sibilants and others—distinguished by such features as long and short measure.**

**Комментарий:**

*Svara* means the vowels which may be high, low and medium pitch. There are short, long and very long vowels. *Ādi* indicates things like the *visarga*. Brahma created these from oṁkāra (*tataḥ*). Omkāra is the cause of various effects. This is repeated to indicate the vaikharī or audible aspect at this time.

**Комментарий:**

The aṁśa is the mahā-puruṣa (Mahā-viṣṇu) and his aṁśa is Viṣṇu. His aṁśa or kalā is Vyāsa (*vibhuḥ*).

### Text 67

*śrī-yājñavalkya uvāca*  
*oṁ namo bhagavate ādityāyākhila-jagatām ātma-svarūpeṇa kāla-*  
*svarūpeṇa catur-vidha-bhūta-nikāyānāṁ brahmādi-stamba-paryantānām*

**Комментарий:**

He offers respects to the sun. It is not a fault, since the sun god is not conceived as an independent entity, but having Paramātmā within.

### Text 74

*yajurbhir akaroc chākhā daśa pañca śatair vibhuḥ*

## Chapter Seven

### Text 4

*nakṣatrakalpaḥ śāntiś ca kaśyapāṅgirasādayaḥ*  
*ete ātharvaṇācāryāḥ śṛṇu paurāṇikān mune*

**Перевод:**

**Nakṣatrakalpa, Śāntikalpa, Kaśyapa, Āṅgirasa and others were also among the** ***ācāryas*** **of the Atharva Veda. Now, O sage, listen as I name the authorities on Purāṇic literature.**

**Комментарий:**

The divisions of the fifth Veda are now described.

### Text 6

*adhīyanta vyāsa-śiṣyāt saṁhitāṁ mat-pitur mukhāt*  
*ekaikām aham eteṣāṁ śiṣyaḥ sarvāḥ samadhyagām*

**Перевод:**

**Each of them studied one of the six anthologies of the Purāṇas from my father, Romahārṣaṇa, who was a disciple of Vyāsadeva. I became the disciple of these six authorities and thoroughly learned everything from them.**

**Комментарий:**

He studied with two groups of three teachers *(ekaikam*). This should be correlated with the list mentioned in verse 23. The saṁhitā is sometimes classed as one group. Bhāgavatam is called the paramahaṁsa-saṁhitā. Sometimes they are divided one by one, such as the Sanatkumāra –saṁhitā in the Skanda Purāṇa. Later Bhāgavatam attained astonishing excellence from the mouth of Śukadeva. Yajur Veda of the Vājasenya branch became excellent through the sun god.

### Text 7

*kaśyapo 'haṁ ca sāvarṇī rāma-śiṣyo 'kṛtavranaḥ*  
*adhīmahi vyāsa-śiṣyāc catvāro mūla-saṁhitāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The sage Kaśyapa and I, along with Sāvarṇi and Akṛtavraṇa, a disciple of Rāma, learned the four basic collections of the Puraṇas from Romaharṣaṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Mūla-saṁhitā refers to special Itihāsas. They are the root of many other Itihāsas.

*itihāsa-purāṇānāṁ pitā me romaharṣaṇaḥ \|\|*

**My father Romaharṣaṇa became learned in the histories and Purāṇas. (SB** [**1.4.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/4.22)**)**

This refers to Mahābhārata and other works. The words *śṛnu paurānikān* is a general statement (including Itihāsas).

### Text 9-10

*sargo 'syātha visargaś ca vṛtti-rakṣāntarāṇi ca*  
*vaṁśo vaṁśānucarītaṁ saṁsthā hetur apāśrayaḥ*  
*daśabhir lakṣaṇair yuktaṁ purāṇaṁ tad-vido viduḥ*  
*kecit pañca-vidhaṁ brahman mahad-alpa-vyavasthayā*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***brāhmaṇ*****a! Authorities on the matter understand a Purāṇa to contain ten characteristic topics: the creation of this universe, the subsequent creation of worlds and beings, the maintenance of all living beings, their sustenance, the rule of various Manus, the dynasties of great kings, the activities of such kings, annihilation, liberation and the supreme shelter. Other scholars state that the great Purāṇas deal with these ten topics, while lesser Purāṇas may deal with five.**

**Комментарий:**

Sarga etc. were explained in the Second Canto.

**Tattva Sandarbha 61:**

Speaking of the Bhāgavatam’s characteristics as a Mahāpurāṇa in another way, it is stated that the Lord (not the jīva) is the shelter or āśraya:

*sargo 'syātha visargaś ca vṛtti-rakṣāntarāṇi ca*  
*vaṁśo vaṁśānucarītaṁ saṁsthā hetur apāśrayaḥ*  
*daśabhir lakṣaṇair yuktaṁ purāṇaṁ tad-vido viduḥ*  
*kecit pañca-vidhaṁ brahman mahad-alpa-vyavasthayā*

**O brāhmaṇa! Authorities on the matter understand a Purāṇa to contain ten characteristic topics: the creation of this universe, the subsequent creation of worlds and beings, the maintenance of all living beings, their sustenance, the rule of various Manus, the dynasties of great kings, the activities of such kings, annihilation, the jīva and the supreme shelter. Other scholars state that the great Purāṇas deal with these ten topics, while lesser Purāṇas may deal with five. (SB** [**12.7.9-10**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/7.9)**)**

*Antarāṇi* means Manvantaras. Also there are five characteristics according to some:

*sargaś ca pratisargaś ca vaṁśo manvantarāṇi ca \|*  
*vaṁśānucaritaṁ ceti purāṇaṁ pañca-lakṣaṇam \|\|*

**The five characteristics of a Purāṇa are sarga, pratisarga, vaṁśa, manvantaras and vaṁśānucaritam.**

The different opinion (five characteristic instead of ten) is based on the distinction between major and minor Purāṇas, whether they are voluminous or small. Though Viṣṇu Purāṇa and others have ten characteristics, some are said to be minor because of the prominence of the five characteristics.

This does not mean that the ten subjects are discussed in order in the different cantos of Bhāgavatam since Bhāgavatam has Twelve Cantos. Since the ten topics are mentioned in the Second Canto, counting from the Third Canto to the Twelfth Canto to make ten topics does not match the contents. For instance destruction *(nirodha*) is discussed in the Tenth, Eleventh and Twelfth Cantos but not in the Eighth Canto (*nirodha* is the eighth topic). It is also not possible to match other topics with other cantos successively. Śrīdhara Svāmī says:

*daśame kṛṣṇa-sat-kīrti-vitānāyopavarṇyate \|*  
*dharma-glāni-nimittas tu nirodho duṣṭa-bhūbhujām \|\|*  
*prākṛtādi-caturdhā yo nirodhaḥ sa tu varṇitaḥ \|*

**In the Tenth Canto in order to spread the pure glory of Kṛṣṇa, destruction (*****nirodha*****) of the evil kings because they caused a decline in dharma is described. The four types of destruction starting with prakṛti were described previously.**

He states that in the Tenth Canto the main description is that of Kṛṣṇa, the *āśraya*. *Daśame daśamaṁ lakṣyam āśritāśraya-vigraham*: in the Tenth Canto there is a description of the āśraya-vigraha, qualified as the tenth topic. One may find the other topics in many cantos as well.

In general, all topics are described in all cantos either secondarily or primarily. That is Śrīdhara Svāmī’s opinion. It has already been stated

*daśamasya viśuddhy-arthaṁ navānām iha lakṣaṇam \|*  
*varṇayanti mahātmānaḥ śrutenārthena cāñjasā \|\|*

**The great devotees such as Vidura and Maitreya describe properly the nine topics in order to impart the highest knowledge of the tenth topic, through the words of the scripture and stories to illustrate their meaning. (SB** [**2.10.1-2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/10.1)**)**

Thus all topics are described in all places. Therefore the First and Second Cantos are included as part of the Bhāgavatam. (They are not excluded because there are ten topics). It is concluded there is no sequence in narrating the ten topics.

### Text 12

*avyākṛta-guṇa-kṣobhān mahatas tri-vṛto 'hamaḥ*  
*bhūta-sūkṣmendriyārthānāṁ sambhavaḥ sarga ucyate*

**Перевод:**

**From the agitation of the original modes within the unmanifest material nature, the mah*****at-tattva*** **arises. From the** ***mahat-tattva*** **comes the element false ego, which divides into three aspects. This threefold false ego further manifests as the elements, the senses and sense** ***devatās.*** **The generation of all these is called primary creation.**

**Комментарий:**

**Tattva Sandarbha 62:**

From the agitation of the guṇas of pradhāna, mahat-tattva arises. From that, the three types of ahaṅkāra arise. From that arise the sense objects or tanmātras (*bhūta-mātra*), the senses *(indriya)* and the elements (*ārthānām*). The devatās of the senses are also indicated by this. This creation (*sambhavaḥ*) is called sarga or kāraṇa-sṛṣṭi.

*puruṣānugṛhītānām eteṣāṁ vāsanā-mayaḥ*  
*visargo 'yaṁ samāhāro bījād bījaṁ carācaram*

**Перевод:**

**The aggregate, the total and individual** ***jīvas,*** **which is effect of the all previous mentioned elements which have been favored by the Lord, which are predominated by impressions of sin and piety, is called** ***visarga.*** **It consists of all moving and non-moving beings who continue like a seed producing a seed.**

**Комментарий:**

**Tattva Sandarbha 62:**

The aggregate (*saṁhāraḥ*) of mahat-tattva and other elements (*eteṣām*), objects of mercy of Paramātmā (*puruṣānugṛhītānām),* filled with impressions of previous karmas of the jīva (*vāsanā- mayaḥ*), is called visarga. It is continuous flow of living beings moving and nonmoving which are like seeds which produce more seeds.

### Text 13

*vṛttir bhūtāni bhūtānāṁ carāṇām acarāṇi ca*  
*kṛtā svena nṛṇāṁ tatra kāmāc codanayāpi vā*

**Перевод:**

***Vṛtti*** **means the process of sustenance, by which the moving beings live upon the nonmoving beings. For a human,** ***vṛtti*** **specifically means acting for one's livelihood in a manner suited to his personal nature, either by desire or according to regulation.**

Generally non-moving entities (*acarāṇi bhūtāni*) are sustenance for the moving beings (*carānām bhūtānām*). The word *ca* indicates that the moving beings also eat other moving beings out of lust. For humans there is regulated livelihood according to one’s nature *(svena*), out of desire or rules. That is called *vṛtti* or sustenance. (This is substituted for *sthāna* or *sthiti* in the first list.)

**Tattva Sandarbha 63:**

The cause of *sarga* of the universe *(asya*) is the jīva because he performs actions out of ignorance. Some call this cause *(yam*) sleeping (in Mahā-viṣṇu) because prominence of consciousness and others say jīva is invisible or covered because of prominence of upādhis.

### Text 19

*vyatirekānvayo yasya jāgrat-svapna-suṣuptiṣu*  
*māyā-mayeṣu tad brahma jīva-vṛttiṣv apāśrayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**That Brahman which is present in all objects as its cause, in states of waking, dreaming and deep sleep, but which is separate from them, is called** ***apāśraya.***

**Комментарий:**

**Tattva Sandarbha 63:**

Because it would be contrary to what Vyāsa realized in samādhi, the pure jīva cannot be described as the shelter in this verse (by taking Brahman to mean the jīva). The meaning is this. In conditions of waking, dreaming and deep sleep produced by māyā-śakti, and in all conditions of māyā, composed of material elements like mahat-tattva *(māyā-mayeṣu),* Brahman, functioning separately (*vyatireka*) as a pure svarūpa or in relationships *(anvaya)* as the supreme witness, is the shelter for all situations of the jīva functioning either in his pure svarūpa or with upādhis*. Apāśrayaḥ* means the Lord is the shelter surpassing everything else. *Apa* means “excluding” and thus means “surpassing.”

### Text 20

*padārtheṣu yathā dravyaṁ san-mātraṁ rūpa-nāmasu*  
*bījādi-pañcatāntāsu hy avasthāsu yutāyutam*

**Перевод:**

**Just as earth is present in a pot and also exists outside the pot, just as sense objects exist in objects with name and form and also exist beyond them, that entity which exists in all states of the body from conception to death is called** ***apāśraya.***

### Text 21

*virameta yadā cittaṁ hitvā vṛtti-trayaṁ svayam*  
*yogena vā tadātmānaṁ vedehāyā nivartate*

**Перевод:**

**When the mind, gives up the three states of waking, dreaming and deep sleep by** ***bhakti-yoga,*** **and spontaneously enjoys the lotus feet of the Lord, at that time he realizes the Lord and gives up all material desires.**

**Комментарий:**

**Tattva Sandarbha 63:**

Just as materials like earth *(dravyam*) exist whether present or not present (*yutāyutam*) in pots or other objects (*padārtheṣu*) with names and forms since these can be perceived without the effect (pot), so the pure consciousness of the jīva (*tan-mātram*) is joined to the body between birth and death in nine states by ignorance and is disunited from the body without effort. Understanding this nature of the pure jīva, a person becomes detached from the body and becomes qualified for searching out the *apāśraya.*

The mind, giving up the three states of waking, dreaming and deep sleep (*vṛtti-trayam*), becomes detached from matter (*virameta).* It then realizes Paramātmā (*ātmānam*) either spontaneously (*svayam*) like Vāmadeva, seeing the Lord in various objects in the world, or by performance of bhakti-yoga (*yogena*) like Devahūti. The person gives up all actions *(ihāyāḥ*) other than cultivating love of the Lord.

## Chapter Eight

### Text 1

**Комментарий:**

Not able to leave this Purāṇa filled with stories of various powers of the Lord which was ending, Śaunaka asks about an unrelated story of Mārkaṇḍeya, famous in his family line.

### Text 2-5

*āhuś cirāyuṣam ṛṣiṁ mṛkaṇḍu-tanayaṁ janāḥ*  
*yaḥ kalpānte hy urvarito yena grastam idaṁ jagat*

*naivādhunāpi bhūtānāṁ samplavaḥ ko 'pi jāyate*

*vaṭa-patra-puṭe tokaṁ śayānaṁ tv ekam adbhutam*  
*eṣa naḥ saṁśayo bhūyān sūta kautūhalaṁ yataḥ*

**Authorities say that Mārkaṇḍeya, the son of Mṛkaṇḍu, was an exceptionally long-lived sage who was the only survivor at the end of Brahmā's day, when the entire universe was merged in the flood of annihilation. But this same Mārkaṇḍeya, the foremost descendant of Bhṛgu, took birth in my own family during the current day of Brahmā, and we have not yet seen any total annihilation in this day of Brahmā. Also, it is well known that Mārkaṇḍeya, while wandering helplessly in the great ocean of annihilation, saw a wonderful personality—an infant boy lying alone within the fold of a banyan leaf. O Sūta, I am most bewildered and curious about this great sage, Mārkaṇḍeya. O great** ***yogī,*** **you are accepted as the authority on all the Purāṇas. Therefore kindly dispel my confusion.**

**Комментарий:**

At the end of the kalpa Mārkaṇḍeya remained (*urvaritaḥ*). He continued into the next day. How is it known that it is the present kalpa? This kalpa has not seen a destruction. At the end of the kalpa there is a necessary destruction and at this time all the species have continued without break. What is the proof that he remained during the destruction? It is well known (*kila*) that he saw the Lord in the water.

**While greed personified, spring and the other servants of Indra all tried to agitate Mārkaṇḍeya's mind, Cupid fixed his five-headed arrow on his bow.**

**Комментарий:**

The servants of Indra began (to try) to agitate his mind.

Puñjikasthali is her name.

### Text 33-34

*tau śukla-kṛṣṇau nava-kañja-locanau*

The tall figures, austerity personified, flashing with yellow rays like lightning, were being worshiped by the foremost** ***devatās.***

### Text 40

*śrī-mārkaṇḍeya uvāca*  
*kiṁ varṇaye tava vibho yad-udīrito 'suḥ*  
*saṁspandate tam anu vāṅ-mana-indriyāṇi*  
*spandanti vai tanu-bhṛtām aja-śarvayoś ca*  
*svasyāpy athāpi bhajatām asi bhāva-bandhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Mārkaṇḍeya said: O Almighty Lord, how can I possibly describe you? You awaken the vital air, which then impels the mind, senses and power of speech to act. This is true for all ordinary conditioned souls and even Brahmā and Śiva. So it is certainly true for me. Nevertheless, you become the intimate friend of those who worship you.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 144:**

O Lord! How can I describe you? How much of your mercy can I describe? Because by you the prāṇa, being inspired, carries out its functions and after that (*anu*), speech, mind and senses act. The word *vai* expresses that this is well known. The śruti also expresses this positively and negatively with *śrotrasya śrotram*: you are the ear of the ear. (Kena Upaniṣad [1.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/kena/1.2)) This is so not only for ordinary living entities but also for Brahmā and Śiva. Therefore it is also true for me. No one is independent. For the person who worships you by speech and mind inspired by you, you are the friend (*bandhuḥ*), because of bhakti (*bhāva)* given by you.

### Text 44

*tad vai bhajāmy ṛta-dhiyas tava pāda-mūlaṁ*  
*hitvedam ātma-cchadi cātma-guroḥ parasya*  
*dehādy apārtham asad antyam abhijña-mātraṁ*  
*vindeta te tarhi sarva-manīṣitārtham*

**Перевод:**

**After giving up the covering on the** ***ātmā*** **such as possessiveness of body and house, which are all useless, insignificant, and temporary, I worship the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord, the** ***guru*** **of the** ***jīva,*** **who has true knowledge. A person should attain you alone, who know the devotee’s service, and he will attain all his desires.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī has *ātma-mātram* instead of *abhijña-matram*. Then the meaning is “A person should attain only you, who is mistakenly perceived to be the ātmā.”

### Text 45

*sattvaṁ rajas tama itīśa tavātma-bandho*  
*māyā-mayāḥ sthiti-layodaya-hetavo 'sya*  
*līlā dhṛtā yad api sattva-mayī praśāntyai*  
*nānye nṛṇāṁ vyasana-moha-bhiyaś ca yābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**O master of life forms! O friend of the** ***jīva*****! The causes of creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe of all beings, including the** ***devatās*****, are the three** ***guṇas*****, made of** ***māyā.*** **Though you accept pastimes of** ***sattva-guṇa*** **for auspiciousness, you do not accept** ***rajas*** **and** ***tama*****s at all, since they produce pain, illusion and fear for mankind.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord’s sattva is different from prakṛti.

**Bhagavat Sandarbha 7:**

O Lord (*īśa*)! Sattva, rajas and tamas are your pastimes created by māyā. The pastime causes creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe. The sattva portion is for great happiness (*praśāntyai)*. The other two, rajas and tamas are not for this (*nānye*). Not only are they devoid of happiness but are undesirable, producing pain, illusion and fear.

### Text 46

*tasmāt taveha bhagavann atha tāvakānāṁ*  
*śuklāṁ tanuṁ sva-dayitāṁ kuśalā bhajanti*  
*yat sātvatāḥ puruṣa-rūpam uśanti sattvaṁ*  
*loko yato 'bhayam utātma-sukhaṁ na cānyat*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! Therefore in this world, the wise worship your pure form and your devotee’s pure form, and not the forms made of material** ***guṇas,*** **since the devotees like Nārada recognize your form as** ***śuddha-sattva*****, from which arises Vaikuṇṭha, a place which has no fear of falling and is endowed with spiritual bliss.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhagavat Sandarbha 7:**

O Lord (*bhagavan*)! Therefore those who are skillful worship your form which controls sattva, the form of Viṣṇu (*śuklam)*, and not other forms like Brahmā and Śiva. Nor do they worship Dakṣa or Bhairava. The form is dear to you *(sva-dayitām*) because it gives joy to the people.

“But my form is famous as sattva. How can it be beyond māyā?” That is not so. The devotees (*sātvatāḥ)* consider that particular sattva to be a manifestation from your form, since from that sattva arises Vaikuṇṭha which is without fear and has as its very nature (*ātmā*) the bliss of Parabrahman. It is not the sattva arising from material prakṛti. This sattva is a function of the svarūpa-śakti and is self-manifesting. Thus it is said:

*sattvaṁ viśuddhaṁ vasudeva-śabditaṁ*  
*yad īyate tatra pumān apāvṛtaḥ*  
*sattve ca tasmin bhagavān vāsudevo*  
*hy adhokṣajo me namasā vidhīyate*

**The pure antaḥkaraṇa (sattvaṁ viśuddham) is called Vasudeva, because there the Supreme Lord appears without coverings. In that antaḥkaraṇa, I worshipped the Supreme Lord Vāsudeva, beyond the material senses, by offering respects. (SB** [**4.3.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/3.23)**)**

Material sattva, which is impure, is the cause of perceiving material objects previously invisible. Viśuddha-sattva is a function of the cit-śakti for perceiving spiritual objects. Since Vaikuṇṭha functions through the svarūpa śakti, it is non-different from the Lord’s svarūpa. Thus the phrase *ātmā-sukham* is used. This is confirmed by the following which indicates that the Lord’s form is śuddha-sattva:

*rūpaṁ yad etad avabodha-rasodayena*  
*śaśvan-nivṛtta-tamasaḥ sad-anugrahāya*  
*ādau gṛhītam avatāra-śataika-bījaṁ*  
*yan-nābhi-padma-bhavanād aham āvirāsam*

**By your mercy to the devotees, by your cit-śakti, you have shown at the beginning this form which is eternally devoid of māyā, which is the seed of countless avatāras, and from whose navel appeared the lotus from which I have appeared. (SB** [**3.9.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/9.2)**)**

If the form of the Lord and Vaikuṇṭha were made of material sattva, such statements would be useless and fearlessness would not be present there. In verse 45 and 46 the words *rajas* and *tamas* are repeated with the word *anye* and *anyat* to emphasis their non-existence in the spiritual world. Different from that (*anyat*) is the sattva arising from the Lord’s svarūpa.

### Text 48

*yaṁ vai na veda vitathākṣa-pathair bhramad-dhīḥ*  
*santaṁ svakeṣv asuṣu hṛdy api dṛk-patheṣu*  
*tan-māyayāvṛta-matiḥ sa u eva sākṣād*  
*ādyas tavākhila-guror upasādya vedam*

**Перевод:**

**Persons with intelligence bewildered by deceptive sense perception cannot know you who are situated in their senses, life airs and hearts. Persons whose intelligence is covered by** ***māyā*** **cannot even know you when you appear as an** ***avatāra*** **within their vision. Even Brahmā who obtained the Vedas from you, the** ***guru*** **of all beings, cannot know you.**

**Комментарий:**

Though you are situated in their action senses (*svakeṣu asuṣu*) and knowledge senses (*dṛk-patheṣu*), and in the heart, consisting of both, they cannot know you*. Nāhaṁ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yoga-māyā-samāvṛtaḥ*: I am not revealed to the demons, being covered by my māyā. (BG [7.25](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/7.25))

## Chapter Nine

**The Supreme Lord said: My dear Mārkaṇḍeya, you are the best of all learned** ***brāhmaṇas.*** **You have perfected your life by practicing fixed meditation, by undeviating** ***bhakti*** **to me, along with austerities, study of the Vedas and adherence to regulative principles.**

You have become perfected by pure bhakti, which produces concentration (*ātma-samādhinā*).

*śrī-ṛṣir uvāca*

**The sage said: O Lord of lords! You have all excellent qualities! O Lord Acyuta, remover all distress for the surrendered! That I have seen you is enough of a benediction.**

**Комментарий:**

I have had sufficient blessings, in that I have seen you.

*gṛhītvājādayo yasya śrīmat-pādābja-darśanam*

**Such** ***devatās*** **as Lord Brahmā achieved success by seeing your lotus feet in their minds by mature** ***yoga*** **practice. And now, my Lord, you have appeared before my eyes.**

From seeing your feet, Brahmā and others have become successful.

### Text 6

*athāpy ambuja-patrākṣa puṇya-śloka-śikhāmaṇe*

**Комментарий:**

I have curiosity arising in this present birth (*athāpi*). “You can see my māyā when you look at this world.” By that māyā, through my inquiry, other people including devatās should understand the destruction (*bidhām*) at the end of what exists (*sat).*

### Text 8-9

*tam eva cintayann artham ṛṣiḥ svāśrama eva saḥ*

But sometimes, overwhelmed by waves of love for the Lord, Mārkaṇḍeya would forget to perform his regular worship.**

**Комментарий:**

Thinking of the spiritual form of the Lord (*tam artham*), not of seeing māyā, he meditated on the Lord. This illustrated that according to one’s actions one obtains results.

### Text 33

## Chapter Ten

### Text 9

*tayor āgamanaṁ sākṣād īśayor jagad-ātmanoḥ*  
*na veda ruddha-dhī-vṛttir ātmānaṁ viśvam eva ca*

**Перевод:**

**Because Mārkaṇḍeya's material mind had stopped functioning, the sage failed to notice that Lord Śiva and his wife, the controllers of the universe, had personally come to see him. Mārkaṇḍeya was unaware of either himself or the external world.**

**Комментарий:**

His samādhi is described. He did not see that Śiva and his wife (*tayoḥ*) had come. They are called souls of the universe (*jagad-ātmanoḥ*) because they are the Lord’s aṁśa and his śakti. Because he had attained pure bhakti as mentioned in verse 6. This samādhi is different from the samādhi of Brahman called asamprajñāta.

### Text 10

*bhagavāṁs tad abhijñāya giriśo yoga-māyayā*  
*āviśat tad-guhākāśaṁ vāyuś chidram iveśvaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Understanding the situation, the powerful and capable Śiva employed his mystic power to enter within the space within Mārkaṇḍeya's heart, just as the wind passes through an opening.**

**Комментарий:**

Śiva (*bhagavān*) understood that though Viṣṇu had disappeared on his own, he could not destroy the concentration of Mārkaṇḍeya. He wanted to show the world Mārkaṇḍeya’s fixed devotion (*tad abhijñāya*). Or the Lord, understanding Śiva’s desire, became Śiva by yoga-māyā, entering his form, and then entered the heart of Mārkaṇḍeya since he is fully capable (*īśvaraḥ*). Śrīdhara Svāmī says that Nārāyaṇa pacified his efforts to see him by his māyā in the form of Śiva.

### Text 20-21

*brāhmaṇāḥ sādhavaḥ śāntā niḥsaṅgā bhūta-vatsalāḥ*  
*ekānta-bhaktā asmāsu nirvairāḥ sama-darśinaḥ*  
*sa-lokā loka-pālās tān vandanty arcanty upāsate*  
*ahaṁ ca bhagavān brahmā svayaṁ ca harir īśvaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The inhabitants, the ruling** ***devatās*** **of all planets, Brahmā, the Supreme Lord and I, glorify, worship and assist the saintly** ***brāhmaṇas*****, who are peaceful, free of material attachment, compassionate to all living beings, purely devoted to us, devoid of hatred and endowed with equal vision.**

### Text 22

*na te mayy acyute 'je ca bhidām aṇv api cakṣate*  
*nātmanaś ca janasyāpi tad yuṣmān vayam īmahi*

**Перевод:**

**These devotees do not differentiate between Viṣṇu, Brahmā and me, nor do they differentiate between themselves and other living beings. Therefore, we know that your devotees are dearer to the Lord than others.**

**Комментарий:**

Because the brāhmaṇas see all equally, they do not see difference among us. That is true, but still we know (*īmahi)* that you devotees are dearer to the Lord than even the brāhmaṇa.

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 106:**

Śiva speaks to the Pracetas:

*atha bhāgavatā yūyaṁ priyāḥ stha bhagavān yathā*  
*na mad bhāgavatānāṁ ca preyān anyo ’sti karhicit*

**You devotees are dear to me just as the Supreme Lord is dear to me. The devotees hold me dear, just as they hold the Lord dear. (SB** [**4.24.30**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/24.30)**)**

If they were completely non different Mārkaṇḍeya would not have broken his samādhi. In the end Śiva distinguishes the Lord from himself. *svayaṁ ca harir īśvaraḥ*: Viṣṇu is directly the Lord. (SB [12.10.21](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/10.20)) Viṣṇu is the main person.

Difference of the Lord from Śiva is also stated in SB [1.2.24](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/2.24).

*pārthivād dāruṇo dhūmas tasmād agnis trayīmayaḥ \|*  
*tamasas tu rajas tasmāt sattvaṁ yad brahma-darśanam \|\|*

**Smoke is superior to dull wood, and fire, sacred to the Vedas, is superior to smoke. Similarly rajas is superior to tamas, and sattva is superior to rajas, since it is favorable for realizing the Lord.**

Śiva also says:

*yo hi māṁ draṣṭum iccheta brahmāṇaṁ vā pitāmaham \|*  
*draṣṭavyas tena bhagavān vāsudevaḥ pratāpavān \|\|*

**The person who desires to see me (Śiva) or Brahmā should first see powerful Vāsudeva. (Brahma Purāṇa 226.46)**

This means that by knowing the Lord all others are known.

### Text 23

*na hy am-mayāni tīrthāni na devāś cetanojjhitāḥ*  
*te punanty uru-kālena yūyaṁ darśana-mātrataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Mere bodies of water do not constitute holy places, nor are lifeless statues actual deities. These things purify a person only after a considerable time. But devotees like you purify immediately, just by being seen.**

**Комментарий:**

*Devāḥ* refers to deities of Indra etc. not the deities of the Lord since the deities of the Lord are worshipped even by you and others.

### Text 24

*brāhmaṇebhyo namasyāmo ye 'smad-rūpaṁ trayī-mayam*  
*bibhraty ātma-samādhāna- tapaḥ-svādhyāya-saṁyamaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore I offer my obeisances unto the** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **who, by meditating upon Viṣṇu, performing austerities, engaging in Vedic study and following regulative principles, sustain within themselves the three Vedas, which are non-different from Viṣṇu, Brahmā and me.**

**Комментарий:**

I respect the brāhmaṇas because they sustain the Vedas (*trayī-mayam*). These assist in realizing the Lord.

### Text 26

*sūta uvāca*  
*iti candra-lalāmasya dharma-guhyopabṛṁhitam*  
*vaco 'mṛtāyanam ṛṣir nātṛpyat karṇayoḥ piban*

**Перевод:**

**Sūta Gosvāmī said: Drinking with his ears Śiva's words, full of** ***bhakti*** **and intimate teachings, Mārkaṇḍeya could not be satiated.**

**Комментарий:**

He drank Śiva’s words full of bhakti for the Lord (*dharma-guhya*), the shelter of the highest bliss (*amṛtāyanam*). He could not be fully satisfied, since he himself was praised.

### Text 27

*sa ciraṁ māyayā viṣṇor bhrāmitaḥ karśito bhṛśam*  
*śiva-vāg-amṛta-dhvasta- kleśa-puñjas tam abravīt*

**Перевод:**

**Mārkaṇḍeya, having been forced by Viṣṇu's illusory energy to wander about for a long time in the water of dissolution, had become extremely exhausted. But Śiva's words of nectar vanquished his accumulated suffering. Thus he addressed Śiva.**

**Комментарий:**

He was made to wander about by Viṣṇu’s māyā, having accepted it by his own will.

Śiva was shown to be absorbed in Viṣṇu.

### Text 29

*dharmaṁ grāhayituṁ prāyaḥ pravaktāraś ca dehinām*  
*ācaranty anumodante kriyamāṇaṁ stuvanti ca*

**Перевод:**

**Generally it is to induce embodied souls to accept religious principles that the authorized teachers exhibit ideal behavior, encouraging and praising** ***dharma*** **which is being performed.**

**Комментарий:**

Generally they teachers act properly to induce people, who think that actions of their bodies are their actions (*dehīnām)* to accept dharma. Sometimes they do so by their own taste. The speakers (*pravaktāraḥ*) are distinguished from them.

### Text 30

*naitāvatā bhagavataḥ sva-māyā-maya-vṛttibhiḥ*  
*na duṣyetānubhāvas tair māyinaḥ kuhakaṁ yathā*

**Перевод:**

**Such behavior of the Supreme Lord, which the Lord produces by his own bewildering potency, does not spoil his power any more than a magician's powers are diminished by his exhibition of tricks.**

**Комментарий:**

Accepting the Lord as the chief of these persons, he denies any fault in the Lord in three verses. The reason that the Lord is not at fault is explained. The power of the Lord is not spoiled by his actions (*vṛttibhiḥ*) which are full of his mercy (*māyā-maya*), being controlled by his affection, for his devotees (*sva)*. He thinks, “I should do this for my devotees.” An opposite example is given. The power of a magician are spoiled by his tricks. Another version has *na duṣyeta* (instead of *praduṣyeta*). The Lord’s powers are not spoiled as the magician’s powers are spoiled.

### Text 31-32

*sṛṣṭvedaṁ manasā viśvam ātmanānupraviśya yaḥ*  
*guṇaiḥ kurvadbhir ābhāti karteva svapna-dṛg yathā*  
*tasmai namo bhagavate tri-guṇāya guṇātmane*  
*kevalāyādvitīyāya gurave brahma-mūrtaye*

**Перевод:**

**I offer my obeisances to that Supreme Lord, who has created this entire universe simply by his desire and has then entered into it as** ***antaryāmī.*** **By making the modes of nature act, he seems to be the direct creator of this world, just as a dreamer seems to be acting within his dream. I offer respects to the Lord, who manifests the three** ***guṇas,*** **who controls the** ***guṇas,*** **who is pure, without equal, who is the** ***guru,*** **the Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses show how the Lord is not touched by fault. Having created the universe by his mind (*manasā)* or his will, using matter made of guṇas, entering it personally (*ātmanā*), establishing it by entering, and destroying it as well, he seemed to be the agent of action, but he is not. The creation takes place by the guṇas (*kurvadbhiḥ*). The guṇas do everything, like consciousness acting by its proximity to the body. He is not directly involved since he acts by his will alone, which is an ābhāsa of being an agent. Even the jīva, the Lord’s taṭastha aṁśa, in creating a dream through ignorance, is actually not related to it. What to speak of the Lord.

I offer respects to the Lord who has three qualities of amṛta, abhaya and kṣema (*tri-guṇāya*), whose qualities like aiśvarya (*guṇa*) arise from his svarūpa *(ātmane*), who is devoid of association with prakṛti (*kevalāya*), who has no equal (*advitīyāya*), and who is thus the teacher of all (*gurave*) and whose svarūpa is the Vedas (*brahma)*, which teach about him.

(SB** [**12.8.40**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/8.40)**)**

Using the word *sākṣāt* differentiates her from māyā (who is a indirect form):

*vimohitā vikatthante mamāham iti durdhiyaḥ \|\|*

**The ignorant jīvas, bewildered by māyā who is ashamed to stand in sight of the Lord, boast about “I” and “mine.” (SB** [**2.5.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/5.13)**)**

Her constant nature is described:

*paramātmā hari devas tac-chaktiḥ śrīr ihoditā \|*  
*śrīr devī prakṛtiḥ proktā keśavaḥ puruṣaḥ smṛtaḥ \|*

**The Lord, ātmā in all beings, arose here with his śakti named Śrī. She is called Devī or Prakṛti, and he is called Keśava. She is never without Viṣṇu and he is never without her. (Hayaśīrṣa-pañcarātra)**

*avatāraṁ karoty eṣā tathā śrīs tat-sahāyinī \|\|*

**Just as the Lord of the universe Janārdana incarnates in this world, his consort also incarnates with him. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa** [**1.9.142**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vp/1.9.142)**)**

*aparaṁ tv akṣaraṁ yā sā prakṛtir jaḍa-rūpikā \|*  
*śrīḥ parā prakṛtiḥ proktā cetanā viṣṇu-saṁśrayā \|\|*  
*tām akṣaraṁ paraṁ prāhuḥ parataḥ param akṣaram \|*  
*harir evākhila-guṇa akṣara-trayam īritam \|\|*

**Prakṛti who is inferior and eternal is unconscious. Śrī is superior prakṛti, conscious and under the direct shelter of Viṣṇu. She is eternal and superior. The Lord endowed with all qualities is superior to her and eternal. Thus there are three eternal entities. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

### Text 3

**Комментарий:**

### Text 5

Having stated the general contents, the topics are listed in order, starting with Parīkṣit’s history.

In other works he is described without much investigation. Described in each verse of Bhāgavatam with various narrations, Bhagavān alone is revealed (*paṭhitah)* completely (*pari*).

### Text 4-9

*brāhmaṁ daśa sahasrāṇi pādmaṁ pañcona-ṣaṣṭi ca*  
*śrī-vaiṣṇavaṁ trayo-viṁśac catur-viṁśati śaivakam*

*catur-daśa bhaviṣyaṁ syāt tathā pañca-śatāni ca*  
*daśāṣṭau brahma-vaivartaṁ laiṅgam ekādaśaiva tu*  
*catur-viṁśati vārāham ekāśīti-sahasrakam*  
*skāndaṁ śataṁ tathā caikaṁ vāmanaṁ daśa kīrtitam*  
*kaurmaṁ sapta-daśākhyātaṁ mātsyaṁ tat tu catur-daśa*  
*ekona-viṁśat sauparṇaṁ brahmāṇḍaṁ dvādaśaiva tu*  
*evaṁ purāṇa-sandohaś catur-lakṣa udāhṛtaḥ*  
*tatrāṣṭadaśa-sāhasraṁ śrī-bhāgavataṁ iṣyate*

**The** ***Brahmā Purāṇa*** **consists of ten thousand verses, the** ***Padma Purāṇa*** **of fifty-five thousand,** ***Viṣṇu Purāṇa*** **of twenty-three thousand, the** ***Śiva Purāṇa*** **of twenty-four thousand and** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **of eighteen thousand. The** ***Nārada Purāṇa*** **has twenty-five thousand verses, the** ***Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa*** **nine thousand, the** ***Agni Purāṇa*** **fifteen thousand four hundred, the** ***Bhaviṣya Purāṇa*** **fourteen thousand five hundred, the** ***Brahma-vaivarta Purāṇa*** **eighteen thousand and the** ***Liṅga Purāṇa*** **eleven thousand. The** ***Varāha Purāṇa*** **contains twenty-four thousand verses, the** ***Skanda Purāṇa*** **eighty-one thousand one hundred, the** ***Vāmana Purāṇa*** **ten thousand, the** ***Kūrma Purāṇa*** **seventeen thousand, the** ***Matsya Purāṇa*** **fourteen thousand, the** ***Garuḍa Purāṇa*** **nineteen thousand and the** ***Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa*** **twelve thousand. Thus the total number of verses in all the Purāṇas is four hundred thousand. Eighteen thousand of these belong to the** ***Bhāgavatam*****.**

**Комментарий:**

The details are given in Matsya Purāṇa:

*aṣṭādaśa-purāṇāni kṛtvā satyavatī-sutaḥ*  
*bhāratākhyānam akhilaṁ cakre tad upabṛṁhitam*

**Having written the eighteen Purāṇas, Vyāsa wrote the Mahābhārata, with a hundred thousand verses, filled with the same material, and reinforcing the meaning of the Vedas. There is the excellent story of Rāma by Vālmīki, told originally by Brahmā in a billion verses. Nārada summarized it, and gave it to Vālmīki. It was recited by Vālmīki to the people to attain dharma, artha and kāma. The total number of verses in the Purāṇas and Itihāsas is 525,000.**

### Text 11-12

*ādi-madhyāvasāneṣu vairāgyākhyāna-saṁyutam*  
*hari-līlā-kathā-vrātā- mṛtānandita-sat-suram*  
*sarva-vedānta-sāraṁ yad brahmātmaikatva-lakṣaṇam*  
*vastv advitīyaṁ tan-niṣṭhaṁ kaivalyaika-prayojanam*

**Перевод:**

**From beginning to end, the** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **is full of the Lord’s pastimes which give bliss to devotees endowed with a sense of renunciation. This** ***Bhāgavatam*** **is the essence of all Vedānta philosophy because its subject matter is one Brahman, a substance with no duality. The main goal of the work is prema alone.**

**Комментарий:**

The Bhāgavatam is described in three verses. It was revealed by Kṛṣṇa (*bhagavatā*) to Brahmā, situated on the lotus in the navel of Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu.

*Sat* (in *sat-suram*) refers to ātmārāmas as in the following:

*itthaṁ satāṁ brahma-sukhānubhūtyā dāsyaṁ gatānāṁ para-daivatena*  
*māyāśritānāṁ nara-dārakeṇa sākaṁ vijahruḥ kṛta-puṇya-puñjāḥ*

**In this way with an abundance of splendor caused by Kṛṣṇa, they played with him, who was pure consciousness known as Brahman for the jñānīs, who was the Supreme Lord for the devotees with** ***dāsya-bhāva*** **and who was an ordinary human child for those covered by material illusion. (SB** [**10.12.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/12.7)**)**

“This jñāna is seen to become blue and then yellow (through awareness of objects). It is temporary. How can Bhāgavatam state that this jñāna is eternal and without a second?”

*sarva-vedānta-sāraṁ yad brahmātmaikatva-lakṣaṇam*  
*vastv advitīyaṁ tan-niṣṭhaṁ kaivalyaika-prayojanam*

**This Bhāgavatam** **is the essence of all Vedānta philosophy because its subject matter is one Brahman, a substance with no duality. The main goal of the work is pure bhakti. (SB** [**12.13.12**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/13.11)**)**

**Brahman’s svarūpa is described in the following** ***satyaṁ jñānam anantaṁ brahma*****: Brahman is existence, knowledge and bliss. (Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**2.1.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.1.1)**)**

He is the cause of the universe. That is stated in the following. *Yenāśrutaṁ śrutaṁ bhavati:* by this entity what was not heard becomes heard. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad [6.1.3](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/6.1.3)) *Yad-vijñānena sarva-vijñānaṁ pratijñātam:* by knowing him, everything becomes known. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad) This is the promise. *Sad eva saumyedam agra āsīt:* O gentle one, the Lord exists before everything else. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad [6.2.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/6.2.1)) The Lord is satya-saṅkalpa (all his desires are fulfilled). *Tad aikṣata bahu syām*: he glanced, thinking “Let me be many.” (Chāndogya Upaniṣad)

In the following, though the jīva is actually separate from the Lord, by calling him *idam (anena*), it is indicated that the jīva is a portion of the Paramātmā by calling him *ātmā.* He accompanies the Lord who is the greatest, who is endowed with his svarūpa and his śakti.

*anena jīvenātmanānupraviśya nāma-rūpe vyākaravāṇi*

**Let me enter with the jīva, ātmā, and divide name and form. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**6.3.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/6.3.2)**)**

In this statement spoken by the Lord, the jīva is considered to be one with the Lord since he is devoid of extreme difference, according to what Vyāsa saw in samādhi. The ātmā is an aṁśa of the Lord. *mamaivāṁśo jīva-loke*: the jīva is my aṁśa. (BG [15.7](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/15.7)) *aṁśo nānā-vyapadeśā*: the jīva is a part of the Lord because of many scriptural statements. (Brahma-sūtra [2.3.41](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/2.3.41)) Thus, in statements like *tattvam asi*: you are that (Chāndogya Upaniṣad [6.8.7](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/6.8.7)) there is identity of the Lord and jīva since the jīva is a conscious aṁśa of the Lord. Speaking of identity is a most effective means of presenting jñāna in the beginning.

The essence of all Vedānta (*sarva-vedānta-sāram*), having *advitīya- vastu* as the sole object (*niṣṭham*), is Bhāgavatam. This is understood from the previous verse:

*ādi-madhyāvasāneṣu vairāgyākhyāna-saṁyutam*  
*hari-līlā-kathā-vrātā- mṛtānandita-sat-suram*

**From beginning to end, the Śrīmad Bhāgavatam is full of the Lord’s pastimes which give bliss to devotees endowed with a sense of renunciation. (SB** [**12.13.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/13.11)**)**

*Kaivalyaika-prayojanam* is explained. *Kevala* means pure, such as pure bhakti which is devoid of cheating (SB [1.1.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/1.2)). That condition is called *kaivalya*. It is devoid of desires for material benefit or liberation. In the commentary on *kaivalya-sammata-pathas tv atha bhakti-yogaḥ* (SB [2.3.12](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/3.12)) Śrīdhara Svāmī says that *kaivalya sammata-patha* means bhakti-yoga. This is the method to attain the Lord. That bhakti is called prema, since that the chief type of bhakti. Thus *kaivalyam* means the goal, prema for Bhagavān. This is understood from the following:

*sālokya-sārṣṭi-sāmīpya- sārūpyaikatvam apy uta*  
*dīyamānaṁ na gṛhṇanti vinā mat-sevanaṁ janāḥ*

**The devotees do not accept living on my planet, having similar powers, staying close to me, or having a similar form, what to speak of merging into me, when these things are offered, unless it involves service to me. (SB** [**3.29.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/29.13)**)**

*kasmai yena vibhāsito 'yam atulo jñāna-pradīpaḥ purā*

*tac chuddhaṁ vimalaṁ viśokam amṛtaṁ satyaṁ paraṁ dhīmahi*

**Перевод:**

**I meditate upon that pure, spotless Nārāyaṇa, who is free from suffering and death and who previously revealed this incomparable lamp of knowledge to Brahmā. Brahmā then spoke it to the sage Nārada, who narrated it to Kṛṣṇa-dvaipāyana Vyāsa. Śrīla Vyāsa revealed this** ***Bhāgavatam*** **to the greatest of sages, Śukadeva Gosvāmī, and Śukadeva mercifully spoke it to Mahārāja Parīkṣit.**

**Комментарий:**

Having shown the general conclusion in verse 12, he gives the conclusion, showing that Bhagavān is the work’s ultimate goal, supporting the first verse *janmady asya* (SB [1.1.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/1.1))

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 106:**

This work was revealed (*vibhasitaḥ)* to Brahmā, situated in the lotus in the navel of Garbhodakaśāyī, by Bhagavān, whose form is described in the Second Canto, and who showed Mahā-vaikuṇṭha to Brahmā. The work was not produced at that time however. The Bhāgavatam (*ayam*) at the beginning of first parārdha (*purā*) was given by Brahmā (*tad-rūpena*) to Nārada and later by Nārada (*tad-rūpinā*) to Vyāsa (*kṛṣṇāya*). And by Vyāsa (*tad-ātmanā*) it was given to Śukadeva (*yogīndrāya*). It was given by Śukadeva (*tad-ātmanā,* understood) to Parīkṣit (*bhagavad-rātāya*). By using the phrases *tad-rūpeṇa, tad-rūpinā,* and *tad-ātmanā* it is indicated that not only was *catuḥślokī* revealed each time but that, absorbed in their forms (*tad-rūpeṇa* etc.), they revealed the complete Purāṇa. It should be understood that finally Sūta speaks to the sages and says “I also speak the Purāṇa to you.” However he does not utter this out of humility. Thus the greatness of the teachers of Bhāgavatam among all teachers is shown. The sampradāya through Saṅkarṣaṇa is not separately mentioned as it is included in the revelation of Vyāsa.

We should meditate on the supreme being known as Bhagavān (*satyam paraṁ dhīmahi*). *Param* means Bhagavān as in the *sahasra nāma stotra. Yat tat param anutttamam:* he is the supreme Bhagavān without compare. *Ādyo ‘vatāraḥ puruṣah parasya:* the first avatāra (Mahā-viṣṇu) is an expansion of Bhagavān (*parasya*). (SB [2.6.42](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/6.42))

This verse also explains that the work is the meaning of gāyatrī, shown by the word *dhīmahi,* since it inspires the intelligence of Brahmā and others. It is said *gāyatrī-bhāṣya-rūpo’ sau bhāratārtha-vinirṇayaḥ:* Bhāgavatam is the commentary on the gāyatrī and explains the Mahābhārata. (Garuḍa Purāṇa)

### Text 20

*namas tasmai bhagavate vāsudevāya sākṣiṇe*  
*ya idam kṛpayā kasmai vyācacakṣe mumukṣave*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Supreme Lord, Vāsudeva, the witness of my task, who mercifully explained this to Brahmā when he desired to give up everything devoid of bhakti.**

**Комментарий:**

Having summarized the meaning of Bhāgavatam, he offers respects to his guru, the supreme Lord. Brahmā desires to give up everything devoid of bhakti.

### Text 21

*yogīndrāya namas tasmai śukāya brahma-rūpiṇe*  
*saṁsāra-sarpa-daṣṭaṁ yo viṣṇu-rātam amūmucat*

**Перевод:**

**I offer my humble obeisances to Śukadeva Gosvāmī, the best of** ***yogīs*****, and the very form of** ***para-brahman*****, who saved Mahārāja Parīkṣit, bitten by the snake of material existence.**

**Комментарий:**

He offers respects to his teacher. Śukadeva was the chief of sages (*yogīndrāya*) spreading bhakti-yoga, concentrating completely on surrender to Kṛṣṇa. He brought about the appearance of the Lord (*brahma-rūpiṇe*). In verse 19 he is also referred to as *yogīndra*. Having remembering Śukadeva’s greatness he remembers his mercy.

*Saṁsāra* means that in which one wanders about (*saṁsarati*). It means the material world. Parīkṣit (*viṣṇu-rātam*) was bitten by the snake of the material world, whose poison spread everywhere, because he could not tolerate existence after Kṛṣṇa’s disappearance. He was pained by separation. Śukadeva freed him. He inspired him to cross over that suffering of separation. The suffering caused by Takṣaka was not considered by Parīkṣit or Śukadeva. The cause of Parīkṣit’s prema was getting mercy from the Lord (*viṣṇu-rātam*). He was a most intimate associate of the Lord.

### Text 22

*bhave bhave yathā bhaktiḥ pādayos tava jāyate*  
*tathā kuruṣva deveśa nāthas tvaṁ no yataḥ prabho*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord of lords! O master! Please grant us pure devotional service at your lotus feet, life after life.**

### Text 23

*nāma-saṅkīrtanaṁ yasya sarva-pāpa praṇāśanam*  
*praṇāmo duḥkha-śamanas taṁ namāmi hariṁ param*

**Перевод:**

**I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Supreme Lord, whose names when chanted, destroy all sins. Offering of obeisances to him destroys all suffering.**

**Комментарий:**

*nāma cintāmaṇiḥ kṛṣṇaś caitanya-rasa-vigrahaḥ \|*  
*pūrṇaḥ śuddho nitya-mukto ’bhinnatvān nāma-nāminoḥ \|\|*

**The name of the Lord is cintāmaṇi. It is Kṛṣṇa himself, a form of consciousness and rasa. It is complete, pure, and eternally liberated since there is no difference between the name and Kṛṣṇa himself. (Padma Purāṇa)**

*suvarṇa-varṇo hemāṅgo varāṅgaś candanāṅgadī \|*  
*sannyāsa-kṛt samaḥ śānto niṣṭhā-śānti-parāyaṇaḥ \|\|*

**He had a form like gold (*****suvarṇa-varṇaḥ*****). He had places desirable as gold (*****hemāṅgaḥ*****). He had beautiful limbs (*****varāṅgaḥ*****). He had armlets which please the devotees’ hearts (*****candanāṅgad*****ī). He took sannyāsa (*****sannyasa-kṛt*****). He reflected on the confidential pastimes of the Lord (*****śamaḥ*****). He was detached from everything except Kṛṣṇa (*****śāntaḥ*****). He was fixed in the sacrifice of chanting the Lord’s names (*****niṣṭhā*****). He pacified the advaitins who were against bhakti by performing kīrtana (*****śāntaḥ*****). He was the highest refuge for person of various bhāvas, up to mahābhāva (*****parāyaṇam*****).**

*evaṁ sahasra-nāmoktaṁ kṛṣṇa-caitanya-saṁjñitaḥ \|*  
*māṁ pāyād aparādhebhyaḥ sva-premāṁśena puṣyatu \|\|*

**Thus Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is described in the thousand names of Viṣṇu. May he protect me from offenses and nourish me with a portion of his prema.**

Here ends Krama Sandarbha, the seventh among the Sandarbhas which use Bhāgavatam as their source, and which contain the expert instructions of Rūpa and Sanātana Gosvāmīs, most revered members of the grand assembly of all Vaiṣṇavas, following the lotus feet of Lord Kṛṣṇa Caitanya-deva, who descended to distribute his worship, in order to purify Kali-yuga. All Glories to the daughter of Vṛṣabhānu. All glories to Kṛṣṇa.
