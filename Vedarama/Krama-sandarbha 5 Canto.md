# Krama-sandarbha 5 Canto

## Chapter One

### Text 1

*rājovāca*  
*priyavrato bhāgavata*  
*ātmārāmaḥ kathaṁ mune*  
*gṛhe 'ramata yan-mūlaḥ*  
*karma-bandhaḥ parābhavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Parīkṣit said: O sage! How did the great devotee and** ***ātmārāma*** **Priyavrata enjoy household life, which is a cause of bondage to** ***karma*** **and defeats even a pure soul?**

**Комментарий:**

Among ātmārāmas Priyavrata was a devotee (*bhāgavataḥ ātmārāmaḥ*).

*muktānām api siddhānāṁ nārāyaṇa-parāyaṇaḥ*  
*su-durlabhaḥ praśāntātmā koṭiṣv api mahā-mune*

**O great sage! Greater than those who are** ***jīvanmukta*** **and greater than those who attain liberation is the devotee of Lord Nārāyaṇa. Such a devotee, who is in** ***śānta*** **or other** ***rasas,*** **is very rare, even among ten million people. (SB** [**6.1.45**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/1.45)**)**

It will also be stated later that Priyavrata was chiefly a devotee. It was not a secondary quality. Because he was ātmārāma it was not possible for him to be attracted to family life. So this was even more impossible if he were a devotee. And thus he did not want liberation. Family life is the root of bondage of karma and covering of the svarūpa (*parābhava*).

### Text 2

*na nūnaṁ mukta-saṅgānāṁ*  
*tādṛśānāṁ dvijarṣabha*  
*gṛheṣv abhiniveśo 'yaṁ*  
*puṁsāṁ bhavitum arhati*

**Перевод:**

**O best of the** ***brāhmaṇas*****! Persons such as him, free from material attachments, should certainly not become absorbed in family life.**

**Комментарий:**

This is made clear in this verse.

### Text 3

*mahatāṁ khalu viprarṣe*  
*uttamaśloka-pādayoḥ*  
*chāyā-nirvṛta-cittānāṁ*  
*na kuṭumbe spṛhā-matiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O best of** ***brāhmaṇas*****! Great souls whose minds are blissfully engaged in meditation on the lotus feet of the Lord, which extinguish** ***saṁsāra,*** **are not so foolish to desire household life.**

**Комментарий:**

For the person whose mind is blissful in the shelter or beauty (*chāyā*) of the Lord’s lotus feet, his mind will not desire family life.

### Text 4

*saṁśayo 'yaṁ mahān brahman*  
*dārāgāra-sutādiṣu*  
*saktasya yat siddhir abhūt*  
*kṛṣṇe ca matir acyutā*

**Перевод:**

**Great** ***brāhmaṇa*****! My doubt is that though he was attached to wife, house and children, he still attained perfection and always thought of Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

He attained sāmpīya and other siddhis but that is not the only doubt. Though he was a household his mind was fixed in trying to attain Kṛṣṇa.

### Text 5

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*bāḍham uktaṁ bhagavata uttamaślokasya śrīmac-caraṇāravinda-makaranda-rasa āveśita-cetaso bhāgavata-paramahaṁsa-dayita-kathāṁ kiñcid antarāya-vihatāṁ svāṁ śivatamāṁ padavīṁ na prāyeṇa hinvanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: What you have said is true, but those whose minds are absorbed in the sweet taste of the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord do not generally give up the auspicious path of topics of the Lord, who is dear to the greatest devotees, though there may be some obstacle.**

**Комментарий:**

*Bhāgavata-paramahaṁsa* means a devotee among paramahaṁsas. They do not give up the most auspicious path of the Lord’s topics. The topics are auspicious since they produce absorption in the Lord.

### Text 6

*yarhi vāva ha rājan sa rāja-putraḥ priyavrataḥ parama-bhāgavato nāradasya caraṇopasevayāñjasāvagata-paramārtha-satattvo brahma-satreṇa dīkṣiṣyamāṇo 'vani-tala-paripālanāyāmnāta-pravara-guṇa-gaṇaikānta-bhājanatayā sva-pitropāmantrito bhagavati vāsudeva evāvyavadhāna-samādhi-yogena samāveśita-sakala-kāraka-kriyā-kalāpo naivābhyanandad yadyapi tad apratyāmnātavyaṁ tad-adhikaraṇa ātmano 'nyasmād asato 'pi parābhavam anvīkṣamāṇaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**O King! When Priyavrata, the son of the King, the greatest of devotees, who had become fully conversant with the highest goal and truth by serving the lotus feet of Nārada, and had attained initiation by meditation on** ***ātmā,*** **was requested by his father Manu to rule the earth because he possessed all the qualities mentioned in the scriptures for kingship, since he was completely absorbed with all his senses and actions in worshipping the Lord through unbroken concentration, he did not welcome the suggestion, though it could not to be refused. He considered that it would be unfavorable for him, since he would display lust and anger in himself when ruling the kingdom.**

**Комментарий:**

He was quite young at this time (*rāja-putraḥ*). As a natural devotee, he served Nārada’s lotus feet and was absorbed in worshipping Vāsudeva. He had begun meditating on the highest ātmā. Thus he was reluctant to rule the world. *Avagata-paramārtha-satattvam* means he had understood the highest truth. The activities of his senses and his body were absorbed in the Lord (*kāraṇāni*), offered to the Lord as mentioned in the Gītā:

*yat karoṣi yad aśnāsi yaj juhoṣi dadāsi yat \|*  
*yat tapasyasi kaunteya tat kuruṣva mad-arpaṇam \|\|*

**Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you sacrifice, whatever you give, and whatever austerity you perform, offer it to Me, O son of Kuntī. (BG** [**9.27**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.27)**)**

*brahmārpaṇaṁ brahma havir brahmāgnau brahmaṇā hutam \|*  
*brahmaiva tena gantavyaṁ brahma-karma-samādhinā \|\|*

**The oblations consisting of Brahman, with Brahman as the ladle, is offered into the fire which is Brahman, by the person who is Brahman. The person Brahman attains Brahman by being absorbed in actions of Brahman. (BG** [**4.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/4.24)**)**

*Brahma-satreṇa* means he meditated on or discussed with others only topics Bhagavān. This became a firm vow (*dīṣiṣyamānaḥ*): he did nothing else. He was intent on doing this as a rule.

### Text 21

*bhagavān api manunā yathāvad upakalpitāpacitiḥ priyavrata-nāradayor aviṣamam abhisamīkṣamāṇayor ātma sam avasthānam avāṅ-manasaṁ kṣayam avyavahṛtaṁ pravartayann agamat.*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā departed to his abode, beyond material dealings, beyond words and mind, after being worshiped suitably by Manu, while engaging Priyavrata and Nārada who looked on,**

**Комментарий:**

He went to Brahmaloka (*kṣayam*), his abode (*ātma-samavasthānam*), beyond words and mind. Having a vision of the Lord’s form, engaging Priyavrata and Nārada, he departed.

### Text 22

*manur api pareṇaivaṁ pratisandhita-manorathaḥ surarṣi-varānumatenātmajam akhila-dharā-maṇḍala-sthiti-guptaya āsthāpya svayam ati-viṣama-viṣaya-viṣa-jalāśayāśāyā upararāma.*

**Перевод:**

**His desire accomplished by Brahmā, Manu, with the permission of Nārada, established his son for maintaining proper rules of conduct on the whole earth, and became detached from the desire to enjoy the poisonous ocean of disturbing material affairs.**

**Комментарий:**

His desires were fulfilled by Brahmā. This took place before installing Priyavrata as the king. This is described in Viṣṇu Purāṇa. The third, fourth and fifth Manus were born from Priyavrata’s descendants. The sixth Manu, Cākṣuṣa was the end of his dynasty. Dakṣa, the son of the Pracetās, in Uttānapāda’s line, ruled during that manvantara. It would mean the younger son (Uttānapāda) marrying before the elder. The elder did not rule first. This is seen in other lines as well. When Vena died and the Pracetās were doing austerities, there was no king. But after a long time a son was born in Uttānapāda’s line.

*priyavratottānapādau*  
*manu-putrau mahaujasau*  
*tat-putra-pautra-naptṝṇām*  
*anuvṛttaṁ tad-antaram*

**Svāyambhuva Manu's two sons, Priyavrata and Uttānapāda, became very powerful, and their sons and grandsons were active during that period. (SB** [**4.1.10**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/1.10)**)**

Svārociṣa is also accepted in this line of Manus (son of Priyavrata, though he was also the son of Agni). Brahmā spoke to Pṛthu:

*na vadhyo bhavatām indro*  
*yad yajño bhagavat-tanuḥ*  
*yaṁ jighāṁsatha yajñena*  
*yasyeṣṭās tanavaḥ surāḥ*

**You should not kill Indra because he is the body of the Lord. You wish to kill Indra by a sacrifice in which the** ***devatās,*** **part of his body, are worshipped. (SB** [**4.19.30**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/19.30)**)**

His rulership with the sacrifice to compete with Indra was during Svāyambhuva’s period. Indra is equated with the Lord to give him respect. He is the ruler of others, empowered by the Lord. Uttama, son of Priyavrata, ruled the third manvantara. Tamasa and Raivata, ruling the fourth and fifth manvantaras, were also his sons. Then Cākṣuṣa of Uttānapāda’s line ruled the sixth manvantara. The events should be seen in order (though the narration is out of sequence), as with the meeting in Kurukṣetra related in the Tenth Canto.

### Text 23

*iti ha vāva sa jagatī-patir īśvarecchayādhiniveśita-karmādhikāro 'khila-jagad-bandha-dhvaṁsana-parānubhāvasya bhagavata ādi-puruṣasyāṅghri-yugalānavarata-dhyānānubhāvena parirandhita-kaṣāyāśayo 'vadāto 'pi māna-vardhano mahatāṁ mahītalam anuśaśāsa.*

**Перевод:**

**Thus Priyavrata, the master of the earth, preoccupied with performance of** ***karma*** **by the desire of the Lord, burned up all impurities in his heart by realization, through constant meditation on the lotus feet of the Lord who displays clear power to destroy the bondage of the whole universe. Though he was completely pure, respect for him increased by his ruling the earth, because he followed the order of Brahmā.**

**Комментарий:**

Though he was free of all faults such as attachment, he ruled the earth, for the purpose of increasing respect for following the orders of Brahmā (*mahatma māna-vardhanaḥ*).

### Text 24

*atha ca duhitaraṁ prajāpater viśvakarmaṇa upayeme barhiṣmatīṁ nāma tasyām u ha vāva ātmajān ātma-samāna-śīla-guṇa-karma-rūpa-vīryodārān daśa bhāvayām babhūva kanyāṁ ca yavīyasīm ūrjasvatīṁ nāma.*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, Mahārāja Priyavrata married Barhiṣmatī, the daughter of the Prajāpati named Viśvakarmā. In her he begot ten sons with abundant conduct, qualities, beauty, bravery, and magnanimity equal to his own, and a daughter, the youngest of all, named Ūrjasvatī.**

**Комментарий:**

In order to rule the kingdom (*athaḥ*), he married Barhiṣmatī.

### Text 27

*tasminn u ha vā upaśama-śīlāḥ paramarṣayaḥ sakala-jīva-nikāyāvāsasya bhagavato vāsudevasya bhītānāṁ śaraṇa-bhūtasya śrīmac-caraṇāravindāvirata-smaraṇāvigalita-parama-bhakti-yogānu-bhāvena paribhāvitāntar-hṛdayādhigate bhagavati sarveṣāṁ bhūtānām ātma-bhūte pratyag-ātmany evātmanas tādātmyam aviśeṣeṇa samīyuḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**In the** ***paramahaṁsa-āśrama,*** **begin free from perception of difference caused by covering of body, these sons with peaceful disposition realized oneness of the self, with the Lord who is perceived within the heart purified by the power of** ***bhakti-yoga*****, fixed in remembrance of the lotus feet of Vāsudeva, the abode of all** ***jīvas*** **and the shelter of those afflicted with fear, and who is the soul of all living beings.**

**Комментарий:**

The sons attained similarity to the Lord (spiritual bodies).

### Text 29

*evam upaśamāyaneṣu sva-tanayeṣv atha jagatī-patir jagatīm arbudāny ekādaśa parivatsarāṇām avyāhatākhila-puruṣa-kāra-sāra-sambhṛta-dor-daṇḍa-yugalāpīḍita-maurvī-guṇa-stanita-viramita-dharma-pratipakṣo barhiṣmatyāś cānudinam edhamāna-pramoda-prasaraṇa-yauṣiṇya-vrīḍā-pramuṣita-hāsāvaloka-rucira-kṣvely-ādibhiḥ parābhūyamāna-viveka ivānavabudhyamāna iva mahāmanā bubhuje.*

**Перевод:**

**While these sons were engaged in the path of sense control, Priyavrata, with powerful mind, ruled the earth for 1,100,000,000 years and conquered his enemies by the mere sound of his bow drawn by two arms filled with strength arising from constant prowess, even though he seemed to have forgotten himself and to have lost his sense of discrimination by the constant, increasing joy expressed by his wife, by her actions, feminine qualities, her laughing glances restricted by her shyness, and her attractive, joking words.**

**Комментарий:**

An arbuda is 100,000,000. This is explained in the Sixth Canto, commentary of Śrīdara Svāmī, when Bali conquers Svarga. As well Amara-koṣa and Kśīrasvāmī agree.

*ekaṁ daśa śata śata sahasrāṇy ayutaṁ prayutākhya lakṣam atha niyutam*  
*arbuda koṭir nyarbuda padme kharvaṁ nikhavam iti daśabhiḥ*  
*gaṇanām mahābja- śaṅkha- samudra -madhyāntaṁ parārdhaṁ ca*  
*svahataṁ parārdham amitam tam svahataṁ bhūryato ‘saṇkhyam*

**Śata=100**

**Sahasra=1000**

**Ayuta=10,000**

**Lakṣa or prayuta=100,000**

**Niyuta=1,000,000**

**Koṭi or arbuda=10,000,000**

**Nyarbuda=100,000,000**

**Padma=1,000,000,000**

**Khava=10,000,000,000**

**Nikharva=100,000,000,000**

**Mahābja-1,000,000,000,000**

**Śaṅkhu=10,000,000,000,000**

**Ssamudra=100,000,000,000,000**

**Antya= 1,000,000,000,000,000**

**Madhya=10,000,000,000,000,000**

**Parārdha=100,000,000,000,000,000**

**One** ***parārdha*** **x one x one** ***parādha*** **x one** ***parārdha=*** **one** ***asaṅkhya.***

Pratyuta and lakṣa are the same meaning. Arbuda and koṭi are also the same meaning. These numbers are also found in the Vedas:

*imā me ’gnayaḥ iṣṭakā dhenavaḥ santy ekā-daśa ca śataṁ ca sahasram ca sahasrāyutaṁ cāyutaṁ cārbudam ca nyarbudaṁ ca samudra-madhyaṁ vāntaś ca parārdhaṁ ca*

**O Agni, may these bricks be my cows: one, and ten, and ten tens, a hundred, and ten hundreds, a thousand, and ten thousand a myriad, a hundred thousand, a million, and a hundred millions, and an ocean’s middle and end, and a hundred thousand million, and a billion. (White Yajur Veda 17.2)**

### Text 30

*yāvad avabhāsayati sura-girim anuparikrāman bhagavān ādityo vasudhā-talam ardhenaiva pratapaty ardhenāvacchādayati tadā hi bhagavad-upāsanopacitāti-puruṣa-prabhāvas tad anabhinandan samajavena rathena jyotirmayena rajanīm api dinaṁ kariṣyāmīti sapta-kṛt vastaraṇim anuparyakrāmad dvitīya iva pataṅgaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**Priyavrata reigned while the sun, going around Meru, lit up half the surface of the earth while the other half was dark. Attaining super-human power by worship of the Lord, Priyavrata, discontent with this condition, thinking that he would make the night into day by his brilliant chariot which travelled at the speed of the sun, becoming like a second sun, followed the movement of the sun seven times.**

**Комментарий:**

He wanted the citizens to be happy. He did not want to compete with the sun. An additional sentence is sometime seen: *evaṁ kurvāṇaṁ priyavratam āgatya caturānanas tavādhikāro na bhavatīti nivārayāmāsa*: he gave up this action when Brahmā came and told him that he was not qualified for this action.

### Text 31

*ye vā u ha tad-ratha-caraṇa-nemi-kṛta-parikhātās te sapta sindhava āsan yata eva kṛtāḥ sapta bhuvo dvīpāḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**The troughs created by the wheels of his chariot produced seven oceans, from which seven continents were formed.**

**Комментарий:**

Each chariot had one wheel, with a size according to his desire.

### Text 32

*jambū-plakṣa-śālmali-kuśa-krauñca-śāka-puṣkara-saṁjñās teṣāṁ parimāṇaṁ pūrvasmāt pūrvasmād uttara uttaro yathā-saṅkhyaṁ dvi-guṇa-mānena bahiḥ samantata upakḷptāḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**Hear about the dimensions of the islands called Jambū, Plakṣa, Śālmali, Kuśa, Krauñca, Śāka and Puṣkara. Each island is twice as large as the one preceding it, and each is surrounded by an ocean on all sides.**

**Комментарий:**

Each successive island was bigger by his desire and each ocean as well.

### Text 33

*kṣārodekṣu-rasoda-suroda-ghṛtoda-kṣīroda-dadhi-maṇḍoda-śuddhodāḥ sapta jaladhayaḥ sapta dvīpa-parikhā ivābhyantara-dvīpa-samānā ekaikaśyena yathānupūrvaṁ saptasv api bahir dvīpeṣu pṛthak parita upakalpitās teṣu jambv-ādiṣu barhiṣmatī-patir anuvratānātmajān āgnīdhredhmajihva-yajñabāhu-hiraṇyareto-ghṛtapṛṣṭha-medhātithi-vītihotra-saṁjñān yathā-saṅkhyenaikaikasminn ekam evādhi-patiṁ vidadhe.*

**Перевод:**

**The seven oceans, respectively containing salt water, sugarcane juice, liquor, clarified butter, milk, yogurt, and drinking water are like trenches surrounding the seven continents of equal breath to its corresponding ocean. Mahārāja Priyavrata, the husband of Queen Barhiṣmatī, gave sovereignty over these islands to his respective sons, namely Āgnīdhra, Idhmajihva, Yajñabāhu, Hiraṇyaretā, Ghṛtapṛṣṭha, Medhātithi and Vītihotra.**

**Комментарий:**

The intervening spaces were filled with the desired liquids.

### Text 39

*tasya ha vā ete ślokāḥ—*  
*priyavrata-kṛtaṁ karma*  
*ko nu kuryād vineśvaram*  
*yo nemi-nimnair akaroc*  
*chāyāṁ ghnan sapta vāridhīn*

**Перевод:**

**The following verses praise Priyavrata. Who could perform the actions of Priyavrata except the Supreme Lord himself? By the rim of his chariot wheel he made seven oceans and destroyed the darkness of night.**

**Комментарий:**

By going in the sky the chariot it made circle like the sun.

## Chapter Two

### Text 2

*sa ca kadācit pitṛloka-kāmaḥ sura-vara-vanitākrīḍācala-droṇyāṁ bhagavantaṁ viśva-sṛjāṁ patim ābhṛta-paricaryopakaraṇa ātmaikāgryeṇa tapasvy ārādhayāṁ babhūva.*

**Перевод:**

**Once, desiring sons, the King, carrying items of worship, began to worship Lord Brahmā by austerities and concentrated mind, in a valley where the wives of the** ***devatās*** **used to play.**

**Комментарий:**

It is understood that his wife was barren.

### Text 12

*lokaṁ pradarśaya suhṛttama tāvakaṁ me*  
*yatratya ittham urasāvayavāv apūrvau*  
*asmad-vidhasya mana-unnayanau bibharti*  
*bahv adbhutaṁ sarasa-rāsa-sudhādi vaktre*

**Перевод:**

**O good friend! Show me your country where people have such astonishing limbs attached to their chest, which are attractive to persons like me. Those people hold very astonishing nectar arising from words in their mouths and have beauty on their lips.**

**Комментарий:**

Instead of *unnayanau* sometimes *kṣobhanaiḥ* is seen. It means astonishing.

### Text 13

*kā vātma-vṛttir adanād dhavir aṅga vāti*  
*viṣṇoḥ kalāsy animiṣonmakarau ca karṇau*  
*udvigna-mīna-yugalaṁ dvija-paṅkti-śocir*  
*āsanna-bhṛṅga-nikaraṁ sara in mukhaṁ te*

**Перевод:**

**What is the means of subsistence of the people of your place? O sage! Because of your chewing, the fragrance of the sacrifice is coming here. You are a portion of Viṣṇu. You have two** ***makara*** **earrings which shine continuously. You have a face like a lake with two restless fishes, two rows of shining teeth like rows of swans, surrounded by locks of hair like swarms of bees.**

**Комментарий:**

“I have not taken food.” Because of your chewing, the fragrance of the ingredients for sacrifice, most pure, is coming here, since you are an expansion of Viṣṇu. Viṣṇu is the enjoyer of sacrifices. *It* (or *in*) is an indeclinable meaning “like.” The alternative *ut* is not correct.

### Text 18

*sā ca tatas tasya vīra-yūtha-pater buddhi-śīla-rūpa-vayaḥ-śriyaudāryeṇa parākṣipta-manās tena sahāyutāyuta-parivatsaropalakṣaṇaṁ kālaṁ jambūdvīpa-patinā bhauma-svarga-bhogān bubhuje.*

**Перевод:**

**Attracted by the intelligence, behavior, beauty, youth, wealth and magnanimity of Āgnīdhra, who was the King of Jambūdvīpa and master of all heroes, Pūrvacitti lived with him for a hundred million years and enjoyed earthly and heavenly happiness.**

**Комментарий:**

*Ayutāyutam* means a hundred million. She enjoyed earthly pleasures as a king’s wife and heavenly pleasures as a devatā.

### Text 22

*āgnīdhro rājātṛptaḥ kāmānām apsarasam evānudinam adhi-manyamānas tasyāḥ salokatāṁ śrutibhir avārundha yatra pitaro mādayante.*

**Перевод:**

**By actions in accordance with the Vedic injunctions, King Āgnīdhra, always thinking of her with great desire, was promoted to the same planet as his celestial wife, where the Pitṛs live in great delight.**

**Комментарий:**

He was promoted to the planet where those who are qualified as Pitṛs live.

## Chapter Three

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*nābhir apatya-kāmo 'prajayā merudevyā bhagavantaṁ yajña-puruṣam avahitātmāyajata.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: Nābhi, son of King Āgnīdhra, desiring sons, worshipped the Supreme Lord with great attention along with his wife Merudevī, who had not produced any sons.**

**Комментарий:**

It is understood that his wife was barren.

### Text 2

*tasya ha vāva śraddhayā viśuddha-bhāvena yajataḥ pravargyeṣu pracaratsu dravya-deśa-kāla-mantrartvig-dakṣiṇā-vidhāna-yogopapattyā duradhigamo 'pi bhagavān bhāgavata-vātsalyatayā supratīka ātmānam aparājitaṁ nija-janābhipretārtha-vidhitsayā gṛhīta-hṛdayo hṛdayaṅgamaṁ mano-nayanānandanāvayavābhirāmam āviścakāra.*

**Перевод:**

**While King Nābhi was performing prescribed duties, out of great affection for his devotee, in order to fulfil the desire of his devotee, the Lord with beautiful form, who is hard to attain but attracted to his devotee, showed King Nābhi his attractive, pleasing form which cannot be brought under control, whose limbs gave bliss to mind and eyes of Nābhi. He showed himself because of Nābhi’s performance of** ***bhakti-yoga*** **along with proper ingredients, place, time, mantras, priests, charity, and rules.**

**Комментарий:**

Though giving bliss to the mind and eye because of his attractive limbs, he was most beautiful (*supratīka*) because of his affection for the devotee.

### Text 4-5

*ṛtvija ūcuḥ*  
*arhasi muhur arhattamārhaṇam asmākam anupathānāṁ namo nama ity etāvat sad-upaśikṣitaṁ ko 'rhati pumān prakṛti-guṇa-vyatikara-matir anīśa īśvarasya parasya prakṛti-puruṣayor arvāktanābhir nāma-rūpākṛtibhī rūpa-nirūpaṇam; sakala-jana-nikāya-vṛjina-nirasana-śivatama-pravara-guṇa-gaṇaika-deśa-kathanād ṛte.*

**Перевод:**

**The priests said: O most exalted Lord! Since we are your followers, you should accept our offering of repeated respects, following the instructions of the great devotees. The incapable man, whose mind is affected the** ***guṇas*** **of** ***prakṛti,*** **cannot describe the form of the Supreme Lord who is beyond both** ***prakṛti*** **and** ***puruṣa*** **by limited names, colors and forms, except by describing a portion of your auspicious qualities which even then destroy the sins of all people.**

**Комментарий:**

In thirteen verses they praise the Lord in prose. Others praise you with prose but we are not capable. They accept to praise him in this manner. Following the path of bhakti we have approached near to you but we have not attained you, since we have material desires. You should accept worship from such people. This is your mercy since we are not qualified. Thinking of his excellence and their lack of qualification, they offer repeated respects (*namo namaḥ*).

“You should describe my excellent qualities.” What man affected by the material guṇas can describe exactly the form of the Lord, using objects which appear similar in name and form in the material world (*arvāktanābhiḥ*)? The Lord’s beauty is beyond him. “Let him describe the Lord in that way.” He is incapable (*anīśaḥ*). What is the value of descriptions by such a person of the Lord’s real form? The description which is a dull reflection (*ekadeśa*) can destroy all people’s sufferings. That is its glory. Thus even such descriptions should be done.

Or the ignorant person can describe the Lord’s form with a little authority (*arvāktamābhiḥ*). Because he can describe the Lord’s unlimited qualities, the person can describe the actual svarūpa of the Lord, which destroys all sufferings. Or the person involved in karma-yoga can describe the form of the Lord by descriptions related to the devatās. Those descriptions can destroy all sufferings.

### Text 8

*ātmana evānusavanam añjasāvyatirekeṇa bobhūyamānāśeṣa-puruṣārtha-svarūpasya kintu nāthāśiṣa āśāsānānām etad abhisaṁrādhana-mātraṁ bhavitum arhati.*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! You, who directy have a form composed of unlimited human goals, the highest bliss, a form which continues without break at all times, should satisfy us, who have desires for benedictions.**

**Комментарий:**

You have a form which manifests (*bobhūyamāna*) without break (*avyatirekena*).

Śruti says *sarva-kāmaḥ sarva-gandhaḥ sarva-rasaḥ*: you are all desires, all fragrances and all rasas. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad [3.14.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/3.14.2))

### Text 9

*tad yathā bāliśānāṁ svayam ātmanaḥ śreyaḥ param aviduṣāṁ parama-parama-puruṣa prakarṣa-karuṇayā sva-mahimānaṁ cāpavargākhyam upakalpayiṣyan svayaṁ nāpacita evetaravad ihopalakṣitaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, just as a wise person appears before ignorant people, you, the person superior to all great persons, out of your causeless mercy, to give liberation in the form of realization of your glories and all other benedictions as well, have appeared here, though not worshipped properly by us. You have appeared like a person curious to see the sacrifice.**

**Комментарий:**

You have appeared to produce direct liberation, which is the wealth of your powers (*mahimānam apavargākhyam*), though you are not worshipped properly with bhakti (*nāpacitaḥ*).

### Text 10

*athāyam eva varo hy arhattama yarhi barhiṣi rājarṣer varadarṣabho bhavān nija-puruṣekṣaṇa-viṣaya āsīt.*

**Перевод:**

**O most worshipable Lord! That you, best giver of benedictions, appeared as the object of our vision in the King’s sacrifice was our benediction.**

**Комментарий:**

Instead of *athāyam* sometimes *atha hāya* is seen. When you, the object of vision for your devotees, have appeared at the sacrifice of the king, that is a blessing.

### Text 12

*atha kathañcit skhalana-kṣut-patana-jṛmbhaṇa-duravasthānādiṣu vivaśānāṁ naḥ smaraṇāya jvara-maraṇa-daśāyām api sakala-kaśmala-nirasanāni tava guṇa-kṛta-nāmadheyāni vacana-gocarāṇi bhavantu.*

**Перевод:**

**May we chant your names describing your qualities, which destroy all sins at the time of death, in order to help us remember you, who are unable to remember you while stumbling, suffering hunger, falling down, yawning, or suffering calamity!**

**Комментарий:**

May we chant your names describing your qualities and pastimes (*guṇa-kṛta-nāmadheyāni*).

### Text 13

*kiñcāyaṁ rājarṣir apatya-kāmaḥ prajāṁ bhavādṛśīm āśāsāna īśvaram āśiṣāṁ svargāpavargayor api bhavantam upadhāvati prajāyām artha-pratyayo dhanadam ivādhanaḥ phalīkaraṇam.*

**Перевод:**

**However, this King, desiring a son, is worshipping you, who can give all benedictions such as Svarga or liberation, since he desires to have a son. He is like a poor man pursing a wealthy man for a few empty husks.**

**Комментарий:**

Reluctant to reveal the offense of the sponsor of the sacrifice, they inform him of the facts out of great intimacy. He, desiring a son like you, pursues you, the Lord, who fulfills desires for Svarga and liberation, just as a poor man pursues a wealthy man for empty husks. However he is not so foolish since he wants a son like you.

### Text 14

*ko vā iha te 'parājito 'parājitayā māyayānavasita-padavyānāvṛta-matir viṣaya-viṣa-rayānāvṛta-prakṛtir anupāsita-mahac-caraṇaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**You are free of** ***māyā,*** **but is there a person who does not worship the great devotees whose intelligence is not covered by unconquerable and indiscernible** ***māyā,*** **and whose nature is not covered by the force of material poison?**

**Комментарий:**

This means that at the present time there are people who take the worship of the devotee as insignificant.

### Text 20

*barhiṣi tasminn eva viṣṇudatta bhagavān paramarṣibhiḥ prasādito nābheḥ priya-cikīrṣayā tad-avarodhāyane merudevyāṁ dharmān darśayitu-kāmo vāta-raśanānāṁ śramaṇānām ṛṣīṇām ūrdhva-manthināṁ śuklayā tanuvāvatatāra.*

**Перевод:**

**O King Parīkṣit! Satisfied with the great sages, wanting to please King Nābhi, and desiring to show the path of the naked performers of austerity, who follow scripture and remain celibate, the Lord then appeared with a body of** ***śuddha-sattva*** **in the womb of Merudevī who was situated in the inner chambers of the palace*****.***

**Комментарий:**

*Vātaraśānānām* refers to sannyāsīs, *śramanānam* refers to vānaprasthas. *Ṛṣīnām* refers to gṛhasthas, who performing sacrifices, revealed mantras. *Ūrdhva-mathinām* refers to brahmacārīs. This he would show by his conduct in the future.

## Chapter Four

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*atha ha tam utpattyaivābhivyajyamāna-bhagaval-lakṣaṇaṁ sāmyopaśama-vairāgyaiśvarya-mahā-vibhūtibhir anudinam edhamānānubhāvaṁ prakṛtayaḥ prajā brāhmaṇā devatāś cāvani-tala-samavanāyātitarāṁ jagṛdhuḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**The ministers, citizens,** ***brāhmaṇas*****, and d*****evatās*** **desired that this son, who displayed qualities of the Supreme Lord when he was born, who increased day by day in great qualities, powers, detachment, sense control and equal vision, rule the earth.**

**Комментарий:**

The qualities described are connected with his birth described at the end of the previous chapter.

### Text 2

*tasya ha vā itthaṁ varṣmaṇā varīyasā bṛhac-chlokena caujasā balena śriyā yaśasā vīrya-śauryābhyāṁ ca pitā ṛṣabha itīdaṁ nāma cakāra.*

**Перевод:**

**Because of his excellent body, praised by many poets, his mental and physical strength, his beauty, fame, influence and courage, he is father called him Ṛṣabha.**

**Комментарий:**

Because he was excellent in body, he was called Ṛṣabha (excellent).

### Text 5

*viditānurāgam āpaura-prakṛti jana-pado rājā nābhir ātmajaṁ samaya-setu-rakṣāyām abhiṣicya brāhmaṇeṣūpanidhāya saha merudevyā viśālāyāṁ prasanna-nipuṇena tapasā samādhi-yogena nara-nārāyaṇākhyaṁ bhagavantaṁ vāsudevam upāsīnaḥ kālena tan-mahimānam avāpa.*

**Перевод:**

**King Nābhi, who took into consideration the opinion of the population and ministers, enthroned his son, for whom the citizens had affection, as the king, in order to protect the laws of proper conduct. Nābhi then worshiped the Lord in the form of Nara-nārāyaṇa in Badarikāśrama by austerities and concentration which were capable of attaining results for the happiness of all beings. In time he attained Vaikuṇṭha, the glory of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Nābhi enthroned him not as the king, but to follow the rules of proper conduct. His coronation as king took place later by brāhmaṇas, since at this time he had not married.

### Text 7

*brahmaṇyo 'nyaḥ kuto nābher*  
*viprā maṅgala-pūjitāḥ*  
*yasya barhiṣi yajñeśaṁ*  
*darśayām āsur ojasā*

**Перевод:**

**Who other than King Nābhi was affectionate to the** ***brāhmaṇas*****? The** ***brāhmaṇas*** **worshipped by him because of his good nature, revealed to him the Lord by the power of their** ***bhakti*** **during the sacrifice.**

**Комментарий:**

They were worshipped because of his good nature (*maṅgala*).

### Text 14

*bhagavān ṛṣabha-saṁjña ātma-tantraḥ svayaṁ nitya-nivṛttānartha-paramparaḥ kevalānandānubhava īśvara eva viparītavat karmāṇy ārabhamāṇaḥ kālenānugataṁ dharmam ācaraṇenopaśikṣayann atad-vidāṁ sama upaśānto maitraḥ kāruṇiko dharmārtha-yaśaḥ-prajānandāmṛtāvarodhena gṛheṣu lokaṁ niyamayat.*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord named Ṛṣabhadeva was independent, eternally free from the succession of material changes, full of bliss, and the sole controller, but like the** ***jīva,*** **performed prescribed activities and taught** ***dharma*** **which had been destroyed by showing an example to persons ignorant of** ***dharma*** **(*****atadvidām*****). He was equal to all, was in control of his senses, was friendly to all and compassionate. He regulated the people in household life by having them attain morality, wealth, fame, offspring and bliss.**

**Комментарий:**

He taught dharma which had been destroyed by time (*kāleṇa anugatam dharmam*).

## Chapter Five

### Text 2

*mahat-sevāṁ dvāram āhur vimuktes*  
*tamo-dvāraṁ yoṣitāṁ saṅgi-saṅgam*  
*mahāntas te sama-cittāḥ praśāntā*  
*vimanyavaḥ suhṛdaḥ sādhavo ye*

**Перевод:**

**Service to saintly persons is the cause of two types of liberation, whereas association with men who associate with women is the cause of bondage. The impersonalist sees no differences and is peaceful whereas the devotee has a sincere heart and his intelligence is fixed in me. Both types of saintly persons are devoid of anger, are friendly to all and do not see faults in others.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. *Brahma-saukhya* was mentioned in verse 1. There are two types: impersonal and personal. Now, the two types are described. One type sees things as non-different (*sama-cittāḥ*). These persons conduct and practice is described. They are peaceful (*praśāntāḥ*).

### Text 3

*ye vā mayīśe kṛta-sauhṛdārthā*  
*janeṣu dehambhara-vārtikeṣu*  
*gṛheṣu jāyātmaja-rātimatsu*  
*na prīti-yuktā yāvad-arthāś ca loke*

**Перевод:**

**The devotees have made their goal of life affection for me, the Lord, and do not show affection for persons who enjoy talks about people attached to eating and drinking and who have houses, wives, sons and wealth. They remain in their houses simply to spend their money on glorifying the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Those with the happiness of personal realization are described. Filled with bhakti, they spend all their wealth on worship (*yāvad-arthāḥ*).

### Text 4

*nūnaṁ pramattaḥ kurute vikarma*  
*yad indriya-prītaya āpṛṇoti*  
*na sādhu manye yata ātmano 'yam*  
*asann api kleśada āsa dehaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The inattentive** ***jīva*** **becomes absorbed in actions for pleasure of the senses and thus engages in sin, by which he receives another body which gives suffering, and which actually does not belong to the** ***jīva*** **at all. I do not consider such actions proper.**

**Комментарий:**

*Vikarma* means prescribed actions for enjoyment. He receives another human body which gives suffering.

### Text 6

*evaṁ manaḥ karma-vaśaṁ prayuṅkte*  
*avidyayātmany upadhīyamāne*  
*prītir na yāvan mayi vāsudeve*  
*na mucyate deha-yogena tāvat*

**Перевод:**

**Thus,** ***karmas*** **make the mind fixed upon further action. Since the** ***jīva*** **is engaged in action by ignorance, having accepted a subtle body, as long as he does not have devotion for me, Vāsudeva, he will not be liberated from the body.**

**Комментарий:**

*Upadhīyamāne* means “engaged.” Even though they may realize ātmā, without affection for me, they have no perfection.

*haṁse gurau mayi bhaktyānuvṛtyā*  
*vitṛṣṇayā dvandva-titikṣayā ca*  
*sarvatra jantor vyasanāvagatyā*  
*jijñāsayā tapasehā-nivṛttyā*  
*mat-karmabhir mat-kathayā ca nityaṁ*  
*mad-deva-saṅgād guṇa-kīrtanān me*  
*nirvaira-sāmyopaśamena putrā*  
*jihāsayā deha-gehātma-buddheḥ*

**One can destroy the subtle body called** ***ahaṅkāra*** **by devotion to** ***guru,*** **namely me as a** ***paramahaṁsa,*** **by lack of desire, by tolerance of dualities, by understanding the suffering of all** ***jīvas*****, by inquiry into** ***bhakti*** **and the Lord, by actions of dharma pleasing to the Lord, and by rejection of everything except the Lord (SB** [**5.5.10-11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/5.10)**).**

One can destroy the subtle body called *ahaṅkāra* by actions dedicated to me, by speaking about me constantly, by association with the *devatās* of whom I am the master, by chanting my glories, by showing no hatred, taking others’ suffering as one’s own, by absence of anger and lamentation, and by desiring to give up identity with house and body, even though it is impossible to give up those identities.

The cause of liberation was described: giving up ahaṅkāra. Affection for the Lord was also described as a cause. Jñāna is the means for persons desiring liberation. Because they have other desires, they have a reflection of affection only. In either case there should be devotion to guru. This is expressed in four verses. The practice of those desiring the Lord is described in five lines. *Vitṛṣṇayā* means “by absence of thirst.” *Jijñāsayā* means “by deliberation on the nature of the Lord through scriptures.” *Tapasā* means “by performing actions pleasing to the Lord.” *Ihānivṛttyā* means “by rejecting all desires for happiness in this life and the next.” *Mat-karmabhiḥ* means “by actions such as deity worship and serving the lotus feet.” The practice of persons desiring liberation is described in two verses.

### Text 12-13

*adhyātma-yogena vivikta-sevayā*  
*prāṇendriyātmābhijayena sadhryak*  
*sac-chraddhayā brahmacaryeṇa śaśvad*  
*asampramādena yamena vācām*  
*sarvatra mad-bhāva-vicakṣaṇena*  
*jñānena vijñāna-virājitena*  
*yogena dhṛty-udyama-sattva-yukto*  
*liṅgaṁ vyapohet kuśalo 'ham-ākhyam*

**Перевод:**

**Endowed with determination, effort and enthusiasm, one gives up the subtle body by** ***adhyātma-yoga*****, living in a solitary place, completely controlling the senses, life airs and mind, faith in the scriptures, constant celibacy, non-rejection of duties, control of speech, seeing my presence everywhere, by knowledge which produces realization, and by** ***aṣṭāṅga-yoga.***

**Комментарий:**

One should give up the subtle body. The phrases before and after are related to this statement*. Brahmacaryeṇa* means “by controlling body, mind and words.” One should be endowed with determination and effort through proper method (*yogena*). This applies to both types. The seed of affection is planted by seeing the presence of the Lord everywhere (*mad-bhāva*).

### Text 14

*karmāśayaṁ hṛdaya-granthi-bandham*  
*avidyayāsāditam apramattaḥ*  
*anena yogena yathopadeśaṁ*  
*samyag vyapohyoparameta yogāt*

**Перевод:**

**According to the instructions of** ***guru,*** **with attention one should completely destroy the bondage created by the knot in the heart in which dwells all** ***karmas*** **and which is produced by ignorance*****.*** **Then one should give up the process as well.**

**Комментарий:**

To become absorbed in the instructions, a summary is given in this verse. By the process previously described (*yogena)*, not by book learning, according to the teachings of guru, one should destroy bondage and give up the process. Persons desiring affection for the Lord destroy the bondage by affection, but the affection or prīti is eternal. Those desiring liberation destroy the bondage, and there is nothing else to achieve. They thus reject the process.

### Text 18

*gurur na sa syāt sva-jano na sa syāt*  
*pitā na sa syāj jananī na sā syāt*  
*daivaṁ na tat syān na patiś ca sa syān*  
*na mocayed yaḥ samupeta-mṛtyum*

**Перевод:**

**He who cannot deliver a person in** ***saṁsāra*** **is not a** ***guru*****, a relative, a father, mother,** ***devatā*** **or husband.**

**Комментарий:**

In rejecting the conventional guru, there is no fault for persons desiring the highest goal.

A person should not make efforts to produce sons. That is the meaning of *pitā na sa syāt.* The emphasis in the verse is on the subject not putting all his effort into these roles.

### Text 19

*idaṁ śarīraṁ mama durvibhāvyaṁ*  
*sattvaṁ hi me hṛdayaṁ yatra dharmaḥ*  
*pṛṣṭhe kṛto me yad adharma ārād*  
*ato hi mām ṛṣabhaṁ prāhur āryāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**My body is difficult to understand since it is spiritual. Where there is** ***bhakti,*** **there my mind resides. Whatever is opposite to** ***bhakti*** **is behind me, far away. Thus the respectable people call me the best, or Ṛṣabha.**

**Комментарий:**

Ṛṣabha is an āveśāvatara.

**Bhagavat Sandarbha 54:**

This real nature of body of mine in human form is certainly *(hi)* difficult to understand. My mind resides where there are qualities of devotion since I reject the opposite qualities. I am averse to lack of devotion. The disappearance of the speaker Rṣabhadeva is described in this chapter according to material perception, through a disappearance pastime, to show how an ātmārāma passes away. *Yogināṁ sāmparāya-vidhim anuśikṣayan sva-kalevaraṁ jihāsuḥ:* teaching the yogīs how to give the material body, he desired to give an appearance of giving up his body (or desired to give up the material world). (SB [5.6.6](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/6.6))

### Text 20

*tasmād bhavanto hṛdayena jātāḥ*  
*sarve mahīyāṁsam amuṁ sanābham*  
*akliṣṭa-buddhyā bharataṁ bhajadhvaṁ*  
*śuśrūṣaṇaṁ tad bharaṇaṁ prajānām*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore you have been born from my chest. You should all worship Bharata, your brother, with pure intelligence, by which my worship and the protection of the citizens will also be accomplished.**

**Комментарий:**

It was previously said that one should reject relatives:

*ye vā mayīśe kṛta-sauhṛdārthā*  
*janeṣu dehambhara-vārtikeṣu*  
*gṛheṣu jāyātmaja-rātimatsu*  
*na prīti-yuktā yāvad-arthāś ca loke*

**The devotees have made their goal of life affection for me, the Lord, and do not show affection for persons who enjoy talks about people attached to eating and drinking and who have houses, wives, sons and wealth. They remain in their houses simply to spend their money on glorifying the Lord. (SB** [**5.5.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/5.3)**)**

Why teach this again? Your brother is a great devotee (*mahīyāṁsaṁ*). It was taught that one should associate with great devotees (SB [5.5.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/5.2)) but among them Ṛṣabha is the best. Thinking of your happiness (*akliṣṭa-buddhyā*), worship him.

### Text 21-22

*bhūteṣu vīrudbhya uduttamā ye*  
*sarīsṛpās teṣu sabodha-niṣṭhāḥ*  
*tato manuṣyāḥ pramathās tato 'pi*  
*gandharva-siddhā vibudhānugā ye*  
*devāsurebhyo maghavat-pradhānā*  
*dakṣādayo brahma-sutās tu teṣām*  
*bhavaḥ paraḥ so 'tha viriñca-vīryaḥ*  
*sa mat-paro 'haṁ dvija-deva-devaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Among the living beings, the snakes are superior to the plants. Animals are superior to the snakes. Humans are superior to animals. Superior to humans are the Gandharvas. Superior to them are the Kinnaras. Indra is superior to all of them. Sons of Brahmā like Dakṣa are superior to Indra. Śiva, son of Brahmā, is superior to Dakṣa and Brahmā is superior to Śiva. I am superior to Brahmā. The best of the** ***brāhmaṇas*** **are worshipped by me.**

**Комментарий:**

The list is in order of increasing excellence. The brāhmaṇa is the best among devatās and twice born, being worshipped by Ṛṣabha.

### Text 23

*na brāhmaṇais tulaye bhūtam anyat*  
*paśyāmi viprāḥ kim ataḥ paraṁ tu*  
*yasmin nṛbhiḥ prahutaṁ śraddhayāham*  
*aśnāmi kāmaṁ na tathāgni-hotre*

**Перевод:**

**I do not see anyone equal to the** ***brāhmaṇas.*** **Who is superior to the** ***brāhmaṇas*****? I do not accept the offerings at the fire sacrifice as much as I accept to full satisfaction what is offered to the** ***brāhmaṇa’s*** **mouth by men.**

**Комментарий:**

Thinking that the brāhmaṇa assists the Lord, he speaks. I will be satisfied by offering to the brāhmaṇa.

### Text 24

*dhṛtā tanūr uśatī me purāṇī*  
*yeneha sattvaṁ paramaṁ pavitram*  
*śamo damaḥ satyam anugrahaś ca*  
*tapas titikṣānubhavaś ca yatra*

**Перевод:**

**There is no person superior to the** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **who supports my body in the form of the Vedas in this world, and in whom reside the purest antaḥkaraṇa*****,*** **control of the senses, control of the mind, truthfulness, mercy, austerity, tolerance and realization of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Why is the Lord satisfied? This verse explains. He supports my body because he is the purest antaḥkaraṇa (*sattvam*), showing mercy to all beings (*anugrahaḥ)*, practice of sva-dharma (*tapas*), and realization of the Lord (*anubhavaḥ*).

### Text 25

*matto 'py anantāt parataḥ parasmāt*  
*svargāpavargādhipater na kiñcit*  
*yeṣāṁ kim u syād itareṇa teṣām*  
*akiñcanānāṁ mayi bhakti-bhājām*

**Перевод:**

**The devotees, who worship only me and have given even themselves to me, who pray for nothing except me, the Lord who possesses infinite qualities and can award lordship over Svarga, need nothing from any other being.**

**Комментарий:**

The special reason for the devotees being most worthy of worship is given. They want nothing except me.

### Text 26

*sarvāṇi mad-dhiṣṇyatayā bhavadbhiś*  
*carāṇi bhūtāni sutā dhruvāṇi*  
*sambhāvitavyāni pade pade vo*  
*vivikta-dṛgbhis tad u hārhaṇaṁ me*

**Перевод:**

**O sons! At all times you should meditate on all moving and non-moving beings with pure vision, since I am situated in all of them. Besides this, you should worship me.**

**Комментарий:**

*Tad u ha* means *tad api.*

### Text 27

*mano-vaco-dṛk-karaṇehitasya*  
*sākṣāt-kṛtaṁ me paribarhaṇaṁ hi*  
*vinā pumān yena mahā-vimohāt*  
*kṛtānta-pāśān na vimoktum īśet*

**Перевод:**

**Serving me directly with mind, speech, sight and other senses belonging to the body is worship of me. Without this, man cannot be freed from the ropes of death or from great illusion.**

**Комментарий:**

*Sākṣat kṛtam* means offering, putting in front.

### Text 28

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*evam anuśāsyātmajān svayam anuśiṣṭān api lokānuśāsanārthaṁ mahānubhāvaḥ parama-suhṛd bhagavān ṛṣabhāpadeśa upaśama-śīlānām uparata-karmaṇāṁ mahā-munīnāṁ bhakti-jñāna-vairāgya-lakṣaṇaṁ pāramahaṁsya-dharmam upaśikṣamāṇaḥ sva-tanaya-śata-jyeṣṭhaṁ parama-bhāgavataṁ bhagavaj-jana-parāyaṇaṁ bharataṁ dharaṇi-pālanāyābhiṣicya svayaṁ bhavana evorvarita-śarīra-mātra-parigraha unmatta iva gagana-paridhānaḥ prakīrṇa-keśa ātmany āropitāhavanīyo brahmāvartāt pravavrāja.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: Thus the great well-wisher of everyone, the Supreme Lord Ṛṣabhadeva, instructed his own sons, although they were highly learned, to set an example for the public. Having given instructions about the path of** ***jñāna, vairāgya*** **and** ***bhakti*** **for great sages who have become detached from material life and have given up material** ***karmas*****, he enthroned the eldest among his sons, Bharata, the topmost devotee and servant of the Vaiṣṇava, to protect the earth. Accepting only bodily maintenance even while remaining at home, and acting like a madman, he then took the sacrificial fire within himself and walked about Brahmāvarta naked, with dishevelled hair.**

**Комментарий:**

The instructions on bhakti were given to Bharata, who instructed the others.

### Text 30

*tatra tatra pura-grāmākara-kheṭa-vāṭa-kharvaṭa-śibira-vraja-ghoṣa-sārtha-giri-vanāśramādiṣv anupatham avanicarāpasadaiḥ paribhūyamāno makṣikābhir iva vana-gajas tarjana-tāḍanāvamehana-ṣṭhīvana-grāva-śakṛd-rajaḥ-prakṣepa-pūti-vāta-duruktais tad avigaṇayann evāsat-saṁsthāna etasmin dehopalakṣaṇe sad-apadeśa ubhayānubhava-svarūpeṇa sva-mahimāvasthānenāsamāropitāhaṁ-mamābhimānatvād avikhaṇḍita-manāḥ pṛthivīm eka-caraḥ paribabhrāma.*

**Перевод:**

**He wandered the earth alone, going here and there, through towns, villages, mines, farms, mountain villages, gardens, military camps, cow pens, cowherd villages, travellers’ refuges, mountains, forests and settlements of sages. He remained indifferent, like an elephant surrounded by flies, though he was humiliated by the insults, scolding, and beatings of low class people, who sometimes urinated on him, spat on him, threw stones, stool or dust on him or passed foul air on him. He was not afflicted since he did not identify with me or mine and was situated in his spiritual body, understanding that his body composed of the universe was temporary though real, while distinguishing the functions of the spiritual and material energies.**

**Комментарий:**

Understanding his body was temporary was an act to teach people since it was said:

*idaṁ śarīraṁ mama durvibhāvyaṁ*  
*sattvaṁ hi me hṛdayaṁ yatra dharmaḥ*  
*pṛṣṭhe kṛto me yad adharma ārād*  
*ato hi mām ṛṣabhaṁ prāhur āryāḥ*

**My body is difficult to understand since it is spiritual. Where there is** ***bhakti,*** **there my mind resides. Whatever is opposite to** ***bhakti*** **is behind me, far away. Thus the respectable people call me the best, or Ṛṣabha. (SB** [**5.5.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/5.19)**)**

### Text 35

*iti nānā-yoga-caryācaraṇo bhagavān kaivalya-patir ṛṣabho 'virata-parama-mahānandānubhava ātmani sarveṣāṁ bhūtānām ātma-bhūte bhagavati vāsudeva ātmano 'vyavadhānānanta-rodara-bhāvena siddha-samastārtha-paripūrṇo yogaiśvaryāṇi vaihāyasa-mano-javāntardhāna-parakāya-praveśa-dūra-grahaṇādīni yadṛcchayopagatāni nāñjasā nṛpa hṛdayenābhyanandat.*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord Ṛṣabha, performing many types of** ***yoga,*** **the master who could award liberation, was always absorbed in the highest bliss. He was filled with perfection of all goals through** ***prema*** **directly experienced within himself, which produced unlimited tears directed to himself in the form of Vāsudeva, situated in all beings. O King! Thus he did not welcome in his mind the mystic powers such as flying in the sky, moving at the speed of the mind, the ability to disappear, entering other bodies, or seeing at a great distance, which suddenly appeared of their own accord within him.**

**Комментарий:**

*Ātmani* means “to his source, the aṁśi, the Lord.”

## Chapter Six

### Text 2

*ṛṣir uvāca*  
*satyam uktaṁ kintv iha vā eke na manaso 'ddhā viśrambham anavasthānasya śaṭha-kirāta iva saṅgacchante.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: You have spoken correctly, but in this world intelligent people do not at all put faith in the unsteady mind, which is like a degraded, cunning person.**

**Комментарий:**

There may or may not be a fault with the siddhis. But he was not happy to state that one should give up controlling the mind optionally. He speaks to teach people, since he speaks from the point of view of sādhana.

### Text 6

*athaivam akhila-loka-pāla-lalāmo 'pi vilakṣaṇair jaḍavad avadhūta-veṣa-bhāṣā-caritair avilakṣita-bhagavat-prabhāvo yogināṁ sāmparāya-vidhim anuśikṣayan sva-kalevaraṁ jihāsur ātmany ātmānam asaṁvyavahitam anarthāntara-bhāvenānvīkṣamāṇa uparatānuvṛttir upararāma.*

**Перевод:**

**Though he was the head of all the protectors of planets, he hid his nature as the Supreme Lord by appearing dull-witted, wearing the dress of an** ***avadhūta*** **and speaking like an** ***avadhūta.*** **Teaching the** ***yogīs*** **how to give the material body, he desired to give an appearance of giving up his body. Constantly seeing himself as non-different from the Lord and having no obstacle of material energy obstructing him, imitating the** ***avadhūta,*** **he ended his pastimes on earth.**

**Комментарий:**

His giving up his body was only an imitation using māyā. Thus he taught how to give up the body, using imitation. Desiring to give up the world (*sva-kalevaram*), he gave up his identity of performing pastimes in the material world (*uparatānuvṛttiḥ*), seeing Ṛṣabha, the aṁśa of the Lord (*ātmānam*) in Vāsudeva, his source (*ātmani*).

### Text 7

*tasya ha vā evaṁ mukta-liṅgasya bhagavata ṛṣabhasya yogamāyā-vāsanayā deha imāṁ jagatīm abhimānābhāsena saṅkramamāṇaḥ koṅka-veṅka-kuṭakān dakṣiṇa-karṇāṭakān deśān yadṛcchayopagataḥ kuṭakācalopavana āsya kṛtāśma-kavala unmāda iva mukta-mūrdhajo 'saṁvīta eva vicacāra.*

**Перевод:**

**The body of the Supreme Lord Ṛṣabhadeva, who hid his qualities as the Lord during his pastimes, wandered over the earth as if moving by the remaining shadow of** ***avidyā’s*** **influence, though actually he was under the influence of** ***yoga-māyā*****. Reaching Koṅka, Veṅka and Kuṭaka in Karṇāṭaka state in South India by his own will, he came to Kuṭakācala. He moved like a madman, naked, with dishevelled hair, and had a stone in his mouth.**

**Комментарий:**

He wandered about with a desire for pastimes (*yoga-māyā-vāsanayā*). He placed a stone in his mouth, so that nothing could be eaten or spoken. That was the external intention.

### Text 8

*atha samīra-vega-vidhūta-veṇu-vikarṣaṇa-jātogra-dāvānalas tad vanam ālelihānaḥ saha tena dadāha.*

**Перевод:**

**An intense forest fire created by the rubbing of bamboos agitated by the force of the wind, devouring everything, burned up the forest along with Ṛṣabhadeva.**

**Комментарий:**

The real meaning is, “The forest fire burned up the forest with his assistance (*saha tena*).” It burned up the gross bodies of the trees (*vanam*). Ṛṣabha burned up their subtle bodies, since he was able to give liberation as with Rāma:

*sa yaiḥ spṛṣṭo 'bhidṛṣṭo vā saṁviṣṭo 'nugato 'pi vā*  
*kosalās te yayuḥ sthāna yatra gacchanti yoginaḥ*

**The inhabitants of Kosala, who touched, saw, sat together with Rāma in friendship and followed Rāma, went to Vaikuṇṭha where the devotees go. (SB** [**9.11.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-9/11.22)**)**

His similarity to fire is mentioned. His disappearance was similar.

### Text 9

*yasya kilānucaritam upākarṇya koṅka-veṅka-kuṭakānāṁ rājārhan-nāmopaśikṣya kalāv adharma utkṛṣyamāṇe bhavitavyena vimohitaḥ sva-dharma-patham akuto-bhayam apahāya kupatha-pākhaṇḍam asamañjasaṁ nija-manīṣayā mandaḥ sampravartayiṣyate.*

**Перевод:**

**King Arhat of Koṇka, Veṅka and Kuṭaka regions, hearing about the activities of Ṛṣabhadeva, would become bewildered when** ***adharma*** **increased in Kali-yuga by the results of bad** ***karma.*** **Foolishly giving up his path of** ***dharma*** **which destroys all dangers, by his own mind he propagated a deviant path which rejected the scriptures.**

**Комментарий:**

Ṛṣabhadeva’s appearance was described in the third chapter. His activities were for the benefit of all humans but appeared otherwise, producing offense to the Vedas and related items.

### Text 12

*ayam avatāro rajasopapluta-kaivalyopaśikṣaṇārthaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**Ṛṣabhadeva appeared in this world to teach liberation to those influenced by passion.**

**Комментарий:**

He appeared in the world to give benefit, by positive and negative means, even to those afflicted with rajas.

### Text 14

*aho nu vaṁśo yaśasāvadātaḥ*  
*praiyavrato yatra pumān purāṇaḥ*  
*kṛtāvatāraḥ puruṣaḥ sa ādyaś*  
*cacāra dharmaṁ yad akarma-hetum*

**Перевод:**

**The dynasty of Priyavrata is shining with fame. In that dynasty the eternal Supreme Lord has appeared. The most excellent Lord enacted** ***dharma*** **by which one can destroy all** ***karma.***

**Комментарий:**

By his nature he is the eternal person, who, though having come as avatāra, is not changed. Rather he is the first person, who is the cause of destroying karma and establishing dharma. Though his actions indicated jñāna, for devotees his actions indicate bhakti.

### Text 17

*yasyām eva kavaya ātmānam avirataṁ vividha-vṛjina-saṁsāra-paritāpopatapyamānam anusavanaṁ snāpayantas tayaiva parayā nirvṛtyā hy apavargam ātyantikaṁ parama-puruṣārtham api svayam āsāditaṁ no evādriyante bhagavadīyatvenaiva parisamāpta-sarvārthāḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**The wise, suffering from various calamities of material life, continually bathe themselves in the lake of** ***bhakti,*** **and because of its intense bliss, they do not strive for liberation, though it considered the highest goal, which comes of its own accord since they have attained everything by being the servants of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

*Yasyām* refers to bhakti previously mentioned. *Nivṛttyā* means because it was the highest goal by nature, being situated in one’s svarūpa. Since liberation follows from bhakti, bhakti is the final bestower of everything.

*hari-bhakti-mahā-devyāḥ sarvā mukty-ādi-siddhayaḥ \|*  
*bhuktayaś cādbhutās tasyāś ceṭikāvad anuvratāḥ \|\|*

**All the perfections like liberation and astonishing material gains follow after the Lord’s bhakti like servants. (Nārada Pañcarātra)**

Thus the devotees have no interest in material gain or liberation.

*yasya bhaktir bhagavati harau niḥśreyaseśvare*  
*vikrīḍato 'mṛtāmbhodhau kiṁ kṣudraiḥ khātakodakaiḥ*

**A person who has** ***bhakti*** **to the Supreme Lord, Hari, the Lord of the highest auspiciousness, swims in the ocean of nectar. For him what use is Svarga, which is like the water in small ditches? (SB** [**6.12.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/12.22)**)**

### Text 18

*rājan patir gurur alaṁ bhavatāṁ yadūnāṁ*  
*daivaṁ priyaḥ kula-patiḥ kva ca kiṅkaro vaḥ*  
*astv evam aṅga bhagavān bhajatāṁ mukundo*  
*muktiṁ dadāti karhicit sma na bhakti-yogam*

**Перевод:**

**O King Parīkṣit! Kṛṣṇa was the protector, instructor, object of worship, giver of affection, and the protector of both the Yadus and Pāṇḍavas. But he was sometimes your servant. The Lord gives liberation but not even** ***bhāva-bhakti*** **to those who worship him.**

**Комментарий:**

When Parīkṣit heard about bhakti he desired it. Śukadeva speaks to him, saying that he should have that most praiseworthy bhakti. Kṛṣṇa gives liberation but not prema (*bhakti-yogam*). That prema has absence of desire for results, and absence of other desires.

## Chapter Seven

### Text 3

*ajanābhaṁ nāmaitad varṣaṁ bhāratam iti yata ārabhya vyapadiśanti.*

**Перевод:**

**The place was known as Ajanābha-varṣa, but from Mahārāja Bharata's reign, it has become known as Bhārata-varṣa.**

**Комментарий:**

It was known as Ajanābha, because of the son of Nābhi. Bhārata was called Ajanābha, by the people, because of their great affection for him.

### Text 7

*evaṁ karma-viśuddhyā viśuddha-sattvasyāntar-hṛdayākāśa-śarīre brahmaṇi bhagavati vāsudeve mahā-puruṣa-rūpopalakṣaṇe śrīvatsa-kaustubha-vana-mālāri-dara-gadādibhir upalakṣite nija-puruṣa-hṛl-likhitenātmani puruṣa-rūpeṇa virocamāna uccaistarāṁ bhaktir anudinam edhamāna-rayājāyata.*

**Перевод:**

**Performing** ***karmas*** **purified by his intentions, the** ***bhakti*** **of Bharata, filled with** ***śuddha-sattva,*** **daily increased to higher levels of intensity. He directed his** ***bhakti*** **to the Lord situated in the heart, to the Brahman, and to Bhagavān, Vāsudeva, who is also seen the Lord of Vaikuṇṭha, endowed Śrīvatsa, Kaustubha, garland,** ***cakra,*** **conch and club, shining in the hearts of his devotees with his human form.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 222:**

By purification through karmas offered to the Lord, the bhakti of Bharata, who was filled with śuddha-sattva, manifested. This bhakti was composed of faith, hearing, chanting etc. To whom was it directed? It manifested as bhakti for Bhagavān, endowed with a complete svarūpa and qualities, for Vāsudeva, dwelling in all beings, for the antaryāmī or Paramātmā manifesting in his special abode (*śarīre)*, the space of heart who is also called Brahman (not the impersonal feature since that could not manifest), for the mahā-puruṣa described in scriptures, for the form decorated with śrīvatsa and other marks. That bhakti had ever increasing excellence (*edhamāna-rayāḥ*).

### Text 9

*yatra ha vāva bhagavān harir adyāpi tatratyānāṁ nija-janānāṁ vātsalyena sannidhāpyata icchā-rūpeṇa.*

**Перевод:**

**There, the Lord is visible even now in the form desired by the devotee, out of affection for his followers who reside in that place.**

**Комментарий:**

*Sannidhāpyate* means he became visible to the eye.

### Text 10

*yatrāśrama-padāny ubhayato nābhibhir dṛṣac-cakraiś cakra-nadī nāma sarit-pravarā sarvataḥ pavitrī-karoti.*

**Перевод:**

**In that place, the Gaṇḍakī, the best of rivers, purifies the hermitages with** ***śilas*** **marked with** ***cakras*** **and holes on both sides.**

**Комментарий:**

*Dṛṣac-cakraiḥ* refers to śalagrāmas.

### Text 13

*itthaṁ dhṛta-bhagavad-vrata aiṇeyājina-vāsasānusavanābhiṣekārdra-kapiśa-kuṭila-jaṭā-kalāpena ca virocamānaḥ sūryarcā bhagavantaṁ hiraṇmayaṁ puruṣam ujjihāne sūrya-maṇḍale 'bhyupatiṣṭhann etad u hovāca.*

**Перевод:**

**With a vow to worship the Lord, his body shining with deer skin, his brown, matted hair wet from his daily bath, he worshipped the golden Lord with a** ***mantra*** **dedicated to Viṣṇu in the sun, reciting it as the sun rose.**

**Комментарий:**

Thinking that ecstatic symptoms would be an obstacle to worshipping the Lord, he repressed them. He performed another type of worship, inspired by his intelligence.

### Text 14

*paro-rajaḥ savitur jāta-vedo*  
*devasya bhargo manasedaṁ jajāna*  
*suretasādaḥ punar āviśya caṣṭe*  
*haṁsaṁ gṛdhrāṇaṁ nṛṣad-riṅgirām imaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I surrender to the effulgent Lord situated in the sun, beyond** ***prakṛti,*** **fulfilling the desires of the** ***jīva,*** **who creates this universe by his will, enters it and maintains the** ***jīva*** **desiring material enjoyment, by inspiring the jīva’s intelligence.**

**Комментарий:**

He chanted a mantra similar to gāyatrī.

## Chapter Eight

### Text 26

*evam aghaṭamāna-manorathākula-hṛdayo mṛga-dārakābhāsena svārabdha-karmaṇā yogārambhaṇato vibhraṁśitaḥ sa yoga-tāpaso bhagavad-ārādhana-lakṣaṇāc ca katham itarathā jāty-antara eṇa-kuṇaka āsaṅgaḥ sākṣān niḥśreyasa-pratipakṣatayā prāk-parityakta-dustyaja-hṛdayābhijātasya tasyaivam antarāya-vihata-yogārambhaṇasya rājarṣer bharatasya tāvan mṛgārbhaka-poṣaṇa-pālana-prīṇana-lālanānuṣaṅgeṇāvigaṇayata ātmānam ahir ivākhu-bilaṁ duratikramaḥ kālaḥ karāla-rabhasa āpadyata.*

**Перевод:**

**His heart afflicted by unfulfilled desire, Bharata fell from his practice of** ***yoga*****, from his detachment from material enjoyment achieved by** ***bhakti,*** **and even from his worship of the Lord, by the Lord’s special arrangement of** ***prārabdha-karma*** **through the appearance of the young deer. How else could he develop attachment for the young deer? While Bharata, who had previously rejected his own sons as an obstacle to the highest goal, and who now produced an obstacle to his practice of** ***yoga*****, forgot himself by petting, showing affection for, protecting and nourishing the young deer, inevitable death with great speed approached him, just as snake approaches a mouse hole.**

**Комментарий:**

Starting with the preliminaries of yoga, with yama and niyama (*yogārambhanasya*), he worshipped the Lord with austerities mixed with yoga. He fell because of prārabdha-karma in the form of aparādha.

### Text 3

*tatrāpi svajana-saṅgāc ca bhṛśam udvijamāno bhagavataḥ karma-bandha-vidhvaṁsana-śravaṇa-smaraṇa-guṇa-vivaraṇa-caraṇāravinda-yugalaṁ manasā vidadhad ātmanaḥ pratighātam āśaṅkamāno bhagavad-anugraheṇānusmṛta-sva-pūrva-janmāvalir ātmānam unmatta-jaḍāndha-badhira-svarūpeṇa darśayām āsa lokasya.*

**Перевод:**

**In that life, fearing the association of his family members, he always remembered the lotus feet of the Lord which destroy the bondage of** ***karma*** **by hearing about and remembering their qualities. Fearing obstructions to his progress and remembering his previous lives by the mercy of the Lord, he assumed the appearance of a dull-minded, blind and deaf mad man for the public.**

**Комментарий:**

Because he was a devotee he feared obstacles to bhakti (*pratighātam*). The phrase *svajana-saṅgāc ca bhṛśam udvijamāna i*s not seen in some editions but this not approved by Śrīdhara Svāmī.

### Text 6

*evaṁ sva-tanuja ātmany anurāgāveśita-cittaḥ śaucādhyayana-vrata-niyama-gurv-anala-śuśrūṣaṇādy-aupakurvāṇaka-karmāṇy anabhiyuktāny api samanuśiṣṭena bhāvyam ity asad-āgrahaḥ putram anuśāsya svayaṁ tāvad anadhigata-manorathaḥ kālenāpramattena svayaṁ gṛha eva pramatta upasaṁhṛtaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**Being very attached to his son, thinking that the son should be taught by the father, though his desire was useless, he taught his son the** ***brahmacārī*** **activities of cleanliness, study, vows, following rules, accepting** ***guru*****, sacrifice and service, though Bharata was not willing. Without fulfilling his desire, he died in his house by the influence of vigilant time.**

**Комментарий:**

The father thought that he should be taught (*anuśiṣṭena hi bhāvyam*). Otherwise there would be fault on his part.

## Chapter Nine

### Text 8

*pitary uparate bhrātara enam atat-prabhāva-vidas trayyāṁ vidyāyām eva paryavasita-matayo na para-vidyāyāṁ jaḍa-matir iti bhrātur anuśāsana-nirbandhān nyavṛtsanta.*

**Перевод:**

**After the death of their father, the nine brothers, not understanding the position of Bharata, and being knowledgeable of the three Vedas but not knowing the highest truth, gave up trying to teach Bharata, thinking that he was dull-witted.**

**Комментарий:**

They thought he was retarded (*jaḍa-matir iti*). They did not know that bhakti was higher than knowledge of the Vedas. His higher knowledge was shown in verse 3. Seeing his devotion, they thought in this way. Integrally he was fixed on the Lord but externally he appeared like an ātmārāma to hide the bhakti. Because of his actions like those of a madman, they gave up teaching him. The aorist ending on the verb is poetic license.

### Text 6

*duḥkhaṁ sukhaṁ vyatiriktaṁ ca tīvraṁ*

*āliṅgya māyā-racitāntarātmā*  
*sva-dehinaṁ saṁsṛti-cakra-kūṭaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The mind, the cause of deception in the repetition of birth and death, the covering on the** ***jīva*** **made by** ***māyā,*** **by embracing the** ***jīva,*** **creates unavoidable distress, happiness, and illusion in the course of time.**

**Комментарий:**

The mind is the covering made of māyā. Vyāsa in samādhi said the same. Also it will be said:

*bhayaṁ dvitīyābhiniveśataḥ syād*  
*īśād apetasya viparyayo 'smṛtiḥ*  
*tan-māyayāto budha ābhajet taṁ*

**For the** ***jīva*** **averse to the Lord, there will be** ***saṁsāra*** **consisting of identity with body and lack of identity with the soul, because of his absorption in the material coverings on the soul, arising from the Lord’s** ***māyā.*** **Therefore, the intelligent person, taking** ***guru*** **as his Lord and very self, should fully worship the Lord with pure** ***bhakti.*** **(SB** [**11.2.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/2.37)**)**

*kṣetrajña etā manaso vibhūtīr*  
*jīvasya māyā-racitasya nityāḥ*  
*āvirhitāḥ kvāpi tirohitāś ca*  
*śuddho vicaṣṭe hy aviśuddha-kartuḥ*

**The liberated** ***jīva*****, free of** ***saṁsāra,*** **impartially sees the transformations of the covering on the** ***jīva*** **known as the mind, which performs actions opposed to the Lord and which is made of** ***māyā.*** **, These transformations are without beginning, sometimes visible and sometimes invisible. (SB** [**5.11.12**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/11.12)**)**

### Text 12

*kṣetrajña etā manaso vibhūtīr*  
*jīvasya māyā-racitasya nityāḥ*  
*āvirhitāḥ kvāpi tirohitāś ca*  
*śuddho vicaṣṭe hy aviśuddha-kartuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The liberated** ***jīva*****, free of** ***saṁsāra,*** **impartially sees the transformations of the covering on the** ***jīva*** **known as the mind, which performs actions opposed to the Lord and which is made of** ***māyā.*** **, These transformations are without beginning, sometimes visible and sometimes invisible.**

**Комментарий:**

Paramātmā is described. Though the Lord exists in Vaikuṇṭha, he manifests as an aṅga of Bhagavān in the material world. This form is described along with the jīva in two verses.

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 1:**

Though beyond māyā (*śuddhaḥ*) the jīva observes with attention (*vicaṣṭe*), and becomes absorbed in the well-known *(etāḥ)* actions (*vibhūtīḥ)* of the antaḥkaraṇa (*manasaḥ*) made of māyā. That jīva is called the *kṣetra-jña* since he is the knowers of the field comprised of his two bodies (gross and subtle). It is said:

*yayā sammohito jīva ātmānaṁ tri-guṇātmakam \|*  
*paro ’pi manute ’narthaṁ tat-kṛtaṁ cābhipadyate \|\|*

**Bewildered by that māyā, the jīva, though separate from the three guṇas, considers himself made of the three guṇas and takes on material existence created by the guṇas*****.*** **(SB** [**1.7.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/7.5)**)**

How is the mind made of māyā? It is undertaken (*racitasya*) by the jīva’s (*jīvasya*) upādhi, by his identity with it. What is the antaḥkaraṇa? It acts against the Lord (*aviśuddha-kartuḥ*). What are the actions of the mind? They continue without beginning (*nityāḥ*). When do the actions take place? They manifest *(avibhūtāḥ*) during waking and dream states and are invisible (*tirohitāḥ*) during deep sleep.

### Text 13-14

*kṣetrajña ātmā puruṣaḥ purāṇaḥ*  
*sākṣāt svayaṁ jyotir ajaḥ pareśaḥ*  
*nārāyaṇo bhagavān vāsudevaḥ*  
*sva-māyayātmany avadhīyamānaḥ*  
*yathānilaḥ sthāvara-jaṅgamānām*  
*ātma-svarūpeṇa niviṣṭa īśet*  
*evaṁ paro bhagavān vāsudevaḥ*  
*kṣetrajña ātmedam anupraviṣṭaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord is the knower of all bodies, is all pervasive, has a human form, is the cause of the universe, is self-revealing, is without material birth and is Lord of all others. He is Mahā-viṣṇu, the Lord of Vaikuṇṭha and the master of Mathurā and Dvārakā. He is the object of meditation for the liberated** ***jīvas,*** **by his mercy.**

**Комментарий:**

Just as the air enters all moving and stationary beings as *prāṇa* and controls them, the Supreme Lord Vāsudeva, the knower of all bodies, pervading everywhere, enters this universe.

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 1:**

The other kṣetra-jña is the puruṣa who is the cause of the universe and who is eternal (*purāṇaḥ*). He is self-manifesting (*sākṣād svayaṁ jyotiḥ),* unlike the jīva who is dependent. He is without birth (*ajaḥ*). He is the master even of Brahmā and others (*pareśaḥ*). He is the shelter of all the jīvas (Nārāyaṇa), since they are under his control. This form is called *bhagavān* since he is an aṁśa of Bhagavān, manifesting a portion of Bhagavān’s qualities such as aiśvarya. He is called *vāsudeva* because he is the abode of all beings. He is established in his svarūpa (*ātmani*) by his svarūpa- śakti (*sva-māyāyā*). The verb has a passive reflexive meaning. Even though the puruṣa enters into illusory māyā as the antaryāmī, he is situated in his svarūpa by his svarūpa-śakti and is never contaminated by māyā. He remains separate because he is the knower of all fields as Vāsudeva (the abode of all beings).

One kṣetra-jña, the jīva, is bewildered by māyā. The other kṣetra-jña, the Paramātmā (*ātmā* in verse [5.11.13](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/11.13)), is pure. Thus the chief kṣetra-jña is Paramātmā. It is said:

*sarvaṁ pumān veda guṇāṁś ca taj-jño*  
*na veda sarva-jñam anantam īḍe*

**The jīva who can know Paramātmā knows all about his body, but does not really know the omniscient Lord. I worship that Lord with infinite qualities. (SB** [**6.4.25**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/4.25)**)**

It the Gītā it is said:

*idaṁ śarīraṁ kaunteya kṣetram ity abhidhīyate \|*  
*etad yo vetti taṁ prāhuḥ kṣetra-jña iti tad-vidaḥ \|\|*

**O son of Kuntī, this body is called the field*****.*** **Those in knowledge call the person who knows this field the knower of the field.**

*kṣetra-jñaṁ cāpi māṁ viddhi sarva-kṣetreṣu bhārata \|*  
*kṣetra-kṣetrajñayor jñānaṁ yat taj jñānaṁ mataṁ mama \|\|*

**O descendent of Bharata, know that I also am the knower of the field in all bodies. According to me, knowledge means knowledge of the field and knowledge of the two types of knowers of the field. (BG** [**13.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/13.1)**,** [**13.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/13.3)**)**

*Kṣetra-jñaṁ cāpi māṁ viddhi* means “know me to be the knower of *all* fields (bodies).” This is not like the jīva who knows only his own body. The statement does not indicate identity of the jīva and the Lord by suggesting that a person should know a single, conscious substance without qualities (eg. Know me to be the jīva). Otherwise the phrase “in all bodies” would have no meaning. Later the Lord explains the distinction between himself and the jīva:

*jñeyaṁ yat tat pravakṣyāmi yaj jñātvāmṛtam aśnute \|*  
*anādi mat paraṁ brahma na sat tan nāsad ucyate \|\|*

**I will now speak about the object of knowledge, knowing which you will attain liberation. This supreme Brahman, Paramātmā, is without beginning, takes shelter of me, and is beyond material cause and effect. (BG** [**13.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/13.13)**)**

*sarvataḥ pāṇi-pādaṁ tat sarvato ’kṣi-śiro-mukham \|*  
*sarvataḥ śrutimal loke sarvam āvṛtya tiṣṭhati \|\|*

**Everywhere this Brahman has his hands and feet. Everywhere Brahman has his eyes, heads and mouths. Everywhere are his ears. Brahman covers everything in the universe. (BG** [**13.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/13.14)**)**

The distinction is also clear where the Lord teaches about the jīva’s practice of knowledge starting with freedom from pride. (BG [13.7-12](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/13.6))

If Kṛṣṇa had desired to identify the jīva with the Lord, saying something similar to *tat tvam asi:* you are that (Chāndogya Upaniṣad [6.8.7](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/6.8.7)), he would not have said *ksetra-kṣetrājñayor jñānam*: I consider knowledge to be knowledge of the field and the two types of knowers of the field. He would have said “I consider knowledge to be knowledge of the field and the two Lords who are knowers of the field.”

In explaining the Chāndogya Upaniṣad, Brahma-sūtra [1.3.20](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/1.3.20) says *anyārthas tu paramarśaḥ*: the mention of the jīva has another purpose, to show ultimately the Lord. Similarly in the Gītā verses, jīva is mentioned to show the one object of jīva’s knowledge, the Lord, and this meaning is suitable to the context.

One should also not take the meaning to be simply knowledge of the field and the jīva, its knower, (excluding the Lord) as in atheistic Sāṅkhya philosophy (which proposes only prakṛti and puruṣa), since the word *mām,* referring to the Lord, is also mentioned. One should also not take the vivarta-vāda meaning--that the jīva is the Lord under illusion, since such a statement is never proved by scriptures like the Gītā and the Vedas. It amounts to Buddhist philosophy. Furthermore, the explanations of vivarta-vāda are illogical, like those of the Buddhists.

*vāyur yathaiko bhuvanaṁ praviṣṭo*  
*rūpaṁ rūpaṁ pratirūpo babhūva \|*  
*ekas tathā sarva-bhūtāntarātmā*  
*rūpaṁ rūpaṁ pratirūpo bahiś ca*

**Just as air enters the world and displays many forms, the one Lord, the soul within all beings, displays many forms. (Katha Upaniṣad)**

### Text 11

He is shown to be the shelter of all things as Vāsudeva.

### Text 12

Without bathing in the foot dust of devotees, one cannot realize the Lord, since that brings about qualification, being the cause of all purification.

### Text 13

*yatrottamaśloka-guṇānuvādaḥ*

**Комментарий:**

Speaking of details, the steps in obtaining the highest truth are described. The topics should be heard by person who give up all except the Lord (*mumukṣoh*).

## Chapter Thirteen

### Text 10

*karhi sma cit kṣudra-rasān vicinvaṁs*  
*tan-makṣikābhir vyathito vimānaḥ*  
*tatrāti-kṛcchrāt pratilabdhamāno*  
*balād vilumpanty atha taṁ tato 'nye*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes, searching for a little honey, he is bitten by bees. Disgraced, he finally obtains it with great difficulty, but by force others steal it from him.**

**Комментарий:**

Others steal what he has obtained. He is ready to fight with them.

### Text 13

*anyonya-vitta-vyatiṣaṅga-vṛddha-*  
*vairānubandho vivahan mithaś ca*  
*adhvany amuṣminn uru-kṛcchra-vitta-*  
*bādhopasargair viharan vipannaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Living with increased enmity because of business transactions for wealth, he marries. Wandering on the path, he almost dies because of the great difficulties, loss of wealth and the sickness that he encounters.**

**Комментарий:**

*Vipannaḥ* means dead.

### Text 23

*namo mahadbhyo 'stu namaḥ śiśubhyo*  
*namo yuvabhyo nama āvaṭubhyaḥ*  
*ye brāhmaṇā gām avadhūta-liṅgāś*  
*caranti tebhyaḥ śivam astu rājñām*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the great devotees, to the babies, to the youths, to the young boys. I offer respects to the** ***brāhmaṇas*** **who wander the earth in the dress of** ***avadhūtas.*** **May there be auspiciousness for the kings!**

**Комментарий:**

The boys (*baṭubhyaḥ*) indicates persons who do not have faith in knowers of Brahman, since they are absorbed in play.

### Text 24

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*ity evam uttarā-mātaḥ sa vai brahmarṣi-sutaḥ sindhu-pataya ātma-satattvaṁ vigaṇayataḥ parānubhāvaḥ parama-kāruṇikatayopadiśya rahūgaṇena sakaruṇam abhivandita-caraṇa āpūrṇārṇava iva nibhṛta-karaṇormy-āśayo dharaṇim imāṁ vicacāra*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: O Parīkṣit! The** ***brāhmaṇa*** **Jaḍa Bharata, having the highest realization, though insulted by the King, out of great mercy instructed the King on spiritual matters. Having been worshipped by the King with his tears of regret, his heart became as quiet as the full ocean after the agitation of the waves of his senses had been calmed, and he again wandered the earth.**

**Комментарий:**

The visarga in *uttarāmātaḥ* is poetic license.

### Text 26

*rājovāca*  
*yo ha vā iha bahu-vidā mahā-bhāgavata tvayābhihitaḥ parokṣeṇa vacasā jīva-loka-bhavādhvā sa hy ārya-manīṣayā kalpita-viṣayo nāñjasāvyutpanna-loka-samadhigamaḥ; atha tad evaitad duravagamaṁ samavetānukalpena nirdiśyatām iti.*

**Перевод:**

**King Parīkṣit said: O great devotee! Possessing great knowledge, you have described with symbolic words the material existence of the** ***jīva.*** **This allegorical presentation for a person with refined intellect cannot be understood by inexperienced persons. Therefore you should explain this presentation using suitable examples.**

**Комментарий:**

*Iti* indicates the King said this.

## Chapter Fourteen

### Text 2

*yasyām u ha vā ete ṣaḍ-indriya-nāmānaḥ karmaṇā dasyava eva te; tad yathā puruṣasya dhanaṁ yat kiñcid dharmaupayikaṁ bahu-kṛcchrādhigataṁ sākṣāt parama-puruṣārādhana-lakṣaṇo yo 'sau dharmas taṁ tu sāmparāya udāharanti; tad-dharmyaṁ dhanaṁ darśana-sparśana-śravaṇāsvādanāvaghrāṇa-saṅkalpa-vyavasāya-gṛha-grāmyopabhogena kunāthasyājitātmano yathā sārthasya vilum-panti.*

**Перевод:**

**In that forest, the thieves are the six senses. Just as thieves plunder a man’s wealth which is meant for service to the Lord and has been attained with great difficulty by rightful means, and just as thieves plunder the wealth of a merchant who is without proper intelligence and has uncontrolled senses, so the senses by their actions of enjoying seeing, touching, hearing, tasting smelling, thinking and deciding, plunder man’s wealth.**

**Комментарий:**

The senses as thieves are described in three verses. Man’s wealth attained with difficult is meant for dharma (*dharmaupayikam*). It is meant for worship of the Lord.

### Text 7

*kvacic cāśeṣa-doṣa-niṣadanaṁ purīṣa-viśeṣaṁ tad-varṇa-guṇa-nirmita-matiḥ suvarṇam upāditsaty agni-kāma-kātara ivolmuka-piśācam.*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes, when his mind becomes absorbed in gold, the color of** ***rajoguṇa*****, which is the source of unlimited faults, he desires to possess gold, just as a person suffering from cold, desiring fire, chases after a glowing ghost.**

**Комментарий:**

He desires gold, a special type of earth just as a person desiring fire chases a ghost. Gold is used for making the Lord’s ornaments. That was explained with the words d*harmaupayikam.*

### Text 9

*kvacic ca vātyaupamyayā pramadayāroham āropitas tat-kāla-rajasā rajanī-bhūta ivāsādhu-maryādo rajas-valākṣo 'pi dig-devatā atirajas-vala-matir na vijānāti.*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes he places on his lap a beautiful woman, like a whirlwind, and by the impulse of passion, like dust, ignoring the rules of good conduct, his intelligence becomes blinded by passion, like getting dust in his eye, and he cannot recognize the** ***devatās*** **of the directions who are witnesses, and have become invisible to him.**

**Комментарий:**

*Rajanī bhūtaḥ* means entities of the night, or action similar to theirs, with the word *iva*.

### Text 30

*yadā pākhaṇḍibhir ātma-vañcitais tair uru vañcito brahma-kulaṁ samāvasaṁs teṣāṁ śīlam upanayanādi-śrauta-smārta-karmānuṣṭhā-nena bhagavato yajña-puruṣasyārādhanam eva tad arocayan śūdra-kulaṁ bhajate nigamācāre 'śuddhito yasya mithunī-bhāvaḥ kuṭumba-bharaṇaṁ yathā vānara-jāteḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**When the person is greatly cheated by imposters who cheat themselves, he settles among** ***brāhmaṇas*****, but has no taste for their way of worshipping the Lord of sacrifice by performance of rituals such as taking the sacred thread according to** ***śruti*** **and** ***smṛti.*** **He then joins the** ***śūdras.*** **In that group he performs what is forbidden in the scriptures, such as forbidden sexual union, simply supporting a family like the apes.**

**Комментарий:**

Because he does not perform pure acts according to scripture (*nigamācare aśuddhitaḥ*) he joins the śūdras.

### Text 33

*evam adhvany avarundhāno mṛtyu-gaja-bhayāt tamasi giri-kandara-prāye.*

**Перевод:**

**On the path, he is restricted to sinful acts, and out of fear of death, he falls into ignorance which is like a mountain cave.**

**Комментарий:**

He is in darkness as if in a mountain cave. Falling sick, in fear of the elephant of death, he remains taking shelter of old karmas, which are like vines.

### Text 45

*yajñāya dharma-pataye vidhi-naipuṇāya*  
*yogāya sāṅkhya-śirase prakṛtīśvarāya*  
*nārāyaṇāya haraye nama ity udāraṁ*  
*hāsyan mṛgatvam api yaḥ samudājahāra*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the personification of sacrifice, the master of** ***dharma*****, who is skilful in the rules for sacrifice, to the personification of** ***yoga*****, to the head of Sāṅkhya, to the Lord of** ***prakṛti,*** **to Nārāyaṇa, to Hari. Wanting to give up his deer body, he chanted loudly this prayer.**

**Комментарий:**

I offer myself in service to the Lord, who is the form of dharma (*yajñāya*), the protector of dharma (*dharma-pataye*), the instituter of dharma (*vidhi-naipuṇyāya*), the form of jñāna-sādhana (*yogāya*), the form of jñāna (*sāṅkhya-śirase*), the controller of māyā (*prakṛtīśvarāya*), the shelter of the universe (*nārāyaṇāya*), the supremely attractive person (*haraye*).

## Chapter Fifteen

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*bharatasyātmajaḥ sumatir nāmābhihito yam u ha vāva kecit pākhaṇḍina ṛṣabha-padavīm anuvartamānaṁ cānāryā aveda-samāmnātāṁ devatāṁ sva-manīṣayā pāpīyasyā kalau kalpayiṣyanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: The son of Bharata named Sumati followed the path of Ṛṣabha. Some inferior, sinful heretics would imagine him by their own minds to be Buddha, who is not approved by the Vedas.**

**Комментарий:**

The future (*kalpayiṣyanti*) is used for the past where there is an opinion that something will not take place or cannot be tolerated. The rule is *anavaklṛptyamarṣayorakiṁvṛtte ’pi*. (Pāṇini 3.3.45)

### Text 7

*sa vai sva-dharmeṇa prajā-pālana-poṣaṇa-prīṇanopalālanānuśāsana-lakṣaṇenejyādinā ca bhagavati mahā-puruṣe parāvare brahmaṇi sarvātmanārpita-paramārtha-lakṣaṇena brahmavic-caraṇānusevayāpādita-bhagavad-bhakti-yogena cābhīkṣṇaśaḥ paribhāvitāti-śuddha-matir uparatānātmya ātmani svayam upalabhyamāna-brahmātmānubhavo 'pi nirabhimāna evāvanim ajūgupat.*

**Перевод:**

**King Gaya, without ego, realizing Brahman in himself and devoid of false conception, ruled the world. His pure mind was constantly filled with love for the Lord through** ***bhakti-yoga*** **achieved by service to the devotees, which was endowed with the spiritual quality of offering everything to the Supreme Lord, Bhagavān, Brahman, the source if high and low creatures, through performing sacrifices and executing** ***dharma*** **in the form of protecting, nourishing, satisfying, caressing, and chastising the citizens.**

**Комментарий:**

Realizing Brahman, experiencing ātmā, he still ruled the kingdom. He hid his realization because he was without pride (*nirabhimānaḥ*).

### Text 13

*yat-prīṇanād barhiṣi deva-tiryaṅ-*  
*manuṣya-vīrut-tṛṇam āviriñcāt*  
*prīyeta sadyaḥ sa ha viśva-jīvaḥ*  
*prītaḥ svayaṁ prītim agād gayasya*

**Перевод:**

**When the Lord is pleased, immediately all** ***devatās*****, humans, plants, grass, and all beings up to Brahmā become satisfied. The Lord, the cause of life in the universe, the very form of satisfaction, became satisfied at the sacrifice of King Gaya.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord, the cause of all life (*viśva-jīvaḥ*), the form of happiness (*prītaḥ*), was pleased with Gaya.

## Chapter Sixteen

### Text 3

*bhagavato guṇamaye sthūla-rūpa āveśitaṁ mano hy aguṇe 'pi sūkṣmatama ātma-jyotiṣi pare brahmaṇi bhagavati vāsudevākhye kṣamam āveśituṁ tad u haitad guro 'rhasy anuvarṇayitum iti.*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***guru*****! The mind fixed on the Lord’s gross form made of matter can absorb itself in the supreme Brahman, Bhagavān, Vāsudeva, light of the soul, who is beyond matter. Therefore, you should explain the structure of the universe.**

**Комментарий:**

The world made of guṇas is the Lord’s gross form (*bhagavato guṇamaye*), but the Lord himself is beyond the guṇas.

### Text 5

*yo vāyaṁ dvīpaḥ kuvalaya-kamala-kośābhyantara-kośo niyuta-yojana-viśālaḥ samavartulo yathā puṣkara-patram.*

**Перевод:**

**The central portion within the seven islands which are situated as circular coverings in the lotus of Bhū-maṇḍala, which is for certain this visible Jambūdvīpa, is 100,000** ***yojanas*** **(*****niyuta*****) wide and is circular like a lotus leaf.**

**Комментарий:**

Though *niyuta* means 1,000,000 sometimes it means 100,000.

### Text 6

*yasmin nava varṣāṇi nava-yojana-sahasrāyāmāny aṣṭabhir maryādā-giribhiḥ suvibhaktāni bhavanti.*

**Перевод:**

**In Jambūdvīpa there are nine divisions of land, each extending to a width of 9,000** ***yojanas*****, completely separated by eight mountain ranges.**

**Комментарий:**

There are different opinions on the divisions. These may vary according to the specific creation of Brahmā.

## Chapter Seventeen

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*tatra bhagavataḥ sākṣād yajña-liṅgasya viṣṇor vikramato vāma-pādāṅguṣṭha-nakha-nirbhinnordhvāṇḍa-kaṭāha-vivareṇāntaḥ-praviṣṭā yā bāhya-jala-dhārā tac-caraṇa-paṅkajāvanejanāruṇa-kiñjalkoparañjitākhila-jagad-agha-malāpahopasparśanāmalā sākṣād bhagavat-padīty anupalakṣita-vaco 'bhidhīyamānāti-mahatā kālena yuga-sahasropalakṣaṇena divo mūrdhany avatatāra yat tad viṣṇu-padam āhuḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**When the Lord appeared as Vāmana and stepped over the earth with his right foot, the water which entered from outside, by piercing the covering of the universe with the big toe of his left foot, tinted red from the color of Lord’s feet and the** ***kuṁkuma*** **coming from the water that washed his lotus feet, which purifies by its touch all sins of the world but remains pure, not known by any other name, was called Bhagavat-padī, “water coming from the foot of the Lord.” After a long time, a thousand** ***yugas,*** **the river descended to Dhruva-loka, which then became known as Viṣṇu-pada, the abode of Viṣṇu.**

**Комментарий:**

*Yuga* here means five years, according to astronomical terms.

### Text 3

*tataḥ sapta ṛṣayas tat prabhāvābhijñā yāṁ nanu tapasa ātyantikī siddhir etāvatī bhagavati sarvātmani vāsudeve 'nuparata-bhakti-yoga-lābhenaivopekṣitānyārthātma-gatayo muktim ivāgatāṁ mumukṣava iva sabahu-mānam adyāpi jaṭā-jūṭair udvahanti.*

**Перевод:**

**The seven great sages, aware of the influence of the water of the Gaṅgā, ignoring all other goals because of their continuous** ***bhakti-yoga*** **to the all-pervading Supreme Lord Vāsudeva, thinking “‘This is the perfection of austerities,” to this day respectfully keep Ganges water in their matted locks, as much as persons desiring liberation respect liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

*Adyāpi* means “at all times, after the appearance of Gaṅgā.”

### Text 14

*navasv api varṣeṣu bhagavān nārāyaṇo mahā-puruṣaḥ puruṣāṇāṁ tad-anugrahāyātma-tattva-vyūhenātmanādyāpi sannidhīyate.*

**Перевод:**

**To show mercy to his devotees in each of these nine tracts of land, the great Lord known as Nārāyaṇa remains near his devotees in various forms.**

**Комментарий:**

It is shown that all the avatāras are eternal manifested. He remains close in his svarūpa (*ātmanā*), not as a deity. This will be described in relation to Pradyumna’s appearance. The Lord is eternally present (*adyāpi*), according to the conditions.

### Text 16

*bhavānī-nāthaiḥ strī-gaṇārbuda-sahasrair avarudhyamāno bhagavataś catur-mūrter mahā-puruṣasya turīyāṁ tāmasīṁ mūrtiṁ prakṛtim ātmanaḥ saṅkarṣaṇa-saṁjñām ātma-samādhi-rūpeṇa sannidhāpyaitad abhigṛṇan bhava upadhāvati.*

**Перевод:**

**In Ilāvṛta-varṣa, Lord Śiva is always encircled by ten billion maidservants of goddess Durgā. Uttering the following** ***mantra*** **in meditation, Śiva worships Saṅkarṣaṇa, his source, the spiritual form in charge of ignorance, among the four forms of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Śiva worships Saṅkarṣaṇa, the form which instigates tamas, when pralaya takes place (*tāmasīṁ mūrtim*).

### Text 17

*śrī-bhagavān uvāca*  
*oṁ namo bhagavate mahā-puruṣāya sarva-guṇa-saṅkhyānāyānantāyāvyaktāya nama iti.*

**Перевод:**

**Śiva said: I offer respects to Bhagavān, the great** ***puruṣa,*** **the revelation of all qualities, the unlimited, and the unfathomable.**

**Комментарий:**

He praises the form of Saṅkarṣaṇa, the first puruṣa with dāsya-bhakti.

### Text 18

*bhaje bhajenyāraṇa-pāda-paṅkajaṁ*  
*bhagasya kṛtsnasya paraṁ parāyaṇam*  
*bhakteṣv alaṁ bhāvita-bhūta-bhāvanaṁ*  
*bhavāpahaṁ tvā bhava-bhāvam īśvaram*

**Перевод:**

**I worship wholeheartedly the Lord with lotus feet, the highest shelter of all six qualities, the ornament of the devotees, the object of meditation for Brahmā, the destroyer of material life, who has** ***prema*** **for me, your servant.**

**Комментарий:**

He is generous with his devotees (*bhakteṣv alam*). He creates or maintains all beings (*bhāvita-bhūta-bhāvanam*).

### Text 20

*asad-dṛśo yaḥ pratibhāti māyayā*  
*kṣībeva madhv-āsava-tāmra-locanaḥ*  
*na nāga-vadhvo 'rhaṇa īśire hriyā*  
*yat-pādayoḥ sparśana-dharṣitendriyāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**For persons with impure vision, the Supreme Lord appears like an intoxicated person by the influence of** ***māyā,*** **but he is not such a red-eyed drunk. The wives of Kāliya were unable to worship the Lord because of shyness, since their senses were agitated by the touch of his feet.**

**Комментарий:**

Saṅkarṣaṇa situated in Pātāla is explained. He does not actually get drunk from liquor. He did not manifest a form of fear to the wives of Kāliya, but a form they approached with shyness and care. They approached him with bewilderment in prema, with special charm.

### Text 22-23

*yasyādya āsīd guṇa-vigraho mahān*  
*vijñāna-dhiṣṇyo bhagavān ajaḥ kila*  
*yat-sambhavo 'haṁ tri-vṛtā sva-tejasā*  
*vaikārikaṁ tāmasam aindriyaṁ sṛje*  
*ete vayaṁ yasya vaśe mahātmanaḥ*  
*sthitāḥ śakuntā iva sūtra-yantritāḥ*  
*mahān ahaṁ vaikṛta-tāmasendriyāḥ*  
*sṛjāma sarve yad-anugrahād idam*

**Перевод:**

**First there was the form of** ***māyā*** **called** ***mahat-tattva.*** **Lord Brahmā is the shelter of** ***sattva-guṇa.*** **From Brahmā I appear. By the Lord’s power and the power of the** ***guṇas,*** **I create the** ***devatās,*** **the elements and the senses. All of us—*****mahat-tattva,*** **the** ***devatās,*** **the elements and senses---are like birds tied by a thread. Under his control, by his mercy, we create this universe.**

**Комментарий:**

*Mahān* means mahat-tattva or citta. It is the basis endowed with guṇas (*guṇa-vigrahaḥ*). It is the form of buddhi (*vijñāna-dhiṣnyaḥ*). Vāsudevan (*bhagavān*) and Brahmā (*ajaḥ*) were there. I become qualified for creation (*sṛje*). I cause creation as fire cooks rice.

## Chapter Eighteen

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*tathā ca bhadraśravā nāma dharma-sutas tat-kula-patayaḥ puruṣā bhadrāśva-varṣe sākṣād bhagavato vāsudevasya priyāṁ tanuṁ dharmamayīṁ hayaśīrṣābhidhānāṁ parameṇa samādhinā sannidhāpyedam abhigṛṇanta upadhāvanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: In Bhadrāśva, Bhadraśravā, the son of Dharmarāja, accompanied by his descendants and all the residents of the land, worships the dear form of Vāsudeva known as Hayaśīrṣa, the embodiment of** ***dharma,*** **seeing him directly by intense concentration and by chanting the following** ***mantra.***

**Комментарий:**

Bhadraśravā worships the Lord with the rasa of astonishment. By complete absorption in the Lord, he sees the Lord directly (*samādhinā sannidhāpya*).

### Text 5

*viśvodbhava-sthāna-nirodha-karma te*  
*hy akartur aṅgīkṛtam apy apāvṛtaḥ*  
*yuktaṁ na citraṁ tvayi kārya-kāraṇe*  
*sarvātmani vyatirikte ca vastutaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**You are uncontaminated. Though you are not the doer, you accept the actions of creating, maintaining and destroying the universe. This is not astonishing for you, the cause of all causes, the soul of all, separate from all. It is reasonable for you.**

**Комментарий:**

It is not astonishing because you have acintya-śakti.

### Text 8

*oṁ namo bhagavate narasiṁhāya namas tejas-tejase āvir-āvirbhava vajra-nakha vajra-daṁṣṭra karmāśayān randhaya randhaya tamo grasa grasa oṁ svāhā; abhayam abhayam ātmani bhūyiṣṭhā oṁ kṣraum.*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Supreme Lord Narasiṁha, the power among all powers. Please appear before me! O Lord with thunderbolt nails and teeth! Burn up the impressions of** ***karma.*** **Destroy ignorance! Please appear in my mind so that it becomes fearless.**

**Комментарий:**

Prahlāda worships the Lord with vīrya, karuṇa and śānta rasas.

### Text 10

*māgāra-dārātmaja-vitta-bandhuṣu*  
*saṅgo yadi syād bhagavat-priyeṣu naḥ*  
*yaḥ prāṇa-vṛttyā parituṣṭa ātmavān*  
*siddhyaty adūrān na tathendriya-priyaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**If we have attachment for devotees of the Lord, let attachment to house, wife, children or wealth not take place. With material attachment, he who is satisfied with simply living reaches perfection. One who satisfies his senses reaches perfection slowly.**

**Комментарий:**

He explains the nature of attachment. He who is satisfied with maintaining his life reaches perfection, whereas a person attached to his senses does not. Though that difference exists, if we have attachment to devotees, attachment to house and other things will not at all take place. There will only be attachment to devotees. *Prāṇa-vṛttyā* means “just by satisfying hunger and thirst.” *Ātmavān* means “without material association.”

### Text 11

*yat-saṅga-labdhaṁ nija-vīrya-vaibhavaṁ*  
*tīrthaṁ muhuḥ saṁspṛśatāṁ hi mānasam*  
*haraty ajo 'ntaḥ śrutibhir gato 'ṅgajaṁ*  
*ko vai na seveta mukunda-vikramam*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, entering into the heart through the ears, removes the desires in the mind of those persons who constantly sip the holy water in the form of the Lord’s pastimes attained by devotee association. Who would not hear the pastimes of Mukunda?**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord *(ajaḥ*) entering the heart through the ears, attracts the mind of persons hearing the exploits of Mukunda, which are like a tīrtha, attained through devotees. He attracts the mind by pure bhakti. Another meaning is “the Lord destroys desires (*aṅgajam*) in the mind.”

### Text 12

*yasyāsti bhaktir bhagavaty akiñcanā*  
*sarvair guṇais tatra samāsate surāḥ*  
*harāv abhaktasya kuto mahad-guṇā*  
*manorathenāsati dhāvato bahiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***devatās*** **constantly dwell with all good qualities in that person who has pure** ***bhakti*** **for the Lord. There are no good qualities in the non-devotee who chases after temporary material objects with desire for material pleasure.**

**Комментарий:**

Moreover the following occurs. The Lord and his associates (*surāḥ*) along with all good qualities (which appear on their own) dwell (*samāsate*), under control of the person, in the person having pure bhakti.

### Text 13

*harir hi sākṣād bhagavān śarīriṇām*

## Chapter Nineteen

### Text 2

*ārṣṭiṣeṇena saha gandharvair anugīyamānāṁ parama-kalyāṇīṁ bhartṛ-bhagavat-kathāṁ samupaśṛṇoti svayaṁ cedaṁ gāyati.*

**Перевод:**

**Hanumān, along with Arṣṭiṣeṇa, constantly hears with rapt attention the most auspicious glories of his master, sung by Gandharvas. Hanumān chants the following** ***mantras.***

**Комментарий:**

He would hear topics of the Lord, deriving special happiness from thinking of Rāma as his master (*bhartṛ-bhagavat-kathām*).

### Text 3

*oṁ namo bhagavate uttamaślokāya nama ārya-lakṣaṇa-śīla-vratāya nama upaśikṣitātmana upāsita-lokāya namaḥ sādhu-vāda-nikaṣaṇāya namo brahmaṇya-devāya mahā-puruṣāya mahā-rājāya nama iti.*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Supreme Lord, most praiseworthy, who is the personification of the best markings, qualities and vows. I offer respects to the Lord who accepted teachings from others, and who is worshipped by all types of people. I offer respects to the Lord who makes all good qualities the most excellent. I offer respects to the Lord worshipped by the** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **who is the great personality, the great king.**

**Комментарий:**

*Oṁ namo bhagavate indicates* his aiśvarya. *Uttamaślokāya* indicates his mādhurya aspect. *Ārya* means worthy of worship.

### Text 4

*yat tad viśuddhānubhava-mātram ekaṁ*  
*sva-tejasā dhvasta-guṇa-vyavastham*  
*pratyak praśāntaṁ sudhiyopalambhanaṁ*  
*hy anāma-rūpaṁ nirahaṁ prapadye*

**Перевод:**

**I surrender with certainly to the Lord who is one alone, who is devoid of** ***māyā*** **by the power of his form, who is invisible, peaceful, realized by the intelligent, and who has no material form and name.**

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarbha 194:**

Svarūpa-jñāna is shown *by yat tad viśuddhānubhava-mātram ekaṁ*: I surrender with certainly to the Lord who is realized as one alone. (SB [5.10.4](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/10.4)) *Yat tat* means the famous, dark green form of Rāma. He is said to be one manifestation only (*mātram*), since he is considered to be made of properties emanating from his svarūpa such as form and qualities, like the qualities of whiteness or brilliance in the light of the sun. That the properties are his svarūpa-śakti was established in Bhagavat Sandarbha. Thus the Lord is one only (*ekam*). His śakti is also beyond māyā. *Sva-tejasā dhvasta-guṇa-vyavastham:* he is devoid of māyā by the power of his svarūpa-śakti. He is devoid of all disturbances(*praśāntam*). He is realized only (*anubhava-mātram*). This is because he is not seen *(pratyak*).

*Na cakṣuṣā paśyanta rūpam asya:* one cannot see his form with the eye. (Kaṭha Upaniṣad [2.39](https://vedarama.com/en/books/katha-bs/5.10.4))

*yam evaiṣa vṛṇute tena labhyas tasyaiṣā ātmā vivṛṇute tanuṁ svām*

**The Lord reveals his form to the person he chooses. (Kaṭha Upaniṣad** [**1.2.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/katha-bs/1.2.23)**)**

Why? He is without material name and form.

*etās tisro devatā anena jīvenātmanānupraviśya nāma-rūpe vyākaravāṇī*

**Entering the three devatās as jīva, I will reveal name and form. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**6.3.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/6.3.2)**)**

This is because he is without ahaṅkāra (*niraham)*. The word *ātmā* mentioned in the Upaniṣad refers to the jīva-śakti of Paramātmā, the aṁśa of Paramātmā. That is made clear with the phrase *anena jīvena ātmanā*. “Entering the three devatās” means “absorption in upādhis like fire, water and earth.” That occurs because of the imposition of ahaṅkāra on the jīva. Situated as antaryāmī, the Lord is devoid of names and forms produced as upādhis, since he is not covered by matter, though situated in it. Since the Lord is devoid of ahaṅkāra, revealing (material) names and forms does not apply to him.

“The form of Rāma is not perceived by everyone.” He is realized by the pure hearted *(sudhiyopalambhanam*). By a pure heart he is realized in his svarūpa. Brahmā says *nātaḥ paraṁ parama yad bhavataḥ svarūpam:* O supreme, I do not see your supreme form. (SB [3.9.3](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/9.3))

### Text 5

*martyāvatāras tv iha martya-śikṣaṇaṁ*  
*rakṣo-vadhāyaiva na kevalaṁ vibhoḥ*  
*kuto 'nyathā syād ramataḥ sva ātmanaḥ*  
*sītā-kṛtāni vyasanānīśvarasya*

**Перевод:**

**The appearance of the Lord in this world as a human was not only for the purpose of killing Rāvaṇa, but to teach the human beings. Otherwise, why would the Lord, the enjoyer who is satisfied in himself, suffer in separation from Sītā?**

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarhba 195:**

“What is the purpose of appearing in this world in that form?” Though there are secondary purposes, the main purpose is to reveal the sweetness of his pastimes to his devotees.

The word *tu* removes doubt. His appearance in the world is not only for killing those who harass the devotees but for teaching the humans. He appears in order to teach the humans. Revealing the difficulty of material association to the materialists is an accompanying purpose. The purpose is to reveal to those with impressions of bhakti the sweetness of his special pastimes with separation and union, which melt the heart. That is the purpose. If the purpose was only to kill demons, then why would he suffer in separation from Sītā (*seta-kṛtāni vyasanāni),* since the Lord enjoys in Vaikuṇṭha in one form, and enjoys within all beings, full of all powers, as Paramātmā? He has the power to kill the demons by his mind without any suffering. However, for revealing his sweetness, all this takes place.

The great sweetness of his mercy and pastimes has been praised. The suffering due to separation from Sītā is included in the sweetness of the pastimes. It is not a fault. That is shown.

### Text 6

*na vai sa ātmātmavatāṁ suhṛttamaḥ*  
*saktas tri-lokyāṁ bhagavān vāsudevaḥ*  
*na strī-kṛtaṁ kaśmalam aśnuvīta*  
*na lakṣmaṇaṁ cāpi vihātum arhati*

**Перевод:**

**Rāma, Paramātmā, the best friend of the devotees, full of six qualities as Bhagavān, Vāsudeva, is not attached to the three worlds. Otherwise, he should not have become bewildered by a woman, and should not have given up Lakṣmaṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarbha 196:**

The pastime is not material. It does not arise from attachment to desire. It arises for showing special mercy to his special devotees.

He is not attached to the three worlds because he is Paramātmā (*ātmā),* full of all powers (Bhagavān) and the shelter of all (*vāsudevaḥ*). He is the best friend of the devotees, who have possessiveness of him *(ātmavatām*), for whom he exists as the master. Therefore he could not undergo suffering caused by attachment to a women, like others. But he would undergo suffering for Sītā, who is the object of his love, which causes such suffering, since she has the greatest possessiveness of him. Giving up Lakṣmaṇa, though he was a great devotee, since he interrupted Rāma’s consultation with Devadūta, could also not have been done. Later he ascended to his abode with Sītā, Lakṣmaṇa and others who were waiting for his coming to Svarga. We also see that he is even now with Sītā in Kimpuruṣa-varṣa. Thus he only appeared to suffer in order to preserve the conventions.

### Text 7

*na janma nūnaṁ mahato na saubhagaṁ*  
*na vāṅ na buddhir nākṛtis toṣa-hetuḥ*  
*tair yad visṛṣṭān api no vanaukasaś*  
*cakāra sakhye bata lakṣmaṇāgrajaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is not pleased by one’s birth in a great dynasty, by wealth, by eloquence, by intelligence or by bodily features, because Rāma, the older brother of Lakṣmaṇa, accepted as his friends us forest dwellers, who are devoid of those assets.**

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarbha 197:**

In order to reinforce this meaning, two verses speak of the supreme position of the highest sweetness predominated by showing mercy because of the devotee’s bhakti alone.

Rāma is not pleased with birth from a great person (*mahataḥ)* or with beauty (*saubhagam*) or by caste *(ākṛṭiḥ*) because (*yat*) he made friends with us, who had been rejected (*visṛsṭān*) by birth (Hanumān speaks) since he was satisfied with our bhakti, which took the form of looking for Sītā, his highest devotee. He was the elder brother of Lakṣmaṇa, qualified with all good qualities and markings, and gave joy to Sumitrā. Though we were qualified as servants, he accepted us as friends, playing with us. Or Sugrīva can be the person indicated in this verse.

### Text 8

*suro 'suro vāpy atha vānaro naraḥ*  
*sarvātmanā yaḥ sukṛtajñam uttamam*  
*bhajeta rāmaṁ manujākṛtiṁ hariṁ*  
*ya uttarān anayat kosalān divam iti*

**Перевод:**

**Whether one is a** ***devatā,*** **demon, monkey or man, one should worship by all methods excellent Rāma, in the form of a human, who accepts worship easily, and who took all the inhabitants of Ayodhyā to Vaikuṇṭha.**

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarbha 198:**

Previously it was shown that one worships the highest svarūpa of the Lord in human form by bhakti with knowledge of the Lord’s svarūpa. Now, one should worship the Lord in human form by bhakti with knowledge of the Lord’s sweetness. But other human forms like Kapila are rejected. Thus Rāma alone is specifically mentioned. He has unsurpassed qualities (*uttamam*). He is satisfied with even a little bhakti *(sukṛtajñam*).

### Text 9

*bhārate 'pi varṣe bhagavān nara-nārāyaṇākhya ākalpāntam upacita-dharma-jñāna-vairāgyaiśvaryopaśamoparamātmopalambhanam anugrahāyātmavatām anukampayā tapo 'vyakta-gatiś carati.*

**Перевод:**

**In Bhārata-varṣa, the Lord in the form of Nara-Nārāyaṇa, whose glories are inconceivable, to favor to the devotees*****,*** **mercifully performs austerities till the end of the** ***yuga*****, which included realization of the Lord*****,*** **abundant** ***dharma*****, knowledge, renunciation, powers, sense control and freedom from false ego.**

**Комментарий:**

Nara and Nārāyaṇa are treated as one entity. Because of mercy to the devotees (*ātmavatām*), who have the Lord (*ātmā)* as their master, giving them auspiciousness, he performs austerities. Such austerity is his show of mercy. That is then shown. That austerity causes the Lord (*ātma*) to manifest (*upalambhanam*) to the devotees through performing dharma, jñāna, vairagya, etc. This is not understood by the common people (*avyakta-gatiḥ*). The Lord does not act for his own benefit. The phrase can also be taken as *vyakta-gatiḥ.*

### Text 10

*taṁ bhagavān nārado varṇāśramavatībhir bhāratībhiḥ prajābhir bhagavat-proktābhyāṁ sāṅkhya-yogābhyāṁ bhagavad-anubhāvopavarṇanaṁ sāvarṇer upadekṣyamāṇaḥ parama-bhakti-bhāvenopasarati idaṁ cābhigṛṇāti.*

**Перевод:**

**The great sage Nārada, while teaching Sāvarṇī Manu descriptions of realization of God by distinguishing spirit from matter and becoming fixed in the Lord as spoken by the Lord, worships Nara-Nārāyaṇa with the greatest** ***bhakti*** **along with the inhabitants of Bhārata-varṣa, who are followers of** ***varṇāśrama.*** **He chants the following** ***mantras.***

**Комментарий:**

Nārada worships the Lord along with sages, not all inhabitants (*prajābhiḥ*), while teaching to Sāvarṇya descriptions of realization of the Lord through becoming fixed in the Lord and by distinguishing the essence (*sāṅkhya-yogābhyām*) as taught by Nara-nārāyaṇa.

### Text 11

*oṁ namo bhagavate upaśama-śīlāyoparatānātmyāya namo 'kiñcana-vittāya ṛṣi-ṛṣabhāya nara-nārāyaṇāya paramahaṁsa-parama-gurave ātmārāmādhipataye namo nama iti.*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord who has controlled senses, who has no attachment to persons not interested in** ***ātmā,*** **who is the treasure for persons possessing nothing, who is the best of sages, the supreme** ***guru*** **of the** ***paramahaṁsas*****, and the leader of the** ***ātmārāmas.***

**Комментарий:**

Showing the Lord’s complete detachment, he indicates that the Lord is also guru.

### Text 12

*gāyati cedam—*  
*kartāsya sargādiṣu yo na badhyate*  
*na hanyate deha-gato 'pi daihikaiḥ*  
*draṣṭur na dṛg yasya guṇair vidūṣyate*  
*tasmai namo 'sakta-vivikta-sākṣiṇe*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada sings: I offer respects to the witness who is unattached and separate from the body, who is not attached to the creation of this universe, who is not overcome by bodily needs though appearing in a body, and whose sight is not polluted by** ***māyā*** **though he sees everything.**

**Комментарий:**

Though he is in the body as the antaryāmī (*deha-gataḥ*) he is not overcome by bodily needs. As the supreme witness (*draṣṭuḥ*), his sight is not polluted by the guṇas.

### Text 13

*idaṁ hi yogeśvara yoga-naipuṇaṁ*  
*hiraṇyagarbho bhagavāñ jagāda yat*  
*yad anta-kāle tvayi nirguṇe mano*  
*bhaktyā dadhītojjhita-duṣkalevaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O my Lord, master of all mystic** ***yoga!*** **Lord Brahmā has explained that expertise in** ***yoga*** **means** **that at the time of death, the person, giving up bodily identification, fixes his mind with devotion in you who are beyond the** ***guṇas.***

**Комментарий:**

He establishes that the Lord is unattached, by showing the jīva’s lack of attachment with knowledge of yoga and its cause. A person, possessing a body of an associate of the Lord, having given up his material body (*ujjita-duskalevaraḥ*), fixes his mind on you with devotion.

### Text 14

*yathaihikāmuṣmika-kāma-lampaṭaḥ*  
*suteṣu dāreṣu dhaneṣu cintayan*  
*śaṅketa vidvān kukalevarātyayād*  
*yas tasya yatnaḥ śrama eva kevalam*

**Перевод:**

**The learned man who worries in this way has wasted his energy in this life. He is like a man attached to enjoyment in this world and the next who worries about his wealth, wife and children and fears the loss of his miserable body,**

**Комментарий:**

General distinctions of matter and spirit which gives rise to knowledge and yoga are explained.

### Text 15

*tan naḥ prabho tvaṁ kukalevarārpitāṁ*  
*tvan-māyayāhaṁ-mamatām adhokṣaja*  
*bhindyāma yenāśu vayaṁ sudurbhidāṁ*  
*vidhehi yogaṁ tvayi naḥ svabhāvam iti*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! May we give up the conception of me and mine concerning this miserable body, so difficult to give up. Please give us concentration on you, which is** ***rati*** **for you. O transcendental Lord!**

**Комментарий:**

He expresses his desire. Giving up conceptions of the body is actually in reference to other people, not Nārada.

### Text 16

*bhārate 'py asmin varṣe saric-chailāḥ santi bahavo malayo maṅgala-prastho mainākas trikūṭa ṛṣabhaḥ kūṭakaḥ kollakaḥ sahyo devagirir ṛṣyamūkaḥ śrī-śailo veṅkaṭo mahendro vāridhāro vindhyaḥ śuktimān ṛkṣagiriḥ pāriyātro droṇaś citrakūṭo govardhano raivatakaḥ kakubho nīlo gokāmukha indrakīlaḥ kāmagirir iti cānye ca śata-sahasraśaḥ śailās teṣāṁ nitamba-prabhavā nadā nadyaś ca santy asaṅkhyātāḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**In the tract of land known as Bhārata-varṣa, just as in Ilāvṛta-varṣa, there are many mountains and rivers. Some of the mountains are Malaya, Maṅgala-prastha, Maināka, Trikūṭa, Ṛṣabha, Kūṭaka, Kollaka, Sahya, Devagiri, Ṛṣyamūka, Śrī-śaila, Veṅkaṭa, Mahendra, Vāridhāra, Vindhya, Śuktimān, Ṛkṣagiri, Pāriyātra, Droṇa, Citrakūṭa, Govardhana, Raivataka, Kakubha, Nīla, Gokāmukha, Indrakīla and Kāmagiri. Besides these, there are many other hills, with many large and small rivers flowing from their slopes.**

**Комментарий:**

Govardhana within Vṛndāvana is famous in the scriptures as the place where Kṛṣṇa performed pastimes. Some versions have Raivata instead of Raivataka. Many mountains are not seen today since they have disappeared.

### Text 19

*asminn eva varṣe puruṣair labdha-janmabhiḥ śukla-lohita-kṛṣṇa-varṇena svārabdhena karmaṇā divya-mānuṣa-nāraka-gatayo bahvya ātmana ānupūrvyeṇa sarvā hy eva sarveṣāṁ vidhīyante yathā-varṇa-vidhānam apavargaś cāpi bhavati.*

**Перевод:**

**In Bhārata-varṣa, many destinations—heavenly, human and hellish—are prescribed for all people, because people take birth according to actions in** ***sattva, rajas*** **and** ***tamas*****. All these destinations are prescribed while offering their dharmas to the Lord. Liberation is then achieved.**

**Комментарий:**

*Yathā-varṇa-vidhānam* means by offering their svadharmas.

### Text 20

*yo 'sau bhagavati sarva-bhūtātmany anātmye 'nirukte 'nilayane paramātmani vāsudeve 'nanya-nimitta-bhakti-yoga-lakṣaṇo nānā-gati-nimittāvidyā-granthi-randhana-dvāreṇa yadā hi mahā-puruṣa-puruṣa-prasaṅgaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**That liberation, whose essential nature is unmotivated** ***bhakti-yoga*** **to the Lord full of qualities, who attracts the minds of all beings, who is not the object of merging, who is not described by material words, who remains beyond destruction of the universe, who is the most excellent** ***ātmā,*** **who is the son of Vasudeva, takes place by destruction of the knot of ignorance which causes various material goals, when there is association with devotees of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Bhakti generally arises when there is association with great devotees of the Lord (*mahāpuruṣa-puruṣa-prasaṅgaḥ*). This is the main cause as in the case of Nārada.

From that association, liberation (*apavargaḥ*-mentioned in previous verse) in the form of bhakti-yoga arises. What to speak of other things arising.

### Text 21

*etad eva hi devā gāyanti—*  
*aho amīṣāṁ kim akāri śobhanaṁ*  
*prasanna eṣāṁ svid uta svayaṁ hariḥ*  
*yair janma labdhaṁ nṛṣu bhāratājire*  
*mukunda-sevaupayikaṁ spṛhā hi naḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***devatās*** **sing: What pious acts did these residents perform, or was the Lord simply pleased with them, by which they attained a birth suitable for service to Mukunda in the area of Bhārata, whereas we can only desire this, but not attain it?**

**Комментарий:**

This is a general statement since it is seen that people are born in Bharata without getting the Lord’s mercy and with bad karma. The next verse also makes this clear. *Svayam* means that since the previous verse indicated that the mercy of devotees was necessary, the Lord is pleased by sādhana aimed at the Lord or by nature dedicated to the Lord.

### Text 23

*kalpāyuṣāṁ sthānajayāt punar-bhavāt*  
*kṣaṇāyuṣāṁ bhārata-bhūjayo varam*  
*kṣaṇena martyena kṛtaṁ manasvinaḥ*  
*sannyasya saṁyānty abhayaṁ padaṁ hareḥ*

**Перевод:**

**A moment’s stay in Bhārata is better than living for a** ***kalpa*** **on Brahmaloka, since on Brahmaloka one must take birth again. Those who fix their minds on the Lord in Bhārata in a body subject to death, offering everything to the Lord, attain Vaikuṇṭha.**

**Комментарий:**

They offer all acts to the Lord (s*annyasya*).

### Text 24

*na yatra vaikuṇṭha-kathā-sudhāpagā*  
*na sādhavo bhāgavatās tadāśrayāḥ*  
*na yatra yajñeśa-makhā mahotsavāḥ*  
*sureśa-loko 'pi na vai sa sevyatām*

**Перевод:**

**Even Brahmaloka should not be accepted as a residence if there are no rivers of sweet topics about the Lord, no devotees who take shelter of those topics, and no great festivals or sacrifices of chanting the name of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Here the idea that only Bhārata has bhakti is refuted. Any planet is praised if it has bhakti. However as Bhārata has profuse bhakti, it is praised in this chapter.

### Text 26

*yaiḥ śraddhayā barhiṣi bhāgaśo havir*  
*niruptam iṣṭaṁ vidhi-mantra-vastutaḥ*  
*ekaḥ pṛthaṅ-nāmabhir āhuto mudā*  
*gṛhṇāti pūrṇaḥ svayam āśiṣāṁ prabhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is one only, but called by different names. Though complete in himself, he is the master of blessings, and thus he happily accepts the pure offering of sacrifice along with materials, methods and** ***mantras*** **which are offered to various** ***devatās&cedil;*** **because of the** ***bhakti.***

**Комментарий:**

Offering to the Lord is shown. The Lord, who is addressed by names of devatās (*pṛthaṅ-nāmabhīh āhutaḥ*) happily accepts the offerings in sacrifice which are offered (*iṣṭam*) by men, directed to devatās (*pṛthak niruptam*), since the names ultimately indicate the Lord.

### Text 27

*satyaṁ diśaty arthitam arthito nṛṇāṁ*  
*naivārthado yat punar arthitā yataḥ*  
*svayaṁ vidhatte bhajatām anicchatām*  
*icchāpidhānaṁ nija-pāda-pallavam*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord certainly gives desired objects to devotees who request them, but he does not give those objects in such a way that the devotee will ask again after finishing his enjoyment. In other words, he gives his lotus feet, which include all desirables, to those worshippers who do not even desire them.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 98:**

Being requested, the Lord gives what humans ask. The Lord never fails in this regard *(satyam*). But the Lord does not just give, for by just giving, material desire will not be extinguished since when the enjoyment is finished without being fully satisfying, the person again will request the desired object (*yataḥ punar arthitā*).

*na jātu kāmaḥ kāmānāṁ upabhogena śāṁyati*  
*haviṣā kṛṣna-vartmāeva bhuya evābhivarddhate*

**Desires are not extinguished by enjoying the objects of desire, just as fire increases by adding ghee. (Manu-saṁhitā)**

The supremely merciful Lord gives these people his lotus feet, just as a mother takes chewed dirt from the mouth of her child and gives him sugar candy. This is because their bhakti is intense, as expressed in the following:

*akāmaḥ sarva-kāmo vā mokṣa-kāma udāra-dhīḥ \|*  
*tīvreṇa bhakti-yogena yajeta puruṣaṁ param \|\|*

**The person desiring destruction of all desires, the person with all desires, and even the person with an intense desire for liberation, if he has good intelligence, will worship the Supreme Lord with pure bhakti. (SB** [**2.3.10**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/3.10)**)**

*yad durlabhaṁ yad aprāptaṁ manaso yan na gocaram \|*  
*tad apy aprārthitaṁ dhyāto dadāti madhusūdanaḥ \|\|*

**Though the worshipper does not ask, the Lord gives him what is most rare, unattainable by the mind and senses. (Garuḍa Purāṇa 2.234.12)**

## Chapter Twenty

### Text 7

*plakṣaḥ sva-samānenekṣu-rasodenāvṛto yathā tathā dvīpo 'pi śālmalo dvi-guṇa-viśālaḥ samānena surodenāvṛtaḥ parivṛṅkte.*

**Перевод:**

**Plakṣadvīpa is surrounded by an ocean of sugarcane juice, equal in breadth to the island itself. Similarly, there is then another island—Sālmalīdvīpa—twice as broad as Plakṣadvīpa (400,000** ***yojanas*****), and surrounded by an equally broad ocean of liquor.**

**Комментарий:**

*Parivṛṅkte* comes from the root *vṛj,* to exclude. Ātmanepada is poetic license. *Parivṛṅkte* means to exist separately.

### Text 20

*tasminn api praiyavrato ghṛtapṛṣṭho nāmādhipatiḥ sve dvīpe varṣāṇi sapta vibhajya teṣu putra-nāmasu sapta rikthādān varṣapān niveśya svayaṁ bhagavān bhagavataḥ parama-kalyāṇa-yaśasa ātma-bhūtasya hareś caraṇāravindam upajagāma.*

**Перевод:**

**The ruler of this island was another son of Mahārāja Priyavrata, named Ghṛtapṛṣṭha. He also divided his own island among his seven sons. After dividing the island into seven parts, named according to the names of his sons, Ghṛtapṛṣṭha Mahārāja completely retired from family life and took shelter at the lotus feet of the Lord, the soul of all souls, who has all auspicious qualities.**

**Комментарий:**

*Svayam* here means he took shelter of the Lord personally. Or *svayam* can be connected with *bhagavataḥ.*

Bhagavān in the verse (referring to the king) means the following:

*utpattiṁ pralayaṁ caiva bhūtānām āgatiṁ gatim*  
*vetti vidyām avidyāṁ ca sa vācyo bhagavān iti*

**A person who knows about the creation and destruction of the universe, the karmic comings and goings of embodied creatures, and the forces of enlightenment and illusion may be called Bhagavān.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa** [**6.5.78**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vp/6.5.78)**)**

### Text 24

*evaṁ purastāt kṣīrodāt parita upaveśitaḥ śākadvīpo dvātriṁśal-lakṣa-yojanāyāmaḥ samānena ca dadhi-maṇḍodena parīto yasmin śāko nāma mahīruhaḥ sva-kṣetra-vyapadeśako yasya ha mahā-surabhi-gandhas taṁ dvīpam anuvāsayati.*

**Перевод:**

**Outside the ocean of milk is another island, Śākadvīpa, which has a width of 3,200,000** ***yojanas.*** **As Krauñcadvīpa is surrounded by its own ocean of milk, Śākadvīpa is surrounded by an ocean of churned yogurt as broad as the island itself. In Śākadvīpa there is a big** ***śāka*** **(teak) tree, from which the island takes its name. Its fragrance scents the entire island.**

**Комментарий:**

The tree spreads fragrance particularly in that portion where it grows, but also spreads its fragrance everywhere.

### Text 28

*antaḥ-praviśya bhūtāni*  
*yo bibharty ātma-ketubhiḥ*  
*antaryāmīśvaraḥ sākṣāt*  
*pātu no yad-vaśe sphuṭam*

**Перевод:**

**May the** ***antaryāmī*****, the Supreme Lord who enters within and directly maintains the living beings by the functions of the** ***prāṇas,*** **under whose control the universe is created, please protect us.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord enters within. This was stated by Nārada in Bhārata-varṣa. May he who controls the manifested universe (*sphutam*) protect us.

### Text 32

*tad-varṣa-puruṣā bhagavantaṁ brahma-rūpiṇaṁ sakarmakeṇa karmaṇārādhayantīdaṁ codāharanti.*

*Sakarmakeṇa* means “with result of their actions.” The results are going to Brahmaloka or attaining sālokya.

### Text 33

*yat tat karmamayaṁ liṅgaṁ*

*ekāntam advayaṁ śāntaṁ*  
*tasmai bhagavate nama iti*

**They say: I offer respects the Supreme Lord whom people, with the idea of servant and object of service, worship as Brahmā, having a deity form, who is attained by** ***karma*****, who is fixed in devotion to the Lord, who is non-different from the Lord, who is peaceful, and by whom the Vedas are known.**

*Liṅgama* means having a deity form.

### Text 40

*teṣāṁ sva-vibhūtīnāṁ loka-pālānāṁ ca vividha-vīryopabṛṁhaṇāya bhagavān parama-mahā-puruṣo mahā-vibhūti-patir antaryāmy ātmano viśuddha-sattvaṁ dharma-jñāna-vairāgyaiśvaryādy-aṣṭa-mahā-siddhy-upalakṣaṇaṁ viṣvaksenādibhiḥ sva-pārṣada-pravaraiḥ parivārito nija-varāyudhopaśobhitair nija-bhuja-daṇḍaiḥ sandhārayamāṇas tasmin giri-vare samantāt sakala-loka-svastaya āste.*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord, the greatest person, the lord of great powers, the** ***antāryāmī,*** **surrounded by his associates such as Viṣvaksena, supporting** ***viśuddha-sattva*** **characterized by** ***dharma, jñāna, vairāgya, aiśvarya*** **and the eight** ***siddhis,*** **possessing four arms decorated with the best weapons, remains on that best of mountains for the welfare of all people and for increasing the various powers of the elephants, who are protectors of the planets and his own expansions.**

**Комментарий:**

He supports viśuddha-sattva endowed with the eight siddhis, and dharma, jñāna, vairāgya etc.

### Text 41

*ākalpam evaṁ gata eṣa bhagavān ātma-yogamāyayā viracita-vividha-loka-yātrā-gopīyāyety arthaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord remains there until the end of the** ***kalpa*** **in order to protect the worldly affairs created by his** ***yoga-māyā.***

**Комментарий:**

The Lord remains there (*gataḥ*) till pralaya (*ākalpam*). *Iti* indicates the Lord’s intentions previously mentioned.

### Text 42

*yo 'ntar-vistāra etena hy aloka-parimāṇaṁ ca vyākhyātaṁ yad bahir lokālokācalāt; tataḥ parastād yogeśvara-gatiṁ viśuddhām udāharanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Lokāloka Mountain extends for the same distance as the total diameter of the mountain range (82,200,000** ***yojanas*****) and distance outside the mountain range is the same as the distance from Meru to the far edge of Lokāloka Mountain. They say that beyond that is the pure destination of those who aspire for liberation from the material world.**

**Комментарий:**

Lokāloka is named because it divides the visible from the invisible half of the universe. It starts just after the golden land as described in Śiva-tantra: *lokālokaṁ tataḥ param*: Lokāloka is just beyond the golden land. Otherwise the diameter inside the universe would exceed 500,000,000 yojanas.

### Text 44

*mṛte 'ṇḍa eṣa etasmin yad abhūt tato mārtaṇḍa iti vyapadeśaḥ; hiraṇyagarbha iti yad dhiraṇyāṇḍa-samudbhavaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**The sun is known as Mārtaṇḍa because he entered the unconscious universe. He is called Hiraṇyagarbha because he is the very existence of the universe called the Hiraṇyāṇḍa.**

**Комментарий:**

*Eṣaḥ* means the sun. The sun is considered non-different from antaryāmī in this verse.

## Chapter Twenty-one

### Text 1

### Text 7

*evaṁ nava koṭaya eka-pañcāśal-lakṣāṇi yojanānāṁ mānasottara-giri-parivartanasyopadiśanti tasminn aindrīṁ purīṁ pūrvasmān meror devadhānīṁ nāma dakṣiṇato yāmyāṁ saṁyamanīṁ nāma paścād vāruṇīṁ nimlocanīṁ nāma uttarataḥ saumyāṁ vibhāvarīṁ nāma tāsūdaya-madhyāhnāstamaya-niśīthānīti bhūtānāṁ pravṛtti-nivṛtti-nimittāni samaya-viśeṣeṇa meroś catur-diśam.*

**Перевод:**

**The wise teach about the city called Devadhānī, east of Mount Sumeru, possessed by King Indra; a place known as Saṁyamanī, possessed by Yamarāja, to the south of Meru; a place known as Nimlocanī, possessed by Varuṇa, to the west of Meru; and a place named Vibhāvarī, possessed by the moon-go to the north of Meru—which are on the path of the suns revolution on Mānasottara Mountain whose circumference is 95,100,000** ***yojanas.*** **They teach about the sunrise, noon and sunset and midnight in these cities for the purpose of engaging and disengaging people in work according to particular times. They teach about the four directions of Meru.**

**Комментарий:**

95.100,100 yojanas circumference of the path of the sun, instead of 94,500,000 is derived by multiplying 31,500,000 by three (pi), since 200 yojanas are added to the circumference of the path on Mānasottara Mountain.

Śrīdhara Svāmī quotes from Vayu Puruṣa about the listing the vīthis or groups of constellations. but the order is not according to the actual position of the constellations since they are seen to be located in other positions and the planets move through them in that order, not according to the list in Vāyu Purāṇa. In ascending and descending periods, Revatī and Aśvinī in adjacent vīthis and Citrā and Aśvinī in distance vīthis should be in the same vīthi. The relationship between these constellations (when tracing the path of the sun) will at that time be different from their well-known order. The list in the Purāṇa refers to the presiding deities of the constellations (rather than the constellations).

The commentary describes travelling on the inner path and going at a slower motion. Because of the smaller distance within the maṇḍala, the sun is visible for longer periods during the day. The slow motion is like the ox going around a pillar at close range. The rotation passes above the varṣa. The sun is thus seen to be overhead at noon during uttarāyaṇa. Because of the great distance to cover on the outer circle, the sun is seen for less time during its rotation. The movement is fast, like the oxen moving at a distance from the central pillar.

*tatratyānāṁ divasa-madhyaṅgata eva sadādityas tapati savyenācalaṁ dakṣiṇena karoti; yatrodeti tasya ha samāna-sūtra-nipāte nimlocati yatra kvacana syandenābhitapati tasya haiṣa samāna-sūtra-nipāte prasvāpayati tatra gataṁ na paśyanti ye taṁ samanupaśyeran.*

**For the people on Meru, the sun give heat at all times, being situated at midday. The sun keeps Meru to the left and to the right. When it rises in one city, it sets at the city diametrically opposite. When it shines with perspiration at noon, it is midnight at the city diametrically opposite. Those who see the sun set and rise do not see where it has gone in the interim. (SB** [**5.21.8-9**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/21.8)**).**

“When it rises” refers to its path on the surface below.

### Text 12

*evaṁ muhūrtena catus-triṁśal-lakṣa-yojanāny aṣṭa-śatādhikāni sauro rathas trayīmayo 'sau catasṛṣu parivartate purīṣu.*

**Перевод:**

**Thus the chariot of the sun-god, meant for worship, travels through the four residences mentioned above at a speed of 3,400,800** ***yojanas*** **in a** ***muhūrta*****.**

**Комментарий:**

This speed is measured from Mānasottara and nowhere else. Considering the movements of the sun and other planets through various places according to astronomical texts, the circle of the sun rotating seems less than it should be.

One cannot reject a small tree at a great distance which is diametrically opposite, as one moves in various places. From far off the distance diametrically opposite seems small. The lesser calculation of the sun’s movement is thus not contradictory. This is not just an optical illusion. The shadow also follows its slower motion. ???

### Text 13

*yasyaikaṁ cakraṁ dvādaśāraṁ ṣaṇ-nemi tri-ṇābhi saṁvatsarātmakaṁ samāmananti tasyākṣo meror mūrdhani kṛto mānasottare kṛtetara-bhāgo yatra protaṁ ravi-ratha-cakraṁ taila-yantra-cakravad bhraman mānasottara-girau paribhramati.*

**Перевод:**

**The chariot of the sun-god has only one wheel, which is known as the year. The wheel has twelve spokes, six rims, and three hubs. One side of the axle carrying the wheel rests upon the summit of Mount Sumeru, and the other rests upon Mānasottara Mountain. Affixed to the outer end of the axle, the wheel continuously rotates on Mānasottara Mountain like the wheel of an oil-pressing machine.**

**Комментарий:**

The wheel cannot go in two different directions simultaneously. By pulling in the opposite direction the chariot would stop. Though the horses are strong, the chariot would go off course. The chariot goes among the constellations, and does not stray elsewhere. One also sees the moon and other planets also moving in through different locations daily. Thus the movements of the sun and other planets is like an ant moving in one direction on a disk revolving in the opposite direction. The axel rests on Mānasottara, bound by the wind, and Meru. On Mānasottara it is ninety thousand and on Meru it is sixteen thousand (yojanas in height).

### Text 14

*tasminn akṣe kṛtamūlo dvitīyo 'kṣas turyamānena sammitas taila-yantrākṣavad dhruve kṛtopari-bhāgaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**As in an oil-pressing machine, the first axle is attached to a second axle, which is one fourth as long. The upper end of this second axle is attached to Dhruvaloka.**

**Комментарий:**

The second axel measuring a fourth of its length is attached to axel of the sun’s chariot

### Text 15

*ratha-nīḍas tu ṣaṭ-triṁśal-lakṣa-yojanāyatas tat-turīya-bhāga-viśālas tāvān ravi-ratha-yugo yatra hayāś chando-nāmānaḥ saptāruṇa-yojitā vahanti devam ādityam.*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The carriage of the sun-god's chariot is estimated to be 3,600,000** ***yojanas*** **long and one-fourth as wide (900,000** ***yojanas*****). The chariot's horses, which are named after Gāyatrī and other Vedic meters, are harnessed by Aruṇadeva to a yoke that is also 900,000** ***yojanas*** **wide. This chariot continuously carries the sun-god.**

**Комментарий:**

The sitting place on the chariot *(nīḍaḥ*) refers the area made of wind following the chariot, not the seat on the chariot where the sun god sits, since the chariot is actually small. Viṣṇu Purāṇa says *yojanānāṁ sahasrāṇi bhāskarasya ratho nava*: the sun’s chariot is nine thousand yojanas in size. The circle is said to be ten thousand yojanas.

### Text 16

*purastāt savitur aruṇaḥ paścāc ca niyuktaḥ sautye karmaṇi kilāste.*

**Перевод:**

**Although Aruṇa sits in front of the sun-god and is engaged in driving the chariot and controlling the horses, he looks backward toward the sun-god.**

**Комментарий:**

He is in front but also looks back. This means he expands his form in two.

### Text 18

*tathānye ca ṛṣayo gandharvāpsaraso nāgā grāmaṇyo yātudhānā devā ity ekaikaśo gaṇāḥ sapta caturdaśa māsi māsi bhagavantaṁ sūryam ātmānaṁ nānā-nāmānaṁ pṛthaṅ-nānā-nāmānaḥ pṛthak-karmabhir dvandvaśa upāsate.*

**Перевод:**

**Similarly, other sages, Gandharvas, Apsarās, Nāgas, Yakṣas, Rākṣasas and** ***devatās,*** **in fourteen groups in some months and pairing up to make seven groups in other months, assume different names every month and continuously perform different ritualistic ceremonies to worship the Supreme Lord as Sūrya, who has many names.**

**Комментарий:**

There are fourteen sages, Gandharvas etc. Becoming seven groups by combining two of each, they worship during the different months.

### Text 19

*lakṣottaraṁ sārdha-nava-koṭi-yojana-parimaṇḍalaṁ bhū-valayasya kṣaṇena sagavyūty-uttaraṁ dvi-sahasra-yojanāni sa bhuṅkte.*

**Перевод:**

**In his orbit through a circumference of 95,100,000** ***yojanas*** **on the circle of the earth, the sun travels 2,000** ***yojanas*** **and two** ***krośas*** **in a moment.**

**Комментарий:**

This ends the section in prose. *Saḥ* refers to the sun.

## Chapter Twenty-two

### Text 1

*rājovāca*  
*yad etad bhagavata ādityasya meruṁ dhruvaṁ ca pradakṣiṇena parikrāmato rāśīnām abhimukhaṁ pracalitaṁ cāpradakṣiṇaṁ bhagavatopavarṇitam amuṣya vayaṁ katham anumimīmahīti.*

**Перевод:**

**King Parīkṣit said: My dear lord, you have already affirmed the truth that the supremely powerful sun-god travels around Dhruvaloka with both Dhruvaloka and Mount Sumeru on his right. Yet at the same time the sun-god faces the signs of the zodiac and keeps Sumeru and Dhruvaloka on his left. How can we accept that the sun-god proceeds with Sumeru and Dhruvaloka on both his left and right simultaneously?**

**Комментарий:**

*Abhimukham* means circling with Meru on his right side.

### Text 3

*sa eṣa bhagavān ādi-puruṣa eva sākṣān nārāyaṇo lokānāṁ svastaya ātmānaṁ trayīmayaṁ karma-viśuddhi-nimittaṁ kavibhir api ca vedena vijijñāsyamāno dvādaśadhā vibhajya ṣaṭsu vasantādiṣv ṛtuṣu yathopa-joṣam ṛtu-guṇān vidadhāti.*

**Перевод:**

**The original cause of the cosmic manifestation, the Supreme Lord, Nārāyaṇa, made of the Vedas worshipped by the sages by recitation of the Vedas, divided himself into twelve forms and six seasons for purifying the activities of the people, and he arranged the qualities of the seasons for enjoyment according to their** ***karma.***

**Комментарий:**

This Nārāyaṇa is Viṣṇu in the form of the sun.

### Text 5

*atha sa eṣa ātmā lokānāṁ dyāv-āpṛthivyor antareṇa nabho-valayasya kālacakra-gato dvādaśa māsān bhuṅkte rāśi-saṁjñān saṁvatsarāvayavān māsaḥ pakṣa-dvayaṁ divā naktaṁ ceti sapādarkṣa-dvayam upadiśanti yāvatā ṣaṣṭham aṁśaṁ bhuñjīta sa vai ṛtur ity upadiśyate saṁvatsarāvayavaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**The sun, soul of worlds, situated in the zodiac belt in the** ***antarikṣa*** **between heaven and earth, enjoys twelve months, indicated by the names of zodiac signs, which are limbs of the year. The wise say that one month is two fortnights by lunar calculation, which is a day and night for the Pitṛs, or the time it takes the sun to pass through two and a quarter constellations. Passing two months it enjoys a season. In this way one limb of the year is described.**

**Комментарий:**

They instruct that two fortnights is one month by the calculation of the lunar calendar. It is one day and night for the Pitṛs. The sun passes through two and a quarter constellations during this time.

### Text 8

*evaṁ candramā arka-gabhastibhya upariṣṭāl lakṣa-yojanata upalabhyamāno 'rkasya saṁvatsara-bhuktiṁ pakṣābhyāṁ māsa-bhuktiṁ sapādarkṣābhyāṁ dinenaiva pakṣa-bhuktim agracārī drutatara-gamano bhuṅkte.*

**Перевод:**

**The moon, situated 100,000** ***yojanas*** **above the rays of the sun, moving with quicker speed, covers the sun’s year in two fortnights, its month in two and half days and its fortnight in one day.**

**Комментарий:**

The moon travels at quicker speed because it becomes cool by moving fast.

## Chapter Twenty-three

### Text 3

*yathā meḍhīstambha ākramaṇa-paśavaḥ saṁyojitās tribhis tribhiḥ savanair yathā-sthānaṁ maṇḍalāni caranty evaṁ bhagaṇā grahādaya etasminn antar-bahir-yogena kāla-cakra āyojitā dhruvam evāvalambya vāyunodīryamāṇā ākalpāntaṁ paricaṅ kramanti nabhasi yathā meghāḥ śyenādayo vāyu-vaśāḥ karma-sārathayaḥ parivartante evaṁ jyotirgaṇāḥ prakṛti-puruṣa-saṁyogānugṛhītāḥ karma-nirmita-gatayo bhuvi na patanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Just as bulls yoked together and tied to a central post to thresh rice tread around that pivot without deviating from their proper positions—one bull being closest to the post, another in the middle, and a third on the outside--so all the planets and luminaries, fixed in the zodiac, revolve around the polestar in their respective orbits, some higher and some lower, till the end of the** ***kalpa,*** **propelled by wind. Just as clouds or eagles, controlled by the wind, pushed by** ***karma*****, move about in the sky, so the planets, supported by matter and the Lord, and moving by their past** ***karmas*****, do not fall to the ground.**

**Комментарий:**

As bulls are made to walk around a post at different positions during three times of the day, morning, noon and afternoon (*tribhiḥ savanaiḥ*) by the farmer, so the planets move around the pole star. Controlled by māyā situated in the Lord (*prakṛti-puruṣa-samñogānugṛhītaḥ*), the planets do not fall.

### Text 4

*kecanaitaj jyotir-anīkaṁ śiśumāra-saṁsthānena bhagavato vāsudevasya yoga-dhāraṇāyām anuvarṇayanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Some describe this zodiac belt to be the form of a dolphin, used for worshipping Vāsudeva in meditation.**

**Комментарий:**

The zodiac conceived as a dolphin, like the universal form, is a means of becoming absorbed in worship. This becomes clear with statements in verse 7 and 8.

### Text 5

*yasya pucchāgre 'vākśirasaḥ kuṇḍalī-bhūta-dehasya dhruva upakalpitas tasya lāṅgūle prajāpatir agnir indro dharma iti puccha-mūle dhātā vidhātā ca kaṭyāṁ saptarṣayaḥ; tasya dakṣiṇāvarta-kuṇḍalī-bhūta-śarīrasya yāny udagayanāni dakṣiṇa-pārśve tu nakṣatrāṇy upakalpayanti dakṣiṇāyanāni tu savye; yathā śiśumārasya kuṇḍalā-bhoga-sanniveśasya pārśvayor ubhayor apy avayavāḥ samasaṅkhyā bhavanti; pṛṣṭhe tv ajavīthī ākāśa-gaṅgā codarataḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**This form of the dolphin has its head downward and its body coiled. On the end of its tail is the planet of Dhruva, on the body of its tail are the planets of the** ***devatās*** **Prajāpati, Agni, Indra and Dharma, and at the base of its tail are the planets of the** ***devatās*** **Dhātā and Vidhātā. On the hips are the seven saintly sages like Vasiṣṭha and Aṅgirā. The coiled body of the dolphin turns toward the right side, on which the fourteen constellations from Abhijit to Punarvasu are located. On its left side are the fourteen stars from Puṣyā to Uttarāṣāḍhā. Thus its body is balanced because its sides are occupied by an equal number of stars on each side. On the back of the dolphin is the group of stars known as Ajavīthī, and on its abdomen is the Gaṅgā that flows in the sky (the Milky Way).**

**Комментарий:**

The dolphin coils around (*kuṇḍalībhūta*). *Udagayanānī* means “they should be conceived.” The right side is described, not the back or front side. The listing of constellations appears to go in reverse because the form twists sideways, up and down. Nārāyaṇa and various planets are situated on its heart and other places. Ajavīthī is the first section on the southern path. It consists of Mūla, Pūrvāṣāḍha and Uttarāṣāḍha. When the constellations are said to be situated on the ears etc. these are to be considered to be various parts of the torso. The Milky Way is also on the torso.

### Text 7

*uttarā-hanāv agastir adharā-hanau yamo mukheṣu cāṅgārakaḥ śanaiścara upasthe bṛhaspatiḥ kakudi vakṣasy ādityo hṛdaye nārāyaṇo manasi candro nābhyām uśanā stanayor aśvinau budhaḥ prāṇāpānayo rāhur gale ketavaḥ sarvāṅgeṣu romasu sarve tārā-gaṇāḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**On the upper chin of the dolphin is Agasti; on its lower chin, Yamarāja; on its mouth, Mars; on its genitals, Saturn; on the back of its neck, Jupiter; on its chest, the sun; and within the core of its heart, Nārāyaṇa. Within its mind is the moon; on its navel, Venus; and on its breasts, the Aśvinī-kumāras. Its life air composed of** ***prāṇa*** **and** ***āpāna*** **is Mercury. On its neck is Rāhu. All over its body are comets, and in its pores are the numerous stars.**

**Комментарий:**

Though the planets like Mars move through the different zodiac signs, they are said to be situated on various limbs, while thinking that the various limbs move about.

## Chapter Twenty-four

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*adhastāt savitur yojanāyute svarbhānur nakṣatravac caratīty eke yo 'sāv amaratvaṁ grahatvaṁ cālabhata bhagavad-anukampayā svayam asurāpasadaḥ saiṁhikeyo hy atad-arhas tasya tāta janma karmāṇi copariṣṭād vakṣyāmaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: The speakers of the Purāṇas say that 10,000** ***yojanas*** **below the sun is the planet known as Rāhu, which moves like one of the constellations. The presiding deity of that planet, who is the son of Siṁhikā, the most abominable of all** ***asuras,*** **has attained the position of a** ***devatā*** **by the mercy of the Lord, though he is not qualified to be a** ***devatā*****. O King! Later I shall speak about him.**

**Комментарий:**

Actually Rāhu does not move like the constellations, since it is beyond the wind which moves them. It moves on its own.

### Text 2

*yad adas taraṇer maṇḍalaṁ pratapatas tad vistarato yojanāyutam ācakṣate dvādaśa-sahasraṁ somasya trayodaśa-sahasraṁ rāhor yaḥ parvaṇi tad-vyavadhāna-kṛd vairānubandhaḥ sūryā-candramasāv abhidhāvati.*

**Перевод:**

**They say that the sun globe measures 10,000** ***yojanas*** **in diameter and the moon measure** ***20,000*** **yojanas in diameter, whereas Rāhu is 30,000** ***yojanas*** **in diameter. Obstructing the sun and moon previously, he has become their enemy, and attacks them on the full and dark moon days.**

**Комментарий:**

The sun glove (*taraṇer maṇḍalam*) is the circle of light situated within seat of the chariot.

### Text 8

*eteṣu hi bila-svargeṣu svargād apy adhika-kāma-bhogaiśvaryānanda-bhūti-vibhūtibhiḥ susamṛddha-bhavanodyānākrīḍa-vihāreṣu daitya-dānava-kādraveyā nitya-pramuditānurakta-kalatrāpatya-bandhu-suhṛd-anucarā gṛha-pataya īśvarād apy apratihata-kāmā māyā-vinodā nivasanti.*

**Перевод:**

**The Daityas, Dānavas and Nāgas, who are householders always enamored by their wives, children, friends and society in beautiful houses, gardens and play grounds, and having greater wealth, power, bliss and sense enjoyment than the higher planets, live there in illusory enjoyment, with desires more unimpeded than in Svarga.**

**Комментарий:**

*Īśvarāt means* “with capability.”

### Text 19

*no evaitat sākṣātkāro bhūmi-dānasya yat tad bhagavaty aśeṣa-jīva-nikāyānāṁ jīva-bhūtātma-bhūte paramātmani vāsudeve tīrthatame pātra upapanne parayā śraddhayā paramādara-samāhita-manasā sampratipāditasya sākṣād apavarga-dvārasya yad bila-nilayaiśvaryam.*

**Перевод:**

**Mere attainment of the enjoyment on Sutala is not the real result of Bali’s giving land to Vāsudeva, the life of unlimited** ***jīvas*****, the supreme source of Paramātmā, the most suitable and most pure. This act was executed with great faith and devotion and is the door to the highest liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

Mere attainment of the enjoyment on Sutala is not the only result (*no evaitad*) of Bali’s giving land to Vāsudeva.

### Text 21

*tad bhaktānām ātmavatāṁ sarveṣām ātmany ātmada ātmatayaiva.*

**Перевод:**

**Enjoying Svarga was not the result of giving land to the Supreme Lord, who gives himself to his devotees and gives realization of Paramātmā to the** ***ātmārāmas.***

**Комментарий:**

Bali’s giving was not the cause of any result. The Lord appears as Paramātmā to all jñānīs, but to the devotees he gives himself, and appears before them, being under their control. His nature is to be the supreme helper to all people. Thus he cannot give insignificant results.

### Text 22

*na vai bhagavān nūnam amuṣyānujagrāha yad uta punar ātmānusmṛti-moṣaṇaṁ māyāmaya-bhogaiśvaryam evātanuteti.*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord did not show mercy to Indra, because his bestowal of enjoyment and wealth, forms of** ***māyā,*** **destroy remembrance of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

He destroys the idea that giving material objects is mercy. Since his giving enjoyment and power full of māyā destroys remembrance of the Lord, he did not show such mercy to Bali. Rather he gave closeness to the Lord as a benediction. The reason is that such enjoyment and power destroys remembrance of the Lord. Or he destroys the idea of giving mercy to Indra. He did not show mercy to Indra, because giving material benedictions destroyed his remembrance of the Lord.

### Text 23

*yat tad bhagavatānadhigatānyopāyena yācñā-cchalenāpahṛta-sva-śarīrāvaśeṣita-loka-trayo varuṇa-pāśaiś ca sampratimukto giri-daryāṁ cāpaviddha iti hovāca.*

**Перевод:**

**Because the Lord, finding no other means, used the trick of begging to take away the three worlds from Bali, leaving only his body as his possession, bound him with Varuṇa’s ropes, and locked him in a mountain cave, Bali spoke as follows.**

**Комментарий:**

Bali’s words in five verses in prose act as proof. *Yat tat* means “it is well known.”

*Iti* indicates “Bali said this.”

### Text 24

*nūnaṁ batāyaṁ bhagavān artheṣu na niṣṇāto yo 'sāv indro yasya sacivo mantrāya vṛta ekāntato bṛhaspatis tam atihāya svayam upendreṇātmānam ayācatātmanaś cāśiṣo no eva tad-dāsyam ati-gambhīra-vayasaḥ kālasya manvantara-parivṛttaṁ kiyal loka-trayam idam.*

**Перевод:**

**Indeed, Indra, who employed Bṛhaspati as his exclusive assistant and minister, is not experienced in affairs. Neglecting the Lord, he instead asked me for the three worlds, material happiness, using the Lord, and not service to the Lord. What value are the three worlds, which change every Manvantara by the great speed of time?**

**Комментарий:**

Indra neglected Upendra (*tam*), not desiring the highest goal. He asked me, the lowest person, through the Lord, for the three worlds.

### Text 25

*yasyānudāsyam evāsmat-pitāmahaḥ kila vavre na tu sva-pitryaṁ yad utākutobhayaṁ padaṁ dīyamānaṁ bhagavataḥ param iti bhagavatoparate khalu sva-pitari.*

**Перевод:**

**Our grandfather accepted only service to the Lord, and not the property of his father, which the Lord offered him when he killed his father. He did not even accept the spiritual world, the place of no fear.**

**Комментарий:**

Our grandfather accepted to be the servant of the servant (*anudāsyam*).

*tasmād amūs tanu-bhṛtām aham āśiṣo 'jña*  
*āyuḥ śriyaṁ vibhavam aindriyam āviriñcyāt*  
*necchāmi te vilulitān uruvikrameṇa*  
*kālātmanopanaya māṁ nija-bhṛtya-pārśvam*

**I do not want these objects of enjoyments, life span, wealth, power, or sense enjoyment of living beings up to that of Brahmā, which are all destroyed by your power as time. Take me to the side of your servant. (SB** [**7.9.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/9.24)**)**

*Sva-pitryam* means ruling the three worlds. He did not even accept liberation (*yad utākutobhayaṁ padam*). Why? He thought it to be something other than the Lord (*bhagavataḥ param*), since it was only a portion of the Lord. This happened when the Lord killed his father.

### Text 26

*tasya mahānubhāvasyānupatham amṛjita-kaṣāyaḥ ko vāsmad-vidhaḥ parihīṇa-bhagavad-anugraha upajigamiṣatīti.*

**Перевод:**

**What materially contaminated person like me, devoid of the Lord’s mercy, desires to follow a path similar to that of the great devotee Prahlāda?**

**Комментарий:**

What person who does not appreciate the Lord’s mercy at all (*parihīṇa-bhagavad-anugrahaḥ*) desires to follow the path Prahlāda? The mercy took place after he was bound up and prayed.

### Text 28

*tato 'dhastāt talātale mayo nāma dānavendras tri-purādhipatir bhagavatā purāriṇā tri-lokī-śaṁ cikīrṣuṇā nirdagdha-sva-pura-trayas tat-prasādāl labdha-pado māyāvinām ācāryo mahādevena parirakṣito vigata-sudarśana-bhayo mahīyate.*

**Перевод:**

**Below Sutala, in Talātala resides Maya, king of the Dānavas, lord of three cities. His three cities were burned by Śiva, who desired the good of the three worlds. By Śiva’s grace Maya, master of the magicians, regained his position. Protected by Śiva, without fear of Sudarśana, he is worshipped.**

**Комментарий:**

Maya or Māyāvi remains in Talātala with material happiness by the shadow of mercy of Śiva. He did not attain spiritual happiness like Bali. That is shown here. Śiva is a great devotee of the Lord. By his request, Śiva acts in this way.

### Text 31

*tato 'dhastāt pātāle nāga-loka-patayo vāsuki-pramukhāḥ śaṅkha-kulika-mahāśaṅkha-śveta-dhanañjaya-dhṛtarāṣṭra-śaṅkhacūḍa-kambalāśvatara-devadattādayo mahā-bhogino mahāmarṣā nivasanti yeṣām u ha vai pañca-sapta-daśa-śata-sahasra-śīrṣāṇāṁ phaṇāsu viracitā mahā-maṇayo rociṣṇavaḥ pātāla-vivara-timira-nikaraṁ sva-rociṣā vidhamanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Below Rasātala, in Pātāla, reside the leaders of Nāga-loka, such as Śaṅkha, Kulika, Mahāśaṅkha, Śveta, Dhanañjaya, Dhṛtarāṣṭra, Śaṅkhacūḍa, Kambala, Aśvatara and Devadatta, headed by Vāsuki. They have many hoods and are very envious. The shining jewels fixed on five, seven, ten, a hundred or a thousand hoods, light up the darkness of the caves of Pātāla with their effulgence.**

**Комментарий:**

*Śīrṣāṇām* comes from *śīrṣan* found in Vedic language, according to *śīrṣaṁś chandasi* (Pāṇini 6.1.60).

## Chapter Twenty-five

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*tasya mūla-deśe triṁśad-yojana-sahasrāntara āste yā vai kalā bhagavatas tāmasī samākhyātānanta iti sātvatīyā draṣṭṛ-dṛśyayoḥ saṅkarṣaṇam aham ity abhimāna-lakṣaṇaṁ yaṁ saṅkarṣaṇam ity ācakṣate.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: 30,000** ***yojanas*** **the planet Pātāla lives Ananta, an expansion of the Supreme Lord, called** ***tamasī*****. The devotees call him Saṅkarṣaṇa because he draws together (*****saṅkaṛṣaṇa*****) the enjoyer and the object to be enjoyed, characterized by the conception of “I.”**

**Комментарий:**

He is called Tāmasī because he is the antaryāmī of Śiva, the deity of tamo-guṇa. He is not in tamoguṇa, since this would be a contradiction to SB [5.25.10](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/25.10).

### Text 3

*yasya ha vā idaṁ kālenopasañjihīrṣato 'marṣa-viracita-rucira-bhramad-bhruvor antareṇa sāṅkarṣaṇo nāma rudra ekādaśa-vyūhas try-akṣas tri-śikhaṁ śūlam uttambhayann udatiṣṭhat.*

**Перевод:**

**When Ananta desires to destroy the entire creation, from between his two moving eyebrows appears three-eyed Rudra, composed of anger, with eleven expansions, carrying a raised trident.**

**Комментарий:**

He displays anger because most of the living entities are opposed to the Lord.

### Text 10

*mūrtiṁ naḥ puru-kṛpayā babhāra sattvaṁ*  
*saṁśuddhaṁ sad-asad idaṁ vibhāti tatra*  
*yal-līlāṁ mṛga-patir ādade 'navadyām*  
*ādātuṁ svajana-manāṁsy udāra-vīryaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**By his great mercy the Lord has shown his form of pure** ***sattva*****, within which the universe of cause and effect exists and by which, in the form of Varāha, performing numerous heroic deeds, he enacted spotless pastimes to attract the minds of his devotees.**

**Комментарий:**

*Mṛgapati* refers to Varāhā.

*dadarśa tatrābhijitaṁ dharā-dharaṁ*  
*pronnīyamānāvanim agra-daṁṣṭrayā*  
*muṣṇantam akṣṇā sva-ruco ’ruṇa-śriyā*  
*jahāsa cāho vana-gocaro mṛgaḥ*

**He saw the Lord, holder of the earth, lifting high the earth on the tips of his tusks, diminishing Hiraṇyākṣa’s splendor with his reddish eyes. Hiraṇyākṣa laughed, “O! You are a beast that lives in the water!” (SB** [**3.18.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/18.2)**)**

Accepting the form of an animal to hold up the earth is a glorification of the Lord.

### Text 12

*mūrdhany arpitam aṇuvat sahasra-mūrdhno*  
*bhū-golaṁ sagiri-sarit-samudra-sattvam*  
*ānantyād animita-vikramasya bhūmnaḥ*  
*ko vīryāṇy adhi gaṇayet sahasra-jihvaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Since the Lord is unlimited, what person, even having a thousand tongues, can count the powers of the great Lord of immeasurable powers, Ananta with a thousand heads, who has placed on just one of his heads the small particle known as the universe with its mountains, rivers oceans and living beings?**

**Комментарий:**

Though the form of the Lord is only of medium size, because of his powers, Bhū-maṇḍala becomes very small.

## Chapter Twenty-six

### Text 2

*ṛṣir uvāca*  
*tri-guṇatvāt kartuḥ śraddhayā karma-gatayaḥ pṛthag-vidhāḥ sarvā eva sarvasya tāratamyena bhavanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: Because of the faith of the doer arising from the three** ***guṇas,*** **different varieties of destinations for action appear. All these destinations appear for all people in proportion to the** ***guṇas.***

**Комментарий:**

“One will get various results in various planets because the Lord is not equal to all beings.” The Lord is equal. That is explained.

### Text 3

*athedānīṁ pratiṣiddha-lakṣaṇasyādharmasya tathaiva kartuḥ śraddhāyā vaisādṛśyāt karma-phalaṁ visadṛśaṁ bhavati yā hy anādy-avidyayā kṛta-kāmānāṁ tat-pariṇāma-lakṣaṇāḥ sṛtayaḥ sahasraśaḥ pravṛttās tāsāṁ prācuryeṇānuvarṇayiṣyāmaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**For the performer of forbidden acts of** ***adharma*****, as with the performer of** ***dharma*****, there are varieties of results for actions according to differences in faith of the performer. The destinations for performing acts out of desire caused by beginningless ignorance are merely transformations of those desires, with thousands of varieties. Among these destinations I shall describe in the detail the various hells.**

**Комментарий:**

The performer of forbidden acts of adharma obtains various results for the actions of adharma, because of various types of faith, as previously described. Performing forbidden acts out of inattention has a small amount of ignorance. If performed out of illusion, there is medium amount of ignorance. If performed out of atheism, there a full amount of ignorance. Because of the ignorance there are transformations in the form of thousands of prescribed destinations. I will describe them in detail. What is the cause? It is beginningless ignorance.

### Text 5

*ṛṣir uvāca*  
*antarāla eva tri-jagatyās tu diśi dakṣiṇasyām adhastād bhūmer upariṣṭāc ca jalād yasyām agniṣvāttādayaḥ pitṛ-gaṇā diśi svānāṁ gotrāṇāṁ parameṇa samādhinā satyā evāśiṣa āśāsānā nivasanti.*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: All the hellish planets are situated within an intermediate space within the three worlds, on the southern side of the universe, beneath the seven lower planets, and slightly above the Garbhodaka Ocean. In that direction, the Pitṛs headed by the Agiṣvāttāsdwell, desiring virtuous blessings for their family members by great concentration on the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The Pitṛs desire peace for their family members since they identify with them.

### Text 6

*yatra ha vāva bhagavān pitṛ-rājo vaivasvataḥ sva-viṣayaṁ prāpiteṣu sva-puruṣair jantuṣu sampareteṣu yathā-karmāvadyaṁ doṣam evānullaṅghita-bhagavac-chāsanaḥ sagaṇo damaṁ dhārayati.*

**Перевод:**

**There, the king of the Pitṛs, the son of Vaivasvata named, Yamarāja, along with his followers, in obedience to the orders of the Lord, awards unavoidable punishment for sin according to the action to the living beings who have been brought to his place after death by his servants.**

**Комментарий:**

The punishment is a transformation of the sin committed. By that frightening experience he makes the *jīvas* turn towards the Lord. Yamarāja cannot avoid the orders given by Brahmā (*bhagavac-chasanaḥ*). Or *bhagavac-chāsanaḥ* means the Vedas.

### Text 10

*yas tv iha vā etad aham iti mamedam iti bhūta-droheṇa kevalaṁ sva-kuṭumbam evānudinaṁ prapuṣṇāti sa tad iha vihāya svayam eva tad-aśubhena raurave nipatati.*

**Перевод:**

**A person who, thinking in terms of me and mine, maintains his own body and the bodies of his wife and children by violence against other living entities, falls into the hell called Raurava because of that violence after giving up his body and his family.**

**Комментарий:**

He who, thinking of himself in terms of I and mine, maintains his family falls to Raurava.

### Text 11

*ye tv iha yathaivāmunā vihiṁsitā jantavaḥ paratra yama-yātanām upagataṁ ta eva ruravo bhūtvā tathā tam eva vihiṁsanti tasmād rauravam ity āhū rurur iti sarpād ati-krūra-sattvasyāpadeśaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**The living beings that were injured by him become** ***rurus*** **and commit violence to him while he suffers the pains of Yama. Therefore this place is called Raurava. Ruru is the name of a living entity more cruel than a snake.**

**Комментарий:**

The beings which become rurus because of their acts of violence commit violence to him. Rurus are a type of animal not well known among the people.

### Text 17

*yas tv iha vai bhūtānām īśvaropakalpita-vṛttīnām avivikta-para-vyathānāṁ svayaṁ puruṣopakalpita-vṛttir vivikta-para-vyatho vyathām ācarati sa paratrāndhakūpe tad-abhidroheṇa nipatati tatra hāsau tair jantubhiḥ paśu-mṛga-pakṣi-sarīsṛpair maśaka-yūkā-matkuṇa-makṣikādibhir ye ke cābhidrugdhās taiḥ sarvato 'bhidruhyamāṇas tamasi vihata-nidrā-nirvṛtir alabdhāvasthānaḥ parikrāmati yathā kuśarīre jīvaḥ.*

**Перевод:**

**The person who, being given means of maintenance by the Lord and being aware of others’ suffering, causes suffering in this life to other entities who have no awareness of giving suffering to others and whose sustenance is arranged by the Lord from other living entities, falls to Andakūpa after death because of his violence. There he is attacked on all sides by the birds, animals, reptiles, mosquitoes, lice, worms, and flies which he injured. Without sleep or resting place, he wanders about like a living entity in a lower body.**

**Комментарий:**

Lower body (*ku-śarīre*) means animal body.

### Text 39

*śrutvā sthūlaṁ tathā sūkṣmaṁ*  
*rūpaṁ bhagavato yatiḥ*  
*sthūle nirjitam ātmānaṁ*  
*śanaiḥ sūkṣmaṁ dhiyā nayed iti*

**Перевод:**

**After hearing of the gross and subtle forms of the Lord, the renunciate, just as he leads the controlled mind by the intelligence to the gross form of the Lord, leads the mind to subtle form of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Hearing of the gross and then subtle forms of the Lord, having focussed the mind on the gross form, leads the mind by steady intelligence to the subtle form. The subtle form is understood by bhakti:

*yāvan na jāyeta parāvare ’smin*  
*viśveśvare draṣṭari bhakti-yogaḥ \|*  
*tāvat sthavīyaḥ puruṣasya rūpaṁ*  
*kriyāvasāne prayataḥ smareta \|\|*

**As long as meditation on the Lord and the witness of all beings, superior to even Brahmā, which has** ***bhakti*** **as an element, does not manifest, one should remember the gross universal form after performing necessary rites of** ***karma-yoga.*** **(SB** [**2.2.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/2.14)**)**
