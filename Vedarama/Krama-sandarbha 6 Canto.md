# Krama-sandarbha 6 Canto

## Chapter One

### Text 1

*śrī-parīkṣid uvāca*  
*nivṛtti-mārgaḥ kathita ādau bhagavatā yathā*  
*krama-yogopalabdhena brahmaṇā yad asaṁsṛtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**King Parīkṣit said: You have already described the path of liberation. By gradually progressing on the path properly, one ends material existence along with Brahmā.**

**Комментарий:**

What was previously stated is repeated in five verses, since it is suitable for the next topic. There are two paths leading to the next life: following the Vedas and going beyond the Vedas. The first has two types: the path of renunciation and the path of material engagement. The second path has only one type: following adharma.

In this verse the path indicates progressing gradually ((*karma-yogopalabdhena*). This does not indicate jñāna or bhakti as the path, since they yield the sādhya or a goal.

### Text 3

*adharma-lakṣaṇā nana narakāś cānuvarṇitāḥ*  
*manvantaraś ca vyākhyāta ādyaḥ svāyambhuvo yataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**You have also described the varieties of hellish life that result from impious activities, and you have described (in the Fourth Canto) the first Manvantara, which was supervised by Svāyambhuva Manu.**

**Комментарий:**

The first line describes adharma. The next line and next verse first line describe the manvantaras incidentally. *Yataḥ* means “in which, following both paths.”

### Text 4-5

*priyavratottānapador vaṁśas tac-caritāni ca*  
*dvīpa-varṣa-samudrādri- nady-udyāna-vanaspatīn*  
*dharā-maṇḍala-saṁsthānaṁ bhāga-lakṣaṇa-mānataḥ*  
*jyotiṣāṁ vivarāṇāṁ ca yathedam asṛjad vibhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**You have described the dynasties and characteristics of King Priyavrata and King Uttānapāda, as well as the islands,** ***varṣas*****, oceans, mountains, rivers, gardens, trees, and the condition of the earth and the luminaries in the sky and the lower planets, with their divisions, characteristics and dimensions, just as the Lord created them.**

**Комментарий:**

The islands are then described in three lines. *Vivarāṇām* (holes) means of hellish planets.

### Text 6

*adhuneha mahā-bhāga yathaiva narakān naraḥ*  
*nānogra-yātanān neyāt tan me vyākhyātum arhasi*

**Перевод:**

**O greatly fortunate Śukadeva Gosvāmī! Now kindly tell me how human beings may be saved from the terrible pains of hell.**

**Комментарий:**

Now the request is made. For people there are two sādhanas. The sādhana which removes unsteadiness should be known.

### Text 7

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*na ced ihaivāpacitiṁ yathāṁhasaḥ*  
*kṛtasya kuryān mana-ukta-pāṇibhiḥ*  
*dhruvaṁ sa vai pretya narakān upaiti*  
*ye kīrtitā me bhavatas tigma-yātanāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: If a person does not perform atonement for sins committed by his mind, words or actions, he will certainly attain those hells which inflict intense pain after death and which were described by me.**

**Комментарий:**

Śukadeva relates other’s opinion in answer to the question, in order to establish his recommendation for atonement.

### Text 9

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*dṛṣṭa-śrutābhyāṁ yat pāpaṁ jānann apy ātmano 'hitam*  
*karoti bhūyo vivaśaḥ prāyaścittam atho katham*

**Перевод:**

**The King said: Though the** ***jīva*** **knows that sinful acts are detrimental by seeing and hearing, he helplessly commits sin again. What is the use of atonement?**

**Комментарий:**

The king asks a question.

### Text 11

*śrī-bādarāyaṇir uvāca*  
*karmaṇā karma-nirhāro na hy ātyantika iṣyate*  
*avidvad-adhikāritvāt prāyaścittaṁ vimarśanam*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: Completely counteracting sin is not at all possible by performing atonement, for the sin is committed out of ignorance. Real atonement is attaining** ***jñāna.***

**Комментарий:**

Śukadeva states his opinion.

### Text 15

*kecit kevalayā bhaktyā vāsudeva-parāyaṇāḥ*  
*aghaṁ dhunvanti kārtsnyena nīhāram iva bhāskaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Persons who surrender to Kṛṣṇa completely destroy sins by pure** ***bhakti*** **alone*****,*** **just as the sun destroys fog.**

**Комментарий:**

Śukadeva states his opinion. This continues till the Third Chapter. There are two types of bhakti: continuous, and occasional. The continuous variety has two types: with attachment and with raga. The occasional variety has three types: rāgābhāsa-mayī, that devoid of bhakti’s svarūpa and that which arises from abhāsa. The abhāsa is the best type of atonement. How much more continuous bhakti with attachment or raga is glorious! With that intention he speaks.

The devotees destroy sin completely (kārstnyena), including desires, by pure bhakti.

The sun represents natural bhakti. The fog represents the incidental sins, which cannot touch bhakti, as fog cannot touch the sun. By the touch of bhakti, the sings are immediately, completely destroyed.

### Text 18

*prāyaścittāni cīrṇāni nārāyaṇa-parāṅmukham*  
*na niṣpunanti rājendra surā-kumbham ivāpagāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Even great atonement does not purify persons who are opposed to Nārāyaṇa, just as many rivers do not clean a liquor pot.**

**Комментарий:**

Atonements definitely do not purify (*niṣpunanti).*

### Text 19

*sakṛn manaḥ kṛṣṇa-padāravindayor*  
*niveśitaṁ tad-guṇa-rāgi yair iha*  
*na te yamaṁ pāśa-bhṛtaś ca tad-bhaṭān*  
*svapne 'pi paśyanti hi cīrṇa-niṣkṛtāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Persons who only once absorb their mind in Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, even if it occurs in dreams, or even though the mind still has material attraction, do not see the servants of Yama since they have accomplished all atonement by that remembrance,.**

**Комментарий:**

The excellence of rāgābhāsa-mayī bhakti is described. Rāga here means ratyābhāsa (likeness to rati or bhava). If real rati arises, then one would continually absorb the mind in the Lord. This verse describes once only thinking of the Lord’s feet.

*bhuvi puru-puṇya-tīrtha-sadanāny ṛṣayo vimadās*  
*ta uta bhavat-padāmbuja-hṛdo ’gha-bhid-aṅghri-jalāḥ*  
*dadhati sakṛn manas tvayi ya ātmani nitya-sukhe*  
*na punar upāsate puruṣa-sāra-harāvasathān*

**Sages free from false pride live on this earth by frequenting the sacred pilgrimage sites. Because such devotees keep your lotus feet within their hearts, the water that washes their feet destroys all sins. Having turned their minds toward you, the ever-blissful soul of all existence, they no longer dedicate themselves to serving family life at home, which simply robs a man of his good qualities. (SB** [**10.87.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/87.35)**)**

The person has slight attachment according to Śrīdhara Svāmī, but no knowledge or proper realization. This is superior to the previous methods. The servants of Yama cannot even glance at persons with bhaky-ābhāsa. This shows bhakti’s power.

### Text 20

*atra codāharantīmam itihāsaṁ purātanam*  
*dūtānāṁ viṣṇu-yamayoḥ saṁvādas taṁ nibodha me*

**Перевод:**

**Concerning this subject, the wise cite the ancient story of Ajāmila. Please here from me that story in which there is a conversation between the servants of Viṣṇu and Yama.**

**Комментарий:**

He relates a story of show the greater power of real bhakti.

### Text 24

*tasya pravayasaḥ putrā daśa teṣāṁ tu yo 'vamaḥ*  
*bālo nārāyaṇo nāmnā pitroś ca dayito bhṛśam*

**Перевод:**

**That old man Ajāmila had ten sons, of whom the youngest was named Nārāyaṇa. He was the object of affection of both his father and mother.**

**Комментарий:**

The mother and father became successful.

### Text 40

*yamadūtā ūcuḥ*  
*veda-praṇihito dharmo hy adharmas tad-viparyayaḥ*  
*vedo nārāyaṇaḥ sākṣāt svayambhūr iti śuśruma*

**Перевод:**

**The Yamadūtas replied: Acts which are prescribed in the Vedas constitute** ***dharma,*** **and acts which are not prescribed by the Vedas are** ***adharma.*** **The Vedas are directly Nārāyaṇa and are born from him. This we have heard from Yamarāja.**

**Комментарий:**

*Praṇihitaḥ* means prescribed. The Vedas are Nārāyaṇa personally (*sākṣāt*), and thus born from him.

### Text 41

*yena sva-dhāmny amī bhāvā rajaḥ-sattva-tamomayāḥ*  
*guṇa-nāma-kriyā-rūpair vibhāvyante yathā-tatham*

**Перевод:**

**By the will of Nārāyaṇa who is situated in his own abode, these living entities composed of** ***rajas, sattva*** **and** ***tamas*** **make their appearance very precisely with qualities, names and actions.**

**Комментарий:**

*Yena* means “by the supreme cause.”

### Text 42

*sūryo 'gniḥ khaṁ marud devaḥ somaḥ sandhyāhanī diśaḥ*  
*kaṁ kuḥ svayaṁ dharma iti hy ete daihyasya sākṣiṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**By the will of Nārāyaṇa, the sun, fire, sky, air, the** ***devatās,*** **moon, evening, day, night, the directions, water, land and Yamarāja—who are the witnesses of the activities of the living entity—make their appearance.**

**Комментарий:**

The witnesses are mentioned in the following:

*āditya-candrāv anilo ‘nalaś ca*  
*dyaur bhumir āpo hṛdayaṁ yamaśc a*  
*ahaśca rātriś ca ubhe ca sandhye*  
*dharmo ’pi jānāti narasya vṛttam*

**The sun, moon, air, fire, sky, earth, water, mind, Yama, day, night, dawn and twilight and the Lord know the actions of man. (Viṣṇu-dharma 55.14)**

They witness the good and bad actions of men.

### Text 43

*etair adharmo vijñātaḥ sthānaṁ daṇḍasya yujyate*  
*sarve karmānurodhena daṇḍam arhanti kāriṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**By these witnesses, acts of** ***adharma*** **can be understood, and the cause of punishment is determined. All performs of sin deserve punishment according to the act.**

**Комментарий:**

*Yujyate* means “arises.”

### Text 46

*yatheha deva-pravarās trai-vidhyam upalabhyate*  
*bhūteṣu guṇa-vaicitryāt tathānyatrānumīyate*

**Перевод:**

**O best of the** ***devas*****! Just as there are three types of people in this world according to the variety of** ***guṇas,*** **so in next life as well one can infer such types.**

**Комментарий:**

Even humans can infer these types.

### Text 47

*vartamāno 'nyayoḥ kālo guṇābhijñāpako yathā*  
*evaṁ janmānyayor etad dharmādharma-nidarśanam*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the qualities at the present time make known the qualities of the past and the future, the qualities of** ***dharma*** **and** ***adharma*** **in this birth indicate those qualities in past and future lives.**

**Комментарий:**

The present time, dawn or spring (*vartamānaḥ*), indicates portions of qualities of the previous time such as night or dewy season and also later time, such as daytime or summer. Similarly the present birth indicates the previous birth and future birth.

### Text 48

*manasaiva pure devaḥ pūrva-rūpaṁ vipaśyati*  
*anumīmāṁsate 'pūrvaṁ manasā bhagavān ajaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Yamarāja, living in his city, sees by his mind the previous condition of a living entity. Knowing everything like Brahmā, he then decides the present and future forms by his mind.**

**Комментарий:**

Yamarāja is called *bhagavān ajaḥ* because he is considered non-different from the Lord because he is a vibhūti of the Lord by his devotion.

### Text 54

*labdhvā nimittam avyaktaṁ vyaktāvyaktaṁ bhavaty uta*  
*yathā-yoni yathā-bījaṁ svabhāvena balīyasā*

**Перевод:**

**The gross and subtle bodies, appear with a particular nature according to the** ***jīva’s karmas*** **through the womb and semen.**

**Комментарий:**

The body arises according to the nature of the womb and semen received by karma.

### Text 55

*eṣa prakṛti-saṅgena puruṣasya viparyayaḥ*  
*āsīt sa eva na cirād īśa-saṅgād vilīyate*

**Перевод:**

**This unfortunate position of the** ***jīva*** **has existed by association with** ***prakṛti.*** **That condition is quickly destroyed by association with the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

This is similar to the following verse:

*bhayaṁ dvitīyābhiniveśataḥ syād*  
*īśād apetasya viparyayo 'smṛtiḥ*  
*tan-māyayāto budha ābhajet taṁ*  
*bhaktyaikayeśaṁ guru-devate ātmā*

**For the** ***jīva*** **averse to the Lord, there will be** ***saṁsāra*** **consisting of identity with body and lack of identity with the soul, because of his absorption in the material coverings on the soul, arising from the Lord’s** ***māyā.*** **Therefore, the intelligent person, taking** ***guru*** **as his Lord and very self, should fully worship the Lord with pure** ***bhakti.*** **(SB** [**11.2.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/2.37)**)**

### Text 56-57

*ayaṁ hi śruta-sampannaḥ śīla-vṛtta-guṇālayaḥ*  
*dhṛta-vrato mṛdur dāntaḥ satya-vāṅ mantra-vic chuciḥ*  
*gurv-agny-atithi-vṛddhānāṁ śuśrūṣur anahaṅkṛtaḥ*  
*sarva-bhūta-suhṛt sādhur mita-vāg anasūyakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Ajāmila was well-versed in the Vedas. He was a reservoir of good character, good conduct and good qualities. Firmly established in executing all the Vedic injunctions, he was very mild and gentle, and kept his mind and senses under control. Furthermore, he was always truthful, knew how to chant the Vedic** ***mantras*****, and was also very pure. Ajāmila was respectful to his** ***guru,*** **the fire-god, guests, and the elderly members of his household. He was free from pride, was upright, benevolent to all living entities, well-behaved, controlled in speech, and without hatred to anyone.**

**Комментарий:**

Śukadeva shows in this section that though Ajāmila was not protected from falling by being fixed in his dharma, he was protected by bhaktyābhāsa. This is mentioned in the words of the Yamadūtas.

## Chapter Two

### Text 2

*śrī-viṣṇudūtā ūcuḥ*  
*aho kaṣṭaṁ dharma-dṛśām adharmaḥ spṛśate sabhām*  
*yatrādaṇḍyeṣv apāpeṣu daṇḍo yair dhriyate vṛthā*

**Перевод:**

**The servants of Viṣṇu said: This is a calamity!** ***Adharma*** **has touched the assembly of authorities on** ***dharma,*** **where punishment is unnecessarily being given to the sinless, who are not to be punished.**

**Комментарий:**

The Yamadūtas had shown that their master as the king of dharma protected the worlds. While agreeing with this, the servants of Viṣṇu criticize them in five verses.

### Text 7

*ayaṁ hi kṛta-nirveśo janma-koṭy-aṁhasām api*  
*yad vyājahāra vivaśo nāma svasty-ayanaṁ hareḥ*

**Перевод:**

**This person has performed atonement for the sins of ten million births since he has spontaneously chanted the auspicious name of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Though he previously chanted the name indicating his son only, the name had great effect.

### Text 8

*etenaiva hy aghono 'sya kṛtaṁ syād agha-niṣkṛtam*  
*yadā nārāyaṇāyeti jagāda catur-akṣaram*

**Перевод:**

**When he chanted the four syllables “Nārāyaṇa,” atonement for the sins that he committed has been accomplished.**

**Комментарий:**

“When he named his son, by uttering the Lord’s name, he destroys ten million lives of sin. And all sins committed after that time were finished as well by uttering that name once. Can that be true?” That is confirmed in this verse. Though he did not chant previously, all the previous sinful acts were also destroyed. Other means are rejected.

### Text 9-10

*stenaḥ surā-po mitra-dhrug brahma-hā guru-talpa-gaḥ*  
*strī-rāja-pitṛ-go-hantā ye ca pātakino 'pare*  
*sarveṣām apy aghavatām idam eva suniṣkṛtam*  
*nāma-vyāharaṇaṁ viṣṇor yatas tad-viṣayā matiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The chanting of the holy name of Lord Viṣṇu is the best process of atonement for a thief, for a drunkard, for one who betrays a friend or relative, for one who kills a** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **for one who indulges in sex with the wife of his** ***guru*** **or another superior, for one who kills women, the king, his father, or cows, and for all other sinful men. Simply by chanting the holy name of Lord Viṣṇu, such sinful persons attract the attention of the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Because of chanting Viṣṇu’s name, Viṣṇu becomes aware of that person (*tad-viṣayā matiḥ*).

### Text 11

*na niṣkṛtair uditair brahma-vādibhis*  
*tathā viśuddhyaty aghavān vratādibhiḥ*  
*yathā harer nāma-padair udāhṛtais*  
*tad uttamaśloka-guṇopalambhakam*

**Перевод:**

**A sinful man is not purified by abundant atonement, or by vows mentioned by Manu and others, as much as he is purified by chanting even the names of the Lord, which produce realization of his qualities.**

**Комментарий:**

He is not purified by karma-kāṇḍa alone (*brahma-vādibhiḥ*) as much as he is purified by chanting words in the form of the nāma (*nāma-padaiḥ*).

*vipro rājanya-vaiśyau vā hareḥ prāptāḥ padāntikam*  
*śrautena janmanāthāpi muhyanty āmnāya-vādinaḥ*

**On the other hand,** ***brāhmaṇas, kṣatriyas*** **and** ***vaiśyas,*** **even after being allowed to study the Vedas by receiving Vedic initiation, become bewildered since they interpret the meaning of the Vedas. (SB** [**11.5.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/5.5)**)**

By chanting one name, there is realization of the qualities of the Lord, with additional words of description.

### Text 12

*naikāntikaṁ tad dhi kṛte 'pi niṣkṛte*  
*manaḥ punar dhāvati ced asat-pathe*  
*tat karma-nirhāram abhīpsatāṁ harer*  
*guṇānuvādaḥ khalu sattva-bhāvanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Since even after atonement the mind again pursues material enjoyment, for people desiring complete destruction of material desire, continuous chanting of the qualities of the Lord is the only means of purification.**

**Комментарий:**

Not only the name, but the qualities of the Lord have this effect. That is explained in this verse.

### Text 13

*athainaṁ māpanayata kṛtāśeṣāgha-niṣkṛtam*  
*yad asau bhagavan-nāma mriyamāṇaḥ samagrahīt*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, do not take away Ajāmila who has performed unlimited atonement. Because of being sinless, he has perfectly chanted the name of the Lord while dying.**

**Комментарий:**

Even though he is dying, he chanted the name. It is not necessary at that time alone to chant, since the name has power as described in the next verse.

### Text 14

*sāṅketyaṁ pārihāsyaṁ vā stobhaṁ helanam eva vā*  
*vaikuṇṭha-nāma-grahaṇam aśeṣāgha-haraṁ viduḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Chanting the name of the Lord while indicating someone else or while joking in a friendly manner, or chanting the Lord’s name in order to fill up space while chanting verses or chanting with neglect destroys unlimited sins and desires.**

**Комментарий:**

*Parihāsa* is words filled with affection for the Lord meant to humiliate another person. There is no criticism of the Lord. It is also called *upahāsa* (mocking). It is joking using the Lord’s name. *Helanam* means using words with neglect. One does not chant with attention. This also is not uttered with disrespect. Thus if Veṇa did not chant with disrespect, it would not have fault. There is fault in teaching to persons devoid of faith. But some say that even if it generates offense, it destroys old sins.

### Text 15

*patitaḥ skhalito bhagnaḥ sandaṣṭas tapta āhataḥ*  
*harir ity avaśenāha pumān nārhati yātanāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**A person who chants the name of the Lord while falling from a cliff, stumbling on the road, receiving injury his body, being bitten by snakes, tormented with fever or being attacked with sticks will not suffer hellish punishment.**

**Комментарий:**

Not only the above mentioned chanting has effect. With a desire to destroy certain sufferings if the Lord’s name is chanted, sins are destroyed. Sometimes *yātanā* is seen instead of *yātanāḥ.*

### Text 16

*gurūṇāṁ ca laghūnāṁ ca gurūṇi ca laghūni ca*  
*prāyaścittāni pāpānāṁ jñātvoktāni maharṣibhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The great sages, understanding the limited nature of atonements, have said that severe atonements are prescribed for grave sins, and light atonements are prescribed for minor sins.**

### Text 17

*tais tāny aghāni pūyante tapo-dāna-vratādibhiḥ*  
*nādharmajaṁ tad-dhṛdayaṁ tad apīśāṅghri-sevayā*

**Перевод:**

**Sins are destroyed by austerity, charity and vows but the root of sin is not destroyed. However by** ***bhakti*** **to the Lord, even the root of sin, namely desire, is destroyed.**

**Комментарий:**

Whatever atonements are prescribed destroy sins, but desire is destroyed by service to the Lord.

### Text 18

*ajñānād athavā jñānād uttamaśloka-nāma yat*  
*saṅkīrtitam aghaṁ puṁso dahed edho yathānalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Chanting the name of the Lord with or without knowledge of its powers destroys a man’s sins, just as fire destroys dry grass.**

**Комментарий:**

Therefore, consciously or unconsciously chanting the Lord’s name burns up the sins of any man, like fire burns up wood completely.

### Text 19

*yathāgadaṁ vīryatamam upayuktaṁ yadṛcchayā*  
*ajānato 'py ātma-guṇaṁ kuryān mantro 'py udāhṛtaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just as powerful medicine when taken without knowledge of its powers still produces its effects quickly, so chanting the name of the Lord also produces effects quickly, without knowing its powers.**

**Комментарий:**

*Mantra* here means the name of the Lord. Normal mantras require faith and other elements to be effective. The name does not depend on that. The word *mantra* is used to indicate its main function (purification). Or this verse continues the example given in the previous verse.

### Text 20

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*ta evaṁ suvinirṇīya dharmaṁ bhāgavataṁ nṛpa*  
*taṁ yāmya-pāśān nirmucya vipraṁ mṛtyor amūmucan*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O King! Having thus perfectly defined the principles of devotional service with reasoning and arguments, the order carriers of Lord Viṣṇu released the** ***brāhmaṇa*** **Ajāmila, from the bondage of the Yamadūtas and saved him from death.**

**Комментарий:**

Though the servants of Yama did not have complete knowledge, the servants of Viṣṇu explained expertly (*suvinirnīya*). The name even if chanted accidently (verses 13) once in nāmābhāsa destroys unlimited sins and even desires. Verse 12 indicates practice of bhakti. This is worship without offenses.

*nāmāparādha-yuktānāṁ nāmāny eva haranty agham \|*  
*aviśrānta-prayuktāni tāny evārtha-karāṇi ca \|\|*

**For those who have committed offenses to the name, chanting the name incessantly will be effective in destroying that sin. (Padma Purāṇa)**

Though the name is completely independent, it is an offense to chant it as an aṅga of karma, for accruing piety. To equate the name with pious acts is an offense mentioned in the Padma Purāṇa. However such offenses are nullified by worship of the Lord:

*tais tāny aghāni pūyante tapo-dāna-vratādibhiḥ*  
*nādharmajaṁ tad-dhṛdayaṁ tad apīśāṅghri-sevayā*

**Sins are destroyed by austerity, charity and vows but the root of sin is not destroyed. However by** ***bhakti*** **to the Lord, even the root of sin, namely desire, is destroyed. (SB** [**6.2.17**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/2.17)**)**

If that is so, why did Ajāmila not immediately go to Vaikuṇṭha as an associate, since while dying he chanted the name? It is said:

*yasyāvatāra-guṇa-karma-viḍambanāni*  
*nāmāni ye ’su-vigame vivaśā gṛṇanti*  
*te ’naika-janma-śamalaṁ sahasaiva hitvā*  
*saṁyānty apāvṛtāmṛtaṁ tam ajaṁ prapadye*

**I surrender to the Lord without birth, whose names, invested with the powers of the Lord’s qualities and activities, chanted at the point of death even without attention, immediately destroy lifetimes of sins and allow men to achieve the uncovered, true form of the Lord. (SB** [**3.9.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/9.15)**)**

But there are two types of chanting the name: kevala (without affection) and sneha-saṁyukta (with affection). The first type keeps the person from going to hell and the second gives nearness to the Lord.

*mayi bhaktir hi bhūtānām amṛtatvāya kalpate*  
*diṣṭyā yad āsīn mat-sneho bhavatīnāṁ mad-āpanaḥ*

**Rendering devotional service to me qualifies any living being for eternal life. But your** ***prema*** **for me, which has existed to bring the highest fortune, has caused you to attain me directly. (SB** [**10.82.44**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/82.44)**)**

But also it is said that the Lord delays in giving his affection:

*nāhaṁ tu sakhyo bhajato ’pi jantūn*  
*bhajāmy amīṣām anuvṛtti-vṛttaye*  
*yathādhano labdha-dhane vinaṣṭe*  
*tac-cintayānyan nibhṛto na veda*

**But the reason I do not immediately reciprocate the affection of living beings even when they worship Me, O** ***gopīs,*** **is that I want to intensify their loving devotion. They then become like a poor man who has gained some wealth and then lost it, and who thus becomes so anxious about it that he can think of nothing else. (SB** [**10.32.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/32.20)**)**

*Amīṣam anuvṛtti* means affection, or continual service which is one’s very life. Thus even Ajāmila had affection in chanting the name because it was also the name of his son.

The Lord has great identity with his own name for it was said in verse 10 that the Lord is attracted to the person who chants (*tad-viṣayā matiḥ*). Since it is said the even the associates have great respect for such persons, the Lord gives his affection to a person when that person chants with longing while uttering the Lord’s name with affection, and desires to take the devotee. The servants of Viṣṇu understood this and thus took him with them forcibly.

At the end they received the highest benefit. It is said:

*kṛṣṇaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ kṛṣṇa ity anta-kāle*  
*jalpan jantur jīvitaṁ yo jahāti \|*  
*ādyaḥ śabdaḥ kalpate tasya muktyai*  
*vrīḍā-namrau tiṣṭhato’nyāvṛṇa-sthau \|\|*

### Text 29

*jihvā na vakti bhagavad-guṇa-nāmadheyaṁ*  
*cetaś ca na smarati tac-caraṇāravindam*  
*kṛṣṇāya no namati yac-chira ekadāpi*  
*tān ānayadhvam asato 'kṛta-viṣṇu-kṛtyān*

**Перевод:**

**Bring to me the non-devotees who have never served Viṣṇu, whose heads have never once bowed to Kṛṣṇa, whose tongues have never pronounced the name and qualities of the Lord, whose minds have never remembered his lotus feet.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 148:**

You should bring those whose tongues never once in this birth uttered the name of the Lord or described his qualities. If they do not have tongues, then bring those who have not remembered the lotus feet of the Lord once. If their minds are disturbed, then bring those who do not offer respects to the Lord with their heads.

*śaṭhenāpi namaskāraṁ kurvataḥ śārṅgadhanvine \|*  
*śata-janmārjitaṁ pāpaṁ tat-kṣaṇād eva naśyati \|\|*

**Those who offer respects to the Lord even with deception instantly destroy the sins of a hundred births. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

Bring those who do not offer respects. The reason they do not offer respects is that they are materialists *(asataḥ*). They are materialists because they have never done activities for the Lord.

Brahmā says:

*sa kartā sarva-dharmāṇāṁ bhakto yas tava keśava \|*  
*sa kartā sarva-pāpānāṁ yo na bhaktas tavācyuta \|\|*  
*pāpaṁ bhavati dharmo ’pi tavābhaktaiḥ kṛto hare \|*  
*niḥśeṣa-dharma-kartā vāpy abhakto narake hare \|*  
*sadā tiṣṭhati bhaktas te brahmahāpi vimucyate \|\|*

**O Keśava! Your devotee has performed all dharma. He who is not your devotee has performed all sin. O Lord! Dharma performed by the non-devotee is sin. O Lord! Though the non-devotee does unlimited dharma he remains eternally in hell. The devotee, though he kills a brāhmaṇa, is liberated. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

*man-nimittaṁ kṛtaṁ pāpam api kṣemāya kalpate \|*  
*mām anādṛtya dharmo ’pi pāpaṁ syān mat-prabhāvataḥ \|\|*

**One who has done sin by acts for me is liberated. One who performs dharma but disrespects me commits sin by my influence. (Padma Purāṇa)**

Therefore it was rightly said in SB [7.11.10](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/11.8) that acts of bhakti must be included in dharma (destroyers of sin). It was also said that the varṇāśrama system originates from the limbs of the Lord. (SB [11.5.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/5.2)) Bhakti must always be performed as per the rule *sarva-vidhi niṣedāḥ syuḥ*: all rules and prohibitions are servants of always remembering the Lord and never forgetting him. This is the highest nitya-karma or dharmic rule.

In the absence of chanting, remembering and offering respects all other devotional actions would also be absent. Thus the verse under discussion says the person is devoid of activities for the Lord (*akṛta-viṣṇu-kṛyān*).

By mentioning the various organs as agents, the order is given for chanting, remembering and offering respects even if the person is unwilling. The lotus feet are particularly mentioned to indicate Yamarāja’s bhakti, not to make a rule of only remembering the Lord’s feet. By ordering his servants to bring the non-devotees he also orders them not to bring devotees since the order to take the non-devotees is final. *Vaivasvataṁ saṁyamanaṁ prajānām*: Yama punishes his citizens. (Ṛg Veda 10.14.1)

*sakṛn manaḥ kṛṣṇa-padāravindayor*  
*niveśitaṁ tad-guṇa-rāgi yair iha*  
*na te yamaṁ pāśa-bhṛtaś ca tad-bhaṭān*  
*svapne ’pi paśyanti hi cīrṇa-niṣkṛtāḥ*

**Persons who only once absorb their mind in Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, even if it occurs in dreams, or even though the mind still has material attraction, do not see the servants of Yama since they have accomplished all atonement (dharma) by that remembrance. (SB** [**6.1.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/1.19)**)**

The power of remembering the Lord is such that it destroys all attraction to objects that may appear to the person (*tad-guṇa-rāgi*). Similarly it is said:

*aham amara-gaṇārcitena dhātrā*  
*yama iti loka-hitāhite niyuktaḥ \|*  
*hari-guru-vimukhān praśāsmi martyān*  
*hari-caraṇa-praṇatān namaskaromi \|\|*

**I am Yama, engaged by the Lord who is worshipped by the devatās, for punishing or rewarding people. I punish those who are averse to the Lord and offer respects to those who respect the Lord’s feet. (Nṛsiṁha Purāṇa 9.2)**

*na brahmā na śivāgnīndrā nāhaṁ nānye divaukasaḥ \|*  
*śaktās tu nigrahaṁ kartuṁ vaiṣṇavānāṁ mahātmanām \|\|*

**Brahmā, Śiva, Agni, Indra, myself (Skanda) or any devatā cannot punish the great devotees of the Lord. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

*kathayām āsa Malaya āsīno harim arcayan*

**Перевод:**

**The sage Agastya, the son of Kumbha, residing in the Malaya Hills and worshiping the Lord, spoke this confidential story to me.**

**Комментарий:**

*Kumbha-sambhavaḥ* is Agastya. He also told this to me. Śukadeva heard from Vyāsa the whole chapter.

## Chapter Four

### Text 1-2

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*devāsura-nṛṇāṁ sargo nāgānāṁ mṛga-pakṣiṇām*  
*sāmāsikas tvayā prokto yas tu svāyambhuve 'ntare*  
*tasyaiva vyāsam icchāmi jñātuṁ te bhagavan yathā*  
*anusargaṁ yayā śaktyā sasarja bhagavān paraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The King said: My dear Lord! I wish to know in detail about the** ***devatās,*** **demons, human beings, Nāgas, beasts and birds created during the reign of Svāyambhuva Manu, which you described briefly in the Third Canto. I also wish to know about the** ***śakti*** **by which Brahmā produces the secondary creation.**

**Комментарий:**

He wants to know because the details of the creation during the sixth manvantara had not be narrated. I desire to know the details of the branches in the lineage from Dakṣa who arose in that manvantara (*vyāsam*). The previous creation of Brahmā had been lost from some reason. Thus he inquires about Brahmā’s creation again (*anusargam*).

### Text 4

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*yadā pracetasaḥ putrā daśa prācīnabarhiṣaḥ*  
*antaḥ-samudrād unmagnā dadṛśur gāṁ drumair vṛtām*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: When the ten sons of Prācīnabarhi emerged from the waters, they saw that the entire world was covered with trees.**

**Комментарий:**

This is a description of what happened after Uttānapāda finished ruling. After that, no one was ruling. Thus the tress covered the world.

### Text 8

*aho prajāpati-patir bhagavān harir avyayaḥ*  
*vanaspatīn oṣadhīś ca sasarjorjam iṣaṁ vibhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The powerful, indestructible Supreme Lord, the master of the progenitors, has created the trees and grass with fruits and grains.**

**Комментарий:**

*Ūrjam* refers to eatables other than grains.

### Text 9

*annaṁ carāṇām acarā hy apadaḥ pāda-cāriṇām*  
*ahastā hasta-yuktānāṁ dvi-padāṁ ca catuṣ-padaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Non-moving plants are food for the moving beings. Beings without legs are food for those with legs. Animals without hands are food for animals with hands. The animals with four legs and plants are food for the human being.**

**Комментарий:**

He created food for strong and weak moving beings.

### Text 23

*śrī-prajāpatir uvāca*  
*namaḥ parāyāvitathānubhūtaye*  
*guṇa-trayābhāsa-nimitta-bandhave*  
*adṛṣṭa-dhāmne guṇa-tattva-buddhibhir*  
*nivṛtta-mānāya dadhe svayambhuve*

**Перевод:**

**Dakṣa said: I offer respects to the self-born Lord, superior to the** ***jīva*** **and** **to** ***māyā*** **and its products, who is real knowledge, who inspires the** ***jīva*** **and matter to act, who has a form unseen by the** ***jīva*** **whose intelligence is absorbed in material objects, and who cannot be known.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord and no other should be worshipped either with desires (higher or lower) or without desires. Since he has śaktis which control māyā and the jīva, other beings have insignificant śakti dependent on māyā. With that purpose, Dakṣa worshipped with Lord with verses of praise.

The Lord has true perception (*avitathānubhūtaye).* This means his jñāna and icchā śaktis are constant in serving him. He inspires the jīvas and māyā to act. This indicates his kriyā-śakti. Since the śaktis controls māyā and the jīva, they arise from his svarūpa, beyond māyā and the jīvas. His form is unseen (*adṛṣṭa-dhamne*). It is impossible to conceive by persons whose knowledge (*tattva-buddhi*) is in the material guṇas. What to speak of realizing that form (*nivṛtta-mānāya*), which manifests such śaktis, a form full of śaktis arising from his svarūpa (*dhāmne*). This is because the form is self-manifesting (*svayambhuve*). The jīva is covered by ignorance (*guṇa-trayābhāsa*). It is clear that the jīva is inferior, and an ābhāsa, since he is touched by ignorance, though he is conscious. He is like a ray of the sun.

### Text 25

*deho 'savo 'kṣā manavo bhūta-mātrām*  
*ātmānam anyaṁ ca viduḥ paraṁ yat*  
*sarvaṁ pumān veda guṇāṁś ca taj-jño*  
*na veda sarva-jñam anantam īḍe*

**Перевод:**

**The body, life airs, senses, internal senses, gross elements and sense objects do not know themselves or other things or the** ***jīva.*** **The** ***jīva*** **knows all of these items and the** ***guṇas*** **which cause them. He also can know Paramātmā, but does not really know the omniscient Lord. I worship that Lord with infinite qualities.**

**Комментарий:**

The body and senses do not know the devatās or other humans (*anyam*).

### Text 26

*yadoparāmo manaso nāma-rūpa-*  
*rūpasya dṛṣṭa-smṛti-sampramoṣāt*  
*ya īyate kevalayā sva-saṁsthayā*  
*haṁsāya tasmai śuci-sadmane namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the pure Lord, perceived by the pure mind, who is perceived by his svarūpa-śakti when the mind which defines names and forms enters** ***samādhi*** **because of complete destruction of seeing and remembering material objects.**

**Комментарий:**

*Sva-saṁsthayā* means by svarūpa-śakti.

### Text 27-28

*manīṣiṇo 'ntar-hṛdi sanniveśitaṁ*  
*sva-śaktibhir navabhiś ca trivṛdbhiḥ*  
*vahniṁ yathā dāruṇi pāñcadaśyaṁ*  
*manīṣayā niṣkarṣanti gūḍham*  
*sa vai mamāśeṣa-viśeṣa-māyā-*  
*niṣedha-nirvāṇa-sukhānubhūtiḥ*  
*sa sarva-nāmā sa ca viśva-rūpaḥ*  
*prasīdatām aniruktātma-śaktiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just as persons manifest fire from wood through** ***mantras*****, the pure devotees, after bringing the personal form of Lord into the heart by hearing, manifest him before their eyes along with his nine and three spiritual** ***śaktis*** **by their** ***prema-bhakti.*** **May that Lord who is realized as bliss without material suffering, devoid of the unlimited varieties of** ***māyā,*** **who is all names and all forms in the universe by his external energy, and who has his spiritual energy beyond mind and words, be pleased with me.**

**Комментарий:**

“Since I am beyond the three and nine śaktis, am I without śakti?” That is not so.

He must be conceived of as blissful, devoid of material suffering (*sukhānubhūtiḥ*). Without that realization of bliss, he could not be the goal or puruṣārtha. By accept this realization of his bliss, one accepts his svarūpa-śakti, with its various aspects. Because of this Dakṣa can say “Be pleased with me.” If he is distinct from his śaktis, how can he embody all names and forms? He has acintya-śakti (*aniruktātma-śaktiḥ*).

### Text 29

*yad yan niruktaṁ vacasā nirūpitaṁ*  
*dhiyākṣabhir vā manasota yasya*  
*mā bhūt svarūpaṁ guṇa-rūpaṁ hi tat tat*  
*sa vai guṇāpāya-visarga-lakṣaṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord manifesting forms and qualities, who cannot be defined by words, intelligence, senses or mind, and who can only be inferred from the creation and destruction of matter.**

**Комментарий:**

“Just realize my form as Brahman, a form of complete bliss. Why bother with three and nine śaktis?” Is that svarūpa full qualities and forms similar to those of the material world? Three verses explain this form.

That svarūpa is full of qualities and forms, unequaled in greatness (*guṇa-rūpaṁ svarūpam*). It is not an object subject to words, senses, mind or intelligence, because that svarūpa full of qualities and forms is only inferred (*lakṣaṇaḥ*) by creation and destruction of the guṇas, and not realized. Or that form is seen in eternal Vaikuṇṭha (*visarga*), in which the guṇas are absent (*guṇāpāya*) since he is its controller.

### Text 30

*yasmin yato yena ca yasya yasmai*  
*yad yo yathā kurute kāryate ca*  
*parāvareṣāṁ paramaṁ prāk prasiddhaṁ*  
*tad brahma tad dhetur ananyad ekam*

**Перевод:**

**Brahman is the supreme cause, ever existing, of all lower and higher causes, in which, from which, by which, of which, unto which everything occurs, who does everything, and who is the desired object of all action. Brahman alone, and nothing else, exists.**

**Комментарий:**

That is shown in this verse.

### Text 31

*yac-chaktayo vadatāṁ vādināṁ vai*  
*vivāda-saṁvāda-bhuvo bhavanti*  
*kurvanti caiṣāṁ muhur ātma-mohaṁ*  
*tasmai namo 'nanta-guṇāya bhūmne*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the great Lord of eternal qualities, whose material energy is the cause of agreement and disagreement among those who make philosophies and then argue with others, and whose material energy continually bewilders them as to the real nature of** ***ātmā.***

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is the cause of various speculative arguments of persons who falsely maintain one point and other persons who argue with those points. This is because he has unlimited qualities and wealth.

### Text 32

*astīti nāstīti ca vastu-niṣṭhayor*  
*eka-sthayor bhinna-viruddha-dharmaṇoḥ*  
*avekṣitaṁ kiñcana yoga-sāṅkhyayoḥ*  
*samaṁ paraṁ hy anukūlaṁ bṛhat tat*

**Перевод:**

**The same supreme Brahman without contradiction is perceived by** ***bhakti*** **and** ***jñāna*** **with contrary qualities because the followers are fixed in a particular faith directed to the same object.**

**Комментарий:**

In the arguments, the Lord’s svarūpa full of qualities and forms is the conclusion. That is explained in this verse.

**Bhagavat Sandarbha 51:**

*Yoga* refers to scriptures which advocate worship of a gross form (*asti*). When the name and form of the Lord are mentioned in scriptures, they meditate on all material names like pot and cloth and think of Pātāla as the Lord’s feet from rash imagination arising from visible objects. Sāṅkhya refers to jñāna scriptures which advocate no form (*nāsti)*. Since the scriptures forbid names and forms, they forbid all name and form for the Lord since they can conceive of only material names and forms. These two types of worship were previously described in the same chapter. *Sa sarva-nāmā sa ca viśva-rūpaḥ*: the Lord is all names and all forms in the universe by his external energy. *Yad yan niruktaṁ vacasā:* his form cannot be defined by words *(nirūpitam*). (SB [6.4.28-29](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/14.28))

Some are fixed in these two concepts (*vastuni niṣṭhāyoḥ*). This produces quarrel. The two parties have contrary qualities (*viruddha-dharmanoḥ*) because of different concepts (*bhinna*) of *asti* and *nāsti.*

“They are arguing about different objects.” No. The two have the same object (*eka-sthayoḥ*). In this quarrel, the object perceived through agreement or compromise (*samam*) should be very favorable for both. What is the agreement? It is a different type of entity *(param*), since it does not have name and form and also has name and form. That object which simultaneously possesses and does not possess name and form is different.

This should be said. Scriptures should cancel each other by advocating name and form or forbidding name and form in one object. Based on the previous reasoning that the supreme Lord had name and form and did not have name and form, the only solution is that the Lord has non-material name and form. This agrees with the conclusions of both types of scripture. They simply quarrel over their positions.

Dhruva has stated the position of no conflict:

*tiryaṅ-naga-dvija-sarīsṛpa-deva-daitya-*  
*martyādibhiḥ paricitaṁ sad-asad-viśeṣam*  
*rūpaṁ sthaviṣṭham aja te mahad-ādy-anekaṁ*  
*nātaḥ paraṁ parama vedmi na yatra vādaḥ*

**O unborn Lord! Previously I knew the variety of gross and subtle elements manifested as animals, plants, birds, snakes, devatās, demons, humans and total universal form composed of mahat-tattva and other elements. O Supreme! But I did not know what was superior to that material form, and did not know the form of Brahman in which sound does not exist. (SB** [**4.9.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/9.13)**)**

### Text 33

*yo 'nugrahārthaṁ bhajatāṁ pāda-mūlam*  
*anāma-rūpo bhagavān anantaḥ*  
*nāmāni rūpāṇi ca janma-karmabhir*  
*bheje sa mahyaṁ paramaḥ prasīdatu*

**Перевод:**

**May the unlimited Supreme Lord who, without material name and form, manifests names and forms by his birth and activities in this world to show mercy to those who worship his lotus feet, be pleased with me!**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhagavat Sandarbha 52:**

He who does not have name and form manifests *(bheje*) names and forms along with his birth and activities. Not to have name and form would be a fault. That is expressed by the word *ananta* (unlimited). If he does not have names and forms, it would mean he does not have śaktis. The Pracetās say *na hy antas tvad-vibhūtīnāṁ so ’nanta iti gīyase*: since there is no end to your splendor (names and forms), you are called Ananta, the unlimited. (SB [4.30.31](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/30.31)) The cause of his manifesting name and form is his śaktis. Bhagavān means possessing auspicious śaktis. The word *paramaḥ* (supreme) excludes śaktis made of material māyā. *Paramaḥ* means “he in whom Lakṣmī (*mā*) exists as the para-śakti.” If his śaktis were material, it would be a fault to his supreme position. It is said:

*tasmān na māyayā sarvaṁ sarvam aiśvarya-sambhavam \|*  
*amāyo hīśvaro yasmāt tasmāt taṁ paramaṁ viduḥ \|\|*

**Therefore everything connected with the Lord is not produced by māyā. Everything arises by his spiritual powers. Since the Lord is without māyā he is known as the supreme. (Viṣṇu-dharmottara)**

“The proof of whether the Lord has names and forms or does not have names and forms is the existence of worshippers. Who are these worshippers?” I manifest my forms and names for giving mercy to those who worship my feet. I reveal names and forms, not to the followers of yoga or Sāṅkhya, but to the devotee. *Bhaktir evainaṁ darśayati*: bhakti alone reveals the Lord. (Māṭhara-śruti) Thus it is correct to say that the two parties are only interested in quarrelling. Later it will be said:

*iti stutaḥ saṁstuvataḥ sa tasminn aghamarṣaṇe*  
*prādurāsīt kuru-śreṣṭha bhagavān bhakta-vatsalaḥ*

**O Mahārāja Parīkṣit! The Supreme Lord, affectionate to his devotees, on being praised by Dakṣa, appeared at that holy place known as Aghamarṣaṇa. (SB** [**6.4.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/4.35)**)**

Because he shows his form to persons who worship his feet, the form must have existed previously to that.

*caraṇaṁ pavitraṁ vitataṁ purāṇaṁ yena pūtas tarati duṣkṛtāni:*

**We become purified and can cross saṁsāra by that pure, eternal foot which extends itself everywhere. (Mahā-nārāyaṇa Upaniṣad)**

*Bheje* is in the past tense to indicate that his limbs are eternal. Since the Lord is *ananta* (eternal and infinite), so are his names and forms. Thus the Lord is devoid of material names and forms.

### Text 34

*yaḥ prākṛtair jñāna-pathair janānāṁ*  
*yathāśayaṁ deha-gato vibhāti*  
*yathānilaḥ pārthivam āśrito guṇaṁ*  
*sa īśvaro me kurutāṁ manoratham*

**Перевод:**

**That Lord, who enters the bodies of persons following an immature path of knowledge, appears to be a** ***jīva*** **according to the nature of their hearts, just as air appears variously after taking shelter of various fragrances. That Lord should fulfill my desires.**

**Комментарий:**

“Forms like Ganeśa, with upādhis, empowered by me, are suitable for your worship since you desire powers like them. Why do you praise me as the soul of all, devoid of upādhis?” This verse answers. Why worship upādhis? You are situated without upādhis, like the air that produces effects.

### Text 35-39

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*iti stutaḥ saṁstuvataḥ sa tasminn aghamarṣaṇe*  
*prādurāsīt kuru-śreṣṭha bhagavān bhakta-vatsalaḥ*  
*kṛta-pādaḥ suparṇāṁse pralambāṣṭa-mahā-bhujaḥ*  
*cakra-śaṅkhāsi-carmeṣu- dhanuḥ-pāśa-gadā-dharaḥ*  
*pīta-vāsā ghana-śyāmaḥ prasanna-vadanekṣaṇaḥ*  
*vana-mālā-nivītāṅgo lasac-chrīvatsa-kaustubhaḥ*  
*mahā-kirīṭa-kaṭakaḥ sphuran-makara-kuṇḍalaḥ*  
*kāñcy-aṅgulīya-valaya- nūpurāṅgada-bhūṣitaḥ*  
*trailokya-mohanaṁ rūpaṁ bibhrat tribhuvaneśvaraḥ*  
*vṛto nārada-nandādyaiḥ pārṣadaiḥ sura-yūthapaiḥ*  
*stūyamāno 'nugāyadbhiḥ siddha-gandharva-cāraṇaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O Mahārāja Parīkṣit! The Supreme Lord, affectionate to his devotees, on being praised by Dakṣa, appeared at that holy place known as Aghamarṣaṇa. The Lord's lotus feet rested on the shoulders of Garuḍa, and he possessed eight long, powerful arms. In his hands he held a disc, conchshell, sword, shield, arrow, bow, rope and club. His garments were yellow and his bodily hue was deep bluish. His eyes and face were very cheerful. His body was covered with a garland of flowers and his chest was decorated with the shining Kaustubha jewel and the mark of Śrīvatsa. On his head was a huge crown, on his feet were anklets, and decorating his ears were shining earrings resembling** ***makaras.*** **The Lord wore a golden belt on his waist, bracelets on his arms, rings on his fingers, and ankle bells on his feet. Having a form attractive the three worlds, the Lord of the three worlds was accompanied by great devotees like Nārada, Nanda and groups of** ***devatās.*** **He was praised by the Siddhas, Gandharvas and Cāraṇas.**

**Комментарий:**

Five and a half verses describe the Lord.

### Text 41

*na kiñcanodīrayitum aśakat tīvrayā mudā*  
*āpūrita-manodvārair hradinya iva nirjharaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Because his senses were filled with intense bliss, like waterfalls filling up rivers, he could not speak at all.**

**Комментарий:**

He could not speak, with his knowledge senses and mind (*manodvāraiḥ*) filled completely with intense (*tivrayā*) bliss, in the form of prema from his ātmā, like full streams of water pouring from a lake.

### Text 45

*brahmā bhavo bhavantaś ca manavo vibudheśvarāḥ*  
*vibhūtayo mama hy etā bhūtānāṁ bhūti-hetavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Lord Brahmā, Lord Śiva, the Manus, you Prajāpatis, the Manus, and the leaders of the** ***devatās*****, who are all my expansions, strive to increase powers of the living entities.**

**Комментарий:**

“Since you are without desire, why do you desire vibhūtis (powers)? You do so out of affection, being devotees. Brahmā and others who are my vibhūtis are causes of increase of powers (*bhūti*) in others.

### Text 46

*tapo me hṛdayaṁ brahmaṁs tanur vidyā kriyākṛtiḥ*  
*aṅgāni kratavo jātā dharma ātmāsavaḥ surāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***brāhmaṇa*****! Austerity is my heart, Vedic knowledge in the form of hymns and** ***mantras*** **constitutes my body, and daily and occasional rites are my form. Sacrifices are the various limbs of my body. The unseen good fortune proceeding from sacrifice constitutes my mind, and the** ***devatās*** **are my life airs.**

**Комментарий:**

Bhakti is my antaraṅga-śakti.

### Text 47

*aham evāsam evāgre nānyat kiñcāntaraṁ bahiḥ*  
*saṁjñāna-mātram avyaktaṁ prasuptam iva viśvataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I alone existed before the creation, I alone will exist after the creation, and I alone exist now. Nothing exists outside of me or inside of me. I am also pure consciousness, imperceptible to the senses, quiet as if sleeping, and all-pervading.**

**Комментарий:**

He shows that the devotee is the cause of his creation. I was concerned with the material world or lotus, not Vaikuṇṭha.

### Text 48

*mayy ananta-guṇe 'nante guṇato guṇa-vigrahaḥ*  
*yadāsīt tata evādyaḥ svayambhūḥ samabhūd ajaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When the universe came into existence from my material energy within the unlimited Lord of unlimited qualities, then Brahmā and others took birth within the universe.**

**Комментарий:**

He shows the devotee.

### Text 49-50

*sa vai yadā mahādevo mama vīryopabṛṁhitaḥ*  
*mene khilam ivātmānam udyataḥ svarga-karmaṇi*  
*atha me 'bhihito devas tapo 'tapyata dāruṇam*  
*nava viśva-sṛjo yuṣmān yenādāv asṛjad vibhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When the chief lord of the universe, Lord Brahmā, born from my energy, was attempting to create and thought himself incapable, he underwent extreme austerities as advised by me. Because of these austerities, Brahmā created nine persons, including you, to help him in the functions of creation.**

**Комментарий:**

*Vīryopabṛṁhitaḥ* means “born from my power.” Brahmā’s bhakti is shown. You can become a vibhūti because you are a devotee.

### Text 53

*tvatto 'dhastāt prajāḥ sarvā mithunī-bhūya māyayā*  
*madīyayā bhaviṣyanti hariṣyanti ca me balim*

**Перевод:**

**After this, captivated by my illusory energy, all the offspring will engage in intercourse and increase. They will also offer me presentations in devotion.**

**Комментарий:**

Just by pairing up, they will be born.

## Chapter Five

### Text 6-8

*uvāca cātha haryaśvāḥ kathaṁ srakṣyatha vai prajāḥ*  
*adṛṣṭvāntaṁ bhuvo yūyaṁ bāliśā bata pālakāḥ*  
*tathaika-puruṣaṁ rāṣṭraṁ bilaṁ cādṛṣṭa-nirgamam*  
*bahu-rūpāṁ striyaṁ cāpi pumāṁsaṁ puṁścalī-patim*  
*nadīm ubhayato vāhāṁ pañca-pañcādbhutaṁ gṛham*  
*kvacid dhaṁsaṁ citra-kathaṁ kṣaura-pavyaṁ svayaṁ bhrami*

**Перевод:**

**He said, “O Haryaśvas! Why should you create progeny? Though you are princes you are inexperienced and have not seen the end of this earth. There is one kingdom where only one man lives. There is a hole from which, having entered, no one emerges. There is a woman there with many forms. There is one man, husband of the unchaste woman. There is a river flowing in both directions, a wonderful homemade of twenty-five materials, a swan that vibrates various sounds, and an automatically revolving object made of sharp razors and thunderbolts.**

**Комментарий:**

Four verses are spoken by Nārada. There is an astonishing house made of twenty-five materials.

### Text 15

*tat-saṅga-bhraṁśitaiśvaryaṁ saṁsarantaṁ kubhāryavat*  
*tad-gatīr abudhasyeha kim asat-karmabhir bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**The man is the** ***jīva*** **who, like a householder with an unfaithful wife (intelligence), loses all his powers in her association. What is the use of insubstantial** ***karmas*** **performed by a person who does not know his destination?**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva has capacity, with a form of self-knowledge etc. but the powers are lost by association with intelligence.

### Text 17

*pañca-viṁśati-tattvānāṁ puruṣo 'dbhuta-darpaṇaḥ*  
*adhyātmam abudhasyeha kim asat-karmabhir bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**The house made of twenty-five elements is the person controlling the twenty-five elements. He is an astonishing mirror. What is the use of** ***karmas*** **performed by a person who cannot discern the Lord situated in the** ***ātmā*** **as his object of worship?**

**Комментарий:**

### Text 34

*duravabodha iva tavāyaṁ vihāra-yogo yad aśaraṇo 'śarīra idam anavekṣitāsmat-samavāya ātmanaivāvikriyamāṇena saguṇam aguṇaḥ sṛjasi pāsi harasi.*

**Перевод:**

**It is difficult to understand that you, though engaged in pastimes in the spiritual world, without a material shelter, without actions in a material body, without the assistance of the** ***devatās*****, and without material** ***guṇas,*** **create, maintain and destroy the universe made of** ***guṇas,*** **without transformation of your** ***svarūpa*****, though you are also the material elements.**

**Комментарий:**

Though you have pastimes in Vaikuṇṭha and are hard to understand, it is logically so, since you are beyond the guṇas. But you also have pastimes in the material world. That is beyond logic, since you are the opposite of the world full of material guṇas. That is explained in this verse.

A potter, endowed with guṇas, taking support of a place, using his body, makes a pot made of guṇas using clay and other materials. But you, without guṇas, without action, without taking support, by your will, create, maintain and destroy the world made of guṇas. The word *iva* indicates that because of his acintya-śakti it is not difficult to understand.

### Text 35

**Комментарий:**

They speak sarcastically of material persons to express the fact that they cannot determine this concerning the Lord. In the activities of creation and destruction, is the Lord like an ordinary jīva in saṁsāra, fallen in the creation of guṇas by ignorance, receiving results of good and bad actions in order to get forms of the avatāras as deva or animal? Or though the Lord has various forms, is he ātmārāma, full of knowledge?

### Text 36

*na hi virodha ubhayaṁ bhagavaty aparimita-guṇa-gaṇa īśvare 'navagāhya-māhātmye 'rvācīna-vikalpa-vitarka-vicāra-pramāṇābhāsa-kutarka-śāstra-kalilāntaḥkaraṇāśraya-duravagraha-vādināṁ vivādānavasara uparata-samasta-māyāmaye kevala evātma-māyām antardhāya ko nv artho durghaṭa iva bhavati svarūpa-dvayābhāvāt.*

**Перевод:**

**Those two conditions seen in you are not a contradiction. What is impossible for you, who are Bhagavān, full of six qualities, full of unlimited qualities, the supreme controller, whose glories cannot be understood by the non-devotee, who are beyond the arguments of stubborn philosophers whose hearts are disturbed by deliberating on scriptures without touching the truth, through speculation, conjecture, judgment and false proofs? You are beyond all material qualities, you are pure spirit, but you place your*****-māyā*** **in the middle*****.*** **Though you possess only one form, nothing is impossible for you.**

**Комментарий:**

They have spoken in order to mock those who claim that the Lord is made of vidyā-śakti, a function of māyā-śakti. They then speak the conclusion, their own view. These conditions are not a contradiction in the Lord, since he does not have two svarūpas—he does not have a conditioned and liberated state like the jīva in saṁsāra. It is not difficult for the Lord to be liberated though in a state bondage-like state, appearing as avatāras which are most attractive, since the Lord places in the middle his own śakti (*ātma-māyām*). He remains, while the śakti assists. For these reasons, it is not impossible for the Lord.

## Chapter Eleven

### Text 16

*śrī-hrī-dayā-kīrtibhir ujjhitaṁ tvāṁ*

**Перевод:**

### Text 25

*na nāka-pṛṣṭhaṁ na ca pārameṣṭhyaṁ*  
*na sārva-bhaumaṁ na rasādhipatyam*  
*na yoga-siddhīr apunar-bhavaṁ vā*  
*samañjasa tvā virahayya kāṅkṣe*

**Перевод:**

**O my Lord, source of all opportunities! I do not desire to enjoy in Dhruvaloka, the heavenly planets or the planet where Lord Brahmā resides, nor do I want to be the supreme ruler of all the earthly planets or the lower planetary systems. I do not desire to be the master of the powers of mystic** ***yoga*****, nor do I want liberation. Burning in separation from you, these things will not satisfy me.**

**Комментарий:**

Bhatki-rasāmṛta-sindhu [1.2.32](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/1.2.32):

*Nāka-pṛṣṭham* (ceiling of the sky) refers to Dhruva-loka. *Sarva-bhaumam* means attaining a great kingdom on earth like Pṛthu. *Rasādhipatyam* refers to ruling over the lower planets starting with Pātāla. “Because I must reject you (*tvā*) by accepting liberation, I do not desire liberation at all (*apunarbhavam).”* By placing the four desirable places (Dhruva-loka, Brahma-loka, earth and the lower planets) in order increasing insignificance, he indicates the greater value of the preceding place. Dhruva-loka is the best, since Viṣṇu resides there, but even that he rejects. The *yoga-siddhi* and liberation are placed after the lower planets. Of these two, *yoga-siddhi* is superior to liberation.

### Text 26

*ajāta-pakṣā iva mātaraṁ khagāḥ*  
*stanyaṁ yathā vatsatarāḥ kṣudh-ārtāḥ*  
*priyaṁ priyeva vyuṣitaṁ viṣaṇṇā*  
*mano 'ravindākṣa didṛkṣate tvām*

**Перевод:**

**O lotus-eyed Lord! As baby birds without developed wings always look for their mother to feed them, as small calves, distressed with hunger, desire to see the cow with milk in her udder, or as a morose wife, desires her husband who is away from home, I long to see you.**

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarbha 72:**

“Birds without wings” indicates a person without any other shelter and who does not have the ability to approach the Lord by himself. The mother has natural compassion and especially has compassion for her children. Thus the Lord has natural compassion, and has special compassion for his devotees. Because of this quality of the mother, the offspring have great affection for the mother. This indicates that the devotee has great desire to see the Lord because of his prīti for the Lord. But the small birds live and taste something else—such as insects. Since they do not crave solely for the mother, another example is given.

The calf desires to see the mother cow and her udder (though only the udder is mentioned). The udder and the mother are considered non-different. This is a better example than the previous one since the mother supplies the milk. The calf lives and tastes by the mother alone. The young calves, prevented by the master, cannot follow the mother cow. They are hungry because a long time has passed without their mother. This is a better example than the previous one. The cow also has great natural affection for the calves.

However a difference of cause and effect—the cow and its udder--makes this example imperfect like the example of the young birds and insects. Seeing the unsteadiness of the prīti in this example, another example is given. Though other meanings exist, when the word prīti is used for two people it is understood that they have natural, permanent love for each other. One sees that they die together in old age or when young. The woman becomes depressed, since her life depends on him alone, and desires to see the man, who is far away (*vyuṣitam).* Tasting him with her eyes, she longs for him. Similarly my mind desires to see you, the Lord. The actual subject is not mentioned. The mind is the agent. This indicates that the mind, without thinking about it, desires the Lord. This indicates steadiness of the prīti since it is natural. The mind is like a bee, living upon and relishing the great sweetness of the Lord.

### Text 27

*mamottamaśloka-janeṣu sakhyaṁ*  
*saṁsāra-cakre bhramataḥ sva-karmabhiḥ*  
*tvan-māyayātmātmaja-dāra-geheṣv*  
*āsakta-cittasya na nātha bhūyāt*

**Перевод:**

**Let me, wandering in the wheel of** ***saṁsāra*** **by my** ***karmas,*** **be attached to the devotees of the Lord. Let my mind not be attached to persons attached to their bodies, children, wives, and houses because of the influence of your external energy.**

**Комментарий:**

Thinking that it is impossible to have the good fortune of seeing the Lord, he will remain in this world. With tears he recites the next verse. Because of its expression of pure prema, the story of Vṛtra’s killing is mentioned in other Purāṇas among the characteristics of Bhāgavatam as an outstanding feature. *Vṛtrāsura-vadhopetaṁ tad bhāgavatam iṣyate:* it is called Bhāgavatam, which includes the story of Vṛtrāsura’s death. (Agni Purāṇa)

## Chapter Thirteen

### Text 4

**Комментарий:**

This happened after they heard the words of Vṛtra.

### Text 5

*indra uvāca*  
*strī-bhū-druma-jalair eno viśvarūpa-vadhodbhavam*  
*vibhaktam anugṛhṇadbhir vṛtra-hatyāṁ kva mārjmy aham*

**Перевод:**

**Indra said: I received extensive sinful reactions killing Viśvarūpa, but I was favored by the women, land, trees and water, and therefore I was able to divide the sin among them. But now, if I kill Vṛtrāsura, another** ***brāhmaṇa*****, how shall I free myself from the sinful reactions?**

**Комментарий:**

As he accrued sin by offending Bṛhaspati, he generated another sin by killing Viśvarūpa. This however is from the ordinary viewpoint. It was also seen that a portion of sin remained, because of killing Vṛtra. Indra personally experienced the remainder of sin, and was internally fearful. Externally he spoke in this way in order teach others as previously.

*śrī-śuka uvāca*

**Комментарий:**

For the good of the universe, for destroying his demonic disposition, they acted on the strength of the Lord’s order.

### Text 7

**Комментарий:**

Because the fault was an ābhāsa, the atonement they spoke of was also an ābhāsa. They spoke to explain to him what would be most powerful: worship Nārāyaṇa.

### Text 8-9

*brahma-hā pitṛ-hā go-ghno mātṛ-hācārya-hāghavān*

After killing even a** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **what to speak of a low rascal, by our performing a horse sacrifice you will not be touched by any sin.**

**Комментарий:**

Other sins which are destroyed are mentioned. The guru mentioned here is a teacher of the Vedas. All the sins are destroyed just by kīrtana. What to speak of many times uttering the Lord’s name while doing sacrifice. They also mentioned their own inclination to perform the sacrifice. They indicate their intention by mentioned killing a rascal (*khala*). There was no fault in this since they were thinking of the welfare of the universe and Indra with this advice.

Though the sin was not too visible in him, its presiding deity appeared in order to overpower him.

### Text 11

*tayendraḥ smāsahat tāpaṁ nirvṛtir nāmum āviśat*  
*hrīmantaṁ vācyatāṁ prāptaṁ sukhayanty api no guṇāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Following the advice of the** ***devatās,*** **Indra suffered and attained no happiness. Good qualities cannot give happiness to a person who has shame and infamy.**

**Комментарий:**

Attacked by the sin, Indra tolerated the pain. He was not conquered by it. But happiness did not come to him, since he was shamed.

### Text 14

*nabho gato diśaḥ sarvāḥ sahasrākṣo viśāmpate*  
*prāg-udīcīṁ diśaṁ tūrṇaṁ praviṣṭo nṛpa mānasam*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Indra first fled to the sky, then in all directions. At last he went to the northeast and quickly entered the Mānasa Sarovara.**

**Комментарий:**

He went into the sky to escape. He went in all directions. He ran far away and entered Mānasa.

### Text 15

*sa āvasat puṣkara-nāla-tantūn*  
*alabdha-bhogo yad ihāgni-dūtaḥ*  
*varṣāṇi sāhasram alakṣito 'ntaḥ*  
*sañcintayan brahma-vadhād vimokṣam*

**Перевод:**

**Always thinking of relief from the sinful reaction for killing a** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **Indra, invisible to everyone, lived for one thousand years in the subtle fibers of the stem of a lotus without food, since the messenger of Agni could not deliver his share of sacrifice in the water.**

**Комментарий:**

The messenger of Agni was his helper but could not survive in water. All others could not detect where he was. The messenger found him, but could not give the share of the sacrifice. Thus Indra could not eat. He always thought of the method of deliverance from the presiding deity of killing a brāhmaṇa. In other words he thought of the Lord. This is mentioned in verse 17.

### Text 16

*tāvat triṇākaṁ nahuṣaḥ śaśāsa*  
*vidyā-tapo-yoga-balānubhāvaḥ*  
*sa sampad-aiśvarya-madāndha-buddhir*  
*nītas tiraścāṁ gatim indra-patnyā*

**Перевод:**

**As long as King Indra lived in the water, Nahuṣa ruled the heavenly kingdom, due to his knowledge, austerity and mystic power. Nahuṣa, however, became blinded by pride in his wealth, and took the body of a snake because of the instruction of Indra’s wife.**

**Комментарий:**

Nahuṣa ruled in his place. The killing of Vṛtra took place in the seventh manvantara. However there is glorification of Indra for killing Namuci and others during the sixth manvantara. Some explain that the name belongs to another person.

### Text 17

*tato gato brahma-giropahūta*  
*ṛtambhara-dhyāna-nivāritāghaḥ*  
*pāpas tu digdevatayā hataujās*  
*taṁ nābhyabhūd avitaṁ viṣṇu-patnyā*

**Перевод:**

**When the reaction to the offense of committing sin on the strength of atonement was destroyed by meditation on the Lord, Indra was invited back to Svarga by the** ***brāhmaṇas.*** **The sin of killing a** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **depleted of its strength by Śiva, guardian of the northeast, could not overcome him, since he was also protected by Lakṣmī, who lives in the lotus.**

**Комментарий:**

The presiding deity of the sin, having lost strength because of the deity of the directions (Śiva), was no longer able to pursue him because of his meditation on Viṣṇu. When he took shelter of the Lord, how could the sin conquer him?

### Text 18

*taṁ ca brahmarṣayo 'bhyetya hayamedhena bhārata*  
*yathāvad dīkṣayāṁ cakruḥ puruṣārādhanena ha*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The** ***brāhmaṇa*** **sages approached him and had him properly perform a horse sacrifice along with worship of the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The sin that remained from public criticism was destroyed by the great sages. That is explained in three verses.

### Text 19-20

*athejyamāne puruṣe sarva-devamayātmani*  
*aśvamedhe mahendreṇa vitate brahma-vādibhiḥ*  
*sa vai tvāṣṭra-vadho bhūyān api pāpa-cayo nṛpa*  
*nītas tenaiva śūnyāya nīhāra iva bhānunā*

**Перевод:**

**When the sages performed the horse sacrifice and Indra worshipped the Supreme Lord, the sin of killing Vṛtrāsura was completely destroyed, like the sun dissipating fog.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses explain the result.

## Chapter Fourteen

### Text 2

*devānāṁ śuddha-sattvānām ṛṣīṇāṁ cāmalātmanām*  
*bhaktir mukunda-caraṇe na prāyeṇopajāyate*

**Перевод:**

***Bhakti*** **to the Lord’s lotus feet rarely develops even in the** ***devatās*** **with minds possessing** ***sattva-guṇa*** **and in pure sages.**

**Комментарий:**

Having described the rarity of knowledge of the Lord, easy access to devotee association is described. It is said in the following statements that sattva is the cause of jñāna:

*sattvāt sañjāyate jñānaṁ rajaso lobha eva ca*  
*pramāda-mohau tamaso bhavato ’jñānam eva ca \|\|*

**Knowledge arises from** ***sattva*****. Greed arises from** ***rajas*****. Inattention, confusion and ignorance arise from** ***tamas*****. (BG** [**14.17**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/14.17)**)**

*kaivalyaṁ sāttvikaṁ jñāna rajo vaikalpikaṁ ca yat*  
*prākṛtaṁ tāmasaṁ jñāna man-niṣṭhaṁ nirguṇaṁ smṛtam*

**Knowledge concerning the** ***jīva*** **apart from the body is in** ***sattva.*** **Knowledge with various options of doubt is in** ***rajas*****. Knowledge of material life is in** ***tamas.*** **Knowledge concerning me, however, is understood to be beyond the** ***guṇas*****. (SB** [**11.25.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/25.24)**)**

*pārthivād dāruṇo dhūmas tasmād agnis trayīmayaḥ \|*  
*tamasas tu rajas tasmāt sattvaṁ yad brahma-darśanam \|\|*

**Smoke is superior to dull wood, and fire, sacred to the Vedas, is superior to smoke. Similarly** ***rajas*** **is superior to** ***tamas*****, and** ***sattva*** **is superior to** ***rajas*****, since it is favorable for realizing the Lord. (SB** [**1.2.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/2.24)**)**

And even if sattva is present, knowledge of the Lord (*bhagavaj-jñāna*) does not arise. That is stated in the present verse and in verse 5. Verse 1 shows that sattva may be absent, and bhakti may be present. Thus sattva is not the cause of bhakti.

One may even get the association of Nārada and others in a previous birth. The cause of bhakti is association with devotees, who are the vessels holding the fragrant, sweet mercy of the Lord:

*naiṣāṁ matis tāvad urukramāṅghriṁ*  
*spṛśaty anarthāpagamo yad-arthaḥ*  
*mahīyasāṁ pāda-rajo-'bhiṣekaṁ*  
*niṣkiñcanānāṁ na vṛṇīta yāvat*

**As long as their intelligence does not accept the dust from the feet of devotees having no material desires, the intelligence of these people will not realize the lotus feet of the Lord, whose secondary goal is destruction of** ***saṁsāra. (*****SB** [**7.5.32**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/5.32)**)**

That association is greater than being nirguṇa. It is the ultimate nirguṇa state:

*tulayāma lavenāpi na svargaṁ nāpunar-bhavam \|*  
*bhagavat-saṅgi-saṅgasya martyānāṁ kim utāśiṣaḥ \|\|*

**Let us not compare even a particle of devotee association to Svarga or liberation, what to speak of any blessings in this world. (SB** [**1.18.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/18.13)**)**

At the beginning of the Seventh Canto Parīkṣit asks if the Lord has real mercy to devatās who are in the guṇas:

*samaḥ priyaḥ suhṛd brahman bhūtānāṁ bhagavān svayam*  
*indrasyārthe kathaṁ daityān avadhīd viṣamo yathā*

**O** ***brāhmaṇa!*** **How did the Lord, the best friend of all beings, who is equal and affectionate to all beings, kill the demons for Indra as if partial? (SB** [**7.1.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.1)**)**

The association of great devotees is clearly beyond the guṇas, since this is shown in examples like Prahlāda.

### Text 4

*prāyo mumukṣavas teṣāṁ kecanaiva dvijottama*  
*mumukṣūṇāṁ sahasreṣu kaścin mucyeta sidhyati*

**Перевод:**

**O best of the** ***brāhmaṇas*****! Out of those follow** ***dharma,*** **only a few desire liberation. Among many thousands who desire liberation, one person becomes a** ***jivanmukta*** **and out of many such persons, only one attains the spiritual world.**

**Комментарий:**

One becomes a jīvanmukta (*mucyeta*). But it is said that the jīvanmukta can also fall:

*ye ’nye ’ravindākṣa vimukta-māninas*  
*tvayy asta-bhāvād aviśuddha-buddhayaḥ*  
*āruhya kṛcchreṇa paraṁ padaṁ tataḥ*  
*patanty adho ’nādṛta-yuṣmad-aṅghrayaḥ*

**O lotus-eyed Lord, although nondevotees, having impure intelligence because of not having devotion to you, think that they are liberated and become** ***jīvanmuktas*** **after severe austerities and penances, they fall down from their position of imagined superiority because they have no regard for the lotus feet of you and your devotees. (SB** [**10.2.32**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/2.32)**)**

Some of them attain a position of having no obstacles at all.

BRS [1.2.52](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/1.2.52):

Among the liberated (*muktānām*) who are still in their material bodies, but without bodily identification, and among those who have attained spiritual bodies (*siddhānām)* in the spiritual world (*sālokyā* etc.), even among ten millions of such persons, a person who desires only service to Nārāyaṇa is very rare.

### Text 21

*ātmanaḥ prīyate nātmā parataḥ svata eva vā*  
*lakṣaye 'labdha-kāmaṁ tvāṁ cintayā śabalaṁ mukham*

**Перевод:**

**O King Citraketu! Your mind is not pleased due to yourself or others. I see that your face is pale with worry, because of not achieving your desires.**

**Комментарий:**

Your mind is not pleased because your face is pale with worry.

### Text 22

*evaṁ vikalpito rājan viduṣā munināpi saḥ*  
*praśrayāvanato 'bhyāha prajā-kāmas tato munim*

**Перевод:**

**O King Parīkṣit! Questioned by the knowledgeable sage in this way, King Citraketu, desiring a son, bent low in great humility and spoke to the great sage as follows.**

**Комментарий:**

Being subject to various arrangements by the sage (*vikalpitaḥ*), the king spoke.

### Text 24

*tathāpi pṛcchato brūyāṁ brahmann ātmani cintitam*  
*bhavato viduṣaś cāpi coditas tvad-anujñayā*

**Перевод:**

**O great** ***brāhmaṇa*****! Inspired by your order, let me explain the worry in my mind to you, though you know everything but are asking me.**

**Комментарий:**

He will speak with the sage’s permission (*coditaḥ*), not with pride.

### Text 27

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*ity arthitaḥ sa bhagavān kṛpālur brahmaṇaḥ sutaḥ*  
*śrapayitvā caruṁ tvāṣṭraṁ tvaṣṭāram ayajad vibhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: In response to the request of Mahārāja Citraketu, the merciful sage, who was born of Lord Brahmā's mind, performed a Tvaṣtṛ sacrifice for the** ***devatā*** **Tvāṣtā, after cooking a rice offering.**

**Комментарий:**

The sage was merciful. He desired that the king become a devotee by producing detachment in him, on the pretext of giving him a son. He later says:

*tadaiva te paraṁ jñāna dadāmi gṛham āgataḥ*  
*jñātvānyābhiniveśaṁ te putram eva dadāmy aham*

**I would have given you spiritual knowledge when I first came to your house, but I gave you a son, knowing that you were absorbed in material objects. (SB** [**6.15.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/15.20)**)**

### Text 29

*athāha nṛpatiṁ rājan bhavitaikas tavātmajaḥ*  
*harṣa-śoka-pradas tubhyam iti brahma-suto yayau*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, the great sage told the King, "O great King, now you will have a son who will be the cause of both jubilation and lamentation." The sage then left.**

**Комментарий:**

The child would give both lamentation and joy in order to show the king his fault.

### Text 61

*evaṁ kaśmalam āpannaṁ naṣṭa-saṁjñam anāyakam*  
*jñātvāṅgirā nāma ṛṣir ājagāma sanāradaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When the great sage Aṅgirā understood that the kingdom was without a leader because the King was almost dead and that the citizens had lost consciousness because of the calamity, he went there with Nārada.**

**Комментарий:**

The kingdom was without a king because of his uncontrolled lamentation (*anāyakam*).

## Chapter Fifteen

### Text 8

*deha-dehi-vibhāgo 'yam aviveka-kṛtaḥ purā*  
*jāti-vyakti-vibhāgo 'yaṁ yathā vastuni kalpitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just as divisions of general and specific are imagined in the absolute, so divisions of body and soul with mutual dependence are imagined because of beginningless ignorance.**

**Комментарий:**

For the pure ātmā, there is no concept of possessing a body (*dehi*). Thus he does not think of himself as a body. Similarly there are no divisions of general and specific in Paramātmā, the supreme cause (*vastuni*).

### Text 10

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*kau yuvāṁ jñāna-sampannau mahiṣṭhau ca mahīyasām*  
*avadhūtena veṣeṇa gūḍhāv iha samāgatau*

**Перевод:**

**King Citraketu said: You two, endowed with knowledge, greatest among the great, have arrived here in the dress of mendicants.**

**Комментарий:**

They dressed in this way to hide their identities.

### Text 11

*caranti hy avanau kāmaṁ brāhmaṇā bhagavat-priyāḥ*  
*mādṛśāṁ grāmya-buddhīnāṁ bodhāyonmatta-liṅginaḥ*

**Перевод:**

***Brāhmaṇas*** **who are Vaiṣṇavas, the dearest servants of the Lord, dressed like madmen, wander the earth at will to enlighten materialist persons like me.**

**Комментарий:**

Brāhmaṇas are dear to the Lord who is affection to the brāhmaṇas in general, and who is particularly affectionate to devotees.

### Text 12-15

*kumāro nārada ṛbhur aṅgirā devalo 'sitaḥ*  
*apāntaratamā vyāso mārkaṇḍeyo 'tha gautamaḥ*  
*vasiṣṭho bhagavān rāmaḥ kapilo bādarāyaṇiḥ*  
*durvāsā yājñavalkyaś ca jātukarṇas tathāruṇiḥ*  
*romaśaś cyavano data āsuriḥ sapatañjaliḥ*  
*ṛṣir veda-śirā dhaumyo muniḥ pañcaśikhas tathā*  
*hiraṇyanābhaḥ kauśalyaḥ śrutadeva ṛtadhvajaḥ*  
*ete pare ca siddheśāś caranti jñāna-hetavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Perfect persons like the following wander the surface of the earth to instruct knowledge: Sanat-kumāra, Nārada, Ṛbhu, Aṅgirā, Devala, Asita, Apāntaratamā, Vyāsadeva, Mārkaṇḍeya, Gautama, Vasiṣṭha, Bhagavān Paraśurāma, Kapila, Śukadeva, Durvāsā, Yājñavalkya, Jātukarṇa and Aruṇi, Romaśa, Cyavana, Dattātreya, Āsuri, Patañjali, the great sage Dhaumya who is like the head of the Vedas, the sage Pañcaśikha, Hiraṇyanābha, Kauśalya, Śrutadeva and Ṛtadhvaja.**

**Комментарий:**

Four verses go together. Vyāsa and others had not yet appeared. They are mentioned from scriptural references to them.

### Text 18-19

*itthaṁ tvāṁ putra-śokena magnaṁ tamasi dustare*  
*atad-arham anusmṛtya mahāpuruṣa-gocaram*  
*anugrahāya bhavataḥ prāptāv āvām iha prabho*  
*brahmaṇyo bhagavad-bhakto nāvāsāditum arhasi*

**Перевод:**

**Remembering you, who are unsuitably drowning in insurmountable ignorance because of sorrow for your son and who can recognize great devotees, we have come here to give mercy to you. The devotee of the Lord, who serves** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **should not lament.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. You recognize devotees (*mahāpuruṣa-gocaram*). He obtained the seed of bhakti by the power of previous association of the sage (*bhagavad-bhaktaḥ*).

### Text 20

*tadaiva te paraṁ jñāna dadāmi gṛham āgataḥ*  
*jñātvānyābhiniveśaṁ te putram eva dadāmy aham*

**Перевод:**

**I would have given you spiritual knowledge when I first came to your house, but I gave you a son, knowing that you were absorbed in material objects.**

**Комментарий:**

*Dadām*i means “I would have given.” Tenses can be interchanged.

### Text 27

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*etāṁ mantropaniṣadaṁ pratīccha prayato mama*  
*yāṁ dhārayan sapta-rātrād draṣṭā saṅkarṣaṇaṁ vibhum*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said: Attentively receive from me a** ***mantra*** **embodying the Upaniṣads. Meditating on it, after seven nights you will be able to see Lord Saṅkarṣaṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Nārada was not satisfied with what Aṅgirasā had taught. Thus he spoke. Seven days means after some days.

### Text 28

*yat-pāda-mūlam upasṛtya narendra pūrve*  
*śarvādayo bhramam imaṁ dvitayaṁ visṛjya*  
*sadyas tadīyam atulānadhikaṁ mahitvaṁ*  
*prāpur bhavān api paraṁ na cirād upaiti*

**Перевод:**

**My dear King, in former days Lord Śiva and other** ***devatās,*** **taking shelter of the lotus feet of Saṅkarṣaṇa, immediately got free from the illusion of duality and achieved unequaled and unsurpassed glory. You will very soon attain that very same position.**

**Комментарий:**

The special effect of the teaching is explained.

## Chapter Sixteen

### Text 1

*śrī-bādarāyaṇir uvāca*  
*atha deva-ṛṣī rājan samparetaṁ nṛpātmajam*  
*darśayitveti hovāca jñātīnām anuśocatām*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O King! Nārada brought the dead son into the vision of all the lamenting relatives and then spoke as follows.**

**Комментарий:**

He showed him the body in its afterlife form, which was manifested by his power of yoga.

### Text 3

*kalevaraṁ svam āviśya śeṣam āyuḥ suhṛd-vṛtaḥ*  
*bhuṅkṣva bhogān pitṛ-prattān adhitiṣṭha nṛpāsanam*

**Перевод:**

**Reenter your body and enjoy the remainder of your life, surrounded by your friends and relatives. Accept the royal throne and all the enjoyments given by your father.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī says he had a remainder of life because he died by accident. This was caused by a particular sin, which reduced his life span. Since it would be contrary to enter his old body, Śrīdhara Svāmī says he was situated in preta-śarīra when he began speaking in the next verse.

### Text 5

*bandhu-jñāty-ari-madhyastha- mitrodāsīna-vidviṣaḥ*  
*sarva eva hi sarveṣāṁ bhavanti kramaśo mithaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One person becomes a relative through marriage, a relative of one’s father, an enemy, a false friend, a well-wisher, and becomes indifferent to or envious of another person in different successive births.**

**Комментарий:**

The *Madhya-sthā* meets with the person but both show false friendship. The *udāsina* does not meet the person at all.

### Text 9

*eṣa nityo 'vyayaḥ sūkṣma eṣa sarvāśrayaḥ svadṛk*  
*ātmamāyā-guṇair viśvam ātmānaṁ sṛjate prabhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is eternal, without change, beyond perception. He is the shelter of all things and self-manifesting. The Lord creates himself as the universe through the** ***guṇas*** **of his material** ***māyā.***

**Комментарий:**

Having described the jīva as dependent, he describes in three verses the supreme Lord to complete the description. *Eṣaḥ* means the supreme Lord, who is known through śruti and logic. The word is repeated for emphasis. *Sūkṣma* means hard to understand.

### Text 11

*nādatta ātmā hi guṇa na doṣaṁ na kriyā-phalam*  
*udāsīnavad āsīnaḥ parāvara-dṛg īśvaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord does not recognize others as friend or enemy, nor is he subject to karmic results. He is situated as if neutral, witnessing the good and bad actions of the** ***jīva.***

**Комментарий:**

*Ātmā* means Paramātmā.

### Text 14

*bāla-ghnyo vrīḍitās tatra bāla-hatyā-hata-prabhāḥ*  
*bāla-hatyā-vrataṁ cerur brāhmaṇair yan nirūpitam*  
*yamunāyāṁ maharaja smarantyo dvija-bhāṣitam*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Queen Kṛtyadyuti's co-wives, ashamed of killing the child, lost their bodily luster, and, remembering the words of Aṅgirā, atoned for the killing of the child on the Yamunā River as directed by** ***brāhmaṇas*****.**

**Комментарий:**

Three lines go together.

### Text 18-19

*oṁ namas tubhyaṁ bhagavate vāsudevāya dhīmahi*  
*pradyumnāyāniruddhāya namaḥ saṅkarṣaṇāya ca*  
*namo vijñāna-mātrāya paramānanda-mūrtaye*  
*ātmārāmāya śāntāya nivṛtta-dvaita-dṛṣṭaye*

**Перевод:**

**We offer respectful obeisances unto Vāsudeva, Pradyumna, Aniruddha and Saṅkarṣaṇa, to the Lord who is endowed with** ***cit-śakti,*** **who has a form of the highest bliss, who delights in himself, who is full of peace, and who is not attached to the material world.**

**Комментарий:**

He offers respects to the Lord as Bhagavān. Listing the caturvyūha shows that these are his worshipable deities. He offers respects to the Lord as Brahman (*vijñāna-mātrāya*), the Lord whose svarūpa is the highest bliss (*paramānanda-mūrtaye*), who does not glance elsewhere.

### Text 20

*Ātmānandānubhūtyaiva nyasta-śakty-ūrmaye namaḥ*  
*hṛṣīkeśāya mahate namas te 'nanta-mūrtaye*

**Перевод:**

**We offer respects to you who are devoid of the waves of** ***māyā*** **since you enjoy in yourself. We offer respects to you who control all the senses, to you, the cause of the universe, who is the universe.**

**Комментарий:**

He shows that the Lord is not completely different from everything else. He is devoid of the disturbance of other śaktis by his experience of the bliss of his svarūpa (*ātmānandānubhūtyā*). This indicates his svarūpa-śakti, different from his svarūpa but accompanying it (instrumental case). He offers respects to the Lord as Paramātmā (*hṛṣīkeśāya*) and as the cause of the universe (*mahate*), and as the universe (*anānta-mūrtaye*).

### Text 21

*vacasy uparate 'prāpya ya eko manasā saha*  
*anāma-rūpaś cin-mātraḥ so 'vyān naḥ sad-asat-paraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord who is one entity with no form, who is pure consciousness, who is the cause of the material world and unapproachable by words and mind protect us!**

**Комментарий:**

He praises the Lord as Brahman again in two verses.

### Text 23

*yan na spṛśanti na vidur mano-buddhīndriyāsavaḥ*  
*antar bahiś ca vitataṁ vyomavat tan nato 'smy aham*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord who spreads inside and outside like the air, and whom the mind, intelligence, senses and life airs cannot touch or know.**

**Комментарий:**

He praises the Lord as Paramātmā in two verses.

### Text 24

*dehendriya-prāṇa-mano-dhiyo 'mī*  
*yad-aṁśa-viddhāḥ pracaranti karmasu*  
*naivānyadā lauham ivāprataptaṁ*  
*sthāneṣu tad draṣṭrapadeśam eti*

**Перевод:**

**Just as unheated iron cannot burn, the body, senses, life airs and intelligence move to their objects only when endowed with consciousness by the Lord. The Lord allows the** ***jīva,*** **who can see in** **waking and dream states, to see him--by endowing him with** ***cit-śakti.***

**Комментарий:**

**Bhagavad Sandarbha 17:**

In the Advaita commentary on Brahma-sūtras ([1.1.5](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/1.1.5)) the Sāṅkhya philosophy is criticized. “Just as, because of fire, a piece of iron has the ability to burn, because of the witness which glances at pradhāna, pradhāna develops the power to create a universe. If that is the case, since Brahman glancing at pradhāna is the cause, Brahman is the principle cause of creating the universe.”

Śruti says:

*na tatra sūryo bhāti na candra-tārakaṁ*  
*nemā vidyuto bhānti kuto ’yam agniḥ*  
*tad eva bhāntam anubhāti sarvaṁ*  
*tasya bhāsā sarvam idaṁ vibhāti*

**There, the sun, the moon, the stars, fire and lightning do not shine. They all shine only by following the shining Lord. His light illuminates all these. (Kaṭha Upaniṣad** [**5.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/katha-bs/5.15)**)**

*ko hy evānyāt kaḥ prāṇyāt yad eṣa ākāśa ānando na syāt*

**Who could breathe without this Brahman, if this blissful Brahman were not present? (Taittarīya Upaniṣad** [**2.7.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.7.1)**)**

*cakṣuṣaś cakṣur uta śrotrasya śrotram*

**He is the eye of the eye, the ear of the ear. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**4.4.18**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/4.4.18)**)**

The commentary then says, “It could be proposed that the jīva knows, because it is a seer. That is not so. The jīva is a seer in name only *(draṣṭrapadeśam*) in waking, dreaming and deep sleep.”

*nānyo jīvo nāmāsti nānyo’to’sti draṣṭā:*

**What is called the jīva is not independent (*****anyaḥ*****). Rather it has a similar nature to Brahman. There is no other seer than the Lord who sees all. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**3.17.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/3.17.23)**)**

Or another meaning of the last line is “The Lord knows the jīva defined as a seer. The jīva cannot know the Lord” for it is said:

*ekam ekatarābhāve yadā nopalabhāmahe \|*  
*tritayaṁ tatra yo veda sa ātmā svāśrayāśrayaḥ \|\|*

**Since we cannot perceive one item in absence of one of the other items (subtle sense, sense deity and gross form), he who is independent of these three, is the ātmā. But the shelter of the ātmā is the Paramātmā. (SB** [**2.10.9**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/10.9)**)**

### Text 25

*oṁ namo bhagavate mahā-puruṣāya mahānubhāvāya mahā-vibhūti-pataye sakala-sātvata-parivṛḍha-nikara-kara-kamala-kuḍmalopalālita-caraṇāravinda-yugala parama-parameṣṭhin namas te.*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord whose lotus feet are affectionately massaged by the lotus hands of groups of excellent devotees! O supreme among supreme lords! We offer respects to the great person, the great authority, the Lord of great powers.**

**Комментарий:**

Bhagavān is directly praised, through worship of Saṅkarṣaṇa. Bhagavān yields the highest results, since he is shown to be the object of worship in the Sātvata scriptures.

### Text 35

*tava vibhavaḥ khalu bhagavan*  
*jagad-udaya-sthiti-layādīni*  
*viśva-sṛjas te 'ṁśāṁśās*  
*tatra mṛṣā spardhanti pṛthag abhimatyā*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! Creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe is your greatness. The creators of the universe, portions of portions of you, compete in vain, thinking themselves separate from you.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahmā, Śiva and Indra (*viśva-sṛjaḥ*) are your very small aṁśas (*aṁśāṁśaḥ*).

### Text 37

*kṣity-ādibhir eṣa kilāvṛtaḥ*  
*saptabhir daśa-guṇottarair aṇḍa-kośaḥ*  
*yatra pataty aṇu-kalpaḥ*  
*sahāṇḍa-koṭi-koṭibhis tad anantaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The universe covered by seven layers, each ten times thicker than the previous layer, wanders about like an atom along with billions of other universes inside of you. Therefore you are unlimited.**

**Комментарий:**

Each layer is ten times thicker than the previous layer.

### Text 40

*jitam ajita tadā bhavatā*  
*yadāha bhāgavataṁ dharmam anavadyam*  
*niṣkiñcanā ye munaya*  
*ātmārāmā yam upāsate 'pavargāya*

**Перевод:**

**O unconquered Lord! When you explained pure** ***bhakti,*** **the path which the devotees who meditate on you and enjoy within you follow for attaining** ***prema,*** **the devotees were conquered by you.**

**Комментарий:**

You spoke bhakti whose excellence was revealed by you (*jitam*). Śrīdhara Svāmī accepts *bhagavan* instead of *bhavatā*, which is accepted by others.

### Text 42

*kaḥ kṣemo nija-parayoḥ*  
*kiyān vārthaḥ sva-para-druhā dharmeṇa*  
*sva-drohāt tava kopaḥ*  
*para-sampīḍayā ca tathādharmaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**What is the benefit of thinking of oneself and others to be objects of one’s possession? That** ***dharma*** **causes violence to oneself and others, for by inflicting pain on one’s own body in the form of austerities in order to afflict enemies one develops anger.**

**Комментарий:**

The person by committing violence to himself cannot deliver himself from the ocean of suffering. He is the cause of his saṁsāra. The word *ca* indicates violence to others including animals. *Kopaḥ* means strong anger. *Adharma* means impure dharma.

### Text 47

*namas tubhyaṁ bhagavate*  
*sakala-jagat-sthiti-layodayeśāya*  
*duravasitātma-gataye*  
*kuyogināṁ bhidā paramahaṁsāya*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to you, the Supreme Lord, the master of creation, maintenance and destruction of all universes, the supreme swan, whose position is hard to understand for false** ***yogīs*** **who see with difference*****.***

**Комментарий:**

His position is hard to discern for false yogīs, since they see in terms of various types of worship (*bhidā*), in terms of modified bhakti. *Paramahaṁsāya* can be related to the next verse. It means that the Lord plays in the lotus of the heart in a pure form.

### Text 48

*yaṁ vai śvasantam anu viśva-sṛjaḥ śvasanti*  
*yaṁ cekitānam anu cittaya uccakanti*  
*bhū-maṇḍalaṁ sarṣapāyati yasya mūrdhni*  
*tasmai namo bhagavate 'stu sahasra-mūrdhne*

**Перевод:**

**May I offer respects to the Supreme Lord with a thousand hoods, after whose actions the creators of the universe can use their action senses, after whose perception, the knowledge senses can gather knowledge, and on whose head Bhū-maṇḍala rests like a mustard seed!**

**Комментарий:**

The senses (*cittayaḥ*) see (*uccakanti*) his form. By a portion of his jñāna-śakti the senses obtain their knowledge.

### Text 51

*ahaṁ vai sarva-bhūtāni bhūtātmā bhūta-bhāvanaḥ*  
*śabda-brahma paraṁ brahma mamobhe śāśvatī tanū*

**Перевод:**

**I am all beings. I am the soul of all beings. I am the cause of all beings. The scripture and impersonal Brahman are my two eternal forms.**

**Комментарий:**

Because śabda-brahma is associated with the Lord, it is considered the Lord’s body. Parabrahman is considered to be his body, since it is his aṁśa.

### Text 52

*loke vitatam ātmānaṁ lokaṁ cātmani santatam*  
*ubhayaṁ ca mayā vyāptaṁ mayi caivobhayaṁ kṛtam*

**Перевод:**

**Paraṁ brahman exists as the shelter of the world. The world takes shelter of it eternally. Both are pervaded by me. And both are defined by my knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

He explains this. Paraṁ brahma (*ātmānam*) exists as the shelter (*vitatam*). The world takes shelter (*santatam*) of param brahma, since para brahma is the Lord’s impersonal manifestation. Both are defined by the Lord’s knowledge (*ubayam kṛtam*).

### Text 53-54

*yathā suṣuptaḥ puruṣo viśvaṁ paśyati cātmani*  
*ātmānam eka-deśa-sthaṁ manyate svapna utthitaḥ*  
*evaṁ jāgaraṇādīni jīva-sthānāni cātmanaḥ*  
*māyā-mātrāṇi vijñāya tad-draṣṭāraṁ paraṁ smaret*

**Перевод:**

**A sleeping person in a dream sees many objects of a dream world within himself, but on waking up realizes that his body is in one particular place and that the dream experience was unreal. Similarly, the states of waking, sleeping and dreaming-- conditions of the** ***jīva--*****are caused by a covering of material intelligence and should be realized to be only** ***māyā.*** **One should therefore meditate only on the supreme seer of all these states, the Paramātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

If one becomes detached from the material universe, what happens? He teaches knowledge of the Lord, along with knowledge of the svarūpa of the pure jīva in two verse. The meaning is this. A person dreaming enters deep sleep and then again sees a dream. By special illusion, he sees mountains and forests (*viśvam*) within himself. He accepts them as his perceptions (*ātmani*). Then, leaving the dream, awaking, he sees himself in one place in a bed (*eka-deśa-sthitam*), different from his vision with mountains and forests. Understanding that the states of waking, dreaming and deep sleep are the Lord’s (*ātmanaḥ*) māyā, situated in the jīva (*jīva-sthānāni*), he should remember me (*param*), the seer of the pure jīva.

### Text 55

*yena prasuptaḥ puruṣaḥ svāpaṁ vedātmanas tadā*  
*sukhaṁ ca nirguṇaṁ brahma tam ātmānam avehi mām*

**Перевод:**

**Know the Paramātmā, by whom the sleeping person knows his sleeping condition and in deep sleep is able to know the happiness of Brahman without objects or qualities.**

**Комментарий:**

This is made clear. Know Brahman, by which the sleeping person knows the nature of dreams, caused by special portion of the great cit-śakti within the jīva, within the dwelling of ahaṅkāra, by which he knows the happiness of a nirguṇa condition (deep sleep). And know me, the Paramātmā (*ātmānām*), the form of Bhagavān. This verse refers to *vadanti tat tattva vidas.* (SB [1.2.11](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/2.11))

### Text 56

*ubhayaṁ smarataḥ puṁsaḥ prasvāpa-pratibodhayoḥ*  
*anveti vyatiricyeta taj jñānaṁ brahma tat param*

**Перевод:**

**That which remains during the waking and deep sleep states of the person observing those states, and which is different from them, is the** ***jīva.*** **Superior to the** ***jīva*** **is Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Thus knowledge of jīva culminates in Brahman. *Taj jñānam* refers to the pure jīva. This culminates in (*tat param*) Brahman.

### Text 57

*yad etad vismṛtaṁ puṁso mad-bhāvaṁ bhinnam ātmanaḥ*  
*tataḥ saṁsāra etasya dehād deho mṛter mṛtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**A person who forgets that my existence is different from the** ***jīva*** **continues in** ***saṁsāra*****, taking body after body, death after death.**

**Комментарий:**

The fault of not knowing the Lord is described. Because (*yat*) Brahman, my existence, different from the jīva, is forgotten by the jīva, through beginningless ignorance, the jīva continues in saṁsāra.

*bhayaṁ dvitīyābhiniveśataḥ syād*  
*īśād apetasya viparyayo 'smṛtiḥ*  
*tan-māyayāto budha ābhajet taṁ*  
*bhaktyaikayeśaṁ guru-devate ātmā*

**For the** ***jīva*** **averse to the Lord, there will be** ***saṁsāra*** **consisting of identity with body and lack of identity with the soul, because of his absorption in the material coverings on the soul, arising from the Lord’s** ***māyā.*** **Therefore, the intelligent person, taking** ***guru*** **as his Lord and very self, should fully worship the Lord with pure** ***bhakti.*** **(SB** [**11.2.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/2.37)**)**

### Text 61-62

*evaṁ viparyayaṁ buddhvā nṛṇāṁ vijñābhimāninām*  
*ātmanaś ca gatiṁ sūkṣmāṁ sthāna-traya-vilakṣaṇām*  
*dṛṣṭa-śrutābhir mātrābhir nirmuktaḥ svena tejasā*  
*jñāna-vijñāna-santṛpto mad-bhaktaḥ puruṣo bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**Understanding the attainment of opposite results for persons doing** ***karma,*** **and understanding the subtle state of spiritual realization beyond the states of waking, sleeping and deep sleep, freeing oneself from all sense objects seen and heard, and being satisfied with scriptural knowledge and realization by one’s** ***sādhana,*** **one should become my devotee.**

**Комментарий:**

The previous description is summarized in two verses. One should see the *verse ātmārāmaś ca munayaḥ*. (SB [1.7.10](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/7.10))

### Text 63

*etāvān eva manujair yoga-naipuṇya-buddhibhiḥ*  
*svārthaḥ sarvātmanā jñeyo yat parātmaika-darśanam*

**Перевод:**

**Humans whose intelligence is fixed in skillful practice of** ***yoga*** **should understand by all efforts this goal consisting only of realization of Paramātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

Realization of the Lord is praised. The goal which gives realization only of the Lord (*parātmaika-darśanam*) should be known.

### Text 64

*tvam etac chraddhayā rājann apramatto vaco mama*  
*jñāna-vijñāna-sampanno dhārayann āśu sidhyasi*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Accepting my words with faith, being attentive, and being endowed with scriptural knowledge and realization, you will quickly attain perfection.**

**Комментарий:**

In verse 50, the Lord stated the same thing: *saṁsiddho ‘’si.* You will now accomplish this (*sidhyasi*). The present is used to express the near future. You should be attentive and understand my words. He indicates future obstructions.

## Chapter Seventeen

### Text 2-3

*sa lakṣaṁ varṣa-lakṣāṇām avyāhata-balendriyaḥ*  
*stūyamāno mahā-yogī munibhiḥ siddha-cāraṇaiḥ*  
*kulācalendra-droṇīṣu nānā-saṅkalpa-siddhiṣu*  
*reme vidyādhara-strībhir gāpayan harim īśvaram*

**Перевод:**

**Praised by great sages and by the inhabitants of Siddhaloka and Cāraṇaloka, Citraketu, the most powerful** ***yogī,*** **with bodily strength and senses free from deterioration, while chanting the glories of the Supreme Lord, enjoyed life with the women of Vidyādhara-loka for a hundred thousand years within the valleys of Sumeru Mountain, which is the place of perfection for various kinds of mystic power.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together.

### Text 4-5

*ekadā sa vimānena viṣṇu-dattena bhāsvatā*  
*giriśaṁ dadṛśe gacchan parītaṁ siddha-cāraṇaiḥ*  
*āliṅgyāṅkīkṛtāṁ devīṁ bāhunā muni-saṁsadi*  
*uvāca devyāḥ śṛṇvantyā jahāsoccais tad-antike*

**Перевод:**

**One time while King Citraketu was traveling in outer space on a brilliant airplane given to him by Lord Viṣṇu, he saw Lord Śiva surrounded by Siddhas and Cāraṇas, embracing Pārvatī on his lap with his arm in an assembly of sages. Citraketu laughed loudly and spoke within the hearing of Pārvatī.**

**Комментарий:**

The bad effect of a great offense committed previously is described.

### Text 9

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*bhagavān api tac chrutvā prahasyāgādha-dhīr nṛpa*  
*tūṣṇīṁ babhūva sadasi sabhyāś ca tad-anuvratāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O King! On hearing Citraketu's statement, powerful Śiva, whose knowledge is fathomless, simply smiled and remained silent. The members of the assembly followed his example.**

**Комментарий:**

The members of the assembly remained silent, in accordance with his intention (*tad-anuvrataḥ*).

### Text 10

*ity atad-vīrya-viduṣi bruvāṇe bahv-aśobhanam*  
*ruṣāha devī dhṛṣṭāya nirjitātmābhimānine*

**Перевод:**

**When Citraketu, not knowing the prowess of Lord Śiva, spoke inauspicious words, Pārvatī angrily spoke to the proud Citraketu, who thought himself in control of his senses.**

**Комментарий:**

Devi could not tolerate the offense. Śiva covered his powers, since he acted like an ordinary person. Citraketu did not know that he was a great devotee (*atad-vīrya-viduṣi*). The previous verse describes Śiva as *agādha-dhiḥ.*

### Text 15

*ataḥ pāpīyasīṁ yonim āsurīṁ yāhi durmate*  
*yatheha bhūyo mahatāṁ na kartā putra kilbiṣam*

**Перевод:**

**O envious fool! Dear son! Take birth immediately in a sinful family of demons so that you will not commit such an offense again toward exalted persons in this world.**

**Комментарий:**

Seeing that he was frightened, she showed mercy so that a great offense would not occur again because of the seed of that offense.

### Text 17

*citraketur uvāca*  
*pratigṛhṇāmi te śāpam ātmano 'ñjalināmbike*  
*devair martyāya yat proktaṁ pūrva-diṣṭaṁ hi tasya tat*

**Перевод:**

**Citraketu said: My dear mother! With folded hands I accept the curse you have uttered. It is ordained by the** ***devatās*** **as a result of my past deeds.**

**Комментарий:**

My bad intelligence arose by previous deeds (*pūrva-diṣṭam*).

### Text 21

*ekaḥ sṛjati bhūtāni bhagavān ātma-māyayā*  
*eṣāṁ bandhaṁ ca mokṣaṁ ca sukhaṁ duḥkhaṁ ca niṣkalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The one Lord, unaffected by** ***māyā,*** **creates the living entities, their bondage, liberation, happiness and distress by his** ***māyā.***

**Комментарий:**

You say that the guṇas are the cause of everything, but the jīvas could not get bondage or liberation without a conscious doer involved. This verse answers.

### Text 24

*atha prasādaye na tvāṁ śāpa-mokṣāya bhāmini*  
*yan manyase hy asādhūktaṁ mama tat kṣamyatāṁ sati*

**Перевод:**

**O angry one! I do not please you to get free from the curse. O chaste lady! Please forgive me for what you consider to be unkind words.**

**Комментарий:**

If you think you are merciful, tolerate my unkind words.

### Text 27

*śrī-rudra uvāca*  
*dṛṣṭavaty asi suśroṇi harer adbhuta-karmaṇaḥ*  
*māhātmyaṁ bhṛtya-bhṛtyānāṁ niḥspṛhāṇāṁ mahātmanām*

**Перевод:**

**Lord Śiva said: My dear beautiful Pārvatī! You have seen the splendor of** ***prema-bhaktas*** **who are devoid of material desire, who are servants of the servants of the Lord possessing astonishing activities.**

**Комментарий:**

Do you wonder that, in spite of your curse, he has no fear?

### Text 28

*nārāyaṇa-parāḥ sarve na kutaścana bibhyati*  
*svargāpavarga-narakeṣv api tulyārtha-darśinaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Devotees solely engaged in the service of Nārāyaṇa never fear any condition of life. For them the heavenly planets, liberation and the hellish planets are all the same.**

**Комментарий:**

BRS [1.2.33](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/1.2.33):

The devotees have the quality of seeing or experiencing (*darśinaḥ*) everything other than Nārāyaṇa as equally worthless (*tulyārtha*), whether it is liberation, heaven, or hell, because the devotees accept or reject everything only on the basis of favorableness to the Lord. *Tulya*, meaning “one,” is similar to the grammatical rule *rasābhyāṁ no ṇaḥ samāna-pade* (Pāṇini 8.4.1).

### Text 30

*aviveka-kṛtaḥ puṁso hy artha-bheda ivātmani*  
*guṇa-doṣa-vikalpaś ca bhid eva srajivat kṛtaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just as a person makes the mistake of identifying with objects in a dream, he makes false identification with happiness and distress in the waking state, like mistaking a garland for a rope.**

**Комментарий:**

Without discernment in a dream, one identifies with happiness or distress, good and bad, like mistaking a garland for a rope.

### Text 32

*nāhaṁ viriñco na kumāra-nāradau*  
*na brahma-putrā munayaḥ sureśāḥ*  
*vidāma yasyehitam aṁśakāṁśakā*  
*na tat-svarūpaṁ pṛthag-īśa-māninaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Neither I \[Lord Śiva\], nor Brahmā, nor the Kumāras, nor Nārada or the other great sages who are Brahmā's sons, nor the** ***devatās,*** **who are all portions of portions of the Lord, who consider ourselves independent controllers, can understand the Lord’s form or activities.**

**Комментарий:**

Considering ourselves independent, we, being small portions of the Lord, cannot understand his activities and his form. But all devotees can understand.

### Text 33

*na hy asyāsti priyaḥ kaścin nāpriyaḥ svaḥ paro 'pi vā*  
*ātmatvāt sarva-bhūtānāṁ sarva-bhūta-priyo hariḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He does not regard anyone with favor or hostility. He has no one as his own relative and no one is separate from him. Because he is the soul of all living entities, he is affectionate to all beings.**

**Комментарий:**

He makes clear the glory of the devotee through the example of Citraketu in two verses. The Lord does not see difference between dear and not dear, self and others. Since he is the soul of all beings, he is the object of unconditional affection of all beings.

### Text 34-35

*tasya cāyaṁ mahā-bhāgaś citraketuḥ priyo 'nugaḥ*  
*sarvatra sama-dṛk canto hy ahaṁ caivācyuta-priyaḥ*  
*tasmān na vismayaḥ kāryaḥ puruṣeṣu mahātmasu*  
*mahāpuruṣa-bhakteṣu śānteṣu sama-darśiṣu*

**Перевод:**

**This fortunate, obedient Citraketu, dear to the Lord, sees all living entities equally and is peaceful. Similarly, I am also very dear to Lord Nārāyaṇa. Therefore, no one should be astonished to see the activities of the most exalted devotees of Nārāyaṇa, who are peaceful, and equal to everyone.**

**Комментарий:**

Later it will be said:

*itihāsam imaṁ puṇyaṁ citraketor mahātmanaḥ*  
*māhātmyaṁ viṣṇu-bhaktānāṁ śrutvā bandhād vimucyate*

**If one hears this pious history of Citraketu, a great devotee from the devotees, one is freed from the bondage of material existence. (SB** [**6.17.40**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/17.40)**)**

### Text 39

*etat te sarvam ākhyātaṁ yan māṁ tvaṁ paripṛcchasi*  
*vṛtrasyāsura-jāteś ca kāraṇaṁ bhagavan-mateḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I have explained everything you asked about concerning the cause of Vṛtrāsura’s birth as a demon, though he was actually a devotee.**

**Комментарий:**

The cause of being born as a demon was his great offense. From the Lord’s point of view however, the cause of his being a demon was great mercy.

## Chapter Eighteen

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*pṛśnis tu patnī savituḥ sāvitrīṁ vyāhṛtiṁ trayīm*  
*agnihotraṁ paśuṁ soma cāturmāsyaṁ mahā-makhān*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: Pṛśni, who was the wife of Savitā, the fifth of the twelve sons of Aditi, gave birth to three daughters—Sāvitrī, Vyāhṛti and Trayī—and the sons named Agnihotra, Paśu, Soma, Cāturmāsya and the five Mahāyajñas.**

**Комментарий:**

Three verses go together.

### Text 20

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*kathaṁ ta āsuraṁ bhāvam apohyautpattikaṁ guro*  
*indreṇa prāpitāḥ sātmyaṁ kiṁ tat sādhu kṛtaṁ hi taiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**King Parīkṣit said: O** ***guru*****! Why did Indra convert person born with demonic mentality into** ***devatās*****? Did they perform any pious activities?**

**Комментарий:**

*Apohya* means making them give up.

### Text 21

*ime śraddadhate brahmann ṛṣayo hi mayā saha*  
*parijñānāya bhagavaṁs tan no vyākhyātum arhasi*

**Перевод:**

**My dear** ***brāhmaṇa*****! All the sages present along with me have faith in you. Therefore, O great soul, kindly explains to us the reason.**

**Комментарий:**

“Why do you want to hear this material topic?” All the sages have faith in you. And mainly the sages want to know.

### Text 22

*śrī-sūta uvāca*  
*tad viṣṇurātasya sa bādarāyaṇir*  
*vaco niśamyādṛtam alpam arthavat*  
*sabhājayan san nibhṛtena cetasā*  
*jagāda satrāyaṇa sarva-darśanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Sūta Gosvāmī said: O sage Śaunaka! After hearing Mahārāja Parīkṣit speak respectful, precise words holding important meaning, omniscient Śukadeva Gosvāmī praised him and, with perfectly concentrated mind, replied.**

**Комментарий:**

He heard his words filled with respect for the sages (*ādṛtam*), which would be related to worship of the Lord (*arthavat*). Thinking of the Lord, Śukadeva concentrated his mind, linking the topic to the Lord with spiritual joy (*nibhṛtena cetasā*). Śrīdhara Svāmī says *arthavat* means “with many meanings, conveying the desires of the sages and Śuka.”

### Text 26

*āśāsānasya tasyedaṁ dhruvam unnaddha-cetasaḥ*  
*mada-śoṣaka indrasya bhūyād yena suto hi me*

**Перевод:**

**Because of his evil actions, may I have a son who can destroy the pride of uncontrolled Indra, who thinks he is eternal!**

**Комментарий:**

She thought that this would be liked by the two brothers.

### Text 45

*śrī-kaśyapa uvāca*  
*putras te bhavitā bhadre indra-hādeva-bāndhavaḥ*  
*saṁvatsaraṁ vratam idea yady añjo dhārayiṣyasi*

**Перевод:**

**Kaśyapa Muni said: My gentle wife! If you execute this vow properly for one year, you will get a son who will kill Indra and will be a friend of the demons.**

**Комментарий:**

If you perform this vrata you will get a son who will kill Indra (*indra-hā*) and be the friend of the demons and devatās (adeva and deva). He would not kill Indra, but be the friend of the devatās.

### Text 51

*nādhauta-pādāprayatā nārdra-pādā udak-śirāḥ*  
*śayīta nāparāṅ nānyair na nagnā na ca sandhyayoḥ*

**Перевод:**

**You should not lie down without having first washed your feet, without being purified, and you should not lie down with wet feet, with your head pointed west, with head pointed north, naked, in the company of others, or during the sunrise or sunset.**

**Комментарий:**

One should not sleep with the head in the western direction (*aparām*).

### Text 63

*tam ūcuḥ pāṭyamānās te sarve prāñjalayo nṛpa*  
*kiṁ na indra jighāṁsasi bhrātaro marutas tava*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The cut pieces pleaded to Indra with folded hands. "O Indra! Why are you are killing your brothers, the Maruts?"**

**Комментарий:**

The one jīva had projected itself into many bodies. Or previously there were many jīvas in one body and then divided into different bodies.

### Text 66-67

*sakṛd iṣṭvādi-puruṣaṁ puruṣo yāti sāmyatām*  
*saṁvatsaraṁ kiñcid ūnaṁ dityā yad dharir arcitaḥ*  
*sajūr indreṇa pañcāśad devās te maruto 'bhavan*  
*vyapohya mātṛ-doṣaṁ te hariṇā soma-pāḥ kṛtāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**If one worships the Supreme Lord even once, he attains the same bodily features as Viṣṇu. Because Diti worshiped Lord Viṣṇu for almost one year, the forty-nine Maruts became fifty** ***devatās*** **along with Indra. Viṣṇu removed the fault of their mother and they became drinkers of** ***soma.***

**Комментарий:**

*Hariṇā* means by Indra or by Viṣṇu.

### Text 72

*kṛtto me saptadhā garbha āsan sapta kumārakāḥ*  
*te 'pi caikaikaśo vṛkṇāḥ saptadhā nāpi mamrire*

**Перевод:**

**First I cut the child in the womb into seven pieces, which became seven children. Then I cut each of the children into seven pieces again, but they did not die.**

**Комментарий:**

*Me* means “by me.”

### Text 73

*tatas tat paramāścaryaṁ vīkṣya vyavasitaṁ mayā*  
*mahāpuruṣa-pūjāyāḥ siddhiḥ kāpy ānuṣaṅgiṇī*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing that great wonder, I discerned the truth. Even liberation is a mere secondary result of worship of Lord Viṣṇu.**

**Комментарий:**

*Vyavasitam* means considered and concluded. Thus liberation is secondary to worship of Viṣṇu.

## Chapter Nineteen

### Text 4

*alaṁ te nirapekṣāya pūrṇa-kāma namo 'stu te*  
*mahāvibhūti-pataye namaḥ sakala-siddhaye*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord, complete in your desires! I offer my respectful obeisances unto you, the independent Lord, the master of great wealth, the giver of all perfections.**

**Комментарий:**

*Te alam* means “nothing else is necessary to attain you.”

### Text 6

*viṣṇu-patni mahā-māye mahāpuruṣa-lakṣaṇe*  
*prīyethā me mahā-bhāge loka-mātar namo 'stu te*

**Перевод:**

**O wife of Lord Viṣṇu! O internal energy of Lord Viṣṇu, possessing his qualities! O goddess of fortune! Please be kind to me. O mother of the world! I offer my respectful obeisances unto you.**

**Комментарий:**

O form of the Lord’s spiritual śakti (*mahā-maye*). O very form of the Lord (*mahāpuruṣa-lakṣaṇe*)

### Text 11

*yuvāṁ tu viśvasya vibhū jagataḥ kāraṇaṁ param*  
*iyaṁ hi prakṛtiḥ sūkṣmā māyā-śaktir duratyayā*

**Перевод:**

**You two are the lords of the universe, the supreme cause of the universe. Lakṣmī has** ***prakṛti*** **or** ***māyā-śakti*****, difficult to understand and hard to overcome, as her ābhāsa.**

**Комментарий:**

You two supreme entities are the cause of the universe. This explained in the next line. Lakṣmī has prakṛti as her ābhāsa.

### Text 12

*tasyā adhīśvaraḥ sākṣāt tvam eva puruṣaḥ paraḥ*  
*tvaṁ sarva-yajña ijyeyaṁ kriyeyaṁ phala-bhug bhavān*

**Перевод:**

**You are the master of Lakṣmī. You are the Supreme Person. You are all sacrifices personified. Lakṣmī is the secular activity of the sacrifice and the worship performed in meditation, whereas you are the enjoyer of the worship**

**Комментарий:**

It is said in Nārada-tantra:

*viṣṇos tu trīṇi rūpāṇi puruṣākhyāny atho viduḥ*  
*prathamaṁ mahataḥ sraṣṭṛ dvitīyaṁ tv aṇḍa-saṁsthitaṁ*  
*tṛtīyaṁ sarva-bhūtasthaṁ tāni jñatvā vimucyate*

**The wise know that the puruṣa consists of three forms of the original Viṣṇu. The first is the creator of the mahat-tattva. The second is situated within the universe. The third is situated within each living entity. Knowing these three, one becomes liberated.**

This is explained in the second line.

### Text 24

*ācāryam agrataḥ kṛtvā vāg-yataḥ saha bandhubhiḥ*  
*dadyāt patnyai caroḥ śeṣaṁ suprajāstvaṁ susaubhagam*

**Перевод:**

**Not in the presence of guru, with controlled speech he should take his meal with friends, and then give his wife the remnants of the cooked rice which will ensure good progeny and very good fortune.**

**Комментарий:**

One should eat, giving up being in front of guru, since it is forbidden to eat in front of him.
