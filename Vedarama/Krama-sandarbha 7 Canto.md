# Krama-sandarbha 7 Canto

## Chapter One

*saptamakramasandarbhe/ sandarbhāṇāṁ samāhṛtiḥ*  
*kriyate yannideśena/ sa me ’nanyagatergatiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He by whose order this collection of sandarbhas is made into the Seventh Krama Sandarbha is the goal of this person with no other goal.**

### Text 1

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*samaḥ priyaḥ suhṛd brahman bhūtānāṁ bhagavān svayam*  
*indrasyārthe kathaṁ daityān avadhīd viṣamo yathā*

**Перевод:**

**The King said: O** ***brāhmaṇa!*** **How did the Lord, the best friend of all beings, who is equal and affectionate to all beings, kill the demons for Indra as if partial?**

**Комментарий:**

As Paramātmā he is equal to all, and Bhagavān he acts for everyone’s benefit (*suhṛt*) and is the object of love. Since he is equal, helps all, and is the object of love for all, how could he act as if partial in killing demons? He did not act as helper or object of love.

### Text 4-5

*śrī-ṛṣir uvāca*  
*sādhu pṛṣṭaṁ maharaja hareś caritam adbhutam*  
*yad bhāgavata-māhātmyaṁ bhagavad-bhakti-vardhanam*  
*gīyate paramaṁ puṇyam ṛṣibhir nāradādibhiḥ*  
*natvā kṛṣṇāya munaye kathayiṣye hareḥ kathām*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva said: O great King! You have asked the correct question, because in answer to that question the astonishing activities of the Lord, which increase** ***bhakti*** **and glorify devotees, are sung by sages like Nārada. Offering respects to Vyāsadeva, I will speak about the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 96-97:**

O King! What you have asked you have considered thoughtfully *(sādhu*). But the activities of the Lord are extraordinary *(adbhutam*)--they are subject to deliberation, for, though the Lord is equal to all, he appears to be unequal. In those activities (*yat)* there is increase of bhakti for the Lord and glorification of devotees such as Prahlāda. The Lord does everything for these devotees and not for others. This will become apparent in the story. In the commentary Śrīdhara Svāmī says “I offer respects to astonishing Nṛsiṁha, the form of the highest bliss, who killed the enemy, showing favor to his devotee.”

*gīyate paramaṁ puṇyam ṛṣibhir nāradādibhiḥ*  
*natvā kṛṣṇāya munaye kathayiṣye hareḥ kathām*

**This topic, giving the highest piety, is sung by sages like Nārada. Offering respects to Vyāsadeva, I will speak about the Lord. (SB** [**7.1.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.4)**)**

I will speak about this topic which *(yat)* is sung by sages like Nārada, giving the highest merit. The pronoun *yat* is understood. It is suggested here that the sages sing this topic to give happiness to the devotees.

### Text 6

*nirguṇo 'pi hy ajo 'vyakto bhagavān prakṛteḥ paraḥ*  
*sva-māyā-guṇam āviśya bādhya-bādhakatāṁ gataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Though the Lord is without material qualities, unborn, unmanifest, and superior to matter, he enters into matter and appears to be cause of subordination or prominence of a guṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 98:**

Since the Lord is beyond prakṛti, he is devoid of material guṇas. Thus he is without birth, eternally existing. And thus he is self-manifesting in his body (*avyaktaḥ*): he cannot be revealed by others, since he does not have material body and senses. He is devoid of attraction and repulsion arising from material guṇas. Taking support of a pure sattva state filled with astonishing pastimes (*guṇam)* which gives mercy (*māyā)* to his devotees (*sva*), the Lord, who eternally manifests his six qualities (*bhagavān*), but occasionally appears to be born and become visible to people’s eyes in this world, takes shelter of a portion of his power, which he personally distributes for producing his assistance or opposition to devatās and demons who are incompetent and unable to stand in his gaze. He arranges for defeat or victory for them (*bādhya-bādhakatām gataḥ*). Giving an additional portion of his power to the opponents for creating variety in fighting pastimes, he arranges for defeat of the devatās. Other times he distributes to the opponents less power, and the devatās are victorious. *Māyā* means mercy and cheating according to Viśva-prakāśa.

Though another meaning can be given to the word *māyā* (illusion) in the verse, that meaning is rejected because the goal of the topic at the beginning and end is mercy to the devotees, and the whole discussion deals with this, and a great fault would arise by making *māyā* (illusion) the agent. Therefore, there is no inequality, because the Lord has no hatred or attachment for anyone in performing pastimes only for the pleasure of his devotees. *Bādhyatām gataḥ* means “he causes oppression or defeat.” Thus, by revealing his pastimes, which are the play of his svarūpa-śakti, benefit results for all people. His quality of friendship to all is not damaged.

### Text 7

*sattvaṁ rajas tama iti prakṛter nātmano guṇāḥ*  
*na teṣāṁ yugapad rājan hrāsa ullāsa eva vā*

**Перевод:**

***Sattva, rajas*** **and** ***tamas*** **belong to** ***prakṛti*** **and do not influence the Lord. O King! They do not increase or decrease simultaneously.**

**Комментарий:**

Why does this pure sattva not become a material guṇa? When and where does it distribute great power? Why does this not become a cause of interfering with karma, giving results not deserved or removing results which are deserved? Two verses answer these doubts.

The guṇas including sattva belong to prakṛti, not the Lord (*na ātmanaḥ*). The material guṇas are not the guṇas which eternally give joy to the Lord. It is said:

*sattvādayo na santīśe yatra ca prākṛtā guṇāḥ \|*  
*sa śuddhaḥ sarva-śuddhebhyaḥ pumān ādyaḥ prasīdatu \|\|*  
*hlādinī sandhinī saṁvit tvayy ekā guṇa-saṁśraye \|*  
*hlāda-tāpa-karī miśrā tvayi no guṇa-varjite \|*

**The material guṇas of sattva, rajas and tamas do not exist in the Lord. May that person, the source of all, purer than the purest, be pleased with me. In you who are the shelter of all spiritual qualities exist the energies of hlādinī, sandhinī and saṁvit. In you who are devoid of material qualities, the mixture of happiness and distress found in the material world does not exist. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa** [**1.9.44-45**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vp/1.9.44)**)**

Since they are not part of him (who contains opposites), they cannot decrease or increase simultaneously (but act according to material principles). One guṇa decreases while another increases, surpassing the first guṇa through transformation.

### Text 8

*jaya-kāle tu sattvasya devarṣīn rajaso 'surān*  
*tamaso yakṣa-rakṣāṁsi tat-kālānuguṇo 'bhajat*

**Перевод:**

**When** ***sattva*** **becomes prominent the Lord favors the** ***devatās*** **and sages. When** ***rajas*** **becomes prominent he favors the demons. When** ***tamas*** **becomes prominent he favors the Yakṣas and Rākṣasas. The Lord acts according to the dictates of time.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 100:**

It has been shown that it is proper for the Lord to help the devatās and fight with the demons. Though it seems that his pastimes are dependent on the increase of sattva in the world, this idea, after being presented, is rejected. (He acts through all guṇas).

When sattva conquers the other guṇas, the Lord favors the devatās and sages. The Lord spreads his power, covered by sattva, to their bodies. By this he helps them. And when rajoguṇa becomes prominent he spreads his power covered by rajas to the demons. When tamas becomes prominent he spreads his power covered by tamas to the Yakṣas and Rākṣasas. These details should be added. By tamas he makes the Yakṣas opponents and arranges for defeat of the devatās. In this way he reveals himself. This illustrates the fact that he arranges defeat and victory (*bādhya-bādhakatām*) for his variegated pastimes to nourish bhakti-rasa.

In churning the Milk Ocean it is said:

*tathāsurān āviśad āsureṇa*  
*rūpeṇa teṣāṁ bala-vīryam īrayan*  
*uddīpayan deva-gaṇāṁś ca viṣṇur*  
*daivena nāgendram abodha-rūpaḥ*

**Thereafter, Viṣṇu entered the demons as the quality of passion. He entered the devatās as the quality of goodness, and entered Vāsuki as the quality of ignorance to stimulate and increase their strength and courage. (SB** [**8.7.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/7.11)**)**

The verse states that the Lord entered the various parties in order create variegated pastimes.

“The independence of his pastimes will be destroyed since the pastimes are controlled by time which awakens the various guṇas. There will clearly be something inappropriate in the Lord by his relating directly with the guṇas.”

In answer to this, it is said in the verse that the Lord is one whom time follows (*tat-kālānuguṇaḥ*). This is a compound like *bhagavac-charaṇa* (he whom the Lord shelters.) When the Lord plays by his independent will, māyā, which is obedient to him, stimulates all actions of the universe, which are a continuous flow obedient to her, and then time which makes the different guṇas arise, is made to operate by her special function. Thus time is dependent on the Lord. That time is a function of māyā is explained:

*kālo daivaṁ karma jīvaḥ svabhāvo*  
*dravyaṁ kṣetraṁ prāṇa ātmā vikāraḥ*  
*tat-saṅghāto bīja-roha-pravāhas*  
*tvan-māyaiṣā tan-niṣedhaṁ prapadye*

**Time, the agitator; fate; action, or cause; the conditioned jīva; impressions; the sense objects; the prakṛti; mahat-tattva; false ego; the eleven senses and five gross elements; the body—all this constitutes your material illusory energy, māyā, an endless cycle like that of seed and plant. I take shelter of you, the negation of this māyā. (SB** [**10.63.26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/63.26)**)**

*Tat-kālānuguṇaḥ* has another meaning. The Lord possesses time, which is his quality of power (*prabhāvam)* for his independent actions for giving mercy to the devotees. According to this quality of his, things are enacted by māyā. It is said:

*yo ’yaṁ kālas tasya te ’vyakta-bandho*  
*ceṣṭām āhuś ceṣṭate yena viśvam*  
*nimeṣādir vatsarānto mahīyāṁs*  
*taṁ tveśānaṁ kṣema-dhāma prapadye*

**O inaugurator of the material energy! Time which starts with second and ends with years, which rotate to make the life time of Brahmā, and by which the universe moves, is said to be your movement. I surrender to the Supreme Lord, the abode of fearlessness and happiness. (SB** [**10.3.26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/3.26)**)**

Thus it should follows that the Lord’s pastimes are not controlled by time by either meaning of *tat-kālānuguṇaḥ.*

Kapila has said:

*etāvān eva saṅkhyāto brahmaṇaḥ sa-guṇasya ha*  
*sanniveśo mayā prokto yaḥ kālaḥ pañca-viṁśakaḥ*

**What I have described as the situation of the Brahman as the material world, of which time is the twenty-fifth element, has thus been enumerated.**

*prabhāvaṁ pauruṣaṁ prāhuḥ kālam eke yato bhayam*  
*ahaṅkāra-vimūḍhasya kartuḥ prakṛtim īyuṣaḥ*

**Time is said to be the Lord’s personal power (*****prabhāvam*****), by which fear arises in the jīva, who has contacted prakṛti and is bewildered by ego. (SB** [**3.26.15-16**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/26.15)**)**

Here the two points of time being manifested by māyā and acting for the Lord are established. In this, the chief aim of time’s actions (directed by the Lord) is giving pleasure to the devotees (*prabhāvam pauraṣam*). The action of awakening the guṇas takes place by time on its own. That is a secondary action. The first action is the real aim, the play of his svarūpa-śakti. The second function of time is a secondary form, included as a shadow śakti of māyā. It should be understood that the verse quoted above *yo yam kālaḥ* (SB 10.3.26) refer to both aspects as one.

Thus the word *anuguṇa* should be explained as follows in this context. Just as *anubhṛtya* means a servant who follows, *anuguṇa* refers to the shadow quality of time which follows after its major aspect (*prabhāva*m) of supporting the Lord’s pastime. The time which influences the guṇas is a secondary form, not the direct form which acts for the devotees.

### Text 9

*jyotir-ādir ivābhāti saṅghātān na vivicyate*  
*vidanty ātmānam ātma-sthaṁ mathitvā kavayo 'ntataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord resides in all beings just as fire resides in wood, water in a cup or ether in a pot, but he is not perceived when one sees material bodies. The wise know the Paramātmā situated in all beings by inferring him through the effects, after discarding false doctrines**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 101:**

“Why does one not see the Lord’s power when it enters the devatās or demons?”

Though he enters living beings by a portion of his power, people cannot discern him because he is mixed with them (*saṅghātāt*). The wise, finally extracting the truth (*mathitvā*), by skill in discrimination, after rejecting what is not possible-- that he helps and also fights the devatās (by favoring demons)--know (*vidanti*) the Lord (*ātmānam*) who has entered the various jīvas (*ātma-stham*) by his portion. A supporting example that indicates the cause is given. They know him just as light is realized by seers.

The meaning is this. First, one says “This is not a jewel,” because one does not see power in it. But by contact with the sun’s heat, the power of the sun enter it. It is then realized by observers as a sunstone by its power to burn grass. The fragrance of air enters the earth. Then it is realized by people by its effects. Similarly the Lord is realized in the devatās and demons by the effects.

Or the example answers another doubt. “Does this mean that he plays by his powers which have entered the demons and devatās? How is it detected that he plays with them?” When form, which is light’s expansion, is revealed by light and the eye, earth made of the five qualities starting with fragrance is revealed. When sound, the expansion of ether, is revealed by ether and the ear, a drum is revealed. Because it mixes with its qualities, it appears, but not directly. Though not detected by others as being present there, the wise know the Lord who plays by expansion of his own power (*ātmā-stham*) by his combination with the devatās and demons.

### Text 10

*yadā sisṛkṣuḥ pura ātmanaḥ paro*  
*rajaḥ sṛjaty eṣa pṛthak sva-māyayā*  
*sattvaṁ vicitrāsu riraṁsur īśvaraḥ*  
*śayiṣyamāṇas tama īrayaty asau*

**Перевод:**

**When the Lord desires to create bodies of the** ***jīva*** **he manifests** ***rajas*** **by his energy. When he desires to have pastimes he manifests** ***sattva.*** **When he desires to destroy, he inspires** ***tamas.***

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 102:**

The goal of the Lord’s pastimes of fighting is pleasure for the devotees. Protecting the universe is also accomplished by that. Having said this, in order to remove all doubts concerning creation and destruction and glancing at prakṛti, he speaks, describing the three functions together.

When at the time of acting, the supreme Lord (*paraḥ*) desires to create his spiritual abodes (*ātmanaḥ puraḥ*)--sādhaka devotees from the previous creation—and he glances by his mercy (*sva-māyayā*) in order manifest them since they had merged with prakṛti, his jīva-māyā śakti (*asau*), different (*pṛthak*) from his svarūpa-śakti, enlivened by the shadow of his power of action (time), creates rajas. This means māyā ejects or awakens rajas from the avyakta, the equilibrium of the three guṇas, which has arisen from the Lord’s aṁśa. Or, time (*asau*) which is separate (*pṛthak*), obedient to māyā, creates rajas. *Asau* also refers to time in this interpretation.

When the Lord desires to enjoy pastimes with the devotees having a variety of qualities and moods (*vicitrāsu*), he creates sattva. When he desires to rest, after meeting with them, māyā-śakti creates tamas. All the actions of the creation, maintenance and destruction are for the devotees. This is accepted in *sasarjoccāvacāny ādyaḥ sva-mātrātma-prasiddhaye*: he creates higher and lower so they can worship the Lord. (SB [11.3.3](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/3.3))

The commentary of Śrīdhara Svāmī says “Why does he create? For perfecting (*prasiddhaye*) the jīva (*ātmā*), by having him worship the Lord (*sva-mātra*).”

Sleeping refers to the yoga-nidrā of the puruṣāvatāra on the ocean of pralaya and sometimes refers to the Lord residing in the jīva. Thought the supreme Lord resides in all jīvas as the antaryāmī, he does not remain out of attachment. He resides in the devotee however out of attachment to the devotee. Since his associating with the jīva just as antaryāmī is not his desire the explanation just given is preferable. Gītā says:

*mat-sthāni sarva-bhūtāni a cāhaṁ teṣv avasthitaḥ \|\|*  
*na ca mat-sthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogam aiśvaram \|*

**All entities are within me. But I am not in them. And the living beings are not in me. See my inconceivable power. (BG** [**9.4-5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.4)**)**

*ye bhajanti tu māṁ bhaktyā mayi te teṣu cāpy aham \|\|*

**But those who worship me with devotion are in me, and I am in them. (BG** [**9.29**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.29)**)**

It is said in Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya:

*bhaktānāṁ hṛdayaṁ śāntam saśriyo me priyam gṛham*  
*vasāmi tatra śobhaiva vaikuṇṭhakhyādi-varṇanā*

**I reside in the gentle hearts of the devotees who are my dear house as well as Lakṣmī’s. Those hearts have the beauty of Vaikuṇṭha.**

### Text 11

*kālaṁ carantaṁ sṛjatīśa āśrayaṁ*  
*pradhāna-pumbhyāṁ nara-deva satya-kṛt*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The Supreme Lord, creator of a real universe, creates time, which acts as a cause, which is an assistant to the Lord, and which exists along with** ***prakṛti*** **and** ***jīva.***

### Text 12

*ya eṣa rājann api kāla īśitā*  
*sattvaṁ surānīkam ivaidhayaty ataḥ*  
*tat-pratyanīkān asurān sura-priyo*  
*rajas-tamaskān pramiṇoty uruśravāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The Lord as time, friend of the** ***devatās*****, increases the** ***devatās*** **in** ***sattva*** **by the Lord’s influence, and destroys the demons inimical to them, who are covered by** ***rajas*** **and** ***tamas.***

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 103:**

The Lord, who manifests real actions for the supreme goal through the play of his svarūpa-śakti (*satya-kṛt*), manifests (*sṛjati*) time, a form of his action. What does time do? It acts along with the combination of prakṛti and the jīva for manifesting the creation to assist the sādhaka devotees related to the avatāras. And by proximity, time is the cause of manifesting (*āśrayam*) the conditions of prakṛti and the jīva. O King (*naradeva*)! When you perform some major action by your efforts, other lesser actions are also automatically fulfilled. Time works like that.

Time, with action as its form, increases the devatās predominated by sattva and decreases (*pramiṇoti*) the demons, who are hostile to the devatās (*pratyanīkān*), and who are predominated by rajas and tamas. Time protects the devotees among the devatās and decreases the enemies of the devotees among the demons. That was previously explained. Since it was explained that time was the representation of the Lord’s action, as the empowered one (*īśitā*), time increases devatās and decreases the demons. O king! The same meaning as before is indicated.

“If the Lord has no goal, why does he sometimes not make the demons his friends and fight with the devatās?” The Lord is affectionate to the devotees who exist among the devatās (*sura-priyaḥ*). Among the devatās predominated by sattva, because of their general loyalty to the Lord, the Lord is attracted to them. Sometimes, when they commit offense to Bṛhaspati and others, their devatā nature becomes covered by contamination. Then those persons among the devatās are not favored by the Lord. This was explained in SB [7.1.8](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.8).

“Why do the demons not follow the devotees?” Because they have no taste, since rajas and tamas (*rajas-tamaskān*) cause extreme rejection of the Lord. “But it is inappropriate for the Lord to punish them constantly.” That is not so. The Lord has fame spread everywhere (*uruśravāḥ*): he gives them mercy also, as in the case of Pūtanā (SB [3.2.23](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/2.23)) and others:

*vaireṇa yaṁ nṛpatayaḥ śiśupāla-pauṇḍra-*  
*śālvādayo gati-vilāsa-vilokanādyaiḥ*  
*dhyāyanta ākṛta-dhiyaḥ śayanāsanādau*  
*tat-sāmyam āpur anurakta-dhiyāṁ punaḥ kim*

**Inimical kings like Śiśupāla, Pauṇḍraka and Śālva, while they were lying down, sitting or engaging in other activities, enviously meditated upon the bodily movements of the Lord, his sporting pastimes, and his loving glances. Being thus always absorbed in Kṛṣṇa, they achieved positions in the spiritual world. What then can be said of the benedictions offered to those who constantly fix their minds on Lord Kṛṣṇa in a favorable, loving mood? (SB** [**11.5.48**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/5.48)**)**

### Text 13

*atraivodāhṛtaḥ pūrvam itihāsaḥ surarṣiṇā*  
*prītyā mahā-kratau rājan pṛcchate 'jāta-śatrave*

**Перевод:**

**Formerly, when Mahārāja Yudhiṣṭhira was performing the Rājasūya sacrifice, Nārada, responding to his inquiry, recited a story in this connection.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 104:**

Having shown the conclusive truth, Śukadeva relates a particular story concerning mercy to Prahlāda, Jaya and Vijaya as an example of the Lord fulfilling the role of acting only for giving mercy to the devotees.

*Atra* here means “since the Lord performs his pastimes at will, s devoid of enmity, in order to give joy to his devotees, and to give the highest benefit to the demons.” The word *eva* (only) indicates that other conclusions are excluded.

### Text 14-15

*dṛṣṭvā mahādbhutaṁ raja rājasūye mahā-kratau*  
*vāsudeve bhagavati sāyujyaṁ cedibhū-bhujaḥ*  
*tatrāsīnaṁ sura-ṛṣiṁ rājā pāṇḍu-sutaḥ kratau*  
*papraccha vismita-manā munīnāṁ śṛṇvatām idam*

**Перевод:**

**O King! At the Rājasūya sacrifice, Mahārāja Yudhiṣṭhira saw Śiśupāla merge into the body of Kṛṣṇa. Therefore, struck with wonder, in the presence of the sages he inquired about the reason for this from Nārada, who was seated there.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together.

### Text 16

*śrī-yudhiṣṭhira uvāca*  
*aho aty-adbhutaṁ hy etad durlabhaikāntinām api*  
*vāsudeve pare tattve prāptiś caidyasya vidviṣaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Yudhiṣṭhira said: It is astonishing that the envious Śiśupāla attained Vāsudeva. This attainment is difficult for even the best jñānīs.**

**Комментарий:**

*Ekāntinam* means the best of jñānīs as in the following:

*tasya putro mahā-yogī sama-dṛṅ nirvikalpakaḥ \|*  
*ekānta-matir unnidro gūḍho mūḍha iveyate \|\|*

**His son, a great** ***yogī,*** **seeing all things equally, fully realized in** ***Brahman*****, with mind concentrated on one goal, having dispelled ignorance, hid himself from the public and appeared to be a fool. (SB** [**1.4.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/4.4)**)**

### Text 18

*damaghoṣa-sutaḥ pāpaḥ ārabhya kala-bhāṣaṇāt*  
*sampraty amarṣī govinde dantavakraś ca durmatiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**From of his childhood, when he could not even speak properly, up until now, evil Śiśupāla, the son of Damaghoṣa, was envious of Kṛṣṇa. Similarly, his brother Dantavakra continued the same habits.**

**Комментарий:**

His criticism of the Lord was an imitation only. Becoming Dantavakra was also according the Kumāras’ curse.

### Text 23

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*nindana-stava-satkāra- nyakkārārthaṁ kalevaram*  
*pradhāna-parayo rājann avivekena kalpitam*

**Перевод:**

**A body subject to criticism and praise, honor and dishonor is created by lack of discrimination between the self and non-self.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 315:**

Foolish people partake of criticism and other acts aimed at persons in the three guṇas. A criticism of persons within the guṇas cannot extend to the Lord whose form is made of spiritual qualities. The Lord does not identify with anything in the material world, unlike the jīva. Therefore he cannot be afflicted.”

### Text 24

*hiṁsā tad-abhimānena daṇḍa-pāruṣyayor yathā*  
*vaiṣamyam iha bhūtānāṁ mamāham iti pārthiva*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Ideas of violence arise by identification with the body. When ideas of punishment and threats arise, there is unequal treatment for all beings in this world. One thinks, “These are my enemies. These are my friends. I will kill these enemies and protect these friends.”**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 315:**

He has no false identity because he is pure, with no material body (*kaivalyāt*). Even the devotees are completely pure:

*dehendriyāsu-hīnānāṁ vaikuṇṭha-pura-vāsinām*

**The inhabitants of Vaikuṇṭha are completely spiritual, having no material body, senses or life airs. (SB** [**7.1.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.35)**)**

He cannot be subject to material criticism because his body is eternity, knowledge and bliss, which is not subject to any criticism. That is also explained in Gītā:

*nāhaṁ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yoga-māyā-samāvṛtaḥ \|*  
*mūḍho ’yaṁ nābhijānāti loko mām ajam avyayam \|\|*

**Being covered by my bewildering power of māyā, I am not visible to all these people. The foolish do not know me, who am without birth and unchanging in qualities. (BG** [**7.25**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/7.25)**)**

*yan-nibaddho 'bhimāno 'yaṁ tad-vadhāt prāṇināṁ vadhaḥ*  
*tathā na yasya kaivalyād abhimāno 'khilātmanaḥ*  
*parasya dama-kartur hi hiṁsā kenāsya kalpyate*

**Because of the bodily conception of life, the conditioned soul thinks that when the body is annihilated the living being is annihilated. Because of having no false identity, the supreme controller, identifying himself as Paramātmā, different from matter and the** ***jīva,*** **though he is a punisher, does not have a concept of being a killer or being killed.**

The Lord is significantly different from jīvas because he is the ātmā of all beings (*akhilātmanaḥ*). He is the ātmā of all beings because he is devoid of material influence (*parasya*). He is also not the subject of violence because he is the punisher of others (*dama-kartuḥ*), since he possesses astonishing, unlimited powers.

### Text 26

*tasmād vairānubandhena nirvaireṇa bhayena vā*  
*snehāt kāmena vā yuñjyāt kathañcin nekṣate pṛthak*

**Перевод:**

**By continuous enmity, by favorable relationships*****,*** **or by conjugal feelings arising from strong spiritual affection out of fear of transgressing morality as an unmarried lover, or even as a married lover, the mind should concentrate on the Lord. One will realize a relationship with the Lord only according to the mode of practice.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 315:**

Thus, since there was no harm to the Lord when Śiśupāla criticized him, because by absorption in the Lord by any means, even from enmity, his sin of criticizing was destroyed. It was thus appropriate that he attained liberation.

*sakṛd yad-aṅga-pratimāntar-āhitā*  
*manomayī bhāgavatīṁ dadau gatim*  
*sa eva nityātma-sukhānubhūty-abhi-*  
*vyudasta-māyo ’ntar-gato hi kiṁ punaḥ*

**If even only once or even by force one brings the form of the Lord into one’s mind, Kṛṣṇa awards himself as the goal. Residing within, he gives thieves the experience of bliss by destroying all māyā completely. What more can be said? (SB** [**10.12.39**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/12.39)**)**

Since affection and love cannot be ordered, the potential case is used to express possibility (*yuñjyāt*). If one can meditate by one of the methods mentioned, one will become absorbed in the Lord (*nekṣate pṛthak*). *Vairānubandhena* means “by continuous enmity.” *Nirvaireṇa* means “by being without enmity, being neutral.” This also means without kāma or vairagya and without any bhāva listed in the verse. One can meditate, representing any aṅga of bhakti-yoga, without bhāva, merely with the idea that it is prescribed. Sneha (affection) means a natural type of mutual prema, distinct from kāma. In the sādhaka, it refers to a taste for such a relationship.

The result is absorption in the Lord for all of them. Since one can quickly attain absorption by these means filled with bhāva, even with enmity and criticism, they are not the same as vaidhī bhakti.

### Text 27

*yathā vairānubandhena martyas tan-mayatām iyāt*  
*na tathā bhakti-yogena iti me niścitā matiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One cannot achieve such intense absorption in thought of the Supreme Lord by devotional service as one can through enmity toward him. That is my opinion.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 315:**

The result is absorption in the Lord for all of them. Since one can quickly attain absorption by these means filled with bhāva, even with enmity and criticism, they are not the same as vaidhī bhakti. Fear should be included with continuous enmity in the above verse. The performance of bhakti-yoga while thinking only that it should be done does not bring about absorption in the Lord (*tan-mayatām*) as quickly as enmity and fear.

### Text 28-29

*kīṭaḥ peśaskṛtā ruddhaḥ kuḍyāyāṁ tam anusmaran*  
*saṁrambha-bhaya-yogena vindate tat-svarūpatām*  
*evaṁ kṛṣṇe bhagavati māyā-manuja īśvare*  
*vaireṇa pūta-pāpmānas tam āpur anucintayā*

**Перевод:**

**A worm confined in a hole in a wall by a bee, by always thinking of the bee in fear and enmity, later becomes a bee. Similarly, if the conditioned souls out of enmity think of Kṛṣṇa, who is merciful to even the sinful, and become free from their sins, they attain him.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 315:**

One may say that this takes place because the Lord or an ābhāsa of the Lord (in the case of enmity) appears before them, endowed with such śakti. A material example is given to show the great result in the form of absorption even from enmity.

By absorption (*yoga)* through hatred or fear one attains a form similar to the Lord’s (*sārūpyam*). *Evam* means “in this way.” Because he is Parabrahman in human form, Kṛṣṇa appears to be a material human by māyā (*māyā-manuje*). “The insect does not incur sin by hating the bee. However if one hates the Lord there is sin.” But by absorption (*anucintayā*) in hatred one becomes purified of sin, since meditation with absorption has such power.

### Text 30

*kāmād dveṣād bhayāt snehād yathā bhaktyeśvare manaḥ*  
*āveśya tad-aghaṁ hitvā bahavas tad-gatiṁ gatāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just as by** ***vaidhi-bhakti*** **one can attain one’s spiritual goals*****,*** **many persons have attained suitable forms after asorbing their minds in the Lord out of lust, hatred, fear, and family relationships filled with affection, and after giving up absorption in enmity of the Lord (in the case of hatred and fear).**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 320:**

One should not say that one can only attain perfection by bhagavad-dharma according to scripture and not by kāma and other bhāvas which are not prescribed in scriptures.

Just as they attain the goal by absorbing the mind in the Lord by bhakti according to rules, many attain the goal by kāma and other bhāvas which are not according to the rules. They gave up hatred and fear (*tad-agham*) among the various bhāvas. Because fear is connected with hatred it also produces sin.

Some consider kāma to be a sin also. The following should be considered. Is kāma for the Lord itself sinful, or is it sinful if kāma means to think of Kṛṣṇa as a husband? Or is it only sinful if one thinks of Kṛṣṇa as one’s illicit lover?

If kāma for the Lord is itself sinful, is it because it is classed with hatred and other qualities, being sinful by its nature? Or is it sinful because one thinks of Kṛṣna as a lusty person or kisses him, though he is most pure? Or is kāma sinful because kāma is said to be a sin in scriptures?

It cannot be sinful by nature, being classed with hatred, anger etc. because it is said:

*uktaṁ purastād etat te caidyaḥ siddhiṁ yathā gataḥ*  
*dviṣann api hṛṣīkeśaṁ kim utādhokṣaja-priyāḥ*

**This point was explained to you previously. Since even Śiśupāla, who hated Kṛṣṇa, again achieved his spiritual body as an associate by thinking of him, then what to speak of the Lord’s dear gopīs. (SB** [**10.29.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/29.13)**)**

In this verse hatred is criticized and kāma is praised. These persons are called *priyāḥ.* The gopīs are not at fault because kāma is essentially filled with prīti or affection. The gopīs’ kāma is actually prema only.

*yat te sujāta-caraṇāmburuhaṁ staneṣu*  
*bhītāḥ śanaiḥ priya dadhīmahi karkaśeṣu*  
*tenāṭavīm aṭasi tad vyathate na kiṁ svit*  
*kūrpādibhir bhramati dhīr bhavad-āyuṣāṁ naḥ*

**O dearly beloved! Your lotus feet are so soft that we place them gently on our breasts, fearing that your feet will be hurt. Our life rests only in you. Our minds, therefore, are filled with anxiety that your tender feet might be wounded by pebbles as you roam about on the forest path. (SB** [**10.31.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/31.19)**)**

They have no desire for their own happiness but think only of what will make Kṛṣṇa happy. The love of Kubjā however is criticized since it lacks the gopīs’ pure intention of pleasing Kṛṣṇa alone, she being predominantly desirous of her own enjoyment. That is made clear when Śukadeva praises her love for its effects (purifying her of lust):

*sānaṅga-tapta-kucayor urasas tathākṣṇor*  
*jighranty ananta-caraṇena rujo mṛjantī*  
*dorbhyāṁ stanāntara-gataṁ parirabhya kāntam*  
*ānanda-mūrtim ajahād ati-dīrgha-tāpam*

**Simply by smelling the fragrance of Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, Trivakrā cleansed away the burning desire Cupid had aroused in her breasts, chest and eyes. With her two arms she embraced between her breasts her lover, Kṛṣṇa, the personification of bliss, and thus she gave up her long-standing distress. (SB** [**10.48.7**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/48.7)**)**

Prīti finally manifested in her:*s*

*ahoṣyatām iha preṣṭha dināni katicin mayā*  
*ramasva notsahe tyaktuṁ saṅgaṁ te ’mburuhekṣaṇa*

**O beloved, please stay here with me for a few days more and enjoy. I cannot bear to give up your association, O lotus-eyed one! (SB** [**10.48.9**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/48.9)**)**

*saivaṁ kaivalya-nāthaṁ taṁ prāpya duṣprāpyam īśvaram*  
*aṅga-rāgārpaṇenāho durbhagedam ayācata*

**Having thus gotten the hard-to-get Supreme Lord by the simple act of offering him body ointment, unfortunate Trivakrā submitted to the bestower of bhakti the following request. (SB** [**10.48.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/48.8)**)**

*durārdhyaṁ samārādhya viṣṇuṁ sarveśvareśvaram*  
*yo vṛṇīte mano-grāhyam asattvāt kumanīṣy asau*

**Kṛṣṇa, endowed with all qualities and powers, able to bestow all benedictions, is ordinarily difficult to approach. One who has properly worshiped him and then chooses the benediction of material enjoyment is certainly of poor intelligence, for he is satisfied with an insignificant result. (SB** [**10.48.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/48.11)**)**

The above verses should be understood as follows. *Kaivalya-nātha* means “he who is to be served by the ekānta devotees.” Previously Kubjā was unfortunate because of her crooked body. How astonishing! Just by offering ointment, a small portion of bhagavad-dharma, her good fortunate was produced. She then asked for the good fortune of having him stay with her. (SB [10.48.8](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/48.8))

The good fortune of Kubjā was something like Śrīdāma’s good fortune as expressed by the citizens of Dvārakā.

*kim anena kṛtaṁ puṇyam avadhūtena bhikṣuṇā*  
*śriyā hīnena loke ’smin garhitenādhamena ca*  
*yo ’sau tri-loka-guruṇā śrī-nivāsena sambhṛtaḥ*  
*paryaṅka-sthāṁ śriyaṁ hitvā pariṣvakto ’gra-jo yathā*

**What pious acts has this unkempt, impoverished brāhmaṇa performed? People regard him as lowly and contemptible, yet the spiritual master of the three worlds, the abode of Lakṣmī, is serving him reverently. Leaving the goddess of fortune sitting on her bed, the Lord has embraced this brāhmaṇa as if he were an older brother. (SB** [**10.80.25-26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/80.25)**)**

“Why should a lusty woman be praised?” This is answered in verse 11. The person who desires material objects (*manograhyam)* is not intelligent *(ku-manīṣī).*She however desired the Lord alone. She was most intelligent. Thus kāma is not to be classed with hatred or anger. Because of that, kāma cannot be considered sinful.

Now, the behavior of Kṛṣṇa being a lusty person involved in kissing cannot considered sinful, for the Lord’s pastimes are accomplished naturally with these acts (not under ignorance). This is their very nature. *Lokavat tu līlā-kaivalyam*: the Lord’s pastimes are similar to actions in this world. (Brahma-sūtra [2.1.33](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/2.1.33))

The Lord has similar pastimes of love with Śrī and Bhū in Vaikuṇṭha. Since these pastimes are eternal there, they should be understood to occur because those pastimes delight the Lord, who enjoys pastimes independently. Kissing the Lord is not unsuitable since the consorts have the most pure forms, not less than the Lord’s form, since they are forms of his svarūpa-śakti. And as previously stated, the Lord enjoys this. And one should not find fault with the women, thinking they are material women. Only after attaining a suitable bhāva and a form arising from his svarūpa-śakti, do these women attain him by his will.

Is kāma sinful because of scriptural statements? The kāma of the consorts is not sinful since it is never stated as such in scriptures. And thus being endowed with the mood that the Lord is the husband is not a fault. Rather it is praised in scriptures.

*yāḥ samparyacaran premṇā pāda-saṁvāhanādibhiḥ*  
*jagad-guruṁ bhartṛ-buddhyā tāsāṁ kim varṇyate tapaḥ*

**And how could one possibly describe the greatness of the worship performed by other women, who serve the Supreme Lord, thinking of him as their husband, by mentally massaging his feet. (SB** [**10.90.27**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/90.27)**)**

It is also recorded that great sages had this bhāva. Madhvācārya quotes the following from Kūrma Purāṇa:

*agni-putrā mahātmānas tapasā strītvam āpire \|*  
*bhartāraṁ ca jagad-yoniṁ vāsudevam ajaṁ vibhum \|\|*

**The saintly sons of Agni attained bodies of women by the path of vaidhi-bhakti*****,*** **and attained as their husband (not lover), the unborn, powerful Vāsudeva, source of the universe.**

Such persons are revered in statements like the following:

*pati-putra-suhṛd-bhrātṛ-pitṛvan mātravad dharim \|*  
*ye dhyāyanti sadodyuktās tebhyo ’pīha namo namaḥ \|\|*

**I pay my respects repeatedly to those who constantly and eagerly meditate upon the Lord as their husband, son, well-wisher, brother, or the meditator as father or mother. (Nārāyaṇa-vyūha-stava)**

Approaching the Lord as an illicit lover is also not sinful. The gopīs say:

*yat paty-apatya-suhṛdām anuvṛttir aṅga*  
*strīṇāṁ sva-dharma iti dharma-vidā tvayoktam*  
*astv evam etad upadeśa-pade tvayīśe*  
*preṣṭho bhavāṁs tanu-bhṛtāṁ kila bandhur ātmā*

**Our dear Kṛṣṇa, as an expert in religion you have advised us that the proper religious duty for women is to faithfully serve their husbands, children and other relatives. We agree that this principle is valid, but actually this service should be rendered to you who give instructions. After all, O Lord, you are the dearest friend of all embodied souls. You are their most intimate friend and their very self. (SB** [**10.29.32**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/29.32)**)**

Śukadeva says:

*gopīnāṁ tat-patīnāṁ ca sarveṣām eva dehinām*  
*yo ’ntaś carati so ’dhyakṣaḥ krīḍaneneha deha-bhāk*

**Kṛṣṇa, appearing directly in this world, moves among the gopīs and their apparent husbands as well as the other inhabitants of Vraja, in his eternal spiritual body performing his pastimes. (SB** [**10.33.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/33.35)**)**

The Lord himself says that the gopīs’ service is flawless:

*na pāraye ’haṁ niravadya-saṁyujāṁ*  
*sva-sādhu-kṛtyaṁ vibudhāyuṣāpi vaḥ*  
*yā mābhajan durjara-geha-śṛṅkhalāḥ*  
*saṁvṛścya tad vaḥ pratiyātu sādhunā*

**I am not able to repay my debt for your spotless service, even within a lifetime of Brahmā. Your connection with me is beyond reproach. You have worshiped me, cutting off all domestic ties, which are difficult to break. Therefore please let your own glorious deeds be your compensation. (SB** [**10.32.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/32.22)**)**

This bhāva is seen in others also.

*purā maharṣayaḥ sarve daṇḍakāraṇya-vāsinaḥ \|*  
*dṛṣṭvā rāmaṁ hariṁ tatra bhoktum aicchat suvigraham \|\|*  
*te sarve strītvam āpannāḥ samudbhūtās tu gokule \|*  
*hariṁ samprāpya kāmena tato muktā bhavārṇavāt \|\|*

**Previously, all the sages living in Daṇḍakāraṇya forest, who after seeing Lord Rāma, desired enjoyment with His form, attained forms of women and appeared in Gokula. Attaining the Lord by that kāma, they became liberated from the ocean of the material world. (Padma Purāṇa 6.245.164)**

Since this mood of being a woman arises even in them, and is directed to the Lord, it does not arise from material Cupid and it is not material lust.

*tāsām āvirabhūc chauriḥ smayamāna-mukhāmbujaḥ*  
*pītāmbara-dharaḥ sragvī sākṣān manmatha-manmathaḥ*

**Then Kṛṣṇa, a smile on his lotus face, appeared before the gopīs, wearing a garland and a yellow garment. He directly appeared as one who can bewilder the mind of Cupid, though Cupid himself bewilders the minds of ordinary people. (SB** [**10.32.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/32.2)**)**

Since the Lord is worshipped in the āgamas as Cupid, this kāma is spiritual, manifested in people by the Lord himself. Great devotees like Uddhava praise this kāma:

*etāḥ paraṁ tanu-bhṛto bhuvi gopa-vadhvo*  
*govinda eva nikhilātmani rūḍha-bhāvāḥ*  
*vāñchanti yad bhava-bhiyo munayo vayaṁ ca*  
*kiṁ brahma-janmabhir ananta-kathā-rasasya*

**Among all persons on earth, these cowherd women alone have attained prefect bodies, for they have achieved mahābhāva for Govinda, the source of all forms of the Lord. Their pure love is desired by those who fear material existence, by great sages, and by ourselves as well. For one who has tasted the narrations of the infinite Lord, what is the use of taking birth as a high-class brāhmaṇa, or even as Brahmā himself? (SB** [**10.47.58**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/47.58)**)**

What is more, it is known from Bṛhad-vāmana Purāṇa that the śrutis had this type of bhāva. It is well known that because the śrutis desired the bhāva of the nitya-siddha gopīs, they became included among them with gopī forms. The śrutis indicate this in the following:

*nibhṛta-marun-mano-’kṣa-dṛḍha-yoga-yujo hṛdi yan*  
*munaya upāsate tad arayo ’pi yayuḥ smaraṇāt*  
*striya uragendra-bhoga-bhuja-daṇḍa-viṣakta-dhiyo*  
*vayam api te samāḥ sama-dṛśo ’ṅghri-saroja-sudhāḥ*

**Simply by constantly thinking of him, the enemies of the Lord attained the same Supreme Truth whom sages fixed in yoga worship by controlling their breath, mind and senses. Similarly, we śrutis, who generally see you as all-pervading, will achieve the same nectar from your lotus feet that your consorts are able to relish because of their loving attraction to your mighty, serpentine arms, for you look upon us and your consorts in the same way. (SB** [**10.87.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/87.23)**)**

The meaning is clear. The entity called Brahman which the sages worship with great effort by scriptural methods was attained by the Lord’s enemies simply by remembering him without worship. The gopīs (*te*) who attached their minds to your two snake-like two arms attained the nectar of your lotus feet, the sweetness of prema generated by your touch. We śrutis, having the same bhāva (*sama-dṛśaḥ*), having attained equality (*samāḥ*) with the gopīs, also attained that sweetness of your feet.

According to the context the verb *yayuḥ* should be conjugated in the first person in reference to the śrutis (*yayima* instead of *yayuh*: we attained). The word *aṅghri* (feet) indicates respect. The enemies attained the Lord quickly by remembering him. This indicates that the path of bhāva quickly yields results. Having the same bhāva *(sama-dṛśaḥ*) indicates rāgānuga, and that it is the most effective. Otherwise the śrutis, who knew all types of sādhanas and sādyas, would have undertaken a different process.

Two groups of people, each with two types, undertake remembrance in the verse. In the first group, the sages who meditate on Brahman are the main subject and the enemies are secondary. In the second group, the gopīs are the main subject and the śrutis are secondary. The word *api* after enemies and śrutis indicates that they attained the same goal as the first subjects in their groups.

The word *śtriyaḥ* means the eternal gopīs. The śrutis saw these gopīs in Kṛṣṇa’s eternal abode according to Bṛhad-vāmana Purāṇa. Thus it is correctly said that among the different bhāvas, hatred and fear are regarded as undesirable and should be given up (*tad-agham hitvā*). (*Kāma* on the other hand has just been explained as most praiseworthy.)

### Text 31

*gopyaḥ kāmād bhayāt kaṁso dveṣāc caidyādayo nṛpāḥ*  
*sambandhād vṛṣṇayaḥ snehād yūyaṁ bhaktyā vayaṁ vibho*

**Перевод:**

**My dear King Yudhiṣṭhira! The** ***gopīs*** **by their conjugal desires, Kaṁsa by his fear, Śiśupāla and other kings by envy, and the Yadus and you Pāṇḍavas by your affectionate family relationships with Kṛṣṇa, and we, by our** ***vaidhi-bhakti,*** **have obtained the mercy of Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 321:**

Nārada stated that many attained the goal (SB [7.1.29](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.28)). That is illustrated.

The gopīs mentioned here are particiular gopīs (not the nitya-siddhas) who previously were performing sādhana. *Vayam* indicates Nārada. He refers to his previous practice by which he attained a spiritual body:

*prayujyamāne mayi tāṁ śuddhāṁ bhāgavatīṁ tanum \|*  
*ārabdha-karma-nirvāṇo nyapatat pāñca-bhautikaḥ \|\|*

**Having been awarded a transcendental body befitting an associate of the Lord, the body made of five material elements, with karmas relating to the present body, fell away. (SB** [**1.6.28**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/6.28)**)**

He had performed vaidhī bhakti. Later, having attained rāga, he attained rāgātmikā bhakti alone without respect for rules. That status is indicated in the following:

*na mayy ekānta-bhaktānāṁ guṇa-doṣodbhavā guṇāḥ*  
*sādhūnāṁ sama-cittānāṁ buddheḥ param upeyuṣām*

**The fully dedicated devotees who see equally everywhere and who have achieved the Lord who is superior to prakṛti do not have the quality which give rise to seeing qualities of good and bad. (SB** [**11.20.36**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/20.36)**)**

Nārada states that those devotees attained the goal. They attained the goal in the past. Like the gopīs, people at the present time can attain that bhāva just by hearing about their qualities. It is said:

*śruta-mātro ’pi yaḥ strīṇāṁ prasahyākarṣate manaḥ*  
*uru-gāyoru-gīto vā paśyantīnāṁ ca kiṁ punaḥ*

**The Lord, whom countless songs glorify in countless ways, forcibly attracts the minds of all women who simply hear about him. What to speak, then, of those women who see him directly? (SB** [**10.90.26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/90.26)**)**

Śiśupāla is called a sādhaka, though he was an associate in Vaikuṇṭha previously, to show the destruction of present, apparent offense by bhāva. Attachment (*snehāt)* arising from being relatives (*sambandhāt)* is illustrated in the Vṛṣṇis and the Pāṇḍavas *(yūyam*). This is one item since the next verse mentions that there are five types of persons. (If sambandha and sneha are taken separately, then there would be six types in the above verse.) Since both are mentioned in the above verse, the purpose is to show the speciality of the attraction as a relative. The Vṛṣṇis and Pāṇḍavas mentioned refers to some of the Vṛṣṇis (who were sādhakas, whereas other Vṛṣṇis were nitya-siddhas) and relatives of the Pāṇḍavas (the Pāṇḍavas were nitya-siddhas) in their state before achieving perfection, when they performed sādhana. “Affection through relationship” means the delight experienced in that relationship. Nārada achieved the goal by bhakti. This means by vaidhī bhakti. Vaidhi is included in the list to show that the path of bhāva can be attained even by following rules.

### Text 32

*katamo 'pi na venaḥ syāt pañcānāṁ puruṣaṁ prati*  
*tasmāt kenāpy upāyena manaḥ kṛṣṇe niveśayet*

**Перевод:**

**Any of the five types of persons, but not King Vena, will attain their objectives in relation to the Lord. Therefore, one should somehow think of Kṛṣṇa, by one of the favorable methods.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 322:**

If one can attain the Lord by hatred, why did Veṇa go to hell? Among the five moods directed to the Lord, Vena is not one of them (*katamaḥ*). He had enmity in the form of a critical attitude towards the Lord, but not constant enmity. Because he lacked intense meditation on the Lord he had to suffer sinful reactions. This means that one should not rashly cultivate an inimical attitude towards the Lord because of demonic nature, in order to gain liberation.

*ye vai bhagavatā proktā upāyā hy ātma-labdhaye*  
*añjaḥ puṁsām aviduṣāṁ viddhi bhāgavatān hi tān*

**Even ignorant living entities can very easily come to know the Supreme Lord if they adopt those means prescribed by the Supreme Lord. The process recommended by the Lord is known as bhāgavata-dharma. (SB** [**11.2.34**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/2.34)**)**

As with the previous verb *yuñjyāt*, the verb *niveśayet* is in the potential mood to express a desire that this should be done as an option, not an order. Among these methods, by the one most suitable, one should think of Kṛṣṇa. What is achieved after a long time by vaidhī bhakti undertaken with great effort is achieved quickly by accepting a particular bhāva, even by hatred. If low persons with enmity towards the Lord, who is filled with the highest qualities, attained the goal, can a low, indifferent person not develop affection for the Lord? Thus rāgānuga is accepted as the most suitable path.

### Text 33

*mātṛ-ṣvasreyo vaś caidyo dantavakraś ca pāṇḍava*  
*pārṣada-pravarau viṣṇor vipra-śāpāt pada-cyutau*

**Перевод:**

**O Pāṇḍava! Your two cousins Śiśupāla and Dantavakra, the sons of your maternal aunt, were formerly associates of Lord Viṣṇu, but because they were cursed by** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **they fell from Vaikuṇṭha to this material world.**

**Комментарий:**

Śiśupāla and Dantavakra were not demons but imitated demons, by the will of the Lord, to increase their bhakti, and show compliance to brāhmaṇas. That is shown through the questions and answers. It was impossible for them to have any inimical feelings for the Lord. That was the meaning of the question. Śruti says *na ca punar āvartate*: one does not return to the material world. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad 8.15.1) *anāvṛttiḥ śabdāt*: one does not return, since that is stated in scripture. (Brahma-sūtra [4.4.22](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/4.4.22)) *Mām upetya tu kaunteya punar janma na vidyate:* coming to me, one does not take birth in the material world again. (BG 8.16)

This is the conclusion, with logic. Therefore this is just a story, which is possible because of the previous reasons. Later it is said:

*tāv atra kṣatriyau jātau mātṛ-ṣvasrātmajau tava*  
*adhunā śāpa-nirmuktau kṛṣṇa-cakra-hatāṁhasau*

**In their third birth, the same Jaya and Vijaya appeared in a family of** ***kṣatriyas*** **as your cousins, the sons of your aunt. When their sins were destroyed by Kṛṣṇa’s** ***cakra,*** **they were free from the curse. (SB** [**7.1.46**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.46)**)**

Commenting on this verse Śrīdhara Svāmī says that the sins of the two were destroyed, but not the two persons.

Though I can nullify the curse of the** ***brāhmaṇa*****, I do not desire to do go against my own rules.” (SB** [**3.16.29**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/16.29)**)**

### Text 7-8

*tasya tyakta-svabhāvasya ghṛṇer māyā-vanaukasaḥ*

**Viṣṇu has given up his neutral nature. Though he was shining, he is now an animal under the control of** ***māyā.*** **He has accepted the nature of an ordinary person and acts unsteadily like a small child. Relieving my pain, I will satisfy my brother who was fond of blood, with the profuse blood from the neck of Viṣṇu severed by my trident.**

**Комментарий:**

These verses are taken together. Actually the meaning is “he has given his nature of sārūpya etc. to his devotees (*tyakta-svabhāvasya*), and thus shines (*ghṛneḥ*).” By his mercy *(māyā*) he accepts pastimes as various animals (*vanaukasaḥ*). By his mercy he assumes many bhāvas according to the bhava of the devotee, and thus appears as an unsteady child. He has a neck which is beyond the reach (*bhinna*) of my trident. I will satisfy my blood-loving brother by my blood, when Nṛsiṁha appears and kills me.

*yathā taror mūla-niṣecanena*  
*tṛpyanti tat-skandha-bhujopaśākhāḥ*  
*prāṇopahārāc ca yathendriyāṇāṁ*  
*tathaiva sarvārhaṇam acyutejyā*

**Just as the branches and twigs are satisfied by watering the root, and just as the senses are satisfied by nourishing the life air, by worshipping the Supreme Lord all are worship is accomplished. (SB** [**4.31.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/31.14)**)**

Because he is killed, the devatās will die. Or “by being killed by the Lord the brother obtained a pious destination. What is the use of doing tarpaṇas?” He then speaks this verse.

The other meaning is as follows. When Viṣṇu who spreads (*hite)* his one form over all time (*kūṭe*) disappears in the heart of the devotee (*naṣṭe*), the inhabitants of the material and spiritual worlds will dry up, since he is their very life. This means they will not enjoy various types of pleasure. They will just worship him.

If you have a desire to see him, then go to earth.

Two verses go together. Though he is not the body but ātmā (*aliṅge*) he identifies with bodies.

## Chapter Three

What is the use of other places? This is the opinion of a demon.

*adrākṣam aham etaṁ te hṛt-sāraṁ mahad-adbhutam*

First his body was bitten by gnats (*daṁśa*) and later by ants (as mentioned previously).

### Text 32

*tvattaḥ paraṁ nāparam apy anejad*  
*ejac ca kiñcid vyatiriktam asti*

*hiraṇyagarbho 'si bṛhat tri-pṛṣṭhaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**No cause or effect, no non-moving or moving being, is separate from you. The Vedas and its** ***aṅgas*** **are all your bodies. You are the person containing the universe in your belly. You are Brahman. You are separate from the three** ***guṇas.***

**Комментарий:**

*Vidyāḥ kāḷāḥ* means the Vedas and Upavedas (*vidyāḥ*) and their aṅgas (*kalāḥ*) according to Śrīdhara Svāmī. Another version is *kalāśaḥ*, but this is not correct.

### Text 34

*anantāvyakta-rūpeṇa yenedam akhilaṁ tatam*  
*cid-acic-chakti-yuktāya tasmai bhagavate namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respect to you, the powerful lord, endowed with spiritual and material potencies by which you pervade the whole world with an unlimited and unmanifest form.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī explains this. O unlimited Brahmā! You are spread through the whole universe with a form undetected by mind and words. This meaning is according to Vaiṣṇava siddhānta. *Cit-śakti* refers to the internal energy and *acit-śakti* refers to the external energy (māyā).

### Text 35

*bhūtebhyas tvad-visṛṣṭebhyo mṛtyur mā bhūn mama prabho*

**O my lord! O best of the givers of benediction! If you will kindly grant me the benediction that I desire, then please let me not die from any of the living entities created by you.**

**Комментарий:**

He asks for a boon by trickery to attain immortality, though Brahmā would say he could not grant immortality. *Tvad-visṛṣtebhyaḥ* means “by beings directly created by you.”

### Text 36

*nāntar bahir divā naktam anyasmād api cāyudhaiḥ*  
*na bhūmau nāmbare mṛtyur na narair na mṛgair api*

**Перевод:**

**Grant me that I not die within any residence or outside any residence, during the daytime or at night, not by any weapon or something other than a weapon, nor on the ground or in the sky, nor by man or animal.**

**Комментарий:**

*Anyasmāt* means “by things created by Śiva or the Prajāpatis.” He did not think that there was anyone above Brahmā. He excludes possible killers.

### Text 37-38

*vyasubhir vāsumadbhir vā surāsura-mahoragaiḥ*  
*apratidvandvatāṁ yuddhe aika-patyaṁ ca dehinām*  
*sarveṣāṁ loka-pālānāṁ mahimānaṁ yathātmanaḥ*  
*tapo-yoga-prabhāvāṇāṁ yan na riṣyati karhicit*

**Перевод:**

**Let me not die by non-living things or living beings,** ***devatās,*** **demons or snakes. Let me not be defeated in battle and have great lordship over all beings and all protectors of planets. Let me have powers of austerity and yoga just as you have, whose potency will never be destroyed!**

**Комментарий:**

Because the Lord has non-material life he is not included in non-living and living beings. These are included in the list of possible killers. *Vyasubhiḥ vāsubhiḥ* does not refer to instruments for killing. Even if the killer had a body like a jīva, avoiding the living body, the nails could be used as an instrument, since they are considered non-living. However, living and non-living are nonexistent in the Lord.

*tvak-śmaśru-roma-nakha-keśa-pinaddham antar*  
*māṁsāsthi-rakta-kṛmi-viṭ-kapha-pitta-vātam*  
*jīvac-chavaṁ bhajati kānta-matir vimūḍhā*  
*yā te padābja-makarandam ajighratī strī*

**A woman who fails to relish the fragrance of the honey of your lotus feet becomes totally befooled, and thus she accepts as her husband or lover a living corpse covered with skin, whiskers, nails, head-hair and body-hair and filled with flesh, bones, blood, parasites, feces, mucus, bile and air. (SB** [**10.60.45**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/60.45)**)**

By condemning material bodily parts, she indicates that the Lord has spiritual body parts.

*na tasya prākṛtā mūrtir māṁsa-medo ‘sthi-sambhavā*  
*na yogitvād īśvaratvāt satya-rūpo ‘cyuto vibhuḥ*

**The Lord does not have a material form made of bones, fat and marrow. It does not arise from yoga practice. Because he is the supreme Lord, his form is eternal, invincible and all-pervading. (Varāha Purāṇa)**

### Text 37

**Комментарий:**

Because his mind was absorbed in Kṛṣṇa, he gave up playing. And later, by that absorption he became like a dull person. Later, completely absorbed in Kṛṣṇa he did not see this world as other people see it. He saw it as a manifestation of the Lord.

### Text 38

He trembled as if embraced by Govinda (*parirambhitaḥ*).

### Text 39

**Комментарий:**

First, he would worry and tremble because of separation. Then when the Lord would manifest and then disappear, he would sometimes weep. He would laugh when the Lord manifested and call out loudly in separation. In meeting and separation he would become absorbed in the Lord (*cintā*).

### Text 41

**Sometimes with hair standing on end, he would remain silent, blissful because of the touch of the Lord’s hand. Without movement, he would remain with his eyes half-closed, filled with tears of joy, expressing his affection for the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

On meeting the Lord sometimes his hair would stand on end. The touch of the Lord means the Lord would manifest and embrace him out of mercy.

### Text 42

*sa uttama-śloka-padāravindayor*

## Chapter Five

### Text 11

*śrī-prahrāda uvāca*  
*paraḥ svaś cety asad-grāhaḥ puṁsāṁ yan-māyayā kṛtaḥ*  
*vimohita-dhiyāṁ dṛṣṭas tasmai bhagavate namaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Prahlāda said: Let me offer my respectful obeisances unto the Supreme Lord, whose external energy has created the distinctions of "my friend" and "my enemy" for men of deluded intelligence, which I can see directly now.**

**Комментарий:**

*Puṁsām* refers to the jīvas as in the following:

*bhayaṁ dvitīyābhiniveśataḥ syād*  
*īśād apetasya viparyayo 'smṛtiḥ*  
*tan-māyayāto budha ābhajet taṁ*  
*bhaktyaikayeśaṁ guru-devate ātmā*

**For the** ***jīva*** **averse to the Lord, there will be** ***saṁsāra*** **consisting of identity with body and lack of identity with the soul, because of his absorption in the material coverings on the soul, arising from the Lord’s** ***māyā.*** **Therefore, the intelligent person, taking** ***guru*** **as his Lord and very self, should fully worship the Lord with pure** ***bhakti.*** **SB** [**11.2.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/2.37)

Men (*pumsām*), low and envious, jīvas who are unfavorable to the Lord, are particularly deluded (*vimohita-dhiyām*), thinking they are the body while forgetter their svarūpa, by the māyā of the Lord (*yan-māyayā).* They make false distincitions of others and relatives (*svaḥ*). I offer respects to the Lord, whose māyā does this. I offer respects so that these people can get relief from their involvement in māyā. In the neuter, *svam* would mean the self, and in masculine it is a relative according to Amara-koṣa.

### Text 12

*sa yadānuvrataḥ puṁsāṁ paśu-buddhir vibhidyate*  
*anya eṣa tathānyo 'ham iti bheda-gatāsatī*

**Перевод:**

**When the Lord is favorable to a** ***jīva*****, his animal intelligence is destroyed. Conceptions of difference between me and others are wrong.**

**Комментарий:**

When the Lord becomes favorable to the person, when the person accepts the Lord by association with devotees, the animal intelligence, created as previously described, is destroyed. That animal intelligence is described: it is thinking this person is another and I am another and that the followers of artha and kāma scriptures who follow devotees are all temporary.

### Text 13

*sa eṣa ātmā sva-parety abuddhibhir*  
*duratyayānukramaṇo nirūpyate*  
*muhyanti yad-vartmani veda-vādino*  
*brahmādayo hy eṣa bhinatti me matim*

**Перевод:**

**People with faulty intelligence, thinking in terms of self and others, cannot know Paramātmā. And even the followers of the Vedas such as Brahmā are bewildered about the path to attain him. That Lord has made my intelligence different from yours.**

**Комментарий:**

He (*saḥ)*, Paramātmā, who seems to have appeared to my vision, is known by bad intelligence which thinks of one’s relatives and others, according to foolish methods (*duratyayānukramaṇaḥ*). But even persons learned in the Vedas are bewildered on this path. And even Brahmā is bewildered. He has made my intelligence different from yours.

### Text 14

*yathā bhrāmyaty ayo brahman svayam ākarṣa-sannidhau*  
*tathā me bhidyate cetaś cakra-pāṇer yadṛcchayā*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***brāhmaṇa*****! As iron moves automatically toward the magnet, my consciousness moves on its own towards Viṣṇu and moves away from your consciousness.**

**Комментарий:**

“He alone has made your intelligence different, not us. He has made only your intelligence different, and he has not made our intelligence different. How is this?” My consciousness on its own (*yadṛcchayā*), not depending on an outside cause, moves to Viṣṇu, just as iron moves to a magnet. That is the nature of both. That is proper.

### Text 15

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*etāvad brāhmaṇāyoktvā virarāma mahā-matiḥ*  
*taṁ sannibhartsya kupitaḥ sudīno rāja-sevakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said: The great soul Prahlāda became silent after saying this to the** ***brāhmaṇa.*** **Angry, the King’s servant, in great distress, chastised him and spoke.**

**Комментарий:**

There are two sentences in this verse.

### Text 16

*ānīyatām are vetram asmākam ayaśaskaraḥ*  
*kulāṅgārasya durbuddheś caturtho 'syodito damaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Oh, a stick should be brought! This Prahlāda is damaging our fame. For such bad intelligence, a cinder in the dynasty of the demons, the fourth method, punishment, is recommended in scriptures.**

**Комментарий:**

The stick should be brought by you, because he damages our fame. He does not say “You should bring a stick” since seeing the boy’s tenderness he was not able to act conspicuously.

### Text 17

*daiteya-candana-vane jāto 'yaṁ kaṇṭaka-drumaḥ*  
*yan-mūlonmūla-paraśor viṣṇor nālāyito 'rbhakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**A thorn tree has been born in a forest of sandalwood. This boy is the handle of the axe named Viṣṇu who wants to cut down the sandalwood forest.**

**Комментарий:**

Moreover, the boy is the handle of Viṣṇu who is endowed with a pure form. This is highly improper.

### Text 23-24

*śrī-prahrāda uvāca*  
*śravaṇaṁ kīrtanaṁ viṣṇoḥ smaraṇaṁ pāda-sevanam*  
*arcanaṁ vandanaṁ dāsyaṁ sakhyam ātma-nivedanam*  
*iti puṁsārpitā viṣṇau bhaktiś cen nava-lakṣaṇā*  
*kriyeta bhagavaty addhā tan manye 'dhītam uttamam*

**Перевод:**

**Prahlāda said: If any of the nine processes of** ***bhakti*** **--hearing, chanting, remembering, menial service, deity worship, offering prayers, servitude, friendship, and offering body and soul—is performed for the Lord by any human without prerequisites, after being offered to Viṣṇu, that should be considered the highest learning.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 169:**

“Hearing, chanting and remembering of the Lord’s names, qualities and pastimes” is meant. *Pāda-sevanam* means serving the deity. *Arcanam* is worship according to rules. *Vandanam* means offering respects. *Dāsyam* means thinking one is the Lord’s servant. *Sakhyam* means desiring benefit for the Lord as his friend. *Ātma-nivedanam* means offering the Lord one’s body and everything else for his worship, just as a horse or cow is offered for sale, and does not worry about being maintained or protected. Examples are given by the sages.

Bhakti with these nine types, which is direct (*addhā*), not bhakti based on family tradition or an offering of karmas, is performed, directed to the Lord *(bhagavati*). It is offered for Viṣṇu, not for dharma, artha etc. If it is performed in this way by the performer, I consider it the highest learning.

*bhaktir asya bhajanam \| tad ihāmutropādhi-nairāsyenaivāmuṣmin*  
*manaḥ-kalpanam \| etad eva ca naiṣkarmyam*

**Bhajanam or service means devotional service to Kṛṣṇa. It is concentration of the mind on Kṛṣṇa without desires for enjoyment in this life or the next. It destroys all karma. (Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad** [**1.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/1.14)**)**

Though there are nine types, it is not necessary to perform all nine, for one hears that by one type one attains the result. Sometimes the types are mixed because of different faith and taste. The term *nava-lakṣaṇa* denotes bhakti in general. One should perform that bhakti. If one performs one type, the others are also included in it.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 256:**

Though one can attain perfection by hearing the name, forms, qualities and pastimes in any order, one should first hear the name in order to purify the antaḥkaraṇa. When the antaḥkaraṇa is pure one develops qualification for hearing about the form. When the form has been manifested fully, the qualities then manifest. When the name, form, qualities and associates manifest completely the pastimes manifest nicely. This is the sequence of sādhana that has been recorded. The same sequence applies to chanting and remembering.

If hearing takes place from the mouths of great devotees, there is great power manifested and the highest bliss for those who have developed taste. Hearing from the mouths of great devotees has two types: hearing something composed by a great devotee and hearing some scripture from the mouth of a great devotee.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 260**

In hearing about the Lord, hearing Bhāgavatam is the best, since its words are most powerful and it is most tasty.

*labdhvānugraha ācāryāt tena sandarśitāgamaḥ*  
*mahā-puruṣam abhyarcen mūrtyābhimatayātmanaḥ*

**Having obtained the mercy of his guru, who reveals to the disciple the injunctions of Vedic scriptures, the devotee should worship the Supreme Lord in the particular personal form of the Lord the devotee finds most attractive. (SB** [**11.3.48**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/3.48)**)**

It is most auspicious to hear about Kṛṣṇa, among all the forms of the Lord, from the mouth of a great devotee with similar inclinations, since Kṛṣṇa is the complete manifestation of Bhagavān. The same should be considered concerning chanting and other types of bhakti. Whatever is being chanted by a person at the present time should follow after what great devotees like Śukadeva have chanted.

Hearing has been shown. Hearing has been explained before chanting and other processes, because without hearing one will not know the other processes. If one does not have the good fortune of hearing from a great devotee, then one should chant for oneself to hear, since hearing is the principal process.

*tad-vāg-visargo janatāgha-viplavo*  
*yasmin prati-ślokam abaddhavaty api \|*  
*nāmāny anantasya yaśo ’ṅkitāni yac*  
*chṛṇvanti gāyanti gṛṇanti sādhavaḥ \|\|*

**The use of words in which, though not perfectly composed, there are the names of the Lord and descriptions of his glories which devotees hear, sing, and again receive, destroys the sins of all people. (SB** [**1.5.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/5.11)**)**

The commentary says, “If a speaker is present devotees hear the names from him, if a hearer is present, the devotees chant the names for his benefit, and if neither is present, the devotees sing for themselves.”

Now chanting will be discussed. As previously, there is chanting of the names, forms, qualities and pastimes of the Lord.

Chanting the name loudly is best:

*nāmāny anantasya hata-trapaḥ paṭhan*  
*guhyāni bhadrāṇi kṛtāni ca smaran \|*  
*gāṁ paryaṭaṁs tuṣṭa-manā gata-spṛhaḥ*  
*kālaṁ pratīkṣan vimado vimatsaraḥ \|\|*

**Giving up shyness, I began to chant the names of the unlimited Lord, and to remember his most excellent, hidden pastimes. I wandered the earth with satisfied mind, without material desires, without pride or selfishness, waiting for that time. (SB** [**1.6.26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/6.26)**)**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 270:**

The miserable condition in Kali-yuga is described:

*ataḥ kalau tapo-yoga-vidyā-yājñādikāḥ kriyāḥ \|*  
*sāṅgāḥ bhavanti na kṛtāḥ kuśalair api dehibhiḥ \|\|*

**In Kali-yuga austerity, yoga, knowledge, sacrifices, duties, and association are not undertaken even by skillful persons. (Brahma-vaivarta Purāṇa)**

Appearing in Kali-yuga for people most unfortunate by nature, saṅkīrtana very easily gives all results among the great processes for the respective yugas and makes those processes successful. Thus in Kali-yuga the Lord is especially pleased by saṅkīrtana.

*tathā caivottamaṁ loke tapaḥ śrī-hari-kīrtanam \|*  
*kalau yuge viśeṣeṇa viṣṇu-prītyai samācaret \|\|*

**Chanting the Lord’s name, the best austerity for the people, should particularly be performed in Kali-yuga to please the Lord. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 273:**

However, one should not say that kīrtana becomes glorious because of association with Kali-yuga since there is no restriction of time and place for bhakti.

Thus in all yugas, kīrtana has the same potency. In Kali-yuga, by the mercy of the Lord, this method is to be accepted. Thus it is praised for Kali-yuga.

Though other processes of bhakti are to be performed, they are done along with kīrtana:

*kṛṣṇa-varṇaṁ tviṣākṛṣṇaṁ sāṅgopāṅgāstra-pārṣadam*  
*yajñaiḥ saṅkīrtana-prāyair yajanti hi su-medhasaḥ*

**The intelligent people worship the Lord, black in color, but shining with brilliance, who is accompanied by his associates, weapons, major and minor limbs, by various types of worship but chiefly through chanting. (SB** [**11.5.32**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/5.32)**)**

Nāma-kīrtana is especially recommended, since it is independent:

*harer nāma harer nāma harer nāmaiva kevalam \|*  
*kalau nāsty eva nāsty eva nāsty eva gatir anyathā \|\|*

**The name of the Lord, the name of the Lord, the name of the Lord alone is the shelter. There is no other shelter in Kali-yuga. (Nāradīya Purāṇa)**

Therefore it was rightly said:

*kaliṁ sabhājayanty āryā guṇa jñāḥ sāra-bhāginaḥ*  
*yatra saṅkīrtanenaiva sarva-svārtho ’bhilabhyate*

**Those who are actually advanced in knowledge, who know quality, who have accepted the best, praise Kali-yuga because in this fallen age all perfection of life can easily be achieved by the performance of saṅkīrtana. (SB** [**11.5.36**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/5.36)**)**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 265:**

One should avoid the ten offenses as stated in Padma Purāṇa. Sanat-kumāra says:

*sarvāparādha-kṛd api mucyate hari-saṁśrayāt \|*  
*harer apy aparādhān yaḥ kuryād dvipada-pāṁsanaḥ \|\|*  
*nāmāśrayaḥ kadācit syāt taraty eva sa nāmataḥ \|*  
*nāmno’pi sarva-suhṛdo hy aparādhāt pataty adhaḥ \|\|*

**A person who commits all offenses is freed by taking shelter of the Lord. If a person, personified evil, commits offenses to the Lord, by taking shelter of the name, is delivered by the name. By offending the name even a person who is friendly to all beings falls to hell.**

*satāṁ nindā nāmnaḥ paramam aparādhaṁ vitanute*  
*yataḥ khyātiṁ yātaṁ katham u sahate tad vigarhām \|*

**One who criticizes devotees commits the greatest offense because how can the name tolerate criticism of those who have spread the glories of the name?**

*śivasya śrī viṣṇor ya iha guṇa nāmādi sakalaṁ*  
*dhiyābhinnaṁ paśyet sa khalu hari nāmāhita karaḥ \|\|*

**He who sees the names and qualities of Śiva and Viṣṇu to be the same injures the Lord’s name.**

*guror avajñā śruti śāstra nindanam*  
*tathārtha vādo hari nāmni kalpanam \|*  
*nāmno balād yasya hi pāpa buddhir*  
*na vidyate tasya yamair hi śuddhiḥ \|\|*

**Disrespecting the guru and criticizing the scriptures are offenses. It is an offense to think that the glories of the name are exaggerated or to give imaginary meanings to the name. One who commits sin on the strength of the name cannot be purified by following rules.**

*dharma vrata tyāga hutādi sarva*  
*śubha kriyā sāmyam api pramādaḥ \|*  
*aśraddadhāne vimukhe ’py aśṛṇvati*  
*yaś copadeśaḥ śiva nāmāparādhaḥ \|\|*

**Thinking that the chanting of the name is no greater than following dharma, practicing vratas and renunciation, or performing sacrifices or other pious acts is madness.**

*śrutvāpi nāma māhātmye yaḥ prīti rahito ’dhamaḥ \|*  
*ahaṁ mamādi paramo nāmni so ’py aparādha kṛt \|\|*

**The low person who after hearing the glories of the name is still without devotion and is greatly entrenched in concepts of me and mine commits offense.**

The atonement for these offenses is stated:

*nāmāparādha-yuktānāṁ nāmāny eva haranty agham \|*  
*aviśrānta-prayuktāni tāny evārtha-karāṇi ca \|\|*

**For those who have committed offenses to the name, chanting the name incessantly will be effective in destroying that sin. (Padma Purāṇa)**

If one offends a devotee, it is advised to continually chant the name in order to satisfy the devotee. It is seen in the story of Ambarīṣa that the offense to Ambarīṣa was mitigated only by Durvāsā approaching Ambarīṣa as directed by the Lord. It is said in Nāma-kaumudī, *mahad-aparādhasya bhoga eva nivartakaḥ tad-anugraho vā*: one can eradicate a great offense by experiencing its results or by obtaining mercy of the devotee.

Dakṣa says to Śiva:

*yo ’sau mayāvidita-tattva-dṛśā sabhāyāṁ*  
*kṣipto durukti-viśikhair vigaṇayya tan mām*  
*arvāk patantam arhattama-nindayāpād*  
*dṛṣṭyārdrayā sa bhagavān sva-kṛtena tuṣyet*

**Though I insulted you with harsh words in the assembly, not knowing the truth about you, you did not consider the insult significant, and you, by your glance of mercy, saved me, who had fallen down by my offense to the most respectable person. You should be satisfied with your show of mercy. (SB** [**4.7.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/7.15)**)**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 275:**

Kīrtana includes recitation of verses expressing one’s low condition and one’s desire for the Lord. Kīrtana of the name, form and qualities from the Bhāgavatam is superior to kīrtana using verses from other sources.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 275:**

If the antaḥkaraṇa has been purified by surrender, one should perform *smaraṇam* without giving up nāma-kīrtana:

*etan nirvidyamānānām icchatām akuto-bhayam \|*  
*yogināṁ nṛpa nirṇītaṁ harer nāmānukīrtanam \|\|*

**O King! Continuous chanting of the name of the Lord has been prescribed for devotees, for those with desires for liberation and material elevation, and for those satisfied with the self. (SB** [**2.1.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/1.11)**)**

*Smaraṇam* means reflecting with the mind. There are many varieties using the name, form etc.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 278:**

As previously explained, there is a sequence in the *smaraṇam* to make it easy: remembering the name and form and then the qualities, the associates, service and pastimes. There are five types of remembrance. Thinking of the Lord somehow or other is called *smaraṇam*. Withdrawing the mind completely from other objects and in a general way concentrating the mind on the Lord is called dhāraṇā. Contemplating the form, qualities etc. particularly is called dhyānam. When this becomes continuous it is called dhruvānusmṛtiḥ. When the Lord appears through meditation it is called samādhi.

Sometimes there is samādhi with an exclusive appearance of the Lord engaged in pastimes.

This type of samādhī is experienced by devotees with dāsya and other rasas. The previous anuccheda described the form (with no pastimes) generally experienced by śānta devotees.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 280:**

Then if one has taste and ability one should engage in serving the feet without giving up smaraṇam etc. This service is sometimes done in order to perfect smaraṇam.

The word *pāda* in pāda-sevanam means “with bhakti.” Thus the service should be performed with respect and devotion. Sevā consists of service according to time and place.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 283:**

Seeing, touching, circling and following after the deity of the Lord, and bathing at the tīrthas of the Lord’s temples, or at the Gaṅgā, Puri, Dvārakā and Mathurā are included in serving the feet of the Lord since they accompany service. Living one’s whole life at a temple or tīrtha is included in śaranāpatti. Living entities residing at these holy places are the highest devotees. Thus serving these places is great service to the devotees. Thus these places are causes of bhakti.

If one has faith in this path of arcana one should ask the details from the mantra guru under whom one has taken shelter.

The path of arcana as described in Pañcarātra scriptures is not necessary according to Bhāgavatam, since even without it one can attain perfection of the puruṣārtha, even by any one among the methods of bhakti like śaranāpatti.

BRS [1.2.265](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/1.2.265):

The ancient sages have said:

Parīkṣit is an example of hearing about the Lord and Śukadeva is an example of chanting the glories of the Lord. Prahlāda is an example of remembering the Lord and Lakṣmī is an example of serving the lotus feet of the Lord. Pṛthu is an example of performing deity worship of the Lord. Akrūra is an example of attaining perfection by offering prayers to the Lord. Hanumān is an example of service with the attitude of a servant of the Lord. Arjuna is an example of friendship with the Lord. Bali is an example of offering the self to the Lord. They achieved Kṛṣṇa by following principally one *aṅga*.

However, if the person desires a specific relationship with the Lord, which is accomplished through the mercy of guru by the process of dīkṣā, following the path of Nārada and other sages, he should necessarily perform arcana after taking dīkṣā,.

Arcana is mainly for wealthy householders. If the wealthy householder does not perform deity worship and instead become fixed in remembering or other processes like a renunciate, there will be the fault of cheating (not using his wealth for the Lord and going beyond his qualification). If he arranges for others to do the worship, it means he is more inclined to material affairs or he is lazy. He is misfortunate, because of his lack of faith

Though there is no difference between paricārya-marga (serving the deity, not direct service) and arcana (directly serving the Lord) in terms of serving through various materials, arcana is the main method for householders because it makes them follow many rules. For householders, devatā worship is like watering the branches of a tree and worshipping the Lord through arcana is like watering the root. Not performing arcana is a great fault.

Persons who have received dīkṣā but do not worship the deity are said to fall to hell.

If one cannot do deity worship or is unqualified, it is said:

*pūjitaṁ pūjyamānaṁ vā yaḥ paśyed bhaktimato harim \|*  
*śraddhayā modayed yas tu so ’pi yoga-phalaṁ labhet \|\|*

**If a person sees with devotion the Lord being worshipped or after he has been worshipped, who permits the worship with faith, he attains the results of performing the worship. (Agni Purāṇa)**

The word *yoga* in the verses means kriyā-yoga explained in Pañcarātra scriptures.

However on the path of arcana, one must follow the rules. Therefore before doing deity worship one must take dīkṣā. The authority for the rules is in accordance with a Vaiṣṇava sampradāya.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 284:**

Someone may question, “The mantra’s essential element is the name of the Lord. That is additionally ornamented by words like *namaḥ.* Endowed with special śakti by the Lord and the sages (who reveal the mantra), it produces a particular relationship between the self and the Lord. If the names of the Lord are independent and have the ability by themselves of bestowing all results up to the highest human goal (prema), and since the mantras have more power than the name, how can the mantras be dependent on dīkṣā?”

Even though by its svarūpa the mantra is not dependent on dīkṣā, generally for persons of low nature and distracted minds because of relationship with their bodies, arising from their nature, the sages have at some time established some rules on the path of arcana for curtailing that low nature. If one breaks those rules one must perform atonement according to scripture.

There is thus no contradiction between the mantra and the name.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 284:**

There are two types of arcana: pure and mixed with karma. Pure arcana is practiced by persons with faith, with no dependence at all on anything.

Mixed worship is performed by persons with faith based on haphazard performance of bhakti with strong inclination to traditional customs. There are also performers of mixed arcana who are householders with fixed faith in scriptural procedures, unlike the first type, who feel obligated to teach ordinary people. They think that one should not disregard performance of ordinary Vedic karmas and thus they put on a show for persons who cannot understand the path of bhakti.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 299:**

Its secondary elements are understood in the āgamas. Included in arcana are observance of Janmāṣṭamī, Kārttika-vrata, Ekādaśī and bathing during Māgha month.

On the paths of pāda-sevana and arcana one must give up offenses as stated in the scriptures.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 303:**

If one happens to offend the Lord by inattention one should perform acts to please the Lord. Vyāsa says:

*ahany ahani yo martyo gītādhyāyaṁ paṭhet tu vai \|*  
*dvātriṁśad-aparādhāṁs tu kṣamate tasya keśavaḥ \|\|*

**The person who recites a chapter of the Gītā daily is excused of the thirty-two offenses by the Lord. (Skanda Purāṇa, Avantī-khaṇḍa)**

*sahasra-nāma-māhātmyaṁ yaḥ paṭhec chṛṇuyād api \|*  
*aparādha-sahasreṇa na sa lipyet kadācana \|\|*

**He who recites or hears the glorification of the thousand names of Viṣṇu cannot be contaminated by a thousand offenses. (Skanda Purāṇa, Dvārakā-māhātmya)**

*dvādaśyāṁ jāgare viṣṇor yaḥ paṭhet tulasī-stavam \|*  
*dvātriṁśad-aparādhāni kṣamate tasya keśavaḥ \|\|*

**He who stays awake on Dvādaśī and reads the tulasī prayers is pardoned of the thirty-two offenses by the Lord. (Skanda Purāṇa Revā-khaṇḍa)**

*tulasyā ropaṇaṁ kāryaṁ śrāvaṇeṣu viśeṣataḥ \|*  
*aparādha-sahasrāṇi kṣamate puruṣottamaḥ \|\|*

**One should especially plant tulasī in Śrāvaṇa month. The Lord pardons a thousand offenses. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

*tulasyā kurute yas tu śālagrāma-śilārcanam \|*  
*dvātriṁśad-aparādhāṁś ca kṣamate tasya keśavaḥ \|\|*

**One who worships śālagrāma with tulasī is forgiven by the Lord for committing the thirty-two offenses. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

*yaḥ karoti hareḥ pūjāṁ kṛṣṇa-śastrāṅkito naraḥ \|*  
*aparādha-sahasrāṇi nityaṁ harati keśavaḥ \|\|*

**The person who, ornamented with Kṛṣṇa’s weapons, worships the Lord is relieved of a thousand offenses by the Lord. (Quoted in Hari-bhakti-vilāsa** [**4.286**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/hbv/2.4.286)**)**

*saṁvatsarasya madhye tu tīrthe śaukarake mama \|*  
*kṛtopavāsaḥ snānena gaṅgāyāṁ śuddhim āpnuyāt \|\|*  
*mathurāyāṁ tathāpy evaṁ sāparādhaḥ śuci bhavet \|*  
*anayos tīrthayor ekaṁ yaḥ seveta sukṛtī naraḥ \|\|*  
*sahasra-janma-janitān aparādhān jahāti saḥ \|\|*

**One who fasts at my tīrtha during the year and bathes in the Gaṅgā becomes pure. If one does the same at Mathurā one becomes purified of offences. The pious person who serves one of these two tīrthas frees himself of offenses produced for a thousand births. (Ādi-varāha Purāṇa)**

*Śaukarake* means “at Varāha’s tīrtha.”

**Bhakti Sandarbha 283:**

Sometimes mānasa-puja is prescribed if one is incapable or unqualified of doing external worship. It is said:

*sādhāraṇaṁ hi sarveṣāṁ mānasejyā nṝṇāṁ priyā*

**Mānasa-pūja is suitable for all ordinary men. (Padma Purāṇa)**

In glorifying mānasa-pūja the Lord says:

*ayaṁ yo mānaso yogo jarā-vyādhi-bhayāpahaḥ*  
*yaś caitat parayā bhaktyā sakṛt kuryān mahāmate \|*  
*kramoditena vidhinā tasya tuṣyāmy ahaṁ mune \|\|*

**Mānasa-pūja counteracts fear of old age and illness. O great soul! I am pleased with the person who even once performs mānasa-pūja with intense bhakti according to the rules at the proper time. (Nārada-pañcarātra)**

Mānasa-pūja may be done independently of the arcana process (which is largely external) since the mental deity is one of the eight forms of the Lord used in deity worship. *Arcādau hṛdaye cāpi yathā-labdhopacārakaiḥ:* the devotee should prepare properly obtained ingredients like the earth and the body and then offer articles to the deity or the mind. (SB [11.3.50](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/3.50))

BRS 182:

There is a story related in the *Brahma-vaivarta* *Purāṇa*. There once lived a *brāhmaṇa* in the city of Pratiṣṭhāna. Though he was poor, he was peaceful, considering that he was reaping his *karma.* Being of sincere mind, one time he heard about Vaiṣṇava prac­tices in an assembly of *brāhmaṇas*. Hearing that these processes could bring perfection just by engaging the mind, the impoverished man began to practice this. Finishing his daily rituals after taking bath in the Godāvarī, becoming peaceful in mind, he would sit down in a solitary place and perform the preliminaries of worship such as *prāṇāyāma*. Becoming steady in mind, he would mentally establish the deity form of his dear Lord. Mentally putting on a fine cloth, he would offer the Lord his respects, pull up his cloth and begin cleaning the Lord’s temple. Offering respects again, he would then bring water in gold and silver pots from all the *tīrthas* headed by the Gaṅgā, and gather various materials for serving the Lord. After bathing the Lord, he would perform *ārātrika* and offer items befitting a king to the deity in his mind. After completing that worship, he would experience great happiness, which would last the whole day. After practicing for some time in this way, he once prepared in his mind some milk rice sprinkled with ghee and put it in a gold pot for the Lord to eat. In doing this, his two thumbs happened to enter into the prepara­tion that was bubbling from the heat. Realizing what had happened, he exclaimed, “How unfortunate! This offering has been spoiled!” With sorrow, he rejected the offer­ing. His *samādhi* then broke, and he felt great pain because of his burned thumbs. Understanding this, the Lord seated in Vaikuṇṭha laughed. Lakṣmī and others asked Him the cause of His laughter. Then the Lord brought him in an airplane to His side and showed Lakṣmī his burnt thumbs. He then fixed the *brāhmaṇa* permanently by His side because of his qualifications.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 303:**

Now vandanam will be discussed. Though vandanam is also a part of arcana, it is dealt with separately since it can be done independent of arcana also, like smaraṇam and kīrtana. This should be understood for other types of bhakti also. Vandanam is prescribed as a separate act for persons who feel too low to engage in serving the Lord’s feet or to inquire further about his qualities, after hearing about the unlimited qualities and powers of the Lord. They thus concentrate on offering respects (namskāra). Namaskāra is a substitute for arcana.

One should avoid offenses in offering respects according to Viṣṇu-smṛti. It is an offense to bow with one hand, with the body covered by cloth, in front of, behind or at the left side of the Lord, too close or in the deity room.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 304:**

Now dāsyam will be discussed. This means thinking oneself to be the servant to Viṣṇu. That is shown in the following statement:

*janmāntara-sahasreṣu yasya syān matir īdṛśī \|*  
*dāso ’haṁ vāsudevasya sarvān lokān samuddharet \|\|*

**The person who thinks “I am the servant of Vāsudeva for a thousand births” delivers all people. (Itihāsa-samuccaya)**

One may put effort in worship. But just by thinking in this way, perfection can be achieved.”

This service as *kaiṅkaryam* means to perform actions of service with respect for the authority of the Lord. There is no real difference between this *dāsya* and *paricaryā* (service, another *aṅga*) in its pure form.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 306:**

Now sakhyam will be discussed. This means an attitude of friendship with a desire to benefit another person. That is the meaning of the word *mitram* in the following:

*aho bhāgyam aho bhāgyaṁ nanda-gopa-vrajaukasām*  
*yan-mitraṁ paramānandaṁ pūrṇaṁ brahma sanātanam*

**How greatly fortunate are Nanda Mahārāja, the cowherd men and all the other inhabitants of Vrajabhūmi! There is no limit to their good fortune, because Kṛṣṇa, their friend, gave them prema, which is the highest bliss, eternal and complete. (SB** [**10.14.32**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/14.32)**)**

*paricaryā-parāḥ kecit prāsādādiṣu śerate \|*  
*manuṣyam iva taṁ draṣṭuṁ vyavahartuṁ ca bandhuvat \|\|*

**A person who is dedicated to serving the Lord, and out of friendship sees and treats him as a human, lies down in the Lord’s temple. (Rāmārcana-candrikā)**

Sakhyam is listed after dāsyam in SB [7.5.23](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/5.23). Thus sakhyam is considered superior to dāsyam because of the attitude of trust and intimacy in the prema (no restraint). The fact that friendship is prescribed even with the Lord is not surprising, for there is a scriptural rule concerning attitude. *Na devo devam arcayet*: if one is not identical with the Lord one cannot worship the Lord. But the pure devotees give no regard to the attitude of complete identity since it is contradictory to the service (but they accept equality as a bhāva). Sakhyam is thus accepted by them as most favorable to service. In the commentary, dāsyam and sakhyam as integral parts of worship are shown in the following:

*tasyaiva me sauhṛda-sakhya-maitrī- dāsyaṁ punar janmani janmani syāt*  
*mahānubhāvena guṇālayena viṣajjatas tat-puruṣa-prasaṅgaḥ*

**Life after life may I attain prema, friendship, assistance and service. May I attain association with his devotees, since he is the reservoir of all qualities and powers. (SB** [**10.81.36**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/81.36)**)**

Śrīdāmā speaks the verse. Śrīdhara Svāmī says in his commentary, “Seeing Kṛṣṇa’s affection for his devotee, Śrīdāmā prays for bhakti to the Lord in this verse. Birth after birth may I have prema (*sauhṛdam*), desire to benefit him (*sakhyam)*, giving assistance (*maitrī*) and service (*dāsyam*). The singular number is viewed in the compound as one item. May I have this relationship, not power.”

Dāsyam should not be explained as offering karmas and sakhya should not be explained as conviction (though this is the definition according to some), since these are not directly part of bhakti. Bhakti is the result of offering karmas. Conviction is a cause of becoming absorbed in bhakti. This was previously explained.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 309:**

Now ātma-nivedanam will be discussed. This means offering to the Lord alone everything from the body up to the ātmā. The action is devoid of actions for the self, offering to the Lord one’s sādhana and sādhya, doing everything only for the Lord. This is an offering of the self to the Lord. The seller of a cow does not perform actions for maintaining the cow which is already sold to someone else. The new owner takes care of the cow. The cow works for the new owner, not for the seller.

Some consider offering the body to be ātmārpaṇam.

*cintāṁ kuryān na rakṣāyai vikrītasya yathā paśoḥ \|*  
*tathārpayan harau dehaṁ viramed asya rakṣaṇāt \|\|*

**Just a purchased animal does not think of its protection a person who offers his body to the Lord gives up protecting himself. (Bhakti-viveka)**

Some say that it means offering the pure ātmā to the Lord.

*vapur-ādiṣu yo ’pi ko’pi vā guṇato’māni yathā-tathā-vidhaḥ \|*  
*tad ayaṁ tava pāda-padmayor aham adyaiva mayā samarpitaḥ \|\|*

Whoever I may be, either a soul inhabiting the body and other material elements, or a deva or a human body made of the guṇas, today I offer that ”I” to your lotus feet. (Stotra-ratnam 49, Yāmunācārya)

BRS [1.2.196](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/1.2.196):

By the word *vā* the first phrase (*vapur ādiṣu yo ’pi ko ’pi*) indicates his *svarūpa* whereas the second phrase indicates the material forms of *deva* and human (*yathā tathā-vidhaḥ*). *Asāni* is the imperative first person of *as* (to be) — “may I be.” This indicates freely follow­ing one’s desires. From grammatical analysis, *tad ayam* should actually be *asau ayam*. This phrase, meaning “that and this,” connected with *aham* indicates the “I” who is the soul and the “I” who is the body. This may be more commonly expressed as *so’ ham*.

BRS [1.2.198](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/1.2.198):

Pure *ātma-nivedanam* is seen in Bali Mahārāja when he gave him­self to the Lord.

*Ātma-nivedanam* mixed with the emotions of *dāsya* is seen in Ambarīṣa and with preyasī-bhāva in Rukmiṇī. In addition, it should be understood that *sakhya* and other *rasas* could also mix with *ātma-nivedana.*

There is no cause (*padam*) for the good and bad acts of children. These actions manifest without a cause, without their thinking if it is good or bad.

## Chapter Six

### Text 1

*śrī-prahrāda uvāca*  
*kaumāra ācaret prājño dharmān bhāgavatān iha*  
*durlabhaṁ mānuṣaṁ janma tad apy adhruvam arthadam*

**Перевод:**

**Prahlāda said: The wise person should practice** ***bhakti-yoga*** **in his life from youth. Human birth is rare and transient but effective for** ***bhakti.***

**Комментарий:**

In human life (*iha)* one should perform bhāgavata-dharma since it gives value to this birth. Devatās on the other hand have excessive sense enjoyment and animals have no power of discrimination. Having attained human birth, one should not delay. Starting from childhood (*kaumāram*) one should practice, since human life is impermanent and rarely achieved. Since scriptures are meant primarily for humans, this statement confirms the importance of scripture. But one is considered a human by having intelligence of a human.

### Text 2

*yathā hi puruṣasyeha viṣṇoḥ pādopasarpaṇam*  
*yad eṣa sarva-bhūtānāṁ priya ātmeśvaraḥ suhṛt*

**Перевод:**

**One should perform** ***bhakti*** **by the methods which allow one to surrender to the lotus feet of the Lord, since the Lord is a dear lover, a son, Brahman, the master and the friend of all beings.**

**Комментарий:**

The second verse shows that human life is suitable for performing bhāgavata-dharma. As a human being, one should surrender to the Lord suitably with bhāgavata-dharma since the Lord is the object of affection *(priyaḥ*) for all beings by his nature, since he is Paramātmā, the supreme soul. He is the object of surrender since he can do anything *(īśvaraḥ)* and he desires the benefit of all beings *(suhṛt*).

### Text 4

*tat-prayāso na kartavyo yata āyur-vyayaḥ param*  
*na tathā vindate kṣemaṁ mukunda-caraṇāmbujam*

**Перевод:**

**One should not endeavor for material happiness since it simply depletes one’s life span. By that method one gains no benefit. One gains benefit from worshipping the lotus feet of Mukunda.**

**Комментарий:**

One gains the benefit *(kṣemam*) of Mukunda’s lotus feet.

### Text 5

*tato yateta kuśalaḥ kṣemāya bhavam āśritaḥ*  
*śarīraṁ pauruṣaṁ yāvan na vipadyeta puṣkalam*

**Перевод:**

**The skillful person, fearful of falling from his position, should endeavor for his benefit as long as he remains capable in the human form.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī has *puruṣa-rūpam* and Sambandhokti has *pauruṣam.*

### Text 7

*mugdhasya bālye kaiśora krīḍato yāti viṁśatiḥ*  
*jarayā grasta-dehasya yāty akalpasya viṁśatiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**For the first ten years one is in ignorance. From the age of ten to fifteen one engages in playing. One spends twenty years afflicted by invalidity due to old age, and another twenty years incapable of doing anything.**

**Комментарий:**

Instead of *kaiśora* sometimes *kaumāra* is seen.

### Text 20-23

*parāvareṣu bhūteṣu brahmānta-sthāvarādiṣu*  
*bhautikeṣu vikāreṣu bhūteṣv atha mahatsu ca*  
*guṇeṣu guṇa-sāmye ca guṇa-vyatikare tathā*  
*eka eva paro hy ātmā bhagavān īśvaro 'vyayaḥ*  
*pratyag-ātma-svarūpeṇa dṛśya-rūpeṇa ca svayam*  
*vyāpya-vyāpaka-nirdeśyo hy anirdeśyo vikalpitaḥ*  
*kevalānubhavānanda- svarūpaḥ parameśvaraḥ*  
*māyayāntarhitaiśvarya īyate guṇa-sargayā*

**Перевод:**

**The unchanging Lord, Brahman, Paramātmā, and Bhagavān, different from** ***prakṛti,*** **is present in higher and lower beings from Brahmā to the plants, in all gross objects, in the gross elements, in the** ***guṇas,*** **in** ***pradhāna,*** **and in its transformations such as** ***mahat-tattva.*** **He is described as the pervader in the form of Paramātmā, and is known as the pervaded in the form of the universe. Though he is beyond definition, he is realized by various types of worshippers. This Supreme Lord, though having a spiritual form of pure knowledge and bliss, is falsely seen as an entity without powers, because of** ***māyā,*** **which creates material sense objects for the** ***jīva.***

**Комментарий:**

Four verses show how the Lord is various forms. He is the ātmā in all these forms (*ātmā*). And as Bhagavān, endowed with his acintya-śakti, he is capable (*īśvara*) of doing the impossible.

Fit for various forms, by his own powers, he appears as the jīva for some jñānīs (*pratyag-ātmā-svarūpeṇa*). For inferior jñānīs he appears as a visible form (*dṛśya-rūpeṇa*), pradhāna or matter. As the jīva, he is indefinable (*nirdeśyaḥ*), being spread everywhere. Also as pradhāna, spread everywhere, he is indefinable (*nirdeśya*ḥ). For the advaita-jñānīs the supreme Lord is indescribable (*anirdeśyaḥ*), devoid of any difference (*avikaplitaḥ*).

He appears as a form of pure bliss and also not as the Lord endowed with power by his svarūpa-śakti. He completely disappears by his mercy (*māyayā*). The covering nature of his māyā is described. He disappears by distributing the guṇas (*guṇa-sargayā*). For brahma-jñānīs he has a covering of sattva-guṇa.

### Text 24

*tasmāt sarveṣu bhūteṣu dayāṁ kuruta sauhṛdam*  
*bhāvam āsuram unmucya yayā tuṣyaty adhokṣajaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Give up your demonic mentality and show mercy and affection to all beings, by which attitude the Lord will be pleased.**

**Комментарий:**

Therefore show mercy to all beings, not just your relatives, since everyone is the residence of the Lord. Think of everyone as a relative (*sauhṛdam*).

### Text 25

*tuṣṭe ca tatra kim alabhyam ananta ādye*  
*kiṁ tair guṇa-vyatikarād iha ye sva-siddhāḥ*  
*dharmādayaḥ kim aguṇena ca kāṅkṣitena*  
*sāraṁ juṣāṁ caraṇayor upagāyatāṁ naḥ*

**Перевод:**

**What cannot be attained if the unlimited original Lord is satisfied? Since we glorify and relish the lotus feet of the Lord, what is the need for** ***dharma, artha*** **and** ***kāma*** **which are achieved automatically by the transformations of the** ***guṇas*****? What is the need of liberation?**

**Комментарий:**

He explains the excellent result of satisfying the Lord, according to what was previously said, using correct spiritual conclusions.

### Text 26

*dharmārtha-kāma iti yo 'bhihitas tri-varga*  
*īkṣā trayī naya-damau vividhā ca vārtā*  
*manye tad etad akhilaṁ nigamasya satyaṁ*  
*svātmārpaṇaṁ sva-suhṛdaḥ paramasya puṁsaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I consider** ***dharma, artha*** **and** ***kāma,*** **knowledge of** ***ātmā,*** **knowledge of** ***karma,*** **argumentation, justice and various occupations to be part of the Vedas recommended for some people. But I consider that surrender to the Supreme Lord, who is one’s friend, is recommended in the Vedas for the saintly people.**

**Комментарий:**

What is the use of methods that produce temporary results? By relationship with the Lord one gets permanent results.

### Text 27

*jñānaṁ tad etad amalaṁ duravāpam āha*  
*nārāyaṇo nara-sakhaḥ kila nāradāya*  
*ekāntināṁ bhagavatas tad akiñcanānāṁ*  
*pādāravinda-rajasāpluta-dehināṁ syāt*

**Перевод:**

**Nārāyaṇa, the well-wisher and friend of all living entities, formerly explained this pure knowledge, difficult to understand, to Nārada. This knowledge can be understood by fully dedicated devotees with no material desire, who bathe their bodies in the dust from the Lord’s lotus feet.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowledge of the Lord is rare. It is attained only by mercy of devotees. By mentioning *nara-sakha* he shows the glory of mercy of the best devotee, Nara. Such devotees are without material desire and bathe in the dust of the Lord’s feet. Even their teaching depends on mercy, what to speak of giving other forms of mercy.

### Text 28

*śrutam etan mayā pūrvaṁ jñānaṁ vijñāna-saṁyutam*  
*dharmaṁ bhāgavataṁ śuddhaṁ nāradād deva-darśanāt*

**Перевод:**

**I received this pure knowledge,** ***bhakti-yoga*****, along with realization, from Nārada Muni, who always sees the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

His attainment of mercy is shown. I heard statements about the Lord (*jñānam*), consisting of bhagavad-dharma, the means to the result, realization (*vijñāna*). This was pure, not mixed with jñāna or karma.

## Chapter Seven

### Text 10

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*ayaṁ niṣkilbiṣaḥ sākṣān mahā-bhāgavato mahān*  
*tvayā na prāpsyate saṁsthām anantānucaro balī*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Nārada said: That great child, sinless and a great devotee, a servant of the unlimited Lord, and full of power, can never be killed by you.**

**Комментарий:**

He is a servant of the unlimited Lord. This statement shows that he had saṁskāras of bhakti from previous time.

### Text 15

*ṛṣiḥ kāruṇikas tasyāḥ prādād ubhayam īśvaraḥ*  
*dharmasya tattvaṁ jñānaṁ ca mām apy uddiśya nirmalam*

**Перевод:**

**The merciful and capable Nārada gave the auspiciousness to her child and delivery after the arrival of her husband, by teaching** ***bhakti-yoga*** **and** ***bhakti-jñāna*** **to me and my mother.**

**Комментарий:**

Nārada gave ordinary knowledge of ātmā and dharma to relieve the mother of lamentation. He taught me spotless knowledge about the Lord. Bhakti is also called jñānam:

*naiṣkarmyam apy acyuta-bhāva-varjitaṁ*  
*na śobhate jñānam alaṁ nirañjanam \|*  
*kutaḥ punaḥ śaśvad abhadram īśvare*  
*na cārpitaṁ karma yad apy akāraṇam \|\|*

**Even the stage of** ***jñāna*** **without the bondage of** ***karma*** **is not glorious if it is devoid of** ***bhakti*** **to the Supreme Lord. What is the use of having destroyed ignorance? What then to speak of** ***sakāma-karma*** **which causes suffering, both during practice and at the stage perfection, and** ***niṣkāma-karma*****, when not offered to the Lord? SB** [**1.5.12**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/5.12)

She however was not able to understand that knowledge.

### Text 16

*tat tu kālasya dīrghatvāt strītvān mātus tirodadhe*  
*ṛṣiṇānugṛhītaṁ māṁ nādhunāpy ajahāt smṛtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Because of the long duration of time that has passed and because of her being a woman, my mother has forgotten all those instructions; but blessed by Nārada, I could not forget them.**

**Комментарий:**

*Tat tu* means *tad api* (however). She forgot. Thus it is understood that direct mercy of Nārada did not manifest in her.

### Text 18

*janmādyāḥ ṣaḍ ime bhāvā dṛṣṭā dehasya nātmanaḥ*  
*phalānām iva vṛkṣasya kāleneśvara-mūrtinā*

**Перевод:**

**Just as fruits of a tree undergo six changes by the influence of time, which is a form of the Lord, while the tree does not change, so the body undergoes six states, but the soul does not undergo these changes.**

**Комментарий:**

It order that they give up bodily identification, he taught only ātma-jñāna as explained to his mother.

### Text 19-20

*ātmā nityo 'vyayaḥ śuddha ekaḥ kṣetra-jña āśrayaḥ*  
*avikriyaḥ sva-dṛg hetur vyāpako 'saṅgy anāvṛtaḥ*  
*etair dvādaśabhir vidvān ātmano lakṣaṇaiḥ paraiḥ*  
*ahaṁ mamety asad-bhāvaṁ dehādau mohajaṁ tyajet*

**Перевод:**

**Paramātmā is without destruction, without decay, pure, the one entity, the knower of the field, the shelter of all things, without change, self-illuminating, the cause of all things, all-pervading, not influenced by matter, and not covered by anything. By contemplating these twelve features which distinguish the Lord from the** ***jīva,*** **one should give up the false identity of me and mine related to the body, arising from ignorance.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 46:**

*Avyayaḥ* means without decay. *Ekaḥ* means without a conglomeration of parts like body and senses. *Kṣetra-jñaḥ* means having the nature of a knower. *Āśrayaḥ* means being the shelter of senses. *Avikriyaḥ* means without action, since he is by nature a knower. *Sva-dṛk* means revealing himself to himself. *Hetuḥ* means the cause of creation. Sūta says:

*hetur jīvo 'sya sargāder avidyā-karma-kārakaḥ*  
*yaṁ cānuśāyinaṁ prāhur avyākṛtam utāpare*

**The cause of sarga, visarga of the universe is the jīva who performs actions out of ignorance and is said to possess a material body, or, alternately, possess a spiritual body. (SB** [**12.7.18**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/7.18)**)**

*Vyāpaka* means pervading by nature. *Asaṅgī anāvṛta* means uninfluenced by matter and uncovered, since he has a form which is self-revealing. One should give up the impermanent generated from illusion related to body etc. but not the sense of “I” inherent in jīva’s svarūpa.

Thus some say that because the jīva is an aṁśa of the Lord, the jīva is compared to a small particle of light, like a part of a Kaustubha jewel. In defining the jīva, Skanda Purāṇa, Prabhāsa-khaṇḍa says:

*na tasya varṇo rūpaṁ vā pramāṇaṁ dṛśyate kvacit*  
*na śakyaḥ kathituṁ vāpi sūkṣmaś cānanta-vigrahaḥ*

**The jīva’s color, form or measureable dimension cannot be seen. One cannot describe it. The jīvas are very small and innumerable in number.**

*bālāgra-śata-bhāgasya śatadhā kalpitasya ca*  
*tasmāt sūkṣmataro jīvaḥ sa cānantyāya kalpate*

**The jīva is smaller than a hundredth part of a hair tip divided a hundred times. They are unlimited in number.**

*āditya-varṇaṁ sūkṣmābhaṁ ab-bindum iva puṣkare*  
*nakṣatram iva paśyanti yogino jñāna-cakṣusā*

**The yogīs with eyes of knowledge see the jīva like a drop of water on a lotus, like a constellation or a small glowing light.**

### Text 21

*svarṇaṁ yathā grāvasu hema-kāraḥ*  
*kṣetreṣu yogais tad-abhijña āpnuyāt*  
*kṣetreṣu deheṣu tathātma-yogair*  
*adhyātma-vid brahma-gatiṁ labheta*

**Перевод:**

**As the expert miner obtains gold from the rocks in a mine by various processes, the knower of** ***adhyātmā*** **attains the Paramātmā in the body by methods of realizing** ***ātmā.***

**Комментарий:**

He describes *tat-padārtha-jñānam*: knowledge of the supreme.

### Text 29

*tatropāya-sahasrāṇām ayaṁ bhagavatoditaḥ*  
*yad īśvare bhagavati yathā yair añjasā ratiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Among the thousands of methods for destroying the material intelligence, the process proclaimed by the Lord himself,** ***bhakti-yoga,*** **is the real method, whose** ***aṅga*** **known as service to** ***guru*** **produces** ***bhakti--*** **from which** ***rati*** **to the Supreme Lord appears as the main result.**

**Комментарий:**

He propounds his surrender to the Lord in the following verses.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 56:**

Among the thousands of methods for destroying the seed of actions made of the three guṇas previously described, this method was taught to me by Nārada. From this method (*yat)*, established by a thousand methods *(yaiḥ*), love (*ratiḥ)* for the Lord easily appears, without obstruction (*añjasā*), in proper form (*yathāvat*).

### Text 30-31

*guru-śuśrūṣayā bhaktyā sarva-labdhārpaṇena ca*  
*saṅgena sādhu-bhaktānām īśvarārādhanena ca*  
*śraddhayā tat-kathāyāṁ ca kīrtanair guṇa-karmaṇām*  
*tat-pādāmburuha-dhyānāt tal-liṅgekṣārhaṇādibhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One performs** ***bhakti-yoga*** **by service to** ***guru*****, offering him all that one possesses, by association with devotees of good conduct, by worship of the Lord, by faith in topics of the Lord, by chanting about the Lord’s qualities and activities, by meditation on the Lord’s lotus feet, and by seeing the deity and making offerings to the deity.**

**Комментарий:**

Bhakti is described in three verses.

### Text 33

*evaṁ nirjita-ṣaḍ-vargaiḥ kriyate bhaktir īśvare*  
*vāsudeve bhagavati yayā saṁlabhyate ratiḥ*

**Перевод:**

***Bhakti*** **is performed by these methods, devoid of lust, anger, greed, illusion, pride and hatred. By this method,** ***rati*** **to Vāsudeva is obtained.**

**Комментарий:**

The aṅgas of the method were described. Other details are now given. Bhakti is performed by people who have conquered desire etc. arising from the seed of karma, by the method described, without separate effort to control lust, anger etc.

### Text 34

*niśamya karmāṇi guṇān atulyān*  
*vīryāṇi līlā-tanubhiḥ kṛtāni*

**Комментарий:**

The activities of the devotee are described in three verses.

### Text 37

*adhokṣajālambham ihāśubhātmanaḥ*  
*śarīriṇaḥ saṁsṛti-cakra-śātanam*  
*tad brahma-nirvāṇa-sukhaṁ vidur budhās*

**Перевод:**

**The wise know that direct contact with the Lord, which includes within it the happiness of Brahman, destroys** ***saṁsāra*** **for the sinful persons possessing material bodies in this world. Therefore worship in your heart the Lord who possesses various** ***bhāvas*****.**

**Комментарий:**

Will saṁsāra be destroyed when a person takes shelter of the Lord? This verse answers.

*Śātanam* means destroying. This happens naturally with surrender to the Lord.

### Text 38

*ko 'ti-prayāso 'sura-bālakā harer*  
*upāsane sve hṛdi chidravat sataḥ*  
*svasyātmanaḥ sakhyur aśeṣa-dehināṁ*  
*sāmānyataḥ kiṁ viṣayopapādanaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O demon boys! What difficulty is there in worshipping the Lord, the friend of the** ***jīva*****, who remains in the heart like ether? What is the use of obtaining material enjoyment for humans who are like animals in their quest for enjoyment?**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is the friend, equally. *Śruti* says *sayujau sakhāyau*: the Lord and the *jīva* are two friends in a tree. (*Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad*  [3.1.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mundaka/3.1.1))

### Text 40

*evaṁ hi lokāḥ kratubhiḥ kṛtā amī*  
*kṣayiṣṇavaḥ sātiśayā na nirmalāḥ*  
*tasmād adṛṣṭa-śruta-dūṣaṇaṁ paraṁ*

**The planets achieved by sacrifices are perishable, and impure, with superior enjoyment awarded only according to the amount of pious acts. Therefore worship with devotion the faultless Lord, to achieve the Lord, the objective of the** ***ātmā.***

**Комментарий:**

Worship the Lord for attaining one’s Lord (*ātma-labdhaye*). Worship for attaining the self. Otherwise one will destroy the self.

### Text 48

*tasmād arthāś ca kāmāś ca dharmāś ca yad-apāśrayāḥ*  
*bhajatānīhayātmānam anīhaṁ harim īśvaram*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, without material desire, worship the independent Supreme Lord, Paramātmā, who gives shelter to** ***artha, kāma*** **and** ***dharma.***

**Комментарий:**

Worship the Lord without conditions (*anīhayā*). This is the cause of unconditional mercy.

*ahaṁ tv akāmas tvad-bhaktas tvaṁ ca svāmy anapāśrayaḥ*  
*nānyathehāvayor artho rāja-sevakayor iva*

**I am your servant, not desiring benefits, and you are my master, without dependence on service. Our relationship should not be anything else--such as that of a material king and servant. (SB** [**7.10.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/10.6)**)**

### Text 51-52

*nālaṁ dvijatvaṁ devatvam ṛṣitvaṁ vāsurātmajāḥ*  
*prīṇanāya mukundasya na vṛttaṁ na bahu-jñatā*

**O sons of demons! Being a** ***brāhmaṇa, devatā*** **or sage, good conduct, learning, charity, austerity, worship, cleanliness and vows cannot please the Lord. The Lord is pleased by pure** ***bhakti.*** **Other than** ***bhakti,*** **everything else is a cause of mockery.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together.

### Text 53

*tato harau bhagavati bhaktiṁ kuruta dānavāḥ*  
*ātmaupamyena sarvatra sarva-bhūtātmanīśvare*

**Перевод:**

**O demons! Perform** ***bhakti*** **to the Lord, Paramātmā, while treating him as one would treat oneself.**

**Комментарий:**

At all places and times (*sarvatra*), worship the Lord using body, mind and words favorably, as if the Lord were one’s own self (*ātmaupamyena*).

### Text 54

*daiteyā yakṣa-rakṣāṁsi striyaḥ śūdrā vr*  
*khagā mṛgāḥ pāpa-jīvāḥ santi hy acyutatāṁ gatāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O sons of demons! The Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, women,** ***śūdras,*** **cowherd men, birds, animals and the sinful living entities can attain spiritual bodies along with the association of the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

*Vrajaukasaḥ* is mentioned with low people. This revers to ābhīras, a particular low birth and occupation.

*kirāta-hūṇāndhra-pulinda-pulkaśā*  
*ābhīra-śumbhā yavanāḥ khasādayaḥ \|*  
*ye ’nye ca pāpā yad-upāśrayāśrayāḥ*  
*śudhyanti tasmai prabhaviṣṇave namaḥ \|\|*

**I offer respects to the Lord of inconceivable power. The Kirātas, Hūṇas, Andhras, Pulindas, Pulkaśas, Abhīras, Śumbhas, Yavanas, Khasas and others of low birth, and those sinful by actions, by taking shelter of the devotees who take shelter of the powerful Lord, become purified of their** ***prārabdha-karmas.*** **(SB** [**2.4.18**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/4.18)**)**

It does not refer to the following description of the vaiśyas:

*kṛṣi-vāṇijya-go-rakṣā kusīdaṁ tūryam ucyate*  
*vārtā catur-vidhā tatra vayaṁ go-vṛttayo ‘niśam*

**The occupational duties of the** ***vaiśya*** **are conceived in four divisions: farming, commerce, cow protection and lending money. Out of these, we are always engaged in cow protection. (SB** [**10.24.21**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/24.21)**)**

### Text 55

*etāvān eva loke 'smin puṁsaḥ svārthaḥ paraḥ smṛtaḥ*  
*ekānta-bhaktir govinde yat sarvatra tad-īkṣaṇam*

**Перевод:**

**The supreme goal of man in this world is considered to be pure** ***bhakti*** **for Govinda, by which one sees Govinda everywhere.**

**Комментарий:**

By this method the Lord appears everywhere (*tad-īkṣaṇam*).

*sarva-bhūteṣu yaḥ paśyed bhagavad-bhāvam ātmanaḥ*  
*bhūtāni bhagavaty ātmany eṣa bhāgavatottamaḥ*

**The most advanced devotee sees Supreme Lord, one’s object of worship, within everything, and sees everything in the Supreme Lord. (SB** [**11.2.45**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/2.45)**)**

## Chapter Eight

### Text 1

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*atha daitya-sutāḥ sarve śrutvā tad-anuvarṇitam*  
*jagṛhur niravadyatvān naiva gurv-anuśikṣitam*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said: After hearing what Prahlāda spoke, all the children did not accept what was taught by their teachers, since Prahlāda’s teachings were faultless.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī mentions in his commentary that Nṛsiṁha caused the king of demons to worship him in the mood of an enemy. This was the external appearance, since he was following the order of the Lord. Verse 3 has an extra line in some versions but this is not in the edition of the Gauḍiyas and devotees of Mithila. Śrīdhara Svāmī does not accept it.

### Text 13

*so 'haṁ vikatthamānasya śiraḥ kāyād dharāmi te*  
*gopāyeta haris tvādya yas te śaraṇam īpsitam*

**Перевод:**

**Because you are speaking so much nonsense, I shall now sever your head from your body. Now let your Lord who is your preferred shelter save you!**

**Комментарий:**

You speak words which ultimately are meant as praise for yourself (*vikatthamānasya*).

### Text 19-22

*mīmāṁsamānasya samutthito 'grato*  
*nṛsiṁha-rūpas tad alaṁ bhayānakam*  
*pratapta-cāmīkara-caṇḍa-locanaṁ*  
*sphurat saṭā-keśara-jṛmbhitānanam*  
*karāla-daṁṣṭraṁ karavāla-cañcala-*  
*kṣurānta-jihvaṁ bhrukuṭī-mukholbaṇam*  
*stabdhordhva-karṇaṁ giri-kandarādbhuta-*  
*vyāttāsya-nāsaṁ hanu-bheda-bhīṣaṇam*  
*divi-spṛśat kāyam adīrgha-pīvara-*  
*grīvoru-vakṣaḥ-sthalam alpa-madhyamam*  
*candrāṁśu-gauraiś churitaṁ tanūruhair*  
*viṣvag bhujānīka-śataṁ nakhāyudham*  
*durāsadaṁ sarva-nijetarāyudha-*  
*praveka-vidrāvita-daitya-dānavam*

**Перевод:**

**The form of Nṛsiṁha appeared in front of Hiraṇyakaśipu, who studied that frightful form having fierce eyes the color of molten gold and dreadful teeth, a face expanded by his brilliant mane, and a tongue with sharp tip waving like a sword. His frown was terrifying and his rigid ears pointed up. His nostrils spread open like mountain caves. His open jaw was terrifying. His body, covered by hairs white like the moon, with broad chest and narrow waist and thick, short neck, reached the sky. He had unconquerable nails as weapons on his hundreds of arms which spread in all directions. By his normal weapons and other excellent weapons he made the demons flee.**

**Комментарий:**

Three and half verses describe the form of Nṛsiṁha. He had hundreds of arms resembling troops (*bhujānīka-śatam*).

### Text 42

*śrī-indra uvāca*  
*pratyānītāḥ parama bhavatā trāyatā naḥ sva-bhāgā*  
*daityākrāntaṁ hṛdaya-kamalaṁ tad-gṛhaṁ pratyabodhi*  
*kāla-grastaṁ kiyad idam aho nātha śuśrūṣatāṁ te*  
*muktis teṣāṁ na hi bahumatā nārasiṁhāparaiḥ kim*

**Перевод:**

**Indra said: Your share of sacrifice has been taken back by you, our deliverer. You have illuminated your residence, our lotus hearts, afflicted by the demon. What is this post of Indra devoured by time? O Narasiṁha! Those who engage in your service do not give any regard to liberation. What then is the use of other things?**

**Комментарий:**

Indra pleases him by recognizing his special assistance to himself. We your servants (*sva-bhāgaḥ*) have become purified (*pratyānītāḥ*). You have illuminated our hearts. The two demons have helped me realize that this post is devoured by time.

### Text 44

*śrī-pitara ūcuḥ*  
*śrāddhāni no 'dhibubhuje prasabhaṁ tanūjair*  
*dattāni tīrtha-samaye 'py apibat tilāmbu*  
*tasyodarān nakha-vidīrṇa-vapād ya ārcchat*  
*tasmai namo nṛharaye 'khila-dharma-goptre*

**Перевод:**

**The Pitṛs said: The demon enjoyed by force the** ***śrāddha*** **offerings which were offered by our sons when visiting holy places. He drank the water with sesame seeds. We offer respects to Nṛhari, protector of all** ***dharma,*** **who obtained those offerings from the stomach of the demon, whose fat was torn by your nails.**

**Комментарий:**

This is the version accepted by Śrīdhara Svāmī.

### Text 46

*śrī-vidyādharā ūcuḥ*  
*vidyāṁ pṛthag dhāraṇayānurāddhāṁ*  
*nyaṣedhad ajño bala-vīrya-dṛptaḥ*  
*sa yena saṅkhye paśuvad dhatas taṁ*  
*māyā-nṛsiṁhaṁ praṇatāḥ sma nityam*

**Перевод:**

**The Vidyādharas said: We eternally offer respects to merciful Nṛsiṁha who in battle killed this animal, this fool, proud of his strength and prowess, who forbade knowledge attained by various types of meditation.**

**Комментарий:**

*Māyā-nṛsiṁham* means Nṛsiṁha whose prominent quality is mercy.

### Text 48

*śrī-manava ūcuḥ*  
*manavo vayaṁ tava nideśa-kāriṇo*  
*ditijena deva paribhūta-setavaḥ*  
*bhavatā khalaḥ sa upasaṁhṛtaḥ prabho*  
*karavāma te kim anuśādhi kiṅkarān*

**Перевод:**

**The Manus said: O Lord! We, who carry out your orders, have had our laws of** ***varṇāśrama*** **reversed by the son of Diti. You have killed this sinner. O master! What can we do for you? Please instruct your servants.**

**Комментарий:**

The Manus speak, thinking of themselves in that position in the future. At that time they had just finished performing austerities together.

### Text 51

*śrī-cāraṇā ūcuḥ*  
*hare tavāṅghri-paṅkajaṁ bhavāpavargam āśritāḥ*  
*yad eṣa sādhu-hṛc-chayas tvayāsuraḥ samāpitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Cāraṇas said: O Lord! Because you have destroyed the demon Hiraṇyakaśipu, who audaciously slept in bed-like the hearts of the devotees, we take shelter of your lotus feet, which award liberation from the material world.**

**Комментарий:**

Previously we could not take shelter of your lotus feet because he had occupied our hearts. We can take shelter now because he has been killed by you.

### Text 52

*śrī-yakṣā ūcuḥ*  
*vayam anucara-mukhyāḥ karmabhis te mano-jñais*  
*ta iha diti-sutena prāpitā vāhakatvam*  
*sa tu jana-paritāpaṁ tat-kṛtaṁ jānatā te*  
*narahara upanītaḥ pañcatāṁ pañca-viṁśa*

**Перевод:**

**The Yakṣas said: O controller of the twenty-four elements! We are among your chief attendants, rendering pleasing services. The demon forced us to be his palanquin carriers. O Narahari! Knowing the misery he inflicted on us, you killed him.**

**Комментарий:**

O Narahari! We, who are among the chief persons headed by Śiva and Kubera, serving you with attractive actions, were forced to carry his palanquin.

### Text 56

*śrī-viṣṇu-pārṣadā ūcuḥ*  
*adyaitad dhari-nara-rūpam adbhutaṁ te*  
*dṛṣṭaṁ naḥ śaraṇada sarva-loka-śarma*  
*so 'yaṁ te vidhikara īśa vipra-śaptas*  
*tasyedaṁ nidhanam anugrahāya vidmaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The associates of Lord Viṣṇu said: O Lord, our supreme giver of shelter! Today we have seen your amazing form as Nṛsiṁhadeva, good fortune for the entire world. O Lord! We can understand that this demon was your servant, who was cursed by the Kumāras and that you killed him as an act of mercy to him.**

**Комментарий:**

Theses associates are understood to be followers of the son of Vikuṇṭhā, who was named Vaikuṇṭha. They joined Nṛsiṁḥa when he appeared.

## Chapter Nine

### Text 2

*sākṣāt śrīḥ preṣitā devair dṛṣṭvā taṁ mahad adbhutam*  
*adṛṣṭāśruta-pūrvatvāt sā nopeyāya śaṅkitā*

**Перевод:**

**Lakṣmī, though requested by all the** ***devatās*****, after seeing him in an astonishing form never seen or heard of before, did not approach the Lord out of fear.**

**Комментарий:**

Even Lakṣmī the consort of the Lord could not approach him because of his unprecedented form, which caused fear in her.

### Text 6

*sa tat-kara-sparśa-dhutākhilāśubhaḥ*  
*sapady abhivyakta-parātma-darśanaḥ*  
*tat-pāda-padmaṁ hṛdi nirvṛto dadhau*  
*hṛṣyat-tanuḥ klinna-hṛd-aśru-locanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**By the touch of Nṛsiṁhadeva's hand on Prahlāda’s head, Prahlāda, who was already completely freed of all material contaminations, developed full realization of Paramātmā. Becoming blissful, with melted heart, with tears in his eyes and hairs standing on end he held the Lord’s lotus feet in his heart.**

**Комментарий:**

Prahlāda had been purified of all sin by directly meeting the Lord. After experiencing Brahman directly, his special bliss on meeting the Lord is described.

### Text 8

*śrī-prahrāda uvāca*  
*brahmādayaḥ sura-gaṇā munayo 'tha siddhāḥ*  
*sattvaikatāna-gatayo vacasāṁ pravāhaiḥ*  
*nārādhituṁ puru-guṇair adhunāpi pipruḥ*  
*kiṁ toṣṭum arhati sa me harir ugra-jāteḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Prahlāda said: How is it possible for me, who have been born in a family of demons, to please the Lord? The** ***devatās*** **headed by Lord Brahmā and all the sages and Siddhas who are immersed in** ***sattva-guṇa*** **could not properly worship the Lord by streams of excellent words.**

**Комментарий:**

He expresses his lack of qualification by mentioned others more qualified. By streams of words predominated by karma-yoga and jñāna, they could not properly worship the Lord.

### Text 9

*manye dhanābhijana-rūpa-tapaḥ-śrutaujas-*  
*tejaḥ-prabhāva-bala-pauruṣa-buddhi-yogāḥ*  
*nārādhanāya hi bhavanti parasya puṁso*  
*bhaktyā tutoṣa bhagavān gaja-yūtha-pāya*

**Перевод:**

**I think that wealth, family, beauty, austerity, learning, power of the senses, splendor, power, strength of the body, effort, intelligence,** ***karma, jñāna*** **or** ***aṣṭāṅga-yoga*** **cannot satisfy the Lord. The Lord was satisfied with the king of the elephants by his devotion to the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Seeing this, he comes to a conclusion in three verses. Wealth etc. do not satisfy (*arādhanāya*) the Lord. What will satisfy him? He shows by an example. The elephant satisfied him by devotion. Though Brahmā and others were devotees, he was not satisfied by their bhakti.

### Text 10

*viprād dvi-ṣaḍ-guṇa-yutād aravinda-nābha-*  
*pādāravinda-vimukhāt śvapacaṁ variṣṭham*  
*manye tad-arpita-mano-vacanehitārtha-*  
*prāṇaṁ punāti sa kulaṁ na tu bhūrimānaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I consider a dog-eater who has dedicated everything—mind, words, activities, wealth and life—to the Supreme Lord superior to a** ***brāhmaṇa*** **has all twelve items of the previous verse but who is averse to the lotus feet of the Lord. He purifies his whole family, whereas the so-called respectable person does not.**

**Комментарий:**

Without bhakti, some people are proclaimed as outstanding in many qualities. This verse rejects them.

### Text 11

*naivātmanaḥ prabhur ayaṁ nija-lābha-pūrṇo*  
*mānaṁ janād aviduṣaḥ karuṇo vṛṇīte*  
*yad yaj jano bhagavate vidadhīta mānaṁ*  
*tac cātmane prati- mukhasya yathā mukha-śrīḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, complete in himself and merciful, does not accept the respect of an ignorant person for his own benefit. Whatever that person gives to the Lord is for that person’s benefit, just as a face’s beauty produces a beautiful reflection.**

**Комментарий:**

The devotees are the dearest to the Lord. Bhakti has been praised in verse 9 as satisfying the Lord. Bhakti is now described in terms of its activities.

**Bhakti Sandarbha 167:**

The Lord does not desire (*vṛṇīte)* his own respect from his devotees *(janāt*), because he is completely satisfied by attaining the devotee *(nija-lābha-pūrṇaḥ*) and he is merciful, not tolerating great endeavor to worship him. These devotees are like children in front of a father. They know nothing *(aviduṣaḥ*). It also expresses humility generally felt by the devotee in front of the Lord (though Prahlāda himself is not ignorant). Or it means a person who does not know anything except the Lord. In both cases, the devotee is the object of the Lord’s compassion.

“Should the devotee not worship the Lord?” Whatever respect the devotee offers the Lord gives benefit to himself. In giving the Lord respect, without regard for his own respect, he thinks of the Lord’s happiness. By respecting the Lord, he gets respected by the Lord (that is his benefit). That is proper for the jīva. An example is given. Whatever beauty is in the face is the beauty in the reflection.

### Text 12

*tasmād ahaṁ vigata-viklava īśvarasya*  
*sarvātmanā mahi gṛṇāmi yathā manīṣam*  
*nīco 'jayā guṇa-visargam anupraviṣṭaḥ*  
*pūyeta yena hi pumān anuvarṇitena*

**Перевод:**

**My confusion is gone. Though low-born, I will glorify the Lord with my entire endeavor, as far as my intelligence allows. By this glorification, a person who has entered** ***saṁsāra*** **by ignorance can purify himself.**

**Комментарий:**

He concludes with this verse.

### Text 13

*sarve hy amī vidhi-karās tava sattva-dhāmno*  
*brahmādayo vayam iveśa na codvijantaḥ*  
*kṣemāya bhūtaya utātma-sukhāya cāsya*  
*vikrīḍitaṁ bhagavato rucirāvatāraiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O my Lord! All the** ***devatās*** **headed by Brahmā are your servants, situated in a position** ***sattva.*** **They are not like us who are full of fear. You have performed your pastime with your pleasing appearance for the protection and prosperity of the universe and your devotee, and for your own pleasure.**

**Комментарий:**

“Brahmā and others are not devotees but you are a devotee.” Worry that the Lord would say this, Prahlāda confirms that Brahmā and others are devotees, using a negative example, in order that the Lord gives mercy to them, while pointing out that he himself is a demon, since he was born in a demonic family. They are your servants, not like us demons. We have attained a condition of hatred for you, on seeing your unequalled powers (*udvijanataḥ*). You however are full of all auspicious qualities and activities.

### Text 14

*tad yaccha manyum asuraś ca hatas tvayādya*  
*modeta sādhur api vṛścika-sarpa-hatyā*  
*lokāś ca nirvṛtim itāḥ pratiyanti sarve*  
*rūpaṁ nṛsiṁha vibhayāya janāḥ smaranti*

**Перевод:**

**O Nṛsiṁha! Cease your anger now that you have killed my father. Since even saintly persons take pleasure in killing a scorpion or snake, all the worlds have achieved great satisfaction because of the death of this demon. Everyone recognizes your action. All people remember this form in order to be free from fear.**

**Комментарий:**

“Brahmā and sages like Marīci, not knowing the truth, though they are devotees, cannot tolerate my killing a great living being. They criticize, saying you are most proper, but have committed violence.” Fearing that the Lord would not give mercy to them, Prahlāda speaks. I am very low, but even devotees permit killing of entities such as scorpions that commit violence against all beings. What to speak of killing this demon. They certain permit this killing. All the people have become satisfied with this. Since everyone has become happy, it was properly done. This is a time to give mercy, not show anger. Moreover, everyone remembers your form.

### Text 16

*trasto 'smy ahaṁ kṛpaṇa-vatsala duḥsahogra-*  
*saṁsāra-cakra-kadanād grasatāṁ praṇītaḥ*  
*baddhaḥ sva-karmabhir uśattama te 'ṅghri-mūlaṁ*  
*prīto 'pavarga-śaraṇaṁ hvayase kadā nu*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord, who are kind to the fallen souls! Excellent Lord! Thrown into the association of devouring demons, bound by my** ***karmas,*** **I am afraid of destruction through the intolerably fierce wheel of** ***saṁsāra*****. When will you call me to the shelter of your lotus feet, which award liberation?**

**Комментарий:**

The wheel of saṁsāra is intolerable because it is based on aversion to the Lord. I have been thrown among the demons who swallow up your devotees (*grasatām*). O excellent Lord! When will you call me to Vaikuṇṭha, the eternal abode of your lotus feet?

### Text 17

*yasmāt priyāpriya-viyoga-saṁyoga-janma-*  
*śokāgninā sakala-yoniṣu dahyamānaḥ*  
*duḥkhauṣadhaṁ tad api duḥkham atad-dhiyāhaṁ*  
*bhūman bhramāmi vada me tava dāsya-yogam*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! Because of this, I am suffering in every single life by the fire of lamentation arising from birth, with experience of separating from and meeting friends and enemies. The solution to suffering is also suffering. But thinking I am happy, I am wandering from body to body. Please tell me how I can be your servant.**

**Комментарий:**

“Until I call you, you should experience the happiness of the kingdom of your father.” But that is all suffering. At that time, I pray in this manner. Tell me about activities as a servant and service to your deity form, the way of meeting you (*dāsya-yogam*).

### Text 18

*so 'haṁ priyasya suhṛdaḥ paradevatāyā*  
*līlā-kathās tava nṛsiṁha viriñca-gītāḥ*  
*añjas titarmy anugṛṇan guṇa-vipramukto*  
*durgāṇi te pada-yugālaya-haṁsa-saṅgaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O Nṛsiṁha! Taking association of persons who take shelter of your feet, hearing pastimes of the Supreme Lord from devotees in** ***dāsya, sakhya*** **and** ***mādhurya-rasa*****, which are sung by Brahmā, becoming free from material attachment, I will easily overcome separation from you.**

**Комментарий:**

The destruction of obstacles will take place by your special appearance, through another spiritual means.

### Text 19

*bālasya neha śaraṇaṁ pitarau nṛsiṁha*  
*nārtasya cāgadam udanvati majjato nauḥ*  
*taptasya tat-pratividhir ya ihāñjaseṣṭas*  
*tāvad vibho tanu-bhṛtāṁ tvad-upekṣitānām*

**Перевод:**

**The mother and father are not the protection for their child. Medicine is not protection for sick person. A boat is not a remedy for a person drowning in the ocean. For those who do not accept you, the remedies accepted by suffering people in this world are not a shelter at all.**

**Комментарий:**

Other things are not effective. Why do other scriptures recommend these methods?

For persons opposed to you (*tad-upekṣitānām*), the remedy, by direct instruction, most desired by them, is for decreasing their determination (*tāvat*). You give protection to them by your supreme śakti which shelters all devotees.

### Text 20

*yasmin yato yarhi yena ca yasya yasmād*  
*yasmai yathā yad uta yas tv aparaḥ paro vā*  
*bhāvaḥ karoti vikaroti pṛthak svabhāvaḥ*  
*sañcoditas tad akhilaṁ bhavataḥ svarūpam*

**Перевод:**

**Recent or ancient objects created or transformed by people with individual natures and the persons who attain the objects are merely forms of you. The place in which the act is done, the indirect cause, the time of doing it, the immediate cause of doing it, the reason for doing it, the doer, for whom it is done, from whatever it is made, to whomever it is offered, whatever is produced, and in what manner it is produced—all of this is you alone.**

**Комментарий:**

He shows the identity of everything with the Lord. You are the material cause, upādāna.

### Text 21

*māyā manaḥ sṛjati karmamayaṁ balīyaḥ*  
*kālena codita-guṇānumatena puṁsaḥ*  
*chandomayaṁ yad ajayārpita-ṣoḍaśāraṁ*  
*saṁsāra-cakram aja ko 'titaret tvad-anyaḥ*

**Перевод:**

***Māyā,*** **whose** ***guṇas*** **are agitated by the glance of your** ***puruṣa*** **expansion in the form of time, creates the insurmountable subtle body made of mind and other elements, composed of desires, which causes secular and religious actions, which in turn becomes the wheel of** ***saṁsāra*** **with sixteen spokes or transformations caused by ignorance. What person, who does not take shelter of you, can surpass this?**

**Комментарий:**

Everything is dependent on the Lord and he is their only protection. Māyā and its form as karma create the mind. Who can surpass the wheal of saṁsāra, made of desires (*chando-mayam*), without attaining your mercy, which is rare? No one can.

### Text 22

*sa tvaṁ hi nitya-vijitātma-guṇaḥ sva-dhāmnā*  
*kālo vaśī-kṛta-visṛjya-visarga-śaktiḥ*  
*cakre visṛṣṭam ajayeśvara ṣoḍaśāre*  
*niṣpīḍyamānam upakarṣa vibho prapannam*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! O supreme power! You conquer the material** ***guṇas*** **contained in the** ***jīva’s*** **intelligence at all times by your** ***svarūpa-śakti*****. You are time which agitates the** ***guṇas.*** **You destroy ignorance in the subtle body. Please bring near you that person who has been thrown in the wheel in the wheel of sixteen spokes by ignorance and is being squeezed like a piece of sugar cane.**

**Комментарий:**

Showing this again, he then reveals his desire.

### Text 23

*dṛṣṭā mayā divi vibho 'khila-dhiṣṇya-pānām*  
*āyuḥ śriyo vibhava icchati yāñ jano 'yam*  
*ye 'smat pituḥ kupita-hāsa-vijṛmbhita-bhrū-*  
*visphūrjitena lulitāḥ sa tu te nirastaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I have seen the life span, wealth and power of the** ***devatās,*** **which people desire. The** ***devatās*** **were destroyed by the movement of the eyebrow of my father, made in anger or jest. But you have destroyed him.**

### Text 24

*tasmād amūs tanu-bhṛtām aham āśiṣo 'jña*  
*āyuḥ śriyaṁ vibhavam aindriyam āviriñcyāt*  
*necchāmi te vilulitān uruvikrameṇa*  
*kālātmanopanaya māṁ nija-bhṛtya-pārśvam*

**Перевод:**

**I do not want these objects of enjoyments, life span, wealth, power, or sense enjoyment of living beings up to that of Brahmā, which are all destroyed by your power as time. Take me to the side of your servant.**

**Комментарий:**

In two verses he expresses his desires, while lamenting his suffering. Covering his one request with humility, he prays for service in prema at the Lord’s lotus feet, following the feet of his servants. This is understood in previous and later statements also.

### Text 25

*kutrāśiṣaḥ śruti-sukhā mṛgatṛṣṇi-rūpāḥ*  
*kvedaṁ kalevaram aśeṣa-rujāṁ virohaḥ*  
*nirvidyate na tu jano yad apīti vidvān*  
*kāmānalaṁ madhu-lavaiḥ śamayan durāpaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**What is the use of material objects which are pleasant only to hear about and to see? What is the use of the body which is a breeding place of unlimited diseases? Though a person knows this condition of life, he pacifies the fire of desire by a drop of hard-earned happiness, but does not become detached.**

**Комментарий:**

Again he shows the misery of jīvas in general to show his own miserable position. The drops of happiness (*madhu-lavaiḥ*) increase the desire, like fire increases by ghee.

### Text 26

*kvāhaṁ rajaḥ-prabhava īśa tamo 'dhike 'smin*  
*jātaḥ suretara-kule kva tavānukampā*  
*na brahmaṇo na tu bhavasya na vai ramāyā*  
*yan me 'rpitaḥ śirasi padma-karaḥ prasādaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I, with prominence of passion, born in the family of demons full of ignorance, have become the object of your mercy, since you placed your merciful lotus hand on my head, and not on the head of Brahmā, Śiva or Lakṣmī.**

**Комментарий:**

Having shown his miserable condition, he shows strength of the Lord’s unconditional mercy. The statement about Brahmā, Śiva and Lakṣmī is made in consideration of the situation as he spoke.

### Text 27

*naiṣā parāvara-matir bhavato nanu syāj*  
*jantor yathātma-suhṛdo jagatas tathāpi*  
*saṁsevayā surataror iva te prasādaḥ*  
*sevānurūpam udayo na parāvaratvam*

**Перевод:**

**You do not have conceptions of high or low birth like other beings, since you are the friend of the whole universe. Your mercy is obtained by service alone, like serving a desire tree. According to the degree of service, you give results, not according to high or low birth.**

**Комментарий:**

You are merciful to the devotees. That is remarkable. But it is not so remarkable, since there are examples. But your mercy to me is most remarkable, without example. That is the power of your mercy. In order to emphasis what he previously said, he speaks.

### Text 28

*evaṁ janaṁ nipatitaṁ prabhavāhi-kūpe*  
*kāmābhikāmam anu yaḥ prapatan prasaṅgāt*  
*kṛtvātmasāt surarṣiṇā bhagavan gṛhītaḥ*  
*so 'haṁ kathaṁ nu visṛje tava bhṛtya-sevām*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! Following people with all material desires who have fallen in the material snake hole, I have also fallen into the hole because of their association. But I have been accepted by Nārada, who has made me part of himself. How can I give up service to your servant?**

**Комментарий:**

Remembering the cause of such mercy, he prays for his desire. I have become the object of your mercy because of Nārada’s mercy. How can I give up service to your servant, since he gave me so much mercy?

### Text 29

*mat-prāṇa-rakṣaṇam ananta pitur vadhaś ca*  
*manye sva-bhṛtya-ṛṣi-vākyam ṛtaṁ vidhātum*  
*khaḍgaṁ pragṛhya yad avocad asad-vidhitsus*  
*tvām īśvaro mad-aparo 'vatu kaṁ harāmi*

**Перевод:**

**O unlimited Lord! I think you protected me and killed my father in order to make the words of your servant Nārada come true. Taking his sword, wanting to kill me, my father said, “Let your Lord, someone other than me, protect you! I will cut off your head.”**

**Комментарий:**

You protected me.

### Text 30

*ekas tvam eva jagad etam amuṣya yat tvam*  
*ādy-antayoḥ pṛthag avasyasi madhyataś ca*  
*sṛṣṭvā guṇa-vyatikaraṁ nija-māyayedaṁ*  
*nāneva tair avasitas tad anupraviṣṭaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**You alone are the universe. You are perceived to be different from the universe with a beginning, middle and end, having created it by transformations of the** ***guṇas.*** **By your** ***māyā*****, you are perceived as the creator, maintainer and destroyer, because of the** ***guṇas.***

**Комментарий:**

He controls Nṛsiṁha’s anger by another rasa. Why did this low person become the object of your anger? You are one with the universe. But there is a harmony with previous statements: appearing in the world because of mercy to devotees, you protect persons like me. And you protect the universe in general as the antaryāmī. By you do not do this directly: it is done by others. Showing this, he explains the Lord’s pastime of creation and destruction related to this.

### Text 31

*tvaṁ vā idaṁ sad-asad īśa bhavāṁs tato 'nyo*  
*māyā yad ātma-para-buddhir iyaṁ hy apārthā*  
*yad yasya janma nidhanaṁ sthitir īkṣaṇaṁ ca*  
*tad vaitad eva vasukālavad aṣṭi-tarvoḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! You are the universe and its effect and the cause, and you are also different, since the conception of self and other, which is illusory, is a product of ignorance. The Lord from whom creation and manifestation arise, in which maintenance and destruction take place, and the universe itself, are like the seed and the tree, or fragrance and earth.**

**Комментарий:**

He establishes the Lord’s special position.

### Text 32

*nyasyedam ātmani jagad vilayāmbu-madhye*  
*śeṣetmanā nija-sukhānubhavo nirīhaḥ*  
*yogena mīlita-dṛg-ātma-nipīta-nidras*  
*turye sthito na tu tamo na guṇāṁś ca yuṅkṣe*

**Перевод:**

**Placing the universe within you, you sleep in the indestructible water, enjoying bliss with your** ***svarūpa-śakti,*** **without interest in material creation. Without material sleep, you remain with closed eyes through your** ***svarūpa-śakti,*** **situated in the fourth state of consciousness. You do not accept material deep sleep or the sense objects of the waking or dream states.**

**Комментарий:**

Related to this, he describes in seven verses the direct creation pastime through the mercy of the great devotee Brahmā. You place the three worlds (*jagat*) inside yourself, as Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu (*ātmani*). Yoga is described. You are situated in the fourth state called Vāsudeva. It is a manifestation that you accept, composed solely of spirit.

### Text 34

*tat-sambhavaḥ kavir ato 'nyad apaśyamānas*  
*tvāṁ bījam ātmani tataṁ sa bahir vicintya*  
*nāvindad abda-śatam apsu nimajjamāno*  
*jāte 'ṅkure katham uhopalabheta bījam*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā, who arose from the lotus, could not see anything except the lotus. He thought that you, who were spread within him, were somewhere outside. Thus searching for a hundred years he could not find you. How can one find the seed in the spout? He then dove into the water to search out the root.**

**Комментарий:**

How can one find the seed which exists as his expansion in the sprout that has just manifested? It cannot be found there. It can be found in the base. It was beyond his comprehension.

### Text 35

*sa tv ātma-yonir ativismita āśrito 'bjaṁ*  
*kālena tīvra-tapasā pariśuddha-bhāvaḥ*  
*tvām ātmanīśa bhuvi gandham ivātisūkṣmaṁ*  
*bhūtendriyāśayamaye vitataṁ dadarśa*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā, astonished, sat on the lotus, and purified by intense mediation upon your form, after some time saw you. You were situated within Brahmā, who is composed of mind, senses and body, just as the** ***tanmātra*** **fragrance is situated in earth.**

**Комментарий:**

Through samādhi, first he realized the Lord as only pure existence known as Brahman.

### Text 36

*evaṁ sahasra-vadanāṅghri-śiraḥ-karoru-*  
*nāsādya-karṇa-nayanābharaṇāyudhāḍhyam*  
*māyāmayaṁ sad-upalakṣita-sanniveśaṁ*  
*dṛṣṭvā mahā-puruṣam āpa mudaṁ viriñcaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**By that method, on seeing the Lord with thousands of faces, feet, heads, hands, thighs, noses, ears, eyes, ornaments, weapons, endowed with spiritual** ***śakti,*** **with perfect arrangement of limbs with auspicious signs, Brahmā derived great bliss.**

**Комментарий:**

Then he saw the Lord’s form of eternity, knowledge and bliss. How did he see the Lord situated as the seed at the base of the sprout? In samādhi, he saw the Lord as a huge form, realizing him to be Viṣṇu, though he viewed him as a form of medium size. Since he was the controller of all the material world, he was full of great śakti (*māyā-maya*).

### Text 38

*itthaṁ nṛ-tiryag-ṛṣi-deva-jhaṣāvatārair*  
*lokān vibhāvayasi haṁsi jagat pratīpān*  
*dharmaṁ mahā-puruṣa pāsi yugānuvṛttaṁ*  
*channaḥ kalau yad abhavas tri-yugo 'tha sa tvam*

**Перевод:**

**In this way, by appearing in various incarnations as a human being, an animal, a great saint, a** ***devatā*****, a fish or a tortoise, you maintain the worlds, kill those who cause disturbance, and protect** ***dharma*** **in all the** ***yugas.*** **Great Lord! Since you will appear covered in Kali-yuga, you are called Tri-yuga.**

**Комментарий:**

Appearing in various forms, the Lord protects the universe.

### Text 39

*naitan manas tava kathāsu vikuṇṭha-nātha*  
*samprīyate durita-duṣṭam asādhu tīvram*  
*kāmāturaṁ harṣa-śoka-bhayaiṣaṇārtaṁ*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord of Vaikuṇṭha! This mind, contaminated with sin, wicked and uncontrollable, is not pleased with topics about you. It is afflicted by lust and disturbed by joy, sorrow, fear and desire for wealth. How can this wretch think of the truth about you in his mind?**

**Комментарий:**

Speaking in this way about the Lord, and then remembering his previous birth condition, thinking of the misery of the birth and, with great thirst thinking of the topics of the Lord, like persons who cannot access them, he criticizes his mind.

### Text 41

*evaṁ sva-karma-patitaṁ bhava-vaitaraṇyām*  
*anyonya-janma-maraṇāśana-bhīta-bhītam*  
*paśyañ janaṁ sva-para-vigraha-vaira-maitraṁ*  
*hanteti pāracara pīpṛhi mūḍham adya*

**Перевод:**

**I see these people who have fallen in the material world by their** ***karma,*** **similar to the Vaitaraṇī River. They are filled with extreme fear concerning eating, birth and death, making friends, enemies, and quarrelling with family members and others. Oh Lord, you can help them cross the river! Deliver these fools today.**

**Комментарий:**

Remembering other’s suffering to be like his own suffering, showing compassion, he ignores his own suffering and requests mercy for them.

### Text 42

*ko nv atra te 'khila-guro bhagavan prayāsa*  
*uttāraṇe 'sya bhava-sambhava-lopa-hetoḥ*  
*mūḍheṣu vai mahad-anugraha ārta-bandho*  
*kiṁ tena te priya-janān anusevatāṁ naḥ*

**Перевод:**

***Guru*** **of all beings! O Lord! What effort is required for you to deliver everyone, since you are the cause of creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe? O friend of the suffering! You are most compassionate to the fools. But why do you have to deliver us? We simply serve your dear devotees and get delivered.**

**Комментарий:**

Thinking of himself deprived of the Lord’s mercy, he implores the Lord, censuring him in affection.

### Text 43

*naivodvije para duratwasyaya-vaitaraṇyās*  
*tvad-vīrya-gāyana-mahāmṛta-magna-cittaḥ*  
*śoce tato vimukha-cetasa indriyārtha-*  
*māyā-sukhāya bharam udvahato vimūḍhān*

**Перевод:**

**I am not disturbed by the insurmountable Vaitaraṇī of the material worlds, since my mind is absorbed in the great sweetness of singing your glories. I lament for the fools, averse to you, who carry the burden of material responsibilities for gaining material happiness from sense objects.**

**Комментарий:**

“You will have disturbance if all my dear devotees are delivered from material existence.” No, not at all. I am not disturbed since my heart is absorbed in the great sweetness of your prowess of which your devotees sing. Though I mentioned I am afraid of saṁsāra ([7.9.16](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/9.16)), that was concerning association with the materialists. At this time I am not disturbed since I am absorbed in the sweetness of your topics. The statement in [7.9.39](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/9.39) expressed the longing to become absorbed. This is the variegated progress of the bhāvas.

### Text 44

*prāyeṇa deva munayaḥ sva-vimukti-kāmā*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! Generally the sages, desiring their own liberation, practice silence and live alone, not interested in others’ welfare. Rejecting those misery sages, I do not desire liberation all alone. I do not see any shelter other than you for those wandering blindly in this world**

**Комментарий:**

As he spoke in verse 42, he now speaks of the sages.

### Text 48

*tvaṁ vāyur agnir avanir viyad ambu mātrāḥ*  
*prāṇendriyāṇi hṛdayaṁ cid anugrahaś ca*

*nānyat tvad asty api mano-vacasā niruktam*

**Перевод:**

**O Supreme Lord! You are the air, the earth, fire, sky and water, the objects of sense perception, the life airs, the senses, the mind,** ***citta*** **and the** ***devatās.*** **You are everything, subtle and gross. Everything expressed, either by the words or by the mind, is nothing but you.**

**Комментарий:**

You are the inner sense organ (*hṛdayam*), composed of mind, intelligence, ego and citta. You are the pure jīva (*cit*) and the śakti that turns people towards you (*anugrahaḥ*). What to speak of being everything in māyā *(saguṇaḥ*) and everything beyond the guṇas (*viguṇa*ḥ).

### Text 50

*tat te 'rhattama namaḥ stuti-karma-pūjāḥ*  
*karma smṛtiś caraṇayoḥ śravaṇaṁ kathāyām*  
*saṁsevayā tvayi vineti ṣaḍ-aṅgayā kiṁ*  
*bhaktiṁ janaḥ paramahaṁsa-gatau labheta*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord worthy of worship! The six parts of** ***bhakti*** **are offering respects, praising you, deity worship, menial service, remembering your lotus feet and hearing about you. How can a person attain** ***prema*** **without these six types of** ***bhakti*** **to you, who also award liberation to the** ***jñānīs*****?**

**Комментарий:**

*Karma* means deity service. *Karma-smṛti* means remembering the pastimes of the Lord. The word *caraṇayoḥ* should be connected with all items to indicate bhakti. Give to all people service consisting of six parts which produces prema-bhakti, the root of all success. By your mercy I have attained success in attaining service directly to you, in attaining your lotus feet directly. By that alone I consider myself successful. Giving mercy to Brahmā and others which was first requested is naturally achieved along with other statements related to it. All other statements are achieved when the one statement is achieved.

### Text 51

*etāvad varṇita-guṇo bhaktyā bhaktena nirguṇaḥ*  
*prahrādaṁ praṇataṁ prīto yata-manyur abhāṣata*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said: Thus, Lord Nṛsiṁhadeva, having no material qualities, whose qualities were described by his devotee with devotion, being pleased with his surrendered devotee, gave up his anger and spoke.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord has qualities like compassion and is endowed with beginningless kriyā-śakti.

**The Lord said: O Prahlāda, most gentle devotee! Best in the family of the demons! Good fortune unto you. I am pleased with you. Please ask for a cherished benediction. I am full of the desires of men.**

**Комментарий:**

Accepting that he give mercy to all jīvas, he will praise Prahlāda till [7.10.14](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/10.14). The Lord becomes eager that his devotee requests some other benedictions.

### Text 54

*prīṇanti hy atha māṁ dhīrāḥ sarva-bhāvena sādhavaḥ*

**Fortunate Prahlāda! The wise devotees, desiring the highest benefit, please me in all different** ***rasas,*** **for I am the master of all blessings.**

## Chapter Ten

### Text 2

*śrī-prahrāda uvāca*  
*mā māṁ pralobhayotpattyā saktaṁkāmeṣu tair varaiḥ*  
*tat-saṅga-bhīto nirviṇṇo mumukṣus tvām upāśritaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Please do not tempt me, attached by my inborn nature to material enjoyment, by offering these benedictions. Detached from material life, fearing association with those desires, and desiring freedom from material desire, I have taken shelter of you.**

**Комментарий:**

*Mumukṣkuḥ* means “desiring to give up material desires.”

### Text 3

*bhṛtya-lakṣaṇa-jijñāsur bhaktaṁ kāmeṣv acodayat*  
*bhavān saṁsāra-bījeṣu hṛdaya-granthiṣu prabho*

**Перевод:**

**What to show the symptoms of your devotee to the world, you have sent me into a world of enjoyment, the seed of** ***saṁsāra,*** **which creates the knot in the heart.**

**Комментарий:**

Desiring to show the people of the world the extraordinary nature of the devotee, you have sent me into the world of enjoyment.

### Text 4

*nānyathā te 'khila-guro ghaṭeta karuṇātmanaḥ*  
*yas ta āśiṣa āśāste na sa bhṛtyaḥ sa vai vaṇik*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***guru*** **of the world! Being merciful, it could not be otherwise. The person who desires benedictions is not your devotee. He is a merchant.**

**Комментарий:**

The person who desires benediction is not your devotee but a merchant.

### Text 6

*ahaṁ tv akāmas tvad-bhaktas tvaṁ ca svāmy anapāśrayaḥ*  
*nānyathehāvayor artho rāja-sevakayor iva*

**Перевод:**

**I am your servant, not desiring benefits, and you are my master, without dependence on service. Our relationship should not be anything else--such as that of a material king and servant.**

**Комментарий:**

An example is given to show the opposite. Our relationship is not like that of a material king and a servant.

### Text 11

*śrī-bhagavān uvāca*  
*naikāntino me mayi jātv ihāśiṣa*  
*āśāsate 'mutra ca ye bhavad-vidhāḥ*  
*tathāpi manvantaram etad atra*  
*daityeśvarāṇām anubhuṅkṣva bhogān*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord said: O Prahlāda! A devotee like you never desires any kind of material benedictions, either in this life or in the next. Nonetheless, I order you to enjoy the luxuries of the demons until the end of the Manvantara.**

**Комментарий:**

The main goal of Nṛsiṁha’s words was that Prahlāda would satisfy all the worlds, according to his prayer that he will not abandon the suffering people:

*prāyeṇa deva munayaḥ sva-vimukti-kāmā*  
*maunaṁ caranti vijane na parārtha-niṣṭhāḥ*  
*naitān vihāya kṛpaṇān vimumukṣa eko*  
*nānyaṁ tvad asya śaraṇaṁ bhramato 'nupaśye*

**O Lord! Generally the sages, desiring their own liberation, practice silence and live alone, not interested in others’ welfare. Rejecting those misery sages, I do not desire liberation all alone. I do not see any shelter other than you for those wandering blindly in this world. (SB** [**7.9.44**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/9.44)**)**

Other intentions are a pretense. Thus he says:

*bhogena puṇyaṁ kuśalena pāpaṁ*  
*kalevaraṁ kāla-javena hitvā*  
*kīrtiṁ viśuddhāṁ sura-loka-gītāṁ*  
*vitāya mām eṣyasi mukta-bandhaḥ*

**Having given up piety by enjoyment and sin by pious actions, and having giving up the previous body by the passing of time, having spread your pure glory praised even in Svarga, and being already free of bondage, you will come to me. (SB** [**7.10.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/10.13)**)**

*ya etat kīrtayen mahyaṁ tvayā gītam idaṁ naraḥ*  
*tvāṁ ca māṁ ca smaran kale karma-bandhāt pramucyate*

**And the person who always remembers your activities and my activities, and who chants the prayers you have offered to please me, becomes free from the bondage of** ***karma*** **in due course of time. (SB** [**7.10.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/10.14)**)**

### Text 12

*kathā madīyā juṣamāṇaḥ priyās tvam*  
*āveśya mām ātmani santam ekam*  
*sarveṣu bhūteṣv adhiyajñam īśaṁ*  
*yajasva yogena ca karma hinvan*

**Перевод:**

**Continuously hearing topics about me and always being absorbed in me in your mind, worship me, the Lord of sacrifice, situated in all beings, by** ***bhakti-yoga*****, while giving up** ***karma-yoga.***

**Комментарий:**

What is the use of all that you previously heard and saw? Whatever he hears is not for glorifying the Lord.

### Text 13

*bhogena puṇyaṁ kuśalena pāpaṁ*  
*kalevaraṁ kāla-javena hitvā*  
*kīrtiṁ viśuddhāṁ sura-loka-gītāṁ*  
*vitāya mām eṣyasi mukta-bandhaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Having given up piety by enjoyment and sin by pious actions, and having giving up the previous body by the passing of time, having spread your pure glory praised even in Svarga, and being already free of bondage, you will come to me.**

**Комментарий:**

You will give up puṇyas to be enjoyed in this life by enjoying them and give up sin by worshipping me (*kuśalena*), the remedy.

*yan-nāmadheya-śravaṇānukīrtanād*  
*yat-prahvaṇād yat-smaraṇād api kvacit*  
*śvādo 'pi sadyaḥ savanāya kalpate*  
*kutaḥ punas te bhagavan nu darśanāt*

**By hearing or chanting your name, by offering respects to you, by occasional remembrance of you, even the dog-eater immediately becomes qualified for performing the** ***soma*** **sacrifice. O Lord! What then to speak of the person who sees you? (SB** [**3.33.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/33.6)**)**

*bhaktyāham ekayā grāhyaḥ śraddhayātmā priyaḥ satām*  
*bhaktiḥ punāti man-niṣṭhā śva-pākān api sambhavāt*

**Only by** ***bhakti*** **with full faith can the devotees obtain me, Paramātmā, the object of love.** ***Bhakti*** **fixed in me purifies even a dog eater of his low birth. (SB** [**11.14.21**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/14.21)**)**

### Text 15-17

*śrī-prahrāda uvāca*  
*varaṁ varaya etat te varadeśān maheśvara*  
*yad anindat pitā me tvām avidvāṁs teja aiśvaram*  
*viddhāmarṣāśayaḥ sākṣāt sarva-loka-guruṁ prabhum*  
*bhrātṛ-heti mṛṣā-dṛṣṭis tvad-bhakte mayi cāghavān*  
*tasmāt pitā me pūyeta durantād dustarād aghāt*  
*pūtas te 'pāṅga-saṁdṛṣṭas tadā kṛpaṇa-vatsala*

**Перевод:**

**Prahlāda said: O bestower of blessings! O great Lord! Please grant this boon. My father, his heart filled with anger, out of ignorance of your power and glory, thinking that you killed his brother, criticized you, the** ***guru*** **of all the planets. He thus committed violence to me. May my father be purified, though he has been purified of his great sins, difficult to remove, by your glance! O Lord, affectionate to the wretched!**

**Комментарий:**

Prahlāda was not aware that his father and uncle were Jaya and Vijaya and thus he asks for this boon.

### Text 18

*śrī-bhagavān uvāca*  
*triḥ-saptabhiḥ pitā pūtaḥ pitṛbhiḥ saha te 'nagha*  
*yat sādho 'sya kule jāto bhavān vai kula-pāvanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord said: O sinless Prahlāda! Your father has been purified, along with twenty-one forefathers in your family. As a devotee, you purify the family. Because you were born in this family, the entire dynasty has been purified.**

**Комментарий:**

He will be purified along with fathers in twenty-one births in the past, if they existed. It is not appropriate to attribute this statement to a past kalpa (when Prahlāda and Jaya and Vijaya had past births). In this birth Prahlāda had no relationship with any previous fathers by birth, and Hiraṇyakaśipu did not have a previous birth.

### Text 20-21

*sarvātmanā na hiṁsanti bhūta-grāmeṣu kiñcana*  
*uccāvaceṣu daityendra mad-bhāva-vigata-spṛhāḥ*  
*bhavanti puruṣā loke mad-bhaktās tvām anuvratāḥ*  
*bhavān me khalu bhaktānāṁ sarveṣāṁ pratirūpa-dhṛk*

**Перевод:**

**King of the demons! Having given up all material desires because of love for me, the devotees do not commit violence to any living entity, whether high or low.**

**Комментарий:**

Those who follow your example will naturally become my devotees. You are the best example for all devotees.

Two verses go together.

### Text 22

*kuru tvaṁ preta-kṛtyāni pituḥ pūtasya sarvaśaḥ*  
*mad-aṅga-sparśanenāṅga lokān yāsyati suprajāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**My dear child! Perform the funeral rites for your father, already purified completely by the touch of my body. Having the best son, he will achieve the best planets.**

**Комментарий:**

You are a good son by your prayers to the Lord. That conduct will make your father attain the highest planet, after destroying his offense to you.

### Text 40

*yathā yathā bhagavato bhaktyā paramayābhidā*  
*nṛpāś caidyādayaḥ sātmyaṁ hares tac-cintayā yayuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just as devotees receive bodies similar to the Lord’s form by** ***bhakti,*** **which is higher than other processes, Śiśupāla, Dantavakra and other kings, by thinking of Kṛṣṇa, achieved the same result.**

**Комментарий:**

Just as others attain sārūpya by the supreme process of bhakti to the Lord, Śiśupāla and other kings attained the same by continuous (*abhidā*) absorption in meditation on the Lord. Still there is a difference between bhakti and continuous thinking. Śiśupāla and Dantavakra attained only sarūpya (whereas others with bhakti attain rasas).

### Text 43-44

*prahrādasyānucaritaṁ mahā-bhāgavatasya ca*  
*bhaktir jñānaṁ viraktiś ca yāthārthyaṁ cāsya vai hareḥ*  
*sarga-sthity-apyayeśasya guṇa-karmānuvarṇanam*  
*parāvareṣāṁ sthānānāṁ kālena vyatyayo mahān*

**Перевод:**

**I have explained the story of the great and exalted devotee Prahlāda, as well as** ***bhakti, jñāna*** **and** ***vairāgya,*** **and the** ***svarūpa*** **of the Lord, as it is*****.*** **I have described the creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe and the qualities and activities of the Lord. I have described the great destruction of the various habitations of the higher and lower creatures by time.**

**Комментарий:**

Three verses describe the contents of this narration. I have explained the actual glories (*yathātmyam*) of the Lord (*hariḥ*), named Kṛṣṇa (*asya*) since he is the source of all the avatāras. Śrīdhara Svāmī mentions the word *asya* in his commentary, and this is the explanation. This is also understood from Prahlḷada’s words.

### Text 48

*yūyaṁ nṛ-loke bata bhūri-bhāgā*  
*lokaṁ punānā munayo 'bhiyanti*  
*yeṣāṁ gṛhān āvasatīti sākṣād*  
*gūḍhaṁ paraṁ brahma manuṣya-liṅgam*

**Перевод:**

**You Pāṇḍavas are most fortunate in this world. Sages who purify the planets come and visit your houses because the Supreme Brahman personally resides in your houses in a human form.**

**Комментарий:**

Prahlāda had such great fortune! Śukadeva speaks three verse to Yudhiṣṭhira who was astonished by the story. He resided with the Pāṇḍavas in a human form, not the form that he showed when he was born.

### Text 50

*na yasya sākṣād bhava-padmajādibhī*  
*rūpaṁ dhiyā vastutayopavarṇitam*  
*maunena bhaktyopaśamena pūjitaḥ*  
*prasīdatām eṣa sa sātvatāṁ patiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The truth about Kṛṣṇa is not perceived as it actually is by Brahmā or others by their intelligence. May the Lord of the devotees, who is worshipped by vows of silence,** ***bhakti*** **and control of the mind, be pleased with us!**

**Комментарий:**

“Someone may think that this form is unreal.” The Lord’s visible form (*sākṣād rūpam*) is not described by Śiva, Brahmā and other most learned persons as a material entity (*avastutayā*). It is described as a spiritual entity.

*tvaṁ brahma paramaṁ sākṣād anādy-antam apāvṛtam*  
*sarveṣām api bhāvānāṁ trāṇa-sthity-apyayodbhavaḥ*

**Uddhava said: You are without beginning or end, superior to Brahman. You are Svayam Bhagavān Kṛṣṇa, unlimited by anything. You are the protection from danger, the giver of maintenance, the destroyer and the creator. (SB** [**11.14.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/14.1)**)**

*aho bhāgyam aho bhāgyaṁ nanda-gopa-vrajaukasām*  
*yan-mitraṁ paramānandaṁ pūrṇaṁ brahma sanātanam*

**How greatly fortunate are Nanda Mahārāja, the cowherd men and all the other inhabitants of Vrajabhūmi! There is no limit to their good fortune, because Kṛṣṇa gave them** ***prema*****, which is the highest bliss, eternal and complete. (SB** [**10.14.32**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/14.32)**)**

*mallānām aśanir nṛṇāṁ nara-varaḥ strīṇāṁ smaro mūrtimān*  
*gopānāṁ sva-jano ’satāṁ kṣiti-bhujāṁ śāstā sva-pitroḥ śiśuḥ*  
*mṛtyur bhoja-pater virāḍ aviduṣāṁ tattvaṁ paraṁ yogināṁ*  
*vṛṣṇīnāṁ para-devateti vidito raṅgaṁ gataḥ sāgrajaḥ*

**The various groups of people in the arena regarded Kṛṣṇa in different ways when he entered it with his elder brother. The wrestlers saw Kṛṣṇa as a lightning bolt, the men of Mathurā as the best of males, the women as Cupid in person, the cowherd men as their relative, the impious rulers as a chastiser, his parents as their child, the King of the Bhojas as death, the unintelligent as the Supreme Lord’s universal form, the yogīs as the Absolute Truth and the Vṛṣṇis as their supreme worshipable deity. (SB** [**10.43.17**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/43.17)**)**

### Text 52

*rājovāca*  
*kasmin karmaṇi devasya mayo 'hañ jagad-īśituḥ*  
*yathā copacitā kīrtiḥ kṛṣṇenānena kathyatām*

**Перевод:**

**Yudhiṣṭhira said: Please explain how the demon Maya Dānava destroyed Lord Śiva's reputation and how Kṛṣṇa restored his reputation.**

**Комментарий:**

Parīkṣit shows how Kṛṣṇa (*anena)* acts as the source of all avatāras and his own shelter in all cases. This is understood later as well.

### Text 70

*evaṁ vidhāny asya hareḥ sva-māyayā*  
*viḍambamānasya nṛ-lokam ātmanaḥ*  
*vīryāṇi gītāny ṛṣibhir jagad-guror*  
*lokaṁ punānāny aparaṁ vadāmi kim*

**Перевод:**

**What more can I say about such astounding acts, which are glorified by sages, and which purify the world, performed by the Supreme Lord who bewilders humans by his** ***māyā*****?**

**Комментарий:**

His activities bewilder the people (*nṛlokam*).

## Chapter Eleven

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*śrutvehitaṁ sādhu-sabhā-sabhājitaṁ*  
*mahattamāgraṇya urukramātmanaḥ*  
*yudhiṣṭhiro daitya-pater mudānvitaḥ*  
*papraccha bhūyas tanayaṁ svayambhuvaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: After hearing about the activities of Prahlāda Mahārāja, the chief of the great devotees, which are adored among the assembly of devotees, Yudhiṣṭhira again inquired from the Nārada with joy.**

**Комментарий:**

Yudhiṣṭhira was joyful because his doubts were cleared. He began to speak about dharma suitable for kings.

### Text 2

*śrī-yudhiṣṭhira uvāca*  
*bhagavan śrotum icchāmi nṛṇāṁ dharmaṁ sanātanam*  
*varṇāśramācāra-yutaṁ yat pumān vindate param*

**Перевод:**

**Mahārāja Yudhiṣṭhira said: My dear lord! I wish to hear from you about the eternal** ***dharma*** **of man,** ***bhakti-yoga,*** **as well as** ***varṇāśrama-dharma*****, by which man achieves the supreme.**

**Комментарий:**

Or he should discuss with Nārada these topics for the benefit of people, while taking shelter of the highest goal (*param*).

### Text 4

*nārāyaṇa-parā viprā dharmaṁ guhyaṁ paraṁ viduḥ*  
*karuṇāḥ sādhavaḥ śāntās tvad-vidhā na tathāpare*

**Перевод:**

**Peaceful** ***brāhmaṇa*** **devotees like you, dedicated to Nārāyaṇa, know the secret** ***dharma.*** **Others do not know.**

**Комментарий:**

“Manu and others are great devotees.” Since it is secret (*guhyam*) they will not reveal it.

### Text 5

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*natvā bhagavate 'jāya lokānāṁ dharma-setave*  
*vakṣye sanātanaṁ dharma nārāyaṇa-mukhāc chrutam*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said: After first offering my obeisances unto Lord Kṛṣṇa, the protector of the dharma for of all living entities, let me explain the principles of** ***bhakti-yoga*** **which I have heard from the mouth of Nārāyaṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is the protector of dharma. This dharma refers to dharma which produces jñāna and bhakti.

### Text 6

*yo 'vatīryātmano 'ṁśena dākṣāyaṇyāṁ tu dharmataḥ*  
*lokānāṁ svastaye 'dhyāste tapo badarikāśrame*

**Перевод:**

**Nārāyaṇa, along with his** ***aṁśa*** **Nara, after appearing in this world through the daughter of Dakṣa, known as Mūrti, with Dharma as his father, engages in executing austerities at Badarikāśrama for the benefit of all living entities.**

**Комментарий:**

In Bhārata-varṣa, the Lord in the form of Nara-Nārāyaṇa, whose glories are inconceivable, to favor to the *jñānīs,* mercifully performs austerities till the end of the *yuga*, which included realization of *ātmā,* abundant *dharma*, knowledge, renunciation, powers, sense control and freedom from false ego. (SB [5.19.9](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/19.9))

*Aṁśena* means with Nara.

### Text 7

*dharma-mūlaṁ hi bhagavān sarva-vedamayo hariḥ*  
*smṛtaṁ ca tad-vidāṁ rājan yena cātmā prasīdati*

**Перевод:**

**For those who know the Lord, the Lord is the cause and proof of** ***dharma*** **since he is essence of all the Vedas and he is the** ***smṛti*** **scriptures as well. By this** ***dharma,*** **the mind** **is pleased.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 58:**

He is the proof for those who know the Lord composed of the Vedas: by śruti and smṛti it is known that dharmas without the Lord are useless and dharma of the Lord is necessary.

Manu says:

*vedo ’khilo dharma-mūlaṁ smṛti-śīle ca tad-vidām \|*  
*ācāraś caiva sādhūnām ātmanas tuṣṭir eva ca \|\|*

**All the Vedas, smṛti and conduct are the root of dharma for those who know. The conduct of the well-behaved is satisfaction of the self. (Manu 2.6)**

However, Prahlāda’s statement is superior to Manu’s statement (satisfaction of the Lord is superior to satisfaction of the self). This is correct since it has been said:

*dharmaḥ projjhita-kaitavo ’tra paramo nirmatsarāṇāṁ satāṁ*  
*vedyaṁ vāstavam atra vastu śivadaṁ tāpa-trayonmūlanam*

**In the Bhāgavatam alone, created by the Lord himself, is presented the real, permanent object which can be understood by those persons without selfish intentions, and which bestows auspiciousness and release from the material world of miseries. In the Bhāgavatam alone is presented the process for attaining that highest object, devoid of all material goals and liberation. (SB** [**1.1.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/1.2)**)**

By this dharma, the mind is pleased. It is also said *yenātmā suprasīdati*: by bhakti the ātmā is very pleased. (SB [1.2.6](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/2.6)) The affix *su* does not merely indicate “very happy” but indicates the excellence of direct bhakti with hearing and other processes (compared to varṇāśrama).

By this dharma the ātmā is pleased. The satisfaction of ātmā is also a proof.

### Text 8-12

*satyaṁ dayā tapaḥ śaucaṁ titikṣekṣā śamo damaḥ*  
*ahiṁsā brahmacaryaṁ ca tyāgaḥ svādhyāya ārjavam*  
*santoṣaḥ samadṛk-sevā grāmyehoparamaḥ śanaiḥ*  
*nṛṇāṁ viparyayehekṣā maunam ātma-vimarśanam*  
*annādyādeḥ saṁvibhāgo bhūtebhyaś ca yathārhataḥ*  
*teṣv ātma-devatā-buddhiḥ sutarāṁ nṛṣu pāṇḍava*  
*śravaṇaṁ kīrtanaṁ cāsya smaraṇaṁ mahatāṁ gateḥ*  
*sevejyāvanatir dāsyaṁ sakhyam ātma-samarpaṇam*  
*nṛṇām ayaṁ paro dharmaḥ sarveṣāṁ samudāhṛtaḥ*  
*triṁśal-lakṣaṇavān rājan sarvātmā yena tuṣyati*

**Перевод:**

**These are the general principles to be followed by all human beings: truthfulness, mercy, austerity, cleanliness, tolerance, discrimination between right and wrong, control of the mind, control of the senses, nonviolence, celibacy, charity,** ***japa*****, simplicity, satisfaction, rendering service to those who see equally, gradually taking leave of unnecessary engagements, observing the futility of activities of human society, avoiding unnecessary talk, considering the difference of the body from the soul, distributing food equally to all living entities, seeing every soul as oneself or a** ***devatā*****, hearing, chanting, and remembering the Lord and his devotees, service, performing deity worship, offering obeisances, becoming a servant, becoming a friend, and surrendering one's whole self. O King Yudhiṣṭhira, these thirty actions have been discerned as the highest** ***dharma*** **of man, by which the Lord is satisfied.**

**Комментарий:**

Five and half verses describe dharma.

### Text 13

*saṁskārā yatrāvicchinnāḥ sa dvijo 'jo jagāda yam*  
*ijyādhyayana-dānāni vihitāni dvijanmanām*  
*janma-karmāvadātānāṁ kriyāś cāśrama-coditāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Those who without fail perform the** ***saṁskāras*** **and are thus approved by Brahmā are** ***dvijas*****, or twice-born. Such** ***brāhmaṇas, kṣatriyas*** **and** ***vaiśyas*****, purified by their family traditions and by their behavior, should worship the Lord, study the Vedas and give charity. They should follow the principles of the four** ***āśramas.***

**Комментарий:**

“Can a śūdra perform saṁskāras and become a dvija?” Brahmā having created him, called him a dvija. A person, in that line, becomes a dvija on obtaining saṁskāras.

### Text 14

*viprasyādhyayanādīni ṣaḍ-anyasyāpratigrahaḥ*  
*rājño vṛttiḥ prajā-goptur aviprād vā karādibhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**For a** ***brāhmaṇa*** **there are six occupational duties. A** ***kṣatriya*** **should not accept charity, but he may perform the other five of these duties. A king or** ***kṣatriya*** **is not allowed to levy taxes on** ***brāhmaṇas*****, but he may make his livelihood by money from his other subjects.**

**Комментарий:**

*Vā* means usually he does not tax the brāhmaṇa but he can take taxes even from the brāhmaṇa.

*vapanaṁ draviṇādānaṁ sthānān niryāpaṇaṁ tathā \|*  
*eṣa hi brahma-bandhūnāṁ vadho nānyo ’sti daihikaḥ \|\|*

**The method of killing the fallen** ***brāhmaṇa*** **is by shaving his head, taking away his wealth, and taking away his living place. One should not physically kill him. (SB** [**1.7.57**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/7.57)**)**

## Chapter Twelve

### Text 5

*sāyaṁ prātaś cared bhaikṣyaṁ gurave tan nivedayet*  
*bhuñjīta yady anujñāto no ced upavaset kvacit*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***brahmacārī*** **should go out in the morning and evening to collect alms and should offer all that he collects to the** ***guru.*** **He should eat only if ordered to take food by the** ***guru;*** **otherwise, if the spiritual master does not give this order, he should fast.**

**Комментарий:**

Not giving the order to eat means that the guru has gone somewhere else etc.

### Text 11

*etat sarvaṁ gṛhasthasya samāmnātaṁ yater api*  
*guru-vṛttir vikalpena gṛhasthasyartu-gāminaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**All these rules described apply equally to the householder and the** ***sannyāsī.*** **But the decision of the householder to maintain** ***brahmacārī*** **vows is according to his decision.**

**Комментарий:**

Following the order of guru, the householder makes a decision to have children or not.

### Text 12

*añjanābhyañjanonmarda- stry-avalekhāmiṣaṁ madhu*  
*srag-gandha-lepālaṅkārāṁs tyajeyur ye bṛhad-vratāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Those who takes a lifetime** ***brahmacārī*** **vow should not indulge in the following: oiling the body, oiling the head, massaging the body, associating with women, painting pictures, eating meat, drinking wine, decorating the body with flower garlands, smearing sandalwood on the body, or decorating the body with ornaments. These they should give up.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī explains that having a woman apply oil to the body is forbidden.

### Text 13-14

*uṣitvaivaṁ guru-kule dvijo 'dhītyāvabudhya ca*  
*trayīṁ sāṅgopaniṣadaṁ yāvad-arthaṁ yathā-balam*

*gṛhaṁ vanaṁ vā praviśet pravrajet tatra vā vaset*

**Перевод:**

**According to the rules and regulations mentioned above, one who is twice-born, namely a** ***brāhmaṇa, kṣatriya*** **or** ***vaiśya*****, should reside in the** ***guru-kula*** **and study and learn all the Vedic literatures along with their supplements and the Upaniṣads, according to his ability and power to study. According to his means, the student should reward the** ***guru*** **with what he desires*****,*** **and then, taking permission from the** ***guru*** **, the disciple should leave and accept one of the other** ***āśramas,*** **namely the** ***gṛhastha-āśrama, vānaprastha-āśrama*** **or** ***sannyāsa-āśrama*** **or remain there as a** ***naiṣṭhika-brahmacārī.***

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together, describing duties of the student.

*Tad vīkṣitāpi va* means you do not hate or criticize them. Gauḍiyas have *na dveṣī.*

### Text 38

*kvacid alpaṁ kvacid bhūri bhuñje 'nnaṁ svādv asvādu vā*  
*kvacid bhūri guṇopetaṁ guṇa-hīnam uta kvacit*  
*śraddhayopahṛtaṁ kvāpi kadācin māna-varjitam*  
*bhuñje bhuktvātha kasmiṁś cid divā naktaṁ yadṛcchayā*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes I eat a small quantity and sometimes a great quantity of food. Sometimes the food is very palatable, and sometimes it is stale. Sometimes it has good qualities and sometimes it does not. Sometimes it is brought with respect, and at other times without respect. I eat it somewhere, during the day or the night, whenever it comes.**

**Комментарий:**

Three lines describe the conditions for obtaining food. Instead of *svādv asvādu* sometimes *sādhv asādhu* is seen.

## Chapter Fourteen

### Text 2

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*gṛheṣv avasthito rājan kriyāḥ kurvan yathocitāḥ*  
*vāsudevārpaṇaṁ sākṣād upāsīta mahā-munīn*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said: O King! A person situated in household life, performing his prescribed duties as an offering to Vāsudeva, should worship the great devotees.**

**Комментарий:**

One should worship the devotees to please them, not for material results for oneself.

### Text 12

*jahyād yad-arthe svān prāṇān hanyād vā pitaraṁ gurum*  
*tasyāṁ svatvaṁ striyāṁ jahyād yas tena hy ajito jitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The unconquerable Lord is conquered by the person who gives up possessiveness of his wife, for whom he would kill himself, his father or his** ***guru.***

**Комментарий:**

By controlling the Lord, the devotee becomes similar.

### Text 13

*kṛmi-viḍ-bhasma-niṣṭhāntaṁ kvedaṁ tucchaṁ kalevaram*  
*kva tadīya-ratir bhāryā kvāyam ātmā nabhaś-chadiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**What is this insignificant body which finally ends in worms, stool or ashes? What is this attraction to the body of a wife? What value is the wife in comparison to the Supreme Lord who covers the sky?**

**Комментарий:**

*Ātmā* means Paramātmā.

### Text 20-23

*ayane viṣuve kuryād vyatīpāte dina-kṣaye*  
*candrādityoparāge ca dvādaśyāṁ śravaṇeṣu ca*  
*tṛtīyāyāṁ śukla-pakṣe navamyām atha kārtike*  
*catasṛṣv apy aṣṭakāsu hemante śiśire tathā*  
*māghe ca sita-saptamyāṁ maghā-rākā-samāgame*  
*rākayā cānumatyā ca māsarkṣāṇi yutāny api*  
*dvādaśyām anurādhā syāc chravaṇas tisra uttarāḥ*  
*tisṛṣv ekādaśī vāsu janmarkṣa-śroṇa-yoga-yuk*

**Перевод:**

**He should perform the** ***śrāddha*** **ceremony at the winter and summer solstices, at spring and autumn equinoxes, in the** ***yoga*** **named Vyatīpāta, on the day in which three lunar** ***tithis*** **are conjoined, during an eclipse of either the moon or the sun, or on the twelfth lunar day when the moon is Śravaṇa-nakṣatra. He should perform this ceremony on the Akṣaya-tṛtīyā day, during the Navamī of the bright fortnight of Kārtika, on the four Aṣṭamīs of the dark fortnight during Hemanta and Śiśira seasons (four months), on the Saptamī of the bright fortnight of the month of Māgha, during the conjunction of Maghā-nakṣatra and the full-moon day, and on the days when the moon is completely full, or not quite completely full, conjoined with the constellations during the month from which the names of the months are derived. He should also perform the** ***śrāddha*** **ceremony on the Dvādaśī when it is in conjunction with any of the constellations named Anurādhā, Śravaṇa, Uttara-phalgunī, Uttarāṣāḍhā or Uttara-bhādrapadā. He should perform this ceremony when the Ekādaśī is in conjunction with either Uttara-phalgunī, Uttarāṣāḍhā or Uttara-bhādrapadā. Finally, he should perform this ceremony when his own birth constellation or Śravaṇa constellation have special astrological combinations.**

**Комментарий:**

Four verses go together, describing śrāddha rites. Though it mentions śrāddha rites on three ekādaśīs, for Vaiṣṇavas śrāddha is forbidden on fasting days.

*śuklākṛṣṇāvibhedaś cāsad-vyāpāro vrate tathā \|*  
*śaktau phalādi-bhuktiś ca śrāddhaṁ caikādaśī-dine \|\|*  
*dvādaśyāṁ ca divā-svāpas tulasyāvacayas tathā \|\|*

**One should not distinguish between the waxing and waning Ekādaśī. One should not perform sinful acts on that day. If possible one should not eat even fruit. One should not perform śrāddha rites on that day. One should not sleep or pluck tulasī leaves during the Dvādaśī day. (Brahma-yamala)**

*ye kurvanti mahīpāla śrāddhaṁ caikādaśī-dine \|*  
*trayas te narakaṁ yānti dātā bhoktā ca prerakaḥ \|\|*

**Those who perform śrāddhas on ekādaśī day go to hells. This includes the donor for the rites, the enjoyer of the rites and the person advising the rites. (Brahma-vaivarta Purāṇa)**

*ekādaśyās tu prāptāyāṁ mātā-pitror mṛte’hani \|*  
*tad dinaṁ tu parityajya dvādaśyāṁ śrāddham ācaret \|*

**When the death of mother and father fall on ekādaśī day, that day is rejected and śrāddha rites are performed on the next day. (Padma Purāṇa)**

*ekādaśyāṁ yadā rāma śrāddhaṁ naimittikaṁ bhavet \|*  
*tad dinaṁ tu parityajya dvādaśyāṁ śrāddham ācaret \|\|*

**O Rāma! When śrāddha rites fall on an ekādaśī, that day is rejected and the rite is performed on the dvādaśī. (Padma Purāṇa)**

### Text 35

*devarṣy-arhatsu vai satsu tatra brahmātmajādiṣu*  
*rājan yad agra-pūjāyāṁ mataḥ pātratayācyutaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King Yudhiṣṭhira! The venerable sages, the** ***devatās*** **and the sons of Lord Brahmā present at your Rājasūya sacrifice decided that Kṛṣṇa was qualified as the first person to be worshiped.**

**Комментарий:**

*Tatra* means “at the rājasūya sacrifice.”

### Text 36

*jīva-rāśibhir ākīrṇa aṇḍa-kośāṅghripo mahān*  
*tan-mūlatvād acyutejyā sarva-jīvātma-tarpaṇam*

**Перевод:**

**The universe is a huge tree filled with billions of** ***jīvas.*** **Worship of Acyuta will satisfy all** ***jīvas*** **since the Lord is the root of the tree.**

**Комментарий:**

Worship of the Lord is the tarpaṇa for all jīvas and yourself.

### Text 37

*purāṇy anena sṛṣṭāni nṛ-tiryag-ṛṣi-devatāḥ*  
*śete jīvena rūpeṇa pureṣu puruṣo hy asau*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord has created bodies for the** ***jīvas*** **in human, animal, sage and** ***devatā*** **forms*****.*** **The Lord resides as Paramātmā in all their bodies.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord resides in the bodies as the antaryāmī of the jīvas (*jīvena rūpeṇa*).

### Text 38

*teṣv eva bhagavān rajas tāratamyena vartate*  
*tasmāt pātraṁ hi puruṣo yāvān ātmā yatheyate*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The Paramātmā resides in all beings proportionately. Therefore he is the object of worship in those beings according to the degree of their knowledge and renunciation.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 289:**

According to degrees of the Lord’s presence, man in general (*puruṣaḥ)* is the object of worship. The jñānī is best because the Lord’s presence is stronger there. And among the jñānīs according to the amount of jñāna, that person is the object of worship.

### Text 39

*dṛṣṭvā teṣāṁ mitho nṛṇām avajñānātmatāṁ nṛpa*  
*tretādiṣu harer arcā kriyāyai kavibhiḥ kṛtā*

**Перевод:**

**My dear King, when great sages saw that people were disrespectful of each other at the beginning of Tretā-yuga, deity worship in the temple was introduced.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 290:**

Though this is so, over time the worshipper has developed faults. The Lord thus revealed a different, special location of the Lord, following the Vedas. Seeing that people thought of themselves while disrespecting others, the wise created the deity form for worship (*kriyāyai)*. This was revealed in order to show the method of serving the Lord. From this it is understood that the deity form is superior to humans as an object of worship since it is effective for people who have the fault of disrespect for humans. *Pratimā svalpa-buddhīnām* means the deity is even for people of less intelligence (and others also). Ambarīṣa and Brahmā worshipped deities according to Nṛsiṁha Purāṇa.

### Text 40

*tato ’rcāyāṁ hariṁ kecit saṁśraddhāya saparyayā*  
*upāsata upāstāpi nārthadā puruṣa-dviṣām*

**Перевод:**

**After that, people offered all the paraphernalia with faith to the deity, but there was no result for those who offended the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 291:**

By the power of the deity (*tataḥ*), some people, dedicated to the highest sādhana, which was superior to previous sādhana, since the deity was the superior dwelling place of the Lord, worshipped the deity. “In that case, this process is also effective for those who hate, since it was meant for people who disrespected each other.” With a desire to refute this suggestion, and with a desire to maintain respect for the human as a residence of the Lord previously mentioned, Nārada says that those who disrespect humans obtain no effect from the worship.

### Text 42

*nanv asya brāhmaṇā rājan kṛṣṇasya jagad-ātmanaḥ*  
*punantaḥ pāda-rajasā tri-lokīṁ daivataṁ mahat*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The** ***brāhmaṇas*****, who purify the three worlds with the dust of their feet, are greatly worshiped by the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhakti Sandarbha 293:**

This previously mentioned brāhmaṇa is praised. Kṛṣṇa is the soul of the universe (*jagad-ātmaṇaḥ*) because he acts as the monitor, spreading dharma within the universe for everyone’s benefit. The brāhmaṇa is shown to be worthy of worship *(daivatam*) by Kṛṣṇa.

## Chapter Fifteen

### Text 1

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*karma-niṣṭhā dvijāḥ kecit tapo-niṣṭhā nṛpāpare*  
*svādhyāye ’nye pravacane kecana jñāna-yogayoḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Some** ***brāhmaṇas*** **are devoted to performing rituals and duties. Some are devoted to austerity. Some are devoted to studying and preaching the Vedas. Some are devoted to** ***jñāna-yoga.***

**Bhakti Sandarbha 294:**

Two verses then speak of the best among the worshipable brāhmaṇas. For those desiring liberation, worship of the jñānī is best. If that person is not available, they should worship other men. From this it is understood that those desiring prema-bhakti should worship prema-bhaktas. For the prema-bhaktas, the deity form is more effective than the manifestation of the Lord in their hearts.

Though he resides in their hearts as well, the Lord’s presence is designated in śālagrāma and other forms, even though he is all-pervading, since those are special places of his manifestation. This is not dependent on seeing the Lord as antaryāmī dwelling in all humans. He is present with his special nature in these deity forms. For instance it is said that even worms become successful by living in places where the deities reside since those places have become mahātīrthas.

*śālagrāma-śilā yatra tat-tīrthaṁ yojana-trayam \|*  
*tatra dānaṁ japo homaḥ sarvaṁ koṭi-guṇaṁ bhavet \|\|*

**Where there is a śālagrāma the area is holy for three yojanas. Charity, japa and sacrifice at that place have ten million times the effect. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

*śālagrāma-samīpe tu krośa-mātraṁ samantataḥ \|*  
*kīkaṭe ’pi mṛto yāti vaikuṇṭha-bhuvanaṁ naraḥ \|\|*

**Even an outcaste who dies within a krośa of a śālagrāma attains Vaikuṇṭha. (Padma Purāṇa)**

This shows the superiority of the deity over humans as an object of worship. Nārada speaks to Yudhiṣṭhira.

### Text 13

*dharma-bādho vidharmaḥ syāt para-dharmo ’nya-coditaḥ*  
*upadharmas tu pākhaṇḍo dambho vā śabda-bhic chalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

***Vidharma*** **means to perform acts which destroy** ***dharma. Para-dharma*** **means to perform another person’s duties.** ***Upadharma*** **means advertising oneself as a follower of** ***dharma,*** **while actually rejecting the Vedas.** ***Chala-dharma*** **means interpreting the words of the scripture incorrectly.**

**Комментарий:**

Three lines describe various distortions of dharma. *Pākaṇḍas* are rejectors of the Vedas. *Dambha* means cheating.

### Text 14

*yas tv icchayā kṛtaḥ pumbhir ābhāso hy āśramāt pṛthak*  
*sva-bhāva-vihito dharmaḥ kasya neṣṭaḥ praśāntaye*

**Перевод:**

***Ābhāsa*** **refers to concocting** ***devatā*** **worship different from what is recommended in one’s** ***āśramas.*** **Can** ***dharma*** **performed according to one’s nature not produce relief from distress?**

### Text 18

*santuṣṭaḥ kena vā rājan na vartetāpi vāriṇā*  
*aupasthya-jaihvya-kārpaṇyād gṛha-pālāyate janaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Why can a self-satisfied person not be happy with drinking only water? A person becomes a dog because of his pursuit of happiness derived from the activities of the genital and tongue.**

**Комментарий:**

Can a person do not satisfied with just water?

### Text 22

*asaṅkalpāj jayet kāmaṁ krodhaṁ kāma-vivarjanāt*  
*arthānarthekṣayā lobhaṁ bhayaṁ tattvāvamarśanāt*

**Перевод:**

**One conquers lust by negative determination. One conquers anger by giving up the desire for violence. One gives up greed by seeing the uselessness of objects of greed. One gives up fear by considering that all suffering is caused by** ***karma.***

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. One gives up fear by understanding that one is dependent on the Lord.

### Text 25

*rajas tamaś ca sattvena sattvaṁ copaśamena ca*  
*etat sarvaṁ gurau bhaktyā puruṣo hy añjasā jayet*

**Перевод:**

**One conquers** ***rajas*** **and** ***tamas*** **by a predominance of** ***sattva.*** **One conquers** ***sattva*** **by being indifferent to the results of actions. One conquers all of these obstacles quickly and easily by devotion to** ***guru.***

**Комментарий:**

One conquers rajas and tamas by sattva, which destroys them (*upaśamen*a). Concerning devotion to guru śruti says:

*yasya deve parā bhaktir yathā deve tathā gurau \|*  
*tasyaite kathitā hy arthāḥ prakāśante mahātmanaḥ \|\|*

**For the person who has bhakti for the Lord and bhakti for guru as much as the Lord the meaning of scripture is revealed. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**6.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.23)**)**

*guru-bhaktyā sa milati smaraṇāt sevyate budhaiḥ \|*  
*milito’pi na labhyeta jīvair paraiḥ \|\| iti \|*

**By devotion to guru one attains the Lord. The intelligent serve the Lord by remembrance. Those subject to pride do not attain the Lord, even though they may encounter him. (Brahma-vaivarta Purāṇa)**

*bhaktir yathā harau me’sti tad variṣṭhā gurau yadi \|*  
*mamāsti tena satyena svaṁ darśayatu me hariḥ \|\|*

**If I have devotion to guru as I have devotion to the Lord, by that strength may the Lord reveal himself to me. (Padma Purāṇa)**

### Text 26

*yasya sākṣād bhagavati jñāna-dīpa-prade gurau*  
*martyāsad-dhīḥ śrutaṁ tasya sarvaṁ kuñjara-śaucavat*

**Перевод:**

**For a person having faulty intelligence who thinks that the** ***guru,*** **who gives the lamp of knowledge and is a spiritual expansion of the Lord, is an ordinary mortal, all that he has heard from** ***guru*** **becomes as useless as cleaning an elephant that merely becomes dirty again.**

**Комментарий:**

It is said by the ancient sages:

*arcye viṣṇau śilā-dhīr guruṣu nara-matir vaiṣṇave jāti-buddhir*  
*viṣṇor vā vaiṣṇavānāṁ kali-mala-mathane pāda-tīrthe 'mbu-buddhiḥ*  
*śrī-viṣṇor nāmni mantre sakala-kaluṣa-he śabda-sāmānya-buddhir*  
*viṣṇau sarveśvareśe tad-itara-sama-dhīr yasya vā nārakī saḥ*

**One who thinks the deity in the temple to be made of stone, who thinks of the gurus as an ordinary man, who thinks the Vaiṣṇava in the Acyuta-gotra to belong to a certain caste, who thinks of caraṇāmṛta or Ganges water as ordinary water, or who thinks that the mantra with the name of Viṣṇu, which destroys all sins is an ordinary sound, is taken to be a resident of hell. (Padma Purāṇa)**

### Text 27

*eṣa vai bhagavān sākṣāt pradhāna-puruṣeśvaraḥ*  
*yogeśvarair vimṛgyāṅghrir loko yaṁ manyate naram*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa and Rāma, whose feet are sought by great** ***yogīs,*** **are certainly the Supreme Lords, though persons who appeared along with them thought of them as human. (Similarly some may think the** ***guru*** **is human, but he is not.)**

**Комментарий:**

This is not astonishing since people even think the Lord was an ordinary human.

### Text 41

*āhuḥ śarīraṁ ratham indriyāṇi*  
*hayān abhīṣūn mana indriyeśam*  
*vartmāni mātrā dhiṣaṇāṁ ca sūtaṁ*  
*sattvaṁ bṛhad bandhuram īśa-sṛṣṭam*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***jñānīs*** **say that the body is a chariot. The senses are the horses; the mind, the master of the senses, is the reins; the objects of the senses are the destinations; intelligence is the chariot driver; and consciousness, which spreads throughout the body, made by the Lord, is the seat for the charioteer.**

### Text 42

*akṣaṁ daśa-prāṇam adharma-dharmau*  
*cakre ’bhimānaṁ rathinaṁ ca jīvam*  
*dhanur hi tasya praṇavaṁ paṭhanti*  
*śaraṁ tu jīvaṁ param eva lakṣyam*

**Перевод:**

**The ten life airs are the spokes.** ***Dharma*** **and** ***adharma*** **are the wheels. The conditioned** ***jīva*** **is the passenger.** ***Oṁ*** **is the bow. They say the pure** ***jīva*** **is the arrow, aimed at the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses describe the body. *Adharma* means sannyāsa—rejection of dharma. Everything is useless without the bow.

### Text 48-49

*hiṁsraṁ dravyamayaṁ kāmyam agni-hotrādy-aśāntidam*  
*darśaś ca pūrṇamāsaś ca cāturmāsyaṁ paśuḥ sutaḥ*  
*etad iṣṭaṁ pravṛttākhyaṁ hutaṁ prahutam eva ca*  
*pūrtaṁ surālayārāma- kūpājīvyādi-lakṣaṇam*

**Перевод:**

**The ritualistic sacrifices known as** ***agni-hotra–yajña, darśa-yajña, pūrṇamāsa-yajña, cāturmāsya-yajña, paśu-yajña,*** ***soma-yajña,*** **Vaiśvadeva and other offerings are the approved path of** ***pravṛtti,*** **producing great material attachment and include killing of animals and offering ingredients to fulfill material desires. It also includes public welfare projects, constructing temples, gardens and wells, and giving sustenance.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses describe pious acts. Building temples and other pious acts are meant for attaining material desires (*pravṛttākhyam*).

### Text 57

*ādāv ante janānāṁ sad bahir antaḥ parāvaram*  
*jñānaṁ jñeyaṁ vaco vācyaṁ tamo jyotis tv ayaṁ svayam*

**Перевод:**

**Brahman is what exists in the beginning and in the end. It is external objects and the** ***jīva*****. It is superior and inferior objects. It is the senses, the sense objects and objects expressed by words. Brahman is the** ***jīva*** **in ignorance and in knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

Everything is described as Paramātmā. This causes destruction of illusion.

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 62:**

Paramātmā, the cause of all things, exists *(sat*) as the beginning of all bodies (*janānām*), being the cause and as the end, as the conclusion. He is himself external things, to be enjoyed and internal objects, the enjoyer. He is superior and inferior. He produces ignorance *(tamas*) and knowledge *(jyotiḥ).* Others, without his sanction, cannot reveal anything on their own.

### Text 58

*ābādhito ’pi hy ābhāso yathā vastutayā smṛtaḥ*  
*durghaṭatvād aindriyakaṁ tadvad artha-vikalpitam*

**Перевод:**

**Just as illusions, though false, are accepted as real, so objects perceived by the senses are accepted as real, because of difficulty in proving them otherwise.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 63:**

“Why do things appear to be different from the Lord?” Though completely (*ā*) negated *(bādhitaḥ*) by contrary logic to be existing independently, a reflection of the sun (*ābhāsaḥ*) is imagined by children to be an independent object (*vastutayā*), since it is seen to manifest separately. In the same way fools imagine objects of the senses to be various independent objects. They cannot see the truth because of difficulty (*durghaṭatvāt*) through thinking the objects to be independent.

### Text 59

*kṣity-ādīnām ihārthānāṁ chāyā na katamāpi hi*  
*na saṅghāto vikāro ’pi na pṛthaṅ nānvito mṛṣā*

**Перевод:**

**The body made of the five elements is neither a combination of the elements nor a whole with the elements as its parts, since it is not completely separate and not intimately connected with them. Thus the body and the world are illusory.**

**Комментарий:**

In the end does the body begin separate from its parts and transform, or is it intimately connected with its parts? The conception that the body is independent is illusory (*mṛṣā*).

### Text 60

*dhātavo ’vayavitvāc ca tan-mātrāvayavair vinā*  
*na syur hy asaty avayaviny asann avayavo ’ntataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Because the elements are only parts, they cannot exist without their sense objects, which are also parts. Since the whole universe and body is unreal, the elements and sense objects are also unreal.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 65:**

*Dhātavaḥ* means the five elements since the word means “those things which support *(dhārayanti*).” The five elements cannot exist without their subtle parts (*avayavaiḥ*), the sense objects (*tan-mātraiḥ),* since the elements are also only parts. Can the parts exist independently? Since the whole cannot be determined (*avayavini*), the parts also cannot finally be determined to exist. The world cannot be proved since the existence of the smallest parts, atoms, cannot be proved. It is said:

*evaṁ niruktaṁ kṣiti-śabda-vṛttam*  
*asan nidhānāt paramāṇavo ye*  
*avidyayā manasā kalpitās te*  
*yeṣāṁ samūhena kṛto viśeṣaḥ*

**What is known by the word “earth” exists in name only because earth is resolved into atomic particles, which are imagined by the mind out of ignorance. Out of their combination, a particular object is made. (SB** [**5.12.9**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/12.9)**)**

Thus the object that can be seen as one by intelligence is Paramātmā, the cause of everything. It was correctly said that Paramātmā is the beginning and end of all beings. (SB [7.15.57](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/15.57)) In the Third Canto it was said:

*iti tāsāṁ sva-śaktīnāṁ satīnām asametya saḥ*  
*prasupta-loka-tantrāṇāṁ niśāmya gatim īśvaraḥ*  
*kāla-sañjñāṁ tadā devīṁ bibhrac chaktim urukramaḥ*  
*trayoviṁśati tattvānāṁ gaṇaṁ yugapad āviśat*

**Seeing the sleeping state in creating the universe because the elements were unmixed, the Lord, first by his energy of cohesion, appearing through time, and then as antaryāmī, entered the twenty-three elements simultaneously*****.***

*so ’nupraviṣṭo bhagavāṁś ceṣṭārūpeṇa taṁ gaṇam*  
*bhinnaṁ saṁyojayām āsa suptaṁ karma prabodhayan*

**The Lord, entering by his kriyā-śakti and awakening the dormant karmas of the jīvas, combined the elements together. (SB** [**3.6.1-3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/6.1)**)**

In the śruti, it is well known that everything is the body of Paramātmā. *Yasya pṛthivī śarīram:* His body is the earth. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad [3.7.3](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/3.7.3)) Thus, what transforms into pradhāna as a part is Paramātmā alone, the *avayavī,* source of the parts, existing everywhere. And thus the world is not false.

### Text 61

*syāt sādṛśya-bhramas tāvad vikalpe sati vastunaḥ*  
*jāgrat-svāpau yathā svapne tathā vidhi-niṣedhatā*

**Перевод:**

**In thinking elements are real, one makes an error by a mistaken attribution of qualities. Just as one has perception of waking and sleep states in a dream, so one has perceptions of good and bad which are stated in the Vedas.**

**Комментарий:**

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 66:**

“If Paramātmā is the body, the source of all parts everywhere, then causes of good qualities and faults and giving designations like brāhmaṇa should not exist. (Only Paramātmā should exist.) Therefore something else must be the source.”

In having doubts about Paramātmā (*vastunaḥ*), not understanding his position as the source, there is the error of thinking of similarity--thinking of identifying with the body, different from the conception that everything is one. After examining the previous and later parts and putting them together, one makes the mistake of thinking “This is a body” because the parts are situated in one place, with a common support, and because one perceives each part as one with the whole body. It is like taking each tree to be the forest.

The Lord says:

*so ’yaṁ dīpo ’rciṣāṁ yadvat srotasāṁ tad idaṁ jalam*  
*so ’yaṁ pumān iti nṛṇāṁ mṛṣā gīr dhīr mṛṣāyuṣām*

**Although the flame is continually changing, foolish people say, “This is the light of the lamp.” Though the river is continually changing, foolish people say, “This is the water of the river.” Similarly, although the material body of a human being is constantly changing, those whose lives are filled with lack of discrimination say “This is so and so.” They lack discrimination and words to express it. (SB** [**11.22.44**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/22.44)**)**

Good and bad and thinking oneself to be a brāhmaṇa etc. exist like perception of waking and sleeping states while in a dream This is how the following verse is understood:

*para-svabhāva-karmāṇi na praśaṁsen na garhayet*  
*viśvam ekātmakaṁ paśyan prakṛtyā puruṣeṇa ca*

**One should neither praise nor criticize the conditioned nature and activities of other persons. Rather, one should see this world along with prakṛti and the jīva** **as one with the Lord. (SB** [**11.28.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/28.1)**)**

*kiṁ bhadraṁ kim abhadraṁ vā dvaitasyāvastunaḥ kiyat*  
*vācoditaṁ tad anṛtaṁ manasā dhyātam eva ca*

**That which is expressed by material words or contemplated by the mind in duality is false. What, therefore, is actually good or bad within this insubstantial world of duality, and how can the extent of such good and bad be measured? (SB** [**11.28.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/28.4)**)**

The verses should be explained in terms of the error of mistaking one object for another because of similarity in the objects (*sādṛśya-bhrama*). Duality is false (*dvaitasya avastunaḥ*). The meaning of this section is that Paramātmā is non-different from the world because it is impossible to determine independence of anything else.

### Text 64

*yad brahmaṇi pare sākṣāt sarva-karma-samarpaṇam*  
*mano-vāk-tanubhiḥ pārtha kriyādvaitaṁ tad ucyate*

**Перевод:**

**My dear Yudhiṣṭhira! When all the activities one performs with mind, words and body are dedicated directly to Brahman, it is called oneness of action.**

**Комментарий:**

Because one’s knowledge has not been perfected, one should perform one’s dharma.

### Text 68

*yathā hi yūyaṁ nṛpa-deva dustyajād*  
*āpad-gaṇād uttaratātmanaḥ prabhoḥ*  
*yat-pāda-paṅkeruha-sevayā bhavān*  
*ahāraṣīn nirjita-dig-gajaḥ kratūn*

**Перевод:**

**Just as you overcame the greatest dangers by service to your Lord, and just as you were able to gather the articles for sacrifice after conquering the directions by serving the Lord’s lotus feet, other devotees also serve the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

By bhakti alone, because of Kṛṣṇa (*prabhoḥ*), your master, you overcame various dangers, and attained ingredients for the sacrifice by service to his lotus feet.

### Text 69

### Text 73

*tāvad dāsyām ahaṁ jajñe tatrāpi brahma-vādinām*  
*śuśrūṣayānuṣaṅgeṇa prāpto ’haṁ brahma-putratām*

**Перевод:**

**I took birth as a** ***śūdra*** **from the womb of a maidservant. By engaging repeatedly in service to Vaiṣṇavas who were well-versed in Vedic knowledge, I became the son of Lord Brahmā.**

**Комментарий:**

By bhakti alone I attained the position of Nārada.

### Text 79

*kṛṣṇa-pārthāv upāmantrya pūjitaḥ prayayau muniḥ*  
*śrutvā kṛṣṇaṁ paraṁ brahma pārthaḥ parama-vismitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada, after being worshiped by Kṛṣṇa and Mahārāja Yudhiṣṭhira, bade them farewell and departed. Yudhiṣṭhira Mahārāja, having heard that Kṛṣṇa was the supreme Brahman, was struck with wonder.**

**Комментарий:**

Yudhiṣṭhira was astonished by understanding Kṛṣṇa’s nature, but now he was even more astonished by understanding Kṛṣṇa’s most exalted nature.
