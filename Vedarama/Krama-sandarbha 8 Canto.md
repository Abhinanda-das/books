# Krama-sandarbha 8 Canto

## Chapter One

### Text 1

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*svāyambhuvasyeha guro vaṁśo 'yaṁ vistarāc chrutaḥ*  
*yatra viśva-sṛjāṁ sargo manūn anyān vadasva naḥ*

**Перевод:**

**King Parīkṣit said: O** ***guru*****! I have heard in detail about the dynasty of Svāyambhuva Manu in which the Prajāpatis carried out creation. Please describe the other Manus.**

**Комментарий:**

*svāmi pādair na yad vyaktaṁ yad vyaktaṁ cāsphuṭaṁ kvacit*  
*tatra tatraiva lokyo ’yaṁ sandarbhaḥ kramanāmakaḥ*

**The Krama Sandarbha is understood to explain what was not explained by Śrīdhara Svāmī, or which was explained unclearly.**

*vaiṣṇavāparitoṣaḥ syād yatra yatra tatas tataḥ*  
*lekhyaṁ vaiṣṇava siddhānta-dākṣiṇyenaiva kiñcana*

**Whatever is not satisfying to the Vaisnavas will be written according to the Vaisnava siddhanta.**

### Text 5

*ākūtyāṁ devahūtyāṁ ca duhitros tasya vai manoḥ*  
*dharma-jñānopadeśārthaṁ bhagavān putratāṁ gataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Svāyambhuva Manu had two daughters, named Ākūti and Devahūti. From their wombs, the Supreme Lord appeared as two sons named Yajña and Kapila respectively, for preaching about religion and knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

The two are identified as Svāyambhuva’s daughters.

### Text 9

*śrī-manur uvāca*  
*yena cetayate viśvaṁ viśvaṁ cetayate na yam*  
*yo jāgarti śayāne 'smin nāyaṁ taṁ veda veda saḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Manu said: The Lord brings the universe to life. The universe does not bring the Lord to life. While the universe sleeps, the Lord is awake. The universe does not know him, but he knows everything.**

**Комментарий:**

The second line explains the first line. The Lord is active while the universe sleeps. During destruction, the Lord alone exists, and he brings the universe to life. *Yena* here means *yaḥ* (he who brings the universe to life.) *supām supo bhavanti*: endings are sometimes used interchangeably. (Vārtika on Pāninī). The world (*ayam*) does not know him. When it is awake, the knowledge in the universe is a shadow of his knowledge and when it sleeps, its knowledge disappears. The Lord on the other hand has all pervading knowledge at all times by his nature.

### Text 11

*yaṁ paśyati na paśyantaṁ cakṣur yasya na riṣyati*  
*taṁ bhūta-nilayaṁ devaṁ suparṇam upadhāvata*

**Перевод:**

**Worship the uncontaminated Lord residing in all beings, who is the friend of the** ***jīva,*** **and who, though seeing all things, is not seen, and whose knowledge is never destroyed.**

**Комментарий:**

*Cakṣuḥ* means his jñāna-śakti arising from his svarūpa. *Svābhāvikī jñāna-bala-kriyā ca*: the Lord is endowed with intrinsic śaktis of jñāna, bala and kriyā. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad [6.8](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.8))

### Text 12

*na yasyādy-antau madhyaṁ ca svaḥ paro nāntaraṁ bahiḥ*  
*viśvasyāmūni yad yasmād viśvaṁ ca tad ṛtaṁ mahat*

**Перевод:**

**He has no beginning, middle or end. He does not consider that anything belongs to him or to anyone else. He has no inside and no outside. From him arise the creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe. He is the form of the universe. He is the permanent truth with eternal form, the perfect Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord’s jñāna-śakti has seen established as eternal. Now his svarūpa is established as eternal.

### Text 13

*sa viśva-kāyaḥ puru-hūta īśaḥ*  
*satyaḥ svayaṁ-jyotir ajaḥ purāṇaḥ*  
*dhatte 'sya janmādy ajayātma-śaktyā*  
*tāṁ vidyayodasya nirīha āste*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord has the universe as his body. He is called by many names. He is the controller. He is truth, and is self-revealing, unborn, and without beginning. He carries out the creation and destruction of the universe by his** ***māyā-śakti*****, and rejecting her by his internal energy, remains indifferent to her.**

**Комментарий:**

This is explained. He has a body which pervades the universe, or he has an unlimited body (*viśva-kayaḥ*). Thus, he is profusely praised in the Vedas (*puru-hūtaḥ*). This is because he is endowed with acintya-śakti (*īśaḥ*). This is because his form is the cause of all existence (*satyaḥ*). This is because he is self-manifesting (*svayam-jyotiḥ*). Thus he is ever fresh (*ajaḥ*), though ancient (*purāṇaḥ*). He is devoid of change. Though fully complete, he carries out creation and destruction of the universe by his māyā-śakti, dependent on him. But he remains unattached to that work by his vidyā. Thus he remains indifferent (*nirīhaḥ*). This is his pastime filled with his acintya nature.

### Text 14

*athāgre ṛṣayaḥ karmāṇ- īhante 'karma-hetave*  
*īhamāno hi puruṣaḥ prāyo 'nīhāṁ prapadyate*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, the sages first perform actions in order to be free of action. A person by regulated action generally attains inaction.**

**Комментарий:**

By learning such pastimes the devotees become happy. Since the person performing actions without attachment accrues no karma, even the sages at the first stage (*agre*) perform actions. They are unattached to the actions and the results, in order to attain the stage of non-action. Why do they not start by performing no action? The reason is (*hi*) that a person’s performing action but being unattached attains non-action. This is gradual not sudden. It is said:

*ārurukṣor muner yogaṁ karma kāraṇam ucyate \|*  
*yogārūḍhasya tasyaiva śamaḥ kāraṇam ucyate \|\|*

**For the sage wishing to attain** ***yoga*****, action is said to be the cause of elevation. For a person who has already attained** ***yoga*****, cessation of action is said to be the cause of maintaining that state. (BG** [**6.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/6.3)**)**

*karmendriyāṇi saṁyamya ya āste manasā smaran \|*  
*indriyārthān vimūḍhātmā mithyācāraḥ sa ucyate \|\|*

**He who, controlling the action senses, remains remembering those objects of the senses in his mind, is bewildered, and is called a pretender. (BG** [**3.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/3.6)**)**

### Text 15

*īhate bhagavān īśo na hi tatra visajjate*  
*ātma-lābhena pūrṇārtho nāvasīdanti ye 'nu tam*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord, the controller, complete in himself, acts but is not bound by action. Those who follow the Lord and perform** ***bhakti*** **do not become bound up by action.**

**Комментарий:**

It was said that the Lord remains unattached to his action of creation by vidyā. The sages remember this. That is explained in this verse. The Lord though performing actions remains unattached, since he is complete, endowed with knowledge which manifests his intrinsic bliss. The sages follow him (*anu*).

### Text 16

*tam īhamānaṁ nirahaṅkṛtaṁ budhaṁ*  
*nirāśiṣaṁ pūrṇam ananya-coditam*  
*nṝñ śikṣayantaṁ nija-vartma-saṁsthitaṁ*  
*prabhuṁ prapadye 'khila-dharma-bhāvanam*

**Перевод:**

**I surrender to the Lord who is** ***ātmārāma,*** **who is without false ego, who is intelligent, without desire, complete, independent, who gives instructions to mankind by his own actions, who is situated in** ***bhakti-yoga,*** **and who teaches** ***bhakti-yoga.***

**Комментарий:**

Finally, after explaining, he shows the result. Verses 13-15 showed the detachment of the Lord *(īhamānam*). Verses 9 and 11 indicated his knowledge (*budham*). Verse 10 indicates his lordship (*prabhum*). Verse 12 indicated his completeness (*pūrṇam*).

Here is the meaning. I surrender to him because he is detached (*īhamānam*), because he is without attachment to creating the world (*nirahaṅkṛtam*), because he experiences his own bliss (*budham*), because he is perfect (*pūrṇam*) and without desire (*nirāśiṣam*). Why does he act? He gives instructions to mankind. “Is instructing mankind an external show?” He is situated completely (*saṁsthitam*) on the path of the devotees *(nija*): he does all actions to favor them. He promotes the root of all dharma (*bhakti*).

### Text 24

*vasiṣṭha-tanayāḥ sapta ṛṣayaḥ pramadādayaḥ*  
*satyā vedaśrutā bhadrā devā indras tu satyajit*

**Перевод:**

**During the reign of the third Manu, Pramada and other sons of Vasiṣṭha became the seven sages. The Satyas, Vedaśrutas and Bhadras became the** ***devatās,*** **and Satyajit became Indra.**

**Комментарий:**

The list continues till the end of the chapter. Śruti says *vasiṣṭha ṛṣiḥ prajāpati-gṛhītayā tvayā* (Black Yajur Veda)

## Chapter Two

### Text 20

*tatraikadā tad-giri-kānanāśrayaḥ*  
*kareṇubhir vāraṇa-yūtha-paś caran*  
*sakaṇṭakaṁ kīcaka-veṇu-vetravad*  
*viśāla-gulmaṁ prarujan vanaspatīn*

**Перевод:**

**The leader of the elephants who lived in the forest of Trikūṭa Mountain once was wandering about with his female elephants, breaking huge trees, bamboos, and reeds having thorns.**

**Комментарий:**

Five verses describe the elephant.

## Chapter Three

### Text 2

*śrī-gajendra uvāca*  
*oṁ namo bhagavate tasmai yata etac cid-ātmakam*  
*puruṣāyādi-bījāya pareśāyābhidhīmahi*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Supreme Lord and meditate upon him, because he makes the universe and body conscious. He is in the form of a human who is the final cause of everything; he is the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Though not normally possible to chant mantras from a previous life, this was possible on this occasion.

### Text 5

*kālena pañcatvam iteṣu kṛtsnaśo*  
*lokeṣu pāleṣu ca sarva-hetuṣu*  
*tamas tadāsīd gahanaṁ gabhīraṁ*  
*yas tasya pāre 'bhivirājate vibhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When in time all causes, effects and all** ***devatās*** **were destroyed, there was deep darkness, difficult to penetrate. The Lord alone remained in Vaikuṇṭha. .**

**Комментарий:**

Three lines describe the destruction. The Lord remains in Mahā-vaikuṇṭha (*pare*).

### Text 6

*na yasya devā ṛṣayaḥ padaṁ vidur*  
*jantuḥ punaḥ ko 'rhati gantum īritum*  
*yathā naṭasyākṛtibhir viceṣṭato*  
*duratyayānukramaṇaḥ sa māvatu*

**Перевод:**

**Just as an ignorant person cannot understand the forms represented by a dancer, the** ***devatās*** **and sages do not know his** ***svarūpa*** **and none among the ignorant people can understand or express his** ***svarūpa*** **in words. May that Lord protect me!**

**Комментарий:**

THz Lord is not understood by ordinary people, as an ignorant person cannot understand the truth about the dancer.

### Text 7

*didṛkṣavo yasya padaṁ sumaṅgalaṁ*  
*vimukta-saṅgā munayaḥ susādhavaḥ*  
*caranty aloka-vratam avraṇaṁ vane*  
*bhūtātma-bhūtāḥ suhṛdaḥ sa me gatiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring his auspicious lotus feet, persons who associate with devotees, who are proper sages, who see all beings as themselves and who are friendly to all, perform devotional vows without failure in the forest. That Lord is my goal.**

**Комментарий:**

*Aloka-vratam* means vratas of bhakti which are not material (*aloka*). *Avraṇam* means without damage.

*yān āsthāya naro rājan na pramādyeta karhicit*  
*dhāvan nimīlya vā netre na skhalen na pated iha*

**O King! One who accepts this process of devotional service will never be affected by pride. Even while running with eyes closed, he will never trip or fall. (SB** [**11.2.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/2.35)**)**

### Text 8-9

*na vidyate yasya ca janma karma vā*  
*na nāma-rūpe guṇa-doṣa eva vā*  
*tathāpi lokāpyaya-sambhavāya yaḥ*  
*sva-māyayā tāny anukālam ṛcchati*  
*tasmai namaḥ pareśāya brahmaṇe 'nanta-śaktaye*  
*arūpāyoru-rūpāya nama āścarya-karmaṇe*

**Перевод:**

**He who has no birth or activities, no name or form, and no qualities or faults, accepts birth and activities as Brahmā and Śiva at the time creation and destruction by his energies of** ***rajas*** **and** ***tamas*** **for creating and destroying the universe. I offer respects to Paramātmā, Brahman, with unlimited powers, who is devoid of material forms but who has many spiritual forms and astonishing activities.**

**Комментарий:**

**Bhagavat Sandarbha 35:**

The meaning is this. A changing state is called transformation. The first transformation is birth (*janma).* The attempts to produce perfection in an imperfect being are called action (*karma).* The sound which somehow indicates an object so that it can be understood by the mind for ordinary transactions is called a name *(nāma*). The quality grasped by the eye is its form (*rūpa*). *Guṇa* is a particular quality causing excellence of an object containing mixture of sattva, rajas and tamas. These qualities are seen in the material world. These do not exist in the Lord since he is always situated in his svarūpa, is perfect, beyond the mind, self manifesting and beyond prakṛti. However the Lord accepts birth, activities, name and form. The statement continues in the next verse.

*tasmai namaḥ pareśāya brahmaṇe ’nanta-śaktaye*  
*arūpāyoru-rūpāya nama āścarya-karmaṇe*

**I offer respects to Paramātmā, Brahman, with unlimited powers, who is devoid of material forms but who has many spiritual forms and astonishing activities. (SB** [**8.3.9**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/13.9)**)**

*niṣkalaṁ niṣkriyaṁ śāntam*

**The Lord is without decay, without material activities, and undisturbed. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**6.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.19)**)**

*aśabdam asparśam arūpam avyayam*

**The Lord is without sound, without touch, without form and indestructible. (Kaṭha Upaniṣad** [**1.3.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/katha-bs/1.3.15)**)**

*sarva-karmā sarva-kāmaḥ sarva-gandhaḥ sarva-rasa*

**The Lord has all activities, all desires, all fragrance and all rasa. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**3.14.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/3.14.4)**)**

All material qualities are actually faults (*doṣa).* The Lord has no fault recognized in the world.

*aiśvarya-yogād bhagavān viruddhārtho’bhidhīyate \|*  
*tathāpi doṣāḥ parame naivāhāryaḥ kathañcana \|*  
*guṇā viruddhā api tu samāhāryāś ca sarvataḥ \|\|*

**Because of his endowment of powers, Bhagavān is said to have contrary characteristics. Still, the fault of birth and transformation can never be found in the Supreme Lord. But contrary qualities are found together in the Lord. (Kūrma Purāṇa)**

*ayam ātmāpahata-pāpmā*

**The Lord is devoid of all sin. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**8.7.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/8.7.1)**)**

*etaṁ saṁyad vāma ity āvakṣate etaṁ sarvāṇi vāmāni nayati eṣa u eva bhāmaṇīḥ eṣa sarveṣu vedeṣu bhāti*

**He is called “full of good qualities.” All qualities come to him. This attractive form is revealed in all the Vedas. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**4.15.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/4.15.2)**)**

The phrase *sarva-gandha* means he is most fragrant. When the Bhāgavatam verse says that the Lord attains birth, activities and qualities, it does not mean that he attains the faults in those qualities, since that is contrary to śruti and the Lord is established as the highest spiritual entity.

“It is contradictory to say that the Lord has birth and activities and does not have birth and activities.” The cause of having contrary natures is explained. He attains this by his māyā. This means that his inconceivable svarūpa-śakti accomplishes what cannot normally be accomplished. One can say that birth and activities do not exist in him since his birth and activities are different from those of prakṛti. They arise from his svarūpa. Commenting on Brahma-sūtra [1.4.15](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/1.4.15), Saṅkara says, “*Sat* is generally used to describe objects which have been transformed by possession of material name and form. *Asat* is that which existed before creation of names and forms. In śruti Brahman is thus called *asat*.”

Viṣṇu Purāṇa says *guṇāṁś ca doṣāṁś ca mune vyatīta*: O sage, the Lord is devoid of good qualities and bad qualities. But later it says *samasta-kalyāṇa-guṇātmako hi*: the Lord is endowed with all auspicious qualities.

*jñāna-śakti-balaiśvarya-vīrya-tejāṁsy aśeṣataḥ \|*  
*bhagavac-chabda-vācyāni vinā heyair guṇādibhiḥ \|\|*

**The word** ***bhagavān*** **means to be endowed with unlimited knowledge, sensory power, bodily strength, power of control, influence and beauty without inferior qualities*****. (*****Viṣṇu Purāṇa** [**6.5.79**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vp/6.5.79)**)**

*yo’sau nirguṇa ity uktaḥ śāstreṣu jagad-īśvaraḥ \|*  
*prākṛtair heya-saṁyuktair guṇair hīnatvam ucyate \|\|*

**It is said in all scriptures that the Lord of the universe is without qualities. He is devoid of inferior qualities. The word “inferior” means material qualities.** ***(*****Padma Purāṇa)**

Thus *sva-māyayā* cannot mean anything except the svarūpa-śakti.

*viśuddha-vijñāna-ghanaṁ sva-saṁsthayā*  
*samāpta-sarvārtham amogha-vāñchitam*  
*sva-tejasā nitya-nivṛtta-māyā-*  
*guṇa-pravāhaṁ bhagavantam īmahi \|\|*

**Let me surrender to you, Bhagavān. You are the condensed form of pure knowledge and by being situated in your bliss you have forms with many qualities. Your desires are always fulfilled. Because your svarūpa-śakti you are never touched by the material guṇas. (SB** [**10.37.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/37.22)**)**

*sva-sukha-nibhṛta-cetās tad-vyudastānya-bhāvo*  
*’py ajita-rucira-līlākṛṣṭa-sāras tadīyam*  
*vyatanuta kṛpayā yas tattva-dīpaṁ purāṇaṁ*  
*tam akhila-vṛjina-ghnaṁ vyāsa-sūnuṁ nato ’smi*

**Let me offer my respectful obeisances unto the son of Vyāsadeva, Śukadeva Gosvāmī, who destroys of all sin, and who, though fixed in his own bliss with no distractions, became attracted to the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa and mercifully spread the Bhāgavatam** **which describes Kṛṣṇa and reveals the rasa of his pastimes. (SB** [**12.12.69**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/12.69)**)**

Thus māyā refers to the cit-śakti since māyā meaning “illusion” would be contrary to the intention of the speaker. Śrīdhara Svāmī explains the word yoga-māyā as cit-śakti.

“But attaining birth and activities means that this happened at a certain time.” He attains these eternally *(anukālam*) and never gives them up. The svarūpa-śakti manifests cause and effect at the same time.

“How can birth and activities be eternal? They are activities. Action is accomplished by a sequence of parts from beginning to end. Without that, action’s very nature would be destroyed.” This is not a fault since the Lord’s forms are always eternal, the manifestations of his form are eternal, the pastimes like birth and activities are eternal, and the manifestations in the spiritual world and material world of his places and associates in unlimited universes and unlimited Vaikuṇṭhas are eternal. Though his birth and activities appear and disappear, with a beginning and end, in some places portions of his birth and activities end, in other places they do not end, and in other places they begin. Since there is no break in continuity of their performance, the birth and activities exist eternally. Sometimes they are slightly different because of different details and sometimes they are exactly the same because of the same details. One form alone performs actions separately in different manifestations. This is Nārada’s intention in saying *citraṁ bataitad ekena vapuṣā*: how astonishing, in one form he performed activities in each palace. In different manifestations where the actions take place, there is a different identity (of Kṛṣṇa and his associates) since separate action takes place. This being the case, the manifestation of rasa generated by the sequence of pastimes is experienced in each place.

“How can the birth and activities be one, since they begin separately in the different manifestations?” There is oneness of the same action arising at different times. Saṅkara in his commentary says:

*dvir-go-śabdobhayam uccarito na tu dvau*  
*go-śabdāv iti pratīti-nirṇītaṁ śabdaikatvam*

**When one says the word “cow” twice, one does not perceive two different words but one.**

It is correct to say that the birth and activities are eternal. In the āgamas it is thus proper to meditate on pastimes which happened in the past (since they are eternal.)

*paramātma-sambandhitvena nityatvāt trivikramatvādiṣv apy upasaṁhāryatvaṁ yujyate*

**It is proper to gather Vāmana and other forms in meditation since everything related to the Lord is eternal. (Madhva’s bhāṣya)**

*yad gataṁ bhavac ca bhaviṣyac ca*

**The Lord was present in the past, is present now and will be present in the future. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**3.8.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/3.8.3)**)**

*Upasaṁhāryatvam* means that it is proper to gather all forms during meditation. The Lord’s birth is different from material birth. It is an appearance of the Lord, which resembles a material birth. Sometime it is not similar to a material birth also. *Ajāyamānā bahudhā vijāyate:* unborn, he is born in many ways. (Puruṣa-sūkta)

*niśīthe tama-udbhūte jāyamāne janārdane*  
*devakyāṁ deva-rūpiṇyāṁ viṣṇuḥ sarva-guhā-śayaḥ*  
*āvirāsīd yathā prācyāṁ diśīndur iva puṣkalaḥ*

**Then the Lord, complete with all his expansions, who is situated in the core of everyone’s heart, responding to the request of his devotees, appeared from Devakī, non-different from the Lord, in the dense darkness of night, like the full moon rising on the eastern horizon. (SB** [**10.3.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/13.8)**)**

*satyaṁ vidhātuṁ nija-bhṛtya-bhāṣitaṁ*  
*vyāptiṁ ca bhūteṣv akhileṣu cātmanaḥ*  
*adṛśyatātyadbhuta-rūpam udvahan*  
*stambhe sabhāyāṁ na mṛgaṁ na mānuṣam*

**To prove the words of his servant--that he is present in all objects--to be true, the Lord showed an astonishing form which was neither animal nor human to the assembly. (SB** [**7.8.17**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/8.17)**)**

*tasyāṁ bahu-tithe kale bhagavān madhusūdanaḥ*  
*kārdamaṁ vīryam āpanno jajñe ’gnir iva dāruṇi*

**After a long time, the Supreme Lord, Madhusūdana, who is like fire in wood, controlled by the strength of bhakti of Kardama, made his appearance. (SB** [**3.24.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/24.6)**)**

By the power of Kardama’s bhakti the avatāra Kapila appeared in this manner. The word *vīrya* (semen) is used only because this is well known for producing a son. The activity is different from material birth, being a pastime of the Lord’s bliss in his svarūpa. The philosophers say *lokavat tu līlā-kaivalyam:* the Lord’s pastimes, without motive, are similar to actions in this world. (Brahma-sūtra [2.1.33](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/2.1.33)) In this world a madman out of intense bliss dances without a particular motive. Similarly the Lord acts without motive.

*sṛṣṭyādikaṁ harir naiva prayojanam apekṣya tu \|*  
*kurute kevalānandād yathā mattasya nartanam \|\|*  
*pūrṇānandasya tasyeha prayojana-matiḥ kutaḥ \|*  
*muktā avyāpta-kāmāḥ syuḥ kim utāsy akhilātmanaḥ \|\|*

**Without a goal, the Lord carries out creation out of bliss alone, like a mad man dancing. What is the question of a goal for the Lord who is complete bliss? Persons who are liberated have no desires to be fulfilled. What then to speak of desires in the Lord, the soul of all beings? (Nārāyaṇa-saṁhitā)**

In this example, the mad man is ignorant. One should not imply this in the Lord. Rather, the example is used to show that the Lord enacts pastimes without considering his goal, simply out of great, inherent bliss. Similarly, one could find fault with the example of inhaling and exhaling (which are performed without motive). This is natural, but in deep sleep the person is also unaware (whereas the Lord is always aware). Thus the Lord’s pastimes arise from the natural bliss of his svarūpa. Śruti says *devasyaiva svabhāvo’yam āpta-kāmasya kā spṛhā*: the act of creation is the nature of the Lord; for one whose desires are fulfilled at all times, what desire does he have? (Māṇḍukya Upaniṣad)

If the Lord’s actions in relation to creation of the universe are unmotivated, then what can one say about his actions in Vaikuṇṭha?

*avyākṛta-vihārāya sarva-vyākṛta-siddhaye*  
*hṛṣīkeśa namas te ’stu munaye mauna-śīline*

**O Lord Hṛṣīkeśa, master of the senses, please let us offer our obeisances unto you, whose pastimes are beyond matter, whose pastimes are proclaimed everywhere, unto you who are ātmārāma but not ātmārāma. (SB** [**10.16.47**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/16.47)**)**

The attraction of Śukadeva and others for the Lord’s pastimes is therefore commendable (since the Lord’s actions are without material motive).

The nature of the Lord’s birth is understood in the following verse from the chapter on the mystery of the Lord’s birth.

*evaṁ ca janmāni karmāṇi hy akartur ajanasya ca \|*  
*varṇayanti sma kavayo veda-guhyāni hṛt-pateḥ \|\|*

**Thus, rejecting the two versions of the universal form, the wise glorify the highest subject of the Vedas--the birth and birth and activities of the Supreme Lord, who resides in the hearts of all beings (unlike the universal form), who has no material birth and no material activities (unlike the jīva). (SB** [**1.3.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/3.35)**)**

The previous two verses are as follows:

*yatreme sad-asad-rūpe pratiṣiddhe sva-saṁvidā \|*  
*avidyayātmani kṛte iti tad brahma-darśanam \|\|*

**When the gross and subtle material forms, which are impositions on the soul in ignorance, are removed by the realization of the devotees, one can realize Brahman.**

*yady eṣoparatā devī māyā vaiśāradī matiḥ \|*  
*sampanna eveti vidur mahimni sve mahīyate \|\|*

**When māyā or ignorance weakens, the Lord bestows his mercy on the jīva. The jīva becomes endowed with this treasure. The wise understand that, being situated in this glorious position, this person is worthy of worship. (SB** [**1.3.33-34**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/3.33)**)**

It is explained here that one attains Brahman by direct knowledge of one’s svarūpa, caused by negating the gross and subtle coverings created by ignorance. When māyā ceases, one realizes the wealth of one’s svarūpa.

The ātmārāmas (*kavayaḥ*) describe the birth and activities of the Lord in the heart, Paramātmā. When the gross and subtle coverings cease, the wealth of realization of his birth and activities arises. Wealth (*sampattī)* means directly seeing. The forms of the Lord are filled with the bliss of the Lord’s svarūpa since they are completely different from material forms. Thus he is described as being without action and without birth (material) and having his birth and actions hidden in the Vedas.

This is explained in Śrīdhara Svāmī’s commentary on the following verses of Akrūra’s praises. (SB [10.48.23-24](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/48.23)) “Are my avatāras and their actions produced by ignorance, like shell being mistaken for silver? No, this is your pastime.”

The Lord’s pastimes are equal to his svarūpa:

*nāma-karma-svarūpāṇi na pariccheda-gocare \|*  
*yasyākhila-pramāṇānāṁ sa viṣṇur garbhagas tava \|\|*

**The Lord’s names, activities and forms are not to be understood any proofs. Viṣṇu has entered your womb.**

Another version has *rūpa-karma.*

Gītā also says *janma-karma ca me divyam evaṁ yo vetti tattvataḥ*: one should know my attractive birth and activities. (BG [4.9](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/4.9))

The Lord’s name is not material. It is self-manifesting, supported by qualities beyond material words and mind.

*aprasiddhes tad-guṇānām anāmāsau prakīrtitaḥ*

**The Lord is glorified as nameless because his qualities are not known in this world. (Vāsudevādhyātma)**

*anāmā so’prasiddhatvād arūpo bhūta-varjanāt*

**He is without material name because he is not known in the world. He is without material form because he is devoid of material elements. (Brahmā Purāṇa)**

This is not contrary to the statement in Viṣṇu Purāṇa:

*na yatra nātha vidyante nāma-jātyādi-kalpanāḥ \|*  
*tad brahma paramaṁ nityam avikāri bhavān aja \|\|*  
*na kalpanām ṛte’rthasya sarvasyādhigamo yataḥ \|*  
*tataḥ kṛṣṇācyutānanta-viṣṇu-nāmabhir īḍyase \|\|*

**You do not have a manufactured name or caste. You are the eternal, unchanging supreme Brahman. O unborn Lord! Since there can be no understanding of meaning without making names, you are worshipped by the names of Viṣṇu, Kṛṣṇa, Acyuta and Ananta (while rejecting manufactured names). (Viṣṇu Purāṇa** [**5.18.53-54**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vp/5.18.53)**)**

In the apparent meaning, the word *kalpana* (manufactured) seems useless. One could simply convey the meaning by saying that the Lord has no name and caste. Moreover by using the words Brahman and *aja*, the names of the supreme Lord are accepted. For instance there is a statement like *ajām ekām lohita-śila-kṛṣṇām* which indicated a person without birth. Saying that Kṛṣṇa’s name is produced (*kalpanā*) would be wrong. How can someone be worshipped with a manufactured name, since anything manufactured is non-eternal. How could Kṛṣṇa be non-eternal? The meaning should thus be “In the Lord’s names like Kṛṣṇa and in his pastimes as a devatā, human or kṣatriya, there is nothing manufactured.”

*viśuddha-vijñāna-ghanaṁ sva-saṁsthayā*  
*samāpta-sarvārtham amogha-vāñchitam*  
*sva-tejasā nitya-nivṛtta-māyā-*  
*guṇa-pravāhaṁ bhagavantam īmahi*

**Let me surrender to you, Bhagavān. You are the condensed form of pure knowledge and because you are bliss itself you have forms with many qualities. Your desires are always fulfilled. Because your svarūpa-śakti you are never touched by the material guṇas. (SB** [**10.37.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/37.22)**)**

According to this statement, the forms are aspects of the Lord’s eternal svarūpa-śakti. Since *(yataḥ)* material understanding cannot take place without making names etc. for all objects seen, therefore (*tataḥ*), because of that, you, who are self-accomplished, are praised by the sages and Vedas through famous names such as Kṛṣṇa, which are also self-accomplished, which are the essence of the Vedas, and which are beyond all material proofs, while rejecting all manufactured meanings and names as your designation. You are not praised by other manufactured names. Your glory is revealed by these names with great power. Or, your glory is made evident by these names.

By these authorized names, well known in scriptures, the Lord is quickly realized. These names have power even if uttered to mean some other object (*sāṅketa).* These names are self-accomplished whereas other names are manufactured.

Another meaning is “O Lord! You are the supreme Brahman, the sole entity, to whom names cannot be applied.” The Lord cannot be the subject of manufactured names because then he becomes qualified (*viśeṣeṇa karoti),* he performs actions, and he changes *(vikāri*). You are *avikāri.* You are not born *(jāyate)* in that form. Thus you are called *aja.*

What can be the reason for giving you names? Without designating names, the meaning of objects cannot be understood. Then how can you be understood without giving you a name? But since manufactured names cannot represent the essential nature of anything, you are praised by names like Kṛṣṇa, which indicate your qualities and which give all puruṣārthas just as you yourself do, even if contemplated by accident. You are praised by the eternally perfect śruti and Purāṇas (using words and names), not by imaginary words indicating impersonalism.

The four names mentioned in the verse—Kṛṣṇa, Acyuta, Ananta and Viṣṇu--are representative of other names, for it is said that other names like Nārāyaṇa also have great power if uttered by accident.

Upavarṣa says *varṇa eva tu śabda*: the syllables are authorative sound.

*tasya ca nityatvāt*

**Jīvas and devatās are controllers because of the eternal will of the Lord. (Brahma-sūtra** [**2.4.16**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/2.4.16)**)**

(Similarly syllables are eternal by the will of the Lord.) Since the syllables are eternal, the names composed of syllables, which are the essence of the Vedas are also eternal. Brahmā says:

*teṣv akṣareṣu bhaviṣyaj-jagad-rūpaṁ prakāśayan*

**The Lord revealed the future universe within the syllables of the mantra. (Gopāla-tāpani Upaniṣad** [**1.26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/gopala-tapani/1.26)**)**

The names of the Lord are shown to be self accomplished since they are unique in causing the universe. These names arose when the Lord appeared. The names have the unique quality of being non-different from the Lord’s svarūpa.

*oṁ āsya jānanto nāma cid viviktan mahas te viṣṇo sumatiṁ bhajāmahe \| oṁ tat sad*

**O Viṣṇu! Your name is spiritual and self-revealed*****.*** **Knowing that name a little*****,*** **not completely, just by chanting it, we have attained fine knowledge of you because that oṁ is self-accomplished. (Ṛg Veda** **1.156.3)**

This is the meaning. O Viṣṇu! Your (*te)* name *(nāma*) is cit-svarūpam (*cit*) and therefore self revealing *(mahaḥ*). We know (*jānantaḥ*) the name (*asya*) slightly *(ā*) by honoring its glories through chanting. However we attain knowledge (*sumatim)* by uttering it *(vivaktan*), just by repeating the syllables, because that oṁ (*tat*) is self-accomplished (*sat*).

Thus it is said that the name gives liberation even by accidental utterance, just as the form of the Lord appears even through fear or hatred.

*apy anya-cittaḥ kruddho vā yaḥ sadā kīrtayed dharim \|*  
*so’pi bandha-ksayān muktiṁ labhec cedi-patir yathā \|\|*

**He who continually chants the name of the Lord inattentively or in anger, destroys bondage and attains liberation just like Śiśupāla. (Padma Purāṇa)**

By chanting the name once, saṁsāra is destroyed, just as by seeing the Lord it is destroyed.

*sakṛd uccāritaṁ yena harir ity akṣara-dvayam \|*  
*baddhaḥ parikaras tena mokṣāya gamanaṁ prati \|\|*

**Whoever chants the two syllables “Hari” only once is ready for attaining liberation. (Skanda Purāṇa)**

The name oṁ is glorified:

*om ity etat brahmaṇo nediṣṭaṁ nāma yasmād uccāryamāṇa*  
*eva saṁsāra-bhayāt tārayati tasmād ucyate tāra*

**Oṁ is another name of Brahman. Since by chanting oṁ one is freed from the fear of saṁsāra, it is called** ***tāra*** **(liberator).**

One should not think that the statement is exaggeration since that is an offense according to Padma Purāṇa (*tathārthavādo harināmni kalpanam*).

*nānuvrajati yo mohād vrajantaṁ jagad-īśvaram \|*  
*jñānāgni-dagdha-karmāpi sa bhaved brahma-rākṣasaḥ \|\|*

**He who does not follow the Lord in his chariot when he moves becomes a brahma-rākṣasa even though he has destroyed all karmas with the fire of knowledge. (Viṣṇu-bhakti-candrodaya)**

Even if a person accepts the Lord’s name, he enters again saṁsāra by committing the offense of thinking that the statements of scripture are exaggeration or imaginary. The name is a form of bliss and the witness in the heart of the devotee, like the form of the Lord. Śaunaka says:

*tad aśma-sāraṁ hṛdayaṁ batedaṁ*  
*yad gṛhyamāṇair hari-nāma-dheyaiḥ \|*  
*na vikriyetātha yadā vikāro*  
*netre jalaṁ gātra-ruheṣu harṣaḥ \|\|*

**That heart which does not transform on hearing the names of the Lord, even though he shows tears in the eyes and hair standing on end, is made of iron. (SB** [**2.3.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/3.24)**)**

In Skanda Purāṇa, Prabhāsa-khaṇḍa, the name of Kṛṣṇa is said to be his svarūpa, giving the results of all the Vedas for those who chant.

*madhura-madhuram etan maṅgalaṁ maṅgalānāṁ*  
*sakala-nigama-vallī-sat-phalaṁ cit-svarūpam \|*  
*sakṛd api parigītaṁ śraddhayā helayā vā*  
*bhṛgu-vara nara-mātraṁ tārayet kṛṣṇa-nāma \|\|*

**The name of Kṛṣṇa is sweeter than the sweetest, the most auspicious of all things auspicious, the highest fruit in the tree of all the Vedas, and is composed entirely of pure consciousness. O best of Bhṛgu’s dynasty! Heard once with faith or in negligence, it can deliver any human being. (Prabhāsa-khaṇḍa, Skanda Purāṇa)**

The name is the svarūpa of the Lord. This is made clear in Nārada-pañcarātra:

*vyaktaṁ hi bhagavān eva sākṣān-nārāyaṇaḥ svayam \|*  
*aṣṭākṣara-svarūpena mukheṣu parivartate \|\|*

**It is clear that the Lord, Nārāyaṇa, is directly in the mouth in the form of the eight syllable mantra.**

Māṇḍukya Upaniṣad describes praṇava. *Oṁ ity etad akṣaram idaṁ sarvaṁ:* oṁ is everything. Chāndogya Upaniṣad *says oṁkāra evedaṁ sarvam*: oṁ is everything.

*praṇavo hy aparaṁ brahma praṇavaś ca paraṁ smṛtam \|*  
*apūrvo’nantaro’bāhyo’naparaḥ praṇavo’vyayaḥ \|\|*  
*sarvasya praṇavo hy ādir madhyam antas tathaiva ca \|*  
*evaṁ hi praṇavaṁ jñātvā vyaśnute tad-anantaram \|\|*  
*praṇavaṁ hīśvaraṁ vidyāt sarvasya hṛdaye sthitam \|*  
*sarva-vyāpinam oṅkāraṁ matvā dhīro na śocati \|\|*  
*amātro’nanta-mātraś ca dvaitasyopaśamaḥ śivaḥ \|*  
*oṅkāro vidito yena sa munir netaro janaḥ \|\|*

**There is nothing compared to oṁ. It is considered supreme Brahman. It has no beginning or end, nothing is beyond it, nothing can compare to it. It is imperishable. Oṁ is the beginning, middle and end of everything. Knowing oṁ, one attains the Lord. Know that oṁ is the Lord situated in everyone’s heart. Understanding that it is all-pervading, the wise man does not lament. It is without measure and of infinite measure. It is all auspicious, destroying duality. He who knows oṁ is the only wise man. (Māṇḍukya-kārikā 26-29)**

One should not think that this is just praise of oṁ, thinking the syllable is unsuitable for the supreme Lord. Like other avatāras, oṁ is an avatāra of the Lord in the form of syllables. It is to be accepted on the strength of śruti that oṁ is non-different from the Lord. The name and the Lord designated by the name (*namī*) are non-different.

*nāma cintāmaṇiḥ kṛṣṇaś caitanya-rasa-vigrahaḥ \|*  
*pūrṇaḥ śuddho nitya-mukto ’bhinnatvān nāma-nāminoḥ \|\|*

**The name of the Lord is cintāmaṇi. It is Kṛṣṇa himself, a form of consciousness and rasa. It is complete, pure, and eternally liberated since there is no difference between the name and Kṛṣṇa himself. (Padma Purāṇa)**

The meaning is this. The name is cintāmaṇi since it bestows everything. Not only that, it is directly Kṛṣṇa, having consciousness like him. The reason is that it is non-different from him. “How can such a name be generated in the senses of a human?” It does not arise from the senses, since it, like the Vedas, is simply made manifest in the senses of a human by the Lord himself.

*śabda-brahma su-durbodhaṁ prāṇendriya-mano-mayam*  
*ananta-pāraṁ gambhīraṁ durvigāhyaṁ samudra-vat*

**The sound of the Vedas is very difficult to comprehend and manifests on different levels within the prāṇa, mind, intelligence and senses. This Vedic sound is unlimited and deep like the ocean. (SB** [**11.21.36**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/21.36)**)**

*mayopabṛṁhitaṁ bhūmnā brahmaṇānanta-śaktinā*  
*bhūteṣu ghoṣa-rūpeṇa biseṣūrṇeva lakṣyate*

**The Vedas, which are spread everywhere by me because I possess many forms, pervade everywhere as Brahman, and possess unlimited powers, are perceived by the wise as nāda within all beings, subtle as the threads within the lotus stalk. (SB** [**11.21.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/21.37)**)**

*kṣīṇāyuṣaḥ kṣīṇa-sattvān durmedhān vīkṣya kālataḥ*  
*vedān brahmarṣayo vyasyan hṛdi-sthācyuta-coditāḥ*

**Observing that people in general were diminished in their life span, strength and intelligence by the influence of time, great sages, inspired by the Lord situated within their hearts, divided the Vedas. (SB** [**12.6.47**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/6.47)**)**

The commentary says, “One may worry that something arising from the power of human intelligence will not be respected. Thus the verse says that the sages were inspired by the Lord in their hearts to divide the Vedas.”

The same form (*tad-rūpeṇa*) was communicated as Bhāgavatam:

*kasmai yena vibhāsito ’yam atulo jñāna-pradīpaḥ purā*  
*tad-rūpeṇa ca nāradāya munaye kṛṣṇāya tad-rūpiṇā*  
*yogīndrāya tad-ātmanātha bhagavad-rātāya kāruṇyatas*  
*tac chuddhaṁ vimalaṁ viśokam amṛtaṁ satyaṁ paraṁ dhīmahi*

**I meditate upon that pure, spotless Nārāyaṇa, who is free from suffering and death and who previously revealed this incomparable lamp of knowledge to Brahmā. Brahmā then spoke it to the sage Nārada, who narrated it to Kṛṣṇa-dvaipāyana Vyāsa. Śrīla Vyāsa revealed this Bhāgavatam to the greatest of sages, Śukadeva Gosvāmī, and Śukadeva mercifully spoke it to Mahārāja Parīkṣit. (SB** [**12.13.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/13.19)**)**

The spiritual nature of the name is indicated in the prayers to Kṛṣṇa in Devakī’s womb:

*na nāma-rūpe guṇa-janma-karmabhir*  
*nirūpitavye tava tasya sākṣiṇaḥ*  
*mano-vacobhyām anumeya-vartmano*  
*deva kriyāyāṁ pratiyanty athāpi hi*

**O Lord! Your name and form representing your qualities, birth and activities, cannot be realized since you are the witness of all things, and can only be inferred by logic and scripture. The devotees realize you through devotional actions. (SB** [**10.2.36**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/2.36)**)**

The form of the Lord is nonmaterial. It appears by the svarūpa-śakti which is self-manifesting. That was shown previously.

*ātma-tattva-viśuddhy-arthaṁ yad āha bhagavān ṛtam \|*  
*brahmaṇe darśayan rūpam avyalīka-vratādṛtaḥ \|\|*

**Worshipped by sincere devotion, revealing his true, spiritual form, the Lord spoke to Brahmā the four essential verses of Bhāgavatam in order to give knowledge about himself. (SB** [**2.9.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/9.4)**)**

The commentary says, “Since it was described in the previous chapter (SB [2.8.8](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/8.8)) that the Lord also has a form, how can bhakti directed to that form produce liberation? This verse answers. For the purpose giving knowledge of the tattva about jīva *(ātmanaḥ*) the Lord spoke to Brahmā about worship through meditation. He showed his eternal form filled with knowledge *(ṛtam*) because he had been served by austerity (*avyalīka-vratādṛtaḥ*). The meaning is this. By ignorance the jīva produces a false relationship with a body. The Lord simply manifests his body of knowledge by yogamāyā. There is a great difference. Thus by worshipping that form one can attain liberation.”

The form was established as spiritual by Vasudeva in SB [10.3.20](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/3.20).

*sa tvaṁ tri-loka-sthitaye sva-māyayā*  
*bibharṣi śuklaṁ khalu varṇam ātmanaḥ*  
*sargāya raktaṁ rajasopabṛṁhitaṁ*  
*kṛṣṇaṁ ca varṇaṁ tamasā janātyaye*

**My Lord, your form is transcendental to the three material modes, yet for the maintenance of the three worlds, you assume the white color of Viṣṇu in goodness; for creation, which is surrounded by the quality of passion, you appear reddish; and at the end, when there is a need for annihilation, which is surrounded by ignorance, you appear blackish.**

The meaning is this. When you (*tvam*), the agent of creation, maintenance and destruction of the material world *(saḥ*), desire to dwell in the world (*triloka-sthitaye*), using māyā-śakti which takes shelter of you (*sva*), you protect the brāhmaṇas (*śukla-varṇam*) dedicated to dharma, created by you (*ātmanaḥ*). This māyā is in material sattva since it is inferior to the Lord and suitable to brāhmaṇas.

When you desire to create, using your māyā in rajas, you protect the various varṇas including brāhmaṇas who have material desires (*raktam varṇam*). When you desire to destroy the population, using māyā in tamas, you protect those engaged in sin (*kṛṣṇam varṇam*).

There is another meaning. When you desire to maintain the worlds, you maintain the pure form of Viṣṇu, without a mixture of the guṇas. Viṣṇu is not associated with guṇas like Śiva and Brahmā. Śukadeva states this:

*śivaḥ śakti-yutaḥ śaśvat tri-liṅgo guṇa-saṁvṛtaḥ*  
*vaikārikas taijasaś ca tāmasaś cety ahaṁ tridhā*

**Śiva is always united with his personal energy, the material nature. Manifesting himself in three features in response to the entreaties of nature’s three modes, he thus embodies the threefold principle of material ego in goodness, passion and ignorance. (SB** [**10.88.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/88.3)**)**

*harir hi nirguṇaḥ sākṣāt puruṣaḥ prakṛteḥ paraḥ*  
*sa sarva-dṛg upadraṣṭā taṁ bhajan nirguṇo bhavet*

**The Lord, however, has no connection with the material modes. He is the Supreme Lord, the all-seeing eternal witness, who is transcendental to material nature. One who worships him becomes similarly free from the material modes. (SB** [**10.88.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/88.5)**)**

*candrikā-viśada-smeraiḥ sāruṇāpāṅga-vīkṣitaiḥ*  
*svakārthānām iva rajaḥ- sattvābhyāṁ sraṣṭṛ-pālakāḥ*

Those Viṣṇu forms, by their smiling glances, which resembled the pure light of the moon, which were also tinged with red, created and protected the desires of their own devotees, as if by the modes of passion and goodness. (SB [10.13.50](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/13.50))

The expressions concerning sattva and rajas are figurative, not real. *Varṇam* means form, not just color (such as white color) since the subject concerns the guṇas and indicates the forms manifested by the guṇas. The form of Viṣṇu for protection, a guṇāvatāra, is well known to be *śyāma* in complexion, and lives in the Milk Ocean. This is shown in Paramātmā Sandarbha. And the destroyer Śiva is known to be white in color, not black. These forms are described in the sandhyopāsāna by Gobhila. Brahmā is golden in complexion. Thus there is no rule for relating the complexions of the personalities to the guṇas. Baka and other entities at the lowest tamas were also blackish.

You manifest (*bibharṣi)* your form to your devotees by mercy (*sva-māyayā*). Red represents the intense passion of creation in rajas. Black represents the complete absence of the svarūpa of the Lord in tamas.

*pārthivād dāruṇo dhūmas tasmād agnis trayīmayaḥ \|*  
*tamasas tu rajas tasmāt sattvaṁ yad brahma-darśanam \|\|*

**Smoke is superior to dull wood, and fire, sacred to the Vedas, is superior to smoke. Similarly rajas is superior to tamas, and sattva is superior to rajas, since sattva is favorable for realizing the Lord. (SB** [**1.2.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/2.24)**)**

“Why do you describe through words having another meaning something which misleads people. For destroying the population, the color black is accepted by tamas. That is the meaning.” The next verse answers.

*tvam asya lokasya vibho rirakṣiṣur*  
*gṛhe ’vatīrṇo ’si mamākhileśvara*  
*rājanya-saṁjñāsura-koṭi-yūthapair*  
*nirvyūhyamānā nihaniṣyase camūḥ*

**O my Lord, proprietor of all creation! You have now appeared in my house, desiring to protect this world. You will kill all the armies that are moving all over the world along with the host of demons dressed as kṣatriya kings. (SB** [**10.3.21**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/3.21)**)**

*Nirvyūhyamānāḥ* means moving about. The meaning is this. Let us forget about other proofs concerning your śuddha-sattva nature as Brahman. You accept a form according to the guṇa, such as sleeping during pralaya since pralaya is a matter of grief. But you do not do that at this time. You should give happiness to the world by protecting it, by destroying the tamasic demons, but instead, on the pretext of killing them, you mercifully give them liberation beyond the guṇas. This is like on the pretext of bringing salt, bringing a horse. It is said:

*jaya-kāle tu sattvasya devarṣīn rajaso ’surān*  
*tamaso yakṣa-rakṣāṁsi tat-kālānuguṇo ’bhajat*

**When sattva becomes prominent the Lord favors the devatās and sages. When rajas becomes prominent he favors the demons. When tamas becomes prominent he favors the Yakṣas and Rākṣasas. The Lord acts according to the dictates of time. (SB** [**7.1.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/1.8)**)**

Thus black is not produced by tamas and red and white are not produced by rajas and sattva. By the last statement it is indicated that the svarūpa-śakti is the ultimate cause. Devakī indicates this with reverence:

*rūpaṁ yat tat prāhur avyaktam ādyaṁ*  
*brahma jyotir nirguṇaṁ nirvikāram*  
*sattā-mātraṁ nirviśeṣaṁ nirīhaṁ*  
*sa tvaṁ sākṣād viṣṇur adhyātma-dīpaḥ*

**That form which they say is invisible, the cause of all causes, the greatest, self-manifesting, without qualities, without change, pure existence, without variety and without action, is directly you, the Supreme Lord, who manifests our intelligence. (SB** [**10 3.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/3.24)**)**

Let us return to the subject. The Lord’s qualities are exceptional, astonishing enough to attract the ātmārāmas.

*ātmārāmāś ca munayo nirgranthā apy urukrame \|*  
*kurvanty ahaitukīṁ bhaktim ittham-bhūta-guṇo hariḥ \|\|*

**Some sages who are ātmārāmas, beyond the scriptures, false ego and rules, also practice unmotivated, pure bhakti to the master of pure bhakti, Kṛṣṇa, since he possesses qualities attractive to even them. (SB** [**1.7.10**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/7.10)**)**

*harer guṇākṣipta-matir bhagavān bādarāyaṇiḥ \|*  
*adhyagān mahad ākhyānaṁ nityaṁ viṣṇu-jana-priyaḥ \|\|*

**Śukadeva, the son of Vyāsa, whose mind became distracted from trance by the qualities of Kṛṣṇa, and who became attracted to the Vaiṣṇavas, studied this great Bhāgavatam. (SB** [**1.7.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/7.11)**)**

*guṇāḥ sarve’pi yujyante hy aiśvaryāt puruṣottame \|*  
*doṣāḥ kathañcin naivātra yujyante paramo hi saḥ \|\|*  
*guṇa-doṣau māyayaiva kecid āhur apaṇḍitāḥ \|*  
*na tatra māyā māyī vā tadīyau tau kuto hy ataḥ \|\|*  
*tasmān na māyayā sarvaṁ sarvam aiśvarya-sambhavam \|*  
*amāyo hīśvaro yasmāt tasmāt taṁ paramaṁ viduḥ \|\|*

**By his power the Lord is endowed with all good qualities and devoid of all faults. Fools say the Lord has good and bad qualities out of bewilderment. There is no māyā or anything made of māyā in the Lord. There is thus no māyā in things related to the Lord. The world is not created by māyā. It arises by the power of the Lord. Because the Lord is beyond māyā he is called supreme by the wise. (Viṣṇu-dharmottara)**

This completes the explanation of SB [8.3.3](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/3.3). Thus it is proved that the qualities and name of Lord are completely different from those in the material world.

“Accepting that the name, form and qualities arise from your svarūpa, since your svarūpa is perfect in itself, what is the purpose of your having these names, forms and qualities?” *Lokāpyaya-sambhavāya:* he accepts them for destruction and attainment. (SB [8.3.8](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/3.8)) He accepts the forms for destroying saṁsāra for the devotees and attainment of the devotees' happiness of bhakti. *Sambhavāya* means “for attaining.” It also means to give special happiness of bhakti to the nitya-siddhas (who have already attained him). Arjuna says:

*tathāyaṁ cāvatāras te bhuvo bhāra-jihīrṣayā \|*  
*svānāṁ cānanya-bhāvānām anudhyānāya cāsakṛt \|\|*

**You appear in this world in this way, in your spiritual form, with a desire to relieve the burden of the earth and to give continuous happiness to your dedicated devotees by letting them worship you. (SB** [**1.7.25**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/7.25)**)**

The meaning is this. Just as with other avatāras and the puruṣāvatāras, so this avatāra, your manifestation as Kṛṣṇa, Svayam Bhagavān, appeared in order to relieve the burden of the earth *(bhuvaḥ*) who has great bhakti and to give happiness of worshipping you constantly *(anudhyānāya)* to the devotees.

“You say that the goal of having forms is to give happiness of bhakti to devotees. Since you are full of bliss, why would you think of their happiness?” The Lord who knows everything is without fault. He would have the fault of lack of compassion by ignoring the devotees who depend only on him (*ananya-bhāvānām*). Compassion appears even in ātmārāmas.

*tathāpi doṣāḥ parame naivāhāryaḥ kathañcana \|*  
*guṇā viruddhā apy ete samāhāryāḥ samantataḥ \|\|*

**Still, the fault of birth and transformation can never be found in the Supreme Lord. But contrary qualities are found together in the Lord. (Kūrma Purāṇa)**

Thus this is possible for the Lord who has a variety of qualities. The śrutis glorify with wonder a portion of this quality spread elsewhere in things related to him. This quality attracts everyone from Brahmā to the lowest entity.

The Lord says:

*bhajato ’pi na vai kecid bhajanty abhajataḥ kutaḥ*  
*ātmārāmā hy āpta-kāmā akṛta-jñā guru-druhaḥ*

**Those individuals who are spiritually self-satisfied, those materially satisfied, those who are ignorant, and those who intentionally offend those who act for others’ welfare do not respond even those who love them, what to speak of those who are inimical.**

*nāhaṁ tu sakhyo bhajato ’pi jantūn*  
*bhajāmy amīṣām anuvṛtti-vṛttaye*  
*yathādhano labdha-dhane vinaṣṭe*  
*tac-cintayānyan nibhṛto na veda*

**But the reason I do not immediately reciprocate the affection of living beings even when they worship me, O gopīs, is that I want to intensify their loving devotion. They then become like a poor man who has gained some wealth and then lost it, and who thus becomes so anxious about it that he can think of nothing else. (SB** [**10.32.19-20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/32.19)**)**

The mercy of the all-powerful Lord whose goal is happiness of the devotee is the Lord’s astonishing excellent nature arising from the play of the bliss of his svarūpa. That is shown in *anukālam ṛcchati:* he eternally accepts birth, in the verse already quoted:

*na vidyate yasya ca janma karma vā*  
*na nāma-rūpe guṇa-doṣa eva vā*  
*tathāpi lokāpyaya-sambhavāya yaḥ*  
*sva-māyayā tāny anukālam ṛcchati*

**He who has no birth or activities, no name or form, and no qualities or faults, eternally accepts birth and activities as Brahmā and Śiva at the time creation and destruction by his energies of rajas and tamas for creating and destroying the universe. (SB** [**8.3.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/3.8)**)**

One should not think that the Lord has any other goal in doing these actions. The goal of the Lord who is most capable is simply bliss.

The details are found in Paramātmā and Bhagavat Sandarbhas. *Arūpāya* means without material form and *ururūpaya* means having unlimited spiritual forms.

### Text 11

*sattvena pratilabhyāya naiṣkarmyeṇa vipaścitā*  
*namaḥ kaivalya-nāthāya nirvāṇa-sukha-saṁvide*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord who is attained by** ***bhakti*** **performed by the devotee free of material desires and endowed with knowledge, to the master of liberation, to the Lord who has knowledge of the bliss of liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

I offer respects to the Lord who is attained by bhakti (*naiṣkarmyeṇa*) made of śuddha sattva (*sattvena*) by the devotee (*vipaścitā).*

*tṛtīyam ṛṣi-sargaṁ vai devarṣitvam upetya saḥ \|*  
*tantraṁ sātvatam ācaṣṭa naiṣkarmyaṁ karmaṇāṁ yataḥ \|\|*

**The Lord, making his appearance among the sages as Nārada, produced the Pañcarātra scriptures from which one learns how devotional activities free one from the bondage of** ***karma.*** **(SB** [**1.3.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/3.8)**)**

*Kaivalya-nāthāya* here means the Lord who gives or does not give pure prema. That is how the word *kaivalya* is used in the following:

*jñānaṁ yad āpratinivṛtta-guṇormi-cakram*  
*āt ma-prasāda uta yatra guṇeṣv asaṅgaḥ \|*  
*kaivalya-sammata-pathas tv atha bhakti-yogaḥ*  
*ko nirvṛto hari-kathāsu ratiṁ na kuryāt \|\|*

**If** ***jñāna,*** **the path approved for attaining the Lord, which creates indifference to the multitude of material** ***guṇas*****, arises and is followed by satisfaction of the self, which creates complete detachment from the** ***guṇas*****, then** ***bhakti-yoga*** **should arise. Experiencing bliss in** ***bhakti,*** **how can that person not have attraction for topics of the Lord? (SB** [**2.3.12**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/3.12)**)**

*sarva-vedānta-sāraṁ yad brahmātmaikatva-lakṣaṇam*  
*vastv advitīyaṁ tan-niṣṭhaṁ kaivalyaika-prayojanam*

**This** ***Bhāgavatam*** **is the essence of all Vedānta philosophy because its subject matter is one Brahman, a substance with no duality. The main goal of the work is prema. (SB** [**12.13.12**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/13.11)**)**

From the Lord arises the experience of the happiness of Brahman.

*madīyaṁ mahimānaṁ ca paraṁ brahmeti śabditam*  
*vetsyasy anugṛhītaṁ me sampraśnair vivṛtaṁ hṛdi*

**By my mercy, you will realize my power known as the impersonal Brahman, which will be disclosed in your heart through questions and answers. (SB** [**8.24.38**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/24.38)**)**

Gītā also mentions both aspects of the Lord. *brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāham*: I am the basis of Brahman. (BG [14.27](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/14.27))

### Text 12

*namaḥ śāntāya ghorāya mūḍhāya guṇa-dharmiṇe*  
*nirviśeṣāya sāmyāya namo jñāna-ghanāya ca*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord who is peaceful and fierce, foolish and endowed with qualities, having no qualities and filled with qualities, and who is condensed knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

He mentions three pairs of contradictory words. By this he shows that the Lord is both cause and effect, and the shelter of mutually contrary śaktis. He has a form of eternity knowledge and bliss (*jñāna-ghanāya*). He has qualities like knowledge (*guṇa-dharmine*). *Sāmyāya* means one who follows after his particular qualities.

He is known is his ābhāsa form by the materialist (*chāyayā*). Or he manifests as an object (*sat-ābhāsāya*) and as something with non-existence (*chāyayā*) like a flower in the sky (*asati*).

The Lord reveals himself to those who give up the rules of the Vedas because they take up jñāna and bhakti (*bhāvena*) for attaining non-action (*naiṣkarmya*).

I offer respects to the most merciful Lord, whose mercy has no end, who is the mind of all beings, who is perceived as Paramātmā, in his expanded form, and who is the ultimate form of Bhagavān, Kṛṣṇa and as Brahman**

### Text 22-24

*yathārciṣo 'gneḥ savitur gabhastayo*  
*niryānti saṁyānty asakṛt sva-rociṣaḥ*  
*tathā yato 'yaṁ guṇa-sampravāho*  
*buddhir manaḥ khāni śarīra-sargāḥ*  
*sa vai na devāsura-martya-tiryaṅ*  
*na strī na ṣaṇḍho na pumān na jantuḥ*  
*nāyaṁ guṇaḥ karma na san na cāsan*  
*niṣedha-śeṣo jayatād aśeṣaḥ*

**Brahmā and other** ***devatās,*** **the actions mentioned in Vedas, the planets, the moving beings and non-moving beings, as lesser expansions of the Lord, are created with different forms and names by the Lord.**

Just as the sparks emanate repeatedly from fire or brilliant rays emanate from the sun, and then merge into the fire or sun again, so intelligence, mind, senses and body are created by the Lord as transformations of the *guṇas* and then merge into him.

The Lord is not a *devatā*, demon, human, animal, female, neuter, male or any other living being. He is not the *guṇas* or *karma.* He remains after everything is negated. May the unlimited Lord remain glorious!

Three verses go together. The body, senses and mind merge into the Lord in a subtle form.

Since he is antaryāmī though he enters the body, he is not like the jīva. Since he does not identify with the body, he remains after all are negated (*niṣedha-śeṣaḥ).* May that unlimited form remain victorious since he is the antaryāmī, giving perfection to the virtuous. Sometimes he also appears by his svarūpa śakti, looking similar to material bodies. May he appear to deliver persons like me.

## Chapter Four

### Text 6

*gajendro bhagavat-sparśād vimukto 'jñāna-bandhanāt*  
*prāpto bhagavato rūpaṁ pīta-vāsāś catur-bhujaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Because Gajendra had been touched directly by Supreme Lord, he was freed from the bondage of ignorance and received the same bodily features as the Lord, with yellow garments and four hands.**

**Комментарий:**

He was liberated from māyā (*vimuktaḥ*), receiving sārūpya.

### Text 9

*yadṛcchayā tatra mahā-yaśā muniḥ*  
*samāgamac chiṣya-gaṇaiḥ pariśritaḥ*  
*taṁ vīkṣya tūṣṇīm akṛtārhaṇādikaṁ*  
*rahasy upāsīnam ṛṣiś cukopa ha*

**Перевод:**

**The sage Agastya arrived there, surrounded by his disciples. When the sage saw Mahārāja Indradyumna, sitting silently engaged in worship in a secluded place without offering him a reception, he became angry.**

**Комментарий:**

He became angry by being disrespected, since this was against the rules. The king had disrespected a brāhmaṇa. It is understood that previously he had disrespected brāhmaṇas.

### Text 10

*tasmā imaṁ śāpam adād asādhur*  
*ayaṁ durātmākṛta-buddhir adya*  
*viprāvamantā viśatāṁ tamisraṁ*  
*yathā gajaḥ stabdha-matiḥ sa eva*

**Перевод:**

**Agastya uttered a curse against the King. “This person does not have proper conduct. He is degraded, without intelligence. He has insulted a** ***brāhmaṇa*** **today. May he therefore enter darkness just like an elephant with little intelligence! Let him become an elephant.”**

**Комментарий:**

The cause was low intelligence (*stabdha-matiḥ*). Agastya became angry in order to relieve him of the period of great suffering arising from the offense. It was mercy on his part.

## Text 11,12(a)

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*evaṁ śaptvā gato 'gastyo bhagavān nṛpa sānugaḥ*  
*indradyumno 'pi rājarṣir diṣṭaṁ tad upadhārayan*  
*āpannaḥ kauñjarīṁ yonim ātma-smṛti-vināśinīm*  
*hary-arcanānubhāvena yad-gajatve 'py anusmṛtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O King! After Agastya had cursed King Indradyumna, the sage left that place along with his disciples. The King accepted the curse as his fate. He obtained the body of an elephant, which generally destroys memory of the Lord, but because of devotional service he remembered his past life.**

**Комментарий:**

Agastya cursed him with full knowledge and compassion (*bhagavān*). Otherwise he would committed many such offenses elsewhere and experience their miserable results. He informed him that he would have little intelligence like an animal, just to teach him. If he really were in that position he would have no chance of deliverance.

## Text 11,12b

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*evaṁ śaptvā gato 'gastyo bhagavān nṛpa sānugaḥ*  
*indradyumno 'pi rājarṣir diṣṭaṁ tad upadhārayan*  
*āpannaḥ kauñjarīṁ yonim ātma-smṛti-vināśinīm*  
*hary-arcanānubhāvena yad-gajatve 'py anusmṛtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O King! After Agastya had cursed King Indradyumna, the sage left that place along with his disciples. The King accepted the curse as his fate. He obtained the body of an elephant, which generally destroys memory of the Lord, but because of devotional service he remembered his past life.**

**Комментарий:**

Though he was an elephant he remembered his past life because he had experience of worshipping the Lord.

### Text 17-24

*śrī-bhagavān uvāca*  
*ye māṁ tvāṁ ca saraś cedaṁ giri-kandara-kānanam*  
*vetra-kīcaka-veṇūnāṁ gulmāni sura-pādapān*  
*śṛṅgāṇīmāni dhiṣṇyāni brahmaṇo me śivasya ca*  
*kṣīrodaṁ me priyaṁ dhāma śveta-dvīpaṁ ca bhāsvaram*  
*śrīvatsaṁ kaustubhaṁ mālāṁ gadāṁ kaumodakīṁ mama*  
*sudarśanaṁ pāñcajanyaṁ suparṇaṁ patageśvaram*  
*śeṣaṁ ca mat-kalāṁ sūkṣmāṁ śriyaṁ devīṁ mad-āśrayām*  
*brahmāṇaṁ nāradam ṛṣiṁ bhavaṁ prahrādam eva ca*  
*matsya-kūrma-varāhādyair avatāraiḥ kṛtāni me*  
*karmāṇy ananta-puṇyāni sūryaṁ somaṁ hutāśanam*  
*praṇavaṁ satyam avyaktaṁ go-viprān dharmam avyayam*  
*dākṣāyaṇīr dharma-patnīḥ soma-kaśyapayor api*  
*gaṅgāṁ sarasvatīṁ nandāṁ kālindīṁ sita-vāraṇam*  
*dhruvaṁ brahma-ṛṣīn sapta puṇya-ślokāṁś ca mānavān*  
*utthāyāpara-rātrānte prayatāḥ susamāhitāḥ*  
*smaranti mama rūpāṇi mucyante te 'ṁhaso 'khilāt*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord said: Freed from all sinful reactions are those who rise from bed at the end of night and fully concentrate their minds with great attention upon my form, your form, this lake, this mountain, the caves, gardens, cane plants, bamboo plants, celestial trees;, my abode, the abodes of Brahmā and Śiva, three peaks of Trikūṭa Mountain; the ocean of milk, Śvetadvīpa, which is always brilliant with spiritual rays, Śrīvatsa, the Kaustubha gem, my garland, my club named Kaumodakī, my Sudarśana disc and Pāñcajanya conchshell, my bearer named Garuḍa, the king of the birds, Śeṣa Nāga, my expansion of energy the goddess of fortune who takes shelter of me, Brahmā, Nārada Muni, Śiva, Prahlāda, the pure, unlimited activities performed by Matsya, Kūrma and Varāha, the sun, the moon, fire, the mantra** ***oṁkāra,*** **the Absolute Truth, the material energy, the cows and** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **devotional service, the wives of Soma and Kaśyapa, who are all daughters of King Dakṣa, the Rivers Ganges, Sarasvatī, Nandā and Yamunā, the elephant Airāvata, Dhruva Mahārāja, the seven sages, and pious human beings.**

**Комментарий:**

Feeling great bliss on delivering a devotee, the Lord gives blessings. It is understood that Śeṣa and others mentioned here also came at that time for it is said:

*taṁ tadvad ārtam upalabhya jagan-nivāsaḥ*  
*stotraṁ niśamya divijaiḥ saha saṁstuvadbhiḥ*  
*chandomayena garuḍena samuhyamānaś*  
*cakrāyudho 'bhyagamad āśu yato gajendraḥ*

**Realizing that Gajendra was suffering in this way, and hearing his prayer, the shelter of the universe, carried by Garuḍa, traveling at desired speed and carrying his weapons, immediately came to the place where Gajendra was struggling while the** ***devatās*** **praised him. (SB** [**8.3.31**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/3.31)**)**

Prahlāda and Akrūra are eternal associates of the Lord. Akrūra’s position is understood from the following:

*te tu brahma-hradam nītā magnāḥ kṛṣṇena coddhṛtāḥ*  
*dadṛśur brahmaṇo lokaṁ yatrākrūro ’dhyagāt purā*

**Kṛṣṇa brought the cowherd men to Brahma-hrada, made them enter the water, and then lifted them up from it. Where Akrūra saw Vaikuṇṭha previously, the cowherd men saw Goloka. (SB** [**10.28.16**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/28.16)**)**

The activities of Matsya, Kūrma and others were being sung by the Gandharvas. Omkāra was being chanted. Gaṅgā etc. refers to the presiding deities of those rivers. Pious humans (*puṇya-ślokān mānavān*) became associates of the Lord and came to see this pastime.

## Chapter Five

### Text 4

*patnī vikuṇṭhā śubhrasya vaikuṇṭhaiḥ sura-sattamaiḥ*  
*tayoḥ sva-kalayā jajñe vaikuṇṭho bhagavān svayam*

**Перевод:**

**From the combination of Śubhra and his wife named Vikuṇṭhā, there appeared the Supreme Lord, Vaikuṇṭha, along with** ***devatās*** **called the Vaikuṇṭhas, who were his expansions.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together.

**Bhagavat Sandarbha 77:**

Just as the Lord’s apparent “birth” is just the appearance of the Lord in this world, so the production of Vaikuṇṭha in this world is simply its appearance and not produced like material objects since both the Lord and his abode are eternal. Just as the Lord was “born” (*jajñe)* so the planet was produced (*kalpitaḥ*). This is the Vaikuṇṭha of Vikuṇṭhā’s son. The original Vaikuṇṭha was seen by Brahmā previous to material creation. This is known from the Second Canto.

However it is said:

*sa tan-niketaṁ parimṛśya śūnyam*  
*apaśyamānaḥ kupito nanāda*  
*kṣmāṁ dyāṁ diśaḥ khaṁ vivarān samudrān*  
*viṣṇuṁ vicinvan na dadarśa vīraḥ*

**Searching for Viṣṇu and finding him absent from his house, Hiraṇyakaśipu became angry and screamed loudly. The hero searched the earth, Svarga, all directions, antarikṣa (sky), the caves and ocean, but did not see Viṣṇu anywhere. (SB** [**8.19.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/19.11)**)**

It should be understood that this place of Viṣṇu which Hiraṇyakaśipu searched was in Svarga.

### Text 15-16

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*yadā yuddhe 'surair devā badhyamānāḥ śitāyudhaiḥ*  
*gatāsavo nipatitā nottiṣṭheran sma bhūriśaḥ*  
*yadā durvāsaḥ śāpena sendrā lokās trayo nṛpa*  
*niḥśrīkāś cābhavaṁs tatra neśur ijyādayaḥ kriyāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O King! When the** ***devatās*****, attacked by demons with their serpent weapons in battle, fell down almost dead and could not get up, after the** ***devatās*** **had been cursed by Durvāsā Muni and the three worlds had lost their prosperity, ritualistic ceremonies such as sacrifices could not be performed.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. *Sendrā lokās trayaḥ* means the three worlds, obedient to Indra, not to the demons. They lost their prosperity.

### Text 19-20

*sa vilokyendra-vāyv-ādīn niḥsattvān vigata-prabhān*  
*lokān amaṅgala-prāyān asurān ayathā vibhuḥ*  
*samāhitena manasā saṁsmaran puruṣaṁ param*  
*uvācotphulla-vadano devān sa bhagavān paraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Upon seeing that the** ***devatās*** **were bereft of all influence and strength, that the three worlds were devoid of auspiciousness, and that the demons were flourishing, powerful Brahmā, who is superior to the** ***devatās,*** **concentrated his mind on the Supreme Lord and then spoke with a joyful countenance.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. Brahmā *(saḥ)* saw that the demons were opposite of what they should be (*ayathā*). This was from the point of view of knowledge, since they did not have knowledge. Brahmā became joyful because he realized the mercy of the Lord in samādhi.

### Text 21

*ahaṁ bhavo yūyam atho 'surādayo*  
*manuṣya-tiryag-druma-gharma-jātayaḥ*  
*yasyāvatārāṁśa-kalā-visarjitā*  
*vrajāma sarve śaraṇaṁ tam avyayam*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā said: We will take shelter of the Lord, from whom I, Lord Śiva, all of you** ***devatās,*** **the demons, the humans, animals, plants and the living entities born of perspiration, have been created through his** ***puruṣa avatāra,*** **expansions (Brahmā and Śiva) and secondary expansions (Marīci and others).**

**Комментарий:**

Brahmā, Śiva and others were created by the aṁśas and kalās of the puruṣa, avatāras of the Lord. From the primary avatarī arises the three puruṣas. From the second puruṣa arises Brahmā, Śiva and Viṣṇu. From Brahmā arises Marīci and others. From them others arise. This statement is made from seeing the avatāri as the first entity.

### Text 22

*na yasya vadhyo na ca rakṣaṇīyo*  
*nopekṣaṇīyādaraṇīya-pakṣaḥ*  
*tathāpi sarga-sthiti-saṁyamārthaṁ*  
*dhatte rajaḥ-sattva-tamāṁsi kāle*

**Перевод:**

**He does not kill, protect, ignore or favor anyone. However, he accepts** ***rajas,*** ***sattva*** **and** ***tamas*** **at the proper time in order to create, maintain and destroy.**

**Комментарий:**

He accepts the guṇas. This statement is made according to *lokavat tu līlā-kaivalyam*: the Lord performs actions similar to humans. (Brahma-sūtra [2.1.33](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/2.1.33))

*sṛṣṭy-ādikaṁ harer naiva prayojanam apekṣya tu*  
*kurute kevalānandād yathā mattasya nartanam*

**The Lord creates the world out of pure bliss, with no purpose, like the creation made by a madman. (Nārāyaṇa Saṁhitā)**

### Text 23

*ayaṁ ca tasya sthiti-pālana-kṣaṇaḥ*  
*sattvaṁ juṣāṇasya bhavāya dehinām*  
*tasmād vrajāmaḥ śaraṇaṁ jagad-guruṁ*  
*svānāṁ sa no dhāsyati śaṁ sura-priyaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**It is now the time for the Lord who accepts** ***sattva*** **to protect proper conduct for the welfare of the living entities. Therefore we should take shelter of the** ***guru*** **of the universe. Being affectionate to the** ***devatās*****, he will give prosperity to us, his devotees.**

**Комментарий:**

“If he acts according to creation and destruction, since at other times, he favored our enemies, we cannot trust the Lord.” We are his devotees (*svānām*). He will not consider us devotees. The Lord has many devotees (*sura-priyaḥ*). He will be merciful to us because his devotees are attached to the devatās.

### Text 24

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*ity ābhāṣya surān vedhāḥ saha devair arindama*  
*ajitasya padaṁ sākṣāj jagāma tamasaḥ param*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O subduer of all enemies! After Brahmā finished speaking to the** ***devatās,*** **he went with them to the abode of Ajita, which is beyond** ***prakṛti.***

**Комментарий:**

They went to the abode of Ajita, Vaikuṇṭha created by the son of Vikuṇṭhā. Though it appeared within the material world, it was not touched by prakṛti, since it was a form of the Lord. They went to a place near this abode.

### Text 26

*śrī-brahmovāca*  
*avikriyaṁ satyam anantam ādyaṁ*  
*guhā-śayaṁ niṣkalam apratarkyam*  
*mano-'grayānaṁ vacasāniruktaṁ*  
*namāmahe deva-varaṁ vareṇyam*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā said: We offer respects to the unlimited, unchanging Lord who is real, the first cause, and who is invisible like a person hiding in a cave, who is without parts, inconceivable, beyond mind and speech, who is the best of the** ***devas,*** **and the most worthy of praise.**

**Комментарий:**

He praises the Lord as Brahman.

### Text 27

*vipaścitaṁ prāṇa-mano-dhiyātmanām*  
*arthendriyābhāsam anidram avraṇam*  
*chāyātapau yatra na gṛdhra-pakṣau*  
*tam akṣaraṁ khaṁ tri-yugaṁ vrajāmahe*

**Перевод:**

**We surrender to the knower of the living entities,** ***prāṇa,*** **mind, intelligence and** ***ahaṅkāra*****, to the Lord who is not available to the senses and sense objects, who is constantly witnessing, but not affected, who has no** ***vidyā*** **and** ***avidyā*** **like the** ***jīva,*** **who is indestructible, and who is the form of happiness appearing as an avatāra in three yugas.**

**Комментарий:**

He praises the lord as Paramātmā in two verses. *Triyugam* means he appears as avatāra in three yugas.

### Text 28

*ajasya cakraṁ tv ajayeryamāṇaṁ*  
*manomayaṁ pañcadaśāram āśu*  
*tri-nābhi vidyuc-calam aṣṭa-nemi*  
*yad-akṣam āhus tam ṛtaṁ prapadye*

**Перевод:**

**We surrender to the real Lord whom they say is the axel of the fast-moving wheel of** ***saṁsāra*** **belonging to the** ***jīva,*** **with sixteen spokes predominated by the mind, three naves made of the** ***guṇas,*** **and eight rims made of the elements.**

**Комментарий:**

The eight elements are those listed in Bhagavat-gītā:

*bhūmir āpo ’nalo vāyuḥ khaṁ mano buddhir eva ca \|*  
*ahaṅkāra itīyaṁ me bhinnā prakṛtir aṣṭadhā \|\|*

**Earth, water, fire, air, ether (as well as their sense objects),** ***pradhāna*****,** ***mahat-tattva*****, and false ego (with the ten senses) are my separated energy in eight divisions. (BG.** [**7.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/7.4)**)**

### Text 29

*ya eka-varṇaṁ tamasaḥ paraṁ tad*  
*alokam avyaktam ananta-pāram*  
*āsāṁ cakāropasuparṇam enam*  
*upāsate yoga-rathena dhīrāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**We offer respects to the Lord who is beyond darkness, whose abode śuddha-sattva, beyond prakṛti, beyond the material world, unlimited and not visible to material eyes, whom the** ***yogī*** **seated on his chariot of** ***yoga,*** **worships using the syllable** ***oṁ*** **, following after Garuḍa*****,***

**Комментарий:**

Two verses praise Bhagavān. We offer respects to the Lord who eternally resides in his abode beyond darkness (*tamas param āsāṁ cakāra*), whom the yogīs worship through the sādhana of yoga following Garuḍa (*upasaparṇam*). This shows their skill. The abode is described. It is the form of śuddha-sattva (*eka-varṇam*), beyond prakṛti, since it is beyond the material world (*alokam*). Thus it is unlimited and not visible to material eyes (*avyaktam)*.

### Text 30

*na yasya kaścātititarti māyāṁ*  
*yayā jano muhyati veda nārtham*  
*taṁ nirjitātmātma-guṇaṁ pareśaṁ*  
*namāma bhūteṣu samaṁ carantam*

**Перевод:**

**We offer respects to the Supreme Lord who is equal to all living entities, who possess his own qualities and forms*****,*** **whose** ***māyā*** **cannot be overcome by anyone unless they surrender to him, whose** ***māyā*** **bewilders men so they do not understand the Lord*****.***

**Комментарий:**

The Lord cannot understood by the foolish. We offer respects to Bhagavān (*pareśam*), by whose māyā people do not know the supreme goal (*artham*). He has qualities arising from himself and forms such as the puruṣāvataras arising from himself (*nirjitātmātma-guṇam*). Though he has such powers, he does not see inferior or superior (*bhūteśu samam carantam*).

### Text 31

*ime vayaṁ yat-priyayaiva tanvā*  
*sattvena sṛṣṭā bahir-antar-āviḥ*  
*gatiṁ na sūkṣmām ṛṣayaś ca vidmahe*  
*kuto 'surādyā itara-pradhānāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**We offer respects to the Lord by whose dear form of** ***sattva-guṇa*** **we have been created but still do not know his subtle nature, though it is manifested externally as time and internally as** ***antaryāmī,*** **and whom the demons with** ***rajas*** **and** ***tamas*** **cannot understand at all*****.***

**Комментарий:**

This verse shows how the Lord is unknowable.

### Text 32

*pādau mahīyaṁ sva-kṛtaiva yasya*  
*catur-vidho yatra hi bhūta-sargaḥ*  
*sa vai mahā-pūruṣa ātma-tantraḥ*  
*prasīdatāṁ brahma mahā-vibhūtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord, with Brahman as his great** ***vibhūti,*** **who manifests as the universal form*****,*** **full of** ***vibhūtis*** **like the earth which is dependent on time, and whose feet represent the earth he created, within which there are four types of living entities, be pleased with us!**

**Комментарий:**

We worship the Lord who is the basis of Brahman, which is in a lower position. This is expressed in twelve verses. Brahman is his mahā-vibhūti. He has a great vibhūti known as Brahman. This is shown in the Eleventh Canto. (SB [11.16.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/16.1))

### Text 33

*ambhas tu yad-reta udāra-vīryaṁ*  
*sidhyanti jīvanty uta vardhamānāḥ*  
*lokā yato 'thākhila-loka-pālāḥ*  
*prasīdatāṁ naḥ sa mahā-vibhūtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord of great** ***vibhūtis*****, from whose semen, in the form of water which possesses great power, living entities and the protectors of the planets are born, live and increase, be pleased with us!**

**Комментарий:**

*Lokās trayaḥ* is commonly seen but Śrīdhara Svāmī accepts *lokā yataḥ.*

### Text 44

*namo 'stu tasmā upaśānta-śaktaye*  
*svārājya-lābha-pratipūritātmane*  
*guṇeṣu māyā-raciteṣu vṛttibhir*  
*na sajjamānāya nabhasvad-ūtaye*

**Перевод:**

**We offer respects to you, possessor of spiritual** ***śaktis,*** **whose expansions have full perfection with all the wealth of your abode*****.*** **We offer respects to you, who remain unattached to the actions of the senses directed to the** ***guṇas*** **made of** ***māyā*** **and whose pastimes are variegated like the air.**

**Комментарий:**

Now giving up the Lord’s relationships with inferior things, they describe his glories of power and pastimes. We offer respects to the Lord endowed with gentle śakti (*upaśānta-śaktaye*), whose expansions (*ātmane*) are completely filled with the attainments of his abode (*svārājya*), whose pastimes are variegated like the air (*nabhasvad-ūtaye).*

### Text 45

*sa tvaṁ no darśayātmānam*  
*asmat-karaṇa-gocaram*  
*prapannānāṁ didṛkṣūṇāṁ*  
*sasmitaṁ te mukhāmbujam*

**Перевод:**

**We are surrendered unto you and wish to see you. Please make your form with its lotus face and smile visible to our senses.**

**Комментарий:**

They state their desire. Show yourself, who will be manifested to our senses.

### Text 46

*tais taiḥ svecchā-bhūtai rūpaiḥ kāle kāle svayaṁ vibho*  
*karma durviṣahaṁ yan no bhagavāṁs tat karoti hi*

**Перевод:**

**O Supreme Lord! You perform activities impossible for us to perform in all cycles of time, through your various incarnations which appear by the desire of your devotees.**

**Комментарий:**

*Svecchā-bhūtaiḥ rūpaiḥ* means “by the desire of your devotees.” The devotees inspire his appearance as avatāra.

## Chapter Six

### Text 3-7

*viriñco bhagavān dṛṣṭvā saha śarveṇa tāṁ tanum*  
*svacchāṁ marakata-śyāmāṁ kañja-garbhāruṇekṣaṇām*  
*tapta-hemāvadātena lasat-kauśeya-vāsasā*  
*prasanna-cāru-sarvāṅgīṁ sumukhīṁ sundara-bhruvam*  
*mahā-maṇi-kirīṭena keyūrābhyāṁ ca bhūṣitām*  
*karṇābharaṇa-nirbhāta- kapola-śrī-mukhāmbujām*  
*kāñcīkalāpa-valaya- hāra-nūpura-śobhitām*  
*kaustubhābharaṇāṁ lakṣmīṁ bibhratīṁ vana-mālinīm*  
*sudarśanādibhiḥ svāstrair mūrtimadbhir upāsitām*  
*tuṣṭāva deva-pravaraḥ saśarvaḥ puruṣaṁ param*  
*sarvāmara-gaṇaiḥ sākaṁ sarvāṅgair avaniṁ gataiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Lord Brahmā along with Lord Śiva and all the** ***devatās*** **bowed to the Lord by prostrating on the ground and then offered prayers. The Lord had a shining, blackish body, His eyes were reddish like the center of a lotus, and his attractive limbs were dressed in garments the color of molten gold. He had a beautiful face and eyebrows. He was decorated with a jeweled crown and bracelets. The cheeks of his lotus face were lit by earrings. He had a belt, arm bands, a necklace and anklets. He wore a garland and the Kaustubha jewel, and held Lakṣmī on his chest. He was worshipped by his weapons personified, such as the** ***cakra.***

**Комментарий:**

Five verses describe the Lord.

### Text 8

*śrī-brahmovāca*  
*ajāta-janma-sthiti-saṁyamāyā-*  
*guṇāya nirvāṇa-sukhārṇavāya*  
*aṇor aṇimne 'parigaṇya-dhāmne*  
*mahānubhāvāya namo namas te*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā said: I offer repeated respects to the Lord who has no destruction of his birth and actions, who is without material qualities but full of auspicious qualities as Bhagavān, who is Brahman full of the happiness of liberation, who is Paramātmā, smaller than an atom, and who has an immeasurable form which manifests him form by his** ***acintya-śakti*****.**

**Комментарий:**

He has without birth, maintenance and destruction, since he was without transformation (*ajāta-janma-sthtiti-saṁyamāya*). Normal maintenance is temporary, having a birth previous to it. *Nirvāṇa-sukhārṇavāya* actually means that the Lord is an ocean of happiness compared to the happiness of mere liberation. This indicates Bhagavān’s supreme position.

### Text 10

*tvayy agra āsīt tvayi madhya āsīt*  
*tvayy anta āsīd idam ātma-tantre*  
*tvam ādir anto jagato 'sya madhyaṁ*  
*ghaṭasya mṛtsneva paraḥ parasmāt*

**Перевод:**

**This universe arises from you, rests upon you and ends in you who are fully independent. You are the beginning, sustenance and end of everything, like the earth, which is the cause of an earthen pot, which supports the pot, and into which the pot, when broken, finally returns. You are superior to Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Bhagavān (*paraḥ*) is superior to Brahman (*parasmāt*). The realization of the Lord surpasses that of Brahman. It is the most excellent manifestation.

*yaḥ paraṁ rahasaḥ sākṣāt tri-guṇāj jīva-saṁjñitāt*  
*bhagavantaṁ vāsudevaṁ prapannaḥ sa priyo hi me*

**He who has completely surrendered to Vāsudeva, who is superior to māyā, to the jīva-śakti and Brahman, is dear to me. (SB** [**4.24.28**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/24.28)**)**

*sa brahma-pāraḥ para-pāra-bhūtaḥ*

**The Lord,** ***para-pāra-bhūta*** **(one who has gone to the other bank, who is beyond the material worlds), is superior to Brahman. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa)**

*akṣarāt tataḥ parataḥ para*

**The Lord is superior to Brahman. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad** [**2.1.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mundaka/2.1.2)**)**

### Text 11

*tvaṁ māyayātmāśrayayā svayedaṁ*  
*nirmāya viśvaṁ tad-anupraviṣṭaḥ*  
*paśyanti yuktā manasā manīṣiṇo*  
*guṇa-vyavāye 'py aguṇaṁ vipaścitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Having created this universe by** ***māyā*** **which is dependent on you, you enter into it. Those who concentrate their mind, who have discernment and who know the conclusion of scriptures, see that you remain untouched by the** ***guṇas*****, though the** ***guṇas*** **transform continually.**

**Комментарий:**

Is the example of earth and pot not contrary to Bhagavān being superior to Brahman?

You have created this universe by māyā, dependent on you *(ātmāśrayāya*), your external form (*svasya*), called prakṛti or pradhāna.

*pṛthivī vāyur ākāśa āpo jyotir ahaṁ mahān*  
*vikāraḥ puruṣo ’vyaktaṁ rajaḥ sattvaṁ tamaḥ param*  
*aham etat prasaṅkhyānaṁ jñānaṁ tattva-viniścayaḥ*

**I am form, taste, aroma, touch and sound;** ***ahaṅkāra*****; the** ***mahat-tattva*****; earth, water, fire, air and sky; the eleven senses; the living entity;** ***prakṛti*****; the modes of goodness, passion and ignorance; and Brahman. I am all of these items, along with knowledge of them, and the determination of truth resulting from that knowledge. (SB** [**11.16.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/16.37)**)**

You enter into it (*tad anupraviṣṭaḥ*). His form is unlimited and limited by his acintya-śakti.

### Text 16

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*evaṁ viriñcādibhir īḍitas tad*  
*vijñāya teṣāṁ hṛdayaṁ yathaiva*  
*jagāda jīmūta-gabhīrayā girā*  
*baddhāñjalīn saṁvṛta-sarva-kārakān*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: When he was thus offered prayers by the** ***devatās*** **headed by Lord Brahmā, the Lord, understanding their hearts, in a deep voice that resembled the rumbling of clouds, replied to the** ***devatās*** **who all stood there attentively with folded hands.**

**Комментарий:**

Understanding that Brahmā had worshipped him, the Lord spoke, as if he were their hearts (*hṛdayam yathā*).

### Text 17

*eka eveśvaras tasmin sura-kārye sureśvaraḥ*  
*vihartu-kāmas tān āha samudronmathanādibhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Although the Supreme Lord, the master of the** ***devatās*****, was capable of doing the activities of the** ***devatās*****, he wanted to enjoy pastimes of churning the ocean, and therefore spoke as follows.**

**Комментарий:**

Sambandhokti has *nṛpeśvaraḥ.*

### Text 20

*arayo 'pi hi sandheyāḥ sati kāryārtha-gaurave*  
*ahi-mūṣikavad devā hy arthasya padavīṁ gataiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***devas*****! To attain an important goal one may even have to make a truce with one's enemies. For attaining one’s goal, one should be like the snake and the mouse.**

**Комментарий:**

During a great flood the mouse and snake live together.

### Text 21

*amṛtotpādane yatnaḥ kriyatām avilambitam*  
*yasya pītasya vai jantur mṛtyu-grasto 'maro bhavet*

**Перевод:**

**Immediately endeavor to produce nectar, which a person who is about to die may drink in order to recover.**

**Комментарий:**

On drinking the nectar (*yasya* *pītasya)* a person about to die recovers.

### Text 24

*yūyaṁ tad anumodadhvaṁ yad icchanty asurāḥ surāḥ*  
*na saṁrambheṇa sidhyanti sarvārthāḥ sāntvayā yathā*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***devatās*****! Since all goals are accomplished by compromise and not with anger, agree to whatever the demons desire.**

**Комментарий:**

*Samrambhena* means “with anger.”

### Text 25

*na bhetavyaṁ kālakūṭād viṣāj jaladhi-sambhavāt*  
*lobhaḥ kāryo na vo jātu roṣaḥ kāmas tu vastuṣu*

**Перевод:**

**You should not fear the poison known as** ***kālakūṭa*** **which will be generated from the ocean of milk. And you should not be greedy, lusty and angry when products arise from churning the ocean.**

**Комментарий:**

One has greed (*lobhaḥ*) for visible objects and one has *kāmaḥ* for unseen objects.

### Text 28

*dṛṣṭvārīn apy asaṁyattāñ jāta-kṣobhān sva-nāyakān*  
*nyaṣedhad daitya-rāṭ ślokyaḥ sandhi-vigraha-kālavit*

**Перевод:**

**Famous Mahārāja Bali, the king of the demons, knowing when to make peace and when to fight, seeing the** ***devatās*** **in a conciliatory mood, restrained his commanders who were ready to kill them.**

**Комментарий:**

Seeing the enemy without weapons and armor, Bali restrained the demons, who were ready to kill the devatās (*jāta-kṣobhān*).

### Text 37

*giri-pāta-viniṣpiṣṭān vilokyāmara-dānavān*  
*īkṣayā jīvayām āsa nirjarān nirvraṇān yathā*

**Перевод:**

**Observing that the demons and the** ***devatās*** **had been crushed by the falling mountain, the Lord rejuvenated them by his glance, so that they were fresh and without injuries.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord brought them to life properly (*yathā*), making them fresh and without injury

## Chapter Seven

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*te nāga-rājam āmantrya phala-bhāgena vāsukim*  
*parivīya girau tasmin netram abdhiṁ mudānvitāḥ*  
*ārebhire surā yattā amṛtārthe kurūdvaha*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O best of the Kurus! The** ***devatās*** **and demons summoned Vāsuki, king of the serpents and promised to give him a share of the nectar. They coiled Vāsuki around Mandara Mountain as a churning rope, and with great pleasure and enthusiasm they began to churn the ocean.**

**Комментарий:**

Three lines go together.

### Text 2

*hariḥ purastāj jagṛhe pūrvaṁ devās tato 'bhavan*

**Перевод:**

**Ajita first grasped the head portion of the snake and the** ***devatās*** **followed.**

**Комментарий:**

The devatās also did that *(tataḥ*).

### Text 3

*tan naicchan daitya-patayo mahā-puruṣa-ceṣṭitam*  
*na gṛhṇīmo vayaṁ puccham aher aṅgam amaṅgalam*  
*svādhyāya-śruta-sampannāḥ prakhyātā janma-karmabhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The leaders of the demons did not like this action of the Lord. “We shall not accept the tail of the snake, which is an inauspicious limb, since we have studied the Vedas and are renowned by birth and activities.”**

**Комментарий:**

They did not like the Lord’s action of grasping the head. They became angry at that.

### Text 5

*kṛta-sthāna-vibhāgās ta evaṁ kaśyapa-nandanāḥ*  
*mamanthuḥ paramaṁ yattā amṛtārthaṁ payo-nidhim*

**Перевод:**

**After adjusting how the snake was to be held, the sons of Kaśyapa, both** ***devatās*** **and demons, began churning the ocean of milk with great enthusiasm, desiring to obtain nectar.**

**Комментарий:**

The sixth and seventh manvantara devatās are considered in this verse as the same. Indra and others are generally different in the two manvantaras, according to greater or lesser puṇya. The living beings created at the beginning of the sixth manvantara (created by Dakṣa) were generally destroyed by the sudden destruction of the three worlds in the middle of that manvantara. The creation of beings after that (by Kaśyapa) continued into the seventh manvantara.

*cākṣuṣe tv antare prāpte prāk-sarge kāla-vidrute*  
*yaḥ sasarja prajā iṣṭāḥ sa dakṣo daiva-coditaḥ*

**He who, inspired by the Lord, created the desired progeny at the time of Cākṣuṣa Manvantara when the previous creation had been destroyed by time, is known as Dakṣa. (SB** [**4.30.49**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/30.49)**)**

They made great efforts (*paramam yattāḥ*).

### Text 6

*mathyamāne 'rṇave so 'drir anādhāro hy apo 'viśat*  
*dhriyamāṇo 'pi balibhir gauravāt pāṇḍu-nandana*

**Перевод:**

**O son of the Pāṇḍu dynasty! While the ocean was being churned, Mandara Mountain, though held by the strong** ***devatās*** **and demons, sank in the water because it had no support, because of its weight.**

**Комментарий:**

The earth at the bottom gradually became mud. It would have immediately sunk if there were no earth there at all.

### Text 8

*vilokya vighneśa-vidhiṁ tadeśvaro*  
*duranta-vīryo 'vitathābhisandhiḥ*  
*kṛtvā vapuḥ kacchapam adbhutaṁ mahat*  
*praviśya toyaṁ girim ujjahāra*

**Перевод:**

**Understanding the solution at the time of an obstacle, the unlimitedly powerful Lord, whose determination is infallible, took the astonishing shape of a tortoise, entered the water, and lifted the great Mandara Mountain.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord understood a possible solution to the obstacle (*vighneśa-vidhi*). *Vighneśa* does not mean it was created by the Lord, since he favored the devatās.

### Text 11

*tathāsurān āviśad āsureṇa*  
*rūpeṇa teṣāṁ bala-vīryam īrayan*  
*uddīpayan deva-gaṇāṁś ca viṣṇur*  
*daivena nāgendram abodha-rūpaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, Viṣṇu entered the demons as the quality of passion. He entered the** ***devatās*** **as the quality of goodness, and entered Vāsuki as the quality of ignorance to stimulate and increase their strength and courage.**

**Комментарий:**

He entered the demons with the śakti of rajas and entered the devatās with the śakti of sattva. He entered Vāsuki as the śakti of ignorance (*abodha-rūpaḥ*).

### Text 12

*upary agendraṁ giri-rāḍ ivānya*  
*ākramya hastena sahasra-bāhuḥ*  
*tasthau divi brahma-bhavendra-mukhyair*  
*abhiṣṭuvadbhiḥ sumano-'bhivṛṣṭaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Situated in another form, like another huge mountain with a thousand hands, the Lord caught Mandara Mountain with one hand and stood on top of it. From the sky, the associates of Viṣṇu showered flowers upon him.**

**Комментарий:**

Śiva was in Kailāsa and Indra and others were on the bank of the milk ocean. Their expansions were in the sky showering flowers.

### Text 14

*ahīndra-sāhasra-kaṭhora-dṛṅ-mukha-*  
*śvāsāgni-dhūmāhata-varcaso 'surāḥ*  
*pauloma-kāleya-balīlvalādayo*  
*davāgni-dagdhāḥ saralā ivābhavan*

**Перевод:**

**Their vitality destroyed by the smoke and fire emanating from the breathing of the thousand cruel mouths of Vāsuki, the demons headed by Pauloma, Kāleya, Bali and Ilvala became like pine trees burned by a forest fire.**

**Комментарий:**

Vāsuki and a thousand mouths. This is a numerical description, not related to his powers (Ananta has more power).

### Text 16

*mathyamānāt tathā sindhor devāsura-varūtha-paiḥ*  
*yadā sudhā na jāyeta nirmamanthājitaḥ svayam*

**Перевод:**

**When nectar did not appear from the ocean of milk from churning by the best of the** ***devatās*** **and demons, Ajita personally began to churn the ocean.**

**Комментарий:**

This and the next verse seem contrary to normal descriptions of the Lord.

### Text 19

*tad ugra-vegaṁ diśi diśy upary adho*  
*visarpad utsarpad asahyam aprati*  
*bhītāḥ prajā dudruvur aṅga seśvarā*  
*arakṣyamāṇāḥ śaraṇaṁ sadāśivam*

**Перевод:**

**O King! That most virulent poison spread in all directions as well as up and down. Seeing that intolerable poison with no cure, all the frightened** ***devatās*** **and demons, along with the Lord himself, took shelter of Śiva.**

**Комментарий:**

The poison spread gradually. If it had spread quickly everything would have been destroyed. The demons and devatās (*prajāḥ*) took shelter of Śiva since normal persons were incapable of crossing the ocean. Those who were situated high up got there by the power of Śiva who was also there. Those situated lower did not go there, fearing the poison would quickly spread upwards. They felt unprotected. The Lord was neutral at this time in order to show the power of Śiva.

### Text 21

*śrī-prajāpataya ūcuḥ*  
*deva-deva mahā-deva bhūtātman bhūta-bhāvana*  
*trāhi naḥ śaraṇāpannāṁs trailokya-dahanād viṣāt*

**Перевод:**

**The Prajāpatis said: O greatest of all** ***devatās*****, Mahādeva! Soul of all living entities! Protector of all beings! Deliver us, who have come here in surrender, from the poison which is burning the three worlds.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī says that the devatās glorify him through the exploits of Viṣṇu. This means that Viṣṇu is the root of his actions. The praises culminate in the names. They praised him in order to destroy the poison.

*mahimnā stūyamānā hi devā vīryeṇa vardhante*

**The devas, praised with greatness, increase in power.**

Śiva himself declares that Viṣṇu is his source.

*puṁsaḥ kṛpayato bhadre sarvātmā prīyate hariḥ*  
*prīte harau bhagavati prīye 'haṁ sacarācaraḥ*  
*tasmād idaṁ garaṁ bhuñje prajānāṁ svastir astu me*

**O gentle Bhavānī! When one performs benevolent activities for others, the Supreme Lord is very pleased. And when the Lord is pleased, I am also pleased, along with all other living creatures. Therefore, let me drink this poison. May all the living entities have auspicious existence because of me! (SB** [**8.7.40**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/7.40)**)**

*vayaṁ na tāta prabhavāma bhūmni*  
*yasmin pare 'nye 'py aja-jīva-kośāḥ*  
*bhavanti kāle na bhavanti hīdṛśāḥ*  
*sahasraśo yatra vayaṁ bhramāmaḥ*

**Śiva said: My dear son! We cannot compete with the Supreme Lord within whom the thousands of universes in which we wander in bewilderment arise and are destroyed with time. (SB** [**9.4.56**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-9/4.56)**)**

*ete vayaṁ yasya vaśe mahātmanaḥ*  
*sthitāḥ śakuntā iva sūtra-yantritāḥ*  
*mahān ahaṁ vaikṛta-tāmasendriyāḥ*  
*sṛjāma sarve yad-anugrahād idam*

**All of us—*****mahat-tattva,*** **the** ***devatās,*** **the elements and senses---are like birds tied by a thread. Under his control, by his mercy, we create this universe. (SB** [**5.17.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/17.22)**)**

Otherwise these statements would be contrary to the rest of the Bhāgavatam.

### Text 25

*tvaṁ śabda-yonir jagad-ādir ātmā*  
*prāṇendriya-dravya-guṇaḥ svabhāvaḥ*  
*kālaḥ kratuḥ satyam ṛtaṁ ca dharmas*  
*tvayy akṣaraṁ yat tri-vṛd-āmananti*

**Перевод:**

**You are the collection of all syllables, the origin of the universe,** ***ahaṅkāra,*** **the** ***prāṇas,*** **the senses the elements, the three** ***guṇas,*** ***svabhāva*****, time, determination, truth, pleasing words, and** ***dharma.*** **The wise determine that you are the syllable** ***oṁ.***

**Комментарий:**

*anyac ca sunṛtā vāṇī kavibhiḥ parikīrtitā*  
*karmasv asaṅgamaḥ śaucaṁ tyāgaḥ sannyāsa ucyate*

***Ṛtam*** **means to speak the truth in a pleasing way, as declared by great sages. Cleanliness is detachment from material actions and renunciation is the** ***sannyāsa*** **order of life. (SB** [**11.19.38**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/19.36)**)**

*daṇḍa-nyāsaḥ paraṁ dānaṁ kāma-tyāgas tapaḥ smṛtam*  
*svabhāva-vijayaḥ śauryaṁ satyaṁ ca sama-darśanam*

**Supreme charity is to give up aggression toward others and real austerity is renunciation of enjoyment. Real heroism is to conquer one’s natural tendency to enjoy material life and truth is seeing all beings as one’s self in happiness or distress. (SB** [**11.19.37**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/19.36)**)**

### Text 26

*agnir mukhaṁ te 'khila-devatātmā*  
*kṣitiṁ vidur loka-bhavāṅghri-paṅkajam*  
*kālaṁ gatiṁ te 'khila-devatātmano*  
*diśaś ca karṇau rasanaṁ jaleśam*

**Перевод:**

**O source of all planets! The wise know that fire, the essence of all** ***devatās*****, is your mouth, the earth is your lotus feet, time is the movement of you who are the source of all the** ***devatās*****, all the directions are your ears, and Varuṇa, master of the waters, is your tongue.**

**Комментарий:**

*Gatim* means activity.

### Text 30

*chāyā tv adharmormiṣu yair visargo*  
*netra-trayaṁ sattva-rajas-tamāṁsi*  
*sāṅkhyātmanaḥ śāstra-kṛtas tavekṣā*  
*chandomayo deva ṛṣiḥ purāṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O lord! Your manifestation is not seen in irreligion. The three modes of nature—goodness, passion and ignorance— which brings about various creations, are your three eyes. All the ancient Vedic literatures, composed of verses, are your glance. You, the soul of all beings, are the creator of scriptures.**

**Комментарий:**

You are not revealed (*chāyā*) in the waves of adharma.

### Text 31

*na te giri-trākhila-loka-pāla-*  
*viriñca-vaikuṇṭha-surendra-gamyam*  
*jyotiḥ paraṁ yatra rajas tamaś ca*  
*sattvaṁ na yad brahma nirasta-bhedam*

**Перевод:**

**O protector of mountains! Your supreme light known as Brahman which is devoid of** ***rajas, tamas*** **and** ***sattva*** **and devoid of distinctions is not known by the** ***devatās,*** **Viṣṇu or Indra.**

**Комментарий:**

The light of Śiva is the quality of Paramātmā situated there. With correct knowledge, it is impossible to say that he can have the light of Brahman.

*dyu-pataya eva te na yayur antam anantatayā*  
*tvam api yad-antarāṇḍa-nicayā nanu sāvaraṇāḥ*  
*kha iva rajāṁsi vānti vayasā saha yac chrutayas*  
*tvayi hi phalanty atan-nirasanena bhavan-nidhanāḥ*

**Because you are unlimited, neither the lords of heaven nor even you yourself can ever reach the end of your glories. The countless universes, each enveloped in its shell, are compelled by the wheel of time to wander within you, like particles of dust blowing about in the sky. The** ***śrutis,*** **following their method of eliminating everything separate from the Supreme, become successful by revealing you as their final conclusion. (SB** [**10.87.41**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/87.41)**)**

### Text 33

*ye tv ātma-rāma-gurubhir hṛdi cintitāṅghri-*  
*dvandvaṁ carantam umayā tapasābhitaptam*  
*katthanta ugra-paruṣaṁ nirataṁ śmaśāne*  
*te nūnam ūtim avidaṁs tava hāta-lajjāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Persons criticize you as a ferocious, violent person obsessed with the cremation grounds and engaged in flirting with Umā, though you are engaged in intense austerity and your feet are the object of meditation by the best of** ***ātmārāmas.*** **Devoid of shame, they do not know your pastimes.**

**Комментарий:**

This is the meaning. Do those who, without shame, praise you as fierce, rough in speech, and addicted to the crematorium, not know your pastimes (*ūtim*) covered by māyā? They do not.

You appear like this to persons whose eyes are covered by māyā. Thus they describe you wrongly. The most respected renounced devotees meditate on your feet. You move about with Umā. You have complete powers (*abhitaptam*) by the power of your great knowledge (*tapasā*).

### Text 35

*etat paraṁ prapaśyāmo na paraṁ te maheśvara*  
*mṛḍanāya hi lokasya vyaktis te 'vyakta-karmaṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O great lord! We do not understand your superior form. We only know that you, whose activities are hard to understand, make your appearance for the happiness of the people.**

**Комментарий:**

*Mṛdanāya* means “for happiness.”

### Text 20

*nūnaṁ tapo yasya na manyu-nirjayo*  
*jñānaṁ kvacit tac ca na saṅga-varjitam*  
*kaścin mahāṁs tasya na kāma-nirjayaḥ*  
*sa īśvaraḥ kiṁ parato vyapāśrayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Some have austerity, but have not conquered anger. Some have knowledge but have not given up attachment. Some have power but have not controlled lust. How can a person without shelter from enemies be considered the Supreme Lord?**

**Комментарий:**

Though others many have a small amount of good qualities that is mixed with faults.

**Prīti Sandarbha 119:**

By all qualities like austerity none is equal to the Lord. Their inferiority is well known. Thus it was said that some people desiring greatness pray for the Lord’s qualities. Even if they have a particle of qualities they have other faults. Thus they are extremely unequal to the Lord. For instance Durvāsā become angry at Ambarīṣa. Bṛhaspati and Śukrācārya in spite of their knowledge could not give up attachment to devatās and demons respectively. Brahmā and Soma manifested kāma. They all depend on others (*paratha vyapāśrayaḥ*), like Indra.

### Text 21

*dharmaḥ kvacit tatra na bhūta-sauhṛdaṁ*  
*tyāgaḥ kvacit tatra na mukti-kāraṇam*  
*vīryaṁ na puṁso 'sty aja-vega-niṣkṛtaṁ*  
*na hi dvitīyo guṇa-saṅga-varjitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Some have** ***dharma*****, but are not friendly to all beings. Some indulge in charity but are not interested in liberation. Some have strength but cannot prevent the attack of time. Some are devoid of attraction to material and spiritual qualities, but cannot be a suitable companion.**

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarbha 119:**

One may have dharma like Paraśurāma, but without compassion for others. One may be charitable like Śibi, but this is not a cause of liberation. One may have valor like Kārtavīrya, but cannot stop time. Though they have qualities, these qualities are made of māyā. The qualities are not beyond the guṇas. Because *(hi)* they are different from Mukunda (*dvitīyaḥ*) they are not free of association with the guṇas. Thus ātmārāmas like the Kumāras are also excluded since their śama and dama were material. Śiva is also excluded:

*śivaḥ śakti-yutaḥ śaśvat tri-liṅgo guṇa-saṁvṛtaḥ*  
*vaikārikas taijasaś ca tāmasaś cety ahaṁ tridhā*

**Śiva is always united with his personal energy, the material nature. Manifesting himself in three features in response to the entreaties of nature’s three modes, he thus embodies the threefold principle of material ego in goodness, passion and ignorance. (SB** [**10.88.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/88.3)**)**

*harir hi nirguṇaḥ sākṣāt puruṣaḥ prakṛteḥ paraḥ*  
*sa sarva-dṛg upadraṣṭā taṁ bhajan nirguṇo bhavet*

**The Lord, however, has no connection with the material modes. He is the Supreme Lord, the all-seeing eternal witness, who is transcendental to material nature. One who worships him becomes similarly free from the material modes. (SB** [**10.88.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/88.5)**)**

In another way Śiva is again excluded.

### Text 22

*kvacic cirāyur na hi śīla-maṅgalaṁ*  
*kvacit tad apy asti na vedyam āyuṣaḥ*  
*yatrobhayaṁ kutra ca so 'py amaṅgalaḥ*  
*sumaṅgalaḥ kaśca na kāṅkṣate hi mām*

**Перевод:**

**Some have long life but do not have piety or auspicious qualities. Some have both, but do not have long life. Some have long life and piety, but do not have auspicious qualities. The person with actual auspicious qualities does not care for me.**

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarbha 119:**

Some like Mārkaṇḍeya have long life, but cannot control their senses (*na śīla-maṅgalam).* The commentary says that they are not auspicious because they cannot control their senses. *Śīla-maṅgala* means attaining enjoyment, because of controlling the senses. Those who cannot attain enjoyment are persons who do not accept the name of the Lord since they cannot control their senses. Or, some like Maya-dānava have long lives but do not have auspiciousness as their nature (*śīla*), since they are demons by nature. Bali had śila-maṅgalam but could not know the length of his life. Śiva has auspiciousness by his nature and knows his life span since he is eternal. But externally he is inauspicious (*amaṅgalaḥ*) since he does actions in the crematorium. He who is most auspicious because of having infinite qualities beyond the guṇas, and because of lacking all faults, he who is the source of auspiciousness for everyone, does not desire me, giver of all wealth, having a form of the highest bliss. He is complete with his wealth of qualities in his svarūpa. Since he is controlled by prema, why could he not desire me? Anyone with auspiciousness would desire me for certain.

**Prīti Sandarbha 120:**

Here is the conclusion. In that form of the highest bliss, the svarūpa-śakti, having unlimited śakti with a wealth of qualities, exists in two forms: internally as his own unmanifested form and externally as the form of manifested Lakṣmī. This form of Lakṣmī is the presiding deity of all qualities. Since the Lord, full of the highest bliss in himself and full of all qualities, is complete by his very nature, he does not depend on this form situated separately either internally or externally, as would be the case of others. But because of his nature of being controlled by his devotees, he depends on Lakṣmī, full of prema.

### Text 23

*evaṁ vimṛśyāvyabhicāri-sad-guṇair*

**Комментарий:**

**Prīti Sandarbha 120:**

She accepted Mukunda as her husband. He is described. He was supreme *(varam*) with faultless (*sadbhiḥ*) qualities permanently fixed, without depending on anything else. His qualities were fixed in his svarūpa (*guṇāśrayam*). Thus these qualities were not material. He was necessarily full of all qualities (*apekṣitam)* since he is naturally the highest bliss. He was independent *(nirapekṣam*). Thus he was desired by her *(īpsitam*).

The life airs and victory by their nature do not have such power. They have meagre power. What is done by the life airs etc. to satisfy the root of everything nourishes all things at all times since they are not separate, since the Lord nourishes everything at all times. The Lord nourishes such victory. The Lord is the root of everything.

*niśumbha-śumbhayor devī bhadrakālī tarasvinī*

**Перевод:**

**Aparājita fought with Namuci, and the two Aśvinī-kumāra brothers fought with Vṛṣaparvā. The sun-god fought with the one hundred sons of Mahārāja Bali, headed by Bāṇa, and the moon-god fought with Rāhu. Vāyu fought with Puloma, and Śumbha and Niśumbha fought the supremely powerful Bhadra Kālī.**

### Text 32-34

*vṛṣākapis tu jambhena mahiṣeṇa vibhāvasuḥ*  
*ilvalaḥ saha vātāpir brahma-putrair arindama*  
*kāmadevena durmarṣa utkalo mātṛbhiḥ saha*  
*bṛhaspatiś cośanasā narakeṇa śanaiścaraḥ*  
*maruto nivātakavacaiḥ kāleyair vasavo 'marāḥ*  
*viśvedevās tu paulomai rudrāḥ krodhavaśaiḥ saha*

**Перевод:**

**O suppressor of enemies! Lord Śiva fought with Jambha, and Vibhāvasu fought with Mahiṣāsura. Ilvala, along with his brother Vātāpi, fought the sons of Lord Brahmā. Durmarṣa fought with Cupid, the demon Utkala with the Mātṛkā demigoddesses, Bṛhaspati (Jupiter) fought with Śukrācārya (Venus), and Śanaiścara (Saturn) fought with Narakāsura. The Maruts fought the Nivātakavacas, the Vasus fought the Kālakeya demons, the Viśvedevas fought the Pauloma demons, and the Rudras fought the Krodhavaśa demons.**

**Комментарий:**

Five verses describe the combatants. The sons of Brahmā, mainly the Prajāpatis fought with Ilvala. Cupid in this case was someone who had sided with Indra, not the original Kāmadeva since he had been turned to ashes previous to Skanda’s birth.

### Text 44

*tataḥ śūlaṁ tataḥ prāsaṁ tatas tomaram ṛṣṭayaḥ*  
*yad yac chastraṁ samādadyāt sarvaṁ tad acchinad vibhuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, one by one, Bali Mahārāja used a lance, a dart, a javelin, swords and other weapons, but whatever weapons he took up, Indra cut them to pieces.**

**Комментарий:**

*Ṛṣṭayaḥ* should be *riṣṭīḥ.*

### Text 47

*mahoragāḥ samutpetur dandaśūkāḥ savṛścikāḥ*  
*siṁha-vyāghra-varāhāś ca mardayanto mahā-gajāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Scorpions, large snakes and many other poisonous animals, as well as lions, tigers, boars and huge elephants, all began falling, crushing the enemy soldiers.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses describe the effects of the mountain.

### Text 49

*tato mahā-ghanā vyomni gambhīra-paruṣa-svanāḥ*  
*aṅgārān mumucur vātair āhatāḥ stanayitnavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In the sky, huge clouds, thundering with deep, cruel sound, harassed by strong winds, began to shower embers.**

**Комментарий:**

*Stanayitnavaḥ* is a species of cloud.

### Text 55

*tasmin praviṣṭe 'sura-kūṭa-karmajā*  
*māyā vineśur mahinā mahīyasaḥ*  
*svapno yathā hi pratibodha āgate*  
*hari-smṛtiḥ sarva-vipad-vimokṣaṇam*

**Перевод:**

**As a dream ceases when the dreamer awakens, the illusions created the demons were vanquished by the power of the Supreme Lord as soon as he entered the battlefield. Indeed, simply by remembrance of the Lord, one becomes free from all dangers.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī has *harer smṛtiḥ*, though *hari-smṛtiḥ* is the usual version.

## Chapter Eleven

### Text 4

*naṭavan mūḍha māyābhir māyeśān no jigīṣasi*  
*jitvā bālān nibaddhākṣān naṭo harati tad-dhanam*

**Перевод:**

**Indra said: O fool! You, like an actor, want to defeat us, who are masters of mystic powers by displaying your power. An actor sometimes binds the eyes of a child and takes away his possessions.**

**Комментарий:**

O fool! Do you, like an actor, desire to defeat us, masters of illusion, by your illusions, suitable to an actor? The actor tries to defeat persons who are ignorant of his power to make real things appear illusory by covering their eyes with his māyā. But you cannot to this with us who are masters of illusion. Thus you are a fool.

### Text 5

*ārurukṣanti māyābhir utsisṛpsanti ye divam*  
*tān dasyūn vidhunomy ajñān pūrvasmāc ca padād adhaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I send ignorant thieves, who want to ascend to Svarga or beyond and want to control Maharloka by creating illusions, to planets lower than their previous residence.**

**Комментарий:**

I send those who want to ascend to Svarga by strength of yoga (*māyābhiḥ*), not by surrender to the Lord, to lower planets.

### Text 6

*so 'haṁ durmāyinas te 'dya vajreṇa śata-parvaṇā*  
*śiro hariṣye mandātman ghaṭasva jñātibhiḥ saha*

**Перевод:**

**O fool! Today, with my thunderbolt, which has hundreds of sharp edges, I, shall sever your head from your body, in spite of your faulty tricks. Endeavor further with your relatives.**

**Комментарий:**

Be engaged (*ghaṭasva*) in the fight using your relatives.

### Text 8

*tad idaṁ kāla-raśanaṁ jagat paśyanti sūrayaḥ*  
*na hṛṣyanti na śocanti tatra yūyam apaṇḍitāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The sages understand that the universe is bound up by time and do not rejoice or lament. You** ***devatās*** **are ignorant of such things.**

**Комментарий:**

The universe has time as its rope (*raśanam*).

### Text 10

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*ity ākṣipya vibhuṁ vīro nārācair vīra-mardanaḥ*  
*ākarṇa-pūrṇairahanad ākṣepair āha taṁ punaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: After thus rebuking Indra, Bali, subduer of heroes, attacked Indra with the iron arrows drawn back to his ear on his bow. Then he again chastised Indra.**

**Комментарий:**

He drew the arrows back fully to the ear.

### Text 11

*evaṁ nirākṛto devo vairiṇā tathya-vādinā*  
*nāmṛṣyat tad-adhikṣepaṁ totrāhata iva dvipaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Defeated by the truthful words of the enemy, Indra, like an elephant struck with a rod, did not mind the chastisement.**

**Комментарий:**

He was attacked by the arrows but was further attacked by the insulting worlds. *Totra* is a bamboo goad.

### Text 15

*gadā-prahāra-vyathito bhṛśaṁ vihvalito gajaḥ*  
*jānubhyāṁ dharaṇīṁ spṛṣṭvā kaśmalaṁ paramaṁ yayau*

**Перевод:**

**In pain from being struck by the club, Indra's elephant became perturbed. It fell on its knees to the ground and became completely unconscious.**

**Комментарий:**

*Spṛṣṭvā kaśmalaṁ ca paraṁ yayau* is a widely accepted version.

### Text 23

*namuciḥ pañca-daśabhiḥ svarṇa-puṅkhair maheṣubhiḥ*  
*āhatya vyanadat saṅkhye satoya iva toyadaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Then Namuci, another demon, attacking Indra with fifteen very powerful golden-feathered arrows just as a cloud pours water, shouted thunderously on the battlefield.**

**Комментарий:**

Namuci had a desire to attack out of intolerance and lamentation.

### Text 30

*aśmasāramayaṁ śūlaṁ ghaṇṭāvad dhema-bhūṣaṇam*  
*pragṛhyābhyadravat kruddho hato 'sīti vitarjayan*  
*prāhiṇod deva-rājāya ninadan mṛga-rāḍ iva*

**Перевод:**

**Angry and roaring like a lion, the demon Namuci, taking up a steel spear which was bound with bells and decorated with ornaments of gold, rushed forward. Roaring loudly like a lion, "Now you are killed!” he threw the spear.**

**Комментарий:**

Three lines go together.

### Text 33

*tasmād indro 'bibhec chatror vajraḥ pratihato yataḥ*  
*kim idaṁ daiva-yogena bhūtaṁ loka-vimohanam*

**Перевод:**

**When Indra saw the thunderbolt was repelled, he became afraid. Had this happened by some supernatural force to bewilder people?**

**Комментарий:**

Another version has *bhūtaṁ lokaṁ vimohayat.*

### Text 34

*yena me pūrvam adrīṇāṁ pakṣa-cchedaḥ prajātyaye*  
*kṛto niviśatāṁ bhāraiḥ patattraiḥ patatāṁ bhuvi*

**Перевод:**

**Formerly, with this same thunderbolt I cut off the wings of mountains when mountains had wings and the mountains would fall on the ground with their weight to kill people.**

**Комментарий:**

The mountains would rise and then fall to earth, to kill people.

### Text 36

*so 'yaṁ pratihato vajro mayā mukto 'sure 'lpake*  
*nāhaṁ tad ādade daṇḍaṁ brahma-tejo 'py akāraṇam*

**Перевод:**

**That same thunderbolt which I released at this insignificant demon has been nullified. Being strong as a** ***brahmāstra,*** **I will not hold this useless stick any longer.**

**Комментарий:**

I will not hold this stick (*adade* means *ādāsye*, future).

### Text 39

*tāṁ daivīṁ giram ākarṇya maghavān susamāhitaḥ*  
*dhyāyan phenam athāpaśyad upāyam ubhayātmakam*

**Перевод:**

**After hearing the ominous voice, Indra, with great attention, began to meditate on how to kill the demon. He then saw that foam would be the means, for it is neither moist nor dry.**

**Комментарий:**

Foam is neither moist nor dry. Since it comes from water it is not dry. It can be perceived as dry since it is not liquid.

### Text 46

*ye 'vaśiṣṭā raṇe tasmin nāradānumatena te*  
*baliṁ vipannam ādāya astaṁ girim upāgaman*

**Перевод:**

**Following the order of Nārada, the demons who remained on the battlefield took defeated Bali to Astagiri, Sunset Mountain.**

**Комментарий:**

At that time they were near Astagiri.

### Text 47

*tatrāvinaṣṭāvayavān vidyamāna-śirodharān*  
*uśanā jīvayām āsa saṁjīvanyā sva-vidyayā*

**Перевод:**

**There, Śukrācārya brought to life all the demons who had not lost their heads and limbs by his knowledge of rejuvenation.**

**Комментарий:**

By the power of his mantras he brought the demons back to life who had not lost their limbs.

## Chapter Twelve

### Text 4

*śrī-mahādeva uvāca*  
*deva-deva jagad-vyāpiñ jagad-īśa jagan-maya*  
*sarveṣām api bhāvānāṁ tvam ātmā hetur īśvaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śiva said: O Lord who performs pastimes among the** ***devatās*****! O all-pervading Lord! O Lord of the universe! O Lord who is the universe! You are the giver of consciousness to all things in the universe and the cause of all things. Thus you alone are the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

First, Śiva indicates the position of the Lord as Bhagavān.

### Text 5

*ādy-antāv asya yan madhyam idam anyad ahaṁ bahiḥ*  
*yato 'vyayasya naitāni tat satyaṁ brahma cid bhavān*

**Перевод:**

**That from which visible things like birth, death and life within this universe arises and that from which invisible things like the conception of I and mine arise, is you alone, the absolute Brahman. You, the indestructible Brahman, do not have beginning, middle and end.**

**Комментарий:**

He indicates the Lord as Brahman.

### Text 6

*tavaiva caraṇāmbhojaṁ śreyas-kāmā nirāśiṣaḥ*  
*visṛjyobhayataḥ saṅgaṁ munayaḥ samupāsate*

**Перевод:**

**The sages who desire to achieve the highest goal,** ***bhakti,*** **and who are completely free from all material desires, giving up material attachment in this and next life, worship constantly only your lotus feet.**

**Комментарий:**

Appearing in this way, one should worship your lotus feet. The sages who desire auspiciousness, in the form of bhakti suitable to you, who are devoid of other desires, worship those feet.

### Text 7

*tvaṁ brahma pūrṇam amṛtaṁ viguṇaṁ viśokam*  
*ānanda-mātram avikāram ananyad anyat*  
*viśvasya hetur udaya-sthiti-saṁyamānām*  
*ātmeśvaraś ca tad-apekṣatayānapekṣaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**You are Brahman, complete, deathless, without material qualities, without lamentation, fully blissful, without change, the only existing entity, different from everything else, the cause of the universe, the cause of creation, maintenance and destruction, the give of results to the** ***jīvas*** **who are dependent on you. You are independent.**

**Комментарий:**

“How can I, one person be both Brahman and Bhagavān?” You are called Brahman, because your aṁśa is pure bliss (*ānanda-mātram*) etc. You are Bhagavān because you are endowed with śakti to create the universe. “I am dependent on the universe?” No. Since the universe is dependent on you, you are independent. The creation and destruction of the universe is simply a pastime, as a person in the material world plays.

### Text 8

*ekas tvam eva sad asad dvayam advayaṁ ca*  
*svarṇaṁ kṛtākṛtam iveha na vastu-bhedaḥ*  
*ajñānatas tvayi janair vihito vikalpo*  
*yasmād guṇa-vyatikaro nirupādhikasya*

**Перевод:**

**You are one alone, the universe, effect and cause. You are not different from the universe just as gold is not different from a gold ornament. But out of ignorance people have many conceptions about you, since you beyond the** ***guṇas*** **whereas the** **universe which is also you is full of misfortunes because of the** ***guṇas.***

**Комментарий:**

There is no difference in you, just as gold and a golden ornament are non-different. Just as gold does not change in its effect, you do not change in creation of the universe. Out of ignorance alone, people may think various things about you who are pure. Why? You are beyond the guṇas (*nirupādhikasya*). Though the guṇas, portions of the external energy, variegated like rays of the sun, produce misfortune, so you remain separate from the guṇas, as the rays do not exist without dependence on the sun. The example of gold is explained in this manner.

### Text 9

*tvāṁ brahma kecid avayanty uta dharmam eke*  
*eke paraṁ sad-asatoḥ puruṣaṁ pareśam*  
*anye 'vayanti nava-śakti-yutaṁ paraṁ tvāṁ*  
*kecin mahā-puruṣam avyayam ātma-tantram*

**Перевод:**

**Some think of you as Brahman, some think of you as** ***dharma*****, some think of you as the Lord of** ***prakṛti*** **and** ***puruṣa.*** **Others think of you as the Supreme Lord, superior to** ***māyā,*** **endowed with nine spiritual** ***śaktis.*** **Others think of you as the great** ***puruṣa,*** **independent and indestructible.**

**Комментарий:**

Who does not know you, since everything is your aṁśa?

### Text 11

*sa tvaṁ samīhitam adaḥ sthiti-janma-nāśaṁ*  
*bhūtehitaṁ ca jagato bhava-bandha-mokṣau*  
*vāyur yathā viśati khaṁ ca carācarākhyaṁ*  
*sarvaṁ tad-ātmakatayāvagamo 'varuntse*

**Перевод:**

**You, the Lord, knowing everything---your creation, the creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe, the actions of all beings, the method of bondage and liberation—accept everything, since you are one with everything, just as air enters ether and all bodies.**

**Комментарий:**

You know and accept everything *(avaruntse).*

### Text 15

*śrī-bhagavān uvāca*  
*kautūhalāya daityānāṁ yoṣid-veṣo mayā dhṛtaḥ*  
*paśyatā sura-kāryāṇi gate pīyūṣa-bhājane*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord said: When the demons took away the jug of nectar, I assumed the form of a woman to bewilder them, acting in the interest of the** ***devatās,*** **in order to take away the pot of nectar.**

**Комментарий:**

In order to cheat the demons (*kautūlahāya*) I took the form of a woman.

### Text 16

*tat te 'haṁ darśayiṣyāmi didṛkṣoḥ sura-sattama*  
*kāmināṁ bahu mantavyaṁ saṅkalpa-prabhavodayam*

**Перевод:**

**O best of the** ***devatās*****! I shall now show you my form that bestows a beautiful woman by worship and is appreciated by those who are lusty, since you desire to see it.**

**Комментарий:**

By worship, this form bestows a beautiful woman.

### Text 18

*tato dadarśopavane vara-striyaṁ*  
*vicitra-puṣpāruṇa-pallava-drume*  
*vikrīḍatīṁ kanduka-līlayā lasad-*  
*dukūla-paryasta-nitamba-mekhalām*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, in a nice forest nearby, full of trees with pink leaves and varieties of flowers, Lord Śiva saw a beautiful woman playing with a ball. She had a belt around her hips which could not be seen because of her bright cloth.**

**Комментарий:**

Four verses describe the woman.

### Text 26

*sā tam āyāntam ālokya vivastrā vrīḍitā bhṛśam*  
*nilīyamānā vṛkṣeṣu hasantī nānvatiṣṭhata*

**Перевод:**

**The woman, with her top piece slipping down, saw Lord Śiva approaching. Very shy, she ran and hid herself among the trees while smiling.**

**Комментарий:**

Her cloth fell off (*vivastrā*).

### Text 29-30

*sopagūḍhā bhagavatā kariṇā kariṇī yathā*  
*itas tataḥ prasarpantī viprakīrṇa-śiroruhā*  
*ātmānaṁ mocayitvāṅga surarṣabha-bhujāntarāt*  
*prādravat sā pṛthu-śroṇī māyā deva-vinirmitā*

**Перевод:**

**Embraced by Śiva like a female elephant embraced by a male, the woman, whose hair was scattered, squirmed. O King, this woman with large hips, who had appeared by** ***yogamāyā*** **of the Lord, freed herself from his embrace and ran away.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. The bewilderment of the form but not its female nature was produced by the Lord’s māyā. The form was like a female elephant embraced by a male elephant.

### Text 34

*sarit-saraḥsu śaileṣu vaneṣūpavaneṣu ca*  
*yatra kva cāsann ṛṣayas tatra sannihito haraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Following Mohinī, Lord Śiva went everywhere—near the shores of the rivers and lakes, near the mountains, near the forests, near the gardens, wherever sages lived.**

**Комментарий:**

The place of gold is distinguished from Śiva’s abode. The sages lived near the lakes and rivers.

### Text 36

*athāvagata-māhātmya ātmano jagad-ātmanaḥ*  
*aparijñeya-vīryasya na mene tad u hādbhutam*

**Перевод:**

**Śiva, understanding the greatness of the Lord of inestimable power, did not consider it astonishing since the Lord resides everywhere in the universe.**

**Комментарий:**

He did not consider this astonishing since the Lord was his own supreme form (*ātmanaḥ*) and the supreme form or antaryāmī of the universe.

### Text 37

*tam aviklavam avrīḍam ālakṣya madhusūdanaḥ*  
*uvāca parama-prīto bibhrat svāṁ pauruṣīṁ tanum*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Lord Śiva unagitated and unashamed, Lord Viṣṇu, in his male form, very pleased, spoke as follows.**

**Комментарий:**

Śiva was not agitated because of the appearance of that form, and was not ashamed by being defeated.

### Text 40

*seyaṁ guṇa-mayī māyā na tvām abhibhaviṣyati*  
*mayā sametā kālena kāla-rūpeṇa bhāgaśaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Umā, consisting of the** ***guṇas,*** **who carries out creation with me as the cause by my portion in the form of time, will not be able to bewilder you.**

**Комментарий:**

You were bewildered by this form, by my desire.

### Text 43

*ayi vyapaśyas tvam ajasya māyāṁ*  
*parasya puṁsaḥ para-devatāyāḥ*  
*ahaṁ kalānām ṛṣabho 'pi muhye*  
*yayāvaśo 'nye kim utāsvatantrāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O Goddess! You have now seen the illusory energy of the unborn Supreme Lord, by which I, the Lord’s principal expansion, have been bewildered. What then is to be said of others, who are fully under the control of** ***māyā*****?**

**Комментарий:**

You have seen the māyā of the Lord. This is completely different from Umā’s māyā, which arises from the Lord’s aṁśa.

### Text 44

*yaṁ mām apṛcchas tvam upetya yogāt*  
*samā-sahasrānta upārataṁ vai*  
*sa eṣa sākṣāt puruṣaḥ purāṇo*  
*na yatra kālo viśate na vedaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When I finished performing mystic** ***yoga*** **for one thousand years, you asked me upon whom I was meditating. Now, here is that Supreme Person whom time and the Vedas cannot approach.**

**Комментарий:**

The supreme position of the Lord is delineated in Bhagavat Sandarbha.

### Text 47

*asad-aviṣayam aṅghriṁ bhāva-gamyaṁ prapannān*  
*amṛtam amara-varyān āśayat sindhu-mathyam*  
*kapaṭa-yuvati-veṣo mohayan yaḥ surārīṁs*  
*tam aham upasṛtānāṁ kāma-pūraṁ nato 'smi*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord who fulfills the desires of the surrendered, who, taking the deceptive form of a young woman bewildered the demons and gave the nectar churned from the ocean to the best of the** ***devatās*** **who had surrendered to the feet of Lord, who is not understood by the materialists, and who is attained by devotion.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse shows the materialists, and devotees without desire and the devotees with desires.

## Chapter Thirteen

### Text 6

*atrāpi bhagavaj-janma kaśyapād aditer abhūt*  
*ādityānām avarajo viṣṇur vāmana-rūpa-dhṛk*

**Перевод:**

**In this Manvantara, the Supreme Lord appeared as the youngest of all the Ādityas, known as Vāmana, the dwarf, from Kaśyapa and Aditi.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī does not accept this verse, but it is seen in all editions.

### Text 14

*yo 'sau bhagavatā baddhaḥ prītena sutale punaḥ*  
*niveśito 'dhike svargād adhunāste sva-rāḍ iva*

**Перевод:**

**With great affection, the Lord bound Bali and then installed him in the kingdom of Sutala, which is greater than Svarga. Mahārāja Bali now resides on that planet like an emperor.**

**Комментарий:**

Bali was installed in Sutala.

## Chapter Fourteen

### Text 4

*catur-yugānte kālena grastāñ chruti-gaṇān yathā*  
*tapasā ṛṣayo 'paśyan yato dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**At the end of every four** ***yugas*****, the sages see by their austerity that the Vedas, by which eternal** ***dharma*** **is established, have been lost with time.**

**Комментарий:**

Vyāsa divides up the Vedas (śruti-*ganān*) (but they disappear at the end of Kali).

### Text 6

*pālayanti prajā-pālā yāvad antaṁ vibhāgaśaḥ*  
*yajña-bhāga-bhujo devā ye ca tatrānvitāś ca taiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The sons of Manu, and their descendants, protect** ***dharma*** **till the end of the Manvantara. The** ***devatās*****, who share the results of sacrifice, also protect** ***dharma*** **along with them*****.***

**Комментарий:**

The normal version is *tatravitāś ca taiḥ* but Śrīdhara Svāmī has *yatrānvitāś ca taiḥ.*

### Text 10

*stūyamāno janair ebhir māyayā nāma-rūpayā*  
*vimohitātmabhir nānā- darśanair na ca dṛśyate*

**Перевод:**

**Though the Lord is sought by persons with various philosophies, whose minds are bewildered by** ***māyā*** **with names and forms, his intentions cannot be understood.**

**Комментарий:**

Though described, he cannot be understood by people whose minds are bewildered by māyā.

### Text 11

*etat kalpa-vikalpasya pramāṇaṁ parikīrtitam*  
*yatra manvantarāṇy āhuś caturdaśa purāvidaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**I have given the evidence for the divisions of a day of Brahmā in which previous scholars describe fourteen Manvantaras.**

**Комментарий:**

I have given evidence of one (*vikalpasya*) among the many types of divisions in the day (*kalpa*) of Brahmā.

## Chapter Fifteen

### Text 10-11

*tulyaiśvarya-bala-śrībhiḥ sva-yūthair daitya-yūthapaiḥ*  
*pibadbhir iva khaṁ dṛgbhir dahadbhiḥ paridhīn iva*  
*vṛto vikarṣan mahatīm āsurīṁ dhvajinīṁ vibhuḥ*  
*yayāv indra-purīṁ svṛddhāṁ kampayann iva rodasī*

**Перевод:**

**When he assembled with his soldiers and the demon chiefs, who were equal to him in strength, wealth and beauty, they appeared as if they would swallow the sky and burn all directions with their vision. After gathering the demonic soldiers, Bali Mahārāja departed for the prosperous city of Indra, making the entire surface of the world tremble.**

**Комментарий:**

Thirteen verses describe the city of Indra.

### Text 22

*yāṁ na vrajanty adharmiṣṭhāḥ khalā bhūta-druhaḥ śaṭhāḥ*  
*māninaḥ kāmino lubdhā ebhir hīnā vrajanti yat*

**Перевод:**

**No one who was sinful, envious, violent toward other living entities, cunning, falsely proud, lusty or greedy could enter that city. The people who lived there were all devoid of these faults.**

**Комментарий:**

None of the faults of this world were present there.

### Text 31

*eṣa vipra-balodarkaḥ sampraty ūrjita-vikramaḥ*  
*teṣām evāpamānena sānubandho vinaṅkṣyati*

**Перевод:**

**Bali, by the result of the power of** ***brāhmaṇas*****, had become powerful at this moment. When he later insults the** ***brāhmaṇas,*** **he will be vanquished along with his assistants.**

**Комментарий:**

Since there is no fault in disobeying the words of conventional gurus in order to favor the Lord, it is called an insult from the conventional point of view.

*gurur na sa syāt sva-jano na sa syāt*  
*pitā na sa syāj jananī na sā syāt*  
*daivaṁ na tat syān na patiś ca sa syān*  
*na mocayed yaḥ samupeta-mṛtyum*

**He who cannot deliver a person in** ***saṁsāra*** **is not a** ***guru*****, a relative, a father, mother,** ***devatā*** **or husband. (SB** [**5.5.18**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/5.18)**)**

Being favorable to the Lord was not characteristic of the demons. Rejecting conventional happiness which is actually suffering, and being given the highest happiness of bhakti, he would attain the highest quality. This was Bali’s thought. This is explained in the Fifth Canto where Bali says:

*nūnaṁ batāyaṁ bhagavān artheṣu na niṣṇāto yo 'sāv indro yasya sacivo mantrāya vṛta ekāntato bṛhaspatis tam atihāya svayam upendreṇātmānam ayācatātmanaś cāśiṣo no eva tad-dāsyam ati-gambhīra-vayasaḥ kālasya manvantara-parivṛttaṁ kiyal loka-trayam idam.*

**Indeed, Indra, who employed Bṛhaspati as his exclusive assistant and minister, is not experienced in affairs. Neglecting the Lord, he instead asked me for the three worlds, material happiness, using the Lord, and not service to the Lord. What value are the three worlds, which change every Manvantara by the great speed of time? (SB** [**5.24.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-5/24.24)**)**

The Lord lived in Sutala as Bali’s door keeper. What is the value of ruling Svarga where Svarga, Pātāla and the Indra change every manvantara?

## Chapter Sixteen

### Text 4

**O most excellent woman! I wonder whether anything inauspicious has now taken place in regard to** ***dharma,*** **the** ***brāhmaṇas*** **or the people in general, who are subject to the whims of death.**

*Api* indicates a question. Has not something inauspicious happened to the brāhmaṇas, to dharma or to the people, who are subject to the whims of death?

### Text 11

*śrī-aditir uvāca*

**Aditi said: O** ***brāhmaṇa*****! All is well with the** ***brāhmaṇas*****, the cows, religion and the welfare of other people. O master of the house! The house is the best place for nourishing** ***dharma, artha*** **and** ***kāma*****.**

**Комментарий:**

Dvija represents other people as well. As well, he asks about the cows.

### Text 12

*agnayo 'tithayo bhṛtyā bhikṣavo ye ca lipsavaḥ*  
*sarvaṁ bhagavato brahmann anudhyānān na riṣyati*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***brāhmaṇa*****! The fires, guests, servants and beggars are all being properly cared for by me. Because I always think of you, there is no possibility that any of the religious principles will be neglected.**

**Комментарий:**

Though nothing was neglected, she grieved for her son because of offense.

### Text 14

*tavaiva mārīca manaḥ-śarīrajāḥ*  
*prajā imāḥ sattva-rajas-tamo-juṣaḥ*  
*samo bhavāṁs tāsv asurādiṣu prabho*  
*tathāpi bhaktaṁ bhajate maheśvaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O son of Marīci! All the demons and** ***devatās*****, possessing** ***sattva, rajas*****, or** ***tamas,*** **are born either from your body or from your mind. O master! You are equal to the all, but the Supreme Lord protects his devotee.**

**Комментарий:**

Addressing him as Marīci’s son, she indicates he has the power of his father.

### Text 15

*tasmād īśa bhajantyā me śreyaś cintaya suvrata*  
*hṛta-śriyo hṛta-sthānān sapatnaiḥ pāhi naḥ prabho*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, O Lord, think of the welfare of your servant. Man of vows! O master! Please protect our places whose wealth has been stolen by the demons.**

**Комментарий:**

“They are wealthy, not devotees.” She responds with this verse.

### Text 18

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*evam abhyarthito 'dityā kas tām āha smayann iva*  
*aho māyā-balaṁ viṣṇoḥ sneha-baddham idaṁ jagat*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: When Kaśyapa Muni was thus requested by Aditi, he slightly smiled and spoke. "Alas, the entire world is bound by affection, the strength of Viṣṇu’s** ***māyā."***

**Комментарий:**

*Kaḥ* means *kaśyapaḥ.*

### Text 19

*kva deho bhautiko 'nātmā kva cātmā prakṛteḥ paraḥ*  
*kasya ke pati-putrādyā moha eva hi kāraṇam*

**Перевод:**

**The body made of elements is not the soul. The soul is different from the body made of matter. Who is the husband and who are the sons? Who do they belong to? Illusion is the cause of this.**

**Комментарий:**

The ātmā is eternal. Affection for the body which is not the ātmā, thinking of husband and sons, and committing violence to others, is not proper. He speaks knowledge of tattva to her. Ātmā refers to the jīva.

### Text 20

*upatiṣṭhasva puruṣaṁ bhagavantaṁ janārdanam*  
*sarva-bhūta-guhā-vāsaṁ vāsudevaṁ jagad-gurum*

**Перевод:**

**Worship the Supreme Lord, who will beg from his devotee, who will inspire Bali from the heart, who will appear in you when you are purified, and who will benefit the whole universe.**

**Комментарий:**

Since she did not pay attention he speaks again, indicating worship of the Lord in two verses. *Upatiṣṭhasva* means “worship.”

### Text 22

*śrī-aditir uvāca*  
*kenāhaṁ vidhinā brahmann upasthāsye jagat-patim*  
*yathā me satya-saṅkalpo vidadhyāt sa manoratham*

**Перевод:**

**Aditi said: O** ***brāhmaṇa*****! Tell me the rules by which I may worship the master of the world, so that the Lord, who fulfills desires, will fulfill all my desires.**

**Комментарий:**

*Upasthāsye* means “I should worship.”

### Text 23

*ādiśa tvaṁ dvija-śreṣṭha vidhiṁ tad-upadhāvanam*  
*āśu tuṣyati me devaḥ sīdantyāḥ saha putrakaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O best of the** ***brāhmaṇas*****! Kindly instruct me in the method of worshiping the Lord, so that he will be quickly satisfied with me, now lamenting with my sons.**

**Комментарий:**

Teach the method of worship of the Lord (*tad-upadhāvanam*) by which he will be pleased.

### Text 24

*śrī-kaśyapa uvāca*  
*etan me bhagavān pṛṣṭaḥ prajā-kāmasya padmajaḥ*  
*yad āha te pravakṣyāmi vrataṁ keśava-toṣaṇam*

**Перевод:**

**Kaśyapa said: I shall explain to you the process by which Keśava is satisfied, which Brahmā instructed to me when I inquired from him with a desire to produce progeny.**

**Комментарий:**

I will explain to you the vrata which Brahmā spoke to me when I asked him, desiring progeny.

### Text 41

*niveditaṁ tad-bhaktāya dadyād bhuñjīta vā svayam*  
*dattvācamanam arcitvā tāmbūlaṁ ca nivedayet*

**Перевод:**

**One should give this food offering to a Vaiṣṇava and take some oneself. After offering the Lord** ***ācamana*****, one should offer the Lord betel nut.**

**Комментарий:**

You should offer the prasāda to a devotee and also you should also eat it.

### Text 43

*kṛtvā śirasi tac-cheṣāṁ devam udvāsayet tataḥ*  
*dvy-avarān bhojayed viprān pāyasena yathocitam*

**Перевод:**

**After touching to one's head all the flowers and water offered to the deity, one should tell the Lord to leave. Then one should feed more than two** ***brāhmaṇas*** **with sweet rice.**

**Комментарий:**

One should feed brāhmaṇas or at least two brāhmaṇas, or offer to brāhmaṇas among whom two are lesser in status, such as women.

## Chapter Seventeen

### Text 7

*prītyā śanair gadgadayā girā hariṁ*  
*tuṣṭāva sā devy aditiḥ kurūdvaha*  
*udvīkṣatī sā pibatīva cakṣuṣā*  
*ramā-patiṁ yajña-patiṁ jagat-patim*

**Перевод:**

**O Mahārāja Parīkṣit! Aditi then began offering her prayers to the Supreme Lord in a faltering voice with great love. She appeared as though drinking through her eyes the Supreme Lord, who is the husband of the goddess of fortune, the enjoyer of all sacrificial ceremonies, and the master of the universe.**

**Комментарий:**

When she looked at the Lord, it appeared she was drinking him through her eyes.

### Text 9

*viśvāya viśva-bhavana-sthiti-saṁyamāya*  
*svairaṁ gṛhīta-puru-śakti-guṇāya bhūmne*  
*sva-sthāya śaśvad-upabṛṁhita-pūrṇa-bodha-*  
*vyāpāditātma-tamase haraye namas te*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to you, who are the universe, and its creation, maintenance and destruction, who accept the material** ***guṇas.*** **I offer respects to you, the great one, situated in your devotees. You are eternally filled with complete knowledge and devoid of ignorance.**

**Комментарий:**

I offer respects to you, the form of the universe, called so because the cause and effect (universe) are non-different. You cause the appearance, maintenance and destruction of the universe, but it is not your svarūpa. Not only do you give śakti to the universe, but you possess without limit (*svairam*) full śaktis such as Śrī and qualities that arise from them such as aiśvarya. Thus you are complete in all ways (*bhūmne*). Thus you are eternally in one form (*svasthāya*). And you destroy (*vyāpādita*) the ignorance of the jīva (*ātma*) by realization (*pūrna-bodha*) which increases constantly in the hearts of the devotees (*śaśvad-upabṛḥmita*) I offer respects to you, who are called Hari because you destroy all suffering and are all attractive.

### Text 17

*athāpy upāyo mama devi cintyaḥ*  
*santoṣitasya vrata-caryayā te*  
*mamārcanaṁ nārhati gantum anyathā*  
*śraddhānurūpaṁ phala-hetukatvāt*

**Перевод:**

**Since I am pleased by your vow, I am thinking of some means. Worship of me according to one’s faith should never go in vain because it should give a result.**

**Комментарий:**

Worship of me should give results according to the śraddha and not otherwise. It is suitable to accept everything which is made the object of perception by the giver of results. Why? The worship, steady bhakti as a cause, should give results (*hetukatvāt*).

### Text 18

*tvayārcitaś cāham apatya-guptaye*  
*payo-vratenānuguṇaṁ samīḍitaḥ*  
*svāṁśena putratvam upetya te sutān*  
*goptāsmi mārīca-tapasy adhiṣṭhitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**You have praised and worshiped me properly by performing the** ***payo-vrata*** **for the sake of protecting your sons. Situated in Kaśyapa Muni's austerities, becoming your son by my portion, I shall protect your other sons.**

**Комментарий:**

“That being so, will defeating enemies in battle, my desire, be accomplished by you, since the worship has been proper.” I have been worshipped with the desire to protect your sons, but now you have a desire to attain me. I have been worshipped and praised appropriately (*anuguṇam*).

### Text 23

*so 'dityāṁ vīryam ādhatta tapasā cira-sambhṛtam*  
*samāhita-manā rājan dāruṇy agniṁ yathānilaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! As the wind gives rise to fire in wood, Kaśyapa Muni, absorbed in the form of the Lord, transferred the potent form of the Lord, held within him for a long time by his austerity, to Aditi.**

**Комментарий:**

He transferred the form of the Lord (*vīryam*) to Aditi.

### Text 24

*aditer dhiṣṭhitaṁ garbhaṁ bhagavantaṁ sanātanam*  
*hiraṇyagarbho vijñāya samīḍe guhya-nāmabhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When Brahmā understood that the eternal Supreme Lord was now within the womb of Aditi, he offered prayers to the Lord by reciting confidential names of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The secret words are the names of the Lord.

*prāyeṇa veda tad idaṁ na mahājano 'yaṁ*  
*devyā vimohita-matir bata māyayālam*  
*trayyāṁ jaḍī-kṛta-matir madhu-puṣpitāyāṁ*  
*vaitānike mahati karmaṇi yujyamānaḥ*

**Other persons, bewildered in intelligence by very powerful** ***māyā,*** **having material intelligence, engaging in great rituals with elaborate arrangements mentioned in the Vedas with flowery language, generally do not know this** ***dharma.*** **(SB** [**6.3.25**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-6/3.25)**)**

## Chapter Eighteen

Three verses go together describing the Lord.

## Chapter Nineteen

### Text 11

*sa tan-niketaṁ parimṛśya śūnyam*  
*apaśyamānaḥ kupito nanāda*  
*kṣmāṁ dyāṁ diśaḥ khaṁ vivarān samudrān*  
*viṣṇuṁ vicinvan na dadarśa vīraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Searching for Viṣṇu and finding him absent from his abode, Hiraṇyakaśipu became angry and screamed loudly. The hero searched the earth, Svarga, all directions,** ***antarikṣa*****, the caves and ocean, but did not see Viṣṇu anywhere.**

**Комментарий:**

*Niketam* here means the Lord’s special place.

### Text 12

*apaśyann iti hovāca mayānviṣṭam idaṁ jagat*  
*bhrātṛ-hā me gato nūnaṁ yato nāvartate pumān*

**Перевод:**

**Unable to see him, Hiraṇyakaśipu said, "I have searched the entire for Viṣṇu, who has killed my brother. Therefore, he must have died."**

**Комментарий:**

He has gone to the place from which man does not return. That means Vaikuṇṭha.

### Text 18

*śrī-balir uvāca*  
*aho brāhmaṇa-dāyāda vācas te vṛddha-sammatāḥ*  
*tvaṁ bālo bāliśa-matiḥ svārthaṁ praty abudho yathā*

**Bali Mahārāja said: O son of a** ***brāhmaṇa*****! Your words are agreeable to the learned and elderly persons. Nonetheless, you are a boy with inexperienced intelligence. You are not aware of your self-interest.**

O son of a brāhmaṇa! There words are not inappropriate in normal dealings. However from the spiritual point of view, they express the Lord’s mercy.

### Text 20

*na pumān mām upavrajya bhūyo yācitum arhati*  
*tasmād vṛttikarīṁ bhūmiṁ vaṭo kāmaṁ pratīccha me*

**Перевод:**

**O small boy! One who approaches me to beg something should not have to ask again. Therefore, ask from me as much land as will suffice to maintain you according to your needs.**

**Комментарий:**

Ask sufficiently (*kāmam*) so that you can maintain yourself.

### Text 41

*parāg riktam apūrṇaṁ vā akṣaraṁ yat tad om iti*  
*yat kiñcid om iti brūyāt tena ricyeta vai pumān*  
*bhikṣave sarvam oṁ kurvan nālaṁ kāmena cātmane*

**Перевод:**

***Oṁ*** **indicates separation from wealth, being devoid of wealth. He who utters** ***oṁ*** **and agrees to give becomes lesser in wealth by that. If he decides to give everything to the person who requests, there will not be enough left for his own enjoyment.**

**Комментарий:**

He explains the word oṁ.

### Text 43

*strīṣu narma-vivāhe ca vṛtty-arthe prāṇa-saṅkaṭe*  
*go-brāhmaṇārthe hiṁsāyāṁ nānṛtaṁ syāj jugupsitam*

**Перевод:**

**In flattering a woman, in joking, in order to get married, in earning one's livelihood, in dangerous circumstances, in protecting cows and brahminical culture, or in protecting a person from an enemy's hand, falsity is never condemned.**

**Комментарий:**

Though lying is famous for low people, and explained when describing what is acceptable and unacceptable, sometimes there are opposite results. That is explained. In four situations, one can lie out of dharma for one’s own benefit. One can lie to protect one’s own life or for protecting cows or brāhmaṇas, when there is destruction of their functions. This also includes devotees. Also when there is violence to others lying is permitted. In those cases lying is not condemned. When truth produces violence, it is condemned.

## Chapter Twenty

### Text 9

*sulabhā yudhi viprarṣe hy anivṛttās tanu-tyajaḥ*  
*na tathā tīrtha āyāte śraddhayā ye dhana-tyajaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O best of the** ***brāhmaṇas*****! Many men have laid down their lives on the battlefield, fearless in fighting, but rarely has one obtained the chance to give his accumulated wealth when there is an opportunity for giving.**

**Комментарий:**

Should one give up one’s life in battle? Giving up life in battle is common. Giving up wealth with faith when there is a suitable candidate is rare. Life is essential in comparison to wealth, but out of illusion people considering wealth essential for everything.

### Text 12

*yadyapy asāv adharmeṇa māṁ badhnīyād anāgasam*  
*tathāpy enaṁ na hiṁsiṣye bhītaṁ brahma-tanuṁ ripum*

**Перевод:**

**Because he has assumed the form of a** ***brāhmaṇa*** **full of fear, even if he irreligiously arrests me, though I am sinless, I shall not retaliate, although he is my enemy.**

**Комментарий:**

He pacifies Śukra in order to get his permission, in two verses. Śrīdhara Svāmī shows the actual meaning of the words. After praying to have the Lord’s feet on his head, he developed real bhakti. This was a conclusion to the bhakti-rasābhāsa.

### Text 13

*eṣa vā uttamaśloko na jihāsati yad yaśaḥ*  
*hatvā maināṁ hared yuddhe śayīta nihato mayā*

**Перевод:**

**Since he is Viṣṇu of imperishable fame, he will not give up that fame. Killing me in battle, he will take the land. I can never kill him.**

**Комментарий:**

He shows Viṣṇu’s fearful nature.

### Text 21

*tad vāmanaṁ rūpam avardhatādbhutaṁ*  
*harer anantasya guṇa-trayātmakam*  
*bhūḥ khaṁ diśo dyaur vivarāḥ payodhayas*  
*tiryaṅ-nṛ-devā ṛṣayo yad-āsata*

**Перевод:**

**The form of the dwarf, the unlimited Lord, containing within himself the whole material realm, within whom existed the earth, ether, the directions, Svarga,** ***antarīkṣa*****, the oceans, animals, humans,** ***devatās*** **and sages, then increased astonishingly.**

**Комментарий:**

That form expanded to include the three guṇas (*tri-guṇātmakam*).

### Text 22

*kāye balis tasya mahā-vibhūteḥ*  
*sahartvig-ācārya-sadasya etat*  
*dadarśa viśvaṁ tri-guṇaṁ guṇātmake*  
*bhūtendriyārthāśaya-jīva-yuktam*

**Перевод:**

**Bali Mahārāja, along with all the priests,** ***ācāryas*** **and members of the assembly, observed within the supreme Lord endowed with six qualities the universe composed of the three** ***guṇas*****--including all gross material elements, the senses, the sense objects, the** ***antaḥkaraṇa*** **and the** ***jīvas.***

**Комментарий:**

Bali observed the universe within the Lord endowed with six qualities (*guṇātmake*).

### Text 23

*rasām acaṣṭāṅghri-tale 'tha pādayor*  
*mahīṁ mahīdhrān puruṣasya jaṅghayoḥ*  
*patattriṇo jānuni viśva-mūrter*  
*ūrvor gaṇaṁ mārutam indrasenaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Bali Mahārāja, who was occupying the seat of Indra, saw the surface of the earth on the soles of the feet of the Lord's universal form. He saw on the Lord's feet objects situated on the earth, on his calves he saw all the mountains, on his knees he saw the various birds, and on his thighs he saw the varieties of winds.**

**Комментарий:**

Indrasena is a name of Bali, as mentioned in Tenth Canto:

*sa indraseno bhagavat-padāmbujaṁ*  
*bibhran muhuḥ prema-vibhinnayā dhiyā*  
*uvāca hānanda-jalākulekṣaṇaḥ*  
*prahṛṣṭa-romā nṛpa gadgadākṣaram*

**Taking hold of the Lords’ lotus feet again and again, Bali, the conqueror of Indra’s army, spoke from his heart, which was melting out of his intense love. O King, as tears of ecstasy filled his eyes and the hair on his limbs stood on end, he began to speak with faltering words. (SB** [**10.85.38**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/85.38)**)**

### Text 25-29

*hṛdy aṅga dharmaṁ stanayor murārer*  
*ṛtaṁ ca satyaṁ ca manasy athendum*  
*śriyaṁ ca vakṣasy aravinda-hastāṁ*  
*kaṇṭhe ca sāmāni samasta-rephān*  
*indra-pradhānān amarān bhujeṣu*  
*tat-karṇayoḥ kakubho dyauś ca mūrdhni*  
*keśeṣu meghāñ chvasanaṁ nāsikāyām*  
*akṣṇoś ca sūryaṁ vadane ca vahnim*  
*vāṇyāṁ ca chandāṁsi rase jaleśaṁ*  
*bhruvor niṣedhaṁ ca vidhiṁ ca pakṣmasu*  
*ahaś ca rātriṁ ca parasya puṁso*  
*manyuṁ lalāṭe 'dhara eva lobham*  
*sparśe ca kāmaṁ nṛpa retasāmbhaḥ*  
*pṛṣṭhe tv adharmaṁ kramaṇeṣu yajñam*  
*chāyāsu mṛtyuṁ hasite ca māyāṁ*  
*tanū-ruheṣv oṣadhi-jātayaś ca*  
*nadīś ca nāḍīṣu śilā nakheṣu*  
*buddhāv ajaṁ deva-gaṇān ṛṣīṁś ca*  
*prāṇeṣu gātre sthira-jaṅgamāni*  
*sarvāṇi bhūtāni dadarśa vīraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! On the heart of Murāri he saw religion; on the chest, both pleasing words and truthfulness; in his mind, the moon; on his chest, the goddess of fortune, with a lotus flower in her hand; on his neck, all the Vedas and all syllables; on his arms, all the** ***devatās*****, headed by Indra; in both ears, all the directions; on his head, Svarga; on his hair, the clouds; in his nostrils, the wind; on his eyes, the sun; and in his mouth, fire. In his words were all the Vedic verses, on his tongue was Varuṇa, on his eyebrows were the rules and prohibitions, and on the opening and closing of his eyelids were day and night. On his forehead was anger, and on his lips was greed. O King! In his touch were lusty desires, in his semen were all the waters, on his back was irreligion, and in his wonderful activities was the fire of sacrifice. On his shadow was death, in his smile was the illusory energy, and on the hairs of his body were all the drugs and herbs. In his veins were all the rivers, on his nails were all the stones, in his intelligence were Lord Brahmā, the** ***devatās*** **and the sages. Throughout his entire body and senses were all living entities, moving and stationary.**

**Комментарий:**

Five verses describe the form. The word *eva* should be added to the other words also. On the lower lip (*adhare eva*) was greed. When the lower lip is very visible it indicates greed. In his shadows or indications of unfavorable moods (*chāyāsu)* was death. In his amusing smile was māyā. Māyā took shelter of his smile.

### Text 32-33

*sunanda-mukhyā upatasthur īśaṁ*

The Lord, who wore a brilliant crown, bracelets, and earrings that resembled fish, with Śrīvatsa and Kaustubha on his chest, wearing a yellow garment covered by a belt, with garland surrounded by bees in the shape of the garland, shone brilliantly. O King! The Supreme Lord, whose activities are wonderful, covered the earth belonging to Bali with one footstep, the sky with his body, and all directions with his arms.**

**Комментарий:**

By kicking up his right foot, he covered the earth (*padaikena*).

### Text 34

*padaṁ dvitīyaṁ kramatas triviṣṭapaṁ*  
*na vai tṛtīyāya tadīyam aṇv api*

**Перевод:**

**As the Lord took his second step, he covered the heavenly planets. And not even a spot remained for the third step, for the Lord's foot extended higher and higher, beyond Maharloka, Janaloka, Tapoloka and even Satyaloka.**

**Комментарий:**

Taking his second step, he covered the heavenly planets (*triviṣṭapam*). There was no space at all for his third step (*tadīyam*). His body covered the sky. There was not space for his request for three steps of land. This is not improper, since the word “land” also includes everything above the land.

## Chapter Twenty-one

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*satyaṁ samīkṣyābja-bhavo nakhendubhir*  
*hata-svadhāma-dyutir āvṛto 'bhyagāt*  
*marīci-miśrā ṛṣayo bṛhad-vratāḥ*  
*sanandanādyā nara-deva yoginaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: O King! After seeing his planet touched by Vāmana’s foot, Lord Brahmā, his abode, his effulgence and his own form became dim by the light from the effulgence of Vāmana’s moon-like toe nails. He offered respects to the Lord and the great sages headed by Marīci and by** ***yogīs*** **like Sanandana also offered respects to the Lord.**

### Text 2-3

*vedopavedā niyamā yamānvitās*  
*tarketihāsāṅga-purāṇa-saṁhitāḥ*  
*ye cāpare yoga-samīra-dīpita-*  
*jñānāgninā randhita-karma-kalmaṣāḥ*  
*vavandire yat-smaraṇānubhāvataḥ*  
*svāyambhuvaṁ dhāma gatā akarmakam*  
*athāṅghraye pronnamitāya viṣṇor*  
*upāharat padma-bhavo 'rhaṇodakam*  
*samarcya bhaktyābhyagṛṇāc chuci-śravā*  
*yan-nābhi-paṅkeruha-sambhavaḥ svayam*

**Перевод:**

**The Vedas, Upavedas, Nyāya scriptures, histories, the** ***aṅgas*** **of the Vedas, Purāṇas, Saṁhitās, along with** ***yamas*** **and** ***niyamas,*** **and persons purified of sinful acts by the fire of knowledge which was ignited by the air of** ***yoga,*** **persons who had attained Brahmaloka (which is devoid of** ***karma-kāṇḍa)*** **by the power of remembering the Lord’s feet, offered respects to the Lord. Brahmā offered foot water to the Lord’s raised feet. Brahmā of spotless fame, who appeared from the Lord’s navel, then worshipped the Lord with devotion and praised him.**

**Комментарий:**

Three verses go together*. Saṁhitāḥ* refers to works like Brahma-saṁhitā. Those who had gone to Brahma-loka, being free of bondage of karma (*akarmakam*), worshipped the Lord’s feet. These persons thus attain liberation along with Brahmā.

### Text 4

*dhātuḥ kamaṇḍalu-jalaṁ tad urukramasya*  
*pādāvanejana-pavitratayā narendra*  
*svardhuny abhūn nabhasi sā patatī nimārṣṭi*  
*loka-trayaṁ bhagavato viśadeva kīrtiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! The water from Lord Brahmā's pot, purified by washing the lotus feet of Vāmanadeva, became the water of the Gaṅgā. Falling down from the sky, it purifies the three worlds like the pure fame of the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

In the Fifth Canto, in the description of Sumeru, the Gaṅgā arose as the current of water from outside the universe, when Vāmana’s toe pierced the shell. That water, after a thousand yugas, descended onto Sumeru. It is said that as the Gaṅgā appears now, it appears at other times also in the same manner. It is described in other Purāṇas that at the sacrifice of Bāṣkali, as at Bali’s sacrifice, Vāmana deva came and cheated him in the same manner. This happened previously. After Satī gave up her body, the description of Gaṅgā falling on Kailāsa happened. *Viśadā* means pure white.

### Text 5

*brahmādayo loka-nāthāḥ sva-nāthāya samādṛtāḥ*  
*sānugā balim ājahruḥ saṅkṣiptātma-vibhūtaye*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā and the lords of the planets and their followers in great respect arranged for worship of their master, who reduced his size again.**

**Комментарий:**

Three verses describe the worship.

### Text 16-17

*nandaḥ sunando 'tha jayo vijayaḥ prabalo balaḥ*  
*kumudaḥ kumudākṣaś ca viṣvaksenaḥ patattrirāṭ*  
*jayantaḥ śrutadevaś ca puṣpadanto 'tha sātvataḥ*  
*sarve nāgāyuta-prāṇāś camūṁ te jaghnur āsurīm*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda, Sunanda, Jaya, Vijaya, Prabala, Bala, Kumuda, Kumudākṣa, Viṣvaksena, Garuḍa Jayanta, Śrutadeva, Puṣpadanta and Sātvata, as powerful as ten thousand elephants, killed the demonic army.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. Because they appeared suddenly, they are understood to be eternal associates of the Lord. They were there but hidden. Jaya and Vijaya are other devotees of Vaikuṇṭha (not the gate keepers). *Nāgas* are elephants who are *vibhūtis* of the Lord in Vaikuṇṭha.

### Text 18

*hanyamānān svakān dṛṣṭvā puruṣānucarair baliḥ*  
*vārayām āsa saṁrabdhān kāvya-śāpam anusmaran*

**Перевод:**

**When Bali Mahārāja saw that his soldiers were being killed by the associates of Viṣṇu and when he remembered the curse of Śukrācārya, he prevented his angry soldiers from fighting.**

**Комментарий:**

This made him remember (*anusmaran*) the curse of Śukrācārya.

### Text 19

*he vipracitte he rāho he neme śrūyatāṁ vacaḥ*  
*mā yudhyata nivartadhvaṁ na naḥ kālo 'yam artha-kṛt*

**Перевод:**

**O Vipracitti, O Rāhu, O Nemi! Please hear my words! Don't fight. Stop immediately, for the present time is not in our favor.**

**Комментарий:**

He speaks to appease them. The words are not the highest truth.

### Text 21

*yo no bhavāya prāg āsīd abhavāya divaukasām*  
*sa eva bhagavān adya vartate tad-viparyayam*

**Перевод:**

**The supreme time factor was previously in our favor and not in favor of the** ***devatās*****, but now that same time factor is the opposite.**

**Комментарий:**

The time factor now exists in an opposite way (*tad-viparyayam*). We will defeat them.

### Text 23

*bhavadbhir nirjitā hy ete bahuśo 'nucarā hareḥ*  
*daivenarddhais ta evādya yudhi jitvā nadanti naḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The many followers of Viṣṇu who were defeated by you, because you had strength by time, have now defeated us, and are roaring in jubilation.**

**Комментарий:**

*Daivena* here means “by time.”

### Text 31

*padaikena mayākrānto bhūrlokaḥ khaṁ diśas tanoḥ*  
*svarlokas te dvitīyena paśyatas te svam ātmanā*

**Перевод:**

**Of these possessions, with one step I have occupied Bhūrloka, and with my body I have occupied the entire sky and all directions. And in your presence, with my second step, I, with my original form, have occupied Svarga owned by you.**

**Комментарий:**

*Svam svarlokaḥ* included other planets he conquered also.

### Text 32

*pratiśrutam adātus te niraye vāsa iṣyate*  
*viśa tvaṁ nirayaṁ tasmād guruṇā cānumoditaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Because you have been unable to give charity according to your promise, you should live in the hellish planets. Therefore, being pleased, enter hell along with your** ***guru.***

**Комментарий:**

*Niraye* (hell) means Pātāla, going somewhere lower.

### Text 33

*vṛthā manorathas tasya dūraḥ svargaḥ pataty adhaḥ*  
*pratiśrutasyādānena yo 'rthinaṁ vipralambhate*

**Перевод:**

**Far from being elevated to the heavenly planets or fulfilling one's desire, one who does not properly give a beggar what he has promised falls down to a hellish condition of life.**

**Комментарий:**

He confirms what he previously said with a general statement in two verses. Some versions do not have the second line but it is accepted by Śrīdhara Svāmī.

## Chapter Twenty-two

### Text 4

*puṁsāṁ ślāghyatamaṁ manye daṇḍam arhattamārpitam*  
*yaṁ na mātā pitā bhrātā suhṛdaś cādiśanti hi*

**Перевод:**

**A father, mother, brother or friend does not punish his subordinate in a thoroughly correct manner. I regard the punishment given by you, the most praiseworthy, as most exalted punishment, since it gives the best results.**

**Комментарий:**

Father, mother, brother or friend do not give proper punishment since they have worldly affection. Or it can also mean “They give proper punishment.” *Na* indicates agreement in this case.

### Text 8

*pitāmaho me bhavadīya-sammataḥ*  
*prahrāda āviṣkṛta-sādhu-vādaḥ*  
*bhavad-vipakṣeṇa vicitra-vaiśasaṁ*  
*samprāpitas tvaṁ paramaḥ sva-pitrā*

**Перевод:**

**My grandfather Prahlāda Mahārāja, honored by your devotees, famous as a devotee, although tortured in many ways by his father Hiraṇyakaśipu, still remained surrendered to you.**

**Комментарий:**

Four verses describe the influence of his grandfather.

### Text 10

*itthaṁ sa niścitya pitāmaho mahān*  
*agādha-bodho bhavataḥ pāda-padmam*  
*dhruvaṁ prapede hy akutobhayaṁ janād*  
*bhītaḥ svapakṣa-kṣapaṇasya sattama*

**Перевод:**

**O best of the saintly! Discerning this, my grandfather, a great devotee, deeply knowledgeable and afraid of worldly association, surrendered to your lotus feet which dispel all fear, though you were the destroyer of his own family.**

**Комментарий:**

Prahlāda was afraid of associating with the demons (*janād bhītaḥ*).

### Text 11

*athāham apy ātma-ripos tavāntikaṁ*  
*daivena nītaḥ prasabhaṁ tyājita-śrīḥ*  
*idaṁ kṛtāntāntika-varti jīvitaṁ*  
*yayādhruvaṁ stabdha-matir na budhyate*

**Перевод:**

**Only by good fortune have I been brought under your lotus feet and deprived of all my wealth, by you, perceived as an enemy of our family. With intelligence blinded by this wealth, a person does not realize the temporary nature of life where death is continually present.**

**Комментарий:**

You were perceived to be my enemy by illusion (*ātma-ripoḥ*). By the good fortune (*daivena*) of being the grandson of Prahlāda I have been brought to your feet.

### Text 14

*tasmai balir vāruṇa-pāśa-yantritaḥ*  
*samarhaṇaṁ nopajahāra pūrvavat*  
*nanāma mūrdhnāśru-vilola-locanaḥ*  
*sa-vrīḍa-nīcīna-mukho babhūva ha*

**Перевод:**

**Being bound by the ropes of Varuṇa, Bali Mahārāja could not offer befitting respect to Prahlāda Mahārāja as he had before. Rather, he simply offered respectful obeisances with his head, while eyes filled with tears and his face lowered in shame.**

**Комментарий:**

He was ashamed because of his low actions and his ignorance on first meeting the brāhmaṇa dwarf.

### Text 15

*sa tatra hāsīnam udīkṣya sat-patiṁ*  
*hariṁ sunandādy-anugair upāsitam*  
*upetya bhūmau śirasā mahā-manā*  
*nanāma mūrdhnā pulakāśru-viklavaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When the great devotee Prahlāda saw the Supreme Lord seated there, being worshiped by his intimate associates like Sunanda, he approached the Lord and offered respects on the ground with his head. He was overwhelmed with tears and his hairs stood on end.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord resumed his form as Vāmana in order to obtain the remainder of the promised land.

### Text 21

*śrī-brahmovāca*  
*bhūta-bhāvana bhūteśa deva-deva jaganmaya*  
*muñcainaṁ hṛta-sarvasvaṁ nāyam arhati nigraham*

**Перевод:**

**Lord Brahmā said: O well-wisher and master of all living entities! O worshipable deity of all the** ***devatās*****! O all-pervading Lord! Please release this person bereft of everything. He does not deserve to be punished further.**

**Комментарий:**

Prahlāda and Vindyavali had implored the Lord for Bali’s offense. Brahmā implored the Lord on Bali’s behalf, seeing him as a devotee.

### Text 26

*janma-karma-vayo-rūpa- vidyaiśvarya-dhanādibhiḥ*  
*yady asya na bhavet stambhas tatrāyaṁ mad-anugrahaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**If a human being is born in an aristocratic family, if he performs wonderful activities, if he is youthful, if he has personal beauty, education or wealth, and if he is nonetheless not proud, it is to be understood that he is especially favored by me.**

**Комментарий:**

One can attain prosperity by karma or by the mercy of the Lord. Verse 24 describes the fault of attaining wealth by karma. Verse 26 describes wealth attained by the Lord’s mercy. This is without fault. Bali would not become proud by ruling Sutaḷa, or again ruling Svarga.

### Text 32

*tāvat sutalam adhyāstāṁ viśvakarma-vinirmitam*  
*yad ādhayo vyādhayaś ca klamas tandrā parābhavaḥ*  
*nopasargā nivasatāṁ sambhavanti mamekṣayā*

**Перевод:**

**Until he achieves the position of king of heaven, he should live on the planet Sutala, which was made by Viśvakarmā. Because it is especially protected by me, in that place the inhabitants are free from mental and bodily miseries, fatigue, exhaustion, defeat and all other disturbances. Bali Mahārāja, you may now go and live there peacefully.**

**Комментарий:**

Instead of *tandrā* sometimes *tandrī* is seen, according to the Unādi-sūtras.

## Chapter Twenty-three

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*ity uktavantaṁ puruṣaṁ purātanaṁ*  
*mahānubhāvo 'khila-sādhu-sammataḥ*  
*baddhāñjalir bāṣpa-kalākulekṣaṇo*  
*bhakty-utkalo gadgadayā girābravīt*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: The great minded, Bali Mahārāja, respected by all devotees, his eyes filled with tears, his hands folded and his voice faltering, filled with devotion, responded to the most ancient Lord, who had just spoken.**

**Комментарий:**

After folding his hands, he blissfully placed his head at the Lord’s feet according to his desire in SB [8.22.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/22.2):

*yady uttamaśloka bhavān mameritaṁ*  
*vaco vyalīkaṁ sura-varya manyate*  
*karomy ṛtaṁ tan na bhavet pralambhanaṁ*  
*padaṁ tṛtīyaṁ kuru śīrṣṇi me nijam*

**O most famous Lord, worshipped by all the** ***devatās*****! If you think that my promise has become false, I shall make it true. I should not be made a cheater. Please, therefore, place your third lotus footstep on my head.**

### Text 2

*śrī-balir uvāca*  
*aho praṇāmāya kṛtaḥ samudyamaḥ*  
*prapanna-bhaktārtha-vidhau samāhitaḥ*  
*yal loka-pālais tvad-anugraho 'marair*  
*alabdha-pūrvo 'pasade 'sure 'rpitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Bali Mahārāja said: Ah! Just my eagerness to offer respects is able to yield results sought by the surrendered devotees. This low demon has received your rare mercy, not received previously even by jīvanmuktas.**

**Комментарий:**

He then offers his prayers. Śruti says:

*idaṁ viṣṇur vicakrame tredhā nidadhe padam*

**Viṣṇu stepped three times, crossing the universe. (Black Yajur Veda)**

Sone persons offer respects with great effort (*samudyamaḥ*), paying attention to this surrender which is the ultimate goal (*puruṣārtha)* of the blissful devotees. They eventually attain that goal. This is astonishing (*aho*). There is something more astonishing. This mercy was offered by you to me, a very low demon (*apasade*). I was boldly accepting your wish, out of a desire for my own fame. This mercy was not attained by even jīvanmuktas, who have surpassed death (*amaraiḥ*).

### Text 6

*śrī-prahrāda uvāca*  
*nemaṁ viriñco labhate prasādaṁ*  
*na śrīr na śarvaḥ kim utāpare 'nye*  
*yan no 'surāṇām asi durga-pālo*  
*viśvābhivandyair abhivanditāṅghriḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Prahlāda Mahārāja said: We have attained mercy that Brahmā, Lakṣmī or Śiva, what to speak of others, have not attained. You, whose lotus feet are worshiped by the most respected person in the universe, are the protector of us demons in all respects.**

**Комментарий:**

Since I am unqualified for this mercy, the cause is only your merciful nature and nothing else. In this way he shows the supreme position of the Lord. Others speak of him as their Lord, I see that you have affection for me as a son. To express this he refers to other great devotees. The meaning is clear. Śukadeva will also refer to the great devotees later:

*nemaṁ viriñco na bhavo na śrīr apy aṅga-saṁśrayā*  
*prasādaṁ lebhire gopī yat tat prāpa vimuktidāt*

**Neither Brahmā, nor Śiva, nor even the goddess of fortune, who is always the better half of the Supreme Lord, can obtain from the Supreme Lord, the deliverer from this material world, such mercy as received by mother Yaśodā. (SB** [**10.9.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/9.20)**)**

None of them have the same type of mercy. Though the Lord binds people, he in this case is bound by the devotee. There is a great difference when the Lord is under control of the devotee.

### Text 7

*yat-pāda-padma-makaranda-niṣevaṇena*  
*brahmādayaḥ śaraṇadāśnuvate vibhūtīḥ*  
*kasmād vayaṁ kusṛtayaḥ khala-yonayas te*  
*dākṣiṇya-dṛṣṭi-padavīṁ bhavataḥ praṇītāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O supreme shelter of all beings! Brahmā and others enjoy their powers simply by tasting the honey of rendering service at your lotus feet. But how did we wicked fellows born of a demonic family, receive such great mercy?**

**Комментарий:**

How can there be any other cause? They predominantly enjoy powers (*vibhūtīḥ*), and not such mercy.

### Text 8

*citraṁ tavehitam aho 'mita-yogamāyā-*  
*līlā-visṛṣṭa-bhuvanasya viśāradasya*  
*sarvātmanaḥ samadṛśo viṣamaḥ svabhāvo*  
*bhakta-priyo yad asi kalpataru-svabhāvaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord with unlimited powers of** ***yoga!*** **Your activities are inconceivable. Though equal to all, you show favoritism. You, who create the universe as your pastime by** ***māyā,*** **who know all beings, and who give life to all beings, show particular affection for your devotees. You have the nature of a desire tree.**

**Комментарий:**

Not only with devotees like me do you act in such a manner beyond logic. You do this for your other devotees.

**Paramātmā Sandarbha 93:**

Though affectionate to devotees *(bhakti-priyaḥ),* you are equal *(samadṛśaḥ*) in nature (*svabhāvaḥ*), without partiality (*aviṣamaḥ*), because your nature is like a desire tree. Though you appear partial you are actually impartial. This is astonishing.

Another meaning is as follows. As the desire tree is equal to all who take shelter of it, because you show partiality to the devotee, and thus have a nature of inequality, you still have a nature of being equal. That conclusion should not be avoided. Thus the word can be taken as *viṣama* instead of *aviṣama.* SB [10.38.22](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/38.22) quoted above shows that the Lord does have partiality to the devotee. Actually, because the Lord has inconceivable powers as his chief quality, this resolves the contradiction in the Lord. It is said:

*namo namas te ’stv ṛṣabhāya sātvatāṁ*  
*vidūra-kāṣṭhāya muhuḥ kuyoginām \|*  
*nirasta-sāmyātiśayena rādhasā*  
*sva-dhāmani brahmaṇi raṁsyate namaḥ \|\|*

**I continually offer respects to you, son of Vasudeva of the Yadus, who remain forever distant from the non-devotees, who are the enjoyer in your spiritual abode in Mathurā and Vraja with your devotees, displaying powers that are unequalled and unsurpassed. (SB** [**2.4.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-2/4.14)**)**

Śrīdhara says in his commentary “Though it appears the Lord is partial, he has no fault, since he has inconceivable powers.” Bhīṣma says:

*sarvātmanaḥ sama-dṛśo hy advayasyānahaṅkṛteḥ \|*  
*tat-kṛtaṁ mati-vaiṣamyaṁ niravadyasya na kvacit \|\|*

**His actions are not a mistake of his judgment for he is faultless, he is the soul of all beings, he sees all equally, he is without a second, and is without pride.**

*tathāpy ekānta-bhakteṣu paśya bhūpānukampitam \|*  
*yan me ’sūṁs tyajataḥ sākṣāt kṛṣṇo darśanam āgataḥ \|\|*

**O king! See his mercy to his dedicated devotees! By that mercy he has come before me directly when I am about to leave my body. (SB** [**1.8.21-22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/8.21)**)**

The Lord says:

*samo ’haṁ sarva-bhūteṣu na me dveṣyo ’sti na priyaḥ \|*  
*ye bhajanti tu māṁ bhaktyā mayi te teṣu cāpy aham \|\|29\|\|*

**I am equal to all living beings. I do not hate anyone nor do I favor anyone. But those who worship me with devotion are in me, and I am in them. (BG** [**9.29**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.29)**)**

Or it is astonishing that you are equal to all and have a partial nature. The partiality is shown in *bhakta-priyo yad as*i: you are affectionate to the devotee. You are perfect in being equal to all in three ways. With your kriyā-śakti you create the unlimited universes by your māyā śakti, an ābhāsa of your unlimited *(amita)* svarūpa śakti (*yoga-māyā*). This is your kriyā-śakti. Next the jñāna-śakti is shown. You know all beings (*viśāradasya*). His svarūpa is then shown. You are the svarūpa of all things (*sarvātmanaḥ*). “Is my nature like the desire tree then?” You are like this because you have the nature of a desire tree. This is astonishing.

### Text 10

*nityaṁ draṣṭāsi māṁ tatra gadā-pāṇim avasthitam*  
*mad-darśana-mahāhlāda- dhvasta-karma-nibandhanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**All bondage of** ***karma*** **has already been destroyed by the great bliss of seeing me. Now you will constantly see me there with a club in my hand.**

**Комментарий:**

Previously your karmas were destroyed by seeing me. This has become your nature. Now you will constantly see me.

## Chapter Twenty-four

### Text 1

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*bhagavañ chrotum icchāmi harer adbhuta-karmaṇaḥ*  
*avatāra-kathām ādyāṁ māyā-matsya-viḍambanam*

**Перевод:**

**Mahārāja Parīkṣit said: O great sage! I wish to hear the story in which the Lord who performs astonishing activities imitates a material fish.**

**Комментарий:**

I wish to hear the story which describes the imitation of a fish by the Lord’s māyā.

### Text 2-3

*yad-artham adadhād rūpaṁ mātsyaṁ loka-jugupsitam*  
*tamaḥ-prakṛti-durmarṣaṁ karma-grasta iveśvaraḥ*  
*etan no bhagavan sarvaṁ yathāvad vaktum arhasi*  
*uttamaśloka-caritaṁ sarva-loka-sukhāvaham*

**Перевод:**

**Why did the Lord accept the seemingly abominable form of a fish in** ***tamoguṇa,*** **condemned by the world, as if controlled by** ***karma*****? O sage! Kindly explain to me everything about this pastime of the Lord which gives happiness to the world.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. Why did the Lord accept the low form of a fish, as if controlled by karma (*karma-graṣṭa iva*), which is criticized by ordinary people? Accepting such a form is not proper. The Lord’s activities should give happiness to all people.

### Text 6

*uccāvaceṣu bhūteṣu caran vāyur iveśvaraḥ*  
*noccāvacatvaṁ bhajate nirguṇatvād dhiyo guṇaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Though moving as Paramātmā within species which are higher and lower by their intelligence and qualities, the Lord does not share those qualities of superior or inferior since he is beyond the** ***guṇas*****, just as the air, though moving everywhere, is untouched by all objects.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord appears as devatā, human and animal (*uccāvaceṣu*), and performs pastimes, but does not partake of the higher or lower qualities displayed in those species or their ordinary intelligence, since he is beyond the guṇas, having a form of eternity, knowledge and bliss.

### Text 7

*āsīd atīta-kalpānte brāhmo naimittiko layaḥ*  
*samudropaplutās tatra lokā bhūr-ādayo nṛpa*

**Перевод:**

**O King! At the end of the previous day of Brahmā, when Brahmā slept, the daily annihilation took place, and the three worlds were covered by the water of the ocean.**

**Комментарий:**

As with Varāha, Matsya appeared twice. When the day of brahma finished, when Brahmā slept, the three worlds were covered by water. This is the periodic destruction.

### Text 8

*kālenāgata-nidrasya dhātuḥ śiśayiṣor balī*  
*mukhato niḥsṛtān vedān hayagrīvo 'ntike 'harat*

**Перевод:**

**At the end of Brahmā's day, when sleepy Brahmā was dozing, the strong demon named Hayagrīva stole the Vedas which emanated from his mouth.**

**Комментарий:**

When Brahmā was sleeping, the demon stole the Vedas which emanated from his mouth by the power of yoga. The Vedas were situated within Brahmā at that time. The reason this happened was that the Lord desired a pastimes of teaching Vedas again at the beginning of the next day.

### Text 9

*jñātvā tad dānavendrasya hayagrīvasya ceṣṭitam*  
*dadhāra śapharī-rūpaṁ bhagavān harir īśvaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Understanding the acts of the great demon Hayagrīva, the Supreme Lord assumed the form of a fish.**

**Комментарий:**

After the flooding was finished, the Lord appeared as the fish:

*atīta-pralayāpāya utthitāya sa vedhase*  
*hatvāsuraṁ hayagrīvaṁ vedān pratyāharad dhariḥ*

**At the end of the last inundation (during Brahmā’s night), at the beginning of Svāyambhuva Manvantara, the Supreme Lord killed the demon named Hayagrīva and delivered the Vedas to Lord Brahmā when Lord Brahmā awoke from sleep. (SB** [**8.24.57**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/24.57)**)**

### Text 10

*tatra rāja-ṛṣiḥ kaścin nāmnā satyavrato mahān*  
*nārāyaṇa-paro 'tapat tapaḥ sa salilāśanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Concerning this topic (during the Cākṣuṣa-manvantara), a great king named Satyavrata, a devotee of Nārāyaṇa, dedicated to Matsya, performed austerities by subsisting only on water.**

**Комментарий:**

Having spoken of the past part of kalpa, he now explains the pastime in the present part of the kalpa. *Tatra* means “in this topic.”

### Text 11

*yo 'sāv asmin mahā-kalpe tanayaḥ sa vivasvataḥ*  
*śrāddhadeva iti khyāto manutve hariṇārpitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In this particular day of Brahmā, King Satyavrata later became the son of Vivasvān, the king of the sun planet, and was known as Śrāddhadeva. By the mercy of the Supreme Lord, he was given the post of Manu.**

**Комментарий:**

Special respect is shown for this day of Brahmā, in order to praise the particular Manu.

### Text 23

*ity uktaḥ so 'nayan matsyaṁ tatra tatrāvidāsini*  
*jalāśaye 'sammitaṁ taṁ samudre prākṣipaj jhaṣam*

**Перевод:**

**When thus requested, King Satyavrata took the fish to a perennial reservoir of water. But when that also proved insufficient, the King at last threw the gigantic fish into the ocean.**

**Комментарий:**

Though he heard the fish speak like a human, he still thought it was a fish, since he was bewildered by the Lord’s māyā.

### Text 34-35

*tvaṁ tāvad oṣadhīḥ sarvā bījāny uccāvacāni ca*  
*saptarṣibhiḥ parivṛtaḥ sarva-sattvopabṛṁhitaḥ*  
*āruhya bṛhatīṁ nāvaṁ vicariṣyasy aviklavaḥ*  
*ekārṇave nirāloke ṛṣīṇām eva varcasā*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, you should collect all types of herbs and seeds of higher and lower species, and accompanied by the seven sages and surrounded by all kinds of living entities, you should get aboard that huge boat, and without distress you shall easily travel on the dark ocean of inundation by the light from the effulgent sages.**

**Комментарий:**

Two verses go together. Being surrounded by and placed together (*upabṛṁhitaḥ*) with all creatures required, you should get in the boat.

### Text 38

*madīyaṁ mahimānaṁ ca paraṁ brahmeti śabditam*  
*vetsyasy anugṛhītaṁ me sampraśnair vivṛtaṁ hṛdi*

**Перевод:**

**By my mercy, you will realize my power known as the impersonal Brahman, which will be disclosed in your heart through questions and answers.**

**Комментарий:**

The impersonal manifestation belongs to the Lord, since everything arises from him, the aṁśī of all things, endowed with acintya-śakti. You will know my power (*mahimānam*), the vibhūti without qualities, since it is directly revealed by me. It is revealed by my mercy in the heart and is described by me through questions made by you. Since realization of Brahman is included in realization of me, it is not different from realizing me. However, by realizing me directly through bhakti, this realization alone does not become apparent. If you have some desire for this manifestation of mine, the desire will be fulfilled. Details related to this will be shown in the Eleventh Canto.

### Text 40

*āstīrya darbhān prāk-kūlān rājarṣiḥ prāg-udaṅ-mukhaḥ*  
*niṣasāda hareḥ pādau cintayan matsya-rūpiṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**After spreading** ***kuśa*** **with its tips pointing east, the saintly King, facing the northeast, sat down on the grass and began to meditate upon the Lord in the form of a fish.**

**Комментарий:**

He faced in the north-eastern direction.

### Text 41

*tataḥ samudra udvelaḥ sarvataḥ plāvayan mahīm*  
*vardhamāno mahā-meghair varṣadbhiḥ samadṛśyata*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, because of gigantic clouds pouring incessant water, the ocean began to overflow onto the land and inundate the entire world.**

**Комментарий:**

At the end of Cākṣusa-manvantara, there was a sudden deluge.

*rūpaṁ sa jagṛhe mātsyaṁ cākṣuṣodadhi-samplave \|*  
*nāvy āropya mahī-mayyām apād vaivasvataṁ manum \|\|*

**When there was an inundation in the period of the Cākṣuṣa Manu the Lord accepted the form of a fish and protected Vaivasvata Manu, placing him on a boat formed from the earth. (SB** [**1.3.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/3.15)**)**

It is said in scriptures that there is a deluge after every manvantara. The proofs are given in the scriptures. However, this deluge came suddenly in the middle of the sixth manvantara, not at the end. This was a separate occurrence, a special case during this manvantara. There was not included in four types of destruction. During the destruction after the manvantara, the three worlds are flooded and Brahmā also sleeps daily for some time. Thus mention of Brahmā’s night (verse 37) is not literal. According to Bhāgavatam, supported by other Purāṇas, this event did not happen in another kalpa.

### Text 46

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*anādy-avidyopahatātma-saṁvidas*  
*tan-mūla-saṁsāra-pariśramāturāḥ*  
*yadṛcchayopasṛtā yam āpnuyur*

**Перевод:**

**The King said: Those who have lost their self-knowledge because of ignorance without beginning, and who because of this ignorance are suffering from fatigue in the material world, after obtaining the mercy of devotee, attain you, who give special liberation and who are the supreme** ***guru*** **who cuts the knot in the heart*****.***

**Комментарий:**

*Avidyā* refers to the Lord’s māyā.

### Text 48

*yat-sevayāgner iva rudra-rodanaṁ*  
*pumān vijahyān malam ātmanas tamaḥ*  
*bhajeta varṇaṁ nijam eṣa so 'vyayo*  
*bhūyāt sa īśaḥ paramo guror guruḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just as gold ore gives up its impurities by fire, the** ***jīva*** **will give up his ignorance by service to you and attains his** ***svarūpa.*** **May that supreme unchanging Lord, the supreme** ***guru*****, be our** ***guru!***

**Комментарий:**

Having attained you, no one else need be sought. You are the supreme guru of the guru.

### Text 49

*na yat-prasādāyuta-bhāga-leśam*  
*anye ca devā guravo janāḥ svayam*  
*kartuṁ sametāḥ prabhavanti puṁsas*  
*tam īśvaraṁ tvāṁ śaraṇaṁ prapadye*

**Перевод:**

**Neither the** ***devatās,*** **parents nor kings, independently or together, can offer mercy that equals even one ten-thousandth of your mercy. Therefore I wish to take shelter of the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Others who think themselves independent are insignificant.

### Text 50

*acakṣur andhasya yathāgraṇīḥ kṛtas*  
*tathā janasyāviduṣo 'budho guruḥ*  
*tvam arka-dṛk sarva-dṛśāṁ samīkṣaṇo*  
*vṛto gurur naḥ sva-gatiṁ bubhutsatām*

**Перевод:**

**Just as a blind man accepts another blind man to lead him, ignorant people accept another ignorant man as their** ***guru*****. We, desiring** ***bhakti*****, accept you, who are like the sun, who reveal all senses and all knowledge, as our** ***guru.***

**Комментарий:**

Worshipping the Lord because of impressions from previous birth of being a king with bhakti attained from a guru, praising the Lord, he has this disposition. You should come personally and be the guru. Approaching you, I desire you as the guru. You are the best among all those who know the truth (*arka-dṛk*).

### Text 57

*atīta-pralayāpāya utthitāya sa vedhase*  
*hatvāsuraṁ hayagrīvaṁ vedān pratyāharad dhariḥ*

**Перевод:**

**At the end of the last inundation (during Brahmā’s night), at the beginning of Svāyambhuva Manvantara, the Supreme Lord killed the demon named Hayagrīva and delivered the Vedas to Lord Brahmā when Lord Brahmā awoke from sleep.**

**Комментарий:**

Again he shows that the avatāra of Matsya occurs twice, in two verses.

### Text 58

*sa tu satyavrato raja jñāna-vijñāna-saṁyutaḥ*  
*viṣṇoḥ prasādāt kalpe 'sminn āsīd vaivasvato manuḥ*

**Перевод:**

**King Satyavrata, endowed with knowledge and realization by the mercy of the Lord during Cākṣuṣa Manvantara, became Vaivasvata Manu in the present Manvantara.**

**Комментарий:**

Attaining mercy of Viṣṇu during the sixth manvantara, he became Vaivasvata Manu in the seventh manvantara.

### Text 59

*satyavratasya rājarṣer māyā-matsyasya śārṅgiṇaḥ*  
*saṁvādaṁ mahad-ākhyānaṁ śrutvā mucyeta kilbiṣāt*

**Перевод:**

**If one hears this great story in the form of a discussion between the great King Satyavrata and the fish incarnation with a horn, he will be delivered from the reactions of sinful life.**

**Комментарий:**

Māyā means mercy.

### Text 61

*pralaya-payasi dhātuḥ supta-śakter mukhebhyaḥ*  
*śruti-gaṇam apanītaṁ pratyupādatta hatvā*  
*ditijam akathayad yo brahma satyavratānāṁ*  
*tam aham akhila-hetuṁ jihma-mīnaṁ nato 'smi*

**Перевод:**

**I offer my respectful obeisances unto Matsya, the cause of all causes, who, after killing the demon, restored to Lord Brahmā the Vedic literature stolen from his mouths while Brahmā had no powers, during sleep at the time of the devastation, and who explained the essence of Vedic literature to King Satyavrata.**

**Комментарий:**

I offer respects to you who appeared as an ordinary fish among beings with crooked hearts *(jihma*).

*nāhaṁ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yoga-māyā-samāvṛtaḥ \|*  
*mūḍho ’yaṁ nābhijānāti loko mām ajam avyayam \|\|*

**Being covered by my bewildering power of** ***māyā*****, I am not visible to all these people. The foolish do not know me, who am without birth and unchanging in qualities. (BG** [**7.25**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/7.25)**)**

*mṛtyur bhoja-pater virāḍ aviduṣāṁ tattvaṁ paraṁ yogināṁ*  
*vṛṣṇīnāṁ para-devateti vidito raṅgaṁ gataḥ sāgrajaḥ*

**The King of the Bhojas saw Kṛṣṇa as death, the unintelligent saw him as the Supreme Lord’s universal form, the yogīs saw him as the Absolute Truth and the Vṛṣṇis saw him as their supreme worshipable deity. (SB** [**10.43.17**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/43.17)**)**
