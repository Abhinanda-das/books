# Krama-sandarbha 9 Canto

## Chapter One

*oṁ namaḥ śrī kṛṣṇāya.*  
*navala-krama-sandarbhe sandarbhāṇāṁ samāhṛtiḥ*  
*kriyate yanni deśena sa me ’nanyagatergatiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He by whose order this collection of Sandarbhas is made into the Ninth Krama Sandarbha is the goal of this person with no other goal.**

### Text 2-3

*yo 'sau satyavrato nāma rājarṣir draviḍeśvaraḥ*  
*jñānaṁ yo 'tīta-kalpānte lebhe puruṣa-sevayā*  
*sa vai vivasvataḥ putro manur āsīd iti śrutam*  
*tvattas tasya sutāḥ proktā ikṣvāku-pramukhā nṛpāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Satyavrata, the saintly king of Draviḍadeśa who received spiritual knowledge at the end of the last Manvantara by the grace of the Lord, later became Vaivasvata Manu, the son of Vivasvān. I have received this knowledge from you. I also understand that such kings as Ikṣvāku were his sons, as you have already explained.**

**Комментарий:**

Three verses go together.

### Text 31

*ṛṣayo 'pi tayor vīkṣya prasaṅgaṁ ramamāṇayoḥ*  
*nivṛttāḥ prayayus tasmān nara-nārāyaṇāśramam*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Lord Śiva and Pārvatī engaged in intimacy, all the sages refrained from approaching and departed for the hermitage of Nara-Nārāyaṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Being embarrassed, considering another place, they went to Nara-nārāyaṇa’s āśrama where such intimacy was absent.

## Chapter Two

### Text 7

*vyāghro 'pi vṛkṇa-śravaṇo nistriṁśāgrāhatas tataḥ*  
*niścakrāma bhṛśaṁ bhīto raktaṁ pathi samutsṛjan*

**Перевод:**

**Because the tiger's ear had been cut by the edge of the sword, the tiger fled in great fear from that place, while bleeding on the path.**

**Комментарий:**

Because they were all crowded together, he could not discern the truth.

### Text 8

*manyamāno hataṁ vyāghraṁ pṛṣadhraḥ para-vīra-hā*  
*adrākṣīt sva-hatāṁ babhruṁ vyuṣṭāyāṁ niśi duḥkhitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In the morning, when Pṛṣadhra, who was quite able to subdue his enemy, saw that he had killed the cow although at night he thought he had killed the tiger, he was very unhappy.**

**Комментарий:**

He became repentant.

### Text 9

*taṁ śaśāpa kulācāryaḥ kṛtāgasam akāmataḥ*  
*na kṣatra-bandhuḥ śūdras tvaṁ karmaṇā bhavitāmunā*

**Перевод:**

**Although Pṛṣadhra had committed the sin unknowingly, his family priest, Vasiṣṭha, cursed him, saying, "In your next life you shall not be able to become a fallen** ***kṣatriya.*** **Instead, you shall take birth as a** ***śūdra*****."**

**Комментарий:**

Though the atonement should have been slight, the reason for losing his caste was the guru’s compassion for the cow. This is expressed in three verses.

### Text 10

*evaṁ śaptas tu guruṇā pratyagṛhṇāt kṛtāñjaliḥ*  
*adhārayad vrataṁ vīra ūrdhva-retā muni-priyam*

**Перевод:**

**When the hero Pṛṣadhra was thus cursed by his** ***guru,*** **he accepted the curse with folded hands. Controlling his senses, he took the vow of** ***brahmacarya.***

**Комментарий:**

He responded humbly to the curse.

### Text 14

*evaṁ vṛtto vanaṁ gatvā dṛṣṭvā dāvāgnim utthitam*  
*tenopayukta-karaṇo brahma prāpa paraṁ muniḥ*

**Перевод:**

**With this way of life, Pṛṣadhra, having entered the forest, saw a blazing forest fire, which had arisen there. The sage, having burned up his body in the fire, achieved Kṛṣṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Since he was a devotee of Vāsudeva, though he was a śūdra, he attained liberation.

## Chapter Three

### Text 5

*śakṛn-mūtra-nirodho 'bhūt sainikānāṁ ca tat-kṣaṇāt*  
*rājarṣis tam upālakṣya puruṣān vismito 'bravīt*

**Перевод:**

**Immediately all the soldiers of Śaryāti could not pass urine and stool. Upon perceiving this, Śaryāti spoke to his associates in surprise.**

**Комментарий:**

The sage did this because he desired the daughter, and desired that his hermitage be free from contamination at that time. It was a slight disturbance.

### Text 30

*āvartamāne gāndharve sthito 'labdha-kṣaṇaḥ kṣaṇam*  
*tad-anta ādyam ānamya svābhiprāyaṁ nyavedayat*

**Перевод:**

**Since a Gandharva was engaged in performing, Lord Brahmā was busy. After a moment, when the Gandharva finished, Kakudmī offered his respects to Brahmā and expressed his desire.**

**Комментарий:**

After a moment he approached Brahmā.

## Chapter Four

### Text 18-20

*sa vai manaḥ kṛṣṇa-padāravindayor*  
*vacāṁsi vaikuṇṭha-guṇānuvarṇane*  
*karau harer mandira-mārjanādiṣu*  
*śrutiṁ cakārācyuta-sat-kathodaye*  
*mukunda-liṅgālaya-darśane dṛśau*  
*tad-bhṛtya-gātra-sparśe 'ṅga-saṅgamam*  
*ghrāṇaṁ ca tat-pāda-saroja-saurabhe*  
*śrīmat-tulasyā rasanāṁ tad-arpite*  
*pādau hareḥ kṣetra-padānusarpaṇe*  
*śiro hṛṣīkeśa-padābhivandane*  
*kāmaṁ ca dāsye na tu kāma-kāmyayā*  
*yathottamaśloka-janāśrayā ratiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Mahārāja Ambarīṣa always engaged his mind in meditating upon the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa, his words in describing the glories of the Lord, his hands in cleansing the Lord's temple, and his ears in hearing the words spoken by Kṛṣṇa or about Kṛṣṇa. He engaged his eyes in seeing the deity of Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa's temples and Kṛṣṇa's places like Mathurā and Vṛndāvana, he engaged his sense of touch in touching the bodies of the Lord's devotees, he engaged his sense of smell in smelling the fragrance of** ***tulasī*** **offered to the Lord, and he engaged his tongue in tasting the Lord's** ***prasāda.*** **He engaged his legs in walking to the holy places and temples of the Lord, his head in bowing down before the Lord, and all his desires in serving the Lord, without desiring material enjoyment, in order to develop** ***rati*** **as possessed by great devotees.**

**Комментарий:**

Three verses go together.

He made the decision (*kāmam*) out of the attitude of dāsyam, so that somehow he would develop rati for the Lord, which is found in the devotees such as Prahlāda, without a desire for material enjoyment.

### Text 21

*evaṁ sadā karma-kalāpam ātmanaḥ*  
*pare 'dhiyajñe bhagavaty adhokṣaje*  
*sarvātma-bhāvaṁ vidadhan mahīm imāṁ*  
*tan-niṣṭha-viprābhihitaḥ śaśāsa ha*

**Перевод:**

**Performing devotional acts daily with** ***rati*** **by his mind and senses to the Supreme Lord, Kṛṣṇa, whose worship is superior to other processes, he ruled the world, taking advice from devotee** ***brāhmaṇas.***

**Комментарий:**

He performed actions for the supreme Lord as previously described, in which there was completely dedicated (*sarvātma*) bhakti (*bhāvam*).

### Text 22

*īje 'śvamedhair adhiyajñam īśvaraṁ*  
*mahā-vibhūtyopacitāṅga-dakṣiṇaiḥ*  
*tatair vasiṣṭhāsita-gautamādibhir*  
*dhanvany abhisrotam asau sarasvatīm*

**Перевод:**

**He worshipped the Supreme Lord by horse sacrifices required by a king for prosperity, with all** ***aṅgas*** **and charities, performed by Vasiṣṭha, Asita, Gautama and other sages in the desert facing the flow of the Sarasvatī River.**

**Комментарий:**

If he performed devotional activities how can he do sacrifices? Are they done together? The sacrifices were done by Vasiṣṭha and other sages, near the Sarasvatī River, respecting the tīrtha (*abhisrotam*). Respect for the river is shown on the path of karma. This took place in holy tīrthas within Dhanvan, which were known for piety.

### Text 25

*saṁvardhayanti yat kāmāḥ svārājya-paribhāvitāḥ*  
*durlabhā nāpi siddhānāṁ mukundaṁ hṛdi paśyataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The material pleasures which surpass those of Svarga and are rare even for Siddhas did not give the citizens bliss because they saw Mukunda in their hearts.**

**Комментарий:**

Material pleasures which surpass those equal to Indra’s position (*svārājya-paribhāvitāḥ*) do not give bliss to the citizens.

### Text 26

*sa itthaṁ bhakti-yogena tapo-yuktena pārthivaḥ*  
*sva-dharmeṇa hariṁ prīṇan sarvān kāmān śanair jahau*

**Перевод:**

**Mahārāja Ambarīṣa, thus satisfying the Lord by devotional service as his main duty along with devotion vows, gradually gave up all material desires.**

**Комментарий:**

*Tapo-yuktena* means “along with Dvādaśī vows in the house, favorable for bhakti.”

### Text 29

*ārirādhayiṣuḥ kṛṣṇaṁ mahiṣyā tulya-śīlayā*  
*yuktaḥ sāṁvatsaraṁ vīro dadhāra dvādaśī-vratam*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring to worship Kṛṣṇa, Mahārāja Ambarīṣa, along with his queen, who was equally qualified, observed the vow of Dvādaśī for one year.**

**Комментарий:**

He performed the Dvādaśī vow for one year in order attain his special goals.

### Text 53-54

*śrī-brahmovāca*  
*sthānaṁ madīyaṁ saha-viśvam etat*  
*krīḍāvasāne dvi-parārdha-saṁjñe*  
*bhrū-bhaṅga-mātreṇa hi sandidhakṣoḥ*  
*kālātmano yasya tirobhaviṣyati*  
*ahaṁ bhavo dakṣa-bhṛgu-pradhānāḥ*  
*prajeśa-bhūteśa-sureśa-mukhyāḥ*  
*sarve vayaṁ yan-niyamaṁ prapannā*  
*mūrdhnyārpitaṁ loka-hitaṁ vahāmaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā said: At the end of my life when the pastimes of the Lord come to an end, Viṣṇu, through time, by a movement of his eyebrows, vanquishes the entire universe, including our places of residence. I, Śiva, as well as Dakṣa, Bhṛgu and Prajāpatis, and also the rulers of the living entities, and the rulers of the** ***devatās*****—all of us surrender to Viṣṇu, whose orders we carry on our heads for the benefit of all living entities.**

**Комментарий:**

Though Brahmā is a guṇāvatāra, he is considered to be an āveśāvatāra (jīva). Thus he says “All of us surrender to Viṣṇu.”

### Text 56

*śrī-śaṅkara uvāca*  
*vayaṁ na tāta prabhavāma bhūmni*  
*yasmin pare 'nye 'py aja-jīva-kośāḥ*  
*bhavanti kāle na bhavanti hīdṛśāḥ*  
*sahasraśo yatra vayaṁ bhramāmaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śiva said: My dear son! We cannot compete with the Supreme Lord within whom the thousands of universes in which we wander in bewilderment arise and are destroyed with time.**

**Комментарий:**

Śiva speaks four verses.

### Text 62

*ajānatā te paramānubhāvaṁ*  
*kṛtaṁ mayāghaṁ bhavataḥ priyāṇām*  
*vidhehi tasyāpacitiṁ vidhātar*  
*mucyeta yan-nāmny udite nārako 'pi*

**Перевод:**

**O supreme controller! Without knowledge of your unlimited prowess, I have offended your most dear devotees. O Lord by whose name a person in hell becomes liberated! Please do something to nullify this offense.**

**Комментарий:**

By chanting the Lord’s name a person situated in hell (*nārako ‘pi*) is liberated. What to speak of a person surrendered to you personally.

*yathā yathā harer nāma kīrtayanti sma nārakāḥ*  
*tathā tathā harau bhaktim udvahanto divaṁ yayuḥ*

**Those who chant the name of the Lord in hell, developing bhakti, attain the spiritual world. (Nṛsiṁha Purāṇa 8.31)**

### Text 63

*śrī-bhagavān uvāca*  
*ahaṁ bhakta-parādhīno hy asvatantra iva dvija*  
*sādhubhir grasta-hṛdayo bhaktair bhakta-jana-priyaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord said: O** ***brāhmaṇa*****! I am completely under the control of my devotees. I am not at all independent. My heart is controlled by the pure devotees. What to speak of my devotee, even those who are devotees of my devotee are very dear to me.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord establishes that he is controlled completely by the devotees in six verses. He makes a promise in this verse. My heart is completely controlled (*grasta*) by devotees who have given up duplicity (*sādhubhiḥ*), who are devoid of even the desire for liberation. I have great affection for persons are devotees of the devotees. What to speak of the devotees themselves.

### Text 64

*nāham ātmānam āśāse mad-bhaktaiḥ sādhubhir vinā*  
*śriyaṁ cātyantikīṁ brahman yeṣāṁ gatir ahaṁ parā*

**Перевод:**

**O** ***brāhmaṇa*****! Without the devotees, who take shelter of only me, I do not desire to enjoy my own bliss by my six great qualities.**

**Комментарий:**

In five verses he establishes this. He shows that the bliss he experiences in his svarūpa is secondary to realizing the bliss the devotee experiences. This bliss of the devotee is the essence of the hlādinī-śakti.

### Text 65

*ye dārāgāra-putrāpta- prāṇān vittam imaṁ param*  
*hitvā māṁ śaraṇaṁ yātāḥ kathaṁ tāṁs tyaktum utsahe*

**Перевод:**

**Since pure devotees give up their homes, wives, children, relatives, riches and even their lives simply to serve me, without any desire for material improvement in this life or in the next, how can I give up such devotees at any time?**

**Комментарий:**

Showing the devotees pitiable condition, he must show gratitude. They even reject sālokya etc. (*param*).

### Text 66

*mayi nirbaddha-hṛdayāḥ sādhavaḥ sama-darśanāḥ*  
*vaśe kurvanti māṁ bhaktyā sat-striyaḥ sat-patiṁ yathā*

**Перевод:**

**As chaste women bring their gentle husbands under control by service, the pure devotees, who see others’ suffering as their own and are completely attached to me in the core of the heart, bring me under their full control.**

**Комментарий:**

He establishes the position of both by an example. Because their hearts are attached to me (*nirbaddha-hṛdayāḥ*), these devotees who are devoid of cheating (*sādhavaḥ*), who are devoid of raga and dveṣa (*sama-darṣanāḥ*), control me by bhakti. The example shows bhakti with prominence of friendship.

### Text 67

*mat-sevayā pratītaṁ te sālokyādi-catuṣṭayam*  
*necchanti sevayā pūrṇāḥ kuto 'nyat kāla-viplutam*

**Перевод:**

**My devotees, who are always satisfied to be engaged in my service, are not interested even in the four principles of liberation \[sālokya, sārūpya, sāmīpya and sārṣṭi\], although these are automatically achieved by their service. What then is to be said of any perishable happiness?**

**Комментарий:**

He shows the extreme sacrifice of the devotees. Sālokya etc. are not perishable. What to speak of perishable happiness?

### Text 68

*sādhavo hṛdayaṁ mahyaṁ sādhūnāṁ hṛdayaṁ tv aham*  
*mad-anyat te na jānanti nāhaṁ tebhyo manāg api*

**Перевод:**

**The pure devotee is my very heart: what pains him pains me. I am the heart of the pure devotee: what pleases him pleases me. My devotees do not know anything else but me, and I do not know more than they do.**

**Комментарий:**

Mutually the Lord and devotee share their hearts.

## Chapter Five

### Text 2

*tasya sodyamam āvīkṣya pāda-sparśa-vilajjitaḥ*  
*astāvīt tad dharer astraṁ kṛpayā pīḍito bhṛśam*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Durvāsā attempting to praise him, Mahārāja Ambarīṣa was ashamed and, seeing him suffering, was greatly aggrieved. Thus he offered prayers to the weapon of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

*Saḥ udyamam* becomes *sodyamam* according to *so ‘ci lope cet pāda-pūraṇam* (Pāṇini 61.134). Also *lopaḥ śākalaysa* (Pāṇini 8.3.19) and *pūrvatrāsiddham* (Pāṇini 8.2.1) should be considered.

### Text 7

*tvat-tejasā dharma-mayena saṁhṛtaṁ*  
*tamaḥ prakāśaś ca dṛśo mahātmanām*  
*duratyayas te mahimā girāṁ pate*  
*tvad-rūpam etat sad-asat parāvaram*

**Перевод:**

**O master of speech! By your effulgence, composed of** ***dharma,*** **the darkness of the world is dissipated, and the knowledge belonging to great souls is manifested. No one can surpass your effulgence. All things, manifested and unmanifested, superior and inferior, are but your various forms.**

**Комментарий:**

By your effulgence composed of dharma, the darkness is seen (*dṛśaḥ*) to be dissipated and the manifestation of the sun and other great things (*mahātmanām*) occurs. Your greatness cannot be surpassed since this world of sat and asat is your manifestation.

### Text 9

*sa tvaṁ jagat-trāṇa khala-prahāṇaye*  
*nirūpitaḥ sarva-saho gadā-bhṛtā*  
*viprasya cāsmat-kula-daiva-hetave*  
*vidhehi bhadraṁ tad anugraho hi naḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O protector of the universe! As the form of all strength, you are engaged by the Supreme Lord in killing the criminals. For the benefit of our entire dynasty, as a favor to us, favor this poor** ***brāhmaṇa.***

**Комментарий:**

You are engaged by the Lord to kill in a decisive manner (*prahānaye*) the criminals.

### Text 22

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*evaṁ saṅkīrtya rājānaṁ durvāsāḥ paritoṣitaḥ*  
*yayau vihāyasāmantrya brahmalokam ahaitukam*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: Thus being satisfied, Durvāsā took permission and left, continuously glorifying the King. Through the sky, he went to Brahmaloka, which is devoid of dry reasoning.**

**Комментарий:**

*Ahaitukam* means “devoid of materialistic persons who interpret the Vedas by dry logic.”

### Text 23

*saṁvatsaro 'tyagāt tāvad yāvatā nāgato gataḥ*  
*munis tad-darśanākāṅkṣo rājāb-bhakṣo babhūva ha*

**Перевод:**

**During the time that Durvāsā Muni had left and had not returned—for one complete year—the King, desiring to see him again, had fasted, having drunken water previously.**

**Комментарий:**

The king, having drunken water previously, remained there anxious to see the sage. It does not mean that he only drank water at that time.

### Text 24

*gate 'tha durvāsasi so 'mbarīṣo*  
*dvijopayogātipavitram āharat*  
*ṛṣer vimokṣaṁ vyasanaṁ ca vīkṣya*  
*mene sva-vīryaṁ ca parānubhāvam*

**Перевод:**

**After one year, when Durvāsā Muni had returned, King Ambarīṣa ate pure food remnants of a** ***brāhmaṇa.*** **When the King saw that the** ***brāhmaṇa*** **Durvāsā had been released from the great danger of being burned, he understood that his power was the Lord’s power alone.**

**Комментарий:**

He ate food pure because it was eaten by brāhmaṇas who were devotees, with whom the Lord is very pleased.

### Text 26

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*athāmbarīṣas tanayeṣu rājyaṁ*  
*samāna-śīleṣu visṛjya dhīraḥ*  
*vanaṁ viveśātmani vāsudeve*  
*mano dadhad dhvasta-guṇa-pravāhaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: Thereafter, the learned Mahārāja Ambarīṣa, having destroyed the agitation of the** ***guṇas*****, divided his kingdom among his sons, who were equally qualified, and entered the forest to concentrate his mind fully upon Lord Vāsudeva.**

**Комментарий:**

Worrying that further such incidents would occur, seeing the qualification of his sons, giving the responsibility to them, he concentrated his mind on the Lord’s service in the forest.

## Chapter Six

### Text 10

*sa tu vipreṇa saṁvādaṁ jñāpakena samācaran*  
*tyaktvā kalevaraṁ yogī sa tenāvāpa yat param*

**Перевод:**

**Instituting the instructions given by Vasiṣṭha, Mahārāja Ikṣvāku became renounced and achieved the supreme perfection after giving up his material body.**

**Комментарий:**

*Babhūva* should be supplied after *samācaran*. Another version has *samācarat*. He attained that which is the supreme truth (*param*).

### Text 11

*pitary uparate 'bhyetya vikukṣiḥ pṛthivīm imām*  
*śāsad īje hariṁ yajñaiḥ śaśāda iti viśrutaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**After his father's disappearance, Vikukṣi returned to the country and thus became the king, ruling the planet earth and worshipped the Lord by performing various sacrifices. Vikukṣi later became celebrated as Saśāda (rabbit eater).**

**Комментарий:**

By the power of performing worship of the Lord, he overcame the fault of rejecting his father.

### Text 25

*bahulāśvo nikumbhasya kṛśāśvo 'thāsya senajit*  
*yuvanāśvo 'bhavat tasya so 'napatyo vanaṁ gataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Nikumbha was Bahulāśva, the son of Bahulāśva was Kṛśāśva, the son of Kṛśāśva was Senajit, and the son of Senajit was Yuvanāśva. Yuvanāśva had no sons, and thus he retired from family life and went to the forest.**

**Комментарий:**

Nikumbha’s son was Bahulāśva. Bahulāśva’s son was Kṛśāśva.

### Text 51

*saṅgaṁ tyajeta mithuna-vratīnāṁ mumukṣuḥ*  
*sarvātmanā na visṛjed bahir-indriyāṇi*  
*ekaś caran rahasi cittam ananta īśe*  
*yuñjīta tad-vratiṣu sādhuṣu cet prasaṅgaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**A person desiring liberation must give up the association of persons interested in sex life and should not employ his external senses. He should always stay in a secluded place, completely fixing his mind at the lotus feet of the unlimited Lord-- if there is association with devotees also engaged in meditation on the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

When will that person acting in that way fix his mind on the Lord? It will happen if he has association with devotees similarly engaged.

### Text 54

*tatra taptvā tapas tīkṣṇam ātma-darśanam ātmavān*  
*sahaivāgnibhir ātmānaṁ yuyoja paramātmani*

**Перевод:**

**Saubhari, knowing the self, performed severe penances in the forest for realizing the self. He gave up his body along with the fires and attained the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

He united his body along with the fires in the Lord. He thought of everything- self and relations—as following the Lord.

## Chapter Seven

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*māndhātuḥ putra-pravaro yo 'mbarīṣaḥ prakīrtitaḥ*  
*pitāmahena pravṛto yauvanāśvas tu tat-sutaḥ*  
*hārītas tasya putro 'bhūn māndhātṛ-pravarā ime*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: The most prominent among the sons of Māndhātā was he who is celebrated as Ambarīṣa. Ambarīṣa was accepted as a son by his grandfather Yuvanāśva. Ambarīṣa's son was Yauvanāśva, and Yauvanāśva's son was Hārīta. In Māndhātā's dynasty, Ambarīṣa, Hārīta and Yauvanāśva were very prominent.**

**Комментарий:**

Three lines go together. This is the version seen in the Gauḍa provinces. Ambarīṣa’s son was Yauvanāśva. Viṣṇu Purāṇa also explains that Yuvanāśva (grandfather of Ambarīṣa) gave birth to Māndhātā. However Viṣṇu Purāṇa also says that Ambarīṣa’s son was Yuvanāśva.

*ambarīṣasya māndhātṛ-tanayasya yuvanāśvaḥ putro’bhūt*  
*tasmād dhāritaḥ, yato’ṅgirasā hārīta*

**Yuvanāśva was the son of Ambarīṣa, who was the son of Māndhātā. From Yuvanāśva came Hārīta. And from Hārīta came Aṅgirasā. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa)**

Śrīdhara Svāmī says that *pravṛta* means “what is accepted by the sages.” The sages accepted Ambarīṣa, Yuvanāśva and Hārīta along with the grandfather as the same line.

### Text 4

*trasaddasyuḥ paurukutso yo 'naraṇyasya deha-kṛt*  
*haryaśvas tat-sutas tasmāt prāruṇo 'tha tribandhanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Purukutsa was Trasaddasyu, who was the father of Anaraṇya. Anaraṇya's son was Haryaśva, the father of Prāruṇa. Prāruṇa was the father of Tribandhana.**

**Комментарий:**

Trasaddasyu came n Māndhātā’s line.

### Text 8

*so 'napatyo viṣaṇṇātmā nāradasyopadeśataḥ*  
*varuṇaṁ śaraṇaṁ yātaḥ putro me jāyatāṁ prabho*

**Перевод:**

**Hariścandra had no son and was therefore extremely morose. Once, therefore, following the advice of Nārada, he took shelter of Varuṇa and said to him "My lord! Please give me a son."**

**Комментарий:**

The story of Hariścandra is told to show the excellent nature of the Lord’s will and the devotee. That is understood for other stories also.

### Text 24

*satyaṁ sāraṁ dhṛtiṁ dṛṣṭvā sabhāryasya ca bhūpateḥ*  
*viśvāmitro bhṛśaṁ prīto dadāv avihatāṁ gatim*

**Перевод:**

**The great sage Viśvāmitra, seeing the fortitude and essence of truth in Mahārāja Hariścandra and his wife, gave them imperishable knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

Absorption in Vedic rites produces merging in Brahman, not a relation with the Lord.

## Chapter Eight

### Text 4

*ājñāyāsyai sapatnībhir garo datto 'ndhasā saha*  
*saha tenaiva sañjātaḥ sagarākhyo mahā-yaśāḥ*  
*sagaraś cakravarty āsīt sāgaro yat-sutaiḥ kṛtaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing that she was pregnant, the co-wives of the wife of Bāhuka gave her poison with her food. The famous son was born along with the poison. Therefore he became famous as Sagara, "one who is born with poison". Sagara later became the emperor. His sons dug up the ocean.**

**Комментарий:**

The last line continues into the next verses.

### Text 5-6

*yas tālajaṅghān yavanāñ chakān haihaya-barbarān*  
*nāvadhīd guru-vākyena cakre vikṛta-veṣiṇaḥ*  
*muṇḍāñ chmaśru-dharān kāṁścin mukta-keśārdha-muṇḍitān*  
*anantar-vāsasaḥ kāṁścid abahir-vāsaso 'parān*

**Перевод:**

**Sagara Mahārāja did not kill uncivilized men like the Tālajaṅghas, Yavanas, Śakas, Haihayas and Barbaras. Instead, following the order of his** ***guru*** **Aurva, he made dress some of them awkwardly, he shaved some of them clean but allowed them to wear mustaches, he left some of them wearing loose hair, he half-shaved some, he left some without underwear, and he left some without external garments.**

**Комментарий:**

Śrīdhara Svāmī does not accept *nāvadhīt* but rather *avadhīt* (he killed). Thus he explains that some were not killed but disfigured. Parāśara says *prāyaśas ca haihayaṁ jaghāna*: he killed most of the Haihayas. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa) Viṣṇu Purāṇa follows the words of Vasiṣṭha, the words of guru.

### Text 9-10

*prāg-udīcyāṁ diśi hayaṁ dadṛśuḥ kapilāntike*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, in the northeastern direction, they saw the horse near the hermitage of Kapila. "Here is the man who has stolen the horse," they said. "He is staying there with closed eyes. This sinful man should be killed!" The sixty thousand sons of Sagara, raising their weapons, attacked the sage. The sage opened his eyes.**

**Комментарий:**

In the north east, they wandered to Gaṅgā-sāgara, on the edge of the ocean, towards the south. Or Kapila had wandered to the northeast.

## Chapter Nine

### Text 5

*kiṁ cāhaṁ na bhuvaṁ yāsye narā mayy āmṛjanty agham*  
*mṛjāmi tad aghaṁ kvāhaṁ rājaṁs tatra vicintyatām*

**Перевод:**

**O King, I do not wish to go down to the planet earth, for there the people will wash their sins into my water. How will I wash away those sins? You must consider some method of washing away those sins.**

**Комментарий:**

They will destroy (*āmṛjanti*) their sin in me or through me. Sin means the suffering of the mind born from not having a taste for associating with the Lord. The Gaṅgā is famous for destroying millions of serious sins. *Agham* means anxiety due to misfortunes of suffering.

### Text 6

*śrī-bhagīratha uvāca*  
*sādhavo nyāsinaḥ śāntā brahmiṣṭhā loka-pāvanāḥ*  
*haranty aghaṁ te 'ṅga-saṅgāt teṣv āste hy agha-bhid dhariḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Bhagīratha said: The peaceful devotees without material attachments, fixed in the Lord, who are purifiers of the world, will remove the sin from your water by bathing in it. The Lord, the destroyer of sin, will destroy the sins that the devotees accumulate.**

**Комментарий:**

Those who are fixed in scriptural conduct according to qualification (*sādhavaḥ*), unattached to results of karma (*nyāsinaḥ*), endowed with pure hearts (*śāntaḥ*), expert at understanding the Vedas (*brahma-niṣṭhāḥ*), purifiers of the world, will remove the sin. The reason for these qualities is that the Lord, who destroys all sins, (*agha-bhit*) exists in them. You will not have suffering because of association at all times with these devotees, by seeing these devotees.

### Text 19

*śrī-rājovāca*  
*kiṁ nimitto guroḥ śāpaḥ saudāsasya mahātmanaḥ*  
*etad veditum icchāmaḥ kathyatāṁ na raho yadi*

**Перевод:**

**King Parīkṣit said: Why did Vasiṣṭha, the** ***guru*** **of Saudāsa, curse that great soul? I wish to know of this. If it is not secret, please describe it to me.**

**Комментарий:**

It should not be revealed (*rahaḥ*) if it makes Vasiṣṭha appear at fault.

### Text 20-21

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*saudāso mṛgayāṁ kiñcic caran rakṣo jaghāna ha*  
*mumoca bhrātaraṁ so 'tha gataḥ praticikīrṣayā*  
*sañcintayann aghaṁ rājñaḥ sūda-rūpa-dharo gṛhe*  
*gurave bhoktu-kāmāya paktvā ninye narāmiṣam*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: Once Saudāsa, while hunting, killed a Rākṣasa but released its brother. That brother, however, departed with the idea of taking revenge. Thinking to harm the King, he became the cook at the King's house. One day, the King's** ***guru,*** **Vasiṣṭha Muni, was invited for dinner, and the Rākṣasa cook served him human flesh.**

**Комментарий:**

The demon entered the cook (*suda-rūpa-dharaḥ*) since he was not familiar with cooking.

### Text 44

*na bālye 'pi matir mahyam adharme ramate kvacit*  
*nāpaśyam uttamaślokād anyat kiñcana vastv aham*

**Перевод:**

**I was never attracted, even in my childhood, to irreligious principles. I did not find any object acceptable except the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

I did not think of anyone except the Supreme Lord. I did not worship devatās. He was busy protecting the devatās and mankind.

### Text 48

*iti vyavasito buddhyā nārāyaṇa-gṛhītayā*  
*hitvānya-bhāvam ajñānaṁ tataḥ svaṁ bhāvam āsthitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Deciding in this way, while giving up ignorance through intelligence absorbed in Nārāyaṇa, he became situated in the mood of surrender.**

**Комментарий:**

His intelligence was absorbed in Nārāyaṇa (*nārāyaṇa-gṛhitayā*).

*tatra mām anumoderan devās tri-bhuvaneśvarāḥ*  
*muhūrtena brahma-lokaṁ khaṭvāṅgaḥ samasādhayat*

**Thus may the presiding** ***devatās*** **of these three worlds kindly show their mercy upon me. Mahārāja Khaṭvāṅga was able to achieve the spiritual world in a single moment. (SB** [**11.23.30**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/23.30)**)**

## Chapter Ten

### Text 4

*gurv-arthe tyakta-rājyo vyacarad anuvanaṁ padma-padbhyāṁ priyāyāḥ*  
*pāṇi-sparśākṣamābhyāṁ mṛjita-patha-rujo yo harīndrānujābhyām*  
*vairūpyāc chūrpaṇakhyāḥ priya-viraha-ruṣāropita-bhrū-vijṛmbha-*  
*trastābdhir baddha-setuḥ khala-dava-dahanaḥ kosalendro 'vatān naḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Rāmacandra, King of Kosala, gave up the kingdom to keep his father’s promise and walked from forest to forest on his lotus feet which were so delicate they could not tolerate the touch of Sitā’s hand. His fatigue while walking was relieved by Hanumān and Lakṣmaṇa. He created fear in the ocean by moving his eyebrow in anger caused by separation from Sitā due his severing Sūrpaṇakha’s nose and ear, and built a bridge over the ocean. May Rāmacandra, who burned the forest of demons, protect us!**

**Комментарий:**

Another version has *patha-rujā.*

### Text 11

*rakṣo-'dhamena vṛkavad vipine 'samakṣaṁ*  
*vaideha-rāja-duhitary apayāpitāyām*  
*bhrātrā vane kṛpaṇavat priyayā viyuktaḥ*  
*strī-saṅgināṁ gatim iti prathayaṁś cacāra*

**Перевод:**

**When Sītā was stolen by wolf-like Rāvaṇa in the forest in Rāma’s absence, Rāma, feeling separation from his wife like a suffering man, wandered in the forest with his brother, announcing to the world the result of association with women.**

**Комментарий:**

He showed the activities of sorrow etc. from association with women. This acted as a stimulus to give mercy to the highest devotees. Separated from her, he wandered about in the forest. It will be said in the next verse that he found out where she was from the monkeys.

### Text 12

*dagdhvātma-kṛtya-hata-kṛtyam ahan kabandhaṁ*  
*sakhyaṁ vidhāya kapibhir dayitā-gatiṁ taiḥ*  
*buddhvātha vālini hate plavagendra-sainyair*  
*velām agāt sa manujo 'ja-bhavārcitāṅghriḥ*

**Перевод:**

**After performing the last rites for Jatāyu who acted on Rāma’s behalf, killing the demon Kabandha, making friends with the monkeys, killing Vāli, and understanding where Sītā was from the monkeys, Rāma, whose feet are worshipped by Brahmā and Śiva, went to the shore of the ocean.**

**Комментарий:**

*Sam-anujaḥ* (instead of *sa manujaḥ*) can mean Rāma who was accompanied by his younger brother Lakṣmaṇa.

### Text 30

*tato dadarśa bhagavān aśoka-vanikāśrame*  
*kṣāmāṁ sva-viraha-vyādhiṁ śiṁśapā-mūlam-āśritām*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, Rāmacandra saw Sītā sitting in a small cottage beneath a** ***śiṁśapa*** **tree in a forest of Aśoka trees. She was thin, aggrieved because of separation from Rāma.**

**Комментарий:**

Rāma saw Sītā.

### Text 39-40

*pāduke nyasya purataḥ prāñjalir bāṣpa-locanaḥ*  
*tam āśliṣya ciraṁ dorbhyāṁ snāpayan netrajair jalaiḥ*  
*rāmo lakṣmaṇa-sītābhyāṁ viprebhyo ye 'rha-sattamāḥ*  
*tebhyaḥ svayaṁ namaścakre prajābhiś ca namaskṛtaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**After placing the wooden shoes before Rāmacandra, Bharata stood with folded hands, his eyes full of tears, and Rāmacandra bathed Bharata with tears while embracing him with both arms for a long time. Accompanied by Sītā and Lakṣmaṇa, Rāmacandra then offered his respectful obeisances to the learned** ***brāhmaṇas*****, who were most respectable. The citizens of Ayodhyā offered their respectful obeisances to the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Bharata had tears in his eyes (*bāspa-locanaḥ*).

## Chapter Eleven

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*bhagavān ātmanātmānaṁ rāma uttama-kalpakaiḥ*  
*sarva-devamayaṁ devam īje 'thācāryavān makhaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: The Supreme Lord, Rāmacandra, accepted an** ***ācārya*** **and performed sacrifices with the best paraphernalia. Thus he worshiped himself, by himself.**

**Комментарий:**

He worshipped himself by himself to set an example for other people.

### Text 8

*kadācil loka-jijñāsur gūḍho rātryām alakṣitaḥ*  
*caran vāco 'śṛṇod rāmo bhāryām uddiśya kasyacit*

**Перевод:**

**Once while Rāmacandra was walking at night incognito to learn the opinion of the people, he heard a man speaking about his wife, Sītādevī.**

**Комментарий:**

What he saw is described, to begin his pastime of renouncing the mortal world, becoming detached from everything. The “someone” was a devious person who suddenly came and lived there.

### Text 10

*iti lokād bahu-mukhād durārādhyād asaṁvidaḥ*  
*patyā bhītena sā tyaktā prāptā prācetasāśramam*

**Перевод:**

**Fearing ignorant men who gossip and are difficult to control, Rāmacandra abandoned Sītā, who then went to the hermitage of Vālmīki.**

**Комментарий:**

He was worried about people from other states (*lokāt*) who would gossip.

### Text 16

*tac chrutvā bhagavān rāmo rundhann api dhiyā śucaḥ*  
*smaraṁs tasyā guṇāṁs tāṁs tān nāśaknod roddhum īśvaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**After hearing the news of mother Sītā's entering the earth, the Supreme Lord Rāma, though capable of checking his grief by intelligence, on remembering her good qualities he could not do so, though he was the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

His inner feelings are revealed to confirm what was previously said: he wanted to broadcast the miseries arising from association with women. (SB 9.10.11) Though he was the Lord he acted in this way. Such was the greatness of her qualities of bhakti.

### Text 17

*strī-puṁ-prasaṅga etādṛk sarvatra trāsam-āvahaḥ*  
*apīśvarāṇāṁ kim uta grāmyasya gṛha-cetasaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Such attraction between man and woman is a cause of** ***saṁsāra*** **even for Brahmā and others in this life and the next*****.*** **What to speak of the common people who are attached to their houses?**

**Комментарий:**

Does one see such results in relationships between men and women elsewhere? Such attraction between Rāma and Sītā produces fear of *saṁsāra (trāsam*) everywhere—now in the next world, even for persons perfected in karma yoga etc. (*īśvarānām*). Naturally for common people (*grāmyasya*), this is the impression. But their bhava was the essence of prema, the highest puruṣārtha.

Or the ordinary relationship of men and women, thought to be similar to theirs, produces fear of saṁsāra*. Pum* with no ending is poetic license. *Trāsam* should be in the genitive but that is absent as poetic license.

### Text 18

*tata ūrdhvaṁ brahmacaryaṁ dhāryann ajuhot prabhuḥ*  
*trayodaśābda-sāhasram agnihotram akhaṇḍitam*

**Перевод:**

**After mother Sītā entered the earth, Rāmacandra observed complete celibacy and performed an uninterrupted sacrifice for thirteen thousand years.**

**Комментарий:**

Rāma’s attachment to Sītā, through external actions, is shown. Observing continuous (*ūrdhvam*) brahmacarya, out of despondency in her absence, he performed sacrifices continuously for thirteen thousand years.

### Text 19

*smaratāṁ hṛdi vinyasya viddhaṁ daṇḍaka-kaṇṭakaiḥ*  
*sva-pāda-pallavaṁ rāma ātma-jyotir agāt tataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Rāmacandra, whose lotus feet were sometimes pierced by thorns when he lived in Daṇḍakāraṇya, placed those lotus feet in the hearts of those who thought of him. Then he entered his own abode.**

**Комментарий:**

After that, giving up the human world filled with rumors causing his separation, he ascended to his own world filled with eternal union. He appeared personally and constantly with great prema to those who remained there with a desire to protect the world. His feet appeared to be pierced, perceived by their prema (*viddham*) in this manner:

*calasi yad vrajāc cārayan paśūn*  
*nalina-sundaraṁ nātha te padam*  
*śila-tṛṇāṅkuraiḥ sīdatīti naḥ*  
*kalilatāṁ manaḥ kānta gacchati*

**Dear master, dear lover, when you leave the cowherd village to herd the cows, our minds are disturbed with the thought that your feet, more beautiful than a lotus, will be pricked by the spiked husks of grain and the rough grass and plants. (SB** [**10.21.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/21.11)**)**

They remembered how he observed the orders given by his father (which caused many problems). He went to his spiritual planet, a form of light (*ātma-jyotiḥ*).

### Text 20

*nedaṁ yaśo raghupateḥ sura-yācñayātta-*  
*līlā-tanor adhika-sāmya-vimukta-dhāmnaḥ*  
*rakṣo-vadho jaladhi-bandhanam astra-pūgaiḥ*  
*kiṁ tasya śatru-hanane kapayaḥ sahāyāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Rāmacandra's reputation for having killed Rāvaṇa with showers of arrows at the request of the** ***devatās*** **and for having built a bridge over the ocean does not constitute the factual glory of Rāma, whose spiritual body is always engaged in various pastimes and who has no equal or superior. He had no need to take help from the monkeys to gain victory over Rāvaṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Four verses recollect the special qualities of Rāma.

### Text 21

*yasyāmalaṁ nṛpa-sadaḥsu yaśo 'dhunāpi*  
*gāyanty agha-ghnam ṛṣayo dig-ibhendra-paṭṭam*  
*taṁ nākapāla-vasupāla-kirīṭa-juṣṭa-*  
*pādāmbujaṁ raghupatiṁ śaraṇaṁ prapadye*

**Перевод:**

**In the assembly of kings, even today, the sages glorify Rāma’s spotless fame, which destroys all sin and which spreads in all directions. I surrender to Rāma, whose lotus feet are served by the crowns of the** ***devatās*** **and earthly kings.**

**Комментарий:**

Remembering the sweetness of his pastimes on earth, even jīvanmuktas sing his glories in great bliss. In relation to Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes it is said:

*sthity-udbhavāntaṁ bhuvana-trayasya yaḥ*  
*samīhite ’nanta-guṇaḥ sva-līlayā*  
*na tasya citraṁ para-pakṣa-nigrahas*  
*tathāpi martyānuvidhasya varṇyate*

**For him who orchestrates the creation, maintenance and destruction of the three worlds and who possesses unlimited spiritual qualities, it is hardly amazing that he subdues insignificant opponents because he is playing a human role in his pastimes. (SB** [**10.50.29**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/50.29)**)**

### Text 22

*sa yaiḥ spṛṣṭo 'bhidṛṣṭo vā saṁviṣṭo 'nugato 'pi vā*  
*kosalās te yayuḥ sthānam yatra gacchanti yoginaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The inhabitants of Kosala, who touched, saw, sat together with Rāma in friendship and followed Rāma, went to Vaikuṇṭha where the devotees go.**

**Комментарий:**

What to speak of Sītā meeting the Lord again, all the inhabitants of Kosala went to the place where the perfected devotees go (*yoginaḥ*). People of Kosala includes others as well.

## Chapter Thirteen

### Text 8

*rājño jīvatu deho 'yaṁ prasannāḥ prabhavo yadi*  
*tathety ukte nimiḥ prāha mā bhūn me deha-bandhanam*

**Перевод:**

**"If you are satisfied with this sacrifice and if you are actually able to do so, kindly bring Mahārāja Nimi’s body back to life." The** ***devatās*** **agreed, but Mahārāja Nimi said, "Please do not imprison me again in a material body."**

**Комментарий:**

He spoke by the power of yoga in his subtle body.

### Text 9

*yasya yogaṁ na vāñchanti viyoga-bhaya-kātarāḥ*  
*bhajanti caraṇāmbhojaṁ munayo hari-medhasaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The sages who recognize the Lord, suffering fear of separation from the body, therefore do not desire to attain a material body. Rather they worship the Lord’s lotus feet.**

**Комментарий:**

Being disturbed with the fear that again they will be separated from the body, the sages do not desire getting even a body (*yoga*), what to speak of the variety of happiness with birth and relatives. Rather they worship the Lord’s lotus feet.

### Text 10

*dehaṁ nāvarurutse 'haṁ duḥkha-śoka-bhayāvaham*  
*sarvatrāsya yato mṛtyur matsyānām udake yathā*

**Перевод:**

**I do not wish to accept a material body, for such a body is the source of all distress, lamentation and fear everywhere in the universe, just a fish in water will die from other aquatics.**

**Комментарий:**

“In worshipping the Lord’s feet, they show desire for happiness of service. Service is not possible without a body. Do they not desire a body suitable for that service?”

No. I do not desire a body which is subject to sorrow and lamentation and fear. This means he desires a body of an associate, filled with eternal bliss and suitable for the highest service to the Lord.

### Text 11

*devā ūcuḥ*  
*videha uṣyatāṁ kāmaṁ locaneṣu śarīriṇām*  
*unmeṣaṇa-nimeṣābhyāṁ lakṣito 'dhyātma-saṁsthitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***devatās*** **said: Let Mahārāja Nimi live without a gross body, and, being situated in a subtle body, let him be manifest or unmanifest to common materially embodied people as he wishes.**

**Комментарий:**

The devas solved the problem. The sages wanted his body to be brought back to life. The king refused this. They were able to bestow a boon so that his desire could be fulfilled.

### Text 12

*arājaka-bhayaṁ nṝṇāṁ manyamānā maharṣayaḥ*  
*dehaṁ mamanthuḥ sma nimeḥ kumāraḥ samajāyata*

**Перевод:**

**Thereafter, understanding the dangerous situation for the people without a king, the sages churned Mahārāja Nimi's material body, from which, as a result, a son was born.**

**Комментарий:**

They did something beyond their own statement of giving him a subtle body.

## Chapter Fourteen

### Text 1

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*athātaḥ śrūyatāṁ rājan vaṁśaḥ somasya pāvanaḥ*  
*yasminn ailādayo bhūpāḥ kīrtyante puṇya-kīrtayaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī: O King, thus far you have heard the description of the dynasty of the sun-god. Now hear the purifying description of the dynasty of the moon-god, in which famous kings like Aila (Purūravā) are glorified.**

**Комментарий:**

*Athātaḥ* is similar to the use in *athāto brahma jijñāsa*: after hearing the Pūrva-mīmāṁsa, hear about Brahman. (Brahma-sūtra [1.1.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/vs/1.1.1)). To begin the story of vaṁśa in which Kṛṣṇa, Svayam Bhagavān appeared, you should hear about the pure dynasty of the moon, who sustains that dynasty, for auspiciousness and for understanding the intermediate causes and details. Soma can also mean “Śiva along with Umā.”

*yac-chauca-niḥsṛta-sarit-pravarodakena*  
*tīrthena mūrdhny adhikṛtena śivaḥ śivo 'bhūt*  
*dhyātur manaḥ-śamala-śaila-nisṛṣṭa-vajraṁ*  
*dhyāyec ciraṁ bhagavataś caraṇāravindam*

**One should meditate continually upon the Lord’s lotus feet from which the Gaṅgā flows and gives extra blessings to Lord Śiva on his head, and whose thunderbolt is released upon mountains of sin in the meditator’s mind. (SB** [**3.28.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/28.22)**)**

Or hear about the dynasty purifying for Śiva and Umā, since Svayam Bhagavān appeared in it. Things related to that are also most excellent by nature, such as the appearance of Aila.

### Text 20-21

### Text 23

*yayātir anabhipretaṁ daivopahṛtam ātmanaḥ*  
*manas tu tad-gataṁ buddhvā pratijagrāha tad-vacaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**King Yayāti did not like it, but because it was arranged by providence and because he considered that his mind was absorbed in** ***dharma,*** **he accepted her request.**

**Комментарий:**

He thought, “She has said it is the will of the Lord. What sin is there in that? Will it not produce adharma, since it encourages the seed of anartha? Since it does not contradict the curse of Kaca, it is not a cause of removing the adharma. How did his behavior arise? ” His mind was absorbed in behavior which took shelter of the Lord.

This arrangement was the desire of the Lord (*daiva*). My mind should not absorbed in actions which yield sinful results. Since I take shelter of the Lord, the Lord would not let adharma happen. My mind also does not enter into such actions. I always consider this my nature. However, since this has happened, it is desire following the independent will of the Lord, and must be considered proper.

*na karma bandhanaṁ janma vaiṣṇavānāṁ ca vidyate*

**The vaiṣṇavas are not bound by karma or birth. (Padma Purāṇa)**

To go against this will is a fault:

*jānāmi dharmaṁ na ca me pravṛttir*  
*jānāmy adharmaṁ na ca me nivṛttiḥ \|*  
*kenāpi devena hṛdi-sthitena*  
*yathā niyukto’smi tathā karomi \|\|*

**I know dharma, but I have no inclination. I know adharma, and do not have an inclination to give it up. I will do as the Lord, situated in my heart, engages me. (Gautamīya-tantra)**

It is not a fault to do this as it is for others, since I am fixed in the Lord.

### Text 48

### Text 49

*yasminn idaṁ viracitaṁ vyomnīva jaladāvaliḥ*  
*nāneva bhāti nābhāti svapna-māyā-manorathaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**In the Lord, this universe is created and is manifested with variety like a cloud in the sky. Then it disappears. The universe is temporary like a dream, an illusion or a desire.**

**Комментарий:**

The universe (*idam*) is produced by the Lord, the shelter of all things. By him the creation manifests variety (*nānā*). The variety does not exist (*na ābhāti*) since it is one with the cause, and since it enters the Lord as the cause. *Nābhāti* means it is not manifested externally. Similar to the universe appearing and disappearing are dreams, illusions and desires (*svapna-māyā-manorathaḥ*) since everything is under the Lord’s shelter. The singular number is used in the compound to express one unit. Because it does not express a collection it is not neuter. The rule is *ū-kāro’c hrasva-dīrgha-plutaḥ.* (Pāṇini 1.2.27)

### Text 50

*tam eva hṛdi vinyasya vāsudevaṁ guhāśayam*  
*nārāyaṇam aṇīyāṁsaṁ nirāśīr ayajat prabhum*

**Перевод:**

**Placing in his heart Vāsudeva, the knower of all** ***jīvas*****, who dwells in the hearts of all, but who is difficult to know, King Yayāti, without desire, worshipped the Lord, his master.**

**Комментарий:**

Vāsudeva means he lives everywhere. Thus there was no effort in worship him, since he dwells in the antaḥkaraṇa (*guhāśayam*). Moreover he inspires all jīvas (*nārāyaṇam*) and is difficult to understand (*anīyaṁsam)*. But still he placed him in his heart with devotion. This means he appeared in his mind. Then he worshipped him. He attained ability gradually by his worship (*prabhuḥ*). This was because he was without material desires (*nirāśīḥ*).

### Text 7

**Комментарий:**

Previously it was said that he saw the Lord everywhere. Now the words are repeated to show his compassion. If he saw the Lord everywhere just as antaryāmī, since there would be no vision of jīvas, compassion for them would be absent. Therefore, he saw the Lord everywhere, as being most compassionate. Being touched by the Lord’s mood, he became compassionate. After the Lord appeared along with Brahmā and Śiva, his devotees presiding over the guṇas, the king’s devotion for the Lord is finally shown.

*vāsudeve bhagavati bhaktyā cakre manaḥ param*

**King Rantideva, with no ambition to enjoy material benefits, offered them obeisances. Free of all material desires, he fixed his superior mind with devotion on the Supreme Lord Vāsudeva. (SB** [**9.21.16**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-9/21.16)**)**

Compassion for jīva’s alone would be an obstacle as in the case of Bharata. He had no respect for liberation:

*na kāmaye ’haṁ gatim īśvarāt parām*  
*aṣṭarddhi-yuktām apunar-bhavaṁ vā*

(SB** [**9.21.12**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-9/21.12)**)**

Thus it is not proper that he would perform bhakti that would show only the quality of compassion for jīvas.

Having received this surety, please come out. You will not be overcome by the Lord’s māyā. Please immediately show your face, dear child!”**

You are the guarantee, now standing outside the womb. The whole universe up to Brahmā is bound by your māyā. You alone destroy the suffering of material existence.”**  
**Sūta said “Hearing his words the Lord spoke while gently smiling.”**  
**The Lord said, “You will not have connection with māyā. O fetus whose limbs are covered by yoga! Come out without worry. You will now be liberated by my mercy. While living you will attain the happiness of liberation which the sages describe. I arrange that suffering will end and not return.”**  
**When he finished speaking, the yogī, twelve years old, came out of the womb immediately. He offered respects to Vyāsa, the Lord and his mother and departed.**  
**After a discussion between father and son, the Lord said, “O Vyasa! Your son speaks attractively like a parrot. He should be called Śuka. Though he may live in a palace, he will not be touched by māyā since my words are in his heart. Do not lament. Let us go home. You should speak what this great devotee spoke to you.”**  
**Informing him in this way, the guru of the three worlds, the supreme person mounted his chariot and saying farewell to Vyāsa, the best of the brāhmaṇas, in a loud voice, departed quickly for Dvārakā, the city of the Yadus.**

For the prosperity of your kingdom and home, you should return the kingdom to him."**

**Комментарий:**

Smṛti says *dārāgnihotra-saṁyogaṁ kurute yo ’graje sthite parivettā sa vijñeyaḥ parivittis tu pūrvajaḥ*: the person who gets married and performs sacrifice while the older brother is present is called *parivettā* and the elder brother is called *parivitti*. He enjoyed before his brother (*agrabhuk*).

### Text 16-17

*evam ukto dvijair jyeṣṭhaṁ chandayām āsa so 'bravīt*  
*tan-mantri-prahitair viprair vedād vibhraṁśito girā*  
*veda-vādātivādān vai tadā devo vavarṣa ha*  
*devāpir yogam āsthāya kalāpa-grāmam āśritaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When the** ***brāhmaṇas*** **said this, Mahārāja Śāntanu requested his elder brother Devāpi to take charge of the kingdom. Previously, however, Śāntanu's minister Aśvavāra instigated some** ***brāhmaṇas*** **to induce Devāpi to transgress the injunctions of the Vedas. He thus blasphemed the Vedic principles. Under the circumstances, Śāntanu again became the king, and Indra, being pleased, showered rains. Devāpi later took to the path of mystic** ***yoga*** **in a village named Kalāpagrāma.**

**Комментарий:**

The instigation was done secretly.

### Text 21-24

*vicitravīryaś cāvarajo nāmnā citrāṅgado hataḥ*  
*yasyāṁ parāśarāt sākṣād avatīrṇo hareḥ kalā*  
*veda-gupto muniḥ kṛṣṇo yato 'ham idam adhyagām*  
*hitvā sva-śiṣyān pailādīn bhagavān bādarāyaṇaḥ*  
*mahyaṁ putrāya śāntāya paraṁ guhyam idaṁ jagau*  
*vicitravīryo 'thovāha kāśīrāja-sute balāt*  
*svayaṁvarād upānīte ambikāmbālike ubhe*  
*tayor āsakta-hṛdayo gṛhīto yakṣmaṇā mṛtaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Citrāṅgada, of whom Vicitravīrya was the younger brother, was killed by a Gandharva who was also named Citrāṅgada. Satyavatī, before her marriage to Śāntanu, gave birth to the master authority of the Vedas, Vyāsadeva, known as Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana, who was begotten by Parāśara Muni. From Vyāsadeva, I, Śukadeva Gosvāmī, was born, and from him I studied,** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*****. The incarnation of Godhead Vedavyāsa, ignoring his disciples headed by Paila, instructed** ***Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **to me because I was free from all material desires. After Ambikā and Ambālikā, the two daughters of Kāśīrāja, were taken away by force, Vicitravīrya married them, but because he was too attached to these two wives, he died of tuberculosis.**

**Комментарий:**

*Śāntāya* means fixed in Brahman.

### Text 41

*tasmāc ca vṛṣṭimāṁs tasya suṣeṇo 'tha mahīpatiḥ*  
*sunīthas tasya bhavitā nṛcakṣur yat sukhīnalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**From Śuciratha will come the son named Vṛṣṭimān, and his son, Suṣeṇa, will be the emperor of the entire world. The son of Suṣeṇa will be Sunītha, his son will be Nṛcakṣu, and from Nṛcakṣu will come a son named Sukhīnala.**

**Комментарий:**

Viṣṇu Purāṇa says:

*nṛ-cakṣus tasyāpi sukhī-balaḥ, tataḥ pariplavaḥ, tasmāc ca sunayaḥ, tasyāpi medhāvī*

**From Nṛcakṣu came Sukhībala. From Sukhībala came Pariplava. From Pariplava came Sunaya. From Sunaya came Medhavī.**

### Text 44-45

*daṇḍapāṇir nimis tasya kṣemako bhavitā yataḥ*  
*brahma-kṣatrasya vai yonir vaṁśo devarṣi-satkṛtaḥ*  
*kṣemakaṁ prāpya rājānaṁ saṁsthāṁ prāpsyati vai kalau*  
*atha māgadha-rājāno bhāvino ye vadāmi te*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Mahīnara will be Daṇḍapāṇi, and his son will be Nimi, from whom King Kṣemaka will be born. I have now described to you the moon-god's dynasty, which is the source of** ***brāhmaṇas*** **and** ***kṣatriyas*** **and is worshiped by** ***devatās*** **and sages. In this Kali-yuga, Kṣemaka will be the last monarch. Now I shall describe to you the future Māgadha dynasty.**

**Комментарий:**

This dynasty is worshipped by devaṭās and sages (*devarṣi-satkṛtaḥ*).

## Chapter Twenty-three

### Text 15

*ārabdhas tasya gāndhāras tasya dharmas tato dhṛtaḥ*  
*dhṛtasya durmadas tasmāt pracetāḥ prācetasaḥ śatam*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Setu was Ārabdha, Ārabdha's son was Gāndhāra, and Gāndhāra's son was Dharma. Dharma's son was Dhṛta, Dhṛta's son was Durmada, and Durmada's son was Pracetā, who had one hundred sons.**

**Комментарий:**

Some versions have *prācetasam* instead of *prācetasaḥ. Prācetasaḥ* is correct.

### Text 29

*teṣāṁ jyeṣṭho vītihotro vṛṣṇiḥ putro madhoḥ smṛtaḥ*  
*tasya putra-śataṁ tv āsīd vṛṣṇi-jyeṣṭhaṁ yataḥ kulam*

**Перевод:**

**Of the sons of Tālajaṅgha, Vītihotra was the eldest. The son of Madhu was named Vṛṣṇi. Madhu had one hundred sons, of whom this Vṛṣṇi was the eldest, from which a dynasty started.**

**Комментарий:**

The hundred sons were called Mādhavas.

### Text 33

*teṣāṁ tu ṣaṭ pradhānānāṁ pṛthuśravasa ātmajaḥ*  
*dharmo nāmośanā tasya hayamedha-śatasya yāṭ*

**Перевод:**

**Among these many sons, six were the foremost (such as Pṛthuśravā). The son of Pṛthuśravā was known as Dharma, and his son was known as Uśanā. Uśanā was the performer of one hundred horse sacrifices.**

**Комментарий:**

Among the sons six were most prominent. *Pradhānam* is neuter.

## Chapter Twenty-four

### Text 9

*babhrur devāvṛdha-sutas tayoḥ ślokau paṭhanty amū*  
*yathaiva śṛṇumo dūrāt sampaśyāmas tathāntikāt*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Devāvṛdha was Babhru. Concerning Devāvṛdha and Babhru our predecessors sang two verses. Though we hear these verses from a distant past, we see them as if they were present.**

**Комментарий:**

Some versions have *amum* instead of *amū*. In that case, the following verses are considered to be Śukadeva’s words.

### Text 10-11

*babhruḥ śreṣṭho manuṣyāṇāṁ devair devāvṛdhaḥ samaḥ*  
*puruṣāḥ pañca-ṣaṣṭiś ca ṣaṭ-sahasrāṇi cāṣṭa ca*  
*ye 'mṛtatvam anuprāptā babhror devāvṛdhād api*  
*mahābhojo 'tidharmātmā bhojā āsaṁs tad-anvaye*

**Перевод:**

**"Among human beings Babhru is the best and that Devāvṛdha is equal to the** ***devatās.*** **Because of the association of Babhru and Devāvṛdha, all of their descendants, numbering 14,065, achieved liberation." In the dynasty of King Mahābhoja, who was exceedingly religious, there appeared the Bhoja kings.**

### Text 28-31

*tasyāṁ sa janayām āsa daśa putrān akalmaṣān*  
*vasudevaṁ devabhāgaṁ devaśravasam ānakam*  
*sṛñjayaṁ śyāmakaṁ kaṅkaṁ śamīkaṁ vatsakaṁ vṛkam*  
*deva-dundubhayo nedur ānakā yasya janmani*  
*vasudevaṁ hareḥ sthāna vadanty ānakadundubhim*  
*pṛthā ca śrutadevā ca śrutakīrtiḥ śrutaśravāḥ*  
*rājādhidevī caiteṣāṁ bhaginyaḥ pañca kanyakāḥ*  
*kunteḥ sakhyuḥ pitā śūro hy aputrasya pṛthām adāt*

**Перевод:**

**Through Māriṣā, King Śūra begot Vasudeva, Devabhāga, Devaśravā, Ānaka, Sṛñjaya, Śyāmaka, Kaṅka, Śamīka, Vatsaka and Vṛka. These ten sons were sinless. When Vasudeva was born, the** ***devatās*** **sounded kettledrums. Therefore Vasudeva, who provided the proper place for the appearance Kṛṣṇa, was also known as Ānakadundubhi (sound of drums). The five daughters of King Śūra, named Pṛthā, Śrutadevā, Śrutakīrti, Śrutaśravā and Rājādhidevī, were Vasudeva's sisters. Śūra gave Pṛthā to his friend Kunti, who had no issue, and therefore another name of Pṛthā was Kuntī.**

**Комментарий:**

Vasudeva is the place of Hari. Śiva says that *viśuddha-sattva* is called *vasudeva*:

*sattvaṁ viśuddhaṁ vasudeva-śabditaṁ*  
*yad īyate tatra pumān apāvṛtaḥ*  
*sattve ca tasmin bhagavān vāsudevo*  
*hy adhokṣajo me namasā vidhīyate*

**The pure** ***antaḥ-karaṇa*** **is called Vasudeva, because there the Supreme Lord appears without coverings. In that** ***antaḥ-karaṇa*****, I worshipped the Supreme Lord Vāsudeva, beyond the material senses, by offering respects. (SB** [**4.3.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-4/3.23)**)**

The appearance of *viśuddha-sattava in* this word is called Ānakadundubhi. One should see Bhagavat Sandarbha for more details.

### Text 37

*śrutadevāṁ tu kārūṣo vṛddhaśarmā samagrahīt*  
*yasyām abhūd dantavakra ṛṣi-śapto diteḥ sutaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛddhaśarmā, the King of Karūṣa, married Kuntī's sister Śrutadevā, and from her womb Dantavakra was born. Having been cursed by the sages headed by Sanaka, Dantavakra had formerly been born as the son of Diti named Hiraṇyākṣa.**

**Комментарий:**

Sambandhokti has *ṛṣi-śapta iti*. He had been cursed in his previous life and became the son of Diti. *Iti* means it is so said.

### Text 57

*na hy asya janmano hetuḥ karmaṇo vā mahīpate*  
*ātma-māyāṁ vineśasya parasya draṣṭur ātmanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O King! But for the mercy of the excellent Lord, who is the witness, there is no cause for the Lord’s appearance or activities.**

**Комментарий:**

“For others, the cause for birth and activities should be old karmas. The cause of the karmas is māyā. Does this apply to the Lord?” No. he has no cause for his appearance and activities except his mercy (*māyām)* to the devotees who are like himself (*ātma*).

*sādhavo hṛdayaṁ mahyaṁ sādhūnāṁ hṛdayaṁ tv aham*  
*mad-anyat te na jānanti nāhaṁ tebhyo manāg api*

**The pure devotee is my very heart: what pains him pains me. I am the heart of the pure devotee: what pleases him pleases me. My devotees do not know anything else but me, and I do not know more than they do. (SB** [**9.4.68**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-9/4.68)**)**

This mercy is manifested because he is the Supreme Lord and witness.

### Text 58

*yan māyā-ceṣṭitaṁ puṁsaḥ sthity-utpatty-apyayāya hi*  
*anugrahas tan-nivṛtter ātma-lābhāya ceṣyate*

**Перевод:**

**Even the actions of the Lord in the form of the** ***puruṣa*** **in relation to** ***māyā*** **for creation, maintenance and destruction of the universe, are his mercy alone. That mercy acts to produce detachment from the world and attainment of the Lord in the spiritual world.**

**Комментарий:**

This mercy is shown by kaimutya. The actions towards prakṛti (*māyā-ceṣṭitam*) consisting of the Lord’s glance at it for creating, maintaining and destroying the universe is mercy to the jīvas and nothing else, since in the process the produces senses and intelligence for the jīvas. That mercy acts so that the jīvas become detached from the material creation (*tan-nivṛtteḥ*) and attain (*lābhāya*) the Lord (*ātma),* what to speak of attaining other things like dharma.

*buddhīndriya-manaḥ-prāṇān janānām asṛjat prabhuḥ*  
*mātrārthaṁ ca bhavārthaṁ ca ātmane ’kalpanāya ca*

**The Supreme Lord manifested the material intelligence, senses, mind and vital air of the living entities so that they could indulge their desires for sense gratification, take repeated births to engage in fruitive activities, become elevated in future lives and ultimately attain liberation. (SB** [**10.87.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/87.2)**)**

### Text 59

*akṣauhiṇīnāṁ patibhir asurair nṛpa-lāñchanaiḥ*  
*bhuva ākramyamāṇāyā abhārāya kṛtodyamaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord acted to relieve the burden of the earth which was being overtaken by demonic leaders of great armies, who posed as kings.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord appeared in this world, showing his mercy specifically to the earth, his highest devotee, who gives shelter to unlimited devotees. Two verses describe this.

### Text 66

*jāto gataḥ pitṛ-gṛhād vrajam edhitārtho*  
*hatvā ripūn suta-śatāni kṛtorudāraḥ*  
*utpādya teṣu puruṣaḥ kratubhiḥ samīje*  
*ātmānam ātma-nigamaṁ prathayañ janeṣu*

**Перевод:**

**When he was born, Kṛṣṇa left Vasudeva’s house and went to Vraja in order to display to the world the highest** ***prema.*** **After killing the demons and liberating them, he married many women and produced hundreds of sons by them. As** ***para brahman*** **in human form, he worshipped himself through sacrifice and established path of the Vedas belonging to him alone, for the benefit of the people.**

**Комментарий:**

He describes all Kṛṣṇa’s activities which manifested suddenly all together, with a desire to appease his own longing and increase the longing of Parīkṣit. Manifesting (*jātaḥ*) in Vasudeva’s house, he went to Nanda’s village. Having described him according to his lineage, and dissatisfied, he describes how Kṛṣṇa increased the sense of fatherhood etc. more than previously (*edhitārthaḥ*). This is shown in Kṛṣṇa Sandarbha and also in the commentaries on SB [10.8.51](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/8.51), [10.5.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/5.1) and [10.9.46](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/39.46).

Devatās are worshipped through sacrifice. Why did he worship himself through sacrifice (*ātmanam samīje kratubhiḥ*)? He resides (*ṣaḥ* for *śete*) permanently as his expansion antaryāmī in all bodies *(puru*) of devatās etc. (*puruṣaḥ*). All worship is ultimately for him.

Since he is completely perfect, what does he gain by such worship? He shows people that he is the root of everything (*ātma-nigamam*). By this statement, his main goal is indicated. Since one derives meaning from contiguous words, how is it possible to separate words and supply words? It is tolerated, there should be no contradiction in meaning. Thus the word *puruṣa* not be completely separated from the context.

It is said that his human form is the highest:

*na cāntar na bahir yasya na pūrvaṁ nāpi cāparam*  
*pūrvāparaṁ bahiś cāntar jagato yo jagac ca yaḥ*  
*taṁ matvātmajam avyaktaṁ martya-liṅgam adhokṣajam*  
*gopikolūkhale dāmnā babandha prākṛtaṁ yathā*

**Thinking of the invisible Lord--who has no inside or outside, has nothing previous or after him, who existed in the past and will exist in the future, inside and outside of the universe, who is the universe-- as her son, appearing with a human form though he was beyond material senses-- she tied him to the mortar like a normal child with rope. (SB** [**10.9.13-14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/9.13)**)**

### Text 67

*pṛthvyāḥ sa vai guru-bharaṁ kṣapayan kurūṇām*  
*antaḥ-samuttha-kalinā yudhi bhūpa-camvaḥ*  
*dṛṣṭyā vidhūya vijaye jayam udvighoṣya*  
*procyoddhavāya ca paraṁ samagāt sva-dhāma*

**Перевод:**

**He destroyed the heavy burden of the earth by annihilating with his glance the armies of kings in battle, by creating enmity within the family, and loudly proclaimed the victory of Arjuna. After speaking spiritual knowledge to Uddhava, he resided in Dvārakā in** ***aprakaṭa-līlā.***

**Комментарий:**

The Lord said:

*ativrajya gatīs tisro mām eṣyasi tataḥ param*

**Help the sages cross beyond the conditions of the three** ***guṇas*** **and finally you will come to me. (SB** [**11.29.44**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/29.41)**)**

Saying this to Uddhava, he went (*agāt*) to his eternal abode or his svarūpa (*sva-dhāma*) along with his associates (*sam*).
