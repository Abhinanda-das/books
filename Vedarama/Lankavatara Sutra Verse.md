# Lankavatara Sutra Verse

## Lankavatara Sutra Verse

“I come within the range of the ignorant people under many names…and they address me by these names not knowing that they are all other names of the Tathagata (Buddha). Of these, Mahamati, some address me as the Tathagata, some as the self-effulgent One…as Buddha, as Rsi, as Govinda, as Brahma, as Visnu, as Isvara, as the Original Source, as Rama… but this is not understood by the ignorant who fail to see that these are the many names of the Tathagata.”

(Lanakavatara-sutra, translated by Dr. Suzuki pp165-166)
