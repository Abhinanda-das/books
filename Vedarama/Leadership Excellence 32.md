## Eagerness for Achieving Desired Success

By Srila Prabhupada

When one is sufficiently eager to achieve success in devotional service, that eagerness is called samutkantha. This means "complete eagerness." Actually this eagerness is the price for achieving success in Krishna consciousness. Everything has some value, and one has to pay the value before obtaining or possessing it. It is stated in the Vedic literature that to purchase the most valuable thing, Krishna consciousness, one has to develop intense eagerness for achieving success. This intense eagerness is very nicely expressed by Bilvamangala Thakura in his book Krishna-karnamrta. He says, "I am eagerly waiting to see that boy of Vrndavana whose bodily beauty is captivating the whole universe, whose eyes are always bounded by black eyebrows and expanded like lotus petals, and who is always eagerly glancing over His devotees and therefore moving slightly here and there. His eyes are always moist, His lips are colored like copper, and through those lips there comes a sound vibration which drives one madder than a mad elephant. I want so much to see Him at Vrndavana!"

\[From Nectar of Devotion\]

## Abhay Babu

Srila Prabhupada had a god-brother by the name of Dol Govind Shastri. He used to teach Sanskrit in the same building where Prabhupada used to live in Calcutta. One day Srila Prabhupada (who was still a working householder at the time) was sitting outside the building chanting japa and Dol Govind Shastri had just completed giving his class and was coming out.

"Hare Krishna Abhay Babu!" he said, "Did you know that Bhaktivinod Thakur has said that one day Russians, Germans, French etc will chant the names of Gauranga in sankirtan?"

"Yes," said Abhay Babu.

"So how...."

"You have doubt?"

"No, no, no. How can I doubt the Thakur, but how?"

"Oh, some surrendered fool among Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati's disciples will do it..." Abhay Babu said. He stood up and began to walk up the steps to go inside, then he turned and said, "...not you."

"Huh?" thought Dol Govind. "What did he mean 'not you'?" Prabhupada had used the word 'You' in plural form as if to say "Not you all" So Dol Govind Shastri was quite confused and sat down and thought "Does that mean HE will do it!?" That's what happens when you are a grammatician.

Anyway, years went by and Dol Govind lost touch with Abhay Babu. The Gaudiya Math became divided and Dol Govind Shastri left and got married, had children, moved to a place called Cuttack where the streets are always crowded with rickshaws.

So one day he was walking along these bustling streets and he saw at a distance in a rickshaw a huge white man dressed in dhoti, wearing neck beads and Gaudiya tilak...and he gulped. He immediately ran to him, stopped the rickshaw and began to shake him, "Who are you? Where are you from? What..." he was so shocked that he was not even coherent.

The white man said, "I am a disciple of Om Visnupada Paramhamsa Parivrajakcarya Astottara Sri Srimad His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada,"

"Huh?" The American accent was too thick for Dol Govind. "Who?"

Then the devotee pulls out a BTG and shows a picture of Prabhupada. "He is a disciple of Bhaktisiddhanta Saravati Maharaj Prabhupada,"

"Abhay Babu!! Oh my Lord!! You did it!!" his head was spinning at this point. He introduced himelf to Bhagavat das (the white man)... "I am also a disciple of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati"

Immediately Bhagavat das fell at his feet and offered his obeisances. By this time there was a huge traffic jam because both of them were right in the middle of the road.
