## Srila Prabhupada's Leadership Pearls

Good leadership means you must learn how to lead people. Yad yad acarati sresthas tat tad evetaro janah. If the srestha, the leader is ideal, then others will follow. But if the leader is not ideal, if he is not in the awareness of things, then people will be misguided.

Real leader means who does not commit mistake, who is not illusioned, who does not cheat and who has no imperfect senses, who has, or, other words, one who has got perfect senses. So if you say, "How it is possible for the conditioned soul?" "Yes, it is possible if you follow the perfect." Just like we are doing. We are following Krishna. He does not commit mistake, He is not illusioned, His senses are not imperfect, and He does not cheat. We are following. Therefore, although we are imperfect, because we are following the perfect, our proposition is perfect. A child may be illiterate, but when he's taught, "Write A like this," and he follows that, he becomes literate. This is the policy.

Leader means authority. His instruction is followed, and actually it happens. That is leader.

Qualified leaders. That is wanted. Leader means better qualified man to lead others. That is leader.

Leader means one who has become first-class disciple. He is leader. Evam parampara-prapta... One who is perfectly following... Our instruction is ara na kariha mane asa. You know this? What is that? Guru-mukha-padma-vakya, cittete kariya aikya, ara na kariha mane asa. Who is leader? A leader, to become leader, is not very difficult, provided one is prepared to follow the instructions of a bona fide guru.

Good manager means he is able to satisfy everyone and live in cooperative manner with all the devotees, so if you manage things nicely he can do tremendous work.

## From the Vyasa Puja 1996 Offering

By Radhanatha Swami

My eternal prayer is that I may be a surrendered puppet in your benevolent hands. I helplessly fall again and again at your lotus feet and pray that these shackles of worldly attachments may be cast away from my crippled heart. At that time I may be empowered to exclusively dedicate my body, mind, words, and life to being a humble instrument in your loving hands. I pray that you may kindly forgive me for my countless faults and allow me to forever be the servant of your servants.

O Srila Prabhupada, if it pleases you, please speak your supreme purifying words through my mouth and perform your divine will through my life. O Srila Prabhupada, please accept this fallen and ungrateful soul as a particle of dust eternally serving at your lotus feet.
