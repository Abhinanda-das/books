## New Ways

By Srila Prabhupada

"*Devotional service is dynamic activity.*"

The expert devotees also can discover novel ways and means to convert the nondevotees in terms of particular time and circumstance. Devotional service is dynamic activity, and the expert devotees can find out competent means to inject it into the dull brains of the materialistic population. Such transcendental activities of the devotees for the service of the Lord can bring a new order of life to the foolish society of materialistic men. Lord Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu and His subsequent followers exhibited expert dexterity in this connection. By following the same method, one can bring the materialistic men of this age of quarrel into order for peaceful life and transcendental realization.

## Bhaktivedanta Hostipal Mumbai

By Bhakti-tirtha Swami

"*How wonderful that you can*  
*earn for your family and also preach.*  
*It's an ideal job for a devotee.*"

It's a huge hospital with almost two hundred doctors. When you first enter the hospital you see a wonderful murti of Srila Prabhupada. It's almost like you walk into a temple. Even nondevotees offer obeisances to him, because they understand the importance of saintly people. Throughout the building you hear Prabhupada's bhajanas, lectures and chanting. All the devotee-doctors wear tilak and all the announcements start with 'Hare Krishna'. The quality of care is better than average. The design of a building is similar to our Mayapur constructions and the devotees have designed it in such a way that it doesn't have an institutional mood. Many of the people who are hired also become devotees.

There are special departments, like the department of Bio-Technology. Some of the previous top abortionists now do campaigns against abortion. Most of the doctors attend the Temple Morning Program, on big festivals they adjust their schedules so they can participate in them. There is also a special Spiritual Care Section. During the day someone is just going around and giving prasadam to visitors and some are trained to discuss with patients. At all times there is someone giving spiritual care. There are devotees trained to deal with those who are leaving their bodies, they give them Tulasi, chandan, sprinkle Ganges water on them, say the prayers etc... Even patients who are not devotees are constantly asking devotees to take care of their loved ones when they leave their body. Because they just see such a caring atmosphere. There is such amazing teamwork.

Since householders need funds for the family, how wonderful it is that you can simultaneously earn money for your family and preach. It's an ideal job for a devotee. In the hospital there is a Temple with regular daily puja's, at any time of the day people can come and have Darsan, have bhajans and kirtans. They also have a little park where people can chant and pray. In a very sattvic area, as part of the spiritual care, you can have discussions, doctors come out sometimes and have bhajans etc... In a special section, people can come to hear and understand how much we really address the soul. They are working at creating a very powerful environment where the dead body is taken, given the fact that the soul hovers around for a while. In this way, there will be more facilitation for every aspect of the care of the individual patient.

## Every Opportunity

"*So Prabhupada would sit in the back*  
*- can you imagine?*"

One ISKCON Swami told a following story. Srila Prabhupada met one yoga teacher, Dr Mishra, who understood that Prabhupada was a very, very great saintly person. So he told him: "You can come and give some lecture at my Yoga Institute." But the problem was, Dr Mishra was a Mayavadi, and he would speak and then Prabhupada would speak and completely defeat everything Dr Mishra had said. This was very embarrassing to Dr Mishra, because he had no way to counteract what Prabhupada answered. They would get into so many philosophical debates.

After some time, he said to Prabhupada: "Better you not speak in my Yoga Institute because it is creating too much confusion, but you can lead kirtans." So Prabhupada would sit in the back - can you imagine? Srila Prabhupada, such a personality, the only facility he was given, he wasn't even allowed to speak, but he was completely enthusiastic. He would lead his kirtan and he would sit in the back. And some of the students of Dr Mishra would come in the back just to meet him, and he would invite them to his home. He would make prasada, and he would preach. It was, just any chance he got - he was enthusiastic to take it!"
