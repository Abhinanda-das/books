# Leadership Excellence 38

## Quote of the Week

\- Srila Prabhupada

## Good People - Good Leaders

By Srila Prabhupada

"*The state taxes should be spent to*  
*build the character of the people.*"

If the people themselves are dishonest, the administrative machinery must be corrupt. Although a damned government of the people may be given a good or fancy name, if the people are not good they cannot have good government, regardless of which party governs the administration. Therefore good character in the consciousness of the mass of people is the first principle necessary for a good government and equal distribution of wealth.

Obedience is the first law of discipline. The people have become disobedient to the laws of God, and therefore neither rain nor wealth is equally distributed. A man who is ultimately disobedient cannot have any good qualifications. When disobedient leaders lead the disobedient people, the whole atmosphere of the administration becomes polluted and full of dangers, as when a blind man leads several other blind men. The state taxes, therefore, should be spent to build the character of the people in general. That will bring happiness to the citizens of the state.

## Interview with Manu dasa - Vice President of Inis Rath Island Temple in Nothern Island

"*What I am getting out of Krishna's*  
*service I want to give to people,*  
*so they can see that all their needs*  
*are fulfilled and plus they get more.*"

**LE:** After so many years of enormous financial contribution to ISKCON projects, what do you feel is the benefit you have received from that giving?

**MD:** On one level, obviously, I have given very little. But on another level, I have given money which Krishna has given me. All I can say is that I have received mainly enjoyment from giving to ISKCON projects. I feel very aligned with Srila Prabhupada's projects and therefore very aligned with him. Krishna has kept sending me laksmi (money) and I have kept using it for devotee based projects, sometimes I get into debt for the projects but mystically Krishna keeps saving me. I find it hard to resist when there is an opportunity to develop something in our Temple and I know we can do it because I am confident that Krishna will help, and He does!

**LE:** Do you ever feel that people in the Temple are taking advantage of your contributions?

**MD:** Other people have felt that and told me so but I can only worry about myself and not about others. My experience has been that when I can give enthusiastically others will similarly work enthusiastically in their service. Slack people then stick out more as an exception and they realize that this is not what we're here for. The cooperative energy of giving is what I seek most amongst the devotees, and this is mostly in alignment with what Srila Prabhupada rewards, i.e. what pleases him most.

**LE:** What is the competitive advantage of ISKCON for the future?

**MD:** If you work for Krishna's pleasure you'll be more happy than if you work only for yourself. What I am getting out of Krishna's service I want to give to people, so they can see that all their needs are fulfilled and plus they get more. They get their deepest needs fulfilled in a very satisfying and intense way - like the need for a pure lasting relationship. Association with devotees and developing our relationship with Lord Krishna is the most exciting thing.
