## Srila Prabhupada's Pearls

Manage cleverly without any personal ambition.

In the *Bhagavad-gita* the Lord has clearly promised that His devotees are never to be vanquished. And He behaves according to the quality or degree of the devotional service rendered by the devotees.

Lord Krishna as Govinda is more inclined to the brahmanas and the cows, indicating thereby that human prosperity depends on those two items, namely brahminical culture and cow protection. Lord Krishna is never satisfied when these are lacking.

A life of freedom in eternity, complete knowledge, and a blissful atmosphere is the heart's desire of an enlightened soul. We should undertake all sorts of penances and austerities to attain that permanent source of happiness.

## Advertising

By Bhakta Octavian

*Bhakta Octavian is from Moldova. He is living with his family and serving in Inis Rath Island Temple community in Northern Ireland.*

In 1997 me and my wife Angela were full time students of Business & Marketing in Swindon College England,and we were also doing a full-time course of Higher National Diploma in Finance and Marketing. At that time we were also practicing bhakti-yoga to the best of our ability. One day we had a wonderful experience, while attending our Market Relations Class. In that unit we were studying effective advertising techniques such as: colours, shapes, names, images, etc. Our lecturer was showing us a research video of the most successful logos by the most famous companies wordlwide. The video was filmed on the High Street of some big city. All of a sudden they showed the Hare Krishna devotees blissfully chanting on the street. They made a strong comment on how successful devotees were to catch the attention of the people, which is the the purpose of expert advertising. The saffron robes, the haircuts of the bramacharies, the ancient instruments and their sounds, the unity of people involved in that blissful activity full of real life and purity. Thus professionals themselves showed us as a case study that the devotees Harinama is an example of excellent advertising.
