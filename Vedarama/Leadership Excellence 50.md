# Leadership Excellence 50

## Excellent Quote

They should be perfect leaders, pure Vaisnavas, free from anarthas, no self interest, they should be selfless.

\- Gour Govinda Swami

## Ramanujacarya's Final Instructions

- Worship all Vaisnavas as you worship your guru.
- Have faith in previous acaryas.
- Never be controlled by the senses.
- Never be satisfied simply by worldly knowledge.
- Study the scriptures that describe the glories of the Supreme Lord.
- Transcendental knowledge can overcome the pushings of the mind and senses.
- Be indifferent to the prompting of the mind.
- Always relish the chanting of the holy name and qualities of the Lord.
- The best way to serve the Lord is rendering service to his devotees.
- Never follow the ways of a Vaisnava for material gain.
- Always endeavor for purity.

\- From The Life of Ramanujacarya, compiled by Naimisaranya Dasa

## Interview with Candramauli Swami

*Candramauli Swami is a travelling preacher based in Chicago.*

**LE:** How you made it until now as a sannyasi?

**CS:** As far as I see myself serving in the role of a sannyasi, I honestly feel I can improve in order to really serve Srila Prabhupada in this asram. But whatever success is there, it is solely due to the mercy of Krishna coming through Srila Prabhupada and the Vaisnavas. In my years of service that mercy has manifested itself most prominently in having the good fortune of the association of my Godbrothers who have assisted me in various ways.

**LE:** What do you do to enhance servant-leadership in ISKCON?

**CS:** Factually our position is only one of being servant, no matter what role one plays or position one has in devotional service. The jiva soul is never anything other than a servant. So leadership means to serve by assisting others in their service to spiritual master and Krishna. Practically, I try to make myself available throughout my day for the devotees. To assist others in their service to Krishna pleases Krishna more than trying to serve directly.

**LE:** We heard you are cleaning your room in Chicago Temple and attending the classes of younger devotees.

**CS:** I always like to clean and keep things orderly. So I do it. But if someone comes forward to help than that's fine. I always feel I can learn something by attending Srimad Bhagavatam class. And of course that is recommended - to hear the classes daily. Also it sets an example for others to follow. Example is higher than precept.

## Editor's Note

I remember when we used to have big public programs in Yugoslavia, Sacinandana Swami used to always carefully thank his disciples for organizing programs. He didn't assume they should do it simply because they are his disciples. This kind of appreciation is exemplary. If we can increase appreciation and gratefulness for devotees' services ISKCON can become much stronger.
