# Leadership Excellence 51

## Excellent Quote

\- Rupa Gosvami

## Able Authorities

By Srila Prabhupada

If the leadership of world affairs is entrusted to the devotees of the Lord, for which a worldwide organization under the name and style of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness has already been started, then by the grace of the Almighty Lord there can be a thorough change of heart in human beings all over the world because the devotees of the Lord are able authorities to effect such a change by purifying the dust-worn minds of the people in general.

The politicians of the world may remain in their respective positions because the pure devotees of the Lord are not interested in political leadership or diplomatic implications. The devotees are interested only in seeing that the people in general are not misguided by political propaganda and in seeing that the valuable life of a human being is not spoiled in following a type of civilization which is ultimately doomed.

If the politicians, therefore, would be guided by the good counsel of the devotees, then certainly there would be a great change in the world situation by the purifying propaganda of the devotees, as shown by Lord Caitanya.

## Maha-Bhagavata Guru

By Purnacandra Dasa

*Purnacandra Dasa is an educator and a preacher in ISKCON.*

We may want to find a maha-bhagavata guru, that's good, but we should know that the path of bhakti would still remain difficult even if we find him because the major part of the job is on us. We're the ones who must control our senses. We're the ones who must pray. We're the ones who must engage in sincere devotional service without ulterior motivations. One doesn't advance simply by the guru touching him with a magic wand, sending him at once to Vaikuntha. Prabhupada was so powerful, still you can see what happened to a large number of his disciples. That's proof that simply by having a maha-bhagavata guru doesn't guarantee your success. Of course, he can help you the most, but the brunt of the responsibility is on you.

## Coach

By Akrura dasa

One of the ways to develop leadership character and skills is to have an experienced devotee as a coach. Your coach is your personal spiritual partner who assists you in living the spiritual life you know you should live, personally, in your family and in your service. He or she is someone you place your trust in to help you hold yourself accountable for your life and to make sure you really do live up to your full spiritual potential. By helping you define your varna and asrama, he helps you focus on what's most important in your spiritual advancement and responsible roles in your family, temple, congregation, team or organization.
