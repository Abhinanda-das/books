# Leadership Excellence 65

## Excellent Quote

There is a need for increased training and assessment of leaders in the ISKCON society, including at the GBC level. ... Emphasis will be on character and competence with a view to developing the full potential of individual leaders.

\- 2001 GBC Resolution

## Fresh Challenge

By Srila Prabhupada

Our leaders shall be careful not to kill the spirit of enthusiastic service, which is individual and spontaneous and voluntary. They should try always to generate some atmosphere of fresh challenge to the devotees, so that they will agree enthusiastically to rise and meet it. That is the art of management: to draw out spontaneous loving spirit of sacrificing some energy for Krishna.

## Coaching Tip

As a leader, what you can do better this month than you have done last month?

## Jump-Starter

What will it take for each and every particular devotee to be enlivened, inspired and protected, so that they could practice Krishna consciousness actively serving Prabhupada's movement till the day they die?

\- Radhanatha Swami
