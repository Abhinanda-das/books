## Interview with Kurma dasa, Vegetarian

**LE:**How do your cooking classes influence preaching in Australia?

**KD:**I teach all over Australia in professional cookery schools, but I am most well known because of my TV cooking shows. Two of my 26-part series were screened twice each, and so they went to air 104 times on one channel, SBS. At one stage, they were the third most popular show on that network. The last series of shows were aired over 500 times on cable TV. This has had a tremendous impact on people, who recognise me wherever I go, and thank me profusely for changing their lives for the better. Most probably I am the most well-known Hare Krishna in Australia. I am indebted to Srila Prabhupada for making me his instrument in this regard. Incidentally, government surveys just released show that meat consumption has declined over 50% in the last 10 years in Australia.

**LE:** Did you train any new expert cooks?

**KD:**I have taught many cooking seminars and classes exclusively for devotees around the world over the last 20 years. Karunamayi Devi Dasi from Cape Town, attended my cooking seminars in Mayapur a couple of years ago. She runs her own cooking classes in South Africa. I also meet many devotee cooks on my travels who run restaurants and cook exclusively from my three cookbooks. Australia has 17 fully operational prasadam restaurants, and prasadam distribution from restaurants is undoubtedly the most successful preaching that we are doing - people love Hare Krishna food in Australia.

**LE:** Who makes the excellent design of your books?

**KD:**Ram Prasad das from near Australia's New Govardhana farm was responsible for design for my first book "Great Vegetarian Dishes", and the third and latest book "Quick Vegetarian Dishes". A professional designer worked on the second book "Cooking with Kurma". There are two new books on the way - stay tuned!

## Coaching Tip

By Srila Prabhupada

If you are working for Krsna, it is Krsna's desire if you be successful or not successful. That means don't think that because you are in Krsna consciousness, you will always be successful. No. It doesn't matter. Even if you are unsuccessful, you must know it firmly that without Krsna's desire, nothing can happen.

## Jump-Starter

Prabhupada was first and foremost a role model of his teachings. If a person wanted to understand his belief system, the only thing they needed to do was watch him. His actions laid the groundwork or the foundation for his words. His preachings were rooted in the actions of his own life.

(Written by a top consultant in FranklinCovey, a major world leadership training firm, after reading Prabhupada Lilamrta)
