# Leadership Excellence 68

## Cutting Edge

It takes sincere intent and skill to create a trusting environment in which open dialogue can occur and underlying issues can be brought to light. A great deal of honest communication and feedback will set the parameters of leadership improvement.

## Hard Question

How many leaders have you developed?

## Mini Interview with Gauri dasa, Temple President of Bhaktivedanta Manor

**LE:**What are, in your view, three main challenges ISKCON leaders face at the present moment?

**GD: One -** there is a natural lack of faith towards ISKCON leaders, arising from our history of various disfunctions in past leadership. Thus, devotees often doubt the spiritual integrity and managerial capabilities of our leaders.

**Two -** a lot of the leadership is often based upon an individual leaders own steam. There is no set program of training and appraisal. It's all a bit ad hoc.

**Three -** few resources in terms of qualified and capable devotees to draw upon.
