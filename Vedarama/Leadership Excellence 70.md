# Leadership Excellence 70

## Coaching Tip

By Srila Prabhupada

It is not very encouraging. Still, I am not a man to be disappointed.

## Cutting Edge

By Srila Prabhupada

Rupa Goswami has introduced this system of regulative principles which I have taught you also. These regulative principles, such as rising before 4 A.M. for mangala-arati, chanting sixteen rounds minimum on beads daily, reading books, going for the street sankirtana, preaching to anyone and everyone, offering the prasada, like that, these principles of devotional service are there to safeguard us from maya's attack by keeping us always enthusiastic. If we strictly observe these principles, we shall remain always enthusiastic. These are the sources and the maintainers of our enthusiasm to serve Krsna.

## Jump-Starter

By Srila Prabhupada

Personally, I am nonentity; I have come here on the order of my spiritual master, and he has kindly sent all you boys to assist me. So whatever is being done, there is no credit for me, but all credit goes to my spiritual master; because he has arranged everything, and I am simply trying to abide by his order.
