# Letters from 1970

## Letter to: Sri Hitsaran Sharma – Allston, Mass., February 17, 70 From: Brahmananda

Delhi

Dear Sri Sharma,

Thank you for your letter dated February 5, 1970. I beg to inform you that two proforma invoices have been duly sent to the S.G. Supply Agency regarding subscriptions to Back to Godhead, the magazine of the Hare Krishna Movement. They have been sent by air mail. One invoice was for back issues, and one for a one year’s subscription. I thank you on behalf of my Spiritual Master, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, for your kind interest in our movement here in the West to spread Lord Caitanya’s Samkirtan Movement.

In my Master's Service,  
Brahmananda das brahmachary  
President
