# Letters from 1971

## Letter to: Shri Krishnadasa Adhikari – Moscow, March 16, 71 From: G. G. Kotovsky

Hamburg

Dear Sir,

Referring your Letter to: Prof. V.V.Balabushevitch, dated 11/2/1971 I am to inform you that Professor V. V. Balabushevitch passed away on the 14th of December, 1970.

I thank you for your information about Swami Bhaktivedanta’s lecturing tour. If he would come to Moscow, the Soviet scholars doing research in ancient Indian culture will be very happy to meet him in the Institute of Oriental Studies, USSR Academy of Sciences. I would be thankful to you for the information on the dates of Swami Bhaktivedanta’s arrival and stay in the USSR.

Yours sincerely  
G.G. Kotovsky  
Head, Department of Indian and South Asian Studies,  
Institute of Oriental Studies,  
USSR Academy of Sciences,  
Armyansky Perlk 2,  
Moscow, USSR.

SRI KRISHNA DASA ADHIKARI  
GOVERNING BODY COMMISSIONER  
INTERNATIONAL SOCIETY FOR KRSNA CONSCIOUSNESS  
SRI SRI RADHA-KRISHNA-TEMPEL  
2 HAMBURG 6 BERTELSTRASSE 65  
B R D

## Letter to: Professor Kotovsky – Moscow, June 10, 1971 From: Syamasundara

My dear Professor Kotovsky,

Please accept our respectful greetings. On Tuesday, June 7th, 1971, on behalf of A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami, I sent you the following cable:

VISITING MOSCOW JUNE 20THTO 25TH.

PLEASE STAY I WISH TO MEET YOU.

I am to inform you that Swami Bhaktivedanta has recently returned to India from a very successful lecture tour through Malaysia and Australia, and that he will be continuing on to lecture in France and England in the latter part of June. En route to these countries, he will halt in Moscow for a 5-day visit in your nation’s lovely capitol.

He will arrive in Moscow on Air India flight number 515 at 11:25 a.m. on 20thJune, 1971, and he is scheduled to leave for Paris on the morning of 25thJune.

Swami Bhaktivedanta would consider it a great pleasure and honor to meet with the Soviet scholars doing research in ancient Indian culture at the Institute of Oriental Studies, USSR Academy of Sciences, at any time between those dates which would be convenient for them.

As he will be leaving in ten days’ it is questionable if your postal reply would reach here before the departure date. With this in mind, may I humbly request you to cable whether or not you and your staff will be available to meet with the Swami, who is widely recognized as one of the world’s foremost authorities on the Vedic culture.

I hope this finds you in good health.

Sincerely,  
Syamasundara das Adhikary  
Secretary to A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami

## Letter to: Mr. Sarkar – Delhi, September 20, 1971 From: Tamal Krishna

My Dear Mr. Sarkar,

Please accept my humble obeisances. I have not heard from you yet as to what is the position of our land in Mayapur since the flooding.

Once again you are being entrusted with a very important mission. Because of the flooding all of the farmers must have lost much of their crops. So they will be in need of money and willing to sell their property. Srila Prabhupada has requested me to see that more land is purchased at this time. As soon, we will begin construction and this is the right time to purchase. So I wish that immediately when you receive this letter you will go to Calcutta and discuss this matter fully with Jayapataka Swami, who will deal with the matter in my absence. I have also written him regarding this matter and he is expecting you.

The land should be purchased from either side, either to the north or to the south of the land we already have, thus making our land wider. Which ever side gives the lower price that we should take. So go now to Calcutta and discuss with Jayapataka Swami. No time should be wasted and we wish to purchase in a months time.

Hoping this finds you in good health.

Your servant  
Tamal Krishna Das

TKD/pdd

P.S. Once the construction begins, the land prices will go higher, so we should purchase before construction begins.

## Letter to: Mr. Sarkar – Delhi, October 24, 1971 From: Tamal Krishna

My Dear Mr. Sarkar Prabhu,

Please accept my humble obeisances. I beg to acknowledge receipt of your letter dated October 18, 1971. Lord Caitanya has got a big plan in store for Mayapur and by the grace of Srila Prabhupada we are all being engaged in the fulfillment of this great plan. So your service in behalf of Srila Prabhupada is certainly significant in helping to fulfill the desire of the Supreme Lord.

I have noted the contents of your letter carefully, but I have one question. You say you are going to Nabadwip with Dinnanath to collect the names of the land owners in the adjacent plots. But it seems that this information has already been included in the documents which we have payed Mr. Sanyal to collect. Srila Prabhupada is now in Calcutta, so you should go immediately to Calcutta to take further instruction from Him on what to do in this matter. I beg to remain obedient.

Your servant,  
Tamal Krishna das Adhikary

TKD/pdd

P.S. It will not be possible for myself or my wife, Madri dasi, to attend the Calcutta function, as we are very much engaged in preparing a similar function here in Delhi. But I shall be returning to Calcutta after some time and I will be sure to see you at that time.
