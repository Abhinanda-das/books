# Letters from 1976

## Letter to: Ramesvara – Bombay, January 12, 1976 From: Tamal Krishna

Vrndavana  
12thJanuary, 1976

Dear Ramesvara Prabhu,

Please accept my humble obeisances.

In regard to Bharghava Prabhu, I discussed with Prabhupada and His Divine Grace asked me to write you. Bharghava is spending money of the BBT frivolously. He is taking and spending more than he is supposed to. Unless he gives very strict account of how he spends, he should be recalled. All money is collected under such difficulty, it should be spent only for Krishna. Srila Prabhupada said he was suprised to see how this boy comes everywhere he goes. Please do the needful.

Also, we received a letter from Ranadhir das regarding the request of Dr. Shyam Sundar das Brahmachari for complimentary subscription to BTG. Prabhupada said we should not correspond with him and not reply.

Also, please find enclosed a letter with insufficient address which he therefore cannot reply.

Seen:  
A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami

Your servant,  
Tamal Krishna Goswami  
Personal Secretary

P.S. Please inform all temples and GBC that Prabhupada’s mail should be sent to Calcutta not Mayapur, as mail to Mayapur takes an extra week.

## Letter to: Tamal Krishna – Los Angeles, January 24, 1976 From: Ramesvara

Calcutta  
ALL GLORIES TO SRI GURU AND GAURANGA!

Dear Tamal Krishna Gosvami,

Please accept my humble obeisances in the dust of your feet. All glories to Srila Prabhupada. I hope you a re enjoying very much the merciful association of Srila Prabhupada.

Regarding Bhargava Prabhu, I am very surprised to hear that he is spending BBT money whimsically. Never before has he had BBT money to spend like this and I assure you it will never again happen. The reason for this unique situation is that Brahmananda Svami requested me to transfer money to Africa for Prabhupada’s plane fare and unfortunately, due to some communications mixup, they never picked up the money and left for India. Since it was too difficult to get the money back to the U.S.A., I arranged for Bhargava to pick up the money and take it with him to Bombay. There he was to repay Ambarish the money that he lent Prabhupada for the plane fare and send the balance to me. He recently sent me one letter accounting for the money he had spent already and I wrote back that he should simply send the balance back to me in Los Angeles. Anyway, if Prabhupada is disturbed by this, you might kindly inform His Divine Grace that this is a unique situation and will never happen again. Jai, Maharaj, I hope this finds you well and always absorbed in the bliss of serving Srila Prabhupada’s lotus feet. Haribol!

Your unworthy servant,  
Ramesvara dasa Brahmacary  
BBT Trustee

/kdd  
Iskcon - Calcutta

## Letter to: Aksayananda – Mayapur, February 12, 1976 From: Dayananda

Vrndavana

My dear Aksayananda Maharaj,

Please accept my humble obeisances. All glories to Sri Guru and Gauranga!

Tamal Krishna Maharaj has just left for the states and I am taking over for him as personal secretary to Srila Prabhupada. (Hridayananda Maharaj is traveling GBC man) Before he left Tamal Krishna Swami asked me to write you concerning this important matter.

If the plans for the Gurukul take a very long time that may not be very good. His Divine Grace has been talking about shifting all the older boys (8 years and above) from Dallas to Vrndavana. The point is that a delay in approval may not be very good for us. So if it is at all possible please try to speed up the approval.

T.K. Maharaj said that when he first got the plans for the temple in Vrndavana approved, he went personally to the District Magistrate and the town planner and made several visits. He gave them literature, prasadam, an explanation of our world wide program and then a request (begging) to kindly approve our plans quickly. Then if it is necessary also go to Lucknow, Agra, etc.

So he felt that that endeavor would save a tremendous amount of time. And he thought the plans would take a month for approval whereas who knows how long they would take otherwise. Actually I have personal experience working with Tamal Krishna Maharaj in the States and seeing him approach people personally. Everyone is a person and somehow if they can be approached in a very amiable, cordial way, they can be induced to do things for us. If we are very sober, sane, refined and business like and of course Krishna Conscious they like to work with us. Please try.

Your servant  
Dayananda das

I am simply trying to see that the framework which I have laid is developing nicely. \[Handwritten note:\] (Don’t mention in BBT newsletter!) Pradyumna is going to meet us in Hawaii direct from Bombay because there is no Australian embassy anymore here in Bombay to get his visa. His Divine Grace further said that of anyplace where he would like to settle down for some time to translate, Prabhupada said “new Vrindavan” however he said that is dangerous also with the people coming with their rifles. Anyway, Prabhupada will not go to all the Rathayatras this year as it is too strenuous, but he mentioned especially that he would attend the rathayatra in N.Y., and then go to New Vrindavan.

Hyderabad is supposed to have opening in Janmastami in August 18, 1976 and Prabhupada will go back to India for that. So the N.Y. Rathayatra should hopefully be in mid-July. Some of the points in the beginning of this letter are not meant for mass distribution to the devotees, so kindly do not write a newsletter with these points. ## Letter to: Ramesvara – Honolulu, May 12, 1976 From: Pusta Krishna

Los Angeles

My dear Ramesvara Maharaj,

Please accept my humble obeisances. I beg to inform you that this morning Srila Prabhupada called both myself and Radhaballabha das into his room at 3:30 a.m. and described something which is very, very disturbing to His Divine Grace. Concerning the advertisement in the Asian Studies Booklet from I believe March, 1975, with which I know you must be familiar, there was one ad for Srila Prabhupada’s books, but it is obvious that His Divine Grace’s name was intentionally avoided in each and every instance. For example, in the quote from Professor Dimock, the name “A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada” was deleted, and in all the cases this was done. ## Letter to: Surendra Kumar (2) – Vrndavana, September 30, 1976 From: Aksayananda

Aligarh

Dear Mr. Surendra Kumar,

Please accept my greetings and prayers for your well-being. I am in due receipt of your notes via to myself and guruji your messenger today, and have noted the contents carefully.

I have already written you a letter dated 30thSept., ’76, giving many details of Srila Prabhupada’s cooking requirements. Our representative Mahaksha das is also in Aligarh and has the same list. This is very important, as Srila Prabhupada’s health is not the best, and we must be very careful!

Yes, the two cars is alright, and arrangement for 8 persons. 2 of the persons are ladies, and therefore isolated quarters are necessary. The cars may come here 8thevening, and the drivers may stay here overnight, and we will leave 4.30 a.m. 9thmorning. Then arrive at your place about 7.00 am for Arti and Guru Puja, and Prabhupada may speak a few words.

Breakfast is better at 8.00 am, and lunch 1.30 pm. Otherwise the program is fine.

The last program in Aligarh with you, there was a large busload of devotees. I think you also arranged for their stay and prasad. So as I have written you, we will be total (incl. Prabhupada’s entourage) about 25 heads. Apart from your two carloads, we will bring one small bus. Please confirm immediately if this is alright. I think you agree it would not be befitting if Prabhupada arrived with only half-dozen devotees. So please kindly confirm this is alright (25 persons). At least 20 are a must.

Any other doubts may be cleared up with our representative, Mahaksha das.

Please drop me a note or cable of confirmation. I hope this meets you well, and looking forward to a nice program with you,

I am,

Yours in the service of the Lord,  
Aksayananda Swami  
Personal Secretary for His Divine Grace  
A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada

## Letter to: Gopal Krishna – Bombay, December 22, 1976 From: Jagadisha

Bombay

Dear Gopal Krishna Prabhu,

Please accept my humble obeisances. I am in receipt of your letter dated 12-12-76.

I have read your whole Letter to: His Divine Grace and the following is an account of His remarks.

About the cox and Kings three day trip to Vrndavana, He said “nice work”. Regarding teaching other kinds of yoga, so that we may advertise an 8 or 9 day course for tourists, Prabhupada liked the idea. He said, “That will be very attractive. Other types of yoga are mentioned in the Bhagavad Gita. We can adopt that. Three or four men I can teach how to do it. Then you’ll be able to attract them. I can teach how to sit down, posture, asana, pranayama, without any difficulty. The yoga system as mentioned in the Bhagavad Gita we can take up, that will be bhakti-yoga, Use one or two of the big rooms in the Gurukula building. I can teach the teachers how to practice.” You, Sarvabhavana, Dhananjaya and Mahavira were mentioned by Prabhupada as prospective teachers. About the Earthquake conference, Prabhupada said, “That’s nice, very good.

Regarding holding up printing due to lack of funds, He said, “Don't hold up printing, money will be provided.”

Regarding Gurudas Prabhupada said, “Gurudas’ work in Poland is more important. So, if you actually require an assistant, I’ll give you one very good assistant. When, I go to Bombay I shall arrange for that. Will Gurudas go to Vrndavana to live at Radha Damodara at least for one week as an experiment?

That’s all. Hoping this meets you in good health.

Your servant,  
Jagadisha Das

HG Gopal Krishna Das Adhikary  
c/o Iskcon Hare Krishna Land  
hand delivered in Bombay

## Letter to: Satadhanya Swami – Bombay From: ?

Calcutta

From: a Letter to: Satadhanya Swami (President of Calcutta)

after receiving an ecstatic report of Sankirtan in the Hiamlayas: "When Srila Prabhupada heard how all over the Himalayas people are hearing of Krishna Consciousness through books, He said, "The devotees all over the world are doing the greatest service to Caitanya Mahaprabhu. There is no comparison to their service. I am only an instrument to give you guidance. Actually you are all born to spread Krishna Consciousness all over the world. Go on more and more. The books are selling. That is our greatest success. I am very much pleased."

Then after hearing the Sankirtan Newsletter Vol IV No 12 Prabhupada stated, "Can any Babaji and Goswami do such distribution? All they can do is wag their tails. They have such big tails!" and..."The big books are selling more than the other sizes. It is beyond my imagination. This news gives me more vital force."
