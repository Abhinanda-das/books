# Mahābhāvasvarūpa

## Supremely Glorious Srimati Radharani

Srimati Radharani is very much honoured and indeed worshipped in the society of Vaisnavas, especially in the Nimbārka-sampradāya, Puṣṭi-mārga or Vallabha-sampradāya and Gauḍīyavaiṣṇava tradition. Radharani is the Supreme Truth and the personal form of Lord Krishna.

*ekātmānāv api bhuvi purā*  
*deha-bhedaṁ gatau tau*  
*caitanyākhyaṁ prakaṭam adhunā*  
*tad-dvayaṁ caikyam āptaṁ*  
*— Cc Ādi-līlā 1.5*

**There is no difference between Lord Krishna and Srimati Radharani. They are actually one. Although Radha and Krishna are one in Their identity, They separated Themselves eternally. For the sake of performing pastimes, They became two.**

Sri Krishna is rasarāja, the personified form of the mellow of loving exchanges. Srimati Radharani is the personification of bhāva, or loving emotions towards Lord Krishna. She is thus called mahābhāvamayī, Sri Radha Thakurani, for She consists purely of the highest degree of love for Lord Krishna. Bhāva means bhakti, loving devotional service. The entirety of bhakti, found in both the spiritual and material worlds, emanates from Srimati Radharani alone. The feelings of love for Krishna found in the hearts of the practising devotees, in the goddesses of fortune and in the gopis of Vrindavan, are nothing but a fragment of Radhika’s love.

Radharani is the personified form of mahabhāva, the complete reservoir of the mellows of the highest love. And Sri Krishna is Rasarāja, the king of the most excellent rasa, the mellow of pure spiritual loving exchanges. Thus They are the transcendental couple, complete in exchanging transcendental mellows.

Even great ṛṣis and sages like Sanaka and exalted personalities like Lord Brahma and Sri Uddhava praise the glories of Srimati Radharani. Not only do they praise Her, but they also express their eagerness to obtain Her mercy or even that of Her sakhīs or maidservants. If they could achieve this, they would consider their lives supremely successful.

## Uddhava praises the Gopis

When the Lord performed His pastimes on this earthly planet, Uddhava was the most intellectual scholar. He went to the heavenly planets and studied from Brihaspati, the teacher of the demigods. Uddhava was Lord Krishna’s minister (or advisor) and close personal friend. It was this dear Uddhava that Krishna sent to Vrindavan in order to console the gopis and mitigate their pain in separation from Krishna.

When Uddhava witnessed the gopis’ extreme love for Krishna (mahabhāva), he felt the vrajagopis much greater than himself and desired to get a touch of their mercy. Uddhava was fascinated and realised that the gopis had far greater love than he had ever seen or could even imagine. Uddhava considered their love to be the highest attainment of life and vowed to make all efforts to attain that love. Then, in great delight, he sang several ślokas regarding the gopis’ separation from Krishna:

*āsām aho caraṇa-reṇu-juṣām ahaṁ syāṁ*  
*vṛndāvane kim api gulma-latauṣadhīnām*  
*yā dustyajaṁ sva-janam ārya-pathaṁ ca hitvā*  
*bhejur mukunda-padavīṁ śrutibhir vimṛgyām*  
*— Bhāg 10.47.61*

**“The gopis of Vrindavan have given up the association of their husbands, sons and other family members, who are very difficult to give up. Furthermore they have forsaken the path of chastity to take shelter of the lotus feet of Mukunda, Krishna, which one should search for by Vedic knowledge. Oh, let me be fortunate enough to be one of the bushes, creepers or herbs in Vrindavan, because the gopis trample on them and thus bless them with the dust of their lotus feet.”**

Uddhava considered himself fortunate if he could get the foot dust of these gopis. The topmost or crest jewel of all the gopis of Vrindavan is Srimati Radharani.

## Universal Praise

The widely respected spiritual teacher Adi Shankaracarya has composed beautiful writings in praise of Srimati Radharani and the gopis.He, who is deeply honoured by all learned persons, has abundantly glorified the love of Srimati Radharani. Many other spiritual leaders and great wise poets (kavis) such as Sripada Vallabhacarya, Sripada Nimbarkacarya and Lord Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu Himself, along with Srila Sanatana Goswami, Srila Rupa Goswami and Srila Jiva Goswami, have also glorified the love of the gopis and Srimati Radharani. Spiritual teachers, great thinkers and vastly learned, educated scholars of different ages and from different places have written about the greatness of Srimati Radharani. All these exalted personalities have praised Her unparalleled qualities.

## The Identity of Srimati Radharani

Who is Radharani? What is the love that She feels for Sri Krishna? Even Brahma and Lord Ananta Shesa are not able to explain this. This can only be somewhat understood by the object of Her love - Shyamasundara, Sri Krishna. He partially understands this love, but yet even He cannot describe it perfectly. Srimati Radharani’s love is unequalled and incomparable. It is meant exclusively for Shyamasundara Himself. The day on which this supremely glorious Radhika appeared is called Rādhāṣṭamī. Radharani did not appear on this planet in her home village Varsana. Radharani’s mother was at her father’s house in Ravala to give birth. So Ravala is the birthplace of Srimati Radharani.

## The Lustre of Molten Gold

Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu is Krishna Himself but the lustre of His body is not His own. Sri Krishna is Shyamasundara - blackish complexioned. It is Sri Radha who is the colour of molten gold - tapta-kāñcana gaurāṅgi. He took on Her golden complexion. Tapta-kāñcana gaurāṅgi became tapta-kāñcana gauranga. The ācāryas explain that the acceptance of Her lustre indicates that the inner mood of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu is that of Srimati Radharani Herself. Thus Shyamasundara became Gaurasundara. Krishna with the mood and complexion of Srimati Radharani - this is Gaura.

Having accepted both the mood and bodily complexion of Srimati Radharani, Chaitanya Mahaprabhu realised Her love for Krishna. That was the primary reason for His descent.

In the last verse of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu’s Śikṣāṣṭakam, Srimati Radharani’s overwhelming emotions are expressed. Considering that verse, we can understand the feelings that Chaitanya Mahaprabhu developed for Krishna - the deepest feelings in the heart of Srimati Radharani. Thus, Chaitanya Mahaprabhu is Krishna, experiencing the topmost loving moods found only in the core of the heart of Srimati Radharani. That is the unique quality of gauraavatāra.

## Radharani’s Feelings in Separation

One day Sri Lalita devi told her dear friend Srimati Radharani, “O Radhe, Krishna is so merciless and cruel, yet You love Him so much! You are so attached to Krishna that You even neglect to take care of Your own body. You have given up all religious duties and social customs. You have given up family, society, and everything for Him. Even you have given up Your personal comfort as Your mind is always restless and disturbed because of thinking about Him. This Krishna is so cruel; He does not care to meet You, nor does He even talk about You. It would be better for You to forget Him - just take Him out of your mind. Only then will You become happy.”

It may seem strange that this kind of advice is being given by Srimatiji’s most confidential and dear friend Sri Lalita devi. To speak against Krishna seems quite contradictory for a dear friend of Sri Radha. How could Lalita advise Sri Radha to give up Shyamasundara? Why did she speak in this way? Actually, this is an expression of Lalita devi’s love. The reason Lalita devi spoke these words is that she feels the acute pain of Sri Radha in separation from Krishna. That is why she spoke this way. Countering the words of Her most beloved friend Lalita, Srimati Radharani replied:

*āśliṣya vā pāda-ratāṁ pinaṣṭu mām*  
*adarśanān marma-hatāṁ karotu vā*  
*yathā tathā vā vidadhātu lampaṭo*  
*mat-prāṇa-nāthas tu sa eva nāparaḥ*  
*—Cc.Antya-lila 20.47*

**“Oh, My friend, Sri Krishna is always the Lord of My life. He may address Me as His beloved, speak sweet loving words to Me or lovingly embrace Me as His beloved. On the contrary, Krishna may trample Me while I am bowing down to His lotus feet, He may refuse to meet Me or even reject Me forever causing Me to burn in the fire of separation. No matter what, He still remains the Lord of my life.”**

She continues, “Dear friend, I know that Krishna is a licentious person (lampaṭa), attached to various lovers. He certainly has relationships with many gopis. Sometimes He even frolics with My rival Chandravali or other gopis right in front of My eyes. However, I do not desire that Krishna stays only with Me. He can do whatever He wants. His happiness is My only happiness. Even though Shyamasundara keeps contact with many gopis, still He remains the Lord of My life. There is no one else for Me but Krishna. He may be present before Me or He may never come to Me, but I will always remain loyally dedicated to Him. By all means, I must make Him happy for His happiness is the goal of My life.”

This is the mood of Srimati Radharani and it is also present in Her gopis. They only want that Krishna is satisfied and never desire their own satisfaction.

## Pure Love

Love in this material world cannot be compared to the pure love for Krishna found in the spiritual world. It can never reach the stage of kṛṣṇa-prema, unadulterated love of Krishna. Whatever affection we experience or express here is simply another form of sense gratification. It is only temporary. After some time, that so-called love expires and disappears.

Sri Narada Muni explains that true love, kṛṣṇa-prema, never dwindles or dies, even in the presence of any amount of opposition or when there is sufficient reason for it to do so. Krishna’s dealings gave the gopis many strong reasons why they should give up their affection for Him, yet they never did so. For example, He unexpectedly left Vrindavan, heartlessly abandoning them, thus causing them great distress. In His absence, the gopis were acutely suffering due to separation from Him. For loving Krishna, the gopis were ostracised by society. Even though there were many reasons to give up this licentious Krishna, the gopis’ love for Him never diminished or decreased. This illustrates the nature of prema, spiritual love. Prema is constantly increasing and is ever fresh. That is it’s natural quality.

*ātmendriya-prīti-vāñchā—tāre bali ‘kāma’*  
*kṛṣṇendriya-prīti-icchā dhare ‘prema’ nāma*

**The desire to gratify one’s own senses is kāma (worldly hungers), but the desire to please the senses of Lord Krishna is prema (love). —Cc.Adi-lila 4.165**

Here, Srila Krishnadasa Kaviraja Goswami says that the desire to satisfy one’s own senses is called kāma (‘worldly desires.’) That’s quite different from love. Prahlada Maharaja has also spoken on this matter. He has compared this self-serving “love” to the behaviour of a businessman. If you go into a merchant’s shop, he may offer you a place to sit and something to drink. He is not doing this because he loves you but rather, his motivation for doing so is that he wants you to buy something from him just to make a profit for himself. There is no love involved at all.

Prahlada Maharaja says in (Bhāg 7.10.4),

*nānyathā te ‘khila-guro*  
*ghaṭeta karuṇātmanaḥ*  
*yas ta āśiṣa āśāste*  
*na sa bhṛtyaḥ sa vai vaṇik*

**“The person who wants a reward in return for devotional service is not a devotee or loving servant, but rather, he is a merchant.”**

Then he prays, “O Lord, I am not serving You with devotion in order to get something from You. I don’t want anything from You. If you insist on giving me a boon, then please give me the blessing that, not even a single selfish desire will ever germinate in my heart.”

## The Gopis’ Love for Krishna

The gopis of Vrindavan are the personified forms of kṛṣṇa-prema. They only want to give to Krishna; they don’t want to get anything from Him. When someone lovingly serves Krishna, He reciprocates accordingly. That is His promise and indeed natural proclivity. However, the pure loving service of the gopis is beyond the reciprocation capacity of Shyamasundara. He cannot adequately reciprocate their love. Therefore Krishna becomes indebted to the gopis. This, He Himself admits in Bhāg (10.32.22)

*na pāraye ‘haṁ niravadya-saṁyujāṁ*  
*sva-sādhu-kṛtyaṁ vibudhāyuṣāpi vaḥ*  
*yā mābhajan durjara-geha-śṛṅkhalāḥ*  
*saṁvṛścya tad vaḥ pratiyātu sādhunā*

**“O my dear gopis, I am unable, even within a lifetime of Brahma, to repay My debt to you for your spotless service. Your connection with Me is beyond reproach. You have worshiped Me, cutting all domestic ties, which are exceedingly difficult to sever. Therefore please let your own glorious deeds be your compensation.”**

Here Shyamasundara is saying that even if He kept serving the gopis for as long as the lifetime of Lord Brahma, He still would not be able to reciprocate their love. He then pleads with them that only they themselves can free Him from the debt of love that He has accrued. That, He says, would be a manifestation of their benevolence and pure affection. Krishna admits that if the gopis would not pardon Him, then He would remain indebted to them forever. This asservation of Krishna shows the super-excellent qualities of the natural and spontaneous, pure love of the gopis.

## Lord of My Life

*āmi—kṛṣṇa-pada-dāsī, teṅho—rasa-sukha-rāśi,*  
*āliṅgiyā kare ātma-sātha*  
*kibā nā deya daraśana, jārena mora tanu-mana,*  
*tabu teṅho—mora prāṇa-nātha*  
*-—Cc.Antya-lila 20.48*

Srimati Radharani expresses the feelings from the depths of Her heart, “O friend, I am a maidservant at the lotus feet of Krishna, āmi—kṛṣṇapada-dāsī and, He, Shyamasundara is rasarāja, the reservoir of all loving mellows, teṅho—rasasukha-rāśi. He is the personified form of spiritual delight. To be with Him; to serve Him; to be loved by Him; all these experiences grant the greatest possible happiness. Aliṅgiyā kare ātmasātha - He may embrace me; He may keep me by His side, hold me to His chest or kibā nā deya daraśana, - He may not grant me His audience. Jārena mora tanu-mana - He may scorch my body and mind in the fire of separation from Him. Tabu teṅho—mora prāṇa-nātha – nevertheless, Sri Krishna is the only Lord of my life.

*sakhi he, śuna mora manera niścaya*  
*kibā anurāga kare, kibā duḥkha diyā māre,*  
*mora prāṇeśvara kṛṣṇa—anya naya*

**“My dear girlfriend, please hear My mind’s steadfast resolution. If He shows Me affection or even devastates Me by causing mental and physical distress, Krishna is still the Lord of My life in all circumstances. — Cc. Antya-lila 20.49**

Srimati Radharani says, “Krishna is the complete master of My life. He is the One who gives joy to My heart. He Himself is My happiness, and He is the love of my life. I know that sometimes My beloved is deceitful but I will never give Him up.”

*chāḍi’ anya nārī-gaṇa, mora vaśa tanu-mana,*  
*mora saubhāgya prakaṭa kariyā*  
*tā-sabāre deya pīḍā, āmā-sane kare krīḍā,*  
*sei nārī-gaṇe dekhāñā*  
*— Cc. Antya-lila 20.50*

**“Sometimes Krishna gives up the company of other gopis and becomes controlled, mind and body, by Me. Thus He manifests My good fortune and gives His other gopi lovers distress by performing His loving affairs with Me.”**

*kibā teṅho lampaṭa, śaṭha, dhṛṣṭa, sakapaṭa,*  
*anya nārī-gaṇa kari’ sātha*  
*more dite manaḥ-pīḍā, mora āge kare krīḍā,*  
*tabu teṅho—mora prāṇa-nātha*  
*—Cc. Antya-lila 20.51*

**“I know that Krishna tends to be a licentious, shameless false lover who appears to be very expert in trickery and cheating. More dite manaḥ-pīḍā, mora āge kare krīḍā, tabu tenho— mora prana-natha. He may sport with other gopis right in front of My eyes, giving Me great pain. Or He may embrace and frolic with Me in front of those gopis, giving them pain. Whatever He does, He remains the Lord of my life.”**

## Selfless Love

*nā gaṇi āpana-duḥkha, sabe vāñchi tāṅra sukha,*  
*tāṅra sukha—āmāra tātparya*  
*more yadi diyā duḥkha, tāṅra haila mahā-sukha,*  
*sei duḥkha—mora sukha-varya-*  
*—Cc. Antya-lila 20.52*

**Srimati Radhika continues, “If Krishna desires to associate with another gopi who can give Him happiness, then I will go to her house, beg at her feet, take her by the hand, and bring her to Krishna. Then I will be happy. I never even think in my dreams that Krishna should make Me happy. The sole purpose of My existence is to give Him pleasure.”**

*mora sukha—sevane, kṛṣṇera sukha—saṅgame,*  
*ataeva deha deṅa dāna*  
*kṛṣṇa more ‘kāntā’ kari’, kahe more ‘prāṇeśvari’,*  
*mora haya ‘dāsī’-abhimāna*  
*—Cc.Antya-lila 20.59*

**“My happiness is in the service of Krishna, and Krishna’s happiness is in union with Me. For this reason, I give My body in charity to the lotus feet of Krishna who sometimes accepts Me as His lover and calls Me His most beloved. It is then that I truly consider Myself His maidservant.”**

Srimati Radharani says, “Listen oh My friend, what do you call happiness? I tell you, My happiness lies only in serving Krishna and kṛṣṇera sukha—saṅgame, Krishna’s happiness is in union with Me. I know that He gets great pleasure from being in My company, from embracing Me, etc. Therefore I give My all to Him. Krishna may say to Me, ‘Sri Radhe! You are very dear to Me. You are the love of My life. You are the ruler of My heart.’ But in My heart there is only one conception; that is that I am His servant. My only happiness is to serve Krishna. It is My duty and great joy to unite Krishna with whichever gopi He desires to be with. I only desire that He will always be happy.”

## The Love of the Chaste Brāhmaṇī

There is a story from the Purāṇas, which Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu narrated feeling the mood of Srimati Radharani:

*kuṣṭhī-viprera ramaṇī, pativratā-śiromaṇi,*  
*pati lāgi’ kailā veśyāra sevā*  
*stambhila sūryera gati, jīyāila mṛta pati,*  
*tuṣṭa kaila mukhya tina-devā*  
*—Cc Antya-lila 20.57*

**“The wife of a brāhmaṇa suffering from leprosy manifested herself as the topmost of all chaste women by serving a prostitute in order to satisfy her husband. She thus stopped the movement of the sun, brought her dead husband back to life and satisfied the three principal demigods \[Brahma, Visnu and Maheshvara\].”**

Once, there was a wife of a brāhmaṇa. She was the crest jewel amongst all chaste ladies. Her husband, on the other hand, was a leper. Because of his leprosy, he had many sores all over his body. His fingernails and toenails were infected and falling off.

One day, that brāhmaṇa desired to relish the company of the most beautiful prostitute in the village. It is astonishing that although he had leprosy, he was still harbouring lusty thoughts! This shows that no amount of disease can put an end to carnal desire.

The brāhmaṇī, being at the pinnacle of chastity, had only the happiness of her husband on her mind. Indeed she could think of nothing else. So she approached that prostitute and requested, “Please fulfil my husband’s desire to associate with you.” The prostitute was extremely beautiful but the brāhmaṇa was a deformed and ugly leper, so naturally, the prostitute flatly refused. In order to change the mind of the prostitute, the chaste brāhmaṇī resolved to serve her. Every day, she swept her house, plastered fresh mud on the walls, and performed all kinds of menial services. Even though she was from a higher-class, this brāhmaṇī was now serving a lower-class person.

After some time, the prostitute became satisfied with the brāhmaṇī’s service. Seeing the prostitute satisfied with her service, the brāhmaṇī again requested the prostitute to associate with her husband. Although pleased, the prostitute had some reservations about associating with the brāhmaṇa. If anyone heard that the prostitute had been in contact with the brāhmaṇa who had a horrible disease, then they would avoid her like the plague. Therefore she agreed to the brāhmaṇī’s request, on one condition. She said, “I will not come to your home; you will have to bring your husband here.” The prostitute went on to tell her to come at a time when nobody could see them. Accordingly, the brāhmaṇī brought her husband in the dark of the night, carrying him on her back.

When the brāhmaṇī was carrying her husband that night, it so happened that his foot touched the impaled sage’s body. Markandeya Ṛṣi roared, “What is this? Who dares to kick me?” The sage became angry and cursed, “Whosoever has kicked me, then let him die with the rising of the sun!” The brāhmaṇī had been trying to please her husband in all innocence and sincerity but now a terrible thing happened – her husband was struck with a mortal curse!

At the thought of her husband’s death with the inevitable rise of the sun, the brāhmaṇī’s heart pounded and her body trembled out of fear and dread. She then began contemplating how she could prevent that unthinkable outcome. “What can I do now?” she thought.

The brāhmaṇī knew that she had gained a lot of power because of her being perfectly faithful to her husband. Gathering all the śakti obtained through her complete chastity, she proclaimed, “If I have never had any thoughts about other men; if I have been solely attached to my husband throughout my whole life; if I have been determined to always keep him happy; and if his happiness has always been the only purpose in my life, then I say that from now on, the sun will no longer rise!”

An amazing situation thus evolved. The universal order was challenged by the declaration of the brāhmaṇī, a simple housewife. On the one hand was the power of the austerities of the great sage, and on the other hand the power of the chastity of the brāhmaṇī. To everyone’s wonderment, the sun indeed did not rise! The great sage was now defeated by the brāhmaṇī. She had forced the universe to grind to a halt by dint of the power gained through her chastity. In all four directions, there arose a hue and cry, “The sun is not rising! The sun is not rising!”

Responding to the uproar, the demigods collectively sought the shelter of Lord Brahma, Lord Visnu and Lord Shankara. The brāhmaṇī had pleased the three Lords by her chastity, and so They were compelled to give her Their rare simultaneous darśana. They said, “Daughter, we are satisfied by your chastity and we would like to give you a boon. Please withdraw your word and let the sun rise. Your husband will die but only momentarily for We will immediately revive him. Rest assured that no harm will come to your husband.”

With that assurance, the brāhmaṇī withdrew her word and the sun now rose. Accordingly, her husband died but Lord Visnu immediately brought him back to life by His compassionate glance. Not only did He bring the brāhmaṇa back to life, but He also took away his leprosy. The brāhmaṇa became beautiful and completely purified by the Lord’s grace. Thus, with the power of her chastity, the brāhmaṇī had saved her husband’s life and freed him from diseases of body and heart.

## Overflow of the Ocean of Happiness

After this, Radharani says -

*kṛṣṇa—mora jīvana, kṛṣṇa—mora prāṇa-dhana,*  
*kṛṣṇa—mora prāṇera parāṇa*  
*hṛdaya-upare dharoṅ, sevā kari’ sukhī karoṅ,*  
*ei mora sadā rahe dhyāna*  
*—Cc. Antya-lila 20.58*

**“Krishna is My life and soul. Krishna is the treasure of My life. Indeed, Krishna is the very life of My life. I therefore keep Him always in My heart and try to please Him by rendering service. That is My constant meditation.”**

Radharani here speaks from Her heart, expressing how dear Krishna is to Her. She calls Him Her very life, the treasure of Her heart, and the one who gives life to Her soul.

*kānta-sevā-sukha-pūra,*  
*saṅgama haite sumadhura,*  
*tāte sākṣī—lakṣmī ṭhākurāṇī*  
*nārāyaṇa-hṛdi sthiti,*  
*tabu pāda-sevāya mati,*  
*sevā kare ’dāsī’-abhimānī”*  
*—Cc.Antya-lila 20.60*

**“Service to one’s lover is the home of happiness and is sweeter than direct union with Him. The goddess of fortune is evidence of this, for although she constantly lives on the heart of Narayana, she still desires to render service to His lotus feet. She therefore considers herself a maidservant and thus serves Him constantly.”**

Kānta-sevā-sukha-pūra, saṅgama haite sumadhura, “Serving My beloved, even from afar, gives Me much more happiness and satisfaction than simply being near Him. I may or may not get His association. I may or may not get His embrace. That is completely up to Him. But for me, these things are insignificant. There is unlimitedly more happiness in serving Him. I experience an ocean of happiness when I get the opportunity to serve My beloved.”

Tāte sākṣī—lakṣmīi ṭhākurāṇī, “Oh sakhī, Sri Laksmi, the goddess of fortune, knows about this. She is never seen in the embrace of Her beloved.” It is known that the Lord of Vaikuntha, Narayana, loves all of His devotees very much. Demonstrating bhakta-vātsalya - love for His devotees, Lord Narayana sometimes leaves His eternal residence Vaikuntha and appears on Earth to bestow His darśana. At that time, there is no possibility of Laksmi having close association with the Lord. In His eternal pastimes in this universe, the puruṣa-avatāra, Bhagavān Visnu, rests on Anantashesa with Sri Laksmi always engaged in the service of His feet.

Although Laksmi devi is generally not seen in the embrace of Her beloved, there is evidence to suggest that there are some exceptions. On one occasion, She saw Her reflection in a crystal jewel. Upon seeing Her own face, She thought, “This very face has been kissed by the Lord!” From this example, it can be seen that sometimes Lord Narayana does grant Sri Laksmi His intimate association. At the same time, She is always engaged in some kind of service to Her Lord. She is usually massaging His feet and is never seen in His embrace. A popular picture shows Lord Visnu reclining with a lotus flower sprouting from His naval, and Lord Brahma sitting on top of it. The Lord rests on Shesashayi and Laksmi devi serves His feet.

Nārāyaṇa-hṛdi sthiti, tabu pāda-sevāya mati, sevā kare ‘dāsī’-abhimānī. “Whilst remaining on the chest of Lord Narayana, Sri Laksmi maintains a strong desire to serve Him. Cherishing the conception of being His servant, She serves Him day and night.” Lord Narayana loves Sri Laksmi so dearly that He has granted Her eternal residence on His chest as the mark of Srivatsa. However, Laksmi is not satisfied to remain on His chest, as Her consciousness is fixed on serving His lotus feet. Laksmi devi does not consider Herself the beloved consort of Lord Visnu; rather She considers Herself His maidservant. Sharing similar sentiments with Sri Laksmi, Srimati Radharani says, “Krishna may show Me affection, addressing Me as priyā - His beloved - or prāṇeśvari - Goddess of His life, but My mood of being His servant - ‘dāsī’-abhimānī – shall never change.”

## Chastisement and Scolding

We may wonder why Radharani sometimes speaks harsh words to Krishna. Srimati Radharani Herself explains

*kāntā kṛṣṇe kare roṣa, kṛṣṇa pāya santoṣa,*  
*sukha pāya tāḍana-bhartsane*  
*yathā-yogya kare māna, kṛṣṇa tāte sukha pāna,*  
*chāḍe māna alpa-sādhane*  
*—Cc Antya-lila 20.54*

**“I sometimes show anger towards Krishna. In my anger, I may even scold Him. But actually, I know that at such times, Shyamasundara experiences even greater joy. His pleasure increases when I chastise Him or do not speak to Him for some time. It is for this reason that I become sulky.”**

Through this service of contrariness, the love between Sri Radha and Krishna expands and is embellished.

One time, Srimati Radharani was in a very intense state of contrariness. Krishna could not break that mood though He repeatedly tried to do so. Thus Krishna was completely defeated, and He openly admitted it saying, in the words of Suradas,

*śrī-rādhe, tabaso tava guṇa nāhi bhayo*

“O Radhe, breaking your māna is the most demanding thing that I have ever had to do. In the form of Varahadeva, I rescued the sunken earth planet from the depths of the Rasatala ocean and then killed the notorious demon Hiranyaksa. That was not as hard as appeasing you. In the form of Nrsimhadeva, I slayed the invincible Hiranyakasipu and saved my devotee Prahlada. In the form of Kurma, I lifted the immovable Mandara mountain, at the time when the demigods and the demons were churning the milk ocean. But, dearest Radharani, all these endeavours are nothing in comparison to what I have to do to pacify You.” Here, Shyamasundara is indirectly praising and expressing His satisfaction at the great māna exhibited by Srimati Radharani. We should thus understand that when Srimati Radharani exhibits extreme loving anger (mahāmāna), She is actually creating great joy for Krishna.

## The Love of the Chataka Bird

In Tulasi das’s Dohāvali the example of the papiha (chataka) bird is given. The chataka bird is exclusively attached to the rainclouds. However, sometimes the clouds do not give water to him. When that happens, the chatakabird stares at them, repeatedly calling out, “Piyu! Piyu! Darling! Oh my Darling!” The chataka bird only drinks the water that comes directly from the clouds. Other than this he does not drink even one drop of ground water. Although the lakes, rivers, and oceans are filled with water, the chataka bird does not even drink one drop of water from there. He loves the clouds so much that he only desires their water. Sometimes the clouds pelt down hail and ice, battering and injuring him. Still, the chataka bird never wavers in his dedication to the rainclouds, remaining ever fixed in his single-pointed love for them.

The chataka bird never finds fault with his beloved clouds. Sri Tulasi das says that real love cannot be measured. Real love is like an ocean of unfathomable depth and immeasurable breadth; it is limitless. Even in the material world, a reflection of the ocean of love can be found in the heart of a small bird!

Once, a chataka bird was flying and a hunter shot him. Being pierced by the hunter’s arrow, the bird fell. The bird tumbled and was falling into the sacred and pure Ganges river. But during his fall the chataka bird turned his beak upwards, away from the water towards the sky, so that no water from the Ganga would enter his mouth. Even until his last breath, the love of the chataka bird for the clouds did not diminish at all. The love of the chataka bird is great but Srimati Radharani’s love is incomparable and unparalleled. It would be an offence to say that Radharani’s love is like that of the chataka bird. But just to get a glimpse of Her love, the example of the chataka bird is given.

*vrajera viśuddha-prema, yena jāmbū-nada hema,*  
*ātma-sukhera yāhāṅ nāhi gandha*  
*se prema jānā’te loke, prabhu kailā ei śloke,*  
*pade kailā arthera nirbandha*  
*— Cc. Antya-lila 20.62*

The pure devotional service in Vrindavan is like the unadulterated golden particles in the River Jambu. In Vrindavan there is not a trace of personal sense gratification.

Srila Krishna Dasa Kaviraja Goswami states: vrajera viśuddha-prema; in the completely pure love of Vrindavan, ātma-sukhera yāhāṅ nāhi gandha, there is not even a scent of selfishness or personal sense gratification. This uncontaminated, perfectly pure prema of Vraja reaches its peak in Srimati Radharani. Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, in these verses, is tasting and explaining these pristine sentiments of Srimati Radharani.

The pure love of Srimati Radharani, the epitome of vraja-bhakti, was experienced and given to the world by Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu. Thus, like Mahaprabhu, our submissive prayer at the lotus feet of Srimati Radharani is that She may bestow upon us a small fraction of Her love for Vrindavanachandra Shyamasundara.

## A Prayer to Vrsabhanu Nandini Sri Radha

*yasyāḥ kadāpi vasanāñcala-khelanottha*  
*dhanyātidhanya-pavanena kṛtārtha-mānī*  
*yogīndra-durgama-gatir madhusūdano’pi*  
*tasyā namo’stu vṛṣabhānu-bhuvo diśe’pi*  
*— Śrī Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhī, 2*

**Obeisance to the direction that faces the daughter of King Vrsabhanu. When the breeze coming from there playfully touches Him, Lord Krishna, who cannot be attained by even the kings of the yogīs, thinks that His life has now become grandly successful.**

Srila Prabodhananda Sarasvati says - yogīndradurgama-gatir madhusūdano’pi - The best of the yogīs would consider themselves fortunate if they could get a glimpse in meditation of Lord Madhusudana. The word “yogīndra” here refers to the greatest of yogīs like Lord Shiva, the four Kumaras and Narada Muni. Even though they are such great yogīs, they are unable to behold or even conceive of Krishna because He never manifests Himself in their meditative hearts. On the other hand, the Vraja-gopis, headed by Srimati Radharani, are far more exalted than these yogīs as they directly offer their flower-like faces to be enjoyed by the bee-like Madhusudana.

Tasyā namo’stu vṛṣabhānu-bhuvo diśe’pi – Here the esteemed author is giving his obeisance to the very direction where the daughter of Maharaja Vrsabhanu, Vṛṣabhānu-nandinī Srimati Radharani, blissfully stays. Dhanyātidhanyapavanena - When the breeze touches the edge of Radharani’s sārī, then it is most fortunate. Shyamasundara too considers Himself extremely fortunate to merely come in contact with that transcendentally scented air. Yasyāḥ kadāpi vasanāñcala-khelanottha – When the breeze carrying the fragrance from the veil of Srimati Radharani touches Krishna, He considers Himself most blessed and fortunate. With the scent of Radharani against His skin, Krishna then feels that His life is now complete.

*yo brahma-rudra-śuka-nārada-bhīṣma-mukhyair*  
*ālakṣito na sahasā puruṣasya tasya*  
*sadyo-vaśīkaraṇa-cūrṇam ananta-śaktiṁ*  
*taṁ rādhikā-caraṇa-reṇum anusmarāmi*  
*— Śrī rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhī, 4*

**“I constantly remember the dust from the feet of Srimati Radharani which is a fragrant transcendental powder possessing unlimited potency and able to immediately subdue the Supreme being, Sri Krishna who is not even seen by great devotee-leaders like Brahma, Shiva, Sukadeva, Narada and Bhishma.”**

Even great personalities such as Lord Brahma, Lord Shiva, Sukadeva Goswami, Narada Muni, and Grandfather Bhismadeva cannot understand the Supreme Lord Sri Krishna. ālakṣito na sahasā, they have difficulty even in meditating on His lotus feet. There is, however, one special substance which has such an astonishing power in that it can bring the otherwise unattainable Personality of Godhead Sri Krishna under control. Sadyo-vaśīkaraṇa-cūrṇam ananta-śaktiṁ. There is unlimited power in that amazing substance. It at once transforms the Supreme Personality of Godhead into Srimati Radharani’s submissive servant. Even Sri Krishna Himself desires to attain it. That substance is called rādhikā-caraṇareṇum - the dust of Srimati Radhika’s lotus feet. Those who attain that dust by becoming the maidservants of Srimati Radharani also control the otherwise uncontrollable Shyamasundara.

It is very difficult to understand the Supreme Lord Sri Krishna. Even to meditate on Him is difficult. Even ṛṣis and munis cannot get His darśana. Where does He live? What is His nature? What are His activities? All these details about Him are very difficult to conceive of. Therefore, He is commonly called alakha nirañjana, inconceivably pure. We cannot see Him, nor can we contemplate on Him as He is on the transcendental plane and we are on the material.

The poet Rasakhansays, ” Look! Look! He, the Supreme Brahman, the all-pervading Supreme Transcendence, who is hardly known by anyone, is sitting in Vrindavan, in Nidhuvana, in sevā-kuñja, in a cottage-like hiding place made of overgrown creepers and flowers (kuñjakuṭira). There, He is massaging Sri Radhika’s feet. If you want to find Him, then look for Him in sevā-kuñja. In searching the entire universe, what can you expect to find?”

*vaidagdhya-sindhur anurāga-rasaika-sindhur*  
*vātsalya-sindhur atisāndra-kṛpaika-sindhuḥ*  
*lāvaṇya-sindhur amṛta-cchavi-rūpa-sindhuḥ*  
*śrī-rādhikā sphuratu me hṛdi keli-sindhuḥ*  
*- Śrī rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhī, 18*

**Srimati Radharani is an ocean of expertise in artistic enjoyment. She is an ocean of the pure mellow of loving attachment. She is an ocean of tender-hearted affection. She is an ocean of intense mercy. She is an ocean of enchantment. She is a nectarean ocean of splendorous beauty. This Srimati Radhika is an ocean of amorous play. She is eternally engaged in love sports with Shyamasundara. May She be manifest in my heart.**

*rādhā-nāma-sudhā-rasaṁ*  
*rasayituṁ jihvāstu me vihvalā*  
*pādau tat-pada-kāṅkitāsu*  
*caratāṁ vṛndāṭavī-vīthiṣu*  
*tat-karmaiva karaḥ karotu hṛdayaṁ*  
*tasyāḥ padaṁ dhyāyatāṁ*  
*tad-bhāvotsavataḥ paraṁ*  
*bhavatu me tat-prāṇa-nāthe ratiḥ*  
*- Śrī rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhī 142*

**May my tongue tremble with the desire to taste the nectar of Sri Radha’s name. May my feet follow Her footprints in the forest of Vrindavan. May my hands work to please Her. May my heart meditate on Her feet. May I celebrate a great festival of love for the Lord of Her life.**

Rādhā-nāma-sudhā-rasaṁ rasayituṁ jihvāstu me vihvalā - May my tongue always be splendidly decorated with the glorious name of Sri Radha. And may that tongue always be eager to taste the nectar of Srimati Radharani’s names. Pādau tat-pada-kāṅkitāsu caratāṁ vṛndāṭavī-vīthiṣu - May my feet always wander where Sri Radharani walked; in these groves, small alleys and lanes of Vrindavan. Tat-karmaiva karaḥ karotu hṛdayaṁ tasyāḥ padaṁ dhyāyatāṁ- May my hands always be engaged in serving Srimati Radharani. May my heart always meditate upon Her reddish lotus feet. Tad-bhāvotsavataḥ paraṁ bhavatu me tat-prāṇa-nāthe ratiḥ May my heart always be inspired by the ecstatic emotions of Srimati Radharani. And may we also obtain such pure love as She has for the Lord of Her life, Shyamasundara Krishna. This is our heartfelt prayer to the lotus feet of Srimati Radharani.

In this way, many beautiful things are expressed and explained in Śrī rādhā-rasa-sudhānidhī. Rasika-bhaktas and even śuddha-bhaktas should read such books. By reading such divine and transcendental scriptures, one can get some knowledge and understanding of the glories of Srimati Radharani. By doing so, one can get darśana of Her bhāvas for Krishna. Greatly realised Vaisnavas have written on the topics of Sri Radha Shyamasundara and such topics are still being spoken about today by highly advanced Vaisnavas. These Vaisnavas have perfectly understood and realised the glories of the divine couple, Sri Radha and Sri Krishna. We should all take shelter of such great Vaisnavas.
