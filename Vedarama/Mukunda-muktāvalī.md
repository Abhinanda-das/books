# Mukunda-muktāvalī

## Mukunda-muktāvalī

*sarvādbhuta-camatkāra-*  
*līlā-kallola-vāridhiḥ \|*  
*atulya-madhura-prema-*  
*maṇḍita-priya-maṇḍalaḥ \|\|*  
*trijagan-mānasākarñi-*  
*muralī-kala-kūjitaḥ \|*  
*asamānordhva-rūpa-śrī-*  
*vismāpita-carācaraḥ \|\|*  
*śrī-vraja-nāgarāya namaḥ*

### Verse 1

*nava-jalada-varṇaṁ campakodbhāsi-karṇaṁ*  
*vikasita-nalināsyaṁ visphuran-manda-hāsyam*  
*kanaka-ruci-dukūlaṁ cāru-barhāvacūlaṁ*  
*kam api nikhila-sāraṁ naumi gopa-kumāram \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I bow to a certain child of the cowherds who happens to be the essence of everything. His color is like that of a new cloud. His ears are adorned with campaka flowers. His face is like a blooming lotus, with a broad, mild smile. His fine garment shines with the splendor of gold. He wears a beautiful peacock feather on the top of His head.**

### Verse 2

*mukha-jita-śarad-induḥ keli-lāvaṇya-sindhuḥ*  
*kara-vinihita-kandur ballavī-prāṇa-bandhuḥ*  
*vapur-upasṛta-reṇuḥ kakña-nikñipta-veṇur*  
*vacana-vaśaga-dhenuḥ pātu māṁ nanda-sūnuḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|mālinī\|\|**

**May the son of Nanda protect me. His face defeats the beauty of the autumn moon. He is the reservoir of playful youth and is dearer than life to the cowherd girls. In His hand He holds a ball. Dust is smeared all over His body. He keeps His flute tucked in the crook of His arm. His words keep the cows under control.**

### Verse 3

*dhvasta-duñṭa-śaṅkhacūḍa ballavī-kulopagūḍha*  
*bhakta-mānasādhirūḍha nīla-kaṇṭha-piñcha-cūḍa*  
*kaṇṭha-lambi-mañju-guñja keli-labdha-ramya-kuñja*  
*karṇa-varti-phulla-kunda pāhi deva māṁ mukunda \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord Mukunda, please protect me. You eliminated the wicked Śaṅkhacūḍa and You embrace all the cowherd girls. Your image is always mounted in the hearts of Your devotees. You wear a peacock feather on Your head and a charming necklace of guñja berries hangs from Your neck. In the course of Your enjoyments You come to the charming groves in the forest. Kunda flowers wind around Your ears.**

### Verse 4

*yajña-bhaṅga-rauñṭa-śakra-nunna-ghora-megha-cakra*  
*vṛñṭi-pura-khinna-gopa-vīkñaṇopajāta-kopa*  
*kñipra-savya-hasta-padma-dhāritocca-śaila-sadma*  
*gupta-goñṭha rakña rakña māṁ tathādya paṅkajākña \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|citrā\|\|**

**O lotus-eyed one, please protect me, just today please protect me. When Indra became furious because You ruined his sacrifice and he sent a horde of fierce rain clouds, You were angered to see the cowherds distressed by the rainfall. Without delay you picked up the whole tall hill in your left lotus hand and thus saved the cowherd community.**

### Verse 5

*muktā-hāraṁ dadhad-uḍu-cakrākāraṁ*  
*sāraṁ gopī-manasi manojāropī*  
*kopī kaṁse khala-nikurambottaṁse*  
*vaṁśe raṅgī diśatu naḥ śārṅgī \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O wielder of the Śārṅga bow, supreme enjoyer, please consider me a member of Your family. You wear a precious pearl necklace shaped like the half moon. You were furious at Kaṁsa, the crown jewel of the whole tribe of cruel fiends.**

### Verse 6

*līloddāmā jaladhara-mālā-śyāmā*  
*kñāmāḥ kāmād abhiracayantī rāmāḥ*  
*sā mām avyād akhila-munīnāṁ stavyā*  
*gavyā-pūrtiḥ prabhor agha-śatror mūrtiḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|jaladhara-mālā\|\|**

**The form of my Lord, the enemy of Agha, is always engaged without restraint in pursuing its pleasure. It is dark blue like a bank of rainclouds and fatigues the young women with the urges of Cupid. May that form, glorified by all sages and always giving pleasure to the cows, protect me.**

### Verse 7

*parva-vartula-śarvarī-pati-garva-rīti-harānanaṁ*  
*nanda-nandanam indirākṛta-vandanaṁ dhṛta-candanam \|*  
*sundarī-rati-mandirīkṛta-kandaraṁ dhṛta-mandaraṁ*  
*kuṇḍala-dyuti-maṇḍala-pluta-kandharaṁ bhaja sundaram \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just worship that beautiful son of Nanda. His face defeats the pride of the full moon. The goddess of fortune offers Him homage. He likes to wear sandal paste. He makes the caves into palaces for amorous sports with beautiful girls. He is the one who lifted Mandara Mountain. His shoulders bask in the effulgence of His earrings.**

### Verse 8

*gokulāṅgana-maṇḍanaṁ kṛta-pūtanā-bhava-mocanaṁ*  
*kunda-sundara-dantam ambuja-vṛnda-vandita-locanam \|*  
*saurabhākara-phulla-puñkara-visphurat-kara-pallavaṁ*  
*daivata-vraja-durlabhaṁ bhaja ballavī-kula-vallabham \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|raṅginī\|\|**

**Just worship the beloved of all the cowherd girls, whom countless demigods can hardly achieve. He decorates the courtyards of Gokula. He freed Pūtana from birth and death. His teeth show the beauty of kunda flowers and His eyes receive the worship of millions of lotus flowers. His tender hands open like two fragrant, blooming lotuses.**

### Verse 9

*tuṇḍa-kānti-daṇḍitoru-pāṇḍurāṁśu-maṇḍalaṁ*  
*gaṇḍa-pālitāṇḍa-bāliśāli-ratna-kuṇḍalam \|*  
*phulla-puṇḍarīka-ñaṇḍa-kḷpta-mālya-maṇḍanaṁ*  
*caṇḍa-bāhu-daṇḍam atra naumi kaṁsa-khaṇḍanam \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I bow down here and now to the one who crushed Kaṁsa. The glow of His smiling mouth exerts punishment on the pale yellow-white rays of the moon. The jewel-like circle of innocent young girls takes shelter of His cheeks. He is decorated with a garland made of many blooming lotus flowers. His arms are fearsome royal clubs.**

### Verse 10

*uttaraṅgad-aṅga-rāga-saṅgamāti-piṅgalas*  
*tuṅga-śṛṅga-saṅgi-pāṇi-raṅganāti-maṅgalaḥ \|*  
*dig-vilāsi-malli-hāsi-kīrti-valli-pallavas*  
*tvāṁ sa pātu phulla-cāru-cillir adya ballavaḥ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|tūṇakam\|\|**

**May that cowherd boy grant You His protection without delay. His body is smeared yellow all over from the cosmetics falling from His face. The sight of Him waving His hand while holding a long buffalo horn is most auspicious. The sprouting creeper of His youthful fame ridicules the pride of the richest possessors of wealth in all parts of the world. His beauty is like that of a blooming cilli flower.**

### Verse 11

*indra-nivāraṁ vraja-pati-vāraṁ*  
*nirdhuta-vāraṁ hṛta-ghana-vāram \|*  
*rakñita-gotraṁ prīṇita-gotraṁ*  
*tvāṁ dhṛta-gotraṁ naumi sa-gotram \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer homage to the restrainer of Indra, the treasure of the King of Vraja, and to all of His family. He dispelled the obstacles sent by Indra, giving shelter from the downpour from Indra’s clouds by lifting the great hill. He thus saved the cowherd community and pleased the cows.**

### Verse 12

*kaṁsa-mahīpati-hṛd-gata-śūlaṁ*  
*santata-sevita-yāmuna-kūlam \|*  
*vande sundara-candraka-cūlaṁ*  
*tvām aham akhila-carācara-mūlam \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I give my respects to You, who were a trident piercing the heart of King Kaṁsa. You resorted always to the banks of the Yamunā, Your head bathed in beautiful moonlight. You are the root of all beings, both moving and nonmoving.**

### Verse 13

*malayaja-ruciras tanu-jita-mudiraḥ*  
*pālita-vidhus toñita-vasudhaḥ \|*  
*mām ati-rasikaḥ kelibhir adhikaḥ*  
*sita-subhaga-radaḥ kṛpayatu vara-daḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Please show Me Your mercy. The beauty of Your body, splendidly decorated with sandal paste, defeats the beauty of the clouds. You are the cooling moon for those under Your shelter, and You give delight to mother earth. You excell in all loving enchanges and know better than all others how to enjoy life. Your sparking white teeth bestow all auspiciousness.**

### Verse 14

*urarīkṛta-kurarī-ruta-bhaṅgaṁ*  
*nava-jala-dhara-kiraṇollasad-aṅgam \|*  
*yuvati-hṛdaya-dhṛta-madana-taraṅgaṁ*  
*praṇamata yāmuna-taṭa-kṛta-raṅgam \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|pajjhaṭikā\|\|**

**Everyone, just offer obeisances to Him as He takes His pleasure on the banks of the Yamunā, imitating the call of the kurarī birds, the effulgence of His limbs outshining the brilliance of new clouds. Thus He produces waves of Cupid’s attraction in hearts of young girls.**

### Verse 15

*navāmbhoda-nīlaṁ jagat-toña-śīlaṁ*  
*mukhāsaṅgi-vaṁśaṁ śikhaṇḍāvataṁsam \|*  
*karālambi-vetraṁ varāmbhoja-netraṁ*  
*dhṛta-sphīta-guñjaṁ bhaje labdha-kuñjam \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Dark blue like a new cloud, He naturally gives joy to the whole world. The flute is His mouth’s constant companion, and He always wears a peacock feather on His head. A herding stick hangs from His hand. His eyes are like the most gorgeous lotuses, and He decorates Himself abundants with guñja berries. I worship Him as He enters the forest groves.**

### Verse 16

*hṛta-kñauṇi-bhāraṁ kṛta-kleśa-hāraṁ*  
*jagad-gīta-sāraṁ mahā-ratna-hāram \|*  
*mṛdu-śyāma-keśaṁ lasad-vāmya-veśaṁ*  
*kṛpābhir na deśaṁ bhaje ballaveśam \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|bhujaṅga-prayātam\|\|**

**I worship that Lord of the cowherds who removes of the burden of the earth and also removes the pains souls have created for themselves. The songs He sings are cherished throughout the world, and He wears necklaces of rare jewels. His hair is soft and dark blue. His dress shines exquisitely. His mercy knows no equal.**

### Verse 17

*ullasad-ballavī-vāsasāṁ taskaras*  
*tejasā nirjita-prasphurad-bhāskaraḥ \|*  
*pīna-doḥ-stambhayor ullasac-candanaḥ*  
*pātu vaḥ sarvato devakī-nandanaḥ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the darling son of Devakī protect you from all dangers. He steals the beautiful garments of the cowherd girls and conquers the radiant sun with His effulgence. His well-formed pillar-like arms are decorated with sandal paste.**

### Verse 18

*saṁsṛtes tārakaṁ taṁ gavāṁ cārakaṁ*  
*veṇunā maṇḍitaṁ krīḍane paṇḍitam \|*  
*dhātṛbhir veñiṇaṁ danava-dveñiṇaṁ*  
*cintaya svāminaṁ ballavī-kāminam \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|sragviṇī\|\|**

**Just think of Him, the deliverer from the cycle of birth and death, the tender of the cows, the expert in playing games who always has His flute with Him. His mothers dress Him nicely, He opposes the hostility of the demons, and He takes pleasure in sporting with the cowherd girls.**

### Verse 19

*upātta-kavalaṁ parāga-śabalaṁ*  
*sad-eka-śaraṇaṁ saroja-caraṇam \|*  
*ariñṭa-dalanaṁ vikṛñṭa-lalanaṁ*  
*namāmi sa-mahaṁ sadaiva tam aham \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I constantly bow down to Him and all His glories. He carries a half-eaten morsel of food and is dirty from the dust of the forest, but His lotus feet are the only shelter of great saints. He beat Ariñṭa down and attracted the gopīs.**

### Verse 20

*vihāra-sadanaṁ manojña-radanaṁ*  
*praṇīta-madanaṁ śaśaṅka-vadanam \|*  
*uraḥstha-kamalaṁ yaśobhir amalaṁ*  
*karātta-kamalaṁ bhajasva tam alam \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|jaloddhata-gatiḥ\|\|**

**You need do nothing other than worship Him, the abode of all transcendental pastimes. He defeats Cupid with His face like the moon and its charming teeth.**

### Verse 21

*duñṭa-dhvaṁsaḥ karṇikārāvataṁsaḥ*  
*khelad-vaṁśī pañcama-dhvāna-śaṁsī \|*  
*gopī-cetaḥ-keli-bhaṅgi-niketaḥ*  
*pātu svairī hanta vaḥ kaṁsa-vairī \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Oh, may that wanton enemy of Kaṁsa protect You. He destroys the wicked as He wears karṇikāra flowers on His head and plays the fifth note on His flute. In Him reside all the permutations of loving sport that flirt in the minds of the gopīs.**

### Verse 22

*vṛndāṭavyāṁ kelim ānanda-navyāṁ*  
*kurvan nārī-citta-kandarpa-dhārī \|*  
*narmodgārī māṁ dukūlāpahārī*  
*nīpārūḍhaḥ pātu barhāvacūḍaḥ \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|śālinī\|\|**

**May He protect Me, a peacock feather on His head, acting like a swarm of Cupids in the minds of young women with His sports in the ecstatic, ever-fresh Vṛndāvana forest, climbing a nīpa tree and then cutting jokes with the girls and stealing their garments.**

### Verse 23

*rucira-nakhe racaya sakhe*  
*valita-ratiṁ bhajana-tatim \|*  
*tvam aviratis tvarita-gatir*  
*nata-śaraṇe hari-caraṇe \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Dear friend, please constantly direct your affection and all your worship at the feet of Hari with their attractive nails, the shelter of surrendered souls.**

### Verse 24

*rucira-paṭaḥ pulina-naṭaḥ*  
*paśupa-gatir guṇa-vasatiḥ \|*  
*sa mama śucir jalada-rucir*  
*manasi parisphuratu hariḥ \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|tvarita-gatiḥ\|\|**

**May Hari manifest in my heart as He, the residence of all good qualities, wears His charming dress, dancing on the shore of the river, moving about with the cowherd boys. His all-pure effulgence shines like the clouds.**

### Verse 25

*keli-vihita-yamulārjuna-bhañjana*  
*sulalita-carita-nikhila-jana-rañjana \|*  
*locana-nartana-jita-cala-khañjana*  
*māṁ paripālaya kāliya-gañjana \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Please protect Me, O insulter of Kāliya. You broke the pair of Arjuna trees in the course of Your playing. You delight the whole world with You most sweet pastimes. The dancing of Your eyes puts to shame the jumping khañjana birds.**

### Verse 26

*bhuvana-visṛtvara-mahimāḍambara*  
*viracita-nikhila-khalotkara-saṁvara \|*  
*vitara yaśodā-tanaya varaṁ varam*  
*abhilañitaṁ me dhṛta-pītāmbara \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Your countless glories fill the ends of the universe. You have managed to subdue every single wicked person. O son of Yaśodā, You wear a yellow garment. Please bestow on me my cherished desire again and again.**

### Verse 27

*cikura-karambita-cāru-śikhaṇḍaṁ*  
*bhāla-vinirjita-vara-śaśi-khaṇḍam \|*  
*rada-rucir nirdhūta-mudrita-kundaṁ*  
*kuruta budhā hṛdi sapadi mukundam \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O wise souls, at once let Mukunda into Your hearts. A splendid peacock feather mixes with the hairs on His head, and His forehead defeats the beauty of the half moon. His teeth shine more brilliantly than the closed blossoms of kunda flowers.**

### Verse 28

*yaḥ parirakñita-surabhī-lakñas*  
*tad api ca surabhī-mardana-dakñaḥ \|*  
*muralī-vādana-khuralī-śālī*  
*sa diśatu kuśalaṁ tava vana-mālī \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|pajjhaṭikā\|\|**

**He who takes care of hundreds of thousands of surabhī cows is also expert in ravishing the fragrant gopīs. As He constantly plays on His flute and wears His garlands of forest flowers, may He bestow auspiciousness on you.**

### Verse 29

*ramita-nikhila-ḍimbe veṇu-pītoñṭha-bimbe*  
*hata-khala-nikurambe ballavī-datta-cumbe \|*  
*bhavatu mahita-nande tatra vaḥ keli-kande*  
*jagad-avirala-tunde bhaktir urvī mukunde \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May you all attain vast devotion for Mukunda, who delights all the cowherd boys, keeps His lips placed on His flute, kills hordes of envious demons, and leaves His kisses on the cowherd girls. He raises the good reputation of Nanda. He is a treasure mine of loving sports. The entire universe is contained in His navel.**

### Verse 30

*paśupa-yuvatī-goñṭhī-cumbita-śrīmad-oñṭhī*  
*smara-taralita-dṛñṭi-nirmitānanda-vṛñṭiḥ \|*  
*nava-jala-dhara-dhāmā pātu vaḥ kṛñṇa-nāmā*  
*bhuvana-madhura-veśā mālinī muktir eñā \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**\|\|mālinī\|\|**

**His beautiful lips are kissed by the assembly of daughters of the cowherds, and He creates a downpour of ecstasy for those whose eyes are disturbed by Cupid. His efflugence is like that of new rain clouds. The way He dresses charms all the worlds. He wears beautiful garlands. May that source of liberation named Kṛñṇa give you His protection.**

*iti śrī-mukunda-muktāvalī*
