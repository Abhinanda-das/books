# Nrsimha-stava

## Śrī Nṛsiṁha-kavaca-stotra from Śrī Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa

### Text 1-2

*nṛsiṁha-kavacaṁ vakṣye*  
*prahlādenoditaṁ purā*  
*sarva-rakṣa-karaṁ puṇyaṁ*  
*sarvopadrava-nāśanam*  
*sarva-sampat-karaṁ caiva*  
*svarga-mokṣa-pradāyakam*  
*dhyātvā nṛsiṁhaṁ deveśaṁ*  
*hema-siṁhāsana-sthitam*

**Пословный перевод:**

*nṛsiṁha*—of Lord Nṛsiṁha; *kavacam*—the armor; *vakṣye*—I will speak; *prahlādena*—by Prahlāda Mahārāja; *uditam*—spoken; *pura*—before; *sarva*—all; *rakṣa*—protection; *karam*—doing; *puṇyam*—sacred; *sarvopadrava-nāśanam*—destroying all obstacles; *sarva-sampat-karam*—bringing all good fortune; *ca*—and; *eva*—indeed; *svarga-mokṣa-pradāyakam*—giving liberation; *dhyātvā*—meditating; *nṛsiṁham*—on lord Nṛsiṁha; *deveśam*—the master of the demigods; *hema-siṁhāsana-sthitam*—sitting on a golden throne.

**Перевод:**

**Now I will speak the sacred armor of Lord Nṛsiṁha, which Prahlāda Mahārāja spoke in ancient times, and which gives all protection, destroys all obstacles, and brings good fortune, opulence, and liberation. First one should meditate on Lord Nṛsiṁha, the master of the demigods, who is sitting on a golden throne, . . .**

**. . . whose mouth is wide open, who has three eyes, who is splendid like an autumn moon, whose left side is embraced by the goddess of fortune, whose form is the shelter of great powers and opulences, . . .**

### Text 7

**Перевод:**

**May Lord Nṛsiṁha, who protects the entire world, protect my head.**

*udaya-ravi-sahasra-dyotitaṁ rūkṣa-vīkṣaṁ*  
*pralaya-jaladhi-nādaṁ kalpa-kṛd-vahni-vaktram*  
*sura-pati-ripu-vakṣaś-cheda-raktokṣitāṅgaṁ*  
*praṇata-bhaya-haraṁ taṁ nārasiṁhaṁ namāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

*udaya*—rising; *ravi*—suns; *sahasra*—thousands; *dyotitam*—splendid; *rūkṣa*—angry; *vīkṣam*—glance; *pralaya*—of devastation; *jaladhi*—ocean; *nādam*—sound; *kalpa*—kalpa; *kṛt*—doing; *vahni*—fire; *vaktram*—mouth; *sura*—of the demigods; *pati*—of the king; *ripu*—of the enemy; *vakṣaḥ*—the chest; *cheda*—breaking; *rakta*—with blood; *ukṣita*—sprinkled; *aṅgam*—limbs; *praṇata*—of the surrendered souls; *bhaya*—the fears; *haram*—removing; *tam*—to Him; *nārasiṁham*—Lord Nṛsiṁha; *namāmi*—I bow down.

**Перевод:**

**I bow down before Lord Nṛsiṁha, who is splendid like thousands of rising suns, whose eyes are filled with anger, whose roar is like the roaring of the ocean at the time of the universe's destruction, whose face is like the blazing fires at the end of the kalpa, whose limbs are sprinkled with blood from the broken chest of Indra's enemy, and who removes the fears of His surrendered devotees.**

### Text 1

*pralaya-ravi-karālākāra-ruk-cakra-vālaṁ*  
*virala-yad-uru-rocī-rocitāśāntarāla*  
*prati-bhayatama-kopāty-utkaṭoccāṭṭa-hāsin*  
*daha daha narasiṁhāsahya-vīrāhitaṁ me*

**Пословный перевод:**

*pralaya*—destruction; *ravi*—sun; *karāla*—fearsome; *ākāra*—form; *ruk*—of light; *cakra-vālaṁcircle; virala-yad-uru-rocī*—great splendor; *rocita*—illuminated; *āśāntarāla*—the directions; *prati-bhayatama*—great fear; *kopā*—anger; *aty-utkaṭoccāṭṭa-hāsin*—with loud laughter; *daha*—burn; *daha*—burn; *narasiṁha*—O Lord Nṛsiṁha; *asahya*—invincible; *vīra*—power; *ahitaṁ*—inauspicious; *me*—of me.

**Перевод:**

**O Lord whose splendor fills the directions with light, O Lord effulgent like the sun at the time of the universe's destruction, O invincible, all powerful Lord whose loud and angry laughter fills the demons with fear, O invincible, all-powerful Lord Nṛsiṁha, please burn, burn into ashes my enemies and my sins.**
