# Nāmāṣṭaka

## Introduction

From the Caitanya-caritamrta, we understand that Srila Rupa Gosvami was recognized by Lord Caitanya Mahaprabhu and His associates for his ability to understand the Lord’s confidential teachings on various topics, one of them being the spiritual sweetness of the holy name (nama-madhurya) when chanted by perfect pure devotees of Lord Krsna. On the Lord’s order, Srila Rupa Gosvami composed extraordinary works such as Bhakti-rasamrta-sindhu and Laghu- Bhagavatamrta establishing the scriptural basis of the Lord’s teachings on the spiritual mellows of the perfection of pure devotional service to Sri Sri Radha Krsna and Their intimate associates. He also wrote several prayers that were later compiled and published by his intimate disciple Srila Jiva Gosvami under the name of Stava-mala (“Prayers”). It appears that following the logic of madhurena samapayet, “One should end a meal with a sweet”, Srila Jiva Gosvami placed the Namastaka, eight verses on the glories of the holy name, at the very end of Stava-mala.

A few generations after the disappearance of the six Gosvamis, when the study of Acarya Rupa’s writings were being neglected, Srila Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura commented on several of his major works, while his powerful student Srila Baladeva Vidyabhusana commented on his remaining major works, one of them being Stava-mala. Srila Vidyabhusana’s commentary reveals deep insights into the power, significance and method of offenselessly chanting the holy names of Lord Krsna.

It was around a century later that Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura started summarizing the teachings of the Gosvamis and highlighting their key precepts through his own several writings. Some of his publications were translations and versified paraphrases of selected prayers of Srila Rupa and Srila Raghunatha dasa Gosvami in Bengali. One of those prayers is this very Namastaka. With his characteristic penetrating intelligence, Srila Bhaktivinoda clarified in simple language some of the complicated concepts presented in Srila Baladeva Vidyabhusana’s Sanskrit commentary through a versified paraphrase in Bengali. When Thakura organized his Nama Hatta preaching, he published the Namastaka with his Bengali translation and this versified paraphrase in pamphlets, and had them distributed in great numbers.

In this edition, we have transliterated Srila Rupa Gosvami’s Namastaka along with Srila Baladeva Vidyabhusana’s commentary and Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura’s translation and versified paraphrase (which we refer to here as “explanation”). For the sake of clarity, we have added various footnotes and added two appendices containing Srila Prabhupada’s statements. We are also separately publishing the Sanskrit texts of Namastaka with the commentary, along with the Bengali translation and explanation. These will be available separately as digital editions free for download from www.ruparaghunathavani.comand together in the form of a printed book available for purchase. In this digital edition, the transliteration is provided in red for easy reading; the annotations contain hyperlinks to quotes from scriptures explained by Srila Prabhupada.

Learned criticisms of the annotated translation along with suggestions for correction are welcome. I also take this opportunity to seek the kind blessings of the Vaisnava community.

On behalf of Rupa Raghunatha Van! Publications,

Vidvan Gauranga dasa

## Mulam 1

*nikhila-sruti-mauli-ratna-mala- dyuti-nlrajita-pada-pahkajanta /*  
*ayi mukta-kulair upasyamanam paritas tvam harinama*

**Перевод:**

**O Hari-nama! The tips of the toes of Your lotus feet are constantly being worshiped by the glowing radiance emanating from the string of gems known as the Upanisads, the crown jewels of all the Vedas. You are eternally adored by liberated souls such as Narada and Sukadeva. O Hari-nama! I take complete shelter of You.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

O holy name of Hari, I take full shelter of You in every way. All chief Srutis refer to the Upanisads. They are themselves the jewels.O holy name, the tips of whose lotus feet in the form of the nails of Your lotus feet are worshipped by their radiances! This should be understood in terms of the nondifference of the holy name and the person referred to by the holy name. Others understand this to be: O holy name, the tips of whose lotus feet are worshipped by the radiances of jewels on the heads of all personified Vedasin accordance with the Smrti text, “wherein reside the personified Vedas”.And the Srutis are the ones such as “All the Vedas glorify whose position in hymns”. The implied meaning is that they do so having established His position as their final intent of description and as the highest goal of human existence. I take full shelter of You of what nature? He states that now: You are worshipped even by the liberated souls.He states this on the basis of Sruti and Smrti texts such as, “He continues singing this Sama hymn”, “Glorification of the Lord’s activities are sung by those released from material thirsts”, “O King, constant chanting of the holy name of the Lord after the ways of the great authorities is the doubtless and fearless way of success for all, including those who are free from all material desires, those who are desirous of all material enjoyment, and also those who are self-satisfied by dint of transcendental knowledge \[yogis\].”“Yogis” in this verse means “those in communion with the Supreme Personality of Godhead”; the meaning is “those who are liberated”.

**Перевод:**

**O holy name of Hari! All of the Upanisads, the jewels adorning the heads of the Vedas, perform an arati with their rays to Your lotus feet. You are being worshiped in every way by eternally liberated living entities; I take shelter of Your lotus feet.**

**Комментарий. Автор Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura:**

The son of Saci preached the glories of His own holy name through the mouth of Sri Rupa Gosvami. Lord Hari is Himself His holy name; there is no difference between them. That holy name is true, thus sing the Vedas. The effulgence of the jewel necklaces of the Upanisads shine brilliantly around Your lotus feet. They perform an auspicious aratrika to You at every moment with a ten-flamed lamp. In the fourteen planetary systems, those devas, humans and danavas whose fortune is strong constantly drink the nectar of the rasa of the holy name, having given up the activities of karma and jnana. And the eternally liberated souls constantly engage in the worship of the holy name by singing Sama hymns. They reside in Goloka and sing the holy names ceaselessly; they don’t know of any separation from the holy name. The two syllables ha-ri are the source of all rasa. In every manner I take shelter of this holy name. Palling at the lotus feet of the holy name, Bhaktivinoda says, I beg for a place at Your lotus feet.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Anticipating the objection, “How can I give My shelter to you who are overcome with sinful reactions?”, he states the text beginning jaya.

## Mulam 2

*jaya namadheya muni-vrnda-geya jana-ranjanaya paramaksarakrte /*  
*tvam anadarad api manag udiritam nikhilogra-tapa-patalim vilumpasi.*

**Перевод:**

**O holy name, O one sung by liberated souls, O one who has assumed a form of transcendental syllables for the pleasure of Your people! Upon being pronounced slightly, even out of disrespect, You destroy all of the terrible miseries of the chanter.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

O holy name, O one sung by the sages, all glories to You! The implied meaning is: Please reveal Your excellence characterized by Your burning up all sinful reactions. Upon being pronounced slightly, even out of disrespect as while chanting You in reference to something other than You, in jest and so on, You destroy all terrible miseries up to the subtle body. This is stated on the basis of Smrti texts such as, “Advanced transcendentalists know that chanting the holy name of the Lord as an assignation or jokingly, as musical entertainment or neglectfully neutralizes the effect of sinful life”, “Even those who utter the holy name of Visnu while joking, ridiculing and so on become perfect in their endeavors. My repeated obeisances to them too”, and “Just as a spark of fire bums simply on being touched, even by mistake, the holy name of Hari burns sinful reactions merely on being touched by the lips”. And so, remembering Your own influence, purify me; 1 will become a preacher of Your glory. This is the implied meaning. O one who has assumed a form of transcendental syllables for the pleasure of Your people! By this statement, His compassionate nature is revealed. Regarding the word paramaksara, a compound of parama and aksara: Because it belongs to the Sakandhv-adi group of compound words, the last vowel of the first component word is deleted and the first vowel of the second component word is left intact.On the basis of the Sruti statement “Om. O Visnu, knowing even slightly Your transcendental name and uttering it, we shall understand the glories of the holy name”and the Smrti statement “It is the transcendental fruit of all the Vedas and is purely spiritual”, the holy name possesses a form of spiritual syllables just as the body of Lord Krsna, the holy named, in forms such as Harhsa and Varaha, is spiritual.

**Перевод:**

**O holy name! All the sages always glorify You. You please the entire universe. You are a form of spiritual syllables. Even if one utters You to a slight extent with disrespect, You Yourself completely destroy all of the terrible sufferings of the conditioned souls. All glories to You!**

**Комментарий. Автор Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura:**

All glories, all glories to the holy name of Lord Hari, the abode of the nectar of spiritual bliss, the Supreme Truth in the form of syllables! Bestowing mercy upon Your own devotees, You have descended in the form of the holy names and bestowed unbounded mercy upon the living entities. All glories to Hari, Krsna and Rama.You are the excellent refuge of the residents of the entire universe. You please the minds of everyone. The sages constantly honor You, the holy name, singing You with their mouths wide open. O syllables of the holy name of Krsna, You possess all potencies to distribute auspiciousness to the living entities. Apart from You, there is no friend to rescue one from the ocean of material existence. You have come to rescue the living entities. You destroy all the living entity’s miseries if anyone even neglectfully calls You out once becoming humble and utterly destitute; I do not see any other means to counteract those miseries of the living entity. When one attains a slight glimpse of Your appearance, the terrible miseries go far away and the subtle body is easily dissolved. Bhaktivinoda says, “All glories, all glories to the holy name of Lord Hari! I remain offering You obeisances with the hope of attaining Your lotus feet.”

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

And the reflection of the holy name not only burns up sinful reactions and stops with that, but it also awakens pure devotional service unto the person referred to by the holy name. With this in mind, he states the text beginning yat.

## Mulam 3

*yad-abhaso ‘py udyan kavalita-bhava-dhvanta-vibhavo drsam tattvandhanam api disati bhakti-pranayinim /*  
*janas tasyodattam jagati bhagavan-nama-tarane krti te nirvaktum ka iha mahimanam prabhavati.*

**Перевод:**

**O sun of the holy name of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, which meritorious person in this world is able to enunciate the exalted glory of Your Lordship, whose mere reflection while arising destroys the powerful darkness of spiritual ignorance due to material existence and grants vision pertaining to pure devotional service even to those blind to the truths of spiritual existence?**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

O sun of the holy name of the Supreme Personality of Godhead = O sun of the holy name of Krsna, in this world, which meritorious = learned person is able to enunciate the exalted glory of Your Lordship? No one. This is the meaning. Anticipating the question, “Why?”, he states: Whose reflection = utterance in order to indicate someone other than You and so on destroys the powerful darkness of spiritual ignorance and offers vision = enlightenment pertaining to Krsna-bhakti even to those devoid of the vision of spiritual truths. Even a learned scholar would doubt such a glory of Your Lordship without the instructions of a person enlightened about You. Therefore, he is not able to enunciate it. This is the meaning. The meaning of this verse is very explicit in the Sruti text beginning Om asya because it is stated there that there is attainment of proper understanding of Lord Visnu for those who utter His holy name even slightly = in order to indicate someone other than You and so on.

**Перевод:**

**Where is that scholar in this world who is able to completely describe Your glory? When Your reflection arises, like sunrays covered by early morning fog, it is manifest in a form covered by darkness. But Your strength is of such an extent that within a short time You remove that covering and make the eyes of persons blind to spiritual truths fit to directly experience the Lord through pure devotional service.**

**Комментарий. Автор Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura:**

The sun of the holy name has arisen in the universe in order to destroy ignorance. All opulences of maya go away then. Therefore the saintly person is attached to the holy name. O holy name of Lord Hari, You remove the dense darkness of ignorance. What can know Your glory? Who is that scholar who can loudly explain Your glories? From the beginning when one sees Your reflection, the dense darkness of material existence is almost destroyed. Quickly, when that darkness is destroyed, You provide spiritual realization to the spiritually blinded eyes of the chanter. That spiritual realization is the state of devotional service and it generates consciousness of Lord Hari. This wonderful pastime is constantly Yours and Bhaktivinoda has understood its essence.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Now, he says that the holy name, when worshipped exclusively, destroys one’s prarabdha-karma, though it can be destroyed only through experience of its results, without such an experience of its results. He says this with the text beginning yad.

## Mulam 4

*yad-brahma-saksatkrti-nisthayapi vinasam ayati vina na bhogaih /*  
*apaiti nama sphuranena tat te prarabdha karmeti virauti vedah.*

**Перевод:**

**O holy name, the Veda loudly announces that prarabdha-karma, which does not attain destruction without experience of the results of that prarabdha-karma despite uninterrupted direct experience of the Supreme Brahman, goes away merely by Your appearance.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

O holy name, prarabdha-karma, which does not attain destruction without experience of the results of that prarabdha-karma even with uninterrupted meditation on the Supreme Soullike the uninterrupted flow of oil by which one can attain direct experience of the Supreme Brahman = the Supreme Soul, goes away = is disconnected simply by Your appearance on the tongue and so on. The Veda loudly announces this. It proclaims this. This is the meaning. By an increase of realization of the Supreme Being, destruction and disconnection of pious and impious reactions, both accumulated and operative, take place. For it is stated in the Sruti, “Certainly, this immortal crosses over both pious and impious reactions.” Pious and sinful reactions that have begun to yield their respective results are called prarabdha-karma. But that is destroyed only through experience of those results not by realization of the Supreme Being for it is stated in the Sruti, “For him who has attained realization of the Supreme Being, there is only that much delay as long as he is not freed from his prarabdha- karma.” This is how it has been ascertained by the great author of the Vedanta-sutra: “When there is realization of the Supreme Being, there is disconnection and destruction of the later and previous impious reactions, because it is declared so.”“But there is such a disconnection and destruction of the pious deeds too upon death.”“But only the previous aprarabdha pious and impious reactions are destroyed by realization of the Supreme Brahman, because that is the limit of the life of the spiritual practitioner.”The meaning of these sutras is as follows: “But upon the attainment of that realization of the Supreme Being, there is disconnection and destruction of the later and previous = of the operative and accumulated impious reactions, because it is declared so = because it is stated so in Sruti.” “There is also disconnection and destruction of the non-pious = of the pious reactions. But upon the fall of the body composed of one’s prarabdha-karma, there will be liberation.” “The previous aprarabdha-karma = the accumulated impious and pious reactions, but not the operative karmic reactions or prarabdha-karma too, are destroyed by realization of the Supreme Being because their destruction ends only in the experience of the results of such prarabdha-karma.” And that prarabdha-karma goes away by uttering the holy name for it is stated in the Sruti, “His name is ‘Ud’. This person is above all sins, and indeed causes others to rise above all sins. He who knows Him thus …” Here, the removal of prarabdha-karma is clear from the statement that all sinful reactions are removed by the worship of the holy name of the Supreme Personality of Godhead named “Ud”. With an intention to express this, the Satyayanis recite these Vedic hymns: “His sons attain his property, his well-wishers his pious reactions and his ill-wishers his impious reactions.” And the Kausitakis recite: “He removes his pious and impious reactions. His loving relatives attain his piety and those hateful of him attain his impiety.”The great author of the Vedanta-sutra states thus: “Therefore, regarding pious and impious reactions, for some, indeed, there is another text too.” Its meaning: It should be accepted that for some = for those exclusively dedicated to the holy name and who are extremely anxious to attain the Supreme Personality of Godhead, without experience of the results of prarabdha-karma, there is disconnection of prarabdha-karma in the form of pious and impious reactions because there is a set of Sruti texts that propound that teaching, such as “His sons attain his property...”, that are different from the set of Sruti texts such as “For him who has attained realization of the Supreme Being, there is only that much delay….” stating that prarabdha-karma can be destroyed through experience of its results.

**Перевод:**

**Despite attaining the state of direct experience of Brahman, one’s: prarabdha-karma is not destroyed without experience of its results. But, O holy name, all of the Vedas state that merely by a glimpse of Your appearance one’s prarabdha-karma is destroyed.**

**Комментарий. Автор Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura:**

The jnani endeavors to carry out jnana-yoga and directly experiences Brahman. Upon such direct experience of Brahman, the practitioner’s aprarabdha-karma is removed by complete development of jnana. Even then, one’s prarabdha-karma is never destroyed without experience of the results of prarabdha-karma. The living entity who has attained Brahman realization attains birth and death so that he can attain experience of the results of his prarabdha-karma. But, O holy name, when You appear, the exclusive devotee no more has any prarabdha- or aprarabdha-karma. The Vedas sing this repeatedly. Upon Your appearance, the living entity’s heart is completely cleansed. All of the bondage of karma and jnana goes away; easily material existence is destroyed. Bhaktivinoda lifts his hands and says, “Take up the banner of the holy name and walk along sounding the drum of the holy name. You will surely obtain the direct audience of Lord Muralidhara, the holder of the flute.”

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

This holy name is very merciful because He has appeared in many forms to bestow a variety of bliss upon His devotees. With this in mind, he states the text beginning agha.

## Mulam 6

*vacyam vacakam ity udeti bhavato nama svarupa-dvayam purvasmat param eva hanta karunarh tatrapi janimahe /*  
*yas tasmin vihitaparadha-nivahah prani samantad bhaved asyenedam upasya so ‘pi hi sad-anandambudhau majjati.*

**Перевод:**

**O holy name, You have two forms—the verbally expressible and the verbal expression. Oh, we know that among them, the latter is more compassionate than the former. Indeed, even that creature who has committed all kinds of offenses to the former immerses into an ocean of spiritual bliss by worshipping the latter with his mouth.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

O holy name, two forms of You shine in existence: the verbally expressible and the verbal expression. The verbally expressible is the all-pervading form of consciousness and bliss—the Supreme Lord. The verbal expression is the collection of syllables such as krsna, govinda, etc. Among them, we know that the latter = the collection of syllables in the form of the verbal expression as mentioned above is more compassionate than the former = the spiritual form of the verbally expressible. Anticipating the question, “How so?”, he states, even that creature who has committed all kinds of offenses to the former = the verbally expressible form, having worshipped = having uttered in his mouth the latter = the verbal expression, his offenses having been destroyed, immerses in an ocean of spiritual bliss = in the happiness of love of Godhead. He become spiritually successful. This is the meaning because of the Smrti text, “There is no doubt that I certainly forgive the millions of offenses committed by him who utters, with faith, My names in this world.”The nondifference of the holy name and the holy named is because of the Smrti text, “The holy name of Krsna is transcendentally blissful. It bestows all spiritual benedictions, for it is Krsna Himself, the reservoir of all pleasure. Krsna’s name is complete, and it is the form of all transcendental mellows. It is not a material name under any condition, and it is no less powerful than Krsna Himself. Since Krsna’s name is not contaminated by the material qualities, there is no question of its being involved with maya. Krsna’s name is always liberated and spiritual; it is never conditioned by the laws of material nature. This is because the name of Krsna and Krsna Himself are identical.”

**Перевод:**

**O holy name, two forms of You, distinguished as the verbally expressible and the verbal expression are manifest; still we know for certain that the verbal expression is more compassionate than the verbally expressible because even if a living entity is an offender unto Your form of the verbally expressible, by worshipping You by uttering the form of the verbally expressed he becomes completely immersed in the ocean of spiritual bliss.**

**Комментарий. Автор Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura:**

You have two forms: the verbally expressible and the verbal expression. The verbally expressible is Your sacred form, a form of knowledge and bliss; the verbal expression is Your holy names such as Sri Krsna in the form of syllables, the blissful refuge for all living entities. Your unlimited manifestations in these two forms mercifully bestow Your pastimes to the living entities. But I know, O Lord, that the verbal expression is more merciful than the verbally expressible. This is an extraordinary fact. The Vedic statements affirm that there is no difference between the holy name and the holy named; still, the holy name is more compassionate than the holy named. If an offender unto Lord Krsna, with faith, and with all his life force calls out, “Rama, Krsna, Hari”, the offenses go far away, and he easily floats in an ocean of happiness that is the ocean of rasa. Having offended the verbally expressible, the form of the Supreme Lord, by taking shelter of the pure holy name, he is saved from those offenses. Bhaktivinoda begs at the lotus feet of Sri Rupa Gosvami for attraction to the holy name, the verbal expression, at every moment.

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

Anticipating the objection, “The offenses to the holy namedcan be destroyedby chanting the holy name consisting of thirty-two syllables.But how can the ten offenses to the holy name beginning with sadhu-ninda be destroyed?”, he states, “They too can be destroyed simply by chanting the holy name”with the text beginning sudita.

## Mulam 7

*suditasrita-janarti-rasaye ramya-cid-ghana-sukha-svarupine /*  
*nama gokula- mahotsavaya te krsna purna-vapuse namo namah.*

**Перевод:**

**O holy name, O Krsna, my repeated obeisances unto You who destroys the sufferings of those who have taken shelter of You, the form of pleasing concentrated spiritual bliss, a great festival for Gokula and one of perfect form.**

**Комментарий. Автор Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa:**

O holy name, O Krsna, my repeated obeisances unto You. “Of what nature are You to whom obeisances are offered?” He states that: To You by whom the sufferings, unto offenses unto Your holy names, of those who have taken shelter of You are destroyed for it is stated in the Smrti, “If somehow, by mistake, an offense to the holy name has occurred, by constantly engaging in nama-sankirtana, one should become exclusively surrendered to Him”and “The holy names when uttered tirelessly destroy the sinful reactions of those who are endowed with offenses to the holy names and they become the causes of attaining the goal of life”. ## Appendix 1

**Srila Prabhupada on Namastaka**

**Excerpt from Srimad-Bhagavatam** [**7.5.23-24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-7/5.23) **purport**

The hearing of the holy name has been described above. Now let us try to understand the chanting of the holy name, which is the second item in the consecutive order. It is recommended that such chanting be performed very loudly. In Srimad-Bhagavatam, Narada Muni says that without shame he began traveling all over the world, chanting the holy name of the Lord. Similarly, Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu has advised:

*trnad api sunicena taror api sahisnuna*  
*amanina manadena kirtamyah sada harih*

A devotee can very peacefully chant the holy name of the Lord by behaving more humbly than the grass, being tolerant like a tree and offering respects to everyone, without expecting honor from anyone else. Such qualifications make it easier to chant the holy name of the Lord. The process of transcendental chanting can be easily performed by anyone. Even if one is physically unfit, classified lower than others, devoid of material qualifications or not at all elevated in terms of pious activities, the chanting of the holy name is beneficial. An aristocratic birth, an advanced education, beautiful bodily features, wealth and similar results of pious activities are all unnecessary for advancement in spiritual life, for one can very easily advance simply by chanting the holy name. It is understood from the authoritative source of Vedic literature that especially in this age of Kali-yuga, people are generally short-living, extremely bad in their habits, and inclined to accept methods of devotional service that are not bona fide. Moreover, they are always disturbed by material conditions, and they are mostly unfortunate. Under the circumstances, the performance of other processes, such as yajna, dana, tapah and kriya—sacrifices, charity and so on—are not at all possible. Therefore it is recommended:

*harer nama harer nama harer namaiva kevalam*  
*kalau nasty eva nasty eva nasty eva gatir anyatha*

**“In this age of quarrel and hypocrisy the only means of deliverance is chanting of the holy name of the Lord. There is no other way. There is no other way. There is no other way.”**

Simply by chanting the holy name of the Lord, one advances perfectly in spiritual life. This is the best process for success in life. In other ages, the chanting of the holy name is equally powerful, but especially in this age, Kali-yuga, it is most powerful. Kirtanad eva krsnasya mukta- sangah param vrajet \[SB [12.3.51](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/3.51)\]: simply by chanting the holy name of Krsna, one is liberated and returns home, back to Godhead. Therefore, even if one is able to perform other processes of devotional service, one must adopt the chanting of the holy name as the principal method of advancing in spiritual life. Yajnaih sankirtana-prayair yajanti hi sumedhasah \[SB [11.5.32](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/5.32)\]: those who are very sharp in their intelligence should adopt this process of chanting the holy names of the Lord. One should not, however, manufacture different types of chanting. One should adhere seriously to the chanting of the holy name as recommended in the scriptures: Hare Krsna, Hare Krsna, Krsna Krsna, Hare Hare/ Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.

While chanting the holy name of the Lord, one should be careful to avoid ten offenses. From Sanat-kumara it is understood that even if a person is a severe offender in many ways, he is freed from offensive life if he takes shelter of the Lord’s holy name. Indeed, even if a human being is no better than a two-legged animal, he will be liberated if he takes shelter of the holy name of the Lord. One should therefore be very careful not to commit offenses at the lotus feet of the Lord’s holy name. The offenses are described as follows: (a) to blaspheme a devotee, especially a devotee engaged in broadcasting the glories of the holy name, (b) to consider the name of Lord Siva or any other demigod to be equally as powerful as the holy name of the Supreme Personality of Godhead (no one is equal to the Supreme Personality of Godhead, nor is anyone superior to Him), (c) to disobey the instructions of the spiritual master, (d) to blaspheme the Vedic literatures and literatures compiled in pursuance of the Vedic literatures, (e) to comment that the glories of the holy name of the Lord are exaggerated, (f) to interpret the holy name in a deviant way, (g) to commit sinful activities on the strength of chanting the holy name, (h) to compare the chanting of the holy name to pious activities, (i) to instruct the glories of the holy name to a person who has no understanding of the chanting of the holy name, (j) not to awaken in transcendental attachment for the chanting of the holy name, even after hearing all these scriptural injunctions.

There is no way to atone for any of these offenses. It is therefore recommended that an offender at the feet of the holy name continue to chant the holy name twenty-four hours a day. Constant chanting of the holy name will make one free of offenses, and then he will gradually be elevated to the transcendental platform on which he can chant the pure holy name and thus become a lover of the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

It is recommended that even if one commits offenses, one should continue chanting the holy name. In other words, the chanting of the holy name makes one offenseless. In the book Nama-kaumudi it is recommended that if one is an offender at the lotus feet of a Vaisnava, he should submit to that Vaisnava and be excused; similarly, if one is an offender in chanting the holy name, he should submit to the holy name and thus be freed from his offenses. In this connection there is the following statement, spoken by Daksa to Lord Siva: “I did not know the glories of your personality, and therefore I committed an offense at your lotus feet in the open assembly. You are so kind, however, that you did not accept my offense. Instead, when I was falling down because of accusing you, you saved me by your merciful glance. You are most great. Kindly excuse me and be satisfied with your own exalted qualities.”

One should be very humble and meek to offer one’s desires and chant prayers composed in glorification of the holy name, such as ayi mukta-kulair upasyamanam (Namastaka 1) and nivrtta-tarsair upagiyamanad (SB [10.1.4](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/1.4)). One should chant such prayers to become free from offenses at the lotus feet of the holy name.

Narada Muni’s direct experience of Godhead while he glorifies Him with Srila Prabhupada’s purports

**Srimad-Bhagavatam** [**1.6.32**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/6.32)

*deva-dattam imam vinam svara-brahma-vibhusitam*  
*murcchayitva hari-katham gdyamanas caramy aham*

deva—the Supreme Personality of Godhead (Sri Krsna); dattam—gifted by; imam—this; vinam—a musical stringed instrument; svara—singing meter; brahma—transcendental; vibhusitam—decorated with; murcchayitva—vibrating; hari-katham—transcendental message; gayamanah—singing constantly; carami—do move; aham—I.

**And thus I travel, constantly singing the transcendental message of the glories of the Lord, vibrating this instrument called a vina, which is charged with transcendental sound and which was given to me by Lord Krsna.**

Purport.

The musical stringed instrument called the vina, which was handed to Narada by Lord Sri Krsna, is described in the Linga Purana, and this is confirmed by Srila Jiva Gosvami. This transcendental instrument is identical with Lord Sri Krsna and Narada because all of them are of the same transcendental category. Sound vibrated by the instrument cannot be material, and therefore the glories and pastimes which are broadcast by the instrument of Narada are also transcendental, without a tinge of material inebriety. The seven singing meters, namely sa (sadja), r (rsabha), ga (gandhara), ma (madhyama), pa (pancama), dha (dhaivata) and ni (nisada), are also transcendental and specifically meant for transcendental songs. As a pure devotee of the Lord, Sri Naradadeva is always fulfilling his obligation to the Lord for His gift of the instrument, and thus he is always engaged in singing His transcendental glories and is therefore infallible in his exalted position. Following in the footsteps of Srila Narada Muni, a self-realized soul in the material world also properly uses the sound meters, namely sa, r, ga, ma, etc., in the service of the Lord by constantly singing His glories. As confirmed in the Bhagavad-Gita ([9.14](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.14)), the mahatmas, or great souls, have no business in life except singing the transcendental glories of the Lord, following in the footsteps of Srila Naradadeva.

**SB** [**1.6.33**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/6.33)

*pragayatah sva-viryani tirtha-padah priya-sravah*  
*ahuta iva me sighram darsanam yati cetasi*

pragayatah—thus singing; sva-viryani—own activities; tirtha-padah—the Lord, whose lotus feet are the source of all virtues or holiness; priya-sravah—pleasing to hear; ahutah—called for; iva—just like; me—to me; sighram—very soon; darsanam—sight; yati—appears; cetasi—on the seat of the heart.

**The Supreme Lord Sri Krsna, whose glories and activities are pleasing to hear, at once appears on the seat of my heart, as if called for, as soon as I begin to chant His holy activities**

Purport.

The Absolute Personality of Godhead is not different from His transcendental name, form, pastimes and the sound vibrations thereof. As soon as a pure devotee engages himself in the pure devotional service of hearing, chanting and remembering the name, fame and activities of the Lord, at once He becomes visible to the transcendental eyes of the pure devotee by reflecting Himself on the mirror of the heart by spiritual television. Therefore a pure devotee who is related with the Lord in loving transcendental service can experience the presence of the Lord at every moment. It is a natural psychology in every individual case that a person likes to hear and enjoy his personal glories enumerated by others. That is a natural instinct, and the Lord, being also an individual personality like others, is not an exception to this psychology because psychological characteristics visible in the individual souls are but reflections of the same psychology in the Absolute Lord. The only difference is that the Lord is the greatest personality of all and absolute in all His affairs. If, therefore, the Lord is attracted by the pure devotee’s chanting of His glories, there is nothing astonishing. Since He is absolute, He can appear Himself in the picture of His glorification, the two things being identical. Srila Narada chants the glorification of the Lord not for his personal benefit but because the glorifications are identical with the Lord. Narada Muni penetrates into the presence of the Lord by the transcendental chanting.

Here Thakura Bhaktivinoda mentions “hari, krsna, rama” while in the explanation to text 6, he mentions “rama, krsna, hari”. Both of them are, in fact, references to the mantra of thirty-two syllables: hare krsna hare krsna krsna krsna hare hare/hare rama hare rama rama rama hare hare. One of the descriptions of Lord Caitanya Mahaprabhu found in the Caitanya Upanisad discovered by Srila Bhaktivinoda is as follows: sa eva mula-mantram japati harir iti krsna iti rama iti. He chants the original mantra “Hari”, “Krsna”, “Rama” in japa. Srila Bhaktivinoda comments: sa gaura-svarupah paramesvarah hare krsna hare krsna krsna krsna hare hare /hare rama hare rama rama rama hare hare iti maha-prabala-mula-mantrarh jivan siksayan japati. That Supreme Lord in the form of Gaura, while teaching the living entities, chants in japa the greatly powerful original mantra hare krsna hare krsna krsna krsna hare hare /hare rama hare rama rama rama hare hare. In his Caitanya-siksamrta (6.4), the Thakura quotes Gopala Guru Gosvami’s explanation of how the holy names hari, krsna and rama become the maha-mantra: hari-sabdasya sambodhane hare, atha va... sri-radha-vacaka-hara-sabdasya sambodhane hare…. krsna-sabdasya sambodhane krsna… rama-sabdasya sambodhane rama. By rules of Sanskrit grammar, while addressing Lord Hari, the name hari is converted into its vocative form hare meaning “O Lord Hari!” Or else, while addressing Srimati Radharani, the name hara referring to Sri Radha is converted into its vocative form hare meaning “O Srimati Radharani!” The name krsna in its vocative form is krsna and the name rama in its vocative form is rama, both meaning “O Lord Krsna!” and “O Lord Rama!” Srila Gopala Guru points out that the name rama here can, in addition to meaning “Lord Rama”, also directly refer to the sportive enchanter of Srimati Radharani, Lord Krsna: sn-radhayas cittam dkrsya ramati kridati iti ramah. In a Bengali book titled Srila Prabhupadera Upadesamrta, statements of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakura compiled by his disciple Bhakti-mayukha Bhagavata Gosvami, we find the following statement on page 306: aisvarya-vicare ye sevonmukhata, tahate hare rama balite dasarathi ramakei bujhaya. kintu madhurya-para bhakta-gana radha-ramanakei rama baliya janena. tini nandera nandana. yekhane rama- sabde radha-ramanera seva vihita haya, sesthale hara-sabdera sambodhana-pade para-saktira akara-vigraha sri-radha-ranikei bujhaya. When one’s service attitude is by the consideration of aisvarya, hare rama refers to Lord Rama, the son of Dasaratha. But devotees in madhurya know Radha-ramana to be Rama. He is Nanda-nandana. When by the holy name rama service to Radha-ramana is provided, hare refers to the vocative form of the original form of the superior energy Sri Radharani. This explains how hare and rama have dual meanings. But we find that Srila Prabhupada only taught us that hare refers to Srimati Radharani and that it means radhe. Why did he provide such a singular explanation? On page 363 of the same above-mentioned compilation of statements of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati, we find the answer to this question in the form of this statement: keha keha hare krsna name hari-sabdera sambodhane hare vicara karena. yanra visaya-tattva apeksa adhikatara asraya-tattva- nistha arthat yandera seva-pravrtti adhikatara prakasita, tanra hara-sabdera sambodhane hare (arthat radhe) pada bujhiya thakena. sri-radha priti-dvara krsnera mana harana karena baliya tanhara ekati nama hara, krsna-mano harati iti hara. Some people consider that the hare in the Hare Krsna mantra is a vocative form of hari. Those who are more fixed in the shelter of prema-bhakti than in the object of prema-bhakti, that is, those whose service inclination is more manifest understand that hare is the vocative form of hara meaning radhe. One of the holy names of Sri Radha is hara as She captivates the mind of Lord Krsna. Here, “the shelter of prema-bhakti” refers to the predominated, the devotee of the Lord who possesses prema-bhakti, while “the object of prema-bhakti” refers to the predominator, Lord Krsna Himself. Radharani is the shelter and Lord Krsna is the object of prema-bhakti respectively.

This is the nama-abhasa stage of chanting. In this regard, an important statement by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakura in his Navadvipa-panjika distinguishing namabhasa from namaparadha and suddha-nama has been quoted in Sri Sri Hari-nama- mahimamrta, a book by Sri Anadi Krsna Bhakti-sastri, a follower of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakura, as follows: aparadha-yukta avasthaya kirtita hari-nama namaparadha-samjhaya abhihita. anicchakrta namaparadhera phale punya-karma- labhya svarga-sukha bhogadi labha haiya thake, icchakrta namaparadhera phale kukarmera phala naraka-yantranadi bhoga karite haya. aparadha-sunya sambandha-jhana-rahita avasthaya kirtita sri-hari-nama namabhasa-name khyata. sanketya, parihasya, stobha va helara sahita sri-nama kirtita haile namabhasa haya. ihdra phale jhani-ganera akanksanlya mukti labha haya. aparadha-sunya avasthaya sambandha-jhanera sahita kirtita namai suddha-nama. tahara phale sunirmala krsna-prema labha haiya thake. The meaning is: “When the holy name of Lord Hari is chanted in the offensive state, it is called namaparadha. As a result of unintentional namaparadha chanting, one can experience heavenly happiness, etc. that can be attained through pious activities; as a result of intentional namaparadha chanting, one has to experience the torments in hell, etc. that are the results of sinful activities. When the holy name of Lord Sri Hari is chanted in the offenseless state and without sambandha-jnana, it is known as namabhasa. When one chants the holy name while referring to something other than the Lord, in jest, for musical entertainment or neglectfully, such chanting is namabhasa. As a result of this, one attains the liberation hankered for by the jnanis. When one chants the holy name in the offenseless state and with sambandha-jnana, it is called suddha-nama (the pure holy name). As a result of such chanting, one attains spotless Krsna-prema.”

We learn from the Vedic scriptures, the higher sources of knowledge about the material world, that the moon bestows nectar. In Bhagavad-Gita 15.13, Lord Krsna describes the moon as rasatmaka, “that which supplies the juice of life”. In his purport, Srila Prabhupada has informed us that this means, “Everything becomes palatable by the agency of the Supreme Lord through the influence of the moon.” So, for the gopis in Goloka Vrndavana serving Lord Krsna in the rasa of conjugal love, He is the source of intense nectarean delight. Therefore Srila Rupa Gosvami has referred to Lord Krsna here as Gopi-candra.

In Sanskrit, the forms of the holy name are in the vocative case, that is, they are in the form with which we address the Supreme Lord. For instance, one of His holy names is nanda-sunu (“Son of Nanda”). But when we address Him in Sanskrit, by the rules of Sanskit grammar, we have to call Him as nanda-suno or he nanda-suno. Therefore we have he krsna (“O Krsna”) karuna-sindho (“O Karuna-sindhu” = “O ocean of mercy”) dina-bandho (“O Dma-bandhu” = “O friend of the lowly”) jagat-pate (“O Jagat-pati” = “O Lord of the universe”) gopesa (“O Gopesa” = “O Lord of the cowherd boys”) gopika-kanta (“O Gopika-kanta” = “O beloved lover of the cowherd girls”) radha-kanta (“O Radha-kanta” = “O beloved lover of Radha”) namo ’stu te (“my obeisances unto You”).

I have literally rendered nami-aparadha into “offenses to the holy named”. It refers to what are more commonly referred to as sevaparadha, offenses committed in Deity worship.

“Offenses... can be destroyed” means “the reactions to the offenses can be destroyed”.

It is a fact that the reactions to offenses committed in Deity worship unknowingly can be removed by chanting the thirty-two syllabled mantra which refers to hare krsna hare krsna krsna krsna hare hare / hare rama hare rama rama rama hare hare. However, if such offenses in Deity worship are committed on the strength of such chanting, they certainly become the equivalent of namaparadhas (committed knowingly) as mentioned by Srila Visvanatha Cakravarti Thakura in his Madhurya-kadambini, Chapter 3, while discussing removal of reactions to sevaparadha: sevaparadhanam tu namabhis tat-tan-nivartaka-stotra-pathaih seva-satatyena ca bhavyasya vivekinah prayah pratidinam evopasamendhkuri-bhavanupalabdheh. kintu tat-tad-upasama- sambhava-balena tatra savadhanata-saithilye sevaparadha api namaparadha eva syuh. tatha hy uktam—namno balad yasya hi papa buddhih iti. tatra nama ity upalaksanam bhakti-matrasyaivopasamakasya. dharma-sastre ’pi prayascitta-balena papacarane na tasya papasya ksayah pratyuta gadhataiva.

This should not be misunderstood. Srila Baladeva Vidyabhusana Prabhu is referring to destruction of the reactions to namaparadhas that have been committed unintentionally, not those that have been committed intentionally. This will become evident in his commentary to text 7.

This is the meaning. One might wonder, “Why bother about namaparadha committed unknowingly in the first place? After all, they were committed unknowingly and unintentionally!” But the matter is not settled so easily and irresponsibly. In the Lord’s system of jurisprudence, sinful activities are taken into account even if they are committed unknowingly or unintentionally as we see in the history of King Nrga found in Chapter 64 of the Tenth Canto of the Srimad-Bhagavatam. However, the holy name of Krsna is so overwhelmingly compassionate upon the conditioned souls that He does not consider namaparadha committed by them unknowingly or unintentionally, as a special exception to the general Vedic arrangement for punishing those who engage in forbidden activities even unknowingly or unintentionally.

This is another statement of Sri Narada to Sanat-kumara in the Padma Purana and it occurs in the Hari-bhakti-vilasa (11.526). But this verse should not be misunderstood.
