# Prasna Upanishad \[BS\]

## Praśna Upaniṣad

## Santi-pathah

*oṁ bhadraṁ karnebhiḥ śṛṇuyāma deva bhadraṁ pasyemākṣabhir yajatrāḥ*  
*sthirair aṅgas tuṣṭuvāsas tanūbhir vyasema deva-hitam yad ayuḥ*  
*om svasti na indro vṛddhaśravāḥ*  
*svasti nah puṣā viśvavedāḥ*  
*svasti nas tārkṣyā’ riṣṭanemiḥ*  
*svasti no bṛhaspatir dadhātu*  
*Om śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ hariḥ oṁ*

**Перевод:**

**Devas of the senses,**  
**By your mercy with our ears we hear the auspicious message of the Upaniṣads and see with our eyes auspicious objects. With firm limbs and healthy body, engaged in praising the Lord, may we live a long spiritual life.**  
**May the lord of unequalled glories bestow blessings on us.**  
**May the Lord, the nourishing sun, giving all knowledge, and help us finish our studies without obstacle.**  
**May Garuḍa, carrier of Viṣṇu, who bears the cakra, take us to our goal.**  
**May the Lord of words, the lord of intelligence, bestow power to learn!**

## First question

## 1.

*oṁ sukeśo ca bhāradvājaḥ śaibyaś ca satya-kāmaḥ*  
*sauryāyaṇī ca gārgyaḥ kausalyaś cāśvalāyanaḥ*  
*bhārgavo vaidarbhiḥ kabandhī kātyāyanas te haite brahma-parā*  
*brahma-niṣṭhāḥ paraṁ brahmānveṣamāṇāḥ eṣa ha vai tat sarvaṁ*  
*vakṣyatīti, te ha samit-paṇayo bhagavantaṁ pippalādam upāsannāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Sukeśa, Satya-kāma, Saurayāṇī, Kausalya, Vaidarbhi and Kabandhī, knowledgeable of the Vedas and understanding the meaning, dedicated to the Vedas, approached Pippalāda with fuel in their hands, thinking that he would explain everything.**

**Комментарий:**

Sukeśa was the son of Bharadvaja (bhāradvājaḥ). The son of Śibi (śaibyaḥ) was Satya-kāmaḥ. Sūryāyaṇa’’s son was Sauryāyaṇī in Garga’s line. Kausalya was the son of Aśvalāyaṇa. The son of Vidarbhih in Bhārgava’s lineage was named Vaidarbhiḥ. Kabandhī came in Kātyāyana’s line. All these sages were famous (ha). These six were dedicated to the Vedas (brahma-parāḥ). They understood the meaning of the Vedas (brahma-niṣṭhāḥ). Or they were skilful in the Vedas (brahma-parāḥ) and fixed in tapas (brahma-niṣṭhāḥ) since knowing Vedānta is called tapas. They enquired about the most attractive (param) object (brahma), supreme by svarūpa and qualities. Pippalāda (eṣaḥ) is famous (ha) as a knower of Brahman. Vai is for emphasis. Thinking that he was able to explain to the six people the meaning, holding fuel in their hands according to scripture rules, they approached worshippable (bhagavantam) Pippalāda.

## 2.

*tān ha sa ṛsir uvāca bhūya eva tapasā brahmacāryeṇa*  
*śraddhayā saṁvatsaraṁ saṁvatsyatha*  
*yathākāmaṁ praśnān pṛcchata*  
*yadi vijñāsyāmaḥ sarvaṁ ha vo vakṣyāma iti*

**Перевод:**

**The sage said, “You should stay here for a year, performing tapas and brahmacārya with faith. Then you may ask as many questions as you wish. If I know, I will explain everything to you.”**

**Комментарий:**

Though you were previously endowed with tapas, brahmacārya and faith, in order to receive brahma-vidyā, you should stay here one year, practicing tapas in the form drying out the body, faith in scriptures and brahmacārya in the form of rejection of eight types of association with women: remembering, glorifying, playing with, seeing, privately speaking with, desiring union with, endeavouring for union and engaging in union with women. Then you can ask whatever you want. If I know the answers, without cheating, I will tell you. If you are not inclined to practice tapas etc. for a year, not being convinced that I have knowledge, then you may go. One should humbly serve without trying to test whether the guru has knowledge. The guru also should not be too eager to accept disciples.

## 3.

*atha kabandhī kātyāyana upetya papraccha*  
*bhagavan kuto ha vā imāḥ prajāḥ prajāyanta iti*

**Перевод:**

**After a year Kabandhī approached and said “Dear sir! From whom do the living beings manifest?”**

**Комментарий:**

After living with the guru for a year (atha), taking permission from the others, Kabandhī approached Pippalāda according to the rules and asked his question.

## 4.

*tasmai sa hovāca*  
*prajā-kāmo vai prajā-patiḥ*  
*sa tapo ‘tapyata sa tapas taptvā*  
*sa mithunam utpādayate rayiṁ ca*  
*prāṇañcety etau me bahudhā prajāḥ kariṣyata iti*

**Перевод:**

**Pippalāda said to Kabandhī, “The Lord, desiring to create offspring, performed tapas, then created prakṛti (rayim) and the jīvas (prāṇa). He thought, ‘These two will produce many offspring for me’.”**

**Комментарий:**

When Kabandhī asked, Pippalāda spoke. The word prajā-patiḥ indicates the Lord, Brahman. This is the usage given by Vyāsa in the Vedānta-sūtra, sarva-vyāhānādhikaraṇa. That famous Paramātmā (saḥ) desiring to create progeny, performed tapas in the form of deliberation on creation. Considering what should be created, he produced a couple, prakṛti and purusa (rayim prāṇam). What was his plan? These two, prakṛti and jīvas, will produce many living beings for me, who desire these offspring.

## 5.

*ādityo ha vai prāṇo rayir eva candramāḥ*  
*rayir vā etat sarvam yan mūrtaṁ cāmūrtaṁ ca*  
*tasmān mūrtir eva rayiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Prāṇa means the enjoyer (sun) and rayi means the enjoyed (moon). Rayi is everything in this world, made of solid elements and subtle elements. Thus the body is to be enjoyed.**

**Комментарий:**

The words rayiḥ and prāṇaḥ are explained. Ādtiyah (sun) means the enjoyer (jīva). This is prāṇa. He enjoys objects (rayiḥ) represented by the moon (candramāḥ). Why the jīva is called prāṇa is explained in the next verse. Rayi is the moon. This is then explained. It has forms (mūrtam)—earth, water and fire. It is devoid of form (amūrtam)—air and ether. Everything produced from the elements is to be enjoyed (rayiḥ). The body made of five elements (mūrtiḥ) is to be enjoyed.

## 6.

*athāditya udayan yat prācīṁ diśaṁ praviśati*  
*tena prācyān prāṇān raśmiṣu sannidhatte*  
*yad dakṣiṇāṁ yat pratīcīṁ yad udīcīṁ yad adho yad ūrddham*  
*yad antarā diśo yat sarvaṁ prakāśayati, tena sarvān*  
*prāṇān raśmiṣu sannidhatte*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva on awakening, illuminates the eastern direction. By that, he enlivened the senses and then objects of the east by his consciousness. Similarly the jīva illuminates the southern, western and northern directions as well as down and up and the intermediate directions and enables the senses to perceive objects in those directions.**

**Комментарий:**

After describing rayi, āditya is now described. Why the enjoyer or āditya is called prāṇa is explained. Waking up from a sleeping state (atha) the jīva manifests in the eastern, southern, western, northern directions, below and above, and in the intermediate directions. He pervades the senses with rays—his consciousness or dharma-bhūta jñāna. Because the jīva enables the senses (prāṇān) to perform their functions, the jīva is called prāṇa.

The jīva illuminates (praviśati) the eastern direction. He inspires the objects of the east, the eyes etc., which reveal objects in the east, by his rays (raśmiṣu), by his dharma-bhūta-jñāna. Without the jīva’s inspiration by dharma-bhūta-jñāna, the senses cannot perceive objects like form. Without consciousness there are no effects.

Illuminating the southern direction, he inspires the senses there by his rays of consciousness. Similarly he illuminates the western direction and inspires the senses there with his rays. As the meaning is easy to understand all the details are not listed.

Paramātmā, desiring to create progeny, created prakṛti and the jīvas. But this is explained indirectly by mentioning rayi and prāṇa, moon and sun, since it is said parokṣa-priyā iva hi devāḥ: the devatās are fond of indirect expression. (Aitareya Upaniṣad) One should not speak clearly matters which are secret.

## 7.

*sa eṣa vaiśvānaro viśvarūpaḥ*  
*prāṇo ‘gnir udayate tad etad ṛcābhyuktam*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is the leader of all jīvas, the soul of all jīvas, the ultimate enjoyer, the topmost leader. This is explained in the mantras.**

**Комментарий:**

Various words are explained to mean Paramātmā. The Lord, previous called prajāpati is now called vaiśvānara, leader of the all the jivas (viśva) and viśva-rūpa, he who is present in all bodies. He is called Agni since he is the topmost (agra) leader (nī). He is prāṇa, the enjoyer. This is Paramātmā, as explained by Vyāsa in the vaiśvānarādikaraṇa of the Vedānta-sūtras. This is explained by the mantras (rcā).

## 8.

*viśva-rūpaṁ hariṇaṁ jāta-vedasam*  
*parāyaṇaṁ jyotir ekaṁ tapantam*  
*sahasra-raśmiḥ śatadhā vartamānaḥ*  
*prāṇaḥ prajānām udayaty eṣa sūryaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is all bodies, is most attractive, is the source of the Vedas, is the shelter of all knowledge, is the revealer of all things. He is topmost, and is the fire of digestion. The jīva, taking up a body, becomes aware of many things. He takes many forms, supports many types of bodies, and enjoys objects.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is all bodies (viśva-rūpam). From him arise all portions of the Vedas (jāta-vedasam). He produces all knowledge (parāyaṇam) and is the highest attainment. He reveals everything (jyotiḥ). He is without a second (ekam). He burns (tapantam) as the fire of digestion.

*santāpayati svaṁ deham āpāda-tala-mastakam*

**He heats up the body from foot to head. Mahā-nārāyaṇa Upaniṣad**

*ahaṁ vaiśvānaro bhūtvā prāṇināṁ deham āśritaḥ*  
*prāṇāpāna-samāyuktaḥ pacāmy annaṁ catur-vidham \|\|*

**Becoming the fire of digestion, I take shelter within all beings and digest the four types of food using the prāṇa and apāna. BG 15.14**

Hariṇam stands for harim.

The jīva takes a body (vartamānaḥ) and becomes aware of many things (sahasra-raśmiḥ). He takes up forms of plants and animals (prāṇaḥ prajānām). He reveals objects (is aware of objects) like the sun (sūryaḥ). This jīva (eṣaḥ) manifests at the time of creation as devatā, human etc.(śatadhā) from his sleeping state.

## 9.

*saṁvatsaro vai prajā-patiḥ tasyāyane*  
*dakiṣṇaṁ cottaraṁ ca tad ye ha vai tad iṣṭāpurte*  
*kṛtam ity upāsate te cāndramasam eva lokam abhijayante*  
*ta eva punar āvartante tasmād ete*  
*ṛṣayaḥ prajā-kāmā dakṣiṇaṁ pratipadyante*  
*eṣa ha vai rayir yaḥ pitṛ-yāṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is the year, which has southern and northern courses. Those who perform pious acts attain the world of the moon and later return to earth. Those attached to material enjoyment follow the southern path. This is the path of material enjoyment.**

**Комментарий:**

Prakṛti and the jīva in subtle form are the body of the Lord. They manifest as prakṛti and puruṣa or rayi and prāṇa. Though the Lord is undivided time, his two parts as divided time are now shown to purify the heart or to show the proper worship. Time is divided into dakṣiṇāyaṇa and uttarāyaṇa, presenting rayi and prāṇa. As the month, they are the waning and waxing phases of the moon. The night represents rayi and the day represents prāṇa.

The year (saṁvatsara), time as the Lord, takes two forms according to the sun: the southern and northern course. Ha vai indicates that it is famous, or remembrance. Persons who perform pious acts such as sacrifices or digging canals (īṣtāpurte kṛtam), attain the planet of the moon and then return to earth. Because of this (tasmāt) those who see only insignificant results (ṛṣayaḥ), who desire material results (prajā-kāmāḥ), go by the southern path or pitṛyāna. This pitṛyāna is filled with material enjoyment, predominated by food (rayi). The word dakṣināyaṇa means the sun’s path during the latter six months of the year, but it is also used to indicate the path of enjoyment or pitṛyāna, which includes the waning moon, the night etc.

## 10.

*athottareṇa tapasā brahmacaryeṇa śraddhayā*  
*vidyātmānam anviṣya ādityam abhijayante*  
*etad vai prāṇānām āyatanam etad amṛtam abhayam etat*  
*parāyaṇam etasmān na punar āvartanta ity eṣa*  
*nirodhas tad eṣa ślokaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Going by the northern path, by austerity, brahmacārya, and faith, seeking the Lord by knowledge, the wise attain the Lord. He is the shelter of all beings, he is immortal and bestows fearlessness. He is the highest goal to be attained. Because of him, the sages do not take birth again. He prevents this. The Lord is explained in this verse.**

**Комментарий:**

Atha indicates a new point. Persons who become disinterested in material enjoyment, by austerities of the body (tapasā), by giving up association with women (brahmacāryeṇa), by faith in the scriptures (śraddhayā), worshipping Paramātmā (ātmānam) by knowledge of pratyagātmā, following the northern path, reach (abhijayante) āditya, the door to attaining Brahman.

*ādityāc chandramasaṁ candramaso vidyutaṁ*  
*tat puruṣo mānavaḥ sa enān brahma gamayati*

From the sun he goes to the moon. From the moon he goes to lightning. From there a divine person leads him to Brahmā. Chāndogya Upaniṣad

As verse 5 has already indicated, āditya means prāṇa. Searching for Paramātmā (ātmānam), the sages attain him and do not return. He is the shelter (āyatanam) of all beings (prāṇānām).

*tad yathā rathasyāreṣu nemir arpito*  
*nābhāvarā arpitā evam evaitā bhūta-mātrāḥ prajñā-mātrāḥ*  
*prajñāmātrāsvarpitāḥprajñāmātrāḥ prāṇe rpitāḥ*

**Just as in a chariot wheel the rim rests on the spokes, and the spokes on the hub, in the same way the material elements rest on prajñā (intelligence), and prajñā rests on prāṇa. Kauṣītaki Upaniṣad**

Paramātmā is the support of all conscious beings.

He is the highest object of attainment (etat parāyaṇam). The worshippers do not return from him (etasmāt). By following the northern path, attaining the Lord, they do not return as is said in the Gītā: punar janma na vidyate (BG 8.160) Rebirth is blocked (nirodhaḥ). Paramātmā, previously designated as Prajāpati, prevents them from taking rebirth. He (eṣaḥ) is the preventer. Eṣaḥ refers to prajāpati, Viṣṇu. Ātmānam in the verse also refers to Prajāpati, Viṣṇu. This is also the conclusion of Vyāsa in the sarva-vyākhyānādhikaraṇa.

There is a verse concerning Prajāpati who is the year (tad eśa ślokaḥ).

## 11.

*pañca-padaṁ pitaraṁ dvādaśākṛtiṁ*  
*diva āhuḥ pare ardhe purīṣiṇam*  
*atheme anya u pare vicakṣaṇaṁ*  
*sapta-cakre ṣaḍ-ara āhur arpitam iti*

**Перевод:**

**Some say that the sun is the creator of everything, has five feet and twelve forms, situated at the covering of the universe beyond Svarga. Other excellent knowers of time say that the universe is fixed on chariot having seven wheels with six spokes.**

**Комментарий:**

The five feet are the vatsara, saṁvatsara, parivatsara idāvatsara and anuvatsara or the six seasons with hemānta and śiśira counted as one season. This samvatsara has five feet. He is the producer of everything (pitaram). He has a form of twelve months (dvādaśākṛtim). He lives beyond the covering of the universe (purīṣiṇam) in a place (ardhe) above Svarga (diva-pare).

On the other hand (atha) other excellent people who know about time (ime anye pare) say that the fixed universe is placed on a chariot called the year with seven wheels (the planets) and six spokes representing the six seasons.

## 12.

*māso vai prajāpatiḥ tasya kṛṣṇa-pakṣa eva rayiḥ*  
*śuklaḥ prāṇah tasmād eta ṛṣayaḥ śukla iṣṭaṁ*  
*kurvantītara itarasmin*

**Перевод:**

**The month is Prajāpati. The waning phase is called rayi and the waxing phase is called prāṇa. Because of this, the sages perform sacrifice in the waxing phase. Others perform sacrifice in the waning phase.**

**Комментарий:**

Just as the year was classed as rayi or prāṇa, the month is divided in the same way for worship. The seers of the objects beyond the senses, who prefer prāṇa and liberation, perform auspicious acts (iṣṭam) during the waxing phase. Others, who are not sages, but ignorant people (itaraḥ), perform rites during the waning phase, desiring impermanent objects.

## 13.

*ahorātro vai prajā-patiḥ tasyāhar eva prāṇo*  
*rātrir eva rayiḥ prānaṁ vā ete praksandanti*  
*ye diva ratyā saṁyujyante brahamcaryam eva*  
*tad yad rātrau ratyā saṁyujyante*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is the day and the night. The day is prāṇa and the night is rayi. Those who engage in sex during the day waste their prāṇa. Those who engage in sex at night maintain brahmacārya.**

**Комментарий:**

The classification of day and night is shown for purposes of worship. The day is prāṇa. Those who engage in sex during the day waste their prāṇa. They destroy prāṇa by acts which violate prāṇa. Should householders not unite with their wives? Those who engage in sex at night are considered to be maintain brahmacarya. It is not sex life. It has no fault.

## 14.

*annaṁ vai prajā-patiḥ tato ha vai*  
*tad retaḥ tasmād imāḥ prajāḥ prajāyante*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is food and its transformation semen. From the Lord all beings arise.**

**Комментарий:**

“The Lord is composed of prakṛti, jīvas and time. How is he the material cause of the progeny? It is seen that semen, a transformation of food cause progeny.”

Food and its transformation semen is also the Lord. All beings are born from the Lord who is prakṛti, jīvas, the year, the month and other aspects of time, food and semen. Thus, the Lord is the material cause.

## 15.

*tad he ha vai tat prajā-pati-vrataṁ*  
*caranti te mithunam utpādayante*  
*teṣāṁ evaiṣa brahma-loko yeṣāṁ*  
*tapo brahmacaryaṁ yeṣu satyaṁ pratiṣṭhitam*

**Перевод:**

**Those who are fixed in acts of enjoyment, produce progeny and enjoyment. They attain a world of enjoyment.**

**Комментарий:**

After criticizing persons who do not desire liberation, the sage praises persons who desire liberation.

Annam vai prajāpati: food is the Lord. Those who undertake the vow of eating food, a form of the Lord, who are without brahmacarya, produce offspring. They attain the place of sons and animals, kārya-bhūta brahman (brahma-lokam).

## 16.

*teṣām asau virajo brahma-loko*  
*na yeṣu jihmām anṛtaṁ na māyā ceti*

**Перевод:**

**Those who practice austerity, brahmacarya, and truth, and are devoid of deceit, untruth and bewilderment, attain the faultless world of Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Those who practice brahmacarya and austerity (not overeating), who are fixed in the truth, who are not crooked in mind, words and actions towards others, who do not speak lies to injure other beings (anṛtam na), attain the faultless (virajaḥ) world of Brahman. Iti indicates the conclusion of the answer.

## Second Question:

## 1.

*atha hainaṁ bhāgravo vaidarbhiḥ papraccha*  
*bhagavan katy eva devāḥ prajāṁ*  
*vidhārayante katara etat prakāśayante*  
*kaḥ punar eṣāṁ variṣṭha iti*

**Перевод:**

**Vaidharibh of Bhargava’s lineage asked Pippalāda, “How many devatās support the living beings? Which devatās manifest functions in the body? Who is supreme among the devatās?”**

**Комментарий:**

Questions are asked in order to clarify the nature of pratyagātma (jīva), which is different from mind, body, senses and prāṇa. This will also be clarified in later chapters.  
After Kabandhī asked his question, Vaidarbhi, in the Bhārgava lineage, asked Pippalāda questions. Dear sir (bhagavan)! How many devatās support the living begins moving and immobile? Among the devatās, which ones manifest their actions in the body? Who is the best among them?

## 2.

*tasmai sa hovāca ākāśo ha vā*  
*eṣa devo vāyur agnir āpaḥ pṛthivī*  
*vāṅ manaś cakṣuḥ śrotraṁ ca te prakāśyabhivadanti*  
*vayam etad vānaṁ avaṣṭabhya vidhārayāmaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Pippalāda spoke to Vaidharbhi.**

**Ether, air, fire, water, earth, voice, mind, eyes and ear, going many places, manifesting the body, say, “We, taking shelter of the body, maintain it.”**

**Комментарий:**

The intention is to show that mukhya-prāṇa is the best supporter and manifestor in the body.

Ha va indicates it is well known. Ākāśa is famous as a devatā. Deva means one who plays and who is able to go many places. Also, air, fire, water and earth are deva. Vāk represents all the action senses. Eyes and ears represent all the knowledge gathering senses. Ākāśa and the others, gathering together, revealing (prakāsya) the body, situated around (abhi), speak. “Supporting this body (vāṇam) we maintain (vidhārayāmaḥ) it by various actions.”

## 3.

*tān variṣṭhaḥ prāṇa uvāca*  
*mā moham āpadyatha aham evaitat pañcadhātmānam*  
*pravibhajyaitad vāṇaṁ avaṣṭabhya vidhārayāmīti*  
*te ‘śraddadhānā babhuvaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The superior prāṇa spoke to them. “Do not be bewildered. Dividing into five types, I take shelter of the body and maintain it.” They did not believe his words.**

**Комментарий:**

O ether and other elements! Do not have such opposite intelligence (moham). I alone, dividing into five forms (pañcadhā), take shelter of the body and support it. The chief prāṇa speaks. You are able to do your particular activities but each of you cannot perform all the actions. But I can do all your activities, since I am the cause of your actions. None of you are able to maintain life. They did not believe his words.

## 4.

*so ‘bhimānād ūrdhvam utkrāmata iva*  
*tasmin utkṛamaty athetare sarva evotkrāmante*  
*tasmins ca pratiṣṭhamāne sarva eva prātiṣṭhante*  
*tad yathā makṣikā madhukara-rājānam utkrāmantam*  
*sarvā evotkrāmante tasminś ca pratiṣthamāne*  
*sarva eva prātiṣthanta evaṁ vāṅ manaś cakṣuḥ śrotraṁ ca*  
*te prītāḥ prāṇaṁ stuvanti*

**Перевод:**

**In pride, the prāṇa moved up slightly. When he moved, all the senses also moved up. When he remained still, they all remained still. When the king of bees flies upward, all the bees fly upwards. When the king of bees remains still, all the bees remain still. All the senses became obedient. Voice, mind, eyes and ears praised the chief prāṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Seeing their pride, prāṇa, being proud himself, moved a little (iva) from his place, in order to show his power over the hundred and eight vital points. Thinking that if he moved out of the body, the body would be destroyed, he moved a little. When the chief prāṇa moved, all the senses moved. Fearing that the body would die, he remained fixed. An example is given. When the king of bees moves up, all the other bees move up. When he stops, all the bees stop. All the senses became obedient to the chief prāṇa (prātiṣtḥanta). Pleased by praṇa’s show of power, voice, mind senses etc. praised the chief prāṇa.

## 5.

*eṣo ‘gnis tapaty eṣa sūrya eṣa parjanyo maghavān eṣa vāyuḥ*  
*eṣa pṛthivī rayir devaḥ sad-asad cāmṛtaṁ ca yat*

**Перевод:**

**Prāṇa, becoming fire, burns. Prāṇa is the sun, the rain, Indra, air, earth, the moon, everything gross and subtle and it is liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

The chief prāṇa in the form of fire burns. This prāṇa is the sun, since everything depends on prāṇa. When something is dependent on another thing, it is equated with it.

Thus prāṇa is the sun means the sun is dependent on prāṇa. Prāṇa is also the moon devatā (rayiḥ). Sad-asat means existing and non-existing, observable and not observable, conscious and unconscious, gross and subtle. Amṛta means liberation. Everything is dependent on prāṇa.

## 6.

*arā iva ratha-nābau prāṇe sarvaṁ pratiṣṭhitam*  
*ṛco yajuṁṣī sāmāni yajñāḥ kṣatraṁ brahma ca*

**Перевод:**

**As spokes are fixed on the center of a wheel, everything is fixed on prāṇa. The verses, sacrificial prayers, songs, sacrifices and all beings are fixed on prāṇa.**

**Комментарий:**

Arā means the wood sticks extending from the center (nābhi) of the wheel to the frame. As spokes are fixed to the center of the wheel, everything is fixed on prāṇa. The word “all” is clarified in the last sentence. Brahma-kṣatra indicates all beings (instead of just brāhmaṇas and kṣatriyas).

## 7.

*prajā-patiś carasi garbhe tvam eva pratijāyase*  
*tubhyaṁ prāṇa prajās tv imā baliṁ haranti*  
*yaḥ prāṇaiḥ pratitiṣthasi*

**Перевод:**

**O prāṇa! As master of the offspring, you move about in the womb. You become the sons. The progeny make offerings to you, who are situated in all the senses as the prāṇas.**

**Комментарий:**

Glorifying prāṇa, they face prāṇa and praise him. As the protector of all beings, you wander about in the womb in the form of air etc. Present as a father, producing the embryo and maintaining it, you are born as sons. O prāṇa! The offspring make offerings to you. You are situated in all the senses through the actions of prāṇa.

## 8.

*devānām asi vahnitamaḥ pitṛṇāṁ prathamā svadhā*  
*ṛṣīnām caritaṁ satyam arthavāṅgirasām asi*

**Перевод:**

**You are the best carrier of offerings to the devatās. You are the chief cause of pleasure of the pitṛs. You are the excellent karmas of the sages of arthavāṅirasā.**

**Комментарий:**

You are the best carrier of offerings to the devatās (vahinitamaḥ devānām). You are the chief cause of pleasure of the pitṛs (svadhā). You are the excellent nitya and naimittika karmas of the sages of arthavāṅgirasā.

## 9.

*indras tvaṁ prāṇa tesasā rudro ‘si parirakṣitā*  
*tam antarikṣe carasi sūryas tvaṁ jyotiṣāṁ patiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O prāṇa! You are the supreme Lord, and you are Rudra. You are the maintainer. You are the best luminary. As the sun, you wander in the sky.**

**Комментарий:**

O prāṇa! You are the supreme lord (indraḥ). You are Rudra, cause of weeping, by power of destruction (tejasā). At the time of maintenance, you are the protector (parirakṣitā). You are the best (patiḥ) of all luminous entities (jyotiṣām). Becoming the sun, you wander in the sky.

## 10.

*yadā tvam abhivarṣasy athemāḥ prāṇa te prajāḥ*  
*ānanda-rūpās tiṣṭhanti kāmāyānnam bhavisyatīti*

**Перевод:**

**O prāṇa! When you shower rain, your progeny become blissful, thinking there will be enough food to fulfil our desires.**

**Комментарий:**

O prāṇa! When you as clouds pour rain everywhere (abhivarṣasi), your (te) progeny rejoice. There will be sufficient food for fulfilling our desires (kāṁāya).

*tad yadā suvṛṣṭir bhavaty ānandinaḥ prāṇa bhavanti annaṁ bahu bhaviṣyati*

**When there is plentiful rain the people become blissful, thinking there will be plenty of food. Chāndogya Upaniṣad**

## 11.

*vrātyās tvaṁ prāṇaikarṣir attā viśvasya sat-patiḥ*  
*vayam ādyasya dātāraḥ pitā tvaṁ mātariśva naḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O prāṇa! You are the brāhmaṇa without saṁskāras. You are the chief sage. You are the destroyer of the universe. You are the protector of the devotees. We give offerings to you. O Mātariśvan! You are our nourisher.**

**Комментарий:**

O prāṇa! You are even the brāhmaṇa devoid of saṁskāras (vrātyaḥ). You are the chief seer of mantras (ekarṣiḥ). You are the destroyer (attā) of the universe. You are the protector of the devotees (sat-patiḥ). We are givers of food or enjoyables (ādyasya) to you. O mātariśvan! You are our father, our nourisher. Mātariśva should be mātariśvan. This is for metrical reasons.

## 12.

*yā te tanūr vāci pratiṣthatā yā śrotre yāca cakṣuṣi*  
*yā ca manasi santaṭa śivāṁ tāṁ kuru motkramīḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Make your power situated at all times in the ear, eye and mind auspicious. Do not move out of the body.**

**Комментарий:**

Your power (tanūḥ), favourable for regulating the senses and which is situated always in the voice, ear, eye and mind, should create auspiciousness. Do not create inauspiciousness. Do not move out of the body.

## 13.

*prāṇasyedaṁ vaśe sarvaṁ tri-dive yat pratiṣṭhitam*  
*māteva putrān rakṣasva śrīś ca prajñāṁ ca vidhehi na iti*

**Перевод:**

**Prāṇa controls the universe and whatever is situated in the heavens. Protect us as a mother protects her children. Bestow to us wealth and wisdom.**

**Комментарий:**

Thus ends the second question.

## Third Question:

## 1.

*atha hainaṁ kausalyaś cāśvalāyanaḥ papraccha*  
*bhagavan kuta eṣa prāṇo jāyate katham āyāty asmin ca*  
*śarīra ātmānaṁ vā pravibhajya kathaṁ prātiṣṭhate*  
*kenotkramate kathaṁ bāhyam abhidhatte*

**Перевод:**

**Kauśalya, son of Āśvalāyana, asked, “Sir! From where is prāṇa born? How does it come into the body? Dividing up, how does prāṇa remain in the body? How does prāṇa leave the body? How does prāṇa exist externally?**

**Комментарий:**

Prātiṣṭhate should be pratiṣṭhati. How is prāṇa present externally (bāhyam abhidatte)?

## 2.

*tasmai sa hovāca atipraśnān pṛcchasi*  
*brahmiṣṭho ‘sīti tasmāt te ‘ham bravīmi*

**Перевод:**

**Pippilāda spoke to Kauśalya. “You ask confidential questions. Since you are fixed in Brahman I speak to you.”**

**Комментарий:**

You ask questions of secret matters (atipraśnān). Because you are almost a knower of Brahman (brahmoṣṭhaḥ) I speak to you. I do not speak to the unqualified. I speak because you are qualified.

## 3.

*ātman eṣa prāṇo jāyate*  
*yathaiṣā puruṣe cchāyaitasminn etad ātataṁ*  
*manokṛtenāyaty asmiṁ ca śarīre*

**Перевод:**

**This prāṇa arises from the Lord. Just as a shadow follows a person, without effort, prāṇa, like the mind, enters the body.**

**Комментарий:**

This prāṇa is born from Paramātmā (ātmanaḥ). (This answers the first question.) Śruti says:

*etasmāj jāyate prāṇo manaḥ sarvendriyāni ca:*

**Prāṇa, mind and all the senses arise from the Lord. Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad**

The second question “How does prāṇa enter the body?” is answered. When a man moves, his shadow moves. The shadow has no independent movement. The mind comes into the body without effort (akṛteṇa). Kṛta means effort. The mind without effort takes shelter (ātatam) in the body. Similarly, prāṇa comes into the body without effort. Mind and prāṇa are like shadows of the jīva, which cannot be separated from the jīva. Because of the relationship of prāṇa to the jīva, it does not require a separate cause for entering the body.

## 4.

*yathā samrāḍ evādhikṛtān viniyuṅkte*  
*etān grāman etān grāmān adhitiṣṭhas veti*  
*evaṁ evaiṣa prāṇa itirān prāṇān prthak prthag eva sannidhatte*

**Перевод:**

**Just as a king engages his agents saying “Rule over this village,” so the chief prāṇa rules over the senses after empowering various prāṇas.**

**Комментарий:**

Dividing itself, how does the prāṇa remain in the body? This verse answers the third question.

Just as a king engages his persons as rulers in various villages, saying, “Rule this village, rule this village” appointing them by his power, so the chief prāṇa, divides himself into āpana, vyānā etc., and rules over the senses (like villages).

## 5.

*pāyūpāsthe ‘pānam cakṣuḥ śrotre mukha-nāsikābhyāṁ*  
*prāṇaḥ svayaṁ prātiṣthate madhye tu samānaḥ*  
*eṣa hy etad dhūtam annaṁ samaṁ nayati*  
*tasmād etāḥ saptārciṣo bhavanti*

**Перевод:**

**Apāṇa rules over the anus and genital. Prāṇa rules over the eye and ear, expelling air through the mouth and nostrils. Samāna rules over the middle of the body. It distributes eaten food to the dhātus. From it arises the seven fires of digestion.**

**Комментарий:**

Apāṇa rules over the anus and genitals (pāyūpāsthe). Apāṇa expels stool and urine. It rules over (prātiṣṭhate) these places. Prāṇa leaves as air through the mouth and nostrils and rules over the eyes and ears. Becoming samāna, it rules over the middle area. Samāna (eṣaḥ) distributes the eaten (hūtam) food among the dhātus. Because of the sāmana air (tasmāt), the seven fires of the digestive fire such as kālī manifest.

## 6.

*hrdi hy eṣa ātmā atraitad ekaśataṁ nāḍīnāṁ tāsāṁ*  
*śata-śatam ekaikasyāḥ dvāsaptatir dvāsaptatiḥ*  
*pratiśākhā-nāḍī sahasrāṇi bhavanty āsu vyānaś carati*

**Перевод:**

**The ātmā resides in the heart. In the heart there are a hundred and one nerves. Each is divided into seventy two nerves, which divide into many thousands each. Vyāna moves in the nerves.**

**Комментарий:**

The ātmā resides in the heart, where samāna resides. In the heart (atra) there are a hundred and one nerves. Each one has seventy-two divisions. Each of these nerves has many thousands (sahasrāni śata-śatam) of divisions. The air called vyāna moves in these nerves.

## 7.

*athaikayorddha udānaḥ puṇyena puṇya-lokaṁ nayati*  
*pāpena pāpam ubhābhyām eva manuṣya-lokam*

**Перевод:**

**The upward moving udāna leads the jīva to Svarga by puṇyas, to hell by sins, and to earth by a mixture.**

**Комментарий:**

Atha introduces the subject. By one nerve (ekayā), the upward udāna leads to Svarga by puṇyas and hell (pāpam) by sins, and by a mixture to earth. This answers the fourth question “How does prāṇa leave the body?”

## 8.

*ādityo ha vai bāhyaḥ prāṇaḥ udayaty eṣa*  
*hy enaṁ cākṣuṣaṁ prāṇam anugṛhnānaḥ*  
*pṛthivyāṁ yā devatā saiṣā puruṣasyāpānam*  
*avaṣṭabhyāntarā yad ākāśah sa samāno vāyur vyānaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Prāṇa, being merciful to the eye, manifests externally as the sun. Apāṇa manifests as the deity of earth. Samāna manifests as the deity of ether, which is situated between heaven and earth. Vyāna manifests as air.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse answers the fifth question, “How does the prāṇa exist externally?”

Prāṇa, being favourable to the eyeball (cākṣuṣam prāṇam) by aiding sight, manifests (udayati) externally as the sun (ādityaḥ). Though the prāṇa cannot be the sun, since they are different, they are taken as one metaphorically, or for purposes of worship.

The apāna air, being merciful to the anus and genitals whose deity is apana, manifests as the devatā of earth.

Samāna manifest as the deity of ether in the middle between the prāṇa and apāna. It divides up the food.

Sun is the external manifestation of prāṇa. Earth is the external manifestation of apāṇa. Ether is the external manifestation of samāna. Air is the external manifestation of vyāna, giving mercy to the skin.

## 9.

*tejo ha vā udānas tasmād upaśānta tejāḥ*  
*punar bhavam indriyair manasi sampadyamānaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Fire is the external manifestation of udāna. Because of lack of fire, the body loses heat when dying. One is again born, with senses which enter the mind.**

**Комментарий:**

Udāna means to lift up. Thus udāna is equated with external fire. Because of lack of fire, loses heat (upaśānta-tejaḥ) when a person dies. According to his desire for being a human or devaṭā he takes birth again. The dying person attains prāṇa along with his ten senses which have attained special contact with the mind.

*vāṅ-manasidarśanāc chabdāc ca*

**Speech enters mind because that is stated in śruti and it is seen that mind operates when speech ceases. Vedānta-sūtra 4.2.1**

(Mind enters prāṇa. Vedānta-sūtra 4.2.3)

*imam ātmānam antakāle sarva prāṇa abhisamāyanti*

**All the prāṇas approach the ātmā at the time of death. Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad**

Though it is said that the prāṇas enter ātmā, actually they do not enter ātmā. The sutra says:

*\|\| so’dhyakṣe tad-upagamādibhyaḥ \|\|*

**Prāṇa enters the jīva since this is seen in the scriptures. Vedānta-sūtra 4.2.4**

The meaning is that the prāṇa meets with the jīva. It is also said that the prāṇas leave the body with the jīva. (Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad 4.4.2) and Praśna Upanaiṣad (6.3)

## 10.

*yac cittas tenaiṣa prāṇam āyati prāṇas tesasā yuktaḥ*  
*sahātmanā yathā saṅkalpitaṁ lokaṁ nayati*

**Перевод:**

**According to desire, the jīva goes to prāṇa. Prāṇa unites with tejas element (which leads the jīva to his next body). The prāṇa leads the jīva, along with the Lord, to the desired planet.**

**Комментарий:**

Desiring birth as a human or devatā, the jīva goes to prāṇa. Prāṇa, joining with the jīva, joins with tejas (udāna). The Yamunā enters the Gaṅgā. But we can say the Yamunā etners the ocean, since Gaṅgā enters the ocean. Because prāṇa becomes endowed with qualities of tejas, it is said to become tejas. When it is said that the jīva enters prāṇa, it is stated in order to praise prāṇa.

*Prāṇas tejasi tejaḥ parasyāṁ devatāyām*

**Prāṇa enters tejas and tejas enters the Lord. Chāndogya Upaniṣad**

Prāṇa, joined with tejas and the Lord, take the jīva to a planet according to the jīva’s desire. Since it is said the prāṇa, endowed with tejas (udāna) takes the jīva, it also means that tejas or udāna takes the jīva to his next birth. The word tejas also indicate the other elements, not just tejas or fire.

*\|\| bhūteṣu tac-chruteḥ \|\|*

**The jīva enters all the elements because this is stated in śruti. Vedānta-sūtra 4.2.4**

*\|\| naikasmin darśayato hi \|\|*

**The jīva does not enter fire alone but enters all the elements since this is stated in śruti. Vedānta-sūtra 4.2.6**

Tejas is used to indicate all the elements since it is the prominent one.

## 11.

*ya evaṁ vidvān prāṇaṁ veda na hāsya prajā*  
*hīyate amrto bhavati tad eva ślokaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The learned man who knows prāṇa in this way, has no destruction of progeny. He becomes liberated. This is the verse.**

**Комментарий:**

He who worships prāṇa understanding (veda) its origin, its entrance into the body and its situation in the body (evam), will have not decrease in progeny. This becomes a cause of liberation, through realizing the nature of ātmā, by affectionate worship of Brahman.

## 12.

*utpattim āyatiṁ sthānaṁ vibhutvaṁ caive pañcadhā*  
*adhyātmaṁ caiva prāṇasya vijñāyāmṛtam āśnute*  
*vijñāyāmrtam aśnuta iti*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing prāṇa’s origin, its entrance into the body, its remaining in the body in five forms and its external forms, one attains liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowing about the origin (utpatim) of prāṇa from the Lord, its entrance (ayatim) into the body along with the mind, its residence in the body in five places (sthānam), its powers (vibhutvam adhyātmam) of five types as well as the five external forms of prāṇa (ca) such as the sun, one attains liberation (amṛtam). Repetition of the last sentence indicates the end of the chapter.

## Fourth Question

## 1.

*atha hainaṁ sauryāyaṇī gārgyaḥ papraccha*  
*bhagavann etasmin puruṣe kāni svapanti kāny asmin jāgarti*  
*katara eṣa devaḥ svapnān paśyati kasyaitat sukhaṁ bhavati*  
*kasmin nu sarve sampratiṣṭhitā bhavanti iti*

**Перевод:**

**Sauryāyanī of Garga’s lineage asked Pippalāda. Dear sir! When a person sleeps what senses sleep and what senses are awake? Which jīvas see dreams? How does the jīva experience happiness? In whom are all the senses fixed?**

**Комментарий:**

When a person sleeps which organs sleep? When a person sleeps, what organs are awake? What type of jīva (devaḥ), having qualities of shining, sees dreams? From what cause does he become happy? All the senses reside in whom?

## 2.

*tasmai sa hovāca*  
*yathā gārgya marīcayo ‘rkasyāstaṁ gacchataḥ*  
*sarvā etasmiṁs tejo-maṇdala ekībhavanti*  
*tāḥ punaḥ punar udayataḥ pracaranti*  
*evaṁ ha vai tat sarvaṁ pare deve manasy ekī bhavati*  
*tena tarhy eṣa puruṣo na śṛnoti na paśyati, na jighrati*  
*na rasayate na śpṛṣate nābhivadate nādatte nānandayate*  
*na viṣrjate neyāyate svapitīty ācakṣate*

**Перевод:**

**Pippalāda replied to him. O Gārgya! Just as the sun’s rays, merge with the sun globe when the sun sets, and illuminate the directions when the sun rises, so all the senses merge with the superior, illuminating mind. Because of this, the person does not hear, does not see, does not smell, does not taste, does not touch, does not speak, does not receive, does not eject semen, does not discharge waste, and does not move about. They say “The senses are sleeping.”**

**Комментарий:**

Just as all the sun’s rays, when the sun sets, become one with the sun globe, and again, when the sun rises, illuminate (pracaranti) all directions, so all the senses become one with the mind, which is superior to the other senses (pare) and which shines (deve). The senses stop functioning. Because the senses stop functioning (tena), the person does not hear or see. Ānandayate indicates the function of the genital. Viṣrajate indicates the function of the anus. Iyāyate means going. This answers the question “Which organs sleep?” All the action senses and knowledge gathering senses, which are external senses, sleep (svapiti). Singular is used to indicate they are together.

## 3.

*prāṇāgnaya evaitasmin pure jāgarti*  
*gārhapatyo ha vā eṣo ‘pāno vyāno ‘nvāhārya pacano*  
*yad gārhapatyāt praṇiyate praṇayanād āhavanīyaḥ prāṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The fires of prāṇa remain awake in the body when a person sleeps. Apāṇa is the gārhapatya fire. Vyāna is the anvāhārya or dakṣina fire. Prāṇa is the āhavanīya fire since it arises from the gārhapatya fire.**

**Комментарий:**

This answers the question “What organs are awake when a person sleeps?”

The five prāṇas, like fires, are awake in the body (pure). The five prāṇas while inhaling and exhaling are compared to the ingredients of the sacrificial fire for purposes of worship. The mind is the sponsor or performer of the sacrifice. Apāna is the gārhapatya fire since apānais situated in the mūladhara cakra.1 Vyāna is called the the anvāhārya fire, or the southern fire (placed in the south, for Pitṛas) and its basis is apāna. Prāṇa is called the āvahanīya fire, which arises from the gārhapatya fire, since it arises from apāna, having that as its basis. The two oblations are exhaling and inhaling. Samāna distributes the two oblations and is known as the adhvarya priest. Udāna is the karma-phala, because it elevates the sponsor to heaven. These are the eight elements of sacrifice.

## 4.

*yad ucchvāsa-niḥśvāsāv etāv āhutī samaṁ nayatīti*  
*sa-samānaḥ mano ha vāva yajamānaḥ iṣā-phalam evāodānaḥ*  
*sa enaṁ yajamānam ahar ahar brahma gamayati*

**Перевод:**

**Samāna keeps the balance of inhaling and exhaling which are the two oblations. The mind is the sponsor of the sacrifice. The udāna is the desire result. The udāna takes the sponsor daily to Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Actually the jīva goes to Brahman not the mind (the sponsor).

*evam evemāḥ sarvāḥ prajā ahar ahar gacchanty etaṁ brahmaloka na vindanti*

**All beings covered by ignorance day after day go to that world of Brahman, but do not know it. Chāndogya Upaniṣad**

However, the mind enters the covering over the heart with the jīva during sleep.

## 5.

*atraiṣa devaḥ svapne mahimānam anubhavti*  
*tad dṛṣṭaṁ dṛṣṭam anupaśyati śrutaṁ śrutaṁ evārtham anuśṛṇoti*  
*deśa-dig-antaiś ca praty anubhūtaṁ punaḥ punaḥ pratyanubhavati*  
*dṛṣṭam cādṛṣataṁ ca śrutaṁ cāśrutaṁ śānubhutaṁ cānanubhūtaṁ ca*  
*sac cāsac ca sarvaṁ paśyati sarvaḥ paśyati*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva experiences great events in dreams. He sees what he previously saw and hears what he previously heard. He experiences what he previously experienced in different places again and again. He experiences both what was previously seen and what was unseen, what was heard and what was not heard, what was experienced and what was not experienced, what was real and what was not real. All the dream senses perceive.**

**Комментарий:**

This answers the question “Who sees the dreams?”

At this time (atra), the jīva (eṣa jīvaḥ) experiences the greatness of elephants and horses in dreams. Whatever he saw previously while awake, he sees later or experiences again in dreams. Whatever he previously heard, he hears again. Whatever he experienced in various places in various directions, he experiences again. There is no rule that he experiences what he saw, heard or experienced previously. Sometimes he may experience things in dreams such as getting his head cut off, which he did not previously experience in this life. He experiences events which took place and which did not take place (sat asat). Everything—the seer, hearer, smeller, speaker and mover—perceive. Though the external knowledge and action senses are inactive, he experiences by body and senses created by the Lord during dreams.

## 6.

*sa yadā tejasā ‘bhibhūto bhavati atraiva devaḥ svapnān na paśyaty*  
*athaitasmiṁ ca śarīra etat sukhaṁ bhavati*

**Перевод:**

**When the jīva is embraced by the Lord, the jīva does not see dreams. The jīva experiences happiness in this body.**

**Комментарий:**

When he is endowed with or embraced by Paramātmā (tejasā) the jīva does not see dreams.

*satā somya tadā saṁpanno bhavati tejasā hi taḍā sampanno bhavati*

**One attains the Lord during sleep. Chāndogya Upaniṣad**

This person does not see objects in dreams. Who sees dreams? If one is devoid of the wealth of Brahman, and the mind remains, one sees dream objects.

What is the cause of happiness? This question is answered. Happiness exists while this body exists (etasmin śarīre). The body is the cause of material happiness.

*aśārīraṁ vāva santaṁ na priyāpriye spṛṣataḥ*

**When devoid of a body happiness and distress do not touch him. Chāndogya Upaniṣad**

## 7.

*sa yathā saumya vayāṁsi vāsovṛkṣaṁ sampratiṣṭhante*  
*evam ha vai tat sarvaṁ para ātmani sampratiṣṭhate*

**Перевод:**

**O gentle one! Just as birds dwell in their nest in a tree, all beings reside in the supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

This answers the question “In what is everything established?”  
Birds (vayāṁsi) dwell in the tree nest. Similarly, everything resides in the Lord.

## 8.

*pṛthivī ca pṛthivī-mātṛā ca āpaś cāpo-mātrā ca tejoś ca tejo-mātrā ca*  
*vāhyuś ca vāyu-mātrā ca ākāśaś cākāśa-mātrā ca*  
*cakṣuś ca draṣtavyaṁ ca śrotraṁ ca śrotavyaṁ ca*  
*ghraṇaṁ ca ghrātavyaṁ ca rasaś ca rasayitavyaṁ ca*  
*tvak ca sparśayitavyaṁ ca vāk ca vaktavyam ca*  
*hastau cādātavyaṁ ca upaṣthaś cānandayitavyaṁ ca*  
*pāyuś ca visarjayitavyaṁ ca pādau ca gantavyaṁ ca*  
*manaś ca mantavyaṁ ca buddhiś ca boddhavyaṁ ca*  
*ahaṅkāraś cāhaṁkartavyaṁ ca citta ca cetayitavyaṁ ca*  
*tejaś ca vidyotayitavyaṁ ca prāṇac ca vidhārayitavyaṁ ca*

**Перевод:**

**Everything means earth, fragrance, water, taste, fire, form, air, touch, ether, sound, the eye and what is seen, the ear what is heard, the nose and what is smelled, the tongue and what is tasted, the skin and what is touched, the voice and what is spoken, the hand and what is grasped, the genital and what is enjoyed, the anus and what is excreted, the mind and what is thought, the intelligence and what is understood, the false ego and what is identified as I, the material consciousness and its object of awareness, light and what is illuminated and prāṇa and what it supports.**

**Комментарий:**

Sarvam (everything rests on the Lord) of the previous verse is explained. Pṛthivi-mātrā means the sense object of earth, fragrance, which exists previous to earth element.

## 9.

*eṣa hi drāsṭā spraṣtā, śrotā, dhrātā rasyitā*  
*mantā boddhā kartā vijñanātmā puruṣaḥ*  
*sa pare ‘ksara ātmani saṁpratiṣṭhate*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva, knowledge itself, is also the seer, toucher, hearer, smeller, taster, thinker, conceptualizer, and the doer. He is established in the supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Everything conscious and conscious, the doer, the instrument of action and the action, takes shelter of the Lord. Words like boddhā, kartā, vijñānātmā indicate that the jīva is not just knowledge (vijñāna—awareness alone) but is a knower (boddhā). Logicians argue that ātmā is a knower but not knowledge. The proponents of illusion argue that the ātmā is knowledge but not a knower. Both views are rejected in this verse.

## 10.

*param evākṣaraṁ pratipadyate sa yo ha vai tad acchāyam*  
*aśarīram alohitaṁ śubhram akṣaraṁ vedayate yas tu*  
*saumya sa sarva-jñaḥ sarvo bhavati tad eṣa ślokaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**O gentle one! He who knows Paramātmā, who is without karma, without a material body with blood, who is without decay, attains the supreme Lord. He becomes omniscient and all his desires are fulfilled. There is a verse concerning this.**

**Комментарий:**

Cchāya means that which limits knowledge or karma. Acchāya means without sin. Paramātmā is without sin, is bloodless (alohitam), and without material body (aśarīram). He is self-manifesting (śubhram) and without decay (akṣaram). He who knows Paramātmā attains the supreme akṣaram, Vāsudeva. He knows everything. All his desires are fulfilled (sarvaḥ bhavati). There is a verse concerning this.

## 11.

*vijñānātmā saha devaiś ca sarvaiḥ*  
*prāṇā bhūtāni sampratiṣṭhanit yatra*  
*tad akṣaraṁ vedayate yas tu saumya*  
*sa sarva-jñaḥ sarvam evāviveśa iti*

**Перевод:**

**"O gentle one! He who knows the self-manifesting unchanging Paramatma in whom reside the pranas along with all the senses, the elements, and the atma, becomes omniscient and obtainsall desires.**

**Комментарий:**

He who knows self-manifesting unchanging Paramātmā (akṣaram) in whom reside the prāṇas along with all the senses (devaiḥ sarvaiḥ), the elements, and the ātmā, becomes omniscient and obtains all desires (sarvam).

*sarveṣu lokeṣu kāmacāro bhavati*

**He wanders at will in all planets. Chāndogya Upaniṣad.**

## Fifth Question

## 1.

*atha hainaṁ śaivyaḥ satyakāmaḥ papracccha*  
*sa yo ha vai tad bhagavan manuṣyeṣu prāyaṇāntam*  
*oṁkāram abhidhyāyīta katamaṁ vāva sa tena lokaṁ jayati iti*  
*tasmai sa hovāca*

**Перевод:**

**Satyakāma, the son of Śibi asked, “What world does the qualified person who meditates on omkāra attain after death?” Pippaḷāda answered him.**

**Комментарий:**

Ha vai means very famous. Tad means what is imperishable. Saḥ means the person with general qualification. O master worthy of worship (bhagavan)! What world does the person qualified among men who mediates on omkāra attain (jayati) by omkāra after death? Vāva indicates emphasis or means famous.

## 2.

*etad vai satyakāma paraṁ cāparaṁ ca brahma yad oṁkāraḥ*  
*tasmād vidvān etenaivāyatanenaikataram anveti*

**Перевод:**

**O Satyakāma! Omkāra is superior and inferior Brahman. From oṁkāra the worshipper attains either superior or inferior Brahman by following that path.**

**Комментарий:**

O Satyakāma! Omkāra is a name indicating superior and inferior Brahman. The name and what is indicated by the name are equivalent (sāmānādhikaraṇyam). That is indicated by Vyāsa in the īkṣita-karmādhikaraṇa of Vedānta-sūtras. What is it? From oṁkāra (tasmāt), by the path (āyatanena) of omkāra (etena), the worshipper (vidvān) attains either superior or inferior Brahman.

## 3.

*sa yady eka-mātram abhidhyāyīta sa tenaiva saṁveditas*  
*tūrnam eva jagatyāṁ abhisampadyate*  
*tam ṛco manuṣya-lokam upanayante*  
*sa tatra tapasā brahmacaryeṇa śraddhayā sampanno*  
*mahimānam anubhavati*

**Перевод:**

**If the worshipper mediates on oṁkāra of one mātrā he attains the material world quickly. The Ṛg mantra makes him attain the human world. If he becomes endowed with austerity, brahmacarya and faith, he attains the superior worship of Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

If the worshipper (saḥ) meditates on oṁkāra of short sound (eka-mātram), which indicates inferior Brahman, he, being excellent (samveditaḥ), attains the material world (jagatyām) and is respected (abhisampadyate). The Ṛg mantras make him attain this mortal world. Brought to the world of men, if he then becomes endowed with fasting (tapasā), brahmacarya, and intelligence that follows scriptures (śraddhayā), he undertakes worship of the superior Brahman (mahimānam).

## 4.

*atha yadi dvi-mātreṇa manasi sampadyate*  
*so ‘ntarikṣaṁ yajurbhir unnīyate soma-lokam*  
*sa soma-loke vibhutim anubhūya punar āvartate*

**Перевод:**

**If the worshiper mediates using the two mātra oṁkāra, he attains Somaloka in the antarikṣa using Yajur mantras. Experiencing the glories of that planet, he then returns to earth.**

**Комментарий:**

If the worshipper mediates in his mind on inferior Brahman using the two mātra oṁkāra, he attains somaḷoka in the antarikṣa by the Yajur mantras. Somaloka indicates other higher worlds as well. The inferior brahman or kārya-brahman has two levels: using the one mātra oṁkāra, one attains glory in this world of men. By the two mātra oṁkāra, one attains Svarga. After experiencing the glories of that world again one returns to earth after the exhaustion of puṇyas.

## 5.

*yaḥ punar etaṁ tri-mātreṇom ity etenaivākṣareṇa*  
*paraṁ puruṣam abhidhyāyīta sa tejasi sūrye sampannaḥ*  
*yathā pādodaras tvacā vinirmucyate evaṁ ha vai sa pāpmanā*  
*vinirmuktaḥ sa sāmabhir unnīyate brahma-lokam*  
*sa etasmād jīva-ghanāt parātparaṁ puriśayaṁ purusam īksate*  
*tad etau ślokau bhavataḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The worshipper who meditates on the supreme Lord using oṁkāra of three mātras, attains the sun planet. Just as snake sheds its old skin, the worshipper sheds his sins. He attains Vaikuṇṭha by the Sāma mantras. He sees the supreme Lord who is superior to the pure jīvas who is superior to the embodied jīvas. There are two verses concerning this.**

**Комментарий:**

He who constantly (abhi) meditates (dhyāyīta) on the supreme Lord using oṁkāra of three mātras, attains the sun, though it gives temporary results by being uttered with one or two mātras.

Just as a snake (pādodaraḥ) becomes free of its skin, this worshipper becomes free of sin and attains Vaikuṇṭha (brahma-lokam) by the Sāma mantras (sāmabhiḥ) predominated with singing, or by persons uttering consoling words. Jīva-ghana means the jīva who has attained a material body by karma. Even Brahmā has such a body.

*brahmāṇaṁ vidadhāti pūrvam:*

**the Lord created Brahmā previously. Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad.**

Thus the phrase jīva-ghana refers to the world of saṁsāra. Ghana refers to a form which is gross. By the material body, the ātmā becomes gross. Thus it refers to a soul trapped in a material body.

Puriśayam means he who resides (śaya) as the ātmā within all living beings (pu). Superior to the embodied jīva is the pure jīva (parāt). Superior to the pure jīva is the Lord (puriśayam).

*bhagavān iti śabdo ‘yam tathā puruṣa ity api*  
*nirupādhī ca vartete vāsudeve sanātane*

**The words Bhagavān and the puruṣa when indicagting a person beyond upādhis refer to eternal Vāsudeva. Padma Purāṇa**

The puruṣa is Bhagavan. He is Vāsudeva. Thus the worshipper does not attain the loka of four-headed Brahmā but Vaikuṇṭha. Those who go to Brahmā’s planet cannot see the Lord. There are two verses concerning meditation using oṁkāra.

## 6.

*tisro mātrā mṛtyu-matyaḥ prayuktā*  
*anyonyasaktā anaviprayukhāh*  
*triyāsu bāhyābhyantara-madhyamāsu*  
*Samyak prayuktāsu na kampate jñaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**If one chants oṁ of three mātras with the syllables merged or extended, one does not achieve the result. If one uses oṁ properly in external actions, mental activity and japa, the wise man is not deprived of results.**

**Комментарий:**

The three mātra oṁ gives death if uttered with syllables merged because of fast chanting (anyonyasaktāḥ) or if the syllables are separated (anuaviprayuktāḥ). In the three activities of external actions such as sacrifices (bāhya), mental actions (abhyantara) and uttering japa (madhyamāsu), if oṁ is uttered properly (samyak prayuktāsu), the person knowing yoga does not waver. He achieves the result.

## 7.

*ṛgbhir etaṁ yajurbhir antarikṣaṁ*  
*sāmabhir yat tat kavayo vedayante*  
*tam oṁkāreṇaivāyatanenānveti vidvān*  
*yat tac chāntam ajaram amṛtam abhayaṁ paraṁ ceti*

**Перевод:**

**The wise man attains this world by the Ṛg mantras, the heavens by the Yajur mantras and the place that the wise know by the Sāma mantras using oṁkāra. That place is supreme, devoid of passions, devoid of old age, devoid of death and devoid of fear.**

**Комментарий:**

He attains the place (or person) that the wise men (kavayaḥ) know by uttering oṁkāra. Tad viṣṇoḥ paramam padam sadā paśyatni sūrayaḥ: the sages see this supreme abode of Viṣṇu. The wise man attains the supreme Brahman (param), the cause of everything, devoid of the six passions (śāntam)2, devoid of old age, devoid of death and devoid of fear.

## Sixth Question

### 1

*atha hainaṁ sukeśā bhāradvājaḥ papraccha*  
*bhagavan hiraṇyanābhaḥ kausalyo raja-putro mām*  
*upetyaitaṁ praśnam apṛcchata*  
*ṣoḍaśa-kalaṁ bhāradvāja puruṣaṁ vettha?*  
*tam ahaṁ kumāram abruvam nāham imaṁ veda*  
*yady aham imam avediṣaṁ kathaṁ te nāvakṣyam iti*  
*samūlo vā eṣa pariśuṣyati yo ‘nṛtam abhivadati tasmān nārhāmy anṛtaṁ vaktum*  
*sa tūṣṇīṁ ratham āruhya pravavrāja*  
*taṁ tvā pṛcchami kvāsau puruṣa iti*

**Перевод:**

**Sukeśa, son of Bharadvāja, asked Pippalāda, “Hiraṇyanābha of Kosala, a prince, approached me and asked if I knew the puruṣa having sixteen parts. I said to the prince that I did not know. If I did know why would I not say? One who speaks untruth dries up to his very root. Thus I cannot tell a lie. In silence, the prince, mounting his chariot, departed. I ask you, where does this puruṣa reside?”**

**Комментарий:**

Dear sir (bhagavan)! The prince of Kosala, named Hiraṇyanābha, approached me and asked this question. O son of Bharadvaja, do you know the puruṣa endowed with sixteen parts starting with praṇava and ending with nāma? I answered the prince. I do not know that puruṣa that you described. If I did know, why would I not speak it to a qualified disciple, a prince? He who speaks a lie dries up completely, at his very root. He destroys his impressions of puṇya which are a cause of auspiciousness. Thus you should have not think that I would lie.”

The prince departed with determination. This indicates Sukeśa’s shame at not knowing. That he rode on a chariot shows the prince’s power. I ask you about this puruṣa with sixteen parts. Where does he reside (kva)? He asks a question about the place or support of the puruṣa to know whether the puruṣa is the jīva or the Lord.

## 2.

*tasmai sa hovāca*  
*ihaivāntaḥ-śarīre somya sa puruṣo yasminn etāḥ*  
*śoḍaśa-kalāḥ prabhavantīti*

**Перевод:**

**He said to him, “The sixteen parts have the power to give enjoyment in this body where the puruṣa exists.”**

**Комментарий:**

The sixteen parts exist in this body where the puruṣa exists. Because of the puruṣa has as his support a body with limited space, the puruṣa refers to the jīva. “How can the jīva have sixteen endowments? Paramātmā fully manifests himself as the creator and supporter of the sixteen endowments.” The sixteen parts have the power (prabhavanti) to assist the jīva’s experience of happiness and distress by close contact with the jīva. Being endowed with sixteen parts means enjoyment of the sixteen parts. This enjoyment belongs to the jīva.

## 3.

*sa īkṣaṁ cakre kasmin aham utkrānta utkrānto bhaviṣyāmi kasmin vā pratiṣṭhate prathiṣṭhāsyāmīti*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva deliberated. When who departs do I depart? When who is established in the body, am I established?**

**Комментарий:**

Jīva is the enjoyer of the sixteen parts by association with them. Paramātmā is not the enjoyer. What is the process?

*\|\| so’dhyakṣe tad-upagamādibhyaḥ \|\|*

**Prāṇa enters the jīva since this is seen in the scriptures. Vedānta sutra 4.2.4**

The jīva is the basis. I will depart when who departs? I will become fixed

when who becomes fixed? This declares that the jīva is the enjoyer. Who is it that leaves and enters a new body when I leave and enter a new body? Thus the jīva deliberated. The jīva is the enjoyer since he creates the sixteen factors to assist him.

*na ca māṁ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya \|*  
*udāsīna-vad āsīnam asaktaṁ teṣu karmasu \|\|*

**O conqueror of wealth, these actions do not bind me. I am situated almost neutrally, uncontaminated by these acts. BG 9.9**

*na māṁ karmāṇi limpanti na me karma-phale spṛhā \|*  
*iti māṁ yo ’bhijānāti karmabhir na sa badhyate \|\|*

**Actions do not contaminate me. I have no desire for the fruits of work. One who knows this about me is not bound by karma. BG 4.14**

Thus Paramātmā is not the enjoyer of the sixteen parts, since he does not create them with an intention of having them assist his enjoyment.

## 4.

*sa prāṇam asṛjata prāṇac craddhāṁ khaṁ vāyur jyotir āpaḥ*  
*pṛthivīndrayaṁ manaḥ annam annāt vīrya tapo mantrāḥ*  
*karma lokāḥ lokeṣu ca nāma ca*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva created prāṇa. From that, he created faith in scriptures, ether, air, fire, water, earth, the senses, mind, food, and from food, vīrya, austerity, mantras, karmas, planets and names in the planets.**

**Комментарий:**

Deliberating the jīva first created mukhya-prāṇa, which exits and enters a body when the jīva exits and enters. Above that he created faith, the five elements, the senses such as speech, mind, external food upon which depends the powers of the body and senses. He created austerities, the mantras of the Ṛg, Yajur and Sāma Vedas, karmas like jyotiṣṭoma sacrifice, the planets where one enjoys the results of karmas, and names like Svarga in those planets. Though Paramātmā is the creator of these, the jīva is the creator since he performs karmas which cause the Lord to create them. Thus the jīva is the creator of the sixteen parts, since he enjoys them.

## 5.

*sa yathemā nadyaḥ syandamānāḥ samudrāyaṇāḥ*  
*samudraṁ prāpyās tam gacchanti bhidyete*  
*tāsāṁ nāma-rūpe, samudra ity evaṁ procyate*  
*evaṁ evāsya paridrasṭur imāḥ śoḍaśa-kalāḥ*  
*puruṣāyaṇāḥ puruṣaṁ prapyās taṁ gacchanti bhidyete*  
*cāsāṁ nāma-rūpe, purusa ity evaṁ procyate*  
*sa eṣo ‘kalo ‘mrto bhavati tad eṣa ślokaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Just as flowing rivers, having the ocean as their support, attaining the ocean, disappear in the ocean, dispersing their names and forms, and are thus simply called “the ocean,” so the sixteen factors of the jīva, having the Lord as their support, on attaining the Lord, disappear in the Lord, dispersing their names and forms, so that he is called simply the Lord. The Lord is not the enjoyer of these factors. He is deathless. There is a verse concerning this.**

**Комментарий:**

The aspirant for liberation should know that the Lord is devoid of these elements. Fḷowing rivers, reaching the ocean (āyanāḥ), attain the ocean. Āyana means that they become one with the ocean in the sense of having the ocean as their support. They do not appear to be different from the ocean. They disappear in the ocean (gacchanti). They cannot produce change in the ocean, such as increasing the size of the ocean. Their names, such as Gaṅgā and Yamunā, and their colors such as white and black, become dispersed (bhidyate). After entering the ocean they no longer exist. Thus it is only called the ocean. All rivers which have entered the ocean are simply called the ocean. They are no longer called Gaṅgā or Yamunā.

The sixteen part of the experiencer (paridraṣṭuḥ), the jīva, which assist him in enjoyment, attain the puruṣa Vāsudeva and disappear in him (gacchanti). As a blade becomes dull on contacting a stone slab, they give no enjoyment to the Lord. Their natures rooted in their existence depend on the will of the Lord (puruṣāyaṇāḥ). Names and forms, places of enjoyment, do not exist for the Lord. They exist for the jīva as assistants for his enjoyment in various places. Thus the Lord is simply called the puruṣa, perceived as a person with a separate existence, devoid of the sixteen parts which produce enjoyment and places of enjoyment. He is called akala, without parts since he is not the enjoyer of these parts. He is without death (amṛtaḥ), since death depends on a relationship with the sixteen factors which make the jīva an enjoyer.

There is a verse concerning the Paramātmā.

## 6.

*arā iva ratha-nābhau kalā yasmin pratiṣṭhitāḥ*  
*taṁ vedyaṁ puruṣaṁ veda yathā mā vo*  
*mṛtyuḥ parivyathā iti*

**Перевод:**

**One should know the Lord who should be known, in whom the sixteen factors are situated like spokes on the nave of a chariot wheel. Death should not afflict you.**

**Комментарий:**

One should know the Lord who should be known. The result is stated. May death not at all afflict (paritvyathā) you, who know Brahman.

## 7.

*tān hovāca*  
*etāvad evāham etaṁ paraṁ brahma veda*  
*nātaḥ param astīti*

**Перевод:**

**He said to the students, “To this extent I know the supreme Brahman. There is nothing superior to this.”**

**Комментарий:**

Pippalāda spoke to the six students (tān). I know the supreme Brahman to this extent. There is nothing more than this.

## 8.

*te tam arcayantaḥ tvaṁ hi nāh pitā yo ‘smākam avidyāyaḥ paraṁ pāraṁ tārayasīti*  
*namaḥ parama ṛsībhyo namaḥ parama ṛṣibhaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The disciples worshipped Pippalāda. “You are our father, since you delivered us from saṁsāra.” We offer respects to the greatest sages. We offer respects to the greatest sages.**

**Комментарий:**

The students worshipped Pippalāda. You are our father, having made us cross over saṁsāra. Birth from you is the best birth. That is the best birth which is produced from knowledge. They worshipped him after hearing. It ends with an invocation for peace. The repetition indicates the end of the Upaniṣad.
