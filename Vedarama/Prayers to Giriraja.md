# Prayers to Giriraja

## Prayers to Giriraja

*namaste girirajaya*  
*sri govardhana namine*  
*ashesha klesha nashaya*  
*paramananda dayine*

**Перевод:**

**I offer my respectful obeisances unto the king of all hills, Govardhana Hill (the source of enjoyment for the senses, land, and cows). He is a servant of Krsna and is Krsna Himself. He puts an end to unlimited sufferings and bestows the supreme bliss. (*****Preyo Bhakti Rasarnava*****)**

*śrī-śailā ūcuḥ*  
*tvaṁ sākṣāt kṛṣṇacandrasya*  
*paripūrṇatamasya ca*  
*goloke go-gaṇair yukte*  
*gopī-gopāla-saṁyute*

**Перевод:**

**In Goloka, which is filled with gopas, gopīs, and cows, you are the mountain of Lord Kṛṣṇacandra, the Original Supreme Personality of Godhead.**

### Text 14

*tvaṁ hi govardhano nāma*  
*vṛndāraṇye virājase*  
*tvaṁ no girīṣāṁ sarveṣāṁ*  
*giri-rājo 'si sāmpratam*

**Перевод:**

**You are named Govardhana. You are splendidly manifest in the world of Vṛndāvana and you are the king of all us mountains.**

### Text 15

*namo vṛndāvanākāya*  
*tubhyaṁ goloka-mauline*  
*pūrṇa-brahmātapatrāya*  
*namo govardhanāya ca*

**Перевод:**

**Obeisances to you, who are the crown of Goloka and who sit on Vṛndāvana's lap! Obeisances to Govardhana, the parasol of the Supreme Personality of Godhead! (Garga-samhita)**
