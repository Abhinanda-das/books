# Prema-samputika \[BS\]

### Text 1

Once, in the early morning, Krsna, assuming the attractive dress of a young woman, boldly went to a tree at the house of his beloved, and covering his face with the edge of a pink cloth, remained there with his eyes lowered.

### Text 2

Vrsabhanu’s daughter, seeing him from a distance, said, «O my friend Lalita! Who is this girl endowed with astonishing ornaments and lotus face, who makes my house into an emerald by her effulgence?»

### Text 3

Hearing their friend’s words, Lalita and Visakha quickly approached and, facing him, said, «Who are you, O thin-waisted girl? Please say where you have come from and what you are doing». But he did not answer at all.

### Text 4

Out of curiosity, Radha herself approached him and in a questioning mood asked, «Who are you? You attract my mind by the splendor of your form. Are you a goddess? Ah! Your form is so beautiful».

### Text 5

Since he remained silent, she again spoke to him, «O beautiful girl! Since you have come here, please quickly tell us about yourself. Please know that we are your most intimate friends. Why should you worry? O girl with a lowered head! Why should you be afraid?»

### Text 6

When he sighed slightly and pretended to lament, exposing his face, she spoke to him as he still remained silent. «Ah! You are in pain. I know that. You would not be in this condition otherwise».

### Text 7

«O lotus-faced girl! Please tell me about it. Have genuine trust in me so that I can attempt to cure you—since, by revealing to a friend the sharp pain caused by misfortune and which has wounded your mind will produce peace».

### Text 8

«Are you separated from your lover? Are you pained because of seeing some faults in him? Are you afraid of having committed some offense which has caused his affection to break? Or have some wicked people created some falsehood?»

### Text 9

«Or is your mind disgusted with your husband who has caused you disappointment, and you have become attached to some excellent, rare man? Then, like me, you are criticized with cruel words by elders and thus feel pain».

### Text 10

”O slender girl! Have you sometimes been pierced by the harsh arrow-like words of a co-wife, who is blinded by intoxication due to a little good fortune? But that is not possible, for what woman other than you could have such incomparable wealth of good fortune?»

### Text 11

«O moon-faced girl! Are you Mohini, of whom I have heard from Paurnamasi? Have you suddenly appeared here to bewilder someone like Siva? If Krsna, who becomes pierced by your glance, glances at you, there will be an amazing situation of mutual bewilderment».

### Text 12

Krsna, whose hairs began to stand on end all over his body on hearing her words, covered himself completely with his upper cloth. Seeing this, Radha spoke. «O friend! Are you suffereing from bodily pain? Is your chest, back or head in pain?»

### Text 13

«In my house there is some precious oil that can cure all diseases, sent by my respected father out of affection? Visakha! Quickly bring that oil and make it fulfill its purpose!»

### Text 14

«Being most affectionate, I will rub on this beautiful girl the oil, which is the personification of my affection. The pain in all her limbs will be removed. O friend! I will gently massage her head with great skill».

### Text 15

«I will bathe the girl in slightly warm, pleasant water sprinkled with many fragrant substances which will destroy pain. I will make her lotus face joyful, devoid of pain. I will make her able to speak».

### Text 16-17

«O friends! If, having been respected by me with tender words, acting for her benefit, with unmotivated affection, she still does not speak at all, and if she remains with a dejected face, deceptive in revealing her disease, let me try a different treatment. By taking this medicine, sickness of body, life airs, mind and all the senses becomes dissipated. She will become immediately strong by this pure medicine sent by Dhanvantari».

### Text 18

«If I can arrange for the lotus palms of the king of the bower to touch her chest all over, this girl with an indescribable ailment will smile, speak and sigh. She will attain an indescribable beauty which will make us smile».

### Text 19

Hearing those words, Krsna lifted his lowered, lotus-like lotus face, while surpressing a smile, and, pushing aside his locks of hair with the attractive fingers of his left hand, slightly raised the veil from his head.

### Text 20

When he spoke a little, revealing great beauty in his voice, Radha and her friends for a long time drank those words like female cakora birds and became astonished.

### Text 21

(Krsna speaks.) «O Radha with a beautiful face! I am a goddess living in Svarga. 1 have come to you with a painful heart. Please hear the reason for that. I want to know about something which I intend to explain. Except for you, who else can do this by any means?»

### Text 22

(Radha speaks.) «Saying that you are a goddess is not untrue. We accept you as such at this time, since among human beings who is there who can be compared to you? You can only be compared to yourself, since no one can compare to you in beauty.

### Text 23

«O girl whose face is as attractive as the autumn moon! Being innocent in heart, I falsely speculated about you and thus mocked you. I may have offended you. If you are affectionate to me, then I have become yours».

### Text 24

Krsna spoke. «O Radha! You have become my friend. Why are you so withdrawn? Please believe that I, a goddess, have become dependent on you. I always consider myself to be your servant since I have realized one drop of the ocean of your love, form and qualities».

### Text 25

«Please carefully hear what I say. Please remove this doubt which gives me intense pain. Even from the sprinkling of your sweet words, the pain in my heart has not dissipated even a little at this time».

### Text 26

«O friend! The force of Krsna’s flute which sounds in Vrndavana enters strongly into Svarga. Because of that sound, the minds of the chaste heavenly women become disgusted just on remembering the embrace of their husbands».

### Text 27

«Drinking the sweet sound of the flute which is halahala poison, the women have fainted due to a great fever generated from Cupid. Embracing their wives, whose limbs are like blazing coals, the husbands quickly give them up, wondering what has happened».

### Text 28

«In that city, no one is an elder. Who will chastise these women? Everyone has similar qualities. Who else will mock them? Since the sound of flute destroys their chastity, the women have been defeated».

### Text 29

«Since the sound of the flute spreads its influence every day in the heavenly women, one day, I thought, ‘What is that? From where does the sound arise? Who is playing that flute? ’”

### Text 30

«Thinking in this way, that very day I descended to earth and comfortably stayed at Vamsi-vata for some days. I saw various incomparable pastimes of Krsna and became familiar with his dear gopis and all the groups of sakhis».

### Text 31

Radha then jokingly spoke sweet words. «O fortunate girl! In Svarga you are counted as most clever. Other women whose senses have been cut by the knife of intense longing have made the meaning of their name sumanas (intelligent) useless».

### Text 32

Slightly moving his brow, and bathing his lips in the streams of beauty of his sweet smile, Krsna spoke. «O Radha! Understand that there is no one like you (in dedication to Krsna). Is it possible that Krsna will see me?»

### Text 33

(Radha speaks.) «Having experienced privately the pastimes of Krsna, the purpose for which you came, what interest should you have in any other man? Please tell me what you want to say? Since you have made me your friend, I have joked with you».

### Text 34

(Krsna speaks.) «O friend! Please joke. Who can defeat you at joking? Friendship is insignificant. You have become my life. You are human, but the women of heaven offer respects to your purifying qualities by bowing their heads».

### Text 35

«This is not just praise. I am not indifferent. Do not be shy. I do not speak a he. Laksmi and Parvati can never be equal to you in beauty and good fortune».

### Text 36

«No woman, even in Vaikuntha, can carry the great weight of prema as much as you can, even in her mind. I have heard everything about your prema in the assembly of Parvati on the peak of Kailasa».

### Text 37

«Hearing about you, I developed a great desire in my mind to see you. This desire has become fulfilled, but the fire in my heart has burst into flame. But my heart did not burst since it is very hard».

### Text 38

Radha said repeatedly, «Please quickly tell me what that pain is». With tears choking his throat, Krsna could not speak. Radha gently wiped his face, whose eyes were filled with tears, using the edge of her cloth.

### Text 39

Remaining silent for a moment, he regained composure. Krsna then spoke to her. «How did you develop strong, incomparable, unconditional love for Krsna, who has strong lust? Though he is full of knowledge, relying on insubstantial things, he gives pain to you and your friends.”

### Text 40

«Though fully endowed with a wealth of beauty, bravery, excellent good fortune and fame, though ornamented with the jewels of all good qualities, he is completely incapable of judging prema, and this causes his lust. One should not take shelter of him».

### Text 41

«Playing profusely during the day, revealing prema constantly, he then made an appointment with you in the bower at night, though you were innocent. Deceitfully he gave you up to enjoy with another girl».

### Text 42

«O friend! You began lamenting and this caused pain to your sakhis. Even the creepers and birds wept. Experiencing great pain, I saw all this, as I was hiding alone at Vamsi-vata».

### Text 43

«Playing in the rasa dance, he showed strong prema for you, giving up others. But after a moment he left you alone in the forest with your limbs suffering from fatigue».

### Text 44

«The pain which you experienced, with loud lamentation, deep fainting and bewildered actions, will remain in my heart during the eight stages till death, which is natural for the body, for many births».

### Text 45

«O famous girl! Since 1 am a devata, how can pain affect my heart? But suddenly I had a desire to see you. Ah! That desire has caused me to be pierced by a stake. ? friend! There is no method for pulling it out».

### Text 46

«My mind, attached to you, does not desire to return to Svarga and does not desire to remain for another second pierced by the stake of lamentation. Reeling at every step, I cannot remain steady. Thus I have become visible to you today, after a long time».

### Text 47

«I fear Krsna because he does not have shame in respect to dharma or people. He does not follow the path of mercy. In his infancy he killed Putana, in his pauganda period he killed Vatsasura and at the beginning of his kaisora age he killed Vrsasura».

### Text 48

Radha spoke. «O fortunate girl! I have an indescribable, constant attraction to you just as I have for Krsna. Though you criticize my beloved, my heart is very attracted to you.»

### Text 49

«If you are my friend, then do not go back to Svarga. You must stay with me always in Vraja. Opening the treasure chest containing my gems of prema, I 'will show them to you. I see no other solution».

### Text 50

(Krsna speaks.) «Ah! Even now you do not trust me. Be merciful. I will be your servant, Make me your friend. Give me good instructions. Let me swear by Visnu. Be pleased with me or beat me. O Radha! You are my shelter».

### Text 51

Hearing these words, Radha began to speak. «Since you inquire about my prema please listen. Prema is not something which can be measured. One who knows the Vedas does not know this prema».

### Text 52

«He who teaches about prema, he who desires to know prema and that knowledge of prema itself are all false. Prema is indescribable. It is an excellent object which disappears on logical inspection and disappears even without inspecting it.

### Text 53

«When the pure mind, attracted to Krsna, is devoid of both discrimination and non-discrimination, it shines on the throne of naturalness. The happiness of the devotee that arises when the Krsna is pleased by the devotee’s actions, being situated in its nature, manifests prema.

### Text 54

«If sufferings as great as Sumeru arise in this life or the next, from others or from one’s self, or from general living beings or from dear ones, very powerful prema immediately conquers those sufferings, just as a lion attains nourishment from subduing elephants.”

### Text 55

The lion of prema, with shining body, full of pride, fearless, full of faith, roaring, defeats dogs and sleeps soundly. What does he care for these troubles? Prema, like a lamp in the darkness, shines amidst all the troubles.

### Text 56

«It causes one to experience newer and newer taste out of lust and it causes intense intoxication. With its rays of nectar, like the moon, it gives joy to the three worlds and like the sun at pralaya it shines and causes pain to the worlds».

### Text 57

«O friend! Except for Krsna, who in the three worlds, or above or below, possesses this prema? The doe-eyed gopis fully taste this prema in Vrndavana, according to the intensity of their bhava. ’

### Text 58

«Prema sometimes superficially appears to be lust. Krsna enjoys unlimited happiness by that. In some people, lust appears to be prema. Krsna, however, being most expert, recognizes that false prema».

### Text 59

«O friend! When some gopis by her nature says ‘Bring me to Krsna, since I am greatly tormented’, by that «lust” she thinks only of his happiness. She is not filled with lust».

### Text 60

«Krsna is an ocean of prema, a mine of jewel-like qualities. His cheating, fickleness and deceit are all attractive. Showing this prema, which appears to be lust, to the women, he strongly attracts them».

### Text 61

«Are hundreds of thousands of intoxicated women, making great endeavors, capable of satisfying the desire of Krsna? Only the gopis have pure prema. I have realized that such prema alone controls Krsna».

### Text 62

«It is well known in the world that he is most attracted to me. That is never false. Because of that, he thinks my prema to be like Meru Mountain. He does not consider the prema of others to be even equal to three or four mustard seeds».

### Text 63

«Since Krsna is attracted to other gopis and plays with them according to their attraction for him, he does not offend any of them. Sometimes by chance irregularity appears. Because of that he is not happy and consequently I suffer.

### Text 64

«When he did not come after making an appointment to meet me, it was because of some obstacle in coming. Prevented from coming by some gopi, he enjoyed with her that night because of her request, while being pained by the forest fire of thinking how I was suffering».

### Text 65

«Because of his suffering I experienced great pain in my heart. Today all my dressing, ornaments, beauty and furniture, meant for his pleasure, have become useless! You have heard how I cried».

### Text 66

«In the morning I scolded him, though he was apologetic. ‘Go away from here. Enjoy with her again. ’ That anger gives him happiness since it arises from affection. Consider this to be the mode of love in Vrndavana».

### Text 67

«Repeatedly I revealed my own desire by saying, «O cheater! Why do you give me up and enjoy with another woman?» Revealing his desire by his body endowed with signs of love, he accepted his fault.

### Text 68

«Ah! Prema is a lamp which illuminates the abode of the heart of the two lovers and shines steadily without flickering. If that prema goes outside through the medium of the face, it quickly gets extinguished or becomes greatly weakened.

### Text 69

«The pleasant rays of prema, situated internally, coming out through the holes of the eyes, illuminating nicely the lips, cheeks, forehead and chest, manifesting an extraordinary nature, being self-revealing, offer themselves to knowing people».

### Text 70

«Constantly emanating from the face of Krsna, who is endowed with many lovers, that prema does not attain peace. The behavior of Krsna, who is skillful at telling lies, like a screen, quickly, covers that prema and reveals lust in its place».

### Text 71

«Krsna always speaks to the other proud women in this way. ‘O beloved! I have incomparable attraction to you only. Other women cannot remain in my heart even in dreams. ’ With signs of other women’s love on his limbs, he then speaks to me, though I am devastated».

### Text 72

«By the power of describing my face, eyes, grace, and unequalled sweet beauty, he desires to enjoy with me. And though he says ‘You are my life’ he does not reveal prema but rather lust in his speech».

### Text 73

«If the woman suffers from the great flames of separation, if her ocean of gravity is reduced to a single drop from longing, she reveals her prema through words such as the verse:

O dearly beloved! Your lotus feet are so soft that we place them gently on our breasts, fearing that your feet will be hurt. Our life rests only in you. Our minds, therefore, are filled with anxiety that your tender feet might be wounded by pebbles as you roam about on the forest path. SB [10.31.19](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/31.19)

### Text 74

«The life air cannot spread out in the unfathomable darkness of great pain. But the excellent lamp of prema produces intense light, since affection, like oil, makes the prema very abundant for a long time».

### Text 75

«During the rasa dance, giving up all others, Krsna enjoyed with me and then gave me up. Please hear the reason for this. The ocean of prema, the son of Nanda, who gives me the most respect, never causes offense to me».

### Text 76

«Seating me on a throne of good fortune made of incomparable, pure gems, decorating me with many types of beauty, going from forest to forest, he enjoyed his lover. He did not even think of anyone else».

### Text 77

«Then I thought something. ‘All my friends have not experienced Krsna, a great joyous, limitless ocean of nectar.. They are afflicted by pain of separation from both of us. Where are they? What will they do? ’”

### Text 78

«‘If we sit here for a few moments, then my friends, who are wandering here and there, will meet up with us quickly. ’ I said, ‘O dear one! I cannot go. Let us rest here for a moment. ’”

### Text 79

«Since he is most clever, he immediately understood clearly what was in my mind. Being an incomparable wealth of cleverness and the best of the rasikas, he immediately began considering in his mind».

### Text 80

«If her friends are suffering, then, if I take Radha, who will also be pained in her heart, what happiness is there for me in wandering in the forest? If I stay here, all the gopis, with frowning brows, will meet me».

### Text 81

«And they will scold her repeatedly for a long time. The taste of pastimes which has just begun will be broken. If they all return to their houses in anger, the pleasurable rasa dance will not be accomplished today».

### Text 82

«Radha had previously made a request in jest. ‘Dearest! You can embrace millions upon millions of gopis at one time. I desire to see that. Please make that happen. ’”

### Text 83

«Therefore, first, I wifi leave Radha for a moment. They wifi not blame her as she is decent. I will take all the criticism on my head. I will be their debtor. I will make them all affectionate to Radha».

### Text 84

«Seeing Radha’s unequalled, insurmountable pain from separation, they will drown in a great ocean of astonishment. I will remove their pride in their own prema and make them realize her most exalted position».

### Text 85

«Her enjoyment in meeting with me is far greater than that of the other gopis. Her suffering in separation ig billions of times more than theirs. Madhurya-rasa is nourished by both these conditions. Let madhurya-rasa shame them profoundly in front of her, and let it give her prominence».

### Text 86

«Some gopis say, ‘Krsna is lusty since he gives us up, who have more love for him, and enjoys alone with Radha. ’ Now they will torment Radha’s friends in anger and criticize her and me profusely».

### Text 87

«I will make them understand that in separation, the flame of Radha’s vadaba-fireof prema has increased millions of times in intensity, and that, being licked fiercely by those flames as they approach, their own prema has become like insignificant lamps».

### Text 88

«In this way my desire that all the gopis become unified will be achieved. When they all assemble in the rasa dance, no one will feel envy on seeing Radha shining with beauty in the center with me».

### Text 89

«Sometimes a person, wishing benefit for his friend, gives him suffering which will later produce happiness for him. Similarly a person gives pain to his eyes by applying ointment, and this makes the eyes shine in the future».

### Text 90

«Thinking in this way, Krsna carried me graciously on his chest and walked for some steps. He said, ‘Sit down a moment. ’” Putting me down in a soft place, he went out of my sight.

### Text 91

«When he desired to show himself, after becoming unsteady on seeing that I was most disturbed, all the gopis, extremely pained, arrived and tried to pacify me».

### Text 92

«Killing Arista, Baka, Agha, and Vatsasura, who torment the whole world, and Putana, who dressed up like a gopi, is not a fault. The sakti of Visnu, which protects the devotees, manifested in Krsna at that time».

### Text 93

«Garga told Nanda that Krsna, Nanda’s son, was similar to Narayana. Krsna’s killing demons and performing extraordinary actions such as lifting Govardhana are the witness to those words.”

### Text 94

«O goddess! Even Narayana cannot be equal to Krsna in form, qualities and sweetness. Though Garga did not say this, this fact has rightly manifested in my heart».

### Text 95

Hearing the most charming words of his beloved Radha, Krsna (still disguised) immediately spoke. «I have understood that the prema which you described with all its qualities has taken shelter of you alone».

### Text 96

«Because of prema the faults of the lover become good qualities, even a hundred pains inflicted by the lover become nectar, and a trace of pain experienced by the lover cannot be tolerated. One cannot give up that prema, even if one must give up one’s body».

### Text 97

«Prema makes one profoundly experience the lover’s incomparable greatness at every moment, even though the lover has no great qualities at all. O Radha! You possess that prema. I heard this in Parvati’s assembly and I have seen it here».

### Text 98

«O friend! Krsna does not have such love for you. That is the truth. By his actions I can infer this. My life airs, burned in the forest fire of your pain, and all your friends are the proof of this».

### Text 99

«I cannot believe what you spoke about Krsna’s intentions when he left you. You did not hear this from Krsna’s mouth or his friends’ mouths. Moreover, in this life neither he nor his friends speak true words».

### Text 100

You may say, «I know whatever is in the mind of Krsna». «O Radha! Do you know the scriptures which describe how to meet with the Lord—by which you will be able to enter into another’s mind and body?»

### Text 101

(Radha speaks.) «You are a goddess and thus always eager to achieve uninterrupted perfection. How can I be like that? Still, I can speak unlimitedly about what you ask if you have faith in me. Otherwise speaking about it is useless».

### Text 102

(Krsna speaks.) «O friend! If there is some cause for gaining faith, or if you have great ability to instill faith, why should I not believe your words? Otherwise, your lover may be an ocean of good qualities, but that he loves you is only your opinion».

### Text 103

(Radha speaks.) «Being expert at joking, you have said that one who has knowledge of entering the mind and body of others can have a dear lover, but you also said that one cannot understand the mind of a person accepted as a lover».

### Text 104

(Krsna speaks.) «O Radha! Why are you lamenting loudly? If you know Krsna’s mind why are you not happy?»

(Radha speaks.) «O goddess! You speak the truth. But listen carefully. Some indescribably sakti that arises from not seeing Krsna, which causes loss of discrimination, has manifested».

### Text 105

(Krsna speaks.) «O Gandarvika! You know Krsna’s mind. I do not quarrel with that. But does he know your mind or not?»

(Radha speaks.) «He knows my mind. What are you saying? Hear this secret, since you have softened my heart».

### Text 106

(Krsna speaks.) «O Radha! Since I am overwhelmed with affection by your prema, I have inquired boldly. Just as my ears desire to hear this secret, so you should tell it. Do not hide it».

### Text 107

(Radha speaks.) «Because we are continually situated within each other, we know each other’s hearts. This is well known among everyone, but it is only a figure of speech. We two are actually one soul. We can never be two».

### Text 108

«In one soul, completely full of rasa and most deep, our two bodies are tied with the rope of one life air, just as, in some lake, two shining lotuses, one blue and the other golden blossom from one stem».

### Text 109

«Just like one vessel filled with oil and having one wick shines with two pure lights, we invisibly destroy each other’s misery and give joy to all our friends, situated around us».

### Text 110

«If the wind of separation arises, it makes our two bodies tremble and causes both of us to faint simultaneously. At that time the disturbed sakhis attempt to remove that separation, having us recover by arranging for a meeting».

### Text 111

«O auspicious girl! Opening the treasure box of my heart, I have today shown you the most secret gem (prema). May the great darkness of your doubts be destroyed! Holding the gem in your heart, never reveal this externally».

### Text 112

Krsna spoke. «O friend! Whatever you said I have understood to be quite reasonable. But my heart is very deceitful. I desire to test what you said. What should I do?»

### Text 113

«You are situated here. Krsna is now in his father’s house or herding the cows in the forest. I believe that you two are one, but how can that be accepted without testing it?»

### Text 114

«O beautiful faced woman! If I can see right now, in this place, that whatever object you think of, in whatever way, Krsna is thinking of in the same way, then I will have faith in what you have said».

### Text 115

«He may be far away or close, but if, simply by thinking ‘Come here quickly’ he comes in front of you, the understanding that you two are truly one will make me happy».

### Text 116

«If when you remember each other, there should by chance arise some obstacle because of subservience to elders, approach of demons, or other causes, and thus you cannot meet, I have no quarrel with that».

### Text 117

«O girl with lovely eyes! O friend! If, restricted in your parent’s house, you cannot call Krsna, you must make an appointment with him in a distant place. You do not desire that he comes for your pleasure. I know that well».

### Text 118

«O girl dear to Krsna! On my request, remember Krsna now. Krsna should come and bestow happiness. There is no opportunity for your elders to come here. You can dispel my misery arising from my doubt».

### Text 119

When requested by Krsna (in disguise) bluntly in this way, Radha spoke with good reasoning. «Do not make me the object of mockery. If I do not do as you say, my prema, being shamed, will give me pain for a long time».

### Text 120

«O sun god, worshipped by all the devatas, my object of worship, who gives happiness to the three worlds by showing yourself! O Lord who fulfills all my desires! O merciful one! O Lord of the lotus! O sun god, known as the witness of all truth and lies!»

### Text 121

«Radha and Krsna are always one entity. If this famous statement is not false, then at this time Krsna should appear, manifesting before my eyes and giving joy to my associates».

### Text 122

Saying this, Radha closed her eyes and meditated on her lover. She stopped her senses like a yogini and remained continously silent.

### Text 123

At that moment, Krsna, suddenly giving up his woman’s dress, making all the sakhis favorable by moving his brow, now that they recognized him, began kissing and embracing Radha constantly.

### Text 124

Her hairs stood on end and she became drenched with tears, on realizing Krsna in meditation and also seeing him externally. Her heart was immersed in bliss. Truly she was a yogini who sees Brahman (or whose collyrium had disappeared).

### Text 125

After a moment Radha came to her senses. Covering her face with the edge of her cloth, beautiful-eyed Radha became embarrassed. Lalita said to Krsna, «Ah playful Krsna! It is most amazing how you have come to this place without being noticed».

### Text 126

«The man is counted as the best of the bold, who fearlessly comes to the inner chambers, accessible only by the virtuous women, where even the wind cannot enter by force».

### Text 127

«There, Radha, the personified Ganga, whose fame purifies all chaste women, is protected by friends like me. Having bathed and seated herself for worshipping the sun, she has closed her eyes to meditate on the sun».

### Text 128

«You have touched her limbs by force. Do you not fear the sun god? Will you give no respect to public opinion or dharma? Are you not familiar with the meaning of shame?»

### Text 129

«O Madhava! Therefore, today I praise your good fortune, since Jatila and her angry husband are not here. What can we, her friends, being young girls, do to you? By good luck, you, the best of rascals, have been saved from these dangers».

### Text 130

Krsna spoke. «I have not committed any fault. Absorbed in playing in the yard of the cow pens, by chance I remembered Radha. Suddenly I was brought here as if by some divine being».

### Text 131

Radha spoke. «O Lalita! Where is that goddess?Seeing her, we can believe in her». (Lalita speaks.) «Seeing you two meet, the goddess gave up her anxiety. She shines within this room and gives us bliss».

### Text 132

Krsna said, «Please introduce this goddess, this sakhi that you mentioned». He then spoke to Lalita who had not answered him. ’’Now I understand. O Lalita! By good fortune, in a short time your crafty nature has become clear.

### Text 133

’’Some perfected woman or goddess who flies in the sky has come. From her, Radha has received some secret mantra. Certainly, Radha wants to bring me under control every day and make me her servant by force.

### Text 134

«O Radha! That goddess should give me the mantra also. O beautiful girl! Be my helper. Take me to some solitary place and make me your disciple. I have surrendered to you and am eager».

### Text 135

(Radha speaks.) «The flute also has the great power of mantras. It brings the most chaste, respectable women to your lap».

(Krsna speaks.) «When you steal the flute, then what should I do? I can no longer fulfill my desires by using that flute».

### Text 136

(Lalita speaks.) «The goddess, being shy, is hiding within the house. How will she teach the mantra to you? If you are eager for it, you must enter the house yourself. If she is merciful, you will attain your desires».

### Text 137

When Krsna, hearing this, entered the house, Radha spoke to Lalita. «O sakhi! Please tell me what is happening, since I have doubts». Lalita said, «Do not fear. Come. We will enter the house and, along with Krsna and the sakhis, look».

### Text 138

After that, the seed of Krsna’s verbal skill was planted by him in the field of Radha’s heart, and it sprouted amidst the showers of sweet, soft smiles of the sakhis. It grew into the tree of Radha’s arguments and then bore fruit with plentiful juice, in the form of all knowledge.

### Text 139

Lalita said, «The goddess has disappeared or has left this house. Therefore, we should go from here quickly and search for her. O Radha! Teach him the mantra and give him bliss». The sakhis quickly left the room.

### Text 140

At that time, Radha and Krsna, decorated with many jewels of pastimes, arising from their treasure box of prema, conquered millions of Cupids, since even the devotees, intensely joyful from hearing, glorifying and contemplating the pastimes in order to attain Radha and Krsna, continually defeat millions of Cupids.

### Text 141

In the month of Phalguna, 1606 Saka era, on the hank of Radha and Krsna’s kunda, nourished by drinking the sweet nectar of Rupa Gosvami’s words, someone has revealed the poem called Prema-samputa.
