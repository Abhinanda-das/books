# Priceless Moments With a Pure Devotee

## Preface

I have decided to write this book because of the many wonderful responses I have received from devotees whenever I tell them some of my personal exchanges with Srila Prabhupada. As a senior travelling sannyasi in ISKCON, I am frequently asked to speak in the temples I visit.

By the greatest good fortune, I had these experiences bestowed upon me by Lord Sri Krishna. I have been blessed to have had a fair amount of personal association with Srila Prabhupada, who awarded me sannyasa in 1971. Naturally, everyone who comes to the temple knows something about Srila Prabhupada. That is natural because he is the Founder-Асаryа of ISKCON, and we base our teachings on the books he translated and explained, like Bhagavad-gita and Srimad-Bhagauatam.

However, many may not know much about his personal dealings. I find great joy in sharing these memories of my time with Srila Prabhupada. As Lord Krishna says in Bhagavad-gita (10.9), “The thoughts of My pure devotees dwell in Me, their lives are fully devoted to My service, and they derive great satisfaction and bliss from always enlightening one another and conversing about Me.”

That same “great satisfaction and bliss” can be experienced by talking about Krishna’s pure devotees like Srila Prabhupada. I sincerely pray that the readers of this book will also get a chance to experience that “great satisfaction and bliss” available at the lotus feet of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, Founder-Acarya of ISKCON.

In His service, His insignificant servant, Trivikrama Swami

## Foreword

Trivikrama Swami’s memoir "Priceless moments with a Pure Devotee" is a wonderful read. I have read it four times, and I want to read it more. In my Foreword, I just want to give you a little taste of the book and invite you to read it on your own.

Trivikrama Swami first met Srila Prabhupada while Trivikrama was a student at the University of Buffalo in New York. There was a newly-opened centre there where Rupanuga dasa was the temple president. Trivikrama attended classes, read Prabhupada’s books, and quickly became attached to him. He met Prabhupada personally a few times and received first initiation from him in 1969. Surprisingly, within only two years, Trivikrama was awarded sannyasa initiation by Prabhupada, who saw how serious this student was. After a while in America, Prabhupada asked Trivikrama Swami to go to London, where he would take up the service of helping to train the new brahmacharis there. Trivikrama Swami immediately obeyed his spiritual master’s request and went to England, where he was assigned to lead nama sankirtana on the streets of London. He felt the kirtana was purifying him very much, and he credits it for keeping him free from falling down for over fifty years.

Maharaja got the opportunity to become Prabhupada’s masseur. He found this service to be extremely ecstatic. Every day he gave Prabhupada a vigorous massage lasting almost an hour, and his love for his spiritual master grew and grew. They had many intimate exchanges, and Maharaja was always an obedient, loving servant. His memoir is really a love story of a student and his spiritual master. Trivikrama Maharaja has immense love and dedication for Prabhupada.

Trivikrama Maharaja is a strong-minded person, and he was sometimes outspoken. He mentioned to Prabhupada that sometimes he had trouble dealing with his GBC representatives. Prabhupada told him that if he had difficulty working with the GBC men, then he could bring up his difficulty by going directly to Srila Prabhupada. This shows the great trust that Prabhupada has in this disciple. Once a devotee was criticising Trivikrama Maharaja to Prabhupada, and Prabhupada defended him by saying, “He goes wherever I send him.” Prabhupada sent him to various places to do the preaching, and, taking the duty of a sannyasi, Maharaja went to countries on his own initiative also. He preached in South Korea, in Japan, in China, and in India.

Throughout "Priceless moments with a Pure Devotee" there are innumerable memories of Prabhupada and Trivikrama Maharaja, which the devotees will love to read and hear. Here are a few:

Trivikrama Maharaja was present during a year when a big controversy took place which threatened to divide ISKCON. The temple presidents in America complained that Tamal Krishna Maharaja and his Radha-Damodara party of travelling buses were stealing brahmacharis from the ISKCON temples and telling them it was better to join the Radha-Damodara party and get the association of brahmacharis and sannyasis. Several sannyasis were present with Prabhupada in his room, and Tamal Krsna Maharaja was defending himself about the controversy. Prabhupada was putting tilak on, and Trivikrama Swami noted that Prabhupada’s hand was shaking. Madhudvisa spoke up and said, “You know, Prabhupada, there are two sides to this story”.—meaning the complaints by the temple presidents about the Radha-Damodara party. Trivikrama Maharaja could not restrain himself and spoke out: “No! There is only one side, Srila Prabhupada’s side!” Trivikrama Swami reports that the other three swamis looked at him with daggers in their eyes.

Throughout the book, Trivikrama Maharaja philosophises and makes comments from the position of a senior devotee. These remarks are full of wisdom, and there are good cautionary statements in them about how we have to stay together in ISKCON and follow Prabhupada’s instructions and not fight with one another.

Trivikrama Swami was present in Vrndavana when Prabhupada passed away at 7:30 PM on November 14, 1977. He felt a great personal loss, but he did not feel lost. He thought how Prabhupada had left us his books, instructions, and the institution. “Now, our sincerity to serve Srila Prabhupada in separation would be tested.”

## Afterword

“After Srila Prabhupada’s passing, all of us understood that he wanted the eleven gurus he had chosen to be his ritvik gurus to become regular gurus and have their own disciples. This was fully accepted throughout ISKCON.” But problems arose because the new gurus, instead of following in Prabhupada’s footsteps, began to imitate him. Many of them insisted on large vyasasanas and honorific titles. Trivikrama Swami took a leading role in what was called “guru reform”. He pushed the point that the new gurus were wrong in imitating Srila Prabhupada instead of following in his footsteps. “Thus,” he writes, “I became persona non grata in many temples.”

Finally, after nine years of imitating Srila Prabhupada, the full GBC made significant reforms to the guru issue in 1986. Trivikrama Maharaja’s name was added to the growing list of devotees who were allowed to initiate and become gurus. He concludes his book by saying that the guru system seems to be working fine, but we are still missing Prabhupada’s personal presence and his ability to give final resolution to any conflict that arises.”

Now I will end my rough Foreword and allow you to taste the actual nectar from an exalted pure devotee who was very dear to Srila Prabhupada. Trivikrama Maharaja has always been kind to me in our dealings, and I wish him and his disciples all good fortune in their mission. May all the devotees in ISKCON benefit from reading this book.

Satsvarupa dasa Goswami

## Introduction

“According to their karma, all living entities are wandering throughout the entire universe. Some of them are being elevated to the upper planetary systems, and some are going down into the lower planetary systems. Out of many millions of wandering living entities, one who is very fortunate gets an opportunity to associate with a bonafide spiritual master by the grace of Krishna. By the mercy of both Krishna and the spiritual master, such a person receives the seed of the creeper of devotional service”. —Caitanya-caritamrita, Madhya 19.151

I was brought up as a Roman Catholic. My faith in God was lost when I joined the US Navy at seventeen. Atheistic literature appealed to my intellect, and the desire to enjoy sex life without feeling guilty appealed to my senses. Life went this way for some years—justifying sense gratification using atheistic philosophy as an excuse. However, I never found real happiness.

Then I got acquainted with some books that spoke of God but in an impersonal way. I also started practicing a form of hatha-yoga and met a yogi who was giving classes. He seemed like a good man. Their practice was done individually, not in a group. I took it seriously and attended classes regularly. Once, this yoga teacher told us, “Be still and know that you are God.” I remember thinking when he said this, “I never thought I was God, but why not try it?” Just at that time, I met the Hare Krishna devotees. I was immediately impressed by their sincerity and simplicity. They were enthusiastic and actively preaching their message of the love of God. Their program included kirtan, prasadam, and the association of the devotees, which were all very attractive to me.

A bit later, the devotees opened a small center near my University, so I started visiting them regularly. Nevertheless, their firm conviction that their holy book, Bhagavadgita As It Is, established the person Krishna as the

Supreme Truth put me off a little. Likewise, it was their belief that “their” Gita was the only one that truly gave the proper message.

It all seemed somewhat akin to the preaching of many Christians that the Bible was God’s only bonafide word. Personally, I had read a little of the Bhagavad-gita and considered it a holy book, and that opinion was confirmed by spiritual people whom I respected. Therefore I went to the library and found 4-5 different editions of the Bhagavad-gita and spent some time doing a comparative study. Part of my motive for this research was to defeat the devotees. However, before long, I became convinced that the devotees were right and that Srila Prabhupada’s translation of, and purports to, the Bhagavad-gita were superior to other Gitas and his Gita was fully accurate.

Before meeting Srila Prabhupada, I had tried LSD and other intoxicants, but they did not give me the lasting bliss I was looking for. Nonetheless, after having met devotees, they gradually convinced me that God was not available by using some chemicals but only by the process of pure devotion to Him. Being interested in getting personal experience, I was determined to try this bhakti-yoga process sincerely. With their permission, I moved into the temple while still attending classes at the University of Buffalo in New York.

Living in the temple was good; even my school work improved. Having smoked cigarettes for ten years, I knew it to be an unhealthy, unclean, and expensive habit, but my attempts to quit had been futile. Now, in the devotees’ association and under the temple’s rules, I succeeded in renouncing this foolish addiction for good.

## Chapter 1. Srila Prabhupada was just like a living fountain (1968-1969)

## It enthused me so much - January 1968

Devotees came to the University at Buffalo. Allen Ginsberg, a famous poet, was also with them. They organized a program in a big hall on campus, and around 300 people were all singing Hare Krishna when I entered.

Ginsberg had been chanting Hare Krishna before he met the devotees. He had gone to India in the early 60s, which is how he got connected. His famous tune was that everybody sang at the same time. There was no call and response. He was singing, “Hare Krishna Hare Krishna Krishna Krishna Hare Hare Hare Rama Hare Rama Rama Rama Hare Hare” while playing the harmonium. Ginsberg kept getting faster and faster, and everybody was dancing. It was really wild.

I felt there was something very unusual in this chanting that deeply attracted me to it. After some time, devotees, headed by Rupanuga Prabhu from Iskcon New York City, again came to the university to open a center there in Buffalo, which they did in the Spring of 1968.

## Discovering the temple - Summer 1968

I discovered the temple address by coincidence when I met an acquaintance I knew from class. He was on the street, and I saw he had some covering on his hand. I approached him and asked, “Did you hurt your hand?”. He said, “No.” He pulled out the beads and said that we chant the Hare Krishna mantra on these beads. Then he told me that they had just recently opened a temple, and it is right here. He pointed to a house just across the street and invited me to come.

## Either willingly or obliged - Summer 1968

My first visit to the Hare Krishna temple was with a fellow student from my university. Other people were also entering the temple, and we saw them bowing down in front of the altar. I also bowed down, thinking this was all new to me, so why not do as the others were doing? My friend, however, did not bow down.

After the program, we left, and I asked him why he had not bowed down like the others. His reply was, “I don’t bow down to anything!” Silently, I remembered thinking at that moment that sometimes I had to bow down whether I liked it or not, like when I had drunk too much and was obliged to bow down to vomit in front of the toilet.

## September 1968

## First Meeting with Srila Prabhupada

Some weeks later, before moving into the temple, I finally got the chance to meet Srila Prabhupada personally in the small storefront temple at 26 2nd Avenue in New York City. When I went on that day, Srila Prabhupada led the kirtan, and his chanting was very enthusiastic. After the chanting, we all sat, and he gave a class from Bhagavadgita. Then he distributed prasadam of small pieces of cut apple. Everyone came forward, and he placed the fruit in our hands. It was so simple yet very satisfying.

Afterward, another devotee from Buffalo, Bhurijana Prabhu, brought me to Srila Prabhupada’s small room upstairs behind the storefront temple and introduced me to him. My first impression was that he was very kind but vulnerable. I knew him to be an exalted person, a great scholar, whom his disciples worshipped. But he was sitting on the floor behind an aluminium trunk he used as a desk. No one else was in the room, and the door was open so that anyone could walk in. My immediate thought was that he should be protected and have a bodyguard or a secretary to check people at his door. It wasn’t a very good neighbourhood in the lower East side of NYC. Of course later on he did, but this was the early days, and anyone could walk in.

After Bhurijana Prabhu told him my karmi name, he said, “Thank you very much”. I thought that was a little strange because I had not done anything. But that was his mood, to cast a large net and warmly invite everyone who was even slightly inclined to participate in the process of devotional service.

## Trying to understand who he was

After Srila Prabhupada had left New York City, he went to the New Vrindavan community in West Virginia. I wanted to see more of Srila Prabhupada and the devotees, so I drove there. I was not disappointed as there were many opportunities to have Srila Prabhupada’s association, which was very relishable. Everything about him was relishable. The way he walked, the way he talked, the way he smiled. Just being with Srila Prabhupada was enlivening. From New Vrindavan, I drove back to Buffalo to continue my studies.

## October 1968

## Moving into the temple

I started visiting the temple sometimes in the morning and sometimes in the evening. I felt inspired by the visits. I found that especially chanting and associating with the devotees was a good way to start the day. I felt inspired by it. Then I started thinking about why not move to the temple so I can be there every morning. After getting permission from the temple president, I gave up my rented apartment and moved into the temple.

## Go on chanting, and this hallucination will disappear

There was one devotee who was living with us in the temple. He helped me a lot at that time. One day he wrote a letter to Srila Prabhupada where he said something to the effect; Srila Prabhupada, when I am chanting my japa, I walk around the temple, and sometimes I see Krishna running in front of me. Soon after that, Srila Prabhupada replied, expressing his appreciation for other points this devotee mentioned. Then he said, “As far as you’re seeing Krishna running in front of you, if you continue chanting, then this hallucination will disappear.”

Seeing Krishna face to face is not so easy. However, the process of chanting Hare Krishna does give us a chance to see Him. But our chanting must be pure. This is what we are trying to achieve. We want to come to the stage of pure chanting. For that, we need to be honest, and we need to avoid the sahajiya philosophy. Sahajiya means one thinks that he is more advanced than he is. But that is a cheating mentality and does not correspond with reality. When we completely remove unwanted things from our hearts and inattentiveness in hearing and chanting, only then will we come to the stage of pure chanting. It all depends on us. Sometimes the example of the sky is given. The sky is impartial, but birds, according to their capacities, can fly higher or lower. If we want to advance quickly, no one will stop us. But generally, we become comfortable at a certain level, and then our progress is relatively slow. But how high and how fast we develop our Krishna consciousness is in our hands.

## November 1968

One person was sitting on the University’s ground practicing yoga. I approached him and asked him to come to the temple where we have bhakti yoga which he did and later became initiated as Prahladananda das.

## Teaching course in Vedic Literature - January 1969

In Jan 1969, Rupanuga Prabhu, the temple president, arranged for a course at the University of Buffalo on the teachings of Vedic Literature. There were about 70 people attending the program, and they received academic credit for taking the course. Some people who participated in the class became devotees like Bhagavan Prabhu and his future wife Krishna Baumani Mataji and Nityananda Prabhu. Devotees would often come to the campus and would have a sit-down harinam. Gunagrahi das Goswami also became involved with ISKCON at that time.

## April 1969, Buffalo

## Srila Prabhupada comes to our temple.

In the spring of 1969, Srila Prabhupada visited our little center in Buffalo. He stayed at the home of an Indian gentleman who had his apartment near the temple. The temple devotees and I saw him there. Initiation was to be given while he was there, and I was on the list for getting the first initiation. Before that meeting, I had briefly seen Srila Prabhupada in New York a few times, but I did not know him very well.

While sitting there, I gazed at him with a piercing look, trying to fathom who he was. Suddenly he raised his head and saw me staring at him. He kept his gaze on me and asked in a somewhat confrontational way, “Yes?” I was exposed, so to speak, and felt trapped. Trying to see into him had turned the tables on me and exposed my foolishness. I lowered my gaze, realizing I could not know him that way, but I had to get his mercy first to understand him better. The real process of knowing the spiritual master comes from hearing, not seeing.

Srila Prabhupada was very personal and kind-hearted. We never felt he was exploiting us. He was always giving more than he took. We could not repay him because he was giving us love. That was something we did not possess. Because where is real love in this material world? But Srila Prabhupada showed that he always loved us, and even when he was angry with us, he still loved us. That was something I had not experienced before. Therefore, I was happy to be his servant, even when he sometimes chastised me.

## He is a good one

The next day, during the initiation ceremony, we were all sitting around the sacrificial arena where the fire was to be started. I thought, “I have not experienced any better way of living before this. I am ready to graduate from the University, but instead of getting involved in my chosen career, I should give this Krishna consciousness an honest try.”

The ceremony began with Srila Prabhupada showing us how to purify ourselves by doing acamana. From a cup of water in front of us, we each placed a drop of water into our right palm and then sipped the water. I was a little enthusiastic and it caught Srila Prabhupada’s eye. He turned to one of his leading servants there, Brahmananda Prabhu, and said, indicating me, “He is a good one.” That was Srila Prabhupada’s mood; he was always ready to appreciate even a small amount of enthusiasm in any of his followers.

In reality, Srila Prabhupada’s most striking quality was his ability to encourage everyone. He would not use flattery, but somehow he could encourage all of us to make an attempt to sacrifice for Krishna, to sacrifice for their own good, for spiritual life. He was an expert at seeing any little good quality in us and fanning it so that it would burn brightly. That was the first impression Srila Prabhupada made. We knew he would see the best in us.

Later, he could correct us, but that was out of love. We always felt he was our well-wisher, whereas teachers, ordinary people, and even parents could be a little envious, or perhaps there was a motive behind their appreciation. But it was nothing like that with, Srila Prabhupada. His most impressive qualification was his compassion, and we needed it. Otherwise, without his mercy, none of us would have ever taken to Krishna consciousness.

## Strong like a thunderbolt

Srila Prabhupada took part in a program at the University of Buffalo and attended one across town at the NY State Teachers College. At the State College program, all the devotees came from the temple. About 200 people were there. Srila Prabhupada started the program in the usual manner with a strong stand-up kirtan. After that, all his disciples bowed down when Srila Prabhupada began to recite the Jaya от prayers. Then we repeated his pranama mantra while still bowing down. After this, Srila Prabhupada gave a class for about forty minutes. Then he asked if anyone had a question.

One boy raised his hand, and Srila Prabhupada recognized him. In a loud mocking voice, he imitated the sound the devotees had made while offering Srila Prabhupada’s mantra. Something like, “What does mamamama mean?” This boy was sitting behind me to my left.

After he made this insulting sound, Srila Prabhupada immediately responded with an angry voice. It felt like a bolt of angry energy flew over my left shoulder right into this boy. I do not recall what Srila Prabhupada said exactly, but it had the desired effect. It silenced the boy completely. He looked dumbfounded—really surprised that this “old man” had the energy to blast him so powerfully and personally.

The next day, back at the temple during the morning program, I looked out the window and saw this same boy across the street. He looked at the temple as if thinking, “What the hell hit me last night?” I think his name was Peter, but later, Srila Prabhupada initiated him with the name Kusakratha Dasa Brahmacari.

And this is proof that praise or chastisement from a pure devotee is always beneficial. Srila Prabhupada could get angry at someone, but he was not doing it for some personal interest but for the good of others.

On the other hand, it shows that a devotee can tolerate offenses made against himself. However, if someone disrespects the Lord, His holy names, or other devotees, a devotee will fiercely protect them. Interestingly, shortly after the departure of Srila Prabhupada from this world, Kusakratha Dasa began to translate and publish classical Sanskrit and Bengali Gaudiya Vaisnava works into the English language. According to available data, Kusakratha Dasa translated over 150 different transcendental works under the name of the Krishna Institute.

## First assignment

During Srila Prabhupada’s stay, I was assigned to be his driver. Every morning Srila Prabhupada took a morning walk, and I drove him to where he was going to walk. The first morning I suggested we drive to Niagara Falls, about thirty minutes away. Srila Prabhupada agreed, so we drove to Niagara Falls every morning during his stay. He liked the atmosphere there with its natural scenic beauty.

On one such drive, Srila Prabhupada asked me what I studied at the University. I told him Sociology, and he asked what that consists of. I mentioned that we studied social problems like alcoholism and crime.

Srila Prabhupada responded.”Yes, they study alcoholism, and after studying the whole day, they say, "now we have finished, let’s go have a drink”.

I could see that he was not very inspired by Universities, by modern education, and what they were teaching. He always emphasized, “For intelligent men, controlling the senses is the most essential qualification. It is the basis of morality... A person cannot be a teacher if he drinks or smokes. In the modern system of education the teacher’s academic qualification is taken into consideration without evaluation of his moral life. Therefore, the result of education is misuse of high intelligence in so many ways” (Srimad-Bhagavatam, 1.9.26, purport).

And we experience in our daily life that example is better than precept. Srila Prabhupada did not want to make us blind followers or hypocrites. If we commit to something, we must follow it strictly. As Srila Prabhupada in his letter to Aniruddha (Los Angeles, February 4, 1969) said, “Our exemplary character depends on strictly following the four regulative principles,(no illicit sex, no intoxication, no eating of meat, fish or eggs, and no gambling) in this way we will conquer the whole world. Our movement is not only for some theoretical teaching, but it is for developing practical character and definite understanding”.

## May 1969, Columbus Ohio

**It was the best kirtan I had ever been in**

All of us at the Buffalo temple were excited as we heard that Srila Prabhupada was booked to have a program at the Ohio State University, and Allen Ginsberg was going to be in attendance. We had met Allen in Buffalo, where he had joined us in a kirtan at the university. The youth especially liked Allen for his outspoken anti-establishment views. This was during the hippy movement’s time, and Allen was like the original hippy.

With great enthusiasm, we drove from Buffalo to attend the program, and we were not the least bit disappointed as it turned out to be even more ecstatic than we had anticipated. The program took place in a large auditorium on campus with the devotees on stage. At center stage sat Srila Prabhupada with Allen on his left.

Mr. Ginsberg began the kirtan playing the harmonium and singing his famous tune, which I mentioned earlier, where everyone chants together. He chanted for a long time and finally brought it to an inspiring crescendo. Then Srila Prabhupada gave a class that everyone appreciated. Next, he led a kirtan. It was the best kirtan I had ever been in. Everyone was dancing, even those on the balcony. It went on and on and on. Then Srila Prabhupada finally finished it and said the Jaya оm prayers. The students were so enlivened by it that many came up on stage and started chanting, “love, peace, happiness”, over and over again.

## May-June 1969, New Vrindavan

## Valuable advice

After my initiation and this program, I became more inspired and decided to fully surrender to assist Srila Prabhupada in pushing on his movement. A month after my initiation, I graduated from the university. I decided to visit Srila Prabhupada, who was in New Vrindavan then, to let him know my desire to be a full-time devotee. As I walked the dirt road in New Vrindavan (it was impossible to drive a car on it), I met my friend and God-brother, Bhurijana Prabhu. He was leaving and gave some valuable advice. He said Srila Prabhupada was sitting alone in the field and that I should go right to him and loudly recite his pranama mantra while bowing down at his feet.

I did that, and when I looked up, I saw Srila Prabhupada smiling happily. Bhurijana was right. Srila Prabhupada was pleased. He then started talking to me. He asked me where I had come from. I responded that I had come from New York City. Then we conversed amiably about how things were going at the temple there. At one point, I said, “Srila Prabhupada, sometimes the people in New York can be very rude”. I recollect how when we approached people, asking them to look at our literature, they often responded in a bad-humoured way. When I said this, Srila Prabhupada replied incredulously, “You’re telling me?”

When Srila Prabhupada came to America in 1965, he was practically penniless. He struggled for almost one year alone, during which time he was subjected to a bitter New York winter, theft of his meager belongings, and abuse from a drug-crazed roommate. He was well acquainted with the people of New York.

## He was just like a living fountain

When I talked to Srila Prabhupada, he was sitting under a tree. There is a photo of him sitting there. Devotees would come to him, and he would talk to them. Normally after some time you would get tired of sitting because your legs would hurt, and then you would stand and go.

I remember sitting with Srila Prabhupada for some time and then going to do some service in the garden. At one point, I turned back and, to my surprise, I saw Srila Prabhupada was still sitting there under the tree talking to the devotees. It went on for hours and hours. He was just like a living fountain. He was constantly giving. Everybody was coming, and he was giving more and more. That was Srila Prabhupada’s demeanor. He always gave us so much; over the years, we increasingly appreciate it.

However, some things we took for granted. Thus, it would require Srila Prabhupada’s leaving us to see how valuable his presence was. There was never any anxiety in a political or philosophical sense when he was with us. Even if we had some differences, we knew that next time we would inquire from Srila Prabhupada, which would resolve the differences. Everybody had faith in him. Therefore, there was hardly any controversy—if there was, it would be only for a few days, and everything would be settled.

## Do you really believe that?

Srila Prabhupada asked me what I had been taught at the University. “We had been taught how the human race could improve itself and its future by open communication and cooperation. We study social problems like racial prejudice, alcoholism, and others to try to find solutions for them”. The idea was that if all humans worked together, we could create a wonderful atmosphere.

Srila Prabhupada responded incredulously, “You really believe that? No, they can’t create any lasting good. They can only destroy things. They can make a bomb, but they cannot make this world a better place”. And it was true: how has the situation gotten better? Over the last sixty-five years, the crime rate has not reduced despite so many intelligent and wealthy people in America trying to solve the problems. Rather problems like intoxication, abortion, slaughtering animals, illicit sex, and gambling have increased.

## Chapter 2. So close to my master (1969)

## On the way to London / June 1969, New Vrindavan

I told Srila Prabhupada that I would like to open a center in San Diego, California. I had visited that city and knew that there were many spiritual seekers there. At that time, Srila Prabhupada responded by saying, “Yes, we need many temples”. However, a few days later, he called me and said he had just gotten a letter from the householder couples he had sent to London. They had finally found a suitable place for a temple and requested that Srila Prabhupada send a brahmacari to help them train the new boys who wanted to join. Srila Prabhupada wanted me to go. So, of course, 1 agreed, and the next day I was on my way to New York to raise money for my flight to London by going on the street to distribute incense and Back to Godhead magazines.

## It is a difficult job because this is Maya's kingdom - July 1969

I arrived in London in July 1969. The temple was in a good location near the city center. Three brahmacaris were living there; the grihasthas had their own apartments but would come daily to the temple.

Shyamasundar Prabhu had connected with the famous Beatles, and the devotees were using their studio, Apple Records, to make a recording. I was also asked to participate in this endeavour and played kartals for one of the songs.

We went out daily on Tottencourt Road, the main shopping street, chanting and selling our Back to Godhead magazine. It was a good experience for us, and many people appreciated our chanting; a few even became devotees.

Srila Prabhupada arrived on September 11th. At that time, we were living at John Lennon’s estate in Tittenhurst Park. It was a lovely big estate with many trees collected from all over the British Empire.

I was not there at the airport for Srila Prabhupada’s reception, but the next morning all the devotees met in Srila Prabhupada’s room. Janaki Devi Dasi, the wife of Mukunda Prabhu, the temple president, spoke first. She welcomed Srila Prabhupada with an emotional plea, “Srila Prabhupada, you are so wonderful; you want to save the whole world!” I was a little shocked that she spoke with so much emotion, but Srila Prabhupada responded in a sober voice, “Yes. But it is a difficult job because this is Maya’s kingdom”.

## September-December 1969

## Just read Bhagavad-gita

I was a senior brahmacari there, and by my great good fortune, I was assigned to give Srila Prabhupada his daily massage. He had a massage on the recommendation of his doctors, who told him after a heart stroke that massage was good for his circulation. The morning after his arrival, I was told to be in Srila Prabhupada’s room at 11:00 a.m.

After I entered his room, he sat on a cloth in the middle of the room and told me what to do. The massage started with his head, where sandalwood oil was used. He poured the proper amount into my hands, and I began this most transcendental service. After a few minutes, he indicated that I should move on to his body. For his body, he preferred mustard seed oil. I started on his back and shoulders. Then to his lower back, where he had me spend more time and wanted a vigorous massage. Then to his arms, and lastly, his legs, finishing with his lotus feet. Srila Prabhupada’s daily massage took anywhere from forty-five minutes to one hour.

I had never given anyone a massage before, so after I finished, I said, “I would like to go to the library today to get a book on how best to give a massage”. He replied by saying it was unnecessary but, “If you read Bhagavad-gita, Krishna will direct you on how to do it.” He seemed satisfied with my humble attempt, and I left his room feeling ecstatic.

Some days later, I came to Srila Prabhupada’s room and thought, “Today I am going to give a really good massage”. I was really enthusiastic about doing it. Srila Prabhupada was sitting on the floor, and I started massaging him. When I finished, I felt in my heart that I had done a good job, but unexpectedly Srila Prabhupada also reciprocated with me. He acknowledged that my massage had been good and patted me gently on my head. I felt very inspired. I was happy to be his dog.

## Get rid of Mr. Lust

The next morning I again had the unfathomable good fortune of vigorously massaging the body of Srila Prabhupada, a pure devotee. After a few days, I realized 1 was becoming purified by this service. I saw practically that his body was absorbing all the dirty things from my heart, and by his causeless mercy, I was becoming free from my greatest enemy, lust.

Everyone is troubled by lust. Bhagauad-gita 3.37 confirms that kama esa krodha esa rajoguna samudbhavah: lust is the great sinful enemy that burns like fire. It is a cruel master, and it is never satisfied. It was wonderful to get free from it. Of course, it was not as easy as that. The lust would and did return, but I had a glimpse of how life could be so happy and simple without the degrading influence of Mr. Lust.

In this relation, I remember once, while massaging Srila Prabhupada, I said to him, “Srila Prabhupada, if I didn’t massage you, if I didn’t join this movement, I might be fighting with other men or trying to seduce women.” That was true. Such close association with Srila Prabhupada was very purifying for me.

## When he is pleased, then we are successful

When the devotees went to South America for the first time, one day, I was alone with Srila Prabhupada in the room, and he told me about that. So, I, being a material-minded person, said to him: “Srila Prabhupada, were they successful?” But he did not like this question. And the reason was that he was pleased that they went there, which is why they were already successful. Because ultimately, our success is that the spiritual master and Krishna are pleased.

## You will never leave if you understand the philosophy

Once again, while massaging Srila Prabhupada, he asked me: “What are the three main energies of Krishna?” Luckily I knew the answer.

The Lord Himself, His particles, the living entities, and material energy.

My point being is that Srila Prabhupada wanted us to know this philosophy. He took so much trouble to write all these books so that we would become conversant in spiritual subjects.

Another time I was in his room when he was asking devotees to stand up and speak something about their knowledge of Krishna consciousness. Somebody stood up and spoke for a few minutes, and Srila Prabhupada said: “Very good.” Then he asked someone else to speak. Therefore, he was testing whether we really Understood the philosophy or it simply went in one ear and out the other.

Thus, if we have this knowledge, we will not leave Krishna consciousness. Sometimes a devotee left, and Srila Prabhupada said: “They never understood the philosophy.” So we need to understand the philosophy, and then we will never leave because we know that there is nothing outside of Krishna. So, where are we going to go?

## Now you will make rapid advancement.

A short time later, I received my second initiation in the hall, which we used as a temple on John Lennon’s estate. After the fire sacrifice, I was invited to come to Srila Prabhupada’s room. He showed me how to chant on my sacred thread and count with my fingers. When I saw Srila Prabhupada the next day, he asked me if I had chanted the gayatri mantra that morning, and when I said I had, he remarked, “Now you will make rapid advancement”.

## What is the use of all his money if he doesn't know how to spend it?

Srila Prabhupada often talked about his childhood when we stayed in Tittenhurst Park on John Lennon’s big estate. I believe some memories about the British in India were evoked there, and he would talk about Calcutta, where he grew up.

Once, 1 remember we looked out, and there was one driver John Lennon had. He was a big man also named John. All he did was drive. Srila Prabhupada enquired about him, and I replied, “Yes. Driving is the only thing he does”. Srila Prabhupada said, “That is the sign of a rich man. He can keep a servant who is always ready to serve, but who might only work a few minutes per day.”

At that moment, I got a little enthusiastic about John’s wealth, and I said, “Yes, Srila Prabhupada. He has so much money”. When I said that, Srila Prabhupada looked at me with mild disgust and said, “What is the use of all his money if he doesn’t know how to spend it?”

And that made sense to me because at that same time, John was excavating a big area to make a lake, and for that, he spent a lot of money. However, a while later, John changed his mind. He decided not to have a lake, but his money had already been wasted. Later on, he was assassinated. Therefore, although he had a really big estate, so many millions of dollars, what was their use if he did not know how to spend them properly? That was a good practiced lesson.

For that reason, we should not envy these rich people. It does not help them. Of course, ‘spending’ means spending to glorify Krishna. In this relation, I remember that once Srila Prabhupada, during a conversation on July 31, 1975, said, “You cannot take that money within the tomb. That is not possible. Then it is zero. You are going empty-handed. You came empty-handed to this material world and went empty-handed. You came with zero, and you are going away with zero. So whatever you have earned, that is zero. But if you have attempted to serve Krishna with all these zeros, then you have taken some value”.

## By Krishna's arrangement

We were staying in John Lennon’s estate, and John and his wife Yoko invited Srila Prabhupada to come over to the main house for a visit. Later, we learned from Purusottama Prabhu that John wanted to have Srila Prabhupada hear the new record that had just been recorded. But by Krishna’s arrangement, the recording machine didn’t work, so Srila Prabhupada had his servant play a recording of Srila Prabhupada singing some bhajans.

During this Srila Prabhupada’s visit, Dhananjaya Prabhu and I were outside the house, looking through the windows while hiding in some bushes. After a few minutes of playing bhajans, we saw Srila Prabhupada say, “I have to go back now. I have some translation work to do”. Then he left the room, and as he came around the building, we jumped out of the bushes and paid our obeisances to Srila Prabhupada. He rubbed us on our backs as we were paying obeisances and then, with a big smile, said, “You saw?”

## I also have my Srila Prabhupada.

The temple on Bury Place was near completion, and we were preparing to move from John Lennon’s estate.

As Srila Prabhupada’s room at the new temple was not quite ready, an apartment not too far from the temple was rented for his use. I drove Srila Prabhupada to that apartment, and when we entered, we saw some ladies busily making last-minute arrangements.

Srila Prabhupada sat on a chair, picked up a tanpura (stringed instrument), and started singing. I remember he still had his winter coat on. I, of course, took the opportunity to sit at his feet. His eyes were closed, and he sang with feelings of pure devotion. At one point, he opened his eyes, saw me sitting there, and asked me, “Oh, you like?” I replied, “Yes, Srila Prabhupada!”

Then the ladies asked Srila Prabhupada where he wanted the lamp to be placed on his desk. He replied, “There behind the picture of Srila Prabhupada”. When he said that, we all looked at him with amazement because we had never heard Srila Prabhupada refer to his spiritual master as “Srila Prabhupada”. He noted our amazement and exclaimed, “Yes, I have my Srila Prabhupada too!” That was a nice memory of how Srila Prabhupada appreciated his spiritual master.

## He obliged me to dance.

Once, we were doing a program with Srila Prabhupada at the house of an Indian gentleman in London in 1969. Srila Prabhupada was sitting in the kirtan, and someone else was leading. So when the kirtan started to develop, everyone stood up and began dancing. Srila Prabhupada remained seated, and I was next to him. I did not stand up with the others. He looked at me at one point and, with a gesture, obliged me to get up and dance. I remember when he was dancing, it was ecstatic.

It also reminds me that when Lord Caitanya was in Jagannatha Puri, they had kirtan for four hours every evening. And it was so joyful with a lot of dancing and sometimes even rolling on the ground. Of course, we cannot imitate that; we can just appreciate it. And following Their example, we set our own goals: to come to the point when our kirtan will be so ecstatic that our hair will stand on end, tears will shoot from the eyes, and our body will tremble. And that is all possible to achieve one day because the kirtan of Caitanya Mahaprabhu has so much potency.

## You can remember me remembering Krishna.

We were in Srila Prabhupada’s room. One devotee named Isana also came in. Srila Prabhupada asked, “So, Isana, are you remembering Krishna?” He replied somewhat hesitantly: “Well... Srila Prabhupada...”. Then Srila Prabhupada spoke. “That’s alright... you can remember me remembering Krishna”.

That was Srila Prabhupada’s position. Remembering him was as good as remembering Krishna, and it will be easier for us. He gave us this wonderful ISKCON society, and we may think about how we can work all together to assist Srila Prabhupada to make it stronger and bigger.

If we serve Srila Prabhupada’s instruction to chant the holy names of Krishna, then we can easily remember Srila Prabhupada. This kind of advice is given in Sri Gurvastakam to remember the guru’s lotus feet three times a day. In this way, we can be Srila Prabhupada conscious, and Krishna will be even more satisfied with us.

## As Srila Prabhupada desired

I was a harinam leader at that time. Yamuna was also there. I knew she was a good singer, so I asked her to come out with us one day. But she replied she could not because she was caring for the Deities. When Sriia Prabhupada came, I complained to him, saying, “Srila Prabhupada, why can’t she come out with us on harinam?” But he defended her. He said, “No, she does not have to go out”.

So she never went out with us on harinam, which we sometimes did 2-3 times a day. Especially in the summer in London, we went out three times a day. We usually started at 10:30 a.m., then we returned for lunch at 2 p.m., took some little rest, and then went out again. In the afternoon, we returned for a short time once more to take some prasadam. On our third harinam, we stayed out late on the streets because crowds in the summer were huge in London. But we did not do that all year round, mainly in summer.

In retrospect, I can understand that Srila Prabhupada wanted to protect Yamuna. She had a husband, Gurudas Prabhu, and the temple president, Mukunda Prabhu, and Srila Prabhupada himself were there. Obliging her to accept me, another male authority may have been too much.

## Prabhupada open to adjustments

I remember another story about the Yamuna, but I heard it second-hand. When Srila Prabhupada was giving class while on tour in India, Yamuna would always sit close to his feet. One devotee, Acyutananda Swami, complained. He asked her, “Why are you sitting so close to Srila Prabhupada? It does not look good. Especially in India, women should not be close to a sannyas?

She took his instruction to heart and sat further away. But later, Srila Prabhupada asked her, “Why didn’t you sit near me?” Then she said, “Because Acyutananda Maharaja said it is not good that a woman sits so close to a sannyasi." Srila Prabhupada said, “Oh, OK.” He accepted it, and she never sat close to him again.

## He was always concerned about the health of the devotees.

One morning, when I came to do my service, Srila Prabhupada asked me if my health was alright. I said I had a little problem. I had a toothache, and Srila Prabhupada said, “Oh, what are you doing about it?” He was obviously concerned. My reply was, “Actually, I am going to the dentist this afternoon.”

The next morning, I came to massage Srila Prabhupada in his room upstairs. Usually, I would call up “Haribol,” and he would say, “Come on.” As I started to go upstairs, I heard, “So, how is your tooth?” It made me feel bad that I was obliging Srila Prabhupada, the person doing the most important work in the world, to worry about my tooth.

Along this same line, Srila Prabhupada asked me if I was getting enough prasadam. I responded a little equivocally saying something like, “Most of the time”. After that, I heard from the temple authorities that Srila Prabhupada had instructed them to cook something at night for all the devotees.

That is the way Srila Prabhupada was. He was always concerned about the health of devotees, about the individuals on that level. And I remember another time in Tokyo (I am jumping ahead a little bit), Shyamsundar Prabhu, who was travelling with Srila Prabhupada, became ill. We did not see him for a few days, and when he returned, he had mentioned to Srila Prabhupada that no one had taken care of him properly while he was sick. Srila Prabhupada immediately called a meeting of all the devotees there.

We had an ista-gosthi and Srila Prabhupada was saying, “Why is this? Why didn’t you take care of him? We have given up everything to help push Lord Caitanya’s movement. We had left our families for this preaching. We have come to this side, and how is that? We should look after each other; we should become a family”. Then he turned to Shyamsundar Prabhu and said with a voice full of genuine feeling, “Why didn’t you call me? I would have helped you”.

We were all embarrassed that we had not been more sympathetic to our Godbrother’s condition. And that was Srila Prabhupada’s mood: he was very compassionate and, at the same time, very bold and very strong if necessary. But we always got the feeling he loved us and was not exploiting us in any way.

## If I had a chance to do this again

Once while massaging Srila Prabhupada, I asked him to tell me a story about his spiritual master. He said, “What is there to say? He was a Vaikuntha man.” Another time also, while massaging him, I remembered my mother bathing me as a child. I related that memory to Srila Prabhupada, and he said, “Yes. This is mother’s work that you are doing”.

Another day when I was nearing the end of his massage, working on his legs towards his feet. I was kneeling in front of him, and he was sitting with his legs in front, and his eyes were closed. Suddenly I had an impulse and took his two lotus feet and placed them on my chest. He opened his eyes and saw what I was doing, and his first reaction was one of shyness. He, of course, understood that my action was one of submission to him, but he was initially hesitant to accept my gesture because he was naturally humble.

But as I was looking at his eyes, I saw them change from shyness to willingness to accept my submission. Then as I watched, his eyes grew brighter and brighter, so bright that I could no longer look at them and had to turn my gaze away. If I had a chance to do this again, I would have offered prayers to glorify him instead of turning away.

## One can be Krishna conscious from any position.

One day Srila Prabhupada asked me, “So you do not like our ladies?” He was alluding to the fact that I was not married. I responded, “No, Srila Prabhupada, I like them too much. Therefore, I try to avoid them”. He smiled nicely.

At that time, I was twenty-seven years old and had been with women previously. In my mind, I was not sure if I should get married or not. When I asked Srila Prabhupada about it, he was non-committal. He said, “It does not matter. You can be Krishna conscious from any position”.

## Now I have so many children and without the botheration of a wife.

A few days later, he gave a class where he talked about himself. He mentioned how his spiritual master came to him in a dream and told him to take sannyasa and go out to preach. He was thinking, “How could I do that? I have my children and wife”, so he didn’t immediately take this instruction.

However, sometime later, his spiritual master again came to him in his dream with the same instruction. This time he took it very seriously, left his home in the middle of the night, went to Vrindavan, and took sannyasa from his God-brother Kesava Maharaja. Then he gestured to us in the class and said, “Now I have so many children and without the botheration of a wife”.

The following morning, when I went for my service, I reminded Srila Prabhupada of how he had said in the class that a wife was a “botheration.” Srila Prabhupada smiled broadly and responded, “I said that?”

## Srila Prabhupada correcting me

When Srila Prabhupada was staying at a hotel in London, we came with other devotees to accompany him on his morning walk. We all left his room and started for the elevator when I realized I had left my beads in his room. I quickly got the key from his servant, got my beads, and raced to the elevator in time to join them there.

Once in the elevator, Srila Prabhupada said to me in a voice; one would use when talking to a small child. He spoke very slowly, asking me, “Did you lock the door?”. All the devotees started smiling, but I could understand that Prabhupada was dealing with me like a child because I had made a mistake and accepted his way of correcting me. I responded, “Yes, Srila Prabhupada.”

## He was a swift walker.

We went down onto the street where we had to cross a busy road. I thought it might be dangerous for Srila Prabhupada to go across.

I looked to see if the traffic was clear and then looked back to where Srila Prabhupada was standing. I saw that he had already crossed the road.

## Dead silence

I have already mentioned that Srila Prabhupada sent me to London in the summer of 1969. First of all, I went to New York City to collect the fare for the flight. To do that, I wore Vaisnava clothes and distributed incense sticks and Back to Godhead magazines on the street. So, once I was approached by someone who Srila Prabhupada had initiated in 1966. I forgot his name: Rishikesh Ananda or something like that. He came over to talk to me, and I noticed a girl with him, but she did not come close.

He said, “Yeah, I got initiated by the Swami. But now I am going to California. It will be good for my head”. I remember thinking, “I do not have to worry about what is good for my head.”. I just have to do what Srila Prabhupada wants, and, together with my faith, everything will be fine for “my head.”. But this person was still searching for his own way.

Later in London, when I was massaging Srila Prabhupada, I related his story of how I had met one of his early initiated disciples. However, Srila Prabhupada’s response was dead silence.

Thinking it over, I can now see Srila Prabhupada’s position. Why should he have to justify to me about someone who left the movement? As I understood, he did not want to think, “Oh, I should have done this or that with the person concerned.” Srila Prabhupada was always perfect. He was merciful to everyone. If someone made a wrong choice, he was not responsible.

## Just by appreciating, they will make spiritual advancement.

Before long, Srila Prabhupada moved to his room on the second floor (British first floor) of our Bury Place temple. Other devotees were sometimes engaged in massaging Srila Prabhupada when I was assigned to lead the chanting party up Oxford Street. Still, I did have my massage service sometimes.

Once while I was massaging him, he asked me how the public was responding to our chanting on the streets. I answered, “Many people seem to appreciate what we are doing.” Srila Prabhupada liked that and said, “Just by appreciating, they will make spiritual advancement.”

## Krishna also has His form.

After having finished a massage on another day in the company of a few devotees, Srila Prabhupada reached over, held me by the shoulders for a second, and said, “Just like our Trivikrama has his form, so Krishna also has a form”.

I was really happy to be touched by him! But I do think it is very important to understand that Krishna has His own form. We should not be impersonalists. He is a person; we can meet Him and see Him face-to-face when we have fully purified our hearts.

Srila Prabhupada was always encouraging us to finish our business in this lifetime. But devotional service is not a mechanical process. We need to love God more than ourselves. In the material world, everyone loves themself the most. We need to change this materialistic mentality, but that requires faith.

From experience, we know there are so many cheaters in this world: if we love someone too much, then that person may take advantage of us. And usually keep this mentality when we approach Krishna or a pure devotee. Because of envying Them, we will not be able to get Their complete mercy. If we have a little envy, then Krishna will not reciprocate fully.

Someone may say, “Oh, but I am not envious.” But envy is very subtle. Krishna is a person who has all good qualities, namely: all beauty, all fame, all strength, all knowledge, all renunciation, and all wealth. As long as we desire to imitate Him, to usurp His position, Krishna will not reveal Himself to us.

## Do not eat more than required.

Srila Prabhupada once told us, “For a young man to overeat is not so serious but for an old man to overeat is very dangerous.” This is how Srila Prabhupada lived: he did not simply talk; he guided us by his own example of accepting everything favourable for spiritual life and rejecting everything unfavourable.

## Here is Krishna

Once I brought Srila Prabhupada’s shoes to his room to help him to go out, but he chastised me immediately. “Don’t you see? Here is Krishna,” said Srila Prabhupada, pointing to his small Deities of Radha-Krishna, Who were in his room then.

And it was true. I did not think about Them at that moment. It was a good lesson: we must always be attentive to everything we do in devotional service, even small details. On the other hand, I learned that no matter what, whenever the Deities are in a private room or temple, we should always treat Them with greatest respect.

## He wanted our loyalty.

I sometimes thought that Srila Prabhupada might not allow me to travel with him when he left London to return to the USA. I remembered that in 1968 in Montreal, I had met Hamsadutta Prabhu and liked his kirtans. I had heard that he had recently become the president at the Berkeley temple in California. I mentioned to Srila Prabhupada, “If

I can’t go back with you to the USA, can I go to Berkeley to help Hamsadutta”?

I was alone with Srila Prabhupada giving a massage. As soon as I said this, he became angry. He spoke firmly. “You are a brahmacari. It is not your duty to contemplate what is your service!”

## I am just a humble servant.

The first public program in London was well attended. About 300 people came—mostly Hindus. Srila Prabhupada spoke with full authority and conviction. Some of the audience did not like this, and one man, in particular, stood up and interrupted the class, presenting his own ideas and making objections to Prabhupada’s points.

He said loudly, “And what about Lord Siva?” But Srila Prabhupada would not tolerate this and told the man with a strong voice, “Sit down. You are nonsense!” We had never heard Srila Prabhupada speak so boldly; he was like a ferocious lion at that moment. He was humble on his account, but when representing Krishna, he was very bold. It was a series of lectures and Srila Prabhupada was to speak again the next day. In the morning I was massaging in the mood of a trainer getting his fighter ready for another knockout performance. I was talking vividly and massaging. I was glorifying and flattering him. My attitude was, “You and I, Srila Prabhupada, we can take over the world.” I did not say it, but that was my mood.

Suddenly Srila Prabhupada broke his silence with an emphatic “No!” My hands froze on his body. Then he spoke again from deep in his heart with an innocent child-like voice, “I am just a humble servant,” he said. Embarrassment came over me, and my first thought was that I should not be allowed to be in the same room with him, what to speak of being allowed to touch his body.

He was such an aristocrat, and I was just an ordinary, low-class person from the streets. There was a throw rug there, and I thought I should go under it to hide. Srila Prabhupada was the perfect Acarya. By being so genuinely humble, he obliged me also to react humbly.

Although we treated him as good as God, he saw himself as a servant of his spiritual master and Krishna. There was nothing artificial in his humility; it came directly from his realized heart. That is why Guru is called “heavy”: he cannot be moved because he is grounded in his understanding of himself — he always thinks he is the servant of his spiritual master and thus can’t be moved from his position. Everybody else is in danger because their position is constantly shifting.

Therefore, Srila Prabhupada was not impressed by any flattery. He was freely offering us the process that had enabled so many in the past to achieve full realization; all we had to do was accept it.

## When you are selling diamonds, you don't expect many customers.

There was another public program in London at Conway Hall. The attendance was not very big, but the program was nice, and Srila Prabhupada gave a wonderful lecture. Then during the question and answers section, one attendee asked, “If this is the absolute truth that you are speaking, then why are not more people here?” Srila Prabhupada responded, “When you are selling diamonds, you do not expect many customers”.

And that is true: this knowledge is a real jewel, but unfortunately, people cannot appreciate it. Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Saraswati also said, “Do not lose your spirit if you see that the majority of people cannot accept the principle of selfless service to the Supreme Lord.” Thus, even if many are going away, we need to appreciate those who remain practising devotees, taking this process seriously and following it faithfully.

## Vaisnava means humble

There was a fight between two devotees, and one was pushed down a few stairs to the landing. I was going to give Srila Prabhupada a massage when I heard about it. As soon as I entered Srila Prabhupada’s room, he commented on it. I was surprised he had heard about it so quickly.

He said, “You have heard about this fighting? This fighting is not at all Vaisnava-like. Vaisnava means humble, but this fighting is just the opposite: 'I will get my way at any cost”. Then he looked directly into my eyes and said, “I do not want you to ever fight with your God-brothers. Is that clear?”. I replied, “Yes, Srila Prabhupada.” He knew my nature was a little confrontational, so it was a good warning that helped me in the future when the temptation to fight arose.

## I thought you were pacifists.

We were coming back from Harinam in London in 1969. We were wearing dhotis, and that was broadcasting who we were. I remember somehow I was alone on my way to the underground.

At the top of the stairs was one man selling newspapers; upon seeing me, he said something insulting about Krishna. I immediately got angry and said, “You better shut up; otherwise, I'll throw you down the stairs.” He did not expect an answer of that kind and, surprised, said, “I thought you were pacifists?” I replied, “Well, you thought wrong.”

Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu taught us to be humble and tolerate insults others offered. However, we should not allow any offenses against Krishna or His pure devotees; otherwise, it will jeopardize our spiritual progress.

## This chanting is the whole essence of our philosophy.

We would go every day to give a massage at Srila Prabhupada’s apartment, and I remember once I chanted while waiting for Srila Prabhupada to finish his correspondence. Suddenly, he looked up and said, “You listen when you chant Hare Krishna?” My response was immediately defensive; I thought, “Of course, I listen; otherwise, why am I doing it?” But he read my mind. Before I could speak, he said, “I know you listen, but you have to listen very carefully. This chanting is the whole essence of our philosophy”.

Over the years, I have seen that this is true. We need to be very serious about chanting our rounds. We need to dedicate quality time to it, not while driving the car or doing something else. These inattentive chantings will not bring us to the desired goal very quickly. Srila Prabhupada made that clear.

If we are serious, we need to wake up early in the morning, take a shower, and then chant Hare Krishna. Early morning is the best time. If we do it regularly and with determination, Krishna will see that we really want to know Him, and then He will reveal Himself more and more.

## You can also be a representative of Krishna.

Srila Prabhupada was giving class every evening on the Bhagavad-gita. During one class, he spoke about how the spiritual master was the representative of Krishna. When he said it with such conviction, I had a mystic experience wherein I could see from a higher dimension that Srila Prabhupada was the genuine representative of Krishna.

It was such an exhilarating experience that it was on my mind when I came the following morning into his room for my service. I said, “Srila Prabhupada, I very much liked your class last night”. He responded, “Oh, what part did you like?” I said, “The part where you said you were the representative of Krishna.” Srila Prabhupada liked that. He accepted it fully and then turned it back to me by saying, “You can also be a representative of Krishna. When you say, ‘Srila Prabhupada said, or Krishna said.’ Then you are also representing Krishna”.

What struck me the most about this incident was that Srila Prabhupada always reciprocated—he always gave us more than we gave him. He was constantly encouraging us. He did not advertise himself. We never got the impression that his level of Krishna consciousness was only available to him and that no one else could achieve it.

He was the leader, guru, and the Acarya. He was sincerely carrying out the orders of his spiritual master as his life and soul. But this principle is open to all. Everyone can be a representative of Krishna. The most important thing is to avoid altering the original meaning of Krishna’s message: we need to be honest and keep it as it is.

## Srila Prabhupada personally saved Them.

On December 14, Their Lordships Sri Sri Radha-London Isvara were installed at the Bury Place temple. Srila Prabhupada was personally involved in the ceremony. The preliminaries took place on the altar behind a drawn curtain. I happened to be upstairs when a devotee ran in and told me that Tamal Krishna Prabhu wanted to see me right away on the altar. Tamal had arrived recently and had been given the position of temple president.

I rushed downstairs, went to the altar curtain, and opened it slightly to see what was happening. As soon as I looked in, I was met by Srila Prabhupada’s stern gaze. I immediately closed the curtain. Later on, I heard what had happened, how Srila Prabhupada had to personally jump up and save the Deities from having the altar fall on Them.

## He truly loves me

Srila Prabhupada’s personal servant, Purusottama Prabhu, approached me and said, “Stop bothering Srila Prabhupada about going to America to be his masseur. Srila Prabhupada has already given permission for Upendra Prabhu to do this service when we arrive there”.

Later that day, I was driving Srila Prabhupada, and we were alone in the car. I told him, “Purusottama has told me Upendra is going to be the person giving you massage when you reach America.” Srila Prabhupada was silent. The silence continued for a while, and I realized it must be true.

Then I felt a little uncomfortable with the silence and decided to break it by changing the topic, at the same time, communicating to him that I was resigned to not going with him. I did not want to bother him anymore. So I changed the subject by saying “Srila Prabhupada, is Upendra a nice boy? I have never met him”.

He was looking straight ahead, but when I said this, he turned to me and said in a voice full of feelings, “Not as nice as you!” He had perfectly read my mind, and I was surprised and crushed in a good way. His words enter straight into my heart. I thought, “Srila Prabhupada truly loves me!”

And it was true. He saw I had sacrificed my desire to be with him. He appreciated it and immediately reciprocated. That was his incredible qualification, he made us all feel he really cared for us, and it was not artificial. He did love us. It was very inspiring to receive that love. When we became convinced that Srila Prabhupada loved us, we did not mind when he chastised us.

## You are such a good boy.

Srila Prabhupada was due to leave the next day, and, as I was giving him a massage, I spoke out, “Srila Prabhupada, we are going to miss you so much when you leave.” The massage was meant to be as a time when Srila Prabhupada was to relax fully, but my comment roused him to respond seriously. He said, “No! Mukunda is here and Shyamasundara and Gurudas”.

I wanted to bring him back to a relaxed mood, so I said, “Yes, Srila Prabhupada, but you are such a good boy; we will miss you.” My comment had the desired result. He relaxed with a slight smile as I continued to massage his transcendental body.

It is said that a pure devotee, as does Krishna, reciprocates according to the love expressed by his disciples. He would accept our words and feelings if he saw they were genuine.

## There will always be some mystery.

Around this time, one devotee used to ask many questions about different parts of the Bhagavatam. One question I remember him asking. The Krishna book describes how Krishna and Balarama jumped from the top of a mountain, which was a few miles high. He asked, “Where is this mountain now?” Srila Prabhupada answered, “How will knowing this help you develop your love for God?”

Anyway, this devotee kept asking questions like that every day. Then Srila Prabhupada asked him, “How many hours a day are you reading?” He proudly replied, “Two hours.” Srila Prabhupada then said, “Read one hour.”

What is the use of being so smart if you cannot understand that there is some mystery? How could Krishna lift Govardhana Hill? If someone does not have faith, then everything will be spoiled. Therefore, we emphasize kirtan. If we have nice kirtan, then questions are minimized. The mind becomes our friend after ecstatic kirtan. Nobody becomes very doubtful because realizations are included in the kirtan process.

On the other hand, if we see our faith being disturbed after reading some passages from the sacred scriptures, we should consider that this is our disqualification for not being able to grasp it. In that case, we need to get help from others, from guru and sadhus. Our goal is to increase our faith, not blindly but through logic and reason. However, we must accept that there will always be something acintya (inconceivable). Otherwise, we can never reach the spiritual world just through logic and reason.

Krishna sometimes gives us this test of faith to see if we are faithful and love Him. If we truly love Him, we will see the lack of understanding as our fault. But if we love our intelligence and way of thinking more, we will easily reject Krishna and take shelter in our intellect instead of surrendering to Him.

## She is the best devotee of Krishna.

We were in the car with Srila Prabhupada going to the London airport when he commented, “Radharani is the best devotee of Krishna.” After that, he paused for a minute and then added: “But mother Yashoda is also a very exalted devotee.”

## The dust of the lotus feet

It was December 1969. Srila Prabhupada was sitting in London’s airport, ready to leave England after staying there for three months. I was with him then, and at one moment, I saw that one of his lotus feet was exposed. I quickly bent and stole some dust from this lotus foot. Srila Prabhupada was somewhat surprised, but he was not displeased with me. He did not chastise me. Therefore, we have to be bold to get Krishna’s, or guru’s, mercy. We must be greedy to receive it whenever there is an opportunity to get some mercy; we need to go for it enthusiastically.

## Chapter 3. This is our process (1970-1971)

## Work as a matter of duty for Krishna - January 1970

I stayed in Europe from 1969-1970. Meanwhile, Srila Prabhupada went back to America. Tamal Krishna Prabhu was then the temple president in London; and he had some authority in Europe. He decided to send me to Hamburg, Germany, to assist in the preaching center there.Before long, he also arrived in Hamburg with his wife and was accompanied by Hamsadutta Prabhu and his wife, Himavati Devi Dasi. One of their first projects was to arrange for the brahmacaris to get married, saying that our lady devotees needed to be protected. They had a nice German girl called Heidi picked out for me, but I was not into it. I wrote to Srila Prabhupada, asking for his help in this regard. He answered:

**Los Angeles**  
**March 7th, 1970**  
**My Dear Trivikrama,**

**Please accept my blessings. I beg to acknowledge receipt of your letter dated 2 March, 1970. Now I think because Himavati is there, she will be first-class caretaker of the Deities, and you can be relieved. So I have no objection if you come back to the States if there is no other urgent necessity for you there.**

**So you can consult in this connection with Krishna Das and Hamsadutta, and with their consent, you can come back. But if there is any urgent necessity for your presence there, you may wait a little more and then come back. I know as you expressed your desire in London, that you are very much anxious to come back to the States; so you can do so at your earliest convenience.**

**I do not think that Hamsadutta is pressing you for marriage. Marriage is a concession for a person who cannot control his sex desires. Of course, it is a difficult job for the boys in this country because they have free access to intermingle with the girls. Under the circumstances, it is my open order for everyone that everyone can marry without any artificial pose. But if somebody is able to remain a Brahmacari, there should not be any canvassing for his marriage.**

**But at the mature age say after 50 years old age, everyone should separate from wife. Married life does not mean that one should continue to live with wife throughout the whole life; at a certain stage, say between 20-25 years, one may accept a wife, live with her to the maximum age of 50 years, and then there should be no more sex relationship—stringently. And at the mature old age, say 65-70 years, everyone must accept the renounced order of Sannyasa; if not in dress, then in action positively.**

**Our students, either Brahmacari or Householder, are being trained up for constant engagement in Krishna Consciousness service without any personal interest. This is the perfect order of Sannyasa. So if everyone is trained up in this line of action, all of us are Sannyasis in all circumstances. This is explained in the Bhagavadgita that anyone who is not after the result of any action, but simply he acts as a matter of duty for Krishna, he is a factual Sannyasi and Yogi.**

**So whatever status of life we may accept, this principle of working for Krishna as a matter of our duty, without being attracted by the result—should be followed by us.**

**Hope this will meet you in good health.**

**Your ever well-wisher, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami**

Srila Prabhupada ki jay! Soon I was on my way back to the USA. I did not have enough money for my ticket to America, but this girl, Heidi, helped me buy it.

## Not very serious about their spiritual life.

Leaving Germany, I stopped in London on my way back to the USA. Gurudas Prabhu was the temple president there, and he enlisted me in service in the temple for some time. I was happy to be of some value to him. Finally, in July 1970,I returned to the USA, arriving in Boston.

The devotees were happy to see me and enquired about my experiences with Srila Prabhupada in London and, of course, about the Beatles. I did have some association with George, John, and Yoko. But, frankly, after associating a good bit with Srila Prabhupada, I could see that the Beatles, although famous and wealthy, were not very serious about their spiritual life. George was undoubtedly the best, and he proved it by donating to the cost of printing the Krishna book. Later, he also donated the large Manor we have been using since that time. Bhakta George ki jay!

## Srila Prabhupada - jiva-tattva, not Visnu-tattva

From there, I went to New Vrindavan as we heard that Srila Prabhupada was coming for Janmashtami. But he changed his plans and instead flew to Japan to negotiate the printing of his books with our printer Dai Nipon. Before leaving for Japan, while he was still in Los Angeles, he initiated seven devotees into the sannyasa order of life. Four of those newly initiated sannyasis came to New Vrindavan. They were Subala Maharaja, Gargamuni Maharaja, Vishnujana Maharaja, and Brahmananda Maharaja. A devotee told me that Gargamuni Maharaja was telling devotees that Srila Prabhupada was God. Initially, I thought it was just an excess of his devotion to Srila Prabhupada, so I did not take it seriously.

But the next morning, the four sannyasis were speaking to all the assembled devotees instead of having Srimad-Bhagavatam class. We sat on the floor, but the four stood striding back and forth while holding their dandas. Alarm bells rang when I realized they were really serious about trying to convince us that Srila Prabhupada was, in fact, the Supreme Personality of Godhead Himself.

They gave some evidence from the Adi Purana, where Krishna states that the Acarya is My very Self. I raised my hand and asked them, “What about that little blue boy, Krishna?” I had given enough massages to Srila Prabhupada to know that Srila Prabhupada’s body was neither blue nor youthful like Krishna’s body. Brahamananda Maharaja responded, “How do you know about Him?” I said, “From Srila Prabhupada.”. He said, “See!” Then I was dismissed, and they continued addressing the assembly with this nonsense idea that Srila Prabhupada was God.

Many devotees accepted their philosophy. On the way out, while putting on my shoes, I heard the wife of a prominent temple president say, “Isn’t it wonderful that we now know that Srila Prabhupada is the speaker of the Bhagavadgita?” Another devotee said, “It will be interesting to see how people react to this when we preach it on sankirtan.” I was starting to get alarmed, but when I spoke with some leading devotees, like Rupanuga Prabhu and Hayagriva Prabhu, I learned they had not accepted it. I was relieved to hear this.

The next day the sannyasis left for our Detroit temple. In the meantime, when Srila Prabhupada was informed of the nature of their preaching, he ordered that these sannyasis should not be allowed to stay in any of his temples. They were instructed to travel and preach the authorized philosophy until they rectified themselves of this mayavadi misconception that the guru is God. They did that and, within a year, were welcomed back into ISKCON.

## Little?

After that incident in New Vrindavan, I spoke to him about it the next time I met Srila Prabhupada. I told him how I had asked these sannyasis about Krishna by saying, “What about that little blue boy?” When I said this to Srila Prabhupada, he responded by saying incredulously, “Little?” Srila Prabhupada knew that Krishna was not “Little.” He is bigger than the biggest.

Srila Prabhupada knew clearly that he was not God. But, as we sing daily saksad-dharitvena samasta-sastraih, because he was completely surrendered to God, we accept him as good as God. Therefore, if he blesses us, then Krishna will be happy to make the necessary arrangements to fulfill that blessing. Krishna plainly says, “One who says that he is My devotee, he is not My devotee, but one who says that he is a devotee of my devotee, he is My devotee.” (Adi Parana)

## Spoken like a true brahmacari

I stayed in New Vrindavan for six months under Kirtanananda Maharaja’s direction. Once he said I should get married. I replied, “Maharaja, you are a very powerful person, but you are not that powerful.” He responded by saying, “Spoken like a true brahmacari.” He never brought the issue up again.

After some time, he asked me to go to our Pittsburg temple and take up the service of acting Temple President. I did that for about six months and learned a lot. Then I was granted permission to be relieved of my position so I could join Srila Prabhupada, who was due to arrive at our Detroit temple in a few days.

## Ecstasy of personal association

Arriving in Detroit in early July 1971, I was immediately given my old service of being Srila Prabhupada’s chauffeur and masseur. I was behind the wheel in the car waiting for Srila Prabhupada when he came out of the airport terminal. The first thing he said after entering the car was to ask me to turn the AC as high as possible. It was a scorching summer day.

While driving back in the car, Srila Prabhupada remarked, “Having these cars and highways are certainly convenient, but along with these conveniences comes inconveniences. We are faced with so much air pollution and accidents”.

It was wonderful being personally with Srila Prabhupada again. Sometimes getting the remnants of prasadam from his plate and touching his transcendental body were two of the great good fortunes of having the opportunity of serving the vapu (the personal form) of a pure devotee.

## He was not embarrassed to be direct.

I remember some more details about that visit. As I said, I was deputed to drive Srila Prabhupada from the airport to the temple, and I reminded him a little about my experience with him in London in case he had forgotten. I was talking quite openly and then asked if I could give him a massage while he was here.

There were three GBC devotees in the back. They were quiet and Srila Prabhupada, saying just a few words, kept silent also. I was the only one talking. Then I asked him, “Oh, I heard, Srila Prabhupada, that you had been to Russia recently; what was it like there?”. Srila Prabhupada didn’t respond at all. He didn’t want to get into a conversation with me. He did not want to become like my buddy and start chatting with me like an equal. Everybody in the back chuckled, seeing how Srila Prabhupada was dealing with me. He was indicating to me, “Okay, cool down, keep calm and just let me talk as I see fit. Do not pump me with questions”. Therefore, although Srila Prabhupada was very humble, he could also be very straightforward. He was never afraid of being personal and getting right to the point. He was not embarrassed to be direct.

## This is our process

During Srila Prabhupada’s stay, a young brahmacari and his mother came to see him. The lady was about fifty years old and confidentially spoke to Srila Prabhupada, like one adult to smother. I heard this first hand as I was eavesdropping at Srila Prabhupada’s window.

She said, “You know, sir, that these young boys and girls... actually, they worship you!” She implied that something was wrong. But Srila Prabhupada immediately understood the point and answered in a non-challenging manner, “Yes,” he said, “That is our process. I am also worshipping my spiritual master”. In this way, the situation was clarified.

Ordinary people would think, “Oh, this is very unusual when a person, a human being, is being worshipped.” However, Srila Prabhupada made it seem like something natural. That this is “our process, I am also worshipping my spiritual master”, and they are worshipping me. It is not something extraordinary. It is our tradition. It is the parampara.

## Srila Prabhupada -temple authorities should be respected.

The next day during a morning walk, Srila Prabhupada made plans with some devotees to do something that day. When we finished the walk, he said, “Now let us get permission from the temple authorities for this plan.” I was surprised that Srila Prabhupada was asking for permission, but it was a learning experience. Srila Prabhupada wanted our ISKCON to be a society that had rules and discipline.

## Just see

There was a brass lota on Srila Prabhupada’s desk. He asked his servant to polish it. Before long, he returned it in sparkling condition. Srila Prabhupada appreciated it by saying, “Just see.”

Srila Prabhupada showed us that whatever service we were doing should be done very nicely. He was teaching us that pure devotional service means we should be fully, twenty-four hours, engaged in service to Krishna, and, whatever we do for Him, should be done with love and to the best of our abilities. Initially, it may be challenging, but this should be our goal.

## They are pink!

Soon Srila Prabhupada was off to our Brooklyn temple in New York. Some of us drove there to be with him. I was very grateful to the devotees there, who allowed me to serve Prabhupada again by giving him a massage.

While massaging Srila Prabhupada, I noticed the bottom of his feet were pink in colour. I mentioned this to him, and he immediately replied, “That is from your pink hands.” I objected, but Srila Prabhupada retorted by saying, “No, you are fair-skinned boys.” I was insisting and, to prove my point, I took one of Srila Prabhupada’s lotus feet and showed the bottom of it to Shyamasundara

Prabhu, who happened to be in the room. “See how pink they are,” I said. Srila Prabhupada smiled and seemed to enjoy this exchange.

Shyamasundara later told me he was amazed that I could turn the foot of Srila Prabhupada to show it and that he actually went along with me in a happy mood. But it is true: out of affection, you could deal with Srila Prabhupada in any way. He was controlled by affection. The next morning, just outside the temple room, Jadurani Devi Dasi quickly approached me, took one of my hands, and said, “They are pink!”

## A Friendly Smile

We were at the Brooklyn temple on Henry street in New York. Srila Prabhupada was heading out for a quick visit to Gainesville, Florida. He left early in the morning. Someone was driving him to the airport, and we were on the street to see him off as he left. He came back the same evening, and again we were there to receive him. After he got out of the car and walked to the temple, I approached him and said, “Srila Prabhupada, we missed you!” So, on the one hand, it was a joke because he was absent just for a few hours, but Srila Prabhupada recognized it and gave me a friendly smile.

## Anger - an offering in the service of Krishna

I entered Srila Prabhupada’s room when he was talking with the members of the art department. He was reassuring them not to be concerned about money. He was being very friendly and encouraging. In the meantime, Srila Prabhupada’s personal servant came to him from behind. Srila Prabhupada stopped talking and gave his full attention to his servant. Something had disturbed Srila Prabhupada, and he directed some anger at his servant.

The interruption took about 15-20 seconds. Then Srila Prabhupada turned back to the artists with a smile as if what he had said to his servant had never happened. Such a surprising reaction confirmed the words from sastra that the spiritual master uses everything in Krishna’s service, including anger. But he does not get carried away by it, though it may seem that way.

Lord Nrisimhadeva seemed to be carried away by anger. He could not stop roaring. Even Lakshmi Devi was afraid to approach her husband. However, Lord Brahma sent Prahlada to Nrsimhadeva, and He quickly became pacified.

I saw Srila Prabhupada getting angry a few times. “Annoyed” would probably be a better word, but also angry. However, the difference was that Srila Prabhupada was not under the control of it but used it fully in Krishna’s service. There was nothing he did not offer as a service to the Lord.

Another situation like that happened in Osaka, a big city south of Tokyo in Japan. One Indian man invited us to do a program there. He was a wealthy businessman, and devotees had asked him for a donation. Srila Prabhupada and all the devotees went there. But I did not go. I stayed back to take care of the temple.

Later on, Bhurijana Prabhu told me what happened in this program. This Indian man had invited his guru also to speak on the same stage with the devotees. First, the devotees did their program: they chanted, then Srila Prabhupada spoke. After that, this Indian guru started speaking. He spoke in Hindi, so our devotees did not know what he was saying, but Srila Prabhupada did.

After speaking for five or ten minutes, Srila Prabhupada spoke to his disciples in a commanding voice and said, “Stop him!” I remember Bhurijana saying he did not know whether he should kill him or what he should do? Then Srila Prabhupada said, “Have kirtan!" They all stood up and performed a loud kirtan for some time. In this way, his guru was obliged to stop talking. This is the system of fighting in the Kali-yuga. We don’t use weapons but the power of the Holy Name to curb the demoniac mentality.

When I saw this Indian man again a week later, he was upset that his guru had been interrupted. This man said something to the effect that, “A sadhu does not get angry, but I saw your guru getting angry. He is not a proper sadhu”. I did not get angry with him, but I explained, “It may be that you and your guru are both impersonalists who believe that a spiritual person does not have any feelings. But we think people do have feelings, and getting angry is justified sometimes. Just like Krishna encouraged Arjuna to fight in the battle of Kurushetra, to fight, one must be ready to display some degree of anger.”

Later, when Srila Prabhupada was asked about it, he said he wanted this person to stop talking because he was minimizing Krishna by saying Ganesh and all the different demigods were on the same level as Krishna. He was a mayavadi, and he was preaching that neither Krishna nor the demigods have an eternal form because everything is one. Srila Prabhupada could not tolerate it, so he stopped him.

When I was there in New York, there was a yogi for whom the temple authorities had gotten permission from Srila Prabhupada to allow him to live in the temple. The condition for his stay was he had to follow the four regulative principles and come every morning to mangala-arati. He stayed for 5-6 months and also came on harinam sometimes. A week or two before Srila Prabhupada was due to return to New York, he moved out of the temple.

I lived on the second floor, and Srila Prabhupada’s room was on the first floor. By chance, I looked out the window and saw this yogi coming into the temple. He was accompanied by a rather large woman who wore saffron.

I did not think much about it as I thought it natural that he would visit Srila Prabhupada.

About 10-15 minutes later, I was passing by Srila Prabhupada’s room, and I saw this yogi being carried out of Prabhupada’s room horizontally by 6-8 brahmacaris. I noticed there was fear in his eyes. I immediately went into Srila Prabhupada’s room. There was only this lady and one meek brahmacari. In a hysterical voice, she was calling out, “Don’t you touch me! Don’t you touch me!” I looked at Srila Prabhupada, sitting behind his desk, and he motioned with his eyes for me to get her out.

I went over to her, and with an angry voice, louder than hers, I told her to “Shut up!” My voice stunned her somewhat, and I was able to get her to the door without touching her. There was a devotee at the door who escorted her out. I immediately turned to Srila Prabhupada, who ordered me to “Call the others.” Meaning the temple president and the GBC man who was also visiting the temple.

The next day before the morning lecture, Srila Prabhupada mentioned, “Yesterday, one of my disciples got angry in my presence, but that was proper, because his guru had been insulted”.

It was then clear to me that one can become angry if one’s guru, or Krishna, was being insulted. Therefore, we can also use anger in the service of the Lord. Srila Bhaktisiddhanta was also asked this question about anger. He instructed us that one can use anger in three ways.

First against our own lower nature for laziness and unwillingness to render service to the Lord.

Second, against our family members who want us to serve them exclusively.

Third, against the people of demoniac mentality.

## Chapter 4. I will go wherever he sends me (1971-1974)

## In the mission: going to Japan

## That is alright then; you can take sannyasa.

A few days later, during a massage session, Srila Prabhupada asked me if I would like to take sannyasa.

I had previously read how Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Saraswati Thakura took sannyasa when he was forty-five. I was twenty-nine then, so I answered Srila Prabhupada’s question by saying, “I do know if I am qualified to take sannyasa, Srila Prabhupada.” He responded in a serious voice, “Do you want to get married?” I said, “No, Srila Prabhupada.” He settled the matter by saying, “That is alright then; you can take sannyasa.”

## Assisting in his mission

The date for my sannyasa initiation was fixed for July 27, 1971. Srila Prabhupada gave many initiations at that time, both first and second, but I was the only one awarded sannyasa. After my initiation, Srila Prabhupada called me into his room and told me he wanted me to go to Japan and help with the preaching there. As it was the system at that time, I was allowed to have one assistant. The devotee who helped me was named Satyadhanya Prabhu. The following day both of us were on the streets of New York City collecting our plane fares to Japan.

After collecting enough funds for the ticket, we decided to drive across the country to our big temple in Los Angeles as we planned to fly from the West Coast to Japan. It took us a few days, and we stopped at some temples. I remember the temple president we met in Dallas named Madhavananda Prabhu. We were all engaged in the great adventure of assisting Srila Prabhupada in his mission. So it was enlightening to talk to each other about our mission.

Soon we reached our Western World Headquarters in Los Angeles on Watseka Avenue. The temple was impressive, as were the facilities connected to it, including housing on the street. We arranged for our visas to Japan and bought our air tickets. Before long, we were on our way. We passed through immigration without any difficulty as we had simple tourist visas.

Our temple in Japan was outside the city. It was a rented house that was quite big but did not have many rooms. It was hard to keep it warm, and winter was approaching. We were going out everyday door to door selling some of Srila Prabhupada’s small books that had been translated into Japanese.

## My ever well-wisher

After I had been in Japan for a few months, in April 1972, Srila Prabhupada arrived. We rented a newly constructed cottage near the temple for his use. The day after his arrival, I positioned myself to see the door to his cottage. He opened it around 6:00 a.m. and then closed it. I took it as an opportunity to present myself personally to him.

I went into his room, paid obeisances, and looked up at him. I felt a little important, thinking I had carried out his orders to come to this foreign country. He immediately understood my mentality and said in a derogatory way, “Oh, it is you!... Call the others.”

Welcome to the association of Srila Prabhupada, a pure devotee! No material sentiment, always preaching. It was my good fortune that I knew him well enough by this time not to be anxious about his chastising me because I was a hundred percent sure he was my well-wisher. Naturally, I called the others, and life went on, and I became a little humbler.

It is worth mentioning that at this time, Srila Prabhupada started having us read the Sanskrit verses of the text we were to discuss in the class. It was hard to get the pronunciation right, but I took it as a challenge and gradually improved. This experience showed me that, even if we sometimes face particular challenges, we must be determined to overcome them. And actually, being humble and very determined will help us to progress spiritually.

## That is alright

I once said to Srila Prabhupada, “Srila Prabhupada, many of my God-brothers do not like me.” Srila Prabhupada replied, “That is alright—I like you.”

Perhaps a year later, Srila Prabhupada said, “I know you cannot work too well with the GBC, so you can work directly with me”. After this, I would get an assignment from Srila

Prabhupada and report to him personally. This way, he would send me wherever and whenever he saw fit.

## Our God is the nicest God

(From the memories of Bhurijana Prabhu)

I was sitting with Trivikrama, who was serving Srila Prabhupada in Japan. We were both sitting outside his room, and suddenly Srila Prabhupada rang his bell. We both went into his room. Before that Trivikrama was showing me a Bible tract that his brother, who was a born-again Christian, had sent him. Trivikrama Maharaja showed it to Srila Prabhupada. This Bible tract said, “As many westerners, Christians, are turning to eastern religions and Hinduism, here is one Hindu PhD who is turning to Christianity”.

Prabhupada looked at it. This tract explained that the Christian PhD presented two objections to so-called Hinduism. One was the caste system, and the other was Deity worship, the worship of idols. Srila Prabhupada just looked at it and laughed. He said, “He is a PhD, but does not know anything”.

Then Srila Prabhupada revealed to us the reason why he had rung the bell and called us in. He had received some photos of Rukmini-Dvarakadhisa from Karanda, who had come from Los Angeles. He pulled out the photo of Rukmini-Dvarakadhisa, showed it to us, and said, “Anyway, our God is the nicest God”. He had called us in just to show us the photo of Rukmini-Dvarakadhisa and share Their beauty with us.

## He expected us to push ourselves.

In Tokyo, I went out daily to distribute books after massaging Srila Prabhupada in the morning. Srila Prabhupada would often ask me how the book distribution was going on. That day when he asked me I said, “I did not go for book distribution yesterday, because I had some correspondence to catch up on”. Srila Prabhupada did not say anything, but his disapproval was immediately apparent by the look on his face. He was pushing himself, getting up in the middle of the night to translate for us, so he expected us to also push ourselves.

## We are official in Japan!

Srila Prabhupada and his entourage left in early May, and we settled into our routine of daily distributing Srila Prabhupada’s magazines and books door to door. We did not ask for large donations, so we were able to distribute a fair amount every day. The Japanese people were friendly toward us and were easy to talk to. I did not know the language but knew enough to sell our books and invite them to our center.

We had to save money because, for us to be an officially recognized religion there, we had to own our property. Soon we hit on the idea of selling our magazines on the street rather than going door to door. By doing this, we were able to distribute many more magazines.

My self-created quota was 250 pieces daily except for Sunday when I did 125. There were usually two or three of us going out every day, and after a year or so, we had enough money to purchase a small house just outside the city. Subsequently, we finally got registered as a bona fide religious organization.

## Don't make any excuse

In January 1974, Srila Prabhupada returned to Japan.

I had been informed Srila Prabhupada would not be coming at this time, so I had gone to India early and was not in Japan when he arrived. When he disembarked from the plane, I was told the first thing he said was, “Where is Trivikrama Swami?”

When I met Srila Prabhupada again in his room in Calcutta a month or so later, he asked me why I was not in Tokyo when he went there. I did not make any excuse and simply said I was a “Big rascal.” He responded by acquiescing, saying, “No, you are a sannyasi, so you can travel.”

## Going to Taiwan

## His enthusiasm was contagious.

At this time. Guru Kripa Maharaja and his party took over managing the Japanese yatra. Srila Prabhupada called me into his room and told me he wanted me to go to Taiwan, the Republic of China. A letter had come from a Mr. Gajar, the president of the chemical company Dupont based there. He was originally from India, had heard of our movement, and wanted to help us establish a center there in the capital city of Taipei.

I was not enthusiastic about going because I had never had a good experience preaching to the Chinese. I knew they were very materialistic. I have never seen Chinese hippies. They only thought about making money. But I did not say anything to Srila Prabhupada. I just listened. However, Srila Prabhupada was very determined to establish a center in the Republic of China and was very enthusiastic for me to go. His enthusiasm was contagious, and I left his room feeling inspired to go there. I remember thinking, “Yes, very good. I am going to China!”

That was Srila Prabhupada’s magic. He was so enthusiastic; it was not something false. He once told us that this whole movement was spread with enthusiasm. And, of course, it was his enthusiasm. He was so happy to spread Krishna consciousness around the world. And from our side, we were pleased to assist him.

Even if we worked so hard, Srila Prabhupada still pushed us. But when we returned and reported to him, and he was satisfied, it all seemed worthwhile. His favourable glance made us feel that all our difficulties meant nothing.

Because it was Srila Prabhupada’s order to start a center there, I was authorized to recruit some men to join me for this service. Three men agreed, and soon we were on our way.

## Leaving Taiwan for Hong Kong

## You are never alone

In 1974, while preaching in Taiwan, we did not have much success, and the devotees who had come with me started to leave. One went back to the USA. Another met a girl there and moved out of our center; another was sick and left for the USA. I thought it would be best to visit our center in Hong Kong, as I need association and so I travelled there. There was one devotee there, and he also decided to leave, so I ended up alone for some time.

Some of the local Hindus, of which there were quite a few, used to come for our Sunday program in Hong Kong. Also, there was one Chinese boy who was serious. He had been initiated by mail and was translating the Bhagavad-gita into Chinese. He also came to our center every Sunday. I was going out daily to distribute some small books in English, mainly to tourists. In this way, we were maintaining the small rented center.

## Srila Prabhupada wrote me a letter

**“So you stay there in Hong Kong and struggle for Krishna. I also received today the Chinese literature On the Way to Krsna. One Chinese literature is 1000 times better preaching than worshipping in the temple. But we have to have a place and the worship must go on. Without having a sitting place, how will people come to see us and talk with us? So try to distribute and publish as much as possible. I am coming there very soon, so please arrange for it.**

**If it can be continued why close it? The book you have published is real preaching. At least the Chinese will have some idea of Krishna. So I will come and see how things are going on.**

**- SPL, 74-12-02, Letter\_to\_Trivikrama\_written\_ from\_Bombay**

Thinking that Srila Prabhupada would be coming. I was very happy but instead of Srila Prabhupada coming, his secretary, who at that time was Brahmananda Maharaja, came. When Maharaja arrived he was surprised to see that I was alone there and sent a telegram to Srila Prabhupada in India saying I was alone and someone should be sent to help me. In a week or so I got a letter from Srila Prabhupada as follows:

**Bombay**  
**December 27, 1974**

**My dear Trivikrama Swami, Please accept my blessings. I have just received one telegram from Brahmananda Swami. He said that there is one Chinese initiated devotee there who is giving money and has already translated three chapters of Bhagavad-gita. This is very wonderful. You can immediately print those first three chapters of Bhagavad-gita into a book. And let him translate three more chapters and then print that into a book. Then three more, etc. In this way we can introduce our Bhagavad-gita As It Is in Hong Kong also. He also stated that the temple can be maintained by the Indians. That is good. Let them give some donation for Krishna’s service and gradually they will also become Krishna Conscious. Brahmananda Swami seemed to think that you need more men there. His telegram reads, “Trivikrama alone, more men required urgently.” I do not understand what he means by alone. What is this alone?**

**Vaisnava is never alone. When I first came to the United States I was seemingly alone for one year. But I never felt alone. I always felt the presence of**

**my Guru Maharaja. Myself, I was cooking, I was printing books, I was selling books, everything seemingly alone. But I did not lose my determination. Actually you should know this, you are never alone. So local men are coming daily. By good association, good preaching, nice prasadam etc., they can all become devotees. This is Lord Caitanya’s mission to go to every town and village and create Vaisnava spirit in the local men. I have also sent one letter to Pancadravida Swami in Bangkok that his party can come there sometimes for big programs if you can arrange. Keep in touch with them. Their address is: Pancadravida Swami, c/o D.H. Carturchand, 238 Samteng St. Bangkok, Thailand. Please keep yourself strong and fixed up in Krishna’s service by following all of our regulative principles, chanting 16 rounds daily, rising early, attending mangala-arati and attending classes. I hope this meets you in good health.**

**Your ever well-wisher,**  
**A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami**

By the mercy of Srila Prabhupada, I could tolerate being alone. Soon, Pancadravida Swami came with a servant and took over the preaching center, and I went to India for a visit.

## Jackpot

I returned to Hong Kong after visiting India and after a few months Srila Prabhupada came there. The temple was just a tiny apartment, so we rented the presidential suite in the Hilton Hotel in Hong Kong. A wealthy Indian man paid for the suite.

From the airport, I rode in the car behind Srila Prabhupada’s. When his car arrived, I saw a hotel staff member come to Srila Prabhupada’s car and open the door. He helped Srila Prabhupada get out of the vehicle. I remember thinking how this person got to serve a pure devotee just by chance. This is called ajnata-sukriti, unknowingly performing devotional service. It was like hitting the jackpot of a slot machine. He opened so many doors, and then by his good fortune, he opened the door for a pure devotee!

## "Not like that."

While in Hong Kong, we went with Srila Prabhupada on a morning walk in the park when we saw a group of Chinese people doing Tai Chi. I said to Srila Prabhupada and the others, “This will not save them at the time of death.” Srila Prabhupada responded to me by saying, “Not like that.”

In other words, he appreciated that they did not waste their time on nonsense things and were doing something to improve themselves.

## What was your most difficult time?

Later we were in Srila Prabhupada’s room with a few devotees, which included Sudama Vipra Maharaja, who had just come to Hong Kong from the Philippines. We had heard he was having some difficulties. To encourage this devotee in his service, Srila Prabhupada asked him to say something about his being in Mayapur the first year after we had acquired the land.

This devotee related how in that year, there was a big flood. They were staying in the bhajan-kutira at the entrance to the property. No brick buildings had been built yet. They were living in the rafter; as the water rose, the snakes also entered. We were all appreciating his ordeal.

Srila Prabhupada was smiling and then turned to me and asked, “What was your most difficult time in Krishna consciousness?” I related how when we first came to Japan; we had a big wooden house in the countryside, which was impossible to keep warm in the winter. We had portable kerosene heaters, but they did not warm up the whole house. If you stood in front of it, your back would get cold, and vice-versa. Srila Prabhupada smiled, and the devotees appreciated also.

Then I asked Srila Prabhupada, “What was your most difficult time in Krishna consciousness?” Suddenly the whole mood changed. Srila Prabhupada became very serious and said with the gravest voice, “You do not want to know about that!” I remember thinking that it was good that he did not tell us, as just hearing about it would have killed us.

Sometimes we think we are doing so much and performing so many austerities for Krishna, but Srila Prabhupada went through many problems to push this movement on. He never told us about his most difficult time in Krishna consciousness. Still, we can just imagine: coming on the boat, seasickness, heart attacks, his God-brothers speaking against him, and wandering alone in New York without knowing anyone. It was one full year before he was able to establish something.

We need to be very grateful, and feel indebted to Srila Prabhupada, and try to pay him back by assisting him in his mission. We should never think of being envious of him because his position was so difficult that we would and could not do what he did. Therefore, it is best to put Srila Prabhupada in the center of our lives and continue cooperating under his guidance.

Sometimes Srila Prabhupada was asked, “Do you know Krishna?” Occasionally he would reply, “No, I just know my spiritual master.” Of course, he knew Krishna, but this is our formula: first, we go to the guru. Srila Prabhupada is the siksa guru for everyone for the next ten thousand years. Yasya prasadad bhagavat-prasado yasyaprasadan na gatih kuto’ pi: first of all, we need to please the representative of Krishna, then we can know that Krishna will be happy. Thus, we need to do something to satisfy Srila Prabhupada: preach, distribute his books, talk to people about him, and cooperate.

## In Vrindavan September 1974

## If we do not get a taste for this kirtan, then simply sex life.

Srila Prabhupada had come to the Krishna Balarama temple as he had hoped to open the temple on Janmasthami day in 1974. However, the construction was still not finished, so the opening date was moved to April 1975. While Srila Prabhupada was there in Vrindavan, he became ill. I arrived from Hong Kong and brought a camphor gift and a jade-handled letter opener for Srila Prabhupada. He used the camphor and said it was a “godsend”.

Srila Prabhupada’s health gradually improved, and he would come to the temple every evening for the program. Nevertheless, he did not participate directly in the program but sat under the Tamal tree along with his servant Sruta-kirti Prabhu. The program started at 6:30 p.m. All temple devotees were expected to attend. There were maybe around thirty or forty devotees at that time. First, there was the arati with kirtan, and after that, one of the sannyasis would give class.

The class was generally 20-30 minutes, but if the sannyasi spoke more, Srila Prabhupada would send Sruta-kirti Prabhu to instruct him to stop and have kirtan. The kirtan went on for another forty-five minutes to an hour. This was the program every night for a couple of weeks. I also spoke on one occasion.

One evening after the program, Srila Prabhupada came back to take some rest, and I joined him. Sruta-kirti was also there, and we both sat on the ground, and Srila Prabhupada was sitting on his wooden bed. The weather was warm, so he was sleeping outside. Suddenly, with an animated voice full of authority, he started to speak, “This kirtan is the special mercy of Lord Caitanya. It gives us life... and if we don’t get life from the kirtan, then simply sex life!”

That instruction helped me over the years. I was a passionate New Yorker, but I threw myself into the kirtan day and night, which helped me remain a sannyasi over the years. If we strictly follow the instructions of Srila Prabhupada we will get his mercy.

Otherwise, bondage to the material world is inevitable. Lust remains with us to the point of death. As Krishna informs us, “The wise living entity’s pure consciousness becomes covered by his eternal enemy in the form of lust, which is never satisfied and which burns like fire.” - BG 3.39

At the same time, Srila Prabhupada sometimes said: “Kirtan is the safest place in the material world.” And it is true. Because it comes from the spiritual world; as Narottam Das Thakur says, “golokera prema-dhana, hari-nama-sankirtana”. Therefore, it is our choice: sex life or spiritual life.

## Do not lie down, or you will sleep.

There was another brief pastime with Srila Prabhupada during my visit. I was sleeping in the room behind Srila Prabhupada’s quarters, and he would go through that room to use his toilet. Once when he came through, I was lying down and reading one of his books. He said, “Yes, always read, but do not lie down, or you will sleep.”

So, it is true: we need to take reading Srila Prabhupada’s books very seriously, but it is better to do it sitting, so we will not fall asleep. Because he gave these books to us, and he spent every night for so many hours translating them. Srila Prabhupada’s biggest personal endeavour was to get these books translated, published, and distributed worldwide. Therefore how can we please Srila Prabhupada if we do not read his books attentively?

## Krishna made my suffering less.

I also remember once in Vrindavan when Srila Prabhupada was walking around, and he saw one devotee whom he had not seen for some time. His name was Bharadwaja Prabhu. Srila Prabhupada said to him, “Oh, you are here, I have not seen you for some time”. The devotee replied; “Srila Prabhupada, I have been sick. It is my karma". Then Srila Prabhupada said: “Yes the devotee should think like that. You can think that because I became a devotee, Krishna made my suffering less. Because I am such a rascal, I should have been punished more, but Krishna has minimized it”.

It is not so easy to have such consciousness, but the goal is to understand that everything that comes into our life is meant for our purification and education.

## Chapter 5. Life-long lessons (1975-1976)

### 1975

## He will go wherever I send him.

This is one more story I would like to relate. I was not there personally, but I heard it from a devotee who was there. A GBC man was criticizing me directly to Srila Prabhupada. But Srila Prabhupada did not want to hear his complaint. To stop the conversation, he said; “No! He will go wherever I send him!”

I was ready to go wherever Srila Prabhupada wanted me to go because I had faith that Krishna would protect me as long as I maintained a sincere desire to please Srila Prabhupada.

## Just chant Hare Krishna.

In 1975, at the Mayapur festival, Srila Prabhupada gave the morning Bhagavatam class. Perhaps 400-500 devotees were listening to him speak. Suddenly he lost external consciousness. The whole room became utterly silent. It went on for a couple of minutes. I felt it was a little awkward, so I stood up and started chanting Srila Prabhupada’s pranama mantra to break the silence. Then Srila Prabhupada slowly came out of his trance; the class was finished, and the devotees began to leave.

Later I was approached by Bhagavan Maharaja with a couple of other devotees, one of whom was Brahmananda Prabhu. They asked me why I had started chanting in the class?. I responded by saying, “I thought...” But, before I could finish, they cut me off and said, “You thought?” It was said in a rather loud, derogatory voice. I just turned away and left them.

Soon after, I came to find out that Sruta-kirti Prabhu asked Srila Prabhupada at lunch that day, “What should we do at such a moment?” Srila Prabhupada replied, “Chant Hare Krishna!”

## No one is dhira

During the festival, Srila Prabhupada gave darshan in his room in Mayapur. There were about 25-30 devotees, and Srila Prabhupada speaking nectarian krishna-katha. A young Bengali couple came into his room. Srila Prabhupada stopped speaking and welcomed them by asking us to make room for them to sit. They sat, and then he continued speaking.

After about thirty seconds, the couple got up and left. I remember thinking they were foolish to miss out on the flow of nectar. Srila Prabhupada also commented, “That is the trouble, people are not dhira, not thoughtful enough to hear this krishna-katha. They cannot sit even for a minute or two”.

## Honoured by such an exchange

Srila Prabhupada had invited his God-brother, B.R. Sridhara Swami, to join him for lunch at our temple in Mayapur. After taking prasadam, they retired into Srila Prabhupada’s room. I decided to join them there. When I entered, there were a couple of servants, and Sridhara Maharaja and Srila Prabhupada were both still standing.

When Srila Prabhupada saw me, he introduced me to Sridhara Maharaja, saying, “Maharaja, this is our Trivikrama Swami.” I moved close to Sridhara Swami. He could not seem to see well, but he reached out and touched me. I felt very happy and honoured by such an exchange.

## You gave up everything but your whim.

At the Mayapur festival in 1975, Gopal Krishna Maharaja, who was the GBC for the whole of India, approached me and said, “Bhavananda Maharaja is going to be leaving Mayapur to renew his visa. Srila Prabhupada wants you to stay and be co-director with Jayapataka”. But I replied, «I am not staying, I have a plan. I am going to preach in the Far East, and I already have some devotees who are going to go with me. So I am not going to stay here”.

Later in the afternoon, I was in Srila Prabhupada’s room during darshan, and Gopal Krishna was also there. Many devotees were there, and Gopal Krishna raised his hand and said, “Srila Prabhupada, Trivikrama Swami said he does not want to stay here in Mayapur.” There was silence. Srila Prabhupada looked at me and said, “So what is the problem? Why do you not want to stay here?” I said, “Well, Srila Prabhupada, I already have a program and promised some devotees...” But Srila Prabhupada immediately interrupted me, saying, “Our formula is plain living and high thinking, but you are simply addicted to the city.” Then I said, “But Srila Prabhupada, I am not enlivened by preaching here in Mayapur. I would rather go...” But he again spoke to me and said, “You’re sannyasi. You’ve given up everything but your whim”. Still, I was objecting. I said, “But Srila Prabhupada, I’ve been a sannyasi for five years, and still I have not had a chance to preach in English. If I stay here in Mayapur, I will not be able to preach in English”. Srila Prabhupada said, “Oh, you have been a sannyasi for five years and you have not had a chance to preach... So what makes you think you know how to preach?” Then something inside me said, “Yes, sir.” When I was in the Navy, I was trained up to respect a superior. At that point, I realized that Srila Prabhupada was my superior in every way, and without his mercy, I would not be able to preach. Thus, it was better just to shut up and do what he wanted me to do. I surrendered and stayed in Mayapur.

Half a year later, when Bhavananda Maharaja returned, I was properly relieved of my service there and went to Vrindavan to meet Srila Prabhupada. He then gave me another assignment.

## Do not criticize unless you have a solution September 1975, Vrindavan

I heard from Upendra Prabhu, who was Srila Prabhupada’s servant at that time, that Guru Kripa and Yashodanandana Maharajas, who had replaced me in Japan, had been deported by the Japanese government. The preaching there had stopped.

This upset me a little because I had served there for three years and went into Srila Prabhupada’s room in that mood. He was sitting alone on his bed. I paid my obeisances and spoke a little strongly, “I knew this would happen!” He looked at me in the mood of someone who had just been approached by an uncultured aborigine. Then I said, “Now we have been thrown out of Japan, but we have Radha Krishna Deities”. Srila Prabhupada was so gentle and refined that it was hard for him to relate to my crudeness.

When I saw his reaction, I explained that I had just heard what had happened in Japan. He was quiet for a moment and then spoke soberly, “I did not want this to happen”. Then again, after a moment more of silence, he said, “Alright, you can go back there”.

I was dumbfounded. I did not want to go back there. But as I sat there, I realised this was Srila Prabhupada’s mood. If someone came to him with a complaint, he expected they

would also have a solution. The natural solution was that I should go back there and try to improve the situation.

I requested to be allowed to take one devotee with me, and the following day I was on the bus leaving Vrindavan on my way to Japan. I went back there with Satyadhanya Prabhu, my old assistant. We kept the center open and continued the Deity worship. I personally was the pujari. But it was a good lesson. It is easy to criticise, but Srila Prabhupada did not appreciate that unless we had a positive solution. Otherwise, it was better to remain silent.

## Are you convinced?

Once in Bombay, Srila Prabhupada was on the roof. There were 25-30 people on darshan. I had just arrived carrying my danda. As soon as Srila Prabhupada saw me, he said: “So, Trivikrama Maharaja, are you convinced?” I replied: “Yes, Srila Prabhupada”. Then he commented: “Yes! We must be convinced of this philosophy. Then we can preach”. This was Prabhupada’s mood: he wanted to make a revolution in the world. He once told us: “Marx and Engels created one Lenin. But I have created 50 Lenins”, meaning 50 devotees - who understand the philosophy and are ready to act in revolutionary way.

We need to be convinced of this philosophy. Because we are the only ones who can accurately understand who God is and what His energies are. Everything is explained very scientifically and cool-headedly in Srila Prabhupada’s books.

### 1976

## Srila Prabhupada had a dream.

We returned from the morning walk with Srila Prabhupada in 1976 in Los Angeles. I had recently heard that someone had said that when Srila Prabhupada was coming on the boat to America, he had a heart attack, and Krishna came to help him. At that time, we were 6-7 devotees in Srila Prabhupada’s room, and I asked him, “Srila Prabhupada, I’ve heard that when you were coming on the boat, you had a dream that Krishna came to you. Is that right?”

Srila Prabhupada’s first reaction was shyness; he did not want to talk about it in front of others. But then, a while later, he replied, “Yes”. He admitted that Krishna had come to him. He was on the boat, and Krishna was rowing this boat and said to America, “Do not worry, I will bring you there”. We can read in Srila Prabhupada’s diary that he did not know whether he would live or die for a few days. But Krishna came to reassure him, “Do not worry, I will bring you to your destination”.

Prabhupada: There was two days I was attacked in heart on the ship. So hardship.

Trivikrama: Then you had a dream?

Prabhupada: Hmm.

Hari-sauri: What was that, Srila Prabhupada?

Prabhupada: That is... (laughs) The dream was I must come here.

Hari-sauri: It was some instruction that you got?

Prabhupada: The dream was that Krsna in His many forms was bowing the row... what is called?

Hari-sauri: Rowing the boat.

Prabhupada: Yes.

Trivikrama: Jaya.

This is the mood of a pure devotee: he does not advertise his accomplishments. But we have to glorify him. It is something we can all be united in: glorifying Srila Prabhupada to express our appreciation to him for how he has saved us. We can understand that he was not an ordinary spiritual master, but he was empowered. Otherwise, how was he able to single-handedly spread this movement all over the world in such a short period?

## They cannot check this movement.

After a morning walk at Santa Monica (Los Angeles) with Srila Prabhupada, we were returning by car to the temple on Watseka Avenue. Srila Prabhupada was in the front seat, and there were three sannyasis in the back, of which I was one. Many devotees were on the street waiting to greet Srila Prabhupada, but we returned from an unexpected direction, so the devotees did not see Srila Prabhupada coming.

When Srila Prabhupada saw all the devotees with their bead bags and Vaisnava dress, he suddenly spoke with full conviction. “They cannot check this movement. It will go unchecked for the next ten thousand years!” This is Srila Prabhupada’s prediction, and we should work hard to make this come true.

Sometimes he said that this movement cannot be checked from the outside but from within. If we fight each other, that can set the movement back. Therefore, Srila Prabhupada left us with the order that we should cooperate together under the authority of the Governing Body Commission. Otherwise, this is the danger: it is Kali-yuga, and it is very easy to quarrel.

## Grateful for his protection 1975, Mayapur festival

I already said that Srila Prabhupada had said at one point, “I know you can’t work too well with the GBC, so you can get your orders directly from me”. At this yearly occasion of the Mayapur Gaura-purnima festival I had approached Srila Prabhupada and requested permission to open a center in Seoul, South Korea. I had been to Korea before to extend my visa for Japan, and I saw that the people were very friendly to Americans and very straightforward. There was a nice brahmacari who wanted to go to South Korea with me, and Srila Prabhupada permitted us to go.

Later that same day, I received instructions from a secretary to the GBC that the EC (Executive Committee) of the GBC wanted to meet me. Naturally, I went to their building to see what they wanted. They invited me to go to the roof of the building for their talk.

Tamal Krishna Maharaja, the Radha-Damodara travelling bus program leader, wanted me to take charge of one of their buses. I explained that I had already received permission from Srila Prabhupada to open a center in Korea, so I cut the meeting short.

The next morning during mangala-arati, Srila Prabhupada’s personal servant, Pusta Krsna Maharaja, tapped me on the shoulder and said that Srila Prabhupada wanted to see me in his room. I was surprised because I had been called to Srila Prabhupada’s room before, but never so early in the morning. When I arrived, I saw that Guru Kripa and Madhudvisa Maharajas were already there. Srila Prabhupada invited me with a hand gesture to sit. Then Srila Prabhupada said, “Now he is here; you can speak on his face!”

They began speaking, and one of the points came from Guru Kripa, who was collecting a lot of money in Japan and did not want me to interfere with his service there.

I explained, “I was not going to be in Japan, but in Korea, a different country separated from Japan by a body of water.” That was all I said. Srila Prabhupada was quiet.

When they saw that Srila Prabhupada was not going to side with them they made another complaint, which was that 1 was liable to travel too much. So when they understood that Srila Prabhupada was not going to side with them, Madhudvisa spoke in a strong voice, “Well, if he is going to go to Korea, he should commit to stay there for at least ten years!”

Then Srila Prabhupada spoke. He said, “Why is this envy starting now even in my presence? I saw this among my God-brothers after my Guru Maharaja had left the planet, but why even in my presence, is this envy coming out?” I felt very grateful to Srila Prabhupada for giving me such strong protection.

## There is only one side, Srila Prabhupada's side.

**March 1976, Mayapur festival**

Srila Prabhupada called me to his room. He said, “So I think you wanted to go to China, the mainland.” I had never seriously considered it because it was under the control of the Communists Party. But I responded, “If you want, I can go, Srila Prabhupada.” He said, “Yes. I want you to go with our Tamal Krishna Maharaja. Go to his room and tell him to come here”.

I went to Tamal’s room and told him that Srila Prabhupada wanted to see him in his room. Immediately he jumped up and said, “I am not going to go,” and ran out of his room. I had not mentioned anything about his going to China. Suddenly I was alone, so I went back down to Srila Prabhupada’s room.

When I reached Srila Prabhupada’s room, he was in the toilet, but Tamal Krishna, Madhudvisa, and Guru Kripa Maharajas were there. They were discussing how to deal with Srila Prabhupada when he returned.

The background of this intrigue is as follows. The day before, the GBC had its annual meeting, after which the temple presidents also met to review the resolutions GBC had passed. In their meeting, temple presidents had complained against the Radha-Damodara travelling party, headed by Tamal Krishna Maharaja, that they were coming to their temples and stealing brahmacharis. When Srila Prabhupada learned of this complaint, he took it seriously and said it would lead to “fratricidal war”. Thus, he spoke to Tamal Krishna Maharaja that evening, and Tamed was defending himself and at one point said, “What am I supposed to do? Go to China?” When he said that, Srila Prabhupada became thoughtful and must have decided to send him to China.

When Prabhupada came out of the toilet, the atmosphere was very tense. Srila Prabhupada was starting to put on tilak, and I saw his hand shaking. I did not like the way they were dealing with him. Madhudvisa spoke first, saying, “You know, Srila Prabhupada, there are two sides to this story.” I could not refrain and spoke out, “No! There is only one side, Srila Prabhupada’s side”. The three sannyasis looked at me with daggers in their eyes.

The conversation continued. Then Srila Prabhupada very expertly released the tension by saying to Tamal, “Alright, you do not have to go.” Immediately the tense atmosphere abated. Then after a few seconds, he spoke again. “So, who will go?” Madhudvisa said, “From the GBC?” Srila Prabhupada said, “Yes.”

Then Guru Kripa said, “Well, I am not very qualified, but if you want, I can go Srila Prabhupada.” Srila Prabhupada did not even look at him but kept his attention focused on Tamal. Then Tamal said, “I think Atreya Rishi can go. He does business, so he can go as a businessman”. Then Srila Prabhupada said, “Oh, you do not want to go, but you want he should go!” Then Madhudvisa said, “You know, Tamal, we are supposed to do what the spiritual master wants us to do.”

Now it was clear... Srila Prabhupada’s will versus Tamal’s will. Then Srila Prabhupada spoke, “It is my intense desire that you go.” Tamal kind of laughed and said, “I can see that. “. But he still did not surrender. Then Srila Prabhupada spoke again. He said, “Alright, then you stay here in Mayapur and chant Hare Krishna. I am taking away your service as the leader of the Radha-Damodara bus party!” After a pause, Tamal spoke. He said, “I can go, but I do not want to go with Trivikrama Swami, but rather with Dhrishtadyumna Maharaja.” Srila Prabhupada immediately agreed.

Then we all came out of Srila Prabhupada’s room and went up a flight of stairs to the roof where Srila Prabhupada had his morning walk. Many devotees joined us, and as everyone began to follow him, Srila Prabhupada stopped and turned to speak to all of us. He said in a friendly voice, “You will all be happy to know that our Tamal Krishna Maharaja is going to China!” The devotees cheered loudly.

## Meeting Prabhupada in Vrindavan

We came to know that Srila Prabhupada was in New Delhi, so I went there to see him and to ask permission to leave Chandigarh and to instead serve at our New Delhi center.

Srila Prabhupada permitted me, and I began happily serving there. Bhagavat Acharya Prabhu was the temple president in Delhi then, and we were making life members, distributing books in the offices, and so on. After a few months, we heard that Srila Prabhupada was again in India visiting Vrindavan, and I decided to go and see him.

When I went into Srila Prabhupada’s room, I paid my obeisances, and Srila Prabhupada recognized me. However, he spoke to me in a mood with some alarm. He said, “Why are you leaving your service in New Delhi? It is not good to be changing your service like this”. However, this was not my real reason for coming, and I spoke in an innocent voice to Srila Prabhupada that I had come to have his association. When Srila Prabhupada saw that this was my reason for arriving there, he looked up to the ceiling and shrugged his shoulders as if gesturing to his superiors and saying, “What can I do.”

I stayed in Vrindavan for about a week, and there was another incident that was noteworthy. I was in Srila Prabhupada’s room when he asked his personal servant to give him the key to the almirah, a steel cabinet, where he kept his valuables. The key was hidden behind a picture on the wall.

By chance, I was in Srila Prabhupada’s room the next day, and he turned to me and asked me to get him the key to his almirah. When he said this, I looked at him a little bewildered, like, “Srila Prabhupada, I am not your personal servant; why are you asking me?” But he immediately replied, “You know where it is!” He remembered perfectly that I was there the previous day when he had his servant get the key.

## Coming to Chandigarh (1976)

I had left Hong Kong for service in India, and Srila Prabhupada sent me to Chandigarh, a city in North India that serves as the capital for two states, Haryana and Punjab. The city gave us an excellent piece of land there for a nominal price. Our leader there was a devotee from Punjab named Bhakti Caitanya Maharaja. I did not get along with him very well, but we cooperated somewhat.

## Like a dhobi man

*A dhobi man is someone who takes the dirty clothes to wash in the river and then carries them back on his donkey.*

On a morning walk in Chandigarh in 1976, Srila Prabhupada by coincidence met the Governor of Punjab. Srila Prabhupada was introduced as the leader of the Hare Krishna movement. Then the Governor spoke first. That was his mistake. He should have listened, but instead, he said, “ We are also doing good work. We have an eye bank helping the blind”. In this way, he was implying that he was on the same level as Srila Prabhupada. Srila Prabhupada replied, “It means that you are like a dhobi man.” It was demeaning to compare the Governor to a dhobi man. Everyone stopped to listen. Did Srila Prabhupada compare the Governor to a dhobi man? But Srila Prabhupada did not pause even for a second. He continued, “Because in Bhagavad-gita Krishna says that this body is like a dress. You are taking care about the dress, but we are taking care about the soul”.

What could the Governor say? He was embarrassed because he should not have spoken. Instead of advertising himself, he should have first listened to what Srila Prabhupada had to say.

## Fighting only on principles of religion

I was with Srila Prabhupada in his room in Bombay in 1976. We had darshan, and Srila Prabhupada spoke with an elderly Indian man. We could see that he was a very respectable person. Srila Prabhupada was explaining to him one problem that he was facing. Two leading devotees in India quarrelled, and due to their differences preaching had stopped. One was the devotee from India, and one was from the West. Then Srila Prabhupada spoke to his friend and said this is the problem with these Western devotees. They simply like to fight. It was a fact. When I heard his words, I was ready to fight. I knew that Srila Prabhupada would be a little annoyed, but I said, “But, Srila Prabhupada, Arjuna was fighting!” Srila Prabhupada immediately looked at me with angry eyes and said, “That fighting is not like this fighting. That fighting is on principles of religion, but this fighting is that I go out of my way to make it inauspicious for the other”.

We can observe that this is true. People do not understand the real principles of religion; they do not have the necessary qualifications. Therefore, all these ongoing wars in the world will not bring about any long lasting peace.

## You are a good driver.

Srila Prabhupada said, “You are a good driver. You can drive for me here”. I responded, “I have not driven for about five years, Srila Prabhupada”. He retorted, “You never forget how to drive.” Then I said, “But I do not have a driver’s license for here in India.” He answered by saying, “That does not matter.” However, I never drove for Srila Prabhupada in India.

## The mind of a pure devotee is hard to understand

We were in Srila Prabhupada’s room in Bombay in 1976. Srila Prabhupada was visited by a yogi, with whom he had a long discussion. At one point, the talk came to the topic of the spiritual master, and then I got into the conversation. I said, “Yes. This is the basic point. We have to accept a spiritual master”. I thought that this man should accept Srila Prabhupada as his spiritual master.

But Srila Prabhupada immediately looked at me as if thinking, better not to get involved in this conversation. I have a relationship with this person, and I am preaching to him in my way. He did not say it, just conveyed it with his eyes. Srila Prabhupada did not want me to interrupt his preaching.

## Appreciating the contribution

During that same period, I was there when Srila Prabhupada was preaching to an influential man from the Indian railways. This man was providing a whole train carriage for taking the devotees and Srila Prabhupada to Allahabad for the upcoming Kumbha-mela.

When there was a pause in their conversation, I started to say something. Immediately Srila Prabhupada looked at me with some alarm. He did not like anyone to interfere when he was preaching. However, I was able to escape his annoyance by providing a sastric reference to the points Srila Prabhupada had been making. The verse I mentioned was tatra laulyam api mulyam ekalam, “One should be very greedy to advance in Krishna consciousness. This is a price we need to pay to progress spiritually”. (CC Madhya 8.70). Srila Prabhupada saw that I contributed to his point and became instantly animated. He integrated it into his preaching.

Thus, we could speak in front of Srila Prabhupada, but we needed to be sure that we understood the points he was trying to make while preaching. Otherwise, we could end up displeasing him.

## We do not change our philosophy.

Something similar happened when we were on a morning walk with Srila Prabhupada in Vrindavan. Srila Prabhupada had been speaking on specific points. At some moment, I got into the conversation and said, “Srila Prabhupada, your words remind me of the teacher I had when I was in the university.” When I said that, he stopped walking and looked at me with that same look, telling me, this better be a good story or else... Everybody stopped, and I told the story about how my philosophy professor changed his philosophy when he became sick. When I said that, Srila Prabhupada laughed and said, “We do not change our philosophy because we have become sick."

## Chapter 6. My sacred duty is to please him (1977)

## 1977, Mayapur, India

## No! You cannot create a perfect person!

Just as the Mayapur festival was beginning, we were in Srila Prabhupada’s room, and there was a God-brother of Srila Prabhupada’s who he was talking to. I noticed that Srila Prabhupada’s manner of talking to him was quite respectful. They discussed what went wrong with the Gaudiya Math after Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Saraswati Thakura left the planet.

Srila Prabhupada mentioned that Srila Bhaktisiddhanta wanted his disciples to form a GBC and cooperate unitedly in the preaching, but this instruction was not followed. Instead, they wanted to create an Acarya and became divided as to who that person would be. The two camps started fighting about who would control the different branches. Then it was taken to court, and the fighting continued in court.

Saying that Srila Prabhupada spoke with full authority and stated, “You cannot create a perfect person! No! Guru Maharaja wanted us to cooperate together and then a perfect person may come out. But you cannot create a perfect person. Because they tried to create this perfect person, everything was spoiled. This mistake was made”.

I remember Srila Prabhupada spoke with full authority about it, which is a crucial philosophical point that helped me over the years. Some people may claim to be an Acarya, but that is not an easy thing to be. Srila Prabhupada gave us the GBC as a workable system.

Although none of us individually may be able to run ISKCON according to the standards Srila Prabhupada expected, if we have faith in Srila Prabhupada, collectively, we can keep it on the right course. I saw that Srila Prabhupada was very strict on that point about not trying to create somebody artificially or voting every year for another Acarya.

## Pray to Lord Krishna for his health.

Srila Prabhupada had a severe health crisis at the Mayapur Gaura Purnima festival in 1977. Generally, he was always giving classes and taking part in the kirtan, but this time, he didn’t even come out of his room because of health issues.

We were all advised to pray to Lord Krishna to improve Srila Prabhupada’s health. The recommended prayer was, “My dear Lord Krishna, if you so desire, please allow Srila Prabhupada’s health to improve.” I did manage to go into Srila Prabhupada’s room a few times, but there was little practical service I could render. Mostly we were with the other devotees engaged in kirtan and classes in the temple room or at the pandal set up for the festival.

Srila Prabhupada’s health did improve somewhat. He continued to travel, preach, and translate.

## 1977, Rishikesh, India

## Associating with the Pure Devotee

After the festival, I returned to my service at the New Delhi temple. In early May 1977, we got word that Srila Prabhupada was coming to Rishikesh from Bombay to get the fresh air and avoid the heat of the summer season. I decided to go and be with him, and with the permission of the temple president, I left New Delhi to meet Srila Prabhupada there.

Srila Prabhupada had been given a nice house right on the bank of the Ganga, and we found space to fit in there. Upon arriving, he had ordered his servant to take his lota and swim to the middle of the river and fill it. After returning, Srila Prabhupada drank that water and, after a minute, burped. He told us, “the burp meant the stomach had accepted the water.”

We were going daily with Srila Prabhupada’s books into the center of town to distribute them and invite people to come to meet Srila Prabhupada in the evening. There were always a good number of people attending. I remember one such meeting where Srila Prabhupada spoke quite heavily to a young man on some point of philosophy. There was a middle-aged couple there who did not like that Srila Prabhupada spoke so strongly to this boy, and they objected. Srila Prabhupada just tolerated their comments and remained silent. In retrospect, I feel that I should have said something in Srila Prabhupada’s defence at that moment, but unfortunately, I did not.

When we were at Rishikesh, every evening, a fair amount of people came to see Srila Prabhupada, and usually, they stayed over an hour. Many people had questions, and Srila Prabhupada always answered them with eloquence and wisdom.

## Vrindavan, India, 1977

## Returning to Vrindavan

After a few days, Srila Prabhupada’s health worsened, and he decided to go to Vrindavan because he wanted to leave his body there. Naturally, we accompanied him, and our party stopped at our temple in New Delhi for the night. As it was the hot season, he decided to sleep on the roof, so arrangements were made for a bed and mosquito net.

One new devotee took it on himself to stand guard near Srila Prabhupada’s bed, but this did not please him. I was resting nearby, and Srila Prabhupada gestured to me; I came to him, and then he asked me to tell this boy that he didn’t need to stand there. After the boy left, Prabhupada rested peacefully.

## Last Will

The next day our party arrived in Vrindavan. After Srila Prabhupada arrived back in Vrindavan in May 1977, he called for all his GBC men to come to be with him. During this period, Prabhupada spoke extensively about his desire on how the moment should go on after his departure, especially regarding initiations.

Then on June 4th, under the guidance of some lawyers, Srila Prabhupada signed his last will. By this time, Srila

Prabhupada was mainly lying in his bed. But for the signing, he sat up with the help of his personal servants.

He would also tell us to keep the Acarya in the center because Srila Prabhupada was fully aware of his position. He knew he was establishing this movement that would go on for the next ten thousand years. We can keep it going nicely if we sincerely follow his instructions. There will naturally be some differences, but it can be worked out if we all have the motive to please Srila Prabhupada.

Even if we are wrong, it can work out in time if we have this motive. If we make it our goal to assist Srila Prabhupada in his mission from whatever position, energy, or resources we have, then all differences will be resolved. This was Srila Prabhupada’s instruction... to keep the Acarya in the center.

## Prabhupada's medicine - kirtan and massage August 1977

Srila Prabhupada did not chant japa at that time because his body was very weak, and he did not have the strength for it. However, he always liked to have devotees singing kirtan in his room. He told us that kirtan and massage were his medicine. We came in shifts of two hours and played with small karatalas in the back of the room. There were no mridangas. The singing was done softly, day and night, even while he was sleeping, and only stopped when Srila Prabhupada wanted to speak.

## Prabhupada's final travelling

Srila Prabhupada stayed in Vrindavan until August 28th, when he flew to London. There was a lot of discussion, back and forth, on the wisdom of Srila Prabhupada’s travelling due to his illness, but in the end, he decided he wanted to go to the Gita Nagari farm in the USA to teach us how to live off the land. He never made it there because his health was deteriorating, and he returned to India from England on September 14th, landing in Bombay.

Then from Bombay, he returned to Vrindavan for the final time on October 2nd. We were there to receive him.

After returning to India, Srila Prabhupada said in front of everyone, “I want to give my special thanks to Tamal Krishna Maharaja for bringing me to London and back.” It is true: he was rendering wonderful service to Srila Prabhupada despite the fact that it was quite difficult. It has been said that Srila Prabhupada was 80% lenient with us, but in the last days, he was stricter. He would correct us very quickly. It was a difficult service Tamala was performing, and I remember thinking that I could not perform such expert service in such a difficult situation, “This service was too difficult for me to perform.” Tamal Krishna Maharaja was very intelligent.

## Prabhupada's final days in Vrindavan

When I saw Srila Prabhupada at this time, after a period of a little over a month, I was shocked. His body had become extremely thin, and he looked very weak. When Tamal Krishna Maharaja saw me, he told Srila Prabhupada that I was there and asked if he wanted me to sing. Srila Prabhupada consented. But I was so disturbed by seeing Srila Prabhupada’s physical condition that I did not sing well, and Srila Prabhupada asked me to stop. I was undoubtedly in maya because I identified Srila Prabhupada with his body and felt terrible that my singing did not please him.

Soon I came out of this maya, by his mercy, and realized that he was the same Srila Prabhupada and that his intelligence and awareness were as sharp as ever. I also realized that my sacred duty was to please him in any condition. So, we went back to the same routine of singing soft kirtan around the clock.

Once Srila Prabhupada stopped our singing and asked why more devotees were not there. Soon after returning to Vrindavan, he wrote a letter asking all his disciples from all over the world to come to be with him in his final days. Later, in Srila Prabhupada’s room, one senior disciple spoke and said, “Srila Prabhupada, I was not here because I had to go to Calcutta.” Srila Prabhupada responded, “Even when you are here, you are not here.” Implying that you are not in my room with me even when you are in Vrindavan.

## Speaking from the maha-bhagavata platform

A few days later, I got sick with a heavy cold and had a lot of coughing and mucus. I decided not to go again to Srila Prabhupada’s room until I recovered so as not to spread my sickness. After 4-5 days, I returned to Srila Prabhupada’s room. Srila Prabhupada recognized my voice and said, “Oh, you are still here!” I thought, “Of course, I am here. Where would I go?” But before I could speak, Srila Prabhupada said in an emotional voice, “I am simply a disturbance. You young men should be out preaching, but because of me, you have to stay here”.

In retrospect, I can see how he was speaking from the maha-bhagavata platform: the maha-bhagavata sees that everyone is serving Krishna except him. It pained me to hear Srila Prabhupada speak like this, and I did not know how to respond. Then he told me, “Call everyone to come.”

## He was determined to go no matter what (November, 1977)

Soon the room was packed full of devotees. Many of the leaders, however, were not there. They had gone back to their zones. I remember sitting in Srila Prabhupada’s room and two or three leaders, among whom Bhagavan Prabhu was the most prominent, came in to tell Srila Prabhupada that they were leaving.

At that time, I was sitting close to Srila Prabhupada and looking into his face. I saw that his first reaction was that he was not happy that they were leaving. But the plea was that we are going back to preach. We need to keep our centers going. Srila Prabhupada did not check them. In this way, many of them left.

Srila Prabhupada announced that as the festival of Lord Krishna’s pastime of lifting Govardhana Hill was coming soon, he wanted to go by bullock cart to circumambulate the Hill. He said staying in one room for a long time was unhealthy, so he wanted to go out.

Then an extensive discussion started. Many devotees, including me, were not in favour of his going. Bhakti Charu Maharaja said, “But Srila Prabhupada, your body is so frail; how will you be able to tolerate the rough ride of a bullock cart?” Srila Prabhupada replied, “We can take a mattress.”

Like this, the discussion went on for 15-20 minutes. I also said, “Srila Prabhupada, now the cold season is here; how will you stay warm at night?” He responded by saying, “We can stay under a tree.”

Then Srila Prabhupada was told that his doctor disapproved of the idea of circumambulating Govardhana hill, but Srila Prabhupada was determined to go. Lokanath Swami was deputed to go to Mathura to arrange for a bullock cart, and he asked me to go with him. The following morning the bullock cart was there.

## Don't speak out of turn

Sometimes I said inappropriate things, and Srila Prabhupada would not hesitate to chastise me, even in those final days when he was lying on his bed. He could not even move, but when anyone said something inappropriate, Srila Prabhupada immediately showed his displeasure.

I remember I was in an adjoining room with Tamal Krishna Maharaja, Srila Prabhupada’s secretary, and Srila Prabhupada called him to ask something. I also came into his room. Srila Prabhupada requested Tamal Krishna about a doctor from Calcutta who was supposed to come. One devotee, Satyadhanya Prabhu, was in charge of bringing him here. He spoke about that for a few minutes and asked Tamal, “Has he left yet?”

I didn’t know that he had changed topics, and I thought he was still talking about the doctor. I should not have spoken, but I jumped into the conversation and said, “Yes, Srila Prabhupada.” Suddenly everyone became quiet. Tamal Krishna was silent, and Srila Prabhupada looked at me and said, “Who did you think I meant?”

I realized I was on thin ice and started back-pedaling and saying, “ I guess I was just speculating, Srila Prabhupada.” But he would not let me off the hook. He just kept on pushing. He held me by the shirt, not physically, but with his words. Thus, I had to say that I was talking about the doctor.

Srila Prabhupada, however, did not say anything. He was lying on his bed; he could hardly move, but when I said that, he just turned on his bed and gave me the cold shoulder. And I felt it go right into my heart, and I thought, “Oh, my God! What have I done? Why am I disturbing my spiritual master, especially on these days when he was so sick? How could I offend him?” I was quiet after that.

Then Tamal Krishna Maharaja wisely said, “Who were you referring to, Srila Prabhupada?” Srila Prabhupada replied, “My son.” His son had also been there, and he was due to leave.

Recalling this situation, I see I was wrong because Srila Prabhupada spoke to his secretary, and I should not have interfered in their conversation. Then, after 10-15 minutes of discussion, Tamal asked me to sing. I started to sing the bhajan “Yasomati-nandana braja-baro-nagara”. Srila Prabhupada liked it. He saw that I was singing with feeling. I was practically crying after being crushed. Even now, whenever I sing it, I remember how Srila Prabhupada corrected me for speaking out of turn.

## See how much they love me

When I entered Srila Prabhupada’s room that evening of November 10th, his God-brother Krishna das Babaji, whom Srila Prabhupada had invited to come, was sitting on Srila Prabhupada’s bed. They were talking softly to one another. At one point, his God-brother showed his amazement that Srila Prabhupada was so cogent and clear in his thoughts and speech. Srila Prabhupada appreciated that and pointed it out to us, his disciples. In other words, most people in that condition go into a coma, but Srila Prabhupada’s consciousness was very clear.

Then suddenly, Bhakti Charu Maharaja and Bhavananda Maharaja came into his room. They were both somewhat emotional. Bhakti Charu spoke first. He said, “Srila Prabhupada, Tamal Krishna Maharaja is very upset. He does not want you to go on the bullock cart, and neither do we. We think that it is wrong”. Bhavananda affirmed his agreement, “Yes, Srila Prabhupada, please, do not go.” Then Srila Prabhupada became sober and said, “I did not want to cause you to be disturbed. If you all do not want me to go, then I will not go”. Then turning to his God-brother, he said, “See how much they love me.”. On the strength of his three intimate servants, Tamal Krishna Maharaja, Bhavananda Maharaja, and Bhakti Charu Maharaja, and the advice of the kaviraja, Srila Prabhupada gave up the idea of going out of the temple on a bullock cart.

Going a little off the main topic, these three devotees later were accused of poisoning Srila Prabhupada. But how is it even possible to think something like that about them? Srila Prabhupada himself is clearly telling his Godbrother, “Just see how much they love me!”

So, it was settled that Srila Prabhupada would not go anywhere. I was happy about this decision, but a few devotees were thinking, “Oh, why is Tamal interfering? If Srila Prabhupada wants, let him go”. But in any case, he decided not to go.

## We all hoped

We were still very hopeful that Srila Prabhupada’s health would improve and he would be able to attend the opening of the Bombay temple, which was set for 1st January 1978. The kaviraja also reported good signs of improvement in Srila Prabhupada’s health. He was drinking more milk, and his heartbeat was stronger.

But on the morning of the 14th of November, that all changed. I woke early that morning and felt compelled to go immediately to Srila Prabhupada’s room, although it was not time for my chanting there. I met Giriraja Maharaja just outside of Srila Prabhupada’s room and asked why he was there so early. He responded that he felt inspired to come for some reason. I felt the same way. I did not have any business in Srila Prabhupada’s room then, but somehow I felt pushed to go there.

Then we both entered his quarters, and we saw that there was a significant change. Srila Prabhupada had lost external consciousness and was making a peculiar sound like "Umm”. The doctor was there, and we asked him, “What is this sound that Srila Prabhupada is making?” This doctor was a devotee, and he said that this sound is the sign of ecstasy that the soul is meeting the Supersoul. It means that Srila Prabhupada was experiencing ecstatic symptoms. The kauiraja said that Srila Prabhupada’s heart was failing and that he would leave his body soon. Of course, we were devastated. Instead of a soft kirtan, a loud one was started, and we all joined in. Srila Prabhupada remained with us the whole day. Without regaining external consciousness, at 7:30 p.m., he departed.

## I was feeling a great personal loss, but I did not feel lost

After his departure, Srila Prabhupada’s personal servants, headed by Upendra Prabhu, asked us all to leave the room as they began to bathe and dress Srila Prabhupada’s body. We retired for the night and saw in the morning his body had been placed on the vyasasana in the temple. We had mangala-arati there, and later in the morning, around 7 or 8 a.m., Srila Prabhupada’s body was carried by palanquin throughout Vrindavan town.

We stopped at all the main temples, and the priests and brahmins all came out to offer their respects. I remember thinking at the time that it is good that they are offering some formal respects to Srila Prabhupada. However, in reality, they did not understand his actual position as the sakty-avesa empowered Acarya. It is evident how Vrindavan has developed so much over the years since Srila Prabhupada’s departure.

Upon returning to the Krishna Balarama temple, we began the ceremony of placing Srila Prabhupada’s body in samadhi. A large hole had been dug at the property’s front side, and his body was carefully placed there and then completely covered with salt. The pure devotee’s body is not burned, as is the general custom, because it is considered pure, and therefore, it is preserved in salt.

After a few days, I left India as my visa was expiring. I remember feeling a great personal loss with Srila Prabhupada’s passing, but I did not feel lost. Srila Prabhupada had left us his books, instructions, and institution. Now our sincerity to continue to serve Srila Prabhupada in separation would be tested.

## After word

After Srila Prabhupada’s passing, we all understood that he wanted that the eleven gurus he had chosen to be his ritvik gurus would now become regular gurus and have their own disciples. This was fully accepted throughout ISKCON.

However, problems started because of how the new gurus assumed their role. Instead of following in Srila Prabhupada’s footsteps, they began to imitate him. They insisted on having daily guru pujas for themselves, which meant that their God-brothers were obliged to worship them at the same time and place where we were worshipping Srila Prabhupada.

Also, many of them insisted on large vyasasanas, and Vyasa-puja books printed for their birthdays. All of which had to be made by their God-brothers because they did not have any disciples yet. Then honorific titles like Vishnupada and Acaryapada were adopted by most.

I took a leading role in what was called “guru reform”. I began to push the point that the new gurus were wrong in imitating Srila Prabhupada instead of following in his footsteps. Thus, I became persona non grata in many temples. In 1979 I stayed in the Berkeley temple, where Hamsadutta Maharaja was in charge. He approached me and said, “Either you have to go, or I have to go, and these people love me, so you better leave.”

I went to Los Angeles, where Ramesvara Maharaja was in charge. He also complained about me and wrote to the other GBC men asking, “If my service was travelling all over the world to find fault with the gurus?”

I decided to return to India, where I had been preaching before Srila Prabhupada departed from this world. I landed in Bombay, and Kirtanananda Maharaja was there. He knew my position on the guru issue as I had gone to New Vrindavan earlier and complained to him about it. At that time, his response was, “The guru is one”. I was obliged to meet him in Bombay by no less a person than Giriraja Maharaja. He told me that he had surrendered to Kirtanananda and was happy he had done so.

I met Kirtanananda, but I was not inspired to surrender to him. I then left Bombay and returned to New Delhi, where I had been serving before moving to Vrindavan to be with Srila Prabhupada in his last days. That night I had a very vivid dream.

In that dream, Srila Prabhupada was sitting on a raised platform with Kirtanananda Maharaja in front of him on his left side, and I was in front on his right. All the leading devotees of ISKCON were in attendance. Kirtanananda was speaking. He was criticizing me for making a disturbance in ISKCON.

In mid-sentence, Srila Prabhupada spoke over him. He said that I was right about the new gurus acting improperly and that they should not be artificially demanding so much respect from their God-brothers. He went on to say that all his disciples could be spiritual masters, but for practical working, he had chosen these eleven. He added that he never wanted this big fanfare for these eleven.

Then (still in the dream), Srila Prabhupada left the scene. Devotees came over to me with smiles saying how I had really gotten Srila Prabhupada’s mercy. One of those devotees I remember clearly was Bhagavan Prabhu. When the dream ended, I awoke feeling satisfied and happy that Srila Prabhupada had come to me in this way. I was still determined to push the issue but in a less confrontational way.

I then travelled back to Chandigarh, North India, where I also served. The temple president there had left his post under unusual circumstances with only three rupees left in the bank account. I assumed the position of temple president and remained there for a couple of years. From there, I wrote letters to all the gurus and GBCs. This was in 1980-1981; no one was very receptive to my points. However, in 1982, starting with Hamsadutta, some gurus began to fall down.

In 1983 I returned to the USA and met with other Godbrothers who were also concerned about the guru issue. Gradually more and more devotees saw that this was a serious problem, and we began to meet to discuss how to improve the situation. As more gurus were unable to maintain their spiritual standards, the issue was taken up by the GBC.

In 1987, the full GBC body made significant reforms to the guru issue at the Mayapur festival. Many more gurus were added. My GBC man was Atreya Rishi Prabhu. I had settled in San Francisco, and he added my name to the list of devotees becoming new gurus.

Srila Prabhupada had personally advised us to “Keep the Acarya in the center”, now we all saw clearly the need for this to keep ISKCON healthy and united. Since then, I have been preaching in various places, mainly in Eastern Europe and the USA.

The guru system seems to be working fine, but still, we are missing Srila Prabhupada’s personal presence and his ability to give the final resolution to any conflict that arises. Naturally, in a large organization, there are bound to be differences of opinion. One such difference that has appeared is whether women can also become diksa-gurus. We pray that Srila Prabhupada will make his opinion on this subject known clearly.

His insignificant servant,

Trivikrama Swami
