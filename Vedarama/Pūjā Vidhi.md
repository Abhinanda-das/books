# Pūjā Vidhi

## Pūjā Vidhi

*prātaḥ prabodhito viṣṇo*  
*hṛṣīkeśena yat tvayā*  
*yad yat karayas īśāna*  
*tat karomi tavājñayā*

**Перевод:**

**"O Viṣṇu, O Iśāna, O Hṛṣīkeśa, You have awakened me, and now I am ready to do whatever You wish today" (*****Viṣṇu-dharmottara,*** **quoted in the** ***Hari-bhakti-vilāsa*** **\[3.91\]).**

Before rising you may pray to Mother Earth:

*samudra-vasane devi*  
*parvata-stana-maṇḍite*  
*viṣṇu-patni namas tubhyaṁ*  
*pāda-sparśaṁ kṣamasva me*

**Перевод:**

**"O Mother Earth, I offer my humble obeisances unto you, who are the wife of Lord Viṣṇu and the residence of the oceans, and who are decorated with mountains. Please forgive me for stepping upon you."**

## 3 - Bhūta-nivaraṇam

*ye bhūtā bhuvi saṁsthitāḥ*  
*ye bhūta-vighna-kartāras*  
*te gacchanty ājñyayā hareḥ*

**Перевод:**

**"May all inauspicious subtle beings that may obstruct our service be gone by the order of the Lord."**

Chant *oṁ astrāya phaṭ* and strike your left heel on the ground three times to dispel subtle beings from earth.

While chanting the *mūla-mantra* of the main Deity you will worship, from the corners of your eyes glance angrily over the paraphernalia and upward. This will dispel subtle beings in the higher realms.

Chant *oṁ astrāya phaṭ* and snap the fingers of your right hand over your head in the ten directions to dispel subtle beings in the atmosphere.

## 4 - Asana-sthāpana

Chant *om astrāya phaṭ* and purify the floor by *prokṣaṇa*.  
Draw a triangular *maṇḍala* on the floor with water or *candana*, using the knuckle of your right middle finger.  
Worship the *maṇḍala* with flower petals and *candana* as you chant:

*ete gandha-puṣpe- oṁ ādhāra-śaktaye namaḥ*  
*om anantāya namaḥ*  
*oṁ kūrmāya namaḥ*

Place the ā*sana* over the *maṇḍala*.  
While touching the ā*sana,* chant the *viniyoga mantra:*

*(oṁ) āsana-mantrasya*  
*meru-pṛṣṭha ṛṣiḥ*  
*sutalaṁ chandaḥ*  
*kūrmo devatā*

Holding the hands in the *praṇāma-mudrā,* chant:

*pṛthvi tvayā dhṛtā lokā*  
*devi tvaṁ viṣṇunā dhṛtā*  
*tvaṁ ca dharāya māṁ nityaṁ*  
*pavitraṁ cāsanaṁ kuru*

**Перевод:**

**"O Mother Earth, O goddess, you support all the world's people, and Lord Vi\\s\\nu sustains you; please give your support to me continuously and provide me with a pure seat upon which to sit while worshiping the Lord."**

Draw a triangle on the ā*sana* with water (or just with your knuckle), and then worship this *maṇḍala* with flower petals and *candana* while chanting:

*ete gandha- puṣpe – oṁ ādhāra-śaktaye namaḥ*  
*oṁ kamalāsanāya namaḥ*

## 6 - Guru-paṅkti-namskāra

Draw the spiritual master's attention by placing flowers before him while chanting:

*eṣa puṣpāñjalī -aiṁ gurave namaḥ*

Then chant:

*śrī-guro paramānanda*  
*premānanda phala-prada*  
*vrajānanda pradānanda*  
*sevayāṁ mā niyojaya*

**Перевод:**

**"My dear spiritual master, who give the fruit of the highest bliss--the bliss of love of Godhead--please engage me in the blissful service of Kṛṣṇa, who bestows bliss upon the land of Vraja."**

## 7 - Kara śuddhi

## 8 - Puṣpa-śuddhi

Chant *oṁ astrāya phaṭ* and sprinkle the flowers with *samānya-arghya* water. Then show the *cakra-* and *dhenu-mudrās* over the flowers.  
While touching the flowers with the fingers of your right hand in the *bījākṣara-mudrā,* chant:

*oṁ puṣpe puṣpe mahā-puṣpe*  
*su-puṣpe puṣpa-sambhave*  
*puṣpe cāyavākīrṇe*  
*ca huṁ phaṭ svāhā*

**Перевод:**

**"O flowers, O great and auspicious flowers who have appeared from a budding creeper, may you be purified" (*****Rg Veda*****).**

Then show the *matsya-mudrā* over the flowers.

## 9 - Dravya-śuddhi

## 10 - Atmā-śuddhi

Sprinkle oneself with water while chanting the *Gopāla Mantra.*

## 11 - Dig bandhanam

Chant *oṁ astrāya phaṭ* and strike your left palm three times with your right forefinger and middle finger. Raise your hands as you do this.  
Show the *cakra-mudrā,* and then snap the fingers of your right hand in the ten directions, beginning from the east and going clockwise.

## 12 - Vahni prakārana

Chant the *agni-bīja* (*ram*) and meditate on a wall of fire spreading all around and over your head, protecting you and the Deity room from all bad influences. At the same time show the *agni-bījākṣara-mudrā.* To show this *mudrā,* keep your right index finger straight, clench the remaining fingers of your right hand into a fist, and move your right hand clockwise in a complete circle around your head.

## 13 - Kara-nyāsa

As you show the *nyāsa-mudrā,* chant the *gopāla-mantra* once.  
Chant *klīṁ aṅguṣṭhābhyāṁ namaḥ* and move your forefingers along the length of your thumbs, from the bases to the tips.  
Chant *kṛṣṇāya tarjanībhyām svāhā* and move your thumbs along the length of your index fingers, from the bases to the tips.  
Chant *govindāya madhyamābhyāṁ vaśaṭ* and move your thumbs along the length of your middle fingers, from the bases to the tips.  
Chant *gopījana anamikābhyāṁ huṁ* and move your thumbs along the length of your ring fingers, from the bases to the tips.  
Chant *vallabhāya kaniṣṭhikābhyāṁ vauṣaṭ* and move your thumbs along the length of your little fingers, from the bases to the tips.  
Chant *svāhā astrāya kara-tala-kara-pṛṣṭhābhyāṁ phaṭ* and touch your right palm to the back of your left hand, then your left palm to the back of your right hand.

## 14 - Aṅga-nyāsa

Chant *klīṁ hṛdayāya namaḥ* and touch your heart with your right palm.  
Chant *kṛṣṇāya śirase svāhā* and touch the top of your head with the fingertips of your right hand.  
Chant *govindāya śikhāyai vaśaṭ* and touch your *śikhā* with your right fist, thumb pointing down toward your neck.  
Chant *gopīijana kavacāya hum* and touch your left upper arm with the fingertips of your right hand and your right upper arm with the fingertips of your left hand.  
Chant *vallabhāya netrābhyāṁ vauṣaṭ* and touch your eyelids with the middle finger and forefinger of your right hand.  
Chant *svāhā astrāya phaṭ* and snap your fingers three times with your right hand as you move it clockwise around your head.  
Show the *cakra-mudrā.*

## 15 - Prāṇāyama

First, chant the *viniyoga mantra*:

*oṁ kāma-bījasya prajāpati ṛṣiḥ*  
*daivī-gāyatrī chandaḥ*  
*śrī kṛṣṇa-caitanyo devatā*  
*ka-kāro bījaṁ*  
*la-kāro kīlakaṁ*  
*i-kāro śaktiḥ*  
*prāṇāyāme viniyogaḥ*

Then one should perform the *prāṇāyāma nyāsa*:

*oṁ kāma-bījāya namaḥ*

*prajāpati-ṛṣaye namaḥ –* (touch head)  
*daivī-gāyatrī chandase namaḥ –* (touch śikhā)  
*śrī kṛṣṇa-caitanya-devatyai namaḥ –* (touch face)  
*ka-kāra-bījātmamane namaḥ –* (touch heart)  
*la-kāra kīlakātmane namaḥ –* (touch right nipple)  
*i-kāra śaktyātmane namaḥ –* (touch left nipple)  
*prāṇayāma-viniyogāya namaḥ –* (touch heart)  
Chant the *kāma-bīja* 16 times inhaling; then 64 times holding the breath and 32 times exhaling.  
Counting in this way, one should begin by inhaling through the left nostril, blocking the right nostril, holding the breath, and then exhaling through the right nostril, blocking the left. Then one should inhale through the right nostril, hold the breath, and exhale through the left nostril, using the same count. This should be repeated three times.

## 16 - Bhūta-śuddhi

*nāhaṁ vipro na ca nara-patir nāpi vaiśyo na śūdro*  
*nāhaṁ varṇī na ca gṛha-patir no vanastho yatir vā*  
*kintu prodyan-nikhila-paramānanda-purṇāmṛtābdher*  
*gopīi-bhartuḥ pāda-kāmalayor dāsa-dāsānudāsaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**"I am not a** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **I am not a** ***kṣatriya,*** **I am not a** ***vaiṣya*** **or a** ***śūdra*****. Nor am I a** ***brahmacārī,*** **a householder, a** ***vanaprastha,*** **or a** ***sannyāsī*****. I identify myself only as the servant of the servant of the servant of the lotus feet of Lord Kṛṣṇa, the maintainer of the** ***gopīs*****. He is like an ocean of nectar, and He is the cause of universal transcendental bliss. He is always existing with brilliance" (*****Padyāvali*** **74).**

## 17 - Ghaṇṭa pūjā

While offering the bell a flower petal dipped in *candana,* chant:

*ete gandha-puṣpe – oṁ jaya-dhvani-mantra-mataḥ svāhā*

Now fasten the flower petal to the body of the bell with the *candana*. Then ring the bell briefly with your left hand and replace it on the plate, all the while thinking of the bell as a servant of the Lord. Then chant:

*sarva-vadya-mayi ghaṇṭe*  
*deva-devasya vallabhe*  
*tvāṁ vinā naiva sarveśāṁ*  
*śubhaṁ bhāvati śobhane*

## 18 - Saṅkha-sthāpana / pūjā

With water from the *samānya-arghya pātra* or with *candana,* draw a small equilateral triangle with one of its angles pointing away from you. Similarly, draw a circle around the triangle and then a square around the circle.  
Chant *oṁ astrāya phaṭ* and sprinkle the conch stand with *samānya-arghya* water.  
Chant *oṁ ādhāra-śaktaye namaḥ* and place the stand on the *maṇḍala*.  
Chant *oṁ astrāya phaṭ* and sprinkle the conch with *samānya-arghya* water. Place the conch on the stand.  
Chant *oṁ hṛdayāya namaḥ* and place flower petals and *candana* into the conch.  
Chant *oṁ śirase svāhā* and pour water from the waterpot into the conch.  
While offering flowers and *candana* to the Lord's bathing conch, chant:

*ete gandha-puṣpe- oṁ huṁ huṁ huṁ namaḥ mahā-śaṅkhāya svāhā*

One should chant over the *śaṅkha* three times:

*oṁ pañcajanyāya vidmahe*  
*mahodarāya dhīmahi*  
*tanno śankhaḥ pracodayāt*  
*nirviśikāraṇārtham takśya mūdra pradarśiyāmi -show Garuḍa ṁudrā*  
*amṛtikāraṇārtham dhenu mūdram pradarśayāmi –show dhenu mudrā*  
*pavitrakāraṇārtham śankha mūdram pradarśayāmi - show śaṅkha mudrā*  
*surakṣanārtham cakra mūdram pradarśayāmi –show cakra mudrā*

*dig bandhanārtham gadā mūdram pradarśayāmi –* show *gadā mudrā*around the *śaṅkha*.  
Worship the Fire, Sun, and Moon *Maṇḍalas* (With Their Ten, Twelve and Sixteen Divisions) Situated Within the Stand, Conch, and Water.  
Chant:  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - oṁ māṁ vahni-maṇḍalāya daśa-kalātmane namaḥ* and offer a flower petal dipped in *candana* to the stand; then afix the petal to the stand.  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - oṁ aṁ arka-maṇḍalāya dvādaśa-kalātmane namaḥ* (place petal on śaṅkha)  
*ete gandha-puṣpe oṁ uṁ soma-maṇḍalāya ṣoḍaśa-kalātmane namaḥ* (place petal in water).  
Do *aṣṭākṣara mantra japa* 12 times holding between middle and first fingers  
a *tulasī* leaf and touching the *śankha.*  
The following mantra is said:

*śankhe candrārkabhyāṁ namaḥ \|*

*madhye vāruṇāya namaḥ* ( at the centre of the śankha Varuna is there.)  
*puṣṭe prajāpatāye namaḥ* (At the bottom Prajapathis is there)  
*āgre gaṅgā sarasvatībhyāṁ namaḥ* . (śankhas at the end)

*trilokigata saṁasta devatābhyo namaḥ \|*  
*āvāhayāmi*

Offer the following items with water to the *śaṅkha*:

*āsanam sa*  
*arghyaṁ sa*  
*padyam sa*  
*ācamanīyam sa*  
*snānīyam sa*  
*vastram sa*  
*gandham sa*  
*tulasī-puṣpāṇi sa*  
*naivedyam sa*  
*āratikyam sa*

Chant the following *stuti*:

*śaṅkhaṁ candrārka daivataṁ*  
*madhye varuṇa devatāṁ*  
*pṛṣṭhe prajāpatiṁ vindyādh*  
*agre gangā sarasvatīṁ*  
*tvaṁ purā sāgarotpanno*  
*viṣṇunā vidhṛtaḥ kare*  
*mānitaḥ sarva-devaiś ca*  
*pāñcajānya namo 'stu te*

**Перевод:**

**"O Pāñcajānya, obeisances unto you, who were born from the ocean! Long ago Lord Viṣṇu seized you in His hand, and thus all the demigods honor you."**

*tava nādena jīmūtā*  
*vitrāsyanti surāsuraḥ*  
*sa-śaṅkha-yuta-diptābha*  
*pāñcajānya namo 'stu te*

**Перевод:**

**"O Pāñcajānya, obeisances unto you, who shine brilliantly like the moon! Your roaring sound makes the mountains, clouds, demigods, and demons tremble in fear."**

*garbhā devāri-nārīṇāṁ*  
*vilayante sahasradhā*  
*tava nādena pātāle*  
*pāñcajānya namo 'stu te*

**Перевод:**

**"O Pāñcajānya, obeisances unto you! Your roaring sound shatters into thousands of pieces the wombs of the demons' wives living in the lower planets."**

*pāñcajānya nija-dhvandva*  
*sthapatākasa nicaya*  
*pāhi māṁ narākād ghorāt*  
*saṁsāraṅrnatāraṇa*  
*śaṅkha devatābhyo namaḥ*  
*sakala pūjārthe akṣatān samarpayāmi*

## 19 - Viśeṣa-arghya-sthāpana

Purify the bathing water with *samānya-arghya* water and *mudrās*. Then call the Lord into the water with the *āvāhana mudrās.* Then perform *gopāla-nyāsa* on the water and worship with 5 *upacāras.*

## 20 - Pīṭha-pūjā

With *candana,* draw a lotus-shaped *maṇḍala* on the *pīṭha,* the place where the Lord will stand for bathing. Then (again with *candana*) write in the center of the *maṇḍala* the *bīja* syllable of the *mūla-mantra* of the Deity being worshiped (use Devanagari script if possible). Now ring the bell and offer flower petals with *candana* as follows:  
Offer petals to the left of the *maṇḍala* and chant:

*ete gandha-puṣpe – aiṁ gurave namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe – oṁ sikṣa-gurubhyo namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - oṁ parama -guruve namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - oṁ paratpara -guruve namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - oṁ mahā -guruve namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - oṁ parameṣṭhi -guruve namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe – oṁ sarva- vaiṣṇavebhyo namaḥ*

Offer petals inside the *maṇḍala* and chant:

*ete gandha-puṣpe – oṁ ādhāra-śaktaye namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe – oṁ anantāya namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - sūrya maṇḍalāya namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - sūrya maṇḍalādhipataye braḥmaṇe namaḥ*  
*ete gandha-puṣpe - soṁa maṇḍalāya namaḥ*

## 21 - Saṅkalpa

One should recite the following *saṅkalpa:*

*oṁ govinda govinda govinda*  
*oṁ tat sat*  
*adya-*  
*māse*  
*tithau*  
*pakṣe*  
*śrī guru-gaurāṅga gandharvikā-giridhari sudarśana sālāgrāma pūjā kariṣye*

He should then throw flower petals in the NE direction.

*oṁ tad viṣṇoḥ paramaṁ padaṁ*  
*sadā paśyanti sūrayaḥ*  
*divīva cakṣur ātatam*

**Перевод:**

**“Just as those with ordinary vision see the sun’s rays in the sky, so the wise and learned devotees always see the supreme abode of Lord Viṣṇu. Because those highly praiseworthy and spiritually awake** ***brāhmaṇas*** **can see that abode, they can also reveal it to others.” (*****Sāma Veda*****)**

## 22 - Svasti-vācana

*yaṁ brahma vedānta-vido vadanti*  
*pare pradhānaṁ puruṣaṁ tathānye*  
*viśvad gateḥ kāraṇam-īśvaraṁ vā*  
*tasmai namo vighna-vināśanāya*

**Перевод:**

**“Obeisances unto Him who is the destroyer of all obstacles, who the knowers of Vedānta describe as the Supreme Brahman, and who others describe as the** ***pradhāna*****, or totality of mundane elements. Some describe Him as the Supreme Male Person, or** ***Puruṣa*****, while others describe Him as the Supreme Lord and the cause of the creation of the universe.” (*****Bṛhad Viṣṇu Purāṇa*****)**

*oṁ kṛṣṇo vai sac-cid-ānanda-ghanaḥ*  
*kṛṣṇa ādi puruṣaḥ*  
*kṛṣṇaḥ puruṣottamaḥ*  
*kṛṣṇo hā u karmādi mūlaṁ*  
*kṛṣṇaḥ saha sarvai-kāryaḥ*  
*kṛṣṇa kāśaṁ kṛd-ādīśā mukha-prabhu-pūjyaḥ*  
*kṛṣṇo nādis-tasmin-ajāndāntar bāhye*  
*yam-mañgalaṁ tal-labhate kṛtī*

**Перевод:**

**“Lord Kṛṣṇa is the color of a new rain-cloud, therefore He is compared to a transcendental cloud full of eternity, bliss and cognizance. He is the original and Supreme Person. He is the origin of all activities and the one and only Lord of all. He is the worshipful Lord of the best of demigods, the controller of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva. Kṛṣṇa is without any beginning. Whatever auspiciousness is found within or beyond this universe the devotee obtains in Kṛṣṇa alone.”(*****Kṛṣṇopaniṣad*****)**

*oṁ svasti no govindaḥ*  
*svasti no ‘cyutānantau*  
*svasti no vāsudevo viṣṇur dadhātu*  
*svasti no nārāyaṇo naro vai*  
*svasti naḥ padmanābhaḥ puruṣottamo dadhātu*  
*svasti no viśvakseno viśveśvaraḥ*  
*svasti no hṛṣīkeśo harir dadhātu*  
*svasti no vainateyo hariḥ*  
*svasti no ‘njanāsuto hanūr bhāgavato dadhātu*  
*svasti svasti sumaṅgalaikeśo mahān*  
*śrī kṛṣṇaḥ saccidānanda ghanaḥ*  
*sarveśvareśvaro dadhātu*

**Перевод:**

**“May Lord Govinda, Acyuta, Ananta Śeṣa, Vāsudeva and Lord Viṣṇu bestow auspiciousness upon us. May Nara-Nārāyaṇa, Padmanābha and Puruṣottama bestow auspiciousness upon us. May Viśvaksena, the Lord of the universe, Hṛṣikeśa and Lord Hari bestow auspiciousness upon us. May Garuḍa and the son of Añjanā, who is the great devotee of Lord Rāma, Hanumān, bestow auspiciousness upon us. May the great and only Lord of auspiciousness, Śrī Kṛṣṇa, who is like a transcendental cloud full of eternity, knowledge, and bliss and who is the Lord of all the demigods, bestow upon us all prosperity and auspiciousness.” (*****Kṛṣṇopaniṣad*****)**

*karotu svasti me kṛṣṇa*  
*sarva lokeśvareśvaraḥ*  
*kārśnadayaś-ca kūrvantu*

Install *viloma-arghya* as follows:  
Fill a conch on a stand with water; perform *prokṣaṇa;* show the *cakra-*, *galinī-,* and *dhenu-mudrās*. Show the *bījākṣara-mudrā,* and over the water chant the Sanskrit alphabet backward (*viloma*):

*kṣaṁ haṁ saṁ śaṁ ṣaṁ vaṁ laṁ raṁ yaṁ*  
*maṁ bhaṁ baṁ phaṁ paṁ*  
*naṁ dhaṁ daṁ thaṁ taṁ*  
*ṇaṁ ḍhaṁ ḍaṁ ṭhaṁ ṭaṁ*  
*ñaṁ jhaṁ jaṁ chaṁ caṁ*  
*gaṁ ghaṁ haṁ khaṁ kam*  
*auṁ oṁ aiṁ eṁ ĪĪṁ Īṁ*  
*ṛṛṁ ṛṁ ūṁ uṁ īṁ Iṁ āṁ aṁ*

Show the *matsya-mudr\\a.*  
To offer the *viloma-arghya,* wave the conch toward the Lord's lotus feet chanting:

*pāda-traya-krama-krānta*  
*trailokyeśvara keśava*  
*tvāt-prasādād idaṁ toyaṁ*  
*pādyaṁ te 'stu janārdana*

**Перевод:**

**"O Keśava, Janārdana, and Trivikrama, Lord of the three worlds, by Your mercy let this water wash Your lotus feet."**

Pour out the *viloma-arghya* water into the *visarjanīya-pātra*.
