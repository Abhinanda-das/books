# Radha-Krsna-sahasra-nama \[San & Eng\]

## Introduction and Meditation

### Text 1

*om asya śrī-rādhā-mādhava-yugma-sahasra-nāma-stotra-mālā-mantrasya sanat-kumāra ṛṣir anuṣṭup-chandaḥ śrī-rādhā-mādhavau devate, klīṁ bījaṁ śrīṁ śaktiḥ hrīṁ kilakaṁ śrī-rādhā-mādhava-prīty-arthe jape viniyogaḥ.*

**Пословный перевод:**

om—Om; asya—of Him; śrī-rādhā-mādhava-yugma-sahasra-nāma-stotra-mālā-mantrasya—of the garland of prayers of the thousand names of zŚrī Rādha'-Kṛṣṇa; sanat-kumāra—Sanat-kumāra; ṛṣiḥ—sage; anuṣṭup-chandaḥ—in the anustup meter; śrī-rādhā-mādhavau—Śrī Śrī Rādha'-Kṛṣṇa; devate—the two Deities; klīm—Kīlm; bījam—the bīja syllable; śrīm—Śrīm; śaktiḥ—the potency; hrīm—hrīm; kilakam—the kilaka; śrī-rādhā-mādhava—of Śrī Śrī Rādha'-Kṛṣṇa; prīty-arthe—for the satisfaction; jape—in chanting; viniyogaḥ—angaged.

**Перевод:**

**Oṁ. Of this garland of prayers, which is the thousand names of Śrī Śrī Rādha'-Kṛṣṇa, the sage is Sanat-kumāra, the meter is anuṣṭup, the Deities are Śrī Śrī Rādha'-Kṛṣṇa, the bīja syllable is klīm, the śakti syllable is śrīm, and the kilaka syllable is hrīm. These prayers should be chanted to please Śrī Śrī Rādha'-Kṛṣṇa.**

### Text 2

*atha dhyānaṁ*  
*mādhuryānāṁ nidhānaṁ parama-rasa-ghanaṁ sac-cid-ānanda-rūpaṁ*  
*ramyaṁ ramyāmbudābhaṁ vimala-śaśi-mukhaṁ pakva-bimbādharoṣṭham*  
*pāṇau vaṁśīṁ dadhānaṁ kanaka-ruci-paṭa-prāpti-ramyaṁ kiśoraṁ*  
*dhyāne rādhā-mukhendau su-vihita-nayanaṁ brahma vedānta-mṛgyam*

**Пословный перевод:**

atha—now; dhyānam—meditation; mādhuryānām—of sweetnesses; nidhānam—the abode; parama-rasa-ghanam—filled with all sweetness; sat-cid-ānanda-rūpam—whose form is eternal and full of knowledge and bliss; ramyam—handsome and charming; ramyāmbudābham—like a handsome raincloud; vimala-śaśi-mukham—with a splendid moon face; pakva-bimba—ripe bimba fruits; adharoṣṭham—lips; pāṇau—hands; vaṁśīm—flute; dadhānam—placing; kanaka-ruci-pata-prāpti-ramyam—wearing galrments splendid like gold; kiśoram—youthful; dhyāne—in meditation; rādhā-mukhendau—at the moon of Śrī Rādhā's face; su-vihita-nayanam—fixing His eyes; brahma—the Supreme Personality of Godhead; vedānta-mṛgyam—sought by the Vedānta.

**Перевод:**

**I meditate on Śrī Kṛṣṇa, who is the resting place of all sweetnesses, whose form is eternal and full of knowledge and bliss, who is handsome and charming, who is splendid like a graceful raincloud, whose face is a splendid moon, whose lips are ripe bimba fruits, who carries a flute in His hand, who is gracefully dressed in golden garments, who is youthful, whose glance is placed on the moon of Śrī Rādhā's face, and who is the Supreme Personality of Godhead sought by the Vedānta scriptures.**

### Text 3

*śyāmāṁ go-rocanābhāṁ sphurad-asita-pata-prāpti-ramyāvaguṇthāṁ*  
*ramyāṁ venī-cikura-nikarālamba-pādāṁ kiśorīm*  
*tarjaṇy-aṅguṣṭha-yuktaṁ hari-mukha-kamale yuñjatīṁ nāga-vallī-*  
*parṇaṁ karṇāyutākṣīṁ tri-bhuvana-madhurāṁ rādhikāṁ bhavayāmi*

**Пословный перевод:**

śyāmām—beautiful; go-rocanābhām—splendid like gorocana; sphurad-asita-paṭa-prāpti-ramyāvaguṇthām—gracefully dressed in dark garments; ramyām—beautiful and charming; venī-cikura-nikarālamba-pādām—with braids down to Her feet; kiśorīm—youthful; tarjaṇy-aṅguṣṭha-yuktam—with Her forefinger; hari-mukha-kamale—on Lord Kṛṣṇa's lotus mouth; yuñjatīm—engaged; nāga-vallī-parṇam—betelnuts and leaves; karṇāyutākṣīm—eyes reaching to Her ears; tri-bhuvana—the three worlds; madhurām—sweet; rādhikām—Śrī Rādhā; bhavayāmi—I meditate.

**Перевод:**

**I meditate on Śrī Rādhā, who is beautiful and charming, gracefully dressed in dark garments, youthful, Her graceful braids reaching Her feet, Her forefinger placed on Lord Kṛṣṇa's lotus mouth as She offers Him betelnuts, Her eyes reaching almost to Her ears, and Her sweetness charming the three worlds.**

## The Names of Śrī Kṛṣṇa

### Text 4

*devakī-nandanaḥ śaurir*  
*vāsudevo balānujaḥ*  
*gadāgrajaḥ kaṁsa-mohaḥ*  
*kaṁsa-sevaka-mohanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Kṛṣṇa is the son of Devakī (devakī-nandana), the descendent of King Śūrasena (śauri), the son of Vasudeva (vāsudeva), the younger brother of Balarāma (balānuja), the elder brother of Gada (gadāgraja), the frustration of Kaṁsa (kaṁsa-moha), and the frustration of Kaṁsa's servants (kaṁsa-sevaka-mohana).**

### Text 5

*bhinnārgalo bhinna-lohaḥ*  
*pitṛ-bahyaḥ pitṛ-stutaḥ*  
*mātṛ-stutaḥ śiva-dhyeyo*  
*yamunā-jala-bhedanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He breaks locks and bolts (bhinnārgala). He breaks iron (bhinna-loha). He was carried by His father (pitṛ-bahya). His father offered prayers to Him (pitṛ-stuta). His mother offered prayers to Him (mātṛ-stuta). Lord Śiva meditates on Him (śiva-dhyeya). He parted the Yamunā's waters (yamunā-jala-bhedana).**

### Text 6

*vraja-vāsī vrajānandī*  
*nanda-bālo dayā-nidhiḥ*  
*līlā-bālaḥ padma-netro*  
*gokulotsava īśvara*

**Перевод:**

**He lives in Vraja (vraja-vāsī), is the bliss of Vraja (vrajānandī), is Nanda's son (nanda-bāla), is an ocean of mercy (dayā-nidhi), is a playful boy (līlā-bāla), has lotus eyes (padma-netra), is a festival of happiness for Gokula (gokulotsava), and is the Supreme Personality of Godhead (īśvara).**

### Text 7

*gopikā-nandanaḥ kṛṣṇo*  
*gopānandaḥ satāṅ-gatiḥ*  
*baka-prāṇa-haro viṣṇur*  
*baka-mukti-prado hariḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is a gopī's son (gopikā-nandana), is all-attractive (kṛṣṇa), is the bliss of the gopas (gopānanda), is the goal of the devotees (satāṅ-gati), takes away Baka's life (baka-prāṇa-hara), is all pervading (viṣṇu), gives liberation to Baka (baka-mukti-prada), and takes away all that is inauspicious (hari).**

### Text 8

*bala-dolāśaya-śayaḥ*  
*śyāmalaḥ sarva-sundaraḥ*  
*padma-nābhī hṛṣīkeśaḥ*  
*krīḍā-manuja-bālakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He sits on a swing (bala-dolāśaya-śaya), is dark (śyāmala), is supremely handsome (sarva-sundara), has a lotus navel (padma-nābhī), is the master of the senses (hṛṣīkeśa), and is a playful boy (krīḍā-manuja-bālaka).**

### Text 9

*līlā-vidhvasta-śakaṭo*  
*veda-mantrābhiṣecitaḥ*  
*yaśodā-nandanaḥ kānto*  
*muni-koṭi-niṣevitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He playfully kicked a cart (līlā-vidhvasta-śakaṭa), was bathed with Vedic mantras (veda-mantrābhiṣecita), is Yaśodā's son (yaśodā-nandana), is handsome (kānta), and is served by millions of sages (muni-koṭi-niṣevita).**

### Text 10

*nitya-madhuvanāvāsī*  
*vaikuṇṭhaḥ sambhavaḥ kratuḥ*  
*ramā-patir madhu-patir*  
*murārir madhusūdanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He always stays in Madhuvana (nitya-madhuvanāvāsī), is the Lord of Vaikuṇṭha (vaikuṇṭha), is the origin of all (sambhava), is the Vedic yajñas personified (kratu), is the husband of the goddess of fortune (ramā-pati), is the king of Mathura' (madhu-pati), is the killer of the Mura demon (murāri), and is the killer of the Madu demon (madhusūdana).**

### Text 11

*mādhavo māna-hārī ca*  
*śrī-patir bhū-dhara prabhuḥ*  
*bṛhad-vana-mahā-līlo*  
*nanda-sūnur mahāśanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is the husband of the goddess of fortune (mādhava), the remover of pride and arrogance (māna-hārī), the husband of the goddess of fortune (śrī-pati), the maintainer of the earth (bhū-dhara), the supreme master (prabhu), the person who enjoys pastimes in the great forest (bṛhad-vana-mahā-līla), the son of Nanda (nanda-sūnu), and the boy with a vigorous appetite (mahāśana).**

### Text 12

*tṛṇāvarta-prāṇa-hārī*  
*yaśodā-vismaya-pradaḥ*  
*trailokya-vaktraḥ padmākṣaḥ*  
*padma-hastaḥ priyaṅkaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is the person who took away Tṛṇāvarta's life (tṛṇāvarta-prāṇa-hārī), the person who filled Yaśoda' with wonder (yaśodā-vismaya-prada), who holds the three worlds in His mouth (trailokya-vaktra), whose eyes are lotus flowers (padmākṣa), whose hands are lotus flowers (padma-hasta), and who is very friendly and affectionate (priyaṅkara).**

### Text 13

*brahmaṇyo dharma-goptā ca*  
*bhū-patiḥ śrī-dharaḥ svarāṭ*  
*ajādhyakṣaḥ śivādhyakṣo*  
*dharmādhyakṣo maheśvaraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is the friend and protector of the brāhmaṇas (brahmaṇya), the protector of religion (dharma-goptā), the king of the earth (bhū-pati), the maintainer of the goddess of fortune (śrī-dhara), independent (svarāṭ), the master of Brahma' (ajādhyakṣa), the master of Śiva (śivādhyakṣa), the master of Yama (dharmādhyakṣa), and the supreme controller (maheśvara).**

### Text 14

*vedānta-vedyo brahma-sthaḥ*  
*prajāpatir amogha-dṛk*  
*gopī-karāvalambī ca*  
*gopa-bālaka-su-priyaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is known by the study of Vedānta (vedānta-vedya), stays with the brāhmaṇas (brahma-stha), is the master of all living entities (prajāpati), is infallible (amogha-dṛk), holds a gopī's hand (gopī-karāvalambī), and is the dear friend of the gopas (gopa-bālaka-su-priya).**

### Text 15

*bālānuyāyī balavān*  
*śrīdāma-priya ātmavān*  
*gopī-gṛhāṅgaṇa-ratir*  
*bhadraḥ su-śloka-maṅgalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is the gopa boys' companion (bālānuyāyī), powerful (balavān), the dear friend of Śrīdāma' (śrīdāma-priya), self-controlled (ātmavān), a boy who plays in the gopīs' courtyards (gopī-gṛhāṅgana-rati), auspicious (bhadra), and glorified with graceful prayers (su-śloka-maṅgala).**

### Text 16

*navanīta-haro bālo*  
*navanīta-priyāśanaḥ*  
*bāla-vṛndī marka-vṛndī*  
*cakitākṣaḥ palāyitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is a butter thief (navanīta-hara), a young boy (bāla), fond of eating fresh butter (navanīta-priyāśana), accompanied by many other other boys (bāla-vṛndī), acompanied by many monkeys (marka-vṛndī), the boy whose eyes showed His fear (cakitākṣa), and the boy who fled (palāyita).**

### Text 17

*yaśodā-tarjitaḥ kampī*  
*māyā-rudita-śobhanaḥ*  
*dāmodaro 'prameyātmā*  
*dayālur bhakta-vatsalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He was rebuked by Yaśoda' (yaśodā-tarjita), trembled (kampī), was charming as He pretended to weep (māyā-rudita-śobhana), was bound at the waist with a rope (dāmodara), is limitless (aprameyātmā), is kind (dayālu), and loves His devotees (bhakta-vatsala).**

### Text 18

*su-baddholūkhalo namra-*  
*śirā gopī-kadarthitaḥ*  
*vṛkṣa-bhaṅgī śoka-bhaṅgī*  
*dhanadātmaja-mokṣaṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He was tied to a grinding mortar (su-baddholūkhala), bowed His head (namra-śirā), was rebuked by a gopī (gopī-kadarthita), broke a tree (vṛkṣa-bhaṅgī), broke His devotee's sufferings (śoka-bhaṅgī), and gave liberation to Kuvera's sons (dhanadātmaja-mokṣaṇa).**

### Text 19

*devarṣi-vacana-ślāghī*  
*bhakta-vātsalya-sāgaraḥ*  
*vraja-kolāhala-karo*  
*vrajānanda-vivardhanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He was praised by Nārada (devarṣi-vacana-ślāghī), is an ocean of love for His devotees (bhakta-vātsalya-sāgara), made a great commotion in Vraja (vraja-kolāhala-kara), and filled Vraja with bliss (vrajānanda-vivardhana).**

### Text 20

*gopātmā prerakaḥ sākṣī*  
*vṛndāvana-nivāsa-kṛt*  
*vatsa-pālo vatsa-patir*  
*gopa-dāraka-maṇḍanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is the life of the gopas (gopātmā), their inspiration (preraka), the witness of all (sākṣī), a person who lives in Vṛndāvana (vṛndāvana-nivāsa-kṛt), the protector of the calves (vatsa-pāla and vatsa-pati), and the ornament of the gopa boys (gopa-dāraka-maṇḍana).**

### Text 21

*bāla-krīḍo bāla-ratir*  
*bālakaḥ kanakāṅgadī*  
*pītāmbaro hema-mālī*  
*maṇi-muktā-vibhūṣaṇaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He plays with the boys (bāla-krīḍa and bāla-rati), is a boy (bālaka), is decorated with golden armlets (kanakāṅgadī), is dressed in yellow garments (pītāmbara), and is decorated with a golden necklace (hema-mālī), and ornaments of pearls and jewels (maṇi-muktā-vibhūṣaṇa).**

### Text 22

*kiṅkiṇī kaṭakī sūtrī*  
*nūpurī mudritānvitaḥ*  
*vatsāsura-pati-dhvaṁsī*  
*bakāsura-vināśanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is decorated with tinkling ornaments (kiṅkiṇī), golden armlets (kaṭakī), flower garlands (sūtrī), anklets (nūpurī). and auspicious marks (mudritānvita). He killed Vatsāsura (vatsāsura-pati-dhvaṁsī), and Bakāsura (bakāsura-vināśana).**

### Text 23

*aghāsura-vināśī ca*  
*vinidrī-kṛta-bālakaḥ*  
*ādyaḥ ātma-pradaḥ saṅgī*  
*yamunā-tīra-bhojanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He killed Aghāsura (aghāsura-vināśī), awakened the boys (vinidrī-kṛta-bālaka), is the first cause (ādya), gives Himself to His devotees (ātma-prada), associates with His devotees (saṅgī), and eats lunch by the Yamunā's shore (yamunā-tīra-bhojana).**

### Text 24

*gopāla-maṇḍalī-madhyaḥ*  
*sarva-gopāla-bhūṣaṇaḥ*  
*kṛta-hasta-tala-grāsī*  
*vyañjanāśrita-śākhikaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is encircled by gopa boys (gopāla-maṇḍalī-madhya), is the ornament decorating all the gopa boys (sarva-gopāla-bhūṣaṇa), takes a morsel of food in His hand (kṛta-hasta-tala-grāsī), and rests under a tree (vyañjanāśrita-śākhika).**

### Text 25

*kṛta-bāhu-śṛṅga-yaṣṭir*  
*guñjālaṅkṛta-kaṇṭhakaḥ*  
*mayūra-piccha-mukuṭo*  
*vana-mālā-vibhūṣitaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He carries a horn and stick under His arm (kṛta-bāhu-śṛṅga-yaṣṭi), and wears a guñja' necklace (guñjālaṅkṛta-kaṇṭhaka), a peacock-feather crown (mayūra-piccha-mukuṭa), and a forest garland (vana-mālā-vibhūṣita).**

### Text 26

*gairikā-citrita-vapur*  
*nava-megha-vapuḥ smaraḥ*  
*koṭi-kandarpa-lāvaṇyo*  
*lasan-makara-kuṇḍalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**His limbs are decorated with pictures and designs drawn in mineral pigments from Govardhana Hill (gairikā-citrita-vapu), His form is like a monsoon cloud (nava-megha-vapu), He is like Kāmadeva (smara), He is more handsome than millions of Kāmadevas (koṭi-kandarpa-lāvaṇya), and He wears glittering shark-earrings (lasan-makara-kuṇḍala).**

### Text 27

*ājānu-bāhur bhagavān*  
*nidrā-rahita-locanaḥ*  
*koṭi-sāgara-gāmbhīryaḥ*  
*kāla-kālaḥ sadā-śivaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**His arms reach down to His knees (ājānu-bāhu), He is the supremely opulent Personality of Godhead (bhagavān), He never closes His eyes in sleep (nidrā-rahita-locana), He is deeper than millions of oceans (koṭi-sāgara-gāmbhīrya), He is the time that marks time's end (kāla-kāla), and He is always auspicious (sadā-śiva).**

### Text 28

*viriñci-mohana-vapur*  
*gopa-vatsa-vapur-dharaḥ*  
*brahmāṇḍa-koṭi-janako*  
*brahma-moha-vināśakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He manifested forms that bewildered the demigod Brahma' (viriñci-mohana-vapu), He manifested the forms of the boys and calves (gopa-vatsa-vapur-dhara), He is the father of millions of universes (brahmāṇḍa-koṭi-janaka), and He removed Brahmā's bewilderment (brahma-moha-vināśaka).**

### Text 29

*brahmā brahmeḍitaḥ svāmī*  
*śakra-darpādi-nāśanaḥ*  
*giri-pūjopadeṣṭā ca*  
*dhṛta-govardhanācalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He is the Supreme Brahman (brahmā), is worshiped by the demigod Brahma' (brahmeḍita), is the supreme master (svāmī), removed Indra's pride (śakra-darpādi-nāśana), ordered the worship of Govardhana Hill (giri-pūjopadeṣṭā), and held Govardhana Hill (dhṛta-govardhanācala).**

### Text 30

*purandareḍitaḥ pūjyaḥ*  
*kāmadhenu-prapūjitaḥ*  
*sarva-tīrthābhiṣiktaś ca*  
*govindo gopa-rakṣakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He was worshiped by Indra (purandareḍita), is the final object of worship (pūjya), was worshiped by a surabhi cow (kāmadhenu-prapūjita), was ritually bathed with the water of all holy streams (sarva-tīrthābhiṣikta), is the pleasure of the cows, land, and senses (govinda), and is the protector of the cowherd people (gopa-rakṣaka).**

### Text 31

*kāliyārti-karaḥ krūro*  
*nāga-patnīḍito virāṭ*  
*dhenukāriḥ pralambārir*  
*vṛṣāsura-vimardanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He troubled the Kāliya serpent (kāliyārti-kara and krūra), was worshiped by Kāliya's wives (nāga-patnīḍita), is the universal form (virāṭ), was Dhenukāsura's enemy (dhenukāri), was Pralambāsura's enemy (pralambāri), and crushed Ariṣṭāsura (vṛṣāsura-vimardana).**

### Text 32

*māyāsurātmaja-dhvaṁsī*  
*keśi-kaṇṭha-vidārakaḥ*  
*gopa-goptā dhenu-goptā*  
*dāvāgni-pariśoṣakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He killed Māyāsura's son (māyāsurātmaja-dhvaṁsī), ripped open Keśī's throat (keśi-kaṇṭha-vidāraka), protected the gopas (gopa-goptā), protected the cows (dhenu-goptā), and extinguished the forest fire (dāvāgni-pariśoṣaka).**

### Text 33

*gopa-kanyā-vastra-hārī*  
*gopa-kanyā-vara-pradaḥ*  
*yajña-patny-anna-bhogī ca*  
*muni-mānāpahārakaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He stole the gopīs' garments (gopa-kanyā-vastra-hārī), gave the gopīs a blessing (gopa-kanyā-vara-prada), ate the food offered by the yajña-brāhmaṇas' wives (yajña-patny-anna-bhogī), and removed the brāhmaṇa sages' pride (muni-mānāpahāraka).**

### Text 34

*jaleśa-māna-mathano*  
*nanda-gopāla-jīvanaḥ*  
*gandharva-śāpa-moktā ca*  
*saṅkhacūḍa-śiro-haraḥ*

**Перевод:**

**He removed Varuṇa's pride (jaleśa-māna-mathana), saved Nanda-gopa's life (nanda-gopāla-jīvana), freed a gandharva from a curse (gandharva-śāpa-moktā), and decapitated Śaṅkhacūḍa (saṅkhacūḍa-śiro-hara).**
