# Rama-raksa Stotram \[San & Eng\]

## Preface

*asya śrī rāma-rakṣā stotra mantrasya*  
*budhakaushika ṛṣiḥ*  
*śrī sītā-rāmacandro devatā*  
*anuṣṭubh chhandaḥ*  
*sītā śaktiḥ*  
*śrīmad hanumān kīlakam*  
*śrī rāmacandra prītyarthe rāma-rakṣā stotra jape viniyogaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The author of this hymn is Budhakaushika. The god is Sitā-Rāmacandra. The metre is anuṣṭubh. The power is Sitā, central pivot is Hanumān and usage is to recite.**

*atha dhyānam*  
*dhyāyedājānubāhuṁ dhṛtaśaradhanuṣaṁ baddhapadmāsanastham*  
*pītaṁ vāso vasānaṁ navakamaladalaspardhinetraṁ prasannam*  
*vāmāṅkārūḍha sītāmukhakamalamilallocanaṁ nīradābham*  
*nānālaṅkāradīptaṁ dadhatamurujaṭāmaśḍanaṁ rāmacandram*

**Перевод:**

**Thus begins the dhyāna of this mantra - One should meditate Rāma who has arms reaching his knees, who is holding a bow and arrow, who is seated in a lotus position, who is wearing yellow clothes, whose eyes compete with petals of a fresh lotus, who looks satisfied, whose eyes are fixed on lotus-like (pretty) face of Sitā sitting in his left lap, whose color is like that of rain cloud, who has adorned different jewelry, who is wearing hair reaching down to his thighs.**

### 1

*caritaṁ raghunāthasya śatakoṭi pravistaram . \\\\*  
*ekaikamakṣaraṁ puṁsāṁ mahāpātakanāśanam .. 1.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**The life story of Sri Rāma has a vast expanse and each and every letter of it is capable of destroying even the greatest sins of mankind. (1)**

### 2

*dhyātvā nīlotpalaśyāmaṁ rāmaṁ rājīvalocanam .*  
*jānakī lakṣmaṇo petaṁ jaṭāmukuṭamaṇḍitam .. 2.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Let us meditate on the lotus-eyed, dark-complexioned Rama who is well- adorned with a crown of hair and has Sita and Lakṣmana alongside. (2)**

### 3

*sāsitūṇadhanurbāṇapāṇīṁ naktaṅcarāntakam . \\\\*  
*svalīlayā jagatrātuṁ āvirbhūtaṁ ajaṁ vibhum .. 3.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**(Let us meditate Rāma) who has a sword in a receptacle and bow, and arrows, who destroyed the demons, who is not born but is incarnated to protect the world with his actions. (3)**

### 4

*rāmarakṣāṁ paṭhetprājñaḥ pāpaghnīṁ sarvakāmadām . \\\\*  
*śirome rāghavaḥ pātu bhālaṁ daśarathātmajaḥ .. 4.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**May the learned read the Rama-rakṣa Stotram', which destroys all sins and grants all desires. (Begin listing details of the body to be protected) May Rāma who is Raghu's descendant protect my head. May Rāma who is Dasharatha's son protect my forehead. (4)**

### 5

*kausalyeyo dṛśau pātu viśvāmitrapriyaśrutī . \\\\*  
*ghrāṇaṁ pātu makhatrātā mukhaṁ saumitrivatsalaḥ .. 5.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord Rāma who is Kausalyā's son, protect my eyes. Rāma who is favorite of Vishvāmitra protect my ears. Rāma who is savior of yajna protect my nose. Rāma who is affectionate to lakṣmana protect my mouth. (5)**

### 6

*jivhāṁ vidyānidhiḥ pātu kaṇṭhaṁ bharatavanditaḥ . \\\\*  
*skaṅdhau divyāyudhaḥ pātu bhujau bhagneśakārmukaḥ .. 6.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**May Rāma who is a sea of knowledge protect my tongue.**  
**May Rāma who is saluted by Bharata protect my neck.**  
**May Rāma who holds divine weapons protect my two shoulders.**  
**May Rāma who broke Shiva's bow protect my two upper arms. (6)**

### 7

*karau sītāpatiḥ pātu hṛdayaṁ jāmadagnyajit . \\\\*  
*madhyaṁ pātu kharadhvaṁsī nābhiṁ jāmbavadāśrayaḥ .. 7.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**May the Rama who is the husband of Sita protect my two hands.**  
**May Rama who conquered Parashurāma protect my heart.**  
**May Rama who killed the Rakṣasa named Khara protect my abdomen.**  
**May Rama who gave refuge to Jambavad protect my navel. (7)**

### 8

*sugrīveśaḥ kaṭī pātu sakthinī hanumatprabhuḥ . \\\\*  
*ūrū raghūttamaḥ pātu rakṣaḥkulavināśakṛt .. 8.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**May Rama who is master of Sugrīva protect my waist.**  
**May Rama who is master of Hanumān protect my two hips.**  
**May Rama who is the best of raghus and who destroyed the lineage of rākṣasa protect my two thighs. (8)**

### 9

*jānunī setukṛtpātu janghe daśamukhāntakaś . \\\\*  
*pādau bibhīṣaṇashrīdaH pātu rāmokhilaM vapuḥ .. 9.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**May Rama who built the bridge protect my two knees.**  
**May Rama who killed ten faced ones protect my two shins.**  
**May Ram awho gave the wealth to Vibhiṣaṇa protect my two feet.**  
**Thus may he protect my entire body. (9)**

### 10

*etām rāmabalopetāṁ rakṣāṁ yaḥ sukṛtī paṭhet . \\\\*  
*sa cirāyuḥ sukhī putrī vijayī vinayī bhavet .. 10.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**May the good man who reads this stotra', which has all the power of Rama, be blessed with long life, happiness, children, success and humility. (10)**

### 11

*pātālabhūtalavyomacāriṇacchhadmacāriṇaḥ . \\\\*  
*na draṣṭumapi śaktāste rakṣitaṁ rāmanāmabhiḥ .. 11.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**No one, who is wandering below the earth or on the earth or above the earth or those who wander surreptitiously changing their forms, will even be able to see the man protected by rāma-nāma. (let alone bring any harm to him). (11)**

### 12

*rāmeti rāmabhadreti rāmacandreti vā smaran . \\\\*  
*naro na lipyate pāpaiḥ bhuktiṁ muktiṁ ca vindati .. 12.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**No sin can attach to the man who sings the praise of the lord and he will prosper in this world and get salvation. (12)**

### 13

*jagajjaitreka mantreṇa rāma-nāmnābhirakṣitam . \\\\*  
*yaḥ kaṇṭhe dhārayetasya karasthāḥ sarvasiddhuyaḥ .. 13.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**One who wears this mantra of rāma-nāma that has conquered the world around his neck will have all the powers at his beck and call. (13)**

### 14

*vajrapañjaranāmedaṁ yo rāmakavacaṁ smaret . \\\\*  
*avyāhatājñaḥsarvatra labhate jayamaṅgalam .. 14.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**He who wears this armour called vajra-pañjara will be unscathed and will win victory everywhere. (14)**

### 15

*ādiṣṭavān yathā svapne rāma-rakṣāṁmimāṁ haraḥ . \\\\*  
*tathā likhitavān prātaḥprabhuddho budhakauśikaḥ .. 15.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Budhakaushika was commanded in his dream by Lord Shiva to compose this hymn and he did so, as soon as he awoke in the morning. (15)**

### 16

*ārāmaḥ kalpavṛkṣāṇām virāmaḥ sakalāpadām . \\\\*  
*abhirāmastrilokānāṁ rāmaḥ śrīmān sa naḥprabhuḥ .. 16.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Rama, who grants all desires, removes all obstacles and is the praise of all three worlds, is our Lord' indeed. (16)**

### 17

*taruṇau rūpasaṁ pannau sukumārau mahābalau . \\\\*  
*puṇḍarīkaviśālākṣau cīrakṛṣṇājināmbarau .. 17.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**May the two brothers ever protect us, who are young, handsome, lotus-eyed and bark and deer-skin dressed. (17)**

### 18

*phalamūlāśinau dāntau tāpasau brahmacāriṇau . \\\\*  
*putrau daśarathasyaitau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau .. 18.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**These two sons of Dasharatha, the brothers Rama and Lakṣmana, the ones who are subsisting on roots and fruits and practicing penance and celibacy… (18)**

### 19

*śaraṇyau sarvasattvānāṁ shreṣṭhau sarva-dhanuṣmatām . \\\\*  
*rakṣaḥ kulanihantārau trāyetāṁ no raghūttamau .. 19.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**…these two scions of Raghu protect us, the foremost among the archers, the destroyers of the demons and the refuge of all beings, (may they) protect us. (19)**

### 20

*āttasajjadhanuṣāviṣuspṛśāvakṣayāśuganiṣaṅgasaṅginau . \\\\*  
*rakṣaṇāya mama rāmalakṣmaṇāvagrataḥ pathi sadaiva gachhatām .. 20.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Rama and Lakṣmana (both), their bows pulled and ready, their hands on the arrows (packed) in ever full quivers (carried on their backs), may they always escort me in my path, for my protection. (20)**

### 21

*sannaddhaḥ kavacī khaḍgī cāpabāṇadharo yuvā . \\\\*  
*gachhanmanorathosmākaṁ rāmaṁ pātu salakṣmaṇaḥ .. 21.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Ever prepared and armed with sword, shield and bows and arrows and followed by Lakṣman , Rama is like our cherished thoughts come to life, may he (along with Lakṣman) protect us. (21)**

### 22

*rāmo dāśarathiḥ śūro lakṣmaṇānucaro balī . \\\\*  
*kākutsthaḥ puruṣaḥ pūrṇaḥ kausalyeyo raghūttamaḥ .. 22.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Rama, the scion of Raghu and the son of Dasaratha and Kausalya, and ever accompanied by Lakṣmana, is all powerful and is the perfect man (Lord). (22)**

### 23

*vedāntavedyo yajñeśaḥ purāṇapuruṣottamaḥ .*  
*jānakī-vallabhaḥ śrīmān aprameya parākramaḥ .. 23.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Rama, who is perceived thru vedānta, who is lord of all yajna, is ancient and the best man who is beloved of Jānakī and whose bravery is immeasurable. (23)**

### 24

*ityetāni japannityaṁ madbhaktaṁ ṇraddhayānvitaḥ . \\\\*  
*aśvamedhādhikaṁ puṇyaṁ saṁprāpnoti na saṁśayaḥ .. 24.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**A horse for a year and defeat all the kings whose kingdoms are visited by the horse - the horse is finally sacrificed and the kingdom won by the king is also offered in donation to the brahmins! My devotee (says Lord Shiva), who recites these (names of Rāma) with faith, will attain more religious merit than one obtainable by horse-sacrifice. There is no doubt about it. (24)**

### 25

*rāmaṁ durvādalaśyāmaṁ padmākṣaṁ pītavāsasam . \\\\*  
*stuvanti nāmabhirdivyaiḥ na te saṁsāriṇo naraḥ.. 25.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Those who sing the praise of Rāma (who is lotus-eyed, dark-complexioned and dressed in yellow clothes) thro' this hymn, are (no longer) ordinary men trapped in the world (they get liberated!) (25)**

### 26

*rāmaṁ lakṣmaṇapūrvajaṁ raghuvaraṁ sītā-patiṁ sundaram . \\\\*  
*kākutsthaṁ karuṇārṇavaṁ guṇa-nidhiṁ vipra-priyaṁ dhārmikam . \\\\*  
*rājendraṁ satyasandhaṁ daśaratha-tanayaṁ śyāmalaṁ śānta-mūrtim . \\\\*  
*vande lokābhirāmaṁ raghu-kula-tilakaṁ rāghavaṁ rāvaṇārim .. 26.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**I salute that Rama who is handsome, the elder brother of Lakṣmana, the husband of Sita and the best of the scions of the Raghu race, Him who is the ocean of compassion, the stockpile of virtues, the beloved of the Brahmans and the protector of Dharma, Him who is the practiser of the Truth, the lord emperor of kings, the son of Dasaratha, dark-complexioned and the personification of Peace and tranquility, Him who is the enemy of Ravana, the crown jewel of the Raghu dynasty and the cynosure of all eyes. (26)**

### 27

*rāmāya rāmabhadrāya rāmacandrāya vedhase . \\\\*  
*raghunāthāya nāthāya sītāyāḥ pataye namaḥ .. 27.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**I salute that Rama who is benevolent and cool as moon, and who is the lord of Sita and the master guardian of all. (27)**

### 28

*śrī rāma rāma raghunandana rāma rāma . \\\\*  
*śrī rāma rāma bharatāgraja rāma rāma . \\\\*  
*śrī rāma rāma raṇakarkaśa rāma rāma . \\\\*  
*śrī rāma rāma śaraṇaṁ bhava rāma rāma .. 28.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**I surender to that Rama who is the delight of the Raghus, elder brother of Bharata and the tormentor of his enemies in the war. (28)**

### 29

*śrī rāmacandra-caraṇau manasā smarāmi . \\\\*  
*śrī rāmacandra-caraṇau vacasā gṛnāmi . \\\\*  
*śrī rāmacandra-caraṇau śirasā namāmi . \\\\*  
*śrī rāmacandra-caraṇau śaraṇaṁ prapadye .. 29.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**The two feet of Rama, I remember them in my mind, I praise them by my speech, I bow to them by my head I take resort in them! (29)**

### 30

*mātā rāmo matpitā rāmacandraḥ . \\\\*  
*svāmī rāmo matsakhā rāmacandraḥ . \\\\*  
*sarvasvaṁ me rāmacandro dayāluḥ . \\\\*  
*nānyaṁ jāne naiva jāne na jāne .. 30.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Rama is (like) my mother, father, master and friend, indeed the kind-hearted Rama is all I have. I know of no other (like him), (I really) don't! (30)**

### 31

*dakṣiṇe lakṣmaṇo yasya vāme tu janakātmajā . \\\\*  
*purato mārutiryasya taṁ vande raghunandanam .. 31.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**I salute that Rama who has Lakṣmana on his right and Sita on the left and who has Hanuman in his front. (31)**

### 32

*lokābhirāmaṁ raṇaraṅgadhīram . \\\\*  
*rājīvanetraṁ raghuvaṁśanātham . \\\\*  
*kāruNyarūpaṁ karuṇākaran tam . \\\\*  
*śrī rāmacandram śaraṇaṁ prapadye .. 32.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**I take refuge in that Rama who is quite pleasing to the sight, the master of the stage of war, lotus-eyed, lord of the Raghu race and compassion personified. (32)**

### 33

*manojavaṁ mārutatulyavegam . \\\\*  
*jitendriyaṁ buddhimatāṁ variṣṭham . \\\\*  
*vātātmajaṁ vānarayūthamukhyam . \\\\*  
*śrī rāmadūtaṁ śaraṇaṁ prapadye .. 33.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**I take refuge in the lord Hanuman who is as fast as the mind, equals his father, the wind-God, in speed, is the master of the senses, the foremost amongst the learned, the leader of the Monkey forces and the great messenger of Sri Rama. (33)**

### 34

*kūjantaṁ rāma rāmeti madhuraṁ madhurākṣaram . \\\\*  
*āruhya kavitāśākhāṁ vande vālmīki-kokilam .. 34.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**I salute the great sage Valmiki who sings the glorious name of Rama resorting to his Ramayana as sweetly as a cuckoo will sing sitting atop a tree. (34)**

### 35

*āpadāṁ apahartāraṁ dātāraṁ sarva-saṁpadām . \\\\*  
*lokābhirāmaṁ śrī rāmaṁ bhūyo bhūyo namāmyaham .. 35.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**I bow again and again to Ram who removes all obstacles and grants all wealth and pleases all. (35)**

### 36

*bharjana bhavabījānā arjana sukhasampadām . \\\\*  
*tarjana yama-dūtānā rāma rāmeti garjanam .. 36.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**The roar of the Rama-nama is the destruction of the cause of rebirth (hence cause of liberation), the earning of all wealth and a scare to Yama's messengers. (36)**

### 37

*rāmo rājamai sadā vijayate rāma rameśaṁ bhaje . \\\\*  
*rāmeṇābhihatā niśācaracamū rāmāya tasmai namaḥ . \\\\*  
*rāmānnāsti parāyaṇaṁ parataraṁ rāmasya dāsosmyaham . \\\\*  
*rāme cittalayaḥ sadā bhavatu me bho rāma māmuddhara .. 37.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**Rama, the jewel among the kings, Him I worship, by Him the hordes demons have been destroyed, to him is said my prayer, beyond Him there is nothing to be worshipped, His servant I am, my mind is totally absorbed in Him, O Ram, please lift me up. This verse gives all the seven declensions of the singular word Rama and gives one way of remembering them! (37)**

### 38

*rāma rāmeti rāmeti rame rāme manorame . \\\\*  
*sahasranāma tattulyaṁ rāmanāma varānane .. 38.. \\\\*

**Перевод:**

**O fair-faced Parvati! I enjoy saying Ramarāma. Uttering but once the name 'Rama' is equal to the uttering of any other name' of God, a thousand times. (38)**

*.. iti śrī budhakauśika viracitaṁ śrī rāma-rakṣā stotraṁ saṁpūrṇam .. \\\\*
