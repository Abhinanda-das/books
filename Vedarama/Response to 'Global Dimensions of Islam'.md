# Response to 'Global Dimensions of Islam'

## Response to 'Global Dimensions of Islam'

Sub: Prof. B.Sheik Ali's article "Global dimensions of Islam"

**The Vedanta Kesari, Jan 2001**

Revered Maharaj,

My humble pranaams. This is with reference to Prof.Sheik Ali's article, "The Global dimensions of Islam". The learned professor would have been more convincing had he quoted some verses from the *Quraan* or the *Hadis* to support his arguments. I wonder if he was in fear of inviting a *fatwa* on his head, like Salman Rushdie did, though his motive was diametrically opposite. Rushdie had to face the wrath of his intolerant community for bringing out the truth, by referring indirectly to some Quraanic verses. On the other hand, the professor has tried to white-wash Islam, solely on the basis of his personal opinions.

At the very beginning, he says that "Islam must be judged by its precepts and principles and not by looking at its modern followers". However, this argument fails to answer the question as to why the practitioners of such a peace loving and egalitarian religion have always been so intolerant and blood-thirsty. Why is the history of Islam soaked in the blood of *kafirs* (notwithstanding the negationist versions of its history propagated by 'eminent historians' in this country)? Why do the followers of Islam carry on jehad and other terrorist activities in the name of Islam? Where did the concept of Jehad originate from?

It is not very difficult to understand this 'paradox', if one turns to the Quran itself and makes a careful study of its precepts and the Prophet's life, which the followers of Islam have been conscientiously emulating. To say that this is not 'real' Islam or that these are merely, "aberrations" would be a complete travesty of truth. Permit me to examine his arguments more closely.

**I. Oneness of God**

First of all, I would like to ask the professor which 'God' he is referring to. Allah, who was hijacked from the Arabic pantheon and converted into a jealous and intolerant God?

*1. "It(Islam) was not conceived by Prophet Mohammed. It was there...ever since the first man, Adam."*

Well, modern science has rejected many such Biblical myths. Still, if the learned professor and his Muslim brethren prefer to believe this dogma, I have no objection. But it does not appeal to a rational intellect nor does it stand a scientific inquiry.

2\. *"Unity of God is a concept of supreme value to man that confers on him equality, solidarity, brotherhood and freedom"*

The three monotheistic religions(Islam, Christianity, Judaism) are even now engaged in a fierce struggle for supremacy. The recent riots between Muslims and Christians in Indonesia and between and the relentless war between Jews and Muslims in Palestine are two examples. In fact wherever the Muslims are in minority that land is a *Dar-ul-Harb*(land of strife), to use their own language and wherever they are a majority it is a *jahiliyyah(*land of darkness), Afghanistan, for example. On the other hand it is polytheistic pagan spiritual traditions like the one which existed in pre-Islamic Arabia that have cherished and practiced values like freedom and tolerance which unfortunately was wiped out or appropriated to a large extent by the advent of Prophet Mohammed.

3\. *"It is a change from heterogeneity to homogeneity, from diversity to unity and from confusion to order."*

The kind of homogeneity and unity that Islam conceives will naturally be at the cost of all diversity and any sensible human being will be repulsed at the destruction of the beautiful diversity that exists in the universe. India's concept of samanvaya is entirely against such a rigid conception of unity. Even Swami Vivekananda never supported such a homogeneity which suppresses all diversity. That is why he used to say that there must be infinite religions and sects so that every one can have a religion of his choice.

**II) Love, Knowledge justice**

*1. "...but God's love to man exceeds the love of seventy mothers...."*

Do kafirs like us stand any chance of tasting this 'unbounded' love without being converted?

*2. "... who does not question the deeds of man until the last day of judgment..."*

Again, the last day of judgment is a dogma like many other dogmas that Islam preaches. Human beings with a sense of responsibility have no need for such a moral judge. Moreover, his judgment will again be different for believers and infidels.

3\. "*The greatest gift of God to man is his intellect ... the conquest of nature through intellect has put man in the category of creators, imitating God's attribute of knowledge which is light..."*

True. But then how is it that Islamic societies are so opposed to the scientific spirit of inquiry? The fact that is always suppressed in India is that the Western world has achieved all this scientific and technological advancement only after rebelling against the Church and the irrational beliefs imposed on them by the intolerant clergy.

4\. "*The third great attribute of God which Islam teaches humanity is justice.... the Quran speaks again and again of retribution..."*

If that had been really true, then Muslims by now should have been persecuted as many times as non-Muslims were, at their hands!

**III. Unity of Man**

*1. "Islam attempts to level down the barriers of caste, colour and class, and takes the stand on the bedrock of humanity."*

This is the greatest lie I have ever heard. Islam is notorious for the gruesome atrocities committed on their slaves, a majority of whom were non-Muslims brought forcibly from Asian countries. The condition of women in Islam(read the reports from Afghanistan in the media for a detailed proof) is another case in point. It might not be so irrelevant to ask here why they are so opposed to singing "Vande Mataram" which eulogizes India as a Mother, if they consider "Mother as the symbol of love in human experience"

**IV Sufism**

What is not told about Sufism is that Sufis have always been more orthodox in persecuting Kafirs to compensate for their heterodox doctrines. In fact Aurangazeb, one of the most tyrannical Muslim rulers of Mughal India, was also hailed as a great Sufi!

**V The Code of Conduct**

*"In Hinduism the history of this mundane world is not all significant" .*

What a statement! It could not have come from any other person other than a professor of his calibre! It was Hindu India that stood as a world leader in arts and sciences. How could this be if Hinduism had no regard for this mundane world?

**VI Prophet Muhammad**

Prophet Muhammad and his life can provide accurate answers to some of the questions that I raised in the beginning of this letter. His life has set a wonderful precedence indeed for all Muslims to follow. Demolish all temples, like the Kaaba temple of pagan Arabs was destroyed by him, plunder, loot and kill all kafirs ruthlessly, as he did during his life time, backed by his militant followers.

**VII Conclusion**

Prof.Sheik Ali's article appears to be a deliberate attempt to throw dust into people's eyes, especially our gullible devotees who might not have the patience or the inclination to verify his claims about Islam by going directly to the source- Islam and Prophet Muhammad's life -and form their opinions about Islam from Sri Ramakrishna's life. It is the same old negationist version of Islam that Marxist historians have tried to project in India that he is trying to sell.

Being the editor of the Vedanta Kesari and a responsible member of the Ramakrishna Mission, I request you to dispel the following doubts that have been thrown up by the study of this article and other similar ones which routinely appear in the journals and other publications of the Ramakrishna Math and Mission:

1\. Why is the Ramakrishna Mission so obsessed with the idea of secularism and harmony of religions? None other than the Holy Mother Sri Sarada Devi herself clarified that the Master had not come for harmony of religions and that tyaaga was his principle message.

*"Great effort should be made to bring even Muslims and Christians into the Hindu fold. But for some time the Samskaras like the Upanayana need not be given to them."*, says Swamiji in the "Rules and Regulations" framed by him. There is enough tolerance and harmony in the blood of the Hindus. Secularism is a burden that Hindus alone have been bearing all along. It is the Muslims and Christians who need this message of tolerance. The secular establishment in India is playing fraudulent games with Hindus and has suppressed their right for self-assertion in the name of secularism and minority rights. The Mission's preaching and over emphasis on harmony of religions has only added salt to this injury. Hindus remain confused and ignorant about the real dark face of Islam and Christianity owing to this.

*2. "The math will not pay much attention to social reform. For social ills or evil customs are a sort of disease in the social body. If that body is nourished by education and food, those evil customs will disappear by themselves. Hence, instead of wasting its energy in vociferously ventilating the social evils, it should be the aim of this Math to nourish the social body",* says Swamiji in his "Rules and Regulations of the Ramakrishna Mission". Why is it that the Mission is vehemently vociferating against social problems like untouchability, ignoring at the same time, equally critical issues faced by the Hindu society in the educational field, for example, owing to the discriminatory policies of the Govt. against Hindus?

*3. "1. If we compile the sayings of Sri Ramakrishna and make them the sole scripture, the only outcome of His broad ideas and ideals and our life-long labour will be that we shall be creating a small and narrow sect and making this society, already divided into many quarreling factions, still more tumultuous.*

*2. Therefore our eternal scripture, the Vedas, will be accepted and preached as the sole scripture. And even as the Gita was in the ancient times, so will Sri Ramakrishna's sayings be the most modern and perfect commentary on the Vedic doctrines. "*

-"The Creed", Rules and Regulations, Swami Vivekananda

The Ramakrishna Mission seems to be doing exactly the opposite in practice. Why has the Mission not given equal importance to the Vedas, the foremost of the Hindu scriptures? Instead lectures on Christ and Muhammad get more weightage.

*4. "When the Muslims first came to India, there were, according to historical records, sixty crores of Hindus in India. This calculation suffers rather from underestimation than exaggeration ; for lots of people perished solely through the persecution of the Muslims. Therefore it is obvious that the number of Hindus was even more than sixty crores - on no account less than that. But today the same Hindus have dwindled into twenty crores. Over and above that, with the ascendancy of the Christian powers, about two crores of people have become Christians and more than a lakh of people are becoming Christians every year. It is specially for the preservation of the Hindu race and religion that Bhagavan Sri Ramakrishna, the embodiment of mercy, has incarnated Himself."* - Rules and Regulations

On the contrary, the Mission has betrayed the faith that millions of Hindus reposed in this great institution founded by Swamiji. In the name of universality or to avoid the wrath of the secular media and the establishment the Mission is maintaining a stoic silence on critical problems being faced by the Hindus today. In what way does expressing solidarity with the Hindus hamper the ideal of universality? One of the heads of a Mission centre even went to the extent of filing a court case to grant 'minority status' to the Mission on the basis of the argument that since the Mission does not follow the caste system, it cannot be considered as Hindu! Is this what Swamiji wanted the Mission to do? Is this what millions of Hindus who have nurtured the Mission through their humble services financially or otherwise get in return for their faith in the Mission?

I hope that you will pardon my audacity. I might not be the right person to ask these questions but I am certain that there are many other devotees who might be thinking about these problems in the same vein. The Mission will be doing us a great service by answering these questions through journals like the Vedanta Kesari

I also take the liberty of attaching with this letter a relevant chapter from Sri Ram Swarup's book "Understanding Islam through the Hadis" in support of my arguments, since it throws more light on some of the issues, related to Islam, discussed in this letter

**Needless to say, there was no response from the editor**
