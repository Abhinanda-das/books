Sequential Method of Remembering the Pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛñṇa during Eight parts of the Day.

# Rādhā-Krṣsṣasta-kālīya-līlā-smaraṇa-krama-paddhati \[DG-BS\]

## Rādhā-Krṣsṣasta-kālīya-līlā-smaraṇa-krama-paddhati

*evaṁ padmopari dhyātvā rādhā-kṛñṇau tatas tayoḥ \|*  
*añṭa-kālocitāṁ sevāṁ vidadhyāt siddha-dehataḥ \|*  
*guru-vargājñayā tatra pūjayed rādhikā-harī \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having meditated on Rādhā and Kṛñṇa on the lotus, in one’s siddha-deha one should perform service according to eight parts of the day. By the order of the group of gurus one should worship Rādhā and Kṛñṇa on the lotus.**

*bāhya-pūjāṁ tataḥ kṛtvā pādyam arghyaṁ krameṇa ca \|*  
*vidhi-pūrvaka-śuśrūñān antaraṁ sādhakaḥ kramāt \|*  
*dvātriṁśad-akñara-mukhān japen mantrān atandritaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Then, after having performed external worship with padyam, arghyam, serving according to the rules, the sādhaka should attentively chant the best mantra of thirty-two syllables.**

*mahā-mantraṁ japed ādau daśārṇaṁ tad-antaram \|*  
*tataḥ śrī-rādhikā-mantraṁ gāyatrīṁ kāmikīṁ tataḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**First he should chant the mahā-mantra and then the ten syllable mantra, then the Rādhā-mantra and kāma-gāyatrī.**

*tato yugala-mantraṁ ca japed rāsa-sthalī-pradam \|*  
*tato ’ñṭānāṁ sakhīnāṁ ca japen mantrān yathā-kramam \|*  
*tato ’ñṭa-mañjarīṇāṁ ca sva-sva-mantrān kramāj japet \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He should chant the yugala-mantra which reveals the place of the rāsa dance. Then he should chant the mantras of the eight sakhīsin order and then the mantras for the eight mañjarīs.**

*añṭa-kālīya-sūtram āha, yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The summary of the eight parts of the day is as follows:**

*niśāntaḥ prātaḥ pūrvāhno madhyāhnaś cāparāhnakaḥ \|*  
*sāyaṁ pradoño rātriś ca kālā añṭau yathā-kramam \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The eight parts of the day in order are – end-of-night, early morning, forenoon, noon, afternoon, twilight, evening, and night.**

*madhyāhno yāminī cobhau ñaṇ-muhūrta-mitau smṛtau \|*  
*tri-muhūrta-mitā jñeyā niśānta-pramukhāḥ pare \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Noon and night last six muhūrtas and other periods last three muhūrtas.**

*teñu siddha-dehena sevanaṁ yathā sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām,*

**Перевод:**

**The service in the siddha-deha is described in Sanat-kumārasaṁhitā.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*  
*bhagavan sarvam ākhyātaṁ yad yat pṛñṭhaṁ tvayā guro \|*  
*adhunā śrotum icchāmi rāga-mārgam anuttamam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**  
**O guru, O master! You have explained everything that I asked. Now I desire to hear about the topmost rāga-marga.**

*śrī-sadāśiva uvāca--*  
*sādhu pṛñṭhaṁ tvayā vipra sarva-loka-hitaiñiṇā \|*  
*rahasyam api vakñyāmi tan me nigaditaṁ śṛṇu \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sadāśiva said:**  
**You have asked nicely, O brāhmaṇa who desires the welfare of all people! I will explain the secret. Please listen to what I say.**

*parakīyābhimāninyas tathāsya ca priyā janāḥ \|*  
*pracureṇaiva bhāvena ramayanti nija-priyam \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The beloveds of Kṛñṇa, thinking themselves married to others, give pleasure to Kṛñṇa with their deep love.**

*ātmānaṁ cintayet tatra tāsāṁ madhye manoramām \|*  
*rūpa-yauvana-sampannāṁ kiśorīṁ pramadākṛtim \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate that one is among them and is an attractive girl of kaiśora age, endowed with beauty and youth.**

*nānā-śilpa-kalābhijñāṁ kṛñṇa-bhogānurūpiṇīm \|*  
*prārthitām api kṛñṇena tato bhoga-parāṅ-mukhīm \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This girl is expert in various arts, gives pleasure to Kṛñṇa, but if requested by Kṛñṇa, does not enjoy with him personally.**

*rādhikānucarīṁ nityaṁ tat-sevana-parāyaṇām \|*  
*kṛñṇād apy adhikaṁ prema rādhikāyāṁ prakurvatīm \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She follows Rādhā at all times and surrenders to serving her. She has more love for Rādhā than for Kṛñṇa.**

*prītyānudivasaṁ yatnāt tayoḥ saṅgama-kāriṇīm \|*  
*tat-sevana-sukhāsvāda-bhareṇātisunirvṛtām \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She makes efforts every day out of affection to bring Rādhā and Kṛñṇa together. She is most blissful by relishing the happiness of serving them.**

*ity ātmānaṁ vicintyaiva tatra sevāṁ samācaret \|*  
*brāhma-muhūrtam ārabhya yāvat sāntā mahā-niśā \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Thinking of oneself in this way, one should perform service, starting from brahma-muhūrta until the calm at midnight.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca--*  
*harer atra gatāṁ līlāṁ śrotum icchāmi tattvataḥ \|*  
*līlām ajānatāṁ sevyo manasā tu kathaṁ hariḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**  
**I desire to hear truthfully the pastimes performed by Kṛñṇa. How can one serve him in the mind if one is ignorant of the pastimes?**

*śrī-sadāśiva uvāca--*  
*nāhaṁ jānāmi tāṁ līlāṁ harer nārada tattvataḥ \|*  
*vṛndā-devīṁ samāgaccha sā te līlāṁ pravakñyati \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sadāśiva said:**  
**O Nārada! Actually I do not know the pastimes of Kṛñṇa. Go to Vṛndā-devī. She will explain the pastimes to you.**

*avidūre itaḥ sthānāt keśī-tīrtha-samīpataḥ \|*  
*sakhībhiḥ saṁvṛtā sāste govinda-paricārikā \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This servant of Kṛñṇa is surrounded by sakhīs near Keśi-tīrtha, not far from here.**

*śrī-sanat-kumāra uvāca--*  
*ity uktas taṁ parikramya guruṁ natvā punaḥ punaḥ \|*  
*vṛndā-sthānaṁ jagāmāsau nārado muni-sattamaḥ \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra said:**  
**When Sadāśiva said this, Nārada, the best of sages, walked around him, offered respects repeatedly and went to Vṛndā’s place.**

*vṛndāpi nāradaṁ dṛñṭvā praṇamyāpi punaḥ punaḥ \|*  
*uvāca taṁ muni-śreñṭhaṁ katham atrāgatis tava \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Nārada, Vṛndā offered him respects repeatedly. She said to the best of sages, “Why have you come here?”**

*śrī-nārada uvāca--*  
*tvatto veditum icchāmi naityikaṁ caritaṁ hareḥ \|*  
*tad ādito mama bruhi yadi yogyo ’smi śobhane \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**  
**I desire to know from you the daily pastimes of Kṛñṇa. O beautiful Vṛndā! If I am worthy, please tell me everything from the beginning.**

*śrī-vṛndā-devy uvāca--*  
*rahasyaṁ tvāṁ pravakñyāmi kṛñṇa-bhakto ’si nārada \|*  
*na prakāśyaṁ tvayā hy etad guhyād guhyataraṁ mahat \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndā-devī said:**  
**I will explain this secret to you. O Nārada! You are devotee of Kṛñṇa. This great secret of secrets should not be revealed by you.**

*atha niśānta-sevā-*  
*madhye vṛndāvane ramye pañcāśat-kuñja-maṇḍite \|*  
*kalpa-vṛkña-nikuñje tu divya-ratna-maye gṛhe \|\|22\|\|*  
*nidritau tiñṭhatas talpe niviḍāliṅgitau mithaḥ \|*  
*mad-ājñā-kāribhiḥ paścāt pakñibhir bodhitāv api \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Service at the end-of-night:**  
**In charming Vṛndāvana, decorated with fifty bowers, in a bower made of desire trees, in a house made of attractive gems, the couple remain sleeping, tightly embracing. They are awakened later by my order carriers, the birds.**

*gāḍhāliṅgana-nirbhedam āptau tad-bhaṅga-kātarau \|*  
*na manas kurutas talpāt samutthātuṁ manāg api \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having attained an unbreakable, tight embrace, they feel pained to break it. They do not have determination to get up from the bed at all.**

*tataś ca śārikā-saṁghaiḥ śukādyair api tau muhuḥ \|*  
*bodhitau vividhaiḥ padyaiḥ sva-talpād udatiñṭhatām \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Finally awakened by parrots and śārikas reciting various verses, they rise from their bed.**

*upaviñṭau tato dṛñṭvā sakhyas talpe mudānvitau \|*  
*praviśya cakrire sevāṁ tat-kālasyocitāṁ tayoḥ \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing them sitting there on the bed in bliss, the sakhīs enter and perform service for them which is appropriate to the time.**

*punaś ca śārikā-vākyair utthāya tau sva-talpataḥ \|*  
*gacchataḥ sva-sva-bhavanaṁ bhīty-utkaṇṭhākulau mithaḥ \|\|27\|\|*  
*iti niśānta-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**By the words of the śārikās they rise from the bed, and go to their houses, filled with fear and longing.**  
**(completion of end-of-night service)**

*atha prātaḥ-sevā--*  
*prātaś ca bodhito mātrā talpād utthāya satvaram \|*  
*kṛtvā kṛñṇo danta-kāñṭhaṁ baladeva-samanvitaḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Early morning service:**  
**Awakened by his mother, Kṛñṇa quickly rises from bed and brushes his teeth along with Balarāma.**

*mātrānumodito yāti gośālāṁ dohanotsukaḥ \|*  
*rādhāpi bodhitā vṛddha-vayasyābhiḥ sva-talpataḥ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With the permission of his mother, he goes to the cow sheds, eager to milk the cows. Rādhā also is awakened by experienced friends from her bed.**

*utthāya danta-kāñṭhādi kṛtvābhyaṅga samācaret \|*  
*snāna-vedīṁ tato gatvā snāpitā lalitādibhiḥ \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She rises, brushes her teeth, and has a massage. Going to the bathing platform she is bathed by Lalitā and others.**

*bhūñā-gṛhaṁ vrajet tatra vayasyā bhūñayanty api \|*  
*bhūñaṇair vividhair divyair gandha-mālyānulepanaiḥ \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She goes to the dressing room. There, friends decorate her with various attractive ornaments, ointments, scents and garlands.**

*tataś ca sva-janais tasyāḥ śvaśrūṁ samprārthya yatnataḥ \|*  
*paktum āhūyate tūrṇaṁ sa-sakhī sā yaśodayā \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Yaśodā’s servants carefully make a request to Rādhā’s mother-inlaw. Rādhā and her sakhīs arecalled by Yaśodā to cook quickly for Kṛñṇa.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca--*  
*katham āhūyate devi pākārthaṁ sā yaśodayā \|*  
*satīñu pāka-kartrīñu rohiṇī-pramukhāñv api \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**  
**O Vṛndā-devī! Why is she called by Yaśodā to cook, since there are many cooks headed by Rohiṇī?**

*śrī-vṛndovāca--*  
*durvāsasā svayaṁ datto varas tasyai maharñiṇā \|*  
*iti kātyāyanī-vaktrāc chrutam āsīn mayā purā \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndā said:**  
**She was given a benediction by the great sage Durvāsa. Previously I heard this from the mouth of Kātyāyanī.**

*tvayā yat pacyate devi tad-annaṁ mad-anugrahāt \|*  
*miñṭaṁ svādv-amṛta-sparddhi bhoktur āyuñkaraṁ tathā \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“O Rādhā! Whatever you cook, by my mercy that food will be sweet, tastier than nectar and will give long life to the eater.”**

*ity āhvayati tāṁ nityaṁ yaśodā putra-vatsalā \|*  
*āyuñmān me bhavet putraḥ svādu-lobhāt tathā satī \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**For this reason Yaśodā, out of affection for her son, calls Rādhā daily. She thinks, “My son will live long because of desiring that tasty food.”**

*śvaśrvānumoditā sāpi hṛñṭā nandālayaṁ vrajet \|*  
*sa-sakhī-prakarā tatra gatvā pākaṁ karoti ca \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Permitted by her mother-in-law, Rādhā joyfully goes to Nanda’s house. Going with her sakhīs, she cooks.**

*kṛñṇo ’pi dugdhvā gāḥ kāścid dohayitvā janaiḥ parāḥ \|*  
*āgacchati pitur vākyāt sva-gṛhaṁ sakhibhir vṛtaḥ \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After milking some cows and having others milk the rest of the cows, Kṛñṇa returns to his house on his father’s instruction along with his friends.**

*abhyaṅga-mardanaṁ kṛtvā dāsaiḥ saṁsnāpito mudā \|*  
*dhauta-vastra-dharaḥ sragvī candanākta-kalevaraḥ \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having been joyfully massaged and bathed by servants, he puts on washed cloth, a garland and sandalwood.**

*dvi-phāla-baddha-keśaiś ca grīvā-bhālopari sphuran \|*  
*candrākāra-sphurad-bhāla-tilakālaka-rañjitaḥ \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Shining with hair bound up in two bundles over his neck and forehead, he is anointed with tilaka in the form of the moon on his forehead.**

*kaṅkaṇāṅgada-keyūra-ratna-mudrālasat-karaḥ \|*  
*muktāhāra-sphurad-vakñā makarākṛti-kuṇḍalaḥ \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His hands shine with jewelled rings, bracelets, armlets and bangles. He has a pearl necklace on his chest and makara shaped earrings.**

*muhur ākārito mātrā praviśed bhojanālaye \|*  
*avalambya karaṁ mātur baladevam anuvrataḥ \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Constantly called by his mother, he enters the dining room, taking support of his mother’s hand, following Baladeva.**

*bhuktvā ca vividhānnāni mātrā ca sakhibhir vṛtaḥ \|*  
*hāsayan vividhair vākyaiḥ sakhīṁs tair hāsitaḥ svayam \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Surrounded by his friends and mother, he eats various foods. He makes his friends laugh by his various words and he is made to laugh by his friends.**

*itthaṁ bhuktvā tathācamya divya-khaṭṭopari kñaṇāt \|*  
*viśramet sevakair dattaṁ tāmbūlaṁ vibhajann adan \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having eaten food and washed his mouth, he rests for a moment on attractive cot. Dividing up betel nut offered by servants, he eats it.**

*rādhāpi bhojanānandaṁ dṛñṭvā yaśodayāhūta \|*  
*lalitādi-sakhī-vṛtā bhuṅkte ’nnaṁ lajjayānvitā \|\|45\|\|*  
*iti prātaḥ-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā, seeing him blissfully eating, is called by Yaśodā. Feeling shyness, she eats, surrounded by Lalitā and other sakhīs.**  
**(end of morning service)**

*atha pūrvāhna-sevā-*  
*gopa-veśa-dharaḥ kṛñṇo dhenu-vṛnda-puraḥ-saraḥ \|*  
*vraja-vāsi-janaiḥ prītyā sarvair anugataḥ pathi \|\|46\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Forenoon service:**  
**Wearing cowherd dress, putting the cows in front, Kṛñṇa goes along the path with all the people of Vraja out of affection for them.**

*pitaraṁ mātaraṁ natvā netrāntena priyā-gaṇān \|*  
*yathāyogyaṁ tathā cānyān sannivartya vanaṁ vrajet \|\|47\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Offering respects to his mother and father, and to the gopīs with the corner of his eye, and pacifying others appropriately, he has them all return and goes to the forest.**

*vanaṁ praviśya sakhibhiḥ krīḍitvā ca kñaṇaṁ tataḥ \|*  
*vañcayitvā ca tān sarvān dvi-traiḥ priya-sakhair yutaḥ \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Entering the forest, playing with his friends for a short time, he then fools them all and goes off with two or three priya-sakhas.**

*saṅketakaṁ vrajed dharñāt priyā-sandarśanotsukaḥ \|*  
*sāpi kṛñṇe vanaṁ yāte dṛñṭvā taṁ gṛham āgatā \|\|49\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He goes for an appointment with joy, eager to see his beloved Rādhā. When Kṛñṇa goes to the forest, Rādhā, after seeing him, goes home.**

*sūryādi-pūjā-vyājena kusumādy-āhṛti-cchalāt \|*  
*vañcayitvā gurūn yāti priya-saṅgecchayā vanaṁ \|\|50\|\|*  
*iti pūrvāhna-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**Fooling the elders, on the pretext of gathering flowers for worship of the sun god, she goes to the forest with a desire to see Kṛñṇa.**  
**(end of forenoon service)**

*atha madhyāhna-sevā--*  
*itthaṁ tau bahu-yatnena militvā sva-gaṇair vṛtau \|*  
*vihārair vividhais tatra vane vikrīḍato mudā \|\|51\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Noon service:**  
**In this way the two, after meeting with great effort, surrounded by their followers, joyfully play in the forest with various amusements.**

*syandolikā-samārūḍhau sakhībhir dolitau kvacit \|*  
*kvacid veṇuṁ kara-srastaṁ priyayā coritaṁ hariḥ \|\|52\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes the two mount a swing and are pushed by the sakhīs. Sometimes Rādhā steals his flute when it drops from his hand.**

*anveñayann upālabdho vipralabdhaḥ priyā-gaṇaiḥ \|*  
*hāsito bahudhā tābhir hṛta-sva iva tiñṭhati \|\|53\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He searches for it, and is criticized and insulted by the gopīs. Mocked by them, he acts as if his very self had been stolen.**

*vasanta-ṛtunā juñṭaṁ vana-khaṇḍaṁ kvacin mudā \|*  
*praviśya candanāmbhobhiḥ kuṅkumādi-jalair api \|\|54\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes they joyfully enter a forest decorated with spring season and sprinkle each other with water mixed with sandalwood and kuṁkuma from syringes.**

*viñiñcato yantra-muktais tat-paṅkenāpi tau mithaḥ \|*  
*sakhyo ’py evaṁ viñiñcanti tāś ca tau siñcataḥ punaḥ \|\|55\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs also sprinkled them with the mixture, and Rādhā and Kṛñṇa sprinkle them.**

*tathānyartusu juñṭāsu krīḍato vana-rājiñu \|*  
*tat-tat-kālocitair nānā-vihāraiḥ sa-gaṇau dvija \|\|56\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The two play in forests which manifest other seasons, along with their followers, performing various activities suitable to the seasons.**

*śrāntau kvacid vṛkña-mūlam āsādya muni-sattama \|*  
*upaviśyāsane divye madhu-pānaṁ pracakratuḥ \|\|57\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O best of sages! Sometimes, becoming tired they approach the base of a tree and, sitting on attractive seats, drink honey.**

*tato madhu-madonmattau nidrayā mīlitekñaṇau \|*  
*mithaḥ pāṇiṁ samālambya kāma-bāṇa-vaśaṅgatau \|\|58\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Becoming drunk from the honey, they close their eyes from drowsiness. Holding each other’s hands, they become controlled by Cupid’s arrows.**

*riraṁsu viśataḥ kuñjaṁ skhalat-pādābjakau pathi \|*  
*tato vikrīḍatas tatra kariṇī-yūthapau yathā \|\|59\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring pleasure, they enter a bower, stumbling on their lotus feet. They then play like a male elephant and his mate.**

*sakhyo ’pi madhubhir mattā nidrayā pihitekñaṇāḥ \|*  
*abhitaḥ kuñja-puñjeñu sārvā eva vililyire \|\|60\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs also become drunk and close their eyes with sleep. They all disappear in bowers everywhere.**

*pṛthag ekena vapuñā kṛñṇo ’pi yugapad vibhuḥ \|*  
*sarvāsāṁ sannidhiṁ gacchet priyāṇāṁ parito muhuḥ \|\|61\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛñṇa, in one body, simultaneously approaches them all individually.**

*ramayitvā ca tāḥ sarvāḥ kariṇī-gajarāḍ iva \|*  
*priyayā ca tathā tābhiḥ sarovaram athāvrajet \|\|62\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Giving them all enjoyment, he then goes to a lake with Rādhā and the sakhīs, like a male elephant with his herd.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca--*  
*vṛnde śrī-nanda-putrasya mādhurya-krīḍane katham \|*  
*aiśvaryasya prakāśo ’bhūd iti me chindhi saṁśayam \|\|63\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**  
**O Vṛndā! Why did Kṛñṇa display power in the pastimes of sweetness? Please dispel my doubt.**

*śrī-vṛndovāca--*  
*mune mādhurya-mayy asti līlā-śaktir harer dṛḍhā \|*  
*tayā pṛthak-kṛtaḥ krīḍed gopikābhiḥ samaṁ hariḥ \|\|64\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndā said:**  
**O sage! The strong līlā-śakti of the Lord is full of sweetness. By that śakti, he can play with the gopīs in separate forms.**

*rādhayā saha rūpeṇa nijena ramate svayam \|*  
*iti mādhurya-līlāyāḥ śaktir neśatāyā hareḥ \|\|65\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He personally enjoys with Rādhā in his own form. The śakti produces sweet pastimes.**  
**It does not show the Lord’s powers.**

*jala-sekair mithas tatra krīḍitvā sa-gaṇau tataḥ \|*  
*vāsaḥ-srak-candanair divya-bhūñaṇair api bhūñitau \|\|66\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sprinkling each other with water, along with their followers, they decorate each other with clothing, sandalwood and attractive ornaments.**

*tatraiva sarasas tīre divya-ratna-maye gṛhe \|*  
*aśnītaḥ phala-mūlāni kalpitāni mayaiva hi \|\|67\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the bank of the lake, in a house made of attractive jewels, they eat fruits and roots prepared by me.**

*haris tu prathamaṁ bhuktvā kāntayā pariveśitam \|*  
*dvi-trābhiḥ sevito gacchec chayyāṁ puñpa-vinirmitām \|\|68\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛñṇa first eats what Rādhā gives him. Served by two or three sakhīs, he goes to a bed made of flowers.**

*tāmbūlair vyajanais tatra pāda-saṁvāhanādibhiḥ \|*  
*sevyamāno bhṛśantābhir moditaḥ preyasīṁ smaran \|\|69\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Offered betel nut, fanned and having his feet massaged by the eager sakhīs, he is pleased and remembers Rādhā.**

*śrī-rādhāpi harau supte sa-gaṇā muditāntarā \|*  
*kānta-dattaṁ prīta-manā ucchiñṭaṁ bubhuje tataḥ \|\|70\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛñṇa sleeps, Rādhā and her friends, joyful within, pleased in mind, eat what has been given by Kṛñṇa.**

*kiñcid evo tato bhuktvā vrajec chayyā-niketanam \|*  
*drañṭuṁ kānta-mukhāmbhojaṁ cakorīvan niśā-karam \|\|71\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After eating a little, Rādhā goes to the sleeping room to see the lotus face of Kṛñṇa, like a cakori bird looking at the moon.**

*tāmbūla-carvitaṁ tasya tatratyābhir niveditam \|*  
*tāmbūlāny api cāśnāti vibhajantī priyāliñu \|\|72\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She chews the betel nuts chewed by Kṛñṇa, which are offered to her by sakhīs there. She divides the betel nuts among the sakhīs as well.**

*kṛñṇo ’pi tāsāṁ śuśrūñuḥ svacchandaṁ bhāñitaṁ mithaḥ \|*  
*prāpta-nidra ivābhāti vinidro ’pi paṭāvṛtaḥ \|\|73\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring to hear the gopīs speak freely together, Kṛñṇa pretends to be asleep, covered with a cloth, though he is wide awake.**

*tāś ca kñvelīṁ kñaṇaṁ kṛtvā mithaḥ kānta-kathāśrayāḥ \|*  
*vyāja-nidrāṁ harer jñātvā kutaścid anumānataḥ \|\|74\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They joke for a while together, exchanging talks. They then understand that Kṛñṇa is awake.**

*vimṛśya vadanaṁ dṛgbhiḥ paśyantyo ’nyonyam ānanam \|*  
*līnā iva lajjayā syuḥ kñaṇam ucur na kiñcanam \|\|75\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Concluding this by looking at his face, they look at each other’s faces. Seeming to disappear out of embarrassment, for a moment they can say nothing.**

*kñaṇād eva tato vastraṁ dūrī-kṛtya tad aṅgataḥ \|*  
*sādhu nidrāṁ gato ’sīti hāsayantyo hasanti tam \|\|76\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After a moment, they remove the cloth from his limbs. They say, “You have slept nicely.” They make him laugh and they laugh.**

*evaṁ tau vividhair hāsai ramamāṇau gaṇaiḥ saha \|*  
*anubhūya kñaṇaṁ nidrā-sukhaṁ ca muni-sattama \|\|77\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! In this way the couple enjoy various jokes with the sakhīs, experiencing the happiness of sleep for a while.**

*upaviśyāsane divye sa-gaṇau vistṛte mudā \|*  
*paṇī-kṛtya mitho hāra-cumbāśleña-paricchadān \|\|78\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sitting on two attractive seats with their followers, expanding their joy, they throw dice for a necklace, a kiss, an embrace and some clothing.**

*akñair vikrīḍitaḥ premṇā narmālāpa-puraḥsaram \|*  
*parājito ’pi priyayā jitam ity avadan mṛñā \|\|79\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They play dice in prema, along with joking conversations. Though he is defeated, he does not admit that she has won, by lying.**

*hārādi-grahaṇe tasyāḥ pravṛttas tāḍyate tayā \|*  
*tayaivaṁ tāḍitaḥ kṛñṇaḥ karṇotpala-saroruhaiḥ \|\|80\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When he tries to take her necklace, she beats him. He is beaten by lotuses and her flower ornament.**

*viñaṇṇa-vadano bhūtvā gata-sva iva nārada \|*  
*jito ’smi ca tvayā devi gṛhyatāṁ yat paṇī-kṛtam \|\|81\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With a sad face, as if having lost everything including himself, he says, “I have been defeated by you. O goddess, you should accept what was wagered by me—the kisses.”**

*cumbanādi mayā dattam ity uktvā ca tathācarat \|*  
*kauṭilyaṁ tad-bhruvor drañṭuṁ śrotuṁ tad-bhartsanaṁ vacaḥ \|\|82\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He does this in order to see her frown and hear her scolding words.**

*tataḥ śārī-śukānāṁ ca śrutvā vāgāhavaṁ mithaḥ \|*  
*nirgacchatas tataḥ sthānād gantukāmo gṛhaṁ prati \|\|83\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing the śārīs and parrots quarrelling, they then leave the place, desiring to return home.**

*kṛñṇaḥ kāntām anujñāpya gavām abhimukhaṁ vrajet \|*  
*sā tu sūrya-gṛhaṁ gacchet sakhī-maṇḍala-saṁyutā \|\|84\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Taking permission from Rādhā, Kṛñṇa heads for the cows. She goes to the temple of the sun god with her sakhīs.**

*kiyad-dūraṁ tato gatvā parāvṛtya hariḥ punaḥ \|*  
*vipra-veśaṁ samāsthāya yāti sūrya-gṛhaṁ prati \|\|85\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After going some distance, Kṛñṇa turns around and, adopting the dress of a brāhmaṇa, goes to the sun temple.**

*sūryaṁ ca pūjayet tatra prārthitas tat-sakhī-janaiḥ \|*  
*tad eva kalpitair vedaiḥ parihāsyāvagarbhitaiḥ \|\|86\|\|*  
*tatas tā api taṁ kāntaṁ parijñāya vicakñaṇāḥ \|*  
*ānanda-sāgare līnā na viduḥ svaṁ na cāparam \|\|87\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Requested by the gopīs, he performs worship of the sun. By his ridiculous, concocted Vedic mantras, the perceptive gopīsrecognize that it is Kṛñṇa. They drown in an ocean of bliss and do not know themselves or anyone else.**

*vihārair vividhair evaṁ sārdha-yāma-dvayaṁ mune \|*  
*nītvā gṛhaṁ vajeñus tāḥ sa ca kṛñṇo gavāṁ vrajet \|\|88\|\|*  
*iti madhyāhna-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! After spending between one and two yāmas playing in various ways, the gopīs go home and Kṛñṇa goes to the cows.(end of noon service)**

*athāparāhna-sevā--*  
*saṅgamya tu sakhīn kṛñṇo gṛhītvā gāḥ samantataḥ \|*  
*āgacchati vrajaṁ karñann uttāna-muralī-ravaiḥ \|\|89\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Afternoon service:**  
**Meeting his friends, Kṛñṇa gathers all the cows and returns to Vraja, while playing his raised flute.**

*tato nandādayaḥ sarve śrutvā veṇu-ravaṁ hareḥ \|*  
*go-dhūli-paṭalair vyāptaṁ dṛñṭvā cāpi nabhaḥ-sthalam \|\|90\|\|*  
*visṛjya sarva-karmāṇi striyo bālādayo ’pi ca \|*  
*kṛñṇasyābhimukhaṁ yānti tad-darśana-samutsukāḥ \|\|91\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda and all others, hearing the sound of the Kṛñṇa’s flute, seeing the sky spread with clouds of cow dust, give up all their activities and move towards Kṛñṇa, eager to see him. Even the women and children go.**

*rādhikāpi samāgatya gṛhaṁ snātvā vibhūñitā \|*  
*sampācya kānta-bhogārthaṁ dravyāṇi vividhāni ca \|*  
*sakhī-saṁgha-yutā yānti kāntaṁ drañṭuṁ samutsukāḥ \|\|92\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā also returns home, bathes, decorates herself, and cooks various items for Kṛñṇa to eat. With her sakhīs, in great eagerness, she goes to see her beloved.**

*rāja-mārge vraja-dvāri yatra sarve vrajaukasaḥ \|*  
*kṛñṇo ’py etān samāgamya yathāvad anupūrvaśaḥ \|\|93\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They go to the main at the entrance of Vraja, where all the people of Vraja are standing. Kṛñṇa meets everyone appropriately in order.**

*darśanaiḥ sparśanair vāpi smita-purvāvalokanaiḥ \|*  
*gopa-vṛddhān namaskāraiḥ kāyikair vācikair api \|\|94\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He greets them by showing himself, touching them, glancing with joy, and by offering respects to the elders with his body and words.**

*sāñṭāṅga-pātaiḥ pitarau rohiṇīm api nārada \|*  
*netrānta-sūcitenaiva vinayena priyās tathā \|\|95\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Nārada! He falls on the ground before his parents and Rohiṇī and greets the gopīs with modesty by signals using the edge of his eyes.**

*evaṁ taiś ca yathā-yogyaṁ vrajaukobhiḥ prapūjitaḥ \|*  
*gavālayaṁ tathā gāś ca sampraveśya samantataḥ \|\|96\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He is worshipped by the people of Vraja in a suitable manner. He then leads the cows to their sheds.**

*pitṛbhyām arthito yāti bhrātrā saha nijālayam \|*  
*snātvā pitvā tathā kiñcid bhuktvā mātrānumoditaḥ \|*  
*gavālayaṁ punar yāti dogdhu-kāmo gavāṁ payaḥ \|\|97\|\|*  
*ity aparāhna-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**Requested by his parents, he goes with his brother to the house. After bathing, drinking and eating a little, with permission of his mother he again goes to the cow sheds, desiring to milk the cows.**  
**(end of afternoon service)**

*atha sāyaṁ-sevā--*  
*tāś ca dugdhvā dohayitvā pāyayitvā ca kāścana \|*  
*pitrā sārdhaṁ gṛhaṁ yāti payo-bhāri-śatānugaḥ \|\|98\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Evening service:**  
**After milking cows, having others milk cows, and having calves drink milk, he goes to the house along with his father and hundreds of milk carriers.**

*tatrāpi mātṛ-vṛndaiś ca tat-putraiś ca balena ca \|*  
*saṁbhuṅkte vividhānnāni carvya-cūñyādikāni ca \|\|99\|\|*  
*iti sāyaṁ-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**Along with the mothers and fathers, their children and Balarāma, he then eats various foods to be chewed and sucked.**  
**(end of twilight service)**

*atha pradoña-sevā-*  
*tan-mātuḥ prārthanāt pūrvaṁ rādhayāpi tadaiva hi \|*  
*prasthāpyante sakhī-dvārā pakvānnāni tad-ālayam \|\|100\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Evening service:**  
**On the request of Yaśodā, through a sakhī, Rādhā sends some cooked food to Kṛñṇa’s house.**

*ślāghayaṁś ca haris tāni bhuktvā pitrādibhiḥ saha*  
*sabhā-gṛhaṁ vrajet taiś ca juñṭaṁ vandi-janādibhiḥ \|\|101\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛñṇa praises and eats these foods along with the elders. He then goes to the assembly hall filled with panegyrists and other performers.**

*pakvānnāni gṛhītvā yāḥ sakhyas tatra samāgatāḥ \|*  
*bahūni ca punas tāni pradattāni yaśodayā \|\|102\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs who have come to the house, take the cooked food remnants which are given profusely by Yaśodā.**

*sakhyā tatra tayā dattaṁ kṛñṇocchiñṭaṁ tathā rahaḥ*  
*sarvaṁ tābhiḥ samānīya rādhikāyai nivedyate \|\|103\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs take Kṛñṇa’s remnants given by Yaśodā and offer it to Rādhā in a solitary place.**

*sāpi bhuktvā sakhī-varga-yutā tad-anupūrvaśaḥ*  
*sakhībhir maṇḍitā tiñṭhed abhisartuṁ mudānvitā \|\|104\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She eats the food in proper order along with the sakhīs. Decorated by the sakhīs, she becomes joyful to meet Kṛñṇa.**

*prasthāpyate ’nayā kācid ita eva tataḥ sakhī \|*  
*tayābhisāritā sā ’tha yamunāyāḥ samīpataḥ \|\|105\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā sends some sakhī to make an appointment and she arrives at the Yamunā at the appointed place.**

*kalpa-vṛksa-nikuñje ’smin divya-ratna-maye gṛhe \|*  
*sita-kṛñṇa-niśāyogya-veśa yāti sakhī-yutā \|\|106\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With a group of sakhīs, dressed in cloth suitable for moonlit nights or dark nights, she goes to a finely jewelled house in desire tree bower.**

*kṛñṇo ’pi vividhaṁ tatra dṛñṭvā kautuhalaṁ tataḥ \|*  
*kavitvāni manojñāni śrutvā ca gītakāny api \|\|107\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meanwhile, Kṛñṇa watches various amusing performances and hears attractive poetry and songs.**

*dhana-dhānyādibhis tāṁś ca prīṇayitvā vidhānataḥ \|*  
*janair ākārito mātrā yāti śayyā-niketanam \|\|108\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Pleasing the performers with wealth and grains according to rules, called by some people, he goes with his mother to his bedroom.**

*mātari prasthitāyāntu bhojayitvā tato gṛhāt \|*  
*saṅketakaṁ kāntayātra samāgacched alakñitaḥ \|\|109\|\|*  
*iti pradoña-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**When his mother having fed him, leaves, unnoticed, going from the room, he meets with Rādhā at the agreed place.**  
**(end of evening service)**

*atha rātri-sevā—*  
*militvā tāv ubhāv atra krīḍato vana-rājiñu \|*  
*vihārair vividhair hāsya-lāsya-gīta-puraḥsaraiḥ \|\|110\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Late night service:**  
**Meeting, the two participate in the forests in various pastimes, while laughing, dancing and singing.**

*sārdha-yāma-dvayaṁ nītvā rātrer evaṁ vihārataḥ \|*  
*suñupsū viśataḥ kuñjaṁ pañca-ñābhir alakñitau \|\|111\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After spending about five hours at night in playing, desiring sleep, they enter unseen into a bower with five or six sakhīs.**

*nirvṛnta-kusumaiḥ kḷpte keli-talpe manorame \|*  
*suptāvatiñṭhatāṁ tatra sevyamānau priyālibhiḥ \|\|112\|\|*  
*iti rātri-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**Served by the sakhīs, they remain sleeping on an attractive pastime bed made of stemless flowers.**  
**(end of night service)**

*śrī-nārada uvāca-*  
*śrotum icchāmi bho deva vraja-rāja-sutasya ca \|*  
*vṛndāvane rasaṁ divyaṁ rādhayaikāntikaṁ saha \|\|113\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**  
**O Sadāśiva! I desire to hear about the attractive, pure rasa of Kṛñṇa with Rādhā in Vṛndāvana.**

*śrī-sadāśiva uvāca*  
*śṛṇu nārada vakñyāmi rādhā-kṛñṇa-rasaṁ śuciṁ \|*  
*su-gopyaṁ paramodāraṁ na vaktavyaṁ hi kasyacit \|\|114\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Nārada! Please listen. I will explain the mādhurya-rasa of Rādhā and Kṛñṇa. It is most exalted, most secret and should not be revealed to others.**

*aikāntika-rasāsvādaṁ kartuṁ vṛndāvane mune \|*  
*vraja-rāja-kumāraṁ ca bahu-kālam abhāvayam \|\|115\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! I have meditated a long time on Kṛñṇa in Vṛndāvana in order to taste that pure rasa.**

*mayi prasannaḥ śrī-kṛñṇo mantra-yugmam anuttamam \|*  
*yugalākhyaṁ dadau mahyaṁ svīyojjvala-rasāplutam \|\|116\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Pleased with me, Kṛñṇa gave me the topmost mantra called the yugala-mantra, full of his mādhurya-rasa.**

*samabravīt tadā kṛñṇaḥ sva-śiñyaṁ māṁ svakaṁ rasam \|*  
*bravīmi tvāṁ śṛṇuñvādya brahmādīnām agocaram \|\|117\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛñṇa then explained to me, his disciple, about his rasa. I speak to you today what is beyond the comprehension of Brahmā and others. Please listen.**

*vraja-rāja-suto vṛndā-vane pūrṇatamo vasan \|*  
*sampūrṇa-ñoḍaśa-kalā vihāraṁ kurute sadā \|\|118\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Nanda, most complete in sixteen parts, living in Vṛndāvana, plays eternally.**

*vāsudevaḥ pūrṇataro mathurāyāṁ vasan puri \|*  
*kalābhiḥ pañca-daśabhir yutaḥ krīḍati sarvadā \|\|119\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Vasudeva, more perfect (pūrṇatara), lives and plays in Mathurā at all times, endowed with fifteen parts.**

*dvārakādhipatir dvāra-vatyāṁ pūrṇas tv asau vasan \|*  
*catur-daśa-kalāyukto viharaty eva sarvadā \|\|120\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord of Dvārakā, living in Dvārakā, being perfect (pūrṇa), plays at all times, endowed with fourteen parts.**

*ekayā kalayā dvābhyāṁ mathurā-dvārakādhipau \|*  
*vṛndāvana-pate rūpau pūrṇau sve sve pade rase \|\|121\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The couple in Vṛndāvana are complete with portions and rasa in comparison to the lords of Mathurā and Dvārakā by having one portions and two portions more.**

*mathurā-nātho vṛndāvanādhipāpekñayā svarūpeṇa līlayā ca ekayā kalayā ūnaḥ \|*  
*mathurā-līlāyāṁ mathurāyāṁ ca sampūrṇa-ñoḍaśa-kalaḥ\|*  
*tathā dvārakā-nātho vṛndāvanādhipāpekñayā svarūpeṇa līlayā ca\|*  
*dvābhyāṁ kalābhyām ūnaḥ \|*  
*dvārakāyāṁ dvārakā-līlāyāṁ ca pūrṇañoḍaśa-kalaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**The lord of Mathurā is one portion less in pastimes and svarūpacompared to the lord of Vṛndāvana. Kṛñṇa of Vṛndāvana is full with sixteen parts, including Mathurā and Mathurā pastimes. The Lord of Dvārakā is two portions less in svarūpa and pastimes compared to the Lord of Vṛndāvana. Kṛñṇa is full in sixteen parts, including Dvārakā and Dvārakā pastimes.**

*śrīr bhū-līlā yogamāyā cintyācintyā tathaiva ca \|*  
*mohinī kauśalīty añṭau bahiraṅgāś ca śaktayaḥ \|\|122\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are eight external śaktis: śrī, bhū, līlā, yogamāyā, cintyā, acintyā, mohinī and kauśalī.**

*līlā prema-svarūpā ca sthāpany ākarñaṇī tathā \|*  
*saṁyoginī viyoginyā-hlādinīty antaraṅgikā \|\|123\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are eight internal śaktis: līlā, prema, svarūpa, sthāpanī, ākarñaṇī, saṁyoginī, viyoginī and hlaḍinī.**

*vraje śrī-kṛñṇa-candrasya santi ñoḍaśa-śaktayaḥ \|*  
*poñikā madhurasyaiva tasyaitā vai sanātanāḥ \|\|124\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All sixteen śaktis of Kṛñṇa are present in Vraja. They are eternal and nourish the sweetness (mādhurya).**

*hlādinī ya mahā-śaktiḥ sarva-śakti-varīyasī \|*  
*tat-sāra-bhāva-rūpā śrī-rādhikā parikīrtitā \|\|125\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hlādinī, which is the greatest śakti, supreme among all, is celebrated as Rādhikā, the personification of prema’s essence.**

*tayā śrī-kṛñṇa-candrasya kriḍāyāḥ samaye mune \|*  
*tad-āviñṭaṁ vāsudevaṁ saha kñīrābdhi-nāyakam \|\|126\|\|*  
*antarīkñya-gataṁ kuryāc chaktir ākarñaṇī hareḥ \|*  
*krīḍānte sthāpayet tan tu sthāpanī kṛñṇa-dehataḥ \|\|127\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! At the time of Kṛñṇa’s pastimes with Rādhā, the ākarñaṇīśakti of Kṛñṇa pulls Kñirodakaśāyī Viñṇu and Vāsudeva22 who are merged in his body23 from Kṛñṇa’s body into the sky. After the pastimes the sthāpanī-śakti places them back in Kṛñṇa’s body.**

*sampūrṇa-ñoḍaśa-kalaḥ kevalo nanda-nandanaḥ \|*  
*vikrīḍan rādhayā sārdhaṁ labhate paramaṁ sukham \|\|128\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Only Kṛñṇa, the son of Nanda, is complete in sixteen portions (śaktis). He plays with Rādhā and attains the highest happiness.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca-*  
*gate madhu-purīṁ kṛñṇe vipralambha-rasaḥ katham \|*  
*vāsudeve rādhikāyāḥ saṁśayaṁ chindhi me prabho \|\|129\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛñṇa goes to Mathurā, how can Rādhā feel the rasa of separation for Vāsudeva (the form of Kṛñṇa in Mathurā)? Please relieve me of this doubt, O master!**

*śrī-sadāśiva uvāca-*  
*śaktiḥ saṁyoginī kāmā vāmā śaktir viyoginī \|*  
*hlādinī kīrtidā-putrī caivaṁ rādhā-trayaṁ vraje \|\|130\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sadāśiva said:**  
**In Vraja, Rādhā exists in three forms:** ***saṁyoginī or kāmā*****(uniting), vāma or viyoginī (separating) and hlādinī or kīrtidāputrī, daughter of Kīrtidā (bliss).**

*mama prāṇeśvaraḥ kṛñṇas tyaktvā vṛndāvanaṁ kvacit \|*  
*kadācin naiva yātīti jānīte kīrtidā-sutā \|\|131\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kīrtidā-sutā thinks, “The lord of my life Kṛñṇa never leaves Vṛndāvana to go elsewhere.”**

*kāmā-vāme na jānīta iti ca brahma-nandana \|*  
*rāsārambha ivāntardhiṁ gatavān nanda-nandanaḥ \|\|132\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O son of Brahmā! Kāma-śakti and Vāma-śakti (her other forms) do not know this. Thus Kṛñṇa leaves, just as he disappeared at the beginning of the rāsa dance.**

*mathurāṁ mathurā-nātho vāsudevo jagāma ha \|*  
*antar-hite nanda-sute śrīmad-vṛndāvane mune \|\|133\|\|*  
*pravāsākhyaṁ rasaṁ lebhe rādhā vai kīrtidā-sutā \|*  
*tato vadanti munayaḥ pravāsaṁ saṅga-vicyutim \|\|134\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The lord of Mathurā, Vāsudeva, went to Mathurā. O sage! When Kṛñṇa disappears Rādhā, kīrtidā-sutā, feels the rasa of prolonged separation in Vṛndāvana. The sages called this type of separation pravāsa.**

*mama jīvana-netā ca tyaktvā māṁ mathurāṁ gataḥ \|*  
*iti vihvalitā vāmā rādhā yā virahād abhūt \|\|135\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Overwhelmed, Vāma Rādhā experiences separation, thinking, “The lord of my life has given me up and gone to Mathurā.”**

*yamunāyāṁ nimagnā sā prakāśaṁ gokulasya ca \|*  
*golokaṁ prāpya tatrābhūt saṁyoga-rasa-peśalā \|\|136\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She submerges herself in the Yamunā and attains Goloka, a manifestation of Gokula, and there enjoys the beauty of the rasa of union.**

*kāmā rādhā ca mathurā-viraheṇa nipīḍitā \|*  
*kurukñetraṁ gatā tīrtha-yātrā-parama-lālasā \|\|137\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kāma Rādhā, extremely pained by separation from Kṛñṇa living Mathurā, goes to Kurukñetra, extremely desirous of going on pilgrimage (to meet him).**

*nanda-nandana-bhāva-jña uddhavo vrajam āgataḥ \|*  
*sāntvayiñyan kīrtidāyāḥ sutāṁ māsa-dvaye gate \|\|138\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Understanding the mood of Kṛñṇa, Uddhava went to Vraja and for two months pacified Kīrtidā-sutā.**

*rādhām āsvādayāmāsa śrīmad-bhāgavatārthakaṁ \|*  
*kathāyāṁ bhāgavatyāṁ tu jātāyāṁ muni-puṅgava \|\|139\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O best of sages! Explaining the meaning Bhāgavatam to her, he pacified Rādhā.**

*vrajendra-nandanaḥ śrīmāṁs tadā pratyakñatāṁ gataḥ \|\|140\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛñṇa, son of Nanda, then revealed himself to Rādhā.**

*ataeva pādmottara-khaṇḍoktaṁ dvārakādhipater vṛndāvanaṁ prati*  
*gamanaṁ kñīrābdhiśāyy āviñṭatvāt kñīrābdhiśāyino droṇādīnāṁ labdha*  
*vara-tvāt, teñāṁ punaḥ sva-sthāna-prāpaṇārtham evety avagantavyam*  
*\| śrīmad-bhāgavata-vākyānām evaṁ vicāro ’vagantavyaḥ padmottara*  
*khaṇḍe tu “kālindi-puline ramye” ity atra śrī-dvārakā-nāthasya śrī*  
*nanda-nandana-madhura-līlā-saṁdarśane sotkaṇṭhatvād vyoma-yānair*  
*etya śrī-vṛndāvane māsa-dvayam uvāsety abhiprāyo jñeyaḥ \| tad yathā*  
*śrī-lalita-mādhave (8.34)--“aparikalita-pūrvaḥ” ityādi \|*

**Перевод:**

**It is understood from Padma Purāṇa that Kṛñṇa (lord of Dvārakā) returned to Vṛndāvana. Kñirodakaśāyī had been merged in Kṛñṇa’s body, just as Droṇa (Vasu) and others who had received the boon of entering into Nanda and others bodies. When Kṛñṇa returned to Vṛndāvana, this was the pretext for their returning to their respective abodes. This return of Kṛñṇa is also understood to be the conclusion of Bhāgavatam.**  
**Padma Purāṇa says:**

*kālindyāḥ puline ramye puṇya-vṛkña-samācite*  
*gopa-nārībhir aniśaṁ krīḍayām āsa keśavaḥ*  
*ramya-keli-sukhenaiva gopa-veśa-dharaḥ prabhuḥ*  
*bahu-prema-rasenātra māsa-dvayam uvāsa ha*

**Перевод:**

**Keśava sported continuously with the Vraja-gopīs on the beautiful bank of the Kālindī, which was embellished with auspicious trees. Dressed as a cowherd boy, Kṛñṇa stayed there for two months immersed in deep love enjoying various pastimes of blissful reciprocation.**  
**From this it is understood that the Lord of Dvārakā, eager to see the pastimes of Kṛñṇa in Vṛndāvana, coming on an air vehicle, stayed two months in Vṛndāvana.**  
**While in Dvārakā, Kṛñṇa says in Lalita-mādhava:**

*aparikalita-pūrvaḥ kaś camatkāra-kārī*  
*sphurati mama garīyān eña mādhurya-pūraḥ \|*  
*ayam aham api hanta prekñya yaṁ lubdha-cetāḥ*  
*sarabhasam upabhoktuṁ kāmaye rādhikeva \|\|34\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have never before seen myself with such astonishing sweetness. I become greedy and impetuously desire to enjoy it like Rādhā.**

*iti te sarvam ākhyātaṁ naityikaṁ caritaṁ hareḥ \|*  
*pāpino ’pi vimucyante smaraṇād yasya nārada \|\|141\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have explained to you all the daily pastimes of Kṛñṇa. Remembering these pastimes even the sinful become liberated, O Nārada!**

*añṭa-kālokta-śuśrūñānantaraṁ sādhakaḥ kramāt \|*  
*dvātriṁśad-akñara-mukhyān japen mantrān atandritaḥ \|\|142\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After practicing diligently the meditation on the pastimes during eight parts of the day, the sādhaka should attentively chant the thirty-two syllable mantra (Hare Kṛñṇa).**

*mahā-mantraṁ japed ādau daśārṇaṁ tad-anantaram \|*  
*tataḥ śrī-rādhikā-mantraṁ gāyatrīṁ kāmakīṁ tathā \|\|143\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**First, the sādhaka should chant the mahā-mantra and then ten syllable mantra. After that he should chant the Rādhā mantra and the kāma-gāyatrī.**

*tato yugala-mantraṁ ca japed rāsa-sthalī-pradam \|*  
*tato ’ñṭānāṁ sakhīnāṁ ca japen mantrān yathā-kramam \|*  
*tataḥ ’ñṭa -mañjarīṇāṁ ca sva-sva-mantrān kramāj japet \|\|144\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Then he should chant the yugala-mantra, which bestows the place of the rāsa dance, and then the mantras of the eight sakhīsin order and then the mantras of the eight mañjarīs.**

*yathā ādi-purāṇe-*  
*gopī-bhāvena ye bhaktā mām eva paryupāsate \|*  
*teñu tāsv iva tuñṭo ’smi satyaṁ satyaṁ dhanañjaya \|\|145\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Ādī-purāṇa says:**  
**O Dhanañjaya! Truly, truly I am pleased with those devotees who worship me in the mood of the gopīs.**

*veśa-bhūñā-vayo-rūpair gopikā-bhāvam āśritāḥ \|*  
*bhāvukīyāś ca tad-bhāvaṁ yānti pāda-rajo ’rcanāt \|\|146\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having taken shelter of the mood of the gopīs with their dress, ornaments, age and form, those persons having a taste for this attain that bhāva, because of worshipping the dust from their feet.**

*yathā ekāmra-purāṇe--*  
*aho bhajana-māhātmyaṁ vṛndāvana-pater hareḥ \|*  
*pumān yoñid bhaved yatra yoñid-ātma-samānikā \|\|147\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Ekāmra Purāṇa it is said:**  
**What power there is in worshipping Kṛñṇa, lord of Vṛndāvana, in which either a man and woman becomes a woman!**

*pādme ca (uttara-khaṇḍe)--*  
*purā maharñayaḥ sarve daṇḍakāraṇya-vāsinaḥ \|*  
*rāmaṁ dṛñṭvā hariṁ tatra bhoktum aicchan su-vigraham \|\|148\|\|*  
*te sarve strītvam āpannāḥ samudbhūtāś ca gokule \|*  
*hariṁ samprāpya kāmena tato muktā bhavārṇavāt \|\|149\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the Padma Purāṇa it is said:**  
**Previously all the great sages living in Daṇḍakāraṇya forest, after seeing Rāma, desired to enjoy that beautiful form there. They all became women and were born in Gokula, attaining the Lord with mādhurya sentiments and become liberated from the ocean of saṁsāra.**

*bṛhad-vāmana-siddhāś ca śrutayo ’pi yathā purā \|*  
*gopī-bhāvena saṁsevya samudbhūtā hi gokule \|\|150\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Similarly the siddhas mentioned in Bṛhad-vāmana Purāṇa and the śrutis long ago serving the Lord in the mood of gopīs, were born in Gokula.**

*yad-uktaṁ śrī-rūpa-gosvāmi-caraṇaiḥ—*  
*hariṁ su-rāga-mārgeṇa sevate yo narottamaḥ \|*  
*kevalenaiva sa tadā gopikātvam iyād vraje \|\|151\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rūpa Gosvāmī says:**  
**The best of men who serves Kṛñṇa according to the path of pure rāga attains a body of a gopīs in Vraja.**

*bhakti-tattva-kaumudyām--*  
*ekasmin vāsanā-dehe yadi cānyasya bhāvanā \|*  
*tarhi tat sāmyam eva syāt yathā vai bharate nṛpe \|\|152\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Bhakti-tattva-kaumudī it is said:**  
**If one thinks of a different body (from his present one) in his mind, he will attain such a body, as in the case of King Bharata.**

*yathā sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām—*  
*śrī-nārada uvāca-*  
*dhanyo ’smy anugṛhīto ’smi tvayā devi na saṁśayaḥ \|*  
*harer me naityikī līlā yato me ’dya prakāśitā \|\|153\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā says:**  
**Nārada said:**  
**O Vṛndā devī! I am fortunate. I have attained your mercy without doubt, because today I have understood the daily pastimes of Kṛñṇa.**

*śrī-sanat-kumāra uvāca--*  
*ity uktvā tāṁ parikramya tayā cāpi prapūjitaḥ \|*  
*antardhānaṁ gato rājan nārado muni-sattamaḥ \|\|154\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra said:**  
**O king! Thus speaking, circumambulating Vṛndā-devī, and being worshipped by her, Nārada, the best of sages, disappeared.**

*mayāpy etad ānupūrvyaṁ sarvaṁ tat parikīrtitam \|*  
*japan nityaṁ prayatnena mantra-yugmam anuttamam \|\|155\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have glorified this incomparable yugala-mantra completely in order, which should be chanted daily with care.**

*kṛñṇa-vaktrād idaṁ labdhaṁ purā rudreṇa yatnataḥ \|*  
*tenoktaṁ nāradāyātha nāradena mayoditam \|\|156\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Long ago Śiva obtained this mantra from Kṛñṇa’s mouth. Śiva spoke it to Nārada. Nārada has spoken it to me.**

*saṁsārāgni-vināśāya mayāpy etat tavoditam \|*  
*tvayā caitad gopanīyaṁ rahasyaṁ paramādbhutam \|\|157\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have spoken it to you, so that you can destroy the fire of saṁsāra. You should conceal this most astonishing secret.**

*śrī-ambarīña uvāca-*  
*kṛta-kṛtyo ’bhavaṁ sākñāt tvat prasādād ahaṁ guro \|*  
*rahasyātirahasyaṁ yat tvayā mahyaṁ prakāśitam \|\|158\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Ambarīña said:**  
**I have become successful since, because of your direct mercy, you have revealed to me the greatest secret.**

*śrī-sanat-kumāra uvāca--*  
*dharmān etān upādiñṭo japan mantram ahar niśam \|*  
*acirād eva tad-dāsyam avāpsyasi na saṁśayaḥ \|\|159\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra said:**  
**Chanting this yugala-mantra day and night, being instructed about this process of serving in eight parts of the day, you will without doubt obtain service to the Lord without delay as a mañjarī.**

*“etān dharmān--añṭa-kāla-sevā-rūpān; mantram--yugala-mantram; tad*  
*dāsyam--tayoḥ śrī-rādhā-kṛñṇayor dāsyaṁ dāsī-bhāvam” iti \|*

**Перевод:**

**Etān dharmā means service in eight parts of the day. Mantra means the yugala-mantra. tad dāsyam means to become a female servant of Rādhā and Kṛñṇa.**

*mayāpi gamyate rājan guror āyatanaṁ mama \|*  
*vṛndāvane yatra nityaṁ gurur me ’sti sadāśivaḥ \|\|160\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O king! I will go to the abode of my guru in Vṛndāvana, where my guru Sadāśiva stays at all times.**

**Results of Chanting the Mantras:**

*dvātriṁśad-akñarādīnāṁ mantrāṇāṁ krameṇa phalaṁ*

**Перевод:**

**The results of the thirty-two syllable and other mantras are now described in order.**

*yathā pādme-*  
*dvātriṁśad-akñaraṁ mantraṁ nāma-ñoḍaśakānvitam \|*  
*prajapan vaiñṇavo nityaṁ rādhā-kṛñṇa-sthalaṁ labhet \|\|161\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Padma Purāṇa says:**  
**The devotee who chants the thirty-two syllable mantra with sixteen names will obtain the abode of Rādhā and Kṛñṇa eternally.**

*gautamīya-tantre ca-*  
*ahar-niśaṁ japen mantraṁ mantrī niyata-mānasaḥ \|*  
*sa paśyati na sandeho gopa-rūpiṇam īśvaram \|\|162\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Gautamīya-tantra says:**  
**Chanting the mantra day and night, the chanter, with controlled mind, without doubt will see the Lord in the form of cowherd.**

*gaurī-tantre ca--*  
*śrīmad-añṭākñaraṁ mantraṁ rādhāyāḥ prema-siddhi-dam \|*  
*prajapet sādhako yas tu sa rādhāntikam āpnuyāt \|\|163\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Gaurī-tantra says:**  
**The sādhaka who chants the eight syllable mantra of Rādhā, which gives prema, will attain the association of Rādhā.**

*sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām--*  
*japed yaḥ kāma-gāyatrīṁ kāma-bīja-samanvitām \|*  
*tasya siddhir bhavet prema rādhā-kṛñṇa-sthalaṁ vrajet \|\|164\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā says:**  
**The person who chants the kāma-gāyatrī with the kāma-bīja will attain prema and go to the abode of Rādhā and Kṛñṇa.**

*etāṁ pañca-padīṁ japtvā śraddhayā ’śraddhayāsakṛt \|*  
*vṛndāvane tayor dāsyaṁ gacchaty eva na saṁśayaḥ \|\|165\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Chanting that five part mantra with faith, or even without faith constantly, one will attain service to Rādhā and Kṛñṇa in Vṛndāvana without doubt.**

*kiśorī-tantre ca--*  
*etān sakhīnām añṭānāṁ mantrān yaḥ sādhako japet \|*  
*śrī-rādhā-kṛñṇayoḥ kñipraṁ vihāra-sthalam āpnuyāt \|\|166\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kiśorī-tantra says:**  
**The sādhaka who chants the mantras of the eight sakhīs will quickly attain the the abode where Rādhā and Kṛñṇa perform their pastimes.**

*tatraiva--*  
*mantrān etān mañjarīṇām añṭānāṁ yo japet sadā \|*  
*prema-siddhir bhavet tasya śrī-vṛndāvanam āpnuyāt \|\|167\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kisori-tantra says:**  
**The person who always chants the mantras of the eight mañjarīs will attain prema and Vṛndāvana.**

*smaraṇānantaraṁ siddha-dehasyaiva ca sādhakaḥ \|*  
*añṭa-kāloditāṁ līlāṁ saṁsmaret sādhakāṅgakaḥ \|\|168\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka, though still possessing a body of a sādhaka, after remembering his siddha-deha, should remember the pastimes in the eight parts of the day.**

*kālau niśānta-pūrvāhnāv aparāhna-pradoñakau \|*  
*vijñeyau tri-tri-ghaṭikau prātaḥ sāyaṁ dvayaṁ dvayam \|\|169\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**End of night, forenoon, afternoon and night each last three hours. Morning and twilight last two hours each.**

*dvi-dvi-praghaṭikau jñeyau madhyāhna-rātrikāv iti \|\|170\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Noon and late night last four hours each.**

*eteñu samayeñv evaṁ yā yā līlā puroditā \|*  
*tāṁ tām eva yathā-kālaṁ saṁsmaret sādhako janaḥ \|\|171\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka should meditate on the pastimes that arise at these times.**

*iti śrī-dhyāna-candra-gosvāmi-viracitā*  
*śrī śrī rādhā-kṛñṇāñṭa-kālīya-līlā-smaraṇa-krama-paddhatiḥ \|*
