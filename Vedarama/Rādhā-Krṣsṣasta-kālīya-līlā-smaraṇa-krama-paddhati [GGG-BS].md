Sequential Method of Remembering the Pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa during Eight parts of the Day.

# Rādhā-Krṣsṣasta-kālīya-līlā-smaraṇa-krama-paddhati \[GGG-BS\]

## Rādhā-Kṛṣṇayor aṣṭa-kālīya-līlā-smaraṇa-krama-paddhati

*śrī-śrī-rādhā-mādhavau jayataḥ*  
*evaṁ padmopari dhyātvā rādhā-kṛṣṇau tatas tayoḥ \|*  
*aṣṭa-kālocitāṁ sevāṁ vidadhyāt siddha-dehataḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having meditated on Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa on the lotus, in one’s siddha-deha one should perform service suitable to eight parts of the day.**

*niśāntaḥ prātaḥ pūrvāhṇo madhyāhnaś cāparāhṇakaḥ \|*  
*sāyaṁ pradoṣo rātriś ca kālā aṣṭau yathā-kramam \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The eight parts of the day in order are: end-of-night, early morning, forenoon, noon, afternoon, twilight, evening, and night.**

*madhyāhno yāminī cobhau ṣaṇ-muhūrta-mitau smṛtau \|*  
*tri-muhūrta-mitā jñeyā niśānta-pramukhāḥ pare \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Noon and night last six muhūrtas and other periods last three muhūrtas.**

*teṣu siddha-dehena sevanaṁ yathā sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām,*

**Перевод:**

**The service in the siddha-deha is described in Sanat-kumārasaṁhitā:**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**

*bhagavan sarvam ākhyātaṁ yad yat pṛṣṭhaṁ tvayā guro \|*  
*adhunā śrotum icchāmi rāga-mārgam anuttamam \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O guru, O master! You have explained everything that I asked. Now I desire to hear about the topmost rāga-marga.**

*śrī-sadāśiva uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Sadāśiva said:**

*sādhu pṛṣṭhaṁ tvayā vipra sarva-loka-hitaiṣiṇā \|*  
*rahasyam api vakṣyāmi tan me nigaditaṁ śṛṇu \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You have asked nicely, O brāhmaṇa who desires the welfare of all people! I will explain the secret. Please listen to what I say.**

*parakīyābhimāninyas tathāsya ca priyā janāḥ \|*  
*pracureṇaiva bhāvena ramayanti nija-priyam \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The beloveds of Kṛṣṇa, thinking themselves married to others, give pleasure to Kṛṣṇa with their deep love.**

*ātmānaṁ cintayet tatra tāsāṁ madhye manoramām \|*  
*rūpa-yauvana-sampannāṁ kiśorīṁ pramadākṛtim \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should meditate that one is an attractive girl of kaiśora age among them and endowed with beauty and youth.**

*nānā-śilpa-kalābhijñāṁ kṛṣṇa-bhogānurūpiṇīm \|*  
*prārthitām api kṛṣṇena tato bhoga-parāṅ-mukhīm \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This girl is expert in various arts, gives pleasure to Kṛṣṇa, but even if requested by Kṛṣṇa, is not inclined to enjoy with him personally.**

*rādhikānucarīṁ nityaṁ tat-sevana-parāyaṇām \|*  
*kṛṣṇād apy adhikaṁ prema rādhikāyāṁ prakurvatīm \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She follows Rādhā at all times and dedicated to serving her. She has more love for Rādhā than for Kṛṣṇa.**

*prītyānudivasaṁ yatnāt tayoḥ saṅgama-kāriṇīm \|*  
*tat-sevana-sukhāsvāda-bhareṇātisunirvṛtām \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Out of affection she diligently arranges for Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa to meet each other every day. She is most blissful by relishing the happiness of serving them.**

*ity ātmānaṁ vicintyaiva tatra sevāṁ samācaret \|*  
*brāhma-muhūrtam ārabhya yāvat sāntā mahā-niśā \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Thinking of oneself in this way, one should perform service, starting from brāhma-muhūrta until the calm at midnight.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**

*harer atra gatāṁ līlāṁ śrotum icchāmi tattvataḥ \|*  
*līlām ajānatāṁ sevyo manasā tu kathaṁ hariḥ \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I desire to hear truthfully the pastimes performed here by Kṛṣṇa. How can one serve him in the mind if one is ignorant of the pastimes?**

*śrī-sadāśiva uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Sadāśiva said:**

*nāhaṁ jānāmi tāṁ līlāṁ harer nārada tattvataḥ \|*  
*vṛndā-devīṁ samāgaccha sā te līlāṁ pravakṣyati \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Nārada! Actually I do not know the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa. Go to Vṛndā-devī. She will explain the pastimes to you.**

*avidūre itaḥ sthānāt keśī-tīrtha-samīpataḥ \|*  
*sakhībhiḥ sahitā sāste govinda-paricārikā \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This servant of Kṛṣṇa along with sakhīs is near Keśi-tīrtha, not far from here.**

*śrī-sanat-kumāra uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra said:**

*ity uktas taṁ parikramya guruṁ natvā punaḥ punaḥ \|*  
*vṛndā-sthānaṁ jagāmāsau nārado muni-sattamaḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Sadāśiva said this, Nārada, the best of sages, circumambulated him, offered respects repeatedly and went to Vṛndā’s place.**

*vṛndāpi nāradaṁ dṛṣṭvā praṇamyāpi punaḥ punaḥ \|*  
*uvāca taṁ muni-śreṣṭhaṁ katham atrāgatis tava \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Nārada, Vṛndā offered him respects repeatedly. She said to the best of sages, “How is that you have come here?”**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**

*tvatto veditum icchāmi naityikaṁ caritaṁ hareḥ \|*  
*tad ādito mama bruhi yadi yogyo ’smi śobhane \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I desire to know from you the daily pastimes of Kṛṣṇa. O beautiful Vṛndā! If I am worthy, please tell me everything from the beginning.**

*śrī-vṛndā-devy uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndā-devī said:**

*rahasyaṁ tvāṁ pravakṣyāmi kṛṣṇa-bhakto ’si nārada \|*  
*na prakāśyaṁ tvayā hy etad guhyād guhyataraṁ mahat \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I will explain this secret to you. O Nārada! You are devotee of Kṛṣṇa. This great secret of secrets should not be revealed by you.**

*atha niśānta-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Service at the end of night:**

*madhye vṛndāvane ramye pañcāśat-kuñja-maṇḍite \|*  
*kalpa-vṛkṣa-nikuñje tu divya-ratna-maye gṛhe \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In charming Vṛndāvana, decorated with fifty bowers, in a bower made of desire trees, in a house made of attractive gems,**

*nidritau tiṣṭhatas talpe niviḍāliṅgitau mithaḥ \|*  
*mad-ājñā-kāribhiḥ paścāt pakṣibhir bodhitāv api \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The couple remain sleeping, tightly embracing. They are awakened later by my order carriers, the birds.**

*gāḍhāliṅgana-nirbhedam āptau tad-bhaṅga-kātarau \|*  
*na manas kurutas talpāt samutthātuṁ manāg api \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having attained an unbreakable, tight embrace, they feel pained to break it. They do not have determination to get up from the bed at all.**

*tataś ca śārikā-saṁghaiḥ śukādyair api tau muhuḥ \|*  
*bodhitau vividhaiḥ padyaiḥ sva-talpād udatiṣṭhatām \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Finally awakened by parrots and śārikas reciting various verses, they rise from their bed.**

*upaviṣṭau tato dṛṣṭvā sakhyas talpe mudānvitau \|*  
*praviśya cakrire sevāṁ tat-kālasyocitāṁ tayoḥ \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing them sitting there on the bed in bliss, the sakhīs enter and perform service for them which is appropriate to the time.**

*punaś ca śārikā-vākyair utthāya tau sva-talpataḥ \|*  
*gacchataḥ sva-sva-bhavanaṁ bhīty-utkaṇṭhākulau mithaḥ \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By the words of the śārikās they rise from the bed, and go to their houses, filled with fear and longing.**

*iti niśānta-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**(completion of end of night service)**

*atha prātaḥ-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Early morning service:**

*prātaś ca bodhito mātrā talpād utthāya satvaram \|*  
*kṛtvā kṛṣṇo danta-kāṣṭhaṁ baladeva-samanvitaḥ \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Awakened by his mother, Kṛṣṇa along with Balarāma quickly rises from bed and brushes his teeth.**

*mātrānumodito yāti gośālāṁ dohanotsukaḥ \|*  
*rādhāpi bodhitā vṛddha-vayasyābhiḥ sva-talpataḥ \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With the permission of his mother, he goes to the cow sheds, eager to milk the cows. Rādhā also is awakened by elders and friends from her bed.**

*utthāya danta-kāṣṭhādi kṛtvābhyaṅgaṁ samācaret \|*  
*snāna-vedīṁ tato gatvā snāpitā lalitādibhiḥ \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She rises, brushes her teeth, and has a massage. Going to the bathing platform she is bathed by Lalitā and others.**

*bhūṣā-gṛhaṁ vrajet tatra vayasyā bhūṣayanty api \|*  
*bhūṣaṇair vividhair divyair gandha-mālyānulepanaiḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She goes to the dressing room. There, friends decorate her with various attractive ornaments, ointments, scents and garlands.**

*tataś ca sva-janais tasyāḥ śvaśrūṁ samprārthya yatnataḥ \|*  
*paktum āhūyate tūrṇaṁ sa-sakhī sā yaśodayā \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Yaśodā’s servants carefully make a request to Rādhā’s mother-inlaw. Rādhā and her sakhīs are called by Yaśodā to cook quickly for Kṛṣṇa.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**

*katham āhūyate devi pākārthaṁ sā yaśodayā \|*  
*satīṣu pāka-kartrīṣu rohiṇī-pramukhāsv api \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Vṛndā-devī! Why is she called by Yaśodā to cook, since there are many cooks headed by Rohiṇī?**

*śrī-vṛndovāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndā said:**

*durvāsasā svayaṁ datto varas tasyai maharṣiṇā \|*  
*iti kātyāyanī-vaktrāc chrutam āsīn mayā purā \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She was given a benediction by the great sage Durvāsa. I heard this previously from the mouth of Kātyāyanī.**

*tvayā yat pacyate devi tad-annaṁ mad-anugrahāt \|*  
*miṣṭaṁ svādv-amṛta-spardhi bhoktur āyuṣkaraṁ tathā \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“O Rādhā! Whatever you cook, by my mercy that food will be sweet, tastier than nectar and will give long life to the eater.”**

*ity āhvayati tāṁ nityaṁ yaśodā putra-vatsalā \|*  
*āyuṣmān me bhavet putraḥ svādu-lobhāt tathā satī \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**For this reason Yaśodā, out of affection for her son, calls Rādhā daily. She thinks, “May my son live long because of desiring that tasty food.”**

*śvaśrvānumoditā sāpi hṛṣṭā nandālayaṁ vrajet \|*  
*sa-sakhī-prakarā tatra gatvā pākaṁ karoti ca \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Permitted by her mother-in-law, Rādhā joyfully goes to Nanda’s house. Going with her sakhīs, she cooks.**

*kṛṣṇo ’pi dugdhvā gāḥ kāścid dohayitvā janaiḥ parāḥ \|*  
*āgacchati pitur vākyāt sva-gṛhaṁ sakhībhir vṛtaḥ \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After milking some cows and having others milk the rest of the cows, Kṛṣṇa, along with his friends, returns to his house by his father’s instruction.**

*abhyaṅga-mardanaṁ kṛtvā dāsaiḥ saṁsnāpito mudā \|*  
*dhauta-vastra-dharaḥ sragvī candanākta-kalevaraḥ \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having been joyfully massaged and bathed by servants, he puts on washed clothes, a garland and sandalwood.**

*dvi-phāla-baddha-keśaiś ca grīvā-bhālopari sphuran \|*  
*candrākāra-sphurad-bhāla-tilakālaka-rañjitaḥ \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Shining with hair bound up in two bundles over his neck and forehead, he is anointed with tilaka in the form of the moon on his forehead.**

*kaṅkaṇāṅgada-keyūra-ratna-mudrā-lasat-karaḥ \|*  
*muktāhāra-sphurad-vakṣā makarākṛti-kuṇḍalaḥ \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His hands shine with jeweled rings, bracelets, armlets and bangles. He has a pearl necklace on his chest and makara shaped earrings.**

*muhur ākārito mātrā praviśed bhojanālaye \|*  
*avalambya karaṁ mātur baladevam anuvrataḥ \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Constantly called by his mother, he enters the dining room, taking support of his mother’s hand, following Baladeva.**

*bhuktvā ca vividhānnāni mātrā ca sakhibhir vṛtaḥ \|*  
*hāsayan vividhair vākyaiḥ sakhīṁs tair hāsitaḥ svayam \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Surrounded by his friends and mother, he eats various foods. He makes his friends laugh by his various words and he is made to laugh by his friends.**

*itthaṁ bhuktvā tathācamya divya-khaṭṭopari kṣaṇāt \|*  
*viśramet sevakair dattaṁ tāmbūlaṁ vibhajann adan \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having eaten food and washed his mouth, he rests for a moment on attractive cot. Dividing up betel nut offered by servants, he eats it.**

*rādhāpi bhojanānandaṁ dṛṣṭvā yaśodayāhūtā \|*  
*lalitādi-sakhī-vṛtā bhuṅkte ’nnaṁ lajjayānvitā \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā, seeing him blissfully eating, is called by Yaśodā. Feeling shy, she eats, surrounded by Lalitā and other sakhīs.**

*iti prātaḥ-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**(end of morning service)**

*atha pūrvāhṇa-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Forenoon service:**

*gopa-veśa-dharaḥ kṛṣṇo dhenu-vṛnda-puraḥ-saraḥ \|*  
*vraja-vāsi-janaiḥ prītyā sarvair anugataḥ pathi \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Wearing cowherd dress, putting the cows in front, Kṛṣṇa goes along the path with all the people of Vraja out of affection for them.**

*pitaraṁ mātaraṁ natvā netrāntena priyā-gaṇān \|*  
*yathāyogyaṁ tathā cānyān sannivartya vanaṁ vrajet \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Offering respects to his mother and father, and to the gopīs with the corner of his eye, and to others as appropriate, he has them all return and goes to the forest.**

*vanaṁ praviśya sakhibhiḥ krīḍitvā ca kṣaṇaṁ tataḥ \|*  
*vañcayitvā ca tān sarvān dvi-traiḥ priya-sakhair yutaḥ \|\|45\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Entering the forest, playing with his friends for a short time, he then fools them all and goes off with two or three priya-sakhas.**

*saṅketakaṁ vrajed dharṣāt priyā-sandarśanotsukaḥ \|*  
*sāpi kṛṣṇe vanaṁ yāte dṛṣṭvā taṁ gṛham āgatā \|\|46\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He goes for an appointment with joy, eager to see his beloved Rādhā. When Kṛṣṇa goes to the forest, Rādhā, after seeing him, goes home.**

*sūryādi-pūjā-vyājena kusumādy-āhṛti-cchalāt \|*  
*vañcayitvā gurūn yāti priya-saṅgecchayā vanaṁ \|\|47\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Fooling the elders, on the pretext of gathering flowers for worship of the sun god, she goes to the forest with a desire to see Kṛṣṇa.**

*iti pūrvāhṇa-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**(end of forenoon service)**

*atha madhyāhna-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Noon service:**

*itthaṁ tau bahu-yatnena militvā sva-gaṇair vṛtau \|*  
*vihārair vividhais tatra vane vikrīḍato mudā \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In this way the two, after meeting with great effort, surrounded by their followers, joyfully play in the forest with various amusements.**

*āndolikā-samārūḍhau sakhībhir dolitau kvacit \|*  
*kvacid veṇuṁ kara-srastaṁ priyayā coritaṁ hariḥ \|\|49\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes the two mount a swing and are pushed by the sakhīs. Sometimes Rādhā steals his flute when it drops from his hand.**

*anveṣayann upālabdho vipralabdhaḥ priyā-gaṇaiḥ \|*  
*hāsito bahudhā tābhir hṛta-sva iva tiṣṭhati \|\|50\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He searches for it, and is criticized and insulted by the gopīs. Mocked by them, he acts as if his very self had been stolen.**

*vasanta-ṛtunā juṣṭaṁ vana-khaṇḍaṁ kvacin mudā \|*  
*praviśya candanāmbhobhiḥ kuṅkumādi-jalair api \|\|51\|\|*  
*viṣiñcato yantra-muktais tat-paṅkenāpi tau mithaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes they joyfully enter a forest decorated with spring season and sprinkle each other with water mixed with sandalwood and kuṅkuma from syringes.**

*sakhyo ’py evaṁ viṣiñcanti tāś ca tau siñcataḥ punaḥ \|\|52\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs also sprinkle them with the mixture, and Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa sprinkle them.**

*tathānya-rtusu juṣṭāsu krīḍato vana-rājiṣu \|*  
*tat-tat-kālocitair nānā-vihāraiḥ sa-gaṇau dvija \|\|53\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The two play in forests which manifest other seasons, along with their followers, performing various activities suitable to the seasons.**

*śrāntau kvacid vṛkṣa-mūlam āsādya muni-sattama \|*  
*upaviśyāsane divye madhu-pānaṁ pracakratuḥ \|\|54\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O best of sages! Sometimes, becoming tired they approach the base of a tree and, sitting on attractive seats, drink honey.**

*tato madhu-madonmattau nidrayā mīlitekṣaṇau \|*  
*mithaḥ pāṇiṁ samālambya kāma-bāṇa-vaśaṁ gatau \|\|55\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Becoming drunk from the honey, they close their eyes from drowsiness. Holding each other’s hands, they become controlled by Cupid’s arrows.**

*riraṁsū viśataḥ kuñjaṁ skhalat-pādābjakau pathi \|*  
*tato vikrīḍatas tatra kariṇī-yūthapau yathā \|\|56\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring amorous pleasure, they enter a bower, stumbling on their lotus feet. They then play like a male elephant and its mate.**

*sakhyo ’pi madhubhir mattā nidrayā pihitekṣaṇāḥ \|*  
*abhitaḥ kuñja-puñjeṣu sārvā eva vililyire \|\|57\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs also become drunk and close their eyes with sleep. They all disappear into the bowers nearby.**

*pṛthag ekena vapuṣā kṛṣṇo ’pi yugapad vibhuḥ \|*  
*sarvāsāṁ sannidhiṁ gacchet priyāṇāṁ parito muhuḥ \|\|58\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, in one body, simultaneously approaches all the gopīs who are situated all around individually.**

*ramayitvā ca tāḥ sarvāḥ kariṇī-gajarāḍ iva \|*  
*priyayā ca tathā tābhiḥ sarovaram athāvrajet \|\|59\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Giving them all enjoyment, he then goes to a lake with Rādhā and the sakhīs, like a male elephant with his herd.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**

*vṛnde śrī-nanda-putrasya mādhurya-krīḍane katham \|*  
*aiśvaryasya prakāśo ’bhūd iti me chindhi saṁśayam \|\|60\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Vṛndā! Why did Kṛṣṇa display power in the pastimes of sweetness? Please dispel my doubt.**

*śrī-vṛndovāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndā said:**

*mune mādhurya-mayy asti līlā-śaktir harer dṛḍhā \|*  
*tayā pṛthak-kṛtaḥ krīḍed gopikābhiḥ samaṁ hariḥ \|\|61\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! The strong līlā-śakti of the Lord is full of sweetness. By that śakti, he can play with the gopīs in separate forms.**

*rādhayā saha rūpeṇa nijena ramate svayam \|*  
*iti mādhurya-līlāyāḥ śaktir neśatāyā hareḥ \|\|62\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He personally enjoys with Rādhā in his own form. The śaktiproduces sweet pastimes. It does not show the Lord’s powers.**

*jala-sekair mithas tatra krīḍitvā sa-gaṇau tataḥ \|*  
*vāsaḥ-srak-candanair divya-bhūṣaṇair api bhūṣitau \|\|63\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sprinkling each other with water, along with their followers, they are decorated with clothing, sandalwood and attractive ornaments.**

*tatraiva sarasas tīre divya-ratna-maye gṛhe \|*  
*aśnītaḥ phala-mūlāni kalpitāni mayaiva hi \|\|64\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the bank of the lake, in a house made of attractive jewels, they eat fruits and roots prepared by me.**

*haris tu prathamaṁ bhuktvā kāntayā pariveśitam \|*  
*dvi-trābhiḥ sevito gacchec chayyāṁ puṣpa-vinirmitām \|\|65\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa first eats what Rādhā gives him. Served by two or three sakhīs, he goes to a bed made of flowers.**

*tāmbūlair vyajanais tatra pāda-saṁvāhanādibhiḥ \|*  
*sevyamāno bhṛśantābhir moditaḥ preyasīṁ smaran \|\|66\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Offered betel nut, fanned and having his feet massaged by the eager sakhīs, he is pleased and remembers Rādhā.**

*śrī-rādhāpi harau supte sa-gaṇā muditāntarā \|*  
*kānta-dattaṁ prīta-manā ucchiṣṭaṁ bubhuje tataḥ \|\|67\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛṣṇa sleeps, Rādhā and her friends, joyful within, pleased in mind, eat what has been given by Kṛṣṇa.**

*kiñcid evo tato bhuktvā vrajec chayyā-niketanam \|*  
*draṣṭuṁ kānta-mukhāmbhojaṁ cakorīvan niśā-karam \|\|68\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After eating a little, Rādhā goes to the sleeping room to see the lotus face of Kṛṣṇa, like a cakorī bird looking at the moon.**

*tāmbūla-carvitaṁ tasya tatratyābhir niveditam \|*  
*tāmbūlāny api cāśnāti vibhajantī priyāliṣu \|\|69\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She chews the betel nuts chewed by Kṛṣṇa, which are offered to her by sakhīs there. She divides the betel nuts among the sakhīsas well.**

*kṛṣṇo ’pi tāsāṁ śuśrūṣuḥ svacchandaṁ bhāṣitaṁ mithaḥ \|*  
*prāpta-nidra ivābhāti vinidro ’pi paṭāvṛtaḥ \|\|70\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring to hear the gopīs speak freely together, Kṛṣṇa pretends to be asleep, covered with a cloth, though he is wide awake.**

*tāś ca kṣvelīṁ kṣaṇaṁ kṛtvā mithaḥ kānta-kathāśrayāḥ \|*  
*vyāja-nidrāṁ harer jñātvā kutaścid anumānataḥ \|\|71\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They joke for a while together, exchanging talks. They then understand that Kṛṣṇa is awake.**

*vimṛśya vadanaṁ dṛgbhiḥ paśyantyo ’nyonyam ānanam \|*  
*līnā iva lajjayā syuḥ kṣaṇam ucur na kiñcanam \|\|72\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Concluding this by looking at his face, they look at each other’s faces. Seeming to disappear out of embarrassment, for a moment they say nothing.**

*kṣaṇād eva tato vastraṁ dūrī-kṛtya tad aṅgataḥ \|*  
*sādhu nidrāṁ gato ’sīti hāsayantyo hasanti tam \|\|73\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After a moment, they remove the cloth from his limbs. They say, “You have slept nicely.” They make him laugh and they laugh.**

*evaṁ tau vividhair hāsai ramamāṇau gaṇaiḥ saha \|*  
*anubhūya kṣaṇaṁ nidrā-sukhaṁ ca muni-sattama \|\|74\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! In this way the couple enjoy various jokes with the sakhīs, experiencing the happiness of sleep for a while.**

*upaviśyāsane divye sa-gaṇau vistṛte mudā \|*  
*paṇī-kṛtya mitho hāra-cumbāśleṣa-paricchadān \|\|75\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sitting on two attractive seats with their followers, expanding their joy, they throw dice for a necklace, a kiss, an embrace and some clothing.**

*akṣair vikrīḍitaḥ premṇā narmālāpa-puraḥsaram \|*  
*parājito ’pi priyayā jitam ity avadan mṛṣā \|\|76\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They play dice in prema, along with joking conversations. Though he is defeated, he does not admit that she has won, by lying.**

*hārādi-grahaṇe tasyāḥ pravṛttas tāḍyate tayā \|*  
*tayaivaṁ tāḍitaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ karṇotpala-saroruhaiḥ \|\|77\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When he tries to take her necklace, she beats him. He is beaten by lotuses and her flower ear ornaments.**

*viṣaṇṇa-vadano bhūtvā gata-sva iva nārada \|*  
*jito ’smi ca tvayā devi gṛhyatāṁ yat paṇī-kṛtam \|\|78\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With a sad face, as if having lost everything including himself, O Nāradā, he says, “I have been defeated by you. O goddess, you should accept what was wagered by me—the kisses.”**

*cumbanādi mayā dattam ity uktvā ca tathācarat \|*  
*kauṭilyaṁ tad-bhruvor draṣṭuṁ śrotuṁ tad-bhartsanaṁ vacaḥ \|\|79\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He does this in order to see her frown and hear her scolding words.**

*tataḥ śārī-śukānāṁ ca śrutvā vāg-āhavaṁ mithaḥ \|*  
*nirgacchatas tataḥ sthānād gantu-kāmo gṛhaṁ prati \|\|80\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing the śārīs and parrots quarrelling, they then leave the place, desiring to return home.**

*kṛṣṇaḥ kāntām anujñāpya gavām abhimukhaṁ vrajet \|*  
*sā tu sūrya-gṛhaṁ gacchet sakhī-maṇḍala-saṁyutā \|\|81\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Taking permission from Rādhā, Kṛṣṇa heads for the cows. She goes to the temple of the sun god with her sakhīs.**

*kiyad-dūraṁ tato gatvā parāvṛtya hariḥ punaḥ \|*  
*vipra-veśaṁ samāsthāya yāti sūrya-gṛhaṁ prati \|\|82\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After going some distance, Kṛṣṇa turns around and, adopting the dress of a brāhmaṇa, goes to the sun temple.**

*sūryaṁ ca pūjayet tatra prārthitas tat-sakhī-janaiḥ \|*  
*tad eva kalpitair vedaiḥ parihāsyāvagarbhitaiḥ \|\|83\|\|*  
*tatas tā api taṁ kāntaṁ parijñāya vicakṣaṇāḥ \|*  
*ānanda-sāgare līnā na viduḥ svaṁ na cāparam \|\|84\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Requested by the gopīs, he performs worship of the sun. By his ridiculous, concocted Vedic mantras, the perceptive gopīsrecognize that it is Kṛṣṇa. They drown in an ocean of bliss and do not know themselves or anyone else.**

*vihārair vividhair evaṁ sārdha-yāma-dvayaṁ mune \|*  
*nītvā gṛhaṁ vrajeyus tāḥ sa ca kṛṣṇo gavāṁ vrajet \|\|85\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! After spending between one and two yāmas playing in various ways, the gopīs go home and Kṛṣṇa goes to the cows.**

*iti madhyāhna-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**(end of noon service)**

*athāparāhṇa-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Afternoon service:**

*saṅgamya tu sakhīn kṛṣṇo gṛhītvā gāḥ samantataḥ \|*  
*āgacchati vrajaṁ karṣann uttāna-muralī-ravaiḥ \|\|86\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meeting his friends, Kṛṣṇa gathers all the cows and returns to Vraja, attracting them by the sound of his upturned flute.**

*tato nandādayaḥ sarve śrutvā veṇu-ravaṁ hareḥ \|*  
*go-dhūli-paṭalair vyāptaṁ dṛṣṭvā cāpi nabhaḥ-sthalam \|\|87\|\|*  
*visṛjya sarva-karmāṇi striyo bālādayo ’pi ca \|*  
*kṛṣṇasyābhimukhaṁ yānti tad-darśana-samutsukāḥ \|\|88\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Nanda and all others, hearing the sound of the Kṛṣṇa’s flute, seeing the sky spread with clouds of cow dust, give up all their activities and move towards Kṛṣṇa, eager to see him. Even the women and children go.**

*rādhikāpi samāgatya gṛhaṁ snātvā vibhūṣitā \|*  
*sampācya kānta-bhogārthaṁ dravyāṇi vividhāni ca \|*  
*sakhī-saṁgha-yutā yānti kāntaṁ draṣṭuṁ samutsukāḥ \|\|89\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā also returns home, bathes, decorates herself, and cooks various items for Kṛṣṇa to eat. With their sakhīs, in great eagerness, they go to see their beloved.**

*rāja-mārge vraja-dvāri yatra sarve vrajaukasaḥ \|*  
*kṛṣṇo ’py etān samāgamya yathāvad anupūrvaśaḥ \|\|90\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They go to the main road at the entrance of Vraja, where all the people of Vraja are standing. Kṛṣṇa meets everyone appropriately in order.**

*darśanaiḥ sparśanair vāpi smita-purvāvalokanaiḥ \|*  
*gopa-vṛddhān namaskāraiḥ kāyikair vācikair api \|\|91\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He greets them by showing himself, touching them, glancing with joy, and by offering respects to the elders with his body and words.**

*sāṣṭāṅga-pātaiḥ pitarau rohiṇīm api nārada \|*  
*netrānta-sūcitenaiva vinayena priyās tathā \|\|92\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Nārada! He falls on the ground before his parents and Rohiṇī and greets the gopīs with modesty by signals through the edge of his eyes.**

*evaṁ taiś ca yathā-yogyaṁ vrajaukobhiḥ prapūjitaḥ \|*  
*gavālayaṁ tathā gāś ca sampraveśya samantataḥ \|\|93\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He is worshipped by the people of Vraja in a suitable manner. He then leads the cows to their sheds.**

*pitṛbhyām arthito yāti bhrātrā saha nijālayam \|*  
*snātvā pitvā tathā kiñcid bhuktvā mātrānumoditaḥ \|*  
*gavālayaṁ punar yāti dogdhu-kāmo gavāṁ payaḥ \|\|94\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Requested by his parents, he goes with his brother to the house. After bathing, drinking and eating a little, with permission of his mother he again goes to the cow sheds, desiring to milk the cows.**

*ity aparāhṇa-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**(end of afternoon service)**

*atha sāyaṁ-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Twilight service:**

*tāś ca dugdhvā dohayitvā pāyayitvā ca kāścana \|*  
*pitrā sārdhaṁ gṛhaṁ yāti payo-bhāri-śatānugaḥ \|\|95\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After milking cows, having others milk cows, and having calves drink milk, he goes to the house along with his father and hundreds of milk carriers.**

*tatrāpi mātṛ-vṛndaiś ca tat-putraiś ca balena ca \|*  
*saṁbhuṅkte vividhānnāni carvya-cūṣyādikāni ca \|\|96\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He then eats various foods to be chewed and sucked along with the mothers and their sons and Balarāma.**

*iti sāyaṁ-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**(end of twilight service)**

*atha pradoṣa-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Evening service:**

*tan-mātuḥ prārthanāt pūrvaṁ rādhayāpi tadaiva hi \|*  
*prasthāpyante sakhī-dvārā pakvānnāni tad-ālayam \|\|97\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the request of Yaśodā, through a sakhī, Rādhā sends some cooked food to Kṛṣṇa’s house.**

*ślāghayaṁś ca haris tāni bhuktvā pitrādibhiḥ saha \|*  
*sabhā-gṛhaṁ vrajet taiś ca juṣṭaṁ vandi-janādibhiḥ \|\|98\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa praises and eats these foods along with the elders. He then goes to the assembly hall filled with panegyrists and other performers.**

*pakvānnāni gṛhītvā yāḥ sakhyas tatra samāgatāḥ \|*  
*bahūni ca punas tāni pradattāni yaśodayā \|\|99\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs who have come to the house take the cooked food remnants which are given profusely by Yaśodā.**

*sakhyā tatra tayā dattaṁ kṛṣṇocchiṣṭaṁ tathā rahaḥ \|*  
*sarvaṁ tābhiḥ samānīya rādhikāyai nivedyate \|\|100\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs take Kṛṣṇa’s remnants given by Yaśodā and offer it to Rādhā in a solitary place.**

*sāpi bhuktvā sakhī-varga-yutā tad-anupūrvaśaḥ \|*  
*sakhībhir maṇḍitā tiṣṭhed abhisartuṁ mudānvitā \|\|101\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She eats the food in proper order along with the sakhīs. Decorated by the sakhīs, she joyfully waits to meet Kṛṣṇa.**

*prasthāpyate ’nayā kācid ita eva tataḥ sakhī \|*  
*tayābhisāritā sā ’tha yamunāyāḥ samīpataḥ \|\|102\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā sends some sakhī to make an appointment and she arrives at the Yamunā at the appointed place.**

*kalpa-vṛksa-nikuñje ’smin divya-ratna-maye gṛhe \|*  
*sita-kṛṣṇa-niśāyogya-veśa yāti sakhī-yutā \|\|103\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With a group of sakhīs, dressed in clothing suitable for moonlight nights or dark nights, she goes to a finely jeweled house in desire tree bower.**

*kṛṣṇo ’pi vividhaṁ tatra dṛṣṭvā kautūhalaṁ tataḥ \|*  
*kavitvāni manojñāni śrutvā ca gītakāny api \|\|104\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meanwhile, Kṛṣṇa watches various amusing performances and hears attractive poetry and songs.**

*dhana-dhānyādibhis tāṁś ca prīṇayitvā vidhānataḥ \|*  
*janair ākārito mātrā yāti śayyā-niketanam \|\|105\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Satisfying the performers with wealth and grains according to rules, called by some people, he goes with his mother to his bedroom.**

*mātari prasthitāyāṁ tu bhojayitvā tato gṛhāt \|*  
*saṅketakaṁ kāntayātra samāgacched alakṣitaḥ \|\|106\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When his mother, having fed him, leaves, unnoticed, going from the room, he meets with Rādhā at the agreed place.**

*iti pradoṣa-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**(end of evening service)**

*atha rātri-sevā—*

**Перевод:**

**Late night service:**

*militvā tāv ubhāv atra krīḍato vana-rājiṣu \|*  
*vihārair vividhair hāsya-lāsya-gīta-puraḥsaraiḥ \|\|107\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Meeting, the two play in the forests at various pastimes, while laughing, dancing and singing.**

*sārdha-yāma-dvayaṁ nītvā rātrer evaṁ vihārataḥ \|*  
*suṣupsū viśataḥ kuñjaṁ pañca-ṣābhir alakṣitau \|\|108\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After spending about five hours at night in playing, desiring sleep, they enter into a bower with five or six sakhīs, unseen.**

*nirvṛnta-kusumaiḥ kḷpte keli-talpe manorame \|*  
*suptāvatiṣṭhatāṁ tatra sevyamānau priyālibhiḥ \|\|109\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Served by the sakhīs, they remain sleeping on an attractive pastime bed made of stemless flowers.**

*iti rātri-sevā*

**Перевод:**

**(end of night service)**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**

*śrotum icchāmi bho deva vraja-rāja-sutasya ca \|*  
*vṛndāvane rasaṁ divyaṁ rādhayaikāntikaṁ saha \|\|100\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Sadāśiva! I desire to hear about the attractive, pure rasa of Kṛṣṇa with Rādhā in Vṛndāvana.**

*śṛṇu nārada vakṣyāmi rādhā-kṛṣṇa-rasaṁ śuciṁ \|*  
*su-gopyaṁ paramodāraṁ na vaktavyaṁ hi kasyacit \|\|111\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Nārada! Please listen. I will explain the mādhurya-rasa of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. It is most exalted, most secret and should not be revealed to others.**

*aikāntika-rasāsvādaṁ kartuṁ vṛndāvane mune \|*  
*vraja-rāja-kumāraṁ ca bahu-kālam abhāvayam \|\|112\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! I have meditated a long time on Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana in order to taste that pure rasa.**

*mayi prasannaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇo mantra-yugmam anuttamam \|*  
*yugalākhyaṁ dadau mahyaṁ svīyojjvala-rasāplutam \|\|113\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Pleased with me, Kṛṣṇa gave me the topmost mantra called the yugala-mantra, full of his mādhurya-rasa.**

*samabravīt tadā kṛṣṇaḥ sva-śiṣyaṁ māṁ svakaṁ rasam \|*  
*bravīmi tvāṁ śṛṇuṣvādya brahmādīnām agocaram \|\|114\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa then explained to me, his disciple, about his rasa. I speak to you today what is beyond the comprehension of Brahmā and others. Please listen.**

*vraja-rāja-suto vṛndā-vane pūrṇatamo vasan \|*  
*sampūrṇa-ṣoḍaśa-kalo vihāraṁ kurute sadā \|\|115\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Nanda, most complete in sixteen parts, living in Vṛndāvana, plays eternally.**

*vāsudevaḥ pūrṇataro mathurāyāṁ vasan puri \|*  
*kalābhiḥ pañca-daśabhir yutaḥ krīḍati sarvadā \|\|116\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The son of Vasudeva, more perfect (pūrṇatara), lives and plays in Mathurā at all times, endowed with fifteen parts.**

*dvārakādhipatir dvāra-vatyāṁ pūrṇas tv asau vasan \|*  
*catur-daśa-kalāyukto viharaty eva sarvadā \|\|117\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord of Dvārakā, living in Dvārakā, being perfect (pūrṇa), plays at all times, endowed with fourteen parts.**

*ekayā kalayā dvābhyāṁ mathurā-dvārakādhipau \|*  
*vṛndāvana-pate rūpau pūrṇau sve sve pade rase \|\|118\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lords of Mathurā and Dvārakā are complete forms in their individual abodes and rasas, but with one and two kalās less than Kṛṣna in Vṛndāvana.**

*mathurā-nātho vṛndāvanādhipāpekṣayā svarūpeṇa līlayā ca ekayā kalayā ūnaḥ \|*  
*mathurā-līlāyāṁ mathurāyāṁ ca sampūrṇa-ṣoḍaśa-kalaḥ\|*  
*tathā dvārakā-nātho vṛndāvanādhipāpekṣayā svarūpeṇa līlayā ca dvābhyāṁ kalābhyām ūnaḥ \|*  
*dvārakāyāṁ dvārakā-līlāyāṁ ca pūrṇa ṣoḍaśa-kalaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord of Mathurā is one portion less in pastimes and svarūpa compared to the Lord of Vṛndāvana.**  
**However, the Lord is complete with sixteen kalās within Mathurā for the Mathurā pastimes.**  
**Kṛṣṇa of Vṛndāvana is full with sixteen parts, including Mathurā and Mathurā pastimes. The Lord of Dvārakā is two portions less in svarūpa and pastimes compared to the Lord of Vṛndāvana. However, the Lord is complete with sixteen kalās within Dvārakā for the Dvārakā pastimes.**

*śrīr bhū-nīlā yogamāyā cintyācintyā tathaiva ca \|*  
*mohinī kauśalīty aṣṭau bahiraṅgāś ca śaktayaḥ \|\|119\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are eight external śaktis: Śrī, Bhū, Nīlā28, Yogamāyā, Cintyā, Acintyā, Mohinī and Kauśalī.**

*līlā prema-svarūpā ca sthāpany ākarṣaṇī tathā \|*  
*saṁyoginī viyoginy āhlādinīty antaraṅgikā \|\|120\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are eight internal śaktis: Līlā, Prema, Svarūpa, Sthāpanī, Ākarṣaṇī, Saṁyoginī, Viyoginī and Hlādinī.**

*vraje śrī-kṛṣṇa-candrasya santi ṣoḍaśa-śaktayaḥ \|*  
*poṣikā madhurasyaiva tasyaitā vai sanātanāḥ \|\|121\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All sixteen śaktis of Kṛṣṇa are present in Vraja. They are eternal and nourish the sweetness (mādhurya).**

*hlādinī ya mahā-śaktiḥ sarva-śakti-varīyasī \|*  
*tat-sāra-bhāva-rūpā śrī-rādhikā parikīrtitā \|\|122\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hlādinī is the greatest śakti, supreme among all śaktis. The form of mahābhāva, the essence of that śakti, is celebrated as Rādhikā.**

*tayā śrī-kṛṣṇa-candrasya kriḍāyāḥ samaye mune \|*  
*tad-āviṣṭaṁ vāsudevaṁ saha-kṣīrābdhi-nāyakam \|\|123\|\|*  
*antarīkṣya-gataṁ kuryāc chaktir ākarṣaṇī hareḥ \|*  
*krīḍānte sthāpayet taṁ tu sthāpanī kṛṣṇa-dehataḥ \|\|124\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sage! At the time of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes with Rādhā, the Ākarṣaṇīśakti of Kṛṣṇa pulls Kṣirodakaśāyī Viṣṇu and Vāsudeva29 who are merged in his body30 from Kṛṣṇa’s body into the sky. After the pastimes, the Sthāpanī-śakti places them back in Kṛṣṇa’s body.**

*sampūrṇa-ṣoḍaśa-kalaḥ kevalo nanda-nandanaḥ \|*  
*vikrīḍan rādhayā sārdhaṁ labhate paramaṁ sukham \|\|125\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Only Kṛṣṇa, the son of Nanda, is complete in sixteen portions (śaktis). Sporting with Rādhā, he attains the highest happiness.**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**

*gate madhu-purīṁ kṛṣṇe vipralambha-rasaḥ katham \|*  
*vāsudeve rādhikāyāḥ saṁśayaṁ chindhi me prabho \|\|126\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛṣṇa goes to Mathurā, how can Rādhā feel the rasa of separation for Vāsudeva (the form of Kṛṣṇa in Mathurā)? Please relieve me of this doubt, O master!**

*śrī-sadāśiva uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Sadāśiva said:**

*śaktiḥ saṁyoginī kāmā vāmā śaktir viyoginī \|*  
*hlādinī kīrtidā-putrī caivaṁ rādhā-trayaṁ vraje \|\|127\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vraja, Rādhā exists in three forms: Saṁyoginī or kāmā(uniting), vāmā or Viyoginī (separating) and Hlādinī or daughter of Kīrtidā (bliss).**

*mama prāṇeśvaraḥ kṛṣṇas tyaktvā vṛndāvanaṁ kvacit \|*  
*kadācin naiva yātīti jānīte kīrtidā-sutā \|\|128\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā, the daughter of Kīrtidā, thinks, “The lord of my life Kṛṣṇa never leaves Vṛndāvana to go elsewhere.”**

*kāmā-vāme na jānīta iti ca brahma-nandana \|*  
*rāsārambha ivāntardhiṁ gatavān nanda-nandanaḥ \|\|129\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O son of Brahmā! Kāmā and Vāmā (her other forms) do not know this. Thus Kṛṣṇa leaves, just as he disappeared at the beginning of the rāsa dance.**

*mathurāṁ mathurā-nātho vāsudevo jagāma ha \|*  
*antar-hite nanda-sute śrīmad-vṛndāvane mune \|\|130\|\|*  
*pravāsākhyaṁ rasaṁ lebhe rādhā vai kīrtidā-sutā \|*  
*tato vadanti munayaḥ pravāsaṁ saṅga-vicyutim \|\|131\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord of Mathurā, Vāsudeva, went to Mathurā. O sage! When Kṛṣṇa becomes invisible in Vṛndāvana, Rādhā, the daughter of Kīrtidā, feels the rasa of prolonged separation. The sages called this type of separation, pravāsa.**

*mama jīvana-netā ca tyaktvā māṁ mathurāṁ gataḥ \|*  
*iti vihvalitā vāmā rādhā yā virahād abhūt \|\|132\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Overwhelmed, Vāmā Rādhā experiences separation, thinking, “The lord of my life has given me up and gone to Mathurā.”**

*yamunāyāṁ nimagnā sā prakāśaṁ gokulasya ca \|*  
*golokaṁ prāpya tatrābhūt saṁyoga-rasa-peśalā \|\|133\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She submerges herself in the Yamunā and attains Goloka, a manifestation of Gokula, and there enjoys the beauty of the rasaof union.**

*kāmā rādhā ca mathurā-viraheṇa nipīḍitā \|*  
*kurukṣetraṁ gatā tīrtha-yātrā-parama-lālasā \|\|134\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kāmā Rādhā, extremely pained by separation from Kṛṣṇa leaving Mathurā (when he went to Dvārakā) goes to Kurukṣetra, extremely desirous of going on pilgrimage (to meet him).**

*nanda-nandana-bhāva-jña uddhavo vrajam āgataḥ \|*  
*sāntvayiṣyan kīrtidāyāḥ sutāṁ māsa-dvaye gate \|\|135\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Understanding the mood of Kṛṣṇa, Uddhava went to Vraja and for two months pacified Rādhā, the daughter of Kīrtidā.**

*rādhām āsvādayāmāsa śrīmad-bhāgavatārthakaṁ \|*  
*kathāyāṁ bhāgavatyāṁ tu jātāyāṁ muni-puṅgava \|\|136\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O best of sages! Explaining the meaning of Bhāgavatam to her, he pacified Rādhā.**

*vrajendra-nandanaḥ śrīmāṁs tadā pratyakṣatāṁ gataḥ \|\| iti \|\|137\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, son of Nanda, then revealed himself to Rādhā when the topics of Bhāgavatam were spoken.**

*ata eva pādmottara-khaṇḍoktaṁ dvārakādhipater vṛndāvanaṁ prati*  
*gamanaṁ kṣīrābdhiśāyy āviṣṭatvāt kṣīrābdhiśāyino droṇādīnāṁ labdha*  
*vara-tvāt, teṣāṁ punaḥ sva-sthāna-prāpaṇārtham evety avagantavyam*  
*\| śrīmad-bhāgavata-vākyānām evaṁ vicāro ’vagantavyaḥ \| padmottara*  
*khaṇḍe tu “kālindi-puline ramye” ity atra śrī-dvārakā-nāthasya śrī*  
*nanda-nandana-madhura-līlā-sandarśane sotkaṇṭhatvād vyoma-yānair*  
*etya śrī-vṛndāvane māsa-dvayam uvāsety abhiprāyo jñeyaḥ \| tad yathā*  
*śrī-lalita-mādhave—“aparikalita-pūrvaḥ” ityādi \|*

**Перевод:**

**It is understood from Padma Purāṇa that the Lord of Dvārakā returned to Vṛndāvana in order to lead Droṇa (Vasu) and others to their respective abodes since they had received boon from Kṣīrodakaśāyī, and since the Lord was filled with the mood of Kṣīrodakaśāyī (who had been merged in Kṛṣṇa’s body). This return of Kṛṣṇa is also understood to be the conclusion of ŚrīmadBhāgavatam. Padma Purāṇa says:**

*kālindyāḥ puline ramye puṇya-vṛkṣa-samācite*  
*gopa-nārībhir aniśaṁ krīḍayāmāsa keśavaḥ*  
*ramya-keli-sukhenaiva gopa-veśa-dharaḥ prabhuḥ*  
*bahu-prema-rasenātra māsa-dvayam uvāsa ha*

**Перевод:**

**Keśava sported continuously with the Vraja-gopīs on the beautiful bank of the Kālindī, which was embellished with auspicious trees. Dressed as a cowherd boy, Kṛṣṇa stayed there for two months immersed in deep love enjoying various pastimes of blissful reciprocation.**  
**From this it is understood that the Lord of Dvārakā, eager to see the pastimes of the son of Nanda in Vṛndāvana, coming on an air vehicle, stayed two months in Vṛndāvana.**  
**While in Dvārakā, Kṛṣṇa says in Lalita-mādhava (**[**8.34**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/lm-rg/8.34)**):**

*aparikalita-pūrvaḥ kaś camatkāra-kārī*  
*sphurati mama garīyān eṣa mādhurya-pūraḥ \|*  
*ayam aham api hanta prekṣya yaṁ lubdha-cetāḥ*  
*sarabhasam upabhoktuṁ kāmaye rādhikeva \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have never before seen myself with such astonishing sweetness. I become greedy and impetuously desire to enjoy it like Rādhā.**

*iti te sarvam ākhyātaṁ naityikaṁ caritaṁ hareḥ \|*  
*pāpino ’pi vimucyante smaraṇād yasya nārada \|\|138\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have explained to you all the daily pastimes of Kṛṣṇa. Remembering these pastimes even the sinful become liberated, O Nārada!**

*iti śrī-śrī-rādhā-kṛṣṇayor aṣṭa-kālīya-sevā-smaraṇa-paddhatiḥ samāptā*

**Перевод:**

**Thus ends the sequential method of remembering the services to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa during eight parts of the day.**

*aṣṭa-kālokta-śuśrūṣānantaraṁ sādhakaḥ kramāt \|*  
*dvātriṁśad-akṣara-mukhyān japen mantrān atandritaḥ \|\|139\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After practicing diligently the meditation on the pastimes during eight parts of the day, the sādhaka should attentively chant the thirty-two syllable mantra (Hare Kṛṣṇa).**

*mahā-mantraṁ japed ādau daśārṇaṁ tad-anantaram \|*  
*tataḥ śrī-rādhikā-mantraṁ gāyatrīṁ kāmakīṁ tathā \|\|140\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**First, the sādhaka should chant the mahā-mantra and then tensyllable mantra. After that he should chant the Rādhā mantra and the Kāma-gāyatrī.**

*tato yugala-mantraṁ ca japed rāsa-sthalī-pradam \|*  
*tato ’ṣṭānāṁ sakhīnāṁ ca japen mantrān yathā-kramam \|*  
*tataḥ ṣaṇ-mañjarīṇāṁ ca sva-sva-mantrān kramāj japet \|\|141\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Then he should chant the yugala-mantra, which bestows the place of the rāsa dance, and then the mantras of the eight sakhīsin order and then the mantras of the six mañjarīs.**

*gopī-bhāvāṅgī-karaṇā-lakṣaṇaṁ yathā—*

**Перевод:**

**The characteristics of accepting the mood of the gopīs:**

*gopī-bhāvena ye bhaktā mām eva paryupāsate \|*  
*teṣu tāsv iva tuṣṭo ’smi satyaṁ satyaṁ dhanañjaya \|\|142\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Dhanañjaya! Truly, truly I am pleased with those devotees who worship me in the mood of the gopīs. (Ādi-purāṇa)**

*veśa-bhūṣā-vayo-rūpair gopikā-bhāvam āgatāḥ \|*  
*bhāvukīyāś ca tad-bhāvaṁ yānti pāda-rajo ’rcanāt \|\|143\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Having taken to the mood of the gopīs with their dress, ornaments, age and form, those persons having a taste for this attain that bhāva, because of worshipping the dust from their feet.**

*yathā ekāmbara-purāṇe—*

**Перевод:**

**In Ekāmbara Purāṇa it is said:**

*aho bhajana-māhātmyaṁ vṛndāvana-pater hareḥ \|*  
*pumān yoṣid bhaved yatra yoṣid-ātma-samānikā \|\|144\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**What power there is in worshipping Kṛṣṇa, Lord of Vṛndāvana, in which a man becomes a woman like the vraja-gopīs!**

*pādme ca—*

**Перевод:**

**In the Padma Purāṇa (Uttara-khaṇḍa) it is said:**

*purā maharṣayaḥ sarve daṇḍakāraṇya-vāsinaḥ \|*  
*rāmaṁ dṛṣṭvā hariṁ tatra bhoktum aicchan su-vigraham \|\|145\|\|*  
*te sarve strītvam āpannāḥ samudbhūtāś ca gokule \|*  
*hariṁ samprāpya kāmena tato muktā bhavārṇavāt \|\| iti \|\|146\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Previously all the great sages living in Daṇḍakāraṇya forest, after seeing Rāma, desired to enjoy that beautiful form there. They all became women and were born in Gokula, and attaining the Lord with mādhurya sentiments, became liberated from the ocean of saṁsāra.**

*bṛhad-vāmana-siddhāś ca śrutayo ’pi yathā purā \|*  
*gopī-bhāvena saṁsevya samudbhūtā hi gokule \|\|147\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Similarly the siddhas (the sages) mentioned in Bṛhad-vāmana Purāṇa and the śrutis long ago serving the Lord in the mood of gopīs, were born in Gokula.34**

*yad-uktaṁ śrī-rūpa-gosvāmi-caraṇaiḥ—*

**Перевод:**

**Rūpa Gosvāmī says (paraphrasing):**

*hariṁ su-rāga-mārgeṇa sevate yo narottamaḥ \|*  
*kevalenaiva sa tadā gopikātvam iyād vraje \|\|148\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The best of men who serves Kṛṣṇa according to the path of pure rāga attains a body of a gopī in Vraja.**

*bhakti-tattva-kaumudyām—*

**Перевод:**

**In Bhakti-tattva-kaumudī it is said:**

*ekasmin vāsanā-dehe yadi cānyasya bhāvanā \|*  
*tarhi tat sāmyam eva syāt yathā vai bharate nṛpe \|\|149\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one thinks of a different body (from his present one) in his mind, he will attain such a body, as in the case of King Bharata.**

*aṣṭa-kāla-sevā-phalam yathā sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām—*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā says concerning the results of serving eight parts of the day:**

*śrī-nārada uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said:**

*dhanyo ’smy anugṛhīto ’smi tvayā devi na saṁśayaḥ \|*  
*harer me naityikī līlā yato me ’dya prakāśitā \|\|150\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Vṛndā devī! I am fortunate. I have attained your mercy without doubt, because today I have understood the daily pastimes of Kṛṣṇa.**

*śrī-sanat-kumāra uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra said:**

*ity uktvā tāṁ parikramya tayā cāpi prapūjitaḥ \|*  
*antardhānaṁ gato rājan nārado muni-sattamaḥ \|\|151\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O King! Thus speaking, circumambulating Vṛndā-devī, and being worshipped by her, Nārada, the best of sages, disappeared.**

*mayāpy etad ānupūrvyaṁ sarvaṁ tat parikīrtitam \|*  
*japan nityaṁ prayatnena mantra-yugmam anuttamam \|\|152\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have glorified this incomparable yugala-mantra completely in order, which should be chanted daily with care.**

*kṛṣṇa-vaktrād idaṁ labdhaṁ purā rudreṇa yatnataḥ \|*  
*tenoktaṁ nāradāyātha nāradena mayoditam \|\|153\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Long ago Śiva obtained this mantra from Kṛṣṇa’s mouth. Śiva spoke it to Nārada. Nārada has spoken it to me.**

*saṁsārāgni-vināśāya mayāpy etat tavoditam \|*  
*tvayā caitad gopanīyaṁ rahasyaṁ paramādbhutam \|\|154\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have spoken it to you for destroying the fire of saṁsāra. You should conceal this most astonishing secret.**

*śrī-ambarīṣa uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Ambarīṣa said:**

*kṛta-kṛtyo ’bhavaṁ sākṣāt tvat-prasādād ahaṁ guro \|*  
*rahasyātirahasyaṁ yat tvayā mahyaṁ prakāśitam \|\|155\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O guru! I have become successful since, because of your direct mercy, you have revealed to me the greatest secret.**

*śrī-sanat-kumāra uvāca—*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra said:**

*dharmān etān upādiṣṭo japan mantram ahar niśam \|*  
*acirād eva tad-dāsyam avāpsyasi na saṁśayaḥ \|\|156\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Chanting this yugala-mantra day and night, being instructed about this process of serving in eight parts of the day, you will without doubt obtain service to the Lord without delay as a mañjarī.**

*‘etān dharmān’—aṣṭa-kāla-sevā-rūpān; ‘mantram’—yugala-mantram;*  
*‘tad-dāsyam’—tayoḥ śrī-rādhā-kṛṣṇayor dāsyaṁ dāsī-bhāvam iti \|*

**Перевод:**

**Etān dharmā means service in eight parts of the day. Mantra means the yugala-mantra. Tad-dāsyam means to become a female servant of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.**

*mayāpi gamyate rājan guror āyatanaṁ mama \|*  
*vṛndāvane yatra nityaṁ gurur me ’sti sadāśivaḥ \|\|157\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O King! I will go to the abode of my guru in Vṛndāvana, where my guru Sadāśiva stays at all times.**

*dvātriṁśad-akṣarādīnāṁ mantrāṇāṁ krameṇa phalaṁ yathā pādme—*

**Перевод:**

**The results of the thirty-two syllable and other mantras are now described in order. Padma Purāṇa says:**

*dvātriṁśad-akṣaraṁ mantraṁ nāma-ṣoḍaśakānvitam \|*  
*prajapan vaiṣṇavo nityaṁ rādhā-kṛṣṇa-sthalaṁ labhet \|\|158\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The devotee who constantly chants the thirty-two-syllable mantra with sixteen names will obtain the abode of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.**

*gautamīya-tantre ca—*

**Перевод:**

**Gautamīya-tantra says:**

*ahar-niśaṁ japen mantraṁ mantrī niyata-mānasaḥ \|*  
*sa paśyati na sandeho gopa-rūpiṇam īśvaram \|\|159\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Chanting the mantra day and night, the chanter, with controlled mind, without doubt will see the Lord in the form of cowherd.**

*gaurī-tantre ca—*

**Перевод:**

**Gaurī-tantra says:**

*śrīmad-aṣṭākṣaraṁ mantraṁ rādhāyāḥ prema-siddhi-dam \|*  
*prajapet sādhako yas tu sa rādhāntikam āpnuyāt \|\|160\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka who chants the eight-syllable mantra of Rādhā, which gives prema, will attain the association of Rādhā.**

*sanat-kumāra-saṁhitāyām—*

**Перевод:**

**Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā says:**

*japed yaḥ kāma-gāyatrīṁ kāma-bīja-samanvitām \|*  
*tasya siddhir bhavet prema rādhā-kṛṣṇa-sthalaṁ vrajet \|\|161\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who chants the Kāma-gāyatrī with the kāma-bīja will attain prema and go to the abode of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.**

*etāṁ pañca-padīṁ japtvā śraddhayā ’śraddhayāsakṛt \|*  
*vṛndāvane tayor dāsyaṁ gacchaty eva na saṁśayaḥ \|\|162\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Chanting that five-part mantra with faith, or even without faith constantly, one will attain service to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana without doubt.**

*kiśorī-tantre ca—*

**Перевод:**

**Kiśorī-tantra says:**

*etān sakhīnām aṣṭānāṁ mantrān yaḥ sādhako japet \|*  
*śrī-rādhā-kṛṣṇayoḥ kṣipraṁ vihāra-sthalam āpnuyāt \|\|163\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka who chants the mantras of the eight sakhīs will quickly attain the abode where Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa perform their pastimes.**

*tatraiva—*

**Перевод:**

**Kiśorī-tantra says:**

*mantrān etān mañjarīṇām aṣṭānāṁ yo japet sadā \|*  
*prema-siddhir bhavet tasya śrī-vṛndāvanam āpnuyāt \|\|164\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who always chants the mantras of the eight mañjarīswill attain prema and Vṛndāvana.**

*smaraṇānantaraṁ siddha-dehasyaiva ca sādhakaḥ \|*  
*aṣṭa-kāloditāṁ līlāṁ saṁsmaret sādhakāṅgakaḥ \|\|165\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sādhaka, though still possessing a body of a sādhaka, after remembering his siddha-deha, should remember the pastimes in the eight parts of the day.**

*tad yathā—*  
*kālau niśānta-pūrvāhṇāv aparāhṇa-pradoṣakau \|*  
*vijñeyau tris-tri-ghaṭikau prātaḥ sāyaṁ dvayaṁ dvayam \|\|166\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**End-of-night, forenoon, afternoon and night each last nine ghaṭikas each (i.e. 3 hours 36 minutes each). Morning and twilight last two ghaṭikas each (i.e. 48 minutes each).**

*siddha-siddha-praghaṭikau jñeye madhyāhna-rātrike \|\|167\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Noon and late night last ten ghaṭikas each (i.e. 4 hours each).**

*eteṣu samayeṣv evaṁ yā yā līlā puroditā \|*  
*tāṁ tām eva yathā-kālaṁ saṁsmaret sādhako janaḥ \|\|168\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Whatever pastimes were described in all these timings, the sādhaka should meditate on them at those appropriate timings.**

*iti śrī-śrīla-gopāla-guru-gosvāmi-kṛtā smaraṇa-paddhatiḥ samāptā*
