# Rāga-vartma-candrikā \[BS\]

## Chapter One

1\. I offer respects to the cakora devotees who taste the nectar of Śrī-rūpa’s words.

2\. By a drop of nectar from these devotees I will speak this booklet about the path of rāga. In a drop from the sweet ocean of bhakti which was previously explained, rāgānuga bhakti is briefly explained.

3\. When scripture is the impetus for bhakti it becomes vaidhī bhakti. When greed becomes the impetus for bhakti, it becomes rāgānugā bhakti.

4\. Here, the impetus for bhakti means the resolute desire to perform bhakti. Those who desire to perform because of scripture become qualified for vaidhī and those who desire to perform because of greed become qualified for rāgānuga bhakti.

5\. Śrī-rūpa Gosvāmī describes greed (lobhā):

*tat-tad-bhāvādi-mādhurye śrute dhīr yad apekṣate*  
*nātra śāstraṁ na yuktiṁ ca tal-lobhotpatti-lakṣaṇaṁ*

**The appearance of that greed is indicated when the intelligence does not depend on rules of scripture and logic, after realizing to some degree the sweetness of their (inhabitants of Vraja) love through the process of hearing from the scriptures. BRS.1.2.292**

When the person hears about the sweetness of conjugal and other bhāvas of the associates of Kṛṣṇa in his Vraja pastimes (*tat-tad-bhāvādi-mādhurye śrute)*, he thinks “I should also attain such a bhāva.” When such lobhā arises, he will not depend on scripture or logic (*apekṣate nātra śāstraṁ na yuktiṁ),* for when there is dependence on scripture and logic, lobhā or greed does not manifest strongly. No one can manifest greed by consulting scripture, and no amount of considering one’s qualification or lack of qualification will produce attainment of the object of greed. Rather greed arises spontaneously on hearing about the object of greed.

6\. There are two causes of greed. The cause of greed is the mercy of the Lord and the mercy of the devotee who has attraction for Kṛṣṇa. A devotee’s mercy as a cause may be either ancient or recent. “Ancient” means that it arises from mercy of a rāgānuga devotee in a previous life. “Recent” means that the greed arises from mercy of a rāgānuga devotee in this life. If the cause is from previous life, in this life, after manifesting greed, the person takes shelter of a guru having similar greed. If the cause is from this life, after taking shelter of the lotus feet of a guru, the greed develops.

*kṛṣṇa-tad-bhakta-kāruṇya-mātra-lābhaika-hetukā*  
*puṣṭi-mārgatayā kaiścid iyaṁ rāgānugocyate*

**The mercy of Kṛṣṇa and his devotees is the only cause of attaining rāgānuga-bhakti. Some call this type of devotion** ***puṣṭi-mārga*****. BRS 1.2.309**

7\. When a greedy devotee desires to know about the method to attain his desired bhāva, he must depend on scripture and logic, since the method is to be known by scriptural rules and by logic based on scripture. It cannot be otherwise. If one desires milk, on inquiring about the method to attain milk, one must depend on the instructions and statements of persons who know about milk. Just from the instruction “Go and buy a cow” one does not spontaneously learn to feed and milk the cow. (A practical method must be taught):

*yathāgnim edhasy amṛtaṁ ca goṣu*  
*bhuvy annam ambūdyamane ca vṛttim*  
*yogair manuṣyā adhiyanti hi tvāṁ*  
*guṇeṣu buddhyā kavayo vadanti*

**As one can derive fire from wood, milk from the cow, food grains and water from the land, and livelihood from endeavor, so, by the practice of bhakti-yoga, humans can realize you in the guṇas and by intelligence proclaim this. SB 8.6.12**

8\. This greed of the devotees following the path of rāga starts from taking shelter of the lotus feet of guru and extends to the time of direct attainment of one’s desired object.

*yathā yathātmā parimṛjyate ’sau*  
*mat-puṇya-gāthā-śravaṇābhidhānaiḥ*  
*tathā tathā paśyati vastu sūkṣmaṁ*  
*cakṣur yathaivāñjana-samprayuktam*

**To the degree that theātmā becomes purified by hearing and chanting my glories, a person is able to perceive my real form and qualities and experience their sweetness, just as the eye when smeared with special ointment is able to see finer objects. SB 11.14.26**

In the above verse the Lord states that one becomes gradually qualified according to the degree of purification of the antaḥkaraṇa by bhakti.

9\. When the greed arises (through mercy of Kṛṣṇa or devotees), the inclination will increase for some practitioners by the words of guru, with scriptural teachings concerning methods of attaining particular bhāvas.

*naivopayanty apacitiṁ kavayas taveśa*  
*brahmāyuṣāpi kṛtam ṛddha-mudaḥ smarantaḥ*  
*yo ’ntar bahis tanu-bhṛtām aśubhaṁ vidhunvann*  
*ācārya-caittya-vapuṣā sva-gatiṁ vyanakti*

**O my Lord! Intelligent persons cannot fully express their indebtedness to you, even if they were endowed with the lifetime of Brahmā, because on remembering what you have done form them, their joy increases even more: appearing externally as guru and internally as Paramātmā, you reveal the goal of prema to the devotees. SB 11.29.6**

For others, instructions from a great devotee with anurāga and knowledge will cause the increase. For others who are well grounded in knowledge, the inclination will joyfully increase by spontaneous revelations in functions of their hearts cleansed with bhakti, just as methods to fulfill desires appear spontaneously to persons filled with lust.

10\. The scripture concerning rāgānuga bhakti is the great Puruṣa Śrīmad-bhāgavatam, the essence of all the Upaniṣads, embodied in statements like the following:

*na karhicin mat-parāḥ śānta-rūpe*  
*naṅkṣyanti no me 'nimiṣo leḍhi hetiḥ*  
*yeṣām ahaṁ priya ātmā sutaś ca*  
*sakhā guruḥ suhṛdo daivam iṣṭam*

**The devotees in the spiritual world are never deprived of any enjoyment. My wheel of time does not afflict those devotees, for whom I am a lover, the ātmā, son, friend, elder, companion or worshipable deity. SB 3.25.38**

Another scripture is the famous Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, which describes this type of bhakti. Three verses explain rāgānuga bhakti.

*kṛṣṇaṁ smaran janaṁ cāsya preṣṭhaṁ nija-samīhitam*  
*tat-tat-kathā-rataś cāsau kuryād vāsaṁ vraje sadā*

**Remembering the Vṛndāvana form of Kṛṣṇa and His dear associates who have inclinations for service similar to one’s own, absorbing oneself in hearing topics related to them, one should always live in Vraja.**

*sevā sādhaka-rūpeṇa siddha-rūpeṇa cātra hi*  
*tad-bhāva-lipsunā kāryā vraja-lokānusārataḥ*

**Following after the inhabitants of Vraja, one should perform service in one’s physical body and in one’s siddha body, with a desire for a particular bhāva.**

*śravaṇotkīrtanādīni vaidha-bhakty-uditāni tu*  
*yāny aṅgāni ca tāny atra vijñeyāni manīṣibhiḥ*

**The discriminating practitioners should accept the aṅgas which were mentioned in vaidhi-bhakti such as hearing and chanting as the aṅgas of rāgānuga-bhakti. BRS 1.2.294-296**

These three verses are explained in relation to kāmānuga bhakti.

11\. The words *kṛṣṇaṁ smaraṇ* are placed first to indicate that remembrance is the chief activity of rāgānuga practice, since rāga is a quality of the mental condition. One should remember Kṛṣṇa, the lord of Vṛndāvana, who performs pastimes appropriate to one’s particular bhāva (*preṣṭham*). One should also remember his (*asya*) associate (*janam*) whom one cherishes as one’s ideal (*nija-samīhitam*), such as Rādhā, Lalitā or Śrī-rūpa-mañjarī. If one cannot live in Vraja (*vraje vāsam*) one can do so in the mind. Living in Vraja with one’s body as a sādhaka is implied in the next verse with the words *sādhaka-rupeṇa*. One should perform service in one’s physical body (*sādhaka-rūpena*) and with a body suitable to one’s desired and direct service to the Lord, produced through meditation (*siddha-rūpena*). One should perform service with a desire to obtain a bhāva which is directed to Kṛṣṇa in the desired form and which takes shelter of one’s cherished associate of Kṛṣṇa (*tad-bhāva-lipsunā*). The service (*sevā*) should be done by suitable articles established in the mind or directly obtained. This service follows after the actions of the people of Vraja (*vraja-lokānusārataḥ*). In his physical body (*sādhaka-rūpena*) he follows Śrī-rūpa-gosvāmī and others. In his siddha body he follows persons like Śrī-rūpa-mañjarī. Also in his physical body he should if possible follow persons who have attained a relationship with Kṛṣṇa such as Candrakantī, the sages of Daṇḍakāraṇya and the personified Vedas, famous in Bṛhad-vāmana Purāṇa. He should follow them by seeing their particular conduct.

Having described how the aspirant should remember the Lord and live in Vraja in two verses, the normal processes of bhakti are described. The word *ādinā* indicates other processes such as surrendering to the lotus feet of guru. Without these processes, following after the inhabitants of Vraja and service there in rāgānuga cannot be accomplished. Those actions of vaidhī bhakti should be performed by the wise who have considered the matter carefully (*manīṣibhiḥ*), selecting aṅgas of bhakti which are suitable to one’s personal bhāva and not contrary.

12\. In the aṅga of arcana, worship of Rukmiṇī, meditation on Dvārakā, performance of mudras and nyāsas and self worship (ahaṁgrahopāsanā), though prescribed in āgama scriptures, should not be performed, for it is said that there is no fault in omitting to some degree certain aṅgas on the path of bhakti.

*yān āsthāya naro rājan na pramādyeta karhicit*  
*dhāvan nimīlya vā netre na skhalen na pated iha*

**O King! One who accepts this process of devotional service will never be affected by pride. Even while running with eyes closed, he will never trip or fall. SB 11.2.35**

*na hy aṅgopakrame dhvaṁso mad-dharmasyoddhavāṇv api*  
*mayā vyavasitaḥ samyaṅ nirguṇatvād anāśiṣaḥ*

**O Uddhava! Because I have personally established it, the process of niṣkāma-bhakti is beyond the guṇas. Thus even by starting and not completing the process, there is no destruction of results. SB 11.29.30**

There is fault however if there is irregularity in the aṅgī (the very nature of bhakti) since it is said *yān śravaṇotkīrtanādīn bhagavad-dharmān āśritya*: one should take shelter of dharma directed to Bhagavān, consisting of hearing and chanting etc.

*śruti-smṛti-purāṇādi-pañcarātra-vidhiṁ vinā*  
*aikāntikī harer bhaktir utpātāyaiva kalpate*

**Even if a person seems to have attained steadiness in practicing pure bhakti to the Lord, that bhakti is a misfortune if it rejects the rules of śruti, smṛti, Purāṇa and Pañcarātra due to lack of faith in them. Brahma-yāmala**

If one thinks that one should not give up all scriptural injunctions which are contrary to one’s bhāva, though one has developed greed, one will attain a position as a servant of the queens in Dvārakā. It is said:

*riraṁsāṁ suṣṭhu kurvan yo vidhi-mārgeṇa sevate*  
*kevalenaiva sa tadā mahiṣītvam iyāt pure*

**A person who serves on the path of vaidhī bhakti with a desire for a conjugal relationship with the Lord and a high position, but without desire for the gopīs’ type of love, after some time becomes a queen in Dvārakā. BRS 1.2.303**

The person who desires a conjugal relationship and serves completely (*kevalena*) by vaidhī bhakti, not giving up worship of the queens which are contrary to his bhāva attains the position of a queen in Dvārakā. According to Amara-koṣa the word *kevala* can mean one or all.

One should not say that by pure vidhi-marga one attains Dvārakā and by vaidhī bhakti mixed with rāgānuga one attains Mathurā. Though queens dwell in Dvārakā, what form would one attain in Mathurā? “Perhaps the person becomes an associate of Kubjā.” One would attain a result from vaidhī bhakti mixed with rāgānuga, which would be inferior to the result from pure vaidhī bhakti in Dvārakā (since Kubjā’s love is inferior to that of the queens in Dvārakā). This would be wrong (since vaidhi mixed with rāgānuga is superior to pure vaidhī bhakti). rāmāniruddha-pradyumna-rukmiṇyā sahito vibhuḥ: Kṛṣṇa resides in Mathurā with Balarāma, Aniruddha, Pradyumna and Rukmiṇī. (Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad) According to this śruti statement, Kṛṣṇa also marries Rukmiṇī in Mathurā. However it is not a normal situation to become an associate of Rukmiṇī in Mathurā. How can the worshipper of Radhā and Kṛṣṇa attain association with Kubjā or Rukmiṇī. This is a second fault in the proposition.

Actually, service inspired by greed on the path of vaidhī is called the path of rāga. Service inspired by rules of scripture on the path of vaidhī is called the path of vidhi. Service without reference to rules is the cause of disaster according to the statements of śruti and smṛti.

13\. Which aṅgas of worship, having what essential form, should be performed or avoided in rāgānuga?

Five types of worship are seen in scriptures: those filled with one’s desired bhāva, those related to one’s bhāva, those favorable to one’s bhāva, those not contrary to one’s bhāva, and those contrary to one’s bhāva. Among these, some are both sādhana and sādhya. Some are the direct causes of prema. Some are indirect causes. Some are characteristics of worship. Some are assistants to worship. Some are impediments. Some are neutral. These will be analyzed and discussed.

14\. Aṅgas of bhakti like dāsya and sakhya, filled with one’s desired bhāva, are both sādhana and sādhya (prema). Chanting mantras and meditating, arising from surrender to the lotus feet of guru, are related to one’s bhāva, since they are direct cause of attaining the goal, prema.

It is said *japen nityam ananya-dhīḥ*: one should daily do japa with concentration. Thus such activities are performed every day. *japyaḥ svābhīṣṭa-saṁsargī kṛṣṇa-nāma mahā-manuḥ*: one should chant the mantra containing Kṛṣṇa’s name, which allows one to attain one’s desired goal. (Rādhā-kṛṣṇa-ganoddeśa-dīpikā) This statement shows that mantra japa is performed by those following the associates of Vraja using their siddha-deha. This is thus also related to one’s bhāva, since it is a direct cause. This mantra is the best of all mantras (*mahā-manuḥ*) containing Kṛṣṇa’s name, which allows one to attain one’s desired goal (*abhīṣṭa-saṁsargī*). This can be either the ten syllable mantra or the eighteen syllable mantra. Giving pleasure to my senses (*govinda*), he is the lover of the young gopīs (*gopījana-vallabha*). This is how the mantra is explained in Ganoddeśā-dīpikā.

Hearing and remembering pastimes, qualities and names suitable to one’s bhāva are aṅga’s related to one’s bhāva since they are direct causes of the goal.

*śṛṇvan su-bhadrāṇi rathāṅga-pāṇer*  
*janmāni karmāṇi ca yāni loke*  
*gītāni nāmāni tad-arthakāni*  
*gāyan vilajjo vicared asaṅgaḥ*

**Hearing the most auspicious birth and activities of Kṛṣṇa which are sung by all people and singing his names which describe those activities while having no attachment to anything else, a person should without shame wander in the world. SB 11.2.39**

*śṛṇvanti gāyanti gṛṇanty abhīkṣṇaśaḥ*  
*smaranti nandanti tavehitaṁ janāḥ*  
*ta eva paśyanty acireṇa tāvakaṁ*  
*bhava-pravāhoparamaṁ padāmbujam*

**Persons who constantly hear, sing, speak, remember and sing to others about your activities—they alone, without delay, are able to see your lotus feet which destroy the influence of material existence. SB 1.8.36**

These are daily activities.

It must be said that remembrance, the chief element of rāgānuga, is necessarily dependent on kīrtana since this is the prescribed method for the yuga and all scriptures declare kīrtana to be most excellent for all paths of bhakti.

The Upaniṣads performed austerity to attain prema with rāga.

*samantāt sūkṣma-darśinyo mahopaniṣado'khilāḥ*  
*gopīnāṁ vīkṣya saubhāgyam asamordhvaṁ suvismitāḥ*  
*tapāṁsi śraddhayā kṛtvā premāḍhyā jajñire vraje*  
*vallavya iti paurāṇī tathaupaniṣadī prathā*

**All the major Upaniṣads, which give elevated philosophy, seeing the unequalled good fortune of the gopīs, became very astonished. After performing austerities with faith, filled with prema, they were born in Vraja as cowherd women. This is the statement of the Purāṇas and Upaniṣads. Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 3.47-48**

However in Kali-yuga other types of austerity are condemned. *mad-arthaṁ yad vrataṁ tapaḥ*: austerity means vows underetaken for me. (SB 11.19.23) This means the fasting on Ekādaśī and Janmāṣṭamī are the austerities for the devotee. These are indirect causes, performed on the prescribed days and done regularly, since not observing them is said to cause decrease in bhakti. Such acts are related to bhāva in part, since they induce remembrance, which is a direct cause. Smṛti says *govinda-smaraṇaṁ nṛṇāṁ yad ekādaśy-upoṣaṇam*: fasting on Ekādaśī is a cause of remembering Govinda. (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa) On the other hand it is said *mātṛhā pitṛhā caiva bhratṛhā guruhā tathā*: one who does not observe Ekādaśī is the killer of his mother, father, brother and guru. (Skanda Purāṇa) Thus non observance makes one an offender to the name.

*brahmaghnasya surāpasya steyino guru-talpinaḥ*  
*niṣkṛtir dharma-śāstroktā naikādaśy-anna-bhojinaḥ*

**There is atonement for the killer of a brāhmaṇa, a drinker of liquor, a thief and a person who violates the wife of his guru. But there is no atonement for eating grains on Ekādaśī. Viṣṇu-dharmottara**

Serious sin is incurred. Thus these vows are necessary. The Ekādaśī vow is a quality of the Vaiṣṇava:

*paramāpadam āpanne harṣe vā samupasthite*  
*naikādaśīṁ tyajed yas tu tasya dīkṣāsti vaiṣṇavī*  
*viṣṇv-arpitākhilācāraḥ sa hi vaiṣṇava ucyate*

**The person who does not give up Ekādaśī vows in times of great danger or rejoicing, who has vaiṣṇava dīkṣā, and who offers all his actions to Viṣṇu is called a Vaiṣṇava. Skanda Purāṇa**

Vaiṣṇavas should not eat food which is not offered to the Lord. But eating food which is offered to the Lord is also forbidden on Ekādaśī.

*vaiṣṇavo yadi bhuñjīta ekādaśyāṁ pramādataḥ*  
*viṣṇv-arcanaṁ vṛthā tasya narakaṁ ghoram āpnuyāt*

**If a vaiṣṇava eats on Ekādaśī by mistake, his worship of Viṣṇu becomes useless. He goes to the worst hell. Gautamīya-tantra**

The Kārttika-vrata is an indirect cause with its austerity and a direct cause because of hearing and chanting. Since Rūpa Gosvāmī often speaks of Kārttika-devatā, Kārttika-devī, Ūrjjā-devī and Urjeśvarī, this vow enables one to attain Rādhā.

Hearing Bhāgavatam is a daily activity.

*ambarīṣa śuka-proktaṁ nityaṁ bhāgavataṁ śṛṇu*  
*paṭhasva sva-mukhenaiva yadīcchasi bhava-kṣayam*

**O Ambarīṣa! Hear constantly the Bhāgavatam spoken by Śukadeva. Read it with your own mouth if you desire destruction of material life. Padma Purāṇa**

*yas tūttamaḥ-śloka-guṇānuvādaḥ*  
*saṅgīyate 'bhīkṣṇam amaṅgala-ghnaḥ*  
*tam eva nityaṁ śṛṇuyād abhīkṣṇaṁ*  
*kṛṣṇe 'malāṁ bhaktim abhīpsamānaḥ*

**The person who desires pure bhaktito Kṛṣṇa should hear the narrations of the Lord’s glorious qualities, the constant chanting of which destroys everything inauspicious. SB 12.3.15**

Thus hearing the tenth canto stories of Kṛṣṇa is, as much as suitable, a daily activity, to be done constantly. It is related to one’s bhāva.

Wearing prasāda tulasī, sandalwood, garlands and cloth is an activity related to one’s bhāva. Wealing tulasī neck beds, tilaka, and putting the name of the Lord and his marks on the body are marks of a Vaiṣṇava, and are favorable acts. Serving tulasī, doing parikrama and offering praṇāmas are favorable acts. Respecting brāhmaṇas, cows and āśvattha and dhatrī trees are not contrary to one’s bhāva and are thus assistants. Service to Vaiṣṇavas has all the qualities.

All these actions should be performed. Yaśodā gave more attention to overflowing milk, yogurt and butter than to Kṛṣṇa, who was to be nourished by these items. Leaving Kṛṣṇa who wanted to drink her breast milk, she went to take the milk off the fire. Similarly the devotees knowing rasa who following the path of rāga sometimes pay more attention to all the aṅgas which nourish the main elements than they do for the main elements of hearing and chanting, which are to be nourished by the supporting elements. This is not improper.

Self worship, nyāsas, mudras, meditation on Dvārakā and worship of the queens are impediments and should not be performed. Hearing from other Purāṇas is neutral.

Though bhakti is without change, since its form is eternity, knowledge and bliss, terms like direct cause (implying change) are used since it is hard to conceive. Similarly, the six states of sneha, maṇa, praṇaya, rāga, anurāga and mahābhava are called the manifestations of prema (prema-vilāsa), using the word vilāsa (indicating transformation). And in devotional scriptures describing rasa, words like vibhāva (preceeding cause) are used. The devotees should forgive me for using words like “direct cause.”

## Chapter Two

1.“Kṛṣṇa is not aware of loss, fatigue, household duties, attachments, fearful situations, unsteadiness or killing. He plays at night in the Vṛndāvana forest with the most beautiful women whom he accepts as his friends. Since the son of Nanda is absorbed complely in manifestations of prema with Rādhā and the gopīs, he cannot pay attention to anyone else. Who will accept the service rendered by countless rāgānuga devotees, situated in various places? Who will hear the recitation of verses of praise and surrender?”

To say that his aṁśa Paramātmā will perform this task since the aṁśa and its source are one would be painful for the devotees following rāgānuga. What is the solution? Uddhava explains:

*mantreṣu māṁ vā upahūya yat tvam*  
*akuṇṭhitākhaṇḍa-sadātma-bodhaḥ*  
*pṛccheḥ prabho mugdha ivāpramattas*  
*tan no mano mohayatīva deva*

**O master! O Lord! You who are always full of knowledge, unaffected by time and complete, would call me for consultation and ask me, just as ignorant people would do, attentive to my advice. When you, though full of knowledge, act in this way, it bewilders my mind. SB 3.4.17**

The meaning is this. For consultations (*mantreṣu*) in preparing to go to the Rājasūya sacrifice and killing Jarāsandha, you would call me and ask (*pṛccheḥ*), “O Uddhava! What should I do? Please tell me.” You are always complete, unrestricted by time or other factors, endowed with saṁvit śakti (*ātma-bodhaḥ*) but you appeared to be bewildered like an ordinary person who asks questions (*mugdha iva*). Being without knowledge and without all knowledge, you certainly (*iva*) bewilder (*mohayati*) my mind.

One should not explain the verse to mean “You seem to be bewildered but are not. This seems to bewilder me but does not.” It would be meaningless in the context since the previous verse states *karmāṇy anīhasya bhavo ’bhavasya te*: you are without action but you perform action; you are without birth, but you take birth. (SB 3.4.16) Just as in his Dvārakā pastimes Kṛṣna is omniscient but also is inexperienced in certain matters, in his Vṛndāvana pastimes also he is both omniscient and inexperienced. This is accomplished by his acintya-śakti. Bilvamaṅgala says:

*sarvajñatve ca maugdhye ca*  
*sārvabhaumam idaṁ mahaḥ*  
*nirviśan nayanaṁ hanta*  
*nirvāṇa-padam aśnute*

**Oh! That effulgence is supreme in omniscience and innocence. Entering through my eyes, it spreads throughout my heart, the abode of the highest bliss. Kṛṣna-karṇāmṛta 83**

2\. Display of the Lord’s omniscience indicates his great power, not his sweetness. The Lord shows sweetness when he displays inexperience or foolishness in his pastime as a human, without showing powers. This is the explanation of persons with gross intelligence.

3\. Sweetness is now defined. Whether great powers manifest or do not manifest, when Kṛṣṇa does not transgress human pastimes, it is called sweet. For instance, though he sucked out the life air of Pūtanā, he performed the pastime of a human child by sucking her breast. In breaking the very tough cart, he showed the infant pastime of lying down and kicking up very soft feet of three months. Though he could not be bound by long cords, he became greatly frightened by his mother. Though he was omniscient, and could bewilder Brahmā and Baladeva, he performed the pastime of herding the calves. Though he possesses all powers, not manifesting powers, he steals yogurt and milk and teases the cowherd girls. One should not say that his ignorance while performing a pastime of a human, without showing powers, is called sweetness, since ignorance shown by foolish, material human children at play would also be called sweetness.

4\. His power is a display of his lordship, without dependence on his performing human pastimes. For instance, having shown power to his mother and father he says:

*etad vāṁ darśitaṁ rūpaṁ prāg-janma-smaraṇāya me*  
*nānyathā mad-bhavaṁ jñāna martya-liṅgena jāyate*

**I have shown you this form so that you will remember my previous appearances before you. You would not understand my birth if I appeared in my human-like form. SB 10.3.44**

To Arjuna he said, “See my powers of yoga.” He then showed his powers. In Vraja, after showing his great sweetness, he showed Brahmā thousands of four armed forms.

5\. Knowledge of the Lord’s power manifests a devotee’s steadiness. Vasudeva says:

*yuvāṁ na naḥ sutau sākṣāt pradhāna-puruṣeśvarau*  
*bhū-bhāra-kṣatra-kṣapaṇa avatīrṇau tathāttha ha*

**You are not our sons but the very Lords of both material nature and its creator \[Mahā-Viṣṇu\]. As you yourself have told us, you have descended to rid the earth of the rulers who are a heavy burden upon her. SB 10.85.18**

Arjuna says:

*sakheti matvā prasabhaṁ yad uktaṁ*  
*he kṛṣṇa he yādava he sakheti*  
*ajānatā mahimānaṁ tavedaṁ*  
*mayā pramādāt praṇayena vāpi*

**I beg pardon from you, for not knowing Your greatness. Through want of consideration or through affection, I looked upon You, the immeasurable, as a friend. I beg pardon for whatever I have said rudely, such as addressing You as “O Kṛṣṇa! O Yādava! O friend!” BG 11.41**

Though one may understand that Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord, when that realization produces increased steadiness in one’s particular bhava--and the realization does not produce a trace of respect which generates disturbance in the heart, it is called knowledge of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness.

*vandinas tam upadeva-gaṇā ye*  
*vādya-gīta-balibhiḥ parivavruḥ*

**The various minor devatās, standing on all sides like panegyrists, offer their music, singing, and gifts of tribute. SB 10.35.21**

*vatsalo vraja-gavāṁ yad aga-dhro*  
*vandyamāna-caraṇaḥ pathi vṛddhaiḥ*

**Out of great affection for the cows of Vraja, Kṛṣṇa became the lifter of Govardhana Hill. Exalted** ***devatās*** **standing along the path worship his lotus feet. SB 10.35.22**

Śrīdama, Subala and other boys remain steady in their bhāva though they see all the devatās and sages like Nārada praising Kṛṣṇa with songs and music, and offering respect at his feet while offering gifts. Hearing about this, the gopīs remain steady in their mādhurya-bhāva. Yaśodā shows not a trace of decrease in her vātsalya, being consoled by the words of Nanda. Rather her affection for her son became stronger, for joyfully she thought, “It is fortunate that the Lord is my son.” Similarly in the material world, a mother’s love for her son remains strong, though he is master of the earth. The cowherd boys think, “We are fortunate that the Lord is our friend.” The gopīs strengthen their love, thinking, “We are fortunate that the Lord is our lover.”

Moreover, on meeting Kṛṣṇa, no awareness of his powers manifests, because meeting creates coolness, like the light of the moon. In separation, awareness of his powers appears because that generates heat, like the light of the sun. However, if there is no trembling of the heart or great reverence and respect, it cannot be called knowledge of his powers (aiśvarya-jñānam). It is said:

*mṛgayur iva kapīndraṁ vivyadhe lubdha-dharmā*  
*striyam akṛta virūpāṁ strī-jitaḥ kāma-yānām*  
*balim api balim attvāveṣṭayad dhvāṅkṣa-vad yas*  
*tad alam asita-sakhyair dustyajas tat-kathārthaḥ*

**Like a hunter, he cruelly shot the king of the monkeys with arrows. Because he was conquered by a woman, he disfigured another woman who came to him with lusty desires. And even after consuming the gifts of Bali Mahārāja, he bound him up with ropes as if he were a crow. So let us give up all friendship with this dark-complexioned boy, even if we can’t give up talking about him. SB 10.47.17**

Before he lifted Govardhana, the people of Vraja were not aware that Kṛṣṇa was the supreme Lord.

After lifting Govardhana and going to Varuṇa’s planet they realized Kṛṣṇa is the Lord. However, realization of his sweetness remained full, as explained above.

Though they became aware of his powers through the words of Varuṇa and Uddhava, Yaśodā never states or thinks, “Kṛṣṇa is not my son.” Vasudeva however says this with *yuvān na naḥ sutau*: you two are not our sons. Thus the people of Vraja always have pure mādhurya-jñānam or knowledge of his sweetness to the fullest extent,. The people of Dvārakā however have mādhurya-jñānam mixed with aiśvarya-jñanam to the fullest extent.

6\. “In Dvārakā though he performs pastimes as a human, Kṛṣṇa, the son of Vasudeva, knows he is the Lord. In Vraja, does Kṛṣṇa, the son of Nanda, know that he is the Lord? If he knows, he could shed tears caused by fear of his mother when she came to tie him up.”

Only a foolish person would say that he is only pretending to cry. A knowledgeable devotee would never say this. The great devotee Kuntī says:

*gopy ādade tvayi kṛtāgasi dāma tāvad*  
*yā te daśāśru-kalilāñjana-sambhramākṣam*  
*vaktraṁ ninīya bhaya-bhāvanayā sthitasya*  
*sā māṁ vimohayati bhīr api yad bibheti*

**My dear Kṛṣṇa, Yaśodā took up a rope to bind you when you committed an offense, and your perturbed eyes flooded with tears, which washed the mascara from your eyes. And you were afraid, though fear personified is afraid of you. This sight is bewildering to me. SB 1.8.31**

She would not say that she was bewildered, if Kṛṣṇa were only pretending. Saying that fear itself fears Kṛṣna shows that she has aiśvarya-jñānam. The words *bhaya-bhāvanayā sthitasya* indicate that the internal fear was real. If he were only pretending, Kuntī would not have been bewildered.

If Kṛṣṇa is not aware that he is the Lord in Vraja, how can the Lord wh is eternally knowledge and bliss in its highest form have an eternal covering on his knowledge? Just as avidyā, the function of māyā, covers the knowledge of the jīva in order that he experience suffering in saṁsāra, and just as yoga-māyā, a function of the cit śakti covers the knowledge of Yaśodā and others, who are associates of Kṛṣṇa, though they are beyond the guṇas, in order that they experience the happiness of his sweet pastimes, so prema, the essence of the cit śakti, covers Kṛṣṇa’s knowledge so that he can experience the greatest bliss, which is his very svarūpa. There is no fault in Kṛṣṇa for such covering, since prema is his svarūpa-śakti. Avidyā binds the jīva by its function of possessiveness in order that he suffer. A person to be punished is bound with ropes and chains and a respectable person is bound with a fine vest and priceless perfumes. In any case, the jiva, under the control of avidyā, suffers. Kṛṣna, under control of prema, enjoys great happiness. One should consider that when Kṛṣṇa is overcome with prema, it gives him a special type of happiness, just as the bee derives special happiness being covered by lotus petals.

*ye tu tvadīya-caraṇāmbuja-kośa-gandhaṁ*  
*jighranti karṇa-vivaraiḥ śruti-vāta-nītam*  
*bhaktyā gṛhīta-caraṇaḥ parayā ca teṣāṁ*  
*nāpaiṣi nātha hṛdayāmburuhāt sva-puṁsām*

**O master! You do not give up the lotus hearts of your devotees who smell through their ears the fragrance of your lotus bud feet brought by the wind of the Vedas, and take your lotus feet as the greatest treasure through prema-bhakti. SB 3.9.5**

*visṛjati hṛdayaṁ na yasya sākṣād*  
*dharir avaśābhihito 'py aghaugha-nāśaḥ*  
*praṇaya-rasanayā dhṛtāṅghri-padmaḥ*  
*sa bhavati bhāgavata-pradhāna uktaḥ*

**He who binds the lotus feet of the Lord by ropes of love, and whose heart the Lord, destroyer of heaps of sin, does not leave, even if beckoned accidentally, is called the best of devotees. SB 11.2.55**

Moreover, just as the jīva experiences degrees of the five types of suffering according to the degree that knowledge is covered by different amounts of avidyā, so the devotee and the Lord (āśṛaya and viṣaya) experience different amounts of happiness which are of unlimited types according to the degree of being covered by aiśvarya-jñāna, caused by different amounts of prema.

The steadiness of Yaśodā’s pure prema binds her and Kṛṣṇa with ropes of possessiveness for each other and makes them controlled by each other. If aiśvarya-jñāna appears it increases her bliss. This does not happen with the love of Devakī and others, which is mixed with aiśvarya-jñāna.

Thus Kṛṣṇa, overcome by vātsalya and other types of prema when in contact with the people of Vraja, does not know that he is the Lord. When his omniscience is manifest at the time of a demon approaching or during the forest fire, it manifests by the līlā-śakti in order to protect his associates and their prema.

It was previously explained that his being omniscient, in order to receive the service of his sādhaka devotees, even though he is absorbed in his pastimes of prema, is accomplished by his acintya-śakti. Vidhi-marga and rāga-marga, aiśvarya and mādhurya, and aiśvarya-jñāna and mādhurya-jñāna were discussed. Svakīya and parakīya states are discussed in detail in Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi.

By worshipping Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa according to vidhi-marga (and no worship of Dvārakā), one attains aiśvarya-jñāna with no distinction of svakīyā or parakīya in Mahāvaikuṇṭha situated in Goloka. If one worships Kṛṣṇa according to vidhi-marga (with worship of Dvārakā), with greed for mādhurya-bhāva, one attains mādhurya-jñāna mixed with aiśvarya-jñānam in svakīyā mode in Dvārakā, as an associate of Satyabhāmā, since Rādhā and Satyabhāmā are one. If one worships with greed for mādhurya-bhava according to rāga-marga, one attains pure mādhurya-jñāna in parakīya mode as an associate of Rādhā, in Vraja.

Since Rādhā is the hlādinī śakti, arising from Kṛṣṇa’s svarūpa, Kṛṣṇa accompanies her. Thus one should worship the two of them with their pastimes, not devoid of their pastimes. The scriptures do not mention them as married during their pastimes in Vraja. Rādhā remains in a parakīya state in both the prakaṭa and aprakaṭa pastimes. That is the final essence in brief.

7\. The devotee practicing rāgānuga bhakti after anartha-nivṛtti, niṣṭhā, ruci and āsakti, comes to the stage of prema. How he directly attains his desired goal is now explained.

*tad-bhāva-baddha-rāgā ye janās te sādhane ratāḥ*  
*tad-yogyam anurāgaughaṁ prāpyotkaṇṭhānusārataḥ*  
*tā ekaśo'thavā dvi-trāḥ kāle kāle vraje'bhavan*

**Those persons who have great attached for the paroḍhā-bhāva and are absorbed in rāgānuga sādhana, develop intense sādhana arising from their longing. After some time they are born in Vraja singly, or in groups of two or three. Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 3.49-50**

*Anurāgaugam* means intense rāgānuga sādhana, not the state of anurāga just before mahābhāva, sinc that state of anurāga is not posible in the body of a sādhaka. They are born in Vraja. Just as when Kṛṣna descends as avatāra, his eternal consorts appear as well, so at that time the devotee appears as a sādhana-siddha devotee in the womb of a gopī in this world. By the power of association with the nitya-siddha gopīs who have mahābhāva, by seeing, hearing and chanting, these devotees develop sneha, māna, praṇaya, rāga, anurāga and mahābhāva in gopī bodies, since these stages are not possible in the body of the sādhaka in his previous birth. Thus in Vraja, the consorts of Krṣṇa have extraordinary qualities. It is said:

*gopīnāṁ paramānanda āsīd govinda-darśane*  
*kṣaṇaṁ yuga-śatam iva yāsāṁ yena vinābhavat*

**The young gopīs took the greatest pleasure in seeing Govinda, since for them even a moment without his association seemed like a hundred ages. SB 10.19.16**

*aṭati yad bhavān ahni kānanaṁ*  
*truṭi yugāyate tvām apaśyatām*

**When you go off to the forest during the day, a tiny fraction of a second becomes like a millennium for us because we cannot see you. SB 10.31.15**

Feeling that a moment is like a hundred yugas is a symptom of mahābhāva. “Why do you not say that a sādhaka who has attained prema, after giving up his material body, attains the states of sneha etc through association with nityasiddha gopīs on accepting a body of a gopī, without having to take birth in the womb of a gopī in the pastimes within the material world?”

It cannot be so. Without birth from the womb of a gopī, the behavior related to human pastimes with identity of a sakhi as a the daughter of a certain cowherd mother and father, and as a daughter-in-law or wife, cannot take place. “The devotee could take birth in the aprakaṭa pastimes (spiritual world).” That cannot be, since one sees that Vṛndāvana manifested beyond the material world can be entered only by the siddhas and not by sādhakas or material persons. It is the place for siddhas. Sneha and other higher bhāvas do not develop there suddenly by a person’s sādhana.

The devotees who have developed prema are brought by Yogamāyā to Vṛndāvana in this world when Kṛṣṇa comes as avatāra in order to perfect those bhāvas, before associating directly with Kṛṣṇa, after being born there. Since one can see that sādhakas, karmīs and siddhas all enter Vṛndavana manifested on earth, it is the place of sādhakas as well as siddhas.

“Until that time when Kṛṣṇa appears in the world, where do the devotees with great longing dwell?” At the time of giving up his sādhaka body, by the mercy of the Lord the devotee who has attained prema and has had great longing for service for a long time is given his desired service and sees the Lord with his associates, without sneha or other bhāvas, as in the case of Nārada. He is then given a body of a gopī which is filled with knowledge and bliss. That body appears by the agency of Yogamāyā in earthly Vṛndāvana from the womb of a gopī when Kṛṣṇa appears in this world with his associates. There is no delay in time at all for this to happen, since there is no break in the prakaṭa-līlā. In whatever universe the Vṛndāvana pastimes are taking place at that time, there in Vraja, he takes birth. Thus the moment the sādhaka prema bhakta gives up his body, Kṛṣṇa always manifests to him with his associates. O devotees with great longing! Do not fear. Remain calm! You have only good fortune.

8\. O bliss of Gokula! I offer respects to you, the ocean of omniscience and ignorance, a bee greedy for the bud of bhakti, expressed by manifestations of pastimes. You have said that you give intelligence, by which a person can come to you. O Lord! Therefore I make this request. O son of Nanda! You are the ornament on the breast of a gopī. Please give me intelligence so that I can be your servant.

Some people say that rāgānuga bhakti surpasses all scriptural rules completely, at all times. They should accept the statements of the Gītā:

*ye śāstra-vidhim utsṛjya yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ*  
*teṣāṁ niṣṭhā tu kā kṛṣṇa sattvam āho rajas tamaḥ*

**O Kṛṣṇa, what is the position of those who give up the rules of scripture but worship with faith? Is this a condition in the mode of** ***sattva*****, or in the modes of** ***rajas*** **and** ***tamas*****? BG 17.1**

*vidhi-hīnam asṛṣṭānnaṁ mantra-hīnam adakṣiṇam*  
*śraddhā-virahitaṁ yajñaṁ tāmasaṁ paricakṣate*

**They say that the sacrifice which is contrary to scripture, with no food given in charity, without proper mantras, in which the priests are not paid, and which is performed without faith, is tamasic. BG 17.13**

Such persons cause disturbance in the past, present and future. What more can be said?

9\. The path of rāgānuga is difficult to attain for devatās. May the intelligent devotees understand this path by this revealing booklet.
