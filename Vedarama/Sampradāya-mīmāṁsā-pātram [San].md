# Sampradāya-mīmāṁsā-pātram \[San\]

## Sampradāya-mīmāṁsā-pātram \[San\]

Author: **gauragovindananda bhagavata svami**

Born in a middle-class brahmin family in Jessore district, he left home at the age of 13. He wore saffron-colored cloth, which was given to him by a tantrik yogi while still very young. He went to live in Nabadwip where he studied Vaishnava scriptures. Wide travels in Vrindavan, etc. Of which seven years were spent living in a gupha in Kamyavana, living on madhukari. Later he preached extensively in East Bengal and Assam--Mymensingh, Silchar, Dhaka, Sylhet and Tripura.

**Books**: Kripa-kusumanjali, Sadhana-kusumanjali, Sri-guru-vaisnava-bhaktti-kusumanjali, Sri-guru-tattva-kusumanjali, Sri-lila-tattva-kusumanjali.

He left this world in 1942. His samadhi is in Nabadwip.

śrīmad-gaura-govindānanda-bhāgavata-svāmi-pādasya

mukhyena sampradāyitvaṁ sampradāya-vidāṁ naye \|

sampradāyi-guror dīkṣā-mantra-grahaṇato bhavet \|\|1\|\|

śiṣṭa-paramparācāryopadiṣṭa-mantrā eva hi \|

sampradāya iti khyātaḥ sudhībhiḥ sampradāyibhiḥ \|\|2\|\|

śiṣṭatvaṁ nāma cāmnāya-prāmāṇyābhyupagantṛtā \|

vedānāṁ viṣṇu-pāramyāt śiṣṭo vaiṣṇava ucyate \|\|3\|\|

atat-parasparatvena vaiṣṇavatvaṁ na siddhyati \|

avaiṣṇavopadiṣṭenety ādi śāstra-prakopaṇāt \|\|4\|\|

tasmāt śiṣṭānuśiṣṭānāṁ paramparāṁ rirakṣiṣuḥ \|

sva-niḥśvasita-vedo’pi gauro mādhva-mataṁ gataḥ \|\|5\|\|

sarva-jagad-guruḥ śrīmad gaurāṅgo loka-śikṣayā \|

purīśvaraṁ guruṁ kṛtvā svīcakre sampradāyakam \|\|6\|\|

kañcin mata-viśeṣo’pi nirastas tattva-vādinām \|

śrīmad-gaurāṅga-devena sampradāyasya tena kim \|\|7\|\|

sampradāyaika-dīkṣāṇāṁ mithaḥ kiñcin matāntarāt \|

śākhā-bhedo bhaven mātraṁ sampradāyo na bhidyate \|\|8\|\|

rāmānandī yathā rāmānujīyāntargato bhavet \|

nimbārka-sampradāye ca harivyāsādayo yathā \|\|9\|\|

gauḍīyas tattvavādī ca tathā mādhva-mataṁ gatau \|

na hy atra bādhakaḥ kaścit dṛśyate tattva-vittamaiḥ \|\|10\|\|

tuṣyatv iti matenāpi sampradāya-viniścaye \|

svīkṛtaṁ sādhakatvena cet sādhyādi-vivecanam \|

tathāpy atyanta-bhedo na śrī-gaura-mādhvayor mate \|\|11\|\|

madhva-mate ca yā muktiḥ sādhyatvena prakīrtitā \|

viṣṇv-aṅghri-prāpti-rūpā sā bhāṣya-kṛdbhiḥ pradarśitā \|\|12\|\|

sādhanaṁ cārpitaṁ karma-jīvādhikāra-bhedataḥ \|

svīkṛtam api madhvena bhakteḥ śraiṣṭhyaṁ bahu-stutam \|\|13\|\|

pramāṇaṁ bhārataṁ mātraṁ madhva-mate’nṛtaṁ vacaḥ \|

yat tena trividhaṁ proktaṁ mukhyaṁ śabda-pramāṇakam \|\|14\|\|

śrīman-nartaka-gopāla-sevā yena pratiṣṭhitā \|

iṣṭatvena kathaṁ tasya nirṇīto dvārakā-patiḥ \|\|15\|\|

niścito dvārkādhīśo yadyapi vā kṣatiḥ kutaḥ \|

yo nanda-nandanaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sa eva dvārakā-patiḥ \|

svarūpayor dvayo aikyaṁ kṛṣṇatvam aviśeṣataḥ \|\|16\|\|

līlābhimāna-bhedena pūrṇatamaś ca pūrṇakaḥ \|

na tu svarūpato bhedas tayor asti kathañcana \|\|17\|\|

bhedābheda-mataṁ yac cācintyākhyaṁ kīrtyate budhaiḥ \|

śrī-caitanya-matābhijñaiḥ tac ca madhva-mateṅgitam \|\|18\|\|

jīvānāṁ brahma-vaijātye guṇāṁśatvād abhinnatā \|

pratiyogitva-bhedatve cin-mātratvāt tad-ekatā \|\|19\|\|

tad-vyāpyatva-tad-āyatta-vṛttikatvādi-hetutaḥ \|

sāmānādhikaraṇyaṁ ca gosvāmi-madhvayoḥ samam \|\|20\|\|

vicāra-mātra-naipuṇyaṁ śakti-śaktimator iha \|

gaura-kṛpodbhavo’cintya-vādo gosvāmibhiḥ smṛtaḥ \|

tattva-nirdhāraṇe mukhyaḥ kāraṇa-vāda ucyate \|\|21\|\|

parākhya-śaktimad brahma nimitta-kāraṇaṁ bhavet \|

upādānaṁ tu tad brahma jīva-pradhāna-śakti-yuk \|

iti kāraṇa-vāde’pi hy ubhayor matayoḥ samam \|\|22\|\|

śrī-govindābhidhaṁ bhāṣyaṁ pramāṇaṁ yadi manyate \|

prameya-ratna-siddhānta-niṣkṛṣṭā tat-samāhṛtiḥ \|\|23\|\|

vakti śrī-gaura-sammatiṁ madhvaḥ prāhety upakrame \|

yadi vopekṣate kaiścit tarhy ardha-kukkuṭi-nayaḥ \|\|24\|\|

iti śrī-śrī-gauḍīya-vaiṣṇava-sāhitye śrī-haridāsa-dāsa-mahodayena dhṛtam \|\|
