# Sankalpa-kalpadruma \[BS\]

## Śrī Saṅkalpa-Kalpadruma

### Text 1

*vṛndāvaneśvari vayo-guṇa-rūpa-līlā-*  
*saubhagya-keli-karuṇā-jaladhe ’vadhehi*  
*dāsī-bhavāni sukhayāni sadā sa-kāntām-*  
*tvām ālibhiḥ parivṛtām idam eva yāce*

**Перевод:**

**О queen of Vrndavana! О ocean of mercy endowed with play, auspiciousness, pastimes, form, qualities and age! Please understand! May I be your maid servant! May I please you, surrounded by your friends, and Krsna! This I request.**

### Text 2

*śṛṅgārayāni bhavatīm abhisārayāni*  
*vīkṣayaiva kānta-vadanam-parivṛtya yāntīm*  
*dhṛtvāñcalena hari-sannidhim ānayāni*  
*samprāpya tarjana-sudhām-hṛṣitā bhavāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I decorate you and arrange appointments for you! May I bring you near Krsna, pulling you by the edge of your cloth when, seeing his face, you turn and leave. May I become joyful ou being scolded by you.**

### Text 3

*pāde nipatya śirasānunayāni ruṣṭām-*  
*taṁ praty apāṅga-kalikām api cālayāni*  
*tad-dor-dvayena sahasā parirambhayāni*  
*romāñca-kañcukavatīm avalokayāni*

**Перевод:**

**Falling with my head at your feet, may I pacify you. May I encourage you to throw angry glances at him. May I have him embrace you strongly with his arms. May I see you with hairs standing on end all over your body as if it were a bodice.**

### Text 4

*prāṇa-priye kusuma-talpam alaṅkuru tvam*  
*ity acyutokti-makaranda-rasaṁ dhayāni*  
*māṁ muñca mādhava satīm iti gadgadārdha-*  
*vācas tavaitya nikaṭam harim ākṣipāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I drink the sweet rasa of Krsna’s words, “O dearest to my life! Please decorate this flower bed!” May I scold him when I come close to you and with choked voice you say, “O Madhava! Let go of me. I am a chaste girl.”**

### Text 5

*vāmām udasya nija-vakṣasi tena ruddhām*  
*ānanda-bāṣpa-timitāṁ muhur ucchalantīṁ*  
*vyastālakām skhalita-veṇīm abaddha-nīvīṁ*  
*tvāṁ vīkṣya sādhu janur eva kṛtārthayāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I make my birth most successful, on seeing you with untied skirt, undone braid, unkempt locks of hair, as you get up, soaked in blissful tears, blocked by him, situated on his chest when you display obstinacy.**

### Text 6

*talpe mayaiva racite bahu-śilpa-bhāji*  
*pauṣpe niveśya bhavatīṁ na na neti vācam*  
*kṛṣṇaṁ sukhena ramayantam ananta-līlam-*  
*vātāyanātta-nayanaiva nibhālayāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I joyfully see with my eyes through the window the unlimited pastimes which give joy to Krsna when he places you on the flower bed made skillfully by me as you say, “No, no.”**

### Text 7

*sthitvā bahir vyajana-yantra-nibaddha-ḍorī-*  
*pāṇir vikarṣaṇa-vasān mṛdu bījayāni*  
*uttuṅga-keli-kalita-śrama-bindu-jālam*  
*ālopayāni maṇitaiḥ smitam udgirāṇi*

**Перевод:**

**Situated outside the room, may I gently fan you by pulling the rope of the fan. May I remove the drops of perspiration arising from intense amorous play. May I release a smile when you moan in ecstasy.**

### Text 8

*śrī-rūpa-mañjari-mukha-priya-kinkarīṇām*  
*ādeśam eva satataṁ śirasā vahāni*  
*tenaiva hanta tulasī-paramānukampā-*  
*pātrī-bhavāni karavāṇi sukhena sevām*

**Перевод:**

**May I carry on my head hundreds of pleasing orders from the maidservants such as Rupa Manjari. May I become the object of Tulasi’s mercy because of that, and may I serve in joy.**

### Text 9

*mālyāni hāra-kaṭakādi-mṛjā-vicitra-*  
*vartīḥ sitāṁśu-ghusṛṇāguru-candanādi*  
*vīṭīr lavaṅga-khapurādi-yutāḥ sakhībhiḥ*  
*sārddhaṁ mudā viracayāni kalāṁ prakāśya*

**Перевод:**

**Along with the sakhis, may I joyfully make garlands, necklaces, bracelets, various ointments for the skin, camphor, kunkuma, aguru, sandalwood as well as betel nut with cloves.**

### Text 10

*tvāṁ srasta-veṣa-vasanābharaṇām sa-kāntāṁ*  
*vīkṣya prasādhana-vidhau drutam udyatābhiḥ*  
*śrī-rūpa-raṅga-tulasī-rati-mañjarībhiḥ*  
*dṛṣṭānayāni tava sammukham eva tāni*

**Перевод:**

**When Rupa, Ranga, Tulasi and Rati Manjaris see that your clothing and ornaments have  fallen off, and quickly attempt to rectify the situation, may I bring those items before you.**

### Text 11

*tvām āśikhā-caraṇa-mūḍha-vicitra-veṣāṁ*  
*spraṣṭuṁ punaś ca dhṛta-tṛṣṇam avekṣya kṛṣṇam*  
*āyāntam eva vikaṭa-bhrukuṭī-vibhaṅga-*  
*huṅkṛty-udañcita-mukhī vinivartayāni*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Krsna approach with thirst to touch you again, who are clothed from head to foot in wondrous dress, may I prevent him, by uttering “Hum” and frowning strongly with deception.**

### Text 12

*tatretya vismayavatīṁ lalitāṁ pratīha*  
*sādhvītva-kanṭaka-viniṣkramaṇārtham asyāḥ*  
*prāptaṁ nyasiddhad ayi mām iyam eva dhūrtety*  
*uktiṁ hareḥ sva-hṛd-alim- rasayāni nityam*

**Перевод:**

**May I constantly give joy to the bee of my heart from the words of Krsna to astonished Lalita. “This rascal servant prevents me when I come to pull out the thorn of Radha’s chastity.”**

### Text 13

*niṣkramya kuñja-bhavanād vipine vihartuṁ*  
*kāntaika-bāhu-parirabdha-tanuṁ prayāntīm*  
*tvām ālibhiḥ saha kathopakathā-praphulla-*  
*vaktrām ahaṁ vyajana-pāṇir anuprayāṇi*

**Перевод:**

**With a fan in my hand, may I follow you, as you walk, embraced by your lover’s arm, leaving the bower in order to play in the forest, your face smiling while you gossip with your friends.**

### Text 14

*gāyāni te guṇa-gaṇāṁs tava vartma-gamyaṁ*  
*puṣpāstarair mṛdulayāni sugandhayāni*  
*sālī-tatīḥ pratipadaṁ sumanobhi-vṛṣṭim-*  
*svāminy ahaṁ pratipadaṁ tanavāni bāḍham*

**Перевод:**

**May I sing about your many qualities. May I perfume and softer your path by sprinkling flowers. О mistress! May I shower flowers at every step on you and your friends.**

### Text 15

*preṣṭha-sva-pāṇi-kṛta-kausuma-hāra-kāñcī-*  
*keyūra-kuṇḍala-kirīṭa-virājitāṅgīm*  
*tvāṁ bhūṣayāni punar ātma-kavitva-puṣpair*  
*āsvādayāni rasikāli-tatīr imāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I decorate you, whose limbs are decorated with a flower necklace belt, armbands, earrings and crown made by Krsna’s kind, with the flower-like poetry which I compose. May I make your friends relish these verses.**

### Text 16

*candrāṁśu-rūpya-salilair avasikta-rodhasy*  
*añcat-kadamba-surabhāv ali-gīta- kīrtau*  
*ārabdha-rāsa-rabhasāṁ hariṇā saha tvāṁ*  
*tat-pāṭhitaiva viduṣī kalayāni vīṇām*

**Перевод:**

**On the bank sprinkled with water, made silver by the moon light, fragrant with kadamba trees and filled with the singing of bees, as taught by you, may I play the vina, when you are eager to begin the rasa dance with Krsna.**

### Text 17

*rāsaṁ samāpya dayitena samaṁ sakhībhir*  
*viśrānti-bhāji nava-mālatikā-nikuñje*  
*tvayy ānayāni rasavat-karakāmra-rambhā-*  
*drākṣādikāni sarasaṁ pariveśayāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I bring you, along with your friends and Krsna, to a new jasmine bower and joyfully serve you juicy pomegranates, mangoes, bananas and grapes, when you finish the rasa dance and are tired.**

### Text 18

*talpam saroja-dala-kḷptam anaṅga-keli-*  
*paryāptim ātma-kalayā racitaṁ tulasyām*  
*tvām preyasā saha rasād adhiśāyayāni*  
*tāmbūlam āśāyitum ulbaṁam ullasāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I joyfully make you rest with your lover on a lotus petal bed, made skillfully by Tulasi after you finish amorous play. May I become most joyful in making you chew betel nut.**

### Text 19

*samvāhayāni caraṇāv alakaiḥ spṛśāni*  
*jighrāṇi saurabha-samūḍha-camatkriyābdhiḥ*  
*akṣṇor dadhāny urasijau parirambhayāni*  
*cumbāny alakṣitam avekṣita-saukumāryāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**May I massage your feet, anoint them with lac, smell them in amazement and, bewildered by their fragrance, gaze upon them, embrace them to my chest, and, seeing the softness of your feet, kiss them unseen by others.**

### Text 20

*ante niśas tanutara-prasṛtālakālyā*  
*tāṭaṅka-hāra-tati-gandhavahāgra-muktāḥ*  
*preṣṭhasya te tava ca saṅgrathitā nibhālya*  
*tatrānayāni paramāpta-sakhīḥ prabodhya*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing your earrings, necklace and nose pearl tangled in Krsna’s fine, disheveled hair at the end of night, may I wake up the intimate sakhis and bring them there.**

### Text 21

*tā dārśayāni sukha-sindhuṣu majjayāni*  
*tābhyāḥ prasādam atulaṁ sahasāpnuvāni*  
*tan-nūpurādi-raṇitair gata-sāndra-nidrāṁ*  
*śayyotthitām sa-cakitāṁ bhavatīṁ bhajāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I show them this scene and drown them in an ocean of happiness. May I suddenly attain their mercy. With trepidation, may I worship you, when your rise from the bed after deep sleep because of the sound of their ankle bells.**

### Text 22

*he svāmini priya-sakhī-trapayākulāyāḥ*  
*kāntāṅgatas tava viyoktam apārayāntyāḥ*  
*udgranthayāny alaka-kuṇḍala-mālya-muktā-*  
*granthīṁ vicakṣaṇatayāṅguli-kauśalena*

**Перевод:**

**О queen! When you are overwhelmed with shame in front of your friends, being unable to free yourself from Krsna’s limbs (because of interwined ornaments, etc.), may I untangle the knots of your locks, earrings, garland and pearls using my skilful fingers.**

### Text 23

*nāsāgrataḥ śruti-yugāc ca viyojayāni*  
*tad-bhūṣaṇaṁ maṇi-sarāṁs tu visūtrayāṇi*  
*prāṇārbudād adhikam eva sadā tavaikaṁ*  
*romāpi devi kalayāni kṛtāvadhānā*

**Перевод:**

**May I take off the ornaments from your nose and ears and untie your jewels. О mistress! May I carefully consider each of your hairs as a million of my lives.**

### Text 24

*tvāṁ sālim ātma-sadanaṁ nibhṛtaṁ vrajantīṁ*  
*tyaktvā harer anupathaṁ tad-alakṣitaitya*  
*taṁ khaṇḍitām anunayantam avekṣya candrāṁ*  
*tad-vṛttam āli-tati-saṁsadi varṇayāni*

**Перевод:**

**Leaving you as you go secretly to your house with your friends, and following Krsna, unseen by him, and then seeing him pacify Candravali, whom he failed to meet, may I describe all this to the group of friends.**

### Text 25

*prakṣālayāni vadanam-salilaiḥ sugandhair*  
*dantān rasālaja-dalais tava dhāvayāni*  
*nirṇejayāni rasanāṁ tanu-hema-patryā*  
*sandārśayāni mukuram nipuṇam pramṛjya*

**Перевод:**

**May I have you wash your mouth with scented water, and have you brush your teeth with mango leaves. May I have you clean your longue with a thin, golden scraper. May I show a skillfully polished mirror.**

### Text 26

*snānāya sūkṣma-vasanam paridhāpayāni*  
*hārāṅgadādy apaghanād avatārayāṇi*  
*abhyañjayāny aruṇa-saurabha-hṛdya-tailair*  
*udvartayāni nava-kuṅkuma-candra-cūrṇaiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**May I dress you in fine cloth for bathing and remove the necklaces and bracelets from your body. May I rub pleasing pink oil on your body and remove it using fresh kunkuma and camphor powder.**

### Text 27

*nīrair mahā-surabhibhiḥ snapayāni gātrād*  
*ambhāṁsi sūkṣma-vasanair apasārayāṇi*  
*keśān javād aguru-dhūma-kulena yatnād*  
*aśoṣayāṇi rabhasena sugandhayāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I bathe you in heavily perfumed water and remove the water from your limbs with a fine cloth. May I dry your hair quickly and carefully and scent it with aguru smoke.**

### Text 28

*vāso mano’bhirucitaṁ paridhāpayāni*  
*sauvarṇa-kaṅkaṭikayā cikurān viśodhya*  
*gumphāni veṇīm amalaiḥ kusumair vicitrām*  
*agre lasac-camarikā-maṇi-jātabhāntām*

**Перевод:**

**May I dress you in cloth that is pleasing to your mind. Cleaning your locks with a golden comb, may I braid your hair with pure flowers, making it shine with a jewel at the tip.**

### Text 29

*cūḍāmaṇim śirasi mauktika-patrapāsyāṁ*  
*bhāle vicitra-tilakaṁ ca mudā viracya*  
*aṅktvākṣiṇī śruti-yugaṁ maṇi-kuṇḍalāḍhyaṁ*  
*nāsām alaṅkṛtavatīṁ karavāṇi devi*

**Перевод:**

**May I joyfully place a jewel on your head, with a pearl along with colorful tilaka. О mistress! Anointing your eyes, may I ornament your nose and place earrings in your ears.**

### Text 30

*gaṇḍa-dvaye makarike cibuke vilikhya*  
*kastūrikeṣṭa-pṛṣataṁ kucayoś ca citram*  
*bāhvos tavāṅgada-yugaṁ maṇi-bandha-yugme*  
*cūḍā masāra-kalitāḥ kalayāni yatnāt*

**Перевод:**

**Drawing makaris on your cheeks, a dot of musk on your chin, and patterns on your breasts, may I carefully place armlets on your arms and bracelets on your wrists.**

### Text 31

*pāṇy aṅgulīḥ kanaka-ratna-mayormikābhir*  
*abhyarcayāni hṛdayaṁ padakottamena*  
*muktota- kañculikayorasijau vicitra-*  
*mālyena hāra-nicayena ca kaṇṭha-deśam*

**Перевод:**

**I worship your fingers with gold and jewel studded rings. Jay I worship your heart with an excellent padaka ornament. May I worship your breast with pearl woven bodice. May I worship your throat with a colorful garland and necklaces.**

### Text 32

*kāñcyā nitambam atha haṁsaka-nūpurābhyāṁ*  
*pādāmbuje dala-tatiṁ kvaṇad-aṅgurīyaiḥ*  
*lakṣā-rasair aruṇam apy anurañjayāni*  
*he devi tat-tala-yugaṁ kṛta-puṇya-puñjā*

**Перевод:**

**O mistress! Accruing piles of piety, may I decorate your waist with a belt, your lotus feet with ankle bells and hamsakas, your toes with jingling toe rings, and the soles of your reddish feet with lac.**

### Text 33

*aṅgāni sāhajika-saurabhayanty athāpi*  
*devy arcayāni nava-kuṅkuma-carcayaiva*  
*līlāmbujaṁ kara-tale tava dhārayāṇi*  
*tvāṁ darśayāni maṇi-darpaṇam arpayitvā*

**Перевод:**

**O mistress! May I worship your naturally fragrant limbs with ointment of fresh kunkuma. May I place a toy lotus in your hand and show you your reflection by offering a jeweled mirror.**

### Text 34

*saundaryam adbutam avekṣya nijaṁ sva-kānta-*  
*netrāli-lobhanam avetya vilola-gātrīm*  
*prāṇārbudena vidhuvartika-dīpakaiś ca*  
*nirmañchayāni nayanāmbu-nimajjitāṅgī*

**Перевод:**

**May I perform aratrika to your trembling limbs using camphor lamps and with millions of my lives as you behold your astonishing beauty which stimulates greed in Krsna’s bee-like eyes.**

### Text 35

*goṣṭheśvarī-prahitayā saha kundavalyā*  
*prabhāṭikā-priyatamāśana-sādhanāya*  
*yāntīm- samam- priya-sakhībhir anuprāyaṇi*  
*tāmbūla-sampuṭa-maṇi-vyajanādi-pāṇiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**May I follow behind you, holding a jeweled fan and betel nut case, as you depart with dear sakhis for preparing Krsna’s breakfast with Kundalata, who has been sent by Yasoda.**

### Text 36

*goṣṭheśvarī-sadanam etya pade praṇamya*  
*tasyās tad-āpta-bhavikāṁ trapayāvṛtāṅgīm*  
*ghratāṁ tayā śirasi tan-nayanāmbu-siktām*  
*tvām vīkṣya tām aham api praṇamāmi bhaktyā*

**Перевод:**

**When I see you going to Yasoda’s house, offering her respects receiving blessing from her, becoming embarrassed, and being showered by her tears as she smells your head, I offer respects to her also with devotion.**

### Text 37

*Mūrtaṁ tapo’si vṛṣabhānu-kulasya bhāgyaṁ*  
*gehasya me’si tanayasya ca me varāṅgi*  
*nairujyadāsy amṛta-pāṇir abhūr vareṇa*  
*durvāsaso yad iti tad-vacasā hasāni*

**Перевод:**

**“You are austerity personified in Vrsabhanu’s family. You are good fortune for my house and give good health to my son, since by Durvasa’s blessing you have developed hands of nectar. O beautiful-limbed girl!” Hearing Yasoda’s words, may I smile.**

### Text 38

*snātānulipta-vapuṣo dayitasya tasya*  
*tat-kālike madhurimaṇy ati-lolitākṣīm*  
*svāminy avetya bhavatīṁ kvacana pradeśe*  
*tatraiva kena ca miṣeṇa samānayāni*

**Перевод:**

**O mistress! When Krsna has bathed and anointed his body, at that sweet moment, may I lead you, endowed with rolling eyes, to some place on some pretext.**

### Text 39

*prakṣālayāni caraṇau bhavad-aṅgataḥ sraṅ-*  
*mālyādi pāka-racanānupayogi yat tat*  
*uttārayāṇi tad idam tu tavā’stv iti tvad-*  
*vācollasāni vikasan-madhu-mādhavīva*

**Перевод:**

**May I wash your feet, and take the garlands off your limbs, as that is more suitable for cooking. When you say “These are yours,” may I become joyful like a blossoming madhavi flower.**

### Text 40

*paktvā sthitāṁ madhura-pāyasa-śāka-sūpa-*  
*bhāji-prabhṛty-amṛta-nindi catur-vidhānnam*  
*tvāṁ lokayāni na na neti muhur vadantīṁ*  
*goṣṭheśayāpi pariveśayituṁ nidiṣṭām*

**Перевод:**

**When you stand there after cooking four types of food that deride nectar such as sweet rice, spinach, soup and fried foods, may I gaze upon you as you say, “No, no,” when Yasoda orders you to serve the meal.**

### Text 41

*tṛpty-utthitāṁ priyatamāṅga-ruciṁ dhayantyā*  
*vātāyanārpita-dṛśaḥ sahasollasantyāḥ*  
*ānandaja-dyuti-taraṅga-bhare manoja-*  
*mañju-kṛte tava mano mama majjayāni*

**Перевод:**

**May my mind become submerged in the huge waves of amorous beauty arising from  your bliss, as you gaze through the window and drink the beauty of Krsna’s beauty arising from satisfaction at eating and from suddenly become joyful.**

### Text 42

*rādhe tavaiva gṛham etad aham ca jāte*  
*sūnoḥ śubhe kim aparāṁ bhavatīm avaimi*  
*tad bhuṅkṣva sammukham iti vrajapā-girā tvad-*  
*vaktraṁ smitam sva-hṛdayam rasayāni nityam*

**Перевод:**

**May I constantly cherish your smiling face in my heart, when Yasoda says, “O Radha! О auspicious daughter! This house and I am yours. How can I see you as different from my son? Please eat in front of me.”**

### Text 43

*yāntam vanāya sakhibhiḥ samam ātma-kāntaṁ*  
*pitrādibhiḥ sa-ruditair anugamyamānam*  
*vīkṣyāpta-gaurava-gehāṁ dinanātha-pūjā-*  
*vyājena labdha-gahanāṁ bhavatīṁ bhajāni*

**Перевод:**

**May I worship you as you go to the forest on the pretext of worshipping the sun god after reaching home, after seeing Krsna go to the forest with his friends while the elders follow him crying.**

### Text 44

*kāntaṁ vilokya kusumāvacaye pravṛttām*  
*ādāya patra-puṭikām anuyāny ahaṁ tvām*  
*kā taskarīyam iti tad-vacasā na kāpīty*  
*uktyā sahārpita-dṛśāṁ bhavatīṁ smarāṇi*

**Перевод:**

**May I follow you as you begin to pick flowers and put them in a basket when you see Krsna. May I remember you as you suddenly glance at him when he says, “Who is this thief?” and you say, “No one.”**

### Text 45

*puṣpāṇi darśaya kiyanti hṛtāni caurīty*  
*uktyaiva puṣpa-puṭikām api gopayāni*  
*tad vīkṣya hanta mama kakṣa-tale kṣipantaṁ*  
*pāṇiṁ balāt tam abhimṛśya bhavāni dūnā*

**Перевод:**

**May I become miserable when he says, “Show how many flowers have been stolen, thief!’ and I hide the flower basket, but seeing that, he forcible thrusts his hand to my belt.**

### Text 46

*rakṣādya devi kṛpayā nija-dāsikāṁ mām*  
*ity ucca-kātara-girā śaraṇaṁ vrajāni*  
*kiṁ dhūrta duḥkhayasi maj-janam ity amuṣya*  
*bāhuṁ kareṇa tudatīṁ bhavatīṁ śrayāṇi*

**Ranga Manjari is Krsna-carana Cakravarti and Prema Manjari is Ganga Miayana Cakravarti. Vilasa Manjari is Narottama Dasa Thakura. О Manjulali! ### Text 93

*he bhānumaty anupama-praṇayābdhi-magnā*

**Bhanumati (Jiva)! You are absorbed in the ocean of incomparable affection. Take me to the feet of your mistress. О Lavanga Manjari (Sanatana)! You have fallen in the river of prema. Give me your sweet glance.**

### Text 94

*he rūpa-mañjari sadāsi nikuñja-yūnoḥ*

**О Rupa Manjari! You are always absorbed in the variety of joyful amorous arts of the couple in the bower. May your mercy have the power to fulfill what I have vowed, being endowed with vision bestowed by you.**

### Text 95

*rādhāṅga-śaśvad-upagūhanatas tad-āpta-*

**Перевод:**

**By constantly embracing Radha’s limbs, holding in your heart and body, her two qualities, you have become a golden ocean of mercy (Caitanya). О son of Nanda! Make the creeper of my desire bear fruit.**

### Text 96

**Перевод:**

**O Lalita! Hearing what is very famous in Vraja—that Radha and Krsna can be attained by the mercy of Lalita —may I take shelter of your lotus feet. Please give me your glance tinged with mercy.**

### Text 97

**Перевод:**

**You are famous in the assembly of beautiful sakhis as being one with Radha in name, form, qualities, character and age. О beautiful Visakha, endowed with unconditional mercy! Therefore, not counting my hundreds of offenses, please accept me.**

### Text 98

**O dear cowherd boys and priya-narma-sakhis, endowed with an unequalled wealth of prema, whose very lives are the youthful couple of Vraja! Bathing my head in the dust from your lotus feet, may I be successful.**

### Text 99

**О crown of Vrndavana, served by the people of Vraja! O Govardhana, guru among mountains! О best servant of Krsna! I am situated near you. May the creepers of desire grow quickly in the rocks of my heart.**

### Text 100

*śrī-rādhayā sama tadīya-sarovara tvat-*

**Перевод:**

**О Radha-kunda, equal to Radha! May I live on your bank and may I worship this place at this time. You should nourish my thirsty creepers, and make them flower and fruit from drinking your water.**

### Text 101

### Text 102

### Text 103

### Text 104
