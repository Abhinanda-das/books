# Santi-sukta-pathah by Bhanu Swami

## Invocation

*Hari oṁ*  
*āpyāyantu mamāṭgāni vāk prāṇaś cakṣuḥ śrotram atho balam indriyāni ca sarvāni*  
*sarvam brahmopaniṣadam*  
*māham brahma nirākuryaṁ*  
*mā mā brahma nirākarot*  
*Anirākāraṇam astu, anirākāraṇam me’astu*  
*Tad ātmani nirate ya upaniṣatsu dharmas te mayi santu, te mayi santu*  
*Oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ hari oṁ*

**Перевод:**

**Om! O para brahma, Paramātmā! By your mercy may all my limbs, power of speech, life airs, eyes, ears--all my action senses and knowledge gathering senses-- all my bodily and mental powers and strength, attain nourishment. All the Upaniṣads are non-different from Brahman itself, from You.**

**May I never reject the Lord described in the Upaniṣads; may the Lord never reject me.**

**May my relationship with you remain unbreakable. May your relationship with me remain unbreakable.**

**May all the principles enunciated in the Upaniṣads remain in me, who am absorbed in the Lord.**

**May they always remain within me.**

**O Paramātmā! May my threefold miseries be destroyed.**

## Part 1

### Verse 1

*oṁ keneṣitam patati preṣitam manaḥ*  
*kena prāṇaḥ prathamaḥ praiti yuktaḥ*  
*keneṣitām vacam imām vadanti*  
*cakṣuḥ śtrotram ka u devo yunakti*

**Перевод:**

**Inspired by whom does the mind run towards its desired object? Inspired by whom does the chief prāṇa carry out its functions? People speak words inspired by whom? Which devatā engages the eye and ear?**

**Комментарий:**

One who is fortunate, inquiring about Brahman, understanding in a general way that the functions of the senses such as the mind are not endowed with their own internal power, and wanting to know details about the conscious instigator, begins by asking “By whom does the mind carry out its functions.”

Inspired by whom (kena preṣitam) does the mind (manas), go (patati) towards objects of desire (iṣitam), whether beneficial or unbeneficial. By the law of causality, someone or something must make the mind move. The jīva cannot be the mover, because the mind moves towards objects in spite of the jīva trying to prevent it. In other words, someone more powerful than the jīva makes the mind move. This will be explained later. Iṣitam is an archaic form of iṣṭam with an additional “i”.

“Prathamah prāṇah” means the chief prāṇa among the five prāṇas. Inspired by whom (kena yukta) does the chief prāṇa (prathamaḥ prāṇaḥ) move (praiti), or carry out its functions. Caused by whom (kena iṣitam) do the jīvas speak (vadanti) either mundane or spiritual words (imām vacam). Eyes and ears are in singular forms as they are indicating limbs of the body. “U” is a particle indicating calling out to the guru. O teacher, what deva (ko devaḥ) makes the eye and ear (cakṣuḥ śtotram) carry out their functions (yunakti)?

The other senses of course are implied though only these are mentioned.

### Verse 2

*śrotrasya śtrotram manaso mano yad*  
*Vaco ha vacam sa u prāṇasya prāṇaḥ*  
*cakṣusas cakṣur atimucya dhīraḥ*  
*Prety asmāl lokād amṛtā bhavanti*

**Перевод:**

**O student, the deva who is the power behind the ear, the power behind the mind, the power behind speech, the power behind prāṇa and the power behind sight is that person alone. The wise persons, who know this, surpass the gross body and become liberated from the subtle body. They attain immortality.**

**Комментарий:**

Being asked by the student, the ācārya here begins to speak .

Yat means yah, he who. Śtotrasya śtotram means he who bestows the power of hearing to the ear. Manaso manaḥ means he who bestows power of thought to the mind. Vaco vacam means he who bestows the power of speaking to the voice. Prāṇasya prāṇaḥ means he who bestows the power of life to the prāṇa. Cakṣusas cakṣur is he who bestows the power of seeing to the eye. The controller of the ear and other senses about whom you inquired, is that person (saḥ).

The srutis say:

*yo manasi tiṣṭan manaso’ ntaro yam mano na veda*  
*yasya manaḥ śarīram yo mano’ ntaro yamayati eṣa ta atmāntaryāmy amṛtaḥ*

**The one who exists in the mind, who is different from the mind, whom the mind does not know, whose body is mind, who is within the mind, is the eternal spirit within, who regulates all. Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**3.7.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/3.7.20)

The same Upaniṣad continues to list the same antaryāmīi for the voice, ear, eye and prāṇa.

*yo vaci tiṣṭhan vacam antaro yamayati*

**situated in the voice, he regulates the voice from within.**

*yaḥ śrotre tisthan chrotram antaro yamayati*

**Situated in the ear, he regulates the ear from within.**

*yas cakṣusi tiṣṭhaṇś cakṣur antaro yamayati*

**Situated in the eye, he regulates the eye from within.**

*yaḥ prāṇe tisṭhan prāṇam antaro yamayati*

**Situated in prāṇa he regulates the prāṇa from within.**

These statements conclude that the person is the eternal antaryāmī, Nārāyaṇa, dwelling within all the senses. Thus in this verse, “saḥ” indicates Nārāyaṇa. The Lord himself says: mattaḥ sarvam pravartate: everything arises from me. (BG) The smṛti-śāstras thus confirm that Vāsudeva pervades all the senses, mind and intellect.

The verse then speaks about the results for the persons in knowledge of the controller of the senses. Dhīrāḥ means those who are intelligent, those who know that Nārāyaṇa is the life behind the ear and other organs. Giving up (pretya) the gross body (asmāl lokāt), proceeding by the path of the sun and giving up the subtle body (atimucya), they attain liberation (amṛtā bhavanti).

### Verse 3

*na tatra cakṣur gacchati na vag gacchati no manaḥ*  
*na vidmo na vijānīmo yathaitad anuśisyāt*  
*anyad eva tad viditād atho aviditād adhi*  
*iti śuśruma purveṣām ye nas tad vyācacakṣire*

**Перевод:**

**The supreme entity cannot be seen by the material eye. The material faculty of speech cannot convey him by speaking. The mind cannot conceive of him. I do not know that supreme entity. Thus I do not know how to teach the students. It is different from what is known and it is different from what is not known. We have heard this from those who explained this knowledge to us.**

### Verse 4

*yad vācā’nabhyuditam yena vāg abhyudyate*  
*tad eva brahma tvaṁ viddhi nedaṁ yad idam upāsate*

**Перевод:**

**That which cannot be revealed by words, by which speech has the power to reveal meaning, know that as Brahman. What common people worship is not Brahman.**

### Verse 5

*yan manasā na manute yenahur mano matam*  
*tad eva brahma tvaṁ viddhi nedaṁ yad idam upāsate*

**Перевод:**

**That which cannot be conceived of by the mind, but which comprehends the mind, know that to Brahman. That which the common people know through their mind and intellect is not Brahman.**

### Verse 6

*yac cakṣuṣā na paśyati yena cakṣuṁṣi paśyati*  
*tad eva brahma tvaṁ viddhi nedaṁ yad idam upāsate*

**Перевод:**

**That which one cannot see by the material eye, by which eyes see, know that to be Brahman. Anything else which common people worship is not Brahman.**

### Verse 7

*yac chrotrena na śṛṇoti yena śrotram idam śrutam*  
*tad eva brahma tvaṁ viddhi nedaṁ yad idam upāsate*

**Перевод:**

**What cannot be heard by the ear, but by which the ear hears, know that to be Brahman. What common people worship is not Brahman.**

### Verse 8

*yat prāṇena na prāṇiti yen prāṇaḥ praṇīyate*  
*tad eva brahma tvaṁ viddhi nedaṁ yad idam upāsate*

**Перевод:**

**What cannot be moved by prāṇa, by which the prāṇa moves, know that as Brahman. What common men worship is not Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Verses 4 to 8 show that Nārāyaṇa, the antaryāmī, is the activator of the mind and senses.

Note: Madhva gives the following meaning to the phrase na idam yad idam upasa te in the above verses.

Idam means jīva. Thus na idam means “Brahman is not the jīva.” Upa means near and asa means exists. Te means you. “Brahman is not jīva. Brahman (idam) exists (asa) near (upa) you (te), as your regulator.”

## Part 2

### Verse 1

*yadi manyase suvedeti daharam evāpi*  
*nūnam tvam vettha brahmaṇo rūpam*  
*tad asya tvaṁ yad asya devesv atha nu*  
*mīmāṁsyam eva te manye viditam*

**Перевод:**

**O student, if you think you know Brahman well, then you know little of the qualities of Brahman. Therefore, since you know very little, you should now inquire into the Brahman which is in you and in the devatās.**

**Комментарий:**

O student, if you think, “I know Brahman completely,” you actually know Brahman very little (daharam). Brahman is infinite, so you cannot know it completely. The word rūpam means the qualities of Brahman. Therefore (atha), now (nu), the form of Brahman (asya) which (yat) is in you (tvam means tvayi, in you ) and in the devas (deveṣu) should be considered (mīmāṁsyam) by you (te).

### Verse 2

*nāham manye suvedeti no na vedeti veda ca*  
*yo nas tad veda tad veda no na vedeti veda ca*

**Перевод:**

**Others say that I know Brahman completely, but I do not think I know Brahman. It is not that I do not know. I know somewhat.**

**Комментарий:**

Being asked “Others claim to know Brahman completely, but do you also know completely?” the teacher replies. “Others may say that I know Brahman completely. (ahaṁ suvedeti manye). But I do not think that I know Brahman (viditam na manye).” “But then you do not know Brahman at all?” the student asks. The teacher replies, “It is not that I do not know (no na veda). I also know.” No means na.

Then he repeats the fact that one cannot know Brahman completely. Among us (nah) he who says that he knows Brahman does not know Brahman (tad veda no), because he thinks God is finite. He who says he does not know indeed knows, because he has understood the infinite nature of God.

### Verse 3

*yasyāmataṁ tasya mataṁ mataṁ yasya na veda saḥ*  
*avijñātaṁ vijānatāṁ vijñātam avijānatām*

**Перевод:**

**The person who thinks he cannot know Brahman completely can know Brahman. He who thinks he knows Brahman completely do not know Brahman. For those who think they know Brahman, Brahman cannot be realized. For those who understand that they cannot know Brahman completely, Brahman can be realized.**

**Комментарий:**

Having stated that Brahman is beyond the hearing process, now in this verse he states that it is beyond the mental process as well. The person who admits he cannot conceive of Brahman, can conceive of Brahman, because he accepts the Lord’s unlimited nature. He who thinks he can conceive of Brahman completely cannot conceive of Brahman because of not understanding Brahman’s infinite nature.

A person can have realization of Brahman, but can never realize Brahman completely. This is stated in the second part of the verse. For those who think that they have complete realization of Brahman (vijānatām) there is no realization of Brahman (avijñātam), because it is not possible to know the infinite Brahman completely. For those who say that they have not had complete realization of Brahman (avijānatām) there is realization of Brahman (vijñātam), because they have understood his infinite nature.

### Verse 4

*pratibodha viditaṁ matam amṛtatvaṁ hi vindate*  
*ātmanā vindate vīryaṁ vidyayā vindate’ mṛtam*

**Перевод:**

**One who has a realization of Brahman knows Brahman. He attains liberation. By determination, he attains power. By bhakti, he attains Paramātmā.**

**Комментарий:**

One may ask how it is possible to attain liberation if one cannot know it fully. Even by partial knowledge one can get liberation. This is what is stated in this verse.

The word śrutam or heard should be supplied.

If the description of Brahman (matam) is heard and understood by persons according to their individual capacity for knowledge (pratibodha), or is realized by them according to their capacity for realization (pratibodha viditam), they attain (vindate) liberation (amṛtatvam). Or the meaning can be: one attains direct realization of Brahman (viditam amṛtatvam vindate) after understanding through worship and incidental knowledge (pratibodha ) .

By determination (ātmanā) he attains strength (vīryam) and by worship or devotion (vidyayā) he attains the Paramātmā (amṛtam).

For it is said in the smṛtis that the ātmā endowed with devotion and determination sees the embodiment of knowledge everywhere.1

### Verse 5

*iha ced avedīdatha satyam asti*  
*ne ced iha vedīn mahati vinaṣṭiḥ*  
*bhūteṣu bhūteśu vicitya dhīrāḥ*  
*prety asmāl lokād amṛtā bhavanti*

**Перевод:**

**If a human attains knowledge of the Lord, he attains the Lord. If he does not attain knowledge, he is subject to repeated birth in the material world. The wise, understanding this, realize the existence of Paramātmā in all souls, and leaving the world, attain immortality.**

**Комментарий:**

There may be true knowledge as a result of seeing the Lord, but when does this happen? This verse answers this question. If a person in this body suitable for knowledge knows the Lord, then he can attain the Lord (satyam), who was described in the previous verse. There is no doubt he can attain this. If he does not attain knowledge, then he attains great destruction. The wise persons (dhīrāḥ), distinguishing between the attainment of Brahman and the temporary world, perceiving (vicintya) the Brahman in all creatures (bhūteṣu), remembering this up till the point of death, leave this world (asmāl lokāt pretya) and attain amṛta, immortality.

## Part 3

### Verse 1

*brahma ha devebhyo vijigye*  
*tasya ha brahmaṇo vijaye devā amahīyanta*  
*ta aikṣantāsmākam evāyam*  
*vijayo ’smākam evāyam mahimeti*

**Перевод:**

**Brahman alone gave victory to the devatās. Though it was Brahman’s victory, astoundingly, the devatās worshipped themselves and became proud. They thought, “This is our victory. This is our glory.”**

**Комментарий:**

With the intention to show that the Lord cannot be fully known because of his astounding qualities, he tells a story.

In a battle between the devatās and daityas, Brahman entered the devatās and for the devatās (devebhyo) defeated (vijigye) the daityas. “Ha” indicates certainty. Then, after the victory, though it was certainly the victory of Brahman who had entered the devatās, the devatās were worshiped (amahīyanta). They became proud. “Ha” here indicates surprise. Next is explained how the devatās became proud. The devas thought (aikṣata) ”This is our victory. This is our glory.”

### Verse 2

*taddhaiṣām vijajñau tebhyo ha*  
*prādur babhuva, tan na vyajānata, kim idam yakṣam iti*

**Перевод:**

**Visnu knew the price of the devatās and , by his inconceivable power he appeared before them in an astonishing form. The devatās could not understand who the worshipable entity was.**

**Brahman knew (vijajñau) the pride (tat) of the devas (eṣām) caused by no knowing that he had entered into them to give victory. He appeared (prādur babhuva) to them (tebhyaḥ) in the form of a yakṣa in order to enlighten them of his true nature. They did not know (na vijānata) what that yakṣa was.**

### Verse 3

*te’gnim abruvan jata veda etad vijānīhi*  
*kim etad yakṣam iti tatheti*

**Перевод:**

**The devas said to Agni, “O Agni, who is the worshipable person in front of us? Go and investigate who he is.” Agni said “Let it be.”**

### Verse 4

*tad abhyadravat*  
*tam abhyavadat ko’ sīti*  
*Agnir va aham asmīty abravīj jātavedā va aham asmīti*

**Перевод:**

**Agni went towards that yakṣa. The yakṣa said to Agni: “Who are you?” Agni answered “I am called Agni, and am known as Jātavedas.”**

### Verse 5

*Tasmiṁs tvayi kim vīryam iti apīdam*  
*sarvam daheyam yad idaṁ prthivyām iti*

**Перевод:**

**Viṣṇu said “What power is in such a famous person as you?” Agni answered, “I can burn anything on the earth.”**

### Verse 6

*tasmai tṛṇam nidadav etad daheti*  
*tad upapreyāya sarva-javena, ta na śaśaka dagdhum*  
*sa tat eva nivavṛte*  
*naitad asśkam vijñātum yad etad yakṣam iti*

**Перевод:**

**Placing a blade of grass before Agni, the yakṣa said, “Please burn it.” With great speed Agni approached the grass. But even using his greatest strength, he could not burn it. Then Agni left the yakṣa and, returning to the devatās said, “I could not understand who that yakṣa was.”**

### Verse 7

*atah vāyum abruvan vāyav etad vijānīhi*  
*kim etad yaksma iti tatheti*

**Перевод:**

**Then the devatās said to Vāyu, “O Vāyu, go and see you that worshipable person is.”**

**Vayu said, “So be it.”**

### Verse 8

*tad abhyadravat tam abhyavadat ko’sīti*  
*Vāyur va aham asmīty abravin mātariśvā va aham asmīti*

**Перевод:**

**Vayu approached the yakṣa. The yakṣa said to Vāyu, “Who are you?”**

**Vāyu said, “I am Vāyu. I am Mātariśvā--because I can travel swiftly in space.”**

### Verse 9

*tasmins tvayi kim vīryam iti?*  
*apīdaṁ sarvam ādadīyam yad idam pṛthvyām iti*

**Перевод:**

**Viṣṇu said, “What is the power in such a famous person as you?”**

**Vāyu said, “I can carry away anything on this earth.”**

### Verse 10

*tasmai tṛṇam nidadhav etad ādatsveti*  
*tad upapreyāya sarva-javena tan na śaśaka dātum*  
*sa tata eva nivavṛte*  
*naitad aśakam vijñātum yad etad yakṣam iti*

**Перевод:**

**Viṣṇu placed a blade of grass in front of Vāyu and said “Pick up this piece of grass.” Vāyu enthusiastically approached the grass and with all his strength could not pick up the grass. He returned to the devatas and said, “I cannot understand who this worshipable person is.”**

### Verse 11

*āthendram abruvan*  
*maghavan etad vijāṇīhi kim etad yakṣam iti*  
*tatheti tad abhyadravat*  
*tasmād tirodadhe*

**Перевод:**

**The devatās said to Indra, “O Maghavan, Indra, please find out who that worshipable person is.”**

**Indra answered, “So be it.”**

**He approached the yakṣa, but the yakṣa disappeared from that place.**

### Verse 12

*sa tasmin evākāśe striyam ajagāma*  
*bahu-śobhamāṇām umām haimavatīm*  
*tāṁ hovaca kim etad yakṣam iti*

**Перевод:**

**Indra, approached a woman, the lustrous Umā, the daughter of Himālaya, who had been established in the sky by the yakṣa to teach, and said to her, “Who is that worshipable person?”**

## Part 4

### Verse 1

*sā brahmeti hovāca*  
*brahmaṇo va etad vijaye mahīyadhvam iti*  
*tato haiva vidāṁcakāra brahmeti*

**Перевод:**

**Umā said to Indra, “That Yakṣa is Brahman. In the war between the devatās and asuras, Brahman gave you victory but you became proud, thinking “It is our victory.” By these words of Umā, Indra could understand that the yakṣa was Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Being asked by Indra, Uma said that the yakṣa is Brahman alone. “Ha” indicates certainty. Brahman alone gave victory by entering into you, but you became proud. “Vai” indicates certainty. Being instructed by Uma, what did Indra do? Then (tato), from the instruction of Umā, he understood that the Yakṣa was Brahman, and that their victory was due to Brahman entering into them.

### Verse 2

*tasmad vā ete devā atitaram ivānyān devān*  
*yad agnir vāyur indras te hy enan nediṣṭham*  
*paspṛsus te hy enat prathamo vidāṁ cakāra brahmeti*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, these devatās are considered superior to others because they (Agni, Vāyu and Indra) understood Brahman who was close by. They first understood that the yakṣa was Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Thus (tasmād vai) having seen Brahman first, these devatās—Agni, Vāyu and Indra --are superior to other devas. Why are they superior? They contacted Brahman (enat), who was the nearest (nediṣṭham). But it is to be understood that Agni and Vāyu only saw the Brahman in the form of the Yaksa whereas Indra understood him as Brahman. Ā further explanation is given: these were the first to understand Brahman.

### Verse 3

*tasmād vā indro’ titaram ivānyān devān*  
*sa hy enan nediṣṭham pasparśa*  
*sa hy enat prathamo vidāṁ cakāra brahmeti*

**Перевод:**

**Indra is considered superior to all other devatās. He contacted the yakṣa who was situated near. He was first to understand that the yakṣa was Brahman.**

**This verse shows how Indra first heard teachings from Umā. Indra then taught Agni and Vāyu and others. This is how they attained knowledge.**

### Verse 4

*tasyaiṣa ādeśo yad etad vidyuto vyadyutad ā*  
*itī nyamīmiṣad ā ity adhidaivatam*

**Перевод:**

**The nature of Brahman is conveyed through examples. Brahman flashed lightning for a moment. And Brahman disappeared like the blinking of an eye.**

**Комментарий:**

Now there is a teaching of the two forms of worship of Brahman: adhidaivata and adhyātmā.

Here is an instruction about Brahman in glowing form. Lightning is in plural form to indicate many types of bright things or to give a hint of its brightness. Literally the sentence means “Brahman flashed (vyadyutat) bright forms such as lightning (vidyutaḥ) all around (ā). Brahman disappeared (nyamīmisat) completely (ā) like the closing of the eye.’”

Or, as Brahman was like a flash of lightning when he appeared suddenly before the devatas, Brahman is described as glowing brightly. Brahman shone like lightening (vidyutaḥ). The particle “ā” can also mean “ like.” As the lightning pierces the thick darkness and reveals everything, Brahman appeared everywhere before the devatās, like lightning.

Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad says: yathā sakṛd vidyut tam.

When Indra approached, the Lord disappeared, like the blinking of the eye. The two syllables “itīt” are an archaic usage with no meaning. Because Brahman showed himself to the devatās in this way, it is called adhidaivata, concerning (adhi) the devatās’ darśana (daivata).

### Verse 5

*Athadhy;atmam Yad etad gacchatīva ca*  
*mano’nena caitad upasmaraty abhīkṣnam saṅkalpaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Now Brahman is described in relation to the mind. The mind goes to Brahman promptly. The mind, equipped with great determination, constantly contemplates Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

*Atha* indicates another example. This involved an example in our body. Therefore it is called adhyātmam. The mind (manas) inspired by Brahman (anena), goes towards Brahman (etad), but its comprehension is not complete. By the mercy of Brahman (anena), Hari in the form of Aniruddha, the mind, can remember objects. The mind is described using the words abhīkṣṇam saṅkalpaḥ. Saṅkalpa means to strongly desire objects. The mind constantly (abhīkṣṇam) displays strong desire towards objects. This instruction highlights Brahman as Aniruddha, or Viṣṇu.

### Verse 6

*tad dha tadvanam nama tadvanam ity upāsitavyam*  
*sa ya etad evaṁ vedābhi hainaṁ sarvāṇi bhūtāni samvāñcanti*

**Перевод:**

**Brahman is well known as tadvana,Viṣṇu, because his dwelling within and is worshippable. All entities respect the person who knows the Lord as tadvana.**

**Комментарий:**

Tad refer to Brahman. Ha means well known. Tad in tadvanam means all pervading (tan--spread out) and vanam means worshipable. The result of worship of tadvana is described. He who, being qualified knows Brahman as tadvana, is respected by all living entities.

### Verse 7

*Upaniṣadam bho bruhīty ukta ta upaniṣad*  
*brāhmīm vava ta upaniṣadam abrumeti*

**Перевод:**

**O teacher, teach me the Upaniṣad.**

**The teacher said: I have spoken Upaniṣad.**

**Комментарий:**

Hearing this, the student again asks the ācārya to speak the Upaniṣad. The student said, “O ācārya, please speak the Upaniṣad explaining the means and the source.“ Thinking in his heart about the means and source of knowledge after hearing the student ask about the essence of the Upaniṣad, the ācārya replied. To you (te) I have spoken without doubt (vāva) the essence of the Upaniṣad (Brahman) with such statements at the beginning of the fourth chapter “brahmeti hovāca….”

### Verse 8

*tasyai tapo damaḥ karmeti pratiṣṭhā*  
*vedāḥ sarvāṅgāṇi satyam āyatanam*

**Перевод:**

**The means of knowledge is austerity, sense control, and action. The Vedas, their supplements and truth are the source of knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

Tasyai means tasyah “of knowledge.” The foundation of knowledge is austerity, sense control and action. Tapa refers to vows such as the kṛcchra and candrāyāṇa vows , or thorough study of scriptures. Dama refers to sense control. Karma means performance of actions according to varṇa and āśrama. Pratiṣṭha means cause of steadiness, the means. Knowledge is founded on austerity etc. Vedāḥ refers to the Vedas starting with Ṛg and to works agreeing with them. Sarvāṅgāṇi refers to śikṣā, kalpa, vyākaraṇa, nirukta, chandas and jyotis. Satya refers to words which do not give pain. Āyatanam is the source, cause of origin of knowledge.

### Verse 9

*yo vā etām evam vedāpahatya pāpmanam anante*  
*svarge loke jyeye pratitiṣṭhati pratitiṣṭhati*  
*Om harih āpyayantv ityadi santipathah*

**Перевод:**

**He who takes shelter of this Upaniṣad becomes freed of all sin and becomes firmly situated in the infinite, blissful, effulgent, supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The fruits of knowing the Upaniṣad are here stated. The qualified person (yaḥ) who knows the Upaniṣad (etām) is purified of all sins, and becomes firmly situated in the supreme (jyeye) most blissful (svarge) place free from all limitation (anante ) of time and space and glowing (loke). Repetition of the last phrase is for emphasis.
