# Saṅgīta Mādhava \[BS\]

## Chapter 1 - Festival in Vṛndāvana

## Introduction

*sadānande vṛndāvana-niviḍa-kuñje nava-taḍid*  
*ghana-jyotiḥ-puñjaṁ kim api kala-guñjaṁ madhukare \|*  
*navonmīlat kaiśoraka-lalita-līlā-rasa-mayaṁ \|*  
*sadā tan me jīyān madhu-madhuraṁ dhāma-yugalaṁ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the two forms, filled with the rasa of youthful, artful pastimes, sweeter than the sweetest, effulgent as a dark cloud and fresh lightning in a dense, ever blissful bower of Vṛndāvana, humming with bees, remain eternally victorious.**

*nava-campaka-gaura-kāntibhiḥ kṛta-vṛndāvana-rūpa-hematāṁ \|*  
*bhaja kām api viśva-mohinīṁ madhura-prema-rasādhidevatāṁ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Worship the presiding deity of mādhurya-rasa, who turns Vṛndāvana golden with her complexion, golden as a fresh campaka flower and who enchants the universe.**

*mahā-prema-rasa-jyotir-apārāvāra-vāridheḥ \|*  
*madhye mohana-kaiśora-sāraṁ rādhābhidhaṁ numaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Rādhā, the essence of charming kaiśora, in the brilliant, shoreless ocean of the rasa of great prema.**

*parama-rati-rasāmbho rāśi-kallola-mālā-*  
*taralita-sukumāraḥ śyāma-sammohanāṅgaḥ \|*  
*vraja-lalita-kiśorī-lampaṭaḥ ko ’pi jīyāt pravada-*  
*madana-līlā-mugdha-mugdhaḥ kiśoraḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the ocean of the highest rasa of love, the youthful boy, endowed with bewitching black limbs, having a host of unsurpassable waves of the highest rasa, whose attractive beauty announces pastimes of love, and who craves the gentle, Vraja girl quivering with passion, remain victorious.**

*uttaraṅga-mahānaṅga-rasābdhi-taralaṁ mahaḥ \|*  
*pulakāñcita-sarvāṅgaṁ bhaja rādhāṅga-bhūṣaṇaṁ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Worship he who is the ornament of Rādhā’s limbs, he whose hairs stand on end, he who is glorious, while trembling in the ocean of ever-increasing waves of intense mādhurya-rasa.**

*parama-rasa-vibhūtyair daivatasyāvatāraṁ*  
*jagati kṛta-rahasyaṁ prema-bhakti-pracāraṁ \|*  
*lavaṇa-jaladhi-kūle kalpitānalpa-līlaṁ sphurad-*  
*aruṇa-dukūlaṁ dhāma-gauraṁ smarāmi\|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I remember the golden form, the avatāra endowed with powers of the highest rasa, who wore red cloth, performed unlimited pastimes on the shore of the salt ocean, and who spread confidential prema-bhakti to the world.**

*nityonmadānanta-rasaikakandaṁ kandarpa-līlādbhūta-keli-vṛndam \|*  
*śrī-rādhikā-mādhavayor didṛkṣus tuṣṭāva vṛndāvanam eva kācit \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One gopī, desiring to see Rādha and Mādhava with their astonishing play in amorous pastimes, the sole root of unlimited, passionate, eternal rasa, praised Vṛndāvana.**

## First song

*vasanta rāgeṇa gīyate yathā*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in vasanta-rāga.**

*adbhuta-surabhi-samaya-sahajodaya- madhura-latā-taru-jālaṁ \|*  
*nava-makaranda-mahādbhuta-parimala-matta-vicalad-ali-mālaṁ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndāvana, a mass of sweet creepers and trees filled with naturally manifesting astonishing spring, is covered with swarms of agitated bees intoxicated with the most astonishing fragrance of fresh honey.**

*vande vṛndāvana-vipinam amandam \|*  
*prema-mahā-rasa-vega-vijṛmbhita-madana-mahotsava-kandaṁ\|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the bright forest, Vṛndāvana, a great festival of love manifesting the most impetuous rasa of prema.**

*vikaśad-aśoka-bakula-kula-campaka-mādhavikābhir anūnaṁ \|*  
*saha nija-vallabhayā vraja-nāgara-lūna-vicitra-visūnam \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndāvana is abundant with various colourful blossom—aśoka, bakula, kula, campaka and mādhavī, picked by Kṛṣṇa and Rādhā.**

*Lalita-kalinda-sutā-laharī-kṛta-mṛdumṛdu-śīkara-varṣaṁ \|*  
*tumula-rati-śramitālasa-tanu-vara-rasika-mithuna-kṛta-harṣaṁ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndāvana is filled with soft and cool showers made by the graceful waves of the Yamunā and which create joy for the enjoying couple whose bodies are fatigued by tumultuous love.**

*adbhuta-rasa-sarasi-lasad-utpala-mukulita-kanaka-sarojaṁ \|*  
*prāṇa-samā kuca-locana-saṁsmṛti-kṛti-hari-tīvra-manojam \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndāvana has blossoming golden lotuses and water lilies gleaming in lake of astonishing rasa, which excite intense love in Kṛṣṇa on remembering Rādhā’s breasts and eyes, which are dear to his life.**

*āstṛt-kusuma-ghaṭita-madhu-bhājana-mañjula-kuñja-kuṭīraṁ \|*  
*rādhā-mādhava-nava-rati-līlā-gāna-mudākula-kīram \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndāvana has cottages within attractive bowers, sheltering honey made by spreading flowers. It has jubilant parrots constantly singing about the fresh love pastimes of Rādhā and Mādhava.**

*kusumita-phalita-kalpa-latāvṛta-surataru-kṛta-parabhāgaṁ\|*  
*vividha-maṇī vṛta-bhūtala-nipatita-nava-karpūra-parāgaṁ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**It is prosperous with flowering and fruiting desire creepers and desire trees. Camphor-like pollen falls from the flowers on the earth covered with a variety of jewels.**

*Śikhi-kula-naṭana-mṛgī-cakitekṣaṇa-pika-pañcama-kṛta-śobhaṁ \|*  
*prema-sudhāmbudhi-dolita-khaga-paśu-gaṇa-saṅga-mahā-muṇi-lobham \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛndāvana is beautiful with dancing peacocks, glancing deer, and cuckoos singing the fifth note. Great sages desire to associate with those birds and animals that are tossed in oceans of sweet prema.**

*Nīla-tamāla-tāla-vanāntara-nilayana-kautuki-piñcha-vataṁśaṁ\|*  
*parimala-hara-mṛdu-malayānila-bhara-kṛta-rādhā-patha-śaṁsam \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vṛndāvana, passionate Kṛṣṇa, wearing a playful peacock feather ornament, hides in a forest at the base of a dark tamāla tree. The forest announces the path of Rādhā, and is laden with the captivating, fragrant Malaya breeze.**

*lalita-kadamba-tale dhṛta-bhaṅgima-mohana-vādita-vaṁśaṁ \|*  
*niravadhi-nijasukha-sāra-rasonmāda-hari-kṛta-parama-praśaṁsam \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There he plays his flute with his figure bent in three places, at the base of a graceful kadamba tree. This most praiseworthy sound from Kṛṣṇa makes the animals insane with the highest happiness.**

*priya-rasa-matta-sarasvati-varṇita-vṛndāvana-mahimānaṁ \|*  
*pibata budhāḥ śravaṇena sudhā-rasa-sāra-mudākara-gānaṁ \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O intelligent people, drink through the ears the joyful song, the essence of sweet rasa, which glorifies Vṛndāvana, described by Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī, intoxicated with mādhurya-rasa.**

*sāndra-prema-rasāmṛtaika-laharī-syandīni mandīkṛta*  
*brahmānanda-samādhi-sam-muni-manohāryaika-keli-sthalī \|*  
*īśenāpy avitarkya-divya-mahimānyā bibrhatī śreyase*  
*bhūyān naḥ paśu-pakṣi-bhūruha-latā vṛndāni vṛndāṭavī \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the forest of Vṛndāvana, filled with many animals, birds, trees and creepers, the place of pastimes decorated with flowing waves of sweet, condensed rasa of prema, the place most attractive to the best sages who experience samādhi in expansive Brahman, the forest possessing an inconceivable, attractive great wealth, manifest auspiciousness for us.**

*sahajādbhuta-sat-prabhāvatas tava vṛndāvana-kevalaṁ kadā \|*  
*tvayi nitya-vihari-tan-mahā-rasika-dvandam ahaṁ vilokaye \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Within your boundaries, when will I gaze upon your couple endowed with great rasa constantly playing within your boundaries, who are dedicated only to Vṛndāvana because of your natural, astonishing, spiritual influence?**

*atha rasamaya rādhā-kṛṣṇadāsyaika-lubdha*  
*niravadhi mṛgayantī kuñja-gehāvalīṣu \|*  
*tad atimadhura-līlānandi-rādhā-sakhīnām*  
*idam akalayad ārāt kīrtanaṁ sā mṛgākṣī \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**A doe-eyed girl longing to serve them searched constantly the many bower houses to find attractive Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. In the distance she heard the singing of Rādhā’s sakhīs who were blissful from the sweet pastimes.**

## Second song

*tathāhi maṅgala-gurjari-rāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in maṅgala-gurjari-raga.**

*praṇata-sakala-sukha-dāyaka vraja-nāyaka he vallabha-rāja-kumāra \|*  
*sphuṭa-sarasī-ruha-locana bhaya-mocana he pālita-nija-parivāra \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, giving all happiness to the surrendered! O hero of Vraja! O prince of the cowherds! O Kṛṣṇa with blossoming lotus eyes! O giver of freedom from fear! O protector of your family!**

*jaya jaya prāṇa-sakhe \|\|dhru\|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Dear friend, remain victorious!**

*vraja-tarunī-nava-nāgara rasa-sāgara he raticita-mahā-rati-raṅga \|*  
*rasika yuvati-parihāsaka kṛta-rāsaka he lalitānaṅga-taraṅga \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O young hero of the young women of Vraja! O ocean of rasa! O performer of pastimes of great love! O enjoyer of rasa! O joy who jokes with the young women! O performer of the rāsa dance! O wave of charming love!**

*maṇi-maya-veṇu-lasan-mukha*  
*nata-samukha he mṛdu-mṛdu-hāsa-vilāsa \|*  
*kula-vanitā-vrata-bhañjana ripu-gañjana*  
*he nava-rati-keli-vilāsa \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, whose face shines with jewelled flute! O Kṛṣna who approaches the girls while they are resting! O Kṛṣṇa with soft, playful smile! O Kṛṣṇa who breaks the vows of cultured women! O destroyer of enemies! O Kṛṣṇa, performing pastimes of fresh love.**

*madhura-madhura-rasa-nūtana hata-pūtana he nava-ghana-nīla-śarīra \|*  
*tapana-sutātaṭa sannaṭa rati-lampaṭa he dhṛta vara-maṇi-gaṇa-hāri\|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa endowed with sweetest fresh rasa! O killer of Pūtanā! O Kṛṣṇa, endowed with a body dark as a fresh cloud! O passionate boy dancing on the bank of the Yamunā! O wearer of necklace made of the best gems!**

*sphurad-aruṇādhara-pallava vraja-vallabha he rādhā-mānasa-haṁsa\|*  
*śrīla sarasvati-gītakaṁ hari-bhāva-daṁ maṅgalam iha vidadhātu \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa with shining red lips! O cowherd boy of Vraja! O swan in Rādhā’s mind! Sarasvatī’s song bestows auspicious love for Kṛṣṇa.**

*rādhāyāḥ stana-hema-kumbha-yugale dattvā karaṁ pallavā-*  
*bhāsaṁ komalam ūru-cāru-kadalīkāṇḍaṁ ca kṛvoditaṁ\|*  
*nirmāya pratirodhi-hasta-valaya-dhvānañca tūryya-svanaṁ*  
*govindaḥ kṛta-maṅgalo rati-raṇārambhe samujjṛmbhate \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When he placed his soft bud-like hands on Rādhā’s golden breasts and attractive banana-stem thighs, the bracelets on her hand which opposed him jingled like drums. Govinda began the auspicious invocation at the beginning of the battle of love.**

*madhuraṁ madhuraṁ madhudviṣam tad idaṁ gītam atīva-maṅgalaṁ\|*  
*śravaṇāñjalibhir nipīya sā smṛta-govinda-padedam ujjagau\|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing this most sweet, auspicious song about Kṛṣṇa, drinking it in handfuls, she sang this song, while remembering Govinda’s feet.**

## Third song

*tathāhi vasanta-rāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**This song is in vasanta-rāga.**

*mada-śikhi-piñcha-mukuṭa-parilāñchita-kuñcita-kaca nikurambe \|*  
*mukharita-veṇuka-satata-parāvadhi nava-nava-keli-kadambe \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has a mass of curly hair with crown and swinging peacock feather. In his fresh pastimes of love, he constantly conquers with the sound of his flute.**

*vasatu mano mama madana-gopāle \|*  
*nava-rati-keli-vilāsa-parāvadhi-rādhā-surata-rasāle \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May my mind dwell upon my Madana-gopāla, the sweet lover of Rādhā, who is the culmination of pastimes of fresh love. (chorus)**

*kalita-kalinda-sutā-pulinojvala-kalpa-mahīruha-mūle \|*  
*kiṅkiṇī-kala-rava-rañjita-kaṭi-taṭa-komala-pīta-dukūle \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He stands at the base of a brilliant desire tree on the bank of the Yamunā. He wears soft yellow cloth around his waist, which jingles with bells.**

*muralī-manohara-madhuratarādhara-ghana-ruci-caura-kiśore \|*  
*śrī-vṛṣabhānu-kumārī hari-mohana-ruci-śrī-mukha-candra-cakore \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has a flute on his attractive sweet lips and has a complexion that defeats a cloud. His face attracts Rādhā just as the moon attracts the cakora bird.**

*guñjāhāraka-makara-maṇikuṇḍala-sukaṅkaṇa-nūpura-śobhe \|*  
*mṛdura-madhura-smita-cāru-vilokana-rasika-vadhū-kṛta-lobhe \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He is glorious with a guñja necklace, jewelled makara earrings, bracelets and ankle bells. He is greedy for the rasika-gopīs who smile sweetly with attractive glances.**

*matta-madhuvrata-guñjita-rañjita-gala-dolita-vanamāle\|*  
*gandhodvardhita-suvalita-sundara-pulakita-bāhu-viśāle\|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On his throat he wears a swinging forest garland covered with intoxicated, humming bees. He has broad, rounded, highly perfumed arms, with hairs standing on end.**

*ujjvala-ratna sulalitālika-sa-kanaka-mauktika-nāse\|*  
*śārada-koṭi-sudhā-kiraṇojjvala-śrī-mukha-kamala-vilāse \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On his charming forehead is a jewel and on his nose a pearl embedded in gold. His lotus-like face shines with millions of rays of the autumn moon.**

*grīvā-kaṭi-pada-bhaṅgi-manohara-nava-sukumāra-śarīre \|*  
*vṛndāvana-nava-kuñja-gṛhāntara-rati-raṇa-raṅga-sudhīre \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His youthful body bends attractively at the neck, waist and ankles. He is diligent at playful amorous battles in the fresh bowers of Vṛndāvana.**

*parimala-sāra-sakeśara-candana-carcita-tarala-sad-aṅge \|*  
*paramānanda-rasaika-ghanākṛti-pravahad-anaṅga-taraṅge \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His pure, trembling limbs are anointed with the excellent fragrance of saffron and sandalwood. He is a cloud of the highest bliss endowed with waves of love.**

*pada-nakha-candra-maṇi-cchavi-lajjita-manasija-koṭī-samāje \|*  
*adbhuta-keli-vilāsa-viśārada-vraja-pura-nava-yuvarāje\|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The beauty of his moon-stone toe nails shames millions of Cupids. He is the young prince of Vraja, skilful at astonishing pastimes.**

*sarasa-sarasvati-varṇita-mādhava-rūpa-sudhā-rasa-sāre\|*  
*ramayata sādhu-budhā nija-hṛdayaṁ bhramatha mudhā kim asāre\|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O people of pure intelligence! Let your hearts enjoy the essence of sweet rasa in the form of Mādhava described by impassioned Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī. Why do you wander uselessly in the temporary world?**

*tathā tatraiva \|*  
*kuvalaya-dala-nīlaḥ koṭi-kandarpa-līlaḥ*  
*kanaka-ruci-dukūlaḥ keki-piñcāvacūlaḥ \|*  
*mama hṛdi kula-bālā-nīvi-viśraṁsi-vaṁśī-*  
*dhvanir udayatu rādhā padminī rāja-haṁsaḥ \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has a complexion like a blue lotus petal. He performs pastimes of a million Cupids. He wears golden cloth. He wears a peacock feather. May the swan that sports in the lotus of Rādhā, whose flute sound loosens the belt of the gopīs, appear in my heart.**

*atyuttuṅga-payodharādri-yugalaṁ vibhrat-karābhyāṁ nijaṁ*  
*trātā gokulam ātma-yoni-viṣayāsārair hato mādhavaḥ \|*  
*rādhām ity abhisāriṇīṁ caturayā sakhyā bhayotkampitāṁ*  
*labdvhaikānta-vanāntare rati-kalāhṛṣṭo hariḥ pātu vaḥ\|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May Kṛṣṇa, who met Rādhā, trembling in fear, through a clever sakhī, who was joyful with arts of love in a lonely forest, who protected himself and Gokula with his two hands which held her raised, mountainous breasts when he was attacked by arrows aimed at him, protect you.**

*iti śrī-saṅgīta-mādhave vṛndāvanotsave nāma prathama sargaḥ*

## Chapter 2 - Festival of Rādhā and Mādhava

## Introduction

*atha sā vraja-bhīrur agataḥ parama-prema-rasāvaśākṛtiḥ\|*  
*samudīkṣya nijeśvarīṁ sakhīṁ pada-mūle tv apatat praharṣataḥ \|\|1\|\|*  
*dūre tava prāṇa-dhanaṁ kiśora-dvandvaṁ mudā krīḍati kutra mohanaṁ\|*  
*itthaṁ samutkaṇṭhitayā tayoktes tās sneha-pūrṇā kathayām babhūva \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The timid gopī, overcome with the highest prema, came, and seeing the friends of Rādhā, fell at their feet out of great joy. When in great longing she asked “Where does your enchanting, youthful couple, the treasure of your life, perform pastimes?” A friend, full of affection, said to her:**

## First song

*yathā gurjari rāgeṇa gīyate\|*

**Перевод:**

**She sang this song in gurjari-rāga.**

*nīla-jalada-rucira-kalevara-virājita campaka-dāma\|*  
*vimala-nijāyudha-parivṛta-nava-hari-maṇi-maṇḍana-dhāma \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The couple is an attractive dark cloud with a campaka garland, a form decorated with a new sapphire surrounded by a spotless flash of lightning (the cloud’s weapon).**

*tīre tapana-duhitur avirāmaṁ khelati mithunam abhirāmaṁ\|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The delightful couple plays constantly on the bank of the Yamunā. (chorus)**

*unmada-madana-mahā-vihvala-mohana-divya-kiśoraṁ\|*  
*adbhuta-rūpa-vilāsa-camatkṛti-siddha-hṛdaya-dhṛti-cauraṁ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The enchanting, attractive, youthful couple, crazed with love, is overwhelmed with great rasa. Their astonishing forms and pastimes destroy the steadiness of the mind.**

*vṛndāvana-nava-kuñja-gṛhodara-durjaya-rati-raṇa-lolaṁ \|*  
*snigdha-tilaka-parikhaṇḍita-maṇi-śara-śrama-jala-kalita-kapolaṁ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They are agitated by the difficult-to-win love battles within the fresh bowers of Vṛndāvana. Their resplendent tilāka is smeared and drops of perspiration like pearl necklaces flow from their cheeks.**

*mitha upadhāya lasat-kadalī-nibha-komalam urum udāraṁ \|*  
*svapiti kadāpi ca gumphati vanajaiḥ kam api manohara-hāraṁ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Placing their delicate, broad thighs, shining like banana stems, upon each other, they sleep, and sometimes string an attractive necklace out of blue lotuses.**

*cumbati gupta-kathā-cchalataḥ kacid avalabdha-śrutimūlaṁ \|*  
*kvacana parasparam atiśaya-kṛta-ruci-paridhāpayati dukūlaṁ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes on the pretext of talking, coming close to the other’s ear, they kiss. Sometimes they dress each other attractively in fine cloth.**

*kvāpi mithaḥ parilumpati maṇḍati paśyati nija-pratibimbaṁ \|*  
*haṁsa-mithunam anuyāti karoti ca naṭa-śikhi-yugma-viḍambaṁ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes they remove each other’s ornaments, decorate each other and look at their reflections. Sometimes they imitate the gait of swans and the dancing of peacocks.**

*kvāpi paraspara-veṇī-vibhūṣaṇa-racitoddāma-vilāsaṁ \|*  
*gāyati nṛtyati hasati vicalati pratipada-madana-vikāśaṁ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes they wildly braid and ornament each other’s hair. They sing, dance, laugh and walk about, revealing constant love.**

*kvacana gatāmbara-vilulita-kuntala-truṭita-mauktika-hāram \|*

## Chapter 3 - Festival of service to Rādhā

**See the intoxicated black bee with the graceful lotus trembling in the wind. See Kṛṣṇa entreating Rādhā in various ways, while she trembles in fear at a new meeting.**

**Перевод:**

**See the play of astonishing new Cupid with beauty of millions of the highest ratis. See the manifestation of autumn moon with the joy of the smiling, joyful lotus.**

*vara-kariṇī-mada-vihavalayā nava-rati-rasa-matta-karīndraṁ \|*

**See the essence of the ocean of the highest happiness with the sweet cloud of the rasa of mādhurya-prema. See the pastime of the new sapphire personified, with an astonishing golden earring.**

*nava-tāruṇya-niveśa-madhurayā mohana-divya-kiśoraṁ \|*

## Second song

*tathāhi rāmakeli rāgena gīyate\|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in rāma-keli-rāga.**

*śikṣaya mām anupama-nija-kalpita-saṁgītaka-bahu-bhaṅgīṁ \|*  
*harim upagāyaya yathā bhavatīm aham īkṣe ghana-pulakāṅgīṁ \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Teach me many incomparable devices in singing that you have discovered. Make me sing about Kṛṣṇa so that I can see your hairs standing on end.**

*vande bhavatīm atula-rasa-rāśiṁ\|*  
*vṛndāraṇya-nikuñja-vilāsinīṁ kuru māṁ nija-pada-dāsīm \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to you, playing in the bowers of Vṛndāvana, a mass of incomparable rasa. Make me your servant. (chorus)**

*kāraya nija-nāgara-caraṇa-dvaya-paricaraṇaṁ sukha-sāraṁ \|*  
*paricāraya hariṇākṣi navaṁ navam abhinava-kuñjam udāraṁ\|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Please let me serve Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, the essence of bliss. O doeeyed Rādhā! Please let me serve the fresh, broad bower.**

*viracita-kusuma-śayanam anukāraya madhura-mukhena nideśaṁ \|*  
*samvāhaya lalitāṅgi mayā nija-padam avabandhaya keśaṁ\|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Let me make a flower bed, imitating this one, receiving the order through your sweet mouth. O tender limbed Rādhā! Let me massage your feet. Let me tie your hair.**

*nava-tāmbūla-sucarvitam abhimatam ānana-candra-nigīrṇaṁ \|*  
*sṛkva-galitam ahaṁ spṛhaye tava kṛpayā kim api vitīrṇaṁ \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I desire fresh betel nut chewed at the side of your mouth, spat from your moonlike mouth, with your permission, by your mercy. Will it be granted to me?**

*tava priya-śyāma-kiśora-rasotsavam aniśam anantam apāraṁ \|*  
*anubhavitāsmi bhavat-pada-paṅkaja-kiṅkarikai rasa-sāraṁ \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I will experience your constant, infinite, boundless festival of rasa with dear Kṛṣṇa, the essence of rasa along with the servants at your lotus feet.**

*mama kalayātma-caraṇa-mahimādita-bahu-caturāyita-rītiṁ \|*  
*melāyitāsmi kadā niśi vā hariṇā bhavatīṁ gatabhītiṁ \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Make me skilful in many ways by the power of your feet. When will I arrange for you, filled with fear, to meet Kṛṣṇa in the night?**

*ayi nava-rasika-yuvati-kula-maṇḍala-pada-nakha-candra-vilāse \|*  
*ārttajane mayi nahi vimukhī bhava nijapada-dāsya-dhṛtāśe \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Do not be indifferent to me, suffering but hopeful of being a servant at your feet, as I sport near the moon-like toenails of the group of young girls endowed with fresh rasa.**

*iti vṛṣabhānu-sutā-caraṇāmbuja-nipatita-vara-tanu-gītaṁ \|*  
*tad-rasa-lubdha-sarasvati-varṇitam atisukha-daṁ śruti-pītaṁ \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The song of the gopī who fell at the lotus feet of Rādhā, as described by Prabodhānanda, greedy for rasa, gives great happiness on being heard.**

*tathāhi ślokaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**There is a verse in this regard.**

*atirasa-mada-vṛndāraṇya-candreṇa śaśvat*  
*pulakita-bhuja-daṇḍenāṅkam āropyamāṇe\|*  
*ayi navakumāra-sphāra-lāvaṇya-mūrte*  
*rasa-mayi mayi rādhe snigdha-dṛṣṭiṁ vidhehi \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are placed by intoxicated Kṛṣṇa on his lap with hair standing on end on his arms. O Rādhā, form of beauty blossoming with new youth! Give me, filled with love, an affection glance.**

*atha sahaja-vivṛddha-sneha-bāṣpākulākṣyā*  
*lalita-lalita-mūrtyā rādhayāliṅgitāṅgī \|*  
*nija-ramaṇa-padābjaṁ vandayantī tadaiva*  
*praṇaya-kala-padaṁ sā prāha govinda-candraṁ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The gopī, embraced by Rādhā, endowed with most charming form, who wept out of great natural affection, offering respects to Rādhā’s delightful lotus feet, she spoke to Kṛṣṇa a song filled with affection.**

## Third song

*saurāṣṭrī-pāhārā-rāgeṇa gīyate*

**Перевод:**

**It is sung in saurāṣtṛī-pāhārā-rāga.**

*vṛndāraṇya-purandara-sundara kunda-kali-dvija-vṛnda \|*  
*manda-hasita bhūvanaika-manohara vadana-vikasad-aravinda \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa the beautiful king of Vṛndāvana, who is endowed with jasmine bud teeth! Kṛṣṇa, who is attractive to the whole world by his mild smile! O Kṛṣṇa with blossoming lotus face!**

*mādhava rasa-maya-paramānanda \|*  
*nija-dayaitā-pada-dāsya-rase mām abhiṣecaya sukha-kanda\|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Mādhava! O supreme bliss filled with rasa! O root of happiness! Sprinkle me with the rasa of servitude to the feet of your lover. (Chorus)**

*rādhā-vadana-saroruha-sambhūta-sīdhu-rasonmada-bhṛṅga\|*  
*pratipadam ucchalitātirasārṇava-samudita-keli-taraṅga \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O bee intoxicated with sweet rasa arising from Rādhā’s lotus face! O Kṛṣṇa, who plays in the waves in the increasing ocean of rasa at every moment!**

*rādhā-pīna-payodhara-girivara-yugma-niyantrita-citta\|*  
*pratimṛdu-vākya-bharodita-durdhara-madana-mahā-mada-matta \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa whose heart is controlled by the full mountainous breasts of Rādhā! O Kṛṣṇa intensely intoxicated with uncontrollable love arising from many soft words.**

*rādhā-keli-kuraṅga tad-ujjvala-guṇa-jālaka-kṛta-bandha \|*  
*indirayāpi sudurlabha-lobhana-pada-makaranda-sugandha \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O pet deer of Rādhā, caught in the net of her brilliant qualities! O Kṛṣṇa, fragrant with the honey from Rādhā’s feet, rarely attained by even Lakṣmī!**

*rādhā-prāṇa-sakhī-gaṇa-sauhṛda-mudita-manohara-veśa \|*  
*tan-mukha-mohana-candra-vilokana-kautuka-nikṛta-nimeṣa\|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa bewildered with bliss through friendship with Rādhā’s prāṇa-sakhīs. O Kṛsṇa, who does not blink because of the joy on seeing the enchanting moon of her face.**

*rādhā-jīvana-bhūṣaṇa-vaibhava-tanu-dhana-bāndhava-mitra \|*  
*niravadhi-rati-raṇa-khelana-rañjita-bahuvidha-cihna-sucitra \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, the life, ornament, power, body, wealth, relative and friend of Rādhā! O Kṛṣṇa, marked in many ways by playing in the constant battle of love!**

*rādhā-māna-garala-parikhaṇḍana-veṇu-ravāmṛta-dhanya \|*  
*rādhā-mahati-lalita-śruti-prema-vikuṇṭhita-kaṇṭha \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, fortunate with sweet music from your flute which counteracts the poison of Rādhā’s anger. O Kṛṣṇa, whose throat becomes choked with prema from the sweet note of Rādhā’s vīṇā!**

*ayi kutukena sarasvati-viracita-gītam idaṁ budha-vṛnda\|*  
*śruti-casakena nipīya mahā-sukham iha nu cirād anuvinda \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O intelligent people! Drink with the cups of your ears this song made in joy by Prabodhānanda and experience great happiness quickly.**

*jaya jaya sukha-dhāma śyāma-kaiśora-līlā*  
*madhura-madhura-bhaṅgī-hrepitānanta-kāma\|*  
*śarad-amṛta-mayūkha-jyotir-ānanda-raśmi*  
*smita-mukha mama dehi sva-priyāṅghryabja-dāsyaṁ \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O abode of happiness! O pastimes of Kṛṣṇa in kaiśora age! May you be victorious! O Kṛṣṇa whose sweet gestures shame unlimited Cupids! O Kṛṣṇa with smiling face, shining in bliss like the sweet rays of the autumn moon! Please give me service to the lotus feet of Rādhā.**

*mahā-rasaikāmbudhi-rādhikāyāḥ krīḍā-kuraṅga smara-vihvalāṅga \|*  
*ānanda-mūrte nija-vallabhāyāḥ padāravinde kuru kiṅkarīṁ māṁ \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O pet deer of Rādhā, the ocean of great rasa! O Kṛṣṇa whose limbs are overwhelmed with love! Make me, a servant at the lotus feet of your true love, the form of bliss.**

*atha śrī-govinde vikasad-aravindekṣaṇa-lasat*  
*kṛpā-dṛśyā pūrṇa-praṇaya-rasa-vṛṣṭyā snapayati \|*  
*sthitā nityaṁ pārśve vividha-paricaryaika-caturā*  
*na keṣāñcid dṛśyaṁ rasika-mithunaṁ sāśritavatī \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Govinda, bathed that gopī in a shower of full, affectionate rasa by the glance of mercy from his blossoming lotus eyes, she, being constantly at their side, skillful in serving in various ways, took shelter of the enchanting couple, unseen by anyone else.**

*rādhāyāḥ kuca-maṇḍale kavalaya-śyāmam svakīyaṁ vapuḥ*  
*saṁkrāntaṁ samavekṣya nīla-vasana-bhrāntyā kareṇa kṣipan \|*  
*mā yatnaṁ kuru no niraṁśūkayituṁ śakyāv urojau mamety*  
*āmoda-smitayā tayā vihasito mugdho hariḥ pātu vaḥ \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing that his blue lotus body was resting on her breasts, and mistaking it for his blue cloth, removing it with her hand, she said with a happy smile, “Don’t try. It is impossible to uncover my breasts.” May Kṛṣṇa, enchanted by that and laughing, protect you.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta mādhave śrī-rādhā-dāsya-mahotsave nāma tṛtīya sargaḥ*

## Chapter 4 - The Festival of seeing Rādhā and Govinda together

## Introduction

*saivaṁ sārvadikānavadya-sahajaprollāsi-kaiśorake*  
*kaumārāvadhi rādhikā-madhupati-svacchanda-vikrīḍane \|*  
*sāndrānanda-rase samāplutavatī vṛndāvanānta-sthayor*  
*divyaṁ darśayator bahir nava-vayo mugdhā tayor utsave \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This innocent young girl became filled with a festival of condensed bliss of rasa of the couple situated within Vṛndāvana, who showed themselves to her attractively. She became filled with the spontaneous youhtful pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, which were filled with faultless, natural joy in the kaiśora age.**

*kadācit śrī-rādhā nava-nava-vayo-rūpa-madhurād-*  
*bhutaṁ śrī-govindaṁ diśi diśi janair varṇita-guṇam \|*  
*svabhāvād udbhuta-praṇaya-rasa-vaiklavya-vanitā*  
*smarantī smodvegaṁ rahasi nijagāda priya-sakhīṁ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One time, Rādhā, agitated by affectionate love arising by her nature, remembering Govinda, who was full of astonishing sweetness and fresh youth, whose qualities are everywhere praised by the people, spoke to a priya-sakhī in private with anxiety.**

## First song

*śyāma-gurjarī rāge gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in śyāma-gurjarī raga.**

*śruti-kuhare vinidhātum atispṛham amṛta-vacana-mayaṁ bahucāṭukam \|*  
*ati kātara-mukha-candram udīkṣitum api ca dṛśā ciram utsukaṁ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**My mind desires to hear his flattering sweet, private words. It desires for a long time to see his confused moon-like face.**

*sakhi śyāmale mama mānasaṁ \|*  
*valati balad iva madana-madākulam-atirasa-khelana-lālasaṁ\|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O friend! My mind, greedy for pleasurable pastimes, overcome with intoxication of love, is drawn to Kṛṣṇa as if by force. (chorus)**

*atirasa-mattatayā gatasādhvasaṁ kalita-ambara-calad-añcalam \|*  
*bhru-yugalaṁ parighūrṇya-bhayaṁ janayitum api dhṛta-ruci-cañcalam \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**My mind is fickle with taste to make him frown in fear, after he grabs the fluttering edge of my dress on becoming bold from intoxication of rasa.**

*tam api hasantam atho kuca-kañcuka-pātana-kṛta-bahu-sambhramam \|*  
*atitarjanam anu tāḍayituṁ kara-vāri-ruheṇa sambhramam \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I am most perplexed when he removes my bodice and laughs. My mind is eager to strike him with the lotus in my hand after scolding him.**

*prahara-śataṁ vacanena tathāpi ca muñcati harir iha na grahaṁ \|*  
*iti vādinam anuharṣayituṁ hasitena samākalam anv ahaṁ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa does not release his grip even by a hundred slaps using words. My speech simply pleases him, while he smiles.**

*nija-rucirāṁ sayugale samapulakitam ādadhataṁ bhujam ujjvalam \|*  
*mām adhiropitavantam ura-sthala upagūhītum ativihavalaṁ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I am eager to be embraced on his chest, after I, most radiant, have been raised in his shining arms with hairs standing on end.**

*mad-adhara-sīdhu-sudhā-rasa-samvadanena samunmada-cetanaṁ\|*  
*modayituṁ parikuñcita-locana-sītkṛti-caya-kṛta-cetanaṁ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His mind rejoices on examining the sweet taste of the nectar of my lips. To please him I will close my eyes and sigh.**

*bahu-vidha-surata-kalā-catura-priya-paritoṣaka-nija-kauśalam \|*  
*darśayituṁ paribhāvana-tat-param atha milituṁ rasa-vihvalaṁ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**My mind is agitated by rasa to meet and embrace him, to show my skill at satisfying him with many skilful arts of love.**

*tad iti sakhīm anurādhikayā parigīta-manoratha-puñjaṁ \|*  
*mudita-sarasvatī-sarabhasa-bhāṣitam astu viṣaya-rati-bhañjakaṁ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May this song expressing her desires, sung to the gopī by Rādhikā, and recorded by joyful Prabodhānanda, break attachment to material pleasure.**

*prasūna-cayanac-chalād atha sakhī-janair nirgatā*  
*viveśa harim īkṣituṁ madana-jīvanaṁ kānanaṁ \|*  
*priyaṁ parivicinvatī kim api puṣpakaṁ cinvatī*  
*ciraṁ cakita-locanā tarutale ca babhrāma sā \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the pretext of picking flowers, Rādhā went out with her sakhīs and entered a forest, the life of Cupid, to see Kṛṣṇa. Searching for him while picking flowers, with frightened eyes, she wandered to the base of a tree.**

*tathā pūrṇaṁ svarṇa-cchavibhir abhivīkṣyākhila-vanaṁ*  
*khagādīn unmattān api madhupa-mālāḥ pracalitāḥ \|*  
*athākasmad ātmany api kim api jātākulatayā*  
*haris tyaktvā khelyāñ ciram akṛta tan-mūla-mṛgayāṁ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing the whole forest filled with the golden glow, seeing the birds and animals intoxicated, and seeing the bees swarming about, suddenly becoming agitated himself, Kṛṣṇa gave up playing and for a long time searched for the cause.**

*tato rūpa-lāvaṇya-sīmāṁ kiśorīṁ*  
*sudūre samālokya kāśmīra-gaurīṁ\|*  
*skhalad barha-maulir galat-pīta-vāsāḥ*  
*kara-bhraṣṭha-venuḥ papātāpta-mūrchaḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the distance he saw the young girl of the highest beauty, with the complexion of gold. His peacock feather fell off and his yellow cloth loosened. The flute fell from his hand and he fainted.**

*tato mandaṁ mandaṁ nava-jalada-vṛndaṁ tanu-rucā*  
*viliṅgītaṁ rājad vadanam aravindaṁ vidadhataṁ \|*  
*tathābhūtaṁ sāpi priyam abhi samīkṣyāntika-gatā*  
*kara-sparśenonmīlita-dṛśi tirodhāt smita-mukhī \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Going close, she saw her lover with the complexion of fresh cloud and lotus face, without his markings (flute, etc). When she touched him with her hand, he opened his eyes. She smiled and disappeared.**

*atha taṁ prāṇa-suhṛdaṁ vicinvan vīkṣya vihvalaṁ\|*  
*luṭhantaṁ bhūtale prāha śrīdāmā snehatāṁ gataḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Searching for his dear friend, seeing him in agitated condition, lying on the ground, Śrīdāmā spoke to him with affection.**

*gatā dūre gāvo dinam api turīyāṁśam abhajat*  
*vayaṁ kṣutkṣāmāḥ smas tava ca jananī vartma-nayanā \|*  
*akasmāt tuṣṇīke sajala-nayane dīna-vadane*  
*tvayi tyakta-khelāṁ nahi nahi vayaṁ prāṇiniṣavaḥ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The cows have wandered far and the fourth part of the day has come. We are hungry and your mother is looking at the road. When you remain silent, weeping with miserable face, giving up playing, we cannot stay alive, desiring to maintain our lives.**

*atha sakhyāḥ paṭī-prānta-mṛṣṭa-vaktraḥ sa mohanaḥ \|*  
*smāraṁ smāraṁ priyām etāṁ sva-gadagadam idaṁ jagau \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After his friend wiped his face with his the edge of his cloth, in bewilderment, Kṛṣṇa remembered his beloved and sang in a choked voice.**

## Second song

*deśī varāḍī rāgeṇa gīyate\|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in deśī-varādi-rāga.**

*stavaka-yugam unnataṁ vahati*  
*rasa-saṁbhṛtaṁ madana-mada-rāśi-sukha-sāraṁ\|*  
*kṣipati rasa-lobhinaṁ bhramaram*  
*anuyayinaṁ dolayati paramam udāraṁ \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The creeper carries two raised flower clusters, the essence of happiness with a heap of intoxicating love. She rejects the bee following her which desires rasa, and trembles greatly.**

*sakhī hema-vallī kāpi*  
*patitādya mama dṛṣṭiṁ \|*  
*calati lalitādbhutaṁ madana-mada-*  
*mantharaṁ kirati mayi kim api rasa-vṛṣṭiṁ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O friend! That golden creeper has fallen into my vision today. It churns most beautifully and amazingly the rapture of love and sprinkles me with a shower of rasa. (chorus)**

*anu-timira-bhavitā vimala-vidhu-maṇḍalaṁ*  
*tatra rasa-laharī-kṛta-dolaṁ \|*  
*dvitaya-vikacotpalaṁ garala-madhu-varṣiṇaṁ*  
*nartayati mugdha-mṛga-lolaṁ \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**It is the spotless moon in the dark of night. It is a swing that creates waves of rasa. It has two shining lotuses. It is a shower of poison and honey. It makes the desire of a foolish deer dance.**

*aruṇa-kamala-dvayaṁ kim api*  
*nava-komalaṁ guñjad-ali-paṭalam avirāmaṁ \|*  
*rucira-śaśi-maṇḍalaṁ lalita-ruci-vibhratī*  
*darśayati mūlam abhirāmaṁ \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The creeper has two red lotuses, very delicate, with hoards of humming bees at all times. It has an attractive moon. Having great beauty it shows a delightful root.**

*dvitayam abhito mahā-madhura-ruci-*  
*kandalaṁ dolayati komala-mṛṇālaṁ\|*  
*sphurad-amṛta-candrikāṁ kirati*  
*rati-dāyinīṁ kāmam upanayati vikarālaṁ \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the creeper swing two soft lotus stems, sprouts of great sweet beauty. It radiates sweet, glowing moonlight which plentifully bestows love. It produces fear (because of overpowering beauty).**

*yadi nikaṭam āgatā vadati mṛdu-līlayā*  
*tvam asi vara-nīla-maṇi-śākhī \|*  
*priya-suhṛdaye tadā bhavati mama cetanā*  
*sthātum aham ahaha-ghana-duḥkhī \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If the creeper came close and said playfully, “You are an excellent sapphire tree for the heart of your lover,” my heart would be filled with sorrow to stand there.**

*anu sutanu-madhyataḥ kim api dadhatī*  
*bharaṁ citram iva caritam iha dṛṣṭā \|*  
*praṇaya-sukha-varṣiṇī mama hṛdaya-karṣiṇī*  
*naiva bata bhavati vidhi-sṛṣṭā \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I see a remarkable quality: the creeper bears a load on the very slim stem. The creeper showers the happiness of love and attracts my heart. It has not been created by Brahmā.**

*sthala-nalina-bandhu-jīvaka-tilaka-sundaraṁ*  
*kunda-kali-paṅkti-vṛta-hāram \|*  
*iha hi vighana-kānane vratati-gṛha-koṭare*  
*vicinu mama jīva-tanu-sāraṁ \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The creeper is beautiful with land lotuses, bandhu-jīva and tilāka flowers as well as has a necklace of jasmine bud clusters. Find the essence of my life in the creeper bower in the deep forest here.**

*priya-suhṛdi rādhikā-rūpam upadeśato madhura-gīta-kamanīyaṁ \|*  
*sarasa-sarasvati-bhaṇitam idam adbhūtaṁ gāyata parama-ramaṇīyaṁ \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sing this astonishing, most attractive song of Kṛṣṇa, who described Rādha’s form to his dear friend, which is recorded by joyful Prabodhānanda.**

*tathāhi ślokaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Here is a verse.**

*uttiṣṭha mā kuru sucañcala-mandirāya*  
*dhenūḥ samāhvaya mahāmuralī-raveṇa \|*  
*prāyo vraje vasati kāpi vimohinī te*  
*no durlabhāpi bhavitā budha mākulo ’bhūḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Stand up and do not be so unsteady. Call the cows with the sound of your great flute. An enchanting girl lives in Vraja. It will not be difficult to find her. Intelligent boy! Do not be disturbed.**

*itthaṁ priyasya suhṛdo vacanāmṛtena*  
*kiñcit sthirīkṛta-matir bhavanaṁ praviṣṭaḥ \|*  
*nātipraphulla-mukha-veditayā jananyā*  
*saṁlālito niśi harir na jagāma nidrāṁ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Settling his mind slightly by the sweet words of his dear friend, he entered his house. Embraced by his mother, who recognized that his face was not filled with joy, he did not sleep at night.**

*viddhaṁ tīkṣṇa-śilīmukhair mṛga-dṛśopāṅgair udīnaiś ciraṁ,*  
*cūrṇī-bhūtam aho payodhara-giri-dvandena vardhiṣṇunā \|*  
*romāṇī-bhūjagena daṣṭam apatan-nābhi-hrade man-mano*  
*rādhāyā iti varṇayan parijanaiḥ sāsro hariḥ pātu vaḥ\|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**My heart was pierced by the sharp arrows of the doe-eyed girl’s glance for a long time and crushed by the mountains of her huge breasts. It was bitten by the snake of her hair line and hurled into the lake of Rādhā’s navel. May weeping Kṛṣṇa, who described her in this way, along with his friends, protect you.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta mādhave rādhā-govinda-mithodarśanotsave nāma caturtha sargaḥ*

## Chapter 5 - Plea to Rādhā’s friend

## Introduction

*athottuṅgaṁ śṛṅgaṁ nija-dayita-govarddhana-gireḥ*  
*samāruhyāśeṣa-vraja-nilaya-vinyasta-nayanaḥ \|*  
*vilokya śrīdāmnā kim api vṛṣabhānu-gṛha-maṇiṁ*  
*karāṅgulyā saṁbhāvita-hṛdaya-cauriḥ samumude \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Climbing his dear Govardhana Mountain to the peak he cast his eyes on the unlimited houses of Vraja. Seeing, along with Śrīdāmā, the jewel in the house of Vṛṣabhānu, pointing with his finger to the supposed thief of his heart, he became joyful.**

*tadārabhya kṣubhyad dhṛdayam atilubhyat-suvidhuraṁ*  
*dadhāno rādhāyāḥ sphurad-adhara-sīdhu-svadayituṁ \|*  
*vrajantīmaikānte vraja-nagara-vīthyāṁ madhupatir*  
*vilokya preyasyāḥ priya-sahacarīṁ prāha kṛpaṇaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**From that time, agitated in his bereaved, greedy heart, he became determined to taste the sweet nectar manifesting from Rādhā’s lips. Seeing the road of Vraja at the very edge of Vraja, Kṛṣṇa spoke in misery to Rādhā’s companions.**

## First song

*tathāhi karṇāṭarāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in karṇāṭa-rāga.**

*vinivedita-bahu-duḥkha-bhareṇa niruttara-helana-yānāṁ*  
*sammukhataram upayātavatā kṛta-kad-uttara-vacana-vitānāṁ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Filled with great suffering on being requested, she goes away without answering in a happy mood, ignoring me. When she comes in front of me, she uses rough words.**

*lalite śrī-vṛṣabhānu-kiśorīm\|*  
*ramaya mayādbhuta-keli-manoratha-mudita-manomaṇi caurīṁ\|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Lalitā! Make the daughter of Vṛṣabhānu, the thief who has stolen the jewel of my mind, which is joyful with desire for astonishing pastimes, play with me. (chorus)**

*kalita-paṭāñcalam ati-madanākula-manasā mauktika-dantīṁ \|*  
*muñca muñca caṭuleti vibhaṅgura-bhru-latikaṁ nigadantīṁ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When I caught the edge of her dress out of passionate love, she spoke with a frown and trembling brow, “Let go let go,” showing her pearl-like teeth.**

*arpayatā maṇi-veṇum athāpara mudrikam api pada-mūle \|*  
*pada-hati-dūra-nivāsa-karīm api mayi vimukhīm anukūle\|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When I placed my jewelled flute and ring at her feet as a gesture of submission, she kicked them far away and turned away from me, though I was gentle.**

*rada-tṛṇa-kāku-śataika-pareṇa nirantara nahi nahi bhāṣāṁ \|*  
*patitavat-ācaraṇe kim apīkṣita-mṛdu-mṛdu-hāsa-vilāsām \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When I begged with grass in my teeth a hundred times, she refused. When I fell at her feet, I saw that she slightly smiled.**

*nītavatā ghana-pulakita-bhujam avalambya nibhṛta-nava-kuñjaṁ\|*  
*rati-tarasākulitāṁ ca mayā bhajatā dyuti-kautuka-puñjaṁ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With the hairs on my arms standing on end, I brought her to a solitary, new bower. She was overwhelmed with passion and experienced intense, radiant joy with me.**

*dṛḍham upaguhya mṛṣā rudatīm api pibatādhara-rasa-sāram \|*  
*kuca-koraka-nakha-lekha-pareṇa vipācita-rucira-vikāraṁ\|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When I tightly embraced her, she wept. I drank the nectar of her lips. She displayed intensely beautiful symptoms when I scratched her breasts with my finger nails.**

*kara-yugaṁ nīvi-niveśayatā bahu-kṛta-vinirodha-prayāsāṁ \|*  
*surata-mahā-rasa-raṅga-pareṇa sasukhada-madana-vikāśāṁ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When I placed my hand within her dress she resisted vehemently. She manifested pleasurable love by my absorption in amorous pastimes of great rasa.**

*iti madhusūdana-rasamaya-vaibhava-bhāvanayā ramaṇīyam \|*  
*mudita-sarasvati-gītam idaṁ rasa-bhāvavatī kamanīyaṁ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The song of joyful Prabodhānanda is attractive with great relishable moods of Kṛṣna and desirable for those having experience of rasa.**

*tathāhi \|*  
*prāṇās tavaiva vapuṣā saha me mṛgākṣi*  
*ratnojjvalaṁ muralikāṁ naya mudrikāṁ ca \|*  
*āstāṁ paraṁ sakṛd api smara-vepitābhyāṁ*  
*dorbhyāṁ tava priya-sakhīṁ dṛḍham āsvajāmi \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O doe-eyed Lalitā! Bring my flute sparkling with gems, my ring, and my life along with my body. I will once embrace your dear friend tightly with my arms trembling with passion.**

*kathaṁ ghana-rasa-prado na valatāṁ taḍid-jyotiṣaṁ*  
*kathaṁ nu madhusūdanas tyajatu tādṛśīṁ padminīṁ\|*  
*kathaṁ ca na hi saṅgato bhavatu rādhayā mādhavas*  
*tad adya lalite yathocita-vicāram evācara \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Should the cloud not be united with the lightning flash? Should the bee give up the lotus? Should Mādhava be separated from Rādhā? Consider this suitably today, O Lalitā!**

*vraja-nṛpati-kumāre koṭi-kandarpa-sāre*  
*vadati vikalam evaṁ prāha sā guḍha-bhāvā \|*  
*sva-para-puruṣa-saṅge sarvadā sā viraktā*  
*bhavati param asādhyā mat-sakhī te vimugdhā \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛṣṇa, the essence of a million Cupids, spoke in this way with agitation, Lalitā with secret meaning said, “She is detached at all times from associating with her husband and all men. My bewildered friend is not at all attainable by you.”**

*atha vartmani loka-saṅgate vimṛṣaṁs tad-vanam āgato hariḥ \|*  
*lalitāpi tadaiva rādhikām idam uce samupetya sādaraṁ\|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**While people gathered on the path, Kṛṣṇa, reflecting on Lalitā’s words, went to the forest. Lalitā approached Rādhā at that time and spoke reverently about Kṛṣṇa.**

## Second song

*gurjarī-rāgeṇa gāyate\|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in gurjari-rāga.**

*santatam abhilāṣuka-kamalādika-sakala-vilāsini-vṛndam \|*  
*ciram apahāya tavaiva padābja-parimala-kṛta-ratibandham \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has become attached to the fragrance of your lotus feet, having for a long time given up Lakṣmī and all other desirable women.**

*rādhe bhaja vraja-rāja-kumāra\|*  
*satvaram abhisara mama vacanena nu mā kuru manasi vicāraṁ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Rādhā! Worship the prince of Vraja. Quickly go to meet him on my order. Do not think about it.**

*pathi pathi tava dāsī-jana-padatala-viluṭhita-mauli-śikhaṇḍaṁ*  
*sāśru-vilocanam avanī-locanam atipāṇḍura-mṛdu-gaṇḍaṁ\|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His peacock feather has fallen on the path at the feet of your servants. He has tears in his eyes as he looks at the earth. His cheeks are soft and pale.**

*viṣama-kusuma-śara-śara-bhara-jarjaram atisukumāra-śarīraṁ \|*  
*niravadhi tava saṅgama rasa-lālasa-mānasam avaśaṁ adhīraṁ\|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His young body had been wounded by the Cupid’s many wicked arrows. He has become unsteady and uncontrolled because his mind longs for the sweetness of your association constantly.**

*adbhuta-koṭī-manobhava-mohana-guṇa-lāvaṇya-nidhānaṁ \|*  
*tava pada-dāsatayārpitavantaṁ sva-vapur galpad-abhidhānaṁ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He is the reservoir of enchanting qualities and beauty that astonish millions of Cupids. He has offered himself in service at your feet and utters your name.**

*nibhṛta-nikuñja-tale tava nāgari nāma-padāni japantaṁ \|*  
*dhyāyantaṁ tava rūpa-vilāsaṁ sāśrutayā nivasantaṁ\|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O clever woman! He chants your name in a solitary bower. Meditating on your form he lives there while weeping.**

*tvayi sahajānurāga-rasa-vihvalam api na spṛha-tanu-gehaṁ \|*  
*analaṅkṛta-mārjita-janitojvalaḥ pītāmbara-vara-dehaḥ \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Disturbed with natural attraction for you he does not desire his house. Wearing yellow cloth, he shines without ornaments.**

*prāṇa-koṭisu nirājita-sulalita-tvat-pada-nakha-ruci-leśaṁ \|*  
*tava parirambhaṇe rasa-param āśā-dhṛta-jīvana-maṇim eṣaṁ \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He worships a particle of the beauty from your gentle toe nail with a million of his lives. He maintains his life with the hope of embracing you, the supreme rasa.**

*śrī-vṛṣabhānu-sutā pada-jīvana-rasada-sarasvatī-gītaṁ \|*  
*janayatu tad-rasa-mūrti-padāmbuja-bhāvam udāram adhītaṁ\|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the song of Prabodhānanda, who bestows rasa, whose life is Rādhā’s feet, being studied, produce love for Rādhā’s lotus feet.**

*ayi kim api kuru tvaṁ rakṣa-lokaṁ ca dharmaṁ*  
*mama tu sakhi gataiva prāyaśo jīvitāśā \|*  
*marakata-maṇi-bhāsā tena jāmbunadābhyāṁ*  
*tava yadi na hi vīkṣe bhūyasīm aṅka-bhuṣāṁ \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O my friend! Will you perform dharma and protect your family? The aspirations of your life are almost finished if you do not see the ornament with a sapphire gem and gold.**

*majjaṁs tat kuca-kuṅkumāṅka-yamunā-tīrthodake reṇubhis*  
*tasyāḥ śrī-pada-paṅkajāṅkita-bhuvaḥ sarvāṅgaṁ sammārjayan \|*  
*tad dvāry eva gatāgataṁ vicarayan nityaṁ satṛṣnekṣaṇe*  
*ko ’pi śyāma-kiśorako vrajapura-strī-lampaṭaḥ pātu vaḥ \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the dark youth, greedy for the women of Vraja, who submerges himself in the water from the Yamunā stained with beautiful kuṅkuma from Rādhā’s breasts and rubs the dust from the earth marked by Rādhā’s foot prints on all his limbs, who constantly considers and peers with thirst at the gate where she will come and go, protect you.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta-mādhave śrī-rādhā-sakhyanunayo nāma pañcama sargaḥ*

## Chapter 6 - Clever Lord of Rādhā

## Introduction

*prati-madhura-vicitra-bhru-latā-narttanena*  
*prahita-samara-saukhyaṁ khelayantī manojaṁ \|*  
*lalita-gati-vilāsair nūpura-dhvana-ramyaiḥ*  
*kṣaṇam api nahi rādhā mādhave dhairyam ādhāt \|\| 1 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā did not give a moment’s peace to Kṛṣṇa, as she played with attractive happiness while inciting war, by each sweet movement of her brow, by her graceful gait and by the pleasant sounds of her ankle bells.**

*bahiḥ kiṁ niryāyād api na bhavitā tad guru-janaḥ*  
*kim u smitvā smitvā mayi kuṭila-dṛṣṭiṁ racayitā \|*  
*vilambya sthānād apy adhika kapaṭena priya-sakhī*  
*yathā pṛcchen mugdhā nava-ghana-ruciḥ ko ’yam iti māṁ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**“Will he come outside where there are no elders? Dear friend! Why does he look at me most crookedly while smiling, delaying to leave this place, using craftiness? Who is this person with the complexion of a fresh cloud?” Will the innocent girl ask this about me?**

*iti bahu-vidha-cintā vyākulo gokulenduḥ*  
*śrama-ja-salila-bindu śrī-mukho bhakta-bandhuḥ \|*  
*vraja-pura upahūtām utsave kvāpi yāntīṁ*  
*rahasi nija-sakhībhir vīkṣya rādhāṁ jagāda \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With many worries, troubled Kṛṣṇa, his face covered in perspiration, separating from his friend, seeing Rādhā secretly going with her friends, on being called to some festival in Vraja, spoke to her.**

## First song

*śyāma-gurjjarī-rāgeṇa gīyate\|*

**Перевод:**

**This is sung in śyāma-gurjari-rāga.**

*dehi vivṛtya kṛpāmṛta-dṛṣṭiṁ*  
*samaya mama smara-śara-śikhi-vṛṣṭiṁ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Glance upon me with mercy, stopping the shower of Cupid’s arrows upon me.**

*rādhe vraja-pati-nanda-kumāraṁ \|*  
*paricinu mām adhigata-rasa-sāraṁ\|\|dhru\|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Rādhā! Recognize me, who knows the essence of rasa, the young son of Nanda.**

*darśaya hāsya-manoharam āsyaṁ \|*  
*vitara sudhākara-koṭi-suhāsyam \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Show your attractive, smiling face. Smile like a million moons.**

*tvayi ruci-dāyini sucira-dhṛtāśaṁ \|*  
*kim api samādiśa nija-pada-dāsyam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Order me to serve your feet, since I have long held the desire for you, who bestow taste.**

*tava kuca-parirambha-para-lolaṁ \|*  
*mām avalokaya galita-nicolaṁ\|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Show me your breasts whose covering has fallen, since I have desired so long to embrace them.**

*nija-kara-gata-tāmbulam-akhaṇḍam \|*  
*vitara kṛpā yadi vikasita-gaṇḍam \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With your own hand, mercifully give me the whole betel nut from your cheek.**

*abhayam alajjam alaṁ kuśa-ceṣṭaṁ\|*  
*katham iva paśyasi mama na kṛteṣṭaṁ\|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Why do you not see my desire filled with fearless, unashamed, drunken action?**

*bhavatu mamādya śubhā rajanīyaṁ\|*  
*tanu tava rati-sukham anubhavanīyaṁ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Let this night now be glorious for me. Bestow the bliss of your love that should be experienced.**

*iti rasa-sāra-sarasvati-gītaṁ\|*  
*racayatu hari-pada-rasam upanītaṁ \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the song of Prabodhānanda, the essence of rasa, which has been presented, produce attraction for Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet.**

*tathāhi \|*  
*vada bahutara-rukṣaṁ paśya māṁ śona-dṛṣṭyā*  
*kuru caraṇa-prahāraṁ gaccha vā cāvahelaṁ \|*  
*pramada-madana-mādyan-mugdha-bāhyāntarātmā*  
*na khalu na khalu kṛṣṇo rādhike tvāṁ jahāti \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Speak very harshly! Glare at me with red eyes! Kick me! Insult me! O Rādhā! Kṛṣṇa2, bewildered internally and externally from the beginning with intoxicated love, will never leave you.**

*na hi nṛpati-bhayo me no punar loka-lajjā*  
*na ca mama kulaśīla khyāti-rakṣādy apekṣā \|*  
*tava kuṭila-kaṭākṣais tīkṣna-bāṇaiḥ kṣato ’haṁ*  
*tava kuca-parirambhe naiva jīvāmi rādhe \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Rādhā! I am not afraid of the king or of shame in front of people. I do not care about protection of my reputation, character or family. I have been wounded by the sharp arrows of your crooked glance. I cannot live without embracing your breasts.**

*atha prema-rasāgādhā rādhā sādhāraṇa-kramāt \|*  
*prāha priya-sakhīm etad upaśṛṇvati mādhave\|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**While Kṛṣṇa overheard, Rādhā, with deep rasa of prema, spoke to a dear friend in ordinary language (ignoring Kṛṣṇa).**

## Second song

*yathā mālava gauḍa rāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**This is sung in mālava-gauḍa-rāga.**

*bhuvana-vidita-pariśuddha-kula-dvayam ayi na dhṛta-kalaṅkaṁ \|*  
*mama ca suviśruta-śīla-mahodayam avadhāraya gata-śaṅkaṁ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Understand that both our families are well known in the world to be completely pure and without any fault and that there is no worry about my famous character and good fortune.**

*sakhi he vāraya vrajapati-sūnum \|*  
*na hi parapuruṣe mama matir udayet yadi kalaye niśi bhānuṁ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Keep the son of Nanda away! I will not think of another man even if I see the sun at night.**

*gokula-bhadra-kṛto’sya yujyate iyam atidurnaya-līlā\|*  
*rājani naṣṭa-guṇe ’pi virājati kim u rati-rasika suśīlā \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This wicked pastime is proposed by he who creates auspiciousness for Gokula. O amorous enjoyer! If the king has lost all qualities, should woman of good qualities serve him?**

*tiṣṭhati patham avaruddhya tathāñcalam api kalayitum anusārī \|*  
*sakalam idaṁ vinivedaya nija-sutam avatu vrajeśvara-nārī \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He blocks the path and, follows me in order to grab my cloth. Report all this. Yaśodā should guard her son.**

*nāsya-kṛte vidadhāmi sukhaṁ sakhi yamunā-nīra-vigāhaṁ \|*  
*kim u phalaṁ cāṭu-śataṁ kurute mayi yad ati niyama kaṭhināhaṁ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I do not happily bathe in the Yamunā when he does so. O friend! What is the result of his hundreds of flattering words since I am most strict with rules?**

*vṛndāvana-vipine ’ti-praphullita-nava-nava-malli-vitānaṁ \|*  
*pratidinam aham upayāmi kusuma-caya iha pratiṣedhati yānaṁ \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I pick fresh, blossoming jasmine flowers in Vṛndāvana forest daily. He prevents me from going to pick flowers.**

*nidritavati jana-nikare yadi mama gṛham ayam eti niśānte \|*  
*aham ati jāgaraṇena kṛta-sthitir api bhavitāsmi niśānte\|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Fearing that he will come into my house at the end of night when the elders are sleeping, I must remain awake till the dawn.**

*yadi nahi nahi vacanena kathañcana dhasyati sakhi mama bāhuṁ \|*  
*kalayasi yadi mama satyam aho sakhi moha-tatiṁ tvaraṇāhaṁ\|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O friend! If he grabs my arm in spite of my protests, you will truly see that I will quickly faint.**

*iti rasa-sāra-sarasvati-varṇita-rādhā-vacana-vilāsaṁ\|*  
*ati caturāyita-cārutaraṁ ciram upagāyata sa-vilāsaṁ \|\|24 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sing with great charm and grace the essence of rasa, charming words of Rādhā described by Prabodhānanda.**

*tathāhi \|*  
*kathaya sakhi yaśodā-nandanaṁ rādhikā te*  
*patati caraṇa-mūle mugdhabālāṁ kṣamasva \|*  
*smara-vaśa-kulajāyāḥ keli-raṅga-prasaṅgāt*  
*virama kuru sa rāmaḥ kānane nitya-khelāṁ \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O friend! Say to the son of Yāśodā. “Rādhikā falls at your feet. Forgive the innocent young girl. Withdraw from these pastimes with a chaste girl who is influenced by love. Play constantly with Balarāma in the forest.”**

*śravaṇena nipīya rādhikā-rasa-vaidagdhya-gabhīra-bhāratīm \|*  
*lalitātha jagada nirbhara-praṇayānanda-rasātipeśalam\|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Hearing Rādhā’s skillful, deep words filled with rasa, Lalitā spoke charming words of rasa filled with affectionate bliss.**

## Third song

*yathā gurjjarī-rāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**This is sung in gurjarī-rāga.**

*nipatati caraṇe guñjābharaṇe gokula-rāja-kumāre \|*  
*vidhu-mukhi bhāgyaṁ parama-vimṛgyaṁ kim apram iha saṁsāre\|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O moon-faced Rādhā! When Kṛṣṇa, decorated with guñja berries, falls at your feet, is there any other good fotune, most sought after, in this world?**

*śyāma-śarīre madanādhīre na kuru haṭhaṁ sakhi rādhe\|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O my friend Rādhā! Do not be stubborn with the dark boy, who is unsteady with love. (chorus)**

*niravadhi sadaye duḥkhita-hṛdaye prakaṭaya karuṇā-stokaṁ \|*  
*na gaṇaya lokaṁ nāśaya śokaṁ kuru sasmitam avalokaṁ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Show a bit of mercy to his suffering heart since you are always merciful. Do not consider people. Destroy his sorrow. Glance at him with a smile.**

*parihṛta-sakalaṁ priyam ati-vikalaṁ ghana-ghana-kṛta-niḥśvāsaṁ \|*  
*pratipada-madhuraṁ smara-śara-vidhuraṁ jīvaya nija-pada-dāsaṁ \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Bring to life your dearest who has given up everything, who sighs deeply, who is the sweet servant at your feet, who is pierced by love’s arrow.**

*mā vada paruṣaṁ kuru mayi na ruṣaṁ viracaya na bhru-bhaṅgīṁ \|*  
*hari-mukha-kamale nava-rasa-vimale khelaya locana-dṛṣṭiṁ \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Do not speak harshly. Do not be angry with me. Do not frown. Glance upon the lotus face of Kṛṣṇa, pure with fresh rasa.**

*darśaya vadanaṁ vikasita-madanaṁ rākā-candra-manojñaṁ \|*  
*vañcaya na janaṁ nijam ati-sujanaṁ lalitānaṅga-rasa-jñaṁ \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Show your face, blossoming with love, attractive as the full moon. Do not cheat your virtuous friend, who knows the rasa of charming love.**

*vraja-pati-tanaye kṛta-bahu-vinaye tribhuvana-mohana-rūpe\|*  
*neyam upekṣā tava śubha-kakṣā jāgrati manmatha-bhūpe \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Do not let your auspicious heart ignore Nanda’s son, most courteous, the king of awaking love, whose form attracts the three worlds.**

*nigada sahāsyaṁ dayita-vayasyaṁ pariracayeṅgitam alpaṁ\|*  
*bhramad ali-puñjaṁ nava-nava-kuñjaṁ kuru sakhi kiśalaya-talpaṁ \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O friend! Speak to your dear friend with a smile. Give a few hints. Make a bed of buds in a fresh bower with swarms of hovering bees.**

*śrutihara-caritā madhurima-bharitā mañjula-rasa-mañjūṣā \|*  
*rasada-sarasvati-vāg ati-madhumatī bhavatu satata-śrutibhūṣā \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the charming pastimes, filled with sweetness and beautiful nectar, the sweet words of Prabodhānanda, which bestow rasa, be constantly heard.**

*trasasi kim u sakhi tvaṁ naiva pītāmbarāt te*  
*kuca-yugam aparasya sparśa-yogyaṁ kathañcit \|*  
*vadana-kamala-gandhaiḥ kṛṣṇa-bhṛṅgo ’yam aṅghrau*  
*bhramati tava kathaṁ vā manyatām vārnāni \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O friend! Why are you afraid? No one except Kṛṣṇa is qualified to touch your breasts. The black bee Kṛṣṇa wanders at your feet because of the scent of your lotus face. Why should you refuse to respect him?**

*ko ’yaṁ dvāri vidhuḥ priyāli tad aśāvālambatāmbaraṁ*  
*bāle nāyaka eṣa te kila tadā hārāntare tiṣṭhatu\|*  
*mugdhe mādhava eṣa hanta vipina-nyasta kathaṁ no bhavet*  
*rādhāya iti vāk-chalena sahasā śleṣī hariḥ pātu vaḥ \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Who is at the door? It is Kṛṣṇa. But the moon stays in the sky. O innocent girl. It is your hero. Let the ornament stay on my necklace. Foolish girl! It is Mādhava. If it is Lakṣmī’s husband, why did you put him in the forest?3 May Kṛṣṇa the object of Rādhā’s puns, protect you.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta mādhave catura-rādheśo nāma ṣaṣṭha sargaḥ*

## Chapter 7 - Bewildered Kṛṣṇa

## Introduction

*gatāyāṁ rādhāyāṁ druta-padam api svīya-bhavanaṁ*  
*sakhī vā tat preṣṭhaṁ tv atula-rasam ābhāṣya kim api\|*  
*praviṣṭaḥ śrī vṛndāvana-vipinam ānanda-sadanaṁ*  
*sa govindo nāvindata rucim apīndor udayataḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Rādhā quickly returned home after Lalitā had described something of her lover of incomparable rasa, Kṛṣṇa entered the Vṛndāvana forest, home of bliss, but did not take pleasure in the rising moon.**

*na candre liptatve na ca parama-sāndre malaya-je*  
*na kālindi-nīrā-nīla-kamala-mālāsu sa hariḥ\|*  
*jalārdraṁ svaṁ pītāmbaram api tanau nyasya na jahau*  
*manāg bādhāṁ rādhā-viraha-dahana-jvāla-vikalaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Afflicted by the burning flames of separation from Rādhā, he could not at all give up his pain, by soaking in moonlight, by the most soothing Malaya breeze, by garlands of blue lotuses from the water of the Yamunā, by placing on his body his yellow cloths soaked in water.**

*lolat-pallavinīṁ vilokya madhupod ghuṣṭāṁ vidūre latāṁ*  
*rādhā māhvayatīti harṣa-vigalat pītāmbaro dhāvitaḥ \|*  
*kālindī-kalahaṁsa-komala-kala-dvānena lolekṣaṇo*  
*bāraṁbaram abhūt pratārita-mati vṛndāvane mādhavaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing a swaying creeper from far away, filled with bees, he ran towards it with his yellow cloth falling off in joy, thinking, “Rādhā is calling me.” Feeling cheated he remains in Vṛndāvana, his unsteady eyes glancing repeatedly on hearing the gentle cooing of the swans in the Yamunā.**

*vakṣojaṁ stavake mukhaṁ himakare netraṁ kuraṅgī-gaṇe*  
*nṛtyat-barha-kalāpa eva kavarīṁ nālīṣu doḥ kandalīṁ\|*  
*itthaṁ kiñcana sādhu tatra katham apy adhyāsya saṁdhukṣito*  
*rādhāyā lalitāṅgakāni sa harir babhrāma vṛndāvane\|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In pain, Kṛṣṇa wandered in Vṛndāvana, seeing Rādhā’s fine limbs in anything beautiful. He saw her breasts in the flower clusters, her face in the moon, her eyes in the doe’s glances, her braid in the feathers of a dancing peacock and her arms in the lotus stems.**

*atha kuca-parirambha-cumba-bimbādhara-*  
*rasa-pānavatotsave nimagnaḥ\|*  
*kim api hṛdi samāhite pikāli-mada-*  
*kalitāvarutaiḥ pramugdha āsīt \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Absorbed in the festival of drinking the rasa of embracing her breasts and kissing her bimba red lips, concentrating his mind, he became enchanted with the soft sounds of the cuckoos and bees.**

*tataḥ kamala-patrākṣaḥ kadamba-tala-ketanaḥ \|*  
*vilalāpāti-karuṇaṁ bāṣpa-gadayā girā \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lotus eyed Kṛṣṇa, stopping at the base of a kadamba tree, wailed most pitifully with tears and choked voice.**

## First song

*śrī rāgeṇa gīyate yathā \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song was sung in śrī-rāga.**

*samabhilaṣāmy aham īkṣitum ujjvala śrī-mukha-himakara-bimbaṁ \|*  
*nayati ca hanta tad-anya-diśaṁ prati nihita-celam avilambam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When I desire to see her moon-like shining face, she turns her head in the other direction and quickly covers her face.**

*harihari katha ahaṁ jīvam idānīṁ \|*  
*rādhā na vadati vāvadānaṁ kim api mitām ayi vāṇīṁ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Oh! How do I live? Rādhā does not speak any word to me.**

*sa-spṛham aham abhiyāmi nikaṭam ayam ahaha calaty ati dūraṁ \|*  
*na ca śṛṇute mama duḥkha-nivedanam api paśyati na krūram \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With desire I approach. She moves far away. She does not hear my revelation of suffering. She does not see her cruelty.**

*yadi kusumāvacayāyaitā vanam iha kusumam aho citraṁ \|*  
*naivaspṛśad api mad upahṛtaṁ vata kim idaṁ madhura-caritraṁ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When she comes to the forest to pick flowers, it is astonishing that she does not touch a flower offered by me. Is this a sweet character?**

*sahajā hriyā lavam api nonnamayati sundara-hāsa-mukhenduṁ \|*  
*kiṁ manojanitam ahaṁ na vagāhe sāndra-rasāmṛta-sindhuṁ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because of natural shyness, she does not lift her moon-like face endowed with a beautiful smile. How will I plunge into the sweet ocean of condensed rasa arising from love?**

*niravadhi duḥsaha-manmatha-lubdhaka-viddhamanaḥ pralapāmi \|*  
*na prasīdati vṛṣabhānu-kiśorī kiṁ karavai kva nu yāmi \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Constantly pierced by the unbearable hunter, Cupid, I lament. Vṛṣabhānu’s daughter does not show kindness to me. What should I do? Where should I go?**

*maṇimaya-mudrikayā’ti-manohara-ratnojjvala-nija vaṁśyā \|*  
*parama-pralobhanam ahaha kṛtaṁ bahu manute naiva vayasyā \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have lured her with my jewelled ring and my flute glittering with attractive gems. She does not even look at them.**

*hāhā jīvita na trapase tvaṁ katham iha duścaritena \|*  
*kṣaṇam api tāṁ rasa-dhāma vinā yat tiṣṭhasi parama-sukhena \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O my life! Why are you not ashamed of your wicked actions—remaining so happy without the abode of rasa even for a moment?**

*iti virahātura-hari-paridevana-rañjita-gītam udāraṁ \|*  
*gāyata sarasvati-viracitam anupama-rasa-nidhi-sāraṁ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sing this noble song, the essence of the ocean of incomparable rasa, containing the lamentation of Kṛṣṇa pained by separation, composed by Prabodhānanda.**

*tatraiva \|*  
*pūro rādhā paścād api ca mama rādhā tata itaḥ*  
*sphuraty eṣā samyag vasati mama rādhāntara-gatā \|*  
*adhaś corddhvaṁ rādhā viṭapiṣu ca rādhā kim aparaṁ*  
*samastaṁ me rādhā-mayam idam aho bhāti bhuvanaṁ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā manifests in front of me. Rādhā is behind me. Rādhā is here and there. She directly resides within me. Rādhā is below and above. Rādhā is in the trees. Everything else is composed of Rādhā. She is the whole universe.**

*karhy ākasmika udbhaviṣyati mṛgī-netre tavātrāgamo*  
*dṛṣṭvā mām atikātaraṁ ca karuṇā kāryaṅkarī bhāvinī\|*  
*utthāpyo’smi kadā ca vāgbhir amṛtair āśvāsya dhṛtvā kare*  
*hā rādhe vṛṣabhānu-nandinī kadā tvām aṅkam āropaye \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O doe-eyed Rādhā! When will you come suddenly here, and, seeing me suffer, be merciful to me and raise me up? O Rādhā! Daughter of Vṛṣabhānu! Pacifying you with sweet words, holding your hand, when will I place you on my lap?**

*vinā prāṇaṁ dehaḥ katham iha bhavet ko nu salilaṁ*  
*vinā mīnaś candro vilasati vinā ko nu rajanīṁ \|*  
*vinā annaṁ nu kā prāṇa-sthitir ahaha kṛṣṇo ’pi nitaraṁ*  
*vinā rādhāṁ premonmada-madana-līlā-rasa-nidhiṁ\|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Can the body live without prāṇa? Can the fish live without water? Can the night exist without the moon? Can life be maintained without food? Kṛṣṇa also cannot live without Rādhā, the ocean of sweet pastimes of love-crazed by prema.**

*yogīndrā mṛgayanti yat pada-rajas tenāpi yan mṛgyate*  
*viśvaṁ yena vimohitaṁ cala-dṛśā tasyāpi yan mohanaṁ\|*  
*pūrṇānanda-mayo ’pi yad rasa-lavāsvādena dhanāyate*  
*tad dhāma-sphuṭa-campaka-cchavi ciraṁ rādhābhidhaṁ pātu vaḥ \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the girl called Rādhā, whose body manifests beauty of the campaka, whose foot dust is sought by Kṛṣṇa, for whom the best of yogīs search, by whose glance Kṛṣṇa who bewilders the universe is bewildered, by tasting a drop of whose rasa Kṛṣṇa, though full of bliss, feels most fortunate, protect you.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta-mādhave mugdha-mādhavo nāma saptama-sargaḥ*

## Chapter 8 - Kṛṣṇa, full of rasa

## Introduction

*atha vyacayad uccakaiś catura-cakra-cūḍāmaṇir*  
*vicintya ruciraṁ ciraṁ mada-calac-cakorī-dṛśaṁ\|*  
*tad-ucca-kuca-kāñcanācalam udañcitenorasā*  
*nipīḍya sahasoccayaṁ viracayan upāyāvalīṁ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The king of cleverness with great cheating, thinking constantly of the beautiful Rādhā with eyes of a restless cakora bird, made many bold plans for pressing her high golden, mountainous breasts to his raised chest.**

*kadācit kālindī-jala-vilasad-indīvara-vane*  
*nilīno majjantyā mada-kalabha-nīlo madhupatiḥ \|*  
*anarghyaṁ rādhāyā vivṛta-kuca-kumbhobhaya-taṭī*  
*parīrambhaṁ rambhā-vijayi-lasat-ūror alabhat \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One day Kṛṣṇa, like a black, intoxicated elephant, hid himself by submerging himself in a forest of shining blue lotuses in the waters of the Yamunā. He embraced the exposed breasts of Rādhā, whose thighs conquered banana stems.**

*puraḥ-sthitvaikānte racitam atha nirvāpya sapadi*  
*pradīpaṁ phutkāraiḥ sa-tamasi gṛhe nīla-vasanaḥ \|*  
*vimudryāsyāmbhojaṁ na paridhṛta-ratnābharaṇakaḥ*  
*kadācid rādhāyā akṛta parirambhotsavam asau \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One time, alone with Rādhā in front of him, suddenly blowing out a lamp, wearing blue cloth in the dark room, devoid of jewelled ornaments, without decorations on his lotus face, he was able to embrace Rādhā.**

*kvacana-nava-nikuñje kṛṣṇa ālakṣya-sakhyā*  
*taralitam ati-khelāṁ kṛṣyamānaḥ kadācit \|*  
*tad ati-nibhṛta-vallī mandirāntar nilīnāṁ*  
*sa-hasitam upaguhyānanda-kāṣṭhāṁ sa lebhe \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One day, Kṛṣṇa was dragged into a fresh bower by a sakhī and observed unsteady Rādhā hiding within the solitary creeper pavilion. Eager for pastimes, embracing her with laughter, he attained the highest bliss.**

*nava-yuvati-suveśaṁ kārayitvā kadācit*  
*parama-catura-sakhyā preṣitaṁ prāṇa-nāthaṁ\|*  
*tad ati-madhura-rūpeṇocchalat prema-sindhuḥ*  
*sva-nikaṭam upayātaṁ prāha rādhā vimugdhā \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One time, Kṛṣṇa was dressed by a most clever sakhī in the clothing of a young girl. Rādhā, enchanted, experiencing an ocean of prema on seeing his sweet form (thinking he was a girl), spoke to him when he came close.**

## First song

*yathā rāmakirī rāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in rāmakirī-rāga.**

*nīla-nalina-dala-komalam ujjvalam-aṅgam-adhika-sukumāram \|*  
*mohana-rūpam idaṁ tava vallavi-harati mamāntara-sāraṁ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O gopī! Your enchanting youthful form, soft as a blue lotus petal, with shining limbs, has stolen my heart.**

*vidhumukhi kā tvam aho madhure*  
*priya-sakhī bhava mama cārutare\|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Moon-faced girl! Who are you? Sweet, beautiful girl! Be my dear friend! (chorus)**

*keyam aho tava viśva-vimohana-lalitāpāṅga-vibhaṅgī \|*  
*janayati khañjana-garva-vibhañjanam atibhayam eti kuraṅgī \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**What is this? Your graceful glance which bewilders the universe breaks the pride of the khañjana bird and frightens the doe.**

*kasya sutāsi calan-maṇi-kuṇḍala-maṇḍita-mṛdula-kapole \|*  
*matta-mataṅgaja-gāmini nava-rati-keli-kalāsu vilole \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Whose daughter are you? O girl whose tender cheeks are decorated with moving jewelled earrings! O girl who walks like an intoxicated elephant! O girl unsteady with the playful arts of new love!**

*mā kuru vañcanam iha sakhi kiñcana tava pṛcchāmi rahasyam \|*  
*tvām api cakitam udaikṣata kim u harir iti mama vācyam avaśyaṁ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O friend! Do not cheat me. I will ask you a secret question. Did Kṛṣṇa, trembling, look at you? I must ask this.**

*locana-tāpa-hare sukha-varṣiṇi dehi niviḍa-parirambhaṁ \|*  
*tat-prāptyaika-rasena mano mama bhajati harer upalambhaṁ\|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O girl who removes suffering from my eyes! O girl who showers happiness! On embracing you with love, my mind perceives Kṛṣṇa.**

*hāsyam aho tava lāsyam aho tava vacanam aho madhu-dhārām \|*  
*snāna-śayana-bhojana-gamanādiṣu vihara mayā tvam udārām \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Your smile, your dancing, and your words are streams of honey. Being generous, you should bathe, lie down, eat and walk with me.**

*iti vara-yuvati-veśa-dharo harir ati-rasa-sindhum agādhām \|*  
*pulakita-bahu-sahāsam amodata ciram upaguhya sa rādhām \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa, dressed as a young girl, embracing Rādhā, enjoyed a deep ocean of great pleasure, smiling as his hairs stood on end.**

*mugdha sarasvati-gīta-mahādbhūta-mādhava-keli-vilāsaṁ \|*  
*kaṇṭha-taṭe kurutātirasaṁ kila śithila-yuvati-bhuja-pāśaṁ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Place in your throats the most astonishing pastime of Kṛṣṇa embracing unsteady Rādhā with his snake-like arms as sung by enchanted Prabodhānanda.**

*vitatya nijam ujjvalaṁ kapiśam uttarīyaṁ tale*  
*tad eka-diśi vaṁśikām api nidhāya rādheritaḥ \|*  
*vicitra kusumāny apātayata nīpam ārūḍhavān*  
*hṛdā kim api bhāvayan rasika-śekharaḥ karhicit \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Spreading his bright yellow top cloth on the ground, and placing his flute in one direction, on Rādhā’s instruction, he climbed the kadamba tree and threw down various flowers. The topmost rasika, he thought something in his heart.**

*sakhyā tatra puraḥ-sthitāṁ kula-vadhū-saṁmohinīṁ śrī-harer*  
*vaṁśīm ātma-hitāya gopaya tad ity ukte kare nyasya tāṁ \|*  
*nīpais taiḥ parimaṇḍitāṁ nija-paṭa-cchannāṁ ca śākhāntare*  
*līnāṁ vīkṣya tato ’vatīrya sahasā yāntīm avārundha saḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One sakhī placed Kṛṣṇa’s flute in Rādhā’s hand and said, “Hide it for your own benefit.” Seeing the flute disappear in the branches, after she had covered the flute with her cloth and decorated it with kadambas, he climbed down the tree and suddenly stopped her from going away.**

*athātimadhurānaṅga-raṅga-bhaṅgī gabhīrayā \|*  
*girā mudā lalitayā prāha rādhāṁ rasākulāṁ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With joyful, gentle words, because of the deep sweet pastimes of love, he spoke to Rādhā, who was overwhelmed with rasa.**

## Second song

*yathā śyāma gurjjarī rāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in śyāma-gurjarī-rāga.**

*maṇi-maya-veṇum udākṣipad atha mama nava-saṁvyānam udāraṁ \|*  
*nīpam analpakam api ca sudūrata iyam akṛtātivisāram \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Someone threw my jewelled flute covered with a large fresh cloth far way into the huge, broad kadamba tree.**

*keyaṁ vrajapura-catura-sucaurī \|*  
*bhramati vane mama lalita-kiśorī \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Who is this clever thief of Vraja? This delicate young girl wanders about in my forest.**

*muñca mamāñcalam iti bahu-cañcala-locana-kamala-vibhaṅgī \|*  
*nigadati hanta mṛṣā pariroditi kalita-mahādbhūta-raṅgī \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She says, “Let go of my cloth,” and moves her fickle, lotus eyes. As a most astonishing gesture, she pretends to weep.**

*soḍhum akhilam atha bata kañcuka-gata-dvitaya-kadambam udāraṁ\|*  
*kim u na dadāti viśaṅkam athāpi tu racayati vividha-vikāraṁ \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Why is she not fearful? She has transformed the flute into the two large kadambas within her bodice to overcome the situation.**

*prathayati bata caturatvam aho vara-yuvatī chadma-matīyaṁ \|*  
*idam adhunāpi śleṣya-pṛthu-kuca-bhara iti katham iva kathanīyaṁ \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She runs for a moment, falls, and faints while her hair comes undone. She tears her clothing, breaks her bangles and her jewel necklace.**

*mṛgayati kām api vallim athecchati yamunā gaman ajasram \|*  
*gaccha gṛhaṁ sakhi tac caraṇe kuru ratim iti gadati ca sāsraṁ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She searches for some creeper and desires to go to the Yamunā constantly. With tears she says, “O friend! Go home and show attraction for his feet.”**

*tvat-pada-niviḍa-prema-rasa-vihavla-hṛdayā hanta na jāne \|*  
*jīvati nātha cirāgamane mama neti ca bādhā-yāne \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because her agitated heart has prema for your feet, I do know if my Rādhā, extremely afflicted, is living or not, since I have been here for a long time.**

*mohana-nitya-mahā-rasa-daṁ kila yuvayor vedmi sva-bhāvaṁ \|*  
*yadi nija-jīva-kalāṁ kalayiṣyasi cañcala cala kṛta-bhāvaṁ\|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I know the natural nature of you two—it is enchanting, eternal, and blissful. O fickle Kṛṣṇa! If you see Rādhā, a portion of your own self, please approach her with love.**

*iti rasa-lola-sarasvati-varṇita-rādhā-priya-sakhī-bhāṣā \|*  
*vilasati mādhava-janita-mahādbhuta-premotkaṇṭha-vilāsā \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The words of the dear sakhī of Rādhā described by Prabodhānanda, who trembles with rasa, shine as a display of most astonishing longing in prema for Kṛṣṇa.**

*rudānti mṛga-pakṣiṇo na vikaśanti valli-drumāḥ*  
*śarad-vimala-candramā malina-bhāvam ālambate \|*  
*vahanti na samīraṇāḥ sahaja-śītalāmodinaḥ*  
*kṣaṇād viraha-kātare nava-rasa-prade dhāmani \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The birds and animals weep, the creepers and trees do not blossom, the pure autumn moonlight becomes contaminated and the breezes do not carry their natural cool fragrances, in the abode which gives fresh rasa, when there is separation.**

*sahasā samupetya mādhavas tata udvela ivāmṛtāmbudhiḥ \|*  
*parirambhana-cumbanādinā ramaṇīṁ tāṁ ramayāṁ babhūva saḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Suddenly Kṛṣṇa, like an overflowing ocean of sweetness, approached. He gave enjoyment to his beloved with embraces and kisses.**

*lajjā-saṅkucitā-bhṛśotttaralayor maunaika-vṛtti-priyā*  
*lāpaikotsukayoḥ supatra-śayanā-saṁspṛg-balātkarṣiṇoḥ \|*  
*pratyākhyāna-parāyaṇāgrahavator dainyokti-niṣpīḍino*  
*rādhā-mādhavayor nave nidhuvane ko ’pi kramaḥ pātu vaḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She withdrew with shyness but he was trembling profusely. She was silent but he was enthusiastic to speak. She contacted the flower bed, but he pulled her strongly. She was reluctant but he was intent. She uttered pitiful words, but he pressed her. May the events of Rādhā and Mādhava in fresh, amorous play, protect you.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta-mādhave vihvala-rādhiko nāmaikādaśa sargaḥ*

## Chapter 12 - Pastimes of Rāsa

## Introduction

*tat-tad-vicitra-rati-kelibhir ullasantī*  
*smitvā kadācid iti kāntam uvāca rādhā\|*  
*ākṛṣya veṇu-virutair vrajabhū-kiśorīr*  
*mad-dāsikā viracaya priya rāsa-goṣṭhyāṁ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One day, joyful with various amorous pastimes, smiling Rādhā said to Kṛṣṇa, “Beloved! Attracting the youthful girls of Vraja, my servants, by the sound of your flute, produce a gathering for the rāsa dance.”**

*atha gāyati mādhave bhujaṁ dayitāṁse vinidhāya veṇunā\|*  
*paramādbhuta-darśanocchalat-kutukena priyam āha rādhikā \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**As Kṛṣṇa sang on his flute, Rādhā, very curious to see something most astonishing, placed her arm on his shoulder and spoke to him.**

## First song

*lalita rāgena gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in lalita-rāga.**

*na vahati sarid api sahaja-javena\|*  
*sthagati śaśī divi nija-vibhavena\|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The river does not flow at its normal speed. The moon stops in the sky by its own power.**

*priya-gāna-rase tava veṇunā \|\|dhru\|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**It is because of your flute, with its dear, sweet song.**

*dravamaya-vapur iha dhṛtam upalena\|*  
*janayati vismayam atikaṭhinena \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The rocks have become liquid. This is astonishing since the rocks were very hard.**

*sakala-bhuvanam idam amṛta sareṇa \|*  
*bhavati bhavitam iva madhura-tareṇa \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The whole world has become filled with the sweetness of the essence of nectar.**

*tava pada-sarasija-kṛta-bhavanena \|*  
*calati gṛhaṁ na hi yuvati-janena \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By the love created by your lotus feet, the young girls cannot go home.**

*dhṛta-tṛṇa-kavala-mukula-nayanena \|*  
*lasati vanaṁ tava surabhi-gaṇena\|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The forest shines with your surabhi cows which hold the fresh grass in their mouths without swallowing and which close their eyes.**

*visṛjati kala-kalam atula-rasena \|*  
*pramada-khagāvalir alam alasena \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The flocks of intoxicated birds chirp softly and languidly.**

*sthira-caram iha bhavati ca kalanena\|*  
*parama-sukhāmṛta-hṛdi milanena \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Moving and non-moving entities experience the highest, sweet happiness in their hearts by hearing the sound of the flute.**

*parapada-rata-munir anutapena \|*  
*bhavati kṛtī tava pada-nayanena \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sages absorbed in Brahman with suffering become successful by being led to your feet.**

*mudita-sarasvatī-gīta-mukhena \|*  
*viśata mahimni hareḥ sva-sukhena \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By uttering the song of joyful Prabodhānanda, enter joyfully into the glories of the Lord.**

*anyonyābaddha-hastair adhikṛta-valayair gopa-bālā-kadambaiḥ*  
*kālindīye virājat-suvipula-puline manda-sad-gandha-vāhe \|*  
*ekaikasyāḥ sa eko ’py aticaturatayā kaṇṭha-saṁśleṣi-bāhur*  
*madhye mugdhaḥ sa rādhā-rati-rabhasa-paras tatra rāse nanartta\|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the shining wide bank of the Yamunā, with a soft, perfumed breeze, while a group of gopīs formed a circle while linking their hands together, Kṛṣṇa by himself, most joyful, with passionate love for Rādhā, very skilfully embracing each of their necks with his arms by placing himself between them, performed the rāsadance.**

*ekā gopī rādhikā-sakhya-hīnā*  
*bhartrā ruddhā tat-kṣaṇojṛmbhitārtiḥ\|*  
*siddhā rādhā-mādhava-dhyāna-yogād*  
*rāse dadhau nirgatāthaivam ūce \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One gopī, not one of Rādhā’s sakhīs, was stopped by her husband and at that time felt great pain. By meditating on Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, she became perfect, and was able to leave the house and join the rāsa dance. She spoke as follows.**

## Second song

*athā vasantarāgeṇa gīyate\|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in vasanta-rāga.**

*vādayate maṇi-veṇum udāraṁ \|*  
*galita-madhura-rava-nava-rasa-sāram\|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He plays the fine jewelled flute, flowing with the sweet sound of the essence of new rasa.**

*ṇrtyati harir iha mohana-rāse \|*  
*rasika-yuvati-tati-racita-vilāse \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa dances in the enchanting rāsa circle, enjoying pastimes with the group of rasika girls.**

*darśayate bahu-vidha-hastaka-bhedaṁ \|*  
*calati lalita-gati-citram akhedaṁ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He shows a variety of hand movements and moves about with graceful, untiring steps.**

*madhya-vilamvita-druta-pada-cālaṁ \|*  
*kalayati gīta-padocita tālaṁ \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He claps the beat according to the meter of the song, at medium, slow and fast speeds.**

*ratna-khacita-paṭṭa-valaya-paṭīraṁ \|*  
*bhramayati nava-ghana-śyāma-śarīraṁ \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He twirls his body dark as a rain cloud, a Cupid in jewel studded cloth and bracelets.**

*saha-nartaka-rādhā-mukha-bimbam \|*  
*cumbati cāru-racita-parirambhaṁ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He kisses the face of Rādhā while she dances, and gracefully embraces her.**

*lalitārpita-tāmbula-karpūraṁ \|*  
*rasayati vihita-priya-mukha-pūraṁ\|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has Rādhā enjoy betel nut with camphor offered by Lalitā, placing it in her mouth.**

*gīta-vāditra kalā-gata-pāraṁ \|*  
*kim api praśaṁsati vara-tanuvāraṁ \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He praises the excellent gopīs who sing and play instruments with the highest excellence.**

*madhura-sarasvati-gītam udāraṁ \|*  
*gaṇaya rasika-jana hari-rasa-sāraṁ \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O rasikas! Please value the sweet, noble song of Prabodhānanda, which is the essence of Kṛṣṇa’s rasa.**

*tathāhi ślokaḥ\|*

**Перевод:**

**There is a verse on this.**

*hṛtvā kuñcuka-mauktikaṁ khara-nakhaiḥ kasyāścid ucca-stanaṁ*  
*cumban kāmapi saṁsvajann api parāṁ nīvīṁ parasyā haran \|*  
*nītvaikām api maṇḍalād bahir aho tanvan vicitrāṁ ratiṁ*  
*rādhā-saurata unmado vijayate rāse rasa-jño hariḥ \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With his rough nails, Kṛṣṇa subdued the high breasts of one gopī covered with a pearl studded bodice. He kissed another, and embraced another. He pulled off the skirt of another. Leading another gopī away from the circle, he enjoyed various amorous pastimes with her. The knower of rasa, Kṛṣṇa, impassioned and enjoying with Rādhā, remains victorious.**

*tataḥ smera-mukhau rādhā-mādhavāv abhivīkṣya tāḥ\|*  
*praviśya maṇḍale tasmin sahasā majjatāṁ rase \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa who were smiling, after gazing at the gopīs, suddenly entered the circle and merged into the rasa.**

*sthānūn aṅkurayan mahājava-sarit-srotāṁsi saṁstambhayan*  
*ābhīrī-dadhi-manthanaṁ vikalayan grāvāvalīṁ drāvayan\|*  
*siñcan prema-rasair diśaḥ kula-vadhū-nīvīñ ca viśraṁsayan*  
*govindasya kavīndra-vismaya-karo vaṁśī-ravaḥ pātu vaḥ \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the sound of the flute, which makes trees sprout, makes flowing rivers stop, interrupts the cowherd women churning yogurt, melts stone, sprinkles the directions with the rasa of prema, loosens the skirts of the cultured women, and amazes the king of poets, protect you.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta-mādhave rāsa-vilāso nāma dvādaśa sargaḥ*

## Chapter 13 - Miserable Mādhava

## Introduction

*tadṛṅ nartana-gāna-matta-yuvatī-sammaṇḍalān mādhavo*  
*niṣkrāntaḥ saha kāntayā druta-padaṁ durgaṁ praviṣṭo vanaṁ \|*  
*rādhāyāḥ kṛpayāti-mugdha-dayitā kācin mudāsīt tayoḥ*  
*kiñcid dūrata eva vīkṣya lalitaṁ dhanyā nilīnānvagāt \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With his beloved, Kṛṣṇa left the circle of girls absorbed in dancing and singing in this way and quickly entered an impenetrable forest. One beloved, joyful, innocent gopī by Rādhā’s mercy was present. Seeing the couple from a little distance, the fortunate gopī followed them quietly while not being seen.**

*āsīnau kva ca kutracic ca śayitau kutrāpi puṣpoccayaṁ*  
*cinvānau suratotsavāya ruciraṁ kuñjaṁ praviṣṭau kvacit \|*  
*anyonyāṁsa-valad-bhujau sahasitau yatau kvacil līlayā*  
*śrī-rādhā-madhusūdanau rasanidhī sā kāpi dhānyānvagat \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes they sat, sometime they lay down and sometime picked clusters of flowers. Sometimes they entered an attractive bower for enjoying amorous pastimes. Placing their arms on each other’s shoulders, they walked playfully while laughing. The fortunate gopī followed these two oceans of rasa.**

*tatra kṣaṇaṁ kīra-gaṇeritāni priyāṇi rādhā-caritāni śṛṇvan \|*  
*yadā harir mīlita-locano ’bhūt tadaiva rādhā kutukāt tirodhāt \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Listening for a moment to the parrots praising Rādhā’s qualities, Kṛṣṇa closed his eyes. At that moment, Rādhā, for fun, disappeared.**

*atho gatāyāṁ lalitaṁ hasantyāṁ \| latāntare mādhava-jīvitāyāṁ \|*  
*nimeṣa-mātraṁ kalayan anantaṁ\| kalpaṁ harir vyākula evam ūce \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the life of Kṛṣṇa, Rādhā, hid among the creepers while laughing, Kṛṣṇa, taking a moment to be an endless kalpa, spoke thus in his suffering condition.**

## First song

*deśavarāḍī rāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in deśa-varāḍi-rāga.**

*tava lalita-kuṇḍalaṁ vidhūta-vidhu-*  
*maṇḍalaṁ cāru-mukham amṛta-nidhi-sāraṁ*  
*smarati mama mānasaṁ kim api*  
*rati-lālasaṁ syandi-mṛdu-hasita-madhu-dhāraṁ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**My mind, eager for love, remembers your attractive trembling moon-like face with graceful earrings, the essence of a sweet ocean, a stream of flowing honey with a soft smile.**

*priye kvāsi rādhe dehi mayi kām api śubha-dṛṣṭiṁ \|*  
*tava nimeṣa-kautuke kirati mayi dāruṇo viṣama-śara ugra-śara-vṛṣṭiṁ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Dear Rādhā! Give me just one auspicious sight of you. With your momentary fun, cruel Cupid showers his fierce arrows on me. (chorus)**

*sphurati tava locanaṁ kamala-mada-*  
*mocanaṁ prema-rasa-laharī-kṛta-dolaṁ\|*  
*kalita-nava-kuntalaṁ calad alaka-*  
*saṁkulaṁ bhāti mama tad api su-kapolam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**My mind reveals your eyes which defeat the pride of the lotus and sways in the waves of prema’s rasa. My mind reveals your fresh locks of hair swaying on your beautiful cheeks.**

*dehi tava darśanaṁ rakṣa mama jīvanaṁ*  
*candra-mukhi kalaya nija-dāsaṁ \|*  
*mayi parama-kātare prakṛti-mṛdulāntare*  
*kalpayasi kati nu parihāsaṁ\|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Show yourself and save my life. O moon-faced Rādhā! See your servant. How many jokes will you play on me, most miserable and naturally soft in heart?**

*kṣaṇa-viraha-duḥkhato mama bhavati*  
*vikṣato dhairya-girir atanu-jaya-keto \|*  
*nava-kanaka-campaka-prakara-ruci-*  
*kampaka śrīla-tanu-sakala-sukha-heto \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The mountain of my steadiness has been wounded by suffering momentary separation. O flag of Cupid! O Rādhā, who makes the beauty of a host of golden campaka’s tremble, whose body is the source of all my happiness!**

*milati kaṭhināyase mayi parama-sadvaśe*  
*pīna-ghana-kaṭhina-kuca-bhāre \|*  
*vivṛṇu hasad-ānanaṁ dīpta-nava-kānanaṁ*  
*kaṇṭha-taṭa-lulita-maṇi-hāre \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Rādhā endowed with hard, full breasts! O Rādhā, with a jewel necklace hanging on your throat! Show your smiling face, which illuminates the fresh forest. You are very harsh with me, dependent on you, when I come to meet you.**

*mama manasi nāparā tava tu guṇa-*  
*tatparā kira-vara-tatibhir upagītā \|*  
*kṣaṇam aramātātra me hṛdayam ati-*  
*vibhrame tatra kim nu bhava viparītā \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Your qualities were glorified by the best parrots. At that time, my heart enjoyed for a moment. There was no one else in my mind. O bewilderer! Why do you oppose me?**

*kalaya vara-candrakin rucira-ruru-śāva*  
*he tvam eva mama mitra bhava sākṣī \|*  
*tyajati bata jīvanaṁ harir atula-yauvanaṁ*  
*nādya yadi milati hariṇākṣī \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O excellent peacock! O attractive fawn! Look! You are my friends. Be my witness. If Kṛṣṇa does not meet doe-eyed Rādhā today, he will give up his incomparable youthful life.**

*iti vadati rādhikā madhujiti rasādhikā prādurbhavati madhura-līlā \|*  
*vetti na sarasvatiḥ kim api paramaṁ tato buddhir iha bhavati śubha-śīlā \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When he said this, Rādhā, filled with additional rasa and sweet pastimes, appeared before him. Prabodhānanda does not know anything beyond this. The intelligence fixed in the pastimes becomes most auspicious.**

*yaṁ vikretum api kṣamāsi kṛpayā yo’yaṁ nijāṅke kṛto*  
*jāgrat-svpna-suṣuptiṣu tvayi rato jānāti yo nāparām \|*  
*so ’yam te mṛdyulāṅgi karkaśa-hṛdi nyastāṅghri-paṅkeruho*  
*lokānāṁ ca nimeṣayoḥ kṣaṇam api kṣobhyāya pakṣīyati \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O tender-limbed Rādhā! He whom you can sell and who has placed you on his lap, who, absorbed in you, does not think of anyone else in waking, dreaming or deep sleep states, placing his lotus feet on your hard heart, considers the moment of blinking of his eyes when seeing you to be a long time.**

*yad devarṣi-śukādi-mṛgyam anīśaṁ yad vāñchanīyaṁ śriyā*  
*kṛṣtā yena vighūrṇitāḥ priya-gaṇā mādyanti mattā iva \|*  
*śambhur yan mṛgayan sukhaṁ ca sakalaṁ tyaktābhavad-bhikṣuko*  
*rādhā-mādhavayos tad-adbhuta-rasa-premā ciraṁ pātu vaḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the prema of astonishing rasa of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, which is sought by Nārada, Śuka and other sages, which is desired by Lakṣmī, by which your associates are attracted and made dizzy, becoming intoxicated like the insane, whose happiness Śiva also seeks, giving up everything and becoming beggar, protect you for a long time.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta-mādhave vidhura-mādhavo nāma trayodaśa sargaḥ*

## Chapter 14 - Pastimes of Rādhā and Mādhava

## Introduction

*artha viraha-vihastās tāḥ samudvīkṣya bhūyaḥ*  
*parama-dayita-rādhā-mādhavau kīrtayantaḥ \|*  
*tad atikaruṇa-dhāmā-dvandvam ānanda-kandaṁ*  
*rasamayam upalabhya prema-magnā babhūvuḥ\|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Helpless in separation, the gopīs searched and then glorified most dear Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. Finding the blissful, most merciful couple full of rasa, they became immersed in prema.**

*kāścin mauktika-mālya-kajjala-varālaṅgkāra-kāśmīrajā*  
*lepālakta-kañcukaiḥ priyatamāṁ rādhāṁ mudā maṇḍayan \|*  
*guñjāharāvataṁsa-piñcha-mukuṭa-srag-gandha-pītāmbarair*  
*anyāḥ sādhu hariṁ prasādhya dayitā santoṣayāṁ cakrire \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some of the gopīs joyfully decorated dear Rādhā with pearl necklaces, collyirum, the best ornaments, kuṅkuma, unguents, lac and bodice. Others decorated Kṛṣṇa nicely with guñja beads, peacock feathered crown, garlands, perfumes and yellow cloth and satisfied him.**

*evaṁ nikuñja-nilaye mṛdulaṁ tadānīm*  
*āstīrya puṣpam atha tatra harau niviṣṭe \|*  
*prāṇeśvarīṁ samupaveśya ca tasya pārśve*  
*yātā bahir mumudire priyayor vilāsaiḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When they spread soft flowers in the bower house and had Kṛṣṇa enter, making Rādhā sit by his side, they then went outside and became overjoyed by the pastimes of the dear couple.**

*rādhā-govinda-nirdvandva-rasānandābdhi-magna-dhīḥ \|*  
*sakhy ekā kathayan modād anyābhyo bhāratīṁ varaṁ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her mind absorbed in the blissful ocean of the union of Rādhā and Govinda, one gopī spoke excellent words to the others in bliss.**

## First song

*vibhāṣa rāgeṇa gīyate\|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in vibhāṣa-rāga.**

*karam avalambya sakhī-nivahe viniveśya latāgṛha-koṭare \|*  
*nava-saṅgama-bhaya-hrī-valitām bahir api yāte priya-gocare\|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Led by his hand into the creeper house, she was fearful and shy at the new meeting, when her group of friends left. He gazed at her.**

*nimamajja sā rasa-sāgare krīḍati vraja-nava-nāgare\|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**As the youthful lover played, she became immersed in an ocean of rasa.**

*pṛcchati kim api kim api kṛta-maunāṁ tadvan āmṛta-lālase \|*  
*nayati balād iva talpam analpaka-keli-kalākula-mānase \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He asked her questions but she was silent. Desiring sweetness, he brought her by force to the bed, his mind overwhelmed with many arts of amorous play.**

*nava-kañcukam avamucya pṛthu-stana-*  
*kṛta-khara-nakha-śikharāṅkane \|*  
*śliṣyati gāḍhataraṁ smara-taralita-*  
*rava-kṛta-vara-kara-kaṅkane\|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He took off her new bodice, scratched her broad breasts with his sharp finger nails, and embraced her tightly, while the bracelets on her fine hands quivered out of passion.**

*pibati tathādhara-sīdhu-rasāmṛtam atha bahu-vidha-kṛta-cumbane\|*  
*nīvi-nihita-kara ākula-dayitā-vihita-jhaṭīty-avalambane \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He drank the sweet nectar of her lips by kissing her in many ways. When his hand touched her skirt, she panicked and quickly held his hand.**

*matta-karīndra-tumula-rati-saṅgara-racita-mahādbhuta-vikrame \|*  
*khidyati mīlati prāṇa-samorasi śiśira-bhāva-kṛta-viśrame \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After valiant, astonishing feats in the tumultuous war of love of the intoxicated elephant, he became tired, closed his eyes, relaxed on the cool chest of his beloved who was equal to his life.**

*rati-rasa-matta-manohara-nāgarī-kāma-kalā-kṛta-toṣaṇe \|*  
*vikasita-kamala-kalinda-sutāpluta-manda-pavana-rasa-poṣaṇe \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He was satisfied with the amorous acts of Rādhā, attractive with intoxication of mādhurya-rasa. His pleasure was nourished by a gentle breeze filled with fragrance from blossoming lotuses of the Yamunā.**

*srasta-śikhaṇḍa-mukuṭa-parikhaṇḍita-mālya-mṛditatara-citrake \|*  
*tām api bhūṣayati praṇayena suviracita-kuṅkuma-patrake \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**While his peacock feather crown fell off. His garland was broken and his tilāka was smeared, he decorated Rādhā affectionately with expert designs of kuṅkuma.**

*rādhā-pada-rati-mugdha-sarasvati-bhāṣita-hari-rasa-vaibhave \|*  
*kuru hṛdayaṁ parabhāva-vibhāvitam mā pata nigama-kaitave \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Absorb your mind, full of the couple’s love for each other, in the wealth of rasa of Kṛṣṇa, who is joyful with attraction to Rādhā’s feet, as described by Prabodhānanda. Do not be contaminated with the cheating of the Vedas.**

*tathāhi ślokaḥ \|*

**Перевод:**

**There is a verse concerning this.**

*nāsvāded yanmatir api sakṛd eva sarvārthasāro*  
*dārā rādhā-madhupati-padāmbhoja-mādhvīka-dhārā \|*  
*yan netrābhyām api na kalitās tan-madānaṅga-khelāḥ*  
*kuñje kuñje satatam iha taj-jīvitanādyaṁ dhig astu\|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The life of a person whose mind does not taste even once the fine essence of all goals, the sweet streams flowing from the lotus feet of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa and whose eyes do not see their pastimes of intoxicated love in the many bowers, is useless at all times.**

*tattvam icchasi cen mugdhe rasa-sindhāv agāhanaṁ \|*  
*mahā-premotsavenaiva bhaja rādhā-padāmbujaṁ\|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If you desire truly to plunge into the ocean of rasa, worship the lotus feet of Rādhā, with the joy of great prema.**

*sarvaiḥ prema-rasāmṛtaiḥ sughaṭitā nūnaṁ vraja-strī-ghaṭā*  
*sā yac chrī-caraṇa- phuran-nakha-maṇi-jyotiḥ-kalāṁśāṁśakā \|*  
*yad govinda-bhujāntare ratikalā-raṅgeṇa dolāyitaṁ*  
*tad-vastu śrutī-mastakair akalitaṁ rādhābhidhaṁ pātu vaḥ\|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The assembly of gopīs, endowed with all types of sweet rasa of prema, are portions of portions of portions of the light from the gleaming toe nails of Rādhā’s feet. May that entity Rādhā, unknown to the Upaniṣads, who sways in the arms of Govinda with artful pastimes of love, protect you.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta-mādhave rādhā-mādhava-vilāso nāma caturdaśa sargaḥ*

## Chapter 15 - Description of one’s own joy

## Introduction

*akhila-bhuvana-sāraṁ prema-sarvasva-bhāraṁ*  
*nava-madana-vikāraṁ saukhya-sindhu pracāraṁ \|*  
*madhura-rasa-vihāraṁ rādhikā-kaṇṭha-hāraṁ*  
*praṇamata sukumāraṁ śrī-yaśodā-kumāraṁ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Please offer respect to the young boy, the son of Yāśodā, who is the essence of the whole world, the load of ultimate prema, a transformation of new love, who spreads an ocean of happiness, who performs pastimes of madhura-rasa, who is necklace on Rādhā’s neck.**

*śrī-vṛndāvana-vipinendra-jīvaśaktiḥ*  
*sat-premojjvala-rasa-sāra-kalpa-vallī \|*  
*sā rādhā satata-mahā-manoja-bādhā*  
*saṁjīyān madhura-vayo-vilāsaśīlāḥ\|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May Rādhā, the śakti of Kṛṣṇa’s life, the desire creeper of the essence of pure mādhurya-rasa, who is always tormented by great and constant love, who performs pastimes of sweet youth, remain victorious.**

## First song

*gurjjarī-rāgeṇa gyate\|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in gurjarī-rāga.**

*pravahad amṛta-rasa-kṛta-parihāse\| śiva-vidhi-śuka-nārada-janitāśe \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Enter the pastimes of joking caused by the flow of sweet rasa, for which Śiva, Brahmā, Śuka and Nārada aspire.**

*rādhā-mādhava-keli-vilāse \|*  
*viśatu mano mama prema-vikāśe \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O my mind! Enter into the pastimes of Rādhā and Mādhava, flowering with prema.**

*vṛndāvana sahaja-nivāse \|*  
*nava-rasa-bhāvita-mati-pratibhāse \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Enter the pastimes whose natural abode is Vṛndāvana, manifested in the mind infused with fresh rasa.**

*tikta-tarīkṛta-mukti-vitāne*  
*\| hari-caraṇojjvala-bhāva-nidāne \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Enter the pastimes which make liberation taste bitter, which are the abode of mādhurya-rasa for Kṛṣṇa’s feet.**

*pratipada-jṛmbhita-madana-vikāre \|*  
*parama-rasāmṛta-sāgara-sāre \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Enter the pastimes which at every moment manifest transformations of love, which are the essence of the sweet ocean of the highest rasa.**

*thutkṛta-vidhi-pada-muni-vara-gīte \|*  
*tuccha-tri-guṇa-jaḍa-prakṛtim atīte \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Enter the pastimes which the best of sages glorify while making Brahmā’s post insignificant and which are beyond insignificant prakṛti with three guṇas.**

*rādhā-priya-parijana-kalanīye \|*  
*suvidita-nigama-samvaraṇīye \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Enter the pastimes enacted by Rādhā’s dear associates, which are hidden to the well-known scriptures.**

*antarahita-mahad-akalita-pāre \|*  
*bahu-vidha-bhaṅgima-prema-prasāre \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Enter the pastimes which are beyond uncountable, unending universes and which are spread with many types of crooked prema.**

*tirasa-lola-sarasvati-gāne \|*  
*kuruta hṛdayam iha parama-nidhāne \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Concentrate your minds on the highest treasure, the song of Prabodhānanda who is greedy for great rasa.**

*dagdho bhīma-bhavāṭavī-bhramaṇato duḥkhaugha-dāvānalair*  
*ādāya priya-mugdha-vṛtti-kariṇīḥ sadvartma-go jīvitaḥ \|*  
*sāndrānanda-rasātiśītalatare tāpa-trayonmūlane*  
*rādhā-keli-sudhāmbudhau mama mano mattaḥ karī majjatu \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the mad elephant of my mind, burned by the forest fire of great suffering after wandering in the forest of terrible existence, and accepting female elephants of infatuated actions, but which remains alive by following the path of devotees, become submerged in the sweet ocean of Rādhā’s pastimes, cooling with the rasa full of condensed bliss, which uproot the three miseries.**

## Second song

*śrī-rāgeṇa gīyate \|*

**Перевод:**

**The song is sung in śrī-rāga**

*vṛndāraṇya-carācara-vṛndaṁ \|*  
*śrayata mahārasa-vaibhava-kandaṁ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Take shelter of the moving and non-moving beings of Vṛndāvana, a massive wealth of great rasa.**

*gāyata rādhā-mādhava-līlām \|*  
*kuruta matiṁ rasa-rañjita-śīlāṁ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sing the pastimes of Rādhā and Mādhava. Concentrate on actions permeated with rasa.**

*paśyata rādhā-keli-nikuñjaṁ \|*  
*prakaṭa-mahādbhūta-rati-rasa-puñjam \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**See the bower of Rādhā’s pastimes, manifesting an astonishing wealth of mādhurya-rasa.**

*carata vaikuṇṭhād api ramaṇīye\|*  
*vraja-valaye śiva-vidhi-kamanīye \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Go around Vṛndāvana, more pleasing than Vaikuṇṭha, and which is desired by Śiva and Brahmā.**

*puline puline tapana-sutāyāḥ \|*  
*bhramata na yatra prasarati māyā \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Wander from bank to bank of the Yamunā, where māyā does not extend.**

*paramānanda-rasāmbudhi-sāre\|*  
*nayata mano vraja-rāja-kumāre \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lead the mind to Nanda’s son, the essence of the ocean of rasa of the highest bliss.**

*muñcata viṣama viṣaya-rasa-gandhaṁ \|*  
*ghaṭayata hari-pade dṛḍha-rati-bandhaṁ\|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Free yourself from any tinge of contrary material rasa. Tie yourself with the ropes of strong attachment to Kṛṣṇa’s feet.**

*mṛgata rādhā-pada-rasa-bhājaṁ \|*  
*paricinotojjvala-nava-rasa-rājaṁ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Search for the receptacle of rasa at Rādhā’s feet. Recognize the king of fresh rasa, mādhurya-rasa.**

*iti hita-sāra-sarasvati-gītaṁ \|*  
*janayatu kañcana bhāvam adhītaṁ \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May this glorified song of Prabodhānanda which establishes the essence produce bhāva.**

*dūrān muñca varāṅganādi-viṣayān karmādi-mārge vṛthā*  
*khedaṁ na prathayendrajāla-sadṛśī viddhīha siddhīr api \|*  
*muktiṁ tiktarāṁ ca thutkuru paraṁ vṛndāvanendraṁ bhaja*

**Перевод:**

**Free yourselves from the material objects such as beautiful women. Do not increase your useless suffering on the path of karma. Know that the siddhis are like magical illusions. Spit on bitter liberation. Rather, worship the king of Vṛndāvana. Devote yourself to being a follower of Rādhā. In that there is some rasa.**

*yā pūrvvaṁ milanād babhūva vimukhī bhāvādi-kākkādikā*  
*yāyā nidtrita-cauravan militayoḥ sadhvāntarātrāvabhūt \|*

**Перевод:**

**May the waves of pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, in which, previous to meeting, Rādhā showed aversion and contempt, by which, like thieves when others are sleeping, their meeting seemed improper, and which flowed within the attractive bowers of Vṛndāvana and in the waves of the Yamunā, protect you for a long time.**

*iti śrī saṅgīta mādhave nijollāsa-varṇanaṁ nāma pañcadaśaḥ sargaḥ*

## Chapter 16 - Conclusion

*asraughair makaranda-bindu-nivahair nisyandibhiḥ sundaraṁ*  
*netrendvīvaram ādadhat supulakotkampaṁ ca bibhrad-vapuḥ \|*  
*vācaś cāpi sa-gadagadā hari-harītyānandinīr udgiran*  
*premānanda-rasotsavaṁ diśatu vo devaḥ śacī-nandanaḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His body trembling with hairs standing on end and his eyes like blue lotuses flowing with tears like drops of honey, uttering in bliss the words “Hari Hari,” in a choked voice, may the Lord, the son of Śacī, bestow to you a festival of the blissful rasa of prema.**

*tat-tad-doṣa-pravala-garalair arthataḥ śabdato vā*  
*kāmaṁ naṣṭāṁ kṛtim ati-śubhāṁ kurvatāṁ ye dvi-jihvāḥ \|*  
*gāḍhaṁ magnāḥ parama-rasa-de rādhikā-keli-sindhau*  
*jāta-snehāḥ stutibhir amṛtair jīvayiṣyanti bhūyaḥ\|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Words born of affection, submerged deeply in the ocean of Rādhā’s pastimes, which bestow the supreme rasa, and which snakes may destroy by the strong poison of pointing out various faults in meaning or words, will return to life by sweet praises.**

*alaṅkuryur alaṅkāraiḥ premnā dadyur manaḥ kvacit \|*  
*kaṇṭhe kurvantu rasikāḥ kṛtiṁ mugdhām imāṁ mama \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the rasikas ornament this charming work of mine with literary ornaments, concentrate their minds on it with prema, and place it on their throats.**

*śrī-rādhikā-mādhavayoḥ padāmbuje*  
*nirmāya yat kāvyam idaṁ mayārpitaṁ \|*  
*tenaiva tau prīta-hṛdau kadāpi mām*  
*adbhutaṁ darśayatāṁ rahaḥ kalām\|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By this poetic work made by me and offered to the lotus feet of Rādhā and Mādhava, may the couple, pleased in their hearts, show to me their astounding private arts.**

*iti prema-rasādhi-devatānucarī bubhūṣatā kenāpi prkaṭīkṛtaṁ \|*

**Перевод:**

**This work was manifested by a person desiring to be the intimate servant of presiding deity of prema-rasa.**

*iti śrī prabodhānanda sarasvati-gosvāminā viracitaṁ*  
*śrī saṅgīta-mādhavākhyaṁ nāma gītikāvya samāptaṁ\|*

## About Śrīla Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī

Śrīla Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī was a great Vaiṣṇava devotee of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu. While traveling in southern India, Caitanya Mahāprabhu met Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī, who had two brothers, Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa and Tirumalaya Bhaṭṭa, who were Vaiṣṇavas of the Rāmānuja-sampradāya. Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī was the nephew of Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī. Empowered by Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu, he taught Śrīmad Bhāgavatam and other devotional works to Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī. His eloquent speech would engladden everyone.

From historical records it is found that Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu traveled in South India in the year 1433 Śakābda (A.D. 1511) during the Cāturmāsya period, and it was at that time that He met Prabodhānanda.

Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī serves Śrī Rādhā in Goloka Vṛndāvana as Tuṅgāvidyā-gopī, one of Her aṣṭa-sakhīs.

Works of Śrīla Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī

His completed works are:

- Aścarya-rāsa-prabandhah
- Caitanya-candrāmṛta
- Commentary on Gīta Govinda
- Kāma-gāyatrī-vyakhya
- Navadvīpa-śataka
- Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi
- Saṅgīta-mādhava
- Śruti-stuti-vākya
- Viveka-śataka
- Vṛndāvana-mahimāmṛta
