# Saṣjagau Veṇunā Devakī-nandanaḥ - except

## Sañjagau Veṇunā Devakī-nandanaḥ - except

*1. aṅganām aṅganām antare mādhavo*  
*mādhavaṁ mādhavaṁ cāntareṇāṅganāḥ*  
*ittham ākalpite maṇḍale madhya-gaḥ*  
*sañjagau veṇunā devakī-nandanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“Lord Mādhava was situated between each pair of gopīs, and a gopī was situated between each pair of His manifestations. And Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the son of Devakī, also appeared in the middle of the circle, playing upon His flute and singing.”**

*36. keki-kekādṛtāneka-paṅkeruhā-*  
*līna-haṁsāvalī-hṛdyatā-hṛdyatā*  
*kaṁsa-vaṁśātavī-dāha-dāvānalaḥ*  
*sañjagau veṇunā devakī-nandanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“Devakīnandana is the forest fire who burned to ashes the bamboo forest of Kaṁsa and his dynasty. As He played upon His flute, He brought extreme delight to the many swans sporting among the lotuses, and the peacocks greeted the sound with their calls of** ***kekā, kekā.*****”**

*37. kvā ’pi vīṇābhir ārāviṇā kampitaḥ*  
*kvā ’pi vīṇābhir ākiṅkiṇī-nartitaḥ*  
*kvā ’pi vīṇābhir āmantaraṁ gāpitaḥ*  
*sañjagau veṇunā devakī-nandanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“When the vibrations of the gopis’ vinas caused Him to tremble in ecstasy, when those vinas inspired Him to dance with His tinkling ankle bells, and when the vinas inspired Him to sing tenderly, Devakinandana played upon His flute.”**

*38. cāru-candrāvalī-locanaiś cumbito*  
*gopa-go-vṛnda-gopālikā-vallabhaḥ*  
*vallavī-vṛnda-vṛndārakaḥ kāmukaḥ*  
*sañjagau veṇunā devakī-nandanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“Devakīnandana is the lover and leader of all the many gopīs, and they love Him as well, as do all the cows and cowherds. As the gopīs kiss Him with their eyes, as with a row of lovely moons, He plays upon His flute.”**

*39. mauli-mālā-milan-matta-bhṛṅgī-latā-*  
*bhīta-bhīta-priyā-vibhramāliṅgitaḥ*  
*srasta-gopī-kucābhoga-sammelitaḥ*  
*sañjagau veṇunā devakī-nandanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“Embraced in a flutter of confusion by the passionate** ***gopīs*****, who were afraid of the swarm of maddened bees buzzing around His chaplet, and pressed close to their uncovered full breasts, Devakīnanda played upon His flute.”**

*40. cāru-cāmīkarābhāsa-bhāmā-vibhur*  
*vaijayantī-latābhāsitoraḥ-sthalaḥ*  
*nanda-vṛndāvane vāsitā madhyagaḥ*  
*sañjagau veṇunā devakī-nandanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“In the middle of the women who live in Nanda’s village of Vṛndāvana, His chest glowing with the Vaijayantī garland, Devīkananda, the Lord of the passionate, gold-complexioned Rādhā, played upon His flute.”**

*41. bālikā-tālikā-tāla-līlālayā-*  
*saṅga-sandarśita-bhrū-latā-vibhramaḥ*  
*gopikā-gīta-dattāvadhānaḥ svayaṁ*  
*sañjagau veṇunā devakī-nandanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“With His dancing eyebrows moving to the beat playfully clapped out by the young** ***gopīs*****, and paying close attention to their singing, Devakīnandana played upon His flute.”**

*42. pārijātaṁ samuddhṛtya rādhāvaro*  
*ropayām āsa bhāmā-gṛhasyāṅgane*  
*śīta-śīte vaṭe yāmunīye taṭe*  
*sañjagau veṇunā devakī-nandanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**“Rādhā’s lover uprooted Indra’s Pārijāta tree and planted it in the courtyard of Satyabhāmā’s home. \[Satyabhāmā is Rādhā reappearing in Dvārakā.\] On the bank of the Yamunā, under a cooling banyan tree, that Devakīnandana plays the flute.”**
