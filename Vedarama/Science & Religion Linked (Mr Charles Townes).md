# Science & Religion Linked (Mr Charles Townes)

## Link exists between science, religion

Deccan Herald, Sunday, January 12, 2003

DH News Service BANGALORE, Jan 11

Nobel Laureate and inventor of laser, Mr Charles Townes felt that there is a "necessary connection" between science and religion and the two will ultimately integrate in our quest to answer the mysteries of the universe.

In his talk on "A scientist’s view of the parallelism and ultimate convergence of science and religion" organised on Friday as a part of the Science and the Spiritual Quest-II Programme by National Institute of Advanced Studies (NIAS) and the John Templeton Foundation, Dr Townes said both religion and science rely on "faith" in different terminologies. Science refers to faith as assumptions, he said pointing out that while quantitatively, they look different, qualitatively they are the same.

Dr Townes said science looks at the structure of the universe and how it works, whereas religion’s purpose is to find out the meaning of life in the universe. "There is a necessary connection between purpose and structure," he said.

Dr Townes, who won the Nobel Prize in Physics for his path breaking discovery of lasers, said scientists the world over are increasingly recognising what is called "the intelligent design" of the universe. "There is something special in this universe. The laws of physics are precisely what they are for us to be here," he said and pointed out that even a small change in the physical constants would have made existence of life impossible.

"What controls all this, is there a special force ?", he questioned and stressed the need for having an open-mind. "Our religious and scientific views need not be static and we should accept the change when we come across new revelations", he added.
