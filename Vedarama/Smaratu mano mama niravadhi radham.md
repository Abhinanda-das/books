# Smaratu mano mama niravadhi radham

## Refrain

***smaratu mano mama niravadhi rādhām***  
***madhupati-rūpa-guṇa-śravaṇodita-sahaja-manobhava-bādhām***  
***(refrain)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*mama manaḥ* – my mind; *smaratu* – may remember; *niravadhi* – incessantly; *rādhām* – Śrī Rādhā; *bādhām* – the impediment; *manobhava* –of (the pangs of) Cupid; *sahaja* – spontaneously; *udita* – arises (in Her heart); *śravaṇa* – upon hearing; *guṇa* – about the qualities; *rūpa* – and beautiful form; *madhupati* – of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the master of *madhu* (honey-filled pastimes).

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā. The affliction of the pangs of Cupid spontaneously arises within Her heart upon hearing about the qualities and beautiful form of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the master of supremely sweet pastimes.**

### Text 1

***vara-sīmanta-rasāmṛta-saraṇī-***  
***dhṛta-sindūra-surekhām***  
***śrī-vṛṣabhānu-kulāmbudhi-sambhava-***  
***subhaga-sudhākara-lekhām (1)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*vara* – on the excellent; *sīmanta* – parting of Her hair; *amṛta* – (which is like the) nectarean; *saraṇī* – pathway; *rasa* – of *ujjvala-rasa*, sweet paramour love; *su-rekhām* – a sublime line; *sindūra* – of red vermil-lion; *dhṛta* – is kept; *sudhākara-lekhām* – She is the moon; *ambudhi* –(arising from) the ocean; *kula* – of the dynasty; *śrī-vṛṣabhānu* – of Śrī Vṛṣabhānu Mahārāja: *sambhava* – bringing; *subhaga* – auspiciousness (to the world).

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā, the fine parting of whose hair, which is like the nectarean pathway of** ***ujjvala-rasa*****, is decorated with a sublime line of** ***sindūra*****. Śrī Rādhā emerges from the dynasty of Śrī Vṛṣabhānu Mahārāja as the moon arises from the ocean, bringing auspiciousness to the world.**

### Text 2

***surucira-komala-kavarī-virājita-***  
***parimala-malli-sumālām***  
***mada-cala-khañjana-khelana-gañjana-***  
***locana-kamala-viśālām (2)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*sumālām* – exquisite garlands; *parimala* – of fragrant; *malli* – jasmine; *virājita* – shine; *surucira* – on Her very lustrous; *komala* – soft; *kavarī* – braid; *viśālām* – Her large; *komala* – lotus; *locana* – eyes; *gañjana* – mock; *mada* –the intoxicated; *khelana* – to-and-fro; *cala* – movement; *khañjana* – of the wagtail bird.

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā, upon whose lustrous and soft braid shine exquisite garlands of fragrant jasmine. The restlessness of Her large lotus eyes mocks the intoxicated to-and-fro movement of the wagtail bird.**

### Text 3

***mada-kari-rāja-virājad-anuttama-***  
***calita-lalita-gati-bhaṅgīm***  
***ati-sukumāra-kanaka-nava-campaka-***  
***gaura-madhura-madhurāṅgīm (3)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*lalita* – the graceful; *bhaṅgīm* – curves; *gati* – of Her gait; *calita* – as She strolls; *anuttama* – are incomparably more sublime; *virājat* – and splendid; *madakari-rāja* – than those of the king of elephants in rut; *aṅgīm* – Her body; *atisukumāra* – at the zenith of very sweet youth; *madhura-madhura* – imbued with ever-increasing charm; *gaura* –shines; *kanaka* – with a golden hue; *nava-campaka* – like fresh *campaka* flowers.

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā, the graceful curves of whose strolling gait are incomparably more sublime and splendid than those of the king of elephants in rut. Her body, being at the zenith of very sweet youth and imbued with ever-increasing charm, shines with a golden hue like fresh** ***campaka*** **flowers.**

### Text 4

***maṇi-keyūra-lalita-valayāvalī-***  
***maṇḍita-mṛdu-bhuja-vallīm***  
***pratipadam adbhuta-rūpa-camatkṛti-***  
***mohana-yuvati-matallīm (4)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*mṛdu* – Her soft; *lalita* – and graceful; *vallīm* – creeper-like; *bhuja* – arms; *maṇḍita* – are ornamented; *avalī* – by -bunches; *valaya* – of bangles; *maṇi* – and bejewelled; *keyūra* – armlets; *adbhuta* – and Her wonderful; *rūpa* –form; *pratipadam* – at every moment; *camatkṛti* – causes astonishment; *mohana* – and enrapture; *yuvati* – (even among) the young girls (of Vraja); *matallīm* – who possess such excellence.

**Перевод:**

**May my mind inces santly recall Śrī Rādhā, whose soft, graceful, creeper-like arms are bedecked with bunches of bangles and bejewelled armlets, and whose wonderful, sweet form at every moment provokes astonishment and enrapture even among the young maidens of Vraja, who are similarly endowed with such excellence.**

### Text 5

***mṛdu-mṛdu-hāsa-lalita-mukha-maṇḍala-***  
***kṛta-śaśi-bimba-viḍambām***  
***kiṅkini-jāla-khacita-pṛthu-sundara-***  
***nava-rasa-rāśi-nitambām (5)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*lalita* – Her lovely; *maṇḍala* – resplendent; *mukha* – face; *mṛdu-mṛdu* –(emanates) very, very gentle; *hāsa* – peals of laughter; *kṛta-viḍambām* – and mocks; *bimba* – the orb; *śaśi* – of the moon; *pṛthu* – on Her broad; *sundara* –beautiful; *nitambām* – rounded hips; *rāśi* – which are filled with; *nava-rasa* –ever-fresh mellows; *jāla* – a net; *kiṅkini* – of tiny bells; *khacita* – is fastened.

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā, the circle of whose lovely face emanates very, very gentle peals of laughter and mocks the orb of the full moon. A net of tiny bells is fastened upon Her charming, rounded hips, which are filled with ever-fresh mellows.**

### Text 6

***citrita-kañculikā-sthagitodbhaṭa-***  
***kuca-hāṭaka-ghaṭa-śobhām***  
***sphurat-aruṇādhara-svādu-sudhā-rasa-***  
***kṛta-hari-mānasa-lobhām (6)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*śobhām* – the beauty; *udbhaṭa* – of Her excellent; *kuca* – breasts; *hāṭaka* –(which resemble) golden; *ghaṭa* – waterpots; *sthagita* – is concealed; *citrita* –by a patterned; *kañculikā* – bodice; *mānasa* – the heart; *hari* – of Śrī Hari; *kṛta-lobhām* – is captivated; *sudhā* – by the nectarean; *rasa* – mellows; *svādu* – of Her relishable; *sphurat* – trembling; *aruṇa* – reddish; *adhara* – lips.

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā, the beauty of whose excellent breasts, which resemble golden waterpots, is concealed by a patterned bodice. By the nectarean mellows of Her relishable, trembling, ruddy lips, She captivates the mind of Śrī Hari.**

### Text 7

***sundara-cibuka-virājita-mohana-***  
***mecaka-bindu-vilāsām***  
***sa-kanaka-ratna-khacita-pṛthu-mauktika-***  
***ruci-rucirojjvala-nāsām (7)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*mohana* – an infatuating; *vilāsām* – pastime; *bindu* – dot; *mecaka* – blue-black like the eye of a peacock; *virājita* – shines; *sundara* – upon Her beautiful; *cibuka* – chin; *pṛthu* – and a large; *ruci* – and lustrous; *mauktika* –pearl ornament; *sa-kanaka* – set in gold; *ratna* – and gems; *khacita* – is affixed; *ujjvala* – upon Her brilliant; *rucira* – and beautiful; *nāsām* – nose.

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā, upon whose beautiful chin is an infatuating pastime-dot that is blue-black like the eye of a peacock, and whose brilliant, beautiful nose is ornamented by a large and lustrous pearl set in jewels and gold.**

### Text 8

***ujjvala-prema-rasāmṛta-sāgara-***  
***sāra-tanūṁ sukha-rūpām***  
***nipatati mādhava-mugdha-mano-mṛga-***  
***nābhi-sudhā-rasa-kūpām (8)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*tanūm* – Her body; *sāra* – which is the essence (churned) from; *amṛta* – the nectarean; *sāgara* – ocean; *rasa* – of the *rasa*; *ujjvala-prema* – of amorous love; *rūpām* – is the essential form; *sukha* – of all happiness (for Mādhava); *mādhava* – Mādhava; *nipatati* – falls; *kūpām* – into that well; *sudhā-rasa* – of ambrosial mellows; *manaḥ* – His mind; *mugdha* –bewildered; *mṛga-nābhi* – like the musk deer.

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā, whose form is the quintessence churned from the nectar-ocean of amorous love and is the very embodiment of all happiness for Mādhava. Indeed, Śrī Mādhava falls into that well of ambrosial mellows, His mind bewildered (by His own internal potency), like the musk deer captivated by its own fragrance.**

### Text 9

***nūpura-hāra-manohara-kuṇḍala-***  
***kṛta-rucim aruṇa-dukūlam***  
***pathi pathi madana-madākula-gokula-***  
***candra-kalita-pada-mūlām (9)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*manohara* – Her enchanting; *nūpura* – anklebells; *hāra* –necklace; *kuṇḍala* – and earrings; *kṛta* – create; *rucim* – an effulgence; *aruṇa-dukūlam* – (She wears) a dress the colour of the rising sun; *gokula-candra* – and Kṛṣṇa, the moon of Gokula; *ākula* – distressed; *mada* – and maddened; *madana* – by (the arrows) of Kāmadeva; *kalita* – is forcibly impelled; *pada-mūlām* – to take shelter of Her feet; *pathi pathi* – by any recourse.

**Перевод:**

**May my mind incessantly recall Śrī Rādhā, whose enchanting anklebells, necklace and earrings create an effulgence. She wears a dress the colour of the rising sun, and at Her lotus feet Gokula-candra, Kṛṣṇa, distressed and maddened by the arrows of Kāmadeva, is forcibly impelled to take shelter by any means possible.**

### Text 10

***rasika-sarasvatī-gīta-mahādbhuta-***  
***rādhā-rūpa-rahasyam***  
***vṛndāvana-rasa-lālasā-manasām***  
***idam upageyam avaśyam (10)***

**Пословный перевод:**

*rahasyam* – this confidential song; *mahā-adbhuta* – about the very wonderful; *rādhā-rūpa* – form of Śrī Rādhā; *gīta* – is sung; *rasika* – by the connoisseur of *rasa*; *sarasvatī* – Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī; *idam* – it; *avaśyam* – should certainly; *upageyam* – be sung; *manasām* – by those whose minds; *lālasā* – eagerly hanker; *rasa* – for the *rasa*; *vṛndāvana* – of Śrī Vṛndāvana-dhāma.

**Перевод:**

**This confidential song about the greatly marvelous form of Śrī Rādhā, sung by the connoisseur of** ***rasa*** **Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī, should by all means be sung in celebration by all those whose hearts eagerly hanker to relish the** ***rasa*** **of Śrī Vṛndāvana.**
