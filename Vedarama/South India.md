# South India

## Tirupati Tirumala hills

## Arrival of Anantalvan to Tirupati

One day Sripad Ramanujacharya was giving a lecture on the songs of Nammalvar's Divya prabandams. In a particular work of Tiruvaimoli, there was a verse on the glories of Tirupati Balaji. There it says that Tirupati abode, the land of Balaji is completely full of powers. According to the Sri Sampradaya, Srirangam is called as the Bhoga mandapam, where the Lord enjoys all the bhoga, he enjoys and performs pastimes and the temple of Kanchi, Kanchipuram is called Tyaga Mandapa, where Lord sacrifices for the devotees sack, as well as Lord appeared from the sacrifice And Tirupati Tirumala the third important holy place is called Pushpa Mandapam or the abode of beautiful flowers. The Lord adores beautiful flowers over His body.

In that ecstasy Ramanujacharya asked his disciples and devotees assembled there, whether any of them was ready to go to Tirupati, the abode of Balaji and start worshiping the Lord by developing beautiful garden and offering flowers from those gardens to the Lord. As soon as Ramanujacharya said this there was a big silence in the whole assembly. Because going to Tirupati was not an easy thing because at that time Tirupati Dham and Tirumala hill was surrounded by dense forest that filled with fierce animals and very cold as well. Living over the Tirumala Tirupati was of great inconvenience and austere. So no one is ready to say yes. Apart from that many were hesitating to leave the association of Ramanujacharya in his head quarters Srirangam. In such a condition of the assembly, one devotee raised his hand, his name is Anantacharya. My dear Ramanujacharya, my dear spiritual master if that is your desire and with your blessings I’m ready to undertake this task. I’m ready to execute this service of developing garden and worshiping Lord Balaji with those beautiful flowers. Immediately Ramanujacharya become so much overwhelmed out of gratitude and affection for his disciple. He exclaimed that you are a rare male you are anpillai, you are the real man of this assembly and from that time onwards Anantacharya’s name has become Anantanpillai. Anpillai in Tamil refers to a male. Anantalvan or Anantacharya or now Anantanpillai preceded to the abode Tirupati hill, the abode of Lord Venkatanatha in Tirumala Tirupati.

## Lord loves to help his own devotees

Anantacharya reached the holy Dham Tirumala Tirupati and as per the order of his spiritual master Ramanujacharya he started executing the service to the Lord. He would worship the Lord in the morning and evening and then he would offer beautiful flowers. For that he developed a beautiful flower garden and named it as Ramanuja Garden. Also he developed a very beautiful pukkur and named that as Ramanuja tank. After some time, he decided to increase the depth of the lake by digging it further. He was taking out the mud and putting it in the basket and the mud had been carried away by his wife. She would carry the mud on her head and throw it away on the other side of the bank and bring the empty basket back. Anantalvan digs the land further and fills the basket with the diged mud, his wife carries it again. Like this, just the husband and wife were working hard the whole day, to make a good pukkur for the Lord Balaji, Venkateswara.

Balaji was very much pleased with the service of his dear devotee. He wanted to participate in his service and wanted to reduce the work load of his dear devotee. Thus Venkateswara, Balaji appeared as a very innocent beautiful looking Brahman brahmachari boy. But this brahmachari Brahman boy was wearing beautiful garment, yellowish Dothi and he is having nice hear rings to his ears. He came in front of Anantalvan and told, “Oh! My dear Brahman, Oh my dear Anantalvan, seems you are working so hard, why don’t take me I can also render some service, I can help you in your endeavor to serve the Lord. Please accept my help. Anantalvan looked at that boy and replied. “My dear boy you are so young and delicate, this kind of hard work is not fit for you. You should enjoy. My body is meant for this kind of hard work I can do this hard work. You go and enjoy”. But the boy was insisting, “No, I would like to help you please take me”. Anantalvan replied, “See this service is only my nourishment, this is my real food. Why do you want to snatch this service from me, please don’t disturb me. If you want to render some service to the Lord do it elsewhere in any way you wish”. After hearing this, the Lord was very disappointed for not able to help his own dear devotee. Then the Brahman boy went away from that place.

Anantalvan’s wife was pregnant and she was doing this service with great difficulty. The Brahman boy approached Anantalvan’s wife and told her, “My dear mother, you are working so hard and you are also carrying a child in your womb, so, why don’t you take help from me? I’ll help you in carrying this mud”. But the lady told, “See my husband has just chastised you, if you do this service for me he will not be happy”. But the boy in very innocent terms enchantingly told her, “My dear mother you are carrying a baby in your womb, so you should not be allowed to do so much of hard work, and by the way your husband does not know that I’m doing this service for you. You just bring the basket of mud, in the mid way I’ll carry the basket and through it in a long distance and bring back the basket to you, you go and get another basket”. By the enchanting speak of the boy she agreed. So she would bring the basket full of mud and Lord would take from her in the middle and through it away and bring the basket back to her. In this way she was doing her service.

Anantalvan recognized the quickness in his wife’s service. He got some doubt and asked her, “My dear wife, previously, you used to do this service very slow but now you are doing it in half of your previous time. How could you do so fast? She told the truth that the Brahman boy was helping her in the middle. Anantacharya was got very upset and angry. He went towards the boy with his spade he was digging with. The boy saw that Anantacharya is coming with anger so the boy started running.

## Anantalvan punishes the Lord

Anantacharya started running behind the boy to catch him. The boy ran and climbed a tree and told Anantalvan, “Oh my dear Anantalvan, you are trying to catch and punish me please don’t punish me, I’ll give you my golden dhoti and the ornaments to you but please don’t punish me. Anantacharya thought that he would pretend that he agreed for the boy’s proposal, as soon as the boy got the tree he would catch him and punish him. So Anantacharya told the boy, “OK I’ll not punish you come down”. So the boy got down of the tree. Immediately Anantacharya started running towards the boy to catch him. The body realized and with fear started running. In this way Anantacharya chased the boy. While chasing Anantacharya through his spade towards the boy it just to make him stop. But some how the spade hit the chin of the boy and the boy with that wound on his chin ran into the altar of Lord Balaji and close the doors.

Anantacharya was waiting at the door thinking that the boy would come out. But the door never opened. In the evening the priest has come to worship the Lord. Anantacharya told the pujari that a boy went inside the altar and closed the door. With lot of endeavor they opened the door and went inside. For their surprise they saw at the chin of Lord Balaji blood was oozing out. Anantacharya was shocked and he realized that the boy who came to help him was nothing but Lord himself. Anantacharya prostrated himself in front of the Lord and begged for forgiveness. He brought some herbs and camphor and kept them at the chin of the Lord. Then the blood flow has stopped. Lord spoke out that my dear Anantacharya I was delighted with your complete absorption in my service and I’m very happy with you service. Now, I’d like to offer you our own clothes as well as my ring. Like this Lord honored Anantacharya. Even to day the spade that Anantalvan used to hit the Lord is hanging near the gopuram entrance of Tirupati Balaji temple. The camphor which adorn the Lord at the chin is even seen today and is distributed to the devotees as Sripadarenu.

## Humility of Ramanujacharya

In the mean time in Srirangam Ramanujacharya heard that his disciple Anantacharya was rendering great service to the Lord as per his order. He wanted to go and see how Anantacharya is performing his service over there. So, Ramanujacharya started to Tirupati. Ramanujacharya was visiting many holy places on the way. At one place Ramanujacharya asked a Sudra person who was pumping up water from a well what is the way to go to Tirupati? That person showed a direction with his hands. Immediately Ramanujacharya paid prostrated obeisances to that person. That sudra person and Ramanujacharya’s disciples were surprised for that gesture of Ramanujacharya. With exclamation Ramanujacharya’s disciples asked him the reaons why did he pay obeisance to a sudra person. Ramanujacharya replied that he showed me the way to Vaikunta, show he has become a guru for me.

Finally Ramanujacharya reached Tirupati, the foot of Tirumala hill. Ramanujacharya stayed there and giving beautiful discourses on the pastimes of Lord Balaji. Meanwhile, Anantacharya was expecting that Ramanujacharya would come up and take darshan of Balaji. But Ramanujacharya never put any endeavor to go up. So Anantacharya requested Ramanujacharya my dear spiritual master please come up to the top of the hill and take darshan of Tirupati Balaji. But Ramanujacharya replied my dear Anantacharya being so fallen how can I even think of climbing this hill? Even our ancestor acaryas, the alvars, never climbed this hill and they only sung the glories from down. Apart from that this hill is none different from Ananta Sesha, the snake bed of the Lord. So how can I even think of climbing this hill? I’m not going to climb this hill but I’d take darshan of this hill from its foot hill. Anantacharya replied my dear gurudev if you don’t come up and take darshan of the Lord we also do follow you and not going to climb on top of the hill. No one else in the future generation will climb as well. If you want the services to the Lord to be continued by your disciples and future generations please come up and take darshan of the Lord. Thus Ramanujacharya reconsidered his decision for the sake of continuation of the services to the Lord and decided to climb the hill. He offered oblations in the next morning and after paying obeisance to the Lord he started climbing the hill. It is said in scriptures after the conditioned living entity achieve the perfection of devotional service upon the Lord, he goes the abode of Vaikunta& he climbs on sinhasan & meets the Lord and sits on his lap. The Lord embraces the living entity. Ramanujacharya started thinking same way that he is climbing the hill which is none different from Lord’s snake bed Adisesha, just to give pleasure to the Lord in Vaikunta. And thus Ramanuja climbed the hill.

## Srisailapoorna’s humility

It is said else where that from a particular point onwards Ramanujacharya started climbing with his knees not even touched by his foot. As soon as Ramanujacharya reached the top of the hill his spiritual master Sri Sailapoorna came with maha prasad of Lord Balaji with his garland and other important paraphernalia to welcome Sripad Ramanujacharya.

Srisailapoorna was not an ordinary person, he is the spiritual master of Ramanujacharya & uncle who gave name Ramanuja to Ramanuja when he was born. The mother of Ramanujacharya was the sister of Srisailapoorna. Ramanujacharya became very ashamed that Srisailapoorna is coming to welcome him in Tirumala hills. So, Ramanujacharya spoke out of shame my dear Gurudev, you are coming to welcome a fallen person like me. You could have sent a neophyte devotee to welcome me. And Srisailapoorna immediately replied my dear Ramanuja I went around the four important streets of Tirumala, and tried to look for some boy who is low and neophyte than me but I could not find anyone so I came myself. All the devotees wonder struck by the humility displayed by Srisailapoorna.

## Tirumala hill is non-different from Vaikunta

He saw the forest, beautiful flower gardens and garden and lake made by Anantacharya. Ramanujacharya was so happy and pleased with the Anantacharya and embraced him and shown his gratitude for the wonderful service he has done. Ramanujacharya understood that this particular hill is so sacred that the Lord of Vaikunta descended down with his concert to this place just to enjoy the beauty of this place. All the residents of this place are none different from the residents of Vaikunta. Thus Ramanujacharya in order not to contaminate this holy abode Tirumala hill he did not eat anything and drink anything and avoided passing urine etc. After staying for one day Ramanujacharya wanted to leave to his place but his spiritual master Srisailapoorna stopped Ramanuja, by saying the sastras say that one should stay in this holy place for three days. By the request of his spiritual master Ramanuja agreed to stay for three days in this place. Ramanujacharya took darshan and performed pooja to the Lord for three days and fasted completely. After taking darshan of the Lord Ramanujacharya asked permission from Lord Balaji for his departure down to the hill. The Lord Balaji replied my dear Ramanuja I’m very happy with you and we have given you a residence in our southern house in Srirangam. You can send whomever you want to back home back to Godhead to the abode of Vaikunta. Ramanujacharya came down and stayed in Tirupati and there he learned for one year from Srisailapoorna the importance and the inner meaning of Sri Ramayana in 24 different ways

## Service attitude towards spiritual master (GOVINDA)

Govinda was the brother of Ramanujacharya. Previously Govinda was a Shaivaite. Upon the request of Ramanujacharya Srisailapoorna made him a devotee again. Govinda is a completely surrendered to Srisailapoorna. One day Ramanujacharya saw Govinda lied on bed of Srisailapoorna. and immediately got up. Govinda was doing this every day. Ramanujacharya reported this to Srisailapoorna who asked Govinda, “Why did you do like that. Do you know what happen by lying down on the bed of spiritual master?” Then Govinda replied my dear gurudev, I know that I’m going to hell but I don’t mind for it, just I want to check is there anything that is pricking from the bed. I don’t mind going to hell but my spiritual master’s body should not be hurt in any way on this bed. Ramanujacharya and Srisailapoorna were amazed by the service attitude of Govinda.

Another time Govinda put his hand in the mouth of a snake and trying to take out a thorn and Ramanuja asked why are you doing this? Govinda replied now this snake is suffering because of thorn in its mouth, I’m trying to remove the thorn. After I remove the thorn from the snake’s mouth, I take bath in this pukkur and come here again (jiva daya)

Govinda and Ramanujacharya went back to Srirangam. Before Ramanujacharya was departing from Tirupati Anantacharya asked Ramanuja “my dear Gurudev how can I live without you in this abode Tirupati?” When can I see you again? How can we have your association again in this place? And Anantacharya asked Ramanujacharya for permission to make a deity of him. By seeing Anantalvan’s attachment and devotion upon him Ramanujacharya did not say anything. Thus Anantacharya made a (first) deity of Ramanujacharya and in order to please his dearest disciple and his associates, Ramanujacharya embraced the deity. Still this deity is kept in Tirupati temple. Thus that is the first deity established in the presence of Ramanuja. It is kept in Tirumala hill on the left side to Lord Balaji. And after establishing the deity Ramanujacharya gave farewell to his disciple and other devotees and went back to Srirangam.

## Lord only can kill the cobra within (devotee marova jivova)

Anantacharya was continuing his service to Lord Balaji in Tirumala hill. One day he was bitten by a very poisonous snake while plucking up Tulasi leaves to the Lord in the garden named Ramanuja Nanda Van. But Anantacharya was not at all bothered about that he continued his service. His disciples saw this and wanted to do some medical treatment but their spiritual master did not accept any medical treatment for that. They went and informed the Lord. When Anantacharya came in front of the Lord with Tulasi garland, immediately Lord Srinivas, Balaji asked Anantacharya, “My dear Anantacharya what is that you are doing? Snake poison is entered into your body. What will happen if you don’t do anything about that? Anantacharya immediately replied “My dear Lord, after the snake bite I’ve taken bath in Pukkur. My dear Lord, which poison are you talking about? There is a vibrant cobra with in me poisoning me with false ego, pride and greed. Should I get rid of the poison of this cobra, or should I make effort to get rid of the poison of the cobra that has bitten my body? The poison of the cobra which has bitten my body cannot harm my soul, it harms only my body. But the former poison, is capable of destroying my soul, you only can be the doctor to getting rid of that poison (ahankar). I cannot go to an ordinary mortal person to cure this poison of false ego and pride. I pray for your guidance my Lord.

Then Lord told Anantacharya, “You are very dear to me. And I enjoy your company in this temple. If you decided to ignore your body and make no effort against the poison of the cobra I’m the loser of your association. Please do something to get cured”. Then Anantacharya replied to the Lord my dear Lord I’m also like a snake emanating the poison. If the bitten snake is stronger than than this snake, I may die and I’d take bath in holy water of Vraja river in Vaikunta and stand before the Lord of Vaikunta. But this snake(my body) proves too tough enough( my body is stronger enough than bitten snake) I’d live & take bath in the sacred lake Swami Puskarani tank here &serve In this holy hills of Tirumala and staying before you Lord Srinivas. So, if I die I’ll take bath in the Vraja river in the spiritual world and serve you as Lord of Vaikunta and if I live I take both in this pukkur in Tirumala hill and serve you as Lord Balaji. In this way in any condition I’m going to serve you my dear Lord. So, please in any condition make me your servant. And Lord Balaji thus revealed his ardent desire to Lord and thus it proved that for a devotee he does not have any desire other than to serve the Lord either while living or after death. But by the mercy of the Lord Anantalvan survived the snake bit and continued to serve the Lord.

## Some beautiful pastime of Lord Balaji, Srinivas and his concert Padmavati

Another time in the season of Vasanta Rutu, spring season Lord Balaji, Srinivas and his concert Padmavati, they would come to this garden and perform their pastimes. So the whole night they would perform their pastimes in the garden of Anantalvan named Ramanuja Garden. And while doing so the Lord and concert, they pick flowers and fruits and enjoy the garden in the morning Anantalvan would come and see the garden was completely, the flowers were taken out the fruits were eaten out it is completely confusing how it was happening. And the watch man was there but he could not reply what exactly happened. And this happen for many nights the morning the garden was very much disturbed.

Then Anantalvan decided that he himself would see who is doing this mischievous activity at night. On that night Anantalvan saw a princely couple with beautiful garments was roaming in that garden for a moment he was ever joyed but he was also taken a rock and chased that couple. He dragged both of them in order to tie them up under the tree but the charmly prince escaped from the clutches of Anantalvan. The princess was caught and she was tied underneath a champaka tree. And Anantalvan enquired from her that who has stolen all the flowers and fruits. Then he told where is your husband? What was his name? So, the girl innocently replied Oh! My dear Anantalvan, I’m like your daughter, I did no wrong. I and my husband are the ruler of these hills. My Lord wonders all over the hills as he pleases. He defiled your garden, I’m not responsible for what has happened please spare me. Anantalvan replied ok I spare you tell me the name of your husband. I want to catch him. The girl replied my dear Anantalvan you know the scriptures how a chased wife can say any time the name of her husband. So, I cannot tell the name of my husband as well. Anantalvan was thinking what to do? Anantalvan replied to this girl of course, you are like my daughter but still I want to catch your husband and take a ransom of amount for the destruction of the garden of the Lord. So, I’m going to tie you to this big champaka tree and sure enough that your husband is going to come to release you and that time I’ll catch him. Thus she was tied to the champaka tree, and in the mean time the pujaris in the temple found that in the chest of the Lord Srinivas, mother Lakshmi was missing (locket). They were thinking that who was taken they were doubting the poojaris.

Then the Lord came in the dream poojaris and replied that that is not the real truth of herself is being caught by the Anantalvan in his garden. Then the pujaris went and saw the beautiful girl who looks like mother Lakshmi was caught by Anantacharya and thus they brought that girl to the altar and immediately they understood this girl was nothing but Mahalakshmi herself. Anantacharya immediately prostrated before her and beg forgiveness for what was happened. But this girl replied Anantalvan that you should considered me as your daughter thus I’ll become you daughter and Anantalvan was surprised and next day in the morning he performed his duty and kept this beautiful girl in a palanquin and took to the altar of Lord Balaji and thus at that time this girl again entered into the chest of the Lord. And occupied her place in the chest of the Lord. From that time onwards it became well known that Anantacharya became the father of the consort of Lord Srinivas, Padmavati. And thus he became the father-in-law of Balaji. And that time onwards the marriage of Padmavati and Balaji takes place keeping Anantalvan as the father-in-law of the Lord. Thus Anantalvan performed lot of pastimes with Balaji in Tirumala hill.

## Give all respect to others without expecting anything in return

Another time Anantacharya started with his disciple toward south India in order to meet his other disciples in the down. So the crew left Tirupati came down the hill and journeying towards south. After long distance of traveling in the bank of the river they were feeling very hungry they opened their prasadam packet to honor prasadam which was brought from Tirumala. And then they saw for their surprise lot of ants were there in their prasadam packets. Immediately Anantacharya stopped them and told let us go back to Tirumala hill. Then they took the prasadam packets them without eating they started walking all the way to Tirumala hills. And devotees surprised walking in the forest long distance again climbing the seven hills back they came all the way and Anantacharya came to Tirumala hill and told them keep the prasadam packets in Tirumala hills. And the prasadam packets were kept on the top of the hill and Anantalvan told ok let us go down for our destination south. And they started walking down. All the devotees were surprised that we first of all started to south and again Anantalvan told us that let us go back to Tirupati. They thought they were going to stay there but he gave and put the prasadam packets and again started going to south so they surprised, confused and they asked Anantalvan we are so tired of walked we did not understand what was exactly happening. We came all the way and putting the prasadam back and going again what is the reason.

Anantalvan replied that scripture says that the great souls and great Rishis and saints are hankering to take birth in Tirumala hill they even pray that in any way some how or other they want to take birth in Tirumala hill. It could be even as an animal or any species. So my dear devotees, king Kulashekhara prays that he prefer to be any specie or a step in front of the Lord in Tirumala hill. So we went all the way down south but we saw the prasadam that we brought from Tirupati is completely infested with ants they must be great saintly Rishis who have taken birth in Tirumala now. So we will do the offense of taking them down from Tirumala hill. So, I came all the way from the river bank and went back to Tirumala keep the prasadam back with the ant so that they are not deprived of association of Tirumala hill. We are now proceeding to our destination south again. Thus the disciples wonder struck how much importance that Anantacharya gave even to a little ants and considered them as nityasuris or the residents of Vaikunta in Tirumala hill without seeing any familiarity in the holy place where he was living.

## Lord personally takes cares of his devotees

Another time Anantalvan started towards Kerala and he was preaching to many people there. And made them disciples of him and then again returning back to the seven hills of Tirumala. And while returning, all the disciples and devotees they felt very hungry and they were so tired. And at that time they were thinking what to do and suddenly from nowhere Lord Srinivas appeared as a small boy and brought nice prasadam from Tirumala hill and himself served the devotees. And after they have eaten he also took the morsel of prasadam, handful of prasadam and immediately disappeared from that place. All the devotees realized that Lord himself came because of the devotion of Anantarya. And Anantarya had number of disciples so the disciples helped, he started doing lot of service to the Lord renovated the temple, corridors they constructed more Gopurams and many services they have done.

## Champaka corridor

Another time in due course of doing construction service they came across a champaka tree. Which was standing on the way of their construction. So, the disciples were about to cut the champaka tree for construction purpose, but Anantarya told them “you cannot cut the tree they are also residents of spiritual world”. And he told knowing what to do? They left but Anantarya prayed to the Lord and all of them slept. In that next day they got up they saw for their surprise the champaka tree has given them a way for the construction to further proceed. That would have obstructed before. Thus they realized the devotion of Anantacharya made the champaka tree to give way to their construction. From that time onwards the corridor of this particular constructed place is called champaka prakara or champaka corridor. And thus Anantacharya performed so many pastimes in Tirumala hills.

## Follow the order of spiritual master

One time Anantalvan was performing his garland service to the Lord. He brought fresh flowers from the garden and was making garland for the Lord. And the Lord through his pujaris sent him a message that call Anantacharya immediately to altar. The pujaris has to Anantacharya and told that Lord Srinivasa is calling him now immediately into the altar.

For that Anantacharya replied, tell the Lord that I’m very busy I cannot come now. And then the pujaris went and told the Lord that Anantacharya is busy he can not come. The Lord was surprised that after me calling he was not coming. So then Anantacharya went to his own time and made a nice garland and went to the altar to offer to Lord. Then Lord asked what do you think Anantacharya, I have told you and given a message and told you to come to altar but you did not come. Do you know I’m the Lord of this universe, why didn’t you follow our order. And Anantacharya replied I’m doing this service on the order my spiritual master Ramanujacharya. So, for me my spiritual master’s order is first then comes your order. Because, I’m doing service here and I got this service here by his mercy and by his order. And his order for me is that I’ve offer you fresh flower garland everyday which I would have failed. So that is my first service to follow my guru’s order. If you order is coming as an obstruct to follow my guru’s order, I prefer to follow my guru’s order. And thus Anantacharya established that service to the deity is not independent that we are doing something to the Lord. But the service to the deity is nothing but the order of the spiritual master. We are assisting the spiritual master in serving the deity. And then the Lord jokingly appealed to Anantalvan Oh! You can not come when I call, do you know Anantacharya I have ability to remove you from this place immediately. Anantacharya further jokingly rebuked the Lord, my dear Lord you yourself have came from Vaikunta to Tirumala hill that is what the sastras were saying. You came from Vaikunta to enjoy with your concert in this Tirumala hill. You came some time before and I’ve come from Srirangam. So, both of us are coming from somewhere else and staying here. How can one tenant say to the other tenant to go out from this place. bhu varaha is the real owner And started having such a nice exchange with the Lord of seven hills, thus enjoyed pastimes in Tirupati.

## Love in separation

Another time Anantalvan became sick and he could not come for the service of the Lord. The Lord was in deep separation and missing his association. Lord sent the pujaris to attend him. The pujaris came with a physician and told Anantalvan that Lord is missing you and he sent a physician and us to care of you. Anantacharya for the surprise told how is possible that a Brahman can employ another person to do his Gayatri? And pujaris requested and Anantacharya told I don’t want any medicines you can leave to the temple. So pujaris and physician came to the Lord told that Anantacharya has refused and he was saying like this. Then Lord understood that Anantacharya wants to see him personally (in palanquin). Then Lord went to, then Lord understood that Anantacharya’s sickness is due to feeling separation from the Lord. So remedy is his darshan to Anantacharya. Then Lord. Then the Lord raised to the abode of Anantacharya and Anantacharya remain silent he did not say anything for some time and then Lord touched his body with his hand and immediately Anantacharya got cured of his illness. And he became physically fit again. And Anantacharya said my dear Lord please forgive me for my silence in front of you, after all I need your darshan and touch and you are always forgiving all offenses committed by your servants and the Lord went back to his altar again. After conversing with Anantacharya.

## What is the symptom of a devotee?

Another time, the son of Kuresh who is the disciple of Ramanujacharya, named Parasharabatta is very dear to Anantalvan. Though Anantalvan is very elderly person to Parasharabatta who is young but they have very close relationship. Thus Parasharabatta became the successor of Sripad Ramanujacharya to deheldas Sri Vaishanva sect after his time. Once one devotee went to Parasharabatta and asked him a question what is the hall mark of a Vaishnava? What is the symptom of a devotee? And Parasharabatta told this devotee Brahman that you go to Tirumala hill and ask this question from Anantacharya. I think I’m not qualified that’s why I’m not getting the answer. So he kept silent and the serene and Anantacharya did not say anything. And this devotee kept doing service.

At one particular time there was a festival took place, so Anantacharya was arranged Tadiyaradanam, or serving all the viashnava’s with excellent prasadam. In such a occasion the prasadam serving was going on and this devotee who came from Srirangam and was sitting in the line for prasadam. Anantacharya casually replied the true Vaishnava is like a crane is like a cock, and like salt and like you. and thus replied and kept silent. And this devotee after serving six months wanted to know answer for this question and got the answer a Vaishnava is like a crane, like a cock, like salt and like you. Not knowing anything any meaning of this the brahmana came back to Srirangam. And Parasharabattar asked did you get answer to your question? And he replied the answer and told that I’m not able to understand what is the answer? What is the understanding of this answer?

Parasharabatta understood the heart of Anantacharya and told the explanation. He said my dear devotee Anantalvan says that Vaishnava should be like a crane, that means see the crane is white in color. So the heart of a vaishnava is like a white, so pure like paramahamsa so clean. The crane always stands on one leg. It just allow the small fishes to go and big fish to come to feast in that similarly a Vaishnava will not hear from a mundane person, who are like a small fish and wait for a really great soul like a mahatma and feast in their wisdom. Thus a devotee waits for a big mahatma like a crane ignore the small fishes and feast in big fishes and the crane also in rainy season immediately leaves the shore of the sea side knowing that there is a danger of flood may come in the rainy season and goes to the near by tank. Similarly a vaishnava when he sees the unwanted flood of bad association comes he leaves that place goes to the place where there is good association. Thus he compared vaishnava to a crane. And he compared vaishnava to a cock that means the cock if you see it goes to the dust bins where the garbage is there but takes only the grains and proper eatables and feeds the younger ones. Similarly a devotee is a saragrahi he just take the essence and understand the essence and gives it to others that is service to the Lord. That is the only thing that can be consumed and other things are like garbage.

And when he said Vaishnava is like salt it means the salt is such a item in the food if it is less it can be noted down and if it is more it is also be noted down. But a devotee like sufficient salt. When it is there in the preparation, it gives a taste to the whole food but remains hidden. Nobody glories the preparation by saying the salt is very nice. But they glorify everything else. Thus the salt gives the taste but remains hidden. Similarly vaishnava does everything but want to be remains hidden, never projects himself as a significant person. And then the salt also ready to dissolve its form, ready to dissolve itself so that it can give taste for the food. It is ready to dissolve its existence for giving taste for the food to be eaten by people. Similarly a devotee is ready to sacrifice his existence, he is ready to sacrifice everything so that he can give pleasure to other Vaishnavas. Thus he compared vaishnava to salt. And he is like you that is the way you conducted prasadam serving though you have been told to get up many times you got up and serve nicely, thus he told vaishnava should be like you. In this way this is the understanding of the Anantalvan as the symptom of a Vaishnava.

## Bhakti is more important than varna and ashrama

## Srirangam

Once upon a time on mount Kailash Lord Siva was surrounded by 33 crore demigods, 41,000 Rishis and 11,000 Siddhas. In such an assembly great saint Narada muni entered. After paying obeisances to Lord Siva Narada muni spoke to Lord Siva, my dear Lord till now you had been mercifully describing all of us the greatness of different holy places. Also we have heard from you there is a holy place called Srirangam. But we have not heard of it until now. So my dear Lord, we are very eager to hear the glories of the Srirangam Dham. Even though you yourself have told us before that even Anantashesha with his thousands of mouths can not completely glorify this holy place. But still we would like to hear the glories of this holy Dham from you whatever is possible. Lord Siva heard Narada muni’s petition and he became happy. With a smiling face Lord Siva spoke to Narada muni, “My dear Narada, as I’ve told you before the glory of this particular Dham is unlimited. I can not tell them completely. Still I’ll take 10000 years to complete the glories that I know. Still I try to tell the glories of this Dham in a summarized form.

## Glories of Srirangam Dham

Oh! Narada this holy place so is powerful which is in the banks of Kaveri and in banks of a pukkur called Chandrapushkarini. Anyone, who visits this place is sure that they never going to see hellish planets and never going to suffer in the name of death. Also it is said anyone who has not seen this holy place called Srirangam for them it is not possible to lead a pious life. They can not respect the elderly people; they can not give charity that is the importance of this holy place.

If a person chants the name of Lord Ranga, in whatever condition he is in either while sneezing, or while yawning or while coughing or while walking or while speaking or even in disease state he will be protected from all kinds of dangers. He will be freed from all sinful reactions. When he chants twice the name of Lord Ranga he is free from all the debts and sins that he has been doing all his life.

If a person who is staying thousands of miles away from this place even if he thinks of this holy place he will become very pious personality. All the people from his generation will be freed from the sinful reactions. If anyone offers obeisances towards the direction of Srirangam with his two hands up will be free from all his sinful activities.

Pitries in Pitriloka, were thinking that is it possible that one person in our family take birth and goes to Srirangam and take bath in the holy river Kaveri and take darshan of the Lord. If anyone could do that, we will be liberated from our sufferings. In this way Pitries are waiting for a long time. In the abode of Yamaraj, Yamaraj was thinking that as long as Srirangam is there no one is coming to Yamaloka. Just by thinking of Srirangam Dham they were freed from all sinful acts just like snow melts down as soon as it see the sun. Similarly all the sinful activities of the people are destroyed just by seeing the Srirangam Dham. Thus Yamaraj was thinking that no one work on his planet.

Even the demigods desire to take birth in Srirangam hence on earth planet. Thus this place is so glorious. Even if a person is speaking lie and a person is association with sinful people and a person totally has circumstance that he has to support the sinful people, but in no stance if he chants the name Lord Ranga once he will be freed from all these sinful reactions.

The previous ancestors in his family were doing austerities thinking that a pious son will born in their family and he will be very intelligent and he will go to Srirangam and take bath in Kaveri and take darshan of Lord Ranganath, Lakshmi and Alvars and he will give charity at least one Brahman in his place, if it is not possible at least he will give little money to a Dham vasi, even if that also not possible at least he will offer some water and sesame seeds, even if this also not possible at least if he feed little grass to a cow in that holy Dham, we will be liberated from our suffering condition. With this hope they were performing the austerities.

If a person some how or other find a pilgrimage who is going to the Srirangam and feeds that pilgrimage even once he will get the same benefit of going to Srirangam by which he will attain the spiritual abode of Vaikunta.

In past, Lord Narayana instructed the people that if they wanted to be freed from the sinful activities of this material world, they should go to Srirangam and take bath in Kaveri River. Even if one goes to Srirangam and chant the astottara mantra Om Namo Narayanaya and take bath in Kaveri and take darshan of Lord Narayana he will be freed from all sinful activities.

And by staying in that place one will be freed from Gohatya, killing of a cow, Sisuhatya: killing of a child, Brahmahatya: killing of a Brahman, Strihatya: killing of a woman and he will be freed from the sinful reactions of steeling, lying, drinking and killing.

Anyone who is hearing the glories of Srirangam Dham will not only be freed from the sinful reactions but also he will get opulences and great span of life. Apart from that he also get devotion to Lord Vishnu. He will be enjoying the opulence of material world and after that he will attain the spiritual Dham Vaikunta.

## Appearance of Lord Brahma and creation

When Narada muni heard the glories of Srirangam Dham from Lord Siva he become enthusiastic and said “I’m eager to hear how the Srirangam Dham appeared and how the Candrapushkarini appeared? Please tell us indetail the appearance of this holy place” and Lord Siva happily started telling the appearance of the holy Dham Srirangam. He said once upon a time Lord Narayana as a child lying on an Ashwardha leaf in Pralaya water and he was in Yoganidra. So many years of Yoganidra, one day, he opened his eyes and saw; how found that the surrounding place is completely filled with water and there was no creation and no living entities around. Immediately Lord could understand that all the creation was with in his womb. And desiring to create all the living entities again, he manifested a lotus flower from his naval. This lotus flower has 1008 petals that resemble golden petals and the stem was like that of Navaratnas and throngs of the stem are like pearls. On top of this lotus flower Lord Brahma appeared with his four heads.

After appearing from this lotus flower, Brahma saw around and could not see even a single entity and thus he was caught up in fear and with so much of anxiety he was looking here and there. At that time Lord Vishnu appeared in front of Lord Brahma in the form of a swan or Hamsa. As soon as Lord Brahma saw Lord in the form swan he became very happy just like a poor man who gets mass of wealth or a blind man get a stick for walking. After seeing this Hamsa, Lord Brahma asked the Hamsa,” what is your identity, who are you my dear Hamsa?” Then Lord replied that I’m Hari, when Brahma heard that Lord Hari has come in the form of a Hamsa, he immediately paid full obeisances. ## Five expansion of Lord

Now you are taking darshan of our form which has descended directly from Vaikunta. Apart from this we exhibit our spiritual form in other places this also including the milk ocean. For the benefit of the conditioned souls in this material world we also take thousands of incarnations in each and every Yuga and deliver them from birth and death and not only that we also enter into the heart of each and every living entity as Paramatma and inspire them to get rid of their karmic activities and let them to come back to our abode. Thus we appear in spiritual, as well as in milk ocean, as well as in Avataras and as Paramatma in the hearts of conditioned souls. But apart from this we again are taking another incarnation specifically to deliver the fallen conditioned souls of this age, that is we also appear in the form of arca avataras, the deity form. These arca avatara forms can be made up of stone or wood, or metal or jewels or paint. Thus this arca form is as good as ours. ## Different types of arca forms

The arca forms are of four types. One is Svayam Vyaktam, that is manifested on its own. Second is daivatam which is being constructed by the demigods. Third is the deity appeared by the prayer of Rishis and fourth and the last is deity carved by human being.

The deities should be made according to the Silpa sastra. The limbs of the deity should not be too long or big or too short of small. The glances of the deity should not be directed towards the sky or towards the earth down, the glance should not be in angry mood. The glance should be straight, divine and full of love. The limbs of the Lord should be beautiful, and thus according to the scripture the deities should be made then we enter into the deity when Pranapratista is done. And whoever worship this deity with devotion according to the rule and regulations in various temples they will eventually achieve the pure devotional service. Thus this deity of Lord Ranganath as we appeared in front of you comes under the category of Svayam Vyakta i.e. which is not sculpted by any personality but is directly manifested from the milk ocean. There are seven other Svayam Vyakta Kshetras, Tirupati Balaji, Saligram, Naimisharanya and Puskara Kshetra, Badrinath; Vanamamalai and Srirangam. These are the eight Svayam Vyakta Kshetras. These are very important deities. Apart from these there are deities which are installed by demigods, Rishies and human beings. Srirangam being a Svayam Vyakta Kshetra or the self manifested deity is having lot of importance.

For a Svayam Vyakta Kshetra, around 100 yojanas are considered as holy place. Anyone who dies within this are certainly get liberation. Other than this the deities which are installed by the demigods, sages or human beings have only 7 yojanas around them is holy place. But the Svayam Vyakta Kshetra such as Srirangam Dham are having the potency to deliver anyone within 100 yojanas. My dear Brahma two yojanas around this holy Dham Srirangam, if anyone within this area taken birth even if they are candelas, even pigs, by the power of this holy place, they will come back to our spiritual world.
