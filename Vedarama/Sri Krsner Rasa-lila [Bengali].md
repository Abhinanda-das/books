# Sri Krsner Rasa-lila \[Bengali\]

## Sri Krsner Rasa-lila \[Bengali\]

(Sri Krsna's Rasa-lila)

(Ramakeli-raga)

### Text 1

*nana barna sampurna sei brndaban*  
*gopi laye krida karibare haila man*

### Text 2

*sarada-purnima-sasi karila udaye*  
*sugandhi-sitala-vayu manohara bahe*

### Text 3

*kokilera kala-raba bhramara jhankar*  
*kusumita dasa-dik basanta abatar*

### Text 4

*naba kisalaya brksa-sobhe brndabane*  
*adhika badila disthi candrera kirane*

### Text 5

*kama abatara hari bamsite nada dila*  
*suniya gokula nari murcchita haila*

### Text 6

*janila govinda bamsi baya brndabane*  
*calila sakala nari eka-citta mane*

### Text 7

*keha ta' swamira kole achila sutiye*  
*keha upakatha kahe bandhu-jana laye*

### Text 8

*keha ta' randhana kare keha karaye bhojan*  
*sisu stana deya kahe sayyaya sayan*

### Text 9

*swamike anna deya keha keha nari*  
*sasudira sange keha grhe karma kari*

### Text 10

*swami sange rase keha karaye su-bes*  
*keha kara mastakera caaye kes*

### Text 11

*alaka-tilaka kare nayane kajal*  
*kanthe hara pare keha sravane kundal*

### Text 12

*tambula khaya keha subasita karpur*  
*mrgamada lepe keha kapale sindur*

### Text 13

*jeijana jemate chila calila sa-tware*  
*brndabane bamsi baya nandera kumare*

### Text 14

*kahare jaite rakhe kara nija-pati*  
*aneka jatane rahe krsne diye mati*

### Text 15

*govinde cintite tara prana karila gaman*  
*mukti-pada paila sei khandila bandhan*

### Text 16

*ara saba nari-gana krsna pase giya*  
*sri-krsne bedi dandaila mandali kariya*

### Text 17

*citrera putali jena cari-dike cay*  
*lajja bhaye keha tara kichu nahi kay*

### Text 18

*kamete pidita tabe gopi-saba haye*  
*dandaila gopi-saba krsnake bediye*

### Text 19

*govinda dekhite gopi eka-drsta haila*  
*hasi hasi govindai tabe kichu baila*

### Text 20

*kena aile gopi-saba ei brndabane*  
*na karile bhaya kichu gahana-kanane*

### Text 21

*ratri-kale ghoratara kanana-bhitare*  
*siba-sata nada kare gahana-gambhire*

### Text 22

*swami chadi nari aila kemana sahase*  
*eta ratre brndabane kahara uddese*

### Text 23

*na kara sahasa suna amara bacan*  
*ghare ghare cahi bule tomara bandhu-jan*

### Text 24

*jhata ghara jaha gopi na thakiha hetha*  
*uddesa na peye swami duhkha pabe tatha*

### Text 25

*swami chadi keha nahi rahe ta' samsare*  
*swamira seba karile haya naraka uddhare*

### Text 26

*swami dharma swami swarga swami se mukti*  
*swami rusta haile haya narake basati*

### Text 27

*ediya ta's swami putra tyaji bandhu-jan*  
*amara thani gopa-badhu aile ki karan*

### Text 28

*jhata cala gopa-bandhu apana bhaban*  
*swami seba kara giya putrera palan*

### Text 29

*eteka bacana jadi govinda balila*  
*heta matha kari gopi kandite lagila*

### Text 30

*buka bahi ankhira jala pade bhumi-tale*  
*basana malina haila nayanera jale*

### Text 31

*ki kariba ki baliba anumana kari*  
*padanguli bhume likhi bale dhiri dhiri*

### Text 32

*kame hata citta gopi anumana guni*  
*laja-santapa-mukhe nahi sare bani*

### Text 33

*saghane niswasa chade kare namaskar*  
*keha nidaya haye prabhu bala byabahar*

### Text 34

*chadiya ta' swami-putra tyaji bandhu-jan*  
*eka-bhabe cinti gosai tomara caran*

### Text 35

*ki kariba ghara-dware swami bandhu-jan*  
*tomaya dekhite prana jauka ekhan*

### Text 36

*chadi jauka swami mora tara nahi katha*  
*tomara nigraha bacan mane lage byatha*

### Text 37

*ki lagi nisthura eta bala cakra-pani*  
*tomara carana cinti chadiba parani*

### Text 38

*janme janme pai jena tomara caran*  
*tumi swamitumi putra tumi bandhu-jan*

### Text 39

*na jaiba ghara saba jata gopa-nari*  
*adhara amrta diya jiyao murari*
