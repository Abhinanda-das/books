# Sudarśana Astottara-śata Nāmāvalli

## Sudarśana Astottara-śata Nāmāvalli

oṁ sudarśanāya namaḥ

oṁ cakra-rājāya namaḥ

oṁ tejo-vyuhāya namaḥ

oṁ mahā-dyutaye namaḥ

oṁ sahasra-bāhave namaḥ

oṁ diptaṅgāya namaḥ

oṁ aruṇākṣāya namaḥ

oṁ pratāpavate namaḥ

oṁ anekāditya-saṅkārśāya namaḥ

oṁ prodva-jvālābhi-rañjitāya namaḥ

oṁ sandāmaṇi-sahasrābhāya namaḥ

oṁ maṇi-kuṇḍala śobhitāya namaḥ

oṁ pañca-bhūta-mano-rūpāya namaḥ

oṁ ṣaṭ-koṇantara-saṁsthitāya namaḥ

oṁ harantaḥ-karaṇadbhūta-roṣabhīṣana-vigrahāya namaḥ

oṁ hari-pāṇi-vatsa-padma-vihārāra-manoharāya namaḥ

oṁ śrākāra-rūpāya namaḥ

oṁ sarvājñāya namaḥ

oṁ sarva-lokārcita prabhave namaḥ

oṁ caturdaśa-sahasrārāya namaḥ

oṁ caturveda-mayāya namaḥ

oṁ analāya namaḥ

oṁ bhakta-cāndramasa-jyotiṣe namaḥ

oṁ bhava-roga-vināśakāya namaḥ

oṁ repadmakāya namaḥ

oṁ mahārātmane namaḥ

oṁ rakṣo-sṛgruṣI-taṅgakāya namaḥ

oṁ sarva-daitya-graivanāla vibhedana mahāgajāya namaḥ

oṁ bhīma daṁstrāya namaḥ

oṁ ujjvalā-karāya namaḥ

oṁ bhīma-karmaṇe namaḥ

oṁ trilocanāya namaḥ

oṁ nīla-vartmane namaḥ

oṁ nitya-sukhāya namaḥ

oṁ nirmala-śriye namaḥ

oṁ nirañjanāya namaḥ

oṁ rakta-mālyām bharadarāya namaḥ

oṁ rakta-candana-ruṣitāya namaḥ

oṁ rāja-guṇākṛtaye namaḥ

oṁ śūrāye namaḥ

oṁ rakṣaḥ-kuleya-mopamāya namaḥ

oṁ nitya-kṣemakarāya namaḥ

oṁ prājñāya namaḥ

oṁ pāṣanda-jana-khaṇḍanāya namaḥ

oṁ nārāyaṇa-jñano-vardhine namaḥ

oṁ naikamārdhana-prakāśakāya namaḥ

oṁ bali-nandana-tordaṇḍa-khaṇḍanāya namaḥ

oṁ vijayā-kṛtaye namaḥ

oṁ mitra-bhāvine namaḥ

oṁ sarva-mayāya namaḥ

oṁ tamo-vidvamsanāya namaḥ

oṁ raja-sattvata-modvartine namaḥ

oṁ triguṇātmane namaḥ

oṁ trilokadṛte namaḥ

oṁ hari-māya-guṇo-bhedāya namaḥ

oṁ avyayāya namaḥ

oṁ akṣisvarūpāje namaḥ

oṁ paramātmane namaḥ

oṁ brahma-jyotiṣe namaḥ

oṁ pañca-kṛtya-parāyaṇāya namaḥ

oṁ jñāna-śakti-balaiśvarya-vīrya-tejaḥ-prabhamayāya namaḥ

oṁ sadasat-paramāya namaḥ

oṁ pūrṇāya namaḥ

oṁ vāṅgmayāya namaḥ

oṁ varadāya namaḥ

oṁ acyutāya namaḥ

oṁ jīvāya namaḥ

oṁ hāraye namaḥ

oṁ haṁsa-rūpāya namaḥ

oṁ pañca-śaṭa-pītha-rūpakāya namaḥ

oṁ mātṛ-kāmaṇḍalā-dhyakṣakāya namaḥ

oṁ madhu-dhvamsine namaḥ

oṁ mano-mayāya namaḥ

oṁ buddhi-rūpāya namaḥ

oṁ siddha-sākṣine namaḥ

oṁ sārāya namaḥ

oṁ haṁsākṣara-dvārāya namaḥ

oṁ mantra-yantra-mayāya namaḥ

oṁ vibhave namaḥ

oṁ sreṣṭhre namaḥ

oṁ kriyāspatāya namaḥ

oṁ śuddhāya namaḥ

oṁ mantre namaḥ

oṁ bhoktre namaḥ

oṁ trivikramāya namaḥ

oṁ nirāyuddhāya namaḥ

oṁ asamrabhāya namaḥ

oṁ sarvai-yuddha samanvitāya namaḥ

oṁ oṁkāra-rūpāya namaḥ

oṁ pūrṇātmane namaḥ

oṁ āṁkārāt-śaṭ-bañjanāya namaḥ

oṁ jayankarāya namaḥ

oṁ vāk-pradāya namaḥ

oṁ vakmine namaḥ

oṁ śrīṁkāraiśvarya-vardhanāya namaḥ

oṁ klīṁkāra-mohanākārāya namaḥ

oṁ huṁ phaṭ-kṣobhaṇā-kṛtaye namaḥ

oṁ indrācita mano-vegāya namaḥ

oṁ dharaṇi-bhāra-nāśakāya namaḥ

oṁ vīrārādyāya namaḥ

oṁ viśvarūpāya namaḥ

oṁ vaiṣṇavāya namaḥ

oṁ viṣṇu-bhaktidāya namaḥ

oṁ satya-vratāya namaḥ

oṁ satya-parāya namaḥ

oṁ satya-dharmāṇu-śañjakāya namaḥ

oṁ nārāyaṇa-kṛpa-vyuha-tejas-cakrāya namaḥ

oṁ sudarśanāya namaḥ
