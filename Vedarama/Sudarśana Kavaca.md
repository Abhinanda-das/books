# Sudarśana Kavaca

## Sudarśana Kavaca

*oṁ namo bhagavate bhoḥ bhoḥ sudarśana*  
*duṣṭa dārīdryam duritam*  
*hana hana jahi jahi pāpaṁ matha matha*  
*samrogyaṁ kuru kuru ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ hrīṁ hīṁ hraṁ*

*oṁ sahasrāraṁ huṁ phaṭ svāhā*  
*oṁ trilokya bhayam kartā tvaṁ*  
*ājñāya janārdana*  
*sarva-dukhāṇi rakṣaṁsi*  
*kṣayam nayaca satvaram*

*oṁ namo bhagavate mahā-sudarśanāya*  
*maho-grāya mahā-cakrāya*  
*mahā-mantra mahā-dhīra*  
*mahā-tejaḥ bhayankarāya*

*sarva-śatrun graśa graśa bhakṣa bhakṣa*  
*para mantrān graśa graśa bhakṣa bhakṣa*  
*para śaktiḥ graśa graśa bhakṣa bhakṣa*  
*mudhan gṛhān graśa graśa bhakṣa bhakṣa*

*daitya dānava rākṣasan graśa graśa bhakṣa bhakṣa*  
*dākiṇī śakiṇī vetalādina graśa graśa bhakṣa bhakṣa*

*gandharva yakṣa rakṣa bhūta preta piśāca brahma-rākṣasādin graśa graśa bhakṣa bhakṣa*

## Sundara Bālā - “Beautiful Boy”

## \<em\>(1)\</em\>

*sundara-bālā śacī-dulālā*  
*nācata śrī-hari-kīrtana meṅ*  
*bhāle candana tilaka manohara*  
*alakā śobhe kapolana meṅ*

**Перевод:**

**The beautiful darling son of mother Śacī is dancing in the kīrtana of Lord Hari’s names. His forehead, adorned with sandalwood tilaka, enchants everyone’s mind as His beautiful locks of hair shine splendrously as they bounce upon His cheeks.**

## \<em\>(2)\</em\>

*śire cūḍā daraśi bāle*  
*vana-phula-mālā hiyāpara dole*  
*pahirana pīta-paṭāmbara śobhe*  
*nūpura ruṇu-jhunu caraṇo meṅ*

**Перевод:**

**His hair is wrapped in a topknot, and a garland of forest flowers sways upon His chest. Wearing brilliant yellow silken garments, He dances as anklets tinkle pleasingly and rhythmically.**

## \<em\>(3)\</em\>

*rādhā-kṛṣṇa eka tanu hai*  
*nidhuvana-mājhe baṁśī bājāy*  
*viśvarūpa ki prabhujī sahi*  
*āota prakaṭahi nadīyā meṅ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, Who dance together in the groves of Nidhuvana with a flute, have combined as one transcendental form. In this form, the Lord of Viśvarūpa has come and manifested Himself in the town of Nadīyā.**

## \<em\>(4)\</em\>

*koi gāyata hai rādhā-kṛṣṇa nām*  
*koi gāyata hai hari-guṇa gān*  
*maṅgala-tāna mṛdaṅga rasāla*  
*bājata hai koi raṅgaṇa meṅ*

**Перевод:**

**Some in that spectacular performance sing the names of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, some glorify Lord Hari by praising His transcendental qualities, while others play fervently the auspicious rhythms of the sweet and relishable mṛdaṅga drums. All this takes place.**

## \<em\>(refrain)\</em\>

*sundara-bālā śacī-dulālā*  
*nācata śrī-hari-kīrtana meṅ*

**Перевод:**

**The beautiful darling son of mother Śacī is dancing in the kīrtana of Lord Hari’s names.**

## “The Preceptor’s Lotus Feet”

## \<em\>(refrain)\</em\>

*śrī guru caraṇ kamal bhaja mana,*  
*guru kṛpā binā nahiṁ koi sādhana-bala,*  
*bhaja mana bhaja anukṣaṇa*

**Пословный перевод:**

śrī guru—of the bonafide spiritual master; caraṇ kamal—the lotus feet; bhaja—please worship; mana—O mind!; guru kṛpā—the mercy of the spiritual master; binā—without; nahiṁ—there is not; koi—any; sādhana-bala—strength to practice devotion; bhaja—please worship; mana—O mind!; bhaja—please worship; anukṣaṇa—incessantly.

**Перевод:**

**O mind, just worship the lotus feet of the bona fide spiritual master. Without receiving the mercy of the guru mahārāja, one will not have the potency to execute regulative spiritual practices. Therefore, O my dear mind, I urge you to please worship him at every moment.**

## \<em\>(1)\</em\>

*milata nahiṁ aisā durlabha janma,*  
*bhramata hī caudaha bhuvana*  
*kisī ko milate hai aho bhāgya se,*  
*hari bhaktoṅ ke darśana*

**Пословный перевод:**

milata—met; nahiṁ—not; aisā—such; durlabha janma—rare human birth; bhramata—wandering; hī—certainly; caudaha bhuvana—fourteen worlds; kisī ko—to someone; milate—gets; hai—is; aho—extremely rare bhāgya—good fortune; se—he; hari bhaktoṅ—the devotees of Hari; ke—of; darśana—audience.

**Перевод:**

**O mind, just worship the lotus feet of the bona fide spiritual master. While the fallen soul wanders throughout the fourteen worlds, it rarely gets an opportunity to receive a human birth. And out of so many human beings, only a few receive the extremely rare good fortune of meeting the devotees of Lord Hari.**

## \<em\>(2)\</em\>

*kṛṣṇa kṛpā kī ānanda mūrti,*  
*dīnana karuṇā-nidhāna*  
*jñāna-bhakti-prema tīno prakāśata,*  
*śrī guru patita pāvana*

**Пословный перевод:**

kṛṣṇa kṛpā kī—of Kṛṣṇa’s mercy; ānanda mūrti—the embodiment of bliss; dīnana—fallen souls; karuṇā-nidhāna—reservoir of mercy; jñāna-bhakti-prema—knowledge, devotion and love; tīno—three; prakāśata—reveals; śrī guru—divine spiritual master; patita pāvana—deliverer of the fallen.

**Перевод:**

**O mind, just worship the lotus feet of the bonafide spiritual master. Śrī Guru is the personification of the bliss inherent in Kṛṣṇa’s causeless mercy, and he is therefore the abode of mercy for souls who are suffering in the material world. He is the revealer of transcendental knowledge, pure devotion, and divine love. Śrī Guru is the savior of fallen conditioned souls.**

## \<em\>(3)\</em\>

*śruti-smṛti iti bhāṣya sabhī mile,*  
*dekhata spaṣṭa pramāṇa*  
*tana-mana-jīvana guru-pade arpaṇa,*  
*sadā śrī hari-nāma raṭana*

**Пословный перевод:**

śruti-smṛti—the Vedic literatures; iti—thus; bhāṣya—explanation; sabhī mile—all together; dekhata—is seen; spaṣṭa pramāṇa—clear evidence; tana-mana-jīvana—body, mind and life; guru-pade—to the feet of the guru; arpaṇa—offering; sadā—always; śrī hari-nāma—the holy names of the Lord; raṭana—repetition.

**Перевод:**

**O mind, just worship the lotus feet of the bonafide spiritual master. His glories are clearly confirmed by the assembly of śruti-śāstras, smṛti-śāstras, as well as in the commentaries of the great ācāryas. Just offer your body, mind, and life itself at the lotus feet of Śrī Guru and always remain engaged in the chanting of Śrī Hari-nāma.**
