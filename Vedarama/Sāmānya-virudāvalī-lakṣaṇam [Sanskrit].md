# Sāmānya-virudāvalī-lakṣaṇam \[Sanskrit\]

## Sāmānya-virudāvalī-lakṣaṇam \[Sanskrit\]

śrī-rūpa-gosvāmi-prabhupāda-praṇītam

The text is taken from Haridas Das's 1941 edition (Nabadwip: Haribol Kutir).

## namaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇāya

praṇamya paramānandaṁ vṛndāraṇya-purandaram \|

likhyate virudāvalyāḥ saṅkṣepāl lakṣaṇaṁ mayā \|\|1\|\|

kalikā-śloka-virudair yutā vividha-lakṣaṇaiḥ \|

kīrti-pratāpa-śauṭīrya-saundaryonmeṣa-śālinī \|\|2\|\|

kalikādy-anta-saṁsargi-padyā doṣa-vivarjitā \|

śabdāḍambara-sambandhā kartavyā virudāvalī \|\|3\|\|

vyutpannaḥ susthiramatir gata-glānir gala-svanaḥ \|

bhaktaḥ kṛṣṇe bhaved yaḥ sa virudāvali-pāṭhakaḥ \|\|4\|\|

tatra **kalikā-**lakṣaṇam——

kalā nāma bhavet tāla-niyatā pada-santatiḥ \|

kalābhiḥ kalikā proktā tad-bhedāḥ ṣaṭ samīritāḥ \|\|5\|\|

kalikā caṇḍa-vṛttākhyā dvigādi-gaṇa-vṛttakā \|

tathā tribhaṅgī-vṛttākhyā madhyā miśrā ca kevalā \|\|6\|\|

tatra **caṇḍa-vṛttavat**——

caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ dvidhā tatra sāmānyaṁ ca sa-lakṣaṇam \|

atra saṁyukta-varṇānāṁ niyamo daśadhā ca te \|\|7\|\|

madhura-śliṣṭa-viśliṣṭa-śithila-hrādinas tv amī \|

bhidyante hrasva-dīrghābhyāṁ te darśyante yathā sphuṭam \|\|8\|\|

hrasvān madhura-saṁyogāḥ śaṅkarāīkuśa-kiṅkarāḥ \|

tasmāt tu śliṣṭa-saṁyuktāḥ darpa-karpara-sarpavat \|\|9\|\|

tato viśliṣṭa-saṁyuktāḥ bhalla-kalyāṇa-cillayaḥ \|

tathā śithila-saṁyuktāḥ paśya kaśyapa-vaśyavat \|\|10\|\|

garhādīn hrādi-saṁyukta-bhedān kecit samūcire \|

dīrghāt tu gāṅgaṁ kārpāsaṁ bālyaṁ viśvaś ca bāhyakam \|\|11\|\|

tatra **kalā-parimāṇam**——

adhikāś cec catuḥṣaṣṭhir nyūnā dvādaśa tāḥ kalāḥ \|

etābhyo nādhikā kāryā nyūnāś cāpi na paṇḍitaiḥ \|\|12\|\|

daśasv api ca saṁyukteṣv ālakṣyo niyamo hi yaḥ \|

ādyāyāṁ sa tu kartavya uttarāsu kalāsv api \|\|13\|\|

tatra **sāmānyaṁ caṇḍa-vṛttam**——

sāmānya-caṇḍa-vṛttākhya-kalikā-lakṣaṇaṁ yathā \|

dalasyaikasya mādīnāṁ1 gaṇānāṁ bandhanaṁ punaḥ \|\|14\|\|

eka-dvi-tri-catuḥ-pañca-kramṇātaḥ paraṁ nahi \|

guror laghor dvayor vātha gaṇās teṣv ekayā dhṛtiḥ \|\|15\|\|

gaṇa-pañcaka-sambaddhā kalā pañca-daśārṇakā \|

laghunā ṣoḍaśa-yutā dvābhyāṁ sapta-daśārṇakāḥ \|\|16\|\|

parā saptadaśārṇebhyaś caṇḍa-vṛtta-kalā nahi \|

nyūnā varṇa-trayāc ceti kalāyāṁ lakṣaṇaṁ tv idam \|\|17\|\|

eko gaṇas tu gurv-anto laghv-anto tad-dviyug yathā \|

tatraiko **gurv-antaḥ**—

kaṁsa-dhvaṁsin puṣpottaṁsin ity ādi \|

laghv-anto, yathā—

bhakti-prīta vakti śrīda ity ādi \|

guru-laghv-anto, yathā—

viśvasya preṣṭha sarvasya śreṣṭha ity ādi \|

guru-dvandvānto, yathā—

raṅgālī-sindho ramyāṅgī-bandho ity ādi \|

laghu-dvandvānto, yathā—

vīra-śrīdhara dhīra-prītida ity ādi \|

dvay trayaś cātha catvāraḥ pañcāpy evaṁ smṛtā matāḥ \|

madhyau gaṇau ca gurv-antau laghv-antau tad-dvayānvitau \|\|18\|\|

tatra **ma-yau kevalau**, yathā—

nanda-preṣṭha viṣṇo kandarpeṣṭa jiṣṇo ity ādi \|

tau **gurv-antau**, yathā—

kālindī-taṭollāsin rolambī-kulodbhāsin ity ādi \|

laghv-antau, yathā—

kālindī-kṛtollāsa sāraṅgī-dhṛtodbhāsa ity ādi \|

guru-dvandvāntau, yathā—

vṛndāraṇya-mallī-nandin mandārambha-khalyā-nindin ity ādi \|

guru-laghv-antau, yathā—

gaurāṅgī kalā-saṅgīta sāraṅgī ghaṭā-saṁvīta ity ādi \|

laghu-dvandvāntau, yathā—

vṛndāraṇya-vallī-priya kundārabdha-vallī-kriya ity ādi \|

gaṇa-trayādikāś caivaṁ budhair jñeyāḥ kramāt kalāḥ \|

sama-vṛttāni padyāni try-akṣarādīni yāni tu \|\|19\|\|

tathā meghākṣarāntāni suśravāṇi nisargataḥ \|

sāmānya-caṇḍa-vṛttasya kalātvaṁ tāni pūrvavat \|

saṁyukta-varṇa-niyamād bhajantīti satāṁ matam \|\|20\|\|

atha **salakṣaṇaṁ caṇḍa-vṛttam**——

dvidhā salakṣaṇaś caṇḍo nakhau viśikha ity api \|

tatra **nakhaḥ**——

nakhākhyaṁ caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ tu proktaṁ viṁśatidhā budhaiḥ \|\|21\|\|

ādau raṇābhidhas tadvad vīrabhadro'parājitam \|

puruṣottama-saṁjñaṁ ca vardhitaṁ veṣṭanaṁ tathā \|\|22\|\|

samagram acyutākhyaṁ ca tathā mātaṅga-khelitam \|

utpalaṁ kandalaḥ kalpa-drumaś cāskhalitaṁ tathā \|\|23\|\|

syāt turaṅgo guṇaratis tathā pallavitaṁ param \|

tarat-samantaṁ kāśākhyaṁ tilakaṁ yati-nartanam \|\|24\|\|

tatra (1) **raṇaḥ**——

ja-ra-tair bhena baddhābhiḥ kalābhiḥ pūrva-saṅkhyayā \|

sarvatra śliṣṭa-yutakaṁ caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ raṇābhidham \|\|25\|\|

yathā——

sa-darpa-sarpa-vispardhi-sphurad-bhuja

prasarpa-vibhrama-dhvasta-dviṣad-dhvaja

sa-darpa-rakta-vipruḍ-vicitrita

praharṣa-cakra-valgaj-jana-stuta \| ity ādi \|

atha (2) **vīrabhadraḥ**——

ma-bha-nair nena baddhābhiś catuḥ śliṣṭa-yutādibhiḥ \|

aṣṭa-mātrāc chidābhis tu kalābhir upakalpitam \|  
vīrabhadratayākhyātaṁ caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ pracakṣate \|\|26\|\|

yathā——

udyad-vidyut pratibhaṭa nava-paṭa

namra-brahma-stuta pada-sarasija

dṛpyad-vatsa stavakita-rasa-bhara

rakṣad-dakṣa priya-jana-parikara \| ity ādi \|

atha (3) **aparājitam**——

bha-sa-ja-glair nibaddhais tair ādau madhura-yogataḥ \|

ṣaṣṭāṣṭama-daśa-sthāne dīrgha-yoga pravolabhiḥ (?) \|

aṁśaiḥ kṛtam idaṁ nāmnā syāc caṇḍam aparājitam \|\|27\|\|

yathā—

daṇḍita-vaka-dānavoru-kāya

khaṇḍita-khala-jāta-bhūr-māya

maṇḍita-vara-nāgarī-vihāra

caṇḍita-pada-dāritāhi-sāra \| ity ādi \|

atha (4) **puruṣottamaḥ**——

sa-sa-bhaiḥ kalpitair aṁśair dīrgha-śliṣṭa-manoharaiḥ \|

kramāt ṣaṣṭhe'ṣṭame tūrye dīrgha-śliṣṭa-samanvitaiḥ \|

nibaddhaṁ caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ tu puruṣottama-nāmakam \|\|28\|\|

yathā—

puruṣottama vīra-vrata

yaumnādbhuta-tīra-sthita

muralī-dhvani-pūra-kriya

surabhī-vraja-nāda-priya \| ity ādi \|

atha (5) **vardhitaṁ**——

bha-na-jair jena laghunā baddhair aṁśaḥ samanvitam \|

ekāṣṭaikādaśa sthāne śliṣṭa-saṁyoga-śālibhiḥ \|

caṇḍa-vṛttam idaṁ nāmnā vardhitaṁ kavayo viduḥ \|\|29\|\|

yathā—

garvita-khala-gaṇa-darpa-vimardana

carvita-danuja-nirargala-vardhana

vikrama-lava-parimardita-pannaga

vibhrama-samaya samuddhṛta-sannaga \| ity ādi \|

atha (6) **veṣṭanaṁ**——

na-ya-lair lena sambaddhaiḥ pañcame dīrgha-śālibhiḥ \|

yena dīrghāt śliṣṭa-yutaiḥ baddhaṁ bhavati veṣṭanam \|\|30\|\|

yathā—

madhurima-pūrādbhuta

yuvati-gaṇopāśrita

ravi-tanayā-tīrthaga

sakhi-paṭalī-veṣṭita \| ity ādi \|

atha (7) **samagram**——

ja-rābhyāṁ kalpitair aṁśais turyān madhura-śālibhiḥ \|

śliṣṭair antyād dvitīyāc ca sarvānte laghu-śālibhiḥ \|

baddhaḥ samagra-nāmāyaṁ caṇḍo mādhava-vallabhaḥ \|\|31\|\|

yathā—

samagra-rañjana sphurat-prabhañjana

praśasta-dāmbara pravalgad-ambara \| ity ādi \|

atha (8) **acyutaḥ**——

na-jābhyāṁ kalpitair aṁśaiū pañcame dīrgha-śālibhiḥ \|

antyāc chliṣṭa-yutopetaiś caṇḍam acyutam ūcire \|\|32\|\|

yathā—

pulina-vihāra sphurad-uru-hāra

priya-parivāra-stuta-gaṇa-bhāra \| ity ādi \|

atha (9) **mātaṅga-khelitam**——

ra-ga-laiḥ kalpitair aṁśaiḥ prathame ca tṛtīyake \|

dīrgha-yuktaiś caturthāt tu madhura-śliṣṭa-yogibhiḥ \|

nibaddhaṁ caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ tu nāmnā mātaṅga-khelitam \|\|33\|\|

tatra (ka) **madhura-yogād**, yathā—

kaṁsa-mātaṅga bheditāraṅga

vīra sāraṅga śīlitāsaṅga \| ity ādi \|

tatra (kha) **śliṣṭa-saṁyogād**, yathā—

gopikā-juṣṭa rādhikā-tuṣṭa

devatā-varga bhāvitānargha \| ity ādi \|

ekatra kecid icchanti nibaddham anayor dvayoḥ \|

yathā—

bhānujā-kuñja-mādhurī-puñja-

lālasākṛṣṭa-rādhikā-dṛṣṭa \| ity ādi \|

atha (10) **utpalaṁ**——

bhābhyāṁ vinirmitair aṁśaiḥ rādyāt tu śliṣṭa-yogataḥ \|

caturthāc ca tathā dhīrā utpalākhyaṁ pracakṣate \|\|34\|\|

yathā—

darpa-samuddhata-sarpa-vinirjaya

dakṣa vilakṣaṇa-pakṣa-hṛta-kṣaṇa \| ity ādi \|

atha (11) **kandalaḥ**——

ādau madhura-saṁyogāt kandalaṁ taṁ vidur budhāḥ \|\|35\|\|

yathā—

khaṇḍita-durjana maṇḍita-sajjana

gañjita-pannaga rañjita-sannaga \| ity ādi \|

atha (12) **kalpa-drumaḥ**——

ta-ja-yaiḥ kalpitair aṁśair baddham ādya-dvitīyataḥ \|

tathā vāṇebha nandebhyaḥ śliṣṭa-saṁyoga-śālibhiḥ \|

etaṁ kalpa-drumaṁ nāma caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ pracakṣate \|\|36\|\|

yathā—

vidyun-nibha-kuṭmala-karṇa

sphūrjaj-jalada-prabha-varṇa

krudhyat-khala-mardana-dakṣa

śruty-uccala-cikkaṇa-pakṣa \| ity ādi \|

atha (13) **askhalitaṁ**——

ta-ra-bhaiḥ lena gaditair aṁśair ādau tu dīrghibhiḥ \|

dvitīyāc ca caturthāc ca ṣaṣṭhāt saptamatas tathā \|

śliṣṭa-saṁyoga-ghaṭitaiś caṇḍo'skhalita-nāmakaḥ \|\|37\|\|

yathā—

kāruṇya-vṛtti-mac-citta-taṭa

tāruṇya-cihna-bhṛd-vaktra-puṭa

cāturya-śikṣita-brahma-hara-

mādhurya-rañjita-svaccha-nara \| ity ādi \|

atha (14) **turaṅgaḥ**——

bha-na-jair lena sambaddhair āder aṣṭamatas tathā \|

sphuran madhura-saṁyuktair aṁśair baddhas turaṅgakaḥ \|\|38\|\|

yathā—

mañjula-vicakila-kuṇḍala

rañjita-varatanu-maṇḍala

saṅgata-yuvati-kadambaka

laṅgima-mukha-śaśi-cumbaka \| ity ādi \|

atha (15) **guṇaratiḥ**——

sa-na-laiḥ kalpitair aṁśais tṛtīye dīrgha-śālibhiḥ \|

baddhaṁ guṇa-ratiṁ nāmnā caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ pracakṣate \|\|39\|\|

yathā—

yamunā-taṭa-cara nava-nāgara-vara

danuja-balihara madhurākṛti-dhara \| ity ādi \|

atha (16) **pallavitam**——

bha-ta-naiḥ nena laghunā baddhair aṁśair manoramaiḥ \|

ādyād viśliṣṭa-yutakaiś caturthe pañcame tathā \|

dīrghodāraiḥ samāyuktaṁ caṇḍaṁ pallavitābhidham \|\|40\|\|

yathā—

vallava-līlā-samudaya-paricita

pallava-rāgādhara-puṭa-vilasita

vallabha-goṣṭhī-parivṛta-muni-gaṇa

durlabha-kelī-bhara-madhurima-kaṇa \| ity ādi \|

atha (17) **tarat-samantaṁ**——

ta-ma-lair la-dvayenotthaiḥ pañcame pada-bhaṅgibhiḥ \|

dvitīyāc chliṣṭa-yogādyaiś caturthān madhurojjvalaiḥ \|\|41\|\|

paūcamāc-chliṣṭa-yogādyaiḥ ṣaṣṭhe dīrgha-manoharaiḥ \|

navame ca tarat-pūrvaṁ samantaṁ paricakṣate \|\|42\|\|

yathā—

vipakṣa-gandha-kṣepaṇa-kovida

vinamra-vṛnda-krīḍana-śobhita

nisarga-raṅga-bhrājita-bhūdhara

samagra-bimba-śrī-rucirādhara \| ity ādi \|

atha (18) **kāśākhyaṁ**——

bha-bh-ra-lair ghaṭitair aṁśair āder madhura-yogibhiḥ \|

caturthāc chliṣṭa-yutakaiḥ saptame navame tathā \|

dīrgha-saṁyogibhir yuktaṁ kāśākhyaṁ caṇḍam ūcire \|\|43\|\|

yathā—

piñchala-sad-ghana-nīla-keśa

candana-carcita-cāru-veśa

khaṇḍita-durjana-vāra-māya

maṇḍita-nirmala-nīla-kāya \| ity ādi \|

atha (19) **tilakaṁ**——

na-na-s-na-naiḥ kṛtair aṁśair navamān madhurojjvalaiḥ \|

nibaddhaṁ caṇḍa-vṛttaṁ tu tilakākhyam athocire \|\|44\|\|

yathā—

vraja-vara-tanu-mukha-cumbana-paṭutara

taraṇi-duhitṛ-taṭa-maṇḍana-parikara

tailaka-pada-vinita-kuṅkuma-parimala

kaca-calad-avikala paṅkaja-nava-dala \| ity ādi \|

atha (20) **yati-nartanam**——

sābhyāṁ reṇa ca lābhyāṁ ca kḷptair aṁśais tṛtīyataḥ \|

ṣaṣṭhāc ca śliṣṭa-saṁyuktaiḥ saptamān madhurānvitaiḥ \|

navamāc ca punaḥ śliṣṭa-yogibhir yati-nartanam \|\|45\|\|

yathā—

kalita-prabala-spanda-vibhrama

yuvati-prakara-skandha-vartita

kara-kuṭmalaka-granthi-sajjita

nava-garbhaka-sukuntala-vraja \| ity ādi \|

atha **viśikhākhyaś caṇḍaḥ**——

syāt pañcadhā sa viśikhaḥ padmaṁ kundaṁ ca campakam \|

vañjulaṁ bakulaṁ ceti kāmasyāṁī śarā matāḥ \|\|46\|\|

tatra (1) **padmam**——

acyutas tu bhavet padmaṁ pañcamād yukta-saṁyutaḥ \|\|47\|\|

yathā—

jaya jaya nanda-vraja-jana-śanda

smara-mada-dhṛṣṭa priya-jana-hṛṣṭa \| ity ādi \|

idam eva budhāḥ kecit padmaṁ dīrghāntimaṁ jaguḥ \|

ataḥ sarva-kalānte tu bhavel laghu-catuṣṭayam \|\|48\|\|

yathā—

jaya jaya padmā-priyatama-padmā-

dbhuta-kara-sandīpita-rasa-vandī-

kṛta-sura... \| ity ādi \|

asyaiva kecid icchanti la-catuṣṭayam uttaram \|\|49\|\|

yathā—

kuṇṭhīkṛta-khala-jhiṇṭi-ruci-paṭa

sambhīṣita khala-kumbhīśvara-gata \| ity ādi \|

idam eva bhavet padmaṁ gaditaṁ ṣaḍvidhaṁ budhaiḥ \|

paṅkeruhaṁ sitaṁ kañjaṁ tathā pāṇḍūtpalaṁ matam \|

indīvarāruṇāmbhoje kahlāraṁ ceti kīrtitam \|\|50\|\|

tatra (a) **paṅkeruham**——

syāc cet ka-vargya-madhuraṁ pūrva-pañcama-varṇakam \|

padmaṁ paṅkeruhaṁ proktaṁ kaikā-vedibhir budhaiḥ \|\|51\|\|

yathā—

jaya gata-śaṅka praṇayi-baṅka

dviṣad-uru-ṭaṅka praṇaya-viṭaṅka \| ity ādi \|

tad-bhedaḥ—

jaya jaya bhṛṅgī mudita-kuraṅgī

dṛśudita-bhaṅgī ity ādi \|

atha (b) **sita-kuñjam**——

ca-vargya-madhuropetaṁ tad bhavet sita-kuñjakam \|\|52\|\|

yathā—

jaya kaca-cañcad-dyuti-samudañcan-

madhurima-pañca stavakita-piñcha \| ity ādi \|

tad-bhedaḥ—

jaya jaya muñjānala-hara guñjā-priya ity ādi \|

evaṁ sarvatra jñeyam \|

atha (c) **pāṇḍūtpalam**——

ṭa-vargya-madhuropetaṁ pāṇḍūtpalam udīryate \|\|53\|\|

yathā—

jaya jaya daṇḍa-priya kaca-ṣaṇḍa

grathita-śikhaṇḍa ity ādi \|

atha (d) **indīvaram**——

ta-vargya-madhuropetaṁ tad evendīvaraṁ viduḥ \|\|54\|\|

yathā—

jaya jaya hanta dviṣad-ibha-hantar

madhurima-santarpita-jagad-antar ity ādi \|

atha (e) **aruṇāmbhojam——**

ta-vargya-madhuropetam aruṇāmbhojam ucyate \|\|55\|\|

yathā—

jaya rasa-sampad-viracita-jhampa

smara-kṛta-kampa priya-jana-śampa ity ādi \|

atha (f) **kahlāram——**

viśliṣṭa-yutam etat tu kahlāraṁ paricakṣate \|\|56\|\|

yathā—

jaya rasa-phulla smara-śara-tulya

prabha-nakha-bhara vraja-kula-malla ity ādi \|

atha (2) **kundam**——

bha-jābhyāṁ kalpitair aṁśaiḥ prāyaś chinnair dviśo'kṣaraiḥ \|

ādya-pañca-ma-yor yuktottaritaiḥ kundam ucyate \|\|57\|\|

yathā—

nanda-kula-candra lupta-bhava-tandra

kunda-jayi-danta duṣṭa-kula-hantaḥ \| ity ādi \|

pañcamaṁ ced dīrgha-yutaṁ kunda-bhedas tadocyate \|

yathā—

rukma-bhava-pīta vastra-parivīta \|

citta-hara śīta mañju-kala-gīta \| ity ādi \|

atha (3) **campakam——**

bha-nābhyāṁ kalpitair aṁśair yukta-pūrvādi-varṇakaiḥ \|

sānuprāsaiḥ pratiyugam ādāv ante'pi campakam \|\|58\|\|

yathā—

sañcalad-aruṇa cañcala karuṇa

sundara-nayana kandara-śayana \| ity ādi \|

atha (4) **vañjulam——**

na-ja-lair ghaṭitair aṁśair madhurottara-pañcamaiḥ \|

nibaddhaṁ vañjulaṁ nāma kalikā-vedināṁ matam \|\|59\|\|

yathā—

jaya jaya sundara vihasita-mandara

nija-giri-kandara rati-rasa-śaṅkara \| ity ādi \|

bhaved vañjula-bhedas tu tad eva śliṣṭa-saṁyutaḥ \|

yathā—

jaya maṇi-darpaṇa nakhara-mud-arpaṇa

ruci-jita-darpaka vipad-apasarpaka \| ity ādi \|

atha (5) **bakulam——**

śṛṅkhalābaddha-ghaṭitaṁ bakulaṁ paricakṣate \|

bhāsuraṁ maṅgalaṁ tuṅgam iti syād bakulaṁ tridhā \|\|60\|\|

atha (a) **bhāsuram—**

mithaḥ śṛṅkhalitair baddhaṁ bhāsuraṁ pajjhaṭī-padaiḥ \|\|61\|\|

yathā—

jaya jaya vaṁśī-vādya viśārada

śārada-sarasīruha-paribhāvaka

bhāva-kalita locana-sañcāraṇa

cāraṇa snigdha vadhū-dhṛti-hāraka \| ity ādi \|

atha (b) **maṅgalam**——

dviśaḥ śṛṅkhalitair aṁśair bhābhyāṁ baddhais tu maṅgalam \|\|62\|\|

yathā—

tvaṁ jaya keśava keśavalastuta

vīrya-vilakṣaṇa lakṣaṇa bodhita \| ity ādi \|

atha (c) **tuṅgaḥ**——

aṁśair mithaḥ śṛṅkhalitaiḥ saṁyukta-niyamojjhitaiḥ \|

nibaddhaṁ kundam evātra tuṅgam ity abhidhīyate \|\|63\|\|

yathā—

nātha jaya kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa-dāna-rāji

rāji-ruci-jāta jāta-rati-bandha

bandhurita netra \| ity ādi \|

evaṁ prakārair aparair madhura-śliṣṭa-yogataḥ \|

viśliṣṭa-śithilādīnāṁ yogataś ca paraḥ śatāḥ \|

caṇḍa-vṛtta-prabandhāḥ syuḥ kas tān kārtsnyena vakṣyati \|\|64\|\|

## atha dvigādi-gaṇa-vṛtta-kalikā

dvigādi-gaṇa-vṛttākhyā kalikātha nigadyate \|

eṣā tu kalikābhijñaiḥ kathyate mañjarīty api \|\|65\|\|

pañcadheyaṁ dvigādiś ca rādir mādiś ca kīrtitā \|

caturthī syān na kalikā gānākhyā pañcamī matā \|\|66\|\|

korako gucchakaś caiva samphullaṁ kusumaṁ tathā \|

gandhaś ceti dvigādyādi-kalikākhyāḥ smṛtāḥ kramāt \|\|67\|\|

caṇḍa-vṛtte tu yaḥ prokto niyamaḥ khalu saṁyute \|

dvigādi-gaṇa-vṛtte tat naiva badhnanti paṇḍitāḥ \|\|68\|\|

nātidīrgheha kalikā nātihrasvā ca sammatā \|

atha **dvigādi-kalikā----**

dvigādi-kalikāṁ dhīrāḥ korakākhyāṁ pracakṣate \|\|69\|\|

dviga-bhasnala-sandarbhair ananya-gaṇa-pātibhiḥ \|

svecchāvasānaiḥ saṅkḷptāṁ dvigādi-kalikāṁ viduḥ \|\|70\|\|

atra gaṇāḥ—śaure mādhava bhagavan murahara \|

tad udāharaṇam—

mānavatī mada-hāri vilocana

dānava maṇḍala ghūka-virocana \| ity ādi \|

atha **rādi-kalikā----**

abhijñai rādi-kalikā gucchākhyā vinigadyate \|

ra-n-ga-bh-na-la-lair baddhair ananya-gaṇa-saṅgibhiḥ \|\|71\|\|

tatra gaṇāḥ—he hare mura-ripo keśihara baka-mathana \|

tad-udāharaṇam—

nava-jalada sañcaya-dyuti-nikara sundara \|

śruti-calita-kuṇḍala prabha-madhura-candraka \| ity ādi \|

atha **mādi-kalikā----**

mādiṁ tu kalikāṁ prāhur dhīrāḥ samphulla-saṁjñakām \|

ma-s-ta-jair na-ma-nair jābhyāṁ na-trayeṇa ca kalpitaiḥ \|\|72\|\|

tṛtīyāyāṁ tu mātrāyām acchinnaiḥ sva-gaṇāṅkitaḥ \|

sveṣṭāvasānaiḥ sambaddhā syān mādi-kalikā dalaiḥ \|\|73\|\|

atra gaṇāḥ—kaṁsāre vana-mālin keśiripo kamala-nayana \|

tad-udāharaṇam—

piñchālī-dala-pālī dhātu-ghaṭā-kusuma-jaṭā \|

mālākula racitātula veśa-ghaṭana pulina-naṭana \|\| ity ādi \|

atha **na-kalikā----**

ācakṣate na-kalikāṁ kusumākhyāṁ vicakṣaṇāḥ \|

nair eva svāvadhi-cchinnaiḥ kḷiptā na-kalikā matā \|\|74\|\|

yāthā—

kusuma-nikara-racita-cikura

kiraṇa-vijita madhura-mudira

kamala-rucira nayana-yugala

vasana-dalita kanaka-paṭala \| ity ādi \|

atha **gāna-kalikā----**

eṣā tu gāna-kalikā gandhākhyā kathitā budhaiḥ \|

ādi-gena tu nenaiva sarvānte ca gaṇa-spṛśā \|

baddhā syād gāna-kalikā kalikā-vedināṁ matā \|\|75\|\|

yathā—

puṣpa-śara-koṭi-ruci-hāri-pada-padma-nakha

gopa-kula-moda-para cārutara-veśa-dhara \|\| deva \|\|

## atha tribhaṅgī-vṛtta-kalikā

atha tribhaṅgī-vṛttākhyā kalikā procyate'dhunā \|

vṛtteṣu kalikānāṁ ca viśeṣe sā sphuṭībhavet \|\|76\|\|

trayāṇām iha bhaṅgānāṁ samāhāras tribhaṅgikā \|

bhaṅgo'nuprāsa-rūpeṇa varṇāvṛttir iheṣyate \|\|77\|\|

saṁyuktaś cātra dīrghaś ca dvayam eva satāṁ matam \|

mālā bālā ca śālā ca mallī vallī ca cillikā \|\|78\|\|

ṣaḍ-vidhā tu tribhaṅgīyam ādau śikhariṇī matā \|

turagākhyā daṇḍakākhyā bhujaṅgākhyā caturthikā \|\|79\|\|

tigmākhyā ca vidagdhākhyā ṣaḍ atra kalikā matāḥ \|

tatra **śikhariṇī—**

śikhariṇyāṁ trayo bhaṅgaś catuḥṣaṣṭha-trayodaśe \|\|80\|\|

tatra padye vṛttir, yathā—

iyaṁ mallī-vallī hasati hari-hallīśaka sakhī

gatā vāṭī nāṭī lalita-paripāṭī maghabhida ity ādi \|

kalikāyāṁ, yathā—

vraja-nārī hārī pulina vara-vihārī mādhava

vara-vaṁśī-haṁsī vilasita-bhava-śaṁsī-nāgara ity ādi \|

atha **turaga-tribhaṅgī**——

gābhyāṁ ja-sa-ra-nais tena bha-la-gais turagaṁ viduḥ \|

bhaṅgas trayo'sya vikhyātā dvitīyāṣṭama-ṣoḍaśe \|

aṣṭādi-ṣoḍaśāntāsya kalikā padya-vṛttigā \|\|81\|\|

yathā—

dhvastāravinda-ruci hastāgra-piṣṭaka-samantāri-maṇḍala hare \| ity ādi \|

atha **daṇḍaka-tribhaṅgī**——

daṇḍakeṣu ca sarvatra daśame bhaṅga īritaḥ \|

tac-caturthāt tac-caturthe tac-caturthe kramād iha \|\|82\|\|

yāvat tad-gaṇa-vṛddhiḥ syāt tāvad bhaṅgāḥ prakīrtitāḥ \|

etādṛśeṣu vṛtteṣu tribhaṅgīty upalakṣaṇam \|\|83\|\|

caturbhaṅgī pañca-bhaṅgī ṣaḍ-bhaṅgī caiva kīrtitā \|

tatra caṇḍa-vṛṣṭi-prapātādi daṇḍake, yathā—

madhu-mathana hare murāre purārer apāre vihāre

surārer udāre ca dāre prabho !

sphurasi ravi-sutā-taraṅge saraṅge sutuṅgetigaṅge

bhujaṅgendra saṅge sad-aṅgena bhoḥ \|\| ity ādi \|

atha **bhujaṅga-tribhaṅgī**——

tṛtīya-ṣaṣṭha-navame bhujaṅge bhaṅga īritaḥ \|

tatra padye bhujaṅga-vṛttir, yathā—

tribhaṅgī suraṅgī kuraṅgīkṣaṇābhir

vilāsena rāse suvāsena mugdha ity ādi \|

aṣṭādi-ṣoḍaśās tā tu kalikā proktayor dvayoḥ \|\|84\|\|

atha **tigma-tribhaṅgī**——

gālā dvādaśadhā proktāś catvāro gās tataḥ parāḥ \|

yatra vṛtte tu bhaṅgaḥ syāt ṣaḍviṁśe tat tṛtīyake \|\|85\|\|

trayoviṁśākṣarāt prokto niyamas tatra saṁyute \|

prathamāc ca tṛtīyāc ca madhura-śliṣṭa-yojanā \|\|86\|\|

evaṁ ṣaḍ-vāram āvṛttā saiṣā tigma-tribhaṅgikā \|

evam aṣṭa-padī proktā virudālyāṁ kvacid bhavet \|\|87\|\|

yathā—

indu-śubhra-danta-paṅktir ambujanma-vṛndārpa-nindi-

rakta-phulla-dṛṣṭi-śālī-mālī \|

sañcalat-kadamba-puṣpa-saṅgi-karṇa-lambi-ramya-

kunda-guccha-bhaṅgir iṣṭa-saṅgī raṅgī \|\| ity ādi \|

atha **vidagdha-tribhaṅgī**——

dvitīyāṣṭamayoḥ sthāne bhaṅgaś caiva caturdaśe \|

bha-dvaye sādhv-anuprāso yatraikā kalikedṛīśī \|\|88\|\|

na-laghvanta-guru-dvandva-try-āvṛttir bha-dvayaṁ tathā \|

evam aṣṭakam ārabhya kalā yāvat tu ṣoḍaśa \|\|89\|\|

yasyām evaṁ vidhaṁ lakṣma sā vidagdha-tribhaṅgikā \|

sameyaṁ virudāvalyāṁ kalikā-padyayos tathā \|\|90\|\|

yathā—

saṅgītaka-nava bhaṅgī-parimala

raṅgītvam adhika phulla-samullasa

vṛndāvana-bhava mandāra-kusuma

vṛndārcita-pada-pallava vallava \| ity ādi \|

evam ādyā tribhaṅgyās tu stava-kāvyeṣu dīptidāḥ \|

vṛtte padye'py anantāḥ syuḥ kas tāḥ kārtsnyena vakṣyati \|\|91\|\|

## atha madhya-kalikā

ādāv ante ca gadyaṁ syāt kalikā madhyataḥ sthitā \|

tāṁ madhya-kalikāṁ prāha piṅgalo bhujageśvaraḥ \|\|92\|\|

yathā—

uddaṇḍa-savya-bāhu-daṇḍa-caṇḍima-khaṇḍita-duṣṭa-turaṅga-dānava muṇḍa \|

daṇḍita-durjana maṇḍita-sajjana kuṇḍali-mardana kuṇḍala-nartana

sundara-gaṇḍa-sthala bhāṇḍīra-piṇḍikā-raṅga-tāṇḍava-paṇḍita ! \|\|vīra\|\| ity ādi \|

## atha miśra-kalikā

tila-taṇḍulavad yatra kalikā-gadyayor yutiḥ \|

sā miśra-kalikā nāma ṣaṇmukhena prakīrtitā \|\|93\|\|

yathā—

durdānta-daitya-kaṇṭaka-kula kula-kṣaya-keli-pūra

nandita-vallava-yauvata-maṇḍala

vandita-nanda-taraṅgita-kuṇḍala

bandhura-manmatha-vibhrama-nadira

mandara-sundara-bhūdhara-kandara

nandita-mañjula-vikrama-ḍambara

ramya-kalinda-nandinī-kulālaṅkāra

gokula-karīṣaṅkaṣa-vāhlāsura-pāna-bhogīndra \|

kuṅkuma-ḍambara visphurad-ambara

baddha-śikhaṇḍaka pannaga-daṇḍaka \|

rāma-śrīdāma-varūthapādi-suhṛd-varūtha-

datta-mahotsava-maṇḍala \|\|vīra\|\| ity ādi \|

athavā miśra-kalikā nānā-vidha-kalāśritā \|

vibhakti-saptakālambi-sambudhy-antā budhaiḥ smṛtā \|\|94\|\|

yathā—

nandati hārī kunda-vihārī

tad bhaja kṛṣṇaṁ bhakti-satṛṣṇaṁ

nāgara-guruṇā mardita-caraṇā \|\| ity ādi \|

## atha gadya-kalikā

kvacin nānā-vidhaṁ gadyaṁ kalikā-sthale iṣyate \|

tasyodāharaṇaṁ spaṣṭaṁ tenālaṁ likhana-śramaiḥ \|\|95\|\|

etābhis tu tathānyābhiḥ kalikābhir nirantarā \|

virudāvalir ānandaṁ racayaty agha-vidviṣaḥ \|\|96\|\|

ādikṣāntāvaliḥ sarva-laghvī cātra vibadhyate \|

tatra **akṣara-mayī**—

a satābhra-sundara ārāma-ramya-sindhura

indra-makha-bhañjana īti-bhava-gañjana

uttama-puruṣa ūrjita-pauruṣa

ṛkṣojjvala-nakha ity ādi \|

sarva-laghvī, yathā—

vraja-vara-tanu-gaṇa rati-paricaya-caṇa

tanu-dhṛta-dadhi-kaṇa nija-jana-paripaṇa

pada-vilasitaraṇa vidalita-phaṇi-phaṇa \| ity ādi \|

iti kalikā-lakṣaṇam

atha **ślokaḥ——**

kalikādy-antayoḥ ślokaḥ sa-guṇotkarṣa īryate \|\|97\|\|

yathā—

jhamaj-jhamiti varṣati stanita-cakra-vikrīḍayā

vimuṣṭa-ravi-maṇḍale ghana-ghaṭābhir ākhaṇḍale \|

rarakṣa dharaṇī-dharoddhṛti-paṭuḥ kuṭumbāni yaḥ

sa dārayatu dāruṇaṁ vraja-purandaras te daram \|\|

atha **virudaḥ——**

vāśikaḥ kalpitaś ceti virudo dvividho mataḥ \|

saṁyukta-niyamo hy atra varṇitaḥ pūrvavad budhaiḥ \|\|98\|\|

dvi-catuḥ ṣaḍ-daśaś cātra kalās tu virude matāḥ \|

daśabhyo nādhikāḥ kāryāḥ kalās tu virude budhaiḥ \|\|99\|\|

kalikābhyas tu virude bhidāsāv eva kīrtitā \|

virudaṁ kavayaḥ prāhur guṇotkarṣādi-varṇanam \|\|100\|\|

virudaḥ kalikā cānte dhīra-vīrādi-śabda-bhāk \|

na pañca-trikato nyūnā nādhikā triṁśatas trikā \|\|101\|\|

ramyayā virudāvalyā prokta-lakṣaṇa-yuktayā \|  
stūyamānaḥ pramudito vāsudevaḥ prasīdati \|\|102\|\|

yaḥ stauti virudāvalyā sal-lakṣaṇa-vihīnayā \|

paṭhantam api taṁ sādhu naivāṅgīkurute hariḥ \|\|103\|\|
