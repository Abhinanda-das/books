# Tattva-Sūtras. Commentary by Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura \[BS\]

## 1. Atha Tattva-Prakaraṇaṁ. Discussion on the Truth

### Text 1

*ekaḥ paro nānyaḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There is only one Supreme Lord and not other.**

**Комментарий:**

*praṇamya kṛṣṇa-caitanyaṁ bhāradvājaṁ sanātanam \|*  
*tattva-sūtraṁ sa-vyākhyānaṁ bhāṣāyāṁ vivṛtaṁ mayā \|\|*

**Offering respects to eternal Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, of Bharadvāja gotra, I write an explanation on Tattva-sūtra with its commentary.**

*sac-cid-ānanda-sāndrātmā sāra-grāhi-jana-priyaḥ \|*  
*dīna-kāruṇya-pūrābdhir jīyān madana-mohanaḥ \|\|*  
*tat-kṛpāmṛta-bindūdyat pipāsa-stokitāśayaḥ \|*  
*prācīna-tattva-sūtrāṇi vivṛṇomi yathā-mati \|\|*

**May Madana-mohana, composed of condensed eternity, knowledge and bliss, dear to persons who take the essence, a full ocean of mercy to the miserable, remain victorious. I write the ancient tattva-sūtras according to my understanding, having a slight thirst for drops of his sweet mercy.**

There is only one Supreme Lord and no other entity is equal to him. Śruti explains this.

*ekam evādvitīyaṁ*

**Previous to creation of the universe only Brahman (sat) existed. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**6.2.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chan-upa/part-6.chapter-2.621)**)**

*neha nānāsti kiñcana*

**In the Lord there is no material variety. (Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**4.4.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/4.4.19)**)**

“Authors like Vyāsa begin their works with the word atha as in athāto brahma-jijñāsā and athāto dharma-jijñāsā, to indicate auspiciousness and the topic of inquiry for the person desiring knowledge of the subject.

One begins the work after making a promise in the form of indicating the subject with a maṅgalācaraṇa, by indicating the topic to be discussed in scriptures concerning dharma and Brahman as knowledge dependent on the desire of the person. Why has the author commenced Tattva-sūtra without this element?”

It is not necessary to present a maṅgalācaraṇa separately since at the very beginning of this work the Supreme Lord, who is the very form of auspiciousness, is presented in a definition. It is improper to indicate the subject with a promise to show the necessity of inquiry about this topic, since the entity who is the goal of this scripture is self-manifesting and thus not a dependent being manifested by the desire of a person to gain knowledge, but rather perceptible by self-accomplished understanding (of the jīva).

Thus the sūtra was written without these traditional elements.

### Text 2

*aguṇo’pi sarva-śaktir ameyatvāt \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is endowed with all śaktis, though he is beyond material guṇas, since he is beyond material perception.**

**Комментарий:**

*parāsya śaktir vividhaiva śrūyate*  
*svābhāvikī jñāna-bala-kriyā ca*

**It is said that he has various superior śaktis. He has natural activity through jñāna and bala. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**6.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.8)**)**

The doubt may arise that it is not possible for the Supreme Lord, who is without a second, without assistance, to carry out various actions of creating the universe, etc. This is answered in the next sūtra.

### Text 3

*viruddha-sāmānyaṁ tasminn acitram \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**It is not surprising that the Lord has contrary qualities in him to assist him.**

**Комментарий:**

*apāṇi-pādo javano gṛhītā paśyaty acakṣuḥ sa śṛṇoty akarṇaḥ*

**Without hands and feet, he moves quickly. Without eyes he sees. Without ears he hears. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**3.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/3.19)**)**

“Though the Lord is beyond the guṇas, and possesses all śaktis, how can contrary qualities exist in him?” That is answered in the next sūtra.

### Text 4

*sa sac-cid-ānando jñānāgamyo, bhakti-viṣayatvāt \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is eternity, consciousness and bliss, beyond material knowledge of words and mind, since he is only understood by bhakti.**

**Комментарий:**

*yad vācā’nabhyuditam yena vāg abhyudyate*  
*tad eva brahma tvaṁ viddhi nedaṁ yad idam upāsate*

**That which cannot be revealed by words, by which speech has the power to reveal meaning, know that to be Brahman. What common people worship is not Brahman. (Kena Upaniṣad** [**1.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/kena/1.4)**)**

*yan manasā na manute yenahur mano matam*  
*tad eva brahma tvaṁ viddhi nedaṁ yad idam upāsate*

**That which cannot be conceived of by the mind, but which comprehends the mind, know that to be Brahman. That which the common people know through their mind and intellect is not Brahman. (Kena Upaniṣad** [**1.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/kena/1.5)**)**

*bhaktyāham ekayā grāhyaḥ*

**I am only understood by bhakti. (SB** [**11.14.21**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/14.21)**)**

“If the Lord is understood or realized by bhakti, he must be realized within the material world only.” The next sūtra refutes this.

### Text 5

*sa ca satyo nityo’nādir ananto, deśa-kālāparicchedāt \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is real, eternal, without beginning and without end, since he is not limited by place and time.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is real, since he makes the nonexistent become existent.

*satyaṁ jñānam anantaṁ brahma*

**Brahma is existence beyond upādhis, is knowledge devoid of restriction, and is unlimited in qualities and svarūpa. (Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**2.1.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.1.2)**)**

*avināśī vā are’yam ātmā*

**The ātmā is indestructible. (Bṛhad āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**4.5.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/4.5.14)**)**

The Lord had no beginning or end since he is devoid of limitations of place and time.

*sa bhūmiṁ viśvato vṛtvātyātiṣṭhad*

**The Lord remained, spreading everywhere. (Puruṣa-sūkta 1)**

*sarvam āvṛtya tiṣṭhati*

**Paramātmā covers everything in the universe. (BG** [**13.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/13.14)**)**

“Since the Lord is not material why does he create the material universe?” The next sūtra answers this.

### Text 6

*paro’pi cij-jaḍābhyāṁ vilāsī, viśva-siddheḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord, though superior to jīvas and prakṛti, has various pastimes related to creation of the universe of matter and jīva.**

**Комментарий:**

*sa aikṣata eko’haṁ bahu syāṁ prajāyeyeti*

**He glanced at prakṛti. May I become many; let me create progeny. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**6.2.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chan-upa/part-6.chapter-2.623)**)**

*yato vā imāni bhūtāni jāyante*

**From the Lord all these beings are born. (Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**3.1.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/3.1.1)**)**

That the śakti is diffferent from the Lord is refuted next.

### Text 7

*tac-chaktis tat-tattvādhikyam iti cet, na, tad-abhedāt \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one proposes that the Lord and his śakti are different, it is not so, since they are nondifferent.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord and his śakti which produces the universe are not different. They are not separate entities, for it is said śaktiśaktimator abheda: the śakti and possessor of the śakti are nondifferent.

“There is no proof of this. The fire’s power to burn and the fire are seen to be different. It is known by everyone.”

*svābhāvikī jñāna-bala-kriyā ca*

**He has natural activity through jñāna and bala. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**6.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.8)**)**

“If the Lord creates the universe, etc., he undergoes change.” This is refuted in the next sūtra.

### Text 8

*kartāpy avikāraḥ, svātantryāt \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though the Lord is the agent, he does not undergo change, since he is independent.**

**Комментарий:**

Though he creates the universe he is not transformed with attachment or repulsion etc. since he is independent of the rules of his creation. Since he is not dependent on the universe’s rules, he is devoid of change, though he creates it.

*niṣkalaṁ niṣkriyaṁ śāntaṁ niravadyaṁ nirañjanaṁ*

**The Lord is without material limbs, has no duties, is devoid of the six urges, is faultless, and is devoid of material contact. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**6.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.19)**)**

In creating and destroying the universe, that the Lord is not increased or decreased is indicated in the next sūtra.

### Text 9

*sadaika-rūpaḥ, pūrṇatvāt \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He is always situated in one form since he is complete.**

**Комментарий:**

Though the Lord creates and destroys the universe he is always in one form, devoid of increase or decrease, just as the ocean does not increase or decrease with the increase or decrease of river water. This is because the Lord is complete.

*pūrṇam adaḥ pūrṇam idaṁ pūrṇāt pūrṇam udacyate*

**The unmanifest Brahman is perfect. The manifested Brahman is perfect. From the unmanifest Brahman appears the manifest Brahman as various avatāras. (Īśopaniṣad** [**1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/iso/1)**)**

“If the Lord is complete, what is his reason for creating the universe?” This is answered in the next sūtra.

### Text 10

*kāruṇyaṁ tat-kriyā-hetur nānyad āpta-kāmatvāt \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The reason for creation is his mercy and nothing else, since he has attained all his desires.**

**Комментарий:**

Jīvas however, because of their various desires, become engaged in action.

*ātmanaḥ kāmāya sarvaṁ priyaṁ bhavati*

**Everything is dear not for loving those objects, but for loving the ātmā. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**2.4.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/2.4.5)**,** [**4.5.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/4.5.6)**)**

The Lord is not like that since he is complete in his desires already.

*satya-kāmaḥ satya-saṅkalpaḥ*

**All his desires are fulfilled. Everything manifests by his will. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**8.7.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chan-upa/part-8.chapter-7.871)**)**

*nānavāptam avāptavyam*

**What results are to be obtained are not already unattained by me. (BG** [**3.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/3.22)**)**

## 2. Cit-Padārtha-Prakaraṇaṁ. Discussion on the Spiritual Object (Jīva)

What is the Lord’s mercy and for what purpose does the Supreme Lord show mercy in creating and destroying the universe? All Vedānta states that the universe is created by the Lord for the jīvas. In order to understand the svarūpa of the jīva, this section on cit-padārtha (conscious entities) begins.

### Text 11

*cetanāḥ parānugatās tad-vidhi-vaśyatvāt \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The many jīvas, conscious beings, are subservient to the Supreme Lord since they are subjected to the rules he has made.**

**Комментарий:**

*ya ātmani tiṣṭhan ātmānam antar yamayati*

**The Lord residing in the jīva controls the jīva from within. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**3.7.22**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/3.7.22)**)**

*īśvaraḥ sarva-bhūtānāṁ hṛd-deśe’rjuna tiṣṭhati*

**The Lord resides in the heart of all beings, O Arjuna! (BG** [**19.61**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/19.61)**)**

“If according to the śrutis, ātmā is Brahman, by saying that jīvas are nondifferent from Brahman, how can the jīva be dependent on the Lord?”

This is explained next.

### Text 12

*te cānādy-anantāḥ para-śakti-viśeṣatvāt \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīvas have no beginning and no end since they are śaktis of the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīvas have no beginning or end because they are the Lord’s śaktis (but dependent on him).

*yathāgner bahavo visphuliṅgā*

**The jīvas are like many sparks from a fire. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**2.1.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/2.1.20)**)**

*mamaivāṁśo jīva-loke jīva-bhūta*

**The jīvas are my parts. (BG** [**15.7**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/15.7)**)**

“Since the jīvas are the superior śakti of the Lord they should be nondifferent from the Lord.” That is refuted in the next sūtra.

### Text 13

*cid-ānanda-svarūpā api parato bhinnā nitya-satyatvābhāvāt \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīvas, having svarūpas of consciousness and bliss, are different from the Supreme Lord since they lack eternal independent existence.**

**Комментарий:**

Though the jīvas are characterized as eternal, the Supreme Lord, who is eternally existing, bestows their existence upon them. They do not have this quality.

*nityo nityānām*

**The Lord is the chief among the eternal beings. (Kaṭha Upaniṣad** [**2.2.13**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/katha-bs/2.2.13)**)**

*satyasya satyam*

**The Lord is the existent among all existing beings. (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**2.1.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/2.1.20)**)**

*parāt param*

**Liberated from matter, (he attains) the supreme. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad** [**3.2.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mundaka/3.2.8)**)**

*nirdvandvo nitya-sattva-sthaḥ*

**Be devoid of desire for gain or preservation, and engage in meditation on Paramātmā. (BG** [**2.45**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/2.45)**)**

The different sampradāya concepts of difference and nondifference are next presented.

### Text 14

*teṣāṁ paratvaṁ kecid apare bhedam itare tūbhayam \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some say the jīvas are the Supreme Lord. Others say the jīvas are different. Others say the jīvas are both.**

**Комментарий:**

Some like Bādarāyaṇa say that the jīvas are Brahman. Others like Kaśyapa say the jīvas are different from the Lord. Others like Śāṇḍilya say that the jīvas are partially different and partially nondifferent form the Lord.

For each view there are scriptural proofs.

*ayam ātmā brahma*

**Jīva is Brahman. (Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad** [**2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mandukya/2)**)**

*dvā suparṇā sayuje sakhāyā*

**There are two birds in the tree. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad** [**3.1.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mundaka/3.1.1)**)**

*ekadhā bahudhā caiva dṛśyate jala-candravat*

**Like water and the moon, the jīvas are one and many. (Amṛta-bindu Upaniṣad 12)**

Living beings will become bewildered by seeing many different opinions. The conclusion which unites all view is next shown.

### Text 15

*sarveṣāṁ sāmañjasyaṁ sātvata-vijñānasya,*  
*bhramatvābhāvāt pramāṇa-sad-bhāvāc ca \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All views of the sages are harmonized into one, since the true knowledge has no error and scriptural proofs support their views.**

**Комментарий:**

*māyāṁ madīyām udgṛhya vadatāṁ kiṁ nu durghaṭaṁ*

**It is not difficult for disputants who have taken shelter of my māyā to argue. (SB** [**11.22.4**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/22.4)**)**

Having defined the nature (svarūpa) of the jīva, there is now a discussion of the method for the jīva to attain the highest goal while destroying all obstacles to it.

### Text 16

*vicāra-rāgau cetana-dharmau svarūpa-pravṛtti-bhāvāt \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīvas have the quality of deliberation and attraction since these are tendencies of their svarūpa.**

**Комментарий:**

Deliberation (vicāra) produces knowledge, based on consciousness, and attraction (rāga) produces bliss and is the jīva’s nature. The jīva is fixed in consciousness since that is his nature.

*satyaṁ jñānam anantaṁ brahma*

**The Lord is eternity and unlimited knowledge. (Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**2.1.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.1.2)**)**

*vijñānam ānandaṁ brahma*

**The Lord is knowledge and bliss. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**3.9.28**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/3.9.28)**)**

Attraction (rāgā) is the root of both artha and anartha.

### Text 17

*pare’nuraktiḥ svābhāvikī śreyastarī ca,*  
*itareṣv aupādhikī duḥkha-pradā ca \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Attraction to the Supreme Lord is natural and most excellent. Attraction to other things is conditional and produces suffering.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīvas follow the Lord. Their natural inclination is to be attracted to the Lord. This makes them most excellent. Attraction to other things like wealth, children and wife are illusory and produce the suffering of saṁsāra.

*tam eva viditvātimṛtyum eti*

**Knowing the supreme person, most effulgent, beyond darkness, one surpasses saṁsāra. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**3.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/3.8)**)**

*ajo hy eko juṣamāno’nuśete*

**Another jīva, having enjoyed, gives up prakṛti. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**4.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/4.5)**)**

“How can something with a form of consciousness and bliss be related to anarthas?”

That is answered in the next sūtra.

### Text 18

*upādhi-kṛtā hi kleśāḥ \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Suffering of the jīvas arises because of the upādhis of saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий:**

*ya iha kapūya-caraṇā abhyāśo ha yatte kapūyāṁ yonim*  
*āpadyeran śva-yoniṁ vā sūkara-yoniṁ vā caṇḍāla-yoniṁ vā*

**Those who did sinful acts will be born in sinful wombs such as those of dogs, pigs or caṇḍālas. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**5.10.7**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chan-upa/part-5.chapter-10.5107)**)**

*buddhi-nāśāt praṇaśyati*

**With destruction of intelligence the jīvas becomes degraded. (BG** [**2.63**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/2.63)**)**

When the jīvas cut their bondage of suffering caused by upādhis, they can attain liberation. That is now explained.

### Text 19

*anartha-nivṛttir muktiḥ sva-pada-prāpakatvāt \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Destruction of anarthas produces liberation from saṁsāra, since this causes the jīva to attain his svarūpa of knowledge and bliss.**

**Комментарий:**

*satā saumya tadā sampanno bhavati*

**One attains the Lord during sleep. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**6.8.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chan-upa/part-6.chapter-8.681)**)**

*ānandaṁ brahmaṇo vidvān na bibheti kutaścana*

**He who knows the bliss of Brahman, which words and mind cannot approach, has no fear at all. (Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**2.9.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.9.1)**)**

The conscious jīvas have independence given by the Lord as their nature. But the jīva’s independence causes him to suffer. That is well known. Thus how is this bestowal by the Lord his mercy? That is answered in the next sūtra.

### Text 20

*jīvānām itarānurakti-hetur īśvara-kāruṇyaṁ,*  
*ata eva teṣāṁ svātantrya-siddheḥ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva’s independence which is given by the mercy of the Lord for the jīva’s benefit is also the cause of being attracted to other things (by forgetting the Lord).**

**Комментарий:**

*anena jīvenātmanā ’nupraviśya nāma-rūpe vyākaravāṇi*

**Let me enter the elements as the jīva, who is my body and let me create names and forms. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**6.3.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chan-upa/part-6.chapter-3.632)**)**

*na taṁ vidāthaya imā yad yuṣmākam antaraṁ babhūva*

**You do not know him who produced these creatures, who dwells within you. (Ṛg Veda)**

## 3. Acit-Padārtha-Prakaraṇaṁ. Discussion on the Non-Spiritual Object (Māyā)

The Lord has a form for creating the universe, but he is beyond the guṇas and sac-cid-ānanda by nature. What type of śakti does he use? To answer this a new section begins.

### Text 21

*māyā-śaktir acid-guṇavatī parāvara-kārya-rūpā ca \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord’s śakti called māyā possesses inert guṇas like sattva, etc., and takes the form of superior and inferior effects.**

**Комментарий:**

*māyāṁ tu prakṛtiṁ vidyāt māyinaṁ tu maheśvaraṁ*

**One should know that māyā or prakṛti is controlled by the Supreme Lord. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**4.10**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/4.10)**)**

But prakṛti, endowed with guṇas like sattva, etc., is different from the jīva and the Lord.

By this form it transforms into effects: mahat-tattva and lower transformations. Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad ([4.5](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/4.5)) explains that prakṛti, called ajā, is different from the Lord.

*ajām ekāṁ lohita-śukla-kṛṣṇāṁ*  
*bahvīḥ prajāḥ sṛjamānāṁ sa-rūpāḥ \|*  
*ajo hy eko juṣamāṇo’nuśete*  
*jahāty enāṁ bhukta-bhogām ajo’nyaḥ \|\|*

**One jīva serves unborn prakṛti which is red, white and black, and which creates many forms similar to it. Another jīva, having enjoyed, gives up prakṛti.**

“That śakti can create the universe on its own, independently. Why is it dependent on the Lord?”

### Text 22

*jaḍatvāt kṛti-śūnyā cetana-preritā bhavati saṁyāvavat \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Since it is unconscious, it is incapable of acting and must be moved by a conscious being like a wheat cake.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva in bondage by māyā-śakti is shown:

### Text 23

*sā pare’nanuraktānāṁ kārāvad dehādi-bandhana-rūpā \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Prakṛti is like a prison for jīvas not attracted to the Lord. They are bound by body, etc.**

**Комментарий:**

Because of their independence, by which they ignore the orders of the Lord, they are not attracted to the Lord.

*ye ca manye prapadyante śarīratvāya dehinaḥ \|*  
*sthāṇum anye’nusaṁyānti yathā-karma yathā-śrutam \|\|*

**Others attain births as brāhmaṇas, etc., in this world in order to accept bodies. Others take bodies of immobile life forms, according to their karmas and what they have heard. (Kaṭha Upaniṣad** [**2.2.7**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/katha-bs/2.2.7)**)**

### Text 24

*anādir anantā ca parameśvara-śaktitvāt \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Prakṛti is without end since it is the śakti of the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

*prakṛtiṁ puruṣaṁ caiva viddhy anādī ubhāv api*

**Know that both prakṛti and the jīva are without origin. (BG** [**13.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/13.20)**)**

Two sūtras describe how prakṛti, though without beginning or end, is limited by place and time.

### Text 25

*kālenārthāntaraṁ baddhānāṁ prakṛti-sambandha-rūpatvāc ca \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Time is not a separate entity since it simply relates the bound jīvas with prakṛti.**

**Комментарий:**

Some claim that time is a separate entity. Prakṛti is said to be a form of time according to the statement in Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa (91.8): kalā-kāṣṭhādi-rūpeṇa pariṇāma-pradāyinī. Time produces transformation in the form of measurements like kāṣṭha, etc. But that idea is refuted in this sūtra. Time is not a separate entity but only a relationship.

### Text 26

*saivādhiṣṭhāna-rūpiṇī deśa-saṁjñitā \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Prakṛti is defined as a place where the jīvas can live.**

**Комментарий:**

*samāne vṛkṣe puruṣo nimagno’nīśayā śocati muhyamānam*

**The jīva absorbed in the tree, bewildered, laments because of prakṛti. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad** [**3.1.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mundaka/3.1.2)**)**

The tree indicates the support made of prakṛti.

The great bewilderment of the jīvas bound by prakṛti is described:

### Text 27

*jaḍe baddhasyānanda-bhramo vaikuṇṭha-bhramaś cāsaṅgāt \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The bound jīva thinks 1. there is happiness in the material world and 2. is ignorant of the spiritual world because of his material attachment.**

**Комментарий:**

The cause of attachment of the bound jīvas (āsaṅgāt) is mistaking that there is happiness in material objects, body and Svarga and to mistake material objects for the Lord and his abode.

*rajo’dhikā karma-parā duḥkhe ca sukha-māninaḥ*

**There is only one class—human beings, who have a predominance of rajas, who are dedicated to work, and who think themselves happy even in distress. (SB** [**3.10.26**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-3/10.26)**)**

Now the sādhana to destroy the illusion mentioned is described.

### Text 28

*vivekena tato vimuktiḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By discernment, one attains liberation from prakṛti.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva is freed from the two types of error by discerning the natures of jīva and Paramātmā.

*ātmānaṁ ced vijānīyād ayam asmīti puruṣaḥ \|*  
*kim icchan kasya kāmāya śarīram anusajjeta \|\|*

**If a person understands that he is ātmā, “I am this,” will he make the body suffer for developing desire for material objects? (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**4.4.12**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/4.4.12)**)**

*bhidyate hṛdaya-granthiś chidyante sarva-saṁśayāḥ \|*  
*kṣīyante cāsya karmāṇi tasmin dṛṣṭe parāvare \|\|*

**The knot in the heart is cut. All doubts are destroyed. Karmas are destroyed on seeing the Lord. (Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad** [**2.2.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/mundaka/2.2.8)**)**

“The sages went to see Vaikuṇṭha. Such statements indicate that many sages went to Vaikuṇṭha and saw the Lord and again returned to this world. Why do you say the world is produced from ignorance?” The next sūtra answers.

### Text 29

*na ca prākṛtavad indriya-grāhyatvaṁ vaikuṇṭhasyādhokṣajatvāt \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vaikuṇtḥa is not perceived by material senses since Vaikuṇṭha and the Lord are beyond matter.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord and the spiritual world are not perceived by the material senses like material pots and cloth since they are beyond matter.

The Vaikuṇṭha accessible to material senses is a Vaikuṇṭha made of māyā.

*vaikuṇṭha-kalpito yena loko loka-namaskṛtaṁ*

**Just to please the goddess of fortune, Vaikuṇṭha, at her request, created another Vaikuṇṭha planet, which is worshiped by everyone. (SB** [**8.5.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-8/5.5)**)**

Otherwise they would not return. Returning would contradict other statements of the Lord:

*yad gatvā na nivartante tad dhāma paramaṁ mama*

**Going to my supreme abode they do not return. (BG** [**15.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/15.6)**)**

*mām upetya tu kaunteya punar janma na vidyate*

**O Kaunteya! Attaining me one does not take birth again. (BG** [**8.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/8.15)**)**

*yato vāco nivartante*

**Material words cannot describe the Lord. (Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**2.4.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.4.1)**)**

In order to confirm that spiritual Vaikuṇṭha is beyond material senses, the material senses are described.

### Text 30

*indriyāṇi tad-viṣayās taj-jāta-bhāvāś ca manasā*  
*saha prākṛtāś cid-upādhitvāj janyatvāc ca \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The action and knowledge senses, their objects such as form, taste, and various actions, and their transformations, along with the mind which accepts and rejects, are made of prakṛti, since they are products of the creation and are upādhis on spiritual substance.**

**Комментарий:**

The transformations arise from the mind in contact with the objects of the senses.

*evam etasmād ātmanaḥ sarve prāṇāḥ*

**From the Lord arose all the senses. (Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad** [**2.1.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bau/2.1.20)**)**

*tan-mano’sṛjata*

**He created the mind. (Maitrayani Saṁhitā)**

## 4. Sambandha-Prakaraṇaṁ. Discussion on the relation between Jīva and the Lord

The jīvas have already been described in the second section. The fourth section deals with the inherent relationship of the jīva with the Lord full of sac-cid-ānanda in order to show the method of attaining the Lord. The characteristics of bhakti are described.

### Text 31

*bhaktiḥ pūrṇānuraktiḥ pare \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Bhakti is continous attraction to the Supreme Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The characteristic of bhakti is described:

*raso vai saḥ, rasaṁ hy evāyaṁ labdhvānandī bhavati*

**The Lord is rasa. Attaining that rasa the jīva becomes blissful. (Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**2.7.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.7.1)**)**

Different types of bhakti are described according to qualification, since the highest bhakti just described cannot be realized by all people.

### Text 32

*tasyā svarūpaṁ phalam upāyaś ceti \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The svarūpa of bhakti is both the result and the means.**

**Комментарий:**

Bhakti has two essential forms because of differing qualifications for liberated and bound jīvas. For liberated jīvas, it is bhakti as the result, prema-bhakti, the chief form. For bound jīvas, bhakti is a means or sādhana. However they are one just as ghee is life, though ghee is the cause and life is the result. That bhakti as a means is bhakti in a secondary sense.

*satataṁ kīrtayanto māṁ yatantaś ca dṛḍha-vratāḥ \|*  
*namasyantaś ca māṁ bhaktyā nitya-yuktā upāsate \|\|*

**Having strict vows, desiring to be my associates, diligent to understand my nature, they worship me at all times with devotion, through singing, offering respects, and other devotional acts. (BG** [**9.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.14)**)**

Bhakti takes different forms in sādhana such as kīrtana, japa, offering respects, etc.

The highest bhakti does not depend on anything else:

### Text 33

*phala-bhakter nānyad aṅgam ekatvāt svataḥ-siddhatvāc ca \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Bhakti as a result (prema) has no other aṅga since it is one incomparable entity, perfect in itself.**

**Комментарий:**

It is perfect in itself (svataḥ siddha) since it is a function of rāga. That means that it does not depend on sādhana (once it is manifested).

*nitya-siddhasya bhāvasya prākaṭyaṁ hṛdi sādhyata*

**The nitya-siddha bhāva manifests in the heart. (BRS** [**1.2.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/rupa-brs1/1.2.2)**)**

This is seen as the conclusion concerning bhakti:

*ānandaṁ brahmaṇo rūpam*

**The form of the Lord is bliss. (Śrī Paraśurāma-kalpasūtram 1.12)**

Having delineated para-bhakti, the form in perfection, bhakti as method is now defined:

### Text 34

*upāya-bhakteḥ parānuśīlanaṁ pratyāhāraś cāṅgam \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The aṅgas of sādhana bhakti are cultivating a favorable relationship with the Lord and avoiding unfavorable elements.**

**Комментарий:**

One should continuously contemplate (anuśīlanam) the Lord with favorable attitude and conquer the senses and being detached (pratyāhāra). This constitutes sādhana.

*man-manā bhava mad-bhakto mad-yājī māṁ namaskuru*

**Think of me, be my devotee, worship me and offer respects to me. (BG** [**9.34**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.34)**)**

The other aṅgas of secondary bhakti, the sādhana of cultivating the Lord, are next shown:

### Text 35

*śravaṇa-kīrtanādīni parānuśīlanopayogitvāt tat-pratyaṅgāni \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sub-aṅgas of bhakti are hearing, chanting, etc., since these enable one to contemplate the Lord favorably in a continuous manner.**

**Комментарий:**

Kīrtana is an aṅga of worship as in satatam kīrtayanto mām: always glorify me.

Now the nature of what is to be avoided, included as an aṅga of sādhana, is described.

### Text 36

*deha-rathaṁ manaḥ-sārathim indriya-hayam āstikya-jñānena yukta-*  
*vairāgyena ca viṣaya-mārgāc chanair nivartayed eṣa eva pratyāhāraḥ \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Withdrawal (pratyāhāra) means to withdraw the chariot of the body, the charioteer of the mind, the horses-like senses, from the path of material objects by knowledge according to scriptures and by yukta-vairāgya.**

**Комментарий:**

The body is like a chariot, inspired by consciousness. The mind is the driver of the chariot, since it controls the senses which are like horses, which make the chariot of the body move. The passenger in the chariot is the jīva.

*ātmānaṁ rathinaṁ viddhi*

**Know that the jīva is the passenger. (Kaṭha Upaniṣad** [**1.3.3**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/katha-bs/1.3.3)**)**

By scriptural knowledge and yukta-vairāgya as sādhana, one gradually withdraws the senses, etc., from the path of material objects pursued by the chariot, etc.

The withdrawal is described.

*śanaiḥ śanair uparamed buddhyā dhṛta-gṛhītayā*

**Gradually make the mind meditate on ātmā by concentrated intelligence. (BG** [**6.25**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/6.25)**)**

“The śrutis proclaim that liberation results from jñāna or knowledge. Why do you qualify this and say scriptural knowledge (āstikya-jñāna)?”

### Text 37

*jñānān muktiḥ jñānād bandhaś ca \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Scriptural knowledge produces liberation from bondage and knowledge of material objects produces bondage.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowledge of the true nature of the Lord from scriptures (āstikyajñāna) produces liberation from bondage. Knowledge of matter and dry knowledge of Brahman produce bondage in saṁsāra.

The Lord says that knowledge produces bondage:

*sukha-saṅgena badhnāti jñāna-saṅgena cānagha*

**The mode of sattva produces knowledge and happiness, because of its purity. It binds the jīva with false conceptions of happiness and knowledge, O sinless one. (BG** [**14.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/14.6)**)**

The word yukta in yukta-vairāgya is explained:

### Text 38

*vairāgyān muktiḥ vairāgyād bandhaś ca \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Yukta-vairāgya leads to liberation from bondage and phalguvairāgya leads to bondage.**

**Комментарий:**

There are two types of vairāgya, yukta and phalgu.

Yukta-vairāgya means to act with pure character according to rules, by following scripture conduct and offering everything to the Lord without attachment to the objects. This leads to liberation from bondage.

*anāśrtya karma-phalaṁ kāryaṁ karma*  
*karoti yaḥ, sa sannyāsī ca yogī ca*

**He who performs his work out of duty, without seeking results, is a sannyāsī and yogī, not one who lights no fire and performs no actions for maintenance of the body. (BG** [**6.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/6.1)**)**

Phalgu-vairāgya is renunciation which causes hardening of the heart and produces pride. It is insignificant and is called markaṭavairāgya or false vairāgya. It produces suffering in saṁsāra.

*na tyāgena eke amṛtatvam ānaśuḥ*

**They will not attain the immortal Lord by renunciation alone. (Mahā-nārāyaṇa Upaniṣad 12.14)**

*sa kṛtvā rājasaṁ tyāgaṁ naiva tyāga-phalaṁ labhet*

**One who gives up the obligatory actions out of fear of bodily pain, or from thinking that they are an unnecessary inconvenience for the body, performs tyāga in the mode of rajas. He will not attain the intended result of that tyāga. (BG** [**8.8**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/8.8)**)**

*mithyācāraḥ sa ucyate*

**He who, controlling the action senses, remains remembering those objects of the senses in his mind, is bewildered, and is called a pretender. (BG** [**3.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/3.6)**)**

“If a person deviates from the prescribed path, he will end up a horrible condition.”

### Text 39

*pratyāhārāsampatteḥ para-bhakty-asiddhāv api nādhaḥ-patanam \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Even if the devotee cannot attain the highest bhakti because of incomplete pratyāhāra, he does not fall.**

**Комментарий:**

If the previously mentioned withdrawal from material life is not complete, the highest bhakti (prema) is not accomplished. But the devotee does not fall. The devotee does not attain a lower birth with absence of bhakti, as is the case of persons attached to karmas who do not completely perform karmas.

*na hi kalyāṇa-kṛt kaścid durgatiṁ tāta gacchati*

**One who has done the auspicious work of yoga is not deprived of the benefits, O my son. (BG** [**6.40**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/6.40)**)**

What is the gradual progress of bhakti?

### Text 40

*pratyāhāra-samṛddhyā sādhanaṁ bhāvas tayaiva bhāvāt prema \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sādhana with increase of sense control leads to bhāva and from that, by further sense control, one attains to prema.**

**Комментарий:**

By practice of sense control, more and more, bhakti attains greater excellence. Sādhana-bhakti is the first state. This becomes bhāvabhakti. By further sense control (tayā) bhāva leads to prema.

*abhyāsa-yogena tato mām icchāptuṁ dhanañjaya*

**If you are not able to concentrate your mind on me steadily, then seek to attain me by the method of withdrawing the mind from sense objects and fixing it on me, O conqueror of wealth. (BG** [**12.9**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/12.9)**)**

## 5. Siddhānta-Prakaraṇam. Discussion on the Conclusion

“Since there are many conclusions of many opinionated philosophers using various scriptures, how can you present only one conclusion as necessarily the best one for the jīvas having with dependence on scriptures? What is the use of the views of Tattva-sūtra?”

The jīvas cannot attain the highest benefit without support of knowledge which is svataḥ siddha. To explain this, the fifth section begins.

### Text 41

*jñāna-sūryasya hi raśmayaḥ śāstrāṇi \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Definitely the scriptures are rays from the sun of knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

The word hi indicates certainly or a cause. Svataḥ-siddha (selfrevealing) knowledge is like the sun for the jīvas since it is the svarūpa of Brahman, is self-manifesting, destroys ignorance and reveals everything.

The rays or portions of the sun are the scriptures that arise from that knowledge.

*ṛcaḥ sāmāni jajñire chandāṁsi jajñire*  
*tasmāt yajus tasmād ajāyata*

**The Ṛg and Sāma Vedas, the meters and the Yajur Veda manifested from the Lord. (Puruṣa-sūkta 10)**

*śāstra-yonitvāt*

**Because the source of understanding the Lord is scriptures. (Brahma-sūtras 1.1.3)**

*yaḥ śāstra-vidhim utsṛjya vartate kāma-cārataḥ \|*  
*na sa siddhim avāpnoti na sukhaṁ na parāṁ gatim \|\|*

**He who, giving up the rules of scripture, acts according to his own whim, does not attain purification, peace, or liberation. (BG** [**16.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/16.23)**)**

The word śāstra is derived from śāsana, or control. How can one attain perfection without following scriptural rules? The jīvas are dependent on being regulated by scriptures.” The next sūtra answers.

### Text 42

*śāstram akṣameṣu balīyaḥ, vivekināṁ naitat, tan-mūla-prāpteḥ \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**For those devoid of self-revealing knowledge, rules are prominent. For persons who can discriminate, the scriptures lose their strength since they have attained the root.**

**Комментарий:**

The rules of scripture are very necessary for jīvas who are incapable of making their own rules, being devoid of svataḥsiddha knowledge. For persons who have attained that knowledge (vivekinām) who have the ability to make rules most beneficial for themselves, the rules are no longer restrictive. Those persons are not under the control of rules since they have attained the root of the scriptures, svataḥ-siddha knowledge.

It is made clear in the Brahma-sūtras that all scriptures are directed to persons in ignorance. For persons attached to freely performing forbidden act, who are in ignorance of their own benefit, dependence on scriptural rules produces a tendency to follow rules.

*yat tu yaḥ śāstra-vidhim utsṛjyeti*

**He who, giving up the rules of scripture, acts according to his own whim, does not attain purification, peace, or liberation. (BG** [**16.23**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/16.23)**)**

It should be understood that scriptures are for controlling jīvas who have no control.

*yadā te moha-kalilaṁ buddhir vyatitariṣyati \|*  
*tadā gantāsi nirvedaṁ śrotavyasya śrutasya ca \|\|*

**When your intelligence has completely renounced the heart filled with the denseness of ignorance, you will become indifferent to all that has been heard and all that will be heard concerning the fruits of action. (BG** [**2.52**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/2.52)**)**

*traiguṇya-viṣayā vedā nistraiguṇyo bhavārjuna \|*

**The subject of the Vedas is action in the three modes. Rise above the action of the three modes, Arjuna, and be free of dualities, by observing the unchanging state of the soul. (BG** [**2.45**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/2.45)**)**

Having explained that the person with knowledge is not under the control of scriptural rules, special consideration of the devotee is now explained.

### Text 43

*bhakter na śāstraṁ tad-vidher jñānāvirodhitvāt \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**For the devotee, scriptural rules do not apply since the rules contradict the knowledge of cultivating a relationship with the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The devotee is endowed with special knowledge that purifies the heart. That devotee is not under the control of the rules of karma-yoga since those rules contradict the knowledge of rules concerning cultivating a relationship with the Lord. The devotee becomes successful by his self-created rule of cultivating a relationship with the Lord. He does not depend on scriptures which give rules created by others.

*kim ahaṁ sādhu nākaravam \| kim ahaṁ pāpam akaravam*

**The person who knows blissful Brahman, from which words and mind turn back, does not fear anything. He does not lament “Why did I not do puṇyas? Why did I commit sin?” (Taittirīya Upaniṣad** [**2.9.1**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.9.1)**)**

*tatra kaḥ śokaḥ ko mohaḥ*

**What lamentation or illusion can exist for that person who sees the Lord pervading everything, who at the time of meditation realizes all beings as parts of the one Lord? (Īśopaniṣad** [**7**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/iso/7)**)**

*yadā te moha-kalilaṁ buddhir vyatitariṣyati \|*  
*tadā gantāsi nirvedaṁ śrotavyasya śrutasya ca \|*

**When your intelligence has completely renounced the heart filled with the denseness of ignorance, you will become indifferent to all that has been heard and all that will be heard concerning the fruits of action. (BG** [**2.52**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/2.52)**)**

“Should the devotee perform varṇāśrama dharma or not?”

### Text 44

*bhaktau na varṇāśrama-vidhiḥ, sa tasyā jñāna-paratvāt \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There is no rule to follow varṇāśrama in bhakti since bhakti produces another type of knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

The material devotee practicing vaidhi-bhakti follows rules. But those persons following bhakti beyond the guṇas should not follow rules of varṇāśrama or give them respect since pure bhakti (tasyāḥ) produces another type of knowledge.

*jñānavān māṁ prapadyate*

**The jñānī, after many births, attains me through devotee association. (BG** [**7.19**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/7.19)**)**

*teṣāṁ jñānī nitya-yukta eka-bhaktir viśiṣyate*

**Of these four types, the jñānī, who is constantly engaged in thinking of me, who is practicing pure bhakti, is the best. This jñānī loves only me, and I love only him. (BG** [**7.17**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/7.17)**)**

**My devotee who is detached even from desire for liberation should reject the āśrama duties based on external rituals or paraphernalia and conduct themselves beyond the range of rules. (SB** [**11.18.28**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/18.28)**)**

*sarva-dharmān parityajya mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja*

**Give up all dharmas and surrender to me alone. (BG** [**18.66**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/18.66)**)**

One should not worry that one will fall by giving up dharma. This is confirmed strongly by the promise of the Lord, whose promises are always true.

*ahaṁ tvā sarva-pāpebhyo mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ*

**Do not worry. I will free you from all sins. (BG** [**18.66**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/18.66)**)**

“Why should one reject karma completely by performing bhakti?”

The rejectable and acceptable portions of karma are shown:

### Text 45

*ajña-hitārthaṁ grāhyaṁ karma na virodhi \|\|45\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Performing karmas for the benefit of the ignorant are accepted, and are not contrary to bhakti.**

**Комментарий:**

Out of mercy, the devotee should perform nitya and naimittika karmas to some degree as prescribed by varṇāśrama for the purpose of destroying lack of faith in the prescribed karmas for the benefit of persons not qualified for jñāna or bhakti—persons contaminated with ignorance.

By giving up karmas and by not attaining the supreme goal, these people become completely vanquished.

*yad yad ācarati śreṣṭhaḥ*

**Whatever actions the superior person performs, other persons will also perform. (BG** [**3.21**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/3.21)**)**

*loka-saṅgraham evāpi sampaśyan kartum arhasi*

**To teach others, you should perform work. (BG** [**3.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/3.20)**)**

The Lord orders in this way, but these acts are not contrary to bhakti. Forbidden actions of lust arising from the fault of hatred of others cause bondage and agitation of the heart which are contrary to bhakti. Those actions should not be done.

*yasmān nodvijate loko lokān nodvijate ca yaḥ \|*  
*harṣāmarṣa-bhayodvegair mukto yaḥ sa ca me priyaḥ \|\|*

**He who does not agitate others, nor is agitated by others, he who is free from material emotions such as joy, jealousy, fear and anxiety, is dear to me. (BG** [**12.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/12.15)**)**

*karśayantaḥ śarīra-sthaṁ bhūta-grāmam acetasaḥ \|*  
*māṁ caivāntaḥ-śarīra-sthaṁ tān viddhy āsura-niścayān \|\|*

**The demons undergo extreme austerities contrary to the scriptures, and afflict the elements in the body and me residing in the body. They have no discriminating scriptural knowledge. (BG** [**17.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/17.6)**)**

“If it is better to give up karmas rather than perform them, the ignorant will become successful by giving up karma. What is the use of performing karmas?”

### Text 46

*yathādhikāram avasthitir nopary-adhastvāt \|\|46\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**According to one’s qualification one should be situated in action, not doing what is above or below one’s qualification.**

**Комментарий:**

One should not perform acts superior to one’s dharma or inferior to it.

*sve sve’dhikāre yā niṣṭhā sa guṇaḥ parikīrtitaḥ \|*  
*viparyayas tu doṣaḥ syād ubhayor eva niścayaḥ \|\|*

**Being fixed according to one’s qualification is a good quality, and to be fixed in something not of one’s qualification is a bad quality. This is how good and bad are determined. (SB** [**11.21.2**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/21.2)**)**

*sva-dharme nidhanaṁ śreyaḥ para-dharmo bhayāvahaḥ*

**It is better to die being situated in one’s own duties. Performing another’s duties is a cause of misfortune. (BG** [**3.35**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/3.35)**)**

“On the path of karma, many benefits are produced by worship of pitṛs and devatās. Why is it said that karmas are completely inauspicious?”

### Text 47

*kāmyetara-bhaktir na śastā citta-vikṣepatvād anitya-phalatvāc ca \|\|47\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Devotion to anyone other than the Lord, because of desires for material results, is not proper, since this causes agitation of the heart and bears temporary results.**

**Комментарий:**

Devotion to those other than the Lord, those classed as jīvas, because of desiring material results, is not beneficial, since it agitates the heart: one must undertake various prolonged karmas with worship and sacrifices according to particular rules for devatās and pitṛs after studying the śrutis and smrṭis which deal with various devatās. It is rajasic karma with great expense.

*bahu-śākhā hy anantāś ca buddhayo’vyavasāyinām*

**O son of the Kurus. The intelligence of those who are irresolute has infinite objects of desire, with many branches. (BG** [**2.41**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/2.41)**)**

And the results are temporary. The results give happiness for a short duration, like the results of cultivating a field. The worship gives temporary planets of devatās and pitrṣ in afterlife and material wealth in this life.

*agniṣṭomena svarga-kāmo yajeta*

**One should perform sacrifice desiring Svarga. (Āpastambaśrauta-sūtra 19.10.14)**

*tad yatheha karma-cito lokaḥ kṣīyata evam*  
*evāmutra puṇya-cito lokaḥ kṣīyate*

**Just as in this world the environment earned by pious acts is destroyed, in the next world also the planet earned by pious acts is destroyed. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad** [**8.1.6**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chan-upa/part-8.chapter-1.816)**)**

*yānti deva-vratā devān pitṝn yānti pitṛ-vratāḥ \|*  
*bhūtāni yānti bhūtejyā yānti mad-yājino’pi mām \|\|*

**The worshippers of the devatās go to the devatās, and the worshippers of the pitṛs go to the pitṛs. The worshippers of ghosts go to the ghosts, and worshippers of me come to me. (BG** [**9.25**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.25)**)**

“In this section, what is considered to be the best conclusion?”

### Text 48

*pratyakṣānumānābhyāṁ bhāgavata-siddhānta eva garīyān*  
*vijñānamayatvāt sarva-siddhāntāśrayatvāc ca \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Bhāgavata-siddhānta is understood to be the greatest by perception and inference since it consists of pure knowledge and is the shelter of all other siddhāntas.**

**Комментарий:**

Among all siddhāntas the bhāgavata-siddhanta is understood to be the greatest by sense perception and inference, which are the basis of discerning everything.

This siddhānta is taught by the Lord to Arjuna in Mahābhārata and in Bhāgavatam to Uddhava as well as to Brahmā in the catuḥślokī. This is determined by the upakrama of janmādy asya yataḥ (SB [1.1.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-1/1.1)) and the upasaṁhāra nāmāni harim param (SB [12.13.23](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/13.23)) as well as by various stories and questions and answers.

This conclusion (siddhānta) determined by the elevated devotees (bhāgavatas) through realization is most profound. This means that it is superior to the conclusions of those propounding karma and jñāna.

It is superior because it consists of pure knowledge and is the shelter of all other conclusions of great devotees endowed with spiritual intelligence in all places and all time, past, present and future. That is because it is the root of all other conclusions. Thus bhāgavata-siddhānta is the best of all. The Lord himself says:

*dadāmi buddhi-yogaṁ taṁ yena mām upayānti te*

**I give you the intelligence by which you can come to me. (BG** [**10.10**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/10.10)**)**

*sarva-vedānta-sāraṁ hi śrī-bhāgavatam iṣyate \|*  
*tad-rasāmṛta-tṛptasya nānyatra syād ratiḥ kvacit \|\|*

**Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam is declared to be the essence of all Vedānta philosophy. One who has felt satisfaction from its sweet rasa will never be attracted to any other literature. (SB** [**12.13.15**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-12/13.15)**)**

\[The writer of the sūtras accepts only two pramānas: pratyakṣa and anumāna. Pratyakṣa is direct perception and anumāna is inference by signs. Upamāna is not considered a pramāṇa since it only a function of deliberation. Nyāya, Vaiśeṣika, Uttaramīmāṁṣā and other scriptures accept śabda also. Manu says:

*pratyakṣaṁ cānumānaṁ ca śāstraṁ ca trividhāgamam*  
*trayaṁ su-viditaṁ kāryaṁ dharma-śuddhim abhīpsatā*

**The three methods well known are accepted for verification of dharma: pratyakṣa, anumāna and scripture. (Manu smṛti 12.105)**

Why does the author not accept any pramāṇas except pratyakṣa and anumāna? Why does he not accept scripture?

When knowledge is called the sun and scriptures are the rays (in the text), the Vedic scriptures are necessarily accepted. But the word śabda is not mentioned anywhere in the text. Because scripture is based on (comprehended through) pratyakṣa and anumāna, the sages and Brahmā have gathered rules and teachings through pratyakṣa and anumāna. Therefore śabda cannot be the third pramāṇa. One understands scripture partially through pratyakṣa and partially through anumāna. Thus it is reasonable to accept two pramānas. But the deliberation on pratyakṣa and anumāna of the author are very different from that of other authors. The direct subject of deliberation (in scripture) is pratyakṣa. In other words, self-manifesting faith (in the scripture) is called pratyakṣa, and everything else (in relation to scripture) is called anumāna.

*ātmano gurur ātmaiva puruṣasya viśeṣataḥ*  
*yat pratyakṣānumānābhyāṁ śreyo ’sāv anuvindate*

**An intelligent person, acting as his own guru, can achieve great benefit by perception and logic. (SB** [**11.7.20**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/7.20)**)**

*uddhared ātmanātmānaṁ nātmānam avasādayet \|*  
*ātmaiva hy ātmano bandhur ātmaiva ripur ātmanaḥ \|\|*

**One should deliver the soul by the mind, not degrade it. The mind is the friend of the soul and its enemy as well. (BG** [**6.5**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/6.5)**)**

“How can this knowledge of the bhāgavata-siddhānta take place without surrender to guru?”

### Text 49

*caitanyasya sarvācāryasyāvirbhāve na gurv-antaram \|\|49\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**After the appearance of the Lord as Caitanya Mahāprabhu, the ācārya of all devotees, there is no need of any other guru.**

**Комментарий:**

One should not worship any other guru after the appearance of Caitanya Mahāprabhu, the ācārya of the devotees who gather the essence, who are qualified for understanding tattva.

*yo brahmāṇaṁ vidadhāti pūrvaṁ*  
*yo vai vedāṁś ca prahiṇoti tasmai \|*  
*taṁ ha devam ātma-buddhi-prakāśaṁ*  
*mumukṣur vai śaraṇam ahaṁ prapadye \|\|*

**Desiring liberation, I surrender to the Lord, who created Brahmā previously and gave him the Vedas, and who reveals intelligence so that one may know him. (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad** [**6.18**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sveta-bs/6.18)**)**

*teṣām evānukampārtham aham ajñānajaṁ tamaḥ \|*  
*nāśayāmy ātma-bhāva-stho jñāna-dīpena bhāsvatā \|\|*

**To show favor to them, I, by myself, situated within them, extinguish the darkness born of ignorance with the shining lamp of knowledge. (BG** [**10.11**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/10.11)**)**

*ācāryaṁ māṁ vijānīyāt nāvanmanyeta karhicit*

**One should know the ācārya as myself and never disrespect him in any way. (SB** [**11.17.27**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-11/17.27)**)**

### Text 50

*pare pūrṇānuraktir, itareṣu tulyā,*  
*jaḍe yukta-vairāgyaṁ ceti sāra-grāhi-matam \|\|50\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should have attraction to the Supreme Lord, show equality to others, and practice yukta-vairāgya in relation to matter. That is the view of the devotees who gather the essence.**

**Комментарий:**

The essence of this section is made clear. The natural tendency of the jīva, to have devotion to the Supreme Lord, full of continuous, full bliss, should manifest in the heart. Ones should view all other jīvas who are different from the Lord as equal to each other. This means one should be attracted to them, but according to their various conditions.

The meaning is this. Three types of attractions should be shown: respect to the elevated devotees, friendship with the intermediate devotees and mercy to the lower devotees. This is for those who see differences (madhyama devotee)

Those who see no difference (uttama devotees) between them, show attraction to them all without envy, without quarrelling and without disrespect. Or this person should see with affection everyone as brothers, since all jīvas manifest from the Lord.

One should practice yukta-vairāgya to material objects which are perceived as dear, such as wealth, sons, wife, friends, house and property. This means one should accept enough material objects to maintain oneself. This is the opinion of the best devotees who are devoid of argument with various sampradāyas. What more can be said? One should see the many proofs from śruti and smṛti presented in the previous sections.

This concludes Tattva-sūtras.

*hārītānvaya-sambhūto gopīnāthābhidhaḥ kṛtī \|*  
*vivṛtiṁ tattva-sūtrāṇāṁ cakāra viduṣāṁ mude \|\|*

**The author named Gopīnātha, arising from Hārita gotra, wrote the Tattva-sūtras for the pleasure of the learned.**

*jagannātha-kṣetra-vāsī mukti-maṇḍapa-paṇḍitaḥ \|*  
*jagannātha-prasādena tattva-vyākhyām acīkarat \|\|*

**Living in Jagannātha Purī, this learned devotee of Mukti-maṇḍapa5 wrote the explanation of tattva by the mercy of Jagannātha.**

*grantha-gaurava-bhītyā ca saṅkṣiptaṁ vivṛtaṁ mayā \|*  
*vistārayantu sudhīyo bahu-vyākhyāna-yuktibhiḥ \|\|*

**I have written a brief commentary, fearing the length of the work. May the intelligent people propagate this work with intelligent explanations.**

*\|\| iti tattva-sūtraṁ samāptam \|\|*

## About Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura

Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura (1838-1914) is a prominent ācārya in Gauḍīya-vaiṣṇava sampradāya. He was a pioneering spiritual leader, a householder, a magistrate working in colonial India under the British rule, a prolific preacher, writer, and poet. He wrote volumes of books reintroducing the pure teachings of Lord Caitanya at a time when those teachings had practically become lost. He composed hundreds of devotional songs glorifying Kṛṣṇa to uplift the consciousness of the suffering people of this world. He corresponded with philosophers, theologians, leaders, scholars, and professors of his time and sent books, including The Life and Precepts of Lord Chaitanya, to university libraries in foreign countries, planting the seeds for a worldwide movement of Krishna consciousness. Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura discovered and excavated the birthplace of Lord Caitanya. Along with his devoted wife, Bhagavati Devī, he raised ten children, including the illustrious Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura, who would become a great spiritual leader in his own time and the spiritual master of ISKCON's founder-ācārya, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda.
