Оглавление выгрузки:
The Twenty-Six Qualities of a Devotee
  Introduction
  1. A devotee is merciful, kṛpālu
  2. A devotee is not defiant, akṛta-droha
  3. A devotee is truthful, satya-sāra
  4. A devotee is equal to everyone, sama
  5. A devotee is faultless, nidoṣa
  6. A devotee is magnanimous, vadānya
  7. A devotee is mild, mṛdu
  8. A devotee is clean, śuci
  9. A devotee is without material possessions, akiñcana
  10. A devotee performs welfare work for everyone, sarvopakāraka
  11. A devotee is peaceful, śanta
  12. A devotee is surrendered to Kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa-śaraṇa
  13. A devotee is desireless, akāma
  14. A devotee is indifferent to material acquisitions, anīha
  15. A devotee is fixed, sthira
  16. A devotee completely controls the six bad qualities, vijita-ṣaḍ-guṇa
  17. A devotee eats only as much as required, mita bhuk
  18. A devotee is without inebriation, apramatta
  19. A devotee is respectful, mānada
  20. A devotee is without false prestige, amānī
  21. A devotee is grave, gambhīra
  22. A devotee is compassionate, karuṇa
  23. A devotee is a friend, maitra
  24. A devotee is a poet, kavi
  25. A devotee is expert, dakṣa
  26. A devotee is silent, maunī
