# The importance of an Institution for successfully propagating Krishna Consciousness all over the world

## Learning outcomes

The purpose of this document is to enable devotees to:

1\. Understand the needs and benefits of having an institution.

2\. Show improved cooperation with one other, and one’s authorities, in fulfilling the seven purposes of ISKCON.

3\. Demonstrate increased appreciation of devotee association.

4\. Recognise the authority of the GBC and understand the concept of parallel lines of authority.

5\. Spread the Krishna Consciousness movement more effectively.

6\. Recognise the unique gift of Srila Prabhupada in the form of ISKCON.

## Executive summary

Srila Prabhupada performed a miracle by spreading Krishna consciousness all over the world in just 11 years. However, his actual mission is to spread it in every town and village. That was not possible during his manifest pastime. He also did not want just one person to inherit his legacy as his successor. Therefore, this mission needs the concept of collective endeavour for future generations. An institution is absolutely essential to hold together a large number of individuals committed to this mission all over the world. We must take Srila Prabhupada’s instruction to heart and collectively continue to push his mission forward.

One of the most salient aspects of Srila Prabhupada’s vision for ISKCON is collective management. Srila Prabhupada wanted the GBC members to collectively manage ISKCON and continue its mission. He very clearly mentioned that he is passing the legacy onto all his followers and whoever follows will have the legacy.

## Summary of key points

1\. Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu had a seemingly impossible prediction that the Krishna Consciousness movement will spread all over the world, in every town and village. Srila Jiva Goswami, as well as our recent acharyas, Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur and Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur, took major steps to fulfil that prediction.

2\. That prediction took a leap forward in the most amazing way by a 70-year-old person in an incredibly short period of time.

3\. Srila Prabhupada knew that the total accomplishment of that prediction will require generations of committed individuals all over the world. Therefore, as soon as the prospect of preaching in America started to unfold, he set up the institution - ISKCON.

4\. Srila Prabhupada saw how his spiritual master’s institution, Gaudiya Math, disintegrate soon after its founder’s disappearance, and the massive preaching mission that the founder started practically stopped. Therefore, Srila Prabhupada emphatically warned his leading disciples not to make the same mistake that his god-brothers made in the past.

5\. Gaudiya Math disintegrated within a few months after Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur’s disappearance, whereas ISKCON, 39 years after Srila Prabhupada’s disappearance, is not only together, but expanding in an inconceivable way all over the world.

## Background

If we really want to spread Krishna Consciousness all over the world and establish it for the next 10,000 years, then we need an institution that will hold for our continuous collective endeavour.

The traditional option of a single successor acharya was not the desire or instruction of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. Srila Prabhupada had the spiritual insight and foresight about the impracticality of such an idea. In ISKCON, eight out of the eleven original initiating spiritual masters appointed by Srila Prabhupada fell down. One can consider the disastrous consequences that that would have had on ISKCON had Srila Prabhupada appointed a single person as a successor head of ISKCON. That becomes more poignant when we reflect that such an event happened in the recent history of the Gaudiya Math after the disappearance of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur. The disintegration of the Gaudiya Math began virtually a few days after Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur disappearance due to the conflict amongst his leading disciples. Although the 27 sannyasis maintained their vows, continued to preach individually and made disciples in their own temples, the remarkable momentum that the Gaudiya Math had under the direction of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur ground to a halt because the collective endeavour was lost.

Yet in ISKCON we observe that 39 years after Srila Prabhupada's disappearance, despite the fall down of some original leaders, and several other crises, ISKCON as an organisation is not only still intact, but continues to expand its preaching projects - due to the unity arising from collective management and commitment of devotee leaders. This indicates that individual endeavours, no matter how efficient they may be, will not produce the desired preaching effect as much as the collective endeavour.

The original impetus for a spiritual institution came from Srila Jiva Goswami, some 500 years ago. Although he lived in the forest, he envisioned the propagation of Krishna Consciousness all over the world. He developed the concept of Vishwa Vaishnav Rajsabha (meaning The Royal Assembly of Worldwide Vaishnavas') which was later re-established by Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur. When Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur started to preach, he developed the Gaudiya Math based on the concept given by Srila Jiva Goswami. Prior to leaving the planet, Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur applied that concept by his instruction that his successful institute, Gaudiya Math, be continued under the concept of collective management.

To hold an institution together after illustrious spiritual personalities like Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur and Srila Prabhupada leave, we have to recognise that no single individual will be able to fill such shoes. One may attempt that for a generation, but that cannot continue perpetually. In the Age of Kali, deviation from the spiritual path will be a phenomenon.

Therefore, there is a need for collective management.

To hold the institution together we need two have fundamental principles in place:

1\. The continued recognition of Srila Prabhupada’s position as Founder-Acharya and pre-eminent siksha guru of everyone in ISKCON. The GBC has emphasised this by publishing the Founder-Acharya paper in 2014.

2\. The collective concept of management in ISKCON and His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada as the permanent head of this institution, with his teachings as the basis.

The GBC, which is the ultimate managing authority of the institution, is tasked with executing the will of Srila Prabhupada. This collective management will ensure that there will always be a majority executing the will and teachings of Srila Prabhupada, and any GBC member deviating will be quickly identified, and the matter rectified.

There are many important considerations that will facilitate the collective endeavour of devotees to give support to the institution of ISKCON. These are described below.

## The importance of the institution

Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur, while reviving Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu’s Sankirtan movement, realised that the massive task of spreading Krishna Consciousness all over the world, in every town and village, would not be possible without the collective endeavours of committed devotees for generations to come. Therefore, he saw the need for an institution, and gave the blueprint of that institution in the form of his “Namahatta” (the market place of the holy name). Furthermore, he revived Srila Jiva Goswami’s concept and fully established the Vishwa Vaishnava Rajsabha.

Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur gave shape to that concept in the form of his Gaudiya Math and when that institution fell apart, Srila Prabhupada revived the spirit of his Guru Maharaj by establishing ISKCON. It is important to note that Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur, Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur and Srila Prabhupada gave more importance to the institution than to their own authority - in spite of being the founders and organisers of their institutions they placed themselves as ordinary members of their respective institutions: Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur identified himself as the sweeper of the Namahatta, Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur called himself the servant of the Gaudiya Math, and Srila Prabhupada identified himself as a mere member of the Governing body of ISKCON.

## ISKCON as an institution

Although Srila Prabhupada spread Krishna Consciousness all over the world within the amazingly short span of ten years, he knew that he would not be able to spread it in every town and village during his lifetime; rather, it would take generations to achieve that. That is why he was so emphatic about the effective continuity of his mission through his ISKCON. Personalities such as Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur, Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur and Srila Prabhupada come to grace this world very rarely. It is Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu’s causeless mercy that these three personalities came in such a succession. Their appearances on this planet were actually Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu's divine arrangement for reviving His sankirtan movement and fulfilling His prediction that Krishna Consciousness would spread to every town and village on this planet. Being empowered by Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, they could have achieved anything and performed incredible miracles, yet, for the sake of fulfilling the ultimate purpose of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu’s mission they emphasised the collective continuity of the movement through an institution. Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur was an extremely brilliant personality who attracted countless luminaries of his time within the fold of his Gaudiya Math. However, soon after his disappearance when the Gaudiya Math disintegrated, in spite of all those luminaries the propagation of Krishna Consciousness practically stopped.

Srila Prabhupada explained that happened since most of the prominent followers of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur could not understand the importance of continuing his mission through the institution he established. They held on to the traditional concept of a single acharya and concluded that a spiritual heritage could not effectively continue through an institution managed by a group of mundane managers. Defying the instruction of their spiritual master, they appointed an Acharya, who eventually fell down, and the institution that he so meticulously structured collapsed.

Srila Prabhupada, in order to protect his ISKCON from such an unfortunate possibility, emphatically told us not to make the same mistake that his god-brothers made after his Guru Maharaja’s disappearance. He advised us to continue the mission under the authority of a collective management structure. Nevertheless, after Srila Prabhupada's disappearance, we in ISKCON made a similar mistake where the original eleven gurus appointed by Srila Prabhupada assumed acharya status and misunderstood his instructions. Fortunately, that mistake was eventually rectified.

Therefore, in order to effectively establish an institution two essential elements are needed: the head of the institution, and its management structure. In ISKCON we have to be absolutely clear that Srila Prabhupada is the head of the institution and he will continue to be so as long as the institution lasts, and the management structure that he established - the GBC, temple presidents, departmental heads, and other leaders - must be fully functional without any deviation.

If we compare the institution of ISKCON to a corporate structure, then we must recognise that Srila Prabhupada is the proprietor of ISKCON, and the other leaders of this institution are the managers. The managers manage, and although they get their remuneration and incentives for that, the profit belongs to the proprietor. It is important that we clearly recognise that everything in ISKCON, including all the devotees, belongs to Srila Prabhupada. Being conscious of this will remove any mistakes that are stifling the growth of our institution. This is what it means to give importance to Srila Prabhupada’s institution over one’s own authority.

Now, by the grace of Krishna, we are expanding and we must work in such a way that our society may stand a solid institution.”

*Srila Prabhupada letter to Brahmananda, 28th September 1969*

## Love and cooperation in the institution

Srila Prabhupada wanted us to show our love for him through our cooperation. What is the practical motivation of such cooperation for the future generations in ISKCON?

Srila Prabhupada left many instructions, but we can consider the following three as especially important for the future of ISKCON.

“Your love for me will be shown how you cooperate with each other."

“No matter what happens, never leave ISKCON ”

“The GBC is the ultimate managing authority of ISKCON.”

These three sublime instructions protect the wonderful institution Srila Prabhupada gave us. This concept of collective management that Srila Prabhupada created is the key to success.

Since the time that Srila Prabhupada established ISKCON there will never be a time when he is actually absent. Srila Prabhupada will continue to be present through his ISKCON. We have to be convinced that this Krishna consciousness movement is the greatest benediction to this world. In the tenth canto of Srimad Bhagavatam, Srila Prabhupada explains that the Lord Krishna descends whenever the demoniac influence lords over the religious atmosphere. The present world atmosphere is highly degraded and Krishna has actually descended in the form of ISKCON. This movement, which is the singular hope, will spread all over the world and establish the religious principles. Therefore, the members of this movement have to become the perfect representatives of Krishna.

“As a GBC man you should become very responsible to see that the highest standards of Krishna Consciousness should be maintained all over the Society, and therefore it is essential to always be corresponding with the others and cooperating with you ail wonderful godbrothers and godsisters to carry out this great mission of Lord Chaitanya”

*Srila Prabhupada letter to Hayagriva, Bombay, 10th January 1972*

## If we remain in ISKCON our spiritual life will be safe.

As ISKCON gurus, we are giving initiation on behalf of ISKCON and thus devotees are becoming part of ISKCON. We must remain faithful to ISKCON. ISKCON is Srila Prabhupada’s divine arrangement. All over the world, people are getting an opportunity to become acquainted with Krishna Consciousness, accepting Krishna Consciousness, and practicing Krishna Consciousness. ISKCON is the perfect platform. If one remains in ISKCON, one’s spiritual life will be safe.

To assist us remain in ISKCON Srila Prabhupada has given the structure of ISKCON in a very wonderful way. The structure is: the world has been divided into different zones and the responsibility of the zones are with the GBC members. In each zone, under the GBC, there are the temples. The temples are the platforms where we are situated, and it is there that we get the opportunity to practice Krishna consciousness, thereby securing our spiritual life.

The unique gift of Srila Prabhupada is that he has spread Krishna consciousness all over the world in such an effective way through ISKCON. As long as one remains in the boat of ISKCON, with Srila Prabhupada as the perfect captain, then one can safely sail through the ocean of material danger.

## ISKCON as a family

As devotees of ISKCON we are members of a very special spiritual family, whose mission is to spread Krishna consciousness all over the world in every town and village. Chaitanya Mahaprabhu predicted that Krishna consciousness movement must spread in every town and village. As members we must consider our role in this institution - how can assist to make this prediction happen? It is by keeping this movement together through our spirit of unity. The expression of our love for Srila Prabhupada is our spirit of cooperation.

Now, we have by Krishna’s grace built up something significant in the shape of this ISKCON and we are all one family. Sometimes there may be disagreement and quarrel but we should not go away. These inebrieties can be adjusted by the cooperative spirit, tolerance and maturity so I request you to kindly remain in the association of our devotees and work together. The test of our actual dedication and sincerity to serve the spiritual master will be in this mutual cooperative spirit to push on this movement and not to make factions and deviate.

*Srila Prabhupada letter to Babru, 9th December 1973*

When we speak of family we have to realise that through the centre of the family we are related to the other members of the family, for example, it is through the father the children become related as brother and sisters. Similarly, in order to maintain this relation as a family we have to find the centre. Krishna is the centre of our family. Upon further reflection, we can see that Srila Prabhupada is also the centre of our family. We are living in a community of devotees, in a Krishna conscious spiritual family.

Srila Prabhupada took so much trouble to create ISKCON. The only purpose of his creating ISKCON was so that devotees can get association of advanced devotees who are completely surrendered to Krishna and who are rendering devotional service to Krishna diligently and sincerely. In the association of devotees, we should hear about Krishna-katha, and plan about how we are going to spread Krishna consciousness. That is the only business of devotees: just remember Krishna, speak about Krishna for the benefit of all.

We recognise each other due to our love for Srila Prabhupada. By keeping Srila Prabhupada in the centre and being committed his institution, then only can people of all nationalities become one family. We recognise each other from the commitment and contribution devotees have made to spread this movement. If we behave as one family with Srila Prabhupada and Krishna in the centre, then this world will become Vaikuntha - the spiritual sky.

## Avoiding conflict within the ISKCON family

Prabhupada told us that the only thing that can destroy this movement is internal conflict. This is the age of the personality known as Kali. Kali spreads his influence by creating quarrel. The word *Kali* means“quarrel” incorporates *kalaha*. *Karala- vadana krurah kalis ca kalaha priyah.* Kali’s description has been given as kalaha priya.He is very fond of creating conflict. That is why Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu came with sankirtan. *Sankirtan* means unite and *kalaha* means divided. Therefore, we must realise that when we are practicing and spreading Krishna consciousness, we are all united in our purpose, which reverts to Srila Prabhupada’s instruction on how our love for him is shown by mutual cooperation.

Our endeavours to become united will reduce the chance of dissension. Srila Prabhupada told us that nothing external can destroy this movement - on the contrary an external attack on ISKCON will make the movement stronger, as that will propel devotees to become united. However internal conflict can be the undoing of the institution as we will be divided and the mission will be lost.

## Work together, you all lead the movement

When Srila Prabhupada went to America and saw the potential of preaching Krishna consciousness there, he wrote to his god-brothers inviting them to preach with the mood that he would assist them. In essence, he was asking them to lead the movement. After much effort, when no one responded Srila Prabhupada decided to continue establishing his mission alone. Simply with firm faith in the instructions of his spiritual master, he began his preaching mission. It can be seen that it was Chaitanya Mahapabhu’s plan that Srila Prabhupada establish Krishna consciousness in the West, and appear the way it did. So many young boys and girls from America came forward to support him in spreading Krishna consciousness. Srila Prabhupada, in his humility, publicly acknowledged that Krishna had sent them all to assist in his spiritual master's mission. Therefore, we can see that this is the real line of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakur, Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur and Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.

## Bringing devotees back to ISKCON

Srila Prabhupada had about 10,000 disciples. Where are they all now? ISKCON devotees should be pained to consider their departure from the movement. How would Srila Prabhupada feel? When Srila Prabhupada would come to Mayapur, he would enquire about the whereabouts of devotees if he did not see them. If he was told that they had left, Srila Prabhupada would exclaim that he was bringing those devotees but others were driving them away. Therefore, we have to realise that devotees are coming here for sake of Srila Prabhupada. They belong to Srila Prabhupada.

It is our solemn responsibility to take care of them and assist them on behalf of Srila Prabhupada. There are reasons that devotees leave that are beyond our control or fault, but we as leaders have to seriously consider why these devotees are leaving and what can we do to keep them in Krishna consciousness. "Leader" means ones who has been given responsibility. Leaders must seriously consider not only how to keep everyone in ISKCON, but how to bring those who have left back to Krishna consciousness. That is Srila Prabhupada’s expectation from the leaders of this movement. We should not think that now that they have left, we have nothing to do with them - that would be insensitive and heartless. We must be broadminded, kind-hearted and compassionate. That is how we can create a society based on love and trust. Love and trust are reciprocal. One person loves and others trust that person, and vice versa - it is only natural.
