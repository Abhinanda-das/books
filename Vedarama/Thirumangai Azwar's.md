## Thirumangai Azwar (Kaliyan)

## Life and Works

Thirumangai azwar’s contribution to 4000 Divya Prabhandham stands alongside Nammazwar, both together accounting for about sixty-percent of the total Prabhandham, and the balance shared by ten azwars and Amudannar (Sri Ramanuja Noothrandhadi). This azwar also known as Kaliyan has the unique distinction of visiting the maximum number of Divya deshas, offering prayers to the deities and composing verses about them. Vedantha Desika has summed his prabhandham as ‘arivum tharum....’ meaning that this azwar’s Prabhandham provides knowledge (about the Lord).

## Birth and Early years

The azwar was born to a robber-leader, a chieftain, by name Neelan in Thirukuraiyaloor near Thiruvali which is a divya desham, in the Kali year 398, Nala samvathsara, Vrischika Pournami. The year is said to be 776 A.D He was considered to be the ‘amsha’ of Lord’s weapon (bow), Sharngam.

The father gave his own name to the child viz. Neelan.

The child underwent all the rituals and started learning from the seventh year. He became proficient in ‘shaasthras’ besides being taught the handling of weaponry as required by a king. The boy started growing and became handsome and brave. After the death of his father he assumed the responsibility and ruled well - the king of Chola gave him lands and honoured him.

Just about this time some nymphs (apsaras) descended on Thiruvali to pray to the Lord and returned, but one of them got left behind and had to stay in this world. A staunch devotee of Thiruvaali who had come to pray happened to see the ten-year-old maiden near the pond and getting to know the facts, agreed to look after her. He took her to his house and the girl, who was given the name ‘Kumudhavalli’ started growing.

## Neelan is infatuated! Love at first sight!

The chieftain, Thirumangai mannan, happened to see Kumudhavalli and fell in love with her instantly and wanted to marry her. Knowing his ways Kumudavalli wished to change the course of his life before she could marry him. Therefore she put forth some conditions, call it ‘Kanya shulka’! She wanted him to embrace (Sri) Vaishnavism; she wanted him to undergo the Panchasamskara and what next? - she wanted him to feed one thousand bhagavathas everyday without break !! Neelan agreed to all the conditions. Watching him change day by day, the juncture arrived when she agreed to marry him and she did.

## Bhagavatha Sevai in Right Earnest

As promised to his spouse, Kumadhavalli, Neelan mended his ways and started doing ‘bhagavatha sevai’. He used money from the treasury but a moment came when he had exhausted everything and had no option but to fall back on his father’s old profession; he waylaid a brahmin couple in a forest to rob them off their gold anklets etc. He had to use his teeth to remove forcefully the jewelry. The moment of opening the consciousness arrived and the Lord, showing His identity, taught him the eight syllabled manthra (Ashtakshari). Fully reformed he started devoting his energies to building temples, feeding bhagavathas and significantly started composing songs extolling the Lord.

He is credited with building covered corridors surrounding the shrine of Srirangam temple (the prakaras).

## Visiting Divya Deshas

Giving up his kingdom for good and accompanied by his wife, he started visiting all the holy shrines along the entire stretch of the country. He sang in praise of the Lord at each place. He has the unique distinction of visiting the maximum number divya deshas.

## His works and Style

Out of a total number of 4000 verses in Divya Prabhandham, Thirumangai azwar has composed 1253 verses, that is more than 30 percent. He had proficiency in all the four broad divisions of Tamil prosody viz. ashu, Madhura, Chithra and Visthaara. He was therefore given the title, ‘Narkavipperumal’. Those who recite the prabhandham experience the beauty and width of his works (Adiyen has been associated with seva kalam in Andavan Ashramam for nearly two decades and enjoying the azwar’s style - no doubt this owes to the grace of the Lord on ‘yours truly’).

## Vedantha Desika’s encomium

Vedantha Desika talks about Thirumangai Azwar in his Prabhanda Saram thus: ‘Born under the Kritthika star in the month of Karthikai, in Tirukuraiyaloor, O Thirumangai azwar, may you bless me to recite, and understand the embedded knowledge in your compositions regularly, comprising 1084 verses of Periya Thirumozhi, the twenty verses of the matchless Thirukurunddhandakam, thirty verses entitled Thirunedunddhandakam, the one verse entitled Thiruvezukotthirukkai, 40 verses of Shiriya Thirumadal and 78 verses of Peria Tirumadal having rare imports.’

## Works by Thirumangai Azwar

Thirumangai Azwar has written six compositions, of which Peria Thirumozhi is of monumental proportions. The other works are Thirunkurunddandakam, Thirunedunddhandakam, Shiriya Tirumadal, Peria Thirumadal and Thiruveyukkoothirukkai. My book presently covers the first three works of Thirumangai azwar.

## Peria Thirumozhi

This is the biggest work in Nalayira Divya Prabhandham accounting for 1084 verses with a total of 108 decads. The azwar brings before our eyes the Lord’s exquisite beauty, His noble qualities, his experience of Lord’s feats in various manifestations - he assumes, for a fuller experience, Nayakibhava that is, as a woman, he experiences the estrangement from the Nayaka, the Lord.

## Broad coverage of verses opposite Divya Deshas

There is no hard and fast division of the number of verses extolling the various holy shrines While it should not be construed that a particular shrine is more important because more verses have been sung on it. It is just that the azwar’s outpourings came out depending on his emotions. For example; there are shrines which have garnered 100 verses as in the case Thirukkannapuram or Thirunaraiyoor. It does not mean that Srirangam or Tirupathi or Ahobilam is less important. He has sung on 86 shrines; there are other azwars who have sung on some of those not visited by him.

## Contents - a keyhole coverage

The azwar starts his work saying that he wasted his life in worldly pleasures ; and wandering thus, owing to Lord’s great blessings, he chanced to receive the teaching of ashtakshari, the eight syllabled manthra, with its full import which propounds the Lord. He lauds the greatness of ‘Narayana Nama’ which confers everything.

‘It is a wasted life praising inferior humans. I used to move with relatives and friends’ says the azwar. Giving them all up, he requests the Lord to grant him doing service to Him and that this was granted by the Lord. If one utters His thousand names, they will not encounter any sufferings. He asks one to reach Badarikashrama before one is old.

He is enchanted and enamoured by Lord’s various divine qualities; His manifestations in Vibhava (Rama, Krishna, Narasimha etc.) which led to various feats for saving His devotees, are described by the azwar. His archa forms for e.g. in Salagrama, entreats his heart to join Him there.

The Lord is the inner soul of all beings composed of five elements. The Lord alone was there before. He (having brought out from His navel) is father to Brahma; He cannot be known fully ; He retrieved Vedas when there was utter darkness in ‘hamsa’ form (as swan).

In Krishnavataara, He went as a messenger of Pandavas; in Vamanavatara, He even begged for three feet of land; as Rama He chopped asunder the ten heads of Ravana as though they were a bunch of coconuts. He also manifested as Koorma (tortoise), Varaha (wild boar), and as Narasimha. He indeed is all Supreme.

He shows His form to those who, with intense wish and devotion, give up worldly pleasures and control rigidly their sense organs.

The entire universe is His body (prakara), including the seven seas, seven mountain ranges etc. etc.

The effulgent Lord has easy accessibility; He is everywhere and has immense kindness ; to achieve doing service to Him (for which He Himself is the route) is the supreme goal. He is always with Sri Mahalakshmi who is ever ready to redeem us by recommending us to His feet. The azwar is in his element when he says : ‘those eyes which do not see Him, that tongue to which He is not a subject, those minds that do not think about Him, those men who are not subservient to Him are all worth a straw.’

In short the azwar has elaborated the elements of tatthva, hitha and Purushaartha. The knowledgeable is one is that who becomes subservient to the Lord: Periya Thirumozhi propounds jnana (arivu), that is, knowledge about God.

## Thirukurunddhandakam

Just as a mountaineer needs a staff to climb the hills, so also these verses provide support to understand the nature of the Lord and the route (upaya) to realise Him. Comprising a mere twenty verses, one finds in this composition the azwar exclaiming: ‘I have found my treasure; the Lord resides in Tirukundanthai; Oh! with what words can the devotees laud Him ; He is sweet as Sugarcane juice! He is the honey!; ‘You are the Universe - the cause and effect in the eight quarters ; You’re the resplendent light; You’re the life breath of Arangam’ the azwar exclaims.

The azwar declares: ‘I know nothing save doing kainkaryam to Him. However sinned one is, one can still pray to the Lord. If one surrenders to Him His grace will flow and one can attain Him.’

## Thirunedunddhandakam

Thirunedundhandakam was a favourite prabhandham to Sri Parashara Bhattar. And the reason is not far to seek. This Prabhandham is considered to belong to a superior class under ‘dwani’ poetry. The azwar chooses to assume again Nayaki stance - dividing the Prabhandham into three parts, the second, third parts belong to the Nayaki’s mother and Nayaki, each ten. The first ten belong to the Azwar.

The attitude of bhakthi assumes shringara stance. Due to deep pangs of separation, the Nayaki wants to convey her mental disposition and to this end she uses birds, bees, clouds as messengers - they are in fact like acharyas undertaking the Purushakara role. The azwar describes the disappointment in not receiving response as Parakala Nayaki in a subdued manner. The Lord’s Supremacy and the urge to mingle with Him being the greatest goal, have been well brought out by the azwar.

## Other works by the Azwar

## The two Madals

‘Madal’ is an extreme action by a person, here Parakala Nayaki, who as a last resort and in utter desperation expresses / displays her love to the Nayaka (The Lord) in a public place giving up all considerations of shyness. By this action the Nayaki hopes to attract her lover’s attention, and she keeps publicising that she will commit suicide. Thirumangai azwar has assumed the role of a Nayaki and composed two ‘madals’ - the shorter version known as 'Shiriya Thirumandal’ and the longer one Peria ‘Thirumadal’. Covered below very briefly are the two madals.

Generally a disappointed male lover smears himself with ash, wears a garland of cheap flowers, carries a photo of his ladylove and sits under a palm tree. Shyness has no place in these actions. The main purpose is to convey ones love to the lover. Although women are known to resort to ‘madal’ in the North, it is said that it is copied in the South. Thirumangai azwar as a Nayaki conveys (her) intense love to the Nayaka, the Lord of Tirunaraiyoor. The real Nayaki is the soul seeking the Supersoul. In the composition running to 155 lines the azwar appeals to the Lord to descend from Paramapadham and accept his ‘flower of love’.

In Peria Thirumandal, which runs to 78 stanzas of irregular length, the azwar notes the uselessness of dharma / artha and considers ‘bhagvath kaama’ as the only route to the eternal Paramapadham (moksha) and he says that he has not heard of anyone telling its attainment since it takes place at the time of departure (death). ‘We will resort to madal since we have not realised the Lord’ says the azwar. ‘If the Lord does not listen I will publicise His lack of attention at all places wherever He resides’ says the Nayaki (the azwar in ‘Nayaki’ bhava (stance).

## Thiruvezukoothirukkai

This last one, though small, is extremely significant comprising 47 lines, but as a single verse in counting for 4000 Divya Prabhandham.

Having been totally disheartened with the samsaric life, the azwar resorts to reciting the Lord’s various noble qualities and appeals to the Lord to remove the obstacle of samsara so that he can experience Him. When queried by the Lord as to whether He is capable of it, the azwar replies : ‘The one who is the original cause / sustenance / dissolution of this universe alone can do this and it is not possible by me’. He talks about Lord’s various avataaras, and finally addresses the Lord in Thirukudanthai, Aravamudhan, who is reclining on Adhisesha. ‘I have surrendered unto Your holy feet. Remove all the inimical forces to end this samsara’ the azwar ends the Prabhandam thus.

## Azwar’s concern for continuity

Thirumangai azwar is credited with having started the ‘Adhyanothsavam’, comprising ten days as ‘Pahal Patthu’ and another ten days as ‘Raa Patthu’ ; during the twenty day period devotees who have learnt reciting four thousand divya prabhandham, assemble in a temple and recite the entire lot of Divya prabhandham. This shows Thirumangai azwar’s concern for continuity in extolling the Lord through the great treasure left behind by all the azwars.

## Thirumangai ‘Mannan’

Thirumangai azwar based on the foregoing, can deservedly be called the king - not only King of Thirumangai but also a leader in ensuring that the ordinary folks get to laud the Supreme Being and reach His holy feet.

## Only Azwar worshipped with His consort

Thirumangai Azwar is the only azwar whose idols appear with his wife, Kumudhavalli. Is it because of the fact that she did not originally belong to this earth? One can only surmise!

## He exceeded Veda Pirayum

According to Vedas one’s life span is a hundred years but Thirumangai azwar lived for 105 years.

**Peria Thirumozhi**

## First Patthu

## 1.1 Vadinen Vadi : On Ashtakshari

In the very first decad the azwar emphasizes the importance of ‘Srimad Ashtakshari’ manthra which declares the ultimate reality as Sriman Narayana - all the Vedas / Smrithis / Ithihasapuranas have emphatically averred thus. The azwar brings out its importance in all its glory and would want us to realise it so that we can get over the ‘samsaara’.

‘I got enmeshed in carnal pleasures. Kept pursuing them, I chanced to hit upon the route of knowledge - I got to know this ‘Narayana nama’. Without knowing this I have wasted all my life ; thanks to praying at Thirukudanthai, I am able to pronounce that name.

Thinking that the body and its protection are all that matter, I committed grave sins. I am fortunate I got to know the ‘Narayana nama’. Considering that, enjoying the women with attractive eyes is the best vocation, I destroyed my life. “What would you find for yourself, if you were to progress in life” if one were to ask, my answer would be Lord’s mercy / kindness. I got the instruction of ‘Thirumanthra’ and its meaning from the Lord.

“I know not how I came to win His protection. I now deserve to be His servant at Paramapadam. I came to understand the mutual relation between Him and me. He is the goal, He is the route to attain it. He is our controller, protector. This is the result of my praying at ‘Thanjaimamani’ Kshethra.\*”

The azwar advises us to utilise this ‘difficultly-obtained’ human life to fulfil one’s life’s goal (of attaining Him).

For all the obstacles coming in the way of moving towards realising the Lord, it is His grace which overcame all. This ‘ashtakshari manthra’, the ‘shabdha brahma’, gives all the good things, including the benefit of kainkarya. Whether in difficulty or otherwise, you keep reciting this ‘manthra’ as that alone will uproot the ‘samsara’.

## 1.2 Vali Mavalatthu: On ‘Thirupiridhi’ Shrine

The Lord elucidated Himself to the azwar by instructing him that He is the master and such helpful attitude is also apparent in His coming Himself near us by being present in so many divya deshas in your vicinity.

While the bow in Lord’s hand gives a protective feeling to His devotees, His/devotee’s enemies get scared of it. Should the Lord for any reason get angry, uncontrollable happenings take place - the oceans split ; instead of nithyasooris, monkeys have to do the kainkaryam.

Just as the Lord facilitated marrying Napinnai by splitting asunder the bull’s horns, so also He is always ready to help His devotees. This place, Himalayas, is also the place where Narasimha who destroyed Hiranyakashipu, is present, and here even the wild boars resemble Narasimha! The azwar tells his mind to go to ‘Tirupiridhi’ and pray to Narasimha’s Two Holy Feet. This Lord of Tirupiridhi is none other than the Lord of Ksheerabhdhi where He is reclining with Sridevi, avers the azwar. Continuing, the azwar says that the very Lord, Vaikuntanatha, is now in Tirupiridhi for the benefit of His devotees - the supreme sign of Lord’s quality of protection.

In this holy place the gods offer pushpanjali to the Lord. There different flowers grow. These flowers are none but Ahimsa, controlling senses, mercy towards other living beings, tapas, dhyana, jnana etc.

The place described, being congenial to one and all, is suitable for serpents as also gods with Brahma onwards. Not just one name of God, but uttering a thousand names will ensure that the sins do not come anywhere near you. Such is the greatness of Lord’s quality of protection and His overlordship. The Lord protects us from our sins.

## 1.3 Muttha Mootthu: On Badari (before old age)

Having propounded in the last two decads the essence of tathva (reality), hitha (route) and Purushartha (the goal to be reached), through the medium of Thirumanthra, the azwar emphasizes the need for us to visit shrines while in this mortal frame enlisting the Lord’s quality of ‘Saulabhya’.

This is an extraordinary verse, similar to the one in Periazwar Thirumozhi (4.5), which describes the ravages of old age which wreck our physical fitness without our knowledge and the azwar is at pains to advise us to visit Badari before old age overtakes you.

The azwar emphasizes that we pray to Sriman Narayana at Badari, the Lord who extracted the milk from Poothana’s breasts. The young women taunt you when you use a stick, saying ‘there goes my father !’ The nerves on your body look like veritable ropes, you are given to coughing too loudly, your eyes roll trying to find your path, you drag your feet, keep mumbling incoherently and phlegm stops the words, the beautiful maidens shoo the old man off asking him not to come near them, and he has no alternative but to find a desolate place.

Before the aforesaid changes take place, the azwar advises one to reach Badari and pray to the Lord. Prostrate to Him who is wearing Tulasi garlands. Before the weakness of old age overtakes you, recite His thousand names fully and reach Badari where bees hum like mukthas singing ‘sama gana’.

## 1.4 Yenamunahi: On Sri Badarikaashrama

The azwar talked about, in the last decad, Lord’s quality of saulabhya, of Badari Narayana who is residing specially there to ward off His devotees’ difficulties. He is none other than Sri Varaha or Sri Rama. As Rama He killed Vali to protect His devotee; He also brought the end of Ravana : and we should surrender ourselves to that God who will grant us Paramapada. There is no need to go in search of Him. He is readily available in Badarikashrama, He being Siddopaya.

Reminding us of the Krishna Leela wherein Gopika sthrees’ clothes were stolen by Krishna, the azwar draws a parallel with Ganges knocking off the clothes of apsaras like Rambha who had kept them on the shore.

Yashodha was under fear as to whether Krishna will mistake her for Poothana and extract her life, but her suckling the young Krishna with her nectary milk warded off that fear. Didn’t He humble the seven bulls? The azwar talks about a big hill coming in the way of Ganges flow which caused havoc and Indra sent Iravatha to move that hill from the river course. The Lord granted Indra his desires. That very Lord is in Badarikashrama giving ‘His darshana’ to one and all.

The very river along which Vishwamithra led Rama and Lakshmana is clutching Badarikashrama.

## 1.5 Kalaiyum: On Saligrama

It is the ardent desire of the azwar to go to various shrines where the Lord has installed Himself to provide His ‘darshan’ easily. One such shrine is Salagrama. Armed with bow and arrows Sri Rama went from one forest to another; although there were others with Him, it is the arrow which finally ended Ravana. Despite Ravana’s army paraphernalia, Rama required just one arrow to bring down Ravana’s heads. That very Lord is in Salagrama to provide protection to succeeding generations. The Lord is world personified and all the things in this world are His ‘prakara’. He is in the form of divine idol with resplendent ‘Sudarshana’ discus and His beautiful form has found shape in Salagrama as ‘Bhuvana sundara’!

The presiding deity of Thirukudanthai bent the bow in such a way that both ends met facilitating sharp discharge of arrows to kill Ravana. That Lord has many names conveying His various ‘leelas’. Wearing garlands attracting nectar-drinking bees He is present in Salagrama where ‘Thaara’ water-birds abound. That very Lord got soorpanakha’s nose cut off by Lakshmana, and later lifted the Govardhana hill to protect the cows. He also drank Poothana’s life and not to forget the butter I curds kept by Yashodha which He relished.

The Lord who measured the world before Bali might change his mind also killed Hiranyakashipu who did not have the knowledge that ‘there is a soul different from body and he would one day join the Lord’. That very Lord, who is in Salagrama saved Rudra by warding off the curse given to him by Brahma. To Him the Srivaishnava groups. Nithyasooris and others address: ‘The Resident of Paramapada, You have left it for our sake and come down here - please show your mercy’. If you want to reign in Srivaikunta recite the Lord’s thousand names and also the divya Prabhandha.

## 1.6 Vanilaa Muruval: On Naimisharanya

Finding that despite azwar’s advice the people of this world did not behave as advised, the azwar assumes that he is subject to all the short-comings of people at large and wants to surrender to the Lord. There are words in this decad to suggest the five ‘angas’ (requirements) for undergoing sharanagathi.

Smitten by the beauty of women, I had thought that to be the goal. Now I realise the blunder and want to surrender to You. I had been a subject to sensual pleasures, wasted time and forgot remembering You. I want the refuge of Your holy Feet which even Nithyasooris seek. ‘You showed Your kindness’, it is indeed surprising. You, in fact, showed the way and inspired me to seek refuge in You.

Shuddering at the type of punishment at Yamaloka having desired other women, I am rid of such fear having surrendered to You and earned Your kindness and generosity. You are a real friend! I do not have anyone else to protect me.

The type of sins committed by me in killing so many lives even left Yama and his henchmen in a quandary as to what punishment should be meted out to me. Alternatively, having done all that since I have surrendered to You, they do not have an idea as to how to deal with me; I am now under Your refuge. You in fact were awaiting my arrival. You stayed in my mind steadfast and You are standing in Naimisharanya so that You are easily available to me.

I was subject to Kali’s arrows in terms of sense organs but the Lord of Thirukkurungudi could ward off all that. With pleasantly worded musical prayers I won Him through my self-surrender.

In order to extricate the soul from this body, one has to do some good thing, that in my case being that I surrendered to You. For this act, You provided me the ‘deep faith’, besides Sridevi providing purushakara (recommendation to accept me). That Lord is present here in Naimisharanya, the shrine where Devendra comes to pray along with other gods.

## 1.7 Angan: On Singavez - Kundram

The Lord responds with the declaration that He is present in places like Singvez Kundram for His devotees to feel His presence and that He is conveniently found along with Sridevi who provides ‘Purushakaara’.

The Lord demonstrated that He Himself finds ways to save His devotees as in the case of Narasimha manifestation. Hiranyakashipu had boons with which he could punish his people. Narasimha is present in this shrine and groups of devotees come and pray to Him to ward off the sins. The Lord assumed a unique appearance to humble Hiranyakashipu who used to create fear as he held his weapon, and he became God’s enemy the moment he became an enemy of Lord’s devotees: It appears the very sight of Narasimha made Hiranyakashipu fall flat, but since there was anger in the Lord towards him, He split his frame into two. In fact even devathas (gods) ran away seeing Narasimha’s terrifying appearance. His anger leapt all over, and unless one is a real devotee of the Lord this shrine is not easily accessible. A unique devotee that Prahlada was, the Lord came to his rescue. The shrine is a dense forest with trees rubbing against each other leading to jungle fire.

Such a Lord is present in this shrine and let us pray to Him there to fulfil over life’s mission. Sridevi is present to pacify the enraged Narasimha who is our Lord and Master praised by Kaliyan, the benevolent being. Those who become proficient in this Prabhandham ‘will be free from evil’.

## 1.8 Kongalarndha Malar: On Thiruvenkatam

Just as the Lord helped His devotee to overcome Hiranyakashipu so also He is ever ready to drive out our enemies and to this end He is in Kaliyuga. He is standing tall in Sri Venkatachala. The very Lord lying supine in Ksheerasagara, He will ward off all our ‘virodhis’ without a trace - didn’t He, as young Krishna, uproot the tree, an asura in disguise? For those who cannot reach Ksheerabdhi He is lying supine in Srirangam. Our Lord assumes various colours in different yugas - white in Krithayuga, blue in dwapara and black in Kaliyuga.

When Krishna walked in between those Arjuna trees, they simply fell. He saved the cows from Indra’s wrath by holding the Govardhana hills. To-day also He has the same purpose in standing on Venkatachala. Even bigger encounters He has won as in Mahabharatha war. The same Lord entertained the gopis with His dance. The Lord, Krishna whose brilliance when He installed Dharma Puthra as king, can be seen to-day in Thiruvidavendhai.\*

Always wanting to help His ‘aashrithas’, just as Vamana, He is readily available as at Venkatadri - the very Lord who eats all the worlds and lies supine as a small child on the ocean. While He drove Himself to reach fast to save Gajendra, He saved, by appearing in an ‘avataara’ the very minute Prahlada thought of Him.

With the very body given by Him, utter His names; He is just waiting at Venkatadri for you to take refuge. He has the entire universe as His body and is its soul.

Just as everyone has the right to utter his mother’s name so also he has the right to recite ‘ashtaksharimanthra’ when confronted with difficulties. Those who recite this, even once, will overcome samsaara.

Looking at the fish jumping up and down in water reservoir in Venkatadri gives the azwar, the picture of mukthas and nithyas. Thirumalai has a number of such water reservoirs.

## 1.9 Thaaye Thandhai: For Thiruvenkatam

Expressing his intention to take refuge in Thiruvenkatam in the last decad, the azwar arrives in Thirumalai and entreats the Lord to excuse him for all his faults and accept his service (kainkarya). Realising the sesha-seshi relationship he takes refuge in the Lord.

Having made the mistake of depending on relatives, he says that the Lord is the real ‘bandhu’ and that his desire to see Him has heightened. He asks the Lord to accept all types of kainkarya and bless him. ‘All types of sins were committed by me and caused pain to others. I surrender at Your holy feet. I know that without looking into my previous history You will protect me’ says the azwar. If he were to be queried by the Lord as to why not he come to Him after a few more ‘janmas’, the azwar would say that he had enough of it all - he had not done anything good or deserved to undertake bhakthiyoga. ‘Fearing another janma to suffer, I am seeking Your refuge’.

Great sins subjected me to deep distress and realising my inability to follow the routes of Jnana or bhakthi, I request You to grant me practising eternal kainkaryam to You. Subject to the ‘dehaatma’ illusion, I underwent suffering; even after acquiring some knowledge I committed sins and unlike Prahlada, Lakshmana, Nammazwar who right from birth attained You, I wasted my life. At least now You accept me as Your servant. Unable to tolerate any more birth-rebirth I have found refuge in Your holy Feet enduring the grave sins committed by people like me ; being in company of Sridevi, ‘You are the refuge for everyone. You get me to do kainkaryam to You’, the azwar tells the Lord who is in Tirumalai granting His darshan to everyone including ‘Nithyasooris’.

## 1.10 Kannar Kadelshooz: Continuing to talk to Tiruvenkata

Having asked the Lord to grant him the ‘Kainkarya Vritthi’ to him, here he asks the Lord to eliminate the opposing elements like ‘I’ and ‘mine’ attachments.

Once the Lord removed the obstacle personified by Ravana the gods came in huge groups to Thirumalai to pray to the Lord. ‘I am suffering from the evil of believing in the freedom of myself (Swatantrabimana). ‘Please free me from this’ the azwar requests the Lord. ‘Avidya and the Karma-link from beginningless time are dogging us. Pray please free us from their clutches’. The azwar declares that Thirumalai is a lot richer than Paramapada. ‘I consider it as the most pleasing ; please bless me to experience it’. Just the same way ‘You got the benefit to Indra from Bali, so also let me have the advantage of doing kainkaryam to You by removing all obstacles ; Just as You eliminated the obstacle of Prahlada in Narasimha avataara, and just as You are receiving kainkaryam from Adisesha, please accept my kainkaryam also’ !

The azwar notes that the Lord as per azwar’s desire installed Himself in the azwar’s mind and accepting him in equal measure for His own sake and thus showed His profound kindness.

Just as He warded off the bulls to win Nappinnai, so also should the Lord remove all obstacles. ‘In order to get us to serve Him, He is standing in Tirumalai; He will not leave my mind wherein He has entrenched Himself’.

He is too far removed for ‘anashrithas’ but too near for ‘aashrithas’. The one living in Vaikunta, came in search of me here and installed Himself in me as though He would like to grant anything I desire. This indeed is a rare jewel which would stay even after acquiring knowledge. Just like Guha guarding Sri Rama, hunters are guarding the Lord of Tiruvenkata hills.

## Second Patthu

## 2.1 Vaanavar Thangal: Tiruvenkatam at heart

The azwar declares that for securing the Lord’s Feet for refuge, it was the mind that worked and he thanks it for the same. He tells it that it resembles the hearts of Nithyasooris. Addressing his mind / heart for facilitating kainkaryam to the Lord who is eternally residing in Thirumalai and who as Vamana went to Bali to get what He wanted he says : ‘You, my heart, have engaged yourself in serving in full measure, and with this I am satisfied.’

The Lord is unique in that He has no relatives whatever in karmic terms, but He loves those devotees who love Him intensely and to prove that, He eliminates their future *janmas / samsara*. As though to facilitate our kainkarya to Him, He has installed Himself in Tirumalai - a place signifying His overlordship - He takes His devotees and their relatives to Nithya Vibhoothi; they have worshipped Him with flowers available from eight directions in Tirumalai.

Continuing admiration of his mind / heart for being firm in doing kainkaryam, he notes that the Lord has shown His grace - He will instill more interest in kainkaryam so that He can take His devotee to Paramapadam. It is the mind again which thought it right not to get entangled with Boudhas / Jains; it secured kainkaryam to the Lord and is blessed ; no amount of praise is great. Not resorting to mere ‘tarka’, the mind decided to do service to the Lord’s feet; the Lord is easily available to everyone at Thirumalai. Further it did not give room for any doubt as to Lord’s nearness or otherwise and ‘without even telling me, it acquired unshakeable faith in the Lord of Tiruvengadam’.

O, listen my mind! There are those who have come into His domain, but have not seen Him; You acquired directly His servicehood!

Clouds fully laden with water and accompanied by lightning are like the Lord accompanied by Mahalakshmi! All those rains reach Tirumalai where the Lord, who once retrieved Vedas in swan form, has made Himself easily available to all.

## 2.2 Kashaiyaadai: On Tiruvallur

Having admired his mind / heart for having secured the servicehood in Venkatanayaka, the azwar worries about those who cannot make it to Venkatadri due to distance. For such devotees the Lord is lying supine in Tiruvevvulur. The Lord who is not even understood by Brahma and others and does impossible feats is facilitating His ‘darshan’ in Tiruvevvulur.

The Lord burnt to ashes the town of Ravana who misbehaved with Lokamatha. He will deal similarly with enemies of His devotees. And to enable, ‘sharanagathi’ at His feet, He is lying supine in Tiruvevvulur. That Ravana believed more in the capacity of a sword given by a god than in Rama’s bravery. Unable to bear the pangs of separation, He sent a monkey as messenger to Lokamatha, and with a single arrow He burnt Lanka. In Krishnavatara, He became a ‘Pandava dhootha’. To win the hand of Nappinnai whose fingers were so soft that even a ball could hurt them, He humbled the seven bulls. He is the same Lord you find in Tiruvevvulur which is filled with the redolence of blossoms.

The Lord in Tiruvevvulur is the controller of the Universe and is having titles of Pundarikaksha, Yajnapurusha and is the inner soul of the first-born Brahma. He is a ‘Poornan’ (Ever-complete). The inner soul of one and all, He is the final receptacle for offer of our souls. The Lord brought to the world the Vedas and the Vedic- supplement, the Bhagavad Gita, all for our benefit.

Sridevi, His consort, who pleads on our behalf to the Lord to excuse our mistakes, is always with the Lord, who has ‘Shriyahpathithva and Sathyakamathva’, and deserving to be worshipped as divine idol, is lying supine in Tiruvevvulur for all to worship with garlands. Those who sing these verses become masters, even in the Paramapada itself.

## 2.3 Virperuvizhavum: Now on Tiruvallikkeni

The Lord is ever enthusiastic to meet His devotees and hurries to ward off their predicament as in the case of Gajendra. He was in front during Bharatha war shielding Arjuna. For His aunt's sake He gave up His kingdom and the azwar finds Him in Tiruvallikkeni.

He eliminates the devotees’ difficulties and keeps up His promises as in the Bharatha war when He did not lift a weapon to protect Pandavas; He grants the desires of those who lead their life as per tenets in Vedas. But He is like the death knell for ‘asuras’. For His devotees’ sake He even assumed the woman’s garb and offered the nectar (amrutha). That very Lord is in Tiruvallikeni which should be mentioned only after uttering the name of the lake where Sridevi lives.

The Gopalakas approached Krishna when Indra poured torrential rain; He held aloft the Govardhana hills to protect them. That very Lord, protector of all is in Tiruvallikkeni. You go and surrender yourselves to Him there.

The consort of Bhoo, Nila and Sridevi became a messenger to Pandavas. Just as we take refuge in Sridevi, so also she is inseparably with the Lord, who is giving His divine ‘darshan’ to all at Tiruvallikkeni.

When Pandavas lost the game of dice, the wicked Dusshasana called Draupadhi his ‘dasi’ and dragged her. Who came to her rescue then? The Lord not only saved her but also saw to it that the wives of Kauravas also suffered.

Listening to the unique feats of Sri Rama, his brothers sing praises of their brother. So also all the gods, rishis, residents of Ayodhya, azwar / acharyas, all sing His praises. But Ravana as also Hiranyakashipu could not stand the name of the Lord and met their natural end at the hands of ‘Purushasimha’, Sri Rama and Narasimha.

When Gajendra, not having been able to find flowers on land, found flower plants in a pond and when he put his foot in water, the crocodile caught him. In a trice the Lord, responding to his prayer saved him with His discus. ‘Sing praises of the Lord who is in Tiruvallikkeni which is adjoining the beautiful ‘Mayilapuram’.’

## 2.4 Anrayar Kulakkodiyodu: On Tiruneermalai

The Lord descends to this world with Sridevi from time to time so that He can uplift us and He is showering all His love to the devotees in Tiruneermalai; the greatness of this shrine is such that all the combined affection of Thirunaraiyoor, Thiruvali, Thirukudanthai and Thirukkovaloor is being shown by the Lord at Tiruneermalai, the right place for everyone to seek His refuge.

The Lord who simultaneously got the asuras burnt in Indra’s Kandavavana and destroyed Indra’s ego, measured this world immediately after ‘dhaanadharai’ fell and also rendered Prahlada’s words as ‘truth’ when He appeared from a wooden pillar. To decimate evil forces and render the Mother Earth lighter, He used Sudarshana discus in such a way that the sun was blanked leading to darkness which the enemy forces mistook as dusk. That very Lord is in Thiruneermalai.

Since Pandavas had taken refuge in Him, the Lord saw to it groups of enemy kings got destroyed. He built a bridge in the swelling ocean. The strongly-built forts of Lanka were in smithereens because of His arrows. The Lord who won us over with His attractive features is in the holy shrine of Tiruneermalai.

The one who without any effort can keep the entire Universe in His stomach, who as Parasurama humbled the wicked Kshathriyas as many as twenty one times, and is the overlord, is in Tiruneermalai.

The wicked Poundrika Vasudeva who was faking the Lord Krishna was humbled. Hiranyakashipu who challenged the Lord as being equal to Him was split asunder by Narasimha who resides in this shrine. To those devotees who with devotion offer their soulflower at His Feet, He is available at Thiruneermalai and assures them ‘Maa shuchah’, ‘His abhayapradhana’ : He will eliminate their sins and His greatness knows no limits. This ‘great’ Lord has chosen to be in Tiruneermalai so that His devotees can easily approach Him - if they are free from desires they will serve Him and attain Him later. Should they wish to get something from Him, they will receive it easily and after enjoying their time in this world they will reach His Feet.

## 2.5 Parayadhundu: On Kadanmallai Talasayanam

The Lord who is easily accessible by the devotees and who wards off obstacles is Himself the upaya (route) to us ‘Do not give Him up thinking that He is inaccessible. Excepting Him everything else is useless. Seek refuge in Him’ says the azwar.

The Lord is supreme help personified. As Krishna He decimated many ‘asuras’; He accepted Gajendra’s flower before it withered, He killed Keshi effortlessly. In order that His devotees may receive what they desire He is present in Kadanmallai Talasayanam. His divine idol is resplendent.

Ignorant fellows who approach lesser gods are advised by azwars to seek refuge in the Lord, who is the inner soul of even Brahma and Rudra. He alone is reality. To attain Him, He Himself is the medium. He will take Kalki avataara shortly.

While He innocently drank Poothana’s poisonous milk. He also ate butter and curds touched by Yashodha and showed such food is staple diet for Him. He is equally at ease amongst Vedic scholars and cowherd women. With protection as His trait He got me to become His servant. ‘I have fulfilled my existence by understanding Him. You also seek refuge in Him’ the azwar advises us thus.

This Lord is not just sleeping on a fig leaf, or lying supine on Adisesha in Ksheerabhdhi but one who has undertaken innumerable manifestations. Having missed the chance of touching His holy Feet when He grew as Trivikrama, I have found Him here to-day.

If, despite His so many manifestations, one does not think He is ‘Sarveshwara’ (overlord), they will be subject to ‘demonic nature’. He assumed a woman’s garb only to help His devotees. Similarly ‘Narasimha avataara’. The very Lord is in Thirumeyya. ‘I got the opportunity of seeing endlessly with my eyes to my utmost satisfaction here’ says the azwar.

The Lord has Himself shown the way in ‘shaastras’ - the Vedas, divya Prabhandas. Puranas glorify His unique qualities. Those who have surrendered to Him should recite His names. In order to let the people know that He is the overlord, He is lying supine in this holy shrine.

## 2.6 Nannadha: More on Talasayanam

The azwar propounds in this decad the all important ‘bhagawathsamashrayanam’, that is, mingling with bhagavathas who are completely enamoured of the Lord This will help in going deep in knowing ‘bhagawadvishayam’. ‘Bhagavathaseshathwa’ is propounded in this decad.

Vidura was witness to the disrespectful and deceitful behaviour of Duryodhana when Krishna entered his house. He goes on missions to dangerous places for the sake of His devotees. He assumed full charge of ‘samundramanthana’ and gave nectar to the gods. For the sake of His ‘aashrithas’ He, who has to be amidst luxury with Nithyasooris, is lying in ‘Kadanmallai sthala’ for the easy accessibility of His devotees.

Sridevi and Bhoodevi who are kindness personified have the Lord on their ‘right’ side so that they can plead with the Lord to excuse our countless mistakes, thereby enabling us to meet the Lord without fear. Didn’t Bhoodevi get an assurance to save His/Her devotees when one surrenders to Him while in good health and of stable mind. Rather than helping us during deluge He appears to assure us now itself in Kadanmallai sthala as ‘seshashayee’.

For getting back ‘Seethadevi’ He demonstrated to Ravana His bravery through killing of the brave Vali, building a bridge over sea and occupying Lanka, but Ravana did not think of Sharanagathi at Lord’s feet and got annihilated. That very Lord has chosen to lie supine in Kadanmallai sthala. ‘And those bhagavathas who think that it is for our sake He is there, are my masters’ says the azwar. Not falling a prey to heretic faiths those who regard Kadanmallai sthalashayanam as the holiest shrine. ‘O my mind feel love and bhakthi towards such bhagavathotthamas’ the azwar tells his mind ; Poothana sought out the overlord for suckling, and Kamsa had too much self-belief in his own strength - both were killed by the Lord who resides in this shrine. O mind, respect those bhagavathas who keep remembering this shrine. The azwar is concerned that the Lord is sleeping on the floor.

The Lord, has such sausheelya that He has place in His body for both who do not respect Him as also Nithyasooris. He is worshipped in this shrine by Nithyasooris and sky-trotting ‘Yakshas’ and ‘gandarvas’. The azwar emphasizes that he is standing with extreme respect glorifying those bhagavathas who worship the Lord’s feet here - an example of ‘bhagavatha seshathva’.

## 2.7 Thivalum: On Thiru-ldavendhai

Having realised the experience of being a servant to the bhagavathas, known as ‘ananyarhaseshathva’, the basic requisite for which is being subservient to God Himself and ruminating on this aspect, the azwar develops a deep desire to experience Him ; it did not materialise. He assumes the Nayaki bhava conveying the pangs of separation, which is conveyed here through the medium of Nayaki’s mother to the Lord at Thiruvidavendhai: ‘My daughter, disregarding all of us just concentrated on You and having not got You is suffering. What do You propose to do with her’ Nayaki’s mother queries the Lord! Ksheerasamudra-born Sridevi glowing endlessly has lodged Herself on the Lord’s chest and knowing this too the Nayaki is not letting her desire vanish. The mother, pointing to her daughter, says ‘I know not of any solution. If there is one You Yourself tell me. Just for the express purpose of looking after (people like) my daughter You have come to Tiruvidavendhal’!

Known as ‘madal’, the azwar brings out the pitiable condition of Nayaki (her viraha vedanai). Nammazwar as also Kulasekhar azwar have resorted to ‘Nayaki bhava’ to convey their longing to mingle with the Lord. Nayaki’s friends try to pacify but the result is ‘added pangs’: The sign of her having become weak is that her bangles slip out of hand ! Cool breeze adds to her pains. Time hangs heavy. She has stopped talking, save uttering Lord’s name, if at all.

‘Her family respect’ is taking a beating. Thinking about Sitadevi’s plight and Her final reunion with the Lord, she entertains the idea that He will come to her. She does not realise that we are also there but keeps mumbling about You only. Haven’t You stationed Yourself in Tiruvidavendhai to get her’ asks Nayaki’s mother.

If the Lord were to tell the mother that she should remind her daughter about family respect, the mother would reply that she has forgotten that I am her mother, ‘She seems to be listening to Krishna’s flute tunes. Which aspect of You, is it Your bewitching looks, Your smile etc. that has captivated her, I do not know. What do You propose to do?’ the mother pines.

As Jnana gets covered or blanked, the azwar thinks of those ‘avataaras’ of the Lord where He has given ‘Jnanopadesha’. He is in Tiruvidavendhai to address to the wants of those who have bhakthi and interest in the Lord. The azwars weapon (Velayudha) is said to protect Srivaishnavas’ interest.

## 2.8 Thiripuram: On Ashtabujakara shrine

Having noticed a little shy look on the Lord’s face the Nayaki’s mother starts lauding His qualities, when the Nayaki gets somewhat awake. Getting some confidence that He may come to her rescue she talks, as it were, to the Lord at Thiruvattapuyakara swamy, and feels that He has replied to her queries. In this decad we find a dialogue between the Nayaki and her mother / her friends.

Tiripuram is one of the towns burnt by Rudra. He is god of annihilation while Brahma is the god of creation. Both of them were amazed at Narasimha, whose holy feet became refuge to all those wanting Hiranya’s end. When queried about Narasimha, the Nayaki is told by the Lord that He, in that form, came to a boy’s rescue and similarly in order to come to her rescue He is present in this shrine.

Sri Rama’s feats and qualities were lauded even by His enemies including Ravana. The Nayaki expresses that He also looks like Vamana who grew as Trivikrama. Who is this then, she queries. The Lord responds: I am not the one who has come here for winning any land. I have come on My own to get you!!

Vibhishana had described the extraordinary looks of Sri Rama when He held His bow. If asked by her who this handsome person was, the Lord would reply: ‘Humbling Kamsa’s Kuvalayapeeda, I warded off the fear of imprisoned women. Not just that. I have come here expressly to eliminate your fear too!’ He also held Govardhana hills to protect the cows and cowherds just as easily as He broke Kuvalayapeeda’s tusks - this elephant was one of thinking type and knew no fear. The Lord continued ‘Do not think I am so far away but I am right here for your sake.’

Bedecked always with Kausthuba, Panchajanya etc. He is glowing like an ocean. The Lord tells the Nayaki : ‘Don’t think I am unapproachable like an ocean. I am right here so that one can consciously know me! She retorts’: Having robbed everything mine, nothing, however beautiful, can match or measure up to Him. ‘Who is this then’, if she asks, the Lord responds: ‘I am standing here so that you may know Me!’ “Looking at Me, My ‘Tulasi mala’ etc. don’t be led to think or not believe as to whether I am the one residing in Paramapada; I am standing here expressly for you.’

When the Nayaki talks about His weaponry, accompaniment of Sridevi and her inability to live without Him, the Lord responds: ‘I cannot sustain without You!’.

All that I have like bangles are not anymore under my control. Everything has become His: ‘Is He a protector or destroyer ?’ The Lord responds : ‘Don’t be scared that I am the Lord. I am one like You standing here.’

The supreme Lord resembling the black clouds is glowing in Ashtabujakara shrine.

## 2.9 Sholluvan: On Parameshwara Vinnagaram

A pallava had undertaken to do service to the Lord by way of getting jewelry to the deity at Vinnagaram in huge numbers. The azwar allows himself to get the feeling of such ‘kainkaryam’ to the Lord.

The one who is the subject of Vedas and who is the inner soul of Brahma is residing at Parameshwara Vinnagaram shrine in Kanchi.

Brahma’s ability for creation was provided to him by the Lord, whose navel was the centre producing the sky, sun, moon, oceans, land etc. That very Lord is in Parameshwara Vinnahara - this is the shrine to which the pallava provided his service. The deity Varadaraja resides in Parameshwara Vinnahara. The azwar known for his bravery himself is appreciative of that of Pallava King who humbled Pandya king.

The Lord who gave place to the entire universe in His stomach; the kind-hearted Lord accepted the service offered by the Pallava king. This Pallava king is famed to have a serpent as symbol in his flag - the Lord had humbled a serpent Himself and let him go to a distant place. This holy place is the one where the Pallava had done his service to the Lord.

The Lord who eliminates “aashrithas’ virodhis” resides in Parameshwara Vinnaharam - He even used His finger nails to ward off Hiranya. The Pallava king with serpent ensign comes to this shrine to offer worship. In this holy shrine resides the Lord who assumed Vamanavataara and turned into Trivikrama; also Sri Rama who assembled the monkeys into an organised army and built a bridge with stones which normally sink in water; also the same Lord humbled the seven bulls for the sake of moon-faced Napinnai.

The King with a durable fame in this world, the pallava, comes with utmost devotion to offer kainkaryam to the Lord at Parameshwara Vinnaharam about which (the Kalikanri) Tirumangai azwar has sung poetically.

## 2.10 Manjadu: On Tirukkovaloor

In order to facilitate the devotees to offer worship in accordance with their ‘ashrama dharmas’, the Lord has stationed Himself with His consort in Tirukkovaloor.

The Lord swallowed the entire Universe with skies, mountains, oceans etc. and slept on a small fig leaf; to this Lord Vedic seers offer ‘soma-sacrifice’. The shrine is on the banks of Pennai river. ‘I have seen Him in Tirukkovaloor’, says the azwar.

The Lord is praised twice a day through seven swaras / six angas / five sacrifices / four Vedas / and three fires. Celestials (devatas) offer Tulasi garlands and Sridevi and Bhoodevi keep pressing His holy Feet in an effort to see that He does not notice the blemishes of His ‘ashrithas’. This Lord, in Tirukkovalur, not only saved Gajendra but also pressed his legs with His own hands using His ‘anga vastram’. Not only one devotee He saves but also hordes of them –didn’t He eliminate ‘Maali’ who was Ravana’s predecessor; similarly Khara, Viradha, Kabandha were slain with His arrow. After ‘Ravanavadha’ the Vedas reemerged in Dandakaranya ; similarly in Tirukkovaloor where He resides Vedas reign supreme.

The azwar thinks of the picture of Krishna’s navaneetha theft when highly secured pots were no match to His skills. Thinking that He will just escape if He is tied ordinarily, Yashodha bound Him to a mortar. If His enemies had done it, He would have simply escaped! However even now there is someone (Sridevi) to watch Him so that He does not notice our blemishes. ‘The Lord in Tirukkovaloor, where the ‘gramadevata’ is Durga was worshipped by me’, says the azwar. The very Lord once humbled Kuvalayapeeda, Keshi, Arjuna trees; He also killed Kamsa.

When Duryodhana ruled that Krishna should not use weapons in war, he became a ‘saarathi’ to Arjuna and those, who knew the inner secret, knew that Arjuna was just a medium through the Lord who won the war for pandavas. This very Lord is in Tirukkovaloor. The Lord’s brilliance is heightened by His entourage and He is present in His divine idol and is seen to grant His blessing to those who keep watching His hands. This very Lord who eliminated Gajendra’s suffering seems to be waiting at Tirukkovaloor expecting devotees to come and seek His ‘anugraha’.

## Third Patthu

## 3.1 Irunddhan: Tiru ayindirapuram (Tiruvahindrapuram)

While the Lord was found accessible to rajo-guna filled Pallava king at Parameshwara Vinnaharam, and for saathvic devotees at Tirukkovaloor, the azwar opines that the Lord Devanayaka at Tiruvahindrapuram offers refuge to all.

The Lord lifted the mother Earth as Varaha and brought it up balancing it on a single tooth and then slept in Milky ocean. He resides eternally in Tiruvahindrapura where one can benefit while here and hereafter too. His overlordship in terms of having Sridevi, holding Sudarshana discus and the substance of Vedas are all in place and He resides in Tiruvahindrapuram. He is ever truthful to His devotees and is known by the epithet ‘Dasasathya’.

Hiranya wreaked enmity and made no secret to the Lord that he was Lord’s enemy. Splitting his chest asunder, the Lord showered His kindness on Prahlada. Learning about Bali ‘dhana offer’ He for His devotee’s sake went to receive the offer of three feet and measured the Earth. He effortlessly finished off seven bulls for Nappinnai’s sake. Tiruvahindrapura is the place where that Lord resides.

The hunch-back Manthara whose thinking was also equally crooked was responsible in sending Rama into exile. Forgetting as though Chithrakoota and Dandakaranya He is residing in Thiruvahindrapuram.

Ravana was priding that his Lanka situated on Trikootachala was inaccessible to anyone, but Sri Rama saw to it that his tens rolled. Not only did He break the bow to win the hand of beautiful Sita, He also protected gopalakas from torrential rain using Govardhana hills. This very Lord is residing in Tiruvahindrapuram.

The Lord took the enemy arrow on to His chest saving His devotee Arjuna. Without wielding a weapon He fought the war for Pandavas. But He declared they won it. He is the inner soul to all including Brahma, Rudra. He is accessible by everyone, including ‘dhanavas’ at Tiruvahindrapuram.

## 3.2 (Oon) Un Vada: On Tillai Tiruchitthirakootam

For ordinary folk who are not capable of practising Bhakthi Yoga, the course open to attain His Lotus feet is Sharanagathi which is open to one and all. The ‘divya desha’, Chithrakoota, will enable one to get to the Lord.

There is no need to resort to harsh penance but worship Him, as per Your ‘Varnashrama dharma’ in a ‘divya desha’ like Chithrakoota where all obstacles to attain Him will vanish - learn Vedas and conduct ‘bhagavath aradhana’ and mingle with Vedic seers at Tillai Tiruchittthirakootam ; all that is required is the innate desire to attain Lord’s holy feet. The Lord will uplift You – didn’t He save Bhoodevi from ocean depths?

If queried whether the Lord will display the same trait as in the case of Bhoodevi to save us, the azwar responds saying that He went as Vamana begging for land for Indra’s sake. Again, didn’t He as Parasurama humble the ego-filled Kshathriyas going round as many as twenty one times. If you desire to attain Him, hurry to Tillai Tiruchitthirakootam, where even young maidens and parrots recite Vedas.

You make haste to the place where the Lord who can dry up Your ocean of samsara (cycle of birth / deaths). If people query as to whether it is at all possible to approach Him with the heavy load of sins committed from beginningless time, the azwar replies that the ever co-operative and kind Sridevi and Nappinnai piratti are waiting to help you.

This divya desha has the unique tradition of three thousand Vedic seers worshipping daily thereby increasing His brilliance. Concentrate on Him alone and attain Him; at Tillai the supreme devotees of the Lord are singing to the great joy of devotees with unlimited love for the Lord.

## 3.3 Vaadamarudhidai: Continuing on Tillai

**Tiruchitthirakootam**

The azwar reassures the chethanas: although the manifestations as Rama and Krishna are over, one can still see the very Lord in Tillai Tiruchitthirakootam.

The Lord who fascinated and captivated all by His ‘leelas’ as Krishna is residing in this shrine. If one were to query whether it is the same Lord who is in Chithrakoota and as to how to ascertain it, the azwar’s response is that the Gopikas knew full well that the young lad was Sriahpathi Himself. Similarly we should regard the deity in Chithrakoota. Didn’t He when Yashodha bound Him, a small boy, to a mortar, drag it, and finish off the two Arjuna trees ; then the ‘devatas’ and cowherds sang His praises. With this feat accomplished, the cowherds considered Him as the Supreme being; besides this feat He had also humbled the Kalinga to earn the tag of ‘Paramathma’.

The azwar says that from the feats just described as also the fact that He helped Napinnai by eliminating the seven bulls, should give one the assurance that He will protect all those who take refuge in Him. He comes playing in the streets with all the glory having accomplished impossible feats.

He has helped those who had not surrendered to Him by showing His divine form. Those who reject Him will face the same fate as Hiranya, and He stood by Prahlada, His devotee.

Having said that the Lord has unique qualities so that one can take refuge in Him; the azwar emphasizes that even otherwise the beauty of His idol is so bewitching, His weaponry so stunning and His consorts adding to His overall attraction that even the ego-filled Brahma and others offer worship to Him Such a Lord is present in Tillai Tiruchitthirakootam where there is abundance of flowers with bees not leaving them.

## 3.4 Oru Kuralai: Kaali Sri Rama Vinnaharam

The azwar talks about the type of place, a paramaikanthi, who has taken refuge in the Lord, should have so that his daily routine centering around service to the Lord is not affected at all. The azwar says that such an appropriate place for a paramaikanthi is Kaali Sri Rama Vinnaharam.

Just for the sake of His ‘aashritha’ (Indra), the Lord went begging to Bali for three feet of land. The azwar suggests that rather than seeking refuge in Him, we could go to the divya desha where He is present, that is, ‘Sri Rama Vinnahara’ shrine and He will bless us with whatever we want. The two ‘Iswaras’ viz. Brahma and Rudra who suffered from self-ego and prided over their long age. Through Romasha Maharshi the Lord taught a lesson to Brahma and told him that Brahmas and Rudras are like water bubbles, they come and go; Rudra who just twisted one of Brahma’s faces and the ‘kapala’ bonded itself to Rudra, were saved by our Lord He is present in Sri Rama Vinnahara, where the women of lower varna while harvesting keep singing and are enamoured of the beauty of one another. The azwar warns us not to take along with you those who are subject to carnal pleasures.

If one fails to go to this shrine he may find himself in the same predicament as a Banasura or a Hiranyakashipu. If you seek refuge here, the Lord gets extremely interested as He does in Nappinnai. The beauty of this place is that the walls are said to be bedecked with jewels of all kinds with the result one cannot distinguish between day and night.

The Lord as Parasurama did ‘tarpana’ with the blood extracted from wicked Kshatriya kings and He is present in this shrine. Captivated by Krishna the women here appear love-stricken. The women here excel the moon in their beauty and the moon feels that he could not add to their glow. Sri Rama who is present in this shrine eliminated Vali along with Kabandha and Viradha; strong-armed Sri Rama took on Ravana who thought he was stronger than Rama Himself.

In this shrine the water of the sea during the high tide flows into the fields and streets and with the result it appears as though the conches and pearls take birth in the field and streets.

If you take refuge in the Lord, He will do the impossible for you as He did for Sathyabhama. The azwar exhorts us to reach Sri Rama Vinnaharam which is blessed with natural beauty. He has such ‘saulabhya’ He has given a permanent place to Sridevi in His chest.

The azwar in a remarkable finish to this decad says that If you sing praises of Kaliyan, that is, Thirumangai azwar, it amounts to praising the Lord and they will be liked by everyone.

## 3.5 Vandhunadhadiyen: On Thiruvali

When the azwar wished that he wanted to do service to the Lord, the Lord installed Himself in azwar’s heart. The azwar tells the Lord not to entertain the idea of leaving him and he starts his daily ‘kainkaryam’ to Him.

Demonstrating the meaning of ‘Pranava’ as it were, the azwar says, the Lord lodged Himself in azwar’s heart to enable him to start the daily kainkaryam. An inesteemable jewel shining on a blue hill, as it were, He appears brilliant and He is in my heart. How wonderful! He is not giving any worry of time limit to the azwar, that is, He has been here till now and how long further He will stay - that is, He is staying in my heart durably.

He changed my conduct of looking at women as objects of enjoyment. He turned my mind towards ‘spiritual’ aspects - what a great help He has rendered to me! That very Lord is in Tiruvali. The azwar entreats that the earlier conduct should not reappear and prays that his mind should always be fresh to serve the Lord.

In Thiruvali shrine the brahmins have been doing from times immemorial daily sacrifices. I worship Your holy Feet with eight types of fragrant flowers - figuratively they are Ahimsa, controlling senses, kindness towards all living beings, Kshama, Jnanam, Tapas, Dhyana and Sathya. I will never leave You! O Lord, I have been wanting You day and night for a long time and now that You have entered my heart by Yourself, there is no question of Your vacating my heart.

Just in case the Lord was toying with the idea of leaving the azwar, the latter says that I am still the winner, since the Lord cannot stay elsewhere and He will return to him.

Having won You through reciting of Your various names and falling on Your Feet singing Your praises, I would like You to tell me: ‘This is the way to get Me’ ; ‘I alone am Upaya’. For me to sustain myself, You please say ‘maa suchah’ (meaning ‘grieve not’). This indeed is quite a loaded request to the Lord by the azwar.

## 3.6 Thooviriya: Thiruvali continued

Having experienced within the heart the azwar now desires external union with the Lord (samslesha). He experiences the pangs of separation as a Nayaki and starts sending messengers like birds, bees.

The Nayaki is not in a position to see anything. Even the blossoms appear like fire, but she finds bees entering that fire (blossoms!). She thinks everyone to have her kind of suffering. She calls the bees and praises the ‘stars and lines’ on its wings. Pat came the response! ‘Why are you praising us? When He does not have sympathy for you as such, would He listen to us?’ Finally she tells them to convey her condition. Whether He comes are not is left to Him. ‘How to broach the subject to the Lord’ query the bees. ‘Just like Lakshmana narrating Rama’s condition to Sugriva whose body colour underwent a change, you should describe my condition to Him so that His body colour also changes’. ‘The Lord does not disregard even those who do not desire Him but messengers going on behalf of those who want Him, will it prove to be a mistake? She tells the bees to tell only intelligent words so that He also finds it difficult to live! Come back and tell me His opinion’.

Not remembering His own devotees might prove to be a blemish. Bees utter sweet words: ‘You convey my condition just the same way Anjaneya did to Sita and Rama’ Nayaki tells the bees.

She gets emotional and seems to feel that the Lord is standing in front of her and heavy tears are flowing blinding the Lord. ‘Didn’t You undergo suffering for the sake of ‘ashrithas’ ? ‘Leave that Govardhana and accept half of my pangs. To the Lord, who is wearing Tulasi garland, there does not appear be any obstacles, other wise they will meet similar fate as Kuvalayaapeeda. Didn’t He show Himself to five lakh gopis and snatch their bangles ? Will I allow Him to take my bangles ? You think to own it - my heart as also jewelry belong to You : enough reason to take them. Similarly she feels ownership towards her bangles.

Just the very mention that the Lord is in Thirukudanthai, robbed away her sleep. But the eye inside continues to be awake. Why isn’t He showing Himself to the inside eye? He is subjecting me to suffering.

The Nayaki desires kainkaryam to the Lord (that is the Prapya). There may be some delay in getting it. In previous ‘avataaras’ He has helped ashrithas and now for the same purpose He is in Thirumeyya. ‘If You don’t accept me will the bangles stay in hand?’ - outpouring of her pangs of separation. The above verses spoken by azwar are a result of his seeing the Lord in front as imagined by the Nayaki.

## 3.7 Kalvan Koi: Thiruvali continued

The Nayaki had ‘felt’ that the Lord had come and that she spoke a few words to Him. But having seen her condition the Lord in fact came and took her away without anyone’s knowledge. The mother could not find her on the bed and pines for her.

The Lord has His own ways of taking someone; after all for one who has undergone bharanyasa, He Himself has to remember His devotee. The mother is not sure of the person who came to take her. If the person happens to be the one whom the daughter was awaiting, then it must be a kalvan (thief) and he may be a young person; he must have been attracted by her beauty and innocence. She stood silent when he called; he held her hand and she left even leaving me. Could they have gone to Thiruvali? the mother queries herself.

Looks like the wild Krishna who used to steal butter has become civilized. ‘He just mentioned ‘I will not leave you anymore’ but did not ask her to follow - she herself went after Him. Looks like the old trick of thieves mesmerizing the young lads with a salt (choorna). She might have entered Tiruvaali, a place from which one probably does not return.

The mother worries as to whether some ‘Soorpanaka’ like character might create problem for them. Although the neighbour tries to convince that He is able to face up to such attacks, He being a brave person humbling Shishupala and others and going to enemy camps without any weapons, the mother is not convinced.

‘How is that she left a mother like you and went with Him?’ queries a neighbour. ‘She might have been enamoured by His handsomeness, but it cannot match even the beauty of a single hand of my daughter’ tells the mother.

“I reared my daughter thinking that she would look after me but she did not show even an iota of sympathy towards me, neither did she express ‘alas, I am leaving You !” the mother pines. ‘She must have walked with Him who takes the enemy arrows aimed at his devotees Himself, nullifying the boons given by gods. After ‘Ravanavadha’, Rama returned to Ayodhya to a tumultous welcome and it appears that He with my daughter were received similarly at Tiruvaali’ the mother reckons. The neighbour tells her ‘She has attained what one should and it does not call for any pining’. The mother expresses ‘Yes, she becomes a Jaganmatha who should care for people like me. Her body glowed when she left. The people of the world look up to Her now and I just stay as a mother. It should make me happy to hear that they entered Tiruvaali.’

‘She left everything and relatives behind. She has eyes just suitable to enjoy the scene of the Lord and Sridevi being together. She would enamour the Lord with her style of walking in such a way that He would forget Himself. If that happens how can they arrive at Tiruvaali and who will assist them to reach Tiruvaali’ the mother worries.

‘May be the mother was upset, but she has gone with One whose mind is affected more with love towards her. Those who study this decad will surely reach paramapadha. One need not fear that he leaves (the world) alone; there are ‘Athivahaka ganas’ who are made to lead by the Lord, and ensure one’s smooth journey to reach Him.’

## 3.8 Nandha Vilakkey: On Manimadakoil

The azwar’s heart not having had the ‘Bhagavadanubhava’ - experiencing the Lord - weakened. Is it not for the sake of people seeking here that great benefit, the Lord is brilliantly shining in ‘Manimadakoil’ shrine? The azwar tells his heart to go there and experience Him.

This is the shrine where gods pray to get their wishes fulfilled where the Nanda vilakku (light) is eternal and where the Lord is characterised by self-illuminated knowledge; His omnipresence and divine qualities are inseparable from Him. His kindness saved Gajendra who could offer his worship by placing the flowers on Lord’s feet.

Describing Thirunangoor, the azwar says that it has such high rise buildings as though they are touching the heavens. There are holes made by pigeons, and the buildings shine with ornamental gold and precious stones bonded to the walls.

Saving Gajendra saw Sridevi embracing the Lord just as Sita Devi did when Sri Rama singlehandedly killed fourteen thousand demons. He also killed Mali, Sumali. The Lord who made the ‘rakshasas’ flee in all directions in Lanka is residing in this holy shrine.

The azwar tells his heart to offer worship at Thirumanimada Koil to the Lord who mingled with gopis. He also drank the poison from the breasts of Poothana who impersonating Yashoda had jewelry hanging on her breasts. He also dived into the pond where Kaaliya had forcibly stationed himself. ‘Go to worship the Lord at Thirunangoor. Here there is abundance of vegetation, cattle and water.’

The azwar urges his heart to go to Manimadakoil where ‘Kurutthi’ women, dressed in their best, are selling pearls going from house to house. They don’t mind ‘white paddy’ in place of red variety! Lord Sri Krishna who experienced oneness with gopis is residing in Manimadakoil ; ‘O my heart, go and worship Him there’ tells the azwar.

This is the shrine where the Lord who humbied seven bulls and won Nappinnai’s hand resides. Here the nectar of flowers, ’shengayuneer’, is flowing having been separated from the flowers due to the movement of swans.

Devotees reciting this decad will obtain all the riches and experience the same joy of climbing an elephant! The riches talked about are “bhagavatha kainkarya”, a purushaartha by itself!

## 3.9 Shalankonda: On Vaikunta Vinnagaram

This holy shrine is chosen by the Lord as He felt that the people deserve to be taken care of. Experiencing the Lord both men and animals are dancing here and therefore ‘You my heart, desire that place and reach it’ tells the azwar.

Just not tolerating even the Lord’s name being mentioned, Hiranya wanted to kill those people reciting it He had a very broad and strong chest which was split asunder by the Lord without any effort. He churned the unmanageable ocean and gave nectar to gods. That very Lord is residing with joy in this shrine. Here there are Vedic seers proficient in Vedas / Vedangas. This shrine comparable to Srivaikunta, Vaikunta Vinnaharam, is shining in Tirunangoor.

The Lord who kept the entire Universe in His stomach and who saved Rudra from the predicament of ‘Brahma kapala’ resides here. Shoorpanaka who tried to create a rift between the Lord and Sita, was made to show her real appearance and had to flee to Lanka with her hands, prominent with super-size finger nails, placed on her head.

The one who broke a bow of supreme strength and who killed demons and decimated Lanka for the sake of Sita Devi, will protect His devotees also. That lovable Lord is present in this shrine.

The one who gave poisonous milk to the Lord herself fell flat as a dead body; the demons in the form of Arjuna trees were in smithereens. ‘Shakata’ also met with the same fate. Throwing aloft ‘Vatsasura’ He finished ‘Kapitthasura’. He ate to His heart’s content the butter which had been hidden as it had the hand contact (sparsha) of His devotees. He also humbled the powerful Narakasura using Sudarshana Chakra. That very Lord is in Vaikunta Vinnaharam.

The azwar lauds ‘Ksheerabhdhi shayana’; the gods and sanakas showering flowers on Him pray for His kindness and He has chosen to reside permanently in Nangoor, which is very prosperous-looking. He has worn all the five weapons for the sake of His devolees.

## 3.10 Thirumadandhai: On Arimeya Vinnaharam

In order to facilitate one and all, the Lord, accompanied by Sridevi, who provides us Purushakaara, is residing in Arimeya Vinnagaram. Bhoodevi is also present to ensure that the Lord disregards our blemishes. Even Nithya sooris visit to worship here. Brahmins, who consider experiencing Him alone is the goal, are in great numbers here.

When Indra and others were made to flee by Narakasura, the Lord decimated him so that the ‘devatas’ could live without fear. He also churned the ocean to provide ‘amrutha’ to them. He is ‘amrutha’ personified to azwars. In this shrine, where the Vedic seers experience Him, He resides.

Ensuring that none is put to any suffering He keeps all in His stomach during the great deluge. He as Krishna entertained gopalas when He humbled Kuvalayaapeeda and finished off Kamsa himself. Such a great benefactor resides here which is abundant in fruits and flowers.

The demon Hiranya who was armed with innumerable boons was split by the Lord appearing as Narasimha, as he challenged his son to show the Lord in a particular wooden pillar. This shrine provided resting abode to the Lord.

Without any distinction of devotees or otherwise, the Lord walked into the ‘yaagashaala’ as Vamana when those who saw Him were in joy. Without giving any hint as to who He was, He just asked for three feet of land and just as ‘Dhana Dhare’ started falling He grew into Trivikrama. This shrine is the abode of that benefactor, the Lord, which is very beautiful with groves.

This is also the shrine of Sri Rama who killed Ravana, with all parts of the body strewn all over, as he had kept the beautiful and delicate Sita Devit in prison. As Krishna He decimated the troublemakers Poothana, Dhenuka. The Lord who killed Vatsasura by using him to finish off Kapitthasura, lives here.

The Lord gobbled the life of Poothana as also that of Kamsa. Cauvery brings to this shrine riches in the form of sandal wood trees by uprooting them. In this shrine resides the Lord who won the hand of Napinnai. This shrine is also famous for Bhagavathas getting the feeling of nearness of Brahma (known for tranquility) and Subramanya (known for attractive features).

## Fourth Patthu

## 4.1 Podalarndha: Tirutthevanar Tohai

The Lord, the azwar said in the last decad, had come to Arimeya Vinnaharam with the express purpose of removing obstacles and granting His proximity. In Tirutthevanar Tohai the Lord is present as in Ksheerasamudra and even Brahma and others come here to fulfil their wants.

Resembling Chithrakoota where Sri Rama spent time with Sita Devi, He is present here and the bees are humming music in terms of ‘thethana’. He is the inner soul to even Brahma and Rudra. Devatas come and worship Him here. The Supreme controller that He is, He has *chethanas / achethanas* as His body and Prakara. This shrine is abundant in nectary blossoms and brahmins who are endowed with never-reducing wealth.

This shrine, Tirutthevanar Tohai, has so much wealth that even heavens are humbled. The devatas come here to reenergise themselves to continue their authority. Why has the Lord, who pockets in His stomach the worlds / oceans / mountains / lands etc. chosen this holy shrine ? The answer is that He wants to make us become His own. This aspect known as ‘apathsakathva’ is not adequately talked about in Vedas. This shrine is home to the Lord who sleeps on a small fig leaf. In Narasimha avataara' He split Hiranya asunder without His nails getting ‘bent’. The wealth of brahmins is such that it can be estimated only by recording it all in a book. Even then details of their wealth cannot be covered.

As a Youth the Lord was of help to Sitadevi by breaking the bow which one could not even just move. This He did for one young girl but in Mathura for the sake of several girls He humbled Kuvalayaapeeda.

This shrine is such even the Lord cannot think of another place!

## 4.2 Kambamaa Kadal: Van Purudotthamam

The Lord helps Brahman and others, when they subject themselves to predicaments. The Lord has chosen this shrine Van Purudotthamam.

The Lord built a bridge in the rough sea in order to humble the ego-filled Ravana whose ten heads having crowns representing countries vanquished by him, and the Lord severed them without any effort. He also humbled the thousand-hooded Kalinga and danced on the hoods. That Lord resides here where brahmins not only practise Vedic rituals but also serve as examples for others to follow. This shrine is deserving of Lord’s presence just as gopalakas’ land where He ate offerings meant for Devendra and protected them from torrential rain.

The Lord eliminated Chanoora, Mushika and Kamsa and crowned Himself the ‘Vijayasree’; He is still removing such elements by residing here.

Even Rudra’s self-ego was dealt with by the Lord by severing all the thousand hands of Banasura. If there is any such thing to be dealt with, He will do it from this shrine wherein He has chosen to reside.

As soon as ‘dhanadhare’ of Mahabali fell, He rose in proportions and His one foot touched Brahma’s Sathyaloka. Brahma worshipped the foot with flowers. That Lord has intended to remove our lots here. In this shrine, the women’s beauty is reflected by red lotuses etc.

The Lord split the chest of Hiranya whose frame was such that Brahma and others used to shudder seeing it. He is residing here. He also helped Rudra to come out of Brahma’s curse. His (nabhikamala) navel brought out Brahma who authored this world.

Taking refuge in Lord's feet, the azwar progressed in life and he is the chief manager of Tiruvaali. Those who recite these verses will experience in never-reducing delight (ananda).

## 4.3 Peranindhu: Shemponsei Koyil

He is residing in ‘shempon sheyy koil’ to enable us to worship Him by uttering His names, fall at His holy feet and take refuge. With the express purpose of protecting devotees from the Lord noticing their blemishes, both Sridevi and Bhoodevi are present on two sides of the Lord. The azwar states that he became satisfied after seeing the Lord who is shining like blue clouds. He is not given to birth death and is Ananda (ecstacy) personified. Unlike a chethana, whose jeevathma is constricted by karma and given to cycle of birth and deaths, the Paramaathma does not undergo any change. The one who is having all things as ‘Prakara’ and is lauded by Vedas is present in this shrine, and to sustain oneself one need not go to any other shrine

The azwar lauds Lord’s presence as antharyami. Sky-principle (tatthwa) leads other tatthwas which ultimately combine with the sky during ‘laya’. The Sun and the Moon have their roles; added to them are men, gods, thiryak / stationary (sthavara) beings. The Lord creates all these and enters them. That Supreme Being is present as divine idol in this shrine and I sustained / progressed seeing Him here.

Lord’s various manifestations like Vamana / Ksheerabhdhinatha does not give rise to any doubt as to whether He is Sarveshwara / Shriyahpathi. Just like the Lord partaking in Govinda pattabhishekha appeared, so does He, with His symbol of crown signifying overlordship, for protection of samsaris here.

The obstacles / difficulties faced by His ‘ashrithas’ will vanish since the Lord Himself will appear to ward them off and He is present here with the purpose of winning all the worids. This very Lord destroyed the Lanka with whatever arrow He got! Seeing Him with Sridevi I overcame all ’samsaric’ obstacles.

He is seen to be enjoying mental peace as it were, having warded off enemies like Poothana, Kamsa, Keshi etc He is looking relaxed in this shrine.

Having dealt with Banasura with His discus, He is residing at top of the Tirumala Hills just in case this kind of problem repeats. ‘He alone is our protector’ so think His devotees and He is present in Shempon Sheyy Koil. All the ten verses are azwar’s outpourings after He visited this shrine and not a single verse can be left off. Those who recite these verses will become masters (Ekachakradhipathya).

## 4.4 Maattharashar: Tirutetri Ambalam

Just in case someone might doubt there is a basic difference between Vibhava (Rama, Krishna etc.) avataaras and the arche (divine idol we see), the azwar clarifies that since we came after Vibhava, we are now being protected by His archavatara, that is, being found in various shrines as divine idol.

The Lord who snatched crowns of Kauravas, the mangala soothras of their wives, fetters of Vasudeva, and who came to Gajendra’s rescue resides in Nangoor shrine. The Balagopala who seemed to relish (!) the poisonous milk of Poothana who came resembling exactly like Yashodha! That Balagopala resides in Thirutthatthiyambalatthu shrine.

That Balakrishna also relished the fresh butter before it changed colour and made sure that the mat-doors were turned to hide Him! The Lord won the hand of Nappinnai of right age (youth) by humbling the seven bulls. That place has groves with trees touching the sky and the perfume of flowers is carried by them stopping the Moon unwilling to move from there. The Lord who got service done by me in that shrine is Lord Krishna Himself.

The azwar also remembers ‘Rukmini vivaha’ close on the heels of ‘Napppinnai vivaha’. Both devis, the azwar experiences, are wearing strings / necklaces made of gold / precious stones in three / five strands.

‘Just a Kshathriya’ remarked Ravana about Sri Rama who cut off all his heads and hands. That Lord resides here. In this shrine women, as attractive as parrots, teach the parrots and the azwar says, words of the one who teaches are sweeter.

In various ‘avataras’ the Lord has dealt with really asuric type elements. But he also adroitly wins over those who do not appear as adversaries. Eg. Vamanavatara. Since the Lord Himself was attending the Yaaga (sacrifice), it was quite auspicious! In that place, the paste used by women on their bosoms get washed during bath in the river and the onward flow of water appeared reddish like mud.

The azwar, after Rama, Krishna, Trivikrama avataaras remembers Varaha’s feet of lifting mother Earth from the deep ocean. The Meru mountains just looked like a spec on His foot and Bhoodevi who had lodged in His chest shook a wee bit. ‘The Lord who achieved this feat has made me into His servitor’ says the azwar and He resides in Tirutthatthiyambalatthu shrine. This unique shrine comprises people who have all Vedas at the tip of their tongue. The shrine has luxurious buildings and Pundarikaksha of this shrine is lauded by the azwar.

## 4.5 Thoombudai: Tirumanik-Koodam

Having lauded the ‘archavatara’ of the Lord, The azwar recounts Krishna leelas: Never ever given to any kind of threat, the elephant, Kuvalaayaapeeda was physically humbled by the young Krishna. He also saved the cowherds from Indra’s wrath by holding aloft the Govardhana mount. He drank the poison from Poothana who came making sounds just as Yashodha would.

As Rama, the Lord saved everyone by using an arrow and killing Ravana.

Just as He was learning to walk, He walked in a style which broke the Arjuna trees; the seven bulls were dealt with as though they were mud-toys. Just as it is a journey of learning and shaastric practices for the Vedic seers in Tirumanikkoodam, so also for the Lord it has been a journey humbling the ‘virodhis’ of ‘aashrithas’, and He spents His time thus.

Priding over the fact that she was Ravana’s sister, Shoorpanaka met with the right end by getting the ears and nose cut off in His presence. Sridevi and Bhoodevi are eternally living with the Lord in this shrine and they see to it that the devotees’ blemishes / sins are not noticed by the Lord.

The one who took Rama-Krishnavataras is residing in this shrine and here the Brahmins themselves are as strong as the kings. It appears that these ‘bhagavathotthamas’ are doing what the Lord Himself would be doing.

He is the very embodiment of all that is there for chethana’s enjoyment, water, light, heat etc. and even the stars which produce good and bad effects. They all constitute His body. The Lord residing in this shrine has everything as His body. He is ‘sarva-niyantha’ (organising every thing) as per the karmas of (chethanas) sentient beings. He is the controller of all qualities of the soul, those which lead to liberation and those which obstruct it. He is the inner soul of all ‘devatas’. He is present in this shrine for all gods and men to come and worship.

Those who understand and recite these verses on Tirunangoor’s Thirumanikkodum shrine, composed by Kalikanri Thirumangai Azwar, will rule this world and finally breaking the ‘Adithya mandala’ reach the Archiradhi maarga to attain Paramapada.

## 4.6 Thaavalandhu: Thirukkavalambadi

‘Didn’t Gajendra call You for protection? I am in the same position as Gajendra. Who else is there to protect me?’ the azwar queries the Lord. ‘The elephant didn’t know that there was a crocodile but seeing big flowers it went into the pond. It had no alternative but recite Your names, when You saved it. You do not neglect those who love You. You alone become my protector’ the azwar tells. The Lord is in Tirunangoor’s Thirukkavalambadi shrine.

For saving Gajendra the Lord appeared with His weapons on Garuda’s back. In Vamanavatara He disguised Himself. Since Bali had ‘generosity’ as the main trait, He did not kill him although he killed devatas and therefore had committed similar crime as Ravana whom the Lord killed.

Unlike Indra and devatas, the people of this shrine risk themselves for Lord’s sake. ‘Since You are one of them become my protector’ the azwar tells the Lord.

Sugreeva who doubted Sri Rama’s power and bravery was blessed by the Lord after He killed Vali and made him the king. Similarly after fierce battle, Ravana was killed and Vibheeshana was crowned ‘Lankadhipatni’.

While He decimates enemies, there are instances of His granting sharanagathi to some adversaries: He danced in such style on Kalinga’s hood that Bharatha’s dance shaasthra may include some styles! ‘When You, Sarveshwara, is present here, why should I seek someone eise’ queries the azwar.

The mother Earth tolerates crimes committed by demonic elements and it is the Lord who can reduce the load on earth by killing them - Chanoora, Mushika, Kamsa, Duryodhana to name a few. That Lord resides here, He being ‘sarva gandah’, the ‘divya desha’ is wafting fragrance.

The azwar takes refuge in the Lord who killed the wrestlers and humbled Kuvalayaapeeda. ‘The eldest must rule the land and the crown should not be on the head of the undeserving’ so thinking the Lord decimated Kauravas when His role as messenger was not fruitful.

While He reduces the Lord of demons who commit crimes, He also deals with the ego of devathas. When Sashi thought ‘Kalpavriksha’ is meant for ‘devatas’ and not for human beings, the Lord had to encounter Devendra who did not give due regard to Lord’s help to him. The Lord defeated him and gave ‘Parijatha tree’ to Sathyabhama. ‘You reside in Kavalampadi shrine and You are my sole refuge’ the azwar tells the Lord.

The azwar emphasizes the Supreme control that the Lord has over everything and He has all sentient / insentient beings as His body and He is present as the inner soul of all and at all times. His readiness to help has reached its zenith as He appears in this shrine and His presence is indicated by fragrant groves and the dancing peacocks. ‘Extremely enjoyable Lord, please protect me’ asks the azwar. The Lord, who is present in Nangoor’s Thirukkavalampadi, is Lord Sri Krishna Himself and those who recite this will live with all respect and regard.

## 4.7 Kannar Kadal: Tiruvellakulam

The azwar notices despite his taking refuge in the Lord at Tirukkavalambadi that atmosphere around him was one of material aspects / samsaric ties. The azwar queries the Lord ‘Despite Your capability, why am I in this situation’, the Lord asks ‘What do you think is suitable to you?’ the azwar responds: ‘You should remove all obstacles on the path to attain You’.

The more the azwar gazes at Lord’s divine idol the more fascinating He appears. ‘I don’t understand You despite my telling You that You only are my protector, You appear unperturbed ; for providing protection You are wearing a special garland, and the Tulasi is indeed growing due to contact with You. You should eliminate my sad state’, the azwar tells the Lord.

Didn’t you overcome gopalakas’ difficulties by holding Govardhana mount? You should Yourself understand the difference between their predicament and my sad state. Nothing is impossible for You. You can make a stone float on water. There is nothing which holds You back as an obstacle. You built a bridge for every one’s ‘uddhara’. “You should eliminate nescience / karmic ties / ‘apacharas’ done by me with You / baghavathas and sins from beginningless time; and bless me” tells azwar to the Lord.

‘The Lord of creation / dissolution that You are, You still moved like a cowherd true to Your profession. Vedic seers live in Nangoor’s Tiruvellakulam’.

‘The mother clutches the body of the baby although it does not know that it is its mother. Similarly even though ‘samsaris’ have not understood You, to them You are the friendly Narayana. Leaving aside Paramapada You’re in our midst’ tells the azwar to the Lord. The azwar does prayer to the Lord keeping Sridevi in front and entreats that the Lord should show kindness feeling that ‘alas, he is in travails of samsara’.

The azwar exhibits his ability for poetry just as he has strong hands for brave acts!

## 4.8 Kavalayaanai: On Parthan Palli

The azwar returns to ‘Nayaki’ state which represents his condition and is described by her mother. ‘My daughter with beautiful lips keeps telling about the Lord having body resembling dark clouds, and Kuvalayapeeda which was killed by Him. There can be no comparison for His body glow. She has gone to Tirunangoor’s Parthan Palli and prays to Lakshmipathy.

Kuvalayaapeeda’s nature is such that it falls on anyone without provocation. The Lord even before attaining youth humbled it. The way He drank Poothana’s milk is amazing. The people in this shrine are truthful and the Lord should have declared that He will not leave her. He is the Supreme controller of the creation who, as in the case of gopis, wins over and experiences eternally. Such an one is not fully grasped by Vedas. Imagining the place where her master may be present, she is singing praises of Parthan Palli.

In praising the Lord she has crossed the limit in terms of coyness, firmness and respects of being a woman and He is indeed a supreme lover! She is unabashedly mentioning the place where He is residing: that Lord destroyed Lanka which was protected by seas all round and His bow sends shivers in enemies’ frames.

He saves us during the great deluge. He is resembling the ocean in colour and is a great benefactor. The place where He is residing, Parthan Palli, is filled with ecstacy and instead of he wanting or seeking her having been enamoured by her, she herself goes after Him. ‘He did seek her and stole her heart “I became a slave”; even Vedas cannot reach Him’ - my daughter sings of Parthan Palli.

The Nayaki responds to questions like as to whether people will not criticise her for her open desire to meet Him, that the entire world wants Him and He is love personified for His devotees: how do you know that He is desired by everyone and what the reason is for everyone to take His refuge - she responds by saying that He resides in every devotee’s mind and is present now in Tirunangoor. She sings about the place so that everyone could hear.

Those who recite these ten verses on Parthan palli will reside without break in Paramapadam.

## 4.9 Nummaithozudhom: On Thiru Indaloor

In order to get away from ‘Prakrithi sambhandha’ (samsara), the azwar realises that He is present in various shrines with the same purpose. Despite daily kainkaryam he did not see that it was materialising. In Nayaki stance, having had a fight with the Nayaka, the azwar expresses his anguish.

We did worship You but just to forget our separation we did not resort to playing dice. Having acquired knowledge, we are not whiling away our time like others. ‘Our athma is special to You’ so saying he entreats that the Lord should get service done by us in keeping with our subservience to Him. ‘Is it any big thing for You, who has instilled in us the knowledge of our relative relationship’ asks the azwar. ‘If You happen to neglect one, it will result in a blemish to Your known Kalyanagunas’. Our longing for You is simply growing. Are You thinking that we might go to someone else to fulfil our want. You are not showing Your divine form which we want to experience’ the azwar tells the Lord. In case he were to ask, ‘have I not revealed the form in terms of the Universe despite being present in the shrine here’, the azwar’s reply is ready: ‘You should grant Your appearance to the full satisfaction of our eyes and speak a few words to us’.

You are the inner soul for everything. When we get to develop interest in Prapya (Supreme Being / Brahman), and wish to experience You, to facilitate us You are in Indaloor, and You are the extraordinary Master.

No doubt we cannot bind the Master, neither can we be subject to inaction. Looks like You consider me as Sita Devi who could wait for You a long period. We cannot miss Your sight even for a moment. Our being subservient to You is something which can be erased. You have not ensured our kainkarya as per our ‘swaroopa’. You are like water for us in a desert. We are caught in ‘samsara’. If you can open Your mouth and speak a few words, we can survive’.

We have been told by shaastras that starting with Kritha Yuga, Your colour has changed from white / golden / blue and that of dark cloud. Why don’t You show us which is Your real colour.

If the Lord were to query, is it not adequate that I am enabling you to do service to My archavataara, the azwar would reply, ‘You have taken various forms for others but not for Your servitors. Why at all You lodged Yourself in my heart? Is it to cause my desire to mount? We are doing service to You for seven generations. You are in this shrine for the purpose of giving ‘darshan’ to us just the same way You do to Nithyasooris’. He is in Indaloor which has a beautiful environment.

## 4.10 Aaychhiyar: On Tiruvelliangudi

While the azwar appeared disappointed that he did not get the darshan of the Lord in the last decad, the Lord told him that after having assured Rama / Krishna manifestations He is present in Tiruvelliangudi where he can go and see Him as he wants. The azwar appeared convinced and prays to Him.

He stole butter in such a way He left the house for the gopi to expect / invite a follow-up visit by Him! As a little lad again, He pocketed the entire Universe in His stomach and slept on a fig leaf. He won the three worlds with His three steps and such a Lord is present in this shrine which glorifies His presence there.

Forgetting that He is the Supreme Being, He looked after cattle and although just through (sankalpa) His thinking He could kill Ravana, He still built a bridge and His body glow registered a change looking like the dark cloud. The Lord, who took avataaras before, is residing in Tiruvelliangudi.

Kaliya who was wreaking poison to far distance and the birds were falling dead, was humbled by Sri Krishna who jumped on its head and danced. He has Garuda as His flag.

The Lord who does feats in the interest of His devotees, like keeping intact everything in His stomach during deluge, becoming a charioteer etc. is having ‘eyes’ that reflect His love for the devotees. The fish here in paddy fields look for safer places just the same way as bhagavathas would attain Viraja river later. They, casting aside the fear of samsara, live happily with the Lord.

This shrine has ‘Kolavillirama’ as the deity. Rama’s arrows tore asunder the columns of ‘demon-army’ and no one could locate the soldiers just as a flower caught in a storm vanishes. The stance of holding the bow by Rama, put doubts in Ravana’s mind as to how to he should hold his bow.

In went the Lord to the Yaagashala reciting Vedas, in response to Bali’s declaration to give whatever one wanted. Despite Sukhracharya’s opposition, the Lord was determined to take the ‘three steps’, and the moment the ‘dhanadhare’ fell He became Trivikrama. That Lord is present here. Since Shukra (Velliyar) worshipped the Lord here, the place acquired this name.

The Lord who should give place in His lap to His consorts, placed Hiranya there and split Him with His finger nails. He lives here. Huge groups of gods come and worship Him here. It is a beautiful shrine with paddy fields, Lotus flowers in blossom, sugarcane and swans in pairs.

The very Lord had lifted the mother Earth and to relax from that feat. He is in Ksheerabhdhi. ‘Vel Kaliyan’ indicates the staff the azwar is holding and is said to be a symbol of his appreciation of Vaishnavas. Those who practise singing this decad, will become masters.

## Fifth Patthu

## 5.1 Arivadhariyaan: Pullam Bhoodhangudi

After all the Lord is present in so many shrines so that someone or other, now or later will get to see the Lord and experience Him.

No one can get to know Him with his own efforts, but He will grant His darshan. The one who walked with a beautiful style to Mahabali’s ‘Yajna shaale’ is present here permanently. He became an ordinary person for the sake of devathas. He the Lord of everything in this world, took a dhana from one who gave it thinking that it belonged to him. He warded off Gajendra’s predicament; He eliminated Ravana who had abducted Sitadevi. As a child, the Lord had decimated demons who came disguised as a tree, a cart etc. by using His hands or feet. That very Lord resides in this shrine, which is beautiful with paddy fields.

Sending torrential rain achieved nothing for Indra. The Lord sent Ravana back grounding his bow so that he may change his mind (and return Sitadevi). He liked to eat butter which had been touched by His ‘ashrithas’. The Lord who did all these, and also humbled seven bulls for winning the hand of Nappinnai, resides here.

To ward off hail-stone rain, He held a bigger stone as umbrella and protected the cows and gopalakas. When Rudra, unable to face the Lord, took to his heels, He did not kill Banasura as his protector had run away. He just cut his hands. He bacame a ‘sarathi’ to Arjuna; He saved Rudra from Brahmakapala by breaking it and pardoned him for his sins. That very Lord resides in this shrine.

When there was an envelope of thick darkness not allowing anything to be seen, and it had spread all over, the Lord appeared as a swan and taught wisdom to Brahma and cleared the darkness - He illuminated the world with Vedas. As swan he could teach anyone including those who may insist on acharyas to learn. The Lord expects nothing from such great help He renders. Similarly in this shrine expensive articles are thrown by Cauvery on the shore.

As a small child the Lord grazed the cattle, drove away Kaaliya, and He has chosen to reside in Pullam Poothangudi on which Thirumangai azwar has written these verses ; those who recite these ensure that sins will not bother them.

## 5.2 Tham Thum: On Koodaloor

The Lord, who has displayed His being the Master by entering my heart, is residing in Tirukkoodaloor. He will look after the interests of the ‘aashrithas’.

Despite being the all-knowing He does not appear to know His greatness (perumai), while His devotees know it - He has come down to the earth ; disregarding His demotion He goes as a messenger to Kauravas. He rersides in Koodaloor where the hair cosmetics used by women spread all over and bees sing ‘Kurinji’ song.

To win the hand of extremely beautiful Nappinnai He humbled the seven bulls. Since as Lord of everything He cannot eat curds and butter, but He as a small boy, ate them up as they had the touch of His ‘ashrithas’. Just as He is in our Brahmapuri (hearts), Koodaloor becomes another Brahmapuri.

Pretending as though having slept when Yashodha used to hide the curds and butter, He would eat and make merry later. Everyone was struck with the appearance of Vamana; he, as my Kulanatha, won me over, and He is residing in this shrine. The bees, which were drinking nectar in groves, drink the honey in flowers worn by women who were tilling the fields.

The ashrithas lauded the Lord who stretched Himself as much as He could. The very Lord saved Rudra from Brahma Kapala sticking to him by breaking it. That very Lord resides here where mango fruits fall from the tree and covered by earth / soil appear hidden.

The world presenting itself as sharp mountains, heavy oceans are held in His stomach by the Young Lord in such a way that oceans do not shake, mountains do not move; and He keeps everything as such. He made me His servant at Thiruneermalai where male and female deer are together in ‘samslesha’. This shrine is also similar to it. Here bunches of coconuts being full are slanting and touch the water.

As everyday passes my heart melts and desire mounts for the Lord who has entered my heart. The ocean-coloured Lord resides in Tirukoodaloor. Singing this decad wards off one’s sins.

## 5.3 Venri mamuzhu: On Thiruvellarai

Having gratefully acknowledged Lord’s help in entering him, the azwar craves for kainkaryam to His holy feet. As it did not materialise immediately he tells the Lord to remove all obstacles to enable the service (kainkaryam).

Is it anything new to You? You have done it twenty one times as Parasurama repeatedly. I want to worship Your feet, with jewels decorated and giving the feeling of sound! The people of this shrine, Thiruvellarai, need not go out for any necessity as everything is available here.

‘Didn’t You, gift to Brahma, the Vedas which talk about kainkaryam. As Hayagreeva didn’t you teach Vedas, Ithihasas, Puranas to Brahma. Not only didn’t You assume Hayagreeva avataara, but for the sake of a small boy You appeared as Narasimha to split Hiranyakashipu’s chest into two using Your nails - Hiranya had the boon that Sudarshana discus will not work on him. Similarly You remove all obstacles in the way of my service to You.

You also became a sarathy to Pandavas and showed Your preference to them. With the sole purpose of granting the wishes of all you are standing in Venkatachala. Even without getting any request from Bhoodevi who had drowned in deep ocean You came to Her rescue as Varaha. Such being the case is it anything big for You to save me.

When the ‘devatas’ wearing lofty crowns fell on Your Feet. You as Koorma, Churned the ocean with Mandara mountain resting on Your back and distributed the ‘amrutha’. And again when the ‘devatas’ were troubled by Ravana You, as Rama, cut off his ten heads in such a way they all fell apart.

The Lord who as Hayagreeva taught Vedas, did a similar job as a swan when deep darkness enveloped the world. He is to be found amidst sweet music made by bumble-bees in Tiruvellarai shrine.

The Lord did not mind seeking a ‘dhana’ from Mahabali as his prime quality was generosity, and did not eliminate him unlike the end He meted out to Ravana and others. He resides in Tiruvellarai where the clouds brush past the high-rise buildings. Even if the Lord were not to be present it is difficult to forget Him; such is His enjoyable personality. Those who recite these ten ‘pasuras’ not missing even one will be welcomed much more than the Vaikuntanatha Himself!

## 5.4 Undhimel: On Tiru-Arangam (1)

‘How should one worship Your holy Feet?’ The azwar queries the Lord. ‘For making the matter simple have I not come to Srirangam to lie supine so that you can worship my feet’ replies the Lord, and He reveals Himself.

How great it is that the Lord who is able to enlist worship from the one whom He Himself created through Brahma lying in him as innersoul? During deluge it is He who swallows the world. He is the controller of Nithyasooris who come to worship Him in this shrine which is surrounded by Cauvery and has beautiful fields.

Not only does He swallow the Universe during deluge, He also sleeps on a fig leaf. He wears the Supreme Person’s insignia, a resplendent crown. He also sleeps on Adisesha whose hoods open due to contact with the Lord. The azwar also remembers the help of making him His own.

As soon as ‘Dhanadhare’ fell, the Lord grew into Trivikrama. It fell on a hand which is normally bedecked with Sudarshana. ‘This is the land of Vamana’ says the azwar. He employed His bow as Rama to decimate Lanka into a burial ground. Rama, known as, Chakravarthy Thirumagan used to worship Ranganatha who is the presiding deity of ‘Peria Koil’, ‘Rama lives here’ says the azwar. This ‘kshethra’ enables large groups of people, who were created by Brahma, to take refuge.

Poothana who impersonated / resembled Yashoda had two extra qualities: cruelty and strength ! This is a place where enemies are eliminated. Similarly it is the land where the Lord can relax with His devotees.

The callous Kamsa and the more cruel wrestlers, and Shakata in disguise, all met with their fate at the young hands of Krishna. The same Lord manifested Himself as Varaha to save the world and also as Matsya. He brought out Vedas from deep ocean, and as Koorma to prevent Mandhara mountains from drowning.

‘Sarveshwara’ that He is, He is at a distance, but He is present in the smallest of small things and He has not been comprehended by even the most knowledgeable scholars. The shrine, Srirangam, where the Lord is lying supine is protected by covered corridors. Sridevi, who used to live in a flower, would fear going back to it leaving the Lord’s chest. Such is Lord’s chest. Those who recite this decad and experience its message will attain Paramapadha after leaving their bodies.

## 5.5 Veruvadhal: On Tiru-Arangam (2)

Despite getting fully submerged in the Lord’s enjoyable greatness, the azwar did not obtain the befitting kainkarya. In the foregoing he had assumed ‘Nayaki bhava’ to express his pangs of separation. Since the Nayaki could not relate directly to Him, the azwar assumes Nayaki’s mother’s stance.

The daughter is mumbling during sleep: ‘vengadame, vengadame’. The question as to why Thirumalai crops up at Tiruvarangam? One can easily think of Amalanadhipiran, where Tiruppam azwar experienced Paramapada and Tirumalai as Tiruvarangam. Tiruppan azwar experiences Kakuthsthan (Rama), and immediately Tirumalai; experiencing Narasimha, he is reminded of Krishna. Similarly Tirumangai azwar declares that all avataaras are of Ranganatha.

The mother says that she cannot, keeping her on the lap, pacify her. She has also lost the sense of sleep. Because of emaciated waist and hands, it is difficult to hold the clothes and bangles. Can I try to persuade Him to accept my daughter as a maid servant?

The Lord can sustain love at all costs. He can instil faith in those who do not have it. ‘My daughter has such bewitching eyes that she can enamour anyone with a single look. She is reminded of Lord’s Tulasi garland and keeps describing it. He is familiar about forests and butter and milk. How can He understand young women? If kings wished for a son to rule the kingdom, Nandagopan probably wished for a son to eat curds and butter!

Neither she listens to me nor to her friends. She has neglected looking after her body. “Where is Srirangam where my Master who deserves my service resides?” the daughter queries. I cannot just figure out as to what He did to her - she has lost the sense of right and wrong.

‘She is not bothered about applying sandal paste to her beautiful bosom, neither corrillium to her eyes. As the Lord is addressed as Shriyahpathi, will it matter to Him as regards being husband to Sridevi, if He shows consideration to my daughter?

My daughter cannot utter anything other than Tiruvarangam. He has already one inseparable from Him. The one who Himself suffers for the sake of ashrithas, I cannot imagine, how He is troubling my daughter. Her disinterestedness towards herself, others, and love for ‘Bhagavadvishaya’ is not open for change. The swamy who became a goal to Bheeshma for lying on arrows, has put my daughter on arrows of ‘kaama’.

It appears she got into darkness caused by her love and that darkness seems to be pushing her towards Him. To all those who criticise her, she tells “amazing sports, that is Madhava”. He has names which when uttered can overcome samsara. But those names are creating sorrow in her.

She has not touched the ball, not fed her parrot: does not look at her dolls: (Is there a similar example? The answer is Vibheeshana who, once he knew what was to be attained, the Supreme Being, he gave up everything). If ‘Sarvajna’ behaves the way an unwise person behaves, where can I go? Could He neglect this way? the mother, that is our azwar, queries.

The Parakala Nayaki who erased her ‘sthreethva’ by deeply thinking, forgetting herself, about the Lord who is in Srirangam is described by her in her mother’s words. It is emphasized that this event of ‘aathmanubhava’ should be learnt through an acharya - such devotees will be garlanded by the Lord Himself and experience Him eternally at Sri Vaikunta.

## 5.6 Kaimmanam: On Tiruvarangam (3)

The Lord tells the azwar that after all He is residing in holy shrines to facilitate devotees like him to have His proximity, and specially so in Srirangam. He filled azwar’s heart and His experiencing the azwar thus made the latter inturn ecstatically feel Lord’s presence; he reaches the conclusion that he should experience - Srirangam.

His lying supine is extraordinarily beautiful; after all He had slept in Ksheerabhdhi - being present with consorts, he saw to it that I fully deserved the experience. I had only heard about His sleeping in Ksheerabhdhi but to-day I have seen it myself in Srirangam - He saved me by stopping the tears due to separation from Him.

The Lord of Tirupper / Tirukkurungudi / Tiruttankal / Karambanur is ‘utthaman’ because He is available to everyone - He shows no sign of the feat of keeping the seven seas / mountains I seven worlds in His stomach and having protected them.

The Lord who lifted the mother Earth, measured the worlds, and won everyone’s heart as Gopala, I saw Him in ‘Srirangam’ says the azwar.

He slept in Ksheerabhdhi which is at the base of all avataras, and not tolerating the travails gone through by Prahladha, He appeared as Narasimha to kill Hiranyakashipu - ‘I have seen Him in Southern Arangam amidst cool waters’ says azwar.

The Lord who has water, fire and earth as His body (as also ether and air) in the yore burnt Lanka with arrows spitting fire in a trice. The Lord who is the goal for sacrificial fires, and who grants paramapadham, I have seen Him in Southern Arangam, says the azwar.

He does not show in any way that He has helped His devotees - He helped Pandavas since they did not display any enimity towards anyone. Not tolerating Kamsa’s crimes He killed him. During deluge He keeps those who have taken refuge in Him and also others who have not in His stomach.

Talking about the route to attain Him (Hita), the azwar mentions the role of Sridevi as one who recommends to get us (Prapya) the Lord. Reminding himself about the connotations in ‘divya manthra’ in terms of ‘Prapyathva’ and Prakpakatha (Being goal and route), he is being thankful to the Lord.

Those who do not approach Him as per tenets of Vedas, He does not show His grace as in the case of Bouddhas, Sramanas. The Lord of my father / mother, nithya sooris (who bear no distinction to Him), I have seen Him in Southern Arangam surrounded by cool waters.

The Lord who removes my falsities, controls my senses and resides in my heart; those who pray with full devotion and knowledge of athma, He shows His full swaroopa.

The Lord who reared the helpless cows is now residing in Southern Arangam. If you have uttered these verses even once, the tongue will not leave them. Those who have mastered these verses sung by the Kalikanri will be saved from karmas.

## 5.7 Pandai: On Tiruvarangam (4)

The Lord, the supreme being, blesses His devotees taking various manifestations and those who miss out during these ‘avataaras’ have been given an alternative by the Lord, that is, His eternal place ; Srirangam, where they can get blessed by Him.

The eternal Vedas, which are the supreme tenets, the sacrifices, the Prasnas, the Vyakaranas, their meanings, the cause of all the foregoing, the fire, waters, the earth, the clouds, the wind, seven seas, seven mountain ranges, the sky, universe as a whole, all form Lord’s Prakaara (His body). He is everything and is lying supine in Srirangam; He is showing His divine appearance there.

His divine qualities are so innumerable that even devatas cannot count. The panacea that He is, we get all our material gains and finally ‘moksha’. All creation and dissolution are done by Him. He is in Srirangam.

By teaching Brahma and others, the Vedas, He removed their sorrow. But devatas like Brahma lost them. As a swan, He cleared the enveloping darkness and brought back the Vedas. That is He brought again to light Pramana (the Vedas) and to-day He, the Prameya, is glittering in Srirangam.

Hugely oblong Mandara mountain as stirrer with Vasuki used as a rope around it, He churned the rough seas with multiple arms producing huge waves which almost covered the sky and seeing this the worlds above, the Moon, the devatas, the celestials watched with wonder. He gave the ‘amrutha’ to devas and that kindness personified Lord is residing in Srirangam.

The outcome of the Lord killing Hiranyakashipu when the blood flowed like a river, is such only His ‘aashrithas’ would think that He has done this job for our sake. That Lord is residing in Srirangam.

Using His special axe, Parashurama granted ‘veeraswarga’ to Kaartivirarjuna. The Lord lauded by devatas in thousand names went to Milky ocean to lie on the serpent bed. He is residing in Srirangam.

The Lord, after getting separated from Sita, determined to kill Ravana and decimate Lanka. Samudraraja came running to surrender to the Lord as He just bent His bow - such was the effect of His handling His bow to clear the way for building a bridge. Thus, the Lord displayed His bravery; He is in Srirangam.

The Supreme controller that He is, He is the inner soul of the sun. He receives the offerings made at yajnas. He wielded the discus to save Gajendra. The same discus which has the brilliance of thousand suns once covered the sun to produce darkness during daytime. That Lord is giving His divine ‘darshan’ at Srirangam.

As though He could not distinguish between His mother’s milk and Poothana’s breast milk, He drank the poisonous milk! As a child once He swallowed the earth and lay on a fig leaf during deluge and brought it out subsequently. Worshipped by devatas wearing crowns, He dispels my karmas by entering my lowly heart. The one who lifted the Govardhan mountain to save the cows now resides in Srirangam which is surrounded by Cauvery waters.

## 5.8 Yezhai Yedhalan: On Tiruvarangam (5)

While Thondaradipodi Azwar’s Thirumalai talks about both the Supreme Being (Praapya) and (Prapaka), and Tiruppan Azwars ‘Amalanadhipiran’ the prapya only, Peria Thirumozhi centres around Prapaka. Owing to Lord’s grace, the azwar comprehended the realities like Brahman, Jeevaathma and the nature of Prakrithi which is an obstacle to attaining the Lord. The Lord Himself is Praapya and Prapaka. The Lord’s various forms viz. Para, Vyooha, Vibhava and Harda are not as suitable as ‘Archa’ which is seen in many ‘divya deshas’. The azwar, indicating his urgency to attain Prapya has surrendered to the Lord at Srirangam.

The azwar is reminded of the kindness showered by Rama on Guha. Although inferior in varna, the Lord appreciated Guha’s help, and the azwar seeks similar grace from the Lord. Why alone talk of His grace to a human being? His grace on a Tiryak viz. Hanuman resulted in Hanuman doing kainkaryam to the Lord, although he had prided once that he was ‘Vayu Puthra’. The azwar seeks similar grace from the Lord.

During drought conditions when there was hardly any flower available, Gajendra came across a beautiful pond which had flower plants and the fragrance was inviting. Gajendra without a second thought stepped into the pond. Not far was a Crocodile who had long been waiting for an elephant’s leg. Gajendra’s strength decreased and Crocodile’s strength increased. The fight went for long. Gajendra thought he himself will fight it out, but when it became unmanageable he uttered ‘Narayana 0 Manivanna’. The Lord felt the pain Himself meted out to Gajendra’s leg. The moment Gajendra’s ego of saving himself vanished, the Lord was on the scene saving him.

Warding off the fear coming from Garuda and saving Sumukha (serpent), goes to show that there is none who is ineligible for sharanagathi. After all the Lord is present in Srirangam to eliminate ‘aashrithas’ sufferings / opponents and show His grace.

Once, a brahmin, Govindaswamy after reading Puranas, wanted to witness Rasakreeda and other leelas of Krishna. He did a rigorous penance and the Lord granted his request. ‘What next, do you want anything’, the Lord asks. ‘I want to be with You and experience You’ replies the brahmin. Finding that the brahmin was not fully rid of pleasure, the Lord tells him to enjoy the pleasures here and then come. But if the Lord had wished He could have removed the brahmin’s worldly desires and taken him. ‘You bless me in such a way that I do not ever suffer separation from Your holy Feet’. To substantiate this aspect the azwar tells the Lord about Markandeya the son ‘Mrukandu muni’, who took refuge in the Lord and eliminating samsara, he attained the Lord’s feet - Yama who had to claim him was told by the Lord not to go anywhere near those who have Vaishnava traits.

Didn’t You retrieve Your guru Sandeepini’s son from the ocean? I have been drowned in the sea of samsaara - You are in Srirangam to accept my kainkaryam by lifting me up from this sea.

A brahmin whose wife used to lose every child that was born to her, approached Lord Sri Krishna who was busy in a ‘Yaaga’. Therefore Arjuna volunteered to help and built an arrow-fort around the would-be mother. The child which was born was nowhere. The Lord had to intervene by bringing back the four children from Paramapadha.

Tundaman Chakravarthy was well known for his smooth and effective administration. He earned grace from the Lord through His revealing the greatness of Tirumanthra to him. It resulted in Tondaman getting busy in meaningful pursuits. ‘You ensured that Tondaman did not waste anytime. I have come to Your Lotus Feet, O Lord, residing in Srirangam which is surrounded by beautiful groves.

‘‘Those who are enthusiastic about ‘Bhagavad Vishayam’ you sing with joy these ten songs. Misery arising out of karmas will vanish once and for all.

## 5.9 Kaiyilangu: On Tirupper

Just as we saw in the last decad that Govindaswamy was asked to return to the Lord later, the azwar was also asked to experience Him in other shrines before coming to Him. The azwar saw the Lord in Tirupper, accordingly, and forgot himself while experiencing Him.

The Lord grants His kainkaryam to all those who do not expect any gains from it. Ten-Tirupper, where the Lord reclines on the hooded serpent and by reciting His names I realised Him.

The Lord who during the great deluge keeps the entire Universe in His stomach and brings it out after the deluge stops, is none other than the One in Tirupper. He loves us and we cannot leave Him. The Supreme Controller that He is brought out Brahma from His navel, and eliminated the curse from which Rudra was suffering. ‘I realised Him by uttering His various names at Tirupper which has equal greatness as Srivaikunta itself.

The Lord’s names guard you from destruction. Rudra appealed to the Lord to erase the curse which was creating a downfall, and the Lord eliminated the curse which was in the form of ‘Brahmakapala’. He also saved the devatas from the clutches of Dantavakra who used to beat them blue. As Narasimha He danced with extreme joy after killing Hiranyakashipu with His nails. Taking refuge in that Omnipotent Lord I attained Him.

For the sake of Sitadevi, He built a bridge using material which used to sink; he chopped off Ravanas hands which were so big as to occupy Lanka, and Ravana himself used to think that his Lanka would survive brahmaastra and that he was the controller for it. ‘I attained the Lord in Tirupper where orthodox brahmins keep reciting Vedas.

As butter was getting wasted Yashodha hankered for a son; she got one, the Lord Sri Krishna. The child, the mother feared may overeat, and to prevent it she used to hide it. Finding that the butter was missing from the safe place, she tied Him with a rope having many knots. The Lord submitted Himself to this and accepted His mistake. He humbled the seven bulls to win Nappinnai’s hand. This very Lord is present in Tirupper. ‘Uttering His various names I attain Him’. ‘The Lord who lives in the hearts of those who do penance, becoming proficient in all ‘vidyas’ and are superior to Brahma and other devatas, that very Lord lives in my heart’ says the azwar.

## 5.10 Theedaru: On Nandipura Vinnaharam

Continuing his worship in holy shrines, the azwar arrives at Nandipura Vinnaharam and rather than allowing the mind to wander in other aspects, he would progress towards the Lord.

The Lord is the controller of dream, sushupthi and death. He is the inner soul of Earth, water, fire, wind and ether. He takes avataaras such as Krishna wherein He stole butter and got tied to a mortar by Yashodha showing Himself to be a simple child! As a child He kept seven worlds in His stomach and derived ‘anandha’. He lives in Nandipura Vinnaharam which is beautiful with paddy fields.

The azwar recounts Lord’s bravery; He decimated like a fistful nutshell hordes of advancing asuras with protruding teeth and huge bodies; others came wreaking fire and He cut them into pieces. He is residing in this shrine.

While walking in Dandakaranya Sri Rama did not feel the blazing heat of the sun as Sitadevi was present with Him!

The Lord who was born in utter darkness of a prison cell removed the agony of His father, Vasudeva, by growing in Nandagopan’s house where He grew as though He was dependent on His new parents.

On a deep inquiry it would be apparent that Sriman Narayana alone is worthy of meditation. And in the world there is no shrine comparable to this shrine where devatas come and worship the Lord.

If the oceans themselves which are supposed to protect the earth rise, those who have understood Lord’s charama sloka (maa shuchah) will not become subject to karma leading to the deluge. ‘O my mind, understand this truth and reach this shrine - if Lord helped Nithyas as Paravasudeva, Brahma, Rudra in Vyooha, Vasudeva and Dasharatha in Vibhava the Lord here is meant to help us! In Nandipura Vinnaharam the clouds brush past the groves.

## Sixth Patthu

## 6.1 Vandunu Narumalar: On Tiruvinnagar (1)

The Lord wanted to see if the azwar slides back to the material world when asked to stay here visiting divine shrines. The azwar wants to reassure the Lord his repulsion towards samsara.

The Lord looks forward to devotees’ prayer to erase the cycle of samsara and sins from beginningless time. ‘To get You, You Yourself have to show Your grace. Do not postpone thinking that he is still caught up in samsaric aspects’, so tells the azwar to the Lord. The azwar wants to make sure that his interest in ‘Bhagavadvishaya’ is firm and stable; he repeats to say that he has no more interest in samsara. ‘The kindness personified that You are, who churned the ocean for devatas’ sake and obtained Sri Mahalakshmi, please show Your magnificent appearance in the company of Sri Mahalakshmi.

The azwar reminds the Lord about His role when Rudra fought the Tripurasura entering the tip of the bow used by him. You however granted him that victory. ‘Please remove the opposing forces and grant me the benefit befitting my ‘swaroopa’, that is to be subservient to You.

For one who controls the Sun and Moon, and takes everything as a child, into the stomach and goes to sleep in Ksheerabdhi (in the ocean), it is nothing big for You to devise a way by which I can see You.

He is the cause for everything; He is having the entire Universe as His body and He is not assessable by even Brahma. He is lauded by the Vedas. ‘You should show Your grace to me to enable me to witness Your extraordinary appearance.

He is connoted by Pranava which is the biggest treasure for brahmins reciting Vedas and when one offers his soul at the feet of the Lord, the pranava becomes ‘Aathma Vachaka’.

The lurid women who charm bring in pain and death. In order to overcome such danger I have taken refuge in Your holy feet. ‘O Lord bedecked with Tulasi garland, and crown, if only You can grant me Your grace with Your presence, I shall overcome the samsara’. I am interested only in You.

The travails during death / in the hell / as unborn in mother’s womb which I have deeply thought about and giving up all desire of life in between, I have come to Your holy Feet. Lord of Brahma, Rudra and other devatas, You grant Your grace showing Yourself and enable me to overcome births / deaths. This, the azwar, says is achievable at Tiruvinnahar shrine.

## 6.2 Porutthen: On Tiruvinnahar (2)

Disappointed that he was still in samsaara, and remembering the cycle of births undergone / the life involving material aspects / sensual pleasures, and impending travails in the hell, the azwar appeals to the Lord: I am incapable of removing these and seek Your refuge disregarding my blemishes / mistakes.

Generally one praises others to be able to receive some monetary benefits from them, and if no benefits come one hates them. The azwar says he vowed to get rid of love and hate, and purging the heart ‘I have come to surrender unto You. Please show Your grace’.

The azwar pines: ‘O Lord I had forgotten You and was just like ‘achethana without any knowledge’. I fell into the abyss of birth and death, and suffered miseries. I have come to Your Feet, Lord, with Sridevi on Your chest.

Addressing the Lord as the leader / master of Nithya and Leelavibhoothis, the azwar says : ‘Realising the uselessness of going after fawn-eyed attractive women, and that compared to time spent in hell, the length of time one lies in mother’s womb seems endless, I have come to Your holy Feet’.

The azwar talks about the futility of near and dear and they would want to know before one dies, the places where the money lies hidden (even Periazwar talks about this in his Thirumozhi). I have ‘weaned away’ from them and with shining dagger of grace You have presented to me, I will cut the hold of the senses on me and fall at Your feet.

In the annals of passing years the so called strong persons / wealthy ones have left without trace. Having no desire for material world anymore, I take refuge in You, the Lord of Tiruvinnahar.

The Lord tells the azwar unless you keep your sense organs under control how can you expect to come to Me. The azwar tells the Lord ‘although I will not wish to be ruled by the senses, You alone can help me to ensure that they do not get hold of me. You are the supre-wrestler amongst wrestlers, and with Your bow You decimated the walled city of Lanka. You erase the dominance of senses and accept me as Your servitor’.

There are two types of candidates seeking moksha - The first, aartha, wants to attain the Lord immediately, while the second, ‘druptha’ after the normal life cycle. The azwar says that he is in the first category and that the Lord should not delay any more.

The very senses which should be used to attain You (as You would have intended) have in fact proved otherwise and created difficulties of ‘I’ and ‘mine’ and are pulling me back into samsara. I do not trust them anymore. I have come to You, O Lord of Tiruvinnahar.

The azwar is trying to convince the Lord the inseparable load of sins which are trying to push him to the hell. The Lord says: ‘after all, it is the outcome of your karmas’. The azwar retorts ‘Haven’t You removed the karmas from Nithyasooris? Aren’t they enjoying Your presence permanently? O, the first-cause Lord of gods, the one without any change!, You removed the misery the Moon was subject to; I have come to Your holy Feet.’

‘Sridevi and Bhoodevi laud You. You walked as a messenger for Pandavas through the columns of parasoled kings’. That very Lord, says the azwar, is residing in Tiruvinnahar. Those who read these verses, will attain Sri Vaikuntam.

## 6.3 Thurappen: Tiruvinnahar (3)

Despite the azwar seeking refuge at His holy Feet, the Lord pauses for a minor examination since the soul from beginningless time may still carry a bit of previous knowledge of good and bad. He acts only after it is ‘nil’. The azwar tells the Lord that ‘he is giving up what is inimical to Him and retain those which are favourable to Him’. I have given up all pleasures, but I cannot forget Your beautiful form. No more births for me, and I have come to You, with Your own generosity.

0, Tirumaal (Lord), Righteousness personified, You cannot complain that I still have interest in external things; I gave up relationships so that I can overcome love-hate aspects. Thanks to my state of being subservient to You, I have become absolutely fit for Your service at Your holy feet and You are firmly lodged in my heart. I have managed to give a slip / get away from the strong clutches of beautiful women, and have come and fallen at Your feet. You the Lord of Tiruvinnahar, are the nectar of Tirunaraiyoor, and You have come to Tirukkurungudi.

All my past actions like seeking pleasure in the sandal pasted bosom of women with long arms ended in the abyss of hell. I took refuge in You, the loftiest, present in Tiruvinnaharam, and reaching You I have overcome all my miseries!

‘I do not think of anything excepting You; the loftiest and fittest refuge, and have kept You firmly in my heart. You, the Lord of Tiruvinnahar, erased the ego-personified Ravana. Is it any big feat for You to eliminate obstacles which I encounter. I have found freedom from the cycle of births and deaths.

Addressing the Lord of Tiruvinnahar, the azwar says: ‘You are everything - dark ocean / mountains / Sun-Moon etc. I meditated You in my mind and obtaining this new existence, (that is, realising that I am subservient to You / You are my master), I cannot think of any other god for my refuge. I do not utter other god’s names; rather than elaboration, as in the case of Your / Arjuna’s dialogues, I would like to hear from You the very core substance of what You want to say.

I shall not ever think of those who are here for a short or long time and finally fall dead. I do not think of Brahma or Indra - I will not think of dead mango trees which fall being hit by an elephant. I am approaching with ‘a clear thought and a loving heart’. When will You grant me ‘moksha’?

O Lord of Tiruvinnahar, Pot-dancer, killer of Madhu, tell me: ‘Are you thinking that I am undergoing the effects of my actions. I have come to undertake kainkaryam at Your feet and have the eligibility for it. Should You not ensure position of not allowing any karmas coming anywhere near me’. ‘The obstacles, for attaining the Lord, will not touch those who have a strong will and desire to eliminate them irrespective of samsaric history’ says the azwar.

## 6.4 Kannum Shuzanru: On Naraiyoor (1)

Although we talked about ‘aartha’ the Lord seemed to favour ‘Vaikunta after death’. It is prapanna’s duty to conduct himself in a way, it pleases the Lord. Visiting ‘divya deshas’ where He is present is the best course and the azwar decides to go to Tirunaraiyoor.

Reminding one of the ten pasuras on old age (1-3 : Muttha mootthu), the azwar talks about old age - eyes beginning to roll, phlegm travelling up the throat, lovely women addressing on ‘Go, father, carefully - one should see the Lord in Tirunaraiyoor! He is mountains / sky / vedas / sacrifices personified.

Before the lovely coiffured-women start taunting thus: ‘old man, coughing to glory, why have you come here’, let us go to the protector of this world, the one who became Panchabhoothas, the Lord who is in Tirunaraiyoor and worship Him.

Before the hordes of women criticise: ‘forgetting your age you want to have yet another look at our beauty and our dresses; let us go to Tirunaraiyoor where the Lord is worshipped by Kings.

Those were the days when beautiful women had desired him, but now they throw him out / shut the door speaking insultingly. It is time that he approached Lord’s Feet at Tirunaraiyoor which is surrounded by golden fruits - orchards and tall Areca trees.

Before the lovely women, whose eyes resemble the fawn in innocence, in movement the fish, in sharpness the spear and in red colour the flower ‘shenguzuneer’ start insulting let us hurry to Tirunaraiyoor, surrounded by fertile fields and home for the dancing fish Malangu, Varal and Valai and where the Lord resides.

That was a time when lightning-thin-waisted women were interested but now insultingly ask, looking at old age, ‘why did You come so far coughing ?’ and chastise. You should now go to the Lord who, through Anjaneya burnt Lanka, is residing in Tirunaraiyoor to offer worship.

Those were the days when the women said we cannot live without him. Before they talk within the ear-shoot, ‘this man is wicked, he is broke’ let us go to the Lord, surrounded by Vedic scholars, in Tirunaraiyoor where He resides and offer our worship. Before so much as use a stick let us hurry to Tirunaraiyoor to offer worship to the Lord who eliminated Moon’s sickness and chose this shrine for the sake of Piraatti; before the women start insulting, let us hurry to grove-filled Tirunaraiyoor to offer worship to the Lord.

## 6.5 Kalanga Munneer: On Naraiyoor (2)

Stating in the previous decad that Tirunaraiyoor is comparable to Paramapadam the azwar goes on to say that if one, understanding his relative position of himself being a servant and the Lord, the master, goes to Tirunaraiyoor and does kainkaryam, he will attain everything. When He can, for the sake of ego-filled devatas, churn the sea upto to the bottom and gave ‘amrutha’ to them who had been defeated by ‘asuras’, would He not help us? He resides here, where brahmins have mastered the Vedas.

Despite Prahlada being a son of an asura, he came to his help as he uttered Lord’s name. He, as Narasimha, split Hiranyakashipu’s chest. The Lord helps us the same way He helped Sitadevi - He decimated columns of huge elephants, army in terms of ‘Chaturanga’. He lives in this shrine.

The Lord prefers to accept offerings having their contact / touch of ashrithas. The butter supposed to have been safely hidden was gobbled up by Him and He was tied to a mortar. He humbled the seven bulls to win nappinnai’s hand. He resides in Naraiyoor where peacocks, it appears, are dancing to the tunes of bumble bees in groves with fragrance. The swans here want to walk in the same style as women and go behind them!

The Lord drank both the poisonous milk and the life of Poothana with a gaping mouth. The Kuvalayaapeeda of mountainous size was killed by Him. He brought back to life the dead son of His teacher, Saandeepani, as ‘guru-dakshina’. He as Sarathy to Arjuna ensured the defeat of eighteen ‘akshohini’ army He smartly managed to see that Jayadhrutha could not execute his plan of killing Arjuna. With this achievement He reduced the load on the earth. This Lord is residing in Tirunaraiyoor - He is bedecked with Tulasi garland and a magnificent crown befitting His being the Master.

## 6.6 Ambaram: On Naraiyoor (3)

In an effort to convince that those who are not excelling in bhakthi can also ensure pleasures while here and hereafter (Paramapadha), the azwar gives the example of Chola king who had worshipped lesser gods achieving nothing.

The Lord, the miracles personified, showed a miracle to Rishi Markandeya. As a small boy He was sleeping with ail the Universe in His stomach. Markandeya saw the child, and as it drew the breath went inside and saw the entire Universe inside. O devotees if you desire to see the Lord hurry to Naraiyoor Manimada Koil. The bees here go first to Champaka flowers and soon after to vakula flowers - Sembianko Chenganan offers worship here.

The Lord as Matsya would swim in the sea and also was moving with utter freedom. The big and strong fish that was, ‘swam over deluge water and over mountains and beyond. The Lord has ocean as His ‘peethambara’, earth as Feet, Vayu as corpus (shareera), eight quarters as arms and the Universe as His head. If you want to attain the Lord go to Naraiyoor Manimadkoil.

While He is, as said in the foregoing, He is also present everywhere. With eyes wreaking / shining as fire with which He wanted to set the fire of fear in the asura. With His nail, He split asunder Hiranya. He had Hiranya below His feet and he reached ‘Veeraswarga’. If you want to become Lord’s favourites, you hurry to Naraiyoor Manimadakoil which is visited by the Chola king.

The Lord becomes what He wishes through His sankalpa (thinking). He created three radiant worlds, became a Trimoorthy, the Lord of Paramapada, and chose to become a cowherd as Krishna giving the opportunity for a couple to call Him their son. He resides in Naraiyoor Manimada koil, which is visited by the Chola King.

O devotees if you desire to attain Him, who drew poisonous milk from Poothana’s breasts, grazed cows, played the flute, took away gopasthrees’ bangles, became Dwaraka’s king, became Nappinnai’s bridegroom, as Parasurama destroyed ego-filled Kshathriyas, go to Tirunaraiyoor’s Manimadakoil, which is visited by the Chola king.

If you desire to hear the praises on the Lord, see Him wearing Vyjayanthi garland having made Srirangam His abode, and He who is the ‘pathi’ of Lakshmi and deserving of Rishis encomiums, and is Nappinnai’s Lord, go to Tirunaraiyoor Manimadkoil which is visited by the Chola king.

Thus extols Kalikanri the Lord of Shenganmal of Tirunaraiyoor Manimadkoil where the Lord is in company of Vedic seers of high merit.

## 6.7 Aalum paniyum: On Naraiyoor (4)

The Lord Himself enlists for His refuge those who have interest and those who worship lesser gods. Commenting on Chola kings taking refuge in Naraiyoor, the azwar says: ‘the Lord has instilled interest in me in keeping with my swaroopa (that is, he is servant and Lord his Master) and to this end He removed inimical forces. These is no protector other than the Lord’

The Lord of Naraiyoor, making me understand my ‘Paaratarlthrya’, that is, the premise that I do not belong to myself and I am subservient to Him, and He made me His own. As regards those not interested in service to Him, He destroys them: He makes them undergo punishments like cutting giving no hands / legs and even then they do not come round, He enters their bodies with fireproducing arrows. Parashurama went round twenty one times to kill, with His axe those who were callous.

The Lord, whose constant contact increased the brightness in Adisesha while lying supine in Ksheerabdhi, left the serpent bed and killed Mareecha, followed by His visit to Mahabali as Vamana and grew as Trivikrama This Lord, who punishes those who do not respect ‘saama’ (Veda), is in Tirunaraiyoor.

The Lord as a child stole white butter (fresh), resulting in His getting tied to a mortar by Yashodha; ‘how lucky that rope was, it got the Lord’s touch’ says the azwar. If those with good disposition towards Him tie Him, He will stand and weep. It is not possible to tie Him down with ‘Nagapasha’ or by Duryodhana. Further He can walk into any enemy camp to decimate them: Kuvalayaapeeda, Kamsa or Kaaliya were no match to Him. This very Lord is in Tirunaaraiyoor giving His ‘darshan’ to all.

The Lord chopped off the thousand hands of Bana, when Shiva and others ran away. The pincer crab male, the story goes, which went in search of nectar for its spouse, found the flowers having closed due to dusk. It remained there whole night and returned in the morning. The female shut the door as its mate was wafting flowers’ fragrance (doubling its fidelity!). The females here have an upperhand since it is in the vicinity of Nachhiyar koil!

The Lord drove the chariot for Arjuna and helped him and Pandavas to win the war; He lifted Govardhana mount to protect cows and cowherds; He wields Sudarshana Chakra (but ensured it did not reach Indra).

The Lord, as though in response to Draupadi’s ‘pratijna’ (vow) that she will gather her hair only after the enemies got killed, did as much. The enemies seated in caprisoned elephants were decimated through His blowing of Panchajanya. Draupadi had uttered ‘Govinda’ and taken refuge in the Lord. Similarly He saved Rudra who had pinched one of Brahma’s heads and invited ‘Brahmahathya dosha’ by pouring profusely His body’s fragrant sweat into the Kapala (Brahma’s skull) and breaking it. This very Lord is in Tirunaraiyoor with sweet-smelling groves - the Vedic scholars have made this their home. Those who master these ten verses will rule the earth.

## 6.8 Mankonda: On Naraiyoor (5)

The azwar goes on to narrate for his preference to Tirunaraiyoor. He queries ‘after having seen His glory and Nacchhiyar’s glory with own eyes how could any one ask that way’. Even when the Lord chooses to step down and undertakes a feat like the one in Vamanavatara, it only reflects His greatness. Just in case anyone missed the contact (sparsha) of His holy Feet, He is in Tirumalai to compensate for it. Going in search of Him I found Him in Tirunaraiyoor, just as a thirsty man getting cool water!

During churning of the ocean enlisting devatas as also asuras for the job, the ocean became unmanageable and seeing this anticlimax, the Lord assumed ‘mathsya roopa’ and drank up the rising waters. All by Himself the Lord saved the world from deluge. ‘To save me from the deluge of samsara He is in Tiruvali with Sridevi. Just as getting cool water when thirsty, I got Him in Tirunaraiyoor.

The Lord answers His devotees’ call fast when in predicament as in the case Gajendra. Garuda’s usual speed was not in tune with Lord’s urgency to reach the spot, He Himself sped pulling along with Him Garuda. He achieved two things in one shot. He saved Gajendra and by splitting asunder the Crocodile it warded off the curse it was subject to so long!

As Narasimha He appeared at the very spot He was to protect His ‘aashritha’. The diamond-like hard body was split asunder without even His nail not bending! To relax and rest, He has come to Tirunaraiyoor.

He came, in Ramavatara, as a bow-wielder used sharp arrows to kill Ravana. When Vibhishana (who left Ravana’s contact) came for Sharanagathi, Sugreeva and others objected, but Sri Rama stuck to His Vratha and accepted him.

The Lord who is the origin for Brahma who creates fourteen worlds, chose to be a son of a cowherd woman. He submits Himself to ‘ashrithas’. The Lord as Krishna ordered Agni to burn ‘Kandava garden’ which was under tight security. He brought Parijatha for Sathyabhama He is in Tirumeyyum. The azwar says that in order to grant his wishes, He is in Tirunaraiyoor.

When Bhoodevi appealed about the burden She was subject to due to ‘asuric’ beings, He as sarathy to Arjuna, ensured that Duryodhana and others were not born again - He gave them ‘Veera swarga’. This Lord is reclining in Thirukkudanthai bedecked with shining discus and white Panchajanya. He is our Lord. I have seen Him in Tirunaraiyoor.

Kaliyan lauds thus the bright child of Vasudeva, who resides in Tirunaraiyoor. Those who recite and master these verses will be respected even by Nithyasooris.

## 6.9 Pedeyadartha: On Tirunaraiyoor (6)

Having realised the Lord here itself (samsaric world) the azwar tells his heart to undertake only such things that are in keeping with Lord’s swaroopa as Master, and his own as His servitor. There may be a query as regards his doing prapatthi at Tirunaraiyoor and his having done in many places before. The explanation given is that he is not expecting anything new in his new prapatthi and he just wants to experience the Lord as and when he wants.

In this shrine, Tirunaraiyoor, resides the Lord who saved Rudra from Brahma kapala (skull). He also slibed off all the ten heads of Ravana using arrows emitting fire (as Agni himself) as he cheated Sitadevi.

The Lord is supremely happy to satisfy Himself with material (like butter) touched by those who are close to Him. And if such people are in any form of trouble, He will go to any length to ward off their difficulties, lift a mountain if need be.

The perennial waters of Cauvery deposit sandal wood, gold nuggets, loads of pearls in Tirunaraiyoor. The Lord who kept the universe in His stomach and held Sudarshana Chakra to produce darkness in the war, the Lord who has lodged Sridevi in His chest, resides here. ‘Reach to His Feet’ tells the azwar.

Bedecked with Tulasi garland and uniquely beautiful in idol form, the Lord is in Tirunaraiyoor where one finds ripe paddy with red lotus in between and sugarcane. One would want to see them over and over again. The Tulasi worn by the Lord on His head shows that He is the master of both Leela and Nithya Vibhoothis.

Unlike His avataras which have an end, He is residing eternally in Manimada koil and the Lord’s holy Feet can win anyone. Brahma and Rudra come to worship Him. Addressing his heart the azwar says ‘take refuge in the Lord’.

In keeping with the presence of the Lord (goal to be attained), the place Tirunaraiyoor has suitable Prakaras (tall mansions).

## 6.10 Kidandha Nambi: On Naraiyoor (7)

The azwar talks about the manthra suitable for all the three varnas. The Lord who is everywhere and is everything is known by the name Narayana and ‘Namah’ added to it would be required by those taking His refuge.

‘I am fully content having seen the Lord in reclining state in Thirukudanthai’ says the azwar. The Lord lifted the mother Earth from ocean depths, decimated Lanka, measured the Earth in just two strides; He also humbled the poison-wreaking Kaliya by jumping on his head. No doubt He has names like Krishna, Varaha, Vamana but to take refuge in Him, His name is ‘Namo Narayana’.

When the Lord pulled out the tusks of the hot-tempered Kuvalayaapeeda it simply shuddered, He churned the ocean; He has Sri on His chest; He stole the butter from cowherd women who had gathered their hair; He is worshipped by all at Tiruppadaha shrine in Kanchi; He sent a torrent of arrows on Ravana, giving the kingdom to Vibheeshana after Ravana’s death; that Lord’s Srinama is ‘Namo Narayana’.

Further He held aloft ‘Govardhanagiri’ to protect cows and cowherds; He humbled the seven roguish bulls. Before any one complains that one could not see the Lord during Trivikrama avataara, the azwar hurries to say that He is present in Tirukkovaloor as Trivikrama; He flung asunder the asuras in Lanka. The Lord with all these abilities has the Srinama ‘Namo Narayana’.

Ravana’s valour drove away Indra. Such a brave asura was humbled by Sri Rama - He built an army with the inhabitants of the jungle like monkey, boars etc. The azwar addresses the Lord as Tirumal, meaning being with Sridevi incessantly. The name Narayana fully elucidates the Lord.

He manages all His creations like oceans, earth etc. The original cause that He is well represented by the name ‘Narayana’ and the azwar says that it is easy to pronounce.

Even before the cowherds could call Krishna by this name, He had lifted Govardhana to protect them. He is known by the name Narayana.

The azwar says that He is the master of Nithyasooris, the devatas and they seek His assistance for sustenance. To be rid of Karmic records chant His name: Namo Narayana. The azwar says that his proximity to the Lord is so much that He rules mind / speech etc. Those who recite these verses, will be able to rid themselves from all sins.

## Seventh Patthu

## 7.1 Karavaa: On Naraiyoor (8)

Realising that he is the servant, and Lord, the master i.e. his swaroopa Jnaana, the azwar cries for Kainkaryam. The Lord assures that for this very purpose He is present in so many divya deshas.

It is said that when in full udder, the cow thinks of its calf - similarly the Lord is conscious of devotees’ urgency. ‘Even being in samsara, and not forgetting You I am calling You’ the azwar tells the Lord. Is my calling itself not enough? During deluge who called You to protect mother Earth? Are you incapable of eliminating further births? If You delay to act, whatever good name You have acquired then will get annulled!’ saying so, the azwar conveys his ‘ananyagathithva’.

The Lord replies: ‘After reclining in Ksheerasagara, standing in Tirumalai, I have now come to Tirukottiyoor’. The azwar says that he will never part with that kindness of the Lord to anyone - this means that for Lord’s ‘krupe’ the azwar alone is the subject and experiencing it; enhances his satiation. ‘He says he can now walk on the heads of Yama’s henchmen!’

Lord’s actions with ‘aashrithas’ cannot be normally analysed. He is living in me and therefore I cannot forget Him. He is ‘rasa’ personified (karumbe: Tamil). He has comprehensively presented His divine idol’s beauty to me. How can I forget such a helpful Lord says the azwar.

‘You walked in the dense jungles with Your consort with the hope of meeting some kinkaras (subservient ones). Your love (vaathsatya) is more than the parents’.

Just tell me once that for my acquiring kainkarya, You will eliminate all the inimical forces. The Lord replied that ‘He is in divya deshas for people like him and He is also present in the North in Himalayas’. He shows various kalyana gunas / His swaroopa in each shrine. He is not the type of amrutha given to devathas; He is that amrutha which Srivaishnavas relish. ‘I am happy I saw You’ says the azwar.

‘You are the goal as well as the route’. Your anugraha alone is Prapaka (route) and Your holy feet the goal (Prapya). I have fulfilled my wish by seeing the ‘divya deshas’ where You reside. ‘You’re saying the same again and again’ if one were to remark so, his answer would be ‘will they give You to me!?’ ‘Even if you are called a mad cap, you are not behaving in a manner I can give you up’ the Lord tells the azwar.

The Lord comparing His protective help with that of one’s parents, queries the azwar about His help. The azwar replies: ‘You are lot greater than even mothers and Your help extends to all beings. Your eyes ooze that kindly love. At times of difficulty You protect everyone. How can one forget such an ‘Apathsaka?’ asks the azwar.

The azwar experienced the perfect Supreme Being (paripoornan) in Tirunaraiyoor. ‘You can also have similar interest and desire, and Parampada will come to you automatically’ the azwar tells us.

## 7.2 Pullai: On Naraiyoor (9)

The Lord had told the azwar that He was residing in ‘divya deshas’ to enable kainkaryam by devotees. The azwar says that he obtained this great benefit despite having this body worth rejecting. ‘You have rendered my heart direct itself as a medium for ‘moksha’. You without my knowledge got inside (a thief at that: kalvaa)! ‘You appeared as a boar and as a swan. Since You are right in front of me, the old knowledge of good and bad cannot just stand / sustain’.

‘Instead of lowly avataras like boar could You not think of taking birth as someone like us?! As Narasimha You entered my heart to reside. You have not only made Prahlada long for You but also me and there is nothing which You cannot achieve going by this. I have left off praising others in my poetry but now, since You are the subject of my sense organs I do not extol anybody excepting You’ declares the azwar.

‘How about praising those who help you’ the Lord questions. The azwar replies ‘even compared to the help rendered by parents Your extraordinary help is such I can laud You only. I have established such relationship with You, which parents are not capable of. You are shining brilliantly in Tirunaraiyoor just as in Paramapadham, and I will not, excepting You, extol any body however much they persuade me.

However much I might have helped in the past, no one remembers me. To Lord’s query that since I am seated in your heart, is it possible that you become ungrateful?!’ The azwar replies: ‘Your feats in swallowing, as a baby, all the worlds and recline on a fig leaf in the ocean is a great service done by You. Lying in my heart, You won my heart. I am not so ungrateful as to forget it. ‘When the Lord in an effort accentuate azwar’s love towards Him moves a few steps, the azwar says ‘I will not allow You to leave’. When the Lord queries: ‘You say You’re my servant but not allowing me to go’. The azwar replies that ‘those who perform ‘madal’ do not distinguish between right and wrong - I lost my shame long ago’. The Lord retorts: ‘Your reply shows you are a swantantra and therefore there is nothing wrong if I leave’. The azwar binds His legs saying ‘that the Lord is His Kulanatha. For seven generations we have been Your ‘dasas’. When You have entered my heart on Your own how can I leave You?’ When the Lord insisted that He will leave, the azwar says, that he cannot then hold on to his life. ‘What strength do you have to stop me?’ the Lord queries ; the azwar points to Mahalakshmi whose ‘anugraha’ is such that ‘even if You try to give me up because of my mistakes, She will ask You to forget them.’

‘Worshipped always by Nithyasooris, You entered my heart and why think of leaving before completing Your task, You Paripoorna. You are residing in Tirunaraiyoor with incomparable splendour’ the azwar tells the Lord.

The Lord stopped there. The azwar tells Him that despite having the mortal body he realised Him. The Lord resides in Tirunaraiyoor which has abundant water sources.

## 7.3 Shinavil Shengan: On Naraiyoor (10)

Just like Yogis, the azwar also got to see the Lord in the mind / heart; he aspires that this should become a real experience: ‘Just showing this much having come to reside in Naraiyoor is too little’ the azwar tells the Lord.

The Lord, rather than meting out some suitable punishment kills the demons because He wants to ensure a smooth running of His Vibhoothi. On the other hand He is not easily satisfied with whatever He gives to Yogis who keep Him in the heart and pray. ‘I saw Him in a dream state; eyes and heart are overjoyed; there was no feeling of disappointment that my eyes did not see’. ‘Due to previous knowledge of good and bad, if I were to seek something else?’ the azwar questions himself. Answering it, he says ‘it is the Lord who has made me remember Him. He looks after everyone during any predicament. Poorna (Nambi) that He is and has blessed me, will I ever seek anyone else?!’

‘The Lord Himself has made my heart His residence, and He is not thinking of an alternative place. It is not my ‘sankalpa’ (desire). It is His desire, and how can I forget His great help of showing / bringing to light His ‘swaroopa, roopa, guna and vibhoothis’.

Even if I want to forget, my heart is reserved for Him. He decimated the ego-filled Kauravas as sarathy to Arjuna; the Lord had compassion for Pandavas as they had lost their father early. My heart will not prostrate to anyone excepting Him. Not only does He absolve us from sins but also assures us that even if we have reached the hell He will protect us. His holy feet are as tender as Lotus and He, as Tiruvengada appears as a jewel to Nithyasooris. He eats only that which has had the contact of His ‘ashrithas’ and allowed His mother to tie Him up to a mortar - He gave a helpless look crying heavily! Leaving such an ‘extraordinary joy’ will I think of anyone else.

The Lord also will not think of separating from my heart. He presented Himself in Tirumalirunjolai to enable everyone to experience Him. My sensory perception (manovakkayai) knows only Him and none else and for this He is responsible.

The Lord who has shown His immense grace asks the azwar about the play of karma in birth and death. The azwar replies that sins are His punishment, but when one has His grace it cannot stay. The Lord, as warder of sins, is Siddopaya (waiting to help). From beginningless time I kept thinking about soul as mine, but in a moment He took me away. For this help one has nothing to give as a return help to Him, because we are ‘paratanthras’. 'I don’t see any way of doing it to Him - the azwar thinks that he himself is Prahlada and for Lord’s help of eliminating Hiranyakashipu what could one give Him. My heart will sing ‘pallandu’ to Him and to no one else.

The Lord who resides in Tirunaraiyoor is full of kalyana gunas. Prostrating again and again to His holy feet, the azwar, intoxicated with joy, started dancing.

## 7.4 Kanshora: On Tiruccherai

Just as Nammazwar eulogised bhagavathaseshathva (payilam shudroli: 3-7), in this decad Tirumangai Azwar talks about the same subject. ‘Srivaishnavas who are totally unaware of themselves while doing service to the Lord are my goal’, so saying the azwar extols ‘bhagavathaseshathva’.

Impersonating Yashodha came Poothana came to kill the Lord but fell dead and the young Lord was seen to be suckling her breasts still!

When the Lord asks the azwar to show his head so that the Lord can place His holy feet, the azwar says that his head was not the place for it, and his head was adequate for His bhaktha’s feet.

When the Lord hurled Vatsasura on Kapitthasura He had to move four steps and that walk resembled the walk of a trained actor! In Tiruccherai there is eternal spring. To a query by the Lord as to ‘whether Sri Vaishnavas who are in this world are worthy or the Nithyasooris?’, the azwar says Srivaishnavas who worship the Lord’s holy feet in Tiruccherai with flowers and water are more worthy than Nithyasooris.

Sri Rama’s arrows became fire wreaking ones as they reached the enemies. The supreme devotees of the Lord cannot survive without chanting Narayana, Krishna, Vaasudeva ‘I cannot survive without talking about them even for a moment’ says the azwar. The azwar says further that those who do not realise the uniqueness of Narasimha and that His devotees are his goal, I cannot stay with them - here the azwar is commenting mainly on Boudhas, Jains and other heretic faiths who cannot be expected to be interested in the ‘bhagavathas’. The bhagavathas while worshipping / going round the holy feet think that the holy feet provide protection. Such people ruminate over Lord’s feat of lifting Mother Earth from the ocean’s depth. ‘They are my goal; I become ecstatic seeing them, I declare and announce that I am a servant to such bhagavathas.

The Lord is reclining on Adhisesha whose spotted skin attracts even the Lord who sees them intently. The Lord despite being in deep ‘Yoganidra’ is able to show His grace. He has a resplendent divine idol and resides in Tiruccherai. ‘I am a servant to those who praise the beauty of Lord’s frame. And if one just remembers the bhagavathas, it will ward off any fear of death. The Lord queries how is it that the azwar has such excessive love towards bhagavathas’. Not able to find a reason, the azwar realises that thinking that Lordgiven soul is his own (aathmapahara), I used to be in sea of sorrow. How I managed to get this extraordinary benefit of remembering the Lord constantly after self-surrender, I do not know. But my heart derives extreme pleasure looking at the bhagavathas who, keeping in mind the lovely Feet of the Lord Saaranatha, residing in Tiruccherai meditate.

In this beautiful shrine, Tiruccherai, swans in pairs try to imitate the coiffured women, but failing to do so, give up and rest under mango trees. Sri Saaranatha, who as Krishna drove Arjuna’s Chariot, resides here.

## 7.5 Thandhaikaal: On Azandhoor

Closely following the ‘bhagavatha seshathva’, came the desire for ‘bhagavad vishayam’. It heightened to an extent of realising the Lord Himself. Not getting it immediately led to the azwar assuming Nayaki’s role once again.

He comes to our rescue when we are in danger. ‘*Eight-birth- long*’ fetters of Vasudeva gave way by themselves when Lord Krishna was born. Besides helping Vasudeva, He was helpful to Himself too! To provide His support for people like Vasudeva He is present in shrines like Tiruazhandhoor. And our target is the place where He resides.

The Lord reduced the burden of ‘asuric’ elements on the Earth by grinding them under the wheels of Arjuna’s chariot which He drove. He did not hanker for name since He was a ‘sarathy’ and victory credit went to Arjuna.

Surrounded by a strong fortress, Ravana’s ego as master of Lanka lost all the ten heads to Rama’s ‘brahmastra’. ‘When the Lord was sleeping on a fig leaf during deluge, He did not have an Yashodha to care for Him’, pines the azwar. This produced urgency in the Lord who entered azwar’s heart. He not only experienced Him mentally but saw Him in brilliant form! When the Lord saw that the azwar was a little ruffled, He asks to consider it as a Vibhoothi (extraordinary might and power). The azwar’s mind started counting: day and night, earth and sky, all these are His vibhoothis. He is therefore the Universe itself. His extraordinary appearance is present in this shrine with a smile comparable to a red lotus. ‘Getting me is His own gain, making me reject what He granted me and coming near me He united with me, entered my heart and is standing. Seeing Him happy, the azwar is delighted.’

The azwar recounts: ‘Coming at the proper time, sleeping on the flower-bed, and while leaving entering my heart thereby creating a situation of separation resulting in tears flowing in my eyes. Seeing the tears He resided in this shrine. In this ‘samlesha’ (union) in a dreamy state, the Nayaki says during the close-union my waist was subject to a slight pain. Ensuring that the mind would not be subject to any dissatisfaction, He ensured that He entered my mind / heart. To give full satisfaction, He came and stood by me. The whole thing is a matter of eternal meditation for me.

He having united with me, took away the knowledge of five senses; and He made sure that He took away everything from me and that, I was not wanting anything at all. Such a noble Being is in Tiru Azandhoor. He does not appear to have been affected in any way by separation from me. ‘The fields here are beyond description. Flowers like lotus compare well with lovely womens’ eyes and lips.

## 7.6 Shingamadhai: On Azandhoor (2)

The azwar was sad that the Lord went away (Podha Punidhar) but the Lord pacifies: ‘Did I not come for help for a boy? Did I not unite with You whenever You wished. And whenever You want to unite and worship, I am here having eliminated all inimical elements. To enable you to become My ‘daasa’ I am in Tiru Azundhoor.

The Lord having eliminated Prahladha’s enemy felt Himself happy, just the same way a mother would be happy when her child drinks milk. Brahmins vying with Brahma who undertakes creation live in Tiru Azandhoor.

The Lord also went round the world twenty one times to decimate ego-filled Kshathriyas. His divine idol has the same colour as azure ocean. I got the opportunity to see the Lord who rules over Nithyasooris having no trace of nescience (ajnana), and achieved ecstacy.

The one who sleeps during deluge, created the world surrounded by ocean waters. Rudra ran away getting defeated at the hands of the Lord seeing His discus. He had to utter ‘Krishna, Krishna Mahabaho’ before being saved. He is residing in Tiruazhandoor in such a way that it excels Nithya Vibhoothi.

The Lord also humbled Indra lifting Govardhanagiri. He saved Gajendra. Enemies who are cunning get decimated with matching smartness from the Lord who is present in Tirukannamangai. The Lord who drank Poothana’s life is present in Tiruazandhoor.

Those gods who think they are big, realise their mistakes with the Lord appearing biggest and beyond their ability to know Him.

Mahalakshmi is inseparable from the Lord who is also the Controller of ‘Panchabhoothas’. Leaving Him, everything else is subservient to Him (He is the one talked about in Vedas). Because of this, He can make one into a servitor, and He is the Prapaka (the route). All ‘shastras’ proclaim Him to be the goal.

The Lord being present in so many shrines, He seems to let us know that He is the Prapya (Supreme Being) and Master of all. He is like Mother-root to all dharmas. He is residing in Tiru Azandhoor amidst beautiful people like Subramanya.

## 7.7 Thiruvakkum: On Azandhoor (3)

The enjoyment of having seen the Lord did not sustain due to inimical sense organs which can have sway at any time. The only route left, being surrender to the Lord with Sri Mahalaxmi as Purushakara medium. Scared of forces of Prakrithi attacking him, he requests the Lord to free him from that and enlist him to His feet.

Being the husband of Lakshmi, Controller of nithyasooris, and ability to hold firmly Sudarshana Chakra are the signs of Supremacy. He protected everyone in His stomach during deluge.

The azwar says when it is so difficult to please even one sense organ, there are five! Scared of them I have come to take refuge in Your holy Feet. The azwar talks about his utter helplessness (Akinchanyathva); the Lord with Mahalakshmi who is always with the Lord, has come to reside in my heart. I have no one to protect (ananyagathi) and You are residing here for the purpose of protecting those with those who are in Ananyagathi. You are extremely strong with hands so fit to handle all the weapons. You have put me in a world where it is difficult to think of doing kainkaryam to Your holy Feet. I find the sense organs regaining their place just as bad thoughts are crossing my mind. I don’t have anyone excepting You.’

Having praised Him for His quality of helping ‘aashrithas’ he now recites His names like ‘Paran’. ‘Kings come here for getting their wishes fulfilled as You are present along with Lakshmi. But I do not have anyone excepting You to protect me.’

‘As “I” and ‘mine’ aspects started ruling Hiranya, You split him into two in a way Prahlada could understand - there is no one superior to Him in protecting people. Having well understood the ways of Kaliyuga / samsaric forces, I know now what is best for me. And that is taking refuge in Your holy feet. Aren’t You here in Tiru Azundhoor just for that express purpose?’ the azwar asks the Lord.

Becoming a subject of ridicule for stealing curds Your mother tied You to a mortar, but even gods and kings cannot reach You, the controller of all the four eras (yugas). I know no other refuge, O Lord who is residing in Tiru Azundhoor.

The azwar declares to the Lord that he cannot just control the sense organs and You can only do it. Lord wants everyone to live fully protected. With such a Lord, Kamsa wanted to play tricks. He as also seven bulls were finished off by the Lord. With such actions You made me Your ‘daasa’. Being here I wish to meditate the way Nithyasooris do in Paramapada, but my sense organs are crushing me and are becoming younger by the day. Instead of You being my Seshi they are controlling me. I cannot tolerate them anymore I want You to eliminate them and shower on me Your kindness’. Those reciting these ten verses will obtain riches which will come in handy for doing service to bhagavathas.

## 7.8 Snengamalatthirumahalum: On Azandhoor (4)

When the azwar was scared of sense organs holding sway over Him, he prayed to God. The Lord assures him that He is present in Tiruazhandoor expressly to grant all he desired. Studying his stand shown by the Lord he becomes satisfied and tells others: ‘There is no one else deserving refuge. Approach Him and live happily’.

Since Adhisesha is closely holding the Lord who has no birth, death etc. and is known as Anantha, Adisesha is also known as Anantha. The Lord is being served by Sridevi and Bhoodevi. Brahmins in this shrine seem to vie with Brahma, as though they can also undertake creation!

When utter darkness, due to theft of Vedas, overtook the earth, the Lord appeared as Hayagreeva and brought back Vedas, thereby destroying darkness. It is the Lord who has been talked about in Vedas which laud kainkaryam to the Lord; Gajendra’s kainkaryam of placing flowers on the Lord’s feet led him to a pond where a crocodile caught his leg. Gajendra prayed to the Lord who saved him; Gajendra placed the flower before it lost its freshnes on Lord’s feet. Such a helpful Lord as Varaha saved the mother earth. Looking like a jewel for Leela Vibhoothi, He resides in Tiruazhundoor.

The Lord eliminates ‘aashrithas’ inimical forces. Responding to a young boy’s prayer he split Hiranya’s chest. When Poothana without any remorse to kill a small child took the Lord for suckling, He sucked her life.

He fulfils the wishes of His devotees. Even for those devatas who claim themselves to be ‘Eeshwara’ and get into problems, the Lord forgetting His supreme status went to take ‘a dhana’ from Mahabali. He protects with the same enthusiasm all chethanas in the same way as He protects His consorts: The Lord chopped off the ten heads of Ravana to bring back Sita Devi. Why is He so keen to help ‘all chethanas’? The answer is He lives eating what they offer with their hands like Yashoda and cowherd women.

He finishes off aashrithas’ enemies: Kamsa, Kuvalayaapeeda, Shakata all met with the same fate at His hands. He is supremely powerful and protects His devotees in multiple ways. Even devatas come with a wishlist to Tiruazhandoor asking the Lord for help.

## 7.9 Kallam manam: Sirupuliyoor shalasayanam

Since the Lord is Supreme, the azwar advises others not to listen to heretic faiths or those with mischievous views, but take refuge in Shriahpathi’s feet at ‘Shalashayanam’ shrine. Do not listen to those whose life’s routine is non-vedic and since you are now starting in your spiritual life take refuge in the Supreme Lord, Shalashayana Swami. Showing that this is the Lord, the azwar advises others thinking that they are friendly, to lodge Him in the heart and remove the guilt feeling. With moon-bedecked Shiva on one side and the controller of ‘devatas’, Indra, on the other side, the Supreme Lord resides in Sirupuliyur Shalashayanam, who is the cowherd, Lord Krishna.

Thinking that his lot could be the same as those in the foregoing, the azwar surrenders to the Lord immediately and seeking His refuge, says that the samsara is the never-leaving deep hell. ‘You protect me so that I do not sink into it. The natural beauty of this shrine, where Brahma onwards come and worship, is such that the flowers remind one of the eyes of lovely women.

In the last decad the Lord witnessed the azwar paying his respects to bhagavathas ; pleased as He was with this gesture, He points out to the Lord that He is present all over, including *distant* places like Tirumalirumjolai (*shei*). ‘Are You in Shalashayana where brahmins are busy in reciting the four Vedas propounding You, or in the hearts of bhagavathas, please tell me’. ‘There is no place where the Lord is not present’ is the meaning. But the azwar is apprehensive that he being in such a situation there could be a break in experiencing Him. Surrendering to the Lord, he appeals for His kindness.

Having surrendered to the Lord, the azwar describes Prapya (the Supreme Being): like a dark rain cloud He cools down His devotees’ suffering with His ‘roopa’ ; it is such that it is inviting to them. His roopa is also like a staid mountain range. His roopa is not fully describable. With Sri Mahalakshmi, He is residing in Siripuliyoor Shalasayanam, which has long avenues.

## 7.10 Perumbura: On Kannamangai

When the azwar surrendered, the Lord tells him: ‘All the things are like prakara to Me. To enable You to see, I am standing in front of you; you cannot shout (!) that you did not get Me’, so saying the Lord consoles him.

The Lord is like a huge ocean containing all the things; He is obedient to His ‘aashrithas’, but He is characterised by unlimited freedom. Those who love Him cannot survive without Him - He is their ‘prana’ (!). He displays childhood and youth at the same time. Despite experiencing Him everyday, He has always something new to offer.

‘*Shenrunadi Kandukondene*’ signifies bhakthi and prapatthi routes which are under His control. Those who are ignorant of these routes, to them He does not reveal Himself. But to others who are concerned with these routes He shows Himself as ‘Divine Idol’. He is like the sugarcane juice - one can experience Him without effort!

The azwar rejoices Lord’s protection to him as also His ‘Kalyanagunas’ (divine qualities). Helpless ones get His mercy without effort. His ‘sausheelya’ is not something occasional; it is the Lord Himself. He has helped Rudra by keeping him on one side and given the sky for the moon to move about. He is the inner soul to everything, and is the kind idol in Venkatadri. The roopa secretly covered by Vedantha which I researched, can be seen in Tirukannamangai shrine.

When asked whether the Lord blessed him only, the azwar recalls the names of earlier azwars like Mudal Azwars etc. who also received His grace. ‘When the devotees take refuge in Him He becomes more resplendent. He squeezed Himself in Tirukkovaloor with His ‘aashrithas’. He is not affected by their blemishes although He is their inner soul. He is present in Tirukkannamangai where I could see Him with my eyes’, says the azwar.

He continues: ‘The Lord is the giver of benefits related to this world and the world above. He showed Himself to me in shrines in Himalayas and won me over. He can give benefits to all in the world as He is all-powerful, and He can lead His aashrithas to Paramapadham. He is death personified to His enemies.

His ability to give is aided by His power, destruction of inimical forces, and Shriyahpathithva (Being with Lakshmi). His decoration when present with Lakshmi like kumkum, garlands, fragrant sandal paste, they give Him a unique glitter.

Shining with the subservience of Brahma and Rudra, He has unique ‘divine’ idols. His method of elimination of enemies is extraordinary. He hurled vatsasura on Kapitthasura in such a way that both got killed. He was death personified to Ravana. He places His Feet on the heads of those He likes Such a unique Lord is residing in Tirukannamangai.

The Lord is extremely impartial and provides pleasure to everyone in the same manner just like a musical note. He tends to forgive the crimes / mistakes / transgression of wrong-doers and gives them chances to take refuge in Him. No one can influence Him to stray or budge from this great quality.

The azwar tells nine times that he saw the Lord at Kannamangai (Kandukonden) - I got to see Him amidst samsara in this shrine. This garland of Tamil pasurams by Kalikanri is overflowing with love. Those mastering it will live with ‘devatas’. He addresses the Lord: ‘O, Krishna having Panchajanya in Your hand, You will also benefit from learning these songs’.

## Eighth Patthu

## 8.1 Shilai llangu: Tirukkannapuram

We have seen that the azwar resorts to Nayaki ‘bhava’ for the purpose of external union. Her mother describes her condition: she is enamoured by His divine qualities and speaks about His elegance, character, manliness; she does not bother about her being a woman. Being in a trance about ‘arche’, it appears that she has seen the ‘Shavari’ deity in Tirukkannapuram and she is bowled over by His ‘Sausheelya’.

There is reference to the upanishadhic episode of Shavethakethu the son of sage Uddalaka. Returning from a twelve-year long study, he displayed a state of arrogance and was conceited. Uddalaka asks his son : ‘Have you asked your teacher about Brahman ‘through which what is unheard becomes heard, and what is unknown - becomes known’ ; ‘knowing that alone, one would have known everything and not knowing it, you have known nothing’. ‘Sath’ (Brahma) which is invisible wanting ‘Bahusyam’, divided into ‘names and forms’. While in my case my daughter has fully understood the Supreme Being.

Looking at her behaviour, she suspects that her daughter must have seen the Lord at Tirukkannapuram.

She expresses happiness over her daughter’s words: ‘During separation one talks about beauty. His weapons are like jewellery (accoutrements) - they also include kaanchee, Noopura, rings etc. Periazwar did mangalashasana to Chakra and Shanku, which reminds one of nectary-lips. The divine idol alone was praised by all azwars’. See, she tells her mother, the Lord's great aspects. ‘How to get her to my way of thinking? She seems to have more relation with Tirukkannapuram, its people than my rearing of her’ the mother says.

You used the bow to free Sita Devi from Lanka, and united with Her. ‘No one to face me’ the bow seems to say! Is it for decoration You’re holding it or for eliminating enemies. What about Sudarshana Chakra in the other hand, which You as Upendra dealt with the wings of mountains? It was just like ring in Your hand when You humbled Kuvalayaapeedaa, and once later You just covered it but it was in Your hand!

‘Don’t get involved so much, but hear good advice from us’, tells the mother. Nayaki replies: Should I listen to you or concentrate on His beauty which I am able to see. The jewelry is shining. Kausthuba is brilliant. His coral lips are looking uniquely perfect. Since you all cannot see the divine form you are talking like this. The thing we want to get is most enjoyable. His hands are lotus-like; His jewelry is such, I cannot take off my eyes from it. He is bedecked in the chest, with Sridevi *who is the one to unite us*. For what ‘reason should I at all leave Him’.

The mother wants her to tell the name of the person who has made her mad. The daughter replies, ‘If there was one name then I could have mentioned’. He is ‘Peralan’ with a thousand names. You cannot ‘separate / divide Him’ from those names - such is His greatness. Though “Four-handed”, He has five weapons / He churned the ocean with a thousand hands as it were I appears to have eight hands! He is beautiful with His Peethambara with a golden belt. In this shrine the people do conduct ‘themselves’ the ‘yajnas’ and the Lord takes His ‘havis’ directly!

The Lord, like Kings, is coming in the main avenue (raajaveedhi) and will visit those who want Him most. He has no restriction. People in this place learn directly from ‘aachaarya’ and not through books!

The ‘Tulasi’ has limited fragrance when in the plant but when it reaches Lord’s crown, it is doubly fragrant! She says I love Him, and can never separate from Him. I remember having seen Him earlier, but where was that? This double-sense talk is because of the subject matter itself! There is no chance that she will return! says the Nayaki’s mother.

## 8.2 Thelliyeer: Tirukkannapuram (2)

When her daughter said that she ‘loved’, ‘the Lord of Tirukkannapuram must have won her over. He has also snatched away her bangles’ says the mother. Listening to her daughter’s words, the mother also develops some thoughts. ‘Were not Sri Rama and Sri Krishna of such bewitching beauty so as to win hearts?’

The mother questions the Lord: ‘The Lord that You are of Nithyasooris, is it befitting of You to think of snatching a weak woman’s bangles. You are supposed to give! Didn’t You tell Guha, when he offered fruits in Shringiberapura, that You were born to give and not to take!? Looking constantly at a lover in the chest, should You snatch a weak woman’s bangles? What a crime!? She just did ‘anjali’ to a place where You’re in ‘arche’. She did not do it to vyooha or other avataaras! ‘I am Your’s’ saying that she did ‘anjali’.”

The Lord responds: ‘Should you not keep your daughter under check as women are known for shyness?’

‘She displays a tendency to unite. Having flung the net, He is successful’ says the mother. Thinking that a safe place would help, she kept her in a strong house (like a palace). The Lord appeared on Garuda and united with her. Before the acharya instils ‘Jnana’, it is usual that due to sensual aspects, fear etc take root. Looking around she found Tirukannapuram and showed it to mother. The acharya guides one to the Lord’s feet saying that they are prapya (the goal) and Prapaka (the route for it). After all these eyes are meant for seeing Tirukkannapuram. Parashar Bhattar came across a person near Srirangavimana doing ‘japam’ with closed eyes! Bhattar told him when you are near the very object (goal) for the eyes, should you close your eyes!? It appears that person lost his eyesight in a couple of months.

‘Pananar’ is one who unites the separated Nayaka and Nayaki. For Chethanas acharya is the Pananar. ‘My daughter’s as also Shavari Perumal’s jobs will be successful. Naraiyoor Lord’s job of providing knowledge about undertaking ‘madal’ is also successful.’

The mother is apprehensive about the end result of her daughter’s actions. Those with ‘thaapa’ approach Ksheerabdhinaatha, while she is approaching ‘archa’ shrines and calls out ‘Vengadam’, ‘Thirumeyyum’, ‘Thirukannapuram with full throat.’ Just calling ‘Madhura nagari’ will ward off sins. ‘For all her tribulations my sins are the cause’ says the mother.

The mother is worried about her daughter’s deteriorating physical condition because she is fasting. Valmiki mentions that ten months of fasting and separation from Rama had made Sita weak; Devendra brought ‘amrutha’ and without rakshasa’s knowledge gave it to Her and She drank. My daughter did not get anything so long Lakshmana looked after Rama and Sita without sleep. During separation from husband food and sleep will be missed. But she is not of that age but too young. Even the rishis think Lord’s name is food. For her the place itself is food.

She lauds those shrines where the Lord has lived, Mathura, Vrindaavana, Dwaraka. Rama was born somewhere and lived somewhere else. The Lord having come to Tirukkannapuram, He considers it to be His place.

‘Her golden beauty oozes out from her cheeks when she hears of Krishna stealing butter and getting punished.’

Imagining herself to be in Gokula and ‘Shavari Perumal’ as Krishna, she enacts Krishna’s ‘leelas’ when she is not able to see Him just like other Gopikas. If someone asks as to wherefrom she came she would reply innocently ‘I come from this place and I am doing this.’

‘Just talking about her ‘separation pangs’ will not achieve anything. We must find a way out to overcome it. She has been experiencing sorrow / pains from a long time and it has become a habit. She would like to utter ‘Tiruvarangam, Turuvarangam’ - if mentioned she might forget herself ; so thinking her relatives are near her trying to ensure that she does not utter that name!; the mother observes.

Having seen people achieving the four purushaarthas (Dharma, Artha, Kaama, Moksha), she also aspires to get the benefit of living in Srirangam for which she went to Tirukkannapuram and prayed. Whatever extraordinary thing one aspires for and prays to Him, He gives that very thing. (Ramanuja first did kainkaryam at Kanchipuram and came to Srirangam thereafter!). It is only right that You, ‘Nanmanivannan’, give Your body brilliance to her rather than snatching her body colour!

‘The Supreme Controller that I am, you want to control Me; you better control your daughter’ the Lord tells the mother. ‘Look at her young age; her mental attitude is such it is not possible to control her. Just because she was born to me, should I control her’ the mother asks the Lord.

Once Garuda found a woman totally engrossed in reciting Lord’s qualities in a desolate place and thinking she was unlucky, wished that she should have been in a holy shrine. As he thought that way and tried to fly, his wings got detached! He felt remorse for his ‘apacharam’. One should not delve into past history of one who is immersed in ‘bhagavad vishayam’ but just praise that one for the present state.

Those who master these ten pasurams, by Mangai King Parakalan, ‘will live long on earth’.

## 8.3 Karaiyeduttha: Tirukkannapuram (3)

Asked repeatedly by the mother whether it was right for Him to snatch the bangles, the Nayaki appeared to get back her old bearings but instead of getting peace, it led to the condition of sadness due to ‘viraha’.

The Lord, who lives in Tirukkannapuram which is having abundance of water, saved gopalakas from torrential rain through lifting of Govardhana; He is wearing highly fragrant Tulasi garland, He has taken my bangles. The mother queries ‘did you say that to the one who lifted Govardhana you lost your bangles. Even gopis lost their bangles in Gokula’. She retorts ‘I went to Mathura following Him! Describing Tirukkannapuram the Nayaki says due to ‘ahil’ incense, everything appears dark. One cannot differentiate between the house and hills which are all over. The Lord took hold of bangles owing to his mischievous sports’.

‘The one who has sixteen thousand queens, will He at all be enamoured of you’ the mother asks. The Nayaki says ‘I am like Nappinnai who was an attraction to Him. I cannot think of leaving Him’. The mother asks ‘Is not He tethered to a mortar. Why, without knowing it, you aspire for Him’. Nayaki says ‘didn’t He use tied-up stance to kill His enemies (two Arjuna trees). Where is the obstruction for Him to come to me’.

The Lord cringed on seeing His mother bringing a stick and tried to cover up by wiping His curds-smeared mouth. Alas, I lost my bangles to Him. When the mother commented that both He, who ran seeing a stick, and she were too young, the Nayaki replies: ‘For a young boy only, who recited His names, He eliminated the huge demon (Hiranya). I have, though young, fully understood His unique nature and am suffering the ‘Viraha’.’

‘Should you, just because He saved a young boy, lose bangles to Him?’ asks the mother. ‘Isn’t He the one who saves all during the great deluge. Therefore I lost to Him my bangles’. Retorting the mother asks ‘where is the deluge. Why at all lose bangles?’!

The shade from Ponnai trees is such that one part appears dark and the other has light and in the latter part waves secrete pearls which appear beautiful from the dark side. In this shrine the black water-lilies are such as to broaden (blossom) the faces of young maidens.

When commented thus ‘The creator of all the worlds has created this ‘viraha’ and one has no alternative but to live with it’, the Nayaki says ‘Isn’t He with one who cannot stand even a moment’s separation? Then why keep me separated’.

Tirukkannapuram is surrounded by the nectar - filled groves and Tirumangai Azwar has sung about the one residing there viz.Vamana, who went to get ‘*His own thing*’through begging. Even reciting the ten songs of the azwar, will eliminate all sins.

## 8.4 Vinnavar Thangal: Tirukkannapuram (4)

Talking about her ‘viraha’ the (parakala) Nayaki fell down in a trance. Sri Rama had asked the wind to blow over Sita, touch Her and ‘then touch Me so that I can console Myself’. The Nayaki tells the dragon-fly that once the Tulasi touches the Lord’s crown, it starts giving fresh leaves and nectar flows. She tells the dragon fly to suck the gragrance from the Tulasi garland the Lord is wearing and ‘blow on me’; the Lord alone is lauded in Vedas and through them we can know Him. He is wearing the holy thread on His divine idol in Tirukkannapuram; suck the fragrance from the garland He is wearing and blow it on her.

When the dragon-fly mentioned that those worshipping deities of heretic faiths are getting results. ‘The Parakala Nayaki replies: ‘What they give is inferior and unsathvic. You go to my Lord of Tirukkannapuram, the Lord of the Universe who is worshipped by celestials, suck the fragrance from His Tulasi garland and blow over me; the Lord, who is beautiful, appeared in fish / turtle / man-lion avataaras in the past; bring back the fragrance from His Tulasi garland and blow over me!’

‘You dragon-fly, understand that there is no point in your engaging with all the lovely flowers. You better go to the Lord of Tirukkannapuram, who once appeared as a turtle in the deep sea encircling the earth, and suck the fragrance from His ‘Tulasi malai’ and blow it over me.’

‘The Lord is the Supreme help personified. He is present as the beautiful divine idol. Go and get the fragrance from His ‘Tulasi malai’, You dragon-fly, and blow it over me.

The Lord has vowed to trample opposing / inimical forces, as He has ‘Shriahpathithva’ (being with Mahalakshmi eternally). Get the fragrance from His Tulasi malai and blow it on me - that garland is the symbol of His protective trait; He, as Rama had a broad chest, was the father of Manmatta, and is residing at Tirukkannapuram.

You dragon-fly, do not waste your time searching for madhu (nectar) in flowers all over; just go to the death personified to enemies, the Lord, suck the fragrance from His Tulasi malai and blow it on me ; He is in Tirukkannapuram. The Lord as Krishna, everyone’s desire personified, destroyed the seven bulls / went as messenger to Duryodhana / whose foot destroyed the asura impersonating a calf / who hurled a wood-apple tree on an asura, is in Tirukkannapuram. Get the fragrance from His Tulasi malai and blow it on me.

The azwar, in the process of describing the nectar in the garland worn by the Lord lost His consciousness, as it were. Assuming Nayaki’s form and addressing the dragon-fly, the azwar sings experiencing the Lord - that itself is the gain /benefit is apparent in this decad!

## 8.5 Thandhai Kalil: Tirukkannapuram (5)

The messengers, dragon-flies sent by the Nayaki, did not return after they left. Subject to pangs of separation she was in the same boat as gopikas who did not see Krishna in the night. The one who flung open the fetters put by someone else (Kamsa) is not doing anything for the fetters He Himself put; will He not help me? If the amrutha itself becomes poison what can one do! The soft breeze did not even have sympathy for a person subject to sorrow.

The Nayaki is enamoured of the beauty of Lord’s divine idol which is bright. Getting lost in it, the heart was not willing to return. There was none from the same town. The ways of separation aggravated.

Saying that ‘I will not leave you’, Krishna had left the gopika, whose bangles slid being loose. His playing of flute seems to finish off one!

The mahout keeps pricking the elephant as it is always filled with ‘madha’. The kings who ride such elephants, who and wearing thick anklets in the legs indicating their strength, are drawn through the sound of Panchajanya, and got killed by its chest-breaking sound. That great Lord has not come to my rescue now! The day is coming to a close; the discus wielder who pierced seven trees and destroyed Lanka for Sita Devi, has shown His grace (by not coming!) to us we have no help, sister, alas, Sister.

Holding His bow and threatening the ocean, He used fire - wreaking arrows to raze down Lanka. Such a great and brave one He is; He is not coming. My large eyes are not getting sleep; the night seems to be longer than an era!

While once He did churning of the sea for His consort, for the same one He built a bridge too! That brave one has not come. I am hearing the clang of bells indicating that cows are returning home. The flute play is accentuating my pangs. When will my pangs end?

Those, who should have kept brahmins in front viz. Kshathriyas started being source of insult. Jamadagni (brahmin) was one of them. Parasurama went round twentyone times to kill them all with His axe. It appears to me that it is better to spend time in hell rather than wait for Him.

In the foregoing, the pains that gopikas were subject to were described. Now the azwar as ‘devi’ says: ‘The Lord of strong-walled Tirukkannapuram gave me heart’s delight in my dreams, and this haunts me resulting in my bangles slipping from my hands. The fearless seven bulls’ neck bells are making my heart weak. The ‘Coo’ sounds of ‘Anril’ birds is bothering my heart - they seem to finish me off.’

These, Kaliyan’s sweet Tamil songs, resembling the lament - filled expressions of coiffured gopikas aspiring for the Lord’s feet, will, on being recited, enable them to enter Sri Vaikunta and live with celestials.

## 8.6 Thondeer: On Tirukkannapuram (6)

Waiting even overnight looked like an era to the azwar in Nayaki ‘bhava’: ‘The sun has not risen yet. Neither is there anyone for company pines Nayaki. The Lord says ‘it is not right when you say that. Am I not residing in Tirukkannapuram. If you remember that then there will be no disappointment.’ The azwar is consoled at having got a way to approach Him.

The two clevis, Sridevi and Bhoodevi are in the chest of the Lord and are constantly trying to ensure that the Lord does not pay much heed to ‘aashrithas’ ’ blemishes by drawing His attention with their beauty.

However much one behaves friendly with the demons, they will by nature not adjust. After the death of Mali in the war, the rakshasas who survived combined with those in Lanka and hid themselves in the Pathalaloka. Sri Rama used His bow to eliminate them.

The Lord as Sri Rama humbled Ravana who thought he was quite safe in the fortress like Lanka surrounded all sides by water. The Lord allowed him to go back when his bow and chariot were destroyed. Rama at a young age itself achieved success - He killed Thataki. He won over Parashurama and got the bow, before which He saved the ‘Yaaga’ conducted by Vishwamithra. The Lord of that fame resides here.

Rama humbled the ocean. Utilising the Kainkarya of (thiryak) Monkeys etc. (just like He gets kainkarya done by Anantha, Garuda etc) He fought the war and killed Ravana, whose major crime was separating the Lord and His consort who are always together. The one who achieved the marvel of building a bridge in the ocean, resides in Tirukkannapuram.

The avataras prior to Sri Rama, viz. Hayagreeva, Koorma Narasimha and Hamsa related to Vidya etc., received offerings in ‘Yaagas’ conducted by brahmins. As Rama He cut off Ravana’s heads / hands. As the father of Kaama, who entices the world with beauty, Krishna, resides in this shrine.

Getting engrossed in own interest, the heart is not appreciating Lord’s greatness in eliminating the enemies. After all having a bagfull of sins, how can one at all stay without being in sorrow. Those who took refuge in the Lord are not troubled by sins. Although Ravana’s misdeeds could have affected Vibheeshana, disregarding that aspect the Lord gave him refuge as also Lanka. The azwar consoles his heart: ‘Thinking about the old sins and fearing one’s impending lot (gathi), do not pine. The Lord has eliminated powerful enemies like Poothana, Arjuna trees etc. He will ward off inimical forces. The Lord, even risking Himself, saves ‘aashrithas’.

The azwar having received the superlative grace from Mahalakshmi is reputed as ‘Arulmari’. Tirukkannapuram has high-rise buildings providing hospitality to water-bearing clouds! Those who recite these ten ‘pasuras’ will get all prosperity and attain Paramapadham.

## 8.7 Viyamudai: Tirukkannapuram (7)

The azwar avers that Paramapadam will be attained as Prapya, the Supreme Being, is residing there. Without any distinction of Brahma and those following, He obliges all and has come to reside in Tirukkannapuram and therefore that divya desha is available to us.

Just as though he is breaking a wet earthen pot He humbled the hill-size seven bulls to win the hand of Nappinnai and He resides in Tirukkannapuram which is uniquely beautiful with ponds where swans abound, and walk in an attractive style imitating lovely womens’ gait.

He hit one Arjuna tree against another so that both the demons in disguise fell dead. The festival in Tirukkannapuram where He resides, draws huge crowds who worship Him and celebrate: How could Tirukkannapuram be final abode for us, since in this Leela Vibhoothi there is sorrow and suffering all over. The impossible feats have been achieved (by the Lord here); it becomes ’Prapya’. Didn’t He hold Govardhana Hills as an umbrella for cowherds? Further He aspires for things like butter touched by devotees, and Mahalakshmi has kindly chosen this place and therefore for the Supreme Being it is an eternal abode.

When the azwar is told: ‘We are not as innocent as gopis nor have such unique love towards Him’, the azwar replies ‘that the Lord will grant those desiring His refuge and we need not have fear on that count’. One need not be apprehensive that he does not have the necessary feeling of love while having ‘darshan’. The Lord has axe which is obedient to Him. When asked thus: ‘We are feeling ashamed since so far we had not shown interest in Him’, the azwar replies : even then He will not leave us. He is omnipresent. Without thinking of our past history we can approach Him. In this shrine He is present along with Mahalakshmi. He measured the entire earth and has made both moving / non-moving things His insignia (Ankitha) to Him.

The Lord accompanied by Mahalakshmi standing on one side and Bhoodevi on the other, resides in Tirukkannapuram where the cargo boats carrying the expensive articles crowd the shore.

The azwar, has sung these songs in praise of the Lord residing in Tirukkannapuram which is even greater than Paramapadham.

## 8.8 Vaanor: Tirukkannapuram (8)

The azwar lauds Tirukkannapuram for having instilled knowledge and although the Lord had been wandering from beginningless time to get us, we, did not understand Him and the entire experience’s effort is getting fulfilled in the form of this holy shrine. Tirukkannapuram has helped in removal of obstacles, providing the route and to reach the goal (The Supreme Being).

The ocean, meant to provide protection, itself rose to the heights of ‘deva loka’ threatening to drown the houses, built by the Lord, for Brahma and others, and the Lord in ‘Matsya’ manifestation drank it up and brought it under control. His eyes were lauded as resembling a Lotus. He further assumed ‘Koorma’ form. Using Mandara mountains, churned the oceans until ‘Amrutha’ appeared. As Varaha, He lifted the Mother Earth from the deep ocean. The poet in azwar says: If we can get a glimpse of the beautiful ‘Varaha moorthy’ ‘it does not matter whether there are thousand deluges!’ Immediately the azwar thinks of Narasimha avataara. The ego – ‘I’ and ‘mine’ lead to further births; the one filled with ego, Hiranyakashipu was humbled by the Lord with His nail.

In order to keep me (azwar) under His holy Feet, He took the Vamana avataara. His appearance was so attractive that no one missed it. He helped His ‘aashrithas’ in this avataara by begging. Those, who used to be a problem to His ‘aashrithas’, were eliminated in His going round twenty one times with His axe as Parashuraama - He is present in Tirukkannapuram.

As Rama, He helped us to laud His being a Master (Seshi) all the time - He took avataara to assist those who worshipped and those who did not pray to Him. The ego-filled Ravana, protected (!) by the seas, was eliminated by the Lord. Balarama, Krishna’s elder brother, always sporting a plough sent to ‘veeraswarga’ ego-filled kings.The Lord as Krishna, reduced the load of lakhs of demons for the mother earth through a war planned by Him - He is residing in Tirukkannapuram where there are pearls on one side of the sea and red paddy plants are waving like fan.

The azwar experienced all the ten avataaras of the Lord. By reciting these honey-filled ‘pasuras’, one can ward off all sins.

## 8.9 Kaimmanam: Tirukkannapuram (9)

‘I am a servant / servitor to the Lord, who is like a blue mountain, residing in Tirukkannapuram, and to no one else’ says the azwar. While the last decad was an effort to acquire jnana, the present decad deals with his being a ‘krithaartha’ through ‘ananya seshathva’ (service only to the Lord).

The proud elephant Gajendra, could intelligently use its front leg. The one which thought the question of fear will never arise had to pray to the Lord during a predicament. He helped - He is a ‘paramopakaraka’! The Lord is a giver of everything (Kalpatharu). He gives even without asking! ‘I shall not be a ‘Sesha’ to anyone excepting to the Lord who humbled seven bulls for winning Nappinnai; held His Sudarshana to create dusk artificially in war, He built a bridge using ‘Thiryanks’ (monkeys / bears etc) - this Tirukannapuram where He resides is an ‘extraordinary’ Vibhoothi !’

The Lord is sugar-personified (so enjoyable), all-powerful. His greatness cannot be assessed even by Vedas which are themselves a measure / eternal / not authored by man. If the Lord were to query the azwar as to what help I’ve rendered to you, for you to be engrossed in me? ; The azwar would reply ‘You came searching for me; You entered my heart and could there be a bigger help?’ - ‘You became my Master (Swami). Just once only I thought of Him. Seeing me drowned in the sea of ‘samsaara’, He said, ‘when I am there, why fear?’ He is the only close friend I have - is there any one who is a protector other than Him?

Jumping from one birth to another I found Him in this life itself. I have no further birth. Neither is there any need for ‘upakara smrithi’! He helped in this manner.

The azwar felt an intense need to experience the Lord in various holy shrines simultaneously, but he could not remove eyes His from Tirukkannapuram! Right here the Lord who gives His darshan to ‘Nithyasooris’ can be experienced by everyone.

Tirumangai azwar urges everyone to recite these ten pasurams, dance and live prosperously.

## 8.10 Vandaar: On Tirukkannapuram (9)

While the azwar has been doing ‘vachaka’ kainkarya (that is, reciting etc.), the Lord queries whether he wants anything more.

Sri Mahalakshmi lives in a lotus which can expand as required. The azwar wants to do kainkaryam to the united couple, the Lord and Lakshmi. The Lord who resides in Kannapuram brought out four Vedas. ‘Exulting in You in many ways my heart seeks to do kainkaryam to You. Please tell (‘shollu’) what You want me to do further?’ The Vedas propound that the goal and route to reach it, are the Lord Himself.

The Lord trying to see azwar’s reaction queries: When there are so many with whom one can take refuge, why Me alone?! ‘Let the people of the world do what they want, but I can think of You only’ the azwar declares. He saves us from deluge, I don’t think there is a similar ‘God’ to come and say I am refuge-worthy.

‘Who gave you this religious practice (anustaana)’ asks the Lord. The azwar reminds Him of His first teaching (ashtakshari). When the Lord tells him that it has lot of connotations, the azwar replies saying he was concerned only with the connotation pertaining to ‘ananyaarha seshathva’ (meaning that azwar is subservient to Narayana only) which is preceded by Bhagavatha seshathva. ‘Is this not what You taught me?’ He knew the Lord will not mistake if bhagavathas precede the Lord. This is a prapanna’s ‘Lakshana’. The Lord avers when Sridevi is there as Purushakaara for My work where is short-coming? She saves a chethana from harsh punishment for his misdeeds by Her attractive glances towards the Lord.

The azwar elaborates Lord’s protective quality by saying that he cannot count on anyone to protect him and that is why he has fallen on His feet. ‘Please show Your protective grace. Do not look at my sins. Just accept me. Let those, whom You have delegated to punish, dare not come near me. In fact looking at You in Ksheerasamudra, they will run like thieves.’

‘When you have loads of sins why should those who should deal with you, go away stealthily’ asks the Lord. The azwar replies: ‘Because You save the aashrithas from their enemies and due to the love You have for them, I could see my sins disappearing!’

‘How were the enemies eliminated and what did you get?’ The Lord queries the azwar: who replies thus: ‘You first made me subservient (daasa) to You. The inner Lord’s (Antharyami), blessing resulted in complete elimination of my sins / enemies, and as a result of that I acquired kainkaryasri through ‘vaachaka’ (oral) means. You made me recite sthothras and You are Mukkottai (this term, when uttered by Malayalam poets, inspires them to give out their poetry freely).

The Lord’s glory can be heard from Vedas, but can be seen and experienced in Tirukkannapuram. He says that if one lauds the Supreme Lord the same as he did that in these ten songs, will achieve the same ‘anugraham’ from the Lord as received by the azwar.

## Ninth Patthu

## 9.1 Vangamaamunneer: Tirukkannangudi

The azwar had desired to visit all ‘divya deshas’ and the Lord asks him to do so and sing his verses. Further He declared that He will take him to Paramapadham on his death.

The Lord is reclining on Adisesha, who has different types of lines (rekhas), in ksheerabhdhi. Adisesha is long and wide enough for the Lord to relax. Bedecked with Lotus on His navel and Panchajanya in one hand, the Lord is residing in Tirukkannangudi where the brahmins are proficient in Vedas. Gajendra egoistically entered a pond which was not his area but crocodile’s and when he was unsuccessful in getting his leg released from the crocodile’s clutches it thought of the Lord who used Sudarshana Chakra to save it. He also saved the Universe from deluge. As Mathsya He saved the world from drinking the oceanic waters. Further as Varaha He saved the mother earth. As Krishna He reduced the burden of the mother earth by planning the Mahabharatha war - He became a sarathy to Arjuna.

In the ‘mahayajna’ conducted by Mahabali wherein he had promised to give anything to anyone, the Lord assuming Vamana avataara got the entire world within His two feet. It did not demean His being the Lord. As Parashurama He went round the world twenty one times to kill the Kshathriyas who were troubling others.

Ravana had created fear to Indra and other devatas. His Lanka was protected by the sea on all sides. Due to boons he had held, the ten heads, were very strong but the Lord, made them fall like the fruits in a bunch. For Him to get relaxed through song and dance, Tirukkannangudi offered the right spot.

Duryodhana, conceited as he was, had arranged an artificial chair for the Lord who went as messenger to sit but the Lord grew in size enormously and set at nought Duryodhana’s plan to insult the Lord. The Lord lost no time in getting the wicked Kauravas into war and as Arjuna’s sarathy reduced the burden for mother Earth! The Lord is residing in Tirukkannamgudi shrine where contrary to popular belief that the bees will die in ‘champaka’ flowers, they make merry in champaka flowers here. Those who recite these ten songs of Tirumangai Azwar will become very close to the Lord.

## 9.2 Ponnivar meni: On Tirunagai

Having lauded for His non-freedom with respect to ‘aashrithas’ (aashritha paratantrya) which draws Him to undertake various feats of protection, the azwar comes to Tirunagai to experience Him. He did not get to do kainkaryam as he wished. Caught in the web of His beauty, he assumes the Nayaki stance.

The Lord’s idol appears as though it is golden; a deeper look shows it to have the emerald’s brilliance. He is a Vedic seer. Seeing me, His eyes noticed my slender waist and my tender bosom. She is scared that her mother might have noticed this attention and that ‘I approve of it’ she tells her friend, who asks her as to whether she ascertained His origin. Nayaki replies ‘He was like Aravamudhar’. ‘Any identifying signs?’ The Nayaki says ‘He has Sudarshana and Panchajanya in His hands just like what you find in Tirukundanthai. He is so enchanting that young women will fall for Him. The beauty was such that it was not possible to describe, but one feels amazed’.

On further querying she says ‘He was like Pundarikaksha’. He resides in Tirumeyyam which is surrounded by bamboos and dense jungles.

The Nayaki and her friend talk about Sudarshana Chakra and the Tulasi garland which is wafting its fragrance. He cannot be mixed with other men. When there is no comparison possible how can any one compare.

He appears to resemble Gopala Krishna residing in Uraiyoor and Mudhurai His hands are as huge as mountains. Looking at His beauty, one would sing His glory: ‘Pallandu, Pallandu’.

She thinks that He is like some king who broke the tusks of Kuvalayapeeda, and attracting women. 'She cannot say whether He is one who entices women or He is under their control. He talks through His eyes!’ says Nayaki. Her friend says ‘You seem to have been charmed by Krishna’s eyes and words. You don’t appear to have seen Him. It may be your imagination. Since I am supposed to assist you both in bringing together, how then, without my knowledge He came?’ Nayaki replies ‘that due to His complete grace, He must have got control of my heart’. When this conversation was going on between Nayaki and her friend, other friends and Nayaki’s mother came to ask what was being talked about. They tried to obstruct further talks, when the Lord appeared on His Garuda. Looking at the spectacle, the Nayaki exults: ‘Did He come from Southern Tirumalai, or Neermalai ; come everyone and witness’.

The hills of Tirumalirumjolai are touching the clouds! The Lord of that shrine, not leaving me even for a moment, came Himself. He also appears to resemble the Lord at Tiruneer malai. You all, who wanted to obstruct me, see for yourselves. Is it possible to leave Him because of you all.

He is indeed an ‘apathsaka’ (friend who eliminates one’s difficulties). I do not understand His wonders. As cowherd child He swallowed the worlds and slept on a fig leaf. With eyes and lips like fragrant lotus, He is fit to be visited by deserving ones. He resides in Tirunagai shrine - His supreme looks are such, those who have delved into it, they can never leave Him.

## 9.3 Thannai neivikkilen: On Pullani (1)

The Lord comes to Nayaki who had been in a trance after describing His beauty. The Lord left her after His union with her allowing her to ‘feel’ it. He did not return and this made her think of visiting despite her coyness. Despite her sakhi (friend) desisting her, the Nayaki, subject to pangs of separation, decides to go.

I cannot think of ‘tit for tat’ with Him, although I can make Him get disappointed. Since this thing (jeevaathma) is His own, there is nothing to be done except going to Him. I am not Sita waiting endlessly for His coming. I should be at that place where I will not be subject to separation from Him. What is the point in expecting someone to know and keep crying. It is more difficult to forget than keep remembering and let us see if by going there it is easy to forget Him ; ‘whether it is sorrow or progress, one has to keep Him in front’ says the Nayaki. He said that He will not leave me. Is it one of the things associated with Samslesha (union).

The friend of Nayaki asks her ‘Is it possible even without me knowing it, He came and had samslesha with you. And why are you getting ready to go? Do not go’. The Nayaki replies: ‘When I was with Him, the bees were drinking nectar; and alas, there is no witness, I am so sinned. After our ‘samslesha, He said if we part, I cannot bear the pain; He said so. ‘May be He said so that I survive. She asks her friend to accompany her to the shrine where He resides. I cannot keep remembering Him, because it gives rise to ‘*Thaapa*’; if He and I are not enjoined by love, it feels that both are finished.’

To a query by the friend that she could spend time remembering His aspects like beauty, the Nayaki says ‘those very aspects make me get engrossed and therefore there is no alternative but to go. His samslesha with me was so powerful that I had better go without bothering about any restrictions.’ She tells her friend to go with her so that she could visit the shrine (Tirupullani) and pass time - for this there will be no criticism. It is inkeeping with our swaroopa (essential attribute). He will also not mistake that.’

Even if He had pretended as having love, it will be wrong not to do service to Him as per our swaroopa (being subservient to the Lord). Doing service itself is the great benefit. Let us do whatever knowledgeable people do - recite His names; if He does not come we will call Him loudly. Any such initiative will not be without good result!

‘Tirupullaani’, the azwar says ‘is abundant in pearl, corals and lotus flowers, cool groves and is a delightful shrine.’

## 9.4 Kaavaar: Tirupullani (2)

Although the Nayaki intended to go to Tirupullaani, the thought just stayed in mind. The situation that she is not able to go to the place she wanted baffles her. Add to this the sweet words of love of ‘anril’ birds; they are like piercing with a sword; these anril birds have made this their home! The friend is now addressed as ‘pavai’ (doll) rather than ‘thozi’. The Nayaki finds this ‘pavai’ experiencing more suffering at the separation of Nayaki and the Nayaka.

Experiencing pain has become a habit to the Nayaki, who is not the type to stay without action. She sends birds as messengers asking them to convey to Him her plight. He is in Nithyavibhoothi beyond the three worlds which He measured. I have fallen for the Supreme God. The local birds are requested to fulfil her job addressing them as ‘pul’, which is a ‘Thiryak’, she is reminded of Ramavathara, wherein the thiryaks (monkeys, bears etc.) did service to bring Rama and Sita together; she tells the birds just to inform, and there was no need for a reply.

She now tells the birds to convey her mentally-upset-state. Seeing the Tulasi garland on Him, she told her mind to worship, but the mind / heart left her to accompany Him?

‘Hiranya’, she says ‘never thought of Supreme ruler of the world or the need to attain Him. Ego-driven Hiranya thought there was none above himself! The Lord helped Prahlada, a lad, by eliminating Hiranya, but did not bother to wipe the tears of a helpless woman.’!

The relatives and friends had a dig at her saying that ‘whatever they said came out true and what He said was proved false. You listen to us at least now’. Nayaki replies saying that let my mind / heart come back so that their advice could be acted upon.’ Looking at His history during Ramavathara, His words ought to be true. The mind / heart which has behind Him will not return. Neither can she give up her kith and kin, nor listen to them. We have nothing to gain from truth or lie of local people. ‘Since the Lord at Tirupullani is lie-filled, He is appearing attractive to me!’ The one who should give assurance, Himself lied and went away. He went away in the chariot and disappeared - the reference here to Sri Krishna who went away in a chariot and the gopis just kept gazing - even the ‘coolness’ personified seems to have changed his habit and he is finishing me off. Even if I were to get inside, the bells tied to the bulls and the ocean’s sound are filling my ears with fire’ says the Nayaki.

Neither could I reach His shrine, nor deal with the powerful inimical forces, supposed to be friendly to her like the Moon. If queried as to whether it is right to criticise Him, He being the benefactor of the entire universe, the Nayaki replies: ‘The one who is the creator of the Universe with Sun, the Moon, Heaven etc. and made us familiar with the “eyes of the world” viz. the Vedas and showing the medium / route to attain Him as propounded in them; no one expected or wished for these but the one we desired He has not done!

The Lord resides in Tirupullani which enjoys the spring all round the year. Those who recite these ten verses will live without anything lacking, here as also in Paramapadham.

## 9.5 Thavalavijam: Tirukkurungudi (1)

When, sending messengers did not meterialise, the Nayaki requests that she be taken to Tirukkurungudi so that she can undertake ‘surrendering her soul to the Lord’. The soul is of knowledge form and its ‘swaroopa’ is to be subservient to the Lord. It is like dirt- stained jewel having collected sins from beginningless time. Conveying its helplessness it has to surrender to the Lord. Just aspiring for a thing led to the entire town going against and the Nayaki counts amongst the virodhis (inimical elements), the Moon, the ocean, the cool breeze and ‘anril’ birds. Instead of being in Nayaka’s hands, she is caught in the hands of the ‘virodhis’.

When queried that there were people like her and since they were not behaving like her, that is, asking to be taken to the shrine, and these people will think otherwise, the Nayaki says: ‘Those who do not know (my condition), let them think what they want; they are ignorant of pangs (of separation).”

It is difficult to spend even mornings. He is too Supreme. He will not be affected by these. We ourselves have to surge forward to get Him! In His creation there are lot of things which lead to suffering. Answering her friend it is not possible to console the mind/heart. ‘The bells in the neck of bulls bother me a lot - my heart is weakening and I am sleepless’. Asked as to whether the bells alone are bothering, she adds to the list Krishna’s flute play, evening time, early Moon; - carrying further, as comparison she says, the extent of sweetness of Lord’s flute is just equal to the suffering caused by the moon.

If you were to be taken to the shrine, the women will question about your womanliness (sthreethva). Let them criticise. They may say that disregarding ‘sthreethva’ she is not able to forget Him. So saying they would have recited His name. The friends question her ‘How did You enjoy His company? How did you separate? Why should you be grieving over the separation?’ ‘On the one hand the beauty of His frame (body) and on the other His eyes, and words will not be able to describe them.’

‘Does His leaving you mean that He has no love for You?’ ask her friends! Nayaki replies ‘He must be thinking that she cannot be finished with flute play. It should happen with My arrows. That manmatha hit those arrows mercilessly. He has achieved it - I am engrossed in Him. Take me to Him.

Nayaki says ‘He plundered my ‘sthreethva’ and left. He did not even tell to which place He was going’. “Despite the senior wife being there, when He willed my heart, He has not moved His feet, - He has not gone to Her, because His grace towards us is limitless" says the azwar. He is the purpose of all his senses. Take me to Tirukkurungudi says the azwar.

## 9.6 Akkum Puliyin: Tirukkurungudi (2)

Since the azwar did not have enough strength in the legs he asked those who were strong to take him. When he perceived that Sri Mahalakshmi’s proximity was there, he received strength in his legs. He is now eager to go along with Srivaishnavas.

The Lord likes everyone. His receptive manners (here, standing stance) seem to have passed to even trees and even little children can climb them. In this holy town, the baby crane perches on a tree branch to receive from its mother, the ‘velliraa’ fish which is just that big to be eaten by the baby crane. The azwar is reminded of milky ocean when he talks about the reclining stance - ocean’s waves come and touch His feet. His shrine is Kurungudi where there are lotus flowers and swans with red feet and they ‘nestle with their mates.’

We have found a way to live without suffering - devotees come and see. Mahavaraha’s mien is like cool cloud’s and just seeing Him will ward off all our worries. He is residing in Tirukkurungudi - the Lord is so powerful that once Ravana’s ten heads rolled. Here in this town the bumble-bees draw nectar from flowers both during day and night.

The Lord disregarding his stepping down from His status, eliminated ‘aashrithas’ ‘opponents’, as in Mahabharatha war - He became a sarathy to Arjuna. The parrots in this shrine want to acquire from the women with bewitching eyes the fluency to talk.

‘What should one do at this shrine?’ devotees ask the azwar. ‘In order to worship the Lord you, adhikaris, bring incense, flowers, sandal powder etc. and do ‘anjali’ to the Lord. By referring to the devotees as ‘adhikaris’ the azwar means those with ‘kainkarya ruchee’. ‘This worship leads to involvement in matters pertaining to the Lord. Because of our link to Him, He will accept us. You reach Tirukkurungudi and undertake worship’ says the azwar, continuing he says: both types of sins ‘Prarabhdha’ as well as ‘sanchitha’ will get destroyed if, with sincere devotion, we pray to the Lord.

While as Rama, the Lord humbled and eliminated the ego-filled Ravana, He as Krishna, with mere hands plucked out tusks from the uncontrollable Kuvalayaapeeda. For the sake of those who came after these eons (yugas), He has come to Tirukkurungudi. Reciting these pasurams will eliminate sins committed knowingly.

## 9.7 Tandhai Thai: Tiruvallavai

Having advised others to give up worldly attractions and reach the holy shrine of the Lord, the azwar reviews his own position: ‘I am still subject to samsaric links and I am not able to distance myself from that all. Please, O Lord, shower Your grace on me so that I can utter the name of the shrine “Tiruvallavai”.’

When there is the real ‘bandhu’ viz. the Lord, one mistakes the transitory relatives for real and carries with the journey of ‘samsara’. Addressing his heart, the azwar asks it to utter the Lord’s abode ‘Tiruvallavai’ with ‘bhakthi’ and sincere heart. But he feels that his physical beauty is such it attracts women and that his heart should desist from it and lose interest in it. It is also possible that the women pretend to have extraordinary love towards you and you will imagine it to be true:- if you will see through their false interest in ‘bhagavadvishayam’, you will learn to laud the glory of Tiruvallavai shrine which happens to be the abode of the Lord who strode into Mahabali’s sacrifice (Yajna), and asked for three feet of land.’ If one can see through the insincerity of women of ‘sweet nothings’, one could give up wicked intentions and extol the Lord in Venkatadri which is comparable to Paramapadham.

Having talked about worldly pleasures, the azwar advises one to give up interest in wealth as it is not permanent, and enlist the Lord’s grace by going to the shrine where He is residing - also if one fears the suffering arising out of sense organs, one could overcome that fear by going to the shrine where the Lord resides permanently.

If one believes wrongly that the corporal frame is eternal, it is untrue and unwise. If you understand this aspect, hurry to reach the ‘divya desham’ where the Lord resides.

Made up of ‘Panchabhoothas’, one should realise, this body does not protect us. ‘Understand this clearly and live happily’ says the azwar. Perfectly matched Mahalakshmi and the Supreme soul (Paramaathma) reside eternally in this shrine and the Lord has appeared in whatever way He could. To be able to attain Him and the shrine where He resides one should perceive the untenableness of heretic faiths (e.g. Bouddha, Jain faiths). ‘Being proficient in Vedas and not subject to any wants, the brahmins reside in Tiruvallavai which the Lord has made it His home. Those who recite this decad will control others here, and after death attain Paramapadham’ says the azwar.

## 9.8 Mundura: Tirumalirumjolai

Despite the azwar telling his heart to reach Tiruvallavai it didn’t reach that shrine. His urgency was greater than His heart’s. Wanting to experience the Lord, he set out to go to Tirumalirumjolai. ‘Give up your mental disposition that without union with women, the resultant disappointment is unbearable. The very embodiment of seven oceans and seven islands is residing in Tirumalirumjolai; He is being worshipped by ‘Nithyasooris’, ‘devathas’ with water and flowers; He is lying on Adisesha; He will grant one’s wants; the sorrow is always increasing in one’s person / frame and if you want to eliminate the life in the body, you should seek Him; He measured the worlds with His tender feet.’

Just as He satisfies your wants, so does He eliminate the bad elements in your life; He eliminated the Poothana with poisonous breast milk, while He did not do the killing of a woman here, He did kill ‘Thataki’ with the aim of protecting the ‘aashrithas’. The one who was boasting that even if he were to be split into two, he will not die, Ravana was killed by Rama, and his ten heads rolled on the ground; the torso danced furiously as though it was ‘possessed’. The Lord’s bravery was revealed to the world. He resides in this shrine.

As Krishna who reclines on Adisesha (characterised by special venom which he spills on demons) left His soft bed for a while; He threw a calf (a demon) against woodapple tree (another demon), killing both; He danced in ‘Rasa Kreede’ with cowherd-women. He resides in Malirumjolai.

As Balarama, He threw Dhenuka against a palm tree and killed Him. Krishna ‘ripped the jaws of the white horse, Keshi. The Lord protects His ‘aashrithas’ from getting into the clutches of heretic faiths (like Bouddhas / Jains) who do not respect Vedas. He resides in Tirumalirumjolai where there is abundance of blossoms offering nectar to the bees. Those who recite these ten ‘pasuras’ are indeed extremely fortunate.

## 9.9 Moovaril: On Tirumalirumjolai (2)

The Lord of the first-cause reclines on Adisesha in the milky ocean in order to enable devatas to listen to their pleas - The one who creates through His navel also protects them all keeping them in the stomach during deluge. He, as Krishna, protected gopalakas lifting Govardhana. He resides in Tirumalirumjolai. The mother of the Nayaki worries as to when her daughter who is endowed with the beauty such as to deserve Him will unite with Him. Will she see Kannan, the Lord?

The Lord reclines on a fig leaf in the Milky ocean keeping the Universe in the stomach. The beauty of the Lord in idol form is such as to remove the fatigue of the devotees. He lives in this shrine where the elephants are so strong as to take on the lions. My daughter, who matches Him equally, should join Him.

As Narasimha He saved Prahlada from Hiranyakashipu, splitting him easily. The pleasure of saving was seen clearly in the Lord’s eyes. He is indeed supreme. As Vamana, He grew into huge form touching the worlds with His feet as soon as ‘dhana dhare’ fell on His hands. He resides in Titumalirumjolai.

Only those who love Him get to know Him. There are those who do not even mention His name. The Lord split asunder Bakasura’s beaks. He killed Kuvalayapeeda of huge size darting to fight the Lord. Shakatasura in disguise was in smithereens on being hit by Krishna. As sarathy to Arjuna, He received the arrows shot at Arjuna to save him.

After experiencing Venkatadri, the azwar prays to ‘Azhahar’ of Tirumalirumjolai where He gives His darshan to ‘samsaris’ and Nithyasooris without distinction. Apsaras’ walking-sound, known as Noopura, seems to be imitated by ‘Noopura Ganga’ which is flowing there. With one’s own effort He cannot be seen. Coming on the back of Garuda, He is revealing Himself in Tirumalirunjolai.

The doubt ‘will the Nayaki be able to see the Lord’ will remain with the azwar, but those reciting this decad will achieve ‘Praapthi’.

## 9.10 Yengal Em Irai: On Tirukkottiyoor

The Lord who is always present in devotees’ hearts helps them to overcome difficulties just as Prahladha had got Lord’s help. He facilitates His devotees to utter His names with love and is never separated from their mind / heart. He is present in Tirukkottiyoor with His consorts with the specific purpose of the latter providing ‘purushakaara’. In respective eons (yugas) He assumes the colours liked by His devotees: White in Krutha, blue in Dwapara and black in Kaliyuga ; he comes to the rescue of His devotees, like saving them during deluge. The beauty of Tirukkottiyoor compares favourably with that of the Lord Himself.

Not only has He given a place to Sri Mahalakshmi but also to Rudra (therefore - ‘Sheelavaan’). He measured the worlds as Trivikrama. The one who helps His ‘aashrithas’ is so powerful that He humbled Ravana. When He saw the deceitful Vathsasura, He simply threw him on Kapitthasura, also in disguise, and both were killed. He lifted the Govardhana mountain for the sake of cowherds. The Lord is in Tirukkottiyoor where the fragrance of flowers fills the air - this makes Him ‘Sarvagandah’ Swami.

The Lord who killed so many enemies of ‘aashrithas’ is present in the heart. Without considering His Supreme status, He has entered a samsaari’s heart! For the sake of brahmins charged with devotion, who are able to vie with Brahma himself, He is present in Tirukkottiyoor.

Reciting these ten verses on Tirukkottiyoor will ensure Paramapadham to the devotees.

## Tenth Patthu

## 10.1 Orunal shuttram: On several holy cities

The Lord residing in Tirukkottiyoor blessed the azwar and the urge to do daily kainkaryam remained steady. Preparing himself to leave for Paramapadham, he decided to visit other divine places (shrines) and as the first one he arrives at Tiruneermalai: Having had darshan of the Lord here, he says, let us go to Tirukkannamangai.

‘One cannot find a more congenial friend than the Lord. Everything is attainable in this place. Neither mother, nor father, nor relatives can be as helpful to one as to themselves. He is my soul. Besides providing purushaarthas here, He will also present Himself at my death’ says the azwar.

The Lord is resplendent like a lightning in His ‘archa’ form. He made me subservient to Him - He is the Controller and ‘Prakaari’. ‘Let us, having seen Him in Tirumalai, go to Tirutthankaa and worship Him there’ says the azwar. Having experienced Him in standing pose, he thinks of His reclining stance on a fig leaf in the Milky ocean as a baby - it is beyond description. It is as sweet as Milk or Amrutha. The Lord is wearing a Tulasi garland which is the insignia of His being the Supreme Lord, He who is not putting restriction on anyone to come and worship Him, is residing at Thirunangoor.

The Lord only can ward off our enemies, like He did for Prahladha by killing Hiranyakashipu. We can see Him in Tirupper. ‘Let us now go to Tiruvellarai and pray to Him’.

‘We saw the Lord who grants benefits to those seeking them in Tiruchherai and now let us proceed to Tirukkudanthai to have His darshan. He is the one who brought out Amrutha. Just like He gives Himself to Nithyasooris so also for me, He brought Himself to me. He killed Kuvalayaapeeda to make life happy for devathas and showed Himself to be ‘Seshi’ to them. I will pray to Him at Tirukkudanthai’ says the azwar.

The azwar recalls Lord’s congenial nature in granting ‘amrutha’ to the ‘devathas’ and eating the butter which had the touch of gopikas, in Tiruvaznendhur. Here on let us go to ‘Tiruvenka’ to see Him reclining on the Adhisesha.

The Lord is the sustenance for His ‘aashrithas’ whom the azwar saw in Tirumalai. He is always perceptible to the soul and the primary cause of the Universe. If He is the sustainer (Dharaka) to the ‘ashrithas’, He is also the destroyer of those inimical to the ‘aashrithas’. The suffering caused to His ashrithas by Kuvalayaapeeda was returned to the uncontrollable elephant by the Lord by pulling out its tusks which were used as a weapon to kill Kamsa and others, and gave a chance to His ‘aashrithas’ to rejoice by dancing.

When one gets instruction of Thirumanthra, it opens the avenue to know about shaastras which are embedded in these verses as a treasure leading to the goal ‘na cha punaravarthathe’ - this will enable eternal experience without any break at the Paramapadham with the Nithyasooris.

## 10.2 Irakkam Inri: Pongattham Pongo (a dance song of the vanquished Rakshasas)

Having visited the Lord’s eternal abode at the shrines the azwar now turns to recount the Lord’s ‘avataara specialities’. Sri Rama’s unique victory is recounted in the words of rakshasas - the azwar assumes Nayaki stance.

While one worships ‘Divya dampathis’ Sita and Rama, Ravana on the other hand chose to separate them. Its result:-he died; whom to tell that Ravana has been killed? There was none amongst ‘rakshasas’ to imitate even a goat’s bleat. ‘No one to plead mercy for us (rakshasas). We can only dance in fear to the sound of wardrum ‘Pongattham Pongo’, just see.’

Having ten heads with crowns, he became a victim of sensual pleasure; he did not take refuge in Sri Rama. We do not know what to do - we have grown without devotion to Rama. Please do not kill us. We dance in fear to the sound of wardrum ‘Pongattham Pongo’.

It is Ravana who abducted Sita Devi and we have not done any crime. Watch our dance, that of defeated. When queried ‘why didn’t you all advise him?’ ; they reply ‘Vibheeshana was bold to advise him asking him to free Sita Devi. When he didn’t listen to him, would he listen to us? Ravana and other warriors were all killed. Please take Sita Devi and do not kill us; we are scared.’

‘You say a man has come as Death-god to kill; when a monkey burnt your Lanka, why could you not make truce? When the water has already gushed, you want to build a bridge?’ They reply: ‘If, so much wisdom was forthcoming, would all this have happened. ‘We do not want this Sita, take her away’ so saying we should have handed Her over to the monkey. We did not have that much sense.’

When Sri Rama’s rain of arrows was showering, ‘in view of ignorance on Ravana’s part you should kill him and leave us. Even looking at the marvel of the bridge built to connect Lanka, he should have thought that you will destroy us. Ravana should have understood that Sita Devi deserves Sri Rama and not himself. Therefore he only should be punished and we should be let free.’

Having seen Ravana getting killed, they request the leaders of monkey army: “Ravana committed sin and fell. He did not listen to good advice. ‘What will those weak ones do if Sita Devi is abducted, so thinking he took her away’. We are scared of the bravest Ramachandra. Please save us.”

The request was conveyed to Sugreeva. Highlighting Sri Rama's Supreme power of the arrows, which were sharper/more effective than Rudra’s in ‘Tripura burning’, they say : ‘We, with Jambuvaan and Sugreeva (who could recommend to Sri Rama) as Purushakaara, are falling at your feet. If you kill us now it will be criticised.

These verses, sung by ‘rakshasas’ as composed by the azwar lauding Sri Rama’s conquest of Lanka, if recited will confer to them Paramapadham.

## 10.3 Yetthuhinrom: dance song - Kuzhamani thooram

‘Kuzhamanithoora kootthu’ is dance performed by those who lose or get vanquished. Describing this dance, the azwar gets engrossed in ‘Bhagavadvishayam’. Performing this dance in front of Sugreeva, the rakshasas plead : ‘The God’s name, the recitation of which cannot be avoided, we shall do till the tongue gets tired Bravery personified that You are, is there any great thing to be achieved by killing us - through you alone, as purushakaara, we will achieve passage to the Lord. Please instruct monkeys not to kill us.

We are not ill-mannered and egoistic as Inderjit and others were. Despite Sugreeva wanting to kill Vibheeshana, Hanuman recommends him to Sri Rama for sharanagathi. We are conscious of the fact that the Sri Rama is the protector of this land and not knowing it they showed their disrespect. Wishing you prosperity, we dance and you enjoy it. Seeing Sri Rama, (a death-knell) kill Ravana, we are scared. Having none to save, we seek your protection.

Lakshmana’s attractive bow, and his mode of discharging arrows was praise-worthy. We like His qualities. To the query, praising now Lakshmana is all right but you may become inimical later due to your nature as in the past. We do not hold that disrespect, and we have left it off. You are already victorious. We do not carry any more, that manliness. Give us our remaining age. See our dance and enjoy.

They (rakshasas) praise Sri Rama in Sugreeva’s court lauding His great noble qualities. Using material that sinks in water, He built a bridge. He is extraordinarily powerful and the way He humbled us is unique. Some rakshasas in Sugreeva’s court advised these rakshasas saying that you do not know how to approach the monkeys. First you erase your egoistic talk and praise Hanuman. Make him feel remorse for you. Then only no one will kill you. Dance ‘Kuzamani thooram’ in such a way Sri Rama also sees it. In response to this advice all rakshasas came together, and the monkey brigade wanted to run over them. But they told them, when Ravana came to fight on the first day, he was allowed to go back by Sri Rama and we understood He indeed is the protector. We are in a group now to dance. Do not attack us.

The azwar has thus described the dance ‘Kuzamani thooram’ by the huge-bodied rakshasas.

## 10.4 Shandhamalar Kuzal: Yashodha’s call to suckle

The last decad highlighted Ramavataara and the defeat of forces of hatred. The azwar now looks at Krishnavataara wherein the ‘Vatsalya’ (love) of Yashodha for the young Krishna reaches a crescendo.

Krishna gets to suckle only one breast when Balarama is also present. In Ayodhya the people used to speak about Sri Rama’s noble qualities, from which the aspect of ‘Sausheelya’ used to get highlighted - it is said Dasharatha could not comprehend son’s ‘Sausheelya’! Those who are subject to rebirths it is great to not to be born again. Similarly for the Lord to take another avataara is specially unique. Yashodha seems to tell Krishna, that the breast milk is more unique than devathas ‘amrutha’.

O Lord, resembling in colour of dark ocean and rain-bearing clouds and having red eyes. ‘O my child, my breasts are full with milk. Despite my calling You, You are not coming. The sweet breast milk is getting wasted. Come and suckle. I will get the Moon for you to play’. Krishna tells ‘first you get me the Moon; then I will come’. Yashodha tells about the pressure she is experiencing in breasts; ‘You went in between, the two Arjuna trees; You understand a mother’s pain. Come quickly’.

‘Your mischiefs are not the type which children of your age do. Your mischiefs / sports are such everyone would like to see to heart’s content. When Krishna queries as to what mischiefs He did, she says: Didn’t you drink Poothana’s breast-milk. Her life was also sucked in the process. You see I have a stick in hand. I can punish You with this, but I will not. You return late and there are demons whose strength increases in the evening! She praises Him saying that You’re the leader for the cows, so that he gets coaxed to suckle.

Just as Yashodha cannot live without Krishna suckling her breasts, so also the azwar cannot survive without praising Krishna.

## 10.5 Poongodhai: The clap-song

Having experienced Krishna’s mischievous sports as a breastfed child, the azwar now turns to His growing years. As the churning of curds by Yashodha was in process, Krishna, hiding beside, stole the butter. Next He was caught and beaten. And He wept and wept. ‘You showed your bewitching beauty and made me ecstatic; now You clap and laugh and make me live. Not only me, all my neighbours come and enjoy Your clapping’, Yashodha tells Krishna.

Although it is not befitting His nature to steal the butter and curds He pretended as though He had done a good job through His innocence. Yashodha was bowled over by it. He was the most goodlooking amongst gopas with His lips and feet resembling the shining lotus. Despite gopis trying to hoodwink Krishna by keeping buttermilk together with ghee and curds, He used to finish off selectively the latter and toddle. The gopis used to tie Him with a rope, which used to leave a mark on His stomach leading to the name ‘Dhamodhara’. ‘So delightful to be tied, You clap and show Your uniqueness’. His divine sports included gulping Poothana’s poisonous milk, cutting off the fetters which Kamsa had tied to His parents’ legs, and looking after the cattle.

‘Mouthing the word ‘anjali’, I pray to You; Gopis are bringing the sweetmeat - large appams - You clap with Your beautiful hands’. Yashodha showed Him ‘appam and puffed rice’ and told Him that she will give them to Him and He should clap - ‘I will not give You just one appam but many; befitting Your caste, You did pot dance and now You clap and delight to me.’

You split asunder the beaks of Bakasura, and uprooted the beautiful blossoming Kurundha tree - an asura in that form, after eating the butter hidden from Him (!), He smeared Yashodha’s breasts with the fat sticking to His hands (!).

No one came to know as to when and how Poothana entered, but she came in Yashodha’s garb and suckled Him. ‘Should You not just the same way as You destroy those who do not love You, You listen and act as to what those You love say’. He also powdered Shakatasura. He kept silent till Poothana neared Him by falling on Her and not letting Her go.

Tirumangai Azwar, who sang in the rainy season, is as generous as rain-bearing clouds.

## 10.6 Yenganum: Miracles of the Lord

In this decad, the azwar continues with Krishnaavataara but through others in place of Yashodha. He himself assumes her garb He talks about Krishna’s Supremacy and His prowess. But he worries that Krishna got Himself tied with women, and praises His easy accessibility.

The Lord as ‘Narayana’ through teaching and practice broadened the scope of Vedas. He taught ‘moola manthra’ as Narayana. That Lord wishes my welfare. The one who harboured everyone including Sun and Moon by widening His stomach is being tied to-day by a frail cowherd woman - that is a surprise!

The churner of the Milky ocean came as a cowherd boy and straightened the hunch-back of a woman. The Lord who swallowed the seven worlds / seas / mountains in a trice is to-day tied to a mortar! The same Lord who tackled Madhu Kaitaba, the moving mountains; He also killed another mountain of a demon, Hiranyakashipu, by slitting his chest. He is tied by a frail woman!

When the ‘devatas’ pleaded with the Lord, He went to Mahabali to beg three feet of land. As Trivikrama He measured the world. Simultaneously He warded off all sins of His aashrithas by touching their heads with His Feet as Trivikrama and distanced them from Yama’s henchmen. As Parashurama He cut off the thousand hands of Karthaveerarjuna. To-day He is tied by a frail woman to a mortar!

As Sri Rama He built a bridge utilising monkey-brigades; He also broke ‘Shiva Danus’. He cut off Ravana’s ten heads, but He cannot snap the rope used by His mother to tie Him up. The said Shiva Danus had the unique distinction of coming into existence just the same time as Sita Devi. He solved both His father’s as well as His own words - by giving Paduka to Bharatha, and going to the forest. But to-day a frail women has tied Him!

The Lord who humbled the fearless Keshi (Kamsa’s horse) with spiralling hair locks, by tearing asunder its mouth, showed His prowess as Rama to Sugreeva by splitting the row of trees with His arrows. The Lord who dismembered the nose of Shoorpanaka, is tied by a frail woman to a mortar!

Krishna when so tied used to attract many people. He was least oothered about it. His only interest was to fill His stomach with curds and butter hidden away from Him in hanging pots. He submitted Himself to be tied by a gopi! Those who read these ‘pasurams’ will be rid of all sorrows, achieve ecstacy and will go beyond the world of celestials (Paramapadham).

## 10.7 Maanamudaitthu: Neighbours Complain !

The azwar rejoices Krishna becoming adolescent with His pranks, bringing complaints to Yashodha. The gopis tell ‘we lost this, we lost that’. Yashodha tells her confidante that since gopas cannot tolerate any complaint she will tell Krishna not to give any room for suspicion while playing with girls. Krishna’s pranks in Gokula have doubled, and she says that she will fall at His feet and request Him not to repeat them. His age is such that He will not listen to me or His father. ‘Don’t you have children. You ought to know’ tells Yashodha to those who complain.

One gopi got up early (Brahmi muhoortha) and churning the curds, kept the butter safely; she did not throw the butter milk and took it out for selling. On the way she saw Krishna and was aghast. ‘Has He finished off my butter and milk? Let me go and check. Lo! The pots were empty! Krishna alone could have done this’ pines that gopi for whom these milk products were like treasure.

Yashodha tells her friends: ‘Look there is no comparison whatever as to how He looks and what He does! There is no way you can stop Him. Look at His hands which are smeared with ghee’. The friend asks: ‘Didn’t you want a son to eat the surplus milk and curds. How do you feel now?!’ ‘My fear is that if He cannot digest it what will happen!’ says Yashodha.

The gopi from the ground floor house complains: We did not experience this trouble when Balarama was just as young. Krishna is a liar. He closely follows the churning process upto the point of hiding butter / milk / curds. Yet another gopi complains: ‘When my man was away and I also had to go out for something without any help for looking after my daughter, Krishna snatches the ball and when she comes near pulls her sari and tears it. He behaves like this in stealth.’

In the cradle song one sings ‘Bhoodevi’s consort Srivallabha....’ and the ‘Nayaka’ that He is to them Himself is troubling us like this! And on top of it He sports the title ‘Nandagopakumara’! The neighbours come in a group and complain when Yashodha replies: ‘You also have children; can my son do anything like this?’ They retort: ‘The one who could eat all the eatables kept for Govardhana hills, can do anything!

In a ‘group discussion’ (!) to prevent thefts by Krishna, one gopi says: ‘I am scared to abuse the one who drank the Ogress’ ‘milk’. ‘The boiled milk especially, butter, curds and ghee simply vanish’, so complain all. ‘Since He is your child why can’t you control Him’ they tell Yashodha. Yashodha tells them: ‘Not only did he drink poisonous milk but also with His small feet pounded Shakata into small bits. When I went to Yamuna for bath He powdered the huge cart. When such sports have been accomplished at a tender age, He appears to be the Supreme Lord! I am scared to call Him’. ‘How’s it you cannot punish and keep under control your own son’ retort the gopis. Yashodha decides to punish Him in front of everyone.

‘You are not leaving even half the quantity of milk products. We are not in a position to send the ghee to Kamsa’ Yashoda tells Krishna. He simply stands as though He is in great fear!! Recalling complaints on His pranks, Yashodha asks Him ‘Is it right that You behave like this?’ continuing, Yashodha tells ‘Is it right to do unspeakable mischiefs?’ “Hiding near the pond, He takes the clothes of gopis and tells them ‘If you want them come and take’, O is it right?” They ask Yashodha. She remembers at the mention of the lotus, Krishna’s prank of dancing on Kaaliya’s head. She says: ‘He has no fear whatever. When Yashodha reminds Him of this, Krishna tells not to get scared as He, as a baby, drank Poothana’s poisonous milk! You need not worry as poison does not do anything to Me. ‘She says what about fighting the seven bulls? You should not do these alone. If Balarama is with You, no one would cast the evil eye on You. If getting You as my son were not enough, there is pain in my stomach. Rememer your tender age - why do adventurous pranks?’

The azwar in the garb of Yashodha forgot himself while describing Krishna’s adventures and added another four verses to this decad! Those reading these verses will get the same experience as Yashodha.

## 10.8 Kaadhil: Lovers’ quarrel

The age (period) of stealing butter etc. came to an end and the gopis themselves came to Him to be united with Him. When He did not come at the appointed time, they experience pangs of separation. The azwar describes as to How Krishna handled such situations, Because of pranks, adventures and sports, Yashodha could not prick the ears of Krishna at the tender age. The gopis used to taunt Him saying that He was of empty ears! Finally he agreed so that He could wear ear-studs. He felt it was easier with the decorated ear to entice gopis! Being late His strides were ten times the normal. He just stood opposite the gopi. ‘Should you come late? Should You come late? and having made a mistake should He not pacify or make up for it?’ the gopi asks angrily.

‘Why did You come now’ asks another gopi. He at once changes into another dress, since the earlier one did not produce happiness in her. He also held a ball in hand! Giving the impression of a different person, He tied His ‘utthareeya’ (longish waist cloth) to the waist as though He was in the flow of a river. ‘Should one pretend to be another? Should He not push-open the door?’ asks the gopi.

The gopi who was churning the curds just started looking at Him as though in a trance. She asks ‘the one who has the Garudadwaja and is supposed to protect others, coming like an unknown person in the night, is it the right thing to do? That too singing loudly. A ‘Poorna’ coming like an ‘Apoorna’!

He came as a young boy keeping soft steps and smiled. The gopi says ‘He came not tying His hair into a knot to entice. He is wearing very costly jewelry; He’s standing and smiling outside the house. ‘Why all this? could You not have come at the right time?’ asks the gopi. Carrying a crooked bow He enters, and the gopi thinks that He came to show His beauty in holding a bow’. ‘Should You come here as though You’re going to a war. When You go to Your lover, why have a weapon?’ says the gopi.

Krishna could not as much ask as to why she was angry with Him. She watched the beautiful hands wielding the bow, and says: ‘It appears that the beauty of Your hands are (born) meant for those who are not your symphathisers! When You united in the night. Your next plan was something else and the action was different. If You had understood Yourself You would have come at the agreed time.’ ‘The delay was unavoidable. Who else is there for me excepting You’ Krishna tells her with all humility. Consoled, the gopi tells Him. ‘The one who humbled Kaliya, is telling He has no one. It is an old story. There are many deserving of You. In front of them we are nothing. The one who has no wants how could You come here?’ Krishna responds ‘It is just your imagination. ‘Don’t You have the inseparable Sri and Bhoo devis. Leaving them, You have partaken in Rasakreede with cowherd women’ the gopi retorts. She is telling her friend (sakhi) ‘Why did you allow Him inside?’ ‘I shall keep her in my heart’ quips Krishna. She replies ‘You do one thing and say something else’.

These are the words of a gopi to Krishna who has bewitching eyes She, who had His separation corresponding to a hundred times of fasting, and spoke these words when there was none to prevent her. These are the words of Tirumangai Azwar which will make the Lord Himself bereft of freedom (subservient). Reciting these will ward off one’s sorrows.

## 10.9 Pulluruvahi: Pasurams as ‘sayings’

First separation, next union followed by separation again leading to sorrow which heightened, and seeing this condition Nayaki’s mother thinks within herself: ‘Shall I get Him or overcome the criticism of the people of the town; which is the direct way?’ She addresses to the Nayaka: ‘You are a virtuous person; You entice and attract with Your beauty; what do You propose to do now; can You feel relaxed despite my daughter’s condition?’

Continuing ‘Are You remembering Your bravery in finishing off Poothana; or being not afraid of Ravana. When Agni could not get food, You helped to get him so much food that he could over eat! Are not my daughter’s glow and coiffure such as to entice You?’

‘I offered Myself to five lakh gopis after killing Poothana’ says Krishna. ‘Is that the reason for making my daughter suffer’ the mother retorts. Krishna tells her about His other feats - Pot-dance, killing the bulls, lifting Govardhana. The mother quips: ‘Should You therefore neglect my daughter.’ Harking back to His dance, Krishna says that the entire crowd rejoiced it and should He get such a calumny. He danced enjoying on behalf of those present as also those absent. Since during His avataara some people were not there, for their sake He is giving darshan in Tirumalai.

‘I came with Chakra (and other weapons) to destroy enemies’ Krishna says. The mother replies saying that it was confined to Kamsa and remembering all that heroics should He trouble her daughter. Krishna recalls His keeping Rudra in His body, and killing of Narakasura for the sake of sixteen thousand women - ‘since I have such love for women, can you criticise me this way?’ The mother continues her refrain that with all that He is making her daughter suffer - ‘She is not a weapon which You may take when You want!’

When Krishna tells that for a woman’s sake He organised a war and she only has found a blemish in Him. ‘You sent to Veeraswarga so many warriors after clearing the grass on the way (!) and You are neglecting my daughter as though she is a berry which can be either picked or not picked!’

To Krishna’s query that whether at all her daughter can know His mind and behave, the mother quips: ‘Does the inseparable Sri Mahalakshmi know ‘Your mind!?’ You killed the ego-filled Ravana who was proof against Brahmastra. After this when Sita stood in front of You, You did not see and the devatas were announcing Her arrival. Is this Your bravery? Do you want to repeat it with my daughter?’

‘The Kings who were intoxicated with the wrong notion that they could travel anywhere were made to fall on Your feet as Parasurama, wielding a single axe. You are neglecting my daughter’ tells the mother.

When Krishna reminded her of His feats as Vamana, and His going to a monkey to help him, the mother says ‘If You were so easily accessible, why did you tell my daughter that You cannot part with her even for a moment; should not one expect all that; is it anything great? Is she like mouthful of water which one can either swallow or spit out?!’

‘Do not criticise Me that way as I am having the nature to create the world’ says Krishna. The mother replies ‘Desirable that my daughter is, You neglect her; what is the use of creation. You wasted her attractive body. She is as enjoyable as ‘Amrutha’. She lacks nothing, maybe it is due to sins committed by us!’

The Lord is helpful to those who pray without break. These verses by Tirumangai Azwar with his staff (a thin metal rod) - who ever holds it, it can make him impersonate the azwar! if recited, will achieve the benefit of service to God.

## 10.10 Thirutthai: Folk verses

In the last decad Nayaki’s mother cried hoarse calling the Lord. She observed omens relating to His arrival. The Nayaki cannot bear the separation anymore and falls at the feet of any bird that comes her way and requests it to bring about the union with the Lord.

Since the mother has said that He will come, she need not go now. She observes the red pheasant go from left to her right and concludes that He would be coming. Asking the bird to get the Lord to come to her, she tells that He, Tulasi garland - wearing Madhava, is inseparably united with Sri Mahalakshmi. Requesting it a second time ‘screech for His arrival’, she thinks it will reduce the intensity of her pangs.

Asking the crow to ‘caw, caw’, she addresses it as ‘pillai’, the little sweet crow, in an effort to praise it - whoever is helpful in matters related to the Lord, one must praise it. The word ‘urai’ stands for Srimad Ramayana and hence Sri Rama’s reputation to save His ‘aashrithas’. But it (crow) ran away to eat the ‘pinda’ rice. She then addresses cuckoo: ‘with your beauty and sweet voice, coo, coo for His arrival. He stopped the hailstorm by lifting Govardhana, and tore Keshi’s mouth into two. Since its ‘cooing’ might cause confusion considering that it was being fed by a crow, it did not coo. She then asks a baby-lizard to call ‘tut, tut’ - lizards are known to wish us good and hence produce a good omen. Call the one who measured the Earth and has well-coiffured Sri Mahalakshmi on His chest.

‘It is not necessary that I should ‘tut, tut’ whenever you want’ said the lizard. ‘Show your beautiful look and with your innocent words, call Him’ she tells a parrot. His strong hands are enough to handle anyone but in addition, He also wears always the Sudharshana Chakra; He has also got a step forward indicating His readiness to help; He is in Southern Venkatam.’

The cock is conscious of time. It crowed. Since crowing a second time might lead to His departure, the Nayaki and her friends were worried. Parashar Bhattar observed meaningfully that ‘Krishna nestling the cock in His lap is coming!’ A conversation ensues -

Friend : Should you not be happy and prepare for His coming.

Nayaki : I am worried about the separation from that Manmathamanmatha. In addition without remembering that a mother is involved, Manmatha seems to be hurling his arrows at me.

Then she toys with the idea of using ‘Manmatha’ himself to inspire love in the Lord!

Friend : Thinking that the cock has crowed and He has already come, you seem to be already worried about separation. Its crowing could also indicate that He may send His messenger!

Nayaki : You seem to ponder well. Tell me for sure whether He is coming as the pangs of separation are unbearable.

Thinking about the Lord at Thirukudanthai, she felt that the Lord had appeared. But she was not able to make out whether It is the Sudarshana-wielding Lord or bow-wielding man. I cannot decide unless I am told about the identity.

Those who are interested in God-connected aspects should sing these verses, as it confers extraordinary benefits. Singing itself is ‘Purushaartha’.

## Eleventh Patthu

## 11.1 Kunrum Yedatthu: Soliloquy of a girl

When the birds failed to bring the Lord, she resigns to her fate and describes her plight. The Lord who could have used an umbrella, lifted Govardhana hills to protect the cowherds. Just because one ‘rakshasa’ misbehaved, He erased the race itself. His bravery seems to have worked against me - the southern breeze which ought to be cool is burning us.

She says the rain-bearing cloud, which gives its name to the Lord as Meghashyama is in fact troubling her. She would rather compare His colour to blue of the azure ocean. For having aspired for the Lord’s Tulasi garland and the chest, is this the punishment? My thinned-down body is being bothered by the rain-bearing cool dew breeze; besides the Moon is throwing red hot coal to my eyes! The bangles are slipping and His Tulasi garland is calling me.

He is troubling me in many ways. The very place dreaded by Vasudeva-Devaki, became a place of protection to them. He got to live in Nandagokula without Kamsa’s fear. He sensed Poothana’s motives and finished her off. Obedient to Him who gave him coolness, the Moon is bothering me. When she heard the roaring of seas, she tells it has lost its mind. He appeared at just the appointed time as Narasimha - He used His nails to split the ‘asura’s’ chest. Such a brave one, brought out the Moon during ocean-churning - may be because the Moon came out, it is roaring! Since the ocean cannot touch the Moon it is screeching! Why did it give up its composure?

Seeing the Moon wreaking fire and the Nayaki talking incogently, the Anril birds also started calling incessantly. Reflecting the Lord’s ability to hit the enemy before he sets out, the Anril bird is bothering me with its calling - it is having a cool nest made of palm leaves! The cool breeze, the troubling Moon, the crying sound of Anril birds did not make her weak. The Lord's errand to Manmatha to go and hurl his arrows at me did not just hit me but pierced my heart. Is this the punishment meted out to me when He appeared and I stood strong? My heart is spiralling around me with my love to Him. The Tulasi accompanied by the cool breeze with cool dew is attacking me. It is just like those who should be your guide (like Vibheeshana should have been to Ravana proving to be the end of Ravana) start guiding you through a thorny path.

It became almost impossible for the Nayaki to hold on to her life. The friends say they can only be sad at her plight. The Nayaki suggests that if the bees go to Him and bring the fragrance of Tulasi garland offered by Srivaishnava devotees and worn by Him, and blow it over her body she might regain her body colour and sleep.

‘The Lord who had ‘samslesha’ (union) with me is not the type to behave thus with me. Since He follows what ‘aashrithas’ tell Him to do. The azwar lauds thus about Lord’s dependence (paratanthrya) - didn’t He demean Himself going as messenger for Pandavas? Those who recite these verses, will always be united with the Lord.’

## 11.2 Kunrum Yedatthu: Yet another soliloquy

‘When the protector has let you down what can at all one do?’ so saying the Nayaki is downcast. Having lifted Govardhana hills, He went round dancing in street-squares, but left me in the middle of the road. Added to this the Anril birds perched in their nests made of palm leaves keep crying. To avoid hearing this, is there anyone to unite me with the Lord. In order to save her from Anril cries, the Lord played His flute. But she was troubled. The flute played by Him, who broke the ‘Kurundha’ tree, humbled the bulls and split the Keshi’s mouth, bothered her.

He killed Kamsa and His wrestlers. I desired to have the victory garland of Tulasi worn by Him but in this evening time the Southern breeze is making me suffer. Densely grown avenue trees were cut off without a trace of the root. It is difficult to fathom His love (like that of cow to a calf) towards ‘aashrithas’. When I recite His names the blue sea is roaring and making fun of me. Added to this, the cool breeze is blowing.

When I had united with Him, the sound of Anril birds was pleasant, mother was not angry, the ocean quietly protected the earth, whereas everyone of them is giving rise to suffering. The reason is my falling for Nappinnai’s consort.

During the day the mother is angry and in the night the anril birds trouble me. Added to this are roaring of the seas, the cool dew breeze. Every moment is like an eon. The one who is symbolised Paanchajanya and Sudarshana invited me and my friend in a dream, held my hands and feet and united with me. If I start thinking about it, it will never end.

I do not like to join the group affected by Manmatha as he will become by virtue of my becoming a consort of the Lord. It is his duty to unite his parents by hurling his arrows on Him. Why criticise the son when the husband is not showing His kindness; He, who resides in Malirumjolai hills, ‘robbed of my heart’s peace.’ 'Whither the sun? Where is he hiding? There is no point in living; if the body withers and falls, it may be good for us.’

‘Although there is disappointment, the desire to live is not gone. That Kaama, without listening to entreaties, is troubling the mother herself. We have become targets of Madana’s arrows, we have no alternative but give up our coyness, fear, humility and feminineness, and go to the place He is residing. We can atleast get Him as a normal devotee - didn’t He declare ‘sharanamvraja’. I somehow must see Him.

Just the way He had union with Nappinnai, there is a suggestion (dhwani) here regarding Prapthi (attainment of goal). To those reciting these verses, the sins will not remain and prapthi will be realised without any obstacles.

## 11.3 Mannilangu: Uniting with the Lord

The Nayaki decided to do meditation to get Him. Nayaka, the Lord, unable to bear this, showed His face and asked: ‘Just a moment’s delay is causing ail this and, I myself made efforts to get you. Even if low on status of birth, and due to the fact that they cannot survive without Me, can I live without them? She talks about His accessibility which resulted in pangs, but at the same time satisfaction since an urgency has been created in getting Him.

The Lord who became a sarathy for Arjuna, to get three feet of land from the one who thought it was his, built a bridge for His ladylove and killed Ravana and his associates, had His brilliance became more resplendent - Once He shows His love to someone, that one would declare I have no interest in victory. That very Lord stole my jewellery. He entered my heart without my permission. He will undertake any dangerous adventure for His people like Gajendra. Even if the most difficult effort is required to see the Lord with delightfully strong hands, uttering His names and dancing when my breasts acquired the same briiliance as that during samslesha with Him.

When cross-questioned by her friend as to whether she knows Him, the Nayaki replies: ‘I don’t know Him. When we were united firmly, I could say He being ‘Seshi’ I know His easy accessibility (Saulabhya). The One who kepi intact the Vedas during deluge, and demonstrated His being a well-wisher, my heart became friendly and He wanted my bangles. Probably He is trying to make the eternal soul His own, but I cannot tell for sure His mind.

The friend insists on knowing any signs or marks, and Nayaki replies saying that we know nothing else other than the ‘dravya’ which had the touch I contact of aashrithas, he is not accessible to one who makes his own effort to know Him. However in our case we know the way He revealed Himself. Didn’t He feel happy at being born amongst cowherds? Who does not know this?

What is the point in saying that we know the Supreme Person who ate curds and butter? insists the friend. Nayaki replies appropriately: ‘Whoever approaches Him with the attitude of Prapaka Prapthi He will grant His proximity. We approached Him that way - whoever meditates / prays to Him not expecting any reward or having no other means in view, He will render you to be Himself.

Continuing her response the Nayaki says: ‘Extraordinary servants became ecstatic by keeping Him in their hearts. The reason for lodging Him in their hearts was that they got the touch of His holy feet as Trivikrama unexpectedly which resulted in their getting to know that they are seshas and He their Seshi.

The friend goes on: “Keeping Him in the heart the devotees rejoiced, you say. In that case should not have your bangles stayed.” The Nayaki replies: Despite He being in heart, the ‘thaapa’ (pangs) continues. Why this short-coming? When He is there why the bangles slip away? Do we still have ‘sthreethva’ intact? If tolerating pangs of separation should stay firmly, we should worship Lord’s ‘archa’ form. Let us laud and sing about Venkataadri and Srirangam. Nayaki’s friend approves of it being a well-wisher. Addressing her heart (manas) the Nayaki says : ‘Do not have any doubts. Agree to join Him.’

The delay to join him was caused by the absence of Lord’s ‘sankalpa’; once His sankalpa and our interest (for Prapya) are there, will we not be uniting with Him?

When we can undertake the effort which is possible, that is with interest to do kainkaryam to the Lord at Thiruvali why aim at something not accessible to us? If not continuously, atleast whenever possible do kainkaryam to Him - we are lucky to have got that chance. Let us worship with flowers the Lord at Thiruvali - the sins accumulated from beginningless time will vanish. The azwar says that one would not be just content and say this is enough after having recited His thousand names.

## 11.4 Nilaiyidam: Lord’s ten manifestations (avataras)

‘Even if my heart and body stop working it is better for us’ tells the azwar. The Lord observes: ‘You don’t have the same worry as I have. Don’t you know the number of avataras I took for your sake? Remembering ‘Parithranaya sadhoonaam.....’, she says ‘Sure you

did manifest for myself’; and she tells her heart to approach the Lord at Tiruvali’ saying thus the azwar consoles himself. But the Lord continues: ‘Are you just concerned with only archavataara; to protect the worlds/ ‘aashrithas’ / to kill aashrithas’ enemies / to give darshana to ‘aashrithas’ I took many ‘avataaras’; can’t you live remembering them?’ Pleased as he was, the azwar lauds Lord’s various ‘avataaras’.

As Mathsya the Lord drank the ever raising waves of the sea during deluge. Carrying the mountains on His back, thereby giving a place to dwell for the devatas, He wandered in the sky! For the devathas again He took Koormavataara ; He used Vaasuki as rope around Mandara mount to churn the sea and gave ‘amrutha’ to ‘devathas’. His hoofs in Varahavataara were so large He could contain the entire Universe with Sun, Moon, Quarters, Oceans, Mountains; He is our controller. Please accept our kainkaryam.

With extraordinarily shining garlands on either side, the intensity of which compared with the fire-wreaking eyes, the mountain size Narasimha scared even Hiranyakashipu. His nails looked like spelling death-knell to enemies. Thinking that Sudarshana may not be enough for the task, He used His nails to split Hiranyakashipu into two.

Mahabali despite being reputed as a generous person rendered the devathas homeless, and the Lord as Vamana went to beg for three feet of Land. He asked for His own land - He is our protector. The wicked Kshathriyas, who had become too strong and troublesome to others, were decimated in as many as twenty one rounds. Kodhandapani, Kakutstha, killed Maricha who wandered in front of their hut to attract Sita Devi’s attention leading to Her abduction by Ravana. He reduced Ravana’s Lanka into ashes and killed him. We need nothing when He is our protector.

When darkness and ‘ajnaana’ ruled the world, the Lord as Hamsa (Swan), a thiryak, brought the Vedas to exhort people. He taught the ultimate realities. He is our protector.

The one who exhorted us in dharma His i.e. Damodhara’s pranks/ adventures are lauded by the azwar. The hard-hearted ogress, Poothana, was killed by Krishna suckling her. When Yashodha tied Him to a mortar, He dragged it in such away that the twin Arjuna trees (asuras in that form) were destroyed. He will, without doubt, eliminate all our sins.

## 11.5 Maan Amarum: Two Nayakis discuss Lord’s nature

While one Nayaki lauds Lord’s easy accessibility, the second one emphasizes His Supremacy.

‘The Lord who was born in Ayodhya and walked in the jungle with Sita Devi’ says one. The other one: ‘For protecting the ‘rishis’ He walked. Even forgetting His appearance He subjects Himself to difficulty while protecting His ‘aashrithas’.

Talking about easy accessibility of Sri Rama, His Supremacy is also being stated. Taking avataara as Krishna, He removed the fetters from Vasudeva’s legs, and grew in Nandagokula to avoid Kamsa’s evil eye. Though beyond Karma, He came to protect those given to Karma.

He killed those who taunted Him praying that He was a cowherd boy. He ate curds and butter hidden by gopis. He protected the worlds by enclosing them in His stomach during deluge. While the first part here shows His easy accessibility, the latter part His Supremacy. As though the worlds were not big enough for His stomach, it craved for more! Yashodha, who looked glorious having got Krishna, could not go in search of a long rope and therefore tied Him with a rope with knots. She tied Him to a mortar. The one who frees samsaric bonds, could not get released by His mother. The one who was easily seen in the midst of gopalakas does not appear to even Brahma and others!

Going as a messenger for Pandavas, He faced insult at the hands of Duryodhana who said: ‘You ate in a shoodra’s house’. He also used foul words in front of others; He even tried to imprison Krishna. The Lord is dependent on His devotees (bhakthaparadheena).

Not only did He tolerate foul words, He even went down in status when He became a Sarathy to Arjuna. Krishnavataara resembles Vamanavataara in respect of easy mixing with all and stunning looks. His looks won Mahabali and His action in begging three feet of land is extraordinary. He indeed is Mahavishnu!

Being intoxicated with the love for the Lord, the azwar forgot to mention the benefit of reciting this decad. He is seen in Venkatadri to facilitate His darshan. He is firmly seated in azwar’s heart.

## 11.6 Maininra: Exhorting seekers

Having experienced the Lord’s extraordinary abilities and easy accessibility, the azwar exhorts the people to take refuge in the Supreme Lord. He tells them that they may not be aware of His help in keeping everything and everyone in His stomach during the deluge. If at all you want to surrender why don’t you go to the extraordinary God: Do not think of the god of enemies (yedhilor daivam).

The worlds are there thanks to His kindness. When the ocean roared He took control of the entire Universe and safely guarded it in His stomach. He kept the so called gods considered as Supreme Gods by some viz. Brahma, Indra and Rudra in His stomach and protected them. They indeed are unfortunate who do not laud His noble qualities. How can one worship the feet of those who were given protection by the Lord in His stomach!? Even if one does not worship, one can at least chant His names (when Trivikrama who kept His holy Feet on everyone is there).

Despite the azwar’s advice not to worship those other than the Lord, there were some who continued to do it. The azwar says: “You ignorant fellows. Is it right to behave this way?” Just like a mother, He protected you all in His stomach. When the mother is there do you worship a wooden stool? You make the Lord get dejected saying: “When the sense organs given by Me are there, you went elsewhere without worshipping Me.” The azwar continues: ‘You failed in your method. There is no point in advising them. If one does not think about them, it is good time! The one who protected from the great deluge, is the Supreme controller of the Universe and kindness personified is residing in Tirukannamangai; those who do not think of Him had better not be remembered - that way it is good time! They belie azwar’s hopes of forsaking other gods, and are called wicked by the azwar. But these people ask the azwar “Why do you blame us? What is the point in your repeating it ten times? The fact is that due to our past sins we are not having any interest in what you say. How to overcome this drawback? The azwar is quick to suggest that they go to Tirukudanthai shrine to eliminate their sins: ‘You laud Tirukudanthai and dance delightfully’.

‘Those who chant these verses will get blessings from Sri Mahalakshmi’ says the azwar.

## 11.7 Neenaahum: Why do we have this body ?

The sense - organs given by the Lord are meant to experience Him but if they are used for other purposes they are not sense organs at all.

The serpent, Vasuki was so long that some length was left after fully encircling the Mandhara mountain which was bodily lifted and brought by the Lord travelling on Garuda’s back. After distributing the amrutha from churning of the ocean, the Lord felt He got a big benefit and rejoiced. When on Garuda’s back, it appeared as though there was one golden mountain on another golden mountain! If one could not see that golden mountain, at least they must listen, through the ears given by Him, about the feat of the Lord as Vamana. If they say ‘we shall not’, then they are no ears at all.

The Lord is uniquely different from the lesser gods. The Vedas acquired greatness because they propound Him. Not just that emanating from Vedas (Pramana), but for seeing with one’s own eyes He is residing in Tiruvaali shrine. If anything other than His greatness is talked about, it is just like the meaningless roaring of the sea!

If compositions of Prabhandha (Divya) are to be sung, should not one sing about the great Lord? It is no song at all if it does not talk about the Lord’s feat of eliminating Prahladha’s enemy!

The Lord’s appearance is such that it calls for not one simile but four because one cannot say that a particular thing resembles to describe His body’s brilliance. To describe something about Lord’s loveliness has become the task of the azwar. Obviating the need to see Him in Paramapada, He is right here to receive Kainkaryam. Is it a hand at all which cannot do service to Him?

The Lord does not attach importance to implements like flowers, but to ‘Bhakthi bhava’. Even Tulasi offered without bhakthi is not liked by Him. ‘Those who do not cut fresh lotus flowers from the ponds to offer kainkaryam with love and shraddha (enthusiasm), or even think of that as fit to be offered to Lord’s holy feet only have no feeling at all’. Those who do not realise and contemplate through their internal and external organs are 'forever ignorant’. Those who do not think of His greatness are ignorant of His easy accessibility (saulabhya).

Having talked about the role to be played by one’s organs to realise the Lord, the azwar talks of the whole person - The Lord may condone ones mistakes but He will not excuse the violation i.e. apacharam by one towards His dasas / baghavathas. ‘This I have understood firmly’ says the azwar.

The azwar has highlighted the servitude to the Lord and stressed that in order to ward off one’s sins, one has to surrender to the Feet of Shriahpathi through Sri Mahalakshmi. His lotus Feet ensure that they make everyone a ‘daasa’ to the Lord, who has Sudarshana in His hand to indicate that He is the Master.

## 11.8 Matrum ula: Ultimate Request to the Lord

A dialogue between the azwar and the Lord runs thus: *The Azwar*: Why did you allow this soul which is precious like Kausthubha fall into deep abyss?

*The Lord* : Thinking that the Lord cannot notice involvement in sensual pleasures, you did not surrender yourself to Me and due to sins accumulated from beginningless time you got caught in the cycle of births and deaths.

*The azwar*: To get interested in You, (manas) mind is responsible. It will act as per Your dictates. Should You not give a good mind / heart. When You have to create interest in You, You cannot say that I did not have it. Is not karma dependent on Your ‘anugraha’ (grace)? Should You not excuse for the Karma resultant from Your ‘sankalpa’ (intention). Who will at all stop You? Protection of shareeris (ourselves) is that of the Shareera (The Lord). Have You forgotten this relationship? For my being in samsara, Your neglect is the reason.

While the Lord talked about karma, the azwar brought in the aspect of Brahman to obviate that argument. While the Lord talked about Sadhyopaaya (route), the azwar talked about Siddopaya (The Lord Himself). The prapannas have greater superiority than the devotees meditating on Him!

The Lord declared that once you have comprehended the ‘dreadful samsara’ and concluded that we cannot tolerate this, I shall take you to a nobler place! You better understand Your position.

The azwar ruminates over the fact that despite his having acquired knowledge, if the Lord co-operates then only he can reach ‘Paramapadham’. Addressing the Lord he says : ‘The obstacle in the form of the body be eliminated ; and protect me’, the azwar falls on Lord’s feet.

The azwar tells the Lord: ‘You have subjected me to the travails of samsara from beginningless time. Your answer is not saving me from the suffering. I am scared that You would drop me in some womb as per my karma’. The Lord rerplies: ‘Just because you had the possibility of another body, You got the understanding’. The azwar: ‘Isn’t it for the same reason I was separated from You? Is it not enough that I was in samsaara so long? Knowing it that You alone can take us out of this samsara, should we subject to this suffering?’ The Lord: ‘I have made you understand that I can snap your samsaaric bond’. The azwar: ‘If the floods uproot a tree, can the tree do anything to protect itself? My position is just the same.’

The Lord appeared a little angry (!) when He said: ‘Your egoism did not allow you to come under my control. You seem to reflect lack of kindness on My part when you say that I might drop you into a womb. You did not realise My greatness’. The azwar realises the mistakes on one’s part - to give pain to others; not following His tenets; doing ‘apachara’ to His devotees ; doing things which He may not excuse ; thinking that the soul belongs to you rather than Him; not understanding the purpose of His avataaras : all these lead to His getting angry. The azwar tells the Lord: ‘I have no other refuge. I am caught in a big storm. You have Sudarshana Chakra in Your hand. Please save me’.

The Lord appeared to say: ‘I will do as I want. You better keep quiet’. The azwar tells the Lord: ‘Having been a subject to countless births and the attendant suffering, I am scared whether You still may not accept me. To save from the hot sun, it is like taking the shade of a serpent’s hood; it is like living in a hut knowing that there is a snake ; my condition is just the same’. The Lord asks him as to ‘what He should do?’ The azwar replies : ‘Vibheeshana took refuge in Sri Rama and adjusted himself there. If You can bless me so as to reduce my ‘thaapa’, I can stay here also! When you look at the place as being yours it is rejectable ; but if you see it as that belonging to the Lord, it becomes our goal.’

I am scared that due to love for the body (dehathmabhimana) generated by births arising out of karma, You may make me enter the cycle again and subject me to suffering. It is like a white ant caught in a firewood burning on both ends. You please save me’. You save me in such a way that this samsara does not ‘flood’ me away. Doesn’t the mother save its child from falling into the well? It is not as big a task as saving one and all during deluge although no one expected it! But here I am expecting You to save me - or do I at all need to expect?

When the Lord suggests that he should go to Vayalaali and worship Him (Vayalali Manavala) rather than going elsewhere, the azwar replies: ‘Having this enemy of a body here and doing kainkaryam having not given the satisfaction, and pangs getting increased, I requested You that way’. I do not like to be a tree chopped for the grazing of animals!

‘Just as when the caterpillar growing on the bitter neem tree happens to consume sugarcane juice dies, are you similarly unique to Me?’ asks the Lord. The azwar replies: ‘Is not my ‘swaroopa’ (appearance opposite You) understandable to You? Am I not deserving? Sita Devi tells Anusooya that even if Sri Rama is characterless and ugly, I will still have ‘bhavabandha’ towards Him - this shows that the Lord is the only refuge. ‘You saved Balachandra (Moon) from Daksha’s curse. Those who desire to overcome inimical forces and take refuge in You, You will bless them accordingly.

The azwar addressing thus: ‘O Lord of flower-groves in ‘Aranganagar’.’ He is fascinated by Srirangam’s loveliness and the Lord’s reclining beauty. He became fearless. He is just waiting for a word of assurance that He is his protector. Natural Superiority / Supremacy characterising the Lord is such that the azwar cannot think of taking refuge anywhere else.

The hell of samsara has no end; we cannot but keep suffering our karma endlessly. All these cannot end by themselves. Does it mean we see, no end for this samsaara? The azwar states: ‘Karma, corresponding hell and us, who have to undergo all that. Is that all that exists? You are there, the Lord; since You, who can end all this, are there, an end could come’. Is He going to give ‘moksha’ to one and all then? Thondaranavarku, that is, to those who are interested in doing daily kainkaryam to Him, He will show His grace’. ‘Please grant Your kindness to me’ entreats the azwar with all humility.

The Lord revealed Himself. Just like in the Geetha He declared ‘ma shuchah’ (grieve not), the azwar has consoled those who were sad in the last nine verses, in his last verse. Those who recite these ten verses will end the bond with the ‘Prakrithi’ without a trace, besides getting experience of the Lord and His daily kainkaryam.

Tirumangai Azwars lotus feet our refuge**.**

She sees the red legs of the pigeons and is reminded of Nayaka’s red feet! She dances singing praise of the Lord in Tirutanka, Thirukkudanthai and Tirukkovaloor.

The mother talks about the daughter being adamant and wants to hear what she wants to say: she says “To start with she is absorbed in Lord’s beauty. His body is of dark colour and His eyes / lips / hands / feet all resemble the lotus petals. Devoted as He is to Bhoodevi, He has forgotten me (having been infatuated by Sridevi’s company). Mention that He resides in Srirangam, pat comes the query ‘where is Srirangam?’; and immediately she states she is proceeding to Tiruneermaiai. Is this is a sure sign of her unsteadiness?”

The friends tell the mother to advise her that since He is in love with others, she cannot get Him. The mother says that such talk had a reverse result. Seeing the noble couple, she overcomes her coyness. Not knowing the route to Srirangam she heaved a sigh. She does not listen to good words coming from anyone. Singing praises of Tirupper and then Tirukkudanthai, she seems to have lost touch of the order. Does anyone’s daughter have such strange behaviour?’

I cannot but say that my daughter is fortunate as she narrates the way He helped Sitadevi in Ramavataara and His grandson in Krishnavataara; also as Varaha to Bhoodevi and to the entire Universe during the deluge; swallowed the earth and took it out; measured the earth as Trivikrama. ‘Peralan’ signifies His rule over all in the Earth. Seeing my daughter recite the countless Srinamas, one would say she came of a group of Nithyasooris or is a Nithyasoori on Earth!’

## Pasuram 21-30 (Nayaki speaks)

The next ten songs are Nayaki pasurams. Without sending a messenger Nayaka Himself comes to meet her. Asked by her friend as to His mode of coming and what did he say while leaving (with the azwar in Sita’s stance), the Nayaki recounts : His hair was very black; this she noticed when the Nayaka not able to face her, turned His face; His fish-like earrings hung from His ears. His hands / lips / eyes and feet all resembled the red lotus. He came with a bow which was like Lakshmana’s, quite unique - Sesha (the Lord) and the Seshi came together. She forgets herself in the sea of His beauty. ‘Seeing His beauty and virtues I understood He was the Supreme Lord.’

The friend asks her regarding her experience of uniting with the Lord. She describes: ‘Despite his displaying His beauty and virtues, I kept a distance fearing that He is the Lord, when He pondered over the fact that having come should He return. His feet didn’t move. If the subservient thing is not received in hand, it means a loss and decided to entice me. He sang a song (Pann Naivalam!). In a trice my feet and heart were at His feet; bangles loosened; waist-band dropped. His fish-shaped ear rings and His four hands were in good view. When queried about His dwelling place after the union. He said it was Tiruvaali. The inner meaning of bangles falling is that the ‘I’ and ‘mine’ egos have been destroyed.

‘Should you not have told Him not to leave after the union’ asks her friend. The Nayaki replies: ‘Despite my telling Him not to leave He left. ‘He being under your control during union how could He leave you?’; The Nayaki answers : ‘Garuda told Him that this place was undeserving of Him and took Him away. He just left me with a mental disease which He did not divide with other consorts. He snatched my bangles and my complexion; the bangles gave way during the union, isn’t it? Having overcome ego, can I say they were my bangles’. ‘Did you ask Him about His town?’ ‘He did not utter your place or My place but simply our place’. His beauty has stolen her mind.

The sakhi (friend) persisted: ‘You said that Garuda took Him away. Why didn’t you stop him?’ He was not alone and was surrounded by Nithyasooris; besides, He had supreme lustre. I could not go near Him. Since I did not have my bangles which were under my control, I could not keep seeing them. How long this plight will continue? He as cloud drew all the water from the ocean, that is me. Even then He was not content. While leaving, His beauty was highly lustrous. During union it was the height of His accessibility, while during His departure it was the height of His Supremacy. All that I could do was sing ‘pallandu’ to Him from a distance. The one who should be imparting ecstacy left me with sorrow. He is kindness personified. That’s why He uttered ‘Our place is Aranga’.’ She tells her sakhi ‘Alas’! You did not see His beauty and loveliness at the time He left me!’

Showing everything around Him, He snatched all around me. First it was His divine idol and His face which was like a lightning in a dark cloud. His lustrous looks were blinding. His broad shoulders, hands, red lips, eyes were a treat to the eyes. Hands signifying strength, and supposed to grant everything in fact snatched every thing - my mind, my virtues. He converted me into a servant to carry all that on my head!

When her pangs of separation was unbearable she saw the six-legged bumble-bee and sent it as messenger - just as Sri Rama sent a messenger. Go right now to ‘aa maruvi appan’ (the deity at Tiruvazundhoor) - she was visibly jealous of the bee enjoying the company of its mate! Just like bees seek honey the acharya seeks ‘bhagavadvishayam’ - the two wings of the bee represent jnaana and anushtaana of the acharya. Unlike Hanuman, you can in fact rest on any part of the Lord or His Tulasi garland and talk to Him. She in fact does ‘pranamam’ to the bee for its role of ‘Purushaakaara’ - role of recommendation to join / become one with the Lord. He had mentioned Tiruvaranga as their place, but now she mentions Tiruvazundhoor - it is unlikely He has left me and gone to Tiruarangam: ‘Tell Him loud and clear that one - not mentioning my name - is in love with You. She loves the Supreme Lord, in archavatara. Wait and see His response. I shall give you a gift’.

Since the wait is quite long before the bee returned, she now enlists the stork (and others she comes across) and praised its legs, like He did when she was united with Him. Hurry to Tirukkannapuram - He may have travelled already with the bee and nearing this shrine. She tells, for identification, He is red lotus-eyed. Tell Him that He is always with me, even when separated. Tell Him of my love - do not forget yourself bewitched by His beauty! When He is told of my love, will He not come? (He will is the implied meaning). I shall benefit you giving all the watery patches and catch fish for you.

When He comes I shall be reticent, since union might lead to separation. She decides to bring an end to herself in His presence. I shall wait for the Lord who killed the demon king Ravana and waged the Bharatha war, and when He comes I shall hold Him in tight embrace of my pale breasts so that He does not break away from the bond ; let my sore limbs come alive and rejoice’. It is an example of ‘anger of love’ (pranaya rosha) cutting across the borders.

Still He did not come. She calls Him clear and loud, mixing manifestations of ‘Vibhava’ and ‘archa’. For His consorts He undertook difficult tasks - for getting Sridevi He did churning of the sea; He destroyed Ravana. ‘Shouldn’t He overcome my plight?’ is implied. The Lord is standing in ‘uppiliappan’ shrine and is reclining in Tirkkudanthai. He is sleeping with a vow, as it were, to root out ‘samsaara’. He is hard to get. In wickedness there is none equal to me.

The azwar talks about the phala / the benefit of studying this prabhandham. ‘Hearing the azwars painful cry, He appeared to him with all coolness, so much is indicated.’ ‘Is it great to grant eternal experience of Yourself to Nithyasooris? I always wanted to have eternal experience with You. You please grant Your grace’ the azwar tells the Lord. Those who master this prabhandham will cut asunder karmas from beginningiess time.

Thirumangai Azwar’s Lotus Feet our refuge.
