# Tirumangai Alvar and Buddhism

## Tirumangai Alvar and Buddhism

*veruppodu samanar mundar vidhiyilsak kiyarkal ninpal*  
*poruppari uanakal pesir povadhe noya dhakik*  
*kurippenak kadaiyu makil koodumel thalaiyai ange*  
*aruppathe karumam kanda yarangama nakaru lane*

**Перевод:**

***"O Lord, when the Buddhists and Jains, due to their intense hatred, happen to utter intolerable blasphemes against You, then I cannot tolerate such offences. When I get the chance, let me cut off their heads at the very place where they blasphemed You. That is certainly the most compassionate thing to do."*** **(Tirumangai Alvar's 'Tirumalai', verse 8)**

The Sri Vaisnava acarya Sri Periyavachan Pillai comments on this verse as follows:

"Tirumangai Alvar explains that the Jains and Buddhists have no gross material reason for hating the Lord -they do it neither for love of money or women. They do so only because they follow evil heretical doctrines. Therefore they are fit to be killed. When they hear the devotees singing the Lord's glories their faces turn red with hatred. When they observe the Lord's festivals in the streets they feel like hanging themselves out of frustration. When they hear the sankirtana of the Lord with various instruments they want to smash their heads against a wall.

They are devoid of all good fortune to understand the Lord and His glories and those who are followers of the Vedas cannot tolerate their offensive statements. Tirumangai Alvar cannot even think of repeating their heinous statements. He says that it is better to kill oneself if he is impotent to defeat such words when they happen to say these things in front of him.

*kuror yatra parivadho*  
*ninda vayapi pravarthate*  
*karnau tatra pidatavyau*  
*ganthavyam va tatoyanyathah*

**Перевод:**

**"One should shut one's ears where superiors are abused or one should at least try to go away from that place." (Manu 2-200)**

If one cannot take ones own life or leave that place and if one gets the opportunity to destroy the blasphemer then one should do so without getting destroyed in the process. The right thing to do is to decapitate those who have abused the Lord. This is an act for the good of that person. Since cutting off the head of the abusers prevents them from doing further 'bhagavan-ninda', it is beneficial for everyone since the innocent public will not have to listen to more offences. This act is even good for the abuser because they cannot abuse the Lord further and increase their suffering in future. Sastra says that, in the agnikshomiya-yajna, both the sacrificial animal as well as the person who sacrifices the animal goes to heaven. Likewise, here, this is good for both the killer and the killed.

Tirumangai tells the Lord, "The reason you have incarnated in this form in Sri Rangam is to destroy Your enemies, is it not? Therefore it is Your duty to destroy the enemies of dharma like the Jains and Buddhists.

A question arises: How can a kind and religious person who follows dharma such as Tirumangai Alvar talk about killing when even the Vedas prohibit killing living entities ('na himsyat sarva bhutaani – 'no living organism should be killed' - Yajur Veda 2-5-5) However, the Vedas have prescribed sacrificing a cow in yajnas like the agniksomiyam-yajna for the benefit of entire humanity. Likewise, the Alvar also talks about killing in the interest of the abusers of our Lord. Therefore, it is not wrong of sinful."
