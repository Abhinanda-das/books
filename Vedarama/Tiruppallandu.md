# Tiruppallandu

## 1. Pallandu

*pallāṇḍu pallāṇḍu pallāyirattāṇḍu palakōti nūṟayiram mallāṇḍa tiṇṭhōḷ maṇivaṇṇā un cēvadi cevvit tirukkāppu*

**Пословный перевод:**

mal – mallars (wrestlers) like cāṇūran, muśtikan; āṇḍa – one who controlled and killed them; tiṇ – strong and most powerful; toḷ – shoulders; maṇivaṇṇā – one who has the color of dark gem; un – your; cēvadi – reddish feet’s; cevvit – beauty; pallāṇḍu – many (countless) years (denotes year count in human); pallāṇḍu – many (countless) years (denotes year count in dēva); pallāyirattāṇḍu – many (countless) years (denotes year count in brahmā); palakōti nūṟayiram – many years of countless brahmās; tirukkāppu – let there be protection; In the first pāsuram, periyāḻvār expresses let there be protection for all auspiciousness to bhagavān’s reddish lotus feet for countless years. āḻvār starts with human years and then moves on to dēva’s (dēvatās – celestial beings) years and for one brahmā’s years and then countless bramhās’ years!

**Перевод:**

**pallāṇḍu – āḻvār is saying āṇḍu (years) instead of days. The reason is that years denote one’s age/life-time. pallāṇḍu (long live), jitam (let there be victory), nama: (I am for you, I surrender unto you), tōṟṟōm \[tiruvāimoḻi 2.1.7\] (you have won), pōṟṟi \[tiruppāvai, etc\] (long live) are all synonyms. pallāṇḍu – āḻvār is again saying owing to āḻvār’s svarūpam (not stopping with once) and īsvara’s svarūpam (on seeing īsvara’s beauty, one cannot say this just once) as well. Also, just like a thirsty person will say “water, water” again and again until his thirst is quenched, āḻvār will repeat “pallāṇḍu” until his fear is eliminated. One big question arises here – it is normally seen that elders/superiors wish well and bless the younger/inferior ones. But when there is too much love and affection towards someone, even if they are superior to one, one could bless them for their well-being. On seeing that āḻvār started fearing for Him and performing mangaḷāsāsanam, perumāḷ thought of removing āḻvār’s fear and showed his strength, similar to the rāmāyana incident. When sugrīva mahārājar feared for perumāḷ and did not want to take vibīśaṇāḻvān, perumāḷ explained his strength and that removed mahārājar’s fears. So, here, perumāḷ shows his strong shoulders using which he killed cāṇūra and muśtika in kamsa’s court. But this increased āḻvār’s fears, similar to a mother who always worries for her brave son who could easily get into fight with others. āḻvār started fearing that if dēvatās surrender to Him worrying about demons, He will go near the demons to win over – āḻvār worries that going near such demons could harm perumāḷ. cevvadi – Contrasting/beautiful colors – divine blackish body and reddish lotus feet – Whenever a sēśabhūta (servant) sees his sēśi (master), he always looks at the tiruvadigaḷ (lotus feet). This pāsuram is like a preface for the rest of the pāsurams.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*periyāḻvār  on seeing bhagavān with most beautiful form which reveals his auspicious qualities such as saundaryam (beauty), etc., in this world which is controlled by kālam (time), out of great fear starts thinking “what bad might happen to him?” and performs mangaḷāsāsanam (well-wishing thoughts) to emperumān‘s divine and beautiful form to stay like that forever. பல்லாண்டு பல்லாண்டு பல்லாயிரத்தாண்டு பலகோடி நூறாயிரம் மல்லாண்ட திண்தோள் மணிவண்ணா! உன் செவ்வடி செவ்வி திருக்காப்பு*

## 2. Adiyomodum

*adiyōmōdum ninnōdum pirivinṟi āyiram pallāṇḍu vadivāy nin valamārbinil vāḻkinṟa mangaiyum pallāṇḍu vadivār cōti valattuṟaiyum cudarāḻiyum pallāṇḍu padaipōr pukku muḻangum appānjasanniyamum pallāṇḍē*

**Пословный перевод:**

adiyōmōdum – adiyen and all other cētanas who are servants for you; ninnōdum – and yourself who is the master for all of us; pirivinṟi – should be always together; āyiram pallāṇḍu – this relationship should exist forever; vadivāy – Beautiful and one who is decorated with jewels; nin valamārbinil vāḻkinṟa – one who always dwells in right side of your chest; mangaiyum – mangai denotes nangai (denotes completeness/wholeness of srī mahālakśmi); “um” (also) – denotes bhūmi and neeḷā dēvis; pallāṇḍu – should be present always; vadivārcōti – cakkarattāḻvān’s tējas (radiance) surrounds the divine form of perumāḷ himself; valattuṟaiyum – one who eternally resides in right hand; cudarāḻiyum – cakkarattāḻvān who destroys the enemies; pallāṇḍu – long live – should always exist; padai – that serves as weapon; pōr pukku muḻangum – in battle by its sound; appānjasanniyamum – the conch by name pāncajanya; pallāṇḍē – should also be there with you always

**Перевод:**

**adiyōmōdum – with this servant – One question might arise, why is āḻvār doing mangalāsāsanam for his own eternal existence? This is because, no one can seek out for the well-being of emperumān like āḻvār can do. Hence, āḻvār is asking that he should be present always so that he can do mangaḷāsāsanam. Why did he not say “ennōdum” (with me)? This is because āḻvār is absolutely clear about his svarūpam (true nature) as a servant of emperumān. Why did he say “adiyōmōdum” in plural instead of “adiyēnōdum” in singular? He is not satisfied by doing mangaḷāsāsanam individually and since everyone is a servant of emperumān (whether they know/accept it or not) he includes everyone in his gōśti (group) to perform mangaḷāsāsanam for emperumān. vadivāy nin valamārbinil vāḻginṟa mangai – sāstram declares “sraddayā devō dēvatvam asnutē” – By being with srī mahālakśmī, emperumān becomes dēva (vorśippable – supreme). Would it minimize emperumān’s greatness by saying this?  Not at all. By saying a gem is great due to its radiance, or flower is great due to its fragrance, the glories of gem/flower is not minimized – its only increased for having such radiance/fragrance. Similarly, bhagavān being the master of srī mahālakśmi, if she bring more glories, it only adds to the greatness of bhagavān. mangaiyum – She is glorified as yuvtisca kumāriṇī and he is glorified as yuvā kumāra: both of which mean “youthful adolescence”. They have ever-youthful and matching divine forms. ap-pānjasanniyamum – āḻvār is saying as if emperumān is not in front of him (“ap” word is used when denoting something which is not in front of us, similar to “that” in English). This is similar to mothers shying away from seeing their children fearing of jinxing (spoiling) their well-being. In this pāsuram, āḻvār has included himself and other cētanas who are part of leelā vibhūti and periya pirāttiyār, tiruvāḻi āḻvān (disc), tiruccangāḻvān (conch) who are in nitya vibhūti (paramapadam). Hence, this pāsuram is mangaḷāsāsanam for emperumān who has both nitya vibhūti (spiritual realm) and leelā vibhūti (material realm). The first 2 pāsurams reveal the meanings of tirumantram. praṇavam is explained by “adiyōmōdum” in this pāsuram. “pallāṇḍu” in the previous pāsuram explains “nama:”. In the previous pāsuram “mallāṇḍa tiṇ tōḷ maṇivaṇṇā” and the second pāsuram explains “nārāyaṇa“. “un cevvadi cevvi tirukkāppu” explains kainkaryam which is requested by “āya”.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*In the previous pāsuram, periyāḻvār did mangaḷāsāsanam for emperumān’s tirumēni (divine form) and his kalyāṇa guṇams (auspicious qualities). In this pāsuram, mangaḷāsāsanam is done for emperumān being with ubhayavibhūti (wealth of both spiritual and material realms). \*அடியோமோடும் நின்னோடும் பிரிவின்றி ஆயிரம் பல்லாண்டு! வடிவாய் நின் வல மார்பினில் வாழ்கின்ற மங்கையும் பல்லாண்டு! வடிவார் சோதி வலத்துறையும் சுடராழியும் பல்லாண்டு! படைப்போர் புக்கு முழங்கும் அப் பாஞ்சசன்னியமும் பல்லாண்டே.*

## 3. Valatpattu

*vāḻātpattu ninṟīruḷḷīrēl vantu maṇṇum maṇamum koṇmin kūḻātpattu ninṟīrkaḷai engaḷ kuḻuvinil pugutalottōm ēḻātkālum paḻippilōm nāngaḷ irākkatar vāḻ ilangai pāḻāḷāgap padai porutānukkup pallānṇḍu kūṟutumē*

**Пословный перевод:**

vāḻāḷ pattu – if you are engrossed in bhagavat kainkaryam; ninṟīruḷḷīrēl – and stand on that niśtai (intention/ways); vantu – join with us (āḻvār); maṇṇum maṇamum koṇmin – and for the utsavams for emperumān, please take soil (tirumuḷait tirunāḷ – sprouting ritual which is done before the utsavam) and get immersed in the utsavam; kūḻāḷ pattu ninṟīrkaḷai – if you are serving other people to fulfill your worldly needs; engaḷ kuḻuvinil pugutalottōm – we won’t allow you to join us; nāngaḷ – us; ēḻātkālum paḻippilōm – We are flawless/faultless for 21 generations; irākkatar vāḻ ilangai – lankā which is filled with demons; pāḻāḷāga – getting destroyed; padai porutānukku – who went to battle field; pallāṇḍu kūṟutum – we are singing pallāṇḍu now

**Перевод:**

**āḻvār first calls for bhagavat prāpti kāmar as ninṟīruḷḷīrēl (Are there any one?). Then āḻvār saw lot of others also joining and stopped such prayōjanāntaraparars (one who wishes for things other than bhagavān himself). This means prayōjanāntaraparars are available in lots and one does not have to search for them like bhagavatprāptikāmar as mentioned in “uḷḷīrēl” (Are there any one?). In this samsāram, vaiṣṇavatvam (being a true vaiṣṇava) is difficult to find. maṇṇum maṇamum  – kainkarayaparars (Servants) lead from front while collecting soil for utsavam. Similarly, when there is a maṇam (marriage/festival) in the master’s house, the servants would fully engage themselves in the events. Similarly, āḻvār is asking everyone to engage in such activities for emperumān. kūḻāḷ – one who comes to the party just for food – this is an example for prayōjanāntaraparars (one who desires benefits other than bhagavān himself). pugutalottōm – We will not include such prayōjanāntaraparars in our group. A question arises here. Is āḻvār not merciful towards such persons? He is merciful – yet such persons cannot be included in the group of devotees (because they may spoil the devotional mood). Even though sage vasiśta may be most merciful, he will not invite a caṇḍāḷa (dog-eater) to perform agni kāryam (yāgam, etc). So, it is emphasised here that, for such devotees of emperumān, relationship with prayōjanāntaraparars is to be avoided. paḻippilōm – We have no defects. Here āḻvār is not talking about basic defects such as viśayāntara prāvaṇyam (attachment to worldly pleasures),  dēvatāntara bhajanam (worshiping other dēvatās), etc. But he is talking about seeking out other benefits than emperumān himself and engaging in other upāyams such as karma, jñāna, bhakti yōgam, etc. pallāṇḍu kūṟutum – When sītā pirātti got separated from perumāḷ (srī rāma), brahmā and other dēvatās were happy that rāvaṇan will soon be destroyed, behaving like prayōjanāntaraparars. Since nobody was there at that time to perform mangaḷāsāsanam, āḻvār is saying that we are doing that now.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*periyāḻvār did mangaḷāsāsanam in the first 2 pāsurams. Not feeling satisfied about that (āḻvār himself singing alone), āḻvār thinking of calling bhagavat prāpti kāmar (one who wishes to serve bhagavān), kaivalyārtti (one who asks for kaivalyam/self-enjoyment) & aisvaryārtti (one who asks for aisvaryam/wealth) as mentioned in bhagavat gītā 7.16 “caturvidā bhajantē mām” and first calls bhagavat prāpti kāmar. As said in “ēkasvādu na bhunjīta” (one should not enjoy alone), he is inviting others so they too can enjoy bhagavat viśayam. He is also inviting them so that he can perform mangaḷāsāsanam for bhagavān along with his devotees instead of doing that alone. வாழாட்பட்டு நின்றீருள்ளீரேல் வந்து மண்ணும் மணமும் கொண்மின் கூழாட்பட்டு நின்றீர்களை எங்கள் குழுவினில் புகுதலொட்டோம் ஏழாட்காலும் பழிப்பிலோம் நாங்கள் இராக்கதர் வாழ் இலங்கை பாழாளாகப் படை பொருதானுக்குப் பல்லாண்டு கூறுதமே*

## 4. Edunilam

*ēdu nilattil iduvatan munnam vaṇṭhu engaḷ kuḻām puguntu kūdu manam udaiyīrgaḷ varamboḻi vantollaik kūduminō nādu nagaramum nangaṟiya namō nārāyaṇāya enṟu pādu manam udaip pattar uḷḷīr vantu pallāṇḍu kūṟuminē*

**Пословный перевод:**

ēdu nilattil iduvatan munnam – Before you are put in the graveyard/crematory ground; vantu engaḷ kuḻām puguntu – come, join our ghōśti (group); kūdu manam udaiyīrgaḷ – if you have that thought (of joining us); varamboḻi vantu – giving up kaivalyam; ollai – soon/immediately; kūduminō – come and join our ghōsti; nādum – people living in country-side; nagaramum – people living in cities; nangaṟiya – for both to be cognizance of your good; namō nārāyaṇā enṟu – chanting tirumantram; pādu manam udaip pattar uḷḷīr vantu – if you have the bhakti to sing tirumantram; pallānṇḍu kūṟuminē – come and sing tirupallāṇḍu

**Перевод:**

**ēdu nilam – “ēdu” is explained as graveyard by piḷḷai amudanār. “ēdu” is also explained as sūskhma sarīram (subtle body). It is explained as,  before placing the body in graveyard or before the body becomes transformed into sūkśma state (as part of praḷayam (annihilation)). This shows the concern of āḻvār towards the helpless state of such kaivalyārttis after attaining their goal. engaḷ kuḻām puguntu – In this ghōśti (group), everyone is happy for others’ pēṟu (welfare)!! Generally, kaivalyārttis would enjoy ones own ātmā individually – there is no sharing/caring for others. But our bhāgavata gōśti is always sharing/caring for others. kūdu manam udaiyīrgaḷ – What is the condition to join in this ghōśti? āḻvār says just “desire” is enough. Since singing mangaḷāsāsanam for emperumān is natural for jīvātmā, one need not do anything even physically – just the desire itself is enough. For example, to serve ones parents, all one needs is the desire to serve them and the parents will happily accept him/her. Similarly, to serve emperumān, we only need desire. ollaik kūdminō – Understanding the greatness of bhagavān, should you not come immediately? nādu – place of uncivilized/uneducated nagaram – place of civilized/educated nangaṟiya – come to us so that your old friends (other kaivalyārttis) give up on you and bhāgavatas accept you for that reason (of giving up old friends). namō nārāyaṇāya – Doing some thing favourable for the master is the real goal. pādu manamudaip pattaruḷḷīr – (After approaching) just thinking would not be enough, you have to release your love/devotion towards emperumān.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*After calling bhagavat prāpti kāmar (one who wants to serve bhagavān), periyāḻvār still not satisfied, starts calling out for kaivalyārtti (ātma prāpti kāmar  – ones who are interested in self-enjoyment) and aisvaryārtti (ones who desire wealth). āmongst those two, first, he calls for ātma prāpti kāmar. Why should āḻvār first call ātma prāpti kāmar instead of aisvaryārtti? It is not because ātma prāpti kāmar are greater than aisvaryārtti as āḻvār very well knows that kaivalya mōkśam (enjoying oneself eternally) is very dangerous (since its a point of no return). The actual reason being that if ātma prāpti kāman relinquishes the sarīram (body), there is no way he can get the parama puruśārttam (ultimate destiny) (because he can never get another sarīram and attain mōkśam eventually). At least for asivaryārtti, if not now, some day he can start thinking about bhagavat kainkarya mōkśam (serving emperumān eternally) – but for kaivalyārtti, that option is not available. So, he is in greater danger and that is why āḻvār invites such kaivalyārttis now. ஏடு நிலத்தில் இடுவதன் முன்னம் வந்து எங்கள் குழாம் புகுந்து கூடு மன முடையீர்கள் வரம்பொழி வந்துஒல்லைக் கூடுமினோ நாடும் நகரமும் நன்கறிய நமோ நாராயணாய என்று பாடு மனம் உடைப் பத்தர் உள்ளீர் வந்து பல்லாண்டு கூறுமினே*

## 5. Andakkulam

*aṇḍak kulattukku adipatiyāgi asurar irākkatarai iṇḍak kulattai eduttuk kaḷainta irudīkēsan tanakku toṇḍak kulattiluḷḷīr vantu aditoḻutu āyira nāmam collip paṇḍaik kulaittait tavirntu pallāṇḍu pallāyirattāṇḍu enminē*

**Пословный перевод:**

aṇḍakkulattukku – collection of all the aṇḍams (worlds); adipatiyāki – one who is the master of such collection; asurar irākkatarai – demons and asuras; iṇḍak kulattai – assemblage of demons and asuras; yeduttuk kaḷainta – garner such assemblage and abolish them; irudīkēcan – one who is master of all iṇḍhriyas (senses); toṇḍak kulattil uḷḷīr – who is present in dāsya kulam (clan of servitors of emperumān); vantu aditoḻutu – come to our ghōśti and invoke lotus feet of accutan; āyira nāmam colli – singing His thousand nāmas (divine names); paṇḍaik kulaittai – you were in a clan, where you would go to puruśōttaman and desire for other puruśārttams (goals) than bhagavān himself; tavirntu – give up that clan; pallāyirattāṇḍu enminē – and perform mangaḷāsāsanam

**Перевод:**

**aṇḍakkulattukku adipadi – Why does an aisvaryārtti call bhagavān as aṇḍādipatayē nama: (I worship the master of all worlds)? When a magnanimous person is holding a lemon fruit and we say to him that the lemon is beautiful, immediately he will give the lemon to us. Similarly aisvaryārttis glorify bhagavān as aṇḍādipati, so that He will give one of the galaxies to them. emperumān is so generous that he glorifies such aisvaryārtti also as “udārā:” in bhagavat gītā 7.18 while talking about the four types of persons who approach him {because such person gives emperumān an opportunity to bring out His generosity!} asurarar irākkatarai – Then why do they again need to glorify as asurar irākkatarai…..? aisvaryārttis are of two types. (please refer to bhagavat gītā 7.16 catur vidā bhajantē mām slōkam). apūrva aisvarya kāman – One who desires for new aisvaryam (the one explained previously) and braśta aisvarya kāman – one who is asking for the aisvaryam he has lost. First type of aisvaryārtti sings “aṇḍātipatayē nama:” and second one will sing as “asura catravē nama:” (I worship the enemy of demons) {like when wealth is lost to demons/others}. irudīkēsan – hruśikēsa (One who controls our senses) – āḻvār is saying to aisvaryārtti “when you go to him for requesting wealth, just see his beauty and forget the wealth – this way, you too can surrender unto him”. toṇḍakkulattil uḷḷīr – the ones who belong to the clan of servitors consider “sēśatvamē svarūpam” (being a servitor is the true nature) unlike considering oneself as the body and seeking out bodily pleasures. vantadi toḻutu āyira nāmam colli – Accepting that serving the lotus feet of  emperumān is the goal and reciting all the names of emperumān joyfully. pallāṇḍu pallāyirattāṇḍu enmin – Now that you too have become ananya prayōjana (not expecting anything other than kainkaryam), what are you waiting for? Start performing mangaḷāsāsanam immediately which is fitting for both your svarūpam (true nature) and bhagavān‘s greatness.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*First periyāḻvār  invited ananya prayōjanar (one who does not have any personal desire and only looks after the welfare of emperumān). Though kaivalyārtti and aisvaryārtti are both prayōjanāntaraparars (ones who go to emperumān to seek out other benefits instead of pure kainkaryam to emperumān), seeing the suffering of kaivalyārtti,  periyāḻvār invited him first. Next, periyāḻvār invites aisvaryārtti. அண்டக் குலத்துக் அதிபதியாகி அசுரர் இராக்கதரை இண்டைக் குலத்தை எடுத்துக் களைந்த இருடீகேசன் தனக்கு தொண்டக் குலத்திலுள்ளீர் வந்து அடிதொழுது ஆயிர நாமம் சொல்லி பண்டைக் குலத்தைத் தவிர்ந்து பல்லாண்டு பல்லாயிரத்தாண்டு என்மினே*

## 6. Entai tantai

**Пословный перевод:**

word-by-word meanings entai – me and my father; tantai – his father; tantai – his father; tam mūttappan – his father (and ancestor); ēḻ padi kāl todangi – starting from seven generations before; vantu – having come (at appropriate time to perform mangaḷāsāsanam); vaḻi vaḻi – in proper manner; āḷ ceyginṟōm – serving; tiruvōṇat tiruviḻavil – on the auspicious day of sravaṇam (star of the tamiḻ month); anti am pōtil – beautiful sun-set time (when the strength of asuras increase); ari uru āgi – assuming the form of lion-man; ariyai – hiraṇyakasipu who is the enemy (of prahlāda who is his devotee); aḻittavanai – one who destroyed him; pantanai tīra – to eliminate his fatigue (from killing hiraṇya); pallāṇḍu pallāyirattāṇḍu enṟu pādutum – we will perform mangaḷāsāsanam to him for eternity Highlights from periyavāccān piḷḷai’s commentary: entai – him and his father tantai tantai tantai – three generations from his father tam mūttappan – “appan” means father of the third generation person, and “mūttappan” indicates his father. They do that to show their affection towards those who performed mangaḷāsāsanam to emperumān. As explained in āpastamba sūtram 1.1.6 “sa hi vidyātastam janayati taccrēśtam janma” (The preceptor makes the disciple become born-again through knowledge; this birth is the best) and manu smruti 2.146 “karīyān brahmata: pitā” (knowledge giving father is greater than body giving father), knowledge-based relationship is greatly glorified; but is it ōK to glorify just body-based relationships which are explained to be given up in vihagēsvara samhita 22 “pitaram mātaram dārān” (Mother, Father, wives, etc., are to be given up)? They can be glorified – if they are connected to bhagavān. Even if one is greatly knovledgable, if he does not accept bhagavān, he is to be given up – so the underlying factor is bhagavat sambandam (being related to bhagavān). ēḻpadikāl todangi – Though the seven generations are explained individually, here collectively the family heritage is thought about due to the attachment for the same. vantu – coming forward at the appropriate time, etc., for mangaḷāsāsanam vaḻi vaḻi āḷ ceyginṟōm – We serve(d) in proper ways without any faults, i.e., without interruption (in generations) for performing mangaḷāsāsanam. Just like a family which did not undergo vēda adyayanam for 3 generations, the ones who are born in such family are not qualified to be brāhmaṇa and cannot engage in any vaidīka karma, when a family does not perform mangaḷāsāsanam for 3 generations, those who are born in such families cannot be qualified as vaiṣṇava. Such defect is not present in our family. “vaḻi vaḻi” (repetition) indicates the path of sāstram and sistācāram (practice of ācāryas) as well. āḷ ceyginṟōm – Serving, means singing “tiruppallāṇḍu” (performing mangaḷāsāsanam). Until now, they explained the glories of their family. Further, they speak about their activities. tiruvōṇat tiruviḻavil – āḻvār saying “sravaṇam” as the day of appearance (which is the general birth-star for srīman nārāyaṇan) instead of the specific time – this is because, āḻvār fears if the enemies know emperumān‘s exact birth date/time, they may try to harm him. antiyam pōtil – When the dēvas are weakened and the asuras are stronger. ari uru āgi – In the beautiful form of narasimha which is to be glorified immediately as explained in sahasranāmam “nārasimha vapu:srīmān” (one who is having a human-lion form, which is extremely beautiful), nānmugan tiruvantādi – 22 “aḻagiyān tānē ari uruvan tānē” (he is the beautiful person; he is in the form of a lion), etc. ariyai aḻittavanai – ari – catru (enemy). He completely destroyed hiraṇyakasipu who had enmity towards him from birth. āḻvār fears highlighting this incident because he thinks if the descendants (or other asuras) come to know about this, they will show enmity towards bhagavān. pantanai tīra – to cool-off the fatigue of killing such evil hiraṇyakasipu; pantanai means certain disease which affects new-born babies – since narasimhan emperumān killed hiraṇyakasipu on the same day of appearance, āḻvār fears for such disease. pallāṇḍu – āḻvār thinks that – on hearing the mangaḷāsāsanam from devotees (who are favourable), such fatigue will be removed quickly. pallāyirattāṇḍu enṟu pādutumē – Not feeling satisfied with saying pallāṇḍu just once, they say it should be said/done for eternity. This is identified as the activity of such devotees. adiyēn sāraty rāmānuja dāsan archived in https://divyaprabandam.koyil.org pramēyam (goal) – https://koyil.org; pramāṇam (scriptures) – http://grantams.koyil.org/; pramātā (preceptors) – https://acaryas.koyil.org/; srīvaiṣṇava education/kids portal – http://pillai.koyil.org

*Previously, āḻvār invited prayōjanāntaraparar (those who are having ulterior goals), i.e., both kaivalyārtti (in 4th pāsuram) and aisvaryārtti (in 5th pāsuram), after inviting ananyaprōjanar (ones who are focussed on kainkaryam only in 3rd pāsuram) – this is because āḻvār sees bhagavān‘s magnanimity in accepting all as mentioned in srī bhagavata gītā 6.18 “udārās sarva ēvaitē” (All of them (who approach me) are generous) and considering his relationship with all entities. ## 7. Tiyil poliginra

*tīyil poliginṟa senjudarāḻi tigaḻ tiruccakkarattin kōyil poṟiyālē oṟṟuṇḍu ninṟu kudi kudi āḷ seyginṟōm māyap poru padai vāṇanai āyiram tōḷum poḻi kurudi pāya suḻaṟṟiya āḻi vallānukkup pallāṇḍu kūṟutumē*

**Пословный перевод:**

tīyil – even greater than fire, sun, etc; poliginṟa – greatly shining; sem sudar – having reddish radiance; āḻi – circular in shape; tigaḻ – shining; tiruccakkarattin kōyil – the abode of srī sudarasana; poṟiyālē – with the symbol; oṟṟuṇḍu ninṟu – having marked with; kudi kudi – generation after generation; āḷ seyginṟōm – came to serve; māyap poru padai – having an army which is able to engage in cunning warfare; vāṇanai – bāṇāsura’s; āyiram tōḷum – from the 1000 shoulders; poḻi kurudi pāya – blood flowing like a flood; suḻaṟṟiya – circling; āḻi – sudarasana cakram; vallānukku – the capable one who holds it; pallāṇḍu kūṟutum – we are singing tiruppallāṇḍu

**Перевод:**

**tīyil poliginṟa sem sudar āḻi – They are remembering sudarsana cakram with gratitude for enabling them to enter the vaiṣṇava gōśti \[Those who wear sankam/cakram as part of tāpa samskāram become part of srīvaiṣṇava gōśti\]. tīyil poliginṟa – Here, “tī” (agni) indicates all items that have radiance, such as moon, sun, etc. paramapadam (spiritual realm) is more radiant than sun, etc. bhagavān‘s divine auspicious form is more radiant than paramapadam. sudarsana cakram’s radiance is even greater than that. He is glorified as “vadivār sōdi valattuṟaiyum sudarāḻi” in tiruppallāṇḍu 1.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*Here, kaivalyārttis (those who are focussed on self-enjoyment) who were invited by āḻvār, arrive highlighting their qualities and āḻvār associates with them. āḻvār, when invited them, requested them to come giving up their boundaries (varamboḻi vantu ollaik kūduminō), accepting bhagavān only as  the ultimate goal (namō nārāyaṇāya) and come so that greatly knovledgable people can accept them and ignorant people can reject them (nādu nakaramum nangaṟiya). Contemplating on that, they think “We can give up insignificant results and become fully focussed on bhagavān. But, how can we make the devotees accept us and others reject us?” and then realize “If we accept the vaiṣṇava symbols such as having sankam and cakram marks in the śouldrers, acceptance/rejection will naturally happen”. They then wear those symbols, announce the same and arrive at āḻvār. தீயில் பொலிகின்ற செஞ்சுடராழி திகழ் திருச்சக்கரத்தின் கோயில் பொறியாலே ஒற்றுண்டு நின்று குடி குடி ஆட்செய்கின்றோம் மாயப் பொரு படை வாணனை ஆயிரம் தோளும் பொழி குருதி பாயச் சுழற்றிய ஆழி வல்லானுக்குப் பல்லாண்டு கூறுதுமே*

While it is stated in katōpaniśat 2.5 “*tasya bhāsā sarvamidam vibhāti*” (Everything acquires its radiance from [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)), how is it possible that bhagavān’s form shines due to sudarsana cakram?

- *sem sudar* – the radiance that is present due to the reddish glow of sudarsana cakram which is a contrasting feature for the dark-cloudiś form, like a lamp lit in a dark room.
- *āḻi tigaḻ tiruccakkaram* – Like a flood in a narrow space, sudarsana cakram who is glowing in a circular space
- *cakkarattin kōyil* – sudarsanāḻvār’s abode
- *poṟiyālē oṟṟuṇḍu* – Being marked with symbol of sudarsana cakram. Only after accepting this mark, he becomes firmly qualified to be liberated instead of being born again in this samsāram (material realm). [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s acceptance too is guaranteed only on having this tāpa samskāram (part of [panca samskāram](http://ponnadi.blogspot.in/2012/07/srivaishnava-lakshanam-2.html)). Once, when kriṣṇa was leaving srī dvārakā to protect his devotees from their enemies, the gate-keepers asked him “Who can enter here and who can not enter here?”, kriṣṇa replied “*cakrāngitā: pravēśtavyā yāvadāgamanam mama \|  nāmudritā: pravēśtavyā yāvadāgamanam mama \|\|*” (hari vamsam 27.24 – Until I return, those who are marked with the symbol of sudarsana cakram are qualified to enter and others are not) – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) himself says “those who have the symbol can enter directly and others need to be tested before being allowed”. So, this chakra dāraṇam (having of sudarsana cakram symbol) is that one which facilitates [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s acceptance, that which keeps the [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) peaceful thinking that “I am his property” and that which creates fear in yamarāja, etc., who want to punish the [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) for his sins. It is also that which reminds the jīvātmā of his true relationship with [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) as stated in kūrma purāṇam “*cakrādi dāraṇam pumsām para sambanda vēdanam \| pativratā nimittam hi vaḷayādi vibhūśaṇam*” (By marking the symbols of cakram, etc., the relationship with [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is revealed. īsn’t ornaments such as bangles, etc., an indication of a chaste wife?)
- *kudi kudi āḷ seginṟōm* – We came here along with our children, grand children, etc., to serve. To sing tiruppallāṇḍu is the service for such persons who are fully existing for [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/).

When asked, for what incident, are you performing mangaḷāsāsanam?

- *māyap poru padai* … – We are singing tiruppallāṇḍu for the valiant aspect of sudarasana cakram which brought us here.
- *māyap poru padai vāṇanai* – māya first explained as magnificent; Explained as bāṇāsaura who is having magnificient army; also explained as bāṇāsura who is having magnificent weapons. māyam is also explained as “deceptive”, i.e., one who is performing deceptive/cunning warfare.
- *āyiram tōḷum poḻi kurudi pāya* – Blood flowing from the thousand shoulders and filling all the earth like water let out from a dam;

What did [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) do to make this happen?

- *suḻaṟṟiya* – He need not send the cakram, just spinning it is sufficient. Just spinning it once caused the thousand shoulders of bāṇāsura to fall. [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) let go of bāṇāsura (without cutting his head) not because of bāṇāsura not committing enough mistakes. But it is because – he did not want uśā (his grand-daughter-in-law) to be father-less and to leave a scar to highlight that “this is the result of vorśipping other dēvatās”. That is, though rudra vowed to protect bāṇāsura, when it came to the battle, he ran away from the battle field along with his subordinates leaving bāṇāsura to defend himself. In the end, bāṇāsura and rudra had to console each other, rudra saying “This is what I got for trying to protect you” and bāṇāsura saying “This is what I got for surrendering on to you”.
- *āḻi vallānukku* – One who is in full control of cakram. This is like “*vilvallān*” (one who is great in bow/arrow), “*vāḷvallān*” (one who is great in sword), etc. Being the husband of [srī mahālakśmi](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sri-mahalakshmi/), climbing on garuda, holding on to sudasana cakram, etc., are unique qualities identifying total supremacy over everyone else.
- *pallāṇḍu kūṟutumē* – There were not anyone who would sing pallāṇḍu for [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s eternal servitor sudarsanāḻvār’s valour and the valour of kriṣṇa who accepted us as servitors – to make-up for such loss, today we are singing tiruppallāṇḍu for you.

## 8. Neyyidai nallator

*neyyidai nallatōr sōṟum niyatamum attāṇic cēvakamum kai adaikkāyum kaḻuttukkup pūṇodu kātukkuk kuṇḍalamum meyyida nallatōr sāntamum tantu ennai veḷḷuyirākka valla paiyudai nāgap pagaik kodiyānukkup pallāṇḍu kūṟuvanē*

**Пословный перевод:**

ney idai – in between ghee; nallatu ōr sōṟum – rice which is seen in there, that which is placed with pure heart and that which tastes delicious; niyatamum – always; attāṇic cēvakamum – inseparable service; kai – placed by (emperumān with his own) hand; adaik kāyum – betel leaves and nuts; kaḻuttukkup pūṇodu kādukkuk kuṇḍalamum – ornaments for the neck and ears; meyyida – fit to be applied on the body; nallatu ōr sāntamum – very fragrant sandalwood paste; tantu – given; ennai – (most lowly) me; veḷ uyir ākka valla – to transform me to be pure natured; pai udai – having hoods; nāgam – for the serpent; pagai – garuda who is the enemy of (serpent); kodiyānukku – one who has it (garuda) in his flag; pallāṇḍu kūruvan – will perform mangaḷāsāsanam

**Перевод:**

**neyyidai – idai means abundance and amidst. This means – rice with abundance of ghee and rice morsels in the midst of ghee (that there is some rice morsels as well in the ghee – very rich food). nallatōr sōṟum – goodness of food is – when the giver of food does not think “I have given” and the receiver does not feel “What will I have to do in return?”. It is like mother feeding her son \[where there is no other expectation than feeding the child\]. ōr sōṟu – Not just the mood of goodness, but also very tasty food. Alternatively, it is the food offered by sēśa bhūta (servitor) to sēśi (master). That is, food with abundant devotion, i.e., offering food to bhagavān thinking that bhagavān exists purely for his devotees as stated in jitantē stōtram “… bhaktānām …” (for the devotees only) and also thinking that bhagavān is vatsalan (having motherly affection towards child, who tolerates the child’s mistakes). How will such food offered by ananya prayōjanars to their master be? That is explained in mahābhāratam udyōga parvam 74 “vidurānnāni bubhujē sucīni guṇavanti ca” (kriṣṇa accepted the pure and delicious food offered by vidura). Here the purity is that vidura did not have any pride while offering food. duryōdana was prideful about his wealth. bhīśma was prideful about his knowledge/wisdom. drōṇa was prideful about his birth in good family. Unlike these men, vidura was filled with devotion only. niyatamum – always. Unlike material wealth, even when given by sarvēsvaran himself, it will not stay permanently. Even when its lost, bhagavān will remedy that situation – that is why two types of wealth seekers are defined namely arttārtti (one who seeks out new wealth) and ārttan (one who longs to retrieve lost wealth). Alternative explanation – samsāris are those who take the favours from bhagavān yet have no gratitude towards him. Still bhagavān protects them and shows his magnanimous nature. attāṇic cēvakamum – attāṇi  means being inseparable. cēvakam means service. Even for aisvaryārtti, always thinking about bhagavān , thinking about bhagavān during the last moments are essential duty just like for a ananya prayōjana.  In that case, why is this said “tantu” (given) like a result derived out of some self-effort? Because it is done after being purified, they do it purely as svayam prayōjanam (kainkaryam – service) instead of upāyam (means) to attain something else. kaiyadaik kāyum – dāraka, pōśaka are explained previously. Now, bhōjñam is explained.  Why is it said as “given by bhagavān himself?” Just like the servitor treats the master with respect/reverence, bhagavān too treats the servitor with dignity/love. kaḻuttukkup pūṇodu kātukkuk kuṇḍalamum – Since he was interested in material wealth, he is decorating himself with ornaments instead of doing the same on emperumān. The reason for separately identifying necklaces and earrings is to distinguish between one self-enjoying an ornament (necklace), and the others seeing and enjoying the same (ear-rings). When srī rāma returns to ayōdya (victoriously), he looks at ornaments that are lovingly presented to them by indra and tells sītā “this is for your neck”, “this is for your ears”, etc. meyyida – After developing taste in the lotus feet, we will joyfully say that “this body is to be given up”. nallatōr sāntamum – Matching his form which is gloried in cāndōjña upaniśat 3.14.2 as “sarva ganta:” (the cause of all fragrances). Like the sandalwood paste from kubhjā.  When kriṣṇa and balarāma was visiting maturā, kriṣṇa asked the hunch-back lady “Would you offer/apply sandal paste for all?” As she was attracted to kriṣṇa, she did not leave but did not want to give the best sandalwood paste considering them as mere covherd boys. She initially thought that kriṣṇa and balarāma being from āyarpādi, they may not know these different types of sandal wood pastes, but realizing their knowledge in this, she gave the best paste to them. Such sandalwood paste was applied by bhagavān in our hands. tantu – While giving, he made us feel overwhelmed by the gait in his face and his magnanimity and made us give up the attachment towards the material wealth and we thus became fully attached to him. ennai veḷḷuyir ākka valla – My situation was as explained in mahābhāratam udyōga parvam 42.35 “…cōrēṇa ātmābahāriṇā” (what sins are not committed by the one who steals the ātmā (considers it to be ones own property) which is really the property of bhagavān?) – I was fully engrossed in worldly pleasures, he purified such person. veḷḷuyir ākka valla – He purified me so much that I looked like a nityasūri and can never be recognized by any one here where I was fully immersed in this worldly activities previously. valla – One who is naturally capable to transform one type of object (being completely immersed in worldly pleasures) into another (being completely immersed in him).**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*āḻvār accepts the aisvaryārttis (those who are after material wealth) who accept āḻvār‘s suggestion and arrive at him after being invited by him in “aṇḍak kulam” pāsuram. ananya prayōjanars (those who are focussed on kainkarayam) who were invited in “vāḻāḷ pattu” arrived in “entai tantai” highlighting their greatness. kaivalyārttis (those who are focussed on self-enjoyment) who were invited in “ēdu nilam” arrived in “tīyil poliginṟa” highlighting their greatness. These people (aisvaryārttis) desired for insignificant results, yet, emperumān responded to them as he would do for ananya prayōjanars and fulfilled their desires. Being overwhelmed with emperumān‘s generosity, they gave up the wealth and with pure heart arrive to glorify bhagavān who is most generous. While they previously spoke about the greatness of (materially) higher worlds, now they are talking about that which is dāraka (sustenance), pōśaka (nourishment) and bhōjña (enjoyment) – this is because of them realizing the insignificant nature of the higher material worlds and also realizing that even for brahmā (who is great) the need is for dāraka, pōśaka and bhōjñam only. If we consider anything other than such basic needs as “mine”, that will only lead to losing ones own true nature/composition. நெய்யிடை நல்லதோர் சோறும் நியதமும் அத்தாணிச் சேவகமும் கையடைக் காயும் கழுத்துக்குப் பூணொடு காதுக்குக் குண்டலமும் மெய்யிட நல்லதோர் சாந்தமும் தந்து என்னை வெள்ளுயிராக்க வல்ல பையுடை நாகப் பகைக் கொடியானுக்குப் பல்லாண்டு கூறுவனே*

When asked what/who is the object of mangaḷāsāsanam?

- *paiyudai* … – performing mangaḷāsāsanam to bhagavān who has the garuda dvaja (flag) – garuda who is the enemy of serpent \[in worldly matters\] – having such serpent with raised hoods due to the touch of bhagavān as his bed. [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyazhwar/) is saying that he is singing tiruppallāṇḍu for the eternal well-being of bhagavān who is in ananta (ādi sēśa) which resembles a gem embossed in a gold plate and to the one who has garuda flag which establishes that even if some one comes to him with ulterior motives, they will become fully surrendered to him. Here, in the end “*kūṟuvanē*” is in singular unlike when singing about ananyaprayōjanars and kaivalyārttis which were in plural. This is because those groups are limited in numbers but aisvaryārttis are unlimited (most common set of people). So, in this case, a single person represents the huge group and speaks on behalf of them.

## 9. Uduttuk kalainta

*uduttuk kaḷainta nin pītaga ādai uduttuk kalattatu uṇḍu todutta tuḻāy malar sūdik kaḷaintana sūdum it toṇḍargaḷōm vidutta tisaik karumam tiruttit tiruvōṇat tiruviḻavil padutta pain nāgaṇaip paḷḷi koṇḍānukkup pallāṇḍu kūṟutumē*

**Пословный перевод:**

uduttu – worn in the sacred waist; kaḷainta – discarded; nin – your (being the master); pītaga ādai – yellowish cloth; uduttu – wearing it; kalattatu – remainder in the vessel (from which you ate); uṇḍu – eating it; sūdik kaḷaintana – that which where worn by you and discarded; todutta – prepared by (us, your servitors); tuḻāy malar – tuḷasi flowers; sūdum – wearing it; it toṇḍargaḷōm – us who are such servitors; vidutta – sent out to; tisaik karumam – the targets in the particular direction; tirutti – complete it properly; padutta – in a lying down position; pai – (due to that) having raised hoods; nāga aṇai – in the bed named tiruvanantāḻvān (ādi sēśan); paḷḷi koṇḍānukku – you who is resting on it; tiruvōṇat tiruviḻavil – on the auspicious sravaṇam day; pallāṇḍu kūṟutum – we will sing tiruppallāṇḍu

**Перевод:**

**uduttu – Wearing the cloth along the waist such that there is good contact and to retain the stains of turmeric and sandalwood paste in it. We are amongst those who pray for (to receive) such cloth which causes auspiciousness for the master and having such stains/symbols in the cloth that is to be constantly remembered by the servitors in a pleasant manner. kaḷainta – When he discards such clothes due to change of postures/situation, the servitors want such clothes to fall on their head. This too is their prayer.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*āḻvār sings tiruppallāṇḍu in this pāsuram along with those ananya prayōjanars (those who are focussed in kainkaryam) whom he invited in “vāḻāḷ pattu”  and who arrived  in “entai tantai”. உடுத்துக் களைந்த நின் பீதக ஆடை உடுத்துக் கலத்ததுண்டு தொடுத்த துழாய் மலர் சூடிக் களைந்தன சூடும் இத் தொண்டர்களோம் விடுத்த திசைக்கருமம் திருத்தித் திருவோணத் திருவிழவில் படுத்த பைந் நாகணைப் பள்ளி கொண்டானுக்குப் பல்லாண்டு கூறுதுமே*

The reason for such clothes being desired by the devotees is explained further.

- *nin pītaga ādai* – Because its the clothes of the real/fitting master, all the clothes are considered as a form of the pītāmbaram (yellow \[waist\] cloth).
- *nin pītaga ādai* – Even the waist cloth of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) has an [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) as explained in  pauskara samhitā “*sragvastrābaṇairyuktam svānurūpairanūpamai: \| cinmayas svaprakāsaisca anyōnta ruciranjitai: \|\|*” ([parabrahmam](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) is with radiantly beautiful garlands, clothes, ornaments which are matching his glories, incomparable, filled with knowledge and self-illuminous).
- *uduttu* – For ananya prayōjanars, such clothes, etc., are ornaments. Alternatively, it is explained as – As said in jitantē stōtram “*bhaktānām*” (for the devotees only), the servitors think about this with utmost care when it comes to their master – just like a servant of the king will set the cloth properly on his own (servant’s) waist first and present it perfectly for the king to wear.
- *kalattatu uṇḍu* – When the food is offered to [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/), the remnants are enjoyable for the devotees. It is explained in stōtra ratnam 42 “*tvadīya bhuktōjjita sēśabhōjinā …*” ([visvaksēnar](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/vishwaksenar/) who \[always\] eats your food remnants …). Those who don’t have true understanding of self, will eat the remnants as ordered in “*gurōrucciśtam bhujjīta*” (one must eat the food remnants of the master), to get their own desired results. But those who are fully devoted towards [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/), will consume the remnants as the ultimate result (with great love). The food remnants of bhāgavatas (being surrendered to them is the pinnacle of being [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s servitor) are to be given to only the most-qualified persons as stated in tirumālai 41 “*taruvarēl*” (if they give, it is a great fortune). But [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s prasādam (remnants) are enjoyable by even those who are focussed on material benefits.
- *todutta* … – When they pluck the tuḷasi and make the garland, they would do it with so much love thinking that it is going to be worn by emperumān. And such garland when  discarded after being worn by [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/), will be happily worn by us. [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) too knowing well the flavour of wearing the garland worn by some one else \[āṇḍāḷ\], he too happily gives out the worn garlands to his devotees.
- *it toṇḍargaḷōm* – We are such devotees who would only eat, wear, etc., his remnants instead of doing it for our own desires. We sustain ourselves by using what is discarded by him.
- *vidutta* … – We are such persons who fulfill the orders of our master and spend our life in that manner.
- *vidutta tisaik karumam tirutti* – As explained in srī rāmāyaṇam āraṇya kāṇḍam 15.7 “*kriyatām*” (… you order me to build the hermitage), we will hold your order as our life and fulfill the task. When we are sent in a particular direction, we will fulfill the task in such a manner that no one needs to go there again. adama – one who does not fulfill the orders of the master; madyama –  one who fulfills the orders of the master as it is; uttama – one who fulfills the orders of the master and also fulfills anything else that is related to that. tiruvadi (hanumān) is such uttama (perfect) servitor. When he was sent to lankā to locate sītā pirātti, not only he located her, he also analysed rāvaṇa’s capabilities (knowing well that srī rāma will subsequently ask for that) and created terror in the minds of the rākśasas.
- *tiruvōṇat tiruviḻavil* – Though they fulfilled the orders, they are not satisfied and are worried for the birth day of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) which is sravaṇam and thus they perform mangaḷāsāsanam for that.
- *padutta* … – Even after that they are not fulfilled; On seeing the beautiful posture of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) lying down on ādi sēśan, they perform mangaḷāsāsanam to avoid some one causing damage for that. The ādi sēśa bed has qualities such as softness, fragrance, coolness, etc., and it even mesmerizes bhagavān who is all-knowing. The combination of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) and ādi sēśan is most beautiful and it is like a dark-cloud seated on a silver mountain. On seeing such contrasting beauty, what else one can do other than perform mangaḷāsāsanam?

## 10. Ennal emperuman

*ennāḷ emperumān un tanakku adiyōmenṟu eḻuttuppatta annāḷē adiyōngaḷ adik kudil vīdu peṟṟu uyntatu kāṇ sennāḷ tōṟṟit tiru maturaiyuḷ silai kunittu aintalaiya pain nāgat talaip pāyntavanē unnaip pallāṇḍu kūṟutumē*

**Пословный перевод:**

emperumān – Oh our master!; un tanakku – to you (who is the master of all); adiyōm enṟu – that “we are servitors”; eḻuttup patta – when we gave that in writing; ennāḷ – which day; annāḷē – that day; adiyōngaḷ – our (who are servitors); kudil – sons, grand-sons, etc., who are in the house; adi – being surrendered to you; vīdu peṟṟu – being freed from kaivalya mōkśam; uyntatu – got liberated/uplifted; sem nāḷ – on that beautiful day; tōṟṟi – descended; tiru maturai uḷ – in the beautiful maturā (of north bhāratam); silai kunittu – broke the bow (in kamsa’s weapons court); ain talaiya – having five heads; pai – having well expanded hoods; nāgam – kāḷiya the serpent; talai – on the head; pāyntavanē – sarvēsvaran (you) who mercifully landed on it; unnai – for you; pallāṇḍu kūṟutum – we will perform mangaḷāsāsanam

**Перевод:**

**ennāḷ – Reason for saying “ennāḷ” (which day? – what a day?) instead of “annāḷ” (that day) is – they are overwhelmed by the time when they surrendered to the right master but asked for insignificant results (kaivalyam – self-enjoyment), yet emperumān transformed them to become kainkaryaparars (servitors) who can perform magaḷāsāsanam. bhagavān‘s greatness is such that, even the day when he accepted us is to be celebrated. Like his appearance day is greatly celebrated \[the day when we surrender unto him and are accepted by him, that day is our real birth day\].**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*In the previous pāsuram, ananya prayōjanars (those are focussed on kainkaryam) arrived proclaiming the glories of their nature being totally dependant on bhagavān instead of being focussed on worldly life. In this pāsuram, prayōjanāntaraparars (those with ulterior motives – specifically kaivalyārtti) who are being discussed here, did not have such nature previously and were fully reformed by bhagavān‘s greatness \[mercy\]. So, they talk about such grace of bhagavān. Even while entering, aisvaryārtti (those who are after material pleasures and wealth) too claimed bhagavān‘s greatness in transforming them by saying “sāntamum tantu ennai veḷḷuyir ākkavalla“. They wondered how emperumān fulfilled their desires and transformed them to be pure. kaivalyārttis (those who are focussed on self-enjoyment) too wonder about their attachment to insignificant result (of enjoying oneself) which is also eternal destruction for them and how emperumān rescued them from that while they surrendered unto him. எந்நாள் எம்பெருமான் உன் ததனக்கு அடியோமென்று எழுத்துப்பட்ட அந்நாளே அடியோங்கள் அடிக் குடில் வீடு பெற்று உய்ந்தது காண் செந்நாள் தோற்றித் திரு மதுரையுள் சிலை குனித்து ஐந்தலைய பைந்நாகத் தலை பாய்ந்தவனே உன்னைப் பல்லாண்டு கூறுதுமே*

How did the one who came for insignificant benefits become transformed into looking for kainkaryam only?

- *emperumān* – When coming for insignificant fruits to the eternal master, the natural servitude became manifested.

Though he is the natural master, when some one desires for something (other than kainkaryam), should they not have some thing in them to receive that?

- *un tanakku adiyōm enṟu eḻuttup patta* – we are qualified to say “*adiyōm*” (we are your dependents). Even, if it is not coming from our hearts, you are capable of fulfilling our desires just for hearing the words from us. Alternatively, when we go to some one seeking some thing from them, we use the term “nama:” (I am for you, I surrender unto you) to glorify them. That word is clear representation of the natural/intrinsic quality of [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/). That is our principal for you to benedict us. Just like it is difficult to find servitors in this world which is filled with ego, it is also rare to say *nama:* for those who are simply discussing useless things.
- *annāḷ* – it should be read as “*emperumān un tanakku adiyōm enṟu eḻuttup patta ennāḷ – annāḷ*” (Oh master! the day when we accepted your lordship, that is the real day). By saying “*annāḷē*” (only that day), it is established that [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)‘s grace alone is the means and we have no other good qualities in us.

What did they get out of this?

- *adiyōngaḷ …* – We became servitors. We became uplifted. Our kudil (house) too became adik kudil (residence of servitors). We got vīdu (liberation).
- *adiyōngaḷ* – though we were filled with ahankāram (ego, pride) and surrendered unto your lotus feet seeking self-enjoyment, we became servitors and gave up our ahankāram.
- *adik kudil* – kudil means gruham (residence).  This indicates, those who are in the house like sons, grand-sons, etc., also became servitors. This is what is explained in [tiruvāimoḻi](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/thiruvaimozhi/) 8.10.10 “*nalla patattāl manai vāḻvar*” (will lead virtuous/devotional gruhasta (married) life). When [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) accepted only them, how did their children, grand-children become purified? When some one is protecting a pearl-spot in the ocean, they will also protect the nearby areas. Similarly, emperumān protects all the related ones of those who surrender unto him and gives them true knowledge too. Even when they arrived, they declared “[*kudi kudi āḷ seyginṟōm*](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2015/11/thiruppallandu-7-thiyil-poliginra/)“.
- *vīdu peṟṟu* – Being liberated; i.e., freed from aisvaryam (worldly wealth) and kaivalyam (self-enjoyment liberation) which are caused by ahankāram (considering oneself as body) and mamakāram (considering [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s property as ones own). Removal of hurdles is also part of attaining the goal.
- *uyntatu kāṇ* – dāsyam (being servitor) and ujjīvanam (upliftment) are synonyms.  Why are they saying to [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) “See I have been uplifted” as if informing to unknown person? You are so magnanimous that you won’t even remember helping us – so hear from us the favour you have bestowed us.
- *sem nāḷ tōṟṟit tirumaturai uḷ silai kunittu* – What are you going to do after being reformed? Our task is to perform mangaḷāsāsanam (pray for your well-being) to you only.

What is the aspect to perform mangaḷāsāsanam?

- *sem nāḷ* – that auspicious day which is beautiful due to you appearing on that day.
- *tōṟṟi* – manifesting the form that is beyond our senses, so that even those who don’t have the desire can see you as explained in gītā bhāsyam introduction “*sakalamanujanayanaviśayatām gata:*“.
- *tirumaturai uḷ* – that too not in ayōdyā which is the safest place, but in maturā where kamsa, the enemy lives.
- *silai kunittu* – leaving maturā immediately after birth and being raised in vrindāvanam, you returned to kamsa’s weapons court in maturā, broke the bow and caused havoc. This is totally fearsome for your devotees to see you enter the palace of kamsa to stir his anger.
- *aintalaiya pain nāgat talaip pāyntavanē*  – They start speaking about the incident in āyarpādi (gōkulam) where he was living peacefully.
- *aintalaiya pain nāgat talaip pāyntavanē! –* He has give mouths to bite and has great hoods which are expanded out of anger.  As said in srīviṣṇu purāṇam 5.7.1 “*ēkatā tu vinā rāmam kruṣṇō brundāvanam yayau \|*” (one day, kriṣṇa went to vrindāvanam in the absence of balarāma), when the elder-brother did not accompany him, he is so mischievous  that he fell in the trap of a snake. When [nanjīyar](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nanjiyar/) asked [bhattar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/11/parasara-bhattar/) “Why are all [āḻvārs](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/azhwars/) caring for kriṣṇa?”, [bhattar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/11/parasara-bhattar/) replied “In rāmāvatāram, the four boys are very capable, with very good qualities, their father being sambarāntaka (the killer of sambara), ministers are vasiśta, etc., the town is ayōdyā (great fort), the time is good time (trētā yugam) – so there is nothing to fear. But in kriṣṇāvatāram, he was born in the prison in enemy’s fort, kamsa is a cruel person who will send different persons to destroy him, father is a cow-herd man, residence is colony of cow-herd men and women, the boys themselves (kriṣṇa, etc) are mischievous and the time (dvāpara yugam) is close to kali yugam. That is why [āḻvārs](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/azhwars/) have nothing else to do other than caring for kriṣṇa”.
- *unnaip pallāṇḍu kūṟutumē* – For such [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) (yourself), we cannot sustain ourselves by anything other than performing mangaḷāsāsanam to you.

## 11. Al valakku

*al vaḻakku onṟum illā aṇi kōttiyar kōn abhimāna tungan selvanaip pōlat tirumālē nānum unakkup paḻa adiyēn nal vagaiyāl namō nārāyaṇā enṟu nāmam pala paravip pal vagaiyālum pavittiranē unnaip pallāṇḍu kūṟuvanē*

**Пословный перевод:**

tirumālē – Oh master of srī mahālakśmi!; al vaḻakku – bad manners/habits; onṟum illā – one who is not having even a tinge of it; aṇi – being an ornament (for the samsāram (material realm)); kōttiyūr – for the residents of tirukkōśtiyūr; kōn – being the leader; abhimāna tungan – being great due to considering himself as “I am a servant of emperumān”; selvanaip pōla – like selva nambi; nānum – me too; unakku – for you who is the master; paḻa adiyēn – am being an eternal servitor; pal vagaiyālum pavittiranē – Oh the one who can eliminate \[my/our\] sins in many ways (through his nature, form, qualities, wealth, etc)!; nal vagaiyāl – through beautiful ways; namō nārāyaṇā enṟu – meditating upon the tirumantram and reciting the same; pala nāmam – your many auspicious names; paravi – saying in different improper ways; unnaip pallāṇḍu kūṟuvan – I will perform mangaḷāsāasnam to you

**Перевод:**

**al vaḻakku onṟum illā – vaḻakku means “nyāyam” (manner, justice, etc). Real justice according to āḻvār is to be constantly performing mangaḷāsāsanam to the master. There are many injustices/ill-manners. They are: Considering the body to be soul is improper. Considering ātmā which is distinct from body, to be independent is improper. Considering dēvatāntarams (other dēvatās such brahmā, rudra, etc) to be supreme is improper. Considering ulterior results for vorśipping emperumān is improper. Considering upāyāntarams (other means such as karma yōgam, etc., which are based on self-effort) as means even after being an ananya prayōjana (one who is fully focussed on kainkaryam) is improper. Considering bhagavat anubhavam (experiencing bhagavān) as for ones own exclusive joy is improper. aṇi kōttiyar kōn – the residents of tirukkōśtiyūr is without any of these defects and fully exist for performing mangaḷāsāsanam to emperumān. The cause for that is selva nambi being the leader of the town. This town is greatly glorified as an ornament for the whole (material) world. abhimāna tungan – abhimānam (pride) is generally a hurdle for being a servitor – then how is he glorified as having abundance of pride? abhimānam which is acquired by karma (material activities, puṇyam (virtues) and pāpam (sins)) are to be given up; But being “dāsōham” (I am a servitor) is good pride which is to be pursued – and selva nambi is having abundance of such servitude. That is , considering the good and bad aspects of the divya dēsams and the lives of srīvaiṣṇavas to be ones own \[and feeling joy and sorrow for them respectively\]. selvan – one who does not lack jñānam (knowledge), bhakti (devotion) and vairājñam (detachment). Also, being “aham sarvam kariśyāmi” (I will serve in all ways (said lakśmaṇa)- srī rāmāyaṇam ayōdyā kāṇṭam 31.25) as said in srī rāmāyaṇam bāla kāṇḍam 18.28 “lakśmaṇō lakśmīsampanna:” (srī lakśmaṇa who has abundance of kainkaryasrī (wealth of servitude)). pōla – Like it is said in srīviṣṇu purāṇam 1.15.157 “upamānamasēśāṇām sādūnām” (prahlādāḻvān who is the prime example for all devotees) – selva nambi is the prime example for sātvikas (srīvaiṣṇavas). Does it not seem contrary that saying “paḻa adiyēn” (eternal devotee) and quoting selva nambi (who is such great devotee coming in great family heritage) as an example for aisvaryārttis who just became servitors? No – when the ahankāram that is acquired by karma is eliminated, the natural quality of all ātmās is to be servitors – that is why here āḻvār says “paḻa adiyēn” for aisvaryārttis who became real servitors. Alternatively, it can also be explained as – bhagavān‘s glories of accepting all irrespective of whether he is a nityasūri or was an aisvarārtti (before), is revealed here. tirumālē – Here, it is explained that “this jīvātmā is a servitor for the divine couple” and thus explains the nature of the lordship who accepts the service. From this, we can understand that being a servitor of mother, father, other dēvatās, etc., are based on ones karma (artificial/temporary). Alternatively, it is explained us – just like you (emperumān) are identified by your relationship with srī mahālakśmi, we are identified with our servitude toward you. nānum – Me who was immersed in worldly pleasures since time immemorial. To expect ulterior benefits from the master is like a chaste wife expecting money for her (intimate) services \[prostitution\] from her husband. unakkup paḻa adiyēn – Just like your lordship is eternal, my servitude is also eternal. unakku – For you, who has the ability to transform even those who come to you for ulterior benefits to be a pure devotee.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*Here, āḻvār is singing tiruppallāṇḍu through the words of aisvaryārttis (those who are focussed on worldly wealth) who were invited in “aṇḍak kulam” pāsuram and who joined him in “neyyidai” pāsuram. அல் வழக்கு ஒன்றும் இல்லா அணி கோட்டியர் கோன் அபிமான துங்கன் செல்வனைப் போலத் திருமாலே நானும் உனக்குப் பழ அடியேன் நல் வகையால் நமோ நாராயணா என்று நாமம் பல பரவிப் பல் வகையாலும் பவித்திரனே உன்னைப் பல்லாண்டு கூறுவனே*

How did we acquire this true knowledge about the self? This is explained by [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyazhwar/) further that it was acquired from nārāyaṇa mantram which is the essence of all of vēdāntam.

- *nal vaigaiyāl namō nārāyaṇā enṟu* – Saying that “I am existing only for nārāyaṇa” and “I am not existing for myself”.
- *nal vagaiyāl* – Previously without understanding the meaning of this mantram, they used it in japam, hōmam, etc – this is called *tī vagai* (improper ways). \[Now they are practicing with proper understanding of the mantram\] this mantram can accomplish all results as explained in nāradīya purāṇam 1.41 “*… namō nārāyaṇāyēti mantras sarvārttasādaka:*” (this mantram of “*namō nārāyaṇāya*” will accomplish all results).
- *nāmam pala paravi* – When [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyazhwar/) invited aisvaryārttis, he called them in 5th pāsuram saying “[*adi toḻutu āyira nāmam solli*](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2015/06/thiruppallandu-5-andakkulam/)“. So, they too are repeating the same saying they will recite the many names of [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/).
- *paravi* – Once when something is “means” (the process), it should be performed properly \[Since [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) is the only means to attain him, all of these are kainkarayams only and not means to attain him\] – so, this can even be recited in improper ways. For those who were fully focussed on material enjoyment, to become focussed on mangaḷāsāsanam, they only need to have the desire for that \[no other rules are applicable\].
- *pal vagaiyālum pavittiranē* – Oh one who is pure in eliminating the dirt of being interested in ulterior motives, removing the ahankāram and mamakāram which are the cause of that, and even in servitude, removing the servitude towards mother, father, other dēvatās, etc.! Even looking at his svarūpam (true nature), rūpam (forms), guṇa (qualities) and vibhūti (wealth), it is understandable that he is capable of eliminating (our) sins.
- *unnaip pallāṇḍu kūṟuvanē* – I am performing mangaḷāsāsanam to you who is with auspicious qualities such as saundaryam (beauty), etc. By saying it in singular, it can be understood that, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyazhwar/) is speaking on behalf of ananya prayōjanars, kaivalyārttis and aisvaryārttis.

## 12. Pallandu enru

*pallāṇḍu enṟu pavittiranaip paramēttiyaic cārngam ennum villāṇḍān tannai villiputtūr vittucittan virumbiya sol nallāṇḍu enṟu navinṟu uraippār namō nārāyaṇāya enṟu pallāṇḍum paramātmanic cūḻntu iruntu ēttuvar pallāṇḍē*

**Пословный перевод:**

pavittiranai – being the one who is (naturally) super pure; paramēttiyai – being the one who is seated in the highest abode of srīvaikuṇṭam; sārngam ennum – having the auspicious name “sārngam”; vil – bow; āṇḍān tannai – towards emperumān who has complete control over that; villiputtūr – one who is born in srīvilliputtūr; vittucittan – periyāḻvār who has the auspicious name of viṣṇucittan; pallāṇḍu enṟu – saying “eternally being auspicious”; virumbiya – mercifully spoken with (such) desire; sol – srīsūkti (divine words); nal āṇḍu enṟu – feeling that this is auspicious time (suitable to sing tiruppallāṇḍu); navinṟu uraippār – who says repeatedly; namō nārāyaṇāya enṟu – meditating upon the tirumantram; paramātmanai – srīman nārāyaṇan who is the paramātmā; cūḻntu iruntu – surrounding him; pal āṇḍum – countless time (in future); pallāṇḍu ēttuvar – will sing tirupallāṇḍu

**Перевод:**

**pavittiranai – Naturally pure and not due to any particular cause/reason. This is explained in “sāsvatam sivam” (eternally auspicious). It is explained through this – when contacted with impure objects, bhagavān still maintains his purity and his supreme purity which transforms such impure objects to be pure as well. That is, even though he is pervaded in a cit and acit, he does not acquire their defects, and him transforming those who are in samsāram from time immemorial into the same level of nityasūri (eternally free). paramēttiyai – One who is seated in the supreme abode. sārngam ennum vil āṇḍān tannai – Highlighting his control over his sārngam (bow) is just a sample of his control over his consorts, ornaments, weapons, servitors, etc.  The bonding of emperumān and nityasūris is so strong that, emperumān is identified by his connection with the nityasūris. sārngam ennum vil āṇḍān tannai – his bow is popularly known as sārngam. Like one who has full control over an elephant and rides it, emperumān has full control over his bow and he is glorified for his handling of sārngam. He is explained in srī rāmāyaṇam yudda kāṇḍam 100.12 “ālikantamivākāsamavaśtabhya mahaddanu:” (One who is tightly holding the great bow which reaches up to the sky). From this, bhagavān‘s nitya vibhūti yōgam (being together with spiritual realm) that is reminded by his purity which was explained in 2nd pāsuram “mallāṇḍa tiṇ tōḷ maṇivaṇṇā” (broad shouldered kriṣṇa who destroyed the wrestlers). villiputtūr vittucittan – pallāṇḍu enṟu – virumbiya sol – Now, āḻvār induces everyone to join his group (unlike before where pure devotees, those with ulterior motives, etc., were coming in sequence) and he himself is speaking with joy. Earlier āḻvār spoke on behalf of them to show that everyone will be qualified to perform mangaḷāsāsanam by the grace of bhagavān and to show his own attachment to performing mangaḷāsāsanam. villiputtūr vittucittan – Due to birth in srīvilliputtūr, close proximity to bhagavān occurred; due to that close proximity, overwhelming love which is cause for mangaḷāsāsanam developed. The name “viṣṇu citta” indicates presence of srīman nārāyaṇan in āḻvār‘s heart. Even if āḻvār leaves bhagavān, bhagavān would consider staying in his heart as his own goal and would never leave him. āḻvār himself says in periyāḻvār tirumoḻi “vittucittan manattē kōyil koṇḍa kōvalanai” (gōpāla (kriṣṇa) who resides in the heart of viṣṇu citta). nallāṇḍu enṟu – Glorifying the time which is suitable to recite this pāsuram as akrūra said in “adya mē sapalam janma …” (Today my birth became meaningful on seeing kriṣṇa). For those who fully seeked out personal senses’ enjoyment, engaging in mangaḷāsāsanam for emperumān is rare. navinṟu uraippār – repeating continuously. namō nārāyaṇāya enṟu – \[meaning of nārāyaṇāya – serving srīman nārāyaṇan is highlighted\]. Since time immemorial we have been seeking for worldly pleasures only and now we are looking for your (emperumān‘s enjoyment). This highlights the qualification required for performing mangaḷāsāsanam (looking for his enjoyment instead of ours). pallāṇḍum – forever. As long as the ātmā exists (which is forever since ātmā is permanent). āḻvār says “many years” in paramapadam where there is no boundary of time (since it is eternal) because he is living in samsāram (material realm) where there are such boundaries in time. paramātmanai – Having no one greater than him, having every one else under his full control. This implies that, in bhagavān, there is no room for inauspiciousness and there is no new glory which will be added by anyone’s mangaḷāsāsanam. sūḻntu iruntu ēttuvar – As explained in bhagavat gītā 11.39 “nama: purastādata pruśtatastē namōstu tē” (I worship you in your front and in your behind; I offer my worships to you), I will enjoy your smile standing in the fore front; I will enjoy the beauty of your back and the ornaments in there; On seeing such beauty and standing still, one will naturally become engaged in mangaḷāsāsanam, fearing for the loss of the same.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*Wanting to declare the results for those who learn this prabandam, āḻvār is performing mangaḷāsāsanam with over-flowing love. First, he invited ananya prayōjanar (those who are fully focussed on kainkaryam) since they too have close proximity towards bhagavān like him.  āḻvār then invited even aisvaryārtti (those who are looking for wealth) and kaivalyārtti (those who are looking for self-enjoyment) with the hope that they too will become focussed on mangaḷāsāsanam by the grace of bhagavān. Now, he mercifully declares the glories of this prabandam which will transform samsāris (those who are not interested in approaching bhagavān for anything) too to become focussed on mangaḷāsāsanam eternally. பல்லாண்டு என்று பவித்திறனைப் பரமேட்டியைச் சார்ங்கம் என்னும் வில்லாண்டான் தன்னை வில்லிபுத்தூர் விட்டு சித்தன் விரும்பிய சொல் நல்லாண்டு என்று நவின்று உரைப்பார் நமோ நாராயணாய என்று பல்லாண்டும் பரமாத்மனைச் சூழ்ந்து இருந்து ஏத்துவர் பல்லாண்டே*

Thus ends the English translation of [periyavāccān piḷḷai](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyavachchan-pillai/)‘s vyākhyānam for [periyāḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyazhwar/)‘s [tiruppallāṇḍu](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2015/06/thiruppallandu/).
