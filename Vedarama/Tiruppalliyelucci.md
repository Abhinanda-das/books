# Tiruppalliyelucci

## 1. Katiravan

*katiravan guṇadisaic cikaram vandaṇaintān kana iruḷ aganṟatu kālai am poḻutāy madu virintu oḻugina māmalar ellām vanavar arasargaḷ vandu vandu īṇḍi etirdisai niṟaintanar ivarodum pugunda irungaḷiṟṟu īttamum pidiyodu murasum aditalil alaikadal pōnruḷadu engum arangattammā paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

arangattammā – Oh lord/master who is lying down in srīragangam!; katiravan – sun; guṇadisai – in the eastern side; cikaram – at the peak (on the udayagiri); vandu aṇaintān – arrived and positioned himself; kana iruḷ – heavy darkness (of the night); aganṟatu – dispelled and driven out; am – beautiful; kālai poḻutu āy – as the morning arrived; mā malar ellām – all the best flowers; virintu – blossomed; madu oḻugina – lots of honey dripped; vānavar – dēvas; arasargaḷ – kings; vandu vandu – arriving quickly pushing each other; īṇḍi – in groups; etirdisai – south side where bhagavān‘s divine vision will reach; niṟaintanar – stood there filling the entire place; ivarodum pugunda – arrived along with them; iru kaḷiṟu īttamum – Big groups of male elephants (which are the vehicles for some the dēvas, kings, etc); pidiyodu – (Big groups of) female elephants; murasum – musical bands; adirtalil – when this crowd makes noise (happily); engum – in all directions; alai kadal pōnṟu uḷatu – alai (waves). resembles the sound of ocean with fierce waves; (ādalāl) paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē – (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings

**Перевод:**

**Simple Translation: Oh lord/master who is lying down in srīragangam! The sun has arrived at the peak of the eastern side dispelling the heavy darkness of the night. As the beautiful morning time has arrived, honey is oozing out of the best of the flowers. The dēvas and kings are arriving quickly in groups pushing each other to the south side of the sannidi (temple) where your divine vision will reach. They stand there filling the entire place. Along with them, big groups of male and female elephants (which are the vehicles of the dēvas, kings, etc) and persons playing musical instruments have arrived. Their sounds and noises that reaches all directions in eager anticipation of your waking up resembles the great sound of an ocean with fierce waves. Thus, you kindly wake up now and give your blessings to all who have assembled here.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that the vorśippers (dēvas) of emperumān have arrived to worship him and thus āḻvār requests emperumān to wake up. Through this pāsuram, it is established that srīman nārāyaṇan is the vorśippable supreme being and all other dēvatas are his vorśippers. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that the sun can dispel the external darkness, but only srīman nārāyaṇan can dispel the internal darkness (ignorance). āḻvār requests that emperumān wakes up to see that the ignorance is dispelled and also to bless the dēvas, kings, etc., who have arrived to worship him. கதிரவன் குணதிசைச் சிகரம் வந்தணைந்தான் கனவிருளகன்றது காலையம் பொழுதாய் மது விரிந் தொழுகின மாமல ரெல்லாம் வானவரரசர்கள் வந்து வந்தீண்டி எதிர் திசை நிறைந்தன ரிவரொடும் புகுந்த இருங்களிற்றீட்டமும் பிடியொடு முரசும் அதிர்தலிலலை கடல் போன்றுள தெங்கும் அரங்கத்தம்மா பள்ளி யெழுந்தருளாயே.*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam**:

- periyāḻvār tirumoḻi 4.1.1 “*katirāyiramiravi kalanderittāl otta nīḷ mudiyan*”  – [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s divine head itself shines radiantly with the brightness of 1000 suns. The Sun arises from the eastern side where [srīranganātan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s lotus feet are.  So, in both sides, [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s radiance is glowing brilliantly.
- Early morning time is highlighted as auspicious time to meditate on [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/).
- dēvas and kings (who are filled with pride) arrive at [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s sannidi hurriedly to have their desires fulfilled.
- [srīranganātan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is glorified as ubhaya vibhūti nātan (one who rules both spiritual and material worlds) independently. [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) identifying himself as ananya gati:, meaning one who does not have any other refuge than [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) himself.

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam**:

- Just like when some one wakes up, an assistant/servitor may bring a lamp to light their day, when you wake up, sun himself lights the world with his rays. It is Sun’s small kainkaryam (service) for you. He also arrived in the eastern side to have your divine darsan and the blessings. As quoted in taitriya upaniśad “*biśōdēti sūrya:*“, he appears on time and performs his duty as a fearful servant of you.
- parāsara smruti “*brāhmē muhurttam ca uttāya cintayēt ātamanō hitam \| harir harir haririti vyāharēd vaiṣṇava: pumān \|\|*” – (A vaiṣṇava will wake up early in the morning (around 4am) and will meditate upon the well-being of the [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) (self). He will recite the name of hari at that time 3 times) and in [tiruppāvai](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/?p=68) 29 “*ciṟṟam cirukālē vandunnaic cēvittu*” (arriving in the early morning to worship you).
- Since the divya dēsam is srīrangam which is the dwelling place of [srīranganātan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) who never gets separated from his divine consort [srīranganāyaki tāyār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/), the male elephants also arrived with their counterparts (female elephants) to serve the divine couple.

## 2. Kolungodi

*koḻungodi mullaiyin koḻu malaraṇavik kūrntatu guṇadisai mārutam ituvō eḻundana malaraṇaip paḷḷikoḷ annam īnpani nanainta tamirum ciṟagudaṟi viḻungiya mudaliyin pilamburai pēḻvāy veḷḷeyiṟu uṟavatan vidattinukkku anungi aḻungiya vānaiyin aruntuyar kedutta arangattammā paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

guṇadisai mārutam – Wind from the east; koḻu kodi – Well nourished creeper; mullai – jasmine plant; koḻu malar – beautiful flowers; aṇavi – touching; ituvō – this; kūrntatu – blowing; malar aṇai – flower-bed; paḷḷi koḷ – sleeping; annam – swans; In pani nanainta – became wet due to the falling snow/fog (like rain); tam – their; iru ciṟagu – beautiful wings; udaṟi – shaking; eḻundana – waking up; viḻungiya – swallowed/held (the legs of elephant); mudalaiyin – crocodile’s; pilamburai – like a cave; pēḻvāy – big mouth; veḷḷeyiṟu uṟa – bitten by white and sharp/hard teeth; atan – that elephant’s; vidattinukku – for the poison (from those teeth); anungi aḻungiya – suffered greatly in pain; ānaiyin – elephant’s (gajēndrāḻvān’s); aru tuyar – big sorrow; kedutta – dispelled; arangattammā – Oh lord/master who is lying down in srīragangam!; (ādalāl) paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē – (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings

**Перевод:**

**Simple Translation: The eastern winds have arrived and is blowing with touching the well nourished jasmine creeper enroute. The swans that sleep in the flower-beds are waking up shaking their wings and feathers which have become wet due to the snow/fog falling like rain. You are the one who removed the sorrows of gajēndrāḻvān who suffered greatly in pain due to the poison from the biting of the crocodile’s sharp/hard teeth and the crocodile trying to swallow of its foot using the cave like huge mouth. Oh lord/master who is lying down in srīragangam who helped the elephant! please wake up now to bless us.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar and periyavāccān piḷḷai highlight that the arrival of eastern winds and the waking up of swans indicate the morning. They highlight that toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār requests that bhagavān being āsrita vatsala (one who is very attached to his devotees), he should wake up now and bless his devotees. கொழுங்கொடி முல்லையின் கொழு மலரணவிக் கூர்ந்தது குணதிசை மாருத மிதுவோ எழுந்தன மலரணைப் பள்ளி கொள்ளன்னம் ஈன் பனி நனைந்த தமிருஞ்சிற குதறி விழுங்கிய முதலையின் பிலம்புரை பேழ்வாய் வெள்ளெயிறுறவதன் விடத்தனுக்கனுங்கி அழுங்கிய ஆனையினருந்துயர் கெடுத்த அரங்கத்தம்மா! பள்ளி யெழுந்தருளாயே.*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) revealed vēdam in his hamsāvatāram as said in periya tirumoḻi 5.7.3 “*annamāy anṟu angu arumaṟai payantāy arangamā nagaramarntānē*“. Since swans have woken up, [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) requests [srīranganātan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) to wake up as well.
- As mentioned in viṣṇu darmam “*paramāpadam āpanna:*” – gajēndrāḻvān faced a great danger of being killed by the crocodile. But [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) arrived there, removed gajēndrāḻvān’s trouble, accepted the flower (which was held by gajēndrāḻvān for [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)) in his lotus feet and his service. This is [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s greatest favour.  Similarly, he descended into srīrangam as [srīranganātan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) in arcāvatāram to uplift all samsāris (who are present in the times after vibhavāvatārams). [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) requests [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) to wake up and fulfill their cravings to be blessed by him (just like he removed the sorrow of gajēndrāḻvān).

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- āḻvār says “*paḷḷikoḷ annam*” – in [divya dēsams](https://koyil.org/) (which are very dear to [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)), everything – even swans, are given great respect. When [emperumānār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/06/emperumanar/) arrived at tiruvēnkatam (tirumalā), [periya tirumalai nambi](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/03/01/periya-thirumalai-nambi/) who is [emperumānār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/06/emperumanar/)‘s maternal uncle and one of the ācāryas, himself came to the streets to welcome him. When [emperumānār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/06/emperumanar/) asked why he himself arrived and did not send some one of lower stature to welcome him, [nambi](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/03/01/periya-thirumalai-nambi/) says “*nammil siṟiyārillai ingu vattippāril*” (among those who live here, there is no one lower to me in stature) –  such was his humility.
- (similar to [nanjīyar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)‘s vyākhyānam) Since the swans have woken up, [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) who also appeared as swan should have woken up by now.
- Here [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) says “*viḻungiya*” (swallowed) when really the crocodile bites and holds the foot tight. This is similar to a mother seeing child very close to an open well and screams “I lost him” out of fear.
- For “*arum tuyar*“, it is explained that the sorrow was so big that, perumāḷ himself jumped directly from paramapadam to rescue gajēndrāḻvān.
- Why are you still sleeping? Would you not wake up and help us who are already fallen in this miserable ocean of samsāram and being bitten by five senses which are like five crocodiles?

## 3. Sudaroli

*sudaroḷi parantana sūḻdisai ellām tunniya tārakai minnoḷi surungip padaroḷi pasuttanan panimadi ivanō pāyiruḷ aganṟatu paimpoḻil kamugin madalidaik kīṟi vaṇpāḷaigaḷ nāṟa vaikaṟai kūrntatu mārutam ituvō adaloḷi tigaḻtaru tigiṟiyan tadakkai arangattammā paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

sūḻdisai ellām – everywhere (in all directions); sudar oḷi – sun’s rays; parantana – have spread/permeated; tunniya – closely located (in the sky); tārakai – stars; min oḷi – the bright light/shine; surungi – reduced/diminished; padar oḷi – well spread light (of); pani madi ivan – even this cool moon; pasuttanan – lost his shine; pāy iruḷ – well spread darkness; aganṟatu – removed; vaigaṟai mārutam idu – this early morning breeze; pai – greenish; poḻil – gardens/groves; kamugin – betel-nut trees; madalidaik kīṟi – cutting through the leaves (flaps); vaṇ pāḷaigaḷ nāṟa – Beautiful spates giving out nice fragrance; kūrntatu – blowing (carrying that fragrance); adal – very strong; oḷi tigaḻtaru – radiantly shining; tigiri – tiruvāḻiyāḻvān (cakkarattāḻvār – sudarasana cakram); am tada kai – (the one with) beautiful big divine hand; arangattammā – Oh lord/master who is lying down in srīragangam!; (ādalāl) paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē – (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings

**Перевод:**

**Simple Translation: The sun’s rays have spread everywhere. The well-spread light of the stars which were closely located have lost their light and even this cool moon have lost its shine. The well-spread darkness have been removed. The early morning breeze which cuts through the flaps of the betel-nut trees in the groves have arrived carrying the beautiful fragrance of the beautiful spates. Oh the one with the radiantly shining and strong sudarasana cakram in your divine hand! Oh lord/master who is lying down in srīragangam! please wake up now to bless us.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar and periyavāccān piḷḷai highlight that the sun has arrived with his radiant rays diminishing the shine of the stars. toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār desires to see the beautiful form of emperumān with the divine disc (sudarsana cakram) in his divine hands. சுடரொளி பரந்தன சூழ் திசையெல்லாம் துன்னிய தாரகை மின்னொளி சுருங்கி படரொளி பசுத்தனன் பனி மதியிவனோ பாயிறுளகன்றது பைம் பொழில் கமுகின் மடலிடைக் கீறி வண் பாளைகள் நாற வைகறை கூர்ந்தது மாருத மிதுவோ அடலொளி திகழ் தரு திகிரியந் தடக்கை அரங்கத்தம்மா பள்ளியெழுந்தருளாயே.*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- When the king appears, the impostors run away (fearing for their life). Similarly, when the sun appears the darkness disappears immediately.
- As the enemies are destroyed by the cakkarattāḻvār (sudarsana cakram), āḻvār requests that the sahaja satru (the enemy which was born along with us), i.e., being controlled by our senses is destroyed to have the senses focussed on [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) only to enjoy him.

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- As the sun’s radiance is shining, your radiance (which is the source for sun’s radiance) should also shine – for that you need to wake up. *tējasām rāśimūrjitam* (viṣṇu purāṇam 1.9.67) and *payilum sudaroḷi* (tiruvāimoḻi 3.7.1) – speaks about the glories of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s radiance.
- Even without the sudarsana cakram, [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s divine hands are so beautiful. With the sudarsana cakram the beauty becomes multifold.
- You have come here to bless the ones who are suffering and are looking for your darsan. Why still sleeping instead of waking up, blessing them and fulfilling the purpose of your descending here?

## 4. Mettila

*mēttiḷa mēdigaḷ taḷai vidum āyargaḷ vēynguḻal ōsaiyum vidaimaṇik kuralum īttiya visaidisai parandana vayaluḷ irintana surumbinam ilangaiyar kulattai vāttiya varisilai vanavarēṟē! māmuni vēḷviyaik kattu avabiratam āttiya adu tiṟal ayōtti emmarasē! arangattammā paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

mēdu iḷa mēdigaḷ – tall and young buffaloes; taḷai vidum – letting them (buffaloes) loose (for grazing); āyargaḷ – covherds (who are blowing); vēynguḻal ōsaiyum – the sound/music from the flute; maṇi – (of the) bells; kuralum – sound; īttiya isai – the sound of the two (covherds’ flutes and bells tied on the buffaloes); disai parantana – spread in all directions; vayaluḷ – in the green-fields; surumbu inam – group of beetles; irintana – started with cheerful sound; ilangiyar kulattai – rākśasa clan; vāttiya – destroyed; vari silai – (one who holds) Beautiful bow named sārngam; vānavar ēṟē! – dēvādi dēva! God of gods!; māmuni – visvāmitra maharishi’s; vēḷviyai – yāga – fire sacrifice; kāttu – protected; avabiratam āttiya – facilitated the holy dip/bathing after successful completion of the yāgam; adu tiṟal – one who has great valour which can destroy enemies; ayōtti emmarasē – My lord! due to you are being the ruler of ayōdyā; arangattammā – Oh lord/master who is lying down in srīragangam!; paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē – (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings

**Перевод:**

**Simple Translation: The sound/music from the flutes of the covherds who have let loose their buffaloes for grazing is spread in all directions. The beetles in the green-fields have started their day with cheerful sounds. Oh srī rāma! you destroyed the rākśsas and protected the yāgam of sage visvāmitra and helped him complete his yāgam and undergo the avabruta snānam (holy bathing done after successful completion of yāgam). You are our lord for being the king of ayōdyā and you have great valour that will easily destroy your enemies. Oh lord/master who is lying down in srīragangam! please wake up now to bless us.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār is praying to emperumān to wake up and help remove the hurdles that stop him from experiencing/enjoying emperumān just like he helped his devotees as srī rāma by destroying their enemies. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that the covherd boys are taking the cattle for grazing (their free roaming and feeding on grass). The sounds of the bells from the cattle and the humming of the bees are heard identifying the dawn. Thus āḻvār requests  emperumān who is the destroyer of enemies and protector of everyone to wake up and bless him. மேட்டிள மேதிகள் தளைவிடு மாயர்கள் வேய்ங்குழ லோசையும் விடை மணிக் குரலும் ஈட்டிய இசை திசை பரந்தன வயலுள் இருந்தின சுரும்பினம் இலங்கையர் குலத்தை வாட்டிய வரிசிலை வானவ ரேறே மாமுனி வேள்வியைக் காத்து அவபிரதம் ஆட்டிய அடு திறல் அயோத்தி யெம்மரசே அரங்கத்தம்மா பள்ளி யெழுந்தரு ளாயே.*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- srī rāma felt great satisfaction after killing rāvaṇa with his bow named sārngam since that was the desire of the dēvas (starting with brahmā) who were being greatly troubled by rāvaṇa. Here [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s quality of being happy when his devotees are protected is highlighted.
- The glories of [arcāvatāra emperumān](http://ponnadi.blogspot.in/p/archavathara-anubhavam.html) is highlighted. While srī rāma in ayōdyā was only visible during his life time, as [srīranganātan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) (in [arcāvatāram](http://ponnadi.blogspot.in/p/archavathara-anubhavam.html)), he is visible for many many generations (who are well after the time of srī rāma’s vibhavāvatāram). Such is his saulabhyam (easy approacability) in [arcāvatāram](http://ponnadi.blogspot.in/p/archavathara-anubhavam.html) that he is waiting for everyone to approach him.

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- Beautiful sense of humour from [periyavāccān piḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/). He says [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) highlighting that the cows and cattles have woken up is to indicate to [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) that “even beings of lower intelligence have woken up. You are the supreme being with supreme intelligence and should you not have woken up yet?”. narasimha purāṇa slōkam 16.13 “*āhāra nidrā bhaya maitunāni tulyāni kalvatra samastajantō: \| jñānāt visiśtō hi nara: parēbhyō jñānēna hīna: pasubis samāna: \|\|*” is quoted. This says: āhāram (eating), nidrā (sleeping), bhayam (out of fear, living together in a community) , maituna (mating and progeny) is common for all beings. But human form is more intelligent than other forms. If such intelligence is not manifested/used, he is as good as cows (other animals).
- Reason for hailing visvāmitra as “māmuni” (great sage) is explained beautifully. muni means who is in constant meditation – through their intense power they can accomplish any goal just by desiring for such things. Here, visvāmitra, instead of using his own svātantriyam (independence) and power to protect his yāgam, he fully submitted to srī rāma and fulfilled his task. This total dependence on [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) gained him the fame of “māmuni” from [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/)‘s words.
- As said in srī rāmāyaṇam bālakāṇḍam 1.97 “*dasavarśa saharāṇi dasa varśa satāni ca \| rāmō rājyam upāsitvā brahmalōkam gamiśyati*” – srī rāma will rule his kingdom for 11000 years and will return his own divine abode, srī rāma lived only for 11000 years. But [srīranganātan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is here for many many milleniums to help everyone. So, to have the fully accomplished, please wake up and bless us.

## 5. Pulambina

*pulambina putkaḷum pūmpoḻilgaḷin vāy pōyiṟṟuk kangul puguntatu pulari kalantatu guṇadisai kanaikadal aravam kaḷivaṇḍu miḻaṟṟiya kalambagam punainta alangalan todaiyal koṇḍu adiyiṇai paṇivān amarargaḷ puguntanar ādalil ammā ilangaiyarkōn vaḻipādu sey kōyil emperumān paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

pū – blossomed; poḻilgaḷin vāy – in the gardens/groves; putkaḷum – birds; pulambina – (woke up and) made cheerful sounds; kangul – the night; pōyiṟṟu – gone; pulari – early morning time; puguntatu – arrived; guṇadisai – in the eastern direction; kanai – noisy; kadal – sea/ocean; aravam – sound; kalantatu – spread; kaḷi – joyful (due to drinking of honey); vaṇḍu – beetles; miḻaṟṟiya – by the humming sound; kalambagam punainta – prepared with different flowers; am – beautiful; alangal todaiyal koṇḍu – having the garlands; amarargaḷ – dēvas; adi iṇai paṇivān – to worship (your) divine lotus feet; puguntanar – arrived; ātalil – thus; ammā – Master of all!; ilangaiyarkōn vaḻipādu sey kōyil – temple served by vibhiśaṇāḻvān who is the king of lankā; emperumān – Oh my lord/master!; paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē – (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings

**Перевод:**

**The birds that are in the well-blossomed-flower-rich groves are making cheerful sounds. The night is gone and the day has arrived. The sound of the noisy sea in the eastern direction is heard everywhere. The dēvas have arrived to worship your lotus feet with garlands prepared using beautiful flowers that still has the beetles that are making joyfully noise after drinking the honey (in the flowers). Thus, you, who are lying down in srīrangam which was vorśipped/served by vibhiśaṇāḻvān who is the king of lankā! Oh my lord/master (who is lying down in srīragangam)! please wake up now to bless us.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār is saying to emperumān that the dēvas have arrived with flowers to worship him and since he (emperumān) does not distinguish between his devotees (high/low), āḻvār requests him to wake up and accept everyone’s kainkaryam. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that in the previous pāsuram, āḻvār spoke about beetles that were sleeping in the fields and in this pāsuram, āḻvār speaks about the birds that are sleeping in the gardens/groves. The beetles that are sleeping in the fields are woken up easily as soon as they see the sun rising – these are like the devotees who have controlled their senses.  But the birds that are sleeping in the gardens/groves are woken up only after full dawn – these are like the materialistic people who are engaged in sense enjoyment. புலம்பின புட்களும் பூம்பொழில்களின் வாய் போயிற்றுக் கங்குல் புகுந்தது புலரி கலந்தது குணதிசை கனைகடலரவம் களிவண்டுமிழற்றிய கலம்பகம் புனைந்த அலங்கலந் தொடையல் கொண்டடியிணை பணிவான் அமரர்கள் புகுந்தனராதலி லம்மா இலங்கையர் கோன் வழிபாடு செய் கோயில் எம்பெருமான் பள்ளி யெழுந்தருளாயே.*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- Night is highlighted as the time when tamō guṇam engulfs everyone and puts them to sleep. Early morning is identified as the time suitable for worshiping [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) as everyone will be situated in satva guṇam early in the morning.
- [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) crowned vibhiśaṇāḻvān as the king of lankā even after him being the brother of rāvaṇa who had great enmity towards him. He only saw the devotion of vibhiśaṇāḻvān and accept him as his brother.

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- [toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) himself says in tirumālai 14th pāsuram “*vaṇḍinam uralum sōlai, mayilinam Alum sōlai, koṇḍal mīdaṇavum sōlai*” – srīrangam is filled with beautiful groves where the beetles make beautiful humming sound, the peacocks dance and the clouded sky keeping the place cool. The sound they make resembles them questioning “why is [periya perumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) still sleeping?”
- periyavāccān piḷḷai says āḻvār is first saying dēvas who are prayōjanāntaraparars (ones who seek out material benefits) are waiting to serve [periya perumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/). But [periya perumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is still not waking up. So he goes on to highlight that [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is lying down facing south where vibhīśaṇāḻvān is crowned by him even though he was the younger brother of rāvaṇan. āḻvār is referring to vibhīśaṇāḻvān saying “You were brought over here by vibhiśaṇāḻvān. Should he himself come here and wake you up?”
- He also explains “*vaḻipādu seygai*” (worshiping) means acting according to the will of [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/). [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is āsrita parādīnan (totally under the control of his devotees). Though vibhiśaṇāḻvān was originally carrying [periya perumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) to lankā, enroute, [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) decided to stay here in srīrangam to uplift everyone. [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) says “to fulfill such desire, you should wake up now and bless us all”.

While in all pāsurams (except last pāsuram), arangattammā is used in the end, only in this pāsuram [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is used. This is to highlight that periya perumāḷ is “[emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)“, i.e., my ([āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/)‘s) dear master.

## 7. Antarattu

*antarattu amararagaḷ kūttangaḷ ivaiyō aruntava munivarum marutarum ivarō indiran ānaiyum tānum vandivanō emperumān una kōyilin vāsal sundarar nerukka viccādarar nūkka iyakkarum mayanginar tiruvadi toḻuvān antaram pār idamillai maṟṟiduvō arangattammā paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

emperumān – my lord; un(a) kōyilin vāsal – at your divine temple’s entrance; indiran tānum – indran (the leader of dēvas); ānaiyum – airāvatam (his elephant vehicle); vandu – not only he has arrived; antarattu amarargaḷ – dēvas who reside in the svarga lōkam (worldly heaven); kūttangaḷ – their vehicles, family, assistants, etc; aru tavam munivarum – very saintly persons such as sanaka, sanandana, etc., riśis; marutarum – marutas with their assistants, etc; iyakkarum – yakśas; sundarar nerukkavum – gandarvas closely standing; viccādarar nūkka – vidyādaras pushing each other (in the crowd); tiruvdi toḻuvān mayanginar – standing there mesmerized in anticipation of vorśipping your lotus feet; antaram – sky; pār – bhūmi (earth/land); idam illai – no space; arangattammā – Oh my lord/master lying down in srīrangam!; paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē – (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings

**Перевод:**

**My Lord! indiran, the leader of dēvas, have arrived at the entrance of your divine temple in his airāvatam (elephant – vehicle). Not only him, the dēvas of the svarga lōkam, their assistants, great saints like sanaka maharishi, etc., the marutas along with their assistants, etc., yakśas, gandarvas, vidyādaras, etc., arrive there standing close to each other and pushing each other filling the sky and the land leaving no space. They all stand mesmerized in anticipation of worshiping your lotus feet. Oh my lord/master lying down in srīrangam! (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar and periyavāccān piḷḷai highlight that what is explained in the 6th pāsuram briefly  is explained in more detail in this pāsuram and the next 2 pāsurams. nanjīyar highlights that indran (leader of dēvas) and sapta riśis (ones who propagate knowledge) arrived filling the whole sky to wake him up by singing auspicious slōkas and to worship emperumān‘s lotus feet.  periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that as mentioned in taitriya upaniśad “bhīśāsmāt…” (dēvas are fearful of emperumān and act according to his orders), indran arrived here to manifest his servitude towards emperumān. அந்தரத் தமரர்கள் கூட்டங்களிவையோ அருந்தவ முனிவரும் மருதரு மிவரோ இந்திரனானையும் தானும் வந்திவனோ எம்பெருமானுன் கோயிலின் வாசல் சுந்தரர் நெருக்கவிச் சாதரர் நூக்க இயக்கரும் மயங்கினர் திருவடி தொழுவான் அந்தரம் பாரிடமில்லை மற்றிதுவோ அரங்கத்தம்மாபள்ளி யெழுந்தருளாயே.*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- By the arrival of indran (who is the leader of dēvas), saintly riśis, gandarvas, yakśas, vidyādaras, etc., to worship [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s lotus feet, it is established that [srīman nārāyaṇan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is “sarva svāmy” (master of all).
- aruntava munivar are explained as sanaka, sanātana, sanandana and sanatkumāra – 4 mānasa putras (born from the mind) of brahmā who have great tapasyā (austerity).
- There is so much crowd that even the dvārapālakas (the gate keepers) do not have space to stand.

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- Though indran is identified in the 3rd line only, according to the essence of the pāsuram where dēvas, riśis, etc., are highligted and indran being the leader of them, his arrival should be explained first. An example is cited for such explanation – In praṇavam, though the actual sequence of the letters (a, u, ma) lead to explaining the nature of “[jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)” that the [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) represented by “ma” is the full servant of [paramātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) represented by “a”, but looking at the essence it explains the full lordship of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) over everyone else.
- aruntava munivar are explained as sapta riśis who have **performed** great tapasyā.
- gandarvas, vidyādaras, yakśas are different types of species who have special abilities for singing, dancing, etc.

## 8. Vambavil

*vambaviḻ vānavar vāyuṟai vaḻanga mānidi kapilai oṇ kaṇṇādi mudalā emperumān padimaikkalam kāṇḍaṟku ēṟpanavāyina koṇḍu nal munivar tumburu nāradar pugundanar ivarō tōnṟinan iraviyum tulangoḷi parappi ambara talattil ninṟu agalginṟatu iruḷpōy arangattammā paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

vaḻanga – to submit to your highness; vāyuṟai – aṟugampul (bermuda grass); mā – the best; nidi – sanga nidi, padma nidi, etc – wealth (having them in their hands); vambu aviḻ – with nice fragrance; vānavar – dēvas; kapilai – kāmadēnu (divine cow); oṇ – radiantly shining; kaṇṇadi mudalā – Mirror, etc; emperumān – my lord who is the master; kāṇḍaṟku – to accept them and bless us; ēṟpana āyina – appropriate (to your stature); padimak kalam koṇḍu – bringing the materials; nalmunivar – good/great saints; tumburu nāradar – tumburu, nāradar, etc (divine musicians who constantly serve emperumān); pungundanar – arrived; iraviyum – the sun too; tulangu oḷi – (his) bright radiance; parappi – spreading everywhere; tōnṟinan – appeared; iruḷ – the darkness; ambara talattil ninṟu – from the sky; pōy agalginṟatu – disappeared; arangattammā – Oh my lord/master lying down in srīrangam!; paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē – (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings

**Перевод:**

**Oh my lord who is the master! the great riśis, tumburu, nārada, dēvas who live in svargam which has a nice fragrance, kāmadēnu (the divine cow), etc., have all arrived with bermuda grass, great wealth, mirror, etc., and all the other materials to submit them for your tiruvārādanam. The sun has risen spreading his rays everywhere and the darkness has disappeared. Oh my lord/master lying down in srīrangam! (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights that morning time which is favourable to worship emperumān has arrived. riśis who are ananya prayōjanas (one who is fully focussed on kainkaryam only) have arrived with the necessary materials for the worshiping. Thus toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār requests emperumān to wake up and accept their tiruvārādanam. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights that toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār identifies that many have arrived with the materials that are required for emperumān‘s tiruvārādanam. வம்பவிழ் வானவர் வாயுறை வழங்க மாநிதி கபிலையொண் கண்ணாடி முதலா எம்பெருமான் படிமைக்கலம் காண்டற்கு ஏற்பன வாயின கொண்டு நன் முனிவர் தும்புரு நாரதர் புகுந்தன ரிவரோ தோன்றினனிரவியும் துலங்கொளி பரப்பி அம்பர தலத்தில் நின்றகல்கின்ற திருள்போய் அரங்கத்தம்மாபள்ளி யெழுந்தருளாயே.*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- “*nalmunivar*” is explained as riśis who are ananya prayōjanas – one who dont expect any other prayōjanam than serving [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) for his pleasure alone.
- The sun dispels the external darkness. But the internal darkness (ignorance) can only be removed by [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) who is the rakśaka (protector) for all. **Note**: [piḷḷai lōkācāryar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/18/pillai-lokacharyar/) explains that in mumukśuppadi 36th sūtram “rakśakatvam” means removing the hurdles (unfavourable aspects) and providing the needs (favourable aspects). In subsequent sūtrams he identifies hurdles/needs for different types of persons. In sūtram 38, among other things, he explains that – for prapannas/mumukśus, samsāra sambandam (existence is this material world due to ignorance) is the hurdle and reaching paramapadam to serve [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) eternally is the need. Such rakśakam can be done by [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) only.

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- “*nalmunivar*” is explained as the ones who are constantly meditating on what is pleasing for [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) – “*attalaikkup pāngānavaṟṟaiyē mananam paṇṇumavargaḷ” (அத்தலைக்குப் பாங்கானவற்றையே மனனம் பண்ணுமவர்கள்*).
- Just like [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) listens to lullaby songs while going to sleep, he listens to suprabātam songs while waking up too. That is why tumburu and nārada have arrived.
- As explained by [nanjīyar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/), [periyavāccān piḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/) also explains about how [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) should help get rid of internal darkness.

## 9. Etamil

*ētamil taṇṇumai ekkam mattaḷi yāḻ kuḻal muaḻavamōdu isai disai keḻumik kītangaḷ pādinar kinnarar kerudar(gaḷ) kadaruvar avar kanguluḷellām mātavar vānavar cāraṇar iyakkar sittarum manginar tiruvadi toḻuvān ātalil avarkku nāḷōlakkam aruḷa arangattammā paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

ētamil – blemiśless (without any defect); taṇṇumai – small one sided drum; ekkam – single string instrument; mattaḷi – mattaḷam (a type of two-sided drum – like a mrdangam/dōlak); yāḻ – vīṇai (string instrument); kuḻal – pullānguḻal (flutes); disai – in all directions; muḻavamōdu – with their sound; isai keḻumi kītangaḷ pādinar – ones who are capable of singing with the music spreading (in all directions); kinnarar – kinnaras; garudar – garudas; gandaruvar avar – gandarvas who are standing there; kanguluḷellām – all through the night; mātavar – great riśis (sages); vānavar – dēvas; cāraṇar – cāraṇas; iyakkar – yakśas; sittar – siddas; tiruvadi toḻuvān – to worship your lotus feet; mayanginar – became mesmerized (in the crowd/close proximity); ātalil – thus; avarkku – for them; nāḷōlakkam aruḷa – to bless them audience in the grand assembly in the morning; arangattammā – Oh my lord/master lying down in srīrangam!; paḷḷi eḻundu aruḷāyē – (Thus,) you kindly wake up and give your blessings

**Перевод:**

**The kinnaras, garudas, gandarvas, etc., are playing many blemiśless musical instruments such as one sided drums, single stringed instruments, double sided drums, vīṇai, flutes, etc., making loud sounds which spread in all directions. Some have been arriving throughout the night and others in the morning. Great riśis, dēvas, cāraṇas, yakśas, siddas, etc., have arrived to worship your lotus feet. Oh my lord/master lying down in srīrangam! (Thus,) you kindly wake up and bless them your audience in the grand assembly this morning.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar and periyavāccān piḷḷai highlight that great musicians and dancers have arrived to wake emperumān up and serve him. Thus, toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār requests emperumān to wake and accept their service. ஏதமில் தண்ணுமை யெக்கம் மத்தளியே யாழ் குழல் முழவமோடிசை திசை கெழுமி கீதங்கள் பாடினர் கின்னரர் கெருடர்கள் கந்தருவரவர் கங்குலு ளெல்லாம் மாதவர் வானவர் சாரணரியக்கர் சித்தரும் மயங்கினர் திருவடி தொழுவான் ஆதலிலவர்க்கு நாளோலக்க மருள அரங்கத்தம் மாபள்ளி யெழுந்தருளாயே.*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- [toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) says to [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) – all the different types of devotees have arrived and are eagerly waiting to have your darsan. So, since you are the protector of all, kindly wake up and bless them and engage them in your service.

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- To establish [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) being the protector of all irrespective them being high/low in stature, [tirumaḻisai āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/16/thirumazhisai-azhwar/)‘s nānmugan tiruvantādi 47th pāsuram is quoted here:

*nanmaṇi vaṇṇanūr āḷiyum kōḷariyum*  
*ponmaṇiyum muttamum pūmaramum*  
*panmaṇi nīrōdu poruturḷum kānamum vānaramum*  
*vēdum udai vēnkatam*

In this pāsuram, tiruvēnkatam (tirumalā) which is the abode of tiruvēnkatamudaiyān (srīnivāsan) who has blakciś-blue bodily-hue (the color of a pure high quality pearl) is also glorified as the abode of yāḷi (a lion like huge animal with an elephant-like-tusk), lions, gold, precious stones, pearls, flower-bearing-trees, forests which is abundant of water-falls that bring along valuable gems, monkeys and hunters. Here, the principle is, [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) has descended to tiruvēnkatam to bless all these different types of entities which are seen in the mountain.

## 10. Kadimalar

*kadimalark kamalangal malarntana ivaiyō katiravan kanaikadal muḷaittanan ivanō tudi idaiyār suri kuḻal piḻintu udarit tugiluduttu ēṟinar sūḻpunal arangā todai otta tuḷavamum kūdaiyum polindu tōnṟiya tōḷ toṇḍaradippodi ennum adiyanai aḷiyan enṟu aruḷi un adiyārkku ātpaduttāy paḷḷi eḻuntaruḷāyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

punal sūḻ – surrounded by the sacred water of cauvery river; arangā – oh srīranganāta who is lying down in srīrangam!; kadi – fragrant; kamalam malargaḷ – lotus flowers; malarntana – have blossomed (fully); katiravan – the sun (who can trigger the blossoming of the lotus); kanai kadal – in the ocean which is by nature making huge noise; muḷaittanan – appeared in the udayagiri (eastern side); tudi idaiyār – the women who have very small waist like a udukkai (hand held small drum which has a thin middle portion with two ends); suri kuḻal – (their) curly hairs; piḻintu udari – dried it fully (removing all water); tugil uduttu – wearing (their) clothes; ēṟinar – climbed the bank (came out of the river); todai otta – properly prepared; tuḷavamum – tiruttuḻāi (tuḷasi) garland; kūdaiyum – flower basket; polindu tōnṟiya – śiningly manifesting; tōḷ – shoulder; toṇḍaradippodi ennum – carrying the auspicious name “toṇḍaradippodi”; adiyanai – dāsan – servant; aḷiyan enṟu aruḷi – acknowledging that I am a suitable candidate for your blessings; un adiyārkku – bhāgavatas who are the servants of your holiness; āḷ paduttāy – engage me in their service; (ataṟkāga) paḷḷi eḻuntaruḷāy – (for that purpose) kindly wake up and bless me

**Перевод:**

**oh srīranganāta who is lying down in srīrangam which is surrounded by the holy water of the cauvery river!  The fragrant lotus flowers have blossomed seeing the rise of the sun in the ocean which by nature makes constant noise. The women who have very small waist like a udukkai (small hand-held drum) have dried their curly hairs (after completing their bathing), worn their clothes and have reached the banks of the river. (me) This servant (of your holiness) named  toṇḍaradippodi (the dust of the lotus feet of the bhāgavatas) is manifesting the shining shoulders which carry the flower-basket that contains properly prepared tiruttuḻāi (tuḷasi) garlands . (Thus,) you wake kindly wake up, acknowledge that I am a suitable candidate for your blessings and engage me fully in the service the bhāgavatas (your dear servants).**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*pāsuram: nanjīyar highlights – toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār explains that “From the first pāsuram to the ninth pāsuram, dēvas, riśis who come with ulterior motives and leave after being fulfilled with those motives are explained. In this pāsuram, kindly wake up and bless me who simply seek out to have the vision of your beauitful face while waking up”. periyavāccān piḷḷai highlights Here the upakrama (beginning) and upasamhāra (ending) are perfectly in sync/harmony in this prabandam like few others such as nammāḻvār‘s tiruviruttam (in the first pāsuram, āḻvār says “adiyēn seyyum viṇṇappam” and in the last pāsuram he says “māṟan viṇṇappam seyta“), āṇḍāḷ‘s tiruppāvai (in the first pāsuram she says “namakkē paṟai taruvān” and in the last pāsuram she says “angap paṟai koṇḍa“) and āḷavandār‘s stōtra ratanam (in the first slōkam he says “nātāya munayē” and in the last slōkam he says “pitāmaham nātamunim vilōkya“). Here also, āḻvār starts with “katiravan guṇadisaic cikaram vantaṇaintān” in the first pāsuram and ends with “katiravan kanaikadal muḷaittanan” in the last pāsuram. For the lotus flowers blossoming seeing the sun, a beautiful example is given. Just like when a father’s face blossoms seeing the return of a son from foreign-land, the lotus flower blossoms seeing the arrival of sun who disappeared the previous evening. கடிமலர்க் கமலங்கள் மலர்ந்தன இவையோ கதிரவன் கனைகடல் முளைத்தனன் இவனோ துடியிடையார் சுரி குழல் பிழிந்துதறித் துகிலுடுத் தேறினர் சூழ்புன லரங்கா தொடையொத்த துளவமும் கூடையும் பொலிந்து தோன்றிய தோள் தொண்டரடிப்பொடி யென்னும் அடியனை அளியனென்றருளி யுன்னடியார்க் காட்படுத் தாய்பள்ளி எழுந்தருளாயே*

**Highlights from the** [**nanjīyar**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- The reason for [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) quoting the incident of women with thin waists and curly hairs is to remind [periya perumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) about the girls of vrindāvan where the gōpikās would bathe in the ghats of yamunā and thus wake him up quickly. bhattar used to say “[periya perumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is kaṇṇan [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) and [namperumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is srī rāma”.
- First [periya perumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s identity is revealed by saying “sūḻ punal arangā!” – he is identified with srīrangam which is an island in the cauvery river and is his dearest abode. Cauvery river is so pleasant that [periya perumāḷ](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) forgets about virajā river (which separates srīvaikuṇṭam and samsāram), sarayu river (ayōdyā) and yamunā river (maturā/vrindāvan).
- Then [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) reveals his own identity of having the flower basket and well prepared garlands which are quite fitting for [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/)‘s glories. Just like iḷaiya perumāḷ (lakśmaṇa) was identified in srī rāmāyaṇam as the one who was holding a spade (for working in the fields) and a basket (to carry the soil) along with his bow/arrows to guide perumāḷ (srī rāma), here [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) is identified with the flower-basket and flowers. This sēśatvam (servitude) is the real wealth for [jīvātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)s.
- toṇḍaradippodi (dust of the lotus feet of bhāgavatas) – as the name suggests [āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/) is situated in the ultimate position of being the servant of [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s devotees.

**Highlights from the** [**periyavāccān piḷḷai**](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/)**‘s vyākhyānam:**

- **kadi**malar – fragrant flowers – It is obvious that the flowers in this srīrangam will be most fragrant since this is the abode of srī mahālakśmi who is identified in [nammāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/18/nammazhwar-english/)‘s tiruvāimoḻi 10.10.2 as  “*vāsam sey pūnguḻalāḷ tiru*” (the abode of [srī mahālakśmi](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) whose hair is most fragrant).
- [tiruppāṇāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/21/thiruppanazhwar/) identifies in amalanādipirān 10th pāsuram “*kōvalanāy veṇṇey uṇḍā vāyan … aṇi arangan*” ([srīranganātan](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) is the same kriṣṇa who as a covherd boy ate butter).
- The divine conch and disc are [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)‘s identities as identified in [tirumangai āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/23/thirumangai-azhwar/)‘s periya tirumoḻi 11.2.6 “*āḻiyum sangumudaiya nangaḷadigaḷ*“. Similarly, the flower-basket is the most important identity of a bhāgavata’s identity. **Note:** Garland making kainkaryam is glorified as the purest kainkaryam to perform for [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) in other places as well. Notably, [periyāḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/20/periyazhwar/), [toṇḍaradippodi āḻvār](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/01/08/thondaradippodi-azhwar/), [anantāḻvān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/03/31/ananthazhwan/), etc., have engaged in this puśpa (flower garlands) kainkaryam.
- When the extraneous aspects of [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) are removed, ultimately the [ātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) is identified by his sēśatvam (servitude) towards [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/) and his devotees only.
- Invalid stage of sēśatvam (servitude) is towards samsāram (materialist people and aspects). First valid stage of sēśatvam (servitude) is towards [emperumān](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi/). Ultimate stage of sēśatvam (servitude) is towards bhāgavatas.
- Why are you still sleeping even after getting a devotee like me who has understood and accepted the ultimate stage of sēśatvam? Should you not get up and bless me now?

Thus ends the English translation for tiruppaḷḷiyeḻucci based on the wonderful vyākhyānams written by [nanjīyar](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/09/14/nanjiyar-english/) and [periyavāccān piḷḷai](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2013/10/05/periyavachan-pillai-english/).
