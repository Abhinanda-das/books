# Tiruvasiriyam

## 1. Sekkar ma mugil

*sekkarmā mugiluduttu mikka senjudarp paridisūdi anjudar madiyam pūṇḍu pala sudar punainda pavaḷac cevvāy tigaḻ pasunjōdi maragadak kunṟam kadalōn kaimisaik kaṇ vaḷarvadupōl pītagavādai mudi pūṇ mudalā mētagu palkalan aṇindu sōdi vāyavum kaṇṇavum sivappa mīdittup paccai mēni migap pagaippa naccu vinaik kavartalai aravinamaḷi ēṟi eṟikadal naduvuḷ aṟituyil amarndu sivan ayan indiran ivar mudal anaittōr deyvak kuḻāngaḷ kaitoḻak kidanda tāmarai undi tanip peru nāyaga! mūvulagaḷanda sēvadiyōyē!*

**Пословный перевод:**

sekkar mā mugil uduttu – wearing a huge red cloud around his waist; mikka sem sudar – with the highly effulgent red rays; paridi sūdi – sun on his head; am sudar – beautiful cool rays; madiyam pūṇḍu – wearing the moon; pala sudar – (stars) with many glitters; punainda – wearing it; pavaḷam sem vāi – red coral like lips; tigaḻ pasum sōdi – bright green color; maragada kunṟam – an emerald mountain; kadalōn kai misai – on the hands (waves) of the ocean king (varuṇa); kaṇ vaḷarvadu pol – just like sleeping; pītaga ādai – pītāmbaram (yellow garment); mudi – crown; pūṇ – necklace; mudalā – etc; mētagu palkalan aṇindu – wearing a variety of worthy jevellery; sōdi vāyavum – glowing coral red lips; kaṇṇavum – eyes; sivappa – being red in color; mīdittu – being victorious (competition); paccai mēni miga pagaippa – green color becomes more visible; naccu vinai – (while destroying enemies) he who has poisonous acts; kavar talai – having fallen heads; aravu amaḷi – on the cozy bed of tiruvanandāḻvān (ādiśēśan); ēṟi – climbing; kadal naduvul – in the midst of sea full of waves; aṟi tuyil amarndu – engaged in yōga nidrā (meditation) to protect the world; Sivan ayan indiran ivar mudal – like Siva, brahmā, indra; anaittōr deyva kuḻāngal – all the group of dēvatās; kai toḻa – to worship with the joined palms; kidanda – lying down; tāmarai undi – having the nābhīkamalam (lotus in the navel, which is the origin for all creation); tani peru nāyaga – Oh emperumān who is greater than all!; mū ulagu aḷanda – measured the three world; sēvadiyōyē – Oh one who is having the divine feet!

**Перевод:**

**sekkar mā mugil uduttu – Like those who saw an elephant would chant incessantly “elephant, elephant”, āḻvār praises emperumān being unable to control the love out of enjoyment. The garment which is (sekkar) red in colour which is a contrasting colour to his blackish complexion; (mā) the garment is having vastness to have folds and fleets; (mugil) the garment is cool like a cloud, matching his tender body; adorning such garment as if it naturally grew out of his waist \[such perfect fit\].**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*The proper sequence of enjoying emperumān is to enjoy his svarūpam (true nature), rūpam (form) and guṇam (qualities); however āḻvār leaves aside the svarūpam and instructs us to enjoy His rūpam directly;and the reason for this is – his attachment towards the same. When āḻvār started experiencing emperumān’s rūpam, that itself blocks him from enjoying it directly like an ocean pushing its waves on those who want to enjoy it; so, just as someone desiring to step in to a flood will use a boat to do so, āḻvār too sets to enjoy emperumān through an example. Since bhagavān is distinctly unique, there is no exact match for bhagavat viśayam; but at least one should compare an object which has some minor similarity; \[hence\] āḻvār is highlighting an emerald mountain as example. Since the emerald mountain lacks the exact qualities of emperumān, āḻvār starts with decorating it \[to make it look more comparable to emperumān\]. Just as he left aside svarūpam and started engaging in the rūpam, here, he leaves aside the rūpam and starts engaging in the object which is cited as example and decorating it. Let us go through the pāsuram and its meanings:*

**mikka sem sudarp paridi sūdi** – As said in [śrī bhagavat gītā 11.12](http://githa.koyil.org/index.php/11-12/) “*divi sūrya sahasrasya bhavēdyugapaduttitā \| yadi bhā: sadruśī sā syād bhāsas tasya mahātmana: \|\|”* (If the splendour of a thousand suns were to blaze out at once (simultaneously) in the sky,that would be the effulgence of the mighty lord) – it is said that the sun has 1000 rays, but for the crown of this emerald mountain ([emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)) appears to be having many thousands of rays; \[the emerald mountain\] wearing the sun which is having reddish rays.

**am sudar madiyam pūṇḍu** – The emerald mountain is wearing the moon which possess cool rays and is unlike the sun which does not allow anyone to approach it because of its fiery nature; further it is unlike the worldly moon which goes through waxing and waning and having taints, and having beauty to be viewed forever without taking one’s eyes off.

**tigaḻ pasum sōdi maragadak kunṟam kadalōn kaimisaik kaṇ vaḷarvadu pōl** – The emerald mountain which appears to be lofty with its shining green color which leaves everything else inferior, appears to be sleeping on the hands (waves) of the ocean king (varuṇan). Though [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) is talking about the emerald mountain, the example for [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/), he is saying “kaṇ vaḷarvadu pōl” (like divinely resting) instead of saying “like sleeping”, as he feels that it will only cause insult to emperumān’s greatness. [tirumalai nambi](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periya-thirumalai-nambi/) when identifying a person to [emperumānār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/emperumanar/), says “do you know the person who cannot wear on his ears, even if he were given golden ear-rings?”, instead of saying the “person with delicate ears”.

**pītagavādai** – This (yellow garment) is what is explained by “*sekkar mā mugil uduttu*“.

**mudi** – This (crown) is what is explained by “*sem sudar paridi sūdi*“.

**pūṇ** – This (necklace) is what is explained by “*am sudar madiyam pūndu*“.

Starting with this,

**mētagu palkalanaṇindu** – These (ornaments) are explained by “*pala sudar punainda*“.

**mētagu** – These ornaments are said to be matching for his svarūpam (true nature); or it can be said to be perfectly suited to His tirumēni (divine form). He is wearing many worthy ornaments like necklace etc as explained previously. Even if [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)’s wealth can be measured, His divine ornaments cannot be measured at all. As [bhāśyakārar](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/emperumanar/) explains “***nūpurādyaparimita divya bhūśaṇa***” in his śaraṇāgadi gadyam listing the ornaments such kirīta, makuta, cūdā, avatamsa, makara kuṇḍala, graivēyaka, hāra, kēyūra, kataka, śrīvatsa, kaustuba, muktādāma, udara bhandana, pītāmbara, kāncīguṇa, nūpur and then ends with “*ādi*” (starting with), here too [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) enlists many ornaments and say “*palkalan*“.

**sōdi vāyavum kaṇṇavum sivappa** – While in the example, only a single entity “red coral like lips” is shown, why is the mouth and eyes are highlighted here? It is for this reason only, there it was explained as “(many) reddish spots on the mountain”.

**paccai mēni mīdittu migap pagaippa** – The divine ornaments and the [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)‘s tirumēni which possess all of those ornaments, compete with each other; his tirumēni wins the competition and shine with blackish complexion.

**naccu vinai** – As said in mūnṟām tiruvandādi 66 “*vāynda madu kaidaparum vayiṟurugi māṇḍār*” (the powerful madu and kaitapa died); tiruvanandāḻvān (ādiśēśan) will single handedly destroy the enemies with his poisonous acts.

**kavartalai** – tiruvanandāḻvān having several hoods; also said as his acquiring many hoods as a result of coming in contact with [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/). Like a mother who keeps child in her lap, here tiruvanandāḻvān (ādiśēśan) keeps [periya perumāḷ](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) on his lap and shows motherly love to Him. When [anantāḻvān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/ananthazhwan/) (disciple of [emperumānār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/emperumanar/)) went to tirukkōśtiyūr and he held [bhattar](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/parasara-bhattar/) (who is the revered son of [kūrattāḻvān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/kuraththazhwan/)) on his lap and manifested motherly affection towards bhattar. āṇḍāḷ, the consort of kūrattālvān had a child first but he died at a very young age. When bhattar was born to them, emperumānār thinks that “we should not let this child suffer the same fate and he should live long” and so he performs tirumanjanam (sacred bathing) to [periya pirātti](https://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/2012/08/17/divya-dhampathi-english/) and feed that holy water to him and offers him in adoption to periya pirātti and [periya perumāḷ](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/). And because of this anantāḻvān would call bhattar as “periya pirattiyār’s son”.

**aravin amaḷi ēṟi** – Climbing on the cozy bed of tiruvanandāḻvān which has nice fragrance, coolness and softness. Here amaḷi refers the bed.

**ēri** – Since [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) considers climbing on tiruvanandāḻvān as his own gain/pleasure, he climbs on him with great desire, as nampermāḷ (utsava deity in śrīrangam) would climb the six legged palanquin with great desire.

**eṟi kadal naduvuḷ** – in the \[milky\] ocean which is throwing up the waves, being \[favourably/happily\] agitated by the presence of [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/).

**aṟi tuyil amarndu** – Being engaged in sleep which is an embodiment of wisdom; for the others, there will be darkness in their heart when they close their eyes; they will enjoy by worldly aspects through their senses such as eyes etc. But if their eyes are closed both the inner mind and their external vision becomes dark for them. Here when [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) reclines on tiruvanandāḻvān closing his divine eyes, it implies that he is contemplating about protecting the world and meditating upon himself since there is none higher to him.

**sivan ayan indiran ivar mudal anaittōr deyvak kuḻāngkaḷ kai toḻak kidanda** – Those who are popularly known as gods in this world, will themselves give up their pride and worship [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/). dēvatās such as brahmā et al, give up their pride and worship him being captivated his beauty while reclining.

When asked “And what will emperumān do when these dēvatās worship Him?”, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says

**kidanda** – When a flock of cranes enter the sea (to catch fish) and produce cheerful noise, it will not affect the sea; similarly even if the dēvatās like Siva etc. and the hordes of gods worship him (who is distinctly unique) in his lying down posture, due to his greatness, he remains undisturbed. [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) too considers both of these aspects \[[emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)‘s tranquil sleep and the dēvatās vorśipping him\] as natural actions \[without giving any specific importance\].

**tāmarai undi** – He who has the divine lotus navel which is the cause for the entire creation;

When asked “Is there any one like him?”, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says

**tanipperu nāyaga** – There is no one who is superior to [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/). If there is no one superior than Him (above Him), is there any one who is equivalent to Him? No, he is distinctly unique; Though other dēvatās are referred as supreme in the scriptures, it is only done as a courteous act. It is said in mahābhāratam “***rudram samāśritā dēvā rudrō brahmāṇamāsrita: \| brahmā māmāśritō rājan nāham kancitupāśrita:\|\|***” (dēvatās are surrendered to rudra; rudra is surrendered to his father, brahmā; brahmā is surrendered to me; but, Oh king! I am not surrendered to any one) – all these dēvatās expect something from others. Unlike everyone who is controlled of him, he is the distinct supreme lord who has none to control him; it is explaining that he is all the three causes \[for creation\]  viz nimitta kāraṇam (efficient cause), upādāna kāraṇam (material cause) and sahakāri kāraṇam (instrumental/ancillary cause).

**mūvulagaḷanta** – For those people who cannot come to him and enjoy [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/), He himself goes to wherever they reside \[and graces them by touching them with His holy feet\].

**sēvadiyōyē** – The sweetness of his divine feet is such that, we cannot leave him, even if he is so cruel that he does not help us.

Thus, in this pāsuram, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) celebrates [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)‘s saundaryam (flawless beauty) and other auspicious qualities.

Let us enjoy the 2nd pāsuram next.

adiyen booma ramanuja dasi

## 2. Ulagu padaittunda

*ulagupadaittuṇḍa endai aṟai kaḻal sudar pūm tāmarai sūdudaṟku avāvār uyirurugi ukka, nēriya kādal anbilinbīn tēṟal amuda veḷḷat tānām siṟappu vittu oru porutku asaivōr asaiga, tiruvodu maruviya iyaṟkai, māyāp peru viṟal ulagam mūnṟinōdu nal vīdu peṟinum koḷvadeṇṇumō teḷḷiyōr kuṟippē?*

**Пословный перевод:**

ulagupadaittu – emperumān who created all the worlds; uṇḍa – and ate them up (during praḷayam (deluge)); endai – emperumān is my lord; aṟaikaḻal – holy feet of the lord resounding with the ankle bells; sudar pū tāmarai – glittering lotus flower; sūdudaṟku – to wear it (beautiful lotus like tiruvadi to wear it on head); avā – with desire; Ar – filled with; uyir urugi ukka – the soul that melts and fall; nēriya kādal – the love/affection (in the form of bhakti) which resulted from it; anbil – in the the desire (to reach emperumān); inbu – that produces the bliss; In tēṟal amuda veḷḷattānām – submerging into nectar-like ocean which is great in sweetness; siṟappu vittu – renouncing the pride; oru porutku – for the meagre goals of life; asaivōr – those who get afflicted; asaiga – wanders; tiruvodu maruviya – in association with the wealth/opulence; iyaṟkai – true nature; māyā peru viṟal – with imperishable immense strength; ulagam mūnṟinōdu – along with the three worlds; nal vīdu peṟinum – even if attaining mōkśam (which is the ultimate goal of life) also; teḷḷiyōr – the vivēki’s who have clear wisdom; kuṟippu – opinion; koḷvadu – to obtain all these; eṇṇumō – will think?

**Перевод:**

**ulagu padaittunda – As said in cāndōjña upaniśad “tadaikśata – bahusyām prajāyēyēti“ (he vowed to become many forms and then creates the entire world), bhagavān (in many forms) with his sankalpam (divine will) creates all the the entities apart from him; and when danger like praḷaya (dissolution) occurs for these entities , he protects all these by placing them inside His stomach; and because of these acts of emperumān, his undiminiśed supremacy (paratvam) is established.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*As said in puruśa sūktam “ādityavarṇam tamasa: parastāt” (The parama puruśa shines brightly like sun and is beyond the primordial matter) , āḻvār explained about the greatness of bhagavān’s (dyēya vastu – object of meditation) distinct form (vigraha vailakśaṇyam) in his first pāsuram. And in the second pāsuram he says that the devotion to emperumān only is required for him, since parabhakti (the growing desire to be with bhagavān) etc on bhagavān, are all part of the ultimate goal. In the first pāsuram he explained emperumān’s auspicious qualitites like saundaryam (bodily beauty), paratvam (supremacy) and sauseelyam (magnanimity); and in the second pāsuram he says that these qualities of emperumān act like karma, jñāna, bhakti yōgams for āḻvār and subsequently lead to āḻvār’s para bhakti, para jñāna and parama bhakti which are considered as part of the ultimate goal. These para bhakti etc are explained as desirable. Now, āḻvār repeats those qualities of emperumān which acts as jñāna, bhakti yōgams in this pāsuram starting with “ulagu padaittuṇḍa endai aṟai kaḻal sudarp pūm tāmarai“. Let us go through the pāsuram and its meanings:*

**endai** – [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says that even though emperumān is distinctly supreme (being lord to everyone – sarva svāmi), he himself came forth and accepted āḻvār; since āḻvār thinks himself as the most inferior person in all the three worlds; and because of this acceptance of emperumān, āḻvār highlights his magnanimity. As said in first pāsuram “*mūvulagaḷanda*” (which states His magnanimity), āḻvār thinks himself as the lowest person in these three worlds.

**aṟai** – emperumān’s tiruvadi (the sacred feet) is wearing ornaments that make jingling sound, are radiant and most enjoyable; with this emperumān’s saundaryam (bodily beauty) is explained.

**tāmarai sūdudaṟku** – As soon as his sacred feet are said to be resembling the lotus flower, one would naturally desire to place them on his/her head as said in [stōtra ratnam 31](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2017/01/sthothra-rathnam-31/) “***kadā punaś śankharatāngakalpaka dvajāravaindānkuśa vajralāncanam \| trivikrama!*** (oh trivikrama! When will my head be decorated with your two divine feet which are having \[the marks of\] śankha , chakra , et al?) \[here āḻvār highlights his desire to place [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)’s lotus-like sacred feet on his head\].

**sūdudaṟku avāvāruyir** – When the jīvātmā meditates upon the distinguished nature of emperumān, it is impossible for him to give up the yearning for Him.

**sūdudaṟku avāvāruyir** – The desire on wearing it.

**āruyir**– The soul that desires it.

**urugi ukka**– The soul which cannot be split or melted, attained a liquid state \[due to melting\] such that it can never be seen as ātmā again.

**nēṟiya kādal** – And that desire lead to a distinguished involvement/attachment \[in emperumān\];  [śrī bhagavat gītā 2-62](http://githa.koyil.org/index.php/2-62/) “ ***sangāt sanjāyatē kāma:*****“** (While contemplating on the objects of the senses, one develops attachment to them and that attachment leads to desire, and from that desire arises the anger); and in cāndōjña upaniśat 7-26-2 “***druvā smriti:”*** (from purity of food comes purity of mind, and from that comes constant remembrance of God); here [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says that attachment (which arise out of desire towards Him) resulted in love/devotion on [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/).

**anbil inbu** – As said in [śrī bhagavad gītā 9-2](http://githa.koyil.org/index.php/9-2/) “***susukham kartum avyayam*****”** (knowledge is the king of wisdom and is most confidential. It can be perceived by self realization, and it is joyous to perform and is everlasting); here āḻvār says that since the love/devotion is on [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who is filled auspicious qualities, it is so sweet; because of the sweetness of emperumān, the meditation upon him would also be sweet.

When asked how sweet this love is? āḻvār says – It is like the nectar.

**In tēṟal** – Due to emperumān’s sweet nature, it will taste like the concentrated nectar which is churned of an ocean of nectar. It is like an ocean of nectar since it is towards emperumān who is said as in taittirīya upaniśat – 5 “***ānandamaya:*****“** (He is filled with bliss); and due to emperumān’s distinguished nature and the devotee’s attachment towards him, it will be like an individual falling into an ocean \[of nectar\] and enjoying it immensely.

**amuda veḷḷattānām**– drowning into the flood of joy.

**siṟappu vitu** – leaving the parabhakti, parajñāna and paramabhakti which is the greatest wealth \[Implies the person who leaves this prosperity is known as foolish person\].

**oru porutku** – If a person leaves the above said greatest wealth, he will have to think obtaining the puruśārtams (goals) such as dharma (righteousness), arta (wealth), kāma (desire) and mōkśa (salvation) only; and here [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says that he doesn’t even consider these goals as worthy; and due to his disregard for them, he calls these goals as “***oru porutku*****”** (just another thing)”.

**asaivōr asaiga** – Unlike the greatest wealth like parabhakti etc., the puruśārtam (dharma etc.) will be very hard for one to obtain; and even if they are attained, the significance and the benefit of them will be very meagre; and those people who are attached to them will have much greater tribulation. Though it is said in [śrī bhagavat gītā 12-5](http://githa.koyil.org/index.php/12-5/) “***klēśō’dikataras*****”** (greatly troublesome); and when asked “why many people desire for them?”, āḻvār says here “am I talking about foolish people? Would the intelligent ones ever think about these goals?“.

**tiruvodu maruviya iyaṟkai māyāp peruviṟal ulagam mūnṟinōdu** – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) is always with the eternal wealth/opulence; and though one may possess such  wealth but if he doesn’t have power/energy to enjoy it, there will be not be any use of it; unlike those emperumān possess everlasting majestic potency; his wealth is not limited to particular location but spread over all three worlds.

Moreover,

**nalvīdu perinum** – As **nalvīdu** indicates uttama puruśārtam (top most goal of enjoying and serving [bhagavān](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/)), here \[**vīdu**\] the inferior goal of kaivalyam (eternally enjoying oneself) should also be included.

**perinum** – it is not only about desiring for these goals, but attaining them.

**koḷvadeṇṇumō** – [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) asks “Can one even think about attaining them?”, and he himself replies “No”; since worldly wealth is temporary, it is discarded; since ātmānubhavam is insignificant \[though permanent\], it is discarded; since bhagavat anubhavam is enjoyed only with a different body \[which is attained in paramapadam\[, and not the current body, it is discarded too. he says “do we even need to think about these goals which are incomparable with the greatest wealth – parabhakti , parajñānam and parama bhakti, which are enjoyed in the current body itself?”.

But when asked “Still, why are some people are desiring for these goals?“, āḻvār replies,

**teḷḷiyōr kurippu ennumō** – āḻvār says that the vivēki (intelligent person) who can distinguish between the sāra (essence) and asāra (residue) will not even mind to think about anything other than parama bhakti (total devotion towards lord); and these viveki’s are born with the ability to differentiate between good and bad. As explained by [emperumānar](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/emperumanar/) in his vēdārta sangraham – 3 “***sārāsāravivēkajñā garīyamsa:*****“** (the intellectual can differentiate between the sāra and asāra), and by [tirumaḻisaip pirān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/thirumazhisai-azhwar/) in his tiruccanta viruttam – 108 “***kēdil sīr varattanāyk kedum … kūdum āsai alladonṟu koḷvanō kuṟippilē*****“** (even though obtaining the wealth of brahmā, Siva and indra and reaching the highest state of liberation, he will never ever entertain any desire other than being with the lord)” \[[āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) concludes at the end that the experience of the divine and service to [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) is the ultimate supreme bliss\].

Let us move on to the 3rd pāsuram.

## 3. Kurippil kondu

*kuṟippil koṇḍu neṟippada, ulagam mūnṟudan vaṇangu tōnṟu pugaḻ āṇai meypeṟa nadāya deyvam mūvaril mudalvanāgic cudar viḷangagalattu varaipurai tiraiporu peruvarai veruvara, urumural olimali naḷirkadaṟ padavara varasu udal tadavarai suḻaṟṟiya, tanimāt teyvattadiyavarkkininām āḷāgavē isaiyum kol, ūḻitōṟ uḻi ōvādē*

**Пословный перевод:**

mūnṟru ulagam – the three worlds; neṟi pada – to live in the path of righteousness (dharma); kuṟippil koṇḍu – (bhagavān) who make sankalpam (divine will) in his divine heart; udan vaṇangu – vorśipped by all the people of the three worlds; tōnṟu pugaḻ – having renowned fame; āṇai mey pera nadāya – conducts his divine rules (śruti) in right way; deyvam mūvaril – among the three gods brahmā, rudra and indra; mudalvan āgi – He is the foremost of the three gods; sudar viḷangu agalattu – having shining bejeveled divine chest; varai purai tirai – the waves as large as mountain; poru – to collide; peru varai veruvu uṟa – to tremble even the giant mountain; urumu ural oli mali – roars like thunderclap; naḷir kadal – the cool ocean; padam aravu arasu – with vāsuki (the king of serpents with expanded hoods); udal – body; tadam varai suḻaṟṟiya – winding round the great mountain (mandara) and churned the ocean; tani māt teyvam – emperumān who is distinctly supreme; adiyavarkku – to the bhāgavatas; ini nām – here after ourselves; ūḻitōṟuḻi – during every kalpa (eon); ōvādu – incessantly; āḷāga – to become servitors; isaiyum kol – will it be possible (being servitors to His devotees)?

**Перевод:**

**kuṟippil koṇḍu neṟippada ulagam mūnṟudan vaṇangu tōnṟu pugaḻ āṇai meypeṟa nadāya deyvam mūvaril mudalvan – In this pāsuram starting from kuṟippil koṇḍu till deyvam mūvaril mudalvan, āḻvār describes emperumān’s true nature and his auspicious qualities. neṟippadak kuṟippil koṇḍu – As said in cāndōjña upaniśad “tadaikśata – bahusyām prajāyēyēti“ (he vowed to become many forms and then creates the entire world), bhagavān (in many forms) creates the entire universe with his own sankalpam (divine will); also explained said as – emperumān desires that all other beings (sentient and insentient, except Him) should live in the path of righteousness (dharma) – to worship Him.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*In the first pāsuram āḻvār describes and celebrates emperumān’s saundaryam (bodily beauty); and in the second pāsuram he says that the devotion towards emperumān (which emerges from these auspicious qualities of Him) is so sweet , since this can be enjoyed here itself with this current body itself (instead of experiencing bhagavat anubhavam by reaching paramapadam with a different body); but when asked “what could be the measure for this devotion (towards Him)?”, āḻvār replies that it begins with Him (servitude towards bhagavān) and will be up to His devotees \[servitude towards bhāgavatas\]. Let us go through the pāsuram and its meanings:*

When asked “will there be some righteous people and some unrighteous (not vorśipping Him)?”, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) replies,

**ulagam mūnṟu** – To have everyone else other than emperumān himself to surrender unto him. Though there are those who surrender and who give refuge \[in this world\], in the case of bhagavān, all other beings (other than Him) should surrender at the holy feet of [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/).

**udan vaṇangu** – Though the group of dēvatās are being worshiped by people, they don’t have the fame of being vorśipped by everybody united together, but in the case of [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) and since He is distinctly unique (having renowned fame), everybody will gather together and worship Him.

When asked “ will this fame lie in a corner unknowable to anyone?” [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says,

**tōnṟu pugaḻ** – It is popular that it is said even in the śruti (vēdam).

**āṇai meypeṟa nadāya** – unlike the others who have fault in executing the commandments, [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) is the one who makes his commandments perfectly.

“When asked who is He (who has renowned fame and perfectly executes his commandments)?”, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says

**deyvam mūvaril mudalvanāgi** – He is the foremost of all the three dēvatās (Siva, brahmā and indra), since he is the one who creates them from his divine navel and protects them by placing in his stomach during praḷayam (deluge).

**nadāya deyvam mūvar** – If the other dēvatās like rudra, brahmā et al who are at times confused to be supreme, it is only because of His generous act.

**mūvaril mudalvanāgi** – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who is distinctly unique from other two dēvatās (Siva, brahmā), rules their śarīram (body) and śarīri (ātmā) with his aprākruta tirumēni (divine body). Alternative explanation – Since brahmā, rudra and indra all together are said as three dēvatās in pramāṇam (authentic texts) and here [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) is said as the foremost (creator) of these three dēvatās.

**sudar viḷangagalattu** – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who has the divine chest which shines with the ornamental beauty. Alternative explanation – the rising waves like mountain which are filling the open space in the sky which shines with the light of the moon and sun.

**varai purai** … – Is this eloquent act of churning the milky ocean for nectar among other actions not enough to show that he is the worthy refuge?

**varai purai tirai** – The waves like the mountain.

**tirai poru** – when the waves collide with one another, it looks like two mountains are colliding with each other.

**peru varai veruvuṟa** – To have the giant mountains tremble.

**urumural oli** – At that time, the noise which resembles the noise of thunder increases.

**naḷir kadal** – \[The cool milky ocean\] Even though sparks flew while churning the ocean, it cooled down because of His merciful glance; implies that the ocean trembled without being agitated.

**pada aravu arasu** – The king of serpents, vāsuki – though it is a sentient being and is used for churning, due to being in contact with emperumān, he did not feel any strain and hence expanded it’s hoods with joy while churning.

**tadavarai** – Placing the great mandara mountain (which appears to cover the entire ocean) as the mortar.

**suḻaṟṟiya** – The ocean got so agitated to have the bottom soil rise up to the top, until the elixir is fetched (while churning), and moreover the mountain is placed in the middle and rotated up and down to make everyone who participated to claim “I churned it” \[actually it was emperumān who appeared with thousand hands and churned the ocean\].

**tanimāt teyvam** – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) is the distinct unique lord.

And when asked “are you desiring to surrender unto such emperumān?”, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) replies,

**adiyavarkku** – emperumān troubled himself for the purpose of those other dēvatās who desired for other benefits and gave them the elixir. śrīvaiṣṇavas become overwhelmed by such greatly generous acts of emperumān and present themselves to be fully existing for him. To such devotees.

**ini nām** – While the jīvātmā should have been the servant since its existence, due to karma (virtues/vices), we were occupied by the ahamkāra (ego) and mamakāra (possessiveness) until now; now that emperrumān has shown our true nature of being servitors to such śrīvaiṣṇavas, we who have seen it with his blessings.

When asked “ shall we desire to be in the similar grade/level of these bhāgavatas?”, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) replies,

**āḷāga** – As their servitors.

When asked “should we be both servitors to them and at the same time be with ahankāram and mamakāram?“, āḻvār replies,

**āḷāgavē** – Only as a servitor being an embodiment of such servitude.

**isaiyum kol** – As said in nānmugan tiruvandādi 60 “*āḷpārttuḻi taruvāy kaṇḍu*” (Oh! lord you are in search of a person who has surrendered unto your divine feet), emperumān is already seeking for such persons who will be ready to serve bhāgavatas, so no need to convince him; āḻvār too is already convinced since he is always looking to serve bhāgavatas; so, what is the need for “isaiyum kol”? Since bhāgavata śēṣatvam (servitude towards bhāgavatas) is rare to achieve, āḻvār is eagerly asking “if he will get it now”.

And this is not for a short time, but

**ūḻitōṟūḻi** – It should happen in every kalpa (forever).\\

And moreover,

**ōvātē** – Without even a momentary break. As śiśupāla et al were annihilated unable to bear the presence of [bhagavān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) when he incarnated here for the protection of samsāris even though he is avāpta samasta kāman (one who has no unfulfilled desires), śrīya:pati (divine consort of śrī mahālakśmi) and the lord of nityasūris in paramapadam, now samsāris, without understanding the greatness of bhāgavatas, are failing due to considering them to be similar to self in thinking “as they too are walking around sustaining themselves by eating, drinking etc, just as we do, what is their distinction from us?”. Only when emperumān can be considered as one amongst the created beings, a bhāgavata can also be considered as one amongst samsāris.

Let us proceed to the 4th pāsuram next.

## 4. Ulitoruli

*ūḻitōṟūḻi ōvādu vāḻiya enṟu yām toḻa isaiyum kol, yāvagai ulagamum yāvarum illā mēlvarum perum pāḻ kālattu, irum porutku ellām arumpeṟal tani vittu, orutān āgit teyva nānmugak koḻumuḷai īnṟu, mukkaṇ īsanōdu dēvupala nudali mū ulagam viḷaitta undi, māyak kadavuḷ māmudal adiyē*

**Пословный перевод:**

yāvagai ulagamum – all the categories of the world; yāvarum illā – all kinds of living beings; illā – without; mēlvarum – the past times; perum pāḻ kālattu – very long; irum porutku ellām – for the countless living beings; peṟal arum – emperuman who is difficult to attain; tani oru vittu – He is the distinct, independent primordial cause; tān āgi – emperumān himself (alone); deyvam nānmugan – the dēvatā, brahmā; koḻu muḷai – perfect sprout; mukkaṇ īsanōdu – along with the three-eyed rudra; pala dēvu – several dēvatās; īnṟu – creates; nudali – and make sankalpam (vow them to be the incarge for creation, destruction etc); mū ulagam – the three worlds; viḷaitta – creates; undi – He who has divine navel; māyan – He who has amazing ability; kadavul – being the supreme god; mā mudal – emperumān who is the supreme primordial cause, his; adi – divine feet; ūḻitōṟūḻi – during every kalpa (eon); ōvadu – incessantly; vāḻiya – to live; enṟu – that; yām – us; toḻa – to praise (by performing mangaḷāśāsanam); isaiyum kol – will it be possible?

**Перевод:**

**ūḻitōṟūḻi –  Every kalpam. It is unlike “nitya agni hōtram” \[which is done at specific times every day\]. \[When asked “can we do mangaḷāśāsanam during every kalpa at a particular time?”, āḻvār replies,\]**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*In the third pāsuram when asked “what is the limit for the bhagavat bhakti?”, āḻvār replies it starts from bhagavat śēśatvam (having servitude towards emperumān) and goes up to bhāgavata śēśatvam (having servitude to his devotees). When asked “what will these bhāgavatas do together?”, āḻvār says that they will be singing mangaḷāśāsanam (ūḻitōṟūḻiyōvādu, vāḻiya enṟu) to emperumān as in tiruppallāṇḍu 2 “adiyōmōdum ninnōdum pirivinṟi āyiram pallāṇḍu” (I should be with you forever). periyāḻvar in his tiruppallāndu, performs mangaḷāśāsanam by placing “pallāṇḍu” (many years) as the base year, and finally multiplies that by many (countless) crores of years; here āḻvār not satisfied at that level, starts with “ūḻi” (kalpa, a day of brahmā which contains 1000 four yuga cycle) and then multiplies that by many times. Let us look at the pāsuram and its meanings.*

**ōvadu** – It should be done incessantly every second (without even a momentary break), and when asked “what are the things to be done further?”, he replies,

**vāḻiyavenṟu** – one should always perform mangaḷāśāsanam to [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) as “pallāndu (long live)”. [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) starts describing about [emperumān’s](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) śēśitvam (lordship) in first pāsuram, and in third pāsuram he says that it goes upto his devotees, and here in this pāsuram he says that those who attain the bhāgavata śēśatvam (having servitude to bhāgavatas) should always perform mangaḷāśāsanam to [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/).

**yām** – We who were thinking for self as said in yajur aśtakam3.5 “***āyurāśāstē***” (praying for long life) until now; leaving that state,

**toḻa** – toḻugai (worship) means performing mangaḷāśāsanam.

**isayum kol** –there is a lot of difference between living for oneself and living for bhagavān;

when asked “which act of [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) is meditated upon by those who desire to attain this impossible state?”, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says,

**yāvagai ….. mūvulagam viḷaitta undi** – Instead of just bestowing specific qualities for an existing entity, for the great favour of creating the entire universe from scratch and facilitating us.

**yāvagai ulagamum** – unlike the deluge where just up to the three lōkas \[bhū:, bhuvar and suvar\] are destroyed and the jana lōkam becomes uninhabitable leading the residents to shift upto the mahar lōkam, here āḻvār is talking about the total deluge \[where everything is destroyed\].

Alternative explanation :

**yāvarumillā** – unlike the deluge where at least riśis such as mārkaṇḍēya et al who are present, the deluge where no one exists.

**mēl varum** – In the past times. Here “mēl“ means past and “varum” means “gone” \[implying the past times which are gone\].

**perum pāḻ kālattu** – Just as a farmer would spend a long time draining the salt from the field \[to make it fertile\] by filling it with water, emperumān makes the ātmā stay attached with primordial matter for the whole life span of brahmā, to eliminate the evil interests of the ātmā.

**irum porutkellām** – for jīvātmās who are all connected with infinite forms such as dēva (celestial), manuśya (human), tiryak (animal) and stāvara (plant).

**arum peṟal** – since there is none other than [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who shows them compassion even at this state, he cannot be attained easily (with self effort).

**tani vittu oru tānāgi** – [bhagavān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) himself being all the three causes \[for creation\] viz nimitta kāraṇam (efficient cause), upādāna kāraṇam (material cause) and sahakāri kāraṇam (instrumental/ancillary cause). Since āḻvār said “vittu” (seed), he is the upādāna kāranam. Since āḻvār says “tani” (singular), as said in **mahā upaniśat 1 “ēkō ha vai nārāyaṇa** (During creation only nārāyaṇa was there and there was no brahmā, no Siva, no water, no fire, no moon, no stars in the sky, no sun), he advitīya (there is no other nimitta kāraṇam). Alternatively, āḻvār is saying there is no one desiring to create us. Since āḻvār says “oru”, it implies that there is no other sahakāri kāraṇam.

**tānāgi** – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) being the cause for everything that is effected. In this manner emperumān performs samaśti sruśti and let brahmā perform vyaśti sruśti as  [emperumānār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/emperumanar/) appoints śrī madurakavi dāsar to take care of the śrīrangam temple activities.

**deyva nānmugan** – Four-headed brahmā who is one among the ātmās

**koḻu muḷai īnṟu** – It is said that [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who created brahma who has great abilities to perform the entire creation of the universe.

Since he cannot perform all three primary tasks viz sruśti (creation), stiti (sustenance) and samhāra (annihilation), for that,

**mukkaṇ īsanōdu dēvu pala īnṟu** – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) created rudra who is capable of performing samhāram and other dēvatās for various purposes.

When asked “what did he do to create all these dēvatās”?, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) replies,

**nudali** – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) vowed as said in cāndōjña upaniśat “bahusyām” (let me become many). Here “nudali” means “to consider”.

**mūvulagam vilaitta undi** – It is the [emperumān’s](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) divine navel which created the three worlds (lower, upper and middle). It is his divine navel which is the independent cause for the entire creation; the divine navel is like a lotus and is the origin for the entire leelā vibhūti (material realm).

**māyak kadavuḷ** – [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who is the supreme lord with amazing abilities.

**māmudal** – Also explained as [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who is the supreme primordial cause. Alternative explanation – the divine feet (māmudaladi) of the supreme lord who is the supreme cause and is with amazing abilities; or it can be explained as – the divine feet of the amazing lord, which are the apt goal.

**adiyē vaḻiyavenṟu yām toḻa isaiyum kol** – [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) out his desire asks “will I be fortunate to perform mangaḷāsāsanam to emperumān’s lotus divine feet?”, similar to how [periyāḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyAzhwAr/) performs mangaḷāsāsanam in [**tiruppallāndu – 1**](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2015/06/thiruppallandu-1-pallandu/) *“un cēvadi cevvit tirukkāppu* (long live! [emperumān’s](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) reddish lotus divine feet)“.

Let us see 5th pāsuram next.

## 5. Mamudal adi

*māmudal adip pōdu onṟu kaviḻttalartti, maṇ muḻudum agappaduttu, oṇ sudar adip pōdu onṟu viṇ seleei, nānmugap puttēḷ nādu viyanduvappa, vānavar muṟai muṟai vaḻipada neṟeei, tāmaraik kādu malark kaṇṇodu kani vāy udaiyadu māy, irunāyiṟu āyiram malarndanna , kaṟpagak kāvu paṟpalavanna , mudi tōḷ āyiram taḻaitta , nediyōykku alladum adiyadō ulagē*

**Пословный перевод:**

mā mudal – emperumān who is the supreme cause, your; adi – divine foot; onṟu pōdu – a (red lotus) flower; kaviḻttu alartti – overturned and spread out; maṇ muḻudum agappaduttu – captured the whole surface of the earth; oṇ – beautiful; sudar – shining; pōdu – like a flower; onṟu adi – the other divine foot; nānmugan puttēḷ – for the dēvatā brahmā; nādu viyandu uvappa – to the amazement and delight of the world; vānavar – and the dēvatās in that world; neṟeei – to walk in the right path; muṟai – ordain; muṟai – as said in śāstras; vaḻipada – to worship; viṇ – in the sky; seleei – sending upwards; tāmarai kādu – forest filled with lotus flowers; malar – blossoming; kaṇṇōdu – with divine eyes; kanivāi udaiyadumāy – having (reddish) divine lips like a fruit; iru – vast (with many rays); āyiram nāyiṟu – thousand suns; malarndanna – like rising; mudi paṟpala – many crowns; kaṟpagakkāvu anna – like a forest with kaṟpaga (celestial, wish-fulfilling) trees; taḻaitta – grown up tall; āyiram tōḷ – having thousand divine shoulders; nediyōykku alladum – for any one except emperumān who is supreme; ulagu – this world; adiyadō – is it subservient?

**Перевод:**

**mā mudal adi pōdu onṟu kaviḻttalartti – As said in irandām tiruvandādi – 61 “ninṟadōr pādam nilam pudaippa” – emperumān’s one divine foot covered the entire earth; and while measuring the world, the divine foot went all the way to āvaraṇa jalam (water surrounding the aṇḍam (oval shaped world)) and covered all under it – tiruneduntāndakam – 5 “oṇmidiyil punaluruvi orukāl niṟpa \[after making the soft step with one divine foot it went all the way to āvaraṇa jalam\]. People will say that the huge divine shoulders permeated and measured all the directions. \[anṟu karumāṇiyāy iranda kaḷvanē! unnaip piramāṇittār peṟṟa pēṟu\] You, the consort of śrī mahālakśmi, went about asking for alms, with happiness, without distinction between the time when people gave you and did not give you alms. Did you not hide your greatness when you took the form of vāmana having scars due to seeking alms!  You did not carry out this act only for indra. Was this act not done for the sake of all your followers who would meditate on your simplicity of lowering your esteem and carrying out their tasks so that they could sleep without a worry!**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*In the previous pāsuram āḻvār expressed his desire to perform mangaḷāśāsanam to emperumān’s divine feet as “māmudaladiyē vāḻiyavenṟu yām toḻa isaiyum kol”. Here, emperumān asks “what for are you performing mangaḷāśāsanam like this?”, āḻvār thinks of replying “even though your great favour (which is like a mother who takes care of her sleeping baby, i.e., the motherly forbearance) is not understood by anyone, you still created jīvātmās who were without a physical form \[at the time of deluge\], appointed dikpālakas (guardians for the eight directions) to protect the jīvātmas, and then appointed indra as the protector for the three lōkam to ensure that the strong persons do not persecute the meek persons. When indra lost his kingdom to the demonic mahābali and was in tears, being incapcitated, instead of being indifferent to his difficulties thinking “I created jīvātmas, and protected them by appointing dikpālakas; let them handle the situation”, you being the śrīya:pati (divine consort of śrī mahālakśmi who is the deity for all wealth) changed yourself as a mendicant, and begged on behalf of indra with the hands which had only been giving to others constantly. Thinking about how you accomplished the task for indra, who else can be offered mangaḷāśānam other than you?”. Since āḻvār had to tell about emperumān measuring the worlds with his divine feet while replying to emperumān, he speaks about that and enjoys the experience. Let us go through the pāsuram and its meanings:*

**mā mudal adip pōdu** – As said in previous pāsuram \[4th\] “*māyakkadavuḷ māmudaladiyē*” since [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) is the supreme primordial cause, it can be explained as the divine feet (māmudaladi) of the supreme lord. Alternatively, *māmudal* could be considered as an adjective for the divine feet themselves, and in this case the meaning could be construed as: since all his followers  attain his divine feet, the divine feet are the apt goal for the followers. *adipōtu* – divine foot which is like a lotus flower that had blossomed that day.

What did he do with the divine foot which was like a flower which blossomed on that day?

**kaviḻttalarti** – When emperumān spread his divine foot to pervade and measure all the places, the smaller entities when pressed under the larger ones could get trampled.

Did not the entities which were under the divine foot suffer?

The entities did not suffer in any way, similar to the situation when a lotus flower, that had blossomed that day, is inverted and its petals are spread out, the pollen inside the flower would have so much of space that they will not get trampled. Did not [tirumangai āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/thirumangai-azhwar/) say in [tiruneduntāndakam – 5](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2017/06/thirunedunthandakam-5/) “*oṇ midiyil”* (the step that did not trample on the entities under it)!

**maṇ muḻudum agapaduttu** – As said in [tiruneduntāndangam – 5](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2017/06/thirunedunthandakam-5/) “*punal aruvi* (it went all the way to āvaraṇa jalam)” his divine foot extended till the water surrounding the aṇḍams (āvaraṇa jalam).

If the planted foot did all these, what did the other divine foot, which was stretched, do?

**oṇsudar adi** – Starting with the verse mentioned in [tiruneduntāndangam – 5](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2017/06/thirunedunthandakam-5/) “*orukālum kāmaṟu seer avuṇan uḷḷattu eṇmadiyum kadandu aṇḍamīdupōgi iruvisumbinūdupōy eḻundu”* (the other foot, going past the thoughts of the great mahābali in his heart, rose to go over the aṇḍam, piercing the expansive sky…) and ending with the verse *“maṇmuḻdum* *agappaduttu*” (measuring all the places) it measured all the worlds above.

**oṇsudar adippōdu onṟu** – the divine foot which is like a flower that had blossomed on that day, and being very radiant. [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) terms the divine foot as being radiant because it removed the arrogance of all the dēvatās (celestial entities), each of whom, starting with indra (lord of all dēvatās) had ego equivalent to the sum of egos of all the people in samsāram (materialistic realm) and bringing them under it.

**viṇseleei** – pervaded the entire sky.

Till which place did it permeate?

**nānmuga puttēl nādu viyanduvappa** – The people in satyalōkam (abode of nānmugan or brahmā) were amazed at the divine foot which extended up to their abode. *viyandu uvappa* – they were amazed saying “we came in search of this abode where [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)‘s auspicious qualities of *saulabhyam* (easy to attain), *sauseelyam* (simplicity) etc would not reach but these qualities are flowing into this place! How wonderful it is this! It seems that there is no one who is not desired by this divine foot. When emperumān came to keep his divine foot on our head in order to establish our *svarūpam* (basic nature), we shunned it since we didn’t need it. But it appears that this divine foot will not leave us! We have got a benefit which we would have never obtained”. They were extremely delighted at this.

[tirumaḻisai āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/thirumazhisai-azhwar/) describes what brahmā, the head of satyalōkam, did then, in the 9th pāsuram in nānmugan tiruvandādi:

*kuṟaikoṇḍu nānmugan kuṇḍigai nīr peydu*  
*maṟaikoṇḍa mandirattāl vāḻtti kaṟaikoṇḍa*  
*kaṇḍattān senni mēlērak kaḻuvinān*  
*aṇḍattān seēvadiyai āngu*

[emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)‘s divine foot settled on brahmā’s hand when he hadn’t thought of that. Having been blessed to get this fortune, brahmā looked around in a perplexed way, wondering how to pay compliment to the divine foot. At the same time, the entity *darmam* (righteousness) looking at the divine foot melted like a liquid and entered brahmā’s kamaṇḍalam (a vessel for holding sanctified water). brahmā washed emperumān’s divine foot with that water. As soon as he saw this, Siva felt that this was the right moment to purify himself and put out his head under the flow of water, coming from emperumān’s divine foot. īsvara samhitai describes this as follows:

*dravībhūtas tadā darmōharibhaktyā mahāmunē*  
*gruhītvā darmapānīyam padam nātasya tuśtayē*  
*kśāḷitam parayā bhaktyā pādyārghyādirarcitam*  
*tadambu patitam drustvā dadāra śirasā hara:*  
*pāvanārtam jatāmadyē yōjñōsmītyavadārāṇāt*  
*varśāyutānyata bahūn na mumōca tatā hara:*

(Oh great sage! At that time (when bhagavān measured the worlds) darmam (righteousness) turned into liquid due to its devotion towards hari. With that darmam in the form of water, for the happiness of jagannātan (emperumān), (his) divine foot was washed (by me \[brahmā\]) with great devotion; it was vorśipped with *pādyam* and *arjñam* (water used for washing feet and hands respectively); looking at the water falling (from emperumān’s divine foot) Siva affirmed that he is qualified (to purify) himself and took the water in the centre of his matted hair to purify himself. After that, for several tens of thousands of years, rudra did not let go of that water). Thus, affirming that people who are lowly are also qualified for these divine feet, to get rid of the sin of having severed the head of his father \[brahmā\], rudra carried the śrīpādatīrtam (purified water from the divine feet of emperumān) on his head. When someone gets a good article, would he let go of it? In the same, way, since he obtained the impossible-to-get śrīpādatīrtam of emperumān’s divine foot, Siva held that water, which is river gangā, on his head for a very long time. Did not [periyāḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyazhwar/), in his periyāḻvār tirumoḻi 4-7-3 mercifully say *“catumukan kaiyil satuppuyan tāḷil sankaran sadaiyinil”* describing the history of gangā – initially at the hands of brahmā, then dwelt on the divine feet of the supreme being [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) and then fell on the head of rudran. The benefit which brahmā accrued out of this was mentioned in the 78th pāsuram of iraṇḍām tiruvandādi *“daraṇi nivandaḷappa nīttiya poṟpādam sivanda than kaiyanaittum ārak kaḻuvinān” –* to realise the benefit of getting eight hands, brahmā washed the divine foot of emperumān. The 9th pāsuram of nanmugan tiruvandādi describes the benefit that brahmā obtained – *(kuṟai koṇḍu*) – brahmā, displaying his emptiness (*ākincanyam*). The meaning implied is that even though brahmā has high levels of knowledge and strength, he thought “there is nothing that I have to honour the divine foot” *(kuṇḍigai nīr peydu maṟai koṇḍa mandirattāl vāḻtti)* – offering mangaḷāśāsanam (praising emperumān) through mantras (incantations or vēdic hymns) such as puruśa sūktam etc which are mentioned in vēdas (sacred texts) directly referring to emperumān, he poured the *darmajalam* (the deity for righteousness in the form of water) on the divine foot of emperumān. (*kaṟai koṇḍa kaṇḍattān senni mēlēṟak kaḻuvinān*) – Just as a father sprinkles sanctified water on his mischievous child to purify him, thinking “being without the power to distinguish \[between right and wrong\] he is carrying out wrongful deeds without realising their effects. Henceforth at least, let not inauspicious incidents come his way” \[reference here is to the act of Siva severing brahmā’s head and suffering due to that\], brahmā let the śrīpādatīrtam fall on his son rudra’s matted hair.

What did the other deities in various lōkams (worlds in the upper reaches) do when brahmā was carrying out this act?

**vānavar muṟai muṟai vaḻipada nereei** – they vorśipped [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) together, in the proper ways mentioned in śāstras. It is implied here that just as people worship the divine feet of periya perumāḷ in śrīrangam by gathering near the tiruvaṇukkan divine entrance near the tirumaṇattūṇ (two divine pillars) inside śrīrangam temple, the dēvatās also vorśipped emperumān as per the [tiruvārādanam](http://ponnadi.blogspot.in/2012/07/srivaishnava-thiruvaaraadhanam.html) way (method of vorśipping emperumān) mentioned in śāstras.

[āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) explains what emperumān did when the dēvatās vorśipped him.

**tāmarai kādu malarkkaṇṇōdu kanivāi udaiyadumāi** – emperumān’s divine eyes will blossom when someone, whoever he may be, places his head on his divine feet, just as a lotus will blossom on seeing the sun. Thus, even if they are arrogant, when the dēvatās vorśipped \[his divine feet\], emperumān’s eyes blossomed like a forest of lotus.

**iru nāyiru āyiram malarndanna** – just as a thousand suns blossomed with their great rays. Unlike the sun which we see in this world, a thousand suns, with all their greatness mentioned earlier, rose together, with their thousands of rays.

**kaṟpagakkāvu paṟpalavanna** – like many orchards of kaṟpaga trees (a celestial wish-fulfilling tree)

What was like this?

**mudi tōḷ āyiram taḻaitta** – The crown of the [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) looked like  thousand suns which rose at once. The thousands of divine shoulders looked like kaṟpaga orchards.

**nediyōykku** – one who has long memory, thinking “I haven’t done anything for him \[his follower\]; what can I do further for him?” Even after changing himself into a mendicant and taking alms for the sake of indra and accomplishing his task, he feels sorry for the dēvatās just like a person robs money from another who had trusted him, thinking “I have not protected him \[indra\] before he was troubled by mahābali. I have lost my sense of righteousness as I have delayed”. The term *nediyōn* refers to one who, feeling ashamed for not having done anything and feeling his shortcoming, thinks about it for a very long time.

**nediyōykkallatum adiyatōvulagē** – [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) is asking “was this world under the feet of someone else, to offer that deity mangaḷāśāsanam (praise) , other than [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who protects everyone? Which is correct – offering worship to you who kept your divine foot on those deities who are trying to convince other people to fall at their feet and worship them or to those other deities who were trampled under your divine foot?” Here the term *nediyōn* refers to trivikrama, the greatest among all.

Let’s see the 6th pāsuram next.

## 6. Oh oh ulaginadiyalve

*ōh ōh ulaginadiyalvē, īnṟōḷirukka maṇai nīrāttip, padaittu idandu uṇḍu umiḻndu aḷandu, tērndu ulagaḷikkum mudaṟperum kadavuḷ niṟpap, pudaippala tānaṟi deyvam pēṇudal, tanādu pullaṟivāṇmai porundakkāttik, kolvana mudalā allana muyalum, inaiya seygai inbu tunbu aḷit tonmā māyap piṟaviyuḷ nīngāp panmā māyattu aḻundumā naḷirndē*

**Пословный перевод:**

ulagu – this earth; padaittu – (in the beginning) creating; idandu – (during varāha avatāram) digging out (from the walls of the universe); uṇḍu – (during deluge) eating it; umiḻndu – (at the time of intervening creation) spitting out; aḷandu – (at the time of trivikrama avatāram) measuring; tērndu – deeply thinking (about its protection); aḷikkum – protecting; mudal perum kadavuḷ – the primary cause and supreme lord, śrīman nārāyaṇan; niṟpa – while he remains (as the refuge); pudai – (considered as his) wealth; pala – of different types; tān aṟi deyvam – (a few) dēvatās (deities) known to him \[jīvātmā\] (as refuge); pēṇudal – supporting them (as protectors); tanādu pul aṟivu – his lowly intelligence; āṇmai porundakkātti – showing in such a way that it enters (the minds of) learned people; īnṟōḷ irukka – when one’s mother is there; maṇai nīrātti – bathing a wooden plank (which is an insentient entity); seygai – the activities of those dēvatās; kolvana mudalā – harmful acts; allana muyalum – carrying out banned activities; inaiya – having qualities like these; aḷi – the strength (given by those dēvatās); tunbu – together with sorrow; inbu – joyful; tol – from time immemorial; mā – being huge; māyam – amazing; piṟaviyuḷ – in this samsāram (materialistic realm); nīngā – instead of leaving; pal mā – being variegated; māyattu – worldly pursuits such as śabdam etc (sound, touch etc) (lustful); naḷirndu – very well; aḻundumā – being fully sunk; ulaginadu iyalvu – nature of this world; Oh Oh – how is it!

**Перевод:**

**Oh Oh – āḻvār goes to those places inhabited by samsāris and calls them out in a loud voice so that they could hear him.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*lakśminātan, the consort of śrī mahālakśmi, after creating the jīvātmās \[making them realise their existence\] and giving them physical bodies and sensory perceptions, instead of remaining “Let them go whichever way they want to”, intervened when a mighty person such as mahābali troubled a weakling such as indra by snatching his kingdom, just as an eagle would snatch a chick. He converted himself into a mendicant, without bothering about his greatness, and protected indra. In the previous pāsuram, āḻvār had said ” it will be apt for jīvātmās, who have knowledge, to meditate on this protective act of emperumān and carry out mangaḷāśasanam (act of praising emperumān)”. After that āḻvār looked at the samsāris (dwellers of materialistic realm) to see whether they, being knowledgeable, are indeed praising emperumān as requested by him. But he felt saddened seeing that the samsāris are making efforts to give up on emperumān, liking anyone other than him, being subservient to their egos and their possessions and going to dēvatās (other than emperumān) for protection. Just as it is said in the pramāṇam “ēkasminnapyatikrāntē muhūrtē dyānavarjitē I  dasyubhir muśitēnēva yuktamākranditum bhruśam īī” (even if a time span of one muhūrtam \[48 minutes\] is spent without meditating on bhagavān, one should cry out loudly just like one would if a robbery were committed by thieves), one should recite the divine names of emperumān if there is a hurdle to meditating on him, as an atonement, and cry out loud as if a great wealth had been stolen, thinking of the hurdle. But āḻvār, looking at the samsāris who did not even think of crying out for their loss, unable to tolerate such behaviour \[of samsāris\] cries out “Oh Oh” in this pāsuram. Let us go through the pāsuram and its meanings:*

**ulagiandiyalvē** – āḻvār feels sad that [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) has created this samsāram with people who are full of hatred towards him, just as in nityavibhūti, the entities present are enjoying him without any hurdles. He says that it is really sad that world is full of people with such nature.

When asked “What did this world do that you have to cry out like this?” he says…

**īnṟōḷ irukka maṇai nīrātti** – their activities are such that they seem similar to a person paying compliments to a plank of wood by giving it a bath etc when the mother who had given birth to him is alive. A mother is one who observes penance before conceiving him, holds him in her womb for ten months, putting up with the pains of labour, nursing and caressing him when he lies on the floor as an infant and protecting him even at the cost of her health. When the child grows up and gains knowledge, being able to recall all the help that she had rendered to him, leaving her aside, he starts giving a bath to a plank of wood which cannot even think of helping him in any way and which cannot appreciate the compliments that he is paying it. In a similar way, when [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who is related to the [cētanas](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) (sentient entities) in all ways, is very much there, these people are praising other dēvatās (deities) who are not related in any way, feels the [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/), sorrowfully.

When asked, “Who did you assume to be like a mother?”

**padaittu idandu uṇḍu umiḻndu aḷandu tērndu ulagu aḷikkum mudaṟ perum kadavuḷ niṟpa** – it is the unique supreme being \[emperumān\] who creates the cētanas after seeing their condition of having integrated themselves with the mūlaprakruti (primordial matter) which is in itself an acētana (insentient) entity; since the cētanas are together with mūlaprakruti during the period of deluge, emperumān takes pity on the cētanas and creates them and when the world gets submerged he takes the incarnation of a boar which can enter water and muddy areas and digs out the earth; when deluge comes again, he takes the earth inside his stomach, keeping it safely; spits it out during the time of creation; when mahābali siezes it \[from indra\], he redeems all the worlds establishing that he is the possessor of all these; despite carrying out all these protective activities emperumān feels saddened that he has not done anything for their welfare, just as a person who steals money from another person who has trusted him and slept next to him would feel sad; he looks for more avenues to protect them, and while protecting them he does not differentiate between great and lowly people; he is the supreme entity who protects everyone. In śrī rāmāyaṇam ayōdyā kāṇḍam 59-3 sumantran says *“guhēna sārtdam tatraiva stitō’smi divasān bahūn* I *āsayā yadi vā rāma puna: śabdāpayēditi* īī” (I stayed with guha for several days in that place in the hope that śrī rāma would call me again). Similar to that state of sumantran, emperumān also waits expectantly, to see if anyone would come to attain him. [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) feels saddened that even if he had not carried out any of these benefits for cētanas, looking at his supreme nature, one would be sure that he has none equalling him. Still, even after he had done all these benefits, the cētanas are not taking efforts to attain him. [periyavāccān piḷḷai](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/periyavachchan-pillai/), who has written the commentary for this prabandam, states the following aidihyam (a traditional happening which is mentioned down the generations) to state the huge difference between emperumān and other dēvatās:

A king by name yayāti, had observed many means to sit on the same seat as indra in svargam (heaven). indra then asked yayāti “When you were the King, in your *karmabhūmi* (earth), who had carried out most puṇya (virtuous acts)?”. yayāti, thinking that he should not tell a lie in the presence of a dēvatā, said “When I was ruling, people would say that I had carried out most puṇya”. Immediately indra cursed him saying “you praised yourself, Hence you shall fall from this world to the earth”. This story is mentioned in mahābhāratam. When [parāśara bhattar](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/parasara-bhattar/) (son of [śrī kūrattāḻvān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/kuraththazhwan/)) was recounting this incident, some people asked him “for explaining which meaning of vēdam did the riśi (sage vyāsa) write this incident?” bhattar replied saying “riśi wrote this only to explain the truth that it  is only [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) who will give a status equal to himself to his followers; it is only he who will tolerate such equal position for his followers. Hence we should attain only emperumān. It is rare for other deities to give an equal status to those who attain them; even if they give, they cannot tolerate that for long ”

Even if [cētanas](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) do not attain him, do they attain, in the least, someone who is somewhat equal to him and not someone too lowly?

**pudaippala tānaṟi deyvam** – in śāstras (sacred texts), in places where the great wealth of emperumān is discussed, in order to explain his greatness, certain qualities are attributed to his possessions \[possessions include both sentient and insentient entities\], which are not present in them. Without knowing these , taking the sentences in the text as authentic, these cētanas consider cētanas who are like themselves, as deities. Even among these, one deity does not have the capability of granting all the wishes of the cētana. It is not like the unique [paramātmā](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/12/thathva-thrayam-iswara-who-is-god/) (supreme being) who grants all the puruśārtams (benefits). As said in brahmāṇḍa purāṇam *“ārōjñam bhāskarādiccēt danamiccēt hutāśanāt I īśvarāt jñānamiccēt gatimiccēt janārdanāt*” (he can desire health from sun; he can desire wealth from agni (deity for fire); he can desire knowledge from īśvaran \[rudra\]; he can desire mōkśam (liberation) from janārdana), if one desires mōkśam, he has to attain the supreme being only. Among other deities, if one desires wealth, he has to attain one deity; if he desires a son, he has to attain another deity; if he desires cattle, he has to attain some other deity, thus attaining several deities for getting each benefit.

**tān aṟi deyvam** – the deity that he (cētana) has attained is not the one mentioned in authentic texts as supreme entity. This deity, like him, is full of rajō guṇam (passion related qualities) and tamō guṇam (ignorance related qualities).

**pēṇudal** – since these deities do not have any greatness in them by nature, it becomes the responsibility of the cētana to get them titles and qualities of īśvaran. This is similar to a poet singing praises on a bald headed person about his hair as *“paniyirum* *kuḻalan*” (he has cool and well spread out hair)!

**tanādu … kātti** – these deities do not become fit to be attained just because the cētana says so. One cannot even say that he has obtained some benefits from them. The only benefit that he has got by attaining them is that he has exposed his ignorance to knowledgeable people. **tanādu pullaṟivu āṇmai porundak kātti** – he has publicised his low level of knowledge to the knowledgeable persons. The real benefit that one should get is only mōkśam (śrīvaikuṇṭam) and that should be obtained only through [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/). bruhadāraṇyaka upaniśat 6-5-6 says *“ātmāvā arē draśtavya: śrōtavya: mantavyō nididyāsitavya:”* (the ātmā which is apt to be heard and thought of, is to be meditated upon until it is seen) \[here ātmā refers to paramātmā, the supreme entity\]. puruśasūktam says *“vedāhamētam puruśam mahāntam āditya varṇam tamasa: parastāt”* (vēdapuruśa says that he knows the supreme entity and that he is beyond the world of tamas and is radiant like the sun). As per these pramāṇams, the entity responsible for the creation of the universes has to be attained only through worship. But the \[other\] deities vorśipped by the cētana are not like that.

[āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) explains their qualities now.

**kolvana mudalā allana muyalum inaiya seygai** – there are deities which demand “get me the meat of goat; get me the meat of your son” etc, thus desiring killing of other [jīvātmās](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/). There are other deities which desire lowly materials such as alcohol, meat etc. Their activities are like these! Attaining such deities is similar to attaining *narakam* (hell).

āḻvār further says that the benefits that one would obtain through attaining these deities would be such that it would be better not to obtain any benefit at all.

**inbu tunbu aḷi** – these deities give benefits wherein joy is mixed with sorrow. They are not capable of granting mōkśam which is pure joy without any sorrow.

Thus, if one were to ask as to what benefit the cētana gets in attaining such deities, the only benefit of attaining such deities who are also [cētanas](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) like himself, who also help in nurturing samsāram and who have been in the quagmire of samsāram from time immemorial, is that he continues to remain in samsāram.

**tonmā māyap piṟaviyuḷ nīngāp pan māmāyattu aḻundumā naḷirnde** – mūlaprakruti (primordial matter) which has been existing since time immemorial and which is responsible for samsāram, is referred to as *māmāyam*. śrī bhagavat gītā 7-14 says *“daivī hyēśā guṇamayī mama māyā duratyayā “* (it is difficult for one to cross the mūlaprakruti which has been created by me and which is composed of three qualities). However hard one tries to get out of, one sinks deeper and deeper into repeated births in this samsāram, due to the connection that he has with physical body which is a dimension of mūlaprakruti which has amazing power, leading to śabda, sparśa, rūpa, rasa, ganda etc (the five sensory perceptions of sound, touch, form, taste and smell). The word *māyam* refers to any object which has amazing nature. Here, the first *māmayam* refers to the amazing prakruti and the second *māmayam* refers to the five sensory perceptions of sound etc.

**ulaginadiyalvē** – [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) says that instead of obtaining eternal joy in keeping with their svarūpam (nature), the [jīvātmās](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/) in this world are attaining deities which are contrary to their \[jīvātmās’\] nature and are trying to obtain meaningless benefits! What an utter loss is this for the ātmā!

We shall take up the last pāsuram in this prabandam, next.

adiyēn kriṣṇa rāmānuja dāsan

## 7. Nalir madiccadaiyanum

*naḷirmadic cadaiyanum nānmugak kadavuḷum, taḷir oḷi imaiyavar talaivanum mudalā, yāvagai ulagamum yāvarum agappada, nilam nīr tī kāl sudar iruvisumbum, malar sudar piṟavum siṟidudan mayanga, oru poruḷ puṟappādinṟi muḻuvadum agappadak karandu ōrālilai sērnda em perumā māyanai alladu, oru mā deyvam maṟṟu udaiyamō yāmē*

**Пословный перевод:**

naḷir madi sadaiyanum – rudra who has donned the cool chandra (moon) on his head; nānmugak kadavuḷum – brahmā, with four heads; taḷir oḷi – having (beautiful) radiance like a tender shoot; imaiyavar talaivanum mudalā – indra, the head of celestial entities et al; yāvagai ulagamum – all types of worlds; yāvarum agappada – including all the sentient entities; nilam – earth; nīr – water; tī – fire; kāl – wind; sudar – due to radiance; iru – pervaded; visumbum – sky also; malar sudar – sun and moon, having bright rays; piṟavum – all other entities; udan – at the same time; siṟidu – on one side (inside the stomach); mayanga – being together; oru poruḷ – even one entity; puṟappādu inṟi – being unable to go out; muḻuvadum – all; agappada – being inside; karandu – eating and hiding (inside the stomach); ōr ālilai – on a banyan leaf; sērnda – sleeping; em – my lord; peru – being great; mā – unable to measure; māyanai alladu – other than śrīman nārāyaṇa, who has amazing power; maṟṟu – anyone else; mā – great; oru deyvam – a deity; yām – we; udaiyamō – do we have (as fit for attaining)?

**Перевод:**

**naḷir madiccdaiyanum – rudra, despite having matted hair to show that he is carrying out penance towards someone, due to his vanity that he is īśvaran, keeps the cool candran (moon) on his head to show that he is in deep joy, just like people will don the fragrant pine leaf on their heads.**

**Комментарий. Автор Periyavāccān Piḷḷai:**

*In the previous pāsuram, āḻvār said “while it is apt for all jīvātmās to praise emperumān, they are attaining other deities and continuing in samsāram. What a huge loss is this to their ātmā! I am not able to tolerate this meaningless activity”. He brings the prabandam to an end in this pāsuram by happily saying “Let the others do whatever they want. It is good that we escaped from this meaninglessness”. He completes the prabandam by saying “while the people of this world fall the feet of all and sundry and pass their time, it is nice to know that we are having no connection with other deities. īsn’t this benefit sufficient? ” āḻvār feels happy that “emperumān does  not look at whether a person is great or lowly. Without making any distinction, he keeps everyone inside his stomach. Is it not enough that I am without the lowliness of attaining one of those who enter his stomach and look up to them for gaining benefits!”. Since there is no further happiness for him, he brings this prabandam to an end, with the satisfaction derived, without completing the full andādi style (the last word of current pāsuram becomes the first word of next pāsuram and so on till the end, where the last word of the last pāsuram becomes the first word of first pāsuram) unlike his other prabandams such as tiruviruttam, periya tiruvandādi and tiruvāimoḻi. The opinion of this pāsuram is that emperumān swallowed all sentient and insentient entities, starting with “naḷirmadic cadaiyanum” Is there any benefit in attaining one of the jīvātmās like oneself, who has entered his stomach and seeking benefits from them? Let us look at the pāsuram and its meanings:*

**nānmugakkadavuḷum** – the deity nānmugan \[brahmā\] who created that rudra too, and who has four faces in order to create entities in the four directions.  āren’t these two entities involved in creation and destruction, respectively, of individual entities within the universe? Of these two, brahmā is like the potter who creates pots and rudra is like the stick which removes the excess mud and gives shape to the pot.

**taḷir oḷi imaiyavar talaivanum mudalā yāvagai ulagamum yāvarum agappada** – starting with indra,  the lord of celestial beings in svargam (heaven) who, due to his involvement in the pleasures of heaven, enjoys the beautiful forms of apsaras (celestial women) and in keeping with that, looks after his own body such that it has radiance; including all the entities in various worlds and all the worlds, without leaving anything or anyone

**nilam nīr tī kāl sudar iruvisumbum** – five elements which are the cause for the existence of all the entities mentioned above within the universe, namely bhūmi (earth), jalam (water), agni (fire), vāyu (wind) and ākāśam (the one with special feature, sky). Among these five, sky is the cause for the other four elements when they are formed; when they are annihilated (during deluge) it remains after the other four have been destroyed and is the last one to be destroyed; keeping these features in consideration, [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) qualifies sky with the term *iru sudar visumbu* (the great radiant sky)

**malar sudar piṟavum** – sun and moon, which have bright, huge number of rays, and other entities such as human beings

**siṟidudan mayanga** – to be within the same space inside \[[emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)’s\] stomach, at the same time. After food is cooked for serving some people, if larger number of people come suddenly, the quantum of food is reduced appropriately to serve everyone. In the same way, while emperumān was waiting impatiently to protect all the entities, the food (all entities) suddenly became too small when compared to his huge expectation. Thus, the entire food was just sufficient to fill a small space inside his stomach. The term *udan* refers to simultaneous time. The term *siṟidu mayanga* refers to the small space in which all the entities were contained since they were unable to compete with his huge expectation. Alternatively, the term *udan mayanga* could be split as *udal mayanga.*  In this case, the meaning will change to: the entities mixed within the small frame which was lying on the banyan leaf \[refers to emperumān’s small form when he slept on the banyan leaf after swallowing all the entities and protecting them in his stomach, during deluge\]. m*ayanga* would refer to mixing of the entities inside emperumān’s stomach.

**oru poruḷ puṟappādinṟi muḻuvadum agappada** – all the entities, without letting any entity to go out

**karandu** – when someone in the world eats a lot, his stomach will show out, by bulging. In his ([emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/)’s) case, since all the entities were hidden in a corner of his stomach, it appeared as if he could show that despite eating all the entities, his stomach actually reduced a bit; in other words, there was no change in his appearance (*nirvikāram*).

**ōr ālilaic cērnda em perumā māyanai alladu** – other than emperumān who has the *agaditagatanā śakti* (ability to carry out deeds which are impossible for the others), as he lies on a little banyan leaf which had just sprouted, after swallowing all the worlds. The [69](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2017/08/mudhal-thiruvandhadhi-69/)[<sup>th</sup>](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2017/08/mudhal-thiruvandhadhi-69/) [pāsuram of mudal tiruvandādi](https://divyaprabandham.koyil.org/index.php/2017/08/mudhal-thiruvandhadhi-69/) which explains this behaviour of emperumān is explained now:

\[*bālan tanadu uruvāy ēḻulagu uṇḍu\]:* In the form of a small child as mentioned in the pramāṇam “*yaśōdāstanandayamatyantam vimuktam*” (yaśōdā’s very beautiful infant), keeping all the worlds in the stomach

\[*ālilaiyin mēlanṟu nī vaḷranda meyyenbar*\]: as mentioned in periya tirumoḻi 2-10-1 “*ālilaiyin mēl ōr iḷam taḷiril kaṇ valarnda īśan*” – on top of a just sprouting banyan leaf, with no one like yaśōdā, who could keep you on her lap with motherly affection, not being present there, looking at the way you were lying, riśis (sages) who write only facts, wrote that it is true, in itihāsam (epics) and purāṇams (ancient sacred texts). “*katam nvayam śiśu: śētē lōkē nāśamupāgatē* I *śākāyām vatavrukśasya pallavē tu śucismita:* īī” is the pāsuram that they wrote, after experiencing your activity, in mahābhāratam

\[*ālanṟu vēlai nīr uḷḷadō?*\]: was that banyan leaf inside the water that had dissolved the soil (earth) and submerged it?

\[*viṇṇadō?*\]: was it in the sky which had no support?

\[*maṇṇadō?*\]: or was it on the earth which had lost its ‘effect’ status and was hidden in the ‘causative’ status?

\[*sōlai sūḻ kunṟu eduttāy sollu*\]: Only you, who had held the gōvardana hill for several days with your hands, in the same position, at the age of seven, even before completing your status as a child, have to tell! īsn’t this also an amazing activity just like swallowing the world as a child! This incident should be quoted as well as that. Swallowing the worlds in the form of an infant, lying on an unsupported banyan leaf, holding a mountain for several days as a child – these are amazing activities which could not have been done by anyone else in the world. The implication here is that only he has to answer as to how he performed these activities. When parāśara bhattar was asked “what was his response to [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/)”, he said “Since āḻvār had asked the question to [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) knowing fully well the answer,  emperumān would also have thought that he would ask āḻvār himself to give the answer, if āḻvār compels him for an answer”.  periyavāccān piḷḷai (the commentator for this prabandam) would quote bhattar saying “while you are the support for all entities, leaving you aside, would those entities support one another? āḻvār feels amazed that  even during the time of deluge, he was the support for all the worlds that he swallowed as well as the banyan leaf on which he lay down”.

āḻvār describes such an amazing entity as *perumā māyan* . . . .

**perumā māyanai alladu oru mā deyvam maṟṟu udaiyamō yāmē** – are we people who have assumed as Lord, apart from [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/), those deities who rule small portions of the world? Those other deities would tell their followers that they would protect them, accept their offerings and after some days pass, would tell them that “I too am like you all, carrying out penance towards one entity” and leave them. Thus he doesn’t have a deity who he can accept and reject later on. The quality of those deities could be seen in the life of mārkaṇḍēya maharishi. mārkaṇḍēya was a follower of Siva, who told him “I will protect you” and was accepting the offerings given by him. When markaṇḍēya got fed up  with the life on earth and desired mōkśam, Siva, showing his form with matted hair, told him “I too am carrying out penance like you, focussing on [śrīman nārāyaṇa](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/); I am also holding on to my Lord. It appears that you got bewildered when some people told you that I am the supreme being, taking it to be true. Wait a while; I will show you the place that you have to attain” and showed mārkaṇḍēya śrīman nārāyaṇa. [nammāḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) himself has clearly shown this incident in his tiruvāimoḻi 7-5-7 “*kaṇḍum teḷindum kaṟṟār*”

In mahābhāratam śānti parvam 350-36 it is clearly stated that other deities will not be attained by knowledgeable persons  “*brahmāṇam neela (śiti)kaṇṭam ca yāścānyā dēvatā: smrutā:* I *pratibhuddā na sēvantē yasmāt parimitam palam* īī  ” (jñānis (knowledgeable persons) will not worship brahmā, rudra and other dēvatās; this is because there is a limit to the benefits that they can give).  In other words, knowledgeable people will not worship these dēvatās since they do not have the capability to grant mōkśam if the followers desire that from such dēvatās. These dēvatās can only grant prākruta (matter relating to samsāram) benefits which are seen in samsāram (materialistic realm). Such benefits will be lowly and temporary. These benefits are such that they can be given by these knowledgeable people to those dēvatās. “*skandarudramahēndrādyā: pratiśiddāstu pūjanē* I  *jñātvaivam bhaktisānkaryam na kuryā dēvamēvahi* īī” (In the matter of worship dēvatās such as skanda, rudra, indra et al have been kept aside by jñānis (knowledgeable persons). Knowing this, let him \[[cētana](https://granthams.koyil.org/2013/03/06/thathva-thrayam-chith-who-am-i/)\] not mix up when he carries out bhakti (devotion)). This is the reason śāstras forbade people from vorśipping such dēvatās. Did not tirumaḻisai [āḻvār](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/nammazhwar/) too mercifully define vaiṣṇava lakśaṇam (the identification for vaiṣṇavas) in his nānmugan tiruvandādi 68 “*maṟandum puṟam toḻā māndar*” (even by mistake, people will not worship other dēvatās). For a wife who has a difference of opinion with her husband, her status as wife will remain so long as she does not go to another man. In the same way, even if the involvement with [emperumān](http://acharyas.koyil.org/index.php/sriman-narayanan/) takes time to set in, it does not matter. It is more important for him not go have any connection with other dēvatās. If this *puṟam toḻāmai* (not vorśipping others) is followed, even for a person who has forgotten emperumān, the qualification to become his servitor will be present. Only to emphasise this, āḻvār brings this prabandam to an end by saying “oru mā deyvam maṟṟu udaiyōmōyāmē” (do we have another deity to attain?).
