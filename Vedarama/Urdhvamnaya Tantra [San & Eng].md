# Urdhvamnaya Tantra \[San & Eng\]

## Śrī Śrī Rādhā-kṛpā-katākṣa-stava-rāja

**(An Appeal to Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī from the Urdhvāmnāya-tantra)**

**Composed by Lord Śiva**

**Translated by Kuśakratha dāsa**

### Text 1

*munīndra-vṛnda-vandite triloka-śoka-hāriṇi*  
*prasanna-vaktra-paṅkaje nikuñja-bhū-vilāsini*  
*vrajendra-bhānu-nandini vrajendra-sūnu-saṅgate*  
*kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-katākṣa-bhājanam*

**Пословный перевод:**

*muni*—of sages; *indra*—of kings; *vṛnda*—by the assembly; *vandite*—worshiped; *tri*—three; *loka*—of the worlds; *śoka*—the distress; *hāriṇī*—removing; *prasanna*—bright; *vaktra*—face; *paṅkaje*—lotus flower; *nikuñja-bhū*—in the groves of Vṛndāvana; *vilāsini*—performing pastimes; *vraja*—of Vraja; *indra*—of the king; *bhānu*—of Vṛśabhānu; *nandini*—the daughter; *vraja*—of Vraja; *indra*—of the king; *sūnu*—of the Son (Kṛṣṇa); *saṅgate*—associated; *kadā*—when?; *kariṣyasi*—will You make; *iha*—here; *mām*—me; *kṛpā—*of mercy; *kaṭākṣa*—of the glance; *bhājanam*—the recipient.

**Перевод:**

**O goddess worshiped by the kings of sages, O goddess who remove the sufferings of the three worlds, O goddess whose face is a blossoming lotus, O goddess who enjoy pastimes in the forest, O daughter of Vṛṣabhānu, O companion of Vraja’s prince, when will You cast Your merciful sidelong glance upon me?**

### Text 2

*aśoka-vṛkṣa-vallarī-vitāna-maṇḍapa-sthite*  
*pravāla-vāla-pallava-prabhārunāṅghri-komale*  
*varābhaya-spurat-kare prabhūta-sampadālaye*  
*kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-katākṣa-bhājanam*

**Пословный перевод:**

*aśoka*—*aśoka; vṛkṣa*—tree; *vallarī*—creeper; *vitāna*—spreading; *mandapa*—pavilion; *sthite*—situated; *pravāla-vāla-pallava*—excellent blossoming flowers; *prabhā*—with the splendor; *aruṇa*—reddish; *aṅghri*—feet; *komale*—delicate; *vara*—excellent; *abhaya*—fearlessness; *sphurat*—manifesting; *kare*—hand; *prabhūta*—abundant; *sampadā*—of opulence; *ālaye*—abode; *kadā*—when?; *kariṣyasi*—will You make; *iha*—here; *mām*—me; *kṛpā—*of mercy; *kaṭākṣa*—of the glance; *bhājanam*—the recipient.

**Перевод:**

**O goddess staying in a vine-cottage by an** ***aśoka*** **tree, O goddess whose delicate feet are as splendid as red blossoms, O goddess whose hand grants fearlessness, O abode of transcendetal opulences, when will You cast Your merciful sidelong glance upon me?**

### Text 3

*anaṅga-raṅga-maṅgala-prasaṅga-bhaṅgura-bhruvāṁ*  
*sa-vibhramaṁ sa-saṁbhramaṁ dṛg-anta-bāṇa-pātanaiḥ*  
*nirantaraṁ vaśī-kṛta-pratīti-nandanandane*  
*kadā kariṣyasīha māṁ kṛpā-katākṣa-bhājanam*

**Пословный перевод:**

*anaṅga*—of cupid; *raṅga*—in the pleasure; *maṅgala*—auspiciousness; *prasaṅga*—contact; *bhaṅgura*—bending; *bhruvāṁ*—of the eyebrows; *sa*—with; *vibhramam*—amorous playfulness; *sa*—with; *saṁbhramam*—movements; *dṛk*—of the eyes; *anta*—from the corners; *bāṇa*—of the arrows; *pātanaiḥ*—with the falling; *nirantaraṁ*—constantly; *vaśī-kṛta*—subdued; *pratīti*—conviction; *nanda-nandane*—of Lord Kṛṣṇa, the Son of Nanda Maharaja; *kadā*—when?; *kariṣyasi*—will You make; *iha*—here; *mām—*me; *kṛpā—*of mercy; *kaṭākṣa*—of the glance; *bhājanam*—the recipient.

**Перевод:**

**O goddess who, playfully shooting the arrows of Your glances from the curved bows of Your auspicious, amorous eyebrows, have completely subdued Nanda’s son \[Kṛṣṇa\], when will You cast Your merciful sidelong glance upon me?**

### Text 4

*taḍit-suvarṇa-campaka-pradīpta-gaura-vigrahe*

**Перевод:**

**O goddess whose form is as splendid as** ***campaka*** **flowers, gold, and lightning, O goddess whose face eclipses millions of autumn moons, O goddess whose eyes are wonderful, restless young** ***cakora*** **birds, when will You cast Your merciful sidelong glance upon me?**

### Text 5

*madonmadāti-yauvane pramoda-māna-maṇḍite*  
*priyānurāga-rañjite kalā-vilāsa-paṇḍite*  
*ananya-dhanya-kuñja-rājya-kāma-keli-kovide*

**Пословный перевод:**

**Перевод:**

**O young girl intoxicated with passion, O goddess decorated with cheerful jealous anger, O goddess who passionately love Your beloved Kṛṣṇa, O goddess learned in playful arts, O goddess expert at enjoying amorous pastimes in the kingdom of the peerlessly opulent forest groves of Vṛndāvana, when will You cast Your merciful sidelong glance upon me?**

### Text 6

*aśeṣa-hāva-bhāva-dhīra-hīra-hāra-bhūṣite*  
*prabhūta-śātakumbha-kumbha-kumbhi kumbha-sustani*  
*praśasta-manda-hāsya-cūrṇa-pūrṇa-saukya-sāgare*

**Пословный перевод:**

*aśeṣa*—complete; *hāva*—amorous gestures; *bhāva*—state of being; *dhīra*—brave; *hīra*—of pearls; *hāra*—with a necklace; *bhūṣite*—decorated; *prabhūta*—great; *śātakumbha*—of gold; *kumbha-kumbhi*—pitcher; *kumbha*—like pitchers; *su*—beautiful; *stani*—breasts; *praśasta*—celebrated; *maṇda*—gentle; *hāsya*—of the smile; *cūrṇa*—aromatic powders; *pūrṇa*—full; *saukhya*—of happiness; *sāgare*—ocean; *kadā*—when?; *kariṣyasi*—will You make; *iha*—here; *mām—*me; *kṛpā—*of mercy; *kaṭākṣa*—of the glance; *bhājanam*—the recipient.

**Перевод:**

**O goddess decorated with a pearl neclace of bold amorous hints, O goddess as fair as gold, O goddess whose breasts are great golden waterpots, O ocean of happiness filled with the scented powders of gentle smiles, when will You cast Your merciful sidelong glance upon me?**

### Text 7

*mṛnāla-vāla-vallarī-taraṅga-raṅga-dor-late*  
*latāgra-lāsya-lola-nīla-locanāvalokane*

**Перевод:**

**O goddess whose arms are lotus stalks dancing on the waves, O goddess whose dark eyes are dancing vines, O playful, beautiful, charming goddess, when will You cast Your merciful sidelong glance upon me?**

### Text 8

*suvarṇa-mālikāñcita-trirekha-kambu-kaṇṭhage*

### Text 13

*itī mamādbhutaṁ stavaṁ niśamya bhānu-nandinī*  
*karotu santataṁ janaṁ kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-bhājanam*  
*bhavet tadaiva sañcita-trirūpa-karma-nāśanaṁ*  
*bhavet tadā vrajendra-sūnu-maṇḍala-praveśanam*

**Перевод:**

**Upon hearing this most astonishing prayer of mine being recited by a devotee, may Śrī Vṛṣabhānu-nandinī constantly make him the object of Her most merciful sidelong gance. At that time all his karmic reactions — whether mature, fructifying, or lying in seed — will be completely destroyed, and then he will gain entrance into the assembly of Nandanandana's eternal loving associates.**

### Text 14-15

*rākāyāṁ ca sitāṣṭamyāṁ daśamyāṁ ca viśuddha-dhiḥ*  
*ekādaśyāṁ trayodaśyāṁ yaḥ paṭhet sādhakaḥ su-dhiḥ*  
*yaṁ yaṁ kāmayate kāmaṁ taṁ taṁ prapnoti sādhakaḥ*  
*rādhā-kṛpā-kaṭākṣeṇa bhaktiḥ syāt prema-lakṣaṇā*

**Перевод:**

**If a sādhaka with purified intelligence recites this stava with a fixed mind on the lunar days known as the full-moon day, the bright aṣṭamī, the daśamī, the ekādaśī, and the trayodaśī, then each and every one of his desires will be fulfilled, one by one. And by the merciful sidelong glance of Śrī Rādhā he will obtain devotional service that has the special symptom of being imbued with pure, ecstatic love of God (prema).**

### Text 16-17

*uru-dagne nābhi-dagne hṛd-dagne kaṇṭha-dagnake*  
*rādhā-kuṇḍa-jale sthitvā yaḥ paṭhet sādhakaḥ śatam*  
*tasya sarvārtha-siddhaḥ syād vāk-sāmarthya tato labhet*  
*aiśvaryaṁ ca labhet sākśād dṛṣā paśyati rādhikām*

**Перевод:**

**That sādhaka who recites this stava 100 times while standing in the waters of Śrī Rādhā-kuṇḍa up to his thighs, navel, chest, or neck will attain complete perfection in the five goals of human existence, namely dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa, and prema. He also will attain the power by which everything he says will come true. He becomes very powerful and opulent due to attaining transcendental majesty, and he gets to meet śrī Rādhikā face to face, seeing Her even with his present eyes.**

### Text 18

*tena sā tat-kṣaṇād eva tuṣṭa datte mahā-varam*  
*yena paśyati netrābhyāṁ tat-priyaṁ śyāmasundaram*

**Перевод:**

**\[By such chanting of this prayer in Rādhā-kuṇḍa\] Śrī Rādhikā becomes so pleased that She instantly bestows a great benediction on the devotee, which is that he sees Her beloved Śyāmasundara with his very own eyes.**

### Text 19

*nitya-līlā-praveśaṁ ca dadāti hi vrajādhipaḥ*  
*ataḥ parataraṁ prāpyaṁ vaiṣṇavānāṁ na vidyate*

**Перевод:**

**Then that Lord of Vṛndāvana grants the devotee entrance into His eternal pastimes. Genuine Vaiṣṇavas hanker for nothing beyond this.**

**Перевод:**

**Thus ends Śrī Śrī Rādhā-kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-stavarāja, spoken by Lord Śiva to Parvatī.**

## Śrī Kṛṣṇa-kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-stotram

**Petition for the Sidelong Glance of Śrī Kṛṣṇa**

**A poetic prayer chanted by Lord Śiva in the Ūrdhvāmnāya-Tantra (sequel to the Rādhā-kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-stava-rāja)**

### Text 1

*bhaje vrajaika-maṇḍanaṁ samasta-pāpa-khaṇḍanam*  
*sva-bhakta-citta-rañjanaṁ sadaiva nanda-nandanam*  
*su-piccha-guccha-mastakaṁ su-nāda-veṇu-hastakam*  
*anaṅga-raṅga-sāgaraṁ namāmi kṛṣṇa-nāgaram*

**Перевод:**

**I always worship Nanda’s son,**  
**Who is the unique ornament of Vraja,**  
**Who smashes everyone’s sins,**  
**Who pleases the hearts of His devotees,**  
**Whose head is adorned with a nice peacock feather,**  
**Who holds a sweet-sounding flute,**  
**Who is an ocean of amorous dalliances—**  
**I bow to Kṛṣṇa, the supreme paramour.**

### Text 2

*manoja-garva-mocanaṁ viśāla-bhāla-candanaṁ*  
*su-pīta-vastra-śobhanaṁ namāmi padma-locanam*  
*karāravinda-bhūṣaṇaṁ smitāvaloka-sundaram*  
*mahendra-māna-dāraṇaṁ smarāmi kṛṣṇa-bālakam*

**Перевод:**

**I bow to Him who relieves Cupid of his pride,**  
**Whose forehead bears sandalwood** ***tilaka*****,**  
**Who is splendorous with nice yellow dress,**  
**Who has lotus-petal eyes,**  
**Who is beautified by lotus hands,**  
**Who is graced by lovely smiling glances,**  
**Who curbs the vanity of the king of demigods—**  
**I remember the child Kṛṣṇa.**

### Text 3

*kadamba-sūnu-kuṇḍalaṁ su-cāru-gaṇḍa maṇḍalaṁ*  
*vrajāṅganaika-vallabhaṁ namāmi kṛṣṇa-durlabham*  
*yaśodayā samodayā sakopayā dayā-nidhim*  
*ulūkhale su-dus sahaṁ namāmi nanda-nandanam*

**Перевод:**

**I bow to rarely-attained Kṛṣṇa,**  
**Who wears** ***kadamba*****-flower earrings**  
**That caress His fine and charming cheeks,**  
**Who is the sole beloved of the residents of Vraja,**  
**Who is the reservoir of mercy for Yaśodā,**  
**Whether she is pleased or angry with Him.**  
**He even tolerated being tied by her to a grinding mortar—**  
**I bow down to Nanda-Nandana.**

### Text 4

*navīna-gopa-nāgaraṁ navīna-keli-sāgaraṁ*  
*navīna-megha-sundaraṁ bhaje vrajaika-mandiram*  
*sadaiva pāda-paṅkajaṁ madīya-mānase nijaṁ*  
*dadāmi nanda-bālakaṁ samasta-bhakta-pālakam*

**Перевод:**

**I worship the only shelter of Vraja,**  
**Who is the fresh young cowherd hero,**  
**Who is the ocean of fresh sportive pastimes,**  
**Who is gorgeous lika a fresh new raincloud.**  
**I have forever offered my mind**  
**Unto the lotus feet of**  
**The dear Nanda’s dear boy,**  
**The protector of all His devotees.**

### Text 5

*samasta-gopa-nāgaraṁ dṛgāmbujaika-mohanam*  
*namāmi kuñja-nādakaṁ prasūna-bhānu-śobhanam*  
*dṛgānta-kānta-rañjanaṁ sadā sadāli-saṅginam*  
*dine dine navaṁ navaṁ namāmi nanda-sambhavam*

**Перевод:**

**I bow to the leader of all the cowherd folk,**  
**Whose lotus eyes are the sole enchantment,**  
**Who plays His flute in the bowers,**  
**Who is nicely decorated with regal flowers,**  
**Whose eye-corners are splendorously colored,**  
**Who always associates with the virtuous** ***gopīs,***  
**Who is newer and newer from day to day—**  
**I bow to the boy of Nanda.**

### Text 6

*guṇākaraṁ sukhākaraṁ kṛpākaraṁ śubhākaraṁ*  
*tvayā sukha-pradāyakaṁ namāmi prema-nāyakam*  
*samasta-doṣa-śoṣaṇaṁ samasta-bhakta-toṣaṇaṁ*  
*samasta-dāsa-mānasaṁ namāmi kṛṣṇa-lālasam*

**Перевод:**

**I bow to Kṛṣṇa,**  
**Who is the abode of all good qualities,**  
**All happiness, all mercy, and all auspiciousness,**  
**O Lord, You are the giver of joy!**  
**I bow to the Lord of Love,**  
**Who absorbs everyone’s faults,**  
**Who pleases all the devotees,**  
**Who remains in all His servants’ minds,**  
**And who is eager for fun.**

### Text 7

*dṛgānta-cāru-śāyakaṁ namāmi prema-nāyakaṁ*  
*nikāma-kāma-dāyakaṁ namāmi veṇu-gāyakam*  
*mahā-bhavāgni-tārakaṁ bhavābdhi-karṇa-dhārakaṁ*  
*yaśomatī kiśorakaṁ namāmi dugdha-corakam*

**Перевод:**

**I bow to the Lord of Love,**  
**Whose eye-corners release lovely arrows and**  
**Who bestows excessive amorous desire.**  
**I bow to the player of the flute,**  
**Who delivers souls from the burning fire of material life,**  
**Who is the captain of the ship that carries His devotees across the ocean of birth and death,**  
**Who is the dear boy of mother Yaśodā—**  
**I bow to the stealer of milk products.**

### Text 8

*samasta-mugdha-gopikā manoja-kāma-dāyakam*  
*namāmi bhakta-vardhanaṁ dadhi-priyaṁ janārdanam*  
*kiśora-kānti-rañjanaṁ su-śobhitaṁ dṛg-añjanaṁ*  
*gajendra-mokṣa-kāriṇaṁ namāmi loka-sammatam*

**Перевод:**

**I bow to Him**  
**Who increases His devotees’ prosperity,**  
**Who imparts amorous desires to all**  
**The hopelessly innocent cowherd girls,**  
**Who is fond of yogurt,**  
**Who is prayed to by all souls,**  
**Who is tinged with the luster of fresh youth,**  
**Who is made nicely splendorous by His dark eye-lining cosmetics,**  
**Who is the deliverer of Gajendra—**  
**I bow to Him who is honored by all the worlds.**

### Text 9

*nikuñja-mañju-mādhurī-priyāli-vṛnda-sundarīṁ*  
*labheham-indirā-stutāṁ tathā-kṛpā vidhīyatām*  
*pramāṇitaṁ stava-dvayaṁ paṭhanti prātar utthitāḥ*  
*ta eva nanda-nandanaṁ milanti bhāva-saṁsthitāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**One who rises early in the morning and recites these two authoritative prayers**  
**(Rādhā-kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-stava-rāja and Kṛṣṇa-kṛpā-kaṭākṣa-stotram)**  
**attains his appropriate mercy by becoming a beautiful girl**  
**among the assembly of dear** ***gopīs*** **absorbed in the sweetness**  
**of the secret forest bowers—**  
**a post cherished even by the Goddess of Fortune.**  
**Such a soul certainly meets Nanda-Nandana face-to-face**  
**and remains permanently situated in ecstatic loving emotions.**
