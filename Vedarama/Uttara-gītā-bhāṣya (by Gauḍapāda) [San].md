# Uttara-gītā-bhāṣya (by Gauḍapāda) \[San\]

## Uttara-gītā-bhāṣya \[San\]

Gauḍapāda is a famous philosopher who was Śaṅkarācārya's teacher's teacher.Acc. to some, he flourished in Bengal in the beginning of the 8th century A.D. or the end of the 7th. Others think that he lived in the 6th century.
