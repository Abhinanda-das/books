# Vilāpa-kusumāñjali (SRS)

*Rendered by Sivarama Swami*

*tvam rupa-masjari sakhi prathita pure 'smin*  
*pumsah parasya vadanam na hi pasyasiti*  
*bimbadhare ksatam anagata-bhartrkaya*  
*yat te vyadhayi kim u ta chuka-pungavena*

**Перевод:**

**“O friend, Rupa-manjari! In this town of Yavata you are well known for being so chaste that you would never even look at another man. Is it not surprising, then, that although your husband is away from home there are fresh bite marks on your bimba-fruit lips? Could the mischievous culprit be that best of parrots?”**

*sthala-kamalini yuktam garvita kanane 'smin*  
*pranayasi vara-hasyam puspa-guccha-cchalena*  
*api nikhila-latas tah saurabhaktah sa muscan*  
*mrgayati tava margam krsna-bhrngo yad adya*

**Перевод:**

**“O land-lotus, Radha! You have every right to smile proudly through Your soft petals when the black Krsna- bee has abandoned every fragrant vine of this forest in His eager search for the pathway that leads to You.”**

*vrajendra-vasati-sthale vividha-ballavi-sankule*  
*tvam eva rati-masjari pracura-punya-pusjodaya*  
*vilasa-bhara-vismrta-pranayi-mekhala-margane*  
*yad adya nija-nathaya vrajasi nathita kandaram*

**Перевод:**

**“O Rati-manjari! You are certainly the most fortunate gopi in the realm of Vraja’s king! When Sri Radha left behind Her favourite belt in the course of overflowing pastimes, it was you whom She asked to retrieve it from a secluded cave.”**

*prabhur api yadunandano ya esa*  
*priya-yadunandana unnata-prabhavah*  
*svayam atula-krpamrtabhisekam*  
*mama krtavams tam aham gurum prapadye*

**Перевод:**

**“I surrender to my spiritual master Yadunandana Acarya, a dear devotee of Yadunandana-Krsna. Surcharged with spiritual power, he kindly showered the nectar of his mercy upon me.”**

*yo mam- dustara-geha-nirjala-maha-kupad apara-klamat*  
*sadyah sandra-dayambudhih prakrtitah svairi krpa-rajjubhih*  
*uddhrtyatma-saroja-nindi-carana-prantam- prapadya svayam*  
*sri-damodara-sa-cakara tam aham- caitanyacandram- bhaje*

**Перевод:**

**“I worship Lord Caitanyacandra, the supremely independent ocean of grace. With the ropes of mercy He suddenly drew me from the waterless well of household life which is full of unlimited sufferings and insurmountable obstacles, He gave me the shelter of His lotus feet which eclipse the beauty of celestial lilies, and He entrusted me to the care of Svarupa Damodara Gosvami.”**

*vairagya-yug-bhakti-rasam prayatnair*  
*apayayan mam anabhipsum andham*  
*krpambudhir yah para-duhkha-duhkhi*  
*sanatanas tam prabhum asrayami*

**Перевод:**

**“I was unwilling to drink the nectar of devotional service laced with renunciation, but Sanatana Gosvami took pity on me and made me do so. Because He is an ocean of kind' ness and an abode of compassion, I yearn to live forever in the shade of his lotus feet.”**

*aty-utkatena nitaram virahanalena*  
*dandahyamana-hrdaya kila kapi dasi*  
*ha svamini ksanam iha pranayena gadham*  
*akrandhanena vidhura vilapami padyaih*

**Перевод:**

**“O mistress! Overwhelmed with love, her heart always burning in the great fire of separation, a certain maidservant now pitifully laments in the following Verses.”**

*devi duhkha-kula-sagarodare*  
*duyamanam ati-durgatam janam*  
*tvam krpa-prabala-naukayadbhutam*  
*prapaya sva-pada-pankajalayam*

**Перевод:**

**“O goddess! Kindly hear this unfortunate person now drowning in an ocean of pain, place him in the sturdy boat of Your mercy, and ferry him to the wonderful realm of Your lotus feet.”**

*tvad-alokana-kalahi-*  
*damsair eva mrtam janam*  
*tvat-padabja-milal-laksa-*  
*bhesajair devi jivaya*

**Перевод:**

**“O beautiful one! Thereafter, with the unfailing medicine of the red lac from Your lotus feet, please revive this soul now dead from the venomous bites of the black snake of not seeing You.”**

*devi te carana-padma-dasikam*  
*viprayoga-bhara-dava-pavakaih*  
*dahyamanatara-kaya-ballavim*  
*jivaya ksana-niriksanamrtaih*

**Перевод:**

**“O effulgent goddess! Since separation from You is also like a blazing forest fire that constantly burns this maidservant of Your lotus feet, please restore her to life with the cooling elixir of a moment’s glance.**

*svapne 'pi kim sumukhi te caranambujata-*  
*rajat-paraga-patavasa-vibhusanena*  
*sobham param atitaram ahahottamanam*  
*bibhrad bhavisyati kada mama sartha-nama*

**Перевод:**

**“Beautiful-faced girl! When, even in a dream, will I decorate my forehead with the perfumed dust of Your lotus feet, the brilliance of which thus confirms my head to be the topmost limb of my body?”**

*amrtabdhi-rasa-prayais*  
*tava nupura-sisjitaih*  
*ha kada mama kalyani*  
*badhiryam apanesyate*

**Перевод:**

**“O auspicious one! And when will the sound of Your ankle bells sprinkle droplets from the ocean of nectar and so cure the aching deafness of my ears?”**

*sasakabhrd-abhisare netra-bhrnascalabhyam*  
*disi vidisi bhayenodghurnitabhyam vanani*  
*kuvalaya-dala-kosany eva klptani yabhyam*  
*kim u kila kalaniyo devi tabhyam jano 'yam*

**Перевод:**

**“One moonlit night, when You leave home to meet Krsna, dashing fearfully from one tree to another like a rabbit, Your bumblebee eyes turning the entire forest blue with their glance, will You, O Goddess, look my way?”**

*yadavadhi mama kascin masjari rupa-purva*  
*vraja-bhuvi bata netra-dvandva-diptim cakara*  
*tadavadhi bata vrndaranya-rajsi prakamam*  
*carana-kamala-laksa-sandidrksa mamabhut*

**Перевод:**

**“O queen of Vrndavana! Ever since a manjari called Rupa filled my eyes with light in this land of Vraja, I have yearned to see the red lac decorating Your lotus feet.”**

*yada tava sarovaram sarasa-bhrnga-sanghollasat-*  
*saroruha-kulojjvalam madhura-vari-sampuritam*  
*sphutat-sarasijaksi he nayana-yugma-saksad-babhau*  
*tadaiva mama lalasajani tavaiva dasye rase*

**Перевод:**

**“And from the instant I beheld Your lake, brimming with nectarean water, splendid with blossoming lotuses, and swarming with humming bees, I began to ache for the honour of serving You.”**

*padabjayos tava vina vara-dasyam eva*  
*nanyat kadapi samaye kila devi yace*  
*sakhyaya te mama namo 'stu namo 'stu nityam*  
*dasyaya te mama raso 'stu raso 'stu satyam*

**Перевод:**

**“Oh Devi! I shall never, never, ask You for anything other than the peerless service of Your lotus feet. Over and over again I offer my respects to the chance of becoming Your girlfriend but I shall only aspire for the honour of being Your maidservant.**

*ati-su-lalita-laksaslista-saubhagya-mudra-*  
*tatibhir adhika-tustya cihniti-krtya bahu*  
*nakha-dalita-haridra-garva-gauri priyam me*  
*carana-kamala-sevam ha kada dasyasi tvam*

**Перевод:**

**“O damsel, the hue of whose golden complexion chastens the pride of crushed turmeric, please understand that my sole desire is to be a certified maidservant of Your lotus feet. Therefore, kindly stamp my arm just once with the lacquer-coloured symbols of Your soles.”**

*pranalim kilalair bahubhir abhi sanksalya madhurair*  
*muda sammarjya svair vivrta-kaca-vrndaih priyataya*  
*kada bahyagaram vara-parimalair dhupa-nivahair*  
*vidhasye te devi pratidinam aho vasitam aham*

**Перевод:**

**“If You acknowledge me thus and charge me with the privilege of rinsing the drains of Your house with pure water, I shall do so with such love and happiness that I shall even dry them with my own hair and then scent Your commode with fragrant incense.”**

*pratah sudhamsu-militam mrdam atra yatnad*  
*ahrtya vasita-payas ca grhantare ca*  
*padambuje bata kada jala-dharaya te*  
*praksalya bhavini kacair iha marjayami*

**Перевод:**

**“As daybreak worships Your home, I shall carefully wash Your two lotus feet with camphor-scented water mixed with clay and then also carefully dry them with my hair.”**

*praksalya pada-kamalam krta-danta-kastam*  
*snanartham anya-sadane bhavatim nivistam*  
*abhyajya-gandhitatarair iha taila-puraih*  
*prodvartayisyati kada kim u kinkariyam*

**Перевод:**

**“Thereafter, may this maidservant brush Your teeth with a twig, wash Your lotus feet with Yamuna water, and, after You have entered the bathroom for a scented oil massage, offer You a seat upon which to sit?”**

*ayi vimala-jalanam gandha-karpura-puspair*  
*jita-vidhu-mukha-padme vasitanam ghataughaih*  
*pranaya-lalita-sakhya diyamanaih purastat*  
*tava varam abhisekam ha kadaham karisye*

**Перевод:**

**“O You, the heavenliness of whose lotus face shames the moon! May I carefully bathe Your entire body with water scented with flowers and camphor that was brought in jugs by one of Your charming and affectionate friends?”**

*paniyam- cina-vastraih sasi-mukhi sanakai ramya-mrdt-anga-yastair*  
*yatnad utsarya modad disi disi vicalan-netra-minascalayah*  
*sronau raktam- dukulam- tad aparam atulam- caru-nilam- siro 'grat*  
*sarvangesu pramodat pulakita-vapusa kim- maya te prayojyam*

**Перевод:**

**“O moon-faced girl! After Your bath is complete, may I continue to serve You by slowly and carefully drying the water from Your beautiful, delicate limbs with a silken towel? And as Your eyes happily wander here and there like restless fish, may I, the hairs on my body standing up in bliss, cover Your hips with an incomparable red petticoat, and Your limbs from head to toe with a beautiful blue sari?”**

*praksalya pada-kamalam tad-anukramena*  
*gosthendra-sunu-dayite tava kesa-pasam*  
*ha narmada-grathita-sundara-suksma-malyair*  
*venim karisyati kada pranayair jano 'yam*

**Перевод:**

**“O beloved of the son of Vraja’s king! After again washing Your lotus feet will this person lovingly braid Your hair with many delicate garlands artistically fashioned by the florist girl Narmada?”**

*subhaga-mrgamadenakhanda-subhramsu-vat te*  
*tilakam iha lalate devi modad vidhaya*  
*masrna-ghusrna-carcam arpayitva ca gatre*  
*stana-yugam api gandhais citritam kim karisye*

**Перевод:**

**“And may I also happily mark Your forehead with a musk- dot of tilaka radiant as the full moon, anoint Your limbs with glistening kunkuma}and decorate Your breasts with delightful pictures in fragrant colours? O Goddess, may I?”**

*sindura-rekha simante*  
*devi ratna-salakaya*  
*maya ya kalpita kim te*  
*salakam sobhayisyati*

**Перевод:**

**“O Divine angel! Kindly permit me to decorate the part in Your hair with a line of vermillion that I draw with a jewelled salaka stick.”**

*hanta devi tilakasya samastad*  
*bindavo 'runa-su-gandhi-rasena*  
*krsna-madaka-mahausadhi-mukhya*  
*dhira-hastam iha kim para-kalpyah*

**Перевод:**

**“Fragrant red dots around the musk tilaka will serve as an aphrodisiac for Krsna. O Devi! With a steady hand may I paint these dots that will madden Your beloved more than the most enchanting herb?”**

*gosthendra-putra-mada-citta-karindra-raja-*  
*bandhaya puspa-dhanusah kila bandha-rajjoh*  
*kim karnayos tava varoru varavatamsa-*  
*yugmena bhusanam aham sukhita karisye*

**Перевод:**

**“From his rope made of bumblebees, the god who carries a flower-bow fashioned hoop earrings for You. O girl of enticing thighs! Will I happily decorate Your ears with those earrings, which will tightly bind a mad elephant also known as the heart of Vraja’s prince?”**

*ya te kasculir atra sundari maya vaksojayor arpita*  
*syamacchadana-kamyaya kila na sa tathyeti vijsayatam*  
*kintu svamini krsna eva sahasa tat tam avapya svayam*  
*pranebhyo 'py adhikam svakam nidhi-yugam sangopayaty eva hi*

**Перевод:**

**“Although the bodice with which I carefully dress You is meant to conceal Your breasts from Krsna’s gaze, He obviously does not understand my intention. O Mistress, what can I do if He tightly embraces You and becomes the garment covering those two treasures dearer to Him than His own life?”**

*nana-mani-prakara-gumphita-caru-pustya*  
*mukta-srajas tava su-vaksasi hema-gauri*  
*sranty-abhrtalasa-mukunda-su-tulikayam*  
*kim kalpayisyatitaram tava dasikeyam*

**Перевод:**

**“Hear me, O golden beauty! This maidservant longs for the time when she can place a necklace of pearls interlaced with gems upon Your breasts, which will later serve as a soft pillow for Mukunda when He is sleepy and fatigued.”**

*mani-caya-khacitabhir nila-cudavalibhir*  
*hari-dayita-kalavid-dvandvam indivaraksi*  
*api bata tava divyair angulir anguliyaih*  
*kvacid api kila kale bhusayisyami kim nu*

**Перевод:**

**“I also long to adorn Your delicate fingers with glittering rings and Your two graceful hands, so dear to Hari and so expert in the arts, with blue bangles studded with jewels. O girl with blue lotus eyes, may I?”**

*padambhoje manimaya-tula-koti-yugmena yatnad*  
*abhyarcyaitad-dala-kulam api prestha-padanguliyaih*  
*kasci-damna kati-tatam idam prema-pitham su-netre*  
*kamsarater atulam acirad arcayisyami kim te*

**Перевод:**

**“Since Your two lotus feet must be worshipped with jewelled anklets, the petals of Your lotus feet with toe rings, and Your hips, Krsna’s sacred place of pilgrimage, with a splendid sash sprinkled with tiny hells, will You award me these rites with a glance from Your enchanting eyes?”**

*lalitatara-mrnali-kalpa-bahu-dvayam te*  
*murajayi-mati-hamsi-dhairya-vidhvamsa-daksam*  
*mani-kula-racitabhyam angadabhyam purastat*  
*pramada-bhara-vinamra kalpayisyami kim va*

**Перевод:**

**“Although I may be overwhelmed with intense bliss from serving You, may I still place jewel-studded armlets on Your lotus-stem arms, which so deftly frustrate the patience of Murajayi’s swan-like intelligence?”**

*rasotsave ya iha gokulacandra-bahu-*  
*sparsena saubhaga-bharam nitaram avapa*  
*graiveyakena kim u tam tava kantha-desam*  
*sampujayisyati punah subhage jano 'yam*

**Перевод:**

**“O blessed girl! Thereafter, will this person place a bejewelled pendant upon Your neck, which attained the pinnacle of good fortune when it was touched by the arm of the moon of Gokula during the festival of the rasa dance?”**

*dattah pralamba-ripunodbhata-sankhacuda-*  
*nasat pratosi-hrdayam madhumangalasya*  
*hastena yah sumukhi kaustubha-mitram etam*  
*kim te syamantaka-manim taralam karisye*

**Перевод:**

**“After killing proud Sankhacuda, Krsna took the Syamantaka jewel from the demon's head and gave it to the enemy of Pralambasura, who with a happy heart gave it to Madhumangala, who, with his own hand, gave it to You. O beautiful-faced one! Since this Syamantaka has become best friends with the Kaustubha gem, may I make it Your chief ornament by placing it around Your neck?"**

*pranta-dvaye parivirajita-guccha-yugma-*  
*vibhrajitena nava-kascana-dorakena*  
*ksinam krudhaty atha krsodari ced itiva*  
*badhnami bhos tava kadabhibhayena madhyam*

**Перевод:**

**“O slender girl! The narrowness of Your waist gives the impression that it may break at any moment. Please allow me to carefully bind it with a protective golden cord, splendid with flower-cluster tassels at each end.”**

*kanaka-gunitam uccair mauktikam mat-karat te*  
*tila-kusuma-vijetri nasika sa su-vrttam*  
*madhumathana-mahali-ksobhakam hema-gauri*  
*prakatatara-maranda-prayam adasyate kim*

**Перевод:**

**“Because it agitates the roving Krsna-bumblebee with its nectar, may I also hang a splendid honey-pearl on Your nose and so put to shame the arrogance of the sesame flower.”**

*angadena tava vama-doh-sthale*  
*svarna-gauri nava-ratna-malikam*  
*patta-guccha-parisobhitam imam*  
*ajsaya parinayami te kada*

**Перевод:**

**“O golden one! After You breathe auspiciousness into Your nose ring, will You order me to tie an amulet of nine gems upon Your left arm with a tasselled silk cord?”**

*karnayor upari cakra-salake*  
*cascalaksi nihite mayaka te*  
*ksobhakam nikhila-gopa-vadhunam*  
*cakravad bhramayatam mura-satrum*

**Перевод:**

**“While Your eyes rove from side to side, shall I also place on Your ears hoop earrings that make Krsna, Who always agitates the gopis, wander in aimless circles?”**

*kada te mrgasavaksi*  
*cibuke mrganabhina*  
*bindum ullasayisyami*  
*mukundamoda-mandire*

**Перевод:**

**“O fawn-eyed one! After decorating Your ears, shall I place a radiant dot of musk upon Your chin, the temple of Mukunda’s happiness?”**

*dasanams te kada rakta-*  
*rekhabhir bhusayamy aham*  
*devi mukta-phalaniha*  
*padmaraga-gunair iva*

**Перевод:**

**“If You kindly part Your lips then the vertical red lines I paint on Your teeth will make them look like pearls inset with lines of rubies. O goddess! Will you oblige me?”**

*utkhadirena nava-candra-virajitena*  
*ragena te vara-sudhadhara-bimba-yugme*  
*gangeya-gatri mayaka parirasjite 'smin*  
*damsam vidhasyati hathat kim u krsna-kirah*

**Перевод:**

**“O girl of golden limbs! With lipstick made from red khadira mixed with camphor, may I also colour Your heavenly nectarean lips, as red as bimba fruit, even though the Krsna parrot may appear at any moment and forcibly bite them?”**

*yat-pranta-desa-lava-lesa-vighurnitena*  
*baddhah ksanad bhavati krsna-karindra uccaih*  
*tat-khasjarita-jayi-netra-yugam kadayam*  
*sampujayisyati janas tava kajjalena*

**Перевод:**

**“All of Vraja knows that the restlessness of Your eyes defeats the capricious flitting of wagtails, but we gopis know that with the slightest movement at their corners, those eyes instantly bind tight the regal elephant known as Krsna. May I worship them with black kajjal?”**

*yasyanka-rasjita-siras tava-mana-bhange*  
*gosthendra-sunur adhikam susamam upaiti*  
*laksa-rasah sa ca kada padayor adhas te*  
*nyasto mayapy atitaram chavim apsyatiha*

**Перевод:**

**“To soothe Your jealous anger, Krsna once placed Your lotus feet upon His head, which then became exceedingly handsome with a red mark of lac. Seated on the floor before You, may I decorate Your lotus feet with that very same nectarean lacquer?”**

*kalavati natamsayoh pracura-kama-pusjojjvalat-*  
*kalanidhi-muradvisah prakata-rasa-sambhavayoh*  
*bhrama-bhramara-jhankrtair madhura-malli-malam muda*  
*kada tava tayoh samarpayati devi dasi-janah*

**Перевод:**

**“O artistic girl! This maidservant has adorned You to the best of her ability. May she conclude her service by joyfully hanging a sweet jasmine garland swarming with humming bees on Your gracefully sloping shoulders, shoulders that were once embraced in the rasa dance by Muradvisah, the glistening ocean of amorous arts?”**

*suryaya suryamani-nirmita-vedi-madhye*  
*mugdhangi bhavata ihali-kulair vrtayah*  
*argham samarpayitum utka-dhiyas tavarat*  
*sajjani kim sumukhi dasyati dasikeyam*

**Перевод:**

**“After You are fully dressed and ornamented, will You, O girl with charming limbs and beguiling face, attend to Your first rites? At that time may this maidservant sit among Your friends and lovingly hand You items of worship as You adore the sun god, who sits on an altar of suryamani jewels?”**

*vraja-pura-pati-rajsya ajsaya mistam annam*  
*bahu-vidham ati-yatnat svena pakvam varoru*  
*sapadi nija-sakhinam mad-vidhanam ca hastair*  
*madhumathana-nimittam kim tvaya sannidhapyam*

**Перевод:**

**“I long for the time when You, of beautiful thighs, request me to personally hand-serve the killer of Madhu with the many delicious foods that You had fastidiously cooked at the request of Vraja’s queen.”**

*nitanna-mad-vidha-lalata-tate lalatam*  
*pritya pradaya mudita vraja-raja-rajsi*  
*premna prasur iva bhavat-kusalasya prccham*  
*bhavye vidhasyati kada mayi tavakatvat*

**Перевод:**

**“Upon arriving for that service, will I first show the queen of Vraja’s king the many sweetmeats that You had cooked? O auspicious girl! When I do, will she then lovingly place her forehead to mine in the way of a mother, and knowing me to be Yours will she enquire about Your well-being?”**

*krsna-vaktrambujocchistam*  
*prasadam param adarat*  
*dattam dhanisthaya devi*  
*kim anesyami te 'gratah*

**Перевод:**

**“O Devi! My mission accomplished, will I return to Your husband’s home and affectionately place before You the remnants tasted by the lotus mouth of Lord Krsna, prasada entrusted to me by Dhanistha-gopi?”**

*nana-vidhair amrta-sara-rasayanais taih*  
*krsna-prasada-militair iha bhojya-peyaih*  
*ha kunkumangi lalitadi-sakhi-vrta tvam*  
*yatnan maya kim u taram upabhojaniya*

**Перевод:**

**“O girl whose golden limbs glisten with the hue of vermillion! If You order me, then shall I attentively serve You, Lalita, and other friends with a variety of ambrosial foods and nectar drinks mixed with the remnants of food directly tasted by Krsna?”**

*panaya vari madhuram nava-pataladi-*  
*karpura-vasitataram taralaksi dattva*  
*kale kada tava mayacamaniya-danta-*  
*kastadikam pranayatah param arpaniyam*

**Перевод:**

**“Then, as your eyes grow wild with ecstasy, shall I lovingly offer You a eucalyptus twig to brush Your teeth and water sweetly scented with a mixture of fresh patala flowers and ground camphor to rinse Your mouth?”**

*bhojanasya samaye tava yatnad*  
*devi dhupa-nivahan vara-gandhan*  
*vijanadyam api tat-ksana-yogyam*  
*ha kada pranayatah pranayami*

**Перевод:**

**“And while You are thus enjoying Your evening meal, O presiding deity of my heart, may I enhance Your pleasure by lighting an abundance of aromatic incense and by fanning You with love and attention?”**

*karpura-pura-paripurita-naga-valli-*  
*parnadi-puga-parikalpita-vitikam te*  
*vaktrambuje madhura-gatri muda kadaham*  
*protphulla-roma-nikaraih param arpayami*

**Перевод:**

**“O sweet-limbed girl! When You finish Your meal, be kind enough to accept a betel leaf filled with betel nuts and camphor that I place in Your lotus mouth. If you do, I shall be in such ecstasy that the hairs of my body will stand on end.”**
