# Visnu Purana 17 Chapter

## Chapter 17 - The Demigods are Defeated by the Demons

Maitreya said - You have told me, venerable sir, that an ancestral rite is not to be looked upon by certain persons, amongst whom you mentioned were *Nagnas*.

I wish to learn what is a *Nagna*, what are their activities and how do they receive such a name? What is the character of the individuals of whom you referred to?

Parasara said -The Rg, Yajur and Sama Vedas constitute the triple covering of the various *varnas*, and the sinner who throws this off is said to be naked (*nagna*).

The three *Vedas* are the garments of all the orders of men, and when they are discarded they are left bare.

I have heard my illustrious grandfather Vasistha speak on this subject matter to the great soul Bhisma.

Indeed, I heard that great soul explain about *nagnas* and thus I shall also explain to you since you have asked me this question.

In ancient times, there was a battle between the demigods and the demons that lasted for one celestial year. Finally the demigods were defeated by the demons that were under the command of Hrada, the son of Hiranyakasipu.

The frightened demigods fled to the northern shore of the milk ocean where they engaged in austerities to propitiate Lord Visnu.

(They prayed) "May the Original Person, the divine Lord Visnu be pleased with our prayers that we offer unto Him in order to propitiate.

"May the first of beings, the divine Vishnu, be pleased with the words that we are about to address to him, in order to propitiate the lord of all worlds; from which mighty cause all created things have originated, and into whom they shall again dissolve! Who is able to declare his praise? We, who have been put to shame by the triumph of our foes, will glorify you although your true power and might be not within the reach of words. You are eareh, water, fire, air, ether, mind, crude matter, and primeval soul: all this elementary creation, with or without visible form, is your body; all, from Brahma to a stock, diversified by place and time. Glory to you, who are Brahma, your first form, evolved from the lotus springing from the navel, for the purpose of creation. Glory to you, who are Indra, the sun, Rudra, the Vasus, fire, the winds, and even also ourselves. Glory to you, Govinda, who are all demons, whose essence is arrogance and want of discrimination, unchecked by patience or self-control. Glory to you, who are the Yakshas, whose nature is charmed with sounds, and whose frivolous heares perfect knowledge cannot pervade. Glory to you, who are all heares perfect knowledge cannot pervade. Glory to you, Janardhana, who are that piety which is the instrument of recompensing the virtues of those who abide in heaven. Glory to you, who are one with the saints, whose perfect nature is ever blessed, and traverses unobstructed all permeable elements. Glory to you, who are one with the serpent race, double-tongued, impetuous, cruel, insatiate of enjoyment, and abounding with wealth. Glory to you, who are one with the Rshis, whose nature is free from sin or defect, and is identified with wisdom and tranquility. Glory to you, oh lotus-eyed, who are one with time, the form that devours, wiyout remorse, all created things at the termination of the Kalpa. Glory to you, who are Rudra, the being that dances with delight after he has swallowed up all things, the gods and the rest, wiyout distinction. Glory to you, Janardhana, who are man, the agent in developing the results of that activity which proceeds from the quality of foulness. Glory to you, who are brute animals, universal spirit that tends to perversity, which proceeds from the quality of darkness, and is encumbered with the twenty-eight kinds of obstructions. Glory to you, who are that chief spirit which is diversified in the vegetable world, and which, as the essence of sacrifice, is the instrument of accomplishing the perfection of the universe. Glory to you, who are every thing, and whose primeval form is the objects of perception, and heaven, and animals, and men, and gods. Glory to you, who are the cause of causes, the supreme spirit; who are distinct from us and all beings composed of intelligence and matter and the like, and with whose primeval nature there is nothing that can be compared. We bow to you. O lord, who hast neither colour, not extension, not bulk, nor any predictable qualities; and whose essence, purest of the pure, is appreciable only by holy sages. We bow to you, in the nature of Brahma, uncreated, undecaying; who are in our bodies, and in all other bodies, and in all living creatures; and besides whom there is nothing else. We glorify that Vasudeva, the sovereign lord of all, who is wiyout soil, the seed of all things, exempt from dissolution, unborn, eternal, being in essence the supreme condition of spirit, and in substance the whole of this universe."

Upon concluding their prayers, Lord Visnu appeared before the demigods holding conch, disc and club, riding on Garuda. The demigods offered their prostrated respects to the Lord and said to Him, " O Lord, have compassion upon us and give us protection. We have come to you in order to attain shelter from the demons who have taken over the three worlds and stolen the offerings in sacrifice which are meant for us. Simultaneously they take great care not to transgress the Vedic injunctions. Although both ourselves and the demons are part of You, since all living beings are dependent upon You, still we behold this world impressed with the ignorance of unity with the belief of it's separate existence. Since the demons are properly engaged in their respective duties, follow the orders of the scriptures and also practice religious austerities, it is impossible for us to destroy them. Your wisdom is beyond limit, thus kindly instruct us and explain how we may eliminate our enemies."(35-40)

When the all-powerful Visnu heard their request, he emitted an illusory form from His body and gave it to the demigods, saying, "This illusory form of Mine will completely confuse the demons so that they will be led away from the Vedic path and thus become ruined. In order to protect the world, I destroy anyone who is opposed to the authority of the Vedas, whether he is a demigod, demon or another being. O great demigods, go now, and do not fear. Let this illusory form go before you and help you attain victory.

## Chapter 18 - The Rise of Heretical Doctrines

Parasara continued – After this, this illusory form traveled to the eareh and saw the demons engaging in austerities upon the banks of the River Narmada.

He approached them in the guise of a naked renunciant, clean-shaven and carrying a bunch of peacock-feathers. In this way, he addressed then in a gentle way

"O Lords of the Daitya race! Why do you practice these austerities? Is it to gain some profit in this world or the next?"

"O sage," replied the demons, "We are performing penances to obtain success in our next birth. Why do you ask?"

"If you truly desire liberation" said the so-called sage, "then listen to me carefully for you are all qualified to hear what is the actual path to attain it. The duties that I will teach you are the secret way to salvation – there is no way greater than this. By following my words you will either attain the heavenly planets or will become exempt from taking another birth in this material world.

Such mighty beings as you are certainly qualified to hear this great doctrine of mine."

By such words, and many false arguments, the illusory being led the demons away from the Vedic injunctions.

He taught them that the same activity may ultimately lead to piety or impiety. It may or may not be. It may lead to liberation, or it may not.

It may be the supreme goal, or it may not. It may be manifest or it may not. It may be correct to go naked, or it may be correct to wear clothes.

Thus, the demons were seduced and neglected to perform their duties by continuously hearing from the illusory being who maintained the equal truth of contradictory statements.

They became known as ‘Arahatas' that stemmed from the word *arhatha* (qualified), since the illusory being told them that the demons were qualified to hear the false philosophy that he persuaded them to embrace.

Thus, the enemies of the demigods who became bewildered by he illusory form of Lord Visnu, rejected the teachings of the Vedas and in turn became teachers of the same heretical beliefs and contaminated others.

Likewise, those that came under their influence also preached this false doctrine to others. In this way within a short time, the Vedas was totally rejected by the race of demons.

Then, the same illusory being put on red cloth and assumed a gentle aspect, speaking mildly and addressed other demons.

"O mighty Asuras! If you wish to attain the heavenly planets or even *nirvana* then you must refrain from offering animals in sacrifice and hear from me what you must do.

You must understand that all that exists is composed of discriminate knowledge. Try to understand these words since they have been uttered by the wise.

The people of this world subsist wiyout support and they pursue falsities that they mistakenly perceive to be knowledge. They are simply working in the modes of nature such as passion etc. and revolve on the path of material existence."

In this way, he exclaimed "*Budhyavam*!" (Understand!) and the demons replied, "*Budhyate*!"(It is understood) and this deceiver to deviate from their religious duties by his false logic and persuasions bewildered these demons.

When they abandoned their faith in the Vedas, they gradually preached to others to do the same and thus this heretical philosophy spread, and many rejected the Vedas and their injunctions.

O Maitreya, the bewildering works of this false teacher did not stop at the demons acceptance of these two false creeds. He preached a variety of erroneous beliefs to others until they were all led astray and deserted the ways of the three *Vedas*.

Some of them blasphemed the *Vedas* while others blasphemed the demigods. Some you out against the Vedic sacrifices and rituals and others offended the *brahmanas*.

"Any law," they stated, " Which leads to the injury of animals is heinous. To claim that by pouring ghee in a fire one will attain some spiritual benefit is simply childish. If Indra, the king of the demigods, attained his high position through the performance of many sacrifices why does he eat wood that is used to fuel the fire? This means he is lower than an animal for at least an animal eats leaves! If an animal, which is slaughtered in a Vedic sacrifice, attains heaven, why doesn't the man performing the ritual benefit his own father by sacrificing him? If the food eaten at a *sraddha* ceremony satisfies another person, then it is unnecessary for a person who lives far away to bring food in person (NB. A *sraddha* may be offered for a man who is abroad by any of his family members who are living at home; it would be of equal benefit as if he offered it himself; he will equally eat the food).

"Let us first determine what is logically believed by all mankind," said their preceptor, "And then you will find satisfaction in my instructions. O mighty demons, words of authority do not come from above. This reasonable philosophy will be expounded by me alone, and then to you all."

By such words and teachings, the demons became perverted so that not one of them acknowledged the authority of the Vedas.

When the demons had thus rejected the true path of righteousness, the demigods took courage and prepared for battle. The war began again, only this time the demons were defeated and slain by the demigods who followed the Vedic laws. Discarding the armor of religion, which had previously protected them, the destruction of the demons, was inevitable.

Thus Maitreya, you should understand that those who reject the Vedic literature are called naked, because they have thrown away the garment of the Vedas. According to the law there are four *asramas* –*brahmacari, grhasta*, *vanaprastha* and *sannyasa*. There is no fifth order, and the sinful man that gives up householder life but does not take up the life of a *vanaprastha* or *sannyasi* is considered to be naked (a heretic). The *brahmana* who neglects his daily duties for one day and one night, alyough he is able to perform them, incurs sin for one day. Should he neglect them for a fortnight, despite being able to perform them, he can only be purified by rigorous penance. Virtuous people must look at the sun to purify themselves after seeing such a person who has neglected to perform his daily duties for a whole year. If by mistake they touch him they must take bath with their clothes on, but for the individual himself no atonement has been mentioned. There is no sinner on eareh who is guiltier than one in whose home the demigods, *rsis* and the forefathers are not worshipped. A man should not associate with, live with, sit with, a man or reside in the house of that person who has rejected the *devas, rsis* and *pitrs*. Conversation, civilities or association with a man who has abandoned his duties for a year produces an equal amount of sin. The person who eats in the house of such a man, sits with him, sleeps on the same bed as him, automatically becomes like him instantly. One who eats wiyout showing respect to the demigods, sages, forefathers and guests commits sin. How great is his sin? *Brahmanas* and men of other castes who turn away from their prescribed duties become atheists and are classed with those who relinquish pious activities. To stay in a place where the four *varnas* liberally mix is detrimental for righteous people. Men fall into hell when they converse with those who eat wiyout offering a portion to the *devas, rsis, pitrs* and guests. Therefore let a wise person carefully avoid speaking to or contacting heretics who have become impure by rejecting the three *Vedas*. Even if one performs a *sraddha* ceremony with faith and determination, neither the demigods nor the sages are pleased if it is looked upon by a heretic.

It is related that there was formerly a king named Satadhanu, whose wife Saivya was a woman of great virtue. She was devoted to her husband, benevolent, sincere, pure, adorned with every female excellence, with humility, and descretion. The Raja and his wife daily worshipped the god of gods, Janardhana, with pious meditations, oblations to fire, prayers, gifts, fasting, and every other mark of entire faith, and exclusive devotion. On one occasion, when they had fasted on the full moon of Kareika, and had bathed in the Bhagirathi, they beheld, as they came up from the water, a heretic approach them, who was the friend of the Raja's military preceptor. The Raja out of respect to the latter, entered into conversation with the heretic; but not so did the princess; reflecting that she was observing a fast, she turned from him, and cast her eyes up to the sun. On their arrival at home, the husband and wife, as usual, performed the worship of Vishnu, agreeably to the ritual. After a time the Raja, triumphant over his enemies, died; and the princess ascended the funeral pile of her husband.

In consequence of the fault committed by Satadhanu, by speaking to an infidel when he was engaged in a solemn fast, he was born again as a dog. His wife was born as the daughter of the Raja of Kasi, with a knowledge of the events of her pre-existence, accomplished in every science, and endowed with every virtue. Her father was anxious to give her in marriage to some suitable husband, but she constantly opposed his design, and the king was prevented by her from accomplishing her nuptials. With the eye of divine intelligence she knew that her own husband has been regenerate as a dog, and going once to the city Vaidisa she saw the dog, and recognized her former lord in him. Knowing that the animal was her husband, she placed upon his neck the bridal garland, accompanying it with the marriage rites and prayers: but he, eating the delicate food presented to him, expressed his delight after the fashion of his species; at which she was much ashamed, and, bowing reverently to him, thus spake to her degraded spouse: "recall to memory, illustrious prince, the ill timed politeness on account of which you have been born as a dog, and are now fawning upon me. In consequence of speaking to a heretic, after bathing in a sacred river, you have been condemned to this abject birth. Do you not remember it?" Thus reminded, the Raja recollected his former condition, and was lost in thought, and felt deep humiliation. With a broken spirit he went forth from the city, and falling dead in the desert, was born anew as a jackal. In the course of the following year the princess knew what had happened and went to the mountain Kolahala to seek for her husband. Finding him there, the lovely daughter of the king of the eareh said to her lord, thus disguised as a jackal, "Dost you not remember, oh king, the circumstance of conversing with a heretic, which I called to your recollection when you were a dog?" The Raja, thus addressed, knew that what the princess had said was true, and thereupon desisted from food, and died. He then became a wolf; but his blameless wife knew it, and came to him in the lonely forest, and awakened his remembrance of his original state. "No wolf are you," she said, "but the illustrious sovereign Satadhanu. You was then a dog, then a jackal, and are now a wolf." Upon this, recollecting himself, the prince abandoned his life, and became a vulture; in which form his lovely queen still found him, and aroused him to a knowledge of the past. "Prince," she exclaimed, "recollect yourself: away with this uncouth form, to which the sin of conversing with a heretic was condemned you!" The Raja was next born as a crow: when the princess, who through her mystical powers was aware of it, said to him, "You are now yourself the eater of tributary grain, to whom, in a prior existence, all the kings of the eareh paid tribute." Having abandoned his body, in consequence of the recollections excited by these words, the king next became a peacock, which the princess took to herself, and petted, and fed constantly with such food as is agreeable to birds of its class. The king of Kasi instituted at that time the solemn sacrifice of a horse. In the ablutions with which it terminated the princess caused her peacock to be bathed, bathing also herself; and she then reminded Satadhanu how he had been successively born as various animals. On recollecting this, he resigned his life. He was then born as the son of a person of distinction; and the princess now assenting to the wishes of her father to see her wedded, the king of Kasi caused it to be made known that she would elect a bridegroom from those who should present themselves as suitors for her hand. When the election took place, the princess made choice of her former lord, who appeared amongst the candidates, and again invested him with the character of her husband. They lived happily together, and upon her father's decease Satadhanu ruled over the country of Videha. He offered many sacrifices, and gave away many gifts, and begot sons, and subdued his enemies in war; and having duly exercised the sovereign power, and cherished benignantly the eareh, he died, as became his warrior birth, in battle. His queen again followed him in death, and, conformably to sacred precepts, once more mounted cheerfully his funeral pyre. The king then, along with his princess, ascended beyond the sphere of Indra to the regions where all desires are for ever gratified, obtaining ever-during and unequalled happiness in heaven, the perfect felicity that is the rarely realized reward of conjugal fidelity.

Such, Maitreya, is the sin of conversing with a heretic, and such are the expiatory effects of bathing after the solemn sacrifice of a horse, as I have narrated them to you. Let therefore a man carefully avoid the discourse of contact of an unbeliever, especially at seasons of devotion, and when engaged in the performance of religious rites preparatory to a sacrifice. If it be necessary that a wise man should look at the sun, after beholding one who has neglected his domestic ceremonies for a month, how much greater need must there be of expiation after encountering one who has wholly abandoned the Vedas? On who is supported by infidels, or who disputes the doctrines of holy writ? Let no a person treat with even the civility of speech, heretics, those who do forbidden acts, pretended saints, scoundrels, skeptics, and hypocrites. Intercourse with such iniquitous wretches, even at a distance, all association with schismatics, defiles; let a man therefore carefully avoid them.

There, Maitreya, are the persons called naked, the meaning of which term you desired to have explained. Their very looks vitiate the performance of an ancestral oblation; speaking to them destroys religious merit for a whole day. These are the unrighteous heretics to whom a man must not give shelter, and speaking to whom effaces whatever merit he may that day have obtained. Men, indeed, fall into hell as the consequence of only conversing with those who unprofitably assume the twisted hair, and shaven crown; with those who feed wiyout offering food to gods, spirits, and guests; and those who are excluded from the presentation of cakes, and libations of water, to the manes.
