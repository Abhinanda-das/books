# Visnu Suktam \[Sanskrit & English\]

### Text 1

*viṣṇor nukaṁ vīryāṇi pravocaṁ yaḥ pārthivāni vimame rajāṁsi yo askabhāyaduttaraṁ sadhasthaṁ vicakramāṇas tredhorugāyo \|*  
*viṣṇor aratāmasi viṣṇor pṛṣṭhamasi viṣṇoḥ śnyaptre stho \| viṣṇoḥ syūrasi viṣṇor dhruvamasi vaiṣṇavam asi viṣṇave tvā \|\| 1 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I enthusiastically glorify the wondrous pastimes of Lord Viṣṇu, who measured the three worlds in three steps; who is praised by those exalted sages who are fortunate to witness His deeds. This amazing Lord created all the worlds which are considered to be like specks of dust on His lotus-feet. He maintains the upper regions and makes them stay firm through His power.**

**Oh my Mind! You serve as the face of the Lord. You are the seat for the Lord. You have become the instrument to know our Lord. You serve as the firm seat made up of the stitching of nerves on both sides to hold Him firm.You have become the permanent place of residence of the Lord of infinite glories. Oh my mind! You are the property of the Lord.**

### Text 2

*tadasya priyamabhi pātho aśyām \| naro yatra deva yavo madanti \| urukramasya sahi bandhur ittha \| viṣṇo pade parame madhva utsaḥ \|\| 2 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May I attain His path, in which transcendentalists delight; (the path) of the wide-stepping Viṣṇu, in whose exalted association there is eternal happiness; for to such a degree is He a friend (of the pious).**

### Text 3

*pra tad viṣṇuḥ stavate vīryāya \| mrgo na bhīmaḥ kucaro giriṣṭhāḥ \| yasyo rūśu triṣu vikramaṇeṣu \| adhikṣiyanti bhuvanāni viśvā \|\| 3 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The all-pervading Viṣṇu dominates by His prowess like a powerful mountain lion. Within His three extended strides, all His creations rest. Just as an extraordinarily powerful lion strutting at the foot of the mountain jumps and reaches in a flash the top of the mountain, Our Lord extended His feet to measure all the Universe. Every one who wishes to attain glory eulogizes the Lord in this way.**

### Text 4

*paro mātrayā tanuvā vṛdhāna\| na te mahittva manvaṣnuvanti \| ubhe te vidma rajasi pṛthivyā viṣṇo deva tvaṁ \| paramasya vitse \|\| 4 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Expanding with a body beyond all measure, Your devotees cannot comprehend your magnitude. We know of the earth and the heavenly planets, but You O transcendental Viṣṇu, are of the highest abode.**

### Text 5

*vicakrame pṛthivīm eṣa etām \| kṣetrāya viṣṇor manuṣe daśasyan(e) \|*  
*dhruvāso asya kīrayo janāsaḥ \| urūkṣitiṁ sujanimā cakāra \|\|5 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With an object of giving a home to every man, the all-pervading traverses the earth in one mighty step . In His benevolence, the humble people repose their confidence for safety. He, who is the noble progenitor, has made spacious dwelling (for His people)". Those people who eulogize Him out of gratefulness for His compassion live blessed lives here.**

### Text 6

*trir devaḥ pṛthivīm eṣa etāṁ \| vicakrame śatarcasaṁ mahitvā \|*  
*pra viṣṇur astu tavasas tavīyān (e) \| tveṣaṁ-hyasyasthavirasya nāma \|\| 6 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In all His majesty, the Lord strides in three steps over this earth that is resplendent with a hundred splendors. May this all-pervading Viṣṇu, the strongest among the strong, rule over us. Sacred and unmatched in glory is the Name of this eternal Lord.**

### Text 7

*ato devā avantu no yato viṣṇur vicakrame \| pṛthivyāḥ sapta dhāmabhiḥ \|\| 7 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the Omnipresent Lord, along with nature's bounties, preserve us on that part of the Earth whence the Lord measured the seven regions. (This refers to that part of the earth on which the divine revelations came to men through seven metres of the Vedic verses).**

### Text 8

*idaṁ viṣṇur vicakrame tredhā nidadhe padam \|*  
*samuḍham asya pāgṁ sure \|\|8 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Omnipresent and the all-powerful Lord dominates over all the three regions, earth, mid-region and the heavens. His one step is deeply rooted in the deep dark mystery, beyond the knowledge of mankind.**

### Text 9

*trīṇi padā vicakrame viṣṇur gopā adābhyaḥ \| ato dharmāṇi dhārayan (e) \|\| 9 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Omnipresent Lord, the preserver of the indomitable, created all the three regions. He sustains and preserves the sanctity of all vital functions that keep life pulsating.**

### Text 10

*viṣṇoḥ karmāṇi paśyata yato vratāni paspaśe \| indrasya yujyaḥ sakhā \|\| 10 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Behold the marvelous creations of the Omnipresent Lord, who fulfills our noble aspirations. He is a true friend of the Soul.**

### Text 11

*tad viṣṇoḥ paramaṁ padaṁ sadā paśyanti sūrayaḥ \|*  
*divīva cakṣur ātatam \|\| 11 \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the suns rays in the sky are extended to the mundane vision, so in the same way the wise and learned devotees always see the abode of Lord Viṣṇu.**

### Text 12

*tad viprāso vipanyavo jāgṛvāṁ saḥ samīndhate \| viṣṇor yat paramaṁ padam \|\| 12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because those highly praiseworthy and spiritually awake devotees are able to see the spiritual world, they are also able to reveal that supreme abode of Lord Viṣṇu.**
