Sri Vraja-riti-cintamani – The Cintamani Jewel of Vraja – a transcendental tour to the spiritual Vrindavana.

Text One: Let me glorify Vrajabhumi, the place where Sri Krishna enjoys transcendental pastimes. Simply by drinking with their ears the nectarean descriptions of Vrajabhumi, the liberated souls abandon the bliss of impersonal Brahman and yearn to attain the land of Vraja.

Vrindavana is described here much different of the Vrindavana we will see with our external senses.

We may not be able to perceive the spiritual world with our present senses but we can take a tour in our mind and heart while relishing the contents of this nectarean book.

# Vraja-rīti-cintāmaṇi \[BS\]

## Chapter One

*yal loka-rīti-śruti-pīti-mātrāl*  
*lokottarātmānubhave pramodam \|*  
*muktvaiva muktāḥ spṛhayanti yasyai*  
*tāṁ kṛṣṇa-līlā-vraja-bhūmim īḍe \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I praise the land of Vraja where Kṛṣna performs his pastimes. Liberated souls, upon hearing the descriptions of that place and it inhabitants, give up the joy of realizing extraordinary Brahman and desire this place.**

*śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-rasa-svarūpam*  
*advaitam ānandam ihādi-nityam \|*  
*lokair amṛśyaṁ vraja-loka-vaśyaṁ*  
*bhajāmy avaśyaṁ śucito’py avaśyam \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I must here worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the form of rasa, who is incomparable bliss, without beginning and without end, who cannot be fully understood by anyone, but who is controlled by the people of Vraja, since he assumes the mood of Rādhā. Being most fallen, I must worship him because he is most purifying.**

*kāvyaṁ kaveḥ kāvyatayā prasiddhaṁ*  
*kāvyasya kāvyaṁ ca yato’pi kāvyam \|*  
*tad eva kāvyaṁ mama kāvya-kāvyaṁ*  
*vrajaika-kāvyaṁ param astu kāvyam \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May I be able to write excellent verses describing only Vraja, since such verses are the only poetry. A poet's verses become famous by poetic skill and become the essence of poetry when they concern Vraja.**

*caitanya-rūpodita-rītim ekām*  
*ekāntam abhyastum ahaṁ samīhe \|*  
*tat tat tadīyānugatokta-bhāvo*  
*bhāvocito dhyeya ito na kāvyam \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have a single desire to supplement the descriptions of Śrī Caitanya and Rūpa Gosvāmī. I should contemplate the mood of love depicted by Kṛṣna das Kavirāja and others as that is most suitable. Without this contemplation, it is not real poetry.**

*vaikuṇṭha-koṭyaś cita āsate’laṁ*  
*brahmāṇḍa-koṭyo’pi cito’cito’pi \|*  
*sarvatra vṛndāvanam etad eva*  
*yathā sa kṛṣṇaḥ pulināśanādau \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**What is the value of millions and millions of Vaikuṇṭhas, and millions and millions of universes? Everything exists in Vṛndāvana, just as Kṛṣṇa expands himself personally to be with each cowherd boy while eating lunch on the bank of the Yamunā.**

*idaṁ ghanībhūta-cid-eka-rūpaṁ*  
*vṛndāvanaṁ yatra vasanti sarve \|*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-līlā-parivāra-rūpā*  
*yathaiva kṛṣṇaḥ sa tathaiva te’pi \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This Vṛndāvana is a form of condensed knowledge. The associates in Kṛṣṇa's pastimes reside there, just as Kṛṣna resides there.**

*sarve ghanībhūta-cid-eka-rūpās*  
*tathāpy aho prākṛtavat pratītāḥ \|*  
*parasparaṁ ceṣṭitam apy amīṣāṁ*  
*vrajaika-līleti camatkaroti \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They all have forms of condensed knowledge, but they appear to be ordinary material beings. Their interactions, the pastimes of Vraja, create great astonishment.**

*yā pañcamāvasthiti-gāmi saṁvit*  
*tad-vṛtti-rūpās tad-ananya-vṛttyā \|*  
*jānanti kṛṣṇaṁ nijam eva bandhuṁ*  
*taṁ prākṛtam anya-janā na citram \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though they are filled with bliss and knowledge, by their great devotion, they know Kṛṣna only as their friend. It is not surprising that ordinary people think that Kṛṣṇa is a material being.**

*sarvottamānām api sarvataḥ syāt*  
*sva-nyūna-bhāvaḥ prabhu-bhakti-śaktyā \|*  
*pratyakṣam īkṣe vraja-loka etaṁ*  
*taṁ prākṛtam manyatame cid-arcye \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By the power of devotion to the Lord, the best devotees think themselves to be the lowest. I see this attitude directly in the people of Vraja, who, though the best persons, to be worshipped by all spiritual persons, think themselves to be material beings.**

*tenaiva tasyaiva vaśas tathaiva*  
*tatraiva nityaṁ vilasaty alaṁ saḥ \|*  
*ātyantikair eva rahasya-rasyai*  
*mādhurya-pūrair amita-prakāśyai \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Controlled by them because of their humility, he constantly plays with them in Vraja, with unlimited manifestations, filled with the highest, most intimate rasas, which produce the greatest sweetness.**

*ato’tra yā prākṛta-lokavat-tal-*  
*līlā paraiśvarya-viśeṣa-varṣā \|*  
*saivātirasyā cid-acit-praśasyā*  
*mādhurya-sasyā mahatām upāsyā \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**These pastimes which appear to be like those of the material world are a special shower of his highest powers. They are most relishable, praised by conscious and unconscious beings. They produce sweetness and are worshipped by great souls.**

*vṛndāvanaṁ gokula-dhāma-goṣṭhaṁ*  
*vrajaṁ ca nāmāni śubhāni yasya \|*  
*tadīya-lokīyam acintya-kṛtyaṁ*  
*vāñchāmi kiñcid dayayaiva teṣām \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By the mercy of the inhabitants of Vraja I desire to describe, know and attain, to a small degree, this place called Vṛndāvana, Gokula or Vraja, the people residing there, and their inconceivable activities.**

*tac cātyacintyaṁ cid-ananya-sāraṁ*  
*cid-anya-bhāsaṁ ca tad ekam eva \|*  
*avāntarāneka-vibhedam ekaṁ*  
*grāmā araṇyāni gavāṁ nivāsāḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This place is completely inconceivable, the essence of pure spirituality, but appears to be material. It is one spiritual entity, but has many divisions--villages, forests and sheds for the cows.**

*sarvasya kaścit prakaṭaḥ prakāśaḥ*  
*sadā bhṛśaṁ prākṛtavaj janādyaiḥ \|*  
*yaḥ prākṛtair eva yuto’pi līlā*  
*prākaṭya-kāle pṛthu-varṇyavat syāt \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some portions of this spiritual abode appear in the material world. The pastimes when manifested on earth always seem to be endowed with material people and will often be described as such, though they are not so.**

*tat-tac-chirobhūtam apāra-śobhaṁ*  
*nandīśvaraṁ sādhu-gaṇā vadanti \|*  
*nandīśvaraṁ taṁ ca yadīya-rūpaṁ*  
*śrī-nanda-rājālaye rājamānam \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The devotees speak of Nandīśvara, the topmost place, unsurpassed in splendor, and considered to be Śiva himself, where the palace of Nanda is situated.**

*yadīya-pūrvottara-dakṣiṇeṣu*  
*vasanti lokā hṛta-sarva-śokāḥ \|*  
*sānau puraḥ śrī-yuta-nanda-rāja-*  
*purī purāṇām ataḥ purāṇā \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**People devoid of all suffering reside to the east, north and south of the hill. At the front of the summit is the city of King Nanda, endowed with all wealth. It is said to be eternal in the Purāṇas.**

*prācīra-rājī racitā-masārair*  
*gṛhāny alaṁ mārakatāni yasyāḥ \|*  
*stambhāḥ pravālaiḥ paṭalāni hemnā*  
*yeṣāṁ vṛttiḥ sā sphaṭikair vibhāti \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The walls of the city are made of sapphires. The houses are made of emeralds. The pillars are made of coral, the roofs are made of gold and the windows are made of crystal.**

*vaidurya-jātā vaḍabhī-samūhā*  
*mahā-maho-nīla-maṇīndra-jāṭṭāḥ \|*  
*mahā-pratīhāra-varo’pi yasyāḥ*  
*tat kauravindo vilasaty alaṁ sā \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The pinnacles are made of vaidurya. The towers are made of the brightest sapphires. The huge, excellent gate is made of rubies.**

*mukhya-prakoṣṭhe caturālaye’syā*  
*bhāṇḍāra-gehaṁ varuṇasya diśyam \|*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-vāsaḥ śubha-dakṣiṇasthaḥ*  
*śrī-rāma-dhāmottara-diśy udeti \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The main courtyard of the city is surrounded by four buildings. To the west is the storehouse. To the south is Kṛṣṇa's residence. To the north is Balarāma's residence.**

*prācyāṁ gṛhaṁ tādṛśam eva yatra*  
*prācyāṁśa yasyānyatara-prakoṣṭhe \|*  
*sva-putra-bhadrāya nijeṣṭa-devaṁ*  
*nārāyaṇaṁ sevata eva nandaḥ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the eastern side is a building like Kṛṣṇa's, with a courtyard on its eastern side. There Nanda worships Nārāyaṇa, his deity, for the welfare of his son.**

*kośālayasyānvita-dakṣiṇāṁśe*  
*kṛṣṇasya dhāmnaḥ śubha-paścime’sti \|*  
*yā pāka-śālā dvaya-madhya eva*  
*viśrāma-dhāmānuru rādhikāyāḥ \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**South of the store house and west of Kṛṣṇa’s house, between the kitchen and Kṛṣṇa's house is a place where Rādhā takes a short rest.**

*kṛṣṇasya dhāmno’nvita-dakṣiṇāṁśe*  
*pākālayasyāpi virājamānaḥ \|*  
*ārāma āste sarasī ca yatra*  
*raho manojñaṁ bahu-geha-vediḥ \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**South of Kṛṣṇa's place and the kitchen is a garden with a pond where there is a pavilion with many rooms. The place is attractive and private.**

*yat pārśvayoḥ santi purāṇi-goduhāṁ*  
*bhūrīṇi cārūṇi samāny alaṁ tayā \|*  
*paścāt tu nandīśvara eva rājate*  
*nandīśvaro’yaṁ satataṁ sa vandate \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the eastern and southern sides of Nanda's palace are many attractive cowherd houses similar to Nanda's house. West is Nandīśvara hill. Śiva constantly worships this hill.**

*teṣām api prānta-gatātyagamyā*  
*prācīra-rājī maṇi-jātajātā \|*  
*ratnālaye ratnaja-dehalīke*  
*muktādi-ratnāvali toraṇālī \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The houses have exterior walls difficult to pass, made of gems. The arches of gem studded terraces and houses are covered with pearls and other gems.**

*yān īkṣamānā nigadanti bhūyo*  
*vimāna-vāsān iti martya-lokāḥ \|*  
*vaikuṇṭha-vāsān iti nāka-nāthāḥ*  
*vaikuṇṭhagās te rasa-sāra-sāram \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing these houses, humans praise them as houses of devatās. The devatās praise them as houses of the inhabitants of Vaikuṇṭha and the inhabitants of Vaikuṇṭha praise them as the essence of all sweetness.**

*puraḥ pratīhāra-varasya yasyāḥ*  
*samantataḥ pārśva-yugasya rasyāḥ \|*  
*purohitānāṁ ca purohitānāṁ*  
*tataḥ pareṣāṁ ca tataḥ pareṣām \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The fronts of the houses are attractive with two guards at each of the doors in the four directions. Beyond the cowherd villages are the towns of the best priests. Beyond that are the houses of other excellent men who protect the town.**

*tataḥ pareṣāṁ ca puraḥ krameṇa*  
*śreṇī mukhānāṁ parītaḥ parītāḥ \|*  
*tataś ca vīthī krama-paṇya-vīthi*  
*vīthī ca madhye parato hi vīthī \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Surrounding this are the excellent merchants' houses. Surrounding this is a main road, other roads used as market places, and many alleys between.**

*prānteṣu yasyā nagarasya rasyāḥ*  
*śṛṅgāṭakākhyān abhito’bhitas tāḥ \|*  
*śreṇī kṛtāḥ sūtra-nipāta-pātā*  
*iva pratītā bahu paṇya-vīthyaḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are many crossroads at the ends of the attractive roads. It is as if the series of shopping streets had been laid out using straight threads as markers.**

*nānā-maṇīnāṁ ghaṭitā ghaṭāni*  
*lasat-patākā vara-mauktikānām \|*  
*prālambakāntās taruvad vasante*  
*pravāla-vāra-praghaṇāḥ vipaṇyaḥ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The shops have pots made of jewels and shining flags. The shops seem to be spring trees with straight trunks, beautified with hanging rows of pearls. The shops are like trees abundant with excellent buds, with doors make of the best coral.**

*kācid vasanta-śriya eva yadvan*  
*nānā-prasūnair atisaurabhās tāḥ \|*  
*kaścin mahā-śaila-varā ivālaṁ*  
*nānā-vidha-dravya-sugandhi-gandhāḥ \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some shops have the beauty of spring, being fragrant with various types of flowers. Some shops are like great mountains endowed with fragrant perfumes and various items.**

*kāścin maṇīnāṁ khanayo yathā vā*  
*nānā-maṇi-dyotita-dīpyamānāḥ \|*  
*kāścid vilāsi pravarā ivānyāḥ*  
*kastūrikā-kuṅkuma-mukha-gandhāḥ \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some shops, lit with shining jewels, are like jewel mines. Other shops, like the best of lovers, are fragrant, chiefly with musk and kuṅkuma.**

*ānanda-vṛndāvana-rīti-leśaṁ*  
*veśaṁ vidhātuṁ vacasāṁ kavīnām \|*  
*vīṇām iva spṛṣṭum ayogyatānāṁ*  
*tān āṁśavat kiṁcana sūcayāni \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I will describe a particle of the activities of blissful Vṛndāvana in order produce beauty, using poetic words. But it will be incomplete, like an inexperienced person playing the vīṇā.**

*sve sve sarasy eva hi yatra matsara*  
*iti prayogaḥ kriyate kvacij janaiḥ \|*  
*strī-mekhalādau khala ity udīryate*  
*yat komalādau mala-śabda ucyate \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They use the word** ***matsara*** **to mean "my lake" (instead of envy). They use the world** ***khala*** **to indicate a woman's belt (instead of a rascal). They use the word** ***mala*** **to indicate soft (instead of contamination).**

*pradoṣa-doṣākara-roṣa-moṣa-*  
*doṣādi-śabda-śrutir asti yatra \|*  
*sāyaṁ śaśāṅka-praṇayākhya-keli-*  
*niśādi keṣv eva kadācid eva \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

***Pradoṣa*** **means evening (not wicked).** ***Doṣākar*****a means the moon (not a heap of faults). The word** ***roṣa*** **(anger) expresses love. The word** ***moṣa*** **(thief) is used to indicate play. The word** ***doṣa*** **(fault) simply means the evening.**

*chatrādi-daṇḍe śubha-cāmarādi-*  
*daṇḍe ca daṇḍe śrutir asti yatra \|*  
*nīvyādi-keśādika eva bandhaḥ*  
*samādhi-yogādika ādhi-śabdaḥ \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The word** ***daṇḍa*** **(punishment) is used to indicate the handle of an umbrella or a cāmara. The word** ***bandha*** **(bondage) is used to indicate tying the hair with a string or tying the waist with a belt. The word** ***ādhi*** **(anxiety) is used to indicate concentration in yoga.**

*kastūrikā-kuṅkuma-candanādi-*  
*paṅkeṣu paṅka-śravaṇaṁ ca yatra \|*  
*kauṭilyam āste vara-kuṇḍalādau*  
*kāṭhinyam apy asti śilādikeṣu \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The word** ***paṅka*** **(mud) is used to indicate the paste of musk, kuṅkuma or sandalwood. The word** ***kauṭilyam*** **(crooked) is used to indicate the shape of the best earrings.** ***Kāṭhinyam*** **(hardness) refers only to the hardness of a stone.**

*puṣpādi-dhulīṣu rajaḥ prayujyate*  
*yatrāndhakāre tama ity udīryate \|*  
*dvandvaṁ ca yugme pavane ca mandatā*  
*cāñcalyam āste hari-lokanāya \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

***Rajas*** **(passion) is used to indicate the pollen of flowers.** ***Tamas*** **(ignorance) indicates darkness of night.** ***Dvandvam*** **(duality) means a couple. The word** ***mandatā*** **(laziness) indicates the speed of the breeze. The word** ***cāṅcalyam*** **(unsteadiness) describes the condition when eager to see Kṛṣṇa.**

*madhyādike kṣīṇaḥ pada-prayogo*  
*yatrodakeṣv eva ca nīcagatvam \|*  
*viṣāda-dainya-śrama-moha-nidrā-*  
*lasyādayo’pi vyabhicāri-bhāve \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The word** ***kṣīṇa*** **(thin) refers to the waist of a gopī.**  ***Nīcatatvam*** **(inferiority) refers to a shallow pond. Words like lamentation, misery, fatigue, bewilderment, sleep and laziness refer only the vyabhicāri-bhāva symptoms.**

*jānanti gā eva hi kāma-dhenuḥ*  
*sāmānya vṛkṣān iti kalpa-vṛkṣān \|*  
*cintāmaṇīn yatra śilāvad eva*  
*vrajasya kā sampadato hi vācyāḥ \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The word cow means a kāma-dhenu for the people of Vraja. The ordinary trees are kalpa-vṛkṣas. The stones are all cintāmaṇi.**

*yadīya-yoṣij-jana-saubhagīya-*  
*śobhāti-śobhāt śrutayo’nvayus tāḥ \|*  
*yan-mādhurīṁ vīkṣya ramā mumoha*  
*vrajasya tasyāstu kim atra varṇyam \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because of the great fortune of the women of Vraja, the śrutis began to follow those women. Seeing the sweetness of the women of Vraja, Lakṣmī became enchanted. What else can be said about Vraja?**

*yatraiva śuddha-praṇaya-svabhāvā*  
*premnaiva jānanti paraṁ sva-bandhum \|*  
*lokottarā lokavad eva taṁ ca*  
*svaṁ svaṁ ca tatrāsti na kiṁ vicitram \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vraja the people, endowed with natural, pure love, see the Supreme Lord as their friend out of prema. Though they are in the highest position, they see Kṛṣṇa and themselves to be ordinary people. Is this not surprising?**

*guṇās tu te prākṛta-dharma-śūnyā*  
*doṣā api syū rasa eva mānyāḥ \|*  
*nanda-vraje kautukam eva sarvaṁ*  
*kharvaṁ vidhatte triguṇo’tha garvam \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Their qualities are devoid of material aspects. What appear to be faults are actually components of rasa. Everything in Vraja is astonishing. The three guṇas become devoid of material pride.**

*smṛtītihāsākhya-purāṇa-vede*  
*santi pramāṇāni paraṁ tv ihāsya \|*  
*traikālikopāsaka-loka-sākṣāt-*  
*kārāt sadedṛk-sthitir eva nityā \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There is proof of what has been stated here in the smṛtis, itihāsas, Purāṇas and Vedas. The position of Kṛṣṇa and his devotees in Vraja is eternal and is confirmed by the realization of devotees in past, present and future.**

*nityaiva sarvā yadi kṛṣṇa-līlā*  
*tathāpy anityaiva matāpi kaiścit \|*  
*adeyatāto’tirahasyatā tasya*  
*svecchayeti pravadanti tajjñāḥ \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though Kṛṣṇa's pastimes are eternal, some say they are not eternal. The wise explain that this misconception arises because of the will of the Lord, since this knowledge should not be given to unqualified people, being most confidential.**

*yatraiva vātsalya-raso’sti mūrtaḥ*  
*śarīra-dhārīva viśuddha-sattvam \|*  
*saubhāgya-sāro nija-rāja-dhānyāṁ*  
*śrī-nanda-nāmā vraja-rāja-rājaḥ \|\|45\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vraja, Nanda, the supreme king of Vraja, resides in his palace. Though he seems to have a material body, it is viśuddha-sattva in nature. Nanda is the personification of vātsalya-rasa and the essence of auspiciousness.**

*śrī-kṛṣṇa-pitre pitṛ-bhāva-bhāvaṁ*  
*jānanti sarve sva-pitṛ-svabhāvam \|*  
*śrī-nanda-rājaṁ vraja-madhya-rājaṁ*  
*sad-eka-rūpaṁ ca sad-eka-rūpam \|\|46\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All understand that Nanda acts affectionately as the father of Kṛṣna and, as the king of Vraja, acts as their father as well. He has one nature and one form, eternally.**

*yasyāsti gehe saha dharma-cāriṇī*  
*cit-kalpa-vallīva śarīra-dhāriṇī \|*  
*vātsalya-sampatti-rasa-svarūpiṇī*  
*yasyāḥ phalaṁ śrī-bhagavān svayaṁ maṇiḥ \|\|47\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Nanda's house resides Yaśodā, his faithful wife. With her spiritual body, she is like a spiritual wish-fulfilling vine. She is the form of the greatest vātsalya-rasa. From her arose the jewel of Svayam Bhagavan as the fruit.**

*yā śrī-yaśodā jagatī yaśodā*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-vātsalya-rasaika-modā \|*  
*tam eva pāśena babandha roṣāt*  
*tat-prema citraṁ vavṛdhe’titoṣām \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She gives fame to the universe with her exclusive maternal devotion to Kṛṣṇa. In anger she bound him with a rope. Since he was very satisfied with this, her astonishing prema increased.**

*yatrāsate gopa-gaṇā agaṇyāḥ*  
*kecid vrajeśasya sa piṇḍa-bandhāḥ \|*  
*sambandha-gandhā api ke’pi teṣāṁ*  
*snehānubandhā akhilā mukunde \|\|49\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vraja reside innumerable cowherds. Some are related through ancestry to Nanda. Some are distantly related to him. But all are bound with affection to Mukunda.**

*sarve satāṁ dharma-viśeṣa-mūrtayaḥ*  
*patnyo’pi teṣām iva bhakti-vṛttayaḥ \|*  
*putrāś ca keṣāṁcana kṛṣṇa-sakhyakāḥ*  
*kanyāś ca keṣāṁcana kṛṣṇa-kāmakāḥ \|\|50\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All of these devotees are forms of the highest bhakti. Their wives are absorbed in bhakti. The sons of some of them are Kṛṣṇa's friends and the daughters of some are Kṛṣṇa's beloveds.**

*ye tat sakhā tat savayor vapuṣkaḥ*  
*guṇaiś ca sarvair api tat-samānāḥ \|*  
*nityaṁ kumārāḥ sanakādivat te*  
*tac-cittatāyāḥ kim aśakyatās te \|\|51\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who are his friends are of the same age and body. They are similar to him in all qualities. They are eternally young like the Kumāras. Since they are absorbed in him, what is impossible for them?**

*kecit sakhāyo’tirahasya-mānyā*  
*rahasya-sevāsv api ke’pi dhanyāḥ \|*  
*sakhī-samānāḥ subalojjvalādyāḥ*  
*sadojjvalā ujjvala-dṛṣṭim āḍhyāḥ \|\|52\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some of the friends are revered for their intimate relationship with Kṛṣna. Others are fortunate to sometimes engage in more confidential service. Subala, Ujjvala and others are like sakhīs. They are completely pure and engaged in seeing mādhurya-rasa.**

*yāḥ kṛṣṇa-kāntāḥ sakalā padābja-*  
*nakhāśu-koṭi-jita-koṭi-candrāḥ \|*  
*saubhāgya-sārātula-kīrti-pārā*  
*vārormi-vārāpluta-veda-vārāḥ \|\|53\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The tip of the ray from one toe nail of a gopī defeats millions of moons. The waves from the ocean of their incomparable qualities, the essence of all auspiciousness, drowns all the Vedas.**

*yābhiḥ svayaṁ prāpta-vara-prasāda-*  
*leśasya yogyā na babhūva lakṣmīḥ \|*  
*nāyaṁ śriyo’ṅga iti yan niruktaṁ*  
*śrīmac-chukenāpi mahā-purāṇe \|\|54\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lakṣmī was not qualified to attain a particle of the great mercy attained by these gopīs. Śukadeva states this in Bhāgavatam with the verse** ***nāyaṁ śriyo ‘ṅga*****. (SB** [**10.47.60**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/sb-10/47.60)**)**

*yāsāṁ na kāpi prabhunāpi śeke*  
*tyaktuṁ vilāsāya kanīyasī yā \|*  
*yāvatya eva vraja-yoṣitas tās*  
*tāvantam ātmānam ataḥ sa cakre \|\|55\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Even the most junior among the gopīs could not give up playing with the Lord. He manifested himself personally for each of the Vraja gopīs.**

*yāsāṁ guṇair eva gṛhīta-cetā*  
*yābhiḥ sa reme bhagavān svayaṁ yaḥ \|*  
*tathaiva tasyaiva guṇais tam ātmā*  
*rāmā bhajante pramadānucittāḥ \|\|56\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord enjoyed with the gopīs whose qualities attracted his heart, though ātmārāmas, attracted by his qualities, worship the Lord.**

*tāsāṁ śiraḥ sad-guṇa-ratna-mālikā*  
*premāndhayārāma-suvarṇa-ketakī \|*  
*mādhurya-dhārā-dhara-vidyud-udyatā*  
*vidyotate śrī-vṛṣabhānu-nandinī \|\|57\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā, daughter of Vṛṣabhānu, shines like the center jewel in the garland of good qualities. Blinded by prema, she shines like a golden ketakī in the flower garden of the gopīs. She shines like lightning in a cloud of sweetness.**

*ānanda-candrodita-kaumudī yā*  
*śrī-mohanasyāpi sumohana-śrīḥ \|*  
*saundarya-nāmno nikaṣopalasya*  
*suvarṇa-rekhā vṛṣabhānu-kanyā \|\|58\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is light arising from the moon of bliss. Her beauty bewilders even Kṛṣna, who bewilders Lakṣmī. The daughter of Vṛṣabhānu is the golden line of the testing stone of beauty.**

*lāvaṇya-pātho-nidhi-sāra-sampat-*  
*kalā-kalāpākara-bhūmir ekā \|*  
*guṇākhya-ratnaugha-khaniḥ prasiddhā*  
*śrī-rādhikā śrī-vraja-candra-kāntāḥ \|\|59\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is the greatest treasure in the ocean of beauty. She is the sole source of all arts. She is famous as the source of all gem-like qualities. Śrī Rādhikā is the beloved of Kṛṣna, the moon of Vraja.**

*gaurī sahasrād adhikāpi gaurī*  
*śyāmā tathāpi śrutiṣu prasiddhā \|*  
*surūpiṇī yāpy asurūpiṇī ca \|*  
*sakhī kadambasya vibhāti rādhā \|\|60\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**She is fairer that thousands of fair women. She is known as Śyāmā in the Vedas. Rādhā has a beautiful form (surūpinī) and is the life of all her friends (asu-rūpinī).**

*kecit parām eva vadanti lakṣmīṁ*  
*līleti kecit kila tāntrikāyām \|*  
*ānandinī śaktir iti śrutiśāḥ*  
*śrī-rādhikābhā vraja-candra-kāntāḥ \|\|61\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some say that she is the supreme Lakṣmī. Others say that she is Līlā-śakti, according to Pañcarātra scriptures. The masters of śrutis say she is the śakti of bliss. Splendorous Rādhā is beloved of the moon of Vraja.**

*yasyā vaśe tasya tu sarva-śaktiḥ*  
*sarvaiva līlā sakalā guṇāś ca \|*  
*saundarya-mādhurya-vidagdhatādyāḥ*  
*sā rādhikā rājati kṛṣṇa-kāntā \|\|62\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All Kṛṣṇa's śaktis, all his pastimes and all his qualities, all his beauty, sweetness and skill, are under her control. Rādhikā shines as the beloved of Kṛṣṇa.**

*yasyā lasan-mādana-bhāva-vaśyā*  
*līlā rasāsvāda-viśeṣa-rasyāḥ \|*  
*kṛṣṇasya nityā vilasanty anantāḥ*  
*sā rādhikā rājati kṛṣṇa-kāntā \|\|63\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her eternal, infinite, shining pastimes with Kṛṣṇa, most attractive with the special flavor of rasa, are subject to brilliant mādana-bhāva (in mahābhāva). Śrī Rādhikā thus shines as the lover of Kṛṣṇa.**

*yathaiva sarvair guṇa-rūpa-kelī*  
*mādhurya-pūrair ati pūrṇa eva \|*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-candraḥ sa tathaiva rasyā*  
*sārādhikā rājati rādhikā sā \|\|64\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa is full of all sweetness, pastimes, beauty, and qualities, but attractive Rādhikā radiates even greater qualities.**

*yasyā viśākhā lalitādayas tāḥ*  
*sakhyaḥ priya-rūpa-guṇaiḥ samānāḥ \|*  
*mānena yasyā api yāḥ samānā*  
*yā vīkṣya rādhā nija-śarma veda \|\|65\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Her sakhīs such as Viśākhā and Lalitā have equally sweet forms and qualities. Respecting them all, when she sees one of them she experiences great joy.**

*yasyāḥ suhṛt-pakṣatayā prasiddhā*  
*śyāmeti nāmā guṇato’pi yā sā \|*  
*yadīya-sauhārda-kṛte vidhatte*  
*nityaṁ prayatnam ati-mādhurībhiḥ \|\|66\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Śyāmā is famous for her friendship with Rādhā and her qualities. She strives constantly to perform friendly actions with utmost intimacy.**

*yābhyāṁ yayor nāma vidhāya*  
*nānā-śilpena kalpyāmbara-bhūṣaṇādi \|*  
*sampreṣitaṁ yat sahasā na veda*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-candro’pi kim anya-vārtā \|\|67\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Rādhā and Śyāmā skillfully made various ornaments and clothing and presented them to Kṛṣṇa, Rādhā said that Śyāmā made them and Śyāma said that Rādhā made them. Kṛṣṇa could not immediately detect which of the two made the ornaments and clothing.**

*premṇe priyasyāram upekṣitaṁ*  
*yat lokeṣu lajjādi tadaiva bhūyaḥ \|*  
*premṇi pragāḍhe tad-apekṣaṇaṁ tāḥ*  
*kurvanti citraṁ kila kṛṣṇa-kāntāḥ \|\|68\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the gopīs immediately gave up shame in front of society because of prema for Kṛṣṇa, they depended even more on him with deeper love. This is most astonishing.**

*yatrāsate sāttvata-śuddha-dharmā*  
*mūrtā ivorvī diviṣad-vareṇyāḥ \|*  
*tad-dharma-mātra-pratipādi-veda-*  
*vaktāra eke rata-pāñcarātrāḥ \|\|69\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The learned, best brāhmaṇas, like personifications of pure Vaiṣṇava dharma, speak the Vedas ,which inculcate devotion to the Lord. Some are followers of Pañcarātra tradition.**

*pratigrahaṁ ye vraja-rāja-dāna-*  
*mātrasya kurvanti tad-eka-yājyāḥ \|*  
*kecit paraiśvarya-parāḥ pare ca*  
*mādhurya-dhūryā vraja-rāja-sūnoḥ \|\|70\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Receiving donations only from Nanda, they perform worship only for him. Some are attracted to the powerful aspect of Kṛṣṇa and others are attracted to Kṛṣṇa's sweetness.**

*tāmbūlikās tailika-mālikādyāś*  
*caitanya-rūpā api nāra-dharmāḥ \|*  
*te nāra-dharmā api devatānāṁ*  
*durlabhya-lābhā vraja-candra-bhāvāḥ \|\|71\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The betel nut sellers, oil merchants and garland makers have spiritual forms with human characteristics. They appearing to be human, endowed with love for Kṛṣṇa. They have attained positions impossible for devatās.**

*gavāṁ gṛhāṇām api yatra bhittiś*  
*catuś-catuṣkaṁ sphaṭikākhya-ratnaiḥ \|*  
*gopānasī mārakatī ca vaṁśyāḥ*  
*svarṇasya koṇeṣu tathā mahāntaḥ \|\|72\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vraja the four walls of the cow sheds in four directions are made of crystal. The roof frames are made of emeralds and the huge cross beams at the angles are made of gold.**

*gopānasīnāṁ pṛthu yac catuṣkaṁ*  
*tat kauṇikaṁ yatra tu kauruvindam \|*  
*mahāvaḍabhyo yata eva lagnā*  
*nānā-maṇīnāṁ paṭalāni yatra \|\|73\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The four corners of the frame are made of rubies. The roofs, covered with various jewels, have huge spires.**

*yeṣāṁ lasanty aṅgaṇa eva gāvaḥ*  
*sarasvatī mūrtivad eva śuklāḥ \|*  
*manaḥ samānā avaśā vicitraṁ*  
*tapasvi-saṅghā iva suvratāś ca \|\|74\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**White cows, like incarnations of Sarasvatī, play in the enclosure. It is surprising that though uncontrollable like the mind, the cows are as steady as ascetics.**

*cintāmaṇi-vyūha-samāś ca kāma-*  
*dughā nidāghā iva phulla-vatsāḥ \|*  
*sat-kāvyavat sundara-bhūri-varṇāḥ*  
*veṇu-svanāyaiva sadorddhva-karṇāḥ \|\|75\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The wish-fulfilling cows are like an array of cintāmaṇi. The joyful calves are like the summer season. Their many beautiful sounds and their various colors are like the best poetry. They prick up their ears at all times to hear the sound of Kṛṣṇa's flute.**

*yatraiva bhūmau patitāḥ pratītāḥ*  
*sa-jīva-garbhā iva kaumudīnām \|*  
*kailāśa-śailasya śila-śikhaṇḍāḥ*  
*sañcāri śilā iva saṁlasanti \|\|76\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The calves appear to be the children of moon light who have fallen to earth. They appear to be scattered rocks which have fallen from the peak of Mount Kailāśa.**

*hiṇḍīra-khaṇḍā iva dugdha-sindhor*  
*ye granthivac chrī-hara-hāsa-jātāḥ \|*  
*viśuddha-sattvasya ca māṁsa-piṇḍa-*  
*tulyā bhramanto bhuvi bhūri-vatsāḥ \|\|77\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The calves are like particles of foam from the milk ocean or like the laughter of Śiva which has formed into knots. The multitudes of calves are piles of śraddhā offerings in pure goodness as they wander over the ground.**

*kiṁ gaṇḍa-śailāḥ sphaṭikācalasya*  
*mahormayaḥ kiṁ tu mahā-payodheḥ \|*  
*sāyaṁ gṛhā ye munivac ca jīvan-*  
*muktā iva svaira-cirā hi ṣaṇḍāḥ \|\|78\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Are the bulls large rocks from a crystal mountain? Are they huge waves of the Milk Ocean? Entering the stalls in the evening, they are like calm sages, and wandering at their will, they are like liberated souls.**

*yatrāsate garva-dharā ivaite*  
*hambād akārā gala-kambalāś ca \|*  
*virakta-lokā iva puṅgavās te*  
*mattā iva stabdha-suśoṇa-netrāḥ \|\|79\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Mooing loudly, they appear to be full of pride. With folds of skin hanging from their necks, appearing like blankets, they appear to be renunciates. With their glaring red eyes, they appear to be intoxicated.**

*nandīśvarasya priya-dakṣiṇasthas*  
*tat saudaraḥ sodaravac cakāsti \|*  
*yatrālayaḥ śrī-vṛṣabhānu-nāmno*  
*nāmnoditas tasya dharādhirājaḥ \|\|80\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**South of Nandīśvara is a similar hill, like a brother. There lies the house of Vṛṣabhānu. The hill is named after him also.**

*śrī-nanda-rājaḥ sa yathā tathā*  
*sa vrajasya rājā vṛṣabhānu-tejāḥ \|*  
*purīva tasyaiva purī ca tasya*  
*putrī ca putraś ca tayor yaśolam \|\|81\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛṣabhānu is like Nanda. Vṛṣabhānu's power is the same as Nanda's. The palace of Vṛṣabhānu is similar to Nanda's palace. The fame of his daughter is equal to the fame of Kṛṣṇa.**

*nandīśvaraḥ śrī-vṛṣabhānu-śaila-*  
*madhye tu mad-dhyeyatama-svarūpam \|*  
*saṅketa-nāmāspadam eva śaṅke*  
*premaiva tad-dvandva-varasya mūrtam \|\|82\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Midway between Nandīśvara and Mount Vṛṣabhānu is the place named Saṅketa, upon which I meditate to the utmost. I think it is the personification of Rādhā’s and Kṛṣṇa's prema.**

*nandīśvarasyeśvara-koṇakande*  
*paryanta-bhūmau vidhu-hṛdya-nāmā \|*  
*tasyaiva cūḍer vasu yāvaṭākhyā*  
*pallī-maṇīnāṁ ghaṭitā ghaṭābhiḥ \|\|83\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**North east of Nandīśvara, in the direction giving joy, adjacent to it, is place called Vidhu-hrḍya. The village called Yāvaṭa, produced by pots of jewels, is the wealth of that place.**

*purī praṇītā vṛṣabhānu-nāmnā*  
*nirmāya yatrollasitā sutāyai*  
*yan-mandirasyoparigā svakāntaṁ*  
*sā lokate tāṁ sa nijālayordhvāt \|\|84\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vṛṣabhānu built an attractive palace in Yāvaṭa and gave it to his daughter. Going to its upper stories, she would gaze at her lover and he would gaze at her from the upper stories of his house.**

*yadā gurūṇāṁ guruṇādareṇa*  
*dareṇa ca vyagratamau purāntaḥ \|*  
*parasparaṁ tarhi vilokamānau*  
*mānaucitāt sva-sva-puroparisthau \|\|85\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When they were agitated out of great respect for and fear of their elders in their houses, standing on the top of their palaces they would gaze at each other.**

*saṅketya yatra priyayā vilasya*  
*prollasya rasyasya baṭasya mūle \|*  
*yāvais tad-aṅghrī racayāṁ cakāra*  
*nāmnāpi taṁ yāvaṭaṁ cakāra \|\|86\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There, at the base of an attractive banyan tree (vaṭa) he would meet with his beloved, play, and move about. He would decorate her feet with lac (yāva). For that reason the place is named Yāvaṭa.**

*śrī-kṛṣṇena kṛpā-bhareṇa guruṇā caitanya-rūpeṇa yā*  
*tal-līlā-rasa-dhāma dhāma-janitā rītiḥ prakāśaṁ gatā \|*  
*tasyā leśā-viśeṣa-veśa-manane śrī-rīti-cintāmaṇau*  
*sargo’yaṁ prathamo’bhavad bhuva-mude nandīśvaroddeśataḥ \|\|87\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By the great mercy of Kṛṣṇa, in the form of my guru Caitanya Mahāprabhu, the conduct of the people of Vraja and the place of the rasas which arise from pastimes has been manifested. The first chapter of Vraja-rīti-cintāmaṇi, which has been written for understanding a small particle of the activities of Vraja, written for the pleasure of the world, describing Nandīśvāra, is now concluded.**

## Chapter Two

*evaṁ vidhaḥ śrī-vraja-rāji-rājo*  
*nandīśvaro’yaṁ parito vanāni \|*  
*nānā-vidhā yeṣu lasanti vṛkṣā*  
*vallyo nikuñjāś ca vihaṅga-raṅgāḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In this way Nandīśvara shines among the fields of Vraja. Around the hill are forests. In the forest there are many types of trees, vines, and bowers where birds play.**

*yatrodgalad-guggula-picchileṣu*  
*vartmasv aṭavyāḥ pracaranti \|*  
*parasparaṁ baddha-karā nijeśa-*  
*yugasya līlā-samayānusārāḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There, the vana-devīs wander on the forest paths among crying peacocks, holding each others' hands while following after Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa as they perform their pastimes.**

*yatrāṭavī-meṣa-mukhād udīrṇa-*  
*saṁjīrṇa-kakkola-phalaiḥ sugandhi \|*  
*diśāṁ mukhaṁ tan-mahiṣaugha-śṛṅga-*  
*kṣuṇṇa-tvag-ādyaiḥ śaralādibhiś ca \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All directions are made fragrant by the kakkola berries crushed by the mouths of wild rams and by pine bark rubbed by the horns of wild buffalo.**

*yatrāṭavī-hasti-ghaṭā-vibhagna-*  
*sac-challako pallavikā vibhānti \|*  
*vanīya-dhenu-gaṇa-jaggha-gandha-*  
*tṛṇaiḥ sugandhīni diśāṁ mukhāni \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the forest, the fresh leaves of the śallaka trees are broken by troops of wild elephants. The directions become fragrant with the aromatic grasses eaten by wild cows.**

*kutrāpi kīśaiḥ kavalī kṛtānāṁ*  
*drākṣā-phalānāṁ bahu-guccha-gucchaiḥ \|*  
*sac-chāditāni cchavim ārabhante*  
*bhuvas-talāny āmra-phalādibhiś ca \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places the ground is covered with clusters of grapes devoured by monkeys and fallen mangos shine radiantly.**

*sat-karṇa-bhūṣā maricādi-gucchāḥ*  
*pulinda-kāntāḥ pracaranti yatra \|*  
*tāmbūla-pūlaiḥ kara-bhaṅga-sadyaḥ*  
*karpūra-rambhā-rasa-vāsitāsyāḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There, the Pulinda women wander about with attractive ear ornaments, carrying pepper clusters. Their faces are perfumed with betel and freshly crushed camphor mixed with plantain juice.**

*rasāla-mālā-panaśāvalībhir*  
*āmrātakaiḥ śrī-phala-jambubhiś ca \|*  
*palāśa-pūgāsana-nārikelair*  
*madhuka-punnāga-yugaiḥ śirīśaiḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are groves of mango, jack fruit, hog plum, bilva, rose apple, palāśa, betel nut, asana, coconut, madhuka, two types of punnāga, and śirīṣa trees.**

*priyā bilvārjuna-karmaraṅgaiḥ*  
*kapittha-lodhra-priyakākhya-yugmaiḥ \|*  
*lavaly-aśokair lakucaiḥ karīrair*  
*yūthī-yugaiś campaka-yugmakaiś ca \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are piyāla, bilva, arjuna, karmaraṅga, kapittha, lodhra, and two types of priyaka. There are lavalī, aśoka, bread fruit, karīra, two types of jasmine and campaka.**

*tamāla-mālā-nava-mālikābhir*  
*lavaṅga-jāti-dvaya-rāya-bilvaiḥ \|*  
*koṣātakī-parkaṭikā-baṭādyair*

**Перевод:**

**There are groves of tamāla trees, nava-mālikā, clove, two types of nutmeg, rāyabilva, kośātakī, parkaṭikā, banyan, aśvattha, śala and khadira trees.**

*nānollasat-puṣpa-phalodya-citrair*  
*vṛkṣaiḥ samantād bahuśo vanāni \|*  
*nānā-vidhaiś cāru-latā samūhaiḥ*  
*śārī-śukādyaiḥ paśubhiś ca bhānti \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The forests are resplendent with trees bearing colorful fruits and flowers. The forests are radiant with various types of attractive creepers and birds like śāris and parrots.**

*sitāsitair lohita-pīta-miśraiḥ*  
*kahlāra-padmotpala-puṣpa-jātaiḥ \|*  
*taḍāga-vāpī-sarasī-nadībhī*  
*rājanti haṁsādi-gaṇaiś ca yāni \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The forests shine with rivers, ponds canals and lakes which are filled with water lilies and white, blue, red, yellow, and white lotuses, and which are inhabited by swans and other birds.**

*karpūra-dhūlī-raciteva bhūmī*  
*kvacit kvacit kuṅkuma-bhāviteva \|*  
*kastūrikā cūrṇa-kṛteva kācit*  
*bhūmīva bhūmī kvacid arcya gandhā \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places the ground seems to be made of camphor dust, in some places of kuṅkuma, in some places of powdered musk and scents used during worship. In some places it looks like ordinary earth.**

*kvacit tu bhūmī vividhaiva ratnair*  
*nānā-vidhair mārakatī ca kāpi \|*  
*kutrāpi sā mārakatpīva ghāsair*  
*gavādibhir bhuktatamaiś ca nūtnaiḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places the earth is made of various gems, and in some places of emeralds. Some ground looks like emeralds because of the fresh grass eagerly eaten by the cows.**

*jāmbu-nadī rājati kāpi bhūmī*  
*sad-indra-nīla-prakṛtiś ca kāpi \|*  
*yatra sthite rādhikayāpi kṛṣṇe*  
*vipakṣa īkṣeta jano janaikam \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some earth shines golden and some earth shines like sapphires. When Kṛṣṇa is with Rādhā, other persons see only one person there.**

*sthalī kvacin mārakatī ca yatra*  
*suvarṇa-gulma-druma-virud-ādyāḥ \|*  
*suvarṇa-bhūmau kvacid eva yatra*  
*vṛkṣādikā mārakatā lasanti \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places the ground is composed of emeralds, the trees and bushes are gold. Sometime the trees are emerald and the ground is gold.**

*kutrāpi sā paṅkaja-rāga-bhūmī*  
*vīrud-drumādyā sphaṭika-prabhūtāḥ \|*  
*kutrāpi bhānti sphaṭikākhya-bhūmī*  
*vally-ādikāḥ paṅkaja-rāga-jātāḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places, the ground is made of rubies and the trees and creepers are made of crystal. In some places, the ground is made of crystal and the trees are made of rubies.**

*kvacid drumā mārakatā lasanti*  
*suvarṇa-vallī-vara-vellitā ye \|*  
*kvacit suvarṇa-druma-sañcayā yān*  
*veveṣṭi sā mārakatī ca vellī \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places, the trees made of emeralds are embraced by vines of gold. In some places, the golden trees are embraced by emerald vines.**

*kutrāpi vṛkṣāḥ sphaṭikā prabhūtā*  
*sa vellitā paṅkaja-rāga-vallyā \|*  
*kvacid drumā paṅkaja-rāga-jātā*  
*āliṅgitā sphaṭikākhya-vallyā \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places the crystal trees are embraced by ruby vines. In some places, the ruby vines are embraced by crystal vines.**

*yo ratna-vṛkṣaḥ puru-ratna-śākho*  
*nānā-maṇī pallavakāś ca śākhāḥ \|*  
*te pallavā bhūri maṇi-prasūtāḥ*  
*prasūna-saṅghā bahu-bheda-gandhāḥ \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jeweled trees have jeweled branches and twigs and buds of various jewels. The buds have flowers made of jewels endowed with many fragrances.**

*vihāra-ratnācalataḥ patadbhir*  
*maṇi-dravais tulya-sāraiḥ prapūrṇam \|*  
*bhū-bhūruhāṁ ratna-pṛthak-suratnair*  
*bhātaṁ maṇī pakṣibhir ālavālam \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The troughs, shining with beautiful gems different from those of the ground and trees, are filled with streams like liquid gems which have fallen from Vihāra-ratna Mountain and are ornamented with jewel birds.**

*svayambhuvo dhūrjaṭayaś ca sarve*  
*prādurbhave suṣṭhu jaṭā ghaṭāyām \|*  
*succhāyakāḥ sūrya-samāś ca vṛkṣā*  
*ābāla-śobhāḥ sanakādivac ca \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The trees are all self manifesting and non-different from Śiva, with tangled masses of branches like hair. The trees are bright like the sun and also give shade. They are effulgent with young age like the Kumāras.**

*bījaṁ vinā ropa-mṛte ca jātā*  
*apālitā snigdhā vivardhitāś ca \|*  
*pakvair apakvair api pacyamānaiḥ*  
*phalaiś ca puṣpaiś ca dalaiḥ sadāḍhyāḥ \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The trees manifest without seed, without planting and have no death. Without cultivation, they increase with thick foliage. They are full of leaves, flowers and fruits in unripe, almost ripe and ripe stages.**

*cid-ātmakās tādṛśa-śaktimantaḥ*  
*sarve’vatārā iva tasya rasyāḥ \|*  
*lokeṣu lokair avalokanīyā*  
*lokā ivaite vraja-bhūmi-vṛkṣāḥ \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All the trees of Vraja are spiritual in nature and attractive, with full powers like the avatāras. Just as the avatāras are seen by people in this world to be ordinary, so the trees of Vraja appear to be ordinary.**

*svādhīna-kāntā iva yāḥ priyeṇa*  
*sadopagūḍhās taruṇātulena \|*  
*vicitra-patrāṅkura-śobhitās tā*  
*latā vilāsinya iva sphuranti \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The playful creepers, decorated with colorful leaves and buds, shine like independent lovers, embraced by their incomparable lovers, the trees.**

*sadā samudyat-kalikāḥ sadaiva*  
*yāḥ puṣpavatyo’pi sadā phalinyaḥ \|*  
*sarvāḥ suparvāṇa utācyutaika-*  
*kāma-pradā yatra lasanti vallyaḥ \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Always with fresh leaves, blossoming flowers and fruits, and with graceful joints, the creepers bestow love of Kṛṣna.**

*evaṁ vicitrais tarubhir latābhir*  
*āmūlam utphulla sadā supuṣpaiḥ \|*  
*kuñjāni sarvatra lasanti yatra*  
*viśrāmyati krīḍati ca vrajenduḥ \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣṇa rests and plays where the bowers are always filled with colorful trees and creepers, having excellent blossoming flowers down to their roots.**

*tā yatra kuñje pika-bhṛṅga-gīte*  
*dīpāyitā oṣadhayo jjvalanti \|*  
*lūmaiś camaryaḥ parimārjayanti*  
*kastūrika-mṛgyaś ca sugandhayanti \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the bower where the cuckoos and bees sing, the shining plants provide light, the camarīs sweep the place with their tails and the musk deer provide fragrance.**

*catvāra evaṁ taravaś caturṣu*  
*koṇeṣu yeṣām ubhayatra pārśve \|*  
*dve dve late yāḥ kusumādibhis tān*  
*ākramya ratnālayatāṁ prayātāḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the bowers, at the four corners, there are four trees with flowering creepers on both sides. These elements become a jeweled cottage.**

*stambhā drumās te vaḍabhī ca teṣāṁ*  
*skandhākhya-śākhā-viṭapaiś chadāṁsi \|*  
*tad-bhittayo dvāś ca latā vibhaṅgyā*  
*prālamba-cūḍā kalasāni puṣpaiḥ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The tree trunks are the pillars and the branches are the roof covering. The walls are made of bent creepers and the doors are openings in the creepers. Pearl ornaments are made of flowers and the pinnacle is made of a pot.**

*evaṁ-vidhaiḥ sundara-sanniveśaiḥ*  
*kuñjāni sarvatra vane vraje’pi \|*  
*vimāna-kārīṇi vimāna-bhānāt*  
*vaikuṇṭha-gānām api lobhitāni \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The bowers with beautiful components which appear everywhere in the forest of Vraja are desired by the devatās in their airplanes and the inhabitants of Vaikuṇṭha.**

*vanāni kuñjāni latā-drumāś ca*  
*ye pakṣiṇo ye paśvavaś ca vānye \|*  
*kṣaṇān mukundekṣaṇa-mātrataḥ syuḥ*  
*sarve praphullās tad-ṛte tv aphullāḥ \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The forests, bowers, trees, and creepers, as well as the birds and beasts, all become joyful on receiving the glance of Kṛṣna and become sorrowful when they do not.**

*vrajeṣu nānā-vidha-sad-vaneṣu*  
*nandīśvarasyeśvara-koṇa ārāt \|*  
*khelā-vanaṁ yatra rahasya-khelā-*  
*khe lālasābhir diviṣadbhir eṣyāḥ \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**North east of Nandīśvara in the pure forests of Vraja, there is a forest for intimate pastimes, which are desired by the eager devatās in the sky.**

*yasmād asau śrī-yamunā vrajendor*  
*didṛkṣayānuvrajam āvrajantī \|*  
*nandīśvarānteṣu gaveṣayantī*  
*babhrāma tatrāsaralā bhavantī \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because of these pastimes, Yamunā comes to Vraja with a desire to see Kṛṣṇa. Looking for Kṛṣṇa in Nandīśvara, she becomes unsteady and wanders about in the pastime forest.**

*yatrodakādi-grahaṇa-cchalādyair*  
*gopījanā te’bhisarantī bhūyaḥ \|*  
*gopī priyo’pi pravadanti gopī*  
*ghaṭṭaṁ na ghaṭṭaṁ kurute sa kaṁ vā \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In that pastime forest, on the pretext of gathering water, the gopīs often come to meet with Kṛṣna and Kṛṣṇa comes to meet them there. They call this place gopī-ghaṭta. Will this place not make everyone unsteady?**

*śrī-kṛṣṇa-kelī-dyuti-vidyutānāṁ*  
*brahmāṇḍa-sammaṇḍana-paṇḍitānām \|*  
*bhāṇḍāra-bhāṇḍaṁ sa vibhāti yatra*  
*bhāṇḍīra-nāmā druma-vidrumaityaḥ \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**A banyan tree named Bhānḍīra stands among the trees. It is a storehouse of Kṛṣṇa's lustrous pastimes which skillfully decorate the whole universe.**

*yatrākhilānām api vallavīnām*  
*ārāma-dhāmāni mano-ramāṇi \|*  
*ramyāni kāmāni nikuñja-puñjā*  
*muñjāṭavī mukhya-vanāni bhānti \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At this place are many bowers and chief forests and muñjā grass fields, which are attractive and fulfill all desires. These forests have many attractive gardens for the gopīs.**

*sva-svābhilāṣeṇa gatāś ca yatrā-*  
*lapanti nṛtyanti milanti tena \|*  
*kāntena kāntāḥ kṛta-kely-anantā*  
*manoratho yāti manorathaṁ yat \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There the gopīs, who perform infinite pastimes, coming with their individual desires, talk with Kṛṣṇa, dance with him, and associate with him. At that time their original desires transform into other desires.**

*vṛndā-vanānāṁ vilasad-vanānāṁ*  
*vaikuṇṭha-kuṇṭhī-kara-vaibhavānām \|*  
*vṛndāvanaṁ nāma-vanaṁ guṇa-śrī-*  
*vṛndāvanaṁ tat katamac cakāsti \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Among all the forests of Vṛndā and all the splendid forests filled with wealth which would intimidate Vaikuṇṭha, the forest named Vṛndāvana, the abode of all good qualities and wealth, is most excellent.**

*yasyāsty aho kajjvala-jāta-jātā*  
*samantataḥ sā parikheva kṛṣṇā \|*  
*namaj-janān majjanato viśuddhān*  
*karoti yā kṛṣṇa-hṛdas tathāpi \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In Vṛndāvana, the Yamunā, like a moat arising from a mass of lamp black, purifies people who offer her respects when bathing in her and makes them attracted to Kṛṣṇa.**

*paryāpta indīvara mālikeva*  
*sasāra-hāra pratimeva devyāḥ \|*  
*vṛndāvanasyāsita-śāṭikeva*  
*virājate śrī-yamunā nadī sā \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Yamunā River is like a garland of blossoming blue lotuses, like deity of a goddess, like a necklace of sapphire, like a blue sash encircling Vṛndāvana.**

*saptaiva sindūn api sapta lokān*  
*saptāvṛtīḥ sāsti vibhidya saurī \|*  
*vaikuṇṭha-goloka-gatā bhramanti*  
*vṛndāvanaṁ sevitum ālavālam \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Coming from spiritual Goloka, piercing the seven coverings of the universe, passing the seven upper planets and seven oceans, Yamunā comes to serve Vṛndāvana as a water trough.**

*yasyā jale dūra-gatā gate’pi*  
*vṛndāvanasyaiva samantataḥ syāt \|*  
*āvarta-lakṣaṁ sthiti-lobha-lakṣaṁ*  
*yathā dhani-dvāri sulobhi-cittam \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as a greedy man may come from far off and wait at the door of a wealthy man with thousands of desires, Yamunā comes from far off and whirls around Vṛndāvana with desire to attain Kṛṣṇa.**

*yasyā asau śyāma-rasa-pravāhaḥ*  
*śyāmasya taṁ śyāma-rasa-pravāham \|*  
*uddīpayann eva nimajjayan syāt*  
*svasmiṁś ca tasmiṁś ca tad-ālibhis tam \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The dark, liquid currents of Yamunā lift and lower Kṛṣṇa, a current of mādhurya-rasa, along with his gopī friends, in the river waters and in mādhurya-rasa.**

*yasyā pravāheṣv api jīva-jātā*  
*muhur muhur mastakam ūrdhvayanti \|*  
*didṛkṣavaḥ śyāma-raseṣu magnā*  
*śyāmāgamāśaṅki-dhiyeva mugdhāḥ \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Submerged in madhurya-rasa and joyful with the thought that Kṛṣna will come, desiring to see him, the water creatures constantly lift their heads above the waters of Yamunā.**

*yasyā dvayoḥ sundara-pārśvayoḥ staḥ*  
*sopāna-māle vara-ratna-jāle \|*  
*śobhākhya-devyā iva danta-paṅktī*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-susmāraka-śuddha-śaktī \|\|45\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On both sides of the river are stair cases imbedded with the best gems. The staircases are like the teeth of the deity of beauty endowed with the pure power to make one remember Kṛṣṇa.**

*sopāna-jātau maṇijau dvi-pārśve*  
*madhye ca yasyāḥ salila-pravāhaḥ \|*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-veṇu-dhvani-pānato’mī*  
*bhavanti sadyo viparīta-rūpāḥ \|\|46\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The two jeweled staircases become liquid on hearing the sound of Kṛṣṇa's flute and the river flowing between them suddenly becomes stationary.**

*sarvāṇi tasyāḥ pulināni candra-*  
*cūrṇāni yac candrikayānvitāni \|*  
*śrī-rāsa-līlā-rasa-saubhagāni*  
*nāmāni yeṣāṁ tad avekṣaṇāni \|\|47\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The banks of the Yamunā are like camphor in the moonlight. They are called by names indicating the auspicious rasas of the rāsa dance.**

*madhye’pi tasyāḥ pulināni yeṣu*  
*kuñjāni kutrāpi lasanti teṣām \|*  
*vihaṅgamā aṅgana ullasanti*  
*govinda-sandarśana-lālasātaḥ \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are sand bars in the middle of the river. On those sand bars there are bowers. With a desire to see Govinda, birds become joyful in the yards of the bowers.**

*vṛndāvanasyaiva samantato’syāḥ*  
*sarve’vatārā iva te’vatārāḥ \|*  
*sva-premadāḥ sva-smaraṇena kintu*  
*sadyo vrajendro’pi cittam atra \|\|49\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Everywhere near the Yamunā of Vṛndāvana, the trees manifest like avatāras. By remembering the trees, the trees give prema for themselves. And remembering Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa gives prema.**

*vṛnāvanāntaṁ paritas taṭe’syā*  
*āmrādi-vṛndāvali-maṇḍalī yā \|*  
*pūrvāsti apūrvā-sakalāntarāntar-*  
*nikuñja-śālā-vidhu-dṛg-rasālāḥ \|\|50\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Within Vṛndāvana, on the bank of the Yamunā, there are groves of mango and other fruits which are eternal and astonishing. They delight the eyes of Kṛṣṇa with their interior bowers.**

*drākṣādikānāṁ kusumāvalīnāṁ*  
*rambhā samūhasya ca maṇḍalāni \|*  
*krameṇa madhye kanakasya bhūmī*  
*ratnācalas tatra ca kalpa-kuñjam \|\|51\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The groves are composed of thickets of grapes, flowers and bananas. In their midst is golden earth, piles of gems and upon that, bowers made of desire trees.**

*kutrādi-dīrghāḥ sama-sūtra-pātā*  
*iva kvacin maṇḍala-bandhanena \|*  
*dvitrāḥ kvacic chreṇaya eka-jātyā*  
*kutrāpi nānā-vidha-jātibhiś ca \|\|52\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sometimes the plants grow in straight lines and sometimes in circles. Sometimes two or three lines are composed of one type of plant and sometimes they are composed of many types of plants.**

*āmrādikānāṁ kva ca pañcaṣā yā*  
*ūrdhveṇa śākhādibhir apy anyūnāḥ \|*  
*tā nātiriktāḥ sukha-kṛtrimā kiṁ*  
*puṣpāvalīnām api tādṛśās tāḥ \|\|53\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Five or six lines of mango and other trees with their branches reach up to the same height as if produced artificially. The rows of flowers are similarly even.**

*tamāla-mālā api tādṛśas tāḥ*  
*kutrāpi nānā-vidha-pādapānām \|*  
*vanaṁ dhanī-bhūtam atīva citraṁ*  
*vipakṣa-dṛṣṭer api duṣpraveśam \|\|54\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The lines of tamāla trees are similarly even. The forests of various trees are dense and most astonishing. Even the glance of the enemies cannot enter these forests.**

*madhye nikuñjāḥ kvacid eva teṣāṁ*  
*kutrāpi ratnottama mandirāṇi \|*  
*kutrāpi vṛndā-parivāra-śālāḥ*  
*sarvāsu dikṣv eva samullasanti \|\|55\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the middle of these forests there are sometimes bowers, sometimes pavilions made of the best gems and sometimes rooms for Vṛndā's associates. They spread splendidly in all directions.**

*vṛndāvanaṁ laukika-vad yadīdaṁ*  
*tathāpi lokottara-loka-hāri \|*  
*rājño yathātyanta-vilāsinaḥ syād*  
*ārāma-ramyatvam atiprasāri \|\|56\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though Vṛndāvana appears to be material, it is attractive to the inhabitants of higher planets. As an enjoying king becomes very attached to his pleasure gardens, Kṛṣṇa is attached to these forests.**

*ṣaṇṇām ṛtūnāṁ pṛthag eva ṣaṭ cet*  
*padāni stat sva-guṇaiḥ sa yatra \|*  
*vṛndāvanenduḥ prati vāsaraṁ tat*  
*sarvartuṁ līlānubhavaṁ tanoti \|\|57\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though the six seasons with their qualities occur at different times, in Vṛndāvana Kṛṣṇa enjoys pastimes of all seasons every day.**

*tathāpi sarvartava eva nityaṁ*  
*vṛndāvane’smin praṇayanti sevām \|*  
*yeṣāṁ prasūnaiḥ kṛta-bhūṣaṇās tā*  
*vṛndāvanenduṁ praṇayanti gopyaḥ \|\|58\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All the seasons render service constantly to Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana. The gopīs serve Kṛṣna by making ornaments from the flowers of all seasons.**

*vṛndāvanendoḥ sakala-priyāṇāṁ*  
*vṛndāvane’smin vilasanti kuñjāḥ \|*  
*brahmānubhūtīr api mocayanti*  
*yeṣāṁ dyutīnāṁ kalayāpi guñjāḥ \|\|59\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The bowers of Kṛṣṇa's gopīs in Vṛndāvana shine brilliantly. The array of light emanating from these bowers, by a small portion, makes one reject Brahman realization.**

*vṛndāvane’smin paśu-pakṣiṇo*  
*ye vṛkṣā latādyā api yatra tatra \|*  
*sarve sadā sammukhayanti tasmin*  
*vṛndāvanendor udayo’sti yasmin \|\|60\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The animals, birds, trees and creepers of this Vṛndāvana always turn towards the place where Kṛṣṇa appears.**

*ekas tu jātyāpi sa kalpa-vṛkṣaḥ*  
*pare guṇair eva na jāti-rūpaiḥ \|*  
*tathāpi vṛndāvana-nāthavat te*  
*līlānusāreṇa guṇodayāḥ syuḥ \|\|61\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The desire tree, though one by breed, makes other trees manifest the same qualities, though they do not change their breed. Similarly, the inhabitants of Vṛndāvana manifest qualities like Kṛṣṇa's according to the pastime.**

*vṛndāvane yadyapi kalpa-vṛkṣāḥ*  
*sarve tathāpy eka udāra-gītaḥ \|*  
*yasyātule tatra tale tad-antas*  
*tan mañju-kuñje maṇi-mandiraṁ tat \|\|62\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though Vṛndāvana has unlimited desire trees, one is famous for its generosity. At the incomparable base of this tree is a jeweled pavilion in a delightful bower.**

*ūrdhvordhvaṁ gehopari geham evaṁ*  
*tat pañca-ṣaḍāṣṭa-navāni citram \|*  
*sarvatra madhye vara-karṇikāvat*  
*samantato’ṣṭau dalavat prakoṣṭhāḥ \|\|63\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In some places, the pavilion is five, six, eight or nine stories high. In the middle is an excellent pericarp and around are rooms fashioned like eight petals.**

*pare pare’nye ca tathaiva koṣṭhāḥ*  
*sevā-parāṇāṁ śayanādi niṣṭhāḥ \|*  
*tan-mandirasyātula-saubhagasya syuḥ*  
*prāṅgane’ṣṭāv api mandirāṇi \|\|64\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are other rooms for the servants to make preparations for sleeping. In the yard of this incomparable pavilion are eight other pavilions.**

*tāny aṣṭa diggāni sumadhyagaṁ tat*  
*svakīya-śākhādibhir eva nityam \|*  
*svāṅgeṣu saṅgopayate drumo’sau*  
*līlānukulebhya ṛte janebhyaḥ \|\|65\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**These pavilions are placed in the eight directions and the main pavilion is in the center. The tree hides the activities within from all persons except those favorable to the pastimes by using its branches.**

*kalpāga-kuñjaṁ nava-mandiraṁ tat*  
*samantatas tasya pare’ṣṭha-kuñjāḥ \|*  
*tathaiva sarve nava-mandirās te*  
*pratyekam anye’pi tataḥ pare’nye \|\|66\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The bower of the desire tree with nine pavilions is surrounded by eight other bowers with nine pavilions each. And each of these bowers is surrounded by eight others with nine pavilions each.**

*yat kuñja-rājaṁ vṛta-kalpa-vṛkṣaṁ*  
*vṛndāvaneśā lasitālayaṁ tat \|*  
*tataḥ sakhīnām atha kiṅkarīṇāṁ*  
*samantat stat parataḥ krameṇa \|\|67\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The main bower, surrounded by desire trees, is the pleasure palace of Rādhā. Surrounding this and separate from this are the bowers of the sakhīs and servants.**

*sarvatra madhye vara-mandiraṁ yad*  
*yūtheśvarī tatra gaṇeśvarī vā \|*  
*kāntena sākaṁ yadi rājate tat*  
*tad ālayas tat-para-mandireṣu \|\|68\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In the center is the excellent pavilion of the head gopī. When she stays there with Kṛṣṇa, the sakhīs stay in the other pavilions.**

*sa kalpa-vṛkṣas tad-adho nikuñjaṁ*  
*tan-mandirāṇy apy aticitrakāni \|*  
*yāvan mukunda-priyayepsitāni*  
*bhavanti tāvanti na tā vidanti \|\|69\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The desire tree has a bower beneath it with many astonishing pavilions. These manifest as the gopīs desire but they cannot count the number of pavilions.**

*govinda-bhūmīti purāṇa-gītaṁ*  
*yad yoga-pīṭhākhyam apīha tantre \|*  
*śrī-rādhayā śrī-vidhu-yoga-dhāma*  
*śrī-kuñja-rājaṁ nigadāma nāma \|\|70\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This place is known as Govinda-bhūmi in the Purāṇas and yoga-pīṭha in the tantras. We call it the king of the bowers, where Kṛṣṇa unites with Rādhā.**

*yad dakṣiṇe rājati rāja-kelī*  
*dola-sthalī yāti vicitra-śilpā \|*  
*ānanda-vṛndāvana eva yasyāḥ*  
*svarūpa-śobhātiśayena rasyā \|\|71\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the south of the king of bowers is the swing pavilion, constructed very skillfully. The attractive beauty of this place is described in Ānanda-vṛndāvana-campū.**

*tad-dakṣiṇe dūrata eva kiṁcit*  
*śrī-svāmi-govinda-pade saro’sti \|*  
*samantato yasya nikuñja-puñjāḥ*  
*yeṣūllasanti bhramarāli-guñjāḥ \|\|72\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**A little distant, to the south, is the lake of Govinda. It is surrounded by many bowers where the bees hum loudly.**

*yad brahma-kuṇḍaṁ śiva-koṇa-tuṇḍaṁ*  
*samantatas tasya ca kuñja-puñjāḥ \|*  
*yad uttare sā sad aśoka-vāṭī*  
*dhāṭīva yā tad-dvaya-dhairya-luṇṭhe \|\|73\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**North east of Govinda-kuṇḍa is Brahma-kuṇda, surrounded by bowers. North of this is an attractive aśoka grove. Like an assailant, it steals way the composure of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.**

*gopīśa īśaḥ sa tad-īśa-koṇe*  
*gopī-janānāṁ varado’rcanīyaḥ \|*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅgāya yathā sa dūtī*  
*jano vinodena mano dhinoti \|\|74\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Gopīśa lies to the north east of Brahma-kuṇḍa. Śiva (īśa), worshipped by the gopīs, gave them blessings and satisfied their minds, just as a messenger satisfies the mind of a person who desires association with Kṛṣna by pastimes of the gopīs.**

*tasyeśa-koṇe tad-adūra eva*  
*taṭe taṭinyā naṭatīva bhāti \|*  
*vaṁśī-baṭo yasya tale sa-vaṁśīṁ*  
*vaṁśīdharo vādayate priyābhyaḥ \|\|75\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**North east of this at a short distance one the bank of the Yamunā is Vaṁśīvaṭa, resplendent like a dancer. At its base Kṛṣṇa plays his flute for the gopīs.**

*yad-uttare naidhuvanaṁ vanaṁ tat*  
*tan-nāma-gītaṁ śruti-citta-nītam \|*  
*so’ntarhito yatra parāṁ priyāṁ prāg*  
*rāsonmukhībhyo ramayan priyābhyaḥ \|\|76\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**North of this is the place known as Nidhu-vana, whose name should be heard and contemplated. Disappearing from the gopīs during rāsa dance, Kṛṣṇa enjoyed with Rādhā at this place.**

*sūryāspade nairṛta-koṇa-kande*  
*kundotthavat kañja-nikuñja-puñje \|*  
*śrī-rādhikā-sūrya-samarcanāyair*  
*navā javā rājati rāga-phullā \|\|77\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the south west, where the sun god resides, in a thick lotus bower beside a pond, fresh red java flowers blossom for Rādhā's worship of the sun.**

*vāyavya-koṇe ca kadamba-mūle*  
*yā bhadrakāly asti tad-arcanāyām \|*  
*sa gopa-kanyādika-cīra-cittaṁ*  
*caurī cakārāsti ca cīra-coraḥ \|\|78\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the north west at the base of a kadamba tree is Bhadrakālī's temple. While worshipping her, Kṛṣṇa stole the young gopīs' clothing and minds.**

*koṇe’nalasyāsti sa vighna-rājo*  
*yam arcayanti prayatāḥ priyaṁ tāḥ \|*  
*priya-prasaṅge’niśa-vighna-śaṅkāḥ*  
*śaṅkā labhante’tra vinodyamena \|\|79\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**South east of this is the temple of Ganeśa. The gopīs intently worshipped Ganeśa since they were worried about constant obstacles in meeting Kṛṣṇa. Without intent worship they had cause to worry.**

*prācyāṁ diśi prema-maya-prapūrṇa-*  
*śrī-veṇu-kūpaṁ vilasaty alaṁ yat \|*  
*śrī-veṇu-vādyaiḥ svayam āvirāsīt*  
*śrī-veṇu-pāṇeḥ parama-priyāyai \|\|80\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**East of this place is the well of the flute, filled with prema. The well manifested by the sound of the flute, played for attracting Rādhā.**

*rāsonmukhe tyakta-samasta-kāntaḥ*  
*kāntas tadā vādayad āśu veṇum \|*  
*raho vilāsena tayā yadābhūt*  
*tasyāḥ pipāsārtiṁ pipāsayārtaḥ \|\|81\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Giving up other gopīs during the rāsa dance, when Kṛṣṇa was alone with Rādhā and she became thirsty, he played his flute, pained by her thirst.**

*śṛṅgāra-śāntau yad-adho nikuñje*  
*śṛṅgārayām āsa parāṁ priyāṁ saḥ \|*  
*śṛṅgāra-nāmā sa baṭo’dhunāpi*  
*saṅgīyate tat tad ivekṣate ca \|\|82\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In a bower below the tree named Śṛṅgāra-śanti, Kṛṣna decorated Rādhā. That banyan tree is still called Śṛṅgāra and can still be seen.**

*yadā sa vaṁśī-baṭagaḥ sva-vaṁśīṁ*  
*vaṁśīdharo’vādayad āśu tarhi \|*  
*dhīraḥ samīro’pi babhūva yatra*  
*sthalaṁ ca tad dhīra-samīra-nāma \|\|83\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Kṛṣṇa left Vaṁśi-vaṭa and played his flute, immediately a gentle breeze arose. That place is called Dhīra-samīra.**

*samantato yadyapi bhāti kṛṣṇā*  
*kṛṣṇānurāgā pulināni tasyāḥ \|*  
*tathaiva sarvatra tathāpi vaṁśī*  
*baṭo’ntimāny eva naṭanti manye \|\|84\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Though the Yamunā, attached to Kṛṣṇa, and its banks all shine brilliantly, the area around Vaṁśī-baṭa dances.**

*sarvatra kuñjottama-mandireṣu*  
*vṛndādayaḥ pratyaham eva sāram \|*  
*śayyādikaṁ yā racayanti citraṁ*  
*citraṁ sa kṛṣṇo’pi yad-īkṣaṇe syāt \|\|85\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Vṛndā and others make excellent, amazing beds every day in the best pavilions of the bowers, Kṛṣṇa becomes surprised.**

*tatraiva sarvāḥ prati vāsarādau*  
*tāmbūla-sarvādy- ayathātathaṁ ca \|*  
*tat sarvam ālokya rasena citraṁ*  
*cittaṁ drutaṁ tāś ca tathā drutāś ca \|\|86\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing every morning the marks of lac and betel and other ingredients in disarray, Vṛndā and others' hearts melted and they themselves melted with blissful prema.**

*śrī-nandīśvara-mandirāntam abhito vṛndāvanendoḥ padaṁ*  
*śrī-vṛndāvanam eva kānana-vanaṁ tatrollasad-rociṣā \|*  
*śrī-vṛndāvana-deśa-leśa-manane śrī-rīti-cintāmaṇau*  
*tal-līlā-rasa-lobha-śobhanādayaḥ sargo dvitīyodayaḥ \|\|87\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This completes the second chapter of Vraja-rīti-cintāmaṇi, a meditation on a small portion of Vṛndāvana, concerning glorious greed for the rasa of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa's pastimes, with revealing descriptions of the special groves within the forests of Vṛndāvana, the place for Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, which surrounds the palace at Nandīśvara.**

## Chapter Three

*vṛndāvaneṣv īdṛśa-vaibhaveṣu*  
*mādhurya-dhūrya-dhvajavad dhinoti \|*  
*govardhano nāma dharādhirājo*  
*rājopacārair yuvarāja-rājam \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Within Vṛndāvana endowed with the powers previously described, the king of mountains named Govardhana delights like the flag of chief sweetness, and pleases Kṛṣna with royal offerings.**

*nānā-vidhā ratna-vareṇya-vārāḥ*  
*śilā yadīyā vilasanti yatra \|*  
*kṛṣṇasya siṁhāsana-pīṭha-*  
*khaṭvā śayyādi-rūpā rasa-rājya-bhūpāḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On Govardhana, the stones, the best of the best gems, with various types, take the form of Kṛṣṇa's throne, seat, couch and bed and are kings in the kingdom of rasa.**

*yat kandarāḥ kuñja-vareṇya-puñjāḥ*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa kelī-rasa-mandirāṇi \|*  
*pṛthak pṛthak tat parama-priyādeḥ*  
*saubhāgya-bhāgyottama-yogyatāni \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Its caves and many excellent bowers are the pavilions which produce rasa during Kṛṣṇa's pastimes. Each place is most suitable for the most intimate pastimes with the most intimate associates.**

*parāga-puṣpair madhubhir marandaiḥ*  
*pravāla-vārair madhuraiḥ phalaiś ca \|*  
*nānā-vidhair yat taravo latābhiḥ*  
*kṛṣṇasya sevāṁ racayanti tābhiḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Its trees and creepers serve Kṛṣṇa with their flowers, pollen, honey, nectar, abundant buds and sweet fruits of various types.**

*yad dhātavo gairika-hiṅgulādyā*  
*manaḥ śilākhyā haritāla-mukhyāḥ \|*  
*atipriyāḥ śyāma-śarīra-śobhā*  
*lobhāya kurvanti vidhu-priyāṇām \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Its minerals, red chalk, vermilion, red arsenic and haritāla, dear to Kṛṣṇa, decorate Kṛṣṇa's body in order to increase the desire of the gopīs.**

*kastūrikās tad-dhariṇair nisṛṣṭā*  
*jātā svayaṁ kuṅkuma-sāra-jātāḥ \|*  
*karpūra-pūrāś ca sugandhayanti*  
*kṛṣṇāya kṛṣṇāgurubhiś ca yatra \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On Govardhana, musk left by deer, natural, excellent kuṅkuma and quantities of camphor act as perfumes for Kṛṣṇa along with kṛṣnāguru scents.**

*yatrāsti sā candana-sāra-jātir*  
*na yatra sarpaḥ sa ca ced viṣaṁ na \|*  
*viṣaṁ ca cet kvāpi na duḥkhadaṁ*  
*tad dhareḥ sukhaikaṁ hari-dāsa-varye \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On Govardhana, the best of his servants, there are sandalwood trees but no snakes. If snakes appear, they have no poison. If they have poison, the poison does not give pain. It only gives joy to Kṛṣṇa.**

*vyāvartate vāmya-vaśān mṛgākṣyāḥ*  
*sarpādi-vīkṣādibhir eva sadyaḥ \|*  
*svayaṁ samāśleṣa-viśeṣa-lābho*  
*hare raso’sau hari-dāsa-varye \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On Govardhana, because of obstinacy, one gopī left Kṛṣṇa. But seeing a snake, she immediately returned and embraced Kṛṣṇa. The embrace gave rasa to Kṛṣṇa.**

*kvacic chilā-nīla-maṇi-pravīṇāḥ*  
*kutrāpi tā mārakataika-tānāḥ \|*  
*tāḥ padma-rāgāḥ sphaṭikāś ca kāścid*  
*govardhanādrau vidhu-keli-kalpāḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On Govardhana in some places the stones are the best sapphires, in other places they are all emeralds, in some places they are rubies and in other places they are crystals. These are used for the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa.**

*jāmbunada-grāva-varān nirīkṣya*  
*kṛṣṇāgrajo’raṁ vṛṣabhānujādhīḥ \|*  
*dūraṁ prayāti sphaṭikoccayāntāt*  
*kṛṣṇāgrajo’trety api sāpi yatra \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When Balarāma sees fine golden stones he thinks that Rādhā is there and goes far away. When Rādhā sees white crystals she thinks Balarāma is there and goes far away from the crystals.**

*kṛṣṇas tu jāmbunada-madhyam eti*  
*muhuḥ sva-kāntā dyuti-viddha-buddhiḥ \|*  
*kāntādi tasyendra-maṇī śilāntaṁ*  
*kānta-bhramāc chrī-hari-dāsa-varye \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On Govardhana, which is the best of servants, Kṛṣna, thinking that the effulgence is coming from Rādhā, approaches gold nuggets. Rādhā and other gopīs, mistaking sapphire stones for Kṛṣṇa, approach the sapphires.**

*yasyāṅga-śobhā na vilobhayanti*  
*kaṁ vā nikuñjeṣu darīṣu dṛṣṭāḥ \|*  
*kṛṣṇasya kāntā-nivahasya hārā-*  
*dibhiḥ padālaktaka-carvitādyaiḥ \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Who would not become attracted to the limbs of Govardhana on seeing Govardhana with the gopīs' necklaces, ornaments, stains of lac and chewed betel scattered in its bowers and caves?**

*yasyendra-nīlī maṇir eva kṛṣṇo*  
*jāmbunadāny eva tadīya-kāntāḥ \|*  
*tat kelayo yasya kuṭumba-bhāvā*  
*govardhanaṁ taṁ kavayantu ke vā \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kṛṣna is the sapphire and the gopīs are the gold links on the necklace of Govardhana. The pastimes of Kṛṣṇa with the gopīs on Govardhana are like its children, never separated from it. Let all men glorify Govardhana!**

*sa tāṇḍavaṁ tāṇḍavinaś caranti*  
*gāyanti bhṛṅgāś ca pikāś ca vīkṣya \|*  
*tanvanti tālaṁ bahu pakṣiṇo’nye*  
*hares tu sevā hari-dāsa-varye \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Seeing Govardhana's service to Kṛṣna, the peacocks dance, the bees and cuckoos sing and many birds keep the rhythm.**

*paryanta-bhūmiṁ parito yadīyāṁ*  
*kṛṣṇasya khelāvali-lālitāni \|*  
*kuṇḍāni kuñjāni padāni bhānti*  
*sannirjharās te paritaḥ sravanti \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The land spreads out around Govardhana. In that land there are ponds and bowers attractive for Kṛṣṇa's pastimes. Cascades from Govardhana flow in these places.**

*tat pūrvato dāna-nivartanākhyaṁ*  
*kuṇḍaṁ yadīya-smaraṇena sadyaḥ \|*  
*śrī-rādhikā-tad-dayito’py udañcad-*  
*romāñca-kampāñcita-sundaraḥ syāt \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the east of Govardhana lies the pond called Dāna-nivartana (where taxes were levied). Remembering this place, Kṛṣṇa immediately attains beauty as he trembles and his hairs stand on end.**

*tato’pi pūrve para-rāsa-līlā-*  
*valī-sthalī-sāti-valīyasī yā \|*  
*smṛtāpi tāṁ taṁ naṭyaty akāṇḍe*  
*svīye rase candra-sarovaraṁ ca \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**East of this pond is a powerful place where the rāsa dance was performed many times. Remembering this place called Candra-sarovara, Rādhā and Kṛṣna both causelessly dance, absorbed in mādhurya-rasa.**

*govardhana-prāg-diśi dakṣiṇāṁśe*  
*saṅkarṣaṇānanda-sarovaraṁ tat \|*  
*tat pūrvato rājati tac ca gaurī*  
*tīrthaṁ na gauri-patir eti yat tu \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**South east of Govardhana is Saṅkarṣāṇāndana-sarovara. East of that lies Gaurī-tīrtha. Śiva does not go there (since Kṛṣna performs intimate pastimes there).**

*sadā tayā tasya vihāra-hetoḥ*  
*saubhāgya-bhāgyāmṛta-bhūri-bhāraḥ \|*  
*tasyāḥ priyaṁ yatra samasta-vidyā*  
*patiṁ cakārāśu nikuñja-vidyām \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because of his pastimes with Rādhā on Śravana full moon, this place is filled with auspiciousness. It gave Kṛṣṇa, who is the master of all knowledge, intimate knowledge of the bowers.**

*saṅkarṣaṇaṁ dāna-nivartanaṁ ca*  
*kuṇḍa-dvayaṁ yat tad-ura-madhye \|*  
*mahānna-kūṭotsavam ātanoti*  
*ānanda-rājo yuva-rāja-rañjī \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Between Saṅkarṣaṇa-kuṇḍa and Dāna-nivartana lies the place called the king of bliss, the festival place of the great pile of rice, which is attractive to Kṛṣṇa.**

*paścāc ca tasyollasitāspadeṣu*  
*kṛṣṇasya kelī-kavalī-kṛteṣu \|*  
*śrī-dāna-ghaṭṭe sumaṇī-sughaṭṭe*  
*yatrobhayor āsa vaco vilāsaḥ \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the west is Dāna-ghaṭta, made of fine jewels, which is situated among the places where Kṛṣṇa performed eating pastimes which cause his delight. At this place Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa had many playful verbal exchanges.**

*tad dāna-kelī-kali-vāg-vilāso*  
*vismāpayāmāsa na kaṁ janaṁ yaḥ \|*  
*tayor api śrota-taṭīm aṭann aṭan*  
*naṭī karoty āśu manas tanūṁś ca \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Who would not be astonished by their pastimes of quarrelling to extract taxes at this place? Even Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, repeatedly hearing those words, dance with their minds and bodies.**

*pucchaṁ mayūrākṛtiṁ yasya tasya*  
*govinda-kuṇḍasya ca dakṣiṇāṁśe \|*  
*gala-sthalī mānasa-jāhnavī sā*  
*nau-khelanaṁ yatra tayoḥ sadā syāt \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Govardhana is shaped like a peacock and Govinda-kuṇḍa in the south is it tail. On the neck is situated Mānasa-gaṅga where Rādhā and Kṛṣna always have boat pastimes.**

*nau khelanaṁ yatra tayoḥ śrutīnāṁ*  
*pathaṁ kathaṁcid yadi tat prayāti \|*  
*tadaiva ruddhaṁ kurute balena*  
*balena ca tyājayituṁ na śakyam \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one somehow hears about their boat pastimes, the pastimes forcefully take possession of his ears. One cannot give up hearing those pastimes even by force.**

*yasyās taṭe puṇḍraka-maṇḍapāni*  
*trailokya-śobhā-rasa-maṇḍapāni \|*  
*tayor dvayor ujjvala-bhāvitāni*  
*bhavanti nityaṁ hṛdi bhāvitāni \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On the bank of Mānasa-gaṅgā there are mādhavī creeper pavilions which are filled with the beauty of the three worlds. There, Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa experience mādhurya-rasa. That place manifests in their hearts at all times.**

*jala-sthala-sthaiḥ kusumair asīmaiḥ*  
*saran-marandaiḥ kusumākarākhyaḥ \|*  
*padmākaro rājati kuñja-puñajair*  
*girīndra-varyānanam eva kiṁ saḥ \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Kusuma-sarovara in the shape of a lotus shines with many bowers and unlimited water flowers filled with nectar. Is it the face of Govardhana?**

*gopī-svarūpāpti-para-prasūna-*  
*praphullatāyai kusumākaro’yam \|*  
*śrī-nārado yatra babhūva gopī*  
*snānaika-mātrād iti mohanoktiḥ \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**It is called Kusuma-sarovara because it causes blossoming of the best flower, which blossoms as a gopī. At this place Nārada attained a gopī form by bathing once in its waters. This is the statement of Kṛṣna himself.**

*yaṁ pāṇinā pālayad īśa eva*  
*yo pālayat tat paricāram eva \|*  
*krīḍaty ajasraṁ svayam eva yatra*  
*sa kena varṇyo hari-dāsa-varyaḥ \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With his hand Kṛṣṇa protected Govardhana to protect his devotees. He constantly plays there. How can one describe the best servant of Kṛṣna, Govardhana?**

*śrī-rādhikā-kṛṣṇa-sarovare te*  
*tat-prema-pūrṇe kila yasya netre \|*  
*govardhano bhāti sa-gopa-gopī*  
*govardhano mādhava-mādhurībhyaḥ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Śyāma-kuṇḍa are Govardhana's eyes, which are filled with prema for them. Govardhana, which nourishes the cowherds, gopīs and cows, shines with the sweetness of spring and Kṛṣṇa.**

*lāvaṇya-vaṇyāmṛta-śuddha-kando*  
*śobhā-samudrātiśayāśayau ye \|*  
*mādhurya-caryā-caya-cāru-mūle*  
*śrī-rādhikā-kuṇḍa-mukunda-kuṇḍe \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Śyāma-kuṇḍa are the splendorous root of all activities of mādhurya-rasa. They are the ultimate seat of an ocean of beauty. They give pure, sweet waters and joy, with a flood of beauty.**

*dvayos tayoḥ prema-maṇī khanī ye*  
*vilāsa-ratnāvali-valli-bīje \|*  
*rasaika-rūpe madhura-svarūpe*  
*kiṁ tau lasanta sarasī bhavantau \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The two kuṇḍas are mines producing jewels of prema. They are the seed of the jeweled creeper of pastimes. They are the very form of sweetness and rasa. How the two kuṇḍas shine with beauty!**

*yayor vilokena tayoḥ pratītis*  
*tayor iva syād anubhūti-bhūtiḥ \|*  
*sākṣād avāptiś ca tathānavadyā*  
*sadyo bhavaty eva vadanti vidyāḥ \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By looking at Rādha-kuṇḍa, Kṛṣṇa sees and realizes Rādhā. By looking at Śyāma –kuṇḍa, Rādhā sees and realizes Kṛṣṇa. The wise say that by seeing these two kuṇḍas one can immediately attain Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in a perfect manner.**

*dvayoḥ sudurlabhyatayā yadi syāt*  
*parasparotkaṇṭthita-bhūri-bhāraḥ \|\|*  
*dvāv eva yad dvandva-samāśrayātau*  
*paraspara-prāpti-rasātisiktau \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Since Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa suffer intensely in separation because of difficulty in meeting, they take shelter of the two kuṇḍas and become saturated with the rasa of meeting each other.**

*rādhaiva kuṇḍaṁ dravatāṁ gatābhūt*  
*kṛṣṇekṣaṇānanda-bhareṇa manye \|*  
*kṛṣṇo’pi rādhekṣaṇa-moda-bhārāt*  
*tenaiva tan-nāma-guṇā dvikuṇḍau \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I surmise that Rādhā, out of great joy on seeing Kṛṣna, turned liquid and became Rādhā-kuṇḍa. Kṛsṇa, out of great joy on seeing Rādhā, turned liquid and became a kuṇḍa. Because of that, the two kuṇḍas have the names and qualities of the couple.**

*kāruṇya-mātrātiśayāj janeṣu*  
*svakīya-mādhurya-bharānubhūtyai*  
*tau stau dravantau sarasī bhavantau*  
*majjanti santas tad ihollasantaḥ \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Out of great compassion to people of this world Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa became these two liquid kuṇḍas so that the people could experience the couple's sweetness. Therefore the devotees in great joy bathe in those waters (to experience the sweetness).**

*premaiva tad yugma-varasya yugmaṁ*  
*kuṇḍasya manye tad ihāśu dhanyāḥ \|*  
*majjanti tan-majjana-mātram eva*  
*premnīti nāmnāpi tayor dvi-kuṇḍau \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I consider that the two excellent kuṇḍas are the prema of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. Therefore the fortunate devotees immediately bathe in the two kuṇḍas. Just by bathing in the two kuṇḍas they bathe in prema. The two kuṇḍas also bear their names.**

*tayo raso yo madhuraḥ sa eva*  
*varṇaṁ dvayādyanta-viparyayeṇa \|*  
*virājate’tyanta-rahasya-bhāvān*  
*majjanti tajjāḥ sara ity udīrya \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sweet** ***rasa*** **of the couple has two syllables. By reversing the syllables, the word** ***sara*** **is formed. Uttering "The kuṇḍas are rasa", the wise bathe in the kuṇda since they have the mood of couple's intimate love.**

*tayor dvayor eva nikuñja-puñje*  
*vilasya nānā-vidha-rasya-bhāvaiḥ \|*  
*nirīkṣituṁ kuṇḍa-yugaṁ sa kuñjaṁ*  
*yugmaṁ tayoḥ syāt tu cakora-yugmam \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa become two cakora birds in order to see the two kuṇḍas with their bowers, and play in the many bowers around the two kuṇḍas with various attractive moods.**

*dvayor ati-prīti-parasparaṁ tac*  
*chrī-rādhikā-kuṇḍam akhaṇḍa-śobham \|*  
*samantato yasya nikuñja-puñjāḥ*  
*sakhī-janānāṁ bhramarāli-guñjāḥ \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All around Rādhā-kuṇḍa, of unlimited beauty, where the couple experience the highest love, are the sakhīs' bowers filled with the humming of bees.**

*tad-uttare śrī-lalitā-pramodaṁ*  
*nikuñja-padmaṁ maṇi-mātra-sadmaḥ \|*  
*īśāna-koṇe kusumaika-kḷptaṁ*  
*kuñjaṁ viśākhā-pramadaṁ prabhāti \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the north is Lalitā-pramoda, the bower of Lalitā, in the form of a lotus, with a cottage made of jewels. To the north east is Viśākhā-pramoda, the bower of Viśākhā.**

*pūrve yadīye’khila-citra-kuñjaṁ*  
*citrā-pramoda-dala-puṣpa-citram \|*  
*pūrṇendu-kuñje’nala-koṇa-mūle*  
*ya indulekhā-pramadaḥ pragītaḥ \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the east of Rādhā-kuṇḍa is Citrā's most colorful bower, Citrā-pramoda, colorful because of variegated leaves and flowers. To the south east is the delight of Indulekhā, the bower of the full moon.**

*yad-dakṣiṇe prema-nikuñja-varyaṁ*  
*śrī-campaka-vallī-sukhadaṁ cakāsti \|*  
*yatra sthite rādhikayāpi kṛṣṇe*  
*rādhām avīkṣyeva gataiva vṛddhā \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**South of Rādhā-kuṇḍa is the excellent bower of prema which gives joy to Campakalatā. When Kṛṣna resides there with Rādhā, Jaṭilā cannot see her (since it is golden like Rādhā.)**

*yat nairṛte nīla-nikuñja-kuñje*  
*śrī-raṅga-devī-sukhadendra-nīle \|*  
*śrī-rādhayāsīnam avekṣya kṛṣṇaṁ*  
*tāṁ ślāghayitvā jaratī gatāram \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the south west is the bower called Nīla-kuñja, a bower made of sapphires which gives joy to Raṅga-devī. Not seeing Kṛṣṇa where he is seated with Rādhā, Jaṭilā quickly leaves after praising Rādhā.**

*śrī-tuṅgavidyā-sukhadaṁ nikuñjaṁ*  
*yatra paścime bhāty aruṇaṁ tadākhyam \|*  
*śyāvaṁ nikuñjaṁ kila vāyu-koṇe*  
*sukhāya yan mārakataṁ sudevyāḥ \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the west lies the pink bower of Tuṅgavidyā, which gives her joy. To the northwest is the bower of Sudevī made of emeralds. Its color is brown.**

*śrī-kuṇḍa-madhye’pi jaloparisthaṁ*  
*vicitra-saṅkhyaṁ maṇi-mandiraṁ yat \|*  
*anaṅga-mañjary-anurāga-bhāgaṁ*  
*bhāgaṁ vinā kas tad avaitu lokaḥ \|\|45\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Floating on the water of Rādhā-kuṇḍa is Anaṅga-mañjarī’s astonishing jeweled pavilion composed of the anurāga. Without good fortune, who can attain this place?**

*śrī-kuṇḍam etan nikhilāś ca kuñjā*  
*padaṁ ca panthāś ca yathā yathaiṣām \|*  
*govinda-līlāmṛta eti sarvaṁ*  
*govinda-līlāmṛta-mānya-garvam \|\|46\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All the details of Rādhā-kuṇḍa, the bowers, places and paths are described in Govinda-līlāmṛta. Everything here is to be highly respected because it contains the nectar of Kṛṣṇa's pastimes.**

*rahasya-līlāvali-valgu-nādāḥ*  
*pramatta-rādhākhya yad ādi-pādāḥ \|*  
*nandīśvarād dakṣiṇa-paścimādau*  
*yan-madhyataḥ kāmyavanādy anādau \|\|47\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Between the villages, south and west of Nandīśvara, are eternal Kāmyavana and other forests along with the place called Pramatta-rādhikā, which reveal the lovely conversations spoken during the many intimate pastimes.**

*yatrākhilaiśvarya-mahārya-līlā*  
*valī valīyasy abaleva līnā \|*  
*mādhurya-caryā-caya-cāru-varyā*  
*cāryasya pādāmbuja-saurabheṣu \|\|48\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At Kāmyavana pastimes of great power and reverence, though powerful, become weak, and merge into fragrance of the lotus feet of the ācārya of the most attractive, sweet pastimes.**

*śrī-rāma-līlāvali-sākṣi-laṅkā*  
*kuṇḍaṁ mahoddaṇḍa-rasābdhi-tuṇḍam \|*  
*yatroditā śrīla-mukunda-kelī*  
*śrī-rādhikā-rañjana-mañju-puñjī \|\|49\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Laṅkā-kuṇḍa, the most excellent, extraordinary ocean of rasa, witnessed the pastimes of Rāma. At this place Kṛṣṇa spoke pleasing stories to attract Rādhā.**

*lukkāyanā khyāti vicitra-kelau*  
*śrī-rādhayā mādhava-mādhurī sā \|*  
*yatroditā citra tad-āli-mālām*  
*ālāpane citrayatīva yātau \|\|50\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At Kāmyavana Rādhā and Kṛṣna revealed great sweetness in performing hide and seek pastimes. When the sakhīs spoke of these pastimes, Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa became stunned like figures in a painting. How astonishing!**

*evaṁ-vidhāneka-rasāspadāni*  
*kuṇḍāni kuñjāni lasanti yatra \|*  
*kṛṣṇasya kāmyāni vanāni yatra*  
*santīti tat kāmyavanaṁ vadanti \|\|51\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In this way, because this place has pleasurable kuṇḍas, bowers and many forests pleasing (kāmya) to Kṛṣṇa, it is called Kāmyavana.**

*sūryasya mūrtiḥ sarasī ca yatra*  
*śrī-rādhikāyāś tad-arcanāyāḥ \|*  
*vadanti yac-chāntvanuvāsa saṁjñaṁ*  
*tadīkṣaṇānīva tad-īkṣaṇāni \|\|52\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In that forest there is a beautiful deity of the sun god worshipped by Rādhā. That place is called Śāntvanuvāsa. Rādhā and her friends saw the sun god and the sun god had the opportunity to see Rādhā.**

*chadiḥ śilā yatra tadāhva-śaile*  
*kṛṣṇasya nānā-kutukāspadāni \|*  
*kṛṣṇasya kautūhala-bāspa-dāni*  
*kṛṣṇasya kau tūha-lavāspadāni \|\|53\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**On a hill called** ***chadiḥ śilā*** **there are many places interesting to Kṛṣṇa, causing tears of joy, causing the earth to wonder what had happened.**

*nandasya navyo nilayo’sti yatra*  
*ratnākaro ratna-saraś chalena \|*  
*līlāvaloke yutayaiva līno*  
*lālitya-lālyaṁ lalitādi-kuṇḍam \|\|54\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At that place is Nanda’s new house and the kuṇḍas of Lalitā, Viśākhā and Paurṇamāsī. Desiring to see Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, the ocean became the pond named Ratna-sara.**

*parasparālāpa-madaṁ nipīya*  
*parasparāṅgālasa-lagna-mattau \|*  
*vilokya tau lokayituṁ tad ālyo*  
*viccheda-gā yatra tad-āhvayaṁ tat \|\|55\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sakhīs came from many places to drink the intoxicating conversations of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa and to see them joyfully stuck in continual embrace from fatigue. It is therefore called Ālokana-sthana (the place of seeing).**

*taptvā na yad bhāvam avāpa lakṣmī*  
*lakṣmī svabhāvaṁ kutukena nītvā \|*  
*sā kāntam ālāpayad eva yatra*  
*kiṁvā na kautūhalam asti tatra \|\|56\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There Lakṣmī performed austerities but did not attain the bhāva of Rādhā and there Rādhā adopted Lakṣmī’s bhāva for fun and spoke to Kṛṣṇa. How amusing it was!**

*kṣīrodadhiḥ śeṣa-viśeṣa-rūpaṁ*  
*sa śeṣa-śāyī svayam asti sāpi \|*  
*tat pāda-saṁvāhana-bhakti-namrā*  
*sakhī janānāṁ kutukāya kamrā \|\|57\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Milk Ocean resides there, and there Kṛṣṇa, who lies on Śeṣa, resides as a special form of Śeṣa. Rādhā, desiring the happiness of her sakhīs, humbly massaged his feet with devotion.**

*mukhyeṣv iha dvādaśa-kānaneṣu*  
*vṛndāvanendor akhila-priyāṇām \|*  
*kuñjādi-nānā-kutukāspadāni*  
*lakṣmyāpi lakṣmyā hṛdi bāspadāni \|\|58\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The many astounding places such as the bowers belonging to the Kṛṣṇa and his beloveds in the major twelve forests are places of great beauty and create tears in Lakṣmī’s heart.**

*śruti-prasiddhāny upakānanāni*  
*hareś caturviṁśatir asti tatra \|*  
*tat kokilā-kānana-nāma yatra*  
*saṅketitaḥ kokila-nāda āsīt \|\|59\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Along with those forests, there are twenty-four smaller forests belonging to the Lord, which are famous in the Vedas. There, in the forest named Kokila-kānana, Kṛṣṇa used a sound like that of the cuckoos to send messages. From this it derives its name.**

*sarvānandathu-mūlam ekam atulaṁ līlaiva tasyā api*  
*rūpaṁ tasya ca dhāma tatra paramaṁ mādhurya-mātraṁ param \|*  
*śrī-vṛndāvanam eva devata iha śrī-rīti-cintāmaṇau*  
*tasyoddeśa-viśesa-leśa-kathane sargas tṛtīyottamaḥ \|\|60\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The pastimes, forms and abodes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are the incomparable source of all bliss. Vṛndāvana shines supreme in sweetness in relation to their pastimes, forms and abodes. Though it exists in the material world, it is beyond the material world. That has been described to a small degree in the third chapter of Vraja-rīti-cintāmaṇi.**
