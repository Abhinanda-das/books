# Yoga-sāra-stava-ṭīkā \[BS\]

## Introduction

*ekadā māsi vaiśākhe ekādaśyāṁ mahā-muniḥ*  
*pūjāṁ kṛtvā hare ramyāṁ vicitrām akarot stutim \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One day in the month of Vaiśākha on Ekādaśi, after performing worship, the great sage recited a variety of attractive praises of the Lord.**

*tadaiva khagam āruhya devo devo hariḥ svayam*  
*ājagāma puras tasya tayā stutyātiharṣitaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**At that time, mounted on Garuḍa, the Lord, pleased by the praise, personally arrived in front of him.**

*taṁ dṛṣṭvā garuḍārūḍhaṁ pratyagra-jalada-cchavim*  
*caturbāhuṁ viśālākṣaṁ sarvālaṅkāra-bhūṣitam \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He saw the Lord mounted on Garuḍa, endowed with the hue of a fresh rain cloud, with four arms, wide eyes and decorated with all ornaments.**

*udbhūtapulako vipraḥ sānanda-jalalocanaḥ*  
*jagāma śirasā bhūmau kṛtakṛtyamanās tadā \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With hairs standing on end, and tears flowing from his eyes in joy, the brāhmaṇa placed his head on the ground, thinking himself successful.**

*na mamau tena harṣeṇa sa brahmāṇḍodare’pi ca*  
*na sasmāra nijaṁ dehaṁ brahmabhūta ivābhavat \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Out of joy, he did not think himself within the universe. He did not remember his body. He seemed to be on the brahma-bhūta level.**

*tataḥ saṁbhāṣitaḥ prītyā hariṇā vaiṣṇavo muniḥ*  
*harir uvāca –*  
*devadyute vijānāmi mad-bhaktas tvaṁ madāśrayaḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Then the Vaiṣṇava sage was addressed by the Lord with affection. The Lord said, “O Devadyuti! I know you are my devotee and have taken shelter of me.**

*man-nyastākhila-karmā hi mad-bhāvo man-manāḥ sadā*  
*varaṁ brūhi prasanno’smi stotreṇānena te’nagha \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You have given up all karmas for me. You have affection for me and think of me at all times. Ask for a benediction. I have been pleased with your praise, O sinless one!**

*lomaśa uvāca –*  
*iti śrutvā harer vākyaṁ pratyuvāca sa tāpasaḥ*  
*devadyutir uvāca –*  
*devadevāravindākṣa sva-māyādhṛta-vigraha \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lomaśa said, “Hearing the Lord’s words, the sage, Devadyuti, said, ‘O Lord of Lord, endowed with lotus eyes! O form supported by your own energy!**

*tvad-darśanāt sadā deva durlabho nāparo varaḥ*  
*brahmādayaḥ surāḥ sarve yoginaḥ sanakādayaḥ \|\|9\|\|*  
*tvāṁ sākṣāt kartum icchanti siddhāś ca kapilādayaḥ*  
*ahaṁ mameti pāśā ye moha-mūlāḥ śubhāśubhāḥ \|\|10\|\|*  
*sahetukāś ca dahyante tvayi dṛṣṭe parāvare*  
*janmanaḥ karmaṇo buddher āvirbhūtaṁ phalaṁ mama \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There is no benediction to compare to you seeing you at any time. The devatās headed by Brahmā, yogīs like the Kumāras, and siddhās like Kapila desire to meet you directly. On seeing you, the totality, they burn up all roots of illusion, either pious or impious, consisting of the ropes of ‘I’ and ‘mine’. The result of my birth, actions and intelligence has appeared.**

*yad dṛṣṭo’si jagannātha prārthaye kim ataḥ param*  
*na varārthaṁ hi deveśa tvat-pādakamalaṁ hṛdi \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O lord of the universe! Since I have seen you, what more could I pray for?**

*cintayāmi sadā bhaktyā tvad-gatenāntarātmanā*  
*imam eva varaṁ yāce tvad-bhaktir acalā mama \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lord of lord! I always think of your lotus feet with devotion, concentrating my mind on you, without desire for benedictions. I pray for this: may I have steady bhakti to you.**

*astu vai kamalānātha prārthaye nāparaṁ varam*  
*lomaśa uvāca –*  
*iti śrutvā vacas-tasya prasanna-vadano hariḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O lord of Lakṣmī! I do not pray for any other benediction. Lomaśa said, “Hearing his words, the Lord with smiling face and pleased mind answered.**

*pratyuvāca prasannātmā evam astu dvijottama*  
*anyas te tapasaḥ kaścit pratyūho na bhaviṣyati \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**‘O brāhmaṇa! Let that be. No other obstacles will appear from austerity.**

*etac ca tvat-kṛtaṁ stotraṁ ye paṭhiṣyanti mānavāḥ*  
*teṣāṁ mad-viṣayā bhaktir niścalā ca bhaviṣyati \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**And those persons who recite the prayers made by you will develop firm bhakti to me.**

*dharma-kāryaṁ ca yat kiñcit sāṅgaṁ sarvaṁ bhaviṣyati*  
*jñāne ca paramā niṣṭhā teṣāṁ sthāsyati niścalā \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All actions of dharma with their limbs will manifest in them. They will develop the highest, immovable steadiness in jñāna.**

*lomaśa uvāca -*  
*ity uktvāntarhitas tatra devadevo janārdanaḥ*  
*devadyutis tadārabhya nārāyaṇa-paro’bhavat \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lomaśa said, “Having said this, the Lord of Lords, Janārdana, disappeared. Devadyuti from that time become absorbed in Nārāyaṇa.**

*vedanidhir uvācamaharṣe’nugṛhīto’smi kathayā pāvanīkṛtaḥ*  
*anayā viṣṇu-saṅgatyā gaṅgayevāham adya vai \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Vedanidhi said, “O great sage! You have been merciful to me by speaking. Today I have been purified as if by the Gaṅgā, who is associated with Viṣṇu.**

*kiṁ tat-stotraṁ samākhyāhi prasanno yena mādhavaḥ*  
*tasyānaghasya viprasya mahat-kautūhalaṁ mama \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Will you explain that prayer of the sinless brāhmaṇa, which gives great joy to me, by which the Lord was pleased?**

*tvat-prasādād ahaṁ vipra manye prāptaṁ manoratham*  
*mahatāṁ saṅgatiḥ kasya mahattvāya na kalpate \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O brāhmaṇa! By your mercy I think that I have attained my desire. There is nothing better than association with great devotees for anyone.**

*kathayasva prasādena viṣṇoḥ stotram anuttamam*  
*yena tuṣṭaḥ sa bhagavān dadau tasya ca darśanam \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Please recite by your mercy the incomparable prayer to Viṣṇu, by which the Lord was satisfied and showed himself.**

*lomaśa uvāca*  
*kathayāmi rahasyaṁ te yaj japtaṁ stotram uttamam*  
*prāg gṛhītaṁ suparṇena garuḍānmayi cāgatam\|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lomaśa said, “I will tell you this excellent secret prayer. It was received by Garuḍa and caused Viṣṇu to come to me.**

*adhyātma-garbha-sāraṁ tan-mahodaya-karaṁ śubham*  
*sarva-pāpa-haraṁ vipra ātma-jñāna-karaṁ param \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O brāhmaṇa! This prayer is the essence of spirituality. It creates auspicious fortune. It destroys all sins and produces the best ātma-jñāna.**

## The Prayer

*oṁ namo vāsudevāya namo viśvāya cakriṇe*  
*bhakta-priyāya kṛṣṇāya jagannāthāya śārṅgiṇe\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to Vāsudeva, to the universe, to the holder of the cakra, to Kṛṣṇa, who is dear to his devotees, to Jagannātha, to the holder of the bow.**

**Комментарий:**

The meaning of the stotra is now given according to what is spoken in verse 9 (of phala-śruti).

*sarvatrāsau samastaṁ ca vasaty atraiva vai yataḥ*  
*ataḥ sa vāsudeveti vidvadbhiḥ paripaṭhyate \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because he lives in all things, everywhere the wise call him Vāsudeva.**

*stotā stutyaḥ stutiḥ sarvaṁ jagad viṣṇu-mayaṁ yadā*  
*tadā saṁstūyase kena bhaktir modakarī nṛṇām \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are the praiser, the praise and the praised. Since the whole universe is full of Viṣṇu, how can you be praised by men? Bhakti alone pleases you.**

**Комментарий:**

In this world, the object of worship and the devotee are different. The prayer cannot have a different object since you are the svarūpa of Brahman.

*yasya devasya niḥśvāso vedāḥ sāṅgāḥ sasūtrakāḥ*  
*kā stutiḥ pramude tasya bhaktyā’haṁ mukharo’bhavam \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Vedas, their aṅgas and the sūtras arise from the Lord’s breathing. What praise can please him? I have become a fool.**

**Комментарий:**

Sūtras refers to Jaimini’s Karma-mīmāṁsa sūtras and Vyāsa’s Brahma-mīmāṁsa sūtras which delineate the meaning of the Vedas. The Vedas are like breathing since they cannot determine the svarūpa of Brahman. Just as breathing takes place without effort, and is nondifferent from the jīva, so the Vedas also are nondifferent from the Lord.

*cakravad-bhramate sarvaṁ trai-lokyaṁ sa-carācaram*  
*atas tvaṁ gīyate deva cakrapāṇir varāyudhaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The three worlds turn like a wheel with all moving and nonmoving beings. Thus, you are glorified as the holder of the wheel, O possessor of the best weapons!**

*vedo na vakti yaṁ sākṣān na ca vāg vetti no manaḥ*  
*mad-vidhas taṁ kathaṁ stauti bhaktimān vā kathaṁ vadet \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Vedas cannot describe the Lord directly nor can words nor mind. How can I praise the Lord? How can the devotee speak of you?**

**Комментарий:**

The Vedas and words cannot directly describe the Lord. They describe him by other words of negation such as asparśa, arūpa, avayaya: he cannot be touched, he has no form, he is without decay. Or to negate other material concepts, they describe him by positive words such as satyaṁ jñānam anantaṁ brahma: the Lord is truth and unlimited knowledge. Thus, ordinary words such as pot or cloth cannot describe the Lord. Words can only indicate the Lord indirectly. One can realize the Lord’s svarūpa only by giving up mind, yato vāca nivartante: words and mind cannot approach the Lord. (Taittirīya Upaniṣad [2.9.1](https://vedarama.com/en/books/taittiriya/2.9.1))

*brahmādi-brahma-viṣṇus tvaṁ tvam brahmaṇo vapuḥ*  
*sraṣṭā brahma-nidānaṁ ca śuddhaṁ brahma tvam eva ca \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are greater that Brahmā, the head of the devatās. You are Viṣṇu, pervading everywhere. You are the body of Brahman. You are the creator, the cause of Brahmā. You alone are pure Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahma means great. You are greater than Brahmā, the head of the devatās. You pervade everywhere (viṣṇuḥ). Or you are the source of Hiraṇyagarbha Brahmā. He is called Brahmā because he arose from your navel lotus. You are Viṣṇu. Viṣṇu has two forms: without qualities and with qualities. Among them, you are also Brahman without qualities. You are the svarūpa of Parabrahman. Brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāham: I am the basis of Brahman. (BG [14.27](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/14.27)) You are the cause of Brahmā, endowed with the quality of being a creator, who is the cause of creation, etc. You are Parāmātmā, neutral to all beings (śuddham).

*kāya-kāyi-yugāṁ yā cid bhittvā spṛśati kāyinam*  
*kāya-doṣair na cāghrātas taṁ bhāsi yoginām \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Destroying the duality of body and possessor of the body and contacting the possessor of the body, untouched by the faults of the material body, you manifest to the yogīs.**

**Комментарий:**

The form of jñāna (cit) present now as various impositions on the body (kāya) and jīva (kāyī), on destroying the identification with the body and the individual jīva, becomes the form of nirviśeṣa “consciousness alone” rather than the body and jīva. Though present in the bodies, it is not touched by the faults of the body just as sky is not touched by the clouds. You manifest to the yogīs by that cit.

*deha-bhāve na jāgarti na nidrāti nijātmani*  
*sukha-sandoha-buddhir yā sā tvaṁ viṣṇo na saṁśayaḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This jīva is not awake to being his body but is constantly conscious of his svarūpa. O Viṣṇu! Without doubt, you are intelligence full of bliss.**

**Комментарий:**

You are intelligence full of bliss. The jīvas is not aware of being a body, that which is not ātmā. He does not identify with the body since he is very different. He is constantly aware (na nidrāti) of his svarūpa as nirviśeṣa ātmā (nijātmani).

*mahadādir dvidhābhāvas tathā vaikārikā guṇāḥ*  
*tvam eva nātha tat-sarvaṁ nānātvaṁ mūḍha-kalpanā \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Lord! You are everything — mahat-tattva and other elements of transformed state of prakṛti, and the guṇas as well. Variety is imagined by the fool.**

**Комментарий:**

Mūla-prakṛti is the untransformed state. Mahat-tattva and other elements are the seven transformed states. Sixteen other elements are also transformations. The puruṣa is not a transformation and is not prakṛti. This is the Sāṅkhya system of twenty-five elements. Prakṛti is a state of equilibrium of sattva, rajas and tamas. From prakṛti arises mahat-tattva. From mahat-tattva rises ahaṅkāra. From ahaṅkara rises the five tan-mātras and the eleven senses. This is the order of development. The word mahat also is called buddhi. Its quality is ahaṅkāra, which identifies itself as I for body and senses. The knowledge and actions senses and mind are the eleven senses. With the five gross elements such as earth, etc., this become sixteen transformations. The two types of transformations and the sixteen items are you. Variety is a product of ignorance. With the seven starting with mahat-tattva (mahat-tattva, ahaṅkāra, five senses objects) there are the two types of transformation of prakṛti.

*keśa-keśava-rūpābhiḥ kalpanā tisṛbhis tathā*  
*tvam eva kalpyase brahma pumān iva sutādibhiḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**People imagine that you, Brahman, have three forms of Brahmā, Śiva and Viṣṇu just as a man is imaged through his sons.**

**Комментарий:**

Ka means Brahmā. Īśa is Śiva and Keśava is Viṣṇu. Śruti says ātmā vai jāyate putraḥ: the self is born as a son. Thus, people imagine that the father becomes sons or grandsons. Similarly they think of Viṣṇu in the form of Brahmā and Śiva.

*vidoṣaṁ viguṇaṁ caikaṁ cinmūrtir akhilaṁ jagat*  
*kavīnāṁ bhāti yad bhānāt taṁ viṣṇuṁ naumi nirmalam \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to pure Viṣṇu-tattva, who manifests to the enlightened sages as a form of consciousness, devoid of faults and qualities, who is the whole universe.**

**Комментарий:**

Persons who have realized ātma-tattva (kavayaḥ) see the Lord as Brahman, one conscious entity devoid of sattva, rajas and tamas, who constitutes the whole universe.

*yasmin jñāte na kurvanti karma cāpi śruti-bhāṣitam*  
*nireṣaṇā jaganmitrāḥ śuddhaṁ brahma namāmi tat \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the pure Brahman, knowing which people without desires for material goals, friends of the universe, reject karmas prescribed in the Vedas.**

**Комментарий:**

Nireṣaṇā means persons who have given up desire for sons, wealth and followers since they are devoid of making external material goals, since they are fixed in Brahman.

*kāmayante prajā naiva eṣāṇābhyaḥ samutthitāḥ*  
*lokam ātmani paśyanto yaṁ buddhaika-carā narāḥ \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who, understanding the Lord, see the whole world in the Lord, having fixed their minds on the Lord, having risen above wives and sons, do not desire children.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowing the Lord (yam) those who have given up wives and sons, do not desire sons.

*svaṁ svetaraṁ ca san-mātraṁ yat prabodhād upāsate*  
*yoginaḥ sarva-bhūteṣu sad-rūpaṁ naumi taṁ harim \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord endowed with eternal form whom the yogīs worship in all beings as existence alone, after realizing the self and the svarūpa different from oneself.**

**Комментарий:**

From realizing the svarūpa different from oneself (svetaram), the yogīs worship pure existence (sanmātram) as the supreme ātmā of all beings.

*brahmāham iti gāyanti yaṁ jñātvaikaṁ varā dvijāḥ*  
*paśyanto hi tvacā tulyaṁ dehaṁ taṁ naumi mādhavam \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects the Lord, Mādhava, whom the best brāhmaṇas knowing him as oneness and seeing their bodies to be like snake skins, sing ‘I am Brahman.’**

**Комментарий:**

They see their bodies like snake skins.

*māyayā moha-vaicitryaṁ tathāhaṁ mamatāṁ nṝṇām*  
*vilīyate viditvā yaṁ namas tasmā amāyine \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord devoid of māyā, knowing whom, the varieties of illusion created by māyā and the concepts of me and mine are destroyed.**

**Комментарий:**

The illusion of māyā (māyāmohaḥ) is that one believes he is the body, senses, and antaḥkaraṇa and develops possessiveness of sons etc. Knowing the Lord by constant remembrance on giving up the body, this illusion arising from māyā is destroyed. I offer respects to the Lord who is beyond the illusions of māyā. The illusion of māyā can also mean ignorance of one’s svarūpa as a spiritual entity.

*prayāṇe vāprayāṇe ca yan nāma-smaratāṁ nṝṇām*  
*sadyo naśyanti pāpaugho namas tasmai cidātmane \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the all-knowing Lord. By remembering his name, men destroy heaps of sin instantly while dying or while living.**

**Комментарий:**

While dying (prayāṇe) or while living (aprayāṇe), any human, without regard for varṇa or āśrama, immediately destroys saṁsāra (pāpaughaḥ) by remembering the name of the Lord. Sadya (immediately) means that there is no return to the material world.

*mohānalollasaj-jvālā jvalal-lokeṣu sarvadā*  
*yan-nāmāmburuha-cchāyāṁ praviṣṭaś ca na dahyate \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Entering the shade of the lotus feet of the name a person is not burned at any time, among people burning with the blazing fire of illusion.**

**Комментарий:**

Now remembering and chanting the Lord’s name as a cause of tasting the highest bliss by destroying all anarthas is described. Among persons who are burning brightly in the fire of the threefold miseries (moha), he who has entered the shade or ābhāsa of lotus feet of the Lord’s name has no suffering. The name is compared to a lotus because it produces a healthy state. This person who somehow takes shelter of the name is not pained (na dahyate). Freed from the fire of the three miseries, he experiences Brahman full of eternal sweet, intense bliss. Moreover, Viṣṇudharmottara says:

*saṅkirtya nārāyaṇa-śabda-mātraṁ*  
*vimukta-duḥkhāḥ sukhino bhavanti*

**Перевод:**

**Just by chanting the sound Nārāyaṇa people become free of suffering and become happy.**

**Комментарий:**

“Just chanting the sound Nārāyaṇa” means without knowing the meaning and without faith.

When Ajāmila was dying, by chanting Nārāyaṇa’s name to indicate his son, he became liberated. It does not depend on understanding the power of the name. If an ignorant person throws fire on his house thinking it is guñja berries (red) the house will burn. If one drinks nectar, thinking it is water, one becomes immortal. If one drinks poison, thinking it is ghee, one will die.

*yasya smaraṇa-mātreṇa na moho naiva durgatiḥ*  
*na rogā naiva duḥkhāni tam ānandaṁ nato’smy aham \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the blissful Lord. Just by remembering him there is no illusion, no suffering in future life, no sickness and no suffering.**

*śabdārthaḥ saṁvid-arthaś ca viṣṇor nāsti paro yadi*  
*tena satyena saṁsāro mā saṁspṛśatu mādhava \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Since there is nothing other than Viṣṇu in terms of meaning of words and conscious objects, then may saṁsāra never touch me by this truth.**

**Комментарий:**

Directly or indirectly (through paramaparā) all words arising from the Vedas mean Viṣṇu. Viṣṇu is the prayojana. Moreover, by knowledge of the form of consciousness (the Lord) one realizes all objects. Brahman is consciousness since everything else is unconscious.

*ikṣāṁ cakre sa evāgre ya ekaḥ syāṁ bahus tathā*  
*praviṣṭo devatāḥ sṛṣṭvā sa prasīdatu me hariḥ \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**First, he glanced. He desired to become many. Having created the devatās, he entered. May that Lord be pleased with me.**

**Комментарий:**

At the beginning of creation (agre) the Supreme Lord (saḥ) glanced or deliberated (īkṣāṁ cakre), “Let me become many as the material world.” After creating devatās in the form of fire, water and earth, making them threefold, he entered as the jīvas.

*nārāyaṇo jagad-vyāpī yadi vedādi-saṁmataḥ*  
*satyena tena nirvighnā viṣṇu-bhaktir mamāstu vai \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Since Nārāyaṇa, pervading the universe is approved by the Vedas, by that truth may I have unobstructed bhakti to Viṣṇu.**

**Комментарий:**

This means that there is no doubt that Nārāyaṇa is approved by the Vedas. One must accept the fact. It is like the expression, “If the Vedas are proof, (then this must be accepted as a fact).”

*yo na bījaṁ na cābījaṁ bījaṁ yo bīja-bhāvitaḥ*  
*sa viṣṇur bhava-bījaṁ me śita-vidyāsinā dyatu \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May Viṣṇu, who is not the cause and also is the cause of the universe, who is inspired to act by the seed of the jīva’s karma, destroy the seed of saṁsāra with the sword of sharp knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is not the cause (bījam) of saṁsāra and is also the cause (na abījam). May Viṣṇu destroy (dyatu) the cause of saṁsāra (bhava-bījam), ignorance, by excellent (śita) knowledge, like a sharp sword. How is the Lord cause and not the cause? That is explained. He is inspired to act by the jīva’s karma. A magnet is not the cause when it does not attract iron. When the magnet is near iron, it is the cause of attraction since its nature manifests at that time. The Lord is inspired to create the universe by the seeds — the karmas of all living beings. He acts when the karmas become ready to give their results.

*tridhā tu naṭavad yas tu sṛṣṭi-sthiti-layeṣu ca*  
*guṇair bhavati kāryeṣu sa prasīdatu me hariḥ \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord who takes three forms through the guṇas, like an actor, during creation, maintenance and destruction, be pleased with me.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord takes the forms of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva, forms of rajas, sattva, and tamas. May that Lord be pleased with me.

*daśa-dehā’vatīrṇo yo dharma-trāṇāya kevalam*  
*abhyarthitaḥ suraiḥ sarvaiḥ sa prasīdatu me hariḥ\|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord who appears with ten forms in this world only for delivering dharma, being requested by all the devatās, be pleased with me.**

**Комментарий:**

The ten forms are Matsya, etc.

*brahmādi-stamba-paryantaṁ prāṇi-hṛn-mandire’malaḥ*  
*eko vasati yo devaḥ sa prasīdatu me hariḥ \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the one pure Lord, who lives in the temple of the hearts of all living beings from Brahmā to a clump of grass, be pleased with me.**

**Комментарий:**

May the one pure Lord, who resides in the hearts of all moving and nonmoving beings in the forms of Brahmā down to grass, be pleased with me.

*hṛt-khagaḥ kha-samaḥ khādiḥ khātītaḥ kha-kriyo ’khagaḥ*  
*khaṁ brahma khādibhuk khānte kha-mūrtis tvam akhāsamaḥ \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are the bird in the heart. You are all-pervading like ether. You are the source of ether. You are superior to ether. You perform actions of creating even ether. You are beyond the senses. You are Brahman, pure like ether. You destroy all the elements including ether. After destruction of ether, you remain as the form with limbs and without material guṇas, since you are the form of pure ether.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord exists (ga) in the sky of the heart (hṛt-kha). He is the object of meditation in this form (in the Upaniṣads). The Lord is like ether (kha-samaḥ) since he has no limbs, is all pervading and moves about internally (i.e. is active inside). He is the origin of ether (khādiḥ) since he is its cause. He exists beyond ether (khātītaḥ). The Lord performs actions of creation, even of the void (kha-kriyaḥ). Even the void is the object of his action. He is not limited by ether (akha-gaḥ) since as Paramātmā he is of greater dimension. Or he is not perceived(gaḥ) by the senses (kha). The supreme Brahman is like pure ether (khaṁ brahma). The Lord is brahma-tattva within which lies ether. The Lord destroys (bhuk) ether and everything (khādi) else at pralaya. Or all effects such as ether merges into him. After destruction (khānte) you exist as the form of ether (khamūrtiḥ) since you have no limbs and are without material guṇas (asaṅgaḥ), since you are not like material ether (khāsamaḥ). You are the form of pure ether.

*yad-bhāsā yan-mudā yasya sattayā santyajej jagat*  
*jāḍyaṁ duḥkham asatyaṁ ca sa bhavān eva tanmayaḥ \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By the Lord’s manifestation, one should give up the material world. By the Lord’s bliss one should destroy all suffering. By the Lord’s existing form, one should destroy nonexistence. You are the creator of the universe.**

**Комментарий:**

By the manifestation (bhāsā) of the creator of the universe (yat) one gives up the material world (jāḍyam). By his bliss one destroys all suffering. By his real form one destroys nonexistence. You are the creator (tanmayah).

*tvat-spṛṣṭaṁ modate viśvaṁ tvat-tyaktam aśucir bhavet*  
*tat-saṅgato’py asaṅgas tvaṁ vikāras tena te na hi \|\|28\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Touched by you, the universe becomes active. Rejected by you, it becomes impure. Though associated with the world, you are not contaminated by it. For that reason, there is no change in you.**

**Комментарий:**

When the world is created by you (spṛṣṭaṁ), when the jīva is made to enter it, it becomes active with you (modate). Rejected by you, when you withdraw the jīva, it becomes like a corpse (aśuciḥ). Like ether, though you associate with the world, you remain unattached to it (asaṅgah). For that reason (tena), you (te) have no change (vikaraḥ).

*kecid buddhiṁ pare prāṇaṁ mano’haṅkāram āntaram*  
*kecic ca kāya-caitanyaṁ kāya-mātraṁ pare ’dhamāḥ \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some say you are intelligence. Others say you are prāṇa, the mind or ahaṅkāra within. Some people say you are the body with consciousness and the lowest say you are just the body.**

**Комментарий:**

Some say you are ahaṅkāra within (antaram). Some say consciousness arises in a bodily form. Some say you are a transformation of the body (kāya-mātraṁ). The word puruṣa is used in Sāṅkhya.

*kecic ca puruṣaṁ śabdaṁ brahma kecit pare śivam*  
*śaktiṁ ca cinmayīṁ kecid govinda tvāṁ kumārga-gāḥ \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some say you are the jīva or the Vedas. Some say you are Śiva and some erring people say you are the conscious Śakti. O Govinda!**

**Комментарий:**

Some say you are the puruṣa. Some say you are the sound of the Vedas. This is presented in Pañcarātra where śabda is said to be the cause of the universe. The pāśu-patas say you are Śiva. The śāktas say that you are conscious śakti.

*tīṛthātīrtham adhiṣtḥāya keśākeśī mitho janāḥ*  
*yad vadanti punas tvaṁ hi cit-sattānanda-lakṣaṇaḥ \|\|31\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some say you, who are sac-cid-ānanda, are established by the Vedas. Others reject the Vedas. They argue with each other.**

**Комментарий:**

Tīrtha means the Veda since it delivers (tārayati) one from adharma. The six darśanas of nyāya, vaiśeṣika, sāṅkhya, pātañjala, karma-mīmāṁsa and brahma-mīmāṁsa and the smṛtis and purāṇas are accepted as tīrtha since they explain the meaning of the Vedas. Buddhism, pāśupata, kāpālikas, Jains and Lokāyatas are excluded from the Vedas. They are atīrtha (mithaḥ). They argue intensely (keśākeśi). Taking shelter of tīrtha or atīrtha, they speak of you without directly knowing your svarūpa. But you are cit-sattānanda. Using statements such as satyaṁ jñānam anantaṁ brahma and vijñānam ānandam brahma, the tīrtha followers come to various conclusions. Those who are atīrtha have doubts about this topic and speak using various names.

*bhūta-yoga-ja-caitanyaṁ cārvākās tvām upāsate*  
*saugatā bruvate tarkais tvāṁ buddhiṁ kṣaṇa-bhaṅgurām \|\|32\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Cārvākas worship you as consciousness arising from combination of material elements. The Buddhists say by logic that you are intelligence which is destroyed at every moment.**

**Комментарий:**

The various philosophies described in the three previous verses (29-31) are now explained in three verses (32-34) while mentioning their foolish names. This verse explains kāyacaitanyaṁ (in verse 29). The followers of Cārvāka, the Lokāyatas, say that consciousness arises from a combination of elements (bhūta) like earth, etc., in the body, just as chuna and betel nut produce red color. They say that the jīva separate from the body does not exist to enjoy results in another birth. The Buddhists (saugatā) by non-Vedic logic say that you are intelligence, which is destroyed at every moment.

*śarīra-parimāṇaṁ ca manyante jina-devatāḥ*  
*dhyāyanti puruṣaṁ sāṅkhyās tvām eva prakṛteḥ param \|\|33\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Jains think that you are a transformation of the body. The followers of Sāṅkhya meditate on you as the puruṣa, superior to prakṛti.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse explains kāya-mātram (in verse 29). The Jains, following Ārhata conclusions, say that you are transformation of the body. Then those who claim you are the puruṣa are described. Followers of Sāṅkhya say that prakṛti is unconscious but active. Puruṣa is inactive but conscious. By their combination, all actions take place and saṁsāra arises. He who can distinguish prakṛti and puruṣa becomes separated from prakṛti and remains as a liberated entity. This is Sāṅkhya philosophy.

*janmādi-rahitaḥ pūrṇaṁ cit-sattānanda-lakṣaṇam*  
*tvām aupaniṣadā brahma cintayanti parātparam \|\|34\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Vedāntists meditate on you as Brahman, complete, beyond birth and other transformations, and characterized by cit, sat and ānanda.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahman is devoid of the six transformations starting with birth. Aupaniṣadā means those who follow the conclusions of Vedānta.

*khādi-bhūtāni dehaś ca mano buddhīndriyāṇi ca*  
*vidyāvidye tvam evātra nānyat tvatto’sti kiñcana \|\|35\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are ether and other elements. You are the body, mind, intelligence, the senses, vidyā and avidyā. Other things also are not different from you.**

**Комментарий:**

The body is a transformation of the elements. You are also other things to be mentioned.

*tvam agnis tvaṁ havis tvaṁ sruk hotā mantraḥ kriyā-phalam*  
*tvam asti nāsti vaikuṇṭha tvām ahaṁ śaraṇaṁ gataḥ \|\|36\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are fire. You are the oblation. You are the ladle. You are the hotā, the mantra and the result of the sacrifice. O Vaikuṇṭha! Nothing except you exists. I surrender to you.**

**Комментарий:**

For performers of sacrifices, you alone are everything—produced from action, the performer, the result and the instruments. You are everything (asti) that is employed since there is nothing except you (nāsti).

*tvaṁ karma-phala-dātā ca dīkṣitānāṁ kriyā-kṣaye*  
*tvaṁ setuḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ tvam eva śaraṇaṁ mama \|\|37\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are the giver of results for persons performing karmas for future results. You are the maintainer of limits for all beings. I surrender to you.**

**Комментарий:**

When persons perform karmas for future results (dīkṣitānām), they do not see results like Svarga just by completing the karma. In another birth (kriyā-kṣaye), you give the results of karma. You are the maintainer of limits for all beings (setuḥ).

*yuvatīnāṁ yathā yūni yūnāṁ ca yuvatau yathā*  
*mano’bhiramate tadvat mano me ramatāṁ tvayi \|\|38\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the minds of young girls are attracted to boys, and the minds of boys are attracted to girls, may my mind take pleasure in you.**

**Комментарий:**

The meaning is easy to understand.

*api pāpaṁ durācāraṁ naraṁ tvat-pravaṇaṁ hare*  
*nekṣante kiṅkarā yāmyā ulūkās tapanaṁ yathā \|\|39\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The servants of Yamarāja do not see the persons who meditates on you, though he has committed sins or bad deeds, just as the owls do not see the sun.**

**Комментарий:**

The meaning is easy to understand.

*tāpa-trayam aghaughaś ca tāvat pīḍayate janam*  
*yāvat śrayati no nātha bhaktyā tvat-pāda-paṅkajam \|\|40\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O Lord! As long as one does not take shelter of your lotus feet with devotion, the three miseries and heaps of sin will afflict the person.**

**Комментарий:**

The three sufferings are ādhidaivika, ādhibhautika and ādhyātmika. Pains arising from lightning bolts, rain, etc., are adhidaivika. Pains from tigers, humans, etc., are adhibhautika. Ādhyātmika pains are two types: physical and mental. Physical pains are those caused by disturbance of air, bile and phlegm. Mental afflictions are caused by lust, anger, etc. Agaughaḥ means all sins.

*yaṁ na spṛśanti guṇa-jāti-śarīra-dharmā*  
*yan na spṛśanti gatayas kham ivendriyāṇām \|*  
*yaṁ ca spṛśanti yatayo gata-saṅga-mohās*  
*tasmai namo bhagavate haraye pratīce \|\|41\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer my respects to the indwelling Lord whom the qualities of the guṇas, birth, and body do not touch, whom, like ether, the actions of the senses do not perceive, and whom the sannyāsīs who have given up illusion realize.**

**Комментарий:**

The qualities of the guṇas are sattva, rajas and tamas, colored white, red and black. Qualities of birth means being born as a cow, human or devatā. Qualities of the body means hands, feet, etc. The functions of the senses do not touch the Lord, just as ether is untouched by the other elements. Moha means attachment to body, etc., by association with sons and wife. I offer respects to the inner form (pratīce) of the Lord whom the sannyāsīs realize.

*sthūlaṁ vilāpya karaṇe karaṇaṁ nidāne*  
*tat-kāraṇaṁ karaṇa-kāraṇa-varjite ca \|*  
*itthaṁ vilāpya munayaḥ praviśanti yatra*  
*taṁ tvāṁ hariṁ viśada-bodha-ghanaṁ namāmi \|\|42\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to you, Lord, a form of pure, condensed knowledge into whom the sages enter after merging the gross elements into to the sense objects, the sense objects into Brahman endowed with ignorance, that Brahman endowed with ignorance into pure Parabrahman, devoid of previous factors.**

**Комментарий:**

Sthūlam refers to earth, etc. Karaṇe means into the sense objects (tan-mātras) such as fragrance, etc. The sense objects are merged into Brahman endowed with ignorance, the original cause (kāraṇam). That original cause is merged into pure Parabrahman, devoid of karaṇa and kāraṇa. I offer respects to you, the svarūpa of pure, condensed knowledge.

*yad-dhyāna-saṁvadana-cūrṇa-vaśīkṛtāntām*  
*aiśvarya-cāru-guṇinīṁ sukha-mokṣa-lakṣmīm\|*  
*āliṅgya śerata ihātma-sukhaika-bhājas*  
*tasmai namo’stu haraye muni-sevitāya \|\|43\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord who is served by the sages, and whom the ātmārāmas easily attain after embracing the wealth happiness of liberation endowed with qualities of beauty and power, which is brought under control by meditation.**

**Комментарий:**

After attaining the happiness of liberation compared to Lakṣmī, and who is the form of tasty bliss devoid of all agitation, by meditation on the Lord the ātmārāmas attain the Lord. That liberation has beautiful qualities because it is full of independent power (aiśvarya-cāru-guṇinīm). It is brought under control merely by meditation on the Lord.

*janmādi-bhāva-vikṛter viraha-svabhāve*  
*yasmin layaṁ paricinoti ṣaḍ-ūrmi-vargaḥ \|*  
*yaṁ tāḍayanti na sadā madanādi-doṣās*  
*taṁ vāsudevam amalaṁ praṇato’smi hārdam \|\|44\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to pure Vāsudeva in the heart, who is devoid by nature of transformations starting with birth, in whom the six urges are destroyed and whom the faults like illusion can never afflict.**

**Комментарий:**

The six transformations are birth, maintenance, growth, change, withering and dying. The Lord is devoid of these (virahasvabhāve). The six urges (ṣaḍ-ūrmi-vargaḥ) are eating, thirst, lamentation, illusion, birth and death. They cannot obstruct the Lord. Hārdam refers to the Lord in the heart, worshipped as antaryāmī.

*yad bhāna-saṅgatam alaṁ vijahāty avidyāṁ*  
*yad bhāna-vahni-janitaṁ jagad eti dāham \|*  
*yad bhānam ullasad asiryati saṁśayāriṁ*  
*taṁ tvāṁ hariṁ viśada-bodha-ghanaṁ namāmi \|\|45\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Lord, a form of pure, condensed knowledge, by whose manifestation ignorance is destroyed, by whose fire the universe is destroyed, and by whose manifestation doubts are destroyed.**

**Комментарий:**

The appearance of the Lord and meeting him (bhānam) destroys ignorance of the Lord -- thinking he is something else. That ignorance, bondage, is the cause of saṁsāra. Realizing directly the Lord destroys saṁsāra or burns it. By manifestation of the Lord, like a gleaming knife (ullasat), doubts concerning ātmā, which are enemies are cut.

## Phala-śrutiḥ

*carācarāṇi bhūtāni sarvāṇi ca hareḥ puraḥ*  
*yathā ca tena satyena puras tiṣṭhatu me hariḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**All moving and nonmoving beings remain in front of the Lord. By this principle may the Lord remain in front of me.**

*yathā nārāyaṇaḥ sarvaṁ jagat sthāvara-jaṁgamam*  
*tena satyena me rūpaṁ pradarśayatu keśavaḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as Nārāyaṇa shows himself to all moving and nonmoving beings in the universe, may he show his form to me.**

*bhaktir yathā harau me’sti tad-variṣṭhā gurau yadi*  
*mamāsti tena satyena svaṁ darśayatu keśavaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If I have more devotion to guru than to the Lord, then by this principle may the Lord show himself to me.**

*tasyaivaṁ śapathaiḥ satyair bhaktiṁ tasyānucintayan*  
*darśayām āsa cātmānaṁ saṁprītaḥ puruṣottamaḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Thinking of the devotee’s bhakti with his own vow, the affectionate Lord showed himself.**

*tato dattvā varaṁ tasya pūrayitvā manoratham*  
*jagāma kamalā-kāntaḥ stutyā vipreṇa toṣitaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Giving him benedictions, fulfilling his desire, satisfied with the brāhmaṇa’s praise, the Lord departed.**

*kṛtakṛtyo dvijaḥ so’pi vāsudeva-parāyaṇaḥ*  
*śiṣyaiḥ sārdhaṁ japan stotraṁ tasminn āste tapovane\|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The brāhmāṇa who was surrendered to the Lord, being successful, stayed in the forest of austerities, chanting the verses with his disciples.**

*kīrtayed ya idaṁ stotraṁ śṛṇuyād yo’pi mānavaḥ*  
*aśvamedhasya yajñasya prāpnoty avikalaṁ phalam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One who chants these prayers and hears these prayers attains the full result of a aśvamedha sacrifice.**

*ātmavidyā-prabodhaṁ ca labhate brāhmaṇaḥ sadā*  
*na pāpe jāyate buddhir naiva paśyaty amaṁgalam \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The brāhmaṇa always attains realization of ātmā. He never thinks of sin and never sees anything inauspicious.**

*buddhi-svāsthyaṁ manaḥ-svāsthyaṁ svāsthyam aindriyakaṁ tathā*  
*nṛṇāṁ bhavati sarveṣām asya stotrasya kīrtanāt \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By chanting these prayers all men develop healthy intelligence, mind and senses.**

*vicāryārthaṁ japed yas tu śraddhayā tat-paro naraḥ*  
*sa vidhūyeha pāpāni labhate vaiṣṇavaṁ padam \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The surrendered person who chants with faith, while deliberating on the meaning of the verses, destroying all sins, attains the spiritual world.**

*vāñchitān labhate kāmān purtān prāpnoty anuttamān*  
*dīrgham āyur balaṁ vīryaṁ labhate sa sadā paṭhan \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Reciting these verses, he attains all desires and excellent sons. He attains long life, strength and courage.**

*tilapātra-sahasreṇa go-sahasreṇa yat-phalam*  
*tat-phalaṁ samavāpnoti ya imāṁ kīrtayet stutim \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who sings these prayers attains the result of giving a thousand vessels of sesame or a thousand cows.**

*dharmārtha-kāma-mokṣāṇāṁ yaṁ yaṁ kāmayate sadā*  
*acirāt tam avāpnoti stotreṇānena mānavaḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By this prayer a man quickly attains whatever one desires in dharma, artha, kāma and mokṣa.**

*ācāre vinaye dharme jñāne tapasi sannaye*  
*nṝṇāṁ bhavati nityaṁ dhīr imāṁ saṁśṛṇvatāṁ stutim \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The intelligence of persons who hear these prayers becomes skillful in conduct, humility, dharma, jñāna, austerity and morality.**

*mahā-pātaka-yukto’pi yukto vā sarva-pātakaiḥ*  
*sadyo bhavati śuddhātmā stotrasya paṭhanāt sakṛt \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**By reciting the verses, the person who has committed great sins or all sins becomes immediately purified.**

*prajñā-lakṣmī-yaśaḥ-kīrti-jñāna-dharma-vivardhanam*  
*duṣṭa-grahopaśamanaṁ sarvāśubha-vināśanam \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The prayers increase wisdom, wealth, fame, glory, knowledge and dharma. They pacify evil planets and destroy all inauspiciousness.**

*sarva-vyādhi-haraṁ pathyaṁ sarvāriṣṭa-niṣūdanam*  
*durgates tāraṇaṁ stotraṁ paṭhitavyaṁ dvijātibhiḥ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The dvijas should recite this prayer which destroys all sickness and all misfortunes. It delivers one from all distress.**

*nakṣatra-grahāpīḍāsu rāja-daiva-bhayeṣu ca*  
*agni-cora-nipāteṣu sadyaḥ saṅkīrtayed idam \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should recite this prayer immediately when afflicted by bad stars, when afraid of kings or devatās, or when in danger from thieves or fire.**

*siṁha-vyāghra-bhayaṁ nāsti nābhicāra-bhayaṁ tathā*  
*bhūta-preta-piśācebhyo rākṣasebhyas tathaiva ca \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There is no fear of lions or tigers and no fear of curses. There is no fear from ghosts, spirits, witches or demons.**

*pūtanā-jṛṁbhakebhyaś ca vighnebhyaś caiva sarvaśaḥ*  
*nṝṇāṁ kvacid bhayaṁ nāsti stave hy asmin prakīrtite \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When chanting these verses, there is no fear from pūtanas or jṛṁbhakas and no fear of obstacles at all.**

*vāsudevasya pūjāṁ yaḥ kṛtvā stotram udīrayet*  
*lipyate pātakair nāsau padma-patram ivāṁbhasā \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who recites this prayer after worshipping Vāsudeva is not touched by sin, like a lotus leaf in water.**

*gaṅgādi-puṇya-tīrtheṣu yā snānair nāpyate gatiḥ*  
*tāṁ gatiṁ samavāpnoti paṭhan puṇyām imāṁ stutim \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one recites this pious prayer one attains the result that one cannot attain even by bathing in holy tīrthas like the Gaṅgā.**

*eka-kālaṁ dvi-kālaṁ vā tri-kālaṁ vāpi yaḥ paṭhet*  
*sarvadā sarva-kāleṣu so’kṣayaṁ sukham aśnute \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who recites this prayer one time, two times or three times always attains eternal happiness continuously.**

*caturṇām sāṅga-vedānāṁ trir āvṛttyā ca yat-phalam*  
*tat-phalaṁ labhate stotram adhīyānaḥ sakṛn naraḥ*  
*akṣayaṁ dhanam āpnoti strīṇāṁ bhavati vallabhaḥ \|\|24\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The person who recites this prayer immediately attains the results obtained by reciting three times the four Vedas with their aṅgas. He attains inexhaustible wealth and becomes beloved of women.**

*pūjyo bhavati loke’smin śraddhayā saṁsmaran stutim*  
*sarvadā saṁpadā yukto vipadaṁ naiva gacchati \|\|25\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Remembering the verses with faith, he becomes worshipped in this world. He is always wealthy and does not meet danger.**

*gobhir na hiyate stotraṁ nityaṁ yaḥ parikīrtayet*  
*alakṣmīḥ kāla-kalanam duḥsvapnaṁ durvicintitam*  
*sadyo naśyanti bhaktānām etaṁ saṁśṛṇvatāṁ stavam \|\|26\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who chants this prayer constantly is not deprived of cows. Devotees who hear this prayer destroy lack of wealth, the effects of time, bad dreams and bad thoughts.**

*prātar utthāya yo’dhīte śucis tad gata-mānasaḥ*  
*akṣayaṁ labhate saukhyam iha loke paratra ca \|\|27\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who recites this prayer with concentration after rising early in the morning and purifying himself, attains eternal happiness in this life and the next.**

*deva-dyuti-praṇītaṁ vai viṣṇu-prītikaraṁ śubham*  
*viṣṇu-prasāda-jananaṁ viṣṇu-darśana-kārakam \|\|28\|\|*  
*yoga-sāram idaṁ nāma stotraṁ parama-pāvanam*  
*yaḥ paṭhet satataṁ bhaktyā viṣṇu-lokaṁ sa gacchati \|\|29\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He who recites constantly with devotion this most purifying prayer called yoga-sāra composed by Devadyuti, which is most auspicious and which pleases Viṣṇu, which invokes Viṣṇu’s mercy and lets one see Viṣṇu, goes to the abode of Viṣṇu.**

*iti te kathitaṁ stotraṁ puṇyam parama-pāvanam*  
*ata ūrdhvaṁ pravakṣyāmi piśācasya ca mocanam \|\|30\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I have recited the purifying prayer filled with auspiciousness. Next I will explain liberation of witches.**

## About Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī

In the Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā (195) it is said that Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī was formerly Vilāsamañjarī gopī. Born in Rāmakeli, he was the nephew of the illustrious brothers Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana, who were the objects of Śrī Caitanya's unlimited mercy. From his very childhood Jīva Gosvāmī was greatly fond of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. He later came to Navadvīpa to study Sanskrit, and, following in the footsteps of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu, he circumambulated the entire Navadvīpa-dhāma. After visiting Navadvīpa-dhāma he went to Benares to study Sanskrit under Madhusūdana Vācaspati, and after finishing his studies in Benares he went to Vṛndāvana and took shelter of his uncles, Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana. This is described in the Bhakti-ratnākara. After the disappearance of Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī and Sanātana Gosvāmī in Vṛndāvana, Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī became the ācārya of all the Vaiṣṇavas in Bengal, Orissa and the rest of the world, and it is he who used to guide them in their devotional service. In Vṛndāvana he established the Rādhā-Dāmodara temple. When Jīva Gosvāmī was still present, Śrīla Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja Gosvāmī compiled his famous Caitanyacaritāmṛta. Later, Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī inspired Śrīnivāsa ācārya, Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura and Duḥkhī Kṛṣṇadāsa to preach Kṛṣṇa consciousness in Bengal. Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī awarded the designation Kavirāja to Rāmacandra Sena, a disciple of Śrīnivāsa ācārya’s, and to Rāmacandra’s younger brother Govinda. His disciple Kṛṣṇadāsa Adhikārī listed all the books of the Gosvāmīs in his diary.

In the year 1476 Śakābda (A.D. 1554) Sanātana Gosvāmī completed the Bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. In the year 1504 Śakābda (A.D. 1582) Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī published an edited version of the Bṛhad-vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī named Laghu-toṣaṇī.
