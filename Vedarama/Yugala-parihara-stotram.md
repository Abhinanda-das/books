# Yugala-parihara-stotram

## Preface

An Appeal to the Divine Couple for the Most Extraordinary Largess

\[This prayer is attributed to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in some old manuscripts. I make no claims for who authored it.\]

### Text 1

*he saundarya-nidāna rūpa-gariman mādhurya-līlā-naṭa*  
*he āścarya-viśeṣa-veśa-dhara he vaṁśī-vibhūṣā-vibho*  
*he vṛndātavi-bhū-vilāsini lasat-kelī-kalā-kaumudi*  
*he rādhe caraṇe vidhehi śaraṇaṁ he kṛṣṇa tṛṣṇāṁ hara*

**Пословный перевод:**

*he—*O; *saundarya*—beauty; *nidāna*—the reservoir; *rūpa*—lovely form; *gariman*—great; *mādhurya-līlā*—sweet pastimes; *naṭa*—dramatic dancer; *he—*O; *ascarya*—amazing; *visesa*—particular; *vesa*—clothing; *dhara*—wearer; *he—*O; *vamsi*—flute; *vibhusa*—decoration; vibho—all-pervading Lord; *he—*O; *vṛndāṭavī*—the forest of Vṛndā-devī; *bhū*—the earth; *vilāsinaḥ*—You who sport; *lasat*—charming; *keli*—amorous pastimes; kala—fine arts; *kaumudi*—moonbeams; *he—*O; *rādhe—*O Rādhā; *caraṇe*—at Your lotus feet; *vidhehi*—please give; *saranam*—shelter; *he—*O; *krsna*—Kṛṣṇa; *trsnam*—my longing (for that shelter); *hara*—please remove.

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, You are a reservoir of beauty. Indeed, Your form is beauty personified, and You dance most gracefully during Your conjugal pastimes. O Kṛṣṇa, Your gorgeous garments are decorated with amazing detailed refinements. O Kṛṣṇa, Your flute music pervades the whole universe. O Rādhā, You sport about on the pathways of Vṛndāvana Forest, and You spread the soothing moonbeams of Your charming amorous pastimes imbued with fine arts.**

**O Rādhā, please give me shelter at Your lotus feet!**

**O Kṛṣṇa, please relieve my yearning!**

### Text 2

*he he kṛṣṇa vrajendra-nandana vibho he rādhike śrīmati*  
*he śrīmal-lalitādi-sakhya-sukhite he śyāmalā-premade*  
*he līlā-kalanātta-lālasa-lasad-bhaṅgī-traya-preyasi*  
*he rādhe caraṇe vidhehi śaraṇaṁ he kṛṣṇa tṛṣṇāṁ hara*

**Пословный перевод:**

*he—*O; *he—*O; *krsna*—all-attractive one; *vraja-indra*—the Kind of Vraja; *nandana*—delightful son; *vibho*—all-powerful Lord; *he—*O; *rādhike*—best worshiper; *śrīmatī*—opulent one; *he—*O; *srimal*—beautiful; *lalita*—Lalitā Sakhī; *ādi*—and others; *sakhya*—friendship; *sukhite*—who are made happy; *he—*O; *śyāmalā-prema*—love for Śyāma Sakhī; *de*—giver; *he—*O; *līlā*—pastimes; *kalana*—to behold; *ātta*—assumed; *lālasa*—hankering; *lasad*—lovely; *bhaṅgī-traya*—bending in three places; *preyasi*—beloved girlfriend.

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, You are the all-powerful Lord, the son of Vraja’s king. O Rādhā, O splendorously opulent goddess, You are the best worshiper of Kṛṣṇa. O Rādhā, the friendship of beautiful Lalitā and the other** ***sahkīs*** **delights You. O Rādhā, You love Your dear friend Śyamalā Sakhī. O Rādhā, You are the dear beloved of Kṛṣṇa, who assumes an alluring threefold-bending pose while eager to behold Your playful pastimes.**

**O Rādhā, please give me shelter at Your lotus feet!**

**O Kṛṣṇa, please relieve my yearning!**

### Text 3

*he pītāmbara-śobhanābja-kara he he nīla-citrāmbare*  
*he vaṁśī-vaṭa-keli-kautuka-paṭo he kuñja-geheśvari*  
*he śrī-rāsa-vilāsa-lampaṭa-guro he sundarī prītide*  
*he rādhe caraṇe vidhehi śaraṇaṁ he kṛṣṇa tṛṣṇāṁ hara*

**Пословный перевод:**

*he—*O; *pīta-ambara*—(dressed) in yellow cloth; *śobhana*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are splendorous; *abja-kara*—You (Kṛṣṇa) with lotus hands; *he—*O; *he—*O; *nila*—blue; *citra*—variegated; *ambare*—You (Rādhā) whose cloth; *he—*O; *vamsi-vata*—near the banyan tree named Vamsi-vata; *keli*—at pastimes; *kautuka*—at pranks; *pato*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are expert; he—O; *kuñja-geha*—of the bower house; *isvari*—O Queen; *he—*O; *sri-rasa-vilasa*—in the festive *rasa* dance; lumpati-guro—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are the master of debauchery; *he—*O; *sundarī*—You (Rādhā) who are beautiful; *prīti-de*—You (Rādhā) who bestow loving affection.

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, You look brilliant in Your yellow garments, and You have soft lotuslike hands. O Rādhā, You wear beautiful variegated blue garments. O Kṛṣṇa, You are expert at performing pranks during Your loving pastimes at Vaṁśī-vaṭa. O Rādhā, You are the Queen of the bower house. O Kṛṣṇa, You are the master of debauchery during the festive** ***rāsa*** **dance. O Rādhā, You are exquisitely beautiful, and You bestow loving affection upon Your devotees.**

**O Rādhā, please give me shelter at Your lotus feet!**

**O Kṛṣṇa, please relieve my yearning!**

### Text 4

*he jāmbūnada-nindi-sundara-tano he he ghana-śyāmala*  
*he he paṅkaja-patra-netra-yugale he khañjanī-locane*  
*he cūḍā-maṇi-baddha-cāmara-kace he hāriṇi svāmini*  
*he rādhe caraṇe vidhehi śaraṇaṁ he kṛṣṇa tṛṣṇāṁ hara*

**Пословный перевод:**

*he—*O; *jāmbūnada*—gold; *nindi*—rebukes; *sundara-tano*—You (Rādhā) whose beautiful body; *he—*O; *he—*O; *ghana-syamala*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are dark like a raincloud; *he—*O; *he—*O; *pankaja*—of the lotus; *patra*—petals; *netra-yugale*—You (Rādhā) whose pair of eyes resemble; *he—*O; *khanjani*—like the female wagtail bird; *locane*—You (Rādhā) whose eyes dart here and there; *he—*O; *cuda-mani*—to Your crest jewel; baddha—tied; *cāmara-kace*—You (Rādhā) whose hair is like a cāmara whisk; *he—*O; *harini*—You (Rādhā) who are like a doe; *svamini*—You (Rādhā) who are my controller.

**Перевод:**

**O Rādhā, Your beautiful bodily effulgence rebukes that of pure gold. O Kṛṣṇa, Your complexion is dark like a raincloud. O Rādhā, Your eyes spread like lotus petals and move restlessly like female wagtails. O Rādhā, Your** ***cāmara*****-like hair cascades from its crest-jewel binding. O Rādhā, You are timid like a doe, and You are my supremely benevolent ruling mistress.**

**O Rādhā, please give me shelter at Your lotus feet!**

**O Kṛṣṇa, please relieve my yearning!**

### Text 5

*he he śārada-pūrṇa-cāndra-vadane he he suramyānana*  
*śrīvatsāṅkita cāru-citra-hṛdaye he citra-lekhāñcite*  
*he bimbādhara cāru-citra-cibuke bhru-bhaṅga-ramyālike*  
*he rādhe caraṇe vidhehi śaraṇaṁ he kṛṣṇa tṛṣṇāṁ hara*

**Пословный перевод:**

*he—*O; *he—*O; *sarada*—of the autumn season; *purna-candra*—the full moon; *vadane*—O You (Rādhā) whose face is like; *he—*O; *he—*O; *su-ramya*—is very pleasing; *ānana*—O You (Kṛṣṇa) whose face; *śrīvatsa*—with the Śrīvatsa mark; *aṅkita*—You (Kṛṣṇa) whose chest is marked; *cāru*—lovely; *citra*—wonderfully; *hṛdaye*—O You (Rādhā) whose heart is; *he—*O; *citra-lekhā*—by Citra-lekhā-sakhī; *añcite*—You (Rādhā) who are honored; *he—*O; *bimba-adhara*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are beautified with cherry-red lips; *cāru*—lovely; *citra*—wonderfully; *cibuke*—You (Rādhā) whose chin is; *bhrū-bhaṅga*—knitted eyebrows; *ramya*—with charmingly; *alike*—You (Rādhā) whose forehead is graced;

**Перевод:**

**O Rādhā, Your face resembles the autumnal full moon. O Kṛṣṇa, Your face is very beautiful, and Your chest is graced with the Śrīvatsa mark. O Rādhā, your heart is wonderfully soft. O Rādhā, You are honored by Your girlfriend Citra-lekhā. O Kṛṣṇa, Your face is beautified with cherry-red lips. O Rādhā, Your chin appears superbly comely, and Your forehead is enhanced by your knit eyebrows (due to Your loving anger).**

**O Rādhā, please give me shelter at Your lotus feet!**

**O Kṛṣṇa, please relieve my yearning!**

### Text 6

*he he bhānu-sutā-yaśomati-sutau rāmānuja śyāmala*  
*he nātha vraja-candra gokula-pate he nāgarī-nāgara*  
*he sarvasva vilāsinī-rati-pare he keśavāmodini*  
*he rādhe caraṇe vidhehi śaraṇaṁ he kṛṣṇa tṛṣṇāṁ hara*

**Пословный перевод:**

*he—*O; *he—*O; *bhānu-sutā*—the daughter of Vṛṣabhānu; *yaśomati-sutau*—the son of Yaśodā; *rāma-anuja*—younger brother of Balarāma; *śyāmala*—dark boy; *he—*O; *nātha*—(Kṛṣṇa, my) Lord; *vraja-candra*—(Kṛṣṇa) the moon over Vraja; *gokula-pate*—(Kṛṣṇa) the master of Gokula; *he—*O; *nāgarī-nāgara*—hero of the supreme heroine (Rādhā); *he—*O; *sarvasva*—(Kṛṣṇa) all-in-all; *vilāsini*—with the playful girls (*gopīs*); *rati-pare*—You (Rādhā) who are fond of loving pastimes; *he—*O; *keśava-āmodini*—You (Rādhā) who eternally give pleasure to Lord Keśava;

**Перевод:**

**O daughter of Vṛṣabhānu and son of Yaśodā! O Kṛṣṇa, O handsome dark boy, younger brother of Balarāma, You are the Lord of my life. O Kṛṣṇa, leader of Gokula, paramour of all clever maidens, O full moon risen over Vraja, You are my all-in-all. O Rādhā, You enjoy many loving pastimes in the association of Your playful girlfriends, and You always give ecstatic pleasure to Lord Keśava.**

**O Rādhā, please give me shelter at Your lotus feet!**

**O Kṛṣṇa, please relieve my yearning!**

### Text 7

*he gāndharve naṭa-vara-vapur manmathānanda-sindho*  
*he vaidagdhyādhika madhurimādhāra he prāṇa-nātha*  
*he rāmā-parame parātpara-parīrambhe sadollāsini*  
*he rādhe caraṇe vidhehi śaraṇaṁ he kṛṣṇa tṛṣṇāṁ hara*

**Пословный перевод:**

*he—*O; *gāndharve*—O You (Rādhā) who are endowed with divine musical talents; *nata-vara*—the best of dancers; *vapur*— O You (Kṛṣṇa) whose body is that of; *manmatha*—of Cupid; *ananda-sindhuh*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are the ocean of the bliss; *he—*O; *vaidagdhya*—with cleverness; *adhika*—You (Kṛṣṇa) are excessively endowed; *madhurimā*—of sweetness; *adhara*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are a repository; *he—*O; *prāṇa-nātha*—O You (Kṛṣṇa) who are the Lord of my life breath; *he—*O; *rāmā*—among all attractive girls; *parame*—You (Rādhā) who are supreme; *paratpara*—by Him who is greater than the greatest; *parirambhe—*You (Rādhā) who are embraced; *sadā*—always; *ullāsinī*—You (Rādhā) who are jubilant.

**Перевод:**

**O Rādhā, You are a celestial music-goddess. O Kṛṣṇa, You have the form of the best of dancers, You are an ocean of passionate conjugal bliss, and You possess exceptional cleverness. O Kṛṣṇa, You are the repository of divine sweetness and the Lord of my life breath. O Rādhā, You are supreme among all attractive young girls, and You are embraced by Him who is greater than the greatest. O Rādhā, You are always blossoming in pure jubilation.**

**O Rādhā, please give me shelter at Your lotus feet!**

**O Kṛṣṇa, please relieve my yearning!**

### Text 8

*kāruṇyāmṛta-candra sundara-vapur lāvaṇya-līlā-naṭa*  
*he gopī-gaṇa-nātha gotra-dhara he govinda gopāla he*  
*he gaurī-guru-gauravākhila-guro gopāṅganāvestite*  
*he rādhe caraṇe vidhehi śaraṇaṁ he kṛṣṇa tṛṣṇāṁ hara*

**Пословный перевод:**

*kāruṇya*—of compassion; *amṛta*—of the nectar; *candra*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are the moon; *sundara-vapuḥ*—You (Kṛṣṇa) whose body is handsome; *lāvaṇya*—and imbued with gracefulness; *līlā-naṭa*—You (Kṛṣṇa) whose dancing is frolicsome; *he—*O; *gopī-gaṇa*—of the assembly of milkmaids; *nātha*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are the Lord; *gotra-dhara*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who hold up Govardhana Hill; *he—*O; *govinda*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who please the cows and senses; *gopāla*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who tend the cows; *he—*O; *he—*O; *gaurī*—of the fair-complexioned girls (*gopīs*); guru—as the guru; *gaurava*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are honored; *akhila*—of all beings; guro—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are the spiritual master; *gopa-aṅganā*—by the cowherd girls; *avestite*—You (Rādhā) who are surrounded.

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, You are the full moon of nectarean compassion, You are stunningly handsome, and You playfully dance with supreme grace. O Kṛṣṇa, Lord of the cowherd girls, lifter of Govardhana, pleaser of the senses and cows, transcendental cowherd, You are honored as the guru of all the fair** ***gopīs*** **and, indeed, you are the guru of everyone. O Rādhā, You are always surrounded by Your innumerable cowherd girlfriends.**

**O Rādhā, please give me shelter at Your lotus feet!**

**O Kṛṣṇa, please relieve my yearning!**

### Text 9

*he he kṛpāla-carita vraja-kalpa-vṛkṣa*  
*kāruṇya-leśa-kṛta-kātara-loka-rakṣa*  
*he kṛṣṇa he ramaṇa he bhuvanaika-nātha*  
*hā hā kadāti-karuṇā bhavator bhaven me*

**Пословный перевод:**

*he—*O; *he—*O; *krpala*—is merciful; *carita*—You (Kṛṣṇa) whose character; *vraja*—of Vraja-maṇḍala; *kalpa-vṛkṣa*—You (Kṛṣṇa) who are the desire tree; *karunya-lesa*—a drop of mercy; *kṛta*—shown; *kātara-loka*—of miserable people; *rakṣa*—O You (Kṛṣṇa), who are the protector; *he—*O; *krsna*—all-attractive Lord; *he—*O; *ramaṇa*—lover; *he—*O; *bhuvana*—of the world; *eka-nātha*—the only Lord; *hā*—alas; *hā*—alas; *kadā*—when; *ati-karuṇā*—extremely merciful; *bhavatoḥ*—of You two (Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa); *bhavet*—will be; *me*—unto me.

**Перевод:**

**O Kṛṣṇa, You are by nature merciful, and You are the bountiful desire tree of Vraja. O Kṛṣṇa, You protect the miserable fallen souls with just a drop of Your mercy. O all-attractive Lord, O dearmost lover, O exclusive Lord of the world, alas, alas, when will You and Rādhā bestow Your mercy upon me?**
