# Śanaiścara-kṛta-śrī-narasiṁha-stutiḥ

## The Prayer to Śrī Nṛsiṁha by Śrī Śanaiścara

*su-labho bhakti-yuktānāṁ*  
*durdarśo duṣṭa-cetasām*  
*araṇya-gatikānāṁ ca*  
*prabhur bhaktaika-vatsalaḥ*  
*śanaiścaras tatra nṛsiṁha-deva-*  
*stutiṁ cakārāmala-citra-vṛttim*  
*praṇamya sāṣṭaṅgam aśeṣa-loka-*  
*kirīṭa-nīrājita-pāda-padmam*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Śanaiścara is easily obtained by those who have devotion, but difficult to see for those whose hearts are wicked. He is also the master of those who have gone to the forest and is exclusively compassionate to his devotees. At that time he composed a prayer to Lord Nṛsiṁha containing various faultless poetic devices, after first bowing down with all eight limbs to Him, whose lotus feet are honored by the helmets of all the planetary rulers.**

*śrī-śanir uvāca*  
*yat-pāda-paṅkaja-rajaḥ paramādareṇa*  
*saṁsevitaṁ sakala-kalmaṣa-rāśi-nāśam*  
*kalyāṇa-kārakam aśeṣa-nijānugānāṁ*  
*sa tvaṁ nṛsiṁha mayi dehi kṛpāvalokam*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Śani said: When the dust of Your lotus feet is served with great reverence, it destroys all the accumulated heaps of one’s contamination. That dust creates auspiciousness for all Your devotees. Lord Nṛsiṁha, please grant me Your merciful glance.**

*sarvatra cañcalatayā sthitayā \[tu\] lakṣmyā*  
*brahmādi-vandya-padayā sthirayā nyasevi*  
*pādāravinda-yugalaṁ paramādareṇa*  
*sa tvaṁ nṛsiṁha mayi dehi kṛpāvalokam*

**Перевод:**

**The goddess Lakṣmī is fickle toward everyone, and her feet are worshiped by Brahmā and the other demgods. But she has engaged her self with steady devotion and great reverence in the service of Your lotus feet. Lord Nṛsiṁha, please grant me Your merciful glance.**

*yad-rūpam āgama-śiraḥ-pratipādyam ādyam*  
*ādhyātmikādi-paritāpa-haraṁ vicintyam*  
*yogeśvarair apagatākhila-doṣa-saṅghaiḥ*  
*sa tvaṁ nṛsiṁha mayi dehi kṛpāvalokam*

**Перевод:**

**Your bodily form is the original form described in the most confidential texts of the Vedas. It dispels all one’s distress of the mind and so on. It is the object of meditation of the great masters of yoga who have freed themselves from all faults. Lord Nṛsiṁha, please grant me Your merciful glance.**

*prahlāda-bhakta-vacasā harir āvir āsa*  
*stambhe hiraṇyakaśipuṁ ya udāra-bhāvaḥ*  
*ūrvor nidhāya tad-udaro nakharair dadāra*  
*sa tvaṁ nṛsiṁha mayi dehi kṛpāvalokam*

**Перевод:**

**In response to the request of Your devotee Prahlāda, You, the most compassionate, appeared in a pillar. You then placed Hiraṇyakaśipu on Your thighs and split apart his abdomen with Your claws. Lord Nṛsiṁha, please grant me Your merciful glance.**

*yo nija-bhaktam analāmbudhi-bhūdharogra-*  
*śṛṅga-prapāta-viṣa-danta-sarīsṛpebhyaḥ*  
*sarvātmakaḥ parama-kāruṇiko rarakṣa*  
*sa tvaṁ nṛsiṁha mayi dehi kṛpāvalokam*

**Перевод:**

**You are the soul of everything and the most merciful. You protected Your devotee from fire, a vast reservoir of water, falling from the terrible peak of a mountain, poison, the teeth of beasts, and snakes. Lord Nṛsiṁha, please grant me Your merciful glance.**

*yan nirvikāra-para-rūpa-vicintanena*  
*yogeśvarā viṣaya-vīta-samasta-rāgaḥ*  
*viśrāntim āpur avināśvatīṁ parākhyāṁ*  
*sa tvaṁ nṛsiṁha mayi dehi kṛpāvalokam*

**Перевод:**

**By meditating on your unchanging supreme form, the masters of yoga become freed from all attachment to sense gratification and attain the topmost perpetual relief from distress. Lord Nṛsiṁha, please grant me Your merciful glance.**

*yad-rūpam ugra-parimardana-bhāva-śāli*  
*sañcintanena sakalāgha-vināśa-kāri*  
*bhūta-jvara-graha-samudbhava-bhīti-nāśaṁ*  
*sa tvaṁ nṛsiṁha mayi dehi kṛpāvalokam*

**Перевод:**

**In Your transcendental form You like to crush the wicked. When remembered with careful attention, that form destroys all sins. It also destroys all fear caused by ghosts, fevers, and malicious planets. Lord Nṛsiṁha, please grant me Your merciful glance.**

*yasyottamaṁ yaśa umā-pati-padma-janma-*  
*śakrādi-daivata-sabhāsu samasta-gītam*  
*śaktyaiva sarva-śamala-praśamaika-dakṣam*  
*sa tvaṁ nṛsiṁha mayi dehi kṛpāvalokam*

**Перевод:**

**Your unexcelled fame is sung constantly in the assemblies of the husband of Umā, lotus-born Brahmā, Indra, and other demigods. By its potency that fame is capable of eradicating all kinds of contamination. Lord Nṛsiṁha, please grant me Your merciful glance.**

*evaṁ śrutvā stutiṁ devaḥ*  
*śaninā kalpitaṁ hariḥ*  
*uvāca barhma-vanda-sthaṁ*  
*śaniṁ taṁ bhakta-vatsalaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**When Lord Hari, always compassionate to His devotees, heard this prayer composed by Śani, who was situated in \[barhma-vanda?\], the Lord spoke to him.**

*śrī-nṛsiṁha uvāca*  
*prasanno ’haṁ śane tubhyaṁ*  
*varaṁ varaya śobhanam*  
*yaṁ vāñchāsi tam eva tvaṁ*  
*sarva-loka-hitāvaham*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Nṛsiṁha said: I am satisfied with you, Śani. Please choose an excellent boon, whatever you want, which I am sure will be beneficial for the whole world.**

*śrī-śanir uvāca*  
*nṛsiṁha tvaṁ mayi kṛpāṁ kuru deva dayā-nidhe*  
*mad-vāsaras tava prīti-karaḥ syāt devatā-pate*  
*mat-kṛtaṁ tvat-paraṁ stotraṁ śṛṇvanti ca paṭhanti ca*  
*sarvān kāmān pūrayetās teṣāṁ tvaṁ loka-bhāvanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Śani said: O Nṛsiṁha, ocean of mercy, please be merciful to me. O Lord of the demigods, may my day of the week be a source of pleasure to You. May You fulfill all the desires of anyone who hears or recites the prayer I have composed for You. You are the sustainer of the worlds.**

*śrī-nṛsiṁha uvāca*  
*tathaivāstu śane ’haṁ vai*  
*rakṣo-bhuvana-saṁsthitaḥ*  
*bhakta-kāmān pūrayiṣye*  
*tvaṁ mamaika-vacaḥ śṛṇu*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Nrṣiṁha said: So be it, dear Śani. I will remain on the planet of the Rakṣas and fulfill the desires of your devotees. Now hear one more thing I have to say.**

*tvat-kṛtaṁ mat-paraṁ stotraṁ*  
*yaḥ paṭhec chṛṇuyāc ca yaḥ*  
*dvādaśāṣṭama-janma-stha-*  
*tvad-bhayaṁ māstu tasya vai*

**Перевод:**

**Anyone who hears the prayer you made to Me and anyone who recites it will never experience the fear of you that comes twelve and eight years after birth \[?\].**

*śanir narasiṁhaṁ devaṁ*  
*tatheti partyuvāca ha*  
*tataḥ parama-santuṣṭo*  
*jayeti manayo vadan*

**Перевод:**

**Śani replied to Lord Nṛsiṁha, “That is good,” and felt fully satisfied, while the sages uttered the word jaya.**

*śrī-kṛṣṇa uvāca*  
*itthaṁ śanaiścarasyātha nṛsiṁha-deva-*  
*saṁvādam etat stavanaṁ ca mānavaḥ*  
*śṛṇoti yaḥ śrāvayate ca bhaktyā*  
*sarvāny abhīṣṭāni ca vindate dhruvam*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Kṛṣṇa said: Any human who with devotion hears or causes others to hear this prayer by Śanaiścara and this conversation between him and Lord Nṛsiṁha will certainly achieve everything he may desire.**

*iti śrī-bhaviṣyottara-purāṇe rakṣo-bhuvana-māhātmye śrī-śanaiścara-kṛta-śrī-nṛsiṁha-stutim.*

**Перевод:**

**Thus ends the prayer to Śrī Nṛsiṁha by Śrī Śanaiścara in the Rakṣo-bhuvana-māhātmya of Śrī Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa.**

*śrī-śanaiścarāntar-gata-śrī-mukhya-prāṇāntar-gata-śrī-lakṣmī-nṛsiṁhau priyetām. śṛī-kṛṣṇārpaṇam astu.*

**Перевод:**

**May Śrī Lakṣmī-Nṛsiṁha be pleased, who are within the prime vital air, who is within Śrī Śanaiścara.**
