Our ācāryas such as Jīva Gosvāmī, Sanātana Gosvāmī and Viśvanātha Cakravartī have given extensive commentaries on the prayers of the personified Vedas, since those prayers refute the idea that Upaniṣads present the supreme Brahman as impersonal. Prabhodānanda Sarasvatī gives a completely different interpretation of the verses. Since the Upaniṣads became gopīs, he gives the meaning of the verses in terms of mādhurya rasa, as spoken by those Upaniṣad-gopis. He also gives a meaning according to the eternal gopis.

# Śruti Stuti Vākya: Sweetness of the Prayers of Personified Vedas

## Śruti-stuti-vākya - Chapter Eighty-seven of SB

### Text 1

*śrī-parīkṣid uvāca*  
*brahman brahmaṇy anirdeśye nirguṇe guṇa-vṛttayaḥ*  
*kathaṁ caranti śrutayaḥ sākṣāt sad-asataḥ pare*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Parīkṣit said: O** ***brāhmaṇa,*** **how can the Vedas (gopīs) directly act in relation to Kṛṣṇa, who cannot be described in words, who is beyond the guṇas and beyond impersonal brahman and prakṛti?**

**Комментарий:**

Prabhodānanda with great care and joy explains the Prayers of the Personified Vedas for producing prema for Rādhā-kānta.

At the end of the last chapter it was explained that the Lord, devoted to his devotees, taught his devotees Bahulaśva and Śrutadeva about san-mārgam and then returned to Dvarakā. He taught them about his svarūpa, about he who is the shelter of sweetness of most amazing qualities and form, who is absorbed in many conjugal pastimes with many cowherd women such as Rādhā, and who were the very forms perfect, pure madhurya-rasa. It has been shown that the Vedas also indicate this conclusion. The devotees seek this form (mārga—search) also but cannot attain it. However, they can attain that form by searching for it with the highest bhakti, after realizing pure brahman by listening to Vedānta. They cannot attain that form by material intelligence. The word sat indicates those endowed with the highest, deepest prema, which is eternal. (The Lord is sought and attained –margam--by those with the highest bhakti-- sat.)

Sanmārga can also meant the path of the Vedas, the proof for the devotees, which indicates the form of Kṛṣna.

Or sanmārgam can mean that person who was sought by the Vedas through their performing austerities. It is stated in Bṛhad-vāmana- Purāṇa that the śrutis performed austerities to attain Kṛṣṇa. The śrutis, taking the form of gopīs attained association with Krṣṇa or Brahman.

Parīkṣit now asks a question about this. How can the śrutis deal with pure Brahman which cannot be described either directly or indirectly (anirdeśya). Brahman is further described. It is superior to impersonal Brahman and prakṛti. (sad asat pare). How do the śrutis, taking the form of gopīs perform activities (caranti) such as seeing and touching that Brahman beyond description? These priya sakhīs (śrutayaḥ) must hear the joking conversations of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in a new bower. But they must perceive him through material bodies and senses (guṇa-vṛttayaḥ). On the other hand Kṛṣna is saccidānanda. They cannot have a material contact with him. But there cannot be a relationship with an illusory Kṛṣna, since it is described that they met him directly (sakṣat).

### Text 2

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*buddhīndriya-manaḥ-prāṇān janānām asṛjat prabhuḥ*  
*mātrārthaṁ ca bhavārthaṁ ca ātmane ’kalpanāya ca*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvamī said: The Supreme Lord manifested the intelligence, senses, mind and vital air of the living entities so that they could indulge their desires for sense gratification, take repeated births to engage in fruitive activities, so that those desiring liberation can attain brahman and so that the devotees can perceive the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Ṣukadeva gives the conclusion. The Lord created the intelligence, senses and mind of karmīs, jñānīs and bhaktas so that materialists can take birth (bhavārtham), so that they can enjoy material objects (mātrārtham), and so that some, using sattvika senses and mind (after destruction of tamas and rajas) , can attain liberation in Brahman. Devotees using sacicdāanda senses can see and touch the form of the Lord (ātmane).

### Text 3

*saiṣā hy upaniṣad brāhmī pūrveśāṁ pūrva-jair dhṛtā*  
*śrraddhayā dhārayed yas tāṁ kṣemaṁ gacched akiñcanaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Those who came before even our ancient predecessors meditated upon this same confidential knowledge of the Absolute Truth. Indeed, anyone who faithfully concentrates on this knowledge will become free from material attachments and attain the final goal of life.**

**Комментарий:**

This secret knowledge (Upaniṣad) concerning brahman (brāhmī) was the object of meditation for previous sages.

**Перевод:**

### Text 10

*śvetadvīpaṁ gatavati tvayi draṣṭuṁ tad-īśvaram*  
*brahma-vādaḥ su-saṁvṛttaḥ śrutayo yatra śerate*

**Перевод:**

**At that time you happened to be visiting the Lord on Śvetadvīpa. A lively discussion fully endowed with the Vedas arose among the sages on Janaloka as to the nature of the Supreme Absolute Truth. The same question arose then that you are asking me now.**

### Text 11

*tulya-śruta-tapaḥ-śīlās tulya-svīyāri-madhyamāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Although these sages were all equally qualified in terms of concentration on the Vedas, and although they all saw friends, enemies and neutral parties equally, they chose one of their number to be the speaker, and the rest became eager listeners.**

### Text 12

*śrī-sanandana uvāca*  
*sva-sṛṣṭam idam āpīya śayānaṁ saha śaktibhiḥ*  
*tad-ante bodhayāṁ cakrus tal-liṅgaiḥ śrutayaḥ param*  
*yathā śayānaṁ saṁrājaṁ vandinas tat-parākramaiḥ*  
*pratyūṣe ’bhetya su-ślokair bodhayanty anujīvinaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śrī Sanandana replied:**

**Kṛsṇa withdrew from his pastimes of love and fell asleep with Rādhā and the gopīs. In the morning they awakened with words indicating the time, though he was unwilling to move.**

**Комментарий:**

Bhagavān is the supreme form of condensed rasa, of saccidānanda, an ocean ruling over madhurya-rasa. His gopīs have similar forms of spiritual rasa. That blissful rasa makes all other bliss insignificant. The full form of pure rati is Rādhā experiences that blissful rasa in a friendly mood. This has been explained.

What he created (sva-sṛṣtam) is astonishing and skillful, but it is not what is famous in works concerned with material lust. This love is eternally perfect and becomes perceptible by me now (idam--this). The word idam also means pure love full of rasa (i—love, da –pure). The lord, withdrawing this pure love (idam āpīya) the Lord went to sleep with his śaktis. This indicates that in a subtle form, the Lord continued pastimes with the gopīs even during sleep. His śaktis (plural) also indicates Rādhā. She is the form of all śaktis in all types of prema. Or since all other rasa śaktis are her aṁśas, they are all included in madhurya-rasa. Thus the plural (śaktibhiḥ) is used. Or the plural is used to indicate Rādhā and her sakhīs. At that time Rādhā slept on his chest. Some sakhīs stayed there to witness their pastimes and fell asleep at the side.

Or the Lord withdrew from the sweet rasa of Rādhā’s lips (sva-sṛṭam), which were not for others to taste, but only for himself (sva). The very form of Cupid, the lover of Vṛndāvana, then slept with his pastime śaktis. They all fell asleep from fatigue after pastimes.

After that (tad-ante), in the morning, or when the śaktis woke up, they woke up Kṛṣṇa because of signs that it was dawn or, they woke him up with words indicated that it was now morning (liṅgaiḥ). He was motionless, not pleased (param) or he was the form of the highest bliss (param).

### Text 13

*yathā śayānaṁ saṁrājaṁ vandinas tat-parākramaiḥ*  
*pratyūṣe ’bhetya su-ślokair bodhayanty anujīvinaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**As ordinary persons are awakened after exhaustion from love, in the morning so the birds, using verses of praise, awakened Kṛṣṇa, splendid in association with Rādhā, after successively awakening the various groups of sakhī.**

**Комментарий:**

Not only the sakhīs woke up the couple. The parrots and sārikās, living the trees, also woke them up by skilful recitation of verses concerning their pastimes.

Just lovers sleep after exhaustion and then are woken up (yathā), the birds (anujīvinaḥ), using verses of praise, woke up Kṛṣṇa who shone in combination with Rādhā (saṁrājam), after waking up sequentially, from chief sakhīs to minor sakhīs, the various sakhīs who were surrendered (parā) to the couple (kramaiḥ).

Or the two verses together indicate the gopīs who were previously śrutis and the eternal gopīs. There are five types of gopīs.

Gopīs with eternally manifested svarūpas.

Sages of Daṇḍakāraṇya forest who attained conjugal love for Rāma and then took gopī forms.

The śrutis attained gopī forms by performing austerities.

Wives of devatās like Candrakānti appeared in Vraja in gopī forms by the mercy of Rādhā who possessed the highest rati.

### Text 14

*śrī-śrutaya ūcuḥ*  
*jaya jaya jahy ajām ajita doṣa-gṛbhīta-guṇāṁ*  
*tvam asi yad ātmanā samavaruddha-samasta-bhagaḥ*  
*aga-jagad-okasām akhila-śakty-avabodhaka te*  
*kvacid ajayātmanā ca carato ’nucaren nigamaḥ*

**Перевод:**

**The** ***śrutis*** **said: Victory, victory to You, O unconquerable one! Destroy the ignorance, whose qualities are filled with faults of covering your cowherd nature since you possess the highest bliss by your very nature. You awaken all śaktis in the jivas who are liberated and conditioned. Somethines the Vedas reavel you as the person who exists with perfect bliss form, and as a cowherd seeming to belong to the material world.**

**Комментарий:**

Though the śrutis are absorbed in bhāva for Kṛṣna, they have remaining impressions of tattva-jñāna. They do not experience Kṛsṇa in his svarūpa with pure prema-rasa. They associate with Kṛṣna in his mixed form of prema rasa. But they aspire for the pure rasa. Other śrutis however experience Kṛṣṇa in his form of pure rasa. Both types of śrutis awakened the Lord. It should be understood that even Rādhā takes these two forms.

These śrutis awaken the Lord with this verse. O unconquered (ajita)! Though we, the great sages, Uddhava and Arjuna and Brahmā and Śiva take shelter of you, we cannot control you. Since your nature is pure prema, you are controlled completely by person having such prema. The word jaya is repeated with great respect to indicate “Show your pure form of rasa which is superior to all other forms of the Lord.” This form is supreme, in terms of śakti, qualities, bliss and astonishment.

Destroy (jahi) ignorance (ajām) which covers the form of the gopāla endowed with pure rasa. But if the covering of brahman is destroyed and the covering of the powerful Lord is destroyed, then the manifestation of pastimes of Gopāla who contains the highest bliss will also disappear. One is left with the quality of ignorance.

Destroy the ignorance whose qualities give rise to faults of being fixed in brahman or the powerful Lord, since (yat) by your svarūpa (ātmanā) alone, by proximity, not by thinking “I have this power” you have complete, astonishing happiness (samavaruddha-samasta-bhāgaḥ). You award that happiness to the purest devotees by manifesting your form to them, devoid of awareness of brahman or the greatness of the Lord.

Or O Lord controlled (ruddha) by pure affection (sam-ava)! By your pure form filled with rasa (ātmanā), you completely (sam) discard (asta) your portion manifesting powers (bhāgaḥ). By having your devotee see and touch your form, you completely discard the bliss of brahman or a majestic lord.

The form full of powers is described for those who cannot see the form of Gopāla, the very soul of Brahman.

(okasām) of those who are without action, (aga), being liberated in brahman, and for the places of those For the places jīvas who are wandering in saṁsāra (jagat), you are the awakener of all śaktis (akhila-śakty-avabodhaka ), since you are the antaryāmī of all the forms of the Lord.

The full śakti called the highest bliss is situated in you, the pure form of Gopāla. All other śaktis depend on this śakti.

*ko hy evānyāt kaḥ prāṇyāt yad eṣa ākāśa ānando na syāt*

**Who could breathe without this Brahman, if this blissful Brahman were not present? Taittirīya Upaniṣad 2.7.1**

Or, you possess the śakti of happiness or bliss (ka) which cuases awakening of all other śaktis (akhila-saktyavabodha).

What is the proof of this pure form of Gopāla, which is superior to all other forms of bhagavān since he has a form of eternal completel happiness, and which is superior to brahman? The vedas (nigamaḥ) reveal (anucaret) you (te), who exist (carataḥ) as an ocean of the highest bliss (ātmānā), with śakti and qualities, and who exists with a form similar to a material cowherd produced by the Lord’s inferior energy (ajayā).

### Text 15

*bṛhad upalabdham etad avayanty avaśeṣatayā*  
*yata udayāstamayau vikṛter mṛdi vāvikṛtāt*  
*ata ṛṣayo dadhus tvayi mano-vacanācaritaṁ*  
*katham ayathā bhavanti bhuvi datta-padāni nṛṇām*

**Перевод:**

**Your form is experienced as the supreme form of Brahman. Some can understand that you are what remains when everything else is rejected. Because of that pur love, the devotees continually perform actions and then more actions to serve you. In comparison to that pure love, all else is like mud. Thus the sages serve you with their words and minds. As men must place their feet on the ground, so that pure love is certainly the highest.**

**Комментарий:**

O son of Nanda! Your form which is experienced (idam) is the supreme brahman (bṛhat) since it is experienced as the form of bliss by the gopīs. Some consider that what remains is Brahman—everything other than brahman, is dull matter, to be rejected by negation. The remainder which reveals all else is understood to be that form of bliss. Considering all else to be māyā and insignificant, the pure brahman cannot be an object made of māyā because of the mercy given from the highest devotion to the Lord to the person fixed in Brahman (the Lord).

Or the gopīs endowed with pure bhāva know your form, the pure brahman realized by us śrutis to be that which is ultimately (śeṣatayā) prema (ava). They know you as the young cowherd endowed with condensed bliss, and reject knowledge of brahman or bhagavān.

“Will I give he gopīs realization of myself as the sonof Nanda, or as Nārāyaṇa?” they know the great brahman which is superior to the forms of brahman or bhagavān (bṛhat), which is realized by others (upalabdham). Or they know the brahman which is experienced by me. Avaśa means he who is not controlled by anyone. Iśa means he desires (avaśeṣatayā). He always desires to give himself as the object of prema, in the form of the pure cowherd.

Brahman does not have such qualities. This form has more bliss and astonishing qualities than brahman. Those fixed in their pure svarūpa are absorbed in pure bhāva. It is impossible even for other forms of the Lord to instill such steadiness in this goal. The Lord does not produce unsteadiness or obstacles, since he is very dear to that person.

Though not being under other’s control, the Lord desires this. As the young cowherd, not as Brahman, he is controlled by great prema (avaśeṣatayā). This is not possible for persons cultivating jñāna.

Or avaśeṣatayā can mean “by ultimately having affection.”

The highest puruṣārtha is liberation, being situated in the svarūpa of brahman. Since this is rejected because it lacks excellence of bliss and astonishing nature, pure bhakti for the Lord which possesses bliss and astonishment is accepted as the puruṣārtha. If the bhakti is mixed with knowledge of brhaman and bhagavān, the possessiveness of the Lord becomes restricted because of lack of strong rati. When the Lord is the object of reverence, there cannot be unlimited wealth of the highest bliss. Rejecting this, only pure rati for the Lord full of affection, Gopāla is accepted. Only he remains. This pure devotion and nothing else remains, by sharp discernment and, by the Lord’s mercy.

Because of that pure rati (yataḥ), there are creation and destruction (udayāstamayau) of various efforts (vikṛteḥ) of some persons who have great fortune, who are the topmost of those having desire for the Lord. Giving up one effort they make another effort. Giving up that effort they make another effort. Out of great longing, they engage in a variety of efforts. Or they perform special activities continuously.

Or, because of pure rati, for the sādhaka who is skilful at excellent actions, there is great good fortune . When that rati appears in an immature state, there is great good fortune (aya). This means there is happiness everywhere.

Or when rati does not completely appear, then it seems to be destroyed. In that case, the various actions which appeared disappear. Or for some who have something related to pure bhāva, the actions manifest. For those engaged in worldly or Vedic actions, which are contrary to the pure bhāva, the actions do not manifest.

Because of engaging in pure rasa without distortion of the pure bhāva, mixing other goals related to karma and jñāna is like covering it with mud (mṛd iva). Everything else is insignificant like mud.

Since you are the pure form of the cowherd, and the object of pure bhāva, the sages, knowing you as the attractive cowherd, supreme among all forms of bhagavān, filled with the most astonishing bliss which derides brahman and even bhagavān, praise you as the son of Nanda only, by their minds and words. Forgetting about brahman and bhagavān, they will not manifest tattva-jñāba in their mind and senses, because of the pure bhāva for the pure form of Gopāla. How can one not place footsteps on the earth?

Without thinking of bhagavān or brahman in pure Gopāla, there must only be pure love for the highest form of the highest bliss without coverings.

According to the eternal gopīs waking up the Lord:

This pastime was realized (upalabdhma) by us, who have ever increasing, pure friendly love for Rādhā. This pastime in the new bower of Vṛndāvana between you and Rādhā is the great ocean of rasa (bṛhat), since other pastimes are distortions (vikṛteḥ), to be regarded as left overs (avaśesatayā). Such pastimes for the immature appear and disappear. Persons like us know that they appear for some and disappear for others.

Even if there is a little distortion (āvikrtāt), it is like death for us (mṛt iva). Persons like us with pure devotion, realizing the rasa of pastimes, engage their mind and words in you.

Our experience of rasa as you play with Rādhā in the bower of Vṛndāvana, shows the greatness of Vṛndāvana. What to speak of us, any living entity on earth can develop breat pure love for you. If one places his feet in Vṛndānava (bhuvi) what else can happen? There will be pure mahābhāva.

### Text 16

*iti tava sūrayas try-adhipate ’khila-loka-mala-*  
*kṣapaṇa-kathāmṛtābdhim avagāhya tapāṁsi jahuḥ*  
*kim uta punaḥ sva-dhāma-vidhutāśaya-kāla-guṇāḥ*  
*parama bhajanti ye padam ajasra-sukhānubhavam*

**Перевод:**

**O master of the three worlds! Because you are the form of madhurya-rasa, men of knowledge bathe your sweet topics which wahs away all contaminations of the universe, and give up practicing austerities. What to speak of those who worship your feet which give an experience of continual bliss, whose qualities qualities bring Kṛsṇa under control and increase the ocean of great rasa like the moon agitating the ocean, in the forest of Vṛndāvana belonging to Rādhā.**

**Комментарий:**

The śrutīs (born as gopīs) speak.

Because (iti) you alone are the highest person, the personification of pure madhurya-rasa, and shower a mass of the highest bliss that defeats all else, those who are fixed in knowledge (surayaḥ), (not those who are fixed in worshiping you through pure mahābhāva), bathe in the sweet ocean of topics about you, topics of pastimes with Rādhā in the bower, which are constantly recited with hair standing on end, trembling and other symptoms as well as astonishment because of matured bhāva, and give up austerities because most astonishing sweet pastimes manifest with continuous waves from the sweet ocean.

Or the give up external sādhana becase of the manifestation of prema by hearing those topics. What more can be said of those who worship your lotus feet, those who are absorbed in the mood of friendship with Rādhā, with a continual experience of happiness. Elsewhere, experience of happiness is occasional and subject to destruction.

They have qualities which bring Kṛsṇa under control and which increase the ocean of great rasa like the moon agitating the ocean, in the forest of Vṛndāvana belonging to Rādhā. How can one describe the wealth of happiness of these gopīs having skills which nourish their clevernesss in love affairs? O Lord situated at th highest level of pure prema (parama)!

Statement of the eternal gopīs:

Persons who know thoroughly the pastimes filled with great rasa (surayaḥ) absorbing themsels in the ocean of sweet topics about Kṛsṇa, give up the pain involved in the pastimes. What to speak of those persons who are free of material desire and material time in Vṛndāvana, who worship his feet which give continual bliss. They do not remember the bondage of the house and unaware of day and night, since they are absorbed in the ocean of rasa of most confidential service to the intense love of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

### Text 17

*dṛtaya iva śvasanty asu-bhṛto yadi te ’nuvidhā*  
*mahad-ahamādayo ’ṇḍam asṛjan yad-anugrahataḥ*  
*puruṣa-vidho ’nvayo ’tra ca ramonnam ayādiṣu yaḥ*  
*sad-asataḥ paraṁ tvam atha yad eṣv avaśeṣam ṛtam*

**Перевод:**

**Those who follow Rādhā actually live. Otherwise their lives are like bellows. You do not give mercy to Brahmā, Śiva and Lakṣmī since they think of you as a powerful creator. One must not think of Kṛṣṇa as a killer of demons. You thnk of your self as an ordinary human being (puruṣa-vidhaḥ) and act in that way. You hide your nature as eternal Brahman.You are rasa itself. You are the best among all good things. . Please protecet the highest truth (śeṣamṛetam) among these human goals other inferior goals.**

**Комментарий:**

Those absorbed in pure rasa of Kṛṣṇa criticize all the nondevotees.

*Anyad eva tad viditāt*

**The Lord is other than what is known.** ***Kena Upaniṣad***

Refuting material powers.

*asthulam anaṇu*

**The Lord is not big and not small.** ***Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad***

*Na tasya kāryam*

**The Lord has no material body.** ***Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad***

Refuting impersonal sādhana

*anyatra dharmād anyan ādharmāt*

**Tell me what is other than dharma and adharma, other than the means and end.** ***Kaṭha Upaniṣad***

Refuting crooked behavior

*naiṣā tarkeṇa matir āpaneyā*

**One cannot obtain truth by argumentation.** ***Kaṭha Upaniṣad***

*Nānudhyāyed bahūn śabdān vāco viglāpanaṁ hi ta*

**Do not contemplate many words; it gives merely fatigue from speaking.** ***Bṛhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad*** **4.7.21**

### Text 42

*śrī-bhagavān uvāca*  
*ity etad brahmaṇaḥ putrā āśrutyātmānuśāsanam*  
*sanandanam athānarcuḥ siddhā jñātvātmano gatim*

**Перевод:**

**The Supreme Lord, Nārāyaṇa Ṛṣi, said: Having heard these instructions about the Supreme Lord, the sons of Brahmā now understood their final goal. They felt perfectly satisfied and honored Sanandana with their worship.**

**Комментарий:**

Having attentively heard (*āśrutvā)* what the *śrutis* said (*evam*), teachings for the benefit of the *jīva* (ātmānuśāsanam), teaching about Svayam Bhagavān, the root form of the Lord and related matters, and therefore understanding the goal of the *jīva*, or the method of understanding the Lord (*ātma-gatim),* the sons of Brahmā felt astonishment (*siddhāḥ)* because of the nature of the descriptions of the Lord’s powers, or feld fully satisfied in all desires.

### Text 43

*ity aśeṣa-samāmnāya- purāṇopaniṣad-rasaḥ*  
*samuddhṛtaḥ pūrva-jātair vyoma-yānair mahātmabhiḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Thus the ancient saints who travel in the upper heavens distilled this nectarean and confidential essence of all the Vedas and Purāṇas.**

**Комментарий:**

Since the b*rāhmaṇas* were born in the very beginning they were the best *(pūrva-jātaiḥ*). They wandered about with no attachments (*vyoma-yānaiḥ*). They had generous minds (*mahātmabhiḥ*).

The best, unattached sages with generous minds, took out the essence completely *(sam--uddhṛtāḥ*) from the *śrutis* studied under a guru. This shows their qualification. *Samāmnāya* indicates scripture in general. Purāṇas indicates *itihāsas* also. This indicates karma and *upāsana.* Upaniṣads indicates *jñāna* in works like *Chāndogya* and *Bhagavad-gītā.* Purāṇa is placed in the middle of the compound to indicate the equality of Purāṇas with Vedas. *Yad brāhmaṇān itihāsān purāṇāni:* scripture consists of Brāhmaṇas, *itihāsas* and Purāṇas. (*Svādhyāyopadeśa śruti*).

*evaṁ vā are asya mahato bhūtasya niśvasitam etad yad ṛg, vedo yajur vedaḥ sāma vedo ‘tharvāṅgirasa itihāsaḥ purāṇam.*

**The Ṛg, Yajur, Sāma and Atharva Vedas, the** ***itihāsas*** **and Purāṇas emanated from the breathing of the Lord.** ***Brhad-āraṇyaka Upaniṣad*** **4.4.10**

*Ṛg vedam bhagavo 'dhyemi yajur-vedam sama-vedam atharvanam caturtham itihāsam puranam pancamam vedanam vedam*

**I have learned the Ṛg, Yajur, Sama and Atharva Vedas, as well as** ***itihāsas*** **and Purāṇas which are called the fifth Veda.** ***Chāndogya Upaniṣad***

*itihāsa-purāṇāni pañcamo veda ucyate*

**The** ***itihāsas*** **and Purāṇas are called the fifth Veda. SB 1.4.20**

Everyone is qualified for these works as in the case of the Lord’s name because of the strength the rule: *varṣāsu rathakāro ‘gnīn ādadhīte:* the carpenter should light the sacrificial fires in the monsoon season. The *rasa* of the words refers to conclusion which is filled with *bhakti* to the Lord.  *Pibata bhāgavataṁ rasam:* drink the rasa of Bhāgavatam. (SB 1.1.3) The *rasa* is of three types: in single statements, in connected statements and in whole sections of a work. The first two types have been shown. The last is in six parts:

*Upakramopasamhārāv abhyāso ‘pūrvatā phalam*  
*Arthavādopapattī ca liṅgaṁ tātparya-nirṇaye*

**Initial statement, conclusion, repetition, uniqueness of the object, gain from the object, praise of the object and proof are the topics used to discern the meaning of a passage.**

The topic is declared by the verses under discussion by deducing one meaning of the famous and lesser known *śrutis,* revealing the meaning of *praṇava,* and explaining topics like creation and destruction which are present from the beginning to the end of the Vedas. Thus, the meanings of the first prayer starting with *jaya jaya* (Victory, you, O unconquerable one! By your very nature you are perfectly full in all powers, verse 14) which is *upakrama,* and the last prayer starting with *dyu-patayaḥ* (the *devatās* cannot reach the end of your glories, verse 41) which is the *upasaṁhāra* are the same. *Sa yad ajayā tv ajām* (the jīva contacts matter by māyā, verse 38) and other verses are the *abhyāsa* (repetition). *Anucaren nigamaḥ* (the Vedas confirm this, verse 14) and *śrutayas tvayi hi phalanti* (the Vedas find success in you, verse 41) are the a*pūrvatā* (showing unique qualities). *Na parilasanti kecit* (some do not care for liberation, verse 21) show the *phala* (result). *Tvam akaraṇaḥ svarāṭ* (you are with material senses and independent verse 28) shows *arthavāda* (praise). *Dṛtayah iva śvasanty asubhṛtaḥ* (they are like bellows breathing if they are not devotees, verse 17) shows *upapatti* (proof). All of this indicates Bhagavān alone, which means Kṛṣṇa, according to previous explanations.

### Text 44

*tvaṁ caitad brahma-dāyāda śraddhayātmānuśāsanam*  
*dhārayaṁś cara gāṁ kāmaṁ kāmānāṁ bharjanaṁ nṛṇām*

**Перевод:**

**And as you wander the earth at will, dear son of Brahmā, you should faithfully meditate on these instructions concerning the Lord, which burn up the material desires of all men.**

**Комментарий:**

Nārada is shown to be qualified since he is a qualified son of Brahmā (*brahma-dāyāda*). Holding the teachings about Kṛṣṇa in your heart, wander the earth as you please, in order to remove the desires of men. Instead of *bharjanam* sometimes *varjanam* is seen. The meaning is the same. Men’s desires will be destroyed by proving *bhakti* through hearing.

### Text 45

*śrī-śuka uvāca*  
*evaṁ sa ṛṣiṇādiṣṭaṁ gṛhītvā śraddhayātmavān*  
*pūrṇaḥ śruta-dharo rājann āha vīra-vrato muniḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Śukadeva Gosvāmī said: When Nārāyaṇa Ṛṣi ordered him in this way, the sage Nārada, with strong vows and peaceful heart, who hearing once remembered everything, who was fixed in the Lord, accepted the command with firm faith in the teachings. Now successful in all his purposes, O King, he replied to the Lord as follows.**

**Комментарий:**

Nārada had an undisturbed heart because he was a *naiṣṭhika-brahmacārī (vīra-vrataḥ*). By hearing once he remembered (*śruti-dharaḥ*) since he was inclined to contemplation (*muniḥ*) and fixed completely on the Lord *(ātmavān*). Therefore he accepted the order given by his guru Nārāyaṇa, with convinced intelligence immediately (ś*raddhayā*) in the subject. With all his desires fulfilled, he then spoke. Instead of *ṛṣiṇā* sometimes *guruṇā* is seen. O king! Śukadeva encourages Parīkṣit after answering his question.

### Text 46

*śrī-nārada uvāca*  
*namas tasmai bhagavate kṛṣṇāyāmala-kīrtaye*  
*yo dhatte sarva-bhūtānām abhavāyośatīḥ kalāḥ*

**Перевод:**

**Nārada said: I offer my obeisances to him of spotless fame, the Supreme Lord Kṛṣṇa, who contains within himself attractive** ***avatāras*** **so that all living beings can extinguish suffering.**

**Комментарий:**

Nārada speaks to show his satisfaction. Understanding that Kṛṣṇa is the conclusion of all the Vedas, he indicates Kṛṣṇa’s excellence. Among all the paths, *bhakti* is the best. Among all worshippers such as *jñānīs* and others, the devotees are the best. Among all objects of worship such as Brahman, Bhagavān is the best. This is deteremined by the words of all the *śrutis.* Among the forms of Bhagavān Kṛṣṇa is determined to be the best.

*sad-asataḥ paraṁ tvam atha yad eṣv avaśeṣam ṛtam*

**Distinct from the gross and subtle material manifestations, You are the reality underlying them all. (SB 10.87.17)**

*striya uragendra-bhoga-bhuja-daṇḍa-viṣakta-dhiyaḥ*

**The gopīs are attracted to your arms. (SB 10.87.23)**

Understanding this, directly in front of Nārāyaṇa, he does not offer respects to Nārāyaṇa, but to Kṛṣṇa, speaking loudly. Kṛṣṇa has fame which eradicates ignorance since he gives liberation even to demons. “Why do you give up me, your guru, standing in front of you, and instead offer respects to Kṛṣṇa?” Kṛṣṇa has within him attractive *avatāra* forms like you for extinguishing material suffering. By offereing respects to him, I offer respects to you. The present tense (*dhatte*) is used to show this is Kṛṣṇa’s eternal position. *Śruti* says:

*tasmāt kṛṣṇa evea parā devas taṁ dhārayet taṁ rasayet taṁ bhajet tam; yajet oṁ tat sat*

**Therefore Kṛsṇa is the Supreme Lord. You should meditate upon him, give him pleasure, worship him and offer sacrifice to him.** ***Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad***

### Text 47

*ity ādyam ṛṣim ānamya tac-chiṣyāṁś ca mahātmanaḥ*  
*tato ’gād āśramaṁ sākṣāt pitur dvaipāyanasya me*

**Перевод:**

**After saying this, Nārada bowed down to Nārāyaṇa Ṛṣi, the foremost of sages, and also to his saintly disciples. He then immediately returned directly to the hermitage of my father, Dvaipāyana Vyāsa.**

**Комментарий:**

He went without delay to the *āśrama* of Vedavyasa, where Śukadeva did not reside. In *Brahma-vaivarta Purāṇa* it is explained that Śukadeva was in the womb of Vyasa’s wife, bu remained there for many years by his power of yoga. His father begged him, and asked why he did not come out of the womb. “Why should I come out? Immediately on coming out I will be covered by the Lord’s *māyā.”* Hearing this, Vyāsa went to Dvārakā on his instructions, and brought Kṛṣṇa, Svayam Bhagavān. The Lord said, “You mother is suffering. Why do you not come out?” Hearing this, and requested by the Lord, he said, “If you remove *māyā*, then I will come out.” This was accepted by the Lord, and Śukadeva came out. He then left Vyāsa’s *āśrama* and wandered about.

### Text 49

*ity etad varṇitaṁ rājan yan naḥ praśnaḥ kṛtas tvayā*  
*yathā brahmaṇy anirdeśye nīṛguṇe ’pi manaś caret*

**Thus I have replied to the question you asked me, O King, concerning how the mind can have access to the Lord, whoh is indescribable by material words and devoid of material qualities.**

The chapter now concludes. The question that you asked me has been answered here. Instead of *ity etat* sometimes *tad etat* is seen. What was the question? How can the mind approach the Lord by *śrutis* or by *bhakti* arising from the Lord’s mercy and the nature of the Lord? How can the *śrutis* approach the Lord? *Tat tu samanvayāt*: that the Lord iss the sole topic of discussion in the Vedas is confirmed by all scriptures. (*Brahma-sūtra* 1.1.4) It has already been shown that the Lord is purport of the Vedas. *Śabda-brahma paraṁ brahma mamobhe śāśvatī tanū*: The scripture and Brahman are my two eternal forms. (SB 6.16.51) Since scripture and the Lord are one, they have the same *śakti.* Even without the *śrutis,* there is manifestation of very great *śakti* in Kṛṣṇa, Svayam Bhagavān:

*nibhṛta-marun-mano-’kṣa-dṛḍha-yoga-yujo hṛdi yan*  
*munaya upāsate tad arayo ’pi yayuḥ smaraṇāt*

### Text 50

*yo ’syotprekṣaka ādi-madhya-nidhane yo ’vyakta-jīveśvaro*  
*yaḥ sṛṣṭvedam anupraviśya ṛṣiṇā cakre puraḥ śāsti tāḥ*  
*yaṁ sampadya jahāty ajām anuśayī suptaḥ kulāyaṁ yathā*  
*taṁ kaivalya-nirasta-yonim abhayaṁ dhyāyed ajasraṁ harim*

**Перевод:**

**He is the Lord who eternally watches over this universe, before, during and after its manifestation. He is the master of both the unmanifest material energy and the spirit soul. After sending forth the creation he enters within it, accompanying each living entity. There he creates the material bodies and then remains as their regulator. By surrendering to him the** ***jīva*** **covered with** ***upādhis*** **can escape the embrace of illusion, just as a dreaming person forgets his own body. One who wants liberation from fear should constantly meditate upon this Lord, who destroys** ***māyā*** **by his pure** ***svarūpa*** **and gives freedom from fear.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is the supreme witness in all three phases of time. He is the *antaryāmī* of the Kāraṇa Ocean and by his two *śaktis, jīva* and *māyā,* he becomes the material cause (*upādāna)* of creation with consciousness (*mahat-tattva*). This is called *sarga.* He is the creator, maintainer and destroyer of the total universe. Entering into it, he becomes the *antaryāmī* of the total universe (Garbhodakaśāyī). This is his pastime of entering the universe. He makes the individual bodies by means of Brahmā (*ṛṣiṇā*). He is thus the creator of *visarga.* He regulates the bodies as *antaryāmī* of the individual soul (Kṣīrodakaśāyī).

Having shown the Lord’s *svarūpa-śakti* as the instigator of the powers of *māyā,* Śukadeva now shows him as the destroyer of *māyā.* Becoming absorbed in the Lord, the *jīva* covered by *upādhis* (*anuśayī)* rejects ignorance, a function of *māyā (ajām).* This shows the Lord’s pastime of liberating *jīvas.* Having indicated the Lord through the powers of his *svarūpa-śakti*, represented by secondary characteristics, Śukadeva then directly indicates the Lord in the last line.

One should meditate on the Lord with his famous *svarūpa (harim),* who throws *māyā* far away permanently by his pure *svarūpa*, the shelter of his pure, powerful *śaktis (kaivalyena),* simply by being absorbed in the Lord. The Lord who has these qualities, is suitable for bhakti with meditation since he has the purest *svarūpa,* the purest *śaktis,* and the highest mercy, which is bestowed on the *jīvas* for their benefit. By rejecting *māya* after describing its powers, by establishing the Lord in the forefront with his pure *śaktis,* by indicating *bhakti* as the *sādhana* represented by meditation, by reliance on the teachings of the Veda, this verse has the same meaning as the ancient prayers of the *śrutis.* One should also see its details. It also has the same meaning as the first verse of the *Bhāgavatam.*

The following also has the same meaning.

*atra sargo visargaś ca sthānaṁ poṣaṇam ūtayaḥ*  
*manvantareśānukathā nirodho muktir āśrayaḥ*
