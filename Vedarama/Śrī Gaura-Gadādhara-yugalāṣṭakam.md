# Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara-yugalāṣṭakam

*kṣitau luṭad-gaura-kalevarābhyāṁ*  
*sadā mahā-prema-vilāsakābhyām*  
*samudra-tīre naṭa-nāgarābhyāṁ*  
*namo’stu me gaura-gadādharābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**“I offer obeisances unto Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara who roam about the earth and are of a golden complexion. They are always immersed in the highest levels of divine love as They dance in bliss along the ocean-shore.”**

*hāhā kva rādheti muhuḥ sthitābhyāṁ*  
*śrī rādhikā-kṛṣṇa-vapurdharābhyām*  
*ānanda-līlā-rasa-raṣjitābhyāṁ*  
*namo’stu me gaura-gadādharābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**“I offer obeisances unto Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara, who constantly cry out, ‘O Rādhā, O Kṛṣṇa –where are You?’ They are the incarnations of Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, and They find unparalleled pleasure in the blissful mellows of transcendental pastimes.”**

*advaita-cintāhara-sambhavābhyāṁ*  
*sadā bhavānanda-manoharābhyām*  
*acintya-līlā-paripūritābhyāṁ*  
*namo’stu me gaura-gadādharābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**“I offer obeisances unto Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara, who have appeared to remove the distress of Śrī Advaita Ācārya. Their minds are always absorbed in divine bliss and They are fully satisfied performing Their inconceivable pastimes.”**

*jīvaika-nistāra-dhṛtavratābhyāṁ*  
*śrī kṛṣṇa-nāmnā-jana-tārakābhyām*  
*hare hare kṛṣṇa mukhāmbujābhyāṁ*  
*namo’stu me gaura-gadādharābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**“I offer obeisances unto Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara, who are determined to rescue all living beings. They liberate all entities by distributing the Holy Names of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. and Their lotus mouths constantly chant ‘Hare Hare’ and ‘Kṛṣṇa’.”**

*aśeṣa-duḥkhāmaya-bheṣajābhyāṁ*  
*kirīṭa-keyura-vibhūṣitābhyām*  
*graiveya-mālā-maṇi-raṣjitābhyāṁ*  
*namo’stu me gaura-gadādharābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**“I offer obeisances unto Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara, who provide the remedy to the seemingly unending misery of the material world. They are adorned with crowns and bracelets, and necklaces of precious jewels decorate Their necks.”**

*śrīvatsa-romāvali-raṣjitābhyāṁ*  
*vakṣaḥ-sthale kaustubha-bhuṣatābhyām*  
*trelokya-sanmohana-sundarābhyāṁ*  
*namo’stu me gaura-gadādharābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**“I offer obeisances unto Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara, Who are decorated by a lock of golden hair known as Śrīvatsa. Their chests are adorned with the Kaustubha gem and Their beauty bewilders the inhabitants of the three worlds.”**

*sphuracchalat-kāṣcana-kuṇḍalābhyāṁ*  
*sadāṣṭabhāvaiḥ pariśobhitābhyām*  
*svedāśru-kampādi-vibhūṣitābhyāṁ*  
*namo’stu me gaura-gadādharābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**“I offer obeisances unto Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara. Their golden earrings swing to-and-fro as They continually exhibit the eight ecstatic symptoms of divine love. Due to such love, Their bodies are decorated with beads of sweat and the hair on Their bodies stands on end.”**

*śrīmacchivānanda-manorathābhyāṁ*  
*sadā sukhānanda-rasa-sphurābhyām*  
*madīya-sarvasva-padāmbujābhyāṁ*  
*namo’stu me gaura-gadādharābhyām*

**Перевод:**

**“I offer obeisances unto Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara, who bring indescribable joy to Śrī Śivānanda. They tremble in ecstasy due to always being absorbed in the highest mellows of divine bliss. I offer everything to Their lotus-feet.”**

*paṭhanti ye gaura-gadādharāṣṭakaṁ*  
*padyaṁ labhante vrajayuḥma-pādam*  
*advaita-putreṇa mayoktametan-*  
*nāmnācyutānanda-janena dhīmatā*

**Перевод:**

**“One who reads these verses known as ‘Gaura-Gadādharāṣṭakam’ will surely attain Their lotus-feet. This prayer was written by the son of Advaita known as Acyutānanda who is said by some to be intelligent.”**
