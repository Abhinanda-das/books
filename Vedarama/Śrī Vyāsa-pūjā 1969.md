# Śrī Vyāsa-pūjā 1969

## Boston

## Uttara Dasi

Just at the end of night, when all is still  
And hung with blindness, breeding dark decay,  
The sun, with rays of cleansing, living light  
Moves forth on the horizon, and brings day;  
So an Acharya comes, bearing the light  
Of love of God, the clearest beams of all.  
He is our Shelter from the eyeless night  
Of nescience, and we know we shall not fall.  
If we but keep His teachings in our hearts  
We shall love Krishna by this one life's end,  
The Master's order is our life and soul  
For He is Krishna's confidential Friend.  
I therefore offer this most humble prayer  
That Srila Prabhupad make my faith strong;  
He is the sun; illuminating all:  
Go back to Godhead, back where you belong!

—Uttara Dasi  
(ISKCON—Boston)

## Lilavati

**For Prabhupadji**

As we walked through the streets of New York, through the heat and the dirt and the waste,  
An indescribable smile came over His beautiful face.  
Not a smile that would last for a moment, or was curled by disdain or desire,  
But perpetual, blissful, secure, shyly gleaming with spiritual fire.

We gazed at that smiling so long, with its peace, its surrender and grace,  
That it seemed that the world of the living had its source in the glow of Your face.  
Now can we praise or describe You, who has plucked us up from our strife,  
Who has lit up this dark place of danger, and has given us back our life.

There was never a basis for living, there was never a reason to act,  
And now You have blessed us with unbound gifts, and every moment is packed  
With meaning, with hope and with joy; one small wish, one stubborn desire:  
To serve You and chant Hare Krishna for the rest of my eternal life.

—Lilavati  
(ISKCON—Boston)

## Satsvarupa

**Vyas Puja Offering**

In the first verse of his Prayers Glorifying the Spiritual Master, Sri Viswanath Chakravarty prays that the spiritual master is like a rain-pour of mercy. The rain is merciful because it alone can extinguish the blazing forest fire. That forest fire is compared to the burning heart of the man in material existence. He is trying to enjoy, but his heart is burning with anxieties: the pleasure of the senses is incomplete and temporary, the course of the mind is uncontrollable and sometimes beset with misery; and to take refuge in philosophic speculations or art and novels is finally too dry and tedious to be completely satisfying by itself. The atmosphere in which we live—the very conditions of existence on any planet in the universe—makes it impossible to relax, be peaceful or attain happiness. Because, although no one desires it, old age devours youth, diseases stop one and all from the pleasures of continual good health and everyone’s duration is at last cut off by the axe of death. Rebirth, old age, disease and death break out suddenly like fires, and no one is needed to start them. Just as in the city no one wants so many fires or accidents, and yet the ambulance or fire engine is always wandering in the street. Everywhere there are miseries!

Yet we learn from Bhagavad Gita that each and every living entity is factually aloof from all these miseries. The real person, the actual self who is eternal spirit-soul, is not subject to old age or death or any other disadvantage of the material world. Lord Krishna, the Personality of Godhead, declares that the real person is His part and parcel and is, as God Himself is, always blissful, eternal and full of knowledge. Our only mistake is to fall into illusion and forget our original position as eternal servitor of the Supreme. The retaining of the position of eternal blissful knowledge remains only theoretical for those souls who continue to wander, birth after birth, through the millions of species of life, as described by Chaitanya Mahaprabhu in Teachings of Lord Chaitanya. It is only by the mercy of a bonafide spiritual master who is coming in disciplic succession from Krishna that one can receive the realized knowledge by which he can cut the knot of ignorance which binds him to continue in his repeated sufferings. By the realized knowledge of the spiritual master, one can himself become self-realized (Gita, 4/34) and at once end his illusory struggle to be happy without God-consciousness. It is only by the mercy of the spiritual master that we can gain the goal of God-consciousness.

We therefore offer our respectful obeisances to our spiritual master, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami, who as our only real and permanent father, has kindly kindled in us the desire to serve eternally in the position of a transcendental worker in the Hare Krishna movement of Lord Chaitanya. The spiritual master descends like a stout rope down into the dark and filthy well where we are loitering; by Krishna’s Grace we can catch hold of that rope and be easily lifted into the invigorating and bright atmosphere of Krishna Consciousness.

But this present age is so rank with the cheating propensity that, even in so-called spiritual affairs, nine times out of ten the “guru” or “yogi” or “rishi” who proposes to revive our original consciousness turns out to be a fraud claiming himself to be God; a fraud who can only collect a fee, who cannot engage us in any full-time transcendental pursuit. We are certain to have escaped the plight of surrendering to a false guru by the mercy of the unbreakable chain of disciplic succession of spiritual masters called the Gaudiya Sampradaya, of which A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami is the present, leading acharya—in direct line from Lord Chaitanya, the Original Teacher of Bhagavad Gita and Srimad Bhagwatam. What our spiritual master teaches us is purely spiritual, one hundred percent confirmed by the purport of the shastras, which is, that Krishna is to be sought out and served in love. The result of this process is seen in the students of Krishna Consciousness, whose lives are externally and internally distinct from all pretender-groups in that they are engaged with mind, body and words in the practical direct loving service of the Personality of Godhead, and wholeheartedly detached from material desires.

We offer our prostrated obeisances to the spiritual master who through his many books is always filling our thoughts with the nectar of the Pastimes of Krishna, the Lifter of the Goverdhan Hill and the Beloved of the gopis. As far as I am concerned, I do not know Sri Krishna, but I know Prabhupad, and he knows Krishna. I offer my humble obeisances because he has engaged us in transcendental Samkirtan Party and given us transcendental dress, because he feeds us on prasadam or spiritual food and gives us spiritual family life. We cannot expect great numbers from the masses—who take a “guru” out of sense gratification or seeking health remedies, mystic powers or business success—to be attracted to the selfless acme of devotion to Krishna. We cannot ourselves ever love him enough or repay him or serve him in measure to the gift he made to us: fixing us onto the goal of service to Krishna. We can, however, make continual prayer to Krishna that our service to Sri Guru expand in enthusiasm and respect and that our home remain at his lotus feet.

—Satsvarupa  
(ISKCON—Boston)

## Woomapati

**Vyas Puja Tribute**

“The Spiritual Master is honored as much as the Supreme Lord on account of His being the Most Confidential Servitor of the Lord. This is acknowledged in all revealed Scriptures and is followed by all authorities. Therefore I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Lotus Feet of my Spiritual Master who is the bona fide representative of Hari.” (prayer)

**“O Suta Goswami, the great sages who have completely taken shelter of the Lotus Feet of the Lord can at once sanctify others who may come into touch with them while the water of the Ganges can do so after a prolonged use only.” (Srimad Bhagwatam, 1.1.15)**

I offer my most humble and respectful obeisances to my beloved Spiritual Master, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Goswami Maharaj, who out of His unlimited compassion and mercy has brought Krishna Consciousness to the most fallen souls of the kaliyuga.

One can only approach Krishna by the mercy of the bona fide representative of Krishna. How can we know how to approach Krishna by ourselves? Only someone who already knows how Krishna wants to be approached can direct others in the proper way of approaching Him. For this reason the Spiritual Master is honored and respected as much as Krishna Himself. This can be easily seen in His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami; for, which of us would know anything of the Supreme Absolute Truth, the All-Attractive Sri Krishna without the causeless Mercy of our Spiritual Master?

“I was born in the darkest of ignorance and my Spiritual Master opened my eyes with the torch of knowledge. I offer my respectful obeisances unto Him.” (prayer)

The genuine Spiritual Master teaches completely in accordance with the revealed Scriptures without any personally motivated interpretations, as the mailman delivers the letter without writing anything on himself. In this way, the Spiritual Master shows the student the Source of Divine Knowledge, so that the student can now see. The Spiritual Master also shows the student how to live in the revealed knowledge according to the directions in the Scriptures Themselves. I was such a fool to think that on my own I could find anything, let alone the Supreme Absolute Truth, but my Spiritual Master, by refusing to compromise with my idiocy, told me to simply take Krishna’s Word for things and see if it wasn’t better that way.

What can I say; what can I do; what can I write to communicate? Krishna Consciousness is perfect. Prabhupada is the perfect Spiritual Master. Everybody should fall at His Lotus Feet in humility and surrender. He has made His Mercy available to everyone and anybody who wastes this opportunity is a fool. Who else is showing so plainly the Beautiful Truth, the Supreme Person Sri Krishna? Who else is teaching us how to love Krishna the way that Krishna wants to be loved?

I beg to offer this most unworthy and inadequate tribute to my Spiritual Master, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Goswami Maharaj on Vyas Puja, 1969. I prostrate myself at His Feet and offer my most respectful obeisances and undying gratitude again and again and again.

Namah Om Vishnupadaya Krishnapresthaya Bhutale Srimate Bhaktivedanta Swamin Iti Namine.

—Woomapati  
(ISKCON—Los Angeles)

We are like robots acting out Maya’s mechanical control in the three modes simply to try to satisfy the perversion of temporary infatuation. How can I, deluded in this robot cage, hope to illuminate the Glories of Sri Guru? It is like the lantern held up to illuminate the sun. I hope my poor representation of His Boundless Mercy and Grace will be understood as only the smallest voicing of His Unlimited Qualities beyond even the great demigods’ scope. All Glories to the Spiritual Master!! May He Grace us with the causeless mercy of His transcendental service for ever and ever.

The most fallen, unworthy servant of His Divine Grace,  
Jayapataka

## Jayadvaitadas

Om Ajana Timiran Dasya Jnanan Jnana Salakaya Chaksurum Militam Yena Tasmai Sri Gurave Namah. I offer my most respectful obeisances unto my Spiritual Master, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, who has opened my eyes with the torchlight of knowledge when I was standing in the darkness of ignorance. This ignorance is forgetfulness of Krishna, and by the grace of the bona fide Spiritual Master is this ignorance dispelled.

Srila Prabhupada is the Personified Mercy of the Supreme Lord and our only hope for achieving the loving devotional service of Krishna. Following in the Footsteps of Lord Chaitanya, He has benedicted us with the Holy Name of Lord Krishna; and following in the Footsteps of Srila Vyasadev, He is presenting us with the essence of the Vedic literature in the form of His treasured books.

In The Bhagavad Gita As It Is, Srila Vyasadev’s basic presentation of the science of spiritual life and the first of Srila Prabhupada’s books to be published in this country, the importance of the Spiritual Master is stressed throughout. Arjuna was in a distressful condition of confusion and perplexity, and so he submitted to Lord Krishna, the Original Spiritual Master, for instruction. “Now I am confused about duty, and have lost all composure because of weakness. In this condition I am asking You to tell me clearly what is best for me. Now I am Your disciple and a soul surrendered unto You. Please instruct me.” (Bhagavad Gita, II/7) Arjuna surrendered to the Spiritual Master. This is the beginning of The Bhagavad Gita and the beginning of spiritual life. It is understood that it is not possible to attain spiritual perfection by one’s own endeavor. Acceptance of the bona fide Spiritual Master is required. When a person desiring to cultivate Krishna Consciousness accepts initiation from a bona fide Spiritual Master, then the connection between the devotee and Krishna is established. Without such initiation, the actual connection with Krishna Consciousness is never achieved.

Therefore, in order to fulfill the mission of the human form of life, one must approach the Spiritual Master, who alone can give transcendental knowledge. The Bhagavad Gita recommends: “Just try to learn the truth by approaching a spiritual master. Inquire from him submissively and render service unto him. The self-realized soul can impart knowledge unto you because he has seen the truth.” (Gita, IV/34)

Spiritual Truth can only be understood from the disciplic succession. “Evam parampara praptum imam rajarsayo viduh.” “This supreme science was received through the chain of disciplic succession, and the saintly kings understood it in that way.” (Gita, IV/2) The Spiritual Master must be coming in an unbroken line of disciplic succession, and he must be a devotee of Krishna. Krishna says to Arjuna, “That very ancient science of the Supreme is today told by Me to you because you are My devotee as well as My friend; therefore, you can understand the transcendental mystery of this science.” (Gita, IV/3) The nondevotees may speculate on the meaning of The Bhagavad Gita, but they cannot understand it. They are licking on the bottle of honey. However, if one accepts initiation from the bona fide Spiritual Master, serving him humbly and inquiring from him submissively, he can actually taste the nectar of Krishna Consciousness.

Arjuna heard The Bhagavad Gita directly from Lord Krishna and his ignorance and illusion were completely dispelled. “Arjuna said: My dear Krishna, O Infallible One, my illusion is now gone. I have regained my memory by Your mercy, and now I am fixed without any doubt, prepared to act according to Your instructions.” (Gita, XVIII/72) A few verses later, Samjaya says, “By the mercy of Vyasa, I have heard these most confidential talks directly from the Master of all mysticism, Krishna, speaking Personally to Arjuna.” (Gita, XVIII/75) As Arjuna was able to see and hear Krishna, similarly Samjaya was able to see and hear Krishna directly with his purified senses, even though he was not personally present on the battlefield, and was thus able to understand the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Samjaya admitted that this was only possible by the mercy of Vyasadev, his Spiritual Master. ## Hansadutta and Himavati

The Spiritual Master is receiving benediction from the ocean of mercy; Just like the cloud pours water on the forest fire to extinguish it, so the Spiritual Master extinguishes the blazing fire of material existence. I offer my most respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of such Spiritual Master.

The bona fide Spiritual Master is the factual recipient of that ocean of mercy because “He is not envious, but a kindly friend to all creatures, and does not think himself a proprietor, and is free from false ego…” (Bhagavad Gita, 12/13) Krishna says, “That kind of person, he is very dear to me.” Now one may be very dear to some person in this material world, but that kind of endearment is not meant here. To be dear to Krishna is extraordinary because He is the Supermost Person in the entire creation, He is the Chief Eternal amongst all the eternals. In the Brahma Samhita He is described in the very first verse, “Krishna who is known as Govinda \[Cowherdsboy\] is the Supreme Godhead. He has an eternal blissful Spiritual body. He is the Origin of all and He is the prime Cause of all causes.” So the form of the Supreme Person is not like the form we are in the understanding of. This material body is temporary as compared with the spiritual form which is eternal; this material body is full of ignorance and the spiritual form of Krishna is full of knowledge; this material form is always miserable with the problems of birth, old age, disease and death as compared with the form of Krishna which is always blissful. This is Krishna—Sat-chit-ananda Vigraha. His body is fashioned of eternity, knowledge and bliss; just like our form is made of the gross and subtle elements earth, water, fire, air, etc., his form is made of Sat-chit-ananda. To know Krishna or to be very dear to Krishna is very extraordinary, because you cannot associate with that Supreme Spiritual Person unless you are also of that Sat-chit-ananda nature. As much as you cannot enter into the sunglobe unless you have got the fiery body suited for that atmosphere, or water—you cannot live in the water because your body is not suited for that nature. Now how one can be reestablished in that spiritual nature is the whole program for the human society.

Krishna is the Cause of all causes and He must necessarily be the Knower of everything. This is confirmed in The Bhagavad Gita: “I am the Compiler of Vedanta and I am the Knower of Vedanta.” Vedanta means knowledge. So whatever knowledge there may be, it is originating from Krishna.

In the beginning of the creation, Krishna imparted the knowledge concerning the constitution of the living entities directly to Brahmaji from within his heart, and thus the disciplic succession in the matter of real knowledge was started with Brahma. Real knowledge means to know about Spirit, which is what I am—“Aham brahmasmi,” I am spirit—and false knowledge or Maya means to know things on the basis of the material senses which are imperfect and cannot extend to the reality of the spiritual world of which I am a part eternally. Maya means that I think I am this body and illusion is to know everything on the basis of this false identification.

So Spiritual Master means that He has heard the narration of the transcendental message as it was originally spoken to Brahma by Krishna. Krishna and anything Krishna speaks is completely identical with Him in every way. The transcendental knowledge which is coming down to us in that line is Sat-chit-ananda; it is not different from Krishna, and therefore anyone who has the good fortune to come in contact with such line and give it submissive aural reception, on such a person it will act. The electric current may be travelling through that wire over many thousands of miles, but still, if you touch it, you will get the shock. So the Spiritual Master is the transparent medium for Whom no one is equal to or greater than. Such Spiritual Master is completely spiritual, and no one should think that there is any trace of material inebriety about such Pure Devotee of the Lord. A live wire is completely electrified, and no one should make the mistake of touching it, lest he be burnt to ashes due to its being connected to the power house; as the live wire is full of potency, similarly the Spiritual Master, being in contact with the Supreme Absolute Truth, the Personality of Godhead Lord Krishna, through the chain of the disciplic succession, becomes completely identical with Krishna, Sat-chit-ananda. He is Krishna Consciousness personified. He is nondifferent from Krishna. The Spiritual Master is eternal, and He has eternal knowledge and He is eternally blissful. Among gentlemen, Prabhupad, You are the most cultured, because as a Pure Vaishnava. you surpass all Varnas and Ashramas.

Among scholars and philosophers You are the supermost learned because you are in perfect knowledge of the very End of Knowledge Himself.

Among singers you are the Best because you only sing of the Name, Fame and Glories of the Supreme Lord.

Among dancers, you are the Most Beautiful because you are eternally dancing in ecstasy of Sri Krishna Samkirtan together with Lord Chaitanya and all His associates.

—Mahapurusha

## Damodar

I offer my most humble obeisances to my Spiritual Master, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupad, Who is so dear to Lord Krishna on this Earth, having taken shelter of His Lotus Feet. Prabhupad is the kindest of all living entities, giving without any restriction, to any person who desires it, pure unalloyed devotional service to God. He has had such mercy upon us, miserable conditioned souls with no qualification, to give us an opportunity to enter into service of Sri Sri Radha and Krishna, the Supreme Absolute Truth. Now have we deserved this gesture of mercy? There is no question of our deserving it. Prabhupad has simply given us this chance causelessly, just by His Grace. We have seen His perfection, and have become attracted to it. He is the transparent medium through Whom we can see Krishna Himself, because Prabhupad is working twenty-four hours daily for Krishna. He is always acting in the realization that everything is Krishna’s property, and is expert in engaging others in devotional service. Prabhupad is our dearmost friend. In working to satisfy Him, we can find actual satisfaction ourselves. When Prabhupad is being fanned, all His devotees are cooled as well. We have tried to satisfy ourselves, our family, our society, but in no case can we satisfy either others or ourselves unless we take shelter of the bonafide Spiritual Master—only He can tell us who we are, and what is the goal of this human form of life. We are trying hard to be big men, but Prabhupad advises us to become great through service of the Greatest. And He is in direct disciplic succession from Lord Chaitanya, the Summum Bonum Supreme Absolute Truth. Prabhupad can deliver Krishna to us. Our Spiritual Master is not God, but He is the rarest among all living entities—a pure devotee of God. Therefore, because He is completely spiritual, He is as good, as worshipable, as God. He is a true Sannyasin. Not that He has renounced work; no, He works hard for Krishna, only for Krishna, and for His Guru Maharaj. He came to this country just to please His Spiritual Master. Even though He was living in the most auspicious holy city of Vrindaban, He left for the United States to do more for His Spiritual Master. So in this way, through the disciplic succession, such Great Souls as Prabhupad have exhibited the qualities of true love of God in their selfless devotion to Krishna. And in this way Prabhupad has become the Spiritual Father of so many children, and is raising them nicely in Krishna Consciousness. He has given us the greatest gift—life. Before we were dead, just attached to a miserable material body, suffering on account of our illusory happiness. But now we are learning how to live. Prabhupad has opened our eyes in the midst of darkness, and given us the torch of knowledge. Therefore, He is the best friend. Prabhupad has given us His whole life, just because, as a pure devotee, He is so kind. Every action, every word, is for our benefit. He cares nothing for Himself, only for serving God, and teaching others how to serve. It is confirmed in the Bhagavad Gita that such a person, who explains the process of surrendering to Krishna, is the dearest to Krishna of all His servants. Therefore I bow down to the Lotus Feet of His Divine Grace, Who has rained down His Mercy upon the forest fire of material existence, and has given us this priceless jewel of Devotional Service of Krishna.

—Damodar  
(ISKCON—Washington)

Limited attempt to appreciate the unlimited Spiritual Master
