# Śrī Vyāsa-pūjā 1971

## The Meaning of Vyāsa-pūjā

*Vyās Pūjā* is a literary offering of praise and gratitude from the students unto the pure Vaiṣṇava spiritual master. By its very nature such a compilation of praise of a pure devotee must face insufficiency. Just as devotees cannot adequately describe the infinite glories of the Supreme Lord, so a disciple cannot do justice to the immeasurable ocean of kind, transcendental instruction who is the spiritual master—the representative of Kṛṣṇa appearing before us conditioned souls. He is stated by Kṛṣṇa in *Bhagavad-gītā* to be “the dearmost” for his preaching *Bhagavad-gītā As It Is* to the devotees, and the Lord says that there will never be one more dear to Him. If one thinks of the *Vyāsa Pūjā* as an attempt to finish up glorification of the spiritual master, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda, then we must inform him that in that endeavor this book must fall far short of the goal. In a preface to his *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* translation and commentary, Śrīla Prabhupāda writes as follows concerning the Supreme Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa:

"We should not forget that the Lord is unlimited. This material world out of which this tiny earth is only a particle, represents a fragmental portion of His unfathomable glories. And on the surface of the earth there are numberless cities, and in each and every one of them there are hundreds of printing houses all engaged in broadcasting the message of this limited space. We can just imagine how many hundreds of thousands of volumes we may need to glorify the activities of the unlimited Lord. Therefore, sixty volumes of *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* are just a sample distribution of His glories presented in a manner understandable to the stagnant brain of the residents of this insignificant globe. The residents of higher planets, who have brains superior to man’s, go through thousands and millions of volumes of *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* to know about the glories of the Lord.”

What is true of the Supreme Lord is true of His pure devotee: the glory of His causeless mercy outmeasures all collections of praise. Just as the Ananta Śeṣa incarnation failed to enunciate a complete description of the glories and varieties of the Supreme Lord although He tried for thousands of years with thousands of heads, so thousands of disciples cannot capture in writing the infinite scope and worth of Śrīla Prabhupāda. Even leaders of nondevotees have come to appreciate the ISKCON movement founded by His Divine Grace, and in newspapers and magazines we read comments such as: “These are good men; we need more of them in our community.” “The Kṛṣṇa consciousness movement is teaching about God on an educated level,” “They are good, peaceful people, who do not go in for intoxication or illicit sex,” etc. Anyone seriously concerned with improving the current degraded times can appreciate that Śrīla Prabhupāda is turning wretched young men and women into flowerlike devotees who do not take part in any sort of vices and who engage twenty-four hours a day in the topmost welfare work by informing everyone of his eternal spiritual nature in love of God. It is a fact that anyone Who takes to Krishna consciousness and who chants Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa, Hare Hare, Hare Rāma, Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma, Hare Hare will get relief from material misgivings and will get free of repeated birth and death and become eligible to go back to the kingdom of God. Śrīla Prabhupāda is freeing everyone from the clutches of the material nature. His potency to distribute elevation from material misery, love of God, comes directly in the line from Lord Caitanya, who was prayed to as follows by Rūpa Gosvāmī: “I offer my obeisances to You. You are the most munificent person because You are directly distributing love of Kṛṣṇa.”

This book is a sampling. Some of the praises are more eloquent than others, some are more expert, some are factual reports, attempts at *ślokas*—but none can completely capture how dearly and strongly our spiritual master loves Kṛṣṇa and how completely Kṛṣṇa loves him and reciprocates with him at every moment. But it is in order with the scriptures and the line of *ācāryas* that we present this book to our spiritual master; for it is a sincere expression from the disciples who are prostrated at his lotus feet. We can understand that as a father likes to hear the talk of his baby child, even if it is broken, infantile speech, so the spiritual master appreciates our attempts at transcendental speech because we are his children, and are sincerely engaged in loving service.

These written pieces are transcendental and confidential. The unsympathetic outsider who pokes his nose into this relationship cannot enter its understanding. One who does not chant Hare Kṛṣṇa cannot understand or feel the relish of chanting because chanting is for those who chant. Similarly, *Vyās Pūjā* is for the disciples and the spiritual master. It remains a great secret to those who are not touched with the association of devotional service. “The line of service as drawn by you is fresh and bracing as morning dew.”

Our object, as you have so carefully taught us, Śrīla Prabhupāda, is not to keep Kṛṣṇa consciousness to ourselves, but to spread it all over the world. This year you have been traveling to India personally speaking and managing ISKCON and showing us the meaning of *ācārya* by example. And now you are traveling and inspiring the devotees and centers in the U.S. and Europe. At *Vyās Pūjā* time we, your intimate children, are gathered at your feet to tell you our feelings as best we can. By your blessing, we can go forth from this *Vyās Pūjā* gathering of 1971 and, all devotees together as one great ISKCON, without faction, truly perform the work with our thoughts, words and deeds. Let us go and distribute this literature of Śrīla Prabhupāda’s—Kṛṣṇa’s message—kindly delivered to the Western countries. Let us cooperate without ill feelings among ourselves. Let us very strictly observe all the regulative principles and stay as pure representatives. Let us celebrate pure *saṅkīrtana* and magazine distribution to please you. All glories to Śrīla Prabhupāda.

All your disciples pray that you will remain in our presence for many years to come, and by our cooperation you will be able to spend time writing volumes of *Bhāgavatam* while we carry on the program and mission of your Guru Mahārāja.

## Trinidad

Dearest Śrīla Prabhupāda,

We offer our prostrated obeisances at the holy lotus feet of His Divine Grace Śrīla Prabhupāda. His Divine Grace, Śrīla Prabhupāda, is a great and pure saint. He is full of godly qualities. He is coming in the *parampara* from Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

It is our joy to know that Lord Kṛṣṇa exists, and the unquestionable proof is to come in contact with Śrīla Prabhupāda. His transcendental qualities are above anything to be experienced in this world. Our incapable words of praise are like trying to show someone the sun with a candle.

If only everyone could come in contact with Śrīla Prabhupāda, that would be the greatest thing that could ever happen.

Before Śrīla Prabhupāda bestowed His mercy upon us, we were simply frustrated, unable to fulfill our hearts’ desire for happiness, eternity and knowledge. Now, His Divine Grace Śrīla Prabhupāda has taken us, who were full of misery and ignorance and afraid of death, and has given us eternal life of bliss and knowledge. For such a gift, what can we say? It is simply mercy upon us. We beg You that we may be allowed to serve You eternally, and by so doing, help other living entities know of You.

Our only happiness is to remember that You are fully present with us always by Your instructions.

We are trying to carry out Your instructions by glorifying the *parampara* system, without which the *Bhagavad-gītā* is not understandable. The people here, being Indian descendants, have been raised hearing Hare Kṛṣṇa and the *Bhagavad-gītā,* but because it was not from the lips of a pure devotee, it had no meaning.

They are astonished that American boys and girls are chanting and taking this message of Lord Kṛṣṇa seriously. This is Your mercy, and this is what we tell and explain to them. You have left India to teach a godless civilization to love Kṛṣṇa. The so-called Hindus and religionists have their own concocted philosophy and teachings; they cannot understand that without Your mercy and grace they cannot know God. Kṛṣṇa says, “Just try to learn the truth by approaching a spiritual master. Inquire from him submissively and render service unto him. The self-realized soul can impart knowledge unto you because he has seen the truth.” (Bg. 4.34)

There are many professional men here who call themselves *gurus* and accept disciples. They completely cover the real teachings of *Bhagavad-gītā.* Milk touched by the lips of a serpent causes poisonous effects. To the public, it appears to be the same teaching, but actually it is all showbottle. In the end, they are all impersonalists and voidists.

*namas te sārasvate deve gaura-vāṇī-pracāriṇe*  
*nirviśeṣa-śūnyavādi-pāścātya-deśa-tāriṇe*
