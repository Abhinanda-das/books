## The Ācāryadeva

When we speak of the fundamental principle of *Gurudeva* or *Ācāryadeva*, we speak of something that is of universal application. There is only one *Guru* who appears in an infinity of forms to teach all men. The *Guru* or *Ācāryadeva,* as we learn from the bonafide scriptures, delivers the message of the absolute world, the transcendental abode of the Absolute Personality where everything non-differentially serves the Absolute Truth. In the *Kaṭha-Upaniṣad* it is said:

*tad-vijñānārthaṁ sa gurum evābhigacchet*  
*samit-pāṇiḥ śrotriyaṁ brahma-niṣṭham*

**‘In order to learn that transcendental science, one must approach the bona-fide Spiritual Master in disciplic succession, fixed in the Absolute Truth.’**

Thus it has been enjoined herewith that in order to receive that transcendental knowledge one must approach the *Guru.* Therefore, if the Absolute Truth is one, about which we think there is no difference of opinion, the *Guru* also cannot be two. The *Ācāryadeva* to whom we offer our humble homage, is not the *Guru* of a sectarian institution or one out of many differing exponents of the truth. On the contrary He is the *Jagatguru* or the *Guru* of all of us; the only difference is that some obey Him wholeheartedly, while others do not obey Him directly.

In the *Bhāgavatam* it is said:

*ācāryaṁ māṁ vijānīyān nāvamanyeta karhicit*  
*na martya-buddhyāsūyeta sarva-deva-mayo guruḥ*

**‘One should understand the Spiritual Master to be as good as I am,’ said the Blessed Lord, ‘Nobody should be jealous of the Spiritual Master or think of Him as an ordinary man because the Spiritual Master is the sum total of all demigods.’ \[*****SB*** **11.17.27\]**

That is, the *Ācārya* has been identified with Godhead Himself, He has nothing to do with the affairs of this mundane world. He does not descend here to meddle with the affairs of temporary necessities but to deliver the fallen and conditioned souls. He appears before us to reveal the light of the *Vedas* and to bestow upon us the blessings of full-fledged freedom after which we should hanker at every step of our life’s journey.”

“The transcendental knowledge of the *Vedas* was first uttered by Godhead to Brahmā, the Creator of this particular universe. From Brahmā the Knowledge descended to Nārada from Nārada to Vyāsadeva, from Vyāsadeva to Madhva and in this process of disciplic succession the transcendental knowledge was being transmitted by one disciple to another till it reached Lord Gaurāṅga or Śrī Kṛṣṇa-Caitanya Who posed as the Disciple and Successor of Śrī Īśvara Puri. The present *Ācāryadeva* is the tenth disciplic representative from Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī, the original representative of this transcendental tradition in its fullness from Lord Caitanya. The knowledge that we receive from Him is not different from that imparted by Godhead Himself and the succession of the *Ācāryas* in the preceptorial line of Brahmā. We adore this auspicious day as Śrī Vyāsa-pūjā Tithi because the *Ācārya* is the living representative of Vyāsadeva, the Divine compiler of the *Vedas,* the *Purāṇas, Gītā, Mahābharata* and the *Bhāgavatam.*

Our knowledge is so poor, our senses are so imperfect and our sources are so limited that it is not possible for us to have even the slightest knowledge of the absolute region without surrendering ourselves at the Lotus Feet of Śrī Vyāsadeva or His bonafide representative. We cannot know anything of the transcendental region by our limited perverted method of observation and experiment. But all of us can lend our eager ears for the aural reception of the transcendental sound transmitted from that region to this through the unadulterated medium of Śrī *Gurudeva* or Śrī Vyāsadeva. Therefore gentlemen, we should surrender ourselves today to the Feet of the representative of Śrī Vyāsadeva for the elimination of all our differences bred by our unsubmissive attitude, It is accordingly said in *Śrī Gītā:*

*tad viddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā*  
*upadekṣyanti te jñānaṁ jñāninas tattva-darśinaḥ*

**‘Just approach the wise and bonafide Spiritual Master. Surrender unto him first and try to understand him by enquiries and service. Such a wise Spiritual Master will enlighten you with transcendental knowledge because he has already known the Absolute Truth.’ (*****Bg*****. 4.34)**

To receive the transcendental knowledge we must completely surrender ourselves to the real *Ācārya* in a spirit of ardent enquiry and service. The actual performance of the service of the Absolute under the guidance of the *Ācārya* is the only vehicle by which we can assimilate the transcendental knowledge. The offering of our humble services and homage to the Feet of the *Acaryadeva* will enable us to be favoured with the capacity of assimilating the transcendental knowledge so kindly transmitted by Him to all persons without distinction.

The Divine message from his holy lips is the congenial thing for the suffering humanity. All of us should hear him patiently. If we listen to the Transcendental Sound without unnecessary opposition, he will surely have mercy upon us. The *Ācārya’s* message is to take us back to our original home and back to God. Therefore, we should hear Him patiently, follow Him in the measure of our conviction and bow down at His Lotus Feet for releasing us of our present causeless unwillingness for serving the Absolute and all souls.

Had he not appeared before us to deliver us from the thraldom of this gross worldly delusion—surely we should have remained for lives and ages in the darkness of helpless captivity. Had he not appeared before us, we would not have been able to understand the eternal truth of the sublime teaching of Lord Caitanya,—had He not appeared before us, we could not have been able to know the significance of the first sloka of *Brahma-saṁhitā:*

*īśvaraḥ paramaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sac-cid-ānanda-vigrahaḥ*  
*anādir ādir govindaḥ sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam*

**‘Kṛṣṇa who is known as Govinda is the Supreme Godhead, He has an eternal blissful spiritual body. He is the Origin of all. He has no other origin and He is the Prime Cause of all causes.’” \[Bs. 5.1\]**

—Abhay Charan Dās  
(A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda)

## Offerings from ISKCON Temples Around the World

## Amherst

Our dearmost Śrīla Prabhupāda, it is only by the dint of your guiding benediction and soul quenching grace that we have been allowed to sip from the pure ocean—our Lord Kṛṣṇa’s causeless mercy. Even though we taste but the tiniest rivulets of realization and devotion it is such a relief from the pangs of material miseries. By following your example as servant to the Supreme Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa, we are slowly but surely learning to control our mind and senses and to be true friends to everyone, by giving them this simple program of chanting, dancing and feasting. Śrīla Prabhupāda, you are Kṛṣṇa’s messenger so kindly paying this earthly kingdom a visit. Our only duty is to give you the warmest possible reception by helping you in your mission of spreading the rays of Lord Caitanya’s benediction moon.

This Amherst *āśrama* is surrounded by a five-college area with about 45,000 students. We’ve been here three and a half months engaging students and townspeople in the joyous process of glorifying the Supreme Enjoyer through dancing, feasting, chanting, and preparation for the real graduation—eternal, blissful life with Kṛṣṇa. What nectar! Everyone is thirsty for the absolutely pure association that you, father Śrīla Prabhupāda, are revealing to your blinded children.

There was an average attendance of forty spirit souls at a Kṛṣṇa class held at the University of Massachusetts. Already there are five new devotees, who are daily distributing books and *prasādam* on campus and at the Om Tat Sat bookstore in Amherst. The semester was highlighted by a visit from the “First Transcendental Exposition” who presented their transcendental rock opera to hundreds of devotees and students. By this modern media many students were enchanted with Kṛṣṇa consciousness and impressed with the practicality of *īśāvāsya.* Even other spiritual movements are attracted to Kṛṣṇa. We were recently invited to a *kuṇḍalinī-yoga* restaurant and everybody engaged in a most ecstatic *kīrtana,* mercy!

This summer will find us singing, dancing, chanting and preaching in the New England seaside resorts, attempting to calm some of the agitating whirlpools of material entanglements with that truly “pacific ocean” of transcendental devotion which you have blessed us with. We are confident that these directions from your heart, dearmost Prabhupāda, will lead many imprisoned souls from the consuming flame into the illuminating light of Godhead.

Throughout our childhood and into our youthful days we watched the world around us. In the schools we heard the instructions of our teachers; how artfully they skirted our questions concerning ourselves and the nature of life around us. “A tale told by an idiot … signifying nothing” echoed from the podiums, announcing the philosophy of the time upon our straining ears, left us baffled and angry, aimless in our wandering. Braving the lowest depths of Māyā’s prison you, with the light of your radiant knowledge, pierced the darkness and we followed you through the maze-like corridors into the sunlight. The fever of *Māyā’s* bite maddened us, but with your cooling strokes of transcendental sound you pacified our lust-crazed hearts, nursing us back to health. With fathomless patience you led us to the bank and let us drink of the ocean of Caitanya’s mercy. Still we cannot see the greatness of your lotus-like heart, nor can we understand your causeless mercy.

You have found us wandering among the crows, picking through the garbage, the chewed remnants of the stagnant material world. You are the *Paramahaṁsa,* the topmost swan-like devotee of the Lord, teaching the art of picking nectar, even from the refuse. Only by your association can we learn this art, only by your mercy can we make any advancement in spiritual life, only by your association can we fulfill the mission of life.

In the Fourth Chapter, 34th verse of the *Bhagavad-gītā,* Lord Kṛṣṇa tells Arjuna that in order to learn the truth, one must approach such a bonafide Spiritual Master, making relevant inquiries with submission and service. Only such a self-realized, Kṛṣṇa conscious Spiritual Master can answer all questions perfectly because by his own deliberation and arguments he himself has realized the conclusion of all revealed scriptures, and has therefore seen the truth. Because he is in the disciplic succession from time immemorial, beginning with Lord Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Truth Himself, he is the direct representative of the Personality of Godhead and the Supersoul. Therefore by serving such a bonafide Spiritual Master, one can serve the Lord directly thus opening the path to the Vaikuṇṭha world.

Lord Kṛṣṇa confirms in the *Bhagavad-gītā,* in the 18th chapter:

**One can understand the Supreme Personality as He is only by devotional service. And when one is in full consciousness of the Supreme Lord by such devotion, he can enter into the Kingdom of God. (Bg. 18.55)**

Some persons wrongly conclude that by dint of their mental power, meditational prowess, or by the study of the Vedic literature that Kṛṣṇa becomes accessible. But this is definitely denied by the Lord Himself in the *Bhagavad-gītā.* He says there:

**The form which you are seeing with your transcendental eyes cannot be understood simply by studying the Vedas, nor by undergoing serious penances, nor by charity nor by worship. No one can see me as I am. Only by undivided devotional service can I be understood directly. Only in this way can you enter into the mysteries of My understanding. (Bg. 11.53-54)**

Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī explains that “First class devotional service is known by one’s tendency to be fully engaged in Kṛṣṇa consciousness, serving the Lord favorably,” In other words devotional service means that one performs activities which are pleasing to the transcendental senses of the Lord. Obviously, if a person desires to become the servant of someone, his first business is to find out what the master wants, to find out what pleases him. It is not that one does just anything which happens to come to his mind, completely ignoring the desires of the master. That is an absurd proposal. Similarly, devotional service to the Lord must be executed in knowledge of what Kṛṣṇa wants. Then it can be accepted as devotional service.

In conditional life, deluded by the three modes of material nature, It is impossible to know the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Who is completely transcendental to all this material world. Nor could we discover what He wants hidden as He is by the curtain of *māyā* (Bg. 7.25). From our situation it is impossible to estimate His position. And that is why Kṛṣṇa has given the instruction for our deliverance, which we found in the 4th chapter of *Bhagavad-gītā.* “You just approach a Spiritual Master …” Kṛṣṇa says that in order to learn the truth, in order to come to the platform of devotional service, we must contact such a surrendered soul, a soul already fixed up in devotional service to the Lord. Such a great soul is 100% engaged in devotional service, and by sincere and steadfast execution of his orders we can also attain this platform. Without following the directions of the Spiritual Master we will surely fail, we will simply waste our valuable time.

This is confirmed in the Caitanya-caritāmṛta,where it is said:

**“By the mercy of the Spiritual Master who is a pure devotee and by the mercy of Kṛṣṇa one can achieve the platform of devotional service, there is no other way. (*****Madhya*****19.151)**

And again:

**“By the mercy of the Spiritual Master one is benedicted by the mercy of Kṛṣṇa. Without the grace of the Spiritual Master no one can make any advancement…”**

So it is confirmed here, and supported in numerous other places in the revealed scriptures, that acceptance of the shelter of the bonafide spiritual master is the crucial point in spiritual life. Without this connection with the spiritual master there is no access to devotional service to the Lord, and therefore no connection with Kṛṣṇa can be made.

From the descriptions in the revealed scriptures, from the statements of the great devotees and *ācāryas* of the past, and from our own experience, Śrīla Prabhupāda, you are that great soul. You are the bonafide Spiritual Master, the direct representative of the Supreme Personality of Godhead. The truth which has baffled all our attempts to discover, you have seen. You have smashed the false knowledge of the mental speculators and are delivering the entire earth from the clutches of the heartless vacuum of the impersonalists. You are the mercy of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, delivering Kṛṣṇa freely to whomever will take. Leaving aside all material considerations you have completely surrendered unto the Lotus Feet of Lord Kṛṣṇa, and are therefore the topmost cream of all great personalities. By your pleasure alone we can be delivered, and by your displeasure no one can be saved. Our success or failure depends entirely on your satisfaction. Therefore we are praying to Lord Kṛṣṇa that by His mercy we may surrender ourselves at your lotus feet. Please accept us as your most menial servants,

My Guru Mahārāja has not asked me to hesitate in any way in fulfilling Lord Kṛṣṇa’s desire. Rather, He has said of Arjuna: “Although he could understand that his duty to fight was awaiting him, still, on account of miserly weakness, he could not discharge the duty.” (*Bhagavad-gītā* 2.7 purport)

## New York

We offer our respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of our spiritual master, who is teaching us the central point of life.

On the day of *Vyāsa-pūjā,* it is our duty and pleasure to glorify our spiritual master, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda. As Śrīla Prabhupāda stated in a lecture some years ago, it is the duty of the disciple to offer respectful obeisances unto his spiritual master, and in doing so, the specific qualifications of the spiritual master should be mentioned. Śrīla Prabhupāda has said, “He (the *ācārya*) has nothing to do with the affairs of this mundane world. He does not descend here to meddle with the affairs of temporary necessities but to deliver the fallen and conditioned souls …”

Therefore, on this day we wish to glorify our divine spiritual master in terms of his factual qualifications. Once in Boston there was a *yoga* student who was attracted to Kṛṣṇa consciousness and was attending Śrīla Prabhupāda’s lectures. He once asked His Divine Grace, “How can I leave my *guru* and take initiation from you?” Śrīla Prabhupāda replied, “That I cannot say. We don’t want sentiment. (If today you come on sentiment, then tomorrow you will change to something else on the same basis). Just try to understand this philosophy.” Therefore we do not wish to present sentimental propositions to be accepted or rejected by the flickering mind. We wish to present factual statements which can be intelligently accepted after careful and serious deliberation. This is the Vedic process. We therefore wish to present the following unshakable facts, which we are prepared to defend against any and all challengers.

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda is the bona fide representative of Lord Kṛṣṇa coming in the disciplic chain. As stated in *Bhagavad-gītā,* if one wants to know the transcendental reality, he must surrender unto a bona fide spiritual master. (*Bg.* 4.34) The bona fide spiritual master is the representative of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, as stated by the Lord Himself (*Bhāg.* 11.17.22). Such a spiritual master appears in the chain of disciplic succession, or *paramparā,* which is explained by Lord Kṛṣṇa in the Fourth Chapter of *Bhagavad-gītā.* The original spiritual master in our line is Lord Brahmā, who was instructed by the Supreme Lord Himself (*tene* b*rahma hṛda ya ādi-kavaye*). Lord Brahmā instructed Nārada Muni, Nārada instructed Vyāsadeva, the author of the Vedic literature, and Vyāsadeva instructed Madhvācārya. This line continues in contemporary times, and just to reaffirm its authenticity, the Personality of Godhead again appeared in this same line of authority as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu. From Lord Caitanya this line continues unbroken through the Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana, Śrīla Bhaktivinode Ṭhākura and Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura.

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda is the bona fide representative of Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Gosvāmī because he not only received initiation from him but is factually carrying out his words of instruction. In 1937 Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Gosvāmī instructed Śrīla Prabhupāda to preach the philosophy of Kṛṣṇa consciousness in the English language. That Śrīla Prabhupāda has done this—despite all risks, difficulties and personal inconveniences—is an indisputable fact, Therefore, because he has wholeheartedly executed the order of his spiritual master, Śrīla Prabhupāda is his bona fide servant and factual representative. Thus he is the bona fide representative of the Lord in the disciplic chain.

Śrīla Prabhupāda is preaching in its pure form the message of Lord Caitanya, which is the only hope for the conditioned souls of the Kali-yuga for realizing the goal of life. Śrīla Prabhupāda’s preaching program is based on four principles: (1) chanting, (2) dancing, (3) distribution of Kṛṣṇa-*prasādam*, and (4) presentation of the philosophical ideas of *Bhagavad-gītā and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* as they are. As stated by Narottama dāsa Thākura, this is the method of Lord Caitanya. Śrīla Prabhupāda has never added or subtracted anything to this method, but has always spread this process as it is, by precept and personal example. Nor has Śrīla Prabhupāda ever sanctioned unauthorized additions or subtractions by anyone else. Although always presenting Kṛṣṇa consciousness in a manner appropriate to time and place, Śrīla Prabhupāda has never compromised the purity of the *saṅkirtana* movement. *Harer nāma, harer nāma, harer nāmaiva kevalam/ kalau nāsty eva nāsty eva nāsty eva, gatir anyathā.* This is the only medicine, and Śrīla Prabhupāda is the onlyone who has brought it for us. Therefore he is our only real benefactor.

Śrīla Prabhupāda is delivering the Western countries from voidism and impersonalism and teaching us the ultimate aim of life. The ultimate goal of life is Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme personality of Godhead (*vasudeva-parā gatiḥ*). Without Kṛṣṇa consciousness, the living entities simply drink the poisonous results of mental speculation and sense gratification, as confirmed by Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura. Thus they are forced to adopt obnoxious practices like meat-eating, gambling, intoxication and illicit sex and glide down to animal life. This poison is being accepted as nectar by the foolish living entities of this age, and they are specifically being encouraged by the so-called political, religious, educational and social leaders. Thus they are committing spiritual suicide by forgetting the purpose of life. And the philosophical rationalizations for such actions are the speculations of impersonalism and voidism. Those who seek escape from the material clutches of sense gratification are ensnared by these philosophies, and thus their mission in life is also defeated.

By teaching *Bhagavata-dharma*, the science of pure devotional service to the Supreme Personality of Godhead, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda is freeing the misguided conditioned souls of this age from the gross and subtle clutches of illusion and bringing them to surrender at the lotus feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the father of all living entities. While the scientists and politicians are propagating sense enjoyment through materialistic advancement, the philosophers are teaching hopeless and impractical ideas of spiritual self-annihilation, and religionists are preaching sectarian dogmatism but at the same time encouraging all forms of sinful life, Śrīla Prabhupāda is giving the eternal life of devotional service unto the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord.

Although we, the residents of the Western world, are among the most degraded members of human society, being addicted to all sinful activities, His Divine Grace is elevating us to the platform of pure Kṛṣṇa consciousness by the transcendental process authorized by the six Gosvāmīs. Even the most degraded can be purified by taking shelter of his lotus feet (*Bhāg*. 2.4.18).

We are drowning ourselves in the poison of the age of Kali, but His Divine Grace has saved our lives and caused us to drink from the ocean of *bhakti-rasāmṛta*. We therefore offer our obeisances unto his lotus feet thousands of times.

Please accept our most humble obeisances at Your Divine Lotus Feet. Here in Trinidad distribution of Your Divine Grace’s books has become the reservoir of all bliss.

There are over four hundred thousand Indians here, most of whom descend from bonded servants brought from India 100 years ago.

The remnants of their religious traditions are professional *Bhāgavatam* and *Rāmāyaṇa* readings—purely social events given by business men who are part-time *paṇḍits,* their qualification being that they were born in a *brāhmaṇa* family, and *pūjās*, where a *paṇḍit* visits a home and says prayers to Vivasvān, Śiva, Gaṇeśa, Durgā, or some other demi-god, for health, education, prosperity, or some other reason.

At such occasions food is usually prepared. A little is thrown into the fire and then they call the food *prasādam.*

Sometimes at such occasions the old men put on *dhotis* and people call them *sādhus.*

Sometimes the people abstain from flesh, sex and intoxication. When the *“paṇḍitas”* come to say prayers they abstain from these things for two weeks so that they become more qualified to receive their desired benediction from the respective demigod.

Almost all the several hundred temples here are Śivite and due to lack of care are falling apart.
