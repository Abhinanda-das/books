# Śrī Vyāsa-pūjā 1974

## Vyāsa-Pūjā homages from the G.B.C.

## Introduction

It is stated in the *Padma Purāṇa* that of all types of worship, the worship of the Supreme Person is the highest, but higher still is the worship of the Lord’s devotees.

Kṛṣṇa so favors His pure, unalloyed devotee that He is more pleased to see honor and respect shown His devotee than to receive such glorification Himself, just as the father is more appreciative of a compliment to his favorite son than to himself.

This Vyāsa-Pūjā is such an offering of worship to the lotus feet of a pure devotee of Kṛṣṇa, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda, on the most auspicious anniversary of his appearance in this world.

In order to offer our meager thanks for the inconceivable and unlimited mercy bestowed upon us by our eternal spiritual father, all of Śrīla Prabhupāda’s disciples have compiled these offerings of glorification to His Divine Grace. It is understood that no amount of praise would be sufficient to properly honor such a pure devotee of Kṛṣṇa, but the following humble attempt has been made and is now offered for his satisfaction. We offer our unworthy obeisances at the lotus feet of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda and pray that he will remain with us many, many more years, because we are so weak and insignificant and are maintained by his mercy alone.

## Bali Mardan dāsa Gosvāmī

To the True Mahātmā:

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda

*nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya kṛṣṇa-preṣṭhāya bhū-tale*  
*śrīmate bhaktivedānta-svāmin iti nāmine*  
*namas te sārasvate deve gaura-vāṇī-pracāriṇe*  
*nirviśeṣa-śūnyavādi-pāścātya-deśa-tāriṇe*

All glories to you, Śrīla Prabhupāda, the true emissary of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Mahāprabhu declared:

*pṛthivīte āche yata nagarādi grāma*  
*sarvatra pracāra haibe mora nāma*  
*\[CB Antya-khaṇḍa 4.126\]*

**“In every town and village the chanting of My name will be heard.”**

It is you who has fulfilled these words of the Supreme Lord.

The great saint Ṭhākura Bhaktivinoda predicted that soon a pure soul would come to benedict the world with Kṛṣṇa consciousness, and verily you are that pure soul, come to deliver us from the bondage of material existence. Constantly you preach to us the message of *Bhagavad-gītā* and describe endlessly the glory of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, thus revealing the meaning of the true *mahātmā*. Kṛṣṇa describes in *Bhagavad-gītā:*

*satataṁ kīrtayanto māṁ*  
*yatantaś ca dṛḍha-vratāḥ*  
*namasyantaś ca māṁ bhaktyā*  
*nitya-yuktā upāsate*

**“Always chanting My glories, endeavoring with great determination, bowing down before Me, these great souls perpetually worship Me with devotion.” (*****Bg.*** **9.14)**

Many are called *guru*, but you actually have the qualification of *guru* and *mahātmā*, as defined in the authoritative scriptures. One who accepts you as his spiritual master attains the perfection of life.

My only claim to your grace is that I am a most fallen soul, a wretched sinner entrapped within the bonds of *tamas* (ignorance) and *rajas* (passion). You are *jagad-guru*, a liberated soul who can save the whole universe. Prostrated, I wait for your causeless mercy to descend.

Bali Mardan dāsa Gosvāmī  
GBC—ISKCON New York  
Trustee, Bhaktivedanta Book Trust

## Brahmānanda dāsa Gosvāmī

Śrīla Prabhupāda, you have written as follows: “No one can give rapt attention who is not pure in mind. No one can be pure in mind who is not pure in action. No one can be pure in action who is not pure in eating, sleeping, mating and fearing.” On this Vyāsa-pūjā day I offer my woefully inadequate appreciation to you, my spiritual master, who is guiding me back to home, back to Godhead. You are so expert a teacher that even such an incompetent as myself can make clear cut advancement. By giving me the practices and routine of spiritual life, you have made me strong in Kṛṣṇa consciousness. The Lord says in the *Bhagavad-gītā* as follows: “My dear Arjuna, winner of wealth, if you cannot fix your mind upon Me without deviation, then follow the regulated principles of *bhakti-yoga.* In this way you will develop a desire to attain to Me.”

Śrīla Prabhupāda, you have made me a GBC secretary, and my duty is to see that in all temples the rules and regulations of *bhakti-yoga* are being carefully followed. You have also made me a *sannyāsī,* which means that I must travel and preach as widely as possible. Actually a preacher of Kṛṣṇa consciousness must preach by his life, not just his speeches. He himself must be perfect, because he is trying to prove that there is God in an age of people who relegate the Lord as an imagination or dead or something impersonal. If I behave badly even in the slightest, people will only be encouraged to doubt or even scoff Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Lord Caitanya taught: *“āpani ācari' bhakti karila pracāra,”* in this connection. (*Cc. Ādi* 4.41)

I have decided to give my life to preaching Kṛṣṇa consciousness here in Africa. I came here alone by your order, and now by your mercy it is developing. We are preaching to the different tribes, visiting their places, and also starting our own self-sufficient community. These people are not hard-boiled like the sophisticated citizens of America. They are soft and have a feeling for God. Actually I feel that they are purifying me, not that I have come to purify them. We stay with them in their cottages made of earth and sticks and chant Hare Kṛṣṇa and preach at six hour long meetings, and then we take *prasādam* of maize, roots, and vegetables. We are just completing our own cottage set on a hilltop overlooking the rolling hills and valleys and the lands and rivers of Kenya. Now we have started the planting. Here the land is so rich that practically everything can be grown. As people start joining us we will build larger buildings and a temple out of stone. We want to develop the African life so that they can get necessities without so much toil and be happy in Kṛṣṇa consciousness.

The people of Africa are very much attracted to Lord Caitanya’s movement. Wherever we go, by seeing them chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa and dancing in spiritual trance, my eyes have been opened to the new day that is coming, a bright future for the world filled with happiness and abundance all because of this great world wide spiritual movement. You began it all back in 1966 in New York’s East Side in such a humble and charming way. How fortunate I am to have been a part of it right from the very beginning. I think my life was meant just to serve You. Please, Prabhupāda, let me remain with You always, and let me somehow or other represent You, to speak what You speak and to act as You do. Let me fall down straight with straw in my mouth and offer You my life birth after birth. I shall be eternally happy in this way.

Brahmānanda Swami  
GBC—Africa

## Karandhara dāsa Adhikārī

I offer my worthless obeisances unto the lotus feet of my spiritual master. Such a wretched, miserable fool am I that I cannot appreciate the significance of my spiritual master and his priceless gift; knowledge of and devotion to the Supreme Absolute Truth Lord Kṛṣṇa. For the most part, I am lost in the ocean of material desires and illusions. Lust and the hankering for adoration and glory cling to my rascal mind and distract me from concentrating on following my spiritual master’s instructions. The countless offenses I have committed at his lotus feet condemn me to the perdition of ingratitude and rebelliousness to his mercy. There is absolutely no good to be found in a scoundrel like myself. Who can comprehend, then, the mercy of Śrīla Prabhupāda? Who can measure his kindness and wisdom? He engages *māyā’s* slaves in *bhakti-yoga* and enlightens the fools of this dark world with the bright sun of *Bhagavān-vidyā.* He is not to be understood by the wrangling mind or self-interested intelligence. Only by chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa on the beads, a minimum of sixteen rounds daily, vibrating softly the *gāyatrī mantra,* bowing down in humility to the *vaiṣṇavas, Tulasī devī* and the Deity, and participating in the spreading of the benediction moon, *Hari-nāma-saṅkīrtana,* to all the suffering, conditioned souls in any way and every way possible, can we marvel in the ecstasy of Śrīla Prabhupāda’s inconceivable level of Kṛṣṇa consciousness.

Certainly volumes should be recorded on the glories of our spiritual master. Descriptions both scientific and esoteric in his praise could be recited. But, ignorant and sinful as I am, Śrīmatī Sarasvatī does not reward me with clear, fixed-up knowledge and remembrance, or poetic sentiment for his position. So I remain a self-preoccupied blubbering idiot, trying to eke out some sincere expression of appreciation of my spiritual master, and failing unforgivingly in doing so.

Karandhara dās adhikārī  
GBC - India

## Bhagavān dāsa adhikārī

I offer my respectful obeisances,  
To my beloved spiritual master Śrīla Prabhupāda,  
Who is especially blessed by Lord Kṛṣṇa  
To show us the beauty of surrender to the Supreme Lord.

To me surrender was something  
Meant only for the defeated and foolish;  
A pitiful submission to physical, mental or intellectual  
Slavery, for those who had no will or creativity.

But by your mercy you showed us how to surrender  
Envy, lust, pride and selfishness;  
In return for a new birth of self  
And a life of superexcellence as one of Kṛṣṇa’s sons.

Being in your presence—so surrendered yet dignified,  
Brought back a lost desire to serve God,  
Which despite the taste left by former religious folly,  
Ever so subtly pushed me closer.

Then our first challenges came,  
From father, brother, friends, priest and outraged mother;  
Who with defiance pulled together  
At the tender creeper of devotion,  
Testing the depth of my conviction and sanity,  
And offering again a path I knew could not tolerate compromise.

But somehow from chanting,  
Came words filled with undeniable knowledge,  
Knowledge I had heard you sing, knowledge that had come  
Through the milleniums, from the original person.

It could not be covered by empty, foolish speculation,  
And at that point I saw them all fall away,  
As birds from a limb  
When they are frightened by an unexpected noise.

Soon however, I realized that the comfort of victory  
Was far from secured; a new combat began,  
Against the urges of the senses, against sleep  
And envious feelings for those who served better than me.  
Against material love, fame and power,  
Against unnecessary comfort and renouncing duty.

How many times have I felt the razor’s edge,  
Of choice between truth and illusion,  
And you were always there to save, encourage and forgive.  
By your mercy only ordeal turned into blessing.

Still I see far, far into the distance,  
A purified image of myself,  
And I pray you will watch for me, for I do not know  
How many lifetimes in front of me that image is.

O my Lord and master, Śrī Gurudeva,  
When will the time come, when your word and instruction  
Will be received by me as the sweetest nectar, and that  
Ocean of bliss will rush in to fill my dry heart.

Please give me the strength and determination,  
To always remember I am just a servant, to remember  
The goal of all this work, to make my life  
Non-different from what I speak.

Let me continue to serve you when you are not here,  
Reciting the glories of your mercy, your blessings,  
Which are overflowing, sweeping away the filth of Kali-yuga.  
All glories to you, my father and master.

Your bliss reveals the repulsive face of  
Material happiness. Your glory embarrasses the sun,  
Moon and lotus. Please hear me, as I stand  
Calling for your shelter.

Bhagavān dās adhikārī,  
GBC Mediterranean

*Our dear Śrīla Prabhupāda,*

All glories to your effulgent divine grace! You have come with the glorious message of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, at the request of Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura Prabhupāda, in order to deliver our wretched selves from this dark world, deep in the grip of impersonalism and voidism. Because you are absorbed in love for Govinda, you emanate ecstatic *prema*, “*prema bhakti yāhā hoi-te*,” and you purify anyone and everyone that comes under your shelter. You rid us of the ignorance that covers us, *“avidyā vināśa yāte.”* The *Bhagavad-gītā* gives us the system for receiving bonafide transcendental knowledge—“*evaṁ paramparā prāptam imaṁ rājarṣayo viduḥ”* (Bg. 4.2), and you are coming in the powerful *Brahmā-Gauḍīya-Vaiṣṇava Sampradāya* disciplic line. ## Dallas Gurukula

We are asked to glorify our spiritual master with a few words for this Vyāsa Pūjā book. Actually anyone with only a little knowledge of ISKCON, Śrīla Prabhupāda or Kṛṣṇa can understand the difficulty of such an endeavor. One cannot speak just a few words in praise of the spiritual master. *Dhyāyan stuvaṁs tasya yaśas tri-sandhyaṁ*; one should glorify the spiritual master three times daily. There are countless praises to be spoken.

The first thing Sanātana Goswami inquired of his spiritual master, Lord Caitanya, was *ātmatattva jijñāsā.*That means he wanted to know the meaning of spiritual life.

He wanted to know how to become free of material entanglement. In the material world, everyone is thinking “*īvaro ‘ham ahaṁ bhogī*,” I am God. Therefore, *karmātmakam* or *puṇyakārya*is the standard; fruitive work or pious activities.

However the sincere souls seek an answer to the real question of life, *brahma jijñāsā*. One must seek out the association of liberated souls and take shelter of a spiritual master (*praṇipātena*). One must take initiation and try to understand spiritual life. Now everyone has the opportunity to surrender to Śrīla Prabhupāda.

Of course one may not be entirely sure what this means. However, the Vedas explain it, so let us try to understand from the Vedic point of view. Prabhupāda can be understood in this way; *pra*means great of fore. Kṛṣṇa says He is *prabhu*, *prabhavaḥ*and *pralayaḥ*– the master, the creation and dissolution. Therefore we can understand that the syllable *pra*indicates the foremost, because Kṛṣṇa is called *īśvara-parama*, and it is said, “*sarva-loka-maheśvaram*,” meaning He is the foremost master. *Prabhavaḥ*means the Kṛṣṇa is the very point of creation. He is called *ādi-puruṣa*(*janmādy asya yataḥ*). That means He is one without a beginning or *anādirādiḥ*. And again He is called *pralayaḥ*or the ultimate dissolution. He is *nidhānam*and *āśraya*or the final resting place of everything. So, because Kṛṣṇa is everything by definition (*vāsudeva sarvam iti*), it is relevant to show what Prabhupāda is in relation to Him.

*Bhū*means the living entity. *Bhū*means being and *bhūta*also means entity. *Ātmavat sarva-bhūteṣu yaḥ paśyati sa paṇḍitaḥ*means that one who sees every entity as soul is really learned. So *yas tu sarvāni bhūtāny ātmany eva nu paśyati*, one may try to see all entities as part and parcel of the Supreme Lord. Therefore in that regard, it is to be understood that the spiritual master is not only a living entity or spirit soul, but a highly elevated *prabhu*or god. Because he is in direct contact with, and because he is loved by Kṛṣṇa (*priya eva tasysa*), he is treated just like Kṛṣṇa (*sākṣād-dhari*).

Lastly the word *pāda*emphasizes that our spiritual master is situated at the feet of Lord Kṛṣṇa always, and that there are many other highly qualified masters situated at the feet of Prabhupāda. So in terms of verbal glorification we may simply try to define Prabhupāda. *Dear Śrīla Prabhupāda,*

The human form of life, obtain after over eight million births in various species, is an immensely valuable opportunity. The body covers our remembrance of past sufferings, but now we are beginning to perceive and understand our precarious situation.

Eternity and bliss are our birthright. Home is a place built of dazzling touchstone. Trees there are called “*kalpa-vṛkṣa*,” or purpose trees, that fulfill all desires, and fields are white with sweet milk that vanquishes all hunger and thirst. In this abode lives our dearmost friend Whose matchless beauty is the subject of Vedic songs; and Whose uncountable glories are always decorating the tongues of the topmost liberated souls.

A long time has assed since we’ve seen His moonlike face or heard His sweet gentle voice. We’ve grown accustomed to the coarse dealings of this world and forgotten our most intimate relationship. Countless days and nights, anger, lust, pain and terror have left us raw and insensitive, eagerly accepting the flickering sensations of blood, mucus and stool in exchange for spiritual communion.We can only mock at Him, deny His existence and scorn His spiritual beauty. Mental concoction and word jugglery have become our signposts for wandering since the dawn of creation.

*oṁ ajñāna-timirāndhasya*  
*jñānāñjana-śalākayā*  
*cakṣur unmīlitaṁ yena*  
*tasmai śrī-gurave namaḥ*

*Like children we stand helpless,*  
*on the shore of material life.*  
*The dense fog of ignorance*  
*hangs like night, and the sea,*  
*its incessant roar and pounding waves,*  
*rings in our ears*

*brahmāṇḍa bhramite kona bhāgyavān jīva*  
*guru-kṛṣṇa-prasāde pāya bhakti-latā-bīja*

We have looked everywhere in Brahmā’s creation, and now we have found the beginning of perfection. Just a moment’s association with Your Divine Grace can deliver us.

*sākṣād-dharitvena samasta-śāstrair*  
*uktas tathā bhāvyata eva sadbhiḥ*  
*kintu prabhor yaḥ priya eva tasya*  
*vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam*

According to revealed scripture, all bona fide *sādhus*and *ācāryas*, you are to be respected and worshiped on the level of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Kṛṣṇa. You are as good as Kṛṣṇa because you alone can deliver Him to the fallen souls. Only by the priceless fortune of your association can anyone in this material world make even the slightest advancement.

*naṣṭa-prāyeṣv abhadreṣu*  
*nityaṁ bhāgavata-sevayā*  
*bhagavaty uttama-śloke*  
*bhaktir bhavati naiṣṭhikī*

You are as good as the Personality of Godhead because you and your association in transcendental service means “gradual promotion on the path of devotion.” And the path of devotion culminates in *kṛṣṇa-prem*, the perfect stage of existence.

Who can properly describe the auspiciousness of your presence here? How is it possible to understand you and your unfathomable mercy? Now, after only a few years’ time, you have raised an army of purified souls from the filth and degradation of Kali-yuga. Following in the footsteps of Caitanya Mahāprabhu you have unleashed a flood of love of Kṛṣṇa, which now threatens to drown the whole world. You are actually the devotee. We can only run behind you begging for your shelter.

*guro-prasādo balavān na tasmād ballavataram*

**“The causeless mercy of the spiritual master is the most powerful; there is nothing stronger that that** ***guru-prasāda*****.”**

## Gainesville

“Prabhupāda;” one who has taken the position of the Lord. Such a great soul is very rare, and if by chance one gets the opportunity to associate with such an exalted personality, he should take it as the ultimate boon of life.

Many times people ask the devotees about their statements the Kṛṣṇa consciousness is for the “intelligent” class of men. Conditioned by the impersonal disease that everything is one and the same, foolish critics try to eliminate these natural distinctions amongst the various species, and put forward the mistaken theory that all paths lead to the same goal; that it does not matter what you do, because everything is illusion anyway. They therefore foolishly criticize the Kṛṣṇa consciousness movement for recognizing the individuality of all living entities, and they say the devotees discriminate by limiting Kṛṣṇa consciousness to the “intelligentsia” only.

In examining this argument, one quickly comes to the conclusion that this belief is simply a product of their mundane conditioning only. By “intelligent,” we do not mean the ability to do well on material I.Q. tests, or make a lot of money, or speak several languages. No, to the contrary, an intelligent man is one who can actually understand that he is suffering, and that the cause of his misery is lack of spiritual knowledge. The perfect example are the two Goswamis, Rūpa and Sanātana. Although they had all facility for material happiness—money, fame, power and knowledge—they were still not satisfied. Why? Because they did not know the purpose of life! They did not even know who they were!

Sanātana Goswami prayed to Lord Caitanya in the following way: “I am born of a lower family, my associations are all abominable, and I am fallen, the most wretched of mankind. I was suffering in the dark well of material enjoyment, and I never knew the actual goal of life. I do not know what is beneficial for me. Although in the mundane sphere, I am what is known as a learned man, I am in fact so much of a fool that I accept that I am learned. You have accepted me as your servant, and You have delivered me from the entanglement of material life. Now You can tell me what my duty is in this liberated state of life.”

From this plea, we can learn a great deal about the nature of the real guru and disciple. Although Sanātana Goswami was such a great personality, still, in the presence of his spiritual master he considered himself a great fool. It is therefore stated that one who remains a fool in the presence of his spiritual master is a real guru, while one who says he is so advanced that he can speak better than his *guru*is a rascal.

Śrīla Prabhupāda, we see your following in the footsteps of Sanātana Goswami as your real qualification. In spite of so much success, in spite of so much honor and respect, you have never personally taken credit for your accomplishments, but have always placed them at the lotus feet of your *guru-mahārāja*for his satisfaction. That is the real *guru*! *Guru*is not one who thinks that “Because my disciples are offering me respect as God (the *guru*is considered as good as God on account of his being the confidential servitor of the Lord), therefore I have become God.” A real *guru*will never speak such nonsense.

The real *guru*knows that God never becomes God, but always is God. God means the Supreme, all-powerful; therefore, hoe can He be controlled by anything? How can God, the highest authority, the Absolute Truth, be controlled by ignorance? It is not possible. Because we ourselves are controlled by material nature, and because we are thinking ourselves to be God, we therefore think that Kṛṣṇa’s form must be material. We try to bring God down to our level.

When Kṛṣṇa told Arjuna that He spoke the science of *yoga*to the sun-god, Arjuna asked “The sun-god Vivasvān is senior by birth to You. How am I to understand that in the beginning You instructed this science to him?” Kṛṣṇa replied, “Many, many births both you and I have passed, Arjuna. I can remember all of them, but you cannot.” Kṛṣṇa’s memory is perfect in all phases of time, and we cannot even remember what we doing a few days before. Kṛṣṇa has the potency of God, but not ourselves.

In the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, it is further stated “*tene brahma hrdā*;” this knowledge was imparted into the heart of the first created being, Lord Brahmā, by the same Kṛṣṇa that spoke to Arjuna 5,000 years ago and the sun-god some 120 million years ago. The fact that there is no difference between these various incarnations is confirmed by Kṛṣṇa Himself in the fourth chapter of the *Bhagavad-gītā*:

*ajo ’pi sann avyayātmā*  
*bhūtānām īśvaro ’pi san*  
*prakṛtiṁ svām adhiṣṭhāya*  
*sambhavāmy ātma-māyayā*

**“Although I am unborn and My transcendental body never deteriorates, and although I am the Lord of all sentient beings, I still appear in every millennium in My original transcendental form.”**

Kṛṣṇa, God, has form. He is not formless, or impersonal, and it is that form that evokes our love. What’s more, Kṛṣṇa’s form is *svarāt*, eternal and full of knowledge. God does not have to take knowledge from anyone. He is the source of all knowledge. We too may also have knowledge, but it is limited. I may know what is going on in my body, and you may know what is going on in your body, but we cannot know what is going on in each other’s body. But Kṛṣṇa says:

*sarvasya cāhaṁ hṛdi sanniviṣṭo*  
*mattaḥ smṛtir jñānam apohanaṁ ca*

**“I am seated in everyone’s heart, and from Me come remembrance, knowledge and forgetfulness.”**

That is the difference between ourselves and God. God’s knowledge is all-pervasive and perfect, whereas our knowledge is limited and subject to illusion. Lord Brahmā therefore prays:

*īśvaraḥ paramaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ*  
*sac-cid-ānanda-vigrahaḥ*  
*anādir ādir govindaḥ*  
*sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam*

**“Kṛṣṇa who is known as Govinda is the Supreme Godhead. He has an eternal blissful spiritual body. He is the origin of all. He has no other origin and He is the prime cause of all causes.”**

How great the difference between directly serving your lotus feet and the lotus feet of the great *ācāryas*who you represent, and the so-called Christian who claims to have a “personal” relationship with God, but who do not know who God is or how He is great. This knowledge which you are giving us, Śṛīla Prabhupāda, is your real contribution to mankind.

Kṛṣṇa says in the *Bhagavad-gītā*; “Those miscreants who are grossly foolish, who are lowest among mankind, whose knowledge is stolen by illusion, and who partake of the atheistic nature of demons do not surrender unto Me.” So, just as the demons are not willing to surrender to Kṛṣṇa, they are just as inimical to the process of surrendering to His pure devotee. Yet, in spite of so many obstacles, your firm dedication to the instructions of your *guru-mahārāja*, and your unlimited compassion for the conditioned souls suffering the pangs of repeated birth and death, has pushed this movement forward with great force and purity. That is your qualification.

There are so many rascals Śrīla Prabhupāda. They publish translations of *Bhagavad-gītā*, and though Kṛṣṇa clearly says “*Sarva-dharmām parityaja mām ekaṁ śaranaṇam vraja*,” “Give up all other nonsense and just surrender unto Me,” they have not taught anyone to become devotees of the Lord.

You are delivering the real message of Godhead; you are the confidential servitor and representative of the Lord; you have never changed one word, one letter of the Vedic knowledge; in every action you prove yourself to be on the top rung of spiritual perfection (although in your own mind you feel yourself to be the most fallen); you are bringing light where there was once darkness; you have been authorized to speak on behalf of the Lord and the disciplic succession; for these and innumerable other reasons, the Kṛṣṇa consciousness movement, under your direction, is the only spiritual shelter for the world today.

The non-devotees can’t touch you Śrīla Prabhupāda. They can’t come up to the standard of the ground on which you walk. That’s all.

So what can we say but thank you. Thank you Śrīla Prabhupāda, for not cheating us, for giving us the reality at a time when everyone is simply concocting imaginary principles. Following in the footsteps of Sanātana Goswami, we are now praying with all earnestness that this knowledge of devotional service may manifest in our hearts, and the desire and determination to spread this movement all over the world, become our sole desire in life. Now we have received so much instruction about our relationship with Kṛṣṇa, and now we are simply hoping that by your causeless mercy, we may somehow develop a taste for serving the lotus feet of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

Our spiritual great-grandfather, His Divine Grace Saccindānanda Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura, has declared in his *daśaūla* that the Absolute truth is one, and cannot be approached with imperfect senses. The living entities have five senses for acquiring knowledge, and five organs of action, and above these, there is another sense, the mind. None of these is competent to enter into the anti-material domain of the Supreme Lord. Conditioned souls are stuck in the temporary varieties of knowledge the material nature provides, and have no means to leap over the barrier of ignorance, save and except being coached by the spiritual master.

Envious persons, with an eye to scrutinizing all phenomenon through their doubting intellect, are prone to inquire as to whether the exclusive passport of the *bhaktis*is fair or non-biased. The doubting souls cannot accept the priority of pure love and would make all means of acquaintanceship with the Lord equally one and the same in their result. Our preceptor exemplar, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, has quoted a verse from the *Purāṇas*which puts the question to rest:

*harer nāma harer nāma harer nāmaiva kevalam*  
*kalau nāsty eva nāsty eva nāsty eva gatir anyathā*

The purport is that there is but one means of deliverance in the age of Kali, and that is to chant Hari’s Name, or specifically, the *mahā-mantra*:

Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa, Hare Hare  
Hare Rāma, Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma, Hare Hare

One who is able to succeed in chanting these anti-material Names without *aparādha*, offense, becomes at once famous throughout the universe. On the other hand, one who advances in mystic power, and employs his talents for material gain, as in the case of Rāvaṇa and Hiraṇyākṣa, becomes infamous, and his life is a failure.

Success in life, and a pure and satisfied mind, are achieved through the association of the *bhaktivedāntas*. Even a moment in the presence of the servants of Kṛṣṇa, or even their causelessly merciful glance, is the most auspicious moment of a soul’s conditioned sojourn, and guarantees access to *bhakti*, the sine qua non of ultimate eternity, bliss and knowledge. Indeed, Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja has stated in his *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*:

*ei brahmāṇḍa bhramite kona bhāgyavān jīva*  
*guru-kṛṣṇa-prasāde pāya bhakti-latā-bīja*

We are endlessly traveling throughout millions of species of life, touching every planet in the universe, but by the mercy of a pure devotee of Kṛṣṇa, one may receive the seed of devotion within the heart, which, if developed, leads to freedom from birth and death. The spiritual master is known as the vice-Lord, because of the plemipotentiary endowment from the Lord Himself. He is able to remold and reform the heart of the disciple and return it to its original godly shape, provided the disciple is surrendered to his instructions and is a willing servant. We must always understand that in matters of spirit there is no undue force applied to unwilling subjects. Those willing to remain in the material world are given facility to suffer for eternity under the hand of Durgā, and those willing to return back to home, back to Godhead, are given the opportunity when Kṛṣṇa sees fit.

Our spiritual master is our life and soul; his order is more dear than our life-breath. No one can afford to accept a cheat, whose orders will warp and destroy the life of devotion. It is exigent therefore, for us to inquire into the specifications of the devotee, so that we may recognize His Divine Grace when we have the opportunity of his audience. In a lecture delivered in London on October 6, 1969 His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda declared that there are but two prerequisites for the spiritual master. He must have thorough realization that he is not the body, that is, he is spirit soul. Secondly, he must have heard from the right sources.

Let us for a moment examine this proposal. If the spiritual master has understood that he is not material but an anti-material living entity, he is not willing to take advantage of material facilities for sense gratification, nor is he willing to exploit others for gain. *Bhagavad-gītā*states that one who sees all living entities as having the same qualities neither laments nor desires. This exalted status of *brahma-bhūta*must be present before we can actually trust anyone. Otherwise there will forever be some kind of inebriation, and this is not acceptable in a spiritual master.

Simple understanding of the spiritual nature of one’s existence is not enough to deliver him from all material difficulties. The further qualification is that the spiritual master must have heard from the right sources. This means sources in direct, unbroken contact with the Absolute Truth. If the truth is one, the alteration of the truth means duality and relativity. A relative truth will not lead one on the progressive path back to home, back to Godhead. But His Divine Grace has heard from Kṛṣṇa directly through disciplic succession, unbroken and unaltered.

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda is possessed of all good spiritual qualifications, and therefore we must accept him as the captain of the ship crossing over the difficult ocean of the age of Kali. So many men have come preaching from India, but they have not borne the diploma of disciplic succession. Consequently, their students have failed in the final examination at death. Let us forever remain obedient to our *guru-mahārāja,*because his service is our constitution, our life-force. Let us pray to never surpass His Divine Grace through meritorious works or fine words, but simply remain available, ready to do his bidding as he sees fit. In this attitude alone can we hope to attract his attention and receive the highest benediction of spiritual life, to serve the servant of the servant of the *gopīs*, a thousand times removed. All glories to Śrīla Prabhupāda! All glories to Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga!

In the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* Śrīla Śukadev Goswami tells Mahārāja Parīkṣit, “My dear king, one should stay at a place where the great *ācāryas* speak about the transcendental activities of the Lord, and one should give aural reception to the nectarean rivers flowing from the moonlike faces of such great personalities.” You have so kindly and tastefully presented this *Bhāgavat-Dharma,* as the parrot makes the taste of a fruit more relishable, by touching it with its beak.

Oh divine spiritual master, we pray that we, who have no qualification to serve you, may be able to meditate upon your body, words and deeds from which *kṛṣṇa-prema* shines forth, destroying our lust, anger and greed. We know that it is impossible for conditioned selves to understand one-tenth of the nature of this divine love relished by Your Divine Grace. Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura has given us a little understanding of this relationship between the Lord and His most confidential servitors by revealing to us that “All the associates and servitors of Kṛṣṇa in Vraja are part of the service of Śrī Rādhikā.” No wonder then that the love of Kṛṣṇa exhibited by your grace is saving the world from hell.

Fully understanding that one can only please Kṛṣṇa by pleasing His devotees, you came to the western world to give everyone a taste of the nectar of *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.* Into this hell of slaughterhouses, casinos, bars and brothels you calmly came with the message of Lord Caitanya, requesting the fallen souls to take up self-realization by the transcendental method of singing, dancing and feasting.

It is stated in the *Nectar of Devotion*that the qualification of a spiritual master is that he must have realized the conclusion of the Vedas by deliberation and argument and thus be able to convince others of these conclusions.

Your fame and glory will be spread all over the world as you are performing the greatest of miracles by turning stone-hearted entities into devotees of Kṛṣṇa. The Lord’s service is described in the *Bhāgavatam* as the ultimate good for the people in general.

Śrīla Prabhupāda, your service to the Lord is unmotivated and uninterrupted, and therefore you are absolutely self-satisfied. This quality makes you entirely unique and worshipable by the whole world.

Oh, Śrīla Prabhupāda, you are the ideal teacher, and we are your unworthy disciples. You are our eternal father and we are your helpless children. You are the supreme spiritual master and we humbly pray that we may be engaged as menial servants at your feet.

**“We think that we have met your grace by the will of Providence, and thus we accept you as the captain of the ship for those who desire to cross over the dangerous ocean of Kali, which destroys all the good qualities of the human being.” (*****Bhāg.*** **1.1.22)**

**“Therefore devotees should constantly hear about, glorify, remember and worship the Personality of Godhead (Bhagāvan), who is their protector.” (*****Bhāg.*** **1.2.14)**

It is that self-same knowledge that Kṛṣṇa imparted to Arjuna on the battlefield of Kurukṣetra, after hearing which Sañjaya declared;

**“And so wonderful is this message that my hair is standing on end.” (*****Gītā*****, 18.74)**

So similarly we are being thrilled by receiving the ecstatic *prema* you are displaying and distributing. It is said in the *Bhagavad-gītā* that learning from the spiritual master is as good as learning from Kṛṣṇa, so we offer our humble obeisances at your feet.

**“Those great sages who have completely taken shelter of the lotus feet of the Lord can at once sanctify those who come in touch with them, whereas the waters of the Ganges can do so only after prolonged use.” (*****Bhāg.*** **1.1.15)**

Similarly, Śrī Advaita told Śrī Caitanya;

“Wherever You are, O Lord, there is Vṛndāvana.”

Śrīla Prabhupāda, you have brought Vṛndāvana to us, by your divine mercy, and you are purifying the atmosphere wherever you go. You are known as a “vice-Lord,” according to the revealed scriptures, because you have come to bring us back home, back to Godhead. In light of your greatness, we cannot help but bow at your feet.

Because your only desire is to please Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa cannot forget you; in fact there is no one more dear to Him than yourself, according to *Bhagavad-gītā,* chapter 18, verse 69.

**"There is no servant in this world more dear to Me than he, nor will there ever be one more dear."**

The Vedic literatures describe the transcendental qualities of the pure devotee. Pure unalloyed devotional service is performed without any motive for material benefit. Śrīla Prabhupāda, you have no desire for *dharma, artha, kāma or mokṣa* (religiosity, economic development, sense gratification or liberation), and therefore we gladly worship your feet, and offer obeisances to you at least three times daily.

A person engaged in devotional service develops all good qualities, generally found in the demigods, as described by Śukadeva Goswami;

**“My dear king, persons who have unflinching faith in Kṛṣṇa and are without duplicity can develop all the good qualities of the demigods. On account of a devotee’s high grade of Kṛṣṇa consciousness, even the demigods like to live with him, and therefore it can be understood that the qualities of the demigods have developed within this body.” (*****Bhāg.*** **5.18.12)**

**“…you are free from all vice. You are well-versed in all religious scriptures, the** ***Puranas*** **and histories, for you have gone through them under proper guidance and have also explained them.” (*****Bhāg.*** **1.1.6)**

Unfortunate as I am, I cannot appreciate your greatness, but I pray that someday I may be able to glorify you in a befitting manner, worthy of a personality described as the sum total of all the demigods.

All glories to your lotus feet, Śrīla Prabhupāda, may I offer my obeisances eternally unto you.

## Tokyo

To His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda  
On the occasion of Śrī Vyāsa-Pūjā

Yudhiṣṭhira Mahāraja was talking to Bhīmasena. The she-jackal was spitting fire; a dog was, without fear, barking at the king. Wind was blowing dust, and the clouds rained blood in their wake. The stars in the sky were fighting; the reservoirs, muddy; the cows, crying. Cities and mines turned ugly and barren. It was the beginning of the *Kali-yuga.*

Invincible time has proceeded on its course. What were omens then are facts now. That we are born into such conditions we must take as great misfortune. We indeed were feeling like that, lamenting, and half-heartedly attempting different remedies, individual or social. But it was all darkness. If not the others, *we*at least felt it to be so. That must be why we turned to you.

In this miserable, degraded age, most merciful Kṛṣṇa sent you. *Kali-yuga* without you would be hopeless. All principles of spiritual understanding had by now perished, being replaced by meat-eating, intoxication, sex, fraud, etc. There was no longer even a clue for reform. We were hopeless and deserving of the worst for our *vikarma.* Eternally, almost without consciousness, like a log, we were floating down the river of material existence. The actual cause, other than pure mercy, we cannot trace out, but somehow or other we came in touch with you, as a log sometimes catches the bank, and its journey downstream halts. That was the beginning of our life, our real birth. That was fortune, and later we came to know it as the only possible fortune.

All respect is due to the *guru*. It is a necessity. But in the *Kali-yuga,* how much more, then, we worship he who mercifully accepts, and what is more, delivers, *jīvas* who have no qualification. You gave us not just Kṛṣṇa, but also Caitanya Mahāprabhu, who, though non-different, is the most merciful, willing to purify the unwilling *mlecchas.* In spite of blasphemy and insult He does not condemn, but simply pleads that all people accept the nectar of chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa, feeling love of God. By Your Divine Grace’s trick we have come to taste that nectar. So as Caitanya is called the most merciful of God’s forms, we must call you the most merciful of all *gurus*, because you have brought us to Him.How can we praise you most? Action, not words, is proof of sincerity. Our actual homage unto you will be our eagerness to follow your orders. Your desire is that we preach this message and transform the world. Not to disrespect your words, we are trying to do this now. We pray even to Kṛṣṇa for help. We must ever thank you for engaging us thusly, giving us this transcendental job, *sanātana-dharma.*
