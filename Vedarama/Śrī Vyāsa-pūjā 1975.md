# Śrī Vyāsa-pūjā 1975

## The Meaning of Vyāsa-pūjā

The day on which a disciple celebrates the appearance of his spiritual master is called Vyāsa-pūjā. The word “Vyāsa” indicates Śrīla Vyāsadeva, a powerful incarnation of Kṛṣṇa (God) who compiled all of the Vedic literature, and “*pūjā*” means worship.

Vyāsadeva appeared five thousand years ago, at the dawn of the present Age of Quarrel, just when widespread moral deterioration was about to begin. He could foresee that the people of this age would be short-lived, unfortunate and always disturbed.

Before Vyāsadeva, all the Vedic scriptures were taught and received verbally in disciplic succession. The memories of both the students and the spiritual masters of those ages were so sharp that once they heard a transcendental message from their spiritual master, the message was immediately imprinted into their brains and considered a written language.

To give the unfortunate people of the modern age a chance to partake of the knowledge contained in the *Vedas*, Śrī Vyāsadeva undertook the enormous task of compilation. He composed the millions of Sanskrit verses of the *Mahābhārata*, the *Vedānta-sūtra* and the *Purāṇas*, including *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*. No one else in history can compare with him as a poet, philosopher or writer, and he is thus known as Mahāmuni (the great sage). He is the spiritual master of all who follow the teachings of the *Vedas*. His final, mature work, *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, is known as the “ripened fruit of the *Vedas*” and is sufficient in itself for God realization. The precious *Bhāgavatam* has been handed down in disciplic succession, from *guru* to disciple, in an unbroken chain, and today His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda, spiritual master of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness, is delivering it in turn to his disciples.

Out of his causeless mercy, Śrīla Vyāsadeva wrote *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* for the benefit of the fallen people of Kali-yuga (the modern age), and out of his causeless mercy Śrīla Prabhupāda is personally translating and expanding this monumental work—and he is preaching it throughout the western world as well. At the advanced age of seventy, he traveled under great hardship from India to America to found the International Society for Krishna Consciousness, and today, nine years later, he tirelessly works eighteen to twenty hours a day, still translating and preaching the eternal message of *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*: pure, unalloyed service to Kṛṣṇa (God).

Oṁ Viṣṇupāda Paramahaṁsa Parivrājakācārya Aṣṭtottara-śata  
Śrī Śrīmad Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Gosvāmī Mahārāja Prabhupāda

His Divine Grace Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī, the foremost devotee and scholar of the previous age, is the spiritual master of His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda. He is the ocean of transcendental mercy and the bestower of devotional service upon the fallen souls. His is the deliverer of devotional service which is enriched with the conjugal love of Śrī-Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. He is the personified teachings of Lord Caitanya. Thus, he never tolerates any statement against the teaching of devotional service as enunciated by Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī.

## Atreya Ṛṣi dāsa adhikārī, GBC, Middle East, Financial Affairs.

I remember you in your association. I remember you in your pictures. I remember you in your smile or anger. I remember you dancing, chanting or crying. But most of all, my beloved master, I remember you in me. You are what I am: you are forming my new desires, my new intelligence and my new actions.

It is your words, Prabhupāda, that have saved me. The more I listen to them, the freer I become. In the dark jungle I have lived all my life. Your words have led to your radiating, walking shoes. Lost I have been for thousands and thousands of years. And all of a sudden, through your words, I discover these beautiful shiny soft shoes. Attracting me in the beginning because they glitter, they enchant me because they dance . … up and down, to and fro. Then, they appear before me, close to me, and humbly invite me to follow. I love these shoes of yours, Prabhupāda. I am still in the dark world: I don’t appreciate you for what you really are—the friend of the *gopīs*, etc. I love you because of these shoes, because they are taking me out of this scary dark jungle. They are beautiful because I have to search no more. I love them because they give me relief. I remember how much I used to run and used to get very bruised. That is how I developed this thick skin, wounds on top of hardly healed wounds. The holes in the ground, the trees, the animals, all those scary things. No more! I have found these two lovely shiny shoes: they are taking me out. I have to worry no more. Every step I know exactly where to put. Walking is so easy in this dense forest—no more risks to take. Your words have proven and have convinced me that I will fall no more, as long as I follow those shoes. And it is such a relief, a happy joyous walk, knowing where to step next and having your words as a guide.

Gurujī, you see how selfish I am. You tell me, “All right son, remain selfish, but tend your real self-interest.” As I hear you say this over and over to me, all of a sudden I start to hear you: I start to believe you. I don’t care, Gurujī, for Lord Brahmā, who is in charge of such a wonderful creation. I don’t care for all the great demigods.

I don’t care for all the magicians around. The ones who walk on the water or fly in the sky. The ones who can go to the moon or make a heart melt on fire. What have they done for me? Nothing! Nothing! They simply made me more and more blind. But your shoes are different. They are my personal magician. They shine into my life. They melt my heart.

I am still in the darkness of ignorance. But lucky I am, I have your shoes. They run in front of me, and they stop and wait for me, and sometimes they dance for me. And in very rare instances, when I let them, they come and sit on my head.

Guru, I have found life. Life is simply my heart beating by your words, for your words. A new taste, a new world. Anything which negates your words is dead. I am far, far away from the ocean, but through your words I have come to believe that it exists. Reality has become your words for me. Because I am poisoned, near you I cannot come. I am not ready. I can’t hear your words when I am near you. I can’t see your shoes. I am so envious, I become deaf and blind. So let me be with your words and shoes.

Atreya Ṛṣi dāsa adhikārī, GBC, Middle East, Financial Affairs.

## Brahmānanda Svāmī

**Vyāsa-Pujā-the Appearance Day of the Spiritual Master**

He reasons ill who tells that Vaiṣṇavas die  
When thou art living still in sound!  
The Vaiṣṇavas die to live, and living try  
To spread the holy name around!

Sometimes, Śrila Prabhupāda, you speak about a subject which is very frightening. You did it last year in San Francisco during your *Ratha-yātrā* speech before five thousand assembled American youths. You said, “You are generally young, whereas I am an old man who may die at any moment.” You say these things in order to make us take this movement more seriously. But it is unimaginable. How could such a thing ever happen? How can I believe that you, who are my master, my life and my soul would ever depart? Do the oceans ever dry up? Does the air ever blow away? Does the sun die? Then why should I believe that Your Divine Grace will?

Can you see what you have done to me, Śrila Prabhupāda? You have made me your eternal servitor. You cannot leave me, ever. Without your service there is no existence for me. The moment you leave I will fall down and begin to suffocate like a flapping fish washed up from the sea. I must have your service. Like a pet house dog I will scamper after you, even though you may try to leave me behind. Where you go, so will I. If you die, then so will I. My life is already yours, so there is no loss for me.

I wont let you die. I will block death, and also old age and disease. I will stand in the way and not let them pass. I am guarding your door, and by your grace I have the strength.

At least I can give you my own duration of life. I can offer you my own youthfulness. Just as the son of Yayāti agreed to offer his aging father his own youthfulness so that his father could prolong his enjoyment, even though it means the end of the son’s, so I also agree. Take my youth, my strength, my health. Give me your agedness. Let all the discomforts come to me. Let all the diseases come to me. Let death come and take me instead. Then there will be some small value to my life. I have no asset other than my death for your life. I am ready.

But Kṛṣṇa proclaims boldly in the *Bhagavad-gītā, kaunteya pratijānīhi, na me bhaktyaḥ praṇaśyati.* “My devotee never perishes.” You Śrila Prabhupāda, are Kṛṣṇa’s devotee, and as far as I have seen, the only pure devotee in the world. So, Kṛṣṇa is making this promise to you. Because you are the lord’s pure devotee, you are not an ordinary person. When Kṛṣṇa certifies that nothing can defeat you, that includes death. “*Ihaiva tair jitaḥ sargo*.” You have already conquered over death. Who else in this world has done this? Even Einstein was not able to outsmart death. Just as God does not die, neither does his devotee. “*Na jāyate mriyate vā kadācin*.” There is no death for the soul. “*Na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre*.” Only the body dies. You alone Śrila Prabhupāda, have fully realized what it means to be spirit soul and not this body, and you are now trying to teach this. Because you are free from death, you want to save the rest of the world. You have dedicated your life to this-every moment, every thought, every word we hear and read as your books. And, I think, you will continue to teach anyone who wants to learn, until you are satisfied that at least one of us has understood that I WILL NOT DIE. Then you will depart, having fulfilled your mission.

So what is the purpose of my trying to keep Your Divine Grace from death? It is a foolish illusion for me to think that you will die? At night there is a tendency to think that the sun is no longer in the sky. Actually the sun is shining during the night as brightly as during the day, only I cannot see it. I know the sun is there even though it is out of my sight. It is a question of accepting authority and rejecting limited experiential knowledge.

Some day you will leave me, but now I know that you cannot die. Simply I will not be able to see you. I may not be able to see you, but I will be able to hear you. What you have tirelessly spoken will echo in my heart and I will know that you are there with Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, in the spiritual world. You will still be living and continuing to please Kṛṣṇa as you are doing in this world. The only thing for me to do after you leave will be to continue to serve you and always do exactly what you have told me to do. Then I will live with you forever,

as your humble servant,  
Brahmānanda Svāmī

## Calcutta

*nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya kṛṣṇa-preṣṭhāya bhū-tale*

We humbly offer our most respectful obeisances unto your two lotus feet. While offering *daṇḍavats,* we fall before you in this way, straight like a stick, with a straw between our teeth, and beg for your mercy. You are the representative of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu, the original spiritual master, and therefore you are very merciful. Your hand of mercy has touched our stonelike hearts and blessed our lives with ecstatic chanting and thoughts of Lord Caitanya. As the representative of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, your mission is to deliver the fallen souls back to Godhead, and by your causeless mercy you have raised us up to the rank of Vaisnava. And now, our dear beloved spiritual master, your glorification is our foremost duty.

You, alone, at seventy years of age, left the all-blissful, all-auspicious abode of Rādhā-Dāmodara Temple in Śrī Vṛindāvana-dhāma and set out in the bottom of a boat to save us, who are not worthy to be saved. You came with Kṛṣṇa in the form of *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam;* you braved the cold New York winters; you tolerated the audacity of a puffed-up hashish *yogī*; you humbly accepted the insults of a drug-crazed teenage hippie, and, like a true Vaiṣṇava, held no malice and called him a friend. You chanted and danced the glories of Kṛṣṇa while water was being poured down upon you from an irate tenant in an upstairs apartment. You cooked and served the *prasāda,* cleaned up after us, and never requested any assistance, until finally someone said, “Can I help, Swamiji?”

Upon visiting Vṛndāvana recently, we could all only wonder at how you could leave such a wonderful place to come to the hellish Western world, but factually we would have never known Vṛndāvana had you not brought us there. It is by your mercy alone that these fallen souls, groping in the black concrete skyscraper depths of Kali Age, are being lifted by the sweet liquid song of the holy names to the golden fields of Vṛndāvana, where the cows are coming home in the cool of the evening and where, in the sweet secret depths of Vraja, the most sought after couple resides, in the perfect ecstasy of loving devotion. You brought us home to Vṛndāvana and Māyāpura.

By the potency of the holy name, you have taken the blazing fire burning within our bodies, our hearts, our minds and our intelligence for so many untold lifetimes and extinguished it. By the ocean of transcendental knowledge emanating from your cooling lotus-like mouth, you are untying the knot of material attachment, and at last, we are being freed from the painful cycle of birth and death. You are the most expert king, guarding your unworthy subjects against the onslaught of *māyā.* You are delicate and gentle, with a lotus-like countenance. You are *Kṛṣṇa’s* ambassador of love, and you are delivering the message of Lord Caitanya. Thus, you have traversed the world with your lotus-like feet and provided shelter for countless fallen souls.

We, here in Calcutta Yātrā, feel most fortunate that we will often throughout the year have the association of the holy *dhāma,* Śrīdhāma Māyāpura. Calcutta itself is a holy place, due to the fact that you have appeared here. Factually, according to the authority of books, we can understand that the pure devotee does not go to the holy places for purification, like the conditioned souls. So many conditioned souls go every day, all year, to relieve themselves of the great burden of their sins, leaving them at the holy places, and a great pure devotee like yourself goes there to take up the burden of those sins, thus relieving the burden of sinful activities from the holy place. Who is there who can estimate how valuable is your association, when you are purifying the holy places?

Also, from your books, we have learned that one can associate with the spiritual master directly or through his instructions, and of the two, association with him in separation, through his instructions, is even more valuable and of greater transcendental benefit. The instruction of the spiritual master can never be neglected. Simply by following his instructions, cent per cent, to his full satisfaction, the disciple is guaranteed to go back to home, back to Godhead. Therefore, we at Calcutta ISKCON again make a firm vow and pledge, as made when we all took our initiation. We will all sincerely chant sixteen rounds of the Hare Kṛṣṇa *māha-mantra* every day; we will follow all of the four regulative principles, cent per cent, without deviation; we will read your books every day, very carefully, and we will follow all other rules, regulations and instructions without deviation. And, without self-motivation or other vested interests, we cent per cent surrender to your lotus feet to perform our devotional service with full enthusiasm, sincerity, determination and patience. Yes, patience! Patiently, we are waiting, working very hard and waiting for you to just simply cast your merciful lotus-like glance upon us. Otherwise, our position is hopeless. You are the deliverer of the fallen and the only hope for those who want to cross over the dangerous ocean of Kali. You are the master of the ship that takes us over this ocean. O master, be merciful unto us, give us the shade of your lotus feet. Your fame is spread all over the three worlds. All glories to Śrīla Prabhupāda. All glories to Jagadguru.

## Dallas

As stated by Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, in the essence of the *Bhāgavatam, catuḥśloki:* a person interested in transcendental knowledge must inquire about it. Intelligent inquiry directed toward the source of all transcendental knowledge, Śrī Kṛṣṇa, is an integral part of the path of self-realization.

Because we are densely covered by the modes of passion and ignorance, it is impossible for us to contact Lord Kṛṣṇa directly, although He resides in the nearness of our hearts.

Therefore, in the words of Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja:

*jīve sākśāt nāhi tāte guru caittya-rūpe*  
*śikṣā-guru haya kṛṣṇa mahānta-svarūpe*

**“Since one cannot visually experience the presence of the Supersoul, He appears before us as a liberated devotee. Such a spiritual master is no one other than Kṛṣṇa Himself.”**

And Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja makes the point that although the spiritual master is a servitor of the Lord, he is also a plenary manifestation (*prakāśa*) of the Lord. Lord Kṛṣṇa manifests Himself in the form of *bhakta-śreṣṭha,* or the topmost devotee of the Lord. Kṛṣṇa Himself says to Uddhava:

*ācāryaṁ māṁ vijānīyān*  
*nāvamanyeta karhicit*  
*na martya-buddhyāsūyeta*  
*sarva-devamayo guruḥ*

**“One should know the** ***ācārya*** **as myself and never disrespect him in any way. One should not envy him, thinking him an ordinary man, for he is the representative of all the demigods.”**

Self-realization being the goal of our human life, we must become intelligent enough to approach the spiritual master and take initiation. He is the exemplary embodiment of the Lord’s mercy, and he is empowered to bestow on us the fulfillment of our desire for self-realization. Kṛṣṇa confirms this in the *Bhagavad-gītā*:

*tad viddhi praṇipātena*  
*paripraśnena sevayā*  
*upadekṣyanti te jñānaṁ*  
*jñāninas tattva-darśinaḥ*

**“Just to try to learn the truth by approaching a spiritual master. Inquire from him submissively and render service unto him. The self-realized soul can impart knowledge unto you because he has seen the truth.”**

Also, there is an important verse in the *Munḍaka Upaniṣad* wherein it is stated:

*tad-vijñānārthaṁ sa gurum evābhigacchet*  
*samit-pāṇiḥ śrotriyaṁ brahma-niṣṭham*

**“To learn transcendental subject matter, one must approach the bona fide spiritual master in disciplic succession, fixed in the Absolute Truth.”**

The objective of the sincere inquirer of the Absolute Truth is established in all the *Vedas* as the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa. The practical acquisition of this objective is realized through devotional service, rendered unrestrictedly to the divine couple, Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Govinda. The means for approaching this perfected platform of knowledge and service is stated in the *Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad*:

*yasya deve parā bhaktir*  
*yathā deve tathā gurau*  
*tasyaite kathitā hy arthāḥ*  
*prakāśante mahātmanaḥ*

**“Only unto those great souls who have implicit faith in both the Lord and the spiritual master are all the imports of the Vedic knowledge automatically revealed.”**

Unfortunately, our intelligence for understanding the Absolute Truth has been covered by ignorance, into which we have been forcefully thrust by our all-devouring enemy, lust. It has covered our real spiritual inclination, and if we factually desire purification we must accept no alternative than to hear eagerly, with great care and attention, the messages of the spiritual master. Kṛṣṇa warns:

*atha cet tvam ahaṁkārān*  
*na śroṣyasi vinaṁkṣyasi*

**“However, if out of false ego you do not hear, you will be lost.”**

Or, as sung by Viśvanātha Cakravartī, “Without the grace of the spiritual master, one cannot make any advancement.”

Therefore, our only hope is to accept the lotus feet of the spiritual master, and have our senses, mind and intelligence enlivened by his instructions. This is confirmed by Narottama dāsa Thākura:

*guru-mukha-padma-vākya, cittete horiyā aikya*

**“My only wish is to have my consciousness purified by the words emanating form his lotus mouth.”**

He further states that the spiritual master destroys all (*avidyā-vināśā*).

Factually, the entire material world is like a burning forest, and the lustful desires within out hearts are equally destructive. We are destined by time to see the frustration of all our materialistic goals, just as the forest animal loses everything, including his life, when the forest burns. But Śrīla Viśvanātha Cakravartī sings:

*saṁsāra-dāvānala-līḍha-loka-*  
*trāṇāya kāruṇya-ghanāghanatvam*  
*prāptasya kalyāna-guṇārṇavasya*  
*vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam*

**“The spiritual master is receiving benediction from the ocean of mercy. Just as a cloud pours water on a forest fire to extinguish it, so the spiritual master delivers the materially afflicted world by extinguishing the blazing fire of material existence. I offer my respectful obeisances unto the lotus feet of such a spiritual master, who is an ocean of auspicious qualities.”**

Our spiritual master, Śrīla Prabhupāda, is bestowing devotional service on the fallen souls. We are seemingly rendered useless by our bad association and lack of practical intelligence. Yet, he is engaging us in devotional service to Lord Kṛṣṇa.

*yasya prasādād bhagavad-prasādaḥ*

**“By the mercy of the spiritual master one receives the benediction of Kṛṣṇa.”**

And further,

*śrī-guru-caraṇa-padma kevala bhakati-sadma*

**“The lotus feet of our spiritual master are the only way by which we can attain pure devotional service.”**

In light of so much evidence of the mercy and potency of our spiritual master, we can only beg forgiveness that we are unable to perform any pleasing service to you. Nevertheless, you are continuing to benedict us with your purificatory instructions, and you continue to engage us in service at the feet of Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

By your causeless mercy only, will our struggling attempts to serve and please you result in pure devotional service unto the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

## Honolulu

All glories to Oṁ Viśnupāda Paramahaṁsa Parivrājakācārya Aṣṭottara-śata Śrī Śrīmad Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda, our beloved spiritual master. **“We think that we have met Your grace by the will of providence (*****sandarśito dhātrā*****), and thus we accept you as captain of the ship (*****darṇa-dharaḥ*****) for those who desire to cross the dangerous ocean of Kali, which destroys all the good qualities of a human being.” (*****Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*** **1.1.22)**

First of all, we have to be able to separate the reality from that which isn’t. In other words, we have to want to know what is what. The *Vedas* succinctly express these veiled yet all-pervading truths. They are so extensive that we couldn’t realize all of them in our lifetime. When somebody decides, without reservations, motives or self-deception, to unravel the mysteries of the *Vedas,* he soon sees that these texts make two things very clear.

1\) The Supreme Personality of Godhead is Kṛṣṇa, the most powerful mystic (*Yogeśvara*) and the possessor of all-attractive transcendental opulences. The goal of our lives is to regain our currently covered personal association with Him. He dwells on innumerable planets of infinite variegatedness in the anti-material world (*nirasta-kuhakam*), with unlimited forms of dazzling splendor and sweetness. We are actually His eternal servants (*jīvera svarūpa haya—kṛṣṇera nitya-dāsa*), being spiritual in essence. Now we have come to the material world, the land of the temporary and painful material energy, due to our original envy and pride (minute independence). This was a great mistake on our part. In the spiritual sky with Lord Kṛṣṇa, everyone (everything) is of the same spiritual nature: eternal, full of knowledge and bliss (*sac-cid-ānanda*).

2\) The only way to reestablish our real position, after having fallen victim to this futile alternative, is to come in contact with a bona fide spiritual master (*guru*). Be wise enough to recognize him, hear from him and surrender to him with favorable service. The *Vedas* affirm this:

*tad vijñānārthaṁ sa gurum evābhigacchet:* If you want to understand spiritual matters, you must accept a *guru.* (*Muṇḍaka Up.* 1.2.12)

*dharmasya tattvaṁ nihitaṁ guhyaṁ mahājano yena gataḥ sa panthāḥ:* It is very difficult to understand the secret of spiritual consciousness, but one who advances by the instruction of the previous *ācāryas* and follows in the footsteps of his predecessors in the line of disciplic succession will have success. Others will not. (*Mahābhārata, Vanaparva* 313.117)

*jīvañ-chavo bhāgavatāṅghri-reṇuṁ na jātu martyo ‘bhilabheta yas tu:* The person who has not at any time received the dust of the feet of a pure devotee upon his heart certainly is a dead body. (*Bhāg.* 7.5.32)

*Sandarśito dhātrā.* We think we have met Śrīla Prabhupāda by the will of providence (the causeless mercy of Kṣīrodakaśāyī Viṣṇu). Roaming fearful and helpless, never sure if we were progressing. Then we saw the mountain range: the *guru*. Streams ran underneath the desert because they would dry up if they had to snake on top of the hot sand exterior. Sometimes the bedrock was broken here and there, and oases bubbled up onto the surface. We sampled some of these oases, but found them ultimately leaving us little more than the mirages which constantly baffled us. We always had some everchanging plan for progress but, horribly, the thought lingered in our conscious substratum that perhaps we were only going in circles. The blemish of our struggle, fear and doubt burst when we finally saw the mountain range. Now the goal is in sight.

In the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam,* which Prabhupāda is translating for us, the story of Kapiladeva, an incarnation of Kṛṣṇa from the spiritual world (*avatāra*), instructing His mother Devahūti about spiritual science is presented. *Sādhu* is a synonym for *mahātmā* (great soul), and Kapila gives important details about these *sādhus* and their elevated position:

“The symptoms of a *sādhu* are that he is tolerant, merciful (*karuṇikāḥ*) and friendly to all living entities. He has no enemies, he is peaceful, he abides by the scriptures and all his characteristics are sublime (*sādhu-bhūṣaṇa*). Such a *sādhu* engages in staunch devotional service to the Lord (*bhaktiṁ kurvanti ye dṛḍhām*) without deviation. For the sake of the Lord he renounces all other connections, such as family relationships and friendly acquaintances within the world. Engaged constantly in chanting and hearing about Me, the *sādhus* do not suffer from material miseries because they are always fixed on Me with their thoughts (*mad-gata-cetasaḥ*). O My mother, O virtuous lady, these are the qualities of great devotees who are free from all attachment. You must seek attachment to such holy men (*saṅgas teṣv artha te prarthyaḥ*), for this counteracts the pernicious effects of material attachment. In the association of pure devotees, discussion of the pastimes and activities of the Supreme Personality of Godhead is very pleasing and satisfying to the ear and the heart (*hṛt-karṇa-rasāyanāḥ kathāḥ*). By cultivation of such knowledge one gradually becomes advanced on the path of liberation, and thereafter he is freed, and his attraction becomes fixed. Then real devotion and service begin (*bhaktir anukramiṣyati*). (*Bhāg.* 3.25.21–25)

Kapila told Devahūti to seek attachment to such a spiritual master. Lord Caitanya also said *lavāmātra sādhu-saṅge sarva-siddhi haya:* “Simply a moment’s association with a pure devotee (*bhagavad-saṅgi-saṅgasya*) cannot be compared to being transferred to heavenly planets or even merging into the Brahman effulgence in complete liberation. For living entities who are destined to die (*martyānām*), association with pure devotees is the highest benediction.” (*Bhāg.* 4.30.34) Any kind of inoffensive contract with Śrīla Prabhupāda, done in fear or ignorance of his prowess, with lavish pomp, or like a pauper, will bring inconceivable benefit (*ajṅāta-sukṛti*). The *Bhāgavatam* elucidates that association for sense gratification is certainly the path of bondage. But the same type of association, performed with a saintly person (*sādhuṣu*), leads to the path of liberation, even if performed without knowledge (*vihito ‘dhiyā*) (*Bhāg.* 3.23.55).

To associate with Śrīla Prabhupāda through his devotees, books, tapes or with him personally presents the opportunity to hear from him. He is constantly hearing about Kṛṣṇa, as confirmed by Kapila Muni in His association with him allows us to have this transcendental message transferred through our ears into our minds.

“My dear Lord, You are glorified by the selected verses uttered by great personalities. Such glorification of Your lotus feet is just like saffron particles. When the transcendental vibration from the mouths of great devotees (*mahan-mukha-cyutah*) carries the aroma of the saffron dust of Your Lotus feet, the forgetful living entity gradually remembers his eternal relationship with You. My dear Lord, I therefore do not need any other benediction but the opportunity to hear from the mouth of Your pure devotee.” (*Bhāg.* 4.20.25) Hearing the *Vedas* from mental speculators, will not help us. By hearing from the spiritual master, everything becomes transparently clear. The submissive disciple (*snigdhasya śiṣyasya*) is endowed with favor from the spiritual master (*Bhāg.* 1.1.8). He lives transcendentally and continues to remember the Lord even at the end of life when the power of remembrance is slackened due to derangement of bodily membranes.

In the *Bhagavad-gītā,* Lord Kṛṣṇa says, *sādhur eva sa mantavyaḥ:* a *sādhu* is a strict follower of devotional service (*sevayā*). It is thus further confirmed in this verse of the *Gītā* (4.34) that he imparts realization because he has already realized the truth.

*Mahat-sevāṁ-dvāram āhur vimuktes:* “Service to a *mahātmā* is the royal road to liberation.” The *Bhāgavatam* says by regular service to the pure Vaiṣṇavas, one can clear the dirt from his heart and thus enjoy supreme peace, liberation from material attachment and satisfaction automatically (*prasīdato ‘tyanta-śamaṁ svataḥ*) (*Bhāg.* 4.21.40). To become enwrapped in the pastimes of the spiritual world, which is the process called Kṛṣṇa consciousness, we have to gain the higher taste of wanting nothing more than to always hear about, glorify and remember Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa and His transcendental pastimes. The methodology of the most intelligent aspirant is service to the *guru.* “O twice born sages, by serving those devotees who are completely freed from all vice, great service is done. By such service, one gains affinity for hearing the message of Vāsudeva, (Kṛṣṇa)” (*Bhāg.* 1.2.16). Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura, a great *ācārya* in our line of disciplic succession says *chāḍiyā vaiṣṇava-sevā nistāra pāyeche kebā:* Unless one serves the spiritual master and *ācāryas,* one cannot be liberated. In the *Bhāgavatam,* Mahārāja Rahūgaṇa inquired from the great saint Jaḍabharata how he could attain this liberation from material entanglement. **Vyāsa-Pūjā Homage**

**“Our Spiritual Master is the Ocean of Mercy, the friend of the poor, the Lord and Master of the devotees. Oh Master, be merciful unto me. Give me the shade of Your Lotus Feet. Your fame is spread all over the three worlds.”**

*Dear Śrīla Prabhupāda,*

No one can estimate the extent of your mercy. The spiritual master is the incarnation of Kṛṣṇa’s mercy and Kṛṣṇa Himself cannot measure His own potency since it is always increasing. Your kindness upon us is always increasing, for you turn no one away. Accepting nothing for yourself, you are always striving in so many ways to entice and inspire, somehow awaken our desire to give more to Kṛṣṇa.

It is said that the test of a great mind is to be able to hold two opposing ideas which are generally taken to be opposite: idealism and practicality. You are distributing, by the grace of Lord Caitanya, the supremely pure gift of love of God. In your books you have intricately described the symptoms of the pure devotees blessed with such love. All this is far beyond our experience. Yet, you do not leave us to daydream or lament, speculate or concoct. But for each one of us you have left concrete instruction, minute for minute outlines for molding our lives.

We have all come without qualification. Still, you have engaged us, given us service. In the material world, they’ve run out of jobs. But there is no unemployment in ISKCON.

We were all born with no sense of duty. Lower than *śudras,* disrespecting everyone—we unbroken dogs, we could only make noise and fight. But you have given us responsibility, put trust in us. Now we have to try to repay you for your confidence.

Devoid of all good qualities, we approached you for the wrong reasons. But you have given us the right means of approach. So we must become humble.

Ignorant of the goal of life, we searched in darkness. Out of compassion, you told us of Kṛṣṇa. We must utilize this knowledge. By right, you are the proprietor of our lives, though we still try to cheat you. Our way of life, our goal, our happiness—our families, friends and means of sustenance—our work, enjoyment, philosophy and satisfaction, these are all gifts from you. There is nothing left from our old lives except for bad habits. You have purchased us thoroughly.

So here we are assembled, a clan of your disciples, ignorant and irresponsible, untrained and devoid of all saintly qualities—and you have told us to build Vṛndāvana. Who can we approach for help? Again we must address our prayers to you. Please open our ears and soften our hard hearts. Your instructions are all here. From your books and example, by the order of your representative we can know how to serve you.

But we are all hopelessly addicted to *māyā*. Even when trying we cannot surrender. Always subject to illusion, we causelessly neglect your wishes although no one could be so kind as yourself. Still, you have ordered us to become devotees, and that is our hope. As a touchstone changes all to gold, you have the power to purify even us. Our faith is in you—we have only to listen and to live as you tell us to.

## Vṛndāvana

Just as the sun rises in the east at the break of day,  
So the brilliant sun of Jagat-guru has risen in our soul.  
Before he came life was like death, dark and grey,  
Trying desperately to satisfy insatiable senses, thinking that was our goal.

Just as moths attracted by the light fly into the fire,  
And just as flies are attracted to the stool,  
So we foolish jīva souls have come into the mire  
Of illusion, and put ourselves under Māyāvādī’s rule.

Forgetting who we were and looking in a desperate attempt for love,  
We have searched on many a mountain, valley and stream,  
Looking like madmen everywhere below and above,  
Becoming frantic, ‘nothing is real, it’s a dream.’

And then when everything was lost, nothing left,  
Nowhere to go, no one to love, no more hope,  
A brilliant light appeared before us (in the West)  
And said “Come on, cross over this ocean, I am your boat.”

Oh, Śrīla Prabhupāda, you have saved us from this hell,  
From degradation, misery and strife.  
You have picked us up out of that deep dark well  
And given us the priceless gift of eternal life.

You have taught us that our dearmost friend and lover is Govinda,  
You have brought us to His abode, Śrī Vṛndāvana-dhāma.  
You have given us back our eternal dhāma  
And have distributed freely Kṛṣṇa prema, no more karma.

We are fallen souls with no qualification.  
Still, we pray to help you serve your Gurudeva,  
And in this way always stay in your association.

And so on this holiest Vyāsa-Pūjā day,  
We honor you with the greatest reverence and love.  
Your glories are known far and wide in every which way  
On the earth, down below and up above.

You are the greatest of all, our father, master and friend,  
And so we wish to offer you the Vyāsa seat.  
To describe your glories there can be no end,  
We simply pray to be situated eternally at your lotus feet.

## Washington, D. C.

*bhavad-vidhā bhāgavatās*  
*tīrtha-bhūtāḥ svayaṁ vibho*  
*tīrthī-kurvanti tīrthāni*  
*svāntaḥ-sthena gadābhṛtā*

**“Saints of your caliber are themselves places of pilgrimage. Because of their purity, they are constant companions of the Lord, and therefore they can purify even the places of pilgrimage.”**

*ācāryaṁ mām vijānīyān*  
*nāvamanyeta karhicit*  
*na martya-buddhyāsūyeta*  
*sarva-deva-mayo guruḥ*

**“One should respect the** ***ācārya*** **as Myself and never disrespect him in any way. One should not envy him, thinking him an ordinary man, for he is the representative of all the demigods.”**

*nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya*  
*kṛṣṇa-preṣṭāya bhū-tale*  
*śrīmate bhaktivedānta-*  
*svāmin iti nāmine*

*namas te sārasvate deve*  
*gaura-vāṇī-pracārine*  
*nirviśeṣa-śūnyavādi-*  
*pāścātya-deśa-tāriṇe*

Our Beloved Master,

Please accept our prostrate obeisances hundreds and hundreds of times at your lotus feet. It is by your mercy that we have any ability to appreciate your qualities. By hearing your divine words, the most ignorant child can distinguish between truth and falsehood. He can escape the most subtle doctrines and theories, which snag the best of the *yogīs, jñānīs,* elevationists and liberationists. He can cross the insurmountable ocean of illusion, and comprehending the ocean of truth, enter into the eternal realm of pure devotional service to Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa, tasting the sweetest transcendental *rasa.* Therefore we bow down to you. Our only wish is to have our consciousness purified by the words emanating from your lotus mouth. Your words are our life and soul.

You are our connection with *śāstra* and Kṛṣṇa, and are to be respected on the same level with the Supreme Lord. With your grace we cross over all impediments. Without your grace we find neither perfection, happiness nor the supreme goal of life. You are the confirmation of all the *śāstras* and the living receptacle of our meager service to Kṛṣṇa, who is otherwise forever beyond our reach. We are praying at your feet that we may never fall victim to a notion or doctrine opposing your teachings.

Before meeting you, we could not distinguish between a spiritual person and an outstanding materialist. Not knowing who or what to follow, we were constant victims of the theories. Constantly cheated by our own minds or by others’ minds, we had no idea what disease was plaguing us, how deadly serious it really was, or what sort of solution would cure it. You came to us when we were blinded by lust. You hold the torchlight of knowledge and discrimination. Now we are gradually seeing, by your grace, that devotional service (*ahaituki-bhakti*) is the only spiritual path, and that the pure *bhakta* is the only true spiritual man. The pure devotee of Kṛṣṇa, who is immersed in the bliss of pure love of God, is totally free of all defects and selfish desires. Being genuinely detached and unmotivated, he can never cheat us or lie. So, such a pure devotee alone (a pure Vaiṣṇava) is qualified as *jagad-guru,* and no one else can ever be. You, Śrīla Prabhupāda, are such a devotee.

Fully relishing the transcendental name, form, qualities and pastimes of Lord Caitanya and of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa, you are the ocean of compassion, and mercy spills out of you in an endless flow of teachings and books. You have come to possess Kṛṣṇa by the strength of your devotion. The maker and the goal of all *śāstra* is your confidential associate. You can deliver the most fallen souls, bringing them to the highest level of Vaiṣṇava. Because of you, anyone in any part of the world can reach the highest perfection and in turn, distribute mercy by delivering your mercy as it is.

## Winnipeg

*Dear Śrīla Prabhupāda,*Please accept our humble obeisances at your lotus feet.

*nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya kṛṣṇa-preṣṭāya bhū-tale*  
*śrīmate bhaktivedānta-svāmin iti nāmine*

*namas te sārasvate deve gaura-vāṇī-pracārine*  
*nirviśeṣa-śūnyavādi-pāścātya-deśa-tāriṇe*

O beloved spiritual master, sometimes, due to the material diseases of the conditioned soul, we find it very difficult to fully appreciate the causeless mercy which you are freely bestowing upon us. We are all so weak and devoid of intelligence; please allow us to be with you always. Being the direct representative of Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu, you also are *patita-pāvana,* friend of the fallen. Since we have no spontaneous attraction for serving the lotus feet of Prabhu Nityānanda (*nitāi pada-kamala*), how else but by your inconceivable mercy can we maintain any kind of enthusiasm and determination in our devotional service? For so many births and deaths we have been maddened by a false concept of bodily life and bound up by hundreds and thousands of material desires. But now we have all the mercy necessary to cross over the ocean of material suffering. We simply have to take it. As Govinda dāsa prays:

*durlabha mānava janama satsaṅge*  
*tāraha e bhava sindhu re*

“The Lord, being full and free from problems, can wholeheartedly care for His devotees. His concern is how to elevate and protect all those who have taken shelter at His lotus feet. The same responsibility is also entrusted to the spiritual master. The bona fide spiritual master’s concern is how the devotees who have surrendered to him as a representative of the Lord may make progress in devotional service. The Supreme Personality of Godhead is always mindful of the devotees who fully engage in cultivating knowledge of Him, having taken shelter at His lotus feet.” (Cc. *Ādi*, Vol. 1, purp)

*sādhavo hṛdayaṁ mahyaṁ*  
*sādhūnāṁ hṛdayaṁ tv aham*  
*mad-anyat te na jānanti*  
*nāhaṁ tebhyo manāg api*

**“Saints are My heart, and only I am their hearts. They do not know anyone but Me, and therefore I do not recognize anyone besides them as Mine.” (Cc.** ***Ādi*****, 1.62)**

You are so compassionate upon the fallen conditioned souls, now it is our duty and responsibility to follow your instructions without deviation. At every moment, let us pray as Bhaktivinoda prayed: “It is so difficult to find the strength to carry on alone the *saṅkīrtana* of the holy name of Hari. Please bless us by giving us one drop of faith with which to obtain the great treasure of the holy name of Kṛṣṇa. Give us all help. Give us all strength. Let us be as you are, without desires or aspirations.”

*sākṣād-dhari-tvena samasta-śāstrair*  
*uktas tathā bhāvyata eva sadbhiḥ*  
*kintu prabhor yaḥ priya eva tasya*  
*vande guroḥ śrī-caraṇāravindam*  
*(Gurv-aṣṭakam, 7)*

*īśvara-svarūpa bhakta tāṅra adhiṣṭhāna*  
*bhaktera hṛdaye kṛṣṇera satata viśrāma*

**“A pure devotee constantly engaged in the loving service of the Lord is identical with the Lord, who is always seated in his heart.” (Cc.** ***Ādi,*** **1.61)**

Being fully absorbed in the transcendental pastimes of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu and being a soul thoroughly surrendered to the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Śrī Kṛṣṇa, we understand that everything emanating from your lotus mouth is the Supreme Absolute Truth. The Lord, being situated within your heart as the Supersoul, reveals perfect absolute knowledge to you from within. We must always pray for the desire to hear from you and to execute your instructions without restriction.

Although we do not deserve your mercy and are so offensive in our actions, you are still benedicting us with your immeasurable loving kindness and blessing us with the most valuable jewel, the *mahā-mantra: Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa, Hare Hare/ Hare Rāma, Hare Rāma, Rāma Rāma, Hare Hare.*

We are humbly thanking you and prostrate ourselves at your lotus feet on this holiest of all holy days. May we please you with our lives and always strive to increase our service to your transcendental feet just as you are always increasing the ecstasies of your beloved Gurudeva Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Gosvāmī Mahārāja Prabhupāda.

Your eternal servants,
