# Śuddha-nāma Bhajana

## Foreword

This book is compiled from lectures by the great Bhakta Bhagavata Śrī Śrīmad Gour Govinda Swami Mahārāja, a swan-like personality, whose only aim in life was to inculcate and spread the teachings of Mahāprabhu to one and all. Personalities like His Divine Grace are rare agents of the Lord's mercy in this world. Such Bhakta Bhagavatas are pure devotees who are absorbed in *bhakti-rasa*, the mellows of loving devotion and are themselves the epitome of humility.

"If you want to understand *Śrīmad-Bhagavatam* you must approach a self realized Vaiṣṇava and study it under his guidance while at the same time taking exclusive shelter of the lotus feet of Lord Caitanya." (*Cc. Antya* [5.131](https://vedarama.com/en/books/cc-antya/5.131))

Developing these qualities will help us on our path of *hari-bhajana*! Śrīmān Mahāprabhu's main teaching was one of humility and tolerance. His Divine Grace Gour Govinda Swami Mahārāja, as a true follower of Śrīmān Mahāprabhu, showed by his own life great humility, tolerance, patience and perseverance for the service of the Lord.

We hope that all who read this book will greatly benefit from it.

## The Importance of the Pañca-tattva

The *ācāryas* have shown this path; to pay obeisances, crores and crores of obeisances to the dear devotees of Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu and beg for their mercy, thereby one will be able to understand and also describe the transcendental pastimes of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

*ei ta’ kahila pañca-tattvera vyākhyāna*  
*ihāra śravaṇe haya caitanya-tattva-jñāna*

**“I thus explain the truth of the Pañca-tattva. One who hears this explanation increases in knowledge of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.”**

In the purport, my revered spiritual master Śrīla Prabhupāda has said,

**“The Pañca-tattva is a very important factor in understanding Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. There are** ***sahajiyās*** **who not knowing the importance of the Pañca-tattva, concoct their own slogans such as** ***bhaja nitāi-gaura*****,** ***rādhe-śyāma*****,** ***japa hare kṛṣṇa hare rāma*** **or** ***śrī kṛṣṇa caitanya prabhu nityānanda hare kṛṣṇa hare rāma śrī rādhe-govinda*****. Such chants may be good poetry, but they cannot help us to go forward in devotional service.”**

These are concocted slogans by the *sahajiyās* and this is not prescribed or accepted by bona fide *ācāryas* in this Caitanya school of thought, therefore we should not do it. The previous *ācāryas* have not done so—*mahājano yena gataḥ sa panthāḥ*; Mahāprabhu has not done so, He chanted the Hare Krsna *mahā-mantra*.

*hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare*  
*hare rāma hare rāma rāma rāma hare hare*

*prabhu bale-kahilāṅa ei mahā-mantra*  
*iha japa giyā sabe kariyā nirbandha*

*iha haite sarva-siddhi haibe sabāra*  
*sarva-kṣaṇa bala ’ithe vidhi nāhi āra*

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said,

**“I say, this is the** ***mahā-mantra*****:** ***Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*****.”**

Mahāprabhu is the supreme authority, He is non-different from Kṛṣṇa and He has said, “Chant this Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* with firm faith, full *nistha*; *iha japa giyā sabe kariyā nirbandha*, *iha haite sarva-siddhi haibe sabara*, *sarva-ksana bala* ‘*ithe vidhi nahi ara*. There are no regulations at all; at any time, any place you may chant *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*, and you can achieve all perfection.” The Gosvāmīs headed by Rūpa Gosvāmī have said this and they are all bona fide *ācāryas*, and therefore bona fide authorities.

## Chant Hare Kṛṣṇa Loudly

*hare kṛṣṇety uccaiḥ sphurita-rasano nāma-gaṇanā-*  
*kṛta-granthi-śreṇī-subhaga-kaṭi-sūtrojjvala-karaḥ*  
*viśālākṣo dīrghārgala-yugala-khelāñcita-bhujaḥ*  
*sa caitanyaḥ kiṁ me punar api dṛśor yāsyati padam*

**“Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu chants the Hare Kṛṣṇa** ***mantra*** **in a loud voice. The holy name is dancing on His tongue as He counts the number of recitations with His effulgent hand. His eyes are large and His long arms, bending as He performs His pastimes, reach down to His knees. Will He again become visible before the path of my eyes?”**

Rūpa Gosvāmī has said in his *Caitanyāṣtaka*, in the fifth verse that Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu was chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* loudly. The *sahajiy*ā*s* say that Mahāprabhu was not chanting loudly.

*śrī-caitanya-mano-’bhīṣṭaṁ*  
*sthāpītaṁ yena bhū-tale*  
*svayaṁ rūpaḥ kadā mahyaṁ*  
*dadāti sva-padāntikam*

**Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī was especially empowered, as Mahāprabhu had instructed him and then sent him to Vṛndāvana.**

*Śrī-caitanya-mano-’bhīṣṭaṁ*; Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī has established the desire of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu on this planet earth. *Svayaṁ rūpaḥ kadā mahyaṁ dadāti sva-padāntikam*. One should beg for mercy at the lotus feet of Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī. He first established the desire of Mahāprabhu on this planet. He is an authority. He has said that Śrīmān Mahāprabhu was chanting the *mahā-mantra* loudly, but the *sahajiyās* say, “No, Mahāprabhu was not chanting loudly.” Nonetheless, they are not in the position of authority as Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī is.

*hare kṛṣṇety uccaiḥ sphurita-rasano nāma-gaṇanā-*  
*kṛta-granthi-śreṇī-subhaga-kaṭi-sūtrojjvala-karaḥ*  
*viśālākṣo dīrghārgala-yugala-khelāñcita-bhujaḥ*  
*sa caitanyaḥ kiṁ me punar api dṛśor yāsyati padam*

This is the fifth verse of Rūpa Gosvāmīs *Caitanyāṣtaka*.

**“Caitanya Mahāprabhu will bless that person who chants the Hare Kṛṣṇa** ***mahā-mantra*** **loudly, He will give his mercy to that person who has the Hare Kṛṣṇa** ***mahā-mantra*** **dancing on his tongue! His left hand is placed on His hip and His right hand stretched. Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu has features that are transcendental. He has large eyes and long arms. If He would kindly give His** ***darśana*** **to me, so that my eyes can see.”**

He was always chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* loudly, all the *ācāryas* have said this.

*hare kṛṣṇeti mantra-pratīka-grahaṇaṁ*  
*ṣoḍaśa-nāmātmanā dvātriṁśad-akṣareṇa*  
*mantreṇoccair-uccāritena*  
*sphurita kṛta-nṛtyā rasanā jihvā yasya saḥ*

**“When the sixteen names and thirty-two syllables of the Hare Kṛṣṇa** ***mantra*** **are loudly vibrated, Kṛṣṇa Himself dances on one’s tongue.”**

## One Should Not Chant Concocted Mantras

Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa Prabhu, who is a Gauḍīya Vedānta *ācārya*, has written:

*hare kṛṣṇeti mantra-pratīka-grahaṇaṁ*  
*ṣoḍaśa-nāmātmanā dvātriṁśad-akṣareṇa*  
*mantreṇoccair-uccāritena*  
*sphurita kṛta-nṛtyā rasanā jihvā yasya saḥ*

The Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* has sixteen-names—*Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*—and each name consists of two syllables. This equals thirty-two syllables; sixteen-names equals thirty-two syllables, this is the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*. Everyone should chant this Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*. Śrīla Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa has cautioned, one should not chant any concocted *mantra*. In the *Kali-santaraṇa Upaniṣad* it is said that the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* is the *tāraka-brahma-mantra* for the age of Kali-yuga.

*hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa*  
*kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare*  
*hare rāma hare rāma*  
*rāma rāma hare hare*

*ṣoḍaśaitāni nāmāni*  
*dvātriṁśad varṇakāni hi*  
*kalau yuge mahā-mantraḥ*  
*sammato jīva-tāraṇe*

*varjayitvā tu nāmaitad*  
*durjanaiḥ parikalpitam*  
*chando-baddhaṁ susiddhānta-*  
*viruddhaṁ nābhyaset padam*

*tārakaṁ brahma-nāmaitad*  
*brahmaṇā guruṇādinā*  
*kali-santaraṇādyāsu*  
*śruti-svadhigataṁ hareḥ*

*prāptaṁ śrī-brahma-śiṣyeṇa*  
*śrī-nāradena dhīmatā*  
*nāmaitad uttamaṁ śrauta-*  
*pāramparyeṇa brahmaṇaḥ*

*utsṛjyaitan-mahā-mantraṁ*  
*ye tvanyat kaepitaṁ padam*  
*mahā-nāmeti gāyanti*  
*te śāstra-guru-laṅghanaḥ*

*tattva-virodha-sanpṛktaṁ*  
*tādṛśaṁ daurjanaṁ matam*  
*sravathā parihāryaṁ syād*  
*ātmā-hitārthinā sadā*

*hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa*  
*kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare*  
*hare rāma hare rāma*  
*rāma rāma hare hare*

This *mantra* consisting of sixteen-names, thirty-two syllables, is the prescribed *mahā-mantra* for the Kali-yuga. It is given in all the *Vedas* and is meant for the deliverance of all living entities in the Kali-yuga.

It is not prescribed by the *mahājanas* and *ācāryas* to concoct other slogans such as *nitāi-gaura*, *rādhe-śyāma*, *hare kṛṣṇa hare rāma,* or *śrī kṛṣṇa caitanya prabhu nityānanda hare kṛṣṇa hare rāma śrī rādhe govinda*. No one should chant these concocted slogans.

This Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* is the *brahma-mantra* for the Kali-yuga. Brahmā, the original *guru,* has said, ‘The *śrutis* have declared this *mantra* to be the best means of deliverance in the age of Kali.’ Then Brahmās disciple Nārada, Brahmās sons and other disciples directly received this *mahā-mantra* and attained perfection.

Therefore this *mahā-mantra* comes in the bona fide disciplic succession. There is no question of concoction at all. Those who do not chant this *mahā-mantra*, and chant some concocted slogan are not following the previous *ācāryas* and the *śāstra*. They have deviated from the path of the *ācārya paramparā*, *śāstra* and *guru*, they are *durjanas*. Their *guru* has not instructed them in this way, therefore why are they concocting such ideas and where does this mental concoction come from? And they praise themselves. As my Guru Mahārāja has said, “Such chants may be good poetry, but they cannot help us to go forward in devotional service.” Their *guru* comes in a bona fide disciplic succession, their *guru* has not said this, so why do they do it? *Śāstra-guru laṅghanaḥ*—they have transgressed their *guru* and the *śāstra*. They are rascals—*durjanas*. Hence one should not associate with such persons and one should not chant these concocted slogans.

## Tāraka-brahma-nāma

*hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa*  
*kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare*  
*hare rāma hare rāma*  
*rāma rāma hare hare*

*iti soḍaśakaṁ nāmnāṁ*  
*kali-kalmaṣa-nāśanam*  
*nātaḥ parataropāyaḥ*  
*sarva-vedeṣu dṛśyate*

**This Hare Kṛṣṇa** ***mahā-mantra*****, consisting of sixteen-names and thirty-two syllables, is available in all the Vedas. The** ***Kali-santaraṇa Upaniṣad*** **is one of the Vedas and such concocted slogans are not available in the Vedas. One should not follow these rascals and chant these concocted names.**

This is most important and one should understand that in every *yuga* there is a *tāraka-brahma-nāma*; and in the Kali yuga the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* is:

*hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa*  
*kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare*  
*hare rāma hare rāma*  
*rāma rāma hare hare*

In the other *yugas* there are different *tāraka-brahma-nāmas*. In the Satya-yuga the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* is:

*nārāyaṇa parā vedā*  
*nārāyaṇa parākṣarā*  
*nārāyaṇa parā muktir*  
*nārāyaṇa-parā gatiḥ*

In the Tretā-yuga the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* is:

*rāma nārāyaṇānanta*  
*mukunda madhusūdana*  
*kṛṣṇa keśava kaṁsāre*  
*hare vaikuṇṭha vāmana*

In the Dvāpara-yuga the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* is:

*hare murāre madhu-kaiṭabhāre*  
*gopāla govinda mukunda śaure*  
*yajñeṣa nārāyaṇa kṛṣṇa viṣṇoṣ*  
*nirāśrayaṁ maṁ jagadīśa rakṣa*

They are all prescribed in the *śāstra*—the Vedic literature. These are the different *tāraka-brahma-nāmas* prescribed in all the *yugas* and this Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* is prescribed for this *yuga*—the Kali-yuga. This Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Kali-yuga and you should know this. As a Vaiṣṇava, someone may ask you, what is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Satya-yuga? What is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Tretā-yuga and what is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Dvāpara-yuga? If this question is asked of you, then you should know the answer. Otherwise, how can you become a Vaiṣṇava and how can you defeat them? You should understand it, you should know it and write it down.

*nārāyaṇa parā vedā*  
*nārāyaṇa parākṣarā*  
*nārāyaṇa parā muktir*  
*nārāyaṇa-parā gatiḥ*

The purport behind it is—Nārāyaṇa is the abode of all *vijñana, mukti (*liberation), and the ultimate goal. This is *aiśvarya-gatah-n*ā*ma*; it is *aiśvarya,* not *mādhurya*, it is all opulence. Nārāyaṇa is *aiśvarya-gatah-nāma-nārāyaṇa*. *Kṛṣṇa-nama* is *mādhurya*, which is only available in the Kali-yuga. In the Tretā-yuga, the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* is:

*rāma nārāyaṇānanta*  
*mukunda madhusūdana*  
*kṛṣṇa keśava kaṁsāre*  
*hare vaikuṇṭha vāmana*

What is the purport in it? The *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Satya-yuga only gives *śānta* and *dāsya-bhāva*. No other *bhāva,* mellow, only *śānta* and *dāsya*. But in the Tretā-yuga, the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* gives *dāsya* (servitorship) and some glimpse of friendship, *sakhya-bhāva*—*sakhya-nāma*. This Treta-yuga *tāraka-brahma-nāma* is also *aiśvarya gatah*, all the names of Nārāyaṇa are there and here it is said, multifarious—*vikrama*, Lord Vāmana—*trivikrama*. Lord Vāmana exhibited three types of *vikrama*, He manifested three wonderful potencies, known as *trivikrama*—Vāmana.

The Dvāpara-yuga’s *tāraka-brahma-nāma* is:

*hare murāre madhu-kaiṭabhāre*  
*gopāla govinda mukunda śaure*  
*yajñeṣa narayaṇa kṛṣṇa viṣṇoṣ*  
*niraśrayaṁ maṁ jagadīśa rakṣa*

Here you will find *śanta*, *dasya*, *sakhya* and *vatsalya—*these four mellows, and *niraśrayaṁ maṁ jagadīśa rakṣa*. Taking complete shelter at the lotus feet of Jagadīśa, the Supreme Lord of the Universe, and no other shelter.

The Kali-yuga—*tāraka-brahma-nāma* is:

*hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa*  
*kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare*  
*hare rāma hare rāma*  
*rāma rāma hare hare*

This is *mādhurya*, it is the best of all, it has such great potential and it is so powerful. It is *mādhurya*, there is no *aiśvarya* at all. In all the names in the other *yugas* you will find there is *aiśvarya*, but here in the Kali-yuga—*hari-nama*: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*; this is completely *mādhurya-nama*, all the mellows are there. Thus Mahāprabhu chanted this: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. In the names of all other *yugas* you will find this *vikrama* and *mukti*, but here in the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* for the Kali-yuga you will only find *prema*, only *prema*. That is said: *bhakti-pade-mukti-dasi*—*mukti* is the maidservant standing at the doorstep of *prema-bhakti*. Those who are really intelligent persons, those who only desire to accept the essence, chant this *mantra*: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. One can chant in any place, at any time and there is no restriction at all.

*nāmnām akāri bahudhā nija-sarva-śaktis*  
*tatrārpitā niyamitaḥ smaraṇe na kālaḥ*  
*etādṛśī tava kṛpā bhagavan mamāpi*  
*durdaivam īdṛśam ihājani nānurāgaḥ*

**“O my Lord, Your holy name alone can render all benediction to living beings, and thus You have hundreds and millions of names, like Kṛṣṇa and Govinda. In these transcendental names You have invested all Your transcendental energies. There are not even hard and fast rules for chanting these names. O my Lord, out of kindness You enable us to easily approach You by Your holy names, but I am so unfortunate that I have no attraction for them.”**

Mahaprābhu has said, there is no restriction at all, in any place and at any time one can chant. It never waits for *dīkṣā*. One can achieve liberation by securing *nāmābhāsa*. It is so powerful. This Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*, the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Kali-yuga, is all *mādhurya*. Most important is that this is the best *mantra* of all *yugas*. If you have this name, it is *mādhurya-pāra*, but without accepting a bona fide *guru*, a *gaura-priya-jana*—a dear devotee of Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu—one cannot aquire it. One may reach the *nāmābhāsa* stage, Ajamila achieved that stage. Then Ajamila associated with a *sādhu*, accepted *sādhu-guru,* then he achieved it. Therefore, this is the most important accomplishment. You should know the *tāraka-brahma-nāmas* in the different ages—*yugas*, otherwise how can you defeat bogus philosophies?

## Mādhurya-tāraka-brahma-nāma

When you say that this is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Kali-yuga, then people will ask, “What is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Satya-yuga? What is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Tretā-yuga and what is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in the Dvāpara-yuga?” If you do not know this, you will look like a fool and therefore you should not preach. The people will not listen, therefore if you can quote what is the *tāraka-brahma-nāma* in each age—and that this is the best of all the *tāraka-brahma-nāmas*, in the Kali-yuga, because it is *mādhurya-brahma-nāma*, whereas the other *tāraka-brahma-nāmas* are not *mādhurya-tāraka-brahma-nāma*, they are *aiśvarya-brahma-nāma*—then people will listen.

In the Satya-yuga this is completely *aiśvarya-pāra*—*nārāyaṇa-pāra*. In the Satya-yuga there is only *śanta* and *dāsya* mellow. In the Treta-yuga there is *śanta*, *dāsya* with just a glimpse of *sakhya,* and in the Dvāpara-yuga it becomes more; *śanta*, *dāsya*, *sakhya* and *vātsalya*. Then completely surrendering; *jagadīśa rakṣa nirāśrayaṁ mām*—completely taking shelter at the lotus feet of Jagadīśa, then it is complete surrender.

## This Is a Special Kali-yuga

This *tāraka-brahma-nāma* of the Kali-yuga is *mādhurya*, it gives *prema*, and all the *rasas* are included in this *tāraka-brahma-nāma*. *Mādhurya* means all the other *rasas* are included. This is the best of all the *tāraka-brahma-nāmas*. The people of the Kali-yuga are very, very fortunate that they can have this *prema*. In the other *yugas* they cannot attain this *prema*. Therefore the people of other *yugas* want to take birth in the Kali-yuga and attain this *prema*. If you cannot attain *prema,* then your life is spoiled because the opportunity is given. Though the Kali-yuga is the worst of all *yugas*, it has this special quality, Mahāprabhu Himself has appeared, and therefore it is a special Kali-yuga. Those who are born in this Kali-yuga are most fortunate. Mahāprabhu does not appear in all Kali-yugas. Therefore they are most fortunate, those who are born in this Kali-yuga, even a dog is fortunate.

Every day he hears the chanting of Hare Kṛṣṇa and takes the remnants of all the Vaiṣṇavas.

## Chapter 1 - Nāma-aparādha

## The Ten Offences against the Holy Name

(1) sādhu-nindā  
(*satāṁ nindā nāmnaḥ paramam aparādhaṁ vitanute yataḥ khyātiṁ yātaṁ katham u sahate tad-vigarhām*)  
To blaspheme the *sādhu*.

(2) anya deva saha viṣṇu manana  
(*śivasya śrī-viṣṇor ya iha guna-nāmādi-sakalaṁ dhiyā bhinnaṁ paśyet sa khalu hari-nāmāhita-karaḥ*)  
To consider the names of the demigods like Lord Śiva or Lord Brahmā to be equal to, or independant of, the name of Lord Viṣṇu.

(3) guru-vākya avajñā  
(*guror avajñā*)  
To disobey the order of the *nāma-tattva-vit sādhu-guru.*

(4) śruti-śāstra-nindā  
(*śruti-śāstra-nindanaṁ*)  
To blaspheme the Vedic literatures.

(5) nāme arthavāda  
(*tathārtha-vādaḥ*)  
To give some interpretation on the holy name of the Lord.

(6) nāma bale pāpa-buddhi  
(*nāmno balād yasya hi pāpa-buddhir na vidyate tasya yamair hi śuddhiḥ*)  
To commit sinful activities on the strength of the holy name of the Lord.

(7) śraddhā hīna jane nāma upadeṣa  
(*aśraddadhāne vimukhe ‘py aśṛṇvati yaś copadeśaḥ śiva-nāmāparādhaḥ*)  
To instruct a faithless person about the glories of the holy name.

(8) anya śubha-karma saha hari-nāma samāna  
(*dharma-vrata-tyāga-hutādi-sarva-śubha-kriyā-sāmyam api pramādaḥ*)  
To consider the chanting of Hare Kṛṣṇa as one of the auspicious ritualistic activities offered in the *Vedas* as fruitive activities, *karma-kāṇḍa*.

(9) nāma grahaṇa samaye asāvadhānatā  
(*anavadhānatā*)  
To be inattentive while chanting.

(10) ‘ahaṁ-mama’ buddhi tyāga na kariba  
*(śrute ‘pi nāma-māhātmye yaḥ prīti-rahito naraḥ ahaṁ-mamādi-paramo nāmni so ‘py aparādha-krt)*  
To not have complete faith in the chanting of the holy names and to maintain material attachments, even after understanding so many instructions on this matter. (The ‘I’ and ‘mine’ mentality).

## Who Is a Vaiṣṇava and Who Is an Avaiṣṇava?

*avaiṣṇavopadiṣṭena*  
*mantreṇa nirayaṁ vrajet*  
*punaś ca vidhinā samyag*  
*grāhayed vaiṣṇavād guroḥ*

**“One who receives his** ***mantra*** **from a** ***guru*** **who is a non-devotee or who is addicted to sensual pleasure is doomed to a life in hell. Such a person must immediately approach a genuine Vaiṣṇava** ***guru*** **and again accept the** ***mantra*** **from him.”**

In the *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa*, Sanātana Gosvāmī has said: One should not receive a *mantra* from an *avaiṣṇava*. Who is an *avaiṣṇava*? One who is *strī-saṅgī*, attached to women, and who is not a devotee of Kṛṣṇa, he is an *avaiṣṇava*. If one takes their *mantra* from that *avaiṣṇava*, he goes to hell. Therefore, according to the *sādhu-śāstra-guru* principle, one should accept *mantra* from a bona fide Vaiṣṇava *guru*!

*nṛ-deham ādyaṁ su-labhaṁ su-durlabhaṁ*  
*plavaṁ su-kalpaṁ guru-karṇadhāram*  
*mayānukūlena nabhasvateritaṁ*  
*pumān bhavābdhiṁ na taret sa ātma-hā*

**“The human body, which can award all benefit in life, is automatically obtained by the laws of nature, although it is a very rare achievement. This human body can be compared to a perfectly constructed boat having the spiritual master as the captain and the instructions of the Personality of Godhead as favourable winds impelling it on its course. Considering all these advantages, a human being who does not utilize his human life to cross the ocean of material existence must be considered the killer of his own soul.”**

This human body, the human form of life is rarely attained. Still, it is a very good boat to cross over this material ocean of existence, therefore an expert navigator, *karṇadhāra*, should be placed on this boat and the bona fide Vaiṣṇava *guru* is that expert navigator. Furthermore, the favourable wind is the mercy of Kṛṣṇa that will help this boat to cross over this ocean of material existence. All these facilities have been offered to you when you received this human form of life. Still, if one does not make any effort to cross over this ocean of material existence, he commits suicide. So one should accept a bona fide spiritual master, *sad-guru*, and accept *mantra-dīkṣā* from that *sad-guru*. When the *guru* gives *mantra-dīkṣā,* he speaks about *nāma-aparādha*, the offences committed against chanting the Holy Name, and one should know what these *nāma-aparādhas* are!

## The Ten Offences

## (1) sādhu-nindā

(*satāṁ nindā nāmnaḥ paramam aparādhaṁ vitanute yataḥ khyātiṁ yātaṁ katham u sahate tad-vigarhām*)

To blaspheme the *sādhu*.

Number one offence is blaspheming a *sādhu, sādhu-nindā*. Who is a *sādhu*? One who is completely surrendered unto the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa is a *sādhu*. One who has dedicated his life to preach the message of Bhagavān Kṛṣṇa throughout the world is a Vaiṣṇava—is a *sādhu*. One should not blaspheme such a *sādhu*.

## (2) anya deva saha viṣṇu manana

(*śivasya śrī-viṣṇor ya iha guna-nāmādi-sakalaṁ dhiyā bhinnaṁ paśyet sa khalu hari-nāmāhita-karaḥ*)

To consider the names of the demigods like Lord Śiva or Lord Brahmā to be equal to or independent of the name of Lord Viṣṇu.

To think that the demigods are the same as Bhagavān and to think that the name of Kṛṣṇa, the form of Kṛṣṇa, the qualities and pastimes of Kṛṣṇa are different from Kṛṣṇa. If someone thinks Lord Śiva and Lord Brahmā are also Bhagavān, that is an offence. If someone thinks the name of Lord Śiva and the name of Lord Brahmā are also equal with the name of Lord Viṣṇu or Kṛṣṇa, or chanting the name of Lord Śiva or Lord Brahmā are as good as chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa, that is also an offence. A *bhakta,* a devotee, a Vaiṣṇava should not think like this. So it is said:

*anya deva saha viṣṇu ke ye māne*  
*se baḍa ajñānī īśa-tattva nāhi jāne*

*a jada jagate viṣṇu parameśvara*  
*girīśādi yata deva tāṅhāra kiṅkara*

*vāsudeva chāḍi yei anya deva bhaje*  
*īśvara chāḍiyā sei saṁsāra te maje*

In the *śastra* it is said that Lord Viṣṇu or Kṛṣṇa is *parameśvara.* The demigods, even Lord Śiva and Brahmā, are all servants of Lord Kṛṣṇa. If Lord Kṛṣṇa, Viṣṇu, is worshiped, automatically all the demigods are worshiped and no separate worship is necessary. Then the question arises, how should one deal or behave with other demigods? Should we disrespect them?

*yata tata anya deva karena darśana*  
*kṛṣṇa-dāsa bali tāre karena vandana*

A Vaiṣṇava should not disrespect any demigod. They are all servants of Kṛṣṇa or Viṣṇu. They are all carrying out the orders of Lord Kṛṣṇa, Viṣṇu. In that way they are all Vaiṣṇavas. Though not pure Vaiṣṇavas, still they are Vaiṣṇavas. So a Vaiṣṇava devotee should not disrespect any demigod, rather, pay respect!

## (3) guru-vākya avajñā

(*guror avajña*)

To disobey the order of the *nāma-tattva-vit sādhu-guru.*

The third offence is to disobey the order of the *nāma-tattva-vit guru*. This is a very great offence. Other offences may be excused. There are some means, some ways, but there is no excuse to disobey the order or instruction of the *guru*. So this is the most important and very severe offence, a very great offence. One should take it seriously and understand it seriously. Therefore one should not consider such a *guru* as an ordinary human being.

*ācāryaṁ māṁ vijānīyān*  
*nāva-manyeta karhicit*  
*na martya-buddhyāsūyeta*  
*sarva-deva-mayo guruḥ*

**“One should know the** ***ācārya*** **as Myself and never disrespect him in any way. One should not envy him, thinking him an ordinary man, for he is the representative of all the demigods.”**

This is the lesson of Lord Kṛṣṇa. One should not think that an *acarya* or a *guru* is an ordinary human being. *Mat-svarūpa*, he is the manifestation of Lord Kṛṣṇa and all the demigods are there in the person of *guru*:

*gurute avajñā yāra tāra aparādha*  
*se aparādhe tāra haye bhakti-badha*

*gurute acala śraddhā kare yei jana*  
*śuddha-nama bale pāya sei prema-dhana*

*sad-guru prāpti ye avajñā ācāre*  
*se pāpiṣṭha aparādhī sarvatra saṁsare*

*sad-guru aparādha ati bhayaṅkara*  
*ei aparādhe naṣṭa haya deva nara*

It is stated in the *śāstra* that one should develop unalloyed *bhakti*, devotion, towards Kṛṣṇa and similar unalloyed devotion towards *guru*. Those who have developed unalloyed devotion towards Kṛṣṇa and *guru* should easily cultivate or have pure devotion—*śuddha-bhakti*. They will very easily go back home, back to Godhead. Therefore one should be very, very careful to accept a *sad-guru*—bona fide *guru*—pure devotee of Kṛṣṇa, *śuddha-bhakte laibena guru-rupe basi*. It is said, accept a *śuddha-bhakta*, pure devotee of Kṛṣṇa, as *guru.* Before accepting *guru,* one should be very careful, otherwise he will be cheated, he will accept a cheater or a hypocrite. So one should not disobey the order, instruction or direction of the bona fide *guru*. That is a very serious offence.

## (4) śruti-śāstra-nindā

(*śruti-śāstra-nindanaṁ*)

To blaspheme the Vedic literatures.

Then the fourth offence is, *śruti-śāstra-nindā*, blaspheming the *śruti-śāstra*, the Vedic scriptures.

*māyā-baddha jīve kṛṣṇa bahu kṛpā kari*  
*veda-purāṇādi dila ārya jñāna dhari*

*māyā-mugdha jīvera nāhi svataḥ kṛṣṇa-jñāna*  
*jīvere kṛpāya kailā kṛṣṇa veda-purāṇa*

**“It is said, the conditioned soul has forgotten Kṛṣṇa, so out of mercy, causeless mercy, Kṛṣṇa has given this Vedic literature. One should not blaspheme such literature or** ***śāstra*****!”**

## (5) nāme arthavāda

(*tathārtha-vādaḥ*)

To give some interpretation on the holy name of the Lord.

*Name arthavāda* means to interpret this *kṛṣṇa-nāma*, *hari-nāma,* in different ways. For example, one who is an offender may say, “Kṛṣṇa means black.” In the *abhidhāna* (dictionary) this meaning is there. This is an offence.

Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Absolute Truth. One should not interpret it in different ways or give different meanings to it. That is an offence. Some also say that “In the *śāstra*, the glories of *hari-nāma* are given and this is an exaggeration. Only to develop some attraction or taste towards *hari-nāma,* this has been given in *śāstra,* but it is not really true, it is an exaggeration.” This is an offence. Those who have developed any doubt, *saṁśaya,* in the holy name, automatically commit an offence. One should not cherish any doubt in it. The name of Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa are non-different—*abhinnatvān nāma-nāminoḥ*, and especially in this age of Kali, there is no other incarnation of Kṛṣṇa. There is only one incarnation, the name incarnation of Kṛṣṇa—*kali-kāle nāma-rūpe kṛṣṇa-avatāra*. This is the *śāstric* injunction, *siddhānta*—conclusion! It is given in all Vedic scriptures, and especially Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, who is Kṛṣṇa Himself, who came as a devotee, as an *ācārya*, has said this. If someone commits such an offence—that means someone has doubt in this holy name of Kṛṣṇa—and those who also give different interpretations to the holy name, they are great offenders, and if by chance you meet such a person and talk with such a person, that becomes an offence. To counteract this offence, one should immediately take bath in the river Ganges. If there is no river Ganges, one should take bath in any holy water. We invoke Ganges and immediately take bath, otherwise that offence will not be counteracted.

## (6) nāma bale pāpa-buddhi

(*nāmno balād yasya hi pāpa-buddhir na vidyate tasya yamair hi śuddhiḥ*)

To commit sinful activities on the strength of the holy name of the Lord.

To commit sinful activities on the strength of the holy name of the Lord, *eka kṛṣṇa-nāme kare sarva-pāpa kṣaya*. It is said in the *śāstra,* one name of Kṛṣṇa has such great potential that it can destroy innumerable sins accumulated from lakhs of lives. So potent! One name of Kṛṣṇa, *eka kṛṣṇa-nāme kare sarva-pāpa kṣaya*. If someone says or thinks, ‘This is a very good weapon that we have now, I will chant Hare Kṛṣṇa and destroy all sinful reaction; then I will commit some new sins, then I will again chant Hare Kṛṣṇa and destroy it’. This mentality is *nāma bale pāpa-buddhi*, that means committing sins on the strength of chanting *hari-nāma*. This is an offence. One should not develop such a mentality.

Those who accept a bona fide *guru* and are initiated by him, taking *hari-nāma*, they should not in thought, in the mind, think of any sin, one should be very careful. Though in the Kali-yuga there is a concession; that in the Kali-yuga by thinking of sinful activity or sinful engagements in the mind one will not get the sinful reaction unless he executes that sin. In the Kali-yuga, if someone in the mind just thinks that I must accept a bona fide spiritual master, *guru*, and take *hari-nāma* from him, just by thinking he attains good results. Kṛṣṇa who is seated as the Paramātmā, the Supersoul, in the heart, immediately starts to shower mercy upon him. Those who are initiated, or taking initiation, they should be very, very careful not to think of anything sinful in the mind. Of course the mind is very, very wicked! Unconsciously it also thinks something, but one should take it seriously that, henceforth, from this moment, I shall not allow the wicked mind to think of anything sinful. Kṛṣṇa, who is in the heart as the Paramātmā, knows how serious one is and if one has really developed faith in the holy name. He knows the heart, this is most important. If there is nothing in the heart, but consciously it enters into the mind and immediately he becomes conscious, “O, this wicked mind does such nonsense,” immediately he chants Hare Kṛṣṇa and prays from the core of his heart to Kṛṣṇa and the holy name to excuse him, then he is excused, but it should not be done repeatedly.

In this respect, those who are not devotees or *sādhus* and those who are hypocrites, outwardly posing as devotees or Vaiṣṇavas but inside they are not so, they are hypocrites, cheaters. By the association of such persons, such cheaters, such hypocrites, those outwardly posing as Vaiṣṇavas, so-called *bhaktas*, chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa but inwardly they are not so, they are cheaters, they are hypocrites, it happens, that the thought of sinful activity enters into the mind. If one is careful in this regard not to associate with such a person who will enable him to commit such an offence, he may avoid such an offence, otherwise it is very difficult.

## (7) śraddhā hīna jane nāma upadeṣa

(*aśraddadhāne vimukhe ‘py aśṛṇvati yaś copadeśaḥ śiva-nāmāparādhaḥ*)

To instruct a faithless person about the glories of the holy name.

It is an offence to present the glories of the holy name to a person who has not developed full faith in *nāma*, full faith in Kṛṣṇa, or full faith in His holy name. One should not speak the glories of the holy name to that person, that will be an offence, *śraddhā hīna jane nāma upadeṣa. O*nly that person who has developed full faith in the name is qualified or eligible to accept the holy name. Therefore, this is the duty of one who will instruct or teach about the holy name, it is for him. Therefore for him, first he should chant this holy name loudly: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*.

By chanting loudly, one can inculcate *śraddhā* or faith into a person who has no *śraddhā.* This is the first thing, do not speak anything about the glory of the name. Do not speak, only chant loudly, do loud *kīrtana*, let that person hear this transcendental sound vibration. This is most important! If it touches the ear of such a person or persons, they will gradually develop *śraddhā*. When Śrīla Prabhupāda went to the West, he did it. He only took a pair of *karatālas* and would go to some park where the hippies were rolling, with drugs, intoxicated. Śrīla Prabhupāda only chanted Hare Kṛṣṇa: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*.

He filled the whole atmosphere with transcendental sound vibration and it is a natural attraction. Kṛṣṇa is all-attractive and His holy name is also all-attractive because His name is not different from Kṛṣṇa, so it has natural attraction. Therefore those hippies were automatically attracted. In this way he inculcated *śraddhā* (faith) in them, and then he instructed them. This is the way, otherwise without this process in place, if you speak to a faithless person about the glories of the holy name, you will commit an offence.

## (8) anya śubha-karma saha hari-nāma samāna

(*dharma-vrata-tyāga-hutādi-sarva-śubha-kriyā-sāmyam api pramādaḥ*)

To consider the chanting of Hare Kṛṣṇa as one of the auspicious ritualistic activities offered in the *Vedas* as fruitive activities, *karma-kāṇḍa*.

The name of Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa are non-different. As Kṛṣṇa is absolute, His name is also absolute. No one is equal with Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa is supreme, the Supreme Personality of Godhead—the Absolute Truth. Similarly, nothing is equal with His holy name. Therefore, if someone thinks that the chanting of Hare Kṛṣṇa is as good as performing any other auspicious activity, he commits an offence.

Generally, *māyāvādīs* (impersonalists) think like this. So if someone associates with a *māyāvādī*, he will naturally commit this offence.

## (9) nāma grahaṇa samaye asāvadhānatā

(*anavadhānatā*)

To be inattentive while chanting.

While chanting this *mahā-mantra*, with your beads given by *guru* at the time of initiation, one should not be inattentive. You should fix your mind on Kṛṣṇa. That means, when we instruct, chant sixteen rounds minimum daily with these beads, this chanting should be done very carefully, with great attention, placing your attention on Kṛṣṇa. The mind is naturally fluctuating and oscillating, it is natural. So in order to complete our rounds, we sometimes become inattentive and the mind is fluctuating, the mind is going here and chanting is going there, that is an offence. One should not do this! Therefore, one who has this nature, whose mind is very fluctuating, fleeting, oscillating, he should be very careful to finish his rounds as far as possible in the early morning period, *brāhma-muhūrta,* from 4:00 am to 6:00 am. That is a very suitable time. At that time the mind will not fluctuate so much, it will very easily concentrate, (at other times of the day, it must fluctuate) but during that time most people are not up from the bed, so no other vibration has come to the atmosphere, the atmosphere is very calm. Thus one can very easily concentrate his mind at that time between 4:00 am and 6:00 am; it is a very congenial period. As far as possible, complete many rounds, then you will not commit such offence. The rest of the rounds you may complete when all have gone to bed, when it is a calm atmosphere, no other thought will enter into the mind. So complete it and then go to bed. This is an easy way.

At noon-time, midday, 10:00 am—11:00 am—2:00 pm, it is very difficult to chant. So this is an easy way to complete your rounds as far as possible during that 4:00 am to 6:00 am and you will feel very nice at that time. If you can chant nicely, that day you will feel very nice, and you can easily face *māyā* and counteract, otherwise you feel very uneasy, *māyā* will overcome you, that is a fact.

Also, if at first while chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa one cannot concentrate on the form of Kṛṣṇa, it is better to chant before the deities in the temple. Look at the deities and chant so that you can very easily concentrate. Or, chant before the photograph of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa and chant in the association of Vaiṣṇavas—*sādhus*. These are the processes, so that your mind will not fluctuate. And when by this practice the mind will become fixed, then there is no harm, you are safe. Such a person can chant in any place, because the mind is fixed, he is never disturbed by outward noise or anything, but as long as you have not achieved this stage, you should chant according to the above instructions.

## (10) ‘ahaṁ-mama’ buddhi tyāga na kariba

To not have complete faith in the chanting of the holy names and to maintain material attachments, even after understanding so many instructions on this matter. (The ‘I’ and ‘mine’ mentality).

I belong to Kṛṣṇa, I do not belong to myself. I belong to Kṛṣṇa. My body, my mind, my speech, everything that I can say is ‘mine’, belongs to Kṛṣṇa. Gurudeva, the spiritual master, repeats this message over and over again. All the *śāstras*, all the *ācāryas*, all the *mahājanas*, they have all said this repeatedly. So, in spite of listening to this repeatedly and being initiated by the *guru*, if someone says ‘I’ and ‘mine’, he commits an offence. That is the last offence, tenth offence.

## Please Give Me Kṛṣṇa

Kṛṣṇa belongs to you. I want Kṛṣṇa, I want Kṛṣṇa, I am very eager, I am very serious how to attain Kṛṣṇa.” So you are running after such a *guru*, “Please, O Gurudeva, O Vaiṣṇava Ṭhākura, please, please shower your mercy, give me Kṛṣṇa, give me. The slightest negligence, carelessness, will cause you to lose Kṛṣṇa. Mind this offence very carefully, otherwise Kṛṣṇa will disappear. Then you will cry, “O, I have lost Kṛṣṇa, where is Kṛṣṇa?” Then what shall be done? The *guru* is so kind, he showers his mercy and gives you Kṛṣṇa, take Kṛṣṇa! Take care to keep it nicely, very carefully, otherwise the slightest negligence will cause you to lose Kṛṣṇa. Be careful!

## Chapter 2 - Nāmābhāsa

If you are really very inquisitive and want to know how to understand the discussion that is going on, then Kṛṣṇa will help you. Mahāprabhu will help you to understand it, the language is not an obstacle at all. This is not a hindrance, and if your heart throbs to know what is going on, Kṛṣṇa within your heart will respond, “O, he is so inquisitive to understand but the language is a barrier for him, so he cannot understand.” Then He will help you to understand, therefore such inquisitiveness should be there, otherwise how will you receive Kṛṣṇa’s help? He is ready to help you, but you are not accepting. This is the whole trouble! He is ready to help you, but you are not accepting His help, you are not prepared to accept it. So, a hint is given here:

*jagat bhariyā loka bale—‘hari’ ‘hari’*  
*sei-kṣaṇe gaurakṛṣṇa bhūme avatari*

**“The whole world was pleased. While the Hindus chanted the holy name of the Lord, the non-Hindus, especially the Mohammedans jokingly imitated the words.”**

## Four Types of Nāmābhāsa

Jokingly; this is one of the *nāmābhāsa* types. There are four types of *nāmābhāsa*. Here in the purport Prabhupāda has said, “Although the Mohammedans or non-Hindus who have no interest in chanting the holy name of the Lord, the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*, while the Hindus in Navadvīpa chanted during the lunar eclipse, the Mohammedans imitated them. Thus the Hindus and Mohammedans joined together in chanting the holy name of the Lord when Srī Caitanya Mahāprabhu advented Himself.”

*sāṅketyaṁ pārihāsyaṁ vā*  
*stobhaṁ helanam eva vā*  
*vaikuṇṭha-nāma-grahaṇam*  
*aśeṣāgha-haraṁ viduḥ*

**“One who chants the holy name of the Lord is immediately freed from the reactions of unlimited sins, even if he chants indirectly \[to indicate something else\], jokingly, for musical entertainment, or even neglectfully. This is accepted by all the learned scholars of the scriptures.”**

## Sāṅketya-nāmābhāsa

The first type of *nāmābhāsa* is *sāṅketya*. *Sāṅketya* means that one does not directly address the Lord whilst chanting the name of the Lord. The example given is Ajāmila, he chanted the name of Nārāyaṇa whilst calling his son. Then three very fearful, fierce-looking Yamadūtas appeared to take him to Yamapura—the abode of Yamarāja. He was such a great sinful person and he had committed so much sin, there was no limit to it, and Citragupta, the record keeper of Yamarāja, had written it all down. Thus when these very fierce-looking Yamadūtas appeared there to take him, he became afraid. In that frightened state, he called out for his youngest son, whose name was Nārāyaṇa. “O, Nārāyaṇa, please come to my side.” He did not directly call Nārāyaṇa the Lord. This is *saṅketya.* He achieved that stage, *nāmābhāsa,* and immediately all his sinful reactions were destroyed. Immediately, as soon as he uttered the name of Nārāyaṇa, he did not commit *nāma-aparādha*. That is *nāma bale pāpa-buddhi.* One of the ten *nāma-aparādhas,* to commit sinful activities on the strength of chanting the holy name.

*eka kṛṣṇa-nāme kare sarva-pāpa kṣaya*  
*nava-vidhā bhakti pūrṇa nāma haite haya*

**“Simply by chanting the holy name of Kṛṣṇa, one is relieved from all the reactions of a sinful life. One can complete the nine processes of devotional service simply by chanting the holy name.”**

One name of Kṛṣṇa has great potency and it will destroy all sinful reactions. Someone may think, “All right, this is a very good weapon I have now. I am a very sinful person, and I have committed many, many sinful activities, so now I will chant: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*, and destroy all my sinful reactions. This is a very good weapon. Then I can commit new sins, then I can chant Hare Kṛṣṇa, and destroy them again.”

This mentality is an offensive mentality. One who develops this mentality, commits this *aparādha*—*nāma bale pāpa-buddhi*. If you commit sinful activities on the strength of chanting the holy name, it is *aparādha*. But Ajāmila did not commit this *aparādha*. He did not utter the name of Nārāyaṇa with this mentality. He uttered Nārāyaṇa just by calling his son, therefore he performed *sāṅketya-nāmābhāsa*. Immediately all his sinful reactions were destroyed and four Viṣṇudūtas appeared there with four-handed forms. They had weapons such as the club and disc.  
They shouted, ”Stop! Stop! Stop! You cannot touch him,” to the Yamadūtas.  
“You cannot touch him. If you touch,” they raised their clubs, “We will smash your head.”  
The Yamadūtas became amazed.  
“What is that?” they wondered.  
“Because he is not a sinful person now, all his sinful reactions are completely destroyed. You cannot take him to the abode of Yamarāja,” the Viṣṇudūtas replied.

That happened! This is the result of *nāmābhāsa*, what to speak of the chanting of the pure name. This is the result of *sāṅketya-nāmābhāsa*.

## Pārihāsya-nāmābhāsa

The second type of *nāmābhāsa* is *pārihāsya*. Jokingly! When the Hindus were chanting Hari, Hari, the Mohammedans were jokingly saying Hari, Hari, Hari. They were joining in the *kīrtana*, but jokingly—*pārihāsya*. Thereby they achieved the result of *nāmābhāsa*. That you will see in the description of Mahāprabhu’s return from Gayā, when He started the *saṅkīrtana* movement. When He performed *saṅkīrtana,* all the *nadīyā-vāsīs* (the inhabitants of Nadia) joined in the *saṅkīrtana*. The Mohammedan ruler, the Kazi, was not interested in this *hari-nāma-saṅkīrtana*, so he broke the *mṛdanga* and he sent his peon to stop the *saṅkīrtana*. Thus because of Mahāprabhu’s *kṛpā*, the peon came when the Hindus were performing *kīrtana*, chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa—Hari, Hari, Hari, Hari. He joked with them, and said, “Why are you chanting Hari, Hari, Hari, Hari? It means *chori*, *chori*. *Hari* means *chori*, *chori*—means stealing! You are chanting Hari, Hari, which means you will go to steal. Don’t chant Hari, Hari, or the Kazi will bind you up and put you in the prison house.” Jokingly, he said this. But by jokingly uttering Hari, he achieved the result of *nāmābhāsa*. So when he returned, his tongue was chanting Hari, Hari, Hari, Hari!

Then the Kazi asked his peon, “Why are you saying Hari, Hari, Hari, you are a Mohammedan?” The peon answered, “O my Lord, what shall I do? I had gone there to stop the Hindus chanting Hari, Hari. I said, ‘Why are you chanting Hari Hari? It means *chori*—stealing. The Kazi will put you in prison, don’t chant Hari, Hari.’ From that moment my tongue is chanting Hari, Hari. What shall I do? I cannot stop it! I am not willfully doing it, but my tongue is chanting Hari, Hari, Hari, Hari!”

This is *nāmābhāsa*, he gets *nāmābhāsa-phala*, the result of *nāmābhāsa*. This is *nāmābhāsa*, the second type of *nāmābhāsa*.

## Stobha-nāmābhāsa

The third type of *nāmābhāsa* is *stobha*, which means a musical performance or a dramatic performance. A musical performance is going on in which one person plays the part of Nārada Muni. “Nā-rā-ya-ṇa, Nā-rā-ya-ṇa, Nā-rā-ya-ṇa.” That person comes to the stage with the *vīṇā,* chanting, “Nā-rā-ya-ṇa, Nā-rā-ya-ṇa.” He is an actor. That is *stobha* and it means musical performance or a theatrical performance. It is not out of *bhakti* that he is doing it, he is just performing a role, yet he achieves the result of *nāmābhāsa*.

## Helana-nāmābhāsa

The fourth type of *nāmābhāsa* is *helana*. *Helana*—neglectfully chanting. These are the four types of *nāmābhāsa*.

## Nāma and Nāmābhāsa

The *Padma Purāṇa* says:

*nāmaikaṁ yasya vāci smaraṇa-patha-gataṁ śrotra-mulaṁ gataṁ vā*  
*śuddhaṁ vāśuddha-varṇaṁ vyavahita-rahitaṁ tārayaty eva satyam*  
*tac ced deha-draviṇa-janatā-lobha-pāṣāṇḍa-madhye*  
*nikṣiptaṁ syān na phala-janakaṁ śīghram evātra vipra*

**“If a person chants the holy name of the Lord once, or if it penetrates his mind or enters his ear, which is the channel of aural reception, that holy name will deliver him from material bondage, whether vibrated properly or improperly, with correct or incorrect grammar, and properly joined or vibrated in separate parts. O** ***brāhmaṇa*****, the potency of the holy name is therefore certainly great. However, if one uses the vibration of the holy name for the benefit of the material body, for material wealth and followers, or under the influence of greed or atheism—in other words, if one utters the name with offences—such chanting will not produce the desired result quickly. Therefore one should diligently avoid offences in chanting the holy name of the Lord.”**

There is a difference between name—*nāma* and *nāmābhāsa*. Name means the pure name. When Srīla Prabhupāda went to the Western world, the hippies were rolling—rolling around intoxicated. He chanted Hare Kṛṣṇa and the hippies woke up and flocked around Srīla Prabhupāda. Prabhupāda chanted and gave them *prasāda*. They said, “O very nice!” They relished the nice taste of Kṛṣṇa *prasāda*. Then they became attracted. Prabhupāda said, “Chant! Chant! Chant!” but being such sinful persons, they could not. Their tongue could not utter *hari-nāma*.

*ataḥ śrī-kṛṣṇa-nāmādi*  
*na bhaved grāhyam indriyaiḥ*  
*sevonmukhe hi jihvādau*  
*svayam eva sphuraty adaḥ*

**“No one can understand the transcendental nature of the name, form, quality and pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa through his materially contaminated senses. Only when one becomes spiritually saturated by transcendental service to the Lord are the transcendental name, form, quality and pastimes of the Lord revealed to him.”**

*ataeva kṛṣṇera ‘nāma’, ‘deha’, ‘vilāsa’,*  
*prākṛtendriya-grāhya nahe, haya sva-prakāśa*

**“The holy name of Kṛṣṇa, His body and His pastimes cannot be understood by the blunt material senses. They are manifest independently.”**

*Prākṛta-indriya*, that means material senses. The senses are material, they are not purified senses. *Prākṛta-indriya*, *jaḍa-indriya*. The material body and the material senses cannot utter the name. *Prākṛtendriya-grāhya nahe*, *haya sva-prakāśa*, the holy name of Kṛṣṇa is self manifested. The senses are material, they are not purified, they are impure senses. The material senses cannot utter the name.

The defective, material senses cannot help one chant the pure name. Therefore, under the guidance of a bona fide spiritual master, one is engaged in the chanting of Hare Kṛṣṇa and taking *kṛṣṇa-prasāda*, these are both activities of the tongue—*sevonmukhe hi jihvādau*. That means that the tongues service is to chant Hare Kṛṣṇa and take *kṛṣṇa-prasāda*. But by one’s own efforts he or she cannot fully realize the full potential of one’s own latent Kṛṣṇa consciousness.

Śrīla Prabhupāda is a bona fide pure devotee, he was chanting the pure name. That pure name, that pure sound vibration touched the ears of the hippies and they woke up, and then they flocked around Śrīla Prabhupāda. He was chanting and the transcendental sound vibration was touching their ears—*śrotra-mūla*. That pure transcendental vibration, the pure name, was being chanted by Śrīla Prabhupāda. Then Śrīla Prabhupāda advised the hippies, “Chant! Chant! Chant!” That is the order of a *gaura-priya*, a dear devotee, on whose order they started chanting. But they could not chant clearly, “Ha...re Kṛ...ṣ...ṇa Ha...re Kṛ...ṣṇ...a.” They chanted, but it was incomplete, they chanted in a faltering way. But one who chants like this goes to the *nāmābhāsa* stage. They will be delivered because they are chanting the holy name under the guidance of a pure devotee. He ordered them, “You chant! You chant! You chant!” They heard the pure chanting and that pure transcendental sound vibration touched their ears and they started chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa.

Though it is not the pure chanting, and the chanting was not complete, and the recitation faulty, still it is *nāmābhāsa*. They must be delivered because *nāmābhāsa* will deliver one who attains the *nāmābhāsa* stage and they will attain *mukti*. This is the glory of the holy name.

## Nāma-aparādha

When *nāma-akṣara*, the alphabet is chanted by very sinful persons or very greedy persons, or *pāṣaṇḍīs*, whose hearts are like blocks of stone, then it becomes *nāma-aparādha*. There is no good result.

There will not be a good result when the *nama* is chanted amongst these *pāṣaṇḍīs*. The atheists—*pāṣaṇḍīs*; *cid-jaḍa*—that means those who equalize *cit* and *acit*. When *nama* is chanted by those whose hearts are like a block of stone, then it becomes *nāma-aparādha*! You cannot expect the result of *nama* there, it becomes *nāma-aparādha*!

## How to Destroy Nāma-aparādha

There are means for destroying *nāma-aparādha*—*aviśrānta nāma laya*, by constantly chanting, and secondly—*anutāpa kare*—by lamenting. He laments, “O, I have committed this *aparādha*, so I will not commit it a second time.” He laments, he becomes repentant, and constantly chants *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. The holy name will shower mercy on him and then he will be free from the effects or reactions of *nāma-aparādha*. Otherwise *nāma-aparādha* will not disappear. Therefore, as long as you have not taken to this process of giving up *nāma-aparādha*, you cannot expect the result of chanting the holy name.

## The Three Stages of Chanting

There is a difference between *nāma* and *nāmābhāsa,* and the *nāmābhāsa* stage is the intermediate stage. There are three stages of chanting; *nāma-aparādha*—offensive chanting, *nāmābhāsa*—the intermediate stage of chanting, and *śuddha-nāma*—pure chanting or the offenceless stage of chanting \[*prema-nāma saṅkīrtana*\].

The first stage is offensive chanting, because one does not know what the *aparādhas*are. There may be many *aparadhas* committed, consciously or unconsciously. You may chant *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*, yet you are committing *aparādha*. You are not aware of *aparādhas*either consciously or unconsciously committed. But then you accept a *guru*, under whose guidance you become conscious of *aparādhas*. The *guru* speaks about the ten *nāma-aparādhas*when *harinama* is given. We give instruction; chant offencelessly the pure name, achieve Kṛṣṇa in this very life and go back home, back to Godhead. So the chanter or *sādhaka*becomes aware of the *aparādhas*and therefore consciously never commits *aparādha*.

So in the intermediate stage, one attains *nāmābhāsa*. Now one is not wilfully committing any *aparādha*—*aparādhas* are there, *aparādhas* are not completely destroyed—but he is not committing any new *aparādhas*. This is the intermediate stage, this is the *nāmābhāsa* stage. In the *nāmābhāsa* stage there are many results. For example Ajāmila, he achieved the *nāmābhāsa* stage and immediately all his sinful reactions were finished! He had committed so many sinful activities and he went to Vaikuṇṭha.

*e nāmābhāse ajāmila vaikuṇṭhe gela re*

*nāmābhāsa yasate aneka maṅgala*  
*jīvera avaśya he sukṛti prabala*

*nāmābhāse naṣṭa haya āche pāpa yata*  
*nāmābhāse mukti haya kali haya hata*

*nāmābhāse nara haya supanti pāvana*  
*nāmābhāse sarva-roga haya nivāraṇa*

*sakala āśaṅkā nāmābhāse dūra haya*  
*nāmābhāse sarvastha haite śānti pāya*

*yakṣa rakṣa bhūta preta graha samudaya*  
*nāmābhāse sakala anartha dūre jaya*

*narake patita loka sukhe mukti paya*  
*samasta prārabdha karma nāmābhāsya jaya*

*sarva vedartika sarva thitai vada bhada*  
*nāmābhāsa sarva śubha-karma ceṣṭā tara*

In the *Hari-nāma-cintāmaṇi,* Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura has explained the results of *nāmābhāsa*: if one attains the *nāmābhāsa* stage, one achieves *sukṛti*—great aupiciousness, all sinful reactions are destroyed. If you attain this *nāmābhāsa* stage, you will have *mukti*, liberation, Ajāmila achieved *mukti*. Therefore it is said:

*dīkṣā-puraścaryā-vidhi apekṣā nā kare*  
*jihvā-sparśe ā-caṇḍāla sabāre uddhāre*

**“One does not have to undergo initiation or execute the activities required before initiation. One simply has to vibrate the holy name with his lips. Thus even a man in the lowest class \[*****caṇḍāla*****\] can be delivered.”**

It never waits for *dīkṣā.* Even a *caṇḍāla*, even such a sinful person as Ajāmila, he had no *guru*, he had not been initiated into the chanting of the holy name but he achieved the *nāmābhāsa*stage. As soon as his tongue uttered the name of Lord Hari, Nārāyaṇa, he attained *mukti*. One who attains the *nāmābhāsa*stage, he achieves *mukti*. No need for *dīkṣa*, but you cannot have the pure name. You may attain up to the *nāmābhāsa*stage but you cannot attain the pure name, the stage of *prema*, this requires *guru-kṛpa*. Thus without accepting *guru* you cannot attain the *prema* stage. You cannot chant the pure name. You do not know what the *aparādhas*are, you are not aware of them, and thus you unconsciously commit *aparādha*. You may have attained the *nāmābhāsa*stage, but this stage is a very, very dangerous stage, because all falldowns are from the *nāmābhāsa*stage.

When someone chants the pure name and has attained *prema*, there is no question of falldown at that stage because of the pure name, *śuddha-nama*, *bhava-maya* or *prema-maya*. *Śuddha-nāma*, the pure name is Kṛṣṇa and is non-different from Kṛṣṇa and it is full of *prema*.

One who is full of *prema*, his heart is filled with that *prema* and his mind is fixed at the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa. He never sees anything but Kṛṣṇa. He sees moving and non-moving, but he never sees the outward form, he sees *iṣṭa,* the Lord of his heart that is Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa says, “He has not lost sight of Me, I have not lost sight of him. He sees Me, I see him.” This is complete Kṛṣṇa consciousness. This is a *premi-bhakta*, one who sees Kṛṣṇa everywhere. He never sees anything but Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa never leaves his heart. He has bound up Kṛṣṇa with the rope of love in his heart, Kṛṣṇa cannot leave his heart.

*sthāvara-jaṅgama dekhe, nā dekhe tāra mūrti*  
*sarvatra haya nija iṣṭa-deva-sphūrti*

**“The** ***mahā-bhāgavata*****, the advanced devotee, certainly sees everything mobile and immobile, but he does not exactly see their forms. Rather, everywhere he immediately sees manifest the form of the Supreme Lord.”**

For one who chants the pure name, who has *prema*, who has bound up Kṛṣṇa with the rope of love, there is no question of falldown at all. He is completely attached. Kṛṣṇa never leaves his heart. *Tomāra hṛdaye sadā govinda-viśrāma*, *govinda kahena—mora vaiṣṇava parāṇa.* This is a song by Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura. “O Vaiṣṇava Ṭhākura, Govinda has very blissfully, peacefully taken rest in your heart. Your *hṛdaya* is Vṛndāvana—your heart is Vṛndāvana. Govinda has blissfully, peacefully taken rest in your heart.”

He never leaves the heart of such a Vaiṣṇava—*premi-bhakta*. Completely fixed and attached—*kṛṣṇa tāṅre śuddha kare, nā karāya prāyaścitta*, *ajñāne vā haya yadi ‘pāpa’ upasthita*. If however unconsciously some *pāpa* (sinful activity) comes, Kṛṣṇa is there in his heart, He is bound up with the rope of love. Kṛṣṇa knows the heart. It is all outward, external. Unconsciously—*pāpa*, it is not in the heart. Kṛṣṇa makes him purified, there is no need of atonement for him.

## Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura

The example is Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura. He was overly attached to a prostitute named Cintāmaṇi. He was a devotee but he was attached to this prostitute. He was very attracted to Cintāmaṇi and so attached to her that he became mad! Once, it was a very bad night, there was torrential rain, it was a storm and the whole land was inundated.

That day was also the day of his *pitṛ-śrāddha-jivasa*. Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura had to offer oblations to his deceased father according to the Vedic scriptures and on that day the person should not commit any sinful activity. He should think of Kṛṣṇa and chant the name of Kṛṣṇa, Viṣṇu, but he was so attached to this prostitute—he was always thinking of that prostitute. How to go there? And inspite of the very bad rain and a stormy dense dark night, still, he was so mad, he left his house to see Cintāmaṇi.

The whole land was inundated, there was so much water, a dead body came floating by, so Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura thought, this is a boat. This mad fellow, he had become like a blindman, *kāmāndha—*very lusty. He is so lusty that he is completely blind, he used that dead body as a boat and floated on it and reached the house of that prostitute. But when he arrived, the door was bolted from the inside, there was no entry for Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura. Then he thought, “All right, I’ll climb up to that one open window in the roof and enter the house.” So one snake was hanging there, and Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura was like a blindman, he thought it was a hanging rope. Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura used that snake and climbed up to the roof and entered into the house. Then the prostitute Cintāmaṇi saw him and said, “Why have you come on such a stormy night, how did you come here, why have you come here? This day is your *pitṛ-śrāddha-jivasa*, but instead of remembering Viṣṇu, Kṛṣṇa, you have come here! Why haven’t you stayed at home, remembering Viṣṇu, Kṛṣṇa, why have you come here?”

Now Kṛṣṇa helps—Kṛṣṇa speaks through Cintāmaṇi; “What attraction is there in my body? Śyāmasundara is all beautiful. His beauty far exels that of crores of Cupids. What beauty is here? There is so much dirt inside my body; bones, marrow, blood, urine, stool, *vāta*, *pitta*, *kapha*—bile, mucus, air. Why are you attracted to this body? If you were attracted to the all beautiful Kṛṣṇa, then you would not be attracted to my body.” Kṛṣṇa is all attractive; *kandarpa-koṭi kamanīya-viśeṣa-śobham*, *śyāmaṁ tri-bhaṅga-lalitam*.

This was a great shock for Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura, a great shock. Immediately he turned his face towards Kṛṣṇa*—*he received such mercy from the prostitute. Such a great shock! Then he completely changed, from that moment he started chanting Hari, Hari, Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa, Govinda, Gopāla. Then he became repentant and thought, “All right, I’ll go to Vṛndāvana.” He was going to Vṛndāvana—he attained this *rāga-mārga-bhakti*, *rāgānugā-bhakti* stage, he was a *sādhaka*.

*Sādhana-bhakti* has two divisions: *vaidhī* and *rāgānugā*. We are all adopting the *vaidhī* process. Very strictly following *vidhi*—regulative principles. We are *vaidhī-sādhaka*. We are not *rāgānugā-sādhaka*, we have not attained that stage, we have not gone there, we are *vaidhī-sādhaka*. Following strictly the four regulative principles. Follow it very strictly. No fish eating, no meat eating, no intoxication, no gambling, no illicit sex—follow the four regulative principles very strictly and chant sixteen rounds of *hāri-nama*: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare,* under the guidance of a bona fide Vaiṣṇava *guru*. That is *vaidhī*, strictly following the rules and regulations.

Today is Ekādaśī, you have to fast; today is the Janmāṣṭamī day, do not drink water, you must very strictly follow, this is *vaidhī*. Then, one must get up at 3:00 am or 4:00 am and have a cold bath, come to the *mandira* and attend *maṅgala-arati*, do this and do that—there are some do’s and there are some don’ts—this is *vaidhī*. In this way gradually your heart will be cleansed of all material contaminations, then you will come to the *rāgānugā*path. When one comes to *rāgānugā*-*bhakti*, there is no *vaidhī*, that is under *sādhana-bhakti*. There are two types of *sādhana*—*vaidhī-sādhana*and *rāgānugā-sādhana*.

## Rāgānugā-bhajana

Bilvamaṅgala was a *sādhaka* in the *sādhana-bhakti* stage, but he is a *rāgānugā-sādhaka*. Hence there is no *vaidhī*. He is going to a prostitute, but we have “no illicit sex”. But he is going to a prostitute! He is doing illicit sex, but he is in *rāgānugā-sādhana*, not *vaidhī*. He had attained that stage, thus he received the mercy from Cintāmaṇi *veśyā*. He completely turned, he became a *sādhu*, his mind became completely absorbed in thoughts of Kṛṣṇa, no other thought was there in the heart, only Kṛṣṇa.

Consequently he was on his way to Vṛndāvana, and on the way he saw a maiden who was taking bath in a nearby pond, because from past habit he was a woman hunter. That lady, she stood up and thought to herself, “A *sādhu* was going along and now the *sādhu* is standing, gazing at me.” Immediately she finished her bath, put on her *sārī* and went to her house. He followed and stood on the doorstep, so the lady reported it to her husband. “I was taking bath, a *sādhu* was going by and gazed at me and now he is standing there at our doorstep.”

The husband said, “A *sādhu* has come, so you please the *sādhu*.” *Sādhu* means a dear devotee of Kṛṣṇa—*śuddha kṛṣṇa-bhakta*—pure devotee of Kṛṣṇa, completely surrendered unto Kṛṣṇa, he is a *sādhu*. We know this is a *sādhu*. “If he’s a *sādhu,* then good fortune has come to us today, a *sādhu* has come to our house, this is good fortune for us, a very glorious day for us. Yes, we have the opportunity to serve a *sādhu*.” In the *śāstra* it is said, and the *mahājanas* and the *ācāryas* have said that ‘If one meets a *sādhu,* then that is a very glorious day, good fortune comes to him.’ “Therefore a *sādhu* has come to our doorstep, so we now have the opportunity to serve this *sādhu*. All right, go to this *sādhu*, pay obeisances, and ask what this *sādhu* wants from you. How can you serve him and satisfy and please him. Then the Lord will be satisfied and pleased, and our life will be successful. Such an opportunity has come to us.”

This is a chaste and devoted wife, *satī pati-vratā nārī*, that lady, a chaste and devoted wife who obeys the order of her husband. The husband says go and then she goes, otherwise she will not go. That is a chaste and devoted wife, she always obeys the husband. Otherwise, a married lady who has a husband cannot step out of the threshold of the door—cannot get out. If she steps out, then she becomes a prostitute. She will be condemned in the society as a prostitute. This is strict Vedic regulation. Therefore her husband said, “Go,” and she went.

She went to the *sādhu* with her husband, paid obeisances to the *sādhu* and asked, “All right, *sādhu mahārāja.* How can we serve you? How can we satisfy you? What do you want from us? Please tell us, we are ready to serve you and give you all pleasure and satisfaction.”

Hearing this, Bilvamaṅgala came to his senses. Immediately he thought, “O, these eyes put me in danger. These nasty eyes, rascal eyes, have put me in danger. Instead of looking at Kṛṣṇa I looked at a woman.” He said, “I don’t need anything from you, just give me your hairpin.” The husband thought, what will he do with the hairpin? Why does a *sādhu* need a hairpin? Maybe it is because he is walking barefoot, he has no shoes, he may have thorns in his feet so he may want to take out the thorns with the hairpin. The husband and wife both thought like that. But the thorns are not in his feet, the thorns are in his heart! The thorns are in the *sādhu’s* heart. So the lady gave the hairpin to Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura and immediately he plucked out his eyes.

Can anyone do this? You cannot do this. You should understand that he is not on the bodily platform of life, he is above the bodily platform of life and Kṛṣṇa knows the heart!

*vidhi-dharma chāḍi’ bhaje kṛṣṇera caraṇa*  
*niṣiddha pāpācāre tāra kabhu nahe mana*

*ajñāne vā haya yadi ‘pāpa’ upasthita*  
*kṛṣṇa tāṅre śuddha kare, nā karāya prāyaścitta*

**“Although the pure devotee does not follow all the regulative principles of** ***varṇāśrama*****, he worships the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa. Therefore he naturally has no tendency to commit sin. If however a devotee accidentally becomes involved in a sinful activity, Kṛṣṇa purifies him. He does not have to undergo the regulative form of atonement.”**

This example of Bilvamaṅgala is *rāgānugā-bhajana*. There is no *vidhi* at all. No regulation at all. The mind is completely absorbed in thought of Kṛṣṇa. He has achieved the *bhāva* stage. Completely absorbed! There is no other thought in the heart, no, nothing else. Kṛṣṇa is there, Kṛṣṇa knows the heart. *Vidhi-dharma chāḍi’ bhaje kṛṣṇera caraṇa*. This is unconsciously done. Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura is completely absorbed and Kṛṣṇa knows the heart. *Kṛṣṇa tāṅre śuddha kare, nā karāya prāyaścitta,* Kṛṣṇa purifies him, there is no need of atonement for him.

The fact that Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura was able to pluck out his eyes shows at what stage he is in. He is not on the bodily platform, he is above the bodily platform, completely absorbed in thought of Kṛṣṇa. No one on the bodily platform of life would be able to pluck out their eyes. This is the proof!

Therefore it is said that one who is completely attached, firmly fixed in Kṛṣṇa consciousness, has bound up Kṛṣṇa with the rope of love. We give that example of the father and the son. The father is holding the hand of the child, it is a pure loving relationship, *vātsalya-rasa*. They are on top of the roof. If by chance, unconsciously the boy goes to the edge of the roof, and he is just about to fall down, the father will be holding on to his son. So there will not be a fall at all. Protection is there, he is firmly fixed. The father helps so the boy will never fall down. The same happened to Bilvamaṅgala, but he did not fall down. “Give me a hairpin,” he said and he plucked out his eyes. So he did not fall down. Where is the falldown? No falldown.

He became blind, then Kṛṣṇa appeared. If there had been any sinful thoughts in his heart or mind, would Kṛṣṇa have appeared there? No! But Kṛṣṇa appeared immediately, “O *sādhu*, you are blind now, how can you go to Vṛndāvana? I am going to Vṛndāvana. All right, hold on to this stick, I’ll lead you to Vṛndāvana.” And Kṛṣṇa did lead him to Vṛndāvana. So do you think that he has any sinful thoughts in his mind?

*vidhi-dharma chāḍi’ bhaje kṛṣṇera caraṇa*  
*niṣiddha pāpācāre tāra kabhu nahe mana*

*ajñāne vā haya yadi ‘pāpa’ upasthita*  
*kṛṣṇa tāṅre śuddha kare, nā karāya prāyaścitta*

**“Although the pure devotee does not follow all the regulative principles of** ***varṇāśrama*****, he worships the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa. Therefore he naturally has no tendency to commit sin. If however a devotee accidentally becomes involved in a sinful activity, Kṛṣṇa purifies him. He does not have to undergo the regulative form of atonement.”**

Unconsciously if some pā*pa* (sin) comes, Kṛṣṇa purifies him, He knows the heart. It is not conscious, it is unconsciously there, only outward, it is not internal, therefore Kṛṣṇa purifies him. There is no need of atonement for Bilvamaṅgala Ṭhākura and Kṛṣṇa led him to Vṛndāvana. This is *rāgānugā*-*bhajana*—*rāgānugā-sādhaka*. Bilvamaṅgala was a *sādhaka*—*sādhana* stage. After the *sādhana* stage comes *bhāva*and when *bhāva*matures it becomes *prema*. The highest stage is when one attains *prema,* and Mahāprabhu gives *prema* through chanting the holy name.

*mukhya-phate jīva pāya kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana*  
*niraparādhe nāma laile pāya prema-dhana*

If your chanting is offenceless, then you chant the pure name, which is non-different from Kṛṣṇa—*śuddha-n*ā*ma*, *bhāva-maya* or *prema-maya*. The pure name is full of *prema*. If your chanting is offenceless, the pure name, then that is *prema*. That is Kṛṣṇa and you will attain Kṛṣṇa. One who chants the pure name, his heart is filled with *prema*—mellow—*prema-rasa*. His eyes are anointed with the ointment of love.

*premañjana-cchurita-bhakti-vilocanena*  
*santaḥ sadaiva hṛdayeṣu vilokayanti*  
*yaṁ śyāmasundaram acintya-guṇa-svarūpaṁ*  
*govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi*

**“I worship Govinda, the primeval Lord, who is Śyāmasundara, Kṛṣṇa Himself with inconceivable innumerable attributes, whom the pure devotees see in their heart of hearts with the eye of devotion tinged with the salve of love.”**

When one becomes endowed with this vision, the ointment of love, *kṛṣṇa-prema*, is smeared on one’s eyes and one sees Śyāmasundara, the all-beautiful Śyāmasundara form everywhere. *Yāhāṅ netra paḍe tāhāṅ kṛṣṇa sphure*, wherever he looks, he sees the beautiful form of Kṛṣṇa, Śyāmasundara. Prahlāda Mahārāja saw the Lord in a stone pillar, whereas his demoniac father, who is the conqueror of the three worlds, could not see Him.

It is a question of vision. One who sees Kṛṣṇa everywhere, sees everything in Kṛṣṇa, and is completely Kṛṣṇa conscious—there is no *vidhi*, he kicks all *vidhi*. What *vidhi* was there? Bilvamaṅgala was a woman hunter. What *vidhi* was there? But he plucked out his eyes. Can you do it? It is in the heart. What is in the heart, Kṛṣṇa knows, so Kṛṣṇa led him. So when you chant the pure name, *prema-maya*—*bhāva-maya*, *prema-maya*—you will be endowed with that vision.

*śrī-nāma smarile rūpa āise saṅge saṅge*  
*rūpa nāma vinā naya nāce nānā raṅge*

Then you will see! As soon as you chant the pure name, immediately the all-beautiful form of Śyāmasundara will manifest before you.

## See Śyāmasundara Immediately

*nāme dṛḍha haile nāi haya pāpe mati*  
*pūrva pāpa dagdha haya citta śuddha ati*

If you are fixed, if you have firm unflinching faith in the holy name, then there is no apprehension of sinful thoughts entering into your mind. *Pūrva pāpa dagdha haya*—if some *pāpa,* sinful reaction is there, it will be destroyed by chanting; *citta śuddha*, *ceto-darpaṇa-mārjanam*, your heart will be cleansed of all material contamination and all material sinful reactions.

*kṛṣṇa rakṣā-kartā eka mātra bali jāne*  
*jīvane pālana-kartā kṛṣṇa eka māne*

He has firm faith, he has unflinching faith that “Kṛṣṇa is my maintainer, Kṛṣṇa is my protector, no one else.” This is firm faith. He has no ‘I’ and ‘mine’ attitude—*‘ahaṁ-mama’ buddhya-śakti*.

*śuddha-nāma yā’ra mukhe tā’ra dṛḍha-mana*  
*kṛṣṇa haite vicalita nahe eka kṣana*

“I am the lowest of the low.” With this mentality, this mood he chants; *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. One whose tongue is chanting *śuddha-nama*—the pure name, his mind is fixed. His mind is not oscillating. Where does your mind go? Your body is sitting here but your mind is there in another place.

*śuddha-nāma yāra mukhe tāra dṛḍha-mana*  
*kṛṣṇa haite vicalita nahe eka kṣana*

By chanting the pure name, his mind is fixed, and there is no oscillation of the mind. He never oscillates, not even for a moment, he is fixed in Kṛṣṇa:

*śrī-nāma smarile rūpa āise saṅge saṅge*  
*rūpa nāma vinā naya nāce nānā raṅge*

If you are really chanting the pure name, it is non-different from Kṛṣṇa,and it is non-different from the form of Kṛṣṇa. The name of Kṛṣṇa, the form of Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa are non-different. *Śrī-nāma smarile rūpa āise saṅge saṅge,* if you are chanting the pure name or remembering the pure name, you will immediately see the all-beautiful form of Śyāmasundara. It will be manifest before you. That is the test that you are chanting the pure name, if you see Śyāmasundara immediately—*rūpa nāma vinā naya*. There is no difference between the name and the form of Kṛṣṇa—*nāce nānā raṅge*—the different *līlās* will be manifest before you.

*ataeva nāma mātra vaiṣṇavera dharma*  
*nāme prasphuṭita haya rūpa guṇa karma*

*kṛṣṇera samagra līlā nāme vidyamāna*  
*nāma se parama tattva tomāra vidhāna*

Therefore, *nāma mātra vaiṣṇavera dharma. Nāma eka śaraṇatā vaiṣṇava—*a Vaiṣṇava who is completely surrendered unto the holy name and nothing else—*nāma mātra vaiṣṇavera dharma*, *nāme prasphuṭita haya rūpa guṇa karma*—he is always with Kṛṣṇa, he sees the all-beautiful form of Śyāmasundara, Kṛṣṇa. He sees all the *līlas* of Kṛṣṇa—*kṛṣṇera samagra līlā nāme vidyamāna.* All *līlās*, all types of *līlās* are there in the holy name. You see *līlā*, you are always in Vṛndāvana seeing Vṛndāvana *līlās*. Then *nāma se parama, kṛṣṇa-parama-tattva*—the Supreme Truth, the holy name is also the Supreme Truth.

## Sūryābhāsa—Nāmābhāsa

Mahāprabhu gives the highest, but before the rise of the holy name comes this *nāmābhāsa* stage. For example, before the rise of the effulgent sun, *sūryābhāsa* comes, you will see a faint glimpse, a faint reflection. Just as the eastern horizon becomes red, the darkness gradually vanishes. Dense darkness prevails in the night, gradually, just prior to the rising of the sun, the darkness gradually vanishes, the eastern horizon becomes red. Those who are active during the night, *niśācaras*—thieves, robbers, dacoits, and owls—they fly away, they leave because the sun is rising. *Ābhāsa* is there, the faint glimpse is there, now it has come out, they gradually fly away.

## Unfailing Medicine

Similarly, before the rising of the holy name—the pure name—*nāmābhāsa*comes, and when *nāmābhāsa*comes, all the sinful reactions fly away and *mukti* comes, liberation comes, all the *prārabdha-karma* is destroyed. When you attain the *nāmābhāsa* stage, all your *prārabdha-karma*, past sinful reactions are destroyed—*nāmābhāse sarva-roga haya nivāraṇ.* Disease, *roga,* comes because of your sinful activities. Disease comes because of sinful reactions. When you attain the *nāmābhāsa* stage, your *prārabdha-karma* is destroyed, your past karmic reactions are destroyed, there is no more disease. All disease is finished and no more going to the doctor to get medicine:

*enechi auṣadhi māyā nāśibāra lāgi'*  
*hari-nāma-mahā-mantra lao tumi māgi'*

I have come with the unfailing medicine, that is *hari-nāma*, *mahā*-*mantra*. It is unfailing. The doctors medicine may fail but this medicine will never fail, if you have firm faith in it. If you have firm faith, it is unfailing, but you are lacking faith, therefore it will fail and you will commit *aparādha*.

Completely annihilate *māyā*. There is no *prārabdha-karma*, no karmic reaction. Finished! No *roga*, no more disease. All diseases are cured—finished. There will be no disease if you have such unfailing medicine with you: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma RāmaHare Hare*.

*nāmābhāse sarvastha haite śānti pāya*  
*sakala āśaṅkā nāmābhāse dūra haya*

You go to the astrologer. “O bad days have come to me, bad stars, evil stars, evil planets are all there in my horoscope.” The astrologer says, “One very bad period has come, a bad planet *rahu* has come. Very bad! You will die, you will die, you will die!” Death is coming to you. Soon it is coming. But if you chant: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare* and if by chanting you attain the *nāmābhāsa*stage, then all your *prārabdha-karma* will be destroyed. This evil planet, evil *graha*, cannot do any harm to you, no more death! No more death! He should have died but he is now surviving. What happened to him? Because he took shelter of the holy name, all his *prārabdha-karma*was destroyed. He attained the *nāmābhāsa*stage, all his karmic reactions were destroyed, no *prārabdha-karma*. No effect of these *grahas*. Finished!

Why are you going to the astrologer saying, you see my horoscope? What good and bad are coming to me? No need! No need! It is gone! You might have died but you are surviving. How is it? What happened? I have taken shelter of the holy name: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare RāmaHare RāmaRāmaRāmaHare Hare*. This is the result of *nāmābhāsa,* all *anarthas* are destroyed.

*yakṣa rakṣa bhūta preta graha samudaya*  
*nāmābhāse sakala anartha dūre jaya*

**All evil stars, evil planets, the ghosts,** ***rākṣasas*****,** ***pretas*****, will all go away. One may be going to hell but he will be saved because all his** ***anarthas*** **are destroyed.**

The example was Ajāmila. He was going to hell but he did not go to hell. He did not die, his lifespan increased and enhanced. *Prārabdha-karma*, all his *prārabdha-karma*is destroyed. This is the result of *nāmābhāsa*. *Nāmābhāsa*produced such a result, what to speak of the pure name—*śuddha-nāma*.

## The Only Boon—Sādhu-saṅga

One who chants the pure name attains the platform of *kṛṣṇa-prema*. He attains Kṛṣṇa. But the *nāmābhāsa*stage is a dangerous stage, because all falldowns are from this *nāmābhāsa*stage. This is the intermediate stage. The *nāmābhāsa*stage is dangerous because if in this stage you commit *aparādha* and you associate with *asādhus*—those who are not *sādhus—*you will fall down. You will definitely fall down. One should always be very, very careful and should associate with a *sādhu*. *Sādhu-saṅga* is a fort. A fort will protect you and it will act like a shield. It will protect you from falldown. If you leave *sādhu-saṅga,* then you welcome danger. If you avoid *sādhu-saṅga,* then where is the protection? *Māyā* is very strong! Then you will definitely commit *aparādha*, definitely*,* and you will definitely fall down. Yes, *sādhu-saṅga*; *sādhu-saṅge kṛṣṇa-nāma ei mātra chaya*, *saṁsāre jinite āra kona vastu naya*. This is the only requirement, *sādhu-saṅge kṛṣṇa-nāma ei mātra chaya*, *saṁsāre jinite āra kona vastu naya*.

This was the only boon the Pracetās asked for. Lord Viṣṇu asked, “What boon do you want?”

*tulayāma lavenāpi*  
*na svargaṁ nāpunar-bhavam*  
*bhagavat-saṅgi-saṅgasya*  
*martyānāṁ kim utāśiṣaḥ*

**“Even a moment’s association with a pure devotee cannot be compared to being transferred to heavenly planets or even merging into the Brahman effulgence in complete liberation. For living entities who are destined to give up the body and die, association with pure devotees is the highest benediction.”**

We are the residents of this *martya-loka*, material world, where there is birth and death. What boon is there for us? The only boon we require is *sādhu-saṅga*. *Sādhu-saṅga* will protect us from all misfortunes, all evils and all afflictions. That is the only boon required.

If you are in the association of a *sādhu,* then no evil will come to you. That is protection. That is a strong fort, *sādhu-saṅge kṛṣṇa-nāma ei mātra chaya*, *saṁsāre jinite āra kona vastu naya.* That is the only boon asked, “May I always have *sādhu-saṅga*. Let me live in the association of *sādhus* and chant *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*, then I will be able to chant the pure name, otherwise I cannot chant the pure name.”

*asādhu-saṅge bhāi kṛṣṇa-nāma nāhi haya*  
*nāmākṣara bāhirāya baṭe nāma kabhu naya*

*kabhu nāmābhāsa sāḍā nāmāparādha*  
*e saba jānihe bhāi kṛṣṇa-bhakti badha*

If you do not associate with a *sādhu* who chants the pure name, who is on the highest stage of *prema-bhakti*, you cannot chant the pure name. If you are associating with *asādhus*, (an *asādhu* is a non-devotee—not a *sādhu*), then your chanting is similar to pronouncing the alphabet: *ha... re... rā... ma... kṛṣ... ṇa...* The alphabet, *ābhidhānika-śabda*, the word available in the dictionary is pronounced—but not the name and the name is non-different from Kṛṣṇa—but Kṛṣṇa will never come. *Nāmākṣara bāhirāya baṭe nāma kabhu naya*. Only the letters of the alphabet are coming, that is not the name. *Kabhu nāmābhāsa sāḍā nāmāparādha.* Sometimes you may achieve the *nāmābhāsa*stage, otherwise your chanting is only *nāma-aparādha*.

Therefore, *sādhu-saṅga*—*sādhu* means a pure devotee, a *śuddha-bhakta* who is completely surrendered, who has complete surrender unto the holy name and has completely surrendered unto Kṛṣṇa, he chants the pure name. Then if you will associate with such a *sādhu*, that is a strong fort, a shield that will protect you from all ills. Then you will be able to chant the pure name, otherwise you cannot chant the pure name. You may be chanting *nāmābhāsa*or *nāma-aparādha*.

## Mahāprabhu Delivers One and All

Mahāprabhu appeared when there was a lunar eclipse. Everyone was chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa—the *nadīyā-vāsīs,* all the inhabitants of Nadia, and even the Mohammedan *mleccha-yavanas* jokingly started chanting, “Hari, Hari.” They achieved the *nāmābhāsa* stage. Mahāprabhu had come to deliver one and all, moving and non-moving—*sthavara-jaṅgama*—*mlecchas*, *yavanas*, *patitas*, the most degraded, the most fallen—this is Mahāprabhu’s mercy. When He appeared in this world, He even delivered the animals, birds, bees, worms, reptiles, insects. All received the opportunity to be delivered.

All received the opportunity to hear *gaura-kathā*, *gaura-kīrtana* and *kṛṣṇa-kathā*, *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*. Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu was chanting *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. He is so merciful, wonderfully merciful. He advented Himself, He incarnated Himself, He appeared here in this material world for the good of all living entities, both moving and non-moving! Even for the birds, beasts and the stones.

*paśu pākhī jhure, pāṣāṇa vidare,*  
*śuni’ yāṅra guṇa-gāthā*

While Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu was moving through that Jhārikhaṇḍa jungle, He made the animals, birds, beasts, snakes, reptiles, lions, deer, tigers, all chant Hare Kṛṣṇa and dance. That is Mahāprabhu’s mercy. *Pāṣāṇa vidare*—even the hard stone melts and you can see the proof there, in the Ālālanātha temple you will see such a big block of stone. The whole body imprint of Mahāprabhu is there. It had melted—*paśu pākhī jhure*, *pāṣāṇa vidare*, *śuni’ yāṅra guṇa-gāthā.*

## Kṛṣṇa-kīrtana—Gaura-kīrtana

Mahāprabhu has come to shower His mercy upon one and all. Not only human beings: birds, beasts, reptiles, and even stones. This is *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*. If you perform real *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana,* the whole world will be delivered. All inauspiciousness will be driven out, all auspiciousness will come into the whole world, but it should be *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*, real *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*, not *māyā-kīrtana*.

*anyābhilāṣitā-śūnyaṁ*  
*jñāna-karmādy-anāvṛtam*  
*ānukūlyena kṛṣṇānu-*  
*śīlanaṁ bhaktir uttamā*

**“One should render transcendental loving service to the Supreme Lord Kṛṣṇa favorably and without desire for material profit or gain through fruitive activities or philosophical speculation. That is called pure devotional service.”**

Therefore, one should join *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*—*gaura-kīrtana*. He should not have any other desires.

*na dhanaṁ na janaṁ na sundarīṁ*  
*kavitāṁ vā jagad-īśa kāmaye*  
*mama janmani janmanīśvare*  
*bhavatād bhaktir ahaitukī tvayi*

That is Mahāprabhu’s teaching. Do not cherish the desire of achieving material wealth, material followers—*na dhanaṁ na janaṁ na sundarīṁ kavitāṁ*—having a beautiful woman to enjoy, and do not cherish the desire of *mukti,* liberation. *Bhukti*, *mukti*, these are two witches; material enjoyment and material liberation, do not take their association. If you want to join *gaura-kīrtana, kṛṣṇa-kīrtana,* then give up all your desires for material enjoyment and all your desires for liberation. But you have these desires and you are chanting, doing *kīrtana* that is not *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*—it is *māyā-kīrtana*!

If we think this *māyā-kīrtana* is *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana,* then we commit a blunder. *Nāma nāhi haya*—the name is not coming out, the word available in the dictionary is coming out. In the dictionary you will find that the name of Kṛṣṇa means black. Hence the dictionary word is coming out, but the name is non-different from Kṛṣṇa. If you think this alphabet or word which is available in the dictionary is Kṛṣṇa, then you will commit a blunder and you will be cheated—*vañcanā*.

The real name of Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa’s form and Kṛṣṇa Himself are non-different. Therefore with *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana, gaura-kīrtana*—Mahāprabhu did *kīrtana*, the name that is coming out is like a volcanic eruption from the lips of Gaura, it is the pure name. This *kīrtana* is not meant for one’s sense gratifiation. It is not meant for providing enjoyment for one’s senses. That is not *kīrtana*.

Nevertheless, if someone achieves the *nāmābhāsa* stage, then all his *prārabdha-karma* is destroyed, all disease is cured. It is still current here in India in our villages. If there is cholera, and many people of the village are attacked by cholera, the villagers come and during the whole night they chant Hare Kṛṣṇa, walking around the village. “O villagers, please come and chant *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*.” For the whole night they move around the village and in this way the cholera is driven out.

But Gaura never speaks of this *kīrtana*. It is not *gaura-kīrtana* that will drive out cholera, drive out our ill fate. It is the pure name. Obtain Kṛṣṇa, *kṛṣṇa-prema*—that is *gaura-kīrtana*. It is not that you will aspire for *mukti* or aspire for material enjoyment. No! Automatically it will be achieved, and that is the proof, when Gaura was chanting, performing *kīrtana*, *sāṅgopāṅgāstra-pārṣadam*. He was surrounded with his *aṅga, upāṅga*—Advaita Ācārya, Nityānanda Prabhu, Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita, Gadādhara Paṇḍita, all *gaura-bhaktas*—surrounded with the chanting of *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*.

You will see the so-called *māyāvādīs* of Kāśī. Their head was Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī. They achieved *mukti*, they became Vaiṣṇavas. Such *gaura-karuṇā, gaura-kṛpā*. Even such materialistic persons like King Pratāparudra, who was associating with *kāminī* and *kāñcana*, women and gold, developed complete Kṛṣṇa consciousness. This is the effect of *gaura-kīrtana*. Even a hard block of stone melted. The birds, bees, reptiles, animals, ferocious animals, they all chanted Hare Kṛṣṇa and achieved liberation. This is the proof. When Srī Caitanya Mahāprabhu was passing through the Jhārikhaṇḍa jungle, it was there that even the trees and the creepers became enchanted. Yes, this is *gaura-kīrtana,* when Mahāprabhu was having *kīrtana* there. Nowadays, however, there is no real *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*. That is the scarcity here. No real *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*. Therefore we are caught up in the laws of *māyā* or Kali, so we are quarrelling and fighting. Why is quarrelling going on in Mahāprabhu’s movement? Mahāprabhu crushed and killed Kali. Why has Kali entered? Because you are not really doing *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*. That is the only reason. Otherwise no Kali!

*kali-kukkura-kadana yadi cāo (he)*  
*kali-yuga-pāvana, kali-bhaya-nāśana*  
*śrī-śacī-nandana gāo (he)*

If you want to crush Kali completely into the dust, the Kali dog (a *kukkura*), then you will chant “Sacīnandana Gaurahari - *kī jaya!*” He is the deliverer of people affected by Kali-yuga. Those who are very afraid of the Kali-yuga should chant Gaurahari’s name and perform real *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*—*gaura-kīrtana*, pure *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*—*gaura-kīrtana*, then Kali will be completely destroyed. Kali the dog will be completely crushed into dust, no fear of Kali’s quarrelling and hypocrisy. Because you are not doing real *kṛṣṇa-kīrtana*, Kali has entered into the movement.

Thus Gaurasundara appeared here in the material world to shower His mercy upon one and all. Even upon the dogs, hogs, stones, trees, creepers, birds and beasts, what to speak of human beings. Follow Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu and chant the pure name of Kṛṣṇa. *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. Then you will acquire everything. You will acquire Kṛṣṇa!

## Questions - The Mercy of Kṛṣṇa

**Devotee**: You said that the *nāmābhāsa* stage is very dangerous?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** Yes, very dangerous.

**Devotee:** You said that all falldowns come from this stage. Previously you said that it is not possible to fall from the *nāma-aparādha* stage because that is already a fallen stage. Is that true?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** The *nāma-aparādha* stage is a fallen stage. The *nāmābhāsa* stage is the intermediatestage. But such result is there in *nāmābhāsa* that the *nāmābhāsa* stage will give you *mukti*, all your *prārabdha-karma* will be destroyed, everything is there. But that is a dangerous stage, because if you are not careful, you will commit *aparādha*. If you associate with *asādhus*, *asat*, you will commit *aparādha*, and you will fall down. Yes!

**Devotee:** If anyone chants *nāma-aparādha*, then he is already fallen, so how can he be fallen?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** Committing *nāma-aparādha* is not the name at all. Offensive chanting is committing *nāma-aparādhas*. What is the result? The result is that you will fall down and you go to hell! *Dharma*, *artha*, *kāma* and *mokṣa*. If you obtain the *nāmābhāsa* stage, all four are there, you get *mokṣa*. So-called *dharma*, religiosity; *artha*, economic development; *kāma*, all your material desires will be fulfilled; everything will be there. In the *nāma-aparādha* stage there is no *mokṣa*, but three are there—*dharma*, *artha* and *kāma*.

**Devotee:** So is it very dangerous when we are chanting and we have some desire. Will that desire manifest?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** The name will fulfill your material desire—*dharma*, *artha* and *kāma*. You are chanting but your chanting is offensive, still your material desires will be fulfilled. Your material desires will be fulfilled means what? Bharata Mahārāja fell down from such an elevated stage. So you are fulfilling your material desires—then too much addicted to material enjoyment and it will cause you to glide down, down, to hell. But if you achieve *nāmābhāsa*, then *mokṣa* is there, you will get liberation. But in this stage you should be very, very careful to always associate with a *sādhu*!

**Devotee:** Can *sāyujya-mukti* be there?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** *Mukti* is already there for you. Always associate with a *sādhu*, remain in the association of a *sādhu*, always chanting the pure name in the association of a *sādhu* and elevate yourself from that stage to the pure name stage. Offenceless chanting means that you are free from fear, otherwise fear is there. At that stage you commit *aparādha*; *nāma-aparādha*, *vaiṣṇava-aparādha*, any *aparādha* you commit. Then you will fall down!

**Devotee:** If he sinks to a lower stage, but not completely. If it is not like the mad elephant offence. Then it is said that one comes one stage lower from *bhāva* stage. If someone reaches *bhāva* or *prema* and commits an offence, he comes one stage lower, is it?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** Bharata Mahārāja attained the *bhāva* stage, some *anarthas* were there, in just a very minute portion, they are not fully eradicated. “*Lābha*”, “*pūjā*”, “*pratiṣṭhā*” will come. They will say, “Great *sādhu.*” Yes! Big garlands will come to you, and so much will be offered to you. Offering *pūjā*, *daṇḍavat*, *praṇāmas*, so much money—*praṇāmī* will come to you. Yes, that is an *anartha*, and if you are addicted to it—finished!

**Devotee:** One devotee was saying recently that you may be chanting sometimes *nāma-aparādha* but then sometimes you chant in *nāmābhāsa*, and sometimes you may chant *śuddha-nāma*. Is that possible that one may chant *śuddha-nāma* and sometimes *nāma-aparādha*?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** No! *Asādhu-saṅge bhāi kṛṣṇa-nāma nāhi haya*, *nāmākṣara bāhirāya baṭe nāma kabhu naya, kabhu nāmābhāsa sāḍā nāmāparādha*. If you are not associating with *sādhus* but associating with *asādhus*, you cannot chant *śuddha-nāma*. Sometimes you may come to the stage of *nāmābhāsa*, otherwise you are always in the *nāma-aparādha* stage. If you are in pure association of a pure *sādhu*, then you will be able to chant the pure name.

**Devotee:** So *śuddha-nāma* means no *nāmā-aparādha*?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** No *nāmā-aparādha* and no *nāmābhāsa,* that is *śuddha-nāma*.

**Devotee:** Mahāprabhu, He said that once you chant the holy name you get the result once?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** Yes, that is a Vaiṣṇava. He is a Vaiṣṇava*—yāṅra mukhe śuni eka-bāra kṛṣṇa-nāma, sei pūjya,—sei vaiṣṇava*—if you have chanted *śuddha-nāma* once, “Kṛṣṇa” once, then you are a Vaiṣṇava. If you are always chanting, you are *vaiṣṇavatara*, it is a comparative degree—*yāṅhāra darśane mukhe āise kṛṣṇa-nāma*,—*vaiṣṇavatama*. If by just seeing such a Vaiṣṇava, automatically the mind and the tongue chants Hare Kṛṣṇa, then he is the topmost Vaiṣṇava; *vaiṣṇavatara*, *vaiṣṇavatama*. Mahāprabhu has said that. He who chants *śuddha-nāma* once is a Vaiṣṇava. Yes, it may be that, by the association of a *sādhu*, once he chanted, then he leaves the association, then he commits *aparādha*. But once he has chanted, he is a Vaiṣṇava also. But one who always chants, how is he? He is *vaiṣṇavatara*, *bhāgavatottama*, yes by seeing him, automatically my tongue chants Hare Kṛṣṇa. He is the topmost Vaiṣṇava.

**Devotee:** Gurudeva, if someone hears the pure name from a *sādhu,* then maybe he can chant immediately?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** Yes, in the association, he will chant.

**Devotee:** Why is it so difficult to stop making *aparādha*? Even though we have the desire to stop, why is it so difficult?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** Because you have some material desire in your mind. The material desires are there, so you commit *aparādha*. Your mind oscillates when you are chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa, your mind is not fixed on Kṛṣṇa, then that is *nāma-aparādha*, ninth *aparādha*, *anavadhānatā*. Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura has said. You may not be committing any other *nāma-aparādha*, out of ten, but you are committing this *apārādha* that your mind oscillates. Chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa, but your mind is not thinking of Kṛṣṇa, then the mind is oscillating. That is *anavadhānatā*, that is the ninth *aparādha*. Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura has said, you are not committing other *aparādhas* but you are committing this *aparādha*, therefore you cannot get *prema*. I say that when I speak about *nāma-aparādha*. This is not there in *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*, but in *Hari-nāma-cintamaṇi*, Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura has said that. Why can’t you develop *kṛṣṇa-prema*? Because of this *aparādha*. We are not committing any other *aparādhas* but we are committing this *aparādha*, therefore we cannot develop *kṛṣṇa-prema*. Otherwise *nama* will give you *kṛṣṇa-prema*. Why have you not attained? Because you are committing this *aparādha*, the mind is oscillating.

**Devotee:** If one is very attentive in the *nāmābhāsa*stage, then very quickly you will achieve the pure name, just like the sun coming up. Is there any indication that one is chanting *nāmābhāsa*?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** Indication is the pure *nāmābhāsa* stage, no diseases, no evil stars or planets are there. Your horoscope says you would have died but you are surviving. This is *nāmābhāsa*. If you are chanting the pure name, then constantly you will see Śyāmasundara. That is the test, chant Hare Kṛṣṇa and see Śyāmasundara. Then you can understand, yes, the pure name is coming out, then I can see the beautiful form.

**Devotee:** If someone is chanting *nāmābhāsa*, can Kṛṣṇa still give him disease etc. just to purify him?

**Gour Govinda Swami:**That is Kṛṣṇa’s mercy!

**Devotee:** So someone may be chanting *nāmābhāsa*, but Kṛṣṇa may still give some reaction?

**Gour Govinda Swami:** Some disease comes, a *sādhu* also has some disease, that is Kṛṣṇa’s mercy:

*tat te 'nukampāṁ susamīkṣamāṇo*  
*bhuñjāna evātma-kṛtaṁ vipākam*  
*hṛd-vāg-vapurbhir vidadhan namas te*  
*jīveta yo mukti-pade sa dāya-bhāk*

**“My dear Lord, one who earnestly waits for You to bestow Your causeless mercy upon him, all the while patiently suffering the reactions of his past misdeeds and offering You respectful obeisances with his heart, words and body, is surely eligible for liberation, for it has become his rightful claim.”**

That is *anukampā*! That is the mercy of Kṛṣṇa. Yes, that is the mercy of Kṛṣṇa. Haridāsa Ṭhākura was chanting the pure name, every moment. Three lakhs of names he chanted. He never slept, he never ate. What type of body does he have? *Siddha-deha—*not a material body. No demand for eating, sleeping, mating and defending. A completely spiritual body—*siddha-deha*. Three lakhs of names—all pure chanting. He was always with Kṛṣṇa but he was persecuted so much. The *pāṣaṇḍīs* dragged him through twenty two bazaars. They beat him and beat him, blood and flesh came out, the bone was visible. Why did such persecution come? He accepted that “Kṛṣṇa is so merciful to me. I am a very sinful person, Kṛṣṇa is very merciful, giving me light punishment. He is so merciful. I deserve very severe, heavy punishment, but He is giving me light punishment. My head would have been cut off but my finger is cut.” He accepted it in that way. A Vaiṣṇava accepts it in that way. This is mercy—*tat te ’nukampāṁ.*He accepts it, he tolerates everything. If you do *hari-kīrtana,* you will get the power of tolerance, *taror iva sahiṣṇunā.* Just tolerate. He was never disturbed. He felt no pain. He was not feeling any pain at all. Anybody else would have died, but he did not die. What body does he have? He was chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa, peacefully, blissfully. In that stage, while the *pāṣaṇḍīs* were whipping, whipping, and dragging him. ## How to Achieve the Pure Name

We must not deviate from the injunctions of the *śāstras*. This is confirmed in the next verse of the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

*anyathā ye māne, tāra nāhika nistāra*  
*nāhi, nāhi, nāhi—e tina ‘eva’-kāra*

**“This verse clearly states that anyone who accepts any other path cannot be delivered. This is the reason for the triple repetition “nothing else, nothing else, nothing else,” which emphasizes the real process of self-realization.”**

*tṛṇa haite nīca hañā sadā labe nāma*  
*āpani nirabhimānī, anye dibe māna*

**“To chant the holy name always, one should be humbler than the grass in the street and devoid of all desire for personal honour, but one should offer others all respectful obeisances.”**

*taru-sama sahiṣṇutā vaiṣṇava karibe*  
*bhartsana-tāḍane kāke kichu nā balibe*

**“A devotee engaged in chanting the holy name of the Lord should practice forbearance like that of a tree. Even if rebuked or chastised, he should not say anything to others to retaliate.”**

*kāṭileha taru yena kichu nā bolaya*  
*śukāiyā mare, tabu jala nā māgaya*

**“For even if one cuts a tree, it never protests, nor even if it is drying up and dying does it ask anyone for water.”**

This practice of forbearance (*tṛṇād api sunīcena*) is very difficult, but when one actually engages in chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mantra*, the quality of forbearance automatically develops. A person advanced in spiritual consciousness through the chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mantra* need not practice to develop it separately, for a devotee develops all good qualities simply by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mantra* regularly.

*ei-mata vaiṣṇava kāre kichu nā māgiba*  
*ayācita-vṛtti, kiṁvā śāka-phala khāiba*

**“Thus a Vaiṣṇava should not ask anything from anyone else. If someone gives him something without being asked, he should accept it, but if nothing comes, a Vaiṣṇava should be satisfied to eat whatever vegetables and fruits are easily available.”**

*sadā nāma la-iba, yathā-lābhete santoṣa*  
*eita ācāra kare bhakti-dharma-poṣa*

**“One should strictly follow the principle of always chanting the holy name, and one should be satisfied with whatever he gets easily. Such devotional behavior solidly maintains one’s devotional service.”**

*tṛṇād api su-nīcena*  
*taror iva sahiṣṇunā*  
*amāninā māna-dena*  
*kīrtanīyaḥ sadā hariḥ*

**“One who thinks himself lower than the grass, who is more tolerant than a tree, and who does not expect personal honour yet is always prepared to give all respect to others, can very easily always chant the holy name of the Lord.”**

The grass is specifically mentioned in this verse because everyone tramples upon it yet the grass never protests. This example indicates that a spiritual master or leader should not be proud of his position; being always humbler than an ordinary common man, he should go on preaching the cult of Caitanya Mahāprabhu by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra.

*ūrdhva-bāhu kari’ kahoṅ, śuna, sarva-loka*  
*nāma-sūtre gāṅthi’ para kaṇṭhe ei śloka*

**“Raising my hands, I declare, ‘Everyone please hear me! String this verse on the thread of the holy name and wear it on your neck for continuous remembrance.”**

When chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*, in the beginning one may commit many offenses, which are called *nāmābhāsa* and *nāma-aparādha*. In this stage there is no possibility of achieving perfect love of Kṛṣṇa by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*. Therefore one must chant the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* according to the principles of the above verse, *tṛṇād api su-nīcena taror iva sahiṣṇunā*. One should note in this connection that chanting involves the activities of the upper and lower lips as well as the tongue. All three must be engaged in chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*. The words “Hare Kṛṣṇa” should be very distinctly pronounced and heard. Sometimes one mechanically produces a hissing sound instead of chanting with the proper pronunciation with the help of the lips and tongue. Chanting is very simple, but one must practice it seriously. Therefore the author of *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja Gosvāmī, advises everyone to keep this verse always strung about his neck.

*prabhu-ājñāya kara ei śloka ācaraṇa*  
*avaśya pāibe tabe śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇa*

**“One must strictly follow the principles given by Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu in this verse. If one simply follows in the footsteps of Lord Caitanya and the Gosvāmīs, certainly he will achieve the ultimate goal of life, the lotus feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.”**

In all the *Vedas* it is said, “*Hari-nāma* is the essence of all the *Vedas*!” Therefore chant: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. Therefore Srila Prabhupāda says here, “Our Kṛṣṇa consciousness movement stresses the chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mantra* only, because this is the secret. This is the secret for this age of Kali.” *Veda pūrṇa bale*; *pāibe parama prīti ānanda apāra—*if you chant the pure name, you will get immense (*apāra-ānanda*) unlimited pleasure because the source of all pleasure and blissfulness is Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa is s*ac-cid-ānanda-maya*. He is *sac-cid-ānanda-maya*—*san-maya, cin-maya, ānanda-maya*—therefore by chanting His name, the pure name, you can constantly come in contact with Kṛṣṇa, then you will get real *ānanda*.

Who is *sac-cid-ānanda-maya*? *Veda pūrṇa bale yata mukta mahājana, paravyome sadākare nāma-saṅkīrtana.* Again the *Vedas* say, those who are liberated, *nitya-mukta,* eternally liberated *mahājanas,* they always and only chant this Hare Kṛṣṇa in the spiritual world: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*.

## Develop Strong Faith

If you chant this holy name, all your *anarthas* will be finished, all your *anarthas* will be destroyed, if you have strong faith, full faith in the holy name. This is a question of faith.

*nāme dṛḍha haile nāi haya pāpe mati*  
*pūrva-pāpa dagdha haya citta-śuddha ati*

If you develop strong faith in the holy name, unflinching faith in the holy name, always chanting the holy name, always keeping the holy name in your mind, then where is the scope that some sinful thought will enter into your mind? Never will any sinful thought enter into your mind, if the holy name is there in your mind. What previous sinful thought will enter into your mind, if the holy name is there in your mind? Whatever previous sinful reactions are there, they are all destroyed! And your consciousness, your heart becomes purified of all contaminations. This is the effect of chanting the holy name, the pure name. When speaking of the name, it means the pure name, not *nāmābhāsa* or *nāma-aparādha*. One should pronounce the name distinctly and concentrate on this transcendental sound vibration, otherwise you will commit *aparādha.* Your chanting will only be *nāma-aparādha*. Therefore chant distinctly and with attention. Then your heart will be purified, your consciousness will be purified and your mind will be purified, there will be no scope that some sinful thought will enter into your mind, rather whatever previous sinful reactions were there, will all be destroyed, and your heart, consciousness and mind will be purified.

*kṛṣṇa rakṣā-kartā eka mātra bali jāne*  
*jīvane pālana-kartā kṛṣṇa iha māne*

*‘ahaṁ-mama’ buddhya-śakti nā rākhe hṛdaye*  
*dīna-bhāve nāma laya sakala samaye*

He has strong faith in Kṛṣṇa—Kṛṣṇa is my only maintainer, Kṛṣṇa is my protector, no one else. He has strong faith in Kṛṣṇa and strong faith in the name of Kṛṣṇa—*jīvane pālana-kartā kṛṣṇa iha māne*. Kṛṣṇa is my maintainer throughout my life! A completely surrendered soul has strong faith in it.

## Everything Belongs to Kṛṣṇa

A completely surrendered soul will never say this ‘I’ and ‘mine’. I am from Australia. Who are you? I am a *jīva*, but I am a *jīva* from Australia. This is your identity. “I have so much bank balance, I am a great person, don’t you know? I have a wife. I have so many girlfriends. I am a great enjoyer, I am great.” This is ‘I’ and ‘mine’, but a Vaiṣṇava is not like that, he never says ‘I’ and ‘mine’. I am Kṛṣṇa’s, I do not belong to myself. I am Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant. He never says ‘I’ and ‘mine’, everything is Kṛṣṇa’s. My body belongs to Kṛṣṇa, my mind belongs to Kṛṣṇa. I myself belong to Kṛṣṇa, everything is Kṛṣṇa’s. Whatever I have received, is all Kṛṣṇa’s. He will never say ‘I’ and ‘mine’, and he always chants: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. *Dīna-bhāve nāma laya sakala samaye*, always, *sakala samaye*, always, day and night, twenty four hours a day, every moment he chants: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rama Hare Rama Rama Rama Hare Hare*; with a very, very humble mind and a humble attitude, more humble than a blade of grass laying on the street.

*kṛṣṇa vinā rakṣā-kartā nahi keha āra*  
*kṛṣṇa ye pālaka mātra jānibe āmarā*

*āmi dīna akiñcana sakalera chāra*  
*durgata adhama kichu nāhika āmāra*

This is the attitude, in a humble state of mind—*kṛṣṇa vinā rakṣā-kartā nāhi keha āra*. Kṛṣṇa is the only protector. Firm faith—*kṛṣṇa ye pālaka mātra jānibe āmāra.* Kṛṣṇa is my maintainer, no one else maintains me, only Kṛṣṇa is my maintainer.

*āmi dīna akiñcana sakalera chāra*  
*durgata adhama kichu nāhika āmāra*

I am most degraded, I am a pauper, I have nothing in this material world—*dīna akiñcana*, I am most fallen and degraded. I am more insignificant than a worm in the stool. Only Kṛṣṇa is mine, I am Kṛṣṇa’s, nothing else! This mentality, this is a humble state of mind. In this state of mind you will chant Hare Kṛṣṇa.

## Have the Same Will as Kṛṣṇa

*kṛṣṇera saṁsāre āmi āchi cira-dāsa*  
*kṛṣṇa-icchā mātra kriyā āmāra prayāsa*

This is the mentality; *kṛṣṇera saṁsāre āmi āchi cira-dāsa*—this is Kṛṣṇa’s *saṁsāra*, I am His eternal servant. I am not anyone elses servant, I am Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant in Kṛṣṇa’s *saṁsāra*. What is your activity? *Kṛṣṇa-icchā mātra kriyā āmāra prayāsa*—what Kṛṣṇa wants from me, I do it! I have no separate will from Kṛṣṇa. This is the mentality; *kṛṣṇa-icchā mātra kriyā āmāra prayāsa*. For example, Kṛṣṇa said to Arjuna, “Go and fight!” This is the teaching in *Bhagavad-gītā,* but Arjuna was not willing, “No, I won’t fight. I’ll go to the forest and become a *sannyāsī* and meditate there.” But Kṛṣṇa did not want that. Kṛṣṇa wants Arjuna to fight and the fighting was very fierce. That is Kṛṣṇa’s will. And he heard *Bhagavad-gītā* from Kṛṣṇa and understood. Then at last he said,

*arjuna uvāca*  
*naṣṭo mohaḥ smṛtir labdhā*  
*tvat-prasādān mayācyuta*  
*sthito ’smi gata-sandehaḥ*  
*kariṣye vacanaṁ tava*

**“Arjuna said: My dear Kṛṣṇa, O infallible one, my illusion is now gone. I have regained my memory by Your mercy, and I am now firm and free from doubt and prepared to act according to Your instructions.”**

O Acyuta, O Kṛṣṇa, all my delusions are now gone. I was thinking ‘I’ and ‘mine’, my brothers, my friends how can I kill them? Now his delusion has disappeared; *naṣṭo mohaḥ smṛtir labdhā*—no forgetfulness now; *tvat-prasādān mayācyuta*, “By Your mercy I can understand it.”

Why shall I stay here, let me go there and do something else. All right, if you want you go, all right, go! Everyone has this, “*Gurudeva*, I will do this thing, I want to do this thing in this way, go there, do this business, like this, then I will do that.” All right, go! Everyone has this separate will. But who accepts Kṛṣṇa’s will? And who knows what is Kṛṣṇa’s will? No separate will! What is Kṛṣṇa’s will, that is what I should accept, and that becomes my will, and one should accept it like that.

*āmi kartā āmi dātā āmi pāla ita*  
*āmāra ye deha geha santāna vanitā*

*āmi vipra āmi śūdra āmi pitā mātā*  
*āmi rāja āmi prajā santānera gati*

*yei sarva-buddhi chāḍi kṛṣṇe kara mati*  
*kṛṣṇa-kartā kṛṣṇa-icchā mātra balavatī*

*kṛṣṇera ye haya icchā tāhāi kariba*  
*nija icchā anusāre kichu nā cintisa*

Giving up all ‘I’ and ‘mine’; “I am the doer. I am a great charitable person. I give so much charity, so many donations to so many organizations, to so many people. I am the maintainer. This is my body, my home, my wife, my son, my daughter. I am a *brāhmaṇa*. I am a *kṣatriya*. I am a *śūdra*. I am a *vaiśya*. I am a father, I am a husband, I am king, I am a citizen, I am the father of so many children.” So much ‘I’ and ‘mine’. Give up all this ‘I’ and ‘mine’! Completely surrender unto Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa is mine, I am Kṛṣṇa’s, nothing else. This is the only thought; Kṛṣṇa is the doer, Kṛṣṇa is the protector, Kṛṣṇa is the maintainer. I am not the doer, I am not the maintainer, I am not the protector. Kṛṣṇa’s will is the Supreme will, very powerful will. What Kṛṣṇa’s will is, that is my will. I have no separate will, I do what Kṛṣṇa wants. I accept Kṛṣṇa’s will. I shall never think according to my will. This is completely surrendered. This is how one should be in a humble, very humble state of mind and chant: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*.

## Complete Surrender—Cry for Kṛṣṇa

This is known as *ātma-nivedana*, completely surrendered dedication. If you develop this state of mind and chant Hare Kṛṣṇa, then there will be no scope of committing *aparādha.* Then you will chant the pure name, otherwise you cannot chant the pure name. You may be chanting for one crore of lives but you cannot attain Kṛṣṇa. It is either *nāmābhāsa* or *nāma-aparādha*. Therefore this instruction is given:

*aparādha nāhi chāḍi nāma yadi laya*  
*sahasra sādhane tāra bhakti nāhi haya*

**You may be performing so much** ***sādhana*****, but if you have not given up** ***nāma-aparādha*****,** ***bhakti*** **will never develop, you cannot develop** ***bhakti*****.**

*ataeva bhakti labhe yadi lobha haya*  
*daśa-aparādha chāḍi kari nāmāśraya*

*eka eka aparādha satarka ha-iyā*  
*yatanete chāḍi citte vilāpa kariyā*

*nāmera caraṇe kare dṛḍha nivedana*  
*nāme kṛpā ha-ile aparādha vidhvaṁsana*

Thus for one who is very, very serious how to develop *bhakti*, if he develops greed for *bhakti*, then he must give up the ten *nāma-aparādhas*. Completely surrender unto the holy name and chant the pure name as instructed by Śrīmān Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Develop that state of mind—*eka eka aparādha satarka ha-iyā, yatanete chāḍi citte vilāpa kariyā*. One after another give up these *aparādhas,* ten *aparādhas,* and cry in your heart, “Yes, I am a great *aparādhī*, I am a great offender, yes, I am a great offender.” Just cry, “My Lord, You will never find such an offender.” Just admit this before Kṛṣṇa, frankly admit it. This admittance is required. That is crying in the heart, *citte vilāpa kariyā.* “I have commited this *aparādha*, that *aparādha*, this *aparādha*, all *aparādhas*, thousands and thousands of times. Not once or twice, O Lord, but thousands and thousands of times. I have committed all *aparādhas*. I am a great offender. You will never find such a great offender as I am in this whole world, O my Lord.” This is how to cry, cry in the heart—*citte vilāpa kariyā*, *yatanete chāḍi citte vilāpa kariyā*.

*nāmera caraṇe kare dṛḍha nivedana*  
*nāme kṛpā ha-ile aparādha vidhvaṁsana*

The means how to destroy *nama-aparadha* is given here. In this way one should cry and admit it. Frankly admit it before Kṛṣṇa that “I have committed this *aparādha*—not once, not twice, but a thousand times.” Cry and chant: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare.*

If you cry before the holy name, cry before Kṛṣṇa, if the holy name will shower His mercy on you, then all your *aparādhas* will be destroyed, otherwise your *aparādhas* will never be destroyed.

## Chant Twenty Four Hours a Day

These instructions are given, on how to give up *nāma-aparādha*, and how *nāma-aparādha* will be destroyed:

*aviśrānte nāme nāma-aparādha jaye*  
*tāhe aparādha kabhu sthāna nahe pāye*

*dibā-rātra nāma laye anutāpa kare*  
*tabe aparādha jaye nāma-phala dhare*

*aparādha gate śuddha-nāmera udaya*  
*śuddha-nāma bhāva-maya āra prema-maya*

These directions are given in the *Hari-nāma-cintāmaṇi* by Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura. *Aviśrānta* means constantly, day and night, twenty four hours chanting. That means when you are taking rest, sleeping, you may not be chanting, otherwise all the time that you are awake you must be chanting, that is *aviśrānta-nāma*. Six hours out of twenty four hours for sleeping, eighteen hours you are awake. In that eighteen hours whilst you are awake never forget the holy name. In these eighteen hours constantly chanting, remembering, thinking, that is *aviśrānta-nāma*. Do like that, then all your *nāma-aparādhas* will be destroyed—*aparādha kabhu sthāna nahe pāye*—there will be no *aparadha*, that is what one can do. Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura gives this instruction:

*diba-rātra nāma laye anutāpa kare*  
*tabe aparādha jaye nāma-phala dhare*

Day and night, *dibā-rātra*—*dibā* means day, *rātra* means night. Day and night you chant and you remember. Sometimes chant, sometimes remember in the mind and repent, repent, repent—repentance. “I am a great offender, I have committed so many *aparādhas*—not once, not twice, but a thousand times I have committed these *aparādha*’s.” Therefore, just admit and just repent.

*āmāra jīvana, sadā pāpe rata,*  
*nāhika puṇyera leśa*  
*parere udvega, diyāchi ye kata,*  
*diyāchi jīvere kleśa*

*nija sukha lāgi’, pāpe nāhi clari,*  
*dayā-hīna svārtha-para*  
*para-sukhe duḥkhī, sadā mithyā-bhāṣī,*  
*para-duḥkha sukha-kara*

*aśeṣa kāmanā, hṛdi mājhe mora,*  
*krodhī, dambha-parāyaṇa*  
*mada-matta sadā, viṣaye mohita,*  
*hiṁsā-garva vibhūṣaṇa*

*nidrālasya hata, sukārye virata,*  
*akārye udyogī āmi*  
*pratiṣṭhā lāgiyā, śāṭhya-ācaraṇa,*  
*lobha-hata sadā kāmī*

*e hena durjana, saj-jana-varjita,*  
*aparādhī nirantara*  
*śubha-kārya-śūnya, sadānartha-manāḥ,*  
*nānā duḥkhe jara-jara*

*vārdhakye ekhana, upāya-vihīna,*  
*tāte dīna akiñcana*  
*bhakativinoda, prabhura caraṇe,*  
*kare duḥkha nivedana*

This is a song by Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura and he has prayed for us. He is not like that, he speaks for us. This is what we are like.

**“I am an impious sinner and have caused others great anxiety and trouble. I have never hesitated to perform sinful acts for my own enjoyment. Devoid of all compassion, concerned only with my own selfish interests, I am remorseful seeing others happy. I am a perpetual liar, and the misery of others is a source of great pleasure for me. The material desires within the core of my heart are unlimited. I am wrathful, devoted to false pride and arrogance, intoxicated by vanity, and bewildered by worldly affairs. Envy and egotism are the ornaments I wear.”**

Six hours sleep is all right, no more than six hours, otherwise laziness will come. Passion and ignorance will develop, no *sattva-guṇa*. Do not sleep more than six hours and eighteen hours awake, eighteen hours chanting and remembering the holy name.

**“Ruined by laziness and sleep, I resist all pious deeds; yet I am very active and enthusiastic to perform wicked acts. For worldly fame and reputation I engage in the practice of deceitfulness. Thus I am destroyed by my own greed and am always lustful. A vile, wicked man such as me, rejected by godly people, is a constant offender. I am devoid of all good works, forever inclined toward evil, worn out and wasted by various miseries. Now in old age, deprived of all means of success, humbled and poor, Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura submits his tale of grief at the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord.”**

You should submit like this; *aparādha gate śuddha-nāmera udaya, śuddha-nāma bhāva-maya āra prema-maya.* When you are free from all *aparādhas,* then you will chant the pure name. Otherwise you will never chant the pure name. When you chant the pure name, definitely you will have Kṛṣṇa, you will have *prema*. The pure name is full of *prema*. Mahāprabhu gives that *prema*, otherwise you cannot be free from these *aparādhas*. This is the only means of how you can free yourself from all *aparādhas*. Therefore it is said, you must cry, always crying in the heart. Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura has said:

*kabe ha'be bolo se-dina āmār*  
*(āmār) aparādha ghuci', śuddha nāme ruci,*  
*kṛpā-bale ha'be hṛdoye sañcār*

*tṛṇādhika hīna, kabe nije māni',*  
*sahiṣṇutā-guṇa hṛdoyete āni'*  
*sakale mānada, āpani amānī,*  
*ho'ye āswādibo nāma-rasa-sār*

*dhana jana āra, kobitā-sundarī,*  
*bolibo nā cāhi deho-sukha-karī*  
*janme-janme dāo, ohe gaurahari!*  
*ahaitukī bhakti caraṇe tomār*

**“Please tell me, when will that day be mine when my offences will end and a taste for the pure holy name will be infused within my heart by the power of divine grace? Feeling myself lower than a blade of grass, summoning up the quality of forbearance in my heart, showing respect to all, and being freed from all false pride, when will I taste the essence of the liquid nectar of the holy name? Wealth, following, beautiful women as described in worldly poetry—I do not want any such bodily pleasures. O Lord Gaurahari, please give me unmotivated devotion to Your lotus feet birth after birth.”**

*Kabe ha’be bolo se-dina āmāra*, when will that day, that auspicious day come in my life? This is repentance, this is crying and begging. You have to do it, otherwise you will never be free from *nāma-aparādha*, the pure name will never come out. This is most important.

*sadā nāma la-iba, yathā-lābhete santoṣa*  
*eita ācāra kare bhakti-dharma-poṣa*

**“One should strictly follow the principle of always chanting the holy name, and one should be satisfied with whatever he attains easily. Such devotional behaviour solidly maintains one’s devotional service.”**

Otherwise there is no hope of developing devotion, this is Mahāprabhu’s teaching.

## Tṛṇād Api Sunīcena...

*tṛṇād api su-nīcena*  
*taror iva sahiṣṇunā*  
*amāninā māna-dena*  
*kīrtanīyaḥ sadā hariḥ*

**One who thinks himself lower than the grass, who is more tolerant than a tree and who does not expect personal honour, yet is always prepared to give respect to others, can very easily and always chant the holy name of the Lord, otherwise he cannot chant the holy name.**

Your chanting will either be *nāma-aparādha* or *nāmābhāsa*, the name is not coming out, it is not coming out at all! The grass is specifically mentioned in this verse because everyone tramples upon it, yet the grass never protests. This is *taror iva sahiṣṇunā*, as tolerant as a tree, and this example Haridāsa Ṭhākura displayed. The *pāṣaṇḍīs* dragged him through twenty two bazaars and were whipping him! Severely! Mercilessly! The whip had cut into his flesh, you could see the bone, still he was chanting blissfully: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*.

He never cursed them, he never became angry with them, rather he offered a prayer at the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa; “Please, they do not know what they are doing, they are devoid of knowledge, they are devoid of proper consciousness, please excuse them.” This is their example, they have set up an example for us. This is how he develops this mentality, never proud, never puffed up of his position, and always humbler than an ordinary common man.

This example indicates that a spiritual master or leader should not be proud of his position. “I am a great leader! I am a great *ācārya*! Don’t you know, rascal?” Rather he should think, “I am the lowest of the low.” Always humbler than an ordinary common man, he should go on preaching. He should go on preaching the cult of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu by chanting: *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*.

*ūrdhva-bāhu kari’ kahoṅ, śuna, sarva-loka*  
*nāma-sūtre gāṅthi’ para kaṇṭhe ei śloka*

**“Raising my hands, I declare, ‘Everyone please hear me! String this verse on the thread of the holy name and wear it around your neck for continuous remembrance.”**

*tṛṇād api su-nīcena*  
*taror iva sahiṣṇunā*  
*amāninā māna-dena*  
*kīrtanīyaḥ sadā hariḥ*

String this verse on the thread of the holy name and wear it around your neck for continuous remembrance. When chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* in the beginning, one may commit many offences, definitely, which are called *nāmābhāsa* or *nāma-aparādha*. In these stages there is no possibility of achieving perfect love of Kṛṣṇa by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*. Therefore one must chant the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* according to the principles of *tṛṇād api su-nīcena taror iva sahiṣṇunā*. One should note in this connection that chanting involves activities of upper and lower lips and tongue. Distinctly chant; not very fast indistinct chanting—no, not like that!

Distinctly; upper lip, lower lip and tongue, these three should be utilized, and in the purport Śrīla Prabhupāda has said, “...All three must be engaged in chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*. The words ‘Hare Kṛṣṇa’ should be very distinctly pronounced and heard. Sometimes one may mechanically produce a hissing sound...” that is *aparādha*, *nāma-aparādha*. Instead of chanting with the proper pronunciation, with the help of the lips and tongue. “Chanting is very simple, but one must practice it seriously. Therefore the author of *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja Gosvāmī, advises everyone to keep this verse always strung around his neck.”

*prabhu-ājñāya kara ei śloka ācaraṇa*  
*avaśya pāibe tabe śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇa*

**“One must strictly follow the principles given by Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu in this verse. If one simply follows in the footsteps of Lord Caitanya and the Gosvāmīs, certainly he will achieve the ultimate goal of life, the lotus feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.”**

So this is a guarantee given here; *avaśya pāibe tabe śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇa*—one must strictly follow the principles given by Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu in this verse. If one simply follows in the footsteps of Lord Caitanya and the Gosvāmīs, certainly he will achieve the ultimate goal of life, which is the lotus feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. If you follow in the footsteps of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, you will definitely attain *kṛṣṇa-prema*.

*uttama hañā āpanāke māne tṛṇādhama*  
*dui-prakāre sahiṣṇutā kare vṛkṣa-sama*

**“These are the symptoms of one who chants the Hare Kṛṣṇa** ***mahā-mantra*****. Although he is very exalted, he thinks himself lower than the grass on the ground, and like a tree, he tolerates everything in two ways.”**

*vṛkṣa yena kāṭileha kichu nā bolaya*  
*śukāñā maileha kāre pānī nā māgaya*

**“When a tree is cut down, it does not protest, and even when drying up, it does not ask anyone for water.”**

*yei ye māgaye, tāre deya āpana-dhana*  
*gharma-vṛṣṭi sahe, ānera karaye rakṣaṇa*

**“The tree delivers its fruits, flowers and whatever else it possesses to anyone and everyone. It tolerates scorching heat and torrents of rain, yet it still gives shelter to others.”**

*uttama hañā vaiṣṇava habe nirabhimāna*  
*jīve sammāna dibe jāni’ ‘kṛṣṇa’-adhiṣṭhāna*

**“Although a Vaiṣṇava is the most exalted person, he is prideless and gives all respect to everyone, knowing everyone to be the resting place of Kṛṣṇa.”**

*ei-mata hañā yei kṛṣṇa-nāma laya*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇe tāṅra prema upajaya*

**“If one chants the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa in this manner, he will certainly awaken his dormant love for Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet.”**

*dambho darpo ’bhimānaś ca*  
*krodhaḥ pāruṣyam eva ca*  
*ajñānaṁ cābhijātasya*  
*pārtha sampadam āsurīm*

**“Pride, arrogance, conceit, anger, harshness and ignorance—these qualities belong to those of demoniac nature, O son of Pṛthā.”**

He gives all respect to everyone, he never demands respect. Respect shoud be commanded, not demanded. If you develop this mentality, this attitude of the lowest of the low, more humble than a blade of grass, and if you become as tolerant as a tree and give respect to one and all and do not demand respect for yourself, then you will command respect. You will command respect, thereby never demanding respect. That is a fact. Why does he give respect? Because he knows, everyone is the resting place of Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa is there in the heart of every *jīva,* therefore he pays respect. Respect is paid to Kṛṣṇa not to the body, a Vaiṣṇava knows that.

*ei-mata hañā yei kṛṣṇa-nāma laya*  
*śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇe tāṅra prema upajaya*

**“If one chants the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa in this manner, he will certainly awaken his dormant love for Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet.”**

Definitely! He will achieve *kṛṣṇa-prema*, this is the process. But if you do not adopt this process, then how can you achieve it? You are committing only *nāma-aparādhas*, no pure chanting—*nāma-aparādha* or *nāmābhāsa*, the name is not coming out.

*kahite kahite prabhura dainya bāḍilā*  
*‘śuddha-bhakti’ kṛṣṇa-ṭhāñi māgite lāgilā*

**“As Lord Caitanya spoke in this way, His humility increased, and He began praying to Kṛṣṇa that He could discharge pure devotional service. Wherever there is a relationship of love of Godhead, its natural symptom is that the devotee does not think himself a devotee. Instead, he always thinks that he has not even a drop of love for Kṛṣṇa.”**

Lord Caitanya is the speaker of the *Śikṣāṣṭaka* and when He spoke in this way, what happened? “Kṛṣṇa, please give me pure devotion.” He begged this and nothing else. He is Kṛṣṇa Himself but He is teaching us, coming in the form of a *bhakta*—*bhakta-rūpa*. Kṛṣṇa cries for Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa is begging at the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa, this is Caitanya, “O Kṛṣṇa, give me *śuddha-bhakti*, give me pure devotion only.” He begged like this and that means He is teaching us. We should only beg like this and nothing else!

## Prema Equals Humility and Pridelessness

*premera svabhāva—yāhāṅ premera sambandha*  
*sei māne,—‘kṛṣṇe mora nāhi prema-gandha’*

**“Wherever there is a relationship of love of Godhead, its natural symptom is that the devotee does not think himself a devotee. Instead, he always thinks that he has not even a drop of love for Kṛṣṇa.”**

One who has developed this loving relationship with Kṛṣṇa, this perfect eternal loving relationship with Kṛṣṇa, has the following symptom; “I have no tinge of *bhakti* at all. I have no tinge of *bhakti* at all for Kṛṣṇa. I am devoid of *bhakti*. I am such a rascal, a most degraded and a most fallen person.” This person is prideless and he never thinks that “I am great.” Rather he thinks, “I am the lowest of the low.” He who has *prema-bhakti*, he says, “I have not developed *bhakti* at all, I have no *bhakti* at all for Kṛṣṇa. I have no *bhakti* at all, I am a rascal.” He will never say, “Don’t you know, I am great! I am such a great *ācārya*, a great Vaiṣṇava, great *sādhu*, don’t you know? Rascal, don’t you know me?” Where is his pride? He will never say this. That is a demon, a demon will speak like this. A Vaiṣṇava will never say that. A Vaiṣṇava is *tṛṇād api su-nīcena*, *taror iva sahiṣṇunā*, *amāninā māna-dena*. He will say, “I am the lowest of the low, I am more fallen, more degraded, more sinful than Jagāi and Mādhāi. I am more insignificant than a worm in stool. One who will say my name will lose all his auspiciousness. Do not say my name. I am a sinful person.” He speaks like that—*uttama hañā vaiṣṇava habe nirabhimāna.* He is the most elevated, but he thinks, “I am the lowest of the low.” This is the symptom! Mahāprabhu has taught us this; *sei māne*,—‘*kṛṣṇe mora nāhi prema-gandha*’. “I have no *bhakti* at all for Kṛṣṇa, I am not a devotee at all, I am not a *bhakta* at all.”

## Dīnabandhu—Bhaktavatsala

I heard, O Kṛṣṇa, I heard You are Dīnabandhu. You are the friend of the distressed. You are the friend of the most distressed—*dīna-bandhu*. When I heard this, I became very enthused. I had so much enthusiasm that I thought, “Let me go and approach.” Therefore when I heard this I became inspired, so I have come here to You. You are the friend of the distressed, but coming here I heard, “O, He is Bhaktavatsala.” He is very dear to His devotee—*bhakta-vatsala*. When I heard this, my heart began to throb, “O, I am not a devotee, I have no devotion, then how can I approach Him?” My heart is throbbing now. When I came here I heard that You are Dīnabandhu, You are the friend of the distressed. I am a most distressed person and You are the friend of those who are distressed. So I was very inspired. So I came here to You. Yes, my friend is here, Dīnabandhu. But coming here I heard these people saying, “No, He’s Bhaktavatsala.” Alas! He is dear to His devotee. What is this? I am not a devotee, I have no devotion at all, so my heart is throbbing now. O, I cannot approach Him. I am not a devotee, I have no devotion.

## Amṛta-sindhu

This is the mentality that should be developed. Then you chant Hare Kṛṣṇa and then the pure name will come out—no more *aparādha*. Definitely you will achieve this *kṛṣṇa-prema* and you will attain Kṛṣṇa. This is the teaching of Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

This is the ocean of *amṛta*, ocean of nectar. One drop, just one drop of that ocean will inundate the whole world with *prema*. There will be an inundation of *prema*. This is *amṛta-sindhu*. This is an ocean of nectar, but you are not obtaining one drop. Such a rascal. Water, water, water everywhere, but not a drop to drink. You are surrounded with water, an ocean of water, but you cannot obtain a drop to drink. *Prema-sindhu*, there is an ocean of nectar, but you cannot obtain a drop, you are such a rascal.

Therefore it is said, *nāhi, nāhi, nāhi*—“there is no other way, there is no other way, there is no other way.” These three are discarded—*karma*, *jñāna* and *yoga.* It is clearly said, a Vaiṣṇava never does these things, he completely surrenders unto the holy name.

## Chapter 4 - Śuddha-nāma—Part II

## Prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana

*tabe prabhu śrīvāsera gṛhe nirantara*  
*rātre saṅkīrtana kaila eka saṁvatsara*

**“Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu regularly led congregational chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa** ***mahā-mantra*** **in the house of Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura every night for one full year.”**

*kapāṭa diyā kīrtana kare parama āveśe*  
*pāṣaṇḍī hāsite āise, nā pāya praveśe*

**“This ecstatic chanting was performed with the doors closed so that nonbelievers who came to make fun, could not gain entrance.”**

Chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra* is open to everyone, but sometimes nonbelievers come to disturb the ceremony of chanting. It is indicated herein that under such circumstances the temple doors should be closed. Only bona fide chanters should be admitted; others should not. But when there is large-scale congregational chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa *mahā-mantra*, we keep our temples open for everyone to join, and by the grace of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu this policy has given good results.

*kīrtana śuni’ bāhire tārā jvali’ puḍi’ mare*  
*śrīvāsere duḥkha dite nānā yukti kare*

**“Thus the nonbelievers almost burned to ashes and died in envy. To retaliate, they planned various ways to give trouble to Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura.”**

## There Is No Place for Envy in the Mission of Mahāprabhu

Let the envious people die, let them burn to ashes with the fire produced out of envy. There is no place for envious people in Mahāprabhu’s *saṅkīrtana* movement. Those who develop envy, will be burned to ashes in the fire produced out of envy, Mahāprabhu will do that.

This *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* is the post graduate study. It is not primary, nor is it a study in the secondary school stage. This is a post-graduate study. It is not meant for ordinary students. It is for those who are students of the post-graduate class. I do not know how many there are in this post-graduate class! Who can understand it, that is my question? No one can understand it because all students are either primary class or pre-primary class. How can they understand *Caitanya-caritāmṛta,* which is the post-graduate study? This is my bad luck. I have primary class or pre-primary class students. I am a fool. I have no students, so to whom shall I teach?

## Parama Āveśe

*kapāṭa diyā kīrtana kare parama āveśe*  
*pāṣaṇḍī hāsite āise, nā pāya praveśe*

**“This ecstatic chanting was performed with the doors closed so that nonbelievers who came to make fun could not gain entrance.”**

This *parama āveśe* is significant. *Parama āveśe* means in English, Prabhupāda has translated, ‘very high in an ecstatic condition’. Mahāprabhu’s *kīrtana* is like that, very high in an ecstatic condition, ecstatic chanting. And who are allowed to take part? Prabhupāda in his purport says, only bona fide chanters. Only bona fide chanters should be admitted, others should not. Who are bona fide chanters? Is there a bona fide chanter? What is meant by bona fide chanter?

He who can chant the pure name, who can taste the nectar, the mellow emanating from the pure name, *prema-nāma*. The name has an adjective to it, this is *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*. It is not mere *nāma-saṅkīrtana*. This is *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*, there is an adjective, adjective to *nāma—prema-nāma. Prema* is the adjective here. So those who can do *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana,* they are bona fide chanters. Only they are admitted, others are not. Mahāprabhu was admitting such chanters, they are bona fide chanters. Those who can do *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*. Others are not admitted, therefore the doors are closed.

Mahāprabhu started His *nāma-saṅkīrtana* when He returned from Gayā. He met His *guru*, Śrīpāda Īśvara Purī in Gayā and He was initiated by him. Coming back from Gayā He started His mission for which He had appeared there. That mission was not *hari-nāma-saṅkīrtana*. That mission was to give *kṛṣṇa-prema*.

*namo mahā-vadānyāya*  
*kṛṣṇa-prema-pradāya te*  
*kṛṣṇāya kṛṣṇa-caitanya-*  
*nāmne gaura-tviṣe namaḥ*

**“I offer my respectful obeisances unto the Supreme Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who is more magnanimous than any other** ***avatāra*****, even Kṛṣṇa Himself, because He is bestowing freely what no one else has ever given—pure love of Kṛṣṇa.”**

He is the most munificent incarnation because He gives *kṛṣṇa-prema*. No *avatāra* gives *kṛṣṇa-prema*, even when Kṛṣṇa came in His own form, He never gave it. He keeps it hidden. But when Kṛṣṇa comes in the form of Gaura, He distributes it to one and all indiscriminately.

## Mahāprabhu Garlands the Entire World

*sei dvāre ācaṇḍāle kīrtana sañcāre*  
*nāma-prema-mālā gāṅthi’ parāila saṁsāre*

**“Thus Mahāprabhu spread** ***kīrtana*** **even among the untouchables. He wove a wreath of the holy name and** ***prema*****, with which He garlanded the entire material world.”**

*Nāma* and *prema* woven together, made a garland. That is *nāma-prema-mālā*. This is like a *mālā*, *nāma* and *prema*, the holy name and *prema* woven together. That is *nāma-prema-mālā*. Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu made a garland and garlanded the entire material world. But one who sleeps, he will lose his share—if you sleep, then you lose your share. Finished!

## Gaura Is the Combination of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa

Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu is the combined form of Rasarāja Kṛṣṇa and Mahābhāvamayī Rādhā, Kṛṣṇa and Rādhā combined together. Kṛṣṇa is *rasa-rāja, śṛṅgāra-rasa-rāja*, the king of conjugal mellow. Rādhārāṇī is *mahābhāva-mayī, mādanākhyā-mahābhāva-mayī*, the personification of *mahābhāva*. These two combined together are Gaura.

You will find that combination means union. Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are combined together so there is union. What is Rādhārāṇīs mood? Rādhārāṇī always feels separation from Kṛṣṇa. She is always crying in that mood, feeling acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa. That is called *vipralambha-bhāva*. In Sanskrit, union is called *sambhoga* and separation is called *vipralamba* or *viraha*.

Therefore Gaurāṅga is the combination of union and separation—*sambhoga* and *vipralamba*. Two opposites, because union is opposite to separation. In other words in Sanskrit, *sambhoga* is opposite to *vipralamba* and Mahāprabhu is the combination of both union and separation, that is *sambhoga* and *vipralamba*. That means two opposites are there in one container, this is Gaura. This is a very deep *tattva*. *Gaura-tattva* is a very, very deep *tattva*. Two opposites in one container—union and separation. That is Gaura.

## Rādhā-bhāva Is Predominating

When there is union, Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa unite together, then there is some relishment of mellow—*milanānanda*—pleasure. They relish it. But when there is separation, there is the acute pangs of separation. All cry. Rādhārāṇī cries, and that is predominating. The separation, the acute pangs of separation, *vipralamba-bhāva* is predominating. *Rādhā-bhāva* means *vipralamba-bhāva*, though there is union of two *bhāvas*, *sambhoga* and *vipralamba*—union and separation, but the mood of separation is predominating. *Rādhā-bhāva* is predominating, so always crying. Rādhārāṇī was always crying, feeling the acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa. Similarly Gaura was always crying, feeling the acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa because He is in *rādhā-bhāva*. That is mentioned in the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*:

*rādhikāra bhāva yaiche uddhava-darśane*  
*sei bhāve matta prabhu rahe rātri-dine*

**“Just as Rādhikā went mad at the sight of Uddhava, so Lord Caitanya was obsessed day and night with the madness of separation.”**

## Uddhava Visits Vrajabhūmi

Kṛṣṇa sent Uddhava from Mathurā to Vrajabhūmi and said, “Go to Vrajabhūmi.” He gave the message of love. “Uddhava, you go! Deliver it. Console them. They are feeling acute pangs of separation from Me. They are dying, they are all dying. You go! Deliver this message of love and console them.” Kṛṣṇa sent His dear devotee Uddhava.

When Uddhava arrived in Vrajabhūmi, he met Nanda Mahārāja and Yasodā Mātā. Then he went to meet the *gopīs*. The chief of all the *gopīs* is Rādhārāṇī, all the *gopīs* are headed by Rādhārāṇī. They were all feeling the acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa and they were always crying. Their bodies had become skinny, very skinny because they had given up eating and sleeping. They were completely mad after Kṛṣṇa. Wherever they went, they would see Kṛṣṇa’s *līlā*, they were always thinking of Kṛṣṇa. This is *vipralambha-bhāva*, the topmost platform. Uddhava went there and saw their condition. He could not say anything. He thought to himself, “What shall I say? They have such great love for Kṛṣṇa.” Uddhava has no love like this, no tinge of love at all. Uddhava was a *jñāni-bhakta*. He is not a *premi-bhakta*, he is a *jñāni-bhakta*, *prema* is absent. *Jñāna* is dry and *prema* is full of mellow.

The *gopīs* and Uddhava spoke about many topics. Then finally the *gopīs* said, “O Uddhava, you have come from Kṛṣṇa, isn’t it? You are the friend of Kṛṣṇa, the messenger of Kṛṣṇa. Your master is Kṛṣṇa. He has sent you from Mathurā to Vrajabhūmi. O Uddhava, we have developed love towards your master. This is pure love; this is pure and spotless love. There is no tinge of any other desire there, no tinge of lust at all. We are not expecting any return from it. Such love we have developed.” This is known as *sādhya-prīti*. *Sādhya-prīti*—*sādhya, sādhana. Sādhana* in English is the ‘means’ and *sādhya* is the ‘end’—means and end.

## Sādhana and Sādhya

*Sādhana* and *sādhya*—hence *nāma-bhajana* is *sādhana* the means and *prema* is *sādhya*. Therefore we chant Hare Kṛṣṇa for what purpose? To achieve this *prema*, the end—*sādhya*. If we cannot have it, then what is the value of this *bhajana*? What is the value? No value at all, it is useless. Similarly, they say this is *sādhya-prīti* because it is pure love only, nothing else, where no tinge of anything else is desired at all. Do you think this is one-sided? When the question of love comes, there are two sides. Lover and beloved. Object of love and abode of love. Two sides are there. Kṛṣṇa is the object of love, and the *gopīs* headed by Rādhārāṇī are the abode of love. There are two sides, therefore there is reciprocation. Loving reciprocation, it is not one sided.

## This Is Pure Love

“So we have developed love towards your master Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa has also developed love towards us. It is not one-sided, there are two sides, but we have no desire, no other desire at all. We love Kṛṣṇa and we have no other desire, it is desireless—*ahaitukī*. There is no *ahaitukī*—no cause at all, it is causeless. Then you tell us, O Uddhava, you tell us why has such cheating come in this love which is causeless? Why has He cheated us? We have no desire at all, this is pure love. We love Kṛṣṇa and we are not expecting anything in return from Him. This is pure love, *ahaitukī*—causeless. Why has this cheating come, please tell us Uddhava, O messenger from Kṛṣṇa.”

The *gopīs* asked this question. “Why are we now dying? Why are we feeling these acute pangs of separation, which are producing a great heat and we are dying, tell us! Tell us! Is your master such a great cheater? Was he only pretending with us? We have heard that where there is such pure love there is no cheating. It is pure love, there is no question of separation. Then tell us Uddhava. You are His friend, you are His messenger now. If you are a *rasika-jana*, then tell us!”

One who enjoys this mellow, he is *rasika*, he tastes this mellow. He is *rasika*, Kṛṣṇa is *rasika*, He enjoys this mellow. *Śṛṅgāra-rasa-rāja*, the king of conjugal mellow. “So you are His *sakhā*, His friend. So if you would have been *rasika,* then you could answer our question. But if you are not *rasika*—you are *verasika*—you cannot answer our question.” They asked this question. “We think you are a great *paṇḍita.*” Because Uddhava is a disciple of Bṛhaspati, who was a *paṇḍita*. Uddhava is *jñāni-bhakta*, he is also a great *paṇḍita* but he is not *rasika*. *Jñāna* means dry, there is no mellow. “So you cannot give an answer to our question, therefore we can understand that you may be a scholar, a great scholar, but you have no knowledge about *rasa-śāstra*. You are completely ignorant of it—*anabhijña*.” The *gopīs* spoke like this.

“All right Uddhava, listen, we are greatly affected and distressed because of the separation from your master, Kṛṣṇa. But we are more distressed that these pure love affairs have become stained. This love has no cause, why has such a stain come? That gives us more pain. In our love there is nothing artificial. It is pure and quite natural. Why has such separation come? It is intolerable. When love is artificial, this is expected. But this is pure love, it is not artificial, it is quite natural. Why has this separation come?” They asked this question.

“Then today when these circumstances come then it is proved that this love is artificial, it is not pure. This is a problem. When we are completely free from any fault, then so many faults will come to us. Therefore the people of the world will never develop love towards Kṛṣṇa at all. This will take place. This gives us too much pain. What will be more painful than that? Tell us Uddhava, tell us! Tell us, Uddhava, why has this taken place?”

Saying this, their minds, their life, their bodies, all their senses and the activities of all their senses became completely filled with the thought of Kṛṣṇa. This is pure love. They became completely forgetful of what is good and what is bad, they were completely mad!

*iti gopyo hi govinde*  
*gata-vāk-kāya-mānasāḥ*  
*kṛṣṇa-dūte samāyāte*  
*uddhave tyakta-laukikāḥ*

They gave up all their shyness and all shame. They were completely mad, they were completely forgetful of what is good or bad. They were so overwhelmed with the thought of Kṛṣṇa; *gata-vāk-kāya-mānasāḥ*, their minds, their bodies, their speech, everything was overwhelmed with the thought of Kṛṣṇa.

Uddhava was a stranger, a newcomer, but they forgot everything; how could they act so shamelessly before a newcomer? Because they had forgotten everything, they became overwhelmed with the thought of Kṛṣṇa. This is the result of *kṛṣṇa-prema*. Then they cried piteously. They cried: *hā kṛṣṇa*! *hā vrajanātha*! *hā gopīvallabha, ārtināśana*! They were sitting and then they stood up calling, “O Kṛṣṇa! O master of Vrajabhūmi! O husband of the *gopīs*! O destroyer of the distress of the *gopīs*!” Calling out and shouting like that, they stood up.

At that time they looked in the direction of Mathurā, raising their hands they were saying, “O Vrajaprāṇa, life of the residents of Vrajabhūmi, please come once and see the condition of Vrajabhūmi. From our very childhood we have developed love towards You, we do not know anything but You. From childhood we are all Yours. Now we are drowning in the ocean of lamentation. This is a very deep ocean, an unfathomable ocean of lamentation. Please come once to Vrajabhūmi, please give us Your lotus feet, that is our life. Let us get life.” They are completely forgetful of all shame.

Śukadeva Gosvāmī therefore says, *tyakta-laukikāḥ*, they have given up all shame and are always thinking of all the *līlās*, all the conjugal loving dealings they had with Kṛṣṇa. They were remembering, describing and singing about them also. Thus they all became completely mad, and the damsels of Vrajabhūmi sang.

*gāyantyaḥ prīya-karmāṇi*  
*rudantyaś ca gata-hriyaḥ*  
*tasya saṁsmṛtya saṁsmṛtya*  
*yāni kaiśora-bālyayoḥ*

**“Thus speaking, the** ***gopīs*****, whose words, bodies and minds were fully dedicated to Lord Govinda, put aside all their regular work now that Kṛṣṇa’s messenger, Śrī Uddhava, had arrived among them. Constantly remembering the activities their beloved Kṛṣṇa had performed in His childhood and youth, they sang about them and cried without shame.”**

Uddhava saw all this and he became amazed, and also he glorified himself; “Coming to Vrajabhūmi I have been glorified. My life has become glorified.” In his own mind he is saying:

*vande nanda-vraja-strīṇāṁ*  
*pāda-reṇum abhīkṣṇaśaḥ*  
*yāsāṁ hari-kathodgītaṁ*  
*punāti bhuvana-trayam*

**“I repeatedly offer my respects to the dust from the feet of the women of Nanda Mahārāja’s cowherd village. When these** ***gopīs*** **loudly chant the glories of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the vibration purifies the three worlds.”**

Uddhava said to himself, I pay my obeisances at the lotus feet of the *gopīs*, the damsels of Vrajabhūmi. I desire to obtain the dust from their lotus feet because the *kṛṣṇa-gīta*, *kṛṣṇa-līlā-kāhinī*, what they are singing, the wonderful pastimes of Vrajabhūmi and Kṛṣṇa, that will purify the three worlds. I want the dust from their lotus feet and I will place it on my head. That will be the ornament for my head. If I can have it then I will think that my life has become successful and my heart, which is full of dry *jñāna*, will be soaked with some mellow. Then my life will become successful.

## Uddhava Sees Rādhārāṇī

Then Uddhava came to a *kuñja*, this was the *kuñja* where Rādhārāṇī was laying down. Rādhārāṇī is the embodiment of *kṛṣṇa-viraha*. If the separation from Kṛṣṇa will assume a body, that is Rādhārāṇī. Rādhārāṇī is the embodiment of *kṛṣṇa-viraha*, the embodiment of separation from Kṛṣṇa. Her eight intimate *sakhīs* were all sitting around Her. Rādhārāṇī is laying on the ground and Her head is on the lap of Her girl companions, Her intimate *sakhīs*. Her whole body is cold as if there is no life—if life leaves the body, then the body becomes cold—she could not speak anything. Her condition is as if She is dying, She is very feeble and She is calling to Her girl companions, the *sakhīs*. “*Sakhī*, what shall I say?” This is the acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa, who is Gokulapati. “What shall I say?” This is acute pangs of separation, great heat and a very high fever. “Such a high fever, such heat! Such pain! More painful than very dreadful poison—*kālakūṭa-viṣa*. It is more unbearable than the falling of the thunderbolt of Indra. So unbearable, such pain I am getting, such pain, acute pangs of separation, and every moment it is piercing My heart, it completely devastates My heart. I cannot tolerate it, I cannot tolerate it anymore, I cannot tolerate, I cannot tolerate. I see there is no need of keeping this body alive. I want to give up this body immediately.”

Then after a few moments Rādhārāṇī said, “O *sakhī*, death is not coming to Me. There is a great obstacle in the coming of death. There is a great obstacle, a great hindrance in the coming of death to Me.”

## Rādhārāṇīs Message to Kṛṣṇa—Mathurānātha

All of a sudden She looked up at the sky and a crow was flying by, this crow happened to be flying towards Mathurā. Pointing at that crow, Rādhārāṇī said, “Hey crow! Are you going to Mathurā? Please hear Me! Don’t go anywhere else, directly go to Mathurā. There is a king named Mathurānātha, the Lord of Mathurā. The king of Mathurā is Kṛṣṇa. He is there now, so meet Him, pay your obeisances to Him and then deliver this message to Him. Whatever message I am giving to you, deliver it. All right? If someone’s house is on fire, what is the first duty of the owner? The first duty is, if he has domestic animals, then he should first release them. You may be burned to ashes, but do not allow the domestic animals to be burnt. The domestic animal is a cow. The householder’s domestic animal is the cow. Hence the first duty of the householder is to release the cows, open the door, release them. Let them get out, do not allow the cows to be burnt. My body is like a house, and this house is now on fire. Who has set it on fire? He has set it on fire. Tell Him, O crow, tell Him that My life is like a domestic animal, it cannot get out now. What is the cause that this animal cannot get out? Why? The door is not open! Why won’t the door open? A strong bolt is there. There is a very strong bolt on the door. So tell Him, let Him come and unbolt it. Do you want to know what that bolt is? When He left Vrajabhūmi, He told us, ‘I will come back, I will come back.’ But He’s not coming back. This is a very strong bolt. So let Him come and unbolt it. This is the only hope by which we are surviving. But He is not coming back. This is a strong bolt.”

This is Rādhārāṇīs mood. The feeling of acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa, She is dying.

“This whole house is on fire, the body and My life is like a domestic animal and it is burning now. It cannot get out, there is a very strong bolt on the door. Let Him come and unbolt it.” That means let Him withdraw His words.

## Śyāma-nāma

Then after sometime, looking at all Her dear girl companions, *aṣṭa-sakhīs—*Her eight intimate girl companions—Rādhārāṇī said, “O My dear girl companions, immediately take Me to the bank of the Yamunā. Place Me underneath the *kadamba* tree there on the banks of the Yamunā. Take Me there immediately because the Yamunā and the *kadamba* are My best friends. They are My friends at the end of My life. Then get some mud from the Yamunā and smear it on My whole body. After that, write Śyāma, write the name of Śyāma, *śyāma-nāma* on My whole body. Then bring *tulasī-mañjarīs* and place them on My body, because *nāma nāmī abhinna*; the name of Śyāma and Śyāma are non-different. Then all of you sit around Me and when My last breath leaves Me, at that time, all of you shout, Hari! Hari! Hari!”

Uddhava was just standing there looking at everything and hearing everything, the acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa, most acute pangs. Rādhārāṇī is in a dying condition, She is going to die. That condition is always felt by Gaurāṅga. Uddhava was looking, standing there, gazing with open eyes, dilated eyes, hearing everything that Rādhārāṇī was saying out of madness. There are various types of insane raving speech, *udghūrṇā citra-jalpa*. Uddhava could understand, “This must be Rādhikā, this must be Rādhikā. I have heard many times from my friend Kṛṣṇa about Her. And when Kṛṣṇa was sleeping, with every breathe that name was being repeated by Him. With every breath of Kṛṣṇa that name is being said—*rādhe, rādhe, rādhe*. I have heard all this in Mathurā from My friend. This must definitely be Rādhikā.” And Gaura, He is also feeling this condition.

## Gaura Also Feels the Madness of Separation from Kṛṣṇa

*rādhikāra bhāva yaiche uddhava-darśane*  
*sei bhāve matta prabhu rahe rātri-dine*

**“Just as Rādhikā went mad at the sight of Uddhava, so Lord Caitanya was obsessed day and night with the madness of separation.”**

*rādhikāra bhāva-mūrti prabhura antara*  
*sei bhāve sukha-duḥkha uṭhe nirantara*

*śeṣa-līlāya prabhura kṛṣṇa-viraha-unmāda*  
*bhrama-maya ceṣṭā, āra pralāpa-maya vāda*

**“The heart of Lord Caitanya is the image of Śrī Rādhikās emotions. Thus feelings of pleasure and pain arise constantly therein. In the final portion of His pastimes, Lord Caitanya was obsessed with the madness of separation from Lord Kṛṣṇa. He acted in erroneous ways and talked deliriously.”**

He who is Kṛṣṇa Himself felt the madness of separation from Kṛṣṇa because *rādhā-bhāva* is predominating. This is *vipralamba-bhāva*, feeling the acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa. Therefore Mahāprabhu feels the same condition, the same *vipralambha-bhāva*, the feeling of separation from Kṛṣṇa because Mahāprabhu’s *līlā* is *rādhā-bhāva* and crying.

*kāhāṅ mora prāṇa-nātha muralī-vadana*  
*kāhāṅ karoṅ kāhāṅ pāṅ vrajendra-nandana*

*kāhāre kahiba, kebā jāne mora duḥkha*  
*vrajendra-nandana vinu phāṭe mora buka*

**“Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu used to express His mind in this way: ‘Where is the Lord of My life, who is playing His flute? What shall I do now? Where should I go to find the son of Mahārāja Nanda? To whom should I speak? Who can understand My disappointment? Without the son of Nanda Mahārāja, My heart is broken.”**

“Where is the Lord of My heart, where is Lord Muralīvadana, Kṛṣṇa, who has a flute on His lips.” *Muralī-vadana kāhāṅ karoṅ kāhāṅ pāṅ vrajendra-nandana.* “What shall I do? Where shall I go, where shall I find the son of Nanda Mahārāja?” Saying and crying, *kāhāre kahiba, kebā jāne mora duḥkha vrajendra-nandana vinu phāṭe mora buka.* “To whom shall I tell this tale of woe? Is there anyone who can feel this pain, the acute pangs of separation? Such an unbearable pain I am feeling in My heart. Is there anyone to whom I can speak and tell this tale of woe? Who can understand the pain I am feeling in My heart?” *Vrajendra-nandana vinu phāṭe mora buka.* “My heart is splitting, feeling the pangs of separation from Vrajendra-nandana, the son of Nanda Mahārāja.” This is Mahāprabhu speaking and crying like that, exactly like Rādhārāṇī. So this is *viraha-bhāva*, the pangs of separation, the acute pangs of separation. Mahāprabhu is the combined form of Rasarāja Kṛṣṇa and Mahābhāvamayī Śrī Rādhā. But why does He feel such acute pangs of separation? These two are combined, there is union, still, why does He feel this separation?

*caturtha ślokera artha ei kaila sāra*  
*prema-nāma pracārite ei avatāra*

**“I have given the essential meaning of the fourth verse: this incarnation \[Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu\] descends to propagate the chanting of the holy name and spread love of God.”**

*dui hetu avatari’ lañā bhakta-gaṇa*  
*āpane āsvāde prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*

*sei dvāre ācaṇḍāle kīrtana sañcāre*  
*nāma-prema-mālā gāṅthi’ parāila saṁsāre*

**“Thus with two intentions the Lord appeared with His devotees and tasted the nectar of** ***prema*** **with the congregational chanting of the holy name. Thus He spread** ***kīrtana*** **even among the untouchables. He wove a wreath of the holy name and** ***prema*****, with which He garlanded the entire material world.”**

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu would express His mind in this way. “Where is the Lord of My life who is playing His flute? What shall I do now? Where should I go to find the son of Mahārāja Nanda? To whom should I speak? Who can understand My disappointment? Without the son of Nanda Mahārāja My heart is broken.”

## The Hidden Treasure

*cirād adattaṁ nija-gupta-vittaṁ*  
*sva-prema-nāmāmṛtam aty-udāraḥ*  
*ā-pāmaraṁ yo vitatāra gauraḥ*  
*kṛṣṇo janebhyas tam ahaṁ prapadye*

**“The most munificent Supreme Personality of Godhead, known as Gaurakṛṣṇa, distributed to everyone—even the lowest of men—His own confidential treasury in the form of the nectar of love of Himself and the holy name. This was never given to the people at any time before. I therefore offer my respectful obeisances unto Him.”**

This is *prema*, the hidden treasure—*gupta-vittam*, it has not been given until now. Now Gaura is distributing this to everyone, even to the untouchables—*pāmaras* and *caṇḍālas*. This is the hidden treasure of Goloka Vṛndāvana. Gaura is now distributing here in the material world, by chanting His own name—*kṛṣṇa-nāma*. He Himself chants. This is not the ordinary name, this is *prema-nāma*, the name that gives *prema*. He relishes the mellow and distributes it.

How did He weave a wreath of *nāma* and *prema*, how did He do it and how did He garland one and all? How is it possible? And this answer should be known. *Nāma* is *sādhana*, it is the means, and *prema* is *sadhya*, it is the end. *Sādhana* and *sadhya*. The name is *sādhana*—the means, and *prema* is *sadhya*—the end, the means and the end. So how are these woven together, the means and the end and how have they become a *māla*—garland? This is the question!

*Nāmera phale kṛṣṇa-pade* ‘*prema*’ *upajaya*: “If you chant the pure name you will develop *kṛṣṇa-prema*.” Chanting the holy name is the means and the end is *kṛṣṇa-prema*; *sādhana, sadhya* woven together and a garland was made. This is the question. How the means and the end can be woven together, how will it be possible?

Do you think that the *prema* that Gaurāṅga gives is ordinary *prema*? No! This is pure *kṛṣṇa-prema*! Spotless. Very, very high. The highest platform. If someone is fortunate and attains this *prema,* he kicks *mukti*, he hates *mukti*, he spits at *mukti*. So out of *kṛṣṇa-prema*, *vraja-prema* is superior, it is the best. And in *vraja-prema*, there are four types: *dāsya*, *sakhya*, *vātsalya* and *mādhurya*. So of these four types of *vraja-prema*, *gopī-prema* is much better. And of *gopī-prema*—*rādha-prema* is the topmost. The topmost is *rādha-prema*.

This *prema* is the hidden treasure—*nija-gupta-vittam*. If someone is fortunate and can achieve this *prema*, *rādhā-prema*, all his desires are fulfilled, he achieves the supreme perfection of life. Śrīmān Nāma is the name and *nāmī* is Kṛṣṇa, the possessor of the name. They are non-different.

*bhajanera madhye śreṣṭha nava-vidhā bhakti*  
*‘kṛṣṇa-prema’, ‘kṛṣṇa’ dite dhare mahā-śakti*

*tāra madhye sarva-śreṣṭha nāma-saṅkīrtana*  
*niraparādhe nāma laile pāya prema-dhana*

**“Among the ways of executing devotional service, the nine prescribed methods are the best, for these processes have great potency to deliver Kṛṣṇa and ecstatic love for Him. Of the nine processes of devotional service, the most important is to always chant the holy name of the Lord. If one does so, avoiding the ten kinds of offences, one very easily obtains the most valuable love of Godhead.”**

If everyone chants the pure name, in other words the offenceless name, then definitely you will get *kṛṣṇa-prema*. So the purport is that if you can chant the offenceless name, the pure name, then all your *anarthas* will be immediately destroyed, otherwise your *anarthas* will never be destroyed. Hence, chant offencelessly the pure name and all your *anarthas* will be immediately destroyed, otherwise they cannot be destroyed.

Then it becomes more condensed, more condensed and more condensed, *anartha-nivṛtti*, then comes *niṣṭhā*. After *anartha-nivṛtti* comes *niṣṭhā*, then comes *ruci*, then comes *asakti*, then *bhāva*, then *prema*, the last attainment is *prema*, that is *sadhya*, the end. Therefore by chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa purely you will attain one stage after another and at last you will reach the topmost platform, that is the platform of *prema*, *kṛṣṇa-prema*. When that *prema* becomes more and more, then it becomes more condensed, more condensed and more condensed. Then what will happen? *Sneha*, *māna*, *praṇaya*, *rāga*, *anurāga*, *bhāva*, *mahābhāva*—the topmost platform is *mahābhāva*.

Therefore, *unnatojjvala-rasa* is the essence of *prema-bhakti*. This is the *śrī* of *sadhya-bhakti*, that is *mādanākhyā*-*mahābhavā*. Then comes *mohanākhyā-mahābhāva. Mohanākhyā*and *mādanākhyā*, these divisions are there. Hence, this is *mādanākhyā*-*mahābhāva* and *mādanākhyā*-*mahābhāva-mayī* is Rādhārāṇī.

Accordingly, how is it that this *sādhana-bhakti*, which is *nāma-saṅkīrtana*, is woven into a garland with *prema*? And what is the skill behind it, and who knows it? *Gaurāṅgera duṭi pada*, *yāra dhana sampada*, *se jāne bhakati-rasa-sāra*. Those devotees who know this are those who have accepted the two lotus feet of Gaurāṅga as their only wealth and asset, they know it—*gaura-prema-rasārṇave*, *se taraṅge yebā ḍube*, *se rādhā-mādhava-antaraṅga*. Those devotees—*premi-bhaktas*—they are always drowning in this ocean of *gaura-prema*—*gaura-prema-rasa*, they know this! Others do not know it! And Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu had come to give us this, the highest type of *prema*—this *radha-prema*.

## Prema-nāma Pracārite

Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu came to propagate this *prema-nāma*, not this ordinary *nāma*—*prema-nāma*, *prema-nāma pracārite*. He Himself tasted the mellow coming out from *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*.

Hence Prema-puruṣottama Gaurāṅga is the father of *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*. The *nāma-saṅkīrtana* that He does, that is *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*! It is not ordinary *saṅkīrtana*. This is not included in it, it is beyond that. This *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana* is filled with *prema*! Whose *saṅkīrtana* is it? This is the *saṅkīrtana* of Mādanākhyā-mahābhāvamayī, Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇīs *saṅkīrtana*. Rādhārāṇī, Mādanākhyā-mahābhāvamayī Rādhārāṇī does this *saṅkīrtana*. This is *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*, this is the essence of *prema-bhakti*, this is the hidden treasure of Goloka Vṛndāvana, this *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*.

There is no difference, *nāma* and *nāmī.* They are non-different. Thus *nāma* feels the acute pangs of separation from *nāmī*. *Nāmī* is Kṛṣṇa. Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī feels the acute pangs of separation from *nāmī*—Kṛṣṇa, so this *nāma-saṅkīrtana* is soaked with Rādhārāṇīs love, pure love. Therefore *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*, *parama-sādhya* is the *sādhya*—the end. The name Himself is *nāmī* therefore Śrīmān Nāma, in this *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana* expresses or manifests His topmost *mūrti*, that is filled with conjugal mellow. So those who are *premika-bhaktas*—*premi-bhaktas*, they know this and they chant, they perform *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*, others cannot perform it. Thus by the chanting of this *prema-nāma* they offer this *prema-bhakti-mālā*, the garland of *prema-bhakti* at the lotus feet of Śrīmān Nāma. They perform this *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*.

The damsels of Vrajabhūmi perform this *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana* under the guidance of Rādhārāṇī, when they feel the acute pangs of separation from *nāmī*—Kṛṣṇa. At that time they chant this *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare*, *Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare* with this feeling of acute pangs of separation—*vipralamba-bhāva*. At that time they chant, thereby they decorate the *nāmī-mūrti* with this garland. Hence the purport is that *abhinna-nāmī*, *nāmī* is non-different from *nāma*. Gaura is also *nāmī*, Kṛṣṇa, non-different from *nāma*, and He chants *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*. Through this chanting He tastes this *prema-rasa*—mellow—the nectarean mellow emanating from conjugal love. He Himself tastes it and thereby He makes a garland of this *nāma-prema* and offers it to everybody. This is *nāma-prema-mālā*.

That means that the *mālā*is woven through the thread of *prema*—*prema-sūtra*. This is all woven together because one thread is there. This *nāma* and *prema* are woven together. What is the thread? The thread is *prema*! In that way *nāma* and *prema* are woven together. This is *prema-bhakti-mālā*and Gaurahari garlands everybody with this *nāma-prema-mālā*.

Thus Mahāprabhu is feeling the same feeling that Rādhārāṇī is feeling, the acute pangs of separation. With that feeling He was chanting *Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare, Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare*. Through this *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana* He is weaving this *mālā*, this garland, and the thread is *prema*. It is not only *nāma-prema-mālā*, but it is also *rasa-maya-mālā*. *Prema* is the fruit. So in *viraha-rasa*, that means the condition of separation, this mellow of this *prema-phala* is tasted. So this *nāma-prema-mālā* is not only *nāma-prema-mālā* but it is also that *prema-rasa* that is being tasted. Therefore this is the *mālā* of the whole mellow—*rasa-maya-mālā*.

*Prema-bhakti* is the essence of *bhakti*. If we prepare a garland of flowers, then one thread is required, and it is woven together. Similarly, when the question of *nāma-prema-mālā*comes, then the thread is *prema*. The *mālā*is woven through the thread of *prema*, it is *nāma-prema-mālā*. This is *japa-mālā*. We are chanting *japa-mālā*. This is the example of *japa-mālā*. That means you should chant this holy name, feeling acute pangs of separation from Kṛṣṇa—then your chanting will be offenseless, the pure name. Then that is *japa-mālā*. This is the most hidden topic there in Goloka. It was given up, until now. Now when Mahāprabhu came, He gave it, *golokera prema-dhana, hari-nāma-saṅkīrtana*—it does not belong to this material world, it belongs to Goloka Vṛndāvana.

## The Impossible Becomes Possible

Hence, *nāma* and *nāmī*, *sādhana* and *sadhya*, *āsvādya* and *āsvāda* (relished and relisher) are non-different, *nāma* and *nāmī* are non-different, means and end are non-different now, *āsvādya* and *āsvāda* are all non-different now! When does it become non-different? In the most ripened stage, in *siddha-rasa* it becomes non-different, otherwise in the beginning there is a difference.

In the most ripened stage this becomes non-different, otherwise there is a difference. Because of the causeless mercy of Gaura we can have it, otherwise we cannot have it. Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu is wonderfully merciful. This is completely impossible, but because of this wonderful mercy the causeless mercy of Gaurāṅga, the impossible becomes possible. The means and end become non-different, otherwise it would not be possible.

*Sambhoga* and *vipralamba*, union and separation, both in one container, otherwise it is impossible. It is only Gaurāṅga’s mercy that is required, otherwise you cannot understand this *tattva*, it is completely impossible, the two cannot be together in one container. If you gain this wonderful mercy, the causeless mercy of Gaurāṅga, then you can understand this *tattva* and you can have it also. How is it that there is no difference between *sādhana* and *sādhya,* means and end? The means is the beginning and the end is the last point. How will they be non-different? How will they be woven together? Where is the beginning? Where is the end? Quite impossible!

## Prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana II

The impossible becomes possible because of the mercy of Prema-puruṣottama Gaurāṅga. Otherwise no one can understand how it is woven together, how He garlanded everyone in this material world and what that *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana* is. It is all possible through *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*. This is a great festival when *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana* goes on. It is a great festival in Goloka Vṛndāvana. That festival descended here in this material world when Mahāprabhu came down here and all His eternal associates came down with Him. They started *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*—the festival that is going on there in Goloka Vṛndāvana descended here.

Though there is no difference between *nāma* and *nāmī*, still *nāma* is more merciful than *nāmī*. One should understand this and Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu has shown it, He has tasted it and He has distributed it. Because of this wonderful mercy of Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhu, this inundation, *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana* brings an inundation of *prema*.

There is a flood here in this material world, thereby He is fulfilling His three desires through *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*. So this is why it is said, *kapāṭa diyā kīrtana kare parama āveśe*, *pāṣaṇḍī hāsite āise*, *nā pāya praveśe.* This ecstatic chanting, *parama āveśe*, is very high in ecstatic condition. This chanting, this *saṅkīrtana* was performed with the doors closed so that non-believers who came to make fun of it could not gain entrance.

Who can take part in this *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana*? Who are bona fide chanters? They are admitted into the congregational party, others are not. Therefore the doors are closed. In the congregation, in the assembly of such *premi-bhaktas*, *prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana* will be possible, otherwise there is no possibility at all!
