# Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad \[BS\]

## Invocation

*auṁ \| saha nāv avatu \| saha nau bhunaktu \|*  
*saha vīryaṁ karavāvahai \| tejasvi nāv adhītam astu \| mā vidviṣāvahai \|*  
*auṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the mantra protect us. May it nourish us. May it give us power. May our studies have power. May we not develop hatred. Peace, peace, peace.**

## Chapter One

### Verse 1

*hariḥ oṁ brahma-vādino vadanti \|*  
*kiṁ kāraṇaṁ brahma kutaḥ sma jātā \|*  
*jīvāma kena kva ca saṁpratiṣṭhāḥ \|\|*  
*adhiṣṭhitāḥ kena sukhetareṣu \|*  
*vartāmahe brahma-vido vyavasthām \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Sages, ascertaining Brahma, speak as follows:**  
**Is the cause of the universe, Brahman, a devatā? From where do we arise and by what do we live, and where do we go? Why do we, controlled by Brahman, exist with this arrangement for suffering?**

**Комментарий:**

With a desire to ascertain the nature of Paramātmā different from conscious and unconscious entities, this Upani?ad begins. In order to glorify knowledge a short story begins.

Sages interested in discussing Brahman speak in the following way.

O knowers or worshippers of Brahman (brahma-vidah)! Is the cause of the universe, Brahman, a devat??

Why do we arise, exist and become destroyed (saṁpratiṣṭhāḥ) by Brahman? Why, controlled by (adhiṣṭhitāḥ) Brahman, do we exist, with this arrangement of birth and suffering?

### Verse 2

*kālaḥ svabhāvo niyatir yadṛcchā \|*  
*bhūtāni yoniḥ puruṣa iti cintyā \|\|*  
*saṁyoga eṣāṁ na tv ātma-bhāvād \|*  
*ātmāpy anīśaḥ sukha-duḥkha-hetoḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some claim time is the cause. Others claim nature is the cause. Others say karma is the cause. Some say there is no cause. Other say the elements are the cause. Others say prakṛti or puruṣa is the cause. If one claims that they all combine because of the jīva, the answer is no. The jīva cannot be the controller of happiness and distress.**

**Комментарий:**

The question “Is the cause Brahman a devatā” is improper since the statement cannot be proved.

Those who know about time say that time (kālaḥ) is the cause of everything. The Lokāyatas say that nature (svabhāvaḥ) is the cause of everything. The mīmāṁsakas think that karma or fate alone (niyati) is the cause. Others say that there is no cause (yadṛcchayā). Others say that the five gross elements (bhūtāni) are the cause. Some say prakṛti is the cause. Some say puruṣa is the cause.

It is not possible to determine any of these as the cause since they cannot withstand good logic. There are many faults in the propositions. It is impossible that any of the items can be the cause of all unconscious or conscious entities.

“Their combination can be the cause of everything. One should not worry about a cause of their combining since the ātmā can be the cause of their combining.” The ātmā cannot independently enjoy happiness or experience suffering. The jīva which experiences happiness and suffering is not the controller of happiness and suffering. The combination cannot take place (na) because of the jīva (ātma-bhāvāt) at all (tu).

### Verse 3

*te dhyāna-yogānugatā apaśyan \|*  
*devātma-śaktiṁ sva-guṇair nigūḍhām \|\|*  
*yaḥ kāraṇāni nikhilāni tāni \|*  
*kālātma-yuktāny adhitiṣṭhaty ekaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Engaged in meditation, the sages saw prakṛti, energy of the Lord, composed of the three guṇas. They saw the Lord who rules over all causes, including time and the jīva.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahman, unchanging, cannot be the material cause. That is explained in this verse. The sages, absorbed in meditation, free of sin, saw prakṛti (śaktim) composed of the three guṇas and which creates the universe on behalf of Brahman. It is called śakti because it has special powers to carry out certain functions. This śakti is also non different from (ātma) Nārāyaṇa (deva), the one attractive Lord, famous in the śrutis. They then saw the Lord, the cause of everything, the cause of all causes to whom no one is superior.

### Verse 4

*tam eka-nemiṁ tri-vṛtaṁ ṣoḍaśāntaṁ \|*  
*śatārdhāraṁ viṁśati-pratyarābhiḥ \|\|*  
*aṣṭakaiḥ ṣaḍbhir viśva-rūpaika-pāśaṁ \|*  
*tri-mārga-bhedaṁ dvi-nimittaika-moham \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They saw the Lord as a wheel with one rim, three layers, sixteen end parts, fifty spokes, twenty intermediate spokes, and six groups of eight. The universal form is the rope. There are three paths, two of which cause illusion.**

**Комментарий:**

They explain the Lord as a wheel to make it easier to understand.

The Lord is endowed with one rim (nemim) called prakṛti endowed with three types (tri-vṛtam), sattva, rajas and tamas. There are sixteen end parts or transformations (elements, senses and mind). There are fifty varṇas of the alphabet as spokes (ara). There are twenty intermediate spokes (pratyarabhiḥ) of twelve months, six seasons and two ayanas, making up the year. There are six groups of eight: there is a group of eight mystic powers and a group of eight directions and eight protectors of the directions. There are eight divisions of prakṛti mentioned in Gītā. There are eight Vasus or eight devatās: Brahmā, Prajāpatis, devatās, gandharavas, Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, Pitṛs and Piśācas. There are eight vastus: apahata-papmā, vijata, vimṛtyu, viśoka, vijaghatsu, apipāsa, satya-saṇkalpa, and satya-kāma. Or there are eight qualities in Brahman and in the jīva: compassion, peace, lack of envy, purity, auspiciousness, lack of effort, generosity, lack of material desire.

The form of the universe is the main rope which binds up the universe so that it cannot move about. The wheel has three paths: the path of the devas, the path of the pitṛs and birth as low beings. The latter two are illusion, making one identify with the body. The sages saw Paramātmā as the wheel with these elements.

### Verse 5

*pañca-sroto’mbuṁ pañca-yony-ugra-vaktrāṁ \|*  
*pañca-prāṇormiṁ pañca-buddhy-ādi-mūlām \|\|*  
*pañcāvartāṁ pañca-duḥkhaugha-vegāṁ \|*  
*pañcāśad-bhedāṁ pañca-parvām adhīmaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**We remember the five streams of the river, the five fierce mouths arising from the elements, five waves, five roots arising from knowledge, five whirlpools, five currents of suffering, with fifty divisions and five divisions.**

**Комментарий:**

Having described the Lord with prakṛti as a wheel they now describe him as a river in order to produce detachment from prakṛti which is designated as śakti. The water divides into five streams: tamas, akṣara, avyakta, mahat and ahaṅkāra. The river has five fierce mouths consisting of the five tānmātrās, arising from the five gross elements (yoni). It has five waves representing the five prāṇas. The river has a root of the five knowledge gathering (buddhi) senses. It has five whirlpools made of the five actions senses. It has five swiftly moving rapids in the form of the five types of suffering—the five gross elements. It has fifty types of syllables. It has five divisions of tama, moha, mahāmoha, tamisra and andha-tamisra. Seeing Paramātmā, we remember (adhīmaḥ) the Lord as the above.

### Verse 6

*sarvājīve sarva-saṁsthe bṛhante \|*  
*asmin haṁso bhrāmyate brahma-cakre \|\|*  
*pṛthag ātmānaṁ preritāraṁ ca matvā \|*  
*juṣṭas tatas tenāmṛtatvam eti \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva is made to wander about in the wheel of great Brahman, who is the life and destruction of all jīvas. Understanding that the jīva, impelled by prakṛti, is different from the Lord, the jīva attains liberation when he receives the mercy of the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The sages contemplated Brahman as the cause and saw the śakti (prakṛti) of the Lord who is the cause of everything. He is Nārāyaṇa. Though unchanging, he designates prakṛti as a cause he is the controller of prakṛti (adhiṣṭhatā). Now the arrangement for the jīva’s suffering is explained.

The Lord gives life to all jīvas (sarvājīve). By whom do we live (kena jīvāmaḥ)? This question is answered. The Lord is the destruction (saṁsṭhe) of everything (sarva). This answers the question of how everything is destroyed. He is great (bṛhante) and thus can destroy everything.

Haṁsa refers to the jīva who goes (ha) through many births in the wheel of Brahman. The jīva is rotated by the wheel. Bhramayan sarva-bhūtāni: I make all living beings wander about. (BG [18.61](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/18.61))

The result of knowledge is explained. Understanding that one is different (pṛthak), being made to wander about by the Lord, and being made the object of love by the Lord (juṣtaḥ), the jīva attains liberation (amrṭatvam). One must understand the difference between the Lord and the self in order to gain liberation.

### Verse 7

*udgītam etat paramaṁ tu brahma \|*  
*tasmiṁs trayaṁ supratiṣṭhā’kṣaraṁ ca \|\|*  
*atrāntaraṁ brahma-vido viditvā \|*  
*līnā brahmaṇi tat-parā yoni-muktāḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**They speak about that supreme Brahman loudly praised as the indestructible, the well-established. Prakṛti, jīva and time take shelter of him. The worshippers of Brahman, those desiring Brahman, knowing the difference of the Lord from everything else, becoming liberated, become similar to Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

Having described the Lord as a wheel, now the sages loudly describe (udgītam) him as Parabrahman, as self-established and as akṣara. There are similar statements:

*nārāyaṇaḥ paraṁ brahma sve mahimni pratiṣṭhitaḥ*

**Nārāyaṇa is the supreme Brahman, situated in his own glory.**

*etad vai tad akṣaraṁ gārgi brāhmaṇābhivadanti*

**The brāhmaṇas call Brahman akṣaram. Bṛhad-ārāṇyaka Upaniṣad**

The three—prakṛti, jīva and time—are situated in Brahman (tasmin), who is fixed in himself. Those who desire to attain Brahman, knowing the difference (antaram) between prakṛti and the jīva, and as well the difference of them from Paramātmā who is the support of all beings, becoming liberated from prakṛti, attain similarity with the supreme Lord, and become invisible to this world (līnāḥ).

### Verse 8

*saṁyuktam etat kṣaram akṣaraṁ ca \|*  
*vyaktāvyaktaṁ bharate viśvam īśaḥ \|\|*  
*anīśaś cātmā badhyate bhoktṛ-bhāvāj \|*  
*jñātvā devaṁ mucyate sarva-pāśaiḥ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord supports the mutually related temporary, visible prakṛti and the eternal, invisible jīva. The jīva is bound by prakṛti because of his nature of enjoying prakṛti. Understanding the Lord, the jīva becomes free from all bondage.**

**Комментарий:**

“Jīva and the Lord are similar. Why is jīva bound by prakṛti and why the Lord is not?”

The Lord (īśaḥ) supports the mutually related (saṁyuktam) temporary, manifest entity (prakṛti) and the unmanifest, eternal entity (jīva). He is not bound up. The jīva (anīśaḥ) is bound by prakṛti since he has a nature of enjoying results of karma (bhktṛ-bhāvāt). The Lord however is untouched by karma since he has no desire to enjoy those results.

*na māṁ karmanī limpati na me karma-phale spṛhaḥ*

**I am not contaminated by karmas. I do not desire the results of karma. BG** [**4.14**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/4.14)

Though the Lord supports prakṛti, he is never subject to karma Thus he is a good adversary of prakṛti, having special jñāna which counteracts it.

### Verse 9

*jñājñau dvāv ajāv īśanīśāv \|*  
*ajā hy ekā bhoktṛ-bhogyārtha-yuktā \|\|*  
*anantaś cātmā viśva-rūpo hy akartā \|*  
*trayaṁ yadā vindate brahmam etat \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord and jīva are both without birth, but the Lord is omniscient and the jīva is ignorant. Prakṛti manifests for giving enjoyment to the jīva. The Lord is unlimited in qualities and is never the doer. If one knows the difference between these three, he becomes liberated.**

**Комментарий:**

The difference between the Lord and the jīva is further explained. Among the two described, one, the Lord (īṣaḥ), is omniscient (jña). The other is ignorant (ajñau) and thus not the Lord (anīśau). Both are without birth. Prakṛti, also without birth, has the purpose of giving enjoyment to the jīva enjoyer. Thought the three are all without birth, they are very different.

“Why does the Lord not enjoy prakṛti like the jīva?” In order to enjoy in this way the jīva must take body after the body. The Lord would have to do the same. The Lord, (viśva-rūpaḥ) has no end to his qualities (anantaḥ). Though the Lord, not dependent on anything, has unlimited qualities such as satya-kāma, he would have to desire results of karma like the jīva. He is not a doer like the jīva (akārtā). Thus he is not an enjoyer of prakṛti like the jīva. The result of knowing the difference between the jīva and the Lord is stated.

If one makes the three (prakṛti, jīva and the Lord) the subject of his meditation, with mutual differences, he becomes liberated (brahmam).

### Verse 10

*kṣaraṁ pradhānam amṛtākṣaraṁ haraḥ \|*  
*kṣarātmānāv īśate deva ekaḥ \|\|*  
*tasyābhidhyānād yojanāt tattva-bhāvād \|*  
*bhūyaś cānte viśva-māyā-nivṛttiḥ \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Prakṛti transforms. The jīva does not transform since he is immortal. The one Lord controls prakṛti and the jīva. By starting meditation on the Lord and developing correct knowledge, at the time of death, one extinguishes the influence of prakṛti.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse further describes the kṣara and akṣara mentioned in previously. This resolves the question, “If knowledge of difference of jīva and Lord gives liberation, what is the use of meditation and contemplation?”

Pradhānam or prakṛti is kṣaram, subject to transformations. The jīva takes what is meant for the Lord’s enjoyment for himself (haraḥ). He is beyond change or transformations because he has no death (amṛtākṣasram). The one Lord, who is bright (devaḥ) because he has no sin, controls (īśate) the jīva and prakṛti. This means only Nārāyaṇa. From starting the cultivation of meditation (abhidhyānāt yojanāt), because of developing correct tattva (tattva-bhāvāt), on giving up the body (ante), one will extinguish all (bhūyaḥ) relationship with prakṛti (viṣṇu-māyā-nivṛttiḥ) or attain liberation. The meaning liberation in the previous verse thus means realizing Brahman (the Lord) while meditating.

### Verse 11

*jñātvā devaṁ sarva-pāśāpahāniḥ \|*  
*kṣīṇaiḥ kleśair janma-mṛtyu-prahāṇiḥ \|\|*  
*tasyābhidhyānāt tṛtīyaṁ deha-bhede \|*  
*viśvaiśvaryaṁ kevala āpta-kāmaḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing the Lord, destroying all piety and sin, destroying all karmas along with weakening of the influence of the body and senses, by this knowledge, he attains a spiritual body full of extreme powers, and becoming liberated, achieves his desire to meet the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

The sequence in attaining liberation is shown. Making the Lord the object by knowing his form as if actually seeing him (devaṁ jñātvā), one destroys all sin (sarva-pāśāpahāniḥ). When one begins to know the form of the Lord as if actually seeing him, all sins and puṇya past and future will be forgiven.

*tad-adhigama uttara-pūrv?rdhayor aśle?a-vin?śau tad-vyapadeś?t \|\|*

**With attainment of Brahman, present sinful actions have no effect and previous karmas are destroyed since that is indicated the śruti. Vedānta-sūtra 4.1.13**

The destruction of karmas happens by the will of the Lord. There is efficient (pra) destruction of karmas (hāniḥ) which cause birth and death (jamana-mṛtu), along with weakening of body and senses which cause suffering (kṣīnaiḥ kleśaiḥ), by the will of the Lord. The Lord decides to destroy all sins and piety, thinking” I will forgive all this when he begins to have knowledge which is like seeing my form directly.”

As a result of this type of knowledge (abhidhyānāt), he develops an extreme (viśva) third power (aiśvaryam) characterized by destruction of all sin etc. and develops a śuddha-sattva body different from the material body (deha-bhede). Liberated (kevalaḥ) from a relationship with prakṛti, his desire to attain Brahma in a particular place (not the material world) is fulfilled (āpta-kāmaḥ).

### Verse 12

*etaj jñeyaṁ nityam evātma-saṁsthaṁ \|*  
*nātaḥ paraṁ veditavyaṁ hi kiñcit \|\|*  
*bhoktā bhogyaṁ preritāraṁ ca matvā \|*  
*sarvaṁ proktaṁ trividhaṁ brahmam etat \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing that the Lord is the antaryāmī of the jīva, nothing else remains to be known. Knowing the Lord as antaryāmī of the jīva, as the antaryāmī of prakṛti, and as having a svarūpa endowed with perfect qualities, one becomes liberated.**

**Комментарий:**

This Parabrahman should be known as the antaryāmī of the jīva (ātma-saṁstham). There remains nothing else to be known, for one who knows this. In Vedārtha-saṁgraha the three types of knowledge of Brahman are: the antaryāmī of the jīvas (enjoyers), antaryāmī of matter (bhogya) and as the Lord having a svarūpa of satyam, jñānam, endowed with qualities such as no karma. Knowing this one becomes liberated (brahmam).

### Verse 13

*vahner yathā yoni-gatasya mūrtir \|*  
*na dṛśyate naiva ca liṅga-nāśaḥ \|\|*  
*sa bhūya evendhana-yoni-gṛhyas \|*  
*tad vobhayaṁ vai praṇavena dehe \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The form of fire within wood is not perceived directly, but its presence is not denied. Later, fire is accepted to have been present within the wood (when the sticks are rubbed). Similarly the Lord as well as the jīva, is perceived, in the jīva, purified by chanting oṁ.**

**Комментарий:**

“That the Lord dwells in the jīva should not be accepted since that cannot be perceived.” Though one cannot see the fire within a stick, by rubbing the stick, fire’s existence is not hidden, since smoke appears (which proves fire was present within). Similarly though one does not have direct perception of the Paramātmā within the jīva, by churning with chanting of oṁ, one realizes Paramātmā.

The svarūpa (mūrtiḥ) of fire which is within the stick, its cause (yoni-gatasya), is not seen directly by the eyes. Later, when the stick is rubbed, fire is accepted as being present within the wood (indhana-yoni-ghṛyaḥ). Similarly by the chanting of oṁ (praṇavena), when the jīva who is like the body of the Lord (dehe), is purified, the Lord is perceived within the jīva, like a gem wrapped in a piece of fine cloth.

### Verse 14

*sva-deham araṇiṁ kṛtvā praṇavaṁ cottarāraṇim \|*  
*dhyāna-nirmathanābhyāsād devaṁ paśyan nigūḍhavat \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Making the jīva the first wood stick, making praṇava the second stick, by rubbing constantly in the form of meditation, one sees the Lord who is hidden.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse shows the method of manifesting the Lord by praṇava. The Lord within the jīva is revealed by meditation using oṁ.

### Verse 15

*tileṣu tailaṁ dadhanīva sarpir \|*  
*āpaḥ srotaḥsv araṇīṣu cāgniḥ \|\|*  
*evam ātmā’tmani gṛhyate’sau \|*  
*satyenainaṁ tapasā yo’nupaśyati \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Oil is obtained from sesame seeds, ghee is obtained from yogurt, water is obtained from currents, and fire is obtained from kindling sticks. Similarly the Lord is obtained from the jīva, by satya and tapas.**

**Комментарий:**

This is further explained. As oil is extracted from seeds by using a pressing machine, the Lord is perceived by the method of satya, tapas etc.

### Verse 16

*sarva-vyāpinam ātmānaṁ kṣīre sarpir ivārpitam \|*  
*ātma-vidyā-tapo-mūlaṁ tad brahmopaniṣat param tad brahmopaniṣat param \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should know the highest truth, Brahman, the all-pervading Lord, who is ghee within milk, through knowledge and tapas.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord who pervades everywhere, like ghee within milk, is to be known (mūlam) by knowledge and tapas. One should know what is promoted as highest truth (param upaniṣat), Brahman. Repetition of the last phrase shows respect and indicates the conclusion of the chapter.

## Chapter Two

### Verse 1

*yuñjānaḥ prathamaṁ manas tattvāya savitā dhiyaḥ \|*  
*agner jyotir nicāyya pṛthivyā adhyābharat \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord, inspiration for intelligence, unite with the mind, after performing agni-hotras, for attaining direct realization of the Lord beyond the earth.**

**Комментарий:**

This is a prayer to the Lord following tapas in the form of sense control which produces realization of the Lord, dwelling within the jīva. May the inspiration (savitā) of intelligence (dhiyaḥ), the Lord, above the earth (adhi pṛthivyāḥ) be joined to the mind (manas), after performing agni-hotra etc (nicāyya jyotir agneḥ) in order to directly perceive the Lord (tattvāya) who is within the jīva.

### Verse 2

*yuktena manasā vayaṁ devasya savituḥ save \|*  
*suvargeyāya śaktyā \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With the permission of the Lord, may we, engaging our minds, be endowed with powers of concentration in order to attain the spiritual world.**

**Комментарий:**

With the permission (save) of the Lord (devasya savituḥ), engaging our minds in the Lord (manasā yuktena), may we become endowed with śakti of meditating on the Lord, for attaining the spiritual world (suvargeyāya).

### Verse 3

*yuktvāya manasā devān suvar yato dhiyā divam \|*  
*bṛhaj jyotiḥ kariṣyataḥ savitā prasuvāti tān \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one offers respect to the great devotees in the spiritual world in one’s mind and concentrates on the shining Lord endowed with greatness, the Lord gives permission to the aspiring persons.**

**Комментарий:**

Why should the Lord give permission simply by addressing him? One asks for permission to make attempts to attain the Lord while offering respects to intimate devotees like Viśvaksena.

Offering respects (yuktvāya) to the previous ācāryas (devān) dwelling in the spiritual world (suvaḥ) by the mind, concentrating on the Lord effulgent (divam), with all powers (bṛhat jyotiḥ) by knowledge in the form of worship (dhiyā), the Lord (savitā) gives permission to those aspiring for liberation (tān). This is what we believe.

### Verse 4

*yuñjate mana uta yuñjate dhiyo \|*  
*viprā viprasya bṛhato vipaścitaḥ \|\|*  
*vi hotrā dadhe vayunāvid eka \|*  
*inmahī devasya savituḥ pariṣṭutiḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The knowers of truth engage their minds and meditations on the Lord. A person who knows the great omniscience of the Lord serves the Lord, who is complete, the greatest, omniscient, and accompanied by the earth.**

**Комментарий:**

At the same time one should perform the activity of concentrating the mind on the Lord.

The knowers of truth (viprāḥ) concentrate (yuñjate) their minds on the Lord. And they engage in meditations (dhiyaḥ) on the Lord. Only the one (eka it) who knows the Lord’s omniscience (vayunāvit) performs services (pariṣṭutiḥ) for the Lord, who is omniscient (vipaścitaḥ) , who is great in qualities (bṛhataḥ), who is complete (viprasya) and who is accompanied by the earth (mahī-devasya), using priests or mantras (hotrā). The person who knows the Lord serves the Lord. By that service he pleases the Lord.

### Verse 5

*yuje vāṁ brahma pūrvyaṁ namobhir \|*  
*viśloka etu pathy eva sūreḥ \|\|*  
*śṛṇvantu viśve amṛtasya putrā \|*  
*ā ye dhāmāni divyāni tasthuḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the sons of Brahmā who reside in radiant places hear my prayer for attaining Brahman. The eternal associates who existed previously also offered prayers to you. May the words composed by me become proper prayers.**

**Комментарий:**

May the sons of Brahmā situated in highest places, hear my prayers for attaining Brahman (yuje brahma). Previously the eternal associates (śūreḥ) offered prayers to you (vām). May the verses made by me (viślokaḥ) have effect. Just as they offered prayers and the Lord accepted those prayers, I also offer prayers, with the hope that they will be accepted. One should offer prayers before starting the practice of attaining the Lord.

### Verse 6

*agnir yatrābhimathyate vāyur yatrādhirudhyate \|*  
*somo yatrātiricyate tatra sañjāyate manaḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The mind can concentrate where fire is destroyed, where the wind does not blow and where the snow is absent.**

**Комментарий:**

One should avoid hot or cold places when starting the practice. The mind can concentrate where fire is destroyed on all sides (abhimathyate), where wind does not blow (adhirudhyate) and where snow is completely absent (atiricyate).

### Verse 7

*savitrā prasavena juṣeta brahma pūrvyam \|*  
*yatra yoniṁ kṛṇavase na hi te pūrtim akṣipat \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You should meditate on Brahman, taking previous permission of the Lord. Concentrate on him. He will not disregard your desire to attain him.**

**Комментарий:**

One should meditate (juṣeta) on Brahman with the previous permission (prasavena) of the Lord (savitrā), since he is pleased by the respects to gurus and prayers to himself. Concentrate (kṛṇavase) the mind (yonim) on the Lord (yatra). The Lord will not reject the fulfilment of your desire (pūrtim).

### Verse 8

*trir unnataṁ sthāpya samaṁ śarīraṁ \|*  
*hṛdīndriyāṇi manasā sanniveśya \|\|*  
*brahmoḍupena pratareta vidvān \|*  
*srotāṁsi sarvāṇi bhayāvahāni \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**With throat, neck and head high, with straight body, one should place the senses in the cavity of the heart by the mind. The wise man will cross the fearful currents of birth by the boat of Brahman.**

**Комментарий:**

What is necessary in yoga is described. The chest, throat and head should be raised (trir unnatam). The body should be straight (samam). One should place all the senses in the cavity of the heart by the mind. By the boat of Brahman, by meditation on Brahman using praṇava, one will cross the fearful currents of birth.

### Verse 9

*prāṇān prapīḍyeha saṁyukta-ceṣṭaḥ \|*  
*kṣīṇe prāṇe nāsikayocchvasīta \|\|*  
*duṣṭāśva-yuktam iva vāham enaṁ \|*  
*vidvān mano dhārayetāpramattaḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Regulating his activities, he should stop the prāṇas, and when the prāṇa is weak, he should exhale through the nostrils. The wise man, being attentive, should control the mind just as a charioteer controls a chariot yoked to a wild horse.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse describes the method of performing prāṇāyāma. One should limit ones endeavors (saṁyukta-ceṣṭaḥ) according to yuktāhāra-vihārasya yukta-cesṭasya karmasu: one should regulate eating, recreation and activities. (BG 6.17) He should stop the prāṇa-vāyu. In order to avoid great pain when stopping the prāṇa, one should exhale through the nose. Just as a charioteer controls a chariot yoked to a wild horse (duṣṭāśva), the wise man with attention should control the mind.

### Verse 10

*same śucau śarkarā-vahni-bālukā- \|*  
*vivarjite śabda-jalāśrayādibhiḥ \|\|*  
*mano’nukūle na tu cakṣu-pīḍane \|*  
*guhā-nivātāśrayaṇe prayojayet \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should practice yoga in a cave without wind, in an even place, devoid of stones, fire and sand, as well as sounds, and not too close to a water body. The place should be favorable for the mind and not cause pain to the eyes.**

**Комментарий:**

Details of the place for practicing yoga are described. The place should be not high or low in some places but level (same). It should be clean (śucau) and devoid of small stones, fire and sand. It should be devoid of the sound of the wind and not too close to a water body. It should be favorable to the mind and not give pain to the eyes. One should practice a place like a cave, which is without wind.

### Verse 11

*nīhāra-dhūmārkānilānalānāṁ \|*  
*khadyota-vidyut-sphaṭika-śaśīnām \|\|*  
*etāni rūpāṇi puraḥ-sarāṇi \|*  
*brahmaṇy abhivyakti-karāṇi yoge \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The first signs that Brahman will be realized in yoga are mist, smoke, the sun, wind, fire, a firefly, lighting, crystal and the moon.**

**Комментарий:**

The traditional signs that Brahman will manifest for the person practicing yoga are described. First Brahman appears like mist, then smoke, then the sun. These are signs that the person will realize Brahman

### Verse 12

*pṛthivy-ap-tejo’nila-khe samutthite \|*  
*pañcātmake yoga-guṇe pravṛtte \|\|*  
*na tasya rogo na jarā na mṛtyuḥ \|*  
*prāptasya yogāgni-mayaṁ śarīram \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**In practicing yoga, the body made of earth, water, fire, air and ether, becomes filled with sattva. The person does not experience disease, old age or death. His body burns up disease by the fire of yoga.**

**Комментарий:**

The results of yoga on the body are described. In practicing yoga, the body made of five elements becomes filled with sattva by the power of yoga. For the person who has begun yoga practice, his body burns up disease by the fire of yoga (yogāgni-śarīram). This means he does not become sick.

### Verse 13

*laghutvam ārogyam alolupatvaṁ \|*  
*varṇa-prasādaṁ svara-sauṣṭhavaṁ ca \|\|*  
*gandhaḥ śubho mūtra-purīṣam alpaṁ \|*  
*yoga-pravṛttiṁ prathamāṁ vadanti \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sages say that the first signs of success in yoga are lightness, freedom from disease, absence of greed for material enjoyment, pleasing complexion, pleasing voice, pleasing fragrance, and minimum of urine and feces.**

**Комментарий:**

The meaning is clear.

### Verse 14

*yathaiva bimbaṁ mṛdayopaliptaṁ \|*  
*tejo-mayaṁ bhrājate tat sudhāntam \|\|*  
*tad vātma-tattvaṁ prasamīkṣya dehī \|*  
*ekaḥ kṛtārtho bhavate vīta-śokaḥ \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as a deity made of clay shines brightly on being smeared with ointments, a person by meditating on the Lord so that he seems to be present, giving up all lamentation, becomes successful.**

**Комментарий:**

The result of realizing Brahman from yoga practice is described. A special deity (sudhāntam) made of (upaliptam) clay or metal, being especially purified by being smeared with colored ointments, shines brightly. Similarly, some person, by meditating on the Lord so that he appears to be present (ātma-tattvam prasamīkṣya), becomes similar to the deity which shines brightly because of the ointments, since he has destroyed the influence of saṁsāra. He becomes successful. Tad vā means “like.”

### Verse 15

*yad ātma-tattvena tu brahma-tattvaṁ \|*  
*dīpopameneha yuktaḥ prapaśyet \|\|*  
*ajaṁ dhruvaṁ sarva-tattvair viśuddhaṁ \|*  
*jñātvā devaṁ mucyate sarva-pāśaiḥ \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When the yogī realizes the Supreme Lord through realization of jīva’s position, similar to a lamp, knowing the pure, unborn, fixed Lord, who is endowed with all tattvas, he becomes free of all bondage.**

**Комментарий:**

When a person engaged in yoga (yuktaḥ) sees within the jīva the infinite Lord with no beginning (param brahma), who is endowed with ornaments and weapons to counteract everything inferior, (sarva-tattvaiḥ), like a jewel wrapped in fine cloth, by chanting oṁ, which is a method to realize the Lord, and which indicates that the jīvātma (ātma-tattvena) is the servant of the Lord, he becomes free of all karmas which bind him. This form of Viṣṇu is described in the chapter describing Viṣṇu’s weapons and ornaments in Viṣṇu Purāṇa.

### Verse 16

*eṣa ha devaḥ pradiśo’nu sarvāḥ \|*  
*pūrvo ha jātaḥ sa u garbhe antaḥ \|\|*  
*sa eva jātaḥ sa janiṣyamāṇaḥ \|*  
*pratyaṅ janāṁs tiṣṭhati sarvato-mukhaḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O sages! He is Viṣṇu. He is all beings in all directions. Appearing previous to all beings, he has appeared within the universe. He will be born as all beings. Spread in all directions, he remains the ātmā of all beings.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse explained that the Lord is the ātmā of everything. O people (janāḥ)! This is an address to the sages. Divyo deva eko nārāyaṇaḥ: there is one shining supreme Lord Nārāyaṇa. (Subāla Upaniṣad) Deva indicates Viṣṇu. The Lord alone is all directions (pradiśaḥ). This means the Lord is all objects in all directions. Though previously he appeared as the form of Hiraṇyagarbha (Brahmā) (pūrvaḥ jātaḥ), spread everywhere (sarvato mukhaḥ), he will be born as all beings in the wombs. “How can all the individual jīvas be one with the Lord?” Pratyak means existing as the inner self. The Lord is the ātmā of all beings like Vāmadeva. Why are the jīvas not aware of this Lord within? He does no manifest when the jīvas have faults. The yogī having no fault, is qualified to see the Lord.

### Verse 17

*yo devo agnau yo’psu \|*  
*yo viśvaṁ bhuvanam āviveśa \|\|*  
*ya oṣadhīṣu yo vanaspatiṣu \|*  
*tasmai devāya namo namaḥ \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I offer respects to the Supreme Lord Viṣṇu, who is Agni, who is water, who has entered into the universe, into all herbs and trees.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is the ātmā of all beings as antaryāmī, but not in his svarūpa.

## Chapter Three

### Verse 1

*ya eko jālavān īśata īśanībhiḥ \|*  
*sarvāṁ lokān īśata īśanībhiḥ \|\|*  
*ya evaika udbhave sambhave ca \|*  
*ya etad vidur amṛtās te bhavanti \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who know that the one Lord Viṣṇu rules over all material and spiritual worlds by his śaktis, who know that he alone is the controller of creation and destruction, attain liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

The result of knowledge of the Lord is described.

Apahata-pāpmā divyo deva ekaḥ nārāyaṇaḥ: the one Lord free of sin is Nārāyaṇa. Subala Upaniṣad

The one Lord is Viṣṇu. That Lord, taking the form of māyā, or being endowed with guṇas (jālavān), rules over the worlds in the realm of prakṛti, and rules over the spiritual worlds which are beyond prakṛti, by his śaktis of jñāna, kriyā and bala. He rules over creation and destruction (saṁbhave). Sam means becoming one. It is another word for laya or merging. Those who know this Lord attain liberation.

### Verse 2

*eko hi rudro na dvitīyāya tasthur \|*  
*ya imāṁl lokān īśata īśanībhiḥ \|\|*  
*pratyaṅ janāṁs tiṣṭhati sañcukocānta-kāle \|*  
*saṁsṛjya viśvā bhuvanāni gopāḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The one Lord, who awards liberation, remained without any assistance. He remains the controller of the worlds by his śakti and, as the protector, creates and destroys the worlds.**

**Комментарий:**

“Others like Brahmā and Śiva are famous as creators and destroyers of the universe.” To refute this claim this verse is spoken.

O people (janāḥ)! The Lord remains directly (pratyaṅ tiṣṭhati) the controller (īśatā) of all planets by his śaktis (īśanībhiḥ). Having created the worlds (viśvā), and at the final time (anta-kale) withdrawing them (sañcukoca), the protector (gopāḥ), that one person, Rudra, remained. Rudra means he who destroys (drāvayati) the sickness (ruja) of saṁsāra. He delivers one from saṁsāra. There is no one else as his assistant (na dvitīyāya). Or there is no other method. No one except Viṣṇu can give liberation. Vedāham etaṁ puruṣaṁ mahāntam: I know the great puruṣa. This is a glorifcation of the Lord. Tam evaṁ vidvān amṛtam iha bhavati nānyaḥ panṭhā vidyate: knowing the Lord one becomes immortal: there is no other way. This forbids any other method for attaining liberation.

The convention meaning of Rudra is Śiva. However the etymological meaning, he who awards liberation, is not contrary to other opinions and makes sense in the present context. And the work’s intention is to show the supremacy of Viṣṇu.

### Verse 3

*viśvataś cakṣur uta viśvato-mukho \|*  
*viśvato-bāhur uta viśvatas-pāt \|\|*  
*saṁ bāhubhyāṁ dhamati sampatatrair \|*  
*dyāv-ābhūmī janayan deva ekaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His eyes are everywhere. His mouths are everywhere. His arms are everywhere. His feet are everywhere. He supplies the jīvas with arms and legs. The one Lord created everything from heaven to earth.**

**Комментарий:**

As also described in Gītā, the Lord has unlimited eyes, hands and feet. He has an eternal, attractive form. He produces the whole universe from heaven to earth without assistance. He supplies (saṁdhamati) the jīvas with arms (bāhubhyām) and legs (sampatatraiḥ). Supplying all beings with bodies and senses, he still remains independent, with unlimited bodies and senses.

### Verse 4

*yo devānāṁ prabhavaś codbhavaś ca \|*  
*viśvādhiko rudro maharṣiḥ \|\|*  
*hiraṇyagarbhaṁ janayāmāsa pūrvaṁ \|*  
*sa no buddhyā śubhayā saṁyunaktu \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord who is the source of the devatās and endows them with powers, who is supreme among all beings, who awards liberation, who is omniscient, who created Brahmā previous to the devatās, supply us with proper intelligence.**

**Комментарий:**

This is a prayer for attaining the Lord.

He is the source of Indra, Varaṇa, Rudra etc. From this Lord arises Rudra. From him the devatās develop excellence (prabhavaḥ). They develop excellence because he is their ruler.

*ahaṁ hi sarva-yajñānāṁ bhoktā ca prabhur eva ca*

**I am the enjoyer of all sacrifices and the master. BG** [**9.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.24)

He is prabhu, giving results of karma, and gives good qualities to Brahmā and Śiva so they can attain him by bhakti. They are all dependent on him. He is greater than all entities just by his small portions (viśvādhikaḥ).

*viṣṭābhāyam idaṁ kṛtsnām ekāṁśena sthito jagat*

**I am situated in this universe by just one of my portions. BG** [**10.42**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/10.42)

He destroys the sickness of saṁsāra (rudraḥ). He is the greatest sage (maharṣiḥ), since he has all knowledge. Previous to the creation of the devatās, he manifested Hiraṇyagarbha (Brahmā). May that Lord supply us with intelligence to understand the highest subject (śubhayā).

### Verse 5

*yā te rudra śivā tanūr aghorā’pāpa-kāśinī \|*  
*tayā nas tanuvā śantamayā giriśantābhicākaśīhi \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O destroyer of the disease of saṁsāra! Your body is auspicious, peaceful and destroys karma. Reveal yourself with a form that bestows joy to us.**

**Комментарий:**

This is a prayer for the Lord to reveal himself with his auspicious attractive form.

*adṛṣṭa-pūrvaṁ hṛṣito ’smi dṛṣṭvā bhayena ca pravyathitaṁ mano me \|*  
*tad eva me darśaya deva rūpaṁ prasīda deveśa jagan-nivāsa \|\|*

**I am delighted, having seen what was unseen before, but at the same time my mind is greatly troubled with fear. O Lord, show me your pleasing form. Be kind to me, O lord of the devas, the resting place of the universe. BG** [**11.45**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/11.45)

O destroyer of the disease of saṁsāra (rudra)! He is then described with the phrase giriśanta in order that he is not identified as Śiva. Giriśanta is analyzed as giriśam tanute: he who manifests Śiva, he who creates Śiva. His body is auspicious (śivā), peaceful (aghorā) and burns up karma (pāpa-kāśinī). This indicates a sāttvika form not a tamasika form. Reveal yourself to us with your form that bestows happiness (śantamayā).

### Verse 6

*yām iṣuṁ giriśanta haste bibharṣy astave \|*  
*śivāṁ giritra tāṁ kuru mā hiṁsīḥ puruṣaṁ jagat \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O protector of Vedānta! Cast the arrow that you hold in your hand (against ignorance). Do not throw me into the well of saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий:**

Giritra means he by whom Vedānta (giri) is protected and explained (trāyate). This has the same meaning as giriśanta. It means Viṣṇu. Throw (astave) the arrow that you hold in your hand, to destroy all obstacles to brahma-jñāna. Do not throw me (puruṣam), wandering in this worlds (jagat), into the well of saṁsāra (mā himsīḥ).

### Verse 7

*tataḥ paraṁ brahma paraṁ bṛhantaṁ \|*  
*yathānikāyaṁ sarva-bhūteṣu gūḍham \|\|*  
*viśvasyaikaṁ pariveṣṭitāram \|*  
*īśaṁ taṁ jñātvā’mṛtā bhavanti \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing the Supreme Lord, para-brahman, the largest being, who enters into the bodies of all beings and pervades the universe, the sages become liberated.**

**Комментарий:**

Though the largest in size (paraṁ bṛhantam) the Lord enters (pariveṣtitāram) into all beings according to the size of their body (yathānikāyam). Knowing the supreme Lord (īśam) who pervades the universe, sages become liberated.

### Verse 8

*vedāham etaṁ puruṣaṁ mahāntam \|*  
*āditya-varṇaṁ tamasaḥ parastāt \|\|*  
*tam eva viditvātimṛtyum eti \|*  
*nānyaḥ panthā vidyate’yanāya \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I know the true meaning. Knowing the supreme person, most effulgent, beyond darkness, one surpasses saṁsāra. There is no other path for attaining liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

Since this knowledge produces liberation, other knowledge is forbidden. Knowing the mahāpuruṣa Nārāyaṇa, one surpasses saṁsāra (ati mṛtum eti). There is no other path for attaining liberation (ayanāya). I know this meaning (etat aham veda). There can be no argument about this. This is a discussion among sages.

### Verse 9

*yasmāt paraṁ nāparam asti kiṁcid \|*  
*yasmān nāṇīyo na jyāyo’sti kaścit \|\|*  
*vṛkṣa iva stabdho divi tiṣṭhaty ekas \|*  
*tenedaṁ pūrṇaṁ puruṣeṇa sarvam \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is situated in the spiritual sky, and is firm like a tree. There is no one superior to him and no one who enters everything as he does. There is no one smaller and no one elder. Everything is pervaded by Viṣṇu.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowledge of ātmā produces liberation. How can knowledge of a shining person produce liberation? He is the actual ātmā of everything.

There is nothing superior to him. Nothing can enter into all things like the Lord and nothing is superior or larger than the Lord. In the spiritual world, making Brahmā and others like bits of grass, the Lord remains like an unbending tree. The puruṣa mentioned here is Vāsudeva. Everything is pervaded by Vāsudeva. He is pūṛṇa: pervading everything and the ātmā of everything. Thus all the jīvas with their bodies are dependent on him. Thus there is no one equal or superior to him.

### Verse 10

*tato yad uttarataraṁ tad arūpam anāmayam \|*  
*ya etad vidur amṛtāste bhavanti athetare duḥkham evāpi yanti \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Therefore, those who know the person who is supreme, who is devoid of a body produced from karma, and who is free of disease, attain liberation. Others simply suffer.**

**Комментарий:**

Therefore he is the highest being, devoid of forms produced by karma, free of suffering. Knowledge of this Lord produces liberation. That is summarized here. The previous verse stated that Viṣṇu is the highest being. This verse states that those who know this form become liberated. Those who do not know it must suffer. This was stated in verse 8. He who is the highest is without a form produced by karma (arūpam) and is free from disease (anāmayam). Those who know this puruṣa-tattva become liberated. Others attain misery. The word tataḥ indicates that this is a conclusion of the previous statements, rather than indicating that there is something higher than Vāsudeva. Yad can also refer to the material universe mentioned in the previous verse. The Lord is superior to that universe.

### Verse 11

*sarvānana-śiro-grīvaḥ sarva-bhūta-guhāśayaḥ \|*  
*sarva-vyāpī sa bhagavāṁs tasmāt sarva-gataḥ śivaḥ \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is all faces, all heads and all necks. He resides in the hearts of all beings. He is all pervading. He is Bhagavān. Thus, though he is all pervading he is most auspicious.**

**Комментарий:**

“It was mentioned that the Lord pervades everything. Thus he is related to material bodies. His relationship with the inferior is unavoidable.” This verse answers.

Since all bodies are his bodies, all faces are his. In this way he pervades everything. This is because he is Bhagavān.

*jñāna-śakti-balaiśvarya-viṛya-tejāṁsy aśeṣataḥ*  
*bhagavac chabda-vācyāni vinā heyair guṇādibhiḥ*

**The word bhagavān means that he is complete with power of mind (omniscience), power of senses, power of body, influence, control, and beauty, and is devoid of all inferior qualities. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 6.5.79)**

The word Bhagavān means that he is devoid of any inferior quality. Thus the Lord though pervading everything does not become impure even if he is the head etc.

### Verse 12

*mahān prabhur vai puruṣaḥ sattvasyaiṣa pravartakaḥ \|*  
*sunirmalām imāṁ prāptim īśāno jyotir avyayaḥ \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The great puruṣa is capable of awarding liberation since he stimulates sattva in the jīva. He is effulgent and indestructible. He desires the liberation of the jīva.**

**Комментарий:**

This verse confirms that this knowledge leads to liberation. The mahāpuruṣa alone (vai) is capable of giving results (prabhuḥ).

*aham hi sarva-yajñānāṁ bhokta ca prabhur eva ca*

**I am the enjoyer of all sacrifices and the most capable. BG** [**9.24**](https://vedarama.com/en/books/bg/9.24)

Since the word mahā is used the great result should be liberation. Thus the Lord alone is the giver of the highest result, liberation (prabhuḥ). Though previously mentioned, this fact is confirmed here. The reason for this is then given. He instigates sattva (sattvasya pravartakaḥ).

*jāyamānaṁ hi puruṣaṁ yaṁ paśyen madhusūdanaḥ*  
*sāttvikaḥ sa tu vijñeyaḥ sa vai mokṣārtha-cintakaḥ*

**The person when born upon whom the Lord glances should be considered sāttvika. He will think of liberation.**

By casting his glance, which produces knowledge, which leads to liberation, the Lord becomes the giver of liberation. Thus the Lord who is most effulgent and who is indestructible (avyayaḥ), rules over (īśānaḥ) pacification of material attraction in the form of liberation (sunirmalām).

### Verse 13

*aṅguṣṭha-mātraḥ puruṣo’ntarātmā \|*  
*sadā janānāṁ hṛdaye sanniviṣṭaḥ \|\|*  
*hṛdā manīṣā manasābhikḷpto \|*  
*ya etad vidur amṛtās te bhavanti \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The ātmā within all beings, the puruṣa measuring the size of a thumb, resides in the hearts of all beings at all times. He is perceived by intense bhakti. Those who know this person attain liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

The practice of this knowledge is explained. This ātmā within all beings, the size of the thumb, having the form of a person (puruṣaḥ), can be perceived by the determined (manīṣā) bhakti (hṛdā) That is the meaning of hṛḍā maniṣā, since that is the famous teaching everywhere.

### Verse 14

*sahasra-śīrṣā puruṣaḥ sahasrākṣaḥ sahasra-pāt \|*  
*sa bhūmiṁ viśvato vṛtvātyatiṣṭhad daśāṅgulam \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The puruṣa has a thousand heads, a thousand eyes and a thousand feet. He spreads over the universe made of ten factors but remains in the spiritual world.**

**Комментарий:**

Hearing that the Lord is the size of the thumb one may think the Lord is limited. This verse refutes that idea. He is the universal form endowed with thousands of heads and eyes. Ten fingers indicates limbs and earth means the universe. He is situated in the spiritual world beyond the universe made of ten limbs: five gross elements and five tanmātras.

### Verse 15

*puruṣa evedaṁ sarvaṁ yad bhūtaṁ yac ca bhavyam \|*  
*utāmṛtatvasyeśāno yad annenātirohati \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is the universe in the past and future. He is the lord of the spiritual world which does not flourish by material objects.**

**Комментарий:**

Since the Lord is spread everywhere, he is the whole universe. Amṛta means the place of liberation, the spiritual world filled with spiritual objects of enjoyment, which does not manifest by increase of material objects. The Lord is the ruler of this place. He is the controller of both the material and spiritual worlds.

### Verse 16

*sarvataḥ pāṇi-pādaṁ tat sarvato’kṣi-śiro-mukham \|*  
*sarvataḥ śrutimal loke sarvam āvṛtya tiṣṭhati \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord’s hands and feet are everywhere. His eyes, head and mouth are everywhere. His ears are everywhere. He covers everything in the universe.**

**Комментарий:**

Since he is the producer of the effects of hands and feet, extends everywhere by those effects. And the jīva in pure state becomes similar to the Lord. Thus their hands and feet are also everywhere.

*tadā vidvān puṇya-pāpe vidhuya*  
*nirañjanaṁ paramaṁ sāmyam upaiti*

**That learned person is free of all sin and piety, and being pure, attains a form similar to the Lord’s. Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad**

### Verse 17

*sarvendriya-guṇābhāsaṁ sarvendriya-vivarjitam \|*  
*sarvasya prabhum īśānaṁ sarvasya śaraṇaṁ suhṛt \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He knows through his senses and is devoid of senses. He gives results to all beings. He is the controller. He is the shelter of all. He is the friend of all.**

**Комментарий:**

He can know by using the senses (sarvendriya-guṇābhāsam). He manifests all senses if he desires. But they is not innate to him: he is devoid of all senses (sarvendriya-vivarjitam). He gives all results (sarvasya prabhum). Because of that, he is the controller (īśānam). He is the shelter of all (śaraṇam). He is to be attained by pure affection. Suhṛd-gatir nārāyaṇaḥ: Nārāyaṇa is the goal and friend. (Subala Upaniṣad) Since īśānam is in the neuter gender it does not mean the devatā īśāna or Śiva.

### Verse 18

*nava-dvāre pure dehī haṁso lelāyate bahiḥ \|*  
*vaśī sarvasya lokasya sthāvarasya carasya ca \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva situated in a body with nine gates wanders from body to body. Beyond him, the Lord is the controller of all beings moving and nonmoving.**

**Комментарий:**

“If the jīva develops similarity with the Lord having hands and feet everywhere, does he also become the giver of all results and become the controller of all?”

The jīva (haṁsaḥ), endowed with nine gates for excretion etc., identifies with his body (pure), which is quite different from the ātmā and comes and goes (lelāyate) in different bodies. He wanders about, dependent on another. How can he give results to all beings? The controller of all beings moving and nonmoving is beyond this (bahiḥ).

### Verse 19

*apāṇi-pādo javano grahītā \|*  
*paśyaty acakṣuḥ sa śṛṇoty akarṇaḥ \|\|*  
*sa vetti vedyaṁ na ca tasyāsti vettā \|*  
*tam āhur agryaṁ puruṣaṁ mahāntam \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Without hands and feet, he moves quickly. Without eyes he sees. Without ears he hears. He knows whatever is to be known. No one knows him. The sages say that he is the original cause, the mahāpuruṣa.**

**Комментарий:**

Thought without hands and feet, he carries out these functions. No one knows him but he knows everything. He is the original cause, the mahāpuruṣa.

### Verse 20

*aṇor aṇīyān mahato mahīyān \|*  
*ātmā guhāyāṁ nihito’sya jantoḥ \|\|*  
*tam akratum paśyati vīta-śoko \|*  
*dhātuḥ prasādān mahimānam īśam \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**-**

**Комментарий:**

He is so small that he can enter into the smallest beings and he is also the largest. He is the stimulator (ātmā) of all jīvas (jantoḥ), situated within their hearts. He is devoid of contamination of karma (akratuḥ). He is the Lord endowed with great glories (mahimānam). When a person sees the Lord, he becomes free of lamentation because of the mercy of the maintainer, the Lord (dhātuḥ).

*prasīdaty acyutas tasmin prasanne kleśa-saṁkṣayaḥ*

**The Lord becomes pleased with the devotee. Being pleased, all miseries of the devotee are destroyed. Viṣṇu Purāṇa**

### Verse 21

*vedāham etam ajaraṁ purāṇaṁ \|*  
*sarvātmānaṁ sarva-gataṁ vibhutvāt \|\|*  
*janma-nirodhaṁ pravadanti yasya \|*  
*brahma-vādino hi pravadanti nityam \|\|*

**Перевод:**

**I know that Lord without age, who is ancient, all pervading, and the ātmā of all beings, whom the sages proclaim is beyond birth and death because of his powers. The expounders of Brahman constantly proclaim this.**

**Комментарий:**

The sages proclaim (pravadanti) the Lord who is eternally devoid of birth (ajaram) because of his powers. I know that Lord the ātmā of all beings (sarvātmānam), who is without age, who is ancient, who is all pervading. Thus the expounders of Brahman constantly speak.

## Chapter Four

### Verse 1

*ya eko’varṇo bahudhā śakti-yogād \|*  
*varaṇān anekān nihitārtho dadhāti \|\|*  
*vi caiti cānte viśvam ādau sa devaḥ \|*  
*sa no buddhyā śubhayā saṁyunaktu \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The one syllable A, indicating the Lord, in the beginning, becomes many sounds of the alphabet by the śakti of the Lord. It destroys the universe at the end. May that syllable nondifferent from the Lord endow us with good intelligence.**

**Комментарий:**

This is a prayer to gain intelligence to understand how the Lord is the cause of everything.

The vowel A is all speech just as fire becomes many forms. The vowel A indicates Parabrahman (nihitārthaḥ).

*akāro vai sarva-vâk saisa sparsosmabhir vyajamânâ bahvī nānārūpa bhavati*

**The syllable A is all sounds. Manifesting as the sibilants and consonants, it assumes many forms. Aitareya Ārāṇyaka**

A-kareṇocyate viṣṇuḥ: Visnu is the vowel A. (Padma Purāṇa) This vowel produces all the sounds of the alphabet. It destroys (eti) the whole universe (viśvam) at pralaya (ante). May the vowel A, nondifferent from the Lord (devaḥ), endow us with good intelligence

### Verse 2

*tad evāgnis tad ādityas \|*  
*tad vāyus tad u candramāḥ \|\|*  
*tad eva śukraṁ tad brahma \|*  
*tad āpas tat prajāpatiḥ \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is fire. He is the sun. He is air. He is the moon. He is the constellations. He is Brahmā. He is water. He is Prajāpati.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is the ātmā of all beings. Śukram means bright, and refers to the constellations.

### Verse 3

*tvaṁ strī pumān asi \|*  
*tvaṁ kumāra uta vā kumārī \|\|*  
*tvaṁ jīrṇo daṇḍena vañcasi\|*  
*tvaṁ jāto bhavasi viśvato-mukhaḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are a woman. You are a man. You are a young man. You are a young woman. You, aged, wander about with a stick. You give birth to all things.**

**Комментарий:**

You, becoming old, wander about (vañcasi) taking support of a stick. You are born as all forms.

### Verse 4

*nīlaḥ pataṅgo harito lohitākṣas \|*  
*taḍid-garbha ṛtavaḥ samudrāḥ \|\|*  
*anādimat tvaṁ vibhutvena vartase \|*  
*yato jātāni bhuvanāni viśvā \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**You are the blue bird, the green bird and the bird with red eyes. You are the lightning in the clouds. You are the seasons and the oceans. You are without beginning. You exist as everything by pervading them. From you, all the worlds are born.**

**Комментарий:**

You are different type of birds. You are the oceans. You are without beginning. You are that from which all worlds are born. You are the cause of everything in the universe. This means that you are the ātmā within all these beings. They are not your svarūpa. You pervade them all by entering as ātmā. This is indicated by vibhutvena vartase: you exist as these things by pervading them.

### Verse 5

*ajām ekāṁ lohita-śukla-kṛṣṇāṁ \|*  
*bahvīḥ prajāḥ sṛjamānāṁ sa-rūpāḥ \|\|*  
*ajo hy eko juṣamāṇo’nuśete \|*  
*jahāty enāṁ bhukta-bhogām ajo’nyaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One jīva serves unborn prakṛti which is red, white and black, and which creates many forms similar to it. Another jīva having enjoyed, gives up prakṛti.**

**Комментарий:**

The distinction between the Lord, jīvas and prakṛti were stated with bhoktā bhogyaṁ preritāraṁ ca matvā and kṣaraṁ pradhāṁ amṛtāksaṛaṁ haraḥ. Ajām is prakṛti, without birth, which creates various forms similar to it, which are red, white and black. The ignorant jīva follows prakṛti (anuśete), serving it (juṣamānaḥ). Another jīva in knowledge having enjoyed prakṛti, gives it up because of detachment.

### Verse 6

*dvā suparṇā sayujā sakhāyā \|*  
*samānaṁ vṛkṣaṁ pariṣasvajāte \|\|*  
*tayor anyaḥ pippalaṁ svādv atty \|*  
*anaśnann anyo abhicākaśīti \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**There are two birds with similar qualities, friends, living in the same tree. One bird eats the pippala fruit and the other bird, not eating, merely observes.**

**Комментарий:**

Jīva is the enjoyer of prakṛti, not the Lord, though both are related to prakṛti. An example is given to show this. Sayujā means the two have similar qualities like knowledge which are like wings (parṇa) by which they move about. They are friends, being close together. They are together in the body, which, like a tree, is cut down. The jīva eats the fruit, or enjoys the results of karma. The Lord, not eating the fruit, remains in his glory.

### Verse 7

*samāne vṛkṣe puruṣo nimagno’ \|*  
*nīśayā śocati muhyamānaḥ \|\|*  
*juṣṭaṁ yadā paśyaty anyam īśam \|*  
*asya mahimānam iti vīta-śokaḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva, absorbed in eating in the same tree, becomes bewildered and laments because of prakṛti. When he sees the Lord who is pleased and understands the Lord’s glory, he gives up lamentation.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva is bewildered by prakṛti (anīśayā). His svarūpa of jñāna, that he is the servant of the Lord and his ānanda have disappeared. The jīva, thinking he is fat or thin, becomes one with that idea in a body which will be cut down like a tree. He experiences suffering from relationship with the body. When the jīva sees the Lord as his support, his controller and his master, when he sees the Lord who is pleased (juṣṭam) with the jīva’s actions, and sees the glory of the Lord as the controller of the universe, then he gives up lamentation. Or the jīva laments because he is incapable (anīśayā). Seeing the glory of the Lord who can deliver him and has affection for him, he gives up lamentation.

### Verse 8

*ṛco akṣare parame vyoman \|*  
*yasmin devā adhi viśve niṣeduḥ \|\|*  
*yas taṁ na veda kim ṛcā kariṣyati \|*  
*ya it tad vidus ta ime samāsate \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**What use are the Vedas to the person who does not know the indestructible Lord, conclusion of the Vedas, under whom the devaṭas take shelter? Those who know the Lord experience happiness.**

**Комментарий:**

Akṣara means that which does not decay. Ṛk means the Vedas. What use are the Vedas to the person who does not know the Lord, under whom (yasmin akṣare) all the devatās take shelter (niṣeduh), and who is the conclusion (parame vyoman) of the Vedas (ṛcaḥ). One who studies the Vedas but does not know the meaning is like an inert pillar or an ass. Those who know the Lord, rejecting what is unfavorable, attain happiness. What is the use of the Vedas for the person who does not know the Lord in the indestructible spiritual sky, which establishes the meaning of the Vedas, where the eternal associates reside?

### Verse 9

*chandāṁsi yajñāḥ kratavo vratāni \|*  
*bhūtaṁ bhavyaṁ yac ca vedā vadanti \|\|*  
*asmān māyī sṛjate viśvam etat \|*  
*tasmiṁś cānyo māyayā sanniruddhaḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**From prakṛti, the Lord creates the Vedas, sacrifices, kratus,vratas and all that the Vedas describe in the past and future. The jīva is bound up in the world by māyā.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord (māyī) creates all the actions related to the Vedas from prakṛti (asmāt). Why can the jīva not be the creator? The jīva (anyaḥ) is bewildered by māyā under which he takes shelter. Since the jīva cannot inspire māyā to act, he cannot be the creator.

### Verse 10

*māyāṁ tu prakṛtiṁ vidyān māyinaṁ ca maheśvaram \|*  
*tasyāvayava-bhūtais tu vyāptaṁ sarvam idaṁ jagat \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One should know māyā or prakṛti and the controller of māyā, Viṣṇu. The whole world is spread with the limbs of the Lord, the jīvas.**

**Комментарий:**

One should know prakṛti made of the three guṇas also called māyā, the cause of various amazing creations and the controller of māyā, who is the great Lord. This does not refer to Śiva.

*yad vedādau svaraḥ prokto vedānte ca pratiṣṭhitaḥ*  
*tasya prakṛti-līnasya yaḥ paraḥ sa maheśvaraḥ*

**He whom the Vedas describe and who is established in the Upaniṣads, he who is supreme, into whom prakṛti merges, is the great Lord. Mahā Upaniṣad**

Thus Nārāyaṇa is maheśvara and the jīvas are his aṁśas. They are called limbs (avayavaiḥ) since they are inseparable from the Lord. These jīvas pervade the whole world.

### Verse 11

*yo yoniṁ yonim adhitiṣṭhaty eko \|*  
*yasminn idam saṁ ca vicaiti sarvam \|\|*  
*tam īśānaṁ varadaṁ devam īḍyaṁ \|*  
*nicāyyemāṁ śāntim atyantam eti \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Respecting the Lord who rules over prakṛti, in whom the universe is dissolved and created with variety, respecting the Lord who controls everything, who awards blessings and shines, and who is worthy of praise, one attains the highest peace.**

**Комментарий:**

“How can the Lord who is without change, be the material cause?” The Lord rules over prakṛti (yonim). The word yonim is repeated so the student can understand. This statement indicates that prakṛti is the body of the Lord.

### Verse 12

*yo devānāṁ prabhavaś codbhavaś ca \|*  
*viśvādhipo rudro maharṣiḥ \|\|*  
*hiraṇyagarbhaṁ paśyata jāyamānaṁ \|*  
*sa no buddhyā śubhayā saṁyunaktu \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the Lord who is the source of the devatās and who gives them powers, who is the ruler of the world, who gives benedictions and is omniscient, who glanced on Brahmā at his birth, endow us with good intelligence.**

**Комментарий:**

Mercy of the Lord is the cause of knowledge. This is a prayer to invoke that mercy. He cast his glance on Brahmā as Brahmā was being born (jāyamānam), blessing him with unprecedented knowledge. May he do the same for me.

### Verse 13

*yo devānām adhipo \|*  
*yasmin lokā adhiśritāḥ \|\|*  
*ya īśe asya dvipadaś catuṣpadaḥ \|*  
*kasmai devāya haviṣā vidhema \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**We worship with oblations the supreme Lord who is the master of the devatās, in whom all planets take shelter and who controls animals and humans.**

**Комментарий:**

We worship with oblations the Lord who is the controller of all, who is the support of all, who is the master. Worship through sacrifices leads to meditation and this leads to attaining the Lord.

### Verse 14

*sūkṣmātisūkṣmaṁ kalilasya madhye \|*  
*viśvasya sraṣṭāram aneka-rūpam \|\|*  
*viśvasyaikaṁ pariveṣṭitāraṁ \|*  
*jñātvā śivaṁ śāntim atyantam eti \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing that the Lord is finer than the finest, that he is the creator of everything within the universe, that he manifests many forms, that he enters everything, that he alone is everything, and that he is most auspicious, one attains liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

Knowing Paramātmā who enters the smallest things, who creates universe using forms like Brahmā and Prajāpati within the universe (kalilasya) and who enters into everything as antaryāmī but is uncontaminated (pariveṣṭitāram), one attains liberation (śāntim).

### Verse 15

*sa eva kāle bhuvanasya goptā \|*  
*viśvādhipaḥ sarva-bhūteṣu gūḍhaḥ \|\|*  
*yasmin yuktā brahmarṣayo devatāś ca \|*  
*tam evaṁ jñātvā mṛtyu-pāśāṁś chinatti \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He liberates the jīva from the world at the appropriate time. He is the master of the universe. He resides all beings. Meditating on the Lord in whom all the sages and devatās engage their minds, one becomes liberated.**

**Комментарий:**

One should know the Lord in whom the great sages and devatās engage their mind (yuktā), who frees person from saṁsāra (bhuvanasya goptā) when their karmas are ripe (kāle), since he is the ruler (adhipaḥ), hidden in all beings as the antaryāmī and master. By knowing the relationship between the master and servant, between the ātmā and the body, one becomes qualified for liberation. Meditating on the Lord in many forms as if directly seeing him (jñātvā), one attains liberation.

### Verse 16

*ghṛtāt paraṁ maṇḍam ivātisūkṣmaṁ \|*  
*jñātvā śivaṁ sarva-bhūteṣu gūḍham \|\|*  
*viśvasyaikaṁ pariveṣṭitāraṁ \|*  
*jñātvā devaṁ mucyate sarva-pāśaiḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing the one lord of the universe, the most auspicious being, who enters everything and remains hidden within, like the essence of ghee in milk, one become liberated from all bondage.**

**Комментарий:**

Just as ghee spreads its fine essence (maṇḍam) in milk, the auspicious Lord spreads himself everywhere in a subtle way, hard to detect, but without being contaminated. Knowing him, auspicious because of faultless qualities (śivam), one becomes liberated.

### Verse 17

*eṣa devo viśva-karmā mahātmā \|*  
*sadā janānāṁ hṛdaye saṁniviṣṭaḥ \|\|*  
*hṛdā manīṣā manasābhikḷpto \|*  
*ya etad vidur amṛtās te bhavanti \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who know this Lord, creator of the universe, the great ātmā residing in the hearts of all people, who is attained by concentrated bhakti, attain liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

Viśva-karmā means the creator of the universe.

### Verse 18

*yadā’tamas tan na divā na rātrir \|*  
*na san na cāsac chiva eva kevalaḥ \|\|*  
*tad akṣaraṁ tat savitur vareṇyaṁ \|*  
*prajñā ca tasmāt prasṛtā purāṇī \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**When only darkens existed, without day or night, without the universe either with form or without form, only the auspicious Lord existed. He is indestructible, to be worshipped in the sun. From him the jīvas’ consciousness has expanded at the time of creation.**

**Комментарий:**

When was darkness alone without the universe with form (sat) or without form (asat), without divisions of day and night, at that time when Brahmā and all beings were destroyed, one entity remained (kevalaḥ), possessing auspiciousness (śivaḥ), whereas jīvas have restricted knowledge etc.

sa tu nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ: Nārāyaṇaḥ is the ātmā of all beings and the master. (Mahābhārata) He is indestructible (akṣaram) situated in the sun (savituḥ) and to be worshiped (vareṇyam). Therefore at the time of creation, from him the consciousness (prajñā) of the jīvas has been expanded (prasṛtā). This explains the gāyātrī mantra: dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt: may he inspire our intelligence.

### Verse 19

*nainam ūrdhvaṁ na tiryañcaṁ \|*  
*na madhye na parijagrabhat \|\|*  
*na tasya pratimā asti \|*  
*yasya nāma mahad yaśaḥ \|\|19\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**No one can grasp the Lord from a high, middle or low position. He, whose name is most famous, has no equal.**

**Комментарий:**

No one can grasp the Lord who is exists in the forms of humans and plants (ūrdhvam tiryañcam). Or no one can grasp the Lord from a high, middle or low position since he has great power. His name is most famous (mahad yaśaḥ). No being is similar to him.

### Verse 20

*na saṁdṛśe tiṣṭhati rūpam asya \|*  
*na cakṣuṣā paśyati kaścanainam \|\|*  
*hṛdā hṛdi-sthaṁ manasā ya enam \|*  
*evaṁ vidur amṛtāste bhavanti \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**His form cannot be seen. No one can see him with their eyes. Those who know the Lord situated in the heart by concentrated bhakti attain liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

The meaning is easily understood.

### Verse 21

*ajāta ity evaṁ kaścid bhīruḥ prapadyate \|*  
*rudra yat te dakṣiṇaṁ mukhaṁ tena māṁ pāhi nityam \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**O destroyer of the disease of saṁsāra! Thinking of you as the unborn, this lowly person, in fear, meditates upon your compassionate face. By that meditation please deliver me from saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий:**

O destroyer of the disease of saṁsāra (rudra)! You are unborn. You are devoid of birth which is characteristic of saṁsāra. Thinking in this way, I meditate upon (prāpadye) on your kind face (dakṣiṇam mukham). By that meditation destroy saṁsāra for me (mām pāhi). Or I surrender to your lotus feet, which are like a face.

### Verse 22

*mā nas toke tanaye mā na āyuṣi \|*  
*mā no goṣu mā na aśveṣu rīriṣaḥ \|\|*  
*vīrān mā no rudra bhāmito \|*  
*vadhīr haviṣmantaḥ sadasi tvā havāmahe \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Do not harm us, though we are attached to sons, lineage, long life, cows and horses. O destroyer of the disease of saṁsāra! Becoming angry, do not harm the powerful processes to attain liberation. Carrying offerings, we worship you in the assembly.**

**Комментарий:**

Do not harm me, though I am inclined to sons and lineage, long life, cows and horses. O destroyer of the disease of saṁsāra (rudra)! Becoming angry (bhāmitaḥ) because of my offenses, do not harm methods for liberation such as jñāna and vairāgya, endowed with power (vīrān). Carrying ingredients for worship, we worship you in the assembly.

## Chapter Five

### Verse 1

*dve akṣare brahma-pare tv anante \|*  
*vidyāvidye nihite yatra gūḍhe \|\|*  
*kṣaraṁ tv avidyā hy amṛtaṁ tu vidyā \|*  
*vidyāvidye īśate yas tu so’nyaḥ \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Jñāna and karma, means to Brahman, have unlimited forms, and are intimately attached to the jīva. Karma gives temporary results and jñāna gives eternal results. He who rules over jñāna and karma is different from them.**

**Комментарий:**

Vidyā (jñāna) which gives eternal results and avidyā (karma) which gives temporary results, are forms of worshipping Brahman (brahma-pare). They appear in many forms (anante). They are attached to the jīva, who cannot get free of them (nihite gūḍḥe). The other, worshipped by them, who gives their results, controls them (īśate).

### Verse 2

*yo yoniṁ yonim adhitiṣṭhaty eko \|*  
*viśvāni rūpāṇi yonīś ca sarvāḥ \|\|*  
*ṛṣiṁ prasūtaṁ kapilaṁ yas tam agre \|*  
*jñānair bibharti jāyamānaṁ ca paśyet \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**That Lord is he who rules over all jīvas, all forms and all births, who cast his glance upon Kapila, born before creation, just as he was being born, and gave him knowledge.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord rules over (adhitiṣṭhati) all forms of jīvas in total and individually (yonim yonim). He bestowed knowledge to Kapilawho was born before creation and before that cast his glance on him as he was being born (jāyamānam).

### Verse 3

*ekaikaṁ jālaṁ bahudhā vikurvann \|*  
*asmin kṣetre saṁharaty eṣa devaḥ \|\|*  
*bhūyaḥ sṛṣṭvā patayas tatheśaḥ \|*  
*sarvādhipatyaṁ kurute mahātmā \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord creates various nets within prakṛti and then withdraws them. Again creating the prajāpatis, the Lord rules of them, since he is endowed with the greatest power.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord makes variety of effects (such as mahattattva) within prakṛti (kṣetre) and then destroys them (saṁharati). Again creating the prajāpatis and others (patayaḥ) in another kalpa within prakṛti, he rules over them all since he is endowed with the greatest powers (mahātmā).

### Verse 4

*sarvā diśa ūrdhvam adhaś ca tiryak \|*  
*prakāśayan bhrājate yad v anaḍvān \|\|*  
*evaṁ sa devo bhagavān vareṇyo \|*  
*yoni-svabhāvān adhitiṣṭhaty ekaḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Just as the sun shines upwards, downwards and horizontally, so the one supreme Lord, to be worshipped by aspirants for liberation, rules over all beings.**

**Комментарий:**

“How can one person rule over all beings at the same time?”

The sun (anaḍvān) shines simultaneously upwards, horizontally and downwards. It is like an ox (anaḍuḥ), since moves the wheel of time. The Lord (bhagavān), to be worshipped (vareṇyaḥ) by the aspirants for liberation, alone (ekaḥ) rules over all beings.

### Verse 5

*yac ca svabhāvaṁ pacati viśva-yoniḥ \|*  
*pācyāṁś ca sarvān pariṇāmayed yaḥ \|\|*  
*sarvam etad viśvam adhitiṣṭhaty eko \|*  
*guṇāṁś ca sarvān viniyojayed yaḥ \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord gives objects their natures and transforms objects that should be transformed. The one Lord rules over the whole universe. He puts the guṇas into motion.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord gives natures such as heat to material elements such as fire by his will. He transforms things which should be changed (pācyān). He puts the guṇas like sattva into motion (viniyojayet).

### Verse 6

*tad veda-guhyopaniṣatsu gūḍhaṁ \|*  
*tad brahmā vedate brahma-yonim \|\|*  
*ye pūrvaṁ devā ṛṣayaś ca tad vidus \|*  
*te tan-mayā amṛtā vai babhūvuḥ \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Brahmā knows the Lord, the source of the Vedas, who is hidden in the Upaniṣads of the Vedas. The sages and devatās who previously knew the Lord, becoming similar to the Lord, attained liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

Brahmā knows the Lord, source of the Vedas (brahma-yonim), who is hidden in the secret Upaniṣads of the Vedas. Others do not know. The sages and devatās who previously knew the Lord, becoming similar (tanmāyā), all became liberated.

### Verse 7

*guṇānvayo yaḥ phala-karma-kartā \|*  
*kṛtasya tasyaiva sa copabhoktā \|\|*  
*sa viśva-rūpas triguṇas trivartmā \|*  
*prāṇādhipaḥ sañcarati sva-karmabhiḥ \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva, associating with the three guṇas, performing actions which yield results, enjoying those results, takes various bodies and becomes afflicted by lust, anger and greed. He follows three paths. Assisted by his prāṇas, he wanders about because of his karmas.**

**Комментарий:**

The svarūpa of the Lord and liberation through knowledge of him has been explained. Now the svarūpa of the jīva is explained.

He has connection (anvayaḥ) with the guṇas. He is influenced by sattva, rajas and tamas, and performs actions that give results (phala-kamra-kartā). He enjoys the results (kṛtasya tasya upabhoktā). He is a doer and enjoyer. According to those actions he takes forms of plants, humans and birds (viśva-rūpaḥ) and becomes endowed with qualities like lust anger and greed (triguṇaḥ). He goes on three paths: that of the devatās, that of the pitrs and that of lower forms (tri-vartmā). Assisted by his prāṇas (prānādhipaḥ) or bound by ropes of lust, he wanders about by his karmas.

### Verse 8

*aṅguṣṭha-mātro ravi-tulya-rūpaḥ \|*  
*saṅkalpāhaṅkāra-samanvito yaḥ \|\|*  
*buddher guṇenātma-guṇena caiva \|*  
*ārāgra-mātro’py aparo’pi dṛṣṭaḥ \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva is the size of a thumb and similar in form to the sun. He is endowed with ahaṅkāra, which decides on obtaining certain results. He is endowed with guṇas of sattva, rajas and tamas in his antaḥkarana and apprehends objects by that consciousness. In scriptures the jīva is said to be the size of the tip of a spoke.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva is endowed with apprehension of various objects (ātma-guṇena), arising from the ātmā’s nature of consciousness, and by the guṇas of sattva, rajas and tamas in the antaḥkarana (buddheḥ guṇena). As a result, the jīva becomes endowed with ahaṅkāra thinking of his identity and deciding to obtain various results (saṅkalpa). According to scriptures the jīva is the size of a thumb. However scriptures also say the jīva is even smaller, the size of the tip of a spoke.

### Verse 9

*bālāgra-śata-bhāgasya śatadhā kalpitasya ca \|*  
*bhāgo jīvaḥ sa vijñeyaḥ sa cānantyāya kalpate \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva is known to be the size of the tip of hair divided by a hundred and again by a hundred. He becomes unlimited in the state of liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

Here the speaker states his own opinion. The jīva is the size of tip of hair divided by a hundred and again a hundred. By expanding dharma-bhūta-jñāna (attributive knowledge) in liberation, the jīva can become unlimited in terms of powers.

### Verse 10

*naiva strī na pumān eṣa na caivāyaṁ napuṁsakaḥ \|*  
*yad yac charīram ādatte tena tena sa yujyate \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva is not female, nor male, not neuter. When he assumes a body, he develops characteristics of male or female.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva becomes man or women only by associating with a material body.

### Verse 11

*saṅkalpana-sparśana-dṛṣṭi-homair \|*  
*grāsāmbu-vṛṣṭyātma-vivṛddhi-janma \|\|*  
*karmānugāny anukrameṇa dehī \|*  
*sthāneṣu rūpāṇy abhisamprapadyate \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**One attains excellent birth plentiful food, water, and rain by performing pious vows, touching pious objects, seeing pious objects and performing sacrifices. The jīva, according to his karma, attains bodies in various families.**

**Комментарий:**

The cause of obtaining a body is explained. Purifying the ātmā by pious vows, touching or seeing pious objects like the Gaṅgā, and by charities and homas, one attains an excellent birth (vivṛddhi-janma). Gradually, according to his karma, he attains bodies (rūpāni) in brāhmaṇa families etc. (sthāneṣu).

### Verse 12

*sthūlāni sūkṣmāṇi bahūni caiva \|*  
*rūpāṇi dehī sva-guṇair vṛṇoti \|\|*  
*kriyā-guṇair ātma-guṇaiś ca teṣāṁ \|*  
*saṁyoga-hetur aparo’pi dṛṣṭaḥ \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva accepts various bodies big and small because of his qualities—qualities of actions and qualities of his mind. The qualities of the mind arise from previous karmas.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva accepts small or big bodies because of various actions such as sacrifices (kriya-guṇaiḥ) which are caused by qualities of his mind (ātma-guṇaiḥ) such as attachment, which cause him to seek various results from his actions. The cause of the qualities of the mind is previous karma (dṛṣtaḥ).

### Verse 13

*anādy-anantaṁ kalilasya madhye \|*  
*viśvasya sraṣṭāram aneka-rūpam \|\|*  
*viśvasyaikaṁ pariveṣṭitāraṁ \|*  
*jñātvā devaṁ mucyate sarva-pāśaiḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Knowing the shining Lord who is without beginning or end, who creates everything within the universe, who has many forms, who is one among all, and enters everything, one becomes liberated.**

**Комментарий:**

Kalilasya means of the material universe.

### Verse 14

*bhāva-grāhyam anīḍākhyaṁ bhāvābhāva-karaṁ śivam \|*  
*kalā-sarga-karaṁ devaṁ ye vidus te jahus tanum \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who know the shining Lord, attainable by bhakti, who is known as having no material support, who is the creator and destroyer, who is most auspicious, and who creates the sixteen kalās, attain liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is attained by bhakti (bhāva-grāhyam). He is famous as a person without a material body, without material support (anīḍākhyam). He creates and destroys the universe (bhāvābhāva-karam). He is at all times the abode of auspiciousness. (śīvam). He is the cause of the kalās starting with prāṇa and ending with name.Those who knew this Lord in previous times became from prakṛti (tanum jahuḥ). Repetition of the last phrase indicates the end of the chapter.

## Chapter Six

### Verse 1

*svabhāvam eke kavayo vadanti \|*  
*kālaṁ tathānye parimuhyamānāḥ \|\|*  
*devasyaiṣa mahimā tu loke \|*  
*yenedaṁ bhrāmyate brahma-cakram \|\|1\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Some say that the cause of the universe operating is svabhāva. Others, bewildered by māyā say that the cause is time and karma. But the wheel of the universe turns by the power of the Lord manifested in this world.**

**Комментарий:**

The qualities of the Lord are again described. The Lokāyatas say that svabhāva (nature of things) is the cause of the universe operating. Others, bewildered by the māyā of the Lord, say that time and karma are the cause. They speak in this way because they have no knowledge of the qualities of the Lord, described in the Upaniṣads, which are causes of worshipping him. The wheel of the universe, taking shelter of Brahman, turns by the power of Paramātmā.

### Verse 2

*yenāvṛtaṁ nityam idaṁ hi sarvaṁ \|*  
*jñaḥ kāla-kālo guṇī sarva-vid yaḥ \|\|*  
*teneśitaṁ karma vivartate ha \|*  
*pṛthivy-ap-tejo’nila-khāni cintyam \|\|2\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He eternally pervades the universe. He is omniscient, the destroyer of time, possesses all good qualities and possesses all types of knowledge. One should meditate on the Lord who creates the universe made of earth, water fire air and ether.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord pervades the worlds (āvṛtam idam sarvam) at all times (nityam). He is omniscient (jñāḥ). He devours time (kāla-kālaḥ).

*na kālas tatra vai prabhuḥ sa kālasya prabhū rajan*

**Time is not the master of the Lord. The Lord is the master of time. Mahābhārata**

He possesses all types of knowledge (sarva-vit).

*tejo-balāiśvaryā-mahāvabodhaḥ suvīrya-śaktyādi-guṇaiekarāśiḥ*

**He has an abundance of beauty, strength, power, intelligence, courage and śakti. Viṣṇu Purāṇa**

One should meditate on the Lord by whom the universe (karma) made of the five gross elements (pṛthivy-ap-tejo’nila-khāni ) has been produced (vivartate).

### Verse 3

*tat karma kṛtvā vinivartya bhūyas \|*  
*tattvasya tattvena sametya yogam \|\|*  
*ekena dvābhyāṁ tribhir aṣṭabhir vā \|*  
*kālena caivātma-guṇaiś ca sūkṣmaiḥ \|\|3\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Giving up other actions, one should engage in karmas centered on the Lord. One should develop knowledge of being the servant of the Lord by his mercy, and become endowed with the Lord’s glance of mercy at birth, with mercy of guru and the Lord, with strength, learning and silence, and with the eight limbs of yoga. One should be endowed with the subtle qualities of ātmā gradually.**

**Комментарий:**

Rejecting other actions (vinivṛrtya) one should perform actions for the Lord in the form of nitya and naimittika karmas. Tattva refers to Nārāyaṇa. tattvaṁ nārāyaṇaḥ paraḥ: Nārāyaṇa is the tattva. (Nārāyaṇa Upaniṣad) One should develop knowledge of one’s servitude to Nārāyana (tattvasya yogam) by the mercy of the Lord (tattvena). One should be endowed with the merciful glance of the Lord at birth (ekena), with bhakti for the Lord and guru (dvābhyām), with strength, learning and silence (tribhiḥ), and with the eight limbs of yoga (aṣṭabhiḥ).

### Verse 4

*ārabhya karmāṇi guṇānvitāni \|*  
*bhāvāṁś ca sarvān viniyojayed yaḥ \|\|*  
*teṣām abhāve kṛta-karma-nāśaḥ \|*  
*karma-kṣaye yāti sa tattvato’nyaḥ \|\|4\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Taking support of his prārabdha-karmas the jīva experiences all conditions of his karmas. In the absence of that experience, when he has destroyed all karma, he attains Brahman. He is truly different from the Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

Taking support of prārabdha karmas (karmānī guṇānvitāni) the jīva experiences all conditions related to himself. With the end of this experience of results of karma, because of destruction of karmas, he attains Brahman. One attains Brahman with destruction of karmas (karma-kṣaye). tasya tāvad eva ciraṁ yāvan na vimokṣye: as long as the body lasts, he is not liberated. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad [6.14.2](https://vedarama.com/en/books/chandogya/6.14.2)) He is truly different (anyaḥ) from Paramātmā.

### Verse 5

*ādiḥ sa saṁyoga-nimitta-hetuḥ \|*  
*paras trikālād akalo’pi dṛṣṭaḥ \|\|*  
*taṁ viśva-rūpaṁ bhava-bhūtam īḍyaṁ \|*  
*devaṁ sva-citta-stham upāsya pūrvam \|\|5\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The jīva is the cause of the meeting with the Lord. He is beyond the three aspects of time. He should from the beginning worship the Lord of infinite forms, the cause of everything, praiseworthy and effulgent, situated in the heart.**

**Комментарий:**

The jīva (saḥ) is the principle (ādiḥ) cause for meeting with the Lord (saṁyoga-nimitta-hetuḥ). The jīva according to scripture is different, without material limbs and superior to his body, since he is not limited by the three aspects of time (trikālāt akalaḥ). One should from the beginning worship the Lord situated in one’s heart, who has many bodies. He is to be praised since he has qualities of being free from sin. He is effulgent, having a svarūpa of bliss and self-revealing knowledge.

### Verse 6

*sa vṛkṣa-kālākṛtibhiḥ paro’nyo \|*  
*yasmāt prapañcaḥ parivartate’yam \|\|*  
*dharmāvahaṁ pāpa-nudaṁ bhageśaṁ \|*  
*jñātvātma-stham amṛtaṁ viśva-dhāma \|\|6\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord from who the universe arises is superior to material forms, time and everything temporary. Knowing the Lord who inspires good actions, who destroys sin, who is the abode of excellent qualities, who is devoid of low qualities, who supports the universe and is situated in within the jīva, one attains liberation.**

**Комментарий:**

He from who this universe arises is superior (paraḥ anyaḥ) to trees, time and material forms. Trees suggests the bodies which are cut down like trees. One should know the Lord who destroys sin which are obstacles to developing knowledge (pāpa-nudam), who inspires good actions favorable to the Lord (dharmāvaham), who is the lord of good qualities such as aiśvarasya samagrasya vīryasyas yaśasaḥ śriyaḥ etc. (bhageśam), who is the support for the universe (viśva-dhāma), who is devoid of low qualities such as death (amṛtam) and who is present as the antaryāmī within the jīva (ātma-stham). Knowing this, one becomes liberated.

### Verse 7

*tam īśvarāṇāṁ paramaṁ maheśvaraṁ \|*  
*taṁ devatānāṁ paramaṁ ca daivatam \|\|*  
*patiṁ patīnāṁ paramaṁ parastād \|*  
*vidāma devaṁ bhuvaneśam īḍyam \|\|7\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May we know that supreme among all controllers, the greatest controller, the supreme among the devatās, the master among all masters and he who is superior to everything great, the Lord of the worlds, worthy of worship, the shining Lord.**

**Комментарий:**

This is a prayer for knowledge. The meaning is clear.

### Verse 8

*na tasya kāryaṁ karaṇaṁ ca vidyate \|*  
*na tat-samaś cābhyadhikaś ca dṛśyate \|\|*  
*parāsya śaktir vividhaiva śrūyate \|*  
*svābhāvikī jñāna-bala-kriyā ca \|\|8\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord does not have a material body or material senses. There is no one equal or superior to him. It is said that he has various superior śaktis. He has natural activity through jñāna and bala.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord does not have a material body (kāryam) or material senses (karaṇam). By jñāna the Lord creates and by bala the Lord destroys. Kriyā means the activities of jñāna and bala.

### Verse 9

*na tasya kaścit patir asti loke*  
*na ceśitā naiva ca tasya liṅgam \|*  
*sa kāraṇaṁ karaṇādhipādhipo*  
*na cāsya kaścij janitā na cādhipaḥ \|\|9\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He has no master in this world. He has no controller. He has no marks by which he can be known. He is the final cause. He is the lord of the jīvas. He has no creator and no ruler.**

**Комментарий:**

Liṅgam means causes of knowing him. Karaṇādhipaḥ is a jīva.

### Verse 10

*yas tantu-nābha iva tantubhiḥ pradhānajaiḥ svabhāvataḥ \|*  
*deva ekaḥ svam āvṛṇoti sa no dadhātu brahmāpyayam \|\|10\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**May the shining Lord, who covers the jīvas with prakṛti as a spider covers himself with a web, arrange that our material bodies disappear.**

**Комментарий:**

As the spider covers himself with his web (tantubhiḥ), the one Lord covers the jīvas (svam) with the material elements (pradhāna-jaiḥ) by his will (svabhāvataḥ). He puts their knowledge to sleep. May that Lord make the difference in form between me and him disappear (brahmāpyayam).

### Verse 11

*eko devaḥ sarva-bhūteṣu gūḍhaḥ \|*  
*sarva-vyāpī sarva-bhūtāntarātmā \|\|*  
*karmādhyakṣaḥ sarva-bhūtādhivāsaḥ \|*  
*sākṣī cetā kevalo nirguṇaś ca \|\|11\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The one Lord, free of karma, resides unknown within all beings. He is all-pervading, residing as the ātmā of all beings. He inspires actions and is the shelter of all beings. He is the witness, the creator of the universe. He is devoid of material guṇas and is free of material action.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is present within all beings, but unknown to them (gūḍhaḥ). Spreading everywhere, he is the ātmā within all beings, entering their bodies. He inspires them to perform activity (karmādhyakṣaḥ). He is the creator (cetā) of the universe. He is not controlled by the guṇas (nirguṇaḥ). He is neutral (kevalaḥ), without performing material actions to gain results. He is free of karma (devaḥ) as in apahata-pāpmā deva ekaḥ: the one Lord is free of karma. (Chāndogya Upaniṣad)

### Verse 12

*eko vaśī niṣkriyāṇāṁ bahūnām \|*  
*ekaṁ bījaṁ bahudhā yaḥ karoti \|\|*  
*tam ātma-sthaṁ ye’nupaśyanti dhīrās \|*  
*teṣāṁ sukhaṁ śāśvataṁ netareṣām \|\|12\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Those who know the one Lord who controls all jīvas, who are beyond actions in in prakṛti, who is the one cause of the universe, who expands as many elements, and who is situated within all beings experience eternal bliss, and not others.**

**Комментарий:**

The one Lord controls all the jīvas who are devoid of prakṛti and thus without material action (niṣkriyānām). He is the one seed of the universe, who expands as mahatattva etc. (bahudhā). Those who know this antaryāmī (ātma-stham) become liberated.

### Verse 13

*nityo nityānāṁ cetanaś cetanānām \|*  
*eko bahūnāṁ yo vidadhāti kāmān \|\|*  
*tat kāraṇaṁ sāṅkhya-yogādhigamyaṁ \|*  
*jñātvā devaṁ mucyate sarva-pāśaiḥ \|\|13\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is the chief eternal, conscious being among many eternal, conscious beings. He is the cause, to be known by sāṅkhya and yoga scriptures. Knowing that Lord one becomes liberated.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is the chief eternal, conscious being among the many eternal, conscious beings. He responds to their desires: he gives liberation through jñāna. He is understood through the scriptures of sāṅkhya and yoga, which have the Lord as their subject. Sāṅkhyo yogaḥ pañcarātraṁ vedāḥ pāśupataṁ tathā ātma-pramāṇāny etani: the sāṅkhya, yoga and pañcarāta texts, the Vedas, pāśupata scriptures give proof of the Lord.

### Verse 14

*na tatra sūryo bhāti na candra-tārakaṁ*  
*nemā vidyuto bhānti kuto’yam agniḥ \|*  
*tam eva bhāntam anubhāti sarvaṁ*  
*tasya bhāsā sarvam idaṁ vibhāti \|\|14\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The sun does not shine there. Nor the moon or the stars. Lightning does not flash, what to speak of fire. Everything shines following the Lord who shines. By his light everything shines.**

**Комментарий:**

The meaning is clear.

### Verse 15

*eko haṁso bhuvanasyāsya madhye*  
*sa evāgniḥ salile saṁniviṣṭaḥ \|*  
*tam eva viditvātimṛtyum eti*  
*nānyaḥ panthā vidyate’yanāya \|\|15\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is like a swan amidst the material world. He is the leader, and has entered into the ocean. Knowing him one becomes liberated. There is no other path except knowing him.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord, like a swan among birds, exists within the world of matter and jīvas (bhuvanasya). He has entered the water. Yam antaḥsamudre kavayo vadanti: The wise describe the Lord residing in the ocean. (Taittirīya Ārāṇyaka) He is the primary leader (agniḥ) who grants liberation. Or he is fire because he burns up the ropes of saṁsāra.

### Verse 16

*sa viśva-kṛd viśva-vid ātma-yonir \|*  
*jñaḥ kāla-kālo guṇī sarva-vid yaḥ \|\|*  
*pradhāna-kṣetra-jña-patir guṇeśaḥ \|*  
*saṁsāra-mokṣa-sthiti-bandha-hetuḥ \|\|16\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**The Lord is the creator of everything. He possesses everything. He dwells in the jīva. He is omniscient. He controls time. He has all good qualities. He inspires all knowledge. He is the master of prakṛti and the jīva. He is endowed with great qualities. He is the cause of liberation, being situated in saṁsāra and creating bondage in saṁsāra.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is the creator of everything (viśva-kṛt). He possesses everything (viśva-vit). Vit means to attain. He is situated in the jīva (ātma-yoniḥ) as antaryāmī. He is omniscient (jñaḥ). He is the controller of time (kāla-kālaḥ). He inspires all knowledge (sarva-vit). He is the Lord of prakṛti and the jīvas (pradhāna-kṣetra-jña-patiḥ). He possesses all good qualities such as knowledge (guṇeśaḥ). He is the cause of freedom from saṁsāra, of being situated in saṁsāra and being bound by saṁsāra.

### Verse 17

*sa tan-mayo hy amṛta īśa-saṁstho \|*  
*jñaḥ sarvago bhuvanasyāsya goptā \|\|*  
*ya īśe’sya jagato nityam eva \|*  
*nānyo hetur vidyata īśanāya \|\|17\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**He is composed of himself. He is untouched by saṁsāra and is situated as the controller. He is omniscient and goes everywhere. He is the protector of the universe. He controls the world eternally. There is no other cause for ruling the world.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is made completely of himself (tan-mayaḥ). He inspires himself. He is not touched by saṁsāra (amṛtaḥ). He is firmly situated as the controller (īśa-saṁstaḥ). He rules the world eternally (īśe nityam). His rulership is eternal even in the liberated state of the jīva.

### Verse 18

*yo brahmāṇaṁ vidadhāti pūrvaṁ \|*  
*yo vai vedāṁś ca prahiṇoti tasmai \|\|*  
*taṁ ha devaṁ ātma-buddhi-prakāśaṁ \|*  
*mumukṣur vai śaraṇam ahaṁ prapadye \|\|18\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Desiring liberation, I surrender to the Lord, who created Brahmā previously and gave him the Vedas, and who reveals intelligence so that one may know him.**

**Комментарий:**

This is prayer of surrender to being the Lord under control. There is nothing other than the Lord. He is the supreme goal.

The Lord produced Brahmā from his lotus navel and by giving him the Vedas, endowed him with the power to create the universe. Desiring liberation, I surrender to the Lord who reveals the intelligence to understand him (ātma-buddhi-prakāśam).

### Verse 19-20

*niṣkalaṁ niṣkriyaṁ śāntaṁ niravadyaṁ nirañjanam \|\|*  
*amṛtasya paraṁ setuṁ dagdhendanam ivānalam \|\|19\|\|*

*yadā carmavad ākāśaṁ veṣṭayiṣyanti mānavāḥ \|*  
*tadā devam avijñāya duḥkhasyānto bhaviṣyati \|\|20\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**If one does not know the Lord who is without material limbs, who has no duties, who is devoid of the six urges, who is faultless, who is devoid of material contact, who is the main means of liberation, and who is self-manifesting, like fire burning wood, one will destroy suffering only when ether can be folded like skin.**

**Комментарий:**

The Lord is without parts (niṣkalam), without duties (niṣkriyam), devoid of the six urges such as hunger (śāntam). He is devoid of the defect of ignoring those who surrender (niravadyam). He is devoid of material association as his very nature (nirañjanam). He is the chief means for liberation (amṛtasya param setum). He is self-manifesting like fire burning wood.

Not knowing this Lord, there will be an end to suffering only when feeble men can fold ether like skin. One can attain liberation without the Lord just as one can fold ether. This means it is impossible.

### Verse 21

*tapaḥ-prabhāvād deva-prasādāc ca \|*  
*brahma ha śvetāśvataro’tha vidvān \|\|*  
*atyāśramibhyaḥ paramaṁ pavitraṁ \|*  
*provāca samyag-ṛṣi-saṅgha-juṣṭam \|\|21\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**Because of his austerity and the mercy of the Lord, learned Śvetāśvatara spoke this most pure knowledge of Brahman, relished by the gathering of great sages, to renounced devotees.**

**Комментарий:**

Manasaś cendriyāṇāṁ caikāgryaṁ paramaṁ tapa: the supreme austerity is concentrating the senses and mind on one point. (Brahma Purāṇa) The sage named Śvetāśvatara, endowed with knowledge by the power of his austerity, spoke about this most pure Brahman, which was relished by a gathering of sages such as Vāmadeva to the highest renounced devotees, most worthy of respect (atyāśramibhyaḥ).

### Verse 22

*vedānte paramaṁ guhyaṁ purā-kalpe pracoditam \|*  
*nāpraśāntāya dātavyaṁ nāputrāyāśiṣyāya vā punaḥ \|\|22\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**This most secret knowledge of Vedānta was spoken to Brahmā in a previous kalpa. One should not teach this knowledge to persons with uncontrolled senses and mind, to person who are not sons or are not disciples.**

**Комментарий:**

The knowledge is hidden in Vedānta because it is most secret. It was taught to Brahmā in a previous kalpa. One should speak this knowledge to those who are peaceful, to sons and to disciples and not to others.

### Verse 23

*yasya deve parā bhaktiḥ yathā deve tathā gurau \|*  
*tasyaite kathitā hy arthāḥ prakāśante mahātmanaḥ \|\|23\|\|*

**Перевод:**

**To the great soul who has great bhakti for the Lord and guru, the meanings of what was spoken are revealed.**

**Комментарий:**

The meanings are revealed to the great soul who has bhakti for the Lord and for guru and not to others. Repetition indicates the end of the chapter.
